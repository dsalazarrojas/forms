# workforce_training_partner_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential workforce training partners. The purpose of this form is to gather contact details and partnership interests from interested partners. This form will be used to facilitate communication and potential partnership opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your organization or company in the "Partner Name" field.
2. Provide the first name of the contact person in the "Contact First Name" field.
3. Provide the last name of the contact person in the "Contact Last Name" field.
4. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Phone" field.
6. Select the relevant training expertise your organization has or is interested in from the "Training Expertise" dropdown menu (e.g., Training and Development, Training and Placement, Workforce Readiness).
7. Briefly describe your organization's interest in the partnership in the "Partnership Interest" field (optional).
8. If necessary, schedule a follow-up meeting or appointment using the "Follow-up" date field.
9. Add any additional comments or information about your organization in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).
10. Enter your organization's name in the "Contact Organization" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Email** (`partner_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person. Please use a valid email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Phone** (`partner_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Training Expertise** (`training_expertise`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more relevant training expertise your organization has or is interested in:
	+ **Training and Development**: Provide expertise in developing training programs.
	+ **Training and Placement**: Provide expertise in placing employees in suitable training programs.
	+ **Workforce Readiness**: Provide expertise in preparing employees for new roles or responsibilities.
* **Partnership Interest** (`partnership_interest`, note, optional): Briefly describe your organization's interest in the partnership.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, date, optional): Schedule a follow-up meeting or appointment using the date picker.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your organization.
* **Contact Organization** (`contact_organization`, text, required): Enter your organization's name.
