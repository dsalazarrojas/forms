# Manuscript Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manuscript Submission Form is designed to collect submissions for manuscripts, such as book proposals or other written materials, from authors and researchers. This form allows you to easily and efficiently submit your work for review and potential publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the type of manuscript you are submitting (e.g. book proposal, other).
2. Enter the title of your manuscript.
3. Provide your name and contact information (email and phone number).
4. Enter a brief abstract of your manuscript.
5. Upload your manuscript file in a supported format (pdf, doc, xls, ppt, xlsx).
6. Select the date and time of submission.
7. Provide any additional comments or notes about your manuscript.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submission** (1):
	+ A brief description of your manuscript, if desired.
* **Manuscript Type** (2):
	+ Select the type of manuscript you are submitting (e.g. book proposal, other).
* **Manuscript Title** (3):
	+ Enter the title of your manuscript.
* **Authors Name** (4):
	+ Enter your name and any co-author names.
* **Email** (5):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (6):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Manuscript Abstract** (7):
	+ Provide a brief summary of your manuscript.
* **Manuscript File** (8):
	+ Upload a copy of your manuscript in a supported format (pdf, doc, xls, ppt, xlsx).
* **File Format** (9):
	+ Select the format of your uploaded manuscript file.
* **Submission Date** (10):
	+ Select the date you are submitting your manuscript.
* **Submission Time** (11):
	+ Select the time of submission.
* **Manuscript Review** (12):
	+ Any additional comments or notes about your manuscript.

Note: Please ensure that your manuscript file is in one of the supported formats before uploading. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
