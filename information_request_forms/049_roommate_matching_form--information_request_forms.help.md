# Roommate Matching Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Roommate Matching Form is designed to help you find a suitable roommate or a place to live with. To increase the chances of a successful match, please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Personal Information page with your details.
2. On the Living Preferences page, select your preferred room type and location.
3. In the Roommate Preferences page, specify your desired roommate characteristics.
4. Add any additional information you'd like to share with potential roommates.
5. Finally, enter your contact email and phone number so that we can reach out to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your full name as you'd like it to be displayed in our matching process.
* **Age**: Please enter your age to help us understand your stage of life and living requirements.
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding potential matches.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number for easier communication.
* **Address**: Enter your current address for us to understand your living situation.
* **Living Status**: Select your current marital status to help us match you with people with similar circumstances.
* **Preferred Room Type**: Choose the type of living space you're looking for (Apartment, House, Condo, Townhouse, or Other).
* **Preferred Location**: Select the location you'd like to live in (City, Suburbs, Rural, or Other).
* **Smokes**: Indicate whether you smoke or not.
* **Pets**: Specify if you have pets or not.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any extra information you think would be helpful for potential roommates to know.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as "required" in the form. Please fill out as much information as you're comfortable sharing to increase the chances of finding a suitable match.
