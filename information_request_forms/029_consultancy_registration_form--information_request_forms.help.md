# Consultancy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consultancy Registration Form is designed to gather essential information about a consultancy that wants to be listed on our services directory. This includes contact details, consultancy needs, and other relevant information to facilitate a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the **Organization name**, which should be the name of the company or consultancy you are representing.
2. Enter the **Contact person** name and **Contact email** address. This information will be used as the primary contact point for our team.
3. If desired, you can provide a **Contact phone** number for additional contact options.
4. In the **Consultancy needs summary** field, please provide a brief description of the services your consultancy provides or the specific needs you have for our services.
5. If you have a preferred contact method (e.g., Email, Phone, or Video call), select the corresponding option from the dropdown list.
6. If you have an **Estimated budget** range or estimate for the engagement, please enter it. This is optional but helps us understand your needs better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization name** (Organization name): The name of the company or consultancy you are registering.
* **Contact person** (Contact person): The name of the primary contact person for the registration.
* **Contact email** (Contact email): The email address of the primary contact person for follow-up and communication.
* **Contact phone** (Contact phone): The phone number of the primary contact person (optional).
* **Consultancy needs summary** (Consultancy needs summary): A brief description of the services your consultancy provides or the specific needs you have for our services.
* **Preferred contact method** (Preferred contact method): Select the method you prefer for communication (Email, Phone, or Video call).
* **Estimated budget** (Estimated budget): Your estimated budget range or amount for the engagement (optional).
