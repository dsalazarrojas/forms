# Do Not Contact Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Do Not Contact Request Form is used to request that a contact method be removed or reduced. You can use this form to indicate your preference for how you would like to be contacted and why.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred contact method (email, phone, or other).
2. Choose how often you would like to be contacted (weekly, biweekly, monthly, or rarely).
3. If you selected "other" for your contact method, please enter your preferred method in the "Other Contact Method" field.
4. Enter a brief description of your reason for submitting this form in the "Reason" field.
5. Provide any additional comments or information you think is relevant.
6. If you want to specify to whom the request should be assigned, select one or multiple individuals from the "Assigned To" dropdown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Method** (email, phone, or other): Select the method you do not want to be contacted by.
* **Frequency Of Contact** (weekly, biweekly, monthly, or rarely): Choose how often you do not want to be contacted.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address you do not want to be contacted by (only if "email" was selected as the contact method).
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number you do not want to be contacted by (only if "phone" was selected as the contact method).
* **Other Contact Method**: If you selected "other" for your contact method, enter the specific method you prefer not to be contacted by.
* **Reason**: Briefly describe why you are submitting this form.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional information or comments that might be helpful.
* **Assigned To**: Specify one or multiple individuals to whom the request should be assigned.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check that you have not submitted this form before for the same reason. If you have already submitted it, there's no need to submit it again.
* If you have any further requests or questions, please let us know in the comments field.
