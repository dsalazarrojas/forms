# ESG Policy Alignment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ESG Policy Alignment Request Form is used to request a review of an organization's policies in relation to Environmental, Social, and Governance principles.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your organization's name in the "Organization Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your organization's policies in the "Description" field.
3. If you have any supporting documents, such as policy statements or reports, please upload them in the "Supporting Documents" field.
4. Choose the review team that will review your policies by selecting one of the options in the "Review Team" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field to ensure that we can contact you regarding the status of your request.
6. If you have any additional information or comments, please provide it in the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your organization's name as it appears on your letterhead or business cards.
* **Policy Details** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of your organization's policies related to Environmental, Social, and Governance principles.
* **Supporting Documents** (Text, Optional): Upload any supporting documents, such as policy statements or reports, that demonstrate your organization's commitment to ESG principles.
* **Review Request** (Text, Optional): If you have any specific requests or comments about the review process, please include them here.
* **Review Team** (Select One, Optional): Choose the review team that will review your policies. Please select one of the available options:
	+ Team A
	+ Team B
	+ Team C
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address to ensure that we can contact you regarding the status of your request.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **Description** (Text, Optional): Please provide any additional information or comments that you think will help the review team understand your policies.

## Tips
* Please note that this form is intended for internal use and is not a public-facing form.
* Only one form per organization should be submitted, and duplicates will be deleted.
* Please ensure that your policies are up-to-date and accurate before submitting this form.
