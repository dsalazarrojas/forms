# Public Awareness Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Awareness Material Request Form is a tool for requesting public awareness materials such as brochures, posters, and digital assets for use in various settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill out the required fields as accurately as possible.
2.  Provide the correct information for the requested fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Public Awareness Material Request** (`page_1`): Please enter a brief description of the reason for your public awareness material request. This field is not required.
*   **None** (`page_2`): This field is intentionally left blank. Please fill out the next field for further information.
*   **Submission Details** (`form_submission`): Enter any additional details about your submission, such as the purpose of the material request.
*   **What information are you requesting?** (`information_requested`): Specify the specific information or materials you are requesting (e.g., brochures, posters, or digital assets).
*   **Digital Assets** (`digital_assets`): Select all the digital assets you are requesting (e.g., brochures, posters, digital assets).
*   **Quantity** (`request_quantity`): Enter the quantity of the requested materials you need.
*   **Request Date** (`request_date`): Enter the date you would like to have the requested materials by.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your contact phone number in case we need to contact you for more information.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your contact email address for any further queries or updates.

## Tips

*   Ensure to provide accurate information to avoid delays in processing your request.
*   If you're requesting digital assets, ensure you have the necessary equipment to download and access them.
*   If you need to request additional items, you can always submit another form.
