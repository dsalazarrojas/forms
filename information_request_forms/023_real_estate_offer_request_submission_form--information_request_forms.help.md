# Real Estate Offer Request Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in making an offer on a property. The information provided will be reviewed by the property owner or their representative, and a response will be sent to the user with more information about the property and the next steps in the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your first name, last name, email address, phone number, address, and any additional comments you may have in the message field.
2. Make sure to enter your email address and phone number correctly, as these will be used to contact you about your offer.
3. If you have any questions or would like to attach additional information, use the message field to do so.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address that you would like to be used to contact you about your offer.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter a phone number that you would like to be used to contact you about your offer.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address where you can be reached during business hours.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have about the property.
