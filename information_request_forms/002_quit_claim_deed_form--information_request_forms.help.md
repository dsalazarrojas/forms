# Quit Claim Deed Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quit Claim Deed Form is a legal document that transfers ownership of a property from one individual (Grantor) to another (Grantee). This form is used to collect the necessary information to complete the transfer process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the property being transferred.
2. Enter the name of the individual granting the property (Grantor).
3. Enter the name of the individual receiving the property (Grantee).
4. Select the date of record for the transfer.
5. Enter the value of the consideration (the value of the transfer).
6. Enter the property's address.
7. Enter the Grantor's address.
8. Enter the Grantee's address.
9. Enter the county where the property is located.
10. Enter the name of the county where the property is located.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Description** (`property_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the property being transferred.
* **Grantor Name** (`grantor_name`, text, optional): The name of the individual granting the property.
* **Grantee Name** (`grantee_name`, text, optional): The name of the individual receiving the property.
* **Date of Record** (`date_of_record`, date, optional): The date the transfer is recorded.
* **Consideration** (`consideration`, text, optional): The value of the transfer.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): The address of the property being transferred.
* **Grantor's Address** (`property_description`, text, optional): The address of the individual granting the property.
* **Grantee Address** (`grantee_address`, text, optional): The address of the individual receiving the property.
* **County** (`county`, text, optional): The county where the property is located.
* **County Name** (`county_name`, text, optional): The name of the county where the property is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): The state where the property is located.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any issues with the transfer process.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please consult a legal expert or the relevant authorities in your area.
