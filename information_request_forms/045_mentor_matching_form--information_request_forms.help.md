# mentor_matching_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mentor_matching_form is a form used to collect demographic and personal details from users to facilitate effective mentorship matching.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "enter_name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "enter_email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "enter_phone" field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "enter_date_of_birth" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Type your name as it appears on your official documents, such as your passport or driver's license.
* **Enter Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached for further contact.
* **Enter Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Enter Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Select your date of birth in the format DD-MM-YYYY.
