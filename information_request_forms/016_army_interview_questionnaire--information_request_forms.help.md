# Army Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Army Interview Questionnaire form is designed to gather essential information about a candidate's background, skills, and motivation for joining the Army. By filling out this form, candidates can showcase their qualifications and interests to assess their suitability for a career in the Army.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the basic information section, which includes full name, date of birth, email address, and phone number.
2. In the educational background section, select your highest level of education and years of professional work experience.
3. If you have any previous military experience, select the corresponding option.
4. In the career interest and motivation section, choose your desired Army career field and explain why you want to join the Army.
5. Describe your long-term career goals.
6. In the personal qualities and skills section, list your key personal strengths, mention any leadership or team experience, and describe your problem-solving approach.
7. In the physical fitness and health section, select your current fitness level and mention any medical conditions or concerns you may have.
8. In the additional questions section, select when you are available to start and whether you are willing to relocate or deploy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`candidate_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`candidate_dob`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email Address** (`candidate_email`, `email`, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone Number** (`candidate_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Highest Level of Education** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your highest level of education from the available options.
* **Years of Professional Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have worked professionally.
* **Previous Military Experience** (`previous_military`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have any previous military experience.
* **Desired Army Career Field** (`desired_career_field`, `select_one`, required): Choose your desired Army career field from the available options.
* **Why Do You Want to Join the Army?** (`motivation_reason`, `text`, required): Describe why you want to join the Army.
* **Long-Term Career Goals** (`career_goals`, `text`, required): Describe your long-term career goals.
* **Key Personal Strengths** (`key_strengths`, `text`, required): List your key personal strengths.
* **Leadership or Team Experience** (`leadership_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of leadership or team experience.
* **Problem-Solving Approach** (`problem_solving`, `text`, required): Describe your problem-solving approach.
* **Current Physical Fitness Level** (`fitness_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your current physical fitness level.
* **Any Medical Conditions or Concerns** (`medical_conditions`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have any medical conditions or concerns.
* **When Are You Available to Start?** (`availability`, `select_one`, required): Select when you are available to start.
* **Willing to Relocate or Deploy** (`commitment_willingness`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are willing to relocate or deploy.
* **Additional Information or Questions** (`additional_info`, `text`, required): Mention any additional information or questions you may have.
