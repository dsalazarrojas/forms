# New Horse Info Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Horse Info Form is a tool used to collect and document information about a horse. This form is designed for horse owners, breeders, or veterinarians to provide accurate and up-to-date details about a horse's breeding history, identification, health, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the horse's information, making sure to complete all required fields (if any).
2. Enter the horse's ID number, if applicable.
3. Provide the horse's breeding history, including its sire and dam.
4. Enter the horse's birthdate in MM/DD/YYYY format.
5. Describe the horse's color and size.
6. Provide information about the horse's breed and bloodlines.
7. Enter your owner information (name, contact details, etc.).
8. Enter the veterinarian's information (name, contact details, etc.).
9. Upload a photo of the horse, if possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Horse Info Form (Page 1)**: This is the main field for entering the horse's information.
	+ Label: Horse Info Form
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Enter a brief description of the horse.
* **Breeding History**: This field is for entering the horse's breeding history, including its sire and dam.
	+ Label: Breeding History
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Provide a detailed account of the horse's breeding history.
* **Sire and Dam**: Select from the options to indicate the horse's sire and dam.
	+ Label: Sire and Dam
	+ Type: Select Multiple
	+ Purpose: Choose the horse's sire and dam from the provided options.
* **Horse ID**: Enter the horse's unique ID number.
	+ Label: Horse ID
	+ Type: Number
	+ Purpose: Identify the horse in the database.
* **Birthdate**: Enter the horse's birthdate in MM/DD/YYYY format.
	+ Label: Birthdate
	+ Type: Date
	+ Purpose: Record the horse's birthdate.
* **Color and Size**: Enter a brief description of the horse's color and size.
	+ Label: Color and Size
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Describe the horse's color and size.
* **Breed and Bloodlines**: Enter a brief description of the horse's breed and bloodlines.
	+ Label: Breed and Bloodlines
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Record the horse's breed and bloodlines.
* **Owner Info**: Enter your contact information as the owner of the horse.
	+ Label: Owner Info
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Provide your contact information for the horse's records.
* **Veterinarian Info**: Enter the veterinarian's contact information.
	+ Label: Veterinarian Info
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Record the veterinarian's contact information for the horse's health records.
* **Photo**: Upload a photo of the horse, if possible.
	+ Label: Photo
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Upload a photo of the horse for visual reference.
