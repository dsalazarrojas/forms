# new_hire_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about new hires for internal tracking and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure you are only entering data for new hires who are currently within the organization.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please refer to your team leader or HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **new_hire_details** (1, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the new hire, including their name, job position, and any other relevant details.
* **job_title** (2, text, required: false): Enter the new hire's job title.
* **job_description** (3, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the new hire's job role and responsibilities.
* **job_type** (4, select_one, required: false): Check if the new hire is 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate their job type.
* **job_type_options** (5, select_multiple, required: false): Select any additional options related to the new hire's job type.
* **employment_status** (6, select_one, required: false): Choose the new hire's employment status from the options provided.
* **employment_status_options** (7, select_multiple, required: false): Select any additional options related to the new hire's employment status.
* **job_location** (8, text, required: false): Enter the new hire's job location.
* **hire_date** (9, date, required: false): Enter the date the new hire was hired.
* **hire_time** (10, time, required: false): Enter the time of day the new hire was hired.
* **new_hire_contact** (11, text, required: false): Enter the new hire's contact information.
* **new_hire_email** (12, email, required: false): Enter the new hire's email address.
* **job_status** (13, select_one, required: false): Choose the new hire's job status from the options provided.
* **job_status_options** (14, select_multiple, required: false): Select any additional options related to the new hire's job status.
* **job_level** (15, number, required: false): Enter the new hire's job level.
* **job_category** (16, select_one, required: false): Choose the new hire's job category from the options provided.
* **job_category_options** (17, select_multiple, required: false): Select any additional options related to the new hire's job category.
* **job_title_2** (18, text, required: false): Enter an alternate job title for the new hire.
* **job_description_2** (19, text, required: false): Enter a detailed description of the new hire's alternate job role.
