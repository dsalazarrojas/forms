# COVID 19 Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from individuals about their experiences, concerns, or questions related to COVID-19. This form helps our organization understand the situation and provide assistance to those in need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Select the category of inquiry from the "Category of Inquiry" dropdown menu.
5. Enter a brief description of your subject in the "Subject of Inquiry" field.
6. Provide a detailed description of your inquiry in the "Detailed Description" field.
7. Select your urgency level from the "Urgency Level" dropdown menu.
8. Indicate whether this inquiry is about yourself or someone else.
9. If applicable, enter the name of the affected person.
10. Indicate whether COVID-19 symptoms are involved.
11. If applicable, describe the symptoms.
12. Indicate whether you have attached any relevant documents.
13. If applicable, describe the documents.
14. Select your preferred method of contact.
15. Enter the best time to contact you.
16. Agree to receive follow-up communications.
17. Finally, enter the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name to help us identify you and address your inquiry.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **required**): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, **required**): Enter your email address so we can reply to your inquiry.
- **Category of Inquiry** (`inquiry_category`, select_one, **required**): Select the category of your inquiry from the options provided.
- **Subject of Inquiry** (`subject`, text, **required**): Enter a brief description of your subject.
- **Detailed Description** (`inquiry_details`, text, **required**): Describe your inquiry in detail.
- **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, **required**): Select your urgency level from the options provided.
- **Is this inquiry about yourself** (`affected_person`, select_one, **required**): Indicate whether this inquiry is about you or someone else.
- **Name of affected person** (`affected_person_name`, text, **optional**): If this inquiry is about someone else, enter their name.
- **COVID-19 symptoms involved** (`related_symptoms`, select_one, **required**): Indicate whether COVID-19 symptoms are involved.
- **Describe symptoms** (`symptoms_description`, text, **optional**): If applicable, describe the symptoms.
- **Relevant documents attached** (`relevant_documents`, select_one, **required**): Indicate whether you have attached any relevant documents.
- **Description of attached documents** (`document_description`, text, **optional**): If applicable, describe the documents.
- **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, **required**): Select your preferred method of contact.
- **Best time to contact you** (`best_time_contact`, text, **optional**): Enter the best time to contact you.
- **Agree to receive follow-up communications** (`follow_up_agreement`, select_one, **required**): Agree to receive follow-up communications.
- **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of submission.
