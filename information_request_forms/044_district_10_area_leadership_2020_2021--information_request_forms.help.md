# District 10 Area Leadership 2020 2021 - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from Area Leaders for the District 10 Area Leadership 2020 2021. It will help the Area Leaders submit their data in a standardized format, making it easier for the organization to track and review their progress.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the area director position from the dropdown menu.
2. Fill in your name.
3. Enter your last name.
4. Enter your first name.
5. Enter your email address in the format "john.doe@example.com".
6. Enter your phone number.
7. (Optional) Enter your address.
8. Enter your goals for the position.
9. (Optional) Enter any additional goals.
10. Enter any further goals.
11. Enter any other additional goals.
12. Enter any other other goals.
13. Enter any other other other goals.
14. Enter any other other other other goals.
15. Enter any other other other other other goals.
16. Enter any further notes.
17. Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
18. Enter the date submitted.
19. Enter the start date of the position.
20. Enter the end date of the position.
21. Enter the title of the person submitting the form.
22. Enter the initials of the person submitting the form.
23. Enter the department of the person submitting the form.
24. Enter the area of the person submitting the form.
25. Enter the region of the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **area director** (`area_director`, `select_one`, required): Select the area director position you are submitting for.
* **name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **last name** (`name_last`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name. (Note: This field is optional, but including it is recommended.)
* **first name** (`name_first`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name. (Note: This field is optional, but including it is recommended.)
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address in the format "john.doe@example.com".
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address. (Note: This field is optional, but including it is recommended.)
* **goals** (`goals`, `text`, required): Enter your goals for the position.
* **other goals** (`goals_other`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional goals.
* **other other goals** (`goals_other_other`, `text`, optional): Enter any further goals.
* **other other other goals** (`goals_other_other_other`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional goals.
* **other other other other goals** (`goals_other_other_other`, `text`, optional): Enter any other goals.
* **other other other other other goals** (`goals_other_other_other_other`, `text`, optional): Enter any further goals.
* **notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any further notes.
* **submitted by** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **submit date** (`submit_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date submitted.
* **start date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the position.
* **end date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the position.
* **submitted by title** (`submitted_by_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the person submitting the form.
* **submitted by initials** (`submitted_by_initials`, `text`, required): Enter the initials of the person submitting the form.
* **submitted by department** (`submitted_by_department`, `text`, required): Enter the department of the person submitting the form.
* **submitted by area** (`submitted_by_area`, `text`, required): Enter the area of the person submitting the form.
* **submitted by region** (`submitted_by_region`, `text`, required): Enter the region of the person submitting the form.
