## Purpose
The Adult Personal Analysis Questionnaire is designed to help you understand yourself better by gathering information about your personality traits, interests, and personal development areas. This form will aid in identifying your strengths, areas for growth, and potential career interests.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by reviewing the form's introduction page, which explains the purpose and instructions for the questionnaire.
- Read through each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
- Select all relevant options that apply to you for each field.
- Ensure that you complete all required fields, specifically the assessment date field, which is required for this form.
- Take your time to provide thoughtful and detailed answers for the text fields, such as key strengths and areas for development.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. -- Personal Analysis --
This is an introduction page that sets the context for the questionnaire. It is not a required field, but it's essential to understand the purpose and instructions before proceeding.

### 2. Name
* **Name** (text, optional): Your name as it appears on your identification documents or how you prefer to be addressed.

### 3. Preferred contact method
* **Preferred contact method** (select one: Email, Phone, None): Select the method that you would like to be contacted for future follow up.

### 4. Dominant personality traits
* **Dominant personality traits** (select multiple: Introvert, Extrovert, Analytical, Creative, Practical): Choose all personality traits that best describe you.

### 5. Key strengths
* **Key strengths** (text, optional): Describe your top 3 strengths that you feel are essential to your personality.

### 6. Areas for development
* **Areas for development** (text, optional): Describe your personal growth goals and areas where you would like to improve.

### 7. Career interests
* **Career interests** (text, optional): Describe the field or industry that interests you.

### 8. Assessment date
* **Assessment date** (date, required): The date you complete this form.

### 9. Interested in follow up assessment
* **Interested in follow up assessment** (select one: True, False): Select yes if you are interested in a follow-up assessment, and no otherwise.
