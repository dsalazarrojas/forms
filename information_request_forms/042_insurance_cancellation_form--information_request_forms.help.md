# Insurance Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Cancellation Form is designed to collect information from users for insurance cancellation purposes. It gathers details such as the reason for cancellation, the insurance company involved, and the effective date of cancellation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the `reason_for_cancellation` field with a clear explanation of why you want to cancel your insurance policy.
2.  Select the insurance company that you are cancelling from the list in the `insurance_company` field.
3.  Enter the effective date of cancellation in the `effective_date` field.
4.  Sign the `signature` field to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
5.  Sign the `customer_signature` field to confirm that the cancellation request is made by the customer.
6.  Sign the `employee_signature` field to confirm that the cancellation request is processed by the insurance company representative.
7.  Enter the date of the insurance cancellation form in the `insurance_cancellation_form_date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **insurance_cancellation_form** (Text): This is the main field where you can type in the reason for your insurance policy cancellation.
*   **reason_for_cancellation** (Text): This field is where you can provide a clear explanation of why you want to cancel your insurance policy.
*   **insurance_company** (Select Multiple): This field allows you to select from a list of insurance companies involved in the cancellation process.
*   **effective_date** (Date): This field is where you enter the date when your insurance policy will be cancelled.
*   **signature** (Note): This is where you sign to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
*   **customer_signature** (Note): This is where the customer signs to confirm that the cancellation request is made by them.
*   **employee_signature** (Note): This is where the insurance company representative signs to confirm that the cancellation request is processed.
*   **insurance_cancellation_form_date** (Date): This field is where you enter the date of the insurance cancellation form.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have the necessary authorization or permission to fill out this form.
*   Fill out all required fields to ensure that your cancellation request is processed efficiently.
*   Review the information provided before submitting the form to ensure that it is accurate and complete.
