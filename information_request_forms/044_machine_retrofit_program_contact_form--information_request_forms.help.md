# Machine Retrofit Program Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information from users who are interested in the Machine Retrofit Program. By filling out this form, you'll be able to get in touch with our team and receive guidance and support on your machine retrofitting needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your relevant information and questions.
2. Ensure you've filled out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
3. Review your information before submitting the form to ensure it's accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Email
* *Email*: <code> (`email`, **optional**): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted by our team. This is where we'll send any follow-up questions or additional resources related to your machine retrofitting needs.

### Phone
* *Phone*: <code> (`phone`, **optional**): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed. This is useful for urgent matters or if you'd like to have a call-back.

### Company
* *Company*: <code> (`company`, **optional**): If you're representing a company, please provide the name of your company. This helps us understand your company's context and needs.

### Machine Type
* *Machine Type*: <code> (`machine_type`, **optional**, select one from the list): Select the type of machine you're interested in retrofitting. This helps us provide you with relevant information and resources.

### Machine Name
* *Machine Name*: <code> (`machine_name`, **optional**): Enter the name of the machine you're working with. This will help us better understand your retrofitting needs.

### Location
* *Location*: <code> (`location`, **optional**): If you're located in a specific area or region, please let us know. This helps us understand your local context and provide more relevant support.

### Name
* *Name*: <code> (`name`, **optional**): Enter your name or the name of the person responsible for the machine. This helps us address you personally and provide more tailored support.

### Email (again)
* *Email*: <code> (`email`, **optional**): Please re-enter your email address here. This ensures we have the correct contact information for follow-up communication.

### Message
* *Message*: <code> (`message`, **optional**): Share any specific questions, concerns, or information about your machine retrofitting needs. This is a great place to provide more context and details.

### Tips
Before submitting the form, please make sure to check the fields with an asterisk (\*) as they are required. Also, if you have any attachments or additional information, please feel free to include them in your message field.
