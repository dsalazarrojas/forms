# # Customer Gift Assistance Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Gift Assistance Contact Form is a tool for customers to contact our organization for gift-related assistance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Name** field with your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your **Customer Email** in the format of [example@email.com](mailto:example@email.com).
3. If you have a **Customer Phone**, please enter it in the format of XXX-XXX-XXXX.
4. Type your message in the **Customer Message** field, providing as much detail as possible about your gift-related concern.
5. If you have any additional information or context that may help us assist you, please add it in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_gift_assistance_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_gift_assistance_email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format [example@email.com](mailto:example@email.com).
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_gift_assistance_phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number associated with your account or gift-related issue, please enter it.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_gift_assistance_message`, text, required): Describe your gift-related concern in this field.
* **Notes** (`customer_gift_assistance_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional context or information that may help our team assist you.
