# Equipment Liability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Liability Form is used to collect information about equipment and its status within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your User Name and User ID.
2. Enter your Company Name.
3. Provide your contact information (Phone and Email).
4. Choose the correct status for the equipment from the following fields:
	* Is this equipment active or inactive? 
	* Who owns the equipment?
	* Is the equipment in use?
	* Has the equipment been maintained recently?
	* Is the equipment certified to a certain level?
	* Is the equipment under warranty?
	* When was the last maintenance performed?
	* When is the next maintenance due?
	* How many maintenance hours were performed in the last maintenance?
5. Enter the equipment description, location, and any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **User Name** (User Name, text, required)
Enter your name as the person filling out this form.

### 2. **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required)
Enter your company's name.

### 3. **Phone** (Phone, text, required)
Enter your contact phone number.

### 4. **Email** (Email, email, required)
Enter your contact email.

### 5. **Contract Date** (Contract Date, date, not required)
Enter the date of the contract.

### 6. **Equipment Type** (Equipment Type, select_one, not required)
Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the equipment is active or inactive.

### 7. **Equipment Description** (Equipment Description, text, not required)
Enter a description of the equipment.

### 8. **Location** (Location, text, not required)
Enter the location of the equipment.

### 9. **Notes** (Notes, note, not required)
Enter any additional notes about the equipment.

### 10. **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, select_one, not required)
Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the equipment is assigned to a tool.

### 11. **Equipment Liability Form** (Equipment Liability Form, number, required)
Enter a unique identifier for the form.

### 12. **Form Version** (Form Version, number, required)
Enter the version of the form.

### 13. **Category** (Category, select_one, not required)
Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the equipment belongs to a certain category.

### 14. **Equipment Status** (Equipment Status, select_multiple, required)
Select 'Active', 'Inactive', 'Yes', 'No' or a combination of them to indicate the equipment status.

### 15. **User ID** (User ID, number, required)
Enter your User ID.

### 16. **User Role** (User Role, select_multiple, required)
Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your role within the organization.

### 17. **Equipment Ownership** (Equipment Ownership, select_one, required)
Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you own the equipment.

### 18. **Equipment Usage** (Equipment Usage, select_one, required)
Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the equipment is in use.

### 19. **Equipment Maintenance** (Equipment Maintenance, select_one, required)
Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the equipment has been maintained recently.

### 20. **Inspection Schedule** (Inspection Schedule, select_multiple, required)
Select 'Yes', 'No' or a combination of them to indicate the inspection schedule.

### 21. **Certification Level** (Certification Level, select_one, required)
Select 'High' or 'Low' to indicate the level of certification.

### 22. **Warranty Status** (Warranty Status, select_one, required)
Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the warranty status.

### 23. **Last Maintenance Date** (Last Maintenance Date, date, required)
Enter the date of the last maintenance performed.

### 24. **Due Date of Next Maintenance** (Due Date of Next Maintenance, date, required)
Enter the date of the next maintenance due.

### 25. **Last Maintenance Hours** (Last Maintenance Hours, number, required)
Enter the number of hours of the last maintenance performed.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with your supervisor or the form's administrator.
* The form is only accessible to authorized personnel within the organization.
