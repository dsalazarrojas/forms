# public_sector_modernization_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from public sector employees and stakeholders about their modernization needs and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out each page sequentially.
2. Ensure that required fields are filled out before proceeding to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **page_1_label** (`page_1`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form.
* **page_2_label** (`page_2`, text, optional): This page is meant to gather more information about your modernization needs and experiences.
* **page_3_label** (`page_3`, text, optional): This page is another opportunity to share your thoughts and opinions.
* **page_4_label** (`page_4`, text, optional): This page is similar to the previous one, allowing you to provide more information.
* **page_5_label** (`page_5`, text, optional): Another page to gather more qualitative data.
* **page_6_label** (`page_6`, text, optional): A page to gather further insights.
* **page_7_label** (`page_7`, text, optional): The last page, where you can provide any final thoughts or comments.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all the questions truthfully and concisely.
* If you are unsure about any field, it's okay to skip it or ask for clarification.
* This form is meant to be a helpful guide for policy decisions and resource allocation within the public sector.
* Your input is valuable, and your participation will contribute to the modernization of the public sector.
