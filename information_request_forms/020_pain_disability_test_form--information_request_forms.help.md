# Pain Disability Test Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pain Disability Test Form is a tool used by healthcare professionals to collect information about a patient's pain experiences. This form helps to assess the severity and impact of pain on daily activities and identify potential areas for treatment and intervention.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
* For select options, choose the one that best describes your current pain experiences.
* If you experience no pain, select "Not at all" for all relevant questions.
* For date fields, use the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* For text fields, enter your response in a clear and concise manner.
* If you have additional comments or information about your pain, please use the "Additional comments about your pain" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Information Request Forms**: This is an introduction page that provides context and instructions for completing the form.
* **Do you experience any pain?**: This question asks about your current level of pain. Please select the option that best describes your pain experience.
* **Do you experience pain when you perform daily activities?**: This question asks about the impact of pain on your daily activities. Please select all options that apply.
* **Do you take any medications for pain?**: This question asks about your current treatment for pain. Please select all options that apply.
* **What is the start date of your pain?**: This question asks for the start date of your pain. Please use the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Additional comments about your pain**: This field is for any additional information or comments you would like to provide about your pain.
* **Phone**: This field is for your phone number.
* **Email**: This field is for your email address.
* **Do you experience pain when you perform activities of daily living?**: This question asks about the impact of pain on your daily living activities. Please select all options that apply.
