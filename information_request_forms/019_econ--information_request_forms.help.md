# ECON - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ECON form is a request form for providing personal information for an ECON application. It is used internally to verify and process user information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your Email (if you have one) in the "Email" field.
5. If applicable, enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Enter the Time of your availability in the "Time" field.
7. Provide your Address in the "Address" field.
8. Enter your City in the "City" field.
9. Enter your State in the "State" field.
10. Enter your Zip code in the "Zip" field.
11. If necessary, add notes about your application in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (1)** (text, required): This is the first part of your name.
* **Last Name (2)** (text, required): This is the second part of your name.
* **Phone (3)** (text, required): This is your contact phone number.
* **Email (4)** (email, optional): If you have an email address, please enter it here.
* **Date of Birth (5)** (date, optional): If you have a valid date of birth, please enter it.
* **Time (6)** (time, optional): This is the time of your availability.
* **Address (7)** (text, required): This is your residential or mailing address.
* **City (8)** (text, optional): Enter your city for our records.
* **State (9)** (text, optional): Enter your state for our records.
* **Zip (10)** (text, optional): Enter your zip code for our records.
* **Notes (11)** (note, optional): If you have any additional information to provide, use this field.
