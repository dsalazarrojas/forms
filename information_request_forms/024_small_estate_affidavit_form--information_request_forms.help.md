# Small Estate Affidavit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a deceased person's estate. It helps to document the executor's appointment, estate value, assets, beneficiaries, and other relevant details. This form is typically filled out by the executor of the estate or other authorized personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form on a computer or mobile device.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Enter the required information, such as the deceased person's name, address, and date of death.
4. Select the correct options for the executor's appointment and relationship to the deceased.
5. Enter any other relevant details about the estate and its administration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **small_estate_affidavit_form**: This field is the title of the form and is required to be filled out by the user.
* **primary_executor**: Select whether the primary executor of the estate is a person ('Yes' or 'No') or another option is not provided.
* **date_of_death**: Enter the date of the deceased person's death.
* **legal_name**: Enter the legal name of the deceased person.
* **address**: Enter the address of the deceased person's estate.
* **phone**: Enter the phone number of the deceased person's estate.
* **email**: Enter the email address of the deceased person's estate.
* **estate_value**: Enter the value of the deceased person's estate.
* **assets**: Enter a list of the deceased person's assets.
* **beneficiaries**: Select whether there are beneficiaries ('Yes' or 'No') or another option is not provided.
* **relationship**: Enter the relationship of the executor to the deceased person.
* **affidavit_number**: Enter a unique number for the estate affidavit.
* **date_filed**: Enter the date the form was filed.
* **name_of_deceased**: Enter the name of the deceased person.
* **place_of_death**: Enter the location of the deceased person's death.
* **time_of_death**: Enter the time of the deceased person's death.
* **executor**: Select whether the executor of the estate is a person ('Yes' or 'No') or another option is not provided.
* **executor_address**: Enter the address of the executor of the estate.
* **relationship_to_deceased**: Select whether the executor has a relationship to the deceased person ('Yes' or 'No') or another option is not provided.
* **estate_administrator**: Enter the name of the estate administrator.
* **administrator_address**: Enter the address of the estate administrator.
* **administrator_phone**: Enter the phone number of the estate administrator.
* **administrator_email**: Enter the email address of the estate administrator.
* **estate_tax**: Enter any estate tax information.
* **other**: Enter any additional information about the estate.
