# Student Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Declaration Form is a document designed to collect personal and academic information from students. This form is meant to ensure that students can accurately declare their details, which can be used for administrative or academic purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are a legitimate student before completing this form.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Double-check your details before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student Number** (`student_number`, number, required/optional): Enter your unique student ID number, if provided by your institution.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your institutional email address or a valid email that can be verified by the institution.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number for any follow-up or verification purposes.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, select_one, required): Select your student ID from the provided options. If not listed, contact your institution's administration for assistance.
* **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`, date, required/optional): If applicable, select the date of your declaration. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Declaration Time** (`declaration_time`, time, required/optional): If applicable, select the time of your declaration. Otherwise, leave this field blank.

## Tips
* Ensure you have permission from your institution to declare your student status before proceeding.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your institution's administration for guidance.
* Double-check your submission before submitting the form to avoid errors.
