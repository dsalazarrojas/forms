# handmarks_personalization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information about a customer's personalization preferences and party details. The purpose of this form is to collect data that will be used to tailor a party experience to the customer's specific needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the text field provided.
2. Provide your **Customer EMail** address if you have one.
3. Enter your **Customer Phone** number if you have one.
4. Enter the **Consultants Name** responsible for the party.
5. Provide any additional **Party details** you would like to share with us.
6. Select the date of the **Party Date**.
7. Select the time of the **Party Time**.
8. For **Party Tags**, select all the tags that apply to your party (e.g. "Yes" for "Tag 01" if it applies).
9. Finally, for **Tag 01**, **Tag 02**, **Tag 03**, **Tag 04**, **Tag 05**, **Tag 06**, **Tag 07**, **Tag 08**, and **Tag 09**, select all the options that apply to your party.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (text): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Customer EMail** (email): Enter your email address if you have one. This is not required.
* **Customer Phone** (text): Enter your phone number if you have one. This is not required.
* **Consultants Name** (text): Enter the name of the consultant responsible for your party.
* **Party details** (text): Provide any additional details about your party that you would like to share with us.
* **Party Date** (date): Select the date of your party.
* **Party Time** (time): Select the time of your party.
* **Party Tags** (select multiple): Select all the tags that apply to your party from the list provided.
* **Tag 01**, **Tag 02**, ..., **Tag 10** (select one): Select all the specific tags that apply to your party from the list provided for each tag.
