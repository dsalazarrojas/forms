# Asset And Liability Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Asset And Liability Information Form is designed to gather and track financial data related to a business's assets and liabilities. This information will be used for internal accounting and tax purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are submitting one form per year to avoid duplication of work.
2. Complete the form accurately and honestly with the requested information.
3. Enter the requested financial data for the current fiscal year.
4. Review the form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the official business name as it appears on official documents.
- **Business ID or EIN** (`business_id`, text, required): Enter the business's tax identification number or Employer Identification Number (EIN).
- **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the report.
- **Fiscal Year** (`fiscal_year`, text, required): Enter the current fiscal year.
- **Prepared By** (`prepared_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person preparing the form.
- **Preparer Title** (`preparer_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the person preparing the form.
- **Total Current Assets** (`total_current_assets`, number, required): Enter the sum of the business's current assets.
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents** (`cash_and_equivalents`, number, required): Enter the liquid assets.
- **Accounts Receivable** (`accounts_receivable`, number, required): Enter the money owed to the business.
- **Inventory Value** (`inventory_value`, number, required): Enter the current inventory worth.
- **Total Fixed Assets** (`total_fixed_assets`, number, required): Enter the business's property and plant equipment.
- **Property Value** (`property_value`, number, optional): Enter the real estate holdings (if applicable).
- **Equipment Value** (`equipment_value`, number, optional): Enter the business's machinery and equipment.
- **Vehicles Value** (`vehicles_value`, number, optional): Enter the company vehicles (if applicable).
- **Total Intangible Assets** (`total_intangible_assets`, number, optional): Enter the business's patents, trademarks, and goodwill.
- **Total Assets** (`total_assets`, number, required): Enter the sum of all assets.
- **Total Current Liabilities** (`total_current_liabilities`, number, required): Enter the short-term obligations.
- **Accounts Payable** (`accounts_payable`, number, required): Enter the money owed to suppliers.
- **Short-Term Debt** (`short_term_debt`, number, required): Enter the due within one year.
- **Accrued Expenses** (`accrued_expenses`, number, required): Enter the outstanding expenses.
- **Total Long-Term Liabilities** (`total_long_term_liabilities`, number, required): Enter the long-term obligations.
- **Long-Term Debt** (`long_term_debt`, number, required): Enter the loans and bonds.
- **Mortgage Obligations** (`mortgage_obligations`, number, optional): Enter the property mortgages (if applicable).
- **Total Liabilities** (`total_liabilities`, number, required): Enter the sum of all liabilities.
- **Owners Equity** (`owners_equity`, number, required): Enter the net worth.
- **Year Over Year Change Percentage** (`year_over_year_change`, number, optional): Enter the percentage change from last year (if applicable).
- **Significant Changes Explanation** (`significant_changes`, text, required): Describe any major changes in the financials.
- **Additional Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter other relevant information.
- **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, text, required): Enter the manager or CFO name.
- **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the form was reviewed.
- **Tips**: Be honest and accurate when entering financial information. Double-check numbers for accuracy before submitting the form.
