# Web Browsing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request help with web browsing issues, ensuring that they are efficiently addressed and resolved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above in the "Completing the Form" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **web_browsing_request** (1): Enter a brief description of the issue or issue that you experienced while browsing the web.
* **description** (2): Select the area where you experienced the issue.
* **research_area** (3): Enter the start date and end date of the issue, in the format of "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **start_date** (4): Enter the start date of the issue, in the format of "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **end_date** (5): Choose the priority level of the issue: "Low", "Medium", or "High".
* **task_priority** (6): Enter your name and email address (if available) to associate with the submission.
* **submitter** (7): Enter your phone number (if available) to help us contact you for further information.
* **comments** (8): Select the tool that was assigned to you to fix the issue (if applicable).
* **submitter_email** (9): Enter a unique identifier for the form and task.
* **submitter_phone** (10): Enter the ID of the page where the issue occurred.
* **assigned_tool** (11): Enter the version of the form that was used to submit the form.
* **form_id** (12): Enter the ID of the user who submitted the form.
* **task_id** (13): Enter the ID of the user who last modified the form.
* **page_id** (14): Enter the date and time when the form was created.
* **form_version** (15): Enter the date and time when the form was last modified.
* **submitted_by** (16): Enter the IP address of the user who submitted the form.
* **modified_by** (17): Enter the IP address of the submission (if different from the submitter's IP).
* **created_at** (18): Enter the user agent of the submitter's browser.
* **modified_at** (19): Enter the operating system of the submitter's device.
* **submitter_ip** (20): Enter the browser type of the submitter's device.
* **submission_ip** (21): Enter the operating system of the submitter's device.
* **submitter_useragent** (22): Enter the platform of the submitter's device.
* **submitter_os** (23): Enter the platform of the submitter's device.
* **submitter_browser** (24): Enter the platform of the submitter's device.
* **submitter_platform** (25): Enter the platform of the submitter's device.

Note: The fields that are not marked as "required" are optional and can be left blank.
