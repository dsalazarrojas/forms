# Sign On Letter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sign On Letter Form is a template designed for organizations or individuals to create a formal letter requesting a specific group to sign on or agree to a particular commitment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the organization in the `organization` field.
2. Enter the name of the group in the `group` field.
3. Enter a brief statement regarding participation in the `participation` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sign On Letter**: This field is where you can write the main text of the formal letter.
  - A brief text to explain the purpose and intent of the request.
  - Keep it concise and clear to convey the key points.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization or entity that is requesting the signature.
  - This could be a company, school, or any other relevant group.
* **Group**: Enter the name of the group being asked to sign on or agree to the commitment.
  - This could be a team, department, or any other relevant group.
* **Participation**: Enter a brief statement explaining the purpose and intent of the request.
  - Be clear and concise to avoid miscommunication.
