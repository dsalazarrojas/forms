# Client Database Update Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for clients to request updates to their database records. It covers various types of updates, including contact information, address changes, insurance details, emergency contact, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select the type of update you are requesting**: Choose the reason for your update from the options provided in the "Type of Update Request" field.
2. **Provide current and new information**: Enter your current and new details for the selected update type. For example, if you are requesting a change of address, enter both your current and new address.
3. **Submit your request**: Click the "Submit" button to send your update request to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your legal first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number for the client.
* **Client ID Number**: Optional, enter your client ID number if applicable.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for verification purposes.
* **Type of Update Request**: Choose the type of update you are requesting (e.g., Contact Information, Address Change, etc.).
* **Previous Address**: Enter your current address, if applicable.
* **New Address**: Enter your new address, if applicable.
* **New City**: Enter the city of your new address, if applicable.
* **New ZIP Code**: Enter the ZIP code of your new address, if applicable.
* **New Email Address**: Enter your new email address, if applicable.
* **New Phone Number**: Enter your new phone number, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter your insurance policy number, if applicable.
* **Insurance Group Number**: Enter your insurance group number, if applicable.
* **Communication Preferences**: Select the methods by which you would like to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone Call, etc.).
* **Language Preference**: Select your preferred language for communication.
* **Reason for Update**: Briefly explain why you are making this change.
* **When Should This Take Effect?**: Enter the desired effective date for your update.
* **I Confirm All Information Is Accurate**: Confirm that all information provided is accurate and true.
* **Request Date**: This field is automatically populated with the current date.

Note: The form will guide you through the necessary steps to request an update to your client database record. Please ensure you provide accurate and complete information to facilitate efficient processing of your request.
