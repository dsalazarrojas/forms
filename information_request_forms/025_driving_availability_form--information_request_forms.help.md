## Driving Availability Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your driving availability for a specific trip. This form will help us understand your schedule and preferences to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Enter your trip details in the "trip_details" field. This is where you can provide any relevant information about your trip, such as the purpose or any specific requirements you have.
2.  Enter the start date of your trip in the "start_date" field. This should be the date you plan to begin your trip.
3.  Enter the end date of your trip in the "end_date" field. This should be the date you plan to end your trip.
4.  Enter the start time of your trip in the "start_time" field. This should be the time you plan to begin your trip on the specified start date.
5.  Enter the end time of your trip in the "end_time" field. This should be the time you plan to end your trip on the specified end date.
6.  Enter the location of your trip in the "trip_location" field. This should be the destination or location you plan to visit.
7.  Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field. This should include your country code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **trip_details** (`trip_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your trip, including any specific requirements or requests you have.
*   **start_date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to begin your trip.
*   **end_date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to end your trip.
*   **start_time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you plan to begin your trip on the specified start date.
*   **end_time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you plan to end your trip on the specified end date.
*   **trip_location** (`trip_location`, text, optional): Enter the destination or location you plan to visit.
*   **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, optional, required to include country code): Enter your phone number, including your country code for international trips.
