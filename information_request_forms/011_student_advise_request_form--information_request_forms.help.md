# Student Advise Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow students to request advice on various topics or programs that interest them. It is intended to help students get guidance from experts or advisors on areas they are curious about or need more information on.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the areas you are interested in or need more advise on from the "Request Advice" dropdown menu. You can choose one or multiple options.
2.  Choose one or multiple topics or programs that interest you from the "Topics or Programs" dropdown menu. For example, you can select "Computer Science", "Biology", or "Engineering".
3.  In the "Message" field, provide a brief description of why you are interested in these topics or programs. Be concise and clear in your request.
4.  Provide your name and contact information in the respective fields. This will help us get back to you for any follow-up questions or further guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Request Advice
* **Request Advice** (`request_advice`, `select_multiple`, **required**: false): 
*   Select the areas you are interested in or need more advise on. This option is a dropdown menu that allows you to choose from various topics or programs. You can select one or multiple options from this menu.

### Topics or Programs
*   **Topics or Programs** (`topics`, `select_multiple`, **required**: false): 
*   Choose one or multiple topics or programs that interest you. This option is also a dropdown menu that allows you to select from various topics or programs. For example, you can select "Computer Science", "Biology", or "Engineering".

### Message
*   **Message** (`message`, `text`, **required**: false): 
*   In this field, provide a brief description of why you are interested in these topics or programs. Be concise and clear in your request.

### Name
*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, **required**: false): 
*   Enter your name so that we can contact you for follow-up questions or further guidance.

### Email
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**: false): 
*   Enter your email address so that we can get back to you for any further guidance or follow-up questions.

### Contact Information
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, **required**: false): 
*   Provide your contact information so that we can get back to you for any further guidance or follow-up questions. 

Tips:
*   Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date contact information so that we can reach you in a timely manner.
*   Be clear and concise in your message, as this will help us better understand your request and provide you with more relevant advice.
