# Customer Arrival Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from customers upon their arrival. This information will help our staff provide the best possible service and ensure a smooth experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the reason for your visit to let us know how we can assist you.
2. If you have a reservation number, please enter it to help us locate your appointment.
3. Rate your experience at our facility.
4. Add any additional instructions or comments that you think would be helpful for our staff to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reason for visit**: Please tell us the reason for your visit, so we can better assist you.
* **Reservation number**: If you have a reservation number, please enter it here to help us locate your appointment. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Rate experience**: Please rate your experience at our facility from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Additional instructions**: If you have any additional information or comments that you think would be helpful for our staff, please let us know here.

## Tips
* Please fill in all the necessary information to help us provide the best possible service.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to ask our staff.
* We appreciate your feedback and will use it to improve our services.
