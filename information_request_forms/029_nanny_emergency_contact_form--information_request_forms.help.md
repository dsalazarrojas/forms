# Nanny Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nanny Emergency Contact Form is a crucial document that helps ensure the well-being and safety of the child in your care. Please complete this form carefully and accurately to provide the necessary contact information for emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to understand the importance of providing accurate and up-to-date emergency contact information.
2. Fill in the required fields with the correct contact information for the emergency contact person.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the emergency contact person, such as "My mother", "My sister", etc.
* **Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, text, optional): Describe the relationship between the emergency contact person and the child.
* **Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact person (e.g., "123 Main St, Apt 101").
* **Notes** (`emergency_contact_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the emergency contact person, such as medical conditions or special needs.

## Tips
- Double-check the information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Make sure to update this form regularly to reflect any changes in emergency contact information.
- Inform the nanny about any changes or updates to this form.
- Keep a copy of this form in a safe and accessible place for easy retrieval in emergency situations.
