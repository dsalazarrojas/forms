# KWL Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The KWL Form is designed to help you assess your current knowledge, skills, and learning goals. It helps you identify areas where you need improvement and outline specific objectives for your development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions on each page with your current knowledge, skills, and learning goals in mind.
2. Provide honest and accurate answers to the best of your ability.
3. If a field is marked as optional, you can skip it if you don't have any information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Title** (`kwl_form_title`, text, optional): A brief description or title that summarizes your current knowledge or skills in the relevant area.
* **What You Know** (`kwl_form_know`, text, optional): A detailed description of your current knowledge or skills in the relevant area.
* **What You Want** (`kwl_form_want`, text, optional): A description of what you aim to achieve or learn in the relevant area.
* **What You Learned** (`kwl_form_learn`, text, optional): A description of the new knowledge or skills you acquired during the relevant process or project.

## Tips

* Be honest with yourself when answering the questions.
* Try to provide as much detail as possible for each field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can ask for clarification or guidance.
* Use the form as a tool to help you reflect on your current knowledge and skills, and identify areas for improvement.
* Review and update your answers periodically to track your progress.
