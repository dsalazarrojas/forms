# Digital Communication Manager Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for users to submit requests to the Digital Communication Manager for tasks, questions, or concerns related to digital communication. The manager will review and respond to each submission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to send your request to the manager.
2. Fill out the form fields as required or necessary to provide clear information about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name for reference purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your work email address to ensure we can contact you efficiently. This will also help the manager identify you in our internal systems.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required: false): Write a brief description of your request, question, or concern in this field. Be as concise as possible.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the department or team that you are contacting the manager about (e.g. Accounting, Customer Support, Marketing, Sales).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your work phone number to facilitate communication.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date when you would like to schedule a meeting or task.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Choose the time of day for the meeting or task.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional information or context that would be helpful for the manager to understand your request.
* **Required Message** (`required_message`, `text`, required: true): This field is mandatory. Please enter a brief description of your request, including your name and a clear message for the manager to review and assign tasks accordingly.

## Tips
- Be specific and clear in your message to help the manager understand your request quickly.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
- If you have any attachments or supporting files, you can include them in your message.
- If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, feel free to contact the manager directly.
- The manager will respond to your request as soon as possible.
