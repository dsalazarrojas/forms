# Property_Buyer_Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property_Buyer_Questionnaire is a form designed to collect property information, helping property owners, managers, and agents to understand and manage properties more effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the **General Info** page, where you will provide a brief description of the property.
2. Proceed to the **Property Details** page, where you will specify the property's price, type, and other necessary information.
3. On the **Contact Info** page, enter your contact details to help us reach you for any further queries.
4. Continue on to the **Email** and **Phone** pages to provide your contact information.
5. On the **Property Owner** page, indicate if the property is owned or managed by you or someone else.
6. The **Property Manager** page will let you specify if you have a property manager or not.
7. On the **Property Type** page, select the type of property you are listing.
8. Move on to the **Property Price** page to enter the price of the property.
9. Next, provide the property's **Address**, **City**, **State**, **Country**, and **Postal** information.
10. On the **Property Description** page, enter a brief description of the property.
11. The **Property Listing Date**, **Property Expiry**, and **Property Revision Date** pages are for internal reference purposes only and do not require input.
12. On the **Property Cancellation Date** page, enter the date the property was cancelled (if applicable).
13. Finally, select the status of the property on the **Property Status** page.
14. Indicate if the property has been submitted for approval on the **Property Submitted** page.
15. Select the revision status of the property on the **Property Revision Status** page.
16. Enter the date of property approval on the **Property Approval Date** page (if applicable).
17. Provide the date of property revision on the **Property Revision Date** page (if applicable).
18. Finally, select the cancellation status of the property on the **Property Cancellation Date** page (if applicable).

* **Property Country** (13): Enter the country where the property is located.

* **Property Listing Date** (16): This field is for internal reference and does not require input.
* **Property Expiry** (17): This field is for internal reference and does not require input.
* **Property Revision Date** (18): This field is for internal reference and does not require input.
* **Property Cancellation Date** (19): Enter the date the property was cancelled (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any doubts or questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
* This form is not a replacement for any existing property listing documents.
* If you have a pre-existing property listing, please do not fill out this form.
