# Hospitality Data Analytics Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hospitality Data Analytics Contact Form is designed to collect feedback, questions, or concerns from users about their experiences with our hospitality services. Please use this form to provide detailed descriptions of your concerns or requests, and our team will be happy to assist you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have filled out all required fields. While none of the fields are required, it is helpful to provide as much information as possible to enable our team to better understand your needs.
2. Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields. This will help us contact you directly to address your concerns.
3. Describe your subject and message in the message field, providing as much detail as possible to help us understand your issue.
4. If you wish to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number in the Phone Number field.
5. If relevant, select a date for your concern or query.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, optional): This field is used to record your name for contact and reference purposes.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so we can respond directly to your concern or query.
* **Subject** (text, optional): Briefly describe the purpose or nature of your message.
* **Message** (text, optional): Provide detailed information about your concern or query.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): If you wish to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number. Please be aware that we may not use this contact method if your concern can be better addressed via email.
* **Date** (date, optional): If relevant, select a date that corresponds to your concern or query. This will help us better understand the context of your message.

## Tips
When providing feedback, please be as descriptive and clear as possible to enable our team to quickly address your concerns. If you have further questions, feel free to ask, and we'll be happy to help.
