# Phone Plan Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Phone Plan Inquiry Form is a tool used to gather information from customers or clients to better understand their phone plan needs. This form helps us understand their current and desired plans, allowing us to provide more accurate and relevant information to support their inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Complete all required fields**: The fields marked as required (**Contact Info**, **Phone Number**, **Carrier**, **Plan Type**, **Service Level**, **Price**, and **Confirm**) are essential for us to process your inquiry.
2. Enter your contact information in the **Contact Info** field.
3. Select the **Phone Plan** that best suits your needs.
4. Enter your **Address** and **Email** for our reference.
5. Enter any additional **Notes** about your current or desired plans.
6. Enter your **Phone Number** and **Carrier** for your current plan.
7. Choose your desired **Plan Type** and **Service Level**.
8. Select the length of your desired **Contract Length**.
9. Choose the start and end dates of your contract in the **Contract Start Date** and **Contract-End-Date** fields.
10. Enter your expected **Price** for the chosen plan.
11. Add any additional **Notes 2** if you need to mention something important.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Contact Info (Required)**: This field is where you should enter your contact information, such as your name, address, and phone number. This will help us contact you to confirm your details.
* **2. Phone Plan (Not Required)**: Select the phone plan that best suits your needs. This will help us understand your current or desired plan.
* **3. Address (Optional)**: Enter your address for our reference, this will help us understand your location.
* **4. Email (Optional)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **5. Notes (Required)**: Enter any additional information about your current or desired plans, such as your concerns or expectations.
* **6. Phone Number (Required)**: Enter your current or desired phone number.
* **7. Carrier (Optional)**: Select your current or desired carrier from the provided options.
* **8. Plan Type (Optional)**: Choose your desired plan type. This will help us understand your needs.
* **9. Service Level (Optional)**: Select your desired service level.
* **10. Contract Length (Optional)**: Choose the length of your desired contract.
* **11. Contract Start Date (Optional)**: Enter the start date of your contract.
* **12. Contract-End-Date (Required)**: Enter the end date of your contract.
* **13. Price (Required)**: Enter your expected price for the chosen plan.
* **14. Notes 2 (Optional)**: Enter any additional information about your contract or plan.
* **15. Confirm (Optional)**: Confirm your details before submitting the form.

This form will help us better understand your needs and provide you with more accurate and relevant information to support your phone plan inquiry.
