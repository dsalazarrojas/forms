# Maintenance Philosophy Document Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Philosophy Document Request Form is used to gather information from users to create a comprehensive Maintenance Philosophy Document (MPD) for a specific facility. This document outlines the maintenance approach and procedures for the facility's equipment, systems, and infrastructure.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form to request a Maintenance Philosophy Document (MPD) for a specific facility.
- Select the purpose of your request, and indicate the urgency of the request.
- Choose the preferred format of the document (e.g., PDF, DOCX, PPT, or other).
- Select the facility for which you are requesting the MPD.
- Provide your contact information so the team can reach out to you for further clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Purpose** (`What is the purpose of this request`, text, optional):
  Enter a brief description of the reason for your Maintenance Philosophy Document request.
  This will help the document creators understand the context and scope of the document.
- **Urgency** (`What is the urgency of this request`, select_one, optional):
  Select the urgency of your request from the following options:
  * High: The request is of high priority and needs to be expedited.
  * Medium: The request is of medium priority and can be completed within a few days.
  * Low: The request is of low priority and can be completed within a week or more.
- **Format** (`What is your preferred format`, select_multiple, optional):
  Choose the format of the Maintenance Philosophy Document you prefer:
  * pdf: Portable Document Format (PDF)
  * docx: Microsoft Word format
  * ppt: Presentation format
  * other: Specify your preferred format
- **Facility** (`Which facility is this request for`, select_one, optional):
  Select the facility for which you are requesting the Maintenance Philosophy Document:
  * Facility 1
  * Facility 2
  * Facility 3
- **Contact Person** (`Who is your contact person`, text, optional):
  Enter your name and title or position to help the document creators contact you.
- **Contact Email** (`Contact email`, email, optional):
  Enter your email address so the document creators can reach out to you for further clarification.
- **Contact Phone** (`Contact phone`, text, optional):
  Enter your phone number to enable easier communication and confirmation.
- **Comments** (`Additional comments`, text, optional):
  Enter any additional comments or information that will help in creating the document.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have filled out the form correctly to avoid delays in processing your request.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the facility management team for clarification.
- The team will review and respond to your request within the specified urgency.
