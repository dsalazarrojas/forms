# Timeshare Exit Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to inquire about the timeshare exit process. Customers can fill out this form to ask questions or provide information about their timeshare contract and exit plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Choose one of the options for each field that best applies to your situation.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
3. If a field is optional, you can choose to leave it blank if you do not have the necessary information.
4. Once you have completed all the fields, click the submit button to send your inquiry to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Timeshare Exit Inquiry Form** (`timeshare_exit_form`, `select_multiple`, optional): This is a multiple-select field where you can choose one or more options. Please select all that apply to your situation.
  * Yes
  * No
* **Lead Qualification** (`lead_qualification`, `select_one`, optional): This field helps our team understand your lead status. Please select one of the following options:
  * Yes
  * No
* **Timeshare Contract Information** (`timeshare_contract_info`, `text`, optional): Please provide detailed information about your timeshare contract, including the contract number, provider, and product.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to determine how you would like to be contacted. Please select one or more of the following options:
  * Yes
  * No
* **Timeshare Contract Status** (`timeshare_contract_status`, `select_one`, optional): This field helps our team understand the status of your timeshare contract. Please select one of the following:
  * Active
  * Inactive
* **Exit Date** (`exit_date`, `date`, optional): If you have an exit date scheduled, please provide it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Exit Method** (`exit_method`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to determine how you would like to exit your timeshare contract. Please select one of the following:
  * Yes
  * No
* **Reason for Exit** (`reason_for_exit`, `text`, optional): Please provide a brief reason for your exit.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to determine how you would like to be contacted. Please select one of the following:
  * Yes
  * No
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Please provide your contact number, if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Please provide your email address, if applicable.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Please provide any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Lead Status** (`lead_status`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to determine the status of your lead. Please select one of the following:
  * Active
  * Inactive
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `text`, optional): If you have a preferred contact, please provide their name.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, `date`, optional): This field is used to track the creation date of your inquiry.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `date`, optional): This field is used to track the last update date of your inquiry.
* **Timeshare Provider** (`timeshare_provider`, `text`, optional): If you have a timeshare provider, please provide their name.
* **Timeshare Product** (`timeshare_product`, `text`, optional): If you have a timeshare product, please provide its name.
* **Timeshare Location** (`timeshare_location`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific location for your timeshare, please provide it.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`, optional): This field is used to track who created your inquiry.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `text`, optional): This field is used to track who last updated your inquiry.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Please provide any comments or questions you may have.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Please provide any additional notes or comments.
* **Jotform URL** (`jotform_url`, `text`, optional): If you have a Jotform URL, please provide it.
