# Outreach Brochure Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to request an outreach brochure for your organization. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary information to send you the brochure you need.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **User Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Enter your contact number in the **Contact Number** field.
4. Select the type of brochure you need in the **Brochure Type** field.
5. Enter the quantity of brochures you require.
6. Enter your delivery address in the **Delivery Address** field.
7. Select the date you would like to receive your brochures in the **Delivery Date** field.
8. Select the time you would like to receive your brochures in the **Delivery Time** field.
9. Enter any additional delivery details in the **Delivery Details** field.
10. Enter your organization's name in the **Organization** field.
11. Enter the name of the school you are affiliated with in the **School** field.
12. Enter the name of the group you are associated with in the **Group Name** field.
13. Enter your street address in the **Street Address** field.
14. Enter the city where you are located in the **City** field.
15. Select the state where you are located in the **State** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting this form.
* **Email** (2, email, required): Enter the email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Number** (3, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Brochure Type** (4, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select the type of brochure you need, such as Brochure A, Brochure B, or Brochure C.
* **Quantity** (5, number, required): Enter the number of brochures you require.
* **Delivery Address** (6, text, required): Enter the address where you would like to receive your brochures.
* **Delivery Date** (7, date, required): Select the date you would like to receive your brochures.
* **Delivery Time** (8, time, required): Select the time you would like to receive your brochures.
* **Delivery Details** (9, note, required): Enter any additional details about your delivery preferences.
* **Organization** (10, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **School** (11, text, required): Enter the name of the school you are affiliated with.
* **Group Name** (12, text, required): Enter the name of the group you are associated with.
* **Street Address** (13, text, required): Enter the street address where you are located.
* **City** (14, text, required): Enter the city where you are located.
* **State** (15, select_one, required):
	+ Select the state where you are located from the list of options.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your email address and contact number accurately so we can contact you to confirm your order.
* Select the brochure type that best fits your needs.
* Enter a specific date and time that works best for you.
* If you have any specific delivery instructions, enter them in the **Delivery Details** field.
* If you are affiliated with a school or group, make sure to enter their name correctly.
