# Documentary Project Archive Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request the archive of a documentary project, gathering essential information about the project and its creator's contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you are requesting to archive the project or not.
2. Enter your name and contact information (email and phone number).
3. Provide the name of the institution associated with the project.
4. Describe the collection associated with the project.
5. Specify if the material type is Yes or No.
6. Enter the material ID (if applicable).
7. Enter the date the project was created.
8. Enter the date the archive request was made.
9. Provide a reason for the archive request.
10. Add any additional information about the project.
11. Enter your name and email address (for submitting user).
12. Enter your phone number (for submitting user).
13. Enter your institution's abbreviation.
14. Enter the department of the institution.
15. Enter your title.
16. Enter any other phone extension (if applicable).
17. Add a note for the submitting user.
18. Enter the archive manager's name.
19. Enter the archive manager's phone number.
20. Enter the archive manager's email address.
21. Enter any other extension for the archive manager (if applicable).
22. Add a signature for the submitting user.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form** (`id: 1`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Select whether you are requesting to archive the project or not. This field is used to determine if the project should be archived or not.
* **Contact Name** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your name to be associated with this request.
* **Contact Email** (`id: 3`, `type: email`, `required: false`): Enter your email address to contact you regarding this request.
* **Contact Phone** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your phone number to contact you regarding this request.
* **Institution** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the institution associated with the project.
* **Collection** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Describe the collection associated with the project.
* **Material Type** (`id: 7`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Specify if the material type is Yes or No.
* **Material ID** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the material ID if applicable.
* **Archive Date** (`id: 9`, `type: date`, `required: false`): Enter the date the project was created.
* **Request Date** (`id: 10`, `type: date`, `required: false`): Enter the date the archive request was made.
* **Reason** (`id: 11`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Provide a reason for the archive request.
* **Additional Info** (`id: 12`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Add any additional information about the project.
* **Submitter Name** (`id: 13`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email** (`id: 14`, `type: email`, `required: false`): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your phone number.
* **Institution Abbreviation** (`id: 16`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the institution's abbreviation.
* **Contact Department** (`id: 17`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the department of the institution.
* **Contact Title** (`id: 18`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your title.
* **Contact Phone Ext** (`id: 19`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter any other phone extension (if applicable).
* **Submitter Note** (`id: 20`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Add a note for the submitting user.
* **Archive Manager** (`id: 21`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the archive manager's name.
* **Archive Manager Phone** (`id: 22`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the archive manager's phone number.
* **Archive Manager Email** (`id: 23`, `type: email`, `required: false`): Enter the archive manager's email address.
* **Archive Manager Ext** (`id: 24`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter any other extension for the archive manager (if applicable).
* **Submitter Signature** (`id: 25`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Add a signature for the submitting user.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Provide clear and concise information about the project.
* Ensure you have the necessary permission to submit this request on behalf of the project's creator.
