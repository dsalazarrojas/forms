# Equipment Receipt Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Receipt Form is used to record information about equipment issued to individuals or departments within an organization. This form helps to keep track of equipment issued, returned, or outstanding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date you received the equipment in the "Issue Date" field.
2. Provide a description of the equipment and its condition in the "Equipment Description" field.
3. Enter the serial number of the equipment in the "Equipment Serial Number" field.
4. Enter the model of the equipment in the "Equipment Model" field.
5. Enter the make of the equipment in the "Equipment Make" field.
6. Indicate if the equipment was issued to someone else in the "Equipment Issue To" field. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
7. Check the "Issued By" field to indicate who issued the equipment. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
8. Select any departments that the equipment was issued to in the "Department" field. You can choose from "Yes" or "No" for each department.
9. Enter the location where the equipment is currently being used in the "Location" field.
10. Enter the asset number of the equipment in the "Asset Number" field.
11. Add any additional remarks or comments about the equipment in the "Remarks" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Issue Date** (issuance_date): Enter the date you received the equipment.
* **Equipment Description** (equipment_description): Provide a brief description of the equipment.
* **Equipment Serial Number** (equipment_serial_number): Enter the serial number of the equipment.
* **Equipment Model** (equipment_model): Enter the model of the equipment.
* **Equipment Make** (equipment_make): Enter the make of the equipment.
* **Equipment Issue To** (equipment_issue_to): Check "Yes" if the equipment was issued to someone else, "No" otherwise.
* **Issued By** (issued_by): Check "Yes" if you issued the equipment, "No" otherwise.
* **Department** (department): Select all departments that the equipment was issued to. You can choose from "Yes" or "No" for each department.
* **Location** (location): Enter the location where the equipment is currently being used.
* **Asset Number** (asset_number): Enter the asset number of the equipment.
* **Remarks** (remarks): Add any additional comments or remarks about the equipment.

Note: This form is not required for completion. It is intended to provide additional information for record-keeping and tracking purposes.
