# diversity_and_inclusion_strategy_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect diversity and inclusion strategy contact information from various companies and stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form details (page 1)
2. Provide your first name (page 2)
3. Provide your last name (page 3)
4. Enter your email address (page 4)
5. Enter your phone number (page 5)
6. Enter a message or comment (page 6)
7. Enter your company name (page 7)
8. Enter your industry (page 8)
9. Select whether your company size is large, medium, or small (page 9)
10. Select the departments that your company has (page 10)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Details (page 1)
* **Form Details** (`form_details`, `text`, required: false): This page is a brief description of the form. Please fill out this field with a short description of your company's diversity and inclusion strategy contact information.

### First Name (page 2)
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name. This will be used to address you in our communication.

### Last Name (page 3)
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your last name. This will be used to address you in our communication.

### Email (page 4)
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address. We will use this to contact you about your diversity and inclusion strategy.

### Phone (page 5)
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number. We may use this to contact you about your diversity and inclusion strategy.

### Message (page 6)
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required: false): If you have any comments or messages, please enter them here. This will help us understand your needs better.

### Company Name (page 7)
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of your company. This will help us know who is contacting us.

### Industry (page 8)
* **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the industry that your company is in. This will help us tailor our diversity and inclusion strategy to your needs.

### Company Size (page 9)
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the size of your company (large, medium, or small). This will help us know the scope of your diversity and inclusion strategy.

### Departments (page 10)
* **Departments** (`departments`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the departments that your company has (Yes or No). This will help us know which departments we should focus on for our diversity and inclusion strategy.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all fields completely and accurately.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to reach out to us.
* This information will be used to help us tailor our diversity and inclusion strategy to your needs.
