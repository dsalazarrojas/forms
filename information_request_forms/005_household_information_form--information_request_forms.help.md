# Household Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Household Information Form is designed to collect essential details about a household, including its address, composition, income, and other relevant information. This form helps us better understand your household's needs and provide you with targeted support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct household type from the options provided.
2. Enter the household's address in the text field.
3. Enter the number of household occupants.
4. Select the source(s) of the household's income.
5. Choose the employment status of the household's primary earner.
6. Enter the household's phone number.
7. Enter the household's email address.
8. Add any additional notes about the household (if necessary).
9. Review and submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Household Address** (`household_address`, text, required/optional): This field collects the household's address. Please enter the street address, apartment number, city, and state for accurate identification.
* **Number of Occupants** (`household_occupants`, number, required/optional): Enter the total number of people living in the household.
* **Annual Income** (`household_income`, number, required/optional): Enter the total annual income of the household.
* **Household Type** (`household_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of household you are submitting information for. Choose from the provided options:
	+ Single family
	+ Multi-family
	+ Single parent
	+ Single parent with children
	+ Other
* **Income Source** (`household_income_source`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the source(s) of the household's income. Choose from the provided options:
	+ Business
	+ Employment
	+ Investment
	+ Other
* **Employment Status** (`household_employment_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the employment status of the household's primary earner. Choose from the provided options:
	+ Employed
	+ Unemployed
	+ Retired
	+ Student
	+ Other
* **Phone Number** (`household_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the household's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (`household_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the household's email address for secure communication.
* **Additional Notes** (`household_note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional information about the household that you think is relevant to your request. This field is optional, but it helps us provide better support.
