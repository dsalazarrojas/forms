# Basketball Roster Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Basketball Roster Form is designed to collect information about a basketball team. It gathers details about the team's name, players, and manager's contact information, helping teams to create and manage their rosters effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Official Team Name field (page 2) with the name of your basketball team.
2. Enter the names and positions of your coaches in the Head Coach and Assistant Coach Names field (page 2).
3. Choose the Competition Level of your team (page 2) from the provided options (Varsity, Junior Varsity, Freshman, Middle School, or Club).
4. For each player, enter their details in the corresponding fields (page 3-6), following the format "Name, Number, and Position".
5. Enter the name of your team's manager in the Team Manager Name field (page 7).
6. Provide the primary emergency contact information (page 8).
7. Finally, select the date when the roster was finalized (page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Official Team Name (page 2)**: The name of your basketball team.
* **Head Coach and Assistant Coach Names (page 2)**: Enter the names of your head coach and any assistant coaches.
* **Competition Level (page 2)**: Choose the level of competition your team participates in (Varsity, Junior Varsity, Freshman, Middle School, or Club).
* **Player 1 - Name, Number, and Position (page 3)**: Enter the details of your first player in the format "Name, Number, and Position".
* **Player 2 - Name, Number, and Position (page 4)**: Enter the details of your second player in the format "Name, Number, and Position".
* **Player 3 - Name, Number, and Position (page 5)**: Enter the details of your third player in the format "Name, Number, and Position".
* **Player 4 - Name, Number, and Position (page 6)**: Enter the details of your fourth player in the format "Name, Number, and Position".
* **Player 5 - Name, Number, and Position (page 7)**: Enter the details of your fifth player in the format "Name, Number, and Position".
* **Player 6 - Name, Number, and Position (page 8)**: Enter the details of your sixth player in the format "Name, Number, and Position". This field is not required.
* **Player 7 - Name, Number, and Position (page 8)**: Enter the details of your seventh player in the format "Name, Number, and Position". This field is not required.
* **Player 8 - Name, Number, and Position (page 9)**: Enter the details of your eighth player in the format "Name, Number, and Position". This field is not required.
* **Player 9 - Name, Number, and Position (page 10)**: Enter the details of your ninth player in the format "Name, Number, and Position". This field is not required.
* **Player 10 - Name, Number, and Position (page 11)**: Enter the details of your tenth player in the format "Name, Number, and Position". This field is not required.
* **Team Manager Name (page 7)**: Enter the name of your team's manager.
* **Primary Emergency Contact for the Team (page 8)**: Enter the primary emergency contact information for your team in the format "Phone number and Name".
* **Date Roster Finalized (page 8)**: Select the date when the roster was finalized.

## Tips

* Be sure to enter each player's details accurately and in the correct format.
* If a player does not have a number, you can leave it blank.
* The Competition Level field is required to help your team participate in the correct tournaments and leagues.
* The Date Roster Finalized field is essential for record-keeping purposes.
