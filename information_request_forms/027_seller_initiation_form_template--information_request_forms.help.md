The purpose of this form is to provide a standardized and structured way to gather relevant information, ensuring accuracy and completeness.

### Seller Initiation Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect seller information for onboarding sellers on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information in the "Seller Information" section.
2. Provide details about your business in the "Business Information" section.
3. Answer questions related to your marketplace presence in the "Marketplace Information" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Seller Information (Page 1)
* **Seller Name** (`seller_info`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the seller.
* **Seller Email** (`seller_info`, `text`, required): Enter your email address associated with your seller account.
* (Optional fields: Please add any additional information you'd like to share about yourself)

### Business Information (Page 2)
* **Business Name** (`business_info`, `text`, required): Enter your business name.
* **Business Address** (`business_info`, `text`, required): Enter your business address.
* **Business Phone** (`business_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your business phone number.
* (Optional fields: Please add any additional information you'd like to share about your business)

### Marketplace Information (Page 3)
* **Marketplace** (`marketplace_info`, `text`, required): Enter the marketplace you are currently or plan to be active on.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible while filling out the form to ensure accurate information.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
