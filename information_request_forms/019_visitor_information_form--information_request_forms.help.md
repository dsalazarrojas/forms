# Visitor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect visitor information for a specified time period. You will be asked to input your time of entry, time of departure, reason for your visit, email address, and other relevant details. This information is essential to provide a smooth and secure experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1.  Enter the time you arrived on the specified date (Time in field).
2.  Enter the time you plan to leave (Time out field).
3.  Specify the reason for your visit (Reason for visit field).
4.  Enter your email address for contact purposes (Email field).
5.  Add any additional visitor information (Visitor information field).
6.  Enter your phone number for contact purposes (Phone field).
7.  Specify who you are visiting (Assigned to field).
8.  Enter any comments or special requests (Comments field).
9.  Select the date of your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Time in**: Enter the time you arrived at the facility. This is the time you entered the premises.
*   **Time out**: Enter the time you plan to leave the facility. This is the expected time of departure.
*   **Reason for visit**: Specify the reason for your visit, such as a meeting, tour, or inspection.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes. This will help us reach out to you with any updates or reminders.
*   **Visitor information**: Add any additional visitor information that may be relevant to your visit, such as any allergies or medical conditions.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This will help us reach out to you in case of an emergency.
*   **Assigned to**: Specify who you are visiting, such as a specific department or team.
*   **Comments**: Enter any comments or special requests, such as accessibility needs or parking instructions.
*   **Date of visit**: Select the date of your visit, which will help us track your visit and provide a smooth experience.
