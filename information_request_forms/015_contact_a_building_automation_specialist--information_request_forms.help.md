# Contact A Building Automation Specialist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you contact a building automation specialist for guidance on your building automation needs. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary information to better understand your requirements and provide you with the best possible solution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the "Contact Information" section with your personal details.
2. Provide information about the building you are working with, including the type of building and its approximate square footage.
3. Let us know about any existing automation systems in place.
4. Describe your primary automation goals for the project.
5. Provide specific requirements and constraints for the project.
6. Select a range of estimated project budget and expected project timeline.
7. Add any additional comments or information that would be helpful for us to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to help us identify you.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Provide your current job title or position.
* **Company/Organization Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company or organization you represent.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your primary business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Provide your business phone number.
* **Type of Building** (`building_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of building you are working with (e.g., Commercial Office, Healthcare/Hospital, etc.).
* **Total Square Footage** (`square_footage`, number, optional): Enter the approximate size of the facility.
* **Existing Automation System** (`existing_automation_system`, text, optional): If there are any existing automation systems in place, provide details about them.
* **Primary Automation Goals** (`primary_automation_goals`, select_multiple, required): Select the primary goals for the automation project (e.g., Energy Efficiency, Occupant Comfort, etc.).
* **Specific Project Requirements** (`specific_requirements`, text, required): Describe your project requirements and constraints in detail.
* **Estimated Project Budget** (`estimated_project_budget`, select_one, optional): Select the range of estimated project budget.
* **Expected Project Timeline** (`project_timeline`, select_one, required): Select when you would like the project to begin (e.g., Immediately, Within 3 Months, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that would be helpful for us to know.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): This field is automatically populated with the current date.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be completed in order to submit the form.
