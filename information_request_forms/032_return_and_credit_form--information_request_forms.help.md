# return_and_credit_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "return_and_credit_form" is used for customers to provide information about their returned products. This form collects data on the reason for return, product details, customer name, and a message for the buyer. The returned product's condition and an optional image are also collected. This data helps merchants and buyers understand and resolve any issues quickly and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the "return_and_credit_form" form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the product details.
2.  Select the reason for return (e.g., Refund and Replace, Store Credit, Shipping Error).
3.  Provide your customer name.
4.  Enter your message to the buyer.
5.  Select the product condition (e.g., New, Used, Refurbished).
6.  Optionally, provide a product image.
7.  Submit the form to complete the return and credit process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reason for Return**: Select the reason for return. Options include:
	+ Refund and Replace: The buyer wants a refund and would like to replace the product.
	+ Store Credit: The buyer would like a credit on their store account.
	+ Shipping Error: The product was shipped incorrectly (e.g., wrong product, late arrival).
* **Product Details**: Enter a brief description of the product to help resolve the issue quickly.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name, as you will be the one initiating the return and credit process.
* **Buyer Message**: Enter a message to communicate with the buyer and resolve the issue.
* **Product Condition**: Select the condition of the returned product (e.g., New, Used, Refurbished).
* **Product Image**: If available, upload a product image to help facilitate the resolution process. This can be an image of the product or a photo of the product's packaging.
