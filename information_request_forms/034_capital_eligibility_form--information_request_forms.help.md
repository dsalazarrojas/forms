# Capital Eligibility Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Capital Eligibility Form is a tool used to determine if your business qualifies for capital funding. It will ask you for information about your business operations, financial history, and capital requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Read the form carefully**: Before starting, review the form to understand what information is required and why.
2. **Fill in your personal and business information**: Provide your first name, last name, date of birth, email address, phone number, and other personal details.
3. **Enter your business details**: Provide your business name, type, address, city, state, zip code, and years in business.
4. **Provide financial information**: Enter your annual revenue, revenue trend, profitability status, personal credit score, business credit score, and total outstanding debt.
5. **Describe your financial situation**: Select your credit history, outstanding debt, and other financial details.
6. **Specify your capital needs**: Choose a purpose for the capital you're seeking and describe how you plan to use the funds.
7. **Submit your application**: Once you've filled out all the required fields, click the submit button to review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your government ID.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your government ID.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your birthdate (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Name**: Enter your business name.
* **Business Type**: Select the type of business you have (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, etc.).
* **Business Address**: Enter your business address.
* **City**: Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State/Province**: Enter the state or province where your business is located.
* **Zip/Postal Code**: Enter the zip code of your business location.
* **Years in Business**: Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Business Start Date**: Enter the date your business started.
* **Annual Revenue (Last Year)**: Enter the dollar amount of your business's revenue for the previous year.
* **Revenue Trend**: Select the trend of your revenue (e.g., Growing steadily, Growing rapidly, Stable, Declining, Uncertain).
* **Profitability Status**: Select your profit status (e.g., Highly Profitable, Profitable, Break-even, Minor losses, Significant losses).
* **Personal Credit Score**: Select your credit score (e.g., 750 plus, 700-749, 650-699, etc.).
* **Business Credit Score**: If available, select your business credit score (e.g., Strong, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Credit History**: Select your credit history (e.g., Excellent - No delinquencies, Good - Minor issues resolved, Fair - Some late payments, etc.).
* **Total Outstanding Debt**: Enter the dollar amount of your outstanding debt.
* **Monthly Debt Obligations**: Enter the total monthly payments you make on your debt.
* **Property Owned**: Select if you own property or not.
* **Approximate Property Value**: If you own property, enter the approximate value.
* **Other Collateral**: If you have other collateral, describe it.
* **Capital Amount Requested**: Enter the dollar amount of capital you are seeking.
* **Purpose of Capital**: Select the purpose of the capital you are seeking (e.g., Working capital, Equipment purchase, etc.).
* **Detailed Use of Funds**: Describe how you plan to use the capital funds.
* **Expected Repayment Timeline**: Select the expected time it will take to repay the capital amount.

Note: This guide is intended to be a general help guide and may require additional guidance based on the specific requirements of the financial institution or entity providing the capital.
