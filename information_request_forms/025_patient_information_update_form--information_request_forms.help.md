# Patient Information Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Information Update Form is used to collect and update patient information for existing patients. This form helps ensure that patient records are accurate and complete.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each field carefully before filling out the form to ensure you understand what each field is used for.
2. Enter the required information for each field accurately and completely.
3. Review the form thoroughly before submitting it to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's current address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Medical Record Number** (`Medical Record Number`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Medical Provider** (`Medical Provider`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the patient's healthcare provider.
* **Relationship Status** (`Relationship Status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the patient's current relationship status (Married, Single, Divorced, or Widowed).
* **Employment Status** (`Employment Status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the patient's current employment status (Employed, Unemployed, Retired, Disabled, or Student).
* **Income Range** (`Income Range`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the patient's income range (Low, Average, High, or Very High).
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the patient's current medical conditions (None, High Blood Pressure, Diabetes, Asthma, or Cancer).
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, required/optional): Enter any significant medical history or conditions.
* **Medical Provider Address** (`Medical Provider Address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the patient's healthcare provider.
* **Medical Provider** (`Medical Provider`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the patient's healthcare provider.
* **Employment Status 2** (`Employment Status 2`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's employment status (if different from their previous employment status).

Note: The fields marked as "required/optional" are only required if you are making changes to that specific field.
