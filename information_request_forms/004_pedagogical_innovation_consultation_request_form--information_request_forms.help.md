# Pedagogical Innovation Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users who are interested in discussing pedagogical innovation with our team. Please fill out this form to request a consultation on a specific pedagogical innovation topic or to ask for guidance on how to implement a particular pedagogical approach in your educational setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Specify your department in the "Department" field.
5. Provide a clear and concise "Message" in the "Message" field.
6. Choose your preferred contact method in the "Contact" field.
7. (Optional) Provide any additional information about your pedagogical innovation request in the "Additional Info" field.
8. Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of our consultation process by selecting either "True" or "False" in the "Confirm" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you would like to receive responses to your pedagogical innovation request.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Specify the department where you work or the area of study you are involved in.
* **Message** (`message`, `note`, required): Clearly and concisely describe the pedagogical innovation you are interested in discussing or implementing.
* **Contact** (`contact`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Phone: We will call or text you.
	+ Email: We will respond via email.
	+ Address: We will send you a response by post.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional information about your pedagogical innovation request.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of our consultation process by selecting either "True" or "False".
