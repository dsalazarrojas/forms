# Saxophone Preference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Saxophone Preference Form is a survey that helps users identify their preferred type of saxophone. It asks users to select one type of saxophone from three options: Alto, Tenor, or Baritone. Additionally, users are asked to provide their student name.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your preferred type of saxophone from the options provided.
2. Optionally, enter your student name, if you are a student.
3. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Alto Saxophone**: Select the type of saxophone you prefer, which is Alto.
* **Tenor Saxophone**: Select the type of saxophone you prefer, which is Tenor.
* **Baritone Saxophone**: Select the type of saxophone you prefer, which is Baritone.
* **Student**: Provide your student name, if you are a student (optional).

## Tips
* Be honest about your saxophone preference.
* If you are unsure about your preference, choose the one that sounds most appealing to you.
* Please do not enter fictional or fake student names.
