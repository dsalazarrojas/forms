# Financial Planning Client Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Planning Client Profile Form is designed to collect essential information about a client's personal and financial details. This form helps us understand their financial situation, goals, and risk tolerance, enabling us to provide personalized services and advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Enter your Date of Birth.
6. Select your Employment Status: Active, Passive, or Disabled.
7. Choose your primary Income Source: Wages, Salary, Interest, Investment, Rental Income, or Other.
8. Select all relevant Financial Institutions: Bank, Credit Union, Trust, or Other.
9. Choose your Financial Planning Goals: Retirement, Wealth Building, Estate Planning, Risk Management, or Other.
10. Determine your Risk Tolerance (on a scale of 1-10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1995-02-12).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select your current employment status: Active, Passive, or Disabled.
* **Income Source** (`income_source`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all relevant income sources: Wages, Salary, Interest, Investment, Rental Income, or Other.
* **Financial Institutions** (`financial_institutions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all relevant financial institutions: Bank, Credit Union, Trust, or Other.
* **Financial Planning Goals** (`financial_planning_goals`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all relevant financial planning goals: Retirement, Wealth Building, Estate Planning, Risk Management, or Other.
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, `number`, optional): Determine your risk tolerance on a scale of 1-10 (where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest).
* **Financial Institutions** (`financial_institutions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all relevant financial institutions: Bank, Credit Union, Trust, or Other.
