# Mobile App Company Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about your mobile app company. Please fill it out accurately and completely to help us understand your needs and provide you with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions or approvals to provide this information.
3. If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Name (Company Name)
* Company Name is the name of your company. This could be a sole proprietorship, partnership, corporation, or any other type of business entity.
### Email (Email)
* This is the email address we will use to contact you. Please make sure it's a valid email address that you check regularly.
### Phone (Phone)
* This is the phone number we will use to contact you. Please provide a valid phone number.
### Website (Website)
* This is the URL of your company's website. If you don't have a website, please provide a valid URL or an explanation.
### App Name (App Name)
* This is the name of your mobile app. Please provide a clear and descriptive name.
### App Description (App Description)
* This is a brief description of your mobile app. Please keep it concise and clear.
### App Platform (App Platform)
* Select the platform on which your app is developed or will be released. This could be Apple, Google, or Other.
### App Type (App Type)
* Select the type of your app. This could be Game, Social, Productivity, or Finance.
### Team Size (Team Size)
* This is the size of your team. Please provide the actual number.
### Company Type (Company Type)
* Select the type of your company. This could be Agency, Independent, or Partnership.
### Contact Name (Contact Name)
* This is the name of the contact person. Please provide a valid name.
### Contact Title (Contact Title)
* This is the title of the contact person. Please provide a valid title.
### Contact Email (Contact Email)
* This is the email address of the contact person. Please make sure it's a valid email address that you check regularly.
### Contact Phone (Contact Phone)
* This is the phone number of the contact person. Please provide a valid phone number.
### Company Street (Company Street)
* This is the street address of your company. Please provide a valid address.
### Company City (Company City)
* This is the city where your company is located.
### Company State (Company State)
* This is the state where your company is located.
### Company Zip (Company Zip)
* This is the zip code of your company.
### Company Country (Company Country)
* This is the country where your company is located.
### Additional Info (Additional Info)
* This is an optional field for any additional information you want to provide.
### App Logo (App Logo)
* Please provide a URL or a description of your app's logo.
### App Screenshots (App Screenshots)
* This is an optional field for providing screenshots of your app. Please provide valid URLs or descriptions.
### Privacy Policy (Privacy Policy)
* This is the link to your company's privacy policy. Please make sure it's a valid URL.
### Terms and Conditions (Terms and Conditions)
* This is the link to your company's terms and conditions. Please make sure it's a valid URL.
### Terms of Service (Terms of Service)
* This is the link to your company's terms of service. Please make sure it's a valid URL.

Please fill out this form accurately and completely. If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
