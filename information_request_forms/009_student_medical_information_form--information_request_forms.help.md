# Student Medical Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information about a student's medical needs. Please complete this form to provide accurate and up-to-date medical information that will help the student's support team assist with any health-related needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the correct fields and filling in the necessary information accurately.
2. For each field, provide the most accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Double-check the form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (Student Name)**: Enter the student's full name as per their official records.
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth)**: Provide the student's date of birth in the standard YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-08-12).
* **Contact Number (Contact Number)**: Enter the student's contact number, including any necessary details to reach them quickly and efficiently.
* **Email Address (Email Address)**: Enter the student's current and valid email address for any communication regarding their health needs.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the student's health needs are properly addressed.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with the relevant authorities or the student directly.
* Complete the form carefully to avoid any potential issues or delays in providing medical support.
