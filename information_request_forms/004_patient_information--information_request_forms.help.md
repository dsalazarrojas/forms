# patient_information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect basic patient information for medical or clinical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the first name of the patient in the "First name" field.
2. Enter the last name of the patient in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter the date of birth of the patient in the "Date of birth" field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
4. Enter the email address of the patient in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the phone number of the patient in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (First name, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the patient.
* **Last name** (Last name, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the patient.
* **Date of birth** (Date of birth, date, required: false): Enter the date of birth of the patient in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the patient.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the patient.
