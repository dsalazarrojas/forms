# Vendor Finance Office Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Finance Office Contact Form is designed to provide a channel for vendors to reach out to the finance team with their financial inquiries, concerns, or requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as it appears on your business cards or correspondence.
2.  Provide your email address that you use for business purposes.
3.  Enter your contact phone number for quick communication.
4.  Select the name of the finance team member you would like to direct your inquiry to (Jimmy, Jane, or John).
5.  Choose the finance department or team that handles your inquiry (Accounting, Accounts Payable, or Budgeting).
6.  Indicate the type of inquiry you are submitting (Request for Payment, Credit Inquiry, or Other).
7.  Briefly describe the nature of your inquiry or request in the provided text area.
8.  Optionally, if you are submitting this form on behalf of someone else, please enter their name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Vendor Name**: This is the name of the company or individual submitting the inquiry.
*   **Contact Email**: This should be the email address you use for business purposes, where the finance team can reach out to you.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for quick and easy communication with the finance team.
*   **Finance Team Member**: Choose the team member you would like to direct your inquiry to, from our finance team members listed.
*   **Finance Department**: Select the department that handles your specific inquiry (Accounting, Accounts Payable, or Budgeting).
*   **Inquiry Type**: This is the type of inquiry you are submitting (Request for Payment, Credit Inquiry, or Other).
*   **Message**: Briefly describe the nature of your inquiry or request in this text area.
*   **Submitted By**: This is optional, but if you are submitting this form on behalf of someone else, please enter their name.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely to facilitate efficient processing of your inquiry.
*   If your inquiry requires attachments or supporting documents, please submit them separately to the finance team.
*   We will respond to your inquiry as soon as possible, but please allow 2-3 business days for us to review and process your request.
