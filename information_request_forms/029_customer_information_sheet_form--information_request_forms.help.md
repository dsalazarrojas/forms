# Customer Information Sheet Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Information Sheet Form is used to collect and store customer information for various purposes. This form is designed to gather essential details about customers, including general contact and customer information, payment and shipping details, and any additional notes or custom fields. Please ensure that you only complete this form for relevant and non-duplicative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) for each page.
2. Enter your information accurately and thoroughly in each field.
3. Review and edit your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date** (*, date): Enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (*, time): Enter the current time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Name** (*, text): Enter your name or the customer's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (*, email): Enter the customer's email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (*, text): Enter the customer's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (*, text): Enter the customer's address as it appears on official documents.
* **City** (*, text): Enter the city where the customer is located.
* **State** (*, text): Enter the state where the customer is located.
* **Zip** (*, text): Enter the customer's zip code.
* **Signature** (date): Enter your digital signature or the customer's signature as it appears on official documents.
* **Notes** (note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the customer.
* **Accept** (*, select_one): Select 'Yes' if you agree to the terms and conditions or 'No' otherwise.
* **Other** (select_multiple): Select any other options as applicable to the customer information.
* **Custom 1-5** (text): Enter any additional custom information as required.
* **General Info** (text): Enter any general information about the customer.
* **Contact Info** (text): Enter the customer's contact information, if applicable.
* **Customer Info** (text): Enter the customer's information, if applicable.
* **Business Info** (text): Enter the business information, if applicable.
* **Payment Info** (text): Enter the payment information, if applicable.
* **Shipping Info** (text): Enter the shipping information, if applicable.
