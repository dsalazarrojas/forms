# Pet Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Information Form is used to collect comprehensive data about pets, including their name, type, age, weight, health, and owner's contact information. This data is used to maintain accurate and up-to-date records about pets in shelters or under veterinary care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the pet's name in the **Pet Name** field.
2. Select the pet's type from the **pet_type** dropdown menu.
3. Enter the pet's age and weight in the **pet_age** and **pet_weight** fields, respectively.
4. Select the pet's health status from the **pet_health** dropdown menu.
5. Enter the owner's name, phone number, email address, and address in the **owner_name**, **owner_phone**, **owner_email**, and **owner_address** fields, respectively.
6. Select the date the pet was registered or last visited the vet from the **date_registered** and **date_last_vet** fields, respectively.
7. Select the reason the pet was released or last visited the vet from the **release_reason** and **last_vet_reason** fields, respectively.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes about the pet in the **additional_comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, required): Enter the pet's name, such as "Bella" or "Max".
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, required): Select the pet's type, such as "Cat", "Dog", or "Other".
* **Pet Age** (`pet_age`, number, required): Enter the pet's age in years or months.
* **Pet Weight** (`pet_weight`, number, required): Enter the pet's weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Pet Color** (`pet_color`, text, optional): Enter the pet's color, such as "Black" or "White".
* **Pet Health** (`pet_health`, select_one, optional): Select the pet's health status, such as "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Enter the owner's name.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, required): Enter the owner's phone number.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, required): Enter the owner's email address.
* **Owner Address** (`owner_address`, text, optional): Enter the owner's address.
* **Pet License** (`pet_license`, select_one, optional): Select the pet's license status, such as "None", "Spayed", or "Neutered".
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Enter any medical conditions or notes about the pet.
* **Veterinarian Clinic** (`vet_clinic`, text, optional): Enter the veterinarian's clinic or hospital name.
* **Shelter Name** (`shelter_name`, select_one, optional): Select the shelter name.
* **Date Registered** (`date_registered`, date, required): Enter the date the pet was registered or last visited the vet.
* **Registered By** (`registered_by`, select_one, optional): Select whether the pet was registered or last visited the vet by someone or not.
* **Date Released** (`date_released`, date, optional): Enter the date the pet was released or last visited the vet.
* **Release Reason** (`release_reason`, select_one, optional): Select the reason the pet was released or last visited the vet.
* **Last Vet Visit** (`date_last_vet`, date, optional): Enter the date the pet last visited the vet.
* **Last Vet Name** (`last_vet_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the last veterinarian.
* **Last Vet Contact** (`last_vet_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the last veterinarian.
* **Last Vet Location** (`last_vet_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the last veterinarian visit.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the pet.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the pet's data is reliable and secure.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the veterinarian or shelter staff for assistance.
