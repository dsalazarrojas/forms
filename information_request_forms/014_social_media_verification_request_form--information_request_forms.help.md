# Social Media Verification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Verification Request Form is used to verify your identity on various social media platforms. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary information to confirm your identity and ensure your account is secure.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select your social media platform from the "Social Media Platform" dropdown menu.
2.  Fill in your "User Name" on the platform.
3.  Enter your "Email" associated with the platform.
4.  Provide your "Phone Number" for verification purposes.
5.  Enter your "User Address" to confirm your location.
6.  Upload "Proof" that verifies your identity (e.g., a screenshot of your profile or a photo with your ID).
7.  Add any "Notes" about your verification request, if needed.
8.  Click the "Submit" button to finalize your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Social Media Platform** (`social_media_platform`, `select_one`, required): This field allows you to select the social media platform you are trying to verify. Please choose the correct platform from the dropdown menu to ensure your request is processed correctly.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name associated with your social media platform account. This helps us confirm your identity on the platform.
* **Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address associated with your social media platform account. This helps us verify your account ownership.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `number`, required): Provide your phone number to complete the verification process. This is an important step for security purposes.
* **User Address** (`user_address`, `text`, required): Enter your home address or location to confirm your identity and prevent impersonation.
* **Proof** (`upload_proof`, `text`, required): Upload a file or photo that proves your identity. Examples include a screenshot of your profile or a photo with your government-issued ID.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional information about your verification request, you can add it here. This is optional but helps us understand your situation better.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, `text`, required): Click this button to finalize your request and start the verification process.
