# Sign In Sheet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect basic information from visitors, including their name, contact details, and a note about their visit, for internal record-keeping and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
	* If required, enter your first name.
	* Enter your last name.
	* Enter your email address.
2. If not required, you can leave the fields blank.
3. If applicable, select the role you are visiting under (Student, Employee, or Guest).
4. Choose the date and time of your visit.
5. Add any additional notes about your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Role** (select_one, optional): Choose the role you are visiting under (Student, Employee, or Guest).
* **Date** (date, optional): Choose the date of your visit.
* **Time** (time, optional): Choose the time of your visit.
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about your visit.
