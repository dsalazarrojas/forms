# Developer Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Developer Enrollment Form is a crucial step in our onboarding process. It allows us to collect essential information about the developer, including their name, company, contact details, and preferences. This form is designed to be filled out by the developer themselves, and it's intended to be completed only once.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use a valid email address that you check regularly.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your program of interest from the "Program" dropdown menu.
6. Choose your level of access to our system from the "Access" dropdown menu.
7. Indicate whether you have a partnership with us from the "Partnership" dropdown menu.
8. Add any additional notes about yourself or your project in the "Notes" field (optional).
9. Select whether you have an assigned tool from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, **required**): Enter your name as it appears on official documents, such as your passport or driver's license.
* **Company** (`company`, text, **required**): Enter your company name where you work.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **required**): Enter your phone number.
* **Program** (`program`, select_multiple, **required**): Select one or more programs that interest you.
* **Access** (`access`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose the level of access you need to our system.
	+ 'Yes' for full access to our system.
	+ 'No' to limited access.
* **Partnership** (`partnership`, select_multiple, **required**): Indicate whether you have a partnership with us.
	+ 'Yes' if you have a partnership agreement with us.
	+ 'No' if you do not have a partnership agreement with us.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, **optional**): Add any additional notes about yourself or your project.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, **required**): Select whether you have an assigned tool from our system.
	+ 'Yes' if you have an assigned tool.
	+ 'No' if you do not have an assigned tool.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your input for accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact us for assistance.
* We will review your submission and follow up with you shortly.
