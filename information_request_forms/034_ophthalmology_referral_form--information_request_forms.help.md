# Ophthalmology Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ophthalmology Referral Form is designed to collect essential information from patients or their representatives for referrals to ophthalmology medical facilities. This information will help the medical staff to assess the patient's needs and provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
2. Specify the medical condition of the patient that requires attention from an ophthalmology specialist.
3. Select the medical facility where the patient will be referred to, either Hospital, Clinic, or Laboratory.
4. Choose the medical specialty that best suits the patient's condition, from the options provided.
5. Enter the date and time of the service requested for the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Patient Information (Page 1)
* **Patient Name**: Enter the full name of the patient, as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Referral Request**: Describe the reason for the referral and the nature of the patient's condition.

### 2. Patient Contact Information (Page 2)
* **Contact Information**: Enter any relevant contact details, such as phone number or email address, to facilitate communication with the patient.
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of the attending doctor or specialist who recommended the referral.
* **Patient Condition**: Describe the patient's condition in more detail, if necessary.
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history or background information about the patient.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of the patient or their representative.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the patient or their representative.

### 3. Referral Details (Page 3)
* **Medical Specialty**: Choose the most relevant medical specialty for the patient's condition from the provided options.
* **Medical Facility**: Select the type of medical facility where the patient will be referred to, either Hospital, Clinic, or Laboratory.
* **Date of Service**: Enter the date when the patient is expected to visit the medical facility.
* **Time of Service**: Enter the scheduled time of the service for the patient.
* **Cardiology, Dermatology, Endocrinology, Gastroenterology, Gynecology, Nephrology, Neurology, Ophthalmology, Rheumatology, Urology, Pulmonology**: Select the specific medical specialty that best suits the patient's condition. 

Note: Please ensure that you have filled out all the required fields and provided accurate information to ensure a smooth referral process. If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the relevant medical staff.
