# personal_financial_snapshot_pdf - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and financial information for the purpose of creating a financial snapshot or profile.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Provide your **Social Security Number** for verification purposes.
4. Enter your **Address**, **City**, and **State** to help us locate you.
5. Enter your **ZIP** code.
6. Add your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
7. Enter your **Email** address.
8. Select your **Employment Status** from the options provided.
9. Enter your **Income**.
10. Fill in **Assets** and **Liabilities**.
11. Describe your **Financial Goals**.
12. Choose your **Financial Status** from the options provided.
13. Add any **Notes**.
14. Enter **Additional Financial Info** if applicable.
15. Provide **Emergency Contact** information.
16. Enter **Contact Name**, **Contact Email**, and **Contact Phone**.
17. Describe your **Contact Relationship**.
18. Choose your **Financial Influencers** from the options provided.
19. Add any additional **Notes 2**.
