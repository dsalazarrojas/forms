# monthly_market_update_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "monthly_market_update_request" form is designed to collect information from subscribers for a monthly market update. The form is only displayed to new or updated preferences from subscribers, ensuring that only relevant updates are delivered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your subscriber details.
2. Select your topic preferences.
3. Choose how often you would like to receive updates.
4. Enter your delivery email address.
5. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
6. Enter your delivery address (if applicable).
7. Add any additional delivery notes (if applicable).
8. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subscriber Details**: Enter your name, surname, or other relevant details.
* **Topic Preferences**: Select all the topics you are interested in by choosing "Yes" from the list.
* **Delivery Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to receive updates:
	+ "Yes" for daily updates
	+ "No" for no updates
* **Delivery Email**: Enter the email address where you would like to receive updates.
* **Delivery Phone**: Enter your phone number to receive updates via SMS (if applicable).
* **Delivery Address**: Enter your delivery address for physical updates (if applicable).
* **Delivery Note**: Add any additional delivery instructions or notes (if applicable).
* **Form Submit**: Please submit the form after completing all fields.
