# Nanny Background Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for background checks on nannies or caregivers. It is intended for parents or caregivers who need to request background checks on their nannies or caregivers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the options carefully and select the correct information for your background check.
2. Enter the required information, such as the name and contact details of the person being checked.
3. Select the type of background check that suits your needs from the options provided.
4. Specify the frequency and reason for the background check.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Name 
*   **Name** (`name_input`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the person being checked.

### 2. Email 
*   **Email** (`email_input`, Email, Required): Enter the email address of the person being checked.

### 3. Phone Number 
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_input`, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the person being checked.

### 4. Address 
*   **Address** (`address_input`, Text, Required): Enter the address of the person being checked.

### 5. Who Is Conducting The Background Check 
*   **Who Is Conducting The Background Check** (`background_check_conducting`, Select One, Required): Select the person or organization conducting the background check from the options provided.

### 6. What Type Of Background Check 
*   **What Type Of Background Check** (`background_check_type`, Select One, Required): Select the type of background check that suits your needs from the options provided.

### 7. How Often Do You Conduct A Background Check 
*   **How Often Do You Conduct A Background Check** (`background_check_frequency`, Select One, Required): Select the frequency of the background check from the options provided.

### 8. Reason For Background Check 
*   **Reason For Background Check** (`background_check_reason`, Text, Required): Enter the reason for the background check.

### 9. Additional Comments 
*   **Additional Comments** (`background_check_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information related to the background check.
