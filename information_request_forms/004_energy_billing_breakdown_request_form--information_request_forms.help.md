# Energy Billing Breakdown Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Billing Breakdown Request Form is used to collect information from employees to facilitate the breakdown process of their energy bills for the current billing cycle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form sections and ensure you have entered all required and optional information.
2. For account details, select the options that apply to your situation.
3. Answer the billing questions in as much detail as possible.
4. Review your form to ensure all fields are accurately filled out before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (`id: 1`, text, required: false):
    This section is the starting point of the form. Please review and acknowledge this page before proceeding.
* **First Page Questions** (`id: 2`, text, required: false):
    This section is not currently used in the form and may be removed. Please ignore it.
* **Account Details** (`id: 3`, text, required: false):
    Enter a brief description of your account details here. This could include any relevant information about your account such as your account name, account number, or billing address.
* **Account Details Fields** (`id: 4`, select multiple, required: false):
    Select the options that apply to your account details:
    + Select One
    + Select Two
    + Select Three
* **Select One** (`id: 5`, select one, required: false):
    Select one of the options for account details.
* **Select Two** (`id: 6`, select multiple, required: false):
    Select multiple options that apply to your account details.
* **Billing Questions** (`id: 7`, text, required: false):
    Enter your billing questions in the space provided.
* **Billing Questions 1-17** (`id: 8-24`, text, required: false):
    These fields are for you to enter any additional billing questions.
* **Submit** (`id: 25`, text, required: false):
    Once you have filled out the form, submit it to the energy billing team.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have reviewed the form carefully before submission.
- Make sure you have answered all required and optional fields.
- Enter accurate and detailed information about your account and billing questions.
- The energy billing team will review your submitted form to provide a breakdown of your energy bill.
