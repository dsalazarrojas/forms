# Confidential Inquiry Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Confidential Inquiry Submission Form" is used to collect information about sensitive matters, such as complaints, grievances, or other concerns. This form is designed to gather information without disclosing the identity of the submitter and to guide the submitter through a structured process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information (fields 1-3) if you wish to remain anonymous, you can leave these fields blank.
2. Select the type of inquiry (field 6).
3. Provide a brief title and detailed description of the inquiry (fields 7 and 8).
4. If applicable, provide the date and time of the incident (fields 9 and 10).
5. Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your inquiry (field 24).
6. Specify whether you have evidence available and describe it (fields 15 and 16).
7. Answer questions about previous reporting (field 17).
8. Select the urgency level of the inquiry (field 18).
9. Describe the desired outcome (field 19).
10. Indicate whether you want to remain anonymous (field 20).
11. Answer questions about potential retaliation concerns (field 21).
12. Choose the preferred contact method (field 22).
13. Specify the best time to contact you (field 23).
14. Read and understand the terms and conditions (field 26).
15. Confirm the accuracy of the information provided (field 27).
16. Finally, submit the form by selecting the submission date (field 28).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Your Full Name (Field 1)
* Optional: If you wish to remain anonymous, you can leave this field blank.

### Email Address (Field 2)
* Optional: This field is required only if you wish to be contacted about your submission. If anonymous, you can leave this field blank.

### Phone Number (Field 3)
* Optional: This field is required only if you wish to be contacted about your submission. If anonymous, you can leave this field blank.

### Organization (Field 4)
* Optional: This field is your affiliation.

### Department (Field 5)
* Optional: This field is your department within the organization.

### Type of Inquiry (Field 6)
* Required: Select the category of your inquiry.

### Brief Title (Field 7)
* Required: Provide a brief title of your inquiry.

### Detailed Description (Field 8)
* Required: Provide a detailed description of your inquiry.

### Date of Incident (Field 9)
* Optional: If applicable, provide the date of the incident.

### Time of Incident (Field 10)
* Optional: If known, provide the time of the incident.

### Location of Incident (Field 11)
* Optional: Where did the incident occur.

### Individuals Involved (Field 12)
* Optional: If known, list the individuals involved.

### Witnesses (Field 13)
* Optional: List any witnesses.

### Evidence Available (Field 14)
* Required: Select whether you have evidence available.

### Evidence Description (Field 15)
* Optional: If yes, describe the evidence.

### Previously Reported (Field 16)
* Required: Select whether you have previously reported.

### Previous Report Details (Field 17)
* Optional: If yes, describe when and to whom.

### Urgency Level (Field 18)
* Required: Select the level of urgency.

### Desired Outcome (Field 19)
* Optional: Describe the desired outcome.

### Anonymity Request (Field 20)
* Required: Select whether you wish to remain anonymous.

### Concern About Retaliation (Field 21)
* Required: Select your level of concern about potential retaliation.

### Preferred Contact Method (Field 22)
* Optional: Choose how you would like to be contacted.

### Best Time to Contact (Field 23)
* Optional: If applicable, provide your preferred contact time.

### Additional Information (Field 24)
* Optional: Provide any other relevant details.

### Supporting Documents (Field 25)
* Optional: List any attachments.

### I Understand This Is Confidential (Field 26)
* Required: Acknowledge the terms and conditions.

### I Certify This Information Is Accurate (Field 27)
* Required: Confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

### Date of Submission (Field 28)
* Required: Select the date of submission.
