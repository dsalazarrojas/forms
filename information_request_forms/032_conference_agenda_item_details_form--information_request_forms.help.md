# Conference Agenda Item Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide detailed information about individual conference agenda items. The information collected will be reviewed by organizers before inclusion in the conference schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact email address.
2. Enter a brief title for your agenda item.
3. Specify the proposed time allocation in minutes.
4. Enter the names and affiliations of the presenters.
5. Describe what attendees will learn from your agenda item (if required).
6. Specify the required audiovisual equipment.
7. Choose the preferred session type (talk, workshop, panel, or poster).
8. Select the target audience level (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
9. Add any additional notes for reviewers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter name**: Enter your name as the person submitting the agenda item.
* **Contact email**: Enter your email address for follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Agenda item title**: Enter a short and descriptive title for your agenda item.
* **Duration in minutes**: Enter the proposed time allocation for your agenda item in minutes.
* **Presenter names**: Enter the names and affiliations of the presenters.
* **What attendees will learn**: Describe what attendees will learn from your agenda item (if required).
* **Required audiovisual equipment**: Specify the audiovisual equipment required for your agenda item (if any).
* **Preferred session type**: Choose the preferred session type from the list (talk, workshop, panel, or poster).
* **Target audience level**: Select the target audience level from the list (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
* **Additional notes**: Add any additional information for reviewers.

## Tips
Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a fair review process. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
