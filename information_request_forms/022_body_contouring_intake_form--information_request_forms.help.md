# Body Contouring Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Body Contouring Intake Form is designed to collect information from patients about their body contouring concerns, goals, and medical history. The form helps our team understand your needs and create a tailored treatment plan to achieve your desired results.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete the Body Contouring Intake Form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please provide your personal contact information: 
    - **First Name**: Your legal name that you would like to be known by in the clinic.
    - **Last Name**: Your surname.
    - **Email Address**: Your email address where you can be reached for communication.
    - **Phone Number**: Your contact number where you can be reached for appointment reminders or follow-ups.
2.  Please provide your medical history: 
    - **Date of Birth**: Your birthdate.
    - **Address**: Your current address.
    - **Emergency Contact Name**: The name of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
    - **Emergency Contact Phone**: The phone number of the emergency contact person.
3.  Please select your desired goals and expectations: 
    - **Body Contouring Goals**: A brief description of what you hope to achieve through the treatment.
    - **Target Areas for Treatment**: Select all areas where you would like to see improvement.
4.  Provide your medical history and lifestyle information: 
    - **How long have you had this concern?**: Select the duration of your concern.
    - **How does this affect your lifestyle?**: A brief description of how this concern impacts your daily life.
    - **Current Health Conditions**: Any medical conditions you are currently dealing with.
    - **Current Medications**: Any medications you are currently taking.
    - **Known Allergies**: Any allergies you have.
    - **Previous Surgeries**: Any previous surgeries you have undergone.
    - **Relevant Family Medical History**: Any relevant medical history from your family.
    - **Current Lifestyle Habits**: A description of your current exercise, diet, smoking, and drinking habits.
    - **Weight History**: Any recent significant changes in your weight.
    - **Previous Body Contouring Procedures**: Any previous body contouring procedures you have had.
    - **Satisfaction with Previous Procedures**: Your satisfaction level with previous procedures.
5.  Review and confirm your information before submitting the form.
