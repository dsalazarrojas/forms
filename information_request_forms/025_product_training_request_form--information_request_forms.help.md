# product_training_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Product Training Request Form.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
2. Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
3. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **product-training-request-form** (`<name>`, `<type>`, required/true): 
  This is the title of the form. Please fill out your name as it appears on your company badge or ID.
* **product-category** (`product_category`, `select_one`, required/true): 
  Select 'Yes' if the product you are requesting training for has a category. Otherwise, select 'No'.
* **product-type** (`product_type`, `select_multiple`, required/true): 
  Select all applicable types of products you are requesting training for. You can select multiple types.
* **training-location** (`training_location`, `select_one`, required/true): 
  Select the location where you would like to receive the training.
* **training-date** (`training_date`, `date`, required/true): 
  Enter the date you would like to receive the training.
* **training-time** (`training_time`, `time`, required/false): 
  Enter the time you would like to receive the training. This field is optional.
* **product-description** (`product_description`, `text`, required/false): 
  Enter a brief description of the product you are requesting training for.
* **contact-full-name** (`contact_full_name`, `text`, required/true): 
  Enter your full name as it appears on your company ID.
* **contact-email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/true): 
  Enter your company email address.
* **contact-phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/false): 
  Enter your company phone number. This field is optional.
* **company** (`company`, `text`, required/true): 
  Enter your company name as it appears on your company ID.
* **department** (`department`, `text`, required/false): 
  Enter your department name as it appears on your company ID.
* **job-title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/true): 
  Enter your job title as it appears on your company ID.
* **job-level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, required/true): 
  Select your job level.
* **employee-id** (`employee_id`, `text`, required/true): 
  Enter your employee ID as it appears on your company ID.
* **manager** (`manager`, `select_multiple`, required/true): 
  Select if you have a manager. You can select multiple managers.
* **manager-email** (`manager_email`, `email`, required/false): 
  Enter your manager's email address. This field is optional.
* **training-facility** (`training_facility`, `text`, required/false): 
  Enter the facility where you would like to receive the training.
* **travel-date** (`travel_date`, `date`, required/false): 
  Enter the date you would like to travel for the training.
* **travel-date-end** (`travel_date_end`, `date`, required/false): 
  Enter the end date you would like to travel for the training.
* **travel-date-end-time** (`travel_date_end_time`, `time`, required/false): 
  Enter the end time you would like to travel for the training.
* **travel-cost** (`travel_cost`, `number`, required/false): 
  Enter the cost of travel.
* **comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/false): 
  Add any additional comments about your training request.
* **training-facility-manager** (`training_facility_manager`, `text`, required/false): 
  Enter the name of the training facility manager.
* **training-facility-manager-email** (`training_facility_manager_email`, `email`, required/false): 
  Enter the email of the training facility manager.
* **travel-cost** (`travel_cost`, `number`, required/false): 
  Enter the cost of travel.

Note: This form is for internal use only. Please review and follow your company's policies and procedures before filling out this form.
