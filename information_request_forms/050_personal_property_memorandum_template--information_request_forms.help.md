# personal_property_memorandum_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the acquisition and ownership of personal properties, such as real estate or vehicles. It ensures accurate and consistent tracking of personal properties within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the property list with a brief description of the property.
2. Enter the property name and its corresponding address.
3. Record the date the property was acquired and the date it was sold (if applicable).
4. Enter the purchase price and the seller's information (if applicable).
5. Record the buyer's information (if applicable).
6. Specify the purpose of the property.
7. Enter any other relevant details.
8. Date the property was received (if applicable).
9. Record the verification status and details (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Property List** (`personal_property_list`): Enter a brief description of the property.
* **Property Name** (`property_name`): Enter the name of the property.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the address of the property.
* **Date Acquired** (`date_acquired`): Record the date the property was acquired.
* **Date Sold** (`date_sold`): Record the date the property was sold (if applicable).
* **Value** (`value`): Enter the value of the property.
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`): Enter the price paid for the property (if applicable).
* **Seller** (`seller`): Enter the name of the seller.
* **Seller Address** (`seller_address`): Enter the address of the seller (if applicable).
* **Buyer** (`buyer`): Enter the name of the buyer.
* **Buyer Address** (`buyer_address`): Enter the address of the buyer (if applicable).
* **Purpose** (`purpose`): Specify the purpose of the property.
* **Other** (`other`): Enter any other relevant details.
* **Other Details** (`other_details`): Enter any other relevant details.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`): Record the date the property was received (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note`): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Date Verified** (`date_verified`): Record the date the property was verified (if applicable).
* **Verified By** (`verified_by`): Enter the name of the verifier.
* **Verification Method** (`verification_method`): Enter the method used to verify the property.
* **Verification Details** (`verification_details`): Enter the details of the verification process.
* **Verification Status** (`verification_status`): Select the status of the verification process.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`): Specify the type of property.
* **Property Type Details** (`property_type_details`): Enter any additional details about the property type.
* **Property Description** (`property_description`): Enter a brief description of the property.
* **Date Last Updated** (`date_last_updated`): Record the date the property information was last updated.
