This form is designed to gather information from individuals who are interested in requesting IV pricing information. It is a necessary step in the process for the medical facility's administration and billing team to gather necessary details before preparing an IV quote. The form is meant to be filled out by the individual making the request, and will be reviewed by a representative of the facility before following up with the individual.

**IV Pricing Information Request - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from individuals who are interested in requesting IV pricing information from our medical facility. By filling out this form, you will be providing us with the necessary details to prepare an accurate quote for your treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your personal details, including your name, contact information, and medical condition.
2. Select the type of IV service you are interested in (IV Drips, IV Infusions, or IV Pushes).
3. Confirm if you have insurance coverage and provide the name of your insurance provider.
4. Enter your best available time to be contacted and your preferred contact method.
5. Describe your medical condition and any relevant details.
6. Enter your phone number, email, and preferred follow-up appointment location (if applicable).
7. Enter the price you are willing to pay for the IV service.
8. Select any follow-up appointments you would like to schedule.
9. Choose a payment method for your follow-up appointment.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
- **IV Type** (`iv_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of IV service you are interested in (IV Drips, IV Infusions, or IV Pushes).
- **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have insurance coverage and enter the name of your insurance provider.
- **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): Describe your medical condition and any relevant details.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Best Time to Call** (`best_time_to_call`, time, required): Enter your best available time to be contacted.
- **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location where you would like to be contacted.
- **Message** (`message`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or questions.
- **IV Pricing Inquiries** (`iv_pricing_inquiries`, number, required): Enter the number of IV pricing inquiries you would like to discuss.
- **IV Pricing Request** (`iv_pricing_request`, number, required): Enter the price you are willing to pay for the IV service.
- **Follow-up Appointments** (`follow_up_appointments`, select_multiple, optional): Select any follow-up appointments you would like to schedule.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, text, required): Choose a payment method for your follow-up appointment.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes or comments.
- **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, required): Enter the name of the user who will be assigned to your case.
- **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of your follow-up appointment (Not Scheduled, Scheduled, or Completed).
- **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your follow-up appointment.
- **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your follow-up appointment.
- **Follow-up Location** (`follow_up_location`, text, required): Enter the location of your follow-up appointment.
- **Follow-up Instructions** (`follow_up_instructions`, text, required): Enter any instructions for your follow-up appointment.
- **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about your follow-up appointment.
- **Follow-up Comment** (`follow_up_comment`, text, required): Enter any additional comments about your follow-up appointment.
- **Follow-up Status Update** (`follow_up_status_update`, text, required): Enter the update status of your follow-up appointment.
- **Follow-up Next Step** (`follow_up_next_step`, text, required): Enter the next step in your follow-up appointment process.
- **Follow-up Last Update** (`follow_up_last_update`, date, required): Enter the last update date of your follow-up appointment status.

Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
