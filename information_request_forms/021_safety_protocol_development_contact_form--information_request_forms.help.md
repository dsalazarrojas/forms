# Safety Protocol Development Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report incidents, near-misses, or other safety concerns that occur during work hours.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with the required information (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2. Enter your name and title in the Staff Member field (page 1).
3. Select 'Yes' or 'No' in the Contact Method field (page 2) to indicate whether you would like to be contacted directly or not.
4. Enter the department and location (page 3) where the incident or concern occurred.
5. If relevant, select a date and time (page 5) for the incident or concern.
6. Enter any relevant safety procedures or improvements (page 7) that could help prevent similar incidents.
7. Optionally, provide additional comments (page 9) if needed.
8. Enter your contact email (page 10) and phone number (page 11) so the safety team can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Member** (`Staff Member`, Text, *): Enter the name and title of the staff member involved in the incident or concern.
* **Contact Method** (Contact Method, Select, *): Select 'Yes' if you would like to be contacted directly regarding the incident or concern, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Department** (Department, Text, ): Enter the department where the incident or concern occurred.
* **Location** (Location, Text, ): Enter the location where the incident or concern occurred.
* **Date** (Date, Date, *): Select the date of the incident or concern, if applicable.
* **Time** (Time, Time, *): Select the time of the incident or concern, if applicable.
* **Safety Procedure** (Safety Procedure, Text, ): Enter any safety procedures that could help prevent similar incidents.
* **Safety Improvement** (Safety Improvement, Text, ): Enter any safety improvements that could help prevent similar incidents.
* **Comment** (Comment, Note, ): Provide any additional comments or notes about the incident or concern.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, Email, *): Enter your contact email address so the safety team can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, Text, ): Enter your contact phone number so the safety team can get in touch with you.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you're filling in all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please reach out to the safety team directly.
