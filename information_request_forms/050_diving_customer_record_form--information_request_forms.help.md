# Diving Customer Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diving Customer Record Form is used to collect and update detailed information from customers about their diving experiences, ensuring accurate and reliable information for administrative and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. For fields that require selection, choose from the provided options that best match your information.
3. Ensure all fields are filled in as thoroughly and accurately as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Customer Name
* **Customer Name**: Please fill in the full name of the customer.
* **Required**: Yes

### Date of Diving Experience
* **Date of Diving Experience**: Please enter the date of the customer's diving experience in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Required**: Yes

### Instructor Name
* **Instructor Name**: Please fill in the name of the instructor or instructor who conducted the dive.
* **Required**: Yes

### Diving Type
* **Diving Type**: Select the type of diving experience from the options provided:
	+ Scuba Diving
	+ Snorkeling
* **Required**: Yes

### Diving Location
* **Diving Location**: Please fill in the location of the dive.
* **Required**: Yes

### Certification Level
* **Certification Level**: Select from the options provided:
	+ none
	+ Open Water Diver
	+ Advanced Open Water Diver
	+ Rescue Diver
	+ Dive Master
* **Required**: Yes

### Number of Dives
* **Number of Dives**: Please fill in the number of dives completed.
* **Required**: Yes

### Hours Dived
* **Hours Dived**: Please fill in the total hours of diving experience.
* **Required**: Yes

### Certification Date
* **Certification Date**: Please enter the date of certification in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Required**: Yes

### Medical Clearance
* **Medical Clearance**: Select from the options provided:
	+ none
	+ True
	+ False
* **Required**: Yes

### Medical Notes
* **Medical Notes**: Please fill in any medical-related notes or concerns.
* **Optional**: No (though recommended for completeness)

### Customer Signature
* **Customer Signature**: Please sign to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Required**: Yes

### Instructor Signature
* **Instructor Signature**: Please sign as the instructor conducting the dive.
* **Required**: Yes

### Customer Comments
* **Customer Comments**: Please add any additional comments or notes.
* **Optional**: No

### Instructor Comments
* **Instructor Comments**: Please add any additional comments or notes as the instructor.
* **Optional**: No

### Dive Number
* **Dive Number**: Please fill in the number of the dive.
* **Required**: Yes

### Dive Depth
* **Dive Depth**: Please fill in the depth of the dive.
* **Required**: Yes

### Dive Duration
* **Dive Duration**: Please fill in the duration of the dive.
* **Required**: Yes

### Diving Date
* **Diving Date**: Please enter the date of the dive in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Required**: Yes

### Diving Time
* **Diving Time**: Please fill in the time of the dive.
* **Required**: Yes

### Diving Location
* **Diving Location**: Please fill in the location of the dive.
* **Required**: Yes

### Notes
* **Notes**: Please fill in any additional comments or notes.
* **Optional**: No 

This guide is designed to be clear and concise, allowing users to quickly understand the purpose and functionality of the form. It's essential to fill in the required fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure data consistency and quality.
