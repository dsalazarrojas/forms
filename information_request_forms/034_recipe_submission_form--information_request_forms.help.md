# Recipe Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Recipe Submission Form is designed to collect user-generated recipes for a platform focused on food or cooking. The form asks for essential information about the recipe, including name, description, cooking time, and cooking temperature, as well as optional attachment of a file (e.g., image or video).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Recipe Submission Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a **Recipe Name**, which will be displayed as the title of the recipe in the public list.
2. Provide a **Description** of the recipe, including any relevant details or notes.
3. Enter the **Cooking Time** in minutes, which will help users estimate the preparation and cooking time.
4. Specify the **Cooking Temperature**, which will help users understand the ideal cooking temperature.
5. List all the **Ingredients** used in the recipe, separated by commas or in a list.
6. Describe the **Cooking Method**, including any steps or techniques.
7. Choose relevant **Tags** for the recipe, which will help users categorize and find similar recipes.
8. Enter your **Name and Contact Information**, including your name, email address, and phone number, so we can contact you for any further questions or clarifications.
9. If desired, attach a **File** (image or video) to the recipe to provide additional context or visuals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recipe Name** (required, text): Enter a unique and descriptive name for your recipe.
* **Description** (required, text): Provide a detailed description of the recipe, including any relevant notes or details.
* **Cooking Time** (required, number): Enter the estimated time it takes to cook this recipe in minutes.
* **Cooking Temperature** (required, number): Specify the ideal cooking temperature for this recipe.
* **Ingredients** (required, text): List all the ingredients used in this recipe, separated by commas or in a list.
* **Cooking Method** (required, text): Describe the steps or techniques used to prepare and cook this recipe.
* **Tags** (required, select_one): Choose relevant tags for the recipe, which will help users categorize and find similar recipes.
* **Name and Contact Information** (required, note): Enter your name, email address, and phone number for any further questions or clarifications.
* **Email** (required, email): Enter your email address for any further questions or clarifications.
* **Phone** (required, text): Enter your phone number for any further questions or clarifications.
* **File** (optional, text): Attach a file (e.g., image or video) to provide additional context or visuals.
* **Notes** (optional, text): Enter any additional notes or comments about the recipe.
