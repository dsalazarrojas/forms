# Event Information Sheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect event-related information to help you keep track of upcoming events, meetings, or activities. Please use this form to provide details about the event, including its title, date, time, location, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Title** field with a brief and descriptive title of the event.
2. Enter the **Event Date** in the format of mm/dd/yyyy or a specific date range (e.g., 01/01/2024 - 01/31/2024).
3. Enter the **Event Time**, if applicable, in the 12-hour clock format (e.g., 02:00 AM or 02:00 PM).
4. In the **Event Description** field, provide a brief summary of the event, including its purpose and any relevant details.
5. Specify the **Event Location**, where the event will take place.
6. Enter the **Event Organizer**'s name or contact information, if applicable.
7. Finally, provide the **Event Contact** information, including their name and contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Title** (`event_information_sheet_1`, text, required: false): Enter a descriptive title of the event, such as a conference, meeting, or workshop.
- **Event Date** (`event_information_sheet_2`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event in the format of mm/dd/yyyy or a specific date range (e.g., 01/01/2024 - 01/31/2024).
- **Event Time** (`event_information_sheet_3`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the event, if applicable, in the 12-hour clock format (e.g., 02:00 AM or 02:00 PM).
- **Event Description** (`event_information_sheet_4`, text, required: false): Provide a brief summary of the event, including its purpose and any relevant details.
- **Event Location** (`event_information_sheet_5`, text, required: false): Specify the location where the event will take place.
- **Event Organizer** (`event_information_sheet_6`, text, required: false): Enter the name or contact information of the event organizer.
- **Event Contact** (`event_information_sheet_7`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the event contact person.
