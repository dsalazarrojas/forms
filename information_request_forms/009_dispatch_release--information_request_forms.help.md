# Dispatch Release - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dispatch Release form is designed to provide a structured and standardized way to document the release of goods and services. It captures essential details necessary for the successful completion of the transaction, ensuring that all parties involved are informed and on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2.  Provide accurate contact information, including the business name, contact email, and contact phone number.
3.  List and describe the goods and services being released.
4.  Enter the value of the goods and services and the currency used.
5.  Specify the release date and dispatch release date if applicable.
6.  Choose the authorized signatory from the options provided.
7.  Sign and date the form with the authorized signature.
8.  Ensure that the timestamp and submit date are correctly filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the business or organization releasing the goods and services.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person or department.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person or department.
*   **Goods and Services** (`goods_services_list`, `text`, optional): List the goods or services being released.
*   **Goods and Services Details** (`goods_services_details`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the goods and services.
*   **Value of Goods and Services** (`goods_services_value`, `number`, optional): Enter the value of the goods and services.
*   **Release Date** (`release_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the release.
*   **Signed By** (`signed_by`, `text`, optional): The person signing for the business or organization.
*   **Business Signature** (`business_signature`, `note`, optional): The signature of the business or organization.
*   **Dispatch Release Date** (`dispatch_release_date`, `date`, optional): The date the dispatch is released.
*   **Authorized By** (`authorized_by`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the authorized signatory from the options provided.
*   **Dispatch Release Number** (`dispatch_release_number`, `text`, optional): A unique identifier for the dispatch release.
*   **Description of Goods and Services** (`goods_services_list_description`, `text`, optional): A brief description of the goods and services being released.
*   **Value in Currency** (`goods_services_value_in_currency`, `text`, optional): The value of the goods and services in the chosen currency.
*   **Currency** (`currency`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the currency used for the goods and services.
*   **Other Currency (Specify)** (`other_currency`, `text`, optional): If the currency is not USD, specify the other currency used.
*   **Dispatch Release Note** (`dispatch_release_note`, `text`, optional): Additional comments or notes regarding the dispatch release.
*   **Authorized Signature** (`authorized_signature`, `note`, optional): The authorized signature for the dispatch release.
*   **Timestamp** (`timestamp`, `time`, optional): The system timestamp of submission.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, optional): The person or department submitting the form.
*   **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`, `email`, optional): The email address of the submitter.
*   **Submitted By Phone** (`submitted_by_phone`, `text`, optional): The phone number of the submitter.
*   **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, `date`, optional): The date the form is submitted.
*   **Submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, optional): The name of the person or department submitting the form.

**Tips**:

*   Make sure to double-check the accuracy of all provided information.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
*   Use the provided options for currencies and authorized signatories to avoid errors.
*   Use the dispatch release number for tracking purposes.
*   Keep the dispatch release note section for any additional comments or notes.
*   Always sign and date the form with the authorized signature for authentication and accountability.
