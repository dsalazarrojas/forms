# Accessibility Resource Center Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accessibility Resource Center Contact Form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations seeking accessibility resources or support. This form helps us understand their needs and provide tailored assistance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Select your preferred name to be called.
3. Indicate how you are connected to accessibility needs.
4. Specify your primary disability category, if applicable.
5. Choose the type of information you are seeking.
6. Describe your specific accessibility need or challenge.
7. Select the environments where you need accessibility support.
8. Indicate the urgency level of your request.
9. If you have contacted other resources before, please specify.
10. Share any accommodation history, if applicable.
11. Choose how you prefer to receive information.
12. Select your preferred language for communication.
13. If you need an interpreter, please specify.
14. Consent to sharing your information with relevant partners.
15. Indicate specific confidentiality requirements, if any.
16. Add any additional comments that would help us assist you.
17. Grant follow-up permission, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1): Enter your full, legal name.
* **Preferred Name** (2): Enter a name you prefer to be called.
* **Email Address** (3): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (4): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Alternate Contact Method** (5): If you don't have a phone or email, specify how you would like to be contacted (e.g., mail, in-person meeting).
* **Relationship to Accessibility** (6): Select how you are connected to accessibility needs (e.g., person with a disability, family member, caregiver).
* **Primary Disability Category** (7): Select the category that best describes your primary accessibility concern (e.g., mobility, vision, hearing).
* **Inquiry Type** (8): Choose the type of information you are seeking (e.g., assistive technology, accommodation strategies).
* **Specific Accessibility Need** (9): Describe your specific accessibility need or challenge.
* **Environment Context** (10): Select the environments where you need accessibility support (e.g., workplace, educational institution, public space).
* **Urgency Level** (11): Indicate the urgency level of your request (e.g., immediate attention needed, within a week).
* **Previous Resources Contacted** (12): If you have contacted other resources before, specify.
* **Accommodation History** (13): Share any accommodation history, if applicable.
* **Preferred Format Response** (14): Choose how you prefer to receive information (e.g., email, phone call, video call).
* **Language Preference** (15): Select your preferred language for communication.
* **Interpreter Needed** (16): If you need an interpreter, specify.
* **Consent to Share Information** (17): Grant consent to sharing your information with relevant partners.
* **Confidentiality Requirements** (18): Indicate specific confidentiality requirements, if any.
* **Additional Comments** (19): Share any additional comments that would help us assist you.
* **Follow Up Permission** (20): Grant follow-up permission, if applicable.
