# Babysitter Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for new babysitters to provide their information and qualifications to be listed on the platform. It's essential to fill it out accurately to increase the chances of getting hired.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **Full Name**, which is your legal name.
2. Provide your **Email Address**, which should be a valid primary contact email.
3. Enter your **Phone Number**, which should be a contact number for potential clients.
4. Fill in your **Home Address**, which should be your full street address.
5. Enter your **Date of Birth**, following the MM/DD/YYYY format.
6. Choose your **Years of Babysitting Experience**:
	* Less than 1 year
	* 1 to 2 years
	* 2 to 4 years
	* 4 to 6 years
	* Over 6 years
7. Select all **Age Groups You Can Care For** (check all that apply):
	* Infants, 0 to 12 months
	* Toddlers, 1 to 3 years
	* Preschool, 3 to 5 years
	* School age, 6 to 12 years
	* Teenagers, 13 plus years
8. Enter your **Maximum Number of Children** you can care for at once.
9. Choose your **Certifications Held** (check all that apply):
	* CPR Certified
	* First Aid Certified
	* Babysitting Course
	* Child Development
	* Lifeguard
	* None
10. If applicable, list your **Certification Expiry Dates**.
11. Enter any **Special Skills or Abilities** you have, such as tutoring or cooking.
12. Select your **Weekday Availability**:
	* Monday
	* Tuesday
	* Wednesday
	* Thursday
	* Friday
13. Select your **Weekend Availability**:
	* Saturday
	* Sunday
14. Choose your **Available Hours**:
	* Mornings only
	* Afternoons only
	* Evenings only
	* Overnight
	* Flexible
15. Enter your **Expected Hourly Rate** in dollars.
16. Choose how you will **Transportation Method**:
	* Personal vehicle
	* Public transportation
	* Walk or bike
	* Need rides
17. Check if you have a **Valid Driver's License**.
18. Enter the **Number of References** you can provide.
19. List the **Reference Names and Contact Info**.
20. Check if you are willing to undergo a **Background Check**.
21. Optionally, share your **Why You Want to Babysit**.
22. Enter any **Additional Information** that clients should know.

Note: The form requires a valid driver's license and background check to be completed. Please answer honestly and accurately to ensure the best possible matches with clients.
