# Smart Security Partner Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Smart Security Partner Contact Form is a tool used to collect necessary information from potential security partners. This form helps us understand their capabilities, and it also helps us follow up on potential partnership opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your partner details in the "Partner Details" field.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Details" field.
3. If applicable, provide additional security partner details in the "Security Partner Details" field.
4. Describe the submission you're making in the "Submission Details" field.
5. Add any additional information or notes about the submission in the "Submission Notes" field.
6. Check the "Submission Agreement" field to acknowledge that you have read and understood the submission guidelines.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Details (Required)**: <label>Enter your company's name, address, and other relevant details.</label>
* **Contact Details (Required)**: <label>Enter your contact person's name, email, and phone number.</label>
* **Security Partner Details (Optional)**: <label>Provide any additional security partner details that might be relevant to your submission.</label>
* **Submission Details (Required)**: <label>Describe the submission you're making, including any relevant information about the product, services, or solutions you offer.</label>
* **Submission Notes (Optional)**: <label>Enter any additional information or notes about your submission that you feel might be helpful to us.</label>
* **Submission Agreement (Optional)**: <label>This checkbox indicates that you have read and understood the submission guidelines. Please check this box to confirm that you have done so.</label>

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and completely.
* Please note that our processing time may take a few days, so please be patient and wait for our response.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
