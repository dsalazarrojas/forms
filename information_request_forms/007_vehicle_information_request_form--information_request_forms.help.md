# vehicle_information_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a vehicle, including its year, make, model, color, condition, engine size, number of doors, transmission, mileage, price, and purchaser's contact details. This form is used to gather essential details about a vehicle for various purposes, such as inventory management, sales, or maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the year of the vehicle from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose the make of the vehicle from the list of options.
3. Enter the model of the vehicle, if applicable.
4. Select the color of the vehicle from the list of options.
5. Choose the condition of the vehicle from the list of options (e.g., New, Used, Certified Pre-Owned).
6. Enter the engine size of the vehicle.
7. Choose the number of doors on the vehicle.
8. Select the type of transmission.
9. Enter the mileage on the vehicle.
10. Enter the price of the vehicle.
11. Enter the email address of the vehicle's purchaser.
12. Enter the phone number of the vehicle's purchaser.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`): Select the year of the vehicle from the dropdown menu.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`): Choose the make of the vehicle from the list of options.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`): Enter the model of the vehicle (if applicable).
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`): Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`): Choose the condition of the vehicle from the list of options.
* **Vehicle Engine Size** (`vehicle_engine_size`): Enter the engine size of the vehicle in a numerical value.
* **Vehicle Doors** (`vehicle_doors`): Enter the number of doors on the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Transmission** (`vehicle_transmission`): Choose the type of transmission.
* **Vehicle Mileage** (`vehicle_mileage`): Enter the mileage on the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Price** (`vehicle_price`): Enter the price of the vehicle in a numerical value.
* **Vehicle Purchaser Email** (`vehicle_purchaser_email`): Enter the email address of the vehicle's purchaser.
* **Vehicle Purchaser Phone** (`vehicle_purchaser_phone`): Enter the phone number of the vehicle's purchaser.
