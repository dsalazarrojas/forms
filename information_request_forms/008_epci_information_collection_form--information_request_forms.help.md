# EPCI Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EPCI Information Collection Form is used to collect information about EPCI (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction and Installation) related activities, such as names, emails, phones, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as **required**) with your relevant information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form once you have filled in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **EPCI Information** (`epci_information`, `text`, required): Please enter a brief description of the EPCI activity or project.
* **EPCI Name** (`epci_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the EPCI activity or project.
* **EPCI Email** (`epci_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address associated with the EPCI activity or project.
* **EPCI Phone** (`epci_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number associated with the EPCI activity or project.
* **EPCI Select** (`epci_select`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options provided, which could be "Yes" or "No" depending on the specific EPCI activity or project.
* **EPCI Select Multiple** (`epci_select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple options that apply to the EPCI activity or project.
* **EPCI Date** (`epci_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date associated with the EPCI activity or project.
* **EPCI Time** (`epci_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time associated with the EPCI activity or project.
* **EPCI Notes** (`epci_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the EPCI activity or project.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* For EPCI Select and EPCI Select Multiple, select the options that best describe your EPCI activity or project.
* Use the notes field to provide any additional context or information that doesn't fit in other fields.
