# Tattoo Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tattoo Inquiry Form is designed for individuals to gather information about potential tattoo services, allowing the studio to understand their needs and preferences better. This guide will help you complete the form efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all required fields filled out. The `email` and `phone_number` fields are required to ensure we can contact you for further information or to confirm appointments.
2. Select a suitable tattoo style from the provided options in the `tattoo_style` field.
3. Choose the size of your desired tattoo from the options in the `tattoo_size` field.
4. If you have any allergies or medical conditions that may impact the tattooing process, please select them in the `tattoo_inquiries` field.
5. For next steps, select the dates and times that work best for you in the `next_steps` field.
6. If you'd like to schedule a studio visit, select the time that works best for you in the `studio_visit` field.
7. Lastly, confirm whether you'd like to schedule an appointment by selecting the `appointment` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you.
* **Tattoo Style** (`tattoo_style`, select_one, required): Choose from the provided styles to best match your needs.
* **Tattoo Size** (`tattoo_size`, select_multiple, optional): Select the size of your desired tattoo.
* **Tattoo Theme** (`tattoo_theme`, text, optional): Describe the theme or style of your desired tattoo.
* **Tattoo Description** (`tattoo_description`, text, optional): Share a brief description of your desired tattoo design.
* **Tattoo Inspirations** (`tattoo_inspirations`, text, optional): Share any inspirations or ideas behind your desired tattoo.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, note): Share any additional contact information for your studio visit.
* **Tattoo Inquiries** (`tattoo_inquiries`, select_multiple, optional): Select from the options if you have any allergies, sensitivities, or medical conditions.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, date, optional): Schedule a follow-up appointment or next step in the process.
* **Studio Visit** (`studio_visit`, time, optional): Schedule a studio visit at a desired time.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, text, optional): Request a follow-up on your inquiry.
* **Appointment** (`appointment`, select_one, optional): Confirm whether you'd like to schedule an appointment.
* **Tattoo Studio** (`tattoo_studio`, text, optional): Share any feedback or comments about the studio.
