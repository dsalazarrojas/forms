# Textbook Adoption Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request textbook adoptions for upcoming classes. The form is designed to gather information about the textbook adoptions, including the department name, bookstore name, and ISBN number. The form also allows users to provide additional information such as the title and author of the textbook, as well as any additional notes or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Enter the department name where the textbook will be used.
3. Enter the name of the bookstore where the textbook will be purchased.
4. Enter the ISBN number of the textbook.
5. Enter the title of the textbook.
6. Enter the author of the textbook.
7. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
8. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
9. Enter any additional information about the textbook.
10. Select the date and time when you would like to place the order.
11. Select the status of the order (pending, completed, or cancelled).
12. Enter any notes or comments about the order.
13. Click on the "submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Textbook Adoption Form** (Text, Optional): This field is optional and is used to provide a title for the form submission.
* **name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name.
* **department name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the department where the textbook will be used.
* **bookstore name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the bookstore where the textbook will be purchased.
* **isbn** (Text, Optional): Enter the ISBN number of the textbook.
* **title** (Text, Optional): Enter the title of the textbook.
* **author** (Text, Optional): Enter the author of the textbook.
* **email** (Text, Optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone number** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **textbook information** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about the textbook.
* **order date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date when you would like to place the order.
* **order time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time when you would like to place the order.
* **order status** (Select, Optional): Select the status of the order (pending, completed, or cancelled).
* **notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any notes or comments about the order.
* **submit** (Text, Required): Click on this button to submit the form.
