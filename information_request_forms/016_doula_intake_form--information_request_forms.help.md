# Doula Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Doula Intake Form is designed to collect information from expectant mothers who are seeking doula services. This form helps the doula understand the mother's medical history, pregnancy information, and other relevant details to provide personalized care and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accurately.
2. For each field, provide the required information and any additional details as requested.
3. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name (e.g., Jane).
* **Last Name**: Your last name (e.g., Doe).
* **Birth Date**: The date of your birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Email**: Your contact email address.
* **Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Address**: Your current address.
* **City**: The city where you reside.
* **State**: Select 'Yes' if you have any medical history or conditions that may impact your pregnancy or birth (e.g., high blood pressure).
* **Zip**: Your postal zip code.
* **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions or allergies you have that may impact your pregnancy or birth.
* **Pregnancy Info**: Provide any relevant information about your pregnancy, such as gestational age or any complications.
* **Delivery Info**: Describe any concerns or preferences you have about your delivery, such as pain management options.
* **Insurance Info**: Provide any relevant information about your insurance coverage.
* **Doula Info**: Describe any specific needs or requests you have for your doula support and care.

## Tips
* Please answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about a question, ask your doula for guidance.
* Review your answers for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

Note: This guide is intended to be a general guide and not a substitute for personalized advice from a healthcare professional. If you have any concerns or questions, please consult your healthcare provider or doula for guidance.
