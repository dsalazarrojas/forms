# Testimonial Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Testimonial Request Form is designed to gather user feedback and testimonials about our services. It's used to understand how we've met or exceeded your expectations, what we could improve on, and to share your positive experiences with others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **Read each question carefully**: Review each field below and make sure you're answering the questions that best fit your experience.
2. **Provide honest answers**: Answer honestly and to the best of your abilities.
3. **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Testimonial
* **Testimonial**: Please share your thoughts about our services. This is a text field where you can write as much as you'd like. It could be a sentence, a paragraph, or even a short story.

### Rate Services
* **Rate Services**: Let us know how we've performed compared to your expectations. You can rate us on a scale from 1 to 10.

### Give Consent
* **Give Consent**: We respect your time and experience with us. By giving us consent, you're allowing us to share your testimonial with others, such as on our website or social media channels. Select 'Yes' if you're comfortable with this.
