# Enablement Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enablement Partner Contact Form is a tool used to collect contact information from partners who are interested in learning about enablement opportunities. This form helps to facilitate communication and provide support to these partners.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "First Page" tab.
2. Enter your "Partner" name.
3. Choose your "Department" (optional).
4. Choose your "Role" (optional).
5. Enter your "Email" address.
6. Enter your "Phone" number (optional).
7. Enter your "First Name" (optional).
8. Enter your "Last Name" (optional).
9. Enter your "Company" name (optional).
10. In the "Message" field, provide any additional information or details about your enablement needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner** (text): Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Department** (text): If applicable, enter the department within your organization that would be responsible for enablement.
* **Role** (select_one): Choose 'Yes' if you are an enablement partner, or 'No' if not.
* **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (text): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number here.
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name (optional).
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name (optional).
* **Company** (text): If applicable, enter the name of your company.
* **Message** (text): In this field, provide any additional information or details about your enablement needs.

## Tips

* Please be sure to enter a valid email address for your "Email" field.
* If you are unsure about your "Role", you can choose 'No' and provide more information in the "Message" field.
* If you have any questions or need clarification about the form, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
