# ESG Reporting Framework Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user and client information, as well as ESG data, for analysis and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information in the "User Info" field.
2. Enter your client information in the "Client Info" field.
3. Enter your ESG information in the "ESG Info" field.
4. Add any additional comments or information in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (required/optional): This field is used to collect your user information.
* **Client Info** (required/optional): This field is used to collect your client information.
* **ESG Info** (required/optional): This field is used to collect your ESG information.
* **Additional Notes** (required/optional): This field is used to provide any additional comments or information that will help in our analysis and reporting.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all the required fields before submitting the form.
* Review your submission carefully before hitting the submit button to avoid errors.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
