# Government Records Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Records Request Form is used by citizens to request access to government records. This form helps the government agency to gather information about the nature of the request, the contact information of the person making the request, and the specific records being requested.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter a brief description of the nature of your request in the "What is the nature of this request" field.
2. Provide your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is the best way for us to contact you regarding your request.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. If applicable, select the type of records you are requesting (public, confidential, or restricted) in the "Type of Records" field.
7. Enter the location of the records you are requesting in the "Records Location" field.
8. Finally, enter any additional comments or messages you have in the "Additional Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the nature of this request**: This field is where you describe the purpose or reason for your request. Please keep it brief.
* **Name**: This is your name as it appears on your identification or as you prefer it to be displayed.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address so we can contact you regarding your request.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you to discuss or clarify any details of your request.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address or the address of the organization you represent.
* **Request Date**: If you know the date of the records you are requesting, please enter it here. If not, leave it blank.
* **Type of Records**: Select the type of records you are requesting from the available options: Public, Confidential, or Restricted. This helps us ensure that we handle your request accordingly.
* **Records Location**: Enter the location of the records you are requesting. This could be a specific department, agency, or building.
* **Additional Message**: Any additional comments or information about your request are welcome here.
 
**Tips**

* Please make sure to enter accurate information in all fields to avoid delays or errors in processing your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will respond to your request as soon as possible, but it may take some time for the records you are requesting to be processed and delivered to you.
