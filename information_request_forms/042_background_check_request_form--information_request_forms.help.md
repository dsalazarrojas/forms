# Background Check Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Background Check Request Form is used to request background checks for candidate applicants, ensuring that they meet your company's hiring standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the department or division that is requesting the background check.
2.  Enter the name of the requesting manager or HR representative.
3.  Enter the full name of the candidate being requested.
4.  Enter the candidate's email address to which the authorization link will be sent.
5.  Enter the title of the position for which the candidate is being considered.
6.  Select the level of screening required based on your company's policies.
7.  Choose the request priority level to indicate the urgency of the request (Standard or Urgent).
8.  Enter the billing/cost center code to track expenses.
9.  Provide any special instructions or concerns related to the background check (optional).
10.  Confirm that a job offer has been made or is pending for the candidate.
11.  Enter the date of the request.
12.  Review and submit the form to complete the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Requesting Manager/HR Name** (`hiring_manager_name`): Enter the name of the person requesting the background check.
*   **Department/Division** (`department_requesting`): Enter the department or division requesting the background check.
*   **Candidate Full Name** (`candidate_full_name_req`): Enter the full name of the candidate being requested.
*   **Candidate Email Address** (`candidate_email_address_req`): Enter the candidate's email address to which the authorization link will be sent.
*   **Position Title** (`position_title_req`): Enter the title of the position for which the candidate is being considered.
*   **Level of Screening Required** (`level_of_screening_required`): Select the level of screening required based on your company's policies.
	+ Standard (Criminal + Employment)
	+ Professional (Standard + Education)
	+ Executive (Professional + Credit + Media)
	+ Driving Position (Standard + MVR)
	+ Healthcare (Standard + FACIS)
*   **Request Priority** (`request_priority`): Choose the priority level of the request (Standard or Urgent) to indicate the urgency of the background check.
*   **Billing/Cost Center Code** (`billing_account_code`): Enter the billing/cost center code to track expenses.
*   **Special Instructions or Specific Concerns** (`special_instructions_screening`): Enter any special instructions or concerns related to the background check.
*   **I confirm that a job offer has been made or is pending** (`manager_confirmation_req`): Confirm that a job offer has been made or is pending for the candidate.
*   **Date of Request** (`date_of_request_req`): Enter the date of the background check request.
