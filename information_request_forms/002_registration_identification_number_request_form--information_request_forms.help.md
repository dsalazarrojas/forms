# Registration Identification Number Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Registration Identification Number Request Form is used to collect information from users for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your details in the form below:
	* User Name: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* Email: Enter your email address.
	* Phone: Enter your phone number (optional).
2. Select the Category that best describes your request:
	* Choose the category that best fits your registration purpose.
3. Fill in the remaining fields with your organization and school information (if applicable):
	* Organization: Enter the name of your organization (if applicable).
	* Agency: Enter the name of your agency (if applicable).
	* School: Enter the name of your school (if applicable).
4. Click the "Submit" button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name (text)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address for verification purposes.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
* **Registration ID (text)**: Enter a unique identifier for your registration (e.g., student ID).
* **Category (select_one)**: Choose the category that best fits your registration purpose.
* **Submitter (text)**: Enter your name as it appears on your submission.
* **Agency (text)**: Enter the name of your agency (if applicable).
* **School (text)**: Enter the name of your school (if applicable).
* **Program (text)**: Enter the name of your program (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information for errors before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the form administrator for assistance.
