# Campaign Details Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Campaign Details Form is a critical tool used to gather and document campaign information for internal review and evaluation. It ensures that all necessary details are collected accurately and thoroughly, enabling efficient campaign management and effective resource allocation. This form is a requirement for every campaign, and its completion is essential for successful project initiation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
2.  Enter your campaign details as accurately as possible.
3.  Select all relevant donor information required for tracking.
4.  Enter your campaign manager's contact information for easy communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Organization Name** (`charity_organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
*   **Official Campaign Title** (`campaign_title_full`, text, required): A concise title that describes your campaign.
*   **Primary Purpose of Campaign** (`campaign_purpose_summary`, text, required): Briefly describe the main objective of your campaign.
*   **Total Funding Goal (USD)** (`funding_goal_amount`, number, required): Enter the target amount to be raised.
*   **Target Start Date** (`campaign_start_date_target`, date, required): Select the starting date of your campaign.
*   **Target End Date** (`campaign_end_date_target`, date, required): Select the ending date of your campaign.
*   **Expected Number of Donors** (`expected_donor_count_est`, number, optional): Estimated number of donors you expect to engage.
*   **Donor Information Required for Tracking** (`donor_info_req_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all donor information required for tracking.
*   **Previous Fundraising History** (`financial_history_summary`, text, optional): Briefly summarize your past fundraising history.
*   **Campaign Manager Name** (`campaign_manager_contact`, text, required): Enter your campaign manager's name.
*   **Manager Email Address** (`campaign_manager_email`, email, required): Enter your campaign manager's email address.
*   **Regulatory Compliance Reporting Needed?** (`compliance_reporting_need`, select_one, required): Indicate if regulatory compliance reporting is needed.
*   **Additional Context or Notes** (`additional_campaign_context`, text, optional): Provide any additional context or notes about your campaign.
*   **Date of Information Submission** (`campaign_details_date_logged`, date, required): Date and time of submission.

## Tips

*   Always ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
*   Be concise and clear in your answers to avoid any confusion.
*   Double-check your information before submitting the form.
*   If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for assistance.
