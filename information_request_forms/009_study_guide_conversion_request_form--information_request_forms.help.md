# Study Guide Conversion Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Study Guide Conversion Request Form is designed to capture information from the submitter to facilitate the conversion of a study guide from one format to another. This includes capturing the submitter's details, the study guide information, and the requested format for the conversion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and details ready before starting.
2. Fill out the User Information Form with your name, email, department, and preferred format (e.g., Epub, Pdf, Word).
3. Provide any additional comments or requests in the Request Comments field.
4. If you need to, fill out the Review Requester's information for context and follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Information Form
* **User Information Form** (`user_information_form`, text, optional): This field is optional, but it helps us understand who is submitting the request and provides a point of contact.
* **Study Guide Info** (`study_guide_info`, text, optional): This field captures any additional information about the study guide being converted.
* **Preferred Format** (`preferred_format`, select_one, optional): Choose the format in which you want the study guide to be converted (Epub, Pdf, Word).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field captures the submitter's email address.
* **Request Comments** (`request_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or requests for the conversion.
* **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`, text, optional): This field is optional and is meant to capture any additional information about the submitter.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): This field captures the submitter's name.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): This field captures the submitter's email address.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the department of the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Capture the submitter's phone number.
* **Submitter Position** (`submitter_position`, text, optional): This field captures the submitter's job title or position.
* **Submitter Title** (`submitter_title`, text, optional): This field captures the submitter's title or designation.
* **Submitter Affiliation** (`submitter_affiliation`, text, optional): This field captures the submitter's affiliation or institution.
* **Submitter Note** (`submitter_note`, text, optional): This field captures any additional comments or notes about the submitter.
* **Submit Date** (`submitter_request_date`, date, optional): This field captures the date the request was submitted.
* **Submit Time** (`submitter_request_time`, time, optional): This field captures the time the request was submitted.
* **Submit Type** (`submitter_request_type`, text, optional): This field captures the type of request.
* **Submit Details** (`submitter_request_details`, text, optional): This field captures any additional details about the submission.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, text, optional): This field captures any additional comments from the submitter.
* **Review Requester Information** (`review_requester_info`, text, optional): This field is optional and captures any additional information about the review requester.
* **Review Requester Name** (`review_requester_name`, text, optional): This field captures the review requester's name.
* **Review Requester Email** (`review_requester_email`, email, optional): This field captures the review requester's email address.
* **Review Requester Department** (`review_requester_department`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the department of the review requester.
* **Review Requester Phone** (`review_requester_phone`, text, optional): Capture the review requester's phone number.
* **Review Requester Note** (`review_requester_note`, text, optional): This field captures any additional comments or notes about the review requester.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill out all the required fields to avoid any delays in processing.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the support team for assistance.
* Please ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
* The review requester's information is not always required, but it is recommended to be filled in for context and follow-up.
* The submitter's information is also not always required, but it is recommended to be filled in to provide context to the reviewer or converter.
