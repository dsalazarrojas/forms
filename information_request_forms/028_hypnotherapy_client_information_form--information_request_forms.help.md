# Hypnotherapy Client Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients about their personal details, contact methods, and session specifics. It's crucial to provide correct and complete information to help our hypnotherapists tailor their services to your unique needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring that you provide all required information.
2. Review each field to ensure that it's relevant to your session or needs.
3. Use the provided options for fields like "Contact Method" and "Preferred Contact Method" to select the most suitable choices.
4. If you're unsure about any field, please ask for assistance from the hypnotherapist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Type your physical address, including street address or postal code.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for communication.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select the methods you are comfortable with (e.g., phone, email, text).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred method for communication (e.g., phone, email, text).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your needs or concerns.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Please enter the assigned tool or equipment for your session.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting this form.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): This is an auto-generated unique identifier.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional): This indicates when the form was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, optional): This indicates when the form was last updated.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, optional): This indicates when the form was submitted.
* **Client Number** (`client_number`, number, optional): Enter your unique client number.
* **Practitioner ID** (`practitioner_id`, number, optional): Enter your practitioner's ID.
* **Practitioner Name** (`practitioner_name`, text, optional): Enter your practitioner's name.
* **Session ID** (`session_id`, number, optional): Enter your session ID.
* **Session Number** (`session_number`, number, optional): Enter the number of your current session.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of your current session.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your current session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your current session.
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, note, optional): Add notes about your current session.
* **Next Session** (`next_session`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of your next session.

This help guide should provide you with the necessary information to complete the form accurately and effectively. If you have any further questions, please don't hesitate to ask your hypnotherapist for guidance.
