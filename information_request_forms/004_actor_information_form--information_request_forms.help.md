# Actor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Actor Information Form is used to collect essential details about an actor's identity, physical attributes, professional affiliation, and skills. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your information is up-to-date and correct.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Enter your legal name as it appears on official documents.
3. If different from your legal name, enter your stage name.
4. Enter your email address, which will be used as your contact information.
5. Provide your primary contact phone number.
6. Enter your date of birth for legal or work permit purposes.
7. Select your height in centimeters or feet/inches.
8. Choose your eye color.
9. Select your current hair color.
10. Enter any standard clothing sizes you may have (optional).
11. Check if you have a union affiliation or select other options.
12. List any special skills you possess (optional).
13. Mention any languages you speak and indicate your fluency level (optional).
14. If applicable, provide a link to your online resume or portfolio.
15. Indicate if you have current headshots available, or if they are scheduled for update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Legal Name** (`legal_name`, required): Your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Stage Name** (`stage_name`, optional): The name used for performances or public appearances.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): The contact email address for communication and notifications.
* **Primary Contact Number** (`contact_phone`, required): Your primary phone number for communication.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): The date of your birth for legal or work permit purposes.
* **Height (in cm or ft/in)** (`height_cm`, required): Enter your height in centimeters or feet/inches.
* **Eye Color** (`eye_color`, required): Select your natural eye color.
* **Current Hair Color** (`hair_color`, required): Select your current hair color.
* **Standard Clothing Sizes** (`clothing_sizes`, optional): List any standard clothing sizes you may have, e.g., shirt, pants, shoes.
* **Union Affiliation** (`union_status`, required): Select your union affiliation or other options.
* **Special Skills** (`special_skills`, optional): Mention any special skills you possess, e.g., accents, martial arts, musical instruments, sports.
* **Languages Spoken** (`languages_spoken`, optional): Indicate any languages you speak and your fluency level.
* **Online Resume / Portfolio Link** (`resume_link`, optional): Provide a link to your online resume or portfolio.
* **Do you have current headshots available?** (`headshot_confirmation`, required): Indicate if you have current headshots available or if they are scheduled for update.

This guide will help you fill out the Actor Information Form accurately and correctly. Please ensure you have all necessary information and follow the instructions carefully.
