# Livestock Monitoring System Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Livestock Monitoring System Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from users who have questions or need assistance with their livestock monitoring systems. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide your contact information and specify your products of interest, inquiry type, and estimated need to receive help and support from our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Specify the name of your farm and its address, city, state, and country.
3. Select the products you are interested in learning more about.
4. Choose the type of inquiry you would like to make (e.g., general information, product support, etc.).
5. Estimate the time and resources you need for the inquiry.
6. Select a follow-up preference to ensure we contact you at the right time.
7. Add any additional comments or notes.
8. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the contact and main page terms and conditions.
9. Finally, sign and date the form to indicate your understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_first_name`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact Last Name (`contact_last_name`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect your phone number. Please enter your phone number in the international format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Farm Name (`farm_name`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect the name of your farm or business.
* **Address (`farm_address`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect the physical address of your farm or business.
* **City (`farm_city`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect the city where your farm or business is located.
* **State (`farm_state`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect the state where your farm or business is located.
* **Country (`farm_country`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect the country where your farm or business is located.
* **Zip (`farm_zip`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect the zip code of your farm or business.
* **Products of Interest (`products_interest`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field allows you to select multiple products that interest you. Choose from the options provided.
* **Inquiry Type (`inquiry_type`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to specify the type of inquiry you would like to make (e.g., general information, product support, etc.).
* **Estimated Need (`estimated_need`, `number`, optional): This field is used to estimate the time and resources you need for the inquiry.
* **Inquiry Date and Time (`inquiry_date_time`, `date`, optional): This field is used to specify the date and time you would like to receive a response.
* **Follow-up Preference (`follow_up_preference`, `select_one`, optional): This field allows you to select a follow-up preference to ensure we contact you at the right time.
* **Additional Comments (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide any additional comments or notes about your inquiry.
* **Contact Confirmation (`contact_confirmation`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to confirm that you have read and agreed to the contact and main page terms and conditions.
* **Contact Terms & Conditions (`contact_terms_conditions`, `note`, optional): This field provides a note that explains the terms and conditions of the contact.
* **Contact Agreement (`contact_agreement`, `note`, optional): This field provides a note that explains the terms of agreement for the contact.
* **Main Page Terms & Conditions (`main_page_terms_conditions`, `note`, optional): This field provides a note that explains the terms and conditions of the main page.
* **Main Page Agreement (`main_page_agreement`, `note`, optional): This field provides a note that explains the terms of agreement for the main page.
* **Signature (`signature`, `note`, optional): This field is used to sign and date the form to indicate your understanding and agreement.
