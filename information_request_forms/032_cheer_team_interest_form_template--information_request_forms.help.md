# Cheer Team Interest Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cheer Team Interest Form is designed to help us understand your interests, skills, and experience as a prospective cheer athlete. This information will be used to assess your suitability for our program and to provide you with the most relevant information about our teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please enter the athlete's full name in the "Athlete Full Name" field.
2. Enter the athlete's age in the "Athlete Age" field.
3. Enter the athlete's current grade level in the "Current Grade" field.
4. Enter the guardian's name in the "Parent / Guardian Name" field.
5. Enter the guardian's email address in the "Email Address" field.
6. Enter the guardian's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7. Select the preferred contact method from the options in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
8. If the athlete has previous cheer or dance experience, enter a description in the "Previous Cheer or Dance Experience" field.
9. If the athlete has specific tumbling skills, enter a summary in the "Current Tumbling Skills" field.
10. In the "Why are you interested in our program" field, tell us why you are interested in joining our cheer team.
11. Select the team level the athlete is interested in from the options in the "Interested Team Level" field.
12. If the athlete has any questions for the coaching staff, enter them in the "Questions for Coaching Staff" field.
13. Finally, enter the date the interest was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Athlete Full Name**: Enter the athlete's full name, as it appears on official documents.
* **Athlete Age**: Enter the athlete's age in years.
* **Current Grade**: Enter the athlete's current grade level.
* **Parent / Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the athlete's parent or guardian.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the athlete's parent or guardian.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the athlete's parent or guardian.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select how you prefer to be contacted (Email, Phone Call, Text Message).
* **Previous Cheer or Dance Experience**: If the athlete has previous experience, enter a brief description.
* **Current Tumbling Skills**: Enter a summary of the athlete's current tumbling skills.
* **Why are you interested in our program**: Explain why you are interested in joining our cheer team.
* **Interested Team Level**: Select the team level the athlete is interested in (Tiny/Mini, Recreational, Competitive All-Star, High School Prep).
* **Questions for Coaching Staff**: If the athlete has any questions, enter them here.
* **Date of Interest**: Enter the date the interest was submitted.
