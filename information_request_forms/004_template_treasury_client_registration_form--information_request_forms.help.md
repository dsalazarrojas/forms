# Template Treasury Client Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Template Treasury Client Registration Form is a tool used to gather essential information from clients to establish a relationship with them. This form is designed to collect details such as name, contact information, and other identifying details to ensure accurate and efficient communication and service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address in the format `example@example.com`.
4. Enter your Phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
6. Enter your Address.
7. Enter your ZIP Code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name (Required: false)**: Enter your first name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name (Required: false)**: Enter your last name.

### Email
* **Email (Required: false)**: Enter a valid email address in the format `example@example.com`.

### Phone
* **Phone (Required: false)**: Enter your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth (Required: false)**: Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### Address
* **Address (Required: false)**: Enter your address.

### ZIP Code
* **ZIP Code (Required: false)**: Enter your ZIP code.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information before submitting.
* Ensure that the email address you provide is valid and up-to-date.
* Use the same format for phone numbers and dates to avoid any confusion.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to our support team.
