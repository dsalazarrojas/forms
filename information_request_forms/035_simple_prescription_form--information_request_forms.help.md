# simple_prescription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "simple_prescription_form" is a form designed to collect information for patient medication management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Medication Order** field (id: 1) with the medication that the patient is taking.
2. Enter the **Patient Name** (id: 2) in the text field provided.
3. Sign the **Doctor Signature** (id: 3) to authenticate the order.
4. Fill in the **Clinic Name** (id: 4) where the patient is visiting.
5. Enter the **Patient Phone** (id: 5) number for emergency contact.
6. Enter the **Patient Email** (id: 6) of the patient for communication.
7. Fill in the **Medication Dosage** (id: 7) in the numeric field.
8. Provide **Medication Instructions** (id: 8) in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medication Order** (id: 1): Enter the name of the medication that the patient is taking.
* **Patient Name** (id: 2): Enter the name of the patient.
* **Doctor Signature** (id: 3): Sign your name as the doctor authenticating the order.
* **Clinic Name** (id: 4): Enter the name of the clinic where the patient is visiting.
* **Patient Phone** (id: 5): Enter the phone number of the patient for emergency contact purposes.
* **Patient Email** (id: 6): Enter the email address of the patient for communication.
* **Medication Dosage** (id: 7): Enter the dosage amount of the medication.
* **Medication Instructions** (id: 8): Provide any special instructions for the medication.
