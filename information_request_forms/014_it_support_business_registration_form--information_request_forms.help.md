# it_support_business_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about your business, enabling us to provide tailored support and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your business name and contact person's name in the corresponding text fields.
2. Provide a valid email address for communication.
3. Enter your phone number for easy contact.
4. Describe your business in a few words in the 'Business Description' field.
5. Select the type of business you operate from the 'Business Type' dropdown menu.
6. Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether your business falls under a specific category.
7. List the services your business offers in the 'Services Offered' field.
8. Provide the website and social media links of your business.
9. Indicate whether your business operates during specific hours of the day or not.
10. Add any additional notes or comments in the 'Notes' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the official name of your business.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for your business.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your business phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your business address.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe your business in a few words.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, text, required/optional): Select the type of business you operate from the dropdown menu.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether your business falls under a specific category.
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, text, required/optional): List the services your business offers.
* **Website** (`website`, text, required/optional): Provide the website URL of your business.
* **Social Media Links** (`social_media_links`, text, required/optional): Enter the social media links of your business.
* **Business Hours** (`business_hours`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether your business operates during specific hours of the day or not.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes for our reference.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
* Once you have completed the form, you will be able to view and manage your business profile.
