# Real Estate Seller Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from real estate sellers, including their contact details, property details, and agency information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct seller type (Homeowner, Real Estate Agent, or Real Estate Company).
2. Enter the seller's name and email address.
3. Enter the seller's phone number.
4. Enter the seller's address.
5. Add any additional seller notes.
6. Choose the property type (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial).
7. Select the property status (For Sale, Rented, or Sold).
8. Choose the real estate agency from the list.
9. Enter the seller's license number.
10. Enter the agency's license number.
11. Enter the agency's address.
12. Enter the agency's phone number.
13. Enter the agency's email address.
14. Add any agency notes.
15. Enter the property's location.
16. Enter the agency's license date.
17. Enter the seller's license date.
18. Choose the agency status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seller Type** (optional): Choose the type of seller (Homeowner, Real Estate Agent, or Real Estate Company).
* **Seller Name** (text, optional): Enter the seller's name.
* **Seller Email** (email, optional): Enter the seller's email address.
* **Seller Phone** (text, optional): Enter the seller's phone number.
* **Seller Address** (text, optional): Enter the seller's address.
* **Seller Notes** (text, optional): Add any additional seller notes.
* **Property Type** (select_one, optional): Choose the type of property (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial).
* **Property Status** (select_one, optional): Choose the status of the property (For Sale, Rented, or Sold).
* **Real Estate Agency** (select_one, optional): Choose the real estate agency from the list.
* **Real Estate Agent** (text, optional): Enter the real estate agent's name.
* **Agency Address** (text, optional): Enter the agency's address.
* **Agency Phone** (text, optional): Enter the agency's phone number.
* **Agency Email** (email, optional): Enter the agency's email address.
* **Agency Notes** (text, optional): Add any agency notes.
* **Property Location** (text, optional): Enter the property's location.
* **Real Estate License** (text, optional): Enter the real estate agent's license number.
* **Seller License** (text, optional): Enter the seller's license number.
* **Agency License** (text, optional): Enter the agency's license number.
* **Agency License Date** (date, optional): Enter the agency's license date.
* **Seller License Date** (date, optional): Enter the seller's license date.
* **Agency Status** (text, optional): Choose the agency's status.
