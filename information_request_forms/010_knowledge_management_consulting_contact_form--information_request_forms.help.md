# knowledge_management_consulting_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request Knowledge Management Consulting services for guidance on knowledge management processes, tools, and best practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "User Input" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "User Email" field.
3. Enter a message describing your needs and requirements in the "User Message" field.
4. Select one or more users to be assigned to your request in the "Assigned User" field (this will be used for follow-up communication).
5. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "User Phone" field.
6. Optionally, include any additional comments or notes in the "User Notes" field.
7. Click "Submit" to send your form to the knowledge management team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (User Input, text, optional): Enter your name to let us know who you are.
* **User Email** (User Email, email, optional): Enter your work email address so we can contact you.
* **User Message** (User Message, text, optional): Describe your needs and requirements for knowledge management consulting.
* **Assigned User** (Assigned User, select multiple, optional): Select one or more users from the list who you would like to be assigned to your request.
* **User Phone** (User Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **User Notes** (User Notes, note, optional): Include any additional comments or notes about your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise information in each field to ensure a smooth process for our team.
* If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is for Knowledge Management Consulting services only. If you have other questions or requests, please use our main contact form.
