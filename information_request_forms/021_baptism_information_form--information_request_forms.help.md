# Baptism Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the baptism process. The form is divided into several sections that gather essential details about the candidate, the baptism process, and other important information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the date of submission for this form.
2. Enter the first name and last name of the candidate being baptized.
3. Provide the candidate's date of birth.
4. Enter the candidate's current age (if applicable).
5. Choose the type of baptism.
6. For infant baptisms, enter the parent or guardian's name.
7. Provide the parent or guardian's contact information (phone and email).
8. Select a preferred baptism date and time.
9. Choose a preferred location for the baptism.
10. Indicate the candidate's faith background.
11. Provide a personal faith statement (if applicable).
12. Indicate if prior religious instruction is needed (if applicable).
13. Confirm if a sponsor is required.
14. Enter the sponsor's name (if applicable).
15. Confirm the sponsor's faith status.
16. Provide the sponsor's contact information (if applicable).
17. Enter the candidate's church affiliation (if applicable).
18. Make any special requests or considerations (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of this Form** (`form_date`): Enter the current date to submit this form.
* **Baptism Candidate - First Name** (`candidate_first_name`): Enter the first name of the person being baptized.
* **Last Name** (`candidate_last_name`): Enter the last name of the candidate.
* **Date of Birth** (`candidate_date_of_birth`): Enter the candidate's date of birth.
* **Current Age** (`candidate_age`): Enter the candidate's current age (if applicable).
* **Type of Baptism** (`baptism_type`): Select the type of baptism you wish to perform: Infant, Adult, Re-baptism, Conditional, or Other.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`): Enter the parent or guardian's name for infant baptisms.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (`parent_phone`): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (`parent_email`): Enter the parent or guardian's email.
* **Preferred Baptism Date** (`preferred_baptism_date`): Select a preferred date for the baptism.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`): Select a preferred time for the baptism.
* **Preferred Baptism Location** (`baptism_location`): Choose a preferred location for the baptism: Church, Outdoor, River or Lake, Home, or Other.
* **Faith Background** (`faith_background`): Select the candidate's current faith status: No Prior Baptism, Baptized in Another Tradition, Seeking to Renew Faith, Recently Converted, or Other.
* **Personal Faith Statement** (`faith_statement`): Enter a brief statement of faith belief (if applicable).
* **Prior Religious Instruction** (`prior_religious_instruction`): Indicate if prior religious instruction is needed (if applicable).
* **Is a Sponsor Required?** (`sponsor_required`): Confirm if a sponsor is required.
* **Sponsor Name (Godparent)** (`sponsor_name`): Enter the sponsor's name (if applicable).
* **Sponsor Faith Status** (`sponsor_faith`): Confirm the sponsor's faith status.
* **Sponsor Contact Information** (`sponsor_contact`): Enter the sponsor's contact information (phone or email) (if applicable).
* **Church Affiliation** (`church_affiliation`): Enter the candidate's church affiliation (if applicable).
* **Special Requests or Considerations** (`special_requests`): Enter any specific needs or preferences (if applicable).

Note: Some fields are not required for specific cases, such as candidate's age for infant baptisms or sponsor contact information.
