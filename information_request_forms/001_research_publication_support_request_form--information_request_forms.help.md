# Research Publication Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Publication Support Request Form is designed to facilitate the submission of research publications for support. This form will be reviewed to ensure that all necessary information is provided, and that the submission is valid and complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all required information before starting the form.
2.  Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3.  Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manuscript Title**: Enter the title of the research manuscript you are requesting support for.
* **Manuscript Status**: Select the current status of your manuscript from the available options.
* **Manuscript Type**: Select all types that apply to your research manuscript.
* **Request For**: Enter a brief description of the support you are requesting.
* **Manuscript Details**: Enter a detailed description of your research manuscript.
* **Author**: Enter your name.
* **Manuscript File**: Enter the file name or identifier for your manuscript.
* **Publication Details**: Enter the details of the publication you are submitting.
* **Publication Date**: Enter the date of the publication.
* **Publication Status**: Select the status of your publication from the available options.
* **Research Group**: Enter the name of the research group associated with the manuscript.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Manuscript Status Update**: Enter any updates to the status of your manuscript.
* **Publication Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the publication.
* **Manuscript Revision**: Enter any manuscript revisions or updates.
* **Manuscript Format**: Enter the format of the manuscript (e.g. PDF, DOCX, etc.).
* **Submission Status**: Select the status of your submission from the available options.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of the submission.
* **Manuscript Revision Status**: Select the status of the revisions from the available options.
* **Research Group Status**: Select the status of the research group from the available options.
* **Submission Note**: Enter any notes or comments about the submission.
* **Submission Status Update**: Enter any updates to the submission status.
