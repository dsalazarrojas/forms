# Contact Information Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Contact Information Request Form is designed to collect contact information from patients or their representatives to facilitate communication and information exchange.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your contact information.
2. Choose the reason for your information request and your preferred contact method.
3. Select the best time for us to reach you (if applicable).
4. Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share.
5. Check the HIPAA consent box to indicate your understanding of the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, **required**): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Patient Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, **required**): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **required**): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Primary Phone Number** (`phone_primary`, text, **required**): Enter your primary phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Secondary Phone Number** (`phone_secondary`, text, **optional**): Enter an alternate phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Home Address** (`address`, text, **required**): Enter your street address that we can use to contact you.
* **City** (`city`, text, **required**): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, **required**): Enter the state where you reside.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip`, text, **required**): Enter your postal code.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, **optional**): If applicable, enter the name of your insurance company.
* **Insurance ID Number** (`insurance_id`, text, **optional**): If applicable, enter your policy number.
* **Reason for Information Request** (`reason_for_request`, select_one, **required**): Choose the reason why you are requesting information.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, **required**): Choose how you would like to be contacted.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time`, select_one, **optional**): Choose a time that works best for you to be contacted.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, **optional**): Enter any other information you would like to share.
* **HIPAA Consent** (`hipaa_consent`, select_one, **required**): Check this box to indicate your understanding of the HIPAA terms.
