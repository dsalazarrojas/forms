# Seller Input Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather feedback and information from customers about our product after they have had an interaction with a seller.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, but only if you have not already provided feedback.
2. For each field, please provide as much detail as you can.
3. If a field is not required, feel free to skip it if you do not have any information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **What do you like about our product?** (`feedback_question_1`, text, optional): Describe what you liked about our product.
* **What do you dislike about our product?** (`feedback_question_2`, text, optional): Describe what you did not like about our product.
* **What are your general comments?** (`customer_feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have about our product.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your interaction with our seller.
