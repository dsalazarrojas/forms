# nutrition_consultation_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users for nutrition consultations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided accurate and relevant information.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Select your preferred contact method from the options provided.
4. Provide your email address and phone number if you wish to be contacted.
5. If you have any medical history or allergies, please list them in the medical history field.
6. If you have any medications, list them in the medication list field.
7. Provide any additional information that you think would be helpful for the consultation.
8. Select your preferred time for the consultation.
9. Once you have filled in all the fields, review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_details** (`user_details`, text, false): Please enter your name and other identifying details.
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **preferred_contact_method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, false): Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Text Message, Mail, or Other).
* **email** (`email`, email, false): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, false): Enter your phone number.
* **additional_info** (`additional_info`, text, false): Provide any additional information that you think would be helpful for the consultation.
* **medical_history** (`medical_history`, note, false): List any medical history or allergies you have.
* **medication_list** (`medication_list`, text, false): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, text, false): Provide any additional contact details.
* **preferred_time** (`preferred_time`, time, false): Select your preferred time for the consultation.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will review your submission and get back to you as soon as possible.
