# Additional Score Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Additional Score Report Request Form is used to request additional score reports for students. This form is typically used by administrators or authorized personnel to verify student records and gather detailed information about a student's performance on a specific test or assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information in the **Requester information** section.
2. Provide your **Requester name** and **Student name**, as they appear on the student's records.
3. Enter the **Test type** (exam or assessment) for which you are requesting the report.
4. Specify the **Number of additional reports** you are requesting.
5. Choose a delivery method for the report (Email, Mail, or Pickup).
6. Enter a **Reason for request** (if required).
7. Enter your **Contact email** for confirmation and tracking purposes.
8. Provide any **Payment details** (if a fee applies).
9. Finally, sign the form with your **Signature name**.

* **Requester information**: This is a note section where you can provide any additional information about the request.
* **Requester name**: Enter the name of the person requesting the report.
* **Student name**: Enter the full name of the student as it appears on their records.
* **Test type**: Enter the name of the exam or assessment for which you are requesting the report.
* **Number of additional reports**: Enter the number of reports you are requesting.

* **Reason for request**: If required, provide a justification for requesting the report.
