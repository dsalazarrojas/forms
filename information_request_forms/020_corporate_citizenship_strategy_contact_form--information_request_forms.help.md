# Corporate Citizenship Strategy Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your company's interests and preferences regarding Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) and Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in the required fields:
  - Company Name: Enter the name of your company.
  - Contact Person: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for CSR and ESG initiatives.
  - Email: Enter the email address of the contact person.
  - Description: Provide a brief description of your company's CSR and ESG goals and objectives.
- Then, select the relevant CSR and ESG initiatives that interest you:
  - CSR Initiatives: Choose one or multiple options from the list.
  - ESG Initiatives: Choose one or multiple options from the list.
- Finally, provide optional information:
  - Phone: Enter your company's phone number (if applicable).
  - Company Size: Select the size of your company.
  - Industry: Select the industry your company operates in.
  - Consultant Type: Choose one or multiple types of consultants that interest you.
  - Company Type: Select one or multiple types of companies that your company is.
  - CSR Goals: Choose one or multiple goals for your CSR initiatives.
  - CSR Budget: Enter your company's CSR budget (if applicable).
  - ESG Budget: Enter your company's ESG budget (if applicable).
  - Company Location: Enter your company's location (if applicable).
  - Time Zone: Select the time zone your company operates in.
  - Consultant Location: Enter your consultant's location (if applicable).
  - Time Zone (Consultant): Select the time zone your consultant operates in.
  - ESG Reporting: Select the frequency of your ESG reporting.
  - ESG Reporting Frequency: Select the frequency of your ESG reporting.
  - Follow-up Date: Enter the date for follow-up contact (if applicable).
  - Follow-up Time: Enter the time for follow-up contact (if applicable).
  - Follow-up Contact Method: Select the method for follow-up contact (if applicable).
  - Follow-up Notes: Add any additional notes for follow-up contact (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for CSR and ESG initiatives.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your company's phone number (if applicable).
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your company's CSR and ESG goals and objectives.
* **CSR Initiatives** (`csr_initiatives`, select_one/ multiple, optional): Choose one or multiple CSR initiatives that interest you:
  - Corporate Social Responsibility
  - Environmental Sustainability
  - Social Impact
* **ESG Initiatives** (`esg_initiatives`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or multiple ESG initiatives that interest you:
  - Corporate Social Responsibility
  - Environmental Sustainability
  - Social Impact
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Select the size of your company:
  - Small
  - Medium
  - Large
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, optional): Select the industry your company operates in:
  - Technology
  - Finance
  - Healthcare
* **Consultant Type** (`consultant_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or multiple types of consultants that interest you:
  - Management Consulting
  - Financial Services
  - Human Resources
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple types of companies that your company is:
  - For-Profit Company
  - Non-Profit Organization
  - Government Agency
* **CSR Goals** (`csr_goals`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or multiple CSR goals for your initiatives:
  - Community Development
  - Environmental Conservation
  - Human Rights
* **CSR Budget** (`csr_budget`, number, optional): Enter your company's CSR budget (if applicable).
* **ESG Budget** (`esg_budget`, number, optional): Enter your company's ESG budget (if applicable).
* **Company Location** (`company_location`, text, optional): Enter your company's location (if applicable).
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, select_one, optional): Select the time zone your company operates in:
  - EST
  - CST
  - PST
* **Consultant Location** (`consultant_location`, text, optional): Enter your consultant's location (if applicable).
* **Time Zone (Consultant)** (`time_zone_consultant`, select_one, optional): Select the time zone your consultant operates in:
  - EST
  - CST
  - PST
* **ESG Reporting** (`esg_reporting`, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of your ESG reporting:
  - Quarterly
  - Annual
  - Bi-Annual
* **ESG Reporting Frequency** (`esg_reporting_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of your ESG reporting:
  - Daily
  - Weekly
  - Monthly
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for follow-up contact (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Enter the time for follow-up contact (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Contact Method** (`follow_up_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method for follow-up contact:
  - Email
  - Phone
  - Chat
* **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes for follow-up contact (if applicable).
