# Seasonal Offer Clarification Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to clarify any doubts or questions they may have about our seasonal offers. It is an open channel for customers to provide information about their specific needs and concerns related to our seasonal promotions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `customer_name` field.
2. Provide your email address in the `email` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field, if applicable.
4. Describe your question or concern about our seasonal offers in the `customer_question` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (customer_name)** (Optional): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed in our response.
* **Email (email)** (Optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone (phone)** (Optional): Enter your phone number for faster communication.
* **Customer Question (customer_question)** (Optional): Briefly describe your question or concern about our seasonal offers. This is the main area for us to understand your specific needs and provide clarification.
