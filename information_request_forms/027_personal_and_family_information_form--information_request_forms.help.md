## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name information.
2. Provide your Address, including Address Line 1 and 2, City, State, Zip, and Phone number.
3. Enter your Date of Birth and Social Security Number (if applicable).
4. Select your Relationship Status (e.g. Single, Married, etc.).
5. If you have dependants, provide their information, including First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, and Address.
6. Enter your Marital Status (e.g. Single, Married, etc.).
