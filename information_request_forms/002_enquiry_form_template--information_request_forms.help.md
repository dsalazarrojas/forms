# Enquiry Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enquiry Form Template is a tool for users to reach out to us with their questions or interest in our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in your information in the required fields and select your preferred contact method from the options provided. You can submit the form once you have filled in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (first_name)**: Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed when we respond to your enquiry.
* **Last Name (last_name)**: Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed when we respond to your enquiry.
* **Contact Method (contact_method)**: Select how you would like to be contacted regarding your enquiry. You can choose between Phone and Email.
* **Subject (subject)**: Briefly describe the topic of your enquiry in this field. This will help us understand your interest.
* **Message (message)**: Provide a detailed message or description of your enquiry. This is the main body of your message.
* **How do you know us (how_do_you_know_us)**: If you've heard about us through social media, a referral, or a search engine, please let us know so we can better understand how to tailor our response to your needs.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address as the primary contact method. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number as a secondary contact method. This will be used only if you've selected Phone as your preferred contact method.
Note: The phone and email fields are both required, and we will use the email address you provide as the primary contact method unless you've selected Phone as your preferred contact method.
