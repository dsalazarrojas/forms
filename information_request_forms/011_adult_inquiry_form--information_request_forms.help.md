# Adult Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult Inquiry Form is a tool used to collect information from adults for various purposes, including general information, support or troubleshooting, partnership requests, or media inquiries. This form helps the organization or business to efficiently handle and respond to requests in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name in the "Full name" field.
2. Enter your organization or group affiliation in the "Organization" field (optional).
3. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact email" field.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Contact phone" field.
5. Select the type of inquiry you are submitting in the "Inquiry type" field.
6. Enter your message or question in the "Message or question" field.
7. If you have a preferred contact time, select it in the "Preferred contact time" field.
8. Decide whether you want to subscribe to updates in the "Subscribe to updates" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (Full name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Organization** (Organization, text, optional): If you are affiliated with an organization or group, enter its name here.
* **Contact email** (Contact email, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact phone** (Contact phone, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Inquiry type** (Inquiry type, select_one, required): Select the type of inquiry you are submitting:
	+ General information
	+ Support or troubleshooting
	+ Partnership request
	+ Media inquiry
* **Message or question** (Message or question, text, required): Provide details about your request.
* **Preferred contact time** (Preferred contact time, time, optional): Select a preferred time for contact:
	+ 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM
	+ 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM
	+ 5:00 PM - 8:00 PM
	+ Flexible/Anytime
* **Subscribe to updates** (Subscribe to updates, select_one, optional): Opt in to receive updates:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Submission date** (Submission date, date, required): This field will be automatically populated with the date and time of submission.
