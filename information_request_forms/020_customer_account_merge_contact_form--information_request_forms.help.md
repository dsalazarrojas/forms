# Customer Account Merge Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for merging customer accounts. It's intended for customers who want to merge multiple accounts or for account representatives who need to update information on existing accounts.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the customer account you're trying to merge. This includes the `customer_account_id`, `customer_email`, `customer_phone`, and `customer_status`.
2. Provide a detailed description of the merge request in the `merge_request_description` field.
3. Choose the correct `resolution1`, `resolution2`, or `resolution3` for the customer request resolution.
4. Select the relevant `merge type1`, `merge type2`, `merge type3`, `merge type4`, `merge type5`, `merge type6`, `merge type7`, `merge type8`, `merge type9` for the account merge type.
5. Choose the relevant options for the `resolution1`, `resolution2`, and `resolution3` for the account request resolution.
6. Finally, click the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Account Information** (`customer_account_merge_contact_form_1`): Enter accurate information about the customer account you're trying to merge.
* **Account Merge Request** (`customer_account_merge_request`): Describe the reason for the account merge request.
* **Merge Request Reason** (`merge_request_reason`): Provide detailed information regarding the merge request.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the customer.
* **Customer Account ID** (`customer_account_id`): Enter the customer's account ID.
* **Customer Representative** (`customer_representative`): Enter the customer's representative's name.
* **Customer Support** (`customer_support`): Enter the customer's support contact.
* **Account Representative** (`account_representative`): Enter the account representative's name.
* **Account Support** (`account_support`): Enter the account support contact.
* **Representative Email** (`customer_representative_email`): Enter the customer representative's email address.
* **Representative Phone** (`customer_representative_phone`): Enter the customer representative's phone number.
* **Account Status** (`account_status`): Enter the account status.
* **Customer Status** (`customer_status`): Enter the customer's status.
* **Customer Request Status** (`customer_request_status`): Enter the customer's request status.
* **Account Request Status** (`account_request_status`): Enter the account request status.
* **Customer Merge Notes** (`customer_merge_notes`): Enter any additional notes regarding the customer merge.
* **Account Merge Notes** (`account_merge_notes`): Enter any additional notes regarding the account merge.
* **Customer Request Resolution** (`customer_request_resolution`): Choose the resolution for the customer request.
* **Account Request Resolution** (`account_request_resolution`): Choose the resolution for the account request.
* **Customer Account Merge Type** (`customer_account_merge_type`): Select the type of customer account merge required.
* **Account Request Merge Type** (`account_request_merge_type`): Select the type of account request merge required.
