# Manufacturing Ecosystem Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from potential partners interested in collaborating with our manufacturing ecosystem. We use this data to assess compatibility and potential partnerships.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form). 
2. Answer each question accurately and completely. 
3. Double-check your responses before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manufacturing Ecosystem Partner Contact Request** (1):
	+ Enter your request or message to be considered as a partner. This should be a clear and concise statement of your company's interests and motivations.
* **Company Name** (2): 
	+ Enter the full name of your company.
* **Email Address** (3): 
	+ Enter your official company email address.
* **Phone Number** (4): 
	+ Enter your company's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Website** (5): 
	+ Enter the URL of your company's website (optional, but recommended).
* **Manufacturing Process** (6):
	+ Choose the type of manufacturing process your company uses (select one option).
* **Production Capacity** (7):
	+ Choose the production capacities that your company can handle (multiple selections allowed).
* **Manufacturing Equipment** (8): 
	+ Enter the specific equipment you use for manufacturing (optional).
* **Manufacturing Location** (9): 
	+ Enter the location of your manufacturing site (optional).
* **Contact Person** (10): 
	+ Enter the name of the person responsible for this submission.
* **Job Title** (11): 
	+ Enter the job title of the contact person.
* **Company Size** (12): 
	+ Choose your company's size (small, medium, or large).
* **Industry** (13): 
	+ Choose the industries your company serves (multiple selections allowed).
* **Company Type** (14): 
	+ Choose the type(s) of company you are (multiple selections allowed) (optional).
* **Company Location** (15): 
	+ Choose the location of your company (optional).
* **Manufacturing Partnership** (16): 
	+ Choose the type(s) of manufacturing partnership(s) you are interested in (multiple selections allowed) (optional).
* **Additional Comments** (17): 
	+ Enter any additional information you would like to share about your company.
* **Contact Information** (18): 
	+ Enter any relevant contact information for the person submitting this form.
* **Manufacturing Process Description** (19): 
	+ Enter a brief description of your company's manufacturing process.
* **Production Capacity Description** (20): 
	+ Enter a description of your company's production capacities.
* **Manufacturing Equipment Description** (21): 
	+ Enter a description of the equipment you use for manufacturing (optional).
* **Manufacturing Location Description** (22): 
	+ Enter a description of your company's manufacturing location (optional).
* **Production Capacity 1** (23): 
	+ Choose the first production capacity of your company.
* **Production Capacity 2** (24): 
	+ Choose the second production capacity of your company.
* **Production Capacity 3** (25): 
	+ Choose the third production capacity of your company.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" must be completed to ensure a successful submission.
