# Night Drop Envelope - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Night Drop Envelope form is used for customers to send a secure message with their name, email address, and a detailed message to be delivered to our organization during night hours.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (optional but recommended).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
5. Type out a detailed message in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: This field is a required field where you need to enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email**: This field is optional, but it's recommended to enter your email address so we can contact you back.
* **Phone**: This field is optional, but it's recommended to enter your phone number so we can contact you during night hours if needed.
* **Message**: This is the main field where you need to type out a detailed message that requires delivery during night hours. Please make sure to be clear and concise in your message.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Be clear and concise in your message to avoid any confusion.
* If you need any assistance or clarification, feel free to contact us directly.
