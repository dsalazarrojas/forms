# Farmers Personal Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and store personal information from farmers, which will be used for administrative or marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Provide your home address in the "address line 1", "address line 2", "city", "state", "postal code", and "country" fields.
6. Enter your birthdate in the "birthdate" field.
7. Enter your birth time in the "birthtime" field.
8. Enter your age in the "age" field.
9. Enter your years of experience in the "years of experience" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name here.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address here.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number here.
* **Address Line 1 (text)**: Enter your street address here (e.g., 123 Main St).
* **Address Line 2 (text)**: Enter your apartment or suite number here (if applicable).
* **City (text)**: Enter the city where you reside here.
* **State (text)**: Enter the state where you reside here.
* **Postal Code (text)**: Enter your postal code here (e.g., 12345).
* **Country (text)**: Enter the country where you reside here.
* **Birthdate (date)**: Enter your birthdate in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Birth time (time)**: Enter your birth time in the format 12:00 AM or 12:00 PM.
* **Age (number)**: Enter your age here.
* **Years of Experience (number)**: Enter the number of years you have been farming here.
