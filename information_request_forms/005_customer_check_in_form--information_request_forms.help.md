# customer_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer_check_in_form is a simple data collection tool designed for customers to check in and provide essential information upon arrival. This form helps the business provide tailored services and support during the visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name, as required in the "Customer Check In" fields.
2. Choose the purpose of your visit from the "Reason for Visit" field.
3. Share any reservation or booking details in the "Reservation Information" field (if applicable).
4. Provide any further instructions you may have in the "Further Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Check In (1)**: Enter your name as you would like to be known during this visit.
* **Reason for Visit (2)**: Choose the reason for your visit to help our staff prepare for your stay.
* **Reservation Information (3)**: If you have a reservation or booking reference, please enter the details here to help our staff locate your booking quickly.
* **Provide Further Instructions (4)**: If you have any specific requests or further information about your visit, please share them here so we can tailor our service to your needs.
* **Customer Check In (5)**: Please confirm your name as you would like to be known during this visit.
* **Customer Check In (6)**: This is a duplicate field, please ignore it and focus on confirming your name in the first "Customer Check In" field.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you need help or have any concerns, our staff will be happy to assist you throughout your visit. 
* You can also ask our staff for further guidance on what information is required and how it will be used during your stay. 
* If you have any trouble accessing the form or submitting it, please don't hesitate to reach out for support. 
* The data collected through this form is used to enhance customer experience and will be treated confidentially and securely. 
* You may modify or delete your submitted data at any time by contacting the relevant authorities.
