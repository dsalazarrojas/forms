# Environmental Education Collaboration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate collaboration requests between schools and other organizations for environmental education initiatives. It collects information on the school's name, contact details, collaboration start and end dates, frequency, and status. This information will help us to better understand the collaboration requests and respond accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **School Name** in the designated field.
2. Provide your **Contact Email** address to enable us to get in touch with you.
3. Enter your **Contact Phone** number for further communication.
4. Select the **Collaboration Type** by choosing between 'Yes' or 'No'.
5. Enter the **Collaboration Name** to identify the specific collaboration request.
6. Provide a brief **Collaboration Description** of the initiative.
7. Enter the **Collaboration Start Date** when the collaboration is expected to begin.
8. Enter the **Collaboration End Date** when the collaboration is expected to end.
9. Select the **Collaboration Frequency** by choosing between 'Yes' or 'No'.
10. Choose the **Collaboration Status** by selecting multiple options from the list (e.g., Active, Inactive).
11. Add any additional **Collaboration Notes** to elaborate on the collaboration request.
12. Enter the **School Address**, **School City**, and **School State** for the school's location.
13. Select whether the school is located in the **School Country** (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
14. Select whether the school is involved in the **Collaboration Involvement Type** (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
15. Choose the **Collaboration Involvement Frequency** by selecting between 'Yes' or 'No'.
16. Provide a brief **Collaboration Involvement Description** of the school's involvement.
17. Add any additional **Collaboration Involvement Notes** to elaborate on the school's involvement.
18. Enter the **Collaboration Additional Notes** to provide any further details.
19. Enter the **Collaboration Involvement Other** to specify any other involvement details.
20. Add any **Submission Notes** to provide additional context.
21. Enter the **Submission Date** when the form is submitted.
22. Finally, provide a brief **Collaboration** description to summarize the collaboration request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school making the collaboration request.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Collaboration Type**: Indicate whether the school is interested in collaboration (yes or no).
* **Collaboration Name**: Enter a unique name for the collaboration request.
* **Collaboration Description**: Provide a brief description of the collaboration request.
* **Collaboration Start Date**: Enter the expected start date of the collaboration.
* **Collaboration End Date**: Enter the expected end date of the collaboration.
* **Collaboration Frequency**: Indicate whether the collaboration will be frequent (yes or no).
* **Collaboration Status**: Choose multiple options from the list to describe the collaboration status (Active, Inactive).
* **Collaboration Notes**: Add any additional notes about the collaboration request.
* **School Address**: Enter the school's address for location information.
* **School City**: Enter the school's city for location information.
* **School State**: Select the school's state for location information.
* **School Country**: Indicate whether the school is located in the specified country (yes or no).
* **Collaboration Involvement Type**: Indicate whether the school is involved in the collaboration (yes or no).
* **Collaboration Involvement Frequency**: Indicate whether the school will be involved frequently (yes or no).
* **Collaboration Involvement Description**: Provide a brief description of the school's involvement.
* **Collaboration Involvement Notes**: Add any additional notes about the school's involvement.
* **Collaboration Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information about the collaboration.
* **Collaboration Involvement Other**: Specify any other involvement details.
* **Submission Notes**: Add any additional context or information.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date the form is submitted.
* **Collaboration**: Enter a brief summary of the collaboration request.
