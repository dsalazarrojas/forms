# Safety Recall Information Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers or their representatives about a product that is being recalled. This helps in following up on the recall effectively and ensures that necessary communication is made with the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the following details about the product and yourself:

1. **Product Identifier**: Enter the product's part number, model number, or any other identifier that you have.
2. **Customer Name**: Fill in your name if you are the customer, or provide the name of the customer's representative.
3. **Customer Email**: Enter your email address or the customer's email address where you would like to receive information and updates.
4. **Customer Phone**: Provide your phone number or the customer's phone number where you can be reached.
5. **Customer Address**: Enter your or the customer's address if you would like to receive mail or packages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Identifier (Product Identifier, number, required)**: Enter the product's part number, model number, or any other unique identifier that you have for the product.
* **Contact Preference (Contact Preference, text, required)**: This is where you can specify how you would like to be contacted. You can type in your preferred method of contact (e.g. phone, email, mail).
* **Customer Information (Customer Information, text, required)**: This field is for you to provide any additional information about yourself or the customer.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments, note, required)**: Here you can provide any additional details or context that you think is relevant to the recall.
* **Customer Name (Customer Name, text, required)**: Fill in your name if you are the customer, or provide the name of the customer's representative.
* **Customer Email (Customer Email, email, required)**: Enter your email address or the customer's email address where you would like to receive information and updates.
* **Customer Phone (Customer Phone, text, required)**: Provide your phone number or the customer's phone number where you can be reached.
* **Customer Address (Customer Address, text, required)**: Enter your or the customer's address if you would like to receive mail or packages.
