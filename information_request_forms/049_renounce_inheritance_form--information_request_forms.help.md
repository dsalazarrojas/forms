# Renounce Inheritance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals who wish to renounce their inheritance. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary information for your request.
## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, make sure you have all the required documents and information ready. This form has several fields that need to be filled out. Please follow the instructions below:
- Start by filling out your personal information (First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth).
- If applicable, provide your current residence address.
- Give a brief explanation for renouncing your inheritance (Reason for Renouncing Inheritance).
- If applicable, provide the names and addresses of any executors involved.
- If applicable, provide the type of will and its number.
- If applicable, provide the date the will was opened.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Current Residence** (`current_residence`, text, optional): Please enter your current residence address.
* **Reason for Renouncing Inheritance** (`reason_for_renouncing_inheritance`, text, optional): Please provide a brief explanation for renouncing your inheritance.
* **Name of Executors** (`name_of_executors`, text, optional): If you have executors involved, please list their names.
* **Executor Addresses** (`executor_addresses`, text, optional): If you have executors involved, please list their addresses.
* **Type of Will** (`will_type`, text, optional): If applicable, please specify the type of will (e.g. will, trust).
* **Will Number** (`will_number`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter the will number.
* **Will Date Opened** (`will_date_opened`, date, optional): If applicable, please enter the date the will was opened.

# Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
