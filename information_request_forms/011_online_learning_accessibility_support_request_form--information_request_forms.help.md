# Online Learning Accessibility Support Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students about their accessibility needs for online learning. This will help our team better understand their requirements and prioritize support requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the request from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter a description of your accessibility needs.
3. Enter your student name, email, phone number, department, and course name.
4. Specify your accessibility needs and other needs.
5. Add any additional comments you want to share with our team.
6. Click the "Submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`select_one`, required): Select the type of request you are making. This will help us understand the context of your request.
* **Description** (`text`, required): Enter a brief description of your accessibility needs.
* **Student Name** (`text`, required): Enter your name as a student.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your university email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Department** (`text`, required): Enter the department you are currently enrolled in or affiliated with.
* **Course Name** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the course you are taking.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`text`, required): Specify your accessibility requirements.
* **Other Needs** (`text`, required): If you have any other needs not listed, specify them here.
* **Comments** (`text`, optional): Add any additional comments or context about your request.
* **Submit** (`text`, optional): Click this button to send your request.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when describing your accessibility needs.
* If you have any other needs not listed, please specify them in the "Other Needs" field.
* We will review and prioritize your request based on the information provided.
