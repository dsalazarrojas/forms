# employment_information_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect employment-related information from employees or job seekers. It helps gather essential details about job positions, employment status, and salary information to process employment requests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Company Details" page to provide information about the company you are employed by or would like to work for.
2. Choose the "Job Position" field to specify your job title or desired job position.
3. Select the "Job Level" field to indicate your job level (C-Level, Director, Manager, etc.).
4. Choose the "Department" field to select the department you are or will be working in (IT, Finance, Marketing, etc.).
5. Enter the "Job Title" field to provide a detailed job title.
6. Describe your "Job Description" in the designated field.
7. Select the "Employment Status" field to indicate your current or desired employment status (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, etc.).
8. Choose the "Employment Type" field to select the type of employment (W2, W1, 1099, etc.).
9. Select the "Job Type" field to specify if you are or will be working on a full-time, part-time, or freelance basis.
10. Enter your "Work Hours" if applicable.
11. Provide the "Employment Start Date" and "Employment End Date" if applicable.
12. Select the "Job Location" field to choose where you will be working (Remote, Office, Both).
13. Enter your desired or actual "Job Level Salary".
14. Enter your "Salary" if applicable.
15. Select the "Employment Status (Salary)" field to indicate your employment status regarding salary (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, etc.).
16. Choose the "Employment Type (Salary)" field to select the type of employment for salary purposes (W2, W1, 1099, etc.).
17. Select the "Job Type (Salary)" field to specify if you are or will be working on a full-time, part-time, or freelance basis regarding salary.
18. Enter the "Employment Start Date (Salary)" and "Employment End Date (Salary)" if applicable.
19. Choose the "Job Location (Salary)" field to choose where you will be working (Remote, Office, Both) regarding salary.
20. Enter the company's "Company Phone" number.
21. Enter the company's "Company Email".
22. Enter the company's "Company Website".
23. Enter any additional "Company Notes" if necessary.
