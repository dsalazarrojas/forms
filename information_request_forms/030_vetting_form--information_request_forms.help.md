# Vetting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vetting Form is a tool used to collect information from individuals for vetting purposes. It is intended to be completed by the individual being vetted, providing the necessary details to facilitate the vetting process. This form will help ensure that you have all the required information in one place and help you make an informed decision.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Vetting Form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the basic contact information: **First Name** (`form_first_name`), **Last Name** (`form_last_name`), and **Date of Birth** (`form_date_of_birth`). Please ensure these fields are accurate to the best of your ability.
2. Next, enter your **Phone Number** (`form_phone`) and **Address** (`form_address`) to help us verify your identity.
3. If applicable, indicate your current **Employer** status by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the drop-down menu (`form_employer`).
4. Provide your **Employer Title** (`form_employer_title`) if applicable.
5. Enter your **Job Title** (`form_job_title`) if applicable.
6. Select the number of **Employees** (`form_employees`) you supervise or work with.
7. Add any **Notes** (`form_notes`) you may have regarding your situation or circumstances.
8. Finally, provide your **Email Address** (`form_email`) for communication purposes.
9. If you have any **Additional Information** (`form_additional_info`), please include it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`form_first_name`):
    *   A field to enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`form_last_name`):
    *   A field to enter your last name.
*   **Date of Birth** (`form_date_of_birth`):
    *   A field to enter your date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YY).
*   **Phone** (`form_phone`):
    *   A field to enter your phone number.
*   **Address** (`form_address`):
    *   A field to enter your address.
*   **City** (`form_city`):
    *   A field to enter the city of your residence or work.
*   **Postal Code** (`form_postal_code`):
    *   A field to enter your postal code.
*   **Employer** (`form_employer`):
    *   Indicate whether you are currently employed or not.
*   **Employer Title** (`form_employer_title`):
    *   A field to enter your title or position in your current or previous job.
*   **Job Title** (`form_job_title`):
    *   A field to enter your job title or occupation.
*   **Employees** (`form_employees`):
    *   Select the number of people you supervise or work with.
*   **Notes** (`form_notes`):
    *   A field to add any notes or comments regarding your situation or circumstances.
*   **Email** (`form_email`):
    *   A field to enter your email address.
*   **Additional Info** (`form_additional_info`):
    *   A field to enter any additional information you may have.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are accurate to the best of your ability.
*   If you are unsure about any field, it is always best to ask for clarification.
*   Complete the form as thoroughly as possible to ensure a smooth vetting process.
