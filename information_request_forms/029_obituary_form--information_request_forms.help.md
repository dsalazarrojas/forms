# Obituary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Obituary Form is a tool for collecting and storing information about deceased individuals. It gathers details about their personal life, passing, and circumstances, to be used for internal purposes such as creating memorial pages.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the deceased individual's first name in the "First name" field.
2.  Enter the deceased individual's last name in the "Last name" field.
3.  Enter the date of death in the "Date of Death" field.
4.  Enter the date of birth in the "Birth date" field.
5.  Enter the place of death in the "Place of Death" field.
6.  Enter the place of birth in the "Place of Birth" field.
7.  Enter the place of residence at the time of death in the "Place of Residence" field.
8.  Enter the city in the "City" field.
9.  Enter the state in the "State" field.
10. Enter the zip code in the "Zip" field.
11. Enter the country in the "Country" field.
12. Enter the time of death in the "Time" field.
13. Enter a brief description or text about the deceased individual in the "Obituary" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name** (First name, text, optional): Enter the first name of the deceased individual.
*   **Last name** (Last name, text, optional): Enter the last name of the deceased individual.
*   **Date of Death** (Date of Death, date, optional): Enter the date of death in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Birth date** (Birth date, date, optional): Enter the date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Place of Death** (Place of Death, text, optional): Enter the location where the deceased individual passed away.
*   **Place of Birth** (Place of Birth, text, optional): Enter the location where the deceased individual was born.
*   **Place of Residence** (Place of Residence, text, optional): Enter the place of residence of the deceased individual at the time of death.
*   **City** (City, text, optional): Enter the city where the deceased individual passed away.
*   **State** (State, text, optional): Enter the state where the deceased individual passed away.
*   **Zip** (Zip, text, optional): Enter the zip code where the deceased individual passed away.
*   **Country** (Country, text, optional): Enter the country where the deceased individual passed away.
*   **Time** (Time, time, optional): Enter the time of death.
*   **Obituary** (Obituary, text, optional): Enter a brief description or text about the deceased individual.
