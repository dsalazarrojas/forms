# Permanent Makeup Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Permanent Makeup Client Intake Form is a questionnaire used to gather information from clients before their first appointment. This form helps the permanent makeup artist or technician to understand the client's needs, preferences, and background to provide an optimal experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Share your contact preferences, such as how you would like to be contacted and by whom.
3. Describe your practice or occupation, including any relevant details about your work or lifestyle.
4. Answer the additional questions about your client information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info** (`client_info`): A text field where you can provide your name, address, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`): This text field is for you to specify how you would like to be contacted, such as by phone, email, or mail, and by whom (e.g., your technician, another client, or your spouse).
* **Practice Details** (`practice_details`): A text field where you can describe your profession or practice, including any relevant details about your work or lifestyle that may impact your treatment.
* **More About Client Info** (`client_info_part_2`): Additional text field to provide further details about yourself, such as any medical conditions, allergies, or other relevant information.
