# Veterinary Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Release Form is used to obtain the consent and authorization from a pet owner for veterinary care and services. This form ensures that both the pet owner and the veterinarian are aware of and agree to the terms of care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, which includes the owner's name, veterinary name, date of release, and any necessary contact information.
2. Select the appropriate authorization options, such as examination and treatment, medication and surgery, or emergency situations.
3. Choose any medications or surgeries that the owner has authorized.
4. Review and sign the agreements, which are the veterinary agreement and the owner's agreement.
5. Ensure that both the owner and the veterinarian have signed and dated their respective sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Owner Name** (text)
*   The owner's name is required to ensure that the form is completed for the correct individual.
*   Please enter the owner's name as it appears on their identification.

### 2. **Veterinary Name** (text)
*   The veterinary name is required to identify the veterinarian providing care.
*   Please enter the name of the veterinarian or clinic providing care.

### 3. **Date of Release** (date, MM/DD/YYYY)
*   The date of release is required to track the date of the authorization.
*   Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 4. **Veterinary Signature** (text)
*   The veterinary signature is required to confirm the veterinarian's agreement to the terms of care.
*   Please sign and date the veterinary signature section.

### 5. **Owner Signature** (text)
*   The owner signature is required to confirm the owner's agreement to the terms of care.
*   Please sign and date the owner's signature section.

### 6. **Veterinary Contact** (text)
*   The veterinary contact information is optional, but recommended for follow-up communication.
*   Please enter the contact information for the veterinarian or clinic.

### 7. **Veterinary Address** (text)
*   The veterinary address is optional, but recommended for follow-up communication.
*   Please enter the address of the veterinarian or clinic.

### 8. **Owner Address** (text)
*   The owner address is optional, but recommended for follow-up communication.
*   Please enter the address of the pet owner.

### 9. **Veterinary Phone** (text)
*   The veterinary phone number is optional, but recommended for immediate contact.
*   Please enter the phone number of the veterinarian or clinic.

### 10. **Veterinary Email** (email)
*   The veterinary email is optional, but recommended for electronic communication.
*   Please enter the email address of the veterinarian or clinic.

### 11. **Date of Last Visit** (date, MM/DD/YYYY)
*   The date of last visit is optional, but recommended for tracking purposes.
*   Please enter the date of the last visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 12. **Veterinary License No** (text)
*   The veterinary license number is optional, but recommended for identification purposes.
*   Please enter the license number of the veterinarian or clinic.

### 13. **Owner License No** (text)
*   The owner license number is optional, but recommended for identification purposes.
*   Please enter the license number of the pet owner.

### 14. **Owner Notes** (text)
*   The owner notes are optional, but recommended for additional information.
*   Please enter any relevant notes from the pet owner.

### 15. **Veterinary Notes** (text)
*   The veterinary notes are optional, but recommended for additional information.
*   Please enter any relevant notes from the veterinarian.

### 16. **Owner Agreement** (select_one)
*   The owner agreement is a selection of options that the pet owner must agree to.
*   Please select all relevant options.

### 17. **Veterinary Agreement** (select_one)
*   The veterinary agreement is a selection of options that the veterinarian must agree to.
*   Please select all relevant options.

### 18. **Veterinary Release** (select_one)
*   The veterinary release is a selection of options that the pet owner authorizes for the veterinarian.
*   Please select all relevant options.

### 19. **Owner Release** (select_one)
*   The owner release is a selection of options that the veterinarian authorizes for the pet owner.
*   Please select all relevant options.

### 20. **Medication List** (select_multiple)
*   The medication list is a selection of medications that the pet owner authorizes.
*   Please select all relevant medications.

### 21. **Surgery List** (select_multiple)
*   The surgery list is a selection of surgeries that the pet owner authorizes.
*   Please select all relevant surgeries.
