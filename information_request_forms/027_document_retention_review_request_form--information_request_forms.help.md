# Document Retention Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a review of documents that are nearing or have exceeded their retention period to ensure they are up to date and in line with company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully, ensuring all fields are completed accurately and completely.
- Select the correct options for each field according to your situation.
- Submit the form once you have reviewed all the fields and are confident in your entries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Introduction): A brief note introducing your request, including the purpose of the document review.
* **Employee** (employee): Select your role in the company, as it may affect the type of document review required (e.g., Manager or Employee).
* **Reason** (reason): Choose the type of document review you require (e.g., Review or Retention).
* **Date** (date): Enter the date the document was created or last updated.
* **Manager** (manager): Select your line manager's name to ensure they are aware of and agree with your review request.
* **Comments** (comments): Add any additional comments or notes about the document review.
* **Retention Date** (retention_date): Enter the date the document should be retained or disposed of.
* **Document Type** (document_type): Select all relevant document types that require review (e.g., Contract, Agreement, Other, Record). 

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult with your line manager or document control officer before submitting the form.
- Duplicate submissions will be rejected.
