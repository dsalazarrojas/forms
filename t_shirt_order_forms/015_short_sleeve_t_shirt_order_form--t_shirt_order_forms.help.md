# short_sleeve_t_shirt_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help users place an order for a short-sleeve t-shirt.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details (Contact Details)**: Enter your name and contact information to help us process your order.
* **Select Product (Select Product)**: Choose the product you want to order from the available options.
* **Payment Details (Payment Details)**: Enter your payment information, including your name, address, and any other required details.
* **Payment Gateway (Payment Gateway)**: Select your preferred payment gateway (e.g., PayPal, Credit Card).
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)**: Choose how you want to pay (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).
* **Payment Amount (Payment Amount)**: Enter the amount you want to pay for your order.
* **Confirm Payment Details (Confirm Payment Details)**: Re-enter your payment details to confirm accuracy.
* **Confirm Payment Method (Confirm Payment Method)**: Re-enter your payment method to confirm accuracy.
