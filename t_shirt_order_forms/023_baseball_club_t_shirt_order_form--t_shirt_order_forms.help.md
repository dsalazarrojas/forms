# Baseball Club T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for teams to order custom baseball t-shirts. Please ensure accurate and clear information is provided to ensure correct order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (*) in the label.
2. Choose the correct shirt style and color.
3. Select the design options for front and back of the shirts.
4. Enter the quantity for each size.
5. Specify the delivery method and date.
6. Select the payment method.
7. Add any special instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your baseball team.
* **Team Manager Name** (`team_manager_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the full name of the team manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, `required`): Enter the team manager's email address for order confirmation.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter the team manager's best contact number.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date for the order.
* **Shirt Style** (`shirt_style`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the style of shirt from the options provided.
* **Shirt Color** (`shirt_color`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the primary color for the shirts.
* **Accent Color** (`accent_color`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the secondary color for trim on the shirts.
* **Small Size Quantity** (`small_quantity`, `number`, `required`): Enter the number of small size shirts needed.
* **Medium Size Quantity** (`medium_quantity`, `number`, `required`): Enter the number of medium size shirts needed.
* **Large Size Quantity** (`large_quantity`, `number`, `required`): Enter the number of large size shirts needed.
* **XL Size Quantity** (`xl_quantity`, `number`, `required`): Enter the number of XL size shirts needed.
* **XXL Size Quantity** (`xxl_quantity`, `number`, `required`): Enter the number of XXL size shirts needed.
* **Youth Small Quantity** (`youth_small_quantity`, `number`, `optional`): Enter the number of youth small size shirts needed.
* **Youth Medium Quantity** (`youth_medium_quantity`, `number`, `optional`): Enter the number of youth medium size shirts needed.
* **Youth Large Quantity** (`youth_large_quantity`, `number`, `optional`): Enter the number of youth large size shirts needed.
* **Front Design** (`front_design`, `text`, `required`): Describe the design to be printed on the front of the shirts.
* **Back Design** (`back_design`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the design to be printed on the back of the shirts.
* **Sleeve Design** (`sleeve_design`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any design to be printed on the sleeves.
* **Player Names on Back** (`player_names`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose whether shirts will have player names on the back.
* **Player Numbers on Back** (`player_numbers`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose whether shirts will have numbers on the back.
* **Upload Team Logo** (`logo_file`, `text`, `optional`): If you have a logo file, upload it.
* **Total Shirt Quantity** (`total_quantity`, `number`, `required`): Enter the total number of shirts needed.
* **Needed By Date** (`delivery_deadline`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date by which the shirts are needed.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose how you want the shirts to be delivered.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose how you will pay for the shirts.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any additional instructions or notes.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
- Choose the correct shirt style and color options.
- Clearly describe design details to ensure accurate printout.
- Be specific about player names and numbers if they are included.
- Verify the delivery method and date.
- Confirm the payment method.
- Add any special instructions for accurate order fulfillment.
