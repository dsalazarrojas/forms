# Sports T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports T Shirt Order Form is used to take orders for sports T-shirts. It collects team information, customer details, and order preferences to ensure accurate and timely processing of orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the team name in the "Team Name" field.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Fill in your address in the "Address 1", "Address 2", "City", and "State" fields for shipping and billing purposes.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Select the date of the order in the "Order Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team you are ordering for.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for contact purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for contact purposes.
* **Address 1** (`address_1`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address line 1.
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address line 2.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city for your shipping address.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state for your shipping address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code for your shipping address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the order.

Note: All fields are optional, but it's essential to fill them out correctly for accurate processing of your order.
