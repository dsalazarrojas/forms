# Summer T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer T Shirt Order Form is a form used to collect information for ordering a Summer T Shirt from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the visuals you want for your T Shirt by selecting the desired options in the "T-Shirt Visuals" field.
2. Enter the quantity of T Shirts you want to order.
3. Select your preferred size for the T Shirt.
4. Enter your shipping address, including your city and country.
5. Enter your credit card details, including the type, number, and expiration date.
6. Enter any additional order notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **T-Shirt Visuals**: Choose the visuals you want for your T Shirt. You can select multiple options from the provided list.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of T Shirts you want to order. Please note that this field is not required.
* **Size Preference**: Enter your preferred size for the T Shirt. Please note that this field is not required.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address, including your city and country.
* **City**: Enter the city where you would like to receive your order.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip or postal code where you would like to receive your order.
* **Country**: Select your country of residence.
* **Credit Card Type**: Choose the type of credit card you are using to pay for your order. 
* **Credit Card Number**: Enter your credit card number for secure payment processing.
* **Credit Card Expiration Date**: Enter the expiration date of your credit card for secure payment processing.
* **CCV**: Enter the 3- or 4-digit code on the back of your credit card for secure payment processing.
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes for your order.
