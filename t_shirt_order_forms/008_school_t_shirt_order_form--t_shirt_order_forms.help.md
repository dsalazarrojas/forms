# School T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School T Shirt Order Form is used to collect information from students, staff, and faculty about their t-shirt orders. This form is used to gather data about the color and size of the t-shirt, the quantity needed, contact information for the customer, and additional notes. Fill out this form to place your order for a school t-shirt.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the School T Shirt Order Form, follow these steps:

1. Select the t-shirt color you prefer.
2. Choose the size of the t-shirt you need.
3. Enter the quantity of t-shirts you would like to order.
4. Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the t-shirt.
5. Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any questions or updates.
6. Enter your email address so we can keep you informed about your order.
7. Select the date you would like to pick up your ordered t-shirt.
8. Select the time you would like to pick up your ordered t-shirt.
9. Add any additional notes about your order.
10. If you are a student, you are a member of a specific group, please indicate by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **t_shirt_color (Select Multiple)**: Select all the colors you would like to order, separated by commas. For example, if you want a blue and red t-shirt, select 'Yes' for both options.
* **size_t_shirt (Number)**: Enter the size you would like to order. Please select from the following options:
	+ 0-2 (Small)
	+ 3-4 (Medium)
	+ 5-6 (Large)
* **t_shirt_quantity (Number)**: Enter the number of t-shirts you would like to order.
* **customer_name (Text)**: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the t-shirt.
* **customer_phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any questions or updates.
* **customer_email (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can keep you informed about your order.
* **order_date (Date)**: Select the date you would like to pick up your ordered t-shirt. This should be in the format of month/day/year.
* **order_time (Time)**: Select the time you would like to pick up your ordered t-shirt. This should be in the format of 12-hour clock (e.g., 3:00 PM).
* **order_note (Note)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **customer_category (Select One)**: If you are a student, select 'Yes' to indicate that you are a student. If you are a staff member or faculty, select 'No'.
* **customer_group (Select One)**: If you are a member of a specific group, select 'Yes' to indicate this. If you are not a member of a specific group, select 'No'.
