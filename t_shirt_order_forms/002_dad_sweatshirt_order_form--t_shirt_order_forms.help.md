# Dad Sweatshirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dad Sweatshirt Order Form is designed to collect order details for custom t-shirts. This form is used to gather information for a single, unique order, and users should be aware that each submission will result in a one-off production process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, simply follow the steps below.

1. Enter your name and email address to receive order updates and confirm your order details.
2. Select your preferred t-shirt size (small, medium, or large).
3. Choose your preferred t-shirt color (Black, White, or Gray).
4. Select your preferred t-shirt design (Design 1, Design 2, or Design 3).
5. If you're feeling generous, choose a quantity of t-shirts you'd like to order.
6. Enter your phone number, so we can contact you with any questions or updates.
7. Add any additional notes about your order in the "Order Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name to receive order updates and confirm your order details.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive order updates and confirm your order details.
* **T-shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of t-shirt you'd like (small, medium, or large).
* **T-shirt Color** (`t_shirt_color`, select_multiple, optional): Select the color of t-shirt you'd like (Black, White, or Gray).
* **T-shirt Design** (`t_shirt_design`, select_one, optional): Choose the design of t-shirt you'd like (Design 1, Design 2, or Design 3).
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you'd like to receive your order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you'd like to receive your order.
* **T-Shirt Quantity** (`t_shirt_quantity`, number, optional): Choose the number of t-shirts you'd like to order.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, so we can contact you with any questions or updates.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your order in this field.

## Tips
* Please select a valid t-shirt size to ensure proper fit.
* Please choose a color and design that you like, as each t-shirt will be unique.
* If you have any questions or need help with your order, don't hesitate to contact us.
