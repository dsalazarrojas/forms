# Band T-Shirt Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to order custom band t-shirts. You can choose your favorite band's merchandise with different design, color, size, style, and price options.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your customer information: **Customer Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**.
2. Select your preferred band: **Band Name**.
3. Choose your design: **Shirt Design** and **Shirt Color**.
4. Select your size and style: **Shirt Size**, **Shirt Style**, and **Fit Type**.
5. Enter your order details: **Quantity** and **Price Per Shirt**.
6. Add any customizations: **Custom Name or Number**.
7. Choose your delivery method: **Delivery Method**.
8. Enter your shipping address: **Shipping Address** and **Country**.
9. Select your payment method: **Payment Method**.
10. Calculate your order total: **Order Total**.
11. (Optional) Enter a promo code: **Promo Code**.
12. (Optional) Add a gift message: **Gift Message**.
13. (Optional) Choose if this is a gift: **Is This a Gift**.
14. (Optional) Sign up for the band's newsletter: **Newsletter Signup**.
15. (Optional) Show interest in additional merchandise: **Additional Merchandise Interest**.
16. Add any special instructions: **Special Instructions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name as you'd like it to be displayed on your order.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Band Name** (Required): Select the band's merchandise you want to order.
* **Shirt Design** (Required): Choose from available designs, e.g., Tour 2024 Design, Album Art Design, etc.
* **Shirt Color** (Required): Select the base color of the shirt, e.g., Black, White, Navy Blue, etc.
* **Shirt Size** (Required): Choose your size, e.g., Small, Medium, Large, etc.
* **Shirt Style** (Required): Select the type of shirt, e.g., Crew Neck T-Shirt, V-Neck T-Shirt, etc.
* **Fit Type** (Required): Choose the fit type, e.g., Unisex, Mens Fit, Womens Fit, etc.
* **Quantity** (Required): Enter the number of shirts you'd like to order.
* **Price Per Shirt** (Required): Select the price per shirt.
* **Custom Name or Number** (Optional): Enter any personalization on the back of the shirt, e.g., your name or nickname.
* **Delivery Method** (Required): Choose how you'd like to receive your order, e.g., Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, etc.
* **Shipping Address** (Required): Enter your full shipping address.
* **Country** (Required): Select your shipping destination country.
* **Payment Method** (Required): Choose how you'd like to pay, e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.
* **Order Total** (Required): The total amount due for your order.
* **Promo Code** (Optional): Enter any available promo code for a discount.
* **Gift Message** (Optional): Enter a message to be included with your order.
* **Is This a Gift** (Optional): Choose if this is a gift and if you'd like it gift wrapped.
* **Newsletter Signup** (Optional): Choose if you'd like to receive band updates.
* **Additional Merchandise Interest** (Optional): Show interest in other band merchandise.
* **Special Instructions** (Optional): Enter any special instructions or notes for your order.
