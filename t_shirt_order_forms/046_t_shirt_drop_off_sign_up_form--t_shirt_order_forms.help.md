# T Shirt Drop Off Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to sign up for the T-Shirt drop off event. Please fill out the form to provide information about yourself and your t-shirt drop off preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, fill out the following fields in sequence. You can submit the form once you have filled out all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address in this field. This will be used for communication regarding your participation in the event.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number in this field.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): Please select the date you plan to drop off your t-shirts in this field. The format should be YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Please select the time you plan to drop off your t-shirts in this field. The format is HH:MM:SS.
* **Number of T-Shirts** (`Number of T-Shirts`, number, optional): Please select how many t-shirts you plan to drop off.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`T-Shirt Size`, select one, optional): Please select the size of the t-shirt you plan to drop off.
	+ Extra Small
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
	+ X-Large
	+ Extra Large
* **Special Instructions** (`Special Instructions`, text, optional): Please provide any special instructions for your t-shirt drop off, such as any specific requirements or notes.
* **How many bags** (`How many bags`, number, optional): Please select how many bags you plan to have for your t-shirt drop off.
* **Preferred Bag Selection** (`t-shirt-drop-off-sign-up-form-page-11`, select multiple, optional): Please select which bags you would like to use for your t-shirt drop off.
