# School T Shirt Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School T Shirt Purchase Form is a standardized template for collecting information from parents and guardians to purchase school T-shirts. It is designed to gather all necessary details accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
2. Enter the student's name, grade, and class name as required.
3. Select the student's T-shirt size from the options provided.
4. Enter the class and student fees as numbers.
5. Confirm that you have reviewed and verified the above information carefully.
6. Enter the school email and phone number (if required).
7. Check the "Submit" options to confirm your identity as a parent or guardian and confirm the accuracy of the provided information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student T Shirt Size** (`school_t_shirt_size`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's T-shirt size.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, required/optional): Select the student's grade level from the provided options.
* **Class Name** (`class_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the class name.
* **Class Fee** (`class_fee`, number, required/optional): Enter the class fee.
* **Student Fee** (`student_fee`, number, required/optional): Enter the student fee.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the school email (if required).
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the school phone number (if required).
* **Submit** (`submit_form`, select_multiple, true): Confirm that you have reviewed and verified the above information carefully, confirm that you are a parent or guardian, and confirm the accuracy of the provided information.
