# Generic T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers place orders for t-shirts, allowing them to select the desired t-shirt style, size, color, and quantity, as well as shipping and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the t-shirt style that suits your preference.
2. Choose the size and color of the t-shirt you want.
3. Specify the quantity of t-shirts you wish to order.
4. Select your preferred shipping method (Home, Work, or Other).
5. Choose your preferred payment method (Card or PayPal).
6. Select the shipping method (USPS, UPS, or FedEx).
7. Enter the date and time of your order.
8. Select your time zone (Eastern, Central, or Pacific).
9. Confirm the order total (this will be calculated based on your selections).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order T-Shirt**: Enter the type of t-shirt you wish to order.
* **T-Shirt Size**: Select the size of the t-shirt you want (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **T-Shirt Color**: Choose one or more colors for the t-shirt (White, Black, Red, or Blue).
* **T-Shirt Style**: Select the style of the t-shirt (Crew Neck, V-Neck, Henley, or Hoodie).
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of t-shirts you wish to order.
* **Price**: This field is not editable; the price is automatically calculated based on your selections. Please do not attempt to modify it.
* **Order Details**: Enter any additional information or notes about your order.
* **Shipping Address**: Select the location where you want the t-shirt shipped (Home, Work, or Other).
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method (Card or PayPal).
* **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping method for your order (USPS, UPS, or FedEx).
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of your order.
* **Order Time**: Enter the time of your order.
* **Time Zone**: Select your time zone (Eastern, Central, or Pacific).
* **Order Total**: This field is automatically populated with the total cost of your order; please do not attempt to modify it.
