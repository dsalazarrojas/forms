# Family Reunion T-Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from family members attending the family reunion, including their name, contact information, and plan to attend. This information will be used to organize and coordinate the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the field "What is your name?"
2. Enter the number of t-shirts you need in the field "How many t-shirts do you need?"
3. Enter your email address in the field "What is your email address?"
4. Enter the date of the family reunion in the field "When is the family reunion?"
5. Enter your phone number in the field "What is your phone number?"
6. If applicable, select how you plan to attend the family reunion from the options "In person", "Virtual", or "Unable to attend".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name? (Text, Required):** Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **How many t-shirts do you need (Number, Required):** Enter the number of t-shirts you would like to order for yourself.
* **What is your email address (Text, Required):** Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **When is the family reunion? (Date, Required):** Enter the date of the family reunion in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **What is your phone number (Number, Required):** Enter your phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX.
* **How do you plan to attend the family reunion? (Select One, Optional):** Select whether you plan to attend in person, virtually, or if you are unable to attend.
