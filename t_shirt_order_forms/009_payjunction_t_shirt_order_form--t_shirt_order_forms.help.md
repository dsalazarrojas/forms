# PayJunction T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about customers who want to order a t-shirt, including their name, contact information, shipping details, and order preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Shipping Address** in the "Shipping Address" field.
5. Enter your **Shipping City** in the "Shipping City" field.
6. Select your shipping status for **Shipping State**. If you're not shipping to a state, select "No".
7. Enter your **Shipping Zip** in the "Shipping Zip" field.
8. Enter your **Phone** in the "Phone" field.
9. Select your preferred **T-Shirt Design** from the options.
10. Enter the quantity of t-shirts you'd like to order in the "Quantity" field.
11. Enter the **Price** of your order in the "Price" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Shipping Address** (optional): Enter your shipping address, including street number and apartment number (if applicable).
* **Shipping City** (optional): Enter the city where you're shipping to.
* **Shipping State** (optional): Select whether you're shipping to a state or not. If you're not shipping to a state, select "No".
* **Shipping Zip** (optional): Enter your zip code where you're shipping to.
* **Phone** (optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **T-Shirt Design Choice** (required): Select the design of the t-shirt you'd like to order.
* **Quantity** (optional): Enter the number of t-shirts you'd like to order.
* **Price** (required): Enter the total price of your order.
