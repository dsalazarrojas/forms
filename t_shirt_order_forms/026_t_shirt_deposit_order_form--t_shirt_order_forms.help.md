# T Shirt Deposit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The T Shirt Deposit Order Form is a form designed to collect necessary information for t-shirt orders. It is used to gather customer and order details, including total amount, date, and billing information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information. 
2. Make sure to provide accurate information in all fields, especially in the fields marked as required.
3. Review your submission before submitting for confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: Enter your personal details, such as name, contact number, etc. (Text)
* **Order Details**: Provide a detailed description of the t-shirt order, including quantity, size, color, and style. (Text, Required)
* **Total**: Enter the total amount of the order, including any applicable taxes or fees. (Number, Required)
* **Date**: Select the date you would like to schedule your order for. (Date, Optional)
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address where you would like to receive your t-shirt order. (Text, Optional)
* **Billing Info**: Enter your billing information, including name, address, and payment method. (Text, Optional)

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid delays in processing your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact our customer support team for assistance.
