# Festival T Shirt Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for pre-ordering T shirts for a festival. Fill out this form to provide us with your order information, delivery preference, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address.
2. Choose how you would like to receive your order:
	* Delivery to home
	* Delivery to store
	* Pick up in store
3. Select your preferred payment method:
	* Credit card
	* PayPal
	* Apple Pay
4. Fill in your first name and last name.
5. Specify the number of T shirts you would like to order.
6. Choose how you would like to receive your order:
	* Home delivery
	* In-store pickup

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Festival T Shirt Pre Order Form** (Text): This is the title of the form, which you can disregard as it is already filled out.
* **Name** (Text): Enter your name in this field.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address to which we will send your order confirmation.
* **Delivery Preference** (Select Multiple):
	+ *Delivery to home*: We will deliver your order to your home address.
	+ *Delivery to store*: We will deliver your order to our store.
	+ *Pick up in store*: Come pick up your order at our store.
* **Payment Method** (Select One):
	+ *Credit Card*: We will charge your credit card for the order.
	+ *PayPal*: You will need to log in to your PayPal account to complete the payment.
	+ *Apple Pay*: You will need to use your Apple Pay account to complete the payment.
* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name as it is required for order processing.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name as it is required for order processing.
* **Order Quantity** (Number): Enter the number of T shirts you would like to order.
* **Delivery Method** (Select One):
	+ *Home delivery*: We will deliver your order to your home address.
	+ *In-store pickup*: Come pick up your order at our store.
