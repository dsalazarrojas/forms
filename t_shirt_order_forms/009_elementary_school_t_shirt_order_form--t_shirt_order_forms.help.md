# Elementary School T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for ordering Elementary School T shirts. It's a simple form that collects user's basic information, favorite color, interests, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your User Name and Email address in the required fields.
2. Select your favorite color from the dropdown list.
3. Choose multiple interests by selecting from the checkboxes.
4. Enter your order date and order time if you want to schedule a specific time for delivery.
5. If you have any special instructions for your order, you can add them in the Special Instructions field.
6. Enter your payment details in the Payment Card field (card number) and the CVV field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Favorite Color** (`select_one`, Select One, Required): Select your favorite color by choosing from the list: red, blue, green, or yellow.
* **Interests** (`select_multiple`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose your interests by selecting multiple checkboxes: math, science, reading, writing, art, or music.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you want to order your T shirt on.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time you want to order your T shirt on.
* **Special Instructions** (`note`, Note, Optional): Add any special instructions for your order.
* **Payment Card** (`payment_card`, Number, Required): Enter your payment card number.
* **CVV** (`payment_cvv`, Text, Optional): Enter your CVV (card verification value).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure you receive your order confirmation.
* Select your favorite color to have it printed on your T shirt.
* Be as specific as possible when choosing your interests to help our staff process your order efficiently.
* If you have any special instructions, please add them so our staff knows how to handle your order.
* Enter your payment details correctly to avoid any delays in processing your order.
