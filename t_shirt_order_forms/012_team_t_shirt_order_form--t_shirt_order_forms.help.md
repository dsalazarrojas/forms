# Team T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Team T Shirt Order Form is designed to collect information for team orders. This form helps team administrators manage and track team orders for events, conferences, or any other team gatherings.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to submit your order.
2.  Complete each field in the form as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Team T Shirt Order Form** (`team_t_shirt_order_form`, `note`, required/optional: not required)
    This is the form title that describes what the form is for.
*   **Team Details** (`order_details`, `text`, required/optional: not required)
    Enter a brief description of the team order, if necessary. You can leave this field blank.
*   **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, required/optional: not required)
    Enter the name of the team that ordered the t-shirts.
*   **Team Image** (`team_image`, `text`, required/optional: not required)
    If the team has a logo or image, add it here.
*   **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required/optional: not required)
    Enter the date of the order.
*   **Order Time** (`order_time`, `time`, required/optional: not required)
    Enter the time of the order.
*   **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required/optional: not required)
    Enter the name of the customer who ordered the t-shirts.
