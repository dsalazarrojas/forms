# Screen Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for ordering custom screen printed t-shirts. It is a multi-page form that collects information for the customer, product details, and order status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Details** section with your name, phone number, and email (if applicable).
2. In the **T-shirt Details** section, select the **T-shirt Design**, **T-shirt Color**, and **T-shirt Size** you want.
3. Choose the **T-shirt Material** and enter the **T-shirt Quantity** you want to order.
4. Provide your **Shipping Address**.
5. Select your **Payment Method** and enter your **Payment Card Number**.
6. Enter the **Order Total**.
7. Select the **Order Status**, **Delivery Status**, and **Customer Note** for your order.
8. Finally, add any additional **Order Comments** or **Shipping Notes** if necessary.
