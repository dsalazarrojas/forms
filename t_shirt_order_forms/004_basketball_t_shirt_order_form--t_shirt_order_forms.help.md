# Basketball T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Basketball T Shirt Order Form is designed to collect information from customers to create custom basketball-themed shirts. This form is used to gather details about the customer, their team, and their order preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your customer information: Enter your full name in the "Customer Name" field, your email address in the "Email Address" field, and your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. These fields are all required for order fulfillment and communication.
2. Enter your team details: In the "Team Name" field, enter the name of your basketball team.
3. Choose your shirt style and color: Select your preferred shirt style from the "Shirt Style" dropdown menu and choose the primary color from the "Shirt Color" dropdown menu.
4. Specify your order quantities: Enter the number of small, medium, large, and extra-large shirts you want to order in the "Quantity - Small", "Quantity - Medium", "Quantity - Large", and "Quantity - XL" fields.
5. Add custom text and logo: If you want to add custom text or a logo to your shirt, enter the text in the "Custom Text" field and upload your logo in the "Logo Upload" field.
6. Choose your delivery method: Select how you would like to receive your order from the "Delivery Method" dropdown menu.
7. Select your payment method: Choose how you will pay for your order from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
8. Add any order notes: Enter any special instructions or notes in the "Order Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where we can send order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for order updates.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your basketball team.
* **Shirt Style** (`shirt_style`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred shirt style from the dropdown menu.
* **Shirt Color** (`shirt_color`, select_one, required): Choose the primary color of your shirt from the dropdown menu.
* **Quantity - Small** (`size_small`, number, optional): Enter the number of small shirts you want to order.
* **Quantity - Medium** (`size_medium`, number, optional): Enter the number of medium shirts you want to order.
* **Quantity - Large** (`size_large`, number, optional): Enter the number of large shirts you want to order.
* **Quantity - XL** (`size_xl`, number, optional): Enter the number of XL shirts you want to order.
* **Custom Text** (`custom_text`, text, optional): Enter any text you want to print on your shirt.
* **Logo Upload** (`logo_upload`, text, optional): Upload your team logo if available.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to receive your order from the dropdown menu.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your full delivery address if selecting "Ship to Address" as your delivery method.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you will pay for your order from the dropdown menu.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or notes for your order.
