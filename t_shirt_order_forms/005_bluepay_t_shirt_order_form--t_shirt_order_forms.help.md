# Bluepay T-Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bluepay T-Shirt Order Form is designed for customers to order t-shirts with custom designs or logos. This form collects information about the customer, their order preferences, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the style of t-shirt you would like to order.
2. Choose your preferred size and color for the t-shirt.
3. Enter the quantity of t-shirts you would like to order.
4. If you need additional sizes, select them in the "Additional Sizes Needed" field.
5. Enter any custom text or upload a logo for printing.
6. Select the location where you would like the design to be printed.
7. Enter your contact information, including first and last name, email address, phone number, and company name (if applicable).
8. Select your preferred shipping method and payment method.
9. Enter your card number for payment.
10. If necessary, enter any special instructions for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **T-Shirt Style**: Select the type of t-shirt style you would like to order, such as Classic Crew Neck, V-Neck, or Polo Shirt.
* **T-Shirt Size**: Choose your preferred size, from XS to 4XL.
* **T-Shirt Color**: Select your preferred color from the available options.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of t-shirts you would like to order.
* **Additional Sizes Needed**: If you need multiple sizes, select them in this field.
* **Custom Text**: Enter any custom text you would like to be printed on the t-shirt (optional).
* **Upload Logo**: Upload your logo or design file for printing (optional).
* **Print Location**: Select where you would like the design to be printed, such as on the front center or back of the t-shirt.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for order-related communications.
* **Company or Organization Name**: Enter your company or organization name (optional).
* **Street Address**: Enter your shipping address.
* **City**: Enter the city of your shipping address.
* **State**: Enter the state of your shipping address.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the ZIP code of your shipping address.
* **Shipping Method**: Select your preferred shipping option, such as standard shipping or express shipping.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment option, such as credit card or debit card.
* **Card Number**: Enter your card details for payment.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any additional notes or instructions for your order (optional).
