# T Shirt Request Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect requests for t-shirts from customers. It is used to gather information about the customer's size, preferred color, and event details such as name, event name, event date, and event time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred shirt size from the available options (S, M, L, XL, XXL).
2. Choose your preferred shirt color from the available options (Blue, Red, Green, Yellow, Black).
3. Enter your name and email address in the required fields.
4. Enter your phone number and school name (if applicable).
5. Provide the name of the event you are requesting the shirt for.
6. Enter the date and time of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shirt Size** (`size`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred shirt size from the available options.
* **Shirt Color** (`color`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your preferred shirt color from the available options.
* **Requestor Details** (`requestor_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the person requesting the shirt.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person requesting the shirt.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the person requesting the shirt.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person requesting the shirt.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required: false): If the shirt is for a school event, enter the name of the school.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the event you are requesting the shirt for.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the event.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields (name and email).
* If you are requesting a shirt for a school event, please enter the name of the school.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the Requestor Details field.
* Make sure to check the form for any errors before submitting it.
