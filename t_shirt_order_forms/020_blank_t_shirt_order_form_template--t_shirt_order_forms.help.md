# Blank T Shirt Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers place custom t-shirt orders with precise details of their preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which include your name, email, phone number, shirt style, shirt color, small, medium, and large shirt quantities, printing method, design upload status, and needed by date.
2. If you want to provide additional information, such as the organization name, shirt brand, and number of colors in your design, it's optional but recommended.
3. You can select the print location and number of colors in your design if you want to specify these details.
4. Finally, fill out your shipping address and provide any special instructions if you have any specific requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name or organization name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, optional): If ordering for a group or company, enter the name.
* **Shirt Style** (`shirt_style`, `select_one`, required): Choose the style of t-shirt you prefer.
* **Shirt Brand** (`shirt_brand`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred shirt brand.
* **Shirt Color** (`shirt_color`, `select_one`, required): Pick your primary shirt color.
* **Small Quantity** (`size_small_qty`, `number`, required): Enter the number of small shirts you need.
* **Medium Quantity** (`size_medium_qty`, `number`, required): Enter the number of medium shirts you need.
* **Large Quantity** (`size_large_qty`, `number`, required): Enter the number of large shirts you need.
* **X-Large Quantity** (`size_xl_qty`, `number`, required): Enter the number of XL shirts you need.
* **XXL Quantity** (`size_xxl_qty`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of XXL shirts you need.
* **XXXL Quantity** (`size_xxxl_qty`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of XXXL shirts you need.
* **Printing Method** (`printing_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you want the design to be applied.
* **Design Upload** (`design_uploaded`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have uploaded your design or will email it later.
* **Print Location** (`print_location`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select where you would like the design to appear.
* **Number of Colors** (`number_of_colors`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of colors in your design.
* **Needed By Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date by which you need the shirts.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where you would like the shirts shipped to.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional notes or requests you have.

## Tips
When filling out this form, make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness to ensure a smooth order process.
