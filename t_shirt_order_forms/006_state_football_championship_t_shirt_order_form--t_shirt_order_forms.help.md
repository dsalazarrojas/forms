# State Football Championship T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers to order t-shirts related to the State Football Championship. It will help us fulfill your order efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your team name in the "Team Name" field.
6. Specify the quantity of t-shirts you would like to order in the "Quantity" field.
7. Choose the desired shirt size from the "Shirt Size" dropdown menu.
8. Select your preferred color(s) for the t-shirt in the "Color" dropdown menu.
9. If you have any special instructions for your order, please type them in the "Special Instructions" field.
10. Enter the date you would like to order your t-shirt in the "Order Date" field.
11. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Required): Please enter your first name as it should appear on the t-shirt.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Required): Please enter your last name as it should appear on the t-shirt.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter your email address where we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, Required): Please enter the name of your team as it should appear on the t-shirt.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, Required): Enter the number of t-shirts you would like to order.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, Optional): Choose your preferred t-shirt size from the dropdown menu.
* **Color** (`color`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the color(s) you would like to have on your t-shirt.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note, Optional): If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, please type them in this field.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, Required): Enter the date you would like to order your t-shirt.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, Required): Click this button to submit your order.
