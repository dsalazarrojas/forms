# Hoodie Size Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hoodie Size Survey is designed to gather information from customers about their preferences and needs regarding hoodies, which can help inform product development and customer service decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions regarding size and color preferences.
2. If you have any additional comments or remarks, you can enter them in the "Note" field.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Size Survey** (select multiple): This is a multiple-choice question asking if you are interested in different sizes of hoodies.
  - Please select all sizes that you are interested in.
- **Hood Color** (select one): This question asks about your favorite hoodie color.
  - Choose the color that you like best.
- **Hoodie Size** (select one): This question asks if you are interested in hoodies of different sizes.
  - Please answer "Yes" if you are interested in hoodies of different sizes, and "No" if you are not.
- **Shirt Size** (select one): This question asks about your shirt size.
  - Please answer "Yes" if you are a certain size, and "No" if you are not.
- **Height** (text): This field is for you to enter your height.
  - Please enter your height in centimeters or inches.
- **Weight** (text): This field is for you to enter your weight.
  - Please enter your weight in kilograms or pounds.
- **Age** (number): This field is for you to enter your age.
  - Please enter your age.
- **Phone Number** (text): This field is for you to enter your phone number.
  - Please enter your phone number.
- **Email** (email): This field is for you to enter your email address.
  - Please enter your email address.
- **Note** (note): This field is for you to enter any comments or remarks.
  - You can enter any comments or remarks here.
- **Favorite Hoodie Color** (select one): This question asks about your favorite hoodie color.
  - Choose the color that you like best.
