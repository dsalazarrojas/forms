About GIC Forms

GIC Forms is a public library of YAML-based form templates designed for data collection workflows. The site combines a large repository of source templates with a curated layer of category pages and guide pages so visitors can identify stronger starting points instead of browsing raw filenames alone.

What The Site Publishes

  • YAML form templates and, when available, XLSForm files.
  • Live previews for reviewing schema structure before download.
  • Curated guide pages for higher-quality templates with clearer descriptions and review notes.
  • Category landing pages that help users compare related template types.

How To Use The Library

Use the browse experience to search broadly, then use the guide pages and live preview to decide whether a template is strong enough for adaptation. Every template should be reviewed and customized before publication, especially when it contains compliance, consent, or organization-specific wording.
