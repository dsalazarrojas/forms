# Community Regeneration Partner Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Community Regeneration Partner Contact Form is a form designed to collect information from potential partners interested in collaborating with our organization on community regeneration projects. This form will help us understand your organization's background, interests, and capabilities, which will inform our partnership decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's fields and ensure you understand what information we're seeking from you.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing all required fields.
3. Make sure to proofread your submission before submitting it to ensure there are no errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Partner Organization** (`partner_organization_name`, required): This is your organization's name.
* **Primary Contact Full Name** (`contact_full_name`, required): Enter your full name as the primary contact for your organization.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email_address`, required): This is your organization's email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone_number`, required): Enter your organization's phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type_selection`, required): Select the most suitable category from the list (Non-Profit, Local Government, Educational Institution, Private Business, Community Group, or Other).
* **Regeneration Focus Area(s)** (`regeneration_focus_selection`, required): Choose all relevant focus areas from the list that best match your organization's interests and capabilities (Infrastructure Development, Affordable Housing, Environmental Restoration, Job Training, Arts and Culture, Public Health, or Youth Services).
* **Partnership Interest Level** (`partnership_interest_level`, required): Select the level of partnership interest you have in working with us (Active Partnership, Resource Sharing, Funding Only, Volunteer Support, or Strategic Planning).
* **Brief Summary of Partnership Proposal** (`proposal_summary_text`, required): Describe how you envision our partnership and what you would like to achieve together.
* **Tell us about your past regeneration experience** (`past_experience_desc`, required): Share your organization's relevant experience in community regeneration projects.
* **Primary Target Demographics for Your Organization** (`target_demographics_info`, optional): Provide information about the demographics your organization primarily serves or targets.
* **Proposed Project Timeline** (`project_timeline_info`, required): Describe the proposed project timeline, e.g., 6 months, 1 year, or ongoing.
* **Does your organization offer matching funds for projects?** (`matching_funds_check`, required): Indicate whether your organization offers matching funds for projects.
* **Would you like to schedule a meeting to discuss your proposal?** (`meeting_request_intent`, required): Select if you're interested in meeting to discuss your proposal.
* **Proposal Submission Deadline (Note)** (`proposal_deadline_note`, optional): Note that proposals are reviewed on a rolling basis.
* **Today's Date** (`submission_date`, required): Ensure to enter today's date for reference purposes.
