# real_estate_client_information_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a real estate client for lead management and sales activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your first name in the field labeled "First name".
2. Enter your last name in the field labeled "Last name".
3. Provide your email address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "Phone".
5. Enter your address in the field labeled "Address".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number that can be used to reach you.
* **Address**: Enter your residential or business address where you can be reached.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* Please use this form only for real estate-related purposes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant team member or supervisor.
