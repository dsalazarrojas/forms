# Vehicle Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Information Request Form is designed to collect detailed information about a vehicle from the user. This form is used to gather both contact and vehicle details, ensuring that we can efficiently and accurately process the vehicle request.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" field. This will help us associate the vehicle information with your identity.
2. Provide the name of the vehicle you're interested in or own in the "Vehicle Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be our primary contact method.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This will help us contact you regarding the vehicle.
5. Enter the address where you can be reached in the "Address" field.
6. Specify the year of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Year" field.
7. Provide the make of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Make" field.
8. Enter the model of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Model" field.
9. Enter the color of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Color" field.
10. Describe the condition of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Condition" field.
11. Specify the type of vehicle (e.g., sedan, SUV, truck) in the "Vehicle Type" field.
12. Enter the price of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Price" field.
13. Select the vehicle's mileage range in the "Vehicle Year Range" field.
14. Enter the mileage of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Mileage" field.
15. Enter the type of engine in the "Vehicle Engine" field.
16. Specify the vehicle's transmission type in the "Vehicle Transmission" field.
17. Enter the number of doors in the "Vehicle Doors" field.
18. Describe the drivetrain of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Drivetrain" field.
19. Select the fuel type of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Fuel Type" field.
20. Select multiple colors of the vehicle in the "What color is the vehicle?" field.
21. Select the condition of the vehicle in the "What is the condition of the vehicle?" field.
22. Select multiple types of vehicles in the "What type of vehicle is it?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Vehicle Name** (`vehicle_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the vehicle you're interested in or own.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding the vehicle.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address where you can be reached.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Specify the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, text, optional): Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, text, optional): Describe the condition of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, text, optional): Specify the type of vehicle (e.g., sedan, SUV, truck).
* **Vehicle Price** (`vehicle_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year Range** (`vehicle_year_range`, select_one, optional): Select the vehicle's mileage range.
* **Vehicle Mileage** (`vehicle_mileage`, number, optional): Enter the mileage of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Engine** (`vehicle_engine`, text, optional): Enter the type of engine in the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Transmission** (`vehicle_transmission`, text, optional): Specify the vehicle's transmission type.
* **Vehicle Doors** (`vehicle_doors`, text, optional): Enter the number of doors in the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Drivetrain** (`vehicle_drivetrain`, text, optional): Describe the vehicle's drivetrain.
* **Vehicle Fuel Type** (`vehicle_fuel_type`, text, optional): Select the fuel type of the vehicle.
* **What color is the vehicle?** (`vehicle_color_options`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple colors of the vehicle.
* **What is the condition of the vehicle?** (`vehicle_condition_options`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple conditions of the vehicle.
* **What type of vehicle is it?** (`vehicle_type_options`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple types of vehicles.
