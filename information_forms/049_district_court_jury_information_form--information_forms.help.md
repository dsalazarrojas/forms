# District Court Jury Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "District Court Jury Information Form" is a critical document used to gather essential information from individuals who are required to serve on a jury in a district court setting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please refer to the instructions below:

1. **User Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
2. **Contact Address**: Provide your complete address, including street number and name, city, state, and zip code.
3. **Phone Number**: Enter your current phone number for contact purposes.
4. **Email Address**: Enter your current email address for contact purposes.
5. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
6. **Jury Service Start Date**: Select the date you are scheduled to begin your jury service.
7. **Jury Service End Date**: Select the date you are scheduled to end your jury service.
8. **Occupation**: Briefly describe your occupation or job title.
9. **Employer**: Enter your current employer or organization name.
10. **Work Address**: Enter your work address, including street number and name, city, state, and zip code.
11. **Work Email**: Enter your work email address.
12. **Work Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
13. **Jury Service Hours**: Select your regular work hours during which you will be available to serve on the jury.
14. **Conflict of Interest**: Declare if you have any conflicts of interest that may impact your ability to serve on the jury.
15. **Jury Service Type**: Confirm whether you are willing to serve on the jury.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required): Provide your complete address, including street number and name, city, state, and zip code.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your current phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your current email address for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Jury Service Start Date** (`jury_service_start_date`, date, required): Select the date you are scheduled to begin your jury service.
* **Jury Service End Date** (`jury_service_end_date`, date, required): Select the date you are scheduled to end your jury service.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, required): Briefly describe your occupation or job title.
* **Employer** (`employer`, text, required): Enter your current employer or organization name.
* **Work Address** (`work_address`, text, required): Enter your work address, including street number and name, city, state, and zip code.
* **Work Email** (`work_email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Jury Service Hours** (`jury_service_hours`, select_multiple, required): Select your regular work hours during which you will be available to serve on the jury.
* **Conflict of Interest** (`conflict_of_interest`, text, required): Declare if you have any conflicts of interest that may impact your ability to serve on the jury.
* **Jury Service Type** (`jury_service_type`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you are willing to serve on the jury.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you complete the form accurately and honestly.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact the court administration team for assistance.
* This form will be reviewed and verified before your jury service commences.
