# Soccer Player Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Soccer Player Information Form is a tool for collecting essential information about soccer players. This form is used to gather details such as player name, position, height, weight, date of birth, contact information, and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field as required.
2. Enter the player's name and position.
3. Enter the player's height and weight in metric units (e.g., 180 cm for height, 70 kg for weight).
4. Enter the player's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
5. Enter the player's contact number and emergency contact number (if applicable).
6. Enter the emergency contact's name and number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **player_name** (Text, Required): Enter the player's full name.
* **position** (Text, Optional): Enter the player's preferred position on the field (e.g., goalkeeper, midfielder, defender).
* **height** (Number, Optional): Enter the player's height in metric units (e.g., 180 cm).
* **weight** (Number, Optional): Enter the player's weight in metric units (e.g., 70 kg).
* **date_of_birth** (Date, Optional): Enter the player's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 1990-02-16).
* **contact_number** (Text, Optional): Enter the player's contact number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **emergency_contact** (Text, Optional): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **emergency_contact_number** (Text, Optional): Enter the emergency contact's number.
