# website_update_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from website owners or managers to submit website update requests. It helps us understand the updates you would like to have on your website, and how frequently you would like them to be performed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* Select the **update_priority** field to choose the priority level of the update (Medium, Low, or High).
	* Choose the **update_frequency** field to select how often you would like to have the website updated (One-time, Weekly, Bi-monthly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
2. Provide any additional details in the **update_info** field, including a description of the required changes.
3. Add any notes in the **update_notes** field.
4. Ensure you have filled out all required fields (marked with \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Website owner or manager** (`submitter_info`, text, optional): *Enter the name of the website's owner or manager.*
* **Description of the required changes** (`update_info`, text, optional): *Describe the changes you would like to make to your website.*
* **Contact email address** (`contact_info`, email, optional): *Enter your email address. Format: name@email.com*
* **Contact phone number** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): *Enter your phone number. Format: 123-456-7890 or international format with country code (e.g. +1 123 456 7890)*
* **Update priority** (`update_priority`, select_one, *): *Choose the priority level of the update (Medium, Low, or High)*
* **How often would you like to have the website updated** (`update_frequency`, select_multiple, *): *Select how often you would like to have the website updated (One-time, Weekly, Bi-monthly, Monthly, or Quarterly)*
* **Other (please specify below)** (`update_frequency_other`, text, optional): *If you have selected "Other" in the above field, enter the frequency here.*
* **Notes** (`update_notes`, note, optional): *Add any additional notes about the update request.*
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): *Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.*
* **Submission date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): *This field will be automatically populated.*
* **Submitter name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): *Enter your name.*
* **Submitter email address** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): *Enter your email address. Format: name@email.com*
* **Submitter phone number** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): *Enter your phone number. Format: 123-456-7890 or international format with country code (e.g. +1 123 456 7890)*
* **Submitter company** (`submitter_company`, text, optional): *Enter your company name or affiliation.*
* **Submitter title** (`submitter_title`, text, optional): *Enter your title or position.*
