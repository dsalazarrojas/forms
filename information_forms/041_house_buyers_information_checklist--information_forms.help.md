# house_buyers_information_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential house buyers about their preferences and requirements for their dream house. It will help identify the perfect match between buyers and sellers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply work through each page and fill out the relevant fields for your desired house. Be sure to consider your answers carefully, as they will be used to identify your ideal home match.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **House Price Range** (2): Select the price range for your dream house from "Low", "Medium", or "High".
* **House Size Range** (3): Choose the size range for your home from "Small", "Medium", or "Large".
* **House Age** (4): Determine how old the house would be. Choose "New", "Old", or "Very Old".
* **House Location** (5): Type in the location of the house you are looking for.
* **House Style** (6): Select the style of the house from "Modern", "Traditional", or "Farmhouse".
* **Number of Bedrooms** (7): Enter the number of bedrooms you need in your house.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (8): Enter the number of bathrooms you need in your house.
* **Number of Bedrooms Sq** (9): Enter the number of bedrooms in square feet.
* **Number of Bathrooms Sq** (10): Enter the number of bathrooms in square feet.
* **Lot Size** (11): Choose the lot size from "Small", "Medium", or "Large".
* **Lot Type** (12): Select the type of lot from "Flat", "Acre", or "Ranch".
* **Year Built** (13): Determine how old the house would be from "1-10 years ago", "10-20 years ago", or "More than 20 years ago".
* **Square Feet** (14): Enter the square footage of the house.
* **Lot Size Range Sq** (15): Select the lot size range from "Less than 500 sqft", "500-1000 sqft", "1000-2500 sqft", or "More than 2500 sqft".
* **House Features** (16): Select which features you want in your house, such as a "Pool", "Fireplace", "Patio", "Yard", or "View".
* **House Amenities** (17): Choose which amenities you want in your house, such as a "Kitchen", "Living Room", "Bathroom", "Bedroom", "Closet", "Laundry Room", "Dining Room", "Backyard", "Office", or "Play Area".
* **Home Type** (18): Select the type of home from "Single Family", "Condominium", "Townhouse", or "Mobile Home".
* **House Furniture** (19): Select the style of furniture you like from "Modern", "Traditional", "Rustic", "Country", or "Contemporary".
* **House Floorplan** (20): Choose the floor plan from "Single Story", "Multi Story", or "2 Story".
* **House Lot Size** (21): Select the lot size from "Small", "Medium", or "Large".
* **House Lot Type** (22): Select the type of lot from "Flat", "Acre", or "Acreage".
* **House Style Type** (23): Select the style of house from "Modern", "Traditional", "Colonial", or "Ranch".
* **House Furniture Color** (24): Select the color of furniture from "White", "Black", "Gray", "Blue", "Red", "Green".
* **House Model** (25): Select the house model from "Single", "Double", "Triple".

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers to ensure the best possible match.
* Consider your needs and priorities when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on a field, don't hesitate to ask.
