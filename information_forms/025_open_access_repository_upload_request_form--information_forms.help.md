# open_access_repository_upload_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The open_access_repository_upload_request_form is used to collect information from authors who want to submit their manuscripts for open access repository upload. This form helps gather essential details for the manuscript submission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required fields filled in accurately.
2. Provide your author details, including manuscript title, submission date, and submission time.
3. Select the repository where you want to submit your manuscript.
4. Choose the access rights for your manuscript.
5. Verify that your email and phone number are filled in accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manuscript Title (manuscript_title)**: This is the title of your manuscript. Please fill in the title accurately and briefly describe your research.
* **Manuscript File (manuscript_file)**: Upload your manuscript file here. Make sure the file is in the correct format and size.
* **Repository Selection (repository_selected)**: Select the repository where you want to submit your manuscript. Choose one from the list provided.
* **Access Rights (access_right)**: Choose the access rights for your manuscript. This determines how your manuscript can be used.
* **Submission Date (submission_date)**: Fill in the submission date accurately. This is the date when you want your manuscript to be submitted.
* **Submission Time (submission_time)**: Fill in the submission time accurately. This is the time when you want your manuscript to be submitted.
* **Notes (notes)**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or notes about your manuscript.
* **Author Email (author_email)**: Fill in your author email accurately. This is how we will contact you about your manuscript submission.
* **Author Phone (author_phone)**: Fill in your author phone number accurately. This is optional but helps us reach you quickly.
* **Email Sent (email_sent)**: This field is automatically filled in once we send you an email about your manuscript submission. Do not fill it in.

Note: Some fields are optional but it's recommended to fill them in to ensure that your manuscript submission is complete and accurate.
