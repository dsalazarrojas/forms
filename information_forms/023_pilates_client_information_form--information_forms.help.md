# Pilates Client Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pilates Client Information Form is a mandatory form that collects essential information about the client. Please ensure that you submit it accurately and thoroughly to avoid any miscommunication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide detailed information in each field, as we will use this information to tailor your Pilates sessions according to your needs.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid delays in processing your application.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Medical History Important Other
* **Medical History Important Other**: Please list any other medical conditions that are important to your health.

### Medical History Device
* **Medical History Device**: Please select any medical devices you are currently using, such as Hearing Aids, Prosthetics, or None.

### Emergency Contact Address 4
* **Emergency Contact Address 4**: Enter the address of your emergency contact, including the city, state, and postal code.

### Emergency Contact Address 5
* **Emergency Contact Address 5**: Enter the address of your emergency contact, including the city, state, and postal code.

### Emergency Contact Address 6
* **Emergency Contact Address 6**: Enter the address of your emergency contact, including the city, state, and postal code.

### Emergency Contact Relationship Other
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other**: Enter any other relationship between you and your emergency contact.

### Medical History Device Other
* **Medical History Device Other**: Please list any other medical devices that you are currently using, such as a pacemaker or implantable cardioverter.

### Medical History Important Other 2
* **Medical History Important Other 2**: List any other medical conditions that are important to your health.

### Emergency Contact City
* **Emergency Contact City**: Enter the city of your emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact State
* **Emergency Contact State**: Enter the state of your emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Postal Code
* **Emergency Contact Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of your emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Country
* **Emergency Contact Country**: Enter the country of your emergency contact.

Please note that some fields are optional and are indicated as such. If you are unsure about any field, please contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
