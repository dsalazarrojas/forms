# Artist Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and display information about artists for the purpose of booking and promoting their performances.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the **Personal Information** section with your real name, surname, and professional name (if applicable).
2. Enter your **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, and **Street Address** for contact purposes.
3. In the **Artist Profile** section, answer questions about your **Primary Genre**, **Artist Bio**, and **Years of Experience**.
4. Choose your **Primary Instrument** from the dropdown menu and add any additional **Secondary Instruments** you play.
5. Add any **Special Skills or Talents** you have.
6. Describe your **Musical Style or Approach** and **Performance Venues or Events**.
7. Provide **Links to Your Music**.
8. Select your **Availability for Performances** (if applicable).
9. Add any **Additional Information or Comments** about yourself and your music.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your real first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Your real surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Artist or Stage Name**: The name you want to be credited professionally.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth, if applicable.
* **Email Address**: Your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Street Address**: Your mailing address.
* **City**: The city where you reside.
* **Primary Genre**: The main music genre you perform.
* **Secondary Genres**: Any other genres you work with.
* **Years of Experience**: How long have you been performing?
* **Artist Biography**: A brief description of yourself and your music.
* **Primary Instrument**: Choose your main instrument from the dropdown menu.
* **Secondary Instruments**: List any other instruments you play.
* **Special Skills or Talents**: Any unique abilities or talents you have.
* **Musical Style or Approach**: Describe your unique style or approach to music.
* **Performance Venues or Events**: Where have you performed?
* **Links to Your Music**: Provide links to your music streaming platforms.
* **Availability for Performances**: Select your availability for performances.
* **Additional Information or Comments**: Any other relevant information about yourself and your music.
