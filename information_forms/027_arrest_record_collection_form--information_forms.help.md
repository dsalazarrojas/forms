# Arrest Record Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Arrest Record Collection Form is used to gather information about an individual's arrest history. This form is crucial for official or personal purposes, such as background checks, employment screening, or other legitimate investigations. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to ensure that the submitted information is reliable and valid.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the individual's **Full Legal Name**, which should match the name on their government-issued ID.
2. Enter the **Date of Birth**, which can be found on their birth certificate or passport.
3. If applicable, provide the **Driver's License Number** or **ID Number or Badge Number**.
4. If the individual has been arrested, complete the **Arrest 1 - Date of Arrest**, **Arrest 1 - Arresting Agency**, **Arrest 1 - Charge or Offense**, and **Arrest 1 - Disposition** fields. For subsequent arrests, repeat the same fields with the corresponding numbers (e.g., **Arrest 2 - Date of Arrest**, **Arrest 2 - Arresting Agency**, etc.).
5. Answer the **Were any arrests violence-related?** question with either **True**, **False**, or **Unsure**. If yes, provide additional details if possible.
6. Answer the **Were any arrests drug-related?** question with either **True**, **False**, or **Unsure**. If yes, provide additional details if possible.
7. Answer the **Any periods of incarceration?** question with either **True**, **False**, or **Currently incarcerated**.
8. If applicable, provide the **Details of Incarceration** field with the relevant information.
9. Answer the **Currently on Parole or Probation?** question with either **Yes - probation**, **Yes - parole**, or **Yes - both**.
10. If applicable, provide the **Parole or Probation Details** field with the relevant information.
11. Sign and date the **Date of Certification**, indicating that you certify the accuracy and completeness of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Legal Name**: Your full name, as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth**: The date of your birth, as recorded on your birth certificate or passport.
* **Driver's License Number**: Your driver's license number, if applicable.
* **ID Number or Badge Number**: Alternative identification numbers, if applicable.
* **Arrest 1 - Date of Arrest**: The date of the first arrest.
* **Arrest 1 - Arresting Agency**: The law enforcement agency responsible for the first arrest.
* **Arrest 1 - Charge or Offense**: The charge or offense related to the first arrest.
* **Arrest 1 - Disposition**: The outcome of the first arrest (e.g., convicted, acquitted, dismissed, deferred, or unknown).
* **Arrest 2 - Date of Arrest**: The date of the second arrest, if applicable.
* **Arrest 2 - Arresting Agency**: The law enforcement agency responsible for the second arrest, if applicable.
* **Arrest 2 - Charge or Offense**: The charge or offense related to the second arrest, if applicable.
* **Arrest 2 - Disposition**: The outcome of the second arrest, if applicable (e.g., convicted, acquitted, dismissed, deferred, or unknown).
* **Were any arrests violence-related?**: Answer with **True** if the arrest was related to violence, **False** if not, or **Unsure** if unsure.
* **Were any arrests drug-related?**: Answer with **True** if the arrest was related to drugs, **False** if not, or **Unsure** if unsure.
* **Any periods of incarceration**: Answer with **True** if you have been incarcerated, **False** if not, or **Currently incarcerated** if you are currently imprisoned.
* **Details of Incarceration**: Additional information about your periods of incarceration, if applicable.
* **Currently on Parole or Probation**: Answer with **Yes - probation** if on probation, **Yes - parole** if on parole, or **Yes - both** if on both.
* **Parole or Probation Details**: Additional information about your parole or probation status, if applicable.
* **Date of Certification**: Sign and date this field, indicating that you certify the accuracy and completeness of the information provided.
* **I certify the information above is accurate and complete**: Answer with **True** if the information is accurate, or **False** if it is not.
