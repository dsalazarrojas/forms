# Smart Metering Initiative Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants in the Smart Metering Initiative. Please note that this form is for informational purposes only and does not imply any form of affiliation or commitment to the initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your Participant Details (Page 1).
2. Confirm your participation by ticking the box for Participant Consent (Page 2).
3. Specify your Participant Preferences (Page 3).
4. Share your Contact Preferences (Page 4).
5. Review your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required false): Please provide your name and any other relevant identifying information.

### Participant Consent
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, text, required false): This field is used to confirm your participation in the Smart Metering Initiative. Please indicate your agreement to share your data and preferences with us.

### Participant Preferences
* **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, text, required false): Please specify any specific preferences you have regarding communication or data sharing. This will help us tailor our approach to your needs.

### Contact Preferences
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, text, required false): Here, you can specify how you would like to be contacted, e.g., phone, email, or mail.
