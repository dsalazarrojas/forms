# Information Lookup Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request or inquire about information. It seems to be for users who want to know something, but more context would be needed for accurate interpretation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the "Information Lookup Request" question with either "Yes" or "No" depending on your need.
2. If yes, please provide your name, surname, email, phone number, date of birth, and time of birth if applicable.
3. You can add some extra information about what you're looking for in the "Notes" field.
4. Select the type of information you're requesting ("Yes" or "No") and the department you're inquiring about ("Yes" or "No") if applicable.
5. Submit the form to complete your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Information Lookup Request** (Select Multiple):
	+ Choose "Yes" if you're requesting information, "No" otherwise.
* **Name** (Text, Optional):
	+ Please enter your name (e.g., John Doe).
* **Surname** (Text, Optional):
	+ Please enter your surname (e.g., Doe).
* **Email** (Email, Optional):
	+ Enter your email address (e.g., johndoe@example.com).
* **Phone Number** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter your phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Date Of Birth** (Date, Optional):
	+ Select your date of birth (e.g., 1990-01-01).
* **Time Of Birth** (Time, Optional):
	+ Select your time of birth (e.g., 00:00).
* **Notes** (Note, Optional):
	+ Add any extra information you'd like to provide about your request.
* **Information Requested** (Text, Optional):
	+ Please provide any extra information about what you are looking for.
* **Information Type** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Choose "Yes" if you're looking for specific information, "No" otherwise.
* **Department** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Choose "Yes" if you're inquiring about something related to a department, "No" otherwise.
