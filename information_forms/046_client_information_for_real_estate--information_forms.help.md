# Client Information For Real Estate - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential client information for real estate purposes. It helps us understand your needs and preferences, enabling us to provide you with personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Enter your contact details (First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Current Address).
3. Specify your role as a Buyer, Seller, Both, Investor, Renter, or Just Looking.
4. Indicate your property type of interest (Single Family Home, Condo or Townhouse, Multi-Family, Commercial, Land, Luxury Property, or Investment Property).
5. Enter your price range (minimum and maximum).
6. Select your desired location or neighborhood.
7. Specify the number of bedrooms and bathrooms needed.
8. Enter your desired square footage (if applicable).
9. List your must-have features and nice-to-have features (if any).
10. Choose your desired move-in timeline and financing status.
11. Optionally, enter your preferred lender name and current home status.
12. Indicate if you have a preferred agent or not.
13. Specify how you heard about us (if applicable).
14. Add any additional comments or questions.
15. Finally, select the form date (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number.
* **Current Address**: Enter your complete mailing address.
* **City**: Enter the city where you currently reside or where you want to live.
* **State**: Enter the state where you currently reside or where you want to live.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the zip code of your current or desired location.
* **Are You a Buyer or Seller?**: Select your role (Buyer, Seller, Both, Investor, Renter, or Just Looking).
* **Property Type of Interest**: Select the type of property you are looking for (Single Family Home, Condo or Townhouse, Multi-Family, Commercial, Land, Luxury Property, or Investment Property).
* **Minimum Price Range**: Enter the lowest price you are willing to pay.
* **Maximum Price Range**: Enter the highest price you are willing to pay.
* **Desired Location or Neighborhood**: Enter the location or neighborhood you prefer.
* **Number of Bedrooms Needed**: Enter the minimum number of bedrooms you require.
* **Number of Bathrooms Needed**: Enter the minimum number of bathrooms you require.
* **Desired Square Footage**: Enter the minimum size you prefer (if applicable).
* **Must-Have Features**: List any essential features you need in your new home.
* **Nice-to-Have Features**: List any desirable features you would like to have.
* **When Do You Plan to Move?**: Select your desired move-in timeline (Immediately, Within 1 Month, 1-3 Months, 3-6 Months, 6-12 Months, or Just Browsing).
* **Financing Status**: Select your pre-approval status (Pre-Approved, Pre-Qualified, Need Pre-Approval, Cash Buyer, or Not Sure).
* **Lender Name**: Enter the name of your preferred lender (if applicable).
* **Current Home Status**: Indicate your current home status (Own - Need to Sell First, Own - Will Rent, Own - Will Keep, Currently Renting, or Other).
* **Do You Have a Preferred Agent?**: Select if you have a preferred agent (True, False, or Not Sure).
* **How Did You Hear About Us?**: Select how you heard about us (Website, Social Media, Referral, Sign, Advertisement, Open House, or Other).
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Form Date**: Enter the date you filled out this form (if applicable).
