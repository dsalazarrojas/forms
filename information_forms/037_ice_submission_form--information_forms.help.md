# ICE Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The ICE Submission Form is used to report incidents, events, or concerns that have occurred within the organization. This form is essential for documenting and tracking incidents, enabling the Incident Response Team to assess, investigate, and manage responses effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the Incident Type**: Choose the type of incident from the options provided (Concern, Incident, or Event).
2. Fill out your First Name, Email, and Phone Number sections.
3. Provide your Address and Department information (if applicable).
4. Enter the Report Date and Incident Date and Time (if known).
5. Enter any Incident Location information (if applicable).
6. Enter any additional comments or notes about the incident.
7. Upload any attachments related to the incident.
8. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your work address.
* **Incident Type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of incident from the options provided (Concern, Incident, or Event).
* **Incident Description** (text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Submitter Info** (text, optional): Enter additional information about the submitter.
* **Submitter Department** (select_one, required): Choose your department from the options provided (Accounting, IT, Operations, or Other).
* **Submitter Position** (select_multiple, optional): Choose your position within the department (Manager, Assistant Manager, Director, or Other).
* **Submitter Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Address** (text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Report Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the report.
* **Incident Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Incident Time Note** (text, optional): Enter any additional details about the incident time.
* **Submitter Time** (time, optional): Enter the time at which the submitter reported the incident.
* **Submitter Time Note** (text, optional): Enter any additional details about the submitter's time.
* **Submitter Location** (text, optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (text, optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Submitter Department Location** (text, optional): Enter the department location.
* **Submitter Comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the incident.
* **Submitter Notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the incident.
* **Submitter Other** (text, optional): Enter any other relevant information about the incident.
* **Submitter Attachments** (text, optional): Upload any attachments related to the incident.
