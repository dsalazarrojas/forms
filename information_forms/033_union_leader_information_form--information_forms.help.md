# Union Leader Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Union Leader Information Form is used to collect personal and professional details from union leaders, such as their full name, email, role, phone number, and other relevant attributes. This information is used for internal purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your full name in the **Full Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Select your role from the list of options in the **Role** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select the date of your birth from the calendar.
6. Select the date you were hired from the calendar.
7. Fill in the address fields **Address 1**, **Address 2**, and **Address 3** with the corresponding information.
8. Answer the yes/no questions with the corresponding options:
	* Do you have a car? (**union_leader_information_form_10**)
	* Do you have any other benefits? (**union_leader_information_form_11**)
	* Do you have a family? (**union_leader_information_form_12**)
	* Do you live with anyone else? (**union_leader_information_form_13**)
	* Do you have a partner? (**union_leader_information_form_14**)
	* Do you have any children? (**union_leader_information_form_15**)
	* Do you live with children? (**union_leader_information_form_16**)
	* Do you have a pet? (**union_leader_information_form_17**)
	* Do you have any other dependants? (**union_leader_information_form_18**)
	* Do you have a disability? (**union_leader_information_form_19**)
	* Do you have any other medical conditions? (**union_leader_information_form_20**)
	* Do you have any other health conditions? (**union_leader_information_form_21**)
