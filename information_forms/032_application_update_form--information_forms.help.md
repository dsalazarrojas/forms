# Application Update Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Application Update Form is used to update customer or account information, including contact details, addresses, payment methods, subscription plans, and notification settings. It helps to ensure that the customer's account data is up-to-date and accurate, allowing our team to provide better service and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your Full Name.
2.  Enter your Customer or Account ID.
3.  Enter your Email Address.
4.  Enter your Phone Number.
5.  Select the type of information you are updating (e.g., Contact information, Address, etc.).
6.  Enter the Current Address (if updating address information).
7.  Enter the New Address (if updating address information).
8.  Select whether you want to update your payment method.
9.  Select your new payment method (if updating payment method).
10.  Select whether you want to update your subscription plan.
11.  Select your new subscription plan (if updating subscription plan).
12.  Select whether you want to update your notification preferences.
13.  Select the types of notifications you want to receive.
14.  Optionally, enter a Reason for update.
15.  Optionally, enter any Additional notes or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer or Account ID** (`customer_id`, `text`, required): Enter your customer or account ID, as provided by our service.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **What information are you updating?** (`information_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply (e.g., Contact information, Address, etc.).
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your current address if you're updating your address information.
* **New Address** (`new_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your new address if you're updating your address information.
* **Update payment method?** (`update_payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you want to update your payment method.
* **New Payment Method** (`new_payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select your new payment method (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
* **Update subscription plan?** (`update_subscription`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you want to update your subscription plan.
* **New Subscription Plan** (`new_subscription_plan`, `select_one`, optional): Select your new subscription plan (e.g., Basic, Standard, etc.).
* **Update notification preferences?** (`notification_preferences`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you want to update your notification preferences.
* **Notification settings** (`notification_settings`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the types of notifications you want to receive (e.g., Email, SMS, etc.).
* **Reason for update** (`reason_for_update`, `text`, optional): Enter a reason for the update (if applicable).
* **Additional notes or requests** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or requests you may have.
