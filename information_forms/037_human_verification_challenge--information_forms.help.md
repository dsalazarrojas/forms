# Human Verification Challenge - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human Verification Challenge form is designed to verify that users are human and not bots or automated scripts. It checks the user's email address and has a simple math challenge to ensure they can interact with the system in a human-like way.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your email address in the "Enter Email" field.
2. Enter the correct answer to the math challenge in the "Answer" field.
3. Select the correct code from the list in the "Verify Captcha" field.
4. If you fail the math challenge, you will be prompted to re-complete it until you succeed.
5. Once you've completed the math challenge and selected the correct code, click submit to proceed with the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Enter Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address to continue with the form.
- **Answer** (text, optional): Enter the correct answer to the simple math challenge. The hint provided will guide you on what to enter.
- **Verify Captcha** (select one, optional): Select the correct code from the list to complete the verification challenge.
- **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
- **Enter Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.

Note: The phone number field is not used in this form and can be ignored.
