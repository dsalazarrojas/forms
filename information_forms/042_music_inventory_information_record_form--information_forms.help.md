# Music Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Inventory Information Record Form is designed to collect data about music records, allowing you to track and manage your music collection efficiently. This form gathers essential information, such as title, music genre, album details, artist, record label, release date, and price.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Review the form**: Before starting, review each section carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information and to prevent duplicate entries.
2. **Fill out each field**: Complete each section as instructed below, providing accurate and concise information.
3. **Verify your answers**: Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1: Music Inventory Information** (`music_inventory_information_record_form_page_1`, text, optional):
	+ This section is the starting point for your music record information.
	+ Please provide a brief description of the music record.
* **Music** (`music_inventory_information_record_form_page_2`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the title of the music record.
* **Genre** (`music_inventory_information_record_form_page_3`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the music genre of the record from the available options: Rock, Pop, or Jazz.
* **Album** (`music_inventory_information_record_form_page_4`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the album name or title.
* **Artist** (`music_inventory_information_record_form_page_5`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the artist or performer's name.
* **Record** (`music_inventory_information_record_form_page_6`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the record label name.
* **Release** (`music_inventory_information_record_form_page_7`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the release date of the music record.
* **Label** (`music_inventory_information_record_form_page_8`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the label name.
* **Price** (`music_inventory_information_record_form_page_9`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the price of the music record.
* **Additional Music Genres** (`music_inventory_information_record_form_page_10`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select any additional music genres that apply to the record (Classical, Country, Instrumental).
* **Additional Information** (`music_inventory_information_record_form_page_11`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional information about the music record.

Note: Fields marked as "optional" can be skipped if not applicable.
