# Former Employee Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from former employees who have left the company. The purpose of this form is to gather details about their reasons for leaving, which helps the company to understand the root causes of employee turnover and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form sections: The form is divided into sections, each with a specific set of questions. Please review each section carefully to understand what information is being requested.
2. Answer each question: For each question, carefully read the label and options (if provided) and provide a clear and concise answer.
3. Submit the form: Once you have completed all the necessary questions, click the submit button to save your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Section 1** (no label provided): This is the first section of the form. It serves as an introduction to the form and is not a required field.
* **employee_name_label** (employee_name, text, not required): Please enter the name of the employee who is providing information about their departure from the company.
* **reason_for_leaving_label** (reason_for_leaving, select_one, not required): Select the reason for leaving from the provided options. Common reasons include:
	+ poor compensation
	+ lack of challenge
	+ poor management
* **job_did_not_fit_label** (job_did_not_fit, select_multiple, not required): Select all that apply from the following options:
	+ lack of growth opportunities
	+ lack of challenge
	+ no match with team
* **company_values_label** (company_values, select_multiple, not required): Select all that apply from the following options:
	+ no clear goals
	+ poor communication
	+ conflicting priorities
* **employee_feedback_label** (employee_feedback, text, not required): Please provide any additional feedback or comments about their experience.
* **manager_name_label** (manager_name, text, not required): If applicable, please provide the name of their direct manager.
* **last_date_worked_label** (last_date_worked, date, not required): Enter the date on which their employment ended.
* **last_date_terminating_label** (last_date_terminating, date, not required): Enter the date on which their employment was officially terminated (if different from last_date_worked).

## Tips
* Please answer each question accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any question, please provide a brief explanation or request clarification.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in the employee_feedback field.
