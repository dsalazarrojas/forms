# Market Update Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Update Form Template is used to collect information from users for market updates. This form is meant to be filled out by internal stakeholders who need to provide updates on the market.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organization** (`organization`, text, false):
This field is where you can enter the name of the organization.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, true\*):
Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the market update.
- **Email** (`email`, email, true\*):
Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, true\*):
Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Market Update** (`market_update`, text, true\*):
Enter the market update information.
- **Market Trends** (`market_trends`, text, false):
This field is optional and you can enter any market trend information you have.
- **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, true\*):
Select all that apply for the category of the market update.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the information is up-to-date and accurate.
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid mistakes.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the internal stakeholders for clarification.
