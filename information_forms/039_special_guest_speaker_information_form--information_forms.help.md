# Special Guest Speaker Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about special guest speakers for events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the event in the "Event Name" field.
2. Enter the speaker's first name in the "Speaker First Name" field.
3. Enter the speaker's last name in the "Speaker Last Name" field.
4. Enter the speaker's title in the "Speaker Title" field.
5. Enter a brief biography of the speaker in the "Speaker Biography" field.
6. Enter the speaker's organization in the "Speaker Organization" field.
7. Enter the speaker's email address in the "Speaker Email" field.
8. Enter the event's date in the "Event Date" field.
9. Enter the event's start time in the "Event Start Time" field.
10. Enter the event's end time in the "Event End Time" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event, e.g., "Annual Conference 2023".
*   **Speaker First Name**: Enter the first name of the speaker, e.g., "John".
*   **Speaker Last Name**: Enter the last name of the speaker, e.g., "Doe".
*   **Speaker Title**: Enter the title of the speaker, e.g., "Dr./Professor".
*   **Speaker Biography**: Enter a brief description of the speaker, e.g., "John Doe is a renowned expert in the field of artificial intelligence."
*   **Speaker Organization**: Enter the name of the speaker's organization, e.g., "University of XYZ".
*   **Speaker Email**: Enter the speaker's email address, e.g., "johndoe@example.com".
*   **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event, e.g., "2023-02-15".
*   **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event, e.g., "10:00 AM".
*   **Event End Time**: Enter the end time of the event, e.g., "11:00 AM".

## Tips
*   Make sure to double-check the email address of the speaker to ensure it is accurate.
*   If the event date or time is not fixed, you can leave the fields empty and update them later.
