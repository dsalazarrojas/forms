# Therapist Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapist Information Form is designed to collect data about clients and their therapists. This form is meant to be used for tracking and reference purposes, ensuring that accurate and up-to-date information is available for each client and therapist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client information, including name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Enter the therapist's name, email, phone number, and address.
3. Select the start and end dates of the therapy sessions.
4. Add any additional notes about the client and therapist (e.g., notes about appointments, progress, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the client.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required: false): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, required: false): Enter the client's address.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the client.
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text, required: false): Enter the therapist's name.
* **Therapist Email** (`therapist_email`, email, required: false): Enter the therapist's email address.
* **Therapist Phone** (`therapist_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the therapist's phone number.
* **Therapist Address** (`therapist_address`, text, required: false): Enter the therapist's address.
* **Therapist Notes** (`therapist_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the therapist.
* **Therapy Start Date** (`therapy_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the therapy sessions.
* **Therapy End Date** (`therapy_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the therapy sessions.
