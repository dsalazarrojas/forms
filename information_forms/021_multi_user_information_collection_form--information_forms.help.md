# Multi User Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from multiple users, likely for internal purposes such as data collection or analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Multi User Information** field, which is a text field where you can enter a brief description of the user.
2. Enter the primary contact information in the **Primary Contact** field, including any relevant details such as name, phone number, and email.
3. Provide user information in the **User Information 1** to **User Information 10** fields, each with a unique text field for capturing specific details about the user.
4. Select the primary purpose of the user in the **Primary Purpose** field, choosing from options such as Sales, Marketing, or Other.
5. Enter the company name in the **Company** field.
6. Enter the department name in the **Department** field.
7. Enter the title in the **Title** field.
8. Enter the email address in the **Email** field.
9. Enter the phone number in the **Phone** field.
10. Enter the address in the **Address** field.
11. Add any additional notes in the **Notes** field.
12. Enter any additional user notes in the **User Notes 1** to **User Notes 4** fields.
13. Finally, select the **Submit** option from the drop-down menu or choose **Other** to submit the form.
