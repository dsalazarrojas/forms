# Teacher Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Information Form is designed to collect basic contact and job information about a teacher.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it is known to you or your family and friends.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it is known to you or your family and friends.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached. Please note that we will not be using this information for any other purposes other than what is specified in this form.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title or designation at the institution or organization you work with.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- You can use a valid email address that you check regularly.
- This form is for general information purposes only. If you need any further assistance, do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
