# Spouse Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your spouse, including their personal details, employment status, and contact information. Please ensure you have your spouse's permission to provide this information before completing the form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your spouse's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your spouse's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your spouse's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter your spouse's date of marriage in the "Date of Marriage" field.
5. Enter your spouse's address in the "Address" field.
6. Select your spouse's employment status from the "Spouse Employment Status" dropdown list.
7. Enter your spouse's occupation in the "Spouse Occupation" field.
8. Enter your spouse's income in the "Spouse Income" field.
9. Enter your spouse's employer's name in the "Spouse Employer" field.
10. Enter your spouse's phone number in the "Spouse Phone" field.
11. Enter your spouse's email address in the "Spouse Email" field.
12. Select your spouse's relationship status from the "Spouse Relationship Status" dropdown list.
13. If necessary, add any additional notes about your spouse in the "Spouse Note" field.
14. If you are a part of a shared household, select "True" or "False" in the "Household Verification" field, depending on whether you share a household with your spouse.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your spouse's first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your spouse's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (3, date, required): Enter your spouse's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Date of Marriage** (4, date, required): Enter your spouse's date of marriage in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Address** (5, text, required): Enter your spouse's home address.
* **Occupation** (6, text, required): Enter your spouse's occupation.
* **Email** (7, email, required): Enter your spouse's email address.
* **Phone** (8, text, required): Enter your spouse's phone number.
* **Social Security Number** (9, text, required): Enter your spouse's Social Security Number.
* **Passport Number** (10, text, required): Enter your spouse's passport number.
* **Passport Expiration Date** (11, date, required): Enter your spouse's passport expiration date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Passport Country of Issue** (12, text, required): Enter the country that issued your spouse's passport.
* **Spouse First Name** (13, text, required): Enter your spouse's first name.
* **Spouse Last Name** (14, text, required): Enter your spouse's last name.
* **Spouse Date of Birth** (15, date, required): Enter your spouse's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Spouse Date of Marriage** (16, date, required): Enter your spouse's date of marriage in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Spouse Address** (17, text, required): Enter your spouse's home address.
* **Spouse Employment Status** (18, select_one, required): Select your spouse's employment status from the dropdown list.
* **Spouse Occupation** (19, text, required): Enter your spouse's occupation.
* **Spouse Income** (20, number, required): Enter your spouse's annual income.
* **Spouse Employer** (21, text, required): Enter your spouse's employer's name.
* **Spouse Phone** (22, text, required): Enter your spouse's phone number.
* **Spouse Email** (23, email, required): Enter your spouse's email address.
* **Spouse Relationship Status** (24, select_one, required): Select your spouse's relationship status from the dropdown list.
* **Spouse Note** (25, note, required): Add any additional notes about your spouse.
* **Household Verification** (26, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you are a part of a shared household with your spouse.

Note: The required fields are marked as such, but please ensure you have your spouse's permission to provide this information before completing the form.
