# Before And After School Care Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's care needs and preferences for the before and after school care program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the child's needs are met, and the program can provide the best possible care.
2. Make sure to complete all the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. If a field is marked as optional, you can choose to provide information or skip it if it's not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`parent_email`, email, required): Please enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Please enter your full street address.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, required): Please enter your child's full name.
* **Child Date of Birth** (`child_dob`, date, required): Please enter your child's date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Child Age** (`child_age`, number, required): Please enter your child's current age.
* **Child Gender** (`child_gender`, select_one, required): Please select your child's gender (Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say, Other).
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the school your child attends.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Please select your child's current grade level (Kindergarten, 1st Grade, 2nd Grade, 3rd Grade, 4th Grade, 5th Grade, 6th Grade, 7th Grade, 8th Grade).
* **Type of Care Needed** (`care_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of care needed (Before School Only, After School Only, Before and After School, Full Day (No School Days), Occasional or As Needed).
* **Before School Days** (`before_school_days`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the days your child will need before-school care (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Not Applicable).
* **After School Days** (`after_school_days`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the days your child will need after-school care (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Not Applicable).
* **Desired Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Please enter the desired start date for care.
* **Before School Arrival Time** (`before_school_arrival`, time, optional): Please enter the time your child will arrive at the program.
* **After School Pickup Time** (`after_school_pickup`, time, optional): Please enter the time your child will be picked up from the program.
* **Transportation Needed** (`transportation_needed`, select_one, required): Please select if you need transportation from school (Yes from school to program, Yes from program to school, No we will transport, Child walks).
* **Authorized Pickup Persons** (`authorized_pickup`, text, required): Please list all authorized pickup persons for your child.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Please list any medical conditions or allergies your child has.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): Please list any medications your child takes.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): Please list any food allergies or restrictions your child has.
* **Snack Provided** (`snack_provided`, select_one, required): Please select if the program will provide snacks (We will provide daily, Program should provide, Child brings own, No snack needed).
* **Emergency Contact 1** (`emergency_contact_1`, text, required): Please enter the name and phone number of the emergency contact 1.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (`emergency_contact_2`, text, required): Please enter the name and phone number of the emergency contact 2.
* **Physician Information** (`physician_info`, text, optional): Please enter the doctor's name and phone number.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`, text, optional): Please enter the provider and policy number.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations** (`special_needs`, text, optional): Please list any special needs or accommodations your child may have.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard`, select_one, optional): Please select how you heard about the program (School Referral, Friend or Family, Online Search, Social Media, Advertisement, Drive By, Other).
* **Previous Childcare Experience** (`previous_care`, select_one, required): Please select if your child has had previous childcare experience (Yes current program, Yes previous program, No first time).
* **Terms and Conditions Acceptance** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): Please accept the program's terms and conditions (I accept, I do not accept).

## Tips

* Please make sure to complete all the required fields accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the program administrators.
* The information collected on this form will help the program provide the best possible care for your child.
* Please make sure to update your information regularly to ensure that we have the most up-to-date information about your child's needs.
