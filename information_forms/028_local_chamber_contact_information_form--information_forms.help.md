# Local Chamber Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Local Chamber Contact Information Form is used to collect and update contact information for local chamber members. This form helps the local chamber maintain accurate and up-to-date member records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required and optional fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to check the "Local Chamber Member" field to confirm your membership status.
3. Select your member position(s) from the "Member Position" field if applicable.
4. Double-check your contact information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Please fill out a brief description of your contact information, such as a brief introduction or a note about your business.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your business email address or a contact email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your business phone number or a contact number that you check regularly.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your business address or a contact address that you receive mail at.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter the city where your business is located or where you can be reached.
* **State** (`state`, text, required: false): Enter the state where your business is located or where you can be reached.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, required: false): Enter the zip code of your business location or where you can be reached.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required: false): Enter the country where your business is located or where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth for verification purposes. Please use the standard date format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required: false): Enter your time of birth for verification purposes. Please use the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Local Chamber Member** (`local_chamber_member`, select_one, required: false): Check 'Yes' if you are a local chamber member.
* **Member Position** (`member_position`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all positions that apply to you as a member, such as 'Yes', 'No', 'Board Member', 'Committee Member', etc.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person for your business or organization.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the local chamber staff for assistance.
