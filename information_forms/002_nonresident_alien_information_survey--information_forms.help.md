# Nonresident Alien Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonresident Alien Information Survey is a form designed to collect essential details about nonresident aliens visiting or living in a particular region. This form will help collect accurate and complete information for internal purposes related to immigration, visa, and stay procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your citizenship status from the available options (US citizen, Non-US citizen, Resident alien, or Other).
2. Choose your current visa status from the available options (Valid visa, Invalid visa, or No visa).
3. Provide the purpose of your stay (Business, Tourism, Study, or Work).
4. Fill in the date and time of your arrival.
5. Enter your expected departure date.
6. Choose the reason for your visit (Business, Tourism, Study, or Work).
7. Enter a brief reason for your visit.
8. Select your country of residence (US or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Citizenship** (`citizen_ship`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your current citizenship status. This will help us understand your background for immigration and stay purposes.

  * US citizen
  * Non-US citizen
  * Resident alien
  * Other

- **Visa Status** (`visa_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your current visa status. This will help us verify your entry and stay details.

  * Valid visa
  * Invalid visa
  * No visa

- **Purpose of Stay** (`purpose_of_stay`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the purpose of your stay. This will aid in understanding your intentions and plans.

  * Please enter your purpose of stay in the space provided.

- **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your arrival in the region. This will help us track your entry and stay history.

  * Format: MM/DD/YYYY

- **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of your arrival. This will help us track your arrival timing.

  * Format: HH:MM

- **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your planned departure from the region. This will aid in scheduling and planning purposes.

  * Format: MM/DD/YYYY

- **Purpose of Visit** (`purpose_of_visit`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the reason for your visit. This will help us understand your intentions and plan accordingly.

  * Business
  * Tourism
  * Study
  * Work

- **Reason of Visit** (`reason_of_visit`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for your visit. This will aid in understanding your intentions and goals.

  * Please enter a brief description of your reason for visit.

- **Country of Residence** (`country_of_residence`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your country of residence. This will help us understand your background and stay details.

  * US
  * Other
