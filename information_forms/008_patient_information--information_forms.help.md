# patient information - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect basic patient information for medical records. The form will help you gather essential details about the patient, including their name, contact number, address, and date of birth. This information will aid healthcare professionals in providing effective and personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
3. Provide the patient's address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field using the standard date format (DD/MM/YY).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name (First and Last name) to help healthcare professionals identify them correctly.
- **Contact Number**: Type in the patient's phone number or other contact method (e.g., email) so we can keep them informed about their care.
- **Address**: Provide the patient's address for any follow-up or correspondence purposes.
- **Date of Birth**: Input the patient's date of birth in the standard DD/MM/YY format to help us keep accurate patient records.
