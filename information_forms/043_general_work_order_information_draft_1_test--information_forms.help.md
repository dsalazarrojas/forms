# general_work_order_information_draft_1_test - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the General Work Order Information Draft 1 Test form, which is used to collect basic customer and work order information for internal purposes, such as testing or pilot runs. This form is not required for production use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "customer" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "customer_email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "customer_phone" field.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "work_order_type" field, as this indicates whether the work order is of a certain type.
5. Enter the date of the work order in the "date" field.
6. Enter the time of the work order in the "time" field.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "equipment_segment" field, as this indicates whether the work order involves specific equipment.
8. Add any additional notes about the work order in the "customer_notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer (customer, text, optional)**: Enter your name as the customer.
* **customer_email (customer_email, email, optional)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **customer_phone (customer_phone, text, optional)**: Provide your phone number for any additional contact.
* **work_order_type (work_order_type, select_one, optional)**: Indicate if the work order is of a specific type ("Yes" or "No").
* **date (date, date, optional)**: Enter the date of the work order.
* **time (time, time, optional)**: Enter the time of the work order.
* **equipment_segment (equipment_segment, select_multiple, optional)**: Indicate if the work order involves specific equipment ("Yes" or "No").
* **customer_notes (customer_notes, text, optional)**: Add any additional notes about the work order.
