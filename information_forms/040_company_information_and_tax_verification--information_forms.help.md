# Company Information And Tax Verification - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Company Information And Tax Verification form is designed to collect and verify essential company details for legitimate purposes. This form is intended to ensure accuracy and legitimacy of the submitted data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the company name as per the legal registered name.
2. Provide the tax identification number as required by your local authorities.
3. Enter the name of the contact person for verification and follow-up purposes.
4. Enter the email address of the contact person for sending verification requests.
5. Optionally, provide the business address if applicable.
6. Summarize the attached supporting documents, indicating what they are and why they support your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company name** (Company name, text, required): Enter the exact name of your company as registered with the local authorities.
- **Tax identification number** (Tax identification number, text, required): This is the VAT, EIN, or local tax ID assigned to your company.
- **Contact person name** (Contact person name, text, required): This is the name of the contact person who will receive notifications and follow-up requests.
- **Contact email** (Contact email, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person to receive verification requests.
- **Business address** (Business address, text, required): Provide the registered office address of your company if applicable.
- **Supporting documents summary** (Supporting documents summary, text, optional): Briefly describe the attached supporting documents, indicating what they are and why they support your claim.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately.
- Attach supporting documents to validate your claim.
- Ensure the contact email is active and monitored for verification requests.
- Verify the accuracy of your submitted data before submitting the form.
