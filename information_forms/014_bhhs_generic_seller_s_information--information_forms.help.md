# BHHS Generic - Seller(s) Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from sellers regarding their properties, including contact details and property specifics. This form is meant to be a comprehensive guide for sellers to provide accurate and complete information about their properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Enter your name in the "Primary Seller Name" field. This field is required and should include your full name.
2.  Enter your phone number in the "Primary Seller Phone" field. This is a required field for contact purposes.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Primary Seller Email" field. This field is also required for communication.
4.  For properties with multiple sellers, you may need to provide additional seller information, including their names, phone numbers, and email addresses.
5.  Fill in the "Property Address" field with the full street address of the property.
6.  Select the type of property from the "Property Type" dropdown menu. You can choose from options like Single Family, Condo, Townhome, Multi-Family, Land, or Other.
7.  Enter the number of bedrooms and bathrooms in the corresponding fields.
8.  Enter the "Asking Price" in dollars.
9.  If applicable, enter the "Outstanding Mortgage Payoff Amount" in dollars.
10.  Choose a desired timeline for the sale from the options available or enter a custom timeline.
11.  Assess the general condition of the property using the "General Property Condition" dropdown menu (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Needs Work).
12.  Add any additional information about the property in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Seller Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Primary Seller Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Primary Seller Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Secondary Seller Name**: If there are multiple sellers, enter the name of the second seller. This field is optional.
* **Secondary Seller Phone**: If there are multiple sellers, enter the phone number of the second seller. This field is optional.
* **Secondary Seller Email**: If there are multiple sellers, enter the email address of the second seller. This field is optional.
* **Property Address**: Enter the full street address of the property.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property from the available options (Single Family, Condo, Townhome, Multi-Family, Land, or Other).
* **Number of Bedrooms**: Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
* **Number of Bathrooms**: Enter the number of bathrooms in the property.
* **Asking Price**: Enter the asking price of the property in dollars.
* **Outstanding Mortgage Payoff Amount**: If applicable, enter the outstanding mortgage payoff amount in dollars.
* **Desired Timeline for Sale**: Choose a desired timeline for the sale or enter a custom timeline.
* **General Property Condition**: Assess the general condition of the property using the available options (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Needs Work).
* **Additional Information**: Add any other relevant details about the property.
