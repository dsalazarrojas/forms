# Online Information Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users seeking guidance or services related to environmental issues such as soil contamination, erosion, water pollution, air pollution, and climate change.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form title to provide a brief description of your inquiry.
2. Enter your first and last name.
3. Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
4. If required, provide your phone number for potential contact.
5. Specify your organization or affiliation (if any).
6. Choose the areas of service you are interested in (at least one option).
7. Select the environmental issue you are inquiring about.
8. Provide a message with details about your query.
9. Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions (or disagree with a valid reason).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, **Required**): Provide a brief description of your inquiry or information request.
* **First Name** (`form_first_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your first name for identification and communication purposes.
* **Last Name** (`form_last_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your last name for identification and communication purposes.
* **Email Address** (`form_email`, email, **Required**): Enter a valid email address for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`form_phone`, text, **Optional**): Provide your phone number for potential contact.
* **Organization** (`form_organization`, text, **Optional**): If you represent an organization, enter its name for reference.
* **Service Area** (`form_service_area`, select_multiple, **Required**): Select at least one area of service you are interested in (Landscaping, Environmental Design, Treatment).
* **Environmental Issue** (`form_environmental_issue`, select_one, **Required**): Select the environmental issue you are inquiring about (Soil contamination, Erosion, Water pollution, Air pollution, Climate change, Other).
* **Message** (`form_message`, text, **Required**): Provide a detailed message about your query or question.
* **Agreed** (`form_agreed`, select_one, **Required**): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions (Agree, Disagree). If you disagree, provide a valid reason.
* **Agreed Other** (`form_agreed_other`, text, **Optional**): If you choose "Disagree" above, provide a brief reason for your disagreement.
* **Submit** (`form_submit`, text, **Required**): Click this button to submit your completed form for processing.
