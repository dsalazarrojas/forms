# independent_consultant_customer_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from independent consultants who are customers of the organization. The form is designed to gather personal details such as name, contact information, and business information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the First name field with your name.
2. Provide your Last name in the Last name field.
3. Enter a valid Email address in the Email field.
4. If applicable, select the Date of birth field and choose your date of birth.
5. Enter your Phone number in the Phone number field.
6. Provide the Address Line 1 field with your address line 1.
7. Provide the Address Line 2 field with your address line 2.
8. Enter your Postal code in the Postal code field.
9. Select the Country field with your country of residence.
10. Select any applicable Industries you provide services in.
11. Provide a brief Description of services in the Description of services field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Date of birth**: If applicable, select your date of birth.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address Line 1**: Enter your address line 1.
* **Address Line 2**: Enter your address line 2.
* **Postal code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Country**: Select the country of residence.
* **Industry**: Select any applicable industries you provide services in.
* **Description of services**: Provide a brief description of the services you provide.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* The information collected will be used to provide better services to independent consultants.
