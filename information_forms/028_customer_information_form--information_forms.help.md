# Customer Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Information Form is designed to collect contact and personal details from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your contact information in the first page.
2. Provide your contact details on the second page.
3. Share your travel plans on the third page.
4. Enter your customer details on the fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, not required): Enter your name, email, phone number, or any other contact information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, not required): Share any additional contact details or notes about your contact.
* **Travel Plans** (`travel_plans`, text, not required): Describe your travel plans, including dates, destinations, or any other relevant information.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, not required): Provide any relevant customer details, such as purchase history or account information.

## Tips
* Please fill in all the fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure that we can serve you better.
* You can add any additional information that you think is relevant to your contact or travel plans.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us.
