# Relationship Statement Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit information about a relationship, including the couple's details, relationship history, and supporting documents. It is intended to be completed by individuals or couples to provide accurate and up-to-date information about their relationship status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the couple's details, including the names of the couple, their ages, and any other relevant information.
2. Describe the couple's relationship history, including how they met, how long they've been together, and any significant events in their relationship.
3. Upload any supporting documents, such as marriage certificates, birth certificates, or divorce decrees, to provide evidence of the couple's relationship status.
4. Select the current relationship status of both partners from the provided options.
5. Enter the date of the current relationship status.
6. Upload any additional documents that may be required.
7. Provide any additional information or notes about the relationship.
