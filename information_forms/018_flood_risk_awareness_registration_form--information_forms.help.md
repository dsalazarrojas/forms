# Flood Risk Awareness Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Flood Risk Awareness Registration Form. The form is used to collect information about you, your emergency contact, your location, and your preferences for emergency situations and contact.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Read the Form carefully**: Before starting, please make sure you are aware of the form's purpose and that you are registered for flood risk awareness purposes or have a legitimate reason to provide emergency contact information.
2. **Fill out User Details**: Enter your personal details, including your [name], [email], and [phone number]. This information will help us to contact you in case of a flood event.
3. **Add Emergency Contact**: Provide information about your emergency contact, including their [name], [relationship], [phone number], and [email].
4. **Enter Location**: Provide your current [city] and [state] or [country] to help us to better understand your flood risk location.
5. **Choose Emergency Preferences**: Select whether you would like to receive notifications from us about flood events and emergencies in your area ([Yes] or [No]).
6. **Answer Flood Risk Questions**: Please answer questions related to your flood risk awareness. This will help us to better understand your needs.
7. **Select Contact Preferences**: Choose whether you would like to receive contact from us about flood-related events and emergencies ([Yes] or [No]).
8. **Provide Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including your [email] and [phone number]. This will help us to contact you in case of a flood event.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* You can also contact us if you would like to update your information or preferences at any time.
