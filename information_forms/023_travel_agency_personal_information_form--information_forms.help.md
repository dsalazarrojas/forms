# Travel Agency Personal Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect personal information from clients of a travel agency to provide them with relevant services and information during their journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the required information.
2. Make sure to enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. Select a travel date in the "Travel Date" field.
7. Select an arrival time in the "Arrival Time" field.
8. Choose the type of travel you are planning (Business, Pleasure, or Leisure).
9. Enter the name of your travel agency.
10. Enter the name of the employee assisting you.
11. Enter the job title of the employee assisting you.
12. Enter the phone number of the employee assisting you.
13. Enter the email of the employee assisting you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`travel_agency_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address as it appears on your official documents.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number as it appears on your official documents.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your physical address as it appears on your official documents.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date of your travel in the format yyyy-mm-dd.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the arrival time in the format HH:MM 24h.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of travel you are planning (Business, Pleasure, or Leisure).
* **Travel Agency Name** (`travel_agency_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your travel agency.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the employee assisting you.
* **Job Title** (`employee_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the job title of the employee assisting you.
* **Phone** (`employee_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the employee assisting you.
* **Email** (`employee_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email of the employee assisting you.
