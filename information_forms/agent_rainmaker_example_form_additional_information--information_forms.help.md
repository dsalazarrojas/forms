# AGENT RAINMAKER Example Form Additional Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation from users about their experience with the "AGENT RAINMAKER Example Form Additional Information". The goal is to understand user satisfaction, preferences, and pain points to improve the form's design, functionality, and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter your complete name in the "Full name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field. This will be used for follow-up communications.
3. Select the date you submitted the form in the "Date of submission" field.
4. Choose how satisfied you are with the form using the "How satisfied are you with AGENT RAINMAKER Example Form Additional Information?" dropdown menu.
5. Rate your overall experience with the form on a scale of 1-10 in the "Overall rating (1-10)" field.
6. Select all that apply from the list in the "Which aspects applied to your experience with AGENT RAINMAKER Example Form Additional Information?" field.
7. If you have any suggestions or feedback, please type them in the "What would you improve or change about AGENT RAINMAKER Example Form Additional Information?" field.
8. If you have any additional comments or notes, please type them in the "Additional comments or notes" field.
9. Choose your preferred contact method from the "Preferred contact method" dropdown menu.
10. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Phone number" field. Include your country code if international.
11. Enter the time of submission in the "Time of submission" field.
12. Describe your primary use case for the form in the "Primary use case for this form" field.
13. Select how often you use this form from the "How often do you use this form?" dropdown menu.
14. Choose how likely you are to recommend this form to others on a scale of 1-10 in the "How likely are you to recommend this form to others?" field.
