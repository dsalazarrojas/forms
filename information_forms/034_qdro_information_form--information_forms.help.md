# QDRO Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The QDRO Information Form is designed to collect relevant details about a participant or individual's circumstances related to a QDRO, which is used in divorce law proceedings to allocate property or assets between parties.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately according to your circumstances.
2. Make sure to provide clear and concise answers for each field.
3. If a field is not applicable to your situation, please leave it blank or indicate that it's not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, required: false): This field is intended for you to provide a brief description of yourself or the individual in question.
### Alternate Payee Info
* **Alternate Payee Info** (`alternate_payee_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide information about any alternate payees, which could include other parties involved in the QDRO process.
### Retirement Plans
* **Retirement Plans** (`retirement_plans`, text, required: false): This field is where you can describe your retirement plans or any relevant details about your retirement status.
### Case Details
* **Case Details** (`case_details`, text, required: false): In this field, please provide any relevant case-specific information or circumstances that are relevant to the QDRO.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any field or value, please consult with the relevant authority or expert.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact form@gic.mx.
* You can modify the form as needed to suit your specific requirements, but ensure that any derived forms do not violate the original form's licensing terms.
