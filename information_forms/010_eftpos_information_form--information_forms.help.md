# eftpos_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a general information-gathering form designed to collect data from businesses or customers. It has three main sections: "Business Details", "Terminal Details", and "Contact Details". Each section has a text field to gather descriptive information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Provide accurate and complete answers to each question.
3. Ensure you've filled out all the required fields to avoid submission errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business Details (Page 1)
* **Business Details (text)**: Provide a brief description of your business or company name, address, and any other relevant information about your business.

### Terminal Details (Page 2)
* **Terminal Details (text)**: Describe the terminal location, including any distinctive features or characteristics.

### Contact Details (Page 3)
* **Contact Details (text)**: Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number, so we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible in each field.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact form@gic.mx.
