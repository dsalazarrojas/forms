# customer_data_protection_program_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers register for our Customer Data Protection Program, which is a program aimed at protecting sensitive customer data. The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from customers to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Participant Details section.
2. Select the relevant Data Protection Initiatives you are interested in.
3. Choose your role within your organization in relation to data protection.
4. Choose your areas of interest in data protection.
5. (Optional) Provide your contact information and phone number.
6. Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: Enter your name and any other identifying information required for registration.
* **Organization Details**: Enter your organization's name and any other identifying information required for registration.
* **Data Protection Initiatives**: Select all the initiatives that interest you. These are the key areas of focus for our Customer Data Protection Program. You can choose one or more from the list:
	+ Customer Data Protection Program
	+ Data Protection Program
	+ Data Protection Initiative
* **Roles**: Select your role within your organization in relation to data protection. This will help us understand your position and responsibilities. You can choose one or more from the list:
	+ Data Protection Officer
	+ Data Protection Manager
	+ Data Protection Coordinator
* **Data Protection Interests**: Select all the areas of data protection that interest you. These are the key topics you want to focus on for our program. You can choose one or more from the list:
	+ Data Protection
	+ Data Security
	+ Data Governance
* **Contact Info** (Optional): Provide your name and email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number for easier contact.
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete and submit your registration.
