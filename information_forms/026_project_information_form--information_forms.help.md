# Project Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and store project-related information in a structured manner. It helps project managers and team members to have a centralized repository of project details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief name for your project in the "Project Name" field.
2. Provide a detailed description of your project in the "Project Description" field.
3. Identify the project manager in charge of the project in the "Project Manager" field.
4. If you have a specific start date for your project, enter it in the "Project Start Date" field.
5. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, optional): Enter a brief name for your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your project, including its goals, objectives, and key details.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, text, optional): Identify the person or team responsible for managing your project.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, optional): If you have a specific start date for your project, enter it here (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Note:** You can fill in as much or as little information as you need, and you can always update your entries later if necessary.
