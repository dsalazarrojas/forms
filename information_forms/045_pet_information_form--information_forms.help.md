# Pet Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Information Form is designed to collect and document essential information about pets, helping pet owners and veterinary clinics provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the pet in the `pet_name` field.
2. Select the type of pet (e.g., cat, dog, other) in the `pet_type` field.
3. Enter the owner's name in the `owner_name` field.
4. Enter the pet's age in the `pet_age` field.
5. Select the pet's breed from the available options in the `pet_breed` field.
6. Select the pet's gender (male, female, other) in the `pet_gender` field.
7. Enter the contact number of the pet owner in the `contact_number` field.
8. Provide any medical conditions that affect the pet in the `medical_conditions` field.
9. Enter any notes or medical history for the pet in the `medical_notes` field.
10. Enter the name and address of the veterinary clinic that cares for the pet in the `veterinary_clinic` field.
11. Enter the pet owner's email in the `email` field.
12. Enter the address of the pet owner in the `owner_address` field.
13. Enter any additional notes for the pet owner in the `owner_notes` field.
14. Document the pet's medical history in the `medical_history` field.
15. Select the vaccination status of the pet (vaccinated, unvaccinated, partially vaccinated) in the `vaccination_status` field.
16. Enter the date of the last vaccination in the `last_vaccination_date` field.
17. Enter the date and time of the next vaccination in the `next_vaccination_date` field.
18. Enter the signature of the pet owner in the `owner_signature` field.
19. Enter the signature of the veterinary clinic in the `veterinary_clinic_signature` field.
20. Enter the date of the current visit in the `date` field.
21. Enter the time of the current visit in the fields `time`.
22. Enter the pet's weight in the `pet_weight` field.
23. Enter the pet's height in the `pet_height` field.
24. Select the pet's diet type (carnivore, omnivore, herbivore) in the `pet_diet` field.
25. Enter the microchip number of the pet in the `microchip_number` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_name** (`pet_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the pet.
* **pet_type** (`pet_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of pet (e.g., cat, dog, other).
* **owner_name** (`owner_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the pet owner.
* **pet_age** (`pet_age`, number, optional): Enter the age of the pet.
* **pet_breed** (`pet_breed`, text, optional): Select the breed of the pet.
* **pet_gender** (`pet_gender`, select_multiple, optional): Select the gender of the pet (male, female, other).
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the pet owner.
* **medical_conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Provide any medical conditions that affect the pet.
* **medical_notes** (`medical_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes or medical history for the pet.
* **veterinary_clinic** (`veterinary_clinic`, text, optional): Enter the name and address of the veterinary clinic that cares for the pet.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the pet owner.
* **owner_address** (`owner_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the pet owner.
* **owner_notes** (`owner_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes for the pet owner.
* **medical_history** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Document the pet's medical history.
* **vaccination_status** (`vaccination_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the vaccination status of the pet (vaccinated, unvaccinated, partially vaccinated).
* **last_vaccination_date** (`last_vaccination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last vaccination.
* **next_vaccination_date** (`next_vaccination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the next vaccination.
* **owner_signature** (`owner_signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the pet owner.
* **veterinary_clinic_signature** (`veterinary_clinic_signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the veterinary clinic.
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the current visit.
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the current visit.
* **pet_weight** (`pet_weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the pet.
* **pet_height** (`pet_height`, number, optional): Enter the height of the pet.
* **pet_diet** (`pet_diet`, select_one, optional): Select the diet type of the pet (carnivore, omnivore, herbivore).
* **microchip_number** (`microchip_number`, text, optional): Enter the microchip number of the pet.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out each field accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the pet's owner or veterinary clinic.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the pet's owner or veterinary clinic for assistance.
