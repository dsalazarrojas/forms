# General Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects general information about your app usage.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, simply answer the questions based on your experience with the app.
- Select your answer to the multiple choice questions as instructed.
- Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Used**: (1, select_multiple) Please select all the applications you have used. 
* **Favorite App**: (2, select_one) Choose your favorite app from the list.
* **Last Used**: (3, date) Enter the date you last used the app.
* **First Opened**: (4, date) Enter the date you first opened the app.
* **Last Time**: (5, time) Enter the time you last interacted with the app.
* **First Time**: (6, time) Enter the time you first interacted with the app.
* **Why Use App**: (7, note) Briefly describe why you use the app.
* **Email**: (8, email) Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: (9, text) Enter your phone number.
* **User ID**: (10, number) Enter your user ID.
* **App Version**: (11, number) Enter the version of the app you are using.
* **Device Model**: (12, text) Enter your device model.
* **Operating System**: (13, text) Enter your operating system.
* **Browser Type**: (14, select_one) Choose whether you used the app on a web browser or app.
* **App Name**: (15, text) Enter the name of the app.
* **App Platform**: (16, text) Enter the platform the app is running on (e.g. Android, iOS, etc.).
* **App Type**: (17, text) Enter the type of app (e.g. productivity, social media, etc.).
* **App Version**: (18, text) Enter the current version of the app.
* **App ID**: (19, number) Enter a unique ID for the app.
* **Screen Size**: (20, text) Enter the screen size of your device.
* **Screen Resolution**: (21, text) Enter your screen resolution.
* **Screen Orientation**: (22, text) Enter the orientation of your screen.
* **App Category**: (23, text) Enter the category of the app.
* **App Rating**: (24, text) Enter your rating of the app.
* **App Rating Count**: (25, text) Enter the total number of ratings the app has received.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you need help, refer back to this guide for assistance.
- If you are unsure about any field, please leave it blank or reach out to the support team for clarification.
