# Real Estate Buyer Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Buyer Information Form is used to collect personal and financial details from potential real estate buyers. This information helps the real estate team understand the buyer's needs and facilitate communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Provide your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Select your Buyer Type (Residential, Commercial, or Both).
5. Fill in the Home Value (if applicable).
6. Choose your Move In Date and Move In Time (if applicable).
7. Enter the name of your Real Estate Agent (if applicable).
8. Provide Additional Notes (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Buyer Type** (`buyer_type`, select_one, required): Select whether you are a Residential, Commercial, or Both buyer.
* **Home Value** (`home_value`, number, optional): Enter the value of your home (if applicable).
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, optional): Choose your desired move-in date.
* **Move In Time** (`move_in_time`, time, optional): Choose your desired move-in time.
* **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, optional): Enter your real estate agent's name (if applicable).
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, note, optional): Add any contact information you think is necessary.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your situation.
* **Real Estate Agent** (`real_estate_agent`, select_one, optional): Select whether you have a real estate agent or not.
* **Has Financials** (`has_financials`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you have financial documents (Financials), don't have financial documents (No Financials), or unknown (Unknown).
* **Has Documents** (`has_documents`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you have documentation (Documents), don't have documentation (No Documents), or unknown (Unknown).
* **Has Other Buyers** (`has_other_buyers`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you have other buyers (Other Buyers), don't have other buyers (No Other Buyers), or unknown (Unknown).
