# Motorcoach Travel Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect travel information from motorcoaches, including trip details, departure and arrival cities, dates, and special requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Trip Details page with the trip's relevant information.
2. Enter the trip date(s) using the date fields.
3. Specify the start and end times for the trip, if applicable.
4. Enter the departure and arrival cities.
5. Enter the departure and return dates, if applicable.
6. Specify the number of passengers.
7. If you have any special requests, enter them in the Special Requests field.
8. Provide any additional notes about the trip in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the trip, including any relevant details about the destination or route.
* **Trip Date** (`trip_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the trip, in the format mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the trip, in the format 12:00 PM or 13:00 PM.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the trip, in the format 12:00 PM or 13:00 PM.
* **Departure City** (`departure_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the trip will depart from.
* **Destination City** (`destination_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the trip will arrive at.
* **Arrival City** (`arrival_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the trip will end.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of departure, in the format mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of return, in the format mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Passenger Count** (`passenger_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of passengers on the trip.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or requirements for the trip.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the trip.

Tips:

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that all necessary information is collected.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
* Avoid duplicating entries if you're filling out multiple forms for the same trip or travel dates.
