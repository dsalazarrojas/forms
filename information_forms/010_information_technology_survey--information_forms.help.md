# Information Technology Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Information Technology Survey is designed to gather information about your experience with various technologies, such as computers, mobile devices, and the internet. This feedback will help us understand your needs and preferences, and inform our decisions about technology development and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of technology you use from the list provided.
2. Rate your experience with the technology by selecting one of the five options: Very Dissatisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Satisfied, or Very Satisfied.
3. If you feel you need any upgrades or improvements, describe them in the text field below.
4. Provide any additional comments you have about your experience with the technology.
5. Enter your email address and phone number for us to contact you if needed.
6. Finally, select a date and time for this form submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Questions**: These are questions that apply to all technologies.
* **What kind of technology do you use?**: Please select the type of technology you are using, such as computers, mobile devices, or the internet.
* **How would you rate your experience with the technology?**: Rate your experience with the technology on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is Very Dissatisfied and 5 is Very Satisfied.
* **Do you think you need any upgrades or improvements for the technology you use?**: If yes, describe the upgrades or improvements you need in this field.
* **If yes, what upgrades or improvements do you need?**: Provide a more detailed description of the upgrades or improvements you need.
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience with the technology.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Date**: Select the date for this form submission.
* **Time**: Select the time for this form submission.
