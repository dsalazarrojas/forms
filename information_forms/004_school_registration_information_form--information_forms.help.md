# School Registration Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect important information from students, schools, or emergency contacts for registration, enrollment, or other administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields marked with a red asterisk (*) as they are mandatory.
2. Enter your personal information (First Name, Last Name, Birth Date, Contact Email, Contact Phone, and Student ID).
3. Provide emergency contact information (Emergency Contact First Name, Emergency Contact Last Name, and Emergency Contact Relationship).
4. Enter school-specific details (School Address, City, State, Zip, School Grade Level, School Program, School Diploma, School Start Date).
5. Enter student-specific details (Student Grade Level, Student Program, Student Diploma, Student Start Date).
6. Confirm your emergency contact's address and relationship.
