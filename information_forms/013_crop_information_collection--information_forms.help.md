# Crop Information Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from farmers regarding their crops. The form is designed to gather basic details about the crops, farmers, and contact information. This information is essential for various agricultural purposes, such as crop management, monitoring, or market analysis.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing your farmer information on page 1. Please fill out the required fields and optional fields as per your preference.
2.  After submitting the farmer information, move on to page 2. Here, you will provide more details about your crops, such as the name and type of crops you grow.
3.  Once you have filled in all the required fields, you can submit your answers. Please make sure to double-check all the filled-in fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Farmer Information

*   **Farmer Information** (`user_info`, text, required): Please fill in your name and other basic details as a farmer.
*   **(No options available)**

### Crop Details

*   **Crop Details** (`crop_info`, text, required): Fill in the name and type of crop you are growing.

### Contact Information

*   **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact details, such as phone number and email, so we can get in touch with you.
*   **(No options available)**

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all the required fields for each page to ensure accurate submission.
