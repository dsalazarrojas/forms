# post_creation_guidelines_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and track information about submissions. It is used to guide creators through the submission process and ensure that their work meets the necessary guidelines.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the required information.
2. Make sure to choose the correct submission type (Blog post, Video, Image, or File) and confirm the category or tags for your submission.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes as required.
4. Confirm the submission status and date/time of submission.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Draft post** (`draft_post`, `note`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your submission, including the title and content.
* **Draft file** (`draft_file`, `text`, required: false): Attach a draft version of the file or post you are submitting, including any necessary images or videos.
* **Confirmation of Guidelines** (`confirmation`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm that your submission meets the guidelines and requirements.
* **Reviewer comments** (`reviewer_comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any comments or feedback from the reviewer.
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Submission time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, `text`, required: false): The person reviewing the submission.
* **Approver** (`approver`, `text`, required: false): The person approving the submission.
* **Submission status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the status of your submission (Approved, Pending, Rejected).
* **Submission type** (`submission_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of submission (Blog post, Video, Image, or File).
* **File** (`file`, `text`, required: false): Attach a file or document relevant to your submission.
* **Category** (`category`, `text`, required: false): Enter the category of your submission.
* **Tags** (`tags`, `text`, required: false): Add any relevant tags or keywords to your submission.
* **Confirmation category** (`confirm_category`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm the category of your submission (Category, Tags, or File).
* **Other** (`confirm_category_other`, `text`, required: false): If the category of your submission is not listed, please enter the correct category.
* **Confirmation tag** (`confirm_tag`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Confirm the tags of your submission.
* **Confirm Tag Other** (`confirm_tag_other`, `text`, required: false): If a tag is not listed, please enter the correct tag.
* **File type** (`file_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of file or media you are submitting (Image, Video, Audio).
* **Confirmation date** (`confirmation_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you confirmed the submission guidelines.
* **Confirmation time** (`confirmation_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you confirmed the submission guidelines.
* **Confirmation status** (`confirmation_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the status of your confirmation (Approved, Pending, Rejected).
* **Submission Date Approver** (`submission_date_approver`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the submission was approved.
* **Submission Time 2** (`submission_time_2`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the submission was approved.
* **Submission Status Approver** (`submission_status_approver`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the status of the submission after approval (Approved, Pending, Rejected).
* **Submission note** (`submission_note`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your submission.
