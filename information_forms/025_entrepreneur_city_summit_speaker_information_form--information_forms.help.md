# Entrepreneur City Summit Speaker Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for speakers to provide their information for the Entrepreneur City Summit. It will be used to promote and organize the summit.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have all the required information before proceeding.
*   Fill out each field as prompted.
*   Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Bio**: A brief description of yourself, including your experience and achievements.
*   **Presentation Topic**: A description of the topic you will be presenting about.
*   **Contact Number**: Your contact information for follow-up and communication purposes.
*   **Photo**: A link to a photo of yourself, not required but recommended for promotional purposes.
*   **Speaker Notes**: Any additional notes you want to share with the summit organizers, not required.
*   **Presentation Date**: The date of your presentation at the summit.
*   **Presentation Time**: The time of your presentation at the summit.
*   **Presentation Location**: The location where your presentation will take place.
*   **Event Details**: Any additional information about the event, such as room number or other logistical details.
