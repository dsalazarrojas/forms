# Open Peer Review Platform Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Peer Review Platform Registration form is designed to allow users to register on the platform, enabling them to participate in peer review as a reviewer, author, or editor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and affiliation in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. Provide your phone number in the Phone field.
4. Select your role(s) from the Role field.
5. Enter your institution's name in the Institution field.
6. If applicable, enter the name of the institution submitting the research in the Submitting Institution field.
7. If applicable, enter your institution's address in the Submitting Institution Address field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Affiliation** (3, text, required/optional): Enter your current or previous affiliation (e.g., University of [Name], Research Institute, etc.).
- **Email** (4, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact and notification purposes. Ensure it is a valid email address that you can access.
- **Phone** (5, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Role** (6, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your role(s) on the platform, such as Editor, Reviewer, or Author.
- **Institution** (7, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your institution, where you are affiliated.
- **Submitting Institution** (8, text, required/optional): If you are submitting research, enter the name of the institution submitting the research.
- **Submitting Institution Address** (9, text, required/optional): If you are submitting research, enter the address of the submitting institution.

## Tips
- Ensure that your email address is valid and can receive notifications from the platform.
- Be accurate when entering your affiliation and institution to ensure you are properly credited on the platform. 
- If you are unsure about your role or institution, refer to your official documents or contact your supervisor for guidance.
