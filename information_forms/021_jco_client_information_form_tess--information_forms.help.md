# jco_client_information_form_tess - Help Guide
## Purpose
The jco_client_information_form_tess is a form designed to collect client information for booking a service or appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select a date and time for your booking in the Booking Date and Booking Time fields.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the Notes field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid Email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Booking Date**: Select a date for your booking. Please use the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Booking Time**: Select a time for your booking. Please use the format hh:mm.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your booking, if necessary.
