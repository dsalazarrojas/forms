# Standard Communication Induction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from new employees during their onboarding process, ensuring that all necessary details are captured in one place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your Onboarding Details.
2. Select your Contact Details and Communication Preferences.
3. Review and acknowledge the policies.
4. Enter your staff ID and emergency contact information.
5. Complete any other required fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Onboarding Details** (`onboarding_details`, text, required: false): This field is used to capture your onboarding details, such as any special requests or requirements you may have.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information to be used as a primary point of contact.
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you prefer emails or calls.
	+ Choose 'No' if you prefer not to be contacted.
* **Policy Acknowledgments** (`policy_acknowledgments`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you have acknowledged and agreed to all policies.
	+ Choose 'No' if you have not yet acknowledged all policies.
* **Manager Details** (`manager_details`, text, required: false): This field is for your manager's name, used for reporting and communication purposes.
* **Manager Contact** (`manager_contact`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you have provided your manager's contact information.
	+ Choose 'No' if you have not provided your manager's contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you have provided your emergency contact information.
	+ Choose 'No' if you have not provided your emergency contact information.
* **Policy Ack** (`policy_ack`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you have acknowledged all policies.
	+ Choose 'No' if you have not acknowledged all policies.
* **Staff ID** (`staff_id`, number, required: true): Enter your unique staff ID, used for identification and record-keeping purposes.
* **Emergency Number** (`emergency_number`, text, required: false): Enter the contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false): This field is for your manager's name.
* **Emergency Name** (`emergency_name`, text, required: false): This field is for your emergency contact's name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your work email address, used for communication and official purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Contact the responsible team if you have any questions or require assistance.
