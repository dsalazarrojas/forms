# Travel Package Booking Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help travel agencies or tour operators collect necessary information from customers for travel package bookings.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please fill out the form with accurate and truthful information about your travel preferences and package interests.
*   Select the correct travel preferences from the options provided.
*   Enter any relevant customer details in the "Customer Details" field.
*   Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field, which will be used for communication and booking purposes.
*   Enter the name of the travel package you are interested in booking in the "Package Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Information**: This field provides the general information about the form and its purpose.
*   **Travel Preferences**: This field allows you to select your travel preferences, such as a beach, city, or cultural experience.
*   **Customer Details**: A note field for you to provide any additional information about your trip.
*   **Email**: A required field to enter your email address, which is essential for communication and booking purposes.
*   **Package Name**: A text field where you can enter the name of the travel package you are interested in booking.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
*   Be as specific as possible when entering your travel preferences and package name.
