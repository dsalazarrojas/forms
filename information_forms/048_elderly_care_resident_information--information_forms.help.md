# Elderly Care Resident Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides instructions on how to complete the Elderly Care Resident Information form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (full_name)**: Enter the resident's full name, including any titles such as Mr., Mrs., or Ms.
* **Email Address (email)**: Enter the resident's email address if applicable. Leave this field blank if not applicable.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter the resident's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age (age)**: Enter the resident's age.
* **Address (address)**: Enter the resident's current address.
* **Care Type (care_type)**: Select the type of care the resident is receiving (Home, Community, or Facility).
* **Medical History (medical_history)**: Provide a detailed description of the resident's medical history, including any conditions, allergies, or medications.
* **Emergency Contact Name (emergency_contact_name)**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (emergency_contact_relationship)**: Describe the relationship between the resident and the emergency contact person (e.g., family member, friend, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number (emergency_contact_phone)**: Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.

## Tips
- Ensure the information is accurate and up-to-date.
- Use a consistent format for dates and phone numbers.
- If the resident has multiple emergency contacts, repeat the process for each contact.
