# Reiki Client Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Reiki Client Information Form is used to collect and store details about clients who are interested in or have already received Reiki sessions. This form helps our team capture and manage client information accurately and consistently, ensuring the best possible service to our clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your Full Name in the field "Full Name" (ID: 2). 
2. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field (ID: 3). Ensure it's a valid email address.
3. Provide your Phone number in the "Phone" field (ID: 4).
4. If you've attended a Reiki session, please enter the date of your last session in the "Last Reiki Session" field (ID: 6). 
5. Add any additional notes about your experience in the "Notes" field (ID: 7). This is an optional field for your personal comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (ID: 2)** (`name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed to us.
* **Email (ID: 3)** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your valid Email address.
* **Phone (ID: 4)** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Type your phone number.
* **Reiki Practitioner (ID: 5)** (`reiki_practitioner`, `text`, optional): If you've attended a Reiki session, please enter the name of the practitioner who conducted the session.
* **Last Reiki Session (ID: 6)** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your last Reiki session.
* **Notes (ID: 7)** (`notes`, `note`, optional): This is a space for any additional comments or feedback about your experience with Reiki.

## Tips
- Please ensure the information you provide is accurate and complete.
- If you've attended multiple Reiki sessions, you can enter the dates of all of them in the "Last Reiki Session" field.
- You can use the "Notes" field for any additional comments or feedback about your experience with Reiki.
