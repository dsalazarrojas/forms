# General Patient Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Patient Information Form is used to collect general information about a patient, including medical conditions, allergies, health habits, medical history, medical specialties, and emergency contact information. This information is crucial for healthcare providers to understand a patient's overall health status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Please provide the patient's basic information, including their medical conditions, allergies, and health habits.
2. Share their medical history, including any previous surgeries, medications, and medical specialties.
3. Provide emergency contact information, including the name, phone number, and address of the contact person.
4. Specify the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact person.
5. If necessary, provide additional contact information for other contacts.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Medical Conditions** (`patient_information`, `text`, required: false)
Provide a brief description of the patient's medical conditions, such as any chronic illnesses or recent injuries.

### 2. **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required: false)
List the patient's current medical conditions, such as diabetes, asthma, etc.

### 3. **Surgeries** (`surgeries`, `text`, required: false)
Record any previous surgeries or medical procedures the patient has undergone.

### 4. **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false)
List any prescription medications the patient is currently taking or has taken in the past.

### 5. **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required: false)
Note any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.

### 6. **Health Habits** (`health_habits`, `text`, required: false)
Describe the patient's general health habits, such as diet, exercise, or smoking history.

### 7. **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false)
Provide a brief history of the patient's medical conditions and treatments.

### 8. **Medical Specialties** (`medical_specialties`, `text`, required: false)
List any medical specialties the patient has, such as cardiology or oncology.

### 9. **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false)
Provide the name of the emergency contact person.

### 10. **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required: false)
Describe the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, spouse, etc.).

### 11. **Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.

### 12. **Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, required: false)
Provide the address of the emergency contact person.

### 13. **Alternate Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_alternate_contact_info`, `text`, required: false)
Provide any additional contact information for the emergency contact person, such as email or fax.

### 14. **Other Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_other_name`, `text`, required: false)
Provide the name of any other contact person.

### 15. **Other Phone** (`emergency_contact_other_phone`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the phone number of any other contact person.

### 16. **Signature** (`patient_signature`, `text`, required: false)
Please sign and date the form to confirm completion.

### 17. **Date Of Birth** (`patient_dob`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the patient's date of birth.

### 18. **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, `text`, required: false)
Provide any additional medical notes or comments.

### 19. **Insurance** (`insurance`, `text`, required: false)
List any insurance information relevant to the patient's care.

### 20. **Other Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other`, `text`, required: false)
If necessary, describe any additional relationships between the patient and the emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and up-to-date information about the patient.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the patient or their healthcare provider.
* If you need to add or remove fields, please do so according to your institution's guidelines.
* Make sure to sign and date the form to confirm completion.
