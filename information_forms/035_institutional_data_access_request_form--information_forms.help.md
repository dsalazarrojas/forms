# Institutional Data Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to institutional data. You are required to provide your user details, the purpose of the access request, the duration of the access, and any additional notes about your request. You will also be asked to consent to the terms and conditions of data access. You will be assigned a tool to access the data after completing this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your user details.
2. Enter the purpose of your data access request.
3. Select the duration of your access.
4. Choose whether you consent to the terms and conditions.
5. Add any additional notes about your request.
6. If applicable, select an assigned tool for data access.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Details**: <label>User Details</label> (text, optional): Enter your name, email, and any other contact information you would like to provide.
- **Purpose**: <label>Purpose</label> (text, optional): Describe the reason you need to access the institutional data.
- **Duration**: <label>Duration</label> (number, optional): Select how long you would like to have access to the data. Please select from the following options:
  * [insert duration options, e.g., 1 week, 1 month, 6 months, 1 year]
- **Consent**: <label>Consent</label> (select one, optional): Indicate whether you consent to the terms and conditions of data access. Please select 'True' or 'False' to indicate your consent.
- **Additional Notes**: <label>Additional Notes</label> (text, optional): Add any additional information that might be helpful for your data access request.
- **Assigned Tool**: <label>Assigned Tool</label> (text, optional): Select an assigned tool to access the data. This will be assigned to you after completing this form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are accessing the data for legitimate purposes.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or system administrator for guidance.
* Please note that this form is for institutional data access requests only.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or system administrator.
