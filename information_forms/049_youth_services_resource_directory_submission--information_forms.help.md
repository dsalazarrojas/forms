# Youth Services Resource Directory Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Services Resource Directory Submission form is designed to collect information about programs and services offered to youth in our community. The form helps us to provide accurate and up-to-date information to youth, parents, and service providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For fields with multiple options, select the correct answer from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the correct information in the text boxes, including dates and times.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): This is the name of the organization or service provider submitting this form.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Please enter the physical address of the organization or service provider.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter the phone number of the organization or service provider.
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, text, required): Describe the services offered by the organization or service provider.
* **Hours** (`hours`, text, required): Enter the operating hours of the organization or service provider.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): This is the name of the contact person for the organization or service provider.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Please enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Please enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Services Offered Benefits** (`services_benefits`, select_one, required): Select the benefits that the services offered provide to the youth.
* **Service Categories** (`service_categories`, select_multiple, required): Select the categories of services offered by the organization or service provider.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, text, required): Please describe the services offered by the organization or service provider.
* **Program Dates** (`program_dates`, date, required): Enter the start and end dates of the program.
* **Program Start Time** (`program_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the program.
* **Program End Time** (`program_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the program.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the program.
* **Capacity** (`capacity`, number, required): Enter the maximum capacity of the program.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Please enter the physical location of the program.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, text, required): Please describe the type of program.
* **Program Goals** (`program_goals`, text, required): Please describe the goals of the program.
* **Program Notes** (`program_notes`, text, required): Please enter any additional notes about the program.
* **Target Populations** (`target_populations`, text, required): Please specify the target populations for the program.
* **Target Age Range** (`target_age_range`, text, required): Please specify the target age range for the program.
* **Eligibility** (`eligibility`, text, required): Please specify the eligibility criteria for the program.
* **Submission Agreement** (`submission_agreement`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you agree to the submission agreement.

Tips:
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
* We will review and update the information regularly to ensure accuracy and consistency.
