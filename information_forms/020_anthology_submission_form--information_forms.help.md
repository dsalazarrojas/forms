# Anthology Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anthology Submission Form is a crucial step in the submission process for authors who want to share their work with our anthology. This form helps us gather essential information about the author, their piece, and the submission process. Please fill out this form carefully, and we will review your work for potential inclusion.

## How To Complete This Form
- To begin, carefully read through each section and answer all required fields (marked as 'required').
- Fill out each section according to the instructions provided with each field.
- Make sure to review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- Once you have completed and reviewed the form, click the 'Submit' button to send us your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contributor Full Name** (`contributor_name`, `text`, required/required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be printed in the anthology.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, required/required): Provide your mailing address for potential royalty payments or physical copies of the anthology. This is where we will send you any necessary documents or materials.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you with updates or requests for further information.
* **Primary Email** (`email_address`, `email`, required/required): Enter your primary email address for us to contact you with updates or requests for further information.
* **Title of the Piece** (`submission_title`, `text`, required/required): Enter the title of your submission as you would like it to be displayed in the anthology.
* **Submission Category** (`submission_category`, `select_one`, required/required): Choose the category your submission falls under (Short Story, Flash Fiction, Poetry, Personal Essay).
* **Core Themes** (`core_themes`, `text`, required/required): Describe the core themes present in your submission.
* **Keywords (comma-separated)** (`search_keywords`, `text`, required/optional): Enter relevant keywords related to your submission separated by commas.
* **How did you hear about this call for submissions?** (`source_discovery`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how you heard about this call for submissions (Social Media, Writing Website, Referral, Newsletter, Other).
* **Are you available for collaborative editing?** (`availability_for_edits`, `select_one`, required/required): Indicate whether you are available for collaborative editing.
* **Author Statement or Intent** (`author_statement`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief statement about the inspiration behind your submission.
* **Copyright Declaration** (`copyright_declaration`, `select_multiple`, required/required): Select the applicable copyright options or choose 'Other' if your copyright status is not listed.
* **Submission Date** (`date_of_submission`, `date`, required/required): Enter the date you would like your work to be considered for submission.

Note: Please make sure to submit only one work per form. If you need to submit multiple works, please fill out a new submission form for each piece.
