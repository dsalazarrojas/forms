# Student Information Sheet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Information Sheet Form is a collection of fields meant to record and store student information. This form gathers details like name, contact numbers, address, date of admission, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field in the form with the required information.
2. Be sure to input accurate information for each field.
3. Review your inputs carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`School Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school the student is currently attending or affiliated with.
* **Student Id** (`student Id`, number, optional): Input the student's ID, which could be their student number or any other identification number issued by the school or institution.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, optional): Type the student's full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, optional): Input the student's contact number for phone calls.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, optional): Enter the student's home address.
* **Birth Date** (`Birth Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the student's birth.
* **Gender** (`Gender`, select_one, optional): Choose the student's gender.
* **Nationality** (`Nationality`, select_multiple, optional): Select the student's nationality.
* **Ethnicity** (`Ethnicity`, text, optional): Type the student's ethnicity.
* **Date of Admission** (`Date of Admission`, date, optional): Input the date the student was admitted to the school or institution.
* **Student Image** (`Student Image`, note, optional): Upload an image of the student.
* **Guardian Name** (`Guardian Name`, text, optional): Type the name of the student's guardian or parent.
* **Guardian Contact Number** (`Guardian Contact Number`, text, optional): Input the contact number of the student's guardian or parent.
* **Guardian Relation** (`Guardian Relation`, select_one, optional): Choose the relation of the guardian or parent.
* **Guardian Address** (`Guardian Address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the guardian or parent.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`Emergency Contact Number`, text, optional): Input the contact number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, optional): Type the name of the emergency contact.
* **Student Grade** (`Student Grade`, select_one, optional): Choose the student's grade level.
* **Student Class** (`Student Class`, select_multiple, optional): Select the student's class.
* **Student Section** (`Student Section`, select_multiple, optional): Select the student's section.
* **Student Stream** (`Student Stream`, select_multiple, optional): Select the student's stream.
* **Student Stream 2** (`Student Stream 2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the second stream of the student.
* **Student Stream 3** (`Student Stream 3`, select_multiple, optional): Select the third stream of the student.
* **Student Stream 4** (`Student Stream 4`, select_multiple, optional): Select the fourth stream of the student.
