# Resource Navigation Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resource Navigation Guide is a form designed to collect information related to resource navigation, including user preferences, contact details, and service needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the user details section with your basic contact information.
2. Provide your contact preferences, such as your preferred contact method and frequency of contact.
3. Enter your service details, including the specific resource you're navigating.
4. Specify your resource preferences, including any specific features or tools you need.
5. Outline your user needs, such as your goals or requirements for using the resource.
6. Outline your service needs, including any support or assistance you require.
7. Finally, provide any additional information on how the resource can best be used to support your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (User Details, text, required/optional): This section collects your basic contact information, including your name, email, and/or phone number. Please fill in your details accurately so we can contact you with relevant information.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, required/optional): This section is used to collect any additional contact information, such as an alternate email or phone number.
* **Contact Preferences** (Contact Preferences, text, required/optional): This section allows you to specify how you'd like to be contacted, such as via email or phone, and how frequently you'd like to be contacted.
* **Service Details** (Service Details, text, required/optional): This section is used to enter specific information about the resource you're navigating, such as its name and description.
* **Resource Preferences** (Resource Preferences, text, required/optional): This section allows you to specify any specific features or tools you need from the resource, such as a particular language or accessibility requirement.
* **User Needs** (User Needs, text, required/optional): This section is used to outline your specific goals or requirements for using the resource, such as achieving a particular skill or outcome.
* **Service Needs** (Service Needs, text, required/optional): This section is used to specify any support or assistance you require from the service provider, such as help with a specific task or problem.
* **User Outreach** (User Outreach, text, required/optional): This section is used to provide any additional information on how the resource can best be used to support your needs, such as any specific training or coaching requirements.
