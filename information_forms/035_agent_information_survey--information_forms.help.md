# Agent Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agent Information Survey form collects information about agents, which is used for internal purposes such as training, evaluation, or compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you're filling out the form for the correct agent.
2. Enter the agent's **Full name** in the "Full name" field.
3. Enter the agent's **Email** in the "Email" field.
4. If applicable, enter the agent's **Phone number** in the "Phone number" field.
5. If applicable, enter the agent's **License number** in the "License number" field.
6. Select the agent's areas of **Focus or expertise** (e.g., Residential, Commercial, Leasing, Property management, or Other).
7. Enter the agent's number of years of **Experience** in the "Years of experience" field.
8. Enter the agent's **Current operating region** in the "Current operating region" field.
9. If applicable, enter the agent's **Brokerage** in the "Brokerage" field.
10. Enter any **Additional notes** about the agent in the "Additional notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter the agent's legal name, which should match their official identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter the agent's preferred contact email address.
* **Phone number**: Enter the agent's mobile or office number (optional).
* **License number**: Enter the agent's professional license or ID number (optional).
* **Focus or expertise**: Select one or multiple areas of the agent's focus or expertise. This could include Residential, Commercial, Leasing, Property management, or Other.
* **Years of experience**: Enter the number of years the agent has been in the industry.
* **Current operating region**: Enter the primary city or region the agent operates in.
* **Brokerage**: Enter the name of the brokerage firm the agent is currently working with (optional).
* **Additional notes**: Enter any further information about the agent that is not covered in the other fields.
