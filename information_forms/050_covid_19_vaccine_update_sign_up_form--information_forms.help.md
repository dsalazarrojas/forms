# COVID 19 Vaccine Update Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you stay up-to-date on COVID-19 vaccine updates tailored to your needs and preferences. We will use the information provided to send you personalized updates via your chosen contact methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and select your preferences. Please note that some fields are required to ensure accurate and efficient communication. You can check the boxes next to each field for additional information on what is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to receive updates via email.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number to receive updates via phone calls or text messages.
* **Contact Preferences**: Select all the ways you would like us to contact you (e.g. email, phone call, text message, mail, push notification).
* **Preferred Language for Communications**: Select your preferred language for updates (e.g. English, Spanish, Mandarin, Vietnamese, Somali).
* **How Often Would You Like Updates**: Select how often you would like to receive updates (e.g. daily, weekly, monthly, as updates become available).
* **Personal Information (Note)**: This section is not required but can be filled in if you choose. It provides additional information about yourself, such as date of birth.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Age Group**: Select your age group.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code to help us send location-specific updates.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state.
* **Eligibility and Interest (Note)**: This section is not required but can be filled in if you choose. It provides additional information about your eligibility and interest in the vaccine.
* **Are you interested in the COVID-19 vaccine**: Select your interest level (e.g. definitely, probably, unsure, probably not, already vaccinated).
* **You May Be Eligible For**: Select all the phases you think you may be eligible for (e.g. Phase 1A, Phase 1B, Phase 1C, Phase 2).
* **Are you a healthcare worker**: Select whether you are a healthcare worker.
* **Are you an essential worker**: Select whether you are an essential worker.
* **Do you have a high-risk health condition**: Select whether you have a high-risk health condition.
* **Vaccine Preferences (Note)**: This section is not required but can be filled in if you choose. It provides additional information about your vaccine preferences.
* **Vaccine Type Preference**: Select your preferred vaccine type (e.g. Pfizer, Moderna, Johnson and Johnson, no preference, unsure).
* **Doses Interested In**: Select all the doses you are interested in (e.g. first dose, second dose, booster dose, all doses available).
* **Current Vaccination Status**: Select your current vaccination status (e.g. not yet vaccinated, first dose only, fully vaccinated, booster received, unsure).
* **Types of Updates You Want**: Select all the types of updates you would like to receive (e.g. vaccine availability in my area, scheduling information, new vaccine types, booster shots, safety information, general health tips, vaccine effectiveness data, special eligibility announcements, clinic locations, event announcements).
* **Preferred Vaccination Locations**: Select all the locations where you would like to receive the vaccine (e.g. hospital, clinic, pharmacy, mobile clinic, healthcare provider office, community center, school, other).
* **Consent and Privacy (Note)**: This section is not required but can be filled in if you choose. It provides additional information about your consent and privacy.
* **I consent to receive vaccine update notifications**: Select whether you agree or disagree to receive updates.
* **My information may be shared with health authorities**: Select whether you understand and agree or do not understand and do not agree to have your information shared.
* **I can unsubscribe from updates anytime**: Select whether you understand and agree or do not understand and do not agree to be able to unsubscribe from updates.

Note that some fields are marked as "Note" which means they are not required but can provide additional information about yourself. Please be sure to review and fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and efficient communication.
