# Company Profile Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect comprehensive information about your company, including its legal name, website, location, and key personnel. This data will be used to create or update your company's profile in our directory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For fields with multiple options, select the most accurate or relevant option.
3. Be accurate and thorough in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Legal Name\***: Enter your company's full legal name as per your incorporation documents.
* **Trade Name/DBA\***: If your company uses a trade name or doing business as (DBA) name, enter it here.
* **Company Website**: Enter your company's URL.
* **Year Founded**: Enter the year your company was established.
* **Company Type**: Choose the type of business structure you have (e.g., Corporation, LLC, Partnership, etc.).
* **Primary Industry**: Select the main industry your company operates in.
* **Sub-Industry/Specialization**: Enter a more specific industry or specialization if applicable.
* **Headquarters Address\***: Enter your company's main address, including street, city, state, zip, and country.
* **Mailing Address (if different)\***: Enter an address that is different from your headquarters address.
* **Main Phone Number\***: Enter your company's main phone number.
* **Fax Number (if applicable)**: Enter a fax number if you have one.
* **General Email\***: Enter your company's general email address.
* **Primary Contact Name\***: Enter the name of your primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Title\***: Enter the primary contact person's job title.
* **Primary Contact Email\***: Enter the email address of your primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Phone\***: Enter the phone number of your primary contact person.
* **Submission Purpose\***: Select the reason for submitting this profile (e.g., directory listing, partnership application, etc.).
* **Submitter Name\***: Enter your name.
* **Submitter Title\***: Enter your job title.
* **Submitter Signature\***: Sign this form electronically by entering your name as it appears on your business card or ID card.
* **Submission Date**: This will be automatically populated with the current date.

## Tips

* Please be accurate and thorough in your responses.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact us.
* Review your responses carefully before submitting.
* This form is for internal use only; your responses will be used to create or update your company's profile in our directory.
* If you need to add or update multiple profiles, please submit each one separately.
* You can save and come back to this form later, but make sure to submit it when you're finished.
