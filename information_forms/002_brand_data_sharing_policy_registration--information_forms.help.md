# Brand Data Sharing Policy Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for Brand Data Sharing Policy Registration. It is used to gather necessary details for data sharing, including organization name, type, contact information, and data sharing policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Organization Name" field with the legal name of your organization.
2. Select the correct "Organization Type" from the list provided.
3. Enter the full name, email address, and phone number of the primary contact person.
4. Choose the reason why you need to share brand data in the "Purpose of Data Sharing" field.
5. Select all the types of data you need access to in the "Types of Data Requested" field.
6. Choose the data retention period in the "Data Retention Period" field.
7. Describe your data security measures in the "Security Measures" field.
8. Indicate if you plan to share data with third parties in the "Third-Party Sharing" field.
9. Confirm your GDPR compliance status in the "GDPR Compliance Status" field.
10. Agree to the data sharing terms in the "Consent Acknowledgment" field.
11. Enter the name and job title of the authorized signatory.
12. Select the date of registration in the "Registration Date" field.
13. Enter any additional terms or comments in the "Additional Terms" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (1, Text, Required): Enter the legal name of your organization.
* **Organization Type** (2, Select One, Required): Select the type of your organization from the list provided.
* **Primary Contact Name** (3, Text, Required): Enter the full name of the primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Email** (4, Email, Required): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Phone** (5, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Purpose of Data Sharing** (6, Select One, Required): Choose the reason why you need to share brand data.
* **Types of Data Requested** (7, Select Multiple, Required): Select all the types of data you need access to.
* **Data Retention Period** (8, Select One, Required): Choose the data retention period.
* **Security Measures** (9, Text, Required): Describe your data security measures in place.
* **Third-Party Sharing** (10, Select One, Required): Indicate if you plan to share data with third parties.
* **GDPR Compliance Status** (11, Select One, Required): Confirm your GDPR compliance status.
* **Consent Acknowledgment** (12, Select One, Required): Agree to the data sharing terms.
* **Authorized Signatory Name** (13, Text, Required): Enter the name of the authorized signatory.
* **Signatory Title** (14, Text, Required): Enter the job title of the authorized signatory.
* **Registration Date** (15, Date, Required): Enter the date of this registration.
* **Additional Terms** (16, Text, Optional): Enter any additional terms or comments.

Please fill out this form accurately and attentively to ensure successful registration of your brand data sharing policy.
