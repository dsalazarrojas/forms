# Lost And Found Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lost And Found Registration form is used to document a lost item so that it can be reunited with its owner if found.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the details about the lost item, including its title, description, location, and item type.
2. Enter the contact information of the person who lost the item, including their name, email, and phone number.
3. Choose the status of the item from the list (e.g. "Found", "Lost", "Returned").
4. Submit the form to register the item.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lost And Found Item Title (text)**: Enter a brief title describing the item that was lost.
* **Found On (date)**: The date the item was last seen in its original location.
* **Item Description (text)**: Describe the item in as much detail as possible.
* **Lost Location (text)**: Enter the location where the item was lost.
* **Contact Name (text)**: The name of the person who lost the item.
* **Contact Email (email)**: The email address of the person who lost the item.
* **Contact Phone (text)**: The phone number of the person who lost the item.
* **Item Status (select_one)**: Choose from the list: "Found", "Lost", "Returned".
* **Item Type (select_one)**: Choose from the list: "Clothing", "Documents", "Electronics", "Keys", "Jewelry", "Money", "Other".

## Tips
* If the item has already been registered, please do not fill out this form again.
* Ensure that all contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Submit the form after completing all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the organization for assistance.
