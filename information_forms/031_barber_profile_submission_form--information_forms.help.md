# Barber Profile Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Barber Profile Submission Form is used to collect and display information about a barber's public profile. Completing this form will allow you to create or update your public profile.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields: Professional Name, Professional Title, Years in the Industry, Signature Style, Core Specialties, Languages Spoken, Private Phone Number, and Private Email Address.
2. Provide a brief description of your signature style in the Signature Style field.
3. Select up to 4 core specialties that you offer.
4. Indicate the languages you speak by selecting multiple options from the Languages Spoken field.
5. Enter your short and full biographies in the Short Biography (Program/App) and Full Biography (Website) fields respectively.
6. If applicable, enter your Instagram Handle and Personal Booking Link.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Professional Name** (`Professional Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name you use with clients, for example John Smith.
* **Professional Title** (`Professional Title`, `text`, required): Enter a title that represents your professional status, such as Master Barber or Senior Stylist.
* **Years in the Industry** (`Years in the Industry`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have been in the industry.
* **Your Signature Style** (`Your Signature Style`, `text`, required): Describe the cut/style that you are most known for, such as "Classic Fade with a twist".
* **Core Specialties** (`Core Specialties`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose up to 4 specialties you offer, such as Classic Fades, Modern Texture, Traditional Hot Towel Shave, etc.
* **Languages Spoken** (`Languages Spoken`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all languages you can communicate with, such as English, Spanish, etc.
* **Short Biography (Program/App)** (`Short Biography (Program/App)`, `text`, required): Enter a brief 2-3 sentence description of your background and experience.
* **Full Biography (Website)** (`Full Biography (Website)`, `text`, required): Enter a 100-300 word description of your story, inspirations, and philosophy.
* **Instagram Handle** (`Instagram Handle`, `text`, optional): Enter your Instagram handle if applicable.
* **Personal Booking Link** (`Personal Booking Link`, `text`, optional): Enter a link to your booking system if applicable.
* **Private Mobile Number** (`Private Mobile Number`, `text`, required): Enter your private mobile number.
* **Private Email Address** (`Private Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter your private email address.
* **Profile Photo Status** (`Profile Photo Status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your profile photo, such as "Photo submitted via email" or "Need to schedule headshot session".
