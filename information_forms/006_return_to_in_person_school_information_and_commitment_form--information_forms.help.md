# Return To In Person School Information And Commitment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect necessary information and commitment from parents or guardians of students returning to in-person school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the following information:
   - **Student Information**
   This is the student's personal details.
2. Enter the student's:
   - **Name**
   - **Email**
3. If applicable, provide the following parent information:
   - **Parent 1 Name**
   - **Parent 1 Email**
   - **Parent 2 Name**
   - **Parent 2 Email**
4. Enter the following school details:
   - **Student Level** (e.g., grade level)
   - **Return Date** (the date the student will return to school)
5. Enter the following commitment from parents or guardians:
   - **Commitment** (a short statement of commitment)
   - **Parent Commitment** (a short statement of commitment from parents or guardians)
6. Provide additional school information:
   - **School Information** (any other important information about the school)
   - **School Address** (the school's physical address)
   - **School Phone** (the school's contact phone number)
   - **School Email** (the school's email address)
7. Enter the teacher's information:
   - **Teacher Name**
   - **Teacher Email**

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Information** (student_info): Provide a brief description of the student.
- **Name (name)**: Enter the student's name.
- **Email (email)**: Enter the student's email address.
- **Parent 1 Name (parent_1_name)**: Enter the name of the first parent or guardian.
- **Parent 1 Email (parent_1_email)**: Enter the email address of the first parent or guardian.
- **Parent 2 Name (parent_2_name)**: Enter the name of the second parent or guardian (if applicable).
- **Parent 2 Email (parent_2_email)**: Enter the email address of the second parent or guardian (if applicable).
- **Student Level (student_level)**: Enter the student's grade level or academic year.
- **Return Date (return_date)**: Enter the date the student will return to school.
- **Commitment (commitment)**: Enter a short statement of commitment from the student.
- **Parent Commitment (parent_commitment)**: Enter a short statement of commitment from parents or guardians.
- **School Information (school_info)**: Provide any other important information about the school.
- **School Address (school_address)**: Enter the school's physical address.
- **School Phone (school_phone)**: Enter the school's contact phone number.
- **School Email (school_email)**: Enter the school's email address.
- **Teacher Information (teacher_info)**: Provide any other important information about the teacher.
- **Teacher Name (teacher_name)**: Enter the teacher's name.
- **Teacher Email (teacher_email)**: Enter the teacher's email address.

## Tips
Please ensure to submit this form only once per student and to follow the school's rules and regulations. If you have any questions, please contact the school administration.
