# Background Information Intake For Immigration Clients - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect background information from immigration clients. The goal is to gather essential details about the client's background, personal history, and immigration status to facilitate the application process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2.  Enter your full legal name as it appears on your passport.
3.  List any other names used or aliases.
4.  Provide your country of birth.
5.  List all countries of citizenship.
6.  Enter your date of last entry to the United States.
7.  Enter the type of visa you used for your last entry.
8.  If you have an I-94 number, provide it.
9.  Answer whether you have been in immigration court or removal proceedings.
10. Answer whether you have ever been arrested or convicted of a crime.
11. If yes, provide details for each arrest.
12. List family members in the United States and their status.
13. Select your highest level of education completed.
14. Enter your email address.
15. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Legal Name** (`client_full_name_immigration`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your passport.
*   **Other Names Used or Aliases** (`other_names_used_immigration`, text, optional): List any other names used or aliases.
*   **Country of Birth** (`country_of_birth_immigration`, text, required): Provide your country of birth.
*   **Country of Citizenship** (`country_of_citizenship`, text, required): List all countries of citizenship.
*   **Date of Last Entry to the United States** (`last_entry_date_us`, date, required): Enter the date of your last entry to the United States.
*   **Visa Type at Last Entry** (`last_entry_visa_type`, text, required): Enter the type of visa you used for your last entry.
*   **Current I-94 Admission Number** (`current_i94_number`, text, optional): If you have an I-94 number, provide it.
*   **Have you ever been in immigration court or removal proceedings?** (`has_been_in_proceedings`, select_one, required): Answer whether you have been in immigration court or removal proceedings.
*   **Have you ever been arrested or convicted of a crime?** (`has_been_arrested_worldwide`, select_one, required): Answer whether you have ever been arrested or convicted of a crime.
*   **If Yes, provide details for each arrest** (`arrests_details_immigration`, text, optional): If you answered yes, provide details for each arrest.
*   **List family members in the US and their status** (`family_in_us_status`, text, optional): List family members in the United States and their status.
*   **Highest Level of Education Completed** (`highest_level_education_immigration`, select_one, required): Select your highest level of education completed.
*   **Your Email Address** (`contact_email_immigration`, email, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Your Phone Number** (`contact_phone_immigration`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
