# Employee Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store employee details in a simple and straightforward manner, suitable for administrative uses such as employee directory listings and contact management.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter the employee's First Name in the first field.
2. Enter the employee's Last Name in the second field.
3. Enter the employee's Email in the third field. Please use the correct email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
4. Enter the employee's Phone number in the fourth field.
5. Enter the employee's Department and Position in the fifth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`employee_first_name`, text, optional): Enter the employee's first name in this field. This field is not required.
* **Last Name** (`employee_last_name`, text, optional): Enter the employee's last name in this field. This field is not required.
* **Email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter the employee's work email in this field using the standard email format (e.g., `example@example.com`). This field is not required.
* **Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Enter the employee's phone number in this field. This field is not required.
* **Department and Position** (`employee_department`, text, optional): Enter the employee's department and position in this field. This field is not required.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
- If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
- If you need help with anything, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
