# Graduation Information Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a student's graduation details, including their name, email, phone number, graduation date and time, and participation in the graduation ceremony. The collected data helps the school administration to efficiently track and process graduation information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the graduation date and time for the ceremony.
3. Indicate whether the student participated in the graduation ceremony or not.
4. Select the location of the graduation ceremony, if applicable.
5. Provide any additional details about the student's graduation, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name as it appears on their diploma.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter the student's email address for communication purposes.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter the student's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the graduation ceremony in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Graduation Time** (`graduation_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the graduation ceremony in 12-hour format.
* **Graduation Ceremony** (`graduation_ceremony`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the student participated in the ceremony.
* **Participated in Ceremony?** (`student_participated`, select_one, optional): Confirm if the student participated in the ceremony.
* **Graduation Location** (`graduation_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the graduation ceremony, if applicable.
* **Student Data** (`student_data`, note, optional): Add any additional information about the student, such as photos or remarks.
* **Graduation Details** (`graduation_details`, text, optional): Enter any other details about the student's graduation experience.
