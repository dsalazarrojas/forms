# Subcontractor Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Subcontractor Information Form is designed to collect and store relevant details about a subcontractor, allowing for accurate and up-to-date record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the subcontractor's information, ensuring that all relevant fields are completed correctly.
2. Check the "category" field to determine whether the subcontractor is new or existing.
3. In the case of a new subcontractor, select all applicable categories to avoid duplication of effort.
4. Double-check the "assigned_tool" field, as this may impact the subcontractor's work assignments and access to resources.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Form Title
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the form, which will be displayed on the form's title bar.

### Category
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select whether the subcontractor is new or existing. If new, select all applicable categories to ensure accurate record-keeping.

### Description
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the subcontractor's work or services.

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Check if the subcontractor has access to any specific tools. This field may impact work assignments and resource access.

### Additional Information
* **Form Title 2** (`form_title_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional details or information about the subcontractor.
