# driver_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The driver_information_form is a form designed to collect information about a driver, including their name, address, license number, vehicle details, insurance information, and insurance provider information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **driver name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the driver.
* **driver address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the driver's address.
* **license number** (`license number`, `number`, required: false): Enter the driver's license number.
* **vehicle details** (`vehicle details`, `text`, required: false): Provide details about the vehicle.
* **insurance info** (`insurance info`, `text`, required: false): Enter information about the driver's insurance.
* **insurance status** (`insurance status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the driver's insurance from the following options:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **insurance provider** (`insurance provider`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the insurance provider.
* **insurance provider address** (`insurance provider address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the insurance provider.
* **policy number** (`policy number`, `number`, required: false): Enter the policy number.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals to complete this form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant authorities or departments for clarification.
