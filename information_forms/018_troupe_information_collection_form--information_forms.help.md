**Troupe Information Collection Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose

The Troupe Information Collection Form is used to gather essential details about a troupe's performances, personnel, and events. This form is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of a troupe, including their name, description, contact information, and event details.

**How To Complete This Form**

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with a **(*)**. These fields are essential for creating a complete troupe profile.
2. Enter your troupe's details in the text fields, following the format guidelines (e.g., First Name, Last Name, Email).
3. Select the required dates for the event(s) the troupe will be performing at in the **Event Dates** field.
4. Set the time for the event(s) in the **Time** field.
5. Provide a brief description of the troupe's performances and events in the **Description** field.
6. Enter the URL of any promotional materials, such as flyers or social media links, in the **Promotional Materials Link** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (*): Enter the performer's first name.
* **Last Name** (*): Enter the performer's last name.
* **Email** (*): Enter the performer's email address.
* **Phone** (*): Enter the performer's phone number.
* **Description** (*): Provide a brief description of the troupe's performances and events.
* **Troupe Name** (*): Enter the troupe's name.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the manager's name.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the manager's email address.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number for the manager.
* **Website**: Enter the troupe's website.
* **Promotional Materials**: Provide any promotional materials, such as flyers or posters.
* **Promotional Materials Link** (*): Enter the URL of the promotional materials.
* **Permissions** (*): Enter any necessary permissions for the troupe's performances.
* **Event Dates** (*): Select the dates for the event(s) the troupe will be performing at.
* **Time** (*): Set the time for the event(s) the troupe will be performing at.
* **Location** (*): Enter the location where the troupe will be performing.
* **Social Media**: Provide any social media links for the troupe.
* **Manager Social Media**: Provide any social media links for the manager.
* **Manager Venue**: Enter the manager's venue name.
* **Manager Venue Email**: Enter the manager's venue email address.
* **Manager Venue Phone**: Enter the manager's venue phone number.
* **Manager Venue Location**: Enter the manager's venue location.
* **Manager Venue Website**: Enter the manager's venue website.
* **Manager Note**: Enter any additional notes for the manager.
* **Manager Permission**: Enter any necessary permissions for the manager.

Note: Fields marked with **(*)** are required.
