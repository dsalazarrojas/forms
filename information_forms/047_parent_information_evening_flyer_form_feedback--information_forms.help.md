# Parent Information Evening Flyer Form Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Information Evening Flyer Form Feedback is a tool designed to collect feedback from parents and carers on the content, design, and overall presentation of the information evening flyer. This feedback is essential for refining the flyer and improving the experience for parents and carers.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide helpful feedback, please follow these steps:

1. Read through the questions and fields carefully to ensure you are providing clear and concise feedback.
2. Answer each field honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
3. Provide as much detail as possible to support your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Feedback** (`parent_evening_flyer_form_feedback`, text, required/optional): This is the main area to provide feedback about the flyer. Please share your thoughts, suggestions, or concerns about the content, design, and overall presentation of the flyer. Be specific about what you liked or disliked about the flyer.
* **Feedback Category** (`feedback_category`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one of the available categories to describe the nature of your feedback. This helps us understand the type of feedback you are providing. Select one of the options:
	+ Option 1: Suggestion
	+ Option 2: Concern
	+ Option 3: Compliment
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): If you'd like to provide any additional comments or suggestions, please type them here. This is an optional field, but it helps us to better understand your thoughts.
