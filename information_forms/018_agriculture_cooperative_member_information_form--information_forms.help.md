# Agriculture Cooperative Member Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and update information from agriculture cooperative members. It helps the cooperative understand their operations, needs, and contact preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as `*Required`) with accurate and complete information.
2. For select_one fields (like Primary agricultural activity), choose the most relevant option from the list.
3. For fields like Address, enter the correct village, town, and region information.
4. If you don't have a preferred contact method, select "In-person" to default to the cooperative's default method.
5. Ensure you provide the correct phone number format (including the country code) for the emergency contact field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member full name** (1, text, *Required*): Enter your legal name as it appears on records.
* **Membership number** (2, text, *Optional*): If you have a cooperative-assigned ID, enter it here. Otherwise, leave it blank.
* **Contact phone** (3, text, *Optional*): Provide your mobile or landline number for the cooperative to contact you.
* **Contact email** (4, email, *Optional*): Enter your email address for cooperative communications.
* **Address** (5, text, *Optional*): Enter the name of the village, town, and region where your farm is located.
* **Primary agricultural activity** (6, select_one, *Required*): Choose your main farming activity from the list.
* **Farm size in hectares** (7, number, *Optional*): Enter the approximate land area of your farm.
* **Preferred contact method** (8, select_one, *Optional*): Select your preferred way for the cooperative to contact you.
* **Emergency contact** (9, text, *Optional*): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Notes** (10, text, *Optional*): Add any additional information about yourself or your farm that's relevant to the cooperative.
