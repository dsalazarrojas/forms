# Budget Transparency Initiative Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide transparency and accountability in financial reporting between an organization and its stakeholders. It outlines the terms and conditions for sharing budget information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the relevant options for each field according to your organization's policies and procedures.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the full legal name of your organization.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for this initiative.
* **Contact Person Title** (`contact_title`, text, required): Enter the job title or position of the primary contact person.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter the official organization email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the direct contact number of your organization.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of organization you represent (e.g., Government Agency, Non-Profit Organization).
* **Fiscal Year** (`fiscal_year`, text, required): Enter the applicable fiscal year for transparency.
* **Budget Scope** (`budget_scope`, select_one, required): Select the level of budget information to be shared (e.g., Full Organizational Budget, Department-Specific Budget).
* **Data Sharing Level** (`data_sharing_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of detail to be shared (e.g., Public - Fully Accessible, Restricted - Member Access Only).
* **Information Categories to Share** (`information_categories`, select_multiple, required): Select all the categories of information to be shared (e.g., Revenue Sources, Expenditure Breakdown).
* **Publication Methods** (`publication_methods`, select_multiple, required): Select the methods through which information will be published (e.g., Organization Website, Public Portal).
* **Purpose of Consent** (`consent_purpose`, text, required): Enter the primary purpose for transparency initiative.
* **Stakeholder Notification** (`stakeholder_notification`, select_one, required): Select whether stakeholders have been notified (e.g., Yes - All Notified, Yes - Key Stakeholders Notified).
* **Legal Review Status** (`legal_review`, select_one, required): Select the status of legal review (e.g., Yes - Approved, Yes - Pending Minor Revisions).
* **Data Protection Compliance** (`data_protection_compliance`, select_one, required): Select the level of compliance with data protection regulations (e.g., Fully Compliant, Partially Compliant).
* **Consent Duration** (`consent_duration`, select_one, required): Select the length of time this consent will remain active (e.g., One Year, Two Years).
* **Authorization Confirmation** (`authorized_signature`, select_one, required): Select whether you are authorized to provide this consent.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date of consent.
* **Additional Requirements or Conditions** (`additional_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any special conditions or requirements for this consent.

Note: Please review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities.
