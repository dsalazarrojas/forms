# Employee Training Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Training Information Form is a mandatory form designed to collect information from employees about their training history, preferences, and needs. This information will help the Training Team to better understand employee requirements and provide tailored training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering your Employer Name, which is your company or organization's name.
2. Next, enter your Employee Name, as it appears on your official identification documents.
3. Select the type of training you have attended or plan to attend from the provided options (Basic Safety, Advanced Safety, or Leadership Skills).
4. If applicable, enter the date you attended or plan to attend the training.
5. Enter the duration of the training, if applicable.
6. Provide the location of the training, if applicable.
7. Enter the name of the facilitator or instructor who conducted the training, if applicable.
8. Enter the venue or location where the training took place, if applicable.
9. If you have attended previous training, select 'True'. If you have not, select 'False'.
10. If you have attended previous training, select the type of training from the options provided, if applicable.
11. Fill in any other information about the previous training you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employer Name (Text)**: Enter your company or organization's name.
* **Employee Name (Text)**: Enter your Employee ID or as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Training Type (Select One)**: Choose the type of training you have attended or plan to attend.
* **Training Date (Date)**: Enter the date of the training if you have attended or will attend the training.
* **Training Time (Time)**: Enter the duration of the training, if applicable.
* **Training Location (Text)**: Enter the venue or location where the training took place, if applicable.
* **Training Facilitator (Text)**: Enter the name of the facilitator or instructor who conducted the training, if applicable.
* **Training Venue (Text)**: Enter the location or venue where the training took place, if applicable.
* **Training Duration (Number)**: Enter the duration of the training, if applicable.
* **Attended Previous Training (Select One)**: Indicate if you have attended previous training by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
* **Previous Training Type (Text)**: Enter the type of training you attended previously, if applicable.
