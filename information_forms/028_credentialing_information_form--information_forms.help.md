# Credentialing Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about a provider's educational background, training, and qualifications related to credentialing. This information is used to verify a provider's credentials and ensure they meet the necessary requirements for a particular role or position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant option for each field:
	* **Provider Education**: Enter your educational background related to credentialing information.
	* **Provider Training**: Describe your relevant training experiences related to credentialing information.
	* **Provider Licenses**: Select all the licenses you hold that are relevant to credentialing information. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
	* **Provider Qualifications**: Enter any other relevant qualifications you possess.
	* **Credentialing Institutions**: Select any institutions that have granted you a credential or license. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
2. Make sure to submit the form after you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Provider Education (provider_education)**: This field is used to record the educational background related to credentialing information.
* **Provider Training (provider_training)**: This field is used to describe any relevant training experiences you have completed related to credentialing information.
* **Provider Licenses (provider_licenses)**: This field is used to select all the licenses you hold that are relevant to credentialing information. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
* **Provider Qualifications (provider_qualifications)**: This field is used to record any other relevant qualifications you possess related to credentialing information.
* **Credentialing Institutions (credentialing_institutions)**: This field is used to select any institutions that have granted you a credential or license. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about your educational background, training, and qualifications.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* This form is not intended for providers with no experience or qualifications related to credentialing information. If you are a new provider, please do not complete this form.
