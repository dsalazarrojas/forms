# pathway_student_admission_information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect pathway information for students who are enrolling in the pathway program. It gathers essential details such as student ID, name, date of birth, and pathway eligibility.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information on each page, starting from the first page, "pathway_student_admission_information_page_1".
2. Make sure to fill in all the fields marked as required.
3. Review the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID (student_id)**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the student. This is typically an 8-digit number.
* **Student Name (student_name)**: Enter the full name of the student as per their official identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (student_date_of_birth)**: Enter the student's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Pathway Eligibility (pathway_id)**: Select 'Yes' if the student is eligible for the pathway program, 'No' otherwise.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the student's information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
* Ensure that the form is filled out completely and correctly to avoid any issues with the admission process.
