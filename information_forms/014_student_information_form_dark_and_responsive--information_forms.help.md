# Student Information Form Dark And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic student information in a way that is dark and responsive, prioritizing the user's experience and adapting to different screen sizes and devices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
*   Fill in your last name in the "last_name" field.
*   Enter your email address in the "email" field.
*   Provide your address in the "address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### first_name
*   **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name.

### last_name
*   **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name.

### email
*   **Email (email)**: Enter your valid email address.

### address
*   **Address (text)**: Enter your address.

## Tips
*   Be sure to enter your information accurately to ensure it appears correctly on your profile.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can contact us for further assistance.
*   This form is designed to be accessible and easy to use on various devices, so don't hesitate to reach out if you have any issues.
