# olli_south_class_proposal_and_information_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "olli_south_class_proposal_and_information_form" is a form designed to collect information and proposals related to a class. This form aims to gather details about a class, including its proposal and relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through the form pages.
2. For each page, fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Class Proposal (Class Proposal)** (`class_proposal`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to submit your class proposal, which can be any relevant details about your class.
* **Class Information (Class Information)** (`class_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to provide any additional information about your class, such as its description, objectives, or any other relevant details.
* **Class Details (Class Details)** (`class_details`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to provide any further details about your class, such as its schedule, location, or any other relevant information.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill in the form accurately and completely to ensure that your proposal and information are well-represented.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
