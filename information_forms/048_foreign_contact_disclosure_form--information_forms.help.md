# Foreign Contact Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about foreign contacts, including their name, company, address, email, phone number, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact name, company, and address fields with the relevant information.
2. Enter the contact's email and phone number, if applicable.
3. Select the contact type (Contact Person, Company, or Other).
4. Choose the foreign country of origin from the list of options.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments about the contact.
6. Select the submit option to save and submit or save and continue editing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact's name.
- **Company** (`company`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact company name.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact's address.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the contact's email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact's phone number.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the contact.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the contact.
- **Contact Type** (`contact_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of contact (Contact Person, Company, or Other).
- **Foreign Country** (`foreign_country`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the foreign country of origin from the list of options.
- **Amount** (`amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount related to the contact.
- **Currency** (`currency`, select_one, required/optional): Select the currency of the amount.
- **Currency Code** (`currency_code`, text, required/optional): Enter the currency code.
- **Amount in USD** (`amount_usd`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount in USD.
- **Other Currency** (`other_currency`, text, required/optional): Enter any other currency.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
- **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether to save and submit or save and continue editing.
- **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of submission.
- **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of submission.
- **Submit Date and Time** (`submit_date_time`, date, required/optional): Enter the date and time of submission.
- **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required/optional): Select who the contact is assigned to (ChatJimmy or Other).
- **Status** (`status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the contact (Open, Pending, or Resolved).
- **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, required/optional): Select the priority of the contact (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the category of the contact (Information Forms or Other).
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any comments or notes about the contact.
