# Patients Demographic Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects essential personal details about a patient, including their first name, last name, date of birth, contact email, and phone number. This information is crucial for our team to effectively communicate and provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. (Optional) Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field if relevant.
4. (Optional) Enter a valid contact email address in the "Email" field, which is required for secure communication.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, which can be optional but helps us contact you more efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name, such as John or Jane.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name, such as Doe or Smith.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, **optional**): If you have a birthdate, enter it in the format mm/dd/yyyy. If not, you can skip this field.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **optional**): Enter a valid email address to receive secure communication from our team.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your phone number for efficient communication, but this is not required.
