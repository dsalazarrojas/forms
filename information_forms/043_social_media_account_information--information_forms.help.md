# Social Media Account Information - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information about social media accounts, including the platform type, account ID, username, and other relevant details. Fill out this form to create or update a social media account.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Social Media Platform**: Select the type of social media platform you want to create or manage an account for (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, etc.).
2. **Platform ID**: Enter the unique identifier for your social media account on the selected platform.
3. **Username**: Enter your social media account username.
4. **Password**: Enter your social media account password.
5. **Email**: Enter the email address associated with your social media account.
6. **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number associated with your social media account (if applicable).
7. **Website**: Enter the URL of your personal website or blog (if applicable).
8. **Profile Picture**: Enter the URL of your social media profile picture (if applicable).
9. **LinkedIn Account Link**: Select the LinkedIn account type you want to link to (e.g., Personal, Company, etc.). If you don't have a LinkedIn account, select 'None'.
