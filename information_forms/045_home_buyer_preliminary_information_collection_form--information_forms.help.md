# Home Buyer Preliminary Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect preliminary information from potential homebuyers, helping us understand their initial preferences and requirements for a home purchase.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your accurate and relevant information.
2. Please ensure that you are not a duplicate entry.
3. Review and correct any errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred contact method:
	+ Mobile
	+ Email
	+ Phone
* **Home Buyer Status** (`home_buyer_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose your homebuyer status:
	+ First-Time Home Buyer
	+ Repeat Home Buyer
	+ Real Estate Investor
* **Loan Type** (`loan_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your loan type:
	+ Primary Residence
	+ Investment Property
	+ Vacation Home
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type of property you are interested in:
	+ Single Family Home
	+ Condo
	+ Townhouse
* **Property Size** (`property_size`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the size of the property you are interested in:
	+ Less than 1000 Sq Ft
	+ 1000-1500 Sq Ft
	+ 1500-2000 Sq Ft
	+ 2000-2500 Sq Ft
	+ 2500-3000 Sq Ft
	+ 3000-4000 Sq Ft
	+ More than 5000 Sq Ft
* **Move-In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, required: false): Enter your target move-in date.
* **Move-Out Date** (`move_out_date`, date, required: false): Enter your target move-out date.
* **Property Purchase Price** (`property_purchase_price`, number, required: false): Enter the purchase price of the property.
* **Down Payment** (`down_payment`, number, required: false): Enter the down payment amount.
* **Property Location** (`property_location`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the location of the property:
	+ North
	+ South
	+ East
	+ West

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and relevant to your situation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your cooperation and look forward to working with you.
