# Tax Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Information Form is designed to collect tax-related information from individuals and businesses. It's intended for tax professionals, accountants, or tax filers to collect and verify tax-related data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the personal details section with your first name, last name, and tax ID number.
2. Enter your address, including address line 1 and 2, city, state, and zip code.
3. Provide your email address and phone number.
4. Select the tax year for which you are submitting information.
5. Choose the type of tax return you are submitting (Self, Spouse, Joint, or Other).
6. Enter the date of your tax return submission.
7. Select the status of your tax return (Active or Inactive).
8. Indicate whether you have income or do not have income.
9. Check the type of business entity you are submitting information for (Yes or No).
10. Enter your business address, phone number, and EIN.
11. Select the business tax filing status (Active or Inactive).
12. Indicate whether you are an active or inactive business.
13. Enter the founding date of your business.
14. Select the type of business tax filing you are submitting (Yes or No).
