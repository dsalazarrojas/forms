# Device Contact Information Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Device Contact Information Update Form is used to update the contact information of a device. This includes details such as the device's name, contact person, email, phone number, address, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the device's updated contact information.
2. Ensure that you are only updating existing entries for the corresponding device.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Device Details** (`device_contact_info`, text, optional):
  Enter the updated details about the device, including any relevant notes or descriptions.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_details`, text, optional):
  Enter the updated contact information for the device, including the contact person and any other relevant details.
- **Device Identifiers** (`device_identifiers`, text, optional):
  Enter any additional identifiers or labels for the device, such as serial numbers or model numbers.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional):
  Enter the name of the person who will be responsible for contacting regarding the device.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
  Enter the updated email address for the device.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
  Enter the updated phone number for the device.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional):
  Enter the updated address for the device.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional):
  Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the device.
- **Device Type** (`device_type`, select_one, optional):
  Select the type of device from the options: Desktop, Laptop, Tablet, Server, Printer, or Other.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are providing accurate and up-to-date information about the device.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the support team for assistance.
- Only update the form once for each device to avoid duplication of effort.
