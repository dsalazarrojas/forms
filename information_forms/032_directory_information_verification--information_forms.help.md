# Directory Information Verification - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to verify information in the directory. The form is used to update or confirm changes in employee directory information. The form is designed to collect and validate necessary details such as name, surname, email, phone number, job title, job function, and job status. The information provided will be used to ensure that the directory information is up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Form Title` field with the relevant title of the form (optional).
2. Enter your `name` in the `name` field.
3. Enter your `surname` in the `surname` field.
4. Enter your `email` in the `email` field in the correct format (e.g. `example@example.com`).
5. Enter your `phone` number in the `phone` field.
6. Select your `Job Title` from the drop-down menu.
7. Select your `Job Function` (if applicable).
8. Select your `Job Status` from the drop-down menu.
9. If you are updating the information, select the `Update Date` and `Update Time` fields if applicable.
10. Enter the `Reason for Update` if you are updating the information (optional).
11. Enter any additional comments in the `Comments` field (optional).
12. Select whether you `Confirm` or `Decline` the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the relevant title of the form, e.g. 'Verification of Name Change'.
* **name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **surname** (`surname`, `text`, required): Enter your surname.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g. `example@example.com`).
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter your department name (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Select your job title from the drop-down menu.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, `text`, optional): Enter your job function (if applicable).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your job status from the drop-down menu (Active, Inactive, Resigned).
* **Update Date** (`update_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date you are updating the information.
* **Update Time** (`update_time`, `time`, optional): Select the time you are updating the information.
* **Reason for Update** (`reason_for_update`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for updating the information.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether you confirm or decline the information provided.
