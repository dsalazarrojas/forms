# Farmer Crop Information Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Farmer Crop Information Collection Form is a tool to gather accurate and up-to-date information about a farm's crop production. This form helps to collect specific details about the farm's crops, seasons, and yields, which is essential for making informed decisions and planning for future crop development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Farm Name**, which is the name of the farm where the crop is being grown.
2. Provide the **Farm Address**, which is the physical location of the farm where the crop is being grown.
3. Select the **Crop Season** during which the crop will be harvested (Winter, Spring, Summer, or Fall).
4. Choose the **Crop Type**, which is the type of crop being grown (Corn, Soybeans, Wheat, or Sorghum).
5. Select all that apply for **Crop Type**, as multiple options can be chosen.
6. Enter the **Crop Yield**, which is the expected or actual yield of the crop.
7. Enter the **Harvest Date**, which is the date the crop will be harvested.
8. Enter the size of the **Farm Size**, which is the total area of the farm where the crop is being grown.
9. Provide the **Farm Location**, which is the location of the farm where the crop is being grown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Farm Name**: Enter the name of the farm where the crop is being grown.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Farm Address**: Enter the physical location of the farm where the crop is being grown.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Crop Season**: Select the season during which the crop will be harvested (Winter, Spring, Summer, or Fall).
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: false
- **Crop Type**: Select all that apply for the type of crop being grown (Corn, Soybeans, Wheat, or Sorghum).
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Required: false
- **Crop Yield**: Enter the expected or actual yield of the crop.
  - Type: number
  - Required: false
- **Harvest Date**: Enter the date the crop will be harvested.
  - Type: date
  - Required: false
- **Farm Size**: Enter the total area of the farm where the crop is being grown.
  - Type: number
  - Required: false
- **Farm Location**: Enter the location of the farm where the crop is being grown.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
