# Fraternity Message Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Fraternity Message Submission form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from members to improve the fraternity experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, providing your name, email, phone number, and any other relevant information.
2. Select the type of message you wish to send (e.g., suggestion, concern, general message).
3. Enter your message in the text area provided.
4. If you have any additional information or details to provide, use the notes field.
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Message**: Enter a clear and concise description of your message.
* **Message Type**: Choose the type of message you are submitting (e.g., suggestion, concern, general message).
* **Email**: Enter your official email address associated with your fraternity account.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Date**: Enter the date of the submission (automatically filled in).
* **Time**: Enter the time of the submission (automatically filled in).
* **Question**: Provide any additional information or questions related to your message.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or details related to your message.
* **Email Hint**: Enter any additional information related to your email (e.g., department or office).
* **Phone Hint**: Enter any additional information related to your phone number (e.g., department or office).
* **Notes Hint**: Enter any additional notes or details related to your message.
* **Submit**: Click to submit your message for review by the fraternity administration.

## Tips

* Please be descriptive and clear in your message to ensure that your concern or suggestion is understood correctly.
* Avoid submitting duplicate messages on the same issue.
* If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, feel free to contact the fraternity administration directly.
