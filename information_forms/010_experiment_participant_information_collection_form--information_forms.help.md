# Experiment Participant Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about the participant in an experiment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields (indicated by *Required*) and the fields that you feel are necessary for the experiment.
2.  Ensure that the email address is entered in a valid format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
3.  Enter the participant's phone number if required or necessary for the experiment.
4.  Review and submit the form once you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant name** (Participant name, text, *Required*): Enter the name of the participant in the experiment.
*   **Email** (Email, email, *Required*): Enter the participant's email address in a valid format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
*   **Participant phone** (Participant phone, text, *Required*): Enter the participant's phone number (if required or necessary for the experiment).

## Tips
*   Ensure that the participant's contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you are unsure about any field, contact the experimenter or the experiment administrator for clarification.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to the experimenter.
