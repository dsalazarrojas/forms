# open_educational_resource_contributor_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for individuals to register their interest in contributing to our Open Educational Resources (OER) project. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide your contact information and expertise to help us connect with potential contributors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form fields and ensure you have all the required information ready.
2. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter your Institution name in the Institution field.
6. Describe your area of expertise in the Expertise field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your institutional email address or a personal email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for further contact purposes.
* **Institution** (`Institution`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the institution you are affiliated with, if any.
* **Expertise** (`Expertise`, text, required/optional): Describe your area of expertise, including relevant fields of study or areas of interest.
