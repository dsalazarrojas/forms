# General Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Information Collection Form is a versatile form designed to gather various types of information from users. It includes fields for general information, background data, contact details, and specific data such as dates, times, numbers, and text inputs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide accurate and complete information in each field.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (text)**: Enter a brief description of yourself or the topic you are providing information about.
* **Background Data (text)**: Provide any relevant background information about yourself or the topic.
* **Contact Details (text)**: Enter your contact details, including your name, address, and phone number.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Select One (select_one)**: Choose one option from the provided options.
* **Select Multiple (select_multiple)**: Select all applicable options from the list.
* **Date (date)**: Enter a date in the format DD/MM/YY.
* **Time (time)**: Enter a time in the format HH:MM.
* **Note (note)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your information.
* **Decimal (number)**: Enter a decimal number, e.g., 5.25.
* **Integer (number)**: Enter an integer number, e.g., 10.
* **Text (text)**: Enter any text information.
* **Textarea (note)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your information.
* **Check Box (select_multiple)**: Select all applicable options from the list.
* **Radio Box (select_one)**: Choose one option from the list.
* **Select One-Text (select_one)**: Choose one option from the list or enter "Other" for a custom option.
* **Date Range (text)**: Enter a date range, e.g., 01/2020-02/2025.
* **Date & Time (date)**: Enter a date and time in the format DD/MM/YY HH:MM.
* **Month (text)**: Enter a month in the format MMMM.
* **Week (text)**: Enter a week in the format WW.
* **Year (text)**: Enter a year in the format YYYY.
* **Select Multiple-Text (select_multiple)**: Select all applicable options from the list.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to submit your information.

## Tips

* Make sure to review your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If a field is marked as required, it is essential to fill it in to prevent form submission errors.
