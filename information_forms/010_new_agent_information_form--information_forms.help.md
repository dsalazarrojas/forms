# New Agent Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a new agent, which may include contact details, work history, skills, and job-related information. The form is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of the agent's profile.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Enter your Job Title.
5. Input your Work History Start Date.
6. Input your Work History End Date.
7. Select your Background Check Status from the options provided.
8. Choose your interests from the list.
9. Enter your Skills, Education, Work Experience, and any other relevant details.
10. Input your Work Experience Years.
11. Input your Years of Service.
12. Fill in your Status.
13. Enter your Department.
14. Enter your Supervisor's name.
15. Enter your Job Title Manager's name.
16. Enter your Direct Supervisor's name.
17. Enter your Department Supervisor's name.
18. Check the Team Member box if you are part of a team.
19. Enter your Job Position.
20. Enter your Job Level.
21. Enter your Job Type.
22. Enter your Job Category.
23. Enter your Employee Number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Work History Start Date** (`work_history_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you started your work history.
* **Work History End Date** (`work_history_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you ended your work history.
* **Background Check Status** (`background_check_status`, select_one, optional): Select your background check status from the options provided.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, required): Choose your interests from the list.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): Enter your skills.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Enter your educational background.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Enter your work experience.
* **Work Experience Years** (`work_experience_years`, number, optional): Enter the number of years of work experience.
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, number, optional): Enter the number of years of service.
* **Status** (`employee_status`, text, optional): Enter your status.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department.
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor`, text, optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Job Title Manager** (`job_title_manager`, text, optional): Enter your job title manager's name.
* **Direct Supervisor** (`direct_supervisor`, text, optional): Enter your direct supervisor's name.
* **Department Supervisor** (`department_supervisor`, text, optional): Enter your department supervisor's name.
* **Team Member** (`team_member`, text, optional): Check the box if you are part of a team.
* **Job Position** (`job_position`, text, optional): Enter your job position.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, text, optional): Enter your job level.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, optional): Enter your job type.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, text, optional): Enter your job category.
* **Employee Number** (`employee_number`, text, optional): Enter your employee number.
