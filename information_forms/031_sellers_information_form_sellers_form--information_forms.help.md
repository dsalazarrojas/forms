# sellers_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sellers_information_form is used to collect detailed information about the properties being sold or listed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your address in the address field.
2. Enter your city in the city field.
3. Enter your zip code in the zip code field.
4. Select your state from the state dropdown menu.
5. Enter your country in the country field.
6. Enter your email address in the email field.
7. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
8. Select the listing type of your property from the listing type dropdown menu.
9. Select the type of your property from the property type dropdown menu.
10. Enter the price of your property in the price field.
11. Enter the square footage of your property in the sq ft field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your property.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your property.
* **Zip Code** (`zip code`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of your property.
* **State** (`state`, select one, optional): Select the state where your property is located. This field is a dropdown menu with the following options: Alaska, Arizona, California, Florida, Hawaii, Louisiana, New York, Oregon, Texas.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter the country where your property is located.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Listing Type** (`listing_type`, select multiple, optional): Select one or multiple listing types for your property. This field is a dropdown menu with the following options: For Sale, Rent, Lease, Sold.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select multiple, optional): Select one or multiple property types for your property. This field is a dropdown menu with the following options: House, Condo, Land, Mobile Home.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the price of your property.
* **Sq Ft** (`sq ft`, text, optional): Enter the square footage of your property.
