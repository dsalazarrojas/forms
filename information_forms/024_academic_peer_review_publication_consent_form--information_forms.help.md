# Academic Peer Review Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain your consent for the peer review process of an academic publication. It is essential to fill out this form carefully and honestly to ensure that your identity and review comments are handled according to your wishes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Provide your name and email address to ensure that you can be contacted for any follow-up questions or concerns.
- Specify your institution or affiliation to help the publisher verify your credentials.
- Provide your relevant qualifications or expertise related to the manuscript.
- Enter the title of the manuscript being reviewed.
- Enter the name of the journal or publication where the manuscript will be published.
- Select the date when you completed the peer review.
- Choose how you want your identity to be handled during the open review process.
- Decide whether you consent to having your review comments published alongside the article.
- Choose whether you consent to sharing anonymized review data for research purposes.
- Select whether you consent to having your review cited in future work.
- Specify if you have any conflicts of interest related to this review.
- Confirm that your review meets professional standards.
- Enter any special conditions you have for your consent.
- Acknowledge that you understand you can withdraw consent under certain circumstances.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Review Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the peer reviewer.
* **Email Address** (`reviewer_email`, email, required): Provide your professional email address for correspondence.
* **Institution** (`reviewer_institution`, text, required): Specify your institution or organization affiliation.
* **Credentials** (`reviewer_credentials`, text, required): List your relevant qualifications or expertise related to the manuscript.
* **Manuscript Title** (`manuscript_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the manuscript being reviewed.
* **Journal or Publication Name** (`journal_publication_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the journal or publication where the manuscript will be published.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required): Select the date when you completed the peer review.
* **Consent to Open Review** (`consent_to_open_review`, select one, required): Choose how you want your identity to be handled during the open review process.
	+ "Yes, I consent to open review"
	+ "Yes, with conditions"
	+ "No, I prefer anonymous review"
	+ "No opinion"
* **Consent to Review Publication** (`consent_to_review_publication`, select one, required): Decide whether you consent to having your review comments published alongside the article.
	+ "Yes, I consent"
	+ "Yes, with editorial modifications"
	+ "No, I do not consent"
	+ "Conditional on anonymity"
* **Consent to Data Sharing** (`consent_to_data_sharing`, select one, required): Choose whether you consent to sharing anonymized review data for research purposes.
	+ "Yes, I consent"
	+ "Yes, with restrictions"
	+ "No, I do not consent"
	+ "Need more information"
* **Consent to Citation** (`consent_to_citation`, select one, required): Select whether you consent to having your review cited in future work.
	+ "Yes, I consent"
	+ "Yes, with attribution"
	+ "No, I do not consent"
	+ "Conditional on context"
* **Review Modification Rights** (`review_modification_rights`, select one, required): Decide whether you want the right to review and approve any modifications before publication.
	+ "Yes, I want approval rights"
	+ "No, I trust the editors"
	+ "Conditional on major changes"
	+ "Unsure"
* **Conflict of Interest Declaration** (`conflict_of_interest_declaration`, select one, required): Declare any conflicts of interest related to this review.
	+ "No conflicts of interest"
	+ "Have declared in review"
	+ "Have potential conflicts"
	+ "Need to clarify"
* **Review Quality Acknowledgement** (`review_quality_acknowledgement`, select one, required): Confirm that your review meets professional standards.
	+ "Yes, I confirm"
	+ "With minor revisions"
	+ "Need to review again"
	+ "Not applicable"
* **Special Conditions** (`special_conditions`, text, required): Enter any special conditions you have for your consent.
* **Withdrawal Rights Acknowledged** (`withdrawal_rights_acknowledged`, select one, required): Acknowledge that you understand you can withdraw consent under certain circumstances.
	+ "Yes, I understand"
	+ "No, need clarification"
	+ "Have questions"
	+ "Not applicable"
