# Employee Personal Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and store employee personal information for administrative and operational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date Of Birth" field in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
4. If applicable, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. Select your Job Title from the dropdown list in the "Job Title" field.
7. Select your Department from the dropdown list in the "Department" field.
8. Enter your Work Location in the "Work Location" field.
9. If applicable, provide the name of your Reporting Manager in the "Reporting Manager" field.
10. Enter your Job Description in the "Job Description" field.
11. If applicable, provide the name and contact information for your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" fields.
12. If applicable, upload a photo of yourself in the "Employee Photo" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as per your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, optional): Select your job title from the list.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Select all departments that apply to you.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): Enter your current work location.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your reporting manager.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your job role.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Relation** (`emergency_relation`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your relation with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Job Title** (`emergency_job_title`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the job title of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Department** (`emergency_department`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the department of your emergency contact.
* **Employee Photo** (`employee_photo`, note, optional): If applicable, upload a photo of yourself.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current employee status from the list.
