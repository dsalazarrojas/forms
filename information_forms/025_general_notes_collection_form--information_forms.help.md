# General Notes Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a collection of general notes and information from users. It's meant to be a catch-all for any non-specific feedback, inquiries, or other internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in the designated pages. Each page has a unique label and purpose.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Make sure to review your answers before submitting to ensure they're correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Notes** (page_1): This is the main content section where you can provide any general notes or comments. It's a text area where you can type in your thoughts, ideas, or concerns.
* **Category** (page_2): Select 'Yes' if the notes are related to a specific category, otherwise select 'No'. This will help us group and filter your notes for better organization.
* **Priority** (page_3): Choose how urgently you need the notes to be addressed. Select 'Yes' if it's high priority, and 'No' if it's low or not time-sensitive.
* **Files** (page_4): If you have any supporting files or attachments, upload them here. Please ensure the files are relevant and necessary for your notes.
* **Notes** (page_5): This is another text area for any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share.
* **Submission Time** (page_6): Provide the date when you submitted the form. This will help us track when we received your notes.
* **Submission Time** (page_7): This is a time field for when you submitted the form. Please ensure it matches the date you provided above.
* **Email** (page_8): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (page_9): Provide your phone number in case we need to reach out to you for further clarification or questions.
* **Submit** (page_10): Click this button to submit your form and we'll review your notes. Make sure to review your answers before submitting to avoid any mistakes.
