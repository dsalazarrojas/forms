# Bi Lingual Client Information Es En Sef - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients or potential clients to better understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, marked as `required` in the form.
2. Select the preferred language from the options provided.
3. Provide your first and last name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
4. Enter your street address, city, state or province, and postal code.
5. Select the services you are interested in.
6. Enter a brief description of your inquiry.
7. Choose how you heard about us and if you have been a client before.
8. Consent to be contacted and to receive email updates.
9. Provide any additional information or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, `select_one`): Select your preferred language from the options provided.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format Month - Day - Year.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, required): Enter your residential street address.
* **Apartment or Unit Number** (`apartment_unit`, `text`, optional): Enter your apartment or unit number if applicable.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, `text`, required): Enter your state or province.
* **Postal Code or ZIP** (`postal_code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Enter your country.
* **Best Time to Contact You** (`best_contact_time`, `select_one`, required): Select the best time to contact you.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred contact method.
* **Services You Are Interested In** (`services_interested`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all services you are interested in.
* **Brief Description of Your Inquiry** (`brief_description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your inquiry.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, required): Select how you heard about us.
* **Have You Been a Client Before** (`previous_client`, `select_one`, required): Select if you have been a client before.
* **I Consent to Being Contacted** (`communication_consent`, `select_one`, required): Consent to being contacted.
* **I Would Like to Receive Email Updates** (`email_consent`, `select_one`, required): Consent to receive email updates.
* **Additional Information or Comments** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information or comments.
