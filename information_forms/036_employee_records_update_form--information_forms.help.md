# Employee Records Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Records Update Form is a tool for updating employee information in the organization's database. Use this form to make changes to existing employee records, ensuring that the data is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Ensure you have the correct employee ID.
	* Provide the correct employee name.
	* Choose the correct department and job title.
2. Review and update the optional fields as necessary:
	* Enter the employee's date of birth.
	* Enter the employee's email address.
	* Enter the employee's phone number.
	* Provide the reason for the update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID (#1)**: Enter the employee's ID number to ensure that the changes are applied to the correct employee record.
* **Employee Name (#2)**: Enter the employee's full name as it should appear in the database.
* **Department (#3)**: Select the employee's current department from the provided options.
* **Job Title (#4)**: Enter the employee's current job title.
* **Date of Birth (#5)**: Enter the employee's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Email (#6)**: Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone Number (#7)**: Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Update Reason (#8)**: Provide a brief description of the reason for the update.

## Tips
* Double-check that you have entered the correct employee ID to avoid errors.
* Be accurate when updating department and job title to avoid misclassifying the employee.
* Use the correct format for the date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD).
* Ensure that the email address and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
