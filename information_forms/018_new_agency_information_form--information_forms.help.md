# New Agency Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about agencies seeking to provide services or collaborate with our organization. Please fill out this form to help us better understand your agency's details and tailor our support accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Agency Name**: Enter the name of your agency.
2. **Contact Person**: Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for your agency.
3. **Agency Phone**: Enter the phone number of your agency.
4. **Agency Address**: Enter the physical address of your agency.
5. **Agency City**: Enter the city where your agency is located.
6. **Agency Region**: Enter the region where your agency is located.
7. **Agency Postal Code**: Enter the postal code (zip code) of your agency.
8. **Agency Email**: Enter your agency's email address.
9. **Agency Website**: Enter the website URL of your agency.
10. **How did you hear about us?**: Describe how you found out about our organization.
11. **What do you offer?**: Briefly describe the services or projects your agency provides.
12. **Number of Students**: Enter the number of students your agency has served (if applicable).
13. **Start Date**: Enter the date you started your agency (if applicable).
14. **End Date**: Enter the date your agency ended (if applicable).
15. **Agency Type**: Select the type of agency you represent (Local, International, or Both).
16. **How long have you worked?**: Select how long you have been working in your position (options: Less than 6 months, 6 months to 1 year, 1 to 2 years, 2-5 years, More than 5 years).
17. **How many students served?**: Enter the number of students you have served (if applicable).
18. **What is your position?**: Enter your role or title within your agency.
19. **What is your role?**: Enter a brief description of your role within your agency.
20. **Agency Description**: Enter a brief description of your agency.
21. **How do you plan to use?**: Select how you plan to use our services (Internship Program, Research Project, Both, or Other).
22. **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about your agency.
23. **Number of Students Served**: Enter the number of students you have served (if applicable).
24. **Start Date**: Enter the date you started providing services (if applicable).
25. **End Date**: Enter the date you ended providing services (if applicable).
