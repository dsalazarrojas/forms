# tzs_foundation_scholarship_2020_information_seeking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about potential partners for the 2020 Tzs Foundation Scholarship. The data collected will be used to verify and update our internal records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
2. Fill in your college name if you have a partnership with Tzs Foundation.
3. Specify the contact person who would be best able to address any questions or concerns regarding the partnership.
4. Provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.
5. Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
6. Give us the address where we can contact you in case of any issues.
7. Select all potential partner universities that you are or will be partnering with.
8. Indicate if you believe you or your institution has the potential to partner with us in the 2020 scholarship.
9. Add any additional comments or information about your partnership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **College Name**: Enter the name of your college or university. Please make sure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Contact Person**: Please specify the person who would be best able to address any questions or concerns regarding your partnership.
* **Email**: Provide a valid email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Address**: Enter your address so we can know where to contact you if needed.
* **Potential Partners**: Select all partner universities that you are or will be partnering with from the dropdown list.
* **Is Potential**: Check if you believe you or your institution has the potential to partner with us.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your partnership.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any of the information, please contact us and we will assist you.
- Please ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
- We will review and verify the information provided before updating our internal records.
