# Parent Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parent Information Form is designed to collect vital information from parents or guardians about their child or student, including contact preferences, emergency contact details, and academic information. This form will help schools or organizations provide personalized support and services to the student.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering the student's **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide the student's **Email** and **Phone Number** for contact purposes, if applicable.
3. Enter the student's **Home Address**, if needed.
4. Select the student's preferred **Contact Preference** (Email, Phone, or Both).
5. Choose the student's **Contact Method** (Email or Phone).
6. Enter the **Emergency Contact First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** (if applicable) to ensure we can reach out to them in case of emergencies.
7. Specify the **Relationship** between the student and the emergency contact (Parent, Guardian, or Other).
8. Enter the student's **Student ID** and **Date of Birth** to keep their records up-to-date.
9. Select the student's **Grade Level** (K-2, 3-5, 6-8, 9-12, High School, or College).
10. If applicable, select the student's **Section** and **Class Level** (K-2, 3-5, 6-8, 9-12, High School, or College).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the student's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the student's last name.
* **Email**: Enter the student's email address for contact purposes.
* **Home Address**: Enter the student's home address, if needed.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the student's phone number, if applicable.
* **Contact Preference**: Choose how the student would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Both).
* **Contact Method**: Choose the primary contact method (Email or Phone).
* **Emergency Contact First Name**: Enter the emergency contact's first name.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name**: Enter the emergency contact's last name.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number, if applicable.
* **Relationship**: Specify the relationship between the student and the emergency contact (Parent, Guardian, or Other).
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Grade Level**: Select the student's grade level (K-2, 3-5, 6-8, 9-12, High School, or College).
* **Class Level**: Select the student's class level, if applicable (K-2, 3-5, 6-8, 9-12, High School, or College).
* **Section**: Enter the student's section, if applicable.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the student.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
* If a field is not applicable, please leave it blank.
* If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
