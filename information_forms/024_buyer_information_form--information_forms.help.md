# Buyer Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Buyer Information Form is a critical step in the buying process, ensuring that the company has accurate and relevant information about the buyer. This form helps the company to better understand the buyer's needs and preferences, facilitating a smooth and efficient buying experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. 
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. 
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field. This should be a valid email address that can receive messages from the company.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
5. If you have a **Company Name**, you may enter it in the "Company Name" field, but this is optional.
6. If you have a **Job Title**, you may enter it in the "Job Title" field, but this is also optional.
7. Enter your **Mailing Address** in the "Mailing Address" field, including the street address.
8. If applicable, enter your **City**, **State or Province**, and **ZIP or Postal Code**. These fields are optional.
9. Select your preferred **Preferred Contact Method** from the options provided.
10. Enter your **Best Time to Contact** if available. If not, you can leave this field empty.
11. Enter your **Industry** if applicable.
12. If applicable, select the size of your **Company Size** from the options provided.
13. Enter any **Additional Notes** you wish to provide, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`buyer_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`buyer_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email Address** (`buyer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address here, ensuring it is valid and can receive messages from us.
* **Phone Number** (`buyer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): If you represent a company, enter the name here.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific job title, enter it here.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your street address here.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter your city here, if applicable.
* **State or Province** (`state`, `text`, optional): Enter your state or province here, if applicable.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, optional): Enter your ZIP or postal code here, if applicable.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, optional): Enter your country here, if applicable.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Text Message, Mail).
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_contact_time`, `text`, optional): Enter the best time to contact you, if you are available.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, optional): Enter your industry here, if applicable.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select the size of your company from the options provided.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information that may be relevant to your inquiry.

Tips:

* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Use valid email addresses and phone numbers for the contact fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the company directly.
