# BHHS Generic - Buyer(s) Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from buyers about their preferences and requirements for a home purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing the primary buyer's information, including their name, phone number, and email.
2. If there is a secondary buyer, you can provide their information in the corresponding sections.
3. Answer the questions about your pre-approval status, down payment amount, and financing needs.
4. Specify your preferred price range and desired location or area.
5. Optionally, you can provide additional notes or special requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Primary Buyer Name**: This is the full name of the primary buyer, as it appears on their identification or driver's license.
* **Primary Buyer Phone**: This is the primary buyer's phone number, including any extensions or area codes.
* **Primary Buyer Email**: This is the primary buyer's email address, where the agent can contact them.
* **Secondary Buyer Name**: If there is a secondary buyer, this field is used to provide their full name.
* **Secondary Buyer Phone**: This is the secondary buyer's phone number, including any extensions or area codes.
* **Secondary Buyer Email**: This is the secondary buyer's email address, where the agent can contact them.
* **Are you pre-approved for a mortgage?**: Please select "True" if you have already obtained pre-approval for a mortgage, "False" if you haven't, or "In Process" if you are currently in the process of getting pre-approval.
* **Down Payment Amount Available**: This is the amount you are prepared to pay as a down payment. Please enter the amount in dollars.
* **Will financing be needed?**: Please select "True" if you will need financing, "False" if you will pay cash, or "Maybe" if you are unsure.
* **Preferred Price Range**: This is the minimum and maximum price you are willing to pay for the home. Please enter the price range in the format "Min-Max" (e.g., "300,000-400,000").
* **Timeline for Purchase**: This is the date you hope to close the purchase by.
* **Preferred Property Type**: Please select the type of property you are looking for, such as single-family home, condo, townhome, or other.
* **Must-Have Features**: Please list any features that the home must have, such as a specific number of bedrooms or bathrooms.
* **Desired Location or Area**: This is the neighborhood or city you are interested in.
* **Additional Notes or Special Requirements**: Please provide any additional information or requirements you have for the home, such as a specific school district or pet restrictions.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the agent.
* This form is designed to be completed in a few minutes, so take your time and be accurate.
