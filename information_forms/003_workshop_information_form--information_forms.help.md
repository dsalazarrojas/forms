# Workshop Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Workshop Information Form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by the *required* label.
2. Enter the Workshop Name, which should be a brief title of the workshop.
3. Enter the name of the Organizer, who is responsible for the workshop.
4. Enter the Start Date and End Date of the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Name (text)**: A brief title of the workshop.
* **Organizer (text)**: The person or organization responsible for the workshop.
* **Start Date (date)**: The date when the workshop starts.
* **End Date (date)**: The date when the workshop ends.
