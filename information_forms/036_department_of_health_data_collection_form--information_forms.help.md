# Department Of Health Data Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients to aid in their medical care and treatment.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the following steps:
1. Fill in your user information (page 1).
2. Enter the date you visited the doctor (page 2).
3. Describe your symptoms (page 3).
4. Choose the department you visited (page 4).
5. Enter the name of your doctor (page 5).
6. Select your doctor's specialty (page 6).
7. Enter your medical record number (page 7).
8. Choose your contact method (page 8).
9. Enter your department location (page 9).
10. Describe your medical condition (page 10).
11. Enter your medical history (page 11).
12. List any allergies you have (page 12).
13. Enter any medical test results (page 13).
14. List any medication you are taking (page 14).
15. List any medical devices you are using (page 15).
16. Enter your contact information (page 16).
17. Enter your doctor's contact information (page 17).
18. Enter your address (page 18).
19. Enter your doctor's address (page 19).
20. Enter your visit frequency (page 20).
21. Enter the duration of your visit (page 21).
22. Indicate if you need a follow-up (page 22).
23. Enter any follow-up notes (page 23).
24. Enter the follow-up date (page 24).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (1)**: Please enter your name, age, and any other relevant information.
* **Date Visited (2)**: Enter the date you visited the doctor.
* **Symptoms (3)**: Describe your symptoms, such as pain, fever, or other health issues.
* **Department (4)**: Choose the department you visited, e.g., cardiology, dermatology.
* **Doctor Name (5)**: Enter the name of your doctor.
* **Doctor Specialty (6)**: Select your doctor's specialty, such as cardiology, endocrinology.
* **Medical Record Number (7)**: Enter your medical record number.
* **Contact Information (8)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted, e.g., email, phone, in-person.
* **Department Location (9)**: Choose the location of the department you visited, e.g., New York, Los Angeles.
* **Medical Condition (10)**: Describe your medical condition, e.g., high blood pressure, diabetes.
* **Medical History (11)**: Enter your medical history, e.g., previous illnesses or surgeries.
* **Allergies (12)**: List any allergies you have, e.g., peanuts, shellfish.
* **Medical Test Results (13)**: Enter any medical test results, e.g., blood work, imaging studies.
* **Medication (14)**: List any medication you are taking, e.g., antibiotics, pain medication.
* **Medical Device Used (15)**: List any medical devices you are using, e.g., pacemaker, oxygen tank.
* **Patient Contact (16)**: Enter your contact information, e.g., email, phone number.
* **Doctor Phone (17)**: Enter your doctor's contact phone number.
* **Doctor Contact Method (18)**: Choose how your doctor would like to be contacted, e.g., email, phone.
* **Patient Address (19)**: Enter your address.
* **Doctor Address (20)**: Enter your doctor's address.
* **Visit Frequency (21)**: Enter the frequency of your visits, e.g., weekly, monthly.
* **Visit Duration (22)**: Enter the duration of your visits, e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour.
* **Follow-up (23)**: Indicate if you need a follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Note (24)**: Enter any follow-up notes or instructions.
* **Follow-up Date (25)**: Enter the date for your follow-up appointment.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your doctor or medical staff.
* This form is for your health and well-being, so take your time and fill it out carefully.
