# Walt Disney World Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from visitors about their experiences at the Walt Disney World theme park. The gathered data will help Disney staff analyze visitors' satisfaction levels and identify areas for improvement to enhance the overall park experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the answer choices as prompted by the options provided for each field.
2. Enter your details in the designated fields for the "Visit Date" and "Visit Time" fields.
3. Choose the relevant options for the "Walt Disney World Address" field (Yes or No).
4. Enter any additional comments or information in the "Contact Information" field, if needed.
5. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Walt Disney World Address**: This field asks if you have visited Walt Disney World. You can select either "Yes" or "No" from the options provided.
* **Visit Date**: This field is for entering the date of your visit to Walt Disney World. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Visit Time**: This field is for entering the time of your visit to Walt Disney World. Please enter the time in the format HH:MM.
* **Park Information**: This field is similar to "Walt Disney World Address" and asks if you have visited any park information or not. You can select either "Yes" or "No" from the options provided.
* **Contact Information**: This field is for any additional comments or information you'd like to share with Disney staff. You can enter any relevant details here.

## Tips

* Please be as accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any issues or concerns during your visit, please contact Disney staff directly.
* The information collected will be used to improve the overall park experience for future visitors.
