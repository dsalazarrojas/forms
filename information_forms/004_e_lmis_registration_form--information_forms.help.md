## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **e_lmis_registration_form_name** (text, Optional): This field allows you to enter a clear and concise name that describes your registration.
* **e_lmis_user_type** (Select One, Required): This field allows you to select whether you are registering as an individual user or an organization.
* **e_lmis_facility_info** (Number, Optional): This field is used to store any relevant information about your facility, such as its name and location.
* **e_lmis_registration_date** (Date, Required): This field is used to store the date of your registration. Please select a valid date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **e_lmis_email** (Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **e_lmis_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number that can be used to contact you.
* **e_lmis_additional_info** (Text, Optional): This field is used to store any additional information that is relevant to your registration. Please be brief but clear in your description.
* **e_lmis_agreed_terms**, **e_lmis_agreed_terms_2, **e_lmis_agreed_terms_3**, **e_lmis_agreed_terms_4**, **e_lmis_agreed_terms_5** (Select Multiple, Required): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms of service for the eLMIS system.
