# Pet Owner Data Correction Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Owner Data Correction Request Form is for pet owners to correct inaccuracies in their pet's data. This form will allow you to update your pet's profile with new information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the fields below and fill in the correct information for your pet.
2. Make sure to check the "Confirm update" field to ensure you want to make the changes.
3. Submit the form to request the updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet information**: Enter a brief description of your pet.
* **Owner information**: Enter your name, address, phone number, and email address.
* **Update request**: Enter a brief explanation of why you are requesting updates.
* **Pet name**: Enter your pet's new name.
* **Pet type**: Select your pet's species (Cat, Dog, or Other).
* **Pet color**: Select your pet's color (Brown, Gray, Black, White, Red, Blue, Green, or Other).
* **Pet breed**: Select your pet's breed (Mixed, Purebred, or Unknown).
* **Age**: Enter your pet's new age.
* **Weight**: Enter your pet's new weight.
* **Sex**: Select your pet's sex (Male, Female, or Unknown).
* **Date of birth**: Enter your pet's new date of birth.
* **Date of death**: Enter your pet's date of death (if applicable).
* **Veterinary info**: Enter any relevant veterinary information.
* **Update note**: Enter any additional comments about the updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Country**: Select your country of residence (USA, Canada, Mexico, or Other).
* **State or Province**: Select your state or province of residence (California, New York, Texas, or Other).
* **City**: Enter your city of residence.
* **Postal Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Confirm update**: Check this field to confirm you want to make the changes.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the updates.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your updates.
