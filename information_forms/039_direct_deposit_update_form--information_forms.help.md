# direct_deposit_update_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to update information related to a direct deposit account, such as updating account numbers, bank details, or account ownership.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields carefully and ensure you are updating the correct information.
2. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Choose the relevant options for the 'Direct Deposit Bank Account' and 'Account Type'.
4. Add any additional information in the 'Update Reason' field to explain the changes being made.
5. Submit the form after completing all necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Direct Debit Account Number**: Enter the direct debit account number for the employee's account.
* **Bank Name**: Enter the name of the bank associated with the direct deposit account.
* **Routing Number**: Enter the routing number for the direct deposit account.
* **Account Number**: Enter the account number for the direct deposit account.
* **Bank Phone**: Enter the phone number for the bank.
* **Bank Account Name**: Enter the name of the direct deposit account.
* **Bank Routing Number**: Enter the routing number for the bank.
* **Direct Deposit Bank Account**: Select the bank that manages the direct deposit account from the options listed.
* **Account Type**: Choose the type of account the update is related to (Checking, Savings, or Other).
* **Update Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for the update (optional).
* **Account Owner**: Choose the owner of the account being updated (Account Owner 1, Account Owner 2, or Account Owner 3).

## Tips

- Double-check the information you enter to ensure accuracy.
- Use the 'Direct Deposit Bank Account' field to select the correct bank from the options.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult with your internal stakeholders or form creators for clarification.
