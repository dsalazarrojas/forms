# Property Move In Information For The Buyer Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about the buyer's move-in details. Please fill out this form accurately to enable the property manager to process your move-in efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information and any additional details required.
2. Ensure you have filled out all the required fields accurately and completely.
3. Double-check your submission for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (1)** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your full first name.
* **Last Name (2)** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your full last name.
* **Move-In Date (3)** (`move_in_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to move-in to the property.
* **Time Of Arrival (4)** (`time_of_arrival`, time, required): Enter the time you plan to arrive at the property.
* **Contact Number (5)** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number for any communication regarding your move-in.
* **Email Address (6)** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for any communication regarding your move-in.
* **Address (7)** (`address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property you are moving into.
* **Property Details (8)** (`property_details`, text, required): Provide any additional details about the property or your move-in experience.
* **Contact Person (9)** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person you are authorized to contact regarding your move-in.
* **Property Manager (10)** (`property_manager`, text, required): Enter the name of the property manager who is handling your move-in.
* **Property Owner (11)** (`property_owner`, text, required): Enter the name of the property owner.
* **Buyer Details (12)** (`buyer_details`, text, required): Provide any additional details about yourself as the buyer.
* **Property Insurance (13)** (`property_insurance`, select_one, required): Check if you have property insurance.
* **Parking Details (14)** (`parking_details`, text, required): Enter any details about parking arrangements or concerns.
* **Vehicle Make (15)** (`vehicle_make`, text, required): Enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model (16)** (`vehicle_model`, text, required): Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **License Plate (17)** (`license_plate`, text, required): Enter the license plate number of your vehicle.
* **Parking Location (18)** (`parking_location`, text, required): Enter the location where you will park your vehicle.
* **Property Access (19)** (`property_access`, select_multiple, required): Check if you have access to the property and if so, what type of access you have.
* **Utilities (20)** (`utilities`, text, required): Enter any utility information or concerns you may have.
* **Notes (21)** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes or concerns you may have.
* **Move In Inspector (22)** (`move_in_inspector`, text, required): Enter the name of the move-in inspector.
* **Move In Inspector Phone (23)** (`move_in_inspector_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the move-in inspector.
* **Property Move-In Date (24)** (`property_move_in_date`, date, required): Select the date of the property move-in.
* **Move In Inspector Time (25)** (`move_in_inspector_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the move-in inspection.
