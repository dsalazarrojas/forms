# Petition for Study Resource Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is designed to collect and track petitions for study resources from students or supporters. It allows users to request resources such as books, software, or equipment for academic or professional development.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by selecting the type of resource you are petitioning for.
2.  Fill out the Student and Supporter Details section with relevant information about yourself or the student you are supporting.
3.  In the Resource Requests section, provide detailed descriptions of the resources you need.
4.  In the Supporter Requests section, if applicable, provide any specific requests from supporters.
5.  Add any additional information in the Petition Description field to explain your petition.
6.  Finally, provide your contact information (Email and Phone number) so that we can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Submit Petition** (select_one): Choose the type of resource you are petitioning for, such as a book, software, or equipment.
*   **Student and Supporter Details** (select_multiple): Provide information about yourself or the student you are supporting, including student name, grade level, class, teacher name, school year.
*   **Resource Requests** (text): Describe the resources you need, including the quantity and any specific requirements.
*   **Supporter Requests** (text): If you are petitioning on behalf of a supporter, provide any requests from them.
*   **List of Supporters** (text): List the names and details of supporters for the resource petition.
*   **Petition Description** (text): Briefly describe the purpose and context of your petition.
*   **Resource Requests Date** (date): If applicable, provide a specific date for the resource you are requesting.
*   **Resource Requests Time** (time): If applicable, provide a specific time for the resource you are requesting.
*   **Email** (email): Provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.
*   **Phone** (text): Provide your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
*   **Teacher Name** (text): If applicable, provide the name and contact information of your teacher or instructor.
*   **School Year** (text): If applicable, provide the school year or academic term for which the resource is being requested.
