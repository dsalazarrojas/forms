# submit_a_forum_post - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the submission of a forum post, allowing users to share their thoughts, ideas, or questions on a specific topic. By filling out this form, users can select a category, add relevant tags, attach files, express their consent, select rules to apply, and specify a date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a category that best describes the nature of your post.
2. Enter a relevant tag to help categorize and search your post.
3. (Optional) Attach a file to support your post.
4. Indicate your consent by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
5. Select any applicable rules that govern your post.
6. Specify a date for your post.
7. Specify a time for your post.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select a category that best describes the nature of your post.
* **Enter Tag** (`tag`, `text`, required/optional): Add a relevant tag to help categorize and search your post.
* **Attach File** (`attachment`, `note`, required/optional): (Optional) Attach a file to support your post. Please ensure the file is in a supported format.
* **I Agree** (`consent`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate your consent by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'. This typically indicates that you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
* **Select Rules** (`rule`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select any applicable rules that govern your post. Check the available options carefully.
* **Select Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Specify a date for your post. This will help with scheduling and organization.
* **Select Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Specify a time for your post. This will help with scheduling and organization.
