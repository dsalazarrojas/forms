# Permanent Makeup Client Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information about the client for permanent makeup services. It helps our team assess and prepare the client for the procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your title (e.g., Mr./Ms./Mrs./Dr.).
2. Fill in your name.
3. Enter your email address (if you have one).
4. Provide your phone number.
5. Enter your address.
6. If applicable, fill in your medical condition, medical history, and other relevant information.
7. If you have any medical devices or implants, please provide details about them.
8. Enter your date of birth.
9. If necessary, provide additional medical history information.
