# Generic Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Generic Information Form is a general-purpose form designed to collect information from users. It is intended to be adaptable and reusable across various contexts, such as surveys, registrations, and data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields marked as "required."
2.  Enter your contact details, including any relevant information such as name, phone number, and email address.
3.  Provide your profile details, if applicable.
4.  Enter your date of birth.
5.  (Optional) Select a time of contact, if applicable.
6.  Enter the date of visit, if relevant.
7.  Add any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Details**: This field is used to collect contact information. If required, please fill in your name, phone number, and email address.
*   **Profile Details**: This field is optional and allows you to provide additional information about yourself.
*   **Date of Birth**: This field is required and collects your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Time of Contact**: This field is optional and allows you to select a time of contact. If you're unsure, you can leave it blank.
*   **Date of Visit**: This field is optional and collects the date of visit in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Notes**: This field is optional and allows you to enter any additional comments or information.
