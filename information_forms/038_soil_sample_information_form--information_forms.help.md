# Soil Sample Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record information about soil samples, including their identification, location, and condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the required fields**: Ensure that you complete all the required fields, marked as "required: true".
2. **Fill in the optional fields**: For optional fields, provide as much information as you need or have available.
3. **Double-check your input**: Review your input carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Sample ID (`Sample ID`, `text`, required: true)
Enter the unique identifier assigned to the soil sample.

### Laboratory Location (`laboratory location`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the location where the laboratory is situated.

### Sample Date (`Sample Date`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date the soil sample was collected.

### Sample Time (`Sample Time`, `time`, required: false)
Enter the time the soil sample was collected.

### Sample Weight (`Sample Weight`, `number`, required: false)
Enter the weight of the soil sample.

### Sample Description (`Sample Description`, `text`, required: true)
Describe the soil sample, including any relevant notes or comments.

### Soil Type (`Soil Type`, `select_one`, required: false)
Choose the type of soil from the available options (Yes/No).

### Sample Container (`Sample Container`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Choose the type of container used to store the soil sample from the available options (Yes/No).

### Sample Condition (`Sample Condition`, `select_one`, required: false)
Choose the condition of the soil sample from the available options (Yes/No).

### Sampling Technique (`Sampling Technique`, `text`, required: true)
Describe the technique used to collect the soil sample.

### Sampling Equipment (`Sampling Equipment`, `text`, required: true)
Describe the equipment used to collect the soil sample.

### Submitted By (`Submitted By`, `text`, required: true)
Enter your name as the person who submitted the soil sample.

### Submit Date (`Submit Date`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date the form was submitted.

### Laboratory Received (`Laboratory Received`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date the laboratory received the soil sample.

### Lab Sample ID (`Lab Sample ID`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the laboratory's internal identifier for the soil sample.

## Tips
- Always fill out the required fields accurately and completely.
- Double-check your input for any errors or inconsistencies.
- Ensure that you understand the purpose of each field before filling it out.
- If you're unsure about a particular field, please consult a supervisor or relevant documentation.
