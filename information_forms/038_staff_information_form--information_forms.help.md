# staff_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and display staff member information for staff management, reporting, and other administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your staff name in the `staff name` field.
2. Enter your job title in the `job_title` field.
3. Select your department from the `department` field.
4. Indicate your employment status by selecting the corresponding option from the `employment_status` field.
5. Enter your contact email address in the `contact_email` field.
6. Enter your contact phone number in the `contact_phone` field.
7. If applicable, fill in your job title ID number in the `job_title_id` field.
8. Enter your hire date in the `hire_date` field.
9. If applicable, enter your termination date in the `termination_date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, Optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Job Title** (`staff_title`, text, Optional): Enter your current or most recent job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, Optional): Select your department or team name.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, Optional): Choose your current employment status from the list:
	+ Active: Currently employed.
	+ Inactive: No longer employed.
	+ Pending: Employment status is pending or under review.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title ID** (`job_title_id`, number, Optional): Enter your job title ID number.
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, Optional): Enter your hire date if applicable.
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, date, Optional): Enter your termination date if applicable.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and consistently to maintain reliable data.
* Use the correct format for dates and times as indicated by the field type.
* If unsure about a field, refer to your official documents or HR records.
* This form is not intended to replace any other system or form used for staff management and reporting.
