# Residency Condition Removal Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help users remove their residency conditions, allowing them to transition out of residency programs. This form is a critical step in the residency removal process and requires accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review each section of the form and ensure that you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, paying attention to format requirements for date and time fields.
3. Use the provided options for select fields to ensure you make the correct choice.
4. Upload any required documents before submitting the form.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Documents**: This is the first section of the form where you can upload any required documents. This could include identification documents, residency agreements, or other relevant paperwork. Ensure that all necessary documents are uploaded and in the correct format.
* **Confirm Residency Requirements**: This section is a selection field where you must choose which residency requirements you are removing. Choose the correct options that apply to your situation.
* **Declare Residency Status**: This is a select multiple field where you will declare your current residency status. Select both Active and Inactive if your status is currently Active but you have also had Inactive periods. 
* **Signed Declaration Form**: This field is for signing a declaration form, but no further action is required here. Please do not attempt to fill out this field. 
* **Submit Form**: This is the last section of the form, which will submit your answers for review and processing. 
* **Upload Additional Documents**: In this section, you can upload any additional documents related to your residency status. This could include letters from authorities, medical documents, or other relevant paperwork. Ensure that all uploaded documents are in the correct format and are relevant to your situation.
* **Confirm Residency Date**: This field is for selecting a date when your residency status changes. Please ensure that you enter the correct date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Confirm Residency Time**: This field is for selecting a time when your residency status changes. Please ensure that you enter the correct time in the correct 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
