# City Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The City Information Collection Form is a standardized form used to submit information about a city or neighborhood where a collection is happening. The form ensures that collected data is accurate, reliable, and meets the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, following the instructions in this guide.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
3. Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.
4. Choose the survey area or neighborhood where the collection is happening.
5. Select the data collection methods used.
6. Enter the estimated number of expected responses.
7. Describe the primary objective of the collection briefly.
8. Describe the data privacy measures in place.
9. Enter the submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Organization name** (`organization_name`, text, required)
The name of the agency or group collecting the data.

*   This field is required and must be filled out accurately.
*   Enter the full name of the agency or group responsible for the data collection.

### **Contact person** (`contact_person`, text, required)
The name of the primary contact person for the collection.

*   This field is required and must be filled out accurately.
*   Enter the name of the primary person responsible for the collection.

### **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required)
The email address of the primary contact person.

*   This field is required and must be filled out accurately.
*   Enter the email address of the primary person responsible for the collection.

### **Survey area or neighborhood** (`survey_area`, text, required)
The geographic coverage of the collection.

*   This field is required and must be filled out accurately.
*   Enter the name of the city or neighborhood where the collection is happening.

### **Target population** (`population_target`, text, optional)
The demographic groups included in the collection.

*   This field is optional and may be filled out if applicable.
*   Enter a brief description of the demographic groups included in the collection.

### **Data collection methods** (`data_collection_methods`, select_multiple, required)
Choose the methods used for the collection.

*   This field is required and must be filled out accurately.
*   Select one or more of the following options: Online survey, Face to face, Phone interview, Administrative records.

### **Estimated sample size** (`estimated_sample_size`, number, optional)
The number of expected responses.

*   This field is optional and may be filled out if applicable.
*   Enter the estimated number of expected responses.

### **Primary objective** (`primary_objective`, text, required)
A brief description of the primary objective of the collection.

*   This field is required and must be filled out accurately.
*   Enter a brief description of the primary objective of the collection.

### **Data privacy measures** (`data_privacy_measures`, text, optional)
Describe the safeguards in place for data privacy.

*   This field is optional and may be filled out if applicable.
*   Enter a brief description of the data privacy measures in place.

### **Submission date** (`submission_date`, date, required)
The date the form is submitted.

*   This field is required and must be filled out accurately.
*   Enter the current date.

Tips:

*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
*   Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.
*   Double-check the form before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
*   If you have any questions or issues with the form, contact the designated contact person.
*   Review the form submission guidelines for additional information.
