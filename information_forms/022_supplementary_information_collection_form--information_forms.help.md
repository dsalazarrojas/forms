# Supplementary Information Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Supplementary Information Collection Form is used to gather additional information required for the case, such as contact preferences, document uploads, and follow-up details. This form is essential for ensuring that all relevant data is collected and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Case Number in the designated field.
2. Select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or None) in the Contact Preference field.
3. Upload the required documents (Attachment 1, Attachment 2, and Attachment 3) by selecting the corresponding options in the Document Upload field.
4. Provide any additional information relevant to the case in the Additional Information field.
5. Confirm the uploads by typing 'Confirm Uploads' in the Confirm Uploads field.
6. Select the follow-up details (True or False) in the Follow Up Details field.
7. Enter the contact person's name in the Contact Person field.
8. Enter the contact person's email address in the Contact Email field.
9. Enter the contact person's phone number in the Contact Phone field.
10. Select the follow-up status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown) in the Follow Up Status field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Case Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the case.
* **Contact Preference**: Select how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or None).
* **Document Upload**: Upload the required documents (Attachment 1, Attachment 2, and Attachment 3).
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information relevant to the case.
* **Upload Document**: This field is reserved for future use, please do not fill it out.
* **Upload Another Document**: This field is also reserved for future use, please do not fill it out.
* **Confirm Uploads**: Confirm the uploads by typing 'Confirm Uploads'.
* **Follow Up Details**: Select whether follow-up is required (True or False).
* **Follow Up Contact**: Enter the contact person's name.
* **Follow Up Note**: Provide any additional follow-up notes relevant to the case.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Follow Up Status**: Select the follow-up status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Upload the correct documents to ensure that all relevant information is collected.
* Be clear and concise when providing additional information.
* Confirm uploads carefully to avoid mistakes.
* Ensure that the contact person's information is up-to-date.
* Choose the correct follow-up status to ensure that follow-up actions are taken accordingly.
