# Confidential Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for confidential information gathering from clients or individuals who are requesting specific services or benefits. It is essential to fill it out accurately and completely to ensure proper processing of your request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have provided accurate and true information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name** (`full_name`, text, required): This field requires your full legal name, as per your identification documents.
* **Date of Submission** (`date_of_submission`, date, required): Please enter the date on which you are submitting this form.
* **National ID or Passport Number** (`id_number`, text, required): For identity verification purposes, please enter your national ID or passport number.
* **Primary Financial Institution** (`primary_financial_institution`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter your primary financial institution's name.
* **Estimated Annual Income Range** (`annual_income_range`, select_one, required): Select one of the following income ranges to provide an accurate estimate:
	+ Below $30,000
	+ $30,000 - $60,000
	+ $60,000 - $100,000
	+ $100,000 - $250,000
	+ Over $250,000
* **Current Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Select one of the following employment statuses:
	+ Employed Full-Time
	+ Employed Part-Time
	+ Self-Employed
	+ Student
	+ Retired
	+ Unemployed
* **Last 4 Digits of Social Security Number** (`social_security_last_four`, number, optional): If applicable, please enter the last 4 digits of your Social Security Number.
* **Primary Email Address** (`secure_email`, email, required): Enter your secure email address.
* **Primary Phone Number** (`secure_phone`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Preferred Secure Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Select one of the following contact methods:
	+ Email
	+ Phone Call
	+ Secure Portal
	+ Postal Mail
* **I declare that the information provided is true and accurate** (`accuracy_declaration`, select_one, required): Declare that the information provided is true and accurate:
	+ I so declare
	+ Other
* **I have read and agree to the Privacy Policy** (`privacy_policy_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the Privacy Policy:
	+ I agree
	+ Other
