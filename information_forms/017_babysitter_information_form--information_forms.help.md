# Babysitter Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from babysitters to ensure a safe and quality experience for children under their care. It is crucial for parents to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to make informed decisions when selecting a babysitter.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all the required information available, including your personal details, professional background, and any relevant certifications.
2. Complete the form in its entirety, paying attention to the required fields (marked as "true").
3. Enter the correct information in the provided fields, taking note of the specific instructions and hints for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name** (`sitter_full_name`, true, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Current Residential Address** (`sitter_address`, true, required): Provide your current home address, including the street number and name, apartment or suite number, and postal code.
* **Mobile Phone Number** (`sitter_phone`, true, required): Enter your mobile phone number for potential contact and communication purposes.
* **Email Address** (`sitter_email`, true, required): Enter your email address for communication and correspondence.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, true, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Government Issued ID Type** (`identification_type`, true, required): Select the type of government-issued ID you possess, such as a driver's license, passport, or state ID.
* **ID Document Number** (`id_number`, false, optional): If you have the option, enter the number on your government-issued ID.
* **-- Professional Background --** (`professional_profile_header`, false, required): This section is a note that introduces the next part of the form, which is about your professional background.
* **Number of Years Babysitting** (`years_experience`, true, required): Enter the number of years you have been babysitting, including any relevant experience as a caregiver or in a similar role.
* **Highest Level of Education Completed** (`highest_education`, true, required): Select the highest level of education you have completed from the provided options, such as high school graduate or college graduate.
* **Relevant Certifications** (`specialized_certifications`, false, optional): List any relevant certifications or courses you have completed that relate to childcare or babysitting, such as CPR or first aid training.
* **Primary Professional Reference** (`reference_primary`, true, required): Enter the name, phone number, and relationship of your primary professional reference.
* **Secondary Professional Reference** (`reference_secondary`, false, optional): Enter the name, phone number, and relationship of your secondary professional reference.
* **Have you ever been convicted of a crime?** (`criminal_record_disclosure`, true, required): Answer truthfully whether you have ever been convicted of a crime, and provide details if applicable.
* **If yes, please provide details** (`disclosure_explanation`, false, optional): If you have answered yes to the previous question, provide an explanation and details about the crime and any relevant circumstances.
