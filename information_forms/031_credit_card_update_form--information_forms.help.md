# Credit Card Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update the information associated with a credit card, either for individual or business cardholders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name to confirm your identity.
2. Enter your credit card number.
3. Select the expiration month and year of your credit card.
4. Enter your Card Verification Value (CVV).
5. Choose the type of card you are using (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Diners, or Discover).
6. Select the brand of your credit card (American Express, Mastercard, or Visa).
7. Enter the name of the cardholder, either for individual or business purposes.
8. Enter the email address of the cardholder.
9. Enter the phone number of the cardholder.
10. Enter the address of the cardholder.
11. Enter the city of the cardholder.
12. Enter the state of the cardholder.
13. Enter the zip code of the cardholder.
14. Enter the country of the cardholder.
15. Enter the name of the business cardholder (if applicable).
16. Enter the address of the business.
17. Enter the city of the business.
18. Enter the state of the business.
19. Enter the zip code of the business.
20. Enter the country of the business.
21. Enter the phone number of the business.
22. Enter the email address of the business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name to confirm your identity.
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, text, required): Enter your credit card number.
* **Expiration Month** (`exp_month`, select_one, required): Select the month in which your credit card expires (1-10).
* **Expiration Year** (`exp_year`, select_multiple, required): Select the year in which your credit card expires (20-25).
* **CVV** (`cvv`, text, required): Enter your Card Verification Value (CVV).
* **Card Type** (`card_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of card you are using (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Diners, or Discover).
* **Card Brand** (`card_brand`, select_one, required): Select the brand of your credit card (American Express, Mastercard, or Visa).
* **Name of Cardholder** (`name_of_cardholder`, text, required): Enter the name of the cardholder.
* **Cardholder Email** (`cardholder_email`, text, required): Enter the email address of the cardholder.
* **Cardholder Phone** (`cardholder_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the cardholder.
* **Cardholder Address** (`cardholder_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the cardholder.
* **Cardholder City** (`cardholder_city`, text, required): Enter the city of the cardholder.
* **Cardholder State** (`cardholder_state`, text, required): Enter the state of the cardholder.
* **Cardholder Zip** (`cardholder_zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code of the cardholder.
* **Cardholder Country** (`cardholder_country`, text, required): Enter the country of the cardholder.
* **Name of Business Cardholder** (`name_of_cardholder_business`, text, required): Enter the name of the business cardholder (if applicable).
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the business (if applicable).
* **Business City** (`business_city`, text, required): Enter the city of the business (if applicable).
* **Business State** (`business_state`, text, required): Enter the state of the business (if applicable).
* **Business Zip** (`business_zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code of the business (if applicable).
* **Business Country** (`business_country`, text, required): Enter the country of the business (if applicable).
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the business (if applicable).
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, text, required): Enter the email address of the business (if applicable).
