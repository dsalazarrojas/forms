# Vetting And Resale Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vetting And Resale Information Form is designed to collect information from vendors about their items for sale. This includes vendor information, item details, payment and shipping terms, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about your item and business.
2. Provide detailed descriptions for each field to ensure that buyers have a clear understanding of what they are purchasing.
3. Ensure that you are providing all necessary information to facilitate a smooth resale process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vetting And Resale Information**: This field is the title of the form and provides a summary of the form's purpose.
* **Vendors Info**: Enter your company or business information, including name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Item Details**: Describe the item you are selling, including its condition, price, and any other relevant details.
* **Resale Terms**: Outline any terms and conditions of the sale, including payment and shipping methods.
* **Payment Details**: Enter the payment terms, including payment method and any other relevant details.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address for the item.
* **Item Condition**: Select the condition of the item, which can be Brand New, Second-Hand, or Refurbished.
* **Item Price**: Enter the price of the item.
* **Item Status**: Select the status of the item, which can be Available, Out of Stock, or Discontinued.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method, which can be Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or Pay Pal.
* **Payment Terms**: Enter any additional payment terms, including any applicable fees or charges.
* **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping method, which can be Free Shipping or Standard Shipping.
* **Item Warranty**: Select the length of the item's warranty, which can be 1 year, 2 years, 3 years, 4 years, or 5 years.
* **Item Description**: Provide a detailed description of the item, including its features and benefits.
* **Item Image**: Upload an image of the item to help buyers have a clearer understanding of what they are purchasing.
* **Item Video**: Upload a video of the item to provide an additional way for buyers to understand the item.
* **Vendor Notes**: Enter any additional notes or instructions for the buyer.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name and title.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Item Category**: Select the category of the item, which can be Electronics, Furniture, Clothing, or Home and Garden.
* **Item Tags**: Select the tags for the item, which can be New, Used, or Refurbished.
* **Item Tags 2**: Select additional tags for the item, which can be Brand New, Used, or Refurbished.
* **Item Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the item.
* **Vendor Notes**: Enter any additional notes or instructions for the buyer.
* **Shipping Terms**: Enter any shipping terms or instructions for the buyer.
* **Resale Terms**: Enter any resale terms or instructions for the buyer.
