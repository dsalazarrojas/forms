# Sender And Recipient Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the sender and recipient of a package or shipment. It gathers essential details that help ensure accurate and efficient delivery and billing processes. Please note that this form is not affiliated with any form platform and is an original adaptation for public use.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the sender's details in the "Sender Details" section.
2. Provide the recipient's information in the "Recipient Details" section.
3. Enter the delivery details in the "Delivery Details" section.
4. Finally, submit your information by filling out the "Submit Information" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Sender Details
* **Sender Details** (`sender_details`, text, required: false): Enter the sender's name and contact information, such as name, address, and phone number, to facilitate contact and delivery.

### Recipient Details
* **Recipient Details** (`recipient_details`, text, required: false): Enter the recipient's name and contact information, including address, phone number, and any relevant notes or instructions.

### Delivery Details
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, required: false): Enter the delivery details, including date, time, and any specific instructions or requirements for delivery.

### Submit Information
* **Submit Information** (`submit_info`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information or comments that you would like to submit with your form.
