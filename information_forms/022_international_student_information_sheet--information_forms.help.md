# international_student_information_sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for international students to provide essential information about themselves.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with your relevant information.
*   Make sure to enter your student name as it appears on your passport.
*   For your date of birth, use the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   Enter your passport details in the corresponding field, including your passport number and any other relevant information.
*   Fill in your current address, including your street, city, state or province, and zip or postal code.
*   Indicate your nationality by entering your country of origin.
*   Select your native language from the provided options (if available).
*   Enter your student ID number (if applicable).
*   Finally, submit the form once all fields are complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional)
    Enter your student name as it appears on your passport or student identification card.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional)
    Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Passport Details** (`passport_details`, text, optional)
    Enter your passport number, country of issue, and any other relevant information.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional)
    Fill in your current address, including street, city, state or province, and zip or postal code.
*   **Nationality** (`nationality`, text, optional)
    Enter your country of origin.
*   **Language** (`language`, text, optional)
    Select your native language from the provided options (if available).
*   **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional)
    Enter your student ID number (if applicable).

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out the form carefully, as any mistakes can lead to errors or delays in processing.
*   Double-check your passport details for accuracy.
*   If you have any issues or questions, feel free to contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
*   The information gathered from this form will be used for student registration or identification purposes only.
