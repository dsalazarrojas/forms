# Hotel Guest Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects important details about hotel guests to ensure a smooth stay. Providing complete information helps the hotel staff to better serve you during your stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the guest name field with your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. If you have a check-in date, enter it in the check-in date field. If not, you can leave it blank.
3. If you have a check-out date, enter it in the check-out date field. If not, you can leave it blank.
4. Enter your email address in the email field.
5. Provide your phone number in the phone number field.
6. Enter your address in the address field.
7. If you wish to inform us about your contact person, enter their name and details in the contact person field.
8. Choose your arrival method by selecting one of the options in the arrival method field.
9. If applicable, enter the time you will arrive in the arrival time field.
10. If you would like to request a specific room type, select it from the room type field.
11. If you have any special requests, such as a room near the lift or a high floor view, enter it in the special request field.
12. Enter your assigned tool, if any, in the assigned tool field.
13. If you have any notes to share with the hotel staff, you can type it in the notes field.
14. Enter the name of the hotel where you are staying.
15. If you are allocated a room number, enter it in the room number field.
16. Enter the floor number of your room.
17. If you are staying in a room with multiple room numbers, enter it in the room number 2 field.
18. Enter the floor number of your room (if it's a multi-storey hotel).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name**: A text field to enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Check-in Date**: A date field to enter your check-in date. If you are unsure, you can leave it blank.
* **Check-out Date**: A date field to enter your check-out date. If you are unsure, you can leave it blank.
* **Email Address**: A text field to enter your email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: A text field to enter your phone number. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Address**: A text field to enter your address.
* **Contact Person**: A text field to enter your contact person's name and details.
* **Arrival Method**: A select-one field to choose your arrival method. Options include Arrival by car, Arrival by walk-in, Arrival by taxi, Arrival by bus, Arrival by train, and Other.
* **Arrival Time**: A time field to enter your estimated arrival time. If you are unsure, you can leave it blank.
* **Room Type**: A select-one field to choose your preferred room type. Options include Single room, Double room, and Suite room.
* **Special Request**: A text field to enter any special requests, such as a room near the lift or a high floor view.
* **Assigned Tool**: A text field to enter your assigned tool, if any.
* **Notes**: A note field to share any additional information with the hotel staff.
* **Hotel Name**: A text field to enter the name of the hotel where you are staying.
* **Room Number**: A text field to enter your room number.
* **Floor**: A text field to enter the floor number of your room.
* **Room Number 2**: A text field to enter your room number 2 (if applicable).
* **Floor 2**: A text field to enter the floor number of your room (if it's a multi-storey hotel).

This form is designed to provide a smooth and convenient experience for hotel guests. By following the steps and field explanations above, you will be able to complete the form efficiently and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the hotel staff for assistance.
