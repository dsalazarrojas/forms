# Inheritance Distribution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inheritance Distribution Form is a tool used to document and manage the distribution of an estate after an individual inherits it. This form helps ensure a fair and efficient distribution process by collecting essential information about the estate and its beneficiaries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all necessary information about the estate and its beneficiaries before starting.
* Begin by filling out the personal details section, including your first name, last name, email address (if applicable), and phone number.
* Next, specify the value of the estate.
* Choose how you would like to distribute your estate among your beneficiaries.
* For each beneficiary, enter their name and specify the share of the estate you wish to allocate to them.
* Finally, review and update your distribution plan as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name to identify yourself as the inheritor.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name to complete your personal details.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, if you have one, to ensure we can contact you about the distribution process.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to facilitate communication about the distribution.
* **Estate Value**: Enter the value of the estate to be distributed.
* **Distribution Preference**: Select how you would like to distribute your estate. You can choose to distribute it among multiple beneficiaries.
* **Distribution Type**: Specify the type of distribution preference you have selected.
* **Beneficiary 1 Name**: Enter the name of your first beneficiary.
* **Beneficiary 1 Share**: Specify the share of the estate you wish to allocate to your first beneficiary.
* **Beneficiary 2 Name**: Enter the name of your second beneficiary (if applicable).
* **Beneficiary 2 Share**: Specify the share of the estate you wish to allocate to your second beneficiary (if applicable).
* **Beneficiary 3 Name**: Enter the name of your third beneficiary (if applicable).
* **Beneficiary 3 Share**: Specify the share of the estate you wish to allocate to your third beneficiary (if applicable).
* **Beneficiary 4 Name**: Enter the name of your fourth beneficiary (if applicable).
* **Beneficiary 4 Share**: Specify the share of the estate you wish to allocate to your fourth beneficiary (if applicable).
* **Review and Update**: Use this field to review and update your distribution plan as needed.

## Tips
* Double-check your information to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.
* You can add or remove beneficiaries as needed, but each beneficiary must have a name and share value.
* The distribution preference and type selections should align with your chosen distribution plan.
