# Emergency Contact Directory Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain your consent to share your information in the emergency contact directory. The emergency contact directory is a list of individuals who may need to be contacted in emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Provide your consent to share your information in the emergency contact directory.
3. If you have any emergency contact information, provide it in the fields below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Contact Directory Consent Form First Page** (`emergency_contact_directory_consent_form_first_page`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your contact information and a short explanation for why it is necessary to be included in the emergency contact directory.
* **Consent to Share** (`consent_to_share`, select_one, optional): Check the box if you give your consent to share your information in the emergency contact directory. You can choose from the following options:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Other (please provide an explanation)
* **Emergency Contact Directory Consent Form Second Page** (`emergency_contact_directory_consent_form_second_page`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your contact information and a short explanation for why it is necessary to be included in the emergency contact directory.
* **Emergency Contact Details** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, optional): Provide your emergency contact details, such as your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Emergency Contact Details First Page** (`emergency_contact_details_first_page`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Details Second Page** (`emergency_contact_details_second_page`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your emergency contact information.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and up-to-date contact information.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact the administrator.

(Note: The "Other" option in the Consent to Share field is intended for users who may have a reason to not share their information in the emergency contact directory but still want to provide a justification for why. This could include reasons such as concerns about personal safety or confidentiality.)
