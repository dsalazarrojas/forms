# sponsor_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sponsor information form is used to collect and store information about sponsors, including their contact details, sponsorship type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary authority to fill out this form.
3. Verify that the information you are entering is not a duplicate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the sponsor's first name in this field.
* **Last Name**: Enter the sponsor's last name in this field.
* **Email**: Enter the sponsor's email address. Please use the correct format for email addresses (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Contact Number**: Enter the sponsor's contact number. This can be a phone number or any other type of contact number.
* **Sponsor Type**:
	+ Select one or more of the available options to indicate the type of sponsorship (e.g., Student Sponsorship, Activity Sponsorship, Event Sponsorship).
* **Sponsor Details**: Enter a brief description or notes about the sponsor, including any relevant details about their sponsorship.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount of the sponsorship. Please use numerical values only.
* **Date of Start**: Enter the start date of the sponsorship.
* **Date of End**: Enter the end date of the sponsorship, if applicable.
* **Purpose**: Enter a brief description of the purpose of the sponsorship.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the sponsorship.
* **Student ID**: Enter the ID number of the student associated with this sponsorship, if applicable.

## Tips
* Double-check that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Verify that you have the necessary authority to fill out this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authority for clarification.
