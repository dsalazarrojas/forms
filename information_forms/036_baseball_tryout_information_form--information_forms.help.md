# Baseball Tryout Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about a baseball player's background, experience, and goals, as well as their contact and school details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid any delays in processing your tryout or team selection.
2. Provide honest and accurate information to the best of your abilities.
3. Please answer each question carefully, as this information will be used to assess your eligibility and suitability for the team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Player First Name (`1`)
* **Player First Name**: Please enter your first name.

### Player Last Name (`2`)
* **Player Last Name**: Please enter your last name.

### Player Date of Birth (`3`)
* **Player Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.

### Player Age (`4`)
* **Player Age**: Enter your current age.

### Height (`5`)
* **Height**: Enter your height in feet and inches.

### Weight (`6`)
* **Weight**: Enter your weight in pounds.

### Parent or Guardian Full Name (`7`)
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name**: Enter the full name of your parent or guardian.

### Parent or Guardian Email (`8`)
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the email address of your parent or guardian.

### Parent or Guardian Phone (`9`)
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.

### Home Address (`10`)
* **Home Address**: Enter your complete home address.

### City (`11`)
* **City**: Enter your city.

### State (`12`)
* **State**: Enter your state.

### ZIP Code (`13`)
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your postal code.

### School Name (`14`)
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your current school.

### Grade Level (`15`)
* **Grade Level**: Select your current grade level from the provided options.

### Current GPA (`16`)
* **Current GPA**: If known, enter your current GPA.

### Years of Baseball Experience (`17`)
* **Years of Baseball Experience**: Select how long you have played baseball from the provided options.

### Primary Position (`18`)
* **Primary Position**: Select your main position from the provided options.

### Secondary Positions (`19`)
* **Secondary Positions**: Select any additional positions you have experience in, if applicable.

### Bats (`20`)
* **Bats**: Select which hand you use to bat with.

### Throws (`21`)
* **Throws**: Select which hand you use to throw with.

### Pitching Velocity (`22`)
* **Pitching Velocity**: If applicable, enter your pitching velocity.

### Last Season Batting Average (`23`)
* **Last Season Batting Average**: If known, enter your last season's batting average.

### Previous Team (`24`)
* **Previous Team**: If applicable, enter the name of your previous team.

### Previous Coach Name (`25`)
* **Previous Coach Name**: If applicable, enter the name of your previous coach.

### Previous Coach Phone (`26`)
* **Previous Coach Phone**: If applicable, enter the phone number of your previous coach.

### Baseball Honors or Awards (`27`)
* **Baseball Honors or Awards**: Enter any recognition you have received.

### Other Sports Played (`28`)
* **Other Sports Played**: Select any other sports you have experience in.

### Medical Conditions or Injuries (`29`)
* **Medical Conditions or Injuries**: If applicable, enter any medical conditions or injuries.

### Baseball Goals (`30`)
* **Baseball Goals**: Enter what you hope to achieve in baseball.

### Why Do You Want to Join This Team (`31`)
* **Why Do You Want to Join This Team**: Share your motivations and reasons for joining the team.

### Additional Information (`32`)
* **Additional Information**: If there is anything else you would like to share, please enter it here.

Please note that all fields marked as required must be filled out accurately to ensure proper processing and consideration. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please do not hesitate to reach out to the team or coaches.
