# online_class_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online_class_information_form is designed to collect relevant information about online classes, including student IDs, course details, and contact information. This data enables efficient management and organization of online classes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and effectively:

1. Ensure you have the required information before starting.
2. Enter the online_class_id, which is a unique identifier for the online class.
3. Provide the student_id, which is also a unique identifier for the student.
4. Choose whether the course is 'Yes' or 'No' by selecting from the dropdown options.
5. Enter the start date of the online class in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
6. Enter the end date of the online class in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
7. Select the time slot for the online class.
8. Enter the location where the online class will take place.
9. Provide a brief description of the online class.
10. Enter the phone number for the online class.
11. Enter the email address associated with the online class.
12. Add any additional notes or comments for the online class.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **online_class_id** (1, number, required: false): Enter a unique identifier for the online class.
* **student_id** (2, number, required: true): Enter a unique identifier for the student.
* **course_id** (3, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the course status.
* **start_date** (4, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the online class in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **end_date** (5, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the online class in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **time** (6, time, required: false): Select the time slot for the online class.
* **location** (7, text, required: false): Enter the location where the online class will take place.
* **description** (8, note, required: false): Provide a brief description of the online class.
* **phone_number** (9, text, required: false): Enter the phone number for the online class.
* **email** (10, email, required: false): Enter the email address associated with the online class.
* **note** (11, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments for the online class.
* **note2** (12, note, required: false): Add any further notes or comments for the online class.
