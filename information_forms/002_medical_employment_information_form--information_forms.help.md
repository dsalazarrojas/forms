# Medical Employment Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Employment Information Form is designed to collect essential details about an employee's contact information, medical insurance, and employment history. This form is meant to gather accurate and up-to-date information for HR purposes, medical record keeping, and other related administrative tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide your medical insurance information, if applicable.
3. Enter your medical provider details, if relevant.
4. Describe any medical conditions you have or have had in the past.
5. List any prescribed medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
6. Share your job title and employer details, if applicable.
7. Enter your start and end dates of employment, if relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name (Contact Name, Text, Required: false)**: This field is for your name, which will be used to identify you in our records.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone, Text, Required: false)**: Please enter your phone number for our contact purposes.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email, Email, Required: false)**: This is your work email address or any other email address you'd like us to use to contact you.
* **Medical Insurance (Medical Insurance, Text, Required: false)**: If you have any medical insurance, please provide details about it here, including policy number or name.
* **Medical Provider (Medical Provider, Text, Required: false)**: List the name and details of your current or past medical provider(s).
* **Medical Conditions (Medical Conditions, Text, Required: false)**: Describe any medical conditions you have or have had in the past. This includes chronic conditions, injuries, illnesses, or any other health issues.
* **Medical Prescription (Medical Prescription, Text, Required: false)**: List any prescribed medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past. Please include the name, dosage, and frequency of each medication.
* **Job Title (Job Title, Text, Required: false)**: Enter your current or past job title(s) if applicable.
* **Employer (Employer, Text, Required: false)**: Provide the name and details of your current or past employer(s).
* **Work Start Date (Work Start Date, Date, Required: false)**: Enter the date you started your current or past job(s).
* **Work End Date (Work End Date, Date, Required: false)**: Enter the date you ended your current or past job(s), if applicable.
