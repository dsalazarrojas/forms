# Liquid Biopsy Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Liquid Biopsy Request Form is used for requesting samples for medical testing. It is designed to collect information from patients, healthcare providers, and other relevant parties involved in the testing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the first page, which is a general information page.
2. Move on to the patient details page and fill out the required fields, such as name, contact information, and other relevant patient information.
3. Proceed to the contact details page and fill out the contact name, contact email, and contact phone number.
4. Move to the healthcare provider page and fill out the healthcare provider name and contact information.
5. Continue to the medical record page and fill out the medical record number, hospital name, and other relevant medical record information.
6. Go to the medical history page and fill out the medical history and other relevant information.
7. Move to the specimen details page and fill out the specimen description, sample size, and sample type.
8. Proceed to the shipping information page and fill out the shipping address, city, state, zip code, and country.
9. Finally, sign and date the form, and click the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information (liquid_biopsy_request_form_page_1)** (Page 1, Not Required): A brief note or description of the form.
- **Patient Details (patient_details)** (Page 2, Optional): This field is used to collect information about the patient, such as name, contact information, and other relevant patient details.
- **Contact Details (contact_details)** (Page 3, Optional): This field is used to collect contact information, such as contact name, contact email, and contact phone number.
- **Healthcare Provider (healthcare_provider)** (Page 4, Optional): This field is used to collect information from healthcare providers, such as name and contact information.
- **Medical Record Number (medical_record_number)** (Page 5, Optional): This field is used to collect the medical record number and hospital name.
- **Medical History (medical_history)** (Page 6, Optional): This field is used to collect medical history and other relevant information.
- **Other Medical History (medical_history_other)** (Page 7, Optional): This field is used to collect other relevant medical history information.
- **Specimen Details (specimen_details)** (Page 8, Optional): This field is used to collect the specimen description, sample size, and sample type.
- **Type of Biopsy (type_of_biopsy)** (Page 9, Optional): This field is used to collect the type of biopsy.
- **Specimen Description (specimen_description)** (Page 10, Optional): This field is used to collect the specimen description.
- **Sample Size (sample_size)** (Page 11, Optional): This field is used to collect the sample size.
- **Sample Type (sample_type)** (Page 12, Optional): This field is used to collect the sample type.
- **Shipping Address (shipping_address)** (Page 13, Optional): This field is used to collect the shipping address.
- **Shipping City (shipping_city)** (Page 14, Optional): This field is used to collect the shipping city.
- **Shipping State (shipping_state)** (Page 15, Optional): This field is used to collect the shipping state.
- **Shipping Zip (shipping_zip)** (Page 16, Optional): This field is used to collect the shipping zip code.
- **Shipping Country (shipping_country)** (Page 17, Optional): This field is used to collect the shipping country.
- **Signature (signature)** (Page 18, Optional): This field is used to collect the signature and date of the form submitter.
- **Date (date)** (Page 19, Optional): This field is used to collect the date the form was submitted.
- **Time (time)** (Page 20, Optional): This field is used to collect the time the form was submitted.
- **Submit** (Page 21-24, Optional): The submit button is used to complete and submit the form.
