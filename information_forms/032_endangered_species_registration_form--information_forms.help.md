# endangered_species_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about endangered species, allowing users to submit data about the species, its conservation status, population trend, habitat type, habitat description, conservation actions taken, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the endangered species name from the list provided in the "Endangered Species Name" field.
2. Choose the conservation status of the species from the list provided in the "Conservation Status" field.
3. Select the population trend of the species from the list provided in the "Population Trend" field.
4. Optionally, provide the habitat type and description of the species in the "Habitat Type" and "Habitat Description" fields, respectively.
5. If applicable, provide conservation actions taken in the "Conservation Actions" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
8. If you have supporting documentation or files, upload them in the "File Upload" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Endangered Species Name** (`endangered_species_name`, select_one, required): Select the name of the endangered species from the provided list.
* **Conservation Status** (`conservation_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the conservation status of the species from the list (Endangered, Vulnerable, Near Threatened, Least Concern).
* **Population Trend** (`population_trend`, select_one, required): Select the population trend of the species from the list (Increasing, Decreasing, Stable).
* **Habitat Type** (`habitat_type`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the habitat type of the species (e.g., forest, savannah, etc.).
* **Habitat Description** (`habitat_description`, note, required): If applicable, provide a description of the species' habitat (e.g., forest, savannah, etc.).
* **Conservation Actions** (`conservation_actions`, text, optional): If applicable, provide conservation actions taken or planned for the species.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, text, optional): If you have supporting documentation or files, upload them here.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the species you are registering is not already registered in our database to avoid duplicates.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us at the provided email address.
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
