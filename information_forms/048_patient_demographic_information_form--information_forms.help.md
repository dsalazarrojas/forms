# Patient Demographic Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Demographic Information Form is designed to collect personal data about patients. 
This form is intended to be used for general patient information collection or for 
a specific medical context where the collected data will be used to create or update a 
patient's medical record.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the patient's name, age, height, weight, and other basic information 
   in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter the patient's Medical Record Number, if available.
3. Provide the name and contact information of the contact person.
4. If necessary, select any relevant medical conditions or allergies the patient 
   may have.
5. If applicable, enter the date and time of the patient's medical record.
6. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (Basic Info):
 	+ This is a text field for entering the patient's basic information, such as 
    their name and other personal details.
* **Age** (Age):
 	+ This field is for the patient's age, which should be entered as a number.
* **Height (cm)** (Height):
 	+ Enter the patient's height in centimeters.
* **Weight (kg)** (Weight):
 	+ Enter the patient's weight in kilograms.
* **Pulse (bpm)** (Pulse):
 	+ This field is for the patient's pulse rate, which should be entered as a number.
* **Blood Pressure (mmHg)** (Blood Pressure):
 	+ This field is for the patient's blood pressure, which should be entered as a number.
* **Medical Record Number** (Medical Record Number):
 	+ If available, enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person):
 	+ Enter the name and contact information of the contact person.
* **Medical Condition** (Medical Condition):
 	+ Select any relevant medical conditions or allergies the patient may have.
* **Allergy** (Allergy):
 	+ This field is for selecting multiple allergies the patient may have.
* **Medical Record Date** (Medical Record Date):
 	+ Enter the date of the patient's medical record.
* **Medical Record Time** (Medical Record Time):
 	+ Enter the time of the patient's medical record.

## Tips
* Ensure the form is filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure it's error-free and contains all necessary information.
* If you're unsure about any field or have additional information to provide, contact the relevant personnel.
