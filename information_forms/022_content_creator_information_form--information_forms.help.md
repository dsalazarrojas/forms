# Content Creator Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about you as a content creator, which will help us determine whether we can work together.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. Please ensure that you are accurate and honest about your details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Creator Full Name** (`creator_full_name`, text, required)
Your legal full name as a creator.

### 2. **Stage Name or Handle** (`stage_name`, text, optional)
Your professional or online name that people use to identify you.

### 3. **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required)
Your business email address that we can use to contact you.

### 4. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required)
Your best contact number for collaborations and projects.

### 5. **Website URL** (`website_url`, text, optional)
Your personal or business website, if you have one.

### 6. **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required)
Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.

### 7. **Country of Residence** (`country_of_residence`, select_one, required)
Where you currently live. Please select from the options provided.

### 8. **Primary Platform** (`primary_platform`, select_one, required)
Your main content creation platform. Choose from the options provided.

### 9. **Social Media Handles** (`social_media_handles`, text, required)
List your social media usernames where people can find you.

### 10. **Total Follower Count** (`follower_count`, select_one, required)
Approximate total followers across all your platforms.

### 11. **Content Niche** (`content_niche`, select_one, required)
The type of content you typically create. Choose from the provided options.

### 12. **Content Types** (`content_types`, select_multiple, required)
The formats you create content in. Select multiple options from the provided list.

### 13. **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, select_one, required)
How long you have been creating content.

### 14. **Audience Demographics** (`audience_demographics`, text, required)
Describe your typical audience in a few sentences.

### 15. **Average Engagement Rate** (`engagement_rate`, select_one, optional)
Your average engagement percentage on your platforms.

### 16. **Previous Brand Collaborations** (`previous_brand_collaborations`, select_one, required)
Have you worked with brands before?

### 17. **Collaboration Examples** (`collaboration_examples`, text, optional)
List any brands you have collaborated with in the past.

### 18. **Rate Card Available** (`rate_card_available`, select_one, required)
Do you have a rate card or pricing sheet available?

### 19. **Content Creation Equipment** (`content_creation_equipment`, text, optional)
List any equipment you use for content creation.

### 20. **Availability for Collaboration** (`availability_for_collaboration`, select_one, required)
When are you available for collaborations?

### 21. **Why Do You Want to Work With Us?** (`why_work_with_us`, text, required)
Tell us why you are interested in collaborating with our platform.

### 22. **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional)
Any extra details you would like to share about yourself.
