# Supplier Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Information Form is used to collect and store information about suppliers. This includes their company name, contact details, supplier type, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the supplier's company name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Choose the supplier type from the "Supplier Type" select option.
3. Enter the supplier's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the supplier's contact details in the "Supplier Contact" field.
5. Enter the supplier's address in the "Address" field.
6. Enter the supplier's phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter a description of the supplier in the "Description" field.
8. Mark the supplier as active or inactive in the "Supplier Status" select option.
9. Optionally, add any additional notes about the supplier in the "Supplier Notes" field.
