# Baptism Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Baptism Information Form" is a crucial document that helps us understand the child's and family's background, preferences, and circumstances. This information will be used to provide personalized care and support during the baptism process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Mark each required field with a red star (*) as you complete it.
3. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Full Name (1)** (*required*): Please enter the legal name of the child to be baptized.
* **Child Date of Birth (2)** (*required*): Enter the child's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Child Place of Birth (3)** (*required*): Provide the city and state where the child was born.
* **Child Gender (4)** (*required*): Select the child's gender from the options provided.
* **Father Full Name (5)** (*required*): Enter the legal name of the child's father.
* **Father Email (6)**: This is an optional contact email for the father, but please provide it for communication purposes.
* **Father Phone (7)** (*required*): Provide the father's phone number for contact.
* **Mother Full Name (8)** (*required*): Enter the legal name of the child's mother.
* **Mother Email (9)**: This is an optional contact email for the mother, but please provide it for communication purposes.
* **Mother Phone (10)** (*required*): Provide the mother's phone number for contact.
* **Family Address (11)** (*required*): Enter the family's street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Family Parish (12)**: If applicable, provide the name of the family's parish.
* **Parish Membership Status (13)** (*required*): Select the correct membership status from the options provided.
* **Preferred Baptism Date (14)** (*required*): Choose the preferred date for the baptism. Please note that this is your first choice.
* **Alternate Baptism Date (15)**: If the preferred date is not available, provide an alternate date.
* **Preferred Baptism Time (16)**: Select the preferred time for the baptism from the options provided.
* **Godparent 1 Full Name (17)** (*required*): Enter the full name of the child's first godparent.
* **Godparent 1 Relationship (18)**: Describe the relationship between the godparent and the child (e.g., parent, relative, friend).
* **Godparent 1 Contact (19)**: Provide a contact phone number or email for the godparent.
* **Godparent 1 Parish (20)**: If applicable, provide the name of the godparent's parish.
* **Godparent 2 Full Name (21)**: Enter the full name of the child's second godparent (if applicable).
* **Godparent 2 Relationship (22)**: Describe the relationship between the second godparent and the child (e.g., parent, relative, friend).
* **Godparent 2 Contact (23)**: Provide a contact phone number or email for the second godparent.
* **Godparent 2 Parish (24)**: If applicable, provide the name of the second godparent's parish.
* **Number of Godparents (25)** (*required*): Select the total number of godparents.
* **Godparent Eligibility (26)** (*required*): Select the eligibility status of the godparents from the options provided.
* **Parents Marital Status (27)** (*required*): Select the marital status of the parents.
* **Baptism Preparation Class (28)** (*required*): Select whether the parents have attended the baptism preparation class.
* **Siblings Baptized Here (29)**: Select whether there are siblings baptized at this parish.
* **Special Requests (30)**: Provide any additional requests or needs.
* **How Did You Hear About Us (31)**: Select how the parents heard about this parish.
* **Additional Comments (32)**: Provide any additional comments or information about the family.
* **Signature Date (33)** (*required*): Date of form completion.
