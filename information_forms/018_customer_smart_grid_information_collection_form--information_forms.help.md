# Customer Smart Grid Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experience with smart grid devices. It will help us understand your needs and preferences for our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details and preferences.
2. For select multiple options, choose all that apply.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Optional): This field is for your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): This field is for your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your address.
* **Utility Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of utility you use for your smart grid device (e.g., Electricity, Gas, Water).
* **Usage Hours** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the hours when you use your smart grid device (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Energy Source** (Select One, Optional): Choose the energy source for your smart grid device (e.g., Solar, Wind, Hydro).
* **Energy Efficiency** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how energy-efficient you find your smart grid device (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Energy Consumption** (Number, Optional): Enter your energy consumption in units (e.g., kWh).
* **Smart Grid Device** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate whether you have a smart grid device (True or False).
* **Customer Service** (Select One, Optional): Rate your experience with our customer service (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Communication Method** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your preferred method of communication (e.g., Phone, Email, Mail, In-person).
* **Follow-up Date** (Date, Optional): Choose a date for a follow-up appointment.
* **Time Preferences** (Time, Optional): Enter your time preference for appointments.
* **Smart Grid Device Installation** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate whether you have had a smart grid device installed (True or False).
* **Smart Grid Device Rental** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate whether you have rented a smart grid device (True or False).
* **Grid Connectivity** (Select One, Optional): Rate your internet connectivity (e.g., Strong, Poor).
* **Grid Connectivity Speed** (Select One, Optional): Rate your internet speed (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Smart Grid Device Status** (Select One, Optional): Choose whether your smart grid device is active (Active) or inactive.
* **Device Replacement** (Select One, Optional): Indicate whether your smart grid device has been replaced (True or False).
* **Device Replacement Reason** (Text, Optional): Enter the reason for the device replacement.
* **Smart Grid Device Return** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate whether you have returned your smart grid device (True or False).
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments about your smart grid experience.
