# CRG_Realty Buyers Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CRG_Realty Buyers Information Form is designed to gather essential information about potential property buyers. This form helps our team understand your preferences, needs, and requirements to better serve you in finding your ideal property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as requested, providing accurate and relevant information about yourself. Make sure to answer each question carefully, as this information will help us understand your needs and provide you with the best possible assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`buyer_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name, including your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Provide your email address where we can contact you about properties.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter the best number to reach you.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like us to contact you: Email, Phone Call, Text Message, or Either.
* **Property Type of Interest** (`property_type_interest`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of property you are looking for: Single Family Home, Condominium, Townhouse, Multi-Family, Land, or Commercial.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required): Choose your approximate price range for the property: Under $200,000, $200,000 - $350,000, $350,000 - $500,000, etc.
* **Desired Location** (`desired_location`, `text`, required): Enter the city or neighborhood where you are looking to buy a property.
* **Number of Bedrooms Needed** (`number_of_bedrooms`, `select_one`, not required): If applicable, select the minimum number of bedrooms required: Studio, 1 Bedroom, 2 Bedrooms, 3 Bedrooms, or 4+ Bedrooms.
* **Number of Bathrooms Needed** (`number_of_bathrooms`, `select_one`, not required): If applicable, select the minimum number of bathrooms required: 1 Bathroom, 1.5 Bathrooms, 2 Bathrooms, or 2.5+ Bathrooms.
* **Timeline to Purchase** (`timeline_to_purchase`, `select_one`, required): Choose when you plan to buy a property: Immediately (0-30 days), 1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, or Just browsing.
* **Current Housing Situation** (`currently_renting_or_owning`, `select_one`, not required): If applicable, select your current housing situation: Renting, Owning, Living with family, or Other.
* **Financing Status** (`financing_status`, `select_one`, not required): If applicable, select your current financing status: Pre-approved, Pre-qualified, Need lender referral, Cash buyer, or Not sure yet.
* **Must-Have Features** (`must_have_features`, `text`, not required): If applicable, list any essential features you need in a property.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): If you have any other information you would like to share with us, use this field to provide it.
