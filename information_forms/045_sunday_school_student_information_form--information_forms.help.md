# sunday_school_student_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "sunday_school_student_information_form" form is used to collect relevant information about a student attending Sunday school. The form includes fields for student name, address, phone number, email, date of birth, grade level, and contact information for a guardian and emergency contact person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. If necessary, enter the student's address in the "Student Address" field.
3. If necessary, enter the student's phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
4. If necessary, enter the student's email in the "Student Email" field. Note that this field requires a valid email address.
5. Enter the student's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field, using the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
6. Select the student's grade level from the drop-down menu in the "Grade Level" field. Options include "Kindergarten", "First Grade", "Second Grade", "Third Grade", "Fourth Grade", and "Fifth Grade".
7. Enter the name of the student's guardian in the "Guardian Name" field.
8. If necessary, enter the guardian's phone number in the "Guardian Phone" field.
9. If necessary, enter the emergency contact person's name and phone number in the "Emergency Contact Name" and "Emergency Contact Phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (required, text): Enter the student's name.
* **Student Address** (optional, text): Enter the student's address, if necessary.
* **Student Phone** (optional, text): Enter the student's phone number, if necessary.
* **Student Email** (optional, email): Enter the student's email address, if necessary. Note: This field requires a valid email address.
* **Date of Birth** (optional, date): Enter the student's date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Grade Level** (required, select one): Select the student's grade level from the drop-down menu.
* **Guardian Name** (optional, text): Enter the name of the student's guardian.
* **Guardian Phone** (optional, text): Enter the guardian's phone number, if necessary.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (optional, text): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (optional, text): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number, if necessary.
