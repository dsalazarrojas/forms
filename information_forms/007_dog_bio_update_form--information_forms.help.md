# Dog Bio Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Bio Update Form is used to update the bio information of a dog. This form helps you provide accurate and up-to-date information about your dog's breed, size, color, fur length, behavior, and photo details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the dog's breed from the options provided in the "Dog Breed" field.
2. Choose the dog's size from the options provided in the "Dog Size" field.
3. Select the dog's color from the options provided in the "Dog Color" field.
4. Choose the dog's fur length from the options provided in the "Fur Length" field.
5. Provide any behavior notes about the dog in the "Behavior Notes" field.
6. Enter the dog's contact information, including their contact and phone number in the "Contact" and "Phone" fields.
7. Enter the dog's email address in the "Email" field.
8. Upload a photo of the dog in the "Photo URL" field.
9. Provide a caption for the photo in the "Photo Caption" field.
10. Enter the photo credit in the "Photo Credit" field.
11. Choose the person who updated the dog's information from the options provided in the "Updated By" field.
12. Enter any additional notes about the dog in the "Notes" field.
13. Select how you want to submit the form: "Submit", "Save Draft", "Cancel", or "Continue".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dog Breed** (`dog_breed`): Select the dog's breed.
* **Dog Size** (`dog_size`): Choose the dog's size.
* **Dog Color** (`dog_color`): Select the dog's color.
* **Fur Length** (`fur_length`): Choose the dog's fur length.
* **Behavior Notes** (`behavior_notes`): Provide any notes about the dog's behavior.
* **Contact** (`contact`): Enter the dog's contact information.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the dog's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the dog's email address.
* **Photo URL** (`photo_url`): Upload a photo of the dog.
* **Photo Caption** (`photo_caption`): Provide a caption for the photo.
* **Photo Credit** (`photo_credit`): Enter the photo credit.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`): Choose who updated the dog's information.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes about the dog.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Select how you want to submit the form.
