# digital_content_metadata_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form collects metadata for digital content to facilitate its proper management and attribution. Please fill in the form with accurate and relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with the correct information.
2. Leave the optional fields blank if not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Digital Content Asset
* **digital_content_asset** (text, required): Enter the name or identifier of the digital content asset.
* **author** (text, optional): Enter the name of the author of the digital content.
* **created_date** (text, optional): Enter the date the digital content was created.
* **updated_date** (text, optional): Enter the date the digital content was last updated.
* **content_type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of digital content, such as Image, Audio, or Video.
* **description** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the digital content.
* **tags** (text, optional): Enter any relevant tags or keywords for the digital content.
* **content_owner** (text, optional): Enter the owner of the digital content.
* **content_license** (select_one, optional): Select the license under which the digital content is released, such as Public Domain or All Rights Reserved.
* **metadata_license** (select_one, optional): Select the license for the metadata, such as No license applied or CC BY 1.0.
* **rights_holder** (text, optional): Enter the name of the rights holder.
* **rights_holder_url** (text, optional): Enter the URL of the rights holder.
* **rights_holder_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the rights holder.
* **rights_holder_email** (email, optional): Enter the email of the rights holder.
* **rights_holder_phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the rights holder.
* **rights_holder_address** (text, optional): Enter the address of the rights holder.
* **rights_holder_country** (text, optional): Enter the country of the rights holder.
* **rights_holder_state** (text, optional): Enter the state of the rights holder.
* **rights_holder_city** (text, optional): Enter the city of the rights holder.
* **rights_holder_zip** (text, optional): Enter the zip code of the rights holder.
* **rights_holder_notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the rights holder.
* **rights_holder_organization** (text, optional): Enter the organization of the rights holder.
* **rights_holder_job_title** (text, optional): Enter the job title of the rights holder.
* **rights_holder_affiliation** (text, optional): Enter the affiliation of the rights holder.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the relevant documentation or contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
