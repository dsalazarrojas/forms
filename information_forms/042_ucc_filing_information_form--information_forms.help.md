# UCC Filing Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information required for Uniform Commercial Code (UCC) filings with state authorities. It gathers necessary details for securing and filing UCC documents, ensuring accuracy and completeness during the filing process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents before proceeding.
* Complete each field carefully, as accuracy is crucial for successful UCC filings.
* Review and confirm your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Business Name (Page 1)
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your business as it is registered with the state.

### File Number (Page 1)
* **File Number** (`file_number`, `text`, required): Enter the UCC file number assigned to your business.

### Effective Date (Page 1)
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the UCC filing becomes effective.

### File Date (Page 1)
* **File Date** (`file_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are filing the UCC document.

### Filing Type (Page 1)
* **Filing Type** (`filing_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of UCC filing (UCC1, UCC3, or UCC5).

### Party Terminated (Page 1)
* **Party Terminated** (`party_terminated`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate if the party is terminated, true or false.

### State (Page 1)
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state where the UCC document is being filed.

### City (Page 1)
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where the UCC document is being filed.

### Zip (Page 1)
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required): Enter the zip code of the location where the UCC document is being filed.

### Email (Page 2)
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of your business or authorized person (if applicable).

### Phone (Page 2)
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your business or authorized person (if applicable).

### Authorized By (Page 2)
* **Authorized By** (`authorized_by`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the authorized person.

### Authorized Date (Page 2)
* **Authorized Date** (`authorized_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the authorized person authorized the UCC document.

### Authorized Sign (Page 2)
* **Authorized Sign** (`authorized_sign`, `text`, optional): Enter the signature of the authorized person.

### Authorized Phone (Page 2)
* **Authorized Phone** (`authorized_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the authorized person.

### Authorized Email (Page 2)
* **Authorized Email** (`authorized_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the authorized person.

### Submission Date (Page 3)
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the UCC document was submitted.

### Submitted By (Page 3)
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the document.

### Submitter Phone (Page 3)
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the document (if applicable).

### Submitter Email (Page 3)
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting the document (if applicable).

### Confirm (Page 3)
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.

Please review and confirm the information you have entered before submitting the form.
