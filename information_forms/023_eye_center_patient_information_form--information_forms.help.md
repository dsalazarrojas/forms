# eye_center_patient_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The eye_center_patient_information_form is a form designed to collect general patient information for administrative purposes, such as contacting the patient and scheduling appointments, at an eye center.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your Email address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your Phone Number in the field labeled "Phone Number".
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the field labeled "Date of Birth".
6. If applicable, provide your Medical History in the field labeled "Medical History".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`general_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter a phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): If you have any significant medical conditions or allergies, please provide a brief description in this field.
