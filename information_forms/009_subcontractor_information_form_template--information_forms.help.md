# Subcontractor Information Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Subcontractor Information Form Template is designed to collect essential details about a subcontractor, including their personal and business information, contact details, and contract dates. This form is used to maintain accurate and complete records of subcontractors for various business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the respective fields. Ensure that you enter your email address correctly, following the format (e.g., example@example.com).
2. Enter your Phone Number in the format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
3. Provide your Address Line 1 and Address Line 2 (if applicable) in the respective fields. Ensure that you enter your address accurately, following the standard format (e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345).
4. Enter your City and State in the respective fields. Ensure that you enter your city and state correctly, following the standard format (e.g., Anytown, CA).
5. Enter your Zip Code in the format (e.g., 12345).
6. If you are a company, provide your Company Name, Company Address Line 1, Company City, Company State, and Company Zip Code in the respective fields. Ensure that you enter your company's address accurately, following the standard format.
7. Enter your Contact Title, if applicable.
8. If applicable, enter the Contract Start Date and Contract End Date in the format (dd/mm/yyyy).
9. Finally, add any relevant Notes in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your personal last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format (e.g., example@example.com).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Address Line 1**: Enter your address line 1 accurately, following the standard format (e.g., 123 Main St).
* **Address Line 2**: Enter your address line 2, if applicable.
* **City**: Enter your city correctly, following the standard format (e.g., Anytown).
* **State**: Enter your state correctly, following the standard format (e.g., CA).
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code in the format (e.g., 12345).
* **Company Name**: If you are a company, enter your company name.
* **Company Address Line 1**: If you are a company, enter your company address line 1 accurately, following the standard format.
* **Company City**: If you are a company, enter your company city correctly.
* **Company State**: If you are a company, enter your company state correctly.
* **Company Zip Code**: If you are a company, enter your company zip code in the format.
* **Contact Title**: Enter your contact title, if applicable.
* **Contract Start Date**: If applicable, enter the contract start date in the format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Contract End Date**: If applicable, enter the contract end date in the format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Notes**: Add any relevant notes about your subcontractor profile.

Please note that all fields are optional, unless marked as required in the form's metadata.
