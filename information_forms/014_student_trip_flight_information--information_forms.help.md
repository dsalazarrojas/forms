# Student Trip Flight Information - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the flight details for students going on a trip. The form helps in gathering essential data such as departure and arrival airports, dates, and emergency contacts. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student flight departure airport by selecting from the available options.
2. Choose the student flight arrival airport from the available options.
3. Enter the student flight departure date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter the student flight arrival date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Provide the student flight vehicle information in the text field.
6. Write a note to provide any necessary information about the student trip leaving.
7. Choose the time for the student trip leaving from the available options.
8. Enter the student flight vehicle information in the text field.
9. Provide the emergency contact information including the name, phone number, and email.
10. Add any additional notes for the student trip emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Flight Departure Airports**: Select the airport where the student will depart from.
* **Student Flight Arrival Airports**: Choose the airport where the student will arrive.
* **Student Flight Departure Date**: Enter the date when the student will depart.
* **Student Flight Arrival Date**: Enter the date when the student will arrive.
* **Student Flight Vehicle**: Provide the student's flight vehicle information.
* **Student Trip Leaving**: Write a note about the student trip leaving.
* **Student Trip Leaving**: Choose the time for the student trip leaving.
* **Student Flight Vehicle**: Provide the student's flight vehicle information.
* **Student Trip Emergency Contact**: This field is for adding emergency contact information.
* **Student Emergency Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Student Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Student Emergency Email**: Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Student Trip Emergency Notes**: Add any additional notes about the emergency contact.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information before submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the travel agent or the emergency contact person.
