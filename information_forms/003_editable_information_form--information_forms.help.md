# Editable Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users, allowing them to provide details about their contacts and emergency procedures. The form is editable, enabling users to enter their personal data and preferences as needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Scroll through each page (contact_records and emergency_details).
2. Enter your information in the corresponding fields.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.
4. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Records**: (`contact_records`, `text`, optional)
  Enter details about your contacts, such as names, phone numbers, or email addresses.

- **Emergency Details**: (`emergency_details`, `text`, optional)
  Provide information about emergency procedures, such as medical conditions or special instructions.

- **Communication Preferences**: (`communication_preferences`, `text`, optional)
  Specify your preferred method of contact or communication. This can include email, phone, or messaging services.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are providing accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you have any issues or concerns while completing the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
- You can modify the form as needed to suit your specific requirements.
