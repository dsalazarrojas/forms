# Emergency Preparedness For Dental Practices - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help dental practices develop and maintain a comprehensive emergency preparedness plan. It is essential to complete this form carefully and accurately to ensure the safety and well-being of patients and staff.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Choose the most relevant emergency plan (Plan A, B, or C) from the options provided.
3. Select the roles and responsibilities of staff members who will be involved in emergency situations.
4. Provide a brief description of the data collection procedures in place.
5. Describe how your practice will manage submissions in emergency situations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **emergency_plans** (`emergency_plans`, select_one, optional): Select the most relevant emergency plan for your dental practice. This could be Plan A, B, or C, depending on your specific needs and circumstances. Choose the option that best fits your practice's emergency situation.
* **roles** (`roles`, select_multiple, optional): Identify the roles and responsibilities of staff members who will be involved in emergency situations. Select all relevant staff members, such as doctors, nurses, or administrative staff.
* **data_collection** (`data_collection`, note, optional): Please provide a brief description of the data collection procedures in place, including how patient information will be collected and stored in emergency situations.
* **submission_management** (`submission_management`, text, optional): Describe how your practice will manage submissions in emergency situations, including how reports will be created and submitted to relevant authorities.
* **title** (this is the title of the form, it is not a field for user input, but for reference): This is a placeholder title for the form, and is for reference only. Do not attempt to input anything into this field. 

## Tips
- Be as thorough and accurate as possible when completing this form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions or fields, please refer to your practice's emergency preparedness plan or seek guidance from relevant authorities. 

This guide is designed to help you complete the form accurately and effectively. If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
