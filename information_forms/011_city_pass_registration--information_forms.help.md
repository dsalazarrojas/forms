# City Pass Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register an individual for a city pass, which is a type of identification that verifies an individual's eligibility to participate in city programs and services. The city pass is issued to local residents, seniors, students, and visitors, and it is required for various city programs and services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full legal name in the "Applicant name" field.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter your current residential address in the "Residential address" field.
4. Upload proof of residency, such as a utility bill or lease agreement, in the "Proof of residency provided" field.
5. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact email" field, which will be used for pass updates.
6. If applicable, enter your contact phone number in the "Contact phone" field.
7. Choose the type of city pass you are requesting (Local resident, Senior, Student, or Visitor) from the "Type of city pass requested" dropdown menu.
8. Enter any additional eligibility notes or circumstances in the "Additional eligibility notes" field.
9. Review and agree to the terms of service by checking the "Agreement and acknowledgement" field.
10. Finally, submit your application and confirm the submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name**: Enter your full legal name.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Residential address**: Enter your current street address, city, and postal code.
* **Proof of residency provided**: Upload proof of residency, such as a utility bill or lease agreement, to verify your eligibility.
* **Contact email**: Enter your contact email address for pass updates.
* **Contact phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
* **Type of city pass requested**: Choose from the dropdown menu: Local resident, Senior, Student, or Visitor.
* **Additional eligibility notes**: Enter any additional eligibility information or circumstances that may be relevant to your application.
* **Agreement and acknowledgement**: Check this field to confirm that you agree to the terms of service.
* **Submission date**: This field will automatically be populated with the current date and time.
