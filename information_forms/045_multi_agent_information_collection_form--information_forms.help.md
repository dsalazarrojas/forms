# multi_agent_information_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "multi_agent_information_collection_form" is designed to collect information from multiple agents or parties involved in a project or collaboration. This form aims to provide a centralized platform for collecting and organizing data on agents, contacts, projects, partnerships, staffing, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "agent_info" section with the relevant information about the agent or party you are collecting data on.
2. Provide contact information in the "contact_info" section.
3. Enter project details in the "project_info" section.
4. Fill in the "partnership_info" section with partnership-related details.
5. Enter information about the staff or team members in the "staffing_info" section.
6. If there's any other information you'd like to share, use the "other_info" section.
7. Finally, click the "submit_info" button to submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **agent_info** (`agent_info`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the agent or party you are collecting data on. Please enter a brief description or name of the agent.
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): In this field, provide contact details for the agent or party, such as email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
* **project_info** (`project_info`, text, optional): Enter information about the project you are working on, including project name, goals, or objectives.
* **partnership_info** (`partnership_info`, text, optional): Use this field to describe the partnership details, such as partnership name, role, or involvement.
* **staffing_info** (`staffing_info`, text, optional): Enter information about the staff or team members involved in the project, including names, roles, or contact details.
* **other_info** (`other_info`, text, optional): If there's any other information you'd like to share that doesn't fit in the above categories, use this field.
* **submit_info** (`submit_info`, text, optional): This is the submission button to finalize your completed form.
