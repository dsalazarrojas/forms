# moving_items_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The moving_items_information_form is used to collect information about items being moved or inspected. This form helps track details such as weight, description, condition, and value of the items.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the form page "moving_items_information_form_page_1" to begin.
2. Enter the weight of the item in the "moving_items_weight" field.
3. Provide a detailed description of the item in the "moving_items_description" field.
4. Select the condition of the item from the options "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" in the "moving_items_condition" field.
5. Enter the value of the item in the "moving_items_value" field.
6. Enter the price of the item in the "moving_items_price" field.
7. Select the person who inspected the item in the "moving_items_inspected_by" field.
8. Enter the date of inspection in the "moving_items_inspection_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **moving_items_weight** (Number): Enter the weight of the item being moved.
* **moving_items_description** (Text): Provide a detailed description of the item being moved.
* **moving_items_condition** (Select Multiple): Select the condition of the item from "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **moving_items_value** (Number): Enter the value of the item being moved.
* **moving_items_price** (Text): Enter the price of the item being moved.
* **moving_items_inspected_by** (Text): Select the person who inspected the item.
* **moving_items_inspection_date** (Date): Enter the date the item was inspected.
