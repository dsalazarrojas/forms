# Air Ambulance Provider Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from air ambulance providers to register their services with our organization. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your provider's name under "Provider name" in the first field.
2. Enter the name of the primary contact person under "Contact person" in the second field.
3. Enter your contact email address under "Contact email" in the third field. This email will be used for communication and documentation.
4. Select all the types of aircraft your provider uses for operations under "Aircraft types".
5. List the regions or zones covered by your service area under "Service area coverage".
6. Describe your regulatory approvals and expirations under "Licenses and certifications".
7. If applicable, enter your typical emergency response time under "Typical emergency response time".
8. If applicable, provide insurance coverage details under "Insurance details".
9. Add any additional comments or notes under "Additional comments".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Provider name** (`provider_name`, text, required): Enter the legal name of your air ambulance provider.
* **Contact person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for operations.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address for dispatch and documentation.
* **Aircraft types** (`aircraft_types`, select multiple, required): Select all the types of aircraft used for operations:
	+ Helicopter
	+ Fixed wing
	+ Rotorcraft
	+ Tiltrotor
* **Service area coverage** (`service_area`, text, required): List the regions or zones covered by your service area.
* **Licenses and certifications** (`licenses_certifications`, text, required): Describe your regulatory approvals and expirations.
* **Typical emergency response time** (`emergency_response_time`, time, optional): Enter the average time it takes for your team to respond to emergency situations.
* **Insurance details** (`insurance_details`, text, optional): Provide insurance coverage details for operations.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any special capabilities, notes, or other relevant information.
