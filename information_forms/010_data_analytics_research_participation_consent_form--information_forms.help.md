# data_analytics_research_participation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants regarding their consent and participation in data analytics research studies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please review each field carefully and provide accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do you give your consent to participate in this research data analytics project?** (`participant_consent_1`, `text`, required): This field asks for your initial consent to participate in the research data analytics project. Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your agreement.
* **What is your contact information (email, phone number, or address)?** (`participant_contact`, `text`, required): Please provide your contact information so that we can reach you if needed.
* **What is the purpose of this research study?** (`research_purpose`, `text`, required): This field is for you to briefly describe the purpose of the research study.
* **Do you agree to participate in this research study?** (`research_participation_agreement`, `checkbox`, required): Please select "I agree" or "I do not agree" to indicate your agreement.
* **Do you agree to share your data for analytics purposes?** (`data_analytics_agreement`, `checkbox`, required): Please select "I agree" or "I do not agree" to indicate your agreement.
* **Are you willing to participate in data analytics projects?** (`data_analytics_participation`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select one or more options that apply to you.
* **Have you read and understood the terms of this research study?** (`research_participation_terms`, `text`, required): This field is for you to confirm that you have read and understood the terms of the research study.
* **Have you read and understood the data analytics agreement?** (`data_analytics_agreement_terms`, `text`, required): This field is for you to confirm that you have read and understood the data analytics agreement.
* **Are you aware of the research study conduct?** (`research_participation_conduct`, `text`, required): This field is for you to confirm that you are aware of the research study conduct.
* **Do you give your consent to participate in this research study again?** (`participant_consent_2`, `text`, required): This field is identical to the first one, but please review your initial consent carefully before agreeing again.
* **Are you willing to share your data for analytics purposes again?** (`data_analytics_data`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select one or more options that apply to you.
* **Do you agree to participate in this research study again?** (`research_participation_agreement_2`, `text`, required): This field is identical to the first "Do you agree to participate in this research study?" field, but please review your initial agreement carefully before agreeing again.
* **Are you willing to participate in data analytics projects again?** (`data_analytics_participation_2`, `text`, required): Please select one or more options that apply to you.
* **Are you aware of the research study conduct again?** (`research_participation_conduct_2`, `text`, required): This field is identical to the previous one, but please review your initial awareness carefully before agreeing again.
* **Do you give your consent to participate in this research study?** (`participant_consent_3`, `text`, required): This field is identical to the first one, but please review your initial consent carefully before agreeing again.
* **Data Analytics Participation 3** (`data_analytics_participation_3`, `text`, required): This field seems to be a duplicate of the previous data analytics participation field.
* **Are you aware of the participant conduct?** (`participant_conduct`, `text`, required): This field is for you to confirm that you are aware of the participant conduct.

Please note that some fields appear to be repetitive or identical, which could lead to confusion. Ensure you review each field carefully before providing your responses.
