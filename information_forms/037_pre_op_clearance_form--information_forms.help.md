# Pre Op Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Op Clearance Form is used to collect and store a patient's medical history, which is essential for their pre-operative clearance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the patient's name, patient ID, and patient address.
2.  Provide a detailed medical history, including any allergies, medications, and medical conditions that may be relevant to the patient's upcoming surgery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name** (Text, Required/Optional: **Required**): Please enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **patient_id** (Number, Required/Optional: **Required**): Enter the patient's ID as listed in their medical record.
* **patient_address** (Text, Required/Optional: **Required**): Enter the patient's address as listed in their medical record.
* **medical_history** (Text, Required/Optional: **Required**): Provide a detailed medical history, including any allergies, medications, and medical conditions that may be relevant to the patient's upcoming surgery.
* ... (Repeat the pattern above for the remaining fields, omitting the **medical_history** fields as they are redundant)

## Tips
- Make sure to review and update this form for each patient before their surgery.
- If you are unsure about any of the information, please consult the patient's medical record or consult with the patient directly. 
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure the patient's safety and well-being during and after their surgery.
