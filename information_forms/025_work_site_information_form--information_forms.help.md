# work_site_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a work site, including its details, location, contact information, and safety notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the site details, including the location and any necessary safety notes.
2. Enter the contact information for the site, including the name, email, phone number, and emergency contact.
3. Select the job status and location type.
4. Enter any access requirements and notes.
5. Enter any site visit notes.
6. Enter the contact address and other contact details.
7. Enter any additional access requirements and notes.
8. Select the form completion status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Details** (1): Enter a brief description of the site, including its location and any relevant details.
* **Job Location** (2): Enter the location of the job or work site.
* **Contact Details** (3): Enter the contact person's name, email, and phone number.
* **Name** (4): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Email** (5): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Phone** (6): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Safety Notes** (7): Enter any safety notes or concerns related to the site.
* **Access Requirements** (8): Enter any access requirements for the site, such as permissions or restrictions.
* **Job Status** (9): Select the current status of the job, including Active, Pending, or Cancelled.
* **Job Location Type** (10): Select the type of location, including Office or Field.
* **Work Hours** (11): Enter the work hours or schedule for the site.
* **Work Dates** (12): Enter the dates of the job or work site.
* **Notes** (13): Enter any additional notes or comments about the site.
* **Form Completion Status** (14): Select the completion status of the form, including Completed, In Progress, or Draft.
* **Site Access Requirements** (15): Enter any additional access requirements for the site.
* **Safety Precautions** (16): Enter any safety precautions or concerns related to the site.
* **Access Equipment** (17): Enter any equipment required for access to the site.
* **Site Visit Notes** (18): Enter any notes from site visits.
* **Emergency Contact** (19): Enter the contact information for emergency purposes.
* **Contact Address** (20): Enter the contact address of the site.
* **Access Requirements Other** (21): Enter any additional access requirements for the site.
* **Access Requirements Notes** (22): Enter any additional notes about the access requirements.
* **Contact Relationship** (23): Enter the relationship between the contact person and the site.
* **Contact Title** (24): Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Contact Company** (25): Enter the company name of the contact person.
