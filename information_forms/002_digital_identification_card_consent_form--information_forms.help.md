# Digital Identification Card Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a digital consent form to collect user information, including details about your identification card, for administrative purposes. You are agreeing to share your information with our team to process and verify your identification card.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:
1. Fill in all required and optional fields with accurate and valid information.
2. Ensure you provide a valid email address for verification and communication purposes.
3. Check the "Consent" field to agree to share your information with us.
4. You can add any additional information in the "Details" field if required.
5. Finally, add a signature in the "Signature" field to confirm your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): This section is where you can provide additional information about yourself. Please fill in any relevant details that will help us process your identification card.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Please select 'True' to indicate that you agree to share your information with us.
* **Photos** (`photos`, text, optional): Please upload any additional photos that are relevant to your identification card, if required.
* **Details** (`details`, text, optional): This section is where you can provide any additional information about your identification card, if required.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Please add a digital signature to confirm your identity. This is usually your handwritten signature.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please fill in a valid email address for verification and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, number, optional): Please fill in a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please fill in your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
