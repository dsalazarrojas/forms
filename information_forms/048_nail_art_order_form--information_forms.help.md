# Nail Art Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for nail art services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the format of yourname@example.com.
4. Enter your **Phone** number.
5. Select the **Service Type** you would like to order (Nail Art, Nail Polish, or Nail Shape).
6. Provide a brief **Description** of your order.
7. Select the price you are **Price** willing to pay.
8. Select your **Availability** (Available Now, Available Soon, or Available By Appointment).
9. Select the date and **Time** you would like to have your order fulfilled.
10. Select your **Nail Type** (Nail Acrylic, Nail Gel, or Nail Tip).
11. Select the length of your **Nail Length** (Short, Medium, or Long).
12. Select the width of your **Nail Width** (Narrow, Wide, or Extra Wide).
13. Select the color of your **Color** (Red, Pink, or Blue).
14. Select the shape of your **Shape** (Oval, Almond, or Squoval).
15. Add any additional **Notes** about your order.
16. List the tools that will be needed for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format of yourname@example.com.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you want (Nail Art, Nail Polish, or Nail Shape).
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your order.
* **Price**: Select the price you are willing to pay.
* **Availability**: Select when you would like to have your order fulfilled (Available Now, Available Soon, or Available By Appointment).
* **Date and Time**: Select the date and time you want to have your order fulfilled.
* **Nail Type**: Select the type of nail you want (Nail Acrylic, Nail Gel, or Nail Tip).
* **Nail Length**: Select the length of your nail (Short, Medium, or Long).
* **Nail Width**: Select the width of your nail (Narrow, Wide, or Extra Wide).
* **Color**: Select the color of your nail (Red, Pink, or Blue).
* **Shape**: Select the shape of your nail (Oval, Almond, or Squoval).
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Tool**: List the tools that will be needed for your order.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately to avoid any issues with your order.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your service provider for clarification.
* This form is only accessible on a specific page to prevent duplicate orders.
