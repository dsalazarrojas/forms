# Personal Information Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect personal information and preferences from users, including contact details and emergency contacts. The form is meant to be used in various settings where users need to provide sensitive information, such as in healthcare or financial institutions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the required fields, marked with \*.
2.  For fields marked as "optional", you can choose to fill them out or skip them.
3.  Make sure to review each field carefully and fill out the correct information.
4.  If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification or assistance.
