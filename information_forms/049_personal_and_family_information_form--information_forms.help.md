# Personal And Family Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personal And Family Information Form is a collection of questions that gather relevant personal and family data. This form is used to provide information to our organization for the purpose of [insert purpose here, e.g., processing, evaluation, or other specific use].

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Personal Information**: This section is meant for the individual or family member to provide their personal details. Please enter your name, phone number, and email address.
2.  **Spouse Information (if applicable)**: If you are filling out this form for a spouse or partner, please provide their name and other relevant details.
3.  **Address**: Enter your home address.
4.  **Relationship Status**: Select your current relationship status from the dropdown options. This is optional.
5.  **Number of Dependents**: If you have dependents, please enter the number of dependents you have.
6.  **Name of Spouse (if applicable)**: If you have a spouse or partner, please enter their name.
7.  **Name of Dependents (if applicable)**: If you have dependents, please enter their names.
8.  **Age**: Enter your age.
9.  **Occupation**: Select any relevant occupation details. This field is optional and not required.
