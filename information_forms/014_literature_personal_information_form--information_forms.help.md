# Literature Personal Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for literature purposes, ensuring that the collected data is accurate and complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide your participant details in the "Participant Details" section. Enter your name, contact information, and any relevant details about your participation.
2. Indicate your participation in programs by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "Participant Programs" dropdown menu.
3. If applicable, provide the name and date of the program you're participating in.
4. Enter your participant name and email address in the respective fields.
5. Provide your participant phone number and address in the "Participant Phone" and "Participant Address" fields.
6. Add any additional notes about your participation in the "Participant Note" field.
7. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: This section allows you to provide your participant details, including your name, contact information, and any relevant details about your participation.
* **Participant Contact Information**: Enter your participant contact information, such as email and phone number.
* **Participant Programs**: Select "Yes" if you are participating in the program, and "No" otherwise.
* **Program Name**: If you are participating in a program, enter the name of the program here.
* **Program Date**: If you are participating in a program, enter the date of the program here.
* **Program Time**: If you are participating in a program, enter the time of the program here.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your participant name.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your participant email address.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your participant phone number.
* **Participant Address**: Enter your participant address.
* **Participant Note**: Add any additional notes about your participation.
