# Wedding Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Quote Request Form is designed to be filled out by potential customers who are interested in requesting quotes for wedding services. This form helps our team understand their needs and provide accurate quotes for their event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter the date of your wedding in the "wedding_date" field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
3. Provide a budget for your wedding in the "wedding_budget" field.
4. Enter your contact email address in the "email" field.
5. Fill out your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Select your preferred vendor from the dropdown list in the "wedding_vendor" field.
7. Choose the services you are interested in getting a quote for in the "wedding_services" field (you can select multiple services).
8. Add any additional comments about your event in the "comments" field.
9. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name for us to address our communication with you.
* **Wedding Date**: Enter the date of your wedding (e.g. 2024-05-15).
* **Wedding Budget**: Provide your estimated budget for the wedding services.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can send you a quote and further information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further questions.
* **Wedding Vendor**: Select your preferred vendor for your wedding services.
* **Wedding Services**: Choose the services you are interested in getting a quote for (e.g. wedding cake, DJ, flowers).
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your event.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your request and get a quote.
