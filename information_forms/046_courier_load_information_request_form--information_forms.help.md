# Courier Load Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and track information about courier loads in a standardized and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your information clearly and accurately in each field.
3. If a field is not marked as "required," it is optional and can be left blank if you do not have the information.
4. Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Load Information** (`load_information`): A brief description of the load you are requesting information about. This field is not required, but it helps the team understand the context of the load.
* **Load ID** (`load_id`): Enter the unique identifier assigned to the load. This field is not required, but it helps the team track and reference the load more efficiently.
* **Trailer Number** (`trailer_number`): The trailer number associated with the load, if applicable.
* **Shipment ID** (`shipment_id`): Enter the shipment ID if you have it. This field is not required, but it aids in tracking and referencing the shipment.
* **Load Date** (`load_date`): Enter the date the load is scheduled or occurred. This field is not required but helps in tracking the timeline.
* **Load Time** (`load_time`): Enter the time the load is scheduled or occurred. This field is not required, but it assists in tracking the timeline.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field or require clarification, do not hesitate to reach out to the form administrators.
* Duplicate submissions will be flagged and rejected to prevent unnecessary work and ensure data consistency.
