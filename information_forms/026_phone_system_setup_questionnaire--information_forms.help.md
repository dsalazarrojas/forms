# Phone System Setup Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information for setting up a phone system for a company or organization. It helps ensure accurate and efficient setup, maintenance, or repair of the phone system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields for your company's details, including:
	* Company Name
	* Contact Name
	* Email
	* Phone Number
	* System Name
	* System Type
2. Choose the correct options for your phone system, including:
	* System Size
	* Frequency of setup (Yes or No)
	* Setup Method (Yes or No)
	* Setup Date
	* Setup Time
	* Service Provider (Yes or No)
3. Add any additional information in the Notes field if needed.
4. Ensure that all fields are filled correctly before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person in charge or responsible for the phone system.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your company's phone number.
* **System Name** (`system_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the phone system you are setting up.
* **System Type** (`system_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of phone system you are setting up (Yes or No).
* **System Size** (`system_size`, number, required): Choose the size of your phone system.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the phone system is being set up (if applicable).
* **Setup Frequency** (`setup_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Choose the frequency of setup for your phone system (Yes or No).
* **Setup Method** (`setup_method`, select_one, required): Choose the method of setup for your phone system (Yes or No).
* **Setup Date** (`setup_date`, date, required): Enter the date of setup for your phone system.
* **Setup Time** (`setup_time`, time, required): Enter the time of setup for your phone system.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, select_one, required): Choose the service provider for your phone system (Yes or No).
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the partner or service provider.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional information or comments about the phone system setup.

Tips:
* Ensure that all fields are filled correctly before submission.
* If you need to add or remove fields, please contact the relevant authorities.
* This form is designed to be completed once for each phone system setup.
