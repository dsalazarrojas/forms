# radiation_dosimetry_information_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The radiation dosimetry information request form is used to collect data for review and analysis in the field of radiation dosimetry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the contact information in the **Contact** section.
	* Enter the contact name.
	* Enter the contact email address.
	* Enter the contact phone number (optional).
2. Enter the date of birth and last exam date in the **Dates** section.
3. Select the radiation field from the list of options.
4. Choose the radiation sources (if applicable) from the list of options. You can also enter an "Other" option if the radiation source is not listed.
5. Enter the dose, dose unit, radiation level, radiation area, and dose rate in the **Radiation Exposure** section.
6. Select the exposure unit and total dose in the **Exposure Duration** section.
7. Enter any additional comments or remarks in the **Comments** section (optional).
8. Upload a file with supporting evidence (e.g., images, videos, or PDFs) if applicable. This is optional.
9. Finally, select the review status of the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person making the request.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person making the request.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person making the request (optional).
* **Contact Department**: Enter the department of the person making the request.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the individual being examined.
* **Date of Last Exam**: Enter the date of the last radiation exam.
* **Radiation Field**: Select the type of radiation exposure (e.g., radiation, external, or other).
* **Radiation Source**: Select the type of radiation source (e.g., X-ray, Gamma ray, or Neutron). If not listed, choose "Other" and enter the source type.
* **Other Radiation Source**: Enter the radiation source if not listed in the previous field.
* **Dose**: Enter the amount of radiation received.
* **Dose Unit**: Select the unit of dose (e.g., mGy or mSv).
* **Radiation Level**: Select the level of radiation exposure (e.g., Low, Medium, or High).
* **Radiation Area**: Select the area of exposure (e.g., Chest or Head). If not listed, choose "Other" and enter the exposure area.
* **Other Radiation Area**: Enter the radiation area if not listed in the previous field.
* **Dose Rate**: Enter the dose rate.
* **Dose Rate Unit**: Select the unit of dose rate (e.g., Gy/hr or Gy/min).
* **Total Dose**: Enter the total dose received.
* **Exposure Time**: Select the exposure time (e.g., seconds or minutes).
* **Exposure Unit**: Select the unit of exposure time (e.g., s or min).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or remarks.
* **Uploaded File**: Upload a file with supporting evidence (optional).
* **Uploaded File Type**: Select the type of uploaded file (e.g., PDF, image, or video).
* **Uploaded File Size**: Enter the size of the uploaded file.
* **Upload Date**: Enter the date of upload (optional).
* **Review Status**: Select the status of the request (e.g., Not reviewed, Reviewed, or Rejected).
