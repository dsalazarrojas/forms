# Patient Demographics And History Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect patient information, including their name, date of birth, medical history, and contact details, to provide comprehensive care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter the patient's first name in the "Patient First Name" field.
* Enter the patient's last name in the next field.
* Select the patient's date of birth using the date picker.
* Provide any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.
* List any medications the patient is currently taking or has been prescribed in the "Medication List" field.
* List any allergies or sensitivities to medications in the "Allergies" field.
* Enter any known medical conditions in the "Medical Conditions" field.
* Provide contact information for the patient, such as phone numbers and addresses, in the "Contact Information" field.
* Enter the name and contact details of the patient's medical contact, if applicable, in the "Medical Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Patient Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Select the patient's date of birth using the calendar picker.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical history, including previous treatments or conditions.
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, text, optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking or has been prescribed.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities to medications the patient has.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Enter any known medical conditions the patient has, such as diabetes or hypertension.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Provide contact information for the patient, such as phone numbers and addresses.
* **Medical Contact** (`medical_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact details of the patient's medical contact, if applicable.
