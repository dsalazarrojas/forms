# Energy Community Feedback Information Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users in the Energy Community. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions to enhance our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your name and email address (if you wish to provide contact information).
2. Share your feedback by selecting the relevant issue or concern category (if applicable).
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the text field.
4. Rate your overall experience with our service.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`name`, `text`, Optional): A text field where you can provide your name if you wish to share it.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Optional): Enter your email address if you would like us to contact you regarding your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, Optional): Select the date you experienced the issue or received the service.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, Optional): Select the time you experienced the issue or received the service.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, Optional): Rate your overall experience with our service from 1-10, with 10 being the best.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Choose from the available options to describe your feedback:
	+ I have an issue
	+ I have a concern
	+ I have a suggestion
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, `text`, Optional): If you do not find a suitable option in the previous field, use this text field to provide your feedback.
* **Other Comments** (`rating`, `select_multiple`, Optional) is actually not required, it was not necessary in the original YAML.

Tips:
* Please be as specific as possible when providing your feedback.
* Use clear and concise language to describe your issue or concern.
* Rate your experience honestly and objectively.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, do not hesitate to share them.
