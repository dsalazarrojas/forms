# M3U List URL Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The M3U List URL Submission Form is designed to collect information from users regarding M3U lists, which are likely related to media or content sharing. This form helps validate or verify the submission of these M3U lists, ensuring they are legitimate and meet certain standards or requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the M3U List URL in the "M3U List URL" field. This is the primary URL associated with the M3U list you wish to submit.
2. Select the usage details for the submitted M3U list from the "Usage Details" field. This field is used to describe the purpose or intent behind the submitted M3U list.
3. Confirm your consent for the submission by selecting "I accept" from the "Consent" field. If you do not wish to provide consent, please select "Do not accept" in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### M3U List URL (m3u_list_url)
* The "M3U List URL" field is the primary input for submitting the M3U list URL.
### Usage Details (usage_details)
* The "Usage Details" field allows you to describe the purpose or intent behind the submitted M3U list.
* You may select multiple options from the drop-down menu.
### Consent (consent)
* The "Consent" field is used to confirm your agreement or acceptance of the submission.
* Select "I accept" to confirm your consent, or "Do not accept" to decline.

## Tips
* Please ensure the M3U list URL is accurate and up-to-date.
* Clearly select the appropriate usage details to ensure the submission is valid.
* Confirm your consent before submitting the form to avoid any potential issues.
