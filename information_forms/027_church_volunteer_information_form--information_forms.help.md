# Church Volunteer Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your background, interests, and availability to help us match you with volunteer opportunities within our church.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply scroll down each page and fill out the required fields. Be sure to read the explanations and examples provided below for each field to help guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Full Name** (page 1)
*   *Label:* Full Name
*   *Type:* text
*   *Required:* true
*   *Description:* Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### **Email Address** (page 1)
*   *Label:* Email Address
*   *Type:* email
*   *Required:* true
*   *Description:* Enter your valid email address where we can contact you regarding volunteer opportunities and important updates.

### **Phone Number** (page 1)
*   *Label:* Phone Number
*   *Type:* text
*   *Required:* true
*   *Description:* Enter your primary contact phone number where we can reach you during working hours.

### **Home Address** (page 1)
*   *Label:* Home Address
*   *Type:* text
*   *Required:* true
*   *Description:* Enter your home address, including street, city, state, and zip code for our records.

### **Date of Birth** (page 1)
*   *Label:* Date of Birth
*   *Type:* date
*   *Required:* true
*   *Description:* Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### **Are You a Church Member?** (page 2)
*   *Label:* Are You a Church Member
*   *Type:* select_one
*   *Required:* true
*   *Description:* Select how you're affiliated with our church: Yes, active member, Yes, but not active, No, but interested, or No, just helping.

### **How Long Have You Been a Member?** (page 2)
*   *Label:* How Long Have You Been a Member
*   *Type:* select_one
*   *Required:* false
*   *Description:* If you're a church member, choose how long you've been actively participating.

### **Which Areas Are You Interested In?** (page 2)
*   *Label:* Which Areas Are You Interested In
*   *Type:* select_multiple
*   *Required:* true
*   *Description:* Select all the areas where you'd like to serve, such as Worship/Music Team, Children's Ministry, etc.

### **When Are You Available?** (page 3)
*   *Label:* When Are You Available
*   *Type:* select_multiple
*   *Required:* true
*   *Description:* Choose your availability for volunteering, such as Sunday mornings, Wednesday evenings, etc.

### **How Many Hours Can You Commit?** (page 3)
*   *Label:* How Many Hours Can You Commit
*   *Type:* select_one
*   *Required:* true
*   *Description:* Indicate the number of hours you can commit to volunteering per week.

### **What Skills Do You Bring?** (page 3)
*   *Label:* What Skills Do You Bring
*   *Type:* select_multiple
*   *Required:* true
*   *Description:* List any skills you bring to the table, such as Teaching, Music, Technical/AV, etc.

### **Please Describe Your Skills** (page 4)
*   *Label:* Please Describe Your Skills
*   *Type:* text
*   *Required:* false
*   *Description:* Provide more details about your skills and how they can be applied in our church.

### **Do You Have Previous Volunteer Experience?** (page 4)
*   *Label:* Do You Have Previous Volunteer Experience
*   *Type:* select_one
*   *Required:* true
*   *Description:* Indicate if you have previous experience as a volunteer, whether at our church or elsewhere.

### **Please Describe Your Experience** (page 4)
*   *Label:* Please Describe Your Experience
*   *Type:* text
*   *Required:* false
*   *Description:* If you have previous experience, please share a brief description of it.

### **Are You Willing to Complete a Background Check?** (page 4)
*   *Label:* Are You Willing to Complete a Background Check
*   *Type:* select_one
*   *Required:* true
*   *Description:* Confirm your willingness to undergo a background check for certain volunteer roles.

### **What Training Would You Need?** (page 5)
*   *Label:* What Training Would You Need
*   *Type:* select_multiple
*   *Required:* true
*   *Description:* Select any training you believe you might need for your volunteer role, such as Volunteer orientation, Child safety training, etc.

### **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (page 5)
*   *Label:* Emergency Contact Name and Phone
*   *Type:* text
*   *Required:* true
*   *Description:* Enter the name and phone number of a person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.

### **Any Medical Conditions We Should Know?** (page 5)
*   *Label:* Any Medical Conditions We Should Know
*   *Type:* text
*   *Required:* false
*   *Description:* If you have any medical conditions that may impact your volunteer role, please share them with us for safety and consideration.

### **Any Physical Limitations or Restrictions?** (page 5)
*   *Label:* Any Physical Limitations or Restrictions
*   *Type:* text
*   *Required:* false
*   *Description:* If you have any physical limitations or restrictions that may impact your volunteer role, please share them with us.

### **Why Do You Want to Volunteer?** (page 6)
*   *Label:* Why Do You Want to Volunteer
*   *Type:* text
*   *Required:* true
*   *Description:* Share your motivation for volunteering and how you can contribute to our community.

### **When Can You Start?** (page 6)
*   *Label:* When Can You Start
*   *Type:* date
*   *Required:* true
*   *Description:* Enter the start date when you can begin volunteering.

### **Any Additional Comments?** (page 6)
*   *Label:* Any Additional Comments
*   *Type:* text
*   *Required:* false
*   *Description:* Is there anything else you'd like to share or express as a concern?

Remember to review each field and its description to ensure you answer every question truthfully and accurately. If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to seek clarification from our team. We're here to support you through this process, and we look forward to exploring volunteer opportunities with you within our church community!
