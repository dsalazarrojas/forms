# Contact Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect contact information from users, which will be used to follow up with them regarding their interests or needs. This form is designed to gather essential contact details, such as name, email addresses, phone numbers, and address information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Primary Email Address and Secondary Email Address (if applicable).
3. Provide your Mobile Phone Number and Work Phone Number (if applicable).
4. Enter your Street Address, City, State or Province, and ZIP or Postal Code.
5. Select your Country from the options provided.
6. Choose your Preferred Contact Method (e.g., Email, Phone Call, Text Message, or Mail).
7. Select the Best Time to Contact (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Anytime).
8. If you are providing this information for a specific purpose, select the appropriate option from the list (e.g., Newsletter Signup, Event Registration, Service Inquiry, or Account Creation).
9. Finally, confirm your consent to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Primary Email Address** (`email_primary`, email, required): Enter your main email address.
* **Secondary Email Address** (`email_secondary`, email, optional): If you have an alternate email address, enter it here.
* **Mobile Phone Number** (`phone_mobile`, text, required): Enter your mobile phone number.
* **Work Phone Number** (`phone_work`, text, optional): If you have a work phone number, enter it here.
* **Street Address** (`address_street`, text, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`address_city`, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (`address_state`, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`address_zip`, text, required): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Country** (`address_country`, select_one, required): Select your country from the options provided.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_preference`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone Call, Text Message, or Mail).
* **Best Time to Contact** (`contact_time`, select_one, optional): Select the preferred time to contact you (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Anytime).
* **Purpose of Information Collection** (`purpose`, select_one, required): Select why you are providing this information (e.g., Newsletter Signup, Event Registration, Service Inquiry, or Account Creation).
* **Consent to Contact** (`consent`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to be contacted.

## Tips
Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your information is collected correctly. If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this guide or ask for assistance.
