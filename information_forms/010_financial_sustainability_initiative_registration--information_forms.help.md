# Financial Sustainability Initiative Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information from users about a financial sustainability initiative. The collected data will be reviewed to ensure it meets the required standards for financial sustainability.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields on the form with accurate and thorough information about your financial sustainability initiative.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide a brief description of your financial sustainability initiative. Please provide a concise and clear summary of the initiative.
* **Initiative Info** (`initiative_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide additional information about your financial sustainability initiative. Please provide any relevant details that will help us understand your initiative better.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide contact information of the person or organization behind the initiative. Please include your name, email, and phone number so that we can get in touch with you.
* **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide any additional submission details that are relevant to the initiative. Please provide any relevant documents or supporting evidence that will help us evaluate your submission.
* **Financial Sustainability** (`financial_sustainability`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide information about the financial sustainability of the initiative. Please provide details about how you plan to sustain your financial operations in the long term.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately to avoid any delays in the registration process.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your submission and contact you if we need any additional information.
