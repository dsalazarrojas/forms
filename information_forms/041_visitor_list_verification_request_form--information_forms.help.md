# Visitor List Verification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visitor List Verification Request Form is a form designed to collect essential information about visitors to verify their identity. This form helps in maintaining accurate records and ensuring that visitors are accounted for.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field. Ensure that the date format is mm/dd/yyyy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you use for communication. This field is required to ensure that we can contact you for any follow-up information or verification.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a phone number that we can use to contact you. This field is not required but recommended for faster verification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of your birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy. This will help us to verify your identity.
 
## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are unable to complete this form, please contact us for assistance.
- Do not submit duplicates for the same individual.
