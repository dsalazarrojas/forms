# New Account Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a new account, enabling you to create a comprehensive profile for the account. Fill in the necessary details for the account you are creating.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the relevant information. Ensure that the "Account Type" field is filled in correctly to reflect the nature of the account. When you've finished, click the "Submit" button to save the new account information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Account Information** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the new account.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the company.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the first name of the person associated with the account.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the last name of the person associated with the account.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address associated with the account.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the account location.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of account from the options provided.
* **Account Notes** (`account_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the account.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number associated with the account.
* **Fax** (`fax`, text, required/optional): Enter the fax number associated with the account.

## Tips
* Double-check the "Company Name" and "Account Type" fields to ensure accuracy.
* Use the "Account Notes" field to provide any relevant information about the account that does not fit into other fields.
* If you need to create multiple accounts for the same company, please contact your administrator for assistance.
