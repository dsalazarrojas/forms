# mailing_address_update_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update a mailing address associated with an account or profile.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access this form.
2. Enter your updates in the fields provided below.
3. Click the "Submit" button to submit your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the first name of the individual or organization associated with the address.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the last name of the individual or organization associated with the address.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address associated with the mailing address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `number`, required: false): Enter the phone number associated with the mailing address.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter the first line of the address.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the second line of the address.

## Tips
* Please double-check all fields before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any information, contact the relevant authority for clarification.
* If you need to update multiple addresses, submit each one separately.
