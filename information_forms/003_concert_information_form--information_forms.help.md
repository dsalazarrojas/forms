# Concert Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Concert Information Form is a comprehensive tool used to gather information about concerts and events. It helps ensure that all required details are collected accurately, facilitating smooth event planning, coordination, and execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the `Organizer name` field, which should reflect the name of the primary contact or organization responsible for the event.
2.  Next, provide the `Contact phone number` of the contact person, serving as the best number to reach for clarifications or any other urgent matters.
3.  Fill out the `Event title`, which should be the official name of the concert or show.
4.  Enter the proposed `Event date`, which will serve as the primary date for the event.
5.  Provide the full `Venue address`, including the city and state, ensuring that all attendees can find their way to the event.
6.  If required, estimate the `Expected attendance` by providing a rough number of attendees.
7.  In the `Technical contact names and phones` field, list the names and phone numbers of those responsible for sound and lighting contacts.
8.  In the `Special requirements or notes` field, indicate any special needs or conditions for the event, such as load-in times or required permits.
9.  Finally, in the `Additional documents required` field, list any extra documents that need to be provided, such as riders or contracts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Organizer name (organizer_name)**: The primary contact or organization name associated with the event.
*   **Contact phone number (contact_phone)**: The preferred phone number to contact the event organizer for any clarifications or urgent matters.
*   **Contact email (contact_email)**: The business email address for official correspondence with the event organizers.
*   **Event title (event_title)**: The official title of the concert or show that is being held.
*   **Event date (event_date)**: The date on which the event is proposed to take place.
*   **Venue address (venue_address)**: The full address of the venue where the concert is being held, including city and state.
*   **Expected attendance (expected_attendance)**: Estimated number of attendees anticipated for the concert or event, if applicable and not required.
*   **Technical contact names and phones (technical_contacts)**: List the names and phone numbers of those responsible for sound and lighting contacts.
*   **Special requirements or notes (special_requirements)**: Any special requirements or conditions that must be met for the event, such as load-in times or required permits.
*   **Additional documents required (additional_documents)**: Indicate any extra documents that are needed for the event, such as riders or contracts.

## Tips
*   Always ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid any confusion or delays in the event planning process.
*   Double-check the form for completeness before submission to guarantee that all necessary information is provided.
*   This form serves as a comprehensive resource for both event organizers and event staff, ensuring a smooth and successful execution of the concert or event.
