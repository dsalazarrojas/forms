# Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect contact information from users, including phone numbers, email addresses, and other relevant details. The form is used to capture a comprehensive understanding of user requirements and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information required for the form to be completed.
2. Enter your contact phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select your address type from the "Address" dropdown menu, which is "Street", "City", "State", or "Zip".
5. For other information you'd like to share about your contact, you can select from "Address", "Phone", and "Email" options in the "Other Contact Info" multi-select field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Phone (1)** (`contact_number`, number, required: false): Enter your contact phone number. This field is used to store your primary contact number.
* **Email (2)** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you. This field is used to capture your email address for communication purposes.
* **Address (3)** (`select_one_address`, select_one, required: false): Select your address type from the provided options: "Street", "City", "State", or "Zip". This field helps us understand your address type for better communication.
* **Other Contact Info (4)** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required: false): You can select additional contact information you'd like to share, such as "Address", "Phone", or "Email". This field allows you to specify multiple contact details for our records.
* **Date & Time (5)** (`date_time`, date, required: false): Enter the date and time of your availability. This field is used to schedule appointments or meetings with you.
* **(6)** (no label, not required): This field is not currently used in the provided form schema. 

## Tips
* Ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date, as this will aid in better communication and service.
* If you experience any issues or have questions, please contact us.
