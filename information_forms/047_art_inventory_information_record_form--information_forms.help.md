# Art Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for recording new artworks entering the inventory. It collects information about the artwork's title, artist, year of creation, medium, dimensions, condition, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Internal Inventory ID (SKU)
	* Title of the Artwork
	* Artist Full Name
	* Year of Creation
	* Medium and Materials
	* Dimensions (Height x Width x Depth)
	* Current Condition
	* Framing Status
	* Current Storage or Display Location
	* Date of Last Inventory Update
	* Recorded By (Staff Name)
2. Provide optional information if applicable:
	* Acquisition Date
	* Acquisition Cost (Currency)
	* Current Estimated Market Value
	* Insurance Provider Name
	* Provenance Summary
	* Detailed Condition Notes
3. Review and submit the form to create a new artwork record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Internal Inventory ID - SKU**: A unique identifier assigned to each artwork for tracking and management purposes.
* **Title of the Artwork**: The title of the artwork as it appears on the artwork itself or in relevant documentation.
* **Artist Full Name**: The name of the artist who created the artwork.
* **Year of Creation**: The year the artwork was created.
* **Medium and Materials**: A description of the materials and medium used in creating the artwork, e.g., oil on canvas.
* **Dimensions (Height x Width x Depth)**: The dimensions of the artwork, including height, width, and depth (if applicable).
* **Current Condition**: The current condition of the artwork, rated from Mint - Perfect to Poor - Requires Restoration.
* **Framing Status**: The current framing status of the artwork, e.g., Framed, Unframed, Stretched Canvas, Mounted.
* **Current Storage or Display Location**: The current location where the artwork is stored or displayed.
* **Date of Last Inventory Update**: The date the artwork's inventory record was last updated.
* **Recorded By (Staff Name)**: The name of the staff member who recorded the artwork's information.
* **Acquisition Date**: The date the artwork was acquired by the institution.
* **Acquisition Cost (Currency)**: The cost of acquiring the artwork, including any currency or conversion rates.
* **Current Estimated Market Value**: The current estimated value of the artwork.
* **Insurance Provider Name**: The name of the insurance provider for the artwork.
* **Provenance Summary**: A brief history of the artwork's ownership and exhibition history.
* **Detailed Condition Notes**: Any specific damage or restoration history of the artwork.
