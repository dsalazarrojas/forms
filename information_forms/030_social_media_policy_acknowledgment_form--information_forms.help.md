# social-media-policy-acknowledgment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture employee acknowledgment of the social media policy, ensuring that employees understand and agree to the company's online usage guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the employee name, email, and department to provide personal and contact information.
2. Answer "yes" to acknowledge that you have read and agree to the social media policy.
3. Provide your employee signature and date signed to confirm your acknowledgment.
4. Fill in the manager's name and department to provide managerial information.
5. Provide the manager's signature and date signed to confirm their approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee name** (employee_name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official company documentation.
* **employee email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address as registered with the company.
* **employee department** (department, text, required): Enter the department you belong to for work purposes.
* **manager name** (manager_name, text, required): Enter your direct manager's name as per company documentation.
* **I have read and agree to the social media policy.** (social_media_policy_sign_off, select_one, required): Select "yes" to confirm that you have read and agree to the social media policy.
* **employee signature** (signature, text, required): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and agree to the social media policy.
* **date signed** (date_signed, date, required): Enter the date you sign this acknowledgment form.
* **time signed** (time_signed, time, required): Enter the time you sign this acknowledgment form.
* **Manager Name 2** (manager_name_2, text, required): Enter your direct manager's name (if applicable).
* **Department 2** (department_2, text, required): Enter your department (if applicable).
* **Date 2** (date_2, date, required): Enter the date (if applicable).
* **Time 2** (time_2, time, required): Enter the time (if applicable).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* If you have not been given the opportunity to review and agree to the social media policy, do not fill out this form.
* Ensure that your manager's approval is obtained before filling out this form.
* Keep the original acknowledgment form for your records.
