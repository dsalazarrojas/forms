# Insurance Policy Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Policy Information Form is used to collect and store information about insurance policies, enabling policyholders, insurance companies, and other stakeholders to manage and track policies efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Insurance Policy Information Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Policyholder's information in the **Policyholder** field.
2. Enter the **Policy Number**, which is the unique identifier for the policy.
3. Select the **Policy Start Date**, which is the date when the policy began.
4. Select the **Policy End Date**, which is the date when the policy is expected to end.
5. Choose the **Policy Status** from the options: Active, Inactive, or Cancelled.
6. Select the **Claim Status** from the options: Paid, Unpaid, or Pending.
7. Enter the **Coverage Start Date**, which is the date when coverage began.
8. Enter the **Coverage End Date**, which is the date when coverage is expected to end.
9. Enter the **Coverage Amount**, which is the total coverage provided by the policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policyholder** (`policyholder`, text, optional): Enter the name and other identifying details of the policyholder.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, optional): Enter the unique identifier for the policy.
* **Policy Start Date** (`policy_start_date`, date, optional): Select the date when the policy began.
* **Policy End Date** (`policy_end_date`, date, optional): Select the date when the policy is expected to end.
* **Policy Status** (`policy_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the policy (Active, Inactive, or Cancelled).
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the claim (Paid, Unpaid, or Pending).
* **Coverage Start Date** (`coverage_start_date`, date, optional): Select the date when coverage began.
* **Coverage End Date** (`coverage_end_date`, date, optional): Select the date when coverage is expected to end.
* **Coverage Amount** (`coverage_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total coverage provided by the policy.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Double-check the accuracy of the data entered in each field to avoid errors.
- Review the form before submitting it to ensure all fields have been completed correctly.
