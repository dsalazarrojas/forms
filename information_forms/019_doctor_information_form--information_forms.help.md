# Doctor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doctor Information Form is a template used to collect contact and professional information from medical professionals. This form will help us gather necessary details about you, which will be used to provide you with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. If applicable, provide your Job Title.
3. Enter your Phone Number and Email for contact purposes.
4. If you have a Medical License, please indicate your License Number.
5. If you are associated with a hospital or medical institution, please provide the name of your Hospital Affiliation.
6. Finally, specify your Medical Specialty.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title** (text): Enter your professional title (e.g., Doctor, Surgeon, etc.) if applicable.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your contact phone number for communication purposes.
* **Email** (email): Enter your professional email address where you can be reached.
* **Medical License** (text): If you have a medical license, enter the license number or a note describing your qualifications (e.g., MD, DO, etc.).
* **Hospital Affiliation** (text): If you are associated with a hospital or medical institution, enter the name of the institution for reference purposes.
* **Medical Specialty** (text): List your area of specialization (e.g., Cardiologist, Orthopedic Surgeon, etc.).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid errors.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
