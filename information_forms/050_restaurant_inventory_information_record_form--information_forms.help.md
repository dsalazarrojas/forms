# Restaurant Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and update inventory information for a restaurant. It consists of three pages: Stock Take, Inventory Check, and Stock Levels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Stock Take" page to record the current stock levels for a particular restaurant.
2. On the "Inventory Check" page, select "Yes" if the inventory is correct, or "No" if there are discrepancies or issues.
3. On the "Stock Levels" page, enter the current stock level as a numerical value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stock Take** (`page_1`, text, optional): Complete this page by entering the current stock levels for the restaurant. This includes the quantity of each item in stock.
* **Inventory Check** (`page_2`, select_one, required): Check the inventory by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu. This helps identify any discrepancies or issues with the inventory.
* **Stock Levels** (`page_3`, number, optional): Enter the current stock level as a numerical value.
