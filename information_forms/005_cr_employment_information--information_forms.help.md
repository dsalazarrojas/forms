# CR Employment Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "CR Employment Information" form is a crucial document used to gather accurate and comprehensive data about employees in the organization. It is essential for maintaining up-to-date records and ensuring compliance with relevant laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2.  Enter your Full Legal Name in the "Full Legal Name" field (ID 2).
3.  Enter your Employee ID Number (ID 3).
4.  Enter the last 4 digits of your Social Security Number (ID 4).
5.  Choose your current Job Title from the dropdown list (ID 6).
6.  Select the Department or Unit where you are employed (ID 7).
7.  Choose your Employment Status (ID 8).
8.  Enter your Date of Hire, which is the first day you joined the company (ID 9).
9.  Select your Primary Work Location (ID 10).
10. Enter your Direct Supervisor's Name (ID 11).
11. Choose your Pay Frequency (ID 13).
12. Enter your Base Salary or Hourly Rate (ID 14).
13. Select your enrollment status in the company health plan (ID 15).
14. If necessary, add any Additional Employment Notes (ID 16).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name (ID 2)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents. This could be your legal name, maiden name, or adopted name.
* **Employee ID Number (ID 3)**: Enter your unique identification number assigned by the company.
* **Social Security Number - Last 4 Digits (ID 4)**: Enter the last 4 digits of your Social Security Number for verification purposes.
* **Job Title (ID 6)**: Select your current role within the company from the provided list.
* **Department or Unit (ID 7)**: Choose the department or unit where you are employed from the dropdown list.
* **Employment Status (ID 8)**: Select your current employment status from the provided list.
* **Date of Hire (ID 9)**: Enter the first date you joined the company as an employee.
* **Primary Work Location (ID 10)**: Select where you primarily perform your job duties.
* **Direct Supervisor Name (ID 11)**: Enter the name of your immediate supervisor.
* **Pay Frequency (ID 13)**: Choose how frequently you are paid.
* **Base Salary or Hourly Rate (ID 14)**: Enter your base salary or hourly pay rate as a numerical value.
* **Enrolled in Company Health Plan (ID 15)**: Select your enrollment status in the company health plan.
* **Additional Employment Notes (ID 16)**: Add any additional comments or information about your employment status, if necessary.
