# Property And Tenant Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a tenant's property and employment history, which is necessary for the rental application process.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please follow the instructions on each page to ensure that you provide accurate and complete information.
*   Please enter your information in the provided fields, and make sure to check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your leasing agent or property manager for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Please enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Required): Please enter your phone number in international format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
*   **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, Text, Required): Please enter the name of your current or past employer.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Required): Please enter your job title as it appears on your identification documents or employment contract.
*   **Work Start Date** (`work_start_date`, Date, Optional): If you have worked for your employer for less than one year, please enter the start date of your employment.
*   **Work End Date** (`work_end_date`, Date, Optional): If you have left or plan to leave your employment, please enter the date you last worked or expect to leave.
*   **Job Duration** (`job_duration`, Number, Required): Please enter the number of months or years you have worked for your employer.
*   **Reference First Name** (`reference_first_name`, Text, Optional): If you have a professional reference, please enter their first name.
*   **Reference Last Name** (`reference_last_name`, Text, Optional): If you have a professional reference, please enter their last name.
*   **Reference Company** (`reference_company`, Text, Optional): If you have a professional reference, please enter their company or organization.
*   **Reference Relationship** (`reference_relationship`, Text, Optional): If you have a professional reference, please describe your relationship with them.
*   **Crime Date** (`crime_date`, Date, Optional): If you have a past offense, please enter the date it occurred.
*   **Crime Location** (`crime_location`, Text, Optional): If you have a past offense, please enter the location where it occurred.
*   **Crime Details** (`crime_details`, Text, Optional): If you have a past offense, please describe the circumstances surrounding it.
*   **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, Date, Required): Please enter the date you moved into the property.
*   **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, Date, Required): Please enter the date you expect to move out of the property.
*   **Lease Term** (`lease_term`, Number, Required): Please enter the number of months or years you expect to rent the property.
*   **Rental Property** (`rental_property`, Text, Required): Please enter the name or address of the rental property.
*   **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, Text, Required): Please enter the frequency of rent payments you plan to make (e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly).
*   **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, Number, Required): Please enter the monthly or annual rent amount you plan to pay.

Note: Please ensure that you have reviewed and completed all fields accurately and truthfully to avoid any delays or rejections in the rental application process.
