# Housing Subsidy Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Housing Subsidy Request Form is designed to gather necessary information from individuals requesting housing subsidies. It is used to collect detailed and relevant data about a person's housing situation, which will be reviewed for assistance eligibility and program suitability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out **Housing Program Details** on Page 1. This section is optional, but please provide any relevant information about the housing program you are requesting assistance for.
- On Page 2, fill out **Household Information**. This should include details about the people living in your household and their relationship to you.
- Next, fill out **Contact Information** on Page 3. This will help the review team get in touch with you about your subsidy request.
- If you have a specific **Reason for Requesting Housing Subsidy**, please explain it in detail on Page 4.
- Finally, if you have any **Additional Information** that may be helpful in processing your request, please provide it on Page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Housing Program Details**: Enter the name of the housing program you are requesting assistance for.
* **Household Information**: List the members of your household, their age, and their relationship to you (e.g., spouse, child, parent, etc.).
* **Contact Information**: Enter your full name, address, phone number, and email address where you can be reached.
* **Reason for Requesting Housing Subsidy**: Provide a clear explanation of why you are requesting housing subsidies.
* **Additional Information**: If there is any other relevant information about your situation that may help the review team in processing your request, please provide it here.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your request is reviewed correctly.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
- Once you have completed the form, click on the **Submit** button to send it for review.
