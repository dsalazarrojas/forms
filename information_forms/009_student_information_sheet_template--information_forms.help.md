# Student Information Sheet Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Information Sheet Template is a form designed to collect essential student information for administrative purposes. It will be used in an academic or educational setting, such as a school or university, to gather and store information about students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read each page carefully and answer all questions accurately and completely.
* Ensure that you have reviewed and agreed to the information being collected.
* Fill in the form with the requested information about the student.
* Submit the completed form to the designated authority.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Personal Details

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter the student's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter the student's last name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Please enter the student's ID number.
* **High School** (`high_school`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the high school the student attended.

### Page 2: Academic Information

* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date of the student's graduation.
* **Academic Interests** (`academic_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Please indicate the student's interests by selecting one or more options from the list.

### Page 3: Contact Information

* **Grades** (`grades`, text, optional): Please enter any relevant academic grades or qualifications.
* **GPA** (`gpa`, number, optional): Please enter the student's current GPA (if applicable).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Please enter the student's address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter the student's phone number.

Note: The student's email and phone number should be accurate and up-to-date.
 
### Tips
* Ensure that all information is accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the designated authority.
* The student's information will be used for administrative purposes only.
 
Please review the form carefully and submit it accurately and completely.
