# Military Missions Volunteer Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Military Missions Volunteer Information Form is a crucial document used to collect information from volunteers who are willing to participate in military missions. This form gathers essential details about the volunteer, their military background, and their availability for deployment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
* Enter your contact information, including your Phone number and Email address.
* Select your Gender from the provided options.
* Enter your Military ID number.
* Choose your Branch of service from the provided options.
* Select any units you are affiliated with (if applicable).
* Choose the Duty Location where you will be deployed.
* (Optional) Enter the Deployment Date if you have already been deployed.
* Finally, select the type of mission you are willing to participate in (Operation, Training, or Deployment).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication with the organization.
* **Birth Date**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Military ID**: Enter your unique Military ID number.
* **Branch**: Choose your branch of service from the provided options.
* **Unit**: Select any units you are affiliated with (if applicable).
* **Duty Location**: Choose the location where you will be deployed.
* **Deployment Date**: (Optional) Enter the date of your current or previous deployment.
* **Mission Type**: Choose the type of mission you are willing to participate in.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for your contact details and military background.
* Choose the correct branch and unit affiliation to ensure accurate processing of your application.
* Select the correct Duty Location and Mission Type to ensure you are matched with suitable deployments.
