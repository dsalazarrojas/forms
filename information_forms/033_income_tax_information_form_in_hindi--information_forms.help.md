# income_tax_information_form_in_hindi - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect tax-related information from the tax payer, which can be used for various tax purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
This form can be completed by filling out the following sections:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tax Income** (`tax_income`, `number`, required: false): Enter your total income from all sources, such as salary, business, or investments.
- **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, `number`, required: false): Please enter the year for which you are filling this tax return.
- **Gross Income** (`gross_income`, `number`, required: false): Enter your total gross income for the selected tax year.
- **Tax Deductions** (`tax_deductions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable income sources from the given options: Income from rent, Income from dividend, Interest on savings, Other income. 
- **Tax Payer's Name** (`tax_payers_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your full name as it appears on your government documents.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your complete address.
- **PAN Number** (`pan_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your Permanent Account Number (PAN) as it appears on your government documents.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
- **Aadhaar Number** (`aadhaar_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your unique Aadhaar number.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
