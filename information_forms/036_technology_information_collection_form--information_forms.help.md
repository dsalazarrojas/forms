# technology_information_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The technology_information_collection_form is designed to collect detailed information about technologies, including their types, locations, and installation dates and times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the technology type from the list of options provided.
2. Enter the email address associated with the technology.
3. Enter the phone number associated with the technology.
4. Choose the location where the technology is installed (on-site, remote, or hybrid).
5. Enter the date and time the technology was installed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Technology** (text, required): Enter the name or description of the technology being reported.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the email address associated with the technology. This will help us keep you informed about any updates or issues related to this technology.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number associated with the technology. This will help us contact you if needed.
* **Technology Type** (select multiple, required): Select all that apply from the list of options (computer, laptop, software, hardware).
* **Location** (select one, required): Choose where the technology is installed (on-site, remote, hybrid).
* **Date Installed** (date, required): Enter the date the technology was installed.
* **Time Installed** (time, required): Enter the time the technology was installed.
* **Technology Description** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the technology, if desired.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the technology.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, required): Enter the tool or equipment number associated with the technology.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- Double-check your selections and entries before submitting.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your supervisor or administrator for clarification.
