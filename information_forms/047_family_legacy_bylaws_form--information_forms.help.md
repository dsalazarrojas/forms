# Family Legacy Bylaws Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect and organize information about family legacy and bylaws, allowing you to gather and review essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about the form in the "Form Information" field.
2. List the shared values of your family or organization in the "Shared Values" field.
3. Describe your decision-making structures and processes in the "Decision-Making Structures" field.
4. Outline your review processes for legacy and bylaws in the "Review Processes" field.
5. Add any additional information about legacy and bylaws in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Form Information**
Form Information (field 1): 
*This field is a text-based description of the form's context.*

### **Shared Values**
Shared Values (field 2): 
*This field is for listing the core values and principles that guide your family's or organization's legacy and bylaws.*

### **Decision-Making Structures**
Decision-Making Structures (field 3): 
*This field is for describing the structures and processes used to make decisions about legacy and bylaws.*

### **Review Processes**
Review Processes (field 4): 
*This field is for documenting the processes used to review and update legacy and bylaws.*

### **Additional Info**
Additional Info (field 5): 
*This field is for adding any additional details or notes about legacy and bylaws.*
