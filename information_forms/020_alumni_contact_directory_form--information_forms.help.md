# Alumni Contact Directory Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about alumni contacts for the purpose of creating a directory that showcases their personal and professional details. The directory will be available to the community, and the information collected will help alumni stay connected and network with each other.

### Note: Before completing this form, please ensure you are not duplicating an existing entry in the directory. If you are unsure, please contact us to check.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields (indicated by a red asterisk \*).
* Select the relevant options for your degree earned and preferred communication method.
* Choose whether you want to be included in the online directory.
* Review the additional sections (Professional Information and Personal Details) for optional fields that you may find relevant to complete.
* Click submit to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email Address**: Your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number (optional).
* **Graduation Year**: The year you graduated.
* **Degree Earned**: Select your degree type (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, PhD, etc.).
* **Field of Study**: Your major or focus area.
* **City of Residence**: The city where you currently live.
* **State of Residence**: The state or province where you currently live.
* **Country of Residence**: The country where you currently live (if applicable).
* **Current Job Title**: Your current position or title.
* **Employer or Organization**: Where you work.
* **Industry or Sector**: The field of your work.
* **Company Location**: Where your company is located.
* **Professional Interests or Expertise**: Areas you specialize in.
* **Hobbies and Interests**: What you enjoy doing in your free time.
* **Family Status**: Information about your family (married, children, etc.).
* **Other Alumni Relatives**: Family members who also attended the institution.
* **Include in Online Directory**: Choose whether you want to be included in the online directory.
* **Preferred Communication Method**: How you would like to be contacted (email, phone, mail, etc.).
* **Receive Event Notifications**: Choose whether you want to receive notifications about events.
* **Additional Notes**: Any other information you'd like to share.

**Tips**

* Make sure to keep your information up-to-date and accurate to ensure you are easily accessible to others.
* You can add any additional fields from the Personal Details section if you wish to provide more information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
