# Basketball Tryout Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Basketball Tryout Information Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect necessary information from players who wish to participate in tryouts. The form helps us gather detailed data for each player, including their contact information, athletic profile, and medical history. By filling out this form, players can help us understand their skills, experiences, and requirements better.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out the basic information section, which includes your full name, email address, school year, and emergency contact phone number.
2. Move on to the athletic profile section, where you will select your primary and secondary positions, as well as list any previous team or club experience.
3. Provide your player height and weight information, including your height in centimeters and your weight in kilograms.
4. In the health and safety section, answer whether you have any asthma or respiratory issues and sign the parent or guardian consent for participation.
5. Finally, confirm the date you submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Full Name** (`player_name`, text, required): Enter your full name, first and last.
* **Player Email Address** (`player_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to help us get in touch with you.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, text, required): Enter your current school year.
* **Primary Position** (`primary_position`, select_one, required): Choose your primary position, such as Point Guard, Shooting Guard, Small Forward, Power Forward, or Center.
* **Secondary Position** (`secondary_position`, select_one, required): Choose your secondary position, such as Guard, Forward, Center, or None.
* **Previous Team or Club Experience** (`previous_experience`, text, optional): List any previous team or club experience you have.
* **Player Height (cm)** (`height_cm`, number, required): Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Player Weight (kg)** (`weight_kg`, number, optional): Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Preferred Jersey Size** (`jersey_size`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred jersey size, such as Adult S, Adult M, Adult L, Adult XL, Youth L, or Youth XL.
* **Does the player have asthma or respiratory issues?** (`asthma_or_respiratory`, select_one, required): Answer whether you have any asthma or respiratory issues.
* **Parent or Guardian Consent for Participation** (`parent_consent_signed`, select_one, required): Sign to confirm that your parent or guardian has given consent for your participation.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, required): Enter the phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Date of Submission** (`date_submitted`, date, required): Confirm the date you submit this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your form and get in touch with you if we need more information.
