# Personal Styling And Fitting Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal styling and fitting information from our clients. It will help us better understand their preferences, height, and other details to provide them with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Client Name** in the designated field. This is the name of the person who will be getting the service.
2. Provide your **Email** address in the correct format (e.g., john.doe@example.com). This is where we will contact you for further instructions or updates.
3. Enter your **Contact Number** in the designated field. This is the best way for us to get in touch with you quickly.
4. Enter your **Height** in numerical format (e.g., 5'8"). This will help us determine the best fitting options for you.
5. If you have any specific **Measurements** that you'd like to provide, enter them here. This can include any additional details that will help us serve you better.
6. Let us know your **Favorite Color**. This is not essential but will help us get a better understanding of your style preferences.
7. Choose a date and time for your **Preferred Fitting Date**. This is when we will schedule your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (Required): Please enter the full name of the person receiving the service.
* **Email** (Optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and instructions about your appointment.
* **Contact Number** (Optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you directly.
* **Height** (Required): Enter your height in numerical format (e.g., 5'8").
* **Measurements** (Optional): Provide any additional measurements or details that will help us serve you better.
* **Favorite Color** (Optional): This is not essential, but it will help us get a better understanding of your style preferences.
* **Preferred Fitting Date** (Required): Choose a date and time for your appointment. Please ensure this date is correct to avoid any conflicts.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out correctly to avoid any delays or errors in processing your information.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to reach out to our team for clarification or assistance.
* Only submit this form once to avoid any duplicate entries.
