# office_inventory_information_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "office_inventory_information_record_form" is used to record office inventory information for accurate tracking and management of office items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the category of the form from the dropdown menu. You can choose between "Information Forms" or "Other Forms".
2. Enter a brief description of the item being recorded.
3. Enter the date the item was submitted or received.
4. Enter the date the item was received.
5. Enter the item's description.
6. Enter the quantity of the item.
7. Enter the value of the item.
8. Enter the serial number of the item.
9. Enter the location of the item.
10. Enter any remarks about the item.
11. Enter the tool assigned to the item (if applicable).
12. Enter a unique form ID, which is a numerical value that identifies the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category**: Select the category of the form from the dropdown menu.
  - This field is used to classify the form into different categories for easier searching and filtering.
  - You can choose between "Information Forms" or "Other Forms".
- **Description**: Enter a brief description of the item being recorded.
  - This field is used to provide a short summary of the item.
  - Keep it concise and relevant to the item being recorded.
- **Date Submitted**: Enter the date the item was submitted.
  - This field is used to record the date the item was submitted or received.
- **Date Received**: Enter the date the item was received.
  - This field is used to record the date the item was received.
- **Item Description**: Enter the item's description.
  - This field is used to provide a detailed description of the item.
  - Enter as much detail as possible about the item being recorded.
- **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the item.
  - This field is used to record the number of items being recorded.
- **Value**: Enter the value of the item.
  - This field is used to record the monetary value of the item.
- **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the item.
  - This field is used to record the serial number of the item if applicable.
- **Location**: Enter the location of the item.
  - This field is used to record the location where the item is stored or kept.
- **Remarks**: Enter any remarks about the item.
  - This field is used to add any additional comments or notes about the item.
- **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to the item (if applicable).
  - This field is used to record the tool assigned to the item, if any.
- **Form ID**: Enter a unique form ID, which is a numerical value that identifies the form.
  - This field is used to uniquely identify the form being filled out.
