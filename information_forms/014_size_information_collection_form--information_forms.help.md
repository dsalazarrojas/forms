# Size Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Size Information Collection Form is designed to gather various size measurements from users for further processing and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand what each field represents.
2. Enter your size measurements according to your actual measurements.
3. Ensure you fill out as many or as few fields as you want, as all fields are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Size Information** (`size_information_collection_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your size preferences or any specific size information you'd like to share.
* **Chest Measurement** (`chest_measurement`, number, optional): Measure around the fullest part of your chest.
* **Waist Measurement** (`waist_measurement`, number, optional): Measure around the narrowest part of your natural waistline.
* **Hip Measurement** (`hip_measurement`, number, optional): Measure around the widest part of your hips.
* **Jacket Length** (`jacket_length`, number, optional): Measure from the base of the neck, over the shoulder, down the arm, and to the tip of the hand.
* **Shirt Length** (`shirt_length`, number, optional): Measure from the base of the neck, over the shoulder, down the back, and to the bottom of the hem.
* **Inseam** (`inseam`, number, optional): Measure from the top of the pant leg, down to the sole of the foot.
* **Pants Length** (`pants_length`, number, optional): Measure from the top of the pant leg, down to the sole of the foot.
* **Sleeve Length** (`sleeve_length`, number, optional): Measure from the base of the neck, over the shoulder, down the arm, and to the tip of the hand.
* **Pant Inseam** (`pant_inseam`, number, optional): Measure from the top of the pant leg, down to the sole of the foot.
* **Jacket Sleeve Length** (`jacket_sleeve_length`, number, optional): Measure from the base of the neck, over the shoulder, down the arm, and to the tip of the hand.

## Tips
* Be accurate when taking your measurements to ensure accurate results.
* If you're unsure about a measurement, you can leave it blank or enter a range (e.g., "30-32" for a chest measurement).
* Remember to fill out as many or as few fields as you want, as all fields are optional.
