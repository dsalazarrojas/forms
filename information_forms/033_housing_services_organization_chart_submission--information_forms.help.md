# Housing Services Organization Chart Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for the Housing Services organization chart submission. The collected data will be used to update the organization chart, ensuring its accuracy and consistency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name.
2. Enter the Department Name.
3. Provide a brief description of the Department Details.
4. Enter the Department Headquarters (if applicable).
5. Indicate whether staff is present or not.
6. If staff is present, enter their Staff Name(s).
7. Enter their Job Title(s).
8. Enter their Email address(es).
9. Enter their Phone number(s).
10. Enter their Job Title (for department head).
11. Enter the Department Head's Email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as you wish to be displayed in the organization chart.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your department.
* **Department Details** (`department_details`, note, required: false): Provide a brief description of your department, e.g., "Housing and Real Estate".
* **Department Headquarters** (`department_headquarters`, text, required: false): Enter the location of your department's headquarters (if applicable).
* **Staff** (`staff`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether staff is present in your department. Select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name(s) of your staff member(s).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title as it appears in the organization chart.
* **Staff Email** (`staff_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address(es) of your staff member(s).
* **Staff Phone** (`staff_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number(s) of your staff member(s).
* **Job Title** (`staff_job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the job title of your staff member(s).
* **Staff Hours** (`staff_hours`, time, required: false): Enter the staff hours of operation (if applicable).
* **Department Head** (`department_head`, text, required: false): Enter your job title as it appears in the organization chart.
* **Department Head Email** (`department_head_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of your department head.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
