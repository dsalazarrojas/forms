# Sustainable Ingredient Development Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register sustainable ingredients, gathering information about the product, its development status, and contact details for follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product name and description.
2. Select the relevant tags and category for the product.
3. Choose the country of origin for the product.
4. Check the relevant certifications and certifying bodies for the product.
5. Enter your contact name, email, phone number, and company details.
6. Set the submission date and time.
7. Select the submission status as Active or Inactive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **product_name** (text): Enter the name of the sustainable ingredient being registered.
* **product_description** (text): Provide a brief description of the product.
* **product_tags** (select multiple): Select the relevant tags for the product.
* **product_category** (select one): Choose the category for the product.
* **product_country_of_origin** (select one): Select the country of origin for the product.
* **product_certifications** (select multiple): Check the relevant certifications for the product.
* **product_certification_ids** (select multiple): Select the relevant certifying bodies for the product.
* **contact_name** (text): Enter your name and title.
* **contact_email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **contact_number** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **contact_company** (text): Enter your company name.
* **submission_date** (date): Enter the submission date and time.
* **submission_time** (time): Enter the submission time.
* **submission_status** (select one): Select the submission status as Active or Inactive.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete registration.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for assistance.
* This form is for registration purposes only, and submissions will be reviewed and verified before approval.
