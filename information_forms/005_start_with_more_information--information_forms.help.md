# Start With More Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather more information from the user in a structured way, allowing the user to provide details and answers to specific questions. The form is likely used in a customer service or support context, where the goal is to understand the user's needs and preferences in order to provide the best possible service or solution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you have all the necessary information and answers.
2. Fill out each field with the requested information, making sure to choose the correct options from the select fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### get the facts (1)
* **get the facts** (`start_with_more_information_form`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the situation or problem you are experiencing.

### more details (2)
* **more details** (`start_with_more_information_form_details`, text, required/optional): Please provide any additional information that you think is relevant to the situation.

### evaluate details (3)
* **evaluate details** (`start_with_more_information_form_evaluation`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you think the details provided are accurate or not.

### offer services (4)
* **offer services** (`start_with_more_information_form_services`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose which services you are interested in or would like to receive.

### date (5)
* **date** (`start_with_more_information_form_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to receive the service or solution.

### time (6)
* **time** (`start_with_more_information_form_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you would like to receive the service or solution.

### note (7)
* **note** (`start_with_more_information_form_note`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your request.

### email (8)
* **email** (`start_with_more_information_form_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.

### phone (9)
* **phone** (`start_with_more_information_form_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.

### services (10)
* **services** (`start_with_more_information_form_services`, select_one, required/optional): Choose which services you are interested in or would like to receive.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Use the correct format for date and time fields (MM/DD/YYYY for dates, HH:MM:SS for times).
- Double-check your email address for accuracy.
- If you have any additional information, please use the note field to provide it.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, don't hesitate to reach out.
