This guide provides a detailed explanation of the Resident Information Form, its purpose, and how to complete it step-by-step. The form is used to collect essential information about residents, and its fields are designed to collect accurate and comprehensive data. 

When completing this form, ensure that all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the "required" label. If you're unsure about a particular field or have questions, please refer to the field explanations below.

Please note that this guide will cover each field in the order they appear in the form.

**Form Purpose:** 
The Resident Information Form is a crucial document used to gather and store resident data. It contains personal details, contact information, and status updates.

**How to Complete This Form:**

1. Start by filling out the form title at the top of the page.
2. Enter the resident's name in the "Resident Name" field. This is a required field.
3. If necessary, enter the resident's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field. This field is not required.
4. Enter the resident's contact number in the "Contact Number" field. This is a required field.
5. Fill out the "Address 1" and "Address 2" fields with the resident's address. "Address 2" is required.
6. Choose the resident's status from the "Resident Status" dropdown menu.
7. Provide emergency contact information for the resident. This includes their name, phone number, relationship, and address.
8. Confirm the emergency contact's status as "Immediate," "Available," or "Unavailable."
9. Sign the "Resident Signature" field (this is optional, but recommended for verification).
10. Select the "Assigned Tool" that the resident is using.
11. Enter a unique "Form ID" and "Version" number for the form.
12. Choose the "Category" of the form.
13. Enter a brief description of the form in the "Description" field. This is required.
14. Enter the "Title" of the form.
15. Enter the name of the person who created the form.
16. Enter the name of the person who modified the form (if applicable).
17. Enter the date the form was created and modified (if applicable).
18. If the form is published, enter the date it was published.
19. Choose the "Status" of the form as "Published" or "Unpublished." This is a required field.

**Field-by-Field Explanation:**

* **Resident Name** (Text, Required): Enter the resident's name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter the resident's date of birth (if known).
* **Contact Number** (Text, Required): Enter the resident's contact number.
* **Address 1** (Text, Optional): Enter the resident's address.
* **Address 2** (Text, Required): Enter the resident's secondary address.
* **Resident Status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the resident's status: Active, Inactive, Retired, or Deceased.
* **Emergency Contact** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Number** (Text, Required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Relationship** (Text, Optional): Describe the relationship between the emergency contact and the resident.
* **Emergency Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the emergency contact's status: Immediate, Available, or Unavailable.
* **Resident Signature** (Note, Optional): Sign the form for verification purposes.
* **Assigned Tool** (Select One, Optional): Choose the tool assigned to the resident.
* **Form ID** (Number, Required): Enter a unique ID for the form.
* **Version** (Number, Required): Enter the version number of the form.
* **Category** (Select One, Optional): Choose the category of the form: Information Forms or Other.
* **Description** (Text, Required): Enter a brief description of the form.
* **Title** (Text, Required): Enter the title of the form.
* **Created by** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Modified by** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person who modified the form (if applicable).
* **Created** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the form was created.
* **Modified** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the form was modified (if applicable).
* **Published** (Number, Optional): Enter the date the form was published.
* **Status** (Select One, Required): Choose the status of the form: Published or Unpublished.
* **Note** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes about the form.
