# Respondent Identification Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Respondent Identification Questionnaire is a form used to collect information about a study, including its status, the date of consent, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the status of the study from the 'study_status' dropdown menu.
2. Enter the date of consent using the calendar icon.
3. Fill out the contact information for the study coordinator or researcher.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments about the study.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **study_info** (Select Multiple, required): Select 'Yes' if the study has been reviewed and approved by the Institutional Review Board (IRB), or 'No' if it has not. 
* **study_id** (Number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the study.
* **respondent_id** (Number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the respondent who filled out the form.
* **study_name** (Text, optional): Enter a brief name for the study.
* **start_date** (Date, optional): Enter the start date of the study.
* **end_date** (Date, optional): Enter the end date of the study.
* **contact_name** (Text, optional): Enter the name of the study coordinator or researcher.
* **contact_email** (Email, optional): Enter the email address of the study coordinator or researcher.
* **contact_phone** (Text, optional): Enter the phone number of the study coordinator or researcher.
* **study_status** (Select One, optional): Select 'Active' if the study is currently active, or 'Inactive' if it is not.
* **consent_given** (Select One, optional): Select 'Yes' if consent was given, or 'No' if it was not.
* **consent_date** (Date, optional): Enter the date consent was given.
* **consent_note** (Text, optional): Enter any notes about the consent process.
* **study_description** (Text, optional): Enter a brief description of the study.
* **study_notes** (Text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the study.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or the study coordinator for clarification.
* This form is not intended for use as a template for future studies. If you need to set up a new study, please fill out the 'Respondent Identification Questionnaire' form for that new study.
