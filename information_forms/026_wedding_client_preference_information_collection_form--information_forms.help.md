# Wedding Client Preference Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about the client's preferences for their wedding planning process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information about your wedding.
2. For select fields, choose the options that best fit your preferences.
3. Use the "wedding_client_notes" field to add any additional comments or requests.
4. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Planning Details**: This is a text field where you can describe your wedding details such as date, time, location, and theme.
* **Wedding Client Name**: Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on your wedding documents.
* **Wedding Client Email**: Enter your contact email address so that we can get in touch with you.
* **Wedding Client Phone**: Enter your contact phone number so that we can reach you.
* **Wedding Planner**: If you have already selected a wedding planner, enter their name here.
* **Wedding Vendor**: If you have already selected a wedding vendor, enter their name here.
* **Wedding Guest Count**: Enter the estimated number of guests you plan to invite.
* **Wedding Client Diet**: Select the dietary restrictions you have as a client.
* **Wedding Client Diet Other**: If you have any other dietary restrictions not listed, enter them here.
* **Wedding Client Special Requests**: If you have any special requests for your wedding, enter them here.
* **Wedding Client Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your wedding.
* **Wedding Planner Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your wedding planner.
* **Wedding Vendor Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your wedding vendor.
* **Wedding Dietary Requirements**: Enter any specific dietary requirements you have.
* **Wedding Client Wants**: If there are any things you want to happen at your wedding, enter them here.
* **Wedding Client Doesn't Wants**: If there are any things you don't want to happen at your wedding, enter them here.
* **Wedding Planner Wants**: If there are any things your wedding planner should know about your wedding, enter them here.
* **Wedding Vendor Wants**: If there are any things your wedding vendor should know about your wedding, enter them here.
* **Wedding Client Has Children**: Select whether or not you have children.
* **Wedding Client Is Venue Owner**: Select whether or not you own the venue for your wedding.
* **Wedding Client Wedding Theme**: Select the theme of your wedding from the list of options.
* **Wedding Client Budget Range**: Select the estimated budget range for your wedding.
* **Wedding Client Has Pet**: Select whether or not you have pets.
* **Wedding Planner Is Venue Owner**: Select whether or not your wedding planner owns the venue for your wedding.
* **Wedding Vendor Is Venue Owner**: Select whether or not your wedding vendor owns the venue for your wedding.
* **Wedding Client Wedding Theme**: Select the theme of your wedding from the list of options.
* **Wedding Client Budget Range**: Select the estimated budget range for your wedding.
* **Wedding Client Has Pet**: Select whether or not you have pets.
