# Racquetball Partner Finder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to find potential racquetball partners for a specific time slot. Please fill out the form with your preferences and we will use it to match you with a suitable partner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Partner Finder Form**: Type in the title of your racquetball session. This will help us identify your session and match you with a partner.
2. **Location**: Enter the location where you'd like to play racquetball. This could be a court, facility, or specific area.
3. **Time**: Select the time you'd like to play.
4. **Days Available**: Check the days you are available to play.
5. **Earliest Start Time**: Enter the earliest time you're willing to start playing.
6. **Latest End Time**: Enter the latest time you're willing to end your session.
7. **Match Duration**: Select the duration of your racquetball session.
8. **Skill Level**: Select your skill level to ensure we match you with a suitable partner.
9. **Max Partners**: Select the maximum number of partners you'd like to play with.
10. **Message**: If you'd like to include any specific preferences or instructions for your session, please add them here.
