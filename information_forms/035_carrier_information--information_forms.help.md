# Carrier Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about a carrier company, including details about their company, physical address, and operational profile. The information collected will be used for tax reporting and other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. Please note that some fields are required and others are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, `required`): Enter the name of your carrier company.
* **DOT Number** (`dot_number`, text, `required`): Enter your carrier company's DOT number.
* **MC Number** (`mc_number`, text, `required`): Enter your carrier company's MC number.
* **Federal Tax ID (EIN)** (`federal_id`, text, `required`): Enter your carrier company's Federal Tax ID (EIN) number. This is required for tax reporting purposes.
* **Physical Street Address** (`office_address`, text, `required`): Enter the physical street address of your carrier company's office.
* **City, State, Zip** (`office_city_state_zip`, text, `required`): Enter the city, state, and zip code of your carrier company's office.
* **Office Phone Number** (`office_phone`, text, `required`): Enter the phone number of your carrier company's office.
* **Total Power Units** (`equipment_count`, number, `required`): Enter the total number of power units (trucks) you have.
* **Total Trailers** (`trailer_count`, number, `required`): Enter the total number of trailers you have.
* **Main Equipment** (`equipment_types`, select multiple, `required`): Select all of the main types of equipment you have (Dry Van, Reefer, Flatbed, Intermodal, LTL).
* **Preferred Lanes or Corridors** (`corridor_preference`, text, `required`: Enter the preferred lanes or corridors you operate on.
* **Safety Contact Name** (`safety_contact_name`, text, `optional`): Enter the name of the person responsible for safety and compliance at your carrier company.
* **Safety Contact Phone** (`safety_contact_phone`, text, `optional`): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for safety and compliance at your carrier company.
* **Date of Last Safety Inspection** (`inspection_status`, date, `optional`): Enter the date of your last safety inspection.
* **I Confirm the Company Information is Current** (`verification_acknowledgment`, select one, `required`): Select 'True' to confirm that the company information is current.
* **Person Verifying Information** (`verified_by_name`, text, `required`): Enter the name of the person verifying the information (this will serve as a digital signature).
