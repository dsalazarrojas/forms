# Website Gather Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information from website visitors. It helps us understand who they are, how to contact them, and why they're visiting our site.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and submit the form. Each field has a label and a description to guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents. This is an optional field, but it helps us get a better understanding of who you are.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents. This field is also optional, but it's useful for us to know your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you. Please use the format `username@domain.com`. This field is required to send you updates and information about our website.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you. This field is optional, but it's helpful for us to get in touch with you if needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to check your email address is formatted correctly before submitting the form.
* If you're experiencing any issues with the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
