# Customer Information Sheet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Information Sheet Form is designed to collect essential details about a customer, including their contact information, job title, department, job function, employment status, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the required information before starting.
* If a field is not required, feel free to skip it or leave it blank.
* Fill in the customer's name, email address, phone number, and company details accurately.
* Select the customer's job title, department, job function, and employment status from the available options.
* Indicate whether the customer has a manager and whether they are directly reportable.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info Sheet Form 1**: This field is a brief description of the customer's information sheet form.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's full name as it appears on their business card or contact information.
* **Customer Email**: Provide the customer's email address where they can be reached.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's primary phone number.
* **Customer Company**: Enter the customer's company name.
* **Customer Job Title**: Select the customer's job title or role within their organization.
* **Customer Department**: Select the customer's department or division within their company.
* **Customer Job Function**: Select the customer's job function or responsibility.
* **Customer Manager**: Indicate whether the customer has a manager and select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
* **Customer Direct Report**: Select all the departments or people who directly report to the customer (if applicable).
* **Customer Employed**: Indicate the customer's employment status by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' (e.g., employed, not employed).
* **Customer Employment Type**: Select the customer's employment type (e.g., 'Yes', 'No', other options may vary).

Note: Please review the form carefully and provide accurate information to ensure a complete and accurate customer profile.
