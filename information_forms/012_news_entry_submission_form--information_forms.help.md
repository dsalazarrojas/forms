# News Entry Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow contributors to submit news entries for publication. The form collects essential information about the contributor, the news story, and any attachments. It is essential to fill this form accurately to ensure that the submitted news entry meets the required standards for publication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before filling out the form.
2. Fill in your name as the contributor.
3. Enter the title of the news story.
4. Provide a brief description of the news story.
5. If applicable, select a category for the news story (e.g., Sports, Politics, or Entertainment).
6. Upload any attachments related to the news story (e.g., images, videos, or documents).
7. Enter the URL of the news story if it is available online.
8. Review and submit the form to complete the submission process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contributor Name** (`contributor_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the contributor of the news story.
* **Story Title** (`story_title`, text, required): Enter a title that accurately represents the news story.
* **Contributor Phone** (`contributor_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Story Description** (`story_description`, text, required): Provide a brief and accurate description of the news story.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select a category for the news story from the options provided (e.g., Sports, Politics, or Entertainment).
* **Attachment Upload** (`attachment_upload`, note, required): Attach any relevant files or documents to the news story.
* **Attachment File** (`attachment_file`, text, optional): Enter the URL of any attached files or documents.
* **Story URL** (`story_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the news story if it is available online.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* Use standard formatting and style guidelines for submitting news entries.
* Attach relevant and high-quality files to support the news story.
* Proofread the submission carefully before submitting it for review.
