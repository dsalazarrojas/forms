## Household Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Household Information Form is designed to collect information about a household, including its address and its members.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **house_number** field with the number of the house or building you are providing information about.
2. Enter the **street_address** field with the street address of the house or building.
3. Enter the **city** field with the city of the house or building.
4. Enter the **state** field with the state of the house or building.
5. Enter the **zip_code** field with the zip code (postal code) of the house or building.
6. Enter the **country** field with the country of the house or building.
7. In the **household_members** field, select all members of the household from the list of options provided.
8. Enter the **email** field with the email address of the household or a contact person.
9. Enter the **phone** field with the phone number of the household or a contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **house_number**: Enter the number of the house or building you are providing information about.
* **street_address**: Enter the street address of the house or building.
* **city**: Enter the city of the house or building.
* **state**: Enter the state of the house or building.
* **zip_code**: Enter the zip code (postal code) of the house or building.
* **country**: Enter the country of the house or building.
* **household_members**: Select all members of the household from the list of options provided.
* **email**: Enter the email address of the household or a contact person.
* **phone**: Enter the phone number of the household or a contact person.
