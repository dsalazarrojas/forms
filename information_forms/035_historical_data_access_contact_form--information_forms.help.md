# Historical Data Access Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information from users who are requesting access to historical data. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant category for your historical data access request from the options provided.
2. Enter a brief description of the data you are requesting access to.
3. Enter the email address you would like to use for communication regarding your request.
4. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your historical data access request.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more categories that describe the nature of your request. This will help the data owner understand the scope of your request.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the data you are requesting access to.
* **Form Requested Data** (`form_requested_data`, text, optional): Enter any additional details or specific data you need access to.
* **Email** (`email_input`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about your request.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone about your request.
* **Notes** (`form_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about your request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have not already submitted this form previously to avoid duplicate requests.
* If you need help with the form, please do not hesitate to contact the data owner directly.
* If you have any concerns or questions about data access, please reach out to the data owner for clarification.
