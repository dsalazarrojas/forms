# Basic Customer Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Basic Customer Information Form is designed to collect essential details from customers for business purposes. This form serves as a standard template for collecting customer information, ensuring accuracy and consistency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "First name" and "Last name" fields with your given names.
2. If applicable, provide your "Middle name" in the designated field.
3. Enter your "Date of birth" in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Provide your "Email address" and "Phone number" for contact purposes.
5. Enter your "Street address" and "City" and "State/Province" and "ZIP/Postal code" for address details.
6. Choose your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, Text Message, or No Contact) and best time to contact (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Weekends).
7. If the billing address is the same as the shipping address, select "True" from the "Is billing address same as shipping?" field. Otherwise, enter your billing address in the designated field.
8. Select your preferred payment method from the available options (Credit Card, Debit Card, Check, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
9. If applicable, provide your company name and business type.
10. Let us know how you heard about us (Search Engine, Social Media, Referral, or Other).
11. If necessary, enter any special requests or notes in the "Special requests or notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your given first name.
* **Last name** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter your given last name.
* **Middle name (optional)** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter your middle name, if applicable.
* **Date of birth** (`5`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email address** (`6`, `email`, required): Provide your valid email address.
* **Phone number** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Street address** (`9`, `text`, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter your city.
* **State/Province** (`11`, `text`, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP/Postal code** (`12`, `text`, required): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Country** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter your country, if applicable.
* **Preferred contact method** (`15`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, Text Message, or Mail).
* **Best time to contact** (`16`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the best time to contact you (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Weekends).
* **Is billing address same as shipping?** (`18`, `select_one`, optional): If the billing address is the same as the shipping address, select "True".
* **Billing address (if different)** (`19`, `text`, optional): Enter the billing address, if different.
* **Preferred payment method** (`20`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, Check, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
* **Company name (if applicable)** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter your company name, if applicable.
* **Type of business (if applicable)** (`23`, `text`, optional): Enter your business type, if applicable.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`24`, `select_one`, optional): Let us know how you heard about us (Search Engine, Social Media, Referral, or Other).
* **Special requests or notes** (`25`, `text`, optional): Enter any special requests or notes.
