# sacrament_information_form_for_students - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a student's sacraments, which are significant events in a person's life, such as baptism, confirmation, marriage, and natural death. The form aims to gather details about these events, including the date, place, and officiant involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. If applicable, provide your date of birth.
3. Answer if you are a member of the church.
4. If applicable, provide the details of your baptism event, including the date, place, and officiant.
5. If applicable, provide the details of your confirmation event, including the date, place, and officiant.
6. If applicable, provide the details of your marriage event, including the date, place, and officiant.
7. If applicable, provide the details of your natural death event, including the date, place, and officiant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If you have a date of birth, please enter it in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 12/25/1990).
* **Member of the church** (`church_member`, select_multiple, required): Answer "yes" or "no" to indicate whether you are a member of the church.
* **Baptism date** (`baptism_date`, date, optional): If you have been baptized, enter the date in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 12/25/1990).
* **Baptism place** (`baptism_place`, text, optional): Enter the place where you were baptized.
* **Baptism officiant** (`baptism_officiant`, text, optional): Enter the person who performed your baptism.
* **Confirmation date** (`confirmation_date`, date, optional): If you have been confirmed, enter the date in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 12/25/1990).
* **Confirmation place** (`confirmation_place`, text, optional): Enter the place where you were confirmed.
* **Confirmation officiant** (`confirmation_officiant`, text, optional): Enter the person who performed your confirmation.
* **Marriage date** (`marriage_date`, date, optional): If you are married, enter the date in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 12/25/1990).
* **Marriage place** (`marriage_place`, text, optional): Enter the place where you got married.
* **Marriage officiant** (`marriage_officiant`, text, optional): Enter the person who performed your marriage.
* **Natural death date** (`natural_death_date`, date, optional): If you have died naturally, enter the date in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 12/25/1990).
* **Natural death place** (`natural_death_place`, text, optional): Enter the place where you died naturally.
* **Natural death officiant** (`natural_death_officiant`, text, optional): Enter the person who reported your natural death.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the events, you can leave the corresponding fields blank.
* If you have any additional information about the events, please provide it in the text fields.
