# Artist Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Information Form is used to collect information about artists participating in events. This form helps event organizers and staff get in touch with artists and plan future events more efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Provide a clear and accurate answer to each field.
3. Make sure your email address is correct.
4. Choose your preferred contact method for future events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your complete legal name.
* **Artist Name or Pseudonym**: How you are known professionally.
* **Email Address**: Your primary contact email for future event opportunities.
* **Phone Number**: Optional contact number for future events.
* **Year of First Participation**: The year you first participated in an event.
* **Years of Participation**: List the years you have participated in events.
* **Total Number of Events Attended**: The number of times you have participated in events.
* **Artist Biography**: A 100-200 word professional biography.
* **Artistic Focus or Discipline**: Your primary area of artistic work (e.g., Painting, Sculpture, Photography).
* **Years Active as an Artist**: How long you have been creating art.
* **Title of Your Artwork**: The title of your primary artwork or current project.
* **Artwork Description**: A brief description of your artwork.
* **Medium or Materials Used**: The materials used to create your artwork.
* **Artwork Dimensions**: The size or scale of your artwork.
* **Portrait Photo**: A high-resolution photo of yourself.
* **Artwork Photo**: A clear photo of your artwork.
* **Additional Artwork Images**: Additional photos or documentation of your artwork.
* **Additional Comments or Notes**: Any other relevant information about yourself or your artwork.
* **Permission to Contact You**: Choose how you would like to be contacted for future events (e.g., Yes, contact me, No, do not contact, Email only, Phone only).
