# Life Design Detailed Information - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information from users who are participating in a life design project. It will be used to assess individual preferences and requirements for a specific program or service. This form is not required for every user, and it's intended for those who wish to provide additional information about themselves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections below.
2. For each section, answer the questions based on your personal assessment and preferences.
3. Select all relevant options that apply to you.
4. Once you've completed all sections, review your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your situation.
5. Submit the form to continue with the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Assessment** (Personal Assessment, note, optional): This section allows you to provide a brief personal assessment or introduction about yourself. Feel free to write a few sentences to give us a better understanding of who you are and what you're looking for.
* **Requirements For Participating** (Requirements For Participating, select multiple, optional): Please select all requirements that you think are necessary for you to participate in the program or service. This could include requirements such as language proficiency, specific software or hardware needs, or any other relevant factor.
* **Health Information** (Health Information, select one, optional): This section is to determine whether you have any health information that may impact your participation in the program or service. Please select 'Yes' if you do have health information that may be relevant, and 'No' otherwise.
