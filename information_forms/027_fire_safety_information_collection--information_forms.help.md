# Fire Safety Information Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect fire safety information for a site. It is essential to fill this form accurately and thoroughly to assess the site's fire safety status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Site Details**: Enter the details about the site, including any relevant information about the site's layout, size, and location.
2. **Equipment Status**: Select the current status of the fire safety equipment, such as whether it is functional or not.
3. **Exit Information**: Provide information about the exits, including the location and number of exits.
4. **Inspection Dates**: Record the dates and times of any previous fire safety inspections or audits.
5. **Safety Equipment**: Enter the number of safety equipment, such as fire extinguishers, available on the site.
6. **Safety Measures**: Select the safety measures in place to prevent or mitigate fires, such as fire alarms or sprinkler systems.
7. **Fire Equipment Status**: Choose the status of the fire equipment, such as whether it is functional or not.
8. **Fire Equipment Location**: Select the location of the fire equipment, such as near an exit or in a specific room.
9. **Emergency Contact Information**: Provide emergency contact information, including name and phone number.
10. **Emergency Contact**: Select the emergency contact person's name.
11. **Fire Hydrant**: Select the status of the fire hydrant, such as whether it is available or not.
12. **Exit Lights**: Choose the status of the exit lights, such as whether they are functional or not.
13. **Fire Exits**: Select the number of fire exits available on the site.
14. **Fire Exit Signs**: Choose the status of the fire exit signs, such as whether they are visible or not.
15. **Fire Extinguishers**: Select the number of fire extinguishers available on the site.
16. **Fire Alarm Systems**: Choose the status of the fire alarm system, such as whether it is functional or not.
17. **Fire Alarm Sensors**: Select the type of fire alarm sensors installed on the site.
18. **Fire Sprinkler Systems**: Choose the status of the fire sprinkler system, such as whether it is functional or not.
19. **Fire Safety Certificate**: Select the type of fire safety certificate obtained for the site.
20. **Fire Safety Certification**: Choose the type of fire safety certification obtained by the site.
21. **Fire Safety Training**: Select the type of fire safety training provided to site personnel.
22. **Fire Safety Inspection**: Choose the type of fire safety inspection conducted on the site.
23. **Fire Safety Equipment Inspection**: Select the type of fire safety equipment inspection conducted on the site.
24. **Fire Safety Plan**: Select the type of fire safety plan in place on the site.
