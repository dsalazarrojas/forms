# Community Center Participant Information Form - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Home Address, City, State, ZIP, Primary Phone Number, and Email Address.
2. Choose your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, Text Message, or Mail) and select the program you're registering for (After School Program, Senior Activities, Fitness Classes, etc.).
3. Indicate your preferred schedule (Weekday Mornings, Weekday Afternoons, Weekday Evenings, Weekends, or Flexible).
4. If you've participated in our programs before, select 'True' for the 'Have You Participated Before' question.
5. If you have any transportation needs, select 'True'.
6. Finally, provide any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your participant's legal first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your participant's legal last name.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your participant's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Gender** (select_one, required): Select your participant's gender (Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer to self-describe, or Prefer not to say).
* **Home Address** (text, required): Enter your participant's street address.
* **City, State, ZIP** (text, required): Enter your participant's city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Primary Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your best contact number.
* **Secondary Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter an alternate contact number (if applicable).
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your participant's email address for communications and updates.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (select_one, required): Select how you'd like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Text Message, or Mail).
* **Program Name** (select_one, required): Select the program you're registering for (After School Program, Senior Activities, Fitness Classes, etc.).
* **Preferred Schedule** (select_one, required): Select your preferred schedule (Weekday Mornings, Weekday Afternoons, Weekday Evenings, Weekdays, or Flexible).
* **Have You Participated Before** (select_one, required): If you've participated in our programs before, select 'True'.
* **Previous Programs** (text, optional): List any programs you've participated in before.
* **Goals and Expectations** (text, optional): Share what you hope to gain from this program.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (text, optional): If you have any dietary needs or restrictions, provide details.
* **Transportation Needs** (select_one, required): If you need assistance with transportation, select 'True'.
* **How Did You Hear About This Program** (select_one, optional): Indicate where you learned about us (Website, Social Media, Friend or Family Referral, Community Event, School, Healthcare Provider, or Other).
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Share any other information you'd like to provide about yourself or your participation.
