# Safety Engineered Needle Information Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and record information about safety engineered needles in a medical setting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the necessary information as prompted.
2. Start by entering the details about the needle in question.
3. Then, provide information about the patient, including any medical staff involved.
4. Record tracking information and the current status of the needle.
5. If necessary, enter additional comments or notes to supplement the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Needle Details** (`needle_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the needle, including its type and purpose.
- **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional): Enter information about the patient who is being treated with the needle.
- **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): Enter information about the medical staff member who is tracking the needle.
- **Tracking Info** (`tracking_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any tracking information related to the needle, such as tracking numbers or dates.
- **Medical Staff** (`medical_staff`, text, required/optional): Check if medical staff are present during the procedure.
- **Medical Department** (`medical_department`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable medical departments involved in the procedure (e.g. ER, ICU, etc.).
- **Facility** (`facility`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the medical facility where the procedure is performed.
- **Needle Status** (`needle_status`, select_one, required/optional): Check the current status of the needle (Active or Inactive).
- **Disposal Method** (`disposal_method`, select_one, required/optional): Check if the needle is disposed of properly (Yes or No).
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the needle.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the needle was used or tracked.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the needle was used or tracked.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email of the person who submitted the form.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form.
- **Assigned** (`assigned`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person assigned to the needle.
- **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the needle was used or tracked.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments about the needle.
- **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the medical facility where the procedure is performed.
- **Tracking Info** (`tracking_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional tracking information.
- **Medical Staff Name** (`medical_staff_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the medical staff member who tracked the needle.
- **Tracking Status** (`tracking_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the current tracking status of the needle (Active or Inactive).
- **Facility Contact** (`facility_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact information for the facility where the procedure is performed.
- **Medical Contact** (`medical_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact information for the medical staff member who tracked the needle.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the needle.
- **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
