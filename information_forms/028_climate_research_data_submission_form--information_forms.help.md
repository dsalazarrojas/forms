# Climate Research Data Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Climate Research Data Submission Form is designed to collect climate research data from researchers. This form is used to ensure that the data submitted is accurate, reliable, and meets the required standards for climate research. Submitters are required to provide information about themselves, the study they are conducting, the data they are collecting, and the quality of that data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the researcher section with your name, email address, and institution/organization information.
2. Provide a brief description of your study, focusing on the objective and methodology.
3. Select the primary research focus and study period of your study.
4. Specify the geographic scope of your research and, if applicable, provide specific locations or coordinates.
5. Indicate the types of data you are collecting and the approximate volume of data.
6. Select the data file format you are using.
7. Describe the quality assurance procedures you applied to your data.
8. Confirm the availability of metadata and documentation.
9. Indicate the status of your data availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Researcher Information
* **Researcher Name** (`researcher_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the primary researcher conducting this study.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Institution/Organization** (`institution`, text, required): Enter the name of your institution or organization where you are affiliated with.
* **Department/Faculty** (`department`, text, optional): If applicable, indicate the department or faculty you are part of.

### Study Information
* **Study Title** (`study_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title of your study.
* **Brief Study Description** (`study_description`, text, required): Provide a concise description of your study, including its objective and methodology.
* **Primary Research Focus** (`research_focus`, select_one, required): Select the primary focus of your research (e.g., Climate modeling, Temperature data, etc.).
* **Study Period** (`study_period`, select_one, required): Select the period of your study (e.g., Historical data, Recent data, etc.).
* **Specific Study Locations/Coordinates** (`specific_locations`, text, optional): If applicable, provide specific locations or coordinates for your study.

### Geographic Coverage
* **Geographic scope of research** (`geographic_scope`, select_one, required): Select the geographic scope of your research (e.g., Global, Regional, etc.).
* **Geographic Scope Note** (`geographic_section`, note, optional): If you'd like to add further information about the geographic scope of your research, this is the place to do so.

### Data Details
* **Types of Data Collected** (`data_types`, select_multiple, required): Select all types of data you are collecting (e.g., Temperature readings, Precipitation measurements, etc.).
* **Approximate Data Volume** (`data_volume`, select_one, optional): Estimate the approximate volume of data you are collecting.
* **Data File Format** (`file_format`, text, required): Enter the format of your data files.

### Data Quality and Documentation
* **Quality Assurance Procedures** (`quality_assurance`, text, required): Describe the quality assurance procedures you applied to your data.
* **Metadata and Documentation Availability** (`metadata_availability`, select_one, required): Indicate the availability of metadata and documentation for your study.
* **Data Availability Status** (`data_availability`, select_one, required): Confirm the status of your data availability (e.g., Ready for submission, In preparation, etc.).

### Optional
* If you have any further questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us.
