# Ethical Advisory Panel Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather necessary information from applicants to the Ethical Advisory Panel. Please ensure that you answer the questions accurately and thoroughly to avoid any delays in the evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your organization name in the "What is your organization" field.
2. Describe your work experience in the "What is your work experience" field.
3. Choose your areas of expertise from the list in the "What areas of expertise do you have" field.
4. Select your consent options for the different channels (email, SMS, postal mail) in the corresponding fields.
5. Add any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" field if necessary.
6. Review your answers and submit the form when ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your organization**: Please enter your organization's name to help us understand your affiliation.
* **What is your work experience**: Briefly describe your relevant work experience in the field of ethics or a related field.
* **What areas of expertise do you have**: Select all areas where you have expertise.
	+ **Yes**: You have expertise in this area.
	+ **No**: You do not have expertise in this area.
* **Consent to panel policies**: Choose how you wish to be contacted about panel policies.
	+ **Option 1**: Yes, I consent to being contacted via all channels.
	+ **Option 2**: I consent to being contacted via email and SMS, but not postal mail.
	+ **Option 3**: I only consent to being contacted via email.
* **Consent to share information**: Choose how you wish to share your information with others.
	+ **Option 1**: I consent to sharing my information with all panel members.
	+ **Option 2**: I only consent to sharing my information with panel administrators.
	+ **Option 3**: I do not consent to sharing my information with anyone.
* **Consent to share notes**: Select how you wish to share additional notes.
	+ **Option 1**: I consent to sharing notes with all panel members.
	+ **Option 2**: I only consent to sharing notes with panel administrators.
	+ **Option 3**: I do not consent to sharing notes with anyone.
* **Consent to email notes**: Select how you wish to share email notes.
	+ **Option 1**: I consent to sharing email notes with all panel members.
	+ **Option 2**: I only consent to sharing email notes with panel administrators.
	+ **Option 3**: I do not consent to sharing email notes with anyone.
* **Consent to SMS notes**: Select how you wish to share SMS notes.
	+ **Option 1**: I consent to sharing SMS notes with all panel members.
	+ **Option 2**: I only consent to sharing SMS notes with panel administrators.
	+ **Option 3**: I do not consent to sharing SMS notes with anyone.
* **Consent to postal mail notes**: Select how you wish to share postal mail notes.
	+ **Option 1**: I consent to sharing postal mail notes with all panel members.
	+ **Option 2**: I only consent to sharing postal mail notes with panel administrators.
	+ **Option 3**: I do not consent to sharing postal mail notes with anyone.
* **Additional Notes**: This field is optional and can be used to provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your application.
* **Goals**: This field is optional and can be used to state your goals for joining the Ethical Advisory Panel.
* **Contact information**: Enter your contact information (name, phone number, and email) to ensure we can contact you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive any further instructions or information.
* **Consent**: Choose how you wish to be contacted via email.
	+ **Option 1**: Yes, I consent to being contacted via email.
	+ **Option 2**: I do not consent to being contacted via email.
* **Consent to save and continue**: Select how you wish to save and continue with the application.
	+ **Option 1**: Yes, I consent to saving and continuing with the application.
	+ **Option 2**: I do not consent to saving and continuing with the application.
* **Back**: You can use this button to return to the beginning of the form.
* **Agreement**: This field is optional and can be used to agree or disagree with our terms and conditions.
* **Submit**: Review your answers and submit the form when ready.
* **Back to start**: Use this button to return to the start of the form.
* **Help**: Use this button to access help resources if needed.
* **Save and Continue**: Save your answers and continue with the next step of the application.
* **Cancel**: Use this button to cancel the application and start over.
* **Save**: Use this button to save your answers and continue with the next step of the application.

This help guide is designed to assist you in completing the Ethical Advisory Panel Application Form. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
