# Department Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Department Information Form is designed to collect and store department-specific data for the organization. Fill out this form to provide details about your department's contact information, status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Navigate to the form.
2.  Fill out each field as prompted, using the following as a guide:
    *   **Department Name:** Enter the name of your department.
    *   **Department Manager:** Choose one of the listed department managers from the dropdown menu.
    *   **Department Email:** Enter your department's email address. This will be used for contact purposes.
    *   **Department Phone:** Enter your department's phone number for contact.
    *   **Department Notes:** Provide any additional comments or details about your department.
    *   **Department Directory:** Enter the physical location of your department's office or workspace.
    *   **Department Directory Location:** Enter the physical location of your department's office or workspace (this field is optional and should only be filled out if the department has a physical location).
    *   **Department Status:** Select the status of your department (Active or Inactive).
    *   **Department Description:** Enter a brief description of your department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Department Name (department_name)**: Enter the name of your department.
*   **Department Manager (department_manager)**: Choose one of the listed department managers.
*   **Department Email (department_email)**: Enter your department's email address.
*   **Department Phone (department_phone)**: Enter your department's phone number.
*   **Department Notes (department_notes)**: Provide any additional comments or details about your department.
*   **Department Directory (department_directory)**: Enter the physical location of your department's office or workspace.
*   **Department Directory Location (department_directory_location)**: Enter the physical location of your department's office or workspace (this field is optional and should only be filled out if the department has a physical location).
*   **Department Status (department_status)**: Select the status of your department (Active or Inactive).
*   **Department Description (department_description)**: Enter a brief description of your department.

**Tips:**

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate department data.
*   Double-check your department's information for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you're unsure about any field, consult with your department manager or contact person.
