# Educational Institution Partnership Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to formalize partnerships between our organization and educational institutions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the title of the partnership in the `form_title` field.
2. Provide your name in the `representative_name` field.
3. Specify your role in the `role` field.
4. Enter the name of the department you are representing in the `department_name` field.
5. Add your contact email address in the `contact_email` field.
6. Provide your contact phone number in the `contact_phone` field.
7. Add your signature in the `signature` field.
8. If you are an authorized representative, enter your name in the `authorized_representative_name` field.
9. If you are an authorized representative, add your signature in the `authorized_representative_signature` field.
10. Indicate whether the authorized representative has signed by selecting `True` or `False` in the `authorized_representative_name_checklist` field.
11. Enter the date of the partnership in the `date` field.
12. Enter the time of the partnership in the `time` field.
13. If you are an authorized representative, enter your consent in the `authorized_representative_consent` field.
14. Click the `submit_button` to submit the form.
15. Click the `cancel_button` to cancel the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educational Institution Partnership Consent Form** (`form_title`): Enter the title of the partnership.
* **Representative Name** (`representative_name`): Enter your name as a representative of the educational institution.
* **Role** (`role`): Specify your role in the partnership.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`): Enter the name of the department you are representing.
* **Contact E-Mail** (`contact_email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Add your signature to the partnership agreement.
* **Authorized Representative Name** (`authorized_representative_name`): Enter the name of the authorized representative (if applicable).
* **Authorized Representative Signature** (`authorized_representative_signature`): Add the signature of the authorized representative (if applicable).
* **Authorized Representative Has Signed** (`authorized_representative_name_checklist`): Select `True` if the authorized representative has signed, otherwise select `False`.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the partnership.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the partnership.
* **Authorized Representative Consent** (`authorized_representative_consent`): If you are an authorized representative, enter your consent to the partnership.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`): Click to submit the form and formalize the partnership.
* **Cancel** (`cancel_button`): Click to cancel the form and exit the partnership agreement.
* **Signature Stamp** (`signature_stamp`): This field is not applicable for end users.
