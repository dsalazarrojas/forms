# Participant Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Participant Information Form is designed to collect relevant details from participants, allowing event organizers to manage participant information efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required fields and provide accurate information where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participants Contact Information** (`primary_contact`): The main contact information of the participant.
* **Name**: The name of the participant.
* **Email**: The email address of the participant.
* **On-Site Contact Information**: Additional contact information for on-site purposes.
* **On-Site Name**: The name of the participant while on-site (if applicable).
* **On-Site Email**: The email address of the participant while on-site (if applicable).
* **Check In Time**: The time the participant checked in.
* **Check Out Time**: The time the participant checked out (if applicable).

## Tips
Please ensure all required fields are filled accurately. Optional fields can be left blank if not applicable.
