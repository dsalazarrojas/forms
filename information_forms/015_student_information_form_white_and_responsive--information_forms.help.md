# Student Information Form White And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Information Form White And Responsive is designed to collect personal details and data about students for various administrative and educational purposes. It helps gather essential information for efficient management and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information for each question.
2. Fill out the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. For select fields (such as "Family Members," "Does He/She Play On A School Sports Team?", and "Graduate Planning"), select the relevant options from the dropdown list.
4. For fields like "Student Address" and "Emergency Contact," provide a detailed and accurate description.
5. Upload a photo for the "Student Photo" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student First Name
* **Student First Name** (`student_first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's first name.

### Student Last Name
* **Student Last Name** (`student_last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's last name.

### Student Email
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the student's email address.

### Student Phone Number
* **Student Phone Number** (`student_phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's phone number.

### Family Members
* **Family Members** (`family_members`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all family members living at the student's home (e.g., "Member 1," "Member 2," etc.).

### Does He/She Play On A School Sports Team?
* **Does He/She Play On A School Sports Team?** (`sports_team`, select_one, required/optional): Select "True" if the student plays on a school sports team, "False" otherwise.

### Does He/She Have A Computer At Home?
* **Does He/She Have A Computer At Home?** (`computer_at_home`, select_one, required/optional): Select "True" if the student has a computer at home, "False" otherwise.

### Does He/She Have Internet Access At Home?
* **Does He/She Have Internet Access At Home?** (`internet_access_at_home`, select_one, required/optional): Select "True" if the student has internet access at home, "False" otherwise.

### What Is He/She Planning After Graduating High School?
* **What Is He/She Planning After Graduating High School?** (`graduate_planning`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the student's graduation plans (e.g., "College," "University," "School," "Vocational," etc.).

### Upload Photo
* **Upload Photo** (`upload_photo`, text, required/optional): Upload a photo of the student.

### Student Address
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's address.

### Family Member Relationship To The Teacher
* **Family Member Relationship To The Teacher** (`family_member_relationship_to_the_teacher`, text, required/optional): Describe the family member's relationship to the teacher.

### Additional Notes
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the student.

### Student Birthdate
* **Student Birthdate** (`student_birthdate`, date, required/optional): Enter the student's birthdate.

### Student Age
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, number, required/optional): Enter the student's age.

### Student Gender
* **Student Gender** (`student_gender`, select_one, required/optional): Select the student's gender (e.g., "Male," "Female," "Other").

### Student Height
* **Student Height** (`student_height`, number, required/optional): Enter the student's height.

### Student Weight
* **Student Weight** (`student_weight`, number, required/optional): Enter the student's weight.

### Student Grade
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, required/optional): Select the student's grade level (e.g., "9," "10," "11," "12").

### Parent Phone Number
* **Parent Phone Number** (`parent_phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the parent's phone number.

### Parent Email
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the parent's email address.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Describe the emergency contact person's details.

### Medical Condition
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required/optional): Provide any relevant medical conditions or details.

### Student Photo
* **Student Photo** (`student_photo`, text, required/optional): Upload or enter the student's photo description.

### Teacher's Note
* **Teacher's Note** (`teacher_s_note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments from the teacher.

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form.
* Check your answers for completeness and consistency.
* Use clear and descriptive language when answering questions that require written responses.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the teacher or administrator for guidance.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
