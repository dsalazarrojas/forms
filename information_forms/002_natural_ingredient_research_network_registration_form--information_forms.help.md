# Natural Ingredient Research Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Natural Ingredient Research Network Registration Form is designed to collect information from researchers who want to join the network. This form is meant for researchers who are interested in collaborating and sharing knowledge with others in the field of natural ingredients research.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Researcher Name** in the "Researcher Name" field. This is where you should enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
2. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This is the email address where you can be reached for communication.
3. Select your **Researcher Role** from the list. This is an important step as it will help us understand your background and expertise.
4. Enter your **Institution Name** if you are affiliated with one. This can be your university, research institution, or company.
5. If you have a specific **Department Name**, please enter it here. This can be your department or lab name within your institution.
6. Describe your **Research Interests** in the text field. This is an opportunity for you to tell us about your research focus or areas of interest.
7. Enter your **Bio-Scientific Name** if you have one. This is your name as it appears in scientific publications.
8. Enter your **Institution Address** if you are affiliated with an institution. This can be your university, research institution, or company address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Researcher Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email**: Enter the email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Researcher Role**: Select one of the options to indicate your background and expertise.
	+ **Academic Researcher**: You are a professor, researcher, or student affiliated with an academic institution.
	+ **Non-Academic Researcher**: You are a researcher outside of academic institutions, such as a private researcher or a research consultant.
	+ **Student Researcher**: You are a student currently pursuing a research-related degree or certification.
* **Institution Name**: Enter the name of the institution you are affiliated with, if any.
* **Department Name**: Enter the name of the department or lab within your institution, if any.
* **Research Interests**: Describe your research focus or areas of interest in this text field.
* **Bio-Scientific Name**: Enter your name as it appears in scientific publications, if any.
* **Institution Address**: Enter the address of your institution, if any.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are already registered with the Natural Ingredient Research Network, please do not submit this form again.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at form@gic.mx.
