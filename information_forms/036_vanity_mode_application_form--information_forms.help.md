# Vanity Mode Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vanity Mode Application Form is a necessary step for users who wish to be considered for vanity mode, a special feature that allows users to customize their experience on the platform. This form collects information about you, which we will use to review your application and determine if you qualify for vanity mode.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Scroll through each page (section) of the form, filling out each field as prompted.
2. Enter your information in the provided fields. If a field is marked as required, you will need to fill it out.
3. If you have a supporting file, upload it as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`form_data_1`, `text`, required: false): This is the first section of the form where you can enter general information about yourself. Please fill out any relevant details that will help us get to know you better.
* **Name** (`name_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (`email_1`, `email`, required: false): Enter your valid email address. We will use this to communicate with you about your application.
* **Phone** (`phone_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Request Reason** (`request_mission`, `text`, required: false): Please tell us why you are interested in vanity mode. This will help us understand your motivations.
* **Supporting File** (`file_1`, `text`, required: false): If you have any supporting documents or files that you would like to attach, please upload them here.
* **Motivation** (`motivation_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your motivation for applying for vanity mode. This will help us understand your interests and goals.
