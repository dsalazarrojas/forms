# pet_information_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pet_information_request_form is a tool for collecting information from pet owners. This form is used to gather details about a pet, such as its type, age, weight, and the owner's contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the pet_information_request_form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of pet from the provided options (cat, dog, bird, or fish).
2. If applicable, provide the pet's breed.
3. Enter the pet's age.
4. Enter the pet's weight.
5. Enter the owner's phone number.
6. Enter the owner's email address.
7. Provide any additional message or notes about the pet.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_type** (select_one): Choose the type of pet you're requesting information about. This is a crucial field to ensure the correct information is collected for the right species.
* **pet_breed** (text): If you know the pet's breed, please enter it here. This helps to provide more specific information about the pet.
* **pet_age** (number): Enter the age of the pet in years. If the pet is a kitten, puppy, or a young bird, you can enter a decimal value (e.g., 0.5).
* **pet_weight** (number): Enter the weight of the pet in pounds or kilograms, depending on the measurement system used.
* **owner_phone** (text): Enter the owner's phone number. This is the primary contact method for reaching out.
* **owner_email** (email): Enter the owner's email address. This is an alternative contact method.
* **message** (note): Provide any additional information or comments about the pet, such as its behavior or health status.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.
* Be as specific as possible when providing the pet's breed, age, and weight.
* Use the correct format for the owner's phone number and email address (e.g., use parentheses for phone numbers with extensions).
