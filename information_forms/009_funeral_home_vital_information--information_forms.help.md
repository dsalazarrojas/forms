# Funeral Home Vital Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funeral Home Vital Information form is designed to collect essential details about a deceased person to ensure accurate and respectful handling of their remains and services by the funeral home.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Double-check for any errors or missing information before submitting.
3. Ensure you have all necessary permissions and authorizations before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Deceased Name (Deceased Name, text, required):** Enter the name of the deceased person as it appears on their official identification documents (e.g., driver's license, passport).
* **Cremation Authorization (Cremation Authorization, text, required):** If the deceased has chosen cremation, enter 'yes' or 'no' to confirm. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Death Date (Death Date, date, required):** Enter the date of the deceased person's passing in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Death Certificate (Death Certificate, text, required):** Enter a brief description or reference number for the death certificate, which is an official document issued by the authorities confirming the deceased person's death.

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any information, contact the family or relevant authorities for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed by funeral home staff, but may be used by other professionals in the funeral industry, such as funeral directors or death registrars.
