# Bus Rider Information And Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bus Rider Information And Permission Form is designed to gather essential details about each student riding the bus, ensuring their safety and well-being while on the trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's full name in the "Student Full Name" field.
2. Select the student's grade level from the options provided.
3. Enter the student's date of birth.
4. Provide the parent or guardian's name and contact information, including phone numbers.
5. If necessary, enter an alternate emergency contact's information.
6. Indicate whether the student has any known medical conditions or allergies.
7. Specify if medication is required during transport.
8. Designate the pickup and drop-off location.
9. Confirm permission for the student to travel by bus.
10. Sign electronically with your full name to certify authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Full Name** (`student_name_full`, text, required): Enter the student's full name as it appears on their official documents.
- **Grade Level** (`school_grade_level`, select_one, required): Choose the student's current grade level from the options provided.
- **Student Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the student.
- **Primary Contact Phone** (`primary_phone`, text, required): Provide your primary contact phone number.
- **Secondary Contact Phone** (`secondary_phone`, text, optional): Enter a secondary contact phone number, if applicable.
- **Alternate Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_alternate`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of an alternate emergency contact.
- **Known Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions_allergies`, text, optional): List any known medical conditions or allergies.
- **Medication Required During Transport** (`medication_required_on_bus`, select_one, required): Choose whether medication is required during transport from the provided options.
- **Designated Pickup/Drop-off Location** (`pickup_dropoff_location`, text, required): Specify the designated pickup and drop-off location for the student.
- **Permission for Travel** (`permission_for_travel`, select_one, required): Confirm your permission for the student to travel by bus.
- **Electronic Signature of Parent/Guardian** (`parent_signature_digital`, text, required): Type your full name to sign electronically and certify authorization.
- **Date Signed** (`form_completion_date`, date, required): The date you complete and sign this form.
