# new_particle_submission_form minimalist title - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_particle_submission_form is used to collect information about a particle submitted to a database. It gathers contact details of the submitters, description and characteristics of the particle, date and time of submission, and details about its status and publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the contact details of the submitter, including their name, occupation, and address.
* Describe the particle in detail, including its location, color, and size.
* Specify the date and time of submission, as well as the date of discovery.
* Choose the particle's status and category from the available options.
* Provide any additional notes or comments about the particle.
* Upload any relevant images or files related to the particle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details (1)**:
	+ **Contact Details (name):** Enter the submitter's name.
	+ **Particle Description (2):** Describe the particle in detail.
	+ **Particle Category (3):** Choose the category of the particle from the available options.
	+ **Particle Type (4):** Choose multiple types of the particle from the available options.
	+ **Submitter (5):** Enter the submitters' name.
	+ **Date of Submission (6):** Enter the date of submission.
	+ **Research Details (7):** Provide any additional research details about the particle.
	+ **Submitter Email (8):** Enter the submitters' email address.
	+ **Submitter Phone (9):** Enter the submitters' phone number.
	+ **Particle Location (10):** Choose the location of the particle from the available options.
	+ **Particle Color (11):** Choose multiple colors of the particle from the available options.
	+ **Particle Size (12):** Choose the size of the particle from the available options.
	+ **Submitter Name (13):** Enter the submitters' name.
	+ **Submitter Occupation (14):** Enter the submitters' occupation.
	+ **Date of Discovery (15):** Enter the date of discovery.
	+ **Submitter Institution (16):** Enter the submitters' institution.
	+ **Date of Publication (17):** Enter the date of publication.
	+ **Particle Status (18):** Choose the status of the particle from the available options.
	+ **Particle Description Details (19):** Provide any additional description details about the particle.
	+ **Submitter Notes (20):** Provide any additional notes or comments about the submitters.
	+ **Particle Image URL (21):** Upload any relevant images of the particle.
	+ **Particle File URL (22):** Upload any relevant files related to the particle.
	+ **Submitter Address (23):** Enter the submitters' address.
	+ **Submitter Comment (24):** Enter any additional comments about the particle.
	+ **Particle ID (25):** Enter any additional identifying information about the particle.
