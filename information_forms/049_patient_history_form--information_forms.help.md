# Patient History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient History Form is used to gather essential information about a patient, including their general information, medical conditions, and medical history. This form helps healthcare providers to understand the patient's background and provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's general information in the space provided.
2. Provide the patient's name and date of birth.
3. Indicate any medical conditions that the patient has.
4. List any treatments that the patient has received.
5. Note any allergies that the patient has.
6. Share the patient's medical history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`<name>: General Information`, `required: true`): Enter the patient's general information, such as their address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Patient Name** (`<name>: Patient Name`, `required: false`): Enter the patient's name, as you would like it to be listed in the medical records.
* **Date of Birth** (`<name>: Date of Birth`, `required: true`): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical Conditions** (`<name>: Medical Conditions`, `required: true`): Check all the boxes for any medical conditions that the patient has, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Treatments Received** (`<name>: Treatments Received`, `required: false`): List any treatments that the patient has received, such as medications or therapies.
* **Allergies** (`<name>: Allergies`, `required: false`): List any allergies that the patient has, such as food or environmental allergies.
* **Medical History** (`<name>: Medical History`, `required: false`): Share the patient's medical history, including any significant events or conditions.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask the patient or their authorized representative for clarification.
* If the patient's information is already available in the system, please do not fill out this form.
* Please keep the information up-to-date and accurate to provide the best possible care.
