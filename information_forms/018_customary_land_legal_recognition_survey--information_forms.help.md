# Customary Land Legal Recognition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customary Land Legal Recognition Survey is a form used to collect and verify information about land ownership, rights, and legal recognition status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Ensure that you are completing this form for the correct land ownership.
3. Fill out the form in its entirety, but do not skip any sections.
4. Review and verify the accuracy of your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Land Rights**: Enter the type of land rights you hold (e.g., freehold, leasehold, etc.).
* **Legal Recognition Status**: Choose the current status of the land recognition (Active or Inactive).
* **Support Needs**: Briefly describe any support needs related to the land (e.g., infrastructure, maintenance, etc.).
* **Land Area**: Provide the area of the land being surveyed (in square meters).
* **Number of Houses**: Enter the number of houses on the land.
* **Family Size**: Indicate if you have any dependents (Yes or No).
* **Gender**: Indicate if you are male (Yes) or female (No).
* **Age**: Enter your age.
* **Occupation**: Briefly describe your occupation.
* **Legal Recognition Level**: Choose the level of legal recognition (High or Low).
* **Legal Recognition Date**: Enter the date of the legal recognition (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Legal Recognition Document**: Attach any relevant legal recognition documents (e.g., deeds, certificates, etc.).
* **Document Location**: Provide the location of the document.
* **Support Services**: Indicate if you have support services (Yes or No).
* **Support Needs**: Briefly describe any support needs related to the land (e.g., infrastructure, maintenance, etc.).
* **Surveyor Location**: Provide the location of the surveyor (e.g., building, office, etc.).
* **Surveyor Contact**: Enter the contact information of the surveyor (e.g., phone number, email, etc.).
* **Surveyor Date**: Enter the date of the survey (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Surveyor Hours**: Enter the survey duration (HH:MM format).
* **Surveyor Comment**: Provide any additional comments from the surveyor.
* **Surveyor Signature**: Attach a handwritten or scanned signature of the surveyor.
* **Surveyor Name**: Enter the name of the surveyor.
* **Community Land Rights**: Indicate if you have community land rights (Yes or No).
* **Community Ownership**: Indicate if you have community ownership (Yes or No).
* **Legal Recognition Status (Confirmation)**: Confirm the status of legal recognition (Active or Inactive).
