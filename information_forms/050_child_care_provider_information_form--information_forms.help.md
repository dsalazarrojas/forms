# Child Care Provider Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Care Provider Information Form is a crucial document that helps us understand your qualifications, work experience, and background as a child care provider. This information will be used to assess your suitability for our certification or registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*). These fields are essential for a successful application.
2. Provide accurate and honest information in each section.
3. Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: *Required* - Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name**: *Required* - Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of birth**: *Required* - Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Current address**: *Required* - Enter your current home address.
* **City**: *Required* - Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **State or province**: *Required* - Enter the state or province where you currently reside.
* **Zip code**: *Required* - Enter your zip code or postal code.
* **Email address**: *Required* - Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone number**: *Required* - Enter your contact phone number.
* **Position or job title**: *Required* - Enter your current or previous position related to child care.
* **Type of employment**: *Required* - Select your current type of employment from the options provided.
* **Employment start date**: *Required* - Enter your start date of employment in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Age groups worked with**: *Required* - Select all age groups you have experience with from the options provided.
* **Highest education level**: *Required* - Select your highest education level from the options provided.
* **Field of study or major**: *Optional* - If applicable, enter your field of study or major.
* **Relevant certifications or licenses**: *Optional* - If applicable, list any relevant certifications or licenses.
* **CPR certification**: *Required* - Select your current CPR certification status.
* **First aid certification**: *Required* - Select your current first aid certification status.
* **Consent for background check**: *Required* - Select whether you consent to a background check.
* **Do you have any criminal history?**: *Required* - Select whether you have any criminal history.
* **Professional references provided**: *Required* - Select whether you have provided professional references.
* **Years of child care experience**: *Required* - Enter the number of years you have experience in child care.
* **Previous employer(s) and facilities**: *Optional* - If applicable, list your previous employers and facilities.
* **Specialized training or skills**: *Optional* - If applicable, list any specialized training or skills.
* **Your child care philosophy or approach**: *Optional* - If applicable, describe your child care philosophy or approach.

Note: The fields marked as *Required* are essential for a successful application. Please ensure you complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
