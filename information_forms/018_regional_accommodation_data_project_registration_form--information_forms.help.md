# Regional Accommodation Data Project Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register and gather information about regional accommodation data projects. It is a crucial step in the project management process, ensuring that all necessary details are collected and recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Ensure that all fields marked as "required" are filled out.
3. Select the correct option for the "participation_consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your organization.
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Participation Consent** (`participation_consent`, `select_one`, required: false):
  + Choose one of the provided options to indicate your consent for the project.
  + Option 1: You can choose one of the available options to select your consent.
  + Option 2: Another option to choose from.
  + Option 3: The last option to choose from.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the project.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Choose the correct option for participation consent carefully, as this will impact your involvement with the project.
