# Nutrition Science Awareness Campaign Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather informed consent from users participating in a nutrition science awareness campaign. The campaign aims to educate users about the importance of nutrition and its impact on health. The form is designed to collect user contact details and obtain their informed consent for the campaign activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form and understand the purpose of the campaign.
2.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3.  Sign and date the form to indicate your informed consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Informed Consent** (Page 1, Field 1):
    - This field is a note-type field where you can provide your informed consent to participate in the campaign.
    - Please type in your consent, explaining that you understand the purpose and implications of the campaign.
*   **Contact Details** (Page 1, Field 2):
    - This field is for entering your contact information, such as your name, email address, and phone number.
    - Please provide accurate and up-to-date contact details so that we can reach out to you for further information or to update you about the campaign.
*   **Additional Informed Consent** (Page 1, Field 3):
    - This field is another note-type field where you can provide any additional information that is relevant to your consent.
    - Please use this space to clarify or add any additional details that you think are important for your participation in the campaign.

## Tips
- Please make sure to sign and date the form after completing it.
