# Child Information Form AH - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Information Form AH is a form used to collect essential information about a child, which is necessary for program administration and updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the child's full name in the "Child Full Name" field.
2. Select the child's date of birth using the calendar icon or manually entering the date.
3. Enter the full legal name of the primary contact person for the child.
4. Provide the primary mobile contact number of the primary contact person.
5. Enter the email address of the primary contact person for administrative updates.
6. (Optional) Add any additional notes or special instructions for the child, such as dietary needs or pickup authorization.
7. Record the date of entry using the calendar icon or manually entering the date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Child Full Name** (`child_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the child participating in the program or event.
* **Date of Birth** (`child_birthday`, `date`, required): Select the child's date of birth using the calendar icon or manually entering the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Primary Contact Person** (`primary_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the full legal name of the primary contact person responsible for the child.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the mobile contact number of the primary contact person.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the primary contact person for administrative updates.
* **Additional Notes** (`section_additional_notes`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional notes or special instructions for the child, such as dietary needs or pickup authorization.
* **Special Instructions or Preferences** (`special_instructions`, `text`, optional): Enter any special instructions or preferences for the child.
* **Today Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Record the date of entry using the calendar icon or manually entering the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information about the child.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the program administrators for assistance.
