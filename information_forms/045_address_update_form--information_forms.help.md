# Address Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Address Update Form is used to update a user's contact information, including their full name, customer ID number, current email address, phone number, and address details (street, city, state/province, zip/postal code, and country).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name in the Full Name field.
2. Enter your customer ID number, if known, in the Member / Customer ID Number field.
3. Enter your current email address in the Current Email Address field.
4. Enter your current phone number in the Current Phone Number field.
5. In the Previous City and State field, enter the city and state of your previous address.
6. In the New Address Details section, enter your new street address in the New Street Address field.
7. Enter your new city in the New City field.
8. Enter your new state or province in the New State / Province field.
9. Enter your new zip or postal code in the New Zip / Postal Code field.
10. Enter your new country in the New Country field.
11. Select the reason for the update from the Reason for Update field.
12. Choose whether you want to use this new address for all communications in the Use this as my primary mailing address for all communications? field.
13. Select the effective date of the change in the Effective Date of Change field.
14. Confirm that you are authorized to update this information in the I confirm that I am authorized to update this information field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Full Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it is currently known.
* **Member / Customer ID Number** (Member / Customer ID Number, text, optional): If you know your customer ID number, enter it here.
* **Current Email Address** (Current Email Address, email, required): Enter your current email address.
* **Current Phone Number** (Current Phone Number, text, required): Enter your current phone number.
* **Previous City and State** (Previous City and State, text, required): Enter the city and state of your previous address for identity verification purposes.
* **New Address Details** (New Address Details, note, optional): This section is for entering your new address details.
	+ **New Street Address** (New Street Address, text, required): Enter your new street address.
	+ **New City** (New City, text, required): Enter your new city.
	+ **New State / Province** (New State / Province, text, required): Enter your new state or province.
	+ **New Zip / Postal Code** (New Zip / Postal Code, text, required): Enter your new zip or postal code.
	+ **New Country** (New Country, text, required): Enter your new country.
* **Reason for Update** (Reason for Update, select_one, required): Select the reason for the update from the options provided.
* **Use this as my primary mailing address for all communications?** (Use this as my primary mailing address for all communications?, select_one, required): Choose whether you want to use this new address for all communications.
* **Effective Date of Change** (Effective Date of Change, date, required): Select the effective date of the change.
* **I confirm that I am authorized to update this information** (I confirm that I am authorized to update this information, select_one, required): Confirm that you are authorized to update this information.
