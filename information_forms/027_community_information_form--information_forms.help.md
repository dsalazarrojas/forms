# Community Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Information Form is a comprehensive tool designed to capture essential information about a community. It helps organizations and stakeholders gain a deeper understanding of the community's characteristics, needs, and opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Answer each question accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Use the provided hints as guidance for each field.
4. Ensure that you have completed all required fields (marked as "true" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Official Name of the Community** (1, `community_name`, **Required**): Enter the official name of the community, such as its neighborhood, town, or city name.
* **Estimated Total Population** (2, `total_population`, **Optional**): Provide an estimated total population of the community, if available.
* **Primary Economic Driver or Industry** (3, `economic_driver`, **Required**): Identify the primary economic driver or industry that supports the community, e.g., Tourism, Agriculture, Manufacturing, Tech.
* **Key Community Leaders or Representatives** (4, `community_leaders`, **Optional**): List the names and roles of key community leaders or representatives.
* **Main Public Services Available within the Community** (5, `public_services`, **Required**): Select all public services available within the community, including:
	+ General Hospital or Clinic
	+ Public Library
	+ Community and Youth Center
	+ Reliable Public Transportation
	+ Public Parks and Playgrounds
	+ Emergency Services (Police/Fire)
* **Geographic Area Description** (6, `geographic_area`, **Optional**): Describe the geographic area of the community, including square miles or physical boundaries.
* **Notable Historical Events or Milestones** (7, `historical_events`, **Optional**): Share notable historical events or milestones that have shaped the community.
* **Current Unique Challenges Facing the Community** (8, `community_challenges`, **Required**): Identify current unique challenges facing the community.
* **Community Motto or Future Vision Statement** (9, `community_vision`, **Optional**): Share the community's motto or future vision statement.
* **Brief Demographic Overview** (10, `demographics_overview`, **Optional**): Provide a brief demographic overview of the community, including diversity and age range.
* **Date of Last Information Update** (11, `last_update_date`, **Required**): Enter the date of the last information update.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the provided hints for guidance.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact your team or supervisor for support.
