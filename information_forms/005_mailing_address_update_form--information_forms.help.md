# mailing_address_update_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the mailing address update form for users to update their address information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Yes" or "No" option for "Form Data".
2. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your Email Address in the "Email Address" field. Make sure to use a valid email address format.
5. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Enter your Street Address in the "Street Address" field.
7. Enter your City in the "City" field.
8. Enter your State in the "State" field.
9. Enter your Zip Code in the "Zip Code" field. Make sure to use a valid zip code format.
10. Enter your Country of residence in the "Country" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Data** (`Form Data`, Select Multiple, required): This field allows you to confirm that you have a valid reason to update your address.
- **First Name** (`First Name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your documents.
- **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your documents.
- **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you use for communication.
- **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Street Address** (`Street Address`, text, optional): Enter your street address where you reside.
- **City** (`City`, text, optional): Enter the city where you reside.
- **State** (`State`, text, optional): Enter the state where you reside.
- **Zip Code** (`Zip Code`, number, optional): Enter your valid zip code.
- **Country** (`Country`, text, optional): Enter your country of residence.
- **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
