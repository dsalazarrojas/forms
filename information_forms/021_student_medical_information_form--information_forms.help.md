# Student Medical Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Student Medical Information Form is a tool used to gather important medical information about students, such as their medical history and contact details. This form helps schools and educational institutions to keep accurate records and provide necessary support in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and honestly, as it will be used to create a student's medical record.
2. Enter your student's information on the "Student Information" page, including their name and any other relevant details.
3. Describe your student's medical history on the "Medical History" page, including any conditions they may have.
4. Select any relevant medical assessments or tests your student has undergone on the "Medical Assessments" page.
5. Enter your contact information on the "Contact Information" page, so we can reach out to you in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (Student Information, text, required): Enter your student's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, required): Describe your student's medical history, including any conditions they may have, such as allergies or asthma.
* **Medical Assessments** (Medical Assessments, select multiple, required): Choose any medical assessments or tests your student has undergone, such as allergy tests or diabetes assessments.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, email, required): Enter your contact information, including your email address, so we can reach out to you in case of an emergency.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully and honestly, as it will be used to create a student's medical record.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your school's administration or healthcare provider for guidance.
* If your student has a medical condition that is not listed, please describe it in the "Medical History" field.
