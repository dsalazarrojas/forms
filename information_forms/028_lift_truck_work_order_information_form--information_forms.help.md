# lift_truck_work_order_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and display information about lift truck work orders. The goal is to provide a standardized way to record and manage lift truck-related work orders, ensuring that all necessary details are captured and accessible for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all the required information ready before starting.
2. Fill in the `customer_name` field with the name of the customer associated with the work order.
3. Input the `work_order_date` field with the specific date the work order was issued or scheduled.
4. Provide a brief description of the work order in the `work_order_description` field.
5. For the `lift_truck_type` field, select all applicable options that apply to the work order.
6. Choose the status of the work order in the `work_order_status` field, selecting between 'active' or 'inactive'.
7. Optionally, input the name of the person or team the work order has been assigned to in the `assigned_to` field.
8. If the work order has been completed, enter the date it was completed in the `completed_date` field.
9. Add any additional comments about the work order in the `comments` field.
10. Enter the email address of the customer or contact person in the `email` field.
11. If applicable, add the phone number of the customer or contact person in the `phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, optional): The name of the customer associated with this work order.
- **Work Order Date** (work_order_date, date, optional): The date the work order was issued or scheduled.
- **Work Order Description** (work_order_description, text, optional): A brief description of the purpose or details about the work order.
- **Lift Truck Type** (lift_truck_type, select_multiple, optional): Select all types of lift truck involved or related to this work order.
- **Work Order Status** (work_order_status, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the work order as 'active' or 'inactive'.
- **Assigned To** (assigned_to, text, optional): The name of the person or team assigned to handle this work order.
- **Completed Date** (completed_date, date, optional): The date the work order was completed.
- **Comments** (comments, text, optional): Any additional comments or notes about the work order.
- **Email** (email, email, optional): The email address of the customer or contact person.
- **Phone** (phone, text, optional): The phone number of the customer or contact person.
