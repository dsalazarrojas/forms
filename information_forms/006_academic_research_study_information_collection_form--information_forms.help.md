# Academic Research Study Information Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data from potential participants for an academic research study. Completing this form will help researchers understand your demographic details, research interests, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Answer all required questions (indicated by an asterisk \*). Optional questions can be left blank.
3. Be accurate and truthfully complete the form.
4. Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required):
	+ Enter your primary contact email address for study updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter your mobile or landline number including area code.
* **Current Age** (`age`, `number`, required):
	+ Enter your age in years.
* **Gender Identity** (`gender`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select the option that best describes you:
		+ Male
		+ Female
		+ Non-binary
		+ Prefer not to say
* **Current Student Status** (`student_status`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select your current academic standing:
		+ Undergraduate
		+ Graduate
		+ Ph.D. Candidate
		+ Not a Student
* **Major or Field of Study** (`major_field_of_study`, `text`, optional):
	+ Your primary area of academic focus.
* **Topics you are interested in researching** (`interested_topics`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select all relevant topics you are interested in researching:
		+ Social Sciences
		+ Natural Sciences
		+ Humanities
		+ Technology
		+ Medicine
		+ Arts
* **Preferred time for study participation** (`participation_availability`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select your preferred time for study participation:
		+ Mornings
		+ Afternoons
		+ Evenings
		+ Weekends
* **Have you participated in a research study before?** (`previous_participation`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select whether you have previous experience as a participant:
		+ True
		+ False
* **How did you hear about this study?** (`discovery_source`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description of how you found out about this study.
* **Do you consent to being contacted for future studies?** (`consent_to_contact`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select whether you consent to be contacted for future studies:
		+ True
		+ False
