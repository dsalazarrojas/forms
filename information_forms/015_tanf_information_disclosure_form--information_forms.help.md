# TANF Information Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the purposes of the Texas Addiction and Recovery Fund (TANF). It is a critical form that requires accurate and relevant data to be filled out correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer all the fields that are marked as "required" to ensure that you have provided all the necessary information.
2. For fields that are not marked as "required", select the options that apply to your situation.
3. Double-check that all fields are filled out accurately before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (1): This field is for general information and comments about your case. Please provide any relevant details about your situation.
* **Client Info** (2): Select "Yes" if you are a client of TANF, and "No" if you are not. This will help us understand who we are working with.
* **Share with** (3): Select "Yes" if you would like to share your information with other parties, and "No" if you would like to keep it private. This will determine who has access to your data.
* **Authorized Parties** (4): If you would like to share your information with specific parties, enter their names or contact details in this field.
* **Signature Date** (5): This field is for the date you signed the form. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Signature Time** (6): This field is for the time you signed the form. Please enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Signature** (7): Select "Yes" if you have signed the form, and "No" if you have not. This will confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms of the form.
* **TANF ID** (8): If you have a TANF ID, please enter it in this field. This helps us track your case.
* **Case Manager Name** (9): This field is for the name of your case manager. Please enter their name and any relevant contact details.

## Tips
* Double-check that all fields are filled out accurately before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the relevant personnel for clarification.
* Please sign the form digitally or physically, and make sure to date and time stamp it.
