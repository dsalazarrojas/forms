# digital_security_assistance_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The digital security assistance contact form is a tool for users to report incidents or issues related to digital security, allowing the digital security team to provide timely and effective support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your device name in the "Device Name" field.
2. Provide the name of the account associated with the issue in the "Account Name" field.
3. Describe the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
4. Select the type of incident you are reporting from the "Incident Type" dropdown menu (Security, Technical, or Administrative).
5. Specify the priority of the incident from the "Priority" dropdown menu (Low, Medium, or High).
6. Choose the level of urgency for the incident from the "Urgency" dropdown menu (Low goals, High goals, or Critical).
7. Select the current status of the incident from the "Incident Status" dropdown menu (New, InProgress, or Closed).
8. Provide any additional details about the incident in the "Incident Details" field.
9. If you have an email address associated with your account, enter it in the "Account Email" field for further contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Device Name** (Text): Enter the name of the device associated with the incident.
* **Account Name** (Text): Enter the name of the account associated with the issue.
* **Incident Description** (Text): Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
* **Incident Type** (Select One): Choose from the following options:
	+ Security: Related to security protocols or threats.
	+ Technical: Technical issues, such as hardware or software problems.
	+ Administrative: Issues related to access or permissions.
* **Priority** (Select One): Choose from the following options:
	+ Low: Less urgent, but still in need of attention.
	+ Medium: Somewhat urgent, but not critical.
	+ High: High priority, requires immediate attention.
* **Urgency** (Select One): Choose from the following options:
	+ Low goals: Not particularly time-sensitive.
	+ High goals: Somewhat time-sensitive.
	+ Critical: Time-sensitive, requires immediate attention.
* **Incident Status** (Select One): Choose from the following options:
	+ New: The incident has just been reported.
	+ InProgress: The incident is currently being worked on.
	+ Closed: The incident has been resolved.
* **Incident Details** (Note): Provide any additional information about the incident that may be helpful for the digital security team.
* **Account Email** (Email): Enter the email address associated with your account, if applicable.
