# Medical Information Sheet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Information Sheet Form is designed to collect general medical information from patients. This information is crucial for healthcare providers to understand a patient's medical history, allergies, and medications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in all the necessary fields.
2. Provide accurate and detailed information for each field.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, required=false): This section is for providing general information about yourself, such as your name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required=false): This field is for listing any previous medical conditions, such as surgeries, hospitalizations, or medical procedures.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required=false): List any allergies or sensitivities you have, including medications, foods, or environmental triggers.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required=false): List any medications you are currently taking, including prescription and non-prescription medications.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required=false): List any medical conditions you have, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, or asthma.
* **Surgical History** (`surgical_history`, text, required=false): List any previous surgeries or surgical procedures you have undergone.
* **Medical Care** (`medical_care`, text, required=false): Describe your medical care, including any ongoing medical conditions, treatments, or therapies.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required=false): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Medical History Other** (`medical_history_other`, text, required=false): Use this field to provide any additional medical history not covered in the previous fields.
* **Medical Conditions Other** (`medical_conditions_other`, text, required=false): Use this field to provide any additional medical conditions not covered in the previous fields.
