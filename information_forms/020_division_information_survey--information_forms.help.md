# Division Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Division Information Survey is a form designed to collect information about your division within our organization. This data will help us to better understand our internal structure and improve our operations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. Fill out each field accurately and concisely.
3. If a field is optional, you can leave it blank or provide additional information if necessary.
4. Review your submission before clicking "Submit" to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Division Name** (`division_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your division.
* **What are your responsibilities?** (`responsibilities`, text, optional): Describe the main responsibilities of your division.
* **Division Structure** (`division_structure`, text, optional): Provide an overview of your division's organizational structure.
* **Division Challenges** (`division_challenges`, text, optional): List any significant challenges or issues your division is facing.
* **Division Head's Name** (`division_head_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the head of your division.
* **Division Head's Position** (`division_head_position`, text, optional): Enter the title or position of the head of your division.
* **Division Head's Email** (`division_head_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the head of your division.
* **Division Head's Phone** (`division_head_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the head of your division.

## Tips
* Be concise and accurate in your responses.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for guidance.
* Please double-check your submission for errors before clicking "Submit".
