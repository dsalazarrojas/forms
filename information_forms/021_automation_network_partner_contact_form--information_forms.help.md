# Automation Network Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Automation Network Partner Contact Form is a tool for potential partners to share their contact and company information, as well as their partnership interests and capabilities. This form helps us understand your company's strengths, goals, and expectations from a partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, job title, and email address.
2. Provide your company's name, website, and headquarters location.
3. Select the type of partnership you are interested in and your level of partnership experience.
4. List any current partnerships you may have with competitors.
5. Choose the areas where you would focus on with our partnership.
6. Indicate the type and size of resources you can dedicate to the partnership.
7. Explain why you are interested in partnering with us.
8. Provide the expected outcome from the partnership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as you would like it to appear in our communication.
* **Job Title** (`contact_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title as it is recognized within your organization.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your work phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, optional): Enter your company's website for more information.
* **Industry** (`company_industry`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following industries: Software, Hardware, Consulting, Manufacturing, Systems Integration, Reselling, Other.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following company size categories: Startup (1-20 employees), Small (21-100 employees), Medium (101-500 employees), Large (500+ employees).
* **Headquarters Location** (`company_headquarters`, `text`, optional): Enter your company's headquarters location.
* **Type of Partnership Interested In** (`partnership_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Technology Integration
	+ Reseller
	+ VAR (Value Added Reseller)
	+ OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer)
	+ Consulting Partner
	+ Channel Partner
	+ Strategic Alliance
* **Partnership Experience** (`partnership_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following partnership experience levels:
	+ No Prior Experience
	+ Limited Experience
	+ Experienced
	+ Highly Experienced
* **Current Partnerships** (`current_partnerships`, `text`, optional): List any current partnerships you may have with competitors.
* **Automation Focus Areas** (`automation_focus`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
	+ RPA (Robotic Process Automation)
	+ Workflow Automation
	+ Integration
	+ Testing Automation
	+ Cloud Automation
	+ Infrastructure Automation
	+ Business Process Automation
	+ Other
* **Target Market Segments** (`target_market`, `text`, required): Enter the target market segments you would like to focus on.
* **Key Capabilities** (`key_capabilities`, `text`, required): Explain what your company brings to a partnership.
* **Dedicated Resources** (`dedicated_resources`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following resource levels:
	+ Full-time Team
	+ Part-time Team
	+ Limited Resources
	+ To Be Determined
* **Partnership Motivation** (`partnership_interest`, `text`, required): Explain why you are interested in partnering with us.
* **Expected Outcome From Partnership** (`expected_outcome`, `text`, optional): Enter any expected outcome from the partnership.
* **Date of Contact** (`contact_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of this contact.
