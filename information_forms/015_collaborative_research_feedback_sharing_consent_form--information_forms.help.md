# Collaborative Research Feedback Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form ensures that all parties involved in a research project are aware of the terms and conditions under which feedback will be shared and used.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and carefully.
2. Review each field carefully and make sure you understand the implications of your choices.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your project leader or ethics board representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`1`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`2`, `email`, `required`): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone Number** (`3`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Institution or Organization** (`4`, `text`, `required`): Enter your institution or organization name.
* **Department** (`5`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your department (if applicable).
* **Your Role** (`6`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your role in the research project (e.g., Principal Investigator, Co-Investigator, etc.).
* **Research Project Name** (`7`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the research project.
* **Project ID Number** (`8`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the project ID number (if applicable).
* **Consent Date** (`9`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date on which you are giving your consent.
* **Types of Feedback You Consent to Share** (`10`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all the types of feedback you are willing to share (e.g., Performance Feedback, Peer Review Comments, etc.).
* **Who Can Receive Your Feedback** (`11`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all the parties who can receive your feedback (e.g., Principal Investigator, Collaborating Institutions, etc.).
* **Preferred Feedback Format** (`12`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select your preferred method of receiving feedback (e.g., Written Report, Email Summary, etc.).
* **Preferred Feedback Frequency** (`13`, `select_one`, `required`): Select how often you want to receive feedback (e.g., After Each Milestone, Monthly, etc.).
* **Anonymity Preference** (`14`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you want to be anonymous or not (e.g., Always Anonymous, Never Anonymous, etc.).
* **Consent to Receive Feedback from Others** (`15`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you agree to receive feedback from others.
* **Scope of Feedback Sharing** (`16`, `select_one`, `required`): Select what can be shared (e.g., All Research Activities, Specific Tasks Only, etc.).
* **Custom Scope Details** (`17`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any specific details about the scope of feedback sharing (if applicable).
* **Consent for Feedback Data Usage** (`18`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you consent to the use of feedback data for research improvement.
* **Consent for Feedback Use in Publications** (`19`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether feedback can be cited in publications.
* **Acknowledgment of Withdrawal Right** (`20`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you understand you can withdraw your consent at any time.
* **Consent Duration** (`21`, `select_one`, `required`): Select how long your consent is valid (e.g., Duration of Project, One Year, etc.).
* **Are You Under 18** (`22`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you are under 18 years old.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`23`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the name of your parent or guardian (if you are under 18).
* **Parent or Guardian Consent** (`24`, `select_one`, `required` if minor, `optional` otherwise): Enter the name and consent of your parent or guardian (if you are under 18).
* **Questions or Concerns** (`25`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any questions or concerns you have about this consent form.
* **Participant Agreement** (`26`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you agree to the terms of this consent form.
* **Witness Name** (`27`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the name of a witness (if applicable).
* **Witness Agreement** (`28`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select whether the witness agrees to the terms of this consent form.
* **Questions or Concerns** (`25`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any questions or concerns you have about this consent form.
* **Participant Agreement** (`26`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you agree to the terms of this consent form.
