# Ideal Weight Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your ideal weight, which will be used for various purposes such as tracking progress or providing health advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your general information in the first field. This field is optional, but it will help us understand your context.
2. Input your ideal weight in the second field. Please enter a numerical value.
3. If you have any special instructions or comments, you can enter them in the third field. This field is also optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (General Information, text, optional): Enter any additional information that you think is relevant to your ideal weight. This could include your age, height, or health goals.
* **Ideal Weight** (Ideal Weight, number, required): Enter your ideal weight. This is the weight you aim to achieve.
* **Special Instructions** (Special Instructions, text, optional): If you have any special circumstances or instructions that are relevant to your ideal weight, you can enter them here. For example, if you have any medical conditions or allergies that affect your weight, please let us know.
