# Background Information Disclosure Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect background information from individuals who wish to apply for positions that require it.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields and sections.
2. Enter your full legal name and date of birth in the respective fields.
3. Enter your social security number in the "Social Security Number" field.
4. Select the correct response for each question regarding your criminal and regulatory history, including convictions, pending charges, and regulatory findings.
5. If you answered "Yes" to any of the questions regarding your criminal or regulatory history, provide details in the "Detailed Disclosure Text" field.
6. List all states you have lived in during the last 5 years in the "Residency History Last 5" field.
7. Select whether you authorize the release of background information for this application.
8. Certify that the information provided is true and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Background Information Disclosure (BID)** (`disclosure_legal_intro`, note, optional): This section serves as an introduction to the form and the importance of providing accurate and truthful information.
* **Full Legal Name** (`applicant_full_legal_name_bid`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Social Security Number** (`ssn_bid`, text, required): Enter your social security number as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`birth_date_bid`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the standard MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender_bid`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **-- Criminal and Regulatory History --** (`convictions_disclosure_header`, note, optional): This section is used to draw attention to the questions that follow regarding your criminal and regulatory history.
* **Have you ever been convicted of a felony or misdemeanor?** (`has_criminal_convictions`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you have been convicted of a felony or misdemeanor.
* **Do you have any pending criminal charges against you?** (`has_pending_charges`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you have any pending criminal charges against you.
* **Have you ever been found to have abused or neglected a child or vulnerable adult by any government or regulatory agency?** (`has_regulatory_findings`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you have been found to have abused or neglected a child or vulnerable adult.
* **If you answered Yes to any above, provide details** (`detailed_disclosure_text`, text, optional): If you answered "True" to any of the previous questions, provide additional details about the incidents in this field.
* **List all states you have lived in during the last 5 years** (`residency_history_last_5`, text, required): List all states you have lived in during the last 5 years.
* **I authorize the release of background information for this application** (`authorization_to_release_bid`, select_one, required): Select "I authorize" to release your background information for this application.
* **I certify that the information provided is true and complete** (`truthfulness_certification_bid`, select_one, required): Select "I certify" to confirm that the information provided is true and complete.
* **Date of Signature** (`bid_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of signature in the standard MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any question, please contact your local authorities for guidance.
* Please note that this form may not be necessary if you have already completed a similar form in the past 5 years.
