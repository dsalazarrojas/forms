# Sustainable Learning Research Feature Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and preferences from participants in a research study on sustainable learning features. Your input will help researchers understand your needs and interests in this area.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately. If you need help or have questions, you can ask.
2. Select the correct options for each field as requested.
3. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. General Information (General Information)
* **General Information** (`General Information`, `text`, required: false): This is a text field where you can provide any general information about yourself.

### 2. Contact Information (Contact Information)
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following options for your contact information:
	+ First Name
	+ Last Name
	+ Email
	+ Phone Number

### 3. Consent (Consent)
* **Consent** (`Consent`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply to indicate your level of consent for the research:
	+ True
	+ False

### 4. Feature Interest (Feature Interest)
* **Feature Interest** (`Feature Interest`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply to indicate your interest in the following features:
	+ Sustainability
	+ Technology
	+ Accessibility

### 5. Feature Preference (Feature Preference)
* **Feature Preference** (`Feature Preference`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following options to indicate your level of interest in the feature:
	+ Highly Interested
	+ Somewhat Interested
	+ Not At All Interested

### 6. Contact Method (Contact Method)
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, `select_one`, required: true): Select one of the following options for how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ In-person

### 7. Email (Email)
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address here.

### 8. Phone (Phone)
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `number`, required: true): Enter your phone number here.

### 9. Address (Address)
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required: true): Enter your address here.

### 10. Notes (Notes)
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required: false): This is an optional field for any additional comments or notes you have.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest in your responses.
* Use the contact method that you are most comfortable with.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out.
* Your input will help shape the future of sustainable learning features, so thank you for participating!
