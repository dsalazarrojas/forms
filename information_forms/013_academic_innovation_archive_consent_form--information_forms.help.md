# Academic Innovation Archive Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Innovation Archive Consent Form is a tool used to collect detailed information about academic innovations, such as teaching methodologies, assessment techniques, and technologies. This form helps contributors provide accurate and comprehensive information about their innovations, which can then be shared and archived for the academic community to learn from and build upon.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Contributor Information**: Provide your full name and contact email address, which will be used for correspondence and communication.
2. **Innovation Details**: Enter a brief title and detailed description of your innovation, as well as its type (e.g., teaching methodology, assessment technique, etc.). Be as specific as possible when describing your innovation.
3. **Academic Discipline**: Identify the primary field or subject area of your innovation.
4. **Innovation Development**: Specify the date when your innovation was developed or implemented.
5. **Consent and Permissions**: Provide your consent to have your innovation archived in the academic database. You can also choose to consent to publication in academic literature, subject to editorial review. If you have intellectual property rights, please indicate who holds them.
6. **Collaboration and Contact**: If applicable, list any co-contributors to your innovation. You can also specify if you are willing to be contacted about your innovation in the future.
7. **Funding and Ethics**: If applicable, mention any funding sources that supported your innovation. If human subjects were involved, confirm that ethical approval was obtained.
8. **Submission and Review**: Finally, confirm that you are willing to be contacted about your innovation and have reviewed the information you have provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Contributor Name**
Contributor Name: `<name>` (`contributor_name`, text, required) - Your full name, which will be used for correspondence and communication.
### 2. **Email Address**
Email Address: `<email>` (`contributor_email`, email, required) - Your contact email address for further correspondence and communication.
### 3. **Institution**
Institution: `<institution>` (`contributor_institution`, text, required) - Your university or organization affiliation.
### 4. **Innovation Title**
Innovation Title: `<title>` (`innovation_title`, text, required) - A brief title describing your academic innovation.
### 5. **Innovation Description**
Innovation Description: `<description>` (`innovation_description`, text, required) - A detailed description of your academic innovation.
### 6. **Type of Innovation**
Type of Innovation: `<type>` (`innovation_type`, select_one, required) - The category or type of innovation (e.g., teaching methodology, assessment technique, etc.).
### 7. **Academic Discipline**
Academic Discipline: `<discipline>` (`academic_discipline`, text, required) - The primary field or subject area of your innovation.
### 8. **Date of Innovation Development**
Date of Innovation Development: `<date>` (`innovation_date`, date, required) - When your innovation was developed or implemented.
### 9. **Outcomes and Results**
Outcomes and Results: `<results>` (`innovation_outcomes`, text, optional) - Any notable results or outcomes from your innovation (if applicable).
### 10. **Consent to Archive**
Consent to Archive: `<consent>` (`consent_to_archive`, select_one, required) - Provide your consent to have your innovation archived in the academic database.
### 11. **Consent to Publication**
Consent to Publication: `<consent>` (`consent_to_publication`, select_one, optional) - Provide your consent to have your innovation details published in academic literature, subject to editorial review.
### 12. **Intellectual Property Rights**
Intellectual Property Rights: `<rights>` (`intellectual_property_rights`, text, required) - The holder of the intellectual property rights for this innovation.
### 13. **Co-Contributors**
Co-contributors: `<co-contributors>` (`co_contributors`, text, optional) - A list of any co-contributors to your innovation (if applicable).
### 14. **Funding Sources**
Funding Sources: `<sources>` (`funding_sources`, text, optional) - Any funding sources that supported your innovation (if applicable).
### 15. **Ethical Approval**
Ethical Approval: `<approval>` (`ethical_approval`, select_one, required) - Confirm if ethical approval was obtained for your innovation (if human subjects were involved).
### 16. **Contact for Inquiries**
Contact for Inquiries: `<inquiries>` (`contact_for_inquiries`, select_one, required) - Indicate your willingness to be contacted about your innovation in the future.

## Tips
* Please be specific and clear when filling out the form to ensure accurate information about your innovation.
* Ensure you provide your correct contact information to facilitate future communication.
* You can choose to have your innovation archived or published according to your preferences.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
