# social_media_information_request - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to capture information related to a social media request, which is a formal way to communicate updates to a social media manager or team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Check that you are using this form for its intended purpose, and that the information requested is not already available through other means.
2. Select whether social media platforms are being requested.
3. Enter your email address and phone number to contact you for further information or questions.
4. Add any additional notes or details about the request if necessary.
5. Provide the date and time of the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Social Media Platforms** (`social_media_platforms`, select_multiple, required): Select all social media platforms that are being requested (e.g. Facebook, Twitter, Instagram).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further information or questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can reach you for further information or questions.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional context or details about the request.
* **Social Media Request Date** (`social_media_request_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the social media update is requested.
* **Time of Request** (`time_of_request`, time, required): Enter the time of the request.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Add any extra information that is not covered by the other fields.

## Tips

* Make sure to select all social media platforms that are relevant to your request.
* Be as specific as possible when entering your email address and phone number.
* Use the additional notes field to provide extra context or details about the request.
* Enter the date and time of the request accurately.
