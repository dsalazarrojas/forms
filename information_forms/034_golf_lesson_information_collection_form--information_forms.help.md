# golf_lesson_information_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The golf_lesson_information_collection_form is designed to collect information about golf lessons, allowing us to manage and optimize these lessons for students. By filling out this form, you will be providing valuable data that will help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Student Details section with your name, contact information, and other relevant details.
2. Provide information about the lesson, including the goals you aim to achieve.
3. Select the instructor who will be teaching you.
4. If applicable, specify the academy where the lesson will take place.
5. Choose the date and time you are available for the lesson.
6. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Student Details**
 Student Details (text, required)
* This field is where you will provide your name and contact information.

### **Lesson Details**
 Lesson Details (text, required)
* This field is where you will describe the lesson, including the goals you aim to achieve.

### **Lesson Goals**
 Lesson Goals (select_multiple, optional)
* This field allows you to select one or more goals for the lesson. Please choose the goals that apply to your lesson.

### **Instructor**
 Instructor (select_one, optional)
* This field allows you to select the instructor who will be teaching you.

### **Academy**
 Academy (text, optional)
* This field is where you can specify the academy where the lesson will take place.

### **Availability**
 Availability (date, required)
* This field is where you will choose the date and time you are available for the lesson.
