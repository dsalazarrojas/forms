# Service Directory Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Directory Submission Form is designed to collect information about service directory submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your submission name and description in the respective fields.
2. Provide a valid email address in the "Submission email" field.
3. Select the category that best describes your submission (Association, Non-profit, or Local network).
4. Enter your submission address.
5. (Optional) Provide a link to your submission website, image, or file.
6. Select the submission status (Approved, Pending, or Declined).
7. (Optional) Provide a note for the reviewers.
8. Add the names and contact information of up to 10 reviewers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Name** (`submission_name`, required): Enter the title or name of your submission.
* **Submission Description** (`submission_description`, required): Provide a brief description of your submission.
* **Submission Email** (`submission_email`, required): Enter a valid email address that will be used for contact purposes.
* **Submission Phone** (`submission_phone`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submission Category** (`category`, required): Select the category that best describes your submission (Association, Non-profit, or Local network).
* **Submission Address** (`submission_address`, required): Enter the address of your submission.
* **Submission Website** (`submission_website`, optional): Provide a link to your submission website.
* **Submission Image** (`submission_image`, optional): Upload or enter a URL for your submission image.
* **Submission Video** (`submission_video`, optional): Upload or enter a URL for your submission video.
* **Submission File** (`submission_file`, optional): Upload or enter a URL for your submission file.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, optional): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, optional): Enter the time of your submission.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, required): Select the status of your submission (Approved, Pending, or Declined).
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, optional): Provide a note for the reviewers.
* **Reviewer 1-10** (`submission_reviewer1-10`, optional): Add the names and contact information of up to 10 reviewers.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* Use the correct category for your submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for assistance.
