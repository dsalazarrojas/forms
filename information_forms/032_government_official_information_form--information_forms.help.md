# Government Official Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Official Information Form is designed to collect basic information from government officials for official purposes. This information may be used for identification, communication, or other official purposes.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field on page 1.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field on page 2.
3. Provide your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field on page 3.
4. Enter your official title in the "official_title" field on page 4.
5. Enter your official email address in the "official_email" field on page 5.
6. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field on page 6.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name. For example, John.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name. For example, Smith.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Official Title** (`official_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your official title or position. For example, Mayor or City Manager.
* **Official Email** (`official_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your official email address. This must be a valid email address in the format of "username@domain.com".
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact number for official communication. This can be a phone number or any other contact number.
