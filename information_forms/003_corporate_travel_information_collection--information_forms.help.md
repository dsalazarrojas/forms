# Corporate Travel Information Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about corporate travel, including company address, travel date, start and end times, travel destination, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required".
2. Enter your travel information as requested in the form.
3. Ensure you have a valid reason for your trip according to our travel policy.
4. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Address
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, `text`, required): Enter your company's address to confirm your travel's relation to work.
### Travel Date
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your trip.
### Start Time
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required): Enter the start time of your trip.
### End Time
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required): Enter the end time of your trip.
### Travel Destination
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, `text`, required): Enter the city, state, or country you will be visiting.
### Travel Reason
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`, `text`, required): Briefly explain the reason for your trip according to our company's travel policy.
### Travel Approver
* **Travel Approver** (`travel_approver`, `select_one`, optional): If you are traveling on company time, select "Yes" for approval.
### Approver Email
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your approver's email address.
### Company Name
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your company's name.
### Manager Name
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's name.
### Manager Email
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's email address.
### Department
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter the department you belong to.
### Approver Phone
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your approver's phone number.
### Travel Details
* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional travel details.
### Total Days
* **Total Days** (`total_days`, `number`, optional): Enter the total number of days you will be traveling.

## Tips
- Ensure you have a valid reason for your trip according to our company's travel policy.
- Check the required fields as these are essential for processing your travel request.
- If any field is missing or incorrect, your request may not be processed properly.
