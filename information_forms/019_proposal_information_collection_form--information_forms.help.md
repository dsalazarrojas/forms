# Proposal Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a proposal. It helps to gather essential details such as title, category, summary, proposal date, contact name, contact email, and contact phone number, as well as any attachments related to the proposal. The collected information will be used for internal purposes and will not be shared with external parties.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of your proposal in the **Summary** field.
2. Select the relevant **Category** from the provided options.
3. Enter a valid **Proposal Date**, using the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
4. Enter your **Contact Name**, as it will be displayed on the form.
5. Enter your **Contact Email**, which will be used for communication purposes.
6. Enter your **Contact Phone**, which will be used for phone calls or other forms of communication.
7. Attach any relevant documents or files to the **Attachment** field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Proposal Title**: Enter a brief and descriptive title for your proposal.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes the proposal. This will help us to filter and categorize the proposals.
* **Summary**: Provide a brief description of your proposal. This should be a short paragraph that gives an idea of what the proposal is about.
* **Proposal Date**: Enter the date on which you submitted or plan to submit your proposal. This date should be in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed on the form.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for phone calls or other forms of communication.
* **Attachment**: Attach any relevant documents or files to support your proposal. This field is optional but can help us to get a better understanding of your proposal.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
* If you have any attachments, make sure they are in a supported format (e.g. PDF, DOCX, JPG).
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* The collected information will be used for internal purposes and will not be shared with external parties.
