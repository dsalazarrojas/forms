# Reading Information Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Reading Information Request" form is used to collect information about a user's reading habits and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information in the first field.
2. Describe your reading background in the second field.
3. Select your reading frequency in the third field by choosing one of the available options.
4. Select your preferred format for reading materials in the fourth field by choosing all that apply.
5. Enter a valid email address in the fifth field.
6. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the sixth field.
7. Add any additional comments or information about your reading habits in the seventh field.
8. In the final field, provide a brief confirmation of your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required): Enter your name, username, or any other relevant identifier to associate with your submission.
  - This field is required to ensure that we can identify you as a user.
- **Reading Background** (`reading_background`, text, optional): Describe your reading experience, including any relevant education or training.
  - This field is optional, but it can help us better understand your reading background.
- **Reading Preferences** (`reading_preferences`, select_one, required): Select how often you read materials:
  - Reading 1-2 times a month
  - Reading 3-4 times a month
  - Reading 5 or more times a month
  - This field is required to understand your reading frequency.
- **Preferred Format** (`preferred_format`, select_multiple, required): Select all the formats you prefer for reading materials:
  - E-book
  - Audio book
  - Print book
  - This field is required to understand your preferred format.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
  - This field is required for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
  - This field is optional, but it can help us contact you if needed.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your reading habits.
  - This field is optional, but it can help us better understand your reading habits.
- **Submission Confirmation** (`submit_comment`, text, optional): Confirm that you have submitted your information accurately.
  - This field is optional, but it can help us verify that you have submitted your information correctly.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter a valid email address in the email field.
- If you have any special requests or preferences, use the additional comments field to express them.
- If you need help with anything, don't hesitate to contact us.
