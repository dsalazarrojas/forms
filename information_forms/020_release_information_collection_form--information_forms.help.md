# Release Information Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a software or product release. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to gather reliable data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the first name and last name fields with the relevant information. If you're unsure about the correct first or last name, try to fill out as much as you can.
2. Enter the name of the organization that is associated with the release.
3. Once you have completed the above fields, review the information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the organization that is associated with the release. If you're not sure, try to enter the most accurate information available.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form to ensure reliable data collection.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
