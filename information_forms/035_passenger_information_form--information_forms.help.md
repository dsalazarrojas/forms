# Passenger Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details from passengers to facilitate their travel plans. Please provide accurate and relevant information to ensure smooth processing of your travel documents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the field marked "First Name". This is a required field where you should enter your full first name.
2. In the "Last Name" field, enter your surname or family name.
3. Enter your official email address in the "Email" field.
4. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field for contact purposes.
5. If you have a Travel Document ID, enter it in the corresponding field.
6. Choose your Nationality from the drop-down list in the "Nationality" field.
7. Enter your travel date in the "Travel Date" field in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
8. Select your travel time from the available options in the "Travel Time" field.
9. Choose your arrival time from the available options in the "Arrival Time" field.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
11. Select the travel agent handling your travel from the list in the "Travel Agent" field.
12. Enter the contact number for any additional contact in the "Contact Number" field.
13. Enter your address for contact purposes in the "Address" field.
14. Choose your country of origin from the drop-down list in the "Country" field.
15. Select your state of origin from the drop-down list in the "State" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your full first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your full last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Travel Document ID** (`travel_document_id`, text, optional): If you have a travel document ID, enter it here.
- **Nationality** (`nationality`, select_one, optional): Choose your nationality from the available options.
- **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, optional): Enter your travel date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
- **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, time, optional): Choose your travel time from the available options.
- **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, optional): Choose your arrival time from the available options.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your travel.
- **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, select_multiple, optional): Select the travel agent handling your travel from the list.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number for any additional contact.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for contact purposes.
- **Country** (`country`, select_one, optional): Choose your country of origin from the available options.
- **State** (`state`, select_one, optional): Choose your state of origin from the available options.
