# Choices's Pregnancy Center Patient Information - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect patient information for the Choices's Pregnancy Center. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and truthfully to ensure that you receive the best possible care and support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for help or clarification.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please fill out your complete name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Enter your age in years.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Has pregnancy been confirmed?** (`pregnancy_confirmed`, `select_one`, required): Choose 'Yes' if the pregnancy has been confirmed, 'No' if not, or 'Unsure' if you are unsure.
* **Date of Last Menstrual Period** (`date_of_last_period`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your last menstrual period.
* **Estimated Due Date** (`estimated_due_date`, `date`, optional): Enter your estimated due date if known.
* **Current Pregnancy Trimester** (`pregnancy_trimester`, `select_one`, required): Choose the current trimester of your pregnancy.
* **Number of Previous Pregnancies** (`number_of_pregnancies`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of previous pregnancies you have had.
* **Number of Living Children** (`number_of_children`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of living children you have.
* **Current Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): List any current medical conditions you have.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, optional): List any current medications you are taking.
* **Known Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): List any known allergies you have.
* **Are you receiving prenatal care?** (`receiving_prenatal_care`, `select_one`, required): Choose 'Yes' if you are receiving prenatal care, 'No' if not, or 'Planning to start' if you are planning to start.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your healthcare provider (OB/GYN or midwife).

## Tips

1. Double-check all fields before submitting the form.
2. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for help or clarification.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
