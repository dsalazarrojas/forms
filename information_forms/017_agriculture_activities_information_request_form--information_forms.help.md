# Agriculture Activities Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather information from users about their specific needs for agricultural activities, such as crop production, livestock, training, input supply, or research. This information will help provide targeted and relevant responses to their queries.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the Requester name field with your personal details.
*   Provide your organization or department name (if applicable) in the Requester organization field.
*   Enter your contact email address in the Contact email field.
*   Select the activity type that best describes your request from the options provided.
*   Specify the area or districts covered by your request in the Geographic scope field.
*   Enter the desired time frame for the information in the Time frame for information field.
*   Explain the reason for collecting the information in the Purpose of request field.
*   Choose your preferred data format from the options provided.
*   Indicate the level of urgency you require for the information in the Urgency level field.
*   Add any additional notes or specifics to help fulfill your request in the Additional notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Requester name**: Enter your name, which will be used to identify you as the requester.
*   **Requester organization (optional)**: If you are representing an organization or department, enter the name here.
*   **Contact email**: This is the email address where you will receive the requested information.
*   **Activity type**: Select the type of activity you are inquiring about (Crop production, Livestock, Training event, Input supply, or Research).
*   **Geographic scope**: Specify the area or districts covered by your request (e.g., country, state, or region).
*   **Time frame for information**: Enter the dates or periods for when you need the information.
*   **Purpose of request**: Clearly explain the reason for collecting the information (e.g., to improve practices, for a specific project, or other legitimate reasons).
*   **Preferred data format**: Select the format in which you would like to receive the information (CSV, PDF, Summary report, or Other).
*   **Urgency level**: Indicate how quickly you need the information (Low, Medium, or High).
*   **Additional notes**: Provide any further details or specifics to help the provider fulfill your request.

## Tips

*   Please fill in all required fields accurately to ensure the best possible response.
*   Be as specific as possible when describing your needs, as this will help the provider provide targeted information.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
