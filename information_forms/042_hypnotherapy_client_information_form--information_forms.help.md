# Hypnotherapy Client Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hypnotherapy Client Information Form is a tool used to collect essential information from clients about their hypnotherapy session. This form is designed to be completed by the client to provide accurate and relevant details about themselves, which will help the hypnotherapy practitioner tailor their session to meet the client's specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields, which are marked as **required**. This is essential to ensure that we have accurate information about you.
2. Enter your personal details in the provided fields. For fields like Email and Phone, please enter the correct format (e.g., `example@gmail.com` for an email address).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (<basic_info>):**
	+ Label: Client Information
	+ Type: Text Field
	+ Description: Please provide a brief description about yourself, including your name, age, and any other relevant information.
* **Address (<basic_info_address>):**
	+ Label: Address
	+ Type: Text Field
	+ Description: Enter your current address to help us get in touch with you.
* **Email (<basic_info_email>):**
	+ Label: Email
	+ Type: Email Field
	+ Description: Enter your email address, which will be used to communicate with you about your sessions.
* **Phone (<basic_info_phone>):**
	+ Label: Phone
	+ Type: Text Field
	+ Description: Provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us for clarification.
* We appreciate your cooperation in providing us with accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the best possible experience for your hypnotherapy sessions.
