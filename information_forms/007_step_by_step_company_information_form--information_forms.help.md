# step_by_step_company_information_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect company information in a step-by-step manner. It is intended for users to provide detailed information about their company, including its name, address, email, phone number, and more. The form is structured to help users navigate through each step, ensuring they provide accurate and comprehensive information about their company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start at Step 1: Form Header, where you can enter a brief title for your company.
2. Proceed to Step 2: Company Name, where you can enter your company's name.
3. Continue to Step 3: Address, where you can enter your company's address.
4. Move on to Step 4: City, where you can enter your city of operation.
5. At Step 5: State, choose the state where your company is located.
6. In Step 6: Zip Code, enter your company's zip code.
7. Step 7: Email is where you can enter your company's email address.
8. Move on to Step 8: Phone, where you can enter your company's phone number.
9. At Step 9: Company Description, describe your company's purpose and business activities.
10. In Step 10: Type of Company, select the type of company you are (e.g., Yes/No).
11. Step 11: Number of Employees, enter the number of employees working for your company.
12. Continue to Step 12: Employee Diversity, select the diversity level of your employees.
13. At Step 13: Industry, choose the industry your company operates in.
14. In Step 14: Company Website, enter your company's website address (if applicable).
15. Step 15: Submit, review and submit your form.
16. Note: You can also add any additional comments or notes in Step 16: Notes.
17. Step 17: Follow Up, select how you would like to be followed up (e.g., Yes/No).
18. At Step 18: Other Inquiries, select any additional inquiries you have.
19. In Step 19: General Inquiries, select general inquiries you have.
20. Step 20: Other Inquiries, select other inquiries you have.
21. Step 21: Collaboration Interest, select your collaboration interest (e.g., Yes/No).
22. At Step 22: Partnership Interest, select your partnership interest (e.g., Yes/No).
23. In Step 23: Service Requests, select any service requests you have.
24. Step 23: Other, select any other inquiries you have.
25. Step 24: Form Submitted, enter the date and time you submitted the form.
26. At Step 25: Form Submitting Device, select the device you used to submit the form (e.g., Yes/No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Step 1 - Form Header**: Enter a brief title for your company that will appear on the form header.
* **Step 2 - Company Name**: Enter your company's name exactly as it is registered.
* **Step 3 - Address**: Enter your company's street address.
* **Step 4 - City**: Enter the city where your company is located.
* **Step 5 - State**: Choose the state where your company operates.
* **Step 6 - Zip Code**: Enter your company's zip code.
* **Step 7 - Email**: Enter your company's email address.
* **Step 8 - Phone**: Enter your company's phone number.
* **Step 9 - Company Description**: Describe your company's purpose and business activities in 1-2 sentences.
* **Step 10 - Type of Company**: Select the type of company you are (e.g., Yes/No).
* **Step 11 - Number of Employees**: Enter the number of employees working for your company.
* **Step 12 - Employee Diversity**: Select the diversity level of your employees.
* **Step 13 - Industry**: Choose the industry your company operates in.
* **Step 14 - Company Website**: Enter your company's website address (if applicable).
* **Step 15 - Submit**: Review and submit your form.
* **Step 16 - Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your company.
* **Step 17 - Follow Up**: Select how you would like to be followed up (e.g., Yes/No).
* **Step 18 - Other Inquiries**: Select any additional inquiries you have.
* **Step 19 - General Inquiries**: Select general inquiries you have.
* **Step 20 - Other Inquiries**: Select other inquiries you have.
* **Step 21 - Collaboration Interest**: Select your collaboration interest (e.g., Yes/No).
* **Step 22 - Partnership Interest**: Select your partnership interest (e.g., Yes/No).
* **Step 23 - Service Requests**: Select any service requests you have.
* **Step 24 - Form Submitted**: Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
* **Step 25 - Form Submitting Device**: Select the device you used to submit the form (e.g., Yes/No).

Note: This form requires no technical knowledge of YAML or any programming skills.
