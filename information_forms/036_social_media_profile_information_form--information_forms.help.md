# Social Media Profile Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Profile Information Form is designed to collect social media profile information for internal purposes, such as tracking and managing social media accounts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the basic information section with your name, social media platform, and account name.
2. Provide the account link (e.g., URL) for the social media profile.
3. If applicable, provide a profile picture URL or upload a profile picture.
4. Add any relevant bio information, such as a brief description of your account.
5. Add any relevant links to your social media profile.
6. If you have a publication or update history, provide the date and time of the last update.
7. Optionally, add any additional information, such as a phone number, email, or account owner details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Basic Info**: Enter your basic information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Social Media**: Select the social media platform your account is on (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn).
* **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information, such as a phone number or email.
* **Account Type**: Select the type of social media account (e.g., None, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn).
* **Account Name**: Enter the name of your social media account.
* **Account Link**: Enter the URL or link to your social media profile.
* **Account Type Description**: Provide a brief description of your social media account type.
* **Profile Picture**: Optionally, upload or provide a URL for your profile picture.
* **Profile Picture URL**: Provide the URL of your profile picture.
* **Profile Picture URL Type**: Select the type of profile picture URL (e.g., External URL, Upload).
* **Bio**: Enter a brief description of your social media profile.
* **Profile Links**: Add any relevant links to your social media profile.
* **Bio Publication**: Provide the date and time of the last publication or update.
* **Bio Updated**: Provide the date and time of the last update.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Account Created**: Provide the date and time of account creation.
* **Account Status**: Select the status of your social media account (e.g., Active, Inactive, Deleted, Suspended).
* **Account Last Updated**: Provide the date and time of the last update.
* **Account Last Updated By**: Enter the person who last updated your social media profile.
* **Account Owner**: Enter the owner of your social media profile.
* **Account Owner Email**: Enter the email address of the account owner.
* **Account Notes**: Add any additional notes about your social media profile.
* **Account Last Note**: Provide the date and time of the last note added to your social media profile.
