# Background Information Sheet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Background Information Sheet Form is designed to collect background information from individuals for various applications such as employment, service, membership, loan, or rental purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
3. Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name as per your identification documents.
* **Preferred Name** (`preferred_name`, `text`, optional): If you prefer to be known by a different name, enter it here.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select your marital status from the provided options.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, `text`, required): Enter your current full street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Enter the country of your residence.
* **Years at Address** (`years_at_address`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of years you have lived at your current address.
* **Home Phone Number** (`phone_home`, `text`, required): Enter your home phone number.
* **Mobile Phone Number** (`phone_mobile`, `text`, required): Enter your mobile phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, `text`, required): Enter the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Current Employer** (`current_employer`, `text`, optional): Enter your current employer's name if applicable.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title or profession if applicable.
* **Annual Income Range** (`annual_income`, `select_one`, optional): Select the annual income range from the provided options.
* **Number of Dependents** (`dependents`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of dependents you have if applicable.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select your highest education level from the provided options.
* **Languages Spoken** (`languages_spoken`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all languages you can speak from the provided options.
* **Purpose of Form** (`purpose_of_form`, `select_one`, required): Select the purpose of this information sheet from the provided options.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Enter any other relevant details that may be helpful.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification or assistance if necessary.
