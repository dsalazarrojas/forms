# Employee Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Contact Information Form is used to collect and update employee contact information for HR purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Be sure to enter your correct information.
3.  Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (text, required/optional): Please enter your first name here.
- **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Please enter your last name here.
- **Email** (email, required/optional): Please enter your work email address.
- **Phone** (text, required/optional): Please enter your work phone number.
- **Date of Birth** (date, required/optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Job Title** (text, required/optional): Please enter your current job title.
- **Employee ID** (number, required/optional): Please enter your employee ID number.
- **Department** (select_one, required/optional): Please select your department from the list (Engineering, Finance, or HR).
- **Manager ID** (number, required/optional): Please enter your manager's ID number.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (text, required/optional): Please enter the name of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, required/optional): Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Email** (email, required/optional): Please enter the email address of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Relation** (text, required/optional): Please enter your relationship with your emergency contact.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you are entering accurate and up-to-date information.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your HR representative for assistance.
*   The form is meant to be used for routine updates and changes in employee information.
