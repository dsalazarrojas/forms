The purpose of this form is to allow individuals to apply for membership in the Learning Equity Research Network (LERN), a platform that aims to connect researchers and organizations dedicated to advancing equity-focused research. By filling out this form, applicants will be able to provide background information, declare their research interests and participation level, indicate their university and nonprofit affiliations, and specify their team's research focus. This application will help the LERN team assess the eligibility and suitability of applicants for membership.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Member Application section with a brief description of your background and research interests.
2. Answer the background information questions to give us a better understanding of your research background.
3. In the Research Interests section, select the relevant research interests that apply to you.
4. Choose your level of participation in the research network, such as Regular Member, Affiliate Member, or Observer Member.
5. Indicate your university and nonprofit affiliations, if applicable.
6. Specify your team's research focus, if you are applying as a team.
7. Finally, provide any additional research interests you have that are not listed in the previous section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Member Application
* **Member Application (Text)**: This is the main description of your background and research interests. Please provide a brief overview of your education, research experience, and relevant skills or expertise. (~50-100 words)

### Background Information
* **Background (Text)**: This section is used to provide more detailed background information about you and your research experience. Please include any relevant education, research experience, or professional certifications. (~100-200 words)

### Research Interests
* **Research Interests (Select)**: Select the research interests that apply to you. Research interests may include, but are not limited to, equity-focused research, educational policy, community engagement, and data analysis. 
  * Yes: Indicates that this research interest is relevant to your work
  * No: Indicates that this research interest is not relevant to your work

### Member Participation
* **Member Participation (Select)**: Choose your level of participation in the research network. 
  * Regular Member: You have a strong connection to the research network and would like to be actively involved in discussions and collaborations.
  * Affiliate Member: You are interested in participating in the network, but not to the extent of a regular member.
  * Observer Member: You are interested in observing the network and learning from its discussions, but not actively participating.

### University Affiliation
* **University Affiliation (Select)**: Indicate if you are affiliated with a university. 
  * Yes: You have a formal relationship with a university, such as being a faculty member or researcher.
  * No: You do not have a formal relationship with a university

### Nonprofit Affiliation
* **Nonprofit Affiliation (Select)**: Indicate if you are affiliated with a nonprofit organization. 
  * Yes: You have a formal relationship with a nonprofit organization, such as being a researcher or employee.
  * No: You do not have a formal relationship with a nonprofit organization

### Team Research Focus
* **Team Research Focus (Select)**: If you are applying as a team, select the research focus of your team. 
  * Yes: This research focus is relevant to your team
  * No: This research focus is not relevant to your team

### Additional Research Interests
* **Additional Research Interests (Text)**: This section is for any additional research interests you have that are not listed in the Research Interests section.
