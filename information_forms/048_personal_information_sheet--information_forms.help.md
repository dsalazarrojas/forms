# personal_information_sheet - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form collects personal details from a user for a variety of purposes, including creating a profile, conducting demographic surveys, or updating contact information. The form includes both general and specific details about the user.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by entering your **Personal Information Sheet** title in the **form_title** field.
2.  Enter your **name** in the **name** field.
3.  Provide your **date_of_birth** in the **date_of_birth** field.
4.  Enter your **address** in the **address** field.
5.  Enter your **city** in the **city** field.
6.  Choose your **state** from the list of options provided.
7.  Enter your **zip** in the **zip** field.
8.  Provide your **email** in the **email** field.
9.  Enter your **phone** in the **phone** field.
10.  Enter your **emergency contact's name** in the **emergency_contact_name** field.
11.  Choose your relationship with the emergency contact in the **emergency_contact_relationship** field.
12.  Enter the emergency contact's **phone** in the **emergency_contact_phone** field.
11.  Enter the emergency contact's **address** in the **emergency_contact_address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_title**: A field to input a title for the form. This field is optional.
* **name**: A field to input your name. This field is optional.
* **date_of_birth**: A field to input your date of birth.
* **address**: A field to input your address.
* **city**: A field to input your city.
* **state**: A field to choose your state from the list of options.
* **zip**: A field to input your zip code.
* **email**: A field to input your email address.
* **phone**: A field to input your phone number.
* **emergency_contact_name**: A field to input your emergency contact's name.
* **emergency_contact_relationship**: A field to choose your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_phone**: A field to input the emergency contact's phone number.
* **emergency_contact_address**: A field to input the emergency contact's address.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in all fields.
* Double-check the fields that require selection options for correctness.
* In fields like date_of_birth, address, and emergency_contact_address, try to provide as much detail as possible.
* In fields like phone and emergency_contact_phone, ensure that the correct format is used to ensure correct input.
