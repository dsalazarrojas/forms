# personal_information_sheet_sample - Help Guide
## Purpose
The personal information sheet is a form used to collect and document personal details of an individual. This form is used to gather information for administrative and insurance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Address in the respective fields.
- Enter your Date of Birth in the format of MM/DD/YY or YYYY-MM-DD.
- If applicable, select your Insurance Provider and Policy Status.
- If applicable, enter your Policy Number, Policy Start Date, and Policy End Date.
- If applicable, select the Policy Type and Policy Duration.
- Select your Policy Status and Policy Type.
- Enter your Address Line 1 and Zip.
- Select your State.
- Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`personal_info_1`, text, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`personal_info_2`, text, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Address** (`address_1`, text, required): Enter your full address in this field.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY or YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Email** (`email_1`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you have one. This field is not required.
* **Phone** (`phone_1`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one. This field is not required.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' if you have insurance coverage.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, optional): Enter your policy number if you have one. This field is not required.
* **Policy Start Date** (`policy_start_date`, date, optional): Enter your policy start date if you have one. This field is not required.
* **Policy End Date** (`policy_end_date`, date, optional): Enter your policy end date if you have one. This field is not required.
* **Policy Status** (`policy_status`, select_one, required): Select your policy status as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, select_one, required): Select your policy type as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Policy Duration** (`policy_duration`, number, required): Enter the number of months or years you've had the policy.
* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`, select_one, optional): Select your insurance company if you have one. This field is not required.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, required): Enter your first line of your address.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, text, optional): Enter your second line of your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city name. This field is not required.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required): Select your state.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Policy Coverage** (`policy_coverage`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of coverage you have. 
* **Premium Paid** (`premium_paid`, number, optional): Enter the amount of premium paid. This field is not required.
* **Claims Made** (`claims_made`, number, optional): Enter the number of claims made. This field is not required.
* **Policy Limit** (`policy_limit`, number, optional): Enter the policy limit. This field is not required.
* **Claims Paid** (`claims_paid`, number, optional): Enter the amount of claims paid. This field is not required.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes. This field is not required.

This help guide is designed to be used by individuals filling out the personal information sheet form.
