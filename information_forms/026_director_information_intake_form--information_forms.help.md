# Director Information Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and professional details of a director, including their name, contact information, and job title.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review and confirm the entered details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Director Name** (`director_name`, text, optional): Enter the director's name.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the director's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the director's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the director's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the director's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the director's date of birth.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select one, optional): Choose the director's job title.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the director's department.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter the director's company address.
* **Job Title Description** (`job_title_description`, note, optional): Enter a brief description of the director's job title.
* **Director Photo** (`director_photo`, note, optional): Upload a photo of the director.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, note, optional): Add any additional details about the director.
* **Job Title Additional Details** (`job_title_additional_details`, note, optional): Add any additional details about the director's job title.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the director's manager.
* **Manager Name**: This field is not actually defined in the provided YAML, but assuming it would be a text field similar to other text fields.
