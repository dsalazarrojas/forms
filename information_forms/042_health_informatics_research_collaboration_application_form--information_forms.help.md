# health_informatics_research_collaboration_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health informatics research collaboration application form is designed to collect information from researchers and collaborators for health informatics research projects. This form helps to streamline the application process and ensure that all necessary information is collected in one place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*).
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. If you have already provided similar information in the past, do not duplicate it. Instead, provide any updated or new information that is not already on file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Institution**: Enter the name of the research institution you are affiliated with.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or business card.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person for your project.
* **Role**: Indicate your role in the project (e.g., PI, Co-PI, Researcher, etc.).
* **Department**: Enter the name of the department where you are affiliated with.
* **Project Title**: Enter a brief title of your health informatics research project.
* **Research Collaboration**: Describe the research collaboration you are seeking (e.g., with which institutions, individuals, or companies).
* **Start Date**: Enter the date when the project started (if applicable).
* **End Date**: Enter the date when the project ended (if applicable).
* **Collaboration Start Date**: Enter the date when the collaboration started.
* **Collaboration End Date**: Enter the date when the collaboration ended.
* **Funding Source**: Indicate the source of funding for your project (e.g., government, industry, other).
* **Collaboration Funding**: If your project is funded, indicate the source(s) of funding (e.g., government, industry, other).
* **Project Description**: Briefly describe your project.
* **Collaboration Description**: Describe the collaboration you are seeking (e.g., with which institutions, individuals, or companies).
* **Technical Requirements**: List any technical requirements for your project (e.g., software, hardware, etc.).
* **Collaboration Technical Requirements**: Describe any technical requirements for the collaboration.
* **Personnel**: Indicate the roles of personnel involved in your project (e.g., PI, Co-PI, Researcher, etc.).
* **Collaborators**: Indicate whether you are collaborating with other institutions or individuals (yes/no).
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for your project.
* **Contact Role**: Describe the role of the contact person (e.g., PI, Co-PI, Researcher, etc.).
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Address**: Enter the physical address of the contact person.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about your project.

Note: Some fields may not be required, but it is recommended to fill out as much information as possible to ensure a complete understanding of your project.
