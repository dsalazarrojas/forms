# Speaker Disclosure And Conflict Of Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a required part of the speaker application process to ensure that all speakers disclose their affiliation with an organization and any potential conflicts of interest.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). 
2. Choose the correct affiliation(s) for each question.
3. Indicate whether you have a financial relationship with any of the organizations listed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`): Enter your full name as it will appear in the speaker list.
* **Speaker Position** (`speaker_position`): Enter your position within your organization.
* **Speaker Organization** (`speaker_organization`): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Affiliation 1** (`affiliation_1`): Select the type of organization you represent, such as Academia or Non-profit.
* **Affiliation 2** (`affiliation_2`): Select a secondary type of organization if applicable.
* **Affiliation 3** (`affiliation_3`): Select multiple types of organizations if applicable (you may choose more than one).
* **Have financial relationship with organization?** (`has_financial_relationship`): Answer 'Yes' if you receive any form of compensation or benefits from the organization.
* **Financial Relationship** (`financial_relationship`): If you answered 'Yes', choose the type of financial relationship you have with the organization.
* **Financial Value** (`financial_value`): Enter the value of the financial relationship.
* **Do you have any financial conflicts of interest?** (`conflict`): Answer 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Type** (`conflict_type`): If you answered 'Yes', choose the type of conflict of interest you have.
* **Details** (`conflict_details`): Enter the details of the conflict of interest.
* **Additional Comments** (`speaker_disclosure`): Enter any additional comments or information you want to disclose.
* **Terms of Service** (`terms_of_service`): Enter the date you signed the terms of service.
* **Speaker Disclosure Agreement** (`speaker_disclosure_agreement`): Enter the date you signed the speaker disclosure agreement.
* **Speaker Contact Information** (`speaker_contact`): Enter your contact information for communication purposes.
* **Speaker Organization** (`speaker_organization`): Enter the name of your organization (this field is optional).
* **Speaker Address** (`speaker_address`): Enter your address.
* **Speaker Phone** (`speaker_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Speaker Email** (`speaker_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Speaker Disclosure Form Submission** (`speaker_disclosure_form_submission_agreement`): Enter your signature and date of your submission of this form. 

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out all required fields. If you need help or have questions, contact the event organizers for assistance.
