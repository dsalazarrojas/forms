# online_debt_collection_placement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help collect and manage debt collection information for customers. Please complete this form to provide accurate and complete data for each customer's debt collection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all the required fields marked as "required" in each section. These are the fields with an asterisks (\*) symbol next to them.
2. Provide accurate and detailed information for each field to ensure the collected data is reliable.
3. Review and verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Info
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the customer's debt collection.

### 2. Debt Info
* **Debt Amount** (`debt_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of debt owed by the customer.
* **Debt Info** (`debt_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional debt information.

### 3. Payment Terms
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Enter any payment terms or conditions associated with the debt collection.

### 4. Collection Status
* **Collection Status** (`collection_status`, text, optional): Enter the current collection status of the debt.

### 5. Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant contact information for the customer.

### 6. Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select the payment method(s) associated with the debt collection. Options include:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Cash
	+ Bank Draft
	+ Wire Transfer
	+ Check

### 7. Payment Date
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the payment.

### 8. Collection Notes
* **Collection Notes** (`collection_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the collection process.

### 9. Collection Agent
* **Collection Agent** (`collection_agent`, select_one, optional): Select the collection agent associated with the debt collection. Options include:
	+ John Doe
	+ Jane Doe
	+ Bob Smith

### 10. Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Enter the tool assigned to the debt collection.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review and verify the information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the collection team for assistance.
