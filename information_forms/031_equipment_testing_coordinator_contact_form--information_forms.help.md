# equipment_testing_coordinator_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from the equipment testing coordinator. It's intended for general contact and coordination purposes. If you're unsure about the purpose or potential overlap with existing forms, please check with your team or supervisors before using this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information:
	* Provide your name and title as the equipment testing coordinator.
	* Enter your email address for easy communication.
	* Enter your phone number for faster response times.
2. Add details about the equipment:
	* Enter the department where the equipment is located or being tested.
	* Describe the position or location of the equipment.
	* Provide any additional information about the equipment being tested.
3. Set the testing schedule:
	* Choose the date and time when the equipment will be tested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Coordinator Name** (`equipment_testing_coordinator_name`): Enter your name and title as the equipment testing coordinator.
* **Coordinator E-mail** (`equipment_testing_coordinator_email`): Enter your email address for easy communication.
* **Coordinator Phone** (`equipment_testing_coordinator_phone`): Enter your phone number for faster response times.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter the department where the equipment is located or being tested.
* **Position** (`equipment_testing_coordinator_position`): Describe the position or location of the equipment.
* **Equipment to be Tested** (`equipment_tested`): Provide any additional information about the equipment being tested.
* **Description** (`equipment_testing_description`): Add a brief description of the equipment.
* **Priority** (`equipment_testing_priority`): Set the priority level of the testing (if applicable).
* **Tested By** (`tested_by`): Choose the person who tested the equipment (Equipment Technician, Maintenance Person, or Other).
* **Testing Date** (`test_equipment_date`): Select the date when the equipment will be tested.
* **Testing Time** (`test_equipment_time`): Select the time of day when the equipment will be tested.

## Tips
* Double-check that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
