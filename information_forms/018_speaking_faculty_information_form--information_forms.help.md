# Speaking Faculty Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaking Faculty Information Form is a data collection tool used by organizers to gather essential information about a speaking event and its participants. It helps ensure accurate and efficient planning and execution of events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Event Title** field, which should clearly describe the event title and topic.
2. Provide your **Speaker Name**, which will be used as a reference for your presentation.
3. Enter the **Presentation Title**, which should be a brief and descriptive title of your presentation.
4. Add a detailed **Presentation Description** that summarizes the main points of your presentation.
5. Enter your **Speaker Contact E-mail**, which should be a valid contact email address for any communication with you.
6. Fill out your **Speaker Phone**, if applicable, to ensure we can reach you for any urgent matters.
7. Provide your **Speaker Address**, which will be used for logistical purposes during the event.
8. Enter the **Event Date**, which should match the actual date of the event.
9. Specify the **Event Start Time** and **Event End Time** of the event.
10. Fill out the **Event Venue**, where the event will be held.
11. Provide the **Event Location** details, which may include the building or room name.
12. Add a brief **Speaker Biography**, which will be displayed in the event materials and marketing materials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title**: The title of the event, e.g., "Annual Conference 2024".
* **Speaker Name**: The name of the speaker, e.g., "John Doe".
* **Presentation Title**: A brief and descriptive title of your presentation, e.g., "Machine Learning Applications in Business".
* **Presentation Description**: A detailed description of your presentation, including the main points and topics covered.
* **Speaker Contact E-mail**: A valid contact email address for any communication with the speaker.
* **Speaker Phone**: A contact phone number, optional and only used for urgent matters.
* **Speaker Address**: The speaker's address, used for logistical purposes during the event.
* **Event Date**: The actual date of the event, in YYYY-MM-DD format, e.g., "2024-03-01".
* **Event Start Time**: The start time of the event, in 24-hour format, e.g., "09:00".
* **Event End Time**: The end time of the event, in 24-hour format, e.g., "10:00".
* **Event Venue**: The location where the event will be held.
* **Event Location**: The building or room where the event will take place.
* **Speaker Biography**: A brief bio of the speaker, to be displayed in event materials and marketing materials.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields (indicated by *) are completed accurately.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form to avoid mistakes.
* Use a valid email address and phone number for contact purposes.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the event organizers for clarification.
* Double-check the event date, time, and location to ensure accuracy.
