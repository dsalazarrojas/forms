# # landlord_and_rental_property_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "landlord_and_rental_property_information_form" is designed to gather information about rental properties to facilitate efficient and accurate processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Property Details**: Provide a description of the rental property, including its address, features, and any other relevant details.
2. **Lease Terms**: Enter the lease terms and conditions of the rental property.
3. **Property Location**: Enter the location of the rental property.
4. **Property Status**: Select the current status of the property (Rent, Own, Vacant, Held for sale, or Held for rent).
5. **Rent Details**: Enter any additional details about the rent, such as the rent amount or payment terms.
6. **Tenant Information**: Provide any information about the tenants, including their names, contact details, and other relevant details.
7. **Lease Start Date**: Enter the start date of the lease.
8. **Lease End Date**: Enter the end date of the lease.
9. **Landlord Information**: Enter any additional information about the landlord, such as their contact details or property management company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Details**: Enter a description of the rental property to help us understand its features and characteristics.
* **Lease Terms**: Describe the lease terms and conditions, such as lease duration, rent amount, and payment methods.
* **Property Location**: Enter the address or location of the rental property to help us identify it accurately.
* **Property Status**: Select the current status of the property (e.g., Rent, Own, Vacant, Held for sale, or Held for rent).
* **Rent Details**: Enter any additional details about the rent, such as the rent amount or payment terms.
* **Tenant Information**: Provide any information about the tenants, including their names, contact details, and other relevant details.
* **Lease Start Date**: Enter the start date of the lease.
* **Lease End Date**: Enter the end date of the lease.
* **Landlord Information**: Enter any additional information about the landlord, such as their contact details or property management company.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, feel free to ask.
* This form is designed to be used for information gathering purposes only. If you need to submit any other type of request, please use the corresponding form.
