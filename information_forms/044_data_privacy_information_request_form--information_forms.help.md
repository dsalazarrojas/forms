# data_privacy_information_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users to help us fulfill their data privacy requests. It's a crucial step in ensuring that we handle your personal data with care and respect your rights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Organization Request on the first page.
2. Provide your contact details (Phone or Email) on the second page.
3. Describe the purpose of your data request on the third page.
4. Upload any supporting documents on the fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Request** (`organization_request`, text, Optional): Enter the name of your organization or entity related to your data request. This will help us understand the context of your request.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, select_one, Required): Choose how you would like to be contacted for further information or updates about your data request. You can select either Phone or Email.
* **Request Description** (`request_description`, text, Optional): Please provide a clear and concise description of the data you're requesting access to. This will help us process your request efficiently.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_multiple, Optional): If you have any supporting documents that relate to your data request, you can upload them here. Please select all relevant documents. 

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
