# Fan Data Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fan Data Information Collection Form is designed to collect essential details about a fan or artist. This form will help us understand more about our audience and provide a better experience for our community.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field in the order provided.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by a *next to the field label.
3. Use the available options for select fields, such as fan type and segment.
4. Enter valid email addresses and phone numbers where applicable.
5. Enter a valid date of birth for the date_of_birth field.
6. For the social media platform, select the correct platform from the options provided.
7. For the platform_url and website fields, enter the URL of the social media platform or website in question.
8. Enter any additional details in the other_details field for any extra information not covered in other fields.
9. Finally, fill out the submitter field with your name and any necessary contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, *): 
  Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or public records.

* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, *): 
  Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or public records.

* **Email** (`email`, email, *): 
  Enter a valid email address that you use for communication.

* **Fan Type** (`fan_type`, select_multiple, ): 
  Select one or more types of fan, such as Fan, Artist, Organizer, or Other.

* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, *): 
  Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, ): 
  Enter your contact phone number if you would like to be contacted.

* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, ): 
  Enter a valid email address to be contacted at.

* **Artist Manager** (`artist_manager`, text, ): 
  Enter the name of your artist manager if you have one.

* **Team Member** (`team_member`, text, ): 
  Enter your team name if you are a team member of an artist.

* **Other Details** (`other_details`, note, ): 
  Add any additional details about yourself not covered in other fields.

* **Social Media Platform** (`social_media_platform`, select_one, ): 
  Select the social media platform you are most active on, such as Instagram, Facebook, or Twitter.

* **Platform URL** (`platform_url`, text, ): 
  Enter the URL of your social media platform profile.

* **Website** (`website`, text, ): 
  Enter the URL of your personal or artist website.

* **Segment** (`segment`, select_one, ): 
  Select the segment you are most associated with, such as General, Artist, Fan, or Team.

* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, *): 
  Enter your name and any necessary contact information for communication purposes.
