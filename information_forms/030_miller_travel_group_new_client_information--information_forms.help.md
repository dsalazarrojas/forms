# miller_travel_group_new_client_information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "miller_travel_group_new_client_information" form is used to collect new client information for the Miller Travel Group. This includes the client's name, travel agent information, email, phone number, and address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "client_name" field.
2. Enter the travel agent's information in the "travel_agent" field.
3. Enter the client's email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter the client's phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter the client's address in the "address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **client_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the client's full name.
* **travel_agent** (Text, Optional): Enter the travel agent's name or contact information.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **address** (Text, Optional): Enter the client's address.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the client.
* Double-check the information before submitting it to ensure it is correct.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
