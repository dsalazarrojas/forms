# Employment Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employment-related information from individuals or organizations. It can be used for job search, recruitment, or human resources purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. Fill in the Company Name field with the name of the company you're representing or the one you're applying to.
3. Enter the name of the contact person in the Contact Person field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name (Company Name)**: Enter the name of the company you're representing or the one you're applying to.
* **Contact Person (contact_person)**: Enter the name of the contact person for the company or the person submitting the form.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you provide.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the form's purpose and context to ensure you're filling it in correctly.
* If you need to modify the form for your specific use case, you can adjust the fields and labels according to your organization's procedures.
