# Valuation Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Valuation Information Form is designed to collect information related to property valuation, including property address, client details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required and optional fields filled in.
2. Be accurate and thorough in your responses, as this information will be used to provide the best possible service.
3. If a field is not required, you can skip it.
4. If a field has a dropdown menu or select options, make sure to select the correct option that best matches your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property in question.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client or the person filling out this form.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property you are dealing with (Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
* **Property Use**: Select the intended use of the property (Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Mixed Use, Other).
* **Valuation Date**: Enter the date of the valuation, if applicable.
* **Valuation Time**: Enter the time of the valuation, if applicable.
* **Valuation Amount**: Enter the amount of the valuation, if applicable.
* **Client Contact**: Enter the email of the client or the person filling out this form.
* **Client Phone**: Enter the phone number of the client or the person filling out this form.
* **Client Email**: Enter the email of the client or the person filling out this form.
* **Property Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the property.
* **Client Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the client.
* **Insurance Company**: Select the insurance company handling the valuation (Company A, Company B, Company C).
* **Adjuster Name**: Enter the name of the adjuster handling the valuation.
* **Adjuster Phone**: Enter the phone number of the adjuster handling the valuation.
* **Adjuster Email**: Enter the email of the adjuster handling the valuation.
* **Adjuster Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the adjuster.
* **Valuation Method**: Select the method used for the valuation (Manual, Automated).
* **Valuation Frequency**: Select the frequency of the valuation (Annually, Semi-Annually, Quarterly, Monthly).
* **Last Update**: Enter the last update date of the valuation, if applicable.
* **Next Update**: Enter the next update date of the valuation, if applicable.
* **Client Status**: Select the current status of the client (Open, Close, In Progress, Waiting).

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to our team for clarification.
* This form is for your convenience, and you can save it for future reference.
* Please note that this form is subject to change, and we may add or remove fields as needed.
