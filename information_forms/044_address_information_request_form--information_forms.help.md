# Address Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Address Information Request Form is used to collect updated address details from customers. This form is essential for our internal records and is required for our follow-up process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the required information.
2. Fill out your full name and email address.
3. Provide your current street address, city, state/region, and zip/postal code.
4. Select the type of verification document you provided (e.g., utility bill, bank statement, etc.).
5. Indicate the length of your residency at the current address.
6. If applicable, provide a secondary address or mailing address.
7. Add any additional comments or instructions.
8. Confirm that the provided address is your current legal residence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name (id: 2)**: This field is used to collect your full name.
* **Email Address (id: 3)**: Please provide your email address for future correspondence.
* **Street Address (id: 5)**: This is where you currently reside. Please provide your street address.
* **City (id: 7)**: Enter the city where you currently live.
* **State / Region / Province (id: 8)**: Enter the state or province where you currently live.
* **Zip / Postal Code (id: 9)**: Enter the zip or postal code of your current address.
* **Country (id: 10)**: Please select the country where you currently reside.
* **Type of Verification Document Provided (id: 12)**: Select the type of document you provided to verify your address (e.g., utility bill, bank statement, etc.).
* **Length of Residency at this Address (id: 13)**: Indicate how long you've been living at this address.
* **Do you have a secondary or mailing address? (id: 14)**: If you have a separate address for receiving mail, select 'Yes (Provide in comments)' and add the address in the comments section.
* **Additional Notes or Instructions (id: 15)**: Provide any additional comments or instructions.
* **I certify the address provided is my current legal residence (id: 16)**: Confirm that the provided address is your current legal residence.
* **Date Completed (id: 17)**: Enter the date when this form is completed.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Double-check your address information for accuracy.
* Enter your verification document type correctly to avoid delays in our follow-up process.
* If you have a secondary or mailing address, provide it in the comments section.
* Please complete this form carefully, as it will help us update our records accurately.
