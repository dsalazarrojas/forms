# Office Information Coordination Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us better understand the communication preferences and challenges of your office. Please fill it out carefully, and provide as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your office department from the options provided.
2. Choose the communication tools you use or want to use for communication.
3. Describe any challenges you are facing with communication.
4. If you have any improvement ideas, feel free to provide them.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have.
6. Specify the communication tool assigned to you by your manager.
7. Provide your feedback to your manager and assistant.
8. Select your manager's contact information and title.
9. Select the department of your manager.
10. Enter your manager's contact information.
11. Enter your manager's email address.
12. Enter your manager's phone number.
13. Enter your manager's address.

# Office Information Coordination Survey - Help Guide
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Office Department**: Select the department you belong to (e.g., Office of Operations, Office of Finance, Office of Human Resources).
- **Communication Tools**: Select all communication tools you use or want to use (e.g., Email, Phone, Chat, Intranet).
	+ Note: You can select multiple options.
- **Challenges**: Describe any challenges you are facing with communication (e.g., difficulty reaching your manager, poor email response rate).
- **Improvement Ideas**: If you have any ideas on how to improve communication, provide them here.
- **Comments**: If you have any additional feedback or comments, enter them here.
- **Assigned Tool**: Choose the communication tool assigned to you by your manager.
- **Feedback to Manager**: Provide your feedback to your manager.
- **Feedback to Assistant**: Provide your feedback to your assistant.
- **Manager's Contact Information**: Enter your manager's name, title, and department.
- **Manager's Email**: Enter your manager's email address.
- **Manager's Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
- **Manager's Address**: Enter your manager's address.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- If a field is not required, you can still provide additional information if you want.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask your manager or assistant for help.
