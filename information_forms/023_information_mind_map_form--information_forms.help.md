# Information Mind Map Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Information Mind Map Form is a tool to collect relevant information for project management and organization. It's designed to be user-friendly and easy to fill out.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form sections and fields below. Each section corresponds to a different part of the form.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (user_input): Enter any information you would like to provide regarding the project or issue at hand.
* **Map Image** (map_image): Upload or enter a URL for a relevant image that illustrates your input.
* **User Name** (user_name): Enter your name as a reference and contact point.
* **Project Name** (project_name): Enter the name of the project or issue being addressed.
* **Project Manager** (project_manager): Enter the name of the person in charge of the project.
* **Collaborators** (collaborators): Choose whether or not you work with collaborators.
	+ Select "Yes" if you have collaborators on the project.
	+ Select "No" if you are the sole individual working on this project.
* **Start Date** (start_date), **End Date** (end_date), **Start Time** (start_time), **End Time** (end_time): Enter the relevant dates and times for the project schedule. If you're unsure about any of these fields, leave them blank.
* **Note** (note): Add any additional comments or information that might be helpful for the project.
* **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address for easy contact.
* **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number for further communication.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the fields that are relevant to your project. This will help in organizing the project's information efficiently.
* If you're unsure about a field, leave it blank or contact the project manager for clarification.
