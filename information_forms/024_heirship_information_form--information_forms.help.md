# Heirship Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a person's relationships with their family members and to document their entitlement to inherit property or assets after their death.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with a star (*), with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. For fields with options, select the correct answer from the dropdown menu.
3. Provide any additional information or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Person** (`heirship_information_form_person`, `number`, required: false): Enter the name of the individual about whom the heirship information is being collected.
* **Relationship** (`heirship_information_form_relationship`, `text`, required: false): Describe the nature of the relationship between the individual and the person filling out the form (e.g., spouse, child, sibling, etc.).
* **Name** (`heirship_information_form_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the individual (first and last name).
* **Age** (`heirship_information_form_age`, `text`, required: false): Enter the age of the individual.
* **Occupation** (`heirship_information_form_occupation`, `text`, required: false): Enter the occupation of the individual.
* **Marital Status** (`heirship_information_form_marital_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current marital status of the individual (married, single, divorced, or widowed).
* **Spouse** (`heirship_information_form_spouse`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the individual's spouse.
* **Children** (`heirship_information_form_children`, `text`, required: false): Enter the names of the individual's children.
* **Other Family Member** (`heirship_information_form_other_family_member`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of any other family members who should be included in the heirship information.
* **Beneficiary** (`heirship_information_form_beneficiary`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the individual who is currently benefiting from the estate.
* **Interest In Estate** (`heirship_information_form_interest_in_estate`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the interest the individual has in the estate (yes or no).
* **Other** (`heirship_information_form_other`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the individual that is relevant to the heirship.
* **Notes** (`heirship_information_form_notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the individual's heirship.
