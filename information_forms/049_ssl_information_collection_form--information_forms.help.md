# SSL Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SSL Information Collection Form is used to gather information related to SSL certificates. This includes the type of SSL, domain, contact information, and other related details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and ensure you have filled in all required information.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your situation.
3. Enter accurate and relevant information for each field.
4. Ensure that you have filled in all fields, especially if they are marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **ssl_type** (`ssl_type`, `select_one`, required: false)
The **ssl_type** field is used to determine the type of SSL you are using. Select the correct option that matches your situation.

### 2. **Domain** (`domain`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
The **Domain** field allows you to select the domain(s) associated with your SSL. Select all applicable options.

### 3. **name** (`name`, `text`, required: false)
The **name** field is used to enter your name. This field is not required and can be left blank if not applicable.
