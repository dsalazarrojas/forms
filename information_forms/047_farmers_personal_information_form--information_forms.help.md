# Farmers Personal Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal information from farmers, including farm addresses, contact details, vehicle information, insurance providers, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill out the form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, as the information will be used for administrative and regulatory purposes.
3. Review the form carefully before submission to avoid any errors or omissions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Farm Address 1**: Enter the farm's physical address.
* **Farm Address 2**: Enter any additional address information, such as apartment or suite number.
* **City**: Enter the city where the farm is located.
* **State**: Select the state where the farm is located from the provided options.
* **Zip**: Enter the farm's zip code.
* **Phone**: Enter the farm's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the farm's email address.
* **Birthdate**: Enter the farmer's birthdate in the required format.
* **Birthplace**: Enter the farmer's birthplace.
* **License**: Enter the farmer's license number or ID.
* **License State**: Select the state where the license was issued from the provided options.
* **License Number**: Enter the farmer's license number or ID number.
* **Vehicle Information**: Enter any additional information about the farm's vehicles, such as make, model, and year.
* **Years Farmed**: Enter the number of years the farmer has been farming.
* **Insurance**: Select the type of insurance coverage the farm has from the provided options.
* **Vehicles**: Select the types of vehicles the farm uses from the provided options.
* **Tractor**: Select the type of tractor(s) the farm uses from the provided options.
* **Equipment**: Select the farm equipment used from the provided options.
* **Insurance Provider**: Select the insurance provider from the provided options.
* **Other Insurance Provider Information**: Enter any additional information about the insurance provider.
* **Insurance Provider Address**: Enter the insurance provider's physical address.
* **Insurance Provider Phone**: Enter the insurance provider's phone number.
* **Insurance Provider Email**: Enter the insurance provider's email address.
* **Insurance Provider License**: Enter the insurance provider's license number or ID.
* **Insurance Provider License State**: Select the state where the insurance provider's license was issued from the provided options.
* **Farming Information**: Enter any additional information about the farm's farming activities.
