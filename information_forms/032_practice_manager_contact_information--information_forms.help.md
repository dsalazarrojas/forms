# Practice Manager Contact Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and manage contact information for Practice Managers, making it easier for users to find and contact them.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review and update your practice manager contact information to ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Fill in the required fields with the correct information about your practice manager.
*   Make sure to enter the correct contact information for each field, including their name, title, email, phone numbers, and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **practice manager name** (`practice_manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your practice manager.
*   **practice manager title** (`practice_manager_title`, text, required): Enter the title or job function of your practice manager.
*   **practice manager email** (`practice_manager_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your practice manager.
*   **practice manager phone** (`practice_manager_phone`, text, required): Enter the primary phone number of your practice manager.
*   **practice manager cell phone** (`practice_manager_cell_phone`, text, required): Enter the cell phone number of your practice manager.
*   **practice manager fax** (`practice_manager_fax`, text, required): Enter the fax number of your practice manager.
*   **practice manager address 1** (`practice_manager_address_1`, text, required): Enter the address line 1 of your practice manager's address.
*   **practice manager address 2** (`practice_manager_address_2`, text, required): Enter the address line 2 of your practice manager's address.
*   **practice manager city** (`practice_manager_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your practice manager's address.
*   **practice manager state** (`practice_manager_state`, select one, required): Select the state of your practice manager's address.
*   **practice manager zip** (`practice_manager_zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code of your practice manager's address.
*   **practice manager country** (`practice_manager_country`, select multiple, required): Select the country of your practice manager's address.
*   **practice manager time zone** (`practice_manager_time_zone`, select one, required): Select the time zone of your practice manager.
*   **practice manager language** (`practice_manager_language`, select one, required): Select the language spoken by your practice manager.

## Tips

*   Make sure to review your practice manager contact information regularly to ensure it is up-to-date and accurate.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields or their requirements, please refer to the field explanations above.
*   Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
