# Consultancy Service Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients about their consultancy service needs. It will be used by our team to understand their requirements and deliver tailored solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through the following sections carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organization name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the organization you represent. This is a required field to ensure we understand which organization you are contacting us on behalf of.
- **Contact person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for this service request. This is a required field to help us communicate effectively with you.
- **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Provide the email address of the contact person for follow-up and communication. This is a required field to ensure we can reach out to you with any updates or questions.
- **Project scope summary** (`project_scope_summary`, text, required): Briefly describe the scope of work required for the consultancy service. This will help us understand the nature of the project.
- **Project scope summary** (no label specified, text, required): This field is not labelled, but it appears to be the same as the previous field. This is a required field to ensure we have a clear understanding of the project's objectives.
- **Desired outcomes** (`desired_outcomes`, text, optional): Describe the expected outcomes or results of the consultancy service. Although not required, providing this information will help us better understand what success looks like for your organization.
- **Timeline expectations** (`timeline_expectations`, text, optional): Enter your expected start and end dates for the consultancy service. While not necessary, this information will help us schedule and plan the engagement more effectively.
- **Budget estimate** (`budget_estimate`, number, optional): Provide an approximate budget for the consultancy service. Although not required, having this information will help us better understand your financial expectations.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submission to ensure we receive complete and relevant information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- Our team will review and follow up with you after form submission to confirm the details.
- Please note that this form is not a contract, but rather a tool to gather essential information for the consultancy service.
