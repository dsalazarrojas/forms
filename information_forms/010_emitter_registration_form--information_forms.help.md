# Emitter Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register an emitter with the system, collecting information about the organization and contact details, as well as the type of emission being registered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your organization's details in the "Organization Details" field.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
3. Select the type of emission from the drop-down menu in the "Emission Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Details** (`organization_details`, text, required: false): Enter your organization's name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name, email, and phone number to be contacted for any follow-up questions or further instructions.
* **Emission Type** (`emission_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of emission from the provided options, such as "Emission Type 1" or "Emission Type 2".

## Tips
* Please ensure that your organization's details are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about the type of emission, please select the most relevant option or contact us for clarification.
* This form is for registration purposes only and will not be used for any other purpose without your consent.
