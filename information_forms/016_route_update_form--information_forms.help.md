# Route Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for updating routes, ensuring that relevant information is up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To update a route, fill out the form completely and accurately. Ensure you select the correct options for each field as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Route Update**: Enter a brief description of the update, including any relevant changes to the route.
* **Route ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the route being updated.
* **Driver ID**: Enter the identifier for the driver associated with the route.
* **Update Reason**: Provide a clear explanation for the update.
* **Update Note**: Add any additional comments or notes related to the update.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the update.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the update.
* **Driver's Update Email**: Enter the email address associated with the driver's update.
* **Driver's Update Phone**: Enter the phone number associated with the driver's update.
* **Route Start Date**: Enter the date the route is scheduled to start.
* **Route End Date**: Enter the date the route is scheduled to end.
* **Route Start Time**: Enter the time the route is scheduled to start.
* **Route End Time**: Enter the time the route is scheduled to end.
* **Route Update Comment**: Add any additional comments or notes related to the update.
* **Update Status**: Choose the status of the update (Active, Inactive, or Completed).
* **Route's Update Email**: Enter the email address associated with the route's update.
* **Route's Update Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes related to the route's update.
* **Route's Update Date**: Enter the date of the route's update.
* **Route's Update Time**: Enter the time of the route's update.
* **Route's Update Location**: Enter the location of the route's update.
* **Update Frequency**: Choose how often the route should be updated (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Driver's Update Frequency**: Choose how often the driver should be updated (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Update Frequency Note**: Add any additional comments or notes related to the update frequency.
* **Route's Update Route Type**: Choose the type of route (Pickup, Delivery, or Both).
* **Driver's Update Route Type**: Choose the type of route the driver is associated with (Pickup, Delivery, or Both).

Note: If a field is marked as "required," it must be filled out for the form to be complete. If a field is marked as "select_one" or "select_multiple," select one or multiple options as needed.
