# Tsd Inventory Update - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update the inventory of a product in the Tsd system, allowing for accurate tracking and management of stock levels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the product and transaction details to ensure accuracy.
2.  Select the correct transaction date.
3.  Enter the transaction ID.
4.  Enter the product ID.
5.  Enter the new quantity.
6.  Enter the old quantity.
7.  Select the reason code for the transaction.
8.  Choose the submit option to finalize the update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Transaction Date** (`transaction_date`, date, required: false): This field is used to select the date of the transaction. Please enter the date in the correct format.
*   **Transaction ID** (`transaction_id`, number, required: false): Enter the ID associated with the transaction.
*   **Product ID** (`product_id`, number, required: false): Enter the ID of the product being updated.
*   **New Quantity** (`new_quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the new quantity of the product after the transaction.
*   **Old Quantity** (`old_quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the old quantity of the product before the transaction.
*   **Reason Code** (`reason_code`, select_one, required: false): Select the reason code for the transaction from the list. This field helps to track the reason for the update.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the submit option to finalize the update. You can choose to submit or select "Other" for further explanation.

**Tips**

*   Please ensure that each field is completed accurately and completely.
*   Review the product and transaction details before filling out this form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant team for clarification.
