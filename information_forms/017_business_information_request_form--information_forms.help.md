# Business Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Information Request Form is used to gather information from businesses or organizations. It's essential to fill out this form to ensure that your request is processed efficiently and you receive the information you need in the format you prefer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and organization (if applicable) in the "Requester name" and "Requester organization" fields.
2. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact email" field to ensure we can get in touch with you.
3. Describe the specific information you need in the "Information requested" field.
4. Provide a brief reason for your request in the "Purpose of request" field.
5. Indicate the urgency of your request (Routine, Urgent, or Immediate) in the "Urgency" field.
6. Choose your preferred format for receiving the information (PDF, Excel, Word, or Email text) in the "Preferred format" field (if required).
7. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can add them in the "Additional comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Requester name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Your full name as the requester.
- **Requester organization** (`requester_organization`, text, optional): Your company or affiliation (if applicable).
- **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address to receive updates and the requested information.
- **Information requested** (`information_requested`, text, required): Describe specifically what information you need.
- **Purpose of request** (`purpose_of_request`, text, required): Briefly explain the reason for your request.
- **Urgency** (`urgency`, select_one, required): Indicate the level of urgency for your request (Routine, Urgent, or Immediate).
- **Preferred format** (`preferred_format`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to receive the information (PDF, Excel, Word, or Email text).
- **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other notes or comments for the information team.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.
- Choose the correct urgency level to help us prioritize your request.
- If you have any additional comments or questions, use the "Additional comments" field to provide more context.
- Our team will review your request and provide the information you need in the format you prefer, based on your requirements.
