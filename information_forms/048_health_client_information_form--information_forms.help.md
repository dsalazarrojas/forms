# Health Client Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Client Information Form is a data collection tool used to gather personal details from individuals seeking medical services or assistance. The form is intended to provide relevant and up-to-date information about the client, enabling healthcare professionals to offer accurate and efficient care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with the required information as prompted.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and legible input.
3. Review the form for completeness before submission.
4. Submit the form as instructed by the healthcare professional or organization handling the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name**: A personal identifier for the client, used to address and reference them in medical records. Enter your full or commonly used first name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name**: A personal identifier for the client, used to address and reference them in medical records. Enter your full or commonly used last name.

### Email
* **Email**: A contact address for the client, used for communication and follow-up purposes. Enter a valid email address associated with the client.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth**: The client's birth date, used to verify age and medical history. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2000).

## Tips
* Ensure the information provided is accurate and legible.
* Double-check the form for completeness before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult the healthcare professional handling the form for guidance.
