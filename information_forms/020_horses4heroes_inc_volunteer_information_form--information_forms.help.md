# Horses4Heroes, Inc. Volunteer Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Horses4Heroes, Inc. Volunteer Information Form is designed to collect relevant information about our volunteers, which will help us best support and engage with them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Please fill out the **First and Last Names** field with your full name as you'd like to be known.
2. Enter your **Volunteer Email** address, which we'll use to communicate with you.
3. Provide your **Volunteer Phone** number, so we can contact you if needed.
4. Enter your **Volunteer Address**, including **Volunteer Address 1**, **Volunteer Address 2** (if applicable), and your **Volunteer City**.
5. Select your **Volunteer State** from the provided options. This will help us tailor our support to your specific area.
6. Enter your **Volunteer Zip** code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First and Last Names**: This is the name you'd like to be known as. Please enter your full name (e.g., John Doe).
* **Volunteer Email**: Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Volunteer Phone**: This is your phone number, which will help us reach you if needed.
* **Volunteer Address 1**: Enter your street address or postal address.
* **Volunteer Address 2**: If you have a suite or apartment number, please enter it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Volunteer City**: Enter the city where you live.
* **Volunteer State**: Select the state where you reside from the options provided.
* **Volunteer Zip**: Enter your postal code.

## Tips
* Ensure that you're entering accurate and up-to-date information to help us best support you.
* If any of the fields are required, please fill them in to allow us to process your form submission.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields or need assistance, feel free to contact us.
