# Student Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect student information for educational purposes. You can use it to create or update student records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's general information, including their name, address, and any other relevant details.
2. Enter the student's contact details, such as their email and phone number.
3. Provide the student's birthday and grade information.
4. Enter the student's course information and teacher details, including their name, ID, email, and phone number.
5. Add any additional information in the "Student Information" fields.
6. Add any notes or comments about the student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`general_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's general information, including their name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's name here.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Address** (`student_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's address.
* **Email** (`student_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Birthday** (`student_birthday`, `date`, required: false): Enter the student's birthday.
* **Grade** (`student_grade`, `number`, required: false): Enter the student's grade level.
* **Course Information** (`course_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant course information.
* **Teacher** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the teacher's ID number.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Teacher Grade** (`teacher_grade`, `number`, required: false): Enter the teacher's grade level.
* **Student Information 1-10** (`student_info_1-10`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional student information in these fields.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any notes or comments about the student.
