# Process Data Integrity Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Data Integrity Contact Form is designed to allow users to report any issues or concerns they have about data accuracy or integrity. This form helps the data team quickly identify and address potential problems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields with your contact information and a detailed description of the issue.
2. Choose the urgency level of the report (Low, Medium, or High) to help the data team prioritize the issue.
3. Provide any supporting details that can aid in resolving the issue.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send the form to the data team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data**: This field is where you should provide a detailed description of the issue you're reporting. Please include as much information as possible about the error or discrepancy you've found.
* **Report Urgency**: This field allows you to let the data team know how quickly you'd like them to address the issue. Please select "Low", "Medium", or "High" based on the impact of the issue on the data's accuracy.
* **Contact Info**: This field is your point of contact for the data team to reach out to you with any questions or updates about the issue.
* **Supporting Details**: This field is optional, but provides extra details that can help the data team resolve the issue. Please use this field to include any relevant screenshots or documents that can aid in the resolution process.
* **Email**: This field is your email address that the data team will use to contact you about the issue.
* **Phone**: This field is your phone number that the data team will use to contact you about the issue.
* **Submit**: This is the final step in submitting the form. Once you've filled out all the fields, click this button to send the form to the data team.
