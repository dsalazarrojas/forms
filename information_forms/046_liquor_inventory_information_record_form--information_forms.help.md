# Liquor Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record information about liquor items in stock. It helps to track and manage inventory levels, making it easier to make informed decisions about restocking and other related tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Liquor Name** field with the name of the liquor item.
2. Select the **Category** from the dropdown menu to categorize the liquor item.
3. Enter the **Purchase Date** in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter the **Purchase Price** as a numerical value.
5. Enter the **Sales Tax** as a numerical value.
6. Enter the **Sales Price** as a numerical value.
7. Enter the **Purchase Quantity** as a numerical value.
8. Enter the **Sales Quantity** as a numerical value.
9. Enter the **Cost** as a numerical value.
10. If necessary, add any additional **Notes** about the liquor item.
11. If you want to, enter your **Email** and **Phone** contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Liquor Name**: Enter the name of the liquor item. (Required)
* **Category**: Select the category of the liquor item from the dropdown menu. (Required)
* **Purchase Date**: Enter the date the liquor item was purchased in the format MM/DD/YYYY. (Required)
* **Purchase Price**: Enter the price at which the liquor item was purchased. (Required)
* **Sales Tax**: Enter the sales tax associated with the liquor item. (Required)
* **Sales Price**: Enter the price at which the liquor item was sold. (Required)
* **Purchase Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the liquor item purchased. (Required)
* **Sales Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the liquor item sold. (Required)
* **Cost**: Enter the cost of the liquor item. (Required)
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the liquor item if necessary. (Optional)
* **Email**: If you want to, enter your email contact information. (Optional)
* **Phone**: If you want to, enter your phone contact information. (Optional)

## Tips
Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely, as this information is used for tracking and managing inventory levels.
