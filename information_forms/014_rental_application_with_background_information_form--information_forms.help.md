# Rental Application With Background Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from a tenant to assess their rental eligibility. Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth rental process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Choose the correct options for the select type fields.
3. Double-check your information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`rental_application_form_1`, text, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`rental_application_form_2`, text, required): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`rental_application_form_3`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
*   **Phone** (`rental_application_form_4`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Date of Birth** (`rental_application_form_5`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
*   **Rental Type** (`rental_application_form_6`, select_one, required): Select the type of rental you are applying for (Studio, One-bedroom, Two-bedroom).
*   **Rent Frequency** (`rental_application_form_7`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of your rent payment (Monthly, Quarterly, Annual).
*   **Move In Date** (`rental_application_form_8`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to move in.
*   **Move In Location** (`rental_application_form_9`, time, required): Enter the location you plan to move to.
*   **Rental Agreement** (`rental_application_form_10`, text, required): Please provide a brief note about your rental agreement.
*   **References** (`rental_application_form_11`, select_multiple, required): Select the people you would like to list as references (Friend, Family, Colleague, Other).
*   **Previous Rental History** (`rental_application_form_12`, select_multiple, required): Select your previous rental history (Yes, No).
*   **Employment Status** (`rental_application_form_13`, select_multiple, required): Select your employment status (Full-time, Part-time, Student, Retired).
*   **Annual Income** (`rental_application_form_14`, number, required): Enter your annual income.
*   **Employment Duration** (`rental_application_form_15`, number, required): Enter the duration of your employment.
*   **Employment History** (`rental_application_form_16`, select_multiple, required): Select your employment history (Employed, Unemployed, Pregnant on Maternity Leave, Self-Employed, Other).
*   **Income Frequency** (`rental_application_form_17`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of your income (Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
*   **Rental Frequency** (`rental_application_form_18`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of your rental (Renting, Leasing, Subleasing).
*   **Move In Reason** (`rental_application_form_19`, select_one, required): Select the reason for your move (Work, School, Travel, Other).
*   **Rental Agreement Terms** (`rental_application_form_20`, text, required): Please provide a brief note about your rental agreement terms.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`rental_application_form_21`, text, required): Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Landlord Information** (`rental_application_form_22`, select_multiple, required): Select the information of your landlord (Friend, Family, Colleague, Other).
*   **Landlord Relation** (`rental_application_form_23`, text, required): Please enter a brief note about your landlord relation.
*   **Rental Frequency 2** (`rental_application_form_24`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of your rental (Renting, Leasing, Subleasing).
*   **Move Out** (`rental_application_form_25`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to move out.

Note: The required fields are marked with an asterisk (*). Please ensure that you complete all required fields before submission.
