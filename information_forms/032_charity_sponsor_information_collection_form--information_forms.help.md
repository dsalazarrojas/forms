# Charity Sponsor Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from organizations that are interested in sponsoring our charity programs. The information collected will help us understand your organization's interest and preferences for sponsorship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your preferences.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Type of Sponsor** (`sponsor_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of sponsor you are (Individual, Small Business, Corporation, Foundation, Non-Profit Organization, or Other).
* **Primary Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your organization.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the job title or role of the primary contact person.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the primary email address of your organization.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your organization.
* **Sponsorship Level Interested In** (`sponsorship_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of sponsorship you are interested in (Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze, Community Partner, or Custom Amount).
* **Sponsorship Amount** (`sponsorship_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the dollar amount or value of the sponsorship.
* **Focus of Sponsorship** (`sponsorship_focus`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the program or area you are interested in sponsoring.
* **Type of Sponsorship** (`sponsorship_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the types of sponsorship you are interested in (Monetary Donation, In-Kind Products, Services, Volunteer Time, Media/Advertising).
* **Recognition Preference** (`recognition_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you would like to be recognized (Logo Placement, Web Listing, Event Mention, All, or Prefer Not to Be Listed).
* **Receive Email Updates** (`email_updates`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you would like to receive email updates from us (Yes or No).
* **Contact Via Phone** (`phone_contact`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you would like to receive phone calls from us (Yes or No).
* **Receive Newsletter** (`newsletter`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you would like to receive our newsletter (Yes or No).
* **Receive Event Invitations** (`event_invitations`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you would like to receive invitations to special events (Yes or No).
* **When Can You Sponsor** (`sponsorship_timing`, `select_one`, required): Choose when you are able to sponsor (Immediately, Within 30 Days, Within 60 Days, Within 90 Days, or To Be Determined).
* **Additional Information or Comments** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information or comments.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): This will be automatically populated with the current date.
