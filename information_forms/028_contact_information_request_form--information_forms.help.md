# Contact Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request contact information from potential patients or attendees at institutions or clinics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Requester name" and "Requester email" fields, respectively.
2. If applicable, provide the name of the institution or clinic you represent.
3. Provide the name of the person whose contact information is being requested (if applicable).
4. Briefly explain the reason for the contact request in the "Reason for contact request" field.
5. Choose your preferred method of contact (Phone, Email, or No preference).
6. Confirm that you have the necessary consent to request contact details.
7. Indicate the urgency of the request (Immediate, Within 24 hours, Within 3 days, or Routine).
8. Add any additional notes you think are relevant for processing the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Enter your name so we can address you properly.
* **Requester email** (`requester_email`, email, required): Provide your email address for communication about this request.
* **Institution or clinic name** (`institution_name`, text, optional): If you're representing an institution or clinic, enter the name here.
* **Patient or contact name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person whose contact information is being requested (if applicable).
* **Reason for contact request** (`contact_reason`, text, required): Give a brief explanation for requesting contact information.
* **Preferred contact method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or No preference).
* **Consent confirmation** (`consent_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have the necessary consent to request contact details.
* **Urgency of request** (`urgency`, select_one, optional): Indicate how quickly the information is needed (Immediate, Within 24 hours, Within 3 days, or Routine).
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Any additional information that would be helpful for processing the request.
