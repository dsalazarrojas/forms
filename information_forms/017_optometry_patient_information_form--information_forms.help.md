# optometry_patient_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The optometry patient information form is a crucial tool for collecting and documenting patient data. It helps our optometry team gather essential information about patients, ensuring they receive the best possible care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your First Name**: Type your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. **Enter your Last Name**: Type your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. **Enter your Email Address**: Type your email address in the "Email" field. This is the best way to contact you.
4. **Enter your Phone Number**: Type your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. **Enter your Date of Birth**: Choose your date of birth from the calendar or enter it manually in the "Date of Birth" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is typically known.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is typically known.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you for any updates or emergencies.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Choose your date of birth from the calendar to ensure accuracy.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us, and we'll be happy to help.
