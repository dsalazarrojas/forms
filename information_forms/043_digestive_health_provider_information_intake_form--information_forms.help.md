# Digestive Health Provider Information Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digestive Health Provider Information Intake Form is designed to collect essential details about a healthcare provider's credentials and contact information for the purpose of standardizing the intake process and avoiding duplication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill in your provider details, including your license number, contact person, and other required fields.
3. Enter your address, practice name, phone number, and email address in the corresponding fields.
4. Select the correct contact person from the options provided.
5. Add any additional comments or information that may be relevant to your practice.
6. Review your submission before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Provider Details** (`provider_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your provider details, including your name and other relevant information.
* **Directory Details** (`directory_details`, text, required/optional): Provide additional details about your practice or directory listing.
* **Referral Details** (`referral_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any referral details relevant to your practice.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information about your practice.
* **License Number** (`provider_license`, number, required/optional): Enter your license number for verification purposes.
* **Contact Person** (`provider_contact`, select_one, required/optional): Select the contact person for your practice (Provider, Receptionist, or Other).
* **Address** (`provider_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your practice address for record-keeping purposes.
* **Practice Name** (`practice_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your practice name for identification purposes.
* **Phone** (`provider_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your practice phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`provider_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Tips** 
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to avoid delays or issues with your submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or support team for assistance.
