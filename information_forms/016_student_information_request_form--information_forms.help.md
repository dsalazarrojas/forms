# Student Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for requesting student information, which will be used to process and tailor our response to individual needs for school administration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** (Student First Name).
2. Enter your **Last Name** (Student Last Name).
3. Select your **Date of Birth** in the required format.
4. Enter your **Email** address, which will be verified for its validity.
5. Optionally, provide your **School Administration Request**, explaining the reason for your request.
6. Provide a detailed **Reason for Request**, explaining why you are making this request.
7. Enter your **Phone** number for further contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Use the date format `mm/dd/yyyy` (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **School Administration Request**: If you have a specific request or concern, please describe it here.
* **Reason for Request**: Please provide a detailed explanation for why you are making this request.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further contact and follow-up discussions.
