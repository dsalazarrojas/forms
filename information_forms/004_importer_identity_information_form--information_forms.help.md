# Importer Identity Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Importer Identity Information Form is used to collect information about importers for administrative and organizational purposes. This form is meant to provide a way for importers to share their details with your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields, if any.
2. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
3. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
4. Enter your email address in the Email field.
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
6. Submit the form once you have filled in all the fields.
