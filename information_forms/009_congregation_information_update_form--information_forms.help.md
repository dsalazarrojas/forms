# Congregation Information Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Congregation Information Update Form is used to collect and update personal and contact information from congregational members. This form helps us keep our records accurate and ensures that we can provide timely and relevant communication to our members. To use this form, please make sure to review and update only information that is relevant to you, and do not submit duplicate or outdated information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each section to identify the fields that require updates.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked as "Required" in the form.
3. For fields with hints, please provide the information as described.
4. For fields with select options, choose the relevant option(s) that best match your information.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Member ID (if known)**: If you have a member ID, enter it here.
* **Primary Email Address**: Enter your primary email address where you can be reached.
* **Mobile Phone Number**: Enter your mobile phone number where you can be reached.
* **Home Phone Number**: Enter your home phone number (if applicable).
* **Residential Address**: Enter your current residential address, including city, state, and zip.
* **Marital Status**: Select your current marital status from the options provided.
* **Number of People in Household**: Enter the number of people living in your household.
* **Names of Children (if any)**: List the names and ages of any children in your household.
* **Current Ministry or Volunteer Groups**: Select all the groups where you are currently engaged.
* **How would you like to receive congregation updates?**: Select all the ways you'd like to receive updates (e.g., Email Newsletter, Text Messages, Physical Mail).
* **I confirm that the updated information is correct**: Select "Confirmed" to indicate that the updated information is accurate.
* **Date of Update**: Enter the date of this update.
