# reference_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Reference Information Form" is designed to collect essential details from users, which may be used for various purposes such as customer onboarding, data analysis, or identification verification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Submit the form once you have filled in all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is known to you.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is known to you.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the standard format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
