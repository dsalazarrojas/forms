# Client Belongings And Medication Policy Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to ensure the safety and security of all clients. It is used to collect information about the client's belongings and medication management policy.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and instructions.
2. Fill in the required information for each field, making sure to provide accurate and complete answers.
3. Be honest and thorough when answering the questions.
4. Sign the form at the designated area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Client Name** (`client_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter the client's full name.
- **Admission/Check-in Date** (`date_of_admission`, `date`, required): Enter the date of admission or check-in.
- **Case Manager/Staff Member** (`case_manager_name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the case manager or staff member responsible for the client.
- **List of Valuables** (`valuables_list`, `text`, required): List the major items or valuables that the client has brought with them.
- **Do you wish to store valuables in the facility safe/locker?** (`locker_storage_requested`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' if you wish to store valuables in the facility safe/locker, 'False' otherwise.
- **I confirm I have no prohibited items (weapons, illicit substances)** (`prohibited_items_ack`, `select_one`, required): Select 'I confirm' if you have no prohibited items, 'Other' otherwise.
- **Are you currently taking any prescription medications?** (`taking_medication`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' if you are currently taking any prescription medications, 'False' otherwise.
- **List Medications and Dosages** (`medication_list`, `text`, optional): List your medications and dosages.
- **Known Allergies (Medication or Food)** (`allergies_list`, `text`, required): List any known allergies.
- **Primary Pharmacy Name and Phone** (`pharmacy_name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name and phone number of your primary pharmacy.
- **I agree to hand over all medications for secure storage/dispensing** (`med_storage_policy_ack`, `select_one`, required): Select 'I agree' if you agree to hand over all medications for secure storage and dispensing.
- **I understand the facility is not liable for lost or damaged personal items** (`liability_waiver_belongings`, `select_one`, required): Select 'I understand and agree' if you understand the facility's policy.
- **I understand the facility reserves the right to search belongings for safety** (`right_to_search_ack`, `select_one`, required): Select 'I understand' if you understand the facility's policy.
- **Client Signature (Type Name)** (`client_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name to confirm your understanding and agreement.
- **Staff Member Witness (Type Name)** (`staff_witness`, `text`, required): Sign your name as a witness.
- **Date signed** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date signed.
- **Safety and Security for All** (`policy_footer`, `note`, optional): This section is for acknowledgement of the facility's safety and security policy.
