# user_data_upload_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The user_data_upload_form is designed to collect user data for uploading files and providing additional information. It is intended to be completed by users who need to upload files for various categories.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. Select the category for your data upload from the "Category" dropdown menu.
3. Enter a brief description of your data upload in the "Description" field (optional).
4. Confirm whether you will be uploading files by checking the "files" field.
5. If uploading files, enter the number of files you will be uploading in the "file_uploads" field(s).
6. Enter the details of your uploaded files in the "file_uploads_2" field(s).
7. Enter your tool assignment in the "assigned_tool" field.
8. Add any additional notes about your data upload in the "notes" field (optional).
9. Enter your email address in the "email" field (optional).
10. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field (optional).
11. Upload additional files as needed in the "file_uploads_3" to "file_uploads_16" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category`, select_one): Select the category for your data upload from the dropdown menu.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your data upload, if required.
* **Files** (`files`, select_multiple): Confirm whether you will be uploading files.

* **File Uploads 2** (`file_uploads_2`, text): Enter the details of your uploaded files.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one): Enter the tool assigned to your data upload.
