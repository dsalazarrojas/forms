# Pet Emergency Contact Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact and medical information for a pet owner's emergency contacts, veterinary details, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Pet Owner Information**
	* This section is where you can enter the pet owner's information, including their name, contact details, and any other relevant details.
2. **Veterinary Details**
	* Here, you can enter the pet's veterinary clinic information, including the contact person, address, and any other relevant details.
3. **Emergency Contacts**
	* In this section, you can list the pet's emergency contacts, including their names, phone numbers, and relationship to the pet owner.
4. **Health Information**
	* This section is for entering the pet's medical history, including any medical conditions, medications, and allergies.
5. **Medical Procedures**
	* Here, you can list any medical procedures the pet has undergone.
6. **Medical Treatments**
	* Enter any medical treatments the pet is currently undergoing or has undergone in the past.
7. **Medical Allergies**
	* List any medical allergies the pet has.
8. **Medication Side Effects**
	* Enter any side effects of the pet's medications.
9. **Diet**
	* Select the pet's diet type.
10. **Vaccinations**
	* List any vaccinations the pet has received.
11. **Microchip Details**
	* Enter the pet's microchip details, including the ID number and type.
12. **Medical Insurance**
	* Select whether the pet has medical insurance and enter the provider's details.
13. **Additional Medical Insurance Information**
	* Enter any additional medical insurance information, including the insurance number, group number, and provider address.
14. **Emergency Contact Information**
	* Enter any emergency contact person's name, phone number, and relationship to the pet owner.
