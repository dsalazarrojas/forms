# Basic Employment Information Sheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic employment information from employees, which will be used for internal purposes such as personnel management and record-keeping. Please note that this form is intended for official records and should be filled out accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out all required fields with your accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct options from drop-down menus where applicable.
3. Ensure you fill out all fields with the correct formatting (e.g., date fields should be in the correct date format).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Full Name** (`employee_full_name`, text, required)
Enter your legal full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, text, required)
Enter your unique employee identification number.

### 3. **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required)
Enter your current position or role within the company.

### 4. **Department** (`department`, select_one, required)
Select your department from the provided options:

*   Human Resources
*   Finance
*   Marketing
*   Sales
*   Operations
*   IT
*   Customer Service
*   Other

### 5. **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required)
Enter the date you were hired in the correct date format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### 6. **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, required)
Select your employment classification from the provided options:

*   Full-time
*   Part-time
*   Temporary
*   Contract
*   Intern

### 7. **Work Location** (`work_location`, select_one, required)
Select your primary work location from the provided options:

*   Office
*   Remote
*   Hybrid
*   Field

### 8. **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required)
Enter the full name of your direct supervisor or manager.

### 9. **Work Email Address** (`work_email`, email, required)
Enter your company email address.

### 10. **Work Phone Number** (`work_phone`, text, optional)
Enter your work contact number, if applicable.

### 11. **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required)
Enter the full name of your emergency contact.

### 12. **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required)
Enter the phone number for your emergency contact.

### 13. **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required)
Enter the relationship to you (e.g., spouse, parent, sibling).

### 14. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional)
Enter any additional information you would like to provide.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns while filling out this form, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
