# New Client Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Client Information Form is used to collect essential details about new clients or businesses. This form is designed to be completed by the client or their representative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. **Contact Information**: Provide your company's name and website (if applicable).
2. **Company Details**:
	* Choose the size of your company.
	* Indicate whether your company is public or private, and whether it's non-profit.
	* Select the title of the contact person.
	* Choose whether the contact person has email and/or phone access.
3. **Company Information**:
	* Choose whether your company has a phone and/or email.
4. **Contact Person Information**:
	* Choose whether the contact person has access to phone and/or email.
5. **Location Information**:
	* Enter the city, state, and zip of your company's location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **contact_information**: Enter your company's name and website (if applicable).
- **company_name**: Enter the name of your company.
- **job_title**: Enter the job title of the contact person.
- **company_website**: Enter your company's website (if applicable).
- **email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **phone**: Enter your company's phone number.
- **why_our_business**: Explain why we are interested in your business.
- **how_did_you_find_us**: Explain how you found us.
- **contact_person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
- **company_address**: Enter the address of your company.
- **city**: Enter the city of your company's location.
- **state**: Enter the state of your company's location.
- **zip**: Enter the zip code of your company's location.
- **company**: Enter your company's name.
- **country**: Enter the country of your company's location.
- **industry**: Enter the industry your company belongs to.
- **company_size**: Choose the size of your company.
- **company_type**: Choose whether your company is public, private, or non-profit.
- **contact_person_title**: Choose the title of the contact person.
- **contact_person_email**: Choose whether the contact person has email access.
- **contact_person_phone**: Choose whether the contact person has phone access.
- **company_phone**: Choose whether your company has a phone.
- **company_email**: Choose whether your company has an email.
- **contact_person**: Choose whether the contact person has access to phone and/or email.

**Tips**:
- Double-check that all fields are completed accurately to avoid duplication of records.
- Ensure that you have the correct information to follow up with the contact person.
- If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to our team for assistance.
