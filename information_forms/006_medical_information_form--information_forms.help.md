# Medical Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Information Form is designed to collect medical information from users, which will be used by healthcare professionals to assess their health status and provide personalized advice or treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "What is your date of birth?" field in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
3. List all your medical conditions in the "Which medical conditions do you have?" field.
4. Enter your medical insurance number in the "What is your medical insurance number?" field.
5. Enter the name of your primary care physician in the "What is your primary care physician?" field.
6. Select how you take your medication in the "How do you take your medication?" field.
7. Enter how often you take your medication in the "How often do you take your medication?" field.
8. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question "Do you have diabetes?".
9. Select any medical allergies you have from the "Do you have any allergies?" field.
10. Enter your blood pressure in the "What is your blood pressure?" field.
11. Enter your medical insurance provider in the "What is your medical insurance provider?" field.
12. Describe any medical history you have in the "Do you have any medical history?" field.
13. Describe any medical procedures you have had in the "Do you have any medical procedures?" field.
14. Describe any supplements you take in the "Do you take any supplements?" field.
15. Enter how long you take your medication in the "How long do you take your medication for?" field in the format of Xx.
16. Select any medical devices or equipment you use from the "Do you have any medical devices or equipment?" field.
17. Select any medical implants you have from the "Do you have any medical implants?" field.
18. Describe any medical tests you have had in the "Do you take any medical tests?" field.
19. Select any medications you take from the "Do you take any medications?" field.
20. Enter how many times you exercise per week in the "How many times do you exercise in a week?" field.
21. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question "Do you eat healthy?".
22. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question "Do you smoke?".
23. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question "Do you drink?".
24. Describe any family medical history you have in the "Do you have any family medical history?" field.
25. Describe any previous illnesses you have had in the "Do you have any medical history of any previous illnesses?" field.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please describe your best estimate or understanding.
* If you have any medical devices or equipment, please list all of them.
* If you have any medical implants, please list all of them.
* If you have any medical history, please describe it in detail.
