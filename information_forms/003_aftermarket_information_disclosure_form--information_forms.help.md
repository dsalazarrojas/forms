# Aftermarket Information Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aftermarket Information Disclosure Form is used to gather essential information about your products or services for aftermarket services such as warranty verification, safety recall information, technical support, legal compliance, or parts compatibility checks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your Full Name, Business Email, Contact Number, and Company Name.
2. Provide the Product Name and Model Number of the product you are requesting information about.
3. Select the reason for your disclosure request from the Reason for Disclosure Request dropdown menu.
4. Specify the detailed description of the data you are requesting and how this data will be utilized.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (2)**: Please enter your full name to ensure we can contact you regarding your request.
* **Business Email (3)**: Enter your business email address so we can get back to you about your request.
* **Contact Number (4)**: Provide your contact number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Company Name (5)**: Enter your company name for our records.
* **Product Name (7)**: Enter the name of the product you are requesting information about.
* **Model Number (8)**: Enter the model number of the product you are requesting information about.
* **Reason for Disclosure Request (10)**: Select the reason for your disclosure request from the provided options (Warranty Verification, Safety Recall Information, Technical Support, Legal Compliance, or Parts Compatibility Check).
* **Detailed Description of Data Requested (12)**: Specify the exact information you need from us, and we will do our best to provide it to you.
* **Requested Disclosure Date (13)**: Enter the date you are requesting the disclosure for.
* **Intended Use of Information (14)**: Select how you plan to use the disclosed information (Internal Research, Customer Service Support, Public Safety Bulletin, Regulatory Filing, or Third-party Audit).

## Tips
* Make sure you have checked our FAQs before submitting this form to avoid duplicate requests.
* Double-check your email and contact number to ensure we can get back to you about your request.
* Be specific when describing the information you need and how you plan to use it to ensure we provide the right support.
