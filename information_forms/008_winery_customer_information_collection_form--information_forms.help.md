# Winery Customer Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect customer information from winery visitors, helping the winery better understand their customers, improve customer experience, and tailor their marketing efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name.
2.  Enter your last name.
3.  Enter your email address.
4.  Enter your phone number.
5.  Select your date of birth.
6.  Choose your wine preferences.
7.  Select your tasting frequency.
8.  Choose your favorite wine style.
9.  Select your visit frequency.
10.  Choose your preferred contact method.
11.  Select your interests in the winery.
12.  Choose your favorite wine variety.
13.  Answer if this is your first visit.
14.  Select your visit frequency from your last visit.
15.  Answer if you have visited before.
16.  Provide a recommendation or suggestion for the winery.
17.  Add any comments.
18.  Select the email opening policy.
19.  Select the phone opening policy.
20.  Select the mail opening policy.
21.  Select other opening policies.
22.  Select your social media presence.
23.  Select your online store presence.
24.  Enter the website.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name as it is known to the public.
*   **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name as it is known to the public.
*   **Email (3)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
*   **Phone Number (4)**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
*   **Date of Birth (5)**: Select your date of birth.
*   **Wine Preference (6)**: Select the type of wine you prefer.
*   **Tasting Frequency (7)**: Select how often you taste wine.
*   **Favorite Wine Style (8)**: Select your favorite type of wine.
*   **Visit Frequency (9)**: Select how often you visit the winery.
*   **Preferred Contact Method (10)**: Select your preferred method of contact.
*   **Winery Interest (11)**: Select your interests in the winery.
*   **Favorite Wine Variety (12)**: Select your favorite type of wine.
*   **First Time Visitor (13)**: Answer yes or no if this is your first visit.
*   **Visit Frequency Last Time (14)**: Select how often you visited last time.
*   **Visited Before (15)**: Answer yes or no if you have visited before.
*   **Winery Recommendation (16)**: Provide a recommendation or suggestion for the winery.
*   **Winery Suggestion (17)**: Add any comments or suggestions.
*   **Winery Comments (18)**: Add any comments.
*   **Email Opening (19)**: Select the email opening policy.
*   **Phone Opening (20)**: Select the phone opening policy.
*   **Mail Opening (21)**: Select the mail opening policy.
*   **Other Opening (22)**: Select other opening policies.
*   **Social Media (23)**: Select your social media presence.
*   **Online Store (24)**: Select your online store presence.
*   **Website (25)**: Enter the website.

Note that all fields are not required, and customers can choose to provide information at their discretion.
