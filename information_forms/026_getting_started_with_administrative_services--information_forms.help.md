# getting-started-with-administrative-services - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide users through the process of requesting administrative services. It collects contact details, service type, urgency level, and comments for administrative services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
2. Select the type of administrative service you require from the "Service Type" dropdown menu.
3. Indicate the urgency level of your issue in the "Urgency" dropdown menu.
4. Provide your consent for the service in the "Consent" field.
5. Enter any comments or additional information about the service in the "Service Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details (`contact_details`)**:
  • A text field to enter your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Service Type (`service_type`)**:
  • A dropdown menu to select the type of administrative service you require. Options include "Administrative", "Non-Administrative", and "Other".
* **Urgency (`urgency`)**:
  • A dropdown menu to indicate the urgency level of your issue. Options are "Low", "Medium", and "High".
* **Consent (`consent`)**:
  • A checkbox field to indicate if you consent to receive services. Options are "Yes" and "No".
* **Service Comments (`service_comments`)**:
  • A text field to enter any comments or additional information about the service you require.

## Tips
- Ensure you complete all mandatory fields.
- Select the correct service type and urgency level to ensure your issue is handled promptly.
- Enter clear and concise comments to help the administrative staff provide the best service.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the administrative team directly.
