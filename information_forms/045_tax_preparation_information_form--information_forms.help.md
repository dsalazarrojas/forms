# tax_preparation_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects tax preparation information for clients. It is a critical form used to gather essential details needed for tax preparation.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the General Information section with any relevant details.
2. Provide the Client Details, including all required fields.
3. Select the Tax Year for which you are preparing the tax information.
4. Enter the client's Income, if applicable.
5. List any expenses incurred.
6. Provide Income Details and Expense Details as required.
7. Sign and confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (`General Information`, `text`, required/optional: false): Fill out this section with any relevant general information about the client.
- **Client Details** (`Client Details`, `text`, required/optional: true): This is a required field where you must provide detailed information about the client.
- **Tax Year** (`Tax Year`, `number`, required/optional: true): Select the tax year for which you are preparing the tax information.
- **Income** (`Income`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the client's income, if applicable.
- **Expenses** (`Expenses`, `text`, required/optional: false): List any expenses incurred by the client.
- **Income Details** (`Income Details`, `text`, required/optional: true): Provide detailed information about the client's income.
- **Expense Details** (`Expense Details`, `text`, required/optional: true): Provide detailed information about the client's expenses.
- **Signatures** (`Signatures`, `select_multiple`, required/optional: false): Sign and confirm the accuracy of the information provided. You can select 'Yes' or 'No'. 

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor for guidance.
