# Child Information Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a child, including demographic data, contact information, and behavioral characteristics. It is intended to be completed by parents or guardians to provide accurate and comprehensive information about the child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the form submission date in the "Form Date" field.
2. Provide the child's first name in the "Child First Name" field.
3. Enter the child's middle name in the "Child Middle Name" field (if applicable).
4. Enter the child's last name in the "Child Last Name" field.
5. Enter the child's nickname or preferred name in the "Child Nickname" field (if applicable).
6. Provide the child's date of birth in the "Child Date of Birth" field.
7. Enter the child's age in years in the "Child Age" field.
8. Select the child's gender in the "Child Gender" field.
9. Select the child's preferred pronouns in the "Child Pronouns" field (if applicable).
10. Enter the place of birth in the "Place of Birth" field (if applicable).
11. Enter the parent or guardian's first name in the "Parent or Guardian First Name" field.
12. Enter the parent or guardian's last name in the "Parent or Guardian Last Name" field.
13. Enter the parent or guardian's email address in the "Parent or Guardian Email" field.
14. Enter the parent or guardian's phone number in the "Parent or Guardian Phone" field.
15. Enter the full street address in the "Home Address" field.
16. Enter the city of residence in the "City" field.
17. Enter the state or province of residence in the "State" field.
18. Enter the postal or zip code in the "Zip Code" field.
19. List any siblings and their ages in the "Siblings Information" field (if applicable).
20. List all languages spoken in the household in the "Languages Spoken at Home" field.
21. Describe the child's interests and hobbies in the "Child Interests and Hobbies" field (if applicable).
22. List any notable strengths or talents the child may possess in the "Child Strengths" field (if applicable).
23. Describe any challenges or areas where the child needs support in the "Child Challenges" field (if applicable).
24. Provide any additional information about the child in the "Additional Information" field (if applicable).

* **Child Middle Name** (Child Middle Name, text, optional): Enter the child's middle name (if applicable).

* **Child Pronouns** (Child Pronouns, select_one, optional): Select the child's preferred pronouns.
* **Place of Birth** (Place of Birth, text, optional): Enter the city and state or country of birth.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name** (Parent or Guardian First Name, text, required): Enter the legal first name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name** (Parent or Guardian Last Name, text, required): Enter the legal last name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (Parent or Guardian Email, email, required): Enter the primary email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (Parent or Guardian Phone, text, required): Enter the primary phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Home Address** (Home Address, text, required): Enter the full street address of the child's residence.
* **City** (City, text, required): Enter the city of residence.
* **State** (State, text, required): Enter the state or province of residence.
* **Zip Code** (Zip Code, text, required): Enter the postal or zip code.
