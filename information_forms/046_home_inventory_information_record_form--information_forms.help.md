# Home Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Home Inventory Information Record Form is used to collect and record information about a user's home inventory, including contact details and property characteristics. This form is intended to provide a comprehensive overview of a user's property holdings for administrative or accounting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Name and other contact details in the designated fields.
2. Provide your home's characteristics, such as phone number, email, address, and zip code.
3. Report your home's details, including type, square feet, number of floors, year built, and date purchased.
4. Enter your insurance information, including the provider's contact details and any other insurance provider's contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, required): Enter the first line of your home's address.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, text, optional): Enter the second line of your home's address (if applicable).
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the name of the city where your home is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state where your home is located.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your zip code for postal purposes.
* **Home Type** (`home_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of your home (House, Townhouse, Apartment, Other).
* **Square Feet** (`square_feet`, number, required): Enter the square feet of your home.
* **Number of Floors** (`number_of_floors`, number, required): Enter the number of floors in your home.
* **Year Built** (`year_built`, time, optional): Enter the year your home was built (if known).
* **Date Purchased** (`date_purchased`, date, optional): Enter the date you purchased your home (if known).
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, number, optional): Enter the price you paid for your home (if known).
* **Current Value** (`current_value`, number, optional): Enter the current value of your home (if known).
* **Mortgage Info** (`mortgage_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant mortgage information.
* **Lease Info** (`lease_info`, text, optional): Enter any lease or rental agreements.
* **Home Insurance Provider** (`home_insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of your home insurance provider.
* **Home Insurance Provider Phone** (`home_insurance_provider_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of your home insurance provider.
* **Home Insurance Provider Email** (`home_insurance_provider_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of your home insurance provider.
* **Home Insurance Provider Address** (`home_insurance_provider_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your home insurance provider.
* **Home Insurance Provider's Morning Phone** (`home_insurance_provider_morning_phone`, text, optional): Enter the morning contact number of your home insurance provider.
* **Home Insurance Provider's Evening Phone** (`home_insurance_provider_evening_phone`, text, optional): Enter the evening contact number of your home insurance provider.
* **Home Insurance Provider Notes** (`home_insurance_provider_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about your home insurance provider.
* **Other Insurance Provider** (`other_insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of any other insurance provider.
* **Other Insurance Provider Phone** (`other_insurance_provider_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of any other insurance provider.
* **Other Insurance Provider Email** (`other_insurance_provider_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of any other insurance provider.
* **Other Insurance Provider Address** (`other_insurance_provider_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of any other insurance provider.
* **Other Insurance Provider's Morning Phone** (`other_insurance_provider_morning_phone`, text, optional): Enter the morning contact number of any other insurance provider.
* **Other Insurance Provider's Evening Phone** (`other_insurance_provider_evening_phone`, text, optional): Enter the evening contact number of any other insurance provider.
* **Other Insurance Provider Notes** (`other_insurance_provider_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about any other insurance provider.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that all relevant information is captured.
* Ensure that all fields with the "required" flag are completed before proceeding.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance from the form's administrator.
* This form is intended to be a one-time data collection instrument; please do not attempt to use it to collect duplicate information.
* Review your input before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
