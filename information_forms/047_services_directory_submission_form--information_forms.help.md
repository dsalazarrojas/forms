## Services Directory Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Services Directory Submission Form is designed to collect business information for inclusion in an online directory. This form helps businesses and organizations create a listing for their services, making it easier for customers to find and interact with them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Business Name**, which is the name of your company or organization.
2.  Provide your **Email** address to enable communication with your business.
3.  Select the correct **Category** for your services from the available options.
4.  Briefly describe your **Service Name** and **Description** to help customers understand what you offer.
5.  Enter your **Address**, **Phone Number**, and **Contact Person** details to facilitate communication and visitation.
6.  Provide your **Email Address** and **Website** for additional contact and information purposes.
7.  If applicable, enter your **Business License** number for verification and credibility.
