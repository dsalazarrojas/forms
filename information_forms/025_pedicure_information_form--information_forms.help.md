# Pedicure Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients or patients about their medical history, medical conditions, allergies, and medications to provide the best possible pedicure experience and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your basic contact information.
2. Answer the questions in each section carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you have any medical conditions, allergies, or medications, please specify them in the relevant sections.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
