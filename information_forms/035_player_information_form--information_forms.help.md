# Player Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Information Form is designed to collect personal and demographic details about a player in a sports-related context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Player Address field with the player's address.
2. Enter the player's phone number in the Player Phone Number field.
3. Provide the player's email address in the Player Email field.
4. Enter the player's name in the Player Name field.
5. Fill in the Player Date of Birth field with the player's date of birth.
6. Enter the player's height in the Player Height field.
7. Enter the player's weight in the Player Weight field.
8. Provide the name of the player's coach in the Coach Name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Address** (`player_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the player's address.
* **Player Phone Number** (`player_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the player's phone number.
* **Player Email** (`player_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the player's email address.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the player's name.
* **Player Date of Birth** (`player_date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter the player's date of birth.
* **Player Height** (`player_height`, `number`, required: false): Enter the player's height.
* **Player Weight** (`player_weight`, `number`, required: false): Enter the player's weight.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the player's coach.

## Tips

* Double-check the information you enter for accuracy and completeness.
* If a field is marked as "optional," you can choose to fill it in or leave it blank.
* If you are unsure about any of the details, please ask the player for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed by authorized personnel only, so please ensure you have the necessary permissions to fill in this form.
