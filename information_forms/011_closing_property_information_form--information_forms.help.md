# Closing Property Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Closing Property Information Form is a tool used to gather detailed information about a property, ensuring accurate and complete documentation for closing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field (page 1).
2. Provide the city, state, and zip code in the "City, State, Zip Code" field (page 1).
3. Fill in the parcel or lot number, if applicable (page 2).
4. Enter the seller's name in the "Seller Name" field (page 2).
5. Enter the seller's email address in the "Seller Email" field (page 2).
6. Enter the seller's phone number in the "Seller Phone Number" field (page 2).
7. Select the correct property type (e.g., Single Family Home, Multi-Family, Condo, etc.) from the "Property Type" field (page 3).
8. Enter the original construction year (if known) in the "Year Built" field (page 3).
9. Enter the total square footage of the property in the "Total Square Footage" field (page 3).
10. Enter the number of bedrooms and bathrooms in the respective fields (page 3).
11. Indicate the lot size in the "Lot Size" field (page 4).
12. Select the overall condition of the property from the "Overall Property Condition" field (page 4).
13. Check if any major repairs or updates were completed in the past 5 years (page 4).
14. Describe any major repairs or updates in the "Describe any major repairs or updates" field (page 4).
15. List any known issues or defects in the "Any known issues or defects" field (page 5).
16. Select which utilities are included with the property from the "Which utilities are included with the property" field (page 5).
17. Enter any additional property information or notes in the "Additional property information or notes" field (page 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the complete street address of the property.
* **City, State, Zip Code** (`city_state_zip`, text, required): Enter the city, state, and zip code of the property's location.
* **Parcel or Lot Number** (`parcel_number`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the parcel or lot number of the property.
* **Seller Name** (`seller_name`, text, required): Enter the full legal name of the seller.
* **Seller Email** (`seller_email`, email, required): Enter the seller's email address.
* **Seller Phone Number** (`seller_phone`, text, required): Enter the seller's phone number.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of property (e.g., Single Family Home, Multi-Family, Condo, etc.).
* **Year Built** (`year_built`, number, optional): Enter the original construction year of the property (if known).
* **Total Square Footage** (`square_footage`, number, optional): Enter the total square footage of the building.
* **Number of Bedrooms** (`number_bedrooms`, number, optional): Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (`number_bathrooms`, number, optional): Enter the number of full and half bathrooms in the property.
* **Lot Size** (`lot_size`, text, optional): Enter the lot size in acreage or square footage.
* **Overall Property Condition** (`overall_condition`, select_one, required): Select the overall condition of the property (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Needs significant repairs).
* **Any major repairs or updates completed in past 5 years** (`major_repairs`, select_one, required): Check if any major repairs or updates were completed in the past 5 years.
* **Describe any major repairs or updates** (`repairs_description`, text, optional): Describe any major repairs or updates completed in the past 5 years.
* **Any known issues or defects** (`known_issues`, text, optional): List any known issues or defects with the property.
* **Which utilities are included with the property** (`utilities_included`, select_multiple, optional): Select which utilities are included with the property (e.g., Water, Sewer, Trash collection, Electricity, Gas, HOA fees, Other).
* **Additional property information or notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional property information or notes.
