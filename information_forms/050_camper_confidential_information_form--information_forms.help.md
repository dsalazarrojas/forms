# Camper Confidential Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information about campers, including their full name, contact details, and medical history. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the camper's safety and well-being during their stay at the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the camper before starting.
2. Complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
3. Double-check the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Full Name**: This field is required and should be filled with the camper's legal name. Please ensure the name is spelled correctly and matches government-issued identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: This field requires the camper's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY). Ensure the date is accurate and complete.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: This field is required and should be filled with the full name of the parent or guardian providing consent for the camper.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: This field is required and should be a valid phone number for the parent or guardian.
* **Allergies or Dietary Restrictions**: If the camper has any allergies or dietary restrictions, list them in this field. This is an optional field, but please be thorough and accurate.
* **Medical Conditions**: If the camper has any ongoing medical issues or chronic conditions, list them in this field. This is an optional field, but please be thorough and accurate.
* **Current Medications**: If the camper is taking any medications, list them in this field, including dosing schedules. This is an optional field, but please be thorough and accurate.
* **Special Care Instructions**: If the camper requires any special care during their stay, list them in this field. This is an optional field, but please be thorough and accurate.
* **Photo Consent**: Select 'True' if you grant permission for the camp to use the camper's photos for camp purposes. Select 'False' if you do not grant permission.
* **Parent Guardian Signature Name**: This field is required and should be filled with the full name of the parent or guardian, as an electronic signature.
