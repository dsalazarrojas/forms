# crowdstrike_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **crowdstrike_information_form** is designed to gather insights on how users interact with CrowdStrike's AI-powered security solutions. Your input will help us improve our services and better support you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your User Information**: Fill out the text field with your name, email address, or any other relevant contact information.
2. **Describe your Product/Service Experience**: In the text field, provide a brief description of your experience with our products or services.
3. **Share Security Insights**: In the text field, share any security concerns or issues you've faced with our services.
4. **Additional Comments**: If you have any further comments or suggestions, use this text field to share them.
5. **Select Multiple Products/Services**: Choose any products or services you've used from the dropdown list.
6. **More Security Insights**: If you have more security-related information to share, use this text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (user_info): Enter your name, email address, or any other relevant contact information.
- **Product Information** (product_info): Describe your experience with our products or services.
- **Service Information** (service_info): Share any details about the specific services you've used.
- **Security Insights** (security_insights): Share any security concerns or issues you've faced with our services.
- **Security Insights 2** (security_insights_2): If you have more security-related information to share, use this text field.
- **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): Use this text field to share any further comments or suggestions.
- **Product/Services** (product_services): Select any products or services you've used from the dropdown list.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when describing your experience with our products or services.
- If you have any further comments or suggestions, share them in the **Additional Comments** field.
- If you have more security-related information to share, use the **Security Insights 2** field.
