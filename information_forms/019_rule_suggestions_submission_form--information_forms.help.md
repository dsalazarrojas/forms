# Rule Suggestions Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rule Suggestions Submission Form is designed to collect suggestions from users for changes, additions, or deletions to rules. The form is meant to gather detailed information about each suggestion, including its type, impact level, category, and priority.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Select the type of suggestion you want to submit (Rule Change, Rule Addition, Rule Deletion, or Other).
*   Provide a detailed description of your suggestion in the Suggestion Text field.
*   Specify the impact level of your suggestion (High, Medium, Low, or None).
*   Choose the category of your suggestion (IT, Finance, Operations, or Other).
*   Indicate the priority of your suggestion (High, Medium, Low, or None).
*   If relevant, provide additional context with Suggestion Image, Suggestion Video, Suggestion Audio, or Suggestion Document.
*   Enter your name, email, phone number, department, job title, organization, location, and date.
*   Provide any additional comments or attach files if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Rule Suggestions Submission** (`rule_suggestions_submission_form`): This is the main section of the form where you can enter your suggestion.
*   **Suggestion Type** (`suggestion_type`): Choose the type of suggestion you want to submit (Rule Change, Rule Addition, Rule Deletion, or Other).
*   **Suggestion Text** (`suggestion_text`): Provide a detailed description of your suggestion in this field.
*   **Impact Level** (`impact_level`): Specify the impact level of your suggestion (High, Medium, Low, or None).
*   **Suggestion Category** (`suggestion_category`): Choose the category of your suggestion (IT, Finance, Operations, or Other).
*   **Suggestion Priority** (`suggestion_priority`): Indicate the priority of your suggestion (High, Medium, Low, or None).
*   **Suggestion Image** (`suggestion_image`), **Suggestion Video** (`suggestion_video`), **Suggestion Audio** (`suggestion_audio`), **Suggestion Document** (`suggestion_document`): Provide additional context for your suggestion, if necessary.
*   **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`), **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`), **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`), **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`), **Submitter Job Title** (`submitter_job_title`), **Submitter Organization** (`submitter_organization`), **Submitter Location** (`submitter_location`): Enter information about yourself as the submitter.
*   **Submitter Date** (`submitter_date`), **Submitter Time** (`submitter_time`): Provide the date and time you are submitting the suggestion.
*   **Submitter Comment** (`submitter_comment`), **Submitter File** (`submitter_file`): Enter any additional comments or attach relevant files, if necessary.

Please note that the form is designed to be completed by submitters of rule suggestions. This guide is meant to be a helpful resource for those who need to provide clear and comprehensive suggestions for changes, additions, or deletions to rules.
