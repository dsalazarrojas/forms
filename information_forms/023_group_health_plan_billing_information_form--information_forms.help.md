# Group Health Plan Billing Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect billing information for group health plans from employers. It is used to ensure accurate and efficient billing processes for both the employer and the insurance company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the necessary billing information in the **Billing Information** field.
2. Enter the employer's details in the **Employer Details** field.
3. Select the **Plan Coverage** option that best describes the coverage provided.
4. Choose the **Payment Method** (ACH or EFT) and the **Payment Term** (Immediate, 30 Days, or 60 Days) that suits your needs.
5. You may add any additional **Notes** for the billing process.
6. Enter your contact information, including **Email** and **Phone**.
7. Provide your **Address** for any follow-up contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Billing Information** (1): Enter the billing information for the group health plan, including any relevant details about the billing process.
* **Employer Details** (2): Provide your company's name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Plan Coverage** (3): Select whether the plan provides **Full Coverage** or **Partial Coverage**.
* **Payment Method** (5): Choose between **ACH (Automated Clearing House)** and **EFT (Electronic Funds Transfer)**.
* **Payment Term** (6): Select when payment is due, either **Immediate**, **30 Days**, or **60 Days**.
* **Notes** (7): Add any additional notes or comments about the billing process.
* **Email** (8): Enter your email address for any communication.
* **Phone** (9): Enter your phone number for any follow-up contact.
* **Address** (10): Provide your company's address for any follow-up contact.
