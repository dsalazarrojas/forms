# Senior Banker Identification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Banker Identification Form is designed to collect information from senior bankers to help our team understand their background, experience, and expertise in the banking industry. This form is intended for internal use by our team and will help us better understand the qualifications and attributes of senior bankers in the field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are answering all required fields (identified with an asterisk \*).
2. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
3. Fill out the form completely and accurately, following the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **executive_profile_details** (text field)
	* Label: *executive_profile_details*
	* Type: text
	* Purpose: This field is used to capture the executive profile details of the senior banker.

This field is for you to briefly describe your experience and background in the banking industry. Please provide a concise and accurate account of your professional history, including your current and past positions, notable achievements, and any relevant certifications or education.
