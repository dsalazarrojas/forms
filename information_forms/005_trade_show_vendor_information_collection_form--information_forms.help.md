# trade-show-vendor-information-collection-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect trade show vendor information for participation in events. Please fill in all required fields to provide accurate and complete data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. Review each field explanation below for specific guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name** (`Vendor Name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Vendor Email** (`Vendor Email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Vendor Phone** (`Vendor Phone`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Booth Number** (`Booth Number`, text, required): Enter your booth number at the trade show.
* **Vendor URL** (`Vendor URL`, text, optional): Enter your company website URL (if applicable).
* **Vendor Description** (`Vendor Description`, note, optional): Enter a brief description of your company or organization (optional).
* **Trade Show Attendance** (`Trade Show Attendance`, select_one, required): Select the number of attendees you expect to attend the trade show:
	+ Option 1: Less than 10
	+ Option 2: 10-50
	+ Option 3: More than 50

* **Trade Show Venue** (`Trade Show Venue`, text, optional): Enter the name of the trade show venue (if applicable).
* **Trade Show Currency** (`Trade Show Currency`, select_one, optional): Select the currency of the trade show:
	+ Option 1

* **Trade Show Language** (`Trade Show Language`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant languages:

* **Trade Show Social Media** (`Trade Show Social Media`, text, optional): Enter any social media handles associated with the trade show (if applicable).
* **Trade Show Instagram** (`Trade Show Instagram`, text, optional): Enter the trade show's Instagram handle (if applicable).
* **Trade Show Facebook** (`Trade Show Facebook`, text, optional): Enter the trade show's Facebook handle (if applicable).
