# Personal And Family Data Update - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal And Family Data Update form is used to collect and update personal and family data from individuals. This form helps ensure that the provided information is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Use the "Next" button to navigate through the form pages.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (2)** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date Of Birth (3)** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Family Status (4)** (`family_status`, select_one, required): Select your current family status from the options:
	+ Single
	+ Married
	+ Divorced
	+ Separated
* **Family Members Count (6)** (`family_members_count`, number, required): Enter the number of family members you have.
* **Email (7)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted.
* **Phone (8)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address (9)** (`address`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of address:
	+ Physical
	+ Mailing
* **Relationship (10)** (`relationship`, select_one, required): Select your relationship to the individual:
	+ Parent
	+ Guardian
	+ Family member
* **Emergency Contact Name (11)** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (12)** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relation (13)** (`emergency_contact_relation`, select_one, optional): Select the relation of your emergency contact person:
	+ Parent
	+ Guardian
	+ Family member
* **Emergency Contact Note (14)** (`emergency_contact_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your emergency contact person.
* **Next Of Kin (15)** (`next_of_kin`, text, optional): Enter the name of your next of kin.
* **Next Of Kin Phone (16)** (`next_of_kin_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your next of kin.
* **Next Of Kin Relation (17)** (`next_of_kin_relation`, select_one, optional): Select the relation of your next of kin:
	+ Spouse
	+ Sibling
	+ Family member
* **Next Of Kin Phone 2 (18)** (`next_of_kin_phone_2`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your next of kin 2.
* **Next Of Kin Note (19)** (`next_of_kin_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your next of kin.
* **Medical Note (20)** (`medical_note`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical notes.
* **Family Medical History (21)** (`family_medical_history`, text, optional): Enter any relevant family medical history information.
* **Medical History Other (22)** (`medical_history_other`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant medical history information.
* **Medical History Note (23)** (`medical_history_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your medical history.
* **Medical History Date (24)** (`medical_history_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your medical history.
* **Medical History Time (25)** (`medical_history_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your medical history.
