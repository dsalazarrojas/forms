# Operator Information Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and professional information about operators for internal purposes such as HR, safety, and operational management.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please fill out the form carefully and accurately. If you need help or clarification on any field, refer to the explanations below.
*   Make sure to provide the required information for each field marked as "required".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`operator_information_collection_form_1`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Last Name** (`operator_information_collection_form_2`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title or position.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1999-02-15).
*   **Operator ID** (`operator_id`, number, required): Enter your unique operator ID.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the format 123-456-7890 (e.g., 123-456-7890).
*   **Operator Location** (`operator_location`, text, required): Enter your current or usual work location.
*   **Operator Department** (`operator_department`, select_one, required): Select your department from the options provided:
    *   Operator HR
    *   Safety
    *   Operations
*   **Operator Supervisor** (`operator_supervisor`, text, optional): Enter the name or ID of your supervisor.
*   **Operator Manager** (`operator_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name or ID of your manager.

## Tips

*   Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you're unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
*   Ensure you have the necessary permissions or access to complete this form.
*   If you have any concerns or issues with this form, please contact your departmental contact person or HR.
