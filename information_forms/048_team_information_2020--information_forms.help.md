# team_information_2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about a team, including team name, team leaders, and their contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Team Name** in the "Team Name" field.
2. Select the **Team Leaders** and enter their contact information.
3. Choose the **Team Members** and provide additional contact information and notes about them.
4. Review and verify the accuracy of all information entered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter the name of the team you are providing information for.
* **Team Leaders (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select "Yes" to include team leaders in this team, and "No" to exclude them.
* **Team Leaders Phone (Text, Optional)**: Enter the phone number of the team leaders. This field is only visible if "Team Leaders" is selected as "Yes".
* **Team Leaders Email (Email, Optional)**: Enter the email address of the team leaders. This field is only visible if "Team Leaders" is selected as "Yes".
* **Team Leaders Notes (Note, Optional)**: Provide any additional notes about the team leaders.
* **Team Leaders Category (Text, Optional)**: Enter any category or description for the team leaders.
* **Team Leaders Title (Text, Optional)**: Enter a title or position for the team leaders.
* **Team Members (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select "Yes" to include team members in this team, and "No" to exclude them.
* **Team Members Category (Text, Optional)**: Enter any category or description for the team members.
* **Team Members Notes (Note, Optional)**: Provide any additional notes about the team members.
* **Team Members Title (Text, Optional)**: Enter a title or position for the team members.
