# Daily Currency Exchange Rate Preferences - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user preferences for receiving daily currency exchange rate updates.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Select Your Currency Preferences

1. Select all the currencies you would like to receive updates for.
2. You can choose multiple currencies.

### Step 2: Choose Your Update Frequency

1. Choose how often you would like to receive updates.
2. You can choose from two options: "Yes" or "No".

### Step 3: Select Your Delivery Method

1. Choose your preferred delivery method for the updates.
2. You can choose from two options: "Yes" or "No".

### Step 4: Enter Your Currency Code

1. Enter the code for the currency you selected in Step 1.
2. This is a required field and should be entered correctly for accurate updates.

### Step 5: Enter Your Exchange Rate

1. Enter the exchange rate for the currency you selected.
2. This is a required field and should be entered correctly for accurate updates.

### Step 6: Enter Another Currency Code

1. Enter the code for another currency you would like to receive updates for.
2. This is a required field and should be entered correctly for accurate updates.

### Step 7: Select Your Time

1. Select the time you would like to receive the updates.
2. You can choose a time that is not too early or too late for your convenience.

### Step 8: Choose Your Time Format

1. Choose the format for the time you selected.
2. You can choose from two options, though this field is not clearly labeled and may have limited options.

### Step 9: Select Your Timezone

1. Select the timezone you are in.
2. This is a required field and should be entered correctly for accurate updates.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter all required fields correctly to ensure accurate updates.
* You can only submit this form once, so make sure to double-check your selections before submitting.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us for assistance.
