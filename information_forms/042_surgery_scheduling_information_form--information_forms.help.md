# Surgery Scheduling Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information for surgery scheduling purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that apply to your situation.
3. Enter your answers in the provided text fields.
4. Ensure that you have filled out all the required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide any relevant medical history that may be important for the surgery.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all the allergies that apply to you.
* **Diagnosis** (`diagnosis`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your diagnosis.
* **Procedure Steps** (`procedure_steps`, text, required: false): This field is for you to describe the steps involved in your procedure.
* **Type Anesthesia** (`type_anesthesia`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of anesthesia you will be receiving.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, required: false): Please enter your weight in kilograms (kg).
* **Height** (`height`, number, required: false): Please enter your height in centimeters (cm).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required: false): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your doctor.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your doctor.
* **Doctor Phone Extension** (`doctor_phone_extension`, select_one, required: false): Check if you have a phone extension for your doctor.

## Tips
* Please fill out all the required fields to ensure that your information is complete and accurate.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your doctor or medical staff for guidance.
* Double-check your answers to ensure that they are accurate and complete.
