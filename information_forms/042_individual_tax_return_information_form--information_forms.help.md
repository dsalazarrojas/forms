# Individual Tax Return Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect individual tax return information for tax filing purposes. It is intended for individuals who need to submit their tax returns to the relevant tax authorities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Review and submit the form carefully, as incorrect or incomplete information may lead to delays or issues with your tax return.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info** (Text): Enter your name and contact information.
* **tax_year** (Number): Enter the tax year for which you are filing your tax return.
* **tax_id_number** (Number): Enter your tax ID number, which is usually a 9-digit number.
* **tax_return_type** (Select One):
	+ Income: If you are filing an income tax return.
	+ W-2: If you are filing a W-2 tax return.
	+ Other: If you are filing another type of tax return.
	+ Partnership: If you are filing a partnership tax return.
	+ Trust: If you are filing a trust tax return.
* **gross_income** (Number): Enter your gross income for the tax year.
* **total_tax_deductions** (Number): Enter your total tax deductions for the tax year.
* **social_security_number** (Text): Enter your social security number.
* **date_of_birth** (Date): Enter your date of birth.
* **mailing_address** (Text): Enter your mailing address.
* **city** (Text): Enter the city where you live.
* **state** (Text): Enter the state where you live.
* **zip_code** (Text): Enter your zip code.
* **phone_number** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **signature** (Text): Sign and date your form.
* **upload_document** (Text): Upload any required supporting documents.
* **confirm_upload** (Select One):
	+ 'True': Confirm that you have uploaded a document.
	+ 'False': Confirm that no document has been uploaded.
* **uploaded_document** (Text): Enter the uploaded document's name and date.
* **tax_return_filing_status** (Select One):
	+ Pending Review: If your return is pending review.
	+ Accepted: If your return has been accepted.
	+ Rejected: If your return has been rejected.
	+ Not Reviewed: If your return is not reviewed.
* **tax_return_notes** (Note): Enter any additional notes about your tax return.
* **review_notes** (Note): Enter any notes from the reviewer.
* **review_comments** (Text): Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **review_decision** (Select One):
	+ 'True': If the reviewer has made a decision.
	+ 'False': If no decision has been made.
* **signature_status** (Select One):
	+ Complete: If you have signed the form.
	+ Incomplete: If you have not signed the form.
* **signature_date** (Date): Enter the date you signed the form.

Please review this form carefully and ensure that all fields are filled out correctly to avoid any issues with your tax return.
