# Secure Information Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for secure information submission and gathering purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with your information
2. Select the relevant option for the "Category" field
3. Upload any documents required for secure information submission, if applicable
4. Click the "submit" button to submit your information

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title or heading for the purpose of this submission.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name for our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Upload Documents** (`document_uploader`, text, optional): If applicable, upload any supporting documents required for this submission.
* **Document List** (`document_list`, select_multiple, optional): Select all documents that apply to this submission.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the information you're submitting.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, time, optional): Schedule a follow-up at your convenience.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Outline the next steps you'd like to take after submission.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes this submission.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click to submit your information for processing.
