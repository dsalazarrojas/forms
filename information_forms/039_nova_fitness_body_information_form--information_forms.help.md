# nova_fitness_body_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The nova_fitness_body_information_form is designed to collect information about a person's fitness goals and preferences. This form helps fitness professionals understand their clients' needs and tailor their services to meet those needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your height, weight, and age as required.
2. Indicate if you have any health issues or concerns.
3. Choose your activity level (High, Low, or None).
4. Select your preferred workout frequency (Yes, No).
5. Select your preferred workout duration (Yes, No).
6. Select your preferred workout style (Yes, No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form** (`form_header`, note, not required): This is just a placeholder header to separate the form fields from the rest of the content.
* **Height** (`height`, number, not required): Enter your height in the corresponding units (e.g., cm or inches).
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, not required): Enter your weight in the corresponding units (e.g., kg or pounds).
* **Age** (`age`, number, not required): Enter your age.
* **Height Unit** (`height_unit`, select_one, required): Check if you have any health issues or concerns related to your height.
* **Weight Unit** (`weight_unit`, select_one, required): Check if you have any health issues or concerns related to your weight.
* **Activity Level** (`activity_level`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your current activity level (High, Low, or None).
* **Preferred Workout Frequency** (`preferred_workout_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select the frequency of your preferred workouts (Yes, No).
* **Preferred Workout Duration** (`preferred_workout_duration`, select_multiple, required): Select the duration of your preferred workouts (Yes, No).
* **Preferred Workout Style** (`preferred_workout_style`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of workout you prefer (Yes, No).
* **Other Workout Style** (`other_workout_style`, text, not required): If none of the above options fit, describe your preferred workout style.
* **Other** (`other`, text, not required): If you have any other information that might be helpful for your fitness professional, enter it here.

## Tips
Remember to provide accurate and honest answers to help your fitness professional tailor their services to your needs.
