# BHHS Generic - Listing Information (Internal Use) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and display essential details about a property, including its listing information and property features.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, paying attention to all required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
- Enter accurate information for each field, using the hints and options as a guide.
- Review your entries for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **MLS Number** (`mls_number`): Enter the Multiple Listing Service (MLS) number, which is a unique identifier for the property.
* **Listing Date** (`listing_date`): Enter the date the property was listed.
* **Listing Agent Name** (`listing_agent_name`): Enter the name of the listing agent.
* **Listing Agent Contact Information** (`listing_agent_contact`): Enter the contact information for the listing agent, including phone and email.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`): Enter the full street address of the property.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`): Select the type of property, such as Single Family, Condo, Townhome, Multi-Family, Land, or Other.
* **Number of Bedrooms** (`bedrooms`): Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (`bathrooms`): Enter the number of bathrooms in the property.
* **Square Footage** (`square_footage`): Enter the total square footage of the property.
* **Lot Size** (`lot_size`): Enter the size of the lot in acres or square feet.
* **Year Built** (`year_built`): Enter the year the property was built.
* **Number of Garage Spaces** (`garage_spaces`): Enter the number of garage spaces available.
* **Appointment System Status** (`appointment_system_status`): Select whether the appointment system is active or inactive.
* **Showing Instructions** (`showing_instructions`): Enter any special instructions for showings.
* **Internal Notes** (`internal_notes`): Enter internal notes for the agent's reference.

Please note that this form is intended for internal use only, and the fields marked as "required" are essential for accurate representation of the property's details.
