# cotc_nursing_information_meeting - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form gathers information about your nursing experience, skills, and any other relevant details related to the Nursing Information Meeting. It will help the meeting organizers to better understand your background and provide a valuable resource for the attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide your Phone number.
4. Indicate if you work in a hospital or not.
5. Select the skills you have mastered from the options provided.
6. Choose a date for the meeting (if applicable).
7. Select a time for the meeting (if applicable).
8. Add any additional notes or comments about your experience or skills.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your Email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your Phone number for contact or reference.
* **Do you work in a hospital?** (`select_one`, select_one, required: false): Check 'True' if you work in a hospital or 'False' if not.
* **Which of the following skills have you mastered?** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the skills you have mastered from the provided options (First Aide, CPR, Medical Record Keeper).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Choose a date for the meeting (if applicable).
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select a time for the meeting (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your experience or skills.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, please consult with the meeting organizers or your colleagues.
* If you have any other relevant information that's not covered in this form, feel free to add it in the 'Note' field.
