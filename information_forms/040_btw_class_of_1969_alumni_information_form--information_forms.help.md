# BTW Class Of 1969 Alumni Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for BTW Class of 1969 alumni to provide their updated contact information and details about their life after graduation.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field as instructed:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name.
2. Provide your current Email Address.
3. If applicable, enter your Maiden Name.
4. Enter your Phone Number (optional).
5. Enter your Current Address (optional).
6. Enter your City (optional).
7. Enter your State and ZIP Code (optional).
8. Enter your Country (optional).
9. Select your Current Employment Status.
10. Enter your Profession or Career Field (optional).
11. Enter your Company or Organization Name (optional).
12. Select your Family Status.
13. Enter the Number of Children you have (optional).
14. Share your memorable School Experiences (optional).
15. List your Notable Achievements Since Graduation (optional).
16. Indicate if you're interested in staying in touch with classmates.
17. Indicate if you're interested in Class Reunion Events.
18. Provide your Social Media Profile (optional).
19. Grant permission to share your photo in the directory (optional).
20. Add any additional information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is currently used.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is currently used.
* **Maiden Name (if applicable)**: If you were married or had a different last name before, enter it here.
* **Email Address**: Enter your current email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your current phone number (optional).
* **Current Address**: Enter your current full street address (optional).
* **City**: Enter your city (optional).
* **State and ZIP Code**: Enter your state and ZIP Code (optional).
* **Country**: Enter your country (optional).
* **Current Employment Status**: Select your current employment status.
* **Profession or Career Field**: Enter your current occupation or career field (optional).
* **Company or Organization Name**: Enter your current company or organization name (optional).
* **Family Status**: Select your current family status.
* **Number of Children**: Enter the number of children you have (optional).
* **Memorable School Experiences**: Share your favorite memories from school.
* **Notable Achievements**: List any notable achievements or awards since graduation.
* **Interested in Staying in Touch with Classmates**: Indicate if you're interested in staying in touch with classmates.
* **Interested in Class Reunion Events**: Indicate if you're interested in attending class reunions.
* **Social Media Profile**: Enter your social media profile (optional).
* **Permission to Share Photo in Directory**: Indicate if you're willing to share your photo in the directory.
* **Additional Information**: Add any other information you'd like to share with the class.
