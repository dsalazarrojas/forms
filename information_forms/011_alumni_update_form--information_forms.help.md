# Alumni Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alumni Update Form is designed to gather information from alumni about their personal and professional updates. This information will be used to stay connected with graduates, provide support, and facilitate networking within the university community.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately with your updated information.
2. Review each section carefully, and ensure you answer all required fields.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, contact [support email or contact information].

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Maiden Name** (`maiden_name`, text, optional): If you have a maiden name, please enter it.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, number, required): Please enter the year you graduated from the university.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, text, optional): If you remember your student ID number, please enter it.
* **Primary Email Address** (`email_primary`, email, required): Please enter your primary email address that you would like to use for communication.
* **Secondary Email Address** (`email_secondary`, email, optional): Enter an optional secondary email address for backup purposes.
* **Mobile Phone Number** (`phone_mobile`, text, required): Please enter your mobile phone number.
* **Home Phone Number** (`phone_home`, text, optional): If you have a home phone number, please enter it.
* **Work Phone Number** (`phone_work`, text, optional): If you have a work phone number, please enter it.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required): Please enter your current street address.
* **Apartment/Suite Number** (`apartment_suite`, text, optional): If you live in an apartment or suite, please enter the number.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Please enter the city where you currently live.
* **State/Province** (`state_province`, text, required): Please enter the state or province where you currently live.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`zip_postal`, text, required): Please enter your current ZIP or postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required): Please select your country of residence.
* **Current Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Please select your current employment status.
* **Employer/Organization Name** (`employer_name`, text, optional): If you have an employer or organization, please enter their name.
* **Job Title/Position** (`job_title`, text, optional): If you have a job title or position, please enter it.
* **Industry/Field** (`industry_field`, select_one, optional): Please select the industry or field where you work.
* **Work Email Address** (`work_email`, email, optional): If you have a work email address, please enter it.
* **Work Address** (`work_address`, text, optional): If you have a work address, please enter it.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, select_one, optional): Please select your marital status.
* **Spouse/Partner Name** (`spouse_name`, text, optional): If you have a spouse or partner, please enter their name.
* **Is your spouse/partner also an alumnus** (`spouse_alumni_status`, select_one, optional): If your spouse or partner is also an alumnus, please select this option.
* **Number of Children** (`children_count`, number, optional): If you have children, please enter the number.
* **Alumni Newsletter Subscription** (`newsletter_subscription`, select_one, required): Please select how you would like to receive the alumni newsletter.
* **Event Notifications** (`event_notifications`, select_one, required): Please select whether you would like to receive event notifications.
* **Include in Alumni Directory** (`directory_listing`, select_one, required): Please select whether you would like to be included in the alumni directory.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Please select your preferred contact method.
* **Interest in Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, optional): If you are interested in volunteering, please select this option.
* **Available to Mentor Students** (`mentorship_availability`, select_one, optional): If you are available to mentor students, please select this option.
* **Interest in Making Donations** (`donation_interest`, select_one, optional): If you are interested in making donations, please select this option.
* **Recent Achievements or News** (`achievements`, text, optional): Please share any recent achievements or news about yourself.
* **Additional Comments or Updates** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Please share any additional comments or updates.
* **Date of Update** (`update_date`, date, required): Please enter the date of your update.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [support email or contact information].
