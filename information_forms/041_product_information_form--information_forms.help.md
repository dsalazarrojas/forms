# Product Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Information Form collects details about products to be used for product management, e-commerce, or sales purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product name in the "Product Name" field.
2. Add a description of the product in the "Product Description" field.
3. Select the product category from the available options in the "Category" field.
4. Choose the status of the product from the available options in the "Status" field.
5. Input the date of the product in the "Date" field.
6. Enter the time of the product in the "Time" field.
7. Add any additional notes about the product in the "Notes" field.
8. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. Input the product's address in the "Address" field.
11. Paste the product's image URL in the "Image" field.
12. If available, paste the product's video URL in the "Video" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter the name of the product.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Add a description of the product.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, Number, Required/Optional: Optional): Input the price of the product (if applicable).
* **Category** (`product_category`, Select One, Required/Optional: Optional): Select the product category from the available options.
* **Status** (`product_status`, Select Multiple, Required/Optional: Optional): Choose the status of the product from the available options.
* **Date** (`product_date`, Date, Required/Optional: Optional): Input the date of the product.
* **Time** (`product_time`, Time, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter the time of the product.
* **Notes** (`product_notes`, Note, Required/Optional: Optional): Add any additional notes about the product.
* **Email** (`product_email`, Email, Required/Optional: Optional): Provide your email address (for product communication).
* **Phone** (`product_phone`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter your phone number (for product communication).
* **Address** (`product_address`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Input the product's address.
* **Image** (`product_image`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Paste the product's image URL.
* **Video** (`product_video`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): If available, paste the product's video URL.
