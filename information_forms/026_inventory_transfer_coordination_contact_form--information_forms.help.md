# Inventory Transfer Coordination Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about inventory transfers, including request details, contact information, and transfer specifics. It helps employees and stakeholders track and manage inventory movement within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the inventory request in the "Request Details" field.
2. Enter your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields, respectively.
3. Specify the department related to the inventory request.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the type of transfer, either internal or external, in the "Transfer Type" field.
6. Enter the date of the transfer in the "Transfer Date" field.
7. Specify the time of the transfer in the "Transfer Time" field.
8. Add any additional comments or notes about the transfer in the "Notes" field.
9. Select the tool(s) assigned to the transfer from the list.
10. Indicate whether a user is assigned to the transfer from the list.
11. Choose the status of the request from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Request Details
**Request Details (1)** (text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the inventory request.

### Name
**Name (2)** (text): Enter your name.

### Email
**Email (3)** (email): Enter your email address.

### Department
**Department (4)** (text): Specify the department related to the inventory request.

### Phone
**Phone (5)** (text): Enter your phone number.

### Transfer Type
**Transfer Type (6)** (select_multiple): Select the type of transfer, either internal or external.

### Transfer Date
**Transfer Date (7)** (date): Enter the date of the transfer.

### Transfer Time
**Transfer Time (8)** (time): Specify the time of the transfer.

### Notes
**Notes (9)** (note): Add any additional comments or notes about the transfer.

### Assigned Tool
**Assigned Tool (10)** (select_one): Select the tool(s) assigned to the transfer from the list.

### Assigned User
**Assigned User (11)** (select_one): Indicate whether a user is assigned to the transfer from the list.

### Request Status
**Request Status (12)** (select_one): Choose the status of the request from the list.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the transfer information is up-to-date and relevant.
* If you are unsure about any field, please review the form again before submitting it.
* If you need help with the form, do not hesitate to contact your supervisor or the inventory management team.
