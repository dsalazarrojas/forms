# research_data_handling_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and record information on the status of research audits. This includes details on the audit date, research team, findings, actions taken, review status, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have access to the form. If you're unsure, please contact your administrator.
2. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. For fields with select options, choose the appropriate option that best describes the information.
4. Enter any additional comments or details as needed in the text fields.
5. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
