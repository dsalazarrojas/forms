# Dental Patient Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect basic patient information for dental or administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2.  Make sure to double-check your entries before submitting.
3.  Do not submit incomplete forms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's full name.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the patient's contact email address.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
*   **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's contact address.
*   **Relationship** (`patient_relationship`, `text`, optional): Select or describe your relationship to the patient, such as family member or guardian.

## Tips
*   Please ensure you have the patient's consent to share their information in the form.
*   In case of any doubts or uncertainties, consult the medical staff before filling out the form.
*   Review the submitted form to ensure all required fields are filled before sending it to the relevant parties.
*   If you need further assistance or clarification, reach out to the form administrators.
