# theater_audience_information_collection - Help Guide
## Purpose

The **theater_audience_information_collection** form is designed to collect basic information about the theater audience. This data can be used for various purposes such as marketing analysis, demographic studies, or customer service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Follow the instructions on each page to fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, Optional): Enter your age in whole numbers, without any symbols or punctuation.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Optional): Enter a valid email address you use to communicate with us.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, Optional): Enter a phone number where you can be reached, if applicable.
* **Performance Date** (`performance_date`, `date`, Optional): Select the date you plan to attend a performance at the theater.

Note: Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that we can serve you best.
