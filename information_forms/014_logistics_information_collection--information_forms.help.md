# Logistics Information Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Logistics Information Collection form is designed to gather essential information about deliveries, shipments, and contact details. This form helps to streamline communication and data collection for logistics-related tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with the relevant information.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated as "required" in the form).
3. If a field is not required, feel free to leave it blank or leave it for later completion.
4. Review your completed form for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the delivery, including any relevant delivery information such as shipping carrier, tracking number, and estimated delivery date.
- **Shipment Info** (`shipment_info`, text, required/optional): Provide details about the shipment, including package weight, dimensions, and any special handling requirements.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the delivery or shipment.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional contact details for the person or company handling the delivery or shipment.
- **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant notes about the delivery, such as special instructions or specific delivery location.

## Tips
- Double-check your contact person's and contact details for accuracy to ensure smooth communication.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to your internal guidelines or contact your team lead for clarification.
- This form is designed to be flexible; you can add or modify fields as per your specific needs.
