# Coaching Position Expression Of Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us assess your eligibility and interest in the coaching position. Please complete the form carefully, making sure to provide accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the personal information section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. In the Position Interest section, select the sport or field of coaching you are interested in.
3. Provide your relevant experience, certifications, and education.
4. Answer the questions about your motivation and goals.
5. List your professional reference's name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Current Location** (`current_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the city and state where you currently live.
* **Coaching Position Title** (`position_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the coaching position you are interested in.
* **Organization or Team Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the organization or team you are interested in coaching with.
* **Sport or Field of Coaching** (`sport_or_field`, `select_one`, required): Select the sport or field of coaching you are interested in (e.g. Baseball, Basketball, Soccer, etc.).
* **Years of Relevant Experience** (`experience_level`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have relevant experience in the coaching position.
* **Relevant Certifications** (`certifications`, `text`, optional): List any relevant certifications you hold.
* **Highest Education Level** (`education`, `select_one`, required): Select your highest education level (e.g. High School, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, etc.).
* **First Aid and CPR Certified** (`first_aid_cpr`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are first aid and CPR certified.
* **Why are you interested in this position?** (`interest_reason`, `text`, required): Explain why you are interested in the coaching position.
* **What are your coaching goals?** (`coaching_goals`, `text`, optional): Enter your short and long-term coaching goals.
* **Availability to Start** (`availability`, `select_one`, required): Select when you can start the coaching position.
* **Reference Name** (`reference_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your professional reference.
* **Reference Contact Information** (`reference_contact`, `text`, required): Enter your reference's email or phone number.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting your application.

Please note that this is a sample help guide, and the form may be subject to changes based on your organization's requirements.
