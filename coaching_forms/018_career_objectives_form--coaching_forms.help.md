# Career Objectives Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you define your career objectives and identify areas where you'd like to focus your coaching sessions. Please answer these questions as accurately as possible to help your coach understand your goals and provide tailored guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, such as your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide information about your current role, including how long you've been in it and your level of satisfaction.
3. Describe your primary career objectives, short-term goals, and long-term goals.
4. Indicate how clear your goals are and whether you're interested in career advancement.
5. Share your strengths, areas for improvement, obstacles to your goals, and work style preferences.
6. Consider how well your current role aligns with your values and what kind of work-life balance you aim for.
7. If applicable, specify your income or financial goals.
8. Outline specific action steps to achieve your objectives.
9. Choose the coaching support you'd find most helpful.
10. Review and submit your answers for your coach to understand your career aspirations and develop a plan to support you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Your personal email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Include your area code for proper identification.
* **Date of Career Coaching Session** (`coaching_date`, date, required): Schedule the coaching session date for optimal preparation.
* **Current Position or Job Title** (`current_position`, text, required): Describe your current job title or position.
* **Company or Industry** (`company_industry`, text, required): State your company or industry name.
* **How Long in Current Role?** (`years_in_role`, number, optional): Estimate the years or months you've been in your current role.
* **Satisfaction with Current Role** (`current_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select a level of satisfaction (Very satisfied - love my job, Satisfied - generally happy, etc.).
* **What Are Your Primary Career Objectives?** (`primary_objectives`, text, required): Specify your main career aims.
* **Short-Term Goals (Next 6-12 Months)** (`short_term_goals`, text, required): Describe what you'd like to accomplish within the next 6-12 months.
* **Medium-Term Goals (1-3 Years)** (`medium_term_goals`, text, required): Indicate where you'd like to be in 1-3 years.
* **Long-Term Goals (5+ Years)** (`long_term_goals`, text, required): Envision your career path beyond 5 years.
* **How Clear Are These Goals?** (`goal_specificity`, select_one, required): Rate your goals' clarity (Very clear - specific and measurable, Somewhat clear - general direction, etc.).
* **Are You Interested in Career Advancement?** (`advancement_interests`, select_one, required): Decide if you're interested in advancement opportunities (Yes - promotion within company, Yes - new role in different company, etc.).
* **Skills You Want to Develop** (`skill_development`, text, optional): List the new competencies you'd like to acquire.
* **Plans for Further Education or Training** (`education_plans`, text, optional): Specify your plans for further education or training (degrees, certifications, courses, etc.).
* **Your Key Strengths** (`strengths`, text, required): Enumerate your key strengths.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_improvement`, text, required): Identify the areas where you'd like to improve.
* **Obstacles or Challenges to Your Goals** (`obstacles`, text, optional): Mention any obstacles or challenges you're facing.
* **Work Style Preferences** (`work_style_preferences`, text, optional): Describe your ideal work environment.
* **How Well Does Your Current Role Align with Your Values?** (`values_alignment`, select_one, optional): Rate the alignment between your role and your values (Perfect alignment, Good alignment, etc.).
* **Work-Life Balance Goals** (`work_life_balance`, text, optional): Describe your desired balance between work and personal life.
* **Income or Financial Goals** (`income_goals`, text, optional): Specify your income or financial targets.
* **Specific Action Steps to Achieve Goals** (`action_steps`, text, optional): Outline concrete steps to reach your objectives.
* **What Coaching Support Would Help Most?** (`coaching_focus`, select_multiple, required): Choose the areas where you need the most coaching support (Career strategy and planning, Skill development, etc.).
