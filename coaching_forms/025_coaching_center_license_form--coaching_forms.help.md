# Coaching Center License Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive document that gathers essential information from coaching centers seeking to obtain a coaching license. It ensures that the center meets the necessary requirements and standards for licensing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. **Read through each section carefully** and ensure you understand the information being requested.
2. **Fill in all required fields**, as indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol next to the field.
3. **Select the correct options** from the dropdown menus for each field.
4. **Double-check your answers** before submitting the form.
5. **Submit the form once you have completed all required fields**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coaching Center Name** (`id: 1`, `name: center_name`): Enter the official name of your coaching center.
* **Business Address** (`id: 2`, `name: business_address`): Enter the full street address of your coaching center.
* **City** (`id: 3`, `name: city`): Enter the city location of your coaching center.
* **State or Province** (`id: 4`, `name: state_province`): Enter the state or province where your coaching center operates.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`id: 5`, `name: zip_postal_code`): Enter the postal code of your coaching center.
* **Country** (`id: 6`, `name: country`): Enter the country of operation of your coaching center.
* **Owner or Director Name** (`id: 7`, `name: owner_name`): Enter the full name of the owner or director of your coaching center.
* **Owner Email** (`id: 8`, `name: owner_email`): Enter the contact email address of your coaching center.
* **Owner Phone** (`id: 9`, `name: owner_phone`): Enter the contact phone number of your coaching center.
* **Business Structure** (`id: 10`, `name: business_structure`): Select the type of business entity your coaching center is registered as.
* **Business Registration Number** (`id: 11`, `name: business_registration_number`): Enter your official business ID number.
* **Tax ID Number** (`id: 12`, `name: tax_id_number`): Enter your tax identification number.
* **License Type Requested** (`id: 13`, `name: license_type_requested`): Select the type of license your coaching center is seeking.
* **Services Offered** (`id: 14`, `name: services_offered`): Select all services provided by your coaching center.
* **Number of Employed Coaches** (`id: 15`, `name: number_of_coaches`): Select the number of coaches employed at your center.
* **Coach Certification Requirement** (`id: 16`, `name: coach_certification_requirement`): Indicate whether your coaches must be certified.
* **Facility Type** (`id: 17`, `name: facility_type`): Select the type of facility your coaching center operates from.
* **Facility Square Footage** (`id: 18`, `name: facility_square_footage`): Select the approximate size of your facility.
* **Liability Insurance** (`id: 19`, `name: liability_insurance`): Indicate whether your coaching center has liability insurance.
* **Insurance Provider** (`id: 20`, `name: insurance_provider`): Enter the name of your insurance company (if applicable).
* **Insurance Policy Amount** (`id: 21`, `name: insurance_policy_amount`): Select the total coverage amount of your insurance policy (if applicable).
* **Safety Protocols** (`id: 22`, `name: safety_protocols`): Indicate whether your coaching center has written safety protocols.
* **Emergency Procedures** (`id: 23`, `name: emergency_procedures`): Indicate whether your coaching center has emergency procedures documented.
* **Years in Operation** (`id: 24`, `name: years_in_operation`): Select the length of time your coaching center has been in operation.
* **Previous Licenses** (`id: 25`, `name: previous_licenses`): Indicate whether your coaching center has held a license before.
* **License Violations** (`id: 26`, `name: license_violations`): Select whether your coaching center has experienced past license violations.
* **Violation Details** (`id: 27`, `name: violation_details`): Enter any details about past license violations (if applicable).
* **Continuing Education** (`id: 28`, `name: continuing_education_program`): Indicate whether your coaching center offers training for coaches.
* **Application Fee Paid** (`id: 29`, `name: application_fee_paid`): Indicate whether you have paid the application fee.
* **I Accept Terms and Conditions** (`id: 30`, `name: terms_and_conditions`): Confirm that you have read and accepted the licensing terms.
* **Application Date** (`id: 31`, `name: signature_date`): Enter the current date.

## Tips
- Please make sure to answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your coaching center's representative for clarification.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors or omissions.
