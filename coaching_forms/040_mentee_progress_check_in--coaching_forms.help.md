# mentee_progress_check_in - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "mentee_progress_check_in" form is a tool for mentors to document the progress of their mentees after each session. It helps to keep track of the mentee's current state, set goals for the next session, and note any progress or areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the session with your mentee and identify the key outcomes and progress.
2. Select the relevant mentoring outcomes from the "Mentoring Outcomes" field.
3. Choose the relevant session outcomes from the "Session Outcomes" field.
4. Enter the date and time of the next session in the "Next Session" field.
5. Provide a brief summary of the progress and any notes in the "Progress Notes" field.
6. Set the frequency of future sessions in the "Session Frequency" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mentoring Outcomes**: Select the mentoring outcomes that were achieved during this session.
* **Session Outcomes**: Select the outcomes that were achieved during this session.
* **Next Session**: Enter the date and time of the next session.
* **Progress Notes**: Briefly describe the progress and any notable points from the session.
* **Session Frequency**: Set the frequency of future sessions (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
