# Beauty Business Mentorship Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the steps to fill out the Beauty Business Mentorship Registration Form. This form is designed to collect information from beauty business professionals who are interested in joining a mentorship program. The data collected will help match mentors with mentees based on their specific needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Applicant Information**: Provide your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Business Location.
2. **Business Information**: Share information about your business, including Business Name, Business Type, Years in Business, Approximate Annual Revenue, and Number of Employees.
3. **Mentorship Goals**: Specify your primary mentorship goals, the areas needing most help, and the main challenge you're currently facing.
4. **Professional Experience**: Share your years of experience in the beauty industry, any relevant certifications, and if you have previous mentorship or coaching experience.
5. **Mentorship Format and Availability**: Choose your preferred mentorship format, meeting frequency, and hours per week available for mentorship.
6. **Commitment and Availability**: Select your desired mentorship duration and confirm your budget (optional).
7. **Agree to Terms**: Finally, confirm that you agree to the mentorship program terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Business Location**: Enter your business location (city and state).
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your beauty business.
* **Business Type**: Choose the type of your business from the provided options (Salon, Spa, Esthetics Studio, Freelance Artist, Online Store, Coaching Service, or Other).
* **Years in Business**: Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Approximate Annual Revenue**: Select the approximate annual revenue of your business from the provided options (Under 50k, 50k-100k, 100k-250k, 250k-500k, 500k+).
* **Number of Employees**: Enter the number of employees you have in your business.
* **Primary Mentorship Goals**: Specify your main reason for seeking mentorship (Business growth, marketing, financial management, etc.).
* **Areas Needing Most Help**: Choose the areas of your business that require the most help (Business Strategy, Marketing, Financial Management, Staff Management, Product Development, Customer Service).
* **Main Challenge**: Describe the main challenge you're currently facing in your business.
* **Years in Beauty Industry**: Enter the number of years you've been working in the beauty industry.
* **Professional Certifications**: Enter any relevant certifications you hold (e.g., esthetician license).
* **Previous Mentorship or Coaching Experience**: Check if you have previous mentorship or coaching experience.
* **Preferred Mentorship Format**: Choose your preferred mentorship format (One-on-One, Group, Hybrid, Online, In-Person).
* **Preferred Meeting Frequency**: Select the frequency of meetings you'd like to have with your mentor (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
* **Hours per Week Available for Mentorship**: Enter the number of hours you can dedicate to mentorship per week.
* **Desired Mentorship Duration**: Choose the duration of the mentorship (3 Months, 6 Months, 12 Months, Ongoing).
* **Mentorship Budget**: Enter your budget for the mentorship program (optional).
* **I Agree to Terms**: Confirm that you agree to the terms of the mentorship program.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the best possible match with a mentor.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
* This form is confidential and will be reviewed internally to match you with a suitable mentor.
