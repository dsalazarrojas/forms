# executive_coaching_interest_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The executive coaching interest form is designed to help executives and leaders express their interest in undergoing coaching. This form will help us better understand their preferences for coaching frequency, location, topic, and duration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the potential participants for coaching. This will help us identify who will be receiving coaching sessions.
2. Choose the coaching topics that interest you the most. Coaching topics can include executive development, team development, leadership development, or communication skills, or other topics you'd like to explore.
3. Select your preferred coaching location. You can choose to have coaching sessions in person, over the phone, or through video conference.
4. Choose your preferred coaching frequency and duration. Select how often you'd like to have coaching sessions (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly) and how long each session should last (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours).
5. Provide your coaching start and end dates. Enter the dates when you'd like to start and end your coaching sessions.
6. Confirm your coaching preferences for frequency and duration. This is a duplicate of step 4 to ensure consistency across both coaches.
7. Enter the name of the participant(s) you'd like to work with. This is a required field to ensure that we know who the coaching sessions are for.
8. Enter the participant's job title to ensure we have accurate information on who the participant is.
9. Enter the participant's email address so we can keep in touch with them.
10. Enter the participant's phone number for easy contact.
11. Optionally, you can enter a note to add any additional information about your coaching preferences.
