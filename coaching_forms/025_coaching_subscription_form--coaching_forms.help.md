# Coaching Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking coaching services. It is intended for those who wish to subscribe to a coaching program, specifying their personal goals, preferred coaching preferences, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "Subscriber Information" section and fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address.
2. Choose a subscription plan that suits your needs from the dropdown list.
3. Enter your plan details in the text box.
4. Specify your monthly cost in the designated field.
5. Select the date you wish to start your subscription.
6. In the "Personal Goals" section, describe your objectives for seeking coaching services.
7. Select all relevant focus areas from the list that apply to you.
8. In the "Coaching Preferences" section, choose your preferred coach gender, coaching format, session frequency, and meeting times.
9. Select your preferred payment method and billing frequency.
10. Read and agree to the subscription terms and conditions, and acknowledge the cancellation policy.
11. Finally, select the date you wish to start your subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Subscription Plan** (`subscription_plan`, `select_one`, required): Select the subscription plan that suits your needs.
* **Plan Details** (`plan_details`, `text`, required): Describe the selected plan.
* **Monthly Cost** (`monthly_cost`, `number`, required): Enter your monthly cost.
* **Subscription Start Date** (`subscription_start_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you wish to start your subscription.
* **What are your coaching goals?** (`coaching_goals`, `text`, required): Describe your objectives for seeking coaching services.
* **Focus Areas** (`focus_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant focus areas that apply to you.
* **Preferred Coach Gender** (`preferred_coach_gender`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred coach gender.
* **Preferred Coaching Format** (`coaching_format`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred coaching format.
* **Preferred Session Frequency** (`session_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred session frequency.
* **Preferred Meeting Times** (`preferred_times`, `text`, optional): Enter your preferred meeting times.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Billing Frequency** (`billing_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose your billing frequency.
* **Subscription Terms Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Read and agree to the subscription terms and conditions.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment** (`cancellation_policy_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge the cancellation policy.
* **Subscription Date** (`subscription_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you wish to start your subscription.
