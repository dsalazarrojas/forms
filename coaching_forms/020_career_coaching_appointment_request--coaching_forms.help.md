# Career Coaching Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a career coaching appointment with our coaching services. We help individuals explore their career goals and aspirations, and provide guidance on career transition, job search strategy, resume and cover letter review, and more. Please ensure you have not previously submitted a request and are not currently under active coaching.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your preferred date and time for the appointment from the provided options.
3. Choose your preferred appointment format (in-person, video call, or phone call).
4. Select your time zone.
5. Answer the required fields regarding your career goals, preferences, and availability.
6. If you have any additional information or special circumstances, please include it in the "Any Additional Information to Share" field.
7. Confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to our coaching terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Please enter your first and last name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for appointment confirmations and future communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for appointment reminders and potential follow-up calls.
* **Date of Request**: Select the date you would like to schedule your appointment. Please note that our coaching team will contact you to confirm the appointment.
* **Current Employment Status**: Choose the status that best describes your current employment situation.
* **Current Industry or Field**: If applicable, enter the industry or field you are currently working in.
* **Current Job Title**: If applicable, enter your current job title.
* **Years of Experience**: Select the number of years you have worked in your field.
* **Highest Level of Education**: Choose the highest level of education you have achieved.
* **Primary Reason for Seeking Coaching**: Select the reason you are seeking coaching services.
* **Specific Goals for Coaching**: Enter the specific goals you hope to achieve through coaching.
* **Preferred Appointment Date**: Select your first choice for the appointment date.
* **Alternative Appointment Date**: If available, select a second choice for the appointment date (optional).
* **Preferred Time of Day**: Select the time of day you prefer for the appointment.
* **Preferred Appointment Format**: Choose how you would like to conduct the appointment (in-person, video call, or phone call).
* **Your Time Zone**: Select your time zone to ensure scheduling accuracy.
* **Have You Had Career Coaching Before**: Indicate if you have had career coaching before.
* **What Would Make Coaching Most Helpful for You**: Select all that apply to help us tailor our services to your needs.
* **How Urgent Is Your Need for Coaching**: Choose how urgent your need for coaching is.
* **Your General Availability**: Select all the times and days you are available for the appointment.
* **How Did You Hear About Our Coaching Services**: Indicate how you heard about our coaching services.
* **Any Special Circumstances**: If you have any special needs or circumstances, please share them with us.
* **Coaching Budget Range**: If you have a budget in mind, select the range.
* **Interested in Coaching Package**: If interested in multiple sessions at a discounted rate, select "Yes - interested in package".
* **Any Additional Information to Share**: If you have any additional information or preferences, please include them here.
* **I Agree to the Coaching Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you have read and agree to our coaching terms and conditions.
* **Preferred Coach**: If you have a preferred coach, please enter their name or ID (optional).

## Tips

- Please ensure that you have not previously submitted a request and are not currently under active coaching.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
- Our team will review your request and contact you to confirm your appointment details.
- Please note that our coaching services are subject to availability and appointment scheduling.
