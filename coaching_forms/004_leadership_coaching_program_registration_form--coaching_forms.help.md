# leadership_coaching_program_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The leadership coaching program registration form is used to collect information from candidates for the leadership coaching program. This form is designed to be completed by the candidate themselves and will be used to create their personalized coaching plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter your address in the "address" field.
6. Provide your birth date in the "birth date" field.
7. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are interested in the coaching program.
8. Enter the name of your coach in the "coach name" field.
9. Enter the start and end dates of your coaching program in the "program start date" and "program end date" fields respectively.
10. Enter the name of the training organization you are affiliated with in the "training organization" field.
11. Enter the name of the training program you are participating in in the "training program name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (first_name, text, optional):** Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (last_name, text, optional):** Enter your last name.
* **Email (email, email, optional):** Enter your email address.
* **Phone (phone, text, optional):** Enter your phone number.
* **Address (address, text, optional):** Enter your address.
* **Birth Date (birth_date, date, optional):** Enter your birth date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Coaching Program (coaching_program, select_one, optional):** Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are interested in the coaching program.
* **Coach Name (coach_name, text, optional):** Enter the name of your coach.
* **Program Start Date (program_start_date, date, optional):** Enter the start date of your coaching program.
* **Program End Date (program_end_date, date, optional):** Enter the end date of your coaching program.
* **Training Organization (training_organization, text, optional):** Enter the name of the training organization you are affiliated with.
* **Training Program Name (training_program_name, text, optional):** Enter the name of the training program you are participating in.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields marked as "optional" are filled out according to your preference.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the coaching team.
* Ensure that you have already read and understood the terms and conditions before proceeding.
