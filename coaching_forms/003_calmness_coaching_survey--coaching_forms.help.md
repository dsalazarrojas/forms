# Calmness Coaching Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences, preferences, and expectations related to calmness and wellness coaching. It will help coaching professionals understand your needs and preferences to provide effective coaching.
## How To Complete This Form
* Please fill out the form carefully, answering each question truthfully.
* You can select multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections.
* Use the hints provided to help you complete each field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address you would like to use for further communication.
* **Age Range**: Select your age range from the provided options.
* **Current Stress Level**: Rate your current stress level from 1 to 10.
* **Calmness Goal**: Select your primary goal for achieving calmness.
* **Sources of Stress**: Select all sources of stress that apply to you.
* **Coping Strategies**: Select all coping strategies that you currently use.
* **Meditation Experience**: Select your experience level with meditation.
* **Coaching Preference**: Select your preferred coaching format.
* **Coaching Frequency**: Select how often you would like to have coaching sessions.
* **Time of Day**: Select all times of day that work best for you.
* **Past Coaching Experience**: Indicate if you have worked with a calmness or wellness coach before.
* **Motivation Level**: Rate your motivation level for achieving calmness.
* **Support System**: Select your level of support system at home.
* **Health Conditions**: Select any health conditions that apply to you.
* **Expectations**: Describe your expectations from the coaching program.
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional comments or thoughts you have.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Take your time to carefully read each question and select the best option.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
* This form is for informational purposes only, and the information you provide will be used to help coaching professionals understand your needs and preferences.
