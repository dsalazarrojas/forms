# Mobility Coaching Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobility Coaching Experience Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from clients about their experience with a mobility coaching session. It's a short, 6-question survey that will help us understand your satisfaction, session quality, and other aspects of your coaching experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question based on your honest feedback.
2. For select_one questions (e.g., "What was your satisfaction with this session", "How would you rate the coach performance"), choose one of the available options.
3. For questions with a note type (e.g., "What is your mobility experience"), you can type as much or as little as you need to answer the question.
4. If a question is not required (indicated by a red "X"), you can skip it if you don't have an answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was your satisfaction with this session** (`client_satisfaction`, select_one): Rate your satisfaction with this session, choosing one of the following options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.
* **How would you rate the session quality** (`session_quality`, select_one): Evaluate the session quality, choosing one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **How long was the session** (`session_length`, number): Enter the length of the session in minutes or hours.
* **What is your mobility experience** (`mobility_exp`, note): Describe your previous experience with mobility exercises or activities.
* **How did you feel during the session** (`client_sentiment`, text): Share your thoughts and feelings about your experience during the session.
* **How would you rate the coach performance** (`coach_performance`, select_one): Rate the coach's performance, choosing one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
