# Team Development Coaching Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for team development coaching sessions. It is meant to collect relevant details from participants, including their name, contact information, and specific session details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing your name in the "name" field.
2.  Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3.  Enter your contact number in the "contact no" field.
4.  Choose the date and time of the coaching session you wish to attend from the "date and time" field.
5.  Finally, provide the name of the coach or facilitator who will be leading the session in the "coach name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
*   **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, `date`, required): Select the date and time of the coaching session you wish to attend.
*   **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the coach or facilitator who will be leading the session.
*   **Contact No** (`contact_no`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number so we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
Please make sure to enter accurate and correct information in all fields to ensure a smooth registration process. If you have any issues or questions, feel free to contact us.
