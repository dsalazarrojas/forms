# leadership_coaching_session_absence_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document the absence or missed sessions of a participant in a leadership coaching session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the coaching session that was missed by the participant from the dropdown options on the first page.
2. Choose the reason for absence for each of the missed session on the second page.
3. Provide the participant's follow-up plan in the optional field on the third page.
4. Fill in the participant's email and phone number on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Coaching Session** (`first_coaching_session`, `select_one`, required):
   Choose the coaching session that was missed by the participant from the dropdown options.
- **Reason for Absence 1** (`reason_for_absence_1`, `text`, required):
   Provide reason for absence for the first coaching session.
- **Reason for Absence 2** (`reason_for_absence_2`, `text`, required):
   Provide reason for absence for the second coaching session.
- **Reason for Absence 3** (`reason_for_absence_3`, `text`, optional):
   Provide reason for absence for the third coaching session.
- **Follow-up Plan** (`follow_up_plan`, `text`, optional):
   Provide the participant's follow-up plan.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required):
   Enter participant's email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required):
   Enter participant's phone number.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are submitting the correct coaching session that was missed.
- If the participant is missing all sessions, please fill in all the reason for absence and follow up plan fields.
