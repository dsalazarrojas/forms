# Financial Coaching Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Coaching Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback from clients about their coaching sessions. It is meant to help coaches understand how well they have performed and areas where they can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read all the questions carefully and fill in your answers as completely as possible.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the answer that best fits your experience.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you can.
4. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction (overall_satisfaction)**: Please rate how satisfied you are with your coaching session, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Session Effectiveness (session_effectiveness)**: Please describe what you felt was the most effective part of the session and what you would like to see improved.
* **Communication Skills (communication_skills)**: How would you rate the coach's communication style? (Select One)
	+ Option 1: Excellent
	+ Option 2: Good
	+ Option 3: Needs Improvement
* **Emotional Intelligence (emotional_intelligence)**: How would you rate the coach's emotional intelligence? (Select Multiple)
	+ Option 1: Excellent
	+ Option 2: Good
	+ Option 3: Needs Improvement
* **Goal Setting (goal_setting)**: Please describe what you felt was the most effective part of setting your goals and what you would like to see improved.
* **Financial Planning (financial_planning)**: How would you rate the coach's financial planning advice? (Select One)
	+ Option 1: Excellent
	+ Option 2: Good
	+ Option 3: Needs Improvement
* **Relationship with Coach (relationship_with_coach)**: How would you rate your relationship with the coach? (Select One)
	+ Option 1: Excellent
	+ Option 2: Good
	+ Option 3: Needs Improvement
* **Next Steps (next_steps)**: Please describe any next steps you would like to take or suggestions for improvement.
* **Other (other)**: Is there anything else you would like to share or suggest that wasn't covered in the previous questions?
