# Theater Coaching Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theater Coaching Registration Form is designed to collect information from users for coaching services related to the theater.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required and optional fields as prompted.
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (<code>1</code>): Enter any relevant information about yourself that you think would be useful for the coaching service.
* **Contact Info** (<code>2</code>): Provide your contact information, such as your name, email, and phone number, so that the coach can get in touch with you.
* **Session Details** (<code>3</code>): Describe the specific details of the session you are interested in, including dates, times, or any other relevant information.
* **Payment Method** (<code>4</code>): If you have a preferred payment method, such as credit card or bank transfer, indicate it here.
* **Notes** (<code>5</code>): If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them here.
* **Confirm** (<code>6</code>): Confirm your submission to ensure that your information is accurate and complete.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your input for accuracy and completeness.
* If you need any help or have questions, contact the form author at <form@gic.mx>.
* The form data will only be used for the purpose of providing coaching services, and the information will not be shared with anyone without your consent.
