# Career Transition Coaching Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information about you as you prepare for a career transition. This information will help us understand your situation, goals, and expectations, enabling us to tailor a coaching program that meets your needs and supports your success.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by answering all required fields**: Each field with a star (\*) symbol is mandatory. Please ensure you complete all these fields before proceeding.
2. **Provide accurate and honest information**: Share your genuine thoughts, feelings, and intentions to help us better understand your situation.
3. **Use clear and concise answers**: Avoid lengthy paragraphs or unnecessary details for each field. Keep your answers concise and focused on the question at hand.
4. **Save and review your progress**: Take breaks if needed, and review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1 - Contact Information
* **Your Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Include your area code for accurate contact.

### Page 2 - Current Situation
* **Current Position or Job Title** (`current_position`, `text`, required): Your current job title or position.
* **Current Industry or Field** (`current_industry`, `text`, required): The industry or field you're currently in.
* **Years of Professional Experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, required): Your years of work experience.
* **Current Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your current employment status.
* **Reason for Career Transition** (`transition_reason`, `text`, required): Explain the motivation behind your career change.
* **Target Career or Industry** (`target_career`, `text`, required): The career or industry you aim to transition into.
* **Target Position or Job Title** (`target_position`, `text`, optional): A specific job title or position you're targeting.

### Page 3 - Transition Details
* **Desired Transition Timeline** (`transition_timeline`, `select_one`, required): Choose your desired transition timeline.
* **Transferable Skills from Current Role** (`transferable_skills`, `text`, required): List skills or abilities you can bring to your new career.
* **Your Key Strengths** (`strengths`, `text`, required): Identify your key strengths and abilities.
* **Preferred Work Environment** (`work_style_preference`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your preferred work environment.
* **Salary Expectations in New Career** (`salary_expectations`, `text`, optional): Your target annual salary range.

### Page 4 - Coaching Details
* **Coaching Focus Areas** (`coaching_focus`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your top priorities for the coaching program.
* **Have You Had Career Coaching Before?** (`previous_coaching`, `select_one`, optional): Share your previous coaching experience.
* **Expectations from Coaching** (`expectations`, `text`, required): Outline your expectations from the coaching program.
* **Your Commitment Level to Transition** (`commitment_level`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your level of commitment to the transition process.
* **Support System** (`support_system`, `select_one`, optional): Describe your support system for the transition.
* **Preferred Coaching Frequency** (`coaching_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred coaching frequency.
* **Preferred Coaching Format** (`coaching_format`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred coaching format.
* **Agree to Coaching Program Terms** (`consent_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your agreement to the coaching program terms.

Note: This guide is designed to be completed in stages, helping you understand and fill out each section progressively. If you have any questions or need clarification on a particular field, please don't hesitate to reach out.
