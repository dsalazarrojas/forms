# Email Coaching Package Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from coaches to register their email coaching packages.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the details of the coaching package you are registering, including its name and description.
2. Provide the client's information, including their name and email address.
3. Click the "Submit" button to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Coach Details**: Enter your coaching credentials, including your name and email address. (Required: false)
* **Package Details**: Enter the details of the coaching package, including its name and description. (Required: false)
* **Client Info**: Enter the client's information, including their name and email address. (Required: false)
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete the registration process. (Required: false)
