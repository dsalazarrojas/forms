# Monthly Coaching Call Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Monthly Coaching Call Check In Form is designed to help coaches and clients collaborate and track progress during their monthly coaching calls. It provides a structured way to document goals, progress, and next steps.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter the Date
- To start, enter the date of the coaching call in the "Date" field. This will help you keep track of when the call took place.

### Step 2: Enter the Start Time
- Next, enter the start time of the coaching call in the "Start Time" field. This will help you record how long the call lasted.

### Step 3: Enter the Call Duration
- Enter the duration of the call in the "Call Duration" field. This will help you track how much time was spent on the call.

### Step 4: Add Notes
- Enter any important notes or comments from the coaching call in the "Notes" field. This will help you remember important details and action items from the call.

### Step 5: Enter Email
- Enter the client's email address in the "Email" field. This will help you contact the client directly if needed.

### Step 6: Add Call Notes
- Enter any additional notes or comments from the coaching call in the "Call Notes" field. This will help you remember important details and action items from the call.

### Step 7: Select the Next Meeting Date
- Finally, select the date of the next coaching call in the "Next Meeting" field. This will help you schedule and plan future calls.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **coaching_call_check_in** (Text, Optional): A free form text field to enter any general comments or notes about the coaching call.
	+ This field is a free form where you can enter any important information about the call, such as key takeaways, action items, or next steps.

	+ Enter the date of the coaching call. This will help you keep track of when the call took place.
* **Start Time** (Time, Optional): 
	+ Enter the start time of the coaching call. This will help you track how long the call lasted.
* **Call Duration** (Number, Optional): 
	+ Enter the duration of the call. This will help you track how much time was spent on the call.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): 
	+ Enter any important notes or comments from the coaching call. This will help you remember important details and action items from the call.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): 
	+ Enter the client's email address. This will help you contact the client directly if needed.
* **Call Notes** (Text, Optional): 
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments from the coaching call. This will help you remember important details and action items from the call.
* **Next Meeting Date** (Date, Optional): 
	+ Enter the date of the next coaching call. This will help you schedule and plan future calls.

## Tips

* Be sure to enter as much detail as possible when completing this form.
* Use the "Call Notes" field to add any additional comments or notes from the call.
* Make sure to select the correct "Next Meeting Date" to schedule the next call.
* Save the form as a record of the coaching call to reference later.
