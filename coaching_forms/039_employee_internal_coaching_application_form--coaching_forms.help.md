# Employee Internal Coaching Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Internal Coaching Application Form is used to provide coaching services to employees based on their specific needs and preferences. This form will help identify your coaching requirements and ensure a tailored coaching experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely, but only provide necessary information.
2. Ensure that all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \* in the field label) are filled out.
3. Select the relevant options for your preferences.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Name
* Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your official employee ID.

### 2. Department
* Department: Type in your department name as per your official employee ID.

### 3. Job Title
* Job Title: Enter your official job title as per your employee ID.

### 4. Reason for Coaching
* Reason for Coaching: Briefly describe the reasons why you are seeking coaching. Be as specific as possible.

### 5. Coaching Preferences
* Coaching Preferences: 
  * one-on-one: You want coaching one-on-one with a coach.
  * group: You are interested in group coaching sessions.
  * phone support: You prefer phone support for coaching sessions.
  * email support: You prefer email support for coaching sessions.

### 6. Coaching Topics
* Coaching Topics: Enter specific topics you want to focus on during coaching sessions.

### 7. Goals and Objectives
* Goals and Objectives: Describe your short-term and long-term goals.

### 8. Coaching Start Date
* Coaching Start Date: Select the date you would like your coaching to start. Please format as MM/DD/YYYY.

### 9. Coaching End Date
* Coaching End Date: Select the date you would like your coaching to end. Please format as MM/DD/YYYY.

### 10. Hours Worked Before Coaching Program
* Hours Worked Before Coaching Program: If you have worked for the company for less than 6 months, please indicate the number of hours you have worked so far.

### 11. Percentage of Coaching Hours
* Percentage of Coaching Hours: If you have worked for less than 6 months, this field is used to calculate the percentage of coaching hours you are eligible for.

### 12. Coaching Frequency
* Coaching Frequency: 
  * bi-weekly: You want coaching sessions every other week.
  * weekly: You want coaching sessions every week.
  * monthly: You want coaching sessions once a month.

### 13. Primary Coach
* Primary Coach: Choose your preferred coach.

### 14. Coaching Dedicated Time
* Coaching Dedicated Time: 
  * morning: You prefer coaching sessions during the morning.
  * afternoon: You prefer coaching sessions during the afternoon.
  * evening: You prefer coaching sessions during the evening.
  * lunch break: You prefer coaching sessions during your lunch break.

### 15. Phone Number
* Phone Number: Enter your contact phone number.

### 16. Email Address
* Email Address: Enter your company email address.

### 17. Location
* Location: Indicate your work location.

### 18. Comments
* Comments: Leave any additional comments for the coaching team.

### 19. Signature
* Signature: Please sign the form.

### 20. Signature Date
* Signature Date: Date of signing.

### 21. Reviewed By
* Reviewed By: Select 'yes' if a supervisor or coach has reviewed this application.

### 22. Reviewed By Employee
* Reviewed By Employee: Select 'yes' if this application has been reviewed by the employee.

### 23. Reviewed By Coach
* Reviewed By Coach: Select 'yes' if this application has been reviewed by the coach.

### 24. Reviewed By Admin
* Reviewed By Admin: Select 'yes' if this application has been reviewed by an administrator.
