# sports_coaching_leave_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports coaching leave form is used by employees to request a sports coaching leave, which enables them to receive coaching in sports-related activities while still being on leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Enter your employee ID**: This is your unique employee identifier.
2. **Enter your employee name**: Please provide your full name as it appears on your employee record.
3. **Select your role**: Indicate whether you are a coach, player, or team manager.
4. **Enter your leave start date**: The date you plan to start your leave.
5. **Enter your leave end date**: The date you plan to end your leave.
6. **Select leave type**: Indicate whether this is a single or recurring leave.
7. **Enter reason for leave**: Please provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
8. **Add any comments**: If you have any additional information or context, please provide it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **sports_coaching_leave_form**: The title of this form is used to indicate the type of leave request being submitted.
* **employee_id**: This field is used to input your unique employee identifier.
* **employee_name**: Please provide your full name as it appears on your employee record.
* **role**: Indicate your role in the sports coaching program.
* **leave_start_date**: This field is used to input the date you plan to start your leave.
* **leave_end_date**: This field is used to input the date you plan to end your leave.
* **leave_type**: Indicate whether this is a single or recurring leave.
* **reason_for_leave**: Please provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
* **comments**: If you have any additional information or context, please provide it here.
