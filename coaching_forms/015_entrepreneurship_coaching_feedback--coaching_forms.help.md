# Entrepreneurship Coaching Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from entrepreneurs who have participated in coaching sessions. Your feedback will help us understand how we can better support you in your entrepreneurial journey.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply select the corresponding fields below and provide your feedback. The form is divided into the following sections:

1. **Coaching Session** (`coaching_session`): This field is for providing information about your coaching session, such as the date and time it took place.
2. **Effectiveness** (`effectiveness`): This field is for rating the effectiveness of your coaching session, with a numerical value between 1 and 10.
3. **Relevance** (`relevance`): This field is for rating the relevance of the coaching session to your business needs, with a numerical value between 1 and 10.
4. **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`): This field is for writing down areas where you feel the coaching session could be improved or areas that you would like to see more focus on.
5. **Feedback** (`feedback`): This field is for providing any additional comments or feedback you have about your coaching session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coaching Session (`coaching_session`):** A numerical field for providing information about your coaching session. Please enter a number to help us understand the context of your feedback.
* **Effectiveness (`effectiveness`):** A numerical field for rating the effectiveness of your coaching session. Please enter a number between 1 and 10 to indicate how effective you felt the session was.
* **Relevance (`relevance`):** A numerical field for rating the relevance of your coaching session to your business needs. Please enter a number between 1 and 10 to indicate how relevant you felt the session was.
* **Areas for Improvement (`areas_for_improvement`):** A text field for providing suggestions or comments on areas where the coaching session could be improved or where more focus is needed.
* **Feedback (`feedback`):** A text field for providing any additional comments or feedback you have about your coaching session.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your feedback.
* You can enter any comments, suggestions, or questions you have about your coaching session.
* Your feedback is anonymous and will be used to improve our coaching services.
