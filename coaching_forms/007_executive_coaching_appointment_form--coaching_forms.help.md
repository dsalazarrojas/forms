# executive_coaching_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for executive coaching appointments. It helps coaches to gather details about the client's preferences and schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the day of the appointment from the list of available options.
2. Choose the time of the appointment from the list of available options.
3. Select the duration of the appointment from the list of available options.
4. Enter the client's name.
5. Enter the client's email address.
6. Enter the client's phone number.
7. Add any additional notes about the client or session.
8. Enter the coach's name.
9. Enter any notes from the coach about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Day**: Choose the day of the appointment.
	+ Select one or more days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday.
* **Select Time**: Choose the time of the appointment.
	+ Select one or more times: 08:00, 09:00, 10:00, 11:00, 12:00, 13:00, 14:00, 15:00, 16:00, 17:00, 18:00.
* **Select Duration**: Choose the duration of the appointment.
	+ Select one or more durations: 15, 30, 60 minutes.
* **Client Name**: Enter the client's name.
* **Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the client or session.
* **Coach Name**: Enter the coach's name.
* **Coach Notes**: Enter any notes from the coach about the session.

## Tips
* Make sure to select a day and time that works for both you and the client.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date contact information for the client.
* Keep notes about the session to ensure you have a record of the discussion.
* Consider sharing the form with your client to confirm their appointment details.
