# Team Coaching Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Coaching Network Registration Form is designed to collect information from team coaches and team administrators to connect them with other teams and resources within the coaching network. This form helps to register teams and their coaches, allowing for easier communication and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the designated text field. This is the name of the person completing the form.
2. Provide your **Email** address in the designated email field. This is the contact information for communication and notification purposes.
3. Enter your **Team Name** in the designated text field. This is the name of the team you are registering.
4. Specify your **Team Location** in the designated text field. This is the physical location of your team.
5. Enter your **Phone** number in the designated text field. This is your contact number for additional communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: This field is for your personal name. Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (email)**: This field is for your email address. Make sure to enter a valid email address for communication and notification purposes.
* **Team Name (text)**: This is the name of the team you are registering. Choose a unique and descriptive name that represents your team.
* **Team Location (text)**: This field is for your team's physical location. Enter the city, state, and country where your team is based.
* **Phone (text)**: This field is for your phone number. Enter your phone number for additional communication and coordination with other teams and network members.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy and validity.
* If you are registering on behalf of a team, ensure that you have the necessary authority to represent the team.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with the registration process, contact us at [insert contact information].
