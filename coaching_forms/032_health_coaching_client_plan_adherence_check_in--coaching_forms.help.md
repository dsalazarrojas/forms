# Health Coaching Client Plan Adherence Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help health coaches track the adherence of their clients to their plans between sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter the session date in the "Session Date" field.
3. Rate the client's current adherence to their plan using the "Adherence Rating" field (e.g., 1-10).
4. Describe the next steps the client will take between sessions in the "Follow-up Plan" field.
5. If applicable, add any additional comments or concerns from the coach or client in the "Notes" field.
6. If necessary, enter any phone numbers or emails for follow-up communication in the "Phone Number" and "Email" fields.
7. Enter any comments or concerns from the coach in the "Coach Notes" field.
8. Enter any comments or concerns from the client in the "Client Notes" field.
9. If scheduled, enter the next session date in the "Next Session Date" field and the next session time in the "Next Session Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (1, text, required):
	+ Enter the client's full name.
* **Session Date** (2, date, required):
	+ Enter the date of the current session.
* **Coach Name** (3, text, required):
	+ Enter the name of the coach.
* **Adherence Rating** (4, number, required):
	+ Rate the client's current adherence to their plan using a numerical rating (e.g., 1-10).
* **Follow-up Plan** (5, text, required):
	+ Describe the next steps the client will take between sessions.
* **Notes** (6, note, optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments or concerns from the coach or client.
* **Phone Number** (7, text, optional):
	+ Enter any phone numbers for follow-up communication.
* **Email** (8, email, optional):
	+ Enter any email addresses for follow-up communication.
* **Coach Notes** (9, text, optional):
	+ Enter any comments or concerns from the coach.
* **Client Notes** (10, text, optional):
	+ Enter any comments or concerns from the client.
* **Next Session Date** (11, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the next scheduled session.
* **Next Session Time** (12, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time of the next scheduled session.

## Tips

* Be sure to rate the client's adherence honestly and objectively.
* Clearly document any next steps and follow-up actions for the client.
* Use this form to track progress and identify areas for improvement.
* Keep the "Notes" field for comments or concerns brief and focused on key points.
* Use the "Coach Notes" and "Client Notes" fields to capture any additional information from the coach or client.
