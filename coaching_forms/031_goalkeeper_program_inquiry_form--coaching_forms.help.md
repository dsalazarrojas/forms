# Goalkeeper Program Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from potential candidates for the Goalkeeper Program, a coaching program aimed at providing guidance and support to individuals seeking to improve their skills or achieve specific goals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about yourself.
2. If applicable, provide the necessary details about your experience, availability, and preferences.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please fill in your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, fill in your last name for identification purposes.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Availability Date
* **Availability Date** (`availability_date`, `date`, optional): If you have a specific availability date in mind, please enter it here.

### Experience
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, optional): Describe your relevant experience, skills, or qualifications that align with the program.

### Training Type
* **Training Type** (`training_type`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Choose one or more of the following options:
		- Training for Academy
		- Personal Training

### Availability Time
* **Availability Time** (`availability_time`, `time`, optional): If you have a specific availability time in mind, please enter it here.

### Academy Name
* **Academy Name** (`academy_name`, `text`, optional): If you are associated with an academy, please enter their name.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional contact information that may be helpful to the program administrators.

### Coach Name
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific coach in mind, please enter their name.

### Coach Email
* **Coach Email** (`coach_email`, `email`, optional): If you have a specific coach's email address, please enter it here.

### Academy Phone
* **Academy Phone** (`academy_phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific academy phone number, please enter it here.

### Coach Phone
* **Coach Phone** (`coach_phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific coach's phone number, please enter it here.

### Academy Address
* **Academy Address** (`academy_address`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific academy address, please enter it here.

### Coach Address
* **Coach Address** (`coach_address`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific coach's address, please enter it here.

### Academy Website
* **Academy Website** (`academy_website`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific academy website, please enter it here.

### Coach Website
* **Coach Website** (`coach_website`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific coach's website, please enter it here.

### Academy Notes
* **Academy Notes** (`academy_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional notes about your academy.

### Coach Notes
* **Coach Notes** (`coach_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional notes about your coach.

### Academy Email
* **Academy Email** (`academy_email`, `email`, optional): If you have a specific academy email address, please enter it here.

### Coach Email Confirmation
* **Coach Email Confirmation** (`coach_email_confirmation`, `email`, optional): If you have a specific coach's email confirmation, please enter it here.

### Academy Confirmation
* **Academy Confirmation** (`academy_confirmation`, `email`, optional): If you have a specific academy confirmation, please enter it here.

### Coaching Hours
* **Coaching Hours** (`coaching_hours`, `text`, optional): If you have specific coaching hours, please enter them here.

### Training Hours
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, `text`, optional): If you have specific training hours, please enter them here.

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose one of the following options:
		- Bank Transfer
		- Cheque
		- Online Payment

### Payment Amount
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, optional): If you have a specific payment amount, please enter it here.
