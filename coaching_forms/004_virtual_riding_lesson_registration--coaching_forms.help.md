# Virtual Riding Lesson Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register users for virtual riding lessons. It collects user details, schedule preferences, and other relevant information to facilitate the coordination of lessons.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, select a preferred date for your virtual riding lesson by choosing one of the available dates.
2. Next, select a preferred time for your virtual riding lesson by choosing one of the available times.
3. Provide your email address, which will be used to communicate with you about your lesson details.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments about your lesson, if necessary.
5. Select your riding school ID to ensure you're associated with the correct school.
6. Choose the coach ID for your virtual riding lesson.
7. Review and complete the other optional fields if you have any relevant details to add.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details (page 1)** (`User Details`, text, required/optional): Please fill in your personal details, such as your name, age, and experience level, so we can better understand your requirements for the virtual riding lesson.
* **Schedule Details (page 1)** (`Schedule Details`, text, required/optional): Describe your schedule availability and constraints for the virtual riding lesson, including your preferred dates and times.
* **Preferred Date (page 2)** (`Preferred Date`, select_one, required/optional): Choose a date for your virtual riding lesson from the provided options. 
* **Preferred Time (page 2)** (`Preferred Time`, select_one, required/optional): Select a time for your virtual riding lesson from the provided options. 
* **Rider Details (page 2)** (`Rider Details`, text, required/optional): Add any relevant information about your riding experience and goals.
* **Riding School ID (page 2)** (`Riding School ID`, select_one, required/optional): Select your riding school ID to associate with the correct school.
* **Coach ID (page 2)** (`Coach ID`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your coach ID to ensure you're matched with the right coach.
* **Notes (page 2)** (`Notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or requests for your virtual riding lesson.
* **Email (page 2)** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to ensure we can communicate with you about your lesson details.
