# Business Transformation Roadmap Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Transformation Roadmap Session Registration form is designed to help business leaders create a personalized road map for their organization's growth and development. It will guide you in identifying your transformation goals, challenges, and timelines to ensure you're on the right path for a successful business transformation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. If you need help or clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
3. Review each field carefully before submitting your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (Participant Name, text, required): Your full name as the business leader submitting the registration.
* **Email Address** (Participant Email, email, required): Your contact email for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone Number** (Participant Phone, text, required): Your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Company Name or Organization** (Company Name, text, required): The name of your business or organization.
* **Job Title or Position** (Job Title, text, required): Your job title or position in the organization.
* **Company Size** (Company Size, select_one, required): Choose the number of employees in your organization.
* **Industry or Sector** (Industry or Sector, text, required): Select your industry or sector.
* **Transformation Focus Areas** (Transformation Focus, select_multiple, required): Identify all the areas you'd like to focus on for transformation.
* **Main Business Challenge** (Main Challenge, text, required): Describe your main business challenge.
* **Desired Outcomes** (Desired Outcomes, text, required): Specify your goals for transformation.
* **Transformation Stage** (Transformation Stage, select_one, required): Choose where you are in the transformation process.
* **Transformation Timeline** (Transformation Timeline, select_one, required): Select your desired timeline for achieving results.
* **Budget Range for Initiative** (Budget Range, select_one, optional): Provide an estimate of your budget for the initiative.
* **Preferred Session Date** (Preferred Session Date, date, required): Choose a date for your session.
* **Preferred Session Time** (Preferred Session Time, select_one, required): Select your preferred time slot for the session.
* **Preferred Session Format** (Preferred Session Format, select_one, required): Choose your preferred session format.
* **Group Participants** (Group Participants, select_one, required): Decide if you'd like a solo or group session.
* **Decision Makers Present** (Decision Makers Present, select_one, required): Indicate if decision-makers will be present in your session.
* **Previous Coaching or Consulting Experience** (Coaching Experience, select_one, required): Tell us about your previous experience with coaches or consultants.
* **Additional Information or Questions** (Additional Information, text, optional): Share any additional information or questions you have.
