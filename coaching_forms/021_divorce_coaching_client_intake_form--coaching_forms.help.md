# divorce_coaching_client_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about you, the client, to better understand your divorce coaching needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing your basic contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Next, select your relationship status and the type of divorce you are undergoing.
3. Provide the date of your divorce and the date of separation, if applicable.
4. Describe your reasons for separation and any grounds for separation.
5. If you have children, please provide information about them.
6. Answer questions about your financial status, including assets, debts, income, and expenses.
7. Finally, select your payment method and any additional terms for payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (2): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (3): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone** (4): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (5): Enter your home address for our records.
* **Relationship Status** (6): Select your current relationship status.
	+ Single: You are single and not in a relationship.
	+ Married: You are married and seeking divorce coaching services.
	+ Divorced: You are divorced and looking to move on with your life.
	+ Separated: You are separated and not yet divorced.
	+ Widowed: You are a widow or widower.
* **Divorce Type** (7): Select the type of divorce you are undergoing.
	+ Collaborative: A collaborative divorce, where both parties work together to reach an agreement.
	+ Mediated: A mediated divorce, where a neutral third party facilitates the process.
	+ Litigation: A litigated divorce, where the parties go to court to resolve issues.
	+ Hybrid: A combination of collaborative and litigated divorce.
	+ Other: Other types of divorce, please describe below.
* **Date of Divorce** (8): If you are already divorced, enter the date of your divorce. If not, enter the date you are expecting to divorce.
* **Date of Separation** (9): If you were separated before divorce, enter the date of separation.
* **Grounds for Separation** (10): Briefly describe the grounds for your separation.
* **Reason for Separation** (11): Describe the reason for your separation, if not listed as a ground for separation.
* **Children** (12): If you have children, please provide information about them, including their names and ages.
* **Assets** (13): List any assets you own or have access to, including real estate, vehicles, and personal property.
* **Debts** (14): List any debts you have, including credit cards, loans, and other financial obligations.
* **Assets and Debts** (15): Summarize your assets and debts.
* **Assets Value** (16): Provide the total value of your assets.
* **Debts Value** (17): Provide the total value of your debts.
* **Income** (18): List your monthly income.
* **Expenses** (19): List your monthly expenses.
* **Assets and Expenses** (20): Summarize your assets and expenses.
* **Financial Support** (21): Describe any financial support you receive or provide.
* **Financial Status** (22): Select your current financial status.
	+ Stable: You have a stable financial situation.
	+ Unstable: You have an unstable financial situation.
	+ Bankrupt: You are bankrupt.
* **Financial Support Type** (23): Select the type of financial support you receive or provide.
	+ Alimony: Spousal support, including alimony or spousal support payments.
	+ Spousal Support: Support for your ex-spouse or ex-partner.
	+ Child Support: Support for your children.
	+ Custody Support: Support for your ex-partner's children.
	+ Other: Describe below.
* **Payment Method** (24): Select how you plan to pay for the divorce coaching services.
	+ Bank: You will pay via bank transfer or online banking.
	+ Credit Card: You will pay via credit card.
	+ PayPal: You will pay via PayPal.
	+ Other: Describe below.
* **Payment Terms** (25): Describe any additional payment terms or arrangements you have.

Please note that some fields are optional, but providing as much information as possible will help us tailor the coaching process to your needs. If you have any questions or concerns about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us.
