# Nutrition Coaching Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to gather information from potential clients interested in using our nutrition coaching services. It is a simple, one-time form to collect contact details and goal description.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Describe your nutrition goals in the "Goal Description" field.
5. Read the "Terms and Conditions" field and select "Yes" if you agree to the terms.
6. Sign the form by entering your name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Your first name, as you wish to be known.
### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Your last name, as you wish to be known.
### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): Your email address where we can contact you.
### Goal Description
* **Goal Description** (`goal_description`, `note`, required: false): Describe your nutrition goals and objectives.
### Terms and Conditions
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `select_one`, required: false): Please read the terms and conditions provided above and select "Yes" if you agree to them.
### E-Signature
* **E-Signature** (`e_signature`, `text`, required: false): This is where you sign the form by entering your name.
### Date
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): The date you sign the form. 

## Tips
* Please fill in your actual name and email address where applicable.
* Be honest and clear when describing your nutrition goals.
* Read the terms and conditions carefully before agreeing to them.
