# Coaching Questionnaire AmandaV - Help Guide

## Purpose

This coaching questionnaire, designed to assess individuals' coaching needs and preferences, is a critical tool for the coaching process. By providing detailed insights into your personal and professional situation, you will be able to gain a deeper understanding of your goals, challenges, and motivations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly**: Each field is designed to help us understand your situation better. Your responses will be kept confidential and will not be shared with anyone without your consent.
2. **Complete one page at a time**: The form is divided into multiple pages for ease of use. Please complete one page before moving on to the next.
3. **Use examples when necessary**: If you're asked to describe your situation, please use specific examples to help us better understand your thoughts and feelings.
4. **Be honest about your challenges**: This questionnaire is designed to help you identify areas for improvement. Be honest about your strengths and weaknesses, and don't be afraid to share your challenges.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your legal full name, as it appears on official documents. (Page 1, Field 1)
* **Email Address**: Your primary contact email for communication. (Page 1, Field 2)
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number for any follow-up questions or scheduling. (Page 1, Field 3)
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth for identification purposes. (Page 1, Field 4)
* **Current Occupation**: A brief description of your current job or profession. (Page 2, Field 5)
* **Employer**: The name of your current organization. (Page 2, Field 6)
* **Type of Coaching Interest**: Choose the type of coaching that interests you most. (Page 3, Field 7)
* **Main Reason for Seeking Coaching**: Describe the primary reason you're seeking coaching. (Page 3, Field 8)
* **Current Goals**: A brief description of your current goals and objectives. (Page 4, Field 9)
* **Biggest Obstacle**: Describe the biggest obstacle currently holding you back. (Page 4, Field 10)
* **Previous Coaching Experience**: Indicate if you have had coaching experience before. (Page 5, Field 11)
* **Previous Experience Details**: Provide more details about your previous coaching experience. (Page 5, Field 12)
* **What Worked Well Before**: Describe what worked well in your previous coaching experience. (Page 6, Field 13)
* **What Did Not Work Before**: Describe what did not work well in your previous coaching experience. (Page 6, Field 14)
* **How Do You Learn Best**: Select your preferred learning style. (Page 7, Field 15)
* **Preferred Communication Style**: Choose how you prefer to communicate. (Page 7, Field 16)
* **Accountability Preference**: Indicate your desired level of accountability. (Page 8, Field 17)
* **What Motivates You**: Select what motivates you. (Page 8, Field 18)
* **Current Stress Level**: Rate your current stress level. (Page 9, Field 19)
* **Main Sources of Stress**: Describe the main sources of stress in your life. (Page 10, Field 20)
* **Support System**: Indicate the strength of your support system. (Page 10, Field 21)
* **Overall Life Satisfaction**: Rate your overall life satisfaction. (Page 11, Field 22)
* **Area You Are Most Satisfied With**: Describe the area where you are most satisfied. (Page 12, Field 23)
* **Area You Are Least Satisfied With**: Describe the area where you are least satisfied. (Page 13, Field 24)
* **Core Values**: Describe your guiding values. (Page 14, Field 25)
* **Key Strengths**: Describe your key strengths. (Page 14, Field 26)
* **Areas for Development**: Describe areas you'd like to improve. (Page 15, Field 27)
* **Expectations from Coaching**: Describe your expectations from coaching. (Page 16, Field 28)
* **Commitment Level**: Indicate your commitment level to the coaching process. (Page 17, Field 29)
* **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional information you'd like to share. (Page 18, Field 30)

Please complete the form and we will use the information to create a tailored coaching plan for you.
