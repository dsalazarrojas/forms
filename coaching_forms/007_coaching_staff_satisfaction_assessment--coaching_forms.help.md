# Coaching Staff Satisfaction Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and assessments from coaching staff members on their experience with the coaching program. The data collected will be used to understand the effectiveness and impact of the program and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with your name and position.
2. Choose the options that best reflect your experience and satisfaction with the coaching program.
3. If you want to provide additional comments or suggestions, you can type them in the "Suggestions for improvement" and "Additional comments" fields.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Respondent Information
* **Your Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Your Position**: Please enter your job title or role within the organization.
* **Department or Team**: Optional field to identify the department or team you belong to.
### Coaching Quality and Leadership
* **Quality of Feedback from Coaching Staff**: Rate the quality of feedback you have received from coaching staff using the options from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Coaching Staff Knowledge and Expertise**: Rate the coaching staff's knowledge and expertise using the same scale.
* **Clarity of Coaching Instructions and Goals**: Rate the clarity of coaching instructions and goals using the same scale.
* **Coaching Goals Align with Organizational Goals**: Rate how well coaching goals align with the organization's overall goals using the same scale.
### Support and Communication
* **Coaching Staff Support and Encouragement**: Rate the level of support and encouragement you have received from coaching staff using the same scale.
* **Ease of Accessing Coaching Staff for Questions**: Rate how easy or difficult it is for you to access coaching staff for questions using the same scale.
* **Effectiveness of Communication**: Rate the effectiveness of communication with coaching staff using the same scale.
### Impact and Outcomes
* **Coaching Has Improved My Performance**: Rate how much the coaching program has improved your performance using the same scale.
* **Coaching Has Developed My Skills**: Rate how much the coaching program has developed your skills using the same scale.
* **Coaching Has Improved My Confidence**: Rate how much the coaching program has improved your confidence using the same scale.
### Overall Satisfaction
* **Overall Satisfaction with Coaching Program**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the coaching program using the same scale.
* **Would You Want to Continue Coaching**: Choose whether you want to continue with the coaching program.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Provide any suggestions for improvement to the coaching program.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the coaching program.

Remember to provide honest and constructive feedback to help us understand your experience and improve the coaching program.
