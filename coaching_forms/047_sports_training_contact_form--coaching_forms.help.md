# Sports Training Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and preferences from individuals seeking sports training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the **first_name** field.
2. Provide your **Email Address** in the **email** field. This field should be in a standard email format (e.g., [name@email.com](mailto:name@email.com)).
3. Choose your **Availability** by selecting the days you are available for training (Monday to Sunday).
4. Select your **Preferred Time** for training (though this field is currently not functional).
5. Briefly describe your **Training Experience** in the **training_experience** field.
6. State your **Goals** for training in the **goal** field.
7. Choose the **Sport** you are interested in (Soccer, Basketball, Tennis, Swimming, or Volleyball).
8. Select the methods you would like to use for contact (Phone, Email, WhatsApp, or Skype).
9. Enter any additional **Comments** about your preferences or requirements.
10. Review the form for any errors, which will be displayed in the error fields at the end of the form.
