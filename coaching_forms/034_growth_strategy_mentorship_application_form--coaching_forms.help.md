# Growth Strategy Mentorship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals applying for a mentorship program within a professional or academic setting.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the "Application Form" page, which includes fields for "Contact Info", "Contact Number", and "Contact Email".
2. Select your preferred "Time Zone" to determine the best time for scheduling meetings or sessions.
3. If you have any "Additional Info" about yourself, please include it in the provided field.
4. On the subsequent page, provide your "Bio and Photo", as well as your "Resume", "Portfolio", and "References".
5. Select your preferred "Mentorship Programs" from the provided options.
6. If you have any "Other Programs" you'd like to be considered for, please include them in the provided field.
7. Confirm that you agree to the "Terms and Conditions" by selecting the "Agree" option.
8. Finally, review and confirm that you have read and agreed to the "Terms and Conditions" by selecting the "Confirm Agree" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Application Form
* **Contact Info**: Please provide your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number to enable smooth communication.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for further communication and updates.
* **Time Zone**: Select your time zone to determine the best time for scheduling meetings or sessions.
* **Additional Info**: If you have any additional information about yourself, please include it here.

### Bio and Photo
* **Bio and Photo**: Please upload a photo and write a brief description about yourself.

### Mentorship Goals
* **Mentorship Goals**: Describe your goals and objectives for the mentorship program.

### Mentorship Programs
* **Mentorship Programs**: Select the programs you are interested in applying for.

### Other Programs
* **Other Programs**: If you have any other programs you'd like to be considered for, please include them here.

### Resume, Portfolio, and References
* **Resume**: Upload your resume to showcase your skills and experience.
* **Portfolio**: Upload your portfolio to showcase your work.
* **References**: Provide references from previous employers or mentors.

### Confirmation
* **Confirm Agree**: Review and confirm that you have read and agreed to the "Terms and Conditions".
* **Confirm Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the "Terms and Conditions".

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check your "Terms and Conditions" before agreeing to them.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the administration.
