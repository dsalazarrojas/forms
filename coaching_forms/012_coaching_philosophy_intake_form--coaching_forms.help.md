# Coaching Philosophy Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Coaching Philosophy Intake Form is designed to capture a coach's unique coaching style, approach, and expectations. This form helps us understand how you approach coaching, which enables us to provide more effective and personalized support and services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your abilities, providing as much information as possible.
2. Ensure that you fill out all required fields marked as "required".
3. If you need help or clarification on any question, do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Coach Information (Section: 1)
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`coach_email`, `email`, required): Provide the email address where you can be reached for any communication.
* **Team or Organization** (`team_or_organization`, `text`, required): Indicate the team or organization you are coaching for.
* **Coaching Position** (`coaching_position`, `text`, required): Describe your coaching role within the team or organization.
* **Years of Coaching Experience** (`years_coaching`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have been coaching.

### Coaching Philosophy (Section: 5)
* **Describe Your Coaching Philosophy** (`coaching_philosophy`, `text`, required): Outline your core beliefs about coaching, such as your approach to motivating athletes, setting goals, and promoting positive behavior.
* **Primary Coaching Style** (`primary_coaching_style`, `select_one`, required): Select the style of coaching that best represents your approach:
	+ Authoritarian
	+ Democratic
	+ Laissez-faire
	+ Motivational
	+ Technical
	+ Adaptive

### Athlete Development (Section: 10)
* **Athlete Development Focus** (`athlete_focus`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the areas you focus on for athlete development:
	+ Skill development
	+ Physical conditioning
	+ Mental toughness
	+ Teamwork
	+ Character building
	+ Leadership development
	+ Nutrition and wellness
* **How Important is Winning to Your Coaching?** (`winning_importance`, `select_one`, required): Rate the importance of winning to your coaching:
	+ Very important
	+ Important
	+ Somewhat important
	+ Not very important

### Athlete Relationships (Section: 13)
* **How Do You Prefer to Interact with Athletes?** (`athlete_interaction`, `text`, required): Describe your approach to interacting with athletes, including your communication style.
* **Discipline and Correction Approach** (`discipline_approach`, `text`, required): Outline your approach to handling mistakes or disciplinary issues.
* **Parent Communication Frequency** (`parent_communication`, `select_one`, required): Select how frequently you communicate with parents:
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ As needed
	+ Minimal

### Expectations and Standards (Section: 16)
* **What Do You Expect from Your Athletes?** (`athlete_expectations`, `text`, required): Describe the performance, behavior, and commitment levels you expect from your athletes.

### Continuous Growth (Section: 19)
* **Professional Development Commitments** (`professional_development`, `text`, optional): List any certifications, courses, or conferences you pursue to develop your coaching skills.

### Form Date (Section: 21)
* **Form Date** (`form_date`, `date`, required): Record the date you submit this form.

Note: Please ensure that you have answered all required fields and provided as much detail as possible to ensure that we can tailor our support and services to your unique coaching style and approach.
