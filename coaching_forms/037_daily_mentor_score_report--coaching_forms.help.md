# Daily Mentor Score Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather daily feedback from mentors regarding their mentees' progress, session quality, and other relevant aspects of their interactions. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Complete the form daily for each mentee.
3. Ensure that you only report on your interactions with the mentee for whom you are reporting.
4. Review the form to ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mentee Progress** (`mentee_progress`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the mentee's progress, noting any successes, challenges, or concerns.
* **Session Quality** (`session_quality`, text, optional): Rate the quality of your session with the mentee, describing what went well and what didn't.
* **Mentee Satisfaction** (`mentee_satisfaction`, text, optional): Describe the mentee's level of satisfaction with the session, including any areas of improvement.
* **Mentor Support** (`mentor_support`, text, optional): Outline the support you provided to the mentee during the session, including any guidance or resources offered.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, optional): Record the length of the session, in minutes.
* **Progress Notes** (`progress_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes on the mentee's progress, including any action items or follow-up tasks.

## Tips

* Be specific and concise in your answers to ensure accurate and informative reporting.
* Avoid using jargon or technical terms that may be unclear to non-experts.
* Proofread your responses to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please reach out to your supervisor or support team for assistance.
