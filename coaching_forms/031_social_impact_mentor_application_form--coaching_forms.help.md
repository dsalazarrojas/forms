# Social Impact Mentor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Impact Mentor Application Form is designed for individuals who want to apply to become a mentor in a social impact program. The form is used to collect information about the applicant's background, experience, and skills, as well as their availability and commitment to mentoring. The goal of this form is to ensure that the applicant understands the program's expectations and requirements before applying.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the applicant information section, which includes their name, email, contact number, address, and years of experience.
2. In the available hours section, please specify how many hours you are available to mentor per week.
3. In the available days section, please specify how many days you are available to mentor per week.
4. In the application reason section, please provide a brief explanation of why you are applying to be a mentor.
5. Select the skills you are offering as a mentor.
6. Answer the experience with mentees section with a brief description of your experience with mentees.
7. Answer the experience with mentorship section with a brief description of your experience with mentorship.
8. In the mentorship style section, please describe your preferred mentorship style.
9. In the availability section, please specify when you are available to mentor.
10. In the bio section, please provide a brief description of yourself.
11. In the education background section, please provide a brief description of your education background.
12. In the certifications section, please list any relevant certifications you have.
13. In the experience with mentoring programs section, please describe your experience with mentoring programs.
14. In the references section, please provide references (optional).
15. In the commitment to mentoring section, please describe your commitment to mentoring.
16. Select your application status as either active or inactive.
17. Finally, please read and agree to the terms and conditions by checking the agreement box.

## Tips

* Please make sure to answer all required fields truthfully and accurately.
* Please proofread your application carefully before submitting.
* Make sure to check your application status carefully and select "Active" if you are available to mentor and "Inactive" if you are not.
