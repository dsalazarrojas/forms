# enablement_consulting_cohort_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from individuals interested in joining the Enablement Consulting Cohort program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
3. If you're not sure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Apply to Cohort**: Select 'Yes' to apply to the cohort, and 'No' to exit the application process.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents (*Required*).
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your identity (*Required*).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes (*Required*).
* **Company**: Enter your current or desired company name (*Required*).
* **Role**: Describe your role or position within the company (*Required*).
* **Location**: Enter your current location (*Required*).
* **Description**: Briefly describe your background and experience related to the cohort (*Required*).
* **Interested In**: Describe what you're interested in, or what you'd like to learn from the program (*Required*).
* **Expected Start**: Enter the date you're expecting to start the program, if known (optional).
* **Actual Start**: Enter the date you'll start the program, if known (optional).
* **Application Notes**: Provide any additional information about your application (optional).
* **Additional Comments**: Add any comments or questions you have about the program (optional).
* **Cohort ID**: Select 'Yes' to identify as a cohort member, 'No' otherwise (*Required*).
* **Cohort Type**: Select 'Yes' to confirm the type of cohort you're applying for (*Required*).
* **Cohort Name**: Enter the name of the cohort you're applying for (*Required*).
* **Cohort Start Date**: Enter the start date of the cohort, if known (optional).
* **Cohort End Date**: Enter the end date of the cohort, if known (optional).
* **Cohort Location**: Select 'Yes' to confirm the location of the cohort (*Required*).
* **Application Status**: Select the status of your application, 'Active' or 'Inactive' (*Required*).
* **Application Type**: Select the type of application, 'Yes' or 'No' (*Required*).
* **Application Category**: Select the category of application, 'Yes' or 'No' (*Required*).
* **Application Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for your application (*Required*).
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name or identifier (*Required*).
* **Submit Date**: Enter the date you submitted your application (*Required*).
