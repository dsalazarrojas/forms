# Coaching Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coaching Registration Form is used to register individuals for a coaching program. It gathers personal and program-related information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information (First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number).
2. Choose your preferred coaching program (Coaching Level, Program Name, Program Start Date, Program Duration).
3. Share your experience and background (Prior Coaching Experience, Sports or Fitness Background).
4. Disclose any health conditions or injuries (Current Fitness Level, Any Health Conditions or Injuries).
5. Set your coaching goals (What are your coaching goals?, Primary Focus Area).
6. Agree to the terms and conditions of the coaching program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email Address**: Your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth**: The date of your birth, if you wish to be disclosed.
* **Coaching Level**: Choose the level of coaching you are interested in:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Professional
* **Program Name**: Name of the coaching program you are interested in.
* **Program Start Date**: The date you plan to start your coaching program.
* **Program Duration**: Choose the length of your coaching program:
	+ 4 weeks
	+ 8 weeks
	+ 12 weeks
	+ 16 weeks
	+ 6 months
	+ 1 year
* **Prior Coaching Experience**: Choose your previous coaching experience:
	+ No experience
	+ Some experience
	+ Moderate experience
	+ Extensive experience
* **Sports or Fitness Background**: Briefly describe any sports or fitness background you have.
* **Current Fitness Level**: Choose your current fitness level:
	+ Sedentary
	+ Light activity
	+ Moderate activity
	+ Very active
* **Any Health Conditions or Injuries**: Disclose any health conditions or injuries you have.
* **Physician Clearance**: Do you have physician clearance for exercise?
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Not sure
* **What are your coaching goals?**: Describe your coaching goals.
* **Primary Focus Area**: Choose your primary focus area:
	+ Fitness improvement
	+ Weight loss
	+ Performance enhancement
	+ Skill development
	+ General wellness
	+ Other
* **I Agree to the coaching program terms and conditions**: Choose whether you agree to the terms and conditions.
	+ I Agree
	+ I Do Not Agree

Note: Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately to ensure a smooth registration process. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
