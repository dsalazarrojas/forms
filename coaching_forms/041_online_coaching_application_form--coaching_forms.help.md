# Online Coating Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect medical coaching application information from users. It contains multiple sections to gather necessary details such as applicant information, application form, exercise form, contact form, and medical information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in the following order: 
   - **Applicant Info Form**
   - **Application Form**
   - **Exercise Form**
   - **Contact Form**
   - **Upload Form**
   - **Medical Form** (including Medical Condition, Medical Condition Details, Medical Certifications, Doctor Information, and Medical Clinic)
2. Answer each question accurately and thoroughly to ensure the information is collected correctly.
3. Select the correct options for the relevant fields (e.g., Yes/No for select_one fields).
4. Upload any required documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Info Form**: This section asks for general information about the applicant, such as name, email, and other details.
	+ **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Application Form**: This section gathers more information about your application.
	+ **Exercise Form**: Provide detailed information about your exercise history, including dates and activities.
* **Contact Form**: Enter the contact information for an emergency contact.
	+ **Name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
	+ **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
	+ **Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Upload Form**: You can upload any additional documents required for your application.
* **Medical Form**:
	+ **Medical Condition**: Describe any medical condition you have or are experiencing.
	+ **Medical Condition Details**: Provide more details about your medical condition.
	+ **Medical Certifications**: List any medical certifications or licenses you hold.
	+ **Doctor Information**:
		- **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of your doctor.
		- **Doctor Phone**: Enter the phone number of your doctor.
		- **Doctor Email**: Enter the email address of your doctor.
		- **Office**: Enter the doctor's office location.
		- **License**: Confirm if your doctor has a license.
		- **Specialty**: Confirm if your doctor has a specialty.
		- **Medical Group**: Confirm if your doctor is part of a medical group.
		- **Medical Clinic**: Confirm if your doctor works in a medical clinic.
* **Relevant Documents**: Confirm if you have any relevant documents.
* **Medical Certificate**: Confirm if you have a medical certificate.
* **Emergency Contact**:
	+ **Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
	+ **Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
	+ **Email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
