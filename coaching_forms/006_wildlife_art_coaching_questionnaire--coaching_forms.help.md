# Wildlife Art Coaching Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from artists and art students seeking guidance on improving their wildlife art skills. It helps coaches provide personalized feedback and guidance to help artists and art students achieve their artistic goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each field carefully and honestly to ensure accurate information is collected.
2. Make sure to provide all required fields, indicated by (<type>: text).
3. Use this form in a professional and respectful manner, adhering to all applicable data protection and privacy laws.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): 
    In this field, please provide details about the session, such as date, time, and location.
* **Artist Details** (`artist_details`, text, optional): 
    Please provide information about the artist or art student, including name, contact information, and any relevant background details.
* **Student Details** (`student_details`, text, optional): 
    If you are a student, please provide your student ID or any other relevant student information.
* **Session Outcome** (`session_outcome`, text, optional): 
    Please describe the outcome or results of the session, including any notable accomplishments or areas for improvement.
* **Studio Details** (`studio_details`, text, optional): 
    If applicable, please provide information about the studio where the session took place, including the name, address, and any relevant contact information.
