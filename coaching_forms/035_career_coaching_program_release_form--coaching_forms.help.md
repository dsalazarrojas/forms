# Career Coaching Program Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Career Coaching Program Release Form is used to obtain consent and release of liability from participants in the career coaching program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's full legal name in the "Participant name" field.
2. Enter the participant's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the name of the coaching program in the "Program name" field.
4. Select "I consent" to indicate that the participant agrees to participate and be released from the program's terms of service.
5. Choose whether or not to allow the use of images and testimonials by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Photo and testimonial release" field.
6. If necessary, enter the emergency contact name and phone number for the participant.
7. Enter the anticipated start date of the program in the "Program start date" field.
8. Sign the form with your printed name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name** (`Participant name`, required): Enter the participant's full legal name, as it appears on their government-issued ID.
* **Email** (`Email`, required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Program name** (`Program name`, required): Enter the name of the coaching program the participant is enrolling in.
* **Consent to participate and release** (`Consent to participate and release`, required): Select "I consent" to indicate that the participant agrees to participate and be released from the program's terms of service.
* **Photo and testimonial release** (`Photo and testimonial release`, optional): Choose whether or not to allow the use of images and testimonials by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Emergency contact** (`Emergency contact`, optional): If necessary, enter the emergency contact name and phone number for the participant.
* **Program start date** (`Program start date`, optional): Enter the anticipated start date of the program.
* **Signature printed name** (`Signature printed name`, required): Sign the form with your printed name to indicate that you have reviewed and agreed to the program's terms of service.
* **Date signed** (`Date signed`, required): Enter the date you signed the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the program's terms of service before signing the form.
* Ensure that you are aware of the program's policies and procedures before enrolling.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the program administrator.
