# Agent Coaching Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agent Coaching Form is designed to help agents grow and develop professionally, providing a structured way for supervisors or coaches to offer feedback and guidance on performance, strengths, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of the coaching session.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), as they are essential for the form's purpose.
3. Provide clear and concise answers to each question, using the space provided for each field.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Service Agent Coaching Session --**: This is a brief introduction to the coaching session, providing context for the rest of the form.
* **Supervisor or Coach Name**: Enter the name of the supervisor or coach who conducted the session.
* **Agent being coached**: Enter the name of the agent who received the coaching.
* **Coaching Date**: Enter the date of the coaching session.
* **-- Session Focus --**: This section is an optional note-taking area for the coach to add context or notes about the session.
* **Primary Coaching Area**: Select the main theme of the session from the provided options: Tone of Voice and Empathy, Technical Knowledge and Accuracy, Efficiency and Handling Time, Process Compliance, or Sales and Upselling Techniques.
* **Specific interaction or ticket number reviewed**: Enter the specific interaction or ticket number that was reviewed during the session.
* **Performance Rating for this session**: Rate the performance of the agent during the session, selecting one of the provided options: Exceptional Performance, Meeting Expectations, Needs Development, or Below Standard.
* **Positive highlights and strengths**: Describe the areas where the agent performed well during the session.
* **Opportunities for development and improvement**: Provide specific areas where the agent can grow and improve.
* **Action items for the agent**: Select tasks for the agent to complete in the next period.
* **Follow-up Review Date**: Optional date to schedule a follow-up review.
* **Agent's comments on the session**: Provide feedback from the agent's perspective.
