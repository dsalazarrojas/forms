# Clarity Reflection Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clarity Reflection Worksheet is a tool to help participants process and apply the information gathered during a coaching or facilitation session. It encourages reflection, exploration, and planning to promote personal growth or development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the session information section with details about the session, such as participant name, date, and topic or focus area.
2.  Answer the reflection questions to share your thoughts, feelings, and insights gained from the session.
3.  Assess your level of clarity and understanding in the clarity and understanding section.
4.  Commit to specific actions you will take away from the session in the action planning section.
5.  Evaluate your success and completion of actions in the success and completion section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, required): Enter your name to associate your responses with your identity.
*   **Session Date** (`session_date`, required): Enter the date of the session for reference.
*   **Session Topic or Focus Area** (`session_topic`, required): Enter the topic or focus area of the session.
*   **What key insights did you gain from this session** (`key_insights`, required): Describe the key insights you gained from the session.
*   **How did this session make you feel** (`emotional_response`, optional): Share your emotions or responses to the session.
*   **What was most valuable about this experience** (`learning_value`, required): Identify what you valued most about the session.
*   **What challenges or obstacles did you identify** (`challenges_identified`, optional): List any challenges or obstacles you encountered during the session.
*   **How much clearer are you about your situation - scale 1-10** (`clarity_level`, required): Assess your level of clarity on a scale of 1-10.
*   **Areas where you have gained new understanding** (`understanding_areas`, optional): Describe areas where you gained new understanding.
*   **Remaining questions or areas of uncertainty** (`remaining_questions`, optional): List any remaining questions or areas of uncertainty.
*   **Specific actions you are committed to taking** (`committed_actions`, required): List specific actions you are committed to taking.
*   **First step you will take** (`first_action`, required): Identify the first step you will take.
*   **Timeline for your action plan** (`timeline`, required): Select a timeline for your action plan.
*   **What support or resources do you need** (`support_needed`, optional): Identify any support or resources you require.
*   **How will you stay accountable to these actions** (`accountability`, optional): Share your plan for staying accountable.
*   **Definition of success and completion** (`success_definition`, optional): Define what success and completion mean to you.
*   **Rate this session - scale 1-5** (`session_rating`, required): Rate the helpfulness of the session on a scale of 1-5.
