# Health Coach Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Coach Client Intake Form is designed to gather essential information from clients, ensuring that the health coach has a comprehensive understanding of the client's needs and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately to provide the health coach with the necessary information.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information provided, especially for medical conditions, medical history, and emergency contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name**: Enter your first name as you prefer to be called.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `true`

- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as you prefer to be called.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `true`

- **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication.
  * Type: `email`
  * Required: `true`

- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
  * Type: `date`
  * Required: `true`

- **Medical Conditions**: If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, list them here.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `false`

- **Medical History**: If you have any pre-existing medical history, list them here.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `false`

- **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `false`

- **Relationship**: Indicate the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
  * Type: `select_one`
  * Required: `false`

- **Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `true`

- **Address**: Enter the address of your emergency contact.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `false`

- **Provider**: Enter the name of your medical insurance provider.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `false`

- **Allergies**: If you have any allergies, list them here.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `false`

- **Medications**: If you are currently taking any medications, list them here.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `false`

- **Height**: Enter your height in centimeters.
  * Type: `number`
  * Required: `true`

- **Weight**: Enter your weight in kilograms.
  * Type: `number`
  * Required: `true`

- **Other Medical History**: If you have any other medical history, list them here.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `false`

- **Relationship Type**: Indicate the type of relationship between you and your emergency contact.
  * Type: `select_one`
  * Required: `true`

- **Phone Type**: Indicate the type of phone number provided for your emergency contact.
  * Type: `select_one`
  * Required: `true`

- **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `true`

- **Emergency Contact Note**: If necessary, provide any additional notes about your emergency contact.
  * Type: `note`
  * Required: `false`

- **Insurance Type**: Indicate whether you have medical insurance.
  * Type: `select_one`
  * Required: `true`

- **Insurance Number**: Enter your medical insurance number.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `true`

- **Permission**: Indicate whether your emergency contact has given permission to share information.
  * Type: `select_one`
  * Required: `true`

- **Note**: If necessary, provide any additional notes.
  * Type: `note`
  * Required: `false`
