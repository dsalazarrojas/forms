# wellness_coaching_interest_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellness Coaching Interest Survey is a form designed to help potential clients express their interests and preferences when seeking coaching services. It will help coaches tailor their approach to meet your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your health goals.
2. Choose your current coaching status (e.g., currently seeking a coach, not seeking a coach, undecided).
3. Select the coaching preferences that interest you the most.
4. Enter your coaching goals in the text field.
5. Choose your ideal client profile (e.g., female, male, non-binary).
6. Select the type of coach you prefer (e.g., personal, group, phone, video).
7. Choose your ideal coaching location (e.g., in-person, phone, video, online).
8. Select how often you'd like to schedule coaching sessions (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
9. Choose the time of day you'd prefer for coaching sessions (this field is not displayed as there are no options provided in the YAML).
10. Select the length of coaching sessions you prefer (e.g., 15, 30, 60 minutes).
11. Choose your preferred communication channels (e.g., text, email, phone, video).
12. Select the language you'd like your coach to use (e.g., English, Spanish, French).
13. Choose the gender of your coach (e.g., female, male, non-binary).
14. Select the level of coaching experience you prefer (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
15. Choose the coach certifications you'd like your coach to have (e.g., certification 1, certification 2, certification 3).
16. Select the areas of coaching your coach has experience in (e.g., health, wellness, fitness).
17. Choose your budget range for coaching sessions (e.g., low, medium, high).
17. Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
