# Athletic Coaching Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athletic Coaching Training Form is a tool used by coaches to gather information about an athlete's current fitness level, training history, and goals. It helps the coach to understand the athlete's needs and create a tailored training plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide an overview of the training program in the "Training Program Overview" section.
2. Input your full name in the "Coach Name" section and the athlete's full name in the "Athlete Full Name" section.
3. Input the name of the training plan or program in the "Training Plan or Program Name" section.
4. Select the athlete's current fitness level in the "Current Fitness Level" section.
5. Input the average previous training volume over the last 4 weeks in the "Average Previous Training Volume - Hours per Week" section.
6. Input any recent performance metrics in the "Recent Performance Metrics" section.
7. Input the athlete's primary athletic goal in the "Primary Athletic Goal" section.
8. Select all the days the athlete is available to train in the "Available Training Days" section.
9. Select the athlete's preferred time of day for training in the "Preferred Training Time of Day" section.
10. Input the athlete's preferred training facility or location in the "Primary Training Facility or Location" section.
11. List any current injuries or persistent niggles in the "Current Injuries or Persistent Niggles" section.
12. Select the athlete's current health and medical clearance status in the "Health and Medical Clearance Status" section.
13. Select the athlete's preferred communication channel in the "Preferred Communication Channel" section.
14. Select how often the athlete wants coaching feedback in the "Desired Frequency of Coaching Feedback" section.
15. Input the athlete's key motivators in the "Key Motivators" section.
16. Select the athlete's plan commitment in the "Plan Commitment" section.
17. Input the athlete's electronic signature in the "Athlete Electronic Signature" section.
18. Input the date of signature in the "Date of Signature" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Coach Name (id: 2)**: This is the field where you input your full name as the coach.
* **Athlete Full Name (id: 3)**: This is the field where the athlete inputs their full name.
* **Training Plan or Program Name (id: 4)**: This is the field where you input the name of the training plan or program.
* **Program Start Date (id: 5)**: This is the field where you input the start date of the training program.
* **Program End Date (id: 6)**: This is the field where you input the end date of the training program.
* **Current Fitness Level (id: 8)**: This is the field where you select the athlete's current fitness level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Elite/Professional).
* **Average Previous Training Volume - Hours per Week (id: 9)**: This is the field where you input the athlete's average training volume over the last 4 weeks.
* **Recent Performance Metrics (id: 10)**: This is the field where the athlete inputs recent performance metrics (e.g. last race time, max lift, VO2 Max).
* **Primary Athletic Goal (id: 11)**: This is the field where the athlete inputs their primary athletic goal for the program.
* **Available Training Days (id: 13)**: This is the field where you select all the days the athlete is available to train.
* **Preferred Training Time of Day (id: 14)**: This is the field where you select the athlete's preferred time of day for training.
* **Primary Training Facility or Location (id: 15)**: This is the field where the athlete inputs their preferred training facility or location.
* **Current Injuries or Persistent Niggles (id: 17)**: This is the field where the athlete lists any current injuries or persistent niggles.
* **Health and Medical Clearance Status (id: 18)**: This is the field where you select the athlete's current health and medical clearance status (Fully Cleared for High Intensity, Conditional Clearance, or Pending Clearance).
* **Preferred Communication Channel (id: 20)**: This is the field where the athlete selects their preferred communication channel (Training App Messaging, Email, Text/WhatsApp, Phone Call, or In-person Meeting).
* **Desired Frequency of Coaching Feedback (id: 21)**: This is the field where the athlete selects how often they want coaching feedback (Daily, 2-3 times per week, Weekly, or Bi-weekly).
* **Key Motivators (id: 22)**: This is the field where the athlete inputs their key motivators (e.g. data and metrics, verbal encouragement, competition).
* **Plan Commitment (id: 24)**: This is the field where the athlete selects their plan commitment (I am committed, Other).
* **Athlete Electronic Signature (id: 25)**: This is the field where the athlete inputs their electronic signature.
* **Date of Signature (id: 26)**: This is the field where the athlete inputs the date of signature.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully read each section and understand what is being asked before inputting information.
* Use clear and concise language when inputting information in each field.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Review the form regularly to ensure it reflects changes in your training program or athlete's progress.
* Use the form as a tool to establish a positive and supportive relationship with your athlete, fostering open communication and a clear understanding of their needs and goals.
