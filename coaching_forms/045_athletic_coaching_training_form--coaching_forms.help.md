# Athletic Coaching Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athletic Coaching Training Form is a tool used by coaches to gather information about an athlete's current fitness level, training history, and goals. It helps the coach to understand the athlete's needs and create a tailored training plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide an overview of the training program in the "Training Program Overview" section.
2. Input your full name in the "Coach Name" section and the athlete's full name in the "Athlete Full Name" section.
3. Input the name of the training plan or program in the "Training Plan or Program Name" section.
4. Select the athlete's current fitness level in the "Current Fitness Level" section.
5. Input the average previous training volume over the last 4 weeks in the "Average Previous Training Volume - Hours per Week" section.
6. Input any recent performance metrics in the "Recent Performance Metrics" section.
7. Input the athlete's primary athletic goal in the "Primary Athletic Goal" section.
8. Select all the days the athlete is available to train in the "Available Training Days" section.
9. Select the athlete's preferred time of day for training in the "Preferred Training Time of Day" section.
10. Input the athlete's preferred training facility or location in the "Primary Training Facility or Location" section.
11. List any current injuries or persistent niggles in the "Current Injuries or Persistent Niggles" section.
12. Select the athlete's current health and medical clearance status in the "Health and Medical Clearance Status" section.
13. Select the athlete's preferred communication channel in the "Preferred Communication Channel" section.
14. Select how often the athlete wants coaching feedback in the "Desired Frequency of Coaching Feedback" section.
15. Input the athlete's key motivators in the "Key Motivators" section.
16. Select the athlete's plan commitment in the "Plan Commitment" section.
17. Input the athlete's electronic signature in the "Athlete Electronic Signature" section.
18. Input the date of signature in the "Date of Signature" section.
