# Public Speaking Coaching Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in seeking public speaking coaching services. If you're looking for help with presentations, meetings, or other professional settings, this form will guide you through the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and fill in the required information on each page.
2. Answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
3. Don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1
* **Title and Name** (`title`, `name`, required): Enter your name and a brief title or heading that you'd like to use for your coaching sessions.
* **Coaching Goals** (`coaching_goals`, required): Describe your goals for the coaching services. What specific areas you'd like to improve or achieve through the coaching.
* **Availability and Schedule** (`availability`, required): Provide your availability for coaching sessions, including your preferred schedule and any constraints you have.

### Page 2
* **Coaching Experience and Background** (`coaching_experience`, required): Share your background and experience in public speaking, including any relevant education or training.
* **Previous Coaching Experience** (`previous_coaching`, required): If applicable, share any previous coaching or mentoring experience you've had in public speaking.

### Page 3
* **Specific Coaching Topics** (`specific_topics`, required): List the specific topics or areas you'd like to focus on in the coaching sessions.
* **Coaching Preferences and Questions** (`coaching_preferences`, required): Provide any additional information about your coaching preferences or questions you have.

## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible in your answers.
* Don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need help throughout the process.
* Once you've completed the form, a coach will review your information and contact you for further instructions.
