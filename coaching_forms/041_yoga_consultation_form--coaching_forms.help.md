# yogaConsultationForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information for yoga consultation or other health-related services. It should only be filled out by users who have had a previous interaction with the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have had a previous interaction with the organization.
2. Fill out the form with relevant information about yourself.
3. Make sure to complete all fields that are marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details (1)**: Enter your name, address, and any other identifying information.
* **Medical Conditions (2)**: Select any relevant medical conditions you have.
* **Allergies (3)**: Select any allergies you have, such as nuts, shellfish, or soy.
* **Current Health (4)**: Select how you would rate your current health as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Medical History (5)**: Enter any relevant medical history you have.
* **Previous Injuries (6)**: Enter any previous injuries you have experienced.
* **Medication Usage (7)**: Enter any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Devices (8)**: Enter any medical devices you are currently using.
* **Emergency Contact (9)**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name (10)**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (11)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Alternate Phone (12)**: Enter an alternate phone number for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email (13)**: Enter the email of your emergency contact.
