# Retail Growth Strategy Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help retail businesses register for a coaching session to discuss their growth strategy and receive personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in its entirety, and make sure to provide as much detail as possible for each field.
- You can add or remove pages as needed, depending on your specific situation.
- Once you have completed the form, submit it to receive a response from our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Retail Leads** (`retail_leads`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to describe the current number of retail leads you are managing. Please provide as much detail as possible, including the type of leads (e.g., phone numbers, email addresses, etc.).
- **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, required/optional): In this field, describe the specific details of the coaching session you are registering for (e.g., topics you would like to cover, specific challenges you would like to address, etc.).
- **Session Schedule** (`schedule_session`, text, required/optional): Here, you can specify the preferred date and time for your coaching session.
- **Lead Capture** (`capture_leads`, text, required/optional): In this field, describe the ways in which you are currently capturing leads (e.g., online forms, in-store promotions, etc.).
- **Data Collection** (`collect_data`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to describe your current data collection methods and what data you are collecting (e.g., customer information, sales data, etc.).
- **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, text, required/optional): Here, you can explain how you follow up with your leads (e.g., phone calls, email follow-ups, etc.).
- **Session Outcomes** (`session_outcomes`, text, required/optional): In this field, describe the specific outcomes you are looking to achieve from your coaching session (e.g., increased sales, improved customer service, etc.).
- **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required/optional): Finally, describe any next steps you are planning to take after the coaching session (e.g., implementing new strategies, tracking progress, etc.).
