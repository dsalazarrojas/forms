# Gymnastics Coach Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings on the performance of gymnastics coaches. It's intended to help coaches improve their skills and provide a better experience for athletes and parents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the answer for "How is the coach performing?" by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the options provided.
2. Add any additional feedback about the coach's performance in the "What feedback do you have?" field.
3. Choose the coaching style of the coach from the options provided.
4. Rate the coach's communication skills by selecting one of the four options.
5. Evaluate your overall experience with the coach by selecting a number rating.
6. Rate the coach by selecting a number rating.
7. Enter the date you completed this evaluation form.
8. Enter the time you completed this evaluation form.
9. Enter the email address of the person who completed this form.
10. Enter the phone number of the person who completed this form.
11. Choose how you will follow up with the coach (if applicable).
12. Enter the follow-up date and time (if applicable).
13. Enter the follow-up phone number (if applicable).
14. Enter the follow-up email address (if applicable).
15. Add any additional comments for follow-up (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How is the coach performing?** (`coaching_performance`, select_one, required)
	+ This field asks if the coach is performing well. Select "Yes" if the coach is doing a good job, "No" otherwise.
* **What feedback do you have?** (`feedback_comments`, note, optional)
	+ This field is for any additional feedback you may have about the coach's performance.
* **What style of coaching is the coach doing?** (`coaching_style`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose the coaching style of the coach from the options provided.
* **How well is the coach communicating?** (`communication_skills`, select_one, required)
	+ Rate the coach's communication skills by selecting one of the four options.
* **Overall experience with the coach?** (`overall_experience`, number, required)
	+ Rate your overall experience with the coach by selecting a number rating.
* **Coach rating?** (`coach_rating`, number, required)
	+ Rate the coach by selecting a number rating.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required)
	+ Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required)
	+ Enter the time you completed this form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required)
	+ Enter the email address of the person who completed this form.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required)
	+ Enter the phone number of the person who completed this form.
* **Follow up?** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose how you will follow up with the coach (if applicable).
* **Follow up date** (`follow_up_date`, date, required)
	+ Enter the follow-up date (if applicable).
* **Follow up time** (`follow_up_time`, time, required)
	+ Enter the follow-up time (if applicable).
* **Follow up phone** (`follow_up_phone`, text, required)
	+ Enter the follow-up phone number (if applicable).
* **Follow up email** (`follow_up_email`, email, required)
	+ Enter the follow-up email address (if applicable).
* **Follow up comments** (`follow_up_comments`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Add any additional comments for follow-up (if applicable).
* **Follow up notes** (`follow_up_notes`, note, required)
	+ Add any additional notes for follow-up (if applicable).
