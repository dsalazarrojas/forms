# Holistic Mastery Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holistic Mastery Agreement form is designed to collect personal and contact information from individuals, as well as their agreement status. This form helps to ensure that the necessary details are collected in a structured and consistent manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete the form, follow these steps:
	* Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
	* Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
	* Provide your Email address in the Email field (required for verification purposes).
	* Enter your Phone number in the Phone field (required for communication).
	* Enter your Address (optional, but recommended for easier communication).
	* Enter the Date of Service for the services provided (required for record-keeping).
	* Enter your Date of Birth (optional, but recommended for record-keeping).
	* Leave the Date Signed field blank, as it will be automatically populated upon submission.
	* Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions by typing "Agreed" in the Terms and Conditions field.
	* Finally, select "Agreed" or "Not Agreed" in the Agreement Signed field to confirm your status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your residential or business address for easier communication.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, required): Enter the date the services were provided or will be provided.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth for record-keeping purposes.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): This field will be automatically populated upon submission.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions by typing "Agreed".
* **Agreement Signed** (`agreement_signed`, select_one, required): Select "Agreed" or "Not Agreed" to confirm your status.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information in the First Name, Last Name, and Email fields for proper identity verification.
* The Date of Service field is required for record-keeping purposes, so ensure you enter the correct date.
* If you have any issues with your phone number or email, please let the relevant party know to avoid delays.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the relevant support team for assistance.
