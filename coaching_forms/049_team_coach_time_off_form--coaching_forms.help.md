# Team Coach Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from team coaches regarding their leave status, team leadership status, and other relevant details to track and manage team coach time off requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Team Coach Name and Team Name.
2. If you are requesting leave, select the correct Role, Team Leadership Status, and Team Leadership Position.
3. Choose the Reason for Leave.
4. Fill in the Leave Start and Leave End dates.
5. Select the correct Status.
6. If you are submitting this form as a team coach, fill in your Coach ID.
7. If you are submitting this form for a team, fill in the Team Coaches List.
8. Choose the Submit Date and Submit By.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Coach Name** (Team Coach Name, text, required): Enter your name as a team coach.
* **Team Name** (Team Name, text, required): Enter the name of the team you are coaching.
* **Role** (Role, select_one, optional): If applicable, select the role you are in.
*   *If you are not requesting leave, leave this field blank.*
* **Leave Start Date** (Leave Start Date, date, optional): If you are requesting leave, enter your leave start date.
* **Leave End Date** (Leave End Date, date, optional): If you are requesting leave, enter your leave end date.
*   *If you are not requesting leave, leave these fields blank.*
* **Team Leadership Status** (Team Leadership Status, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, select your team leadership status.
* **Team Leadership Position** (Team Leadership Position, select_one, optional): If applicable, select your team leadership position.
*   *If you are not in a team leadership position, leave this field blank.*
* **Reason for Leave** (Reason for Leave, text, required): Enter a brief description of why you are requesting leave.
* **Return Date** (Return Date, date, optional): If you are requesting leave, enter your expected return date.
* **Status** (Status, select_one, optional): If applicable, select your status.
*   *If you are not requesting leave, leave this field blank.*
* **Coach ID** (Coach ID, number, required): If you are submitting this form as a team coach, enter your Coach ID.
*   *If you are not a team coach, leave this field blank.*
* **Team Coaches List** (Team Coaches List, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, select the team coaches you are submitting this form for.
*   *If you are submitting this form as an individual coach, leave this field blank.*
* **Submit Date** (Submit Date, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Submit By** (Submit By, select_one, optional): If applicable, select who is submitting this form.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are filling out this form only when you are officially on leave and have discussed your leave with your team.
* Please note that this form should only be filled out once for each leave period.
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, contact your team lead or HR representative for guidance.
