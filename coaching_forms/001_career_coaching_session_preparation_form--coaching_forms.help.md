# Career Coaching Session Preparation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Career Coaching Session Preparation Form is designed to gather essential information about you, allowing your coach to tailor the coaching session to your needs and goals. This form helps your coach understand your schedule, preferences, and challenges, ensuring you receive personalized guidance and support during the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date for your next coaching session.
2. Provide your full name and email address.
3. Enter your current job title.
4. If applicable, link to your LinkedIn profile.
5. List your top 3 professional challenges.
6. Share any recent achievements or wins you're proud of.
7. Choose your primary goals for the session (e.g., Resume Review, Interview Prep, Career Transition).
8. Select the most valuable outcome from this session.
9. Indicate your current career satisfaction level (1-10).
10. Choose your desired frequency of future sessions (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, As needed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of Coaching Session**: Select the scheduled date for your next session.
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email Address**: Provide your email address.
* **Current Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
* **LinkedIn Profile URL**: If you have a LinkedIn profile, provide the link. (Optional)
* **Top 3 Professional Challenges**: List the main obstacles you are currently facing.
* **Any Recent Achievements or Wins?**: Share anything you've been proud of lately.
* **Primary Goals for Today**: Select all relevant goals (e.g., Resume Review, Interview Prep, Career Transition).
* **Is your resume up to date?**: Indicate if your resume is up to date or in progress.
* **Specific Questions for your Coach**: If you have any specific questions or advice you're seeking, provide them here.
* **Most Valuable Outcome from this Session**: Choose the most valuable outcome from this session.
* **Current Career Satisfaction (1-10)**: Indicate your current career satisfaction level.
* **Desired Frequency of Future Sessions**: Choose your desired frequency of future sessions (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, As needed).
