# Powerlifting Coach Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us gauge interest in the powerlifting coaching program. We need your basic information, coaching experience, and training details to evaluate your qualifications and suitability for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. Make sure to provide your correct contact information and coach details.
2. **Be concise and clear** in your responses. This will help us understand your interest and suitability for the program.
3. **Double-check your answers** before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **Name** (`text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be known.
* **Email** (`text`, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it. This will help us contact you for further questions and discussions.
* **Social Media Handle** (`text`, optional): If you have a social media handle (e.g., Instagram, Facebook, etc.), please enter it. This will help us connect with you on your preferred platform.

### Coach Details
* **Coaching Experience** (`text`, required): Describe your relevant experience as a powerlifting coach. This can include certifications, qualifications, or years of experience.
* **Coaching Style** (`text`, optional): Briefly describe your coaching style and what you enjoy doing most in your coaching sessions.
* **Specialties** (`text`, optional): If you have any specialties or areas of expertise, please mention them here (e.g., powerlifting, weightlifting, strength training, etc.).

### Training Information
* **Training Philosophy** (`text`, required): Describe your training philosophy, including your favorite exercises, routines, and approaches to training.
* **Training Experience** (`text`, required): Describe your experience with various training methods, including years of experience, certifications, or notable achievements.
