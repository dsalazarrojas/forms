# Coaching Course Assignment Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from students regarding their coaching course assignments. It is used to gather details about the assignment submission, including student information, assignment details, and feedback preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form carefully and accurately**. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting.
2. **Double-check your information**. Review each field carefully to ensure it is correct and complete.
3. **Submit the form**. Once you have filled out the form, click the submit button to send it to your instructor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your legal full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number, if applicable.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your student identification number.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the coaching course you are enrolled in.
* **Course Code** (`course_code`, text, required): Enter the course identifier code.
* **Instructor or Coach Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your instructor.
* **Assignment Title** (`assignment_title`, text, required): Enter the name or title of the assignment.
* **Assignment Number** (`assignment_number`, select_one, required): Select which assignment this is, from the provided options.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Select the date you are submitting this assignment.
* **Assignment Due Date** (`due_date`, date, required): Select the date the assignment was due.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required): Select whether this submission is on time or not.
* **Word Count** (`word_count`, number, optional): Enter the total number of words in your submission, if applicable.
* **Assignment Description** (`assignment_description`, text, required): Provide a brief summary of your submission.
* **Learning Objectives Addressed** (`learning_objectives`, text, required): Describe what you learned from this assignment.
* **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, text, optional): Describe any difficulties you encountered while working on this assignment.
* **Key Takeaways** (`key_takeaways`, text, optional): Summarize your main learnings from this assignment.
* **Self-Rating of Work** (`self_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your work from the provided options.
* **Time Spent on Assignment** (`time_spent`, select_one, optional): Select the amount of time spent on this assignment, from the provided options.
* **Resources Used** (`resources_used`, text, optional): Describe any resources that helped you with this assignment.
* **Did You Collaborate With Others** (`collaboration`, select_one, required): Select whether you worked on this assignment with others.
* **Collaboration Details** (`collaboration_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your collaboration if applicable.
* **I Confirm This Is My Original Work** (`original_work`, select_one, required): Confirm that this work is your original submission.
* **All Citations Are Complete** (`citations_complete`, select_one, required): Confirm that all citations are properly included.
* **File Format Submitted** (`file_format`, select_one, required): Select the format in which you submitted this assignment.
* **File Name** (`file_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your submitted file.
* **Additional Comments for Instructor** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments or notes for your instructor.
* **Feedback Preference** (`feedback_preference`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to receive feedback, if applicable.
