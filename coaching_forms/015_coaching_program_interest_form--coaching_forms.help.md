# Coaching Program Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coaching Program Interest Form is designed to help us understand your interest and goals for the coaching program. Please fill out the form accurately to help us guide you effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Contact Information**: This section is to collect your basic contact information.
2. **Program Interest**: Choose the type of coaching you're interested in (e.g., Executive, Life, Career, etc.).
3. **Goals and Challenges**: Describe your goals and challenges in as much detail as possible.
4. **Availability**: Select your preferred coaching format (in-person, virtual, phone, or no preference) and your availability for coaching.
5. **Previous Coaching Experience**: If you have worked with a coach before, please let us know.
6. **Budget and Next Steps**: If you have a budget in mind, please select it (optional).
7. **Schedule a Discovery Call**: Let us know if you'd like to schedule a discovery call.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name (required).
* **Last Name**: Your last name (required).
* **Email Address**: Your contact email address (required).
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number (required).
* **Company/Organization**: If you're representing a company or organization, please provide it (optional).
* **Job Title**: Your job title (optional).
* **Type of Coaching Interested In**: Select the type of coaching that best suits your needs.
* **What are your coaching goals?**: Describe your coaching goals in as much detail as possible.
* **Current Challenges or Obstacles**: Describe your current challenges or obstacles.
* **Preferred Coaching Format**: Select your preferred coaching format (in-person, virtual, phone, or no preference).
* **When are you available for coaching?**: Select your availability for coaching.
* **Preferred Coaching Frequency**: Select your preferred coaching frequency (optional).
* **Have you worked with a coach before?**: If you have, please specify.
* **Details of Previous Coaching Experience**: If you have worked with a coach before, describe your experience.
* **Budget Range**: If you have a budget in mind, select it (optional).
* **Would you like to schedule a discovery call?**: Let us know if you'd like to schedule a discovery call.
* **Best Time to Contact You**: If you have a preferred time to be contacted, please specify (optional).
* **Additional Information**: If there's anything else we should know, please provide it (optional).

By following these instructions and filling out the form accurately, you'll be able to get the most out of the coaching program and have a better experience. If you have any questions or doubts, do not hesitate to contact us.
