# Executive Coaching Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from executive coaching sessions, aiming to provide an objective understanding of the team's performance and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1.  Read through the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2.  Select the answers that best describe your experiences and opinions.
3.  Answer all fields, but do not worry about leaving some fields blank if they do not apply to you.
4.  Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they accurately reflect your thoughts and feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Executive Coaching Feedback Form Intro (Page 1)**: This field serves as a brief introduction to the form. Please do not complete this field, as it is intended for informational purposes only.
- **Coaching Session Details (Page 2)**: In this field, describe the coaching session that took place. Provide as much detail as you feel is necessary, including any relevant context or circumstances.
- **Team Feedback (Page 3)**: Rate the level of helpfulness of the team during the coaching session. Choose from the following options:
  • Team was very helpful and collaborative
  • Team was partially helpful
  • Team was not helpful
- **Personal Growth (Page 4)**: Rate your level of personal growth and development as a result of the coaching session. Choose from the following options:
  • Very high
  • High
  • Neutral
  • Low
- **Session Outcome (Page 5)**: Rate the overall outcome of the coaching session. Choose from the following options:
  • Very positive
  • Positive
  • Negative
  • Neutral
- **Session Improvement Areas (Page 6)**: Identify the areas where you feel the coaching session could be improved. Choose from the following options:
  • Communication
  • Time management
  • Problem solving
  • Leadership
- **Next Steps (Page 7)**: Describe any next steps or actions you plan to take as a result of the coaching session. Be as detailed as possible.
- **Overall Satisfaction (Page 8)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the coaching session. Choose from the following options:
  • Very satisfied
  • Satisfied
  • Neutral
  • Dissatisfied
