# Cheerleading Conduct Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the conduct of cheerleaders during events, focusing on their performance, behavior, and adherence to safety and stunting rules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the athlete's name in the "Athlete Name" field.
2. Identify yourself as the reviewing coach or manager in the "Reviewing Coach/Manager" field.
3. Enter the date of the conduct review in the "Date of Conduct Review" field.
4. Evaluate the athlete's conduct based on the provided criteria, selecting the corresponding options for:
	* Attendance and Punctuality
	* Effort and Focus during Practice
	* Attitude towards Teammates
	* Adherence to Safety and Stunting Rules
	* Professionalism and Dress Code
5. If necessary, provide feedback on the athlete's performance and behavior in the "Performance and Behavioral Highlights" and "Areas for Behavioral Improvement" fields.
6. Select the current standing status of the athlete based on this review.
7. Confirm if a follow-up meeting is required.
8. Finally, sign the form digitally to confirm the review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Athlete Name** (Required): Enter the name of the athlete being reviewed.
* **Reviewing Coach/Manager** (Required): Identify yourself as the reviewing coach or manager.
* **Date of Conduct Review** (Required): Enter the date of the conduct review.
* **Attendance and Punctuality** (Required): Evaluate the athlete's attendance and punctuality during events.
	+ Exemplary: The athlete has perfect attendance and punctuality.
	+ Satisfactory: The athlete attends most events, but with some issues with punctuality.
	+ Needs Improvement: The athlete often misses events or is late frequently.
	+ Unsatisfactory: The athlete regularly misses events or is consistently late.
* **Effort and Focus during Practice** (Required): Evaluate the athlete's effort and focus during practices.
	+ High Effort: The athlete consistently demonstrates high effort and focus.
	+ Moderate Effort: The athlete shows moderate effort and focus.
	+ Lacks Focus: The athlete often lacks focus or effort.
	+ Lacks Effort: The athlete rarely shows any effort.
* **Attitude towards Teammates** (Required): Evaluate the athlete's attitude towards teammates.
	+ Very Positive: The athlete demonstrates a very positive attitude towards teammates.
	+ Generally Positive: The athlete has a generally positive attitude.
	+ Occasionally Negative: The athlete sometimes displays a negative attitude.
	+ Disruptive: The athlete frequently displays a negative, disruptive attitude.
* **Adherence to Safety and Stunting Rules** (Required): Evaluate the athlete's adherence to safety and stunting rules.
	+ Always Compliant: The athlete always follows safety and stunting rules.
	+ Mostly Compliant: The athlete mostly follows safety and stunting rules.
	+ Safety Violations Noted: The athlete has been noted for safety rule violations.
	+ Not Compliant: The athlete rarely follows safety and stunting rules.
* **Professionalism and Dress Code** (Required): Evaluate the athlete's professionalism and adherence to dress code.
	+ Perfect Compliance: The athlete consistently demonstrates perfect professionalism and dress code.
	+ Minor Issues: The athlete has minor issues with professionalism and dress code.
	+ Frequent Violations: The athlete frequently violates dress code and professionalism standards.
	+ Not Compliant: The athlete rarely demonstrates any professionalism or follows dress code.
* **Performance and Behavioral Highlights** (Optional): Provide feedback on the athlete's performance and behavior, highlighting strengths and areas for improvement.
* **Areas for Behavioral Improvement** (Optional): Identify specific areas for the athlete to improve their behavior.
* **Current Team Standing** (Required): Select the athlete's current standing status based on this review.
	+ Good Standing - Eligible for All Events: The athlete is in good standing and eligible for all events.
	+ Probationary Status - Needs Review: The athlete is on probation and requires a review.
	+ Suspended from Next Event: The athlete is suspended from the next event.
	+ Recommended for Dismissal: The athlete's conduct is not suitable and they may be dismissed.
* **Follow-up Meeting Required** (Required): Confirm if a follow-up meeting is required with the athlete.
* **Coach Digital Signature** (Required): Sign the form digitally to confirm the review.
