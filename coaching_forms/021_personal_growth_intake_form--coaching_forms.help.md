# Personal Growth Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients seeking personal growth coaching.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. If you are already in a coaching program, please check with your coach before filling out this form.
3. Please do not fill out this form if you have previously completed a similar form.
