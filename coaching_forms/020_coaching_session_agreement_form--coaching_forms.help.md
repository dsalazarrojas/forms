# Coaching Session Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to establish a mutual understanding between the coach and client regarding the coaching relationship, objectives, and expectations. It ensures both parties are clear on the coaching process, duration, format, and financial terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing your details and agreeing to the terms and conditions outlined.
2. Enter your name and contact information (email address) to confirm your identity.
3. Choose your preferred session frequency (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or Custom schedule).
4. Decide on the length of each session (in minutes).
5. Select the format for each session (In-person, Phone, Video call, or Flexible).
6. Specify the total number of sessions (if applicable).
7. Describe the coaching objectives, primary focus areas, and expected outcomes.
8. Agree to the financial terms, including the coaching fee and payment terms.
9. Confirm the cancellation notice period and refund policy.
10. Sign the confidentiality agreement and commit to active participation in coaching.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`): Enter your name as the coach.
* **Coach Email** (`coach_email`): Enter your email address as the coach.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`): Enter your name as the client.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`): Enter your email address as the client.
* **Agreement Start Date** (`agreement_start_date`): The date this coaching agreement takes effect.
* **Session Frequency** (`session_frequency`): Choose how often the sessions will occur (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or Custom schedule).
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`): Specify the length of each session in minutes.
* **Session Format** (`session_format`): Choose the format for each session (In-person, Phone, Video call, or Flexible).
* **Total Number of Sessions** (`total_sessions`): Enter the total number of sessions, if applicable.
* **Coaching Objectives** (`coaching_objectives`): Describe the specific objectives for the coaching relationship.
* **Primary Focus Areas** (`primary_focus_areas`): Identify key areas to address during coaching.
* **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`): Specify the results you expect from the coaching relationship.
* **Coaching Fee** (`coaching_fee`): Agree to the coaching fee for each session or as a total package.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Confirm the payment terms and conditions.
* **Cancellation Notice** (`cancellation_notice`): Specify the number of hours' notice required for cancellation.
* **Refund Policy** (`refund_policy`): Describe the refund policy if applicable.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`): Agree to maintain client confidentiality.
* **Client Commitment** (`client_commitment`): Confirm your commitment to actively participate in coaching.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`): Confirm the date this coaching agreement takes effect.
