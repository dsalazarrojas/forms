# Parent Coaching Intake Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Coaching Intake Survey is a form used to gather information from parents seeking coaching services. It helps us understand their goals, expectations, and needs for the coaching process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as a parent.
2. Select the number of family members you are responsible for.
3. Provide your birthdate.
4. Choose the type of coaching you are interested in (one-on-one, group, or other).
5. Indicate how frequently you would like to have coaching sessions.
6. Specify how long each session will be.
7. Describe your coaching goals and expectations.
8. Provide an emergency contact person and their relationship with you.
9. Enter the emergency contact's phone number (optional).
10. Enter the emergency contact's email (optional).
11. Add any additional comments or remarks.
12. Describe your coaching goals in more detail.
13. Provide notes on session frequency.
14. Specify the type of relationship between you and your emergency contact.
15. Choose your coaching goals and expectations.
16. Provide notes on your coaching type.
17. Add any session notes.
18. Choose a date and time for your coaching sessions.
19. Describe your parent goals and expectations.
20. Specify the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
21. Add any session notes.
22. Enter any other coaching details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a parent.
* **Family Members** (`family_members`, select_one, required): Select the number of family members you are responsible for.
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, date, required): Enter your birthdate.
* **Coaching Type** (`coaching_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of coaching you are interested in (one on one, group, or other).
* **Session Frequency** (`session_frequency`, number, required): Indicate how frequently you would like to have coaching sessions.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, required): Specify how long each session will be.
* **Coaching Goals** (`coaching_goals`, note, required): Describe your coaching goals and expectations.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Provide an emergency contact person and their relationship with you.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number (if applicable).
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, optional): Enter the emergency contact's email (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks.
* **Coaching Goals Details** (`coaching_goals_details`, note, required): Describe your coaching goals in more detail.
* **Session Frequency Notes** (`session_frequency_notes`, note, required): Provide notes on session frequency.
* **Parent Counselor Relationship** (`parent_counselor_relationship`, select_multiple, required): Specify the type of relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Session Length Notes** (`session_length_notes`, note, required): Add any notes on session length.
* **Coaching Goals Goals** (`coaching_goals_goals`, select_multiple, required): Choose your coaching goals and expectations.
* **Coaching Type Notes** (`coaching_type_notes`, note, required): Provide notes on your coaching type.
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, note, required): Add any session notes.
* **Coaching Session Date** (`coaching_session_date`, date, required): Choose a date for your coaching sessions.
* **Coaching Session Time** (`coaching_session_time`, time, required): Specify a time for your coaching sessions.
* **Parent Goals and Expectations** (`parent_goals_and_expectations`, select_multiple, required): Describe your parent goals and expectations.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, required): Specify the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Session Notes 2** (`session_notes2`, note, required): Add any session notes.
* **Other Coaching Details** (`other_coaching_details`, text, optional): Enter any other coaching details (if applicable).
