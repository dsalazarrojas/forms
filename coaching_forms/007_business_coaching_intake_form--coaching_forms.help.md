# Business Coaching Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your business and your coaching goals and expectations. It will help your coach understand your business situation and needs, ensuring that your coaching journey is personalized and effective.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If you are unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask your coach for clarification.
3. Once you have completed the form, click submit to send it to your coach.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email Address**: Your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number for contact.
* **Company Name**: The name of your business.
* **Job Title**: Your current position in the business.
* **Years Experience**: The number of years you have been in business.
* **Current Business Stage**: The stage of your business (e.g. Planning, Just Starting, Scaling Up, Established, Changing Direction).
* **Current Annual Revenue**: Your approximate annual revenue.
* **Main Coaching Goals**: Select all the goals you want to achieve through coaching (e.g. Business Growth, Leadership Development, Profitability, Team Management).
* **Biggest Current Challenge**: Describe your top challenge.
* **Major Obstacles**: Select all obstacles you are currently facing (e.g. Time Management, Financial Constraints, Team Issues).
* **What Success Looks Like**: Describe what success looks like to you.
* **Coaching Frequency**: How often you would like to meet with your coach (e.g. Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
* **Commitment Level**: Indicate how committed you are to the coaching process.
* **Budget for Coaching**: Your budget for coaching (e.g. Under 5k, 5k-15k, 15k-30k, 30k+).
* **Expected Timeline**: How long you expect to work with your coach (e.g. 3 Months, 6 Months, 12 Months, Ongoing).

## Tips
* Be as honest and detailed as possible in your answers.
* Don't hesitate to ask your coach for clarification if you are unsure about any of the questions.
* This form is designed to help your coach understand your business situation and needs, so please take the time to complete it thoroughly.
