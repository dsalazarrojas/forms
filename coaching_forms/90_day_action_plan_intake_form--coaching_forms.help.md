# 90 Day Action Plan Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The 90 Day Action Plan Intake Form is a tool designed to help clients or participants create a customized 90-day action plan for achieving their goals. This form is intended to gather essential information for coaches or advisors to create an effective 90-day plan tailored to the client's specific needs and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your **Client or Participant Name** (Field 2).
2. Enter the **Plan Start Date** (Field 3) when you plan to begin working on your 90-day action plan.
3. Clearly state your **Primary 90-Day Goal** (Field 4), being specific and measurable in your description.
4. Select the **Priority Level** (Field 5) for your goal, choosing from High, Medium, or Low.
5. Specify how **Success will be Measured** (Field 6) through metrics or Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
6. Describe the **Milestone 1** (Field 7) you want to accomplish within the 90-day timeframe, including the target date for completion.
7. If applicable, describe the **Milestone 2** (Field 8) you aim for within the 90-day period.
8. Indicate any **Anticipated Obstacles** (Field 9) you foresee, such as Time constraints, Budget limitations, Resource availability, Skill gaps, or Competing priorities.
9. Identify any **Support or Resources Needed** (Field 10) for achieving your 90-day goals, such as weekly coaching sessions, project management support, training, or accountability partners.
10. Choose your **Preferred Accountability Cadence** (Field 11), selecting from Weekly check-in (30 minutes), Bi-weekly check-in, Monthly summary email, or Self-updates only.
11. If you have a **Preferred Regular Check-in Time** (Field 12), specify it in the format "Local time (HH:MM)".
12. If necessary, list **Interim Indicators of Progress** (Field 13) to help track your progress, even though it's not required.
13. Finally, add any **Additional Notes or Context** (Field 14) for your coach to understand your situation better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client or Participant Name (Field 2)**: Enter your name to be associated with this 90-day action plan.
* **Plan Start Date (Field 3)**: Set the date when you plan to start working on your 90-day action plan.
* **Primary 90-Day Goal (Field 4)**: Describe your main objective, ensuring it is specific, measurable, and achievable within the 90-day timeframe.
* **Priority Level (Field 5)**: Choose the priority level for your goal: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Success will be Measured (Field 6)**: Specify how your progress and success will be evaluated using metrics or KPIs.
* **Milestone 1 (Field 7)**: Describe the first significant objective or task you want to complete within the 90-day period.
* **Milestone 2 (Field 8)**: If applicable, describe the second significant objective or task you want to achieve within the 90-day period.
* **Anticipated Obstacles (Field 9)**: Identify any challenges or roadblocks you expect to face during the 90-day period.
* **Support or Resources Needed (Field 10)**: Indicate any assistance or tools you need to overcome obstacles or achieve your goals.
* **Preferred Accountability Cadence (Field 11)**: Choose how frequently you prefer to communicate with your coach or advisor about your progress.
* **Preferred Regular Check-in Time (Field 12)**: If you prefer a regular check-in schedule, specify the local time in the format "HH:MM".
* **Interim Indicators of Progress (Field 13)**: If necessary, list short signs or indicators that your progress is on track.
* **Additional Notes or Context (Field 14)**: Add any additional information or context that your coach should know about your situation.

## Tips
- Be specific and concrete in your answers, ensuring clarity for your coach and the effectiveness of your 90-day action plan.
- Use the provided fields to provide a comprehensive overview of your goals, challenges, and needs, ensuring your coach understands your situation fully.
- Don't hesitate to add or modify fields as per your requirements for the most personalized coaching experience.
