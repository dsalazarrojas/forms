# foundational_coaching_program_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to register for the foundational coaching program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the registration form with your personal details.
2. Select the program you are interested in.
3. Provide any additional notes or comments.
4. Select your program type and program status.
5. Fill out the payment terms and coach rating.
6. Complete the program completion status and program status fields.
7. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
### **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
### **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
### **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
### **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
### **Program Selection** (`program_selection`, select_one, optional): Select the program you are interested in (Coach, Coach 2, Coach 3).
### **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
### **Date of Program Completion** (`date_of_program_completion`, date, optional): Enter the date of program completion.
### **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments.
### **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the program type (Program A, Program B, Program C).
### **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, number, optional): Enter your payment terms.
### **Coach Rating** (`coach_rating`, number, optional): Enter your coach rating.
### **Program Rating** (`program_rating`, number, optional): Enter your program rating.
### **Program Completion Status** (`program_completion_status`, select_one, optional): Select the program completion status (Completed, Not Completed).
### **Program Status** (`program_status`, select_one, optional): Select the program status (Active, Inactive, Completed).

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
- Make sure to review your submission before submitting.
- If you need any help, contact the program administrators.
