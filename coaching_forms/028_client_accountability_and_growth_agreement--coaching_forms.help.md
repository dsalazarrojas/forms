# Client Accountability And Growth Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a mutual understanding between the client and coach to define the terms of their coaching relationship. It helps establish clear expectations, boundaries, and a growth plan that will guide the coaching sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Client Full Name** (required).
2. Provide the **Coach/Consultant Name** (required) of your coach or consultant.
3. Select an **Effective Date** for the agreement (required).
4. Identify your **Primary Goal** and define specific areas of growth and focus (required).
5. Indicate how you will measure your **growth and success** with specific indicators (required).
6. Specify your **weekly time commitment** for coaching activities (required).
7. Choose your **preferred check-in frequency** for progress tracking (required).
8. Select your **accountability style** to best fit your needs (required).
9. List any **potential barriers or distractions** that may affect your progress (optional).
10. Acknowledge and agree to the **terms of confidentiality** for coaching sessions (required).
11. Take ownership of your **results and actions** and acknowledge that you are responsible for them (required).
12. Agree to provide **24-hour notice** for any session rescheduling (required).
13. Sign with a **digital signature** to confirm your understanding of the agreement (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to establish your identity in the coaching relationship.
* **Coach/Consultant Name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your coach or consultant.
* **Effective Date** (`agreement_start_date`, date, required): Select the date when the agreement comes into effect.
* **Primary Goal** (`primary_goal_1`, text, required): Describe your main outcome or target for the coaching sessions.
* **How will we measure your growth and success?** (`growth_metrics`, text, required): Specify the indicators you will use to measure your progress.
* **Weekly time commitment for growth activities** (`time_commitment`, number, required): Indicate the time allocated for coaching activities each week.
* **Preferred Check-in Frequency** (`check_in_frequency`, select_one, required): Select your preferred frequency for progress tracking.
* **Accountability Style** (`accountability_preference`, select_one, required): Choose a style that best suits your needs.
* **Potential barriers or distractions** (`barriers_to_growth`, text, optional): List any obstacles that may affect your progress.
* **Terms of Agreement** (`coaching_terms_header`, note, required): This section outlines the terms of the coaching agreement.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm your understanding of confidential coaching sessions.
* **Ownership of Results** (`ownership_of_results`, select_one, required): Acknowledge responsibility for your actions and results.
* **Reschedule Policy** (`reschedule_policy`, select_one, required): Agree to provide 24-hour notice for session rescheduling.
* **Digital Signature** (`electronic_signature`, text, required): Sign with a digital signature to confirm your understanding of the agreement.

Note: Please review each field carefully and ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
