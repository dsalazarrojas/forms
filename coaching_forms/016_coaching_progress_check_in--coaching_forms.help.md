### Coaching Progress Check In - Help Guide

### Purpose

The "Coaching Progress Check In" form is designed to facilitate a regular progress check-in between you and your coach. This form helps you track your progress, set goals, and identify areas where you need support. It's an essential part of the coaching process, ensuring you and your coach are on the same page and working towards achieving your goals.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  **Client Name**: Enter your name to identify yourself as the client.
2.  **Coach Name**: Enter your coach's name, whom you are working with.
3.  **Check-In Date**: Select the date of the current coaching session.
4.  **Date of Last Coaching Session**: If applicable, enter the date of your previous coaching session.
5.  **Overall Progress Since Last Check-In**: Choose how much progress you've made since the last check-in.
6.  **Goals Achieved Since Last Session**: Describe any progress you've made towards your goals.
7.  **Challenges Encountered**: Share any obstacles or challenges you faced.
8.  **Progress Rating on Primary Goal**: Rate your progress on the most important goal.
9.  **Confidence Level Moving Forward**: Rate your confidence level in achieving your goals.
10.  **Action Items Completed from Last Session**: List what you accomplished since the last session.
11.  **New Action Items**: List the actions you plan to take next.
12.  **Timeline for Next Goals**: Describe when you plan to achieve your next goals.
13.  **Support or Resources Needed**: If you need help, specify what kind of support or resources you require.
14.  **Coaching Adjustments Needed**: If needed, suggest changes to the coaching approach.
15.  **Overall Satisfaction with Coaching**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the coaching process.
16.  **Scheduled Next Session Date**: If applicable, enter the date of your next coaching session.
