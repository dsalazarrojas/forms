# Private Coaching Session Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to sign up for private coaching sessions with our coaching service. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure you don't miss any important details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Session Details section with any additional information about the session you would like to schedule.
2. Enter the Client Name and Client Email to ensure we can contact you about your session.
3. Provide your Client Phone number so we can reach you to confirm the session details.
4. Select a Preferred Session Time that suits you best.
5. Choose a Session Date for your coaching session.
6. Describe your Goals for the coaching session.
7. Confirm your Coaching Agreement to ensure you are committed to the session.
8. Choose the type(s) of coaching you would like to receive (Life, Career, or Relationship).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details**: 
  * This is the section where you can add any additional information about the session you want to schedule.
  * Please use this space to note any specific topics or areas you would like to focus on during the session.
* **Client Name**: 
  * This is the name of the client (you) booking the session.
  * Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Client Email**: 
  * This is the email address we will use to contact you about your session details and reminders.
  * Please ensure it's a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Client Phone**: 
  * This is the phone number we will use to reach you to confirm the session details.
  * Please enter a number that we can contact you with.
* **Preferred Session Time**: 
  * This is the time slot you prefer for your coaching session.
  * Please choose a time that suits you best, and we will do our best to accommodate it.
* **Session Date**: 
  * This is the date you would like to schedule your coaching session.
  * Please enter a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 02/22/2024).
* **Goals**: 
  * This is the area of focus you would like to cover during the coaching session.
  * Please describe your goals and objectives for the session in brief.
* **Coaching Agreement**: 
  * This is a confirmation that you are committed to the coaching session.
  * Please select 'Yes' to confirm your commitment.
* **Coaching Type**: 
  * This is the type of coaching you would like to receive.
  * Please select one or more of the options (Life, Career, Relationship) to indicate your preference.
