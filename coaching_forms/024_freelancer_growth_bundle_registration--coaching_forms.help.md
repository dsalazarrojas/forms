# Freelancer Growth Bundle Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Freelancer Growth Bundle Registration" form is designed to collect information from freelancers about their growth bundles and program owners. This form will help identify relevant information to aid in the development and improvement of growth bundles and programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form in the "Form Title" field.
2. Fill in your first and last name in the respective fields.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. State your goals in the "Goals" field.
5. Select your experience level from the options provided in the "Experience Level" field.
6. Choose your preferred growth bundles from the options provided in the "Growth Bundle" field.
7. Choose your program owner from the options provided in the "Program Owner" field.
8. (Optional) Add any additional notes about your registration in the "Notes" field.
9. If you are completing the form at a later date, select the date and time you finished in the "Form Completion Date" and "Form Completion Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a title for your registration that will help us identify your submission.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, required): Describe your goals and objectives for using the growth bundle and program.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Select your experience level from the options provided:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-2 years
	+ 2-5 years
	+ More than 5 years
* **Growth Bundle** (`growth_bundle`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more growth bundles that interest you:
	+ Bundle 1
	+ Bundle 2
	+ Bundle 3
	+ Bundle 4
* **Program Owner** (`program_owner`, select_one, required): Choose the program owner that offers the growth bundle you are interested in:
	+ Owner 1
	+ Owner 2
	+ Owner 3
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Form Completion Date** (`form_completion_date`, date, optional): If you are completing the form at a later date, select the date you finished.
* **Form Completion Time** (`form_completion_time`, time, optional): If you are completing the form at a later date, select the time you finished.

## Tips
Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date. If you are completing the form at a later date, ensure that the date and time are correct.
