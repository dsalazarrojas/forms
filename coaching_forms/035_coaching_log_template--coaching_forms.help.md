# Coaching Log Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This template provides a structured way for coaches to record and reflect on their coaching sessions, ensuring accurate and consistent records.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, using the following steps:
   1. Enter the Coach Name and Client Name.
   2. Select the Session Date and Time.
   3. Choose the Session Duration and Format.
   4. Enter the Client's Mood, Engagement Level, and Progress Made.
   5. Describe the topics covered, goals addressed, breakthroughs, and challenges discussed.
   6. List the coaching techniques used and homework assigned.
   7. Note any breakthroughs, risk or safety concerns, and resources provided.
   8. Determine if follow-up is needed and specify the next session date and goals.
   9. Rate the session effectiveness.
   10. Complete and sign off the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coach Name**: Enter the name of the coach conducting the session.
* **Coach Email**: Enter the email address of the coach.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client being coached.
* **Client ID Number**: If applicable, enter the client's ID number.
* **Session Date**: Enter the date of the coaching session.
* **Session Time**: Enter the start time of the coaching session.
* **Session Duration**: Select the duration of the coaching session (30, 45, 60, 90, or 120 minutes).
* **Session Number**: Select the number of the current coaching session (1-7 or more).
* **Session Format**: Select the format of the coaching session (In Person, Video Call, or Phone Call).
* **Session Location**: Enter the location where the coaching session took place.
* **Topics Covered**: Describe the topics discussed during the session.
* **Goals Addressed**: List the goals addressed during the session.
* **Breakthroughs**: Note any significant insights or breakthroughs made during the session.
* **Challenges Discussed**: Describe any challenges addressed during the session.
* **Coaching Techniques Used**: List the coaching techniques used during the session.
* **Homework Assigned**: Enter any homework or actions assigned to the client.
* **Client Commitments**: Describe the commitments the client made.
* **Follow-Up Needed**: Indicate if follow-up is needed.
* **Follow-Up Details**: Describe the follow-up actions.
* **Resources Provided**: List any resources provided to the client.
* **Next Session Date**: Enter the date of the next coaching session.
* **Next Session Goals**: Enter the goals for the next coaching session.
* **Coach Notes and Observations**: Enter any professional observations or notes.
* **Risk Concerns**: Note any risk or safety concerns.
* **Session Effectiveness**: Rate the effectiveness of the session (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Log Completed Date**: Enter the date the form was completed.

## Tips

* Please ensure accuracy and consistency when completing this form.
* Review the form regularly for any necessary updates or revisions.
* Use this form for every coaching session to maintain accurate records.
* Keep sensitive or confidential information secure.
