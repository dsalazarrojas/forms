# Coaching Service Guidelines Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect coaching service guidelines from coaches, which will be used to showcase on the platform for client selection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each field with accurate and thorough information about your coaching services.
2. Review each section carefully to ensure that you have provided all necessary information.
3. Ensure that you have reviewed the provided guidelines and that your submissions are accurate and complete.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Coach Name
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.

### Coach Email
* **Coach Email** (`coach_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.

### Coach Phone Number
* **Coach Phone Number** (`coach_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact and scheduling purposes.

### Business Name
* **Coaching Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your coaching business name, if applicable.

### Session Length
* **Standard Session Length** (`session_length`, `number`, required): Enter the standard length of each coaching session in minutes.

### Session Format
* **Session Format** (`session_format`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all formats in which you offer coaching sessions (in-person, phone, video call, email).

### Session Frequency Options
* **Available Session Frequency Options** (`session_frequency_options`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all frequencies at which you offer coaching sessions (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, custom schedule).

### Cancellation Policy
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, `text`, required): Describe your cancellation policy and terms.

### Session Rate
* **Session Rate** (`session_rate`, `number`, required): Enter the rate per hour or per session of your coaching services.

### Package Pricing
* **Package Pricing Options** (`package_pricing`, `text`, optional): Describe any package pricing options you offer.

### Payment Methods Accepted
* **Payment Methods Accepted** (`payment_methods`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all payment methods you accept (credit card, bank transfer, check, PayPal, cash, other).

### Payment Schedule
* **Payment Schedule** (`payment_schedule`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment schedule you use (per session, monthly, upfront for package, flexible).

### Certifications
* **Relevant Certifications** (`certifications`, `text`, optional): List any relevant certifications you hold.

### Years of Experience
* **Years of Coaching Experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the years of coaching experience you have.

### Coaching Specializations
* **Coaching Specializations** (`specializations`, `text`, optional): List areas of expertise you have as a coach.

### Client Expectations
* **Client Expectations** (`client_expectations`, `text`, required): Describe your expectations from clients.

### Confidentiality and Privacy Notice
* **Confidentiality and Privacy Notice** (`confidentiality_notice`, `text`, required): Describe your approach to client privacy.

### Guidelines Date
* **Guidelines Date** (`form_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date these guidelines were created or last updated.

Please note that this form is designed to be filled out once for each coach. Duplicate submissions will be automatically flagged and removed from the platform. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
