# Social Media Coaching Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and social media information from users to provide tailored coaching services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in a new tab or window to avoid losing your progress.
2. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
4. Enter your Email in the Email field.
5. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
6. Select all the Social Media Platforms you are active on in the Social Media Platforms field.
7. Choose your Coaching Level in the Coaching Level field.
8. Describe how you know us in the How do you know us? field.
9. Enter your goal for using our service in the What is your goal? field.
10. Provide a brief description of yourself in the Tell us a little about yourself field.
11. If desired, add any additional comments in the Any additional comments? field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_input`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you use and check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number that you can be reached at.
* **Social Media Platforms** (`social_media_platforms`, select_multiple, required): Select all the social media platforms you are active on (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, etc.).
* **Coaching Level** (`coaching_level`, select_one, required): Choose your level of coaching experience (High, Low).
* **How do you know us?** (`experience`, text, required): Describe how you know us, such as through a previous coaching session or by referral.
* **What is your goal?** (`what_is_your_goal`, text, required): Enter your goal for using our coaching service.
* **Tell us a little about yourself** (`about_yourself`, note, required): Provide a brief description of yourself and your goals.
* **Any additional comments?** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have about our coaching services.
