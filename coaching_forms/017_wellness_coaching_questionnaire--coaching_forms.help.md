# Wellness Coaching Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wellness Coaching Questionnaire is a comprehensive form designed to gather essential information about you, the client. This form is meant to be completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the coach can provide the best possible support and guidance throughout the coaching process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and any other personal details in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field using the correct date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
3. For the "Exercise Frequency" field, select one of the options provided (High or Low) to indicate your current exercise frequency.
4. Enter your sleep duration in the "Sleep Duration" field using the correct time format (e.g., HH:MM).
5. For the "Medications and Allergies" field, list any medications you are currently taking and any allergies you have.
6. In the "Medical Conditions" field, list any medical conditions you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
7. In the "Medical History" field, provide a brief description of your medical history.
8. For the "Emergency Contact" field, provide the name and phone number of a trusted emergency contact.
9. For the "Emergency Phone" field, enter the phone number of a trusted emergency contact.
10. For the "Alternate Emergency Phone" field, enter an alternate phone number for the same emergency contact.
11. Finally, fill in the address fields (Address 1 and Address 2) with the address where you can be reached for coaching sessions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth using the correct date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **height** (`height`, number, required/optional): Enter your height in inches.
* **weight** (`weight`, number, required/optional): Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the options provided (High or Low) to indicate your current exercise frequency.
* **Sleep Duration** (`sleep_duration`, time, required/optional): Enter your sleep duration in hours and minutes (e.g., 08:00).
* **Medications and Allergies** (`medications_allergies`, text, required/optional): List any medications you are currently taking and any allergies you have.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required/optional): List any medical conditions you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your medical history.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of a trusted emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of a trusted emergency contact.
* **Alternate Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone_alternate`, text, required/optional): Enter an alternate phone number for the same emergency contact.
* **Address 1** (`address_1`, text, required/optional): Enter the address where you can be reached for coaching sessions.
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional address information (e.g., apartment number, floor).
