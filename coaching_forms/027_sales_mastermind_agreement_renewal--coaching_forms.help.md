# Sales Mastermind Agreement Renewal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Mastermind Agreement Renewal form is used to confirm participants' understandings and preferences for the sales mastermind agreement, which is a crucial part of the coaching process. This form ensures that all parties involved understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Confirm your participation in the sales mastermind program by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the participant confirmations fields.
2. Choose your preferred terms of the agreement by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the term preferences fields.
3. Sign the agreement electronically using the e_signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Confirmations** (`participant_confirmations`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field asks you to confirm your participation in the sales mastermind program. Select 'Yes' to confirm your participation or 'No' if you do not wish to participate.
* **Term Preferences** (`term_preferences`, `select_one`, optional): This field asks you to choose your preferred terms for the agreement. Select 'Yes' for the terms you agree to or 'No' for those you do not.
* **E-Signature** (`e_signature`, `text`, optional): This is where you sign the agreement electronically. Type your signature to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Participant Confirmations 2** (`participant_confirmations_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is another set of participant confirmations. Select 'Yes' to confirm your participation or 'No' if you do not wish to participate.
* **Term Preferences 2** (`term_preferences_2`, `select_one`, optional): This field is another set of term preferences. Select 'Yes' for the terms you agree to or 'No' for those you do not.
