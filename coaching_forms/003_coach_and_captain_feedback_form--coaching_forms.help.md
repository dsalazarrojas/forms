# Coach And Captain Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from team members about their performance, communication, and overall satisfaction with the team dynamic.

## How To Complete This Form
1. On the first page (section_eval), provide a brief performance evaluation of the team.
2. On the subsequent pages, fill out the required fields:
	* **Athlete Name**: Optional - Enter the name of the athlete providing feedback (leave blank for anonymous feedback).
	* **Team Name**: A required field where you enter the name of the sports team or club.
	* **Coach Name**: A required field where you enter the name of the coach you are evaluating.
	* **Coach's Performance (Rating)**: Rate the coach's effectiveness (1-5).
	* **Communication Effectiveness**: Rate the coach's communication (1-5).
	* **Captain Name**: A required field for the team captain's name.
	* **Captain's Leadership (Rating)**: Rate the captain's leadership (1-5).
	* **Team Dynamic Satisfaction**: Rate the team's overall atmosphere (1-5).
	* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Optional - Provide any suggestions for improvement.
	* **Positive Highlights**: Optional - Share any positive highlights from the season.
	* **Additional Comments**: Optional - Share any other thoughts or comments.
