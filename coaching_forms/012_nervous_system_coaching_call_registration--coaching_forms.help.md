# nervous-system-coaching-call-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for clients to register for a coaching call with a coach. It collects information such as coaching call scheduling preferences, client goals, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the desired coaching call duration from the "Coaching Call Scheduling" dropdown menu.
2. Describe your client goals and objectives in the "Client Goals" field.
3. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
4. Specify your session expectations in the "Session Expectations" field.
5. Choose the day of the week for the coaching call from the "Coaching Call Scheduling" dropdown menu.
6. Select an available date for the coaching call in the "Available Date" field.
7. Specify the available time for the coaching call in the "Available Time" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
10. Select the type of coaching you are interested in from the "Coaching Type" dropdown menu.
11. Enter your name as it should appear on our records in the "Client Name" field.
12. Enter your email address in the "Client Email" field.
13. Enter your phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
14. Confirm your phone number in the "Client Phone Confirmed" field.
15. Enter the coach's name as it should appear on our records in the "Coach Name" field.
16. Enter the coach's email address in the "Coach Email" field.
17. Enter the coach's phone number in the "Coach Phone" field.
18. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
19. Select the desired duration for the coaching call from the "Coaching Call Scheduling" dropdown menu.
20. Select an available date for the follow-up coaching call in the "Follow-up Date" field.
21. Specify the available time for the follow-up coaching call in the "Follow-up Time" field.
22. Answer any follow-up questions in the "Follow-up Questions" field.
23. Add any follow-up notes in the "Follow-up Notes" field.
24. Add any session notes in the "Session Notes" field.
25. Specify the next steps to be taken in the "Next Steps" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coaching Call Scheduling** (`coaching_call_scheduling`, select_one): Choose the duration of the coaching call you are interested in (15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 60 minutes).
* **Client Goals** (`client_goals`, text): Describe your goals and objectives.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Session Expectations** (`session_expectations`, text): Specify your expectations for the coaching call.
* **Coaching Call Scheduling** (`coaching_call_scheduling`, select_multiple): Select the day of the week for the coaching call.
* **Available Date** (`available_date`, date): Select an available date for the coaching call.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, time): Specify the available time for the coaching call.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Coaching Type** (`coaching_type`, select_multiple): Select the type of coaching you are interested in (General Wellness, Stress Management, Anxiety Management, or Sleep Coaching).
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text): Enter your name as it should appear on our records.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, text): Enter your email address.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Client Phone Confirmed** (`client_phone_confirmed`, note): Confirm your phone number.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text): Enter the coach's name as it should appear on our records.
* **Coach Email** (`coach_email`, text): Enter the coach's email address.
* **Coach Phone** (`coach_phone`, text): Enter the coach's phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Add any additional notes.
* **Coaching Call Schedule** (`coaching_call_schedule`, select_multiple): Select the duration of the coaching call (15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 60 minutes).
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date): Select an available date for the follow-up coaching call.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time): Specify the available time for the follow-up coaching call.
* **Follow-up Questions** (`follow_up_questions`, text): Answer any follow-up questions.
* **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, text): Add any follow-up notes.
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, text): Add any session notes.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text): Specify the next steps to be taken.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your availability and preferences before submitting the form.
* Ensure that your contact details are accurate and up-to-date.
