# grant-proposal-coaching-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for registering participants for grant proposal coaching services. Please fill out the form accurately to help us understand your needs and provide the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic details, including your name and email address.
2. Specify your scheduling preferences, including date and time that suits you best.
3. Share your goals and objectives for the grant proposal coaching service.
4. Provide your contact information, including phone number and physical address.
5. Choose a date and time for the coaching session that works for you.
6. If you need grant writing support, specify the level of support you require.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name to confirm your identity and help us understand your background.
* **Scheduling Preferences** (`scheduling_preferences`, `text`, required: false): Specify the dates and times when you are available for the coaching session. Please format the date and time as per your regional standards (e.g., MM/DD/YY HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Goals** (`goals_and_goals`, `text`, required: false): Describe your goals and objectives for the grant proposal coaching service. Be as specific as possible so we can better understand your needs.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false): Provide your name, phone number, and physical address to enable easy communication.
* **Scheduling Date and Time** (`scheduling_date_time`, `text`, required: false): Choose a date and time for the coaching session that fits your schedule. Be sure to format it as per your regional standards (e.g., MM/DD/YY HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Grant Writing Support Needs** (`grant_writing_support_needs`, `text`, required: false): If you need additional support with grant writing, specify the level of assistance you require, such as research, drafting, or editing.
* **Scheduling Availability** (`scheduling_availability`, `text`, required: false): Specify when you are available for the coaching session to accommodate our scheduling. 

## Tips
