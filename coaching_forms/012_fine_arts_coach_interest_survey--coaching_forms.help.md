# fine arts coach interest survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your interest and fit for our coaching program. It's used to evaluate your suitability as a potential coach and to gather information about your preferences and availability.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Read each question carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Make sure to select only one option for each field that best represents your preferences.
3. If you have any additional information that you'd like to share, you can do so in the "Additional Contact Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **What do you think is the best use of a coach?** (1)
- **Introduction** (Text, Optional): This question is used to help us understand your perspective on how a coach should be used in our programs. Please type a brief description of what you think is the best use of a coach.

### **What level of experience do you have with coaching?** (2)
- **Experience Level** (Select One, Optional):
  - Beginner: You have little to no experience with coaching.
  - Intermediate: You have some experience with coaching.
  - Advanced: You have extensive experience with coaching.
  - No Experience: You have no experience with coaching.

### **What type of program are you interested in?** (3)
- **Program Type** (Select Multiple, Optional):
  - Coaching: You are interested in coaching programs.
  - Mentoring: You are interested in mentoring programs.
  - Both: You are interested in both coaching and mentoring programs.

### **Which art form do you have experience in?** (4)
- **Art Form** (Select Multiple, Optional):
  - Music: You have experience in music arts.
  - Visual: You have experience in visual arts.
  - Dance: You have experience in dance arts.
  - Theater: You have experience in theater arts.

### **What is your preferred language to communicate?** (5)
- **Preferred Language** (Select One, Optional):
  - English: You prefer to communicate in English.
  - French: You prefer to communicate in French.
  - Spanish: You prefer to communicate in Spanish.

### **When can you start the program?** (6)
- **Availability** (Date, Optional): This question is used to determine when you can start the program. Please select the earliest date you can start.

### **Which time slot do you prefer?** (7)
- **Preferred Time** (Select One, Optional):
  - Morning: You prefer morning time slots.
  - Afternoon: You prefer afternoon time slots.
  - Evening: You prefer evening time slots.
  - Any: You have no preference for time slots.

### **Do you have any additional contact information?** (8)
- **Additional Contact Information** (Text, Optional): If you have any additional contact information that you'd like to share, please type it here.

### **Are you interested in other programs offered by our organization?** (9)
- **Other Programs** (Select One, Optional):
  - True: You are interested in other programs offered by our organization.
  - False: You are not interested in other programs offered by our organization.

### **How would you like to be contacted for follow-up questions?** (10)
- **Follow-up Contact** (Select One, Optional):
  - Email: You'd like to be contacted via email.
  - Phone: You'd like to be contacted via phone.
  - Chat: You'd like to be contacted via chat.
  - Text: You'd like to be contacted via text.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We'll review your application and get back to you soon.
