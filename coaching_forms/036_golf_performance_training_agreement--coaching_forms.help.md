# golf_performance_training_agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The golf performance training agreement form is used to gather essential information from clients for coaching performance training programs. It collects details about the client, coach, and program specifics, as well as terms of service and signing information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the required information to complete the form accurately.
2. Enter the client's details, including name, address, contact email, and phone number.
3. Provide the coach's information, including name and license status.
4. Specify the coaching services offered and the program start and end dates.
5. Enter the program duration and fees.
6. Select a payment plan and provide any additional payment details.
7. Review and agree to the terms of service.
8. Sign and date the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (1)**: Enter your name as the client.
* **Client Address (2)**: Enter your address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Person (3)**: Enter the name of the person to contact for the program.
* **Contact Email (4)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (5)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Coach Information (6)**: Enter the name of the coach.
* **Coach License (7)**: Indicate if the coach has a license or not.
* **Coaching Services (8)**: Specify the services offered by the coach.
* **Services Offered (9)**: Select which services are being offered.
* **Program Start Date (10)**: Enter the start date of the program.
* **Program End Date (11)**: Enter the end date of the program.
* **Program Duration (12)**: Enter the duration of the program in numbers.
* **Program Fees (13)**: Enter the fees for the program.
* **Payment Plan (14)**: Select a payment plan.
* **Payment Details (15)**: Provide any additional payment details.
* **Terms of Service (16)**: Review and agree to the terms of service.
* **Agreements Signed (17)**: Indicate if you have signed the agreement.
* **Client Signature (18)**: Sign as the client, indicating agreement.
* **Coach Signature (19)**: Sign as the coach, indicating agreement.
* **Date of Signing (20)**: Date and time of signing.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the terms of service before signing.
* Keep a copy of the signed agreement for your records.
