# performance_coaching_training_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The performance coaching training form is a guide for participants and coaches to collect and document necessary information for a coaching session.

## How To Complete This Form

This form is divided into two parts: participant information and training details. Please fill in the required fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Participant Information** (Form Participant Information, text, required/optional): Provide your name, email, and phone number to ensure we can reach you with any updates or information about the coaching session.
* **Participant First Name** (Participant First Name, text, required): Enter your first name as it is spelled on your ID or government-issued identification.
* **Participant Last Name** (Participant Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name as it is spelled on your ID or government-issued identification.
* **Participant Email** (Participant Email, email, required): Enter your valid email address for communication and updates.
* **Participant Phone** (Participant Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for updates or additional information.
* **Training Goals** (Training Goals, text, optional): Describe your specific goals for the coaching session, including what you hope to achieve or improve.
* **Training Objectives** (Training Objectives, text, optional): Specify the key objectives or outcomes you expect from the coaching session.
* **Training Plan** (Training Plan, text, optional): Outline your proposed plan for achieving your training goals, including specific steps or actions you will take.
* **Training Schedule** (Training Schedule, date, optional): Provide your preferred dates or timeframe for the coaching session.
* **Training Coach** (Training Coach, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you will be working with a coach or not.
* **Participant Agreement** (Participant Agreement, select_multiple, optional): Confirm whether you agree or do not agree to participate in the coaching session.
* **Coach Agreement** (Coach Agreement, select_multiple, optional): Confirm whether you agree or do not agree to participate in the coaching session.
* **Participant Additional Comments** (Participant Additional Comments, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information you would like to share with the coach.
* **Coach Additional Comments** (Coach Additional Comments, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information you would like to share with the participant.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be filled out to complete the form. Optional fields are for your reference and can help the coach better understand your needs and goals.
