This help guide aims to provide an overview of the Youth Sports Tryout Evaluation form, its purpose, and how to complete it, along with a detailed explanation of each field.

## Purpose
The Youth Sports Tryout Evaluation form is designed to collect information and evaluate a player's performance during a tryout for a youth sports team. The form is used to assess the player's skills, abilities, and overall fit for the team. It helps coaches and evaluators provide feedback and score the player's performance during the tryout.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information about the player's tryout performance.
2. Review and understand each field's purpose and requirements before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Below is a detailed explanation of each field in the form:

* **Player Info (1)**: Enter the player's name and any other relevant information.
* **Tryout Score (2)**: Score the player's performance out of 10, indicating their overall fit for the team.
* **Comments (3)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the player's performance.
* **Date (4)**: Enter the date of the tryout.
* **Time (5)**: Enter the time of the tryout.
* **Coach Name (6)**: Enter your name and title as the coach evaluating the player.
* **Score (7)**: Score the player's performance out of 10, indicating their overall fit for the team. 
* **Tryout Date (8)**: Enter the date of the tryout.
* **Tryout Time (9)**: Enter the time of the tryout.
* **Evaluation Comments (10)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the player's performance.
* **Evaluation Score (11)**: Score the player's performance out of 10, indicating their overall fit for the team.
* **Tryout Type (12)**: Select the sport the player tried out for.
* **Sport (13)**: Select all sports the player is interested in or skilled in.
* **Tryout Details (14)**: Add any additional information about the tryout.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and honestly.
