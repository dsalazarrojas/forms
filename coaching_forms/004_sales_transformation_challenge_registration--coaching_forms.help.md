# Sales Transformation Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Transformation Challenge Registration form is a tool used to gather information about your sales program and goals. It helps us understand your sales team's progress and challenges, allowing us to provide tailored support and guidance to help you achieve your sales targets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your role from the options provided under "Sales Leaders" or "Coaching Coaches".
2. Write a brief description of your sales goals in the "Sales Goals" field.
3. Choose the sales program or challenge that you're interested in from the "Sales Programs and Challenges" dropdown menu.
4. Provide your sales coach's name and email address.
5. Enter your participant details, including your name, email address, phone number, and job title (if applicable).
6. Select your participant manager's name and email address.
7. Enter the start and end dates of your sales program, if applicable.
8. Choose the challenge start and end dates, which are essential for tracking progress.
9. Finally, click the submit button to complete the form.
