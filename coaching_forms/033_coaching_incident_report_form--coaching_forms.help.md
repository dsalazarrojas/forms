# Coaching Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report any incidents that occurred during coaching sessions. It is essential to report incidents promptly to ensure that we can take immediate action and prevent similar incidents from happening again in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Ensure that you are reporting the incident as soon as possible after it happens.
3.  Be honest and thorough in your reporting.
4.  If you are unsure about any part of the form, do not hesitate to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name** (`reporter_name`): Your name and contact information will be used for follow-up and communication purposes.
    *   This is the name of the person reporting the incident.
    *   Enter your name as it appears on your profile.
*   **Your Role** (`reporter_role`): Please select your role in the incident.
    *   Choose from the options: Coach, Client, Witness, Staff Member, or Other.
*   **Email Address** (`reporter_email`): Your email address will be used for follow-up and communication purposes.
    *   Enter your work email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`reporter_phone`): Your phone number will be used for follow-up and communication purposes.
    *   Enter your work phone number (optional).
*   **Date of Incident** (`incident_date`): The date the incident occurred.
    *   Select the date the incident happened.
*   **Time of Incident** (`incident_time`): The time the incident occurred.
    *   Enter the time the incident happened.
*   **Location of Incident** (`incident_location`): Where the incident occurred.
    *   Enter the location of the incident.
*   **Type of Session** (`session_type`): The type of session where the incident occurred.
    *   Select from the options: Individual Coaching, Group Coaching, Team Coaching, Workshop, Assessment, or Other.
*   **Coach Name** (`coach_name`): The name of the coach involved in the incident.
    *   Enter the name of the coach.
*   **Clients Involved** (`clients_involved`): The names of the clients involved in the incident.
    *   List the names of the clients involved (optional).
*   **Type of Incident** (`incident_type`): The category that best describes the incident.
    *   Select from the options: Physical Injury, Emotional Distress, Property Damage, Safety Violation, Policy Violation, Conflict Between Participants, Medical Emergency, Harassment or Discrimination, or Other.
*   **Detailed Description of Incident** (`incident_description`): A detailed description of what happened.
    *   Describe the incident as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
*   **What Led to This Incident** (`incident_cause`): Describe the contributing factors to the incident.
    *   Enter any information that may have contributed to the incident (optional).
*   **Were There Witnesses** (`witnesses_present`): If others were present during the incident.
    *   Select whether there were witnesses (True or False).
*   **Witness Names** (`witness_names`): The names of the witnesses.
    *   List the names of the witnesses (if applicable).
*   **Witness Contact Information** (`witness_contact`): Information on how to reach the witnesses.
    *   Enter contact information for the witnesses (if applicable).
*   **Were There Any Injuries** (`injuries_sustained`): If there were injuries sustained during the incident.
    *   Select whether there were injuries (No Injuries, Minor Injury, Moderate Injury, or Serious Injury).
*   **Injury Details** (`injury_details`): Describe the injuries sustained.
    *   Enter a description of the injuries (if applicable).
*   **Immediate Action Taken** (`immediate_action_taken`): The action taken immediately after the incident.
    *   Enter the action taken after the incident (e.g., calling for help, providing first aid).
*   **Was Medical Attention Required** (`medical_attention`): If anyone needed medical attention after the incident.
    *   Select whether medical attention was required (False, Yes First Aid Only, Yes Professional Medical Care, Yes Emergency Services Called).
*   **Medical Attention Details** (`medical_attention_details`): A description of the medical attention received.
    *   Enter details about the medical attention provided.
*   **Who Was Notified** (`notifications_made`): A list of people who were notified about the incident.
    *   Enter a list of people or teams that were informed about the incident (if applicable).
*   **Is Follow-Up Required** (`follow_up_required`): Whether follow-up is needed after the incident.
    *   Select whether follow-up is required (True, False, or Unsure).
*   **Recommended Follow-Up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`): Suggestions for what should be done next.
    *   Enter suggestions or recommendations for follow-up actions (if applicable).
*   **Could This Have Been Prevented** (`preventability`): Whether the incident was preventable.
    *   Select whether the incident was preventable (Yes Definitely, Probably, Not Sure, Probably Not, or False).
*   **Suggestions for Prevention** (`prevention_suggestions`): Suggestions for preventing similar incidents in the future.
    *   Enter suggestions or recommendations for preventing similar incidents (if applicable).
*   **Have There Been Similar Incidents** (`previous_similar_incidents`): Whether this is a pattern or not.
    *   Select whether this is a recurring incident (False or True).
*   **Date of Report** (`report_date`): The date the report is being submitted.
    *   Select the current date.
*   **I Confirm This Report Is Accurate** (`signature`): A confirmation that the report is accurate.
    *   Select Yes I Confirm or False.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Any other relevant information.
    *   Enter any other relevant information or comments about the incident (if applicable).
