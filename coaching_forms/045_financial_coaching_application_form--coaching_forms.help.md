# Financial Coaching Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Coaching Application Form is a tool designed to gather detailed information about your financial situation, goals, and challenges. This information will help our financial coaches understand your specific needs and provide personalized guidance and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out each section carefully, providing accurate and detailed information about your financial situation.
2.  Please answer all questions honestly, as this information will remain confidential.
3.  If you're unsure about any field, please ask a financial coach for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Fill in your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Fill in your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your valid email address to receive updates and notifications from our financial coaches.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Provide your phone number for additional contact and follow-up discussions.
* **Income** (Number, Optional): Report your annual income from all sources, such as employment, investments, and other sources.
* **Goals** (Text, Optional): Describe your short-term and long-term financial goals, such as saving for a specific purpose or achieving financial independence.
* **Financial Status** (Select One, Optional): Choose your current financial status from the options provided.
* **Financial Education** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all relevant financial education you've received, such as high school, bachelor's degree, or master's degree.
* **Employment Status** (Select One, Optional): Choose your current employment status from the options provided.
* **Employment Investments** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all relevant employment investments you've made, such as stocks, bonds, or mutual funds.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time of Birth** (Time, Optional): Enter your time of birth in the 24-hour format.
* **Financial Goals** (Text, Optional): Describe your financial goals, such as saving for a specific purpose or achieving financial independence.
* **Financial Challenges** (Text, Optional): Discuss any challenges you're currently facing in achieving your financial goals.
* **Financial History** (Text, Optional): Share your financial history, including income, expenses, and any significant financial events.
* **Employment Income History** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief history of your employment income, including job changes and income variations.
* **Financial Education Background** (Text, Optional): Share your financial education background, including relevant courses, certifications, or training.
* **Employment History** (Text, Optional): Describe your employment history, including job titles, duration, and reason for leaving.
* **Financial Net Worth** (Number, Optional): Report your current financial net worth.
* **Financial Assets** (Text, Optional): List your financial assets, such as investments, savings, or property.
* **Financial Debts** (Text, Optional): List your financial debts, including outstanding loans, credit cards, or mortgages.
* **Financial Credit Score** (Number, Optional): Report your current financial credit score.
* **Financial Liabilities** (Text, Optional): List your financial liabilities, such as outstanding debts or financial commitments.
* **Financial Property** (Text, Optional): Describe your financial property, such as real estate or investments.
* **Financial Incomes** (Text, Optional): List your financial incomes, including regular income and any irregular income sources.
