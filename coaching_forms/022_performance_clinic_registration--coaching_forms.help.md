# Performance Clinic Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Clinic Registration form is designed for participants to register for a performance clinic event. This form collects information about the participant, their preferred clinic time, consent, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your participant details in the "participant_details" section.
2.  Select your preferred clinic time from the "preferred_clinic_time" dropdown menu.
3.  Choose your consent status by selecting "I am 18 or older" or "I am under 18".
4.  Enter your participant name, email, phone number, address, city, and state in the respective fields.
5.  Select your program interest from the "participant_program_interest" dropdown menu.
6.  Choose the clinics you are interested in participating in from the "participant_clinics_1" and "participant_clinics_2" dropdown menus.
