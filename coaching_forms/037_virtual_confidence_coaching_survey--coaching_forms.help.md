# virtual_confidence_coaching_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's self-confidence and self-efficacy in various areas, providing a valuable tool for coaching purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction on page 1.
2. On page 2, select your current coaching level.
3. On pages 3-4, identify which areas you struggle with (select multiple options) and where you feel confident with (select one option).
4. On pages 5-7, rate your confidence levels using the numerical scales provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Survey** (`introduction`, `note`): This page serves as an introduction to the survey.
* **What is your current coaching level?** (`coaching_level`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Please select your current coaching level.
	+ Select "High" if you have a strong coaching background and experience.
	+ Select "Low" if you have limited coaching experience or feel unsure about your abilities.
* **Which areas do you struggle with?** (`challenge_areas`, `select_multiple`, **Optional**): Identify the areas where you feel challenged or uncertain.
	+ Select multiple options that apply to you.
* **Where do you feel confident with?** (`confidence_areas`, `select_one`, **Required**): Indicate where you feel confident.
	+ Select one option that best describes your confidence level.
* **Do you feel confident in taking action?** (`self_efficacy`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Indicate your confidence level in taking action.
	+ Select "True" if you feel confident in taking action.
	+ Select "False" if you feel uncertain or lack confidence.
* **How would you rate your confidence score?** (`confidence_score_1`, `number`, **Required**): Rate your confidence level using the numerical scale provided.
* **What is your overall confidence level?** (`confidence_level`, `number`, **Required**): Rate your overall confidence level using the numerical scale provided.
* **How would you rate your self-confidence?** (`self_confidence`, `number`, **Required**): Rate your self-confidence using the numerical scale provided.
* **Confidence Score 2** (`confidence_score_2`, `number`, **Optional**): Rate your confidence level using the numerical scale provided.
* **What is your confidence level?** (`confidence_level_2`, `number`, **Optional**): Rate your confidence level using the numerical scale provided.
* **Self Confidence 2** (`self_confidence_2`, `number`, **Optional**): Rate your self-confidence using the numerical scale provided.
* **What is your confidence area?** (`confidence_area`, `text`, **Optional**): Provide a brief description of your confidence area.
* **Do you feel confident in your abilities?** (`efficacy`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Indicate your confidence level in your abilities.
	+ Select "True" if you feel confident in your abilities.
	+ Select "False" if you feel uncertain or lack confidence.
* **What is your level of self-efficacy?** (`self_efficacy_level`, `text`, **Optional**): Provide a brief description of your self-efficacy level.
* **How would you rate your confidence in yourself?** (`confidence_self`, `number`, **Required**): Rate your confidence in yourself using the numerical scale provided.
