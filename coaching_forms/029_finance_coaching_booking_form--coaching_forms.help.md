# Finance Coaching Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients or potential clients for coaching services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First name**, **Last name**, and **Email**.
2. Provide your **Phone number**.
3. Select your **Preferred language** for communication.
4. Choose the type of **Coaching session** you would like to book (e.g., Career coaching, Financial coaching, Retirement planning).
5. Select a **Preferred session date** and **Preferred time** for your coaching session.
6. Specify the **Session duration** and **Session time** for the coaching session.
7. Choose the type of **Coaching program** or **Package** you would like to book (e.g., Initial consultation, Coaching program, Coaching package).
8. Select the **Package type** (e.g., Basic, Premium, Elite).
9. Specify the **Package duration**.
10. Choose how often you would like to have **Coaching frequency** (e.g., One session, Multiple sessions, Ongoing support).
11. Select your **Payment method** (e.g., Bank transfer, Credit card, PayPal).
12. Specify the **Payment status** (e.g., Paid, Unpaid, Pending).
13. Add any **Additional notes** regarding your coaching needs.
14. If assigned a coach, fill in the **Assigned coach ID**, **Assigned coach name**, and **Assigned coach email**.
