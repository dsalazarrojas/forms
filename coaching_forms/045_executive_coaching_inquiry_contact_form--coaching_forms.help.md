# Executive Coaching Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for executives interested in exploring coaching services to help them navigate their professional development and improve their leadership skills. By submitting this form, you'll be able to get in touch with a representative for further guidance and information.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the text field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the text field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your email address in the text field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your phone number in the text field labeled "Phone".
5. Enter your company in the text field labeled "Company".
6. Enter your job title in the text field labeled "Job Title".
7. Select the best method of contact for you by choosing from "Phone", "Email", or "Other - Please specify" from the drop-down list labeled "Best Method of Contact".
8. If you select "Other" as your preferred contact method, specify it in the text field labeled "Other Method of Contact".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Your first name for identification purposes.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Your last name for identification purposes.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): The email address you'd like to use to communicate with us.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Your work or personal phone number for contact purposes.
- **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Your current or previous company name for context.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): A brief description of your job title to better understand your context.
- **Best Method of Contact** (`best_method_of_contact`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Other - Please specify).
- **Other Method of Contact** (`other_method_of_contact`, text, required: false): If you choose "Other" as your preferred contact method, specify the alternative contact method in this field. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure that we can better understand and address your inquiry.
- If you have any specific questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us directly.
- We will review and respond to your inquiry according to your selected contact method.
