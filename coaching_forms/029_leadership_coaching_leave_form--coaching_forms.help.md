# leadership-coaching-leave-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "leadership-coaching-leave-form" is used to collect information for coaching sessions in the context of leadership coaching.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the day of the coaching session from the options provided.
2.  Enter the start and end dates of the coaching session.
3.  Choose the type of coaching session (Individual or Group).
4.  Select the relationship of the employee with the coach (manager or peer).
5.  Enter the location of the coaching session.
6.  Provide a reason for leaving.
7.  Enter your name as the employee.
8.  Confirm if your manager has approved the leave.
9.  Set the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Coaching Session** (`coaching_session`, select_one, required): Select the day of the coaching session from the options provided.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the coaching session.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the coaching session.
*   **Coaching Type** (`coaching_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of coaching session (Individual or Group).
*   **Coaching Relationship** (`coaching_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship of the employee with the coach (manager or peer).
*   **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the coaching session.
*   **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, required): Provide a reason for leaving.
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the employee.
*   **Manager OK** (`manager_ok`, select_one, required): Confirm if your manager has approved the leave.
*   **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Set the date of submission.
*   **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Set the time of submission.

## Tips
- Be sure to select the correct day for the coaching session.
- Ensure that you enter the correct start and end dates for the session.
- Choose the correct coaching type and relationship accurately.
- Enter your employee name correctly.
- Confirm your manager's approval before submitting.
- Set the accurate date and time of submission.
