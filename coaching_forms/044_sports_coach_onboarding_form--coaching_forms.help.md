# sports_coach_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential sports coaches onboarding to your platform. It will help us understand your contact and professional details.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, follow these steps:
  1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email fields.
  2. Enter your Phone number, including any required international dialing codes.
  3. If applicable, provide your address for future correspondence.
  4. Select your level of coaching qualification (Coach Level, Certified Coach, Trained Coach, or Other).
  5. Choose the days you are available to coach.
  6. (Optional) Add any additional notes about your availability.
  7. Enter your coaching experience in years.
  8. Briefly describe your educational background.
  9. (Optional) Enter any relevant certifications.
  10. If necessary, add a note about your availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number with the country code.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to be contacted at your work or home address, enter it here.
- **Qualification** (`qualification`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of your coaching qualification:
  - Coach Level: You are a coach with a specific level of coaching experience.
  - Certified Coach: You hold a coaching certification.
  - Trained Coach: You have coaching training or education.
  - Other: Other coaching qualification not listed above.
- **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the days you are available to coach:
  - Monday
  - Tuesday
  - Wednesday
  - Thursday
  - Friday
  - Saturday
  - Sunday
- **Note** (`note`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about your availability.
- **Bio** (`bio`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of yourself and your coaching experience.
- **Experience** (`experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years of coaching experience you have.
- **Education** (`education`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your educational background.
- **Certification** (`certification`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant coaching certifications.
- **Availability Note** (`availability_note`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about your coaching availability.
