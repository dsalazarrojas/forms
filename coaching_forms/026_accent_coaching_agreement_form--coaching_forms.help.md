# Accent Coaching Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Accent Coaching Agreement Form" is used to establish a coaching agreement between you, the client, and your coach. It captures essential details about your coaching program, payment schedule, and terms of service. Please take the time to read and understand each section before filling out the form.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your full name as it appears on your legal identification.
2. Enter your email address for scheduling and communication purposes.
3. Input your primary phone number for contact.
4. Select your chosen coaching program from the provided options.
5. Choose your preferred session format (online, in-person, or hybrid).
6. Enter the total number of sessions you have agreed upon.
7. Select your meeting frequency (weekly, bi-weekly, or twice weekly).
8. Enter the total program investment in USD.
9. Choose your preferred payment schedule.
10. Confirm your understanding of the cancellation policy.
11. Agree to maintain confidentiality of sessions and materials.
12. Sign with your full name to acknowledge your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name of Client** (`client_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your legal identification, including both first and last names.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, Email, Required): Provide your email address for scheduling and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, Text, Required): Enter your primary contact phone number for communication and scheduling.
* **Selected Coaching Program** (`program_type`, Select One, Required): Choose your preferred coaching program from the available options (Standard Accent Modification, Business Communication, Presentation Skills, Narrative and Dialect).
* **Session Format** (`session_format`, Select One, Required): Select the format in which sessions will be conducted (Online, In-person, Hybrid).
* **Total Number of Sessions** (`number_of_sessions`, Number, Required): Enter the total number of sessions included in the coaching package.
* **Frequency** (`frequency_of_sessions`, Select One, Required): Choose how often you would like to meet (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Twice Weekly).
* **Total Program Investment** (`total_investment`, Number, Required): Enter the agreed-upon total investment in USD.
* **Payment Plan** (`payment_plan`, Select One, Required): Select your preferred payment schedule (Full Payment Upfront, Monthly Installments, Pay-per-session).
* **I Agree to Provide at Least 24 Hours Notice for Cancellations** (`cancellation_policy`, Select One, Required): Confirm your understanding and agreement with the cancellation policy.
* **I Understand that All Sessions and Materials are Confidential** (`confidentiality_agreement`, Select One, Required): Agree to maintain confidentiality of sessions and materials.
* **Client Digital Signature** (`digital_signature`, Text, Required): Type your full name to acknowledge your agreement.
* **Date of Signature** (`signing_date`, Date, Required): Enter the current date to signify your agreement.
