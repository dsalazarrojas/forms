# Business Coaching Program Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Coaching Program Admission Form is a tool to help you and our coaching program team get a clear understanding of your current business situation, goals, and needs. Please fill out this form to provide us with accurate and thorough information about your business.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please answer all the questions honestly and thoroughly. You may need to gather some information before starting, so take your time and be as accurate as possible. If you need help or clarification on any question, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Applicant Information
* **-- Applicant Information --**: This is an introduction section that briefly explains the form's purpose.
*   **Full Name**: This is your complete name that we will use to contact you.
*   **Date of Birth**: Please enter your date of birth, this will help us understand your business experience in relation to your age.
*   **Email Address**: This is your primary email address that we will use for communication.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Current Address**: Please enter your residential address.
*   **Do You Currently Own a Business**: Check if you are currently a business owner.
*   **Business Name**: If you are a business owner, enter your business name.
*   **Business Stage**: Choose the current stage of your business.
*   **Industry or Niche**: Enter your business industry or niche.
*   **Annual Revenue**: Enter your current or projected annual revenue.
*   **Years of Business Experience**: Enter your total business experience.
*   **Coaching Program Track**: Choose the program track that suits your needs.
*   **Preferred Program Duration**: Select how long you want the program to last.
*   **Primary Goal for This Program**: Enter what you want to accomplish through the program.
*   **Secondary Goals**: Enter any other objectives you have.
*   **Current Business Struggles**: Describe the challenges you are facing.
*   **Previous Coaching Experience**: Check if you have worked with a coach before.
*   **Commitment Level**: Choose how committed you are to the program.
*   **Time Availability**: Select how many hours you can dedicate to the program per week.
*   **Program Budget**: Choose your budget range for the coaching program.

## Tips

*   Make sure to answer all questions accurately and thoroughly.
*   If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
*   Please provide us with your contact information so we can reach out to you if needed.
*   If you need help or guidance, contact us for support.
