# Breastfeeding Latch Coaching Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us better understand your breastfeeding concerns and concerns so we can provide personalized coaching to support your lactation goals. Please answer the questions honestly, and we'll work together to address any latch issues you may be experiencing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section carefully, making sure to provide as much information as possible.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. If you're unsure about any question, choose "Unsure" as your answer.
4. Please don't leave any sections blank unless explicitly marked as optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mother's Name** (1, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): Enter your email address for appointment confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Preferred Coaching Date** (4, date, required): Choose a date for coaching.
* **Baby's Name** (5, text, optional): Enter your baby's name if you'd like.
* **Baby's Age** (6, select_one, required): Choose your baby's age from the options provided.
* **Primary Latch Concern** (7, text, required): Describe your main latch issue.
* **Latch Problems Experienced** (8, select_multiple, required): Select all latch problems you're experiencing.
* **Current Feeding Frequency** (9, select_one, optional): Choose how often your baby feeds.
* **Breast or Nipple Pain During Feeding** (10, select_one, required): Rate the pain level during feeding.
* **Pain Location** (11, select_multiple, optional): Indicate where you're experiencing pain.
* **Nipple Condition** (12, select_multiple, optional): Describe your current nipple condition.
* **Milk Production Concerns** (13, select_one, optional): Choose any milk supply issues you may have.
* **Baby's Feeding Cues Recognition** (14, select_one, optional): Indicate how well you recognize your baby's hunger cues.
* **Has Baby Been Introduced to Bottle** (15, select_one, optional): Choose your baby's bottle feeding experience.
* **Birth and Delivery Method** (16, select_one, optional): Choose how your baby was born.
* **Any Birth Complications** (17, text, optional): Describe any complications during birth.
* **Previous Coaching or Support** (18, select_one, optional): Choose any previous lactation support you've received.
* **Your Goals for This Session** (19, text, required): Describe your goals for this coaching session.
* **Questions or Concerns** (20, text, optional): Enter any specific questions or concerns you have.

Please fill out this form thoroughly and honestly, and we'll work together to address your latch concerns and achieve your breastfeeding goals!
