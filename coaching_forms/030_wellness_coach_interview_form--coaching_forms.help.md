# wellness_coach_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients about their health and wellness history, medical history, and goals and objectives. It is intended to be used by a wellness coach to understand the client's needs and provide personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. In the "Goals And Objectives" field, describe your goals and objectives for your wellness journey, such as improving physical health, mental well-being, or achieving a specific lifestyle goal.
3. In the "Wellness History" field, provide a brief description of your current wellness history, including any previous experiences with wellness programs or services.
4. If you have a referral from a wellness coach or healthcare professional, please provide the name and contact information of the person who referred you.
5. In the "Medical History" fields, provide a brief description of your medical history, including any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or treatments.
6. In the "Mental Health History" field, provide a brief description of your mental health history, including any previous mental health conditions, therapies, or treatments.
7. In the "Health Risk Factors" field, describe any health risk factors that you are concerned about, such as family medical history, lifestyle habits, or environmental factors.
8. In the "Wellness Coach Info" field, provide any relevant information about your current wellness coaching experience or qualifications.
9. In the "Wellness Coach Experience" field, describe your experience in the wellness industry, including any relevant education, training, or certifications.
