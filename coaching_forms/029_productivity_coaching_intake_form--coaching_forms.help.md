# Productivity Coaching Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a client's productivity needs, goals, and preferences for coaching.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the client information section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your preferred communication method (email, phone, or in-person).
3. Choose your availability for coaching sessions.
4. Enter your work hours.
5. Select your goals for coaching (you can choose multiple goals).
6. Enter your start and end times for coaching sessions.
7. Share any additional notes or comments.
8. Confirm your preferred communication method method (yes or no).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info**: Fill out your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Goals**: Select one or more goals you want to achieve through coaching. This will help your coach understand what you want to focus on.
* **Work Hours**: Enter the hours you work each day or week. This will help your coach understand your schedule.
* **Availability**: Enter your availability for coaching sessions.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time for your coaching sessions.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time for your coaching sessions.
* **Coach Info**: This is a space for any additional information about your coach or coaching preferences.
* **Notes**: Share any additional comments or thoughts you have about your coaching experience.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Preferred Communication**: Select how you prefer to communicate with your coach (email, phone, or in-person).
* **Preferred Communication Method**: Select a method for your coach to contact you (email, phone, or in-person).
* **Preferred Communication Method Method 1/2/3**: Confirm your preferred communication method for each method (yes or no).
