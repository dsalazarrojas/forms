# Agile Coaching Certification Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals applying for Agile Coaching Certification.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Full name" field with your name as it should appear on the certificate.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field to ensure we can contact you about your application.
3. In the "Current role" field, enter your current job title and employer.
4. In the "Years of agile coaching experience" field, enter the total number of years you have been practicing agile coaching.
5. In the "Qualifications" field, list any relevant certifications or education you have completed.
6. In the "Statement of intent" field, enter a brief statement about why you are applying for the certification.
7. In the "Professional references" field, enter the names and contact details of two professional references.
8. In the "Supporting documents" field, describe any attachments you may have, such as a CV or case studies.
9. If applicable, select a "Preferred start date" in the field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it should appear on the certificate.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for application communication.
* **Current role** (`current_role`, text, optional): Enter your current job title and employer.
* **Years of agile coaching experience** (`years_experience`, number, required): Enter the total years of experience.
* **Qualifications** (`qualifications`, text, optional): List any relevant certifications or education.
* **Statement of intent** (`statement_of_intent`, text, required): Enter a brief statement of why you are seeking the certification.
* **Professional references** (`references`, text, required): Enter the names and contact details of two professional references.
* **Supporting documents description** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Describe any attachments such as CV or case studies.
* **Preferred start date** (`preferred_start_date`, date, optional): Select a date when you are available to begin the program.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as required).
* Attach any relevant supporting documents and describe them in the "Supporting documents description" field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
