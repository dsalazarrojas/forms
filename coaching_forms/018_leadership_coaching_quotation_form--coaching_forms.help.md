# Leadership Coaching Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Coaching Quotation Form is a tool used to collect detailed information about quotations for leadership coaching services or other business quotations. This form will help you document and track essential details related to quotations, ensuring accurate and efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Quotation Request** (page 1) with a brief description of the client's quotation request.
2. Provide **Quotation Details**, including any additional information required for the client's request.
3. Enter the **Quotation Date and Time** (page 2) using the format `MM/DD/YYYY` for date and `HH:MM AM/PM` for time.
4. Select whether the client has accepted the **Quotation Service** offered.
5. Enter the **Quotation Amount** and select the **Quotation Currency**.
6. Specify the **Quotation Currency Symbol**, **Currency Decimal Places**, **Currency Position**, **Currency Rounding**, and **Currency Format** according to your organization's standards.
7. Confirm whether the client has agreed to the **Quotation Terms** and whether the **Quotation was Issued by** and **Issued to** specific individuals.
8. Choose the **Quotation Date Format** and **Quotation Time Format**.
9. Add any additional **Quotation Comments**.
10. Finally, enter any **Quotation Agreement** made with the client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quotation Request**: A brief description of the client's quotation request. 
* **Quotation Details**: Enter any additional details about the quotation.
* **Quotation Date**: Enter the date of the quotation in `MM/DD/YYYY` format.
* **Quotation Time**: Enter the time of the quotation in `HH:MM AM/PM` format.
* **Quotation Service**: Select whether the client has accepted the service offered.
* **Quotation Amount**: Enter the amount of the quotation.
* **Quotation Currency**: Select the currency of the quotation.
* **Quotation Currency Symbol**: Enter the currency symbol (e.g., USD).
* **Quotation Currency Decimal Places**: Enter the number of decimal places for the currency.
* **Quotation Currency Position**: Select whether the currency symbol is before or after the amount.
* **Quotation Currency Rounding**: Select whether to round the currency amount.
* **Quotation Currency Format**: Select the format of the currency (e.g., $, €, ¥).
* **Quotation Comments**: Add any additional comments about the quotation.
* **Quotation Agreement**: Enter any agreement made with the client.
* **Quotation Issued By**: Select whether the quotation was issued by a specific individual.
* **Quotation Issued To**: Select whether the quotation was issued to a specific individual.
* **Quotation Date Format**: Select the date format used for the quotation.
* **Quotation Time Format**: Select the time format used for the quotation.
