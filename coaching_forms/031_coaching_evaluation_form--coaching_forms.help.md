# Coaching Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients about their coaching experience. It's intended for coaches to understand their strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your name and contact email address.
2. Answer the questions about your coaching relationship, such as the duration and number of sessions you've had.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction and experience with your coach.
4. Provide feedback on your coach's expertise, communication, and support.
5. Describe areas where your coach excelled and where they could improve.
6. Answer questions about the impact of coaching on your life and your goals.
7. If applicable, mention any additional support you need or suggest improvements.
8. Finally, rate whether you would recommend your coach to others and continue coaching with them.
