This is a crucial form for tracking family coaching sessions, capturing important information about the family and their progress. It should only be used for genuine family coaching sessions, and not for other purposes such as meetings, events, or general notes. To avoid duplication, please ensure that each session log is created with a unique family name.

# Family Coaching Session Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record key details about family coaching sessions, including family name, coach name, session date, and session duration. It helps us track progress and provide valuable insights for future coaching sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the family's name in the "Family Name" field.
2. Enter the coach's name in the "Coach Name" field.
3. Select the session date in the "Session Date" field.
4. Enter the session duration in the "Session Duration" field.
5. For "Focus Areas", select all relevant areas the family is working on.
6. Add any relevant "Notes" about the session.
7. For "Action Items", select all tasks or next steps for the family.
8. Enter any "Feedback" from the family or coach.
9. Enter the name of the family coach in the "Family Coach" field.
10. Enter the email address of the family coach in the "Family Coach Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Name** (`family_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the family being coached.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the coach leading the session.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the coaching session.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the coaching session in minutes.
* **Focus Areas** (`focus_areas`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all relevant areas the family is working on, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any important notes about the session.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all tasks or next steps for the family, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Enter any feedback from the family or coach.
* **Family Coach** (`family_coach`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the family coach.
* **Family Coach Email** (`family_coach_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the family coach.
