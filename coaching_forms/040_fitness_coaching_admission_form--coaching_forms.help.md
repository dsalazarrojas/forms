# Fitness Coaching Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from potential coaching clients, including their personal details, fitness goals, and availability for training. By filling out this form, you will be helping us to understand your needs and preferences, allowing us to provide you with the best possible coaching experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Please fill out all the required fields marked as required.
2. For each field, please enter your information accurately and completely.
3. If you have any doubts or uncertainties, refer to the field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Birth Date**: Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Height in cm**: Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight in kg**: Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Fitness Goals**: Describe your fitness goals, such as weight loss, muscle gain, or endurance training.
* **Preferred Training Days**: Select all the days you are available for training, including Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.
* **Preferred Training Time**: Enter your preferred training time in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 or 12:00).
* **Emergency Contact Info**: Provide the contact information of an emergency contact person, including their name and phone number.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
* We will review your application and be in touch soon to discuss your coaching needs.
