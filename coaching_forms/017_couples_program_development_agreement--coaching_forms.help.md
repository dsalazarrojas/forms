# Couples Program Development Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the fields in the Couples Program Development Agreement form. This form is designed to gather essential information from individuals participating in a couples program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read through each field and select the most accurate response based on your understanding.
- Fill in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
- Review your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details (id: 1)**: This field is used to collect basic information about the participant. Please provide your name, email address, and other relevant contact details.
* **Program Expectations (id: 2)**: This field will ask you to select the most relevant expectation that suits your needs. Please choose the option that best reflects your goals and objectives for participating in the program.
* **Confirm Participant Details (id: 3)**: This field is a note field where you can confirm your participant details. This is optional but it's recommended to review and agree to the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Confirm Participant Agreement (id: 4)**: This field is a multiple-choice field where you will confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions of the program. Please review and select the options that apply to you.
* **Program Expectations 2 (id: 5)**: Similar to "Program Expectations", this field is used to select the most relevant expectation for your needs. Please choose the option that best suits your goals.
* **Participant Commitment (id: 6)**: This field is a text area where you can express your commitment to participating in the program. Please provide a clear and detailed statement of your commitment.
* **Participant Commitment Date (id: 7)**: This field is used to specify a date for your participant commitment. Please enter a date in the correct format.
* **Participant Commitment Time (id: 8)**: This field is used to specify a time for your participant commitment. Please enter a time in the correct format.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you're unsure about any field, please consult the program administrator or seek guidance.
- Review the terms and conditions of the program before proceeding.
