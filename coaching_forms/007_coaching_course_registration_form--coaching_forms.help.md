# Coaching Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the coaching course registration process. To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the personal information section by entering your First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and Date of Birth.
2. Select the course you wish to enroll in from the Course Name dropdown menu. Ensure you choose the correct course level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced) based on your experience.
3. Choose your preferred start date for the course.
4. If you have previous coaching experience, indicate if you have any experience and specify the number of years you've been coaching.
5. Describe your learning objectives for this course.
6. Enter the course fee you are willing to pay.
7. Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, or Check).
8. Agree to the course terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. First Name
*   **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### 2. Last Name
*   **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### 3. Email Address
*   **Email Address** (Email, Required): We will use this for course communications. Please ensure this is a valid email address you check regularly.

### 4. Date of Birth
*   **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): If you are under 18, please do not fill out this field. Otherwise, enter your date of birth.

### 5. Course Name
*   **Course Name** (Select One, Required): Select the coaching course you wish to enroll in from the dropdown menu.

### 6. Course Level
*   **Course Level** (Select One, Required): Choose your course level based on your experience: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.

### 7. Preferred Start Date
*   **Preferred Start Date** (Date, Required): Choose a start date for the course.

### 8. Do you have previous coaching experience?
*   **Do you have previous coaching experience?** (Select One, Required): If you have any coaching experience, indicate yes. If not, select no.

### 9. Years of Coaching Experience
*   **Years of Coaching Experience** (Number, Optional): If you have coaching experience, enter the number of years you've been coaching. If no experience, enter 0.

### 10. Relevant Certifications
*   **Relevant Certifications** (Text, Optional): List any coaching or related certifications you hold.

### 11. What are your learning objectives for this course?
*   **What are your learning objectives for this course?** (Text, Required): Describe your goals for taking this course.

### 12. Course Fee
*   **Course Fee** (Number, Required): Enter the course fee you are willing to pay.

### 13. Payment Method
*   **Payment Method** (Select One, Required): Choose your preferred payment method: Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Check, or Company Sponsored.

### 14. I agree to the course terms and conditions
*   **I agree to the course terms and conditions** (Select One, Required): Select I Agree to confirm you have read and understand the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
