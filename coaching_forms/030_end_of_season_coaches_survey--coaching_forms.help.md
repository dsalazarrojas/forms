# end_of_season_coaches_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The end of season coaches' survey is designed to gather feedback on various aspects of the coaching process. It helps coaches reflect on their strengths and weaknesses, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions for future seasons.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, if applicable.
3. Submit the form when you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Experience**: Briefly describe your overall experience as a coach during this season.
* **Team Performance**: Describe your team's performance during this season, including any notable strengths and weaknesses.
* **Team Spirit**: Describe the team's spirit and morale during this season, including any notable challenges or successes.
* **Coaches Performance**: Evaluate your own performance as a coach, including any areas where you excelled or struggled.
* **Player Development**: Describe how your players developed and grew throughout this season, including any notable progress or challenges.
* **Communication**: Describe the effectiveness of communication within your team, including any areas where improvement is needed.
* **Coaches' Leadership**: Assess the leadership quality of the coaching staff, including any areas where you felt supported or challenged.
* **Sportsmanship**: Rate the sportsmanship exhibited by your team and your opponents, including any areas where improvement is needed.

## Tips
* Please be thorough and honest in your responses.
* If you're unsure about any question, please select 'Not Applicable' and add a brief explanation in the text field.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the text field at the end of the form.
