# การรักษา - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's medical history, allergies, and other relevant details for treatment or diagnosis purposes. It is intended for healthcare professionals to assess a patient's medical condition and provide appropriate care. Please ensure you are using this form for its intended purpose and not for any other use cases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies.
2. Answer the questions about the patient's allergies, medication, medical condition, and medical procedure.
3. Provide the name of the doctor and any relevant notes about the patient's treatment.
4. Enter the patient's name and contact information so the healthcare professional can follow up with them if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Medical History**: (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false)
 Enter a brief summary of the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies.
- **Allergies**: (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
 Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the patient has any allergies.
- **Medication**: (`medication`, `text`, required: false)
 Enter any medications the patient is currently taking or has taken in the past.
- **Medical Condition**: (`medical_condition`, `text`, required: false)
 Describe the patient's current medical condition or any relevant health issues.
- **Medical Procedure**: (`medical_procedure`, `text`, required: false)
 Detail the medical procedure or treatment the patient is undergoing or has undergone.
- **Doctor Name**: (`doctor_name`, `text`, required: false)
 Enter the name of the doctor or healthcare professional treating the patient.
- **Doctor Note**: (`doctor_note`, `text`, required: false)
 Write any relevant notes or comments from the doctor or healthcare professional.
- **Patient Name**: (`patient_name`, `text`, required: false)
 Enter the patient's name.
- **Patient Phone**: (`patient_phone`, `text`, required: false)
 Enter the patient's phone number for follow-up purposes.
- **Patient Email**: (`patient_email`, `text`, required: false)
 Enter the patient's email address for communication and follow-up purposes.
