# Values Alignment Reflection - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Values Alignment Reflection form is used to help individuals reflect on their personal values and how they align with the company's core values. This exercise is designed to promote self-awareness, alignment, and growth within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the sections below. Each section is designed to help you reflect on your personal values and how they align with the company's core values.
2. Be honest and authentic in your responses. This exercise is for your personal reflection and growth.
3. Complete each section thoroughly, but feel free to skip any sections that don't apply to you.
4. Once you've completed all sections, submit the form to review and reflect on your responses.

## Tips
- Be patient and take your time. Reflection is a process that takes effort and practice.
- Remember, this form is for your personal reflection and growth. There's no right or wrong answers.
- If you need help or have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to your team or supervisor.
