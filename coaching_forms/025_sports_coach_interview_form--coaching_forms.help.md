# Sports Coach Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Coach Interview Form is used to collect information from sports coaches who may be considered as potential candidates for a job opening or volunteer position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and other basic contact information in the Candidate Information section.
2. Select your contact information in the Contact Info section.
3. Choose your relevant qualifications, experience, and certifications in the Qualifications, Experience, and Training Certification sections.
4. Indicate your years of experience in coaching and your coaching certification level.
5. Select your availability to work in the Availability section.
6. Provide an emergency contact person's information in the Emergency Contact section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Candidate Information**: This section is where you enter your basic contact information, including your name and other relevant details.
  - **Contact Info**: This section is where you provide your contact information, such as phone number, email address, or other relevant details.
  - **Qualifications**: Select all the qualifications you hold, such as Certified Coach, Certified Trainer, or Certified Mentor.
  - **Experience**: Choose how many years of experience you have as a coach, and select all the relevant certifications you have.
  - **Availability**: Indicate whether you are available to work on a Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance basis.
  - **Training Certification**: Enter the number of training certifications you have.
  - **Coaching Certification**: Enter the number of coaching certifications you have.
  - **Coaching Experience**: Enter the number of years you have as a coach.
  - **Availability**: This is a duplicate section, please ignore it.
  - **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
