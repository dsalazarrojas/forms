# Coaching Relationship Expectations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide coaches with a clear understanding of a client's expectations and preferences for a coaching relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client you are working with.
2. **Coach Name**: Enter your name as the coach or coaching professional.
3. **Coaching Relationship Start Date**: If applicable, enter the start date of the coaching relationship.
4. **Primary Coaching Goals**: Describe the primary goals you want to achieve through coaching.
5. **Definition of Success**: Explain how you define success in the coaching relationship.
6. **Expected Timeline for Goal Achievement**: Select the expected timeframe for achieving your goals (1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2+ years, or Ongoing).
7. **Coaching Style**: Choose the coaching style you prefer (Direct and directive, Collaborative and partnership-based, Supportive and nurturing, Challenging and growth-focused, or Mixed approach).
8. **Communication Frequency**: Select the frequency of communication you prefer (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, As needed, or Flexible).
9. **Communication Methods**: Select all communication methods you prefer (in-person meetings, phone calls, email, text messaging, video calls, or voice messages).
10. **Feedback Expectations**: Describe how you prefer to receive feedback (Direct, Gentle, Written, Verbal).
11. **Desired Level of Accountability**: Choose the level of accountability you desire (High accountability, Moderate accountability, Light accountability, Coach-directed).
12. **Concerns or Worries**: If you have any specific concerns or worries, describe them here.
13. **Personal Boundaries and Limits**: If you have personal boundaries or limits that you want the coach to respect, describe them here.
14. **Time Commitment for Coaching**: Enter the number of hours you can commit to the coaching relationship per week.
15. **Commitment Level**: Indicate your level of commitment to the coaching relationship (Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree).
16. **Survey Date**: Enter the date you completed this survey.
