# Coaching Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coaching Feedback Form is a crucial tool for coaches to get honest and constructive feedback from clients after each session, enabling them to adjust their approach, improve the coaching process, and ultimately provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your details: Client Name, Email Address, and Phone Number (if applicable).
2. Select the Session Number and Date (if applicable).
3. Rate your mood before and after the session, indicating how you felt during and after the session.
4. Evaluate the helpfulness of the session, the coach's listening and questioning skills, and the quality of feedback provided.
5. Share any insights you gained, actions you committed to, and any additional feedback you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your coach.
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, date, required): Select the date of this feedback.
* **Session Number** (`session_number`, select_one, optional): Select which session this feedback is for (1-6).
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the session (if applicable).
* **Your Mood Before Session** (`mood_before_session`, select_one, required): Rate your mood before the session (Very Positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, Very Negative).
* **Your Mood After Session** (`mood_after_session`, select_one, required): Rate your mood after the session (Very Positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, Very Negative).
* **How Helpful Was This Session** (`session_helpfulness`, select_one, required): Rate the helpfulness of the session (Extremely Helpful, Very Helpful, Somewhat Helpful, Not Very Helpful, Not Helpful At All).
* **Were Your Goals Discussed** (`goals_discussed`, select_one, required): Indicate if your goals were discussed in the session (Yes Extensively, Yes Briefly, No).
* **Do You Feel You Made Progress** (`progress_made`, select_one, required): Rate your progress (Yes Significant Progress, Yes Some Progress, Neutral, No Not Really, No Not At All).
* **Coach Listening Skills** (`coach_listening_rating`, select_one, required): Evaluate the coach's listening skills (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Coach Questioning Skills** (`coach_questioning_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's questioning skills (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Coach Feedback Quality** (`coach_feedback_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the coach's feedback (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Insights Gained** (`insights_gained`, text, required): Share any insights you gained during the session.
* **Actions Committed** (`actions_committed`, text, required): Describe any actions you committed to do before the next session.
* **What Insights Did You Gain** (`insights_gained`, text, required): Share any additional insights you gained about yourself.
* **What Actions Are You Committed To** (`actions_committed`, text, required): Describe any additional actions you committed to do before the next session.
* **What Obstacles Were Identified** (`obstacles_identified`, text, optional): List any obstacles you identified that might hinder your progress.
* **What Support Do You Need** (`support_needed`, text, optional): Share any support you require to overcome obstacles.
* **How Was the Session Pace** (`session_pace`, select_one, optional): Rate the pace of the session (Too Fast, Just Right, Too Slow).
* **How Focused Was the Session** (`session_focus`, select_one, required): Rate how focused the session was (Very Focused, Mostly Focused, Somewhat Focused, Not Very Focused).
* **What Should Be Discussed Next** (`topic_for_next_session`, text, optional): Suggest any topics that should be discussed in the next session.
* **Your Confidence Level Now** (`confidence_level`, select_one, required): Rate your confidence level (Very Confident, Confident, Somewhat Confident, Not Very Confident, Not Confident).
* **Your Motivation Level Now** (`motivation_level`, select_one, required): Rate your motivation level (Very Motivated, Motivated, Somewhat Motivated, Not Very Motivated, Not Motivated).
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional feedback or comments you have.

## Tips
* Answer each question truthfully and honestly, and provide specific examples when asked.
* Rate and describe your feelings and experiences thoroughly.
* Be open and receptive to feedback and suggestions.
* Share your thoughts and insights freely, and don't hold back.
* This form is a collaborative effort - work together with your coach to improve the coaching process!
