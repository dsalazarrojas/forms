# Community Program Leadership Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Program Leadership Award Nomination Form is used to collect information for a leadership award nomination. This form helps us understand the qualifications and achievements of the nominee and provide a strong case for their recognition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information about the nominator and the nominee.
2.  Choose the correct options for the nominee's relationship to you, their organization, and award category.
3.  Provide specific examples and details about the nominee's leadership qualities, achievements, and impact on the community.
4.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**): Enter the first name of the person nominating.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **required**): Enter the last name of the person nominating.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter the email address of the person nominating.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, **required**): Enter the phone number of the person nominating.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, **optional**): If applicable, enter the name of the organization the person nominating is associated with.
* **Relationship to Nominee** (`relationship`, select_one, **required**): Choose the type of relationship you have with the nominee (e.g. colleague, supervisor, community member, etc.).
* **Nominee First Name** (`nominee_first_name`, text, **required**): Enter the first name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Last Name** (`nominee_last_name`, text, **required**): Enter the last name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, email, **optional**): If available, enter the email address of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Phone** (`nominee_phone`, text, **optional**): If available, enter the phone number of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Organization** (`nominee_organization`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the organization or group the person being nominated is associated with.
* **Nominee Title or Role** (`nominee_title`, text, **required**): Enter the title or role of the person being nominated.
* **Years of Community Service** (`years_service`, select_one, **required**): Choose the number of years the nominee has been serving the community.
* **Award Category** (`award_category`, select_one, **required**): Choose the category for which the nominee is being nominated (e.g. outstanding leadership, emerging leader, etc.).
* **Nomination Statement** (`nomination_statement`, text, **required**): Provide a brief statement explaining why the nominee deserves the award.
* **Leadership Qualities Demonstrated** (`leadership_qualities`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all the leadership qualities demonstrated by the nominee (e.g. vision, integrity, communication, etc.).
* **Key Achievements** (`achievements`, text, **required**): Provide specific examples of the nominee's achievements.
* **Describe the Impact on the Community** (`impact_description`, text, **required**): Describe the impact the nominee has had on the community.
* **Programs or Initiatives Led** (`programs_led`, text, **required**): List and describe the programs or initiatives the nominee has led.
* **Challenges Overcome** (`challenges_overcome`, text, **optional**): Describe any challenges the nominee has overcome.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select any supporting documents that will be included with the nomination (e.g. letters of support, photos, etc.).
* **Number of Additional Supporters** (`additional_supporters`, number, **optional**): Enter the number of additional supporters endorsing this nomination.
* **Has the Nominee Received Awards Before** (`previous_awards`, select_one, **optional**): Check if the nominee has received awards before.
* **Previous Awards Details** (`previous_awards_details`, text, **optional**): If yes, provide details about previous awards.
* **Will the Nominee Attend the Award Ceremony** (`nominee_availability`, select_one, **optional**): Check if the nominee will attend the award ceremony if selected.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, **optional**): Provide any additional comments or information about the nominee.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date and time of submission.

**Tips**

* Ensure all required fields are filled out completely.
* Provide specific examples and details about the nominee's leadership qualities, achievements, and impact on the community.
* Review the form before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Submit the form only after reviewing it carefully.
