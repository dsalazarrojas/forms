# Most Creative Branding Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Most Creative Branding Nomination Form is designed to collect nominations for a creative branding project. This form helps to identify and celebrate innovative and impressive branding efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Nomination Form section with a brief description of the nominated project.
2. Choose a category for the nominated project from the available options.
3. Provide the name and date of the nomination.
4. Enter the nominee's name and details.
5. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and submitter details.
6. Optionally, add a message if needed.
7. Agree to the terms and conditions of the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form** (nomination_form): A brief description of the nominated project.
* **Nomination Title** (nomination_title): A title for the nominated project.
* **Nomination Description** (nomination_description): A more detailed description of the nominated project.
* **Nomination Category** (nomination_category): Select a category for the nominated project. Choose from:
	+ Category 1
	+ Category 2
	+ Category 3
* **Date of Nomination** (nomination_date): The date the project was nominated.
* **Nominee Name** (nominee_name): The name of the person or team nominated.
* **Email** (email): The email address of the nominee.
* **Phone** (phone): The phone number of the nominee.
* **Submitter Name** (submitter_name): Your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (submitter_email): Your email address as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (submitter_phone): Your phone number as the submitter.
* **Submitter Job Title** (submitter_job_title): Your job title as the submitter.
* **Submitter Organization** (submitter_organization): Your organization as the submitter.
* **Submitter Department** (submitter_department): Your department as the submitter.
* **Submitter Address** (submitter_address): Your address as the submitter.
* **Submitter City** (submitter_city): Your city as the submitter.
* **Submitter State** (submitter_state): Your state as the submitter.
* **Submitter Postal** (submitter_postal): Your postal code as the submitter.
* **Submitter Country** (submitter_country): Your country as the submitter.
* **Submitter Message** (submitter_message): Any additional message you'd like to provide.
* **Agree** (submitter_agree): Confirm that you agree with the terms and conditions of the submission.
