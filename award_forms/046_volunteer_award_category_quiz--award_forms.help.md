# Volunteer Award Category Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Award Category Quiz form is designed to collect detailed information about awards received by volunteers. This form helps in categorizing and tracking awards in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Begin by selecting the category of the award from the dropdown menu.
2. Fill in the name of the volunteer who received the award.
3. Select the date the award was received.
4. Choose the type of award from the dropdown menu.
5. If applicable, select the category and type of award for additional categories.
6. Enter a description of the award.
7. Select the date of the award submission.
8. Enter the name of the person or entity submitting the award.
9. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the category of the award?** (`volunteer_category`, select_one, required: false)
This field allows you to select the category of the award from a dropdown menu. Select the most accurate category for the award.
* **What is the name of the volunteer?** (`volunteer_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the volunteer who received the award.
* **When was the award received?** (`award_date`, date, required: false)
Select the date the award was received.
* **What is the award type?** (`award_type`, select_one, required: false)
Choose the type of award from the dropdown menu.
* **What is another category of the award?** (`category_2`, select_one, required: false)
If the award has multiple categories, select the category for this field.
* **What is the award type of the second category?** (`award_type_2`, select_one, required: false)
Choose the type of award for the second category selected.
* **What is the description of the award?** (`award_description`, text, required: false)
Enter a brief description of the award.
* **What is the date of the award submission?** (`award_submission_date`, date, required: false)
Select the date the award was submitted.
* **What is the name of the award submitter?** (`award_submitter_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the person or entity submitting the award.
* **What is the award type of the third category?** (`award_type_3`, select_one, required: false)
Choose the type of award for the third category selected.
* **What is the category of the third award?** (`category_3`, select_one, required: false)
Select the category of the third award.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields for accurate submission.
* If an award has multiple categories, select each category and provide the corresponding award type.
* Enter the correct date for both the award reception and submission dates.
* Double-check the submitted information for accuracy before submitting the form.
