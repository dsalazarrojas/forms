# open_source_contribution_award_nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate individuals or teams for their contributions to open-source projects or initiatives. The nomination should be a genuine recognition of their efforts and not for personal or self-promotional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Nomination Title** in the space provided. This is the title of your nomination that will be used to represent the nominee's contribution.
2. Provide a detailed **Nomination Description** of the nominee's contribution. This should include any relevant details about their work and how it has impacted the open-source community.
3. Enter the **Nominator Name** as the person or team who is submitting the nomination.
4. Enter the **Nominator Email** of the person or team who is submitting the nomination.
5. Enter the **Nominator Organization** name if applicable.
6. Enter the **Nominator Title** if applicable.
7. Enter the **Nominee Name** as the individual or team being nominated.
8. Enter the **Nominee Email** of the individual or team being nominated.
9. Enter the **Nominee Organization** name if applicable.
10. Enter the **Nominee Title** if applicable.
11. (Optional) Enter the **Nominee Phone** number if applicable.
12. Enter the **Nomination Link** to a relevant webpage or repository where the contribution can be found.
13. Enter a brief **Nomination Reason** why the nominee deserves recognition.
14. Enter the date of the **Nomination Date** when the contribution was made.
15. (Optional) Enter any **Reviewer Notes** for internal review.
16. Enter the **Nominator Address** if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Title** (`nomination_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for your nomination.
* **Nomination Description** (`nomination_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the nominee's contribution.
* **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or team submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person or team submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Organization** (`nominator_organization`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization associated with the nominator.
* **Nominator Title** (`nominator_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the person or team submitting the nomination.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the individual or team being nominated.
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the individual or team being nominated.
* **Nominee Organization** (`nominee_organization`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization associated with the nominee.
* **Nominee Title** (`nominee_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the individual or team being nominated.
* **Nominee Phone** (`nominee_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the individual or team being nominated.
* **Nomination Link** (`nomination_link`, text, optional): Enter a link to a relevant webpage or repository where the contribution can be found.
* **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, text, required): Enter a brief reason why the nominee deserves recognition.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the contribution was made.
* **Reviewer Notes** (`reviewer_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes for internal review.
* **Nominator Address** (`nominator_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the person or team submitting the nomination.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- If you have any doubts or questions, contact your internal team for assistance.
- Duplicate nominations will be discarded.
