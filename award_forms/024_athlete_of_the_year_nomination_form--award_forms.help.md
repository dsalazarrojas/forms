# Athlete of the Year Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Athlete of the Year Nomination Form is used by individuals to nominate an athlete for this recognition. This form gathers information about the nominee's athletic achievements, characteristics, and other relevant details to help the selection committee decide on the Athlete of the Year.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the nominator information: Enter your name, email address, and phone number as the nominator.
2.  Choose your relationship with the nominee: Select how you know the nominee (e.g., teammate, coach, teacher).
3.  Provide the nominee's information: Enter the nominee's name, primary sport(s), academic year or grade, and team affiliation.
4.  Highlight the nominee's athletic achievements and performance: Describe their significant athletic achievements and performance this year.
5.  Share the nominee's leadership qualities, sportsmanship, and attitude: Describe how the nominee leads on and off the field and their sportsmanship and attitude.
6.  Optional: Provide any supporting documents or media links and mention the nominee's community involvement and academic excellence.
7.  Write a compelling nomination reason: Provide a brief explanation for why you believe this athlete should be selected as Athlete of the Year.
8.  Set the date of nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominator Full Name** (`nominator_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the nominator.
* **Nominator Email Address** (`nominator_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as the nominator.
* **Nominator Phone Number** (`nominator_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as the nominator.
* **Relationship to Nominee** (`nominator_relationship`, select_one, required): Choose how you know the nominee (e.g., Coach, Teammate, Teacher / Professor).
* **Nominee Full Name** (`nominee_name`, text, required): Enter the nominee's name.
* **Nominee Primary Sport(s)** (`nominee_sport`, text, required): Enter the primary sports(s) the nominee participates in.
* **Nominee Academic Year or Grade** (`nominee_academic_year`, text, required): Enter the nominee's academic year or grade.
* **Nominee Team or Club Affiliation** (`nominee_team`, text, required): Enter the team or club the nominee is affiliated with.
* **Significant Athletic Achievements this Year** (`athletic_achievements`, text, required): Describe the nominee's significant athletic achievements this year.
* **Statistical Highlights** (`statistical_highlights`, text, optional): Enter the nominee's statistical highlights (e.g., points scored, personal best times).
* **Other Team or Individual Awards Won** (`awards_won`, text, optional): Enter any other awards won by the nominee.
* **Description of Nominee Leadership Qualities** (`leadership_qualities`, text, required): Describe the nominee's leadership qualities.
* **Description of Nominee Sportsmanship and Attitude** (`sportsmanship_attitude`, text, required): Describe the nominee's sportsmanship and attitude.
* **Community Involvement and Academic Excellence** (`community_academic_excellence`, text, optional): Enter any contributions to the community and the nominee's academic excellence.
* **Why should this athlete be named Athlete of the Year?** (`nomination_reason`, text, required): Provide a compelling reason for why the nominee should be selected.
* **Supporting Documents or Media Links** (`supporting_links`, text, optional): Share links to supporting documents or media related to the nominee.
* **Date of Nomination** (`nomination_date`, date, required): Set the date of the nomination.

This help guide should be easy to follow and allow end-users to understand the form's purpose and complete it accurately.
