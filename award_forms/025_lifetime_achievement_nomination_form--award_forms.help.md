# Lifetime Achievement Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for submitting a lifetime achievement nomination, where you are recognizing an individual or organization for their outstanding contributions to their field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (Name, Nominee, Award Title, and Nomination Date)
2. Choose the correct format for the Nominee field (First name, Last name, Last name first then first name, or Last name, First name)
3. Select the award title from the available options
4. Enter your email address to ensure that you can be contacted with any updates or questions about your nomination

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name
* **Name** (`text`, required): Enter the full name of the person or organization being nominated.
### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your email address so that you can be contacted about your nomination.
### Nominee
* **Nominee** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose how to format the Nominee's name (First name, Last name, Last name first then first name, or Last name, First name).
### Title of Nominee
* **Title of Nominee** (`text`, optional): Enter a title or description of the person or organization being nominated.
### Nominee Organization
* **Nominee Organization** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the organization the person is affiliated with.
### Award Title
* **Award Title** (`select_one`, optional): Select the award title that the nominee is being considered for (Best in Field, Most Valuable Player, Best Team, Honorary Award).
### Nomination Date
* **Nomination Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date you are submitting the nomination.
## Tips
* Make sure to review the award criteria to ensure the nominee meets the qualifications.
* If you need help or have questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
