# recognition_award_claim_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The recognition award claim request form is a tool for employees to submit requests for recognition and rewards for their outstanding work and contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Determine if your work has already been recognized or rewarded through other means within the past [X] months. If yes, do not submit this form.
2. Choose the correct Award Type from the dropdown menu:
	* Select 'Yes' if you believe your work deserves recognition and reward
	* Select 'No' if you do not believe your work merits recognition and reward at this time
3. Write a brief justification for your request, explaining why you believe your work should be recognized and rewarded.
4. Upload supporting documents as needed to further support your claim. You may upload up to 4 separate documents for this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Award Type** (`award_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose 'Yes' if you believe your work deserves recognition and reward, or 'No' if you do not believe it does.
* **Justification** (`justification`, text, required: false): Write a brief explanation of why you believe your work should be recognized and rewarded. This should include specific details and achievements.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, required: false): Upload up to 4 supporting documents to further justify your claim. These can include, but are not limited to, project reports, testimonials, or emails from colleagues.

## Tips
* Ensure that your work has not already been recognized or rewarded within the past [X] months before submitting this form.
* Be concise and specific when writing your justification, providing details about your achievements and contributions.
* Upload relevant supporting documents to strengthen your claim.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the recognition committee.
* Please ensure all fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Note that this form is not intended for duplicate requests.
