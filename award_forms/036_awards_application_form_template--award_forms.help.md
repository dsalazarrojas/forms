# Awards Application Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Awards Application Form Template is designed to collect information from applicants for an awards program. This form helps applicants provide detailed information about themselves and their projects, as well as their acceptance of the award rules and terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting the form.
2.  Review each section carefully and fill in the required information for your project.
3.  For fields with multiple options, select the most relevant and accurate choice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_name`): Fill in your full name as the applicant.
    Please use your official name as per your identification documents.

*   **Email Address** (`applicant_email`): Enter a valid email address for official communication.
    This could be your personal or work email.

*   **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`): Fill in your phone number for contact purposes.
    You can also include international dialing codes if necessary.

*   **Organization or Business Name** (`applicant_org`): If you represent an organization or business, fill in their name here.
    Leave blank if you are an individual applicant.

*   **Award Category** (`award_category`): Choose the most relevant category for your project from the provided options.
    Select carefully, as this will determine the evaluation process.

*   **Project or Submission Title** (`project_title`): Enter the name of the work being submitted.
    Be concise and descriptive.

*   **Project Summary (One Sentence)** (`project_summary_short`): Briefly describe the project in one sentence.
    Focus on the main objectives and achievements.

*   **Detailed Project Description** (`project_description_long`): Provide a full narrative of the project goals and achievements.
    Be as detailed as possible without overwhelming the reviewer.

*   **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`): Fill in the date when the project began.
    Use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

*   **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`): If applicable, fill in the date when the project was completed.
    This is optional, but helps in the evaluation process.

*   **Team Members or Key Contributors** (`team_members_list`): List the team members or key contributors to your project.
    Be as detailed as possible.

*   **Supporting Documents/Portfolio Link** (`supporting_materials_link`): If applicable, provide a URL to any relevant supporting documents or media.
    This could be a link to a website or a Dropbox link.

*   **Social Media or Website Links** (`social_media_links`): Provide any relevant social media or website links.
    This helps in verifying information about your project.

*   **How did you hear about these awards?** (`referral_source_awards`): Select one of the provided options on how you heard about the awards.
    This helps in tracking the effectiveness of our promotion efforts.

*   **I agree to the Award Rules and Terms of Entry** (`terms_agreement_awards`): Select "Yes, I agree" to confirm your acceptance of the award rules and terms.
    This is a mandatory field to ensure compliance.

*   **Digital Signature** (`application_signature`): Sign the form with your full name to certify your submission.
    This is a mandatory field for verification purposes.

## Tips

*   Ensure you answer all fields accurately and truthfully.
*   Be as detailed as possible without overwhelming the reviewer.
*   Proofread your form before submitting to ensure accuracy.
