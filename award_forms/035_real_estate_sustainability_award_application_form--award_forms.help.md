# Real Estate Sustainability Award Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Sustainability Award Application Form is designed to collect information about your project's environmental impacts and sustainability initiatives. This form helps us evaluate your project's eligibility for the award and assess its potential for sustainability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your project details in the "Project Details" section.
2. Indicate whether your project has environmental impacts in the "What are the environmental impacts of your project?" section.
3. Describe how your project will address sustainability in the "How will your project address sustainability?" section.
4. Select the award programs you are interested in applying for in the "Award Programs" section.
5. Enter your project's location in the "Project Location" section.
6. Fill in your name and contact information in the "Applicant Name" and "Contact Information" sections.
7. Submit the form with your project's supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents" section.
8. Finally, select a date for submission in the "Submission Date" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details**: This section is where you provide a brief description of your project.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: False
* **What are the environmental impacts of your project?**: Please select all the environmental impacts that apply to your project.
	+ Type: Multiple selection
	+ Required: True
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Supporting Documents**: Attach any relevant documents that support your project's sustainability claims.
	+ Type: Note field
	+ Required: False
* **How will your project address sustainability?**: Describe how your project will address sustainability.
	+ Type: Single selection
	+ Required: True
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Award Programs**: Select the award programs you are interested in applying for.
	+ Type: Single selection
	+ Required: True
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Project Location**: Enter the location of your project.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: False
* **Applicant Name**: Please enter your name as the applicant for this project.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: True
* **Contact Information**: Provide your contact information for communication purposes.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: True
* **Submission Date**: Select the date you would like to submit your application.
	+ Type: Date field
	+ Required: True
