# Travel Planning Excellence Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Planning Excellence Award Nomination Form is used to recognize outstanding travel planning and coordination efforts. This form is designed to collect necessary information to submit a nomination for an individual or organization.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the First Name of the nominee.
2. Enter the Last Name of the nominee.
3. Select the Award Name that the nomination is for.
4. Enter the Organization Name associated with the nominee.
5. Enter the Address of the nominee.
6. Select the type of Award you are nominating for.
7. Enter the Email of the contact person.
8. Enter the Phone number of the contact person.
9. Enter the Award Date (date the award was given or is scheduled to be given).
10. Enter the Award Recipient name.
11. Enter the Awarding Body name (organization or company giving the award).
12. Optional: Enter the Nomination Deadline for the award, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name <name> (text)**: Enter the first name of the nominee.
* **Last Name <name> (text)**: Enter the last name of the nominee.
* **Award Name <name> (text)**: Enter the name of the award you are nominating for.
* **Organization Name <name> (text)**: Enter the name of the organization associated with the nominee.
* **Address <name> (text)**: Enter the address of the nominee.
* **Award Type <name> (select_one)**: Select the type of award you are nominating for (e.g., Award, Certificate, Plaque, Trophy, Medal).
* **Email <name> (email)**: Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Phone <name> (text)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Award Date <name> (date)**: Enter the date the award was given or is scheduled to be given.
* **Award Recipient <name> (text)**: Enter the name of the award recipient.
* **Awarding Body <name> (text)**: Enter the name of the organization or company giving the award.
* **Nomination Deadline <name> (date)**: (Optional) Enter the deadline for the nomination, if applicable.
