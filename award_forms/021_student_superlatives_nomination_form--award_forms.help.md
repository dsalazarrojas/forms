# Student Superlatives Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Superlatives Nomination Form is designed to collect nominations for students who have demonstrated exceptional qualities and traits that make them stand out among their peers. This form is used to recognize and reward students for their achievements and contributions to the school community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the relevant options for each field.
3. Provide justification and supporting evidence for your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form`, `text`, required): Fill in the name of the student you are nominating.
* **Nomination Form 2** (`nomination_form_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the category that best describes the student's traits or achievements, such as "Most Inspiring Lecturer", "Midnight Warrior", "Future CEO", or "Student Leader".
* **Nomination Form 3** (`nomination_form_3`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the award or recognition you are nominating for.
* **Nomination Form 4** (`nomination_form_4`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the award or recognition you are nominating for.
* **Nomination Form 5** (`nomination_form_5`, `text`, required): Provide a brief justification for the nomination, including specific examples of the student's achievements.
* **Nomination Form 6** (`nomination_form_6`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the student being nominated or the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nomination Form 7** (`nomination_form_7`, `text`, optional): This field is used for additional comments or information.
* **Nomination Form 8** (`nomination_form_8`, `text`, required): List the student's achievements and contributions to the school community.
* **Nomination Form 9** (`nomination_form_9`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of years the student has been nominated for this award.
* **Nomination Form 10** (`nomination_form_10`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of people who have nominated this student for this award.
* **Nomination Form 11** (`nomination_form_11`, `select_one`, required): Select one category that best describes the student's traits or achievements, such as "Most Inspiring Lecturer", "Midnight Warrior", "Future CEO", or "Student Leader".
* **Nomination Form 12** (`nomination_form_12`, `text`, optional): This field is used for optional justification for the nomination.
* **Nomination Form 13** (`nomination_form_13`, `text`, optional): This field is used for additional comments or information.
* **Nomination Form 14** (`nomination_form_14`, `text`, optional): This field is used for more information about the student's achievements.
* **Nomination Form 15** (`nomination_form_15`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the nomination.
* **Nomination Form 16** (`nomination_form_16`, `text`, optional): This field is used for a list of the student's achievements.
* **Nomination Form 17** (`nomination_form_17`, `text`, optional): This field is used for more information about the student's contributions.
* **Nomination Form 18** (`nomination_form_18`, `text`, optional): This field is used for additional comments or information.
* **Nomination Form 19** (`nomination_form_19`, `text`, optional): This field is used for more information about the student's qualifications.
* **Nomination Form 20** (`nomination_form_20`, `text`, optional): This field is used for an optional justification for the nomination.
* **Nomination Form 21** (`nomination_form_21`, `text`, optional): This field is used for additional comments or information.
* **Nomination Form 22** (`nomination_form_22`, `text`, optional): This field is used for more information about the student's achievements.
* **Nomination Form 23** (`nomination_form_23`, `text`, required): Provide a list of the student's achievements and contributions to the school community.
* **Nomination Form 24** (`nomination_form_24`, `text`, optional): This field is used for more information about the student's contributions.
* **Nomination Form 25** (`nomination_form_25`, `text`, required): Fill in the final justifications and comments about the student's nomination.
