# Science Fiction Awards U21 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a structured way to collect information for the Science Fiction Awards U21.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the general information field with the title of the nomination.
2. Enter a brief description of the science fiction work being nominated in the nomination title field.
3. Select a category from the list for the nomination category field.
4. Select one or more subcategories from the list for the nomination subcategory field.
5. Enter your email address in the nominator's email field (optional).
6. Enter your phone number in the nominator's phone field (optional).
7. Enter the submission date and time in the submission date and submission time fields (optional).
8. Enter any additional comments or information in the submission notes field.
9. Select a score for the nomination from the review panel.
10. Enter a brief explanation for the scoring system in the review panel score hint field.
11. Enter your email address in the review panel's email field (optional).
12. Enter your phone number in the review panel's phone field (optional).
13. Enter the review date and time in the review date and review time fields (optional).
14. Enter any comments or feedback from the review panel in the comments field.
15. Enter a numerical score for the nomination out of the range defined by Max Score and Min Score.
16. Enter any additional comments or feedback from the review panel in the review panel comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (id: 1, text, required): Enter the title of the nomination.
* **Nomination Title** (id: 2, text, required): Enter a brief description of the science fiction work being nominated.
* **Nomination Category** (id: 3, select_one, required): Select a category from the list.
* **Subcategory** (id: 4, select_multiple, required): Select one or more subcategories from the list.
* **Nomination Submitter** (id: 5, text, required): Enter your name or alias.
* **Nomination Email** (id: 6, email, required): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Nomination Phone** (id: 7, text, required): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Submission Date** (id: 8, date, optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (id: 9, time, optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submission Notes** (id: 10, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Review Panel** (id: 11, text, optional): Enter the name or alias of the review panel member.
* **Review Panel Email** (id: 12, email, optional): Enter the email address of the review panel member (optional).
* **Review Panel Phone** (id: 13, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the review panel member (optional).
* **Review Comments** (id: 14, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback from the review panel.
* **Review Date** (id: 15, date, optional): Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Time** (id: 16, time, optional): Enter the time of the review.
* **Review Score** (id: 17, number, optional): Enter a numerical score out of the range defined by Max Score and Min Score.
* **Review Score Hint** (id: 18, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation for the scoring system.
* **Max Score** (id: 19, number, optional): Enter the maximum score for the nomination.
* **Min Score** (id: 20, number, optional): Enter the minimum score for the nomination.
* **Panel Score** (id: 21, number, optional): Enter the score given by the review panel.
* **Panel Score Hint** (id: 22, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation for the panel score.
* **Submitter Email** (id: 23, email, optional): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Submitter Phone** (id: 24, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Submitter Comments** (id: 25, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback from the submitter.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out to ensure a valid nomination.
* Double-check the category and subcategory fields for accuracy.
* Use the submission date and time to keep track of the submission timeline.
* Ensure that the review panel's score is within the range defined by Max Score and Min Score.
* Enter any additional comments or feedback in the submission notes, review panel comments, and submitter comments fields for clarity and reference.
