# Library Services Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate individuals or teams for a library services award. The purpose of this form is to recognize and reward outstanding achievements in library services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the nominator's information, including their name, email, and institution.
2. Enter the nominee's information, including their name, title, department, institution, and award category.
3. Enter the nomination name, which will be recognized and rewarded.
4. Review and confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Nominator
* **Nominator**: Enter your name, which will be displayed as the nominator.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Library**: Enter the name of the library or institution you are affiliated with.
* **Nominator's Name**: Enter your name as the nominator.
* **Nominator's Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Nominator's Institutional Email**: Enter your institutional email address.
* **Nominator's Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Nominator's Institutional Phone**: Enter your institutional phone number.
* **Nominator's Fax**: Enter your fax number.
* **Nominator's Institutional Fax**: Enter your institutional fax number.
* **Nominator's Department**: Enter your department or unit.
* **Nominator's Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Nominator's Supervisor**: Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Nominator's Institutional Address**: Enter your institutional address.
* **Nominator's Institutional City**: Enter your institutional city.

### Nominee
* **Nominee**: Enter the name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee's Title**: Enter the nominee's title or position.
* **Nominee's Department**: Enter the nominee's department or unit.
* **Nominee's Institution**: Enter the nominee's institution or library system.
* **Award Category**: Select the category of the award.

### Additional Information
* **Nominator's Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about the nominee.
* **Nominee's Agency**: Enter the nominee's institution or library system.
* **Nominee's Position**: Enter the nominee's position.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for all fields.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the award committee.
