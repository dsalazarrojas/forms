# jewish_professional_award_nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jewish Professional Award Nomination form is used to submit nominations for Jewish professionals who deserve recognition for their outstanding contributions to their field. This form helps to gather essential information about the nominee, their work, and any supporting documents that highlight their achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Nomination Title** to briefly describe the reason for the nomination.
2. Provide a detailed description of the **Nomination Description**.
3. Fill in the **Nominee Name** and their **Nominee Address**.
4. Enter the **Contact Name** and **Contact Email** for the person or organization submitting the nomination.
5. Upload any **Supporting Documents** that demonstrate the nominee's achievements.
6. Select the type of document being uploaded from the **Document Type** dropdown menu.
7. Enter the **Nomination Date** if the nomination is related to a specific event or period.
8. Fill in the **Organization Name**, **Organization Address**, **Organization City**, **Organization State**, and **Organization Country** for the nominee's workplace.
9. Enter the **Nominee Title**, **Nominee Job Title**, and **Nominee Job Description** for the nominee.
10. Enter the **Nominee Years of Service** if applicable.
11. Provide the **Nominee Organizational Affiliations** and **Nominee Publication List**.
12. Enter the **Nominee Award Recipient URL** if the nominee has received an award.
13. Enter any additional **Nominee Notes** about the nominee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Title** (Text): Briefly describe the reason for the nomination.
* **Nomination Description** (Text): Provide a detailed description of the nomination.
* **Nominee Name** (Text): Enter the name of the nominee.
* **Nominee Address** (Text): Enter the address of the nominee.
* **Contact Name** (Text): Enter the name of the person or organization submitting the nomination.
* **Contact Email** (Email): Enter the email of the person or organization submitting the nomination.
* **Supporting Documents** (Text): Upload any documents that support the nomination.
* **Document Type** (Select One): Select the type of document being uploaded.
* **Nomination Date** (Date): Enter the date of the nomination if it's related to a specific event or period.
* **Organization Name** (Text): Enter the name of the nominee's workplace.
* **Organization Address** (Text): Enter the address of the nominee's workplace.
* **Organization City** (Text): Enter the city of the nominee's workplace.
* **Organization State** (Text): Enter the state of the nominee's workplace.
* **Organization Country** (Text): Enter the country of the nominee's workplace.
* **Nominee Title** (Text): Enter the title of the nominee.
* **Nominee Job Title** (Text): Enter the job title of the nominee.
* **Nominee Job Description** (Text): Provide a description of the nominee's job.
* **Nominee Years of Service** (Number): Enter the number of years the nominee has served.
* **Nominee Organizational Affiliations** (Text): Provide a list of the nominee's organizational affiliations.
* **Nominee Publication List** (Text): Provide a list of the nominee's publications.
* **Nominee Award Recipient** (Text): Enter the URL of the award the nominee received.
* **Nominee Award Recipient URL** (Text): Enter the URL of the award the nominee received.
* **Nominee Notes** (Note): Enter any additional notes about the nominee.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the nomination is complete and accurate.
* Use clear and concise language to provide detailed descriptions and explanations.
* Upload any relevant supporting documents that demonstrate the nominee's achievements.
* Double-check the accuracy and completeness of the nomination before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
