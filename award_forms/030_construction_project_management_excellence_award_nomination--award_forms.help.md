# Construction Project Management Excellence Award Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a Construction Project Management Excellence Award Nomination, which recognizes outstanding projects and project managers in the construction industry.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form section by section, starting with the nominee and project details.
- Provide specific information about the nominee, their experience, and the project they are being nominated for.
- Select the correct award category and upload supporting evidence, such as photos and testimonials.
- Review and acknowledge the eligibility rules before submitting the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nominee Full Name** (`nominee_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the individual being nominated.
* **Nominee Company or Organization** (`nominee_company`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the company or organization being nominated.
* **Nominee's Total Years of Project Management Experience** (`nominee_years_exp`, `number`, required): Enter the total years of project management experience of the nominee.
* **Featured Project for this Nomination** (`nomination_project_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the project being nominated.
* **Project Location/Address** (`project_location_address`, `text`, required): Enter the location or address of the project.
* **Award Category** (`award_category_ident`, `select_one`, required): Select the most appropriate award category for the nomination.
* **Summary of Project Success and Impact** (`project_success_summary`, `text`, required): Provide a brief summary of the project's success and impact (max 500 characters).
* **Describe a specific innovation or unique PM approach used** (`specific_innovation_desc`, `text`, required): Describe a specific innovation or unique project management approach used in the project (max 1000 characters).
* **Demonstrate Budget and Schedule Adherence** (`budget_schedule_adherence_stmt`, `text`, required): Provide brief metrics or data points demonstrating the project's budget and schedule adherence.
* **Safety Performance Metrics for the Featured Project** (`safety_performance_metrics`, `text`, required): Provide safety performance metrics for the project (e.g., zero incidents, specific safety award).
* **High-resolution Project Photos Ready for Upload** (`project_photos_ready`, `select_one`, required): Select whether high-resolution project photos are ready for upload.
* **Your Full Name (Nominator)** (`nominator_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the nominator.
* **Your Email Address** (`nominator_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Professional Relationship to the Nominee** (`relationship_to_nominee`, `select_one`, required): Select your professional relationship to the nominee.
* **Date of Nomination** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the nomination.
* **Acknowledge and Agree to Eligibility Rules** (`nominator_ack_ident`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge and agree to the eligibility rules.
* **Additional Comments for the Committee** (`nomination_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information for the committee (max 1000 characters).
