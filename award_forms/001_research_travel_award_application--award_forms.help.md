## Research Travel Award Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Travel Award Application form is used to gather information about research travel details in support of funding for researchers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Research Travel Details**: This field is where you will provide a brief description of your research travel details, including any relevant background information.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Date of Travel**: This field is where you will enter the dates of your travel, including the start and end dates.
  - Type: date
  - Required: false
- **Travel Duration**: This field is where you will enter the duration of your travel, including the number of days or weeks.
  - Type: time
  - Required: false
- **Travel Destination**: This field is where you will enter the destination of your travel, including the city, state, or country.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Additional Expenses**: This field is where you will enter any additional expenses you expect to incur during your travel, including meals, accommodations, etc.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Attachments**: This field is where you will upload any attachments or supporting documents required for your application.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Personal Expenses**: This field is where you will enter any personal expenses you expect to incur during your travel, including expenses for meals, accommodations, etc.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Personal Expense 1** and **Personal Expense 2**: These fields are where you will enter any personal expenses you expect to incur during your travel.
  - Type: number
  - Required: false

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.

Note: The following fields are not required but are meant to provide additional information about your travel: Research Travel Details, Date of Travel, Travel Duration, Travel Destination, Additional Expenses, Attachments, Personal Expenses, Personal Expense 1, and Personal Expense 2.
