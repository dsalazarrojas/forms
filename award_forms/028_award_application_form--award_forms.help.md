# Award Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Award Application Form is designed to gather information about the applicant's qualifications, achievements, and experiences. This form is used to evaluate applicants for various awards, and it's essential to fill it out accurately to increase the chances of being considered for recognition.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and ensure you understand all the fields before starting.
- Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information about yourself.
- Make sure to complete all the required fields.
- Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Applicant Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your valid email address that can be used for communication.
* **Applicant Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Award Category** (`award_category`, select_one, required): Select the primary category for which you are applying.
* **Organization or Affiliation** (`affiliation`, text, required): Enter your current or past affiliation related to your work.
* **Nomination Type** (`nomination_type`, select_one, required): Choose how you are being nominated (Self-Nomination, Nominated by Colleague/Peer, Nominated by Supervisor, or Community Member).
* **Nominator Full Name** (`nominator_name`, text, optional): Enter the full name of the person nominating you, if not a self-nomination.
* **Nominator Email Address** (`nominator_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person nominating you.
* **Key Achievements and Contributions** (`achievement_summary`, text, required): Summarize why you deserve this award with a brief description of your key achievements and contributions.
* **Years of Experience in Field** (`years_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have worked in the field related to your application.
* **Link to Portfolio or CV** (`portfolio_link`, text, optional): Upload a link to your portfolio or CV to support your application.
* **Professional References** (`references`, text, required): Provide the name and contact information for 2-3 professional references.
* **Consent to Verify Information** (`verification_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm your consent to verify the information provided in the form.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submit this application.
* **Digital Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Type your full name to certify the application.
