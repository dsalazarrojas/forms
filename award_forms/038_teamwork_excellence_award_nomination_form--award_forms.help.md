# Teamwork Excellence Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teamwork Excellence Award is given to recognize and reward team members who demonstrate exceptional teamwork skills and achievements. This form is used to nominate a team member for this award.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information:
	* Nominator Name: Enter your name as the nominator of the team member you are nominating.
	* Nominator Email: Enter your email address as the nominator of the team member you are nominating.
	* Nominator Phone: Enter your phone number as the nominator of the team member you are nominating.
2. Fill in the nominee's information:
	* Nominee Name: Enter the name of the team member you are nominating for the award.
	* Nominee Title: Enter the title of the team member you are nominating.
	* Nominee Department: Enter the department of the team member you are nominating.
3. Provide additional details about the nominee's supervisor:
	* Nominator Supervisor Name: Enter the name of the nominee's supervisor.
	* Nominator Supervisor Title: Enter the title of the nominee's supervisor.
	* Nominator Supervisor Email: Enter the email address of the nominee's supervisor.
	* Nominator Supervisor Phone: Enter the phone number of the nominee's supervisor.
	* Nominee's Supervisor's Organizational Impact: Describe the nominee's supervisor's impact on the organization.
	* Nominee's Supervisor's Direct Manager: Enter the name of the nominee's supervisor's direct manager.
	* Nominee's Supervisor's Indirect Manager: Choose whether the nominee's supervisor has an indirect manager.
	* Nominee's Supervisor's Number of Employees: Choose whether the nominee's supervisor has direct reports.
	* Nominee's Supervisor's Employees Count: Enter the number of employees managed by the nominee's supervisor.
	* Nominee's Supervisor's Leadership Skills: Describe the leadership skills of the nominee's supervisor.
	* Nominee's Supervisor's Achievement: Describe the achievement of the nominee's supervisor.
	* Nominee's Supervisor's Additional Comments: Enter any additional comments about the nominee's supervisor.
