# Best Patient Care Team Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for nominating a team for the Best Patient Care Team Award. It allows you to provide information about the team being nominated, their performance, and their impact on patient experience or health.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the required sections first**: Complete the fields that are marked as "required" on the form, as they are necessary to ensure a complete nomination.
2. **Provide specific examples and details**: When possible, provide concrete examples and stories to support your nomination, such as a specific instance where the team provided exceptional care.
3. **Be as clear and concise as possible**: Use plain language to explain your reasons for nominating the team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Your Relationship to the Team**: Select one of the following options to describe how you know the team:
	+ Patient
	+ Patient Family Member
	+ Colleague or Staff Member
	+ Management or Administration
	+ Other
* **Your Email Address**: Enter your official email address.
* **Your Phone Number**: Enter your official phone number.
* **Team or Department Name**: Enter the official name of the care team being nominated.
* **Primary Facility or Ward**: Enter the location where the team provides care.
* **Name of Team Lead**: If known, enter the name of the team lead.
* **Compassion and Empathy (1-5)**: Rate the team's compassion and empathy from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Professionalism and Medical Skills (1-5)**: Rate the team's professionalism and medical skills from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Communication and Clarity (1-5)**: Rate the team's communication and clarity from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Teamwork and Coordination (1-5)**: Rate the team's teamwork and coordination from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Responsiveness and Timeliness (1-5)**: Rate the team's responsiveness and timeliness from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding).
* **Describe a Specific Instance**: Provide a detailed story or observation of a specific instance where the team provided exceptional care.
* **What Makes this Team Stand Out**: Describe unique qualities or strengths that make this team stand out from others.
* **Impact on Patient Experience or Health**: Describe the results or feelings shared by patients as a result of the team's care.
* **Why Should this Team Win**: Summarize your nomination and explain why this team deserves the Best Patient Care Team Award.
* **Approximate Date of Care Experience**: Enter the date of the care experience.
* **Date of Nomination**: Enter the date of the nomination.

Please ensure that you are only nominating a team that you have direct knowledge of, and that you are providing genuine and honest feedback.
