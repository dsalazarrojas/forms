# Nonprofit Impact Award Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Nonprofit Impact Award Nomination Form is used to collect nominations for the Nonprofit Impact Award. This award recognizes outstanding nonprofit organizations that have demonstrated significant impact in their respective fields.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk *) with your information.
2. Select the nominee and submitter details accurately.
3. Provide a thorough nomination description, highlighting the nominee's impact and achievements.
4. Attach any supporting documents that demonstrate the nominee's work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Details**: Enter a brief description of the nomination (max 100 characters).
* **Nominee Name**: Enter the name of the nonprofit organization being nominated.
* **Nominee Organization**: Enter the name of the nonprofit organization being nominated.
* **Nomination Date**: Select the date the nominee started making an impact (format: MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Impact Area**: Select one or more areas where the nominee has made an impact (Community Development, Education, Environment, Health, or Other).
* **Nomination Description**: Enter a detailed description of the nominee's impact and achievements (max 2000 characters).
* **Supporting Documents**: Attach any relevant documents that demonstrate the nominee's work.
* **Nomination Type**: Select the type of award the nominee is being nominated for (Impact Award, Innovation Award, Leadership Award, or Lifetime Achievement Award).
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the person submitting the nomination.
* **Submitter Organization**: Enter your organization's name (if applicable).
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submitter Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about the submission.
* **Submitter Address**: Enter your address for contact purposes.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are accurately filled out to avoid errors and re-submission requests.
* Attach clear and concise supporting documents to demonstrate the nominee's impact.
* Be specific when describing the nominee's impact and achievements.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are completed.
