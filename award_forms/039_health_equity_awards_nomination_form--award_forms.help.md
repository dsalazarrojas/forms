# health_equity_awards_nomination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Equity Awards nomination form is designed to collect information about individuals who have made outstanding contributions to health equity. This form helps to recognize and celebrate their achievements and contributions to the field.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the nominator's information, including their name, email, and organization.
*   Enter the nominee's information, including their name, title, and organization.
*   Describe the nominee's contributions to health equity in the Contribution Description field.
*   Select a category that best fits the nominee's work from the list provided.
*   If none of the categories fit, specify the category in the Other Category field.
*   Enter the nomination date and time.
*   Add any additional comments or information about the nomination.
*   Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`, text, required: false): The name of the person nominating the individual.
*   **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, email, required: false): The email address of the person nominating the individual.
*   **Nominator Organization** (`nominator_organization`, text, required: false): The organization associated with the person nominating the individual.
*   **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, required: false): The name of the individual being nominated.
*   **Nominee Title** (`nominee_title`, text, required: false): The title or role of the individual being nominated.
*   **Nominee Organization** (`nominee_organization`, text, required: false): The organization associated with the individual being nominated.
*   **Contribution Description** (`contribution_description`, text, required: false): A brief description of the nominee's contributions to health equity.
*   **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): A selection of categories that best fit the nominee's work.
*   **Other Category** (`category_other`, text, required: false): If none of the categories fit, specify the category in this field.
*   **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, required: false): The date of the nomination.
*   **Nomination Time** (`nomination_time`, time, required: false): The time of the nomination.
*   **Nomination Time Hint** (`nomination_time_hint`, text, required: false): Additional information about the nomination time.
*   **Nominator Comments** (`nominator_comments`, text, required: false): Additional comments or information about the nomination.
*   **Nominator Signature** (`nominator_signature`, text, required: false): The signature of the person nominating the individual.
*   **Nominator Title** (`nominator_title`, text, required: false): The title or role of the person nominating the individual.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): The name of the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): The phone number of the contact person.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): The email address of the contact person.
