# Movie Awards Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Movie Awards Nomination Form is a tool for submitting movie award nominations. It's used to collect information about a movie, its title, and the nominee's contact details for award consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Category of the nomination (Yes or No).
2. Enter the title of the movie being nominated.
3. Enter the year the movie was released.
4. Enter the name of the nominee.
5. Enter the email address of the nominee.
6. Enter the phone number of the nominee.
7. Enter a brief Nomination Message to accompany the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category**: This field indicates whether the movie should be considered for a nomination (Yes) or not (No).
* **Movie Title**: Enter the title of the movie being nominated.
* **Movie Year**: Enter the year the movie was released.
* **Nominee Name**: Enter the name of the person or team being nominated.
* **Nominee Email**: Enter the email address of the nominee.
* **Nominee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the nominee.
* **Nomination Message**: Enter a brief message explaining why this movie is being nominated.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* If the movie is not eligible for a nomination, select "No" for the Category field.
* Enter the correct email address and phone number for the nominee.
* Keep the Nomination Message brief and clear, providing a compelling reason for the nomination.
