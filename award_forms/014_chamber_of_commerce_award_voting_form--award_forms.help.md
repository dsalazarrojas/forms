# Chamber Of Commerce Award Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chamber Of Commerce Award Voting Form is designed to collect votes for different award categories from chamber members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and email address in the fields provided.
2. Select your membership status with the Chamber.
3. Nominate businesses or organizations for each award category.
4. Provide a reason for each nomination, if applicable.
5. Confirm that this is your first time voting on this form and that you understand that votes are confidential.
6. Submit your votes by selecting "Yes, Submit My Votes".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Voter Name**: Enter your full name as a chamber member.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address associated with your chamber membership.
* **Company or Organization (optional)**: If you are representing a business, enter its name.
* **Chamber Membership Status**: Select your current membership status with the Chamber.
* **Chamber Membership Number (optional)**: Enter your chamber membership ID, if applicable.
* **This Voting Period is Still Open**: Confirm that the voting period is still open.
* **Business of the Year Nominee**: Nominate a business for the Business of the Year award.
* **Community Service Nominee**: Nominate an organization for the Community Service award.
* **Young Professional Nominee**: Nominate a young professional for the Young Professional award.
* **Entrepreneur of the Year Nominee**: Nominate an entrepreneur for the Entrepreneur of the Year award.
* **Leadership Excellence Nominee**: Nominate a business leader for the Leadership Excellence award.
* **Innovation Nominee**: Nominate a business for the Innovation award.
* **Customer Service Nominee**: Nominate a business for the Customer Service award.
* **Nomination Justification 1-6**: Provide a reason for nominating each business or organization.
* **Additional Nominations or Comments**: Provide any additional nominations or comments.
* **I Have Not Voted Before on This Form**: Confirm that this is your first time voting on this form.
* **I Understand Votes Are Confidential**: Confirm that you understand that votes are confidential.
* **Submit My Votes**: Confirm that you want to submit your votes.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that your nominations are valid.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the Chamber for assistance.
