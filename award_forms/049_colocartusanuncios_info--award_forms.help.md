# Colocartusanuncios.info - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an advertisement for display on the Colocartusanuncios.info platform. To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and surname.
2. Enter your email address and phone number.
3. Select the category of your advertisement from the provided options.
4. Provide a title and detailed description of your advertisement.
5. Specify the location or city where the service or product is offered.
6. If applicable, enter the price and currency of the advertisement.
7. Select the type of business or product being advertised.
8. Choose your preferred method of availability for potential customers to contact you.
9. Decide if you want to include an image or photo with your advertisement.
10. Select how long you want your advertisement to be displayed on the platform.
11. Check if you want your advertisement to be featured or premium.
12. If applicable, enter the cost of the featured advertisement.
13. Select your preferred payment method for the advertisement.
14. Agree to the terms and conditions of the platform.
15. Decide if you want to display your contact information in the advertisement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nombre (poster_first_name)**: Enter your complete name.
* **Apellido (poster_last_name)**: Enter your surname.
* **Correo Electronico (poster_email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Numero de Telefono (poster_phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Categoria del Anuncio (category)**: Select the category of your advertisement from the options provided: Empleos, Vivienda, Vehiculos, Articulos Varios, Servicios, Negocios, Otro.
* **Titulo del Anuncio (job_title)**: Enter a title for your advertisement.
* **Descripcion Detallada (announcement_description)**: Provide a detailed description of your advertisement.
* **Ubicacion o Ciudad (location)**: Enter the location or city where the service or product is offered.
* **Precio (price)**: Enter the price or price range of your advertisement. (Optional)
* **Moneda (price_currency)**: Select the currency of the price from the options provided: USD, EUR, MXN, ARS, CLP, COP, Otro.
* **Tipo de Negocio o Producto (business_type)**: Enter a classification or additional information about the business or product.
* **Disponibilidad (availability)**: Select your preferred method of availability for potential customers to contact you from the options provided: Inmediato, Proximo, Flexible, Negociable.
* **Preferencia de Contacto (contact_preference)**: Select your preferred method of contact from the options provided: Telefono, Correo Electronico, Mensaje de Texto, Whatsapp, En Persona.
* **Incluye Imagen o Foto (image_included)**: Decide if you want to include an image or photo with your advertisement.
* **Duracion del Anuncio (posting_duration)**: Select how long you want your advertisement to be displayed on the platform from the options provided: 1 Semana, 2 Semanas, 1 Mes, 3 Meses, 6 Meses, 1 Ano.
* **Anuncio Destacado o Premium (featured_listing)**: Check if you want your advertisement to be featured or premium.
* **Costo de Anuncio Destacado (featured_cost)**: Enter the cost of the featured advertisement. (Optional)
* **Metodo de Pago (payment_method)**: Select your preferred payment method from the options provided: Tarjeta de Credito, Transferencia Bancaria, PayPal, Efectivo, Otro.
* **Acepto los terminos y condiciones (terms_acceptance)**: Agree to the terms and conditions of the platform.
* **Mostrar informacion de contacto en anuncio (contact_information_public)**: Decide if you want to display your contact information in the advertisement.
