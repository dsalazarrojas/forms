# Award Tie Breaker Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Award Tie Breaker Review Form is used to ensure that the selection process for an award is fair and unbiased.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your information in the Reviewer and Award Context section.
2. Provide the names of the individuals tied for the award in the Tied Candidates section.
3. Select which candidate excels in each area of tie-breaker criteria in the Tie-Breaker Criteria section.
4. Provide a final recommendation and reason for your choice in the Final Selection and Reason section.
5. Date and time of the review in the Review Date and Time section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reviewer Full Name (2)**: Enter your full name as the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email Address (3)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Award Name (4)**: Enter the name of the award being evaluated.
* **Award Category (5)**: Enter the category of the award.
* **Tied Candidates (6)**: Provide the names of the individuals tied for the award.
* **Candidate A Full Name (7)**: Enter the full name of Candidate A.
* **Candidate B Full Name (8)**: Enter the full name of Candidate B.
* **Candidate C Full Name (9)**: If applicable, enter the full name of Candidate C.
* **Tie-Breaker Criteria (10)**: Select which candidate excels in each area of tie-breaker criteria.
* **Social Impact and Contribution (11)**: Select which candidate has the highest measurable impact.
* **Level of Innovation (12)**: Select which candidate showed the most creativity or novelty.
* **Professionalism and Leadership (13)**: Select which candidate excels in professionalism and leadership.
* **Narrative Review for Candidate A (14)**: Summarize the strengths and weaknesses of Candidate A.
* **Narrative Review for Candidate B (15)**: Summarize the strengths and weaknesses of Candidate B.
* **Final Selection for Award (16)**: Select your ultimate choice for the winner.
* **Reason for Final Choice (17)**: Explain why you picked this candidate over the other(s).
* **Review Date and Time (18)**: Date and time of the review.
