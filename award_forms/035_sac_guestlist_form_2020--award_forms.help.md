# Sac Guestlist Form 2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sac Guestlist Form 2020 is used to provide essential contact information and award details for the award winners. It is a crucial document that helps ensure accurate record-keeping and recognition of the award winners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Sac Guestlist Form 2020, follow these steps:
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with all the required and optional fields.
- Review and submit the form once you have finished filling it out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Award Name** (`award_name`, text, optional):
    Award Name is a field where you can provide a brief description of the award you are submitting.
    
- **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional):
    Category is a field where you select a specific category from the provided options to accurately identify the award.
    Please select the category that best matches the award you are submitting.
    
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
    Email is a field where you can enter your email address for contact purposes.
    
- **Name** (`name`, text, optional):
    Name is a field where you can enter your name or the name of the award winner.
    
- **Title** (`title`, text, optional):
    Title is a field where you can enter a title or position that the award winner holds.
    
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional):
    First Name is a field where you can enter the first name of the award winner.
    
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional):
    Last Name is a field where you can enter the last name of the award winner.
    
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
    Phone is a field where you can enter a phone number for contact purposes.
    
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional):
    Address is a field where you can enter the address of the award winner.
    
- **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional):
    Zip is a field where you can enter the zip code of the award winner's address.
    
- **City** (`city`, text, optional):
    City is a field where you can enter the city of the award winner's address.
