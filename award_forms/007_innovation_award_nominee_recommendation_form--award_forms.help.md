# Innovation Award Nominee Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Innovation Award Nominee Recommendation Form is used to submit nominations for an innovation award. It's a multi-page form that collects information about the nominee, the nominator, and the innovation being nominated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Start by filling in the Nominee Name field with the name of the person or organization being nominated.
2. Select the Category of the innovation from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the Nomination Date and Time, which will automatically be recorded on the form.
4. Fill in the Nominee Title field with the title of the nominee.
5. Enter the Nominee Email and Nominee Phone fields with the contact information of the nominee.
6. Fill in the Recommender Name, Recommender Title, Recommender Email, and Recommender Phone fields with the information of the person submitting the nomination.
7. Enter the Recommender Organization and Recommender Job Title fields with the information of the nominator's organization and job title.
8. Fill in the Recommender Relationship field with a brief description of the relationship between the nominator and the nominee.
9. Enter the Nominee Innovation and Nominee Description fields with a brief description of the innovation and its significance.
10. Add any additional comments from the nominator in the Recommender Comment field.
11. Finally, submit the form by filling in the Date Submitted, Time Submitted, and Form Completed fields, and click the Submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization being nominated.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of the innovation from the dropdown menu.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the nomination was made.
* **Nomination Time** (`nomination_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the nomination was made.
* **Nominee Title** (`nominee_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the nominee.
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the nominee.
* **Nominee Phone** (`nominee_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the nominee.
* **Recommender Name** (`recommender_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Recommender Title** (`recommender_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Recommender Email** (`recommender_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Recommender Phone** (`recommender_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Recommender Organization** (`recommender_organization`, text, optional): Enter the organization of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Recommender Job Title** (`recommender_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Recommender Relationship** (`recommender_relationship`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the relationship between the nominator and the nominee.
* **Nominee Innovation** (`nominee_innovation`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the innovation.
* **Nominee Description** (`nominee_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the nominee's work or innovation.
* **Recommender Comment** (`recommender_comment`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments from the nominator.
* **Recommender Signature** (`recommender_signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, optional): Click this button to submit the nomination.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): The date when the nomination was submitted.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, optional): The time when the nomination was submitted.
* **Form Completed** (`form_completed`, date, optional): The date when the form was completed.
* **Form Received** (`form_received`, date, optional): The date when the form was received.

## Tips
When filling out the form, make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about the nominee and the innovation. Also, ensure that the form is complete and submitted with all required fields filled in. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the award committee.
