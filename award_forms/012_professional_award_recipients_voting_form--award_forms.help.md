# Professional Award Recipients Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The professional award recipients voting form is designed to collect opinions from award recipients on who they believe should receive a company-wide award. This is an anonymous and secure platform where participants can vote on their peers without influencing each other's decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the award category you are voting on.
2. Enter the name of the recipient(s) you would like to vote for.
3. Provide a rating for each recipient (1-3).
4. Enter your contact information if you want to receive a confirmation email after submitting your vote.
5. Click "Submit" to submit your vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Award Category**: Select the award category you are voting on.
* **Recipient Name**: Enter the name of the recipient(s) you would like to vote for.
* **Rating 1**: Provide a rating from 1-3 for the recipient, where 1 is the lowest and 3 is the highest.
* **Rating 2**: Provide a rating from 1-3 for the recipient, where 1 is the lowest and 3 is the highest.
* **Rating 3**: Provide a rating from 1-3 for the recipient, where 1 is the lowest and 3 is the highest.
* **Submit**: This is the submit button to cast your vote. 
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive a confirmation email after submitting your vote.
* **Phone**: This field is not currently used for this form.

Note: The form will only be visible to users who have received a professional award, and your vote will only be counted if you have been selected as a recipient for a professional award.
