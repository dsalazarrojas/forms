# Scientific Discovery Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate a scientific discovery for an award. It collects information about the person nominating the discovery, the details of the discovery, and any supporting materials that can attest to its significance or relevance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information on **page 1: Nominator Information**.
2. Enter your relationship with the nominee on **page 9: Nominator's Relationship with the Nominee**.
3. Describe the scientific discovery you are nominating on **page 4: Nomination Reason**.
4. Specify the category and year of the award on **page 5: Award Nomination Category** and **page 6: Award Nomination Year**.
5. Write a description of the discovery on **page 7: Award Nomination Description**.
6. Provide any supporting documents or letters, such as a letter of recommendation, a copy of the discovery, or a CV of the nominee, on **page 10: Nomination Supporting Letter**, **page 11: Nomination Supporting Document**, **page 13: Nomination Supporting Letter 2**, and **page 15: Nomination Supporting Document 2**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **page 1: Nominator Information**: Enter your name and contact information to allow us to contact you regarding your nomination.
  * **Name**: Your name and title/position.
  * **Contact Information**: Your email address and phone number.
  * **Email Address**: Your email address.
  * **Phone Number**: Your phone number.
- **page 2: Award Nominator Information**: Enter any additional information about yourself and your relationship with the nominee.
- **page 3: Nominator's Contact Information**: Enter your email address and phone number.
- **page 4: Nomination Reason**: Describe the scientific discovery you are nominating and why it is significant.
- **page 5: Award Nomination Category**: Enter the category of the award for which you are nominating.
- **page 6: Award Nomination Year**: Enter the year of the award for which you are nominating.
- **page 7: Award Nomination Description**: Enter a description of the discovery and its significance.
- **page 8: Award Nomination URL**: Enter a link to any online resources that support the discovery.
- **page 9: Nominator's Relationship with the Nominee**: Describe your relationship with the person being nominated.
- **page 10: Nomination Supporting Letter**: Enter a letter of recommendation for the nominee.
- **page 11: Nomination Supporting Document**: Enter any supporting documents for the discovery.
- **page 12: Nomination Supporting Document URL**: Enter a link to any online supporting document.
- **page 13: Nomination Supporting Letter 2**: Enter any additional letters of recommendation for the nominee.
- **page 14: Nominee's CV or Bio**: Enter the nominee's CV or bio.
- **page 15: Nomination Supporting Document 2**: Enter any additional supporting documents for the discovery.
- **page 16: Nomination Supporting Document 2 URL**: Enter a link to any additional online supporting document.
- **page 17: Nomination Supporting Letter 2**: Enter any additional letters of recommendation for the nominee.
- **page 18: Nomination Supporting Letter 2 URL**: Enter a link to any additional online supporting letter.
