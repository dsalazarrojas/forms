# Marine Engineer Award Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Marine Engineer Award Nomination Form is a tool used for nominating deserving individuals for special awards within the marine engineering field. It is used to gather information about the nominee, their achievements, and supporting documentation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Marine Engineer Award Nomination Form**:
Start by entering the title of the nomination form, which should clearly state the purpose and scope of the award.
2. **Category**:
Select the category that best fits the award or recognition being sought. Choose from the available options:
	* Category 1
	* Category 2
	* Category 3
3. **Name**:
Enter the full name of the nominee.
4. **Title/Position**:
Enter the title or position of the nominee (e.g., Chief Engineer, Project Manager, etc.).
5. **Email**:
Enter the email address of the nominee or the individual submitting the nomination.
6. **Phone**:
Enter the phone number of the nominee or the individual submitting the nomination.
7. **Nomination Statement**:
Provide a brief statement explaining why the nominee is being recognized. This should include their achievements and the reasons for the nomination.
8. **Supporting Document**:
If applicable, upload any supporting documentation that demonstrates the nominee's qualifications or achievements. This can include letters of recommendation, certifications, or other relevant materials.
9. **Supporting Comments**:
Provide any additional comments or context that support the nomination.
10. **Submit**:
Once all required fields are completed, click the "Submit" button to finalize the nomination.
