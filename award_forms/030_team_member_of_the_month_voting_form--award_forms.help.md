# Team Member Of The Month Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Team Member Of The Month Voting Form" allows team members to nominate and vote for their peers, providing a subjective evaluation of who deserves recognition for outstanding contributions to the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select "Team Member 1" from the dropdown menu. This is the team member you wish to nominate for recognition.
2. In the "Nomination Reason" field, provide a brief explanation for why you're nominating this team member. This could be a specific project they led, a remarkable achievement, or an outstanding quality that makes them deserving of recognition.
3. In the "Nomination Form 1" field, provide any additional details or context that support your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member 1 (Select One)**: Choose one of the available team members from the dropdown menu for whom you wish to nominate. This field is optional but is required to have a valid nomination.
* **Nomination Reason (Text)**: Provide a brief explanation for why you're nominating this team member. This could be a specific project they led, a remarkable achievement, or an outstanding quality that makes them deserving of recognition.
* **Nomination Form 1 (Text)**: In this field, provide any additional details or context that support your nomination.
