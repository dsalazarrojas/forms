# Maintenance Professional Award Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and nominations for employees who have demonstrated exceptional performance, leadership, or innovative work. Nominations are submitted on behalf of a specific individual and should be accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the nominator's information (name, email, and phone number).
2. Enter the nominee's information (name, email, and phone number).
3. Select the type of award nomination (Outstanding Performance, Outstanding Leadership, Outstanding Innovation, or Other).
4. If applicable, upload any supporting documents.
5. Confirm or decline the submission by selecting the "Consent" field.
6. Add any additional comments about the nomination.
7. Select the type of award (text field for a specific type of award).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominator Information** (`nominator_info`): Enter the name of the person nominating the employee.
* **Email** (`nominator_email`): Enter the email address of the person nominating the employee.
* **Phone** (`nominator_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person nominating the employee.
* **Nominee Information** (`nominee_info`): Enter the name of the employee being nominated.
* **Title** (`nominee_title`): Enter the job title or position of the employee being nominated.
* **Email** (`nominee_email`): Enter the email address of the employee being nominated.
* **Phone** (`nominee_phone`): Enter the phone number of the employee being nominated.
* **Award Nomination Form** (`award_nomination_form`): Select the type of award nomination (Outstanding Performance, Outstanding Leadership, Outstanding Innovation, or Other).
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`): If applicable, upload any documents that support the nomination.
* **Consent** (`consent`): Confirm that the information is accurate and up-to-date by selecting "True".
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments about the nomination.
* **Type of Award** (`award_type`): Select the specific type of award (text field).
