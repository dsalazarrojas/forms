## Scholarship Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a Scholarship Application form, designed for users to submit their application for a scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details on the first page.
2. Enter your contact details on the second page.
3. Provide information about your references on the third page.
4. Share an idea about your character on the fourth page.
5. List the books you have read on the fifth page.
6. Add a picture of yourself on the sixth page.
7. Answer a short question on the seventh page.
8. Choose an option for submission on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details (1)**: Enter your basic personal details such as name, address, and email.
* **Contact Details (2)**: Fill in your contact details such as phone number and emergency contact person.
* **References (3)**: List the names and contact information of 2-3 people who can vouch for you.
* **An Idea About Your Character (4)**: Write a short description about a time when you demonstrated your character, for example, helping others.
* **List of Books You Have Read (5)**: Select the books you have read from the list of options provided.
* **Picture(s) of You (6)**: Upload or attach a picture of yourself.
* **Short Answer Question (7)**: Answer the question: "Why do you deserve this scholarship?"
* **Submit (8)**: Choose to either submit your application or save it for later and continue editing.
