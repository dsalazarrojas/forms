# Therapeutic Care Excellence Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Therapeutic Care Excellence Award Nomination Form is designed to recognize outstanding therapeutic care teams who have made significant contributions to the field of care. The form is used to gather information about the team's achievements and experiences, which is then reviewed and evaluated by the award committee.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the required contact information in the top sections of the form.
2. Select the relevant Category and Subcategory from the provided options.
3. Provide a brief title for the form to be used for identification.
4. Fill in the details about the therapeutic care team you are nominating.
5. Enter the details of the award you are nominating the team for, including the award year.
6. Provide a brief description of the award nomination.
7. Enter the details of the nominator, including their contact information and relationship with the team.
8. Enter the signature of the nominator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the form to be used for identification.
* **Nominating Organization** (`nominating_organization`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization nominating the team.
* **Nominating Person** (`nominating_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person nominating the team.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the contact number of the person nominating the team.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person nominating the team.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the category under which the team's work falls.
* **Subcategory** (`subcategory`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more subcategories that best describe the team's work.
* **Name of Award** (`name_of_award`, text, required): Enter the name of the award you are nominating the team for.
* **Award Year** (`award_year`, date, required): Enter the year the award was given or is being nominated for.
* **Award Nomination** (`award_nomination`, note, required): Provide a brief description of the reasons why the team is being nominated for this award.
* **Award Nomination Details** (`award_nomination_details`, text, optional): Provide additional details about the award nomination.
* **Nominating Caregiver** (`nominating_caregiver`, text, required): Enter the name of the caregiving team member who is being nominated.
* **Nominating Caregiver Email** (`nominating_caregiver_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the caregiving team member who is being nominated.
* **Nominating Caregiver Number** (`nominating_caregiver_number`, text, required): Enter the contact number of the caregiving team member who is being nominated.
* **Nominating Caregiver Relationship** (`nominating_caregiver_relationship`, text, required): Describe the relationship between the nominator and the team member being nominated.
* **Award Nominator** (`award_nominator`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or organization awarding the prize.
* **Award Nominator Relationship** (`award_nominator_relationship`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the relationship between the award nominator and the team member being nominated.
* **Nominator Title** (`nominator_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the person awarding the prize.
* **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person awarding the prize.
* **Nominator Institution** (`nominator_institution`, text, required): Enter the name of the institution or organization awarding the prize.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person awarding the prize.
* **Nominator Number** (`nominator_number`, text, required): Enter the contact number of the person awarding the prize.
* **Nominator Relationship** (`nominator_relationship`, text, required): Describe the relationship between the nominator and the team member being nominated.
* **Nominator Job Title** (`nominator_jobtitle`, text, required): Enter the job title of the person awarding the prize.
* **Nominator Signature** (`nominator_signature`, text, required): Enter the signature of the person awarding the prize.

Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields and provided sufficient details to support the nomination. If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
