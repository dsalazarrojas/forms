# Awards Nomination 1 Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the awards nomination process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the nomination category from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the nominator's and nominee's information, including name, email, phone, and job title.
3. Provide the nominee's achievements, impact, and relevant supporting documents.
4. Answer questions about the nominee's qualities and how they made a difference.
5. Confirm that you are aware of the submission deadline and certify that you are submitting accurate information.
6. Add any additional references or previous awards received.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Category**: Choose the correct category for the nominee from the dropdown menu.
* **Nominator Full Name**: Enter the complete name of the person nominating the person.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the nominator.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the nominator (optional).
* **Your Relationship to Nominee**: Select how you know the nominee from the dropdown menu.
* **Nominee Full Name**: Enter the complete name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Email**: Enter the email address of the person being nominated (optional).
* **Nominee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person being nominated (optional).
* **Nominee Organization**: Enter the school, company, or institution of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Title or Position**: Enter the job title or role of the person being nominated.
* **Years of Service**: Select how long the person has been in their role from the dropdown menu.
* **Nomination Statement**: Explain why the nominee is deserving of the award.
* **Key Achievements**: List 3-5 major accomplishments of the nominee.
* **Impact Description**: Describe how the nominee has made a difference.
* **Supporting Documents**: Provide any relevant supporting documents, such as resumes or letters of support.
* **Additional References**: List names of people who can provide references for the nominee.
* **Previous Awards Received**: List any previous awards received by the nominee.
* **Outstanding Qualities**: Select the qualities that make the nominee stand out from the dropdown menu.
* **Confidentiality Preference**: Check if the nominee should be informed about this nomination.
* **Are You Aware of Deadline**: Confirm if you are aware of the submission deadline.
* **How Did You Hear About This Award**: Select where you heard about the award from the dropdown menu (optional).
* **I Certify This Information Is Accurate**: Check to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Submission Date**: The current date will be automatically populated.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to complete this form accurately.
- Double-check the information provided to avoid errors.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
- Remember to submit the form before the deadline.
