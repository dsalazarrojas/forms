# Community Service Award Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Community Service Award Nomination Form is used to nominate individuals or groups for a community service award. This form is designed to gather information about the nominee, their service to the community, and the impact of their work.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully before filling it out to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2.  Fill out the nominator information in the first section, including the nominator's name, email, and phone number.
3.  In the second section, provide the nominee's information, including their name, contact details, and category of service.
4.  Describe the nominee's impact on the community in the "Describe the Nominee's Impact" field.
5.  Provide a brief explanation for why the nominee deserves the award in the "Nomination Rationale" section.
6.  List three key accomplishments of the nominee in the "List 3 key accomplishments" field.
7.  Specify the number of years the nominee has served the community in the "Years of Service" field.
8.  If required, provide two references with their names and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominator Name** (id: 2): Enter your name as the person nominating the individual or group.
* **Nominator Email** (id: 3): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Nominator Phone** (id: 4): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Nominee Name** (id: 7): Enter the name of the individual or group being nominated.
* **Nominee Contact Information** (id: 8): Enter the nominee's contact information, such as phone or email.
* **Award Category** (id: 9): Choose the category that best fits the nominee's service, from the options provided.
* **Describe the Nominee's Impact** (id: 10): Describe how the nominee's work has positively impacted the community.
* **Nomination Rationale** (id: 12): Explain why the nominee deserves the award.
* **List 3 key accomplishments** (id: 13): List three key accomplishments of the nominee.
* **Years of Service** (id: 14): Specify the number of years the nominee has served the community.
* **Reference 1 Name** (id: 16): Enter the name of the first reference.
* **Reference 1 Contact** (id: 17): Enter the contact information of the first reference.
* **Reference 2 Name** (id: 18): Enter the name of the second reference (if applicable).
* **Reference 2 Contact** (id: 19): Enter the contact information of the second reference (if applicable).
