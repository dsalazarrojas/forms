# Supply Chain Efficiency Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Chain Efficiency Award is an internal recognition program that acknowledges and rewards outstanding efforts and achievements in the supply chain domain. This form is used to nominate teams or individuals for this award.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for the nomination, e.g., "Team X's Supply Chain Efficiency Achievement".
2. Select the team that is being nominated (e.g., "Team Lead", "Other" if not a team).
3. Enter the team lead's contact information (email and phone number).
4. Select the award category that best fits the nomination (e.g., "Supply Chain Efficiency", "Operational Excellence", or "Other").
5. Provide a brief description of the nomination (e.g., "This team has achieved outstanding results in supply chain efficiency by...".
6. Optionally, attach supporting documents or other relevant files to support the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supply Chain Efficiency Award Nomination** (`1`, `text`, `false`): A brief title for the nomination (e.g., "Team X's Supply Chain Efficiency Achievement").
* **Nominating Team** (`2`, `text`, `false`): The team being nominated (e.g., "Team Lead", "Other" if not a team).
* **Team Lead** (`3`, `text`, `false`): The team lead's name.
* **Team Lead Email** (`4`, `email`, `false`): The team lead's email address.
* **Team Lead Phone** (`5`, `text`, `false`): The team lead's phone number.
* **Nominators** (`6`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Select all nominators (e.g., team lead, other team members).
* **Award Category** (`7`, `select_one`, `false`): The award category that best fits the nomination (e.g., "Supply Chain Efficiency", "Operational Excellence", "Other").
* **Nomination Description** (`8`, `note`, `false`): A brief description of the nomination.
* **Supporting Documents** (`9`, `text`, `false`): Attach supporting documents or other relevant files to support the nomination.
