# Wildlife Conservation Impact Award Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for the Wildlife Conservation Impact Award nominations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1.  Enter a title for the nomination, describing the project or person you are nominating.
2.  Provide a project description, including a brief summary of the project's goals, outcomes, or achievements.
3.  Specify the project's location, including the city or region where it took place or where it has been implemented.
4.  Select team members involved in the project, if applicable.
5.  Enter the name of the award committee or organization handling the nomination.
6.  Provide contact information for further inquiries or follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Nomination Details** (`nomination_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief title for the nomination, describing the project or person you are nominating.
*   **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the project or person you are nominating, including a brief summary of their goals, outcomes, or achievements.
*   **Project Location** (`project_location`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the location of the project, including the city or region where it took place or where it has been implemented.
*   **Team Members** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select team members involved in the project, if applicable.
*   **Award Committee Name** (`award_committee_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the award committee or organization handling the nomination.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide contact information for further inquiries or follow-up.
