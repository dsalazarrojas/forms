# Customer Service Award Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Service Award Nomination form is used to recognize and reward outstanding customer service efforts. The form is designed to collect information about the nominee, the nominator, and the reasons for the nomination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Nomination Title** in the text field provided. This title should clearly and concisely describe the nomination.

2.  Enter the **Nominee Name**, which is the name of the individual being nominated for the award.

3.  Enter the **Nominator Name**, which is the name of the person submitting the nomination.

4.  Enter the **Nominator Email**, which is the email address of the nominator. This will be used to contact the nominator if further information is needed.

5.  Enter the **Nominator Phone Number**, which is the phone number of the nominator. This can be used for further communication if needed.

6.  Enter the **Nomination Details**, which is a description of the nominee's outstanding customer service efforts. This field should clearly and concisely describe the reasons for the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Title**: A brief and descriptive title for the nomination.
* **Nominee Name**: The name of the individual being nominated for the award.
* **Nominator Name**: The name of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Email**: The email address of the nominator, used for further communication.
* **Nominator Phone**: The phone number of the nominator, used for further communication.
* **Nomination Details**: A description of the nominee's outstanding customer service efforts.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and relevant information to the best of your abilities.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid duplication or incorrect submissions.
* If you're unsure about the eligibility of a nominee or have any doubts, please reach out to the designated authority.
