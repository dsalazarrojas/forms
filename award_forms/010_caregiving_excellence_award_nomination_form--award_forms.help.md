# Caregiving Excellence Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for the Caregiving Excellence Award nomination.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Please ensure that you are authorized to nominate someone and that the person you are nominating is aware that they are being nominated.
3. Review our award criteria to ensure the nominee meets the requirements for the selected award category.
4. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (id: 2, label: Your Full Name, type: text, required: true)
  This field is required and should contain your name as the nominator.
* **Your Email Address** (id: 3, label: Your Email Address, type: email, required: true)
  This field is required and should contain your email address for contact purposes.
* **Your Phone Number** (id: 4, label: Your Phone Number, type: text, required: false)
  This field is optional and should contain your phone number for additional contact.
* **Your relationship to the nominee** (id: 5, label: Your relationship to the nominee, type: select_one, required: true)
  Select one of the provided options (Supervisor, Peer, Client, Client Family, Community Member, Other) to indicate your relationship with the nominee.
* **Nominee's Full Name** (id: 7, label: Nominee's Full Name, type: text, required: true)
  This field is required and should contain the full name of the nominee.
* **Nominee's Department or Unit** (id: 8, label: Nominee's Department or Unit, type: text, required: true)
  This field is required and should contain the department or unit where the nominee works.
* **Nominee's Job Title** (id: 9, label: Nominee's Job Title, type: text, required: true)
  This field is required and should contain the job title of the nominee.
* **Nominee's years of service** (id: 10, label: Nominee's years of service, type: number, required: false)
  This field is optional and should contain the total years of service of the nominee with the organization.
* **Award Category** (id: 12, label: Award Category, type: select_one, required: true)
  Select one of the provided options (Rookie of the Year, Compassion Award, Leadership in Care, Community Impact, Innovation in Care, Lifetime Achievement) to choose the award category.
* **Primary Strengths** (id: 13, label: What are the nominee's primary strengths?, type: select_multiple, required: true)
  Select up to 3 of the provided options (Empathy, Problem Solving, Reliability, Clinical Excellence, Teamwork, Advocacy) to indicate the nominee's primary strengths.
* **Reason for Nomination** (id: 14, label: Why are you nominating this person for this award?, type: text, required: true)
  This field is required and should contain a brief summary of why you are nominating this person for the selected award category.
* **Specific Example** (id: 16, label: Please share a specific example of when this person went above and beyond, type: text, required: true)
  This field is required and should contain a detailed example of the nominee's exceptional care and dedication.
* **Impact on Patients or Clients** (id: 17, label: Describe the impact of the nominee's care on patients or clients, type: text, required: false)
  This field is optional and should contain information on how the nominee's care has improved outcomes or lives.
* **Impact on Colleagues or Team** (id: 18, label: Describe the impact of the nominee on their colleagues or team, type: text, required: false)
  This field is optional and should contain information on how the nominee has influenced the work environment.
* **Supporting Documents** (id: 20, label: List any supporting documents you can provide, type: text, required: false)
  This field is optional and should contain any supporting documents such as letters of recommendation or patient testimonials.
* **Submission Date** (id: 23, label: Today's Date, type: date, required: true)
  This field is required and should contain the current date.
* **Confirmaion** (id: 22, label: I certify that the information provided is accurate, type: select_one, required: true)
  Select 'True' to confirm that the information is accurate.
