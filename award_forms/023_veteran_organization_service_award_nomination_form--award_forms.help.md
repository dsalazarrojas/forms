# Veteran Organization Service Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veteran Organization Service Award Nomination Form is used to collect information and nominations for the Veteran Organization Service Award. This form gathers details about a nominee's service and submits the nomination for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Nominee Information**: Enter the nominee's name and any other relevant details in the "Nominee Details" field.
2. **Enter Service Information**: In the "Service Information" field, describe the nominee's service and its relevance to the Veteran Organization Service Award.
3. **Enter Award Information**: In the "Award Information" field, provide any additional details about the award being nominated for.
4. **Enter Nominator Information**: Fill in the nominator's name, email, phone number, organization, and job title in the corresponding fields.
5. **Review and Status**: Enter the date of submission and status of the nomination in the corresponding fields.
6. **Submit the Form**: Click the "Submit" button to submit the nomination for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nominee Information**: Enter the nominee's name and any other relevant details.
* **Nominee Details**: Enter the nominee's name, date of birth, service branch, service dates, and any other relevant details.
* **Service Information**: Describe the nominee's service and its relevance to the Veteran Organization Service Award.
* **Award Information**: Provide any additional details about the award being nominated for.
* **Nominator Information**: Enter the nominator's name, email, phone number, organization, and job title.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Status**: Enter the status of the nomination.
* **Submission ID**: Confirm that the submission is not duplicated.
* **Submitter Information**:
	+ **Submitter Name**: Enter the name of the person submitting the nomination.
	+ **Submitter Email**: Enter the email address of the submitter.
	+ **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the submitter.
	+ **Submitter Organization**: Enter the organization of the submitter.
	+ **Submitter Job Title**: Enter the job title of the submitter.
* **Submitter 1 Information** (for co-submitters): Enter the name, email, phone number, organization, and job title of the first co-submitter.
* **Organization**: Enter the organization of the submitter (this field is only required for primary submitters).

## Tips

* Ensure that the submission is not duplicated.
* Verify the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Ensure that the submission meets all the requirements for the Veteran Organization Service Award.
