# Outstanding Team Member Of The Year Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow team members to nominate their peers for the Outstanding Team Member of the Year award. The form collects information about the nominee, the nominator, and the nomination period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Select the team member you wish to nominate from the dropdown list on page 1.
2.  Fill in the nominator's information on pages 2-5, including their name, title, email, and phone number.
3.  Provide the team member's information on pages 6-9, including their name, job title, email, and phone number.
4.  Enter the number of awards and recognition received by the team member in the current year.
5.  Choose the year of the nomination.
6.  Double-check the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member Nomination** (`team_member_nomination`, select_one, required): Select the team member you wish to nominate for the Outstanding Team Member of the Year award.
* **Nomination Period Start Date** (`nomination_period_start_date`, date, required): This field is not currently required, but it is a date field for the start date of the nomination period.
* **Nomination Period End Date** (`nomination_period_end_date`, date, required): This field is not currently required, but it is a date field for the end date of the nomination period.
* **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person nominating the team member.
* **Nominator Title** (`nominator_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the person nominating the team member.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the person nominating the team member.
* **Nominator Phone** (`nominator_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person nominating the team member.
* **Team Member Name** (`team_member_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the team member being nominated.
* **Team Member Job Title** (`team_member_job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the team member being nominated.
* **Team Member Email** (`team_member_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the team member being nominated.
* **Team Member Phone** (`team_member_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the team member being nominated.
* **Team Member Annual Awards Count** (`team_member_annual_awards_count`, number, required): Enter the number of awards received by the team member in the current year.
* **Team Member Annual Recognition Count** (`team_member_annual_recognition_count`, number, required): Enter the number of recognitions received by the team member in the current year.
* **Nomination Year** (`nomination_year`, number, required): Select the year of the nomination.
* **Nominator Job Title** (`nominator_job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the person nominating the team member.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check the information before submitting the form.
- If you need assistance or have questions, contact your supervisor or HR department.
