# Workplace Safety Innovation Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to nominate a safety innovation from your workplace for recognition. It is meant to gather necessary information to facilitate the evaluation of innovative solutions to workplace safety problems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a clear and concise title for your nomination in the "Nomination Title" field.
2. Enter your company's name in the "Company Name" field.
3. Enter the name of the contact person or individual responsible for the nomination in the "Contact Name" field.
4. Enter your company's email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your company's phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Title** (`award_title`, text, optional): Enter a clear and concise title for your nomination. This field will be used to help the evaluation committee identify and categorize your nomination.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company's name as it appears on their website or business cards.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person or individual responsible for the nomination.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your company's email address. This will be the primary contact method for follow-up information and evaluation committee communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your company's phone number. This will be used for follow-up communication when necessary.
