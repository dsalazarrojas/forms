# Public Speaking Excellence Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Speaking Excellence Award Nomination Form is designed to collect information about an event and its speaker to determine their eligibility for the Public Speaking Excellence Award. This form is used to gather data about the speaker, the event, and the submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the speaker's name in the **Speaker Name** field.
2.  Provide the speaker's affiliation in the **Speaker Affiliation** field.
3.  Enter the title of the event in the **Nomination Title** field.
4.  Enter the title of the speaker's talk in the **Title of Speaker Talk** field.
5.  Enter the date of the event in the **Date of Event** field (if applicable).
6.  Enter the time of the event in the **Time of Event** field (if applicable).
7.  Provide a brief description of the speaker in the **Speaker Description** field (if applicable).
8.  Enter the speaker's email address in the **Email** field.
9.  Enter the speaker's phone number in the **Phone Number** field (if applicable).
10. Enter the submission date in the **Submission Date** field.
11. Enter the title of the event in the **Event Title** field.
12. Enter a brief description of the event in the **Event Description** field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the speaker being nominated.
* **Speaker Affiliation** (`speaker_affiliation`, text, **Required**): Enter the speaker's affiliation or organization.
* **Nomination Title** (`nomination_title`, text, **Required**): Enter the title of the event being nominated.
* **Title of Speaker Talk** (`title_of_speaker_talk`, text, **Required**): Enter the title of the speaker's presentation or speech.
* **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`, date, **Optional**): If available, enter the date of the event. Use the standard date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Time of Event** (`time_of_event`, time, **Optional**): If available, enter the time of the event. Use the 12-hour clock format (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Speaker Description** (`speaker_description`, text, **Optional**): Provide a brief description of the speaker.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **Required**): Enter the speaker’s email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **Optional**): If available, enter the speaker’s phone number.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date you are submitting this nomination.
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, **Required**): Enter the title of the event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, **Optional**): Provide a brief description of the event (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please ensure that each field is filled out correctly and completely.
* Double-check your submission date to ensure it is correct.
* Review the award guidelines to confirm that your nomination meets the criteria.
