# Employee Recognition Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Recognition Award Nomination Form is used to recognize and reward outstanding employees for their hard work and contributions to the team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name**: Enter the first name of the colleague you are nominating.
2. **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the colleague you are nominating.
3. **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the colleague you are nominating.
4. **Nomination Description**: Briefly describe the reason for the nomination, including the colleague's outstanding contributions and achievements.
5. **Recipient Department**: Select the department of the colleague you are nominating.
6. **Recognition Type**: Select the type of recognition award you are nominating the colleague for (e.g. Team Member, Team Leader, Leadership Award, Special Award).
7. **Award Nominator Name**: Enter your name as the person nominating the colleague.
8. **Award Nominator Title**: Enter your title as the person nominating the colleague.
9. **Award Nominator Department**: Select the department of the person nominating the colleague.
10. **Award Nominator Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person nominating the colleague.
11. **Recipient Name**: Enter the name of the colleague being nominated.
12. **Recipient Title**: Enter the title of the colleague being nominated.
13. **Recipient Department**: Select the department of the colleague being nominated.
14. **Reason for Recognition**: Briefly describe the reason for the recognition.
15. **Recognition Date**: Enter the date of the recognition.
16. **Recognition Time**: Enter the time of the recognition.
17. **Recognition Frequency**: Select the frequency of the recognition (e.g. One time, Regular, Periodic).
18. **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
19. **Submitter Title**: Enter your title as the person submitting the form.
20. **Submitter Department**: Select the department of the person submitting the form.
21. **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
22. **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address as the person submitting the form.
23. **Submitter Date**: Enter the date of submission.
24. **Submitter Time**: Enter the time of submission.
25. **Submitter Message**: Leave this field blank for your message to the submitter.
