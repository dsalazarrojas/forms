# CSR Community Impact Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CSR Community Impact Award nomination form is used to submit a nomination for the award. The award recognizes individuals or organizations that have made a significant positive impact in the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address so that we can contact you about your nomination.
2. Describe your relationship with the nominee.
3. Provide the nominee's name, contact email, and category.
4. Summarize the nominee's impact and accomplishments in 2-3 sentences.
5. Provide a detailed description of the nominee's impact and accomplishments.
6. Share any quantitative results achieved by the nominee.
7. Explain how the nominee's impact aligns with the organization's CSR values.
8. Attach any supporting evidence or testimonials.
9. Confirm that the information provided is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (text, required): Your name is required to complete this form.
* **Your Email Address** (email, required): Your email address is required so that we can contact you about your nomination.
* **Relationship to Nominee** (text, required): Describe your relationship with the nominee, such as colleague, friend, or supervisor.
* **Nominee Name** (text, required): The name of the person or organization being nominated.
* **Nominee Category** (select_one, required): Select the category that best fits the nominee: Individual Employee, Department/Team, Community Partner, or Non-profit Organization.
* **Nominee Contact Email** (email, required): The email of the person or organization being nominated.
* **Award Category** (select_one, required): Select the award category that best fits the nominee: Sustainability Champion, Volunteer of the Year, Excellence in Partnership, Innovation in Impact, or Youth Mentorship.
* **Executive Summary** (text, required): Summarize why the nominee deserves the award in 2-3 sentences.
* **Detailed Nomination Narrative** (text, required): Provide a detailed description of the nominee's impact and accomplishments.
* **Quantitative Results** (text, optional): Share any numeric results or metrics that demonstrate the nominee's impact, such as number of people served or hours volunteered.
* **Alignment with Corporate Values** (text, required): Describe how the nominee's impact aligns with the organization's CSR values.
* **Supporting Evidence** (text, optional): Attach any relevant documents or links that support the nomination, such as testimonials or media coverage.
* **Confirm Information Accuracy** (select_one, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate to the best of your knowledge.
* **Date Submitted** (date, required): The date the form was submitted. This field is automatically populated by the system.
