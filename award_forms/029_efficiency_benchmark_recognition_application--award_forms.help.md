# Efficiency Benchmark Recognition Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Efficiency Benchmark Recognition Application form is a tool used to recognize outstanding contributions in different categories. It is used to nominate individuals or teams for their achievements and provide a platform for recognition and reward.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the Efficiency Benchmark Recognition Application Form title and description.
2. Enter the applicant's name.
3. Enter the organization name.
4. Provide nomination details.
5. Upload evidence or provide a URL for evidence.
6. Select a category for the nomination (Finance, Operations, or IT).
7. Briefly describe the category.
8. Confirm the applicant's consent for nomination.
9. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Efficiency Benchmark Recognition Application Form** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the recognition application.
* **Efficiency Benchmark Recognition Application Form** (`form_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the recognition application.
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the applicant being recognized.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization where the applicant works.
* **Nomination Details** (`nomination_details`, text, optional): Provide details about the nomination.
* **Evidence** (`evidence`, text, optional): Upload evidence or provide a URL for evidence related to the nomination.
* **Applicant Consent** (`applicant_consent`, select_one, required): Select "I consent to being nominated for the Efficiency Benchmark Recognition Application".
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the applicant or organization.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the applicant or organization.
* **Date/Time** (`date_time`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the nomination.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required): Select the category for the nomination (Finance, Operations, or IT).
* **Category Description** (`category_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the selected category.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Submit the form.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form. This includes the Category and Applicant Consent fields.
