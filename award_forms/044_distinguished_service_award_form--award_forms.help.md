# Distinguished Service Award Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Distinguished Service Award form is designed to collect information about a deserving individual or team who has provided exceptional service to the organization. This award recognizes outstanding service, leadership, and dedication to the organization's mission and values.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the award nominee's name in the "Nominee name" field (field 3).
2. Provide the nominee's title in the "Nominee title" field (field 4).
3. Enter the nominee's department in the "Nominee department" field (field 5).
4. Enter your name and title in the "Nominator name" field (field 6) and "Nominator title" field (field 7).
5. Enter your department in the "Nominator department" field (field 8).
6. Enter the relationship between you and the nominee in the "Nominator relationship" field (field 13).
7. Enter the date of the nomination in the "Nomination date" field (field 9).
8. Enter your contact email and phone number in the "Nominator contact email" and "Nominator contact phone" fields (fields 19 and 20, respectively).
9. (Optional) Provide additional justification or context for the nomination in the "Justification text" field (field 10).
10. (Optional) Indicate the nominee's relevant experience and award level in the "Nominee relevant experience" and "Nominee award level" fields (fields 11 and 12, respectively).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominee name** (field 3, required): Enter the name of the individual or team being nominated for the award.
* **Nominee title** (field 4, required): Enter the title of the individual or team being nominated.
* **Nominee department** (field 5, required): Enter the department of the individual or team being nominated.
* **Nominator name** (field 6, required): Enter your name as the person making the nomination.
* **Nominator title** (field 7, required): Enter your title.
* **Nominator department** (field 8, required): Enter your department.
* **Nomination date** (field 9, required): Enter the date of the nomination.
* **Nomination date**: Enter the date of the nomination, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Justification text** (field 10, optional): Provide additional context or justification for the nomination.
* **Nominee relevant experience** (field 11, optional): Describe the nominee's relevant experience.
* **Nominee award level** (field 12, optional): Select the award level that best fits the nominee's service.
* **Nominator relationship** (field 13, optional): Describe your relationship with the nominee.
* **Nominator supervisor** (field 14, required): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Nominator supervisor title** (field 15, required): Enter your supervisor's title.
* **Nominator department** (field 16, required): Enter your department.
* **Nominator job function** (field 17, optional): Describe your job function.
* **Nominator work location** (field 18, optional): Enter your work location.
* **Nominator contact email** (field 19, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Nominator contact phone** (field 20, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Nominator note** (field 21, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
