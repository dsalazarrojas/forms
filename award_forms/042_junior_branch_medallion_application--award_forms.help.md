# Junior Branch Medallion Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Junior Branch Medallion Application form is used to collect information from junior members who are applying for a junior branch medallion. The medallion is awarded to recognize and celebrate the achievements of junior members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3.  Enter your age in the "Age" field.
4.  Enter the name of your branch in the "Branch" field.
5.  Enter the year of your application in the "Year" field.
6.  Describe your achievements in the "Achievement" field.
7.  Attach any supporting documents if necessary in the "Supporting Documents" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Age** (number, required): Enter your age.
*   **Branch** (text, required): Enter the name of your branch.
*   **Year** (number, required): Enter the year of your application.
*   **Achievement** (text, required): Describe your achievements.
*   **Supporting Documents** (text, required): Attach any supporting documents if necessary.
