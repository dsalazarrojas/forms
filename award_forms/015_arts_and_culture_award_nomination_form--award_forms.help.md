# Arts And Culture Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Arts And Culture Award Nomination Form is designed to gather information for the nomination process of individuals, groups, or organizations for the arts and culture awards. This form is used to submit a nomination for the awards, and the information collected will be reviewed and considered for selection.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps:

1. Fill in the Nominator Details section, which includes the nominator's full name and email address.
2. Enter the nominee's details, including their full name and category.
3. Provide the reason for the nomination and list the key achievements of the nominee.
4. If applicable, upload supporting materials such as a link to a website or portfolio.
5. Specify your relationship to the nominee (if any).
6. Select the date of submission.
7. Certify that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominator Full Name** (Text, Required): Please enter the full name of the person nominating the award candidate.
* **Nominator Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the person nominating the award candidate.
* **Nominee Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter the full name of the individual, group, or organization being nominated.
* **Award Category** (Select One, Required): Choose the award category that best fits the nominee's achievement. 
  - **Visual Arts Excellence**
  - **Performing Arts Achievement**
  - **Literary Arts Contribution**
  - **Community Cultural Impact**
  - **Emerging Artist Award**
  - **Lifetime Achievement in Culture**
* **Reason for Nomination** (Text, Required): Explain why this nominee deserves the award. Please provide a brief and clear description of their achievement and why they stand out in their field.
* **Key Achievements** (Text, Required): List 3 to 5 key achievements or accomplishments of the nominee that demonstrate their qualifications for the award.
* **Link to Supporting Documentation** (Text, Optional): If applicable, provide a link to a website, portfolio, or other supporting materials that showcase the nominee's work.
* **Relationship to Nominee** (Text, Optional): If you have a relationship with the nominee (e.g. colleague, teacher, friend), describe it here.
* **Date of Submission** (Date, Required): Enter the date you are submitting this nomination.
* **I Certify** (Select One, Required): Select this option to certify that the information provided is accurate.
