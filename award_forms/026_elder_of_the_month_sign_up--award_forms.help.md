The purpose of this form is to nominate an employee for the "Elder Of The Month" award. The "Elder Of The Month" award is given to an employee who has made significant contributions to the team and has demonstrated outstanding performance. This form is used to submit a nomination for a deserving employee.

Before filling out this form, please ensure that you are not duplicating a nomination that has already been made for the same employee. If you are unsure about the employee's status, please check with the internal team to avoid unnecessary work.

## Elder Of The Month Sign Up - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the nomination in the "Nomination" field.
2. Provide the name of the candidate in the "Candidate Name" field.
3. Share a brief story about the candidate's achievements and why they deserve the award in the "Candidate Story" field.
4. Select the date of review from the calendar picker.
5. Choose whether the candidate has been reviewed or not in the "Reviewer" field.
6. Choose whether the candidate has been awarded or not in the "Award Category" field.
7. Select the reason for awarding the candidate in the "Award Reason" field.
8. Enter the date of submission.
9. Enter the name of the person submitting the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination** (text, required): Enter a brief title for the nomination.
* **Candidate Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the candidate.
* **Candidate Story** (note, required): Share a brief story about the candidate's achievements.
* **Review Date** (date, required): Select the date when the review will take place.
* **Reviewer** (select_one, optional): Choose whether the candidate has been reviewed or not.
* **Award Category** (select_one, optional): Choose whether the candidate has been awarded or not.
* **Award Reason** (select_multiple, optional): Select the reason for awarding the candidate.
* **Submit Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitted By** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the nomination.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise title for the nomination.
* Keep the "Candidate Story" field brief and focused on the candidate's achievements.
* Ensure that the "Review Date" is accurate and realistic.
* Be honest in the "Reviewer" field, if the candidate hasn't been reviewed, it's okay to select "No".
* Choose the correct "Award Category" and "Award Reason" based on the candidate's performance.
* Enter the correct "Submit Date" and "Submitted By" fields.
