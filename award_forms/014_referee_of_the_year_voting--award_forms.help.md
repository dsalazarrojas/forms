# Referee Of The Year Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referee Of The Year Voting form is designed to collect ratings and feedback about referees from various categories. This form is used to gather opinions from multiple stakeholders, such as coaches, players, and parents, to identify top-performing referees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each field carefully to understand its purpose and the type of input expected.
* Fill in the required fields (if applicable).
* For fields with options, select the most accurate answer.
* Ensure that you have not already submitted a form for this referee.
* Submit your vote by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the first name of the referee.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the last name of the referee.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the referee on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the referee.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Select a category for the referee from the provided options.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose multiple options from the provided list. (Yes/No)
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the contact email of the referee.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number of the referee.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required/optional): Select one option from the provided list.
* **Select Multiple 2** (`select_multiple2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose multiple options from the provided list. (Yes/No)

## Tips

* Ensure that you have read and understood the form instructions before submitting your vote.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the relevant sports organization or team administrator.
* The feedback and ratings collected will be used to identify top-performing referees and provide feedback for improvement.
