# Teacher Of The Year Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Teacher Of The Year Nomination Form is designed to gather information for nominating a deserving teacher for the Teacher Of The Year award. This form requires information about the teacher being nominated, the student nominating the teacher, and the reason for the nomination. It helps identify the qualities, characteristics, and contributions of the nominated teacher, as well as the nominator's relationship and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the required information for the teacher being nominated, including their first name, last name, title, department, school, and email.
2. Enter the student's name, grade, and email.
3. Describe the reason for the nomination, highlighting the teacher's qualities, characteristics, and contributions.
4. Choose the awards and honors the teacher has received.
5. Select the student's relationship with the teacher (Student, Faculty, or Parent).
6. Enter the nominator's title, email, department, school, and message (if desired).
7. Add any supporting documents or messages from the student (if desired).
8. Select the characteristics and qualities of the nominee from the provided options.
9. Choose the date of the nominee's contribution(s).
10. Provide the nominator's signature, which serves as an electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the first name of the teacher being nominated.
* **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the teacher being nominated.
* **Teacher Title**: Enter the title of the teacher being nominated (e.g., Mr., Ms., Dr., etc.).
* **Department**: Enter the department of the teacher being nominated (e.g., math, science, language arts, etc.).
* **School**: Enter the school where the teacher is employed.
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student nominating the teacher.
* **Student Grade**: Enter the grade level of the student nominating the teacher.
* **Teacher Email**: Enter the email address of the teacher being nominated.
* **Nominee Email**: Enter the email address of the teacher being nominated (this is optional, as it may already be provided by the teacher in the previous field).
* **Reason for Nomination**: Describe the qualities, characteristics, and contributions of the teacher being nominated.
* **Awards and Honors**: Select the awards and honors the teacher has received (e.g., Outstanding Service, Distinguished Service, Meritorious Service).
* **Nominee Qualities**: Select the qualities of the teacher being nominated (e.g., Team Player, Innovative, Supportive, Collaborative).
* **Nominee Characteristics**: Select the characteristics of the teacher being nominated (e.g., Kind, Patient, Supportive, Empowering).
* **Nominee Contribution**: Enter the date of the teacher's contribution(s).
* **Teacher Nominator**: Select the student's relationship with the teacher (Student, Faculty, or Parent).
* **Nominator Title**: Enter the title of the person nominating the teacher.
* **Nominator Email**: Enter the email address of the person nominating the teacher.
* **Nominator Department**: Enter the department of the person nominating the teacher.
* **Nominator School**: Enter the school of the person nominating the teacher.
* **Nominee Suggestions**: Add any additional suggestions or comments for the teacher being nominated.
* **Supporting Documents**: Add any supporting documents or evidence for the nomination.
* **Nominee Awards**: Select the additional awards and honors the teacher has received.
* **Nominee Characteristics**: Select the additional characteristics of the teacher being nominated.
* **Teacher Nominator Signature**: Provide the electronic signature of the person nominating the teacher, which serves as a formal acknowledgment of the nomination.
* **Student Nominator Message**: Add any additional comments or messages from the student nominating the teacher.
* **Student Nominator Signature**: Provide the electronic signature of the student nominating the teacher, which serves as formal acknowledgment of the nomination.
