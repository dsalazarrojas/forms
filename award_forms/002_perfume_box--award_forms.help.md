# perfume_box - Help Guide
## Purpose

The perfume box form is designed to collect information about perfume boxes, including details such as name, email, phone number, payment type, and subscription length. This form is likely used for administrative or sales-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **First Name** field with your name.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the email field.
3. Provide your **Phone** number.
4. Select your preferred **Payment Type** from the dropdown menu.
5. Choose the desired **Subscription Length** from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter your **Perfume Type** in the text field.
7. Provide any additional **Notes** about the perfume box.
8. Enter a **Description** of the perfume box.
9. Select the **Category** of the perfume box.
10. Enter the **Category ID** of the perfume box.
11. Choose the **Subcategory** of the perfume box.
12. Enter the **Subcategory ID** of the perfume box.
13. Enter the **Price** of the perfume box.
14. Enter the **Quantity** of perfume boxes.
15. Select the **Status** of the perfume box.
16. Enter the **Status ID** of the perfume box.
17. Select the shipping details for the perfume box in the **Shipping** field.
18. Enter the **Shipping ID** of the perfume box.
19. Add any **Order Comments** about the perfume box.
20. Enter the **Form ID** of the perfume box.
21. Enter the **Form ID** of the perfume box.
22. Enter the **Created At** date of the perfume box.
23. Enter the **Updated At** date of the perfume box.
24. Enter a **Form Title** for the perfume box.
