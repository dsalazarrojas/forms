# Team Collaboration Award Recommendation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Team Collaboration Award Recommendation Form is designed to allow team members to recognize and appreciate each other's contributions to team projects and goals. It's a way to formally submit nominations for team members who have demonstrated exceptional collaboration skills or have made significant contributions to the team.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, please ensure you are nominating a team member who has demonstrated exceptional collaboration skills or has made significant contributions to the team. To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the award (if applicable).
2. Select the team member you are nominating.
3. Enter a brief description of the team member's contributions.
4. Select whether the team member has made significant contributions.
5. Enter the date when the team member's contributions were made.
6. Select the time when the team member's contributions were made.
7. Select multiple options for the team member's skills or qualities.
8. Enter a note to provide additional context or information.
9. Enter the team member's email for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **None** (Page 1, Field 1): Enter the title of the award if applicable.
* **None** (Page 1, Field 2): You may enter any number to select the team member or enter None for no selection.
* **field_4** (Page 3, Field 1): Enter a brief description of the team member's contributions, e.g., "Has consistently demonstrated exceptional teamwork and communication skills."
* **field_5** (Page 4, Field 1): Select 'Yes' if the team member has made significant contributions.
* **field_6** (Page 5, Field 1): Enter the date when the team member's contributions were made, e.g., "2022-02-15."
* **field_7** (Page 6, Field 1): Select the time when the team member's contributions were made, e.g., 08:00.
* **field_8** (Page 7, Field 1): Select multiple options for the team member's skills or qualities, e.g., "Exceptional teamwork, Communication, Leadership."
* **field_9** (Page 8, Field 1): Enter a note to provide additional context or information about the team member's contributions.
