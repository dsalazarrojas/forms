# Heritage Honoree Nominations - Help Guide
## Purpose
The heritage honoree nominations form is used to collect information about individuals or organizations being nominated for heritage honors or awards. This information will be reviewed and considered by a committee or panel to determine the suitability of the nominees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are required to ensure that the nomination is complete and accurate.
2. For the Category of Honoree field, select the most appropriate option from the list provided.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the Nomination Details field to support the nomination.
4. Enter the Nominator's contact information, including their name, organization, email, and phone number.
5. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Community Nominations**: This is the starting page of the form. Please enter your nomination's name.
* **Name of Honoree**: Enter the name of the individual or organization being nominated.
* **Nomination Title**: Enter a brief title or description of the nomination.
* **Category of Honoree**: Select the most appropriate category from the list provided to describe the honoree's work or achievements.
* **Nominator Name**: Enter the name of the person or organization submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Organization**: Enter the name of the organization or institution submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Email**: Enter the email address of the person or organization submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person or organization submitting the nomination.
* **Nomination Details**: Provide as much detail as possible to support the nomination. This can include a brief description of the honoree's work or achievements, their qualifications, or any other relevant information.

## Tips
* Be as specific and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are complete before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid mistakes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or administrators for assistance.
