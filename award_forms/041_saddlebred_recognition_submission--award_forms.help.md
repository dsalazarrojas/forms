# Saddlebred Recognition Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the Saddlebred Association recognize and celebrate outstanding Saddlebred horses. It collects information about the horse's achievements, owner details, and supporting files.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have all necessary information and supporting documents before starting the submission process.
2. Check our database to see if the horse has already been recognized before submitting this form.
3. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing all required and optional fields.
4. Review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Information** (Owner Information, text, required: false): This field is for the owner's contact information. Please enter your name and other relevant details.
* **Horse Details** (Horse Details, text, required: false): This field is for describing the horse's achievements and characteristics.
* **Achievements Prophets Awards** (Select from: Award, Achievement, Recognition, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more of the available options to describe the horse's achievements.
* **Supporting Files** (Supporting Files, text, required: false): This field is for uploading or referencing any supporting files, such as certificates or photographs.
* **Horse Breed** (Select from: American Saddlebred, American Miniature, Standardbred, select_one, required: false): Choose the correct breed of the horse.
* **Height** (Number, required: false): Enter the horse's height in inches.
* **Color** (Select from: Bay, Black, Blue, Brown, select_one, required: false): Choose the horse's color.
* **Age** (Number, required: false): Enter the horse's age in years.
* **Supporting Files (Repeat)** (Supporting Files, text, required: false): This field is for uploading or referencing any additional supporting files.
* **Horse Name** (Horse Name, text, required: false): Enter the horse's name.
* **Year of Birth** (Number, required: false): Enter the horse's year of birth.

Please note that all fields are optional, but accuracy and completeness are essential for a successful submission.
