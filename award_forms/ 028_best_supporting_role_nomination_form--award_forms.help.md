# Best Supporting Role Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for the purpose of nominating individuals for a supporting role. The supporting role is an award that recognizes and acknowledges the contributions and achievements of individuals who have provided exceptional support to others. The form is used to collect personal and relevant details about the nominee, their selection, and any additional context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your legal documents.
2. Provide a valid email address for contact purposes.
3. If applicable, enter your phone number including the country code.
4. Select the relevant date for the nomination.
5. Choose the primary selection from the available options.
6. If necessary, provide a whole number or rating for the nomination.
7. Add any additional details that are relevant to the nomination.
8. Select any of the options that apply for agreement.
9. If applicable, provide an estimated value for the nomination.
10. Enter a preferred time if scheduling is required.
11. Add any final comments or notes about the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Full name
* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal full name as it appears on your documents.

### 2. Email address
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.

### 3. Phone number
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Include your country code if you are outside the local region.

### 4. Date
* **Date** (`reference_date`, date, required): Select the relevant date for the nomination.

### 5. Primary selection
* **Primary selection** (`primary_selection`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best applies to the nominee.

### 6. Quantity or rating
* **Quantity or rating** (`quantity_or_rating`, number, optional): Provide a whole number or rating for the nomination.

### 7. Additional details
* **Additional details** (`additional_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional relevant information about the nomination.

### 8. Agreement
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, required): Select any of the options that apply.

### 9. Estimated value
* **Estimated value** (`estimated_value`, number, optional): If applicable, provide an estimated value for the nomination.

### 10. Preferred time
* **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Enter a preferred time if scheduling is required.

### 11. Final comments
* **Final comments or notes** (`final_comments`, text, optional): Add any final comments or notes about the nomination.

### 12. Please confirm agreement
* **Please confirm agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, required): This field ensures that you have read and agree with the terms of the nomination.

Please make sure to review all fields carefully and provide accurate information to ensure a successful nomination.
