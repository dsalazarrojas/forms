# District Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The District Award Nomination Form is a tool used to recognize and acknowledge the outstanding achievements and contributions of individuals within the district. This form is used to submit nominations for district awards, which are given to individuals who have demonstrated exceptional performance, leadership, or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the award nomination form.
2. Select the type of award that the nominee is being nominated for (Award for Excellence, Award for Outstanding Achievement, or Other (please specify)).
3. Enter the name of the nominee as it appears in official records.
4. Enter the year the nominee achieved this award.
5. Provide a detailed description of the nominee's achievements and accomplishments.
6. Enter the email address of the nominee.
7. Enter the phone number of the nominee.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.
10. Select the department you are submitting the nomination from (District Management Team, District Operations, Facilities, or Other (please specify)).
11. Enter your name as it appears in official records.
12. Enter your title.
13. Enter your organization (this can be your department or office within the district).
14. Enter any additional information about the nominee's achievement in the notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **District Award Nomination Form**:
	+ This is the title of the form, which should be filled out to provide a clear indication of what this form is for.
* **Award Type**:
	+ This is the type of award that the nominee is being nominated for. Please select from the provided options:
		- Award for Excellence
		- Award for Outstanding Achievement
		- Other (please specify)
* **Recipient Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the individual being nominated. This should be entered as it appears in official records.
* **Award Year**:
	+ Enter the year the award was achieved.
* **Nomination Description**:
	+ Provide a detailed description of the nominee's achievements and accomplishments.
* **Nominee E-mail**:
	+ Enter the email address of the nominee.
* **Nominee Phone**:
	+ Enter the phone number of the nominee.
* **Submitter E-mail**:
	+ Enter the email address of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Submitter Phone**:
	+ Enter the phone number of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Submitter Department**:
	+ Select the department submitting the nomination from:
		- District Management Team
		- District Operations
		- Facilities
		- Other (please specify)
* **Submitter Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the person submitting the nomination as it appears in official records.
* **Submitter Title**:
	+ Enter the title of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Submitter Organization**:
	+ Enter the department or office within the district that the person submitting the nomination belongs to.
* **Nominee's Achievement**:
	+ Provide any additional information about the nominee's achievement in the notes section.
* **Submitter Note**:
	+ Provide any additional comments or information that the submitter would like to include.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form. If you have any questions or need clarification about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact the award committee.
