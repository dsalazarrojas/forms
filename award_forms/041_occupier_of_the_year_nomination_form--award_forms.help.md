# Occupier Of The Year Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit nominations for the "Occupier Of The Year" award, which recognizes outstanding individuals in a specific industry or community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the designated fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Provide a brief description of the individual you are nominating for the award in the Award Nomination field.
4. Specify the reason for your nomination in the Reason for Nomination field.
5. If you have any supporting evidence for your nomination, you can upload it in the Supporting Evidence fields (you can add up to three pieces of evidence).
6. Finally, indicate who nominated the individual for the award in the Nominating Committee Member field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the individual you are nominating.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the individual you are nominating.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your Email address so we can contact you for further information or to confirm your nomination.
* **Award Nomination** (`award_nomination`, text, required): Briefly describe the individual you are nominating for the award.
* **Reason for Nomination** (`reason_for_nomination`, text, required): Explain the reason why you are nominating this individual for the award.
* **Supporting Evidence** (`supporting_evidence`, text, optional): You can upload up to three pieces of supporting evidence to strengthen your nomination.
* **Supporting Evidence 2** (`supporting_evidence_2`, text, optional): Another opportunity to upload supporting evidence for your nomination.
* **Supporting Evidence 3** (`supporting_evidence_3`, text, optional): Add any final pieces of supporting evidence for your nomination.
* **Nominating Committee Member** (`nominating_committee_member`, text, required): Enter your name and position within the nominating committee.
