# Wellness Practitioner Impact Award Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a wellness practitioner who is being nominated for an award. The form is divided into different sections, each with its own purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the nominee.
2. Provide a clear title for the nomination.
3. Enter the nominee's name and email address.
4. Select the type of practitioner the nominee belongs to.
5. Enter the date of the nomination.
6. Fill in the nominator's contact information, including name, email, phone number, and organization.
7. Provide a brief description of the nominee's achievements and services.
8. Optionally, select the location of the practitioner's practice.
9. Add any additional comments or supporting information for the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Title** (`1`, required): A clear and concise title for the nomination, summarizing the purpose and context of the nomination.
* **Nominee Name** (`2`, required): The name of the individual being nominated.
* **Nominee Email** (`3`, required): The email address of the individual being nominated.
* **Practitioner Type** (`4`, optional): Select the type of practitioner the nominee belongs to, such as Practitioner (Wellness Program), Practitioner (Non-Profit or Community Organization), Practitioner (Private Practice), or Practitioner (Academia).
* **Practitioner Location** (`5`, optional): Select the location of the practitioner's practice, such as New York, NY, Los Angeles, CA, Chicago, IL, or San Francisco, CA.
* **Nomination Date** (`6`, required): The date of the nomination, in the format of year, month, and day.
* **Nominator** (`7`, required): The name of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Email** (`8`, required): The email address of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Phone** (`9`, required): The phone number of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Organization** (`10`, required): The organization the nominator belongs to.
* **Nominator Title** (`11`, required): A brief description of the nominator's title or role.
* **Nominator Address** (`12`, optional): The address of the nominator's organization.
* **Nominator City** (`13`, optional): The city of the nominator's organization.
* **Nominator State** (`14`, optional): The state of the nominator's organization.
* **Nominator Zip** (`15`, required): The zip code of the nominator's organization.
* **Nominator Country** (`16`, optional): The country of the nominator's organization.
* **Practitioner Achievements** (`17`, optional): A brief description of the nominee's achievements.
* **Practitioner Services** (`18`, optional): A brief description of the services provided by the nominee.
* **Practitioner Certifications** (`19`, optional): A list of relevant certifications held by the nominee.
* **Nomination Comments** (`20`, required): Any additional comments or supporting information for the nomination.
