# Outstanding Surgeon Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for the Outstanding Surgeon Award Nomination, which aims to recognize and honor exceptional surgeons who have demonstrated remarkable achievements and contributions to the field of surgery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required" in the form) with accurate and detailed information about the nominee.
2. Review and provide supporting evidence for the nomination, if applicable.
3. Ensure that the information provided is accurate and truthful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Form** (`surgeon_nomination`, text, required: false): This section is an optional description of the nomination.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Provide any relevant contact information about yourself, in case the nominee needs to be reached for further inquiries.
* **Nomination Date** (`date_nomination_made`, date, required: false): The date on which this nomination was made.
* **Surgeon Information** (`surgeon_information`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the nominee's work, achievements, and contributions to the field of surgery.
* **Surgeon Name** (`surgeon_name`, text, required: false): The name of the surgeon being nominated.
* **Surgeon Specialty** (`surgeon_specialty`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the nominee's specialty is relevant to the award criteria.
* **Award Nomination Reason** (`award_nomination_reason`, text, required: false): Provide a brief reason for nominating the surgeon.
* **Supporting Evidence** (`supporting_evidence`, text, required: false): Upload supporting evidence for the nomination, such as articles, presentations, or other relevant documents.
* **Supporting Evidence Attachment** (`supporting_evidence_attachment`, select_multiple, required: false): Select 'Yes' if supporting evidence is attached.
* **Nominee Contact Information** (`nominee_contact_information`, text, required: false): Provide the contact information for the nominee, in case they need to be reached for further questions.
* **Name** (`nominee_name`, text, required: false): The name of the nominee.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance.
- Supporting evidence should be attached whenever possible, to provide a comprehensive understanding of the nominee's work and achievements.
