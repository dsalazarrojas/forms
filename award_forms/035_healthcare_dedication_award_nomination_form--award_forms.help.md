# Healthcare Dedication Award Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Healthcare Dedication Award Nomination Form is designed to recognize and appreciate the hard work and dedication of staff members in the healthcare industry. To be eligible, nominations must be submitted by a staff member or their supervisor and include specific information about the nominee's dedication and accomplishments. Nominations will be reviewed for relevance and non-duplication, and only approved nominations will be considered for awards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the staff member you wish to nominate.
2. Enter your name and title as the nominator.
3. Enter your email address and the nominee's email address.
4. Provide a brief description of the reason for the nomination, including the staff member's dedication and accomplishments.
5. Select the award category that best fits the nominee's achievements.
6. Enter the date the nomination was made and the date the form was submitted.
7. Sign and date the form as the nominator.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes you'd like to add as the nominator or as the submission team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Name**: Enter the name of the staff member you wish to nominate. This field is required.
* **Nominator Name**: Enter your name as the person submitting the nomination. This field is required.
* **Nominator Title**: Enter your title as the person submitting the nomination. This field is required.
* **Nominator Email**: Enter your work email address as the person submitting the nomination. This field is required.
* **Staff Email**: Enter the staff member's email address. This field is required.
* **Staff Phone**: Enter the staff member's phone number. This field is required.
* **Reason for Nomination**: Briefly describe the staff member's dedication and accomplishments. This field is required. Please keep your description concise and within a few paragraphs.
* **Award Category**: Select the award category that best fits the staff member's achievements. You can choose from:
	+ Outstanding Service Award
	+ Outstanding Leadership Award
	+ Exceptional Teamwork Award
* **Date of Nomination**: Enter the date the nomination was made. This field is required.
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the date the form was submitted. This field is required.
* **Nominator Signature**: Sign and date the form as the person submitting the nomination. This field is required.
* **Nominator Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes you'd like to add as the nominator. This field is optional.
* **Submission Signature**: Sign and date the form as the submission team. This field is required.
* **Submission Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes you'd like to add as the submission team. This field is optional.
