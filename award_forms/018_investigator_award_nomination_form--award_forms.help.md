# Investigator Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for nominating investigators for awards. It helps gather relevant information to ensure a fair and informed decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the investigator's name in the "Investigator Name" field.

*   Select the type of award the investigator is being nominated for in the "Award Type" field.
*   Select a category for the award in the "Award Category" field.
*   Enter the date of the nomination in the "Nomination Date" field.
*   Provide the reason for the nomination in the "Reason for Nomination" field.
*   Upload supporting files in the "Supporting Files" field.
*   Provide a supporting statement in the "Supporting Statement" field.
*   Enter the nominator's name in the "Nominator Name" field.

*   Upload supporting files in the "Supporting Files" field.
*   Select "Submit" to complete the nomination.
