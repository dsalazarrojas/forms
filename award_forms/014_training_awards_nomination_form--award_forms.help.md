# training_awards_nomination_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The training awards nomination form is used to collect information about an employee's achievements and nominate them for a training award. It is essential to provide accurate and complete details to help the review committee evaluate the nomination.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (Employee Name, Employee Email, Nominator, Nominator Email, Award Description, and Nomination Date) with the correct information.
2. If applicable, select the correct Award Category from the dropdown list.
3. Provide a detailed description of the award-worthy achievement or behavior.
4. Ensure that the submission date and time are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form`, text, required): This is the main section where you can provide a detailed description of the employee's achievements or behaviors that warrant an award.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee being nominated.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the employee being nominated.
* **Award Category** (`award_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best fits the award-worthy achievement or behavior. (Award for Excellence, Award for Innovation, Award for Teamwork)
* **Award Recipient** (`award_recipient`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee receiving the award.
* **Nominator** (`nominator`, text, required): Enter the name of the person nominating the employee.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person nominating the employee.
* **Nominator Phone** (`nominator_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person nominating the employee (if applicable).
* **Award Description** (`award_description`, note, required): Provide a detailed description of the award-worthy achievement or behavior.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the submission was made (if applicable).
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, required): Enter the date the nomination was made.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current status of the submission (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about the submission.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool assigned to the reviewer (if applicable).
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments from the reviewer.
* **Award Recipient Notes** (`award_recipient_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the award recipient.
* **Submission Status Date** (`submission_status_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the submission status was updated (if applicable).
* **Submission Status Time** (`submission_status_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the submission status was updated (if applicable).
* **Submission Date/Time** (`submission_date_time`, text, optional): Enter the date and time the submission was made (if applicable).
* **Submission Status Updated** (`submission_status_updated`, date, optional): Enter the date the submission status was updated (if applicable).
* **Submission Status Updated By** (`submission_status_updated_by`, text, optional): Enter the person who updated the submission status.
* **Submission Status Updated Reason** (`submission_status_updated_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for updating the submission status.
* **Submission Status Comment** (`submission_status_comment`, note, optional): Add any comments about the submission status.

Note: Some fields are optional, but it is highly recommended to fill them in for a more complete understanding of the nomination.
