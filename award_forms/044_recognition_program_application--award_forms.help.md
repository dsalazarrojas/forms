> # Recognition Program Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Recognition Program Application form is designed to collect information and supporting documents from applicants for recognition programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the `user_info` field (page 1).
2. Provide additional application details in the `application_details` field (page 1).
3. Select the supporting documents relevant to your application from the `supporting_documents` field (page 1).
4. Record your submission in the `submission` field (page 1).
5. Provide any additional comments about your application in the `review_comments` field (page 2).
6. Select the decision about your application in the `decision` field (page 2).
7. Enter any additional comments from the reviewer in the `additional_comments` field (page 2).
8. Enter the email of the person submitting the application in the `submitted_by` field (page 2).
9. Enter the date and time of submission in the `submitted_date` and `submitted_time` fields (page 2).
10. Enter the name and role of the reviewer in the `reviewer_name` and `reviewer_role` fields (page 2).
11. Enter the email of the person submitting the application in the `submitted_by_email` field (page 2).
12. Enter the date and time of submission in the `submitted_by_date` and `submitted_by_time` fields (page 2).
13. Enter any additional information about the reviewer in the `reviewer_info` field (page 2).
14. Select the category of the award in the `award_category` field (page 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information.
* **application_details** (`application_details`, text, optional): Describe your application and provide any relevant details.
* **supporting_documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_multiple, optional): Select the supporting documents relevant to your application.
* **submission** (`submission`, text, optional): Record your submission and provide a brief summary.
* **review_comments** (`review_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments about the application.
* **decision** (`decision`, select_one, optional): Select the decision about your application (Approved or Not Approved).
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments from the reviewer.
* **submitted_by** (`submitted_by`, email, required): Enter the email of the person submitting the application.
* **submitted_date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **submitted_time** (`submitted_time`, time, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **reviewer_name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **reviewer_role** (`reviewer_role`, text, required): Enter the role of the reviewer.
* **submitted_by_email** (`submitted_by_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the person submitting the application.
* **submitted_by_date** (`submitted_by_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **submitted_by_time** (`submitted_by_time`, time, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **reviewer_info** (`reviewer_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the reviewer.
* **award_category** (`award_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of the award.
