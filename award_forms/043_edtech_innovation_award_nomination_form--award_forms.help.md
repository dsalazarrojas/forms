# EdTech Innovation Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EdTech Innovation Award Nomination Form is a tool for nominating individuals or projects for recognition and celebration in the field of Education Technology. This form is designed to collect information about the nominee, their work, and their impact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email (if you are nominating someone else, fill in the nominee's information).
2. Select the category that best describes the nominee's work (Technology, Education, Research, or Innovation).
3. Choose the type of nomination (Nomination, Recommendation, or Self-Nomination).
4. If applicable, provide the date and time of the innovative work.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about the nominee's work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name or the nominee's first name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name or the nominee's last name.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email or the nominee's email (if you are nominating someone else).

### Organization
* **Organization** (`prophets`, text, optional): This field is currently mislabeled. The intended label is likely to be "organization" or "affiliation".

### Category
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes the nominee's work (Technology, Education, Research, or Innovation).

### Nomination Type
* **Nomination Type** (`nomination_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of nomination (Nomination, Recommendation, or Self-Nomination).

### Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): If applicable, provide the date of the innovative work.

### Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If applicable, provide the time of the innovative work.

### Note
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the nominee's work.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or context about the nominee's work.
