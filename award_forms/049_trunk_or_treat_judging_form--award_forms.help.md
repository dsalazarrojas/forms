# Trunk Or Treat Judging Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and scores from judges on the quality of decorations, food, entertainment, and overall experience at a Trunk or Treat event. Judges will provide scores and comments for each category, which will help the event organizers evaluate and improve the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information: name and email (though not required, but recommended for contact purposes).
2. Choose a category from the dropdown menu for the overall experience.
3. For each category (decoration, food, and entertainment), provide a brief description and a score (1-10).
4. In the "Comments" field, provide any additional comments or feedback about each category.
5. Review the form to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, select_one, not required): Choose a category for the overall experience.
* **Decoration** (`decoration`, text, not required): Briefly describe the quality of decorations at the event.
* **Food** (`food`, text, not required): Describe the quality of food provided at the event.
* **Entertainment** (`entertainment`, text, not required): Describe the quality of entertainment at the event.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the event.
* **Decoration Score** (`decoration_score`, number, not required): Score the quality of decorations on a scale of 1-10.
* **Food Score** (`food_score`, number, not required): Score the quality of food on a scale of 1-10.
* **Entertainment Score** (`entertainment_score`, number, not required): Score the quality of entertainment on a scale of 1-10.
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, text, not required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the event.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips

* Please be as descriptive as possible in each category to help us understand your feedback better.
* Scores should be based on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, feel free to include them in the "Comments" fields.
