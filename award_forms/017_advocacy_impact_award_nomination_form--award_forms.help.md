# Advocacy Impact Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advocacy Impact Award Nomination Form is designed to help identify and recognize individuals or groups who have made significant contributions to the field of advocacy. The form allows nominators to provide detailed information about the nominee, including their name, contact information, and relationship to the nominee. The form also asks for a description of the nominee's impact, key achievements, and supporting links to further demonstrate their work.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by filling in the nominator's information**: Enter your name and email address in the "Your Name" and "Your Email" fields, respectively. This information will help us contact you if we need more clarification or additional details.
2. **Select the relationship you have with the nominee**: Choose the most accurate description from the list to explain how you know the nominee. For example, if you are a colleague, select "Colleague", if you are a member of the same organization, select "Member of the same organization".
3. **Enter the nominee's information**: Fill in the nominee's name, title/role, and organization. This is the person or group you are nominating for the Advocacy Impact Award.
4. **Choose the award category**: Select the category that best fits the nominee's work. For instance, if the nominee has made a significant impact on policy, select "Policy Change".
5. **Describe the nominee's impact**: Write a 500-word description of the nominee's specific advocacy work and its results. This is your opportunity to tell their story in detail.
6. **List key achievements**: Summarize 3 key accomplishments that demonstrate the nominee's impact.
7. **Optional: Add supporting links**: If you have any online resources, such as news articles or websites, that showcase the nominee's work, you can add them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Your Name (`nominator_name`)

* Your name as the nominator

### Your Email (`nominator_email`)

* Your email address where we can contact you for further questions or clarification

### Relationship to Nominee (`relationship`)

* This explains how you know the nominee (e.g. colleague, member of the same organization)

### Nominee Name (`nominee_name`)

* The name of the person or group being nominated

### Nominee Title/Role (`nominee_title`)

* The title or role of the person or group being nominated

### Nominee Organization (`nominee_organization`)

* The organization the nominee is associated with

### Award Category (`nomination_category`)

* Choose the category that best fits the nominee's work (e.g. policy change, rising star)

### Description of Impact (`impact_description`)

* A detailed description of the nominee's specific advocacy work and its results

### Key Achievements (`specific_achievements`)

* A summary of 3 key accomplishments that demonstrate the nominee's impact

### Links to Supporting Material (`supporting_links`)

* Optional: add online resources that showcase the nominee's work
