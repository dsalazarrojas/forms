# Agricultural Innovation Recognition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on agricultural innovations that deserve recognition. It will help us identify and appreciate innovations that contribute to the progress of agriculture.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the nominee's name and organization in fields 1 and 2.
2. Enter your name and email address in fields 3 and 4.
3. In field 5, enter the title of the innovation being nominated.
4. Select the primary category for the innovation in field 6.
5. Provide a detailed description of the innovation in field 7.
6. Enter the implementation date for the innovation in field 8.
7. If applicable, enter the farm size affected by the innovation in field 9.
8. Select all the areas of impact for the innovation in field 10.
9. Provide measurable outcomes in field 11.
10. Briefly describe the sustainability impact of the innovation in field 12.
11. Choose the scalability of the innovation in field 13.
12. Rate the cost effectiveness of the innovation in field 14.
13. If applicable, select the barriers to wider adoption for the innovation in field 15.
14. Indicate if supporting documentation is available in field 16.
15. Confirm if the innovation has received prior recognition in field 17.
16. Choose whether you have the contact permission to allow us to contact the nominee in field 18.
17. Finally, provide any additional comments in field 19.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nominee Name**: Field 1. Enter the full name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Organization**: Field 2. Enter the farm, company, or institution name where the innovation is implemented.
* **Nominator Name**: Field 3. Enter your full name, the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Email**: Field 4. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Innovation Title**: Field 5. Enter the name of the innovation being nominated.
* **Innovation Category**: Field 6. Select the primary category for the innovation (Sustainable Farming Practices, Technology Integration, etc.).
* **Innovation Description**: Field 7. Provide a detailed description of the innovation.
* **Implementation Date**: Field 8. Enter the date the innovation was implemented.
* **Farm Size Affected**: Field 9. If applicable, enter the number of acres affected by the innovation.
* **Impact Areas**: Field 10. Select all the areas where the innovation has had an impact (Increased Yield, Cost Reduction, etc.).
* **Quantifiable Results**: Field 11. Provide measurable outcomes of the innovation.
* **Sustainability Impact**: Field 12. Briefly describe how the innovation improves sustainability.
* **Scalability**: Field 13. Choose how scalable the innovation is (Highly scalable, Moderately scalable, etc.).
* **Cost Effectiveness**: Field 14. Rate the cost effectiveness of the innovation (Very cost-effective, Neutral, etc.).
* **Adoption Barriers**: Field 15. If applicable, select barriers to wider adoption for the innovation (High initial cost, Technical complexity, etc.).
* **Supporting Documentation**: Field 16. Indicate if supporting documentation is available for the innovation.
* **Previous Recognition**: Field 17. Confirm if the innovation has received prior recognition.
* **Contact Permission**: Field 18. Choose whether you have the permission to allow us to contact the nominee directly.
* **Additional Comments**: Field 19. Provide any additional comments or information about the innovation.
