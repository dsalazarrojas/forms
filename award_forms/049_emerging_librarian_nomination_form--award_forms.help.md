# Emerging Librarian Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emerging Librarian Nomination Form is used to collect information for the Emerging Librarian nomination process. It gathers details about the nominee, nominator, and nomination year to evaluate the nominee's work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the Nomination Title in the designated field. This is the title of the nomination.
2. Fill in the Nominee Name field with the name of the nominated librarian.
3. Enter the Nominee Email in the designated field. This is the email address of the nominated librarian.
4. In the Nominee Institution field, enter the institution where the nominated librarian works.
5. Enter the Nominee Department, if applicable. This is the department where the nominated librarian works.
6. Enter the Nominee Position, if applicable. This is the position held by the nominated librarian.
7. Fill in the Nominator Name field with the name of the nominator.
8. Enter the Nominator Email in the designated field. This is the email address of the nominator.
9. In the Nominator Institution field, enter the institution where the nominator works.
10. Enter the Nominator Position, if applicable. This is the position held by the nominator.
11. Enter the Nomination Year in the designated field. This is the year of the nomination.
12. Attach or link the Supporting Document, as relevant to the nomination.
13. Write a statement explaining why the nominee is being nominated in the Nomination Statement field.
14. Enter the URL of the Supporting Document, if applicable.
15. Enter the Nominator Signature in the designated field. This is the signature of the nominator.
16. Enter the Nominator Phone number, if applicable. This is the contact number of the nominator.
17. Enter the Nominee Phone number, if applicable. This is the contact number of the nominee.
18. Enter the Nominee Email, if applicable. This is the contact email of the nominee.
19. Enter the Nomination Date in the designated field. This is the date of the nomination.
20. Enter any additional comments from the nominator in the Nominator Comment field.
21. Enter the title of the nominator in the Nominator Title field.
22. Enter the title of the nominee in the Nominee Title field.
23. Select the correct Nomination Form Version from the dropdown menu.
24. Enter the name of the Nominator Institution 2, if applicable. This is the second institution of the nominator.
25. Select the correct Nomination Form Version 2 from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Title** (1, text, required): Enter a brief title for the nomination.
* **Nominee Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the librarian being nominated.
* **Nominee Email** (3, email, required): Enter the email address of the librarian being nominated.
* **Nominee Institution** (4, text, required): Enter the institution where the nominated librarian works.
* **Nominee Department** (5, text, optional): Enter the department where the nominated librarian works.
* **Nominee Position** (6, text, optional): Enter the position held by the nominated librarian.
* **Nominator Name** (7, text, required): Enter the name of the person nominating the librarian.
* **Nominator Email** (8, email, optional): Enter the email address of the nominator.
* **Nominator Institution** (9, text, optional): Enter the institution where the nominator works.
* **Nominator Position** (10, text, optional): Enter the position held by the nominator.
* **Nomination Year** (11, number, required): Enter the year of the nomination.
* **Supporting Document** (12, text, required): Attach or link relevant supporting documents.
* **Nomination Statement** (13, note, required): Explain why the nominee is being nominated.
* **Supporting Document URL** (14, text, optional): Enter the URL of the supporting document, if applicable.
* **Nominator Signature** (15, text, required): Enter the signature of the nominator.
* **Nominator Phone** (16, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the nominator.
* **Nominee Phone** (17, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the nominee.
* **Nominee Email** (18, email, optional): Enter the contact email of the nominee.
* **Nomination Date** (19, date, required): Enter the date of the nomination.
* **Nominator Comment** (20, text, optional): Enter any additional comments from the nominator.
* **Nominator Title** (21, text, optional): Enter the title of the nominator.
* **Nominee Title** (22, text, optional): Enter the title of the nominee.
* **Nomination Form Version** (23, select_one, required): Select the correct nomination form version from the dropdown menu.
* **Nominator Institution 2** (24, text, optional): Enter the second institution of the nominator.
* **Nomination Form Version 2** (25, select_multiple, optional): Select the correct nomination form version 2 from the dropdown menu.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in.
* Review the form before submitting to avoid mistakes.
* Attach or link relevant supporting documents.
* Ensure that the nomination year is correct.
* Enter the correct signature and phone number of the nominator and nominee.
* Enter the correct email addresses of the nominator and nominee.
* Select the correct nomination form version.
* Enter any additional comments from the nominator.
