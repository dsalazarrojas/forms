# 2019/20 SLSNSW Awards Of Excellence - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to assist you in completing the 2019/20 SLSNSW Awards Of Excellence nomination form. Please read through the guide carefully before starting to fill out the form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that the nominee meets the eligibility criteria.
2. Fill out the Nomination Details section with the required information about the nominee.
3. Provide a brief nomination statement (200-500 words) explaining why the nominee deserves to be recognized for their achievements.
4. Describe the nominee's key achievements and impact.
5. Select the relevant attributes that the nominee has demonstrated (e.g., Leadership, Community Impact, Skill Excellence).
6. Provide any supporting evidence or links to further support the nomination.
7. If applicable, provide the name and contact information of a referee who can verify the nominee's achievements.
8. Provide an overall recommendation rating (1-10) for the nominee.
9. Confirm whether the nominee consents to public recognition if they are shortlisted.
10. Provide any additional comments or notes that the awards committee should consider.
