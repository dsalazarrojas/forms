**Yarn Awards Nomination Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The form is used to submit nominations for the yarn awards, which recognize excellence in the field of yarns. Fill out the form to help us recognize outstanding contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information (Name, Email, and Phone Number).
2. Choose the category of the nomination (Best Yarn, Best Dye, or Best Yarn Related Product).
3. Enter the contact information of the nominee.
4. Enter a brief statement supporting the nomination.
5. Review the submission carefully before submitting.
