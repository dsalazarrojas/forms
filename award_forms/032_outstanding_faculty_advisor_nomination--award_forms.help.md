# Outstanding Faculty Advisor Nomination - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Outstanding Faculty Advisor Nomination Form is designed to collect information about a faculty member who has demonstrated exceptional guidance, mentorship, or contributions to students or the academic community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the faculty member you wish to nominate.
2. Choose their departmental affiliation(s).
3. Write a brief statement about the nominee's contributions.
4. Provide justification for the nomination.
5. Describe the impact of the nominee's contributions.
6. List the nominee's past awards and honors (if applicable).
7. Add a photo or supporting document (if applicable).
8. Provide the nominator's name, contact information, and institutional affiliation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Faculty Member** (Faculty Member, select_one, **required**): Select the faculty member you wish to nominate from the list of options.
* **Department** (Department, select_multiple, **required**): Choose the department(s) with which the faculty member is affiliated.
* **Narrative** (Narrative, text, **optional**): Write a brief statement about the nominee's contributions. This should be a concise, 100-200 word description of their exceptional performance.
* **Justification** (Justification, text, **optional**): Provide a clear and specific reason why this faculty member is being nominated. Be concise and focus on their exceptional qualities.
* **Impact** (Impact, text, **optional**): Describe an example or specific instance of the impact of the nominee's contributions. This should be a concrete example rather than a general statement.
* **Awards and Honors** (Awards and Honors, text, **optional**): List any notable awards or honors received by the nominee.
* **Photo** (Photo, text, **optional**): Add a photograph of the nominee, if available.
* **Supporting Document** (Supporting Document, text, **optional**): Attach any supporting documents that can further demonstrate the nominee's exceptional performance.
* **Faculty Nominator** (Faculty Nominator, text, **optional**): Enter the name and contact information of the person nominating the faculty member.
* **Nominator Email** (Nominator Email, email, **optional**): Enter the email address of the person nominating the faculty member.
* **Nominator Phone** (Nominator Phone, text, **optional**): Enter the phone number of the person nominating the faculty member.
* **Nominator Institution** (Nominator Institution, text, **optional**): Enter the institution of the person nominating the faculty member.
* **Nominator Department** (Nominator Department, text, **optional**): Enter the department of the person nominating the faculty member.

## Tips

* Be sincere and specific when describing the nominee's exceptional performance.
* Ensure that your nomination is well-supported by concrete examples.
* Proofread your nomination carefully before submitting it.
