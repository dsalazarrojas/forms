# Organization Awards Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organization Awards Nomination Form is used to gather information for internal and external awards nominations. Fill out this form to nominate individuals or teams for recognition of their achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the type of award you are nominating for, either "Industry Award", "Internal Award", or "External Award".
3. Choose the category of the award, either "Team Award", "Individual Award", or "Both".
4. Enter the year the award was achieved, if applicable.
5. Enter the date the award was nominated, if applicable.
6. Provide a description of the award.
7. Add any additional details about the organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization being nominated.
* **Award Type** (`award_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of award you are nominating for, such as "Industry Award", "Internal Award", or "External Award".
* **Award Category** (`award_category`, select_one, required): Choose the category of the award, such as "Team Award", "Individual Award", or "Both".
* **Year of Achievement** (`year_of_achievement`, date, required): Enter the year the award was achieved, if applicable.
* **Award Nomination Date** (`award_nomination_date`, date, required): Enter the date the award was nominated, if applicable.
* **Description of Award** (`description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the award.
* **Organization Details** (`organization_details`, note, required): Add any additional details about the organization being nominated.

## Tips
* Be accurate and complete when filling out the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting. 
* Contact the relevant team if you have any questions or need further clarification.
