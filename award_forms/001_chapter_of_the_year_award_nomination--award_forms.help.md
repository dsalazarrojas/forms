# Chapter Of The Year Award Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chapter Of The Year Award is given to recognize and celebrate the achievements of chapters within the organization. This form is used to collect nomination information and support the evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and fill it out completely.
- Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Nominator Information (Section)
* **Your Full Name** (`nominator_name`, text, required): Please provide your complete name.
* **Your Email Address** (`nominator_email`, email, required): We will use this to confirm your nomination.
* **Your Phone Number** (`nominator_phone`, text, optional): Optional contact number for further communication.
* **Your Chapter Name** (`nominator_chapter`, text, required): Which chapter are you from.
* **Chapter Contact Person** (`nominee_contact_person`, text, required): Primary contact for the chapter.
* **Chapter Contact Email** (`nominee_contact_email`, email, required): Email for the chapter representative.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, required): When did you submit this nomination.
* **Additional Comments or Information** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other details you would like to share.

### Nominee Information (Section)
* **Chapter Name Being Nominated** (`nominee_name`, text, required): Full legal name of the chapter.
* **Chapter Location** (`nominee_location`, text, required): City and state or region of the chapter.
* **Chapter Contact Person** (`nominee_contact_person`, text, required): Primary contact for the chapter.
* **Chapter Contact Email** (`nominee_contact_email`, email, required): Email for the chapter representative.
* **Summary of Nomination** (`nomination_summary`, text, required): Brief overview of why this chapter deserves the award.
* **Primary Achievement Category** (`achievement_category`, select_one, required): What area did the chapter excel in (e.g., Community Impact, Membership Growth, Event Excellence, etc.).
* **Membership Growth Percentage** (`membership_growth`, number, optional): Year-over-year growth in percentages.
* **Key Accomplishments and Highlights** (`key_accomplishments`, text, required): Describe the chapter's major achievements this year.
* **Community Impact Description** (`community_impact_description`, text, optional): How has this chapter made a difference in their community.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_one, optional): Do you have supporting documents such as photos, reports, or testimonials? (Yes/No)
* **Additional Comments or Information** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other details you would like to share.
