# Economic Excellence Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for an Economic Excellence Award Nomination. It is used to gather details about the nominee and the nominator to facilitate the nomination process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Award Category" to determine whether or not to submit a nomination.
2. Fill out the "nominator" fields with your details if you are submitting the nomination. 
   - If you are the nominee, fill out the "nominee" fields with your details.
3. Enter the "Award Year" of the nomination.
4. Complete any additional fields as required and relevant to your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Award Category**: This field determines whether or not to submit a nomination.
  * `Award Category`: <select_one> field, with options "Yes" and "No" - select "Yes" to submit a nomination.
- **Nominator Name**: This field is for your name as the nominator.
  * `nominator_name`: <text> field, enter your full name.
- **Nominator Organization**: This field is for your organization name as the nominator.
  * `nominator_organization`: <text> field, enter your organization name.
- **Nominator Job Title**: This field is for your job title as the nominator.
  * `nominator_job_title`: <text> field, enter your job title.
- **Nominator Email**: This field is for your email as the nominator.
  * `nominator_email`: <email> field, enter your email address.
- **Nominator Phone**: This field is for your phone number as the nominator.
  * `nominator_phone`: <text> field, enter your phone number.
- **Nominator Address**: This field is for your address as the nominator.
  * `nominator_address`: <text> field, enter your address.
- **Submittor Name**: This field is for your name as the submittor.
  * `submittor_name`: <text> field, enter your name.
- **Submittor Organization**: This field is for your organization name as the submittor.
  * `submittor_organization`: <text> field, enter your organization name.
- **Submittor Job Title**: This field is for your job title as the submittor.
  * `submittor_job_title`: <text> field, enter your job title.
- **Submittor Email**: This field is for your email as the submittor.
  * `submittor_email`: <email> field, enter your email address.
- **Submittor Phone**: This field is for your phone number as the submittor.
  * `submittor_phone`: <text> field, enter your phone number.
- **Submittor Address**: This field is for your address as the submittor.
  * `submittor_address`: <text> field, enter your address.
- **Award Year**: This field is for the year of the award.
  * `award_year`: <number> field, enter the year.
- **Nominee Name**: This field is for the nominee's name.
  * `nominee_name`: <text> field, enter the nominee's name.
- **Nominee Organization**: This field is for the nominee's organization.
  * `nominee_organization`: <text> field, enter the nominee's organization name.
- **Nominee Job Title**: This field is for the nominee's job title.
  * `nominee_job_title`: <text> field, enter the nominee's job title.
- **Nominee Email**: This field is for the nominee's email.
  * `nominee_email`: <email> field, enter the nominee's email address.
- **Nominee Phone**: This field is for the nominee's phone number.
  * `nominee_phone`: <text> field, enter the nominee's phone number.
- **Nominee Address**: This field is for the nominee's address.
  * `nominee_address`: <text> field, enter the nominee's address.
- **Supporting Materials**: This field is for any supporting materials that can be provided.
  * `supporting_materials`: <text> field, enter any supporting materials.
- **Supporting Materials URL**: This field is for any URL of supporting materials.
  * `supporting_materials_url`: <text> field, enter the URL.
- **Submittor Notes**: This field is for any additional comments from the submittor.
  * `submittor_notes`: <text> field, enter any additional comments.
- **Submittor Agreed**: This field determines if the submittor agreed to the submission.
  * `submittor_agreed`: <select_one> field, with options "Yes" and "No".
