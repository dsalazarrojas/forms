# Telecommunications Support Excellence Award Nomination - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit a nomination for the Telecommunications Support Excellence Award. The nomination should be specific to a single individual or team within the company's telecommunications support department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information about the nominee.
2. Select the type of award that you believe the nominee deserves from the list.
3. Provide any additional comments or reasons for the nomination in the "Comments" field.
4. Review and confirm the information provided before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form** (`telecomm_support_award_nomination`):
	+ This is the main section of the form where you will provide the details of the nomination.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`):
	+ Enter the name of the individual or team being nominated.
* **Nominee Title** (`nominee_title`):
	+ Enter the title or position of the individual or team being nominated.
* **Department** (`department`):
	+ Enter the department of the individual or team being nominated.
* **Email** (`nominee_email`):
	+ Enter the email address of the individual or team being nominated.
* **Phone** (`nominee_phone`):
	+ Enter the phone number of the individual or team being nominated.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`):
	+ Enter the date of the nomination.
* **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`):
	+ Enter your name and title.
* **Nominator Title** (`nominator_title`):
	+ Enter your title or position.
* **Nominator Department** (`nominator_department`):
	+ Enter your department.
* **Type of Award** (`select_type_of_award`):
	+ Select one of the following types of awards:
		+ Telecommunications Support Excellence Award
		+ Outstanding Service Team Award
		+ Exceptional Service Award
		+ Best of the Best Award
		+ Teamwork Award
		+ Customer Support Team Award
		+ Quality of Work Award
* **Comments** (`comments`):
	+ Provide any additional comments or reasons for the nomination.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information to support the nomination.
* Ensure that the nominee meets the eligibility criteria for the award.
* Review and confirm the information provided before submitting the form.
