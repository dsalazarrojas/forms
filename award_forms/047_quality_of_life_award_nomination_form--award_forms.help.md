# Quality Of Life Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect nominations for the quality of life award. Please provide all necessary details to help us understand the nominee's contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the provided fields and ensure that you have filled in all required fields marked as "Required". You can also add supporting documentation or statements to help us get a better understanding of the nominee's work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Details (Required)**: This section is used to provide a brief summary of the nomination.
* **Nominee Name (Required)**: Please provide the full name of the person being nominated.
* **Contact Email (Required)**: Please provide the email address of the contact person for this nomination.
* **Nominee Category (Required)**: This field is used to determine if the nominee is a 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Supporting Statement (Required)**: This section is used to provide a brief supporting statement from the nominee.
* **Nominee Address (Required)**: Please provide the address of the nominee.
* **Contact Phone (Optional)**: This field is optional and can be used to provide additional contact information.
* **Contact Name (Optional)**: Please provide the name of the contact person.
* **Nominee Position (Optional)**: This field is optional and can be used to provide the position of the nominee.
* **Nominee Company (Optional)**: This field is optional and can be used to provide the company of the nominee.
* **Award Recipient (Optional)**: This field is used to determine if the award is to the nominee or not.
* **Award Year (Optional)**: This field is optional and can be used to provide the year of the award.
* **Submitted By (Required)**: Please provide the name of the person submitting this nomination.
* **Submitter Email (Required)**: Please provide the email address of the submitter.
* **Submitter Name (Required)**: Please provide the name of the submitter.

Note: All required fields are marked as "Required" in the Field-by-Field Explanation section. Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your nomination is properly processed.
