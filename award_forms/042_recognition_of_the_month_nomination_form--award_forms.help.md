# Recognition Of The Month Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Recognition Of The Month Nomination Form is used to recognize and reward employees for their outstanding contributions and achievements within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the nomination form fields with the required information.
2. Choose the correct options for "Nomination form" (Section 2) to select the employee you wish to nominate.
3. Select the nomination date for which you are nominating the employee.
4. Provide the name and email address of the employee being nominated.
5. Enter your name and email address as the nominator.
6. Enter your department name.
7. Describe the reason for nominating the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination form** (Nomination form, select multiple): Select the employee you wish to nominate from the list of options. This is the employee you are nominating for recognition.
* **Nomination date** (Nomination date, date): Enter the date for which you are nominating the employee for recognition. This should be the date of the specific achievement or behavior that you are recognizing.
* **Nominated employee name** (Nominated employee name, text): Enter the name of the employee being nominated. This is the person you are recognizing for their efforts.
* **Nominated employee email** (Nominated employee email, email): Enter the email address of the employee being nominated. This will allow them to receive notifications and updates about their nomination.
* **Nominator name** (Nominator name, text): Enter your name as the person nominating the employee. This is who is recognizing the employee's efforts.
* **Nominator email** (Nominator email, email): Enter your email address as the person nominating the employee. This will allow them to receive notifications and updates about their nomination.
* **Nominator department** (Nominator department, text): Enter your department name. This is the team or division you belong to.
* **Nomination reason** (Nomination reason, text): Provide a brief description of why you are nominating the employee. This should include specific examples or behaviors that demonstrate their outstanding contributions.
