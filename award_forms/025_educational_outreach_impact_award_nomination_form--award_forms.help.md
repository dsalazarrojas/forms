# Educational Outreach Impact Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a school's educational outreach program for the Educational Outreach Impact Award nomination.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out this form by following these steps:
1. Enter the School Program Name.
2. Enter the School Contact Person's name and email.
3. Enter the School Phone number.
4. Enter the Nominee's name and position.
5. Enter the Nominee's contact information.
6. Enter the School Address.
7. Enter the Program Start Date and End Date.
8. Enter the Program Duration.
9. Provide a brief description of the program's impact.
10. Specify the evaluation criteria used to measure the program's impact.
11. Describe the evaluation process used.
12. Provide the nominee's qualifications.
13. Enter the Committee's name and email.
14. Enter the Committee's Phone number and Address.
15. Enter the name and email of the person submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Program Name** (`school_program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school's educational outreach program.
* **School Contact Person** (`school_contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the school's contact person.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): Enter the school's email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Enter the school's phone number.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the nominee.
* **Nominee Position** (`nominee_position`, text, optional): Enter the position of the nominee.
* **Nominee Contact Info** (`nominee_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the nominee.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter the school's address.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the program.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the program.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the program.
* **Impact Description** (`impact_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the impact of the program.
* **Evaluation Criteria** (`evaluation_criteria`, text, optional): Specify the evaluation criteria used to measure the program's impact.
* **Evaluation Process** (`evaluation_process`, text, optional): Describe the evaluation process used.
* **Nominee Qualifications** (`nominee_qualifications`, text, optional): Provide the nominee's qualifications.
* **Committee Recommendation** (`committee_recommendation`, text, optional): Enter the committee's recommendation.
* **School Admin Name** (`school_admin_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school administrator.
* **School Admin Email** (`school_admin_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the school administrator.
* **School Admin Phone** (`school_admin_phone`, text, optional): Enter the school administrator's phone number.
* **Committee Name** (`committee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the committee.
* **Committee Email** (`committee_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the committee.
* **Committee Phone** (`committee_phone`, text, optional): Enter the committee's phone number.
* **Committee Address** (`committee_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the committee.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name and email of the person submitting this form.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If any field is not applicable, please enter "N/A" in the text field.
* If you need help or have any questions, please contact the relevant authorities.
