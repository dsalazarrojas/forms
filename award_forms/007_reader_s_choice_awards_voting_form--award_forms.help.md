# Readers Choice Awards Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Readers Choice Awards Voting Form is used to collect feedback and ratings from readers about a book. This form helps us understand your experience with the book, and your input will contribute to the book's rating and recommendation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each section carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For questions that ask for a number of books read, please enter the total number of books you read in the past year.
3. For questions that ask for a date, please enter the date you finished reading the book.
4. For questions that ask for a time, please enter the total time you spent reading the book.
5. For questions that ask for a selection, please choose all that apply.
6. For questions that ask for a recommendation, please choose only one option.
7. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How many books do you read this year** (`reader_s_choice_1`, `number`, required): Please enter the total number of books you read in the past year.
- **Which category is your favorite** (`reader_s_choice_2`, `select_one`, required): Please choose the category that best describes your favorite type of book.
- **How much of the book did you read** (`reader_s_choice_3`, `number`, required): Please enter the percentage of the book you completed.
- **When did you finish reading the book** (`reader_s_choice_4`, `date`, required): Please enter the date you finished reading the book.
- **How much time did you spend reading the book** (`reader_s_choice_5`, `time`, optional): Please enter the total time you spent reading the book.
- **What did you like most about the book** (`reader_s_choice_6`, `select_multiple`, required): Please choose all that apply from the list of options.
- **What did you dislike most about the book** (`reader_s_choice_7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please choose all that apply from the list of options.
- **Would you recommend this book** (`reader_s_choice_8`, `select_one`, optional): Please choose only one option.
- **Would you recommend this book** (`reader_s_choice_9`, `select_one`, required): Please choose only one option.
- **Any additional comments** (`reader_s_choice_10`, `note`, optional): Please provide any other feedback or comments about the book.
- **Email** (`reader_s_choice_11`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`reader_s_choice_12`, `text`, optional): Please enter your phone number.
- **Your website** (`reader_s_choice_13`, `text`, optional): Please enter your website URL.
- **How much do you like this book** (`reader_s_choice_14`, `number`, optional): Please enter a number from 1 to 10, where 1 is "not at all" and 10 is "very much".
- **How much do you like this book** (`reader_s_choice_15`, `number`, required): Please enter a number from 1 to 10, where 1 is "not at all" and 10 is "very much".
