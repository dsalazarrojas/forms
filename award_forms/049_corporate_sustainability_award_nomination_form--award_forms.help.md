# corporate_sustainability_award_nomination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Sustainability Award Nomination Form is a tool used by the sustainability team to collect and evaluate nominations for corporate sustainability awards. The form is used to gather information on companies that are leaders in corporate sustainability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required"
2. Ensure that you have permission to nominate the company, and that the nominator has the correct contact information.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Page 1, required):
	+ This field is the name of the company being nominated.
* **Award Title** (Page 1, required):
	+ This field is the title of the award being nominated for.
* **Company Type** (Page 1, required):
	+ Select the correct company type from the options provided.
* **Company Size** (Page 1, required):
	+ Select the correct company size from the options provided.
* **Sustainability Category** (Page 1, required):
	+ Select the correct sustainability category that the company falls under.
* **Sustainability Area** (Page 1, required):
	+ Select the correct sustainability area that the company is focusing on.
* **Nominator Name** (Page 2, required):
	+ This field is the name of the person nominating the company.
* **Nominator Email** (Page 2, required):
	+ Enter the email address of the person nominating the company.
* **Nominator Phone** (Page 2, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of the person nominating the company.
* **Nomination Date** (Page 2, required):
	+ Select the date the nomination is being submitted.
* **Submission Date** (Page 2, required):
	+ Select the date the form is being submitted.
	+ This field is used to track when the nomination was submitted.

## Tips
- Double-check all required fields before submitting the form.
- Ensure that the nominator's contact information is accurate. 
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the sustainability team directly for clarification.
