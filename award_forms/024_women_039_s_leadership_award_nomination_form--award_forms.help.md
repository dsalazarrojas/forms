# Women's Leadership Award Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit nominations for the Women's Leadership Award. The purpose of this form is to provide a structured and organized way to gather information about the nominee's achievements and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the accurate and up-to-date information about the nominee.
2. Make sure to provide a clear and concise title for the nomination.
3. Enter the contact information of the nominator.
4. Provide details about the nominee, including their name, organization, phone number, and email address.
5. Select a category that best fits the nominee's achievements.
6. Enter the date of the nomination and the year it relates to.
7. Write a detailed narrative about the nominee's leadership achievements.
8. Sign and date the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Title** (Required): Provide a clear and concise title that best describes the nomination.
* **Nominator Name** (Required): Enter your name and title as the nominator.
* **Nominator Organization** (Required): Enter the name of your organization or institution.
* **Nominator Phone** (Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Nominator Email** (Required): Enter your email address.
* **Nominee Name** (Required): Enter the name of the person you are nominating.
* **Nominee Organization** (Required): Enter the name of the organization or institution the nominee is associated with.
* **Nominee Phone** (Required): Enter the nominee's phone number.
* **Nominee Email** (Required): Enter the nominee's email address.
* **Nomination Category** (Required): Select a category that best fits the nominee's achievements.
* **Nomination Date** (Required): Enter the date of the nomination.
* **Nomination Year** (Required): Enter the year the nomination relates to.
* **Nomination Narrative** (Required): Write a detailed and specific narrative about the nominee's leadership achievements.
* **Nominator Signature** (Required): Sign your name as the nominator.
* **Nominator Date** (Required): Date and sign the nomination.

Note: Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
