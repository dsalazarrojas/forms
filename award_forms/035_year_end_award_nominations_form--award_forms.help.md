# Year End Award Nominations Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit year-end award nominations for employees. A nomination is a formal recognition of an employee's outstanding performance or contributions to the company.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the team that the employee you are nominating is a part of.
2.  Enter the employee's name in the required field.
3.  Briefly describe the reason for the nomination in the provided field.
4.  Choose the type of award you would like the employee to receive.
5.  Select the employee that the award is for.
6.  Enter the date the employee was nominated.
7.  Enter any comments or additional information about the nomination.
8.  Enter the name, title, department, and email of the person submitting the nomination.
9.  Choose the submitter's designation.
10. Select the submitter's department.
11. Select the submitter's position.
12. Enter the submitter's company information.
13. Choose the submitter's designation (if applicable).
14. Select the submitter's department (if applicable).
15. Select the submitter's position (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **award_forms** (Select Multiple): Select the team that the employee you are nominating is a part of. This field is used to narrow down the nomination to a specific team.
*   **employee_name**: Enter the name of the employee you are nominating. This is a required field to ensure that the nomination is for a specific individual.
*   **employee_title**: Enter the employee's title. While not required, this field can be used to provide additional context for the nomination.
*   **nomination_reason**: Briefly explain why you are nominating the employee. This can include their outstanding performance, contributions to the company, or other relevant reasons.
*   **award_type**: Choose the type of award you would like the employee to receive. This will help determine which category the nomination falls under.
*   **award_recipient**: Select the employee that the award is for. This ensures that the nomination is for the correct person.
*   **nomination_date**: Enter the date the employee was nominated. This is used to track when the nomination was submitted.
*   **nomination_time**: Enter the time the employee was nominated. While not required, this can be used to track the exact time of the nomination.
*   **comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about the nomination.
*   **submittor_name**: Enter the name of the person submitting the nomination.
*   **submittor_title**: Enter the title of the person submitting the nomination.
*   **submittor_department**: Enter the department of the person submitting the nomination.
*   **submittor_email**: Enter the email of the person submitting the nomination.
*   **submittor_phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the nomination.
*   **submittor_company**: Enter the company of the person submitting the nomination.
*   **submittor_designation**: Choose the submitter's designation.
*   **submittor_department_name**: Select the submitter's department.
*   **submittor_position**: Select the submitter's position.
*   **submittor_designation_2**: Choose the submitter's designation (if applicable).
*   **submittor_department_2**: Select the submitter's department (if applicable).
*   **submittor_position_2**: Select the submitter's position (if applicable).
*   **submittor_company_2**: Enter the submitter's company information (if applicable).
*   **submittor_designation_3**: Choose the submitter's designation (if applicable).
*   **submittor_department_3**: Select the submitter's department (if applicable).
*   **submittor_position_3**: Select the submitter's position (if applicable).

Note: Some fields are optional, but are still required for accurate data collection and nomination tracking. Ensure all required fields are filled out properly to avoid any issues with the nomination.
