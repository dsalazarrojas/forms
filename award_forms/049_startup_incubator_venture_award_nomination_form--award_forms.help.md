The Startup Incubator Venture Award Nomination Form is a crucial tool for nominating startups for the award, helping the incubator team identify and acknowledge outstanding startups that are pushing the boundaries of innovation in the region.

Before proceeding, please ensure that:

* The nominated startup has not been previously nominated for the same award.
* The nomination meets the eligibility criteria outlined in the award guidelines.

To complete the form correctly, please follow the steps below.

## Purpose
The Startup Incubator Venture Award Nomination Form is designed to gather information about startups that have shown exceptional growth, innovation, and impact in the region. By completing this form, the nomination team is able to evaluate and recognize the most outstanding startups that deserve to be acknowledged.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please fill in the required fields as follows:

1. **Nomination Name**: Enter the name of the startup that you would like to nominate.
2. **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company that owns or is associated with the nominated startup.
3. **Nomination Date**: Select the date on which you nominated the startup (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
4. **Nomination Type**: Choose the category under which you would like to nominate the startup (e.g., Venture Accelerator or Incubator).
5. **Reviewer Score**: Rate the nominated startup based on its performance (1-5).
6. **Reviewer Comment**: Provide a brief statement explaining why the startup deserves the award.
7. **Nomination Status**: Select the current status of the nomination (e.g., New, In Progress, or Awarded).
8. **Reviewer**: Enter the name of the person or team that performed the review.
9. **Reviewer Email**: Enter the email address of the reviewer.
10. **Reviewer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
11. **Reviewer Department**: Enter the department or team that the reviewer belongs to.
12. **Nominee Details**: Provide a brief description of the nominee (e.g., founder, team size, etc.).
13. **Nominee Email**: Enter the email address of the nominee.
14. **Nominee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the nominee.
15. **Nominee Title**: Enter the job title of the nominee.
16. **Nominee Address**: Enter the address of the nominee's company.
17. **Nominee Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the nominee's company.
18. **Nominee City**: Enter the city of the nominee's company.
19. **Nominee State**: Enter the state of the nominee's company.
20. **Nominee Country**: Enter the country of the nominee's company.
21. **Nomination Reason**: Provide a brief description of why the startup deserves the award.
22. **Nominee Company**: Enter the name of the company that owns or is associated with the nominated startup.
23. **Nominee URL**: Enter the URL of the nominee's website.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Name** (`nomination_name`, `text`, required): The name of the startup being nominated.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): The name of the company that owns or is associated with the nominated startup.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, `date`, required): The date on which the nomination was submitted.
* **Nomination Type** (`nomination_type`, `select_one`, optional): The category under which the startup is being nominated (e.g., Venture Accelerator or Incubator).
* **Reviewer Score** (`reviewer_score`, `number`, required): A rating of the nominated startup based on its performance (1-5).
* **Reviewer Comment** (`reviewer_comment`, `text`, optional): A brief statement explaining why the startup deserves the award.
* **Nomination Status** (`nomination_status`, `select_one`, optional): The current status of the nomination (e.g., New, In Progress, or Awarded).
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, `text`, required): The name of the person or team that performed the review.
* **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`, `email`, required): The email address of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone** (`reviewer_phone`, `text`, optional): The phone number of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Department** (`reviewer_department`, `text`, optional): The department or team that the reviewer belongs to.
* **Nominee Details** (`nominee_details`, `text`, optional): A brief description of the nominee (e.g., founder, team size, etc.).
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, `email`, optional): The email address of the nominee.
* **Nominee Phone** (`nominee_phone`, `text`, optional): The phone number of the nominee.
* **Nominee Title** (`nominee_title`, `text`, optional): The job title of the nominee.
* **Nominee Address** (`nominee_address`, `text`, optional): The address of the nominee's company.
* **Nominee Zip Code** (`nominee_zip_code`, `number`, optional): The zip code of the nominee's company.
* **Nominee City** (`nominee_city`, `text`, optional): The city of the nominee's company.
* **Nominee State** (`nominee_state`, `text`, optional): The state of the nominee's company.
* **Nominee Country** (`nominee_country`, `text`, optional): The country of the nominee's company.
* **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, `text`, optional): A brief description of why the startup deserves the award.
* **Nominee Company** (`nominee_company`, `text`, optional): The name of the company that owns or is associated with the nominated startup.
* **Nominee URL** (`nominee_url`, `text`, optional): The URL of the nominee's website.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the award guidelines or seek help from the incubator team.
* The reviewer's email address and phone number are required for follow-up purposes.
* The reviewer's department is optional and should be left blank if not applicable.
