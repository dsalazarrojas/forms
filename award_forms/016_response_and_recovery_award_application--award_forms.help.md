# Response And Recovery Award Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Response And Recovery Award Application form is used to nominate an organization or individual for an award in recognition of their response to or recovery from a challenging situation. This form is used to gather details about the nominee and the award recipient, to facilitate the award decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Response And Recovery Award Application Form** (page 1) with a brief summary of the nominee's actions and impact.
2. Provide a detailed **Nomination Description** (page 2) to further explain the nominee's actions and their significance.
3. Indicate whether **Supporting Documents** are required (page 3) and upload any relevant documents if necessary.
4. Fill out the **Organization Details** section (pages 4-6), including:
	* **Organization Name**
	* **Organization Address**
	* **Contact Name**
	* **Contact Email**
	* **Contact Phone** (if necessary)
5. Fill out the **Award Recipient Details** section (pages 7-9), including:
	* **Committee Name**
	* **Committee Address**
	* **Committee Email**
	* **Award Recipient Name**
	* **Award Recipient Title**
	* **Award Recipient Organization**
	* **Award Recipient Address**
	* **Award Recipient Country**
	* **Award Recipient Region**
	* **Award Recipient Postal Code** (if necessary)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Response And Recovery Award Application Form**:
	+ This is the starting point for your nomination. Please provide a brief summary of the nominee's actions and impact.
* **Nomination Description**:
	+ This is a more detailed description of the nominee's actions and their significance.
* **Supporting Documents**:
	+ Indicate whether supporting documents are required and upload them if necessary.
* **Organization Details**:
	+ **Organization Name**: The name of the organization being nominated.
	+ **Organization Address**: The address of the organization being nominated.
	+ **Contact Name**: The name of the contact person for the organization.
	+ **Contact Email**: The email of the contact person for the organization.
	+ **Contact Phone** (if necessary): The phone number of the contact person for the organization.
* **Award Recipient Details**:
	+ **Committee Name**: The name of the committee or organization awarding the recognition.
	+ **Committee Address**: The address of the committee or organization awarding the recognition.
	+ **Committee Email**: The email of the committee or organization awarding the recognition.
	+ **Award Recipient Name**: The name of the person or organization receiving the award.
	+ **Award Recipient Title**: The title of the person or organization receiving the award.
	+ **Award Recipient Organization**: The organization receiving the award.
	+ **Award Recipient Address**: The address of the organization receiving the award.
	+ **Award Recipient Country**: The country where the award recipient organization is located.
	+ **Award Recipient Region**: The region where the award recipient organization is located.
	+ **Award Recipient Postal Code** (if necessary): The postal code of the award recipient organization.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by the nominator, and the award recipient will not need to fill out any part of this form.
