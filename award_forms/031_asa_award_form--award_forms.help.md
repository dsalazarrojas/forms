# ASA Award Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The ASA Award Form is designed to collect information for award nominations within the organization. This form helps to streamline the nomination process, ensuring that all necessary details are captured and considered for the award evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form accurately and completely**. Each field is essential for a thorough evaluation of the nominee.
2. **Use clear and concise language** when answering the questions. Avoid using jargon or overly complex explanations.
3. **Be specific** about the nominee's achievements and impact on the team or organization.
4. **Attach any supporting evidence** if available, such as certificates, letters, or documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Nominator Name**: Enter your name as the person nominating the award recipient.
* **2. Nominator Email**: Provide your email address for communication and follow-up regarding the award nomination.
* **3. Nominee Name**: Enter the name of the person being nominated for the award.
* **4. Nominee Department**: Specify the department where the nominee works.
* **5. Award Category**: Select the award category that best suits the nominee's achievements.
	+ Options: "Employee of the Year", "Innovation Excellence", "Team Collaboration", "Customer Service", "Leadership", "Safety Champion", "Other"
* **6. Nomination Reason**: Describe in a few sentences the reasons why you're nominating this person for the award.
* **7. Specific Achievements**: Provide detailed descriptions of the nominee's specific achievements and accomplishments.
* **8. Impact on Team/Organization**: Explain how the nominee has positively impacted the team or organization.
* **9. Duration of Contribution**: Specify how long the nominee has been making these contributions.
* **10. Peer Recognition**: Indicate whether the nominee has received recognition from peers or other departments.
	+ Options: "Yes", "No", "Unknown"
* **11. Previous Awards**: If the nominee has received previous awards, describe them briefly.
* **12. Supporting Evidence**: Attach any relevant documentation or evidence to support the nomination (optional).
* **13. Nomination Date**: Enter the date of the nomination.

## Tips

* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to reach out to [contact email].
* Review your nomination carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Ensure that the nominee is aware of the nomination and is comfortable with it.
