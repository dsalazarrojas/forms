# Employee Of The Month Award Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to nominate an employee for the Employee of the Month award. This form will be used by management to gather information about the reasons why an employee should be considered for this recognition. The award is given to employees who consistently show excellent performance, outstanding contributions, or exceptional service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide a brief nomination title**:
	* Enter a clear and concise title that summarizes the reasons why you're nominating the employee for the Employee of the Month award.
2. **Enter employee information**:
	* Enter the name of the employee you are nominating.
3. **Specify the nomination date**:
	* Select the date when the nominee's outstanding performance or contribution was recognized.
4. **Describe the nomination reason**:
	* Give a brief explanation of why the employee deserves the award.
5. **Provide additional context**:
	* Enter any additional details or circumstances that support the nomination.
6. **Submit the nomination**:
	* Once you have completed all the fields, submit the form for review and consideration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### employee_nomination
* **Nomination Title**: Enter a brief title that summarizes the reasons why you're nominating the employee for the award.
*   Type: text
*   Required: no

### employee_name
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee you are nominating.
*   Type: text
*   Required: no

### nomination_date
* **Nomination Date**: Select the date when the employee's outstanding performance or contribution was recognized.
*   Type: date
*   Required: no

### nomination_reason
* **Nomination Reason**: Enter a brief explanation of why the employee deserves the award.
*   Type: text
*   Required: no

### nomination_description
* **Additional Context**: Enter any additional details or circumstances that support the nomination.
*   Type: text
*   Required: no

### nominator_name
* **Nominator's Name**: Enter your name and title/position.
*   Type: text
*   Required: no

## Tips

* Make sure to provide specific examples and details to support your nomination.
* Keep the explanation concise and to the point.
* Be fair and unbiased in your nomination.
* Only nominate employees who you have worked with or have direct knowledge of their performance.
