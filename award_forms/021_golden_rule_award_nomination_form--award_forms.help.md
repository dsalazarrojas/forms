### Golden Rule Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golden Rule Award Nomination Form is a tool to help recognize individuals who embody the Golden Rule of our organization. This form is used to collect nominations from colleagues, peers, and team members who believe an individual deserves this award.

Before starting, please take a moment to review the form to ensure it's not already been submitted for this nominee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Award Nomination" page.
2. Provide a numerical value for the "Award Nomination" page.
3. Enter the name of the individual you would like to nominate.
4. Describe why this individual deserves the award, using the "Reasons" field.
5. Select the appropriate submission option from the "Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Award Nomination (1)
* **Award Nomination** (`award_nomination`, `number`, required/optional): This field is a numerical value that indicates the level of award nomination, such as 1-3. Enter a number from 1 to 3.
*   **Note:** This field is required, so please ensure it is filled in before proceeding.

### Nominee (2)
* **Nominee** (`nominee`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the individual you would like to nominate.
*   **Note:** While not required, it's essential to provide the nominee's name for proper recognition.

### Reasons (3)
* **Reasons** (`reasons`, `text`, optional): Describe why the individual deserves the Golden Rule award. Be as detailed as possible, explaining their actions and behaviors that demonstrate the Golden Rule.

### Submit (4)
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required): Choose the submission option that best aligns with your nomination. Options are:
	+ Option 1: Submit nomination
	+ Option 2: Review and edit nomination
	+ Option 3: Discard nomination

### Tips
*   Take your time to provide a thorough and well-written description in the "Reasons" field, as this will help the review committee make an informed decision.
*   Be sure to review the form for accuracy before submitting.
