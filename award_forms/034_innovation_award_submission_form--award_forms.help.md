# Innovation Award Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Innovation Award Submission Form is designed to collect necessary information from individuals or companies seeking to submit their innovative projects or ideas for a particular award. This form serves as a centralized platform to gather details about the submission, ensuring that all relevant information is captured.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by filling in the required fields, which are indicated by the "required" tag in the field labels. Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
2.  Review each field carefully to ensure that you are providing the correct type of information. For instance, if a field is marked as a "text" type, provide written answers or descriptions. If a field is marked as "note," use this space to add any additional comments or notes about your submission.
3.  Attach any relevant supporting materials or documents to the "Submission Attachments" field, if applicable.
4.  Double-check your form before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled in correctly and all required information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Info** (`general_info`, text, required: false): This field is where you can provide a brief introduction or overview of your innovative project or idea.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your name, organization, and email address, so that the submission review committee can get in touch with you with any questions or clarifications.
*   **Innovation Info** (`innovation_info`, text, required: false): This field is for providing detailed information about your innovative project or idea, including its features, benefits, and any other relevant details.
*   **Award Info** (`award_info`, text, required: false): If your project or idea is related to a specific award, provide the relevant details about the award, including its name, category, and any other related information.
*   **Submission Info** (`submission_info`, text, required: false): Describe your submission in a few sentences, providing context about why it's innovative, what problem it solves, or how it meets the award criteria.
*   **Submission Attachments** (`submission_attachments`, text, required: false): Attach any documents, videos, or images that support your submission.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Once you have filled in all the required fields, click "Submit" to send your completed form to the review committee.
*   **Review Notes** (`review_notes`, note, required: false): Use this field to add any additional comments or notes for the review committee, if applicable.

## Tips

*   Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all necessary fields are filled in correctly.
*   Provide all required information for the submission to be processed efficiently.
*   Double-check that you are submitting a unique and relevant idea or project.
*   Attach any relevant supporting materials or documents to your submission.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification about any field, do not hesitate to contact the form creator at <form@gic.mx>.
