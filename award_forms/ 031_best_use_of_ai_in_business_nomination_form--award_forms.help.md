# Best Use Of AI In Business Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect nominations for the "Best Use Of AI In Business" award, recognizing outstanding examples of AI innovation and impact in the business sector.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and email address to identify yourself as the nominee or the individual submitting the nomination.
2. Provide a valid date to select the relevant period for the nomination.
3. Choose the primary selection from the options provided.
4. Enter a whole number if applicable, or provide any additional information.
5. Confirm agreement to terms and conditions by selecting at least one of the options.
6. Optionally provide an estimated value or preferred time for scheduling.
7. Add any final comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Full name
 * **Full name**: A field for your legal name.

### Email address
 * **Email address (Email)**: A field for a valid contact email.

### Phone number
 * **Phone number**: A field for your phone number, including the country code if outside the local region.

### Date
 * **Date**: A field for selecting the relevant date.

### Primary selection
 * **Primary selection**: A field to choose the option that best applies from the options provided.

### Quantity or rating
 * **Quantity or rating (Quantity or rating)**: A field to provide a whole number if applicable.

### Additional details
 * **Additional details**: A field for providing any additional relevant information.

### Please confirm agreement
 * **Please confirm agreement**: A field to select at least one option that applies.

### Estimated value
 * **Estimated value**: A field to enter an estimated value in decimal format.

### Preferred time
 * **Preferred time**: A field to enter a time if scheduling is required.

### Final comments
 * **Final comments or notes**: A field for providing optional closing remarks.

**Tips**

* Please provide accurate and concise information for each field.
* You can provide additional details about the nominee or your selection.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed to avoid delays in processing the nomination.
