# Book Award Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Book Award Application form is designed to collect information for a book award submission. The application is open to students and academics who have written or published books in specific categories.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields:
    * Full Name: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the award certificate.
    * Student ID Number: Enter your university or school ID number.
    * Email Address: Enter the email address you want to receive updates about your application.
    * Department or Major: Enter the academic department or major you are associated with.
    * Academic Level: Select your current academic level (e.g., Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, Graduate Student, or Doctoral Candidate).
    * Book Title: Enter the title of the book you are submitting.
    * Book Author: Enter the author of the book.
    * Award Category: Select the category that best fits your work (e.g., Fiction, Non-Fiction, Poetry, Academic Research, or Creative Writing).
    * Abstract or Summary: Write a brief 250-word summary of your work.
    * Current GPA: Enter your current grade point average.
    * Previous Awards: Select "True" if you have received any literary awards before.
    * Personal Statement: Explain why you deserve this award.
    * Submission Date: Enter the date of application.
2. If applicable, fill out the following non-required fields:
    * Faculty Sponsor Name: Enter the name of the faculty sponsor associated with your work.
    * Previous Award Details: If you have received literary awards, list them here.
3. Confirm your submission:
    * Writing Sample Attached: Confirm that you have attached your writing sample.
    * I Agree to Terms and Conditions: Select "Yes I agree" to confirm that all information is accurate.
