# Employee Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Nomination Form is designed to collect information about employees who deserve recognition and rewards for their outstanding work. It helps in acknowledging and appreciating their efforts, promoting a positive work environment, and encouraging team members to contribute towards a culture of appreciation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's unique ID number.
2. Provide the name of the person nominating the employee.
3. Enter the email address of the nominator.
4. Optionally, include the phone number of the nominator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's unique identification number, if available. This will help in verifying their identity and ensuring only one nomination per employee is processed.
* **Nominator Name**: Provide the name of the person submitting the nomination, as well as the name of the employee being nominated.
* **Nominator Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the nomination, to facilitate further communication and follow-up actions.
* **Nominator Phone**: Provide the phone number of the nominator, if available, to ensure easy contact for any further questions or actions related to the nomination.

## Tips
* Verify that the employee has not been previously nominated before submitting a new nomination.
* Only submit a new nomination if it's a genuine and new recognition opportunity.
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure the nomination process is smooth and efficient.
