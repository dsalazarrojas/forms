# pharmaceutical_innovation_award_nomination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pharmaceutical Innovation Award nomination form is used to collect information and supporting documents for the Pharmaceutical Innovation Award nomination process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information and documents.
2. Fill in the Nomination Details section with the award recipient's name and any other relevant details.
3. Select the Award Category that best fits the nomination.
4. Enter the Award Recipient's Email address.
5. Provide the Name of the Contact Person responsible for the nomination.
6. Enter the Contact Email and Phone number.
7. Write a brief Nomination Reason explaining why the award recipient deserves the award.
8. Attach supporting documents as required.
9. Set the Submission Date and Time.
10. Select the Submission Status.
11. Add any Submission Comments.
12. Attach any additional files or notes as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Details**: Enter the award recipient's name and any other relevant details.
* **Award Category**: Select the category that best fits the nomination from the list of options: Innovation in Drug Development, Innovation in Manufacturing, or Innovation in Medical Devices.
* **Award Recipient Name**: Enter the name of the award recipient.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company or organization associated with the award recipient.
* **Award Recipient Email**: Enter the email address of the award recipient.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the nomination.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Nomination Reason**: Write a brief explanation of why the award recipient deserves the award.
* **Supporting Documents**: Attach any supporting documents as required.
* **Submission Date**: Set the date of submission.
* **Submission Time**: Set the time of submission.
* **Submission Status**: Select the current status of the submission from the options: Pending, Approved, or Rejected.
* **Submission Comments**: Add any additional comments about the submission.
* **Submission Date Time**: Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Submission Notes**: Attach any additional notes or information as necessary.
* **Submission Attachments**: Attach any additional files or notes as necessary.
* **Attachments Count**: Enter the number of attachments.
* **Attachment Type**: Select the type of attachment from the options: Image, Document, Video, or Audio.
* **Attachment URL**: Enter the URL of the attachment.
* **Attachment Size**: Enter the size of the attachment.
* **Attachment MIME Type**: Select the MIME type of the attachment from the options: application/pdf, application/msword, application/vnd.openxmlformats-officedocument, image/jpeg, image/png, application/x-zip.
* **Attachment Description**: Enter a brief description of the attachment.
* **Attachment Tags**: Enter any tags or keywords for the attachment.
* **Attachment Comment**: Enter any additional comments about the attachment.
