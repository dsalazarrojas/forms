# Gaming Industry Developer Award Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a nomination for the "Gaming Industry Developer Award." This award recognizes outstanding contributions and achievements in the gaming industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Nomination form" field with a detailed description or nomination details.
2. If you have any supporting materials, such as documents or links, you can upload them in the "Supporting materials" field. This is optional.
3. Select one of the three options for the "Submit" field:
	* "Submit" to send the nomination for review.
	* "Save" to save the nomination without sending it.
	* "Cancel" to start over.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination form** (`nomination_form`, `text`, required): A detailed description or nomination details for the individual or team being nominated. Please provide as much information as possible.
* **Supporting materials** (`supporting_materials`, `text`, optional): Any supporting documents or links that can further explain the nomination.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required): Select "Submit" to send the nomination for review, "Save" to save the nomination without sending it, or "Cancel" to start over.
