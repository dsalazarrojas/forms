# Student Award Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information for a student award recommendation.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
- Enter the student's name and email address in the "Student Information" section.
- Enter the nominator's name, email address, and phone number in the "Nominator Information" section.
- Enter the award name, description, and select the award recipient(s) in the "Award Information" section.
- Enter any additional comments or remarks about the student in the "Reviewer Comments" section.
- Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`student_information`, text, optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Last Name** (`student_last_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's last name.
* **Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Enter the student's address.
* **Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Nominator Information** (`nominator_information`, text, optional): Enter your name as the nominator.
* **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the nominator.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as the nominator.
* **Nominator Phone** (`nominator_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as the nominator.
* **Comments** (`nominator_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the student.
* **Signature** (`nominator_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date the form to confirm your nomination.
* **Award Name** (`award_name`, text, optional): Enter the award name.
* **Description** (`award_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the award.
* **Recipients** (`award_recipients`, select_one, optional): Select the award recipient(s), i.e., "First Name" or "Last Name" or "Both".
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the reviewer.
* **Review Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the student as the reviewer.

## Tips
- Double-check the student's and nominator's contact information for accuracy.
- Ensure the award description is clear and concise.
- Be specific about why you think the student deserves the award.
- Proofread carefully before signing and submitting the form.
