# Theatrical Design Excellence Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theatrical Design Excellence Award Nomination Form is designed to help the nominator nominate individuals for a Theatrical Design Excellence Award. This form will ask for details about the nominee, including their name, email, phone number, and institution, as well as details about the nomination itself, such as category, subcategory, and supporting materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the required fields with your name and contact information.
2. Select the nomination category and subcategory that best fit your nominee.
3. Provide details about the nominee's work, including their role in the production.
4. If you would like to provide supporting materials, such as photos or videos, you can do so in the "Supporting Materials" field.
5. Finally, add any additional comments about the nomination, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Date** (1, date): The date of the nomination. This is a required field, so make sure to fill it out accurately.
* **Theatre Name** (2, text): The name of the theatre or production that the nominee worked on. This is a required field.
* **Nomination Year** (3, number): The year of the nomination. This is a required field.
* **Nomination Category** (4, select_one): Choose the category that best fits the nominee's work. You can select from Nomination Category 1, Nomination Category 2, or Nomination Category 3.
* **Nomination Subcategory** (5, text): A more specific subcategory that further describes the nominee's work. Please specify a subcategory, this field is required.
* **Nominator Name** (6, text): The name of the person nominating the individual. This is a required field.
* **Nominator Email** (7, email): The email address of the nominator. This is a required field.
* **Nominator Phone** (8, text): The phone number of the nominator. This is a required field.
* **Nominator Institution** (9, text): The institution or school that the nominator is affiliated with. This is a required field.
* **Nominator Role** (10, text): The role of the nominator in the production. This is a required field.
* **Supporting Materials** (11, text): If you would like to provide additional information or materials to support the nomination, you can do so here.
* **Nomination Details** (12, text): Any additional details about the nomination you would like to provide. This field is optional.
* **Nominator Comments** (13, text): Any additional comments about the nomination or nominee. This field is optional.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use a clear and concise format for your answers.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
