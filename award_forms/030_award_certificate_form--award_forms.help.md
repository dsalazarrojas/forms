# Award Certificate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to create a personalized award certificate for a recipient, allowing you to recognize and celebrate their achievements and milestones. It gathers essential information to create a customized certificate that can be delivered digitally or printed and mailed physically.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the recipient's full name as it should appear on the certificate.
2. Provide the recipient's email address for digital delivery of the certificate.
3. Select the type of award and its title to describe the achievement or accomplishment.
4. Specify the reason for the award, briefly describing the reason for the recognition.
5. Enter the date the award was given.
6. Provide the issuing institution or organization's name and department responsible for the award.
7. Enter the name and title of the person presenting the award.
8. Choose the desired format for the certificate (digital, physical, or both).
9. Select a certificate template style to match your preference.
10. If applicable, provide the mailing address for the physical certificate.
10. If desired, include any special instructions or customizations for the certificate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recipient Full Name**: Enter the name of the person receiving the award, as it should appear on the certificate.
* **Recipient Email Address**: Enter the recipient's email address for digital delivery of the certificate.
* **Type of Award**: Select the type of award from the provided options (e.g. Excellence, Service, Completion).
* **Award Title**: Enter the official name of the award.
* **Reason for the Award**: Briefly describe the reason for the award.
* **Date of the Award**: Enter the date the award was given.
* **Issuing Institution or Organization**: Enter the full name of the company or school issuing the award.
* **Issuing Department**: Enter the department responsible for the award (e.g. HR, Sales, Academic Affairs).
* **Presenter Name**: Enter the name of the person signing or presenting the award.
* **Presenter Title**: Enter the title of the person presenting the award.
* **Certificate Format**: Choose how you would like the certificate to be delivered (Digital PDF Only, Physical Print Only, or Both).
* **Certificate Template Style**: Select the visual theme of the certificate (Classic and Traditional, Modern and Bold, Minimalist and Clean, or Elegant and Ornate).
* **Mailing Address for Physical Copy**: Enter the address to which the physical certificate should be mailed, if applicable.
* **Special Instructions or Customizations**: Enter any special wording or logo requests for the certificate, if desired.
