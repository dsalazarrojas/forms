# Data Solutions Excellence Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Solutions Excellence Award is an annual recognition of outstanding individuals or teams that have made significant contributions to the field of data solutions. The purpose of this form is to submit a nomination for an individual or team that has demonstrated exceptional data-driven solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your name and contact information in the **Nominator Details** section.
2. Provide the name of the individual or team being nominated in the **Nominator Name** field.
3. Enter the date of the event or project for which you are nominating the individual or team in the **Nomination Date** field.
4. Select the category that best describes the award nominator category in the **Award Nominator Category** field.
5. Choose the relationship between you and the individual or team being nominated in the **Nominator Relationship** field.
6. Provide a description of the award nominator in the **Award Nominator Description** field.
7. Enter the URL of the award nominator's image in the **Award Nominator Image URL** field.
8. Enter the URL of the award nominator's organization in the **Award Nominator URL** field.
9. Describe the achievements of the individual or team being nominated in the **Award Nominator Achievements** field.
10. Enter any additional details about the achievements in the **Award Nominator Achievements Details** field.
11. Enter the name of the individual or team's organization in the **Nominator Organization** field.
12. Enter the URL of the individual or team's organization in the **Nominator Organisation URL** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominator Details**: Please fill in your name and contact information for the purpose of this nomination.
* **Nominator Name**: The name of the individual or team being nominated.
* **Nomination Date**: The date of the event or project for which you are nominating the individual or team.
* **Award Nominator Category**: The category that best describes the award nominator.
* **Nominator Relationship**: The relationship between you and the individual or team being nominated.
* **Award Nominator Description**: A description of the award nominator.
* **Award Nominator Image URL**: The URL of the award nominator's image.
* **Award Nominator URL**: The URL of the award nominator's website.
* **Nominator Achievements**: A description of the achievements of the individual or team being nominated.
* **Award Nominator Achievements Details**: Additional details about the achievements of the individual or team being nominated.
* **Award Nominator Organisation**: The name of the individual or team's organization.
* **Nominator Organisation**: The name of your organization.
* **Nominator Organisation URL**: The URL of your organization.
* **Award Nominator Organisation URL**: The URL of the award nominator's organization.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly, as the award committee will be using this information to review and consider your nomination.
