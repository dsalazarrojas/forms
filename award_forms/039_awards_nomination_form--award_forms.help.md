# Awards Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Awards Nomination Form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to nominate someone for an award. The form helps to ensure that all necessary details are provided to make a well-rounded nomination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal information in the **Nominator Information** section, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter the nominee's full name in the **Nominee Full Name** field.
3. Provide the nominee's email address and phone number in the **Nominee Email Address** and **Nominee Phone Number** fields, respectively.
4. Enter the nominee's affiliation or organization in the **Nominee Affiliation or Organization** field.
5. Describe your relationship with the nominee in the **Describe your relationship with the nominee** field.
6. Choose the most relevant award category in the **Award Category** dropdown menu.
7. Provide a detailed reason for nominating the individual in the **Detailed Nomination Reason** field.
8. If applicable, add a supporting document or portfolio link in the **Supporting Document or Portfolio Link** field.
9. Select the **Date of Nomination** from the calendar.
10. Choose whether to keep the nomination confidential or share your name with the nominee.
11. Agree to the nomination terms and conditions by selecting **Yes, I agree** in the **I agree to the nomination terms and conditions** field.
12. Finally, sign the form by typing your full name in the **Digital Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominator Full Name**: This is where you enter your own full name to be associated with the nomination.
* **Nominator Email Address**: This is your email address where the nomination will be sent to.
* **Nominator Phone Number**: This is your phone number where the nomination will be sent to.
* **Nominee Full Name**: Enter the full name of the person you are nominating.
* **Nominee Email Address**: Enter the email address of the person you are nominating.
* **Nominee Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person you are nominating.
* **Nominee Affiliation or Organization**: Enter the affiliation or organization of the person you are nominating.
* **Describe your relationship with the nominee**: Describe your relationship with the person you are nominating, e.g. colleague, manager, community member.
* **Award Category**: Choose the award category that best fits the nominee's achievements.
* **Detailed Nomination Reason**: Write a detailed explanation of why you are nominating this person.
* **Supporting Document or Portfolio Link**: Attach any supporting documents or links to a portfolio that supports the nomination.
* **Date of Nomination**: Select the date you are making this nomination.
* **Is this nomination confidential?**: Choose whether to keep the nomination confidential or share your name with the nominee.
* **I agree to the nomination terms and conditions**: Select **Yes, I agree** to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
* **Digital Signature**: Type your full name to sign the nomination form.
