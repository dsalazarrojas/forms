# Digital Pedagogy Innovation Award Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Pedagogy Innovation Award Registration Form is designed to collect information and applications for the award. This form helps the applicant to register for the award by providing details about the project. By filling out this form, applicants demonstrate their project meets the criteria for the award.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through the following steps:

1. Enter the title of your project in the "Project Title" field.
2. Enter the name of the school in the "school_name" field.
3. Choose the award category that best describes your project from the "Award Category" field.
4. If your project has team members, list them in the "Team Members" field.
5. Enter the location of your project in the "Project Location" field.
6. Upload any supporting files for your project in the "Submission File" field.
7. List the outcomes of your project in the "Project Outcomes" field.
8. If you have additional outcomes to upload, enter them in the "Project Outcomes Uploads" field.
9. Check if your project has been evaluated by someone else in the "Project Evaluated By" field.
10. Enter the start date of your project in the "Project Start Date" field.
11. Enter the completion date of your project in the "Project Completion Date" field.
12. Check if your project has ended in the "Project Ended By" field.
13. Enter any other award category that does not exist in the "Award Category Other" field.
14. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
15. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
16. Enter the name of your institution in the "Institution Name" field.
17. Enter the URL of your institution in the "Institution URL" field.
18. Enter the address of your institution in the "Institution Address" field.
19. Upload any other supporting file in the "Submission File" field.
20. Enter the date of submission in the "Submission Date" field.
21. Enter your name in the "Submitter Name" field.
22. Enter your title in the "Submitter Title" field.
23. Enter your email address in the "Submitter Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school you are representing.
* **Award Category** (`award_category`, select_one, required): Choose the award category that best describes your project.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): List the names of team members who contributed to the project.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the project.
* **Submission File** (`submission_file`, text, optional): Upload any supporting files for the project.
* **Project Outcomes** (`project_outcomes`, select_multiple, optional): List the outcomes of your project.
* **Project Outcomes Uploads** (`project_outcomes_uploads`, text, optional): Upload any additional outcomes.
* **Project Evaluated By** (`project_evaluated_by`, select_one, required): Check if your project has been evaluated by someone else.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project Completion Date** (`project_completion_date`, date, required): Enter the completion date of your project.
* **Project Ended By** (`project_ended_by`, select_one, required): Check if your project has ended.
* **Award Category Other** (`award_category_other`, text, optional): Enter any other award category that does not exist.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your institution.
* **Institution URL** (`institution_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your institution.
* **Institution Address** (`institution_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your institution.
* **Submission File** (`submission_file`, text, optional): Upload any supporting file.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Title** (`submitter_title`, text, required): Enter your title.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
