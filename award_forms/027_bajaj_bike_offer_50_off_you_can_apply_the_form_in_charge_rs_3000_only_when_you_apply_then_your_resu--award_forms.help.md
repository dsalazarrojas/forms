# Bajaj Bike Offer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants interested in purchasing Bajaj bikes. The form will be used to assess the applicant's eligibility for financing or purchase and to provide personalized pricing offers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand the questions asked.
2. Fill out all required fields marked as required.
3. Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information.
4. Review your application before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, required/required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, required/required): Enter your active email address that you check regularly.
* **Contact Number** (`applicant_phone`, required/required): Enter your active phone number that you can be reached at.
* **Full Address** (`applicant_address`, required/required): Enter your current address as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **City** (`city`, required/required): Enter the name of the city where you reside.
* **Preferred Bajaj Bike Model** (`preferred_model`, required/required): Select your preferred Bajaj bike model from the available options.
* **Model Variant or Color Preference** (`model_variant`, not required/optional): If you have any specific model variant or color preferences, enter them here.
* **Purchase Method** (`purchase_method`, required/required): Select the method of purchase that you prefer.
* **Valid Driving License Number** (`driving_license`, required/required): Enter your valid driving license number.
* **Aadhar Number** (`aadhar_number`, not required/optional): Enter your Aadhar number for identity verification.
* **PAN Number** (`pan_number`, not required/optional): Enter your PAN number for identity verification.
* **Application Submitted Successfully** (`application_confirmation`, required/required): Confirm that your application has been submitted successfully.
* **Extended Warranty Interest** (`warranty_preference`, not required/optional): Indicate your interest in extended warranty.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, required/required): Accept or decline the terms and conditions of the offer.
* **Other preferences** (`delivery_preference`, not required/optional): If you have any other preferences, enter them here.

Note: The form may be subject to change or updated at any time without prior notice. It is essential to review and understand each field before filling it out.
