# Excellence Awards Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Excellence Awards Survey is a form used to collect details about companies participating in the awards. Please complete this form to provide accurate and complete information about your company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of your company (Company, Agency, or Individual).
2. Enter your company's name.
3. Enter your company's website (optional).
4. Enter your email address (this will help us to contact you).
5. Enter your company's phone number (optional).
6. Enter your company's address (optional).
7. Enter your representative's name (optional).
8. (You do not need to fill out this field for Individual companies, as it is not relevant.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Type** (Company, Agency, Individual):
	+ This field is used to categorize your company's type.
* **Company Name**:
	+ Enter the name of your company.
* **Company Website**:
	+ If you have a website, enter the URL here.
* **Email**:
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Company Phone**:
	+ If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Company Address**:
	+ If you have a physical address, enter it here.
* **Company Representative Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the representative who will receive communications.
* **Role**:
	+ This field is not currently used in the survey, so it can be left empty.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your company's information for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
