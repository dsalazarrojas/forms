# Research Methodology Innovation Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate individuals or teams for the Research Methodology Award.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the nominee details:
	* If the nominee is a individual or a team, answer "Yes" to the first question.
2. Enter the nominee's details:
	* Nominee First Name
	* Nominee Last Name
	* Nominee Organization
3. Enter the nominator's details:
	* Nominator First Name
	* Nominator Last Name
	* Nominator Title
	* Nominator Email
	* Nominator Phone
4. Select the award(s) the nominee is being nominated for:
	* Research Methodology Award
	* Research Excellence Award
	* Innovation Award
	* Impact Award
	* Other (please specify)
5. Select the nomination status:
	* Active
	* Inactive
6. Select the nomination committee:
	* Yes (if you are a member of the nomination committee)
7. If you are a member of the nomination committee, enter your contact details:
	* Nomination Committee Member
	* Nomination Committee Email
	* Nomination Committee Phone
8. Enter the nomination message:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Details** (`research_methodology_award_nominations`): This field allows you to select whether the nominee is an individual or a team.
* **Nominee First Name** (`nominee_first_name`): Enter the first name of the nominee.
* **Nominee Last Name** (`nominee_last_name`): Enter the last name of the nominee.
* **Nominee Organization** (`research_methodology_award_nominations_organization`): Enter the organization or institution the nominee is affiliated with.
* **Nominator First Name** (`award_nominator_first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Nominator Last Name** (`award_nominator_last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Nominator Title** (`award_nominator_title`): Enter your title or position.
* **Award Nomination Year** (`research_methodology_award_nominations_year`): Enter the year the award nomination is for.
* **Award Nominator Email** (`award_nominator_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Award Nominator Phone** (`award_nominator_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Award Nomination Description** (`research_methodology_award_nominations_description`): Enter a brief description of the nominee's work or research.
* **Awards Nominated For** (`research_methodology_awards_nominated_for`): Select the award(s) the nominee is being nominated for.
* **Nomination Status** (`research_methodology_nomination_status`): Select the nomination status (Active or Inactive).
* **Nomination Committee** (`research_methodology_nominations_committee`): Select whether you are a member of the nomination committee.
* **Nomination Committee Member** (`research_methodology_nominations_committee_member`): If you are a member of the nomination committee, enter your name.
* **Nomination Committee Email** (`research_methodology_nominations_committee_email`): If you are a member of the nomination committee, enter your email address.
* **Nomination Committee Phone** (`research_methodology_nominations_committee_phone`): If you are a member of the nomination committee, enter your phone number.
* **Nomination Message** (`research_methodology_nominator_message`): Enter a brief message or justification for the nomination.
