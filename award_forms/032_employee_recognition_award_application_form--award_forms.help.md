# employee_recognition_award_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect employee recognition and award applications. It gathers information about proposed awards or recognitions for outstanding work or contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the Award Application field (Page 1) with a brief description of the award or recognition you're applying for.
2. Select the Category for the award or recognition from the available options (Page 2).
3. Provide a detailed Description of the award or recognition (Page 3).
4. Enter the Department related to the award or recognition (Page 4).
5. Add Department Details, if necessary (Page 5).
6. Upload any relevant File Uploads, if applicable (Page 6).
7. Provide Nomination Details, if relevant (Page 7).
8. Enter Recognition Details, if required (Page 8).
9. Finally, Submit the form to complete the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Award Application** (`Award Application`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the award or recognition you're applying for. This could be a proposed award name, a description of the award, or any other relevant information.
* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, optional): Select the category for the award or recognition from the available options: Award, Cash Award, Gift, or Service.
* **Description** (`Description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the award or recognition, including any specific criteria or qualifications it meets.
* **Department** (`Department`, text, optional): Enter the department related to the award or recognition. This will help the nominator or reviewer understand the context and relevance of the award.
* **Department Details** (`Department Details`, text, optional): Add any additional information or context about the department related to the award or recognition.
* **File Uploads** (`File Uploads`, text, optional): Upload any relevant files, documents, or attachments related to the award or recognition. This could include supporting documents, references, or evidence.
* **Nomination Details** (`Nomination Details`, text, optional): Provide any additional nomination details, such as nomination rationale or supporting statements.
* **Recognition Details** (`Recognition Details`, text, optional): Enter any recognition details, such as award criteria or evaluation criteria.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, optional): Review and submit the form once all necessary information has been provided.
