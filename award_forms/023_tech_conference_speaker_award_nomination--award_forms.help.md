This help guide is intended to be a reference for end-users who are submitting nominations for the Tech Conference Speaker Award. Before submitting a nomination, please ensure that you have not already submitted a nomination for the same individual in the past. If you are unsure, you can check the internal nomination records to avoid duplication.

The form is designed to gather information about the nominee and the nominator. It is essential to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that the nomination is processed correctly. Here is a step-by-step guide to completing the form:

## Purpose
The Tech Conference Speaker Award is an annual recognition of outstanding speakers who have delivered exceptional talks at our tech conference. The purpose of this form is to submit a nomination for an individual who deserves this recognition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Speaker Name field with the name of the individual you are nominating.
2. Enter the nominee's Email address in the Email field.
3. Select the Nomination Category that best describes the nominee's contribution.
4. Provide a brief explanation of why you are nominating this individual in the Nomination Reason field.
5. Fill in the Nominee Name field with the name of the individual you are nominating.
6. Enter the Nominee Title field with the title of the individual you are nominating.
7. Choose the Conference Year when the nominee delivered their exceptional talk.
8. If applicable, provide the Nominee Organization where the individual works or represented.
9. If applicable, provide the Nominee LinkedIn, Twitter, Website, or Facebook profile links.
10. Fill in the Nominator Name field with your name.
11. Enter your Email address in the Nominator Email field.
12. Enter your Title field with your title.
13. Enter your Organization field with the organization you represent.
14. If you want to provide additional information, enter it in the Nominator Message field.
15. Finally, confirm that you agree with the terms of the nomination by selecting the correct option in the prophets Agreement field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Name**: Enter the name of the individual you are nominating.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the individual you are nominating.
* **Nomination Category**: Select the category that best describes the nominee's contribution.
* **Nomination Reason**: Provide a brief explanation of why you are nominating this individual.
* **Nominee Name**: Enter the name of the individual you are nominating.
* **Nominee Title**: Enter the title of the individual you are nominating.
* **Conference Year**: Choose the year when the nominee delivered their exceptional talk.
* **Nominee Organization**: If applicable, provide the organization where the individual works or represents.
* **Nominee LinkedIn**: If applicable, provide the individual's LinkedIn profile link.
* **Nominee Twitter**: If applicable, provide the individual's Twitter profile link.
* **Nominee Website**: If applicable, provide the individual's personal or professional website.
* **Nominee Facebook**: If applicable, provide the individual's Facebook profile link.
* **Nominator Name**: Enter your name.
* **Nominator Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Nominator Title**: Enter your title.
* **Nominator Organization**: Enter the organization you represent.
* **Nominator Phone**: If you want to provide additional contact information.
* **Nominator Message**: If you want to provide additional information, enter it here.
* **prophets Agreement**: Select the option that best represents your agreement to the terms of the nomination.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact our support team.
* We appreciate your participation in the Tech Conference Speaker Award process!</thinking>
