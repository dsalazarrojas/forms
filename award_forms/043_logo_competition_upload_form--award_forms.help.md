# Logo Competition Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Logo Competition Upload Form is designed to collect logo design submissions from participants in a logo competition. This form allows participants to upload their design file and provide additional information about themselves and their design.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Upload your logo design file in the "Participant Design File" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
5. Provide a brief description of your design in the "Design Description" field (optional).
6. Enter any additional contact information in the "Contact Information" field (optional).
7. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your design.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Design File**: Upload your logo design file (.jpg, .png, etc.).
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your design submission.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for additional contact (optional).
* **Design Description**: Provide a brief description of your design, including any design inspiration or process used (optional).
* **Contact Information**: Enter any additional contact information, such as a website or social media handles (optional).
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your design and complete the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and valid contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* Use a clear and high-quality image of your design for the best chance of winning.
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise description of your design.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
