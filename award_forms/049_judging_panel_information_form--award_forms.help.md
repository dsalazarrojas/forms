# Judging Panel Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about the judging panel, including event details and panel member information. 

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
- Start with the Event Name field, which is the title of the event being judged. 
- Next, move on to select the type of event from the list of options. 
- Choose the date and time of the event. 
- Enter the contact name, email, and phone number of the person responsible for the event. 
- Add any additional notes about the event or panel. 
- If a judge is involved, provide their name, email, and phone number. 
- Finally, select the members of the judging panel from the list of options, and enter the number of panel members.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, required/optional): Enter the title of the event being judged.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, required/optional): Select the type of event from the list of options. 
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, required/optional): Choose the date of the event. 
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, required/optional): Choose the time of the event. 
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for the event. 
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the person responsible for the event. 
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the event. 
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the event or panel. 
* **Judge's Name** (`judge's_name`, required/optional): Enter the name of the judge involved. 
* **Judge's E-mail** (`judge's_email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the judge involved. 
* **Judge's Phone** (`judge's_phone`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the judge involved. 
* **Judging Panel Members** (`judging_panel_members`, required/optional): Select the members of the judging panel from the list of options. 
* **Judging Panel Members Count** (`judging_panel_members_count`, required/optional): Enter the number of panel members.
