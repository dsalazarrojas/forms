# Financial Innovation Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Innovation Award Nomination Form is designed to collect information about innovative financial projects or products that demonstrate exceptional financial innovation. This form is used to identify and recognize outstanding achievements in the financial industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of your nomination in the "Nomination Details" field.
2. Enter the name of the company associated with the innovation (if applicable).
3. Select the category that best describes the innovation from the "Nomination Category" dropdown menu.
4. Choose any relevant subcategories from the "Nomination Subcategory" dropdown menu (select multiple if applicable).
5. If necessary, provide a description of the file you are uploading (in the "File Description" field).
6. Attach any relevant files to support your nomination (in the "File Upload" field).
7. Enter the contact information for the person or organization submitting the nomination, including their email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Details** (1, Text, Required)
	+ A brief description of your financial innovation that demonstrates its impact and relevance to the industry.
* **Company Name** (2, Text, Optional)
	+ The name of the company associated with the innovation (if applicable).
* **Nomination Category** (3, Select One, Required)
	+ The category that best describes your innovation (e.g., "Digital Payment Solutions" or "FinTech").
* **Nomination Subcategory** (4, Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ Any relevant subcategories that apply to your innovation (e.g., "Mobile Payment" or "Blockchain").
* **Description** (5, Text, Optional)
	+ A more in-depth description of your innovation, including its features and benefits.
* **File Upload** (6, Text, Optional)
	+ Attach any relevant files, such as images, videos, or documents, to support your nomination.
* **File Description** (7, Text, Optional)
	+ A brief description of the file you are uploading.
* **Judging Team** (8, Text, Optional)
	+ The team of individuals responsible for developing or implementing the innovation.
* **Contact Email** (9, Email, Required)
	+ The email address of the person or organization submitting the nomination.
* **Contact Phone** (10, Text, Optional)
	+ The phone number of the person or organization submitting the nomination.
