# Nomination Form For Deserving Individual - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about an individual who deserves recognition or an award. This form is designed to help gather details about the nominee's background, achievements, and reasons for nomination.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, start by providing your name and contact information.
*   Then, move on to the "Nomination Form" page, where you can add any relevant details about the individual being nominated.
*   Next, go to the "Nomination Details" page, where you can provide more specific information about the individual's background and achievements.
*   On the "Nominee Bio" page, add a brief description of the individual's bio, including their relevant skills, experience, and accomplishments.
*   Proceed to the "Nomination Date" page, where you can select the date of the nomination.
*   On the "Nomination Reason" page, provide a clear and concise explanation of why you are nominating this individual.
*   Finally, on the "Supporting Documents" page, you can add any relevant supporting documents or evidence that demonstrate the individual's qualifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Nomination Form** (`Nomination Form`, text, required): A text field where you can enter any relevant details about the individual being nominated.
    *   Please provide a brief description of the individual's background, including their job title, department, and any relevant experience or achievements.
*   **Nomination Details** (`Nomination Details`, text, required): A text field where you can provide more specific information about the individual's background and achievements.
    *   Please include any relevant skills, experience, and accomplishments that demonstrate the individual's qualifications.
*   **Nominee Bio** (`Nominee Bio`, note, required): A text field where you can add a brief description of the individual's bio.
    *   Please include their name, job title, department, and a brief description of their relevant skills, experience, and accomplishments.
*   **Nomination Date** (`Nomination Date`, date, required): A date field where you can select the date of the nomination.
    *   Please select the date on which you are nominating the individual.
*   **Nomination Reason** (`Nomination Reason`, text, required): A text field where you can provide a clear and concise explanation of why you are nominating this individual.
    *   Please explain why you are nominating this individual, including any specific achievements or qualifications that make them deserving of recognition.
*   **Supporting Documents** (`Supporting Documents`, text, optional): A text field where you can add any relevant supporting documents or evidence that demonstrate the individual's qualifications.
    *   Please attach any relevant documents or evidence that support the individual's nomination.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you are clear and concise in your answers to each field.
*   Proofread your answers carefully before submitting the form.
*   Make sure you are not duplicating information that is already known to the organization.
*   If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please contact [support email].
