# Outstanding Young Alumni Recognition Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outstanding Young Alumni Recognition Nomination form is used to collect information about the achievements and contributions of young alumni who have made a significant impact in their field or community. This form will be reviewed by a panel of judges to select the outstanding young alumnus who will receive recognition for their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the nomination form and start filling out the fields as instructed below.
2. Provide all required information to ensure that the nomination is complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form`, text): This is a text field where you can write your nomination letter.
* **Nominee** (`name_of_nominee`, text): Please provide the name of the young alumnus you are nominating.
* **University Affiliation** (`university_affiliation`, text): Indicate the university or institution the nominee is affiliated with.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, text): If available, please provide the student ID number of the nominee.
* **Degree Date** (`degree_date`, date): If the nominee has completed their degree, please provide the date of graduation.
* **Award Name** (`award_name`, text): If the nominee has received a specific award, please provide its name.
* **Award Year** (`award_year`, number): If the nominee received an award in a specific year, please provide that year.
* **University** (`university`, text): This is the university or institution the nominee is affiliated with.
* **Faculty Department** (`faculty_department`, text): Indicate the department of the nominee's faculty member.
* **Faculty Member Name** (`faculty_member_name`, text): Please provide the name of the faculty member who nominated or is aware of the nominee's achievements.
* **Faculty Title** (`faculty_title`, text): If available, please provide the faculty member's title or position.
* **Nominee Address** (`nominee_address`, text): Please provide the address of the nominee.
* **Nominee Phone** (`nominee_phone`, text): If available, please provide the nominee's phone number.
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, email): Please provide the email of the nominee.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Check your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant department or faculty member for assistance.
