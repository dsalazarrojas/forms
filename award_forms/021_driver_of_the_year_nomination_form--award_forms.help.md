# Driver Of The Year Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Of The Year Nomination Form is a tool for nominating deserving colleagues for recognition and awards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the nominee's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the nominee's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Select a title for the nominee from the "Title" drop-down menu.
4. If you wish to include the nominee's department, enter it in the "Department" field.
5. Enter the nominee's job title in the "Job Title" field (optional).
6. Enter the nominee's email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter the nominee's phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Enter the nominee's title in the "Nominee Title" field.
9. Enter the nominee's first name in the "Nominee First Name" field.
10. Enter the nominee's last name in the "Nominee Last Name" field.
11. Enter the number of years the nominee has been with the company in the "Years Experience" field (optional).
12. Select the nominee's department from the "Nominee Department" drop-down menu (optional).
13. Enter a brief explanation for why you are nominating the employee in the "Why Nominate" field.
14. Enter the nominee's phone number in the "Nominee Phone" field.
15. Enter your name in the "Nominator Name" field.
16. Enter your email address in the "Nominator Email" field.
17. Enter your phone number in the "Nominator Phone" field.
18. If you wish to include any additional comments or notes about the nominee, enter them in the "Nominee Note" field (optional).
19. Select a title for yourself from the "Nominator Title" drop-down menu.
20. Enter your first name in the "Nominator First Name" field.
21. Enter your last name in the "Nominator Last Name" field.
22. Select your department from the "Nominator Department" drop-down menu (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the first name of the employee being nominated.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the last name of the employee being nominated.
* **Title** (`title`, `select_one`, required): Select a title for the nominee from the provided options.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter the department of the employee being nominated.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the job title of the employee being nominated.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the employee being nominated.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the employee being nominated.
* **Nominee Title** (`nominee_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the employee being nominated.
* **Nominee First Name** (`nominee_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the first name of the employee being nominated.
* **Nominee Last Name** (`nominee_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the last name of the employee being nominated.
* **Years Experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of years the employee has been with the company.
* **Nominee Department** (`nominee_department`, `select_one`, optional): Select the department of the employee being nominated.
* **Why Nominate** (`why_nominate`, `text`, required): Enter a brief explanation for why you are nominating the employee.
* **Nominee Phone** (`nominee_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the employee being nominated.
* **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Nominator Phone** (`nominator_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Nominee Year** (`nominee_year`, `number`, optional): Enter the year the employee began working with the company (optional).

* **Nominee Time** (`nominee_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the employee is normally available (optional).
* **Nominee Note** (`nominee_note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the employee (optional).
* **Nominator Title** (`nominator_title`, `text`, required): Select a title for yourself from the provided options.
