# Inclusive Excellence Award Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Inclusive Excellence Award Application Form" is designed to collect information and supporting documents from applicants for award applications. It's a comprehensive form that helps applicants showcase their skills, experience, and achievements in a specific category.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by filling out the Award Form Data section with any relevant information about your award application.
*   Provide your name and contact email address in the Applicant Name and Contact Email fields.
*   Enter your contact phone number in the Contact Phone field.
*   Select the Award Category and Award Subcategory that best fit your application from the respective dropdown menus.
*   Provide detailed information about your project in the Project Details field.
*   Attach a cover letter or resume that highlights your achievements and qualifications.
*   Upload any supporting documents that demonstrate your expertise and skills.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Award Form Data** (`award_form_data`, text, optional): This field is for any relevant information about your award application. Please provide a brief description of your application.
*   **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name as it should be displayed on the award.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, number, optional): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
*   **Award Category** (`award_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best fits your award application. Please choose one from the dropdown menu.
*   **Award Subcategory** (`award_subcategory`, select_multiple, optional): If your application falls under multiple subcategories, select all that apply. You can choose one or more from the dropdown menu.
*   **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your project, including any relevant context or background information.
*   **Application Cover Letter** (`application_cover_letter`, text, optional): Attach a cover letter that highlights your achievements and qualifications. This is your opportunity to introduce yourself and your project.
*   **CV/Resume** (`cv_resume`, text, optional): Attach a copy of your resume or CV that showcases your skills and experience.
*   **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Upload any additional documents that support your application, such as publications, certifications, or references.
