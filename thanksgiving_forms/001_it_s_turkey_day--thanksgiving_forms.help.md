# Its Turkey Day Thanksgiving Forms - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to capture details about a Thanksgiving dinner and its related activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Turkey Dinner Guests**: This page is dedicated to inputing the number of guests that will be attending the thanksgiving dinner. Please enter the number of guests in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Course** (`main_course`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the main course of the meal, such as '1', '2', or '3'.
* **Side Dishes** (`side_dishes`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the side dishes that will accompany the main course, such as '1', '2', or '3'. You can choose multiple options by holding down the Ctrl key while selecting.
* **Dessert Course** (`dessert_course`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the dessert course of the meal, such as '1', '2', or '3'.
* **Drinks** (`drinks`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the drinks that will be served with the meal, such as '1', '2', or '3'. You can choose multiple options by holding down the Ctrl key while selecting.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Enter any comments or notes about the dinner or event.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of someone you would like to contact.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of someone you would like to contact.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the Thanksgiving dinner event.

Please note that all fields except **Main Course** are optional. You can choose to fill out as many or as few fields as you need for your specific use case.
