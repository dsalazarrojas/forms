# Thanksgiving Photo Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Thanksgiving Photo Upload Form" is designed for users to upload photos and provide related information for Thanksgiving-related events or activities. This form will be used to collect and organize photos, assign tasks to team members, and generate output files in various formats.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a valid username.
2. Upload the photo you want to submit.
3. Provide a brief description of the photo.
4. Select the date and time the photo was taken.
5. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).
6. Add any additional notes about the photo.
7. Choose the assigned user, team, and tool for the photo.
8. Select the category of the photo (e.g., Thanksgiving Forms).
9. Fill in any required fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **username** (1): Enter your username to identify yourself in the system.
* **photo** (2): Upload a photo related to the Thanksgiving event or activity.
* **description** (3): Provide a brief description of the photo (e.g., "Family gathering" or "Turkey parade").
* **date** (4): Select the date the photo was taken.
* **time** (5): Select the time the photo was taken.
* **email** (6): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about the photo.
* **phone** (7): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about the photo.
* **notes** (8): Add any additional notes about the photo (e.g., " Taken during the Turkey Trot").
* **assigned user** (9): Choose the team member or user responsible for managing this photo.
* **assigned team** (10): Select the team that will work on this photo.
* **assigned tool** (11): Choose the tool used to manage this photo.
* **output file** (12): Select the format of the output file you want to generate (e.g., CSV, JSON, YAML).
* **form id** (13): A unique identifier for this form submission.
* **assigned form id** (14): The unique identifier of the form associated with this submission.
* **category** (15): Select the category for this photo (e.g., Thanksgiving Forms, Christmas Forms, Summer Camp Forms).

## Tips

* Make sure to upload a high-quality photo related to the event or activity.
* Be brief and concise in your photo description.
* Ensure that you fill in all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your assigned team member or user for clarification.
