# Thanksgiving Drinking Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information about your preferences and habits regarding drinks during the Thanksgiving holiday.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred drink types from the available options.
2. Choose your preferred beverage from the list.
3. Select your preferred time to drink from the available options.
4. If you have any other drinks you'd like to mention, type them in the "Other Drinks" field.
5. Choose the size of your drink from the list.
6. Click "Submit Form" to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Drink Preference** (`drink_preference`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more of the available drink types (Bourbon, Iced tea, Soda, Beer, Wine, Champagne) that you enjoy during Thanksgiving.
* **Preferred Beverage** (`preferred_beverage`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more of the beverages you typically prefer during Thanksgiving (Apple Cider, Coffee, Iced tea, Soda, Wine).
* **Preferred Time to Drink** (`preferred_time_to_drink`, select_one, required: false): Choose when you usually drink during Thanksgiving (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Before bed).
* **Other Drinks** (`other_drinks`, select_multiple, required: false): If you have any other drinks that are not listed, type them in this field (e.g., sparkling water, fruit punch, or juice).
* **Drink Sizes** (`drink_sizes`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the size of your drink from the available options (Small, Medium, Large, XL).
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, text, required: false): Type in your name or message to submit the form and complete the survey.

## Tips
* You can select one or multiple options for each field.
* You can type in any other drinks you'd like to mention in the "Other Drinks" field.
