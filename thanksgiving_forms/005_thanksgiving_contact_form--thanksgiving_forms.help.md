# thanksgiving_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The thanksgiving_contact_form is a contact form used to gather information from customers or users about their thanksgiving-related inquiries, concerns, or suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the text field provided for this purpose.
2. Enter your email address, which will be used to get in touch with you.
3. Enter your phone number for further discussion or follow-up.
4. Write a brief comment to provide context about your inquiry.
5. Select a category for your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (Text)** (Optional): Please enter your name so we can get in touch with you.
* **Email (Email)** (Optional): Enter your email address so we can respond to you.
* **Phone (Text)** (Optional): Enter your phone number for further discussion or follow-up.
* **Comment (Note)** (Optional): Write a brief comment to provide context about your inquiry.
* **Category (Select One)** (Optional): Select a category that best describes your inquiry, such as "Category 1" or "Category 2".
