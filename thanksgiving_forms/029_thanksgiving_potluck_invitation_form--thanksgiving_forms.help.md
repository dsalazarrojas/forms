# Thanksgiving Potluck Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect RSVPs and personal details from individuals invited to a Thanksgiving potluck event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your **first name** in the text field above.
2. Enter your **last name** in the text field below.
3. Choose whether you **will attend** by selecting "True", "False", or "Undecided" from the dropdown menu.
4. If you will be attending, enter the **number of guests** you plan to bring.
5. Provide your **phone number** in the text field.
6. Enter your **email** in the format of a valid email address.
7. If you have any additional comments or messages, type them in the **comment** field.
8. Enter the date of the event in the **date** field.
9. Select the time of the event from the time dropdown menu.
10. Finally, enter your **address** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Will Attend**: Choose whether you will attend the event by selecting "True", "False", or "Undecided".
* **Number of Guests**: If you will be attending, enter the number of guests you plan to bring.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Comment**: Type any additional comments or messages you have.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the event in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time**: Select the time of the event from the time dropdown menu.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
