# Thanksgiving Dinner Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a Thanksgiving dinner nomination. It's used to identify families or individuals in need and provide them with a special holiday meal.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully and gather all necessary information before starting.
2.  Fill out the form in sections, starting with the nomination title and contact information.
3.  Provide detailed information about the family or individual being nominated, including their address, city, state, and zip code.
4.  Describe the family's demographics, such as family size and income level.
5.  Explain the family's needs and any special circumstances that may affect their holiday celebration.
6.  If applicable, provide information about the family's volunteer hours and frequency.
7.  Select the relevant categories that best describe the family or individual being nominated.
8.  Provide a description of the holiday theme and message.
9.  Confirm the nomination details and submit the form.
