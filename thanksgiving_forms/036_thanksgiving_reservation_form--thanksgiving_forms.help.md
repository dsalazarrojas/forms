# Thanksgiving Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Thanksgiving Reservation Form" is a simple form designed to collect basic customer information for Thanksgiving reservations. This form helps users to submit their reservation details, including dates, times, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the reservation date and time for the desired Thanksgiving reservation.
2.  Enter the guest count for the reservation.
3.  Provide any additional message or comment for the reservation.
4.  Enter the phone number of the customer.
5.  Enter the email of the customer.
6.  Enter a unique form ID (optional but recommended for tracking purposes).
7.  Enter the customer's name.
8.  Enter the customer's phone number.
9.  Enter the customer's email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Reservation Date <small>(optional)</small>
- **Reservation Date**: Enter the date for the reservation. Please use the date format `MM/DD/YYYY`.

### Time <small>(optional)</small>
- **Time**: Enter the time for the reservation. Please use the 24-hour format `HH:MM`.

### Guest Count <small>(optional)</small>
- **Guest Count**: Enter the number of guests for the reservation.

### Message <small>(optional)</small>
- **Message**: Provide any additional information or comments for the reservation.

### Phone Number <small>(optional)</small>
- **Phone Number**: Enter the customer's phone number.

### Email <small>(optional)</small>
- **Email**: Enter the customer's email address.

### Form ID <small>(optional)</small>
- **Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the form (optional but recommended for tracking purposes).

### Customer Name <small>(optional)</small>
- **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name.

### Customer Phone <small>(optional)</small>
- **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.

### Customer Email <small>(optional)</small>
- **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.

## Tips
- Ensure to enter the correct date and time format as specified above.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the 'Message' field.
- The 'Form ID' field is optional but recommended for tracking purposes.
