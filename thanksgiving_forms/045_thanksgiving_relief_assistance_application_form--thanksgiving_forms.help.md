# Thanksgiving Relief Assistance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thanksgiving Relief Assistance Application Form is designed to aid individuals and families in need receive help for the holiday season. If you are struggling to afford a traditional Thanksgiving meal, you can fill out this form to request relief assistance. Our team will review your application and provide aid accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Be honest in your answers, as we will review your responses to provide the best possible assistance.
- If you need help, please submit your application well before the Thanksgiving holiday.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Thanksgiving Relief** (`thanksgiving_relief_assistance_form`):
	+ Enter a brief description of your situation, explaining why you need relief assistance for Thanksgiving.
* **Family Name** (`family_name`):
	+ Enter your name and names of all family members you are including in your application.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`):
	+ Enter your email address where we can contact you for further information or to provide updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`):
	+ Enter your phone number, including the country code, so we can contact you if needed.
* **Why are you requesting relief?** (`request_reason`):
	+ Explain the reasons for your request, including any financial difficulties or other extenuating circumstances.
* **Available Funds** (`available_funds`):
	+ Enter the amount of money you have available for food, as this will help us assess your need.
* **Financial Support** (`financial_support`):
	+ Choose how much financial support you need, with options None, Some, or A lot.
* **Select food items** (`food_items`):
	+ Choose the types of food you need help with, such as Turkey, Stuffing, Mashed Potatoes, Gravy, or Pumpkin Pie.
* **Special occasions** (`special_occasions`):
	+ If you have a special occasion, such as a birthday or anniversary, on or around Thanksgiving, enter the date.
* **Special Occasion Time** (`special_occasions_time`):
	+ Enter the time of your special occasion, if applicable.
* **Relief Status** (`relief_status`):
	+ Please note any other relevant information about your relief status.
* **Form Notes** (`form_notes`):
	+ Add any additional comments or details about your application.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate in your application.
- Ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
- If you need help, please submit your application well before the Thanksgiving holiday.
- We will review your application and provide aid accordingly.
