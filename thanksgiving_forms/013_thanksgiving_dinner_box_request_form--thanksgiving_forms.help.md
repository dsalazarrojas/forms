# Thanksgiving Dinner Box Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thanksgiving Dinner Box Request Form is designed to collect information from users about their preferences for a customized Thanksgiving dinner box. This form helps the business or organization offering the service to understand the users' needs and provide a personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Please enter your name in the **Name** field. This field is required.
2.  Enter your email address in the **Email** field. Please make sure to enter a valid email address.
3.  Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field. This field is not required, but it will help us to contact you in case we need to.
4.  In the **Message** field, please write any special requests or instructions you may have for your Thanksgiving dinner box.
5.  If you have any additional special requests, please enter them in the **Special Requests** field. This field is not required.
6.  Specify the number of guests you are expecting for the holiday in the **How many guests** field.
7.  Select a date for your Thanksgiving dinner box in the **Select a date** field. Please make sure to choose a date that works for you.
8.  Select a time for your pickup in the **Select a time** field.
9.  If you have any further special requests, please enter them in the **Special Requests** field. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Name**: This field is required and should contain your name.
*  **Email**: Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
*  **Phone**: This field is not required, but it will help us to contact you in case we need to.
*  **Message**: This field is for any special requests or instructions you may have.
*  **Special Requests**: This field is not required, but it will help us to understand your needs better.
*  **How many guests**: Please specify the number of guests you are expecting for the holiday.
*  **Select a date**: Please choose a date for your Thanksgiving dinner box pickup.
*  **Select a time**: Please choose a time for your pickup.
*  **Special Requests**: This field is not required, but it will help us to understand any further special requests you may have.

## Tips
*  Please be honest when filling out the form, as this will help us to provide you with a better experience.
*  Make sure to submit the form only once per person to avoid duplication of requests.
*  If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
