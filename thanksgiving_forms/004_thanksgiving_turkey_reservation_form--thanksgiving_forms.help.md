# thanksgiving_turkey_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thanksgiving Turkey Reservation Form is used to reserve a turkey for the holiday. This form helps us track which turkeys are reserved and which sizes are needed to ensure we have enough turkeys for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the reservation date for your turkey.
2. Choose the reservation time for your turkey.
3. Choose the size of the turkey you would like to reserve.
4. Enter your name so we can contact you about your reservation.
5. Enter your email address so we can reach out to you about your reservation.
6. Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you about your reservation.
7. Add any additional information about your reservation in the "Reservation Note" field.
8. Finally, select the tool you will be using for your turkey reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required: false): Please select the date you would like to reserve your turkey. Note that we will not allow multiple reservations for the same date.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time you would like to reserve your turkey. Please ensure that this time is available for your reservation.
* **Turkey Size** (`turkey_size`, select_one, required: false): Choose the size of the turkey you would like to reserve. Options: Small, Medium, Large.
* **Your Name** (`reservation_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name so we can contact you about your reservation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you about your reservation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you about your reservation.
* **Reservation Note** (`reservation_note`, note, required: false): Add any additional information about your reservation.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): Please select the tool you will be using for your turkey reservation.

## Tips
* Please note that we will not allow multiple reservations for the same time slot or date.
* If you have already submitted a reservation, please do not resubmit.
* If you need to make changes to your reservation, please reach out to us.
