# thanksgiving_turkey_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for a thanksgiving turkey pre-order. It will ask for general information such as user details, shipping information, and payment details to facilitate the pre-order process.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill in the requested information for each field.
- Start with the **User Information** page, where you will provide your personal details.
- Next, move on to the **Shipping Details** page, where you will enter your shipping information.
- Then, move to the **Payment Information** page, where you will enter your payment details.
- Finally, review the **Order Confirmation** page to ensure all information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Information
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other personal details you wish to share with us.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are available.

### Shipping Details
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional shipping information or instructions.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address.
* **Shipping Provider** (`shipping_provider`, select_one, optional): Select whether you have a preferred shipping provider.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, optional): Select the shipping method you prefer.

### Payment Information
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter any payment information or instructions.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method you prefer.

### Order
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of turkeys you wish to order.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, optional): Enter the shipping cost of your order.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your order.
* **Status Reason** (`status_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for your order status.
* **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, text, optional): Review and confirm your order details.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill in all required fields for a successful submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
