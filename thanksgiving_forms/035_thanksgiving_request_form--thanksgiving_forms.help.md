# thanksgiving_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employees to submit their holiday requests, including a brief description of their desired holiday schedule and personal contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a page to fill out (there's only one page in this form).
2. Enter your **Name** in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Provide a brief **Description** of your holiday request in the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Holiday Request (Holiday Request)** (`holiday_request`, `text`, optional): This is where you can write a brief description of your holiday request.
* **Name (Name)** (`name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your employee records.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your holiday request.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Description (Description)** (`description`, `text`, optional): Please provide a short description of your holiday plans and schedule for your absence.
