# Thanksgiving Party Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users about their Thanksgiving party plans and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your family's name in the **family_name** field.
2. Enter the address of your relatives' residence in the **relatives_address** field.
3. Enter the address of your friends' residence in the **friends_address** field.
4. In the **will_bring** field, select the dish you plan to bring to the party.
5. In the **will_share** field, select whether you will share food with others.
6. In the **will_arrive** and **will_leave** fields, select the time you plan to arrive and leave the party.
7. Enter the number of guests you plan to bring in the **guest_count** field.
8. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field.
9. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
10. In the **note** field, add any additional notes or comments about your party plans.
11. Select who you are assigned to in the **assigned** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **family_name**: Enter your family's name.
* **relatives_address**: Enter the address of your relatives' residence.
* **friends_address**: Enter the address of your friends' residence.
* **will_bring**: Select the dish you plan to bring to the party.
	+ Options: Apple Pie, Pumpkin Pie, Mashed Potatoes, Stuffing, Cranberry Sauce
* **will_share**: Select whether you will share food with others.
	+ Options: True, False
* **will_arrive**: Select the time you plan to arrive at the party.
* **will_leave**: Select the time you plan to leave the party.
* **guest_count**: Enter the number of guests you plan to bring.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your party plans.
* **assigned**: Select who you are assigned to.
	+ Options: chatjimmy, nobody, somebody
* **form_id**: This field is not intended for user input.
