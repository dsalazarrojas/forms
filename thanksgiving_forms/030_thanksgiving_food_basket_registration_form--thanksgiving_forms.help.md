# thanksgiving_food_basket_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for the Thanksgiving food basket program. Please fill out the form with your personal details and order preferences to be included in the food basket delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields marked with \*.
2. Ensure that email is a valid email address.
3. Select options for select_one and select_multiple fields accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, not marked as required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not marked as required): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not marked as required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, not marked as required): Enter your address for delivery purposes.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, note, not marked as required): Enter any additional order details or special requests.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, not marked as required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Age** (`age`, number, not marked as required): Enter your age.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, not marked as required): Select only one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, not marked as required): Select multiple options from the list.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not marked as required): Enter the time of your availability for delivery.
