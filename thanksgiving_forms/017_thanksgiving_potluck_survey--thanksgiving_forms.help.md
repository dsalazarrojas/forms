# Thanksgiving Potluck Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Thanksgiving Potluck Survey" form is designed to gather information from team members about the upcoming potluck event. This form helps hosts and attendees provide and share information about the event, ensuring a successful and enjoyable potluck.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your details about the potluck, including:
	* Host information
	* Potluck name
	* Date and time
	* Location
	* Food you plan to bring (if applicable)
	* Number of guests attending
	* Any additional notes or comments
2. If you're the host, provide your contact information so others can reach out to you with questions or updates.
3. Be as detailed as possible, but feel free to skip any fields that don't apply to you or your contribution to the potluck.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title (text, Optional)**: Enter the title or name of the potluck event.
* **Potluck Details (text, Optional)**: Share any specific details about the potluck, such as the theme or any special requests.
* **Host Info (text, Optional)**: If you're hosting the potluck, enter your name and contact information so others can reach out to you with questions or updates.
* **Potluck Name (text, Optional)**: Enter the name of the dish or contribution you plan to bring to the potluck.
* **Date (date, Optional)**: Enter the date of the potluck event.
* **Time (time, Optional)**: Enter the time of the potluck event.
* **Location (text, Optional)**: Enter the location where the potluck will take place.
* **Food (text, Optional)**: If you're contributing food, describe your dish here, including any ingredients or dietary restrictions.
* **Number of Guests (number, Optional)**: Estimate the number of guests you're planning to bring, or the number of people you expect at your contribution.
* **Number of Guests (text, Optional)**: A duplicate field for "Number of Guests" (use one or the other).
* **Notes (text, Optional)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your contribution or the event.
* **Email (email, Optional)**: Provide your email address so others can reach out to you with questions or updates.
* **Phone (text, Optional)**: Provide your phone number in case others need to contact you about the potluck.
* **Location (text, Optional)**: Enter the location where the potluck will take place (if not already provided).
* **Contact Info (text, Optional)**: Enter your name and any other contact information (if you're hosting the potluck).
