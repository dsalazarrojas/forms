# Thanksgiving Bake Sale Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather pre-order information from customers for the Thanksgiving Bake Sale. Please use this guide to complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling in your contact information:**
	* Fill in your **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** accurately and completely.
2. **Select the item(s) you'd like to pre-order:**
	* Choose the **Item** you would like to pre-order from the list provided.
3. **Specify the quantity:**
	* Enter the **Quantity** of the selected item.
4. **Add any additional notes (optional):**
	* If you have any additional notes or requests, you can add them in the **Note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Name
* **Name**: Required. Enter your full name as it should be spelled and formatted correctly.
* This field helps us ensure that we can accurately address you and fulfill your order.

### Email
* **Email**: Required. Enter your email address as it is registered with your email provider.
* This field helps us contact you with updates and information about your order.

### Phone
* **Phone**: Required. Enter your phone number as it should be formatted correctly (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* This field allows us to contact you quickly and efficiently if needed.

### Item
* **Item**: Required. Choose the item you would like to pre-order from the list provided.
* This field helps us know what you would like to purchase.

### Quantity
* **Quantity**: Required. Enter the number of the selected item you would like to pre-order.
* This field ensures we have enough of the item for you and can process your order correctly.

### Note
* **Note**: Optional. If you have any additional notes or requests, you can add them here.
* This field is for any additional information you would like to provide to help us fulfill your order.

### Tips
* Please double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any issues or questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Thank you for participating in the Thanksgiving Bake Sale!
