# Thanksgiving Break Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thanksgiving Break Check Out Form is used to gather information from students and staff members involved in the thanksgiving break checkout process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student section with the student's name and student ID number.
2. Provide the student's contact information, including parent/guardian's name and relationship.
3. Confirm if an emergency contact is available.
4. Enter the student's room number.
5. Choose the checkout and return dates.
6. Add any comments or additional information.
7. Sign off with your student and staff signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter the student's student ID number.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's contact phone number.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade`, select_one, optional): Choose the student's grade level (High or Low).
* **Parent Or Guardian** (`parent_or_guardian`, text, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's name.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm if an emergency contact is available.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's relationship to the student (e.g., "Father", "Mother", etc.).
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Student Room Number** (`student_room`, number, required): Enter the student's room number.
* **Checkout Date** (`checkout_date`, date, required): Enter the checkout date (MM/DD/YY).
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required): Enter the return date (MM/DD/YY).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, required): Sign off with your student signature.
* **Contact Signature** (`contact_signature`, text, required): Sign off with your contact signature.
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`, text, required): Sign off with your staff signature.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, required): Enter your staff name.
* **Staff Title** (`staff_title`, text, optional): Enter your staff title.
* **Staff Department** (`staff_department`, text, optional): Enter your staff department.
* **Staff Phone** (`staff_phone`, text, optional): Enter your staff phone number.
* **Staff E-mail** (`staff_email`, email, optional): Enter your staff email address.
* **Staff Extension** (`staff_ext`, text, optional): Enter your staff extension.
* **Staff Notes** (`staff_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional staff notes.
* **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional student notes.
* **School** (`school`, text, optional): Enter the school name.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter the form ID (not required).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* Make sure to sign off with your student and staff signature.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the staff.
