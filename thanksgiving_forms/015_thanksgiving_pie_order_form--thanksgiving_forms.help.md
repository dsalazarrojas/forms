The form is designed to collect information from customers who want to order a pie for Thanksgiving. The purpose of this form is to gather the customer's contact information, their preferred type of pie, the quantity they want to order, and their preferred pickup time. This form will help the pie shop to know how many pies to bake and when to deliver them.

## Thanksgiving_Pie_Order_Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to order a pie for Thanksgiving. Please fill out the form with your information to place your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields with the required information.
2. Select your preferred pie type from the options provided.
3. Enter the quantity you want to order.
4. Choose your preferred pickup time.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer_Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your contact information. Please provide any relevant details so we can reach out to you regarding your order.
* **Pie_Type** (`pie_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of pie you want to order from the options provided: Apple Pie, Pumpkin Pie, Pecan Pie, or Cherry Pie.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of pies you want to order.
* **Pickup_Time** (`pickup_time`, time, optional): Choose the time you want to pick up your pie(s).
* **E-Mail** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the fields with accurate information so we can process your order correctly.
* If you have any special requests or questions, please contact us through the provided email or phone number.
