# Thanksgving rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to RSVP to a Thanksgiving gathering and indicate if you plan to bring a dish to share with the group.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a page to fill out the form. The first page will have a single field for your response.
2. Enter your name in the "guest_name" field.
3. If you plan to bring a dish, type it in the "dish_brought" field.
4. Answer the question "Will you bring a dish?" in the "will_bring" field. This is a select-one field with two options: "bring" and "not bring".
5. Optionally, you can add a message in the "message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **thanksgiving_rsvp**: This is a field to enter your RSVP response (e.g. "yes", "no", etc.).
* **guest_name**: Enter your name so we can address you properly.
* **dish_brought**: If you plan to bring a dish to share with the group, type it in this field. Be as specific as possible so others can plan accordingly.
* **will_bring**: Answer "bring" if you plan to bring a dish, and "not bring" if you don't.
* **message**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or messages you'd like to share with the group.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out the form completely to ensure your RSVP is recorded accurately.
* If you plan to bring a dish, try to be specific about what you're bringing, so others can plan accordingly.
* Don't hesitate to add any comments or messages you'd like to share with the group.
