# Thanksgiving Lunch Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information for the Thanksgiving lunch event, ensuring that everyone's needs are considered when planning the meal.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the first page of the form, "Event Details."
2. Fill in the required information for the event details.
3. Proceed to the second page, "Guests."
4. List the names of the guests attending the lunch.
5. On the third page, "Dietary Needs," describe the specific dietary needs of each guest.
6. Move to the last page, "Food Preferences," and list any food preferences for each guest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details (thanksgiving_lunch_sign_up)** (required, Text) 
  Enter a brief description of the Thanksgiving lunch event, such as "Company-wide lunch celebration" or "Family gathering."
* **Guests** (optional, Text) 
  List the names of the guests attending the lunch. You can separate multiple guests with commas or enter each name on a new line.
* **Dietary Needs** (required, Text) 
  Describe the specific dietary needs for each guest, such as "vegetarian" or "gluten-free."
* **Food Preferences** (optional, Text) 
  Specify any food preferences for each guest, such as "no nuts" or "more turkey."
