# thanksgiving_diy_kits_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to pre-order DIY kits for Thanksgiving. It helps us gather information about your preferences and needs to ensure that we can provide you with the correct quantities and types of kits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your preferred order type (Turkey, Hams, Stuffing, Mashed Potatoes, or Gravy).
3. Choose a date and time for your order.
4. Indicate the quantity of kits you would like to order.
5. If you have any special requests or additional comments, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.
6. Confirm your order by selecting "I confirm my order" or "I need help with my order" from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name (text)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like it to appear on your order.
- **Email (email)**: Enter your email address where we can contact you with order updates and instructions.
- **Order Type (select multiple)**: Select all the items you would like to pre-order (Turkey, Hams, Stuffing, Mashed Potatoes, or Gravy).
- **Order Date (date)**: Choose a date when you would like to receive your order.
- **Order Time (time)**: Select a time when you would like to receive your order.
- **Quantity (number)**: Enter the number of kits you would like to order.
- **Special Requests (select one)**: Indicate if you have any special requests or needs.
- **Additional Comments (text)**: Provide any additional information or comments about your order.
- **Order Confirmation (select one)**: Confirm your order by selecting "I confirm my order" or "I need help with my order".
