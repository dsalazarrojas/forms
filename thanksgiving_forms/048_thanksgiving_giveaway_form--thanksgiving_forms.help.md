# thanksgiving_giveaway_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and preferences for a Thanksgiving giveaway event. It's an opportunity for users to enter and potentially win a prize. The form is intended to be user-friendly and efficient.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by providing your User Info, including your name and any other details you'd like to share.
2. Next, enter your Contact Info, such as your email address and phone number.
3. You'll also be asked to provide your Address, where you'd like to receive the prize.
4. Select your preferred prize type from the Prize Preferences options.
5. If you've chosen a Gift Card prize, enter the Gift Card Value.
6. Specify the type of Gift Card you'd like, such as Amazon or Visa.
7. If you've chosen a Physical Item prize, enter a brief Description of Physical Item.
8. Enter the value of the Physical Item.
9. Enter any Participant Info, such as how you'd like to be notified as a winner.
10. Enter the Participant's Phone number and Email.
11. Provide the Participant's Address.
12. Choose whether you'd like Automatic or Random Selection for the winner.
13. Enter the Winner's Email address.
14. Enter the Winner's Phone number.
15. Write a brief Winner Message to be displayed as a notification.
16. Add any notes about the winner in the Winner Note field.
17. Select the Submission Date and Time when you're filling out the form.
18. Enter the Submission IP address from which you're accessing the form.
19. Enter your User IP address.
20. Enter the User Agent from your browser.
21. Optionally, specify the Submission Referrer, such as how you got to the form.
22. Finally, enter any additional information about yourself in the Submission User field.
23. Enter the Browser type and version you're using to access the form.

* **User Info (1)** (`User Info`, required): Enter your name and any other details you'd like to share.

* **Participant Phone (10)** (`Participant Phone`, optional): Enter the Participant's phone number.
* **Participant Email (11)** (`Participant Email`, optional): Enter the Participant's email address.
* **Participant Address (12)** (`Participant Address`, optional): Enter the Participant's address.
* **Winner Selection (13)** (`Winner Selection`, optional): Choose whether you'd like Automatic or Random Selection for the winner.
* **Winner Email (14)** (`Winner Email`, optional): Enter the Winner's email address.
* **Winner Phone (15)** (`Winner Phone`, optional): Enter the Winner's phone number.
* **Winner Message (16)** (`Winner Message`, optional): Write a brief message to be displayed as a winner notification.
* **Winner Note (17)** (`Winner Note`, optional): Add any notes about the winner.
