# Environmental Sabbatical Leave Of Absance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Sabbatical Leave Of Absance Form is a tool for employees to request time off from work to focus on environmental projects, and for the management to track and manage these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your company email address in the "Email" field.
3. Fill in your current job title if applicable.
4. If you have a supervisor, enter their name and title in the "Supervisor" field.
5. Select the start and end dates of your leave in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
6. Choose the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., environmental project, family and medical, personal).
7. Enter a brief reason for your leave in the "Reason" field (if required).
8. If you have a project related to your leave, fill in the "Project Name", "Project Description", "Project Start Date", and "Project End Date" fields.
9. Select the agreement date in the "Agreement" field.
10. Set the submission date in the "Submission Date" field.
11. Select the approval level required for your leave request (e.g., Manager, Team, Department).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (employee_first_name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (employee_last_name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (employee_email, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Job Title** (employee_job_title, text, optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Supervisor** (employee_supervisor, text, optional): If you have a supervisor, enter their name and title.
* **Start Date** (leave_start_date, date, required): Select the start date of your leave.
* **End Date** (leave_end_date, date, required): Select the end date of your leave.
* **Duration** (leave_duration, text, optional): Enter the duration of your leave (if required).
* **Type** (leave_type, select_one, required): Choose the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., environmental project, family and medical, personal).
* **Reason** (leave_reason, text, optional): Enter a brief reason for your leave (if required).
* **Project Name** (project_name, text, required): If you have a project related to your leave, enter its name.
* **Project Description** (project_description, text, optional): Describe the project in more detail.
* **Project Start Date** (project_start_date, date, optional): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (project_end_date, date, optional): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Project Location** (project_location, text, optional): Enter the location of your project.
* **Agreement** (agreement, date, required): Select the agreement date for your leave.
* **Submission Date** (submission_date, date, required): Set the submission date for your leave request.
* **Manager Notes** (manager_notes, text, optional): Leave comments for your supervisor.
* **Departmental Approval** (departmental_approvement, select_one, required): Select the departmental approval level required for your leave request.
* **Approved by Manager** (approval_by_manager, select_one, optional): Select if your leave request is approved by your manager.
* **Approved by Team** (approval_by_team, select_one, optional): Select if your team has approved your leave request.
* **Approved by Department** (approval_by_department, select_one, optional): Select if your department has approved your leave request.
