# Media Investigative Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by employees to request a leave of absence for investigative purposes, allowing them to take time off from work while being investigated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
3. Select your department from the "Department" field, which is a critical field to ensure that your request is properly routed to the correct team.
4. Choose whether the leave is for "Yes" or "No" in the "Reason" field.
5. Enter your desired start date in the "Start Date" field.
6. Enter your desired end date in the "End Date" field.
7. Provide your manager's name in the "Manager Name" field (this is optional but recommended).
8. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field (this is optional but recommended).
9. Enter your contact email in the "Contact Email" field (this is optional but recommended).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Details** (Request Details):
  * This field allows you to provide a brief description of your request.
  * Please provide a concise and clear explanation of your need for a leave of absence.
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name):
  * Enter your name as it appears on your personnel documents.
  * This field is optional but recommended.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID):
  * Enter your employee ID as it appears on your personnel documents.
  * This field is optional but recommended.
* **Department** (Department):
  * Select your department from the available options.
  * This field is required to ensure that your request is routed to the correct team.
  * Options: News, Photos, Audio, Video.
* **Reason** (Reason):
  * Select "Yes" if you are requesting a leave for investigative purposes.
  * Select "No" otherwise.
  * This field is required to help managers understand your request.
* **Start Date** (Start Date):
  * Enter the date you wish to begin your leave.
  * This field is optional but recommended.
* **End Date** (End Date):
  * Enter the date you expect to end your leave.
  * This field is optional but recommended.
* **Manager Name** (Manager Name):
  * Enter your manager's name if you have one assigned.
  * This field is optional but recommended.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone):
  * Enter your contact phone number for managers and HR to get in touch with you.
  * This field is optional but recommended.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email):
  * Enter your contact email for managers and HR to get in touch with you.
  * This field is optional but recommended.
