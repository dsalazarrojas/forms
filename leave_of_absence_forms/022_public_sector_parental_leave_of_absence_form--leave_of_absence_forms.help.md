# Public Sector Parental Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Sector Parental Leave Of Absence Form is designed to collect information for processing public sector parental leave requests. It is essential for employees to complete this form to ensure that their leave is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Employee Information section with your details.
2. Enter your Employee ID and Job Title.
3. Select the Start and End Dates for your leave.
4. Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Parental or Other).
5. Enter the Leave Duration and Return Date.
6. Add any additional Notes about your leave.
7. Enter your Email and Phone number for contact.
8. Click on the Submit button to send your request to your manager for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Public Sector Parental Leave Of Absence Form** (Page 1): This is the main title of the form.
* **Employee Information** (Page 2): Enter your personal details, such as name, and any other relevant information.
* **Employee ID** (Page 2): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Job Title** (Page 2): Enter your job title.
* **Start Date** (Page 3): Enter the date you would like your leave to start.
* **End Date** (Page 3): Enter the date you would like your leave to end.
* **Leave Type** (Page 3): Select the type of leave you are requesting (Parental or Other).
* **Leave Duration** (Page 4): Enter the number of leave days you are requesting.
* **Return Date** (Page 4): Enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **Notes** (Page 4): Add any additional notes about your leave.
* **Email** (Page 5): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Page 5): Enter your phone number.
* **Submit** (Page 5): Click this button to send your request to your manager for review.
* **Review** (Page 6): This is where your manager will review your request.
* **Employee Information** (Page 6): This section will display the employee information you entered earlier.
* **Manager** (Page 6): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Title** (Page 6): Enter your manager's job title.
* **Manager Notes** (Page 7): Add any notes from your manager.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that your leave request is processed correctly.
* Be accurate when entering dates and employee information.
* Check with your manager to ensure that they have reviewed and approved your leave request.
