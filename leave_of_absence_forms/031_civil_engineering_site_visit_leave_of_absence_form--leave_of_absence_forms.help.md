# Civil Engineering Site Visit Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used by Civil Engineering employees to request a Leave of Absence for site visits. The form must be completed to ensure that all necessary information is collected and approved before the employee proceeds with the site visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Employee Full Name** in the first field. This is your full name as it appears on your employee identification badge.
2. Select your **Job Title/Role** from the list provided.
3. Enter the name of the **Associated Project Name** related to the site visit.
4. Provide the **Site Visit Location (Address or Coordinates)** for the visit.
5. Choose the **Visit Duration** by clicking on the date range calendar.
6. Enter your **Leave Start Date** and **Leave End Date**.
7. Select the **Primary Purpose of Site Visit** from the list.
8. Enter your **Personal Phone/Contact** number so that we can reach you during the site visit.
9. Provide the **Immediate Supervisor Name**, usually your direct report or someone you will be meeting on-site.
10. Select the **Mode of Transportation** used for the site visit.
11. Confirm that you have the necessary **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)** for the site visit.
12. Finally, confirm the date you submit this **Request**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee identification badge.
* **Job Title/Role** (`employee_role`, text, required): Enter your job title or role within the company.
* **Associated Project Name** (`project_name_info`, text, required): Enter the name of the project you are visiting.
* **Site Visit Location (Address or Coordinates)** (`site_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the site visit, either as an address or in coordinates.
* **Visit Duration** (`leave_duration_header`, note, required): This is a reminder that you need to choose the duration of your leave.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your first day of absence.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your last day of absence.
* **Primary Purpose of Site Visit** (`purpose_of_visit`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options for your site visit reason:
	+ Initial Site Assessment
	+ Construction Inspection
	+ Surveying/Mapping
	+ Environmental Sampling
	+ Final Project Walkthrough
* **Personal Phone/Contact** (`contact_during_leave`, text, required): Enter your phone number or contact information so we can reach you during the site visit.
* **Immediate Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_approval_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your immediate supervisor.
* **Mode of Transportation** (`transportation_method`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options for your mode of transportation:
	+ Company Vehicle
	+ Personal Vehicle (Mileage Reimbursement)
	+ Public Transport
	+ Air Travel
* **I have the necessary PPE (Safety gear) for this visit** (`safety_equipment_check`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have the necessary safety equipment for the site visit.
* **Request Date** (`request_submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submit this request.

**Tips**

* Make sure to double-check your **Leave Start Date** and **Leave End Date** to ensure accuracy.
* Ensure that you have the necessary **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)** for the site visit.
* Keep your **Personal Phone/Contact** number up to date.
* Confirm that you have the necessary **Safety Equipment** for the site visit.
