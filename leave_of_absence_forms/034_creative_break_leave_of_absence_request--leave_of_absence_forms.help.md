# Creative Break Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a leave of absence to recharge and focus on your creative work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your Employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Provide the reason for your request in the "Request Reason" field.
4. Select the start date of your break in the "Start Date" field.
5. Select the end date of your break in the "End Date" field.
6. Enter the duration of your break in the "Duration" field.
7. Check with your manager and select "Yes" or "No" in the "Manager Approval" field.
8. Check with HR and select "Yes" or "No" in the "HR Approval" field.
9. If applicable, select your department in the "Department" field.
10. Add any additional notes for your manager in the "Manager Notes" field.
11. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
12. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your unique employee identification number.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or employee records.
* **Request Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Explain why you need a creative break. Be specific about your creative pursuits or projects.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Select the date you want your break to start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Select the date you want your break to end.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, required): Enter the number of days or weeks you need for your break.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required): Check with your manager to confirm your break request.
* **HR Approval** (`hr_approval`, select_one, required): Check with HR to confirm your break request.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required): Select your department if it applies to your break request.
* **Manager Notes** (`manager_notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes for your manager.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number in case your manager or HR needs to reach you.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address in case your manager or HR needs to reach you.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Enter your personal phone number (if you'd like to be contacted). 

This guide is intended to help you complete the form in an efficient and accurate manner to ensure your creative break request is approved.
