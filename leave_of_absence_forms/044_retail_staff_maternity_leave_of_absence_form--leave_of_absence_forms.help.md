# Retail Staff Maternity Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help retail staff inform their employer about their maternity leave plans. It will be used to process and manage the leave request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Provide all required information in the relevant fields.
3. Attach any necessary supporting documents, such as medical certificates.
4. Submit the form to your supervisor/manager for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Information (1)
* **Form Information**: This field is for providing general information about your leave request.
* **Employee Details (2)**: Please enter your name and other relevant employee information.
* **Date Of Expected Delivery (3)**: Enter the date of your expected delivery.
* **Start Date (4)**: Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **Duration Of Leave (5)**: Enter the number of days you will be taking off.
* **End Date (6)**: Enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **Return To Work Date (7)**: Enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **Department (8)**: Select your department (e.g., Sales, Store Operations, etc.).
* **Supervisor Name (9)**: Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Manager Name (10)**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Leave Request Details (11)**: Provide a brief description of your leave request.
* **Medical Certificate (12)**: If applicable, please attach a medical certificate to support your leave request.
* **Attach Medical Certificate (13)**: Attach the medical certificate here.
* **Coverage Details (14)**: Select the managers who will be handling your coverage while you are on leave.
*   **Manager 1 (15)**: Enter name of manager 1.
*   **Manager 2 (16)**: Enter name of manager 2.
*   **Manager 3 (17)**: Enter name of manager 3.
*   **Manager 4 (18)**: Enter name of manager 4.
*   **Other (19)**: Enter name of other manager.
* **Approval Status (20)**: Select the status of your leave request (e.g., Approved, Denied, Pending).
* **Leave Request Date (21)**: Enter the date you submitted your leave request.
* **Leave Request Reason (22)**: Provide a brief description of the reason for your leave request.
* **Leave Requester Name (23)**: Enter your name.
* **Manager Approval Note (24)**: Enter any notes from your manager about your leave request.
* **Store Manager Approval (25)**: Enter approval from the store manager about your leave request.
