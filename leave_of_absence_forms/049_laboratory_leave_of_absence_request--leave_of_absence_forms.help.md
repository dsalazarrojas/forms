# Laboratory Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence from laboratory work, allowing you to take time off for emergency, vacation, bereavement, or other reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the type of leave you are requesting.
2. Provide your estimated start and end dates of the leave.
3. Enter the date you expect to return to work.
4. Choose whether your supervisor and manager have approved your request.
5. Select the reasons for your leave.
6. Enter any additional information or attach supporting documentation (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Type of Leave Request** (`type_of_leave_request`, select_multiple): 
  This field allows you to select the type of leave you are requesting. 
  Choose from emergency, vacation, bereavement, or other.
* **Start Date of Leave** (`start_date`, date): 
  Enter the date you plan to begin your leave.
* **End Date of Leave** (`end_date`, date): 
  Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Estimated Return Date** (`return_date`, date): 
  Enter the date you expect to return to work after your leave.
* **Supervisor Confirmation** (`supervisor_confirmation`, select_one): 
  Please select whether your supervisor has approved your request.
  Options are approved, not approved, or waiting on approval.
* **Manager Confirmation** (`manager_confirmation`, select_one): 
  Please select whether your manager has approved your request.
  Options are approved, not approved, or waiting on approval.
* **Reasons for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, select_multiple): 
  Select all applicable reasons for your leave from the available options.
  Options are medical leave, vacation, personal leave, family leave, bereavement leave, or other.
* **Laboratory Leaving Request Note** (`lab_leaving_request_note`, note): 
  Enter any additional information you would like to include with your request.
* **Laboratory Leaving Request Attachment** (`lab_leaving_request_attachment`, text): 
  Upload any supporting documentation or attachments related to your leave request.
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, note): 
  Enter any notes or comments from your manager.
* **Supervisor Note** (`supervisor_note`, note): 
  Enter any notes or comments from your supervisor.
* **Manager Approval Note** (`manager_approval_note`, note): 
  Enter any notes or comments from your manager regarding the approval or denial of your request.
* **Supervisor Approval Note** (`supervisor_approval_note`, note): 
  Enter any notes or comments from your supervisor regarding the approval or denial of your request.
* **Laboratory Leaving Request Attachment 2** (`laboratory_leaving_request_attachment_2`, text): 
  Upload any additional supporting documentation or attachments related to your leave request.

Note: This form is meant to be a template and should be reviewed and edited according to your organization's specific leave policies and procedures.
