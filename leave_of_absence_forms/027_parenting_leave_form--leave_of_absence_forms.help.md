# Parenting Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by employees to request a parenting leave, providing essential information to their managers and HR department for processing their leave request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the **Request Info** section with a brief description of your leave request.
2. Provide your **Employee Info** and **Employee Details** in the corresponding sections.
3. In the **Leave Request** section, describe the reason for your leave and the dates of absence.
4. Enter the **Leave Start Date**, **Leave End Date**, and **Leave Duration** for your requested leave.
5. Choose the **Leave Type**, **Leave Frequency**, and **Leave Request Type** as per your organization's policies.
6. Add any additional comments or information in the **Leave Request Comments** section.
7. Finally, review your **Leave Request Status**, **Leave Request Type**, **Leave Request Start Date**, **Leave Request End Date**, and **Leave Request Frequency** to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Info**: A brief description of your leave request.
* **Employee Info**: Your name and contact information.
* **Employee Details**: Additional information about yourself, such as job title, department, and manager's name and contact information.
* **Leave Request**: A detailed description of your leave request, including reason and dates of absence.
* **Leave Start Date**: The date you wish to start your leave.
* **Leave End Date**: The date you wish to end your leave.
* **Leave Duration**: The duration of your leave (e.g., number of days or weeks).
* **Leave Type**: The type of leave (e.g., maternity, paternity, adoption).
* **Leave Frequency**: How often you take leave (e.g., daily, weekly, bi-weekly).
* **Leave Start Time**: The time of day you wish to start your leave.
* **Leave End Time**: The time of day you wish to end your leave.
* **Leave Request Comments**: Any additional comments or information about your leave request.
* **Leave Request Status**: The status of your leave request (e.g., active, inactive).
* **Leave Request Type**: Whether your leave request is approved or denied.
* **Leave Request Start Date**: The date you wish to start your leave.
* **Leave Request End Date**: The date you wish to end your leave.
* **Leave Request Frequency**: How often you take leave (e.g., daily, weekly, bi-weekly).

**Tips**

* Review your organization's leave policy and eligibility criteria before filling out this form.
* Ensure you have provided all required information to avoid delays in processing your leave request.
* Double-check your dates and duration to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative or manager.
