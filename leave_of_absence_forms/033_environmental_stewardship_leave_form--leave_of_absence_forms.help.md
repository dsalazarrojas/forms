# Environmental Stewardship Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Stewardship Leave Form is used to record employee absences related to environmental stewardship activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee information in the "employee_info" field.
2. Select the start and end dates of your leave in the "start_date" and "end_date" fields, respectively.
3. Enter the reason for your leave in the "reason" field.
4. Specify the activity you will be undertaking during your leave in the "activity" field.
5. Enter the location where you will be undertaking the activity in the "location" field.
6. Add any additional comments or details about your leave in the "comments" field.
7. If your manager is able to approve your leave, select the "manager_approvals" field.
8. Enter any comments from your manager in the "manager_comments" field.
9. Finally, select the status of your leave request from the "status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_info** (`employee_info`, text, optional): Enter your employee name, job title, and department.
* **start_date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you will start your leave.
* **end_date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you will end your leave.
* **reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for your leave.
* **activity** (`activity`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the activity you will be undertaking during your leave.
* **location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you will be undertaking the activity during your leave.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or details about your leave.
* **manager_approvals** (`manager_approvals`, select_multiple, optional): Select all managers who can approve your leave.
* **manager_comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from your manager.
* **status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your leave request.

## Tips

* Please complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please provide as much detail as possible.
* If you are requesting a leave that spans multiple dates, please enter the start and end dates accordingly.
* If your manager has any comments, please enter them in the "manager_comments" field.
