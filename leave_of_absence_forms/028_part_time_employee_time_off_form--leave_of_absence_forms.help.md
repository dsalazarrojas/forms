# part_time_employee_time_off_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request time off for part-time employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate options for "time_off_request" and "time_off_type" fields to indicate your time off request status and type.
2. Enter the start and end dates of your time off in the "time_off_start_date" and "time_off_end_date" fields.
3. Enter the duration of your time off in the "time_off_duration" field.
4. Select your time off frequency in the "time_off_frequency" field, or enter a custom frequency in the "time_off_frequency_other" field.
5. Select your time off type in the "time_off_type" field, or enter a custom time-off type in the "time_off_type_other" field.
6. Enter any additional details about your time-off request in the "time_off_request_details" field.
7. Enter any additional details in the "time_off_request_details_additional" field.
8. Enter your name, email, and phone number in the "submitter_name", "submitter_email", and "submitter_phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **time_off_request**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your time off request status.
	+ If you select "Yes", you will be asked to provide further details about your time off request.
	+ If you select "No", you will not be able to proceed with your time off request.
* **time_off_start_date**: Enter the start date of your time off.
* **time_off_end_date**: Enter the end date of your time off.
* **time_off_duration**: Enter the duration of your time off.
* **time_off_frequency**: Select "Yes", "No", or enter a custom frequency in the "time_off_frequency_other" field.
* **time_off_frequency_other**: Enter a custom frequency value if you have selected "Yes" in the "time_off_frequency" field.
* **time_off_type**: Select "Yes", "No", or enter a custom time-off type in the "time_off_type_other" field.
* **time_off_type_other**: Enter a custom time-off type value if you have selected "Yes" in the "time_off_type" field.
* **time_off_request_details**: Enter any additional details about your time-off request.
* **time_off_request_details_additional**: Enter any additional details about your time-off request.
* **submitter_name**: Enter your name.
* **submitter_email**: Enter your email address.
* **submitter_phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **submitter_work_email**: Enter your work email address.
* **submitter_work_phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **submitter_work_phone_ext**: Not currently used.
