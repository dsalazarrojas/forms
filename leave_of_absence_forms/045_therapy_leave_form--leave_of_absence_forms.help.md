# Therapy Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapy Leave Form is designed to help employees request therapy leave from work. This form will guide you through the required fields and provide explanations for each section to ensure you complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the therapy leave form link, provided to you by your HR department or a designated approver.
2.  Fill out the required fields:
    -   **therapy_leave_form_1**: This section is not required, but if you choose to use it, please provide a brief description of your leave request.
    -   **start_date** :  Enter the date you wish to begin your leave.
    -   **end_date** : Enter the date you expect to return to work after the leave.
    -   **reason_for_leave**: Please provide the reason for your leave in detail to aid the approver in processing your request.
    -   **medical_document**: Attach any relevant medical documents, if required by your HR department.
    -   **doctor_name** : Provide the name of your doctor or healthcare provider.
    -   **appointment_date**: Enter the date of your appointment with the doctor.
    -   **doctor_phone_number** : Enter your doctor's contact number, if necessary.
    -   **therapy_session_notes** : Briefly describe the progress of your therapy sessions.
3.  Review your answers and ensure you're accurate and complete.
4.  Submit the form, and wait for your approver's decision.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **therapy_leave_form_1** (`therapy_leave_form_1`, text, optional)
    This field allows you to provide a brief description of your leave request. If you don't have a specific reason for your leave, you can skip this section.
*   **start_date** (`start_date`, date, required)
    Enter the date you wish to begin your leave. Ensure it's in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **end_date** (`end_date`, date, required)
    Enter the date you expect to return to work after the leave. Make sure it's in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **reason_for_leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required)
    Provide a detailed explanation for your leave request.
*   **medical_document** (`medical_document`, text, optional)
    Attach any relevant medical documents to support your leave request, if required by your HR department.
*   **doctor_name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional)
    Provide the name of your doctor or healthcare provider.
*   **appointment_date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional)
    Enter the date of your appointment with the doctor.
*   **doctor_phone_number** (`doctor_phone_number`, text, optional)
    Enter your doctor's contact number, if necessary.
*   **therapy_session_notes** (`therapy_session_notes`, note, optional)
    Briefly describe the progress of your therapy sessions.
*   **employee_signature** (`employee_signature`, select_one, required)
    Please confirm that you've filled out this form accurately and truthfully by selecting "Yes" from the dropdown menu.
*   **approver_name** (`approver_name`, select_one, optional)
    If an approver has already been assigned, select "Yes" here.
*   **approver_phone_number** (`approver_phone_number`, select_one, optional)
    If an approver has already been assigned, enter their contact number here.
*   **approver_email** (`approver_email`, select_one, optional)
    If an approver has already been assigned, enter their email address here.
*   **approver_signature** (`approver_signature`, select_one, optional)
    If an approver has already been assigned, select "Yes" here to confirm their approval.

## Tips

-   Always attach relevant medical documents, if required by your HR department.
-   Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
-   Ensure you've selected the correct options for "employee_signature" and "approver_signature".
-   Submit the form once complete and await approval from your approver.
-   If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your HR department or designated approver.
