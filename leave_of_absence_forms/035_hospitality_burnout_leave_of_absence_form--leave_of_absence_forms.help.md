# Hospitality Burnout Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospitality Burnout Leave Of Absence Form is used to submit requests for a leave of absence due to burnout.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety.
2. Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and honestly.
3. Discuss your request with your manager and have them sign the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Details** (request_details, text, not required):
	+ Use this field to explain your request for a leave of absence due to burnout.
* **Employee** (employee, text, not required):
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your official record.
* **Employee Type** (employee_type, select_one, not required):
	+ Select whether you are a management or non-management employee.
* **Start Date** (start_date, date, not required):
	+ Enter the date you are planning to start your leave of absence.
* **End Date** (end_date, date, not required):
	+ Enter the date you are planning to end your leave of absence.
* **Reason** (reason, text, not required):
	+ Provide more information about your request.
* **Comments** (comments, text, not required):
	+ Add any additional comments or details about your request.
* **Manager Comments** (manager_comments, text, required):
	+ Enter any comments or feedback from your manager.
* **Request Status** (request_status, select_one, not required):
	+ Select the status of your request (approved, declined, under review).
* **Employee Signature** (employee_signature, text, not required):
	+ Sign your name to indicate that you have submitted this form.
* **Manager Signature** (manager_signature, text, required):
	+ Sign your name to confirm that you have reviewed and approve the request.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have discussed your request with your manager before submitting this form.
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and honestly.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please reach out to your manager or HR representative.
