# Absentee Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is here to help you fill out the Absentee Report Form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the required information about yourself, such as your employee full name, employee ID, and email.
2.  Select your department and job title from the provided options.
3.  Enter the name of your reporting manager.
4.  For absence details, choose the type of absence that applies to you, select the dates of your absence, and fill in the total number of workdays missed.
5.  Provide the reason for your absence and whether prior notification was given.
6.  If applicable, indicate whether supporting documentation has been attached.
7.  For HR-related fields, select the action taken and provide any necessary notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee full name** (`Employee full name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee ID** (`Employee ID`, `text`, required): Enter your unique employee ID.
* **Employee email** (`Employee email`, `email`, required): Enter your official company email address.
* **Department** (`Department`, `text`, required): Select your department from the provided list.
* **Position or job title** (`Position or job title`, `text`, required): Select your job title from the provided list.
* **Reporting manager name** (`Reporting manager name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your reporting manager.
* **Date this report is filed** (`Date this report is filed`, `date`, required): Enter the date you're submitting this report.
* **First date of absence** (`First date of absence`, `date`, required): Enter the first date of your absence.
* **Last date of absence (or expected return date)** (`Last date of absence (or expected return date)`, `date`, required): Enter the last date of your absence or your expected return date.
* **Total workdays missed** (`Total workdays missed`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of workdays you've missed due to your absence.
* **Type of absence** (`Type of absence`, `select_one`, required): Choose the reason for your absence from the provided options.
* **Reason for absence** (`Reason for absence`, `text`, required): Fill in a detailed reason for your absence.
* **Was prior notification given before the absence** (`Was prior notification given before the absence`, `select_one`, required): Select whether prior notification was given.
* **Has supporting documentation been attached** (`Has supporting documentation been attached`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether supporting documentation has been attached, such as a medical certificate.
* **HR reviewer name** (`HR reviewer name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the HR reviewer.
* **Action taken** (`Action taken`, `select_one`, optional): Select the action taken by your supervisor or HR department.
* **HR notes** (`HR notes`, `text`, optional): If necessary, enter any notes from your HR department.

Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly, and if you have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out to your HR department for assistance.
