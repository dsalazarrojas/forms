# Newborn Care Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Newborn Care Leave Of Absence Request form is used to request a leave of absence for a new-born care-related reason. This form is designed to collect specific details about the employee's leave of absence, such as new-born care details, start and end dates, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Yes" option for the "Employee" field if the person requesting the leave is an employee of the organization.
2. Fill out the "Newborn Care Details" field with information about the new-born, including any relevant medical notes or doctor's orders.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the leave in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
4. Provide the employee ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
5. Enter a brief reason for the leave in the "Reason for Leave" field.
6. Select the department of the manager who will be managing the leave in the "Department" field.
7. Enter the name and email of the manager in the "Manager Name" and "Manager Email" fields, respectively.
8. Enter the phone number of the manager in the "Manager Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee (1)**: Select "Yes" if you are an employee of the organization.
* **Newborn Care Details (2)**: Provide information about the new-born, including any relevant medical notes or doctor's orders.
* **Start Date (3)**: Enter the start date of the leave.
* **End Date (4)**: Enter the end date of the leave.
* **Employee ID (5)**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **Reason for Leave (6)**: Enter a brief reason for the leave.
* **Department (7)**: Select the department of the manager who will be managing the leave.
* **Manager Name (8)**: Enter the name of the manager who will be managing the leave.
* **Manager Email (9)**: Enter the email of the manager who will be managing the leave.
* **Manager Phone (10)**: Enter the phone number of the manager who will be managing the leave.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have completed all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your manager or HR representative for guidance.
* This form is for internal use only, and any submitted data will be used to process the leave request internally.
