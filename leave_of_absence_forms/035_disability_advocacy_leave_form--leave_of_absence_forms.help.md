# Disability Advocacy Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist employees who need to take a leave of absence due to a disability-related reason. It will gather essential information for the employee's disability advocacy process. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure accurate representation of your situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the fields and their purpose.
2.  Enter your request form data on page 1.
3.  Select the reason for leaving (disability related or other) and provide any relevant details on pages 2 and 7.
4.  If applicable, enter your return to work date on page 3 and select your return-to-work status on page 8.
5.  If required, attach a medical certificate and sign your employee signature on page 4 and page 5, respectively.
6.  Enter the date of your leaving on page 6.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Request Form Data** (`request_form_data`): Enter a brief description of your leave request, including relevant details and concerns. 
*   **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`): Select 'Disability related' to indicate your reason for leave due to a disability-related reason, or choose 'Other - specify' to provide more detail. 
    If you select 'Other - specify', please enter a brief description of your reason for leaving. 
*   **Return to Work Date** (`return_to_work_date`): If you have a planned date to return to work, enter it here. This will help your employer prepare for your return. 
*   **Medical Certificate** (`medical_certificate`): Attach a medical certificate or document that supports your disability claim, if applicable. 
*   **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`): Please sign this form to confirm your identity and authenticate your leave request. 
*   **Date of Leaving** (`date_of_leaving`): Enter the date you plan to leave on. This will help your employer prepare for your absence. 
*   **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`): Select 'Disability related' to indicate your reason for leave due to a disability-related reason, or choose 'Other - specify' to provide more detail. 
*   **Return to Work Status** (`return_to_work_status`): Select 'Yes' if you plan to return to work after your leave, or 'No' if you do not plan to return.
