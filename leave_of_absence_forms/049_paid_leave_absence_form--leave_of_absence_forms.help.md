# Paid Leave Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request paid leave absence for various reasons such as vacation, sick leave, or other types of absences. It helps gather necessary information from employees in a structured manner, making it easier for managers to review and approve their requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully to understand each field's purpose.
2. Click on the "Submit Request" button to indicate if you are ready to submit your request.
3. Select the start and end dates of your leave.
4. Provide a brief reason for your leave absence.
5. Add any additional comments or details.
6. If your manager's approval is required, select the approval status.
7. Enter your name and your manager's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Request**: (Yes/No) - Indicates whether you are ready to submit your request for paid leave absence.
* **Start Date**: (Date) - Enter the start date of your leave absence.
* **End Date**: (Date) - Enter the end date of your leave absence.
* **Reason**: (Text) - Briefly describe the reason for your leave absence.
* **Comment**: (Text) - Add any additional comments or details about your leave absence.
* **Manager Approval**: (Yes/No) - Select the status of your manager's approval for your leave absence.
* **Employee Name**: (Text) - Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Manager Name**: (Text) - Enter your manager's name for identification purposes.

## Tips
- Be accurate and truthful when filling out the form.
- Make sure to provide all necessary information.
- Submit your request only once.
- Review your form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
