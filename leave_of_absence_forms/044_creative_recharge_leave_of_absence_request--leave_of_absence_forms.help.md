# Creative Recharge Leave of Absence Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit a leave of absence request to your manager for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a leave of absence request, please fill out the form with the required information and provide a valid reason for your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Info** (`base_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, department, job title, location, manager, and work number to validate your identity.
* **Time Off Request** (`time_off_request`, text, optional): Provide a clear and concise description of your request.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to start your leave of absence.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to end your leave of absence.
* **Reason for Time Off Request** (`reason_for_time_off_request`, text, optional): Enter a valid reason for your request, such as vacation, sick leave, or personal reasons.
* **Team Coverage** (`team_coverage`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you need team coverage during your absence.
* **Team Coverage Options** (`team_coverage_options`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate which team members will be covering your responsibilities while you are away.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about your request.
* **Approver Comments** (`approver_comments`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the approver's comments.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager who will be reviewing your request.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of your manager who will be reviewing your request.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your manager who will be reviewing your request.
* **Approval** (`approval`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate approval or denial of your request.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about your request.
* **Submitter Title** (`submitter_title`, text, optional): Enter your title.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, optional): Enter your department.
* **Submitter Job Title** (`submitter_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Submitter Location** (`submitter_location`, text, optional): Enter your location.
* **Submitter Manager** (`submitter_manager`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Submitter Work Number** (`submitter_work_number`, text, optional): Enter your work number.
* **Approver Manager** (`approver_manager`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Approver Work Number** (`approver_work_number`, text, optional): Enter your manager's work number.

Note: This form is intended to be filled out by employees, not managers. If you are a manager, please do not submit this form.
