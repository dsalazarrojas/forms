This form is used to request bereavement leave for an employee who is dealing with a family member's passing. The form is designed to collect essential information from the employee, including their relationship to the deceased, dates of absence, total work days missed, and any supporting documentation. This form is typically used by HR departments to review and process bereavement leave requests.

Before filling out this form, please ensure that you have not already submitted a request for this type of leave in the past 6 months. If you are unsure about the purpose or scope of bereavement leave in your company, please consult your HR representative or manager for guidance.

This form is divided into the following sections:

## Purpose
The Bereavement Leave Absence Form is used to request time off due to a family member's passing. This form is usually filled out by the employee to notify their HR department of their absence and to request the necessary time off.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Employee Information section with your name, ID number, and department.
2. Specify your relationship to the deceased.
3. Enter the dates of your absence (start and end dates).
4. Provide the total number of work days you will be missing.
5. Check if you have any supporting documentation (such as an obituary or death certificate).
6. Enter your expected return to work date.
7. If applicable, provide an emergency contact number for urgent business matters.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Full Name (2)**: This field is required and should be filled out with your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee ID Number (3)**: This field is required and should be your unique ID number issued by your HR department.
* **Department (4)**: This field should be filled out with the name of your department.
* **Immediate Supervisor or Manager (5)**: This field should be filled out with the name of your direct supervisor or manager.
* **Relationship to Deceased (7)**: This field is required and should be specified as one of the options provided (e.g., Parent, Spouse or Partner, Child, etc.).
* **Leave Start Date (8)**: This field is required and should be filled out with the first day of your absence.
* **Leave End Date (9)**: This field is required and should be filled out with the last day of your absence.
* **Total Work Days Requested (10)**: This field is required and should be filled out with the total number of work days you will be missing.
* **Documentation Provided (12)**: This field is required and should be checked if you have any supporting documentation (such as an obituary or death certificate).
* **Expected Return to Work Date (13)**: This field is required and should be filled out with your expected return date to work.
* **Contact Number During Absence (14)**: This field is optional and should be filled out with your contact number for urgent business matters only.
* **HR Approval Status (16)**: This field is not required and should be left blank until reviewed by the HR department.
* **HR Reviewer Comments (17)**: This field is not required and should be left blank until reviewed by the HR department.

Please note that this form is for internal use only and is intended to be filled out by employees to notify their HR department of their bereavement leave.
