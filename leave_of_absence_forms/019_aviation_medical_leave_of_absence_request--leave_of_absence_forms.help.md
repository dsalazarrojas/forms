# Aviation Medical Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for medical reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your personal information, including your name, ID, and email address.
2. Select your primary department and job title.
3. Specify the details of your leave request, including the start and end dates, type of leave, and medical certification status.
4. Provide your expected return date.
5. Confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name, including any relevant title (e.g., Mr./Mrs./Ms./Dr.).
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Employee Email Address** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your email address used for official communication.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your primary work group, such as Flight Operations (Pilots/Crew) or Ground Operations (Ramp/Gate).
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Enter your job title, such as Pilot or Maintenance Technician.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, required): Enter the first day of your absence.
* **Leave End Date (Estimated)** (`leave_end_date`, date, required): Enter the last day of your estimated absence.
* **Type of Medical Leave** (`leave_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of leave you are requesting, such as Short-term Medical Leave or Long-term Medical Leave.
* **Medical Certification Attached?** (`medical_certification`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have a physician's note attached.
* **Treating Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, optional): If you have a physician's note, enter the name of your treating physician.
* **Expected Return to Duty Date** (`return_date_estimate`, date, required): Enter the date you expect to be cleared for work.
* **Direct Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any extra information or clarification about your leave request.
* **Acknowledgment** (`employee_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
