# Unpaid Leave Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Unpaid Leave Absence Form is used to record the details of an employee's unpaid leave of absence from work. This form is used to inform management of the employee's leave and to document the necessary information for processing the leave.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's details: Please enter the employee's Employee Id, Name, and Email.
2. Provide leave details: Enter the start and end dates of the leave, as well as the number of days absent.
3. Add comments and notes: Provide any additional comments or notes from both the manager and employee.
4. Manager approval: Select the manager's approval status.
5. Employee acknowledgement: Select whether the employee acknowledges the leave.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Id**: Enter the employee's unique identification number.
* **Name**: Enter the employee's full name.
* **Email**: Enter the employee's email address.
* **Leave Reason**: Enter a brief description of the reason for the leave.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date the leave begins.
* **End Date**: Enter the date the leave ends.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date the employee is expected to return to work.
* **Days Absent**: Enter the number of days the employee will be absent.
* **Manager Comments**: Add any comments from the manager.
* **Employee Notes**: Add any comments from the employee.
* **Manager Signature**: Enter the manager's signature.
* **Manager Date**: Enter the date the manager signed the form.
* **Manager Approval**: Select 'Yes' to approve the leave, or 'No' to deny it.
* **Manager Approval Choices**: Add any additional comments for the manager's approval decision.
* **Employee Acknowledgement**: Select 'Yes' to acknowledge the leave, or 'No' to deny it.
* **Employee Acknowledgement Choices**: Add any additional comments for the employee's acknowledgement decision.
* **Manager Status**: Select the manager's status as either Active or Inactive.
* **Employee Status**: Select the employee's status as either Active or Inactive.
* **Leave Type**: Select whether the leave is 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Manager Comments**: Add any additional comments from the manager.
* **Employee Comments**: Add any additional comments from the employee.
* **Form Submitted By**: Add any additional comments about who submitted the form.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely before submitting the form.
