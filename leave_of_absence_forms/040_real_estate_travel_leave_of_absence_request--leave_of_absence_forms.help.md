# Real Estate Travel Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for travel related to real estate activities, such as visiting clients or property sites.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Travel Request**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are requesting a leave of absence for travel related to real estate activities.
2.  **Travel Dates**: Enter the start and end dates of your travel.
3.  **Travel Destination**: Enter the location you will be visiting.
4.  **Travel Description**: Briefly describe the purpose of your travel and who you will be visiting or meeting with.
5.  **Return Date**: Enter the date you will return to work.
6.  **Status**: If you are actively working or not during your leave, select the corresponding option. If this option is not relevant to you, just leave it as is.
7.  **Manager Review**: If your manager needs to review your travel request, select "Yes" or "No". If you're unsure about this, just leave it as is.
8.  **Approved By**: Select whether you want your request approved by your manager or not. If you're unsure, just leave it as is.
9.  **Reason for Leave**: Enter the reason for your leave of absence.
10.  **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your travel plans.
11.  **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
12.  **Supervisor Email**: Enter the email of your supervisor.
13.  **Supervisor Phone**: Enter the phone number of your supervisor.
14.  **Department**: Select "Yes" or "No" if your department is related to real estate.
15.  **Location**: Enter the location you will be traveling to.
16.  **Reason for Traveling**: Enter the reason you are traveling.
17.  **Purpose of Travel**: Enter the purpose of your travel.
18.  **Travel Days**: Enter the number of days you will be on leave.
19.  **Travel Hours**: Enter the number of hours you will be on leave.
20.  **Travel Cost**: Enter any estimated travel costs.
21.  **Approval Date**: Enter the date your request was approved.
22.  **Comments 2**: Enter any additional comments about your travel plans.
23.  **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager.
24.  **Manager Email**: Enter the email of your manager.
25.  **Manager Phone**: Enter the phone number of your manager.
