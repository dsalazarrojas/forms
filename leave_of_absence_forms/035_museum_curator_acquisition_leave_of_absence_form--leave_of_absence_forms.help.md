# Museum Curator Acquisition Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Museum Curator Acquisition Leave Of Absence Form is used for curators to request and submit information for leaves of absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. If a field is optional, you can leave it blank if not applicable.
3. Double-check that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your leave request for?** (`leave_request`, `text`, `required`): Briefly describe the purpose or reason for your leave request.
* **What type of leave?** (`leave_type`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., academic, personal, family, bereavement, jury duty, military, or other).
* **Start date** (`start_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the start date of your leave.
* **End date** (`end_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Reason for leave** (`reason`, `text`, `required`): Provide a detailed reason for your leave request.
* **Status of approval** (`approval_status`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the status of your approval (e.g., pending, approved, rejected, or denied).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, `optional`): Add any additional information or comments about your leave request.
* **Curator name** (`curator_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your name as the curator.
* **Curator contact** (`curator_contact`, `text`, `required`): Enter your contact information as the curator.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, `required`): Enter the department you are associated with.
* **Institution** (`institution`, `text`, `required`): Enter the institution you are associated with.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, `required`): Enter the contact person's name and information.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, `required`): Enter the contact email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Coverage Plan** (`coverage_plan`, `text`, `required`): Provide a plan for how your absence will be covered during your leave.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, `date`, `optional`): Enter the date when your leave was approved.
* **Approval Time** (`approval_time`, `time`, `optional`): Enter the time when your leave was approved.
* **Approval Status Date** (`approval_status_date`, `date`, `optional`): Enter the date when your leave status changed.
* **Curator Signature** (`curator_signature`, `text`, `required`): Sign your leave request as the curator.
* **Approver Signature** (`approver_signature`, `text`, `required`): Sign the leave request as the approver.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the approver.
* **Approver Contact** (`approver_contact`, `text`, `required`): Enter the contact information of the approver.
* **Approver Department** (`approver_department`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the department of the approver (if applicable).
* **Approver Institution** (`approver_institution`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the institution of the approver (if applicable).
* **Approver Signature Date** (`approver_signature_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date when the approver signed the leave request.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the museum's HR or management team.
