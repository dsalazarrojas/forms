# Retail Employee Burnout Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for retail employees to request a burnout leave of absence, which must be approved by both their manager and team lead before being reviewed by HR.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. On page 1, fill in the **retail_employee_burnout_leave_request** field with a brief description of your burnout leave of absence request.
2. On page 2, select your **manager_approval**. Choose the manager that you will be meeting with.
3. Select your **team_lead_approval**. Choose the team lead that will be reviewing your request.
4. If you have a specific start and end date for your burnout leave, enter the dates in the **retail_employee_burnout_leave_request_start_date** and **retail_employee_burnout_leave_request_end_date** fields.
5. Enter the number of days you will be taking for **retail_employee_burnout_leave_request_number_of_days**.
6. If you would like to add any additional comments, enter them in the **retail_employee_burnout_leave_request_comments** field.
7. Finally, select the **retail_employee_burnout_leave_request_status** and choose the status of your request. Please choose "Pending" if you have not received approval or denial from your manager and team lead, "Approved" if your request has been approved, or "Denied" if your request has been denied.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your information accurately and clearly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your manager or HR representative for guidance.
* Please ensure that your manager and team lead have reviewed and approved your request before selecting "Approved" as the status of your request.
