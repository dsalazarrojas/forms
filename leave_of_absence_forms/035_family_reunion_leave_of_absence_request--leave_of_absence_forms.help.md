# Family Reunion Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for a family reunion. It allows you to provide necessary information for your manager's review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your form title.
2. Provide your full name.
3. Enter your manager's full name.
4. Specify your relationship with the individuals you are visiting.
5. Enter the start date of your family reunion.
6. Enter the end date of your family reunion.
7. Describe the reason for your leave.
8. Specify the duration of your leave.
9. If necessary, include any additional information for your manager's review.
9. Click the "Submit" button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your leave request.
* **Full Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID or other official documents.
* **Manager Full Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the full name of your direct supervisor.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, text, optional): Specify how you are related to the individuals you are visiting (e.g., parent, sibling, child).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to start your family reunion.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to end your family reunion.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Briefly explain why you are requesting a family reunion leave of absence.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, optional): Enter the number of days you will be away.
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, note, optional): If necessary, include any additional information for your manager's review.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): This will automatically be populated with the current date and time.

## Tips
- Please ensure you submit your request at least 7 days in advance of your family reunion.
- Ensure you have reviewed and understood company leave of absence policies before submitting your request.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative.
