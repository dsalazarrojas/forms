# Symposium Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting a leave of absence for a symposium event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the form title.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter the start and end dates of your leave.
5. Provide a reason for your leave.
6. Specify the number of days you will be taking off.
7. Add any additional information that may be relevant to your leave request.
8. Select your manager from the dropdown list.
9. Choose the approval option for your leave.
10. Select the status of your leave request (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (1, text, optional): Enter a title for your leave request.
* **Requester Name** (2, text, optional): Enter your name so that your manager can identify you.
* **Requester Email** (3, email, optional): Enter your email address so that your manager can contact you.
* **Start Date** (4, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date** (5, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Reason** (6, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
* **Number of days** (7, number, optional): Enter the number of days you expect to be off.
* **Additional Information** (8, note, optional): Add any additional information that may be relevant to your leave request.
* **Manager** (9, select_one, optional): Select your manager from the dropdown list.
* **Approval** (10, select_multiple, optional): Choose the approval option for your leave request.
* **Status** (11, select_one, optional): Select the status of your leave request (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields, even if they are optional.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing information about your leave request.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always contact your manager for clarification.
