# Product Manager Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Manager Leave Of Absence Form is a document used to record and track employee leaves of absence. It captures essential information for managing employee absences, including start and end dates, reasons for leave, team coverage, and more. This form is a crucial tool for HR or management teams to process and approve leave requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the **Employee Name** field.
2. Enter the employee's ID in the **Employee Id** field.
3. Select your team member's manager name in the **Manager Name** field.
4. Choose the start date of the leave in the **Start Date** field.
5. Choose the end date of the leave in the **End Date** field.
6. Provide the reason for the leave in the **Reason For Leave** field.
7. Select the team member responsible for covering your team during your absence in the **Team Coverage** field.
8. Enter the number of days off in the **Days Off** field.
9. Choose the current status of your leave request in the **Status** field.
10. If necessary, provide additional comments or notes in the **Comments** and **Notes** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee requesting leave.
* **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the employee requesting leave.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Select the name of the manager responsible for the employee's team.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Choose the date when the leave starts.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Choose the date when the leave ends.
* **Reason For Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required): Provide the reason for the employee's leave.
* **Team Coverage** (`team_coverage`, select_one, required): Choose the team member responsible for covering the team during the employee's absence.
* **Days Off** (`days_off`, number, required): Enter the number of days off.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Choose the current status of the leave request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the leave request.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of approval for the leave request.
* **Approval By** (`approval_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person approving the leave request.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the leave request.
