# post-surgery_recovery_leave_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's post-surgery recovery progress to facilitate their return to work after surgery.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully review the form fields and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
- Complete each field accurately and clearly, and do not leave any fields blank unless specifically noted as optional.
- If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Information**: Enter your name and employee ID number. This is the primary contact information for your return to work process.
	* Label: Employee Information
	* Type: text
	* Required: Yes
	* Explanation: Enter your name and employee ID number.
- **Surgery Date**: Select the date of your surgery. This date will be used to track your recovery progress.
	* Label: surgery_date
	* Type: date
	* Required: Yes
	* Explanation: Select the date of your surgery.
- **Surgery Type**: Select the type of surgery you underwent. This will help us understand the nature of your recovery process.
	* Label: surgery_type
	* Type: select_one
	* Required: Yes
	* Explanation: Choose the type of surgery from the options provided.
- **Surgeon Name**: Enter the name of your surgeon. This is an optional field, but it's helpful to keep accurate medical records.
	* Label: surgeon_name
	* Type: text
	* Required: No
	* Explanation: If you wish to include the name of your surgeon, enter it here.
- **Hospital Name**: Enter the name of the hospital where your surgery took place. This is an optional field.
	* Label: hospital_name
	* Type: text
	* Required: No
	* Explanation: If you wish to include the name of the hospital, enter it here.
- **Surgery Length**: Describe the length of your surgery. This is an optional field, but it's helpful for our records.
	* Label: surgery_length
	* Type: text
	* Required: No
	* Explanation: If you have any relevant information about the surgery length, enter it here.
- **Medical Certificate**: Attach any relevant medical certificates or documents. This is an optional field.
	* Label: medical_certificate
	* Type: text
	* Required: No
	* Explanation: If you have any medical certificates or documents relevant to your recovery, attach them here.
- **Return Date**: Select the date you expect to return to work. This date is subject to change based on your recovery progress.
	* Label: return_date
	* Type: date
	* Required: No
	* Explanation: Select the expected date of your return to work. This date may be subject to change based on your recovery progress.
- **Doctor Contact**: Enter the contact information for your doctor. This is an optional field.
	* Label: doctor_contact
	* Type: text
	* Required: No
	* Explanation: If you wish to include your doctor's contact information, enter it here.
- **Medical Notes**: Enter any additional medical notes or comments. This is an optional field.
	* Label: medical_notes
	* Type: note
	* Required: No
	* Explanation: If you have any additional medical notes or comments, enter them here.
- **Employee Comments**: Enter any additional comments or requests. This is an optional field.
	* Label: employee_comments
	* Type: text
	* Required: No
	* Explanation: If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them here.
