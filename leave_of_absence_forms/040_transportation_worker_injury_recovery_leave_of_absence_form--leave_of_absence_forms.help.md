# Transportation Worker Injury Recovery Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and document an employee's injury recovery leave of absence, which is a period of absence from work due to an injury or illness. This form helps track and manage the employee's recovery progress, work status, and related medical documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the employee's personal details, including their name and occupation.
2. Provide a detailed description of the injury or illness.
3. Indicate the employee's current work status.
4. Enter the start and end dates of the leave of absence.
5. Specify the date when the employee is expected to return to work.
6. Attach a medical document with the date it was received.
7. Enter the doctor's name.
8. Specify the work rehabilitation period, which is the expected duration of time needed for the employee's recovery.
9. Indicate the employee's and manager's work status follow-up dates.
10. If applicable, enter the employee's and manager's email and phone numbers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the employee who is on leave.
* **Managers Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the manager responsible for the employee's leave.
* **Employee Occupation** (`employee_occupation`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's occupation or job title.
* **Injury Description** (`injury_description`, `text`, required): Describe the nature of the injury or illness in detail.
* **Employee Work Status** (`employee_work_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether the employee is currently active or inactive.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the employee's leave of absence began.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the employee's leave of absence ended.
* **Return To Work Date** (`return_to_work_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the employee is expected to return to work.
* **Medical Document Date** (`medical_document_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the medical document was received.
* **Doctors Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the doctor who provided the medical documentation.
* **Work Rehabilitation Period** (`work_rehabilitation_period`, `number`, required): Enter the expected duration of time needed for the employee's recovery.
* **Work Status Follow Up** (`work_status_follow_up`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the work status for follow-up purposes.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the manager's phone number.
