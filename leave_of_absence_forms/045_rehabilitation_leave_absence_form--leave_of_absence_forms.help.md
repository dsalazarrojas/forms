# Rehabilitation Leave Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of the Rehabilitation Leave Absence Form is to collect information on employees taking rehabilitation-related leaves of absence for medical or mental health reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the required approval from your supervisor before submitting this form.
2. Fill in all the required fields as accurately as possible.
3. If the employee has already been approved for this reason of absence in the past 12 months, do not submit a duplicate form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Number, Optional): Enter the employee's unique ID number. If you're unsure, please contact HR for the correct ID.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, Date, Optional): Select the date when the employee's leave of absence begins.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, Date, Optional): Select the date when the employee's leave of absence is expected to end.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, Text, Optional): Briefly describe the reason for the employee's leave of absence. Please provide a clear and concise explanation, e.g., "Rehabilitation for back surgery" or "Mental health treatment".
