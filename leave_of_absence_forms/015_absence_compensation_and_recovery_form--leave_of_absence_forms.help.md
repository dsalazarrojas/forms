# Absence Compensation And Recovery Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and track information about employee absences, including compensation and recovery plans. It is essential to accurately complete this form to ensure accurate and timely processing of absence claims and compensation adjustments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's information, including their full name, employee ID number, department, and email address.
2. Provide the absence details, such as the start and end dates of the absence, total number of days absent, type of absence, and reason for absence.
3. Indicate whether the absence was paid or unpaid and if a compensation or pay adjustment is needed.
4. If an adjustment is required, specify the amount of leave balance used (in hours or days) and describe the adjustment needed.
5. Confirm if a recovery or make-up plan is required.
6. If a recovery plan is required, provide a detailed description of the plan, including tasks to be completed and the expected completion date.
7. Update the supervisor approval status and add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee full name** (`employee_name`): Enter the employee's full name.
* **Employee ID number** (`employee_id`): Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter the department the employee belongs to.
* **Supervisor or manager name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter the name of the employee's supervisor or manager.
* **Employee email address** (`employee_email`): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Absence start date** (`absence_start_date`): Enter the date the absence started.
* **Absence end date** (`absence_end_date`): Enter the date the absence ended.
* **Total number of days absent** (`total_days_absent`): Enter the total number of days the employee was absent.
* **Type of absence** (`absence_type`): Choose the type of absence (Sick leave, Personal leave, Family or medical leave, Bereavement, Jury duty, or Other).
* **Reason for absence** (`absence_reason`): Provide a brief description of the reason for absence.
* **Was this absence paid or unpaid** (`paid_or_unpaid`): Indicate whether the absence was paid or unpaid.
* **Leave balance used (hours or days)** (`leave_balance_used`): If the absence was paid, specify the amount of leave balance used (in hours or days).
* **Is a compensation or pay adjustment needed** (`compensation_adjustment_needed`): Indicate if a compensation or pay adjustment is required.
* **Is a recovery or make-up plan required** (`recovery_plan_required`): Indicate if a recovery or make-up plan is required.
* **Expected date for recovery plan completion** (`recovery_completion_date`): If a recovery plan is required, enter the expected date for completion.
* **Supervisor approval status** (`supervisor_approval`): Update the supervisor's approval status (Approved, Pending review, or Denied).
* **Additional notes or comments** (`additional_notes`): Add any additional notes or comments related to the absence.
