# Mining Industry Vacation Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to help users understand how to complete the Mining Industry Vacation Leave Of Absence Form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the employee's ID in the **Employee ID** field.
2. Type the employee's name in the **Employee Name** field.
3. Select the start date of the leave using the calendar icon next to the **Start Date** field.
4. Select the end date of the leave using the calendar icon next to the **End Date** field.
5. Provide a brief description of the reason for the leave in the **Reason** field.
6. Choose the status of the leave by selecting either **Active** or **Inactive** from the dropdown menu next to the **Status** field.
7. If required, provide additional comments in the **Mining Industry Vacation Leave Request Comments** field.
8. Choose whether the leave was approved by selecting **Yes** or **No** from the dropdown menu next to the **Approval By** field.
9. If approved, select the date of approval using the calendar icon next to the **Approved Date** field.
10. If necessary, choose the actions to be taken on the leave by selecting multiple options from the dropdown menu next to the **Mining Industry Vacation Leave Form Actions** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter the unique identifier assigned to the employee.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Type the name of the employee as it appears on their official documents.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Select the date when the leave is expected to start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Select the date when the leave is expected to end.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the reason for the leave.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the leave from the available options (Active or Inactive).
* **Mining Industry Vacation Leave Request Comments** (`mining_industry_vacation_leave_request_comments`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or clarifications about the leave.
* **Approval By** (`approval_by`, select_one, required): Choose whether the leave was approved or not (Yes or No).
* **Approved Date** (`approved_date`, date, required): If approved, select the date of approval.
* **Mining Industry Vacation Leave Form Actions** (`mining_industry_vacation_leave_form_actions`, select_multiple, required): Choose the actions to be taken on the leave by selecting multiple options.
