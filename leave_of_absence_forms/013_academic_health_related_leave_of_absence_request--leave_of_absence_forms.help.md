# Academic Health Related Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a leave of absence due to health-related reasons. It is essential to provide accurate and truthful information to ensure a smooth review and approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Identify Yourself
Complete the form by providing your:
- Student full name
- Institutional student ID

### Step 2: Provide Contact Information
Enter your contact email address for notifications and follow-up purposes.

### Step 3: Describe Your Health Issue
Provide a brief description of your health issue and its impact on your studies.

### Step 4: Attach Supporting Documentation (Optional)
If applicable, provide supporting documentation such as medical certificates or notes.

### Step 5: Specify Your Leave Duration
Enter the duration of your requested leave.

### Step 6: Request Accommodations (Optional)
If needed, specify any accommodation requests you require when you return.

### Step 7: Provide Emergency Contact Information (Optional)
Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.

### Step 8: Confirm Consent
Select your consent option to confirm the accuracy of the provided information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student full name** (required): Enter your full name as it appears on your institution's records.
- **Student id** (required): Enter your institutional student ID number.
- **Contact email** (required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for notifications and follow-up.
- **Summary of health issue** (required): Provide a brief description of your health condition and its impact on your studies.
- **Supporting documentation** (optional): Attach any relevant medical certificates, notes, or other supporting documents.
- **Requested duration** (required): Specify the duration of your leave of absence.
- **Accommodation requests** (optional): Enter any accommodation requests you have, such as support services or equipment.
- **Emergency contact** (optional): Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Consent and signature** (required): Select 'I agree' to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Tips
- Be truthful and accurate when filling out this form.
- Ensure you have not submitted a similar request in the past.
- Provide supporting documentation when applicable.
- Specify your accommodations needs clearly.
- Confirm your consent carefully.
