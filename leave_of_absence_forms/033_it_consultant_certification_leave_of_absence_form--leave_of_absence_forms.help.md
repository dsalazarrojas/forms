# IT Consultant Certification Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The IT Consultant Certification Leave Of Absance Form is a tool for HR and other departments to manage IT consultant certification leave requests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your name.
2. Select the date when you obtained your certification.
3. Provide a reason for your leave.
4. Indicate when your leave starts and ends.
5. Choose how your manager will be contacted (IT Consultant, Team Manager, Senior Manager).
6. Confirm if your manager will be contacted.
7. If your leave was denied, choose the reason for the denial.
8. If the reason for denial was "Other", provide additional details.
9. Fill in any other comments or details about your leave.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **it consultant certification leave of absence form**:
	+ This field is the title of the form.
* **it consultant name**:
	+ This is your name as an IT consultant.
* **certification date**:
	+ Enter the date when you obtained your certification.
* **reason for leave**:
	+ Provide a brief explanation for your leave.
* **start date**:
	+ Enter the date your leave starts.
* **end date**:
	+ Enter the date your leave ends.
* **status**:
	+ Choose the status of your leave (Approved, Denied, Pending).
* **manager approval**:
	+ Select how your manager will be contacted (IT Consultant, Team Manager, Senior Manager).
* **training offered**:
	+ Confirm if any training will be offered during your leave.
* **reason for denial**:
	+ If your leave was denied, choose the reason for the denial.
* **other reason denial**:
	+ If "Other" was selected for the reason for denial, provide more details.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and clear information to ensure a smooth process.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your manager or HR representative.
* Make sure to review and submit your form carefully before finalizing it.
