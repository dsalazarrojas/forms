# Absence Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Absence Request Form is used to submit a formal request for leave from work. This form is used to request time off for various reasons such as vacation, sick leave, or personal reasons. The form helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and communicated to your supervisor and HR department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your personal information**: Fill in your employee name, ID, and email address.
2. **Choose your absence type**: Select the type of leave you are requesting, such as vacation, sick leave, or family medical leave.
3. **Enter dates and duration**: Specify the start and end dates of your absence, as well as the total number of days you are requesting.
4. **Explain your reason**: Provide a brief explanation for your absence, such as a vacation or a medical issue.
5. **Arrange coverage**: If possible, arrange for someone to cover your responsibilities during your absence.
6. **Provide additional details**: If needed, provide any additional information or comments about your absence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee full name** (`employee_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee identification number.
* **Email address** (`employee_email`): Enter your official email address.
* **Department** (`department`): Select your department name.
* **Supervisor name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Date of request** (`request_date`): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Type of leave requested** (`leave_type`): Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., vacation, sick leave, etc.).
* **Requested start date of absence** (`start_date`): Enter the date you would like your absence to start from.
* **Requested end date of absence** (`end_date`): Enter the date you would like your absence to end on.
* **Total number of days requested** (`total_days_requested`): Enter the total number of days you are requesting for your absence.
* **Is any day a partial day absence** (`partial_day`): Select if any day of your absence is a partial day (e.g., morning only).
* **Reason for absence** (`reason_for_absence`): Provide a brief explanation for your absence.
* **Have you arranged for someone to cover your responsibilities?** (`coverage_arranged`): If possible, arrange for someone to cover your responsibilities during your absence.
* **Name of person covering your duties** (`coverage_person`): Enter the name of the person covering your duties (optional).
* **Can you be contacted during your absence?** (`contact_while_absent`): Select if you can be contacted during your absence.
* **Additional notes or comments** (`additional_notes`): Provide any additional information or comments about your absence (optional).
