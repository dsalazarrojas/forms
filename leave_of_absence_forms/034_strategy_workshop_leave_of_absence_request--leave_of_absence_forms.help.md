# Strategy Workshop Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Strategy Workshop Leave Of Absence Request" form is used to submit a leave of absence request for a specified period. This form helps the employee communicate their absence to the organization, allowing them to plan and manage work tasks accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee ID number in the "employee_id" field.
2. Select the start and end dates of your leave of absence in the "start_date" and "end_date" fields, respectively.
3. Enter the reason for your leave in the "reason_for_leave" field.
4. Specify the duration of your leave in the "leave_duration" field.
5. Enter your supervisor's email address in the "supervisor_email" field.
6. Optionally, add any comments about your leave request in the "comments" field.
7. Select the person who submitted the request (you, your supervisor, or someone else) in the "submitted_by" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_id** (Number, Required): Enter your unique employee ID number to identify yourself.
* **start_date** (Date, Required): Select the start date of your leave of absence.
* **end_date** (Date, Required): Select the end date of your leave of absence.
* **reason_for_leave** (Text, Optional): Provide a clear reason for your leave of absence.
* **leave_duration** (Number, Optional): Specify the duration of your leave of absence (in days).
* **supervisor_email** (Email, Optional): Enter your supervisor's email address for notifications.
* **comments** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your leave request.
* **submitted_by** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select who submitted the request (you, your supervisor, or someone else).

Note that some fields are optional, but it's essential to fill in all required fields to ensure that your leave request is processed correctly.
