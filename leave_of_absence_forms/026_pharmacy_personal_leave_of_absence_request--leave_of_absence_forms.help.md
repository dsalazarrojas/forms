Pharmacy Personal Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
=====================================================

## Purpose

The Pharmacy Personal Leave Of Absence Request form is used to request a leave of absence for a pharmacy employee. The form is completed by the employee and their manager to request a leave, and to track the status of the leave request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name and email address in the "Employee Name" and "Employee Email" fields.
2. Select your job title from the "Job Title" dropdown menu.
3. Specify whether you are requesting a leave in your department by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Department" field.
4. Indicate whether your reporting manager is requesting a leave by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Reporting Manager" field.
5. Enter the start and end dates of your leave in the "Leave Start Date" and "Leave End Date" fields.
6. Specify the duration of your leave in the "Leave Duration" field.
7. Select the type of leave you are requesting from the "Leave Type" dropdown menu.
8. Select the status of your leave from the "Leave Status" dropdown menu.
9. Indicate whether your leave has been approved by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Leave Approved Manager" field.
10. If your leave has been approved, enter the date it was approved in the "Leave Approved Date" field.
11. Enter any additional comments or reasons for your leave in the "Leave Comment" field.
12. Acknowledge receipt of the form by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Employee Acknowledgement" field.
13. Acknowledge approval or rejection of the form by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Manager Acknowledgement" field.
14. Enter any reason for rejection of your leave in the "Leave Rejection Reason" field.
15. Enter any comments from the leave requesting manager in the "Leave Requesting Manager Comment" field.
16. Enter any comments from the leave approving manager in the "Leave Approved Manager Comment" field.
