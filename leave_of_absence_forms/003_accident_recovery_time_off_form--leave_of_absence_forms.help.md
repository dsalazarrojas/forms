# Accident Recovery Time Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Accident Recovery Time Off Form is designed to help employees report accidents or injuries that occurred on or off the job. This form gathers essential information necessary for a smooth recovery process, including details about the employee's condition, requested leave dates, and supporting documentation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you are authorized to access and complete this form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the questions and the required fields.
* Fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Submit the form for review and approval by your supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: This field is required to identify the employee who experienced the accident or injury. Enter their full name as listed in HR records.
* **Employee ID**: If applicable, enter the internal employee number. This helps HR track employee information and ensures accurate record-keeping.
* **Department**: Enter the department or team the employee belongs to. This helps HR understand the employee's role and responsibilities.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the best number to reach the employee. This enables HR and supervisors to communicate effectively with the employee.
* **Email Address**: Enter the employee's work or personal email address for notifications and updates.
* **Date of Accident**: Enter the date when the accident or injury occurred.
* **Injury Description**: Describe the nature of the injury or accident.
* **Treating Provider**: If applicable, enter the name of the doctor, clinic, or hospital providing care.
* **Anticipated Return Date**: If known, enter the expected date of return to work.
* **Requested Leave Start Date**: Enter the first date of requested time off.
* **Requested Leave End Date**: Enter the last date of requested time off.
* **Paid Leave Requested**: Indicate whether paid leave is requested.
* **Supporting Documents Uploaded**: Indicate if supporting documents, such as medical notes or accident reports, have been uploaded.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the supervisor's name for approval and follow-up.
* **Employee Signature**: Type your full name to sign electronically. This confirms that you have reviewed and submitted the form accurately.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any extra details for HR consideration.

Note: This form requires electronic signatures. Ensure that you sign it carefully and accurately.
