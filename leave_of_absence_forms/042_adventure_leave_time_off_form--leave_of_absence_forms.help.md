# Adventure Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adventure Leave Time Off Form is a request to take a leave of absence from work, allowing employees to take breaks or time off for various personal or professional reasons. This form is used to record and manage employee leave requests, ensuring that the necessary information is collected and reviewed by managers for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name in the "Employee name" field.
2.  Select the department you belong to in the "Department" field.
3.  Choose the start and end dates of your leave in the "Leave start date" and "Leave end date" fields, respectively.
4.  Select the type of leave you are requesting from the "Leave type" dropdown menu.
5.  If you have a brief explanation for your leave, enter it in the "Reason for leave" field.
6.  If you wish to provide contact information for when you are out, enter it in the "Contact method during leave" field.
7.  If you want your manager to be notified of this form submission, you can select "Approved" or "Denied" from the "Manager approval status" field.
8.  Finally, select the "Submit" button to submit your leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **-- Leave Request --** (Note): This is a title section that provides a heading for the form.
*   **Employee name** (`employee_name`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documentation.
*   **Department** (`department`, required): Select the team or unit you belong to for better processing and tracking of your leave request.
*   **Leave start date** (`leave_start_date`, required): Enter the date when you plan to start your leave, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Leave end date** (`leave_end_date`, required): Enter the date when you plan to end your leave, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Leave type** (`leave_type`, required): Choose the applicable leave reason from the list:
    *   **Vacation**: For planned vacations or breaks.
    *   **Personal**: For personal reasons, such as family matters or health issues.
    *   **Medical**: For health reasons, such as illness or recovery periods.
    *   **Unpaid**: For unpaid leave or other reasons not listed above.
*   **Reason for leave** (`reason_for_leave`, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your leave request, if needed.
*   **Contact method during leave** (`contact_during_leave`, optional): Provide an optional contact method, such as phone number or email, for when you are out.
*   **Manager approval status** (`manager_approval`, optional): Select "Approved" or "Denied" for your manager's review and approval.
*   **Submission date** (`submission_date`, required): This field is automatically populated with the date you submit this form.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your leave request is properly processed.
*   Double-check your leave dates for accuracy to avoid any confusion or miscommunication.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR department.
