# software_conference_leave_of_absence_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The software conference leave of absence form is used to request time off for employee attendance at software conferences. This form helps the HR department to track and manage such requests, ensuring that employee absences are approved or denied in a timely and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the conference you wish to attend.
2. Select the start and end dates of your requested absence.
3. Provide your name and the duration of your requested absence.
4. Specify the conference duration you will be attending.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Conference Name (text)** (`conference_name`): Enter the name of the software conference you wish to attend.
- **Conference Dates (date)** (`conference_dates`): Enter the start and end dates of your absence.
- **Employee Name (text)** (`employee_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your employee card.
- **Start Date (date)** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of your absence.
- **End Date (date)** (`end_date`): Enter the end date of your absence.
- **Conference Duration (number)** (`conference_duration`): Enter the duration of the conference you will be attending.
- **Approval Status (select_one)** (`approval_status`): Select the status of your request: Approved, Pending, or Denied.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure your request is processed correctly.
- If your request is pending, please allow sufficient time for the approval process to be completed.
- If your request is denied, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for further guidance.
