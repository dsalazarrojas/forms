# National Holiday Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by employees to request national holiday absence, providing necessary details to their manager or HR department for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name as it appears on their official ID.
2. Choose the national holiday for which the absence is requested.
3. Select the start and end times of the absence, in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00, 17:30).
4. Provide the number of days off requested.
5. Choose the purpose of the leave (National Holiday or Other).
6. If other, describe the reason in the notes field.
7. Provide a valid email address and phone number for the employee.
8. Confirm that you are the employee submitting this request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **National Holiday Absence Form**: This is the main field to input your reason for filling out this form, it is a title.
* **employee_name (employee_name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official ID.
* **date_of_absence**: Select the date of the national holiday you want to take off.
* **start_time**: Select the start time of your absence.
* **end_time**: Select the end time of your absence.
* **number_of_days_off**: Enter the number of days you want to take off.
* **purpose_of_leave**: Choose whether the absence is for a National Holiday or Other.
*   If you choose Other, describe your reason in the **notes** field.
*   National Holiday: Select this option if you're taking national holiday absence.
*   Other: Select this option if your absence is for other reasons.
*   **manager_approval**: This field will ask you to approve the leave. 
*   If you want to take leave, choose National Holiday and if you want to take leave for other reasons, choose Other.
*   **notes**: Enter any additional information that will help to process your request.
*   If you've chosen "Other" as the purpose of leave, enter your reason here.
*   **email (email)**: Enter your email address.
*   This will help us to contact you if there's an issue with your request.
*   **phone_number (phone_number)**: Enter your phone number.
*   This will help us to contact you in case of emergency.
*   **employee_confirmation (employee_confirmation)**: Confirm that you are the employee submitting this request.
*   Select True if you are the employee.
*   Select False if this is not your request.
