**Legal Publication Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for a legal publication. Please fill out the form with the required information to ensure that your request is properly processed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including your First name and Last name.
2. Choose the correct Publication type from the list.
3. Select the Request start and Request end dates for your leave.
4. Specify the Reason for your leave request.
5. Choose the Publication schedule you require during your leave (Print, Digital, or Both).
6. Select the teams that need to approve your leave (Production team, Editorial team, Production manager, or Editor-in-chief).
7. Choose the Publication frequency (Weekly, Fortnightly, Monthly, Quarterly, or As needed).
8. Select the Leave duration for your request (Less than 1 day, 1 to 3 days, 4 to 7 days, or More than 7 days).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Publication type** (`publication_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of publication you are requesting leave for (Book, Magazine, Newspaper, or Other).
* **Request start date** (`request_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your leave request.
* **Request end date** (`request_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your leave request.
* **Reason for leave request** (`reason_for_leave_request`, text, optional): Please provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
* **Publication schedule** (`publication_schedule`, select_multiple, optional): Select which publication schedule you require during your leave (Print, Digital, or Both).
* **Leave approvals** (`leave_approvals`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the teams that need to approve your leave request (Production team, Editorial team, Production manager, or Editor-in-chief).
* **Publication frequency** (`publication_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose the frequency of your publication (Weekly, Fortnightly, Monthly, Quarterly, or As needed).
* **Leave duration** (`leave_duration`, select_multiple, optional): Select the duration of your leave request (Less than 1 day, 1 to 3 days, 4 to 7 days, or More than 7 days).
