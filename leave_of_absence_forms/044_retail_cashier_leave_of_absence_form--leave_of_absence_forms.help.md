# Retail Cashier Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Cashier Leave Of Absence Form is a document used to report a leave of absence for a cashier, providing essential details necessary for processing the request. This form serves as a communication channel between the cashier and management, ensuring that all relevant information is captured and reviewed.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, please make sure you have all the necessary information ready. Here's a step-by-step guide to completing the form:

1.  Fill in your **Employee Name**, which is your official name as per your HR records.
2.  Enter your **Employee Email**, which is your company email address.
3.  Provide your **Employee Phone**, which should be your work or personal phone number where you can be reached.
4.  Choose the **Start Date** and **End Date** of your leave, ensuring you are accurate with the dates.
5.  Select the reason for your **Reason For Leave** from the available options. Please be specific about your reason to facilitate processing.
6.  Ensure your **Manager Approval** is obtained or indicate that it is not required.
7.  Choose your current **Employee Status** as either Active or Inactive.
8.  Enter the **Retail Store Name** where you are based.
9.  Enter your **Employee ID**, a unique identifier issued by the company.
10. If you are a store-based manager, select **Store Manager Approve** to indicate your approval.
11.  Enter the **Manager ID** of the person approving or not approving your leave.
12.  Select the type of leave you are taking, such as vacation or sick leave.
13.  Finally, indicate whether your **Store Approve** is required or not.
