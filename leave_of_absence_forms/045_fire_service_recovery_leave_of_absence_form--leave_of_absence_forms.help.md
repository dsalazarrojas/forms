# Fire Service Recovery Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Service Recovery Leave of Absence Form is used to document and track employee leave of absence information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and approvals before proceeding.
2. Fill in the Employee Name field with the name of the employee taking the leave.
3. Enter the Start Date and End Date of the leave, using the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Provide a brief Reason For Leave, describing the reason for the employee's absence.
5. If applicable, enter the Return Date, when the employee is expected to return to work.
6. Select the type of leave the employee is taking (Yes/No).
7. Indicate the Department the employee is affiliated with (Yes/No).
8. Choose the Leave Status of the employee (Active/Inactive).
9. If applicable, enter the Fire Date, the date the employee's employment is terminated.
10. Check if Supervisor Approval is required (Yes/No).
11. Enter the Request Date, when the request was made.
12. Enter the Employee ID, a unique identifier for the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fire Service Recovery Leave Form**: Enter the details of the employee's leave of absence.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee taking the leave.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date the employee began their leave, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **End Date**: Enter the date the employee is expected to end their leave, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Reason For Leave**: Enter a brief description of the reason for the employee's absence.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date the employee is expected to return to work, if applicable.
* **Leave Type**: Select whether the employee is taking a leave of absence (Yes) or not (No).
* **Department**: Indicate which department the employee is affiliated with (Yes) or not (No).
* **Leave Status**: Select whether the employee is Active or Inactive.
* **Fire Date**: If the employee's employment is terminated, enter the date of termination, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Supervisor Approval**: Check if supervisor approval is required for the leave (Yes) or not (No).
* **Request Date**: Enter the date the request was made, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employee ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the employee.
