# Banking Personal Leave of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for a personal reason, which may include but not limited to, family leave, medical leave, bereavement, educational leave, sabbatical, military leave, or other reasons. The completed form will be reviewed and approved by HR and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee information: 
   - Fill in your full name as it appears on your personnel file.
   - Enter your employee ID as issued by the company.
   - Enter your work email address.
   - Enter your contact phone number.
   - Select your department from the provided options.
2. Enter work details: 
   - Enter your job title.
   - Enter the date you were hired.
   - Select the type of leave you are requesting.
3. Provide leave details: 
   - Enter the first day of your leave.
   - Enter the last day of your leave.
   - Select the length of your leave.
   - Select whether you want your leave to be paid or unpaid.
   - Enter the number of days you are requesting to be away.
   - Select the schedule of your leave.
4. Confirm benefits: 
   - Select whether you want your benefits to be continued or suspended.
5. Confirm with your manager: 
   - Enter your manager's name.
   - Select whether your manager has approved your request.
6. Submit the form: 
   - Date and sign the form.
