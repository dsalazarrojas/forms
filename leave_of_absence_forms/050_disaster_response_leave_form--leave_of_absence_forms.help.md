# Disaster Response Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disaster Response Leave Form is a tool for employees to request leave due to emergency situations that affect their ability to work. It is used to gather necessary information for the HR department to process and manage leave requests effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information in the form accurately and completely.
2. Be sure to use the correct format for dates (YYYY-MM-DD).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Your full name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Your employee ID number.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, required): The date you plan to start taking leave. This date should be inclusive, meaning you will not work on this day or any subsequent days listed in the form.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, required): The date you plan to end taking leave. This date should be inclusive, meaning you will not work on this day or any subsequent days listed in the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact your HR representative for assistance.
* Be sure to submit your form as soon as possible to ensure timely processing of your leave request.
