# Leave Without Pay Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record a leave without pay absence from work. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure the correct processing and categorization of absences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **start date** of your leave without pay absence.
2. Fill in the **end date** of your leave without pay absence.
3. Indicate whether you are taking a **leave with reason** ("Yes" or "No") and provide a brief explanation in the **comments** field if necessary.
4. Select the **assigned tool(s)** that you will be using during your absence (if applicable).
5. Choose the **category** that best describes your leave without pay absence (Option 1 or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **leave_without_pay_absence**: Enter a brief description of your leave without pay absence.
* **start_date**: The date you will begin your leave without pay absence.
* **end_date**: The date you will end your leave without pay absence.
* **reason_for_absence**: Select "Yes" if you are taking a leave with a reason, or "No" if you are not. If "Yes", please provide a brief explanation in the **comments** field.
* **comments**: Provide a brief explanation for your leave without pay absence, if applicable.
* **assigned_tool**: Select the tool(s) that you will be using during your absence (if applicable).
* **assigned_tool_id**: Select the tool ID associated with the tool you will be using during your absence (if applicable).
* **form_id**: Select the ID that best describes your leave without pay absence (Option 1 or Other).
* **category_id**: Select the ID that best describes your leave without pay absence (Option 1 or Other).
* **category_name**: Enter the name that best describes your leave without pay absence.
* **category_name**: This is a placeholder field and should be removed in the production version.
