# Development Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Development Leave Of Absence Request form is designed to help employees request time off for development purposes. It will guide you through the necessary information to facilitate a smooth and efficient approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee details in the **Employee Details** section.
2. Provide the start and end dates for your leave in the **Start Date** and **End Date** fields, respectively.
3. Specify the reason for your leave in the **Reason For Leave** field.
4. Select the duration of your leave in the **Duration of Leave** field.
5. Choose the leave type from the **Leave Type** dropdown menu.
6. Select the teams that will be covered during your leave in the **Coverage Plan** field.
7. Add any comments or notes in the **Comments** field.
8. Ensure that your supervisor or team leader has approved your request by selecting the status in the **Approval Status** field.
9. Provide any additional comments or explanations in the **Manager Comments** and **Team Leader Comments** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details**:
  * **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
  * **Name**: Enter your full name.
  * **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your leave.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Reason For Leave**: Enter a clear and valid reason for your request.
* **Duration of Leave**: Select the duration of your leave.
* **Leave Type**: Choose the type of leave you are requesting.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your leave.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your leave.
* **Leave Type**: Choose the type of leave you are requesting.
* **Manager Approval**: Choose the status of your request.
* **Team Leader Approval**: Choose the status of your request.
* **Coverage Plan**: Select the teams that will be covered during your leave.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or notes.
* **Manager Comments**: Add any comments or notes from your manager.
* **Team Leader Comments**: Add any comments or notes from your team leader.
* **Approval Status**: Select the status of your request.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you submitted the request.
* **Return Date**: Enter the expected return date.
* **Status**: Select the status of your request.
* **Comments 1**: Add any comments or notes.
* **Status 2**: Select the status of your request.
* **Reason 2**: Enter the reason for the status.
* **Employee Comments**: Add any comments or notes from the employee.
* **Manager Comments**: Add any comments or notes from the manager.
* **Team Leader Comments**: Add any comments or notes from the team leader.

Note: The **Comments** field is a text area where you can add any additional information or explanations for your request. The **Manager Comments** and **Team Leader Comments** fields are also for comments or notes from your manager and team leader, respectively.
