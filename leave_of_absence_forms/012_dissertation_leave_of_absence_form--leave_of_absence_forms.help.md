# dissertation_leave_of_absence_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for students to request a leave of absence from the university. Fill out the form with your details and information to facilitate the leave of absence process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully, providing accurate information.
- Make sure to complete each field according to the field-by-field explanation below.
- Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Info** (Student Info, text, **optional**):
    This field is for providing your personal details.
* **Dissertation Details** (Dissertation Details, text, **optional**):
    This field is for specifying your dissertation-related information.
* **Absences Details** (Absences Details, text, **optional**):
    This field is for specifying your absences-related information.
* **University Details** (University Details, text, **optional**):
    This field is for providing university-related information.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide clear and concise answers.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your university administration.
