# Court Appearance Leave Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Court Appearance Leave Form is used to request time off for an employee's court appearance. This form helps to track and approve leave requests for employees who need to attend court hearings or other court-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have a legitimate court appearance scheduled and only submit the form for actual court appearances, not for other reasons.
2. Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields.
3. Submit the form to your supervisor or HR representative for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Case Type** (`Case Type`, select_one, Required): Select the type of court appearance (Civil or Criminal).
	+ This field determines the type of court appearance the employee is undertaking.
* **Case Number** (`Case Number`, text, Required): Enter the case number associated with the court appearance.
	+ This field helps identify the specific case the employee is appearing in.
* **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, text, Required): Enter the employee's name.
	+ This field identifies the employee requesting time off for the court appearance.
* **Employee ID** (`Employee ID`, text, Required): Enter the employee's ID number.
	+ This field helps track the employee's unique identifier for approval and processing.
* **Court Location** (`Court Location`, text, Optional): Enter the address or location of the court where the appearance will be held.
	+ This field provides additional context for the court appearance, but is not required.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, Optional): Enter the date of the court appearance.
	+ This field indicates when the court appearance is scheduled to take place.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, Optional): Enter the date when the employee expects to return from the court appearance.
	+ This field indicates when the employee expects to be back at work.
* **Judge Name** (`Judge Name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the presiding judge.
	+ This field provides additional context for the court appearance, but is not required.
* **Case Citation** (`Case Citation`, text, Optional): Enter any relevant case citations or citations relevant to the case.
	+ This field provides additional information about the case, but is not required.
* **Court Appearance Form** (`Court Appearance Form`, text, Required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the court appearance.
	+ This field is a text box for any additional information about the court appearance.
