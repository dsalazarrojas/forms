# Stress-Leave-Time-Off-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stress-Leave-Time-Off-Form is a form used to request a leave of absence due to work-related stress. This form is for employees who need time off to manage their stress levels and return to work feeling refreshed and ready to perform their job duties effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have a legitimate reason for requesting time off due to stress.
2. Fill out the required fields:
	* **User ID**: Enter your employee ID.
	* **Leave Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to start your leave of absence.
	* **Leave End Date**: Enter the date you plan to end your leave of absence.
	* **Stress Reason**: Enter a brief explanation of your stress-related reason for requesting time off.
	* **Manager Approval Status**: Select "Active" if your manager has approved your request.
	* **Employee Approval Status**: Select "Active" if you have approved your own request.
	* **Approval Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your request.
	* **Approval Date**: Enter the date your request was approved.
	* **Approval Time**: Enter the time your request was approved (optional).
	* **Approval Status Description**: Enter any additional comments about your approval status (optional).
	* **Employee Approval Date**: Enter the date you received approval for your request (optional).
	* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email address.
	* **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
	* **Manager Extension**: Enter your manager's extension number (if applicable).
	* **Manager Department**: Enter your manager's department.
	* **Manager Team**: Enter your manager's team.
	* **Manager Division**: Enter your manager's division.
	* **Manager Location**: Enter your manager's location.
	* **Manager Building**: Enter your manager's building.
	* **Manager Floor**: Enter your manager's floor number (if applicable).
	* **Manager Office**: Enter your manager's office number (if applicable).
	* **Manager Extension 2**: Enter your manager's extension number 2 (if applicable).
	* **Manager Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your manager's involvement.
