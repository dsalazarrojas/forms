# Sabbatical Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a sabbatical leave, which is an extended period of absence from work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the request information (Page 1).
2. Provide employee information, including your name and department (Page 2).
3. Specify the dates of your sabbatical leave (Page 3).
4. Provide details about your sabbatical leave, including the type, duration, and frequency (Page 4).
5. Specify the status of your leave, including whether it is active or inactive (Page 5).
6. Attach any supporting documents, such as a letter from your manager or departmental leave policy (Page 6).
7. Specify the date you would like to start your leave and the date it will be approved (Page 7 and 8).
8. Provide any additional comments or details about your leave request (Page 9 and 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Information (Page 1)** 
    * This is the starting point of your form. Fill in a brief description of your sabbatical leave request.
* **Employee Information (Page 2)** 
    * Please enter your name and department to ensure your request is correctly attributed to you.
* **Dates (Page 3)** 
    * Specify the start and end dates of your sabbatical leave. Ensure they are accurate and consistent.
* **Leave Details (Page 4)** 
    * This is where you provide information about your sabbatical leave, including the type, duration, and frequency of your leave.
* **Sabbatical Type (Page 4)** 
    * Indicate whether you are requesting a sabbatical or not.
* **Start Date and End Date (Page 4)** 
    * Enter the start and end dates of your sabbatical leave.
* **Reason for Leave (Page 4)** 
    * Provide a clear reason for your sabbatical leave.
* **Duration (Page 4)** 
    * Specify the length of your sabbatical leave in months or years.
* **Frequency (Page 4)** 
    * Indicate whether your sabbatical leave will be for a one-time period or recurring.
* **Sabbatical Frequency Details (Page 4)** 
    * If your sabbatical leave is recurring, provide details about the frequency and start and end dates.
* **Leave Status (Page 5)** 
    * Specify whether your sabbatical leave is active or inactive.
* **Sabbatical Status Details (Page 5)** 
    * Provide any additional comments or details about your sabbatical leave status.
* **Manager Approval (Page 6)** 
    * Attach any supporting documents, such as a letter from your manager or departmental leave policy.
* **Manager Info (Page 6)** 
    * Provide your manager's name and department.
* **Employee Information 2 (Page 6)** 
    * This is a duplicate of the information provided in Page 2.
* **Departmental Leave Policy (Page 6)** 
    * Attach any relevant documents or policies related to your department's leave policy.
* **Leave Request Date (Page 7)** 
    * Specify the date you submitted your leave request.
* **Approved Date (Page 7)** 
    * Specify the date your leave was approved.
* **Denied Date (Page 7)** 
    * Specify the date your leave was denied.
* **Reason for Denial (Page 7)** 
    * If your leave was denied, provide a clear reason why.
