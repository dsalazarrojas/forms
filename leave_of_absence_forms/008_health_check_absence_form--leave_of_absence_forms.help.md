# Health Check Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Check Absence Form is a mandatory form to be completed by employees taking a leave of absence due to health reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information and documentation ready before starting this form.
2.  Fill in the form with accurate and complete information.
3.  If you are unsure about any section, please ask your HR representative for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (`Medical`, `text`, required:false): This field is a text area where you can provide any relevant medical information.
* **Symptoms** (`Symptoms`, `text`, required:false): This field is also a text area where you can describe any symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required:false): Provide the necessary contact information for the employee.
* **Absence Start** (`Absence Start`, `date`, required:false): Enter the start date of your absence.
* **Absence End** (`Absence End`, `date`, required:false): Enter the end date of your absence.
* **Absence Duration** (`Absence Duration`, `number`, required:false): Enter the duration of your absence.
* **Absence Reason** (`Absence Reason`, `select_one`, required:false): Select the reason for your absence from the provided options.
* **Leave Type** (`Leave Type`, `select_one`, required:false): Select the type of leave you are taking from the provided options.
* **Leave Duration** (`Leave Duration`, `number`, required:false): Enter the duration of your leave.
* **Return to Work Date** (`Return to Work Date`, `date`, required:false): Enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **Follow-up Visit** (`Follow-up Visit`, `select_one`, required:false): Indicate whether you need a follow-up visit with a medical professional.
* **Medical Certificate** (`Medical Certificate`, `select_one`, required:false): Indicate whether you have a medical certificate from a medical professional.
* **Medical Contact Person** (`Medical Contact Person`, `text`, required:false): Provide the name and contact information for the medical contact person.
* **Medical Contact Person Phone** (`Medical Contact Person Phone`, `text`, required:false): Enter the phone number of the medical contact person.
* **Medical Contact Person Email** (`Medical Contact Person Email`, `email`, required:false): Enter the email of the medical contact person.
* **Medical Certificate Provider** (`Medical Certificate Provider`, `text`, required:false): Provide the name and contact information for the medical certificate provider.
* **Medical Certificate Provider Address** (`Medical Certificate Provider Address`, `text`, required:false): Enter the address of the medical certificate provider.
* **Medical Certificate Provider Phone** (`Medical Certificate Provider Phone`, `text`, required:false): Enter the phone number of the medical certificate provider.
* **Medical Certificate Provider Email** (`Medical Certificate Provider Email`, `email`, required:false): Enter the email of the medical certificate provider.
* **Medical Certificate Provider Facility** (`Medical Certificate Provider Facility`, `text`, required:false): Enter the facility where the medical certificate was provided.
* **Medical Certificate Provider License** (`Medical Certificate Provider License`, `text`, required:false): Enter the license number of the medical certificate provider.
