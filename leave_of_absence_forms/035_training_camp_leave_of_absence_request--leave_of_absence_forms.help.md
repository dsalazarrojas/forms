# Training Camp Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Camp Leave Of Absence Request form is used to submit a leave request for a specific reason. This form should be filled out by employees who need to take a leave of absence and have discussed their request with their manager and coach.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Request Form" page with your leave details.
2. Enter your start and end dates of absence.
3. Choose the reason for your leave.
4. Add any additional comments about your absence.
5. Select your coach, team, and manager approvals.
6. Choose the status of your request.
7. Click "Submit" to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form** (`request_form`, `text`, required): Fill out this page with your leave details. This is where you will enter your leave start and end dates.
* **start_date** (`start_date`, `date`, false): Enter the start date of your leave. Please use the date format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **end_date** (`end_date`, `date`, false): Enter the end date of your leave. Please use the date format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **reason_for_leave** (`reason_for_leave`, `text`, false): Choose the reason for your leave. This will help your manager and coach understand your request.
* **comment** (`comment`, `text`, false): Add any additional comments about your absence. This is optional.
* **coach_approvals** (`coach_approvals`, `select_multiple`, false): Select the coaches that need to approve your request.
* **team_approvals** (`team_approvals`, `select_multiple`, false): Select the team members that need to approve your request.
* **manager_approvals** (`manager_approvals`, `select_multiple`, false): Select the manager(s) that need to approve your request.
* **request_status** (`request_status`, `select_one`, false): Choose the status of your request. This will help track the approval process.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, false): Click this button to submit your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to discuss your request with your manager and coach before filling out this form.
* Be clear and concise in your request and comments.
* Double-check your dates and reason for leave before submitting your request.
