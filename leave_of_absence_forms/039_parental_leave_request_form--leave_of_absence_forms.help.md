# Parental Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Leave Request Form is a form designed for employees to request parental leave, which is a temporary absence from work to care for a family member. The form collects relevant information about the employee's request and serves as a centralized platform for managers to approve or reject the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Request Form** page with the required information, which includes your name, reason for request, and requested dates for leave.
2. On the **Name** page, enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. On the **Date of Birth** page, enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. On the **Email** page, enter your work email address.
5. On the **Return Date** page, enter the date you plan to return to work.
6. If applicable, select your **Job Title** and enter any additional comments or information.
7. On the **Manager Approvals** page, select the names of your managers who need to approve your request.
8. Sign the **Employee Signature** page with your actual signature.
9. Sign the **Manager Signature** page with the signature of your manager who approved your request.
10. Enter the **Date Submitted** of your request.
11. Select the status of your request from **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**.
12. If necessary, enter any additional **Notes** or comments.
13. Enter the **Approval Date** of your request if your manager has approved it.
14. On the **Manager Comments** page, enter any additional comments from your manager.
15. On the **Form Completed By** page, enter the name of the person who completed this form.
16. On the **Form Completed On** page, enter the date when this form was completed.
17. On the **Last Updated** page, enter the date and time when this form was last updated.
18. Select the **Form Status** from **Active**, **Inactive**, or **Archived**.
19. Enter your **Employee ID**, **Employee Name**, **Manager ID**, and **Department** if required.
20. Enter your **Job Title Alt** if applicable.
21. Enter your **Company** name if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form**: This is the main page where you fill out the required information for your parental leave request. 
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address.
* **Reason for Request**: Provide a brief description of why you are requesting parental leave.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title if applicable.
* **Manager Approvals**: Select the names of your managers who need to approve your request.
* **Employee Signature**: Sign this page with your actual signature.
* **Manager Signature**: Sign this page with the signature of your manager who approved your request.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date when you submitted this form.
* **Status**: Select the status of your request from **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information if necessary.
* **Approval Date**: Enter the date when your manager approved your request.
* **Manager Comments**: Enter any additional comments from your manager.
* **Form Completed By**: Enter the name of the person who completed this form.
* **Form Completed On**: Enter the date when this form was completed.
* **Last Updated**: Enter the date and time when this form was last updated.
* **Form Status**: Select the status of your form from **Active**, **Inactive**, or **Archived**.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID if required.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your employee name if required.
* **Manager ID**: Enter your manager's ID if required.
* **Department**: Enter your department name if required.
* **Job Title Alt**: Enter your job title alternate if applicable.
* **Company**: Enter your company name if required.

## Tips

* Make sure to submit your request correctly and thoroughly filled out to avoid any delays or rejections.
* Ensure that your manager has approved your request before marking it as completed.
* Keep an eye on the status of your request and follow up with your manager if it's pending.
* Do not submit duplicate requests.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative for assistance.
