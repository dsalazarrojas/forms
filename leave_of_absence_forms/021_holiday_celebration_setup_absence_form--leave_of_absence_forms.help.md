# Holiday Celebration Setup Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Holiday Celebration Setup Absence Form" is a tool used to inform employees about their holiday plans, ensuring a harmonious work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your employee information in the "Employee Information" page (Page 1).
2. Provide the dates of your holiday celebration in the "Holiday Dates" page (Page 2).
3. Specify the reason for your absence in the "Absence Reason" page (Page 3).
4. Enter the start date of your holiday celebration in the "Holiday Start Date" page (Page 4).
5. Enter the end date of your holiday celebration in the "Holiday End Date" page (Page 5).
6. Indicate the duration of your holiday celebration in the "Holiday Duration" page (Page 6).
7. Select the frequency of your holiday celebration in the "Holiday Frequency" page (Page 7).
8. Provide the name of the contact person in the "Contact Person" page (Page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (Page 1, text, required: false)
  Enter your employee ID or name to identify yourself.
* **Holiday Dates** (Page 2, text, required: false)
  Provide a brief description of your holiday celebration dates.
* **Absence Reason** (Page 3, text, required: false)
  Describe the reason for your absence.
* **Holiday Start Date** (Page 4, date, required: false)
  Enter the start date of your holiday celebration.
* **Holiday End Date** (Page 5, date, required: false)
  Enter the end date of your holiday celebration.
* **Holiday Duration** (Page 6, number, required: false)
  Specify the number of days or duration of your holiday celebration.
* **Holiday Frequency** (Page 7, select_multiple, required: false)
  Select the frequency of your holiday celebration (year, quarter, month, week).
* **Contact Person** (Page 8, text, required: false)
  Enter the name of the person to contact in case of any issues.
