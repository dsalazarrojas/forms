# short_term_disability_leave_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to initiate a short-term disability leave request. The form should only be completed if the employee has a medical condition that prevents them from performing their job duties and requires a temporary leave of absence.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete the employee section, which includes your employee ID, reason for leave, and start and end dates of the leave.
2. If applicable, enter your doctor's information and any relevant medical notes.
3. Enter your manager's contact information, including name, phone number, and email.
4. Select the type of leave you are requesting, which will be reviewed and approved or denied by your manager.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments for your manager's review.
6. Sign and date the form, and ensure that your manager signs and dates it as well.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee**: <i class="fa fa-id"></i> This section requires your employee ID and your name.
* * **Reason for Leave**: Describe the reason you are requesting a short-term disability leave, including any supporting medical documentation.
* * **Start Date**: Enter the date you would like to start your leave, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* * **End Date**: Enter the date you would like to end your leave, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* * **Return Date**: Enter the date you plan to return to work, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* * **Doctor's Note**: If you are requesting a short-term disability leave, you may need to include a note from your doctor.
* * **Doctor's Name**: If you are providing a doctor's note, enter your doctor’s name.
* * **Doctor's Phone**: If you are providing a doctor’s note, enter your doctor’s phone number.
* * **Doctor's Email**: If you are providing a doctor’s note, enter your doctor’s email.
* * **Manager's Name**: Enter your manager’s name.
* * **Manager's Phone**: Enter your manager’s phone number.
* * **Manager's Email**: Enter your manager’s email.
* * **Department**: Enter the department you work in.
* * **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* * **Supervisor's Name**: Enter your supervisor's name.
* * **Supervisor's Phone**: Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* * **Supervisor's Email**: Enter your supervisor's email.
* * **Manager Approval**: Select whether you are requesting approved, pending, or denied by your manager.
* * **Employee Note**: Provide any additional comments or notes for your manager's review.
* * **Manager Note**: Your manager will use this field to provide any comments or notes for your review.
* * **Manager Signature**: Your manager will sign and date this field to approve or deny your request.
* * **Date**: The date this form was completed.
* * **Employee Signature**: Sign and date this field to indicate that you have reviewed and completed this form.
* * **Assigned To**: Enter the person or team that will be assigned to handle your leave request.

## Tips

* Make sure to include all necessary supporting documents and information before submitting this form.
* Ensure that your manager reviews and signs the form to approve or deny your request.
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form to avoid any delays or issues with your leave request.
