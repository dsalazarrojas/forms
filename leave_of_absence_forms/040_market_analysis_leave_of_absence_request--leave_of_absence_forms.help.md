# Market Analysis Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a market analysis leave of absence request. It requires the employee to provide their name, team members affected, start and end dates, reason for leave, and a manager review. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Employee Name** field with your actual name as it appears on your employee record.
2.  For the **Team Members** field, select all team members that will be affected by your leave of absence.
3.  Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** for your market analysis leave of absence.
4.  Provide a brief **Reason** for your leave of absence.
5.  If applicable, select **Yes** for the **Manager Review** field to indicate that your manager's approval is required.
6.  Finally, select **Yes** for the **Approved** field to indicate that your request has been approved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): Enter your actual name as it appears on your employee record.
*   **Team Members** (Team Members, select multiple, optional): Select all team members that will be affected by your leave of absence.
*   **Start Date** (Start Date, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to start your market analysis leave of absence.
*   **End Date** (End Date, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to end your market analysis leave of absence.
*   **Reason** (Reason, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your market analysis leave of absence.
*   **Manager Review** (Manager Review, select one, optional): Select **Yes** if you need your manager's approval for your leave of absence.
*   **Approved** (Approved, select one, optional): Select **Yes** to indicate that your request has been approved.

## Tips
*   Be sure to fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
*   If you need assistance, contact your HR representative or the system administrator.
*   Ensure that you have filled out all necessary fields before submitting the form.
*   If you select **Yes** for the **Manager Review** field, be prepared to discuss your request with your manager.
*   If you select **Yes** for the **Approved** field, ensure that all necessary steps have been taken to approve your request.
