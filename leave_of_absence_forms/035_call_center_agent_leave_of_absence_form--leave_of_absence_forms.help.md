# Call Center Agent Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and plan for leaves of absence for call center agents, to be submitted to their team lead for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the employee information section with your name and ID number.
2. Choose the type of leave and dates you will be taking.
3. Select the reason for your leave and provide any additional details if necessary.
4. If you are providing medical documentation, confirm if it is attached.
5. Provide the name of the colleague who will be covering your responsibilities while you are away.
6. Confirm if you have completed any handover of duties.
7. Confirm your expected return date.
8. Select the date you are submitting the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (2, text, Required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Employee ID** (3, text, Required): Enter your call center ID number.
* **Department** (4, text, Optional): If applicable, enter your department name.
* **Team Lead Name** (5, text, Required): Enter the name of your team lead.
* **Email Address** (6, email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (7, text, Optional): If applicable, enter your phone number.
* **Type of Leave** (9, select_one, Required): Choose the type of leave you are requesting (e.g. Medical leave, Personal leave, Family leave, etc.).
* **Leave Start Date** (10, date, Required): Enter the date you will be starting your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (11, date, Required): Enter the date you will be returning from leave.
* **Total Duration (days)** (12, number, Optional): If applicable, enter the total number of days you will be taking off.
* **Reason for Leave** (14, select_one, Required): Choose the reason for your leave (e.g. Illness or medical condition, Family emergency, etc.).
* **Additional Details** (15, text, Optional): If necessary, provide additional details about your reason for leave.
* **Medical Certificate Attached?** (17, select_one, Required): Confirm if you are attaching medical documentation.
* **Doctor/Hospital Contact** (18, text, Optional): If applicable, provide the contact information for your doctor or hospital.
* **Contact Person During Leave** (20, text, Required): Enter the name of the colleague who will be covering your responsibilities while you are away.
* **Have You Completed Handover of Duties?** (21, select_one, Required): Confirm if you have completed any handover of duties before going on leave.
* **Expected Ready to Return Date** (23, date, Required): Enter the date you expect to return to work.
* **Request Submission Date** (25, date, Required): Enter the date you are submitting this request.
