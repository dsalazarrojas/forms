# Occupational Health Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document an employee's absence from work for extended periods.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please ensure you have the required information and attachments before submitting this form.
- Fill in the form fields with accurate and complete information.
- Review and submit the form once all fields are filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee requesting the absence.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the employee's department.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee's physician.
* **Physician Email** (`physician_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the employee's physician.
* **Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the employee's physician.
* **Absence Name** (`absencename`, text, optional): Enter a unique name for the absence.
* **Absence Type** (`absencetype`, text, optional): Enter the type of absence being requested.
* **Absence Status** (`absencestatus`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the absence (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Absence Start Date** (`absence_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the absence.
* **Absence End Date** (`absence_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the absence.

**First Day Request** (`first_day_request`, date, optional):
*   Enter the date when you wish to start your absence.
*   This will be the first day of your absence.

**Last Day Request** (`last_day_request`, date, optional):
*   Enter the date when you wish to end your absence.
*   This will be the last day of your absence.

**Reason For Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, text, optional): Provide a clear explanation for your absence.
*   This is the reason for your absence.

**Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager.
*   This field is used to identify your manager.

**Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your manager.
*   This field is used to contact your manager for further information.

**Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your manager.
*   This field is used to contact your manager.

**Physician Certification** (`physician_certification`, text, optional): Upload a physician's certification for your absence.
*   Attach a physician's certification or letter to support your absence request.

**Return to Work Certification** (`return_to_work_certification`, text, optional): Upload a return-to-work certification.
*   Attach a return-to-work certification or letter to support your absence request.

**Supervisor Confirmation** (`supervisor_confirmation`, select_multiple, optional):
*   Select options for your supervisor's confirmation.
*   This will be your supervisor's confirmation for your absence.

**Employee Acknowledgement** (`employee_acknowledgement`, note, optional):
*   Please acknowledge that you have read and understood this form and the policies related to absence.

**Manager Acknowledgement** (`manager_acknowledgement`, note, optional):
*   Please acknowledge that you have reviewed and approved this form and the policies related to absence.

**Physician Confirmation** (`physician_confirmation`, select_multiple, optional):
*   Select options for your physician's confirmation.
*   This will be your physician's confirmation for your absence.

Note: The fields with `required: false` are optional and can be left blank if not applicable. The fields with `type: select_one` or `type: select_multiple` are used to select one or multiple options.
