# Foster Care Leave Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to request leave for an employee who is taking care of a foster child. The form is used to provide the necessary information for leave approval and to keep track of the leave details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name, Date Start, and Date End of your leave.
2. Specify the Number Of Days you will be taking leave for.
3. Enter your Reason For Leave, which should include the name of the foster child and your relationship to them (e.g., Parent, Guardian, or Other).
4. Provide the Foster Child Name and the contact information for the Foster Parent (Foster Parent Name and Contact Number).
5. Select the Relationship type from the dropdown menu (e.g., Parent, Guardian, or Other).
6. Enter any additional comments or notes in the Comments field.
7. If your manager or supervisor needs to approve your leave, select their names from the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as an employee.
* **Date Start**: Enter the start date of your leave in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Date End**: Enter the end date of your leave in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Number Of Days**: Enter the number of days you will be taking leave for.
* **Reason For Leave**: Enter the reason for your leave, including the name of the foster child and your relationship to them.
* **Foster Parent Name**: Enter the name of the foster parent.
* **Foster Child Name**: Enter the name of the foster child.
* **Relationship**: Select the type of relationship you have with the foster child (e.g., Parent, Guardian, or Other).
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your leave.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager.
* **Department**: Enter the name of your department.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Location**: Enter the location where you will be taking your leave.
* **Date Of Return**: Enter the date you expect to return to work.
* **Reason For Return**: Enter the reason for your return to work.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your leave.
* **Manager Approval**: Select the approval status of your manager (e.g., Approve, Reject, or Pending).
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Approval**: Select the approval status of your supervisor (e.g., Approve, Reject, or Pending).
* **Employee Status**: Enter your current employee status.
