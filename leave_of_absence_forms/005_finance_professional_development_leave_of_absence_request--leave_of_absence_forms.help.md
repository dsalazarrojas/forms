# Finance Professional Development Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for finance professionals to request a leave of absence for personal or professional development purposes. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select a valid phone number from the provided options in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter the start date of your absence in the "Date of Absence Start" field.
5. Enter the end date of your absence in the "Date of Absence End" field.
6. Explain your reason for taking the leave in the "Reason for Leave" field.
7. Choose your manager's approval status from the provided options in the "Manager Approval" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Text, Required):** Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification card or business card.
* **Email (Email, Required):** Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted regarding your leave request.
* **Phone (Select One, Optional):** Choose a valid phone number from the provided options that you can be reached at during your absence.
* **Date of Absence Start (Date, Optional):** Enter the start date of your leave, including the date you want to begin your absence.
* **Date of Absence End (Date, Optional):** Enter the end date of your leave, including the date you want to return to work.
* **Reason for Leave (Text, Optional):** Provide a brief explanation of why you need to take this leave, including any relevant details.
* **Manager Approval (Select One, Optional):** Choose your manager's approval status, either "Approved" or "Not Approved".

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any supporting documents or evidence, attach them to your request.
* Submit your form only after carefully reviewing your information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR department for assistance.
