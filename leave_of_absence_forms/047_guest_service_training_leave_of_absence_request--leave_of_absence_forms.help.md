# Guest Service Training Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Guest Service Training Leave Of Absence Request" form is used by employees to request leave for guest services training. This form ensures that the employee's leave request is processed efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the correct employee from the drop-down menu.
2.  Choose the training date for the leave request.
3.  Select the type of training the employee is undertaking (e.g., Service Skills, Product Knowledge, Food and Beverage).
4.  Choose whether the supervisor's approval is required and select 'Approve' or 'Reject' accordingly.
5.  If required, select the dates for the leave start and end.
6.  Provide a reason for leaving (if applicable).
7.  Add any additional comments (if applicable).
8.  Indicate whether the employee belongs to a department and team that requires approval.
9.  Select the leave status (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected).
10.  Choose the training location (if applicable).
11.  Enter the number of leave days and hours (if applicable).
12.  Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee**: Select the correct employee from the drop-down menu.
*   **Training Date**: Enter the date of the training event.
*   **Training Type**: Choose the type of training the employee is undertaking (e.g., Service Skills, Product Knowledge, Food and Beverage).
*   **Supervisor Approval**: If required, select 'Approve' or 'Reject' based on the supervisor's approval.
*   **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the leave.
*   **End Date**: Enter the end date of the leave.
*   **Reason for Leaving**: Enter the reason for the leave (if applicable).
*   **Comments**: Add any additional comments.
*   **Department**: Select whether the employee belongs to a department that requires approval.
*   **Team**: Select whether the employee belongs to a team that requires approval.
*   **Approval**: If required, select 'Approve' or 'Reject' based on the approval process.
*   **Submit**: Click this button to complete the form.
*   **Cancel**: Not required but can be used to cancel the form.
*   **Leave Status**: Select the current leave status (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected).
*   **Status Date**: Enter the date of the status update.
*   **Leave Days**: Enter the total number of leave days.
*   **Leave Hours**: Enter the total number of leave hours.
*   **Training Location**: Select the location of the training event.
