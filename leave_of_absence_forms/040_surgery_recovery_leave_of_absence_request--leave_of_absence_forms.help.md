# Surgery Recovery Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to request a leave of absence for an employee due to surgery. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary information to facilitate the approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name in the "Employee Name" field, as this is your official name used in company records.
2. Provide your employee ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
3. Select your start and end dates for the leave in the respective "Start Date" and "End Date" fields. Please enter the exact dates of your surgery and expected recovery period.
4. Enter a brief explanation of the reason for your leave in the "Reason for Leave" field. This will help your manager understand the circumstances surrounding your surgery.
5. If you have any specific surgeon's name, please enter it in the "Surgeon Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Surgery Recovery Form**: This is the title of the form, indicating its purpose.
* **Employee Name** (text, required): Your official name as used in company records.
* **Employee ID** (text, required): Your employee ID number.
* **Start Date** (date, required): The date of your surgery.
* **End Date** (date, required): The date you expect to return to work.
* **Reason for Leave** (text, optional): A brief explanation of the reason for your leave.
* **Surgeon Name** (text, optional): The name of your surgeon, if applicable.
* **Surgical Facility** (text, optional): The name of the surgical facility where your surgery took place, if applicable.
