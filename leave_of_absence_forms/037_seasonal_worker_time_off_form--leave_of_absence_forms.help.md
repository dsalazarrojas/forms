# seasonal_worker_time_off_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for seasonal workers to request time off, such as vacations, medical leaves, or other types of leaves. It is crucial to fill out this form accurately to ensure timely processing of your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., vacation, medical, or other).
2. Enter your employee name.
3. Enter your employee ID.
4. Select the start and end dates of your leave.
5. Provide a reason for your leave.
6. Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., vacation, medical, or other).
7. Choose whether your manager approves or declines your request, or requests more information.
8. (Optional) Add a note about the business impact of your leave.
9. Sign the form to confirm your request.
10. Sign as a manager to approve or decline the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee request** (`employee_request`):
	+ Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., vacation, medical, or other).
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`):
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your employee documentation.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`):
	+ Enter your employee ID as it appears on your employee documentation.
* **Start date** (`start_date`):
	+ Enter the start date of your leave (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **End date** (`end_date`):
	+ Enter the end date of your leave (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`):
	+ Provide a clear explanation for your leave request.
* **Leave type** (`leave_type`):
	+ Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., vacation, medical, or other).
* **Manager approval** (`manager_approval`):
	+ Choose whether your manager approves or declines your request, or requests more information.
* **Business impact** (`business_impact`):
	+ (Optional) Add a note about the business impact of your leave.
* **Employee signature** (`employee_signature`):
	+ Sign the form to confirm your request.
* **Manager signature** (`manager_signature`):
	+ Sign as a manager to approve or decline the request.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are accurately and completely filled out to avoid delays in processing your request.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that you have selected the correct type of leave and provided a clear reason for your request.
* If your manager requests more information, please provide it promptly to avoid delays in processing your request.
