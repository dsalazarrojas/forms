# Marina Resort Employee Leave Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marina Resort Employee Leave Application Form is designed to be used by employees to request a leave of absence from work. This form will help the management process your leave request and ensure a smooth and efficient return to work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information**: Enter your employee ID or your full name and email address. This will help the management system identify you correctly.
* **Reason for Leave**: Provide a clear and detailed reason for your leave. This will help the management team understand your situation and process your request effectively.
* **Leave Duration**: Enter the number of days you will be away from work. This will help us schedule any necessary tasks or arrangements for your absence.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you will start your leave. This should be a specific date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you will return to work. This should be a specific date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date you will return to work. This should be a specific date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Days Off**: Enter the number of days you will be absent from work.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact phone number or email address where we can reach you with any questions or updates.
* **Comments (Optional)**: If you have any additional information or comments regarding your leave, you can enter them here.
* **Submit**: Choose 'Submit' to submit your leave application form.
* **Cancel**: Choose 'Cancel' to cancel your leave application form.

Remember to review your submission carefully before selecting 'Submit'. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR department.
