# Trade Skill Enhancement Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and manage employee leave of absence for trade skill enhancement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields with accurate information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee** (`employee`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the start date of your leave. If unsure, select the current date.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the end date of your leave. If unsure, select the current date plus one week.
* **Skill Level** (`skill_level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your current trade skill level (High, Low).
* **Manager** (`manager`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your manager's name and contact information, if applicable.
* Note: If the form asks for any additional information not provided in this guide, please contact your supervisor for guidance.

## Tips
- Double-check all entered information for accuracy.
- Ensure that you have all necessary approvals before submitting.
