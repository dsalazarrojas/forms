# Leave Of Absence Request For Estate Affairs - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for estate affairs, such as family issues or property disputes. It helps collect and document all necessary information for efficient processing and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information:
	* **Leave Of Absence Request For Estate Affairs** (Title): Enter the title of the leave request.
	* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name.
	* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as the employee requesting the leave.
	* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
	* **Start Date**: Enter the date you want to start your leave.
	* **Start Time**: Enter the time you want to start your leave.
	* **End Date**: Enter the date you want to end your leave.
	* **End Time**: Enter the time you want to end your leave.
	* **Purpose**: Enter a brief description of the estate affair reason for your leave.
	* **Department**: Enter the department you belong to.
	* **Leave Type**: Select the type of leave (Estate Affairs, Personal, or Other).
	* **Leave Duration**: Enter the duration of your leave.
2. Select the status of your leave request:
	* **Status**: Choose the status of your leave request (New, In Progress, Approved, or Rejected).
3. Add any comments or attachments (optional):
	* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about your leave request.
	* **Attachments**: Upload any relevant attachments (e.g., supporting documents).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Leave Of Absence Request For Estate Affairs**: Enter a brief title of your leave request.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager who will be handling your leave request.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as the employee requesting the leave.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number for identification purposes.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you want to start your leave in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Start Time**: Enter the time you want to start your leave in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **End Date**: Enter the date you want to end your leave in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **End Time**: Enter the time you want to end your leave in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Purpose**: Enter a brief description of the estate affair reason for your leave.
* **Department**: Enter the department you belong to for reference purposes.
* **Leave Type**: Select the type of leave (Estate Affairs, Personal, or Other).
* **Leave Duration**: Enter the duration of your leave in working hours or days.
* **Status**: Choose the status of your leave request (New, In Progress, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about your leave request.
* **Attachments**: Upload any relevant attachments (e.g., supporting documents).
* **Approval Date**: Not required, but displays the date the form was approved.
* **Approval Time**: Not required, but displays the time the form was approved.
* **Approved By**: Not required, but displays the name of the person who approved the form.
* **Approval Note**: Not required, but displays any notes made during the approval process.
* **Form Submitted By**: Displays the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted Date**: Displays the date the form was submitted.
* **Form Last Updated**: Displays the date the form was last updated.
* **Form Last Updated Time**: Displays the time the form was last updated.
* **Form Last Updated By**: Displays the name of the person who last updated the form.
