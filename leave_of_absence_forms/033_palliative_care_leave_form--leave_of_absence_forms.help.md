# Palliative Care Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the completion of the Palliative Care Leave Form. This form is used to request a leave of absence for palliative care and must be completed accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the reason for the leave in the "Reason for Leave" field.
3. Enter the start date of the leave in the "Start Date" field.
4. Enter the end date of the leave in the "End Date" field.
5. If applicable, enter the palliative care provider's name in the "Palliative Care Provider" field.
6. If applicable, upload a medical certificate in the "Medical Certificate" field.
7. Enter the return to work date in the "Return to Work Date" field.
8. Enter the number of days off in the "Days Off" field.
9. Select the status of the leave (Pending, Approved, or Denied) in the "Status" field.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
11. If approved, enter the name of the person who approved the leave in the "Approved By" field.
12. If approved, enter the date and time of approval in the "Approved Date" and "Approved Time" fields.
13. Enter the leave start date in the "Leave Start Date" field.
14. Enter the leave end date in the "Leave End Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Palliative Care Leave Form** (`palliative_care_leave_form`, text, optional): This is the title of the form.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee requesting the leave.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the palliative care leave, such as a terminal illness or end-of-life care.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the leave is expected to start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the leave is expected to end.
* **Palliative Care Provider** (`palliative_care_provider`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the palliative care provider.
* **Medical Certificate** (`medical_certificate`, text, optional): If applicable, upload a medical certificate or documentation to support the leave request.
* **Return to Work Date** (`return_to_work_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the employee is expected to return to work.
* **Days Off** (`days_off`, number, optional): Enter the number of days off requested.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the leave (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the leave request.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, optional): If approved, enter the name of the person who approved the leave.
* **Approved Date** (`approved_date`, date, optional): If approved, enter the date of approval.
* **Approved Time** (`approved_time`, time, optional): If approved, enter the time of approval.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the leave is expected to start.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the leave is expected to end.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Employee Extension** (`employee_extention`, text, optional): Enter the employee's extension number.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the employee's department or job title.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the employee's location or work site.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee's manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter the manager's phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth processing of the leave request.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with your HR representative or manager.
* Ensure that you have all necessary supporting documentation before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your HR representative or manager.
