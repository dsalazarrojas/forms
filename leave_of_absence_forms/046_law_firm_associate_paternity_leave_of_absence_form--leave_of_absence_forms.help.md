# Law Firm Associate Paternity Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a template for law firm associates to request a leave of absence for personal reasons. It is used to gather necessary information from the associate, including contact information, employee ID, start and end dates of the leave, type of leave, and manager and employee approval status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by filling out the "Contact Information" section with your name and contact details.
2. Enter your "Employee ID" number in the designated field.
3. Select the start and end dates of your leave in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields respectively.
4. Choose the type of leave you are requesting in the "Leave Type" dropdown menu. Please select one of the following options: Paternity Leave, Maternity Leave, or Other Leave.
5. If your leave is approved, your manager will fill out the "Manager Approval" field with their approval number.
6. You will be notified when your manager has approved or declined your leave request.
7. If your leave is approved, you will be required to fill out the "Employee Approval" field with your approval number.
8. Select the "Return to Work Date" field with the date you plan to return to work after your leave.
9. Finally, select the reason for your leave in the "Reason for Leave" dropdown menu. Please choose one of the following options: Personal reasons, Medical reasons, or Other.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, not required): Enter your name and contact details, including your email address and phone number.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, not required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, not required): Select the date you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `text`, not required): Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the type of leave you are requesting. Please select one of the following options: Paternity Leave, Maternity Leave, or Other Leave.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, `number`, not required): Your manager will fill out this field with their approval number after reviewing your leave request.
* **Employee Approval** (`employee_approval`, `number`, not required): Fill out this field with your approval number after receiving approval from your manager.
* **Leave Request Date** (`leave_request_date`, `date`, not required): The system will automatically populate this field with the date your leave request was made.
* **Return to Work Date** (`return_to_work_date`, `date`, not required): Select the date you plan to return to work after your leave.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose the reason for your leave from the following options: Personal reasons, Medical reasons, or Other.
* **Leave Status** (`leave_status`, `select_one`, not required): This field is automatically updated by the system and indicates the status of your leave request. Please select one of the following options: Pending, Approved, Declined, or Denied.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out this form accurately and honestly, and that you follow the required procedures and approval processes for leave requests. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your manager or HR representative for assistance.
