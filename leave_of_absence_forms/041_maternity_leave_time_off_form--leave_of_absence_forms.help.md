# Maternity Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request maternity leave from work. It is a crucial document that outlines the necessary information required for the leave request process. Please ensure that you have reviewed the company's leave policies and procedures before submitting this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the start date and end date of your leave.
2. Provide the reason for your leave.
3. Specify the expected start and end dates of your leave.
4. Indicate whether you will be paid during your maternity leave.
5. Provide any additional comments or requests.
6. Select your manager's name, email, and phone number.
7. Select the approval manager's name, email, and phone number.
8. Enter the date of approval.
9. Indicate whether you have any other leave type.
10. Fill in the leave start and end dates.
11. Indicate whether you will be paid during your leave.
12. Provide any additional comments or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date (1)**: The date you plan to start your maternity leave. Please enter the date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **End Date (2)**: The date you plan to end your maternity leave. Please enter the date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Reason (3)**: The reason for your maternity leave. Please provide a brief explanation.
* **Expected Start Date (4)**: The expected start date of your leave.
* **Expected End Date (5)**: The expected end date of your leave.
* **Pay During Maternity (6)**: Indicate whether you will be paid during your leave. If you select "Yes", you will be paid as per company policy.
* **Pay During Maternity Options (7)**: Select any additional options related to pay during maternity.
* **Manager Comments (8)**: Any comments or requests from your manager.
* **Employee Comments (9)**: Any comments or requests from you.
* **Approval Status (10)**: The status of your leave request.
* **Approval Comments (11)**: Comments from the approval manager.
* **Employee Status (12)**: Your current status.
* **Manager Name (13)**: The name of your manager.
* **Manager Email (14)**: The email address of your manager.
* **Manager Phone (15)**: The phone number of your manager.
* **Approval Manager Name (16)**: The name of the approval manager.
* **Approval Manager Email (17)**: The email address of the approval manager.
* **Approval Manager Phone (18)**: The phone number of the approval manager.
* **Approval Date (19)**: The date of approval.
* **Leave Type (20)**: Indicate whether you have any other leave type.
* **Leave Start Date (21)**: The date you plan to start your leave.
* **Leave End Date (22)**: The date you plan to end your leave.
* **Pay During Leave (23)**: Indicate whether you will be paid during your leave.
* **Pay During Leave Options (24)**: Select any additional options related to pay during leave.
* **Manager Comments 2 (25)**: Any additional comments or requests from your manager.
