# Intermittent Leave Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit an intermittent leave of absence request. Fill out this form to notify management of your intention to take an intermittent leave and track the status of your leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your employee ID and job title.
2. Provide a reason for your leave.
3. Select the start and end dates of your leave.
4. Choose the type of leave (multiple selections allowed).
5. Enter the shift number for your leave.
6. (Optional) Provide manager comments if necessary.
7. Select the status of your leave.
8. (Optional) Confirm with employee and manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`Employee ID`, text, required): Enter your unique employee ID number.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Reason for Leave** (`Reason for Leave`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise reason for your leave.
* **Leave Start Date** (`Leave Start Date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your leave in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Leave End Date** (`Leave End Date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your leave in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Leave Type** (`Leave Type`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of the available leave types.
* **Shift** (`Shift`, number, required): Enter the shift number for your leave.
* **Manager Comments** (`Manager Comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information for your manager.
* **Status** (`Status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of your leave.
* **Employee Confirmation** (`Employee Confirmation`, text, optional): Confirm with your manager that you are requesting this leave.
* **Manager Confirmation** (`Manager Confirmation`, text, optional): Confirm with your employee that you have received this leave request.
* **Manager Email** (`Manager Email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address for notification.
