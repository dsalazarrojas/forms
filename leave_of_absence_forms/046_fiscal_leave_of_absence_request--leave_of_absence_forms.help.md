# Fiscal Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a leave of absence from work due to illness, vacation, family reasons, or other personal circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form

* Enter your employee name and ID to identify you as the requestor.
* Choose the type of leave you are requesting (e.g. sick leave, vacation, family leave, etc.).
* Select the start and end dates of your leave.
* Specify the reason for your absence.
* Select the duration of your leave.
* Choose the start and end times of your leave.
* Enter any additional comments or reasons for your absence.
* Select the return date.
* Optionally, enter any comments from your manager.
* Choose the status of your leave request (approved, not approved, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as an employee.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID as assigned by the company.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required): Choose from the options "Approve", "Reject", or "Request Additional Info" to indicate the status of your request.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the reason for your leave.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you will begin your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you will end your leave.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, required): Choose from the options "Sick Leave", "Vacation", "Family Leave", "Maternity Leave", "Paternity Leave", or "Other".
* **Leave Duration** (`leave_duration`, number, required): Enter the number of days or weeks you will be on leave.
* **Leave Start Time** (`leave_start_time`, time, required): Enter the time of day you will begin your leave.
* **Leave End Time** (`leave_end_time`, time, required): Enter the time of day you will end your leave.
* **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the reason for your absence.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required): Enter the date you will return to work.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): If your manager has any comments for you, enter them here.
* **Manager Approval Date** (`manager_approval_date`, date, optional): If your manager has approved your request, enter the date of approval.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): Choose from the options "Approved" or "Not Approved" to indicate the status of your request.
* **Approval Status Note** (`approval_status_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments regarding the status of your request.
* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the requestor.
* **Requestor Title** (`requestor_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Job Position** (`job_position`, text, required): Enter your job position.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text, required): Enter the title of your manager.
* **Team** (`team`, text, required): Enter the team you belong to.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
