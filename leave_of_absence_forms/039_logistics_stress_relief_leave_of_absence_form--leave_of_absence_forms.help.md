# Logistics Stress Relief Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and manage the process of taking a logistics stress relief leave of absence, which is a period of temporary leave from work to address logistics-related stress and mental health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and the date you are completing the form.
2. Enter a brief title for your leave of absence.
3. Provide a description of the reason for your leave, including any relevant details or context.
4. Specify whether any team members will be impacted by your leave.
5. Select the start and end dates for your leave.
6. Estimate the duration of your leave.
7. Choose the status of your leave (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
8. Add any additional comments or notes as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your leave of absence, such as "Logistics Stress Relief Leave" or "Mental Health Leave".
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the reason for your leave, including any relevant details or context.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager or supervisor.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, optional): Describe the reason for your leave, including any relevant details or context.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select multiple, optional): Check all applicable box(es) if any team members will be impacted by your leave.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Duration of Leave** (`duration_of_leave`, text, optional): Estimate the duration of your leave, including any relevant details or context.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select one, optional): Choose the status of your leave (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes as needed.
