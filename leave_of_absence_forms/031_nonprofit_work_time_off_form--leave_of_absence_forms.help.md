# Nonprofit Work Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help nonprofit employees request time off for various reasons, such as vacation, sick leave, or personal time off.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of time off you are requesting (Vacation, Sick Leave, or Personal Time Off).
2.  Choose the start and end dates of your time off.
3.  Enter the duration of your time off in days.
4.  Enter a brief description of your time off request.
5.  You can also add a comment if you want to provide additional information about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Time Off Type**
**(`time_off_type`, `select_one`, required)
This field allows you to choose the type of time off you are requesting. Please select "Vacation" for a planned vacation, "Sick Leave" for an unexpected illness or injury, or "Personal Time Off" for a personal reason not related to work.

### 2. **Vacation Requests**
(`vacation_requests`, `select_multiple`, optional)
This field is not applicable as it's a separate section on the form.

### 3. **Time Off Request Info**
(`time_off_request_info`, `text`, optional)
This field is used to enter a brief description of your time off request. Please provide a short paragraph explaining why you need this time off.

### 4. **Start Date**
(`start_date`, `date`, required)
This field is used to select the start date of your time off. Please enter the date you plan to start your time off.

### 5. **End Date**
(`end_date`, `date`, required)
This field is used to select the end date of your time off. Please enter the date you plan to end your time off.

### 6. **Duration**
(`duration`, `number`, required)
This field is used to enter the number of days you plan to take off. Please enter the total number of days you need for your time off.

### 7. **Time Off Type**
(`time_off_type`, `select_one`, required)
This field is used to confirm your time off type selection from earlier. Please select the same type of time off you chose in step 1.

### 8. **Reason**
(`reason`, `text`, optional)
This field is used to enter a brief description of your reason for taking time off. Please provide a short paragraph explaining why you need this time off.

### 9. **Approval Status**
(`approval_status`, `select_one`, optional)
This field is used to select the current status of your time off request. Please select "Pending" for an active request, "Approved" if your request is approved, or "Denied" if your request is denied.

### 10. **Comment**
(`comment`, `text`, optional)
This field is used to enter a comment about your time off request. Please provide any additional information that may be helpful to your supervisor or HR representative.
