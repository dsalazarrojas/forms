# training_leave_time_off_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for training leave time off. Please complete it if you need to take a leave from work for training or professional development purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the employee's name and identification information in the **employee** field.
2. Enter the date of the training in the **training_date** field. This should be the date when the training is scheduled to start.
3. Enter the date when the training is scheduled to end in the **end_date** field.
4. Enter the number of hours you will be taking off for training in the **training_hours** field.
5. Enter the location of the training in the **training_location** field.
6. Enter a brief description of the training topic or activity in the **training_topic** field.
7. Enter the department where you work (e.g., HR, Finance, Sales, etc.) in the **department** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee**: This is the employee's name and any other identification information required to process the leave request. Please enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* **training_date**: Enter the date when the training is scheduled to take place.
* **end_date**: Enter the date when the training is scheduled to end.
* **training_hours**: Enter the number of hours you will be taking off for training. Please be accurate in your count.
* **training_location**: Enter the location where the training will be taking place.
* **training_topic**: Enter a brief description of the training topic or activity.
* **department**: Enter your department where you work, e.g., HR, Finance, Sales, etc. This helps us process the leave request correctly.

## Tips
* Ensure you have permission to take training leave before submitting this form.
* Be accurate and clear in your submissions to avoid any issues with processing your leave request.
