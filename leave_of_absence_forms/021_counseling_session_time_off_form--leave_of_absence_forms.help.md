# Counseling Session Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a counseling session with an employee. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure the session is properly scheduled and processed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and realistic information.
2. Ensure that the session date and time are correct and feasible.
3. Select the appropriate session type (individual, group, or family).
4. Enter a realistic duration for the session.
5. Double-check the start and end times for accuracy.
6. If necessary, provide a valid reason for the time off.
7. Review and submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the session, if necessary.
* **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the employee scheduling the session.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the session.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of session (individual, group, or family).
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the session.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the session.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the session.
* **Time Off Start Date** (`time_off_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the time off, if applicable.
* **Time Off End Date** (`time_off_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the time off, if applicable.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Enter a valid reason for the time off, if necessary.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the session.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, required): Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
* **Modified By** (`modified_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who modified the form, if applicable.
* **Modified On** (`modified_on`, date, optional): Enter the date and time the form was modified, if applicable.
* **Form Data Id** (`form_data_id`, number, required): Enter a unique ID for the form data, automatically generated.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, number, required): Enter the version of the form, automatically generated.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Created On** (`created_on`, date, required): Enter the date and time the form was created.
* **Modified By User** (`modified_by_user`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who modified the form, if applicable.
* **Modified On User** (`modified_on_user`, date, optional): Enter the date and time the form was modified, if applicable.
* **Submitted By User** (`submitted_by_user`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form, if applicable.
* **Submitted On User** (`submitted_on_user`, date, optional): Enter the date and time the form was submitted, if applicable.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, date, optional): Enter the date and time the form was deleted, if applicable.
