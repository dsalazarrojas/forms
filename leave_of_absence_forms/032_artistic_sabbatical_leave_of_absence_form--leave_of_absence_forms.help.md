# Artistic Sabbatical Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Artistic Sabbatical Leave Of Absance Form is used to request a sabbatical leave for an employee, allowing them to take a break from work to focus on artistic pursuits. This form helps to document essential details about the leave, including the start and end dates, sabbatical duration, type, and coverage plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your employee ID or staff number.
3. Select your department or division.
4. Enter your position or title.
5. Enter your direct supervisor's name.
6. Enter the requested start date of the sabbatical.
7. Enter the requested end date of the sabbatical.
8. Select the duration of the sabbatical in months.
9. Choose the type of sabbatical you are requesting: Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid.
10. Describe the artistic project or purpose you wish to pursue during your sabbatical.
11. Outline the scope and goals of the project.
12. If applicable, select the location where you will be during the sabbatical.
13. If applicable, enter the year of your previous sabbatical.
14. Provide information on how your work will be covered during your absence.
15. Indicate your commitment to return to work after the sabbatical.
16. Select the method of contact during your sabbatical (email, phone, emergency contact only).
17. If applicable, enter the budget or financial support you require.
18. If applicable, describe any additional support or resources you need.
19. Your supervisor will review and approve or deny your request based on the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`employee_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Last Name** (`employee_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Employee ID or Staff Number** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID or staff number as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Department or Division** (`department`, text, required): Select your department or division as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Position or Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Enter your position or title as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Direct Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Requested Sabbatical Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you would like to begin your sabbatical.
* **Requested Sabbatical End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you would like to end your sabbatical.
* **Sabbatical Duration - Months** (`sabbatical_duration_months`, number, required): Enter the duration of your sabbatical in months.
* **Type of Sabbatical** (`sabbatical_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of sabbatical you are requesting: Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid.
* **Creative Project or Purpose** (`creative_project`, text, required): Describe the artistic project or purpose you wish to pursue during your sabbatical.
* **Project Scope and Goals** (`project_scope`, text, required): Outline the scope and goals of the project.
* **Sabbatical Location or Setting** (`location`, text, optional): If applicable, select the location where you will be during the sabbatical.
* **Year of Last Sabbatical (if any)** (`year_of_previous_sabbatical`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the year of your previous sabbatical.
* **Coverage or Succession Plan During Absence** (`coverage_plan`, text, required): Provide information on how your work will be covered during your absence.
* **Commitment to Return After Sabbatical** (`return_commitment`, select_one, required): Indicate your commitment to return to work after the sabbatical. Select 'Yes', 'Possibly', or 'Uncertain'.
* **Contact Method During Sabbatical** (`contact_during_sabbatical`, text, optional): Select the method of contact during your sabbatical (email, phone, emergency contact only).
* **Budget or Financial Support Requested** (`budget_needed`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the budget or financial support you require.
* **Additional Support or Resources Needed** (`additional_support`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any additional support or resources you need.
* **Supervisor Preliminary Approval** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): Your supervisor will review and approve or deny your request based on the information provided. Select 'Approved', 'Under Review', or 'Needs Discussion'.
