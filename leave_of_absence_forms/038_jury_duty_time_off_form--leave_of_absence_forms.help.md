# Jury Duty Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employees who need to request time off for jury duty. It is used to provide information about their jury duty obligations so that their time off can be processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee ID number in the `employee_id` field.
2. Enter the start date of your jury duty in the `start_date` field.
3. Enter the end date of your jury duty in the `end_date` field.
4. If you have been summoned for jury duty, enter your juror's ID, name, address, city, state, and zip code in the `juror_s_id`, `juror_s_name`, `juror_s_address`, `juror_s_city`, `juror_s_state`, and `juror_s_zip` fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Your employee ID number, used to identify you in our system.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): The date when you will start your jury duty.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): The date when you will end your jury duty.
* **Juror's ID** (`juror_s_id`, text, optional): The ID number of the juror who summoned you, if applicable.
* **Juror's Name** (`juror_s_name`, text, optional): The name of the juror who summoned you, if applicable.
* **Juror's Address** (`juror_s_address`, text, optional): The address of the juror who summoned you, if applicable.
* **Juror's City** (`juror_s_city`, text, optional): The city of the juror who summoned you, if applicable.
* **Juror's State** (`juror_s_state`, text, optional): The state of the juror who summoned you, if applicable.
* **Juror's Zip** (`juror_s_zip`, text, optional): The zip code of the juror who summoned you, if applicable.
