# Personal Development Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for personal development purposes. It's essential to complete this form if you're planning to take time off to focus on your personal growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as it appears on your employee ID.
2. Choose the start and end dates of your requested leave.
3. Indicate the duration of your leave.
4. Select the purpose of your leave (Family Care, Personal Development, or Other).
5. Enter your manager's name.
6. Enter the name of your team lead (if applicable).
7. Specify the department you belong to.
8. Mention your team name.
9. Select the type of leave you're requesting (Medical, Personal Development, or Other).
10. Choose the status of your leave request (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
11. Add any additional comments about your leave request.
12. Enter your employee ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Name (1) (`employee_name`, text, required: false)
- This is your name as it appears on your employee ID. Please enter it accurately.

### Start Date (2) (`start_date`, date, required: false)
- Enter the date you plan to start your leave.

### End Date (3) (`end_date`, date, required: false)
- Enter the date you plan to end your leave.

### Duration (4) (`duration`, number, required: false)
- Specify the number of days or weeks you need for your leave.

### Purpose Of Leave (5) (`purpose_of_leave`, select_one, required: false)
- Select the reason for your leave from the provided options:
	+ Family Care
	+ Personal Development
	+ Other

### Manager Name (6) (`manager_name`, text, required: false)
- Enter the name of your manager.

### Team Lead Name (7) (`team_lead_name`, text, required: false)
- If applicable, enter the name of your team lead.

### Department (8) (`department`, text, required: false)
- Specify the department you belong to.

### Team (9) (`team`, text, required: false)
- Mention your team name.

### Leave Type (10) (`leave_type`, select_one, required: false)
- Select the type of leave you're requesting:
	+ Medical
	+ Personal Development
	+ Other

### Leave Status (11) (`leave_status`, select_one, required: false)
- Choose the status of your leave request:
	+ Approved
	+ Denied
	+ Pending

### Comments (12) (`comments`, note, required: false)
- Add any additional comments about your leave request.

### Employee ID (13) (`employee_id`, text, required: false)
- Enter your employee ID.

Remember, the form is designed to be as clear and concise as possible. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your manager or HR representative for assistance.
