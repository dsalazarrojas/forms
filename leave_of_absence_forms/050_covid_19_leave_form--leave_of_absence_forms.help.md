# COVID 19 Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Leave Form is a document used by employees to request leave related to the COVID-19 pandemic. It helps ensure that employees provide accurate and complete information about their leave request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee information in the "Employee Information" section, including your name, ID, department, and position.
2. In the "Leave Request Details" section, select the type of COVID-19 related leave you are requesting and provide the dates.
3. In the "Supporting Documentation" section, select which documents you can provide to support your leave request.
4. Confirm that you understand the COVID-19 leave policy and that the information provided is accurate and complete in the "Acknowledgment" section.
5. Date the "Form Submission Date" in the final section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department**: Select your department or work unit.
* **Position or Job Title**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Date of Request**: Enter the date you are requesting leave from.
* **Type of COVID-19 Related Leave**: Select the reason for your leave request from the options provided.
* **Leave Start Date**: Enter the date you would like to start your leave.
* **Leave End Date**: Enter the date you would like to end your leave.
* **Total Days Requested**: Enter the number of days you are requesting for leave.
* **Detailed Reason for Leave**: Provide a clear and concise reason for your leave request.
* **COVID-19 Status**: Select your current COVID-19 status from the options provided.
* **Supporting Documentation**: Select all the documents you can provide to support your leave request.
* **Requesting Paid Leave**: Select 'Yes' if you are requesting paid leave, 'No' otherwise.
* **Type of Compensation**: Select the type of compensation you are requesting.
* **Form Submission Date**: Date and sign the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* **Employee Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you understand the COVID-19 leave policy and that the information provided is accurate and complete.
