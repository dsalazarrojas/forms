# Accrued Leave Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to report an employee's accrued leave absence, which is a period of time off from work that an employee has earned and is entitled to take. The form is used to request approval for the leave and to keep track of the employee's leave balance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name and ID in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your email address to ensure that you receive updates on your leave request status.
3. Select the type of leave you are requesting (Vacation, Sick Leave, Personal Leave, Bereavement, or Other).
4. Enter the number of hours or days you currently have in your accrued leave balance.
5. Provide the start and end dates of your leave period.
6. Calculate the total number of days you are requesting based on the dates provided.
7. Briefly explain the reason for your absence (optional).
8. Provide emergency contact information in case we are unable to reach you.
9. Select the name of your supervisor who must approve this request.
10. (Optional) Enter your supervisor's approval decision (for HR/Manager use only).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your official company documents.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, Text, Required): Enter your company ID as assigned to you.
* **Employee Email** (Employee Email, Email, Required): Enter your official company email address.
* **Leave Type** (Select, Required): Select the category of leave you are requesting. Examples include:
	+ Vacation
	+ Sick Leave
	+ Personal Leave
	+ Bereavement
	+ Other
* **Accrued Balance Available** (Number, Required): Enter the number of hours or days you currently have available in your leave balance.
* **Leave Start Date** (Date, Required): Enter the start date of your leave period.
* **Leave End Date** (Date, Required): Enter the end date of your leave period.
* **Total Days Requested** (Number, Required): This field will be calculated based on the dates provided.
* **Reason for Absence** (Text, Optional): Briefly explain the reason for your absence.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (Text, Required): Enter the name and phone number of a person we can contact in case we are unable to reach you.
* **Supervisor Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your supervisor who must approve this request.
* **Approval Decision** (Select, Optional): (For HR/Manager use only) Select the approval status of your request. Examples:
	+ Pending
	+ Approved
	+ Denied

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the dates and numbers to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your HR representative for guidance.
