# Hospitality Overseas Family Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for an employee who is part of a hospitality overseas team. It captures necessary details to facilitate the request and approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information. You may need to provide manager approval, which will be indicated by a "Yes" or "No" selection in the "Manager Approval" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Leave Reason** (`employee_leave_reason`): Briefly describe the reason for your leave, so your team and manager can understand your absence.
- **Return Date** (`return_date`): Enter the date you plan to return to work after your leave.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Provide a contact number where you can be reached during your absence.
- **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so your team can reach you.
- **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Enter your manager's name for easy reference.
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID number for accurate tracking.
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel file.
- **Manager Department** (`manager_department`): Enter your manager's department for easy reference.
- **Employee Department** (`employee_department`): Enter your department for accurate tracking.
- **Manager Title** (`manager_title`): Enter your manager's title for easy reference.
- **Employee Title** (`employee_title`): Enter your title as it appears on your personnel file.
- **Team Member** (`team_member`): Select 'Yes' if you are a team member, and 'No' otherwise.
- **Leave Duration** (`leave_duration`): Enter the length of your leave, in days or hours.
- **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`): Enter the start date of your leave.
- **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`): Enter the end date of your leave.
- **Leave Type** (`leave_type`): Select 'Yes' if your leave is for a family-related reason, and 'No' otherwise.
- **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your manager's approval status.
- **Employee Approval** (`employee_approval`): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your approval status.
