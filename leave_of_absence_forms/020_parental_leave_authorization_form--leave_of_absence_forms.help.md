# Parental Leave Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Parental Leave Authorization Form" is used to request authorization from an employee's manager and department head for a parental leave of absence. This form is only intended for leaves due to parental responsibilities and should not be used for other types of leave requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Employee Name** (Field 1) and **Job Title** (Field 2) as accurately as possible.
2. Enter the **Start Date** (Field 3) and **End Date** (Field 4) for the proposed leave period.
3. Provide a brief **Reason for Leave** (Field 5) explaining the need for the leave.
4. Enter the **Number of Days Off** (Field 6) for the proposed leave period.
5. Select the necessary **Manager Approvals** (Field 7) by choosing all that apply from the list (e.g., Leave of Absence, Vacation, Business).
6. Select the necessary **Department Head Approvals** (Field 8) by choosing all that apply from the list (e.g., Leave of Absence, Vacation, Business).
7. Check the **Company Policies** (Field 9) section to ensure you understand any relevant policies that may affect your leave request.
8. Sign and date the **Employee Signature** (Field 10) to confirm your understanding of the form and its implications.
9. Have your manager sign and date the **Manager Signature** (Field 11) to confirm their approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (1)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or other official documents.
* **Job Title (2)**: Enter your current job title within the company.
* **Start Date (3)**: Enter the start date of your proposed leave period (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **End Date (4)**: Enter the end date of your proposed leave period (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Reason for Leave (5)**: Provide a brief explanation for your leave request, e.g., "To care for a new baby" or "To care for a family member."
* **Number of Days Off (6)**: Enter the number of working days you will be taking off for the leave period.
* **Manager Approvals (7)**: Select all that apply from the list to indicate the type(s) of approval your manager needs to sign off.
* **Department Head Approvals (8)**: Select all that apply from the list to indicate the type(s) of approval your department head needs to sign off.
* **Company Policies (9)**: Familiarize yourself with any relevant company policies that may affect your leave request.
* **Employee Signature (10)**: Sign and date this section to confirm your understanding of the form and its implications.
* **Manager Signature (11)**: Have your manager sign and date this section to confirm their approval.

## Tips
* Make sure to submit this form well in advance of your proposed leave period to allow enough time for processing and approval.
* Ensure you have discussed and agreed upon the details of your leave with your manager and department head before submitting this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your leave request, contact your HR representative for assistance.
