# Part Time Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Part Time Absence Form is used to request a part-time absence for personal or family matters, appointments, or health reasons. This form helps employees to inform their manager about their planned absence and obtain approval before taking the leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your reason for absence (absence_type) from the drop-down menu.
2. Choose the start and end dates of your absence.
3. Specify the duration of your absence (duration).
4. If your absence is not a one-time event, select how frequently you will be absent (frequency).
5. Enter your start time and end time of your absence.
6. Provide any additional notes about your absence (notes).
7. Enter your email address and phone number for contact information.
8. Confirm whether you have obtained approval from your manager for this absence (confirm_absence).
9. Select the managers who need to approve your absence (manager_approvals).
10. Choose the approver level required for your absence (approval_level).
11. Enter any message for your manager's approval (approval_message).
12. Select your manager's approval status (manager_approval).
13. Enter your employee ID and manager ID for tracking purposes.
14. Select the status of your absence (status).
15. Enter the date and time when this form was updated (updated_at).
16. Enter the date and time when this form was created (created_at).
17. Select who updated this form (updated_by).
18. Select who created this form (created_by).
19. Enter a unique form ID for tracking purposes (form_id).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **absence_type**: Select the reason for your absence from the drop-down menu. This can be Annual Leave, Sick Leave, or Other Leave.
* **start_date**: Enter the start date of your absence in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **end_date**: Enter the end date of your absence in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **duration**: Enter the number of days you will be absent.
* **frequency**: If your absence is not a one-time event, select how frequently you will be absent (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
* **start_time**: Enter the start time of your absence in the format of 24-hour clock (e.g., 08:00).
* **end_time**: Select the end time of your absence from the drop-down menu (e.g., 08:00, '09:00', '10:00', '11:00', '12:00', etc.).
* **notes**: Enter any additional information about your absence.
* **email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **confirm_absence**: Confirm whether you have obtained approval from your manager for this absence.
* **manager_approvals**: Select the managers who need to approve your absence (e.g., Manager, Manager 2, etc.).
* **approval_level**: Choose the approver level required for your absence (e.g., Approver 1, Approver 2, etc.).
* **approval_message**: Enter any message for your manager's approval.
* **manager_approval**: Select your manager's approval status for this absence.
* **employee_id**: Enter your employee ID for tracking purposes.
* **manager_id**: Enter your manager's ID for tracking purposes.
* **status**: Select the status of your absence (e.g., Pending, Approved, Denied).
* **updated_at**: Enter the date and time when this form was updated.
* **created_at**: Enter the date and time when this form was created.
* **updated_by**: Select who updated this form (e.g., Employee, Manager).
* **created_by**: Select who created this form (e.g., Employee, Manager).
* **form_id**: Enter a unique form ID for tracking purposes.
