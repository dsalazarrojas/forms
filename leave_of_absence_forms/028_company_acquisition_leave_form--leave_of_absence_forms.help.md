# Company Acquisition Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Company Acquisition Leave Form is used to request leave of absence due to a change in company ownership or merger. This form helps the employee to formally notify their manager and HR department of their intention to leave the company and to provide a clear reason for their departure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your employee name as it appears on your official records.
2.  Enter your employee ID, if you have one (not required but recommended for internal reference).
3.  Specify your department to help your manager understand your role and responsibilities.
4.  Enter the start date of your leave, which should be the date you will start your leave.
5.  Enter the expected return date, which should be the date you plan to return to work.
6.  Provide a brief reason for your leave, explaining why you are leaving the company.
7.  Enter the name of your manager who will approve your leave request.
8.  If applicable, list any supporting documents that can aid in the approval process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee full name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): If you have an employee ID, enter it for internal reference.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Specify your department to help your manager understand your role and responsibilities.
* **Leave start date** (`leave_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you will start your leave.
* **Leave end date** (`leave_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **Reason for leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required): Provide a brief reason for your leave, explaining why you are leaving the company.
* **Manager name for approval** (`manager_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of your manager who will approve your leave request.
* **Supporting documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): If applicable, list any supporting documents that can aid in the approval process.
