# Medical Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Leave Of Absence Request form is a formal request to take time off due to health reasons, and it is used to capture essential details for the approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Base Information page with the requested information.
2. Provide your Employee Info on the next page.
3. Enter your Medical Info and any relevant details.
4. Select the Manager Approval from the options on the next page.
5. Add any Comments about your leave of absence.
6. Fill out the Employee Name and Email fields.
7. Enter your Phone number.
8. Select the Medical Leave Start and End dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Base Information** (`base_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing any additional context or information about your medical leave request.
* **Employee Info** (`employee_info`, text, required/optional): Please provide your employee details, such as your role, department, or any other relevant information.
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text, required/optional): Describe your health condition, doctor's note, or other medical information related to your leave request.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required): Choose your manager's approval from the dropdown options to indicate their approval or denial.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information about your leave request.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your employee name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your official work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **Medical Leave Start Date** (`medical_leave_start_date`, date, required/optional): Select the start date of your medical leave.
* **Medical Leave End Date** (`medical_leave_end_date`, date, required/optional): Select the end date of your medical leave.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your manager can review and approve your request efficiently. If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative for assistance.
