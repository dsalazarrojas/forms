# Cancer Treatment Leave Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employees to request a leave of absence for cancer treatment. It will help HR understand the specifics of your request and provide necessary information for the leave approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your employee information, including name, ID, department, job title, manager's name, email address, and phone number.
2. Specify the details of your leave request, including the type of leave, start date, and return date (if known).
3. If applicable, provide a medical documentation to support your leave request.
4. Describe any urgent projects that need coverage during your absence.
5. Choose a preferred method of communication during your leave.
6. Confirm that you understand the company's leave policies.
7. Date and sign the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`, required/true): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, required/true): Type in your official company identification number.
* **Department** (`employee_dept`, required/true): Mention the department you work in.
* **Position** (`job_title`, required/true): Type in your current job title.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, required/true): Fill in the name of your immediate manager.
* **Email Address** (`employee_email`, required/true): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`employee_phone`, required/true): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Requested Leave Type** (`leave_type`, required/true): Choose the type of leave you require, such as Full-time Leave, Intermittent Leave, or Reduced Schedule.
* **Expected Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, required/true): Enter the date when your absence will begin.
* **Expected Return to Work Date** (`return_date`, required/false): Estimate the date you will return to work.
* **Total Number of Work Days** (`total_work_days`, required/false): Enter the number of work days you will be absent.
* **Nature of Leave Requirement** (`leave_nature`, required/true): Choose the category of your medical need, such as Surgery Recovery, Chemotherapy Cycle, or Chronic Condition Management.
* **Documentation Provided** (`documentation_status`, required/true): Select the status of your medical documentation, such as attaching proof or needing more time to provide it.
* **Urgent Projects to Transition** (`urgent_projects`, required/false): Briefly describe any tasks that need urgent coverage.
* **Preferred Communication Method** (`contact_method_during_leave`, required/true): Choose how you would like to be contacted by HR, such as through personal email or phone.
* **I Understand the Leave Policies** (`policy_understanding`, required/true): Confirm that you have read and understand the company's leave policies.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, required/true): Enter the date you submit this form.

Please note that some fields are marked as required but have a false value, this indicates that they might be optional or have a default value. Always double-check the form requirements to avoid missing essential information.
