# Worker Advocacy Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Advocacy Time Off Form is used to document and track time off requests from employees. This form collects information about the employee's time off, including the start and end dates, reason for the request, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the start and end dates for your time off.
2. Select the reason for your time off from the options provided.
3. Enter your employee ID and manager's ID.
4. Choose the approver for your time off from the list of available options.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments about your time off.
6. Enter your email address and phone number.
7. Enter your fax number and address.
8. Enter your city and state.
9. Enter your zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Date** (Date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your time off.
* **End Date** (Date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your time off.
* **Reason** (Text, required/optional): Enter the reason for your time off. This field is optional, but it helps managers understand your needs.
* **Prophets Id** (Text, required/optional): Enter your employee ID.
* **Approval Id** (Text, required/optional): Enter your manager's ID.
* **Start Time** (Time, required/optional): Enter the start time of your time off.
* **End Time** (Time, required/optional): Enter the end time of your time off.
* **Duration** (Number, required/optional): Enter the duration of your time off.
* **Leave Type** (Select One, required/optional): Select the type of leave you are requesting, either "Leave of Absence" or "Other".
* **Leave Reason** (Text, required/optional): Enter the reason for your leave, if different from the "Reason" field.
* **Employee Id** (Text, required/optional): Enter your employee ID.
* **Manager Id** (Text, required/optional): Enter your manager's ID.
* **Department Id** (Text, required/optional): Enter your department ID.
* **Manager** (Text, required/optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Approver** (Select Multiple, required/optional): Choose the approver for your time off from the list of available options.
* **Request Date** (Date, required/optional): Enter the date you are requesting your time off.
* **Approved Date** (Date, required/optional): Enter the date your time off was approved.
* **Notes** (Note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your time off.
* **Email** (Email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Fax** (Text, required/optional): Enter your fax number.
* **Address** (Text, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (Text, required/optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (Text, required/optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (Text, required/optional): Enter your zip code.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your manager or HR department.
