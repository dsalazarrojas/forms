# technical-training-leave-of-absence-request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting a technical training leave of absence, allowing employees to take time off for training and development purposes. It is intended for employees to request a leave of absence for technical training, non-technical training, conferences, or other related activities. The form is used to gather information necessary for approval and processing of the request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee information, including your Employee ID and Name.
2. Select the type of training you are requesting (Technical Training, Non-Technical Training, Conference, or Other).
3. Choose the start and end dates of your leave of absence.
4. Enter the training provider and location (if applicable).
5. Select the status of your request (Approved, Denied, Pending, Draft, or Submission Required).
6. Enter any necessary comments or notes.
7. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee**: Enter your employee ID and name. This is your unique identifier and name as an employee.
* **Manager**: Enter the name of your manager who will be reviewing your request.
* **Leave Duration**: Enter the number of days you will be absent for training. This is the number of days you will be taking off.
* **Training Type**: Select the type of training you are requesting (Technical Training, Non-Technical Training, Conference, or Other).
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your leave of absence.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your leave of absence.
* **Training Provider**: Enter the name of the training provider, if applicable.
* **Training Location**: Enter the location of the training, if applicable.
* **Manager Approval**: Select the status of your request in the eyes of your manager (Approved, Denied, Pending).
* **People Team Approval**: Select the status of your request in the eyes of the people team (Approved, Denied, Pending).
* **Approval Date**: Enter the date of approval or denial.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes related to your request.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date your form was submitted.
* **Form Submitted By**: Enter the person who submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted To**: Enter the person or team to whom the form was submitted.
* **Form Status**: Select the status of your request (Approved, Denied, Pending, Draft, Submission Required).
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as an employee.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Department**: Enter your department.
* **Location**: Enter your location.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email.
* **People Team Email**: Enter the email of the people team.
* **Form Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Submission Comments**: Enter any comments related to your submission.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information for spelling and date errors.
* Be clear and concise in your comments and notes.
* Ensure that your manager and people team are aware of your request.
* Follow up with your manager and people team after submitting your form to discuss any updates or concerns.
