# Injury Recovery Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record an injury recovery absence for an employee.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields and provide the necessary information.
2. Ensure that all the information is accurate and complete.
3. Get approval from your manager or supervisor before submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (employee_name): Enter your name as it appears in your employee directory.
* **Employee ID** (employee_id): Enter your employee ID as it appears in your HR system.
* **Job Title** (job_title): Enter your job title as it appears in your employee directory.
* **Start Date** (start_date): Enter the start date of your injury recovery absence.
* **End Date** (end_date): Enter the end date of your injury recovery absence.
* **Reason For Absence** (reason_for_absence): Describe the reason for your injury recovery absence.
* **Supervisor Name** (supervisor_name): Enter your supervisor's name as it appears on their business card or in your employee directory.
* **Manager Name** (manager_name): Enter your manager's name as it appears on their business card or in your employee directory.
* **Doctor Name** (doctor_name): Enter your doctor's name as it appears on their business card or in your employee directory.
* **Doctor License** (doctor_license): Enter your doctor's name as it appears on their business card or in your employee directory.
* **Doctor Phone** (doctor_phone): Enter your doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Note** (doctor_note): Enter your doctor's note or medical certification.
* **Doctor Email** (doctor_email): Enter your doctor's email address.
* **Supervisor Note** (supervisor_note): Enter any notes from your supervisor.
* **Manager Note** (manager_note): Enter any notes from your manager.
* **Approved By Manager** (approved_by_manager): Check 'Yes' if your manager has approved this absence.
* **Approved By Supervisor** (approved_by_supervisor): Check 'Yes' if your supervisor has approved this absence.

Note: Please ensure that all the fields are filled out accurately and completely before submitting this form.
