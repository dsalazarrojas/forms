# Factory Visit Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a factory visit leave of absence for a specific period. This form is used to notify the management and HR department of the planned absence and ensure that the necessary arrangements are made for the factory's operation during the absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the details of your factory visit leave request in the text field.
2. Select the technician ID from the list of available technicians.
3. Choose the start and end dates of your visit leave.
4. Provide a brief reason for your request.
5. Select your manager's ID from the list of available managers.
6. Choose the status of your request (Awaiting approval, Approved, or Rejected).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Factory Visit Leave Request** (`factory_visit_leave_request`): A short description of your factory visit leave request.
* **Technician ID** (`technician_id`): Select the ID of the technician who will be on factory visit leave.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): The date when your factory visit leave will begin.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): The date when your factory visit leave will end.
* **Reason** (`reason`): Briefly explain the reason for your factory visit leave request.
* **Manager ID** (`manager_id`): Select the ID of your manager who will be responsible for approving your request.
* **Status** (`status`): Select the status of your request (Awaiting approval, Approved, or Rejected).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information in the form to avoid any issues with your request.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the relevant personnel or manager before submitting the form.
* The status of your request will be updated by the system once approved or rejected by the manager.
