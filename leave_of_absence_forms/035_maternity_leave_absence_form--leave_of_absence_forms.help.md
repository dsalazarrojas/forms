# Maternity Leave Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to notify the company of an employee's maternity leave. This form should be completed by the employee to provide necessary information about their leave, including start and end dates, reason for leave, and expected return date. This form is used to ensure that all relevant parties are informed and that the employee's leave is properly processed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name and manager's name in the respective fields.
2. Select the start and end dates of your maternity leave using the date picker.
3. Provide a clear reason for your leave, as it will help the company understand your needs.
4. If applicable, specify the notice period and expected return date.
5. Enter your manager's email and phone number for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (Text, Required)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID card or company records.
* **Manager Name (Text, Required)**: Enter the name of your direct manager or the person who will be responsible for your leave.
* **Start Date (Date, Required)**: Select the date when your maternity leave will begin.
* **End Date (Date, Required)**: Select the date when you are expected to return to work.
* **Reason (Text, Required)**: Briefly describe your reason for taking maternity leave. This will help your manager understand your needs and provide necessary support.
* **Notice Period (Number, Optional)**: If you are planning to give a notice period before your leave, specify it here.
* **Expected Return Date (Date, Required)**: Select the date when you are expected to return to work.
* **Manager Email (Email, Optional)**: Enter your manager's email address for communication purposes.
* **Manager Phone (Text, Optional)**: Enter your manager's phone number for communication purposes.

## Tips

* Make sure to give a proper notice period to avoid any disruptions to your work and team.
* Keep your reason for leave clear and concise to facilitate understanding.
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your HR representative or manager.
