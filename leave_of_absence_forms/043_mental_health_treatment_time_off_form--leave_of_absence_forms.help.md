# mental_health_treatment_time_off_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to document the details of an employee's mental health treatment and leave of absence. It is used to collect information about the treatment type, start and end dates, treatment location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Request Details** field with a brief description of the reason for the mental health treatment and leave of absence.
2. Select the type of mental health treatment you are undergoing in the **Mental Health Treatment Type** field. Options include Therapy, Counseling, Doctor's visit, and Hospitalization.
3. Enter the location where you will be receiving treatment in the **Treatment Location** field.
4. Enter the start date of your treatment in the **Treatment Start Date** field.
5. Enter the end date of your treatment in the **Treatment End Date** field.
6. Enter the date you plan to return to work in the **Return to Work Date** field.
7. Upload any relevant doctor's certificate or note in the **Doctor's Certificate** field.
8. Enter your name and ID number in the **Employee Name** and **Employee ID** fields.
9. Enter your manager's name in the **Manager Name** field.
10. Select the status of your manager's approval in the **Manager Approval** field. Options are Approved, Declined, and Not Required.
11. Enter any additional notes or comments in the **Notes** field.
12. Enter your manager's comments or feedback in the **Manager Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Details** (id: 1, text, optional): A brief description of the reason for the mental health treatment and leave of absence.
* **Mental Health Treatment Type** (id: 2, select_multiple, optional): The type of mental health treatment you are undergoing. Options include Therapy, Counseling, Doctor's visit, and Hospitalization.
* **Treatment Location** (id: 3, text, optional): The location where you will be receiving treatment.
* **Treatment Start Date** (id: 4, date, optional): The start date of your treatment.
* **Treatment End Date** (id: 5, date, optional): The end date of your treatment.
* **Return to Work Date** (id: 6, date, optional): The date you plan to return to work.
* **Doctor's Certificate** (id: 7, text, optional): Upload any relevant doctor's certificate or note.
* **Employee Name** (id: 8, text, optional): Your name.
* **Manager Name** (id: 9, text, optional): Your manager's name.
* **Employee ID** (id: 10, text, optional): Your ID number.
* **Manager Approval** (id: 11, select_one, optional): The status of your manager's approval. Options are Approved, Declined, and Not Required.
* **Notes** (id: 12, note, optional): Any additional notes or comments.
* **Manager Comments** (id: 13, text, optional): Your manager's comments or feedback.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all relevant fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with your HR representative or manager.
* Remember to upload any relevant documents or certificates, such as doctor's certificates.
* Double-check your manager's name and ID number for accuracy.
* Ensure that you select the correct manager approval status.
* Enter your return to work date correctly, taking into account company policies and procedures.
