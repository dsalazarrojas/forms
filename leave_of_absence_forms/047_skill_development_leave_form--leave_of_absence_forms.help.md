# Skill Development Leave Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Skill Development Leave Form is a tool used to request a leave of absence for skill development purposes. This form will capture the necessary information to review and approve your leave request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the start and end dates of your leave in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
3. Provide a brief reason for your leave in the "Reason" field.
4. If your manager needs to approve your leave, select "Yes" in the "Manager Approval" field.
5. If your employee needs to approve your leave, select "Yes" in the "Employee Approval" field.
6. If your manager has any comments, enter them in the "Manager Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to return from your leave.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Briefly explain why you are taking a leave for skill development.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if your manager needs to approve your leave.
* **Employee Approval** (`employee_approval`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you need to approve your leave.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, required): If your manager has any comments, enter them here.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate that your manager has reviewed and approved your leave.
* **Employee Approval** (`employee_approval`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have reviewed and approved your leave.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting your form.
* Ensure that you have not already submitted a leave request for the same period.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR department.
