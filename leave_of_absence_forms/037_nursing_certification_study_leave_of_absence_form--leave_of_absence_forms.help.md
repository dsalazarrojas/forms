# Nursing Certification Study Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for a nursing certification study, which is a temporary absence from work to focus on obtaining a nursing certification. The form is intended for employees who are taking a break from work to pursue further education or training in the field of nursing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Provide your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Explain the reason for your leave in the Reason for Leave field.
6. Enter the date you plan to start your leave in the Start Date field.
7. Enter the date you plan to return from your leave in the End Date field.
8. If applicable, enter the name of the certification you are studying for in the Certification Name field.
9. Enter the name of the institution where you will be studying in the Institution field.
10. If you expect to be away from work for a specific duration, enter the number of months or weeks in the Duration field.
11. Choose the status of your leave request from the Status field (approved, denied, or pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name** (Last name, text, required): Enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached during your leave.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached during your leave.
* **Reason for Leave** (Reason for leave, text, required): Explain why you are requesting leave for a nursing certification study.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, required): Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, required): Enter the date you plan to return from your leave.
* **Certification Name** (Certification Name, text, optional): If you are studying for a specific nursing certification, enter its name.
* **Institution** (Institution, text, optional): Enter the name of the institution where you will be studying.
* **Duration** (Duration, number, optional): If you expect to be away from work for a specific duration, enter the number of months or weeks.
* **Status** (Status, select multiple, optional): Choose the status of your leave request from approved, denied, or pending.
