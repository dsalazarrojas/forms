## Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Off Request Form is used to request time off for a specific period, allowing you to notify your manager and HR of your intention to take time off. This form is used to ensure a smooth transition of work and to request approval for your time off.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your employee information in the "Employee Information" field.
2. Select the start and end dates of your time off in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
3. Enter the length of your time off in the "Time Interval" field.
4. If necessary, provide contact information for someone who can be reached during your time off.
5. Add any additional comments in the "Comments" field, if needed.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Employee Information (Employee Information)
* This field is used to input your employee ID, name, and other relevant information.
### 2. Start Date (Start Date)
* Enter the start date of your time off in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
### 3. End Date (End Date)
* Enter the end date of your time off in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
### 4. Time Interval (Time Interval)
* Enter the number of days of time off you are requesting.
### 5. Contact Information (Contact Information)
* If someone needs to be contacted during your time off, enter their email, phone number, or other contact information.
### 6. Comments (Comments)
* Add any additional comments or notes regarding your time off request.
## Tips
* Please ensure that you have approval from your manager before submitting this form.
* Double-check that your start and end dates are correct before submitting the form.
* This form is for official requests, not for personal use.
