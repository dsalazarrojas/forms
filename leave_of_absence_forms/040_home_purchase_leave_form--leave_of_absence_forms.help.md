# Home Purchase Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Purchase Leave Form is used to document a leave request for a home purchase, which can include home repairs, renovations, or maintenance. This form helps to collect necessary information for HR or management to process and approve the leave request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide the correct dates for the start and end of the leave.
3. Enter a clear reason for the leave in the "Reason for Leave" field.
4. Enter the purchase price of the home.
5. Specify the type of home being purchased or improved.
6. Add any additional comments or notes regarding the leave.
7. Enter the address of the home being purchased or improved.
8. Provide the name and contact information of the person handling the home purchase or improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Purchase Leave Form**: This is the main section of the form.
* **Home Purchase Date**: Enter the date when the home purchase or improvement started.
* **Reason for Leave**: Enter a clear and concise reason for taking this home purchase leave.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date when the leave started.
* **End Date**: Enter the date when the leave will end.
* **Purchase Price**: Enter the total cost of the home purchase or improvement.
* **Home Type**: Enter the type of home being purchased or improved (e.g., single-family home, condo, apartment).
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding the leave.
* **Home Address**: Enter the address of the home being purchased or improved.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person handling the home purchase or improvement.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
