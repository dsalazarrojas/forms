# Faculty Sabbatical Leave Of Absence Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Sabbatical Leave Of Absence Application form is for faculty members who are requesting a sabbatical leave of absence for a specific period. This form helps us understand the reasons behind the request and ensures that the necessary steps are followed to approve or decline the leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in your faculty information in the "Faculty Info" section, providing your name and other relevant details.
*   Enter the start and end dates of your sabbatical leave, making sure to provide a specific date range.
*   In the "Reason" section, clearly state the reason for your sabbatical leave, such as research, family, or other.
*   For the "Department" section, confirm whether you have a department or not.
*   In the "Academic Terms" section, indicate which academic terms you plan to take off.
*   Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" section so we can reach out to you with any questions or requests.
*   Finally, in the "Submitter Info" section, enter your email address so we can contact you about the status of your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Faculty Info** (Faculty Info, Text, Required/Optional): This is where you can enter your faculty information, such as your name and other relevant details.
*   **Start Date** (Start Date, Date, Required/Optional): This is the start date of your sabbatical leave, which should be a specific date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **End Date** (End Date, Date, Required/Optional): This is the end date of your sabbatical leave, which should be a specific date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Reason** (Reason, Text, Required/Optional): Provide a clear explanation for your sabbatical leave, such as research, family, or other. This will help us understand your request.
*   **Department** (Department, Select One, Required/Optional): Confirm whether you have a department or not.
*   **Academic Terms** (Academic Terms, Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Indicate which academic terms you plan to take off. You can select one or multiple terms.
*   **Contact Info** (Contact Info, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you with any questions or requests.
*   **Submitter Info** (Submitter Info, Email, Required/Optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the status of your application.
