# strike_participation_time_off_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record an employee's participation in a strike, including start and end dates, hours absent, and reason for absence.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employee(s) you wish to report participation for from the list of available options.
2. Enter the start date of the employee's participation in the strike.
3. Enter the end date of the employee's participation in the strike.
4. Enter the number of hours the employee was absent from work.
5. Provide a reason for the employee's absence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee** (`employee`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the employee(s) you wish to report participation for from the list of available options.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the employee's participation in the strike.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of the employee's participation in the strike.
* **Hours Absent** (`hours_absent`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of hours the employee was absent from work.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required/optional): Provide a reason for the employee's absence.
## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct employee(s) and enter accurate dates and hours to ensure accurate reporting.
* If the employee's absence reason is not provided, you may be able to leave it blank if it is not applicable or you are unsure of the reason.
