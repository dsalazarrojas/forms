# unpaid_leave_of_absence_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Unpaid Leave of Absence Form is intended for employees to notify their employer of their intention to take unpaid leave of absence. This form helps managers and HR teams keep track of employee leave requests and associated dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Click on the "Start Date" field.
*   Select or enter the date when you expect to start your leave of absence.
*   Click on the "End Date" field.
*   Select or enter the date when you expect to end your leave of absence.
*   Enter the "Reason" field, describing the reason for your unpaid leave of absence.
*   Click on the "Return Date" field.
*   Select or enter the date when you expect to return to work after your leave of absence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when you expect to start your leave of absence. If you are unsure, you can enter a date in the future when you know you will be able to return to work.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when you expect to end your leave of absence. If you are unsure, you can enter a date in the future when you know you will be able to return to work.
*   **Reason** (`reason`, text, required/optional): Describe the reason for your unpaid leave of absence in a few sentences. Be as specific as possible, so your manager can understand your situation.
*   **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when you expect to return to work after your leave of absence. If you are unsure, you can enter a date in the future when you know you will be able to return to work.

## Tips
*   Be honest when describing your reason for leave, as this will help your manager understand and support your needs.
*   Enter accurate dates so that your HR team can process your leave request smoothly.
*   If you need to provide additional information or attachments, you can include them with your form submission.
