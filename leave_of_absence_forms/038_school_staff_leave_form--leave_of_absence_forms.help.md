# School Staff Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Staff Leave Form is designed to allow school staff to request and manage leaves of absence, including medical, annual, and other types of leaves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Request Information" section with your contact details.
2. Select the "Leave Type" and "Reason" for your absence.
3. Enter the "Date of Absence" and "Leave End Date" for your leave.
4. Provide a valid "Reason for Absence" to support your request.
5. If applicable, enter the number of "Leave Hours Absorbed" and "Leave Days Absorbed".
6. Confirm with your supervisor and school admin that your leave request has been approved or denied.
7. Enter any additional "Comments" if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Request Information
* **Request Information** (`request_info`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide any additional information about your leave request.
* **Date of Absence** (`date_of_absence`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you will be absent from school.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, `text`, optional): Provide a valid reason for your absence.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional contact information that may be helpful for your school staff to reach out to you.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as an employee.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Supervisor Confirmation** (`supervisor_confirmation`, `select_one`, optional): Check "Yes" if your supervisor has approved your leave request.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **School Admin Confirmation** (`school_admin_confirmation`, `select_one`, optional): Check "Yes" if your school admin has approved or denied your leave request.
* **School Admin Name** (`school_admin_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your school admin's name.
* **School Admin Email** (`school_admin_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your school admin's email address.
* **School Admin Phone** (`school_admin_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your school admin's phone number.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the approval status of your leave request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments about your leave request.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of leave you are requesting.
* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for your leave request.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Leave Hours Absorbed** (`leave_hours_absorbed`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of hours you will be absent.
* **Leave Days Absorbed** (`leave_days_absorbed`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of days you will be absent.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of leave you are requesting.
* **Leave Reason** (`leave_reason`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for your leave request.
* **Supervisor Confirmation** (`supervisor_confirmation`, `select_one`, optional): Check "Yes" if your supervisor has confirmed your leave request.
* **School Admin Confirmation** (`school_admin_confirmation`, `select_one`, optional): Check "Yes" if your school admin has confirmed your leave request.
