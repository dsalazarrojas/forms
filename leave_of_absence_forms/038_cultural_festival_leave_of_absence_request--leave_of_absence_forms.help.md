# Cultural Festival Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the process of requesting leave of absence for cultural festivals. It is a flexible and user-friendly way to ensure that your leave request is submitted correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your thoughts on the festival details in the "Festival Details" field. This is where you can provide the name of the festival you are attending, its dates, and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your employee information in the "Employee Information" field. This is where you can enter your name, employee ID, and any other relevant personal details.
3. Select the dates you wish to take off for the festival in the "Dates Offered" field. You can choose all dates or select specific dates that you would like to take off.
4. Enter the duration of your leave in the "Duration" field. This is where you can specify how many days you would like to take off for the festival.
5. If you have any specific shift requests, please enter them in the "Shift Requests" field.
6. You can add any additional leave approvals or notes in the "Leave Approvals" field.
7. If you have any additional comments or requests, please enter them in the "Notes" field.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Festival Details** (`festival_details`, text, optional): This is where you can enter the details of the festival you are attending, such as its name, dates, and any other relevant information.
* **Employee Information** (`employee_info`, text, optional): This is where you can enter your employee ID, name, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Dates Offered** (`dates_offered`, select_multiple, required): Select the dates you wish to take off for the festival. You can choose all dates or select specific dates that you would like to take off.
* **Duration** (`duration`, text, optional): This is where you can specify how many days you would like to take off for the festival.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, text, optional): This is where you can select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., cultural festival, vacation, etc.).
* **Shift Requests** (`shift_requests`, text, optional): If you have any specific shift requests, please enter them here.
* **Leave Approvals** (`leave_approvals`, text, optional): If you have any additional leave approvals or notes, please enter them here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, please enter them here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to complete and submit your form.
