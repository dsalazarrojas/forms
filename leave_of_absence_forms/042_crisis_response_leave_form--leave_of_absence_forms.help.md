# crisis_response_leave_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a request for a crisis response leave. This type of leave is typically granted for extraordinary circumstances such as natural disasters, family emergencies, or other urgent situations that require immediate attention. Please fill out this form to initiate the leave request process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant Emergency Type from the options provided.
2.  Enter the duration of your leave in the number of days or hours you need.
3.  Enter the Start Date and End Date for your leave.
4.  Provide a clear Reason for Leave explaining why you need this type of leave.
5.  Enter your Contact Number so we can reach out to you for further questions or clarification.
6.  If applicable, provide any supporting documentation that may be relevant to your leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Type**: Select the type of emergency you are responding to (e.g. natural disaster, family emergency).
* **Duration**: Enter the number of days or hours you need for your leave.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to begin your leave.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **Reason for Leave**: Enter a clear and concise explanation of why you need this type of leave.
* **Contact Number**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached for further questions or clarification.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the emergency is occurring (if applicable).
* **Supporting Documents**: Attach any relevant supporting documents that may be required for your leave request.
