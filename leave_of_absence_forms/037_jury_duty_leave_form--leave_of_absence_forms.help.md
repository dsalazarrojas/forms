# Jury Duty Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jury Duty Leave Form is a document used to request time off for jury duty. It is an official request to the HR department, stating the dates of absence and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct "Jury Duty Location" where you will be attending jury duty (e.g. Court, Juror's Home, Jail, or Other).
2. Enter the "Request Start Date" and "Request End Date" accurately, as these dates will be used to determine your leave of absence period.
3. Enter your "Employee ID" number, which can be found on your employee ID card or in your HR records.
4. Enter your name in the "Employee Name" field, which is the name as it appears on your ID card or in your HR records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Jury Duty Location**: Select the location where you will be attending jury duty. This helps us to understand where you will be and ensures that we can properly coordinate your leave.
* **Request Start Date**: Enter the date you will start serving on the jury duty. This is the date you will start your leave of absence from work.
* **Request End Date**: Enter the date you will end serving on the jury duty. This is the date you will return to work after your leave.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your unique ID number, which is used to track your attendance and leave records.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID card or in your HR records.
* **Return Start Date**: Enter the date you will return to work after your leave of absence.
