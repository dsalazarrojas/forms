# urban_development_symposium_leave_of_absence_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The urban development symposium leave of absence form is a tool used to request time away from work for an event. This form helps event organizers track and manage leave requests, ensuring a smooth and organized event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Event Details" section with information about the event.
2. Select the team members involved in the event under "Event Team".
3. Provide absence details, such as dates, in the "Absence Details" section.
4. Select the absence dates for the event in the "Absence Dates" section.
5. Enter your start and end dates in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
6. Enter a reason for your leave in the "Reason" field.
7. Add any additional comments in the "Comment" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. Specify the assigned tool for the event in the "Assigned Tool" field.
11. Enter the name of the event coordinator in the "Coordinator" field.
12. Enter the name of the person submitting the form in the "Submitter" field.
13. Enter the email address of the person submitting the form in the "Submitter Email" field.
14. Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form in the "Submitter Phone" field.
15. Enter the event ID in the "Event ID" field.
16. Enter the name of the event in the "Event Name" field.
17. Enter the location of the event in the "Location" field.
18. Select the time zone for the event in the "Time zone" field.
19. Enter the time zone offset for the event in the "Time Zone Offset" field.
20. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**: This section is where you will enter information about the event. Please provide a brief description of the event.
* **Event Team**: Select the team members involved in the event from the provided options. This will help event organizers keep track of team members' responsibilities and roles.
* **Absence Details**: Provide details about your absence, such as the reason and impact on the event.
* **Absence Dates**: Select the dates of your absence for the event.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your absence.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your absence.
* **Reason**: Enter a brief reason for your leave.
* **Comment**: Add any additional comments or notes about your absence.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool**: Specify the tool assigned for the event.
* **Coordinator**: Enter the name of the event coordinator.
* **Submitter**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Event ID**: Enter the ID of the event.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Time zone**: Select the time zone for the event.
* **Time Zone Offset**: Enter the time zone offset for the event.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit the form.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as "required".
