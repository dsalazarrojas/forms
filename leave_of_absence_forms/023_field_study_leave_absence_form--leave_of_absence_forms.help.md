# Field Study Leave Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Study Leave Absence Form is used to notify the University of a student's field study or absence. This form is required to be completed by students to inform the University about their planned absence or field study, which is crucial for the student's academic and personal well-being. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your User ID, which is your unique identifier at the University.
2. Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
3. Provide your email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter the start and end dates of your field study or absence.
5. Briefly describe the reason for your field study or absence in the "Reason" field.
6. Specify the location where you will be conducting your field study or absence.
7. If you are traveling to a different country, select your destination country from the provided options.
8. Select the name of the university you will be associated with during your field study or absence.
9. Enter the name of the faculty where you will be conducting your research.
10. Provide the name of the department where you will be conducting your field study or absence.
11. Enter the duration of your field study or absence in days.
12. Select the status of your field study or absence (Approved, Rejected).
13. Select who submitted the form (Student, Administrator, Faculty).
14. Finally, select the date when you submitted the form and the date when it was last updated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User ID (1)**: Enter your unique identifier at the University.
- **Name (2)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email (3)**: Provide your email address for communication purposes.
- **Study Start Date (4)**: Enter the start date of your field study or absence.
- **Study End Date (5)**: Enter the end date of your field study or absence.
- **Reason (6)**: Briefly describe the reason for your field study or absence.
- **Location (7)**: Specify the city or region where you will be conducting your field study or absence.
- **Country (8)**: If you are traveling to a different country, select your destination country.
- **University (9)**: Select the name of the university you will be associated with during your field study or absence.
- **Faculty (10)**: Enter the name of the faculty where you will be conducting your research.
- **Department (11)**: Provide the name of the department where you will be conducting your field study or absence.
- **Duration (12)**: Enter the duration of your field study or absence in days.
- **Status (13)**: Select the status of your field study or absence (Approved, Rejected).
- **Submitted By (14)**: Select who submitted the form (Student, Administrator, Faculty).
- **Submitted On (15)**: Enter the date when you submitted the form.
- **Updated By (16)**: Select who last updated the form (Student, Administrator, Faculty).
