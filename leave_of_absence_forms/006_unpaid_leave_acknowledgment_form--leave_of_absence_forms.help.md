# Unpaid Leave Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Unpaid Leave Acknowledgment Form is used to document and process an employee's request for unpaid leave. This form ensures that both the employee and management are aware of the arrangement and its details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your employee information, including their name and any other details as required.
2.  Enter the manager's name and contact information.
3.  Specify the start and end dates of the requested leave period.
4.  Provide the reason for taking the unpaid leave.
5.  Acknowledge and agree to the company's policy and procedure related to unpaid leave.
6.  Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, text, optional): Provide your personal details, including your name, for identification purposes.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your direct manager, who will process your request.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Specify the start date of your leave period.
*   Note: Ensure the date is in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY or DD/MM/YY).
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Specify the end date of your leave period.
*   Note: Ensure the date is in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY or DD/MM/YY).
*   **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, optional): Explain the reason for your unpaid leave, if required.
*   **Company Policy** (`company_policy`, text, optional): Read and agree to the company's policy on unpaid leave.
*   **Company Procedure** (`company_procedure`, text, optional): Read and agree to the company's procedure on unpaid leave.
*   **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, text, optional): Sign to acknowledge and agree to the company's policy and procedure.
*   **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, optional): The manager's signature confirms their approval of your leave request.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the form is completed.
*   **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the form is completed.
*   **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, optional): Sign to confirm your agreement and understanding of the form and its terms.
*   Note: This field is for the employee to sign and date the form.

**Tips**: Ensure that you have completed all required fields and reviewed the company's policy and procedure before submitting the form.
