# Seasonal Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a seasonal leave of absence request for approval by management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your employee information and leave details.
2. Select the type of leave you are requesting (Sick Leave, Vacation, or Family Leave).
3. Choose whether or not your manager has approved your request (Approved or Denied).
4. Provide any additional comments or information you'd like to share with your manager.
5. If your manager is reviewing your request, provide their comments and approval status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`employee`, text, optional): Enter your employee name and ID number for identification purposes.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title to help your manager understand your role.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Provide a brief reason for your leave request.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of leave you are requesting (Sick Leave, Vacation, or Family Leave).
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, optional): Select whether or not your manager has approved your request (Approved or Denied).
* **Employee Comment** (`employee_comment`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share with your manager.
* **Manager Comment** (`manager_comment`, text, optional): If you are a manager, add any comments or information you'd like to share with your employee.
