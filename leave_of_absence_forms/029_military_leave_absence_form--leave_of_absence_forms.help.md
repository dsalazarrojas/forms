# Military Leave Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to process leave requests for military personnel in the company. It gathers information needed for leave approval, including employee details, leave dates, duration, type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field. This is a required field.
2. If necessary, provide a reason for the leave in the "Reason for Leave" field.
3. Enter the "From Date" and "To Date" for the leave period.
4. Specify the "Duration of Leave" in the number of days.
5. Choose the type of military leave (Army, Navy, or Air Force) in the "Military Leave Type" field.
6. Select the leave approver(s) (CO, XO, or OIC) in the "Leave Approver" field.
7. Enter the name of the military unit in the "Military Unit" field. This is a required field.
8. If required, enter the military unit address in the "Military Unit Address" field.
9. Choose the leave requester (Commanding Officer, Officer In Charge, or Department Head) in the "Leave Requester" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee taking the leave.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for the leave, if necessary.
* **From Date** (`from_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the leave period.
* **To Date** (`to_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the leave period.
* **Duration of Leave** (`duration_of_leave`, number, optional): Specify the number of days for the leave.
* **Military Leave Type** (`military_leave_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of military leave (Army, Navy, or Air Force).
* **Leave Approver** (`leave_approver`, select_multiple, optional): Select the person(s) approving the leave (CO, XO, or OIC).
* **Military Unit** (`military_unit`, text, required): Enter the name of the military unit.
* **Military Unit Address** (`military_unit_address`, text, optional): If required, enter the military unit address.
* **Leave Requester** (`leave_requester`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the person requesting the leave (Commanding Officer, Officer In Charge, or Department Head).
