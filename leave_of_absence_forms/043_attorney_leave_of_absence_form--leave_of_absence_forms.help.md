# Attorney Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Attorney Leave Of Absence Form is used to notify the firm of an attorney's temporary absence and to arrange for case coverage during that time.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your name, bar number, position title, and office location in the "Attorney Information" section.
2. Select the type of leave and provide a reason for taking the leave in the "Leave Request Information" section.
3. List any active cases that need coverage in the "Case Coverage" section.
4. Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" section.
5. Specify the managing partner's name and prior approval status in the "Approval Information" section.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Additional Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attorney Name** (1, text, required): Your full name.
* **Bar Number** (2, text, required): Your state bar number.
* **Position Title** (3, text, required): Your current role at the firm.
* **Office Location** (4, text, required): Your office location.
* **Leave Start Date** (7, date, required): The date your leave will begin.
* **Leave End Date** (8, date, required): The date your leave will end.
* **Total Leave Days** (9, number, required): The number of business days you will be absent.
* **Type of Leave** (10, select_one, required): Choose from: Vacation, Medical, Family, Maternity/Paternity, Sabbatical, Unpaid, Other.
* **Reason for Leave** (11, text, required): A brief reason for your leave.
* **Active Cases and Matters** (13, text, required): List any active cases that need coverage.
* **Coverage Attorney** (14, text, required): Name of the attorney who will cover your cases.
* **Client Notification** (15, select_one, required): Choose from: Yes, No, In Progress.
* **Pending Deadlines** (16, text, optional): List any urgent deadlines during your leave.
* **Court Appearances During Leave** (17, text, optional): List any scheduled court appearances during your leave.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (19, text, required): Name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (20, text, required): Phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Office Access During Leave** (21, select_one, required): Choose from: Full Access, Limited Access, No Access.
* **Managing Partner Name** (23, text, required): Name of the partner approving your leave.
* **Prior Approval** (24, select_one, required): Choose from: Approved, Pending Approval, Conditional Approval.
* **Additional Notes** (25, text, optional): Any other relevant information.
