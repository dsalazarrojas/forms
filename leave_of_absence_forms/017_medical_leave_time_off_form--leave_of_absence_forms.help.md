# Medical Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a medical time off, a temporary leave of absence due to medical reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields: 
   - Employee Name, 
   - Start Date, 
   - End Date, 
   - Reason for Medical Leave, 
   and 
   - Submit the form.
2. Fill in the optional fields: 
   - Employee ID, 
   - Manager Name, 
   and 
   - Department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Request Time Off
* **Request Time Off**: This field is used to enter the time period you would like to take off due to medical reasons.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date when you would like to start your medical time off.
* **End Date**: Enter the date when you would like to end your medical time off.
* **Reason**: This field is used to explain your reason for requesting a medical time off.
* **Employee Name**: This field is used to enter your name as an employee.
* **Employee ID**: If you have a company ID, you can enter it here for reference.
* **Manager Name**: If you have a manager, enter their name here for their reference.
* **Department**: If you have a department, enter it here for reference.
* **Medical Document**: This field is used to attach any relevant medical documents to support your request.
* **Submit**: Choose "Yes" to submit your request and "No" to cancel it.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have attached all required medical documents before submitting this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your manager or HR department.
