# Legal Representation Leave Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Legal Representation Leave Form is designed to capture information related to an employee's legal representation leave, which is a type of leave that allows employees to take time off for personal or legal-related reasons while still being covered by their company's policies and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Legal Representation Leave Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your legal representation leave request details.
2. Fill out all the required fields to the best of your ability.
3. Review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Legal Representation Leave Form 1 (Page 1)
* **Legal Representation Leave Form** (`legalrepresentationleaveform`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of your legal representation leave request.
* **Employee Name** (`employeename`, text, not required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or company records.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leavestartdate`, date, not required): Enter the date you would like your leave to start.
* **Leave End Date** (`leaveenddate`, date, not required): Enter the date you would like your leave to end.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reasonforleave`, text, not required): Provide a brief explanation of your reason for requesting legal representation leave.
* **Legal Representative Name** (`legalrepresentativenames`, text, not required): Enter the name of your legal representative (e.g., lawyer or attorney).
* **Legal Representative Phone** (`legalrepresentativephone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of your legal representative.
* **Employee Email** (`employeeemail`, email, not required): Enter your company email address.
* **Legal Counsel Name** (`legalcounsellenam`, text, not required): Enter the name of your legal counsel.
* **Legal Counsel Phone** (`legalcounsellphone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of your legal counsel.
* **Legal Counsel Email** (`legalcounsellemail`, email, not required): Enter the email address of your legal counsel.
* **Legal Documents** (`legaldocuments`, text, not required): Attach any relevant legal documents related to your leave request.
* **Employee Signature** (`employeesignature`, number, not required): A unique number assigned to your employee ID or company ID.
* **Legal Signature** (`legalsignature`, number, not required): A unique number assigned to your legal representative or legal counsel.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your HR or legal department for assistance.
* Attach relevant legal documents if applicable.
