# Short Term Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Short Term Absence Form is used to notify your manager and HR of a short-term absence from work. This form helps you to record the dates of your absence, the reason for it, and any necessary approvals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the start date of your absence in the "Start Date" field.
2. Enter the end date of your absence in the "End Date" field.
3. In the "Reason" field, briefly describe the reason for your short-term absence.
4. In the "Manager Request" field, if your manager has specifically requested to fill out this form for you.
5. In the "Manager Approvals" field, enter the number of approvals received from your manager.
6. In the "Employee Request" field, if you are requesting time off for a specific reason.
7. In the "Employee Approvals" field, enter the number of approvals received from your employee representative (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Start Date (1)**: Enter the date you will be absent from work.
- **End Date (2)**: Enter the date you will return to work.
- **Reason (3)**: A brief description of the reason for your short-term absence.
- **Approved (4)**: A note indicating if your manager has approved your absence.
- **Manager Request (5)**: If your manager has requested you to fill out this form, enter the details here.
- **Manager Approvals (6)**: Enter the number of approvals received from your manager.
- **Employee Request (7)**: If you are requesting time off for a specific reason, enter the details here.
- **Employee Approvals (8)**: Enter the number of approvals received from your employee representative (if applicable).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to avoid confusion and ensure that your work responsibilities are covered while you're away.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult your manager or HR representative for clarification.
- Review and submit the form carefully before saving it.
