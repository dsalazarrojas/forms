# Pet Groomer Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Groomer Leave Of Absence Request form is used by staff members to request a leave of absence from work. This form is essential for informing management of your leave and ensuring a smooth transition of tasks and responsibilities during your absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the Submitter Name and Submitter Email fields.
2. Choose the correct Category (Yes/No) and reason for leave in the Reason for Leave field.
3. Select the start and end dates for your leave in the Start Date and End Date fields.
4. Provide a brief description of your leave in the Description field (optional).
5. Enter your staff member ID in the Staff Member ID field.
6. Ensure that your manager approves of your leave by selecting 'Yes' in the Manager Approval field.
7. Enter your manager's name and email address in the Manager Name and Manager Email fields (optional).
8. Enter your manager's phone number in the Manager Phone field (optional).
9. Provide any additional notes for your manager in the Manager Notes field (optional).
10. Finally, review your form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category (Yes/No)**: This field is used to indicate whether the leave is related to a personal or professional reason.
* **Description**: This optional field is used to provide a brief description of your leave.
* **Staff Member ID**: This field is used to identify the staff member requesting the leave.
* **Start Date**: This required field is used to select the start date of your leave.
* **End Date**: This required field is used to select the end date of your leave.
* **Reason for Leave**: This required field is used to select the reason for your leave.
* **Leave Duration**: This required field is used to enter the duration of your leave.
* **Manager Approval**: This required field is used to ensure that your manager approves of your leave.
* **Manager Name**: This optional field is used to enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email**: This optional field is used to enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone**: This optional field is used to enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Notes**: This optional field is used to provide any additional notes for your manager.
* **Submitter Name**: This required field is used to enter your name.
* **Submitter Email**: This required field is used to enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone**: This optional field is used to enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Notes**: This optional field is used to provide any additional notes for your submission.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for help or clarification before submitting the form.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
