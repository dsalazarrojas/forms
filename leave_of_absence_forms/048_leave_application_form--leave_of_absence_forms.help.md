# leave_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The leave application form is a document used to request a leave of absence for various reasons, including vacation, sick leave, or parental leave. This form helps HR and management review and process leave requests, ensuring all necessary information is collected and the request is properly evaluated and approved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your employee name in the **employee_name** field.
2.  Select the department you belong to in the **department** field (if applicable).
3.  Fill out the **contact_information** field with your current contact details.
4.  Enter your **employee_number**.
5.  Choose the **type_of_leave** that you are applying for (vacation, Sick, Parent, etc.).
6.  Set the **start_date** and **end_date** for your leave.
7.  Enter the reason for your **reason_for_leave**.
8.  If traveling, fill out the **travel_destination** field (if applicable).
9.  If returning, specify the **return_date**.
10.  Optionally, provide the duration of your stay/travel with **travel_duration** (if traveling).
11.  Click submit to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **employee_name**: Enter your name as per your personnel records.
*   **department**: Select the department you belong to (if applicable).
*   **contact_information**: Provide your contact details, including phone number and email.
*   **employee_number**: Enter your employee identification number.
*   **type_of_leave**: Choose the type of leave you are applying for (Vacation, Sick, Parent, etc.).
*   **start_date**: The date you plan to start your leave.
*   **end_date**: The date you plan to end your leave.
*   **reason_for_leave**: Briefly explain why you are requesting the leave.
*   **travel_destination**: Enter your travel location if applicable.
*   **return_date**: If applicable, enter the date you plan to return.
*   **travel_duration**: If you are traveling, provide the length of your stay.
*   **Note**: This form is meant for actual leave requests, please do not use it for other purposes.
