# Staff Leave Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Leave Application Form is used for staff to submit leave applications to their manager for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of leave you are applying for (Annual, Casual, or Sick leave).
2. Enter the start and end dates of your leave.
3. Enter the number of leave days you are requesting.
4. Provide a valid reason for your leave.
5. Add any additional comments or notes if necessary.
6. Select the status of your leave (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
7. Enter any notes or comments for your manager.
8. Enter your name.
9. Enter the name of your manager.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments for yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Type of Leave** (leave_type): Choose the type of leave you are applying for (Annual, Casual, or Sick leave).
* **Start Date** (start_date): Enter the start date of your leave.
* **End Date** (end_date): Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Leave Days** (leave_days): Enter the number of leave days you are requesting.
* **Reason for Leave** (reason): Please describe the reason for your leave.
* **Additional Comments** (comment): Any other details or comments about your leave.
* **Manager Status** (manager_status): Select the status of your leave (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
* **Manager Note** (manager_note): Any notes or comments for your manager.
* **Submitted By** (submitted_by): Enter your name.
* **Manager** (manager): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Submitter** (submitter): Enter the name of the submitter (you).
* **Submitter's Note** (submitter_note): Any notes or comments from you.
