# Performer Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence as a performer. It captures relevant information to process your request and facilitate communication between you and your manager.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in all required fields.
- Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performer Information** (`performer_info_page`, text, required): Please fill in your name and any other relevant details about yourself as a performer.
* **Manager Information** (`manager_info_page`, text, required): Please fill in your manager's name and contact information.
* **Dates** (`dates_page`, text, required): Specify the start and end dates of your requested leave of absence.
* **Duration and Reason** (`duration_reason_page`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation of why you are requesting the leave of absence and how long you need it for.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status_page`, text, required): This field will be filled in by your manager after they have reviewed your request. Please do not fill this in yourself.
