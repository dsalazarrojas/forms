# Arbitration Attendance Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employees to request time off from work for arbitration-related reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and supporting documentation ready.
2. Fill in your employee name, ID number, department, and supervisor's name.
3. Provide your leave start date, end date, and total days requested.
4. Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g. arbitration attendance, legal proceedings, etc.).
5. If necessary, provide additional details about your arbitration session.
6. Confirm whether the leave should be paid, unpaid, or partially paid.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`): Please enter your full name as it appears on your HR records.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID number as issued by HR.
* **Department** (`department`): Specify the department where you work.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter the name of your direct supervisor or manager.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`): Enter the date when your time off begins.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`): Enter the date when you return to work.
* **Total Days Requested** (`total_days_requested`): Enter the number of business days you are requesting for your leave.
* **Type of Leave** (`leave_type`): Select the reason for your leave from the list provided. If you are unsure, please contact HR.
* **Arbitration Session Date** (`arbitration_date`): Enter the scheduled date of your arbitration session.
* **Arbitration Location** (`arbitration_location`): If applicable, enter the city or venue name where the arbitration session will take place.
* **Supporting Documentation Provided** (`documentation_provided`): Indicate whether you have provided supporting documentation (e.g. arbitration notice, subpoena).
* **Leave Should Be** (`paid_unpaid`): Select whether the leave should be paid, unpaid, or partially paid.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_notes`): If needed, enter any additional relevant information about your leave.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth processing of your leave request.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, contact HR or your supervisor for guidance.
* Keep in mind that this form is for arbitration-related time off only, and other types of leave requests may have different forms and procedures.
