# Employee Personal Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a leave of absence from work. It is intended for employees to submit their requests for vacation, sick leave, bereavement, jury duty, or other types of leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the leave type (e.g. Vacation, Sick Leave, etc.)
2. Enter your start and end dates of leave
3. Specify the duration of your leave in days
4. Enter your employee name and department
5. Select your leave request status (e.g. pending, approved, etc.)
6. Select the approver (e.g. manager, director, etc.)
7. Enter the approver's name and contact information (if necessary)
8. Enter any additional comments or notes
9. Submit the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Leave Request** (`leave_request`, text): Enter a brief description of your leave request.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one): Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g. Vacation, Sick Leave, etc.).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date): Enter the date you want your leave to start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date): Enter the date you want your leave to end.
* **Duration Days** (`duration_days`, number): Enter the number of days you want to take for your leave.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or personnel file.
* **Department** (`department`, text): Enter your department.
* **Location** (`location`, text): Enter your work location.
* **Leave Request Status** (`leave_request_status`, select_one): Select the status of your leave request (e.g. pending, approved, etc.).
* **Approver** (`approver`, select_multiple): Select the person who will be approving your leave request.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, text): Enter the name of the person who will be approving your leave request.
* **Leave Request Date** (`leave_request_date`, date): Enter the date the leave request was submitted.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text): Enter the name of your department.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, text): Enter the email of your manager.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of your manager.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, text): Enter the email of the approver.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the approver.
* **Leave Request Notes** (`leave_request_notes`, note): Enter any additional comments or notes about your leave request.
* **Approver Comments** (`approver_comments`, note): Enter any comments or notes from the approver.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date): Enter the date the leave request was approved.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, select_one): Select the person who approved your leave request.
* **Approval Time** (`approval_time`, time): Enter the time of approval.
* **Approver Time** (`approver_time`, time): Enter the time of approval.
* **Leave Request Reason** (`leave_request_reason`, text): Enter a brief reason for your leave request.
