# Community Service Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a community service leave time off from work. Please use this form when you plan to volunteer for a non-profit or community group.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your employee information, including your name, ID, department name, and email address.
2. Select the organization where you will be volunteering.
3. Provide the start and end dates of your leave.
4. Specify the total hours you expect to be away.
5. Briefly describe the service activity you will be doing.
6. Choose the type of leave (paid or unpaid).
7. Indicate if this is a recurring commitment (weekly, monthly, or one-time event).
8. Enter the name of your manager who will review and approve your request.
9. Confirm that you have discussed the work impact with your team.
10. If applicable, confirm if your request is approved or denied by your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your department.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Organization where you will volunteer** (`organization_benefiting`, text, required): Enter the name of the non-profit or community group you will be volunteering with.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Total Hours Requested** (`total_hours_requested`, number, required): Enter the total number of hours you expect to be away (e.g., 8, 16).
* **Description of Service Activity** (`description_of_service`, text, required): Briefly describe what you will be doing during your volunteer work.
* **Is this leave paid or unpaid?** (`leave_type`, select_one, required): Choose whether your leave is paid or unpaid.
* **Is this a recurring commitment?** (`recurring_leave`, select_one, required): Choose if this is a recurring commitment (weekly, monthly, or one-time event).
* **Name of your manager** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Have you discussed the work impact with your team?** (`business_impact_assessed`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have discussed your duties with your team.
* **Approval Status** (`manager_approval_status`, select_one, required): For HR and management use only - do not fill out this field.

Note: The last two fields are for internal use only and should not be filled out by the employee. 

Also, note that this form is for volunteering, so if you are not planning to volunteer, please do not fill out this form.
