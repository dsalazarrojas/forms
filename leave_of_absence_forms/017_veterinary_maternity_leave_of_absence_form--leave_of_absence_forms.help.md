# Veterinary Maternity Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a structured format for employees to submit their maternity leave requests, allowing us to process and manage leave-related tasks efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your Employee ID, which is a unique identifier assigned to you by the company.
2.  Choose the Leave Start Date and Leave End Date of your maternity leave.
3.  Provide a brief Reason For Leave, which will help us understand your situation.
4.  Indicate your Expected Return Date, which is the date when you plan to return to work after your leave.
5.  (Optional) If applicable, provide the Doctor's Name and any relevant medical information.
6.  (Optional) If your request requires manager approval, provide the Manager’s Name and comments, and vice versa.
7.  Select the status of your leave request (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
8.  Add any relevant Comments or Comments from Manager.
9.  Specify the Veterinary Maternity Leave Policy Version if you know it.
10. Enter the updated by field if you are editing an existing submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Employee ID** (number): Enter your unique company ID.
*  **Leave Start Date** (date): The date when you plan to start your leave.
*  **Leave End Date** (date): The date when you plan to end your maternity leave.
*  **Reason For Leave** (text): Briefly describe your reason for taking maternity leave.
*  **Expected Return Date** (date): The date when you expect to return to work.
*  **Doctor Name** (text): If applicable, provide your doctor's name and any relevant medical information.
*  **Manager’s Name** (text): If required, provide your manager's name and comments.
*  **Leave of Absence Status** (select_one): Choose one of the following options: Pending, Approved, Denied.
*  **Comments** (text): Enter any comments you would like to make regarding your request.
*  **Comments from Manager** (text): If your manager has any comments or feedback, add them here.
*  **Veterinary Maternity Leave Policy Version** (text): Specify the version of the policy that applies to your case.
*  **Updated By** (text): Enter your name if you are editing an existing submission.

## Tips

*   Before submitting this form, make sure to review the company's policy on veterinary maternity leave.
*   Ensure that your submission aligns with the policy and company procedures.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to HR or your manager for guidance.
