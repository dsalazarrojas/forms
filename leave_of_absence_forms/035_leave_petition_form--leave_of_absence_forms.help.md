# Leave Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to create a leave petition for employees in the organization. It helps gather the necessary information for a leave of absence request, which can be due to various reasons such as medical emergencies, family-related concerns, or personal issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the start and end dates of your leave in the **Start Date** and **End Date** fields.
2. Provide a clear reason for your leave in the **Reason For Leave** field.
3. Enter your name as it appears on your employee identification badge.
4. Select your department from the options provided in the **Department** field.
5. Enter your supervisor's name in the **Supervisor Name** field.
6. Enter your manager's name in the **Manager Name** field.
7. Enter your manager's email address in the **Manager Email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Date** (Date): Enter the date when you would like your leave to start.
* **End Date** (Date): Enter the date when you would like your leave to end.
* **Reason For Leave** (Text): Provide a brief description of your reason for requesting a leave of absence.
* **Employee Name** (Text): Enter your name as it appears on your employee identification badge.
* **Department** (Text): Select your department from the provided options.
* **Supervisor Name** (Text): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Manager Name** (Text): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Email** (Email): Enter your manager's email address.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have discussed your leave of absence with your manager before submitting this form.
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in the form to ensure a smooth leave request process.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your HR department for guidance.
