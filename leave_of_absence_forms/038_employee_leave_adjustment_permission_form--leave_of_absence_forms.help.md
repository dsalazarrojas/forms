# Employee Leave Adjustment Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request changes to an employee's leave schedule. Employees can use this form to request adjustments to their leave dates or cancel existing leave.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by entering your employee name.
2.  Select the type of leave you are requesting adjustments for (vacation, sick leave, or personal leave).
3.  Provide the reason for your leave adjustment.
4.  Enter the start and end dates of your original leave period.
5.  Enter the date you are requesting the adjustment.
6.  Choose the type of adjustment you are requesting (to add or remove leave).
7.  Enter the reason for the adjustment.
8.  Sign and date your request.
9.  Have your HR representative sign and date their approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (1): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Leave Type** (2): Choose the type of leave you are requesting adjustments for. You can select from the options: Vacation, Sick Leave, or Personal Leave.
* **Reason for Leave** (3): Provide a clear and concise reason for your leave adjustment. This will help the HR department understand your request.
* **Start Date** (4): Enter the start date of your original leave period.
* **End Date** (5): Enter the end date of your original leave period.
* **Adjustment Date** (6): Enter the date you are requesting the adjustment.
* **Adjustment Type** (7): Choose whether you are requesting to add or remove leave from your schedule.
* **Adjustment Reason** (8): Enter a clear and concise reason for the adjustment.
* **Employee Signature** (9): Sign and date your request.
* **HR Signature** (10): Sign and date your HR representative's approval.
* **Comments** (11): This field is optional and can be used to provide any additional comments or information about your request.
* **Form ID** (12): This field is not relevant for end-users and should be ignored. 

## Tips

* Please ensure your request is accurate and complete before submitting this form.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of this form, please reach out to your HR department for clarification.
* Be clear and concise in your responses to ensure your request is processed efficiently.
