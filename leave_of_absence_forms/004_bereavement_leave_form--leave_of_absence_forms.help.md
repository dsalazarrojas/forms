# Bereavement Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bereavement Leave Form is used by employees to request leave from work following the passing of a family member or loved one. This form is necessary to ensure that your request is properly processed and handled in accordance with company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Ensure you have all necessary information, such as the deceased's name and your relationship with them.
2.  Fill in your full name and job title.
3.  Select your department to help us process your request efficiently.
4.  Provide the start and end dates of your requested leave.
5.  Enter your expected return to work date.
6.  Provide additional details or reasons for your request, if necessary.
7.  Enter your work email address and mobile phone number.
8.  Select your availability for urgent inquiries.
9.  Indicate whether you would like HR to provide information about the Employee Assistance Program.
10.  Certify that your request is in accordance with company policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`employee_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID or company records.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title as listed on your employee ID or company records.
* **Department** (`department_selection`, `select_one`, required): Select the department you belong to from the list to help us process your request efficiently.
* **Relationship to the Deceased** (`deceased_relationship_text`, `text`, required): Describe your relationship to the deceased person.
* **Start Date of Leave** (`start_date_leave`, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to start your bereavement leave.
* **End Date of Leave** (`end_date_leave`, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to end your bereavement leave.
* **Return to Work Date** (`reentry_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you expect to return to work after your bereavement leave.
* **Additional Details or Reason** (`leave_reason_description`, `text`, optional): Provide any further information regarding your request, if necessary.
* **Work Email Address** (`work_email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Mobile Phone Number** (`mobile_phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your mobile phone number.
* **Availability for Urgent Inquiries** (`availability_status`, `select_one`, required): Select how you will be available for urgent inquiries.
* **Grief Counseling Support** (`counseling_support_interest`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you would like HR to send information about the Employee Assistance Program.
* **Certification of Request** (`certification_statement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that your request is made in accordance with company policy.
