# Vacation Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record employee's leave of absence or vacation time off requests. It captures essential details such as employee name, dates, and other relevant information to help managers and HR track and manage employee's leave of absence or vacation time off.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name.
2. Enter your Start Date and End Date for your leave of absence or vacation time off.
3. Provide a Reason for your leave.
4. Enter the number of Vacation Days you plan to take.
5. Optional: Enter Start Time and End Time if applicable.
6. Enter your Email, Phone, and Address if you want to include it.
7. Enter the number of Vacation Days Required (if any).
8. Select the Leave Type (e.g., Annual Leave, Medical Leave, etc.).
9. Select Manager Approval status.
10. Optional: Enter Departmental Leave status and Employee Notes.
11. Enter Approver Notes (if manager or approver).
12. Select Status (e.g., Pending, Approved, Denied, Cancelled).
13. Enter Form ID, Assigned Tool, and Output File (if any).
14. Optional: Enter Category and Description.
15. Enter the Assigned User (if any).
16. Enter Created Date and Updated Date (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): The date you want to start your leave of absence or vacation time off.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): The date you want to end your leave of absence or vacation time off.
* **Reason** (`reason`): A brief description of your reason for taking leave of absence or vacation time off.
* **Vacation Days** (`vacation_days`): The number of days you plan to take for your leave of absence or vacation time off.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`): The time you plan to start your leave of absence or vacation time off (if applicable).
* **End Time** (`end_time`): The time you plan to end your leave of absence or vacation time off (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`): Your contact email address (if you want to include it).
* **Phone** (`phone`): Your phone number (if you want to include it).
* **Address** (`address`): Your workplace or home address (if you want to include it).
* **Vacation Days Required** (`vacation_days_required`): The number of vacation days required (if any).
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`): Select the type of leave you are taking (e.g., Annual Leave, Medical Leave, etc.).
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`): Select the approval status from your manager.
* **Departmental Leave** (`departmental_leave`): Select the departmental leave status (if applicable).
* **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments about your leave of absence or vacation time off.
* **Approver Notes** (`approver_notes`): Enter any notes or comments from the approver.
* **Status** (`status`): Select the status of your leave of absence or vacation time off (e.g., Pending, Approved, Denied, Cancelled).
* **Form ID** (`form_id`): Enter the form ID (if any).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Enter the assigned tool (if any).
* **Output File** (`output_file`): Enter the output file (if any).
* **Category** (`category`): Enter the category (if any).
* **Description** (`description`): Enter a brief description of your leave of absence or vacation time off (if any).
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`): Enter the assigned user (if any).
* **Created Date** (`created_date`): The date this form was created.
* **Updated Date** (`updated_date`): The date this form was last updated.
