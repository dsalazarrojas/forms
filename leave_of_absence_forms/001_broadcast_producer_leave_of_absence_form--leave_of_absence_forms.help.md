# Broadcast Producer Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to streamline the leave of absence process for Broadcast Producers. It helps HR and management to efficiently gather necessary information and ensure a smooth transition of duties while the producer is away.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the steps outlined below:

1. Fill in your details in the relevant fields. 
2. Select the correct department from the list. 
3. Choose the appropriate type of leave from the dropdown menu. 
4. Enter the start and end dates of your leave. 
5. If necessary, provide a brief explanation of your leave purpose. 
6. Fill in your supervisor's name and email address for notification. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): This is your full legal name as it appears on your ID or contract.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): This is the unique identifier assigned by HR for tracking purposes.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department you are currently working in from the list. This will help us ensure you are properly affiliated with the correct team.
* **Type of Leave** (`leave_type`, select_one, required): Choose the appropriate category for your leave. This will help us process your request accordingly.
* **Leave Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when your leave begins. 
* **Leave End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when your leave ends. 
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason`, text, optional): Briefly explain the purpose of your leave. This will help us better understand your needs and ensure a smooth transition.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor who will approve your leave. 
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your supervisor for notification purposes. 
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): If you have any extra information for HR, feel free to add it here. 

## Tips

* Please ensure all information is accurate and up-to-date. 
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your HR representative for guidance. 
* This form is intended for leave of absence purposes only. Do not use it for other uses.
