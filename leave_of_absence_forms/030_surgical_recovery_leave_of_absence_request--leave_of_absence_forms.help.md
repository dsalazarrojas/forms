# Surgical Recovery Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a surgical recovery leave of absence for an employee. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary information from the employee and the doctor performing the surgery to facilitate the leave process. This form will help ensure that the necessary details are provided to initiate a smooth and accurate leave processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the employee's name as it appears on their personnel record.
2. Provide the supervisor's ID number.
3. Enter the name of the doctor performing the surgery.
4. Enter the doctor's title or position.
5. Enter the name of the hospital or facility where the surgery will take place.
6. Enter the date of the surgery.
7. Enter the expected date of return to work.
8. Provide the reason for the leave, which will help us process the leave correctly.
9. Enter the doctor's signature to validate their medical information.
10. Enter the doctor's license number.
11. Enter the doctor's contact phone number.
12. Enter the doctor's email address.
13. Enter the employee's signature to acknowledge their request.
14. Enter the reason for the surgery.
15. Enter the doctor's facility name (if different from the original hospital).
16. Enter the date of the request.
17. Select the current status of the employee (Active, Inactive, Retired, or Terminated).
18. Select the type of leave (Short-term, Medical, or Other).
19. Enter the start date of the leave.
20. Enter the end date of the leave.
21. (Optional) Select additional leave type (Short-term, Medical, or Other) and enter the corresponding dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: The name of the employee requesting the leave.
* **Supervisor ID**: The ID number of the supervisor who will be responsible for processing this leave request.
* **Doctor Name**: The name of the doctor performing the surgery.
* **Doctor Title**: The title or position of the doctor performing the surgery.
* **Hospital Name**: The name of the hospital or facility where the surgery will take place.
* **Date Of Surgery**: The date of the scheduled surgery.
* **Return Date**: The expected date of return to work after the surgery.
* **Reason For Leave**: A brief explanation for the reason for the leave.
* **Doctor Signature**: The digital signature of the doctor to validate their medical information.
* **Doctor License**: The doctor's license number.
* **Doctor Phone**: The contact phone number of the doctor.
* **Doctor Email**: The email address of the doctor.
* **Patient Signature**: The digital signature of the employee to acknowledge their request.
* **Employee Signature**: The digital signature of the employee to confirm their request.
* **Reason For Surgery**: A brief explanation for the reason for the surgery.
* **Doctor Facility (2)**: The name of the second doctor's facility (if different from the original hospital).
* **Date Of Request**: The date of the request for the leave.
* **Employee Status**: The current status of the employee (Active, Inactive, Retired, or Terminated).
* **Leave Type**: The type of leave (Short-term, Medical, or Other).
* **Leave Start Date**: The start date of the leave.
* **Leave End Date**: The end date of the leave.
* **Leave Type 2**: (Optional) Additional leave type.
* **Leave Start Date 2**: (Optional) The start date of the second leave.
* **Leave End Date 2**: (Optional) The end date of the second leave.
* **Doctor Facility 2**: (Optional) The name of the second doctor's facility.
