# Parental Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request parental leave time off, allowing employees to inform their managers about their plans for taking leave due to family or parental responsibilities. It is intended for employees who need to take time off to care for their children, parents, or other family members. This form should only be used for personal or family-related reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your start date for the leave period. This is the date when you plan to start taking time off from work.
2. Enter your end date for the leave period. This is the date when you plan to end taking time off from work.
3. Provide a brief explanation for your leave, stating the reason you are taking time off.
4. If you are a manager, please enter your email address to receive notifications about this leave request.
5. Enter your job title and employee name to ensure accurate tracking of leave requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date**: (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when you plan to start taking time off from work. Please format the date according to the standard format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **End Date**: (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when you plan to end taking time off from work. Please format the date according to the standard format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Reason for Leave**: (`reason_for_leave`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief explanation for your leave, stating the reason you are taking time off. Please keep it concise and clear.
* **Job Title**: (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title to ensure accurate tracking of leave requests.
* **Manager Email**: (`manager_email`, email, required/optional): If you are a manager, please enter your email address to receive notifications about this leave request.
* **Employee Name**: (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name to ensure accurate tracking of leave requests.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate dates for your start and end dates.
* Keep your reason for leave brief and clear.
* If you are a manager, please make sure to update your email address if it has changed.
* This form is for personal or family-related reasons only. If you have any other reason for leave, please contact your HR department or manager.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your HR department or manager for assistance.
