# Request For Leave - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting leave from your work. It's used to inform your team members and superiors about your planned absence. Please make sure to fill it out accurately and only submit if you're planning to be away from work for a specific period.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a successful request for leave, follow these steps:
1. In the **Request For Leave** field, describe your reason for requesting leave in brief.
2. In the **Name** field, enter your name as it appears in our company records.
3. Choose the **Leave Reason** that best applies to your situation.
4. Select the **Dates Of Absence** for which you'll be away from work.
5. Enter the **Duration** of your leave in working days.
6. Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** for your leave period.
7. If applicable, select the **Assigned Tool** you've been using during your leave.
8. Add any additional **Notes** about your leave request if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request For Leave** (`request_for_leave`, text, required): Describe your reason for requesting leave in this field.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears in our company records.
* **Leave Reason** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required): Choose the leave reason that best applies to your situation.
* **Dates Of Absence** (`dates_of_absence`, select_multiple, required): Select the dates you'll be away from work.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, required): Enter the number of working days you'll be absent.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you'll start your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you'll end your leave.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): If you've been using an assigned tool during your leave, select it here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional information or notes about your leave request if needed.

## Tips
* Please be accurate and truthful when filling out this form.
* If your leave request is accepted, you'll receive an email confirmation.
* If your leave request is denied, you'll receive an email explaining the reasons for the denial.
