# Personal Crisis Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Crisis Leave Of Absence Request form is used to submit a request for a leave of absence due to personal crisis, such as a bereavement, medical emergency, or family crisis. This form is intended for employees who require time away from work to address personal issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee information, including your name and employee ID.
2. Describe the personal crisis that requires your leave of absence.
3. Select the start and end dates of your leave of absence.
4. Provide a reason for your leave of absence.
5. Enter your expected return date.
6. Select the status of your leave of absence (Active or Inactive).
7. Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
8. Assign the request to the person or department responsible for reviewing and processing your request.
9. Submit your request by entering your employee ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Information (employee_information)
* **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, `text`, required): Enter your name and employee ID.

### Personal Crisis (personal_crisis)
* **Personal Crisis** (`personal_crisis`, `text`, required): Describe the personal crisis that requires your leave of absence, e.g., bereavement, medical emergency, or family crisis.

### Start Date (start_date)
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to begin your leave of absence.

### End Date (end_date)
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to return to work after your leave of absence.

### Reason For Leave (reason_for_leave)
* **Reason For Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your leave of absence.

### Return Date (return_date)
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, `date`, optional): Enter your expected return date, if known.

### Status (status)
* **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, required): Select Active (if you are currently working) or Inactive (if you are on leave).

### Comments (comments)
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your request.

### Assigned To (assigned_to)
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, optional): Assign the request to the person or department responsible for reviewing and processing your request.

### Submitted By (submitted_by)
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required): Enter your employee ID to submit your request.
