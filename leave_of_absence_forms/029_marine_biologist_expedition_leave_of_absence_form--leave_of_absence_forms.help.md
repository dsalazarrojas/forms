# Marine Biologist Expedition Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Marine Biologist Expedition Leave Of Absence Form is for requesting a leave of absence for a Marine Biologist expedition. This form will help us process your request and ensure that the necessary arrangements are made for the expedition team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have authorized to request a leave of absence and have obtained the necessary approval from your team lead or manager.
3. If you are unsure about any information, please contact your HR representative for clarification.
4. Double-check that you have provided all the required contact information for the expedition team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Expedition Leave Of Absence Form** (form_header, note, required: false)
	+ This is the title of the form.
* **Applicant Name** (applicant_name, text, required: false)
	+ Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email, email, required: false)
	+ Enter the email address of the contact person for the expedition team.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the phone number of the contact person for the expedition team.
* **Expedition Leader Name** (expedition_leader_name, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the leader or team manager for the expedition.
* **Expedition Start Date** (expedition_start_date, date, required: false)
	+ Enter the start date of the expedition.
* **Expedition End Date** (expedition_end_date, date, required: false)
	+ Enter the end date of the expedition.
* **Expedition Location** (expedition_location, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the location of the expedition.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any information, please contact your HR representative for clarification.
- Double-check that you have provided all the required contact information for the expedition team.
- If you attempt to request multiple leaves for the same expedition, please contact us for further assistance.
