# Military Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for military personnel to report their upcoming leave, providing necessary details such as leave date, duration, status, and contact information. It helps track and manage leave effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your current status among Army, Navy, Air Force, or other branches.
2. Enter your leave date, which should be a valid date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Specify how long you will be away.
4. Choose your military status: Active Duty, Reserve Duty, or Disabled Veteran.
5. Select your branch of military service (Army, Navy, Air Force, or Coast Guard).
6. (Optional) Enter your duty station and military unit.
7. Enter your military position.
8. Choose your leave type: Military Leave or Vacation.
9. Choose your leave status: Active or Inactive.
10. Provide additional information about your leave, if necessary.
11. Enter your contact details: email, phone, and submitter department (HR or Other).
12. Enter your position and any additional notes about your leave.
13. Enter the date when you will return from leave.
14. Enter the reason for your leave.
15. Finally, submit the form with your name, email, and ID to ensure accurate tracking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your current status?**: Select the branch you belong to (Army, Navy, Air Force, or other branches).
- **Leave Date**: Enter a valid date in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Leave Duration**: Specify how long you will be away in numerical values.
- **Military Status**: Choose your military status (Active Duty, Reserve Duty, or Disabled Veteran).
- **Military Service Branch**: Select your branch of military service (Army, Navy, Air Force, or Coast Guard).
- **Duty Station**: (Optional) Enter your duty station (e.g., base, location, or address).
- **Military Unit**: (Optional) Enter your military unit.
- **Military Position**: Enter your military position.
- **Leave Type**: Choose whether your leave is for Military Leave or Vacation.
- **Leave Status**: Choose whether your leave is Active or Inactive.
- **Additional Info**: Provide any additional details about your leave, if needed.
- **Submitter**: Enter your contact information (email).
- **Submitter ID**: Enter your ID number.
- **Submitter Email**: Enter your work email address.
- **Submitter Phone**: (Optional) Enter your phone number.
- **Submitter Dept**: Choose whether you belong to HR or Other department.
- **Submitter Position**: Enter your work position.
- **Submitter Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your leave.
- **Date**: Enter the date when you will return from leave.
- **Reason for Leave**: Explain why you are taking this leave.

Please fill in all required fields accurately and honestly.
