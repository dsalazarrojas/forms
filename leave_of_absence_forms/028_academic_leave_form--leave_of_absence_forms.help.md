# Academic Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from students about their leave of absence from academic activities. It is essential for proper leave processing and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your student name, student ID, and contact email.
2. Enter your program or major of study.
3. Specify the start and end dates of your leave, if known.
4. Choose the primary reason for your leave from the provided options.
5. Indicate if you have any supporting documents to attach or mention.
6. Confirm that the information provided is accurate by selecting "I agree" from the consent options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Personal Information --**
  - **Student full name** (`student_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the student's legal full name.
  - **Student identification number** (`student_id`, `text`, `required`): Enter the student's institutional ID or registration number.
  - **Contact email** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter the student's preferred email for updates.
* **Program or major** (`program_of_study`, `text`, `required`): Enter the student's department and program name.
* **Leave start date** (`leave_start_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the anticipated first day of leave.
* **Leave end date** (`leave_end_date`, `date`, `optional`): Enter the expected return date if known.
* **Primary reason for leave** (`leave_reason`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the primary reason for your leave from the options: Medical, Personal, Family, Academic, or Other.
* **Supporting documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, `optional`): List documents or indicate if attaching via portal.
* **Consent confirmation** (`consent_and_signature`, `select_one`, `required`): Confirm that the information provided is accurate by selecting "I agree".
