# Law Enforcement Stress Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Law Enforcement Stress Leave Of Absence Form is used for documenting and reporting employee leave of absence related to stress in the workplace. This form helps to gather information on employee's name, department, start and end dates of the leave, reason for the leave, and any supporting attachments that may be relevant to the case.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name as it appears on their official personnel record.
2. Select the department where the employee is currently working.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the leave, ensuring that the leave starts and ends on the selected dates.
4. Briefly describe the reason for the leave, being as clear and concise as possible.
5. If attachments are relevant, select yes and upload any supporting documents.
6. Select all relevant reviewers to ensure that they are aware of the leave.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee's Name**: The employee's full name as it appears on their official personnel record.
* **Department**: The department where the employee is currently working. Select "Yes" if the employee is from an affected department, or "No" otherwise.
* **Start Date of Leave**: The date when the employee's leave starts. Ensure that this date is correct and consistent with the employee's leave period.
* **End Date of Leave**: The date when the employee's leave ends. Ensure that this date is correct and consistent with the employee's leave period.
* **Reason for Leave**: A brief description of the reason for the leave. Be as clear and concise as possible.
* **Attachments**: If there are any supporting documents that may be relevant to the case, select "Yes" and upload the attachments.
* **Reviewers**: Select all relevant reviewers to ensure that they are aware of the leave. This includes HR representatives, team leaders, and other relevant personnel.
