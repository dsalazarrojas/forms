# Medical Leave Of Absence Request (Nurse) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a medical leave of absence as a nurse. It helps in maintaining accurate records and ensures that the necessary approvals and comments are collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
- Select the relevant employee from the provided options.
- Choose the start and end dates for the leave period.
- Specify the reason for requesting the leave.
- Choose the desired status for each required approval: Approve, Deny, or Pending.
- Provide comments for each approval, if necessary.
- Submit the form once you have gathered all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee**: Select the name of the relevant employee who is requesting the leave.
* **Start Date**: Choose the date when the leave will start. Please ensure that it is in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **End Date**: Choose the date when the leave will end. Please ensure that it is in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Reason For Leaving**: Provide a clear and concise reason for requesting the leave.
* **Physician Certification**: Please provide any necessary certification from the physician.
* **Nurse Manager Approval**: Choose the status of the approval: Approve, Deny, or Pending.
* **Manager Signature**: Provide the signature of the nurse manager.
* **Date Of Return**: Choose the date when the employee will return from the leave.
* **Comments**: If necessary, provide any comments for the nurse manager or other stakeholders.
* **Employee Approval**: Choose the status of the employee's approval: Approve, Deny, or Pending.
* **Supervisor Approval**: Choose the status of the supervisor's approval: Approve, Deny, or Pending.
* **Manager Comments**: Provide any comments from the manager.
* **Employee Comments**: Provide any comments from the employee.
* **Physician Comments**: Provide any comments from the physician.
* **Form Submitted**: Choose if the form has been submitted. Please note that this can only be checked once.
* **Form Approved**: Choose the status of the form approval: Approve, Deny, or Pending.
* **Form Denied**: Choose the status of the form denial: Approve, Deny, or Pending.
* **Form Returned**: Choose the status of the form return: Approve, Deny, or Pending.

Note: Please ensure that all fields with a status of Approve, Deny, or Pending are filled in correctly to avoid any delays or issues with the leave request.
