# Coursework Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Coursework Leave Of Absence Request" form is used by students to submit a formal request for a leave of absence from their coursework. This form will help us process your request and keep track of your absence.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields. If a field is optional, fill it in if you feel it is relevant to your request.
2. Ensure you select a valid reason for your leave of absence.
3. Enter a valid start and end date for your leave.
4. Provide a valid phone number and student email address.
5. Enter the name of the school you are attending.
6. Enter your student name.
7. Enter the name of the course for which you are requesting the leave.
8. Optionally, select who should approve your request.
9. Add any comments or notes you would like to include with your request.
10. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Coursework Leave Request** (text): Enter a brief explanation of your leave request.
* **Start Date** (date): Enter the date you would like to start your leave. This should be a valid date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **End Date** (date): Enter the date you would like to end your leave. This should be a valid date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Reason** (select_one): Select a valid reason for your leave from the provided options. Selecting "Other" will allow you to provide your own reason.
* **Email** (email): Enter your student email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (text): Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **School Name** (text): Enter the name of the school you are attending.
* **Student Name** (text): Enter your full name.
