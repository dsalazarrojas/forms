# Request For Approval And Verification Of Absence - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request and verify absences from work for employees. It collects necessary information from the employee and the approver to ensure that the absence is approved and verified correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of absence you are requesting (e.g. Leave of Absence, Sick Leave, etc.)
2. Enter the start and end dates of your absence
3. Provide a reason for your absence
4. Enter your approver's contact information (name, phone number, and email)
5. Enter your approver's note (optional)
6. Sign and date the form with your approver's signature
7. Select the "Submit" option to send the form for approval

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Absencetype** (Select One):
	+ Select the type of absence you are requesting (e.g. Leave of Absence, Sick Leave, etc.)
* **Start Date** (Date):
	+ Enter the date when your absence will start
* **End Date** (Date):
	+ Enter the date when your absence will end
* **Reason** (Text):
	+ Provide a brief explanation for your absence
* **Approver's Name** (Text):
	+ Enter the name of the person who will be approving your absence
* **Approver's Phone** (Text):
	+ Enter the phone number of the person who will be approving your absence
* **Approver's Email** (Email):
	+ Enter the email address of the person who will be approving your absence
* **Approver's Note** (Note):
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments from the approver (optional)
* **Approver's Signature** (Text):
	+ Sign and date the form with the approver's signature
* **Assigned Tool** (Text):
	+ Enter the name of the tool assigned to this form (e.g. chatjimmy)
* **Submit** (Select Multiple):
	+ Select the "Submit" option to send the form for approval, or cancel the form if you need to make changes or updates.
