# Writing Residency Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request and manage writing residencies, which can be on-campus (residents) or off-campus (non-residents). It ensures that necessary information is provided and reviewed to grant a leave of absence.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required".
2. Select the correct "residency_type" (Resident or Non-Resident).
3. Enter the "start_date" and "end_date" for the requested residency period.
4. Provide a valid reason for the leave in the "reason_for_leave" field.
5. If applicable, specify the "residency_length" in the requested period.
6. Select the approver(s) that will review and approve your request.
7. Optionally, add comments for the approver(s) in the "comments_for_approvers" field.
8. Enter the "submit_date" and provide your name as the "submitter".
9. Finally, review the "approval_status" and add any necessary "approval_note".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Writing Residency Request Form** (residency_request_form): A text field where you can enter your writing residency request.
* **Residency Type** (residency_type): Select whether you are requesting a resident (on-campus) or non-resident (off-campus) position. This determines where your residency will be located.
* **Start Date** (start_date): Enter the date you plan to begin your writing residency. This must be within the specified time frame.
* **End Date** (end_date): Enter the date you plan to end your writing residency. This must be within the specified time frame.
* **Reason for Leave** (reason_for_leave): Provide a valid reason for your leave of absence. This is essential for approvers to understand your request.
* **Residency Length** (residency_length): If applicable, specify the length of your residency period. This helps approvers understand the duration of your leave.
* **Approved By** (approved_by): Select the approver(s) that will review and approve your request. This ensures your request is reviewed by the right people.
* **Comments** (comments): Optionally, add comments for the approver(s) to provide additional context or information.
* **Submit Date** (submit_date): Enter the date you submit your request. This is a required field for tracking purposes.
* **Submitter** (submitter): Enter your name as the submitter of this request. This is a required field for accountability.
* **Approval Status** (approval_status): Review the status of your request. This will be updated as approvers review and approve your request.
* **Approval Note** (approval_note): If applicable, add any notes or comments from the approver(s) that require your attention.
* **Comments for Approvers** (comments_for_approvers): Optionally, add comments or notes for the approver(s) to help them understand your request.

By following these steps and understanding the purpose and requirements of each field, you will be able to complete the Writing Residency Leave Of Absence Form accurately and effectively.
