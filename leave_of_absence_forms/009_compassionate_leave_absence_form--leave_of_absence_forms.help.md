# Compassionate Leave Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Compassionate Leave Absence Form is used to request a leave of absence due to unexpected or extraordinary events, such as the death of a family member, critical illness of a family member, or other compassionate grounds. This form gathers information about the employee's situation, their manager's information, and the type of leave requested.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields. 
2. Provide accurate and truthful information about your situation.
3. Ensure that all fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as per your official records.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, `number`, required): Enter your official employee identification number.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current department from the dropdown options.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Manager or Supervisor Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, required): Enter your direct supervisor's email address.
* **Type of Compassionate Leave** (`leave_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the reason for your compassionate leave request from the dropdown options.
* **Relationship to Affected Person** (`relationship_to_affected`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you are related to the person affected.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you wish to start your leave.
* **Expected Return Date** (`leave_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **Total Days Requested** (`total_days_requested`, `number`, required): Enter the number of working days you are requesting for your leave.
* **Leave Duration Type** (`leave_duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of leave duration you are requesting.
* **Brief Description of Situation** (`situation_description`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about your situation if needed.
* **Can you Provide Documentation** (`documentation_available`, `select_one`, required): If you have any supporting documentation, choose the appropriate option.
* **Do you Need Urgent Contact during Leave** (`urgent_contact_needed`, `select_one`, required): If you will be reachable during your leave, choose the appropriate option.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who can contact you in emergency.
* **Work Handover Status** (`work_handover`, `select_one`, required): Choose the status of your work handover, and whether you need assistance.
* **Handover Notes** (`handover_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any necessary notes about the work handover.
* **Colleague Covering Your Duties** (`colleague_coverage`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the colleague covering your duties.
* **Out of Office Message Set** (`out_of_office`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you will set up an out-of-office auto-reply.
* **Do You Understand Benefits Impact** (`benefits_impact`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand how your leave will affect your benefits.
* **Pay During Leave** (`pay_during_leave`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you will be paid during your leave.
* **Do You Need Return to Work Support** (`return_to_work_support`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you need support when returning to work.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form with your full name.
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Select today's date.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Check with HR or your manager if you are unsure or need clarification on any fields.
* Provide accurate and truthful information to ensure your leave is processed correctly.
* If you have any supporting documentation, please attach it with your form.
