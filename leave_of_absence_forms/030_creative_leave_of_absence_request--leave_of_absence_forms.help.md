# Creative Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Creative Leave Of Absence Request" form is designed to collect essential information for employee leave requests, including leave type, start and end dates, reason for leave, leave duration, and approval from supervisors and managers. This form helps streamline the leave request process, reducing duplication of effort and making it easier for employees and HR staff to handle leave-related matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name and other required details in the respective fields.
2. Choose the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., Annual, Sick, Emergency, Parental).
3. Select the start and end dates of your leave.
4. Provide a reason for your leave.
5. Specify the duration of your leave.
6. Enter your expected return date.
7. Obtain approval from your supervisor and manager.
8. Confirm your acknowledgement of the leave request.
9. Confirm your manager's acknowledgement of the leave request.
10. Sign and date the form to confirm your commitment to the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1

* **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, required): This is where you enter your employee name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID or company records.
* **Leave Type** (leave_type, select_one, required): Select the type of leave you are requesting, such as Annual, Sick, Emergency, or Parental.
* **Start Date** (start_date, date, required): Enter the date you want your leave to start.
* **End Date** (end_date, date, required): Enter the date you want your leave to end.
* **Reason For Leave** (reason_for_leave, text, required): Provide a clear and concise reason for your leave request.
* **Leave Duration** (leave_duration, number, required): Specify the number of days or weeks you will be away from work.
* **Return Date** (return_date, date, required): Enter the date you expect to return to work.

### Page 2

* **Supervisor Approval** (supervisor_approval, select_one, required): Obtain approval from your supervisor. Please select either Approved, Denied, or Pending.
* **Manager Approval** (manager_approval, select_multiple, required): Obtain approval from your manager. Please select Approved, Denied, Pending, or No Response.
* **Employee Acknowledgement** (employee_acknowledgement, select_multiple, required): Confirm your acknowledgement of the leave request. Please select Acknowledged, Not Acknowledged, Declined, or No Response.
* **Manager Acknowledgement** (manager_acknowledgement, select_multiple, required): Confirm your manager's acknowledgement of the leave request. Please select Acknowledged, Not Acknowledged, Declined, or No Response.

### Page 3

* **Comments** (comments, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please enter them here. This field is optional.
* **Employee Signature** (employee_signature, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm your commitment to the request.
* **Manager Signature** (manager_signature, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm your manager's approval or denial.
* **Date Signed** (date_signed, date, required): Enter the date the form was signed by the employee.
* **Date Reviewed** (date_reviewed, date, required): Enter the date the form was reviewed by the manager.
* **Manager Comments** (manager_comments, text, optional): If the manager has any comments or concerns, please enter them here. This field is optional.
* **Employee Comments** (employee_comments, text, optional): If you have any comments or questions, please enter them here. This field is optional.
