# Evacuation Alert Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect critical information during emergency situations to ensure swift and effective emergency response. It is intended for those who have been affected by or are responsible for the emergency response.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2.  Complete all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3.  Enter accurate and detailed information in each field.
4.  Use the provided options for fields with options.
5.  Save the form when completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required): This field is for entering the contact details of the person or organization responsible for responding to the emergency.
    *   Enter the name and contact details of the person or organization responsible for emergency response.
*   **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, required): This field is for describing the incident in detail.
    *   Provide a clear and concise description of what happened.
*   **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `select_one`, required): This field is for choosing a location related to the incident.
    *   Select the correct location from the provided options.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering the name of the person responsible for the incident or the person to contact.
    *   Enter the name of the person responsible for the incident or the person to contact.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): This field is for entering the email address of the person responsible for responding to the emergency.
    *   Enter the email address of the person responsible for emergency response.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): This field is for entering the phone number of the person responsible for responding to the emergency.
    *   Enter the phone number of the person responsible for emergency response.
*   **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, required): This field is for selecting the time of the incident.
    *   Choose the correct time of the incident from the provided options.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering any emergency contact information that may be useful.
    *   Enter any additional emergency contact information that may be helpful.
*   **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is for selecting the status of the incident.
    *   Choose the status of the incident from the provided options.
*   **Incident Status Note** (`incident_status_note`, `note`, optional): This field is for entering any additional notes about the incident status.
    *   Enter any additional information about the incident status.
*   **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required): This field is for selecting the date of the incident.
    *   Choose the correct date of the incident.
*   **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, optional): This field is for selecting the time of the incident.
    *   Choose the correct time of the incident.
*   **Emergency Response** (`emergency_response`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering any information about the emergency response.
    *   Enter any information about the emergency response.
*   **Response Time** (`response_time`, `time`, optional): This field is for selecting the time of the response.
    *   Choose the correct time of the response.
*   **Response Status** (`response_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is for selecting the response status.
    *   Choose the status of the response from the provided options.
*   **Response Note** (`response_note`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering any additional notes about the response.
    *   Enter any additional information about the response.
