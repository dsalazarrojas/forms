# Hotel Guest Security Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Guest Security Incident Report Form is designed to collect information about security incidents that occurred during a guest's stay. This form helps hotel management document and manage incidents efficiently, ensuring the safety and security of guests.

## How To Complete This Form

### Complete Page 1: Guest Information

* **Guest Information**: Please enter your name, email address, and phone number as a guest of the hotel.
* **Incident Information**: Describe the incident briefly.
* **Location**: Provide the location where the incident occurred.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the incident.
* **Witness Information**: If there was a witness, provide their name and contact information (if available).

### Complete Page 2: Incident Summary and Details

* **Incident Summary**: Please provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Details**: Add any additional details about the incident.
* **Solutions**: If the incident has been resolved, describe what was done to address it.

### Complete Page 3: Incident Outcome and Additional Notes

* **Outcome**: Select one of the three options:
	+ Not Resolved
	+ Solved
	+ Closed
* **Incident Notes**: Add any additional notes about the incident.

### Complete Page 4: Submitter Information

* **Form Submitted**: Confirm that the form has been submitted.
* **Date**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Time**: Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **User**: Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Department**: Enter your department (if applicable).
* **Title**: Enter your title (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about submitting the form.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident.
* Use the provided fields to ensure that all necessary information is included.
* If unsure about any field, contact hotel management or your supervisor for assistance.
* Double-check your submission for accuracy before submitting the form.
