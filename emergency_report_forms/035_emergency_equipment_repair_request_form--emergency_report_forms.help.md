# Emergency Equipment Repair Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Equipment Repair Request Form is a tool for employees to report equipment malfunctions that require immediate attention from technical teams. This form helps technical teams quickly identify and address issues, ensuring minimal downtime and optimal productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with as much detail as possible, providing accurate information about the equipment and its current status.
2. Select the correct category and equipment type to help technical teams quickly identify the issue.
3. Choose the equipment status, condition, and location to aid in prioritization and scheduling.
4. Provide a brief description of the problem, highlighting any safety concerns or risks.
5. If possible, select a recommended repair date or actual repair date to help technical teams schedule maintenance.
6. If necessary, enter the employee responsible for requesting the repair.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Description** (`emergency_equipment_repair_request_form_equipment_description`): Describe the equipment that is malfunctioning, including its model, serial number, or any other relevant details.
* **Category** (`emergency_equipment_repair_request_form_category`): Select the category that best matches the type of equipment or issue reported. This will help technical teams quickly identify the issue.
* **Equipment Type** (`emergency_equipment_repair_request_form_equipment_type`): Choose the type of equipment that best matches the reported issue. This will aid in prioritization and scheduling.
* **Equipment Status** (`emergency_equipment_repair_request_form_equipment_status`): Select the current status of the equipment, from "Working" to "Non-Working" or other options.
* **Equipment Condition** (`emergency_equipment_repair_request_form_equipment_condition`): Indicate the level of severity of the issue, from "Critical" to "Minor" or other options.
* **Equipment Location** (`emergency_equipment_repair_request_form_equipment_location`): Select the room or area where the equipment is located.
* **Equipment Priority** (`emergency_equipment_repair_request_form_equipment_priority`): Choose the priority level of the issue, from "High" to "Medium" or "Low".
* **Repair Description** (`emergency_equipment_repair_request_form_repair_description`): Provide a detailed description of the problem, including any safety concerns or risks.
* **Recommended Repair Date** (`emergency_equipment_repair_request_form_recommended_repair_date`): If possible, select a date for when the repair is expected to be completed.
* **Actual Repair Date** (`emergency_equipment_repair_request_form_actual_repair_date`): If known, enter the date when the repair was actually completed.
* **Requesting Employee** (`emergency_equipment_repair_request_form_requesting_employee`): If necessary, enter the name of the employee requesting the repair.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that technical teams can effectively address the issue.
- If you're unsure or have a non-emergency repair request, do not submit this form.
- If the equipment is already in the repair queue, do not submit a duplicate form.
- Keep the form for reference and follow-up on the status of your equipment with the technical team.
