# fire_rescue_aerosolized_procedure_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fire_rescue_aerosolized_procedure_report_form is used to document emergency rescue procedures involving aerosolized procedures. This form captures essential information about the patient, the crew, and the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Enter the patient's age in the **Patient Age** field.
3. If necessary, enter the patient's weight in the **Prophets Weight** field.
4. Select the crew's name from the **Crew Name** dropdown menu.
5. Select the crew member's role from the **Crew Type** dropdown menu.
6. Select the procedure type from the **Procedure Type** dropdown menu.
7. Enter the date and time of the incident in the **Date** and **Time** fields.
8. Enter the location of the incident in the **Location** field.
9. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the **Incident Description** field.
10. Enter any comments from the medical crew in the **Medic Comments** field.
11. Enter any comments from the chief or supervisor in the **Chief Comments** field (if required).
12. Enter the email address of the recipient in the **Email To** field (if required).
13. Enter the phone number of the recipient in the **Phone To** field (if required).
14. Add any additional comments or notes in the **Note** field (if required).
15. Add any additional comments or notes in the **Fire Rescue Aerosolized Procedure Report Form Part II** field (if required).
16. Enter the signature of the medical crew member 1 in the **Medic 1 Signature** field (if required).
17. Enter the signature of the medical crew member 2 in the **Medic 2 Signature** field (if required).
18. Enter the signature of the chief or supervisor in the **Chief Signature** field (if required).
