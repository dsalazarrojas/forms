# Infectious Disease Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Infectious Disease Report Form" is a tool used to collect information about patients suspected or diagnosed with infectious diseases. It is designed to help healthcare providers and reporting facilities report cases of infectious diseases in a timely and accurate manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the patient or individual suspected or diagnosed with an infectious disease.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, paying attention to the required and optional fields.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required: false)
Report the date you are reporting the case, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### 2. **Disease Symptoms** (`disease_symptoms`, text, required: false)
Describe the symptoms the patient is experiencing or has experienced, such as fever, rash, or respiratory distress.

### 3. **Exposure Date** (`exposure_date`, date, required: false)
Report the date the patient was exposed to the infectious disease, if known.

### 4. **Disease Type** (`disease_type`, select_one, required: false)
Select the type of infectious disease the patient has been diagnosed or suspected with.

### 5. **Reporting Facility** (`reporting_facility`, select_one, required: false)
Indicate whether the case is being reported from a healthcare facility or a non-healthcare facility.

### 6. **Disease Severity** (`disease_severity`, select_one, required: false)
Select the severity of the disease, if known.

### 7. **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, number, required: false)
Enter the patient's identification number, if available.

### 8. **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, required: false)
Name and title of the healthcare provider who reported the case, if applicable.

### 9. **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select how the case was reported, such as by phone, email, or in-person.

### 10. **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_one, required: false)
Indicate whether you are the healthcare provider or a representative of the facility.

### 11. **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false)
Report the date you are submitting the form, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult the relevant guidelines and protocols.
- If you need help, contact your local health authority or a medical expert.
