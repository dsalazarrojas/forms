# Power Outage Survival Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power Outage Survival Guide form is designed to help households prepare for and respond to power outages. This form assesses and records the household's readiness and preparedness for an unexpected power outage.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and provide accurate information about your household's preparedness.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and completely.
3. Use the space provided to elaborate on any additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Household Preparedness** (`household_preparedness`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of how your household prepares for power outages.
- **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, select_multiple, optional): Check the box for family members or contacts who have medical conditions that require special attention during a power outage. 
  * 'Yes' indicates you have a family member or contact with a medical condition requiring special attention.
  * 'No' indicates you do not have a family member or contact with a medical condition requiring special attention.
- **Food Preparedness** (`food_preparedness`, text, optional): Describe how you and your household plan to prepare for food storage and conservation during a power outage.
- **Medical Preparedness** (`medical_preparedness`, select_multiple, optional): Check the box for any medical equipment or supplies that you have in your household. 
  * 'Yes' indicates you have the specified medical equipment or supplies.
  * 'No' indicates you do not have the specified medical equipment or supplies.
- **Safety Precautions** (`safety_precautions`, text, optional): Write about any special safety precautions you and your household take to prevent accidents and injuries during an outage.
- **Water Preparedness** (`water_preparedness`, number, optional): Indicate how much water (in gallons or liters) your household has prepared or is prepared for.
- **First Aid Kits** (`first_aid_kits`, select_one, optional): Check which type of first aid kit you have in your household. 
  * 'Yes' indicates you have a first aid kit.
  * 'No' indicates you do not have a first aid kit.
- **Power Outage Plan** (`power_outage_plan`, note, optional): Describe your household's power outage plan, including any specific steps or procedures you follow during an outage.
- **Emergency Services** (`emergency_services`, select_multiple, optional): Check which emergency services your household has, such as a fire alarm or sprinkler system.
  * 'Yes' indicates you have the emergency service.
  * 'No' indicates you do not have the emergency service.
- **Household Assets** (`household_assets`, select_one, optional): Check which assets are in your household, such as generators or backup power systems. 
  * 'Yes' indicates you have the asset.
  * 'No' indicates you do not have the asset.
- **Power Outage Precedence** (`power_outage_precedence`, number, optional): Indicate the number of hours or days you have prepared supplies or equipment for.
- **First Aid Kits Location** (`first_aid_kits_location`, text, optional): Specify where you have first aid kits located in your household.
- **Power Outage Precedence 2** (`power_outage_precedence_2`, number, optional): Indicate the number of hours or days you have prepared supplies or equipment for.
- **Household Medical Conditions** (`household_medical_conditions`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions that you or your household members have that may affect your ability to prepare for or respond to a power outage.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide a name and contact information for a family member or contact who can assist you or your household during an outage.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, select_multiple, optional): Check the box if you have contact information for emergency services or authorities.
  * 'Yes' indicates you have contact information.
  * 'No' indicates you do not have contact information.
- **Household Preparedness 2** (`household_preparedness_2`, text, optional): Describe your household's preparedness plan for a second power outage event.
- **Household Preparedness 3** (`household_preparedness_3`, text, optional): Describe your household's preparedness plan for a third power outage event.
- **Household Preparedness 4** (`household_preparedness_4`, text, optional): Describe your household's preparedness plan for a fourth power outage event.
- **Household Preparedness 5** (`household_preparedness_5`, text, optional): Describe your household's preparedness plan for a fifth power outage event.
- **Household Preparedness 6** (`household_preparedness_6`, text, optional): Describe your household's preparedness plan for a sixth power outage event.
- **Household Preparedness 7** (`household_preparedness_7`, text, optional): Describe your household's preparedness plan for a seventh power outage event.
- **Household Preparedness 8** (`household_preparedness_8`, text, optional): Describe your household's preparedness plan for an eighth power outage event.
- **Household Preparedness 9** (`household_preparedness_9`, text, optional): Describe your household's preparedness plan for a ninth power outage event.
- **Household Preparedness 10** (`household_preparedness_10`, text, optional): Describe your household's preparedness plan for a tenth power outage event.

## Tips
1. Review each section carefully and truthfully to ensure accurate representation of your household's preparedness.
2. Be as detailed as possible, especially in sections like 'Household Preparedness' and 'Power Outage Plan'.
