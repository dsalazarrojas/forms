# Munitions Discovery Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Munitions Discovery Report is used to document the discovery of potentially explosive or hazardous materials in the field. This report helps ensure accurate and efficient recording of incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Enter the location** of the discovery in the "location" field. This can be a physical address or a GPS coordinate.
2.  **Select the time** of the discovery using the available date and time fields.
3.  **Describe the discovery** in the "description" field. Provide as much detail as possible, including any notable features or characteristics of the discovered item.
4.  **Indicate whether photos were taken** during the discovery process. Select "Yes" if photos were taken, or "No" if not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **location**: Enter the location where the item was discovered.
* **time**: Select the time of day and date of the discovery.
* **description**: Provide a detailed description of what was discovered, including any notable features or characteristics.
* **photos**: Indicate whether photos were taken during the discovery process.
