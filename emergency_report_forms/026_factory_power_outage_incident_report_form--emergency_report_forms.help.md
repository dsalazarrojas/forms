# Factory Power Outage Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and track factory power outage incidents. It provides information on the cause, duration, affected areas, and follow-up status of the incident.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Report Details**: Enter a brief description of the incident.
2. Fill in all relevant fields, especially those marked as required.
3. Provide accurate dates and times where possible.
4. Select relevant options for affected areas and maintenance status.
5. Enter any follow-up notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Details** (factory_power_outage_incident, text, required): A brief description of the power outage incident.
* **What was the cause of the power outage?** (power_outage_cause, text, optional): Enter the reason for the power outage, if known.
* **How long did the power outage last?** (power_outage_duration, number, optional): Enter the duration of the power outage in minutes or hours.
* **Start Date** (power_outage_start_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the power outage start time.
* **Start Time** (power_outage_start_time, time, optional): Enter the start time of the power outage.
* **End Date** (power_outage_end_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the power outage end time.
* **End Time** (power_outage_end_time, time, optional): Enter the end time of the power outage.
* **Affected Areas** (affected_areas, select_multiple, optional): Select areas affected by the power outage.
* **Number of Employees Affected** (power_outage_employees_affected, number, optional): Enter the number of employees affected.
* **Outage Maintenance Status** (power_outage_maintenance_status, select_one, optional): Select the status of the maintenance.
* **Follow up Status** (power_outage_follow_up_status, select_one, optional): Select the follow-up status.
* **Follow up Date** (power_outage_follow_up_date, date, optional): Enter the follow-up date.
* **Follow up Time** (power_outage_follow_up_time, time, optional): Enter the follow-up time.
* **Follow up Person** (power_outage_follow_up_person, text, optional): Enter the person responsible for follow-up.
* **Follow up Note** (power_outage_follow_up_note, note, optional): Enter any follow-up notes.
* **Reporting Person** (power_outage_reporting_person, text, optional): Enter the person who reported the incident.
* **Reported By** (power_outage_reported_by, select_one, optional): Select who reported the incident.
* **Comments** (comments, select_multiple, optional): Select if comments were made.
* **Reporting Team** (power_outage_reporting_team, select_one, optional): Select if the incident was reported by the team.
* **Reporting Date** (power_outage_reporting_date, date, optional): Enter the reporting date.
* **Reporting Time** (power_outage_reporting_time, time, optional): Enter the reporting time.
* **Last Update** (power_outage_last_update, date, optional): Enter the last update date.
* **Last Update By** (power_outage_last_update_by, text, optional): Enter the person who last updated the incident.
* **Last Update Note** (power_outage_last_update_note, note, optional): Enter any last update notes.
* **Follow up Note** (power_outage_follow_up_note, note, optional): Enter any follow-up notes.

## Tips

* Be accurate and up-to-date with the information provided.
* Fill in all required fields.
* Select relevant options for affected areas and maintenance status.
* Enter follow-up notes and comments as necessary.
* This form is not meant for general reporting, but for specific outages that directly impact factory operations.
