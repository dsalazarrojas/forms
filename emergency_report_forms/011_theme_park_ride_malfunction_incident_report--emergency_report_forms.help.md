# Theme Park Ride Malfunction Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theme Park Ride Malfunction Incident Report form is a tool used for reporting and documenting malfunctions that occur within theme park rides. This form helps ensure that incidents are reported and addressed promptly, contributing to guest safety and ride operational efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category for the malfunction from the provided options.
2. Provide a detailed description of the malfunction, including the circumstances leading up to the incident.
3. Enter the date and time the malfunction occurred, using the format "YYYY-MM-DD" for dates and "HH:MM:SS" for times.
4. Choose the individual or team assigned to address the issue.
5. (Optional) If desired, enter any follow-up details regarding the actions taken or progress made.

* **Description (Description)**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the malfunction.

* **Time (Time)**: Enter the time the malfunction occurred using the format "HH:MM:SS".
* **Assigned To (Assigned To)**: Choose the individual or team responsible for addressing the issue.
* **Follow-up Details (Follow-up Details)**: (Optional) Enter any additional details regarding the actions taken or progress made after the initial report.
