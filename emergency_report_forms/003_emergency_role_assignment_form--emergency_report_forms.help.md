# Emergency Role Assignment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who will be in a position of emergency contact. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details about the person, including their name, phone number, and contact information, so that they can be reached and informed during a crisis or emergency situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "contact_name" field.
2. Provide your phone number in the "emergency_phone" field.
3. Enter your address in the "emergency_address" field.
4. Describe your relationship with the individual in the "contact_relationship" field.
5. Enter your job title in the "contact_job_title" field.
6. Enter the department you are in in the "contact_department" field.
7. Enter the location of your department in the "contact_location" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "contact_email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **contact_name** (Text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official ID or employee directory.
* **emergency_phone** (Text, required): Enter your current phone number where you can be reached during an emergency.
* **emergency_address** (Text, required): Enter your current physical address where you can be reached during an emergency.
* **contact_relationship** (Text, required): Describe your relationship with the individual in need of emergency contact information (e.g., supervisor, team lead, colleague).
* **contact_job_title** (Text, required): Enter your job title or role within your department.
* **contact_department** (Text, required): Enter the name of your department or team.
* **contact_location** (Text, required): Enter the location of your department or team (e.g., floor number, building name, etc.).
* **contact_email** (Email, required): Enter your work email address where you can be reached during an emergency.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure you can be reached and contacted in case of an emergency.
* If you have any changes to your contact information, please update this form immediately.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
