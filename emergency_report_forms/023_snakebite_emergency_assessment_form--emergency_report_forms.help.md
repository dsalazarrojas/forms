# Snakebite Emergency Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snakebite Emergency Assessment Form is designed to collect information related to a snakebite incident, including patient information, incident details, and medical treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form for **each new, unrelated incident**. This will ensure accurate and consistent reporting. 
2.  Begin with **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's personal details, including name, contact information, and any relevant medical history.
3.  Proceed to **Incident Information**: Provide a description of the incident, including location and time.
4.  Move on to **Medical History**: Document the patient's pre-existing medical conditions.
5.  Continue with **Physical Condition**: Describe the patient's current physical condition.
6.  Next, enter **Wound Description**: Describe the wound caused by the snakebite.
7.  Enter **Snakebite Location**: Specify the location of the snakebite on the body.
8.  Provide **Symptoms**: Describe the patient's symptoms after the bite.
9.  Enter **Treatment**: Report any medical treatment received by the patient.
10. Select the **Medical Team**: Choose the type of medical professionals involved in the patient's care.
11. Enter **Patient Call**: Record the time of the patient's initial call to emergency services.
12. Report **Time to Treatment**: Indicate the time from the snakebite to treatment.
13. Record **Date of Injury**: Enter the date of the snakebite.
14. Select **Snake Species**: Identify the type of snake involved.
15. Enter **Snake Color**: Describe the color of the snake.
16. Choose **Wound Depth**: Select the depth of the wound.
17. Enter **Wound Width**: Measure the width of the wound.
18. Select **Patient Status**: Choose the current status of the patient.
19. Enter **Vital Signs**: Record the patient's vital signs, such as pulse rate and blood pressure.
20. Enter **Wound Care**: Describe the treatment and care given to the wound.
21. Enter **Medical History Additional**: Document any additional medical conditions the patient may have.
22. Enter **Patient Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the patient's condition.
23. Enter **Medication Administered**: Document any medication given to the patient.
24. Schedule **Follow Up**: Record the schedule for follow-up care.
25. Finally, enter **Form Completed By**: Record the name of the person completing the form.
