# Community Care Unusual Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report and document unusual incidents that occur within a care setting, such as a nursing home or assisted living facility.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, please have all necessary information ready, including the date and time of the incident, the location, and the names and contact information of individuals involved. If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Incident**: The date on which the incident occurred. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Incident**: The approximate time the incident occurred. Please enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Location of Incident**: The specific area or room where the incident occurred.
* **Reporter Name**: The full name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Reporter Role**: The position or relationship of the person reporting the incident. Please select one of the following options: Staff Member, Supervisor, Volunteer, Family Member, Resident, or Other.
* **Reporter Contact Information**: The phone number or email address of the person reporting the incident.
* **Type of Incident**: The primary category of the incident. Please select one of the following options: Fall, Medication Error, Injury, Behavioral Incident, Property Damage, Missing Person, Medical Emergency, or Other.
* **Persons Involved**: The names of all individuals involved in the incident.
* **Detailed Description of Incident**: A complete account of what happened.
* **Witnesses**: The names and contact information of any witnesses.
* **Injuries Sustained**: Describe any injuries that resulted from the incident.
* **Immediate Actions Taken**: What was done immediately after the incident?
* **Medical Attention Required**: Was medical attention sought or provided? Please select one of the following options: Yes - Emergency Services Called, Yes - First Aid Provided, Yes - Medical Professional Consulted, No Medical Attention Needed, or Refused Medical Attention.
* **Notifications Made**: Who was notified about this incident? Please select all that apply: Supervisor, Family Member, Physician, Emergency Services, Regulatory Agency, or Other.
* **Contributing Factors**: What factors may have contributed to this incident? (Optional)
* **Recommended Preventive Measures**: Suggestions for preventing similar incidents in the future. (Optional)
* **Follow-up Required**: Is additional follow-up needed? Yes or No.
* **Follow-up Details**: Describe the follow-up actions needed. (Optional)

Note to the reader: Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form. If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask for assistance.
