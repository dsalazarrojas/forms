# Healthcare Facility Emergency Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of an incident that has occurred in a healthcare facility. It helps the emergency team to assess the situation and provide necessary assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Use the options provided to choose the correct response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Conditions** (`incident_conditions`): Describe the conditions of the incident, including any relevant details about the patient's situation.
* **Urgent Needs** (`urgent_needs`): Check all the urgent needs that apply, such as first aid, medical attention, or other necessary assistance.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`): Choose 'Yes' if the incident is related to a medical condition, such as an injury or an allergic reaction.
* **Medical Condition Urgency** (`medical_condition_urgency`): Choose 'Yes' if the medical condition requires immediate attention.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`): Choose the type of incident that occurred, such as a fall, a spill, or an electrical issue.
* **Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Emergency Team** (`emergency_team`): Check all the emergency teams that need to be notified.
* **Emergency Team Leader** (`emergency_team_leader`): Enter the name and contact information of the emergency team leader.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`): Enter the age of the patient.
* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`): Choose the gender of the patient.
* **Location** (`location`): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Nature** (`incident_nature`): Choose the nature of the incident, such as a physical issue or an equipment malfunction.
