# Kite Damage Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kite Damage Report Form is a tool used to document and report any damage to a kite. This form helps authorized personnel quickly and accurately gather the necessary information to address the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and equipment ID, as these are required fields.
2. Select the type of damage (minor or major) that has occurred to the kite.
3. Describe the damage in as much detail as possible.
4. Select the location where the damage occurred.
5. Enter the date when the damage occurred.
6. If you wish, provide your email and phone number for follow-up communication.
7. Finally, click "Submit" to send the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name so that we can quickly identify you as the reporter of this issue.
* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`, number, required): Enter the unique ID of the kite that has been damaged.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide as much detail as possible about the damage to help us better understand the issue.
* **Damage Type** (`damage_type`, select_multiple, required): Select whether the damage is minor or major.
* **Damage Location** (`damage_location`, text, optional): Specify where on the kite the damage occurred.
* **Date** (`damage_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the damage occurred.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about the repair, enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about the repair, enter your phone number.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the type of kite that was damaged (Kite, Line, or Other).
