# Medical Emergency Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report a medical emergency incident, allowing users to provide critical information about the incident, including patient details, contact information, and relevant context such as date, time, location, and medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
### To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Gather all the necessary information about the medical emergency incident before starting.
*   Select the correct page (Patient Information).
*   Enter the patient's name in the corresponding field.
*   Enter the contact person's name in the corresponding field.
*   Enter the contact person's phone number in the field.
*   Enter the contact person's email address in the corresponding field.
*   Select the date of the incident from the calendar.
*   Select the time of the incident from the time picker.
*   Enter the location of the incident in the text field.
*   Select one or more goals related to the incident.
*   Enter a brief narrative about the incident in the text field.
*   Select the medical condition related to the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient who experienced the medical emergency.
*   **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you are contacting to report the incident.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person you are contacting.
*   **Contact E-mail**: Enter the email address of the person you are contacting.
*   **Date**: Select the date of the incident from the calendar.
*   **Time**: Select the time of the incident from the time picker.
*   **Location**: Enter a brief description of the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Goal**: Select one or more goals related to the incident, such as providing medical treatment or preventing future incidents.
*   **Narrative**: Enter a brief description of what happened during the incident.
*   **Medical Condition**: Select the condition that led to the medical emergency, such as a heart attack or allergic reaction.

## Tips
*   Make sure to enter all the required fields to ensure a complete report.
*   Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, refer to the provided instructions or seek guidance from a supervisor or colleague.
