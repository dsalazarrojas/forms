# emergency_response_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency_response_report_form is a document used to gather information about incidents that require immediate attention from emergency responders. This form helps to ensure that accurate and timely responses can be made to these events. By filling out this form, you can report incidents to our emergency response team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
2. Enter the time the incident occurred in the "Incident Time" field.
3. Add any relevant contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
4. Enter the name of the officer reporting the incident in the "Reporting Officer Name" field.
5. Enter the phone number of the reporting officer in the "Reporting Officer Phone" field.
6. Enter the email address of the reporting officer in the "Reporting Officer Email" field.
7. Enter the location of the reporting officer in the "Reporting Officer Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the incident, including as much detail as possible.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required): Enter the time the incident occurred in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Add any contact information that may be helpful for follow-up communication.
* **Reporting Officer Name** (`reporting_officer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the officer reporting the incident.
* **Reporting Officer Phone** (`reporting_officer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the reporting officer.
* **Reporting Officer Email** (`reporting_officer_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the reporting officer.
* **Reporting Officer Location** (`reporting_officer_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the reporting officer.
