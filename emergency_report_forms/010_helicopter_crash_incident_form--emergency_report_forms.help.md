# Helicopter Crash Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about helicopter crash incidents to ensure accurate and consistent reporting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the general information section, providing a brief overview of the incident.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident using the date and time fields.
3. Specify the location where the incident occurred in the location field.
4. Provide details about the number and types of helicopters involved in the crash in the helicopters involved field.
5. Select the type of incident that occurred from the incident type field.
6. Enter a detailed description of the incident in the incident description field.
7. Provide any witness statements or additional information related to the incident in the witness statement field.
8. Enter any relevant pilot information, such as the pilot's name, experience, and qualifications, in the pilot info field.
9. Enter any relevant aircraft information, such as the aircraft type, make, and model, in the aircraft info field.
10. Specify the injury status of those involved in the crash in the injury status field.
11. Enter the cause of the crash in the crash cause field.
12. Enter the current investigation status in the investigation status field.
13. Specify the location where the incident occurred in the incident location field.
14. Enter any relevant weather conditions at the time of the incident in the weather conditions field.
15. Enter any relevant flight plan information, such as the flight route and departure and arrival times, in the flight plan field.
16. Enter any information related to the flight recorder in the flight recorder field.
17. Enter any details about the crash site, such as the terrain and condition of the area, in the crash site details field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, optional): Briefly describe the incident.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Helicopters Involved** (`helicopters_involved`, text, optional): Provide details about the number and types of helicopters involved.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of incident that occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, note, optional): Enter a detailed description of the incident.
* **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, text, optional): Provide any witness statements or additional information related to the incident.
* **Pilot Information** (`pilot_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant pilot information.
* **Aircraft Information** (`aircraft_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant aircraft information.
* **Injury Status** (`injury_status`, select_multiple, optional): Specify the injury status of those involved.
* **Crash Cause** (`crash_cause`, text, optional): Enter the cause of the crash.
* **Investigation Status** (`investigation_status`, text, optional): Enter the current investigation status.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Weather Conditions** (`weather_conditions`, text, optional): Enter any relevant weather conditions at the time of the incident.
* **Flight Plan** (`flight_plan`, text, optional): Enter any relevant flight plan information.
* **Flight Recorder** (`flight_recorder`, text, optional): Enter any information related to the flight recorder.
* **Crash Site Details** (`crash_site_details`, text, optional): Enter any details about the crash site.
