# Disaster Recovery Readiness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disaster Recovery Readiness Assessment Form is designed to evaluate your organization's readiness for disaster recovery. This form will help you assess the current state of your disaster recovery plan and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
3. Specify the person to be contacted in case of an emergency in the "Emergency Contact Person" field.
4. Provide the contact number for the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Number" field.
5. Enter the location of the incident in the "Incident Location" field.
6. Specify the date of the incident in the "Incident Date" field.
7. Specify the time of the incident in the "Incident Time" field.
8. Enter the expected time for recovery in the "Recovery Time" field.
9. Describe your recovery plan in the "Recovery Plan" field.
10. Enter the number of backup data in the "Backup Data" field.
11. Specify if you have recovery equipment available ("Yes" or "No") in the "Recovery Equipment" field.
12. Select the types of backup equipment you have available ("Generators", "Backup Power Systems", or "Air Conditioning Systems") in the "Backup Equipment" field.
13. Enter any other provisions or equipment you have available in the "Other Provisions" field.
14. If you have backup provisions available, specify the status ("Yes" or "No") in the "Backup Provisions" field.
15. Select the types of backup provisions you have available ("Backup Power Systems", "Backup Generators", etc.) in the "Backup Provisions Type" field.
16. Specify the status of backup provisions available ("Active" or "Inactive") in the "Backup Provisions Status" field.
17. If you have backup provisions with a status of "Active", specify the status in one of the "Backup Provisions Status 2", "Backup Provisions Status 3", "Backup Provisions Status 4", "Backup Provisions Status 5", "Backup Provisions Status 6", "Backup Provisions Status 7", "Backup Provisions Status 8" fields.
18. Specify the status of your recovery plan ("Active" or "Inactive") in the "Recovery Plan Status" field.
