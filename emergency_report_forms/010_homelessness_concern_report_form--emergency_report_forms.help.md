# Homelessness Concern Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Homelessness Concern Report Form is a tool for reporting homelessness concerns in your area. This form helps us gather information about situations that might be affecting individuals or groups, and allows us to provide support and resources to those in need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Client Details** section with any relevant information about the individual or group in need.
2. In the **Address** section, provide the exact address where the homelessness concern is located.
3. Enter the **Phone** number and **Email** (if available) of the individual or group in need.
4. Select the date of the homelessness concern in the **Date** field.
5. Describe the **Report Details** in the space provided, providing as much context as possible.
6. If an emergency contact is involved, fill out the **Emergency Contact** field with their name, phone number, and relationship.
7. If a witness is involved, answer the following questions:
	* If a witness is present, select "True" or "False" in the **Witness Present** field.
	* If the witness is present, enter their contact information in the **Witness Contact** field.
	* Select the relationship between the witness and the individual or group in need in the **Witness Contact Relationship** field.
8. Enter any additional notes about the witness in the **Witness Notes** field.
9. Confirm if the witness is present or not by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the **Witness Present 2** and **Witness Present 3** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details**: Enter any relevant information about the individual or group in need, such as name, age, and any other identifying characteristics.
* **Address**: Enter the exact address where the homelessness concern is located.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the individual or group in need.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the individual or group in need (if available).
* **Date**: Select the date of the homelessness concern.
* **Report Details**: Describe the situation in detail, providing as much context as possible.
* **Emergency Contact**: If an emergency contact is involved, enter their name, phone number, and relationship.
* **Witness Present**: If a witness is present, select "True" or "False".
* **Witness Contact**: If the witness is present, enter their contact information.
* **Witness Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship between the witness and the individual or group in need.
* **Witness Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the witness.
* **Witness Present 2** and **Witness Present 3**: Confirm if the witness is present or not by selecting "Yes" or "No".
