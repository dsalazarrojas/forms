# Disaster Recovery Medical Coordination Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disaster Recovery Medical Coordination Registration form is designed to collect information from medical volunteers during disaster recovery efforts. It ensures that volunteers are properly registered and coordinated for optimal support and safety.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have a legitimate reason to fill out this form and are not duplicating efforts already underway.
2.  Provide accurate and truthful information about yourself to ensure proper coordination and support.
3.  Complete the form in its entirety, paying attention to all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Volunteer Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Please provide your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
*   **Volunteer role** (`2`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your role or position in the medical field, e.g., doctor, nurse, paramedic, etc.
*   **Medical Facility Name** (`3`, `text`, optional): If you are affiliated with a medical facility, please provide their name.
*   **Contact Number** (`4`, `text`, required): Provide your contact number where you can be reached for coordination.
*   **Medical Volunteer Type** (`5`, `text`, required): Indicate your medical volunteer type (e.g., medical provider, support staff, etc.).
*   **Shift Start Time** (`6`, `time`, optional): If you are scheduled for a specific shift, please provide the start time.
*   **Shift End Time** (`7`, `time`, required): Indicate the end time of your scheduled shift.
*   **Medical Certification Level** (`8`, `text`, required): Specify your medical certification level, e.g., basic life support, advanced life support, etc.
*   **Medical Specialty** (`9`, `text`, optional): If applicable, specify your medical specialty, e.g., surgery, pediatrics, etc.
*   **Shift Location** (`10`, `text`, required): Provide the location of your scheduled shift, e.g., hospital, disaster zone, etc.
*   **Medical License Number** (`11`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide your medical license number.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`12`, `text`, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name.
*   **Emergency Contact Number** (`13`, `text`, optional): If you have an emergency contact, provide their number.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`14`, `select_one`, optional): If you have an emergency contact, indicate their relationship to you.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship Other** (`15`, `text`, optional): If the relationship is not listed, describe it in this field.
*   **Medical Supervision** (`16`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you require medical supervision, indicate the type(s).
*   **Medical Supervision Other** (`17`, `text`, optional): If your medical supervision is not listed, describe it in this field.
*   **Notes** (`18`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments regarding your medical volunteer status.
*   **Date Of Birth** (`19`, `date`, required): Please provide your date of birth as it appears on your official identification documents.
*   **Sex** (`20`, `text`, required): Indicate your sex (Male, Female).
*   **Age** (`21`, `number`, required): Provide your age.
*   **Nationality** (`22`, `text`, required): Indicate your nationality.
*   **Race** (`23`, `text`, required): Indicate your race.
*   **Emergency Declaration** (`24`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you are willing to make an emergency declaration.
*   **Comments** (`25`, `note`, optional): Provide any comments or additional information regarding your medical volunteer status.
