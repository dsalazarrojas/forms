# Disaster Recovery Plan Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disaster Recovery Plan Authorization Form is a document used to confirm the authorization of a contact person to take action and represent the organization in the event of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Organization Name to identify the organization affected by the disaster.
2. Provide the Contact Person's Name, Title, Email, Phone Number, and Department to ensure accurate contact information.
3. Indicate whether there is an emergency contact person by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the Emergency Contact field.
4. If there is an emergency contact person, enter their extension and level of emergency contact (High or Low).
5. Provide any additional information about the emergency contact person in the Emergency Contact Information field.
6. Confirm whether the contact person is authorized to act on behalf of the organization by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the Authorized to Act field.
7. Enter the Email Subject and Email Body for the disaster recovery plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, required text): Enter the name of the organization affected by the disaster.
* **Contact Person's Name** (`contact_person_name`, required text): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Person's Title** (`contact_person_title`, required text): Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Contact Person's Email** (`contact_email`, required text): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Person's Phone** (`contact_phone`, required text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Department** (`contact_department`, required text): Enter the department or role of the contact person.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, required select_one): Check if there is an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Extension** (`emergency_contact_extension`, false text): Enter the extension of the emergency contact person (if applicable).
* **Contact Level** (`emergency_contact_level`, false select_one): Indicate the level (High or Low) of the emergency contact person (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_info`, false text): Enter any additional information about the emergency contact person (if applicable).
* **Authorized to Act** (`authorized_to_act`, required select_one): Confirm whether the contact person is authorized to act on behalf of the organization.
* **Email Subject** (`email_subject`, required text): Enter the email subject for the disaster recovery plan.
* **Email Body** (`email_body`, required text): Enter the email body for the disaster recovery plan.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the emergency contact information and authorization to act fields for correct responses.
* The disaster recovery plan will be shared with stakeholders, so ensure the email subject and body are clear and concise.
