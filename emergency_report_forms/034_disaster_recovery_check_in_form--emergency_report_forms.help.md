# Disaster Recovery Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about individuals or families affected by a disaster, enabling emergency responders and recovery personnel to provide aid and resources effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the individual or family member.
2. Provide their address.
3. Enter the emergency contact number.
4. Enter the date of birth (in MM/DD/YYYY format).
5. Select the relationship to the individual or family member (e.g., Parent, Spouse, Sibling, Child, or Other).
6. Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
7. Enter the emergency contact number 2 (if applicable).
8. Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, SMS, or Do Not Contact).
9. Select the preferred contact method (Phone, Email, SMS).
10. Enter a message for the individual or family affected.
11. Select the emergency status of the individual or family (e.g., No Emergency, Emergency Response Needed, Sheltered, Evacuated, Deceased).
12. Select the shelter status (Sheltered, Not Sheltered).
13. Enter the shelter name.
14. Enter the shelter address.
15. Enter the shelter contact number.
16. Enter the second shelter name.
17. Enter the second shelter address.
18. Enter the second shelter contact number.
19. Enter the third shelter name.
20. Enter the third shelter address.
21. Enter the third shelter contact number.
22. Select the shelter contact method (Phone, Email, SMS).
23. Enter the fourth shelter contact number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Report** (ID 1): Enter a brief description of the emergency situation.
* **Individual Name** (ID 2): Enter the name of the individual or family member affected.
* **Address** (ID 3): Enter the physical address of the individual or family member.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (ID 4): Enter the primary contact number for the individual or family member.
* **Date of Birth** (ID 5): Enter the date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Relationship to Individual** (ID 6): Select the relationship to the individual or family member (e.g., Parent, Spouse, Sibling, Child, or Other).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (ID 7): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number 2** (ID 8): Enter the secondary contact number for the individual or family member (if applicable).
* **Would You Like to be Contacted** (ID 9): Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, SMS, or Do Not Contact).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (ID 10): Select the preferred contact method (Phone, Email, SMS).
* **Message to Leave** (ID 11): Enter a message for the individual or family affected.
* **Emergency Status** (ID 12): Select the emergency status of the individual or family (e.g., No Emergency, Emergency Response Needed, Sheltered, Evacuated, Deceased).
* **Shelter Status** (ID 13): Select the shelter status (Sheltered, Not Sheltered).
* **Shelter Name** (ID 14): Enter the name of the shelter.
* **Shelter Address** (ID 15): Enter the address of the shelter.
* **Shelter Contact Number** (ID 16): Enter the contact number of the shelter.
* **Shelter Name (2)** (ID 17): Enter the second shelter name.
* **Shelter Address 2** (ID 18): Enter the address of the second shelter.
* **Shelter Contact Number 2** (ID 19): Enter the second shelter contact number.
* **Shelter Contact Name** (ID 20): Enter the name of the contact person at the shelter.
* **Shelter Contact Number 3** (ID 21): Enter the third shelter contact number.
* **Shelter Contact Method** (ID 22): Select the contact method of the shelter (Phone, Email, SMS).
* **Shelter Contact Number 4** (ID 23): Enter the fourth shelter contact number (if applicable).
* **Shelter Contact Method 2** (ID 24): Enter the second shelter contact method (if applicable).

Note: If you have any additional shelter information, please enter it in the provided fields.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you need assistance or have further questions, contact the emergency response team.
* If you are reporting on behalf of another person, please ensure that you have their permission to provide their information.
