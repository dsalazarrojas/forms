# Theme Park Food Poisoning Incident Report - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to report incidents of food poisoning that have occurred within the theme park. It helps gather information about the incident, including the date and time it happened, the food involved, symptoms reported, and any medical attention received. The form also collects contact information for follow-up and notes from theme park staff.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the date and time the incident occurred in the "Reported Date" and "Reported Time" fields.
2. Select the type of food that was consumed that led to the incident from the "Food Type" field.
3. Choose the restaurant where the incident occurred from the "Restaurant" field.
4. Select any symptoms experienced from the "Symptoms" field.
5. Indicate if medical attention was received by selecting the relevant options in the "Medical Attention" field.
6. Choose the treatment provided for the incident from the "Treatment" field.
7. Enter the follow-up date for the incident, if applicable.
8. Provide the contact information of the person reporting the incident in the "Contact Person", "Contact Email", and "Contact Phone" fields.
9. Select the theme park staff member involved in the incident from the "Theme Park Staff" field.
10. Identify who submitted the report from the "Submitted By", "Submitted By 2", "Submitted By 3", "Submitted By 4", and "Submitted By 5" fields.
11. Enter any notes from theme park staff in the "Theme Park Staff Notes" field.
12. Enter any additional notes from the theme park manager in the "Theme Park Manager Notes" field.
13. Enter any notes from the food and beverage manager in the "Food and Beverage Manager Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reported Date** (1, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Reported Time** (2, time, required): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Food Type** (3, select_multiple, required): Select the type of food that was consumed that led to the incident.
* **Restaurant** (4, select_one, required): Choose the restaurant where the incident occurred.
* **Symptoms** (5, select_multiple, required): Select any symptoms experienced as a result of the incident.
* **Medical Attention** (6, select_multiple, required): Indicate if medical attention was received by selecting the relevant options.
* **Treatment** (7, select_multiple, required): Choose the treatment provided for the incident.
* **Follow Up** (8, date, required): Enter the follow-up date for the incident.
* **Contact Person** (9, text, required): Provide the contact information of the person reporting the incident.
* **Contact Email** (10, email, required): Enter the email address of the person reporting the incident.
* **Contact Phone** (11, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person reporting the incident.
* **Theme Park Staff** (12, select_one, required): Select the theme park staff member involved in the incident.
* **Submitted By** (13, select_one, required): Identify who submitted the report.
* **Submitted On** (14, date, required): Enter the date the report was submitted.
* **Submitted By 2** (15, select_one, required): Identify who submitted the report.
* **Theme Park Location** (16, select_one, required): Select the location within the theme park where the incident occurred.
* **Notes** (17, text, required): Enter any additional notes regarding the incident.
* **Theme Park Staff Notes** (18, text, required): Enter any notes from theme park staff regarding the incident.
* **Submitted By 3** (19, select_one, required): Identify who submitted the report.
* **Theme Park Manager Notes** (20, text, required): Enter any notes from the theme park manager regarding the incident.
* **Submitted By 4** (21, select_one, required): Identify who submitted the report.
* **Food and Beverage Manager Notes** (22, text, required): Enter any notes from the food and beverage manager regarding the incident.
* **Submitted By 5** (23, select_one, required): Identify who submitted the report.
* **Submitted On 2** (24, date, required): Enter the date the report was submitted.
