# Report Storm Drain Issues In Your Community - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help report storm drain issues in your community, allowing you to notify facility managers and authorities about problems with storm drains in your area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the "Category" as either "Emergency Report Forms" or "Other Reports" depending on the nature of your issue.
2. Provide a brief "Description" of the issue, describing the problem you are experiencing.
3. If applicable, add up to 3 photos of the issue by typing in the "Photos" field. Note that these fields are not required.
4. Enter the "Facility Location" where the issue is occurring, including the address or any other relevant information.
5. Describe the "Issue Description" in more detail, providing additional information about the issue.
6. Select the "Issue Priority" as High, Medium, or Low to indicate the severity of the issue.
7. If you have taken photos of the issue, enter them in the "Issue Photo 1", "Issue Photo 2", and "Issue Photo 3" fields.
8. Enter the name and contact information of the "Facility Manager" or contact person responsible for the facility.
9. Provide the phone number and email of the facility in the "Facility Phone" and "Facility Email" fields.
10. Enter the "Issue Date" and "Issue Time" when the issue occurred.
11. Specify the "Issue Location" where the issue is occurring.
12. Provide any additional "Issue Details" about the issue in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: This field allows you to choose the type of report you are submitting. Choose "Emergency Report Forms" for urgent issues and "Other Reports" for less urgent matters.
* **Description**: This field is for a brief description of the issue you are experiencing.
* **Photos**: If you have taken photos of the issue, enter them in this field. You can add up to 3 photos.
* **Facility Location**: Enter the address or location of the facility with the issue.
* **Issue Description**: Provide a more detailed description of the issue.
* **Issue Priority**: Select the severity of the issue as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Issue Photo 1**, **Issue Photo 2**, **Issue Photo 3**: If you have taken photos of the issue, enter them in these fields.
* **Facility Manager**: Enter the name and contact information of the facility manager or contact person.
* **Facility Phone**: Enter the phone number of the facility.
* **Facility Email**: Enter the email of the facility.
* **Issue Date**: Enter the date when the issue occurred.
* **Issue Time**: Enter the time when the issue occurred.
* **Issue Location**: Specify the location where the issue is occurring.
* **Issue Details**: Provide additional details about the issue.
* **Report**: Click this button to submit your report.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible when describing the issue.
* If you have taken photos, make sure they are clear and relevant to the issue.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can contact the facility manager or authority for guidance.
* Please be patient as responses may take some time to process.
