# Marina Boat Collision Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marina Boat Collision Incident Report Form is a critical document used to report and document incidents involving boats, ensuring prompt response and resolution of incidents affecting marinas. It is essential to complete this form accurately, as it helps our team to investigate and address boat collisions efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Start by selecting the correct date of the incident** from the "Incident Date" field.
- **Enter the name of the boat** involved in the collision in the "Boat Name" field.
- **Select the type of boat** (Sail, Motor, or Other) that was involved in the "Boat Type" field.
- **Specify the color of the boat** involved in the collision in the "Boat Color" field.
- **Select the type of collision** (Grounding, Contact) that occurred in the "Collision Type" field.
- **Provide a detailed description** of what happened, including any relevant information in the "Report Details" field.
- **Enter the location** where the incident occurred in the "Incident Location" field.
- **Enter the name of the boat operator** involved in the "Boat Operator" field.
- **Provide any witness details** if applicable in the "Witness Details" field.
- **Enter the witness's statement** if available in the "Witness Statement" field.
- **Attach any supporting documents** related to the incident, such as photos or videos, in the "Supporting Documents" field.
- **Enter any photos or videos** related to the incident in the "Photos Videos" field.
- **Describe the weather conditions** at the time of the incident in the "Weather Conditions" field.
- **Add any additional notes** regarding the incident in the "Notes" field.
- **Mark the status of the incident** as "Open" or "Closed" in the "Incident Status" field.
- **Check "Yes" if follow-up is required** in the "Follow-up Required" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
- **Boat Name**: Enter the name of the boat involved in the collision.
- **Boat Type**: Select the type of boat involved (Sail, Motor, or Other).
- **Boat Color**: Select the color of the boat involved (Red, Blue, or Green).
- **Collision Type**: Select the type of collision (Grounding, Contact).
- **Report Details**: Provide a detailed description of what happened.
- **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
- **Boat Operator**: Enter the name of the boat operator.
- **Witness Details**: Provide any witness details if applicable.
- **Witness Statement**: Enter any witness statement if available.
- **Supporting Documents**: Attach any supporting documents related to the incident (e.g., photos, videos).
- **Photos Videos**: Enter any photos or videos related to the incident.
- **Weather Conditions**: Describe the weather conditions at the time of the incident.
- **Notes**: Add any additional notes regarding the incident.
- **Incident Status**: Mark the status of the incident as "Open" or "Closed".
- **Follow-up Required**: Check "Yes" if follow-up is required.
- **Incident Closed**: Not applicable for this form; this field is for internal use only.
