# Critical Incident Response Session Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to schedule appointments for critical incident response sessions. Critical incidents are significant events that require immediate attention and support from our team. This form is the primary means of requesting an appointment for these sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of incident that you are responding to (Critical Accident or Critical Incident) or describe the incident if it's not listed.
2.  Enter the date of the incident.
3.  Enter the time of the incident.
4.  Provide a brief summary of the incident.
5.  Select the session location.
6.  Enter the participant(s) name(s) who will be attending the session.
7.  Choose the participant's role in the incident (Client, Employee, or Other).
8.  Enter the participant's phone number for easy contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident that you are responding to. This can be Critical Accident or Critical Incident, or if not listed, describe the incident in the "Incident Summary" field.
    *   **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YY.
    *   **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident in the format 24-hour clock HH:MM AM/PM.
    *   **Incident Summary**: Provide a brief description of what happened.
    *   **Session Location**: Choose a location for the session to be held at.
    *   **Session Start Time**: Enter the time of the session start.
    *   **Session Duration**: Enter the expected duration of the session.
    *   **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant(s) who will be attending the session.
    *   **Participant Role**: Choose the role of the participant in the incident (Client, Employee, or Other).
    *   **Participant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant for easy contact.

Note to the form designer and writer: Please ensure that the fields are filled in correctly and that the form is properly validated before submitting it. This will help prevent errors and ensure that the session is scheduled correctly.
