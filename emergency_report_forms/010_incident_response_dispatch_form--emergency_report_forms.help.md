# Incident Response Dispatch Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Response Dispatch Form is used to report and dispatch incidents to the appropriate team, ensuring a prompt and effective response. This form collects information about the incident, including the dispatching team, priority level, response time, and incident details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the dispatching team that will respond to the incident.
2.  Choose the priority level of the incident (High, Medium, or Low).
3.  Enter the expected response time in hours.
4.  If necessary, select the team members who will be dispatched to respond to the incident.
5.  Provide a brief description of the incident.
6.  Enter the date of the incident.
7.  Enter the time of the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dispatching Team**: Select the team that will respond to the incident. This field is required as it determines who will take action on the incident.
* **Priority Level**: Choose the priority level of the incident, which affects the urgency and speed of the response. High priority indicates a critical situation, Medium for a less critical situation, and Low for a routine response.
* **Response Time**: Enter the expected time for the response, in hours. This helps the responding team plan their response and allocate resources accordingly.
* **Dispatching Team Member**: Select the team member(s) who will be dispatched to respond to the incident. This field is not required but can be useful for tracking who is responding.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a brief description of the incident, including relevant details and context. This helps the responding team understand the situation.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
