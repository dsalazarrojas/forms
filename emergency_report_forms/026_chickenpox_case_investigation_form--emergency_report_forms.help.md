# Chickenpox Case Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for reporting and documenting details of a chickenpox case.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **-- Chickenpox Case Investigation Form --**: This is a note page that introduces the form and provides instructions for completion.
2. **Report date**: Enter the date when the case was reported. This is a mandatory field.
3. **Reporting agency**: Enter the name of the agency or organization that is reporting the case. This is a mandatory field.
4. **Investigator name**: Enter the name of the investigator completing the form. This is a mandatory field.
5. **Patient first name**: Enter the first name of the patient. This is a mandatory field.
6. **Patient last name**: Enter the last name of the patient. This is a mandatory field.
7. **Date of birth**: Enter the date of birth of the patient. This is a mandatory field.
8. **Age**: Enter the age of the patient. This is a mandatory field.
9. **Gender**: Select the gender of the patient (Male, Female, Other). This is a mandatory field.
10. **Residential address**: Enter the patient's home address. This is a mandatory field.
11. **City**: Enter the city where the patient resides. This is a mandatory field.
12. **State or province**: Enter the state or province of the patient's residence. This is a mandatory field.
13. **Postal code**: Enter the postal code of the patient's residence. This is a mandatory field.
14. **Phone number**: Enter the patient's phone number. This is a mandatory field.
15. **Email address**: Enter the patient's email address (optional field).
16. **Date of symptom onset**: Enter the date when the symptoms started. This is a mandatory field.
17. **Date of rash appearance**: Enter the date when the rash appeared. This is a mandatory field.
18. **Fever present**: Check if the patient has a fever. This is a mandatory field.
19. **Highest temperature recorded**: Enter the highest temperature recorded for the patient (optional field).
20. **Symptoms experienced**: Select all the symptoms experienced by the patient (Fever, Fatigue, Headache, Loss of appetite, Muscle aches, Malaise, Chickenpox rash). This is a mandatory field.
21. **Varicella vaccination status**: Select the patient's vaccination status (Unvaccinated, 1 dose only, 2 doses, Status unknown). This is a mandatory field.
22. **Prior varicella**: Check if the patient has had a prior varicella infection. This is a mandatory field.
23. **Known exposure source or contact**: Enter any known exposure source or contact (optional field).
24. **School or work attendance status**: Select the patient's school or work attendance status (Currently attending, Absent, Not applicable). This is a mandatory field.
25. **Was hospitalization required**: Check if the patient required hospitalization. This is a mandatory field.
26. **Any complications**: Select any complications that occurred (None, Bacterial infection, Encephalitis, Pneumonia, Other). This is a mandatory field.
27. **Number of household contacts**: Enter the number of household contacts (optional field).
28. **Follow-up action needed**: Select the follow-up action needed (No follow-up, Household contact monitoring, Workplace notification, School notification, Other). This is a mandatory field.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the mandatory fields accurately and completely.
- Double-check your entries for any errors before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
- This form is for reporting and documenting details of a chickenpox case.
