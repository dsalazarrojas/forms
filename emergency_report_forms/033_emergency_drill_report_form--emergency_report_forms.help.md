# emergency_drill_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for reporting on emergency drills conducted in emergency situations. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drill Date** (1, Date, required): Enter the date the drill was conducted.
* **Drill Location** (2, Select One, required): Choose the location where the drill took place.
* **Drill Time** (3, Time, required): Enter the time the drill was conducted.
* **Observer Count** (4, Number, required): Enter the number of observers who attended the drill.
* **Safety Standards Met** (5, Select One, required): Choose whether safety standards were met during the drill.
* **Observations** (6, Note, required): Write down any notable observations made during the drill.
* **Actions Taken** (7, Select Multiple, required): Choose the actions taken as a result of the drill.
* **Conclusion** (8, Text, required): Summarize the outcome of the drill.
* **Recommended Improvements** (9, Select Multiple, required): Suggest any recommended improvements for future drills.
* **Additional Comments** (10, Text, required): Enter any additional comments or thoughts about the drill.
* **Drill Conducted By** (11, Select One, required): Choose who conducted the drill.
