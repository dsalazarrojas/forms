# Safety Drill Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals participating in a safety drill, ensuring they have provided consent to participate and providing a way for the organization to contact them and for them to provide any medical conditions or notes. The purpose of this form is to ensure the organization can effectively contact and care for participants during the drill, and to provide any necessary medical information in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Please fill in your Name in the first field. This will help us to identify you during the drill.
* Enter your Contact Number in the second field. This will allow us to contact you if we need to during the drill.
* Enter your Email in the third field. This will be used as a secondary contact method in case we are unable to reach you by phone.
* In the Medical Conditions field, if you have any medical conditions that we should be aware of, please enter them here.
* In the Medical Notes field, if you have any medical notes or details you'd like to provide, please enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Text)**: Please enter your name to identify you during the drill.
* **Contact Number (Text)**: Enter your primary contact number. Please ensure this is a number you can be reached on during the drill.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address as a secondary contact method. This will be used if we are unable to reach you by phone.
* **Medical Conditions (Text)**: If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them here. This will help us to provide necessary care during the drill.
* **Medical Notes (Text)**: If you have any medical notes or details you'd like to share, please enter them here. This will help us to provide personalized care during the drill.

## Tips
* Please ensure you complete all required fields accurately to ensure we can effectively contact and care for you during the drill.
* If you have any medical conditions or notes, please ensure you enter them accurately and clearly. This will help us to provide proper care.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
