# Disaster_recovery_plan_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on the disaster recovery plan for an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the recovery strategies that will be implemented in case of a disaster.
2. Check the boxes for the personnel involved, specifying whether they are part of the recovery plan or not.
3. Select the services that will be affected during the disaster.
4. Enter the restoration time and personnel contacts information.
5. Specify the restoration services that will be provided.
6. Choose the status of the recovery plan.
7. Add any additional notes relevant to the disaster recovery plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recovery Strategies**: <u>Recovery Strategies</u> (`recovery_strategies`, text, required/optional)
    Enter a text description of the recovery strategies to be implemented in case of a disaster.
* <u>Personnel Involved</u> (`personnel_involved`, select_one, required/optional)
    Specify whether the personnel listed are involved in the disaster recovery plan or not.
* <u>Services Affected</u> (`services_affected`, select_multiple, required/optional)
    Select the services that will be affected during the disaster.
* <u>Restoration Time</u> (`restoration_time`, time, required/optional)
    Enter the time required for restoration.
* <u>Personnel Contacts</u> (`personnel_contacts`, select_multiple, required/optional)
    Specify the personnel contacts involved in the recovery plan.
* <u>Restoration Services</u> (`restoration_services`, select_multiple, required/optional)
    Select the services that will be restored.
* <u>Recovery Plan Status</u> (`recovery_plan_status`, select_one, required/optional)
    Choose the status of the recovery plan.
* <u>Additional Notes</u> (`additional_notes`, note, required/optional)
    Enter any additional notes related to the recovery plan.
* <u>Services Restored</u> (`services_restored`, select_multiple, required/optional)
    Specify the services restored.
* <u>Personnel Involved Email</u> (`personnel_involved_email`, email, required/optional)
    Enter the email address of the personnel involved.
* <u>Personnel Involved Phone</u> (`personnel_involved_phone`, text, required/optional)
    Enter the phone number of the personnel involved.
* <u>Services Affected ID</u> (`services_affected_id`, text, required/optional)
    Enter the ID of the services affected.
* <u>Restoration Time ID</u> (`restoration_time_id`, text, required/optional)
    Enter the ID of the restoration time.
* <u>Personnel Contacts ID</u> (`personnel_contacts_id`, text, required/optional)
    Enter the ID of the personnel contacts.
* <u>Services Restored ID</u> (`services_restored_id`, text, required/optional)
    Enter the ID of the services restored.
* <u>Recovery Plan Status ID</u> (`recovery_plan_status_id`, text, required/optional)

* <u>Additional Notes ID</u> (`additional_notes_id`, text, required/optional)

* <u>Services Restored Name</u> (`services_restored_name`, select_multiple, required/optional)
