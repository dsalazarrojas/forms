# wildfire_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wildfire Incident Form is used to collect information about wildfires, including the type, location, and characteristics of the fire, to support decision-making and resource allocation for wildfire management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of wildfire incident from the "Incident Type" field.
2. Enter the location of the fire in the "Location" field.
3. If applicable, select the fire size from the "Fire Size" field.
4. Indicate if the fire is spreading in the "Fire Spread" field.
5. Choose the direction the wind is blowing in the "Wind Direction" field.
6. Enter any available wind speed information in the "Wind Speed" field.
7. Select the type of fuel (e.g., trees, grass, brush, other) contributing to the fire in the "Fire Fuel" field (note: this field allows multiple selections).
8. Select the type of fire (wildfire, prescribed burn, or other) from the "Fire Type" field.
9. Enter your name and contact information in the "Incident Reporter" field.
10. Upload any photos, videos, or documents related to the incident in the "Photos", "Videos", and "Documents" fields (note: these fields allow multiple selections).
11. Provide any additional information about the incident in the "Other Info" field.
12. Select the incident status from the "Incident Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Type**: Select the type of wildfire incident (Fire, Flood, Other).
* **Location**: Enter the location of the wildfire incident.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the wildfire (if known).
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the wildfire (if known).
* **Fire Size**: Select the size of the fire (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Fire Spread**: Indicate if the fire is spreading (True, False).
* **Wind Direction**: Choose the direction the wind is blowing (North, South, East, West).
* **Wind Speed**: Enter any available wind speed information.
* **Fire Fuel**: Select the type of fuel contributing to the fire (Trees, Grass, Brush, Other, Structures).
* **Fire Type**: Select the type of fire (Wildfire, Prescribed burn, Other).
* **Incident Reporter**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Contact Information**: Enter any additional contact information.
* **Photos**: Upload photos related to the incident.
* **Videos**: Upload videos related to the incident.
* **Documents**: Upload any relevant documents related to the incident.
* **Other Info**: Provide any additional information about the incident.
* **Incident Status**: Select the current status of the incident (Open, Closed, Held, Released).
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident (if known).
