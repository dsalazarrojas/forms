# Emergency Road Service Call Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency road service call form is used to collect information about emergencies, incidents, and road conditions reported by the public. It helps emergency services, maintenance crews, and road authorities to respond promptly and effectively to such incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by selecting the incident location using the text field provided.
2.  Provide the caller's contact information, including their name, phone number, and any other relevant details.
3.  Enter the caller's phone number for easy contact and follow-up.
4.  Indicate if the caller's vehicle was involved in the incident or not.
5.  Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details such as weather conditions, road conditions, and vehicle involved.
6.  Enter the date and time of the incident.
7.  Select the caller's gender and language.
8.  Indicate if the incident occurred on a clear or congested road section.
9.  Specify the type of incident that occurred, such as an accident or animal collision.
10.  Describe the condition of the road section where the incident occurred.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Incident Location (Field 1)
*   **Incident Location**: Enter the exact location of the incident, including the address or a description of the location.
    *   Type the location in the text field.
*   **Hint:** Please provide the exact location to help emergency services respond effectively.

### Caller Contact Information (Field 2)
*   **Caller Contact Information**: Provide the caller's name and phone number for easy contact and follow-up.
