# Fire Hazard Report Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fire Hazard Report Contact Form is a form used for reporting fire hazards and incidents to the relevant authorities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to submit the form to ensure it is received by the authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info**:
	+ (Required: `false`)
	+ A text field to enter any relevant contact information for the person in charge of reporting the incident.
* **Emergency Services**:
	+ (Required: `false`)
	+ Select multiple options from Fire Department, Police Department, and Ambulance Service to indicate which services are relevant to the incident.
* **Fire Details**:
	+ (Required: `false`)
	+ A text field to enter any details about the fire, such as its type and size.
* **Fire Type**:
	+ (Required: `false`)
	+ Select one option from Electrical, Gas, or Other to indicate the type of fire.
* **Fire Size**:
	+ (Required: `false`)
	+ Enter the size of the fire on a numerical scale.
* **Fire Location**:
	+ (Required: `false`)
	+ Enter the location where the fire occurred.
* **Contact Person**:
	+ (Required: `false`)
	+ Enter the name of the contact person in charge of reporting the incident.
* **Contact Person Email**:
	+ (Required: `false`)
	+ Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Phone**:
	+ (Required: `false`)
	+ Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Fire Description**:
	+ (Required: `false`)
	+ Enter a brief description of the fire incident.
* **Fire Status**:
	+ (Required: `false`)
	+ Select one or multiple options from Active or Inactive to indicate the status of the fire.
* **Submit**:
	+ (Required: `false`)
	+ Click to submit the form and send the report to the relevant authorities.

## Tips

* Make sure to submit the form with accurate and complete information.
* If you have already reported the incident, please do not submit this form again.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities directly.
