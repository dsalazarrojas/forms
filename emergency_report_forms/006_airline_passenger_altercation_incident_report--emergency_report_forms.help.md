# Airline Passenger Altercation Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a passenger altercation incident that occurred on one of our flights. It is used to gather details about the incident, including the date, time, location, and parties involved. This information will help our team to investigate and respond to the incident properly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the full name of the person reporting the incident in the **Full Name of Reporter** field.
2.  Enter the role or position of the person reporting the incident in the **Role or Position** field.
3.  Enter the contact number or email of the person reporting the incident in the **Contact Number or Email** field.
4.  Enter the flight number on which the incident occurred in the **Flight Number** field.
5.  Enter the date of the incident in the **Date of Incident** field.
6.  Enter the time of the incident in the **Time of Incident** field.
7.  Enter the location of the incident, e.g., onboard the flight, at the terminal, or at the check-in desk, in the **Location of Incident** field.
8.  Enter the name of the primary passenger involved in the incident in the **Primary Passenger Involved** field.
9.  Enter the name of the second passenger involved in the incident in the **Second Party Involved** field.
10.  Select the type of altercation that occurred in the **Type of Altercation** field.
11.  Enter a detailed description of the incident in the **Detailed Description of the Incident** field, including what led to the conflict and any specific actions taken.
12.  Enter any action or intervention taken in response to the incident in the **Action or Intervention Taken** field.
13.  Indicate if any injuries were reported in the **Injuries Reported** field.
14.  Indicate if a police or security report was filed in the **Was a Police or Security Report Filed** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Reporter**: Enter the full name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Role or Position**: Enter the role or position of the person reporting the incident, e.g., Lead Flight Attendant, Gate Agent, or Passenger.
* **Contact Number or Email**: Enter the contact number or email of the person reporting the incident.
* **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number on which the incident occurred.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date of the incident, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time of Incident**: Enter the time of the incident, in 24-hour format, e.g., 12:00.
* **Location of Incident**: Enter the location where the incident occurred, e.g., onboard, at the terminal, or at the check-in desk.
* **Primary Passenger Involved**: Enter the name of the primary passenger involved in the incident.
* **Second Party Involved**: Enter the name of the second passenger involved in the incident.
* **Type of Altercation**: Select the type of altercation that occurred.
* **Detailed Description of the Incident**: Enter a detailed description of the incident, including what led to the conflict and any specific actions taken.
* **Action or Intervention Taken**: Enter any action or intervention taken in response to the incident.
* **Injuries Reported**: Indicate if any injuries were reported.
* **Was a Police or Security Report Filed**: Indicate if a police or security report was filed.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
*   If the incident is still ongoing, please indicate this in the **Was a Police or Security Report Filed** field.
*   If the incident is not yet known to the airline, please ensure that this form is submitted as soon as possible.
