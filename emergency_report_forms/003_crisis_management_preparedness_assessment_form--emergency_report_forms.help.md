# Crisis Management Preparedness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crisis Management Preparedness Assessment Form is designed to collect contact information from organizations in case of emergencies. This information will help ensure that the organization can be contacted and assisted promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your organization's name in the "Organization Name" field.
2. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field. Please ensure this is a valid email address where you can be reached.
3. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field. This can be a cell phone or a landline.
4. Enter the name of the person who should be contacted in case of an emergency in the "Contact Person" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name (Text)**: This is the name of your organization. Please enter the full name as it appears on your business cards or letterhead.
* **Contact Email (Email)**: This is the email address where you can be contacted. Please ensure this is a valid email address where you can receive messages.
* **Contact Phone (Text)**: This is the phone number where you can be contacted. This can be a cell phone or a landline.
* **Contact Person (Text)**: This is the name of the person who should be contacted in case of an emergency. Please ensure this is the person who will be available to respond to messages and take actions.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the email address and phone number are up-to-date and accessible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to your crisis management team.
