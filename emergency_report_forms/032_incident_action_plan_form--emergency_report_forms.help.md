# Incident Action Plan Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Incident Action Plan Form, a tool used to document and report incidents that have occurred.

## How To Complete This Form

### Before You Start
Before beginning, make sure you have all the necessary information about the incident, including its title, date, time, location, and a brief summary of what happened.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Enter the title of the incident in the "incident title" field.
2. Select the date and time the incident occurred in the "incident date" and "incident time" fields.
3. Provide the location of the incident in the "incident location" field.
4. Enter a brief summary of the incident in the "incident summary" field.
5. Enter the date for the action being taken in the "action date" field.
6. Enter a brief summary of the action being taken in the "action summary" field.
7. Select the status of the action in the "action status" field (Waiting, Complete, or In Progress).
8. Enter the urgency of the action in the "action urgency" field.
9. Select the priority of the action in the "action priority" field.
10. Enter the details of the action being taken in the "action details" field.
11. Enter the outcome of the action in the "action outcome" field.
12. Select the responsible party for the incident in the "incident responsible party" field (Customer, Employee, or Public).
13. If the incident is not attributed to one of the listed parties, enter "other" and provide the name of the responsible party in the "incident responsible party other" field.
14. Select the responsible party for the action in the "action responsible party" field (Customer, Employee, or Public).
15. If the action is not attributed to one of the listed parties, enter "other" and provide the name of the responsible party in the "action responsible party other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Title**: Enter a brief title for the incident, such as "Equipment Malfunction in Warehouse".
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred, in the format of YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred, in the format of 24-hour clock (e.g., 14:00).
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred (e.g., Warehouse 3).
* **Incident Summary**: Enter a brief summary of the incident, including what happened and how it affects the business.
* **Action Date**: Enter the date for the action being taken to address the incident.
* **Action Summary**: Enter a brief summary of the action being taken.
* **Action Status**: Select the status of the action (Waiting, Complete, or In Progress).
* **Action Urgency**: Enter a number indicating the urgency of the action, with higher numbers indicating greater urgency.
* **Action Priority**: Enter a number indicating the priority of the action, with higher numbers indicating higher priority.
* **Action Details**: Enter the details of the action being taken.
* **Action Outcome**: Enter the outcome of the action.
* **Incident Responsible Party**: Select the party responsible for the incident (Customer, Employee, or Public).
* **Incident Responsible Party Other**: If the incident is not attributed to one of the listed parties, enter "other" and provide the name of the responsible party.
* **Action Responsible Party**: Select the party responsible for the action (Customer, Employee, or Public).
* **Action Responsible Party Other**: If the action is not attributed to one of the listed parties, enter "other" and provide the name of the responsible party.
