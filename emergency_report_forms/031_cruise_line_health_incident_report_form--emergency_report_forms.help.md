# Cruise Line Health Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report health-related incidents that occurred on a cruise line. It helps us track and analyze incidents to ensure a safe and healthy environment for all passengers and crew members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date and time of the incident.
2.  Provide the location where the incident occurred.
3.  Describe the incident in detail.
4.  Specify whether the incident was of a particular type (e.g., medical emergency).
5.  Choose the status of the incident (active or inactive).
6.  Indicate whether follow-up actions are required for the incident.
7.  Specify the severity of the incident.
8.  Add any additional notes or comments about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date the incident occurred. This is the date when the incident happened, not the date you are submitting the report.
*   **Report Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the time of the incident.
*   **Location** (`incident_location`): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): Describe the incident in detail.
*   **Incident Type** (`incident_type`): Choose whether the incident was of a particular type (e.g., medical emergency).
*   **Incident Status** (`incident_status`): Choose the status of the incident (active or inactive).
*   **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`): Indicate whether you are reporting the incident or not.
*   **Incident Severity** (`incident_severeness`): Choose the severity of the incident.
*   **Follow Up Status** (`incident_follow_up`): Indicate whether follow-up actions are required for the incident.
*   **Notes** (`incident_notes`): Add any additional comments or information about the incident.
