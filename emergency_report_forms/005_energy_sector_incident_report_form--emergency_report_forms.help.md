# Energy Sector Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Energy Sector Incident Report Form is a tool used to report incidents that have occurred or are currently happening in the energy sector. This form collects essential details about the incident, including date, time, location, and type of incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the incident in the `energy_sector_incident_date` field.
2. Select the time of the incident using the `energy_sector_incident_time` field.
3. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the `incident_description` field.
4. Specify the damage caused by the incident using the `incident_damage` field.
5. Indicate the sector of the energy industry where the incident occurred using the `energy_sector_incident_reporter` field.
6. Identify the type of incident using the `incident_type` field.
7. Report the status of the incident using the `incident_status` field.
8. Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for handling the incident in the `contact_person`, `contact_phone`, and `contact_email` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Energy Sector Incident Report Form** (`energy_sector_incident_date`):
	* This is the title of the form that explains the purpose of the report.
	* Please ignore this field and focus on providing information in the subsequent fields.
* **Energy Sector Incident Date** (`energy_sector_incident_date`):
	* Enter the date of the incident using the format MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Energy Sector Incident Time** (`energy_sector_incident_time`):
	* Select the time of the incident using the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`):
	* Provide your name or identity as the person reporting the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`):
	* Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`):
	* Describe the incident in detail, including what happened and any relevant circumstances.
* **Incident Damage** (`incident_damage`):
	* Select the level of damage caused by the incident (Minimal, Moderate, Severe).
* **Incident Reporter** (`energy_sector_incident_reporter`):
	* Indicate which sector of the energy industry is involved (Utility, Energy, Other).
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`):
	* Specify the type of incident (Operational, Non-Operational, Other).
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`):
	* Report the status of the incident (Active, Resolved).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`):
	* Enter the name of the person responsible for handling the incident.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`):
	* Provide the phone number of the person responsible for handling the incident.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`):
	* Enter the email address of the person responsible for handling the incident.

## Tips
* Please ensure that each report is a new, unique occurrence.
* Fill in all required fields to provide a complete report.
* If you need help or have questions, you can contact us at form@gic.mx.
