# emergency_access_key_issue_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency_access_key_issue_request_form is used to report lost, stolen, or damaged access keys. Fill out this form to report an issue and provide the necessary details for resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of access key issue you are reporting (Lost, Stolen, or Damaged).
2. Provide your organization ID number.
3. Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
4. Describe the issue and its details.
5. Select the category and assigned team member for the issue.
6. Add any additional comments or notes.
7. Set a due date for the issue.
8. Set the issue priority (Low, Medium, or High).
9. Enter the issue priority reason, if applicable.
10. Enter any comments or additional information.
11. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization ID** (`organization_id`, number, required): Enter your organization's unique ID number.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Describe the issue and its details.
* **Issue Date** (`issue_date`, date, required): Enter the date the issue occurred.
* **Issue Time** (`issue_time`, time, required): Enter the time the issue occurred.
* **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of issue (Lost, Stolen, or Damaged).
* **Issue Status** (`issue_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of the issue (Pending, Resolved, Closed).
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select the team member assigned to resolve the issue.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required): Enter the category of the issue.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Email Subject** (`email_subject`, text, required): Enter the subject of the email notification.
* **Email Body** (`email_body`, text, required): Enter the body of the email notification.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_at`, date, required): Enter the date the issue was submitted.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_at`, date, required): Enter the date the issue was assigned to a team member.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, required): Enter the date by which the issue must be resolved.
* **Issue Priority** (`issue_priority`, select_one, required): Select the priority of the issue (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Issue Priority Reason** (`issue_priority_reason`, text, required): Enter the reason for the issue priority.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, text, required): Enter the team member or department to which the issue was submitted.
* **Submitted From** (`submitted_from`, text, required): Enter your location or department.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes.
