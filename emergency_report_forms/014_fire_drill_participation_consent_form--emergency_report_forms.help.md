# fire_drill_participation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fire drill participation consent form is a document that collects information about an individual's participation in a fire drill. It is essential for individuals participating in the drill to complete this form, which helps to ensure their safety and the effectiveness of the drill.

## How To Complete This Form
Complete the form by following these steps:

1. Fill in the fire drill information section (page 1) with your name, the fire drill date and time, and any other relevant details.
2. Indicate whether you grant permission to share your contact information with emergency contacts (page 2).
3. Provide your emergency contact's name and phone number (page 3).
4. Write your acknowledgment of the form's content and purpose (page 4).
5. Sign and date the form (page 5).
6. Enter the fire drill date (page 6).
7. Enter your emergency contact's name and phone number (page 7 and 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **fire_drill_information** (page 1, text): This section is where you enter your name and any other relevant details about the fire drill, such as the date and time.
* **permission_to_share** (page 2, select_one): Check "yes" if you grant permission to share your contact information with emergency contacts; check "no" if you do not.
* **emergency_contacts** (page 3, text): Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **acknowledgment** (page 4, text): Write a brief statement acknowledging that you understand the purpose and risks of the fire drill.
* **signature** (page 5, note): Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement to participate.
* **fire_drill_date** (page 6, date): Enter the date of the fire drill.
* **emergency_contact_name** (page 7, text): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **contact_phone** (page 8, text): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
