# Mining Operation Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mining Operation Incident Report Form is designed to collect information about incidents or problems that occur during mining operations. This form is used to report incidents, such as accidents, injuries, or near-misses, to facilitate a prompt and effective response.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter the incident report**: Start by describing the incident that occurred during the mining operation. This will help us understand the circumstances surrounding the incident.
2. **Provide the location**: Enter the location where the incident took place. This will help us identify the specific area where the incident occurred.
3. **Specify the date and time**: Enter the date and time when the incident occurred. This will help us understand the timeline of events.
4. **Explain the incident cause**: Describe the cause of the incident. What led to the event? Was it a equipment failure, a human error, or something else?
5. **Describe the incident effect**: Explain the impact of the incident. Were there any injuries, damage to equipment, or other consequences?
6. **Attach any relevant files**: If there are any relevant documents or images that support your report, please attach them.
7. **Enter your name and email**: Finally, please enter your name and email so we can contact you if we need more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mining Operation Incident Report**: This is the main description of the incident. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Location**: Where did the incident occur?
* **Date**: Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Time**: Enter the time when the incident occurred.
* **Incident cause**: What led to the incident? Was it human error, equipment failure, or something else?
* **Incident effect**: Describe the impact of the incident. Were there any injuries, damage to equipment, or other consequences?
* **Attached files**: Please attach any relevant documents or images that support your report.
* **Submitter name**: Enter your name so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Email**: Enter your email so we can contact you for further questions or updates.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident.
* Attach any relevant files that support your report.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for help.
* If you need to report multiple incidents, you can submit multiple reports.
