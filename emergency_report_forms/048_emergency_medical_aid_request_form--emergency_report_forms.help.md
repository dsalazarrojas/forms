# Emergency Medical Aid Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals or organizations requesting emergency medical aid. It is used to gather essential details to facilitate the provision of timely and adequate medical attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. **Please read the form carefully** to understand what information is required.
2. Fill in the form with accurate and truthful information.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Emergency Medical Aid Request Form**
This is the title of the form. No action is required here, just acknowledge the form title.

### 2. **Medical Need**
This field is used to describe the level of medical assistance needed. Please choose one of the three options provided: **Normal**, **Severe**, or **Critical**.

### 3. **Location**
Please enter the exact location where the medical assistance is required.

### 4. **Contact Name**
Enter the name of the person or organization requesting emergency medical aid.

### 5. **Contact Email**
Enter the email address of the person or organization requesting emergency medical aid. This will help us reach out to you for further information if needed.

### 6. **Contact Phone**
Enter the phone number of the person or organization requesting emergency medical aid.

### 7. **Medical Facility**
Please choose from the options provided: **Clinic**, **Hospital**, **School**, or **Nonprofit**. This will help us understand the context of your request.

### 8. **Medical Supplies Required**
Enter the quantity of medical supplies required for the emergency situation. This will help us gauge the level of assistance needed.

### 9. **Medical Equipment Required**
Enter the quantity of medical equipment required for the emergency situation. This will help us gauge the level of assistance needed.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* Please be as accurate as possible with the information provided.
