# Metro Security Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Metro Security Report Form is a document for reporting incidents that occur on Metro premises. It's intended to aid in efficient investigation and resolution of incidents by providing relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To start, select the type of incident from the "Incident Type" dropdown list.
*   Enter the date the incident occurred or is currently ongoing.
*   Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
*   If applicable, enter the location where the incident occurred in the "Incident Location" field.
*   If there were witnesses, include their details in the "Witness Details" field.
*   If a suspect is involved, provide their details in the "Suspect Details" field.
*   If the incident involves a reportee (someone who reported the incident), include their details in the "Reportee Details" field.
*   Specify the status of the incident as "Active", "Resolved", or "Under Investigation" from the "Incident Status" dropdown list.
*   If you have further information, you can categorize the incident by selecting a category and subcategory from the "Category" and "Subcategory" fields respectively.
*   Finally, enter the reportee's name in the "Reportee Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Type** (`Security Threat`, `Misconduct`, `Other`, required: false): Choose the type of incident being reported.
*   **Incident Date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date the incident occurred or is currently ongoing.
*   **Incident Description** (`text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
*   **Incident Location** (`text`, required: false): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Witness Details** (`text`, required: false): Provide details of any witnesses.
*   **Suspect Details** (`text`, required: false): Provide details of the suspect involved in the incident.
*   **Reportee Details** (`text`, required: false): Enter details of the reportee involved in the incident.
*   **Incident Status** (`Active`, `Resolved`, `Under Investigation`, required: false): Choose the status of the incident from the dropdown list.
*   **Category** (`text`, required: false): Select a category for the incident.
*   **Subcategory** (`text`, required: false): Select a subcategory for the incident.
*   **Reportee Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the reportee's name.

Note: All fields are optional unless explicitly marked as required. Please fill out as much information as possible to aid in efficient investigation and resolution of the incident.
