# emergency_response_system_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "emergency_response_system_consent_form" is designed to collect essential contact information and consent for emergency response systems.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Contact Details" section with accurate information for the participant.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Emergency Contact Person" to indicate if there is an emergency contact person.
3. If an emergency contact person is selected, enter their title and phone number.
4. In the "Address" section, enter the participant's address.
5. Select "Consent Given" or "No Consent Given" for "Participant Consent".
6. Fill in "Emergency Contact Relationship" to specify the relationship between the participant and the emergency contact person.
7. Enter the emergency contact person's phone number and email address.
8. If there is another person who can be contacted in case of emergencies, fill in their details in the "Other Consent" section.
