# Emergency Services Schedule Preference - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for setting up individual preferences for emergency services scheduling. This is to ensure that you, as a staff member, are scheduled accordingly for emergency services. Please note that this form is not intended to be filled out more than once, and any updates will override previous preferences. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each field and ensure you have filled out all required fields (if any).
2. For each field, provide the information as requested.
3. Once you have completed all fields, submit the form to save your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Preferences
* General Preferences (`general`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information you would like to provide about your general preferences.

### Availability
* Availability (`availability`, text, optional): This field is for specifying your availability for emergency services, e.g., days you are available, time slots, etc.

### On-Call Availability
* On-Call Availability (`oncall_availability`, text, optional): This field is for specifying your on-call availability, e.g., times when you are available to be on-call.

### Constraints
* Constraints (`constraints`, text, optional): This field is for specifying any constraints you have for scheduling, e.g., medical conditions, family obligations, etc.

### Shift Preferred
* Shift Preferred (`shift_preferred`, select_multiple, optional): Select the shifts you prefer to work, e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Night.

### Work Hours
* Work Hours (`work_hours`, number, optional): This field is for specifying the number of hours you are willing to work in a shift, e.g., 8 hours.

### Max Shifts Per Week
* Max Shifts Per Week (`max_shifts`, number, optional): This field is for specifying the maximum number of shifts you are willing to work per week, e.g., 3 shifts.

### Emergency Services Schedule Preference
* Emergency Services Schedule Preference (`emergency_services_schedule_preference`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional information about your emergency services schedule preference.

### Emergency Services Schedule Preference Comment
* Emergency Services Schedule Preference Comment (`emergency_services_schedule_comment`, note, optional): This field is for providing any comments or feedback about your emergency services schedule preference.

Tips:
* Please ensure that you save your updated preferences to ensure the latest information is reflected in your schedule.
* If you have any conflicts or concerns with your schedule, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
