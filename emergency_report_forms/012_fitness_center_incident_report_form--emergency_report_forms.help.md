## Fitness Center Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents that occur at the fitness center. It helps fitness center staff to quickly and accurately gather details about what happened, which is essential for identifying and addressing potential risks and hazards.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date and time of the incident in the "incident_date" and "incident_time" fields, respectively.
2.  Provide a brief summary of what happened in the "incident_summary" field.
3.  Check if any injuries were sustained during the incident and describe them in the "injury_description" field.
4.  List any witnesses of the incident in the "witness_list" field.
5.  Check if any staff members were present during the incident and list them in the "staff_present" field.
6.  Indicate the location where the incident occurred in the "incident_location" field.
7.  Describe the actions taken after the incident in the "action_taken" field.
8.  Specify the status of the actions taken in the "action_status" field.
9.  Provide any relevant comments about the actions taken in the "action_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the incident.
• **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the incident.
• **Incident Summary** (`incident_summary`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe what happened.
• **Injury Description** (`injury_description`, `select_one`, optional): Check if any injuries were sustained during the incident.
• **Witness Details** (`witness_details`, `text`, optional): List any witnesses of the incident, including their contact information if available.
• **Witness List** (`witness_list`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if any witnesses were present during the incident.
• **Staff Present** (`staff_present`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if any staff members were present during the incident.
• **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the location where the incident occurred.
• **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, `text`, required): Describe the actions taken after the incident.
• **Action Status** (`action_status`, `select_one`, optional): Specify the status of the actions taken.
• **Action Comments** (`action_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant comments about the actions taken.
