# Forest Fire Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for reporting forest fire incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of incident that occurred (e.g. fire, weather, manmade).
2. If applicable, enter the date of the incident.
3. Provide a brief description of the incident.
4. Identify the location where the incident occurred (e.g. forest, park).
5. Enter your name and contact information so that we can get in touch with you.
6. Choose the status of the incident (e.g. active, resolved, closed).
7. Indicate the priority of the incident (e.g. high, medium, low).
8. Provide any additional information about the incident.
9. If applicable, attach a photo or video of the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g. fire, weather, manmade).
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident, if applicable. This is optional but helpful for tracking purposes.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a brief description of the incident, including any details that are relevant to the incident type and location.
* **Incident Location**: Choose the location where the incident occurred, such as a forest or park.
* **Incident Reporter**: Enter your name so that we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further information.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you for further information.
* **Contact Message**: Provide any additional information or comments about the incident.
* **Incident Status**: Choose the current status of the incident (e.g. active, resolved, closed).
* **Incident Priority**: Indicate the priority of the incident (e.g. high, medium, low).
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information about the incident that is not covered by the other fields.
* **Incident Comments**: Enter any comments or observations about the incident.
* **Incident Photo**: If applicable, attach a photo of the incident to help us better understand the situation.
* **Incident Video**: If applicable, attach a video of the incident to help us better understand the situation.

Note: Please ensure that you are providing accurate and up-to-date information to help us respond efficiently and effectively to the incident.
