# fire_alarm_incident_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The fire_alarm_incident_form is used to report incidents that have occurred on the premises. It is a necessary tool for staff or individuals to document and report incidents that require immediate attention from the authorities or management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Go to the page where the form is located and click on it to start filling it out.
2. Fill out each field as it appears, starting from the top of the page. Be sure to read the label for each field carefully to understand what information it is asking for.
3. Enter the required information for each field, and use the options provided to select the most accurate response.
4. Review your form before submitting it for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Incident Details
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of what happened, including any relevant details about the incident.
### Incident Location
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, not required): Enter the location where the incident occurred, including the address, floor, or any other relevant details.
### Incident Description
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, not required): Enter a detailed description of what happened, including the circumstances and any relevant details.
### Incident Type
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, text, not required): Select the type of incident that occurred, such as fire, gas leak, or other.
### Incident Date
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, not required): Enter the date the incident occurred or started.
### Incident Time
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, not required): Enter the time the incident occurred or started.
### Alarm Level
* **Alarm Level** (`alarm_level`, select_one, not required): Select the level of alarm that was raised during the incident, such as High or Low.
### Alarm Type
* **Alarm Type** (`alarm_type`, select_one, not required): Select whether an alarm was raised or not.
### Alarm Location
* **Alarm Location** (`alarm_location`, text, not required): Enter the location where the alarm was sounded or where the alarm can be heard.
### Alarm Description
* **Alarm Description** (`alarm_description`, text, not required): Enter a detailed description of the alarm, including any sounds, lights, or other sensory details.
### Responders
* **Responders** (`incident_responders`, select_multiple, not required): Select the individuals or teams that responded to the incident.
### Incident Status
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, not required): Select the current status of the incident, such as Active or Inactive.
### Investigation Status
* **Investigation Status** (`investigation_status`, select_one, not required): Select the current status of the investigation, such as Active or Inactive.
### Investigation Notes
* **Investigation Notes** (`investigation_notes`, text, not required): Enter any additional notes or information relevant to the investigation.
### Outcome
* **Outcome** (`incident_outcome`, text, not required): Enter the outcome or resolution of the incident.
### Notes
* **Notes** (`incident_notes`, text, not required): Enter any additional notes or information relevant to the incident.

## Tips
* Always read each field label carefully to understand what information it is asking for.
* Use the options provided for select fields to select the most accurate response.
* Review your form before submitting it to ensure it is complete and accurate.
* Do not submit multiple times for the same incident.
* If you are unsure about any information or have questions, please reach out to your supervisor or management for guidance.
