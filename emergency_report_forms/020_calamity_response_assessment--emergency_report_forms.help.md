# Calamity Response Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Calamity Response Assessment form is designed to collect information about emergency response efforts following a disaster or calamity. This form is intended to gather data on the severity of the event, the response efforts taken, and the needs assessment for the affected areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Report Date" field and choose the date of the calamity using the calendar picker.
2. Select the "Report Time" field and choose the time of the calamity using the time picker.
3. Enter the "Reporter Name" field with the name of the person submitting the report.
4. Enter the "Reporter Contact Information" field with the contact information of the person submitting the report.
5. Select the "Incident Location" field and enter the location of the calamity.
6. Select the "Date and Time of Incident" field and choose the date and time of the calamity using the calendar and time picker.
7. Select the "Type of Calamity" field and choose the type of calamity (e.g., Earthquake, Flood, etc.).
8. Select the "Severity Level" field and choose the severity level of the calamity (e.g., Low, Moderate, High, etc.).
9. Enter the "Number of Houses Damaged" and "Number of Houses Destroyed" fields with the number of houses damaged or destroyed.
10. Select all applicable options in the "Utilities Affected" field for the types of utilities affected by the calamity.
11. Enter the "Number of Deaths", "Number of Injured Persons", "Number of Missing Persons", and "Number of Displaced Persons" fields with the number of casualties.
12. Select the "Types of Relief Provided" field and choose all the types of relief provided (e.g., Food assistance, Medical aid, etc.).
13. Enter the "Additional Needs Assessment" field with any additional needs or concerns.
14. Enter the "Recommended Follow-up Actions" field with any recommended follow-up actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the calamity using the calendar picker.
* **Report Time** (`report_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the calamity using the time picker.
* **Reporter Name** (`reporter_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the report.
* **Reporter Contact Information** (`reporter_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact information of the person submitting the report.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the calamity.
* **Date and Time of Incident** (`incident_date_time`, date, required): Enter the date and time of the calamity using the calendar and time picker.
* **Type of Calamity** (`incident_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of calamity (e.g., Earthquake, Flood, etc.).
* **Severity Level** (`severity_level`, select_one, required): Choose the severity level of the calamity (e.g., Low, Moderate, High, etc.).
* **Number of Houses Damaged** (`houses_damaged`, number, optional): Enter the number of houses damaged.
* **Number of Houses Destroyed** (`houses_destroyed`, number, optional): Enter the number of houses destroyed.
* **Utilities Affected** (`utilities_affected`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the types of utilities affected by the calamity (e.g., Water supply, Electricity, etc.).
* **Number of Deaths** (`number_deceased`, number, optional): Enter the number of deaths.
* **Number of Injured Persons** (`number_injured`, number, optional): Enter the number of injured persons.
* **Number of Missing Persons** (`number_missing`, number, optional): Enter the number of missing persons.
* **Number of Displaced Persons** (`displaced_persons`, number, optional): Enter the number of displaced persons.
* **Response Measures** (`response_measures`, text, optional): Enter any response measures taken.
* **Types of Relief Provided** (`relief_provided`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the types of relief provided (e.g., Food assistance, Medical aid, etc.).
* **Additional Needs** (`additional_needs`, text, optional): Enter any additional needs or concerns.
* **Recommended Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_action`, text, optional): Enter any recommended follow-up actions.
