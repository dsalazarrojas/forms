# Initial Fire Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and details about an incident involving a fire department. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the incident is properly documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the incident type from the options provided.
2. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
3. Choose the date and time of the incident.
4. Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
5. Indicate if any injuries were sustained.
6. Choose if the fire department was involved.
7. Provide any additional images, videos, or audio recordings relevant to the incident.
8. Add any additional documents or witness statements.
9. If necessary, provide location coordinates, address, city, state, zip, and country information.
10. Add any notes about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fire Department**: The name of the fire department involved in the incident.
* **Incident Type**: Choose the type of incident that occurred (Fire, Other, or Unknown).
	+ Fire: An incident involving fire or flames.
	+ Other: An incident not related to fire or flames.
	+ Unknown: The type of incident is unknown or not specified.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date the incident occurred in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Incident**: Enter the time the incident occurred (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Time of Incident Note**: Provide any additional notes or details about the time of the incident.
* **Description of Incident**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Injuries Sustained**: If any injuries were sustained, enter a brief description of the injuries.
* **Fire Department Involvement**: Indicate if the fire department was involved in the incident (True or False).
* **Incident Image**: Upload any images relevant to the incident.
* **Incident Video**: Upload any videos related to the incident.
* **Incident Audio**: Upload any audio recordings relevant to the incident.
* **Incident Additional Documents**: Upload any additional documents related to the incident.
* **Witness Statements**: Upload any witness statements or reports.
* **Witness Statements Video**: Upload any witness statements in video format.
* **Witness Statements Audio**: Upload any witness statements in audio format.
* **Witness Statements Additional Documents**: Upload any additional documents related to witness statements.
* **Incident Location Coordinates**: Enter the location coordinates (latitude and longitude) of the incident.
* **Incident Location Address**: Enter the physical address of the incident location.
* **Incident Location City**: Enter the city where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location State**: Enter the state where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location Zip**: Enter the zip code of the incident location.
* **Incident Location Country**: Enter the country where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location Map**: Upload a map of the incident location.
* **Incident Notes**: Add any additional notes or details about the incident.

This form is designed to collect accurate and comprehensive information about a fire incident. Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly to ensure the incident is properly documented.
