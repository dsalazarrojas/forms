# emergency_fund_utilization_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency_fund_utilization_report is a form used to track and document expenses or disbursements made from the emergency fund.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all required information and supporting documents before completing the form.
2. Fill out the form in a sequential manner, one field at a time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **emergency_fund_utilization** (`emergency_fund_utilization`, note, required: false): This field is not currently populated with any information, it is intended for notes and comments related to the emergency fund.
* **emergency_fund_utilization_date** (`emergency_fund_utilization_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the emergency fund utilization event.
* **emergency_fund_utilization_amount** (`emergency_fund_utilization_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the amount of the emergency fund utilized.
* **supporting_documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, required: false): Upload any supporting documents related to the emergency fund utilization event.
* **outcomes** (`outcomes`, note, required: false): This field is not currently populated with any information, it is intended for notes and comments related to the outcomes of the emergency fund utilization event.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of the entered information before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required information is filled out to avoid form submission errors.
* If you are unsure about any field or process, please contact your administrator for clarification.
