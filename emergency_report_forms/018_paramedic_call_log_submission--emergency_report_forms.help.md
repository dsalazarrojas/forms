# Paramedic Call Log Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit log entries for paramedic responses to emergency calls. The form captures relevant information about the call, including call details, patient information, medical history, and other vital details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the call details, including the call type, location, and emergency number.
2. Provide patient information, including name and status.
3. Enter the patient's medical history and vital signs.
4. Record any medications administered and interventions performed.
5. Report the outcome of the call and any additional comments.
6. Enter the paramedic's name, ID, unit, and shift.
7. Select the date and time of the call.
8. Enter the paramedic supervisor's name, ID, and contact information.
9. Optionally, select the call type from the available options (Ambulance, Police, or Fire).
10. Click the "Submit" button to save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Call Details**: Enter a brief description of the emergency call, including call type, location, and emergency number.
* **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's name and status.
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history for the patient.
* **Vital Signs**: Enter the patient's vital signs, such as blood pressure, pulse, etc.
* **Medication Administered**: Enter any medications administered to the patient.
* **Interventions Performed**: Enter any interventions performed during the call.
* **Outcome**: Report the outcome of the call, including any relevant information about the patient's condition.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the call.
* **Patient Status**: Enter the patient's status after the call.
* **Call Location**: Enter the location of the call.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name.
* **Paramedic Name**: Enter your name as the paramedic responding to the call.
* **Paramedic ID**: Enter your paramedic ID.
* **Paramedic Unit**: Enter your paramedic unit number.
* **Paramedic Shift**: Enter your shift number.
* **Date**: Select the date of the call using the date picker.
* **Time**: Select the time of the call using the time picker.
* **Emergency Number**: Enter the emergency number.
* **Paramedic Supervisor**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor ID**: Enter your supervisor's ID.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Call Type**: Select the call type from the available options (Ambulance, Police, or Fire).
* **Location**: Enter the location of the call.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the call.
* **Submit**: Click this button to save the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of your entries before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your supervisor or medical protocol for guidance.
