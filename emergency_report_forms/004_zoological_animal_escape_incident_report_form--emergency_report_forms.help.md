# zoological_animal_escape_incident_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents involving escaped zoological animals. It helps zoo staff to gather information and take corrective measures to prevent future escapes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the date of the incident in the "Incident Date" field.
2. Describe the location where the animal escaped in the "Escape Location" field.
3. Specify the type of animal that escaped in the "Animal Type" field.
4. If applicable, enter the ID number of the enclosure where the animal escaped from.
5. Choose the type of escape in the "Escape Type" field.
6. Report the status of the animal in the "Animal Status" field.
7. Select whether zoo staff were involved or not in the "Zoo Staff Involvement" field.
8. Enter the time it took for the animal to escape in the "Escape Time" field.
9. Add any additional comments or details about the incident in the "Notes" field.
10. Enter the phone number of the contact person.
11. Enter the name of the contact person in the "Contact Person" field.
12. Enter the email of the contact person in the "Contact Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (incident_date): Date of the incident.
* **Escape Location** (escape_location): Location where the animal escaped.
* **Animal Type** (animal_type): Type of animal that escaped.
* **Enclosure ID** (enclosure_id): ID number of the enclosure where the animal escaped from. (If not available, enter "N/A")
* **Escape Type** (escape_type): Type of escape (e.g., Wild animal escaped from the enclosure, etc.)
* **Animal Status** (animal_status): Status of the animal (Alive, Dead, Injured, Missing, Unknown).
* **Zoo Staff Involvement** (zoo_staff_involvement): Whether zoo staff were involved (Staff member was not involved, Staff member involved, etc.)
* **Escape Time** (escape_time): Time it took for the animal to escape.
* **Notes** (notes): Additional comments or details about the incident.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number): Phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Person** (contact_person): Name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email): Email of the contact person.
