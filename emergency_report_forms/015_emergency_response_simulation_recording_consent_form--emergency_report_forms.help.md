# Emergency Response Simulation Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Response Simulation Recording Consent Form is designed to collect necessary information about emergency response simulations. It is intended to help us track and evaluate the progress of the simulated event and ensure that all relevant information is documented accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "User" field.
2. Enter the name of the emergency or simulated event in the "Emergency" field.
3. Enter the location where the simulation took place in the "Location" field.
4. Select the date of the simulation in the "Date" field.
5. Select the time of the simulation in the "Time" field.
6. Enter the contact phone number of the person in charge or responsible for the simulation in the "Contact Phone" field.
7. Enter the contact email of the person in charge or responsible for the simulation in the "Contact Email" field.
8. Enter the name of the drill or simulation in the "Drill Name" field.
9. Select the type of simulation (e.g. Fire, Earthquake, etc.) in the "Drill Type" field.
10. Enter a brief description of the simulation in the "Drill Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User**: Enter your name as the person completing this form.
* **Emergency**: Enter the name of the emergency or simulated event, e.g. "Fire Drill" or "Earthquake Simulation".
* **Location**: Enter the location where the simulation took place, e.g. "123 Main St, Anytown USA".
* **Date**: Enter the date of the simulation, in MM/DD/YYYY format, e.g. "02/16/2023".
* **Time**: Enter the time of the simulation, in 12-hour format, e.g. "14:30" (2:30 PM).
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the person in charge or responsible for the simulation.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of the person in charge or responsible for the simulation.
* **Drill Name**: Enter the name of the drill or simulation, e.g. "Fire Drill 2023".
* **Drill Type**: Select the type of simulation from the available options: Fire, Earthquake, Tornado, Flood, Snowstorm, or Other.
* **Drill Description**: Enter a brief description of the simulation, e.g. "This is a fire drill to test emergency response procedures."

Tips:
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that we have all the necessary information to track and evaluate the simulation.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the person in charge or responsible for the simulation for clarification.
* This form is intended to be used for legitimate purposes only, and should not be used for personal or commercial gain.
