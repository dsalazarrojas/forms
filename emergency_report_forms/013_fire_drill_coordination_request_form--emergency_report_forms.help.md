# Fire Drill Coordination Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Drill Coordination Request Form is a tool used to collect and record essential information for fire drills. It helps to ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately, and that the fire department is prepared and informed in case of an actual emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Fire Drill Request field with a brief description of the purpose of the fire drill.
2. Select a date for the fire drill, using the date field.
3. Choose a time for the fire drill, using the time field.
4. Enter the location where the fire drill will take place, in the Fire Drill Location field.
5. Identify the person responsible for the fire drill, in the Fire Drill Responsible Person field.
6. Provide detailed information about the location, if necessary, in the Fire Drill Location Details field.
7. Check if the fire department will be involved, using the Fire Department field.
8. Enter contact information for emergency situations, including name, phone number, and email, in the Emergency Contact fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fire Drill Request** (`fire_drill_request`, `text`, optional): A brief description of the purpose of the fire drill.
* **Fire Drill Date** (`fire_drill_date`, `date`, optional): The date of the fire drill.
* **Fire Drill Time** (`fire_drill_time`, `time`, optional): The time of the fire drill.
* **Fire Drill Location** (`fire_drill_location`, `text`, optional): The location where the fire drill will take place.
* **Fire Drill Responsible Person** (`fire_drill_responsible_person`, `text`, optional): The person responsible for the fire drill.
* **Fire Drill Location Details** (`fire_drill_location_details`, `text`, optional): Detailed information about the location, if necessary.
* **Fire Department** (`fire_drill_fire_department`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if the fire department will be involved in the fire drill.
* **Emergency Contact Details** (`emergency_contact_details`, `text`, optional): Detailed information about emergency contact, if necessary.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): The name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): The phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, optional): The email address of the emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* If unsure about any information, contact the relevant authorities or your supervisor for guidance.
* This form is not intended for actual emergency situations but for scheduled or planned fire drills.
