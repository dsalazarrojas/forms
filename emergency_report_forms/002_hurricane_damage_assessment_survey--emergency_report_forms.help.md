# Hurricane Damage Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hurricane Damage Assessment Survey is a form used to collect information about the damage caused by a hurricane to a property. This form is intended to help assess the extent of the damage and facilitate the claims process with insurance companies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your current home address.
2. Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you with any questions or updates.
3. Enter your email address to keep you informed about the status of your claim.
4. Describe the damage you experienced during the hurricane.
5. If possible, provide damage photos for further assessment.
6. Provide the date and time of when the damage occurred.
7. If an inspector visited your property, please enter their name.
8. If an inspector organization was involved, please enter their name.
9. If you have a property manager, please enter their name and organization.
10. Select the type of damage you experienced (wind, storm surge, flood, or other).
11. Enter the approximate amount of damage to your property.
12. Describe the location of the damage (e.g., roof, walls, etc.).
13. If you have an insurance company, please enter their name.
14. Indicate if you have filed an insurance claim (yes or no).
15. If you would like to add any comments about the inspection, please do so.
16. Finally, if you have any additional comments about the property or damage, please enter them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Address** (`1`, `text`, `required`): Enter the address of your home or property that was affected by the hurricane.
* **Phone Number** (`2`, `text`, `required`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any updates or questions.
* **Email Address** (`3`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address so we can keep you informed about your claim status.
* **Damage Description** (`4`, `text`, `required`): Describe the damage you experienced during the hurricane. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Damage Photos** (`5`, `note`, `required`): If possible, attach photos of the damage to help with the assessment process.
* **Date of Damage** (`6`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date when the damage occurred.
* **Time of Damage** (`7`, `time`, `required`: Enter the time when the damage occurred.
* **Inspector Name** (`8`, `text`, `required`): If an inspector visited your property, please enter their name.
* **Inspector Organization** (`9`, `text`, `required`): If an inspector organization was involved, please enter their name.
* **Property Manager Name** (`10`, `text`, `required`): If you have a property manager, please enter their name.
* **Property Manager Organization** (`11`, `text`, `required`): If you have a property manager, please enter their name.
* **Community Organization** (`12`, `text`, `required`): If you are part of a community organization, please enter their name.
* **Damage Type** (`13`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the type of damage you experienced (wind, storm surge, flood, or other).
* **Damage Amount** (`14`, `number`, `required`): Enter the approximate amount of damage to your property.
* **Damage Location** (`15`, `text`, `required`): Describe the location of the damage (e.g., roof, walls, etc.).
* **Insurance Company** (`16`, `text`, `required`): If you have an insurance company, please enter their name.
* **Insurance Claim Status** (`17`, `select_one`, `required`): Indicate if you have filed an insurance claim (yes or no).
* **Inspector Comments** (`18`, `text`, `required`): If you have any comments about the inspection, please enter them.
* **Property Owner Comments** (`19`, `text`, `required`): If you have any additional comments about the property or damage, please enter them.

Note: This guide is intended to be used in conjunction with the actual form fields, which may have additional guidance and validation. Always refer to the actual form for the most accurate and up-to-date information.
