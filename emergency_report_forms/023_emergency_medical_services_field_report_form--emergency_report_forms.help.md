# Emergency Medical Services Field Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's medical condition and response to emergency services in a structured and organized manner. It is intended to be filled out once per incident, and any missing or incorrect information may lead to incomplete or inaccurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the relevant information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before proceeding.
3. Review and verify the accuracy of the information provided before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Name** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical records or identification.

### 2. **Medical Condition** (Text, Required: False)
Describe the patient's current medical condition, including any relevant symptoms or diagnoses.

### 3. **Patient Address** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the patient's address as it appears on their medical records or identification.

### 4. **City** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the city where the patient is located.

### 5. **State** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the state where the patient is located.

### 6. **Zip** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the patient's zip code as it appears on their medical records or identification.

### 7. **Phone** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the patient's phone number as it appears on their medical records or identification.

### 8. **Medical History** (Text, Required: False)
Describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments.

### 9. **Emergency Contact Person** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.

### 10. **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.

### 11. **Medical Supervising Officer** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the name and title of the medical supervising officer.

### 12. **Emergency Services Provided** (Select Multiple, Required: False)
Select from the list of emergency services provided, such as:
	* Cardiac
	* Goals of care
	* Medical supervising officer
	* Patient education
	* Psychological services
	* Social services

### 13. **Additional Services** (Select Multiple, Required: False)
Select from the list of additional services provided, such as:
	* Cardiac
	* Goals of care
	* Medical supervising officer
	* Patient education
	* Psychological services
	* Social services

### 14. **Incident Date** (Date, Required: False)
Enter the date of the incident.

### 15. **Incident Time** (Time, Required: False)
Enter the time of the incident.

### 16. **Patient Age** (Number, Required: False)
Enter the patient's age.

### 17. **Patient Gender** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the patient's gender.

### 18. **Patient Weight** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the patient's weight.

### 19. **Patient Height** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the patient's height.

### 20. **Vital Signs** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the patient's vital signs, including temperature, blood pressure, and oxygen saturation.

### 21. **Blood Pressure** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the patient's blood pressure.

### 22. **Temperature** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the patient's temperature.

### 23. **Oxygen Saturation** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the patient's oxygen saturation.

### 24. **Medical Provider** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the name and title of the medical provider.

### 25. **Medical Provider Department** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the department or unit where the medical services were provided.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before proceeding.
* Review and verify the accuracy of the information provided before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the patient's medical records or contact the emergency services team for guidance.
