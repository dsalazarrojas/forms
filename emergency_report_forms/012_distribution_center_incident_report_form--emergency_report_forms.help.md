# Distribution Center Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is to be used by distribution centers to document and report incidents that have occurred within their facilities or affecting their assets. Please fill out this form to provide detailed information about the incident and help us track and manage incident reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Select the date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields.
*   In the "Incident Description" field, provide a detailed description of what occurred.
*   Select the location where the incident took place in the "Incident Location" field.
*   Choose which asset was affected by the incident in the "Asset Affected" field.
*   Enter the name of any employees or individuals involved in the incident in the "Employee Involved" field.
*   If there were any witnesses, enter their names and comments in the "Witness" field.
*   Select the type of incident that occurred in the "Incident Type" field.
*   If applicable, attach any relevant images, videos, or documents related to the incident in the "Incident Image", "Incident Video", and "Incident Attachment" fields.
*   Choose the level of damage to the asset affected in the "Asset Damage" field.
*   Select the status of the incident in the "Incident Status" field.
*   Enter any comments or updates related to the incident in the "Incident Comment" field.
*   Enter your name as the reporter of the incident in the "Incident Reporter" field.
*   Select the status of the asset affected in the "Asset Location Status" field.
*   If the incident has been resolved, select the date and time of resolution in the "Incident Report Date" and "Incident Report Time" fields.
*   Choose the priority level of the incident in the "Incident Priority" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   <u>Incident Date</u> (<font color="green">required</font>): The date of the incident.
*   <u>Incident Time</u> (<font color="green">required</font>): The time of the incident.
*   <u>Incident Description</u> (<font color="green">required</font>): A detailed description of what happened.
*   <u>Incident Location</u> (<font color="green">required</font>): The location where the incident took place.
*   <u>Asset Affected</u> (<font color="green">required</font>): The asset that was affected by the incident. This could be a specific warehouse or piece of equipment.
*   <u>Employee Involved</u> (<font color="green">optional</font>): The names of any employees or individuals involved in the incident.
*   <u>Witness</u> (<font color="green">optional</font>): The names of any witnesses to the incident.
*   <u>Incident Type</u> (<font color="green">required</font>): The type of incident that occurred (e.g. Equipment, Environmental, Human).
*   <u>Incident Image</u> (<font color="green">optional</font>): Attach any relevant images related to the incident.
*   <u>Incident Video</u> (<font color="green">optional</font>): Attach any relevant videos related to the incident.
*   <u>Incident Attachment</u> (<font color="green">optional</font>): Attach any relevant documents or files related to the incident.
*   <u>Asset Damage</u> (<font color="green">required</font>): The level of damage to the asset (Minor, Significant).
*   <u>Incident Status</u> (<font color="green">required</font>): The current status of the incident (New, In Progress, Resolved).
*   <u>Incident Comment</u> (<font color="green">optional</font>): Any comments or updates related to the incident.
*   <u>Incident Reporter</u> (<font color="green">required</font>): The name of the person reporting the incident.
*   <u>Incident Review Status</u> (<font color="green">optional</font>): The current status of the incident review.
*   <u>Asset Location Status</u> (<font color="green">optional</font>): The current status of the asset location.
*   <u>Incident Report Date</u> (<font color="green">optional</font>): The date the incident was reported.
*   <u>Incident Report Time</u> (<font color="green">optional</font>): The time the incident was reported.
*   <u>Incident Priority</u> (<font color="green">optional</font>): The level of priority of the incident (Low, Medium, High).
*   <u>Incident Attachment</u> (<font color="green">optional</font>): Attach any relevant documents or files related to the incident.
*   <u>Witness Comment</u> (<font color="green">optional</font>): Any comments from the witness.
*   <u>Asset Status</u> (<font color="green">optional</font>): The current status of the asset.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the "Incident Description" field.
*   If the incident has already been resolved, select "Resolved" as the status of the incident.
*   If there are any attachments or images, please ensure they are relevant and related to the incident.
*   If an incident is still in progress, do not select "Resolved" as the status of the incident.
*   Please enter your name as the reporter of the incident in the "Incident Reporter" field.
