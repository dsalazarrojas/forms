# River Overflow Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The River Overflow Incident Form is designed to collect information about incidents that occur in a river or a water body. It is used by response teams and incident commanders to report and track incidents such as overflows, flooding, or other significant water-related events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. Each field is essential for the efficient response and recovery efforts.
2. **Select "Yes" or "No" for boolean fields**, such as "incident_type" and "assigned_user".
3. **Choose the correct options** from the dropdown menus, such as "Low", "Medium", "High" for "severity", "Team A", "Team B", "Team C" for "response_team", and "Goal A", "Goal B", "Goal C" for "goals".
4. **Provide dates and times** in the "incident_date" and "incident_time" fields using the calendar picker.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): Describe the location of the incident.
* **Description** (`description`): Provide a brief description of what happened.
* **Severity** (`severity`): Select the level of severity of the incident (Low, Medium, High).
* **Affected People** (`affected_people`): Enter the number of people affected by the incident.
* **Affected Animals** (`affected_animals`): Enter the number of animals affected by the incident.
* **Affected Properties** (`affected_properties`): Enter the number of properties affected by the incident.
* **Affected Structures** (`affected_structures`): Enter the number of structures affected by the incident.
* **Response Team** (`response_team`): Select the response team(s) that responded to the incident (Team A, Team B, Team C).
* **Incident Image** (`incident_image`): Attach an image or document related to the incident.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): Enter any additional information about the incident.
* **Incident Video** (`incident_video`): Attach a video related to the incident.
* **Goals** (`goals`): Select the goals for the response and recovery efforts (Goal A, Goal B, Goal C).
* **Objectives** (`objectives`): Select the objectives for the response and recovery efforts (Objective A, Objective B, Objective C).
* **Actions** (`actions`): Select the actions taken during the incident (Action A, Action B, Action C).
* **Resources** (`resources`): Select the resources used during the incident (Resource A, Resource B, Resource C).
* **Status** (`status`): Select the status of the incident (Status A, Status B, Status C).
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any comments or notes about the incident.
* **Incident ID** (`incident_id`): Enter the incident ID.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Select the tool(s) assigned to the incident (Tool A, Tool B).
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`): Select if the incident is assigned to a user ("Yes" or "No").
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`): Select if the incident is a type ("Yes" or "No").
* **Incident Date End** (`incident_date_end`): Enter the end date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the time of the incident.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or response team leader for guidance.
