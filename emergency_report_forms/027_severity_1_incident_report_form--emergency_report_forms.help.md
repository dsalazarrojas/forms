# Severity 1 Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report Severity 1 incidents that require immediate attention from the team. It helps to track and manage these critical incidents and ensure they are resolved promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title of the incident in the "Incident Title" field.
2. Select the date and time the incident occurred in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields, respectively.
3. Describe the incident in detail in the "Incident Description" field.
4. Choose the priority of the incident from the options: high, medium, or low.
5. Select the teams or individuals who are responsible for responding to the incident from the options: it, operations, or management.
6. Indicate the severity of the incident from the options: critical, high, medium, or low.
7. Select the current status of the incident from the options: open, in progress, closed, or reopened.
8. Enter the name and ID of the person reporting the incident in the "Reporter" field.
9. Enter the name and ID of the person assigned to resolve the incident in the "Assignee" field.
10. Select the category of the incident in the "Category" field.
11. Add any additional tags or keywords related to the incident in the "Tags" field (optional).
12. Provide any comments or notes about the incident in the "Comments" field (optional).
13. Attach any relevant files or documents to the incident in the "Attachments" field (optional).
14. Indicate whether the incident has been resolved from the options: fixed or not fixed.
15. If the incident has been reopened, select the team or individual who reopened it from the options: it, operations, or management.
16. Enter the date and time the incident was closed in the "Closed At" field (optional).
17. Enter the date and time by which the incident was due to be resolved in the "Due By" field (optional).
18. Select the current priority of the incident from the options: high, medium, or low.
19. Enter the status history of the incident in the "Status History" field (optional).
20. Enter the tags history of the incident in the "Tags History" field (optional).
21. Enter the comments history of the incident in the "Comments History" field (optional).
22. Enter the attachments history of the incident in the "Attachments History" field (optional).
23. If the incident has been reopened, select the team or individual who reopened it from the options: it, operations, or management.
24. Enter the reason for closing the incident in the "Close Reason" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Title** (1, text, required): Enter a brief title of the incident.
* **Incident Date** (2, date, required): Select the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (3, time, optional): Select the time the incident occurred (optional).
* **Incident Description** (4, text, required): Describe the incident in detail.
* **Priority** (5, select_one, required): Choose the priority of the incident: high, medium, or low.
* **Respondent** (6, select_multiple, required): Select the teams or individuals responsible for responding to the incident: it, operations, or management.
* **Severity** (7, select_one, required): Indicate the severity of the incident: critical, high, medium, or low.
* **Status** (8, select_one, required): Select the current status of the incident: open, in progress, closed, or reopened.
* **Reporter** (9, text, required): Enter the name and ID of the person reporting the incident.
* **Assignee** (10, text, required): Enter the name and ID of the person assigned to resolve the incident.
* **Category** (11, text, required): Select the category of the incident.
* **Tags** (12, text, optional): Add any additional tags or keywords related to the incident (optional).
* **Comments** (13, text, optional): Provide any comments or notes about the incident (optional).
* **Attachments** (14, text, optional): Attach any relevant files or documents to the incident (optional).
* **Resolution** (15, select_one, required): Indicate whether the incident has been resolved: fixed or not fixed.
* **Reopened By** (16, select_multiple, optional): If the incident has been reopened, select the team or individual who reopened it: it, operations, or management.
* **Closed At** (17, date, optional): Enter the date and time the incident was closed (optional).
* **Due By** (18, date, optional): Enter the date and time by which the incident was due to be resolved (optional).
* **Priority** (19, text, required): Select the current priority of the incident: high, medium, or low.
* **Status History** (20, text, optional): Enter the status history of the incident.
* **Tags History** (21, text, optional): Enter the tags history of the incident.
* **Comments History** (22, text, optional): Enter the comments history of the incident.
* **Attachments History** (23, text, optional): Enter the attachments history of the incident.
* **Reopened By History** (24, select_multiple, optional): If the incident has been reopened, select the team or individual who reopened it: it, operations, or management.
* **Close Reason** (25, text, optional): Enter the reason for closing the incident (optional).
