# emergency_notification_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency notification form is used to report and document emergency incidents. It provides a structured format for collecting crucial information, such as the incident location, time, and severity, as well as the contact details of the reporting party and witness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the required fields**: Each field marked as "required" is necessary for the form to be considered complete.
2. **Enter accurate information**: Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
3. **Save and submit**: Once complete, click the "Submit" button to send the form to the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **name** (text, required)
Enter the name of the person or entity reporting the emergency.

### 2. **contact** (text, required)
Enter the contact information of the person or entity reporting the emergency.

### 3. **message** (text, optional)
Optional field for any additional information or comments.

### 4. **contact_email** (email, required)
Enter the email address of the person or entity reporting the emergency.

### 5. **contact_phone** (text, optional)
Enter the phone number of the person or entity reporting the emergency.

### 6. **emergency_contact** (text, required)
Enter the contact information of the person or entity involved in the emergency.

### 7. **location** (text, required)
Enter the location where the emergency occurred or is taking place.

### 8. **incident_date** (date, required)
Enter the date of the emergency incident.

### 9. **incident_time** (time, required)
Enter the time of the emergency incident.

### 10. **witness_details** (text, optional)
Provide any additional information about the witness or witnesses.

### 11. **reporting_officer** (text, required)
Enter the name and title of the officer reporting the emergency.

### 12. **officer_notes** (text, optional)
Enter any additional notes or comments from the reporting officer.

### 13. **officer_signature** (text, required)
Enter the signature of the reporting officer.

### 14. **emergency_status** (select_one, required)
Select the emergency status (Emergency, Non-Emergency, or Urgent).

### 15. **emergency_type** (select_multiple, required)
Select the type of emergency (Fire, Police, or Medical).

### 16. **emergency_details** (text, optional)
Enter any additional details about the emergency.

### 17. **witness_signature** (text, optional)
Enter the signature of the witness (if applicable).

### 18. **witness** (text, required)
Enter the name and contact information of the witness.

### 19. **contact_relationship** (select_multiple, required)
Select the relationship between the witness and the reporting party (Family Member, Colleague, or Stranger).

### 20. **witness_details** (text, optional)
Enter any additional information about the witness.

### 21. **reporting_date** (date, required)
Enter the date the emergency was reported.

### 22. **reported_by** (text, required)
Enter the name and title of the person or entity reporting the emergency.

### 23. **reported_by_title** (text, optional)
Enter the title of the person or entity reporting the emergency.

### 24. **reported_by_phone** (text, optional)
Enter the phone number of the person or entity reporting the emergency.

### 25. **reported_by_email** (email, optional)
Enter the email address of the person or entity reporting the emergency.
