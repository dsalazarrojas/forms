# Office Space Tour Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to capture information about office space tours. It helps us keep track of tour requests, assign them to specific individuals, and follow up on their status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as best you can.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Name", "Contact Email", and "Contact Phone" fields.
3. Choose a tour date and time.
4. Select the tour duration.
5. Choose the tour type.
6. Add any additional notes if needed.
7. If applicable, select the status and follow-up status of the tour.
8. Enter any follow-up date and time.
9. Add any follow-up notes if needed.
10. Select the next step for the tour.
11. Enter the tour schedule.
12. Enter the submitted form ID (this is automatically populated).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tour Request** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the tour request.
* **Contact Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it will be used in contact.
* **Contact Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Tour Date** (`5`, `date`, required): Choose the date for the tour.
* **Tour Time** (`6`, `time`, required): Choose the time for the tour.
* **Tour Duration** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the duration of the tour.
* **Tour Type** (`8`, `text`, required): Select the type of tour.
* **Notes** (`9`, `note`, required): Add any additional notes about the tour.
* **Assigned To** (`10`, `text`, required): Select who the tour is assigned to.
* **Status** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the tour (In Progress, Completed, Rejected, Cancelled).
* **Follow Up Status** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the follow-up status of the tour (In Progress, Completed, Rejected, Cancelled).
* **Follow Up Date** (`13`, `date`, required): Choose the follow-up date.
* **Follow Up Time** (`14`, `time`, required): Choose the follow-up time.
* **Follow Up Notes** (`15`, `note`, required): Add any follow-up notes.
* **Next Step** (`16`, `text`, required): Select the next step for the tour.
* **Tour Schedule** (`17`, `text`, required): Enter the tour schedule.
* **Submitted By** (`18`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On** (`19`, `date`, required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Assigned Tool** (`20`, `text`, required): Select the tool assigned to the tour.
* **Created By** (`21`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Created On** (`22`, `date`, required): Enter the date the form was created.
* **Updated On** (`23`, `date`, required): Enter the last updated date.
* **Updated By** (`24`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who updated the form.
* **Submitted Form ID** (`25`, `text`, required): Enter the submitted form ID.
