# School Tour Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Tour Appointment Form is a tool used to schedule appointments for school tours. It collects information from users to ensure a smooth and efficient tour process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the School Name field with the name of the school you wish to visit.
2. Select the type of tour you are interested in (Parent, Student, or Parent and Student).
3. Choose the start and end times for the tour.
4. Enter the email address and phone number of the school you are visiting.
5. Provide the name of the tour guide or administrator who will be leading the tour.
6. Select the tour capacity (number of people that can attend the tour).
7. Choose the frequency of the tour (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
8. Add any additional note or comments for the tour guide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name (2)**: Enter the name of the school you wish to visit.
* **Date (3)**: Select the date you would like to schedule the tour for.
* **Tour Type (4)**: Choose the type of tour you are interested in (Parent, Student, or Parent and Student).
* **Start Time (5)**: Select the start time of the tour.
* **End Time (6)**: Select the end time of the tour.
* **School Email (7)**: Enter the email address of the school you are visiting.
* **School Phone (8)**: Enter the phone number of the school you are visiting.
* **Tour Guide (9)**: Provide the name of the tour guide or administrator who will be leading the tour.
* **Tour Capacity (10)**: Select the number of people that can attend the tour.
* **Tour Frequency (11)**: Choose the frequency of the tour (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Note (12)**: Add any additional note or comments for the tour guide.
* **Submitted By (13)**: Enter your name or username.
* **Submitted At (14)**: The date and time the form was submitted.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Double-check your selections for tour type, tour capacity, and tour frequency to ensure they match your needs.
- If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to the tour administrator.
- This form is a tool to aid in tour scheduling, please provide complete and accurate information to ensure a smooth tour experience.
