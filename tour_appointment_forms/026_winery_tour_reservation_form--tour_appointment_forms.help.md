# Winery Tour Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to reserve a tour at a winery. It collects information from both the user and the winery to confirm the details of the reservation.

## How To Complete This Form

- To complete this form, start by filling out your information in the **User Input** field.
- Next, provide your tour details, such as date, time slot, and booking type, in the **Reservation Details** and **Time Slot** fields.
- Then, provide your contact information, including your email address, in the **User Email** field.
- If you have any special requests or notes, you can add them in the **User Notes** field.
- Confirm that you have read and agree to the **Winery Terms** by selecting "Yes" in the corresponding field.
- Finally, submit your form by clicking the **Submit** button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **User Input (Text)** 
  (User Input, Text, Optional): 
  This is where you can enter any additional information you'd like to provide about your tour reservation.
  
- **Winery Details (Text)** 
  (Winery Details, Text, Optional): 
  If there's any additional information you'd like to add about the winery, enter it here.
  
- **Reservation Details (Date)** 
  (Reservation Details, Date, Optional): 
  Enter the date of your tour in the format MM/DD/YY.
  
- **Time Slot (Time)** 
  (Time Slot, Time, Optional): 
  Select the time slot for your tour.
  
- **Booking Type (Select One)** 
  (Booking Type, Select One, Optional): 
  Select the type of booking you'd like to make (Standard, Private, Group).
  
- **Number of Guests (Number)** 
  (Number of Guests, Number, Optional): 
  Enter the number of guests attending your tour.
  
- **Winery Phone (Text)** 
  (Winery Phone, Text, Optional): 
  Enter the phone number of the winery.
  
- **Winery Email (Email)** 
  (Winery Email, Email, Optional): 
  Enter the email address of the winery.
  
- **User Email (Email)** 
  (User Email, Email, Optional): 
  Enter your email address.
  
- **Winery Notes (Note)** 
  (Winery Notes, Note, Optional): 
  Add any special notes about the winery here.
  
- **User Notes (Note)** 
  (User Notes, Note, Optional): 
  Add any special notes about yourself here.
  
- **Winery Confirmation (Select Multiple)** 
  (Winery Confirmation, Select Multiple, Optional): 
  Confirm that you have contacted the winery and agreed on the tour details.
  
- **User Confirmation (Select Multiple)** 
  (User Confirmation, Select Multiple, Optional): 
  Confirm that the winery has confirmed the tour details.
  
- **Winery Terms (Select Multiple)** 
  (Winery Terms, Select Multiple, Optional): 
  Confirm that you have read and agree to the winery's terms.
  
- **User Terms (Select Multiple)** 
  (User Terms, Select Multiple, Optional): 
  Confirm that you have read and agree to your own terms.
  
- **Submit (Select Multiple)** 
  (Submit, Select Multiple, Optional): 
  Click this button to submit your form.
 
**Tips**: Please fill out each field clearly and accurately to avoid any miscommunication. Ensure to double-check your contact information to avoid any mistakes.
