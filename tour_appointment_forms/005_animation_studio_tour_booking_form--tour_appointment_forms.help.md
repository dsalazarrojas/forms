# Animation Studio Tour Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animation Studio Tour Booking Form is designed to collect information from visitors to book a tour at the animation studio. This form is required to be completed for all tour bookings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  On the first page, provide the visit details.
2.  Move on to the second page and fill in the following fields:
   *   **Organizer full name** (`organizer_name`): This is the name of the person booking the tour.
   *   **Contact email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the person booking the tour, for booking confirmation purposes.
   *   **Contact phone number** (`contact_phone`): This is an optional field for urgent changes or contact outside of email.
3.  Move on to the third page and fill in:
   *   **Group size** (`group_size`): Enter the number of attendees expected for the tour.
   *   **Preferred tour date** (`preferred_date`): Choose a preferred date for the tour.
   *   **Preferred tour time** (`preferred_time`): This field is optional, and you can choose a preferred time for the tour if desired.
4.  On the last page, fill in:
   *   **Special requests or accessibility needs** (`special_requests`): List any special requests or accommodations required for the tour.
   *   **Consent to photography** (`consent_photos`): Choose whether the animation studio can take photos during the tour.
   *   **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional information the animation studio should know.
5.  Click "Submit" to complete your tour booking request.
