# Facility Tour Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Tour Request Form is designed to collect information from users who want to visit a facility. This form will help us understand your needs and requirements for the tour.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details in the User Information section.
2. Select the date you would like to visit the facility.
3. Choose a suitable time for the tour.
4. Enter the facility's details, including name, address, and contact information.
5. Specify the group name and size, if applicable.
6. Mention any special requirements or notes about the tour.
7. Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
8. Submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (Page 1)** (`user_info`, text, Optional):
	+ Enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you regarding your tour request.
* **Date (Page 2)** (`date`, date, Optional):
	+ Choose a date for the facility tour. If you don't specify a date, we'll get back to you with available options.
* **Time (Page 3)** (`time`, time, Optional):
	+ Select a time slot for the tour that suits you. Please note that time slots might be limited based on availability.
* **Facility Information (Page 4)** (`facility_info`, text, Optional):
	+ Provide any relevant information about the facility you'd like to visit, such as its purpose or notable features.
* **Facility Name (Page 5)** (`facility_name`, text, Optional):
	+ Enter the official name of the facility you're interested in.
* **Facility Address (Page 6)** (`facility_address`, text, Optional):
	+ Enter the address of the facility, which will help us locate it.
* **Facility Contact (Page 7)** (`facility_contact`, text, Optional):
	+ Provide the contact information of the facility's representative or administrator.
* **Group Name (Page 8)** (`group_name`, text, Optional):
	+ If you're part of a group, enter the name of your group to help us identify your visit as a group.
* **Group Size (Page 9)** (`group_size`, number, Optional):
	+ If you're part of a group, indicate the number of people in your group.
* **Tour Requirements (Page 10)** (`tour_requirements`, text, Optional):
	+ Mention any specific requirements or requests you have for the tour, such as accessibility needs or special equipment.
* **Facility Notes (Page 11)** (`facility_notes`, text, Optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes about the facility you'd like to share.
* **User Agreement (Page 12)** (`user_agreement`, select_one, Optional):
	+ Please read and agree to the terms and conditions to proceed with your tour request.
* **Submit (Page 13)** (`submit`, text, Optional):
	+ Once you've filled out all the necessary fields, click the submit button to send your tour request.

**Tips**

* Please make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you've provided all necessary information to facilitate a smooth tour experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the facility management team.

Note: This help guide is for internal use only and is subject to change based on the facility's specific needs and requirements.
