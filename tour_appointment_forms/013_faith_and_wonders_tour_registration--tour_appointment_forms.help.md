# faith_and_wonders_tour_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information for a tour or excursion. It includes fields for personal details, emergency contact, and participant consent for different aspects of the tour.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the participant details section, including any required fields.
2. Answer the emergency contact questions, indicating whether an emergency contact is required.
3. Answer the tour details and dates questions, if applicable.
4. Indicate your consent for different aspects of the tour, which will be presented in a series of text fields.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required): Enter your name and other required personal details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select multiple, required): Indicate whether an emergency contact is required for the tour.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, select multiple, optional): Not all fields with an option, this field is optional.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Enter your preferred tour date.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, select multiple, required): Indicate whether you need help with emergency contact information.
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, select multiple, required): Indicate whether you need help with participant information.
* **Participant Consent 1** (`participant_consent`, text, required): Provide your consent for the first aspect of the tour.
* **Tour Details** (`tour_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the tour, if necessary.
* **Tour Dates** (`tour_dates`, text, optional): Enter the tour dates, if they are not provided by the tour operator.
* **Tour Type** (`tour_type`, select multiple, required): Indicate the type of tour you are joining.
* **Participant Consent 2**-**15** (`participant_consent_2`-`participant_consent_15`, text, optional): Provide your consent for the remaining aspects of the tour.
* **Participant Consent 16-21** (`participant_consent_16`-`participant_consent_21`, text, optional): Note: These fields do not have a label, which might indicate they are not relevant or were left out of the design.
