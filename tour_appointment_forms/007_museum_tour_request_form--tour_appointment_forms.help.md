# Museum Tour Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Museum Tour Request Form is used to gather information from individuals or groups interested in taking a tour of the museum. It allows museum staff to track and process these requests, ensuring accurate and timely communication with the requesters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with the required details:
   - **User Name**: Enter your name, as the person or representative of the group making the request.
   - **Tour Date**: Select the desired date for the tour.
   - **Tour Time**: Choose the preferred time slot for the tour.
   - **School Name**: If you're from a school or educational institution, provide the name to help the museum cater to your needs.
   - **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email to ensure smooth communication.
   - **Contact Phone Number**: Your phone number for any additional queries.
   - **Tour Request Details**: Briefly describe the purpose of your visit (e.g., educational group, field trip, family visit).
   - **Tour Requester**: Indicate your relationship with the tour group (Parent, Teacher, Student).
   - **Tour Requester Contact**: If you're representing someone else, provide their name or details.
   - **Tour Booking Request**: Choose whether you're requesting a new booking or confirming an existing one.
   - **Tour Booking Confirmation**: If applicable, confirm that a booking has been made.
   - **Tour Cancel Reason**: If you need to cancel, provide a reason for cancellation.
   - **Additional Comments**: Any further details or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Your name or the name of the representative for the tour group.
* **Tour Date**: Choose a date for the tour within the available schedule provided by the museum.
* **Tour Time**: Pick a time slot that suits your group's schedule and preference.
* **School Name**: If you're representing a school or educational institution, provide their name to help the museum cater to your needs.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email to enable smooth communication between you and the museum staff.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Your phone number for any additional queries or questions from the museum staff.
* **Tour Request Details**: Briefly describe the purpose of your tour visit (e.g., educational group, field trip, family visit).
* **Tour Requester**: Choose how you're related to the tour group (Parent, Teacher, Student).
* **Tour Requester Contact**: If you're representing someone else, provide their name or contact details.
* **Tour Booking Request**: Decide whether you're making a new booking request or confirming an existing one.
* **Tour Booking Confirmation**: Confirm whether a booking has been made for your tour.
* **Tour Cancel Reason**: If applicable, select a reason for cancellation.
* **Additional Comments**: Any further details or requests you might have for the tour.

Remember, the museum staff will review and process your form submission to ensure they can provide you with the most suitable tour experience.
