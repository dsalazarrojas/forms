# promotional_tour_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The promotional tour reservation form is designed to collect information for promotional tours reservations, allowing users to book and manage promotional tours efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the start date of the promotional tour.
2. Enter the end date of the promotional tour.
3. Enter the customer's name.
4. Enter the customer's email address.
5. Enter the tour ID.
6. Select the promotional tour type (Yes/No).
7. Enter the promotional tour price.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Date (`start_date`, date, required: false):**
Enter the date you want the promotional tour to begin.
* **End Date (`end_date`, date, required: false):**
Enter the date the promotional tour will end.
* **Customer Name (`customer_name`, text, required: false):**
Enter the name of the customer booking the promotional tour.
* **Customer Email (`customer_email`, email, required: false):**
Enter the email address of the customer booking the promotional tour.
* **Tour ID (`tour_id`, number, required: false):**
Enter the ID number for the promotional tour.
* **Promotional Tour Type (`promotional_tour_type`, select_one, required: false):**
Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the promotional tour is a promotional tour or not.
* **Promotional Tour Price (`promotional_tour_price`, number, required: false):**
Enter the price of the promotional tour.
