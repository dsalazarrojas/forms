# Brewery Tour Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a tour at a brewery. Please fill it out to provide the necessary information for us to schedule a tour that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
- Make sure to choose the correct options for your group's needs and preferences.
- If you have any additional questions or comments, use the "Additional Message or Questions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`inquiry_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`inquiry_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number** (`inquiry_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization or Company** (`organization`, `text`, optional): If you're representing a business or group, enter their name here.
* **Type of Inquiry** (`inquiry_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of tour you're interested in (e.g. Group brewery tour, Private tour booking, etc.).
* **Expected Group Size** (`group_size`, `number`, required): Enter the number of people in your group.
* **Preferred Tour Dates** (`preferred_dates`, `text`, required): Enter the dates that work best for your tour.
* **Are Your Dates Flexible** (`date_flexibility`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how flexible you are with your dates.
* **Preferred Tour Time** (`preferred_time`, `select_one`, required): Choose the time of day you prefer for your tour.
* **Preferred Tour Duration** (`tour_duration`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the length of your tour.
* **Tour Type Interest** (`tour_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of tour that interests you (e.g. Standard brewery tour, Premium tasting experience, etc.).
* **Are You Interested in Tastings** (`tasting_options`, `select_one`, optional): If you're interested in beer tastings, choose "Yes, include tastings".
* **Budget Range Per Person** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your budget expectation for the tour.
* **Dietary Restrictions in Group** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, optional): If your group has any dietary restrictions, choose from the options provided.
* **Special Accommodations or Needs** (`special_accommodations`, `text`, optional): If your group has any special needs or requirements, enter them here.
* **Event Occasion** (`event_occasion`, `select_one`, optional): If this tour is for a special event, choose the occasion.
* **Group Beer Experience Level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your group's beer experience level.
* **Specific Interests or Focus Areas** (`specific_interests`, `text`, optional): If you have any specific interests or focus areas for the tour, enter them here.
* **Additional Activities Interest** (`additional_activities`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you're interested in any additional activities, choose from the options provided.
* **Best Time to Contact You** (`preferred_contact_time`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the best time for us to contact you.
* **Additional Message or Questions** (`additional_message`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional questions or comments, use this field.
