# historic tour visitor information form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about historic tours, including their titles, dates, start and end times, visitor counts, visitor types, contact details, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the historic tour title in the "Historic Tour Title" field.
2. Select the tour date using the date picker or enter the date manually.
3. Enter the start and end times of the tour in the corresponding fields.
4. Specify the number of visitors expected for the tour.
5. Select the type of visitor (e.g., Historic Tour, Self Guided, Guided, Group).
6. Fill in the contact information, including first name, last name, email, and phone number.
7. Provide the visitor comments and notes (if applicable).
8. Select the tour operators and museums participating in the tour.
9. Choose the heritage sites included in the tour.
10. Select the languages spoken by the tour guides.
11. Add any special requests or emergency contact information.
12. Review and agree to the visitor agreement.
13. Choose the payment method and enter the payment details.
14. Enter the payment included and terms (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Historic Tour Title** (id: 1, text, required: false): Enter the title of the historic tour in this field.
* **Tour Date** (id: 2, date, required: true): Select or enter the date of the tour using the date picker or type it manually.
* **Start Time** (id: 3, time, required: true): Enter the start time of the tour.
* **End Time** (id: 4, time, required: true): Enter the end time of the tour.
* **Visitor Count** (id: 5, number, required: true): Enter the expected number of visitors.
* **Visitor Type** (id: 6, select_one, required: true): Choose the type of visitor (e.g., Historic Tour, Self Guided, Guided, Group).
* **Contact First Name** (id: 7, text, required: true): Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Last Name** (id: 8, text, required: true): Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (id: 9, email, required: true): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (id: 10, text, required: true): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Visitor Comments** (id: 11, note, required: false): Provide any comments or feedback from the visitors.
* **Visitor Notes** (id: 12, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the tour.
* **Tour Operators** (id: 13, select_multiple, required: true): Choose the tour operators participating in the tour.
* **Museum** (id: 14, select_multiple, required: true): Select the museums included in the tour.
* **Heritage Sites** (id: 15, select_multiple, required: true): Choose the heritage sites participating in the tour.
* **Languages** (id: 16, select_multiple, required: true): Select the languages spoken by the tour guides.
* **Special Requests** (id: 17, text, required: false): Add any special requests or instructions for the tour.
* **Emergency Contact** (id: 18, text, required: true): Enter the emergency contact information.
* **Relationship** (id: 19, text, required: true): Specify the relationship of the emergency contact person.
* **Visitor Agreement** (id: 20, select_one, required: true): Review and agree to the visitor agreement.
* **Payment Method** (id: 21, select_one, required: true): Choose the payment method (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Details** (id: 22, text, required: true): Enter the payment details (e.g., amount, method).
* **Payment Included** (id: 23, text, required: true): Specify what is included in the payment (e.g., tour, guide, amenities).
* **Payment Terms** (id: 24, text, required: false): Add any payment terms or conditions.
* **Notes** (id: 25, text, required: false): Provide any additional notes about the tour.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is correct.
* Use the date picker for date fields to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask for clarification before submitting.
