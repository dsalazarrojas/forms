# Aquarium Tour Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather contact information and tour preferences from users who are interested in booking a tour at the aquarium. This form helps us understand the users' needs and preferences to provide a personalized tour experience.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out the form completely and accurately, making sure to include all required fields.
- Select the appropriate options for "Inquiry Type" and "Preferred Tour Time" based on your needs.
- Enter your name, email, and phone number correctly.
- Enter the preferred date for the tour.
- Enter the number of visitors for the tour.
- If applicable, specify any special interests or accommodations needed.
- If you have any additional questions or comments, enter them in the "Additional Questions or Comments" field.
- Review your submission before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Required): This field is where you should enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address where we can contact you with tour information and updates.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you with tour information and updates.
* **Inquiry Type** (Required): Choose from the options provided to specify the type of tour you're interested in, such as Individual Tour, Group Tour, or School Field Trip.
* **Preferred Tour Date** (Required): Enter the date you would like to take the tour on.
* **Preferred Tour Time** (Required): Select the time slot that best suits your schedule for the tour.
* **Number of Visitors** (Required): Enter the number of people who will be attending the tour.
* **Predominant Age Group** (Optional): If you're leading a group with a mix of age groups, select "Mixed". Otherwise, choose the predominant age group of your group.
* **Special Interests or Focus** (Optional): Specify any specific interests or focus areas for your tour.
* **Any Special Accommodations Needed** (Optional): Mention any special requirements or needs you have for your tour experience.
* **Additional Questions or Comments** (Optional): Enter any additional information or questions you may have about the tour.

**Tips**

- Make sure to review your submission before sending it to ensure all fields are filled out accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns after filling out the form, contact us directly for support.
