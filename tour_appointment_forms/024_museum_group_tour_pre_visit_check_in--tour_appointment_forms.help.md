# Museum Group Tour Pre Visit Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from tour leaders and group organizers to ensure a smooth and enjoyable visit experience for both the tour group and the museum staff.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out all required fields**: This form requires you to fill out all the fields marked as "Required" for successful submission.
2. **Enter group details**: In the "Group Details" field, provide a brief description of your tour group, including the number of people attending and the type of tour you are leading.
3. **Enter tour leader information**: In the "Tour Leader" section, provide your personal details including first name, last name, email, and phone number.
4. **Enter group arrival and departure times**: Choose the date and time of your tour group's arrival and departure from the calendar or clock picker.
5. **Specify special requests**: If your group has any special requests or needs, please enter them in the "Special Requests" field.
6. **Enter contact information for the tour requestor**: In the "Tour Requestor" section, provide the contact information of the person who requested the tour, including their name, email, phone number, and any additional details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Group Details** (`group_details`, text, Required): A brief description of your tour group, including the number of people attending and the type of tour you are leading.
* **Tour Leader First Name** (`tour_leader_first_name`, text, Required): Enter your first name as the tour leader.
* **Tour Leader Last Name** (`tour_leader_last_name`, text, Required): Enter your last name as the tour leader.
* **Tour Leader Email** (`tour_leader_email`, email, Required): Enter your email address as the tour leader.
* **Tour Leader Phone** (`tour_leader_phone`, text, Required): Enter your phone number as the tour leader.
* **Group Leader Phone** (`group_leader_phone`, text, Optional): If applicable, enter the phone number of the group leader. (Note: This field is not required but can be useful for communication.)
* **Group Details Date** (`group_details_date`, date, Required): Choose the date of your tour group's visit from the calendar.
* **Tour Leader Time** (`tour_leader_time`, time, Required): Choose the time of your tour group's arrival from the clock picker.
* **Group Size** (`group_size`, number, Required): Enter the number of people in your tour group.
* **Group Tour Type** (`group_tour_type`, select_one, Required): Select the type of tour you are leading (Guided Tour, Self-Guided Tour, Group Tour).
* **Group Tour Type Other** (`group_tour_type_other`, text, Optional): If your tour type is not listed, provide a brief description in this field.
* **Tour Arrival Date** (`tour_arrival_date`, date, Required): Choose the date of your tour group's arrival from the calendar.
* **Tour Arrival Time** (`tour_arrival_time`, time, Required): Choose the time of your tour group's arrival from the clock picker.
* **Tour Departure Date** (`tour_departure_date`, date, Required): Choose the date of your tour group's departure from the calendar.
* **Tour Departure Time** (`tour_departure_time`, time, Required): Choose the time of your tour group's departure from the clock picker.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, Optional): If your group has any special requests or needs, enter them in this field.
* **Group Notes** (`group_notes`, text, Optional): Enter any additional notes about your tour group.
* **Tour Requestor First Name** (`tour_requestors_first_name`, text, Required): Enter the first name of the person who requested the tour.
* **Tour Requestor Last Name** (`tour_requestors_last_name`, text, Required): Enter the last name of the person who requested the tour.
* **Tour Requestor Email** (`tour_requestors_email`, email, Required): Enter the email address of the person who requested the tour.
* **Tour Requestor Phone** (`tour_requestors_phone`, text, Required): Enter the phone number of the person who requested the tour.
* **Tour Requestor Company** (`tour_requestors_company`, text, Optional): Enter the company of the person who requested the tour (if applicable).
* **Tour Requestor Title** (`tour_requestors_title`, text, Optional): Enter the title of the person who requested the tour (if applicable).
* **Tour Requestor Department** (`tour_requestors_department`, text, Optional): Enter the department of the person who requested the tour (if applicable).
* **Tour Requestor Comments** (`tour_requestors_comments`, text, Optional): Enter any additional comments about the person who requested the tour.
