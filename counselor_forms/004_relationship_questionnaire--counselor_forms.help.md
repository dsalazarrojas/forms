# Relationship Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about relationships, focusing on feelings experienced and characteristics of the relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all required questions.
2. For sections with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Relationship Feelings** (`relationship_feelings`, text, optional): Describe your feelings in your relationship.
* **Partner's Interests** (`partner_interests`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether your partner has interests that align with or differ from yours.
* **Relationship Type** (`relationship_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of relationship you have with your partner (Romantic, Family, Friend).
* **Partner's Satisfaction** (`partner_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to how satisfied your partner is with the relationship.
* **Relationship Length** (`relationship_length`, number, required): Enter the length of your relationship in years.
* **Conflict Type** (`conflict_type`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you experience conflicts in your relationship.
* **Feelings Type** (`feelings_type`, select_one, required): Choose how you usually feel in your relationship (Happy, Sad, Neutral).
* **Feelings Frequency** (`feelings_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to how often you experience these feelings in your relationship.
* **Feelings Intensity** (`feelings_intensity`, number, optional): Rate how intense you experience these feelings.
* **Relationship Duration** (`relationship_duration`, time, required): Enter the duration of your relationship in the format "X years, X months".
* **Feelings Description** (`feelings_description`, text, optional): Describe your feelings in your relationship.
* **Feelings Important** (`feelings_important`, select_one, required): Choose how important these feelings are to you (Very Important, Not Important).
* **Partner's Feelings** (`partner_feelings`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether your partner usually experiences these feelings.
* **Relationship Quality** (`relationship_quality`, select_one, optional): Evaluate the quality of your relationship (Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of your relationship (Active, Inactive).
* **Feelings Satisfied** (`feelings_satisfied`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to how satisfied you are with the relationship.
