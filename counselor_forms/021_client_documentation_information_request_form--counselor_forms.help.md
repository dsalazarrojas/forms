# Client Documentation Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Documentation Information Request Form is used to gather information from clients requesting specific documents, such as account statements, tax documents, or treatment records. This form helps us understand their needs and provide the required documents efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Ensure that you provide accurate information in all the fields.
3. Review each section carefully to ensure that you have included all the necessary details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required):
	+ Enter your legal first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required):
	+ Enter your legal last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required):
	+ Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required):
	+ Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Client ID or Account Number** (5, text, optional):
	+ If you have a client ID or account number, enter it here.
* **Date of Birth** (6, date, required):
	+ Enter your date of birth for verification purposes.
* **Type of Documentation Requested** (7, select_one, required):
	+ Select the type of document you are requesting (e.g., Account Statements, Tax Documents, etc.).
* **Document Period Start Date** (8, date, required):
	+ Enter the start date of the document period you are requesting.
* **Document Period End Date** (9, date, required):
	+ Enter the end date of the document period you are requesting.
* **Specific Documents Needed** (10, text, required):
	+ List the specific documents you are requesting.
* **Purpose of Request** (11, select_one, required):
	+ Select the purpose of your request (e.g., Personal Records, Tax Preparation, etc.).
* **Preferred Delivery Method** (12, select_one, required):
	+ Choose how you would like to receive the documents (e.g., Email, Secure Portal, etc.).
* **Mailing Address** (13, text, optional):
	+ If you choose to receive the documents by mail, enter your mailing address here.
* **Urgency Level** (14, select_one, required):
	+ Select how soon you need the documents (e.g., Standard, Expedited, etc.).
* **I Understand There May Be Fees for This Request** (15, select_one, required):
	+ Acknowledge that you understand there may be fees associated with this request.
* **I Authorize the Release of These Documents to Me** (16, select_one, required):
	+ Confirm that you authorize the release of these documents to yourself.
* **Are Documents Being Released to a Third Party?** (17, select_one, required):
	+ Indicate if the documents are being released to a third party.
* **Third Party Name** (18, text, optional):
	+ If you selected "Yes" for the above question, enter the name of the third party.
* **Third Party Relationship** (19, text, optional):
	+ Describe the relationship between you and the third party.
* **I Confirm All Information Is Accurate** (20, select_one, required):
	+ Confirm that you have provided accurate information.
* **Request Date** (21, date, required):
	+ Enter the date you are submitting this request.
* **Electronic Signature** (22, text, required):
	+ Type your full name for electronic signature purposes.
