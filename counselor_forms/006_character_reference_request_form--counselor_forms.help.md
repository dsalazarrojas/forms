# Character Reference Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help gather information about a character from a person who has a relationship with them. This form will be used for official character references, which can be submitted to various organizations or individuals for evaluation and consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, ensure you have the character's permission to submit this form. The following steps will guide you through the process:

1.  Enter your name and email address as the requester.
2.  Select the purpose of the reference, such as employment or educational applications.
3.  Enter the name of the organization or institution where the reference will be sent.
4.  Provide the contact person's name, if applicable.
5.  Specify the submission deadline for the reference.
6.  Describe your relationship with the character, such as former supervisor or friend.
7.  Select the duration of your relationship with the character.
8.  Choose relevant work qualities and character qualities that you have observed in the character.
9.  If applicable, provide specific examples of the character's strengths.
10.  Indicate whether you prefer the reference to be delivered in a written letter, email, or other format.
11.  Provide any additional context or information about the character.
12.  Grant permission for follow-up questions or contact from the organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Requester Name (`requester_name`, text, required)
Your full name as the requester of the character reference.
### Email Address (`requester_email`, email, required)
Your contact email address, where the character reference will be sent.
### Phone Number (`requester_phone`, text, optional)
Your phone number, where the character reference can be reached.
### Purpose of Reference (`reference_purpose`, select_one, required)
Select the reason for the character reference, such as employment or educational applications.
### Organization Name (`organization_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the organization or institution where the reference will be sent.
### Contact Person Name (`organization_contact`, text, optional)
Enter the name of the contact person at the organization.
### Submission Deadline (`submission_deadline`, date, required)
Enter the deadline for submitting the character reference.
### Relationship Type (`relationship_type`, select_one, required)
Select how you know the character, such as former supervisor or friend.
### Relationship Duration (`relationship_duration`, select_one, required)
Enter the duration of your relationship with the character.
### Work Qualities (`work_qualities`, select_multiple, optional)
Select relevant work qualities you have observed in the character, such as reliability or teamwork.
### Character Qualities (`character_qualities`, select_multiple, optional)
Select character qualities you have observed in the character, such as honesty or compassion.
### Specific Examples (`specific_examples`, select_one, required)
Indicate whether you would like to provide specific examples of the character's strengths.
### Preferred Reference Format (`reference_format`, select_one, required)
Select the preferred format for the character reference, such as written letter or email.
### Additional Context (`additional_context`, text, optional)
Enter any additional information or context about the character.
### May We Follow Up with Questions (`follow_up_permission`, select_one, required)
Grant permission for follow-up questions or contact from the organization.

Note: The fields marked as optional may still be required in specific situations, such as when providing a contact number or additional context.
