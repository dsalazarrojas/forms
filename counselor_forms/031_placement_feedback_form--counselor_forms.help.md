## Placement Feedback Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the placement process from participants and assessors. It helps us understand their experience and satisfaction with the placement process. Please fill out this form to provide your honest feedback and suggestions on how we can improve our services.

### How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter your name and date of the placement in the respective fields.
2. Select your placement counselor and rate your overall satisfaction with the placement process.
3. Provide feedback on your experience, including any suggestions for improvement.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the placement process again, this time as an assessor.
5. Provide comments on the placement process if you have any.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name to help us identify you.
* **Date of Placement** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of your placement.
* **Placement Counselor** (Select One, Optional): Select the counselor who assisted you with the placement process.
* **Feedback** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from the options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor) to indicate your level of satisfaction with the placement process.
* **Suggestions** (Text, Optional): Provide any suggestions for improving our services.
* **Participant Rating** (Select One, Optional): Rate your overall satisfaction as a participant with the placement process.
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any comments or thoughts you have about the placement process.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Select One, Optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the placement process as a participant.
* **Assessor Tool** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the tool used by the assessor.
* **Assessor Rating** (Select One, Optional): Rate the assessor's performance with the placement process.
* **Assessor Comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any comments or thoughts you have about the assessor's performance.

### Tips
* Please be honest and concise in your feedback.
* Provide specific examples or examples of what worked well or poorly for you.
* If you have any suggestions, please include them in the "Suggestions" field.
* If you are an assessor, please rate your own performance with the placement process and provide comments if necessary.
* If you are a participant, please rate your overall satisfaction with the placement process and provide any comments or thoughts you have about the process.

---

This guide is intended to be a helpful resource for participants and assessors to complete the Placement Feedback Form. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
