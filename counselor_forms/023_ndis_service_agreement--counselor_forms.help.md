# NDIS Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NDIS Service Agreement form is a crucial document that outlines the terms and conditions of the service between a participant and their provider. This form helps ensure that both parties are on the same page and understand their responsibilities and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the terms and conditions before proceeding.
- Enter your participant name in the **Participant Name** field.
- Enter your provider name in the **Provider Name** field.
- Provide the start date and start time for the session in the **Start Date** and **Start Time** fields, respectively.
- Enter the end date and end time for the session in the **End Date** and **End Time** fields, respectively. Please note that **End Time** is optional as it's not a required field.
- Enter the session duration in minutes in the **Session Duration** field. This is an optional field.
- If you have any participant notes, please enter them in the **Participant Notes** field.
- If you have any provider notes, please enter them in the **Provider Notes** field. Both of these fields are optional.
- Select either **I accept the terms and conditions** or **I do not accept the terms and conditions** in the **Service Agreement** field. This is a required field, ensuring both parties are in agreement.
- Read and acknowledge the **Terms and Conditions** by entering them in the **Terms and Conditions** field. This is a required field.
- Sign with your digital signature in the **Signature** field to confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions. This is a required field.
- Finally, the **Provider Signature** field is for the provider to sign and confirm their understanding of the terms and conditions. This is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as per your identification documents.
* **Provider Name**: Enter your name as per your provider identification documents.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date of the start of the session.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the session.
* **End Date**: Enter the date of the end of the session.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the session. This field is optional, as session duration can be determined by other means.
* **Session Duration**: Enter the duration of the session in minutes. This field is optional.
* **Participant Notes**: Enter any notes relevant to your session with the participant. This field is optional.
* **Provider Notes**: Enter any notes relevant to your session with the participant. This field is optional.
* **Service Agreement**: Select whether you accept or do not accept the terms and conditions of the service agreement.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and enter the terms and conditions of the service agreement. This field is for your acknowledgement.
* **Signature**: Sign with your digital signature to confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions. This is a required field for you.
* **Provider Signature**: Sign with your digital signature to confirm your understanding and agreement with the terms and conditions. This is a required field for the provider.
