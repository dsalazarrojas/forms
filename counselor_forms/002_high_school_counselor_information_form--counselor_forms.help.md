# High School Counselor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High School Counselor Information Form is used by school administrators to collect information from high school counselors, allowing us to better understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form and ensure you are authorized to fill it out.
2. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
7. Select your grade level from the drop-down menu in the "Grade Level" field.
8. Enter any relevant notes in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" field.
10. Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the "Counselor Name" field.
11. Enter your title as you would like it to be displayed in the "Counselor Title" field.
12. Enter the name of your school in the "School" field.
13. Enter the name of your department in the "Department" field.
14. Enter your room number in the "Room Number" field.
15. Enter your email address in the "Counselor Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: This field is for your first name. Please enter the name that you prefer to be called.
* **Last name**: This field is for your last name. Please enter the name that you prefer to be called.
* **Email**: This field is for your email address. Please enter the email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: This field is for your phone number. Please enter the number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth**: This field is for your date of birth. Please enter the date in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Grade Level**: Select the grade level that you are currently working with.
* **Notes**: This field is for any additional information that you would like to share with us.
* **Additional Notes**: This field is for any additional information that you would like to share with us.
* **Counselor Name**: This field is for the name that you would like us to display when addressing you.
* **Counselor Title**: This field is for the title that you would like us to display when addressing you.
* **School**: This field is for the name of your school.
* **Department**: This field is for the name of your department.
* **Room Number**: This field is for your room number.
* **Counselor Email**: This field is for your email address where you can be reached.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are using the correct format for your date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to receive any relevant communications.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
