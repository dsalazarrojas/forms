# Shadowing Sign Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shadowing Sign Off Form is a document used to record and document an individual's shadowing experience. It helps in maintaining accurate and up-to-date records of shadowing hours, hours worked, hours earned, and hours paid, as well as supervisor and trainee signatures. This form is essential for tracking and managing shadowing experiences effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand each field's purpose and requirements.
2. Ensure that you have all necessary information before proceeding.
3. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
4. Sign the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shadowing Experience**: This field is used to enter a brief description of the shadowing experience. Provide a concise summary of the experience, including the shadowing site and any notable events or interactions.
* **Shadowing Site**: Enter the name or location of the shadowing site where the experience took place.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the shadowing experience. This is the date when you began shadowing at the site.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the shadowing experience. This is the date when you completed shadowing at the site.
* **Hours Worked**: Enter the total number of hours worked during the shadowing experience.
* **Hours Earned**: Enter the total number of hours earned during the shadowing experience. This includes any additional hours earned beyond the regular work hours.
* **Hours Paid**: Enter the total number of hours paid for the shadowing experience.
* **Supervisor Signature**: A supervisor's signature is required to verify the accuracy of the shadowing experience. Please obtain the supervisor's signature at the end of the shadowing experience.
* **Trainee Signature**: Enter your own signature to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely to maintain accurate records.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or administrator for assistance.
