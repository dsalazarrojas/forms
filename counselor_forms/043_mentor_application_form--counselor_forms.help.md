# Mentor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mentor Application Form is designed to collect information from potential mentors for a specific program or initiative. This form helps to gather relevant details about the mentor's availability, skills, and preferences, making it easier for organizers to pair them with mentees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Mentor Name" and "Email" fields respectively.
2. Check your availability by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Availability" field.
3. Choose your preferred skills by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Skills" field.
4. Provide your available dates and times in the "Available Dates" and "Available Times" fields.
5. Enter your emails and phone numbers to repeat with in the "Emails to Repeat" and "Phones to Repeat" fields.
6. Add any additional notes in the "Mentor Notes", "Additional Notes", and "Mentor Form Note 3" fields.
7. Select your next available date and time in the "Next Date" and "Next Time" fields.
8. Choose "Yes" or "No" for "Mentor Form Select Multiple 2" and "Mentor Form Select One 2" fields.
9. Enter your email and phone number in the "Mentor Form Email 2" and "Mentor Form Phone 2" fields.
10. Add additional notes in the "Mentor Form Note 3" field.
11. Select your available date and time in the "Mentor Form Date 3" and "Mentor Form Time 3" fields.
