# Couples Counseling Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect necessary information for couples counseling appointments. It helps us understand your needs and preferences for the therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and answer all required questions.
2. Provide accurate and honest answers to help us better understand your situation.
3. If you're unsure about any question or require clarification, please ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (1)** (`text`, optional): Enter your name and any additional contact information you'd like to share.
* **Session Details (2)** (`text`, optional): Write a brief description of what you'd like to work on during the sessions.
* **Schedule Session (3)** (`text`, optional): If you have a preferred schedule for the sessions, please include it here.
* **Client Contact (4)** (`text`, required): Provide your emergency contact information, so we can reach you if needed.
* **Client Notes (5)** (`text`, optional): Add any important notes about your situation or preferences.
* **Therapist Notes (6)** (`text`, optional): If you'd like to share any background information or context about you or your partner, please do so here.
* **Appointment Date (7)** (`date`, optional): If you have a preferred appointment date, please let us know.
* **Appointment Time (8)** (`time`, optional): Choose an available appointment time for the session.
* **Session Duration (9)** (`number`, required): Specify the number of sessions you'd like to have per week.
* **Session Frequency (10)** (`number`, required): Decide how many sessions you want to have per week.
* **Appointment Type (11)** (`select_one`, required): Choose between "Yes" or "No" to indicate if this is an initial appointment.
* **Payment Method (12)** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate how you'd like to pay for the sessions.
* **Session Location (13)** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose between "Yes" or "No" for each of the available locations for the sessions.
* **Emergency Contact Info (14)** (`text`, optional): Provide contact information for an emergency contact.
* **Payment Terms (15)** (`text`, optional): If you'd like to discuss payment terms, please provide details here.

Please answer all required questions and provide accurate information to ensure a smooth and effective therapy process.
