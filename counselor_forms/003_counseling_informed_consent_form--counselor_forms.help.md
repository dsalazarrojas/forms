# Counseling Informed Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Counseling Informed Consent Form is a crucial document that outlines the essential information required for counseling services. It helps establish a clear understanding between the client and counselor, ensuring that both parties are aware of the expectations, boundaries, and services offered.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, fill in your contact details, including your email and phone number.
2. Provide details about your current situation and concerns in the counseling details section.
3. If applicable, select the frequency of sessions you prefer (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
4. Indicate how you prefer to pay for sessions.
5. If you have insurance, provide the provider name.
6. Specify your preferred session duration.
7. If you wish to terminate the counseling services, provide a reason for termination.
8. Finally, sign below to confirm you have read and understood the terms of this agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details**: Please fill in your name, email, and phone number to enable us to contact you effectively.
* **Email**: Your email address will be used for communication and sending relevant information. Please ensure it is accurate.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number will be used for scheduling sessions and contacting you. Please enter a valid phone number.
* **Counseling Details**: This is an optional field where you can provide details about your current situation and concerns. It helps your counselor understand your needs better.
* **Goal**: If you have a specific goal you wish to achieve, please provide it here. This will help your counselor focus on your objectives.
* **Counselor Information**: If you have any concerns or questions about your counselor, please provide them in this section.
* **Agreement**: You have the option to agree to the confidentiality and other terms stated. Please read and sign below to confirm.
	+ **I understand the importance of confidentiality**: Your sessions and records will be kept confidential.
	+ **I understand that all records will be kept confidential**: Your records, including notes and recordings, will not be shared with anyone outside of the counselor.
	+ **I understand I can leave counseling at any time**: You have the right to terminate counseling services at any time.
* **Emergency Contact**: Please provide an emergency contact's name and phone number. This information will be kept confidential and used only in emergency situations.
* **Signature**: Your signature confirms that you have read and understood the terms of this agreement. Please sign with your initials or full name.
* **Date**: The date you sign this document ensures the agreement is in effect from that date onwards.
* **Time**: The time of signing this document is recorded for reference.
* **IP Address**: This field is not essential but can be useful for future reference.
* **Client Agreement**: Your signature confirms that you understand the terms of this agreement.
* **Counselor Agreement**: This section is not essential but can be used for future reference.
* **Date of Last Session**: This field keeps track of the date of your last session.
* **Number of Sessions**: This field keeps track of the number of sessions attended.
* **Session Length**: The session duration is crucial for scheduling and payment.
* **Frequency**: Select the frequency of sessions you prefer (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Payment Terms**: Please indicate how you prefer to pay for sessions (e.g., cash, credit card, or insurance).
* **Insurance**: If you have insurance, please provide the provider name.
* **Insurance Provider**: This field is not essential but can help with billing and insurance claims.
* **Session Fees**: The session fees are essential for payment and record-keeping.
* **Session Duration**: This field keeps track of the duration of each session.
* **Termination Reason**: If you wish to terminate counseling services, please provide a reason for termination.
