# GCIC Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GCIC Consent Form is a crucial document that collects necessary information and consent for participation in the General Counselor Information Center program. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name**: Enter your first name. 
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name. 
3. **Address 1**: Enter your address, which is essential for identifying you in our records. 
4. **City**: Enter the city where you reside. 
5. **State**: Enter the state where you reside. 
6. **Zip**: Enter your zip code for mailing purposes. 
7. **Phone**: Optional, enter your contact phone number. 
8. **Email**: Enter a valid email address. 
9. **Confirm Email**: Confirm the email address entered above. 
10. **Date of Birth**: Optional, enter your date of birth. 
11. **Consent**: Indicate your consent for participation in the GCIC program by selecting "I consent" or "No". 
12. **Signature**: Sign your name, which is a required condition for form submission. 
13. **Name on Document**: Enter your full name as it will be displayed on any documents generated from this form. 
14. **Document Number**: Enter a unique identifier for the document. 
15. **Date of Document**: Enter the date the document was created or last updated. 
16. **Time of Document**: Enter the time the document was created or last updated. 
17. **Expiration Date**: Enter the date this document expires. 
18. **Expiration Time**: Enter the time this document expires. 
19. **Background Check ID**: Enter a unique identifier for your background check. 
20. **Background Check Date**: Enter the date of your background check. 
21. **Background Check Time**: Enter the time of your background check. 
22. **Background Check Agency**: Optional, enter the agency that performed the background check. 
23. **Background Check Notes**: Enter any notes or remarks from the background check. 
24. **Notes**: Optional, add any additional comments or information. 

## Tips
* Ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form. 
* Double-check your email address for accuracy. 
* Be cautious when selecting "I consent" or "No" for the "Consent" field, as it is a critical condition for participation in the GCIC program. 
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
