# intervention planning form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intervention Planning Form is used to gather information for creating an effective intervention plan for a client. This form helps clinicians and counselors document the client's background, goals, and treatment objectives, and provides a framework for planning and implementing interventions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the client's information and background, including their client info, family history, mental health status, medical history, substance history, and social support.
2. Identify the client's goals and objectives for treatment.
3. Develop an intervention plan that takes into account the client's background, goals, and treatment objectives.
4. Enter the client's information and background in the corresponding fields.
5. Enter the client's goals and objectives in the "goals" field.
6. Enter the client's intervention plan in the "intervention plan" field.
7. Review and update the form as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info** (Yes/No): Check this box if the client has a specific condition or situation that may impact their treatment.
* **Family History**: Enter a brief description of the client's family history, including any relevant background information.
* **Mental Health** (Select One): Choose whether the client's mental health status is:
	+ none
	+ current
	+ history
* **Medical History**: Enter a brief description of the client's medical history.
* **Substance History** (Yes/No): Check this box if the client has a substance use history.
* **Goals**: Enter the client's specific goals and objectives for treatment.
* **Treatment Planning**: Enter a brief description of the client's treatment plan.
* **Family Dynamics** (Yes/No): Check this box if the client's family dynamics are a significant factor in their treatment.
* **Social Support**: Enter a brief description of the client's social support system.
* **Intervention Plan**: Enter a detailed description of the client's intervention plan.
* **Assessment**: Choose whether the client's assessment status is:
	+ none
	+ yes
	+ unknown
* **Follow-up** (Optional): Enter the date and frequency of follow-up appointments.
* **Follow-up Method** (Optional): Enter the method for follow-up appointments (e.g. phone, email, in-person).
