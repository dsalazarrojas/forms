# Behavior Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Behavior Specialist Referral Form is designed to collect relevant information for referrals to behavior specialists, ensuring that you receive the support you need for specific behavioral issues or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please start by selecting the relevant section (e.g., Personal Information, Additional Details).
2.  Fill out the **Full name**, **Email address**, and **Phone number** fields to provide your personal contact information.
3.  Select the **Date** for your referral.
4.  Choose the **Primary selection** that best applies to your situation.
5.  If necessary, provide a whole number for **Quantity or rating**.
6.  Enter any **Additional details** that are relevant to your situation.
7.  Select any that apply in the **Agreement** field.
8.  If applicable, enter your estimated **Estimated value** in decimal format.
9.  If scheduling is required, enter your **Preferred time**.
10.  Finally, add any **Final comments or notes** you'd like to provide in the comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, `required`): Enter your legal full name.
*   **Email address** (`email`, email, `required`): Enter a valid email for contact purposes.
*   **Phone number** (`phone`, text, `false`): Include your phone number, including the country code if you're outside the local region.
*   **Date** (`reference_date`, date, `required`): Select the date that's relevant to your referral.
*   **Primary selection** (`primary_selection`, select_one, `required`): Choose the option that best applies to your situation.
*   **Quantity or rating** (`quantity_or_rating`, number, `false`): Provide a whole number for quantity or rating if applicable.
*   **Additional details** (`additional_details`, text, `false`): Provide any additional relevant information.
*   **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, `required`): Select any that apply.
*   **Estimated value** (`estimated_value`, number, `false`): Enter your estimated value in decimal format if applicable.
*   **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`, time, `false`): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
*   **Final comments or notes** (`final_comments`, text, `false`): Add any final comments or notes you'd like to provide.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields marked `required` are filled out correctly to ensure a smooth referral process.
*   Use decimal format for estimated value if applicable.
*   Enter your phone number with the country code if you're outside the local region.
*   Choose the primary selection that best applies to your situation.
