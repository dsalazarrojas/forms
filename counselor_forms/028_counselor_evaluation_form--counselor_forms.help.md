# Counselor Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counselor Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from clients on their experiences with a counselor. This form helps identify areas for improvement and recognizes strengths in the counselor's performance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:
*   Enter the client's name in the `Client Name` field.
*   Enter the date of the counseling session in the `Date` field.
*   Rate the counselor's overall performance by selecting one of the options under `Rating Scale`. You can choose from: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   Provide additional comments about the session in the `Comments` field.
*   Enter the client's email address in the `Email` field.
*   Enter the client's phone number in the `Phone` field.
*   Rate the counselor's performance on five specific questions (Q1-Q5) using the corresponding fields. For each question, rate the performance on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Client Name (1)**: Please enter the client's name as it appears on record.
*   **Date (2)**: Enter the date of the counseling session.
*   **Overall Rating (3)**: Rate the counselor's overall performance.
*   **Rating Scale (4)**: Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Comments (5)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the session.
*   **Email (6)**: Enter the client's email address.
*   **Phone (7)**: Enter the client's phone number.
*   **Q1 (8)**: Rate the counselor's performance on the first question. Use a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
*   **Q2 (9)**: Rate the counselor's performance on the second question. Use a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
*   **Q3 (10)**: Rate the counselor's performance on the third question. Use a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
*   **Q4 (11)**: Rate the counselor's performance on the fourth question. Use a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
*   **Q5 (12)**: Rate the counselor's performance on the fifth question. Use a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
## Tips
*   Please be honest and objective when providing feedback.
*   Ensure all required fields are completed.
*   Use the same scale for each question.
*   Provide clear and concise comments.

Note: The form is designed to be user-friendly and help ensure that clients can provide accurate and constructive feedback on the counselor's performance.
