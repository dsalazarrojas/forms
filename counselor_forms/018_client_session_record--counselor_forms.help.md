# Client Session Record - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record information about client sessions, including client details, session specifics, and facilitator information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have a clear understanding of the client's background and session details.
2.  Enter the client's full name and internal reference number (if applicable).
3.  Enter the date and time of the session.
4.  Choose the type and location of the session (e.g., individual, group, in-person, phone call, or video call).
5.  Describe the session duration and topics covered.
6.  Report the client's emotional state during the session.
7.  Indicate the client's participation level and engagement during the session.
8.  Specify any homework or tasks assigned and their completion status.
9.  Describe any behavioral observations or changes noted.
10.  Note any areas of concern or challenges.
11.  Indicate any risks or safety concerns.
12.  Schedule the next session date (if applicable).
13.  Provide any follow-up actions or recommendations.
14.  Indicate if any referrals were made.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's full name.
*   **Client ID** (`client_id`, text, optional): Enter the client's internal reference number (if applicable).
*   **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the session.
*   **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the session.
*   **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, required): Enter the length of the session in minutes.
*   **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of session (e.g., individual, group, initial consultation, follow-up, crisis session).
*   **Session Location or Method** (`session_location`, select_one, required): Choose the session location or method (e.g., in-person, phone call, video call).
*   **Facilitator or Practitioner Name** (`facilitator_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the facilitator or practitioner who conducted the session.
*   **Co-Facilitator** (`co_facilitator`, text, optional): Enter the name of any co-facilitators who participated in the session (if applicable).
*   **Topics or issues discussed in this session** (`topics_covered`, text, required): Describe the topics or issues discussed during the session.
*   **Progress toward established goals** (`goals_addressed`, text, optional): Report the client's progress toward their stated goals.
*   **Key insights or breakthroughs in this session** (`key_insights`, text, optional): Describe any key insights or breakthroughs gained during the session.
*   **Client participation level** (`participation_level`, select_one, required): Choose the client's participation level (e.g., not engaged, minimally engaged, moderately engaged, well engaged, highly engaged).
*   **Client emotional state during the session** (`emotional_state`, select_one, optional): Choose the client's emotional state (e.g., distressed, anxious, neutral, positive, empowered).
*   **Was homework or tasks assigned?** (`homework_given`, select_one, required): Indicate if homework or tasks were assigned.
*   **Describe the assigned task or homework** (`homework_description`, text, optional): Describe the assigned task or homework.
*   **Previous homework completion status** (`homework_completion`, select_one, optional): Indicate the client's completion status of previous homework.
*   **Behavioral observations or changes noted** (`behavioral_observations`, text, optional): Describe any behavioral observations or changes noted during the session.
*   **Client strengths or positive attributes observed** (`strengths_noted`, text, optional): Note any client strengths or positive attributes observed during the session.
*   **Areas of concern or challenges** (`areas_of_concern`, text, optional): Describe any areas of concern or challenges identified during the session.
*   **Any risks or safety concerns?** (`risk_assessment`, select_one, optional): Indicate if any risks or safety concerns were identified.
*   **Scheduled next session date** (`next_session_date`, date, optional): Schedule the next session date (if applicable).
*   **Follow-up actions or recommendations** (`follow_up_actions`, text, optional): Provide any follow-up actions or recommendations.
*   **Referrals made?** (`referrals_made`, select_one, optional): Indicate if any referrals were made.
*   **Details of referrals** (`referral_details`, text, optional): Describe any referrals made.

## Tips
*   Always ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Be thorough in describing the session specifics and client details.
*   Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
*   Review and update the form regularly to reflect any changes in the client's status or session details.
