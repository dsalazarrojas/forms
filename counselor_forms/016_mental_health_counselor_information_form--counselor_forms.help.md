# Mental Health Counselor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from mental health counselors to create a comprehensive profile of their qualifications, experience, and certifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
*   Start by filling out the "Client Information" page.
*   On this page, provide a brief description of yourself as a mental health counselor.
*   Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next page.
*   On subsequent pages, fill out the required fields with accurate and detailed information about your qualifications, experience, and certifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
*   **Client Information** (`client_information`, `text`, required:false): This field is a brief description of yourself as a mental health counselor. Please provide a concise summary of your qualifications and experience.

### Client Mental Health Condition
*   **Client Mental Health Condition** (`client_mental_health_condition`, `text`, required:false): This field is used to describe the mental health conditions you are qualified to treat. Please list the conditions you are proficient in treating.

### Counselor Relationship
*   **Counselor Relationship** (`counselor_relationship`, `text`, required:false): This field is used to describe your relationship with your clients. Please indicate the type of relationship you have with your clients (e.g., individual, couples, family).

### Treatment Plan
*   **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, `text`, required:false): This field is used to describe your treatment plan for mental health conditions. Please provide a detailed description of the plans you use to treat clients.

### Mental Health Counselor Certifications
*   **Mental Health Counselor Certifications** (`mental_health_counselor_certifications`, `select_one`, required:false): Please select the certifications you hold from the options provided:
    *   LCSW (Licensed Clinical Social Worker)
    *   LPCC (Licensed Professional Clinical Counselor)
    *   LMFT (Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist)
    *   LMFT-Associate (Associate Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist)
    *   MFT (Mental Health Counselor)
    *   MFT-Associate (Associate Mental Health Counselor)
    *   MFT-Assistant (Assistant Mental Health Counselor)

### Mental Health Counselor Experience
*   **Mental Health Counselor Experience** (`mental_health_counselor_experience`, `number`, required:false): This field is used to describe your experience as a mental health counselor. Please enter the number of years you have been practicing.

### Mental Health Counselor Education
*   **Mental Health Counselor Education** (`mental_health_counselor_education`, `text`, required:false): This field is used to describe your educational background as a mental health counselor. Please provide a brief summary of your education and training.

### Mental Health Counselor Licensing
*   **Mental Health Counselor Licensing** (`mental_health_counselor_licensing`, `text`, required:false): This field is used to describe your licensure as a mental health counselor. Please provide your license number or a brief description of your licensure status.
