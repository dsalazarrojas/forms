# College Counselor Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Counselor Recommendation Form is designed to collect information from a school counselor or academic advisor to assess a student's potential for college success.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each field carefully and understand its relevance to the form's purpose.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about the student.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
4. If you're unsure about any field, use the hints or examples provided to guide your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Counselor Full Name**: Enter your name as a school counselor or academic advisor.
* **Email Address**: Enter your school email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number (optional).
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position at the school.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school where you work.
* **School Address**: Enter the address of the school where you work.
* **Years as School Counselor**: Select the number of years you have been working as a school counselor.
* **Student Full Name**: Enter the full name of the student you are recommending.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Graduation Year**: Enter the year the student is expected to graduate.
* **Relationship to Student**: Select the relationship you have with the student (e.g., School Counselor, College Counselor, Academic Advisor).
* **How Long Have You Known the Student**: Select the duration of your relationship with the student.
* **Frequency of Contact with Student**: Select how often you meet or contact with the student.
* **Academic Performance Rating**: Select how the student's academic performance compares to their peers.
* **Course Rigor Rating**: Select the level of challenge of the courses the student takes.
* **Personal Qualities Rating**: Select how the student demonstrates exceptional personal qualities.
* **Leadership Qualities Rating**: Select how the student demonstrates leadership potential.
* **Extracurricular Involvement Rating**: Select how involved the student is in extracurricular activities.
* **Community Service Involvement**: Select how involved the student is in community service.
* **Resilience Rating**: Select how well the student handles challenges.
* **Student Top Strengths**: Enter a list of the student's strengths or talents.
* **Areas of Growth**: Enter areas where the student has demonstrated growth or improvement.
* **Challenges the Student Has Overcome**: Enter any notable challenges or obstacles the student has faced.
* **School Context**: Enter information about the school's context or environment.
* **How Does Student Rank Among Peers**: Enter a description of the student's ranking among their peers.
* **College Readiness Assessment**: Enter a description of the student's college readiness level.
* **Overall Recommendation Level**: Select how strongly you recommend the student for college.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information or context that may be helpful.
* **Counselor Certification**: Select that you certify the accuracy of the recommendation.

By following these instructions and using the provided hints, you should be able to complete the form accurately and effectively.
