# Parenting Style Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parenting Style Survey is designed to help parents understand their own parenting style and identify areas for improvement. This survey assesses your responses to various aspects of child-rearing, providing insight into how often you give in to your child's demands, how responsive you are to their needs, time spent with your child, showing positive emotions, using positive reinforcement, and giving attention to their requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the answer that best describes your typical response to each question.
2. Answer all questions as accurately and honestly as possible.
3. Make sure to review each question carefully before submitting your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### question_1 - How often do you give in to your child's demands?
**Never**: I never give in to my child's demands.
**Rarely**: I rarely give in to my child's demands.
**Sometimes**: I sometimes give in to my child's demands.
**Often**: I often give in to my child's demands.
**Never**: I never give in to my child's demands.

### question_2 - How responsive are you to your child's needs?
**Very**: I am very responsive to my child's needs.
**Somewhat**: I am somewhat responsive to my child's needs.
**Not very**: I am not very responsive to my child's needs.

### question_3 - How much time do you spend with your child?
This question is a numerical value.

### question_4 - How often do you show positive emotions to your child?
**Always**: I always show positive emotions to my child.
**Often**: I often show positive emotions to my child.
**Sometimes**: I sometimes show positive emotions to my child.
**Rarely**: I rarely show positive emotions to my child.

### question_5 - How often do you use positive reinforcement?
**Always**: I always use positive reinforcement.
**Often**: I often use positive reinforcement.
**Sometimes**: I sometimes use positive reinforcement.
**Rarely**: I rarely use positive reinforcement.
**Never**: I never use positive reinforcement.

### question_6 - How much attention do you give to your child's requests?
**Always**: I always give attention to my child's requests.
**Often**: I often give attention to my child's requests.
**Sometimes**: I sometimes give attention to my child's requests.
**Rarely**: I rarely give attention to my child's requests.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you're unsure about a question, try to think about your typical behavior as a parent.
* Remember that this survey is not a judgment, but a tool to help you understand your parenting style.
