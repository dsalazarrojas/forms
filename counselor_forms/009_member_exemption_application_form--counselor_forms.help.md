# member_exemption_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from members who need to apply for exemption.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill out the fields.
2. Answer the required questions accurately and thoroughly.
3. Ensure you have permission to submit on behalf of the member (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Member Exemption Application Form Page 1**
* **Member Exemption Application Form Page 1** (`member_exemption_application_form_page_1`, note, not required): This page serves as a placeholder and does not require any specific information.
* **Member Exemption Application Form First Name** (`member_exemption_application_form_first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name as it is recorded on your identification documents.
* **Member Exemption Application Form Last Name** (`member_exemption_application_form_last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name as it is recorded on your identification documents.
* **Member Exemption Application Form Email** (`member_exemption_application_form_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address that you wish to be contacted on.
* **Member Exemption Application Form Phone** (`member_exemption_application_form_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Member Exemption Application Form Member Type** (`member_exemption_application_form_member_type`, select one, not required): Select 'Yes' if you are a member, 'No' otherwise.
* **Member Exemption Application Form Exemption Reason** (`member_exemption_application_form_exemption_reason`, select one, required): Choose the reason for your exemption request (option 1 or option 2).
* **Member Exemption Application Form Exemption Reason Other** (`member_exemption_application_form_exemption_reason_other`, text, not required): If the reason for your exemption is not listed, provide a brief description in this field.
 
## Tips
* Be accurate and complete in your responses to ensure a smooth exemption process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
