# online_screen_addiction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online_screen_addiction_survey is a self-report instrument designed to assess an individual's risk of addiction to screens and their impact on daily life. It is intended for use in a clinical setting to help professionals evaluate a patient's screening results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each page as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Screening
* **First Screening** (`1`, `text`, required): This is the first question in the screening process. Please answer this question honestly and to the best of your ability.
### Screening 2
* **Screening 2** (`2`, `text`, required): This is the second question in the screening process. Please answer this question honestly and to the best of your ability.
### Harmful Behaviors
* **Harmful Behaviors** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the listed behaviors that you feel best describes your current behavior.
### Frequency
* **Frequency** (`4`, `text`, optional): This question asks about your current frequency of screen usage. Please answer this question to the best of your ability.
### Screen Addiction
* **Screen Addiction** (`5`, `number`, optional): This question asks about your current level of screen addiction. Please answer this question to the best of your ability.
### Impact on Lifestyle
* **Impact on Lifestyle** (`6`, `number`, optional): This question asks about the impact of screens on your daily life. Please answer this question to the best of your ability.
### Screen Addiction Risk
* **Screen Addiction Risk** (`7`, `date`, required): Please select a date that represents when you first started to feel that you might have a problem with screens.
### Screen Time
* **Screen Time** (`8`, `time`, optional): This question asks about the amount of time you spend on screens per day. Please answer this question to the best of your ability.
### Tips
* Take your time, and answer each question carefully.
* Be honest in your answers.
* If you need help or have questions, please don't hesitate to ask.
* Please note that this form is not a diagnosis tool, but rather a screening instrument to identify potential issues. If you're concerned about your screen usage, please consult a healthcare professional for personalized advice.
