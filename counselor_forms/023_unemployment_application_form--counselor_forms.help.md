# Unemployment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Unemployment Application Form is a document used for individuals seeking unemployment benefits. This form collects information about your employment history, reason for separation, and financial details to process your claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2.  Enter your social security number for identification purposes.
3.  Enter the date of your last day of work.
4.  Choose the reason for your separation from the available options.
5.  Enter the number of weeks you worked in your last job.
6.  Enter your gross earnings for the week you last worked.
7.  Enter the number of hours you worked in your last week.
8.  Enter your gross earnings for the last week.
9.  Choose the reason for your job loss from the available options.
10. Enter the date of your job separation.
11. Enter the title of your last job.
12. Enter your current or former employer's name.
13. Enter your mailing address.
14. Enter the city of your mailing address.
15. Enter the state of your mailing address.
16. Enter your zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Application Name** (application_name): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Social Security Number** (social_security_number): Enter your social security number for identification purposes.
*   **Date Last Worked** (date_of_last_day_worked): Enter the date of your last day of work.
*   **Reason for Separation** (reason_for_separation): Choose the reason for your separation from the available options.
*   **Last Weeks Worked** (last_during_weeks_worked): Enter the number of weeks you worked in your last job.
*   **Gross Earnings** (gross_earnings): Enter your gross earnings for the week you last worked.
*   **Hours Worked Last Week** (hours_worked_last_week): Enter the number of hours you worked in your last week.
*   **Gross Earnings Last Week** (gross_earnings_last_week): Enter your gross earnings for the last week.
*   **Reason for Job Loss** (reason_for_job_loss): Choose the reason for your job loss from the available options.
*   **Job Separation Date** (job_separation_date): Enter the date of your job separation.
*   **Last Job Title** (last_job_title): Enter the title of your last job.
*   **Employer** (employer): Enter your current or former employer's name.
*   **Mailing Address** (mailing_address): Enter your mailing address.
*   **City** (city): Enter the city of your mailing address.
*   **State** (state): Enter the state of your mailing address.
*   **Zip** (zip): Enter your zip code.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   If you're unsure about any of the information, please contact your local unemployment office for guidance.
*   This form is part of the process of filing for unemployment benefits, and your input will help determine your eligibility.
