# Counselor Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Counselor Exit Offboarding Form" is designed to collect information about an employee's departure from their position, including their personal details, reasons for leaving, and feedback. This form helps maintain accurate records and ensures a smooth transition process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number in the relevant fields.
2. Provide your job title and department.
3. Enter the name of your manager.
4. Specify the date of your departure.
5. Choose the reason for your leaving from the options provided (if any).
6. Provide any feedback or comments about your departure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (Form Title, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the form or any additional comments.
* **Name** (Name, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Department** (Department, text, optional): Enter the department you are currently part of.
* **Manager Name** (Manager Name, text, optional): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Date of Departure** (Date of Departure, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to leave your position. Format: YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Reason for Leaving** (Reason for Leaving, text, optional): Select one of the provided options for your reason for leaving (e.g., retirement, resignation, termination).
* **Feedback** (Feedback, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your departure.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): Enter any other relevant comments about your departure.
* **Form Completion Date** (Form Completion Date, date, optional): Enter the date you completed this form. Format: YYYY-MM-DD.
