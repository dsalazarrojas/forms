# minor_travel_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The minor travel consent form is a document that collects essential information from minors and their parents before embarking on travel. The form aims to gather details about the parent's contact information, travel details, travel destinations, and emergency contacts, ensuring a smooth and safe travel experience for the minor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the parent's information on the first page, including their contact number and name.
2. Enter the travel details on the second page, providing the travel agency, flight number, and other essential information.
3. On the third page, specify the travel dates and times, including the return dates and times if applicable.
4. Enter the contact information for emergency purposes, including the contact number and name of the emergency contact.
5. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please specify them on the respective pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information** (`parent_information`, `text`, required): This section is where the parent's name and contact number are entered. Ensure that the parent's contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, `text`, required): Enter the travel agency or company name, as well as any other relevant travel-related information.
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, `text`, required): Specify the location or destination of the travel.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the travel, using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the travel, using the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the return date of the travel, using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Return Time** (`return_time`, `time`, optional): If applicable, enter the return time of the travel, using the 24-hour format (e.g., 18:00).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number for the parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the name and contact number of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_condition`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical conditions, enter them here.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): If you have any allergies, specify them here.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, `text`, required): Enter the travel agency or company name that you will be traveling with.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, `text`, required): Enter the flight number or travel booking reference number. 

**Tips**

* Ensure that all fields are accurately and completely filled out to avoid any issues or delays during travel.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to the travel agency or your parents for assistance.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct and complete.
