# Vocational Rehabilitation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vocational Rehabilitation Intake Form is designed to gather information from clients seeking vocational rehabilitation services. This form helps to assess the client's needs and develop a treatment plan tailored to their specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions in the form, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Review each page carefully before proceeding to the next one.
3. Sign and date each signature block accordingly.
4. Upload any supporting documents as required.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: Please provide your personal details, including name, address, phone number, and email address.
* **Employment Information**: Describe your current employment status, job title, and work hours.
* **Education History**: Provide information about your educational background, including institutions attended and degrees earned.
* **Identification**: Please provide your identification details, including government ID number and date of birth.
* **Social Situation**: Describe your social situation, including family members, marital status, and any dependencies.
* **Medical History**: Provide information about your medical history, including any chronic conditions, allergies, or medical interventions.
* **Functional Capabilities**: Describe your current functional capabilities, including any limitations or disabilities.
* **Personal Contact Information**: Provide your emergency contact information, including name, phone number, and address.
* **Service Provider Information**: Please provide information about your current service provider, including name, address, and contact number.
* **Agreement**: Please review and sign the service agreement form, indicating your understanding of the terms and conditions.
* **Service Provider Assessment**: This section is for the service provider to assess your eligibility for services. Please do not complete this section.
* **Submit**: Review and submit the form once you have completed all sections.
* **Additional Questions**: This section is for any additional information you may wish to provide.
* **Signature Blocks**: Please sign and date each signature block accordingly.
* **Upload Documents**: Upload any supporting documents as required.
* **Submit Button**: Click the submit button to complete and submit the form.

Please note that each section of the form is designed to gather specific information, and please answer accordingly. If you are unsure about any question or section, please contact your service provider for clarification.
