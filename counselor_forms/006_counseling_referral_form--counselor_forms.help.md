# Counseling Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for counseling referrals, which are used to track and document the process of referring students to external counselors or physicians for further support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the student's name, school, and grade level.
2.  Provide the reason for referral.
3.  Select the referral status and date.
4.  Enter the student school email, phone, and address.
5.  Enter the counselor's name, title, office phone, and email.
6.  Enter the submitter's name.
7.  Enter the submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name.
*   **Student School** (`student_school`, text, required): Enter the student's school.
*   **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, number, required): Enter the student's grade level.
*   **Reason for Referral** (`reason_for_referral`, text, required): Enter the reason for referral.
*   **Student Counseling Status** (`student_counseling_status`, select\_one, optional): Select the student's counseling status.
*   **Physician Referral Status** (`physician_referral_status`, select\_one, optional): Select the physician referral status.
*   **Counselor Referral Status** (`counselor_referral_status`, select\_one, optional): Select the counselor referral status.
*   **Counselor Referral Date** (`counselor_referral_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the counselor referral.
*   **Physician Referral Date** (`physician_referral_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the physician referral.
*   **Counselor Referral Note** (`counselor_referral_note`, note, required): Enter a note about the referral.
*   **Physician Referral Note** (`physician_referral_note`, note, required): Enter a note about the referral.
*   **Student School Email** (`student_school_email`, email, required): Enter the student school email address.
*   **Student School Phone** (`student_school_phone`, text, required): Enter the student school phone number.
*   **Student School Phone Extension** (`student_school_phone_ext`, text, optional): Enter the student school phone extension.
*   **Student School Street Address** (`student_school_street_address`, text, optional): Enter the student school street address.
*   **Student School City** (`student_school_city`, text, optional): Enter the student school city.
*   **Student School State** (`student_school_state`, text, optional): Enter the student school state.
*   **Student School Zip** (`student_school_zip`, text, optional): Enter the student school zip.
*   **Counselor Name - First Name** (`counselor_first_name`, text, required): Enter the counselor's first name.
*   **Counselor Name - Last Name** (`counselor_last_name`, text, required): Enter the counselor's last name.
*   **Counselor Title** (`counselor_title`, text, required): Enter the counselor's title.
*   **Counselor Office Phone** (`counselor_office_phone`, text, required): Enter the counselor's office phone number.
*   **Counselor Office Email** (`counselor_office_email`, text, required): Enter the counselor's office email address.
*   **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, optional): Enter the submission date.
*   **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter the submitter's name.
