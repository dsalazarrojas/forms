# Baby Names Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from parents or guardians about their preferences and plans for naming their baby. It covers various aspects of naming, including style, origin, length, and meaning. The survey aims to help you explore your options and make informed decisions about your child's name.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter your name and email address (fields 1 and 3).
* Select the baby's gender (field 4).
* Choose the due date or birth date (field 5).
* Enter your preference for the baby's last name (field 6).
* Select your preferred name style (field 7).
* Choose the cultural origin of the name (field 8).
* Indicate your preference for name length (field 9).
* Decide on the use of a middle name (field 10).
* List any family names you'd like to honor (field 11).
* Provide any boy or girl name options you're considering (fields 12 and 13).
* Select your top choice for a boy or girl name (fields 14 and 15).
* Indicate the importance of the name's meaning (field 16).
* State your preference regarding nicknames (field 17).
* Decide on whether to match sibling names (field 18).
* Consider the pronunciation of the name (field 19).
* Add any additional preferences or notes (field 20).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent or Guardian One Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Parent or Guardian Two Name**: Enter the full name of the other parent or guardian, or leave blank if not applicable.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for receiving survey results.
* **Baby Gender**: Select the baby's gender (Boy, Girl, Twins or Multiples, Keeping it a surprise).
* **Due Date or Birth Date**: Enter the expected or actual birth date of the baby.
* **Baby Last Name**: Enter the last name you plan to give the baby.
* **Name Style Preference**: Select your preferred name style (Traditional, Modern, Unique or Uncommon, Classic, Nature-inspired, International, No preference).
* **Name Origin Preference**: Select the cultural origin of the preferred name (English, Spanish, French, Italian, Irish, Scottish, Hebrew, Greek, Latin, German, Scandinavian, Asian, African, Any origin).
* **Name Length Preference**: Select the preferred name length (Short, 1 to 2 syllables, Medium, 3 to 4 syllables, Long, 5 plus syllables, No preference).
* **Middle Name Plan**: Decide whether to give a middle name (Yes, one middle name, Yes, two middle names, No middle name, Undecided).
* **Family Naming Tradition**: List any family names you'd like to honor.
* **Boy Name Options**: List any boy names you're considering.
* **Girl Name Options**: List any girl names you're considering.
* **Favorite Boy Name**: Enter your top choice for a boy name.
* **Favorite Girl Name**: Enter your top choice for a girl name.
* **Name Meaning Importance**: Select how important the name's meaning is to you (Very important, Somewhat important, Not important).
* **Nickname Preference**: State your preference regarding nicknames (Prefer names with nickname options, Dislike nicknames, Do not care).
* **Sibling Name Matching**: Decide whether to match the baby's name with siblings (Yes, should match style, No, each name is unique, Not applicable).
* **Pronunciation Concerns**: Select whether you want the name to be easy to pronounce (Yes, must be easy to pronounce, Somewhat important, Not important, unique is fine).
* **Additional Preferences or Notes**: Provide any other thoughts or preferences regarding the baby's name.

This form aims to help you explore your preferences and make informed decisions about your baby's name. Please answer each question truthfully, and feel free to add any additional comments or notes in the final field.
