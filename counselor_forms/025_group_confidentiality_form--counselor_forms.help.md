# Group Confidentiality Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Group Confidentiality Form is used to gather information from group participants about their level of understanding and agreement to keep certain information confidential within their group. This form helps establish a foundation for trust and open communication within the group.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your User Information (ID, Name, etc.)
2. Enter the Group Name
3. Identify the Group Leader
4. Choose whether or not you agree to keep certain information confidential
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your understanding of confidentiality
6. Indicate the size of your group (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, Text, Optional): Enter your name, ID, or any other identifier that you want to be associated with this form.
* **Group Name** (`group_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of your group.
* **Group Leader** (`group_leader`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person who leads your group.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose whether you agree to keep certain information confidential by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.
* **Confidentiality Agreement 2** (`confidentiality_agreement_2`, Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or explanations about your understanding of confidentiality.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Text, Optional): Add any other relevant comments or notes about your understanding of confidentiality within your group.
* **Group Size** (`group_size`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of members in your group (if applicable).
