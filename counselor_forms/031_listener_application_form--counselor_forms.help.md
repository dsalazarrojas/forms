# Listener Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Listener Application Form is a collection of fields that gather information from listeners who are applying to be a part of a team. This form is designed to gather relevant details about the listener, including their contact information, employment history, education, skills, references, and other relevant documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" fields.
2. Provide details about your employment history and current employment status in the "Employment Information" field.
3. Share your education background and relevant qualifications in the "Education" field.
4. List your skills and areas of expertise in the "Skills" field.
5. Provide references that can vouch for your work experience and skills.
6. Upload your resume to showcase your professional experience.
7. Upload your transcript to provide further education qualifications.
8. Upload your ID card to verify your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* Contact Information (`Contact Information`, `text`, required:false): This field is used to collect your contact details, including your name, address, phone number, and email. Please enter your information accurately and completely.

### Employment Information
* Employment Information (`Employment Information`, `text`, required:false): This field is used to gather details about your current employment status. Please provide a brief description of your current job and how long you have been in this role.

### Work History
* Work History (`Work History`, `text`, required:false): This field is used to collect your work history, including any previous job titles, company names, and dates of employment.

### Education
* Education (`Education`, `text`, required:false): This field is used to share your education background, including any degrees or qualifications you have obtained.

### Employment Details
* Employment Details (`Employment Details`, `text`, required:false): This field is used to provide further details about your employment, including job responsibilities and job performance.

### Skills
* Skills (`Skills`, `text`, required:false): This field is used to list your skills and areas of expertise. Please be specific about what you can do.

### References
* References (`References`, `text`, required:false): This field is used to provide references that can vouch for your work experience and skills. Please provide at least two references.

### Resume
* Resume (`Resume`, `text`, required:false): This field is used to upload your resume, which should showcase your professional experience and qualifications.

### Transcript
* Transcript (`Transcript`, `text`, required:false): This field is used to upload your transcript, which should provide further education qualifications.

### ID Card
* ID Card (`ID Card`, `text`, required:false): This field is used to upload your ID card, which is used to verify your identity.
Tips:
* Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that your application is processed efficiently.
* Be sure to upload your resume, transcript, and ID card to provide a complete picture of your qualifications and identity.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
