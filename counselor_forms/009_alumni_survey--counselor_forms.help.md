# Alumni Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Alumni Survey is designed to gather information about your experiences, perceptions, and opinions about your education, career development, and relationship with your alma mater.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1.  Review each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2.  For each question, choose the answer that best matches your experiences and opinions.
3.  If a question is not applicable to you, please select "N/A" or leave it blank.
4.  If you need more information or clarification about a question, please contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Alumni Survey Introduction --** (Section 1): This section welcomes you to the survey and informs you about the purpose and importance of your feedback.
