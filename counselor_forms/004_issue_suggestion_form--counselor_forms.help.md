# issue_suggestion_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and manage issue suggestions from users. It gathers information about the issue, including its title, description, category, and priority level, as well as contact information from the submitter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the issue in the **Issue Title** field.
2. Provide a detailed description of the issue in the **Description** field.
3. Select the category of the issue from the options provided in the **Category** field.
4. Select the sub-category of the issue from the options provided in the **Sub Category** field.
5. Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number in the **Submitter Name**, **Submitter Email**, and **Submitter Phone** fields respectively.
6. Select the priority level of the issue from the options provided in the **Issue Priority** field.
7. Provide any additional comments about the issue in the **Submitter Comments** field.
8. If applicable, attach any supporting documents or files to the issue in the **Submitter Attachment** field.
9. Enter your organization or affiliation in the **Submitter Organization** field.
10. Enter the location where the issue occurred in the **Submitter Location** field.
11. Select the status of the issue from the options provided in the **Issue Status** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Issue Title (1)
* **Issue Title** (text, required: false): Enter a brief title of the issue.
### Description (2)
* **Description** (text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the issue.
### Category (3)
* **Category** (select_one, required: false): Select the category of the issue.
### Sub Category (4)
* **Sub Category** (select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable sub-categories of the issue.
### Submitter (5)
* **Submitter** (text, required: false): Enter your name.
### Submitter Email (6)
* **Submitter Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address.
### Submitter Phone (7)
* **Submitter Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
### Date Reported (8)
* **Date Reported** (date, required: false): Enter the date the issue was first reported.
### Date Resolved (9)
* **Date Resolved** (date, required: false): Enter the date the issue was resolved.
### Submitter Name (10)
* **Submitter Name** (text, required: false): Enter your name.
### Submitter Organization (11)
* **Submitter Organization** (text, required: false): Enter your organization or affiliation.
### Issue Priority (12)
* **Issue Priority** (select_one, required: false): Select the priority level of the issue.
### Submitter Comments (13)
* **Submitter Comments** (text, required: false): Provide any additional comments about the issue.
### Submitter Attachment (14)
* **Submitter Attachment** (text, required: false): If applicable, attach any supporting documents or files to the issue.
### Submitter Location (15)
* **Submitter Location** (text, required: false): Enter the location where the issue occurred.
### Issue Status (16)
* **Issue Status** (select_one, required: false): Select the status of the issue.

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
