# Women's Comp Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Women's Comp Order Form Template is designed to collect information for creating new orders in the system.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review the form**: Before starting, make sure you have all the necessary information to complete the form accurately.
2. **Enter contact information**: Complete the contact information section, including customer details and communication methods.
3. **Provide order information**: Enter the specific details of the order, including any relevant notes or comments.
4. **Add payment information**: Fill in the payment information, which is a crucial part of the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. contact_info**: Enter the customer's name and contact details. This section is not required.
* **2. customer_info**: Enter the customer's information, including their name, email, and phone number. This is a required field.
* **3. order_info**: Enter the specific details of the order, including any relevant notes or comments. This is a required field.
* **4. shipping_info**: Enter the shipping details, including any additional notes or comments. This is not a required field.
* **5. order_items**: Enter the items included in the order. This is a required field.
* **6. payment_info**: Enter the payment method and details. This is a required field.
* **7. notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments. This is not a required field.
* **8. customer_email**: Enter the customer's email address. This is not a required field.
* **9. customer_phone**: Enter the customer's phone number. This is not a required field.
* **10. customer_cell**: Enter the customer's cell phone number. This is not a required field.
