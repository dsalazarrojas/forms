# School Counselor Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for assessment and planning purposes by the school counselor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to understand the required fields and the type of information needed.
2. Answer each question and provide clear, concise responses.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Student Information
* Student Information (student_information): Provide any relevant student information, such as demographic data or any other relevant background information.
* **Student Signature (student_signature):** Provide your signature to confirm that the information you provided is accurate.

### 2. Parent Information
* Parent Information (parent_information): Provide any relevant parent information, such as contact details or any other relevant background information.
* **Student Signature (student_signature):** Provide your signature to confirm that the information you provided is accurate.

### 3. Student Needs
* Student Needs (student_needs): Select any relevant needs or concerns that the student may have.

### 4. Counselor Notes
* Counselor Notes (counselor_notes): Provide any notes or comments from the counselor about the student.

### 5. Email
* Email (email): Provide the student's email address.
* **Counselor Signature (counselor_signature):** Provide your signature to confirm that the information you provided is accurate.

### 6. Phone
* Phone (phone): Provide the student's phone number.
* **Student Signature (student_signature):** Provide your signature to confirm that the information you provided is accurate.

### 7. Additional Comments
* Additional Comments (additional_comments): Provide any additional comments or concerns related to the student.

### 8. Follow Up Plan
* Follow Up Plan (follow_up_plan): Indicate a number indicating when the next step or follow-up will occur.
* **Counselor Signature (counselor_signature):** Provide your signature to confirm that the information you provided is accurate.

### 9. Assessment Date
* Assessment Date (assessment_date): Provide the date of the assessment.
* **Counselor Signature (counselor_signature):** Provide your signature to confirm that the information you provided is accurate.

### 10. Assessment Time
* Assessment Time (assessment_time): Provide the time of the assessment.
* **Counselor Signature (counselor_signature):** Provide your signature to confirm that the information you provided is accurate.

### 11. School Information
* School Name (school_name): Provide the name of the school.
* School Address (school_address): Provide the address of the school.
* School Phone (school_phone): Provide the phone number of the school.
* School Email (school_email): Provide the email address of the school.
* School Fax (school_fax): Provide the fax number of the school.
* School Notes (school_notes): Provide any additional notes or comments about the school.
* School Comments (school_comments): Provide any additional comments or concerns related to the school.
* School Signature (school_signature): Provide your signature to confirm that the information you provided is accurate.

### 12. Form Complete
* Form Complete (form_complete): Indicate that you have completed the form and that the information provided is accurate.
* **Student Signature (student_signature):** Provide your signature to confirm that the information you provided is accurate.
* **Counselor Signature (counselor_signature):** Provide your signature to confirm that the information you provided is accurate.
