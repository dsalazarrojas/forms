# Autism Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Autism Survey is a collection of questions designed to understand the experiences and needs of individuals with autism, as well as their relationships with people with autism. The survey aims to gather information about your daily life, relationships, and preferences to improve support services and resources for individuals with autism.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the survey honestly and to the best of your ability.
- Answer each question carefully, considering your personal experiences and feelings.
- Use a valid email address for communication purposes.
- Select the most accurate response for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Respondent Information
* **Respondent Information**: This page provides an overview of the survey and a space to record your name (not required).
  (No explanation needed as this is a note type field)

### Full Name
* **Full Name (Full Name)**: Please enter your full name.
  This field asks for your full name as a way to identify you for communication purposes.

### Age
* **Age (Age)**: Enter your current age in years.
  This field is to understand your age for demographic purposes.

### Email Address
* **Email Address (Email Address)**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
  This field is to ensure we can communicate with you effectively.

### Relationship to Autism
* **Relationship to Autism (Relationship to Autism)**: Select how you are related to autism.
  This field helps us understand your connection to autism and gather data on different types of relationships.

### Living Situation
* **Current Living Situation (Living Situation)**: Select where you currently live.
  This field aims to understand your daily life circumstances.

### Openness to Living with Someone with Autism
* **Openness to Living with Someone with Autism (Openness to Living with Someone with Autism)**: Choose your comfort level with living with someone who has autism.
  This question helps us understand your comfort with co-habiting with someone with autism.

### Social Life Concerns
* **Social Life Concerns (Social Life Concerns)**: Select all concerns you have about your social life.
  This field aims to uncover social challenges you face related to autism.

### Previous Support Experience
* **Previous Support Experience (Previous Support Experience)**: Select if you have supported someone with autism before.
  This question assesses your past experience in supporting someone with autism.

### Communication Comfort
* **Communication Comfort Level (Communication Comfort Level)**: Choose your comfort level with different communication styles.
  This field explores your adaptability with communication styles when interacting with someone with autism.

### Routine Flexibility
* **Routine Flexibility (Routine Flexibility)**: Select how flexible you are with daily routines.
  This question gauges your adaptability with routines.

### Sensory Environment Preferences
* **Sensory Environment Preferences (Sensory Environment Preferences)**: Select all sensory considerations important to you.
  This field explores your sensory sensitivities and their impact on your daily life.

### Community Resources Awareness
* **Community Resources Awareness (Community Resources Awareness)**: Select how aware you are of autism support resources in your community.
  This question assesses your knowledge of local resources that support individuals with autism.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)**: Enter any other thoughts or comments you would like to share.
  This field is for any additional thoughts or feedback you may have.
