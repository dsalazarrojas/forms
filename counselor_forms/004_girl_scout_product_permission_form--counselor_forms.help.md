# Girl Scout Product Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Girl Scout Product Permission Form is used to obtain permission from a Girl Scout to sell Girl Scout products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the title of the event or activity where you will be selling Girl Scout products.
2. Select the category of the product being sold.
3. Enter a brief description of the product.
4. Select the products being sold.
5. Enter the price per unit of the product.
6. Enter the discount rate for the product.
7. Enter the start and end dates for the sale.
8. Select the counselor who will be responsible for the sale.
9. If you select "Other" as the counselor, enter their name in the "Other" field.
10. Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Girl Scout Product Permission Form** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the event or activity where you will be selling Girl Scout products.
- **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the category of the product being sold (e.g., Girl Scout, Non-Girl Scout, Other).
- **Description** (`form_description`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe the product being sold.
- **Products** (`form_products`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the individual products being sold (e.g., Cookie, Magazine, Other).
- **Price per Unit** (`form_price`, number, required/optional): Enter the price per unit of the product.
- **Discount** (`form_discount`, number, required/optional): Enter the discount rate for the product.
- **Date Start** (`form_date_start`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date for the sale.
- **Date End** (`form_date_end`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date for the sale.
- **Counselor** (`form_counselor`, select_one, required/optional): Select the counselor who will be responsible for the sale (e.g., Jim, Jane, Other).
- **Other Counselor** (`form_counselor_other`, text, required/optional): If you select "Other" as the counselor, enter their name here.
- **Signature** (`form_signature`, note, required/optional): Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the terms.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you select "Other" for the category, enter a brief description of the product.
* If you select "Other" for the counselor, enter the name of the person who will be responsible for the sale.
* Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the terms.
