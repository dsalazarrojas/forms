# External Factor Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The External Factor Record Form is used to collect and record information about external factors that can impact an organization or project. This form helps gather data on these factors to inform strategic decisions and risk assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the list of external factors on each page (political, economic, social, technological, legal, and environmental).
2.  Select the factors that are relevant to your organization or project.
3.  Answer the questions on each relevant page.
4.  Fill out the fields completely, but only one entry per factor is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. political (Page 1)
 Political factor

*   **political**: This field is for recording any political factors that affect your organization or project. Please fill in your answer briefly.

### 2. economic (Page 2)
 Economic factor

*   **economic**: This field is for recording any economic factors that affect your organization or project. Please fill in your answer briefly.

### 3. social (Page 3)
 Social factor

*   **social**: This field is for recording any social factors that affect your organization or project. Please fill in your answer briefly.

### 4. technological (Page 4)
 Technological factor

*   **technological**: This field is for recording any technological factors that affect your organization or project. Please fill in your answer briefly.

### 5. legal (Page 5)
 Legal factor

*   **legal**: This field is for recording any legal factors that affect your organization or project. Please fill in your answer briefly.

### 6. environmental (Page 6)
 Environmental factor

*   **environmental**: This field is for recording any environmental factors that affect your organization or project. Please fill in your answer briefly.

### 7. threats (Page 7)
 Threats

*   **threats**: Choose all applicable threats:
    *   Yes
    *   No

### 8. opportunities (Page 8)
 Opportunities

*   **opportunities**: Choose all applicable opportunities:
    *   Yes
    *   No

### 9. category (Page 9)
 Category

*   **category**: Choose one category:
    *   Political
    *   Economic
    *   Social
    *   Technological
    *   Legal
    *   Environmental

### 10. description (Page 10)
 Description

*   **description**: Briefly describe the external factor.

### 11. assigned_tool (Page 11)
 Assigned Tool

*   **assigned_tool**: This field is for recording any tools or resources assigned for managing the external factor. Please fill in your answer briefly.

### 12. category (Page 12)
 Category

*   **category**: This field is for recording the category of the external factor. Please fill in your answer briefly.

## Tips
- Be sure to only fill out this form once for each external factor.
- If a factor is not relevant, leave it blank.
- Use clear and concise language when filling in the description field.
- For the category field, choose one of the provided options.
- For the threats and opportunities fields, select all applicable options.
