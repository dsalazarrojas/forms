# Homeownership Readiness Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about your financial and employment history, which will be used to assess your readiness to become a homeowner. Please complete this form carefully and accurately to ensure a thorough evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled correctly before submission.
3. Double-check the form before submitting it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (client_info)** (`client_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your name, address, phone number, and email for contact purposes.
* **Household Income (household_income)** (`household_income`, `number`, optional): This field is used to collect your gross household income before tax. Please enter this amount accurately.
* **Assets (assets)** (`assets`, `text`, optional): List your assets, including properties, bonds, stocks, or other valuables to help us understand your financial stability.
* **Debt (debt)** (`debt`, `text`, optional): Disclose any outstanding debts, including credit cards, loans, or mortgages.
* **Credit (credit)** (`credit`, `text`, optional): Share information about your credit history, including any outstanding accounts, collections, or inquiries.
* **Rental History (rental_history)** (`rental_history`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your rental history, including the length of tenancy and previous addresses.
* **Mortgage History (mortgage_history)** (`mortgage_history`, `text`, optional): If applicable, share information about your past mortgage experiences, including loan terms and repayment history.
* **Employment Status (employment_status)** (`employment_status`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your current employment status, including your job title, employer, and years of service.
* **Income Verification (income_verification)** (`income_verification`, `text`, optional): Provide evidence or proof of your income, such as pay stubs or tax returns.
* **Income Proof (income_proof)** (`income_proof`, `text`, optional): Upload or link to evidence of your income, such as bank statements or W-2 forms.
* **Employment Verification (employment_verification)** (`employment_verification`, `text`, optional): Provide verification of your employment, including a letter from your employer or HR department.
* **Employment Proof (employment_proof)** (`employment_proof`, `text`, optional): Upload or link to proof of your employment, such as an employment contract or letter from your employer.
* **Rental Property Info (rental_property_info)** (`rental_property_info`, `text`, optional): Share information about any rental properties you own or lease.
* **Employment History (employment_history)** (`employment_history`, `text`, optional): Provide a detailed employment history, including job titles, employers, and years of service.
* **Employment Details (employment_details)** (`employment_details`, `text`, optional): Describe your current and past employment situations, including job responsibilities and reasons for employment changes.
