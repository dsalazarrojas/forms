# Spiritual Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spiritual Survey form is designed to collect information about your spiritual background, beliefs, and experiences. This information will help us understand your personal and spiritual journey, which is essential for us to provide you with the best possible support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Spiritual Survey form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Provide detailed and honest responses to each question.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Spiritual Survey** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This field is a brief introduction to the form. Please type a short paragraph describing your spiritual background.
* **Beliefs** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the options provided:
	+ Christianity
	+ Buddhism
	+ Islam
* **Practices** (`3`, `note`, required/optional): Write a few sentences describing your spiritual practices.
* **Experiences** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your spiritual experiences in detail, including any significant events or moments that have shaped your spiritual journey.
* **Date of Birth** (`6`, `date`, required/optional): Type your date of birth in the mm/dd/yy format (e.g., 02/15/1990).
* **Spiritual Practices** (`13`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the options provided:
	+ Every day
	+ Several times a week
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Spiritual Background** (`14`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the options provided:
	+ Christian
	+ Buddhist
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Reflections** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Describe how you feel about your spiritual experiences.
* **Date** (`18`, `date`, required/optional): Type your current date in the mm/dd/yy format (e.g., 02/15/2023).
* **Email** (`19`, `email`, required/optional): Type your email address.
* **Additional Comments** (`20`, `text`, required/optional): Write any additional comments or reflections related to your spiritual journey.
* **Spiritual Identity** (`21`, `note`, required/optional): Write any additional thoughts or reflections related to your spiritual identity.
* **Spiritual Practices** (`22`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the options that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Frequency of Practice** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): Describe how often you engage in spiritual practices.
* **Time** (`24`, `time`, required/optional): Type the duration of your spiritual practice (e.g., 1 hour, 30 minutes).
* **Frequency of Spiritual Practice** (`25`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the options provided:
	+ Yes
	+ No

Please note that this is a self-reporting form, and the information provided is kept confidential. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
