# Character Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Character Reference Form is designed to gather information about a person's character, strengths, and areas of concern from a referee who knows them well. This form helps in evaluating a person's character and making informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start by filling in the required fields: Referee name, subject name, relationship to subject, strengths, overall recommendation, and reference date.
* You can optionally provide referee contact information for verification purposes.
* If you have known the subject for a certain amount of time, please specify the length of your relationship with the subject in the "Duration of relationship" field.
* Use the "Relationship to Subject" field to select how you know the subject (Employer, Colleague, Friend, Teacher, or Other).
* Provide any notable positive characteristics (strengths) and observed weaknesses or issues (areas of concern) about the subject in the corresponding fields.
* Finally, indicate your overall recommendation for the subject, selecting from the options: Strongly recommend, Recommend, Recommend with reservations, or Do not recommend.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referee Name** (`referee_name`, `text`, required): The name of the person providing the reference.
* **Referee Email** (`referee_email`, `email`, optional): Optional contact information for verification purposes.
* **Subject Name** (`subject_name`, `text`, required): The name of the person being referenced.
* **Relationship to Subject** (`relationship_to_subject`, `select_one`, required): How you know the subject (Employer, Colleague, Friend, Teacher, or Other).
* **Duration of Relationship** (`duration_of_relationship`, `text`, optional): The approximate length of your relationship with the subject.
* **Strengths** (`strengths`, `text`, required): Notable positive characteristics about the subject.
* **Areas of Concern** (`areas_of_concern`, `text`, optional): Any observed weaknesses or issues about the subject.
* **Overall Recommendation** (`overall_recommendation`, `select_one`, required): Select your overall recommendation for the subject (Strongly recommend, Recommend, Recommend with reservations, or Do not recommend).
* **Reference Date** (`reference_date`, `date`, required): The date you submitted the reference.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when providing your character reference.
- Ensure the information you provide is consistent and fair.
- The information collected will be used for evaluation purposes, so please keep it professional and respectful.
