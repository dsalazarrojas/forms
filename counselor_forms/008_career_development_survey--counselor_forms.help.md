# Career Development Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Career Development Survey is designed to gather information about your career aspirations, goals, and challenges. This data will help your manager and HR create a development plan tailored to your career goals and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Be specific about your goals and aspirations.
3. Select all relevant options that apply to your career development.
4. If a question does not apply to you, select "Not Applicable" or "Not Interested" if available.
5. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Employee Name
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.

### 2. Employee ID
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID number.

### 3. Current Department
* **Current Department** (`current_department`, text, required): Enter the department you are currently working in.

### 4. Current Position or Job Title
* **Current Position or Job Title** (`current_position`, text, required): Enter your current job title or position.

### 5. Years with Company
* **Years with Company** (`tenure_years`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have worked with the company.

### 6. Overall Career Satisfaction
* **Overall Career Satisfaction** (`career_satisfaction`, select_one, required):
	+ Very Satisfied: Highly satisfied with my current role.
	+ Satisfied: Somewhat satisfied with my current role.
	+ Neutral: Neutral about my current role.
	+ Unsatisfied: Somewhat dissatisfied with my current role.
	+ Very Unsatisfied: Highly dissatisfied with my current role.

### 7. Growth Opportunities
* **Do you see growth opportunities in your current role?** (`growth_opportunity`, select_one, required):
	+ Yes - strong opportunities: Many growth opportunities available.
	+ Some - limited opportunities: Some growth opportunities available.
	+ No - very limited opportunities: No growth opportunities available.
	+ Unsure: Not sure about growth opportunities.

### 8. Career Aspirations
* **What are your long-term career aspirations?** (`career_aspirations`, text, required): Describe your career aspirations and goals.

### 9. Promotion Interest
* **Are you interested in a promotion or advancement?** (`promotion_interest`, select_one, required):
	+ Yes - promotion within current department: Interested in promotion within current department.
	+ Yes - promotion to different department: Interested in promotion to different department.
	+ Yes - lateral move for new skills: Interested in lateral move for new skills.
	+ No - content in current role: Not interested in a promotion.
	+ Unsure: Not sure about a promotion.

### 10. Skills Development
* **What Skills Do You Want to Develop?** (`skills_gaps`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Technical or software skills: Develop technical or software skills.
	+ Leadership and management: Develop leadership and management skills.
	+ Communication and presentation: Develop communication and presentation skills.
	+ Project management: Develop project management skills.
	+ Industry-specific expertise: Develop industry-specific expertise.
	+ Problem-solving abilities: Develop problem-solving abilities.
	+ Customer service: Develop customer service skills.
	+ Sales skills: Develop sales skills.
	+ Data analysis: Develop data analysis skills.
	+ Other professional skills: Develop other professional skills.

### 11. Training Needs
* **Training or Development Needs** (`training_needs`, text, optional): Describe your training or development needs.

### 12. Preferred Learning Methods
* **Preferred Learning Methods** (`preferred_learning`, select_multiple, required):
	+ In-person workshops or seminars: Prefer in-person workshops or seminars.
	+ Online courses or webinars: Prefer online courses or webinars.
	+ One-on-one coaching or mentoring: Prefer one-on-one coaching or mentoring.
	+ On-the-job training: Prefer on-the-job training.
	+ Self-paced learning materials: Prefer self-paced learning materials.
	+ Group team training: Prefer group team training.
	+ Certification programs: Prefer certification programs.

### 13. Mentorship Interest
* **Interest in Mentorship** (`mentorship_interest`, select_one, required):
	+ Yes - I want a mentor: Interested in being mentored.
	+ Yes - I want to mentor someone: Interested in mentoring someone.
	+ Both: Interested in both mentoring and being mentored.
	+ No - not interested: Not interested in mentorship.

### 14. Networking and Professional Development Goals
* **Networking and Professional Development Goals** (`networking_goals`, text, optional): Describe your networking and professional development goals.

### 15. Barriers to Growth
* **Barriers to Career Growth** (`barriers_to_growth`, text, optional): Describe any barriers that prevent you from advancing your career.

### 16. Manager Support
* **Does your manager support your development?** (`manager_support`, select_one, required):
	+ Yes - very supportive: My manager is very supportive.
	+ Somewhat - some support: My manager shows some support.
	+ Minimal - little support: My manager shows minimal support.
	+ No - not supportive: My manager does not support my development.

### 17. Company Resources
* **Are you aware of company development resources?** (`company_resources`, select_one, required):
	+ Yes - aware and using them: I am aware of and use company resources.
	+ Yes - aware but not using them: I am aware of company resources but do not use them.
	+ No - not aware: I am not aware of company resources.

### 18. Certification Interest
* **Interested in Professional Certifications?** (`certification_interest`, select_one, optional):
	+ Yes - considering one: Interested in obtaining a professional certification.
	+ Yes - already pursuing: Already pursuing a professional certification.
	+ Maybe - need more information: Interested in obtaining a professional certification but need more information.
	+ No - not interested: Not interested in obtaining a professional certification.

### 19. Certification Type
* **What Certifications Are You Interested In?** (`certification_type`, text, optional): Describe the type of professional certification you are interested in.

### 20. Career Change
* **Considering a Career Change Within the Industry?** (`industry_change`, select_one, required):
	+ Yes - definitely: Considering a career change within the industry.
	+ Maybe - open to opportunities: Considering a career change within the industry but open to opportunities.
	+ No - content with current path: Not considering a career change within the industry.

### 21. Work-Life Balance Satisfaction
* **Work-Life Balance Satisfaction** (`work_life_balance`, select_one, optional):
	+ Excellent: Work-life balance is excellent.
	+ Good: Work-life balance is good.
	+ Acceptable: Work-life balance is acceptable.
	+ Needs improvement: Work-life balance needs improvement.
	+ Unsustainable: Work-life balance is unsustainable.

### 22. Flexible Work Arrangements
* **Are Flexible Work Arrangements Important to You?** (`flexible_arrangements`, select_one, optional):
	+ Yes - essential: Flexible work arrangements are essential.
	+ Yes - important: Flexible work arrangements are important.
	+ Somewhat - nice to have: Flexible work arrangements are nice to have.
	+ No - not important: Not interested in flexible work arrangements.

### 23. Career Goals Timeline
* **Timeline for Career Goals** (`career_goals_timeline`, select_one, required):
	+ 1-2 years: Achieve career goals within 1-2 years.
	+ 3-5 years: Achieve career goals within 3-5 years.
	+ 5+ years: Achieve career goals within 5+ years.
	+ No specific timeline: Do not have a specific timeline for career goals.

### 24. Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments or Concerns** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Describe any additional comments or concerns you have about your career development.
