# unemployment_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The unemployment_claim_form is designed to collect accurate and complete information from individuals claiming unemployment benefits. Completing this form ensures that your claim is processed correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Personal Details section, which includes basic information about yourself.
2. Next, provide your Contact Information, which is essential for communication and follow-up.
3. Then, describe your Work History, including any relevant job-related details.
4. Specify the Unemployment Duration, which is the time you have been out of work.
5. Finally, provide information about your Previous Employment, which helps to understand your current situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details** (`title`, `text`, not required): This section includes your name, address, and any other identifying information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, not required): Please provide your phone number and email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Work History** (`work_history`, `text`, not required): Describe your previous work experience, including job titles, employers, and job responsibilities.
* **Unemployment Duration** (`unemployment_duration`, `text`, not required): Specify the exact duration of your unemployment, including the start date and end date (if applicable).
* **Previous Employment** (`previous_employment`, `text`, not required): Provide information about your last job, including employer name, job title, and reason for leaving (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays in processing your claim.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your employment records or other relevant documents.
* You can refer to your employment records or other relevant documents for any information not available in this form.
* If you need assistance, do not hesitate to contact us for support.
