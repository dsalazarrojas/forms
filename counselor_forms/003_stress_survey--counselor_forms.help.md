# Stress Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you understand your stress experiences, including your stress triggers, level, frequency, duration, impact, management techniques, and any other stressors not listed. Please fill out this survey to gain a better understanding of your stress levels and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your stress triggers from the list on the first page.
2. Choose how you feel right now from the scale on the second page.
3. Choose how often you experience these feelings on the third page.
4. Select how long you've been feeling stressed on the fourth page.
5. Choose how much stress has impacted your daily life on the fifth page.
6. Select what helps you manage stress on the sixth page.
7. If you experience other stressors not listed, type them in on the seventh page.
8. Describe the other stressors you've listed on the eighth page.
9. If you have any other comments or questions, type them in on the ninth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are your stress triggers?** (`stress_survey_questions`, select_multiple, required)
	+ This question asks you to select all the things that cause you stress, such as work, home, or social media.
	+ Choose all that apply.
* **How do you feel right now?** (`stress_level`, number, required)
	+ This question asks you to select a number on the scale to indicate your current stress level.
	+ Choose a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being "not stressed at all" and 5 being "extremely stressed".
* **How often do you experience these feelings?** (`stress_frequency`, select_one, required)
	+ This question asks you to select how often you experience these feelings.
	+ Choose a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being "rarely" and 5 being "almost always".
* **How long have you been feeling stressed?** (`stress_duration`, select_one, required)
	+ This question asks you to select how long you've been feeling stressed.
	+ Choose a number from 1 to 10, with 1 being "a few days" and 10 being "over a year".
* **How much has stress impacted your daily life?** (`stress_impact`, select_one, required)
	+ This question asks you to select how much stress has impacted your daily life.
	+ Choose a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being "not at all" and 5 being "extremely".
* **What helps you manage stress?** (`stress_management`, select_multiple, required)
	+ This question asks you to select what helps you manage stress.
	+ Choose all that apply.
* **Are there any other things that stress you that weren't listed?** (`other_stressors`, text, required)
	+ This question asks you to type in any other things that stress you that weren't listed.
	+ Be as specific as possible.
* **If yes, please describe them** (`other_stressors_comments`, text, required)
	+ This question asks you to describe the other stressors you've listed.
	+ Be as specific as possible.
* **Do you have any other comments or questions about this survey?** (`stress_survey_comments`, text, required)
	+ This question asks you to type in any other comments or questions you have about this survey.
	+ Be as specific as possible.
