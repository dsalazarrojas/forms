# counselling_initial_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals collect and understand a patient's background and health situation for informed decision-making and effective care.
## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form with accurate and relevant information about the patient, including their personal details, medical history, and treatment goals.
2. Answer each question with the relevant details, and do not leave any sections blank.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, Optional): Enter the patient's basic details such as name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, Optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous treatments or conditions.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, Optional): List the patient's current symptoms or conditions.
* **Mental Status** (`mental_status`, `text`, Optional): Share any relevant mental health status information.
* **Emotional Status** (`emotional_status`, `text`, Optional): Provide details on the patient's emotional state.
* **Social History** (`social_history`, `text`, Optional): Share the patient's social history, including family details.
* **Treatment Goals** (`treatment_goals`, `text`, Optional): Specify the patient's treatment goals and objectives.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, Optional): List any medical conditions or allergies.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, Optional): Describe any allergies or sensitivities.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, Optional): Provide details on medications taken.
* **Medical History Details** (`medical_history_details`, `text`, Optional): Add any additional medical history details.
* **Patient Support System** (`patient_support_system`, `text`, Optional): Share information on the patient's support system.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, `text`, Optional): Share the patient's family medical history.
* **Mental Health Services** (`mental_health_services`, `text`, Optional): Specify any mental health services received.
* **Mental Health Diagnosis** (`mental_health_diagnosis`, `text`, Optional): Describe any mental health diagnosis.
* **Mental Health Plan** (`mental_health_plan`, `text`, Optional): Provide details on mental health plans.
* **Mental Health Prognosis** (`mental_health_prognosis`, `text`, Optional): Share mental health prognosis.
* **Mental Health Recommendations** (`mental_health_recommendations`, `text`, Optional): Specify mental health recommendations.
* **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, `text`, Optional): Share the patient's medical record.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, Optional): Provide an emergency contact's information.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `text`, Optional): Sign the form to indicate consent.
* **Patient Demographics** (`patient_demographics`, `text`, Optional): Add patient demographics.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, `text`, Optional): Specify the healthcare provider's information.
* **Patient Counselor Relationship** (`patient_counselor_relationship`, `text`, Optional): Describe the patient's relationship with their counselor.
* **Patient Counseling Goals** (`patient_counseling_goals`, `text`, Optional): Specify the patient's counseling goals.

Note: All fields marked as "Optional" can be left blank if no information is available.
