# pre-assessment-questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre-assessment-questionnaire is a comprehensive form designed to gather essential information from language learners or students. This form helps us understand your background, language skills, and learning preferences to provide a tailored learning experience. Please take a few minutes to complete this form, and we'll use the information provided to assess your current level and create a personalized plan for your language development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with your best answers.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and clear information.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (3, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (4, select_one, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Phone** (5, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (6, email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Address** (7, text, required): Enter your current address for contact and record purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (8, text, required): Provide emergency contact information, including name and relationship.
* **Medical Condition** (9, text, required): If you have any medical condition that may affect your learning, please describe.
* **Medical Treatment** (10, text, required): If you have any medical treatment or allergies, please specify.
* **Language Competency** (11, text, required): Describe your language competency level.
* **Course English Level** (12, select_one, required): Select your current English level from the options provided.
* **Course English Readiness** (13, select_one, required): Indicate whether you're ready to start the course.
* **Other Languages Spoken** (14, text, required): List any other languages you speak.
* **Languages Spoken at Home** (15, select_one, required): Indicate whether you speak any languages at home.
* **Language Skill Proficiency** (16, text, required): Describe your language skill proficiency level (optional).
* **Language Learning Goals** (17, text, required): Specify your language learning goals.
* **Prior English Education** (18, text, required): Describe your prior English education.
* **English Language Assessment** (19, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **English Language Assessment Date** (20, date, required): Enter the date of your English language assessment.
* **English Proficiency Exam** (21, text, required): Enter the name of your English proficiency exam.
* **English Proficiency Date** (22, date, required): Enter the date of your English proficiency exam.
* **Language Translation Assistance** (23, select_one, required): Indicate whether you need language translation assistance.
* **Language Translation Assistance Date** (24, date, required): Enter the date you require language translation assistance.
* **Other Language Translation Assistance** (25, text, required): Specify any other language translation assistance you need.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us. We're here to support you throughout your learning journey.
