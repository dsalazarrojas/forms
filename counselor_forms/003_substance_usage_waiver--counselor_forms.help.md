# Substance Usage Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Substance Usage Waiver" form is a tool for individuals to disclose their past or current substance usage. This information will be used to assess their suitability for certain privileges or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please provide your user information, including your name and contact details.
2. Answer questions regarding your emergency contact's name and relationship to you.
3. Disclose any information about the substance used, if applicable.
4. If you have any medical conditions, please provide a brief description.
5. Answer the waiver question regarding the signing of the waiver.
6. If necessary, select a medical facility where you are currently receiving treatment.
7. If necessary, provide additional details about your medical facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: This field is a text area where you can enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Please enter the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Individual**: Describe your relationship to the emergency contact (e.g., mother, father, etc.).
* **Substance Used**: Describe the substance you are using, including the type and amount used.
* **Medical Condition**: If you have any medical conditions, provide a brief description.
* **Waiver Signed**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have signed the waiver.
* **Date**: Enter the date you signed the waiver.
* **Time**: Enter the time you signed the waiver.
* **Medical Facility**: If you are currently receiving treatment at a medical facility, select the option that best describes the location.
* **Relationship Type**: If you have a relationship type, please specify.
* **Other Medical Facility**: If you select "Other" as your medical facility, please enter the name of the facility.
