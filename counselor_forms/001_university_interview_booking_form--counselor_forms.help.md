# University Interview Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to fill out the University Interview Booking Form for students to book an interview with a counselor.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Student Name** field with your full name.
2. Select a preferred interview time from the **Preferred Interview Time** dropdown menu.
3. Check **Confirm Interview** to confirm your booking.
4. (Optional) Fill in any additional information in **Additional Notes**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (Text)**: Enter your full name.
* **Preferred Interview Time (Select Multiple)**: Choose the time slot you prefer for the interview. Options are 10:00 am, 11:00 am, 12:00 pm, 2:00 pm, 3:00 pm.
* **Confirm Interview (Select One)**: Check this box to confirm your booking.
* **Additional Notes (Note)**: Enter any additional information or questions you have for the counselor.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address so the counselor can contact you.
* **Phone Number (Text)**: Enter your phone number for the counselor to contact you.
* **Address (Text)**: Enter your address for the counselor to contact you.
* **Additional Notes (Note)**: Enter any additional information or questions you have for the counselor.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (Student Name and Confirm Interview).
* Select a preferred interview time that suits you best.
* You can use the Additional Notes field to enter any special requests or questions you have for the counselor.
