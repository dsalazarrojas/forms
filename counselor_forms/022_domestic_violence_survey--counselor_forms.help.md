# Domestic Violence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals who have witnessed or experienced domestic violence gather information on their experiences and access support.

## How To Complete This Form

- **Please take your time and answer each question to the best of your ability.**
- **This form is anonymous and confidential, and your responses will not be shared without your consent.**
- **You can skip any questions that you do not feel comfortable answering or that are not relevant to your experiences.**
