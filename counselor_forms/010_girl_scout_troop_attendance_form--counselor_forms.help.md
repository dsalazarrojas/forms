# Girl Scout Troop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Girl Scout Troop Attendance Form is a tool for troop leaders to track attendance and notes for their meetings. It helps to gather information on who was present or absent and the reasons for absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the Meeting ID to identify the meeting being recorded.
2. Enter the Meeting Date of the meeting being recorded.
3. Enter the Meeting Location where the meeting was held.
4. If applicable, enter the Troop ID, Leader ID, and Present Count for your reference and tracking purposes.
5. Indicate if any of the troop members were absent and choose the Absent Reason.
6. Enter any Attendance Notes about the meeting.
7. Enter any Meeting Notes about the meeting.
8. Enter any Comments about the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting ID**: Enter the unique identifier for this meeting.
* **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Location**: Enter the location where the meeting was held.
* **Troop ID**: If applicable, enter the ID of the troop being recorded.
* **Leader ID**: If applicable, enter the ID of the leader leading the meeting.
* **Troop Members**: Indicate which troop members were present or absent.
* **Attendance Notes**: Enter any notes about the attendance of the troop members.
* **Absent Reason**: Choose the reason for any absent members.
* **Present Count**: Enter the number of members who were present.
* **Absent Count**: Enter the number of members who were absent.
* **Late Count**: Enter the number of members who were late.
* **Excused Count**: Enter the number of members who were excused for absence.
* **Notes**: Enter any notes about the meeting.
* **Meeting Notes**: Enter any notes about the meeting.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the meeting.
