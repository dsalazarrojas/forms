# mental_health_evaluation_form_for_supervisors - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by supervisors to evaluate the mental health and performance of their team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
* Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
* Complete the form in a quiet and private space where you can focus on the questions without distractions.
* Save your progress frequently to avoid losing your work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_performance** (<type>: Note): This field is for you to take notes on the employee's performance. Please consider the employee's strengths and areas where they need improvement.
* **mental_health_issues** (<type>: Select Multiple): Check all the boxes that apply to the mental health issues you are concerned about. Please note that "Yes" does not necessarily mean a serious issue, but rather an area where the employee may need support or guidance.
* **supervisor_comments** (<type>: Note): In this field, you can add any comments or suggestions you have for the employee. Please be specific and provide concrete examples or ideas for improvement.

## Tips
* Please be honest and objective in your assessment.
* Consider the employee's perspective and feelings when providing feedback.
* Use this form as a tool to help the employee grow and improve, not to criticize or judge.
* Save your progress frequently to avoid losing your work.
