# University Recourse Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Recourse Form is designed to collect information from students about their university resources, including academic records, contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "User Input" section with your personal details.
2. Provide a clear reason for your appeal or request in the "Reason for Appeal" field.
3. Enter your academic record, including dates, in the "Academic Record" field.
4. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
5. Enter your university email address in the "University Email" field.
6. Enter your student ID number in the "Student ID" field.
7. Select your current student status from the options provided in the "Student Status" field.
8. Select the departments or programs you are involved with in the "Department Involved" field.
9. Enter the date you submitted this form in the "Date Submitted" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: This is a text field where you can enter any additional information that is not covered in the other fields.
* **Reason for Appeal**: Please provide a clear and concise reason for your appeal or request.
* **Academic Record**: Enter your academic record, including dates, such as courses taken, grades, or other relevant academic details.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your address, phone number, or other contact details.
* **University Email**: Enter your university email address.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number.
* **Student Status**: Select your current student status from the options provided: Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior.
* **Department Involved**: Select the departments or programs you are involved with.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **University Email**: Enter your email address registered with the university.
* **Student Status**: This field is used to determine your current standing within the university.
* **Department Involved**: Select one or multiple departments or programs you are involved with, such as Business, Engineering, Science, or Arts.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date you submitted this form, in MM/DD/YYYY format.
