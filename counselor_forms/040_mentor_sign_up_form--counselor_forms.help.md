# Mentor Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users who want to become mentors in a mentorship program. It helps us match mentors with mentees based on their expertise, skills, and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each field explanation below.
- Fill in your details accurately and truthfully.
- Make sure you have an email address that you regularly check to ensure smooth communication with us and your potential mentee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Email** (`user_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address that we will use to contact you about your mentorship journey.
* **Enter Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as you would like to be known in the mentorship program.
* **Enter Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required: false): This is an optional field, but it's useful for us to reach out to you for any urgent matters.
* **Enter Location** (`user_location`, text, required: false): Share your location to help us match you with mentees from your area.
* **Enter Education** (`user_education`, text, required: false): Share your educational background to help us get a better understanding of your expertise.
* **Enter Bio** (`user_bio`, note, required: false): Briefly describe yourself, your interests, and what you can offer as a mentor.
* **Enter Experience** (`user_experience`, note, required: false): Share your experience in your chosen field or areas of expertise.
* **Enter Skills** (`user_skills`, note, required: false): List any relevant skills you have that would be beneficial to our mentees.
* **Enter Specialties** (`user_specialties`, select_multiple, required: false): Check the relevant specialties that you have. 
* **Why Do You Want To Be A Mentor?** (`user_why_mentor`, text, required: false): Share your motivation for wanting to be a mentor, and what you hope to achieve through this program.
