# Discipleship Pathway Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help individuals reflect on their current discipleship pathway, which is a key part of discipleship and spiritual growth. It will help identify areas where an individual may need guidance and support to grow in their faith.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question by selecting the relevant options from the drop-down menu or typing your response in the text box. You may select multiple options for certain questions. Make sure to answer all questions that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Spiritual Growth** (Select Multiple):
This question is about your current spiritual growth level. Please select all areas where you feel you are growing in your faith.
	+ 'Yes' if you feel you are growing in this area
	+ 'No' if you feel you are not growing in this area
* **Discipleship Needs** (Text):
Please describe any specific needs you feel you have in your discipleship journey.
* **Discipleship Programs** (Select One):
This question is about whether you are currently participating in any discipleship programs.
	+ 'Yes' if you are participating in a program
	+ 'No' if you are not participating in a program
* **Engagement and Connection** (Text):
Please describe your current level of engagement and connection with others in your discipleship journey.
* **Effectiveness** (Text):
Please describe how effective you feel you are in your discipleship journey.
* **Spiritual Growth Goals** (Select Multiple):
This question is about your spiritual growth goals. Please select all areas where you feel you want to grow in your faith.
	+ 'Yes' if you feel you want to grow in this area
	+ 'No' if you feel you do not want to grow in this area
* **Discipleship Focus Areas** (Select One):
This question is about the area where you currently focus your discipleship efforts.
	+ 'Yes' if you currently focus on this area
