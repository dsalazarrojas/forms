# Counselor Honorarium Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit honorariums for events and presentations. Please fill out the form below to provide necessary information for processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the title of the honorarium in the **Counselor Honorarium Submission Form** field.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your university ID or personnel record in the **Counselor Name** field.
3. Enter the amount of the honorarium in the **Honorarium Amount** field.
4. Select the date of the submission in the **Date** field.
5. Choose the payment method from the dropdown menu in the **Payment Method** field. You may choose from Cash, Check, or Credit Card.
6. Enter a brief reason for the submission in the **Reason** field.
7. Enter a brief description of the submission in the **Description** field.
8. Enter your name in the **Submitter** field as it appears on your university ID or personnel record.
9. Select the submission date in the **Submission Date** field.
10. Choose whether the payment status is **Paid** or **Unpaid** in the **Payment Status** field.
11. If desired, enter any additional notes or comments about the submission in the **Notes** field.
12. Enter any unique identifier for the form, if provided (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Counselor Honorarium Submission Form**: This is the title of the submission. Enter a brief title describing the event or presentation for which you are submitting the honorarium.
* **Counselor Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your university ID or personnel record.
* **Honorarium Amount**: Enter the amount of the honorarium you are requesting.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the submission. Please use the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you plan to pay for the honorarium. Options include Cash, Check, and Credit Card.
* **Reason**: Enter a brief reason for the submission.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the submission.
* **Submitter**: Enter your name as it appears on your university ID or personnel record.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of the submission.
* **Payment Status**: Choose whether the payment status is Paid or Unpaid.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the submission.
* **Form ID**: Enter any unique identifier for the form, if provided.
