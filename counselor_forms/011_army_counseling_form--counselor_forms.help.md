# Army Counseling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Army Counseling Form is a tool for mental health professionals and counselors to gather information about a soldier's mental health status, personal history, and counseling goals. This form helps the counselor understand the soldier's mental state, identify areas for improvement, and develop targeted interventions to support their mental well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have the necessary information before starting.
2. Complete each section of the form according to the instructions provided.
3. Enter information in the relevant fields, using the provided options where applicable.
4. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Soldier Name**
   - Label: Soldier Name
   - Type: Text
   - Required: True
   - Explanation: Enter the soldier's full legal name.

### 2. **Military Rank**
   - Label: Military Rank
   - Type: Text
   - Required: True
   - Explanation: Enter the soldier's current military rank.

### 3. **Service Number or ID**
   - Label: Service Number or ID
   - Type: Text
   - Required: True
   - Explanation: Enter the soldier's service number or ID.

### 4. **Unit or Company**
   - Label: Unit or Company
   - Type: Text
   - Required: True
   - Explanation: Enter the soldier's unit or company.

### 5. **Date of Counseling Session**
   - Label: Date of Counseling Session
   - Type: Date
   - Required: True
   - Explanation: Enter the date of the counseling session.

### 6. **Counselor or Mental Health Professional Name**
   - Label: Counselor or Mental Health Professional Name
   - Type: Text
   - Required: True
   - Explanation: Enter your name and qualifications as a mental health professional.

### 7. **Primary Reason for Counseling**
   - Label: Primary Reason for Counseling
   - Type: Select Multiple
   - Required: True
   - Explanation: Select one or more reasons for counseling from the provided options.

### 8. **Overall Severity of Symptoms**
   - Label: Overall Severity of Symptoms
   - Type: Select One
   - Required: True
   - Explanation: Choose the level of severity of the soldier's symptoms from the provided options.

### 9. **Duration of Issues**
   - Label: How long have these issues been present
   - Type: Select One
   - Required: True
   - Explanation: Choose how long the soldier has been experiencing the issues.

### 10. **Impact on Daily Functioning**
   - Label: Impact on daily functioning
   - Type: Select One
   - Required: True
   - Explanation: Choose the level of impact on the soldier's daily functioning.

### 11. **Previous Counseling or Therapy Experience**
   - Label: Previous counseling or therapy experience
   - Type: Select One
   - Required: True
   - Explanation: Indicate whether the soldier has had previous counseling or therapy experience.

### 12. **Current Medications**
   - Label: Current medications
   - Type: Text
   - Required: False
   - Explanation: List any medications the soldier is currently taking.

### 13. **Substance Use or Concerns**
   - Label: Substance use or concerns
   - Type: Text
   - Required: False
   - Explanation: Indicate if the soldier has any substance use or concerns.

### 14. **Counseling Goals**
   - Label: Counseling Goals
   - Type: Text
   - Required: True
   - Explanation: Enter the soldier's goals for counseling.

### 15. **Recommended Actions or Treatment Plan**
   - Label: Recommended Actions or Treatment Plan
   - Type: Text
   - Required: True
   - Explanation: Develop a plan for the soldier's treatment and recommended actions.

### 16. **Follow-up Needed**
   - Label: Follow-up needed
   - Type: Select One
   - Required: True
   - Explanation: Indicate whether follow-up counseling is needed and the urgency of the situation.

## Tips
- Double-check the required fields before submitting the form.
- Ensure all fields are completed thoroughly and accurately.
- Review the form with the soldier to ensure they understand the purpose and process.
- Use the gathered information to develop an effective treatment plan for the soldier.
