# Client Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Survey Form is designed to gather feedback and data from clients about their experiences with a counselor or therapist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Full Name and Email Address in the first two fields.
2. Select the Session Date of the counseling session you wish to provide feedback on.
3. Enter the Counselor Name (if you wish to provide feedback).
4. In the "Counseling Experience" section, you can provide any additional comments or information about your experience with the counselor.
5. Answer the questions in the "Goals and Progress" section about your goals and progress during the counseling sessions.
6. In the "Satisfaction Ratings" section, provide your feedback on the counselor's understanding of your issues, their support, comfort with the environment, and overall satisfaction with the counseling service.
7. In the "Feedback" section, you can provide any additional comments or suggestions about your experience with the counselor.
8. Finally, indicate if you would recommend this counselor to others.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the counseling session you are providing feedback on.
* **Counselor Name** (`counselor_name`, `text`, optional): If you wish to provide feedback, enter the name of the counselor who provided your services.
* **-- Counselling Experience --** (`experience_section`, `note`, required): You can provide any additional comments or information about your experience with the counselor.
* **Number of sessions attended** (`sessions_attended`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of sessions you attended.
* **Main reason for seeking counseling** (`reason_for_counseling`, `text`, optional): Enter the main reason you were seeking counseling for.
* **What were your goals when you started?** (`counseling_goals`, `text`, required): Enter your goals at the beginning of the counseling process.
* **Progress toward your goals** (`goal_progress`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of progress you have made toward your goals.
* **Counselor understanding of your issues** (`counselor_understanding`, `select_one`, required): Select how well you felt the counselor understood your issues.
* **Counselor provided adequate support** (`counselor_support`, `select_one`, required): Select how well you felt the counselor supported you.
* **Comfort with the counseling environment** (`environment_comfort`, `select_one`, required): Select how comfortable you felt with the counseling environment.
* **Overall satisfaction with the counseling service** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Select how satisfied you are with the counseling service.
* **What did the counselor do well?** (`strengths`, `text`, optional): Enter any strengths you observed in the counselor's performance.
* **What could be improved?** (`improvements`, `text`, optional): Enter any suggestions for improvement.
* **Would you recommend this counselor?** (`recommendation`, `select_one`, required): Select if you would recommend this counselor to others.
