# how_well_do_you_know_your_partner_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a quiz designed to assess how well you know your partner. It is not intended to be a comprehensive test, but rather a fun and informative way to reflect on your relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review each question carefully and answer based on your knowledge about your partner.
2. Select all the options that apply to your partner's characteristics, interests, and situation.
3. Don't worry if you're not sure about something - just do your best and mark the options that you think might be true.
4. When you're finished, you can review your results and learn something new about your partner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
