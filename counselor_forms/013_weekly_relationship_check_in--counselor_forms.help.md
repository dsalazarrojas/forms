# Weekly Relationship Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Relationship Check In form is a tool for relationship partners to record and reflect on their progress, challenges, and goals. It aims to provide a structured and consistent way of maintaining regular check-ins between the relationship partner and their counselor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* `Relationship Goals`: Describe your relationship goals and objectives for the current week.
	* `Progress Last Week`: Describe your progress from the previous week.
	* `Next Steps Last Week`: Outline your next steps for the current week.
	* `Relationship Check In Date`: Enter the date of the current relationship check in.
	* `Relationship Check In Time`: Enter the time of the current relationship check in.
	* `Relationship Check In Form ID`: Enter the form ID (unique identifier for this form).
	* `Assigned User`: Enter the name of the assigned user (relationship partner's name).
	* `Client ID`: Enter the client ID (unique identifier for the client).
2. Fill in the optional fields:
	* `Last Week`: Provide a brief overview of your relationship from the previous week.
	* `Challenges Last Week`: Describe any challenges you faced in the previous week.
	* `Successes Last Week`: Describe any successes or achievements from the previous week.
	* `Therapist Comments`: Add any comments or feedback from your therapist.
	* `Submitted By`: Enter your name (relationship partner's name).
	* `Submitted At`: Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
	* `Updated By`: Enter the name of the user who last updated the form.
	* `Updated At`: Enter the date and time the form was last updated.
	* `Created By`: Enter the name of the user who created the form.
	* `Created At`: Enter the date and time the form was created.
	* `Relationship Check In Phone`: Enter your phone number (optional).
	* `Relationship Check In Email`: Enter your email address (optional).
	* `Relationship Check In Address`: Enter your address (optional).
