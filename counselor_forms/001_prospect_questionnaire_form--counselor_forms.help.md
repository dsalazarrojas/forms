# Prospect Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a prospect or potential client for business purposes. It includes contact information, background, interests, and skills to help connect with them effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely, ensuring you have the necessary permissions and comply with all relevant data protection laws and regulations.
2. Review each page carefully before proceeding to ensure you understand what information is being asked for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contact Information (Page 1)
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): This page requires your contact information. Please fill in your name, email, and any other relevant contact details to help the prospect or client know how to get in touch with you.
### Background (Page 2)
* **Prospect Background** (`prospect_background`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the prospect's background, including their job title, company, or relevant professional experience.
### Interests (Page 3)
* **Prospect Interests** (`prospect_interests`, `text`, required/optional): List the prospect's interests, hobbies, or areas of expertise.
### Skills (Page 4)
* **Prospect Skills** (`prospect_skills`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the prospect's relevant skills, including software proficiency, languages spoken, or other relevant abilities.
### Tips
* Double-check your contact information to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Be specific and honest when describing the prospect's background, interests, and skills.
* Review each page carefully before proceeding to ensure you understand what information is being asked for.
