# Miscarriage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Miscarriage Survey is a form designed to collect information about experiences related to miscarriage, including pain levels, medical interventions, and stillbirths. This form is intended for use in a healthcare setting to gather data for research or clinical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Miscarriage Survey, follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose the relevant option from the provided list.
3. For text fields, provide a brief explanation or description.
4. Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
