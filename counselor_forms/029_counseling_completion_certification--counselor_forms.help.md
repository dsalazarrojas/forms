# Counseling Completion Certification - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the completion of a counseling program for a client. It includes fields to gather information about the client, program details, and completion dates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documentation before starting the form.
2. Fill in the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
3. Enter the program's name in the "Program Name" field.
4. Enter the date the program was completed in the "Date Completed" field.
5. Enter the counselor's name in the "Counselor Name" field.
6. If applicable, enter the program start date in the "Program Start Date" field.
7. If applicable, enter the program end date in the "Program End Date" field.
8. Enter the total hours completed by the client in the "Total Hours Completed" field.
9. Enter the number of sessions completed in the "Sessions Completed" field.
10. Enter the number of sessions attended in the "Sessions Attended" field.
11. Enter the total hours attended in the "Hours Attended" field.
12. Enter any progress notes about the client's program in the "Progress Notes" field.
13. Finally, enter the date of certification in the "Certification Date" field, and the name of the person who certified the completion in the "Certified By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional: false): This field is used to enter the client's name.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required/optional: false): This field is used to enter the name of the program the client is participating in.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, required/optional: false): This field is used to enter the date the client completed their program.
* **Counselor Name** (`counselor_name`, text, required/optional: false): This field is used to enter your name as the counselor.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, required/optional: false): This field is used to enter the start date of the client's program.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, required/optional: false): This field is used to enter the end date of the client's program.
* **Total Hours Completed** (`total_hours_completed`, number, required/optional: false): This field is used to enter the total number of hours completed by the client.
* **Sessions Completed** (`sessions_completed`, number, required/optional: false): This field is used to enter the number of sessions completed.
* **Sessions Attended** (`sessions_attended`, number, required/optional: false): This field is used to enter the number of sessions attended.
* **Hours Attended** (`hours_attended`, number, required/optional: false): This field is used to enter the total number of hours attended.
* **Progress Notes** (`progress_notes`, text, required/optional: false): This field is used to enter any relevant progress notes about the client's program.
* **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, date, required/optional: false): This field is used to enter the date of certification.
* **Certified By** (`certified_by`, text, required/optional: false): This field is used to enter the name of the person who certified the completion of the program.
