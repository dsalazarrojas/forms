# personal_values_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on personal values and priorities to help individuals reflect on their personal growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin with the introduction page, where you will be asked to introduce yourself or provide context for your responses.
2. On the "What matters to you most in life?" page, provide a brief description of what matters most to you.
3. On the "What are your top three priorities?" page, select up to three options that align with your current priorities.
4. On the "Which of the following values are most important to you?" page, select up to three options that align with your values.
5. On the "Which of the following values are least important to you?" page, select up to three options that align with your values.
6. On the "What are your current goals?" page, describe your current goals.
7. On the "What goals do you plan to achieve in the next 1-3 years?" page, describe your long-term goals.
8. On the "How do you take care of yourself?" page, describe how you take care of your physical, mental, and emotional well-being.
