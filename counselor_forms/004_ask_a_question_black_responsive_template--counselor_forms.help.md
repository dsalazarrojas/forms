# Ask A Question Black Responsive Template - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from users who want to ask a question or seek assistance from our practice. By filling out this form, you will be able to:

* Provide us with your contact information and a description of your question or concern.
* Specify your preference for a counselor or therapist.
* Indicate the urgency level of your inquiry.
* Consent to being contacted by our team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Your Full Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the type of inquiry you would like to make from the "Type of Inquiry" dropdown menu.
5. Choose your preferred counselor from the "Preferred Counselor" dropdown menu (if you have a preference).
6. Indicate the level of urgency for your inquiry using the "Urgency Level" dropdown menu.
7. Describe your question or concern in the "Your Question or Message" field.
8. If you have visited our practice before, select "Yes" in the "Are You a Previous Client" field.
9. If applicable, enter your insurance provider in the "Insurance Provider" field.
10. Choose your preferred contact method in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
11. Select a time that works best for you to be contacted in the "Best Time to Contact" field.
12. If you would like to share how you heard about us, select from the "How Did You Hear About Us" dropdown menu.
13. Finally, confirm your consent to being contacted by checking "Yes" in the "I Consent to Being Contacted" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name (full_name)**: Please enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address (email_address)**: Enter your valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number (phone_optional)**: This is an optional field for urgent matters. If you provide your phone number, we may contact you directly by phone.
* **Type of Inquiry (inquiry_type)**: Select the type of inquiry you would like to make, such as General Counseling, Couples Therapy, or Crisis Support.
* **Preferred Counselor (preferred_counselor)**: Choose your preferred counselor from the list. If you do not have a preference, select "No Preference".
* **Urgency Level (urgency_level)**: Indicate the level of urgency for your inquiry, such as Emergency, Urgent, or Flexible.
* **Your Question or Message (your_question)**: Describe your question or concern in detail.
* **Are You a Previous Client (previous_client)**: If you have visited our practice before, select "Yes".
* **Insurance Provider (insurance_provider)**: If applicable, enter your insurance provider information.
* **Preferred Contact Method (preferred_contact_method)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted, such as by email, phone call, or text message.
* **Best Time to Contact (best_contact_time)**: Select a time that works best for you to be contacted, such as morning, afternoon, or evening.
* **How Did You Hear About Us (how_heard_about_us)**: If you would like to share how you heard about us, select from the list.
* **I Consent to Being Contacted (consent_to_contact)**: Confirm your consent to being contacted by checking "Yes".
