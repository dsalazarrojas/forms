# Faith-Based Counseling Session Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for collecting information about faith-based counseling sessions between counselors and clients. It is essential to ensure that both parties are aware of the session details, which helps in the counseling process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your name as the counselor.
2. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
3. Provide your phone number for easy communication.
4. Enter the client's name.
5. Enter the client's email address for contact purposes.
6. Enter the client's phone number for easy communication.
7. Select the date of the counseling session.
8. Choose the time of the session.
9. Indicate the duration of the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Counselor Name** (`counselor_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the counselor who will be conducting the session.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about the session details and any follow-ups.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number so we can easily contact you about the session.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name to help us keep track of the session records.
* **Email (Client's Email)** (`email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address for easy communication about the session.
* **Phone (Client’s Phone)** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the client’s phone number for us to contact them about the session.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date when the counseling session will take place.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose the time of the counseling session.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, text, required): Enter the expected duration of the session.

## Tips

- Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately to avoid any miscommunication or confusion.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
- Remember to review and verify the session details before proceeding with the counseling session.
