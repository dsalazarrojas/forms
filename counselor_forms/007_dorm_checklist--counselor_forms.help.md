# Dorm Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from students to ensure a smooth and safe stay at the dorm. The form will help the dorm staff to track and provide the necessary support and resources to the students. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the student's dorm. 
2. Select the type of room the student is staying in (Single, Double, or Suite). 
3. Enter the date the student will arrive at the dorm.
4. Enter the date the student will depart from the dorm.
5. Select the items the student wants to bring (Bed, Desk, Lamp, Alarm Clock, etc.).
6. Enter the student's phone number.
7. Enter the student's email address.
8. Enter the student's emergency contact information.
9. Enter the student's emergency phone number.
10. Enter the student's emergency email address.
11. Enter any additional notes about the student's needs or requirements.
12. Enter the tool assigned to the student.
13. Enter the name of the person who created the form.
14. Enter the date the form was created.
15. Enter the date the form was updated.
16. Enter the name of the person who updated the form.
17. Enter any comments about the form.
18. Enter the ID of the form.
19. Enter the version of the form.
20. Enter the category of the form.
21. Enter the description of the form.
22. Enter any other form IDs (if applicable).
23. Enter the name of the user assigned to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **dorm_name** (Text): Enter the name of the student's dorm.
* **room_type** (Select One): Select the type of room the student is staying in (Single, Double, or Suite).
* **room_number** (Number): Enter the room number of the student.
* **date_arrival** (Date): Enter the date the student will arrive at the dorm.
* **date_departure** (Date): Enter the date the student will depart from the dorm.
* **items_list** (Select Multiple): Select the items the student wants to bring (Bed, Desk, Lamp, etc.).
* **phone** (Text): Enter the student's phone number.
* **email** (Email): Enter the student's email address.
* **emergency_contact** (Text): Enter the name of the student's emergency contact.
* **emergency_phone** (Text): Enter the student's emergency phone number.
* **emergency_email** (Email): Enter the student's emergency email address.
* **notes** (Note): Enter any additional notes about the student's needs or requirements.
* **assigned_tool** (Text): Enter the tool assigned to the student.
* **created_by** (Text): Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **created_date** (Date): Enter the date the form was created.
* **updated_date** (Date): Enter the date the form was updated.
* **updated_by** (Text): Enter the name of the person who updated the form.
* **updated_by_name** (Text): Enter the name of the person who updated the form.
* **comments** (Text): Enter any comments about the form.
* **form_id** (Text): Enter the ID of the form.
* **version** (Text): Enter the version of the form.
* **category** (Text): Enter the category of the form.
* **description** (Text): Enter the description of the form.
* **form_ids** (Text): Enter any other form IDs (if applicable).
* **assigned_user** (Text): Enter the name of the user assigned to the form.
