# rehab_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rehab_application_form is designed to gather information from patients, including their details, medical history, treatment plans, and contact information. This form helps healthcare professionals collect relevant data to provide effective treatment and ensure patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, contact information, and any relevant medical history.
2. Select your treatment plan and treatment start date.
3. Provide your contact information, including your address and preferred contact method.
4. Answer any additional questions regarding your treatment goals, medical information, and discharge plans.
5. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.
