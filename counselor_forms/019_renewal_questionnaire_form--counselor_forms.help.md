# Renewal Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Renewal Questionnaire Form is designed to collect information about customer subscription renewals. This form is meant to be used by customers to provide their renewal information in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Subscription ID.
2. Enter your Customer Name.
3. Enter your Customer Email.
4. Enter your Customer Phone.
5. Select the Renewal Date.
6. Choose the Renewal Type (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
7. Enter any additional Notes.
8. Answer the Renewal Question.
9. Enter your Customer Notes.
10. Enter the Next Renewal Date.
11. Select the Renewal Status (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
12. Select the Renewal Date Status (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
13. Enter the Renewal Status Date.
14. Enter the Renewal Status Note.
15. Enter the Last Renewal Date.
16. Select the Last Renewal Status (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
17. Select the Last Renewal Date Status (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
18. Enter the Last Renewal Note.
19. Answer the Last Renewal Question.
20. Enter your Last Customer Notes.
21. Enter the Last Renewal Date.
22. Select the Last Renewal Status (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
23. Select the Last Renewal Date Status (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
24. Enter the Last Renewal Note.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subscription ID** (`subscription_id`, `number`, required): Enter your Subscription ID to identify your subscription.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address associated with your subscription.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number associated with your subscription.
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the renewal.
* **Renewal Type** (`renewal_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of renewal (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes related to the renewal.
* **Renewal Question** (`renewal_question`, `text`, required): Answer the renewal question.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, `text`, required): Enter your customer notes.
* **Next Renewal Date** (`next_renewal_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the next renewal.
* **Renewal Status** (`renewal_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the renewal status (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Renewal Date Status** (`renewal_date_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the renewal date status (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Renewal Status Date** (`renewal_status_date`, `date`, required): Enter the renewal status date.
* **Renewal Status Note** (`renewal_status_note`, `note`, required): Enter the renewal status note.
* **Last Renewal Date** (`last_renewal_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the last renewal.
* **Last Renewal Status** (`last_renewal_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the last renewal status (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Last Renewal Date Status** (`last_renewal_date_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the last renewal date status (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Last Renewal Note** (`last_renewal_note`, `note`, required): Enter the last renewal note.
* **Last Renewal Question** (`last_renewal_question`, `text`, required): Answer the last renewal question.
* **Last Customer Notes** (`last_customer_notes`, `text`, required): Enter your last customer notes.
* **Last Renewal Date** (`last_renewal_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the last renewal.
