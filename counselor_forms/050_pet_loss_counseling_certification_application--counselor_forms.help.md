# Pet Loss Counseling Certification Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Loss Counseling Certification Application is designed to assess your qualifications and experience in the field of pet loss counseling. Please provide accurate and honest information to ensure a smooth and efficient application process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete each section carefully, ensuring that you have all the necessary information and documentation ready.
2.  Fill in your personal details, including your name, address, phone number, and email address.
3.  Provide accurate information about the date and time of death, as well as the cause of death.
4.  Specify the number of years you have been experienced in pet loss counseling.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`):
    *   This section requires your name, which will be used to address communications and certification documents.
*   **Address** (`address`):
    *   Please provide your address, which will be used for official correspondence and communication.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`):
    *   This is your phone number, which will be used for contact and verification purposes.
*   **Email** (`email`):
    *   Your email address will be used for official communication and notification.
*   **Date of Death** (`date_of_death`):
    *   Please provide the date of death for the pet you are applying to be certified for.
*   **Time of Death** (`time_of_death`):
    *   Specify the time of death, if known.
*   **Cause of Death** (`cause_of_death`):
    *   Provide the cause of death, if applicable.
*   **Years Experienced** (`years_experienced`):
    *   Specify the number of years you have been experienced in pet loss counseling.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you have all the necessary documentation and information before submitting the application.
*   Double-check the accuracy of your entries before submitting the form.
*   If you have any doubts or queries, contact the administration team for assistance.
