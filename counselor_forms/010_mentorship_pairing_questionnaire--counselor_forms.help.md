# Mentorship Pairing Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mentorship Pairing Questionnaire is designed to help match mentors and mentees based on their preferences and goals. It is a tool to ensure a successful and productive partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through the following steps:

* Read through each page carefully and answer all the questions honestly.
* For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose the options that best fit your preferences.
* For date questions, enter the desired start and end dates for the mentorship.
* Make sure to answer all questions, as they are all optional but necessary for matching purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is mentorship pairing?** (`introduction`, `note`, not required)
	+ This is a brief introduction to the mentorship program and its purpose.
* **What kind of mentor are you?** (`mentor_preferences`, `select_one`, not required)
	+ Choose the type of mentor you are, such as a mentor with a technical background or non-technical background.
* **What kind of mentee are you?** (`mentee_preferences`, `select_one`, not required)
	+ Choose the type of mentee you are, such as with a technical focus or non-technical focus.
* **How would you prefer to communicate with your mentee?** (`communication_style`, `select_multiple`, not required)
	+ Choose the communication channels you prefer, such as email, phone calls, or video calls.
* **How often do you want to meet up in person?** (`meeting_frequency`, `select_one`, not required)
	+ Choose how often you would like to meet with your mentee, such as daily, weekly, or biweekly.
* **What days of the week are you available?** (`time_availability`, `select_multiple`, not required)
	+ Choose the days of the week you are available to meet with your mentee.
* **When would you like to start the mentorship?** (`start_date`, `date`, not required)
	+ Enter the desired start date for the mentorship.
* **When would you like to end the mentorship?** (`end_date`, `date`, not required)
	+ Enter the desired end date for the mentorship.
* **Who do you prefer to mentor?** (`preferred_mentees`, `select_one`, not required)
	+ Choose who you prefer to mentor, such as beginners, intermediate, or advanced.
* **What level of experience do you have?** (`experience`, `select_one`, not required)
	+ Choose your level of experience, such as beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
* **What skills would you like to focus on?** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, not required)
	+ Choose the skills you would like to focus on, such as technical, soft, or business skills.
* **What are your mentorship goals?** (`mentorship_goals`, `select_multiple`, not required)
	+ Choose your mentorship goals, such as career advancement, professional development, or personal development.
* **What channels would you like to use for communication?** (`communication_channels`, `select_multiple`, not required)
	+ Choose the channels you would like to use for communication, such as email, phone, or video calls.
* **Where would you like to meet up in person?** (`meeting_location`, `select_multiple`, not required)
	+ Choose the location you would like to meet with your mentee, such as on-site, off-site, or virtual.
* **Tips**
	+ Be honest and specific with your answers to ensure the best possible match.
	+ Take into account your goals and preferences for the mentorship.
	+ If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
