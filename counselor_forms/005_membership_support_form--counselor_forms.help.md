# Membership Support Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Membership Support Form is designed to help us understand your concerns regarding your membership. This form allows you to provide a clear and detailed description of your issue, your contact information, and any relevant attachments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide a concise and accurate description of your membership issue in the *Membership request* field.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your membership records in the *Name* field.
3. Enter your email address associated with your membership in the *Email* field.
4. Enter your phone number for contact in the *Phone* field.
5. Describe the specific issue you're facing in the *Issue description* field.
6. If applicable, attach any relevant documentation or images to the *Attachment* field.
7. Specify when you joined as a member in the *Member since* field, if known.
8. Describe any actions you've taken or plan to take to resolve your issue in the *Action taken* field.
9. Indicate if a follow-up is needed for your issue in the *Follow up* field, and select the correct option.
10. If a follow-up is needed, enter the specific date you'd like us to follow up with you in the *Follow up date* field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Membership request**: Enter a brief description of your membership issue or concern.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your membership records.
* **Email**: Enter your email address associated with your membership.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Issue description**: Provide a detailed description of the issue you're facing.
* **Attachment**: If applicable, attach any relevant documentation or images to help us understand your issue.
* **Member since**: If known, enter the date you joined as a member.
* **Action taken**: Describe any actions you've taken or plan to take to resolve your issue.
* **Follow up**: Indicate if you need a follow-up on your issue and select the correct option.
* **Follow up date**: If you need a follow-up, enter the specific date you'd like us to follow up with you.
