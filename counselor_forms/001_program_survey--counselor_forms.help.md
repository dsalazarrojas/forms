# program_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your preferences for programs and services offered by our organization. It will help us understand your needs and provide you with more tailored support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question in the provided format.
2. If you have a question about a specific field, feel free to ask.
3. You can select multiple options for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your favorite program?**: Please indicate if you have a favorite program by selecting "Yes" or "No". This will help us understand which programs you are most interested in.
* **Which services do you need?**: Describe the services you are interested in (e.g., counseling, workshops, events, etc.). This will help us tailor our support to better meet your needs.
* **counseling**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in counseling services. This will help us understand your interest in counseling.
* **workshops**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in workshop services. This will help us understand your interest in workshops.
* **events**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in events services. This will help us understand your interest in events.
* **classes**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in classes services. This will help us understand your interest in classes.
* **Support**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in support services. This will help us understand your interest in support.
* **comments**: Please provide any additional comments about your preferences or experiences with our organization. This will help us better understand your needs and provide more personalized support.
Note: Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability. Your feedback is greatly appreciated.
