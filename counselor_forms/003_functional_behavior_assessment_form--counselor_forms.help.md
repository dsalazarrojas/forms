# Functional Behavior Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Functional Behavior Assessment Form is used to collect information about an individual's behavior in different scenarios. It helps to identify potential functional impairments or abilities that may affect their daily activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and select the most accurate answer from the options provided.
* Answer all questions thoroughly, using the provided options to guide you.
* If a question does not apply to your situation, select "No" or "Not Applicable" if possible.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

* **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the assessment.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Assessor Position** (`assessor_position`, `text`, optional): Enter the position or title of the person conducting the assessment.
