# Relationship Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your satisfaction with your romantic relationship. It's a self-assessment tool to help you better understand your relationship dynamics and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions honestly, as they are designed to help you reflect on your relationship.
2. Complete each field as accurately as possible based on your current feelings.
3. Review each field carefully before proceeding to the next one.
4. If you're unsure of a question or answer, feel free to skip the question or ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your overall sense of the relationship?** (`relationship_satisfaction_survey`, text, required): Answer this question with a brief description of your current feeling about your relationship.
* **What do you value most in your relationship?** (`relationship_satisfaction_survey_1`, select_one, required): Select one of the options (Trust, Intimacy, Shared interests) that you value most in your relationship.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of your relationship?** (`relationship_satisfaction_survey_3`, number, required): Rate the overall quality of your relationship on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Do you feel supported by your partner?** (`relationship_satisfaction_survey_4`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply: True, False.
* **Do you feel heard by your partner?** (`relationship_satisfaction_survey_5`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply: True, False.
* **Do you feel understood by your partner?** (`relationship_satisfaction_survey_6`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply: True, False.
* **How important is it to you that your partner has feelings of guilt or shame?** (`relationship_satisfaction_survey_7`, select_one, required): Select one of the options (Not at all important, Somewhat important, Very important) based on how you feel about your partner's emotions.
* **How often do you have conflicts or disagreements with your partner?** (`relationship_satisfaction_survey_8`, time, required): Select a duration or frequency of conflicts.
* **How long have you been in the relationship?** (`relationship_satisfaction_survey_9`, number, required): Enter the number of months or years you've been in the relationship.
* **Has there been a point in the relationship where things were really tough?** (`relationship_satisfaction_survey_10`, date, optional): If yes, select the date.

## Tips
* Be honest and open when completing this form.
* If you're unsure or unsure about a question, skip it or ask for help.
* This form is not a diagnostic tool, but rather a self-assessment instrument to help you understand your relationship dynamics.
