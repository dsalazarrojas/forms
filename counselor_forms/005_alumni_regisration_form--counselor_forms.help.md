# Alumni Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alumni Registration Form is designed to collect personal, educational, and professional information from alumni members to create a comprehensive database for potential networking, events, and other activities. This form is intended for current or former students, or through a specific link or invitation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select Registration Type**: Choose the type of registration that best fits your needs:
	* New Alumni Registration
	* Alumni Profile Update
	* Event Registration
	* Membership Renewal
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Personal Information**:
		+ **First Name**: Enter your first name.
		+ **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
		+ **Address Line 1**: Enter your street address or PO Box.
		+ **City**: Enter your city name.
		+ **State**: Select your state.
		+ **ZIP Code**: Enter your ZIP code.
		+ **Country**: Select your country.
3. **Educational Background**:
	+ **School/College Attended**: Enter the name of the institution you attended.
	+ **Graduation Year**: Enter the year you graduated.
	+ **Degree Type**: Select the type of degree earned.
	+ **Major/Concentration**: Enter your major or concentration.
4. **Professional Information**:
	+ **Current Employer**: Enter the name of your current employer.
	+ **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
	+ **Industry Sector**: Select your industry sector.
	+ **Years of Professional Experience**: Enter the number of years you've worked.
5. **Additional Information**:
	+ **Special Accommodations Needed**: Enter any special accommodations required for events or services.
	+ **How Did You Hear About This Program**: Select how you heard about this program.
	+ **Additional Comments or Questions**: Add any additional comments or questions.
6. **Confirm Terms and Conditions**: Select "I agree to abide by the alumni association terms" to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Type**: Choose the type of registration that fits your needs. This will help us understand your reason for registering.
* **First Name**: Enter the name given at birth.
* **Last Name**: Enter the name given at birth.
* **Primary Email Address**: Enter the email address you want us to use for communication.
* **Alternate Email Address**: Enter a secondary email address for optional use.
* **Mobile Phone Number**: Enter your mobile phone number.
* **Work Phone Number**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Address Line 1**: Enter the first line of your address or PO Box.
* **Address Line 2**: Enter the second line of your address or unit number.
* **City**: Enter the city name.
* **State**: Select the state where you reside.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the ZIP code of your address.
* **Country**: Select the country where you reside.
* **School/College Attended**: Enter the name of the institution you attended.
* **Graduation Year**: Enter the year of graduation.
* **Degree Type**: Select the type of degree earned.
* **Major/Concentration**: Enter the major or concentration of study.
* **Minor (if applicable)**: Enter any minor field of study.
* **Current Employer**: Enter the name of your current employer.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Industry Sector**: Select the industry sector of your work.
* **Years of Professional Experience**: Enter the number of years you've worked.
* **Name Suffix**: Enter any name suffix (e.g., Jr, Sr, etc.).
* **Primary Contact Method**: Select how you want us to contact you primarily.
* **Subscribe to Alumni Newsletter**: Select "Yes" to subscribe to the alumni newsletter.
* **Receive Event Notifications**: Select "Yes" to receive event notifications.
* **Special Accommodations Needed**: Enter any special accommodations required.
* **How Did You Hear About This Program**: Select how you heard about this program.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Enter any additional comments or questions.
* **Terms and Conditions Agreement**: Select "I agree to abide by the alumni association terms."
