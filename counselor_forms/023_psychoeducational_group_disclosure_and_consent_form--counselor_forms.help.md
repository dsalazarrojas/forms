# Psychoeducational Group Disclosure And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect sensitive medical information from patients or their representatives to ensure a safe and effective treatment environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please carefully read each section and provide the requested information. The form is divided into the following sections:
- Introduction
- Purpose of Group
- Leader Information
- Goals and Objectives
- Confidentiality
- Attendance and Attendance Policy
- Emergency Contact Information
- Medical Conditions
- Allergies
- Medications
- Emergency Treatment Procedure
- Insurance Information
- Payment
- Emergency Contact Methods
- Emergency Contact Person's Alternative Phone
- Emergency Contact Person's Alternative Email
- Emergency Treatment Procedure
- Emergency Contact Person's Notes
- Insurance Provider
- Emergency Treatment Procedure
- Authorization
- Authorization Methods
- Authorization Person's Name
- Authorization Person's Phone
- Authorization Person's Alternative Phone
- Authorization Person's Alternative Email
- Authorization Notes

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction**: This section is where you can provide any necessary introduction or context about the form and its purpose.

### Purpose of Group
* **Purpose of Group**: Please describe the purpose of the group you are attending. This will help the treatment team understand the focus and goals of the group.

### Leader Information
* **Leader info**: Please provide information about the group leader, including their name and contact details.

### Goals and Objectives
* **Goals and Objectives**: Describe the goals and objectives of the group, including what you hope to achieve or work on during the group.

### Confidentiality
* **Confidentiality**: Choose one of the options to indicate how confidentiality is managed within the group:
	+ Confidentiality is kept within the group
	+ Confidentiality is kept outside the group
	+ Confidentiality is not kept

### Attendance and Attendance Policy
* **Attendance and Attendance Policy**: Please describe the group's attendance policy, including any rules or expectations for attendance.

### Emergency Contact Information
* **Emergency Contact Info**: Provide information about who to contact in case of an emergency, including their name, contact details, and any other relevant information.

### Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions**: Describe any medical conditions you have or are currently being treated for, including any relevant medical history.

### Allergies
* **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities you have, including any medications or substances that you are allergic to.

### Medications
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking, including their dosage and frequency.

### Emergency Treatment Procedure
* **Emergency Treatment Procedure**: Describe any emergency treatment procedures that may be necessary in case of an emergency.

### Insurance Information
* **Insurance Info**: Provide information about your insurance provider, including their contact details.

### Payment
* **Payment**: Describe your payment arrangement for the group, including any payment method or frequency.

### Emergency Contact Methods
* **Emergency Contact Methods**: List any alternative contact methods, such as a phone number or email, that can be used to contact you in case of an emergency.

### Emergency Contact Person's Alternative Phone
* **Emergency Contact Person's Alternative Phone**: Provide an alternative phone number for your emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Person's Alternative Email
* **Emergency Contact Person's Alternative Email**: Provide an alternative email address for your emergency contact person.

### Emergency Treatment Procedure
* **Emergency Treatment Procedure**: Describe any additional emergency treatment procedures that may be necessary in case of an emergency.

### Emergency Contact Person's Notes
* **Emergency Contact Person's Notes**: Provide any additional notes or information about your emergency contact person.

### Insurance Provider
* **Insurance Provider**: Provide information about your insurance provider, including their contact details.

### Emergency Treatment Procedure
* **Emergency Treatment Procedure**: Describe any additional emergency treatment procedures that may be necessary in case of an emergency.

### Authorization
* **Authorization**: Provide information about who is authorized to receive emergency contact information and treatment in case of an emergency.

### Authorization Methods
* **Authorization Methods**: List any methods for authorization, such as phone calls or email.

### Authorization Person's Name
* **Authorization Person's Name**: Provide the name of the person authorized to receive emergency contact information and treatment.

### Authorization Person's Phone
* **Authorization Person's Phone**: Provide the contact phone number of the person authorized to receive emergency contact information and treatment.

### Authorization Person's Alternative Phone
* **Authorization Person's Alternative Phone**: Provide an alternative phone number for the person authorized to receive emergency contact information and treatment.

### Authorization Person's Alternative Email
* **Authorization Person's Alternative Email**: Provide an alternative email address for the person authorized to receive emergency contact information and treatment.

### Authorization Notes
* **Authorization Notes**: Provide any additional notes or information about the person authorized to receive emergency contact information and treatment.

### Signature
* **Signature**: Please sign and date this form to confirm that you have read and understood the information collected.
