# Post Graduation Career Outcome Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about post-graduation career outcomes. The goal is to gather data on graduates' job titles, locations, salaries, and satisfaction levels in their current positions. This will help us better understand the career paths and job market trends among our graduates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in your graduate name, which is the name of the person you are reporting on.
*   Enter the date of graduation, which should be the date the graduate completed their studies.
*   Provide the current job title of the graduate.
*   Describe the graduate's current job description.
*   Enter the location of the graduate's current job.
*   Enter the graduate's current employer.
*   Enter the graduate's current salary range.
*   Answer how satisfied the graduate is with their current job on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is extremely dissatisfied and 10 is extremely satisfied.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Graduate Name (text, required/optional)**: Enter the name of the graduate you are reporting on.
*   **Graduation Date (date, required/optional)**: Enter the date the graduate completed their studies.
*   **Current Job Title (text, required/optional)**: Enter the graduate's current job title.
*   **Job Description (text, required/optional)**: Describe the graduate's current job responsibilities and tasks.
*   **Job Location (text, required/optional)**: Enter the location of the graduate's current job.
*   **Current Employer (text, required/optional)**: Enter the name of the graduate's current employer.
*   **Current Salary Range (number, required/optional)**: Enter the graduate's current annual salary in dollars.
*   **Job Satisfaction (number, required/optional)**: Answer how satisfied the graduate is with their current job on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is extremely dissatisfied and 10 is extremely satisfied.
