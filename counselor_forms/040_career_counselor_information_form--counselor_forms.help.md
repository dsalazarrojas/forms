## Career Counselor Information Form - Help Guide
### Purpose
The Career Counselor Information Form is used to collect information from career counselors about themselves. This information is shared with the team to help them understand the counselor's qualifications, areas of specialty, and availability. This form should only be completed by authorized career counselors.

### How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Select relevant areas of specialty from the provided options.
4. Enter a valid email address for referrals and scheduling.
5. Provide a clear summary of the services you offer.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Counselor name**: Enter your full name, including first and last names.
* **Qualifications**: List your degrees, certifications, and licenses obtained.
* **Years of experience**: Enter the total number of years you have spent in counseling or career services.
* **Areas of specialty**: Select all areas of focus you specialize in, such as Resume and job search, Interview coaching, Career transition, and Skills assessment. You can select multiple options.
* **Contact email**: Enter a valid email address for referrals and scheduling. This will be used by the team to send you updates and requests.
* **Office location**: Enter your office or campus address. This is optional but helpful for in-person meetings.
* **Availability**: Describe your typical days and hours available for meetings and consultations.
* **Services offered**: Provide a clear and concise summary of the services you offer, such as resume building, job search assistance, or interview preparation.
* **Additional notes**: Enter any other information about yourself or your services that the team should know.

### Tips
* Ensure you save your progress frequently to avoid losing any entered information.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the team for clarification.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
* This form should not be used to share confidential or sensitive information about your clients or any other individuals.
