# Grief Impact Assessment For Children - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help professionals evaluate the impact of grief on children and identify effective ways to support them in their recovery.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by reading the entire form carefully and ensuring you have a clear understanding of the questions and options provided.
2.  Complete each section thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible for each question.
3.  Be sure to select the relevant options that best fit the child's situation.
4.  Leave the "Notes" section for any additional information that might be helpful for the child's assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction**: This is a brief overview of the form's purpose and your role in the assessment process. Please take a moment to read it carefully before proceeding.
*   **Child Name**: Enter the child's name as it is written on their identification documents or as they commonly refer to themselves.
*   **Age**: Provide the child's age as of their last birthday.
*   **Parent or Caregiver Name**: Enter the name of the parent or primary caregiver of the child.
*   **Relationship**: Indicate your relationship with the bereaved child, such as "Bereaved Child," "Bereaved Adult," or "Other" if not applicable.
*   **How long has the child been experiencing grief?**: Select the timeframe that best describes how long the child has been experiencing grief:
    *   Less than 1 month
    *   1-3 months
    *   3-6 months
    *   More than 6 months
*   **What impact has the grief had on the child?**: Indicate the level of impact on the child's life:
    *   None or Minimal
    *   Mild
    *   Moderate
    *   Severe
*   **How has the grief impacted the child's daily life?**: Describe the level of grief's impact on the child's daily activities:
    *   None or Minimal
    *   Mild
    *   Moderate
    *   Severe
*   **Current Emotional State**: Indicate the child's current emotional state:
    *   None or Minimal
    *   Sadness
    *   Anxiety
    *   Anger
    *   Guilt
    *   Fear
*   **How does the child cope with their grief?**: Choose the ways in which the child deals with their emotional state:
    *   None or Minimal
    *   Talking to others
    *   Drawing or Art
    *   Playing or Acting
    *   Writing or Journaling
    *   Other ( specify)
*   **Support System**: Describe the support system the child has for dealing with their grief:
    *   None or Minimal
    *   Parent or Caregiver
    *   School
    *   Friends
    *   Family
*   **Notes**: Provide any additional information that might be helpful for the child's assessment.

## Tips

*   Be as specific as possible when answering each question to ensure an accurate assessment.
*   Consider the child's developmental stage and cognitive abilities when answering some questions.
*   Use the assessment results as a guide for creating a personalized support plan for the child.
