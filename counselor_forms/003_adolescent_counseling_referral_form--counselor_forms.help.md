# Adolescent Counseling Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Adolescent Counseling Referral Form is designed to gather information from individuals or entities referring an adolescent to counseling services. This form ensures that the referring individual provides accurate and necessary information about the adolescent and the reason for the referral.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you are the authorized referrer for the adolescent.
3. Enter your name and contact information so we can follow up with you.
4. Provide details about the adolescent's concerns and the reason for the referral.
5. Assess the urgency level of the referral.
6. Confirm parental consent has been obtained (if applicable).
7. Provide any additional contact information for the adolescent's parent or guardian (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referrer Name** (`referrer_name`): Enter your name as the referring individual or entity.
* **Referrer Role or Relationship** (`referrer_role_or_relationship`): Describe your relationship with the adolescent, e.g. Teacher, Parent, Physician.
* **Referrer Email** (`referrer_email`): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Adolescent Full Name** (`adolescent_full_name`): Enter the full name of the adolescent being referred.
* **Adolescent Age** (`adolescent_age`): Enter the age of the adolescent.
* **School Name** (`school_name`): Enter the current school the adolescent is attending.
* **Primary Concerns** (`primary_concerns`): Select all concerns that apply from the list:
	+ Anxiety
	+ Depression
	+ Academic struggles
	+ Behavioral issues
	+ Family conflict
	+ Social isolation
	+ Substance use
* **Description of Concerns** (`description_of_concerns`): Provide details about the observed behaviors or issues.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`): Assess the urgency level of the referral:
	+ Urgent
	+ Standard
	+ Monitoring
* **Parental Consent Obtained** (`parental_consent_obtained`): Confirm if parental consent has been obtained:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Pending
* **Parent Contact Number** (`parent_contact_number`): Enter any additional contact information for the adolescent's parent or guardian (if applicable).

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask a supervisor or colleague for assistance.
- Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and consents before filling out the form.
