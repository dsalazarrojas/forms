# idp_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Driving Permit (IDP) application form is used to gather information from individuals who wish to apply for an IDP. The form is designed to collect personal and vehicle-related details that are required for the IDP issuance process. Please ensure you are eligible and only submit the form once to avoid duplication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the IDP application form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Yes" or "No" option to indicate if you are applying for an IDP.
2. Fill in your personal information in the "personal info" field, using the format `MM/DD/YYYY` for dates.
3. Enter your vehicle information in the "vehicle info" field.
4. Provide your vehicle's registration details in the "vehicle registration" field.
5. Enter any relevant IDP information in the "idp info" field.
6. Enter your payment details in the "payment info" field.
7. Fill out the review form with any additional comments or questions in the "review form" field.
8. Confirm your application by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "confirm" field.
9. Enter your date of birth using the `MM/DD/YYYY` format, without any dashes or slashes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **application info**: (`required/optional`) This field is used to indicate your application status. Select "Yes" to apply or "No" to indicate you are not applying.
* **personal info**: (`text`) Enter your personal details, including your name, address, and contact information.
* **vehicle info**: (`text`) Enter your vehicle's details, including make, model, and year.
* **vehicle registration**: (`text`) Enter your vehicle's registration details, including license plate number and state of issuance.
* **idp info**: (`text`) Enter any relevant information about your IDP, such as its status or any issues you've encountered.
* **payment info**: (`text`) Enter your payment details, including method and amount.
* **review form**: (`text`) Use this field to provide any additional comments or questions you have about your application.
* **confirm**: (`required/optional`) Confirm your application by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **date of birth**: (`date`) Enter your date of birth in the `MM/DD/YYYY` format.
