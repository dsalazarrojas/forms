# Girl Scout Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Girl Scout Interest Form is a form designed to gather information about the Girl Scouts, their families, and their councils. This form will help us better understand their needs and provide a more tailored experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to enter the correct email address for the Girl Scout.
3. Fill out the phone number with the primary contact method.
4. Enter the troop number and council name of the Girl Scout.
5. Select the correct grade level for the Girl Scout.
6. Enter the address of the Girl Scout.
7. Enter the city where the Girl Scout resides.
8. Select the state where the Girl Scout resides.
9. Enter the zip code of the Girl Scout.
10. Provide the name and relationship of the emergency contact person.
11. Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact person.
12. List any medical conditions or allergies.
13. Enter any medical information.
14. List any dietary restrictions.
15. List any medication the Girl Scout is taking.
16. Enter the name and signature of the parent or guardian.
17. Enter the name and signature of the leader or guiding council.
18. Enter the troop number 2 (if applicable).
19. Enter the council name 2 (if applicable).
20. Select the correct grade level 2 (if applicable).
21. Enter the date and time of the form completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the Girl Scout's first name.
* **Email**: Enter the Girl Scout's email address. This will be the primary contact method for the Girl Scout.
* **Phone**: Enter the Girl Scout's phone number. This can be used for emergency contact purposes.
* **Troop Number**: Enter the number of the Girl Scout's troop.
* **Council Name**: Enter the name of the Girl Scout's council.
* **Grade Level**: Select the correct grade level for the Girl Scout. (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
* **Address**: Enter the Girl Scout's current address.
* **City**: Enter the city where the Girl Scout resides.
* **State**: Select the state where the Girl Scout resides. (Yes or No)
* **Zip**: Enter the Girl Scout's zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Specify the relationship of the emergency contact person (e.g., mother, father, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions or allergies the Girl Scout has.
* **Medical Information**: Enter any relevant medical information.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies the Girl Scout has.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: List any dietary restrictions the Girl Scout has.
* **Medication**: List any medication the Girl Scout is taking.
* **Medical Insurance**: Enter any medical insurance information.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature**: Enter the name and signature of the parent or guardian.
* **Leader/Guiding Council Signature**: Enter the name and signature of the leader or guiding council.
* **Troop Number 2**: Enter the number of the second troop (if applicable).
* **Council Name 2**: Enter the name of the second council (if applicable).
* **Grade Level 2**: Select the correct grade level 2 (if applicable).
* **Date**: Enter the date of form completion.
* **Time**: Enter the time of form completion.
