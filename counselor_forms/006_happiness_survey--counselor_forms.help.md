# Happiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a Happiness Survey form designed to gauge the happiness level of individuals who interact with our organization. The form is meant to be completed by customers, clients, or visitors to provide feedback on their overall satisfaction and happiness during their experience with us. This feedback helps us understand what we're doing well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your First Name**: Enter your first name in the first name field.
2. **Enter your Last Name**: Enter your last name in the last name field.
3. **Provide your Email**: Enter your email address in the email field.
4. **Provide your Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the phone number field.
5. **Select your Happiness Level**: Choose how happy you are on the happiness level dropdown menu.
6. **Provide Comments (Optional)**: If you'd like to share more about your experience, you can add comments in the comments field.
7. **Choose your Satisfaction Level**: Select how satisfied you are with your experience on the satisfaction level dropdown menu.
8. **Add Comments on Satisfaction (Optional)**: If you'd like to elaborate on your satisfaction, you can add comments here.
9. **Select a Date**: Choose a date for the form (this may be automatically filled in based on the current date).
10. **Select a Time**: Choose a time for the form (this may be automatically filled in based on the current time).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text, Required):** Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name (Text, Required):** Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email (Email, Optional):** Enter your email address in this field. Please format it as you normally would (e.g., example@example.com).
* **Phone Number (Text, Optional):** Enter your phone number in this field.
* **How happy are you? (Select One, Required):** Select one of the options to choose how happy you are.
* **Comments (Note, Optional):** Add any comments or feedback you'd like to share about your experience.
* **Are you Satisfied? (Select One, Required):** Select how satisfied you are with your experience.
* **Comments on Satisfaction (Note, Optional):** Add any comments or feedback about your satisfaction.
* **Date (Date, Required):** Select a date from the calendar picker. The date will be displayed as mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time (Time, Optional):** Select a time from the time picker. The time will be displayed as hh:mm.
