# Dating Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your dating history and preferences. It will ask questions about your relationship status, how you met your partner, what you expect from a relationship, and your overall satisfaction with your current relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and select the options that best suit your experiences and feelings. The form has multiple sections, each focusing on a different aspect of your dating history and preferences. You can answer as many or as few questions as you like, depending on what feels comfortable for you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your first name, which will help us better understand your identity.

## Tips
Take your time and answer the questions honestly. You can skip any questions that feel uncomfortable or invasive. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us. We appreciate your participation and hope to learn more about you!
