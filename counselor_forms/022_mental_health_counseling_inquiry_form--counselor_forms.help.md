# Mental Health Counseling Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from individuals seeking mental health counseling services. It helps our organization understand their needs and provide appropriate support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, especially the fields marked as required.
2. Be honest and clear in your responses.
3. If you have any specific concerns or questions, do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (text, required: false)**: Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Contact Email (email, required: false)**: Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Phone (text, required: false)**: Provide your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Preferred Language (select_one, required: false)**: Choose your preferred language from the options provided (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Client Reason for Inquiry (text, required: false)**: Briefly explain why you are seeking mental health counseling services.
* **Other (please specify) (text, required: false)**: If your reason for seeking counseling is not listed, please provide a detailed explanation.
* **Therapist Name (text, required: false)**: If you have a specific therapist in mind, please provide their name.
* **Therapist Email (email, required: false)**: If you have a specific therapist's email, please provide it.
* **Therapist Phone (text, required: false)**: If you have a specific therapist's phone number, please provide it.

## Tips
* Please be honest and clear in your responses.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* This form is not a substitute for in-person consultation or professional medical diagnosis.
* If you need urgent assistance, please contact our organization directly.
