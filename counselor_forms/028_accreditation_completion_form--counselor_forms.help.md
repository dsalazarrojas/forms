# Accreditation Completion Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be completed by institutions or organizations seeking accreditation. It collects information necessary for the accreditation process and helps track the progress of the process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked with an asterisk \*) for institution name, accrediting body, program or department, contact person, contact email, and completion date.
3. Optionally, provide a brief summary of the results or findings and recommended next steps.
4. List any key documents submitted to the accreditor.
5. Type your full name to sign the form electronically.
6. Add any additional notes or information relevant to the accreditation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Institution name** (`institution_name`, required): Enter the legal name of the institution.
* **Accrediting body** (`accreditation_body`, required): Enter the name of the accrediting organization.
* **Program or department** (`program_or_department`, required): Enter the program or department seeking accreditation.
* **Contact person** (`contact_person`, required): Enter the person handling accreditation correspondence.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, required): Enter the email address for accreditation notices.
* **Completion date** (`completion_date`, required): Enter the date accreditation requirements were completed.
* **Documents submitted** (`documents_submitted`, required): List key documents provided to the accreditor.
* **Outcomes summary** (`outcomes_summary`, optional): Briefly summarize the results or findings (if applicable).
* **Next steps** (`next_steps`, optional): Suggest recommended follow-up actions (if applicable).
* **Authorized signature** (`authorized_signature`, required): Type your full name to sign electronically.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, optional): Enter any other information relevant to the accreditation process.
