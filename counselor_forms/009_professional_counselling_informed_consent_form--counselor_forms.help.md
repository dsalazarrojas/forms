# Professional Counselling Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about professional counseling sessions, including session details, client and counselor information, payment details, and session duration and frequency. It aims to provide both the client and counselor with informed consent and agreement on the terms of the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter Session Details**: Provide information about the counseling session, including the session ID (if applicable).
2. **Enter Client Information**: Provide your name, phone number, email, address, date of birth, and any other required client information.
3. **Enter Counselor Details**: Provide your counselor's name, payment details, and any other required counselor information.
4. **Choose Payment Method and Amount**: Select the payment method and amount for the session.
5. **Choose Frequency of Sessions**: Indicate how frequently you would like to have sessions.
6. **Enter Session Notes**: Any additional notes about the session.
7. **Sign and Date**: Sign the form and date it to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the session (if applicable).
* **Client Name**: Enter your name as the client.
* **Client Phone**: Enter your phone number as the client.
* **Email**: Enter your email address as the client.
* **Address**: Enter your address as the client.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth as the client.
* **Counsellor Details**: Enter the counselor's name and any other required information.
* **Session Duration**: Indicate the length of the session.
* **Frequency Of Sessions**: Choose how frequently you would like to have sessions.
* **Payment Details**: Choose the payment method and amount for the session.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment (e.g., Option 1, Option 2).
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount for the session.
* **Session Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the session.
* **Counsellor Name**: Enter the counselor's name.
* **Client Consent**: Choose your level of agreement with the session terms.
* **Counsellor Consent**: Choose the counselor's level of agreement with the session terms.
* **Client Signature**: Sign here to confirm your agreement with the session terms.
* **Counsellor Signature**: Sign here to confirm the counselor's agreement with the session terms.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Time Signed**: Enter the time you signed the form.
* **Signature Of**: Enter the name of the person signing the form.
* **Signature Position**: Enter the position of the signature on the form.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the session.

Please note that some fields may not be required, depending on the specific context and requirements of the session.
