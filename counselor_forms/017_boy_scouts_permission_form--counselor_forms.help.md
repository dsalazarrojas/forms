# Boy Scouts Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from a youth (or their parent/guardian) to ensure their safety and well-being during scouting activities or events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section completely and accurately. Ensure that all required fields (indicated by asterisks: *) are filled in correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Youth Full Name** (*): Enter the full name of the youth, as it appears on their birth certificate or identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the youth's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Scout Rank**: Select the youth's current rank from the available options.
* **Troop Number**: Enter the youth's troop identification number.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the full legal name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email Address**: Enter the parent or guardian's email address for communication purposes.
* **Parent Phone Number**: Enter the parent or guardian's primary contact phone number.
* **Activity or Event Name**: Enter a brief description of the activity or event.
* **Activity Description**: Optional, but provide a detailed description of the activity or event.
* **Activity Dates**: Enter the dates of the activity or event.
* **Activity Location**: Enter the location of the activity or event.
* **Known Allergies**: List any known allergies the youth has, including food, medication, or environmental.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any known medical conditions the youth has.
* **Current Medications**: List any current medications the youth takes.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **I grant permission**: Select "I grant permission" to authorize the youth's participation in the activity or event.

## Tips
* Always fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* Double-check the information for any errors or inconsistencies.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
