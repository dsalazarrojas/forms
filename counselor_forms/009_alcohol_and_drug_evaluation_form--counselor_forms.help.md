# Alcohol And Drug Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a patient's substance use history, motivations, and readiness for treatment. It helps the evaluator to assess the patient's needs and provide a comprehensive treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following pages:

### Page 1:
1. **Patient Full Name**: Enter the legal name of the patient.
2. **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. **Gender**: Select the patient's gender from the provided options.
4. **Contact Phone**: Enter the patient's primary contact phone number.
5. **Email Address**: Enter the patient's email address (optional).
6. **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the patient's emergency contact.
7. **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact phone number.

### Page 2:
1. **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of this evaluation in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
2. **Evaluator Name**: Enter the name of the counselor or evaluator.
3. **Reason for Evaluation**: Select the reason for this evaluation from the provided options.
4. **Substance Use History**: Select the duration of the patient's substance use from the provided options.
5. **Primary Substance of Concern**: Select the main substance being evaluated.
6. **Frequency of Use**: Select the frequency of the patient's substance use.
7. **Previous Treatment**: Select if the patient has had treatment before.
8. **Withdrawal Symptoms**: Select all the withdrawal symptoms experienced by the patient.
9. **Current Mental Health Diagnosis**: Select all diagnosed mental health conditions.
10. **Current Medications**: List all current medications taken by the patient.

### Page 3:
1. **Family History of Addiction**: Select if the patient has a family history of addiction.
2. **Employment Status**: Select the patient's current employment status.
3. **Living Situation**: Select the patient's current living situation.
4. **Motivation for Change**: Rate the patient's motivation for change.
5. **Treatment Recommendation**: Select the evaluator's recommended treatment plan.
6. **Follow-up Appointment Date**: Enter the scheduled follow-up appointment date (optional).

### Page 4:
1. **Patient Acknowledgment**: Confirm that the patient acknowledges the accuracy of the information provided.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
- If you are unsure about any question, please consult with the counselor or evaluator.
- Please note that the form is not a replacement for professional clinical assessment.
