# Morality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Morality Survey is a tool designed to gather information on users' moral values, feelings, and opinions on various topics. It is meant to be used for academic and research purposes to gain a deeper understanding of moral decision-making and its impact on individuals' lives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction page, which provides some background information on the survey.
2. Answer the questions on the "feelings" page, which ask you to express your emotional responses to certain moral dilemmas.
3. Move on to the "opinions" page, where you are asked to share your thoughts on moral issues.
4. Finally, proceed to the "sense" page, where you will be prompted to describe your moral framework or perspective.
