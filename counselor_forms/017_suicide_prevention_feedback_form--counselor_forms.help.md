# Suicide Prevention Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Suicide Prevention Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from clients after a session with a counselor. This feedback will help counselors assess their practice and identify areas where they can improve.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name.
2. Enter the name of the organization you are associated with.
3. Provide any feedback you may have about your experience.
4. Rate your experience from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
5. Enter any additional comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Organization Name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the organization you are associated with.
* **Feedback** (text, required: false): Share any thoughts, concerns, or observations you had during the session. This will help us improve our practice.
* **Rating (1-5)** (number, required: false): Rate your experience from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Comments** (text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback, as it will help us improve our practice.
* You can enter up to 2000 characters in the Feedback and Comments fields.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
