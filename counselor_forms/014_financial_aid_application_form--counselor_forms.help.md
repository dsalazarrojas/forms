# Financial Aid Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Aid Application Form is used to gather information from students or staff members to apply for financial aid.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the eligibility criteria to ensure you are eligible to fill out this form.
2. Ensure that this is not a duplicate application.
3. Fill out the form in one or multiple pages, depending on the number of required fields.
4. Be accurate and complete when filling out each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **Financial Aid Year** (`number`, required: false): Enter the year for which you are applying for financial aid.
* **Financial Aid Type** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the type of financial aid you are applying for:
	+ Pell Grant
	+ Federal Work-Study
	+ Direct Loans
	+ Other
* **Financial Aid Amount** (`number`, required: false): Enter the total amount of financial aid you are requesting.
* **Financial Aid Reason** (`text`, required: false): Explain the reason for your financial aid application.
* **Student ID** (`number`, required: false): Enter your student ID number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Date of Application** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date you are applying for financial aid in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Signature** (`note`, required: false): Sign and date your application for official confirmation.

## Tips
* Be sure to review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the financial aid office.
* This form is for financial aid applications only, not general inquiries or information gathering.
