# Co Parenting Communication Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Co Parenting Communication Log is a tool to document and facilitate clear communication between parents in a co-parenting situation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name and the name of the other parent involved.
2. Choose the date and time of the communication.
3. Select how you communicated with the other parent (e.g., phone call, text, email).
4. Indicate who initiated the conversation (e.g., you, the other parent, child, or a third party).
5. Enter the main topic or category of the conversation.
6. Summarize the communication and any agreements or decisions made.
7. If necessary, indicate if follow-up is needed and when.
8. Describe the tone of the interaction.
9. Check if concerns were raised and describe them.
10. Enter any action items and who is responsible for them.
11. If applicable, indicate if a mediator was notified.
12. Enter any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name**: Enter your name and the name of the other parent involved.
* **Other Parent Name**: Enter the name of the other co-parent.
* **Child Name**: Enter the name of the child or children involved.
* **Date of Communication**: Enter the date when this communication occurred.
* **Time of Communication**: Choose the time of the communication.
* **Communication Method**: Select how you communicated with the other parent (e.g., phone call, text message).
* **Initiated By**: Indicate who started this conversation (e.g., you, the other parent, child, or a third party).
* **Topic Category**: Choose the main topic or category of the conversation (e.g., schedule, health, education).
* **Communication Summary**: Briefly describe what was discussed.
* **Decisions or Agreements Made**: Summarize any decisions or agreements made during the conversation.
* **Is Follow-Up Needed**: Check if follow-up is required.
* **Follow-Up Date**: If applicable, enter the date when follow-up is needed.
* **Follow-Up Details**: Describe what needs to be addressed.
* **Tone of Communication**: Describe the interaction as cooperative and respectful, neutral, tense, hostile, or one-sided.
* **Were Any Concerns Raised**: Check if concerns were raised.
* **Describe Concerns**: Summarize any concerns discussed.
* **Action Items**: Enter any action items that need to be completed.
* **Who Is Responsible for Actions**: Specify who is responsible for completing action items.
* **Was Mediator Notified**: Check if a mediator was informed about this conversation.
* **Counselor or Mediator Name**: Enter the name of your counselor or mediator.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any other relevant information.
* **Initiated By**: Enter the name of the person who initiated the conversation.
* **Action Responsibility**: Specify who is responsible for completing action items.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any other relevant information.

Note: This guide is a general overview. Please refer to your specific co-parenting agreements and circumstances for more detailed information.
