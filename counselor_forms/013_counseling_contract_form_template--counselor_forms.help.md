# counseling_contract_form_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "counseling_contract_form_template" form is designed to document the details of a counseling contract between a counselor and a client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready, including the client's contact details, services requested, dates, and times.
2. Fill out the form accurately and honestly, making sure to complete all required fields.
3. Sign the form with your digital signature after completing it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **form_id** (1, number, required: false)
This field is used to uniquely identify the form.

### 2. **counselor information** (2, text, required: false)
Provide your name and contact information as the counselor.

### 3. **client information** (3, text, required: false)
Enter the client's contact information.

### 4. **services_requested** (4, select multiple, required: false)
Select the services the client has requested.

### 5. **signature 1** (5, note, required: false)
This field is intended for the counselor's digital signature.

### 6. **signature 2** (6, note, required: false)
This field is intended for the client's digital signature.

### 7. **date_signed** (7, date, required: false)
Enter the date the contract was signed.

### 8. **time_signed** (8, time, required: false)
Enter the time the contract was signed.

### 9. **assigned_tool** (9, select multiple, required: false)
Select the assigned tool or medium for the counseling services.

### 10. **notes** (10, note, required: false)
Add any additional notes about the contract.

### 11. **client_email** (11, email, required: false)
Enter the client's email address.

### 12. **client_phone** (12, text, required: false)
Enter the client's phone number.

### 13. **date_of_service** (13, date, required: false)
Enter the date of the counseling service.

### 14. **time_of_service** (14, time, required: false)
Enter the time of the counseling service.

### 15. **counselor_signature** (15, note, required: false)
This field is intended for the counselor's digital signature.

### 16. **counselor name** (16, text, required: false)
Enter your name as the counselor.

### 17. **contract_version** (17, select one, required: false)
Select the version of the contract.

### 18. **client_acknowledgement** (18, select one, required: false)
Choose the client's acknowledgement.

### 19. **counselor_acknowledgement** (19, select one, required: false)
Choose the counselor's acknowledgement.

### 20. **contract_version** and **counselor_acknowledgement** fields are used to track and version control the contract between the parties.
These fields should be reviewed and edited according to the organization's policies and procedures.

Please make sure to review and understand the form's purpose and fields before completing it. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your organization's management or support team.
