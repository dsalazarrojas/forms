The International Student Application Form is intended for international students who wish to apply for a course of study at the institution. This form is used to gather information about the student's background, qualifications, and motivations for applying. Before completing the form, please note that you should only submit it if you are eligible and have the necessary documents ready.

# International Student Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information about international students who wish to apply for a course at the institution. Please fill it out carefully and thoroughly to ensure that your application is complete and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form in a new tab or window, or print it out if you prefer to work on paper.
2. Enter your information in the required fields. Each field is marked as "required" or "optional".
3. Use the options provided for fields like "Select A Course" to choose the relevant course.
4. Be as detailed as possible when filling out the fields, especially in sections like "Student Bio Data" and "Passport Details".
5. Upload any relevant supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Student Bio Data
* **Student Bio Data** (`student_bio_data`, text, optional)
	+ This section is used to describe your background, interests, and experiences. Be honest and detailed in your response.
### Page 2: Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
	+ Please enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
### Page 3: Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
### Page 4: Address
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your current address.
### Page 5: Passport Details
* **Passport Details** (`passport_details`, text, optional)
	+ Please enter your passport details as per your country's regulations.
### Page 6: Medical Data
* **Medical Data** (`medical_data`, text, optional)
	+ If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please describe them here.
### Page 7: Qualifications
* **Qualifications** (`qualifications`, text, optional)
	+ List any relevant qualifications or skills you have that relate to your application.
### Page 8: Motivation For Application
* **Motivation For Application** (`motivation_for_application`, text, optional)
	+ Explain why you are interested in applying for this course.
### Page 9: Supporting Documents
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional)
	+ Upload any relevant documents that support your application.
### Page 10: Select A Course
* **Select A Course** (`selected_course`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose the course you are interested in applying for.
### Page 11: Further Comments
* **Further Comments** (`further_comments`, text, optional)
	+ If you have any additional information or comments, please include them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to proofread your application before submitting it to ensure that it is complete and accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the institution's admissions team.
* You can submit this form multiple times, but please note that each submission will be reviewed individually.
