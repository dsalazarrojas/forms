# Personality Quiz Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personality Quiz Template is a tool to understand individual personality traits and characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select each question carefully based on your personal preferences and habits.
2. Enter your information in the required fields (indicated by *Required*).
3. Be honest about your responses to get accurate results.
4. Once you have answered all the questions, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, Required): Enter your thoughts and experiences that you want to share.
* **How do you feel about mornings?** (`quiz_question_1`, select_one): Choose how you usually feel about mornings. You can select "Very good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **How do you feel about evenings?** (`quiz_question_2`, select_one): Choose how you usually feel about evenings. You can select "Very good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **How do you feel about weekends?** (`quiz_question_3`, select_one): Choose how you usually feel about weekends. You can select "Very good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **What do you do on weekdays?** (`quiz_question_4`, select_one): Choose how busy you are on weekdays. You can select "Very busy", "Fairly busy", or "Not too busy".
* **What time do you go to sleep?** (`quiz_question_6`, time): Enter the time you usually go to sleep.
* **What do you do in your free time?** (`quiz_question_7`, text): Enter your activities or hobbies that you enjoy doing in your free time.
* **What do you do on weekends?** (`quiz_question_8`, select_one): Choose how busy you are on weekends. You can select "Very busy", "Fairly busy", or "Not too busy".
* **What time do you wake up?** (`quiz_question_9`, time): Enter the time you usually wake up.
* **What can I tell you about yourself?** (`result`, note, Required): This is the result of your personality quiz. Enter your thoughts or comments about your own characteristics.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Full Name** (`user_name`, text, Required): Enter your full name.
* **Age** (`user_age`, number, Required): Enter your age.
* **Job Title** (`user_job`, text, Required): Enter your job title.
* **Location** (`user_location`, text, Required): Enter your location or city.
* **Gender** (`user_gender`, select_one): Choose your gender. You can select "Male", "Female", or "Non-binary".
* **What are your interests?** (`user_interests`, text): Enter your hobbies or interests.
* **What is your language?** (`user_language`, select_multiple): Choose your languages. You can select "English", "Spanish", "French", or "Mandarin".
* **Other languages** (`user_language_other`, text): Enter other languages that you speak.
* **What is your education?** (`user_education`, select_multiple): Choose your education level. You can select "High School", "Some college", "College", or "Postgraduate".
* **What is your occupation?** (`user_occupation`, text): Enter your job or occupation.
* **Other occupation** (`user_occupation_other`, text): Enter other occupation or job title.
* **Notes** (`user_notes`, text): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Comments** (`user_comments`, text): Enter any additional comments or thoughts.

Please note that some fields are required, as indicated by *Required*. Ensure that you fill out all required fields to get the most accurate results.
