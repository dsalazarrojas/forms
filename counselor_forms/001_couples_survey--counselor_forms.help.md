# Couple Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the dynamics of a couple's relationship, covering aspects such as conflict frequency and intensity, love level, and other interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question based on your perception of the situation.
- Select all options that apply to each question.
- Answer all questions to the best of your ability and honestly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Couple Dynamics**: (`select_multiple`, required: false)
  This question is about the general atmosphere in your relationship. Please choose all that apply:
  - Calm and peaceful: The relationship is characterized by a sense of serenity and tranquility.
  - Stable and stable: The relationship is steady and consistent, with little to no conflict.
  - Unpredictable: The relationship is characterized by surprises and unexpected events or moods.
  - Stressful: The relationship is often tense and overwhelming.
- **Conflict Frequency** (`number`, required: false)
  This question is about how often conflicts or disagreements occur in your relationship. Please answer with a number (e.g., 1-5, where 1 = very rarely, 5 = very often).
- **Couple Dynamics Frequency** (`number`, required: false)
  This question is about how often you and your partner interact in a way that maintains or improves your relationship. Please answer with a number (e.g., 1-5, where 1 = very rarely, 5 = very often).
- **Love Level** (`select_one`, required: false)
  This question is about the level of love or affection you and your partner have for each other. Please choose one:
  - High: You have a strong and deep emotional connection.
  - Moderate: Your love is average or moderate, not necessarily strong or weak.
  - Low: Your love is weak or minimal.
- **Partner Closeness** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
  This question is about how close or distant you feel from your partner. Please choose all that apply:
  - Very close: You feel extremely connected and close to your partner.
  - Close: You feel connected and close to your partner, but not extremely so.
  - Somewhat close: You feel somewhat connected but also have some space or distance.
  - Somewhat distant: You feel somewhat disconnected or distant from your partner.
  - Distant: You feel completely disconnected or distant from your partner.
- **Couple Dynamics 3** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
  This question is about the overall mood or atmosphere in your relationship. Please choose all that apply:
  - Happy: The relationship is generally happy and joyful.
  - Neutral: The relationship is neither happy nor unhappy.
  - Sad: The relationship is often sad or negative.
- **Couple Dynamics 4** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
  This question is about the overall energy or dynamics in your relationship. Please choose all that apply:
  - Calm: The relationship is characterized by calmness and serenity.
  - Tense: The relationship is tense or stressful.
  - Chaotic: The relationship is unpredictable and chaotic.
- **Relationship Status** (`select_one`, required: false)
  This question is about your current relationship status. Please choose one:
  - Single: You are single or not in a relationship.
  - Dating: You are dating but not in a committed relationship.
  - In a relationship: You are in a committed relationship.
  - Married: You are married.
- **Love Languages** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
  This question is about how you and your partner communicate and show love to each other. Please choose all that apply:
  - Words of affirmation: Giving compliments and words of encouragement.
  - Quality time: Spending quality time together.
  - Quality gifts: Giving gifts that show thought and care.
  - Quality touch: Physical touch or intimacy.
  - Quality experiences: Sharing experiences and adventures.
