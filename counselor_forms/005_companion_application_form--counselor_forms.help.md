# Companion Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Companion Application Form is for individuals applying to become a companion. It gathers information about your personal details, travel experience, languages spoken, medical training, availability, previous experience, reference contact information, background check consent, and application date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** (Legal name as it appears on your passport) in the **Full Name** field.
2. Enter your **Primary Contact Email** in the **Email Address** field.
3. Provide your **Phone Number**, including the country code.
4. Select your **Passport Issuing Country** from the list.
5. Choose your **Travel Experience Level** (Novice, Moderate, or Expert).
6. Select all languages you speak fluently from the list.
7. Answer whether you have any **Medical or First Aid Training** (Yes, Certified, Yes, Basic Knowledge, or No Training).
8. Choose your **Availability Preferences** (Weekdays, Weekends, Overnight Stays, or Long-term assignments).
9. If applicable, provide a brief description of your **Previous Companion Experience**.
10. Enter your **Reference Contact Information** (Name and Phone/Email of a professional reference).
11. Confirm your consent to a comprehensive **Background Check** as part of the application process.
12. Select today's **Date of Application**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your passport.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Include your country code in the phone number.
* **Passport Issuing Country** (`passport_country`, text, required): Select the country that issued your passport.
* **Travel Experience Level** (`travel_experience_level`, select_one, required): Choose your level of international travel experience:
	+ Novice (Few trips)
	+ Moderate (Frequent travel)
	+ Expert (Extensive global travel)
* **Languages Spoken Fluently** (`languages_spoken`, select_multiple, required): Select all languages you speak fluently:
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ French
	+ Mandarin
	+ German
	+ Japanese
	+ Other
* **Medical or First Aid Training** (`medical_training_status`, select_one, required): Choose your level of medical or first aid training:
	+ Yes, Certified
	+ Yes, Basic Knowledge
	+ No Training
* **General Availability** (`availability_preference`, select_multiple, required): Choose your availability preferences:
	+ Weekdays
	+ Weekends
	+ Overnight Stays
	+ Long-term assignments (1 week+)
* **Previous Companion Experience** (`previous_companion_experience`, text, optional): Briefly describe any similar roles you have held.
* **Reference Contact Information** (`references_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and phone/email of a professional reference.
* **Background Check Consent** (`background_check_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm your consent to a comprehensive background check as part of the application process.
* **Date of Application** (`application_date`, date, required): Select today's date.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and comprehensively to provide the necessary information for the application process.
