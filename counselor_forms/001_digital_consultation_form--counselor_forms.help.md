# Digital Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for digital consultations. It is intended for counselors to gather necessary details to provide personalized services to their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review and select the services you are interested in.
3. Provide any special instructions or follow-up comments.
4. Confirm your next steps and any additional services selected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information (id: 1)
* **Client Information (id: 1)**: Enter your basic contact information.
* **Client Name (id: 2)**: Enter your name.
* **Client Email (id: 3)**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number (id: 4)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Appointment Time (id: 5)**: Enter the preferred appointment time for your consultation.
* **Services Offered (id: 6)**: Select the services you are interested in.
* **Services Selected (id: 7)**: Confirm the services you selected.
* **Special Instructions (id: 8)**: Provide any special instructions or requests.
* **Follow-up Comments (id: 9)**: Add any additional comments for follow-up purposes.
* **Next Step (id: 10)**: Confirm the next step in your consultation process.
* **Next Step Selected (id: 11)**: Confirm any additional next steps selected.
* **Appointment Date (id: 12)**: Enter the preferred appointment date.
* **Staff Name (id: 13)**: Enter the name of the counselor you would like to consult with.
* **Staff ID (id: 14)**: Enter the counselor's ID number.
* **Staff Phone (id: 15)**: Enter the counselor's phone number.
* **Staff Email (id: 16)**: Enter the counselor's email address.
* **Shop Information (id: 17)**: Enter any shop information.
* **Shop Address (id: 18)**: Enter the shop's address.
* **Shop Phone (id: 19)**: Enter the shop's phone number.
* **Shop Email (id: 20)**: Enter the shop's email address.
* **Shop Hours (id: 21)**: Enter the shop's hours of operation.
* **Shop Name (id: 22)**: Enter the shop's name.
* **Services Available (id: 23)**: Select the services available at the shop.
* **Services Price (id: 24)**: Enter the price for the services selected.
* **Total Hours (id: 25)**: Enter the total hours worked for the services.

**Tips**
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the services selected.
* Provide accurate information to ensure smooth communication.
