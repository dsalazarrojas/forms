# Ls54 Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ls54 Form is a comprehensive form used to collect detailed information from individuals or agencies about labor laws and employment-related work history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form completely, ensuring you provide accurate information for each field.
2.  Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors or omissions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Location** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your current or previous workplace location. This information will help us understand your employment history.
*   **Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Work Start Date** (`3`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of your work. This will help us determine the duration of your employment.
*   **Work End Date** (`4`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of your work. This will help us calculate the duration of your employment.
*   **Work Hours** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter the number of hours you worked during your employment.
*   **Employer Name** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your employer. This will help us identify the organization you worked for.
*   **Employer Address** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of your employer, if available.
*   **Employer Phone** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your employer, if available.
*   **Employer Email** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the email address of your employer, if available.
*   **Labor Law Section** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter the section of the labor law that applies to your employment.
*   **Agency Name** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the agency you worked with, if applicable.
*   **Agency Address** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the agency, if applicable.
*   **Agency Phone** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the agency, if applicable.
*   **Agency Email** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the agency, if applicable.
*   **Form Submitter** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter your name, as the person submitting this form.
*   **Signature** (`16`, `text`, required): Sign this form to confirm your identity and submission accuracy.
*   **Date Submitted** (`17`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
