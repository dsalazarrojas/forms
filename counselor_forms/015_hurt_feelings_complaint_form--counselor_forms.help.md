# Hurt Feelings Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hurt Feelings Complaint Form is a confidential and safe space for users to express their concerns and submit a complaint, if necessary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Name**: This is the first step in completing the form. Please provide your name to help us identify and address your concerns.
2. Enter your **Email**: If you have an email address, please provide it so we can contact you to discuss your complaint further.
3. Enter your **Phone Number**: If you have a phone number, please provide it so we can contact you to discuss your complaint further.
4. Describe your **Problem**: Please provide a detailed description of the issue that has caused you to feel hurt.
5. **Submit**: Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send your complaint to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, `text`, optional): This field is not required but can be used to provide any additional information about your complaint.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name to help us identify and address your concerns.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you have an email address, please provide it so we can contact you to discuss your complaint further.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, please provide it so we can contact you to discuss your complaint further.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Please provide a detailed description of the issue that has caused you to feel hurt.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `note`, optional): Click this button to send your complaint to us once you have completed all the fields.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when describing the issue that has caused you to feel hurt.
* If you have any additional information or supporting documents, please attach them to the form or provide them separately.
* Our team is committed to addressing your concerns in a timely and respectful manner.
