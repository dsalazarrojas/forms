# Future Husband Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your ideal future husband's characteristics, preferences, and habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, thinking about what you'd want in a future husband.
2. Choose the best option for each field from the provided list.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "required").
