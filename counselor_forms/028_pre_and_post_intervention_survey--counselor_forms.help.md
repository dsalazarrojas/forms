# Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey is a form used to collect information about participants in an intervention program. The survey aims to gather demographic and behavioral data, including employment status, occupation, education level, income, gender, age, and more.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
2. For fields with multiple options, select the relevant answer from the dropdown list.
3. Enter any additional information in the text fields as required.
4. If you have any pre- and post-intervention changes, describe them in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Title** (`title`, `text`, *required*): Enter the title of the intervention, which will be used to identify the survey.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Participants** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_participants`, `text`, *optional*): Enter any information about the participants, such as their names, ages, or contact information.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Participants Gender** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_participants_gender`, `select_one`, *optional*): Select the gender of the participants from the following options:
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Other
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Participants Age** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_participants_age`, `number`, *optional*): Enter the age of the participants.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Participants Occupation** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_participants_occupation`, `text`, *optional*): Enter the occupation of the participants.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Participants Education** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_participants_education`, `text`, *optional*): Enter the education level of the participants.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Participants Income** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_participants_income`, `number`, *optional*): Enter the income of the participants.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Participants Employment Status** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_participants_employment_status`, `select_one`, *optional*): Select the employment status of the participants from the following options:
	+ Employed
	+ Unemployed
	+ Other
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Participants Employed Hours** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_participants_employed_hours`, `number`, *optional*): Enter the number of hours worked by the participants if they are employed.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Pre Intervention Date** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_pre_intervention_date`, `date`, *optional*): Enter the date of the pre-intervention.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Pre Intervention Time** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_pre_intervention_time`, `time`, *optional*): Enter the time of the pre-intervention.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Post Intervention Date** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_post_intervention_date`, `date`, *optional*): Enter the date of the post-intervention.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Post Intervention Time** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_post_intervention_time`, `time`, *optional*): Enter the time of the post-intervention.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Intervention Type** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_intervention_type`, `select_one`, *optional*): Select the type of intervention from the following options:
	+ Counseling
	+ Support
	+ Other
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Intervention Type Other** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_intervention_type_other`, `text`, *optional*): Enter any other type of intervention not listed above.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Intervention Frequency** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_intervention_frequency`, `select_multiple`, *optional*): Select the frequency of the intervention from the following options:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Pre-Post Change** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_pre_post_change`, `number`, *optional*): Enter any pre- and post-intervention changes experienced by the participants.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Pre-Post Change Type** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_pre_post_change_type`, `text`, *optional*): Enter any additional information about the pre- and post-intervention changes.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Pre-Post Change Notes** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_pre_post_change_notes`, `note`, *optional*): Enter any additional notes about the pre- and post-intervention changes.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Pre-Post Changes** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_pre_post_changes`, `text`, *optional*): Enter any additional information about the pre- and post-intervention changes.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Pre-Post Changes 1** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_pre_post_changes_1`, `text`, *optional*): Enter any additional information about the pre- and post-intervention changes.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Pre-Post Changes 2** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_pre_post_changes_2`, `text`, *optional*): Enter any additional information about the pre- and post-intervention changes.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Pre-Post Changes** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_pre_post_changes`, `number`, *optional*): Enter any pre- and post-intervention changes experienced by the participants.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Pre-Post Changes** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_pre_post_changes`, `number`, *optional*): Enter any pre- and post-intervention changes experienced by the participants.
* **Pre-and-Post Intervention Survey Notes** (`pre_and_post_intervention_survey_notes`, `note`, *optional*): Enter any additional notes about the pre- and post-intervention changes.

**Tips**

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Use the correct units for income and hours worked (e.g., dollars for income, hours worked).
* If you have any pre- and post-intervention changes, describe them in as much detail as possible.
* If you have any additional comments or information, please include them in the text fields.
