# Romantic Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to walk you through the Romantic Survey form, helping you understand what information is being gathered and how to complete it successfully.

## How To Complete This Form

To get the most out of this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and respond thoughtfully.
2. Answer all questions that apply to you and your relationship.
3. Provide clear and concise answers for each question.
4. Be honest and accurate in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Romantic Survey** (`intro_page`, note, not required): This is the introduction to the survey, which briefly explains its purpose and importance.
* **What keeps romance alive?** (`romance_level_1`, text, not required): Please respond with a few words or short phrase describing what keeps romance alive in your relationship.
* **What keeps our romance alive?** (`romance_level_2`, text, not required): Similar to the previous question, respond with a few words or short phrase describing what keeps your romance alive.
* **How often do you do these things?** (`romance_level_3_1`, number, not required): This question is asking about the frequency of certain behaviors in your relationship. Please provide the number of times you engage in these activities per week or month.
* **How long have you been doing these things?** (`romance_level_3_2`, text, not required): Please describe how long you have been doing these specific behaviors.
* **How long have we been married?** (`romance_level_4`, number, not required): This question is asking about the length of your marriage.
* **How long have you had your best friend?** (`romance_level_5`, text, not required): Similar to the previous question, respond with the length of time you've had your best friend.
* **Best friend?** (`best_friend`, select_multiple, not required): Please select all the relationships that apply to you and your best friend.
* **How do you show love through?** (`love_language_what`, text, not required): Respond with a few words or short phrases describing how you show love to your partner.
* **What's the most effective way you express love?** (`love_language`, select_one, not required): Select the most effective way you express love to your partner.
* **How do you show love?** (`love_language_what_1` through `love_language_what_4`, text, not required): These questions are asking about the specific ways you show love to your partner. Respond with a few words or short phrases.
* **How long to complete the survey?** (`survey_completion`, time, not required): This question is asking about the estimated time it will take to complete the survey.
* **How long will it take?** (`survey_completion_1` through `survey_completion_3`, text, not required): These questions are asking about the estimated time it will take to complete the survey. Respond with a few words or short phrases.

Note: All questions are optional unless specified as required in the form's design. Please answer all questions that apply to you and your relationship.
