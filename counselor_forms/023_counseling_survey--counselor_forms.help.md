# Counseling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counseling Survey is a form designed to collect information and feedback from individuals or organizations that have interacted with a counseling service. This form aims to understand their experience, effectiveness of the service, and their availability for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Counseling Survey, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill out all required fields.
2. For each field, make sure to enter accurate and relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro**:
	+ (Note): This is a static field for a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Questions**:
	+ (Select Multiple): Please answer "Yes" or "No" to the following questions:
	+ This field is likely meant to gauge the client's experience and satisfaction with the service.
* **Effectiveness**:
	+ (Select One): How would you rate the effectiveness of our service?
	+ This field is likely meant to evaluate the quality of the service provided.
* **Feedback**:
	+ (Text): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
	+ This field is meant for clients to provide their thoughts and suggestions for improvement.
* **Suggestions**:
	+ (Text): Any other suggestions you have for our service?
	+ This field is similar to the Feedback field, but is more focused on suggestions.
* **Contact**:
	+ (Email): Please enter your email address to enable us to contact you.
	+ This field is likely meant for clients to be contacted for follow-up or additional information.
* **Scheduling**:
	+ (Date): Please select a date for your next session.
	+ This field is meant for scheduling future sessions.
* **Time**:
	+ (Time): Please select a time for your next session.
	+ This field is likely meant for scheduling specific times for future sessions.
* **Availability**:
	+ (Number): How many sessions do you have available for this month?
	+ This field is meant to gauge the client's availability for future sessions.
* **Comments**:
	+ (Select One): Do you have any other comments about your experience?
	+ This field is similar to the Feedback field, but is more focused on providing additional comments.
