# Support Resources For Teens - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Support Resources For Teens form is designed to gather information from teens about their support resources and next steps. This form is intended to be completed by individuals seeking support, such as students, family members, or caregivers. The form will be used to help identify and provide relevant support resources and services to the individual.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the Introduction page to understand the purpose and process of the form.
* Provide your name and any other relevant contact information (optional).
* Select the resources that you are currently using or have used in the past (Resource 1, Resource 2, Resource 3, Resource 4).
* Choose the resources that you think would be helpful to you (Resource 1, Resource 2, Resource 3, Resource 4).
* Provide any additional notes or comments in the Notes field.
* Describe your next steps in the Next Steps field.
* If applicable, select a follow-up option for further support.
* Provide the submission date and time.
* Select who submitted the form (Submitted By).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page provides an overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Name**: Enter your name or the name of the individual seeking support.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address (if applicable).
* **Age**: Enter your age or the age of the individual seeking support.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number (if applicable).
* **Resource 1**: Select the resources you are currently using or have used in the past (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
*   * **Resource 2**: Select the resources that you think would be helpful to you (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
*   * **Resource 3**: Select the resources that you are currently using or have used in the past (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
*   * **Resource 4**: Select the resources that you think would be helpful to you (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your support resources and next steps.
* **Next Steps**: Describe your next steps or actions you plan to take.
* **Follow-up**: Select a follow-up option for further support (if applicable).
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted the form (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when providing information about your support resources and next steps.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask for help or clarification.
* If you need further support or follow-up, select an option in the Follow-up field.
* Make sure to submit the form in a timely manner to ensure that your support resources are addressed promptly.
