# Mentor Matching Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mentor Matching Form is designed to help users get matched with a suitable mentor who can provide guidance and support in their desired area of expertise. By filling out this form, you will be providing essential information about your session preferences and goals, which will help our system to find the best match for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide information about the number of sessions you are looking for.
2. Select your primary session goals.
3. Indicate your areas of expertise.
4. Choose your matching criteria.
5. Select the date and time for your first session.
6. Choose your desired session frequency.
7. Enter any session notes that you would like to share with your mentor.
8. Provide feedback from your mentee after the session.
9. Enter any comments from your mentee after the session.
10. Enter feedback from your mentor after the session.
11. Add any comments about the form completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details**: Enter the number of sessions you are looking for. This will help us understand your expectations and ensure that we can provide you with the right amount of support.
* **Session Goals**: Select one of the options to indicate the primary focus of your sessions. For example, if you select "Option 1", our system will look for mentors with expertise in that area.
* **Mentor Expertise**: Select all the areas of expertise that you are looking for in your mentor. This will help us narrow down the search results to ensure that we find someone with the right skills and experience.
* **Matching Criteria**: Choose the criteria that you would like to use to match with a mentor. This could be language proficiency, industry experience, or other relevant factors.
* **Session Scheduling**: Enter the date for your first session. This will help us schedule the initial meeting.
* **Time Scheduling**: Select the time for your first session. This will help us determine the most convenient time for you and your mentor.
* **Session Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to meet with your mentor. For example, weekly, biweekly, or monthly.
* **Session Notes**: Enter any notes or comments you would like to share with your mentor before the session.
* **Mentee Feedback**: Enter any feedback or comments from your mentee after the session. This will help us improve our services.
* **Mentee Comments**: Enter any comments or suggestions from your mentee about the session.
* **Mentor Feedback**: Enter any feedback or comments from your mentor after the session.
* **Form Completion**: Enter any comments or suggestions about the form completion process. This will help us improve our form and services.
