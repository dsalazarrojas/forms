# Couples Program Development Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information and agreements from couples participating in a program or development initiative. It serves as a foundation for both participants and program organizers to understand their responsibilities and expectations, ensuring a smooth and successful program execution.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, providing detailed information where necessary.
2.  Review the form carefully to ensure you have addressed all required and optional fields.
3.  If you're unsure about any field, refer to the explanations below for clarity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Info** (`General Info`, `text`, required: `false`): This section is for providing any general information about the program or development initiative you're participating in. Include any relevant details that might be helpful for the organizers or other participants to know.
*   **Participants Info 1** (`Participants Info 1`, `text`, required: `false`): Here, provide information about the first participant in the couple. This might include names, contact details, or any other relevant personal or contact information.
*   **Participants Info 2** (`Participants Info 2`, `text`, required: `false`): Similarly, this section is for providing details about the second participant in the couple. Ensure you include any necessary personal or contact information.
*   **Program Details** (`Program Details`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe the specific details of the program or development initiative you're participating in. This could include its objectives, duration, or any unique aspects.
*   **Program Expectations** (`Program Expectations`, `text`, required: `false`): Clearly outline your expectations from the program or initiative. This could include what you hope to achieve, what you're willing to commit to, or any specific requirements you have.
*   **Program Outcome** (`Program Outcome`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Indicate whether the program or initiative is expected to have a positive outcome ('Yes') or not ('No'). If 'Yes', you might want to provide further details in the next field.
*   **Program Outcome Other** (`Program Outcome Other`, `text`, required: `false`): Use this field to specify any other outcome or result you expect from the program or initiative if you've selected 'Other' in the previous field.
*   **Program Notes** (`Program Notes`, `note`, required: `false`): Add any additional notes or comments about the program or initiative. This can be helpful for organizers to remember specific details or to refer back to later.
*   **Participant Agreement** (`Participant Agreement`, `text`, required: `false`): Confirm your agreement to participate in the program or initiative. This is your commitment to abide by the program's rules, guidelines, and expectations.
*   **Program Agreement** (`Program Agreement`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is meant for the program organizers to outline their commitments and responsibilities towards the participants. Ensure you read and agree to these terms before proceeding.
*   **Participant Signature** (`Participant Signature`, `date`, required: `false`): Date and sign this field upon completion of the form, indicating your agreement and commitment to participate.
*   **Program Signature** (`Program Signature`, `date`, required: `false`): Similarly, this field is for program organizers to date and sign, affirming their agreement and commitment to the terms provided.
