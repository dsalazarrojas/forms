# Counseling Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect personal and medical information from clients for counseling services.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
2.  Ensure all fields are filled out completely, even if they do not apply to you.
3.  Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Information** (`1`): 
    *  Enter your name as it appears on your identification. 
*   **Demographics** (`2`): 
    *  Enter your age, date of birth, sex, and other personal details relevant to your demographic status.
*   **Contact Information** (`3`): 
    *  Enter your current contact number and other personal contact details.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`4`): 
    *  Enter the name and contact information of a family member or friend you would like to have contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`5`): 
    *  Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
*   **Medical History** (`6`): 
    *  Enter any medical history you have, such as past illnesses or injuries.
*   **Mental Health History** (`7`): 
    *  Enter any mental health history you have, such as past traumas or mental health conditions.
*   **Other Medical History** (`8`): 
    *  Enter any other medical history not included in medical history.
*   **Medical Procedure** (`9`): 
    *  Check if you have had any medical procedures, such as surgeries or hospitalizations.
*   **Other Medical Procedure** (`10`): 
    *  Enter any other medical procedures you have had that are not listed.
*   **Medical Procedure Details** (`11`): 
    *  Enter details about any medical procedures you have had.
*   **Allergies** (`12`): 
    *  Enter any allergies you have.
*   **Medical Condition** (`13`): 
    *  Enter any medical conditions you have.
*   **Medication** (`14`): 
    *  Enter any medications you are currently taking.
*   **Insurance** (`15`): 
    *  Enter your insurance information.
*   **Name** (`16`): 
    *  Enter the name of your emergency contact.
*   **Relationship** (`17`): 
    *  Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
*   **Phone** (`18`): 
    *  Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Email** (`19`): 
    *  Enter the email of your emergency contact.
*   **Address** (`20`): 
    *  Enter the address of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Urgent Contact** (`21`): 
    *  Check if you would like your emergency contact to be contacted in case of an emergency.
*   **Rationale** (`22`): 
    *  Enter the reason for any medical procedures.
*   **Procedure** (`23`): 
    *  Enter any emergency procedures you have experienced.
*   **Details** (`24`): 
    *  Enter details about any emergency procedures you have experienced.
*   **Rationale for Emergency Procedure** (`25`): 
    *  Enter the reason for any emergency procedure. 

**Tips:**

*   Please provide as much detail as possible for each field.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your counselor for assistance. 

Note: If you are unsure about a field or have any issues, please consult with your counselor for guidance. This form is designed to collect information to assist your counselor in providing the best possible care.
