# Boy Scouts Rank Advancement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to track a Boy Scout's progress towards rank advancement. It collects information about the Scout's current rank, the rank they are advancing to, and other requirements completed to achieve this advancement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Scout's first name in the "Scout First Name" field.
2. Enter the Scout's last name in the "Scout Last Name" field.
3. Enter the troop number in the "Troop Number" field.
4. Select the Scout's current rank from the "Current Rank" dropdown menu.
5. Select the rank the Scout is advancing to from the "Rank Advancing To" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the date the Scoutmaster conference was completed in the "Scoutmaster Conference Date" field.
7. Enter the name of the Scoutmaster who completed the conference in the "Scoutmaster Name" field.
8. Enter the date the Board of Review was completed in the "Board of Review Date" field.
9. Enter the names of the Board of Review members in the "Board of Review Members" field.
10. Select how long the Scout has been in their current rank from the "Time in Current Rank" dropdown menu.
11. Enter the number of merit badges earned for this rank in the "Merit Badges Earned" field.
12. Enter a list of merit badges earned for this rank in the "Merit Badge List" field.
13. Enter the number of service hours completed in the "Service Hours Completed" field.
14. Describe the service project completed in the "Service Project Description" field (optional).
15. If applicable, enter the leadership position held in the "Leadership Position" field.
16. Enter how long the Scout held the leadership position in the "Leadership Duration" field (optional).
17. Enter the number of outdoor participation hours completed in the "Outdoor Participation" field.
18. Enter the total number of camping nights completed in the "Camping Nights" field.
19. Select whether the Scout has met their physical fitness requirements from the "Physical Fitness" dropdown menu.
20. Select whether the Scout has completed their citizenship requirements from the "Citizenship Requirements" dropdown menu.
21. Select whether the Scout has completed their personal management requirements from the "Personal Management" dropdown menu.
22. Select whether the Scout has completed their communications requirements from the "Communications Requirements" dropdown menu.
23. Enter any additional requirements completed in the "Additional Requirements" field (optional).
24. Enter the name of the unit leader who approved this advancement in the "Unit Leader Approval" field.
25. Enter the electronic signature of the unit leader in the "Unit Leader Signature" field.
26. Enter the date of approval from the "Approval Date" field.
27. Enter the name of the committee chair who approved this advancement in the "Committee Chair Name" field.
28. Enter the electronic signature of the committee chair in the "Committee Chair Signature" field.
29. Have the Scout sign the form in the "Scout Signature" field.
30. Have the parent or guardian sign the form in the "Parent or Guardian Signature" field.
