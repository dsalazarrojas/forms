# Counseling Confidentiality Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Counseling Confidentiality Form is a document used to collect information from clients about their counseling sessions. This form is designed to establish clear boundaries and expectations between the client and the counselor, ensuring a safe and effective therapeutic relationship.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your contact information: Enter your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Read and agree to the confidentiality agreements: Carefully review the agreements and select all that apply.
3. Select the session frequency: Choose the frequency of your sessions, either weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.
4. Select the session duration: Choose the duration of your sessions, either 30, 60, or 90 minutes.
5. Enter your emergency contact information: Provide the name, phone number, and email of your emergency contact.
6. Review and agree to the billing and payment agreements: Carefully review the agreements and select all that apply.
7. Review and agree to the cancellation policies: Carefully review the policies and select all that apply.
8. Provide your payment information: Enter any payment details if applicable.
