This is the user-facing help guide for the "Summer Camp Financial Assistance Application Form". The purpose of this form is to collect financial information from applicants for summer camp assistance. It's crucial that end users understand what the form is for and what's required to fill it out correctly.

## Purpose
The Summer Camp Financial Assistance Application Form is used to gather information about applicants' financial situations, aid amounts, aid types, and application status. This form is essential to determine eligibility and provide necessary support for summer camps.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:

* Read the instructions carefully before starting.
* Answer each question accurately and truthfully.
* Ensure all required fields are completed.
* Review your answers for any inconsistencies or errors.
* Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Summer Camp Financial Aid Amount (2)**: Enter the amount of financial aid you are requesting.
- **Financial Aid Status (3)**: Select the status of your financial aid application: Financial Aid Application, Denied, or Pending.
- **Financial Aid Type (4)**: Choose the type of financial aid you are applying for: Full Scholarship, Partial Scholarship, or Grant.
- **Financial Aid Grant (5)**: Select True or False if you are applying for a grant.
- **Financial Aid Aid Amount (6)**: Enter the amount of financial aid you are requesting.
- **Financial Aid Aid Status (7)**: Select the status of your financial aid application: Financial Aid Application, Denied, or Pending.
- **Financial Aid Aid Type (8)**: Choose the type of financial aid you are applying for: Full Scholarship, Partial Scholarship, or Grant.
- **Financial Aid Other (9)**: Enter any additional information or details about your financial aid application.
- **Financial Aid Other Amount (10)**: Enter the amount of any additional financial aid you are requesting.
- **Financial Aid Other Type (11)**: Choose the type of additional financial aid you are applying for: Full Scholarship, Partial Scholarship, or Grant.
- **Financial Aid Notes (12)**: Provide any additional comments or explanations about your financial aid application.
- **Financial Aid Aid Date (13)**: Enter the date you received financial aid.
- **Financial Aid Aid Time (14)**: Enter the time you received financial aid.
- **Financial Aid Start Date (15)**: Enter the start date of your financial aid period.
- **Financial Aid Start Time (16)**: Enter the start time of your financial aid period.
- **Financial Aid End Date (17)**: Enter the end date of your financial aid period.
- **Financial Aid End Time (18)**: Enter the end time of your financial aid period.
- **Financial Aid Application Date (19)**: Enter the date you applied for financial aid.
- **Financial Aid Application Time (20)**: Enter the time you applied for financial aid.
- **Financial Aid Application Status (21)**: Select the status of your financial aid application: Financial Aid Application, Denied, or Pending.
- **Financial Aid Application Type (22)**: Choose the type of financial aid application you are making: Full Scholarship, Partial Scholarship, or Grant.
