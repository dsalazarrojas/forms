# couples_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "couples_questionnaire" form is a tool designed to help couples assess and improve their relationship dynamics. It is a comprehensive questionnaire that covers various aspects of relationships, including communication styles, conflict resolution, emotional intelligence, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best describe your current relationship status or experience.
3. Take your time to fill out the form, and do not rush.
4. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### relationship_status (id: 1)
* **What is your relationship status?** (`select_one`, optional)
	+ Choose the option that best describes your current relationship status: Single, In a relationship, Married, Widowed, Divorced, or Other.

### partners_gender (id: 2)

	+ Select all applicable options for the gender of your partner: Male, Female, Non-binary, Other.
