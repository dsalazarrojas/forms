# Client Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Details Form is a crucial part of the counseling process. It collects essential information about the client's demographics, background, and counseling needs. This data will help the counselor create a personalized plan for supporting the client.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form carefully, providing accurate and truthful information about yourself.
2. Ensure that you answer all required fields to ensure a complete and thorough intake process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`client_first_name`, text, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`client_last_name`, text, required): Enter your surname.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter your best contact number.
* **Street Address** (`client_address`, text, required): Provide your complete mailing address.
* **City** (`client_city`, text, required): Enter your city name.
* **State** (`client_state`, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP Code** (`client_zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Choose your gender identity.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current marital status.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, optional): Enter your current job title or occupation.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, optional): Enter your current employer's name.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, optional): Select your highest level of education completed.
* **Assigned Counselor** (`counselor_name`, text, optional): If assigned, enter the name of your assigned counselor.
* **Service Type Needed** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of counseling service you require.
* **Primary Issue or Concern** (`presenting_issue`, text, required): Describe the primary issue or concern bringing you to counseling.
* **Have You Had Counseling Before?** (`previous_counseling`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have had previous counseling experience.
* **Current Stressors** (`current_stressors`, text, optional): Describe the current stressors affecting you.
* **Do You Have a Support System?** (`support_system`, select_one, required): Choose how much support you have.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Choose how you learned about our services.
* **Available Days and Times** (`availability`, text, required): Describe your available days and times for counseling sessions.
* **Intake Date** (`intake_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your initial intake session.

Please note that some fields are marked as "required" or "optional", indicating the importance of providing accurate and complete information for a successful counseling process.
