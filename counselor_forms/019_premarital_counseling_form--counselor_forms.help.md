# Premarital Counseling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals seeking premarital counseling. The form aims to assess the current relationship status, communication style, and conflict resolution skills of the couple, as well as their expectations and goals for counseling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please provide as much information as you can.
4. Once you have completed the form, review your answers carefully to ensure they accurately reflect your thoughts and feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`):
	+ This question asks about your current relationship status, including how long you have been together and your level of commitment.
* **Communication Style** (`communication_style`):
	+ This question asks about how you communicate with your partner, including how you handle disagreements and conflicts.
* **Conflict Resolution** (`conflict_resolution`):
	+ This question asks about how you and your partner resolve conflicts, including whether you have a resolution style that works for both of you.
* **Goals For Counseling** (`goals_for_counseling`):
	+ This question asks about your goals for counseling, including what you hope to achieve and what you would like to work on.
* **Expectations For Counselor** (`expectations_for_counselor`):
	+ This question asks about your expectations for the counselor, including what you hope they will bring to the counseling process.
* **Relationship Background** (`relationship_background`):
	+ This question asks about your relationship background, including how you met and any significant events that have shaped your relationship.
* **Spiritual Practices** (`spiritual_practices`):
	+ This question asks about your spiritual practices, including any religious or spiritual beliefs that are important to you.
* **Family History** (`family_history`):
	+ This question asks about your family history, including any relationships with family members that may impact your current relationship.
* **Relationship Dynamics** (`relationship_dynamics`):
	+ This question asks about the dynamics of your relationship, including how you interact with each other and any common interests or activities.
* **Relationship Strengths** (`relationship_strengths`):
	+ This question asks about the strengths of your relationship, including what you appreciate about your partner and how you support each other.
* **Relationship Challenges** (`relationship_challenges`):
	+ This question asks about any challenges you are facing in your relationship, including areas where you may need support and guidance.
* **Counseling Goals** (`counseling_goals`):

## Tips

* Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please provide as much information as you can.
* Review your answers carefully to ensure they accurately reflect your thoughts and feelings.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please let the counselor know.
