# Health And Consent Form For Summer Camp - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form collects health and consent information from parents or guardians of campers to ensure their safety and well-being during summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or assistance from the camp staff.
3. Make sure to include any relevant medical history, allergies, or special needs of the camper.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer Camp Activity** (`summer_camp_activity`, text, optional): 
This field is for any activities or programs your child will be participating in during summer camp. Please list any specific activities or programs that may pose a risk or require special attention.
* **Camper Health Issues** (`camper_health_issues`, text, optional): 
If your child has any health issues, please list them here. This may include chronic illnesses, disabilities, or other medical conditions that may affect their ability to participate in activities or interact with others.
* **Parents Consent** (`parents_consent`, select_multiple, optional): 
Please indicate if you give consent for your child to participate in activities, receive medical treatment, or interact with others. Choose all applicable options.
* **Immediate Treatment** (`immediate_treatment`, select_one, optional): 
Indicate if your child will receive immediate medical treatment in case of an emergency.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): 
Provide a brief medical history of your child, including any significant medical events or diagnoses.
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, text, optional): 
List any medications or substances that your child is allergic to.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): 
Provide the contact information of a person to reach in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Emergency Contact** (`medical_emergency_contact`, text, optional): 
Provide the contact information of a person to reach in case of a medical emergency.
* **Camper Medical History** (`camper_medical_history`, text, optional): 
Please provide any recent medical history, including any diagnoses, treatments, or procedures.
* **Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment`, select_multiple, optional): 
Indicate if your child will receive any medical treatment during summer camp, such as medication or physical therapy.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): 
List any allergies or sensitivities your child has.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): 
Provide any medical conditions your child has, such as diabetes, epilepsy, or other conditions.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, text, optional): 
List any special needs or accommodations your child requires, such as wheelchair accessibility or extra support staff.

## Tips

* Please ensure accuracy and completeness when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or assistance from the camp staff.
* You can contact the camp staff for help if you need to update or modify any information provided.
