# Ethics Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ethics Complaint Form is used to report incidents or grievances related to unethical behavior or actions within the organization. This form is intended to ensure that complaints are handled promptly and fairly, and to provide a clear and concise record of the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the complainant category from the options provided in the "Complainant Information" field. This will help us to understand who the complainant is (Counselor, Staff, or Client).
2. Enter a detailed description of the incident in the "Complaint Information" field.
3. Provide any relevant supporting evidence or documentation in the "Incident Details" field.
4. In the "Supporting Evidence" field, please attach any relevant files or documents that support your complaint.
5. In the "Submitter Information" field, select the category of the person submitting the complaint (Staff or Counselor).
6. Enter your name, email, phone number, and job title in the "Submitter Information" fields.
7. Type your message or explanation for submitting the complaint in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complainant Information (Counselor, Staff, or Client)**: Select the category of the person who is reporting the incident.
* **Complaint Information**: Enter a detailed description of the incident, including dates, times, and specific events.
* **Incident Details**: Provide any relevant supporting details about the incident.
* **Supporting Evidence**: Attach any files, documents, or images that support your complaint.
* **Submitter Information (Staff or Counselor)**: Select the category of the person submitting the complaint.
* **Name**: Enter your name and title.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Organization**: Enter your organization or department.
* **Message**: Type a clear and concise message or explanation for submitting the complaint.
