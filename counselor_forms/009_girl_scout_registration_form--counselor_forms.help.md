# Girl Scout Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a Girl Scout, including their medical and emergency contact information, and to ensure that the Girl Scout's grade level is accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Girl Scout's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the Girl Scout's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the Girl Scout's address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter the Girl Scout's email in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the Girl Scout's phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select the Girl Scout's grade level from the options provided.
7. If the Girl Scout's grade level is not listed, please use the field "Please select grade level if not listed" to provide the correct grade level.
8. Enter the Girl Scout's troop number and name if available.
9. Select the relationship of the emergency contact person in the "How do you know this child?" field.
10. Enter the emergency contact person's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
11. Enter the emergency contact person's email in the "Emergency Contact Email" field.
12. Enter the parent's first name in the "Parent First Name" field.
13. Enter the parent's last name in the "Parent Last Name" field.
14. Enter the parent's email in the "Parent Email" field.
15. Enter the parent's phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
16. If the Girl Scout has any medical conditions, please list them in the "Medical Conditions" field.
17. If the Girl Scout has any allergies, please list them in the "Allergy" field.
18. If the Girl Scout is on any medication, please list it in the "Medication" field.
19. If there are any medical notes, please provide them in the "Medical Notes" field.
20. If the Girl Scout has any special medical needs, please list them in the "Special Medical Needs" field.
21. Finally, please select who signed this form from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): This is the Girl Scout's first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): This is the Girl Scout's last name.
* **Address** (Address, text, required): This is the Girl Scout's address.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): This is the Girl Scout's email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): This is the Girl Scout's phone number.
* **Birth Date** (Birth Date, date, required): This is the Girl Scout's birth date.
* **Grade Level** (Grade Level, select_one, required): This is the Girl Scout's grade level.
* **Please select grade level if not listed** (Please select grade level if not listed, text, optional): If the Girl Scout's grade level is not listed, please use this field to provide the correct grade level.
* **Troop Number** (Troop Number, text, required): This is the Girl Scout's troop number.
* **Troop Name** (Troop Name, text, required): This is the Girl Scout's troop name.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (Emergency Contact First Name, text, required): This is the emergency contact person's first name.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (Emergency Contact Last Name, text, required): This is the emergency contact person's last name.
* **How do you know this child?** (How do you know this child?, select_one, required): This is how you know the Girl Scout.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, required): This is the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Emergency Contact Email, email, required): This is the emergency contact person's email address.
* **Parent First Name** (Parent First Name, text, required): This is the parent's first name.
* **Parent Last Name** (Parent Last Name, text, required): This is the parent's last name.
* **Parent Email** (Parent Email, email, required): This is the parent's email address.
* **Parent Phone** (Parent Phone, text, required): This is the parent's phone number.
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, text, required): This is any medical conditions the Girl Scout may have.
* **Allergy** (Allergy, text, required): This is any allergy the Girl Scout may have.
* **Medication** (Medication, text, required): This is any medication the Girl Scout may be on.
* **Medical Notes** (Medical Notes, text, required): This is any medical notes about the Girl Scout.
* **Special Medical Needs** (Special Medical Needs, text, required): This is any special medical needs the Girl Scout may have.
* **Signed By** (Signed By, select_one, required): This is who signed this form.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance.
