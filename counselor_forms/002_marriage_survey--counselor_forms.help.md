# Marriage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marriage Survey form is designed to gather information about users' marital status, relationship categories, and other related details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select your current marital status from the options provided in the "What is your current marital status?" field.
2. In the "Describe your relationship" field, please provide a brief description of your relationship.
3. Answer the "Is this form assigned to you?" question with either "True" or "False".
4. In the "What is your ID number?" field, enter your identification number as required.
5. Select how you categorize your marriage in the "How do you categorize your marriage?" field.
6. In the "What is your name?" field, enter your name as required.
7. Please describe yourself in the "Please describe yourself" field.
8. Select your marital category in the "Marital Category" field.
9. Answer the "Is this form assigned to you again?" question with either "True" or "False".
10. In the "Are you in a relationship?" field, select whether you are in a relationship or not.
11. In the "Other Description" field, provide any additional information that does not fit into the other fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Marriage Survey** (`id: 1`, `label: Marriage Survey`, `type: note`): This is the title of the form and a note field, providing a brief introduction to the form.
* **What is your current marital status?** (`id: 2`, `label: What is your current marital status?`, `type: select_one`): This field asks you to select your current marital status from the options provided (Married, Single, Divorced, Widowed).
* **Describe your relationship** (`id: 3`, `label: Describe your relationship`, `type: text`): Please provide a brief description of your relationship.
* **Is this form assigned to you?** (`id: 4`, `label: Is this form assigned to you?`, `type: select_one`): Answer this question with either "True" or "False".
* **What is your ID number?** (`id: 5`, `label: What is your ID number?`, `type: number`): Enter your identification number as required.
* **How do you categorize your marriage?** (`id: 6`, `label: How do you categorize your marriage?`, `type: select_one`): Select how you categorize your marriage from the options provided (Traditional, Modern, Online, Other (please specify)).
* **What is your name?** (`id: 7`, `label: What is your name?`, `type: text`): Enter your name as required.
* **Please describe yourself** (`id: 8`, `label: Please describe yourself`, `type: text`): Please describe yourself in this field.
* **Marital Category** (`id: 9`, `label: Marital Category`, `type: select_one`): Select your marital category from the options provided (Traditional, Modern, Online, Other (please specify)).
* **Assigned Tool 2** (`id: 10`, `label: Assigned Tool 2`, `type: select_one`): Answer this question with either "True" or "False".
* **Self Description** (`id: 11`, `label: Self Description`, `type: text`): Please describe yourself in this field.
* **Are you in a relationship?** (`id: 12`, `label: Are you in a relationship?`, `type: select_one`): Select whether you are in a relationship or not.
* **Other Description** (`id: 13`, `label: Other Description`, `type: text`): Please provide any additional information that does not fit into the other fields.
