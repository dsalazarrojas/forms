# Mental Health First Aid Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register individuals for a Mental Health First Aid Course. The course aims to provide individuals with the skills and knowledge to support their mental health and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name and Last Name.
2. Provide your Email address.
3. Enter your Phone number.
4. Select the date and time you would like to attend the course.
5. Submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you would like to receive course updates and information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted if needed.
* **Course Date** (`course_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to attend the course from the calendar.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time you would like to attend the course.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate information, especially your name and email address.
* If you have any dietary requirements or special needs, please contact the course organizers separately.
* Once you have submitted your registration, you will receive a confirmation email with further instructions.
