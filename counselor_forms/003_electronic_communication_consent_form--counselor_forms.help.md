# Electronic Communication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electronic Communication Consent Form is used to gather information about a client's electronic communication preferences for counseling sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. Select the options that best fit your communication preferences.
3. Enter your email address and phone number, if applicable.
4. Review your choices carefully to ensure you are comfortable with the selected methods of communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): This field is where you provide a brief title to the electronic communication consent form.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of counseling form you are using this form for (e.g., Counselor Forms, Other).
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the form, if desired.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the form, if required.
* **HIPAA Compliance** (`hipaa_compliance`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you are willing to share electronic communications with your healthcare provider (True/False).
* **Email Consent** (`email_consent`, select_one, optional): Select whether you are willing to receive electronic communications via email (True/False).
* **Text Consent** (`text_consent`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are willing to receive electronic communications via text messaging (True/False).
* **Portal Consent** (`portal_consent`, select_one, optional): Select whether you are willing to access electronic client portals (True/False).
* **Other Consent** (`other_consent`, select_one, optional): Check if you have any other communication preferences not listed (Yes/No).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns about your electronic communication preferences.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you have given consent to receive electronic communications via email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you have given consent to receive electronic communications via text messaging.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you, if applicable.

## Tips
* Be honest about your electronic communication preferences to ensure you receive the information you need in a format that works for you.
* Review and update your preferences as your needs change.
* If you have any concerns or questions about electronic communication, discuss them with your healthcare provider.
* If you need help with this form or any other aspect of the counseling service, contact your healthcare provider.
