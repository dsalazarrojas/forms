# WhatsYourLoveLanguageQuiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The WhatsYourLoveLanguageQuiz is a self-assessment tool designed to help you discover your unique love language, which is the way you give and receive love and affection. The results will provide insights into how you prefer to express and receive love in relationships, helping you build stronger, more meaningful connections with others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question on each page with the corresponding option that best describes your preference.
2.  Move on to the next page after completing the current one.
3.  Continue until you have answered all questions.
4.  Review the results to understand your love language and how you prefer to express and receive love.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1.  **what_s_your_love_language_quiz**
    *   Type: note
    *   Required: false

This is an introduction page to the form. It is here to provide an overview of the quiz and its purpose.

### 2.  **love_language**
    *   Type: select_multiple
    *   Required: false

Choose how often you give love and receive love. The options are:

    *   Yes - You frequently give and receive love in this way
    *   No - You rarely or never give or receive love in this way

### 3.  **time_spent**
    *   Type: time
    *   Required: false

Estimate the amount of time you spend giving and receiving love. The options are:

    *   Less than 1 hour
    *   1-2 hours
    *   2-4 hours
    *   More than 4 hours

### 4.  **quality_of_time_spent**
    *   Type: number
    *   Required: false

Rate the quality of time spent giving and receiving love. The options are:

    *   1 - Very Poor
    *   2 - Fair
    *   3 - Good
    *   4 - Very Good
    *   5 - Excellent

### 5.  **gifts_and_gestures**
    *   Type: select_multiple
    *   Required: false

Choose how often you give and receive gifts and gestures. The options are:

    *   Yes - You frequently give and receive gifts and gestures
    *   No - You rarely or never give or receive gifts and gestures

### 6.  **acts_of_service**
    *   Type: select_multiple
    *   Required: false

Choose how often you give and receive acts of service. The options are:

    *   Yes - You frequently give and receive acts of service
    *   No - You rarely or never give or receive acts of service

### 7.  **words_of_affirmation**
    *   Type: select_multiple
    *   Required: false

Choose how often you give and receive words of affirmation. The options are:

    *   Yes - You frequently give and receive words of affirmation
    *   No - You rarely or never give or receive words of affirmation

## Tips

- Be honest with your answers, as this will give you accurate results.
- Take your time to think about your preferences, as this will help you complete the form accurately.
- Review your results carefully, as this will provide you with valuable insights into your love language.
