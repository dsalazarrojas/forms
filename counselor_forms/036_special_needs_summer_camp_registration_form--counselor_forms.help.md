# special_needs_summer_camp_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "special_needs_summer_camp_registration_form" is used to gather information about a participant's contact details, medical history, and special needs for the summer camp. This form helps the camp staff understand any potential concerns and prepare for the participant's stay to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields with your information. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date details about your contact information, medical history, and special needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required): This field is used to gather your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Medical Info** (Medical Info, text, required): Please enter any relevant medical history or conditions that may affect your stay at the camp.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, select multiple, required): Check all the allergens or sensitivities that apply to you (e.g., "Yes" for "Peanuts" if you are allergic to peanuts).
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, text, required): Provide information about any ongoing medical conditions that may impact your camp experience.
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of a trusted individual who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Special Needs** (Special Needs, select one, required): Check "Yes" if you have special needs that require extra support or accommodations.
* **Special Needs Info** (Special Needs Info, text, required): Provide detailed information about your special needs.
* **Camp Info** (Camp Info, text, required): Enter any information about your previous or upcoming camp experience, including camp dates or specific camp activities.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, required): Share any additional information about yourself that may be helpful for the camp staff to know.
* **Camp Experience** (Camp Experience, text, required): Tell us about your previous experience with the summer camp or any relevant skills you may have.
* **Previous Camp Experience** (Previous Camp Experience, text, required): If you have attended the summer camp before, provide details about your experience, including dates and notable events.

## Tips
* Make sure to update your contact information if it changes.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Please be honest about your medical history and special needs.
* If you have any additional comments or information, feel free to share it with us.
