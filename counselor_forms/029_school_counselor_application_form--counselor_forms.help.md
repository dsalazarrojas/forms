# school_counselor_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The school_counselor_application_form is a simple application form for school counselors to apply for a position at an educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Candidate Information" page.
2. Provide your personal details in the "Personal Details" page.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Contact" page.
4. List your educational background in the "Education" page.
5. Describe your work experience in the "Work Experience" page.
6. Mention your professional certifications in the "Professional Certifications" page.
7. List your references in the "References" page.
8. Upload your resume in the "Upload Resume" page.
9. Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Information**: Enter your name, email, and any other identifying information. This is the first page of the form.
* **Personal Details**: Provide a brief description of yourself, including your interests, hobbies, and any other relevant information. This page is optional, but it helps us get to know you better.
* **Contact**: Enter your contact information, including your phone number and address. This information will be used to contact you for further evaluation or interviews.
* **Education**: List your educational background, including your degree, institution, and dates of attendance. This will help us understand your academic background.
* **Work Experience**: Describe your previous work experience, including the job title, institution, and dates of employment. This will help us evaluate your work experience and relevance to the position.
* **Professional Certifications**: List any professional certifications you hold, such as teaching certifications or counseling certifications. This will help us evaluate your qualifications for the position.
* **References**: List at least two professional references, including their name, job title, and contact information. This will help us evaluate your reputation and work ethic.
* **Upload Resume**: Upload your resume in PDF or docx format. This will help us review your detailed work history and experience.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to submit your application. Make sure to review all pages before submitting.

# Tips:
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields, even if they are optional.
* Use clear and concise language to fill out the form.
* Double-check your resume for accuracy and completeness.
