# Dating Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help couples or individuals assess the quality and compatibility of their romantic relationship.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions on each page. For each question, provide a detailed and honest answer. If you are unsure about a question or answer, you can always consult with a partner or a trusted friend.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

Here is an explanation of each field in the form:

	+ Label: introduction
	+ Type: text
	+ Description: Briefly introduce yourself and your relationship.
* **Relationship Status** (page 2):
