# Member Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Member Interest Survey is a form used to collect information about your interests as a member. It helps us understand what services or activities we should offer or plan to make your experience more valuable and enjoyable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form, which includes questions about your preferences, interests, and availability.
2. For each question, select the options that best describe your situation or preferences.
3. Provide any additional information in the "Notes" section if you have any specific comments or questions.
4. Finally, click "Submit" to send your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title to identify your survey completion.
* **Page One** (`page_one`, text, required/optional): Please provide any comments or information that might be helpful in understanding your situation.
* **Member Preferences** (`member_preferences`, text, required/optional): Share your preferences regarding any services or activities we can offer.
* **Events** (`what_events_are_you_interested_in`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" for the events that interest you.
* **Programs** (`what_programs_are_you_interested_in`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" for the programs that interest you.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or questions you might have.
* **Services** (`what_services_are_you_interested_in`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" for the services that interest you.
* **Trends** (`trends`, text, required/optional): Please provide any comments or information about trends you're interested in.
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, required/optional): Share your preferred method of communication.
* **Events** (`events`, date, required/optional): Select a date for an event you might be interested in attending.
* **Programs** (`program`, time, required/optional): Choose a time for a program you'd like to attend.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click this button to send your completed form.
