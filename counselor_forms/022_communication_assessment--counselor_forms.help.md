# Communication Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Communication Assessment form is used to evaluate a client's ability to communicate effectively. This form is typically completed by a trained assessor, who will provide their professional credentials and assess the client's communication skills.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Assessment Information** (`assessor_name`, `assessor_credentials`, `assessment_date`, `assessment_reason`): Provide details about the assessment, including the assessor's name and credentials, the assessment date, and the primary reason for the assessment.
2. **Client Information** (`client_name`, `client_age`, `client_id`, `referral_source`): Enter personal details about the client, such as their name, age, and identification number.
3. **Assessment Reason** (`assessment_reason`): Select the primary reason for this assessment from the provided options.
4. **Receptive Language** (`receptive_language`): Note any comments or observations about the client's understanding of language.
5. **Following Directions** (`following_directions`): Evaluate the client's ability to follow verbal directions.
6. **Comprehension Level** (`comprehension_level`): Assess the client's overall language comprehension using the provided options.
7. **Expressive Language** (`expressive_language`): Note any comments or observations about the client's language production.
8. **Vocabulary Use** (`vocabulary_use`): Evaluate the client's vocabulary using the provided options.
9. **Sentence Structure** (`sentence_structure`): Assess the client's ability to form complete sentences.
10. **Articulation** (`articulation`): Evaluate the clarity of the client's speech sounds.
11. **Social Communication** (`social_communication`): Note any comments or observations about the client's social interaction skills.
12. **Eye Contact** (`eye_contact`): Evaluate the client's quality of eye contact during interaction.
13. **Turn Taking** (`turn_taking`): Assess the client's ability to take turns in conversation.
14. **Topic Maintenance** (`topic_maintenance`): Evaluate the client's ability to stay on topic.
15. **Pragmatic Skills** (`pragmatic_skills`): Assess the client's use of language in social contexts.
16. **Fluency** (`fluency`): Note any comments or observations about the client's speech fluency.
17. **Stuttering Presence** (`stuttering_presence`): Evaluate the client's stuttering presence.
18. **Overall Impression** (`overall_impression`): Provide a summary of the assessment.
19. **Recommendations** (`recommendations`): Suggest next steps or interventions based on the assessment.
20. **Follow-Up Needed** (`follow_up_needed`): Indicate whether follow-up assessment or therapy is needed.
21. **Client Name**: Enter the client's name.
22. **Client Age**: Enter the client's age.
23. **Client ID**: Enter the client's identification number.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure or lack information about a particular field, please select "Unable to Assess" and provide a brief explanation.
* Please provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers where possible.
* This form is designed to be completed by a trained assessor. If you are not a trained assessor, please do not complete this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact your supervisor or a trained assessor for guidance.
