# Love Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Love Survey form is designed to gather information about your preferences and needs when it comes to matters of the heart. It's meant to be completed once, providing a snapshot of your current situation and goals in the context of love and relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Love Survey form, follow these steps:

* Fill out each section as completely and honestly as possible.
* Make sure to select all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
* For fields that have options, choose the one that best represents your current situation.
* If a field is not applicable to you, simply leave it blank.
* Once you've completed the form, click submit to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you like** (`love_survey_form`, text, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of what you're looking for in a love interest or relationship.
* **When do you want to start** (`love_survey_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date when you're interested in starting a relationship or meeting someone.
* **At what time** (`love_survey_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time of day when you're most available to meet or start a relationship.
* **Love Counselor** (`love_survey_counselor`, select_one, required): Select the love counselor that you're most interested in seeking advice from.
* **Where do you want to go** (`love_survey_location`, text, required/optional): Provide the name of a location or setting where you'd like to go on dates or meet.
* **Additional note** (`love_survey_note`, note, required/optional): Write a brief note or comment about anything else you'd like to add or mention.
* **Email** (`love_survey_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address (if you're comfortable sharing it).
* **Phone** (`love_survey_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number (if you're comfortable sharing it).
* **Required fields must be filled** (`love_survey_required`, select_multiple, required): Select all fields that are required for you (Love Counselor, Additional note, Email, and Phone).
* **Do you have any Emojis?** (`love_survey_emojis`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose whether you're interested in someone with or without Emojis. 

Note: This form is designed to help you identify the best match for your relationship goals, and your input will help us better understand your needs and preferences.
