# Receipt Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Receipt Claim Form is used to claim business expenses for reimbursement. This form is designed to be completed by the employee who has incurred the expense, and it will help to process the reimbursement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with all required and optional fields as applicable to your situation.
2. Ensure you are filling out the form accurately and truthfully.
3. Make sure to attach any required supporting documents, such as receipts.
4. Submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Expenses** (id: 2): Select all the business expenses you are claiming for reimbursement from the list of options provided.
* **Date Submitted** (id: 3): Enter the date you submitted the claim.
* **Submitted By** (id: 4): Select the person who submitted the claim.
* **Receipt Image** (id: 5): Attach a clear image of the receipt.
* **Total Amount** (id: 6): Enter the total amount of the claim.
* **Reimbursed Amount** (id: 7): Enter the amount that has already been reimbursed.
* **Remaining Amount** (id: 8): Enter the remaining amount to be reimbursed.
* **Notes** (id: 9): Provide any additional information or comments about the claim.
* **Submit Date** (id: 10): Enter the date the claim was submitted.
* **Status** (id: 11): Select the current status of the claim.
* **Assigned To** (id: 12): Select the person assigned to process the claim.
* **Email** (id: 13): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (id: 14): Enter your phone number.
* **Account Number** (id: 15): Enter your account number.
* **Claim Date** (id: 16): Enter the date the expense was incurred.
* **Receipt Description** (id: 17): Provide a brief description of the receipt.
* **Claimant Name** (id: 18): Enter your name.
* **Claimant Email** (id: 19): Enter your email address.
* **Claimant Phone** (id: 20): Enter your phone number.
* **Claimant Address** (id: 21): Enter your address.
* **Claimant Status** (id: 22): Select your current status.
* **Claimant Type** (id: 23): Select your claimant type.
* **Assigned To** (id: 24): Select the person assigned to process the claim.
* **Notes** (id: 25): Provide any additional information or comments about the claim.
