# Financial Audit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a Financial Audit Checklist designed to collect relevant information for auditing a company's financial activities. It ensures that all necessary details are gathered to facilitate an efficient and thorough audit process. Please use this form to input accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a successful audit.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Financial Audit Checklist, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure all required fields are filled out before submission.
2. Fill out the form with the company information in the **Company Info** section.
3. Input the start and end dates of the audit in the **Audit Dates** section.
4. Record the total hours worked and total cost of the audit in the **Work Hours and Cost** section.
5. Select the audit manager responsible for the audit in the **Audit Manager** section.
6. Check the financial statements and accounting systems used during the audit in the **Financial Statements** and **Accounting Systems** sections.
7. Provide the current financial audit status in the **Financial Audit Status** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Info** (`company_info`, text, optional): Enter the company's name and identification number to provide context for the audit.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Provide the contact person's name and title to ensure communication with the audited company.
* **Date Start Audit** (`date_start_audit`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the audit to track the beginning of the audit process.
* **Date End Audit** (`date_end_audit`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the audit to track the completion of the audit process.
* **Total Hours Worked** (`total_hours_worked`, number, optional): Record the total hours worked during the audit to track time spent.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, optional): Record the total cost of the audit to track expenses.
* **Audit Manager** (`audit_manager`, select_one, optional): Select the audit manager responsible for the audit to track ownership and accountability.
* **Financial Statements** (`financial_statements`, select_multiple, optional): Check the financial statements reviewed during the audit to identify areas of focus.
* **Accounting Systems** (`accounting_systems`, select_multiple, optional): Check the accounting systems used during the audit to identify areas of focus.
* **Financial Audit Status** (`financial_audit_status`, select_one, optional): Provide the current financial audit status to track progress and completion.

## Tips

- Double-check the accuracy of all inputted data to ensure a successful audit process.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submission.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the relevant sections in the **Field-by-Field Explanation** for clarification.
- If you need additional assistance, please contact your audit manager or contact person.
