# Financial Statement Access Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Statement Access Registration Form is designed to collect information about account holders and their financial institutions to determine access rights to their financial statements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your account information, including account holder's name and contact details.
2. Provide details about the financial statement you are trying to access, including the type of statement (e.g., financial statements, bank statements, tax returns).
3. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
4. Select the financial institution and branch you are associated with.
5. Specify the account type and status.
6. Optional: Add any additional comments or notes about the registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **account_information** (Account Information, required: false): Enter the account holder's name, as well as any other relevant contact details.
*account_information* (`account_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter the account holder's address.
*contact_information* (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and contact information.
*statement_details* (`statement_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter details about the financial statement you are trying to access.
*date_of_access* (`date_of_access`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you are trying to access the financial statement.
*access_type* (`access_type`, required: false): Select the type of financial statement you are trying to access (e.g., Financial Statements, Bank Statements, Tax Returns).
*email* (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
*phone* (`phone`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
*financial_institution* (`financial_institution`, required: false): Select the type of financial institution (e.g., Bank, Credit Union).
*branch_name* (`branch_name`, required: false): Select the branch name of the financial institution.
*account_holder_name* (`account_holder_name`, required: false): Enter the account holder's name.
*account_number* (`account_number`, required: false): Enter the account number.
*account_type* (`account_type`, required: false): Select the type of account (e.g., Checking, Savings, Credit).
*account_status* (`account_status`, required: false): Select the status of the account (e.g., Active, Inactive).

## Tips

* Ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date to avoid any issues with access rights.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
* Please note that this form is only intended to verify access rights to financial statements and will not be used for any other purpose.
