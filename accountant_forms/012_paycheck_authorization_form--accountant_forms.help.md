# Paycheck authorization form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to authorize the payment of a paycheck for an employee. It is meant to be filled out by authorized employees to verify their details before processing the payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your Employee Name.
2.  Select the Pickup Date of your paycheck.
3.  Enter your Pickup Location (e.g. office, bank).
4.  Enter your Employee Employees Number.
5.  Add your Authorized Signature to confirm your identity and request the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, required): Select the date when you would like to receive your paycheck.
- **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, text, required): Enter the location where you would like to receive your paycheck (e.g office, bank).
- **Employee Employees Number** (`employee_employees_number`, text, required): Enter your employee number or ID as assigned by your company.
- **Authorized** (`authorized_signature`, text, required): Add your signature to confirm your identity and request the payment.
