# travel expense reimbursement form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit travel expense reimbursement requests for work-related travel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your company information.
2. Enter the date of your trip.
3. Enter your ID number.
4. Select the type(s) of expenses you are claiming for reimbursement.
5. Enter the amount of each expense.
6. Choose the currency of the expense.
7. Enter a brief description of the purpose of your trip.
8. Add any additional notes about your trip.
9. Enter your email and phone number.
10. Enter the date and time of travel.
11. Select the submit option to send your request for approval.
12. If applicable, select "Yes" to indicate the request was submitted to someone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company** (`Company`, Text, optional): Enter your company information.
* **Date** (`Date`, Date, optional): Enter the date of your trip.
* **Traveler ID** (`Traveler ID`, Number, optional): Enter your ID number.
* **Expense Type** (`Expense Type`, Select Multiple, optional): Select the type(s) of expenses you are claiming for reimbursement.
* **Amount** (`Amount`, Number, optional): Enter the amount of each expense.
* **Currency** (`Currency`, Select One, optional): Choose the currency of the expense (e.g., USD, CAD, EUR).
* **Purpose** (`Purpose`, Text, optional): Enter a brief description of the purpose of your trip.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, Note, optional): Add any additional notes about your trip.
* **Email** (`Email`, Email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, Text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Travel Date** (`Travel Date`, Date, optional): Enter the date of travel.
* **Travel Time** (`Travel Time`, Time, optional): Enter the time of travel.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, Select One, optional): Select "Submit" to send your request for approval.
* **Submitted** (`Submitted`, Text, optional): Indicate if this request was submitted to someone (if applicable).
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, Select One, optional): Indicate if this request was submitted by someone else.
* **Submitted Date** (`Submitted Date`, Date, optional): Enter the date this request was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`Submitted Time`, Time, optional): Enter the time this request was submitted.
* **Status** (`Status`, Select One, optional): Select the status of your request (e.g., pending, approved, rejected).
* **Submitter** (`Submitter`, Select One, optional): Indicate if you are the submitter of this request.
* **Submitted To** (`Submitted To`, Select One, optional): Indicate if this request was submitted to someone else.
* **Travel Agency** (`Travel Agency`, Text, optional): Enter the name of the travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Phone** (`Travel Agency Phone`, Text, optional): Enter the phone number of the travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Email** (`Travel Agency Email`, Email, optional): Enter the email of the travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Fax** (`Travel Agency Fax`, Text, optional): Enter the fax number of the travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Address** (`Travel Agency Address`, Text, optional): Enter the address of the travel agency.

## Tips
Please ensure all fields with an asterisk (*) are completed accurately to ensure smooth processing of your request.
