# Accounting Data Management Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your company's accounting data management needs. It will help us understand your current practices and requirements, allowing us to provide you with tailored services and support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your company's name and contact details.
2. Select your primary contact person and their email address.
3. Enter your company's phone number and business address.
4. If applicable, provide your tax ID number and the month and day of your fiscal year end.
5. Choose your primary accounting software in use.
6. Select your preferred data collection frequency.
7. Check the services you require from us.
8. Finally, provide an estimated range of your annual revenue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Company Name** (`client_company_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your company as registered on your tax returns and business documents.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact_name`, text, required): The full name of the person we can contact regarding your accounting needs.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): The email address you would like us to use for correspondence.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Your primary business phone number.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Your complete business address including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Tax ID Number** (`tax_id_number`, text, optional): Your federal tax identification number (leave blank if not applicable).
* **Fiscal Year End** (`fiscal_year_end`, date, optional): The month and day of your fiscal year end (e.g., 12/31).
* **Primary Accounting Software Used** (`accounting_software_used`, select_one, optional): The main accounting software used in your business (e.g., QuickBooks Desktop, QuickBooks Online, Xero, etc.).
* **Preferred Data Collection Frequency** (`data_collection_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you would like us to collect financial data (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Services Needed** (`services_needed`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the services you require from us (e.g., Bookkeeping, Payroll Processing, Tax Preparation, Financial Reporting, Budget Planning, Audit Support).
* **Estimated Annual Revenue Range** (`estimated_annual_revenue`, select_one, optional): Select the range that best represents your annual revenue.
* **Additional Notes or Special Requirements** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Any special requirements or notes about your accounting needs.
