# Tax Dispute Resolution Services Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients about their tax disputes. It helps accountants understand the issue, gather necessary details, and resolve the dispute efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client's details in the Client Details section.
2. Provide information about the tax dispute in the Tax Dispute Info section.
3. Specify whether you have already submitted a tax return in the Tax Return Info section.
4. If you have already submitted a tax return, upload supporting documents in the Tax Dispute Docs section.
5. Confirm your authorization to represent the client in the Authorization Info section.
6. If you have additional documents that support your claim, upload them in the Additional Docs section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Details
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information in this field. This is the primary point of contact for the accountant handling your tax dispute.

### Tax Dispute Info
* **Tax Dispute Info** (`tax_dispute_info`, note, required): Provide a detailed description of the tax dispute issue you're facing. Be as specific as possible to help the accountant understand the issue.

### Tax Return Info
* **Tax Return Info** (`tax_return_info`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' if you have already submitted a tax return. This will help the accountant understand your tax return status.

### Tax Dispute Docs
* **Tax Dispute Docs** (`tax_dispute_docs`, text, required): Upload any supporting documents related to your tax dispute here. This can include proof of income, receipts, or other relevant documents.

### Authorization Info
* **Authorization Info** (`auth_info`, select_multiple, required): Confirm if you are authorized to represent the client. Choose 'Yes' to confirm authorization.

### Additional Docs
* **Additional Docs** (`additional_documents`, text, required): If you have any additional supporting documents, upload them here. This can include letters, contracts, or other relevant documents.
