# Direct Debit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Direct Debit Form is a financial form used to collect information for direct debit operations. It's used to gather details of the customer's account and bank information, including the direct debit date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in all the required fields with accurate information.
* For optional fields, provide any additional details or comments that are relevant to the direct debit operation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Direct Debit Request** (`direct_debit_form`, text, required/optional): Enter a description of the direct debit request.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Account Holder Name** (`account_holder_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the account holder.
* **Account Holder Email** (`account_holder_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the account holder's email.
* **Account Holder Phone** (`account_holder_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the account holder's phone number.
* **Account Holder Address** (`account_holder_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the account holder's address.
* **Account Holder Postal Code** (`account_holder_postal_code`, text, required/optional): Enter the account holder's postal code.
* **Bank Name** (`bank_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the bank.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the account number.
* **Sort Code** (`sort_code`, text, required/optional): Enter the sort code.
* **Branch Code** (`branch_code`, text, required/optional): Enter the branch code.
* **Debit Amount** (`debit_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount to be debited.
* **Bank Account Number** (`bank_account_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the bank account number.
* **IBAN** (`iban`, text, required/optional): Enter the bank's IBAN (International Bank Account Number).
* **Swift** (`swift`, text, required/optional): Enter the bank's SWIFT code.
* **BIC** (`bic`, text, required/optional): Enter the bank's BIC (Bank Identifier Code).
* **Direct Debit Date** (`direct_debit_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the direct debit date.
* **Direct Debit Time** (`direct_debit_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the direct debit time.
* **Direct Debit Frequency** (`direct_debit_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of the direct debit operation. Options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the direct debit operation.
* **Bank IBAN** (`bank_iban`, text, required/optional): Enter the bank's IBAN.
* **Account IBAN** (`account_iban`, text, required/optional): Enter the account's IBAN.
* **Bank Swift** (`bank_swift`, text, required/optional): Enter the bank's SWIFT code.
* **Account Swift** (`account_swift`, text, required/optional): Enter the account's SWIFT code.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click this button to submit the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields with accurate information.
* Double-check the direct debit date and time to ensure it aligns with your bank's operations.
* Use the notes field to add any additional comments or details about the direct debit operation.
