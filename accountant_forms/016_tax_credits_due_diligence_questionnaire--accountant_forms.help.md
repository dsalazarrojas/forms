# Tax Credits Due Diligence Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Credits Due Diligence Questionnaire is a form used to gather information to assess an entity's eligibility for tax credits. It is typically used by accountants or tax professionals to verify the accuracy of an entity's tax credit claims.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions to the best of your ability. Each section is designed to collect specific information about your entity. Please provide detailed and accurate answers to each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (1) 
* **General Information** (`title`, required: false): This is the title of the form. You can enter any title you like.

### Financials (2) 
* **Financials** (`financials`, required: false): This is a text field to enter any financial information related to your entity.

### Qualifying Activities (3) 
* **Qualifying Activities** (`qualifying_activities`, required: false): This is a text field to enter any qualifying activities related to your entity.

### Supporting Documents (4) 
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, required: false): This is a text field to enter any supporting documents related to your entity.

### Tax Credits (5) 
* **Tax Credits** (`tax_credits`, required: false): This is a text field to enter any tax credits related to your entity.

### Tax Credits Diligence (6) 
* **Tax Credits Diligence** (`tax_credits_diligence`, required: false): This is a text field to enter any tax credits diligence related to your entity.

### Financial Institution (7) 
* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, required: false): This is a text field to enter any financial institution related to your entity.

### Bank Statement (8) 
* **Bank Statement** (`bank_statement`, required: false): This is a text field to enter any bank statement related to your entity.

### Tax ID (9) 
* **Tax ID** (`tax_id`, required: false): This is a text field to enter your tax ID number.

### Business Type (10) 
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, required: false): This is a select-one field to select the type of business (Individual, Partnership, Corporation, Limited Liability Corporation).

### Legal Name (11) 
* **Legal Name** (`legal_name`, required: false): This is a text field to enter your legal name.

### Address (12) 
* **Address** (`address`, required: false): This is a text field to enter your address.

### Business Address (13) 
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, required: false): This is a text field to enter your business address.

### Phone (14) 
* **Phone** (`phone`, required: false): This is a text field to enter your phone number.

### Fax (15) 
* **Fax** (`fax`, required: false): This is a text field to enter your fax number.

### Email (16) 
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): This is an email field to enter your email address.

### Website (17) 
* **Website** (`website`, required: false): This is a text field to enter your website.

### Industry (18) 
* **Industry** (`industry`, required: false): This is a text field to enter your industry.

### Number of Employees (19) 
* **Number of Employees** (`number_employees`, required: false): This is a text field to enter the number of employees in your entity.

### Annual Revenue (20) 
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, required: false): This is a number field to enter your annual revenue.

### Year Established (21) 
* **Year Established** (`year_established`, required: false): This is a number field to enter the year your entity was established.

### Tax Credits Claimed (22) 
* **Tax Credits Claimed** (`tax_credits_claimed`, required: false): This is a number field to enter the tax credits you have claimed.

### Tax Credits Due (23) 
* **Tax Credits Due** (`tax_credits_due`, required: false): This is a number field to enter the tax credits due.

### Tax Credit Percentage (24) 
* **Tax Credit Percentage** (`tax_credit_percentage`, required: false): This is a number field to enter the tax credit percentage.

### Notes (25) 
* **Notes** (`notes`, required: false): This is a note field for any additional comments.

## Tips
* Please provide detailed and accurate answers to each question.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting.
