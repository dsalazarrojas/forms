# Invoice And Earnings Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to calculate the earnings of an individual or company based on an invoice and tax rate. It collects basic information about the invoice and client, and then calculates the gross income and earnings after tax.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the basic information about the invoice and client on **Page 1**.
2. Move on to **Page 2** and select the options for **Tax Calculator** and **Earnings Calculator**.
3. Enter the tax rate percentage and earnings percentage as applicable.
4. Calculate the **Net Gross Income Amount** using the calculated **Net Gross Income** and **Earnings Percentage**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1**
* **Invoice-Section** (id: 2): A text field for entering the details about the invoice.
* **Invoice Date** (id: 3): A date field for entering the invoice date.
* **Client Name** (id: 4): A text field for entering the client's name.
* **Client Email** (id: 5): An email field for entering the client's email.
* **Items** (id: 6): A select multiple field for selecting whether the invoice includes items or not.
* **Item Name** (id: 7): A text field for entering the item's name.
* **Item Price** (id: 8): A number field for entering the item's price.
* **Item Quantity** (id: 9): A number field for entering the item's quantity.
* **Total** (id: 10): A number field for entering the total amount.
* **Sub Total** (id: 11): A number field for entering the sub-total.
* **Tax Rate** (id: 12): A number field for entering the tax rate.
* **Gross Income** (id: 13): A number field for entering the gross income.
* **Gross Income** (id: 14): A number field for entering the gross income.
* **Net Gross Income** (id: 15): A number field for entering the net gross income.
* **Page Two** (id: 16): A text field for entering additional information on page 2.
* **Tax Calculator** (id: 17): A select one field for selecting whether to calculate tax or not.
* **Tax Calculator Options** (id: 18): A select one field for selecting tax calculator options.
* **Tax Percentage** (id: 19): A number field for entering the tax percentage.
* **Earnings Calculator** (id: 20): A select one field for selecting whether to calculate earnings or not.
* **Earnings Calculator Options** (id: 21): A select one field for selecting earnings calculator options.
* **Tax Rate Percentage** (id: 22): A number field for entering the tax rate percentage.
* **Net Gross Income Amount** (id: 23): A number field for entering the net gross income amount.
* **Earnings Percentage** (id: 24): A number field for entering the earnings percentage.
* **Net Gross Income** (id: 25): A number field for entering the net gross income.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the fields on **Page 1** before moving on to **Page 2**.
* Select the correct options for **Tax Calculator** and **Earnings Calculator** on **Page 2**.
* Enter the correct tax percentage and earnings percentage in the respective fields.
* Calculate the **Net Gross Income Amount** using the calculated **Net Gross Income** and **Earnings Percentage**.
