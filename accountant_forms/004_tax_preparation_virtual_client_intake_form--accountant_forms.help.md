# Tax Preparation Virtual Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from virtual clients who need tax preparation services. Please fill it out accurately and completely to ensure that we can provide you with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form with your information and details.
2. Make sure to double-check your input for accuracy.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number for us to reach you.
* **Email** (4, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Address** (6, text, required: false): Enter your address where we can send any necessary documents or information.

## Tips
* Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to use the correct format for date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
