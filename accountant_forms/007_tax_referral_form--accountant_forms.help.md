# Tax Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Referral Form is used to collect information about clients who are being referred to a tax firm.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter the client's last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter the client's email in the **Email** field.
4. Enter the referrer's name in the **Referral Name** field.
5. Enter the referrer's email in the **Referral Email** field.
6. Enter the referrer's phone number in the **Referral Phone** field.
7. Enter the referrer's address in the **Referral Address** field.
8. Select how you met this client from the **How did you meet this client?** dropdown menu.
9. Enter the date you met this client in the **When did you meet this client?** field.
10. Enter the name of the tax firm being referred to in the **Tax Firm Name** field.
11. Enter the website of the tax firm being referred to (optional).
12. Select the person assigned to this referral from the **Assigned to** dropdown menu.
13. Enter any notes about the referral in the **Notes** field (optional).
14. Select the source of the referral from the **Referral Source** dropdown menu.
15. Select the type of referral from the **Referral Type** dropdown menu.
16. Select the person who submitted this form from the **Submitted by** dropdown menu.
17. Enter the date and time this form was submitted in the **Submitted Date** and **Submitted Time** fields.
18. Enter the IP address of the user who submitted this form in the **Submitted IP** field.
19. Enter the user agent of the user who submitted this form in the **Submitted User Agent** field (optional).
20. Enter the country where this form was submitted from the **Submitted Country** field (optional).
21. Enter the city where this form was submitted from the **Submitted City** field (optional).
22. Enter the state where this form was submitted from the **Submitted State** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter the client's first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter the client's last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Referral Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person who referred this client.
* **Referral Email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the person who referred this client.
* **Referral Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the person who referred this client.
* **Referral Address** (Text, Required): Enter the address of the person who referred this client.
* **How did you meet this client?** (Select Multiple, Required): Select how you met this client from the provided options.
* **When did you meet this client?** (Date, Required): Enter the date you met this client.
* **Tax Firm Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the tax firm being referred to.
* **Tax Firm Website** (Text, Optional): Enter the website of the tax firm being referred to (if available).
* **Tax Firm Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the tax firm being referred to (if available).
* **Tax Firm Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the tax firm being referred to (if available).
* **Assigned to** (Select One, Required): Select the person assigned to this referral from the provided options.
* **Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes about the referral.
* **Referral Source** (Select One, Optional): Select the source of the referral from the provided options.
* **Referral Type** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of referral from the provided options.
* **Submitted by** (Select One, Required): Select the person who submitted this form.
* **Submitted Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time this form was submitted.
* **Submitted IP** (Text, Required): Enter the IP address of the user who submitted this form.
* **Submitted User Agent** (Text, Optional): Enter the user agent of the user who submitted this form (if available).
* **Submitted Country** (Text, Optional): Enter the country where this form was submitted from.
* **Submitted City** (Text, Optional): Enter the city where this form was submitted from.
* **Submitted State** (Text, Optional): Enter the state where this form was submitted from.
