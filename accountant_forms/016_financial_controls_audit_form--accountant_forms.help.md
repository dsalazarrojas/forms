# financial_controls_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The financial_controls_audit_form is used to document and track the status of internal financial control processes within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Review Status" field to indicate the current status of the review (Completed, In Progress, or Pending).
2. Choose the "Risk Level" field to select the level of risk associated with the control (High, Medium, or Low).
3. In the "Risk Category" field, select all that apply (Financial Control, Operational, or Compliance).
4. In the "Financial Controls" field, select all that apply (Control of Financial Reporting, Control of Financial Planning and Budgeting, or Control of Accounting and Financial Information System).
5. Enter the "Review Date" and "Follow-Up Date" fields to record the dates of the review and follow-up.
6. In the "Reviewed By" field, enter the name of the person who performed the review.
7. Enter the "Review Comments" and "Follow-Up Comments" fields to record any comments or notes made during the review and follow-up.
8. In the "Follow-Up Planning" field, provide a detailed description of the follow-up plan.
9. Enter the "Review Planning" field to provide a detailed description of the review plan.
10. In the "Review Notes" field, enter any notes or comments made during the review.
11. In the "Follow-Up Review Notes" field, enter any notes or comments made during the follow-up review.
12. Enter the "Follow-Up Review" field to record the status of the follow-up review.
13. In the "Follow-Up Review Details" field, provide a detailed description of the follow-up review.
