# Profit Distribution Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Profit Distribution Calculator" form is designed to calculate the profit distribution among partners in a business. This form helps you determine the net profit of a business after considering capital share, profit share, tax rate, and other factors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the business name in the "Business Name" field.
2. Indicate if there are partners in the "Partners" field. You can select multiple partners.
3. Fill in the capital share of each partner in the corresponding "Capital Share" field.
4. Fill in the profit share of each partner in the corresponding "Profit Share" field.
5. Enter the tax rate of the business in the "Tax Rate" field.
6. Fill in the total capital, remaining capital, and tax in the "Total Capital," "Remaining Capital," and "Tax" field.
7. Calculate the "Net Profit" by subtracting the tax from the total profit.
8. Fill in the number of partnerships in the "Partnerships" field.
9. Enter the details of each partner in the "Partner 1," "Partner 2," "Partner 3," etc. fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`title`, text, required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Partners** (`form_input_2`, select_multiple, required): Check the boxes for partners involved in the business.
* **Capital Share** (`form_input_3`, number, required): Enter the capital share of each partner.
* **Profit Share** (`form_input_4`, select_one, required): Fill in the profit share of each partner (in percentage).
* **Tax Rate** (`form_input_5`, number, required): Enter the tax rate of the business.
* **Total Capital** (`form_input_6`, number, required): Fill in the total capital of the business.
* **Total Profit** (`form_input_7`, number, required): Calculate the total profit.
* **Remaining Capital** (`form_input_8`, number, required): Calculate the remaining capital.
* **Tax** (`form_input_9`, number, required): Calculate the tax paid.
* **Net Profit** (`form_input_10`, number, required): Calculate the net profit.
* **Partnerships** (`form_input_11`, number, required): Fill in the number of partnerships.
* **Partner 1, 2, 3, etc.** (`form_input_12`, text, required): Enter the details of each partner, e.g., name, capital share, profit share, etc.
* **Tax** (`form_input_24`, number, optional): Enter any additional tax.
* **Total Partners** (`form_input_25`, number, required): Fill in the total number of partners.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* Make sure that the capital share of each partner adds up to 100% (or the total capital).
* Use the correct profit share for each partner to ensure accurate profit distribution.
* Be aware that this form is designed for businesses with multiple partners; if you are a solo entrepreneur, you can skip the partner fields. 
* If you have any doubts or need help, please contact our support team.
