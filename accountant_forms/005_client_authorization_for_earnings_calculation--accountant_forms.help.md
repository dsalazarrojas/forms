# Client Authorization For Earnings Calculation - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to obtain client authorization for earnings calculation. It ensures that clients understand the purpose and scope of the calculation, and confirms their authorization for the accountant to access their personal information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Provide your personal details such as name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
3. Specify the calculation period start and end dates.
4. Choose the purpose of the calculation.
5. Select all income sources to be included in the calculation.
6. Confirm your authorization for the accountant to access your personal information.
7. Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding of the confidentiality agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name**: Your legal first name.
- **Last Name**: Your legal last name.
- **Email Address**: Primary contact email.
- **Phone Number**: Best contact number.
- **Mailing Address**: Complete street address.
- **City**: The city of your mailing address.
- **State**: The state of your mailing address.
- **ZIP Code**: ZIP code of your mailing address.
- **Social Security Number**: For tax identification purposes.
- **Calculation Period Start Date**: Beginning of earnings period.
- **Calculation Period End Date**: End of earnings period.
- **Purpose of Earnings Calculation**: Choose one or multiple purpose for the calculation.
  - **Tax Preparation**: For tax purposes.
  - **Loan Application**: For loan purposes.
  - **Financial Planning**: For financial planning.
  - **Divorce Proceedings**: For divorce purposes.
  - **Insurance Claim**: For insurance purposes.
  - **Business Valuation**: For business valuation.
  - **Other Legal Matter**: Other legal purposes.
  - **Other**: Specify other purpose.
- **Income Sources to Include**: Select all income sources to be included in the calculation.
  - **W-2 Employment Income**: Employment income.
  - **Self-Employment Income**: Self-employment income.
  - **Investment Income**: Investment income.
  - **Rental Income**: Rental income.
  - **Retirement Distributions**: Retirement distributions.
  - **Social Security Benefits**: Social Security benefits.
  - **Alimony or Child Support**: Alimony or child support.
  - **Other**: Specify other income sources.
- **Current Employer Name**: If applicable.
- **Employer Address**: Business address of employer.
- **Pay Frequency**: How often you are paid.
  - **Weekly**: Weekly pay.
  - **Bi-Weekly**: Bi-weekly pay.
  - **Semi-Monthly**: Semi-monthly pay.
  - **Monthly**: Monthly pay.
  - **Quarterly**: Quarterly pay.
  - **Annually**: Annually pay.
  - **Irregular**: Irregular pay.
- **I Authorize the Calculation of My Earnings for the Specified Period**: Confirm your authorization for the accountant to access your personal information.
- **May We Contact Your Employer for Verification?**: If needed for accuracy.
- **I Understand This Information Will Be Kept Confidential**: Understand that this information will be kept confidential.
- **Authorization Date**: Date of signing.
- **Electronic Signature**: Type your full name as signature.
- **Witness Name (Optional)**: If applicable.
- **Assigned Accountant Name**: For internal use.
- **I Understand**: Confirm your understanding of the confidentiality agreement.
