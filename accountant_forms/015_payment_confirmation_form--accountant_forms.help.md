# Payment Confirmation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Payment Confirmation Form is used to confirm and document payments received by the organization. This form is used to ensure accurate recording and processing of payments, and to verify the details with customers or clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, including:
	* The date of payment
	* The amount of payment
	* The method of payment (Online, Phone, or In-Person)
2. Review and verify the information carefully to ensure accuracy and consistency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Confirmation** (`payment_confirmation_form_fields`): This is the main section of the form where you can type in a brief description of the payment.
* **Date of Payment** (`date_of_payment`): Enter the date on which the payment was made.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the amount of the payment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the method by which the payment was made (Online, Phone, or In-Person).
* **Confirmation Message** (`confirmation_message`): Enter any additional information or notes about the payment.

## Tips

* Please enter accurate and complete information to ensure that payments are properly recorded and processed.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask a staff member for assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact our accounting department.
