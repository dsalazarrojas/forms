# Life Insurance Tax Reporting Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this life insurance tax reporting questionnaire is to collect relevant information for accountants to report life insurance tax-related information. Please complete this form carefully and accurately to provide the required details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, go through each section and fill in the required information as instructed on the following page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
* **Client Information**: (`client_info`, `text`, Required)
Provide a brief description of the client and their relationship to you or your organization.

### Insurance Details
* **Insurance Details**: (`insurance_details`, `text`, Required)
Enter a detailed description of the client's life insurance policy, including policy type, amount, and any other relevant information.

### Policy Info
* **Policy Info**: (`policy_info`, `text`, Required)
Enter a detailed description of the policy, including policy number, policy start date, and any other relevant information.

### Tax Info
* **Tax Info**: (`tax_info`, `text`, Required)
Enter a detailed description of the tax information associated with the policy, including tax year, tax type, and any other relevant information.

### Policy Beneficiaries
* **Policy Beneficiaries**: (`policy_beneficiaries`, `text`, Required)
Enter a detailed description of the beneficiaries of the policy, including names, relationship to the client, and any other relevant information.

### Financial Info
* **Financial Info**: (`financial_info`, `text`, Optional)
This field is optional. Enter any relevant financial information related to the policy if it's applicable. If not applicable, you can skip this field.

### Tax Returns
* **Tax Returns**: (`tax_returns`, `text`, Required)
Enter any tax returns associated with the policy.

### Tax Year
* **Tax Year**: (`tax_year`, `number`, Required)
Enter the tax year associated with the policy.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- If unsure about any field, refer to the explanation or consult with the accountants.
- This form is designed for single-page input and does not require multiple pages of input. Complete it carefully and accurately.
