# Child Tax Credit Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Tax Credit application form is designed to collect information required to process and approve the Child Tax Credit, a tax credit provided by the government to support families with dependents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete the Applicant Details section with your primary taxpayer's name and Social Security Number.
2. Provide your contact email address for application status updates.
3. For each qualifying child, enter their name, date of birth, and whether they lived with you for more than half the year.
4. Select the total household adjusted gross income range to determine financial eligibility.
5. If applicable, list any other dependents and their ages.
6. Choose a submission date for your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Primary Taxpayer Name** (`primary_taxpayer_name`): Enter your full legal name as the primary taxpayer.
* **Social Security Number - Last 4 Digits** (`ssn_last4`): Enter the last 4 digits of your Social Security Number for verification purposes.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address for application status updates.
* **Child 1 Name** (`child_1_name`): Enter the name of your first dependent child.
* **Child 1 Date of Birth** (`child_1_dob`): Enter the date of birth of your first dependent child to confirm their age eligibility.
* **Did child 1 live with you for more than half the year?** (`child_1_residency_status`): Select 'True' if your first dependent child lived with you for more than half the year.
* **Child 2 Name** (`child_2_name`): Enter the name of your second dependent child.
* **Child 2 Date of Birth** (`child_2_dob`): Enter the date of birth of your second dependent child to confirm their age eligibility.
* **Did child 2 live with you for more than half the year?** (`child_2_residency_status`): Select 'True' if your second dependent child lived with you for more than half the year.
* **Total Household Adjusted Gross Income Range** (`agi_range`): Select the total household adjusted gross income range to determine financial eligibility.
* **Any other dependents to list?** (`additional_dependents_text`): If you have additional dependents not listed, provide their names and ages.
* **Submission Date** (`application_date`): Choose the date of submission for your application.
