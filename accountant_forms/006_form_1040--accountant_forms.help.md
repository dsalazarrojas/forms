# Form 1040 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Form 1040 is a tax-related form used for reporting income and claiming deductions. It's likely intended for individuals or businesses to provide information to tax authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Form 1040, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand the different sections and fields.
2. Answer the questions and select the options that apply to your situation.
3. Ensure you fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Reporting Income (Page 1)
* **Reporting Income** (`reporting_income`, `number`, required: false): Enter your total income for the reporting period.
* **Claiming Deductions** (`claim_deductions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the deductions you are claiming, such as charitable donations or business expenses.
* **Tax Liability** (`tax_liability`, `number`, required: false): Enter your total tax liability.
* **Payments and Refunds** (`payments_and_refunds`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether you have received payments or refunds.
* **Tax Law Compliance** (`tax_law_compliance`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate your tax law compliance status.
* **Amount Owed** (`amount_owed`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount you owe.
* **Reporting Payments** (`reporting_payments`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether you have made payments.
* **Claiming Credits** (`claiming_credits`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the credits you are claiming.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer to the relevant tax authority's guidelines or consult a tax professional.
