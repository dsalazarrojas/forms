# Business Structure Election Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Structure Election Form is used to document a business's election of a new structure, which can impact its tax liabilities and regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information about your business.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
3. Attach any supporting documentation that may be required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Entity Name**: Enter the official name of your business.
* **Entity Registration Number**: Enter your business's EIN, Tax ID, or registration number.
* **Entity Formation Date**: Enter the date your business was formed.
* **Primary Contact Name**: Enter the full name of the main contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address for the contact person.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number for the contact person.
* **Form Preparer Name**: Enter the name of the accountant or preparer filling out the form (optional).
* **Preparer Firm or Company**: Enter the name of the accounting firm or company (optional).
* **Current Business Structure**: Select the current structure of your business from the options provided.
* **Number of Members or Owners**: Enter the number of owners or members of your business.
* **Names of Members or Owners**: List the names of all members or owners of your business.
* **Ownership Percentages**: List the percentage ownership for each member or owner.
* **Reason for Structure Election**: Explain why you are making this election.
* **Tax Year for This Election**: Enter the tax year this election applies to.
* **Effective Date of Election**: Enter the date this election is to take effect.
* **Other Current Structure**: If your business does not fit into one of the listed categories, provide a brief explanation.
* **Other Elected Structure**: If you elect a structure that is not listed, provide a brief explanation.
* **Supporting Documentation**: List any supporting documentation attached to the form.
* **Form Election Date**: Enter the date this election form is submitted.
* **Entity Formation Date**: Enter the date your business was formed.
* **Effective Date of Election**: Enter the date this election is to take effect.
* **Other Reason for Election**: If other than the listed categories, provide a brief explanation.

## Tips

* Make sure to attach any supporting documentation required for the form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
