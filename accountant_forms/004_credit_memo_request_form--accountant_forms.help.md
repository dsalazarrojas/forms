**Credit Memo Request Form - Help Guide**
====================================

## Purpose
The Credit Memo Request Form is used to record and manage credit memos for specific accounts. 
This form helps the user to enter details about a credit memo including the account number, 
account owner, date range, reason, total amount, refund amount, amount of credit to be applied, 
credit applied options and any supporting documentation. 

This form is typically used by accounting and administrative staff to process and track credit memos. 
It is essential to ensure accuracy and consistency in filling out this form to ensure proper 
processing and tracking of credit memos.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the account number of the account for which the credit memo is being requested.
2. Enter the name of the account owner.
3. Select the date range for which the credit memo is applicable.
4. Enter the reason for the credit memo.
5. Enter the total amount of the credit memo.
6. Enter the amount to be refunded.
7. Select the options for credit applied.
8. Enter any supporting documentation.
9. Enter the amount of credit to be applied.
10. Select the option to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_input** (text, optional): This field is used to enter any additional information about the credit memo.
* **account_number** (text, optional): Enter the account number for which the credit memo is being requested.
* **account_owner** (text, optional): Enter the name of the account owner.
* **date_range** (time, optional): Select the date range for which the credit memo is applicable.
* **reason** (text, optional): Enter the reason for the credit memo.
* **supporting_document** (text, optional): Enter any supporting documentation.
* **total_amount** (number, optional): Enter the total amount of the credit memo.
* **amount_refund** (number, optional): Enter the amount to be refunded.
* **amount_credit** (number, optional): Enter the amount of credit to be applied.
* **credit_applied** (select multiple, optional): Select the options for credit applied.
* **notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the credit memo.
* **submit** (select one, optional): Select the option to submit the form.
