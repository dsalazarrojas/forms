# Expense Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow employees to submit expense reports for reimbursement. The form captures necessary information such as company, category, description, and cost associated with each expense. It also includes sections for uploaded files, email, phone number, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to initiate the form.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as necessary.
3. Select the correct category for each expense from the drop-down menu.
4. Enter a brief description of the expense in the description field.
5. Enter the cost of the expense in the cost field.
6. Select a date for the expense from the calendar.
7. If applicable, upload files related to the expense.
8. Enter your email address and phone number.
9. Add any additional notes regarding the expense.
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company (Company)** (`company_1`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Category (Category)** (`category_1`, select_one, optional): Choose the category for this expense from the list (e.g., Travel, Meeting, etc.).
* **Description (Description)** (`description_1`, text, optional): Briefly describe the expense or activity.
* **Cost (Cost)** (`cost_1`, number, optional): Enter the cost associated with this expense.
* **Cost 2 (Cost 2)** (`cost_2`, number, optional): Enter any additional cost associated with this expense.
* **Date (Date)** (`date_1`, date, optional): Select the date of the expense from the calendar.
* **Time (Time)** (`time_1`, time, optional): Select the time of the expense from the calendar.
* **Uploaded Files (Uploaded Files)** (`uploaded_files_1`, text, optional): Upload any relevant documents or files related to this expense.
* **Uploaded Files 2 (Uploaded Files 2)** (`uploaded_files_2`, text, optional): Upload any additional files related to this expense.
* **Email (Email)** (`email_1`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`phone_1`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Note (Note)** (`note_1`, note, optional): Add any additional notes regarding this expense.
