# Educational Institution Financial Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Educational Institution Financial Audit Form" is a tool used to document and report on the financial status of educational institutions. This form helps auditors and institutions to accurately and thoroughly record their financial transactions, identify areas of improvement, and maintain transparency in their financial dealings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the institution being audited in the **institution_name** field.
2. Enter the name of the auditor performing the audit in the **auditor_name** field.
3. Enter the date of the audit in the **audit_date** field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
4. Enter the address of the institution in the **institution_address** field.
5. Select the type of institution being audited from the **institution_type** select list.
6. Enter the location of the institution's campus in the **campus_location** field.
7. Enter the phone number of the institution in the **institution_phone** field.
8. Enter the email address of the institution in the **institution_email** field.
9. Enter the website of the institution in the **institution_website** field.
10. Enter the phone number of the auditor in the **auditor_phone** field.
11. Enter the email address of the auditor in the **auditor_email** field.
12. Enter any notes or comments from the auditor in the **auditor_notes** field.
13. Enter any notes or comments from the institution in the **institution_notes** field.
14. Enter the goals of the audit in the **auditor_goals** field.
15. Enter any comments or observations from the auditor in the **auditor_comments** field.
16. Enter any comments or observations from the institution in the **institution_comments** field.
17. Enter the signature of the auditor in the **auditor_signature** field.
18. Enter the signature of the institution in the **institution_signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **institution_name**: Enter the name of the institution being audited.
* **auditor_name**: Enter the name of the auditor performing the audit.
* **audit_date**: Enter the date of the audit in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **institution_address**: Enter the address of the institution.
* **auditor_title**: Enter the title or position of the auditor.
* **audit_results**: Enter any notes or comments from the audit, such as results or observations.
* **supporting_documentation**: Enter any supporting documentation or evidence related to the audit.
* **reporting_frequency**: Enter the frequency of reporting, such as monthly, quarterly, etc.
* **institution_type**: Select the type of institution being audited, such as High School, College, or University.
* **campus_location**: Enter the location of the institution's campus.
* **institution_phone**: Enter the phone number of the institution.
* **institution_email**: Enter the email address of the institution.
* **institution_website**: Enter the website of the institution.
* **auditor_phone**: Enter the phone number of the auditor.
* **auditor_email**: Enter the email address of the auditor.
* **auditor_notes**: Enter any notes or comments from the auditor.
* **institution_notes**: Enter any notes or comments from the institution.
* **auditor_goals**: Enter the goals of the audit, such as identifying areas of improvement.
* **auditor_comments**: Enter any comments or observations from the auditor.
* **institution_comments**: Enter any comments or observations from the institution.
* **auditor_signature**: Enter the signature of the auditor.
* **institution_signature**: Enter the signature of the institution.
* **auditor_title**: Enter the title or position of the auditor.
* **institution_address**: This field is a duplicate of **institution_address** and should not be filled in.
* **institution_frequency**: Enter the frequency of reporting, such as monthly, quarterly, etc. This field is not necessary as it is not a required field and is likely a duplicate of **reporting_frequency**.
