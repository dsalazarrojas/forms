# Tax Reconciliation Disclosure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Reconciliation Disclosure Consent Form is designed to collect information from clients and accountants for tax reconciliation and disclosure purposes. This form is used to gather personal details, tax-related information, and accountants' details to facilitate the tax reconciliation process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form data section with your name and any relevant information.
2. Enter your client's name, address, phone number, and email (if applicable).
3. Provide the tax year and any other tax-related information as required.
4. If applicable, sign the form to acknowledge your agreement and provide any comments or notes.
5. Enter your accountant's name, phone number, email, and address (if applicable).
6. Sign the form to acknowledge your agreement and provide any comments or notes.
7. Select the accountants' agreement status as either Active or Inactive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Data
* **Form Data**: A section for entering any additional information about the form.
### Client Information
* **Client Name**: Enter your client's name.
* **Client Address**: Enter your client's address.
* **Email**: Enter your client's email (if applicable).
* **Phone**: Enter your client's phone number.
* **Tax Return**: Enter any additional tax return information.
### Tax Information
* **Tax Year**: Enter the tax year for which you are providing reconciliation and disclosure information.
### Accountant Information
* **Accountant Name**: Enter your name as the accountant.
* **Accountant Phone**: Enter your phone number as the accountant.
* **Accountant Email**: Enter your email as the accountant.
* **Accountant Address**: Enter your address as the accountant.
* **Client Phone Number**: Enter your client's phone number.
* **Accountant Agreement**: Enter any notes or comments from the accountant.
* **Accountant Agreement Date**: Enter the date of the accountant's agreement.
* **Accountant Agreement Status**: Select Active or Inactive as the account's status.
### Agreement and Signatures
* **Client Signature**: Sign to acknowledge your agreement and provide any comments or notes.
* **Accountant Signature**: Sign to acknowledge your agreement and provide any comments or notes.
* **Form Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the form.
* **Form Submit**: Select Yes or No to submit the form.

Note: Fields marked as required are essential to complete the form, while optional fields are for additional information or clarification.
