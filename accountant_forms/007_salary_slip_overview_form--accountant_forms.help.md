# Salary Slip Overview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Salary Slip Overview Form provides an overview of an employee's salary details, including their pay date, salary rate, department, job title, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's company ID.
2. Select the employee's department from the options provided.
3. Enter the employee's job title.
4. Enter the employee's salary rate.
5. Enter the employee's pay date.
6. Enter the employee's name.
7. Enter the employee's gross salary.
8. Enter the employee's net pay.
9. Enter the employee's tax rate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company ID** (`company_id`, text, not required): Enter the company ID of the employee. This is a unique identifier for the company and helps to identify the employee's company affiliation.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, not required): Select the department that the employee belongs to. The options are:
	+ Sales
	+ Marketing
	+ IT
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required): Enter the job title of the employee.
* **Salary Rate** (`salary_rate`, number, not required): Enter the employee's salary rate. This is the amount of money the employee is paid per hour.
* **Pay Date** (`pay_date`, date, not required): Enter the date the employee was paid. This helps to track payment history.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, not required): Enter the employee's name.
* **Gross Salary** (`gross_salary`, number, not required): Enter the employee's gross salary, which is the total amount of money they were paid before taxes.
* **Net Pay** (`net_pay`, number, not required): Enter the employee's net pay, which is the amount of money they are left with after taxes have been deducted.
* **Tax Rate** (`tax_rate`, number, not required): Enter the tax rate applied to the employee's gross salary.
* **Salary (USD)** (`salary_dollars`, number, not required): Enter the employee's salary in USD.
* **Net Pay** (`net_pay`, number, not required): Enter the employee's net pay.
