# Tax Compliance Confirmation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a tax compliance confirmation from the system, which will be reviewed and updated accordingly.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, fill out the required information in the following sections:
- **Submitter Information**: This section is for providing information about the person submitting the request.
  - **Submitter Information**: A short text field to enter the name and/or description of the submitter.
- **Contact Information**: This section is for providing the contact information of the person who will be responsible for following up on the request.
  - **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person who will be handling the request.
  - **Date Of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the person who will be handling the request.
  - **Address Information**: This section is for providing the address of the person who will be handling the request.
    - **Address Line 1**: Enter the first line of the address.
    - **Address Line 2**: Enter the second line of the address.
    - **Address Line 3**: Enter the third line of the address (if applicable).
    - **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code associated with the address.
- **Tax Status**: This section is for selecting the current tax status.
  - **Tax Status**: Choose the current tax status from the drop-down menu:
    - **Active**
    - **Inactive**
- **Tax Year**: This section is for selecting the tax year.
  - **Tax Year**: Enter the tax year for which the request is related to.
- **Signature**: This section is for adding a digital signature of the submitter.
  - **Signature**: Upload a scanned copy of your signature (not implemented, but can be used as a note).
- **Uploaded Documents**: This section is for uploading any supporting documents.
  - **Uploaded Documents**: Upload any relevant documents supporting the request (not implemented, but can be used as a note).
- **Submitter Id**: This section is for entering the submitter's ID.
  - **Submitter Id**: Enter the ID of the person submitting the request.

Please note that this form is designed to be completed by authorized personnel within the company, and all information entered will be reviewed and verified before updating the tax status.
