# payment_notice_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The payment_notice_form is used to document and communicate payment notices for accounts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Start" to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out the "Landlord Information" page with the relevant details about the account holder.
3. On the "Late Rent Notice" page, provide a clear notice of the late rent payment, including any relevant details.
4. In the "Rent Status" section, select the status of the rent payment (Due, Overdue).
5. Choose the payment method used for the payment (Cash, Check, Bank Transfer, Other).
6. Enter the account number of the payment.
7. Provide the address of the account holder.
8. Enter the contact number of the account holder.
9. Enter the email address of the account holder.
10. If necessary, enter the date and time of the payment.
11. Add any additional signature or notes in the "Signature" and "Notes" sections.
12. In the "Payment Instructions" section, provide any specific instructions or comments about the payment.
13. Enter the account holder's name and the name of their bank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landlord Information** (text, required/optional): Fill out the name and contact information of the account holder.
* **Late Rent Notice** (text, required/optional): Provide a clear and concise notice of the late rent payment.
* **Rent Status** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select the status of the rent payment (Due, Overdue).
* **Payment Method** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the method used for the payment (Cash, Check, Bank Transfer, Other).
* **Account Number** (number, required/optional): Enter the account number of the payment.
* **Address** (text, required/optional): Provide the address of the account holder.
* **Contact Number** (text, required/optional): Enter the contact number of the account holder.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the account holder.
* **Date** (date, required/optional): If necessary, enter the date of the payment.
* **Time** (time, required/optional): If necessary, enter the time of the payment.
* **Signature** (note, required/optional): Provide any additional signature or comments about the payment.
* **Notes** (text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the payment.
* **Payment Instructions** (text, required/optional): Provide any specific instructions or comments about the payment.
* **Account Holder Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the account holder.
* **Bank Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the bank.
* **Branch Number** (text, required/optional): Enter the branch number of the bank.
* **Account Number 2** (text, required/optional): Enter the second account number.
* **Routing Number** (text, required/optional): Enter the routing number of the bank.
