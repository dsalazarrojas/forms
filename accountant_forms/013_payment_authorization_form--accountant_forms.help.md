# payment_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The payment authorization form is used to authorize a payment for accounting and financial purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form purpose and instructions carefully.
2.  Enter the exact amount of the payment in the **payment_amount_label** field.
3.  Select the date the payment is due or has been made in the **payment_date_label** field.
4.  Verify that all fields are complete and accurate before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **payment_amount_label** (`payment_amount_label`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the exact amount of the payment, including any applicable taxes or fees.
*   **payment_date_label** (`payment_date_label`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date the payment is due or has been made.

## Tips
*   Ensure that the payment authorization form is not completed for payments that do not need authorization or have already been authorized.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and consistency.
*   If you are unsure about a payment's authorization status, contact the accounting department for clarification.
