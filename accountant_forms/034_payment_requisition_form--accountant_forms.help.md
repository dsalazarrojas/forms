# Payment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for payment requests, providing a clear and concise way to gather the necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by providing the following information:
2. Click the "Submit" button to send the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Request** (`payment_request`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the payment request.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the payment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method of payment (e.g., Bank Transfer, Check, Wire Transfer).
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, optional): Enter the due date for the payment.
* **Paid To** (`paid_to`, text, optional): Enter the name or identifier of the person or entity receiving the payment.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or details about the payment request.

## Tips

* Double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed if they are marked as required.
* If you are unsure about any field or the process, feel free to contact the relevant department for assistance.
