> # Government Financial Records Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Financial Records Audit Form is designed to collect information from auditors and agency staff to facilitate a thorough review of government financial records. This form is intended to be used in conjunction with the relevant government agency's internal audit guidelines and procedures.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide agency information, including the agency name, address, and contact details.
2.  Complete the auditor information section, including your name, badge number, and role within the agency.
3.  Describe the audit finding, including the specific financial record(s) being audited and any notable findings.
4.  Provide a detailed explanation of the evaluation, including your analysis and recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Agency Information (id: 1)** (`Agency Information`, text, required: false): Enter your agency's name, address, and contact details, including the phone number and email address.
*   **Auditor Information (id: 2)** (`Auditor Information`, text, required: false): Enter your name, badge number, and role within the agency.
*   **Audit Finding (id: 3)** (`Audit Finding`, text, required: false): Describe the specific financial record(s) being audited and any notable findings.
*   **Financial Record (id: 4)** (`Financial Record`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the financial record being audited.
*   **Evaluation (id: 5)** (`Evaluation`, text, required: false): Enter your analysis and recommendations for the financial record audit.

## Tips
*   Make sure to review and update your responses to ensure they are accurate and complete.
*   Use specific examples and details to support your responses.
*   Ensure your responses are consistent with your agency's internal audit guidelines and procedures.
