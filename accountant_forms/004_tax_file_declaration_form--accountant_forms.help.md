# Tax File Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect tax-related information from individuals or businesses for tax declaration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **business_info** page with the required information about your business or identity.
2. Provide your **contact_details** on the next page, including any relevant contact information.
3. Move on to the **tax_info** page and input any tax-related details required.
4. Finally, select a **date** for the tax declaration, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business Information (Page 1)
* **business_info** (Text, Optional): Enter the name and address of your business or personal identity. Please ensure the information is accurate and up-to-date.

### Contact Details (Page 2)
* **contact_details** (Text, Optional): Add your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address. This helps us to reach out to you with any updates or inquiries.

### Tax Information (Page 3)
* **tax_info** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant tax-related details, such as tax ID numbers or tax filing status. Be as specific as possible to ensure accurate tax declaration.

### Date (Page 4)
* **date** (Date, Optional): Select a date for the tax declaration, if applicable. This is an optional field and can be left blank if you are unsure.

## Tips
* Please double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, seek the advice of a tax professional.
* Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and consistently with your tax declaration.
* Save a copy of the form for future reference.
