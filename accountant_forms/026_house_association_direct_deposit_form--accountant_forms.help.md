**House Association Direct Deposit Form - Help Guide**

**Purpose**
--------

This form is used to set up or update direct deposit information for a member of the House Association.

**How To Complete This Form**

1. Fill in the Member First Name and Last Name fields with the member's full name as it appears on their account.
2. Select whether or not the member would like to set up direct deposit ("Yes" or "No").
3. If selecting "Yes", enter the Account Number and Routing Number for the member's bank account. Please enter the Account Number as it appears on the member's check or bank statement.
4. Enter the Direct Deposit Date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
5. Enter the Direct Deposit Amount that the member would like to deposit.
6. Once all fields are filled in, click submit to finalize the direct deposit setup.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **Member First Name** (`member_first_name`, text, optional): Enter the member's first name as it appears on their account.
* **Member Last Name** (`member_last_name`, text, optional): Enter the member's last name as it appears on their account.
* **Direct Deposit Routing** (`direct_deposit_routing`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to set up direct deposit or "No" to decline.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, optional): Enter the member's account number as it appears on their check or bank statement.
* **Account Name** (`account_name`, text, optional): Enter the member's account name as it appears on their bank statement.
* **Routing Number** (`routing_number`, text, optional): Enter the member's routing number as it appears on their check or bank statement.
* **Direct Deposit Date** (`direct_deposit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for direct deposit in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Direct Deposit Amount** (`direct_deposit_amount`, number, optional): Enter the direct deposit amount.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to avoid errors in processing the direct deposit.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the House Association office for assistance.
* Direct deposit will be processed on the selected date and amount.
