# Billing Dispute Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Billing Dispute Form is used to inform the accountant of a disputed charge on your account. This form helps us to quickly and efficiently gather the necessary information to review and resolve the dispute.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter the account number associated with the billing charge being disputed.
3. Enter the invoice number or reference associated with the disputed charge.
4. Select the date shown on the invoice being disputed.
5. Enter the amount being disputed, using decimal places if necessary.
6. Provide a clear explanation of why this charge is being disputed.
7. Choose the preferred resolution for the dispute.
8. Include any supporting details or notes that may help us to review the dispute.
9. Acknowledge that you authorize the review of your account records as part of the dispute process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your account.
* **Email address** (email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates.
* **Phone number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number or mobile number for further contact.
* **Account number** (text, required): Enter the customer account or client ID associated with the billing charge.
* **Invoice number** (text, optional): Enter the invoice number or reference associated with the disputed charge.
* **Billing date** (date, optional): Enter the date shown on the invoice being disputed.
* **Disputed amount** (number, required): Enter the amount being disputed, using decimal places if necessary.
* **Dispute reason** (text, required): Explain why this charge is being disputed.
* **Preferred resolution** (select_one, required): Choose the outcome you are requesting for the dispute (Refund, Account credit, Invoice adjustment, or Explanation of charge).
* **Supporting details** (text, optional): Include any transaction IDs, dates, or other relevant details that may help us to review the dispute.
* **Authorization** (note, required): Acknowledge that you authorize the review of your account records as part of the dispute process.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your dispute is processed correctly.
* Be clear and concise in your explanation of why the charge is being disputed.
* Include any supporting details that may help us to review and resolve the dispute efficiently.
* Acknowledge that you authorize the review of your account records as part of the dispute process.
