# Payment Receipt Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Receipt Upload Form is designed to upload payment information for documentation and tracking purposes. Please ensure you have the necessary permissions to upload payment receipts for your organization before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Upload your payment receipt by typing or pasting the file in the "Upload Payment Receipt" field.
2. Select the date of the receipt using the calendar icon next to the "Receipt Date" field.
3. Select the time of the receipt using the clock icon next to the "Receipt Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Payment Receipt** (upload_payment_receipt, text, optional): Upload your payment receipt by typing or pasting the file in this field. You can upload receipts in various formats, such as PDF, JPEG, or PNG.
* **Receipt Date** (upload_payment_receipt_date, date, optional): Select the date of the receipt using the calendar icon. This is the date when the payment was made.
* **Receipt Time** (upload_payment_receipt_time, time, optional): Select the time of the receipt using the clock icon. This is the time when the payment was made.

## Tips
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions to upload payment receipts for your organization.
* Use the correct format for the uploaded file to ensure it can be read correctly.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form or payment receipt process, please contact your organization's accounting department for further assistance.
