# Client Tax Document Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect tax-related information and documents from clients. Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure accurate tax calculations and processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in all required fields (marked with an asterisk \*).
2. For each field, read the explanation below to understand what is expected of you.
3. Upload relevant documents as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (Required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your tax return.
	+ This field is required to identify you as the client.
- **Tax ID/SSN** (Required): Enter your Tax Identification Number (TIN) or Social Security Number (SSN).
	+ This field is required to verify your identity.
- **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address.
	+ This field is required for communication and account management purposes.
- **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number.
	+ This field is required for communication and account management purposes.
- **Tax Year** (Required): Select the tax year you are reporting for.
	+ This field is required to determine the tax period.
- **Filing Status** (Required): Select your filing status.
	+ Single, Married Filing Jointly, Married Filing Separately, Head of Household, or Qualifying Widow/Widower.
	+ This field is required to determine your tax filing status.
- **Filing Status Options**: If you are Self-Employed, you will have additional fields to complete.
- **Upload W-2 Forms** (Optional): Upload your W-2 forms if applicable.
	+ These forms show your income from employment.
- **Upload 1099 Forms** (Optional): Upload your 1099 forms if you have contractor income.
	+ These forms show income from self-employment or other sources.
- **Interest and Dividend Statements** (Optional): Upload statements showing your interest and dividend income.
	+ This is an optional field for additional income sources.
- **Business Information** (Optional): If you are self-employed, please complete the following fields:
- **Are you Self-Employed?** (Required): Select Yes or No.
	+ This field determines whether you have business income and expenses to report.
- **Business Income and Expense Records** (Optional): Upload records of your business income and expenses.
	+ If you are self-employed, this field is required for business income and expense reporting.
- **Business Schedule C or K-1** (Optional): Upload your business schedule C or K-1 form.
	+ This form shows business income and expenses.
- **Charitable Contributions** (Optional): Upload documentation of your charitable contributions.
	+ This is an optional field for charitable contributions.
- **Mortgage Interest Statement** (Optional): Upload your mortgage interest statement (Form 1098).
	+ This is an optional field for mortgage interest statement.
- **Education Expense Documentation** (Optional): Upload documentation of your education expenses (Form 1098-T).
	+ This is an optional field for education expense documentation.
- **Medical Expense Records** (Optional): Upload records of your medical expenses.
	+ This is an optional field for medical expenses.
- **Property Tax and State Income Tax Paid** (Optional): Upload records of your property tax and state income tax paid.
	+ This is an optional field for property tax and state income tax paid.
- **Other Relevant Tax Documents** (Optional): Upload any other tax documents relevant to your submission.
	+ This is an optional field for uploading other tax documents.

Note: This help guide is a general explanation of the form and its fields. Please review the form and its fields carefully to ensure accurate and complete completion.
