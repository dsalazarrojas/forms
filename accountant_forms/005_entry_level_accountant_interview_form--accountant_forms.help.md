# Entry Level Accountant Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates for entry-level accountant positions. It will help the hiring manager to quickly assess the candidate's background, experience, and availability to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the **First name** field with your full first name.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the corresponding field.
3. Provide your **Email** address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your **Phone** number so we can contact you for an interview.
5. Attach your **Resume** if you have one.
6. Specify your **Job title**.
7. Enter the number of **Years experience** you have in accounting.
8. Describe your **Education** background.
9. Provide information about your **Current salary** and **Expected salary**.
10. Specify your **Available date** and **Available time** for an interview.
11. Select your **Availability** status (e.g., Available, Unavailable, On vacation).
12. Choose the **Notice period** you require for a notice of termination (e.g., 1 week, 2 weeks, 3 weeks).
13. Select the **Available days** you are available to work (e.g., Monday to Friday, Monday to Thursday).
14. List your relevant **Skills**.
15. Describe your **Work experience**.
16. Provide names and contact information for your **References**.
17. Specify the **Job type** you are looking for (e.g., Full-time, Part-time).
18. Enter a brief description of your **Job Role**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your full first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your full last name.
* **Email**: Provide a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Resume**: Attach your resume if you have one. This will help us get a better understanding of your background and experience.
* **Job title**: Specify the job title you are interested in.
* **Years experience**: Enter the number of years you have experience in accounting.
* **Education**: Describe your education background, including degrees and institutions.
* **Current salary**: Enter your current salary.
* **Expected salary**: Enter the salary you expect for this position.
* **Available date**: Enter the date you are available to start work.
* **Available time**: Choose a time slot for an interview.
* **Availability**: Select your availability status:
	+ Available
	+ Unavailable
	+ On vacation
* **Notice period**: Select the notice period you require:
	+ 1 week
	+ 2 weeks
	+ 3 weeks
* **Available days**: Select the days you are available to work:
	+ Monday to Friday
	+ Tuesday to Thursday
* **Skills**: List your relevant skills, such as proficiency in accounting software, languages, etc.
* **Work experience**: Describe your previous work experience, including job titles, companies, and responsibilities.
* **References**: Provide names and contact information for your references.
* **Job type**: Select the type of job you are looking for:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
* **Job Role**: Enter a brief description of your ideal job role.

Please note that some fields are mandatory and must be filled in to proceed with the application.
