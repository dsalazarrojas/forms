# income_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for tracking income payments received by the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the `payment_details` field with the description or notes about the payment.
2. Enter the date of the payment in the `payment_date` field.
3. Input the amount of the payment in the `payment_amount` field.
4. Select the payment method from the `payment_method` field.
5. Choose the reconciled status of the payment from the `reconciled_status` field.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the payment in the `notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **payment_details** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description or notes about the payment.
* **payment_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date the payment was received.
* **payee_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the individual or organization receiving the payment.
* **payment_amount** (Number, Required): Enter the total amount of the payment.
* **payment_method** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the method of payment used, such as Bank Transfer, Check, PayPal, or Cash.
* **reconciled_status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the reconciled status of the payment, such as Pending, Reconciled, Partially Reconciled, or Not Reconciled.
* **notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the payment.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct payment date and amount to ensure accurate tracking and reconciliation.
* Use the payment method field to indicate the specific method used for each payment.
* Choose the reconciled status that best reflects the current state of the payment.
