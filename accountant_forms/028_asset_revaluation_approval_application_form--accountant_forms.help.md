# Asset Revaluation Approval Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit an application for asset revaluation, ensuring that all required information is provided for accurate and timely processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the **Application Date**, which is the date of the asset revaluation application.
2. Enter the **Applicant Name**, **Applicant Title**, and **Department** to identify the applicant and their position.
3. Provide the **Company Name** and **Fiscal Year** to ensure accurate tracking and accounting.
4. Enter the **Asset ID**, **Asset Name**, and **Asset Location** to specify the asset being revalued.
5. For the **Original Acquisition Date**, **Original Cost**, and **Accumulated Depreciation**, fill in the corresponding dates and values for accurate calculation.
6. Enter the **Proposed Fair Value**, which is the new estimated value of the asset.
7. Specify the **Revaluation Reason** and provide a **Detailed Explanation** for why the revaluation is required.
8. Choose the **Valuation Method Used** to indicate the approach taken for the revaluation.
9. If an external **Appraiser** was involved, provide their **Name** and **Qualifications**.
10. Select the **Supporting Documents** attached to the application, such as an appraisal report or market analysis.
11. Assess the **Tax Impact** and **Financial Statement Impact** of the revaluation.
12. Choose the **Accounting Standard** applicable to the revaluation process.
13. Select the **Department Head Approval**, **Finance Director Approval**, and **CFO Approval** status to track the approval process.
14. Finally, provide any **Additional Notes** and propose an **Effective Date** for the revaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date**: The date the asset revaluation application was submitted.
* **Applicant Name**: The name of the individual or department submitting the application.
* **Applicant Title**: The job title of the applicant.
* **Department**: The department associated with the asset.
* **Company Name**: The legal name of the company owning the asset.
* **Fiscal Year**: The fiscal year in which the asset is being revalued.
* **Asset ID**: A unique identifier for the asset.
* **Asset Name**: A brief description of the asset.
* **Asset Location**: The location where the asset is situated.
* **Original Acquisition Date**: The date the asset was initially acquired.
* **Original Cost**: The original purchase price of the asset.
* **Accumulated Depreciation**: The total depreciation accumulated on the asset.
* **Proposed Fair Value**: The new estimated value of the asset.
* **Revaluation Reason**: The reason for the revaluation, such as market value increase or regulatory requirement.
* **Detailed Explanation**: A detailed description of the revaluation reason.
* **Valuation Method Used**: The approach taken to value the asset, such as market approach or cost approach.
* **Appraiser Name**: The name of the individual who performed the valuation, if applicable.
* **Appraiser Qualifications**: The qualifications and credentials of the appraiser, if applicable.
* **Appraisal Date**: The date the asset was valued.
* **Supporting Documents**: Attach relevant documents, such as appraisal reports or market analysis, to support the revaluation application.
* **Tax Impact**: An assessment of the potential tax implications of the revaluation.
* **Financial Statement Impact**: An evaluation of the effect on financial statements.
* **Accounting Standard**: The applicable accounting standard, such as GAAP or IFRS.
* **Department Head Approval**: The status of department head approval.
* **Finance Director Approval**: The status of finance director approval.
* **CFO Approval**: The final approval status.
* **Proposed Effective Date**: The proposed date the revaluation will take effect.
* **Additional Notes**: Any additional comments or information related to the revaluation application.
* **Supporting Documents**: Select the documents attached to support the application.
* **Revaluation Surplus**: The increase in value of the asset due to revaluation.
* **Valuation Method Used**: The method used to value the asset.
