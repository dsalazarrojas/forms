# online_debt_collection_placement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and debt details from users, as well as their preferred payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and fields below to understand what information is requested.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" sections (pages 1 and 2).
3. Enter your debt details in the "Debt Details" sections (pages 2 and 3).
4. Add any comments in the "Comments" sections (pages 3 and 4).
5. Select the contact person from the "Contact Person" dropdown menu.
6. Select the payment terms you agree to from the "Payment Terms" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (page 1): Enter your name, address, email, and phone number to help us contact you regarding your debt.
* **Debt Details** (page 2): Provide a brief description of your debt, including the amount and due date.
* **Comments** (page 3): If you have any additional comments or notes about your debt, enter them here.
* **Contact Information 2** (page 4): Enter your contact information if you are not currently able to contact.
* **Debt Details 2** (page 5): If you have any additional debt details, enter them here.
* **Comments 2** (page 6): If you have any additional comments or notes about your debt, enter them here.
* **Contact Person** (page 7): Select the person you would like to contact regarding your debt. Choose from one of the three options: Bob, John, or Jane.
* **Payment Terms** (page 8): Select the payment terms you agree to. Choose from one or more of the three options: term 1, term 2, or term 3.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate contact information so we can reach out to you regarding your debt.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, you can contact us after completing the form.
