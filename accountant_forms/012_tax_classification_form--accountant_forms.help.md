# Tax Classification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Classification Form is a crucial document used to classify tax statuses, payment methods, and other relevant details for both vendors and contractors. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure proper tax classification and payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Tax Classification Form, follow these steps:

* Provide general information about the taxpayer (page 1 - General Information)
* Enter vendor-specific information (page 2 - Vendor Information)
* Enter contractor-specific information (page 3 - Contractor Information)
* Provide payment information (page 4 - Payment Information)
* Classify tax statuses for vendors and contractors (pages 5-11)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)** (`General Information`, `text`, required: false)
Provide your name and other general contact information.
* **Vendor Information (2)** (`Vendor Information`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your business name and other relevant details.
* **Contractor Information (3)** (`Contractor Information`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your business name and other relevant details.
* **Payment Information (4)** (`Payment Information`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your payment method and other relevant details.
* **Vendor Tax Status (5)** (`Vendor Tax Status`, `select_one`, required: false)
Classify the tax status of your vendor (Active or Inactive).
* **Contractor Tax Status (6)** (`Contractor Tax Status`, `select_one`, required: false)
Classify the tax status of your contractor (Active or Inactive).
* **Vendor EIN (7)** (`Vendor EIN`, `number`, required: false)
Enter your Employer Identification Number (EIN) for your vendor business.
* **Vendor SSN (8)** (`Vendor SSN`, `select_one`, required: false)
Classify whether your vendor business has a Social Security Number (Yes or No).
* **Contractor EIN (9)** (`Contractor EIN`, `number`, required: false)
Enter your EIN for your contractor business.
* **Contractor SSN (10)** (`Contractor SSN`, `select_one`, required: false)
Classify whether your contractor business has a Social Security Number (Yes or No).
* **Vendor Tax Exempt (11)** (`Vendor Tax Exempt`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select all that apply for your vendor business (Yes, No, and other options).
* **Contractor Tax Exempt (12)** (`Contractor Tax Exempt`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select all that apply for your contractor business (Yes, No, and other options).
* **Vendor Tax ID (13)** (`Vendor Tax ID`, `select_one`, required: false)
Classify whether your vendor business has a tax ID (Yes or No).
* **Contractor Tax ID (14)** (`Contractor Tax ID`, `select_one`, required: false)
Classify whether your contractor business has a tax ID (Yes or No).
* **Vendor Tax Status 2 (15)** (`Vendor Tax Status 2`, `select_one`, required: false)
Classify the second tax status of your vendor (Active or Inactive).
* **Contractor Tax Status 2 (16)** (`Contractor Tax Status 2`, `select_one`, required: false)
Classify the second tax status of your contractor (Active or Inactive).
* **Vendor Payment Method (17)** (`Vendor Payment Method`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select all that apply for your vendor payment method (Yes, No, and other options).
* **Contractor Payment Method (18)** (`Contractor Payment Method`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select all that apply for your contractor payment method (Yes, No, and other options).
* **Vendor IRS (19)** (`Vendor IRS`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select all that apply for your vendor IRS (Yes, No, and other options).
* **Contractor IRS (20)** (`Contractor IRS`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select all that apply for your contractor IRS (Yes, No, and other options).
* **Vendor State (21)** (`Vendor State`, `select_one`, required: false)
Classify whether your vendor business is in a state (Yes or No).
* **Contractor State (22)** (`Contractor State`, `select_one`, required: false)
Classify whether your contractor business is in a state (Yes or No).
* **Vendor Zip (23)** (`Vendor Zip`, `number`, required: false)
Enter your vendor's zip code.
* **Contractor Zip (24)** (`Contractor Zip`, `number`, required: false)
Enter your contractor's zip code.
* **Submit (25)** (`Submit`, `text`, required: false)
Submit your form. 

Please note that you can review the form's fields and requirements carefully before proceeding. If you are still unsure, please seek assistance from a qualified tax professional.
