# Tax Return Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Tax Return Questionnaire is a form used to collect information from individuals and businesses to determine their tax return filing status. This form helps the accountant gather necessary details for accurate and efficient tax processing. Please fill out this form only if you are an authorized representative or the individual/business whose tax return is being processed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Choose the correct tax year from the dropdown menu for the tax year you are filing your return for.
2. Select your income source from the dropdown menu (e.g., Salary, Self-Employed, Business).
3. Enter the amount of tax credit you are claiming, if any.
4. Specify if you have any deductions to claim, if so, enter the amount.
5. Choose your tax withholding status (e.g., Withholding, Non-withholding).
6. Enter the total tax amount owed, if applicable.
7. Select your tax return filing status (e.g., Filed, Unfiled, Pending).
8. Enter your Social Security Number.
9. Provide your address and contact information.
10. Indicate that you have submitted your tax return (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, select_one): Select the year you are filing your tax return for.
* **Income Source** (`income_source`, select_one): Choose the source of your income, such as Salary, Self-Employed, or Business.
* **Income Tax Credit** (`income_tax_credit`, number): If you are claiming income tax credit, enter the amount here.
* **Deductions Claim** (`deductions_claim`, number): If you have deductions to claim, enter the amount here.
* **Tax Withholding** (`tax_withholding`, select_one): Select your tax withholding status, either Withholding or Non-withholding.
* **Tax Amount** (`tax_amount`, number): Enter the total tax amount owed, if applicable.
* **Tax Returns Filing Status** (`tax_returns_filing_status`, select_one): Select your tax return filing status, either Filed, Unfiled, or Pending.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, number): Enter your Social Security Number.
* **Address** (`address`, text): Enter your address.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text): Provide any additional contact information, such as phone number or email.
* **Tax Return Submitted** (`tax_return_submitted`, note): Indicate if you have already submitted your tax return.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
