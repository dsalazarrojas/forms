# Royalty Accounting Pre-Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a client or contact's current royalty accounting processes and expectations. This information will help accountants and accounting firms better understand the client's needs and requirements before engaging in a new project or partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page carefully and answer all questions relevant to your current royalty processes and expectations.
2. Select the options that best match your current royalty processes and expectations.
3. Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre Engagement Details** (`pre_engagement_details`, text, required: false): This field is for providing any additional information about the pre-engagement process.
* **Current Royalty Processes** (`current_royalty_processes`, select_multiple, required: true): Select all current royalty processes that apply to your business. You can select multiple options.
* **Current Process Description** (`current_process_description`, text, required: false): Provide a description of your current royalty processes.
* **Current Process Frequency** (`current_process_frequency`, select_one, required: true): Select the frequency of your current royalty processes.
* **Current Process Owners** (`current_process_owners`, select_multiple, required: true): Select all current process owners.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Provide the name of the contact person responsible for the pre-engagement process.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Provide the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Provide the email address of the contact person.
* **Expectation 1** (`pre_engagement_expectation_1`, text, required: false): Enter your expectation for the pre-engagement process.
* **Expectation 2** (`pre_engagement_expectation_2`, text, required: false): Enter your expectation for the pre-engagement process.
* **Expectation 3** (`pre_engagement_expectation_3`, text, required: false): Enter your expectation for the pre-engagement process.
* **Requirement 1** (`requirement_1`, text, required: false): Enter a requirement for the pre-engagement process.
* **Requirement 2** (`requirement_2`, text, required: false): Enter a requirement for the pre-engagement process.
* **Requirement 3** (`requirement_3`, text, required: false): Enter a requirement for the pre-engagement process.
* **Availability 1** (`availability_1`, text, required: false): Enter the availability of the pre-engagement process.
* **Availability 2** (`availability_2`, text, required: false): Enter the availability of the pre-engagement process.
* **Availability 3** (`availability_3`, text, required: false): Enter the availability of the pre-engagement process.
* **Engagement Start Date** (`engagement_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the engagement.
* **Engagement Duration** (`engagement_duration`, text, required: false): Enter the duration of the engagement.
* **Engagement Frequency** (`engagement_frequency`, select_one, required: true): Select the frequency of the engagement.
* **Location 1** (`location_1`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the engagement.
* **Location 2** (`location_2`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the engagement.
* **Location 3** (`location_3`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the engagement.
* **Pre-Engagement Comments** (`pre_engagement_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments about the pre-engagement process.
* **Engagement Status** (`engagement_status`, select_one, required: true): Select the status of the engagement.
* **Engagement Notes** (`engagement_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any notes about the engagement.
* **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, text, required: false): Enter feedback from the client.
* **Pre-Engagement Survey Comments** (`pre_engagement_survey_comments`, text, required: false): Enter comments about the pre-engagement survey.
* **Survey Completion Status** (`survey_completion_status`, select_one, required: true): Select the status of the survey completion.
* **Survey Creation Date** (`survey_creation_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the survey was created.
* **Survey Creation Time** (`survey_creation_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the survey was created.
* **Survey Last Update Date** (`survey_last_update_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the survey was last updated.

* **Survey Last Update User Time** (`survey_last_update_user_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the survey was last updated by the user.
