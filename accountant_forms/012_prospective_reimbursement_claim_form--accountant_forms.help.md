# Prospective Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prospective Reimbursement Claim Form is designed to collect information from users who have expenses to be reimbursed by an organization.

## How To Complete This Form

### Completing the Form

1.  **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): This field is not currently required, but it's still a good idea to fill it out to ensure you can be contacted about your claim.
2.  **Expense Date** (`expense_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your expense.
3.  **Expense Description** (`expense_description`, text, required/optional): Describe your expense in a few words.
4.  **Receipt Upload** (`receipt_upload`, text, required/optional): Upload a receipt or supporting document for your expense.
5.  **Expense Claim Amount** (`expense_claim_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of your expense.
6.  **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person claiming the expense.
7.  **Organization ID** (`organization_id`, text, required): Enter your organization ID.
8.  **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you would like to submit your form (Submit or Other).
9.  **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your claim.
10. **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
11. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
12. **Expense Category** (`expense_category`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the category for your expense.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your claim can be processed correctly.
* Use a valid email address and phone number so we can contact you about your claim.
* Upload any supporting documents or receipts to help us process your claim.
* Choose the correct category for your expense to ensure it is processed correctly.
* Review your form before submitting to make sure all fields are filled out correctly.
