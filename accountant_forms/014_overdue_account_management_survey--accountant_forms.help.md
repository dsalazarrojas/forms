# Overdue Account Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Overdue Account Management Survey form is designed to help account managers and stakeholders track and manage client accounts that are overdue or require follow-up. It collects information about communication practices, data collection, follow-up frequency, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as needed. You will need to provide information about each question, including your feedback, communication channels, and frequency of follow-up. You can select the relevant options from the dropdown menus or type in your answers in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Collection Workflows**: Describe the collection workflows you use for your account. (text field)
* **Communication Practices**: Explain your communication practices with clients. (text field)
* **Client Responsiveness**: Rate how responsive your clients are. (text field)
* **Internal Follow-up**: Describe your internal follow-up practices. (text field)
* **Data Collection**: Explain your data collection methods. (text field)
* **Feedback**: Rate your level of agreement or disagreement with the statements (select_one field)
* **Communication Channels**: Select the channels you use for communication with clients. (select_multiple field)
* **Frequency of Follow-up**: Select the frequency of follow-up actions. (select_one field)
* **Follow-up Reminders**: Confirm whether you use follow-up reminders. (select_multiple field)
* **Data Quality**: Enter the quality of data you collect. (number field)
* **Client Contact Information**: Enter any additional client contact information. (text field)
* **Client Email**: Enter the client's email address. (email field)
* **Follow-up Frequency**: Enter any other follow-up frequency. (time field)
* **Other Communication Channels**: Describe any other communication channels you use. (text field)
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments. (note field)
* **Client Relationship Manager**: Confirm whether you have a client relationship manager. (select_multiple field)
* **Other Follow-up Reminders**: Describe any other follow-up reminders. (text field)
* **Data Collection Reminders**: Confirm whether you use data collection reminders. (select_multiple field)
* **Internal Follow-up Reminders**: Confirm whether you use internal follow-up reminders. (select_multiple field)
* **External Follow-up Reminders**: Confirm whether you use external follow-up reminders. (select_multiple field)
* **Other Follow-up Frequency**: Enter any other follow-up frequency. (time field)
* **Other Follow-up Reminders Description**: Describe any other follow-up reminders. (text field)
* **Other Follow-up Reminders**: Describe any other follow-up reminders. (text field)
* **Other Follow-up Reminders Frequency**: Enter the frequency of other follow-up reminders. (time field)

Note: This form requires no technical knowledge, and the instructions are designed to be clear and concise. Users can easily navigate through each field and provide the necessary information.
