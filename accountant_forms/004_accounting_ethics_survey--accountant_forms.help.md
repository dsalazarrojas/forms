# Accounting Ethics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights and opinions from accounting professionals on various aspects of ethics in the accounting profession.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the corresponding answer to each question.
2. Be honest and thorough in your responses.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Role in Accounting** (`respondent_role`, select_one, required)
	+ Select your primary role in the accounting profession, such as CPA, Accountant, Auditor, Finance Manager, Student, Educator, or Other.
* **Years of Experience in Accounting** (`years_experience`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose how many years of professional experience you have.
* **Type of Organization You Work For** (`workplace_environment`, select_one, required)
	+ Select the type of organization where you primarily work, such as Public accounting firm, Private corporation, Non-profit organization, Government agency, Academic institution, or Consulting firm.
* **Have you received Formal Ethics Training in the Past 2 Years** (`ethics_training_received`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose if you have received formal training on professional ethics and conduct in the past 2 years.
* **How Often Do You Encounter Ethical Dilemmas in Your Work** (`ethical_dilemma_frequency`, select_one, required)
	+ Select the frequency of situations where you have to make tough ethical choices.
* **Sources of Pressure That Could Compromise Ethics** (`pressure_sources`, select_multiple, required false)
	+ Choose all sources of pressure you experience in your work that could compromise your ethics, such as Client demands, Management expectations, Time constraints, Revenue targets, Job security concerns, Competitive pressures, or None of the above.
* **How Comfortable Are You Reporting Unethical Behavior** (`reporting_unethical_behavior`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose how comfortable you are with reporting unethical behavior.
* **Importance of Ethics Resources** (`ethics_resources_importance`, select_one, required)
	+ Select the importance of formal ethics resources to your work.
* **Do You Think Ethics Education Should be Mandatory Continuing Education** (`continuing_education_ethics`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose if you think ethics education should be a requirement for maintaining certification.
* **Impact of Technology on Ethical Standards** (`technology_impact_on_ethics`, select_one, required)
	+ Select how technology affects ethical considerations in accounting.
* **Biggest Ethics Challenge** (`biggest_ethics_challenge`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose what you see as the biggest ethics challenge in accounting today.
* **Suggestions for Improving Ethical Standards** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional)
	+ Provide any ideas you have for strengthening ethics practices in accounting.

## Tips
1. Be honest and thorough in your responses.
2. Select all relevant options when answering questions with multiple options.
3. If you experience difficulty or have questions, contact form@gic.mx.
