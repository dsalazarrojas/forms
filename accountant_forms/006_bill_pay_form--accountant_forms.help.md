# Bill Pay Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bill Pay Form is used to collect and process payments from payers. It ensures accurate and secure payment information, reducing errors and enabling timely payments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the payer's contact details, including their first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide the customer or account number associated with the payment.
3. Enter the bill payment details, including the bill number, date, and total amount due.
4. Select the payment method and frequency (one-time or recurring).
5. Confirm the payment authorization and agree to the terms and conditions.
6. Specify any additional payment notes or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payer Information**: Enter the payer's contact details, including their first and last name, email address, and phone number.
* **First Name**: Enter the payer's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the payer's last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter the payer's email address for payment confirmation.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the payer's contact number for payment issues.
* **Company Name**: Enter the business name if applicable.
* **Account Number**: Enter the customer or account number associated with the payment.
* **Bill Information**: Enter a brief description of the bill or payment details.
* **Bill Number**: Enter the invoice or bill reference number.
* **Bill Date**: Enter the date on the bill or invoice.
* **Due Date**: Enter the payment due date.
* **Bill Amount**: Enter the total amount due.
* **Bill Description**: Enter a brief description of charges.
* **Payment Details**: Enter any additional payment information or notes.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount to be paid.
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date of the payment.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment type (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Check, Cash, PayPal, Venmo).
* **Payment Frequency**: Select whether this is a one-time or recurring payment (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **AutoPay Authorization**: Confirm whether to enable automatic payments.
* **Payment Notes**: Enter any additional information about this payment.
* **Late Fee Acknowledgment**: Acknowledge understanding of potential late fees.
* **Confirmation Preference**: Select how you want to receive confirmation (Email, SMS, Both, No Confirmation).
* **Authorization Agreement**: Confirm payment authorization and certify that funds are available.
