# Paperless Billing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paperless Billing Request Form is used to request a switch to electronic billing and to select your preferred billing cycle. By filling out this form, you can choose to receive electronic bills instead of paper bills and select the frequency of your billing cycle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name
2. Enter your Last Name
3. Enter your Email Address
4. Enter your Phone Number
5. Enter your Street Address 1 (Street Address Line 1)
6. Enter your Street Address 2 (Street Address Line 2)
7. Enter your City
8. Select your State (using the dropdown menu)
9. Enter your Zip/Postal Code
10. Enter your Account Number
11. Choose to Switch to Electronic Statements (Yes or No)
12. Select your Billing Cycle (Monthly, Every Other Month, or Quarterly)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Street Address 1**: Enter your street address line 1.
* **Street Address 2**: Enter your street address line 2, if applicable.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Choose your state from the dropdown menu.
* **Zip/Postal Code**: Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Account Number**: Enter your account number.
* **Switch to Electronic Statements**: Choose to switch to electronic statements (Yes or No).
* **Billing Cycle**: Choose your preferred billing cycle (Monthly, Every Other Month, or Quarterly).

## Tips
* Ensure that your email address and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any special requests or comments, please include them in the space provided (not included in this form).
* If you experience any issues with your billing, contact your account manager or support team.
* If you're unsure about your preferred billing cycle, please refer to your account documentation or contact your account manager for guidance.
