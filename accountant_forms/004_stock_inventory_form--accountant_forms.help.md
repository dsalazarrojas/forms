# Stock Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Inventory Form is used to track and manage stock levels, quantities, and other relevant information for a business or organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please enter a brief description of the stock in the **description** field.
2. Enter the stock quantity in the **stock** field.
3. Enter the name of the item in the **item name** field.
4. Enter the description of the item in the **item description** field (if applicable).
5. Enter the unit of measurement for the item in the **item unit** field. (e.g., kg, lbs, etc.).
6. Select the status of the item (e.g., available, out of stock, etc.) in the **item status** field.
7. Enter the reorder level for the item in the **reorder level** field.
8. Select the reorder quantity options in the **reorder quantity** field (if applicable).
9. Select the date of the inventory update in the **date** field.
10. Enter any additional comments or notes about the stock in the **notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **description** (text, required): A brief description of the stock.
* **stock** (text, required): Enter the current stock quantity.
* **item name** (text, required): The name of the item.
* **item description** (text, optional): A detailed description of the item (if applicable).
* **item unit** (select_one, required): The unit of measurement for the item (e.g., kg, lbs, etc.).
* **reorder level** (number, optional): The level at which the item should be reordered.
* **reorder quantity** (select_multiple, optional): Select the quantity to reorder (if applicable).
* **item status** (select_one, required): The status of the item (e.g., available, out of stock, etc.).
* **date** (date, optional): The date of the inventory update.
* **notes** (note, required): Additional comments or notes about the stock.
* **email** (email, optional): Contact email of the person completing the form (if applicable).
* **phone** (text, optional): Contact phone number of the person completing the form (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any information or have questions, please contact the inventory team for guidance and support.
* This form will be reviewed and updated regularly to ensure that the information is up-to-date and accurate.
