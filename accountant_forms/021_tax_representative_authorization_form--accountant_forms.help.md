# Tax Representative Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Representative Authorization Form is a document used to grant or revoke authorization for a tax representative to act on a taxpayer's behalf. This form ensures that the taxpayer's actions and decisions are accounted for and recorded accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete each field as instructed below.
2. Review and confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tax Payer Authorization** (1. Tax Payer Authorization):
	+ Enter the taxpayer's name and identification number as required.
* **Tax Representative Name** (2. Tax Representative Name):
	+ Enter the name of the tax representative who will be authorized to act on the taxpayer's behalf.
* **Scope of Service** (3. Scope of Service):
	+ Describe the specific services to be performed by the tax representative, including any relevant details about the scope of their authority.
* **Duration of Service** (4. Duration of Service):
	+ Specify the time period during which the tax representative will have authority to act on the taxpayer's behalf.
* **Email Address** (5. Email Address):
	+ Enter the email address of the tax representative, to be used for communication and authorization updates.
* **Phone Number** (6. Phone Number):
	+ Enter the phone number of the tax representative, to be used for communication and authorization updates.
* **Date of Authorization** (7. Date of Authorization):
	+ Enter the date on which the tax representative's authority is granted or revoked.
* **Scope of Authorization** (8. Scope of Authorization):
	+ Describe the specific aspects of the taxpayer's account that the tax representative has authority to manage or act upon.
* **Duration of Authorization** (9. Duration of Authorization):
	+ Specify the time period during which the tax representative has authority to act on the taxpayer's behalf.
* **Notes** (10. Notes):
	+ Enter any additional comments or instructions related to the authorization.

## Tips
- Make sure to review and confirm the accuracy of the information provided before submitting the form.
- If you are revoking authorization, ensure that you have the necessary clearance from the tax representative to do so.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please consult with a tax expert before proceeding.
