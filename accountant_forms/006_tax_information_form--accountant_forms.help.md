# Tax Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Information Form is used to collect and store tax-related information from clients. This form helps accountants to efficiently gather the necessary details for tax preparation and filing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name and other identification details such as Social Security Number, Client ID, and Tax File Number.
2. Select the client's tax return frequency (e.g., W-4, W-2).
3. Enter the client's address and contact information (e.g., phone, email).
4. Choose the client's tax filing status and tax residence status.
5. Enter the tax filing date and date of birth.
6. Specify how the client will file their tax return (e.g., online, offline, phone, email, or in-person).
7. Enter the accountant's name and contact information (e.g., phone, email).

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and efficient tax filing.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact your accountant for clarification.
* This form is for client information only and should not be used for any other purpose.
* Ensure you save the form after completing it to keep a record of the client's information.
