# Payroll Processing Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Payroll Processing Checklist" form is used to process new hires and collect essential information for payroll purposes. This form is designed to guide users through the required steps and fields to ensure accurate and complete processing.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in the "Employee Number" field, as this is a required field.
- Proceed with the "Compensation" field, selecting 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the new hire is eligible for compensation.
- Fill in the "tax_federal", "tax_state", and "tax_local" fields with the corresponding tax rates.
- In the "Pay Date" field, select the date of the pay period.
- Fill in the "Pay Amount" field with the total pay amount.
- Enter the benefits details in the "Benefits" field and provide a "Benefits Dollar Value" and "Benefits Percentage" for accurate calculation.
- For tax liability, select 'Yes' or 'No' in the "Tax Liability" field and choose options as applicable.
- Fill in the "Notes" and "Notes Options" fields with any additional information.
- Enter the "Email", "Phone", "Address", "City", "State", and "Zip" fields with the new hire's contact information.
