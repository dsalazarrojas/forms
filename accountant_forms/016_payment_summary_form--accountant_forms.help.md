# Payment Summary Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information about a payment, including the payment date, account holder, payment amount, payment method, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the payment date from the calendar or manually enter it in the provided field.
2. Enter the name of the account holder who made the payment.
3. Enter the amount of the payment.
4. Choose the method of payment from the available options (Bank Transfer, Check, or Cash).
5. Add any additional notes about the payment, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the payment was made.
* **Account Holder** (`account_holder`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person or organization making the payment.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the total amount of the payment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose the method used to make the payment (Bank Transfer, Check, or Cash).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or information about the payment.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date payment information to ensure correct records.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can always contact the relevant authority for guidance.
* This form is designed to be simple and easy to use; if you have any issues, feel free to reach out for assistance.
