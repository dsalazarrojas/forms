# Sole Member Borrowing Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sole Member Borrowing Confirmation form is used to collect information from the sole member of an entity for the purpose of confirming their agreement with terms related to borrowing or investment. This form ensures that the entity has a formal record of the member's approval and understanding of the terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant information for the sole member's confirmation, including the entity name, approval date, amount, purpose, and any other relevant details.
2. Confirm your agreement with the given terms by selecting the appropriate option from the "Consent" field.
3. If you have any other terms or conditions that do not appear in the list, please specify them in the "Other (please specify)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Entity Name
* **Entity Name** (`entity_name`, text, optional): This field is for the name of the entity that is borrowing or investing. Please enter the exact name as it appears on official documents.

### 2. Approval Date
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): This field is for the date when the sole member approved the borrowing or investment terms. Please use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### 3. Amount
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, optional): This field is for the total amount of the borrowing or investment. Please enter the exact amount without any commas or decimal points.

### 4. Purpose
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, optional): This field is for the purpose of the borrowing or investment. Please provide a brief description of the reason for the loan or investment.

### 5. Signatures
* **Signatures** (`signatures`, note, optional): This field is for any other relevant notes or comments regarding the borrowing or investment.

### 6. Consent
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): This field is for confirming your agreement with the terms. Please select "I confirm I have reviewed and agree with the terms" or "I do not confirm" from the provided options.

### 7. Other (please specify)
* **Other (please specify)** (`other_consent`, select_multiple, required for "I do not confirm"): If you selected "I do not confirm" in the "Consent" field, please specify any other terms or conditions that are not listed in the provided options.
