# Financial Service Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Service Appointment Form is designed to collect essential details about a client's appointment with a financial advisor or accountant. This form helps to streamline the appointment scheduling process, ensuring that both the client and the financial advisor are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, which is marked as "required" in the form.
2. Select the relevant financial service from the provided options (Accounting, Financial Planning, or Tax Services).
3. Enter the appointment date and time, using the format specified for the "Date" and "Time" fields.
4. Enter the expected duration of the appointment.
5. Provide any additional contact details for the client.
6. Add any relevant notes or reminders for the appointment.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Financial Service** (`Financial Service`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of financial service you want to provide (Accounting, Financial Planning, or Tax Services).
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the appointment in the format specified.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the appointment in the format specified.
* **Duration** (`Duration`, `number`, required): Enter the expected duration of the appointment.
* **Client** (`Client`, `text`, required): Enter the client's name or contact information.
* **Location** (`Location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the appointment (e.g., office, home, or online meeting).
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required): Enter any additional contact information for the client.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required): Add any notes or reminders for the appointment.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required): Review and submit the form to schedule the appointment.
* **Save and Submit** (`Save and Submit`, `text`, required): Save the form and submit it for approval.
* **Back** (`Back`, `text`, optional): Go back to the previous page or cancel the appointment.
* **Cancel** (`Cancel`, `text`, optional): Cancel the appointment and return to the start.
* **Save and Back** (`Save and Back`, `text`, optional): Save the form and go back to the previous page.
* **Save and Cancel** (`Save and Cancel`, `text`, optional): Save the form and cancel the appointment.
* **Back and Save** (`Back and Save`, `text`, optional): Go back to the previous page and save the form.
