# Expense Reimbursement Form With Calculations - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees or individuals track and reimburse expenses related to their work activities.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with the required details such as expense type, date, amount, and other relevant information.
- Ensure that all the required fields are completed before proceeding.
- Review the calculated fields for accuracy and consistency with the input information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Expense Type** (`expense_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of expense you are claiming reimbursement for (e.g. Business trip, Travel, etc.).
- **Expense Date** (`expense_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the expense.
- **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`, number, required): Enter the actual cost of the expense.
- **Calculated Value** (`calculated_value`, number, required): This field will display the calculated reimbursement amount based on the entered expense amount.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Provide any additional comments or details about the expense.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your expense claim.
- **Calculated Field 1** (`calculated_field_1`, text, required): This field will display the calculated total expenses.
- **Calculated Field 2** (`calculated_field_2`, text, required): This field will display the calculated total expenses with tax included.
- **Calculated Field 3** (`calculated_field_3`, text, required): This field will display the calculated average expense amount.
- **Calculated Field 4** (`calculated_field_4`, text, required): This field will display the calculated total expenses for the current month.
- **Calculated Field 5** (`calculated_field_5`, text, required): This field will display the calculated average expense amount for the current month.
- **Calculated Field 6** (`calculated_field_6`, text, required): This field will display the calculated total expenses for the current year.
- **Calculated Field 7** (`calculated_field_7`, text, required): This field will display the calculated average expense amount for the current year.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have all necessary documents and supporting evidence for the expense before submitting the form.
- Double-check the accuracy and consistency of the input information before submitting the form.
- If you have any doubts or questions, please contact your supervisor or HR department for assistance.
