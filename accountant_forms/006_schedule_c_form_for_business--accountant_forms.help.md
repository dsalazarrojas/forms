# Schedule C Form For Business - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Schedule C Form For Business is a tax form used to report income from self-employment or business activities. This form is used to calculate and report business income, expenses, and tax liabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections to understand what information is required.
2. Ensure you have all necessary documents and records, such as business financials and receipts.
3. Fill out the form section by section, starting with the first page.
4. For each section, provide the required information accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Schedule C Form
* **Schedule C Form** (`schedule_c_form`, `text`, required/optional): This is the main form section where you can enter your business name and other relevant information.

### Page 2: Form Details
* **Form Details** (`form_details`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please choose 'Yes' if you are reporting income from self-employment or 'No' if you are not. This section helps us understand your reporting requirements.

### Page 3: Business Name
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your business name as it appears on your business license or registration.

Note: The "required/optional" label indicates that the field is either required or optional, depending on the user's circumstances. The user should consult the form instructions or a tax professional if they are unsure about any field.
