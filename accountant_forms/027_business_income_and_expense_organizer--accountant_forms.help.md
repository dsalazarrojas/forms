# Business Income And Expense Organizer - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Income And Expense Organizer is a financial tracking tool designed to help businesses monitor and analyze their income and expenses. This guide will walk you through each section of the form, explaining what information is required and how to complete it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Select the reporting period for which you want to track your income and expenses.
*   Enter the start and end dates of the reporting period.
*   Choose your primary income source, which is the largest contributor to your revenue.
*   Calculate your total gross income and enter it in the respective field.
*   Select the largest expense categories that make up your operating expenses.
*   Enter the total operating expenses.
*   Optionally, select any deductible expenses that you have recorded for tax purposes.
*   Verify that all your receipts and invoices are organized for review.
*   Enter any significant financial notes or comments.
*   Enter the date you completed the form.
*   Finally, enter your full name as the preparer of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **-- Reporting Period --** (`section_reporting_period`): Select the reporting period for which you want to track your income and expenses.
*   **Reporting Period Start Date** (`reporting_period_start_date`): Enter the start date of the reporting period. This date should mark the beginning of the period for which you are reporting.
*   **Reporting Period End Date** (`reporting_period_end_date`): Enter the end date of the reporting period. This date should mark the end of the period for which you are reporting.
*   **-- Income Summary --** (`section_income_summary`): This section provides an overview of your income data.
*   **Total Gross Income (USD)** (`total_gross_income_amount`): Enter the total gross income before any deductions.
*   **Primary Income Source** (`primary_income_source_category`): Choose the largest contributor to your revenue.
*   **-- Expense Summary --** (`section_expense_summary`): This section provides an overview of your expenses data.
*   **Total Operating Expenses (USD)** (`total_operating_expenses_amount`): Enter the sum of all business-related costs.
*   **Largest Expense Categories** (`breakdown_top_expenses_selection`): Select the top 3 categories with the highest expenses.
*   **Payroll Expenses (USD)** (`payroll_expenses_total_amount`): Enter the total payroll expenses including wages, taxes, and benefits.
*   **Rent and Utilities (USD)** (`rent_utilities_total_amount`): Enter the total rent and utilities expenses.
*   **Deductible Expense Verification** (`tax_deductible_expenses_selection`): Select any deductible expenses that you have recorded for tax purposes.
*   **-- Financial Result Notice --** (`net_profit_loss_notice`): This section provides information on your net profit/loss.
*   **Financial Notes or Comments** (`financial_organizer_notes`): Enter any significant financial variations or one-time events.
*   **Documentation Availability** (`documentation_availability_status`): Choose whether all your receipts and invoices are organized for review.
*   **Date Completed** (`organizer_completion_date`): Enter the date you completed the form.
*   **Preparer Full Name** (`preparer_name_full`): Enter your full name as the preparer of the form.
