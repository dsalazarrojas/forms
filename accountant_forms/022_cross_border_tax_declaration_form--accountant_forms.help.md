# Cross Border Tax Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cross Border Tax Declaration Form is a document used by accountants to report cross-border taxes. It gathers essential information about the business, tax rates, and other relevant details for tax declarations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all relevant options on the "Tax Status" field as needed.
2. Enter the "Tax Year" corresponding to the tax period you are declaring.
3. Fill in the "Tax Amount" with the exact amount you are declaring.
4. Enter your email address, phone number, and business address if required.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
6. Click the "Submit" button to save your declaration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page (1)**: This page serves as a starting point for your tax declaration. 
* **Business Details (2)**: Select the type of business you are declaring taxes for. This field is required.
* **Tax Info (3)**: Enter the date when the tax was declared. 
* **Tax Date (4)**: This field is not used in the given YAML. 
* **Tax Status (5)**: Choose all applicable tax statuses for the declared tax. This field is required.
* **Tax Period (6)**: Enter the period for which the tax is declared, if required. 
* **Tax Year (7)**: Enter the year of tax declaration. This field is required.
* **Tax Amount (8)**: Enter the amount of tax declared. This field is required.
* **Tax Rate (9)**: Enter the tax rate, if applicable. 
* **Notes (10)**: Add any additional comments or information about the tax declaration. 
* **Email (11)**: Enter your email address, if you want to be contacted. 
* **Phone (12)**: Enter your phone number, if needed. 
* **Address (13)**: Enter your business address, if required. 
* **Submit (14)**: Click this button to save your tax declaration. 
* **Cancel (15)**: Click this button to cancel your tax declaration and start over. 

## Tips
Please make sure to review and submit the form carefully as incomplete or inaccurate declarations may lead to issues with your tax records. If you have any questions or concerns, contact your accountant or tax advisor for guidance.
