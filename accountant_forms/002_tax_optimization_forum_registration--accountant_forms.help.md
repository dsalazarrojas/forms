# Tax Optimization Forum Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for the Tax Optimization Forum, a platform for professionals to share knowledge and discuss relevant topics in the tax field.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a title for your forum in the "Tax Optimization Forum Title" field.
2. Enter the date of your event in the "Tax Optimization Forum Date" field.
3. Specify the time of your event in the "Tax Optimization Forum Time" field.
4. Enter the location of your event in the "Tax Optimization Forum Location" field.
5. Provide a brief description of your forum in the "Tax Optimization Forum Description" field.
6. Choose a category for your forum from the options provided in the "Tax Optimization Forum Category" field.
7. Select the interests you would like to share and discuss in the "Tax Optimization Forum Interests" field. (Note: This field is mandatory)
8. Enter any additional details about yourself or your organization in the "Tax Optimization Forum Attendee Details" field (optional).
9. Confirm your consent to the terms and conditions of the event by selecting an option in the "Tax Optimization Forum Consent" field. (Note: This field is mandatory)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tax Optimization Forum Title** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for your Tax Optimization Forum.
* **Tax Optimization Forum Date** (`tax_optimization_forum_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your event.
* **Tax Optimization Forum Time** (`tax_optimization_forum_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of your event.
* **Tax Optimization Forum Location** (`tax_optimization_forum_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of your event.
* **Tax Optimization Forum Description** (`tax_optimization_forum_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your forum.
* **Tax Optimization Forum Category** (`tax_optimization_forum_category`, select_one, required/optional): Choose a category for your forum from the options provided.
* **Tax Optimization Forum Interests** (`tax_optimization_forum_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select the interests you would like to share and discuss. (Note: This field is mandatory)
* **Tax Optimization Forum Attendee Details** (`tax_optimization_forum_attendee_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about yourself or your organization.
* **Tax Optimization Forum Consent** (`tax_optimization_forum_consent`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm your consent to the terms and conditions of the event. (Note: This field is mandatory)

## Tips

* Be clear and concise when filling out the form.
* Make sure to provide accurate information to avoid any confusion or inconvenience.
* Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure that everything is correct.
