# reasons_for_choosing_accounting_services - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients about their preferences and requirements for accounting services. This information will help the accounting services provider to understand the client's needs and provide tailored services that meet their expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure that you provide accurate and relevant information.
2. Complete all required fields (marked as required).
3. Select the most suitable options for each field from the provided choices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Perspective** (`Client Perspective`, text, required): This field is an open-ended text field where you can describe your perspective or view on the accounting services you require.
- **What services do you require?** (`What services do you require?`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the services that apply to you. The available options are:
    - Yes: You require the selected service.
    - No: You do not require the selected service.
- **What level of service do you require?** (`What level of service do you require?`, select_one, required): Please select the level of service that best suits your needs. The available options are:
    - High: High-level service with regular communication and frequent check-ins.
    - Low: Low-level service with minimal communication and less frequent check-ins.
- **How often do you prefer to communicate with us?** (`How often do you prefer to communicate with us?`, select_one, required): Please select how often you would like to communicate with us. The available options are:
    - Option 1: Frequently.
    - Option 2: Occasionally.
    - Option 3: Rarely.
- **How do you prefer to communicate with us?** (`How do you prefer to communicate with us?`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the communication methods that you prefer. The available options are:
    - Yes: You prefer this communication method.
    - No: You do not prefer this communication method.
- **How often do you require our services?** (`How often do you require our services?`, select_one, required): Please select how often you need our services. The available options are:
    - Yes: You require our services frequently.
    - No: You do not require our services frequently.
- **How often do you prefer to communicate with us?** (`How often do you prefer to communicate with us?`, select_one, optional): This field is optional and allows you to specify how often you prefer to communicate with us. The available options are:
    - Yes: You prefer communication frequently.
    - No: You do not prefer communication frequently.
- **How do you prefer to communicate with us?** (`How do you prefer to communicate with us?`, select_multiple, optional): This field is optional and allows you to select all the communication methods that you prefer. The available options are:
    - Yes: You prefer this communication method.
    - No: You do not prefer this communication method.
- **How often do you require our services?** (`How often do you require our services?`, select_one, optional): This field is optional and allows you to select how often you require our services. The available options are:
    - Yes: You require our services frequently.
    - No: You do not require our services frequently.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when providing information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
- This form is designed to be completed by you, the client. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
