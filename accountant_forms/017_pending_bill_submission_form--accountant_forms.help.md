# Pending Bill Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pending Bill Submission Form is used to submit new bills that are awaiting payment. It ensures that all necessary information is recorded accurately and on-time, helping to streamline the payment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct department and project from the options provided.
2. Enter the date of the invoice.
3. Enter the amount due for payment.
4. Choose the payment terms (e.g., Net 30, Net 60).
5. Choose the payment method (e.g., Bank Transfer, Check, Credit Card).
6. Select the person submitting the bill (e.g., Finance Team, Project Manager, Department Manager).
7. Enter the due date for payment.
8. Verify that the form is complete and accurate before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Form**: This field is used to confirm that the form has been filled out correctly and is ready for submission.
* **Date of Invoice**: Enter the date of the invoice in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Department**: Select the department that the invoice belongs to.
* **Project**: Select the project associated with the invoice.
* **Invoice Number**: Enter the unique invoice number.
* **Amount**: Enter the total amount due for payment.
* **Payment Terms**: Choose the payment terms (e.g., Net 30, Net 60).
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method (e.g., Bank Transfer, Check, Credit Card).
* **Due Date**: Enter the date by which the payment is due.
* **Status**: Choose the current status of the bill (e.g., Pending, Paid, Cancelled).
* **Submitted By**: Select the person submitting the bill (e.g., Finance Team, Project Manager, Department Manager).
* **Submitted On**: Enter the date the bill was submitted.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the bill.
* **Submitted By Full Name**: Enter the full name of the person submitting the bill.
* **Submitted By Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the bill.
* **Submitted By Extension**: Enter any additional information about the person submitting the bill.
* **Submitted By Job Title**: Enter the job title of the person submitting the bill.
* **Submitted By Department**: Enter the department of the person submitting the bill.
* **Submitted By Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the bill.
* **Submitted By Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the person submitting the bill.
* **Submitted By Company**: Enter the company of the person submitting the bill.
* **Submitted By Address**: Enter the address of the person submitting the bill.
* **Submitted By Job Title 2**: Enter the job title of the person submitting the bill.
* **Submitted By Phone 2**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the bill.

Note: The "Submitted By" fields are for administrative purposes only and will be populated automatically when the form is submitted. They do not need to be filled out by the user.
