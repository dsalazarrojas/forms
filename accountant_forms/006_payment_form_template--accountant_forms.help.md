# Payment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Form Template is designed to gather payment information from users for processing transactions or payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your credit card type from the options provided.
2. Enter your expiration month and year of your credit card.
3. Choose your payment method from the available options.
4. Enter your CVV (security code) for verification.
5. Enter your expiration date (CVV) for verification.
6. Enter your zip code for verification.
7. Select your payment currency.
8. Enter your payment amount.
9. Enter your phone number for verification.
10. Enter your address for verification.
11. Enter your city for verification.
12. Enter your country code for verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Form 1**: Enter any additional information that needs to be displayed on the payment form. (text field, not required)
* **Credit Card Label**: Select your credit card type (Credit card type 1, Credit card type 2, or Credit card type 3). (select_one, not required)
* **CVV Label**: Enter your CVV for verification. (text field, not required)
* **Exp Month Label**: Select your credit card expiration month (exp month 1, exp month 2, or exp month 3). (select_one, not required)
* **Exp Year Label**: Select your credit card expiration year (exp year 1, exp year 2, or exp year 3). (select_one, not required)
* **Payment Method Label**: Choose your payment method (payment method 1, payment method 2, or payment method 3). (select_multiple, not required)
* **CVV CVV Label**: Enter your CVV for verification. (text field, not required)
* **CVV Icode Label**: Enter your CVV code for verification. (text field, not required)
* **Payment Amount Label**: Enter your payment amount. (number field, not required)
* **Payment Currency Label**: Select your payment currency (payment currency 1 or payment currency 2). (select_one, not required)
* **CVV Zip Label**: Enter your CVV zip code for verification. (text field, not required)
* **CVV Country Label**: Enter your CVV country for verification. (text field, not required)
* **CVV Tel Label**: Enter your phone number for verification. (text field, not required)
* **CVV Addr Label**: Enter your address for verification. (text field, not required)
* **CVV City Label**: Enter your city for verification. (text field, not required)
* **CVV Country Code Label**: Enter your country code for verification. (text field, not required)
