# Tax Filing Service Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Filing Service Acknowledgment form is used to record the tax filing service provided to a client. It ensures that all parties involved are aware of the details of the service and the terms of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the client's name and tax year (if applicable) to initiate the service acknowledgment process.
2. Select the method of acknowledgement that best suits your service provider's preference (e.g., electronic, written, phone call, in-person meeting, or mail).
3. Enter any additional details as required for the selected acknowledgement method.
4. Review and ensure all fields are accurately filled before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the client's full name as it appears on their identification or as you have been instructed by the client.
* **Tax Year**: If the tax filing service is for a specific year, enter the year. This field is optional and only necessary for tax purposes.
* **Service Provider**: Enter the name and contact details of the service provider (if applicable).
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date the service provider signed the acknowledgement document (if applicable).
* **Signature Time**: Enter the time the service provider signed the acknowledgement document (if applicable).
* **Acknowledgement Date**: Enter the date the service provider acknowledged the client's tax filing service.
* **Tax Return Due Date**: Enter the date the client's tax return is due (if applicable).
* **Acknowledgement Method**: Select the method of acknowledgement that suits your service provider's preference (e.g., electronic, written, phone call, in-person meeting, or mail).
* **Acknowledgement Number**: Enter the number assigned to the acknowledgement document (if applicable).
* **Client Phone**: Enter the client's phone number (if applicable).
* **Service Provider Phone**: Enter the service provider's phone number (if applicable).
* **Signature**: Enter the service provider's signature (if applicable).
* **Acknowledgement Signature**: Enter the client's signature (if applicable).
* **Acknowledgement Email**: Enter the service provider's email (if applicable).
* **File Name**: Enter the name of the file or document related to the tax filing service (if applicable).
* **File Version**: Enter the version of the file or document related to the tax filing service (if applicable).

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before proceeding.
- Ensure all necessary fields are filled out according to the selected acknowledgement method.
- If any discrepancies or issues arise during the form completion process, contact the support team for assistance.
