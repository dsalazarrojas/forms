# Accounting Document Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect metadata about uploaded documents for efficient processing and accurate accounting. It helps ensure that the uploaded documents are properly handled and indexed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form for any special instructions or additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the company or individual name that the documents are related to.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address that the documents should be delivered to.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter an optional phone number for urgent clarifications.
* **Document types** (`document_types`, select multiple, required): Select the type of document being uploaded, such as Invoices, Receipts, Bank statements, Payroll records, or Contracts.
* **Period covered by documents** (`period_covered`, text, optional): Enter the period covered by the documents, such as Jan 2025 to Dec 2025.
* **Number of files** (`number_of_files`, number, optional): Enter an approximate count of uploaded files.
* **Preferred delivery method** (`preferred_delivery_method`, select one, required): Choose how the client prefers to receive files, such as Secure upload link, Email, or Physical delivery.
* **Confidentiality notice** (`confidentiality_notice`, note, optional): Enter a brief instruction about sensitive data handling.
* **Special instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special instructions for handling the uploaded documents.
* **Uploaded by** (`uploaded_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person uploading the files.
* **Upload date** (`upload_date`, date, required): Enter the date the files were submitted.
* **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, select one, required): Confirm that the documents are complete to the best of your knowledge.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any extra information about the upload.
