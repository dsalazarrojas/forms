# Depreciation Schedule Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Depreciation Schedule Form is used to record and track the depreciation of fixed assets, such as buildings, equipment, and vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the asset type from the options provided in the "Asset Type" field.
2. Enter the asset code in the "Asset Code" field.
3. Provide a description of the asset in the "Asset Description" field.
4. Enter the asset's cost in the "Asset Cost" field.
5. Select the "Depreciation Method" used for the asset (e.g. straight line, declining balance, etc.).
6. Enter the depreciation percentage for the asset.
7. Enter the remaining life of the asset.
8. Calculate the total depreciation of the asset.
9. Enter the current value of the asset.
10. Select the asset status (Active or Inactive) and enter the retirement date if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Asset Code** (Text): Enter the unique code assigned to the asset.
* **Asset Type** (Select One): Select the type of asset (Building, Equipment, Vehicle).
* **Asset Description** (Text): Provide a description of the asset.
* **Asset Cost** (Number): Enter the original cost of the asset.
* **Date Purchased** (Date): Enter the date the asset was purchased.
* **Purchase Price** (Number): Enter the purchase price of the asset.
* **Depreciation Method** (Select One): Select the method used for depreciation (e.g. Yes, No).
* **Depreciation Percentage** (Number): Enter the depreciation percentage for the asset.
* **Remaining Life** (Number): Enter the remaining life of the asset.
* **Total Depreciation** (Number): Calculate the total depreciation of the asset.
* **Current Value** (Number): Enter the current value of the asset.
* **Asset Status** (Select One): Select the asset status (Active or Inactive).
* **Retirement Date** (Date): Enter the retirement date of the asset if it is inactive.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct asset type to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
- Enter the asset code and description to help identify the asset.
- Use the depreciation method that best suits the asset's nature and purpose.
- Enter accurate depreciation percentages to ensure accurate calculations.
- Update the remaining life and current value as necessary to reflect the asset's condition.
- Mark the asset as inactive and enter the retirement date when it reaches its useful life or is no longer in use.
