# Treasury Reconciliation Approval Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate the treasury reconciliation process by gathering information from users to verify and approve financial transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your username in the "Username" field.
2. Enter the account number in the "Account Number" field.
3. Describe the supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents" field.
4. Enter any additional details about the transaction in the "Details" field.
5. Enter the reviewer's name in the "Reviewer Name" field.
6. Enter the reviewer's email address in the "Email Address" field.
7. Enter the reviewer's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
8. Select the date of the reconciliation in the "Date" field.
9. Select the time of the reconciliation in the "Time" field.
10. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Username** (text, required): This is the field for the user's username.
* **Account Number** (number, required): This is the field for the account number that needs to be reconciled.
* **Supporting Documents** (text, optional): This is the field for attaching supporting documents to the reconciliation application.
* **Details** (note, optional): This is the field for entering any additional details about the transaction.
* **Reviewer Name** (text, optional): This is the field for the reviewer's name who will be reviewing the reconciliation application.
* **Email Address** (email, optional): This is the field for the reviewer's email address.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): This is the field for the reviewer's phone number.
* **Date** (date, optional): This is the field for selecting the date of the reconciliation.
* **Time** (time, optional): This is the field for selecting the time of the reconciliation.
* **Submit** (text, required): This is the button to submit the form and initiate the reconciliation process.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and correctly to avoid any issues during the reconciliation process.
* Please attach any supporting documents to the "Supporting Documents" field if necessary.
* Please note that all fields are optional except for "Submit" which is required to submit the form.
* Please ensure that the reviewer's contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
