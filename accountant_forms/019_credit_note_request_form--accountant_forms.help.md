# Credit Note Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Credit Note Request Form is used to request a credit note from the account department for a company's financial records.

## How To Complete This Form
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out, even if they are not marked as required.
* Review the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company making the request.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person associated with the company.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Company Address**: Enter the address of the company making the request.
* **Request Date**: Select the date the credit note is being requested for.
* **Request Time**: Select the time the credit note is being requested for.
* **Amount Request**: Enter the amount of the credit note being requested.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the credit note being requested.

## Tips
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please reach out to the account department.
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure the credit note is processed correctly.
