# Investment Balance Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Balance Confirmation Form is used by accountants to collect information from clients regarding their investment balance. The form helps to confirm whether the client has agreed on the balance or not.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are authorized to complete the form. 
2. Review the form fields carefully to ensure you understand their purpose.
3. Enter the required information in the corresponding fields. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investment Balance Confirmation Form**: This is the title of the form and is for identification purposes only.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client whose investment balance is being confirmed.
* **Account Number**: Enter the account number associated with the client's investment balance.
* **Balance Confirmation Date**: Enter the date on which the balance was confirmed with the client.
* **Total Investment**: Enter the total investment amount agreed upon with the client.
* **Client Confirmation**: Select the confirmation option that best describes the client's status:
	+ Client confirmed: The client has agreed on the balance.
	+ Client not confirmed: The client has not agreed on the balance.
	+ Client unable to confirm: The client was unable to confirm the balance.
* **Accountant Confirmation**: Select the confirmation option that best describes the accountant's status:
	+ Accountant confirmed: The accountant has confirmed the balance.
	+ Accountant not confirmed: The accountant has not confirmed the balance.
	+ Unable to confirm: The accountant was unable to confirm the balance.
* **Third-Party Confirmation**: Select the confirmation option that best describes the third-party's status:
	+ Third-party confirmed: The third-party has confirmed the balance.
	+ Third-party not confirmed: The third-party has not confirmed the balance.
	+ Unable to confirm: The third-party was unable to confirm the balance.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the confirmation process.
* **Email Sent To**: Enter the email address of the person or organization that was sent a confirmation email.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person or organization that was contacted.
