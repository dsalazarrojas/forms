# Payment Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Declaration Form is a tool used to capture details about payments made to the organization. This form helps to update the organization's financial records and is essential for accurate accounting and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Payment Type** that best describes the payment made (e.g., cash, credit, bank transfer, etc.).
2. Enter the **Payment Date** when the payment was made.
3. Fill in the **Amount Paid**, the amount of money that was paid.
4. Choose the **Payment Method** used to make the payment.
5. Enter the **Account Number** associated with the payment.
6. Fill in the **Account Holder Name**, the name of the account holder.
7. Enter the **Bank Name** and **Branch Name** where the payment was made.
8. Provide the **Bank Branch Address** where the payment was received.
9. Select the **Bank Account Type** (e.g., checking, savings, money pooling) that applies to the payment.
10. Enter the **Payment Reference Number**, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Type**: Select the type of payment made (e.g., cash, credit, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date when the payment was made.
* **Amount Paid**: Enter the amount of money paid.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the method used to make the payment (e.g., cash, credit, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Account Number**: Enter the account number associated with the payment.
* **Account Holder Name**: Enter the name of the account holder.
* **Bank Name**: Enter the name of the bank where the payment was made.
* **Branch Name**: Enter the name of the branch where the payment was received.
* **Bank Branch Address**: Enter the address of the branch where the payment was made.
* **Bank Account Type**: Select the type of bank account used for the payment (e.g., checking, savings, money pooling, etc.).
* **Payment Reference Number**: Enter the payment reference number, if applicable.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* If unsure about any field, consult with the relevant team or department for guidance.
