# Form 843 Tax Abatement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Form 843 Tax Abatement Form is designed to facilitate the tax abatement process, reducing or eliminating tax owed on a given tax account.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the "Claim Tax Refund" section (Page 1) and decide if you would like to claim a tax refund. If yes, select "Yes" from the available options.
2. If claiming a tax refund, proceed to the "Abatement Form" section (Page 2) and choose the appropriate abatement form from the available options.
3. Fill in your details in the "Claimant Information" section (Pages 3-5), including your name, address, phone number, and email.
4. Enter the tax refund amount and date in the respective fields.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" section (Page 9).
6. Select the category and assigned tool for the form (Pages 10-11).
7. Set the submission time (Page 12).
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claim Tax Refund** (Page 1):
	+ This field asks if you would like to claim a tax refund. If yes, select "Yes" from the available options.
* **Abatement Form** (Page 2):
	+ This field allows you to select the appropriate abatement form for your tax refund claim.
* **Name** (Page 3):
	+ Enter your name as it should appear on the abatement form.
* **Address** (Page 4):
	+ Enter your address as it should appear on the abatement form.
* **Phone** (Page 5):
	+ Enter your phone number as it should appear on the abatement form.
* **Email** (Page 6):
	+ Enter your email address as it should appear on the abatement form.
* **Tax Refund Amount** (Page 7):
	+ Enter the amount of the tax refund you are claiming.
* **Tax Refund Date** (Page 8):
	+ Enter the date of the tax refund you are claiming.
* **Note** (Page 9):
	+ Add any additional notes or comments regarding your tax refund claim.
* **Category** (Page 10):
	+ Select the category for your tax refund claim (e.g., "Accountant Forms").
* **Assigned Tool** (Page 11):
	+ Select the tool assigned for your tax refund claim (e.g., "chatjimmy").
* **Submission Time** (Page 12):
	+ Set the time of submission for your tax refund claim.
* **Form ID** (Page 13):
	+ Enter a unique identifier for your tax refund form.
* **File Name** (Page 14):
	+ Enter a name for your tax refund file.
* **Download Link** (Page 15):
	+ Enter a link to download the tax refund file.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
* Ensure that you have a clear understanding of tax abatement policies and procedures at your institution.
* Review the tax refund status and form before proceeding to avoid duplicating efforts.
