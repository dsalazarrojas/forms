# Tax Preparation Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information from clients seeking tax preparation services. It is intended to be a general intake form, not a comprehensive tax return or audit.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- Review each field for clarity and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (`tax_prep_client_intake_form_1`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **What is your address?** (`tax_prep_client_intake_form_2`, text, required): Provide your current address, including your street address, apartment number (if applicable), city, state, and ZIP code.
* **What is your Social Security Number?** (`tax_prep_client_intake_form_3`, number, required): Please enter your Social Security Number as it appears on your identification documents.
* **What is your occupation?** (`tax_prep_client_intake_form_4`, select_one, required): Select your current occupation from the options provided.
	+ Self Employed
	+ Employee
	+ Unemployed
* **What is the nature of your business?** (`tax_prep_client_intake_form_5`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of business you have from the options provided.
	+ Sole Proprietor
	+ Partnership
	+ LLC
	+ S Corporation
	+ C Corporation
* **What is your income?** (`tax_prep_client_intake_form_6`, number, required): Enter your total annual income, including all sources and amounts.
* **What is your tax filing type?** (`tax_prep_client_intake_form_7`, select_one, required): Select the type of tax return you will be filing from the options provided.
	+ Form 1040
	+ Form 1065
	+ Form 1040A
* **What is your date of birth?** (`tax_prep_client_intake_form_8`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **What time of day do you file your taxes?** (`tax_prep_client_intake_form_9`, time, required): Select the time of day you typically file your taxes.
* **What is your email?** (`tax_prep_client_intake_form_10`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **What is your phone number?** (`tax_prep_client_intake_form_11`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Do you have a spouse or dependent?** (`tax_prep_client_intake_form_12`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you have a spouse or dependent from the options provided.
	+ Yes
	+ No
