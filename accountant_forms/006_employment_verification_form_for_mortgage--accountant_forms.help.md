# Employment Verification Form For Mortgage - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an applicant's employment verification for mortgage purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the current employment status section.
2. Then, enter details about the job history.
3. Finally, review and verify the entered information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the application.
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_information`, text, optional): Provide details about the applicant.
* **Job History** (`job_history`, text, optional): Enter details about the job history, including job title, start and end dates, and reason for leaving.
* **Verification Details** (`verification_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional verification details.
* **Employee Verification** (`employee_verification`, text, optional): Enter employee verification information.
* **Employee Identification** (`employee_identification`, number, optional): Enter employee identification number.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, optional): Enter years of experience.
* **Annual Salary** (`annual_salary`, number, optional): Enter annual salary.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current employment status.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter job title.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, text, optional): Enter work phone number.
* **Work Email** (`work_email`, email, optional): Enter work email.
* **Current or Previous Employer** (`job_history_employer`, text, optional): Enter name of employer.
* **Job History Job Title** (`job_history_job_title`, text, optional): Enter job title.
* **Start Date** (`job_history_start_date`, date, optional): Enter start date.
* **End Date** (`job_history_end_date`, date, optional): Enter end date.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`job_history_reason`, text, optional): Enter reason for leaving.
* **Current Employer** (`current_employer`, text, optional): Enter current employer name.
* **Years at Current Employer** (`years_at_current_employer`, number, optional): Enter years at current employer.
* **Annual Salary Current** (`annual_salary_current`, number, optional): Enter annual salary.
* **Employment Status Current** (`employment_status_current`, select_one, optional): Select employment status.
* **Job Title Current** (`job_title_current`, text, optional): Enter job title.
* **Work Phone Current** (`work_phone_current`, text, optional): Enter work phone number.
* **Work Email Current** (`work_email_current`, email, optional): Enter work email.
