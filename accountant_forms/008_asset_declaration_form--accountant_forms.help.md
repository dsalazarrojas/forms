# Asset Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Asset Declaration Form is used for an individual to declare and report their assets, including real property, financial accounts, investments, and liabilities. It is essential to complete this form accurately to provide a clear picture of one's assets and financial position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your personal details: First name, last name, and national ID number.
2. Select the type of declaration (Personal Assets, Business Assets, Corporate Assets, Trust Assets, or Estate Assets).
3. Provide your real property address and its type (residential, commercial, land, etc.).
4. If applicable, enter the value of your real property.
5. Report your financial accounts, including cash balances, savings, stock investments, bond investments, mutual funds, and retirement accounts.
6. Enter any other significant assets or liabilities not included in the form.
7. Confirm the accuracy of the information by selecting "Yes" on the declaration accuracy question.
8. Sign the form with your name as the declarant.
9. Finally, submit the form with the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (id: 1, <type:text>, required: true)
  Your first name is required to identify you as the declarant.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (id: 2, <type:text>, required: true)
  Your last name is required to identify you as the declarant.

### Declaration Date
* **Declaration Date** (id: 3, <type:date>, required: true)
  The date of this declaration.

### National ID Number
* **National ID Number** (id: 4, <type:text>, required: true)
  Your tax ID or national identification number.

### Type of Declaration
* **Type of Declaration** (id: 5, <type:select_one>, required: true)
  Select the type of declaration (Personal Assets, Business Assets, Corporate Assets, Trust Assets, or Estate Assets).

### Real Property Address
* **Real Property Address** (id: 6, <type:text>, required: false)
  Address of real estate owned.

### Type of Real Property
* **Type of Real Property** (id: 7, <type:select_one>, required: false)
  Type of real property (residential, commercial, land, etc.).

### Real Property Value
* **Real Property Value** (id: 8, <type:number>, required: false)
  Current market value of the real property.

### Cash in Bank Accounts
* **Cash in Bank Accounts** (id: 9, <type:number>, required: true)
  Total balance in savings and checking accounts.

### Savings Account Value
* **Savings Account Value** (id: 10, <type:number>, required: true)
  Total savings account balances.

### Stock Investments
* **Stock Investments** (id: 11, <type:number>, required: false)
  Value of stock portfolio.

### Bond Investments
* **Bond Investments** (id: 12, <type:number>, required: false)
  Value of bonds held.

### Mutual Fund Investments
* **Mutual Fund Investments** (id: 13, <type:number>, required: false)
  Value of mutual fund holdings.

### Retirement Account Value
* **Retirement Account Value** (id: 14, <type:number>, required: false)
  401k, IRA, or similar balances.

### Vehicles and Equipment Value
* **Vehicles and Equipment Value** (id: 15, <type:number>, required: false)
  Cars, boats, machinery, etc.

### Jewelry, Artwork, and Collectibles
* **Jewelry, Artwork, and Collectibles** (id: 16, <type:number>, required: false)
  Value of personal collectibles.

### Mortgages and Loans
* **Mortgages and Loans** (id: 17, <type:number>, required: true)
  Total outstanding loan balance.

### Credit Card Debt
* **Credit Card Debt** (id: 18, <type:number>, required: true)
  Total credit card balances.

### Personal Loans Outstanding
* **Personal Loans Outstanding** (id: 19, <type:number>, required: false)
  Other personal loan balances.

### Other Liabilities
* **Other Liabilities** (id: 20, <type:number>, required: false)
  Any other debts or obligations.

### Net Worth
* **Net Worth** (id: 21, <type:number>, required: true)
  Total assets minus total liabilities.

### Description of Additional Assets
* **Description of Additional Assets** (id: 22, <type:text>, required: false)
  Any other significant assets not listed.

### I Declare That the Above Information is Accurate and Complete
* **I Declare That the Above Information is Accurate and Complete** (id: 23, <type:select_one>, required: true)
  Confirm that the provided information is accurate and complete.

### Declarant Signature
* **Declarant Signature** (id: 24, <type:text>, required: true)
  Sign the form with your name as the declarant.

### Submission Date
* **Submission Date** (id: 25, <type:date>, required: true)
  Date declaration is submitted.

This help guide is designed to assist end-users in completing the Asset Declaration Form accurately. It provides a clear and concise explanation of each field, ensuring that users understand the form's purpose and requirements.
