# Tax Preparation Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Preparation Quote Request form is designed to gather essential information from clients to provide an accurate quote for tax preparation services. This form helps our team understand the client's needs and tailor the services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the type of tax return you require from the "Tax Return Type" field (e.g., Individual, Business, Non-resident, Partnership, Corporate, or Trust).
5. Specify the tax year for which you need the services in the "Tax Year" field.
6. Indicate the frequency of your tax returns from the "Tax Return Frequency" field (e.g., Annual, Bi-Annual, Quarterly, Monthly, or As Needed).
7. Choose the level of service you require from the "Service Level" field (e.g., Basic, Deluxe, or Premium).
8. If applicable, specify any additional services you require in the "Additional Services" field.
9. Provide any other frequency or service level information in the "Other Frequency" or "Other Service Level" fields.
10. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
12. Confirm the tax return date and time in the "Confirm Tax Return Date" and "Confirm Tax Return Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`tax_preparation_quote_request_form_1`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`tax_preparation_quote_request_form_2`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Tax Return Type** (`tax_return_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of tax return you require (e.g., Individual, Business, Non-resident, Partnership, Corporate, or Trust).
* **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, number, required: false): Specify the tax year for which you need the services.
* **Tax Return Frequency** (`tax_return_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate the frequency of your tax returns (e.g., Annual, Bi-Annual, Quarterly, Monthly, or As Needed).
* **Service Level** (`service_level`, select_one, required: false): Choose the level of service you require (e.g., Basic, Deluxe, or Premium).
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, text, required: false): If applicable, specify any additional services you require.
* **Other Frequency** (`tax_return_frequency_other`, text, required: false): Provide any other frequency information.
* **Other Service Level** (`service_level_other`, text, required: false): If applicable, specify any other service level information.
* **Other Tax Return Frequency** (`tax_return_frequency_other`, text, required: false): Provide any other tax return frequency information.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Tax Return Date** (`confirm_tax_return_date`, date, required: false): Confirm the tax return date.
* **Confirm Tax Return Time** (`confirm_tax_return_time`, time, required: false): Confirm the tax return time.
