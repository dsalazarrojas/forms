# Tax Filing Extension Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Filing Extension Application Form is a tool used by taxpayers to request an extension for tax filing. This form helps gather the necessary information from the taxpayer to evaluate their application and determine the validity of their claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select one of the available options for "Tax Filing Application" (Tax Filing Extension, Other).
2.  Enter the tax year for which you are applying for an extension.
3.  Choose one of the given options for "Reasons For Extension" (Less Than 6 Months, More Than 6 Months, Less Than 12 Months, More Than 12 Months).
4.  Select your tax filing status (Married, Single, Joint).
5.  Provide your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
6.  Add your signature below.
7.  Sign and confirm that you are the taxpayer by adding your name below "Taxpayer Signature".
8.  Confirm your identification as a tax professional by signing below "Tax Professional Signature".
9.  Enter your name as a tax professional below "Tax Professional Name".
10. Select your tax professional designation (Certified Public Accountant, Chartered Accountant, Certified Financial Accountant).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tax Filing Application** (`tax_filing_application_form`): Select the reason for your tax filing extension application (Tax Filing Extension or Other).
* **Tax Year** (`tax_year`): Enter the tax year for which you are applying for an extension (e.g., 2022).
* **Reasons For Extension** (`reasons_for_extension`): Choose a reason for your tax filing extension application (Less Than 6 Months, More Than 6 Months, Less Than 12 Months, More Than 12 Months).
* **Taxpayer Details** (`taxpayer_details`): Enter your details (optional, but recommended for easier verification).
* **Tax Filing Status** (`tax_filing_status`): Select your tax filing status (Married, Single, Joint).
* **Signature** (`signature`): Add your signature to confirm that you are the taxpayer.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Taxpayer Signature** (`taxpayer_signature`): Sign and confirm that you are the taxpayer.
* **Tax Professional Signature** (`tax_professional_signature`): Sign and confirm that you are a tax professional.
* **Tax Professional Name** (`tax_professional_name`): Enter your name as a tax professional.
* **Tax Professional Designation** (`tax_professional_designation`): Select your tax professional designation (Certified Public Accountant, Chartered Accountant, Certified Financial Accountant).
* **Tax Professional License** (`tax_professional_license`): Enter your tax professional license (optional, but recommended for easier verification).

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and clear information to avoid any delays or rejections in your application.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact our support team for assistance.
* This form is designed to gather the necessary information to evaluate your tax filing extension application. Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
