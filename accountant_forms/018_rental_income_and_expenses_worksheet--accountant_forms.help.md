# Rental Income And Expenses Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Income And Expenses Worksheet is a form used by accountants to track and calculate financial data for rental properties. It is designed to gather all the necessary information to calculate net rental income.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the relevant financial data for the rental property.
2. Enter the rental income earned from the rental property.
3. Enter any other income earned from the rental property.
4. Enter the expenses related to the rental property.
5. Enter any utilities expenses.
6. Enter any other expenses.
7. Calculate the total utilities expenses.
8. Enter the percentage of rent expenses as a part of total expenses.
9. Enter the percentage of other income as a part of total income.
10. Calculate the net rental income.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Rental Income**: Enter the income earned from renting the property. (`1`, `number`, required: false)
    • Enter the total income earned from the rental property, if applicable.
- **Rental Property**: Enter a brief description of the rental property. (`2`, `text`, required: false)
    • Enter a short description of the rental property, e.g., "123 Main St. Apartment".
- **Rent Received**: Enter the income earned from rent. (`3`, `number`, required: false)
    • Enter the total income earned from rent, if applicable.
- **Other Income**: Enter any other income earned from the rental property. (`4`, `number`, required: false)
    • Enter any other income earned from the rental property, e.g., "Parking fees or other rental income".
- **Rent Expenses**: Enter the expenses related to rent. (`5`, `number`, required: false)
    • Enter the total expenses related to rent, if applicable.
- **Other Expenses**: Enter any other expenses related to the rental property. (`6`, `number`, required: false)
    • Enter any other expenses related to the rental property, e.g., "Maintenance or repairs".
- **Utilities Expenses**: Enter the expenses related to utilities. (`7`, `number`, required: false)
    • Enter the total expenses related to utilities, if applicable.
- **Utilities Received**: Enter the income earned from utilities. (`8`, `number`, required: false)
    • Enter the income earned from utilities, if applicable.
- **Utilities Expenses Total**: Enter the total utilities expenses. (`9`, `number`, required: false)
    • The total utilities expenses will be calculated automatically.
- **Rent Expenses Percent**: Enter the percentage of rent expenses as a part of total expenses. (`10`, `number`, required: false)
    • Enter the percentage of rent expenses as a part of total expenses, e.g., "10% of total expenses".
- **Other Income Percent**: Enter the percentage of other income as a part of total income. (`11`, `number`, required: false)
    • Enter the percentage of other income as a part of total income, e.g., "10% of total income".
- **Net Rental Income**: Enter the net rental income. (`12`, `number`, required: false)
    • The net rental income will be calculated automatically.
 
## Tips
- Double-check all fields to ensure all relevant financial data is included.
- Use the calculated net rental income for further analysis or reporting.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the accounting team for assistance.
