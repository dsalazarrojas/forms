# Service Invoice Generator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Invoice Generator form is designed to create professional-looking service invoices for clients. The form collects information about services provided, including date, hours worked, client details, service cost, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields first, such as service date, client name, and service cost.
*   Optional fields can be filled in at your discretion.
*   Use the select options for payment method to choose from available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Service Date** (required, date):
    Enter the date of the service provided. This will be the date on which the service was rendered.
*   **Service Hours** (optional, text):
    Enter the total hours worked on the service. This can be a helpful reference for clients to understand the scope of work provided.
*   **Client Name** (optional, text):
    Enter the name of the client. This can be a helpful reference for your records.
*   **Service Details** (optional, text):
    Enter a brief description of the services provided. This can help clients understand what they paid for.
*   **Service Cost** (required, number):
    Enter the total cost of the service provided. This will be the amount due for the client.
*   **Address** (optional, text):
    Enter the address of the client. This can be a helpful reference for your records.
*   **Client Email** (optional, email):
    Enter the email address of the client. This can be used for communication purposes.
*   **Payment Method** (optional, select_one):
    Choose how the client will pay for the service. Options include 'Yes' or 'No', indicating if payment will be made immediately or not.
*   **Invoice Number** (optional, number):
    Enter a unique invoice number for the service provided. This can be helpful for tracking purposes.
*   **Due Date** (optional, date):
    Enter the due date for payment. This can be the date by which payment is expected.

## Tips
*   Make sure to accurately record all services provided to ensure accurate billing and payment.
*   Use the form as a template to create multiple invoices for different clients and services.
*   Review and revise your entries before finalizing the invoice.
*   Save and store completed invoices for future reference.
