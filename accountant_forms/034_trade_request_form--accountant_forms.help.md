# Trade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a trade request, which is a request for goods or services from a vendor. This form is intended to facilitate internal communication and record-keeping within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the first name field.
2. Enter your last name in the last name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
5. Enter your address in the address field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **last name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **address** (Text, Optional): Enter your address.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* This form is for internal use only, so do not use it to solicit external requests or other non-business purposes.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the relevant internal teams or department for assistance.
