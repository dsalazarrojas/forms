# Auditor Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auditor Estimate Form is a document used to gather essential information about an organization's financial and operational activities. It is designed to be completed by the client, providing details about their business, including the type of audit services required, financial information, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

- Read each field carefully and understand what information is being requested.
- Answer each question accurately and honestly.
- Use the provided hints as a guide to understand what is being asked.
- Do not hesitate to ask for clarification if you are unsure about any field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Estimate Date 
**Estimate Date** (`estimate_date`, `date`, required): This field is used to record the date when this estimate request is made.

* Please enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### 2. Client Name 
**Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to record the name of your organization.

* Please enter the name of your organization.

### 3. Industry Type 
**Industry Type** (`client_industry`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to determine the type of industry your business operates in.

* Select the corresponding option that best describes your industry:
	+ Retail
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Services
	+ Technology
	+ Healthcare
	+ Finance
	+ Non-profit

### 4. Approximate Annual Revenue 
**Approximate Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to estimate the revenue range of your organization.

* Select the corresponding option that best describes your revenue:
	+ Under $500k
	+ $500k - $1M
	+ $1M - $5M
	+ $5M - $10M
	+ $10M - $50M
	+ Over $50M

### 5. Company Size (Employees) 
**Company Size (Employees)** (`company_size`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to determine the number of full-time employees in your organization.

* Select the corresponding option that best describes the number of employees:
	+ 1-10
	+ 11-50
	+ 51-100
	+ 101-250
	+ 251-500
	+ Over 500

### 6. Number of Locations 
**Number of Locations** (`number_of_locations`, `number`, optional): This field is used to record the number of office or facility locations.

* Enter the number of locations.

### 7. Type of Audit Needed 
**Type of Audit Needed** (`audit_type`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to determine the type of audit services required.

* Select the corresponding option that best describes the type of audit required:
	+ Financial Audit
	+ Operational Audit
	+ Compliance Audit
	+ Tax Audit
	+ Internal Audit
	+ Other

### 8. Audit Scope 
**Audit Scope** (`audit_scope`, `text`, required): This field is used to determine the areas to be audited.

* Describe the areas to be audited.

### 9. Fiscal Year Ending Date 
**Fiscal Year Ending Date** (`fiscal_year_ending`, `date`, required): This field is used to record the date when your fiscal year ends.

* Enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### 10. Audit Frequency 
**Audit Frequency** (`audit_frequency`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to determine the frequency of the audit.

* Select the corresponding option that best describes the audit frequency:
	+ Annual
	+ Semi-Annual
	+ Quarterly
	+ Monthly
	+ As Needed
	+ Multiple Year Contract

### 11. Financial Statement Complexity 
**Financial Statement Complexity** (`financial_statement_complexity`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to determine the complexity of the financial operations.

* Select the corresponding option that best describes the complexity:
	+ Simple
	+ Moderate
	+ Complex
	+ Very Complex

### 12. Have You Been Audited Before 
**Have You Been Audited Before** (`prior_audit_experience`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to determine the prior audit experience.

* Select the corresponding option that best describes the prior audit experience:
	+ Yes - Recently
	+ Yes - Several Years Ago
	+ First Time
	+ Yes - Multiple Years

### 13. Required Auditor Certifications 
**Required Auditor Certifications** (`required_certifications`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to determine any required auditor credentials.

* Select any of the options that apply:
	+ CPA
	+ CIA - Internal Audit
	+ CISA - IT Audit
	+ Professional Licensure
	+ No Specific Requirements

### 14. Preferred Audit Timeline 
**Preferred Audit Timeline** (`timeline_preference`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to determine the preferred audit timeline.

* Select the corresponding option that best describes the preferred timeline:
	+ Immediate - Within 30 Days
	+ Flexible
	+ Specific Dates
	+ To Be Determined

### 15. Budget Range for Audit 
**Budget Range for Audit** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to estimate the budget range for audit services.

* Select the corresponding option that best describes the budget range:
	+ Under $5k
	+ $5k - $10k
	+ $10k - $25k
	+ $25k - $50k
	+ $50k - $100k
	+ Over $100k
	+ No Budget Limit

### 16. Additional Services Needed 
**Additional Services Needed** (`additional_services`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to determine any additional audit services required.

* Select any of the options that apply:
	+ Management Letter
	+ Internal Control Review
	+ Fraud Investigation
	+ Tax Recommendations
	+ Systems Review
	+ Training
	+ None

### 17. Accounting Software Used 
**Accounting Software Used** (`software_systems`, `text`, optional): This field is used to record the accounting software used.

* Enter the name of the accounting software.

### 18. Primary Contact Name 
**Primary Contact Name** (`contact_person_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to record the name of the contact person.

* Enter the name of the contact person.

### 19. Contact Person Title 
**Contact Person Title** (`contact_person_title`, `text`, required): This field is used to record the title of the contact person.

* Enter the title of the contact person.

### 20. Contact Email 
**Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): This field is used to record the contact email.

* Enter the email of the contact person.

### 21. Contact Phone 
**Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): This field is used to record the contact phone number.

* Enter the phone number of the contact person.

### 22. Special Requirements or Considerations 
**Special Requirements or Considerations** (`special_requirements`, `text`, optional): This field is used to record any unique needs or constraints.

* Enter any special requirements or considerations.

## Tips

* Please review each field carefully before completing the form.
* Use the provided hints as a guide to understand what is being asked.
* Do not hesitate to ask for clarification if you are unsure about any field.
* The information provided in this form will be used to prepare for the audit, ensure all necessary information is collected.
