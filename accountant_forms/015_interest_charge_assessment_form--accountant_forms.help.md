# Interest Charge Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interest Charge Assessment Form is used to calculate and assess the interest charge on a financial account that has fallen behind on repayments or exceeded its credit limit.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Form Details**: This field is not required, but it is a good idea to provide a brief description of the account and the purpose of the assessment.
- To complete the form, start by filling out the **Form Details** field with any relevant information about the account.
- Next, enter the **Principal Amount** field with the amount of the account that is past due or exceeds the credit limit.
- After that, enter the **Interest Rate** field with the relevant interest rate that applies to the account.
- Provide a clear explanation for the **Reason For Charge** field, stating the cause for the interest charge.
- Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in their respective fields to identify yourself as the person completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Details (optional)**: Enter a brief description of the account and the purpose of the assessment.
- **Principal Amount (number, optional)**: Enter the current balance of the account that is past due or exceeds the credit limit.
- **Interest Rate (number, optional)**: Enter the interest rate that applies to the account.
- **Reason For Charge (text, optional)**: Enter a clear explanation for the interest charge, e.g., "Late payment", "Overdraft".
- **First Name (text, optional)**: Enter your first name.
- **Last Name (text, optional)**: Enter your last name.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date to avoid errors in the interest charge calculation.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, it is best to consult with the relevant account manager or financial advisor.
- Double-check your calculations to ensure that the interest charge is accurately reflected.
- If you need assistance with filling out this form, do not hesitate to contact our support team.
