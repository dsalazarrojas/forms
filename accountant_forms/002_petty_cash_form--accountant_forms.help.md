# Petty Cash Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Petty Cash Form is a tool used by accounting personnel or financial managers to document and track petty cash transactions within an organization. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name in the "Company" field.
2. Enter the account number associated with the transaction in the "Account Number" field.
3. Select the date of the transaction from the calendar picker.
4. Enter the amount of the transaction in the "Amount" field.
5. Provide a brief description of the transaction in the "Description" field.
6. Select the category of the transaction from the "Category" dropdown list.
7. Optionally, you can add additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter the email address of the person responsible for the transaction in the "Email" field.
9. Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the transaction in the "Phone" field.
10. Enter the total amount of the transaction in the "Total Amount" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company** (`Company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company or organization associated with the transaction.
* **Account Number** (`Account Number`, text, optional): Enter the account number associated with the transaction.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): Select the date of the transaction from the calendar picker.
* **Amount** (`Amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the transaction.
* **Description** (`Description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the transaction.
* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of the transaction from the dropdown list.
	+ Select "Cash" for cash transactions.
	+ Select "Travel" for travel-related transactions.
	+ Select "Other" for other types of transactions.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the transaction.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the transaction.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the transaction.
* **Total Amount** (`Total Amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount of the transaction.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, note, optional): This field is not a form field, but rather a confirmation button to submit the form. 

Note: Fields marked as "optional" are not required for form submission.
