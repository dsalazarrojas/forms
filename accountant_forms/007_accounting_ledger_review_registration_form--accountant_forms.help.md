# Accounting Ledger Review Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a ledger review, which is a crucial process in accounting and financial management. The form helps in registering and planning a ledger review, ensuring that all relevant details are captured accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Select the correct options for the type of ledger to review and the scope of the review.
3. Choose the correct priority level and expected outcomes for the review.
4. Provide any special requirements or instructions for conducting the review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the full name of the person conducting the ledger review.
* **Reviewer Employee ID** (`reviewer_employee_id`, text, **Required**): Enter the internal employee identification number of the person conducting the review.
* **Reviewer Department** (`reviewer_department`, text, **Required**): Enter the department the reviewer belongs to.
* **Type of Ledger to Review** (`ledger_type`, select_one, **Required**): Select the type of ledger that requires review from the options provided, such as General Ledger, Accounts Receivable, etc.
* **Ledger Period to Review** (`ledger_period`, text, **Required**): Specify the accounting period for the ledger review.
* **Review Start Date** (`review_start_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date when the ledger review will begin.
* **Expected Review End Date** (`review_end_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date when the ledger review is expected to be completed.
* **Ledger Owner** (`ledger_owner`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the person responsible for maintaining this ledger.
* **Purpose of the Review** (`review_purpose`, select_one, **Required**): Select the reason why this ledger review is being conducted from the options provided, such as Monthly reconciliation, Quarterly audit preparation, etc.
* **Scope of the Review** (`review_scope`, select_multiple, **Required**): Select the aspects of the ledger that will be reviewed from the options provided, such as Account balances, Transaction accuracy, etc.
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, select_one, **Required**): Select the level of urgency for the review, such as Low, Medium, High, or Critical.
* **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, **Optional**): Describe the expected outcomes from this review, if any.
* **Special Requirements or Instructions** (`special_requirements`, text, **Optional**): Provide any specific requirements or instructions for conducting the review, if any.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Double-check the selected options for the type of ledger, scope, and priority level.
* Enter any special requirements or instructions for conducting the review.
* This form is designed to help in registering and planning the ledger review effectively.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact form@gic.mx.
