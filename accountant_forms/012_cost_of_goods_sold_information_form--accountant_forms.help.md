# Cost Of Goods Sold Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed cost of goods sold (COGS) information from businesses for financial reporting and analysis purposes. It's essential to fill this form accurately to ensure correct calculations and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. Complete each section carefully, following the label's instructions.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Business Id (Page 1)
* **Business Id** (`business_id`, text, required): Enter your business's unique identifier.
* Explanation: A unique identifier for your business, typically assigned by the system or organization.

### Business Name (Page 1)
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your business's full name as it appears on your business cards or registration documents.

### COA Accounting Period (Page 1)
* **COA Accounting Period** (`coa_accounting_period`, text, required): Select or enter the accounting period for which you are reporting COGS information.

### Accounting Start Date (Page 1)
* **Accounting Start Date** (`accounting_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the accounting period for which you are reporting COGS information.

### Accounting End Date (Page 2)
* **Accounting End Date** (`accounting_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the accounting period for which you are reporting COGS information. Leave blank if not applicable.

### Inventory Cost Of Goods Sold (Page 2)
* **Inventory Cost Of Goods Sold** (`inventory_cost_of_goods_sold`, number, required): Enter the total cost of goods sold for the inventory.

### Cost Of Goods Sold (Page 2)
* **Cost Of Goods Sold** (`cost_of_goods_sold`, number, required): Enter the total cost of goods sold for the period.

### Total Cost Of Goods Sold (Page 2)
* **Total Cost Of Goods Sold** (`total_cost_of_goods_sold`, number, required): Enter the total cost of goods sold for the period.

### COA Gross Profit (Page 2)
* **COA Gross Profit** (`coa_gross_profit`, number, required): Enter the gross profit for the period.

### COA Net Profit (Page 2)
* **COA Net Profit** (`coa_net_profit`, number, required): Enter the net profit for the period.

### COGS Detail Criteria (Page 3)
* **COGS Detail Criteria** (`cogs_detail_criteria`, text, required): Enter any criteria that may affect COGS calculation, such as material, labor, or overhead costs.

### Sales Income Per Unit (Page 3)
* **Sales Income Per Unit** (`sales_income_per_unit`, number, required): Enter the sales income per unit for the period.

### COA COGS Detail (Page 3)
* **COA COGS Detail** (`coa_cogs_detail`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about COGS for the period.

### COA COGS Detail 2 (Page 4)
* **COA COGS Detail 2** (`coa_cogs_detail_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about COGS for the period.

### COA COGS Detail 3 (Page 4)
* **COA COGS Detail 3** (`coa_cogs_detail_3`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about COGS for the period.

### COA Terms Conditions (Page 4)
* **COA Terms Conditions** (`coa_terms_conditions`, note, required): Enter any terms and conditions that apply to COGS calculation.

### COA Terms 2 (Page 4)
* **COA Terms 2** (`coa_terms_2`, note, optional): Enter any additional terms and conditions that apply to COGS calculation.

### COA Comments (Page 4)
* **COA Comments** (`coa_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about COGS for the period.

Note to end-users: This help guide is not intended to be a substitute for professional financial advice. If you have any doubts or concerns about your business's COGS, please consult your accountant or financial advisor.
