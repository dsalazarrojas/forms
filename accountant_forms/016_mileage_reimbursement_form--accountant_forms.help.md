# Mileage Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for mileage reimbursement claims from employees. It is used to track business-related travel expenses, including trip details and total miles driven.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the start date and start time of your trip in the "Start Date" and "Start Time" fields, respectively.
3. Enter the end date and end time of your trip in the "End Date" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
4. Enter the odometer reading at the start and end of your trip in the "Odometer Reading" field.
5. Enter the fuel price per mile in the "Fuel Price" field.
6. Enter the total miles driven during your trip in the "Total Miles" field.
7. Enter the mileage rate in the "Mileage Rate" field.
8. Calculate or enter the total reimbursement amount using the mileage rate and total miles, or enter the reimbursement amount received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): Enter your employee ID or name so we can track your mileage reimbursement correctly.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, optional): Enter the date of when your trip started.
* **Start Time** (Start Time, time, optional): Enter the time of when your trip started.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, optional): Enter the date of when your trip ended.
* **End Time** (End Time, time, optional): Enter the time of when your trip ended.
* **Odometer Reading** (Odometer Reading, number, optional): Enter the odometer reading at the start and end of your trip.
* **Fuel Price** (Fuel Price, number, optional): Enter the fuel price per mile.
* **Total Miles** (Total Miles, number, optional): Enter the total miles driven during your trip.
* **Mileage Rate** (Mileage Rate, number, optional): Enter your company's approved mileage rate.
* **Reimbursement Amount** (Reimbursement Amount, number, optional): Enter the reimbursement amount received, or calculate using the mileage rate and total miles.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your trip.
