# Account Balance Confirmation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request confirmation of account balances from a financial institution. The information collected will be used to verify the account details and confirm the balance on the specified date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the requestor's information (section 1) with your name, title, company or firm name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the requestor's role from the list provided (internal auditor, external auditor, finance manager, accountant, legal representative, or other).
3. Enter the account holder's name and account number.
4. Select the account type (checking, savings, loan, line of credit, investment, or other).
5. Enter the financial institution's name.
6. Specify the date for which the balance should be confirmed.
7. Select the purpose of the confirmation (annual audit, quarterly review, loan application, legal proceeding, internal reconciliation, or other).
8. Enter the response deadline.
9. Choose the preferred method of response (email, mail, fax, or secure portal upload).
10. Enter any additional instructions or notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Information** (`section_requestor_info`, note): This section collects the information about the person or entity making the request.
* **Requestor Full Name** (`requestor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Title or Position** (`requestor_title`, text, required): Enter your title or position.
* **Company or Firm Name** (`requestor_company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or firm.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Requestor Role** (`requestor_role`, select_one, required): Select your role from the list (internal auditor, external auditor, finance manager, accountant, legal representative, or other).
* **Account Details** (`section_account_details`, note): This section is a header for the account details.
* **Account Holder Name** (`account_holder_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the account holder (individual or business).
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, required): Enter the account number.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of account (checking, savings, loan, line of credit, investment, or other).
* **Financial Institution Name** (`financial_institution`, text, required): Enter the name of the financial institution.
* **Balance Confirmation Date** (`confirmation_date`, date, required): Enter the date for which the balance should be confirmed.
* **Confirmation Details** (`section_confirmation_details`, note): This section collects additional details about the confirmation.
* **Purpose of Confirmation** (`confirmation_purpose`, select_one, required): Select the purpose of confirmation (annual audit, quarterly review, loan application, legal proceeding, internal reconciliation, or other).
* **Response Deadline** (`response_deadline`, date, required): Enter the date by which the confirmation is needed.
* **Preferred Response Method** (`preferred_response_method`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred method of response (email, mail, fax, or secure portal upload).
* **Mailing Address for Response** (`mailing_address`, text, optional): Enter the mailing address for response if applicable.
* **Authorization or Engagement Letter Reference** (`authorization_reference`, text, optional): Enter the reference number of the authorization letter if applicable.
* **Additional Instructions or Notes** (`additional_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any additional instructions or notes if necessary.
