# Tax Professional Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Professional Communication Survey is designed to gather feedback from tax professionals on the communication quality, responsiveness, and clarity of information provided by our team. This survey aims to help us better understand your experiences and needs, which will inform our efforts to improve communication and provide more effective support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start on the "Introduction" page, where you can review the purpose of the survey and any additional instructions.
2. On the "How would you rate the communication quality of our team?" page, select the rating that best reflects your experience with our team. You can choose from 5 options: Very poor, Poor, Neutral, Good, or Excellent.
3. On the "How responsive are we to your phone calls and emails?" page, select the rating that best reflects your experience with our team's responsiveness. You can choose from 5 options: Very unresponsive, Somewhat unresponsive, Neutral, Somewhat responsive, or Very responsive.
4. On the "How clear is the information provided to you?" page, select the rating that best reflects your experience with the information provided by our team. You can choose from 5 options: Very unclear, Somewhat unclear, Neutral, Somewhat clear, or Very clear.
