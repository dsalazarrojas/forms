# Tax And Financial Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax And Financial Service Request Form is designed to capture essential information from clients who require financial services such as accounting, tax filing, or investment planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") completely and accurately.
2. For fields that are not marked as "required", you may leave them blank or fill them out if you have relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person for your business.
* **Financial Service** (`financial_service`, select_one, optional): Select the type of financial service you require (e.g., Accounting and Bookkeeping, Tax Filing, Investments, Financial Planning).
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, optional): Enter the date you require the financial service (optional).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the contact person (not applicable for all financial services, but may be required for tax planning services).
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of your business (e.g., LLC, Corporation, Sole Proprietorship).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your business.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your business location.
* **Zip/Postal Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of your business location.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your business.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for your business.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or information about your financial service request.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, text, optional): If required by the assigned financial service provider, upload a file relevant to your financial service request.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, optional): Select the financial service provider to whom this request will be assigned (e.g., chatjimmy, chatbot, other).
* **Category** (`category`, text, required): Select the category for your financial service request (e.g., Accounting, Tax, Investments).
* **Form 1-10** (`form_id_1`-`form_id_10`, number, optional): Enter the form ID of the relevant financial service or product.

**Tips**

* Ensure that you are submitting a complete and accurate request for financial services.
* If you are unsure about any information or fields, please contact the assigned financial service provider for assistance.
* This form is intended for business-related services and should not be used for personal services.
