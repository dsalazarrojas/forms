# Music Royalty Audit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Royalty Audit Request Form is a tool for artists, producers, and rights holders to request a royalty audit from a music distributor, publisher, or other music industry entity.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read through each field and provide accurate, up-to-date information.
- Ensure that all fields are filled out thoroughly, including any optional fields.
- Double-check for any duplicate or incomplete submissions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data (1)** (`Form Data`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter a brief description of the purpose and scope of the royalty audit request.
* **Artist Info (2)** (`Artist Info`, `text`, required/optional: false): Provide details about the artist or rights holder requesting the audit, including name, contact information, and any other relevant information.
* **Audit Info (3)** (`Audit Info`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter specific details about the audit, such as the date range, account period, and any other relevant information.
* **Attachments (4)** (`Attachments`, `text`, required/optional: false): Upload any supporting documents or files related to the audit, such as contracts, invoices, or reports.
* **Tracking Info (5)** (`Tracking Info`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter any tracking numbers or reference numbers associated with the audit, such as tracking IDs or account codes.
* **Notes (6)** (`Notes`, `text`, required/optional: false): Provide any additional comments or context related to the audit request.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled out thoroughly.
- Double-check for accuracy and completeness.
