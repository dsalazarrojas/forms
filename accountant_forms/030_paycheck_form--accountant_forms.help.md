# paycheck form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's paycheck, including their employment status, employer details, salary, tax, date of payment, time of payment, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form title to access the form pages.
2. On the first page, select the relevant options for "Employee Status" (Yes/No) and choose the correct "Employer" (ABC Company, DEF Company, or GHI Company).
3. Enter the "Salary" and "Tax" values in their respective fields. These fields expect numerical values.
4. On the second page, select the "Paycheck Form Date" using the date picker.
5. Choose the "Paycheck Form Time" using the time picker.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Paycheck Form Note" field.
7. Finally, provide your "Email" and "Phone" contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Status** (`paycheck_form_employee`, Select_Multiple, **Yes/No**): Indicates whether the employee has received a paycheck or not.
* **Employer** (`paycheck_form_employer`, Select_One, **Company Name**): Choose the correct employer from the provided options.
* **Salary** (`paycheck_form_salary`, Number): Enter the employee's salary.
* **Tax** (`paycheck_form_tax`, Number): Enter the tax amount.
* **Date** (`paycheck_form_date`, Date): Select the date of payment using the date picker.
* **Time** (`paycheck_form_time`, Time): Choose the time of payment using the time picker.
* **Note** (`paycheck_form_note`, Note): Provide any additional comments or notes about the paycheck.
* **Email** (`paycheck_form_email`, Email): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`paycheck_form_phone`, Text): Enter your contact phone number.
