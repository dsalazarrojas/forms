# Dependent Tax Credit Eligibility Intake - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dependent Tax Credit Eligibility Intake form is designed to collect information from individuals or their representatives to determine eligibility for tax credits related to dependents. This form will help you provide relevant details to assess your eligibility for tax credits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. On the first page, provide the details of the dependent for whom you are claiming the tax credit. This is the "dependent_details" page.
2. On the second page, provide a brief summary of your credit eligibility status. This is the "credit_eligibility" page.
3. On the final page, provide any additional submission details. This is the "submission_details" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dependent Details** (`dependent_details`, text, optional): Please provide the name, age, and relationship of the dependent person for whom you are claiming the tax credit. This information will help us understand the context of your claim.
* **Credit Eligibility Status** (`credit_eligibility`, text, optional): Briefly describe your current credit eligibility status for the dependent. This will help us understand your situation and make a more accurate assessment.
* **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, text, optional): If you have any additional information or comments about your submission, please provide them here. This will help us process your claim more efficiently.
