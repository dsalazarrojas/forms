# vat_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vat_registration_form is used to collect information from vendors for VAT purposes. 
The form is used to identify a vendor and capture their tax information for accounting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Fill out each field carefully and accurately.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tax Identification** (`tax_identification`, text, required/optional): Enter your tax identification number.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your business name as it appears on your tax documents.
* **Vat Number** (`vat_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your VAT number as issued by the relevant tax authority.
* **Vat Percentage** (`vat_percentage`, number, required/optional): Enter the applicable VAT percentage for your business.
* **Vat Date** (`vat_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you were registered for VAT.
* **Vat Address** (`vat_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your business address as it appears on your tax documents.
* **Vat Phone** (`vat_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your business phone number as it appears on your tax documents.
* **Vat Email** (`vat_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your business email address as it appears on your tax documents.
* **Vat Notes** (`vat_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your VAT registration.

Note: Some fields are optional, but they are all required for accurate VAT registration purposes.
