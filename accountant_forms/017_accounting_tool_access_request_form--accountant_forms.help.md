# Accounting Tool Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accounting Tool Access Request Form is designed to collect information from employees requesting access to various accounting tools and systems within the organization. This form ensures that employees have the necessary access to perform their job duties effectively while maintaining the security and integrity of the organization's financial data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your name, employee ID, and department.
2. Specify the tool or system you are requesting access to and select the required access level.
3. Provide a business justification for why you need this access.
4. Choose the duration of access you need and select a start date for when you want to begin using the tool.
5. If applicable, list specific modules or functions within the tool that you require.
6. Confirm that you have completed any required training for the tool.
7. Acknowledge that you will follow all security policies for the tool.
8. Get your manager's approval for this access request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge or business card.
* **Requester Employee ID** (`requester_employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID as assigned to you by HR.
* **Requester Department** (`requester_department`, text, required): Type in the department you belong to.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor who will approve this request.
* **Tool or System Requested** (`tool_requested`, select_one, required): Choose the specific accounting tool or system you are requesting access to from the list provided.
* **Required Access Level** (`access_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of access you need for the tool, which may be "Read-only", "View and Print", "Data Entry", "Edit and Modify", "Approve Transactions", or "Administrative".
* **Business Justification** (`business_justification`, text, required): Clearly explain why you need access to this tool for your role.
* **Duration of Access Needed** (`duration_of_access`, select_one, required): Choose how long you need access to the tool, which may be "Temporary - Less than 1 month", "Short-term - 1-6 months", "Long-term - 6-12 months", "Permanent - Ongoing basis", or "Project-based - Until project completion".
* **Requested Access Start Date** (`requested_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you want to begin using the tool.
* **Specific Modules or Functions Needed** (`specific_modules_needed`, text, optional): If you need access to specific modules or functions within the tool, type them in here.
* **Has Required Training Been Completed** (`training_completed`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have completed any required training for the tool.
* **Security Policy Acknowledgment** (`security_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Confirm that you will follow all security policies for the tool.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required): Get your manager's approval for this access request.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide clear and concise answers for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your manager or supervisor for guidance.
* If you need help with completing this form, contact your IT department or accounting team.
