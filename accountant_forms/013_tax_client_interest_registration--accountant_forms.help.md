# Tax Client Interest Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Client Interest Registration form is designed to collect information from potential clients interested in tax services, allowing us to assess their interest and provide them with relevant information and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and address in the respective fields.
2. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select the services you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
4. Choose your preferred language for communication.
5. Indicate how you found out about our company.
6. Select a date and time of interest for the tax services.
7. Choose the tax year you are interested in.
8. Add any additional information you would like to share with us.
9. Read and agree to our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and other legal documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Please enter your address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Services of Interest** (`services_of_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select the services you are interested in (e.g., Tax Return Preparation, Tax Planning, Tax Representation).
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred language for communication.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_multiple, required): Indicate how you found out about our company (e.g., Google Search, Referral, Online Ads, Facebook, Other).
* **Date of Interest** (`date_of_interest`, date, required): Select a date and time you are interested in for the tax services.
* **Tax Year of Interest** (`tax_year_of_interest`, number, required): Choose the tax year you are interested in.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, required): Add any additional information you would like to share with us.
* **Consent to Terms** (`consent_to_terms`, select_one, required): Read and agree to our Terms of Service.
* **Terms of Service** (`terms_of_service`, select_multiple, required): Read and agree to our Terms of Service.
* **Privacy Policy** (`privacy_policy`, select_one, required): Read and agree to our Privacy Policy.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, required): Read and agree to our Agreement.
* **Review** (`review`, select_one, required): Please review our terms and conditions.

Note: All fields are required unless otherwise specified. Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
