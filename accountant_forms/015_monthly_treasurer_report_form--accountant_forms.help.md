# Monthly Treasurer Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for accountants to report monthly financial transactions, including expenses and income, to keep track of the financial balance. The form is used to record and summarize financial data for the month.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the month for which you are reporting.
2. Select or enter the category of the transaction (e.g., "Rent", "Salaries", etc.).
3. Enter the amount of expense or income for the transaction.
4. Provide a description of the expense or income.
5. Enter the current balance of the account.
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date (1)**: Enter the date (month and year) for which you are reporting. Please use the format `mm/dd/yyyy` (e.g., 07/01/2024).
* **Category (2)**: Select the category of the transaction, such as "Office Expenses" or "Sales Revenue". This helps track and organize your financial data.
* **Expense Amount (3)**: Enter the total amount of expenses for the month. This includes any deductions or reductions from revenue.
* **Income Amount (4)**: Enter the total amount of income for the month.
* **Expense Description (5)**: Briefly describe the expense, such as a purchase or service rendered.
* **Income Description (6)**: Briefly describe the income, such as a sale or service provided.
* **Balance (7)**: Enter the current balance of the account, including any outstanding expenses or income.
* **Submit (8)**: Review and submit your report to update the financial records.
