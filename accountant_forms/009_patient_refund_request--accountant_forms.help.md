# Patient Refund Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a refund from a patient's account for medical services rendered.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Reason** field, describing the reason for the refund request.
2. Enter the **Date of Service**, which is the date the medical services were provided.
3. Fill in the **Amount** field with the total amount of the refund being requested.
4. Select the **Payment Method** used by the patient to make the original payment.
5. If the payment method was not one of the listed options, fill in **Other Payment Method** field with the actual payment method used.
6. Fill in the **Patient Name** field to identify the patient account holder.
7. Enter the **Account Number** of the patient's account.
8. Fill in the **Refund Date**, which is the date the refund will be applied to the account.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reason**: A brief description of the reason for the refund request.
* **Date of Service**: The date when the medical services were provided to the patient.
* **Amount**: The total amount of the refund being requested.
* **Payment Method**: The method used by the patient to make the original payment. Options include:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Check
	+ Other (if not one of the listed options)
* **Other Payment Method**: If the payment method was not one of the listed options, enter the actual payment method used.
* **Patient Name**: The name of the patient whose account is being refunded.
* **Account Number**: The account number of the patient's account.
* **Refund Date**: The date the refund will be applied to the account.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check the **Amount** field to ensure it is accurate.
* If the **Payment Method** is **Other**, provide a clear description of the actual payment method used.
