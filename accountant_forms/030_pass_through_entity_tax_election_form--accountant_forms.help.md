# Pass Through Entity Tax Election Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pass Through Entity Tax Election Form is used to elect to be treated as a pass-through entity for tax purposes. This is a critical form for entities that are required to file Form 1120S, such as S corporations and their owners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the state in which you are filing the election.
2. Select the tax year for which you are filing the election.
3. Provide your name and contact information.
4. Provide any additional information required by your state or tax authority.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **state** (Required): Select the state in which you are filing the election. This will help us determine the applicable tax laws for your entity.
* **tax_year** (Required): Select the tax year for which you are filing the election. This will help us ensure that the election is filed on time and in the correct year.
* **name**: Enter your name and contact information, including your address, phone number, and email address. This will help us contact you with any questions or follow-up information.
* **address**: Enter your contact address. This will help us send you any additional information or follow-up materials related to your election.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number. This will help us contact you with any questions or follow-up information.
* **email**: Enter your email address. This will help us send you any additional information or follow-up materials related to your election.
* **state_election_form**: This field is not currently used in the form.
