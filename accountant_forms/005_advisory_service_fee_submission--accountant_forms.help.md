# Advisory Service Fee Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advisory Service Fee Submission form is used to record and submit advisory service fees for clients. This form captures essential details about the client, advisor, and services provided, allowing for accurate invoicing and payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's information, including their name, company name, and email address.
2. Provide the advisor's information, including their name and firm name.
3. Specify the services provided, including the start and end dates of the service period, a detailed description of the services, and the category of the advisory service.
4. Calculate and enter the total amount due, including any hourly rate or flat fee amount.
5. Select the payment terms and add any additional invoice notes.
6. Sign and submit the form electronically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Client Information**
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client receiving advisory services.
* **Client Company**: Enter the name of the company the client represents.
* **Client Email Address**: Enter the primary email address for invoice delivery and communication.
* **Client Phone Number**: Enter the primary phone number for the client.
* **Billing Address**: Enter the complete billing address for the client.

### **Advisor Information**
* **Advisor Name**: Enter the name of the advisor providing services.
* **Advisor Firm**: Enter the name of the firm the advisor represents.
* **Advisor ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the advisor.

### **Service Details**
* **Service Period Start Date**: Enter the beginning date of the advisory service period.
* **Service Period End Date**: Enter the ending date of the advisory service period.
* **Description of Services Provided**: Enter a detailed description of the advisory services rendered.
* **Category of Advisory Service**: Select the type of advisory service provided (e.g., Strategic Planning, Financial Consulting, etc.).

### **Billing Details**
* **Hourly Rate (USD)**: Enter the hourly rate for advisory services (optional).
* **Total Hours Worked**: Enter the total number of hours spent on advisory services (optional).
* **Flat Fee Amount (USD)**: Enter a fixed fee amount if applicable (instead of hourly rate).
* **Total Amount Due (USD)**: This amount will be calculated based on the hourly rate or flat fee amount and hours worked/fee amount.

### **Payment Terms and Additional Notes**
* **Payment Terms**: Select the terms for payment of the advisory fees (e.g., Net 15 days, Net 30 days, etc.).
* **Additional Invoice Notes**: Enter any additional notes for the invoice.

### **Approval and Signature**
* **Advisor Signature**: Enter the electronic signature of the advisor.

### **Expense Reimbursements**
* **Expense Reimbursements (USD)**: Enter the total amount for expense reimbursements (optional).

Note: The form requires a valid electronic signature from the advisor to ensure authenticity and compliance with regulatory requirements.
