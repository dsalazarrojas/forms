# Cash Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cash Audit Form is used to ensure accuracy and accountability in cash handling. It serves as a comprehensive tool for auditors to conduct thorough audits and identify potential discrepancies.

## How To Complete This Form
### To complete the Cash Audit Form:

1. Enter the required information in the relevant fields.
2. Review and verify the accuracy of the information provided.
3. Check for any discrepancies or issues that require further investigation.
4. Record any discrepancies or issues found and provide a clear explanation.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm completion and review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Audit ID Number** (id: 2, required: false): A unique reference number for the audit.
* **Audit Date** (id: 3, required: true): The date of the audit.
* **Audit Time** (id: 4, required: false): The start time of the audit.
* **Location Information** (id: 5, required: false): A note describing where the audit occurs.
* **Location or Branch Name** (id: 6, required: true): The name of the facility being audited.
* **Department** (id: 7, required: true): The department being audited.
* **Register or Till Number** (id: 8, required: false): A specific register or till being audited.
* **Auditor Information** (id: 9, required: false): A note describing the auditor's information.
* **Auditor Name** (id: 10, required: true): The full name of the auditor.
* **Auditor Title** (id: 11, required: true): The job title of the auditor.
* **Witness Name** (id: 12, required: false): If applicable, the full name of the witness.
* **Cashier Information** (id: 13, required: false): A note describing the person responsible for the cash handling.
* **Cashier or Custodian Name** (id: 14, required: true): The name of the person responsible for the cash handling.
* **Cashier ID Number** (id: 15, required: false): The employee number of the cashier or custodian.
* **Shift Time** (id: 16, required: false): The time period of the shift being audited.
* **Type of Audit** (id: 17, required: true): A select-one question indicating the type of audit.
*   - **Scheduled audit**: An expected audit.
*   - **Random audit**: Unexpected audit.
*   - **Surprise audit**: Unexpected audit.
*   - **Shift change audit**: Audit performed during a shift change.
*   - **Incident investigation**: Audit of a specific incident.
*   - **Closing audit**: Last audit of the day or week.
*   - **Opening audit**: First audit of the day or week.
* **Total Cash Counted** (id: 37, required: true): A physical count of the cash present.
* **Expected Amount per Records** (id: 38, required: true): The expected total from system records.
* **Variance Amount** (id: 39, required: true): The difference between the physical count and system records.
* **Variance Type** (id: 40, required: true): A select-one question indicating the type of variance.
*   - **No variance - balanced**: No difference found.
*   - **Over - cash excess**: More cash is present than expected.
*   - **Under - cash shortage**: Less cash is present than expected.
* **Variance Percentage** (id: 41, required: false): The percentage difference between the physical count and system records.
* **Explanation for Variance** (id: 42, required: false): A text field explaining the variance.
* **Transactions Review** (id: 43, required: false): A note describing the review of transactions.
* **Total Sales Recorded** (id: 44, required: false): A total of sales recorded.
* **Total Refunds Processed** (id: 45, required: false): A total of refunds processed.
* **Total Voids Processed** (id: 46, required: false): A total of voids processed.
* **Void Count** (id: 47, required: false): A count of voids processed.
* **Discrepancies Found** (id: 48, required: true): A select_multiple question indicating the issues found.
*   - **None**: No issues found.
*   - **Cash shortage**: Less cash is present than expected.
*   - **Cash overage**: More cash is present than expected.
*   - **Missing receipts**: Receipts are missing.
*   - **Unauthorized voids**: Unauthorized voids have been made.
*   - **Unauthorized refunds**: Unauthorized refunds have been made.
*   - **Counterfeit bills**: Counterfeit bills are present.
*   - **Check issues**: Check issues have been found.
*   - **Documentation errors**: Documentation errors have been found.
*   - **Other**: Other issues have been found.
* **Describe Discrepancies** (id: 49, required: false): A text field explaining the discrepancies found.
* **Compliance Check** (id: 50, required: false): A note describing the compliance status.
* **Were Cash Handling Procedures Followed** (id: 51, required: true): A select-one question indicating compliance.
*   - **Yes - fully compliant**: Procedures were followed correctly.
*   - **Mostly - minor issues**: Some minor issues were found.
*   - **Partially - some issues**: Some procedures were not followed.
*   - **No - significant issues**: Significant procedures were not followed.
* **Security Measures in Place** (id: 52, required: false): A select_multiple question indicating the safety protocols in place.
*   - **Safe secured**: Safe and secure environment.
*   - **Register locked**: Register is locked.
*   - **Limited access**: Limited access to cash handling.
*   - **Surveillance active**: Surveillance is active.
*   - **Two-person rule**: Two-person rule is in place.
*   - **Drop procedures followed**: Drop procedures are followed.
*   - **Other**: Other safety measures are in place.
* **Recommendations** (id: 53, required: false): A text field for improvements needed.
* **Is Follow-Up Required** (id: 54, required: true): A select-one question indicating follow-up required.
*   - **No - audit complete**: Audit is complete.
*   - **Yes - minor follow-up**: Minor follow-up required.
*   - **Yes - investigation needed**: Investigation is needed.
*   - **Yes - retraining required**: Retraining is required.
* **Auditor Signature Date** (id: 55, required: true): The date the auditor signs.
* **Cashier Signature Date** (id: 56, required: false): The date the cashier signs.
* **Manager Review** (id: 57, required: false): A select-one question indicating manager's approval.
*   - **Reviewed and approved**: Audit is reviewed and approved.
*   - **Reviewed with comments**: Audit is reviewed with comments.
*   - **Pending review**: Review is pending.
*   - **Escalated**: Audit is escalated.
* **Manager Signature Date** (id: 58, required: false): The date the manager reviews.

### Tips
* Ensure accuracy and attention to detail when completing the form.
* Verify the physical count of cash with the system records.
* Document all discrepancies or issues found and provide clear explanations.
* Check for any potential security measures that may need to be taken.
* Follow up on any recommended improvements or actions required.
