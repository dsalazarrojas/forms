# Invoice Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invoice Request Form is used to collect contact and payment information from customers for invoice requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Contact Information** in the first field.
2. Provide **Order Information** in the second field.
3. If you'd like to be contacted via a specific method, select your preferred **Contact Method** from the third field.
4. Enter your **Email Address** in the fourth field, if applicable.
5. Enter your **Phone Number** in the fifth field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name and other contact details.
* **Order Information**: Provide details about the order you're requesting an invoice for.
* **Contact Method**: Select how you'd like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Text).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
