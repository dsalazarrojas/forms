# Form 4868 Tax Extension Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Form 4868 Tax Extension Form is used to submit a request for an extension on tax filing. This form collects taxpayer information, address, and contact details required for the extension process.

## How To Complete This Form

- **Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information and documentation required for the extension.**
- **Fill in your taxpayer identification details, such as name, address, and contact information (fields 16-23).**
- **Select the correct tax filing status (field 3).**
- **Enter a valid reason for the tax extension (field 4).**
- **Specify the date for the tax extension (field 5).**
- **Enter the address where you can be reached for tax purposes (fields 6-8).**
- **Upload any supporting documents required for the extension (field 12).**
- **Agree to the terms and conditions of the tax extension (field 15).**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Tax Filing Status** (`tax_filing_status`, select_one, required): Select the correct tax filing status.
* **2. Reason for Tax Extension** (`tax_extension_reason`, text, required): Enter a valid reason for the tax extension.
* **3. Tax Extension Date** (`tax_extension_date`, date, required): Specify the date for the tax extension.
* **4. Tax Extension Reason Description** (`tax_extension_reason_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for tax extension.
* **5. Supporting Document** (`supporting_document`, text, optional): Upload any supporting documents required for the extension.
* **6. Tax Extension Agreement** (`tax_extension_agreement`, text, required): Agree to the terms and conditions of the tax extension.
* **7. Tax Filing Address** (`tax_filing_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where you can be reached for tax purposes.
* **8. Tax Filing City** (`tax_filing_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you can be reached for tax purposes.
* **9. Tax Filing State** (`tax_filing_state`, text, optional): Enter the state where you can be reached for tax purposes.
* **10. Tax Filing Zip** (`tax_filing_zip`, number, optional): Enter the zip code where you can be reached for tax purposes.
* **11. Tax Filing Country** (`tax_filing_country`, text, optional): Enter the country where you can be reached for tax purposes.
* **12. Taxpayer Name** (`taxpayer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **13. Taxpayer Address** (`taxpayer_address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **14. Taxpayer Street** (`taxpayer_street`, text, optional): Enter your street.
* **15. Taxpayer City** (`taxpayer_city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **16. Taxpayer State** (`taxpayer_state`, text, optional): Enter your state.
* **17. Taxpayer Zip** (`taxpayer_zip`, number, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **18. Taxpayer Country** (`taxpayer_country`, text, optional): Enter your country.
* **19. Taxpayer Phone** (`taxpayer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **20. Taxpayer Email** (`taxpayer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **21. Tax Extension Submit** (`tax_extension_submit`, text, required): Submit the tax extension request.

Note: This form collects various information required for tax extension purposes. Ensure to review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any potential issues during the process.
