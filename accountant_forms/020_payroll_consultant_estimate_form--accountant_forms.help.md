# Payroll Consultant Estimate Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used by payroll consultants to create an estimate for their services, including hours worked and fees charged. It is used to gather information about the consultant's services, including the number of employees being served, hours worked, and fees charged. The form will help to generate an accurate estimate of the total cost for the consultant's services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct options for each field to ensure accurate estimates.
2. Provide accurate information about the number of employees being served, hours worked, and fees charged.
3. Choose the correct service rate and currency for the estimates.
4. Provide a description of the services performed.
5. Select the correct estimates and consultant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Employee Count
* **Employee Count** (`number`, `required`): Enter the total number of employees being served.

### 2. Hours Worked
* **Hours Worked** (`number`, `required`): Enter the total hours worked by the payroll consultant.

### 3. Fees
* **Fees** (`number`, `required`): Enter the total fees charged for the services.

### 4. Consultant Name
* **Consultant Name** (`text`, `required`): Enter the name of the payroll consultant.

### 5. Email
* **Email** (`email`, `required`): Enter the email address of the consultant.

### 6. Phone
* **Phone** (`text`, `required`): Enter the phone number of the consultant.

### 7. Comments
* **Comments** (`note`, `not required`): Provide any additional comments about the services performed.

### 8. Estimates
* **Estimates** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Select the services included in the estimate, such as Accounting and Bookkeeping, Payroll Consulting, and more.

### 9. Form Comments
* **Form Comments** (`note`, `not required`): Provide any additional comments about the form.

### 10. Submit
* **Submit** (`select_one`, `required`): Confirm that the estimate is complete and accurate.

### 11. Employee Details
* **Employee Details** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Select the type of employee being served, such as Accountant, Contractor, and more.

### 12. Service Rate
* **Service Rate** (`number`, `required`): Enter the service rate for the consultant's services.

### 13. Currency
* **Currency** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the currency for the estimates, such as USD, CAD, or EUR.

### 14. Service Description
* **Service Description** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Select whether the services include a description.

### 15. Rate
* **Rate** (`number`, `required`): Enter the rate for the consultant's services.

### 16. Total Hours
* **Total Hours** (`number`, `required`): Enter the total hours worked by the consultant.

### 17. Consultant Service Hours
* **Consultant Service Hours** (`number`, `required`): Enter the total service hours worked by the consultant.

### 18. Payroll Consultant Service Rate
* **Payroll Consultant Service Rate** (`number`, `required`): Enter the service rate for the payroll consultant's services.

### 19. Total Amount
* **Total Amount** (`number`, `required`): Enter the total amount for the services.

### 20. Consultant Service Amount
* **Consultant Service Amount** (`number`, `required`): Enter the total service amount for the consultant.

### 21. Payroll Consultant Estimates 2
* **Payroll Consultant Estimates 2** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Select the services included in the second estimate.

### 22. Estimate Form 6
* **Estimate Form 6** (`note`, `not required`): Provide any additional comments.

### 23. Form Comments 2
* **Form Comments 2** (`note`, `not required`): Provide any additional comments.

### 24. Submit 2
* **Submit 2** (`select_one`, `required`): Confirm that the second estimate is complete and accurate.

### 25. Estimate Form 7
* **Estimate Form 7** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Select the type of employee being served.

# Tips

- Ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure an accurate estimate.
- Use the correct service rate and currency for the estimates.
- Select the correct services included in the estimates.
- Review and confirm the estimate before submitting it.
