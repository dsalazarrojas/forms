# Accounting Technology Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accounting Technology Survey is designed to gather information about the current state and challenges of accounting technology in organizations. This survey helps to understand the adoption and usage of various accounting software and tools, the level of satisfaction with current accounting software, and the top technology priorities for investment.

## How To Complete This Form
- Take a few minutes to read each question carefully and select the most accurate answer.
- Please select only one option for multiple-choice questions.
- If you need more time or have additional comments, feel free to provide them in the "Additional Feedback" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Role in Accounting** (`respondent_role`, select_one): Select your primary role in the accounting field, such as Staff Accountant, Senior Accountant, Manager, Director, CFO, IT Specialist, Student, or Other.
* **Size of Your Organization** (`organization_size`, select_one): Choose the approximate number of employees in your organization, such as 1-10 employees, 11-50 employees, 51-200 employees, 201-500 employees, 501-1000 employees, or Over 1000 employees.
* **Primary Accounting Software Used** (`primary_accounting_software`, select_one): Select the accounting software your organization primarily uses, such as QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, Xero, Sage, SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics, FreshBooks, Wave, or Other.
* **Satisfaction with Current Accounting Software** (`software_satisfaction`, select_one): Rate your level of satisfaction with your current accounting software, such as Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **Automation Tools Currently Used** (`automation_tools_used`, select_multiple): Select all the automation tools your accounting team currently uses, such as Robotic Process Automation (RPA), Automated bank reconciliations, Invoice automation, Expense management automation, Payroll automation, Tax preparation software, None, or Other.
* **Level of Cloud Adoption** (`cloud_adoption_level`, select_one): Choose how extensively your organization uses cloud-based accounting solutions, such as No cloud adoption, Limited cloud use, Partial cloud adoption, Mostly cloud-based, or Fully cloud-based.
* **Data Analytics Tools Used** (`data_analytics_tools`, select_one): Select the data analytics tools your team uses for financial analysis, such as Excel only, Excel with Power Query/Power Pivot, Tableau, Power BI, QlikView, SAS, Python/R, None, or Other.
* **Cybersecurity Measures Implemented** (`cybersecurity_measures`, select_multiple): Select all the cybersecurity measures in place for accounting data, such as Two-factor authentication, Role-based access controls, Regular security audits, Data encryption, VPN access, Employee training, None, or Other.
* **Biggest Technology Challenges** (`technology_challenges`, select_multiple): Select the top technology challenges your accounting team faces, such as Integration between systems, Data security concerns, Training and adoption, Cost of technology, Keeping up with updates, Lack of IT support, Resistance to change, Data accuracy.
* **Adoption of AI/ML Technologies** (`ai_ml_adoption`, select_one): Choose how extensive your organization's adoption of artificial intelligence or machine learning technologies is, such as Yes, extensively, Yes, in limited applications, Currently evaluating, No, but planning to adopt, No, not interested.
* **Percentage of Budget Allocated to Technology** (`technology_budget_percentage`, number): Enter the approximate percentage of your accounting budget that goes to technology.
* **Future Technology Priorities** (`future_technology_priorities`, select_multiple): Select the top technology priorities for investment, such as Cloud migration, Process automation, Data analytics capabilities, Enhanced security, Mobile accessibility, AI/ML implementation, Integration improvements, Reporting tools.
* **Additional Feedback on Accounting Technology** (`additional_technology_feedback`, text): Provide any other thoughts or comments about accounting technology.
