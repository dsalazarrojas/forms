# Disbursement Correction Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Disbursement Correction Form is designed to correct errors in disbursement amounts. This form is used to provide a revised disbursement amount for processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields to provide accurate and correct information for processing.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and the form is complete before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Disbursement Amount**: Enter the corrected disbursement amount. (~ 1)
	+ This field is used to enter the new disbursement amount that needs to be processed.
* **Correct Amount**: Enter the correct disbursement amount. (~ 2)
	+ This field is used to enter the amount that was previously reported incorrectly.
* **Date Disbursed**: Enter the date the disbursement was made. (~ 3)
	+ This field is used to specify the date the disbursement was made.
* **Reason for Correction**: Enter a brief description of why the disbursement amount was incorrect. (~ 4)
	+ This field is used to provide a clear explanation for the correction.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the email address of the user who initially submitted the disbursement. (~ 5)
	+ This field is used to track the person who submitted the original disbursement.
* **Corrected By**: Enter the email address of the person who is correcting the disbursement. (~ 6)
	+ This field is used to track the person who is making the correction.
* **Reason for Submission**: Enter a brief description of why this correction is being made. (~ 7)
	+ This field is used to provide a clear explanation for the correction.
* **Disbursed Date**: Enter the date the disbursement was made. (~ 8)
	+ This field is used to specify the date the disbursement was made.
* **Account Number**: Enter the account number associated with the disbursement. (~ 9)
	+ This field is used to identify the account related to the disbursement.

## Tips

* Make sure to review all fields before submitting the form to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Double-check the fields for correctness to avoid any discrepancies in the account records.
