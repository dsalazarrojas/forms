# Payment Withdrawal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Payment Withdrawal Form is used to submit a payment withdrawal request to the bank.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Basic Information** section with your basic details.
2.  Enter your **Account Information** in the respective fields.
3.  Provide **Payment Information**, including the payment type and status.
4.  Select a payment method (**Online** or **Offline**).
5.  Enter the **Transaction Date** and **Transaction Time**.
6.  Enter the **Payment Amount**.
7.  Enter your **Account Number**, **Account Holder's Name**, and **Account Holder's Email**.
8.  Enter the **Account Holder's Address** and **Account Holder's Phone**.
9.  Upload any additional files required for the payment withdrawal request.
10. Select a **Payment Status** (**Active** or **Inactive**).
11. Enter a **Note** if necessary.
12. Click the **Submit** button to submit your payment withdrawal request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Basic Information** (id: 1): This section contains the basic information about the account holder. Enter your name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Account Information** (id: 2): Enter the account number and other account-related details.
* **Payment Information** (id: 3): This section contains the payment information, including the payment type and status.
* **Upload Information** (id: 4): Upload any additional files required for the payment withdrawal request.
* **Payment Type** (id: 6): Select the payment type (**Credit** or **Debit**).
* **Payment Status** (id: 7): Select the payment status (**Active** or **Inactive**).
* **Transaction Date** (id: 8): Enter the date of the transaction.
* **Transaction Time** (id: 9): Enter the time of the transaction.
* **Payment Amount** (id: 10): Enter the payment amount.
* **Account Number** (id: 11): Enter the account number.
* **Account Holder's Name** (id: 12): Enter the account holder's name.
* **Account Holder's Email** (id: 22): Enter the account holder's email address.
* **Account Holder's Phone** (id: 23): Enter the account holder's phone number.
* **Account Holder's Address** (id: 24): Enter the account holder's address.
* **Bank Name** (id: 14): Enter the bank name.
* **Transaction ID** (id: 15): Enter the transaction ID.
* **Upload File** (id: 16): Upload any additional files required for the payment withdrawal request.
* **Upload File Alt** (id: 17): Upload any additional files required for the payment withdrawal request.
* **Payment Method** (id: 18): Select the payment method (**Online** or **Offline**).
* **Payment Date** (id: 19): Enter the payment date.
* **Payment Status Alt** (id: 21): Select the payment status (**Active** or **Inactive**).
* **Account Holder's Address Alt** (id: 24): Enter the account holder's address.
* **Note** (id: 25): Enter any additional notes about the payment withdrawal request.
* **Submit** (id: 20): Click the submit button to submit your payment withdrawal request.
