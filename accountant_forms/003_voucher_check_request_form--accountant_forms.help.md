# voucher_check_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The voucher check request form is designed to capture the necessary information for requesting a voucher check for business expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your Business Name in the first field.
2. Select the correct Date Range in the calendar picker.
3. Specify the Service Provided by your business.
4. Enter the Amount Requested for your voucher check.
5. Choose your preferred Payment Method.
6. Enter your Email Address in the correct format (e.g., [name]@domain.com).
7. Enter your Phone Number in the correct format (e.g., +1 555 123 4567).
8. Select the Assigned Tool or Date for your voucher check.
9. Optionally, add any additional Notes regarding your request.
10. Click the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name (Text)**: Enter the name of your business.
* **Date Range (Date)**: Select a date range from the calendar picker. This is required to ensure the voucher check request is correctly dated. 
* **Service Provided (Text)**: Briefly describe the service provided by your business.
* **Amount Requested (Number)**: Enter the total amount you are requesting for the voucher check.
* **Payment Method (Select One)**: Choose how you would like to receive your voucher check (Cash, Check, or Credit Card).
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., [name]@domain.com). This is required to ensure we can contact you regarding your request.
* **Phone Number (Text)**: Enter your phone number in the correct format (e.g., +1 555 123 4567). This is required for contact purposes.
* **Assigned Tool (Select One)**: Choose the tool assigned to your voucher request (chatjimmy or other).
* **Assigned Date (Date)**: Select the assigned date for your voucher request. This date is required to ensure the request is correctly dated. 
* **Notes (Text)**: Add any additional comments or information regarding your voucher request.
