# internal-audit-function-evaluation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The internal-audit-function-evaluation-form is designed to gather feedback and information from users on internal audit functions. This form is used to evaluate the performance, efficiency, and effectiveness of internal audit functions, enabling internal audit teams or management to identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the relevant options or enter the required information for each field.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Answers** (`user_answers`, `note`, required: false): This section allows you to provide any additional comments or answers that are not covered in the other fields. You can type in your thoughts, observations, or suggestions related to the internal audit functions.
* **Evaluation Method** (`evaluation_method`, `text`, required: false): Please describe the method or approach used to evaluate the internal audit functions. This could include the specific criteria or standards used for evaluation.
* **Performance Questions** (`performance_questions`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer 'Yes' if the internal audit function performed well, and 'No' if it did not perform well. This field helps assess the overall performance of the function.
* **Risk Questions** (`risk_questions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that apply to the internal audit function. These options represent different types of risks associated with the function. Answer 'Yes' if you have identified any of these risks.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date on which the evaluation was conducted. This helps to track the timing of the evaluation and identify any patterns or trends over time.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and honest as possible when answering each question.
* Ensure that you answer all the required fields to provide a comprehensive evaluation.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please include them in the User Answers section.
