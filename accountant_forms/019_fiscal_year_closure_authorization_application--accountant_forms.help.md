# fiscal_year_closure_authorization_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting approval for fiscal year closure. It is a straightforward form designed to gather necessary information from the user and present it in a clear manner for approvers to review and act upon.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the **fiscal year** field by selecting the fiscal year for which you are requesting approval.
- Choose the **authorization type** that best describes the purpose of the request (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
- Enter the **approver name** of the person who will be reviewing and approving your request.
- Provide a brief **reason** for why the fiscal year closure is being requested.
- Optionally, attach any relevant **documentation** related to the request.
- Enter the date of the fiscal year closure, using the format **MM/DD/YY**.
- Enter the time of the fiscal year closure, using the format **HH:MM AM/PM**.
- Enter the **approver's email** address, for easy communication.
- Enter the **approver's phone number** for additional contact.
- Finally, enter the **approver's department** to help locate the approver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **fiscal_year** (`title`, select_multiple, optional): Select the fiscal year for which you are requesting approval.
* **authorization_type** (`title`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of authorization you are requesting (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **approver_name** (`title`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who will be reviewing and approving your request.
* **reason** (`title`, text, optional): Enter a brief reason for why the fiscal year closure is being requested.
* **documentation** (`title`, note, optional): Attach any relevant documentation related to the request.
* **date** (`title`, date, optional): Enter the date of the fiscal year closure, using the format **MM/DD/YY**.
* **time** (`title`, time, optional): Enter the time of the fiscal year closure, using the format **HH:MM AM/PM**.
* **approver_email** (`title`, email, optional): Enter the approver's email address for easy communication.
* **approver_phone** (`title`, text, optional): Enter the approver's phone number for additional contact.
* **approver_department** (`title`, text, optional): Enter the approver's department to help locate the approver.

## Tips
- Ensure all mandatory fields are filled in before submitting the request.
- Be clear and concise with your answers to help the approver understand your request.
- Attach any relevant documentation to support your request, if applicable.
