# Payroll Account Validation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to validate employee payroll account details and obtain their consent for processing their payroll information. It is a mandatory requirement for new or updated employee records to ensure accurate and compliant payroll management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your employee name, job title, date of birth, email, phone number, and pay account details in the respective fields.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, taking care to review all fields for correctness and completeness.
3. Once you have filled out all the fields, review the form for any errors or discrepancies before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Name (<employee_name>, text, required/optional: Required)
Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### Job Title (<job_title>, text, required/optional: Required)
Enter your current job title within the company.

### Date of Birth (<date_of_birth>, date, required/optional: Required)
Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Email (<email>, email, required/optional: Required)
Enter your company email address.

### Phone (<phone>, text, required/optional: Required)
Enter your office phone number or mobile number.

### Pay Account Details (<pay_account_details>, text, required/optional: Required)
Please provide your current pay account details, including account name, account number, or any other relevant information.

### Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for a smooth payroll processing experience.
* Ensure that your phone and email are current and valid.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
