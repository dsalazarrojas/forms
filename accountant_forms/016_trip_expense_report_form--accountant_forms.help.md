# Trip Expense Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Trip Expense Report Form is used to report business trip expenses for company reimbursement. To complete this form, please select the relevant fields below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of trip you took (business or non-business).
2.  Enter the start and end dates of the trip.
3.  Describe your trip in the Trip Details field.
4.  Enter the amount of your expenses, if applicable.
5.  Provide a description of your expenses.
6.  Select a category for your expenses (e.g., meals, travel, lodging).
7.  Enter the total expenses incurred during the trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Business Trip** (Select Multiple, `id: 1`): 
    Check the box if this trip was for business purposes.
*   **Start Date** (Date, `id: 2`): 
    Enter the start date of your trip.
*   **End Date** (Date, `id: 3`): 
    Enter the end date of your trip.
*   **Trip Details** (Text, `id: 4`): 
    Provide a brief description of your trip.
*   **Expense Amount** (Number, `id: 5`): 
    Enter the total amount of expenses incurred during the trip.
*   **Expense Description** (Text, `id: 6`): 
    Describe the specific expenses you incurred during the trip.
*   **Expense Category** (Select One, `id: 7`): 
    Select a category for your expenses (e.g., meals, travel, lodging).
*   **Total Expenses** (Number, `id: 8`): 
    Enter the total amount of expenses for the trip.

## Tips

*   Be accurate when completing the form, as this information will be used to process your reimbursement.
*   If you have questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR department.
