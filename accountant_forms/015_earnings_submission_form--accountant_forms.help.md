# Earnings Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit earnings data for payments made to employees. It collects information about payment methods, amounts, and dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your main information in the **Main Information** section.
2. Provide payment information in the **Payment Information** section.
3. Select your payment method from the dropdown menu in the **Payment Method** field.
4. Choose a payment amount from the dropdown menu in the **Payment Amount** field. This will affect the corresponding **Payment Amount Integer**, **Payment Amount Decimal**, and **Payment Amount Text** fields.
5. If you are submitting a payment date and time, select the date and time in the **Payment Date and Time** field.
6. Enter any relevant notes in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Main Information (Page 1)
* **Email**: A text field for entering your email address.
* **Phone**: A text field for entering your phone number.
* **Notes**: A text field for any additional notes or comments.

### Payment Information (Page 2)
* **Payment Method**: A select menu to choose your payment method: Cash, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Check. This field is required.
* **Payment Amount**: A select menu to choose your payment amount. This field affects the corresponding **Payment Amount Integer**, **Payment Amount Decimal**, and **Payment Amount Text** fields.
* **Payment Amount Integer**: A field for entering a whole number payment amount.
* **Payment Amount Decimal**: A field for entering a decimal payment amount.
* **Payment Amount Text**: A field for entering a text payment amount.
* **Payment Date and Time**: A date field for selecting a payment date and time, if applicable.

### Payment Method and Amount (Page 3)
* **Cash**: Choose this option if you are paying in cash.
* **PayPal**: Choose this option if you are paying via PayPal.
* **Bank Transfer**: Choose this option if you are paying via bank transfer.
* **Check**: Choose this option if you are paying via check.
* **Less than $1000**: Choose this option if your payment amount is less than $1000.
* **$1000-$5000**: Choose this option if your payment amount is between $1000 and $5000.
* **$5000-$10000**: Choose this option if your payment amount is between $5000 and $10000.
* **$10000-$20000**: Choose this option if your payment amount is between $10000 and $20000.
* **$20000-$50000**: Choose this option if your payment amount is between $20000 and $50000.
* **More than $50000**: Choose this option if your payment amount is more than $50000. 

### Tips
* Please ensure that your payment method and amount are accurate to ensure proper tracking and reporting. 
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask your supervisor or accounting team for assistance.
