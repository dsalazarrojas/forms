# Mileage Log And Expense Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to track an employee's mileage log and expenses for reimbursement purposes. It collects information on mileage driven, expenses incurred, and relevant details to facilitate reimbursement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete the **Mileage Log Form** section.
2. Enter the **Date** of the trip in the **Date** field.
3. Enter the **Miles Driven** in the **Miles Driven** field.
4. Select the **Rate** for the mileage in the **Rate** field.
5. Enter the **Start Location** and **End Location** of the trip in their respective fields.
6. Move on to the **Expense Log Form** section.
7. Enter the **Expense Amount** for each expense in the **Expense Amount** field.
8. Select the **Category** for each expense from the options provided.
9. Select the **Subcategory** for each expense from the options provided.
10. Enter the **Expense Description** for each expense in the **Expense Description** field.
11. Select the **Rate** for each expense from the options provided.
12. Enter the **Expense Status** for each expense from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mileage Log Form**
	+ **Mileage Log Form**: A form to record mileage-related information.
	+ **Date**: The date the trip took place.
	+ **Miles Driven**: The total distance traveled.
	+ **Rate**: The rate used for reimbursement (e.g., 0.25, 0.5, 0.75).
	+ **Start Location**: The starting point of the trip.
	+ **End Location**: The ending point of the trip.
* **Expense Log Form**
	+ **Expense Amount**: The cost associated with each expense.
	+ **Category**: The category of the expense (e.g., Car, Gas, Meals).
	+ **Subcategory**: A more specific category for the expense (e.g., Other, Food, Transportation).
	+ **Expense Description**: A description of the expense.
	+ **Rate**: The rate used for reimbursement (e.g., 0.25, 0.5, 0.75).
	+ **Expense Status**: The status of the expense (e.g., Approved, Reimbursed, Rejected).
	+ **User Signature**: A field for the employee to sign and date their submission.
	+ **User Name**: The name of the employee submitting the form.
	+ **Company**: The company associated with the expense.
	+ **Department**: The department associated with the expense.
	+ **Email**: The email address of the employee or company.
	+ **Phone Number**: The phone number of the employee or company.
	+ **Address**: The address of the employee or company.
	+ **Notes**: Any additional information related to the expense.
	+ **Date (2)**: A second date field for the expense.
	+ **Rate (3)**: A second rate field for the expense.

Note: The required fields are marked as required. Ensure all required fields are completed to avoid form rejection.
