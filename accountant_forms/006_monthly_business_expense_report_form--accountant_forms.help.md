# Monthly Business Expense Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly Business Expense Report Form is designed to collect data for reporting and analysis of business expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the expense date.
2. Select the expense category.
3. Enter a detailed description of the expense.
4. Enter the expense amount.
5. Calculate the total amount (this will be calculated automatically).
6. Enter any additional remarks or comments about the expense.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.
9. Enter any additional notes you would like to add.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Expense Date (Date)** (`expense_date`, optional): Enter the date of the expense. This is an optional field, but it helps in organizing expenses by date.
* **Expense Category (Select One)** (`expense_category`, optional): Select the category for the expense from the provided options (Account, Miscellaneous, Travel, Utilities, or Other).
* **Description (Text)** (`expense_description`, optional): Enter a detailed description of the expense. This helps in identifying the expense and understanding its nature.
* **Expense Amount (Number)** (`expense_amount`, optional): Enter the amount of the expense. This is an important field for reporting and analysis.
* **Total (Number)** (`expense_subtotal`, optional): This field will be automatically calculated based on the expenses entered.
* **Remarks (Note)** (`expense_remarks`, optional): Enter any additional remarks or comments about the expense.
* **User Email (Email)** (`user_email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **User Phone (Text)** (`user_phone`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **User Notes (Note)** (`user_notes`, optional): Enter any additional notes you would like to add.
