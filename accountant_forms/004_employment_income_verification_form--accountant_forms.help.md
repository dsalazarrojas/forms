# Employment Income Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Income Verification Form is used to collect information from employees for employment income verification. This form is usually used by HR or accounting departments to gather data for benefits, tax purposes, or other financial calculations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name and Employee Number.
2. Select the date you joined the company.
3. Choose the type of income you would like to verify (Salary, Benefits, or Other Income).
4. Select the verification period for which you would like to verify your income (Within 1 year, Within 2 years, or Within 5 years).
5. Enter your current employment status (Currently Employed, Previously Employed, or Not Employed).
6. Select the result of the verification (Verified, Not Verified, or Pending).
7. Enter any additional notes about your employment income.
8. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (1)** (`employee_name`, Text, Not Required): This is the employee's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Joining (2)** (`date_of_joining`, Date, Not Required): The date the employee joined the company.
* **Employee Number (3)** (`employee_number`, Number, Not Required): The employee's unique identification number within the company.
* **Income Verification (4)** (`income_verification`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Select all types of income you would like to verify (Salary, Benefits, or Other Income).
* **Verification Period (5)** (`verification_period`, Select One, Not Required): Select the period for which you would like to verify your income (Within 1 year, Within 2 years, or Within 5 years).
* **Income Amount (6)** (`income_amount`, Number, Not Required): Enter the total income amount you would like to verify.
* **Employment Status (7)** (`employment_status`, Select One, Not Required): Select your current employment status (Currently Employed, Previously Employed, or Not Employed).
* **Verification Result (8)** (`verification_result`, Select One, Not Required): Select the result of the verification (Verified, Not Verified, or Pending).
* **Notes (9)** (`notes`, Note, Not Required): Enter any additional information or notes about your employment income.
* **Email (10)** (`email`, Email, Not Required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (11)** (`phone`, Text, Not Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Employee Address (12)** (`employee_address`, Text, Not Required): Enter your address for contact purposes.
