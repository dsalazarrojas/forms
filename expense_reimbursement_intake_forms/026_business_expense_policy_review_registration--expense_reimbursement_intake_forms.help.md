# Business Expense Policy Review Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is part of the company's expense policy review process, and its purpose is to gather information from attendees who are participating in a workshop on the policy. By completing this form, you will be able to provide feedback on your current experience with the policy and suggest potential areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields to ensure your registration is complete.
2. Select your preferred workshop date and format.
3. Indicate your level of familiarity with the current policy.
4. If you have any questions or concerns about the policy, please let us know.
5. Finally, let us know if you have any specific questions or topics you'd like to learn more about.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Please enter your email address, which will be used for event updates and materials.
* **Phone Number**: Please enter your phone number.
* **Company Name**: Please enter your company name.
* **Department**: Please enter your department.
* **Job Title**: Please enter your job title.
* **Company Size**: Please select the number of employees in your company.
* **Preferred Workshop Date**: Please select your preferred workshop date.
* **Preferred Format**: Please select how you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Familiarity with Current Policy**: Indicate how well you know the current policy.
* **Have Questions about Current Policy**: Please indicate if you have any questions about the policy.
* **Specific Questions about Policy**: If you have specific questions, please enter them here.
* **Topics of Interest**: Please select all topics that apply to you.
* **Challenges with Expense Reporting**: If you face any challenges with expense reporting, please describe them.
* **Feedback on Current Process**: If you have any feedback on the current process, please enter it here.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, please select them.
* **Accessibility Accommodations Needed**: Please indicate if you need any accessibility accommodations.
* **Accessibility Details**: If you need any accessibility accommodations, please describe them here.

Note: All fields marked as required are mandatory and must be filled out to complete your registration. Please ensure you answer all required fields to avoid any delays in processing your registration.
