# Health Reimbursement Account Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Reimbursement Account Claim Form is a tool for employees to claim reimbursement for health-related expenses from their employer-sponsored health reimbursement account. This form helps the management team to track and process claims efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the required details ready before starting.
* Fill in your name and contact details in sections **claimant_name**, **claimant_phone**, **claimant_email**.
* Choose the **claim_type** that best describes your claim (e.g., Healthcare, Disability, or Other).
* Enter the **amount** you are claiming for reimbursement.
* Provide a detailed **description** of the expenses you are claiming for.
* Fill in the dates **submitted_on**, **submitted_date**, and **manager_date** with the relevant dates.
* Select the **approved_by** manager from the available options.
* Enter any additional **notes** about your claim.
* Finally, click "Submit" to submit your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name**: Enter your name as it appears on your company ID or employee card.
* **claimant_name**: Enter your name again, as this will be used for communication and approval purposes.
* **date**: Enter the date you incurred the expense or the date you are submitting this claim.
* **amount**: Enter the total amount you are claiming for reimbursement, up to the limit allowed by the company policy.
* **category**: Select the category that best describes your claim (e.g., Healthcare, Disability, or Other).
* **claim_type**: Select the type of claim you are submitting (e.g., Healthcare, Disability, or Other).
* **submitted_on**: Enter the date you are submitting this claim.
* **submitted_by**: Enter your name again, as this will be used for tracking purposes.
* **claimant_phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **claimant_email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **manager_name**: Enter the name of your manager, if applicable.
* **manager_phone**: Enter your manager's phone number for contact purposes.
* **manager_email**: Enter your manager's email address for communication purposes.
* **manager_date**: Enter the date your manager has reviewed and approved this claim.
* **approved_by**: Select the manager who has approved or rejected your claim.
* **notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about your claim.
* **submitted_date**: Enter the date you submitted this claim.
* **submitted_time**: Enter the time you submitted this claim.
* **claim_id**: Enter a unique identifier for this claim, if required.
* **claim_status**: Enter the current status of your claim (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **claim_status_comments**: Enter any comments about the status of your claim.
* **claim_status_date**: Enter the date the claim status was updated.
* **created_by**: This field is not used in the claim process and is not required to be filled.
* **claimant_email**: Enter your email address again, as this will be used for communication purposes.
