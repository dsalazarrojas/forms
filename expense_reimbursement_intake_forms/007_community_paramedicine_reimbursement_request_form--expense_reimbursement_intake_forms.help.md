# Community Paramedicine Reimbursement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for Community Paramedicine Program employees to request reimbursement for work-related expenses.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all necessary documents and information before filling out this form.
2.  Select the correct expense category for your expense.
3.  Fill out your employee information (name, employee ID, email, and phone number).
4.  Enter the date of the expense occurrence.
5.  Choose the correct expense category.
6.  Enter the total amount to be reimbursed.
7.  Provide a detailed description of the expense.
8.  Describe the business purpose of the expense.
9.  Enter the vendor or supplier name.
10. Check if a receipt is attached.
11. If a receipt is not attached, provide the reason.
12. If applicable, enter the supervisor's name and approve the submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **-- Reimbursement Request Form --** (`reimbursement_intro`): This is an introductory note explaining that the form should only be submitted once per expense category.
*   **Paramedic Full Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your full name as a paramedic.
*   **Employee Identification Number** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID number as a paramedic.
*   **Work Email Address** (`employee_email`): Enter your work email address.
*   **Contact Phone Number** (`employee_phone`): Enter your work contact phone number.
*   **Select Your Department** (`department_selection`): Choose your department from the list: Emergency Medical Services, Mobile Integrated Healthcare, Community Paramedicine, Community Health Outreach, or Other.
*   **Date of Expense Occurrence** (`expense_date`): Enter the date the expense occurred.
*   **Expense Category** (`expense_category`): Select the most relevant expense category for this expense.
*   **Total Amount to be Reimbursed** (`expense_amount`): Enter the total amount you are requesting reimbursement for.
*   **Detailed Description of Expense** (`expense_description`): Provide a detailed description of the expense you are requesting reimbursement for.
*   **Business Purpose for Expense** (`expense_purpose`): Describe how this expense benefits the Community Paramedicine program.
*   **Vendor or Supplier Name** (`vendor_name`): Enter the name of the vendor or supplier you purchased from.
*   **Have you attached a copy of the receipt?** (`receipt_attached_check`): Check if you have attached a copy of the receipt.
*   **Reason for Missing Receipt** (`missing_receipt_reason`): If you haven't attached a receipt, enter the reason here.
*   **Supervisor or Department Head Name** (`supervisor_name`): If applicable, enter the name of your supervisor or department head.
*   **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`): If applicable, enter the date you are submitting this form for approval.

Note that fields with a `hint` attribute are not directly used in the help guide but are included for internal reference and consistency.
