# Transportation Reimbursement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transportation Reimbursement Request Form is used to submit requests for reimbursement for transportation-related expenses incurred on official business travel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information for each field.
2. Provide as much detail as possible, especially for fields like start_location and end_location.
3. Attach any necessary receipts to support your claim, if applicable.
4. Review your submission carefully before hitting the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Details (`travel_details`)**: A brief description of your travel-related expense.
* **Date of Travel (`date_of_travel`)**: Enter the date(s) when you traveled.
* **Travel Purpose (`travel_purpose`)**: Select the reason for your travel from the provided options: Business, Personal, or Other.
* **Mode of Transport (`mode_of_transport`)**: Choose the mode of transportation used during your trip, such as Car, Train, Flight, or Bus.
* **Start Location (`start_location`)**: Enter the starting point of your trip.
* **End Location (`end_location`)**: Enter the destination of your trip.
* **Reimbursement Amount (`reimbursement_amount`)**: Enter the total expense for reimbursement.
* **Receipts (`receipts`)**: Attach any supporting documents or receipts for your claim, if applicable.
* **Expense Claimant (`expense_claimant`)**: Provide your name for reimbursement purposes.
* **Expense Department (`expense_department`)**: Enter your department or team for expense reimbursement purposes.
* **Submit Date (`submit_date`)**: Enter the date you are submitting your reimbursement request.
* **Comments (`comments`)**: Add any additional information or comments about your travel-related expense.
* **Status (`approved`)**: Leave this field for system administrators to update once your request has been reviewed.

Note: The "Approved", "Denied", and "Pending" options for the "Status" field are for system administrators' use only.
