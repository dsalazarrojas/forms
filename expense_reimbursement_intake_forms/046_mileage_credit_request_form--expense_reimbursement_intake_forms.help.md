# Mileage Credit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request mileage credit after a business or personal trip. To ensure accurate reimbursement, please fill out the form carefully and completely.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form instructions and ensure you understand what each field is for.
2. Enter your trip details, including the start and end dates, type of trip (business, personal, or other), and destination(s).
3. Enter the total number of miles traveled and the expense amount you are requesting.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trip Details**: Enter a brief description of your trip, including dates and any relevant context.
* **Trip Type** (`trip_type`): Select 'Business: Business travel', 'Personal: Personal', or 'Other: Other' to indicate the type of trip.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of your trip in the required date format (MM/DD/YY).
* **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the end date of your trip in the required date format (MM/DD/YY).
* **Destination** (`trip_destination`): Select the city or cities you visited during your trip.
* **Mileage (miles)** (`mileage_miles`): Enter the total number of miles you traveled for the trip.
* **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`): Enter the total expense amount you are requesting for reimbursement.


## Tips
* Be sure to review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are complete and accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the reimbursement process, contact your approver.
