# Prospective Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from claimants who are seeking reimbursement for expenses related to company-provided travel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your claimant name in the "Claimant Name" field.
*   Enter the date you are submitting this claim in the "Date of Claim" field.
*   Enter your email address in the "Claimant Email" field.
*   Enter your phone number in the "Claimant Phone" field.
*   Enter your address in the "Claimant Address" field.
*   Enter your city in the "Claimant City" field.
*   Enter your state in the "Claimant State" field.
*   Enter your zip code in the "Claimant Zip" field.
*   Enter your country in the "Claimant Country" field.
*   Enter the amount of your expenses in the "Claimant Amount" field.
*   Upload any relevant receipts or supporting documents in the "Claimant Receipts" field.
*   Enter the date of service in the "Date of Service" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it should appear on your reimbursement claim.
*   **Date of Claim** (`date_claim`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this claim.
*   **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Claimant Address** (`claimant_address`, text, required): Enter your address.
*   **Claimant City** (`claimant_city`, text, required): Enter your city.
*   **Claimant State** (`claimant_state`, text, required): Enter your state or province.
*   **Claimant Zip** (`claimant_zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
*   **Claimant Country** (`claimant_country`, text, required): Enter your country.
*   **Claimant Amount** (`claimant_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of your expenses.
*   **Claimant Receipts** (`claimant_receipts`, text, required): Upload any relevant receipts or supporting documents.
*   **Date of Service** (`claimant_date_of_service`, date, required): Enter the date of service.
