# Student Organization Reimbursement Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by students to request reimbursement for expenses related to their student organization activities. The form allows students to provide details about their expenses, such as description, category, and amount, as well as submitter information for approval and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Description** field with a brief description of the expenses you are requesting reimbursement for.
2. Select the **Category** of your expenses from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter the **Student ID** of the student who is requesting reimbursement.
4. Enter the **Club Name** of the student organization related to the expenses.
5. Enter the **Date** of the expenses.
6. Enter the **Amount** of the expenses.
7. Provide a detailed **Description of Expenses**.
8. Enter your **Submitter** name.
9. Enter your **Submitter Email**.
10. Enter your **Submitter Phone**.
11. Enter your **Submitter Address**.
12. Enter your **Submitter Department**.
13. Enter your **Submitter Position**.
14. Enter the **Date of Submission** of the form.
15. Select the **Approvals** status of the form from the drop-down menu.
16. Select **Student Organization Approved** and **Category Approved** statuses from the drop-down menu.
17. Select **Student Approvals** status from the drop-down menu.
18. Enter any **Approver Note**.
19. Enter the **Approver** name.
20. Enter the **Student Organization Approver** name.
21. Enter the **Status** of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the expenses you are requesting reimbursement for. (Text)
* **Category**: Select the type of category your expenses belong to from the drop-down menu. (Select One)
* **Student ID**: Enter the ID of the student who is requesting reimbursement. (Text)
* **Club Name**: Enter the name of the club related to the expenses. (Text)
* **Date**: Enter the date of the expenses. (Date)
* **Amount**: Enter the total amount of the expenses. (Number)
* **Description of Expenses**: Provide a detailed description of the expenses. (Text)
* **Submitter**: Enter your name as the submitter of the form. (Text)
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address as the submitter of the form. (Email)
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number as the submitter of the form. (Text)
* **Submitter Address**: Enter your address as the submitter of the form. (Text)
* **Submitter Department**: Enter your department as the submitter of the form. (Text)
* **Submitter Position**: Enter your position as the submitter of the form. (Text)
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the date and time of submission of the form. (Date)
* **Approvals**: Select the status of the form from the drop-down menu. (Select Multiple)
* **Student Organization Approved**: Select the status of the student organization from the drop-down menu. (Select One)
* **Category Approved**: Select the status of the category from the drop-down menu. (Select One)
* **Student Approvals**: Select the status of the student from the drop-down menu. (Select One)
* **Approver Note**: Enter any note from the approver. (Text)
* **Approver**: Enter the name of the approver. (Text)
* **Student Organization Approver**: Enter the name of the student organization approver. (Text)
* **Status**: Enter the status of the form. (Text)

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your student organization or the submitter.
