# Mileage Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Mileage Reimbursement Claim Form is designed to help employees track and claim their work-related mileage expenses for reimbursement. This form gathers information about your travel dates, locations, and vehicle usage to process your mileage expenses accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the instructions carefully to ensure you are completing the form correctly.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately for each page.
3. Double-check your entries to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Travel Dates

* **Travel Dates** (travel_dates, date, required/optional): Please enter your travel dates in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` (e.g., 02/15/2022). This field is not required.

### Page 1: Travel Dates

* **Start Location** (start_location, text, required/optional): Enter the starting location of your trip (e.g., office, client site, etc.). This field is not required.

### Page 1: Travel Dates

* **End Location** (end_location, text, required/optional): Enter the ending location of your trip (e.g., office, client site, etc.). This field is not required.

### Page 1: Travel Dates

* **Odometer Reading** (odometer_reading, number, required/optional): Enter the initial odometer reading of your vehicle at the start of your trip. This field is not required.

### Page 1: Travel Dates

* **Mileage Rate** (mileage_rate, select_one, required): Select the mileage rate applicable to your travel (0.5, 0.55, 0.6).

### Page 1: Travel Dates

* **Total Miles** (total_miles, number, required/optional): Enter the total miles driven for your trip. This field is not required.

### Page 1: Travel Dates

* **Start Date** (start_date, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your trip in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` (e.g., 02/15/2022). This field is not required.

### Page 1: Travel Dates

* **End Date** (end_date, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your trip in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` (e.g., 02/15/2022). This field is not required.

### Page 1: Travel Dates

* **Start Time** (start_time, time, required/optional): Enter the start time of your trip in the format `hh:mm AM/PM` (e.g., 8:00 AM). This field is not required.

### Page 1: Travel Dates

* **End Time** (end_time, time, required/optional): Enter the end time of your trip in the format `hh:mm AM/PM` (e.g., 5:00 PM). This field is not required.

### Page 1: Travel Dates

* **Distance** (distance, text, required/optional): Enter the total distance of your trip (e.g., miles, kilometers, etc.). This field is not required.

## Tips

* Ensure you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
