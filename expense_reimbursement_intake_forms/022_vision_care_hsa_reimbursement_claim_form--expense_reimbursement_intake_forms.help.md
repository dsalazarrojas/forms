# Vision Care Hsa Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vision Care Hsa Reimbursement Claim Form is a form used to submit reimbursement claims for Vision Care related expenses. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your claim is processed and approved correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with your personal details (first name, last name, email, phone number, date of claim, description of incident, medical receipts, and claimant signature).
2. Provide any supporting documents necessary to support your claim (if required).
3. Fill out the manager's section with their own personal details and signature.
4. Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (1)** (`first_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name (2)** (`last_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (3)** (`email`, email, **Required**): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further questions or updates.
* **Phone (4)** (`phone`, text, **Required**): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further questions or updates.
* **Date of Claim (5)** (`date_of_claim`, date, **Required**): Enter the date of the incident that you are claiming reimbursement for.
* **Description of Incident (6)** (`description`, text, **Required**): Briefly describe the incident or reason for your claim.
* **Medical Receipts (7)** (`medical_receipts`, text, **Required**): Attach any medical receipts or related documents that support your claim.
* **Supporting Documents (8)** (`supporting_documents`, text, **Not Required**): Provide any additional supporting documents that may be necessary for your claim.
* **Claimant Signature (9)** (`claimant_signature`, text, **Required**): Sign your name to indicate that you are submitting this claim in good faith.
* **Claim Date (10)** (`claimant_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date you are submitting this claim.
* **Claim Time (11)** (`claimant_time`, time, **Not Required**): The time you are submitting this claim (if not, leave blank).
* **Claim Location (12)** (`claimant_location`, text, **Not Required**): The location where the incident occurred (if not, leave blank).
* **Claim Note (13)** (`claimant_note`, note, **Not Required**): Any additional information you may have regarding your claim.
* **Manager Name (14)** (`manager_note`, text, **Not Required**): A note for your manager's reference (if not, leave blank).
* **Manager Signature (15)** (`manager_signature`, text, **Required**): Sign your manager's name to indicate that they have reviewed and approved this claim.
* **Manager Date (16)** (`manager_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date your manager reviewed and approved this claim.
* **Manager Time (17)** (`manager_time`, time, **Not Required**): The time your manager reviewed and approved this claim (if not, leave blank).
* **Manager Location (18)** (`manager_location`, text, **Not Required**): The location where your manager reviewed and approved this claim (if not, leave blank).
* **Manager Claimant Name (19)** (`manager_claimant_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your manager's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Manager Claimant Email (20)** (`manager_claimant_email`, email, **Required**): Enter your manager's email address where they can be reached for further questions or updates.
* **Manager Claimant Phone (21)** (`manager_claimant_phone`, text, **Required**): Enter your manager's phone number where they can be reached for further questions or updates.
* **Manager Claimant Date (22)** (`manager_claimant_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date your manager reviewed and approved this claim.
* **Manager Claimant Time (23)** (`manager_claimant_time`, time, **Not Required**): The time your manager reviewed and approved this claim (if not, leave blank).
* **Manager Claimant Location (24)** (`manager_claimant_location`, text, **Not Required**): The location where your manager reviewed and approved this claim (if not, leave blank).
* **Manager Claimant Note (25)** (`manager_claimant_note`, note, **Not Required**): A note for your manager to reference when reviewing this claim (if not, leave blank).

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* Attach any necessary supporting documents to your claim.
* If required fields are not filled out, the form will not be processed.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your manager for guidance.
* Please note that this form is for Vision Care related expenses only, and any unrelated expenses will not be processed.
