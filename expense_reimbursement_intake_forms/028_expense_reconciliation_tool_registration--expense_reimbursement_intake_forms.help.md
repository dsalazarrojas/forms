# Expense Reconciliation Tool Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for expense reconciliation tool registration and is intended for internal use by employees. It is used to confirm an employee's acceptance of the terms of the expense reimbursement policy and to provide necessary information for reconciliation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields. While most fields are optional, please make sure to fill out any marked as required.
2. Make sure to fill in the correct information for each field, especially the fields related to your employment information.
3. If you have any questions or issues, please contact the relevant department for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Information** (`employee_info`, text, required/optional): This field is where you will enter your employee information.
  * Please enter your name, title, and any other relevant details about your employment.
  * This field is required, as it is necessary for identifying you as an employee.
- **Expense Tool** (`tool_info`, text, required/optional): This field is where you will enter information about the expense tool you are registering.
  * Please enter a brief description of the tool you are registering.
  * This field is not required, but it is recommended for clarity and ease of reference.
- **Date Of Registration** (`date_of_registration`, date, required/optional): This field is where you will enter the date of your registration.
  * Please enter the date of your registration using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
  * This field is not required, but it can help with tracking and reconciliation purposes.
- **Registered By** (`registered_by`, select_one, required/optional): This field is where you will choose whether you are registering yourself or someone else.
  * Please choose "Yes" if you are registering yourself, and "No" if someone else is registering for you.
  * This field is not required, but it can help with tracking and reconciliation purposes.
- **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is where you will choose who submitted this form.
  * Please choose "Yes" if this form was submitted by yourself, and "No" if someone else submitted it.
  * This field is not required, but it can help with tracking and reconciliation purposes.
- **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required/optional): This field is where you will enter the date of submission.
  * Please enter the date of submission using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
  * This field is not required, but it can help with tracking and reconciliation purposes.
- **Status** (`status`, select_one, required/optional): This field is where you will choose the status of the registration.
  * Please choose "Active" if the registration is active, and "Inactive" if it is not.
  * This field is not required, but it can help with tracking and reconciliation purposes.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): This field is where you can enter any additional notes about the registration.
  * Please enter any relevant details that may help with reconciliation.
  * This field is not required, but it is recommended for clarity and reference.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): This field is where you will enter your email address.
  * Please enter your work email address.
  * This field is optional, but it can help with contact and notification purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): This field is where you will enter your phone number.
  * Please enter your work phone number.
  * This field is optional, but it can help with communication purposes.
- **Expense Reimbursement Policy** (`expense_reimbursement_policy`, select_one, required/optional): This field is where you will choose whether you have read and agreed to the expense reimbursement policy.
  * Please choose "Yes" if you have read and agreed to the policy.
  * This field is not required, but it is recommended for clarity and reference.
- **Expense Category** (`expense_category`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is where you will choose the category of expenses you are registering.
  * Please choose any applicable categories.
  * This field is not required, but it can help with tracking and reconciliation purposes.
- **Expense Type** (`expense_type`, select_one, required/optional): This field is where you will choose the type of expense you are registering.
  * Please choose "Yes" if it is applicable.
  * This field is not required, but it can help with tracking and reconciliation purposes.
- **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`, number, required/optional): This field is where you will enter the amount of the expense.
  * Please enter the amount using numerical format.
  * This field is not required, but it can help with tracking and reconciliation purposes.
- **Currency** (`currency`, select_one, required/optional): This field is where you will choose the currency of the expense.
  * Please choose "Yes" if it is applicable.
  * This field is not required, but it can help with tracking and reconciliation purposes.
- **Tax Rate** (`tax_rate`, number, required/optional): This field is where you will enter the tax rate of the expense.
  * Please enter the tax rate using numerical format.
  * This field is not required, but it can help with tracking and reconciliation purposes.
- **Tax Rate Value** (`tax_rate_value`, number, required/optional): This field is where you will enter the tax rate value of the expense.
  * Please enter the tax rate value using numerical format.
  * This field is not required, but it can help with tracking and reconciliation purposes.
- **Currency Code** (`currency_code`, select_one, required/optional): This field is where you will choose the currency code of the expense.
  * Please choose "Yes" if it is applicable.
  * This field is not required, but it can help with tracking and reconciliation purposes.
- **Expense Reimbursement Amount** (`expense_reimbursement_amount`, number, required/optional): This field is where you will enter the expense reimbursement amount.
  * Please enter the amount using numerical format.
  * This field is not required, but it can help with tracking and reconciliation purposes.
- **Expense Reimbursement Status** (`expense_reimbursement_status`, select_one, required/optional): This field is where you will choose the status of the expense reimbursement.
  * Please choose "Active" if the expense reimbursement is active, and "Inactive" if it is not.
  * This field is not required, but it can help with tracking and reconciliation purposes.
