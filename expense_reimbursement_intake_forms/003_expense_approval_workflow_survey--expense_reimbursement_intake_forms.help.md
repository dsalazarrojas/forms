# Expense Approval Workflow Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Expense Approval Workflow Survey is used to collect relevant information from approvers, managers, and submitters for the process of expense reimbursement. It captures essential details for efficient and transparent approval workflows.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2.  Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing accurate details when required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Approver Information** (`approver_info`, required): Enter your name and contact information as the approver.
    *   This field is required and must be completed to ensure that your submission is properly attributed and contactable.
*   **Manager Information** (`manager_info`, required): Enter the name and contact information of your manager who will be involved in the expense approval process.
    *   This field is required and must be completed to ensure that the correct manager is notified of your submission.
*   **Department** (`department`, optional): Select the department related to your expense approval.
    *   You may optionally provide the department name if it's relevant to your submission.
*   **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`, required): Enter your name and contact information as the submitter.
    *   This field is required and must be completed to ensure that your submission is properly attributed and contactable.
*   **Expense Details** (`expense_details`, required): Enter a detailed description of the expense you are seeking reimbursement for.
    *   This field is required and must be completed thoroughly for proper expense approval.
*   **Welcome to the Expense Approval Workflow Survey** (`introduction`, optional): A text field for introducing yourself or providing any additional context.
    *   This field is optional, but may be used to provide additional background information about your submission.
