# Fuel Expense Reimbursement Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for employees who need to submit fuel expense reimbursement requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your fuel expense details, including dates, mileage readings, fuel type, and total fuel cost.
2. Provide your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
3. Select the fuel type used.
4. If applicable, provide your manager's information.
5. Review and submit your form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fuel Expense Submission** (Page 1, fuel_expense_submission): Describe the fuel expense you are submitting for reimbursement.
* **Start Date** (Page 1, start_date): Enter the start date of your fuel expense trip or use.
* **Start Mileage Reading** (Page 1, start_mileage_reading): Enter the start mileage reading of your vehicle for the trip or use.
* **End Date** (Page 1, end_date): Enter the end date of your fuel expense trip or use.
* **End Mileage Reading** (Page 1, end_mileage_reading): Enter the end mileage reading of your vehicle for the trip or use.
* **Date of Fuel Purchase** (Page 2, date_of_fuel_purchase): Enter the date you purchased the fuel that was used for this expense.
* **Fuel Purchase Description** (Page 2, fuel_purchase_description): Describe the fuel purchase.
* **Total Fuel Cost** (Page 2, total_fuel_cost): Enter the total cost of the fuel purchased.
* **Reimbursement Amount** (Page 2, reimbursement_amount): Enter the amount you are requesting for reimbursement.
* **Mileage Reimbursement** (Page 2, mileage_reimbursement): If you are requesting mileage reimbursement, enter the number of miles and the rate per mile.
* **Fuel Type** (Page 3, fuel_type): Select the type of fuel used (e.g., Diesel, Gas, Electricity, Other).
* **Submitter's Name** (Page 3, submite_name): Enter your name as the person submitting the reimbursement request.
* **Submitter's Email** (Page 3, submite_email): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter's Phone** (Page 3, submite_phone): Enter your phone number.
* **Department** (Page 3, department): Enter your department or team name.
* **Submitter's Job Title** (Page 3, submite_job_title): Enter your job title.
* **Submitter's Company** (Page 3, submite_company): Enter your company name.
* **Submitter's Address** (Page 3, submite_address): Enter your address.
* **Submitter's City** (Page 3, submite_city): Enter your city.
* **Submitter's State** (Page 3, submite_state): Enter your state or province.
* **Submitter's ZIP** (Page 3, submite_zip): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Submitter's Country** (Page 3, submite_country): Enter your country.
* **Manager's Name** (Page 4, manager_name): Enter your manager's name (if applicable).
* **Manager's Email** (Page 4, manager_email): Enter your manager's email address (if applicable).
* **Manager's Phone** (Page 4, manager_phone): Enter your manager's phone number (if applicable).

Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays in processing your reimbursement request.
