# Expense Allowance Adjustment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to adjust expense allowances for reimbursement. It's essential to fill it out for legitimate reasons, such as correcting previous reimbursements or requesting changes to existing policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category for your adjustment (Expense Reimbursement Intake Forms or Other).
2. Provide a clear description of the reason for the adjustment.
3. Enter the expense stipend amount and the actual amount.
4. Add any supporting documents or attachments if necessary (optional).
5. Enter any review comments, if required by the reviewer.
6. Verify your identity and the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the correct category for your adjustment. This field helps to categorize your request for reimbursement.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise description of the reason for the adjustment.
* **Expense Stipend** (`expense_stipend`, number, required): Enter the amount of expense stipend you are requesting.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required): Enter the actual amount for which you are requesting adjustment.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Enter a detailed reason for the adjustment.
* **Attachment Upload** (`attachment_upload`, text, optional): If necessary, upload any supporting documents or attachments to back your request.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or suggestions for the reviewer.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter your name or identity for verification purposes.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission for tracking purposes.

## Tips
* Be clear and concise in your description and reason fields.
* Attach only relevant supporting documents or attachments.
* Ensure you fill out the form with correct and accurate information to avoid delays.
* Submit your form only for legitimate reasons and after reviewing the requirements and guidance provided.
