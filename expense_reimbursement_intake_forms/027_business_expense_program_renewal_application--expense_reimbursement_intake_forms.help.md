# Business Expense Program Renewal Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to renew an employee's business expense program, providing details about their current program status, expenses, and requested changes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the required fields with your personal and company information.
2. Provide details about your current program status, including the program name, enrollment date, and expiration date.
3. Estimate your annual expenses and the total amount reimbursed.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the current program.
5. Specify the duration for which you would like the program to be renewed.
6. Estimate your expected annual expenses for the renewed period.
7. Suggest any changes you would like to see made to the program.
8. Confirm that you are in compliance with company policy.
9. Confirm that you have discussed this with your manager and are ready for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your company phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department name.
* **Manager/Supervisor Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your direct manager's name.
* **Cost Center** (`cost_center`, text, optional): Enter your cost center code if applicable.
* **Current Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your current program.
* **Program Enrollment Date** (`enrollment_date`, date, required): Enter the date you enrolled in the program.
* **Current Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, required): Enter the current expiration date of your program.
* **Annual Expenses Submitted** (`annual_spend`, number, optional): Estimate your annual expense amount.
* **Total Reimbursements Received** (`reimbursements_received`, number, optional): Enter the total amount reimbursed.
* **Satisfaction with current program** (`program_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the current program.
* **Requested Renewal Duration** (`renewal_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the duration for which you would like the program to be renewed.
* **Expected Annual Expenses (Renewed Period)** (`expected_annual_spend`, number, optional): Estimate your expected annual expenses for the renewed period.
* **Requested changes to program** (`program_changes`, text, optional): Suggest any changes you would like to see made to the program.
* **Suggestions for program improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Suggest ways the program can be improved.
* **I confirm program usage complies with policy** (`compliance_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that your program usage complies with company policy.
* **Ready for manager approval** (`approval_ready`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have discussed this with your manager and are ready for approval.
