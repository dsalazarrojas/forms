# Fuel Expense Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Fuel Expense Review Request Form" is used for employees to request reimbursement for fuel expenses incurred for official company business. This form is used to gather information about the employee's fuel expenses, including details about the expense date, fuel type, cost, and status of reimbursement. It also captures information about the employee's ID, name, and company name. This form is designed to ensure accurate and transparent tracking of company fuel expenses.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee ID, which is a unique identifier assigned to you by the HR department.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your official records.
3. Enter the name of the company you are traveling on behalf of.
4. Enter the date of the expense, which is the date you incurred the fuel expense.
5. Enter the current odometer reading of your vehicle.
6. Indicate if you are claiming reimbursement for a fuel expense ("Yes" or "No").
7. Enter the total cost of the fuel expense.
8. Select the status of your reimbursement request ("Active", "Inactive").
9. (Optional) Enter any comments about your request.
10. (Optional) Enter the date you submitted your request.
11. (Optional) Enter the date your request was reviewed.
12. (Optional) Enter the date your request was approved.
13. (Optional) Enter a reason for rejection, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID (1)**: Enter your unique employee ID assigned by the HR department.
* **Employee Name (2)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Company Name (3)**: Enter the name of the company you are traveling on behalf of.
* **Date of Expense (4)**: Enter the date you incurred the fuel expense.
* **Odometer Reading (5)**: Enter the current reading of your vehicle's odometer.
* **Fuel Type (6)**: Indicate if you are claiming reimbursement for a fuel expense ("Yes" or "No").
* **Fuel Cost (7)**: Enter the total cost of the fuel expense.
* **Reimbursement Status (8)**: Select the status of your reimbursement request ("Active", "Inactive").
* **Comments (11)**: Enter any comments about your request, if applicable.
* **Submitted By (9)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Approved By (10)**: Enter the name of the person who approved your request, if applicable.
* **Submitted Date (12)**: Enter the date you submitted your request.
* **Reviewed Date (13)**: Enter the date your request was reviewed.
* **Approved Date (14)**: Enter the date your request was approved.
* **Rejected Reason (15)**: Enter a reason for rejection, if applicable.
* **Rejected Date (16)**: Enter the date your request was rejected, if applicable.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete reimbursement processing.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the reimbursement process, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
* If your request is rejected, do not resubmit without addressing the reason for rejection first.
