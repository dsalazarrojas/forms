# Stripe Church Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stripe Church Donation Form is designed to facilitate donations to a church via Stripe. Users can input their personal and donation details to support the church's activities and initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Select the donation amount in the "amount" field.
5. Choose your preferred currency from the list in the "currency" field.
6. Choose the frequency of your donation from the list in the "frequency" field.
7. Add any additional notes about your donation in the "notes" field (optional).
8. Click the "submit" button to complete your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: A required field to display your name.
* **Email**: A required field to display your email address.
* **Phone**: An optional field to display your phone number.
* **Amount**: A required field to select the donation amount.
* **Currency**: An optional field to select the currency for your donation (USD, GBP, EUR, JPY).
* **Frequency**: A required field to select the frequency of your donation (monthly, one time).
* **Notes**: An optional field to add any additional notes about your donation.
* **Submit**: A required field to complete your donation.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact the church administrator for assistance.
* The form is designed for secure and fast processing of donations.
