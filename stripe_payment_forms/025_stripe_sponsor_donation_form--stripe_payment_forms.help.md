# Stripe Sponsor Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect donation information and payment details from users who want to support a sponsor. Fill out this form to provide your donation amount, contact details, and payment method preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the donation amount you wish to provide. You can choose any value from the available options (e.g., $10, $20, $50).
2. Enter your donor name for registration purposes.
3. Provide your email address so we can contact you about your donation.
4. Share your phone number for any follow-up or confirmation needs.
5. Choose your preferred payment method from the available options (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
6. If you've selected a payment method that requires additional details, please provide them in the payment details field.
7. Finally, confirm whether you wish to submit your payment by selecting "Submit Payment" and confirming that you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount**: A required field for specifying the amount of your donation. This should be a whole number, e.g., $10, $20, etc.
* **Donor Name**: This field is for your personal contact details, please provide your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **E-Mail**: Please provide your email address for communication purposes, e.g., [your_email@example.com](mailto:your_email@example.com).
* **Phone**: Your phone number will be used for any follow-up or confirmation needs, e.g., (123) 456-7890.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method from the available options (Stripe, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
	+ If you choose Stripe, you can proceed to the payment page for processing.
	+ If you choose PayPal, you will be redirected to the PayPal website for payment processing.
	+ If you choose Bank Transfer, please provide the required payment details.
* **Payment Details**: This field is for specifying any additional payment details if your selected payment method requires them. Please refer to the payment method options for more information.
* **Submit Payment**: This field confirms that you agree to the terms and conditions and want to proceed with the donation payment.
