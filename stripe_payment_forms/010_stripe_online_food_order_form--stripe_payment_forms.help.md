# stripe_online_food_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "stripe_online_food_order_form" is designed to collect information for online food orders. This form is used to gather essential details from customers, including contact and payment information, order specifics, and billing/shipping addresses.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required and optional information.
2. Ensure that you select one of the available payment methods (Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
3. Enter your order details, such as date and time of the order, and any additional notes.
4. Provide your shipping address, including your country.
5. If necessary, enter your billing address.
6. Select your phone number and email address.
7. Enter your name, state, and city.
8. Review your form before submission to ensure all information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false)
Select one of the available payment methods to complete your order: Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Credit Card, Cash, Bank, or Wire Transfer.

### 2. **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false)
Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, email, and address.

### 3. **Payment Method Confirmation** (`payment_method_confirmation`, select_one, required: false)
Confirm your selected payment method to ensure accurate processing.

### 4. **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required: false)
Enter any additional information about your order, such as date and time of the order.

### 5. **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, text, required: false)
Enter your shipping address, including your country, state, city, and zip code.

### 6. **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required: false)
Enter the total cost of your order, including any applicable taxes and fees.

### 7. **Payment Total** (`payment_total`, number, required: false)
Enter the total cost of your payment, including any applicable fees.

### 8. **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, required: false)
Enter your billing address, including your state and zip code.

### 9. **Billing Phone** (`billing_phone`, text, required: false)
Enter your billing phone number.

### 10. **Billing Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
Enter your billing email address.

### 11. **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false)
Enter your first name.

### 12. **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false)
Enter your last name.

### 13. **Address** (`address`, text, required: false)
Enter your billing address.

### 14. **City** (`city`, text, required: false)
Enter the city of your billing address.

### 15. **State** (`state`, select_one, required: false)
Select the state of your billing address.

### 16. **Zip** (`zip`, text, required: false)
Enter the zip code of your billing address.

### 17. **Country** (`country`, text, required: false)
Enter the country of your billing address.

### 18. **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, required: false)
Enter any additional notes or instructions for your order.

### 19. **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false)
Enter your phone number.

### 20. **Country** (repeated, text, required: false)
Enter the country of your shipping address.

### 21. **State** (repeated, select_one, required: false)
Select the state of your shipping address.

### 22. **City** (repeated, text, required: false)
Enter the city of your shipping address.

### 23. **Zip** (repeated, text, required: false)
Enter the zip code of your shipping address.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Review your form carefully to avoid errors.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy to prevent missed communications.
