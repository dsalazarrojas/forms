# Stripe Cake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stripe Cake Order Form is designed for customers to place an order for a cake online. This form will guide you through the process of providing the necessary information to complete your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with the required information (if any).
2. Review each field carefully and make sure to fill in the correct information.
3. Once you're done, submit the form to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Cake Order Form** (`order_details`, `text`, not required)
Fill in the description of your cake order, including any special requests or instructions.

### 2. **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, `text`, not required)
Provide your name and contact information to contact you about your order.

### 3. **Order Summary** (`order_summary`, `text`, not required)
Enter a brief summary of your order, including any additional details you'd like to provide.

### 4. **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, not required)
Enter your payment method and any relevant payment details (e.g., card number, expiration date).

### 5. **Cake Options** (`cake_options`, `select_multiple`, not required)
Select the types of cake you'd like to order. You can choose from the options provided.

### 6. **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, `text`, not required)
Provide your shipping address and any special shipping instructions.

### 7. **Billing Details** (`billing_details`, `text`, not required)
Enter your billing address, which may be the same as your shipping address.

### 8. **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, not required)
Enter the total amount you're willing to pay for the order.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
- This form is for ordering cakes online; if you're looking for in-store pickup, please visit our bakery in person.
