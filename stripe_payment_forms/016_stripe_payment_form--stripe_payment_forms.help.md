# Stripe Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for processing Stripe payment information. It may be used for online checkout, subscription services, or other scenarios where payment information is required.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Choose the payment method you prefer (Credit Card, PayPal, or Stripe).
3. Enter your credit card number and expiration details, if using a credit card.
4. Enter your email address if it's required.
5. Enter your phone number if it's required.
6. Enter your name as it appears on your card (if required).
7. Enter your address if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Method (Payment Method, required)**: Select the payment method you prefer from the options provided.
* **Card Number (Card Number, not required)**: Enter your credit card number in the format 4242-4242-4242-4242.
* **Expiration Month (Expiration Month, not required)**: Enter the two-digit month of your credit card expiration.
* **Expiration Year (Expiration Year, not required)**: Enter the four-digit year of your credit card expiration.
* **Cvv (Cvv, not required)**: Enter the Cvv number on your credit card.
* **Zip Code (Zip Code, not required)**: Enter your zip code (if required).
* **Email (Email, not required)**: Enter your email address (if required).
* **Phone (Phone, not required)**: Enter your phone number (if required).
* **Name on Card (Name on Card, not required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your credit card (if required).
* **Address (Address, not required)**: Enter your address (if required).
* **City (City, not required)**: Enter your city (if required).
* **State (State, not required)**: Enter your state (if required).
* **Zip (Zip, not required)**: Enter your zip code (if required).
