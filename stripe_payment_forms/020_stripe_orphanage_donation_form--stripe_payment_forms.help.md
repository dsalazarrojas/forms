# Stripe Orphanage Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stripe Orphanage Donation Form is a secure online form designed to accept donations from users to support the orphanage organization's causes. The form collects essential information from the donor to process the donation efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a donation amount (page 1).
2. Select the donation frequency (page 1).
3. Enter a donation date (page 1) (if applicable).
4. Choose a payment method (page 1).
5. Enter your email address (page 1).
6. Enter your phone number (page 1) (if required or if you want to provide a contact method).
7. Add any additional notes (page 1).
8. Confirm your donation (page 1).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount you wish to donate to support the orphanage organization. This field is optional; however, please note that a donation amount is required for the donation process to proceed.
- **Frequency** (`frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how often you want to make the donation - One-time, Monthly, or Weekly. This selection affects the frequency of future donations.
- **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, `date`, optional): If you're making a one-time donation, you can enter the desired donation date. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your preferred payment method - Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and confirmations related to your donation. This is an optional field but is recommended for future contact purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide an alternative contact method, enter your phone number here. This is not required but can be useful for additional contact options.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to your donation. This field is optional and not required for the donation process.
- **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm that you understand and agree to your donation details. Select "Yes, I confirm my donation" to proceed with the donation.
