# Stripe Newsletter Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from subscribers to create a personalized newsletter, allowing you to tailor your content and distribution strategy to their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your name in the `Subscriber Name` field.
2.  Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
3.  Choose your preferred subscription format (print, digital, or both) in the `Subscription Format` field.
4.  Select the duration of your subscription (Month, 3 months, 6 months, or 12 months) in the `Subscription Duration` field.
5.  Select your preferred newsletter format (print, digital, or both) in the `Newsletter Format` field.
6.  Choose your preferred print format (A5, A6, or A7) in the `Print Format` field.
7.  Select how frequently you would like to be contacted via email (daily, weekly, or monthly) in the `Email Frequency` field.
8.  Choose your preferred digital format (e-book, PDF, or video) in the `Digital Format` field.
9.  Read and agree to the `Terms and Conditions`.
10. Check the `Consent` box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Subscriber Name** (`subscriber_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name in this field.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address here.
*   **Subscription Format** (`subscription_format`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how you would like to receive the newsletter, either print, digital, or both.
*   **Subscription Duration** (`subscription_duration`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how long you would like your subscription to be, either Month, 3 months, 6 months, or 12 months.
*   **Newsletter Format** (`newsletter_format`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose how you would like to receive the newsletter, either print, digital, or both.
*   **Print Format** (`print_format`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the size of the print format you prefer, either A5, A6, or A7.
*   **Email Frequency** (`email_frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how frequently you would like to be contacted via email, either daily, weekly, or monthly.
*   **Digital Format** (`digital_format`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose your preferred digital format, either e-book, PDF, or video.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `note`, required/optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the subscription.
*   **Consent** (`consent`, `note`, required/optional): Check this box to confirm your consent.

## Tips

- Please ensure you fill out all required fields correctly to complete your subscription.
- Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
