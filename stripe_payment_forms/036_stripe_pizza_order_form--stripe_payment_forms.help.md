# Stripe Pizza Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stripe Pizza Order Form is a user-facing form designed to collect order information and payment details for a pizza order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `order_details` field with your order description, if applicable.
2. Provide your delivery information, including any delivery instructions, in the `delivery_info` field.
3. Select the payment method you would like to use from the `payment_method` field.
4. Enter the total order cost in the `total` field.
5. Enter any applicable tax on the order in the `tax` field.
6. If desired, add a tip for the order in the `tip` field.
7. Choose the delivery time for your order from the available options.
8. Enter your delivery address in the `delivery_address` field.
9. Add any delivery notes or messages in the `delivery_note` field.
10. If you have specific delivery instructions, enter them in the `delivery_instructions` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`): Enter a brief description of your order. If you are not sure what to enter here, simply leave this field blank.
* **Delivery Information** (`delivery_info`): Provide any specific instructions for your delivery, such as "Please bring the order to the back door" or "Please put the order on the table by the door".
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select from the options to indicate how you would like to pay for your order. You can choose from Stripe, PayPal, or cash.
* **Total** (`total`): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Tax** (`tax`): Enter any applicable tax on your order.
* **Tip** (`tip`): If you would like to show your appreciation for good service, you can enter a tip amount here.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`): Choose the time at which you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`): Enter your delivery address to ensure your order is delivered correctly.
* **Delivery Note** (`delivery_note`): Enter any additional notes or messages for the delivery person to know about your order.
* **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`): If you have specific instructions for the delivery person, enter them here.

Note: Some fields may not be required, depending on your specific needs and preferences.
