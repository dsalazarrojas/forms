# Stripe Webinar Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stripe Webinar Sign Up Form is a tool for webinar hosts to sign up for webinars using the Stripe Webinar platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the webinar in the "Webinar Title" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Optionally, enter any additional notes about the webinar in the "Note" field.
5. Select the date and time of the webinar using the "Webinar Date" and "Webinar Time" fields.
6. Choose the location of the webinar, either "Online" or "In-person".
7. Click the "Submit" button to confirm your sign up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Webinar Title (form_webinar_title)**: Enter the title of the webinar you wish to sign up for.
* **Email (form_email)**: Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about the webinar.
* **Phone (form_phone)**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Note (form_note)**: Enter any additional notes or information about the webinar you wish to provide.
* **Webinar Date (form_webinar_date)**: Select the date of the webinar.
* **Webinar Time (form_webinar_time)**: Select the time of the webinar.
* **Webinar Location (form_webinar_location)**: Choose the location of the webinar, either "Online" or "In-person".
* **Submit (form_submit)**: Click this button to confirm your sign up and submit your information.
