# Stripe Email Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stripe Email Subscription Form is a tool for users to provide their contact information and subscription preferences for a service offered by Stripe.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field by retyping it.
5. Choose whether or not you wish to subscribe by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Subscribe" field.
6. Click the "Subscribe" button to submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email** (email, required/optional): This is where you enter your email address.
* **Name** (text, optional): This is where you enter your name.
* **Phone** (text, optional): This is where you enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Email** (email, optional): This is where you confirm your email address by retyping it.
* **Subscribe** (select_one, optional): This is where you choose whether or not you wish to subscribe to the service.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address and phone number accurately, as they will be used to contact you.
* If you wish to unsubscribe, simply select "False" in the "Subscribe" field.
