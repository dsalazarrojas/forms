# Stripe Clothing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stripe Clothing Order Form is designed for customers to provide essential details about their clothing orders. This form will be used to gather information for processing and fulfilling clothing orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Order Details" section with the necessary information about your order, such as the item(s) you are ordering and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" section, including your name, address, and any other required details.
3. Select your payment method in the "Payment Method" section.
4. Add any additional order notes or comments in the "Order Notes" section, if necessary.
5. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Order Details (1)**: Enter the details about your order, such as the item(s) you are ordering.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Shipping Address (2)**: Provide your shipping address, including your name, address, and any other required details.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Payment Method (3)**: Select your preferred payment method.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Order Notes (4)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
  - Type: note
  - Required: false
- **Customer Email (5)**: Enter your email address.
  - Type: email
  - Required: false

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in each field to ensure smooth order processing.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
- Please review your order carefully before submitting it to ensure it is accurate.
- We appreciate your help in keeping our form up-to-date and accurate.
