# Stripe Meal Kit Delivery Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The stripe_meal_kit_delivery_subscription_form is a form for users to sign up for a meal kit delivery subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your address, which will be used for delivery and billing purposes.
2. Provide your name and contact email address.
3. Input your phone number, which will be used for any follow-up communication.
4. Enter your credit card details, including card number, expiration date, and CVV.
5. Select the month and year for your subscription period.
6. Choose your CVV options (if applicable).
7. Enter your zip code for delivery purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Address**: Enter your home address for delivery and billing purposes.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you own or have access to.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for any follow-up communication.
* **Card Number**: Enter your credit card number for payment purposes.
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the expiration date of your credit card.
* **Month**: Select the month for your subscription period.
* **Year**: Enter the year for your subscription period.
* **CVV**: Select your CVV options (if applicable).
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code for delivery purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Make sure to double-check your credit card details for correct expiration date and CVV.
* Select the correct month and year for your subscription period.
* Enter your email address correctly for follow-up communication.
* If you select multiple CVV options, ensure they are separated by commas.
* If you have any issues while filling in the form, contact our support team.
