# Executive Relationship Management Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Executive Relationship Management Contact Form is a tool to capture and store contact information and relationship details about executives with whom your team interacts. This information helps staff members to quickly find and contact executives for future collaborations or communications.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Enter your name and email address in the respective text fields.
3. Select the relationship type from the options provided.
4. Add any additional notes about the executive or your relationship with them.
5. Complete any other fields as necessary, depending on your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your name as you wish to be contacted.
* **Email** (`2`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number.
* **Relationship Type** (`4`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose the type of relationship you have with the executive: Client, Vendor, or Partner.
* **Interests** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): List any specific interests or areas of focus you have about the executive.
* **Communication Preferences** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe how you prefer to communicate with the executive (e.g., email, phone, meetings).
* **Notes** (`7`, `note`, required: `false`): Add any additional notes about the executive or your relationship with them.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out as much information as possible.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure the information is up-to-date and accurate.
* Use the form to help you find and connect with executives effectively.
