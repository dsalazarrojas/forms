# Finance Task Assignment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Finance Task Assignment Form is used to assign tasks to team members, track task details, and ensure that tasks are completed on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the assignment date for the task.
2. Choose the person who will assign the task (finance manager or accounting team).
3. Set the due date for the task.
4. Provide a brief description of the task.
5. Select the priority level for the task (high, medium, or low).
6. Choose the status of the task (pending, assigned, or completed).
7. Identify the person who submitted the task.
8. Set the submitted date for the task.
9. Add any comments or notes about the task.
10. Select the team members to whom the task is assigned (finance manager or accounting team).
11. Specify the type of task (accounting, reporting, or finance).
12. Add any relevant tags for the task.
13. Set the reporting date for the task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assignment Date**: The date the task is assigned to team members.
* **Assigned By**: The person or team that assigns the task (finance manager or accounting team).
* **Due Date**: The date the task is due to be completed.
* **Task Description**: A brief summary of the task's purpose and objectives.
* **Task Priority**: The level of importance or urgency of the task (high, medium, or low).
* **Task Status**: The current state of the task (pending, assigned, or completed).
* **Submitted By**: The person who submitted the task.
* **Submitted Date**: The date the task was submitted.
* **Task Comments**: Additional notes or comments about the task.
* **Assigned To**: The team members to whom the task is assigned (finance manager or accounting team).
* **Task Type**: The category or type of task (accounting, reporting, or finance).
* **Task Tags**: Relevant keywords or tags for the task.
* **Reporting Date**: The date the task's progress will be reported on.
* **Finance Task Type**: Although similar to "Task Type", this field is specific to the "Finance" task category.
* **Finance Task Status**: Although similar to "Task Status", this field is specific to the "Finance" task category.
* **Finance Task Comments**: Although similar to "Task Comments", this field is specific to the "Finance" task category.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid incomplete tasks.
* Review and update task status regularly to reflect progress.
* Use task tags to categorize and prioritize tasks efficiently.
* Report progress on task's due date to ensure timely completion.
