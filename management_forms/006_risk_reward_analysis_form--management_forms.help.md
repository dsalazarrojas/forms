# Risk Reward Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Reward Analysis Form is a tool used to evaluate the potential risks and benefits associated with a project. It helps project managers identify potential threats and opportunities and determine the best course of action to take.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Project Name field with the actual name of the project you are working on.
2. In the Risk Factors field, select all the factors that apply to the project from the list provided. You can select multiple factors, so make sure to choose all the relevant ones.
3. In the Risk Factors List field, describe any additional risk factors that are not listed in the provided options.
4. Enter the frequency of each identified risk factor (if applicable) in the Risk Factors Frequency field.
5. Assess the impact of each risk factor (if applicable) in the Risk Factors Impact field.
6. Answer the Reward Analysis question with either "Yes" or "No", indicating whether the reward analysis has been conducted.
7. In the Mitigation Strategies field, select all the strategies that can be used to mitigate the identified risks from the list provided. You can select multiple strategies, so make sure to choose all the relevant ones.
8. In the Mitigation Strategies List field, describe any additional mitigation strategies that are not listed in the provided options.
9. Assess the effectiveness of each mitigation strategy (if applicable) in the Effectiveness of Mitigation Strategies field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name** (project_name): Enter the actual name of the project you are working on.
* **Risk Factors** (risk_factors): Select all the risk factors that apply to the project from the list provided.
* **Risk Factors List** (risk_factors_list): Describe any additional risk factors that are not listed in the provided options.
* **Risk Factors Frequency** (risk_factors_frequency): Enter the frequency of each identified risk factor.
* **Risk Factors Impact** (risk_factors_impact): Assess the impact of each risk factor.
* **Reward Analysis** (reward_analysis): Answer with either "Yes" or "No", indicating whether the reward analysis has been conducted.
* **Mitigation Strategies** (mitigation_strategies): Select all the strategies that can be used to mitigate the identified risks from the list provided.
* **Mitigation Strategies List** (mitigation_strategies_list): Describe any additional mitigation strategies that are not listed in the provided options.
* **Effectiveness of Mitigation Strategies** (mitigation_strategies_effectiveness): Assess the effectiveness of each mitigation strategy.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form carefully and honestly, as the information collected will help the project team make informed decisions about project risks and rewards.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, please consult with your team lead or project manager.
* Please refer to the project's actual situation and use your best judgment to answer the questions truthfully.
