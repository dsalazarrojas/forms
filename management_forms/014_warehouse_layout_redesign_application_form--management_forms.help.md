# Warehouse Layout Redesign Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for warehouse layout redesign applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you understand what it is asking.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately, making sure to provide specific details whenever possible.
3. If you have any files that support your answers, please upload them to the appropriate field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information
* **General information** (`general_info`, `text`, required: false):
    The first field is where you can provide any relevant background information about your project.
    This could include the current state of your warehouse, its current issues, and any goals or objectives you have in mind.
    This field is optional, but it can help the team better understand the context of your project.

### Warehouse Details
* **Warehouse details** (`warehouse_details`, `text`, required: false):
    In this field, you can describe your warehouse in detail, including its size, location, and any specific features or constraints.
    This information will help the team design a more effective and efficient layout.

### Layout Issues
* **Layout issues** (`layout_issues`, `text`, required: false):
    Here, you can list the specific layout problems you are experiencing in your warehouse.
    This could include bottlenecks, inefficient use of space, or other issues that hinder your operations.

### Goals
* **Goals** (`goals`, `text`, required: false):
    This field is where you can outline your desired goals and outcomes for the warehouse layout redesign.
    This could include increasing productivity, improving safety, or reducing costs.

### Required Improvements
* **Required improvements** (`required_improvements`, `text`, required: false):
    In this field, you can list the essential improvements you want to see in the new layout.
    This could include features like better accessibility, improved workflow, or enhanced storage capacity.

### File Uploads
* **File uploads** (`file_uploads`, `text`, required: false):
    If you have any supporting files, such as floor plans or pictures, you can upload them here.
    This will help the team better understand your warehouse's specific needs and constraints.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false):
    Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send your application.
    Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to reach out for clarification.
* The more detailed and specific your responses, the better we can serve you and your team.
