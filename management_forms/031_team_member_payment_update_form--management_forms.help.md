# Team Member Payment Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Member Payment Update Form is a form used to update payment information for team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Employee Name** field with the team member's full name.
2. Select a date for the payment update in the **Effective Date** field (this is where the payment update will take effect).
3. Enter the **Salary Amount** field with the new payment amount for the team member.
4. Provide a reason for the payment update in the **Reason** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`team_member_payment_update_form_0`, text, required): Enter the team member's full name.
* **Effective Date** (`team_member_payment_update_form_1`, date, required): Select a date for the payment update to take effect.
* **Salary Amount** (`team_member_payment_update_form_2`, number, required): Enter the new payment amount for the team member.
* **Reason** (`team_member_payment_update_form_3`, text, optional): Provide a reason for the payment update (optional, but recommended for record-keeping).

## Tips
* Ensure you enter the correct employee name to avoid updating the wrong team member's information.
* If updating multiple team members' payment information, use a specific date for each update.
* If you need further assistance, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
