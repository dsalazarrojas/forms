# Workstream Ownership Assignment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assign ownership or responsibility to a workstream, ensuring that all team members are aware of who is responsible for a particular workstream.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Workstream Name" field with the specific name of the workstream you want to assign ownership to.
2. Enter the "Owner Name" field with the name of the person or team you are assigning ownership to.
3. Enter the "Owner Email" field with the email address of the person or team you are assigning ownership to.
4. If you have it, enter the "Owner Phone" field with the phone number of the person or team you are assigning ownership to.
5. If applicable, enter the "Assigned Date" field with the date when the workstream was assigned to the owner.
6. If applicable, enter the "End Date" field with the expected date when the workstream ownership will expire or end.
7. Enter the "Manager Name" field with the name of the person overseeing the workstream (if different from the owner).
8. Enter the "Manager Phone" field with the phone number of the person overseeing the workstream (if different from the owner).
9. Enter the "Team Name" field with any relevant team names or departments involved in the workstream.
10. If needed, add any "Notes" about the workstream ownership assignment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workstream Name** (`workstream_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the workstream you want to assign ownership to.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or team you are assigning ownership to.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person or team you are assigning ownership to.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or team you are assigning ownership to (if applicable).
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the workstream was assigned to the owner (if applicable).
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the expected date when the workstream ownership will expire or end (if applicable).
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person overseeing the workstream (if different from the owner).
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person overseeing the workstream (if different from the owner).
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter any relevant team names or departments involved in the workstream.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the workstream ownership assignment (if needed).
