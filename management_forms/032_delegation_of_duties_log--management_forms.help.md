# Delegation Of Duties Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Delegation Of Duties Log form is used to document the delegation of tasks and responsibilities within the organization. This form helps management to keep track of assigned tasks, ensuring that everyone is aware of their responsibilities and deadlines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the details of the assigned task in the "Task Description" field.
2. Enter the date when the task was assigned in the "Assignment Date" field.
3. Enter the name of the person responsible for the task in the "Assignee Name" field.
4. Select the department of the person responsible for the task from the dropdown list in the "Assignee Department" field.
5. Enter the role of the person responsible for the task in the "Assignee Role" field.
6. Select the status of the task from the dropdown list in the "Task Status" field.
7. Enter the due date of the task in the "Due Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assignment Details (1)** (`task_assigner`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the task to be assigned.
* **Date Assigned (2)** (`assignment_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the task was assigned.
* **Assignee Name (3)** (`assignee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person responsible for the task.
* **Assignee Department (4)** (`assignee_department`, select_one, required: false): Select the department of the person responsible for the task from the dropdown list. Options: Sales, Marketing, IT.
* **Task Description (5)** (`task_description`, text, required: false): Enter a detailed description of the task.
* **Due Date (6)** (`task_due_date`, date, required: false): Enter the due date of the task.
* **Assignee Role (7)** (`assignee_role`, select_one, required: false): Select the role of the person responsible for the task from the dropdown list. Options: Team Lead, Manager, Assistant.
* **Task Status (8)** (`task_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the task from the dropdown list. Options: Not Started, In Progress, Completed.
