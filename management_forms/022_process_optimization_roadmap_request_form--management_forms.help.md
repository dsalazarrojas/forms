# Process Optimization Roadmap Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from end-users on their process optimization roadmap requests. This information will be used to create a comprehensive road map to improve process efficiency and effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant operation team members that are involved in the process you wish to optimize.
2. Describe the goal of the optimization, including what you want to achieve and why.
3. Specify the desired completion date for the optimization project.
4. Provide a detailed description of the process you want to optimize.
5. Indicate the level of priority for the optimization project (High, Medium, or Low).
6. Choose whether an internal or external consultant will be used to assist with the optimization.
7. If an external consultant is chosen, provide their name and contact information (email and phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Operation Team** (`operation_team`, select_multiple, optional): Select the team members involved in the process you wish to optimize. This will help us understand who will be impacted by the process changes.
* **Goal** (`goal`, text, optional): Describe the goal of the optimization, including what you want to achieve and why. This will help us understand the purpose and scope of the project.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, date, optional): Specify the desired completion date for the optimization project. This will help us plan the project timeline and resource allocation.
* **Process** (`process`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the process you want to optimize. This will help us understand the current process and identify areas for improvement.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, optional): Indicate the level of priority for the optimization project (High, Medium, or Low). This will help us prioritize the project and allocate resources accordingly.
* **Consultant** (`consultant`, select_one, optional): Choose whether an internal or external consultant will be used to assist with the optimization. This will help us determine the resources needed to support the project.
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, text, optional): If an external consultant is chosen, provide their name. This will help us communicate with the consultant and ensure they have the necessary information to start work on the project.
* **Consultant Email** (`consultant_email`, email, optional): If an external consultant is chosen, provide their email address. This will help us communicate with the consultant and ensure they have the necessary information to start work on the project.
* **Consultant Phone** (`consultant_phone`, text, optional): If an external consultant is chosen, provide their phone number. This will help us communicate with the consultant and ensure they have the necessary information to start work on the project.
