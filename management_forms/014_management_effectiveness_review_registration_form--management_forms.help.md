# management_effectiveness_review_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a management effectiveness review. It gathers information about the review, including the focus areas, expectations, date, start and end times, location, and additional details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the relevant options for "Focus Areas" and "Expectations".
3. Choose the correct date and times for the review.
4. Enter any additional information you want to provide.
5. Click "Submit" to complete the registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Register Leader** (`text`, `optional`): This field is for entering the name or ID of the leader being reviewed.
* **Focus Areas** (`select_multiple`, `optional`): Select all areas that will be covered during the review (e.g., "Yes" to indicate that the review will focus on this area).
* **Expectations** (`select_one`, `optional`): Indicate whether the review has specific expectations (e.g., "Yes" to indicate that there are expectations).
* **Date Of Review** (`date`, `optional`): Enter the date of the review.
* **Start Time Of Review** (`time`, `optional`): Enter the start time of the review.
* **End Time Of Review** (`time`, `optional`): Enter the end time of the review.
* **Location Of Review** (`text`, `optional`): Enter the location where the review will take place.
* **Additional Info** (`text`, `optional`): Enter any additional details about the review.
* **Submit** (`select_one`, `required`): Confirm that you are ready to submit the review registration.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Double-check your selections and entries for accuracy.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the review administrator.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all necessary information is included.
