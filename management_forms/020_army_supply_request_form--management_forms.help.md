# Army Supply Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Army Supply Request Form is used to collect information about supply requests from army personnel, allowing for efficient allocation of resources and prioritization of requests.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the requester's information:
	* Full Name: Enter the name of the person making the request.
	* Rank: Enter the rank of the person making the request.
	* Unit/Department Name: Enter the name of the unit or department making the request.
2. Enter the request details:
	* Date of Request: Enter the date the supply is needed.
	* Date Supply Needed By: Enter the date the supply is needed by.
	* Priority Level: Select the level of priority for the request (Routine, Standard, Urgent, or Critical).
3. List the supply items:
	* Item 1 - Description: Enter a brief description of the item being requested.
	* Item 1 - Quantity: Enter the quantity of the item needed.
	* Item 1 - Unit of Measure: Enter the unit of measure for the item (e.g., each, box, case).
	* Item 1 - SKU or Part Number: Enter the SKU or part number for the item (optional).
	* Add additional items as needed.
4. Enter the justification for the request:
	* Purpose for this supply request: Enter a brief explanation of why the supply is needed.
	* Impact on operations if not fulfilled: Enter an explanation of the impact on operations if the supply is not fulfilled.
5. Approvals:
	* Supervisor Name - Approving: Enter the name of the supervisor approving the request.
	* Supervisor Approval Date: Enter the date of approval.
6. Additional information:
	* Supervisor Signature Date: Enter the date of supervisor's signature.
	* Special instructions or notes: Enter any additional comments or instructions.
