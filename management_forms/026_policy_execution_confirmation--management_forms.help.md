# Policy Execution Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Policy Execution Confirmation form is used to document and confirm the execution of policies within an organization. This form helps ensure that policies are being implemented correctly and in accordance with the organization's guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the policy details and confirm that you have executed the policy according to the organization's guidelines.
2. Provide an account of your actions regarding policy execution in the "Execution Details" field.
3. Enter the date and time you executed the policy in the "Execution Date" and "Execution Time" fields, respectively.
4. Select the compliance teams that you reported to in the "Compliance Teams" field. If none, select "None".
5. If necessary, enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
6. Review the "Staff Acknowledgments" and "Manager Acknowledgments" fields for accuracy.
7. Save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **policy_execution_confirmation** (Text): A brief description of the policy being executed.
* **execution_details** (Text): A detailed account of your actions taken to execute the policy.
* **execution_date** (Date): The date you executed the policy.
* **execution_time** (Time): The time you executed the policy.
* **staff_acknowledgments** (Text): List any staff members or teams you have acknowledged regarding policy execution.
* **manager_acknowledgments** (Text): List any managers or supervisors you have acknowledged regarding policy execution.
* **compliance_teams** (Select Multiple): Select all compliance teams that you have reported to regarding policy execution.
* **notes** (Note): Additional comments or information related to policy execution.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
* If unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or relevant department for guidance.
* Please save the form after completion to keep a record of policy execution.
* This form is not intended to be a substitute for regular policy execution reports or other documentation. It is meant to supplement these processes and provide additional verification.
