# IT Room Weekly Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Room Weekly Inspection Checklist is a form designed to ensure the maintenance and upkeep of the IT room's equipment, cleanliness, and security standards. This helps identify potential issues before they become significant problems and prevents equipment malfunctions or security breaches.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Inspector Details section with your name and any other relevant information.
2. Select the inspection date from the calendar.
3. Check the equipment condition for each item, selecting "Yes" if it's in good condition and "No" otherwise.
4. Evaluate the cleanliness of the IT room, choosing "Yes" if it meets the standards and "No" if it doesn't.
5. Check for any security issues, selecting "Yes" if you identify any and "No" otherwise.
6. Add any additional comments or observations in the space provided.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspector Details** (`inspector_details`, text, optional): This section is for you to enter your name, title, and any other relevant information about yourself or the person conducting the inspection.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the inspection from the calendar provided.
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Check the condition of the equipment, selecting "Yes" if it's in good working order and "No" if it's not. Examples include:
	+ Servers: Check the server rooms, network equipment, and other machinery.
	+ Cables and connectors: Ensure all cables and connectors are securely attached and not damaged.
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the IT room's cleanliness, selecting "Yes" if it meets the standards and "No" if it doesn't. Standards may include:
	+ Floors: Are the floors clear of debris and obstructions?
	+ Walls and ceilings: Are they free of dust, dirt, or water damage?
	+ Surfaces: Are the surfaces clean and dry?
* **Security Issues** (`security_issues`, select_multiple, optional): Look for any potential security threats, selecting "Yes" if you identify any and "No" otherwise. Examples include:
	+ Unlocked doors or cabinets
	+ Unplugged cables or equipment
	+ Unsecured sensitive data or confidential materials
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations you have about the inspection. This can include recommendations for improvement or questions you have about the equipment or process.
