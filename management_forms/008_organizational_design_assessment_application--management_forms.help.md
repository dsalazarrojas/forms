# Organizational Design Assessment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Design Assessment Application is designed to gather information about your organizational structure, team members, and submitted data. This form is used to assess the organizational design of a company or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Choose the correct options for each select field.
3. Fill out any additional information in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main (\*):** This is the title of your organizational design assessment. Enter a brief description of your assessment.
* **Management (\*):** Choose 'Yes' if you have a management team in place.
* **Description:** Provide a detailed description of your organizational structure.
* **Goals (\*):** Choose 'Yes' if you have specific goals for your organization.
* **Challenges:** List any challenges your organization faces.
* **Key Challenges (\*):** Choose 'Yes' if key challenges are a priority in your organization.
* **Team Members (\*):** List the team members involved in your organizational design assessment.
* **Organizational Structure:** Describe your organizational structure.
* **Engagement Goals:** List any engagement goals for your organization.
* **Assessment Type (\*):** Choose 'Yes' if this assessment is part of a larger program.
* **Assessment Duration:** Choose 'Yes' if the assessment is ongoing.
* **Priority (\*):** Choose 'Yes' if this assessment is a priority for your organization.
* **Engagement Status:** Choose 'Active' if your organization is actively engaged in the assessment process.
* **Notes:** Add any additional comments or information about your assessment.
* **Next Steps:** Describe any next steps or actions you plan to take after completing this assessment.
* **Assigned Tool:** Choose 'Yes' if you have assigned a tool for this assessment.
* **Submitted By:** List the people who submitted the assessment data.
* **Submitted Date (\*):** Choose the date you submitted the assessment data.
* **Submitted Time:** Choose the time you submitted the assessment data.
* **Submitted Email:** Enter the email of the person who submitted the data.
* **Submitted Phone:** Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the data.
* **Submitted Location:** Enter the location where the data was submitted.
* **Submitted Note:** Add any additional comments or information about submission.
* **Submitted Comment:** Add any comments about the submission.
* **Submitted File:** Attach any relevant files or documents.

Please note that some fields are required (\*), while others are optional. Ensure you fill out all required fields correctly to avoid any issues with data submission. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
