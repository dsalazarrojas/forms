# Fulfillment Error Reduction Pilot Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fulfillment Error Reduction Pilot Application is a form designed to gather information from applicants to help our team identify areas of improvement and reduce errors in our fulfillment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first and last names in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide a brief description of your application if necessary.
4. Read and agree to our "Terms and Conditions".
5. Confirm your agreement by selecting "I understand and agree to the terms and conditions" in the "Consent" field.
6. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
7. Select the time of application and time of completion from the respective dropdowns.
8. Enter your contact address and phone number.
9. Select the time of submission (if applicable).
10. Identify the submitter by filling in your name.
11. Finally, fill in the "Time Zone Name" and "Time Zone Offset" fields for your location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (1)**: This field is used to collect the applicant's first name. Please fill this in accurately.
* **Last Name (2)**: This field is used to collect the applicant's last name. Please fill this in accurately.
* **Email (3)**: This field is used to collect the applicant's email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Description (4)**: This field is optional and allows the applicant to provide any additional information about their application.
* **Terms and Conditions (5)**: This field is a dropdown menu with two options: "I understand and agree to the terms and conditions" and "I do not understand and do not agree to the terms and conditions". Please select the correct option.
* **Consent (6)**: This field is used to confirm the applicant's agreement to our terms and conditions. Please select "I understand and agree to the terms and conditions".
* **Date of Birth (7)**: This field is used to collect the applicant's date of birth. Please enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time of Application (8)**: This field is used to track when the application was submitted. Please select the correct time from the dropdown.
* **Time of Completion (9)**: This field is optional and used to track when the application was completed. Please select the correct time from the dropdown if applicable.
* **Contact Address (10)**: This field is used to collect the applicant's contact address. Please fill this in accurately.
* **Phone Number (11)**: This field is used to collect the applicant's phone number. Please enter a valid phone number.
* **Time of Submission (12)**: This field is used to track when the application was submitted. Please select the correct time from the dropdown.
* **Submitter (13)**: This field is used to identify who submitted the application. Please fill this in accurately.
* **Time Zone (14)**: This field is used to collect the applicant's time zone. Please select the correct time zone.
* **Time Zone Offset (15)**: This field is used to collect the time zone offset. Please select the correct offset.
* **Time Zone Name (16)**: This field is used to collect the applicant's time zone name. Please fill this in accurately.
* **Time Zone 2 (17)**: This field is used to collect the applicant's time zone. Please select the correct time zone.
* **Time Zone Name 2 (18)**: This field is used to collect the applicant's time zone name. Please fill this in accurately.
* **Time Zone Offset 2 (19)**: This field is used to collect the time zone offset. Please select the correct offset.
* **Submitter 2 (20)**: This field is used to identify who submitted the application on their behalf. Please fill this in accurately.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any issues or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your cooperation and help in reducing errors in our fulfillment process.
