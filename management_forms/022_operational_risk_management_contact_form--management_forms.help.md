# Operational Risk Management Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report operational risks and incidents to facilitate risk management within the organization. It helps in tracking and analyzing risks over time, allowing for more informed decision-making and mitigating potential risks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of report you are submitting. This can be an Operational Risk Report, an Incident Risk Report, or a Near Miss Report.
2. Choose one or more risk areas that are relevant to your report. These include Operations, Finance, Technology, and Compliance.
3. Provide the name and contact information of the person submitting the report.
4. Enter the date when the incident or risk occurred.
5. Describe the details of the reported incident or risk in the space provided.
6. Indicate the category of risk (High, Medium, or Low) that this incident or risk poses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Type** (`report_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of report you are submitting.
* **Risk Area** (`risk_area`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more areas that are affected by the risk. 
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of the person who is reporting the incident or risk.
* **Contact E-Mail** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the incident or risk occurred.
* **Report Details** (`report_details`, note, optional): Describe in detail what happened or why you consider this a risk.
* **Risk Category** (`risk_category`, select_one, optional): Indicate the level of risk, whether High, Medium, or Low.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible when describing the incident or risk.
- If multiple risk areas are affected, select all relevant options.
- Use the date field for the actual date of the incident or risk, if known.
- The form is not intended for use by anonymous users, as it requires contact information for follow-up purposes.
