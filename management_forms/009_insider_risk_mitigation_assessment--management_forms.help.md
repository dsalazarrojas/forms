# Insider Risk Mitigation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess and document insider risk mitigation measures to help the organization better understand and mitigate insider threats.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each field.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Ensure you have all necessary information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Risk Mitigation Controls**: Describe the controls in place to prevent insider threat.
* **Incident Tracking**: Select the incident tracking method used.
* **Improvement Ideas**: Suggest any ideas you have to improve insider threat mitigation.
* **Team Review**: Select the team members involved.
* **Threat Levels**: Choose one or more threat levels (High, Medium, Low).
* **Risk Categories**: Choose one or more risk categories (Financial, Operational, IT, etc.).
*   **Incident Description**: Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
*   **Mitigation Plans**: Outline the plans to reduce insider threat.
*   **Risk Level**: Select a risk level (High, Medium, Low).
*   **Incident Impact**: Choose one or more impact levels (High, Medium, Low).
*   **Action Taken**: Describe the actions taken to mitigate the insider threat.
*   **Follow-up**: Schedule follow-up activities.
*   **Incident Status**: Select the incident status (Open, Closed, In Progress).
*   **Threat Classification**: Choose one or more threat classifications (Malicious, Accidental, etc.).
*   **Threat Vector**: Choose one or more threat vectors (Insider, Outsider, etc.).
*   **Threat Detection**: Describe the method used to detect the threat.
*   **Responders**: Select the responders involved.
*   **Responders Details**: Describe the responder's role in responding to the incident.
*   **Incident Closure**: Select the incident closure status (Closed, Not Closed).
*   **Incident Cause**: Choose one or more incident causes (Human error, etc.).
*   **Incident Effect**: Describe the incident effect.
*   **Incident Effectiveness**: Choose one or more incident effectiveness (High, Medium, Low).
*   **Incident Frequency**: Choose one or more incident frequencies (High, Medium, Low).
*   **Incident Type**: Choose one or more incident types (Phishing, etc.).
*   **Incident Subtype**: Describe the incident subtype.

Please note that some fields are not required and are only for additional information or reference. If a field is not relevant to your incident, please skip it. 

This guide should provide enough information to help you complete the form effectively. If you still have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or IT department for assistance.
