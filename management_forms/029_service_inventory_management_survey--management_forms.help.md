# Service Inventory Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Inventory Management Survey is a form used to gather information about services in the organization's inventory. This information helps the management team to understand the status and categorization of services, which can be useful for various purposes such as budgeting, resource allocation, and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Service Name** in the provided field. This is the name of the service you are managing.
2. Select the **Service Category** from the options provided. This will help categorize the service for easier tracking and reporting.
3. Choose the **Service Status** from the options provided. This indicates the current status of the service.
4. Enter the **Service Owner** in the provided field. This is the person responsible for managing the service.
5. Select the **Review Date** from the calendar. This is the date when the service was last reviewed.
6. Enter any additional **Notes** about the service in the provided field. This can be used to store any relevant information about the service.
7. Select the **Service Category 2** from the options provided. This is an optional field for additional categorization of the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the service you are managing.
* **Service Category** (select_one, required): Select the category that best describes the service. This will help categorize the service for easier tracking and reporting.
* **Service Status** (select_one, required): Choose the current status of the service. This can be Active or Inactive.
* **Service Owner** (text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for managing the service.
* **Review Date** (date, required): Select the date when the service was last reviewed.
* **Notes** (note, required): Enter any additional notes or information about the service.
* **Service Category 2** (select_multiple, required): Select any additional categories that apply to the service. This is an optional field, but can be useful for further categorization.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct categories and status for each service to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
* Use the **Notes** field to store any additional information that is not included in the other fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the management team for assistance.
