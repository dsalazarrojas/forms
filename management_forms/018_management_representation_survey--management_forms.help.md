# Management Representation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Management Representation Survey is designed to gather feedback from employees on their manager's representation of the company culture. This survey helps to identify trends and areas of improvement, and inform management decisions to ensure that the company culture is properly represented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select your department from the "Department" field.
3. Share your feedback in the "What is your feedback" field.
4. If you are a manager, enter your own name in the "Manager Name" field.
5. Choose how you see the trend in your department from the "Trend" field.
6. Select your department(s) for the "Department Trend" field.
7. If you are an employee who wants to provide feedback on their manager, enter your name in the "Employee Name 2" field and their name in the "Manager Name 2" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (if applicable).
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (if applicable).
10. Select the date of when you are providing the feedback in the "Date" field.
11. Enter the time of when you are providing the feedback in the "Time" field.
12. Enter any additional notes in the "Note" field.
13. If you are an employee who wants to provide feedback on your manager, enter your email address in the "Email 2" field (if applicable).

## Tips
- Be honest and open with your feedback.
- Provide your name and contact information to ensure we can follow up with you.
- Select the correct department and trend for your feedback.
