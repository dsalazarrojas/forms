# Supplier Sustainability Audit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Sustainability Audit Application Form is designed to collect information for the purpose of auditing and assessing a supplier's sustainability performance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the supplier's details in the **Supplier Details** section.
2. Enter the supplier's certification information in the **Certification Info** section.
3. Describe the supplier's sustainability practices in the **Sustainability Practices** section.
4. Enter the supplier's contact information in the **Contact Info** section.
5. Select whether the supplier has been certified (or not) and enter the certification date in the **Certification** section.
6. Enter any additional certification information in the **Certification Info 1**, **Certification Info 2**, and **Certification Info 3** sections.
7. Select the supplier's sustainability rating in the **Sustainability Rating** section.
8. Enter any audit notes and sustainability notes in the **Audit Notes** and **Sustainability Notes** sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Details (1)**: Enter the supplier's name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Certification Info (2)**: Enter any relevant certification information.
* **Sustainability Practices (3)**: Describe the supplier's sustainability practices, such as their recycling policies, energy consumption reduction, and environmental policies.
* **Contact Info (4)**: Enter the supplier's contact information, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **Reporter (5)**: Select 'Yes' if you are the person reporting on the supplier's sustainability practices, or 'No' if someone else is.
* **Supplier name (6)**: Enter the supplier's name.
* **Supplier address (7)**: Enter the supplier's address.
* **Supplier email (8)**: Enter the supplier's email.
* **Supplier phone (9)**: Enter the supplier's phone number.
* **Certification Date (10)**: Select the date the supplier was certified.
* **Sustainability Rating (11)**: Select a rating from 1 to 5 to evaluate the supplier's sustainability performance.
* **Certification (12)**: Select 'Certified' if the supplier has been certified, or 'Not Certified' if they have not.
* **Audit Notes (13)**: Enter any notes about the audit process.
* **Sustainability Notes (14)**: Enter any notes about the supplier's sustainability performance.
* **Certification Info 1 (15)**: Enter any additional certification information.
* **Certification Info 2 (16)**: Enter any additional certification information.
* **Certification Info 3 (17)**: Enter any additional certification information.
* **Certification Info 4 (18)**: Enter any additional certification information.
* **Supplier Sustainability (19)**: Enter a general description of the supplier's sustainability performance.
* **Sustainability Practices 1 (20)**: Enter one of the supplier's sustainability practices.
* **Sustainability Practices 2 (21)**: Enter another one of the supplier's sustainability practices.
