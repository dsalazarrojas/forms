# Property Management Privilege Assignment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Management Privilege Assignment form is used to assign or modify privileges to properties within our organization. This form helps property managers, administrators, and other relevant personnel to efficiently manage access to specific properties and their associated information.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Scroll through the form pages and fill out each page accordingly.
2.  Ensure that you have selected the correct option from the dropdown fields.
3.  Enter accurate and up-to-date information in the text and date fields.
4.  Review your entries to ensure that they are correct before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **assigned_tool**: This field allows you to add a reference tool for the assigned property. Enter the name or ID of the tool that will be used for the property.
- **property_manager**: Choose 'Yes' if the property is managed by our organization's staff, or 'No' if not.
- **assigned_date**: This field is used to record the date the property was assigned. Please use the format 'MM/DD/YYYY' for the date entry.
- **privilege_type**: Indicate the type of privilege being assigned (e.g., access, modification, or others). Choose 'Yes' or 'No' depending on the privilege type.
- **assigned_time**: If necessary, enter the specific time the property was assigned. Format the entry as 'HH:MM:SS'.
- **assigned_by**: Select whether the property was assigned to an individual or a team. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
- **assigned_contact**: Enter the contact person or team in charge of the assigned property.
- **Note**: If you're unsure about any field, please consult the relevant department or manager for clarification.

## Tips
- Always check our internal database for any existing privilege assignments before filling out this form.
- Be specific and accurate when filling out the form to avoid any confusion.
- Double-check your entries before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
- If you have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant personnel for assistance.
- This form can be accessed at any time through our internal system.
- Make sure to save your progress frequently to avoid losing any changes made.
