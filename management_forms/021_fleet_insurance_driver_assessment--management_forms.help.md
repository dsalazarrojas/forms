# Fleet Insurance Driver Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Fleet Insurance Driver Assessment" form is designed to collect relevant information about drivers within a fleet management context, such as insurance assessment purposes. This form is intended for drivers and fleet managers who want to ensure accurate and complete data is provided for fleet insurance evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling in the required fields as much as possible. If a field is marked as "optional", you can skip it or leave it blank.
2. For fields with "text" input types, provide a brief but detailed answer to the question. Use the "hint" provided for reference.
3. For "note" fields, type in a more detailed explanation or comment about the field.
4. Review your answers for completeness and accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Driver Details (id: 1)**: Provide your name, date of birth, and any other relevant driver details.
* **Driving Experience (id: 2)**: Share your experience with driving, including duration and frequency.
* **Driving History (id: 3)**: Describe your driving history, including any accidents or tickets you've received.
* **Vehicle Information (id: 4)**: Enter your vehicle make, model, and year of vehicle you use for work or personal purposes.
* **Contact Information (id: 5)**: Enter your contact details, including phone number and email address.
* **Additional Comments (id: 6)**: If you have any additional information that supports your driver assessment, enter it here.
* **Review Comments (id: 7)**: This field is for you to review your answers and add any final comments or questions before submitting the form.
