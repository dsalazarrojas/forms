# Manufacturing Task Management Board - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Manufacturing Task Management Board form is designed to help production team members create, edit, and view tasks related to manufacturing processes. This form allows you to track the status and progress of tasks, assign responsibilities, and communicate with team members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Click on the "New Task" button** to create a new task.
2. **Fill in the required fields** to ensure you have enough information to track and manage the task.
3. **Save your changes** after completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manufacturing Task Description**: Enter a brief description of the task, such as "Production of widgets" or "Assembly of parts".
* **Assigned By**: Enter the name or email of the person assigned to the task, e.g., "John" or "john.doe@example.com".
* **Task Status**: Select the current status of the task, such as "In Progress", "Completed", or "Not Started".
* **Task Priority**: Select the priority level of the task, such as "High", "Low", or "Medium".
* **Task Due Date**: Enter the expected completion date of the task, in date format (e.g., 2024-03-15).
* **Completed By**: Enter the name or initials of the person who completed the task, e.g., "John" or "JD".
* **Task Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the task, such as additional details or reminders.
* **Task Category**: Select the category of the task, such as "Electronics", "Plastics", or "Metals".
* **Responsible**: Select the team members assigned to the task, such as "John", "Jane", or both.
* **Task Start Date**: Enter the start date of the task, in date format (e.g., 2024-03-01).
* **Task End Date**: Enter the end date of the task, in date format (e.g., 2024-03-15).
* **Assigned To**: Enter the name or email of the person assigned to the task, e.g., "John" or "john.doe@example.com".
