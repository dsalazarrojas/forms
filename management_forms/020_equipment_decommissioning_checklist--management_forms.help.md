# Equipment Decommissioning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and document the decommissioning process of company assets. It captures key details about the asset being decommissioned, including start and end dates, location, and status updates. The form is designed to be comprehensive and help ensure that all necessary steps are taken when decommissioning an asset.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the start date of the decommissioning process in the "Start Date" field.
2. Enter the end date of the decommissioning process in the "End Date" field.
3. Provide the asset name in the "Asset Name" field.
4. Select the equipment type from the options provided in the "Equipment Type" field.
5. Provide the location where the asset is currently located in the "Location" field.
6. Update the equipment status as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Equipment Status" field.
7. Indicate whether the asset has been assigned to a user in the "Assigned User" field.
8. Add any additional comments about the asset in the "Comments" field.
9. Update the asset's status as "Yes" or "No" in the "Is Active" field.
10. Indicate whether the asset was decommissioned by someone in the "Decommissioned By" field.
11. Select the decommissioning status as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Decommissioning Status" field.
12. Enter the date when the asset was decommissioned in the "Decommissioning Date" field.
13. Provide the equipment model in the "Equipment Model" field.
14. Provide the equipment serial number in the "Equipment Serial" field.
15. Enter the asset tag number in the "Asset Tag" field.
16. Update the asset tag status as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Asset Tag Status" field.
17. Enter the reason for decommissioning in the "Decommissioning Reason" field.
18. Select whether decommissioning approvals were obtained in the "Decommissioning Approvals" field.
19. Enter the date when the asset was decommissioned again in the "Decommissioning Date 2" field.
20. Indicate whether the asset was decommissioned by someone else in the "Decommissioning By 2" field.
21. Select the decommissioning status again in the "Decommissioning Status 2" field.
22. Provide the equipment category in the "Equipment Category" field.
23. Add any additional notes about the asset in the "Notes 2" field.
24. Enter the decommissioning date again in the "Decommissioning Date 3" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date**: Enter the date when the decommissioning process starts.
* **End Date**: Enter the date when the decommissioning process ends.
* **Asset Name**: Enter the name of the asset being decommissioned.
* **Equipment Type**: Select the type of equipment being decommissioned.
* **Location**: Enter the current location of the asset.
* **Equipment Status**: Update the status of the equipment as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Assigned User**: Indicate whether the asset is currently assigned to a user.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the asset.
* **Is Active**: Update whether the asset is active or not.
* **Decommissioned By**: Indicate whether the asset was decommissioned by someone.
* **Decommissioning Status**: Select the decommissioning status as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Decommissioning Date**: Enter the date when the asset was decommissioned.
* **Equipment Model**: Enter the model of the equipment being decommissioned.
* **Equipment Serial**: Enter the serial number of the equipment being decommissioned.
* **Asset Tag**: Enter the asset tag number.
* **Asset Tag Status**: Update the status of the asset tag as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Decommissioning Reason**: Enter the reason for decommissioning the asset.
* **Decommissioning Approvals**: Indicate whether decommissioning approvals were obtained.
* **Decommissioning Date 2**: Enter the date when the asset was decommissioned again.
* **Decommissioning By 2**: Indicate whether the asset was decommissioned by someone else.
* **Decommissioning Status 2**: Select the decommissioning status again.
* **Equipment Category**: Enter the category of the equipment being decommissioned.
* **Notes 2**: Enter any additional notes about the asset.
* **Decommissioning Date 3**: Enter the decommissioning date again.
