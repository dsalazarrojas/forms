# Manager Feedback Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manager Feedback Request form is a tool for managers to provide feedback on team member performance, strengths, and areas for improvement. This form allows managers to provide objective and unbiased feedback that can be used to support employee development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Review the instructions and purpose of the form to ensure you understand the feedback is about manager performance and areas for improvement.
* Scroll through each page to find the fields that apply to the feedback you wish to provide.
* Answer each field according to your knowledge of the team member's performance and your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manager Performance**: Select whether the manager's performance is meeting your expectations or if there are areas for improvement. (Required)
	+ "Yes" - The manager's performance is meeting your expectations.
	+ "No" - The manager's performance is not meeting your expectations.
* **Strengths**: Identify the manager's strengths, such as leadership, communication, or problem-solving skills. (Optional)
	+ Select the strengths you've observed from the list of options provided.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Identify areas where the manager could improve, such as communication, delegation, or time management. (Optional)
	+ Select the areas for improvement from the list of options provided.

## Tips

* Provide specific examples of strengths and areas for improvement to make your feedback more concrete and actionable.
* Be objective and unbiased in your feedback, focusing on facts and observed behaviors rather than personal opinions.
* Remember that the feedback you provide will be used to support employee development and performance evaluations.
