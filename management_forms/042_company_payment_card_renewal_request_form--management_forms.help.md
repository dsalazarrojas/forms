The "Company Payment Card Renewal Request Form" is used for employees to request a payment card renewal. This form is intended for employees who need to renew their existing payment card due to various reasons such as card expiration, card damage, or changes in their employment status. This form helps ensure that the request is properly documented and submitted to the finance department for processing.

The form is divided into several sections, each with specific fields for employees to fill out. Here is the guide for end-users:

# Company Payment Card Renewal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request a payment card renewal for various reasons such as card expiration, card damage, or changes in employment status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Review the form before submitting it for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`employee_full_name`): Fill out your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID.
* **Email Address** (`employee_email`): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone Number** (`employee_phone`): Enter your contact number for any follow-up questions.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter your department name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your position title.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Enter your direct supervisor's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): Enter your manager's email address for approval purposes.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`): Enter the current date (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Type of Card** (`card_type`): Select the type of card you want to renew (Corporate Credit Card, Corporate Debit Card, Purchasing Card (P-Card), Travel Card, Fuel Card, or Other).
* **Card Issuer/Bank** (`card_issuer`): Enter the bank that issued your current card.
* **Current Card Last Four Digits** (`card_last_four`): Enter the last 4 digits of your current card number.
* **Current Card Expiry Date** (`card_expiry_date`): Enter the current card expiry date (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Reason for Renewal Request** (`renewal_reason`): Select the reason for your renewal request (Card expiring soon, Card damaged, Card lost/stolen, Need replacement, Upgrading card type, or Other).
* **Renewal Details** (`renewal_details`): Enter any additional information about your renewal request.
* **Card Usage Information** (`section_card_usage`): Enter information about your card usage (e.g., how often you use the card).
* **How often do you use the card?** (`card_usage_frequency`): Select how often you use the card (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Occasionally, or Rarely).
* **Average Monthly Spend** (`average_monthly_spend`): Select the approximate amount you spend on average per month (Under $500, $500-$1,000, $1,001-$2,500, $2,501-$5,000, $5,001-$10,000, or Over $10,000).
* **Primary Expense Categories** (`primary_expense_categories`): Select the categories of expenses you make with your card (Travel, Meals and entertainment, Office supplies, Software subscriptions, Equipment, Services, Training, or Other).
* **Have you been compliant with expense policies?** (`expense_compliance`): Select whether you have been compliant with expense policies (Yes, fully compliant, Yes, with minor issues, No, some violations, or Not sure).
* **Do you have outstanding expense reports?** (`outstanding_expenses`): Select whether you have outstanding expense reports (No, all submitted, Yes, 1 pending, or Yes, multiple pending).
* **Do you want to keep the same credit limit?** (`request_same_limits`): Select whether you want to keep the same credit limit (Yes, keep same limit, No, request increase, or No, request decrease).
* **Current Credit Limit** (`current_limit`): Enter your current credit limit (if applicable).
* **Requested Credit Limit** (`requested_limit`): Enter your requested credit limit (if applicable).
* **Justification for Limit Increase** (`limit_increase_justification`): Enter a reason for increasing your credit limit (if applicable).
* **How should the new card be delivered?** (`delivery_preference`): Select how you want the new card to be delivered (Mail to office, Mail to home, Pick up at Finance, or Pick up at HR).
* **Home Address (if mailing to home)** (`home_address`): Enter your home address (if applicable).
* **Name as it should appear on card** (`name_on_card`): Enter the name as it should appear on your new card.
* **What is the status of your old card?** (`old_card_status`): Select the status of your old card (Still in possession, Will return to Finance, Already returned, Lost, or Stolen).
* **When will you return the old card?** (`card_return_date`): Enter the date you will return your old card.
* **I have read and agree to the card policy** (`agree_card_policy`): Select whether you have read and agree to the card policy (Yes, I Agree, or No, I Need to Review).
* **I agree to use the card responsibly** (`agree_responsible_use`): Select whether you agree to use the card responsibly (Yes, I Agree, or No, I Do Not Agree).
* **I agree to submit expense reports on time** (`agree_timely_reporting`): Select whether you agree to submit expense reports on time (Yes, I Agree, or No, I Do Not Agree).
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`): Sign the form with your electronic signature.
* **Manager Approval Status** (`manager_approval_status`): Enter your manager's approval status (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
* **Manager Approval Date** (`manager_approval_date`): Enter the date of your manager's approval (if applicable).
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`): Enter any comments from your manager (if applicable).
* **Finance Approval Status** (`finance_approval_status`): Enter the finance department's approval status (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
* **Date New Card Requested** (`new_card_requested_date`): Enter the date the new card was requested from the bank (if applicable).
* **Expected Delivery Date** (`expected_delivery_date`): Enter the expected delivery date of the new card (if applicable).
* **Processed By** (`processed_by`): Enter the finance staff member who processed the request (if applicable).

Note: Fields marked as "required" must be filled out for the form to be submitted. Fields marked as "optional" can be filled out or left blank.
