# pre_execution_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre-execution checklist is a form used to gather essential information and confirm tasks before they are executed by the team. It helps to ensure that all necessary details are in place, reducing the risk of errors, delays, and miscommunication. The form is designed to be completed before commencing work on a project, making it a critical step in the project management process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each field carefully and ensure you have the necessary information to complete it accurately.
2.  Start with the task description fields (Task Description, Step 1 Description, etc.), providing a clear and concise overview of the task.
3.  Update progress by selecting the correct status (Complete, In progress, Not started) for each task.
4.  Confirm the task deadline and confirm readiness by selecting either "True" or "False" accordingly.
5.  Enter the assignee's contact details, including their name, phone number, and signature.
6.  Provide team details such as team name, team location, and team leader's contact information.
7.  Set a sign-off date and confirm completion status.
8.  Confirm the team's safety status.
9.  Add any additional comments, if necessary.
10. Review and submit the form once all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Task Description** (`task_description`, `text`, required): Provide a clear and concise description of the task.
* **Step 1 Description** (`step_1_description`, `text`, required): Describe the first step of the task.
* **Step 2 Description** (`step_2_description`, `text`, optional): Describe the second step of the task, if applicable.
* **Task Status** (`task_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of the task (Complete, In progress, Not started).
* **Task Deadline** (`task_deadline`, `date`, optional): Enter the deadline for the task, if applicable.
* **Confirm Readiness** (`confirm_readiness`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "True" to confirm that the task is ready to be executed, or "False" otherwise.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, optional): Leave a note or signature, if necessary.
* **Assignee** (`assignee`, `email`, required): Enter the assignee's name.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, required): Enter the team name.
* **Team Leader** (`team_leader`, `text`, required): Enter the team leader's name.
* **Sign-Off Date** (`sign_off_date`, `time`, required): Set a time for the sign-off.
* **Team Location** (`team_location`, `text`, required): Enter the team location.
* **Confirm Completion** (`confirm_completion`, `select_one`, required): Select "Complete" to confirm task completion, or "Not complete" otherwise.
* **Confirm Safety** (`confirm_safety`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "True" to confirm safety, or "False" otherwise.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments, if necessary.
* **Team Lead Signature** (`team_lead_signature`, `note`, optional): Leave a signature or note from the team lead, if necessary.
* **Assignee Phone** (`assignee_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the assignee's phone number.
* **Team Name 2** (`team_name_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the team name (if applicable).
* **Team Location 2** (`team_location_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the team location (if applicable).
* **Task Priority** (`task_priority`, `select_one`, required): Select the priority level of the task (High, Low, Medium).
* **Team Lead Phone** (`team_lead_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the team lead's phone number.
* **Task Description 2** (`task_description_2`, `text`, required): Provide a clear and concise description of the task.
* **Step 3 Description** (`step_3_description`, `text`, required): Describe the third step of the task.
* **Step 4 Description** (`step_4_description`, `text`, optional): Describe the fourth step of the task, if applicable.
* **Step 5 Description** (`step_5_description`, `text`, optional): Describe the fifth step of the task, if applicable.
