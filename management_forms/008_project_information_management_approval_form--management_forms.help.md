# Project Information Management Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather project information for approval from project approvers. Please fill out this form to provide accurate and up-to-date information about your project.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the project details and ensure they are accurate and up-to-date.
2. Fill out the form with the required information.
3. Submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Project Category** (`project_category`, text, required/optional): Enter the category of your project.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the current status of your project (Active, Inactive, Pending, or Cancelled).
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your project manager.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, text, required/optional): Enter the names of team members involved in your project.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your team lead.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your client.
* **Client Contacts** (`client_contacts`, text, required/optional): Enter the names and contact information of client contacts.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of your project.
* **Team Location** (`team_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of your team.
* **Project Cost** (`project_cost`, number, required/optional): Enter the estimated cost of your project.
* **Project Notes** (`project_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional project notes or comments.
* **Project Approver** (`project_approver`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the project approvers (Approver 1, Approver 2, or Approver 3).
* **Approver Notes** (`approver_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional approver notes or comments.
* **Approver Status** (`approver_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the approver status (Active, Inactive, Pending, or Cancelled).
* **Approver Comments** (`approver_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional approver comments.
* **Approver Date** (`approver_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the approver has approved or rejected the project.
* **Approver Time** (`approver_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the approver has approved or rejected the project.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the approver.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the approver.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the approver.
* **Approver Signature** (`approver_signature`, text, required/optional): Enter the digital or physical signature of the approver.

Note: Please ensure you only fill out the form once the project has progressed to the review stage to avoid duplication and ensure accurate project information.
