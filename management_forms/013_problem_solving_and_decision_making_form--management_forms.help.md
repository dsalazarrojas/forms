# Problem Solving And Decision Making Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Problem Solving And Decision Making Form is a tool for documenting and analyzing problem-solving processes and decisions made within the organization. It helps teams and individuals reflect on their approach to problem-solving, identify areas for improvement, and track progress over time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each section based on your actual problem-solving process or decision.
2. Answer each question honestly and as accurately as possible.
3. Review the form before submitting to ensure all required and optional sections are complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Problem Solving** (Text):
	+ A brief description of the problem you were trying to solve.
* **Solutions** (Select Multiple: 'Yes', 'No'):
	+ List each solution you considered, with 'Yes' indicating you implemented it and 'No' indicating you did not.
* **Actions** (Text):
	+ Describe the steps you took to address the problem.
* **Outcomes** (Select One: 'Yes', 'No'):
	+ Indicate the outcome of your problem-solving process.
* **Solution Solved** (Text):
	+ Describe the solution you chose or implemented.
* **Problem Description** (Text):
	+ Provide a more detailed explanation of the problem you were trying to solve.
* **Solution Description** (Text):
	+ Describe the selected solution in more detail.
* **Actions Taken** (Text):
	+ Outline the actions you took to implement the solution.
* **Outcomes Observed** (Text):
	+ Report on any observed outcomes or results.
* **Lessons Learned** (Text):
	+ Share any lessons you learned from the experience.
* **Next Steps** (Text):
	+ Outline any next steps or follow-up actions needed.
* **Final Outcome** (Text):
	+ Summarize the final outcome of your problem-solving process.
* **Decision Making Process** (Text):
	+ Describe the process you used to make your decision.
* **Problem Solving Approach** (Text):
	+ Explain how you approached the problem.
* **Solution Solving Process** (Text):
	+ Describe how you implemented the solution.
* **Date Started** (Date):
	+ Enter the date you started the problem-solving process.
* **Date Completed** (Date):
	+ Enter the date you completed the problem-solving process.
* **Time Required** (Text):
	+ Report the time required to solve the problem.
* **Time Spent** (Number):
	+ Estimate the time spent on solving the problem.
* **Time Savings** (Text):
	+ Calculate any time savings resulting from your solution.
* **Resources Allocated** (Text):
	+ List any resources (e.g., people, budget) allocated to solve the problem.
* **Cost Benefit Analysis** (Text):
	+ Perform a cost-benefit analysis of your solution.
* **Risk Factors** (Text):
	+ Identify any risk factors you considered.
* **Solution Owners** (Text):
	+ List the individuals or teams involved in solving the problem.
* **Review Status** (Select One: 'Active', 'Inactive'):
	+ Indicate the current status of your review of the problem-solving process.
* **Review Status Hint:** The review status indicates whether the problem-solving process has been reviewed and approved, or if it's still pending or not reviewed yet.

Note: Please ensure you fill out all required fields (marked as 'Required') and any other fields that are necessary to provide a complete and accurate account of your problem-solving process.
