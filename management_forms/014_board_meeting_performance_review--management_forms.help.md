# Board Meeting Performance Review - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and performance metrics on the board meeting from a reviewer, typically an executive director or another board member. The review period, reviewer's name and role, and specific aspects of board performance are evaluated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the review period from the list.
2. Enter the name of the person completing the review.
3. Select your role on the board.
4. Enter the total number of meetings held during the review period.
5. Rate the average attendance rate of board members.
6. Evaluate the board's preparedness, strategic discussions, financial oversight, risk management, and executive director support.
7. Rate the quality of decisions made by the board.
8. Indicate whether action items were completed.
9. Describe the board dynamics during the meetings.
10. Rate the board's compliance with governance policies.
11. Rate the overall performance of the board.
12. Provide any additional feedback on key strengths, areas for improvement, priorities for the next period, or any other suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Review Period** (`review_period`, `select_one`): Select the time period being reviewed, such as Q1 2026 or Custom Period.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, `text`): Enter the name of the person completing the review.
* **Reviewer Role** (`reviewer_role`, `select_one`): Select your role on the board, such as Board Chair, Vice Chair, or Board Member.
* **Number of Meetings Held** (`meetings_held`, `number`): Enter the total number of meetings held during the review period.
* **Average Attendance Rate** (`average_attendance_rate`, `select_one`): Rate the average attendance percentage of board members, such as 100%, 90-99%, or Below 70%.
* **Board Preparedness** (`board_preparedness`, `select_one`): Evaluate the board's preparedness, such as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Strategic Discussions Quality** (`strategic_discussions`, `select_one`): Rate the quality of strategic conversations, such as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Financial Oversight** (`financial_oversight`, `select_one`): Evaluate the board's financial review, such as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Risk Management Review** (`risk_management_review`, `select_one`): Rate the board's risk management, such as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Executive Director Support** (`executive_director_support`, `select_one`): Evaluate the board's support for the executive director, such as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Not Applicable.
* **Committee Effectiveness** (`committee_effectiveness`, `select_one`): Rate the committee reports, such as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or No Committees.
* **Decision Quality** (`decision_quality`, `select_one`): Evaluate the quality of decisions made by the board, such as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Follow-Through on Actions** (`follow_through_on_actions`, `select_one`): Indicate whether action items were completed, such as All actions completed, Most actions completed, Some actions completed, Few actions completed, or No actions completed.
* **Board Dynamics** (`board_dynamics`, `select_one`): Describe the board interactions, such as Highly Collaborative, Collaborative, Neutral, Somewhat Contentious, or Highly Contentious.
* **Governance Compliance** (`governance_compliance`, `select_one`): Rate the board's compliance with governance policies, such as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`overall_performance_rating`, `select_one`): Rate the overall board performance, such as 5 - Outstanding, 4 - Exceeds Expectations, 3 - Meets Expectations, 2 - Below Expectations, or 1 - Unsatisfactory.
* **Key Strengths** (`key_strengths`, `text`): Provide any additional feedback on what the board did well.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`): Indicate any areas that require improvement.
* **Priorities for Next Period** (`priorities_next_period`, `text`): Suggest any priorities for the next review period.
* **Additional Recommendations** (`additional_recommendations`, `text`): Provide any other suggestions or comments.
