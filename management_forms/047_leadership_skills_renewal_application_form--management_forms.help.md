# leadership_skills_renewal_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The leadership skills renewal application form is designed to collect information about your leadership skills and experiences. This form will help your organization assess your leadership development and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the **introduction** field, which is a text field where you can provide a brief background about yourself and your leadership experience.
2.  Enter your **name** in the corresponding field.
3.  Provide your **email** address in the email field.
4.  Enter your **phone number** in the phone number field.
5.  Describe your **work experience** in this field.
6.  Select your relevant **leadership certification** from the provided options.
7.  Choose relevant **training providers** that you've received training from (you can select multiple providers).
8.  Select your **certification level**, which may affect the evaluation of your leadership skills.
9.  Submit the form once you've completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Introduction** (Text): Briefly describe yourself and your leadership experience.
*  **Name** (Text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
*  **Email** (Email): Enter your professional email address.
*  **Phone Number** (Text): Enter your primary phone number.
*  **Work Experience** (Text): Describe your work experience and relevant leadership roles.
*  **Leadership Certification** (Select One): Choose the leadership certification that best describes your level of certification. 
*  **Training Provider** (Select Multiple): Select the training providers where you've received relevant leadership training.
*  **Certification Level** (Select One): Choose the level of your leadership certification, which may impact the evaluation of your leadership skills.
