# Organic Supplier Renewal Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Organic Supplier Renewal Application Form is used to collect information from organic suppliers for the purpose of renewing their supplier agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Carefully review the form**: Take your time to ensure you understand the questions and provide accurate information.
2. **Fill in the form**: Enter the required information in each field, making sure to provide clear and concise answers.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send it to our team for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Details (<id: 1>):**
	+ Label: Supplier Details
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Enter your company name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Certification (<id: 2>):**
	+ Label: Certification
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Purpose: Select the applicable certification(s) you hold, such as Organic 1.2 or Organic 1.3.
* **Product Categories (<id: 3>):**
	+ Label: Product Categories
	+ Type: Select Multiple
	+ Purpose: Select all applicable product categories that you offer, such as Category 1 or Category 2.
* **Certifications (<id: 4>):**
	+ Label: Certifications
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: List any additional certifications you hold, if applicable.
* **Product Information (<id: 5>):**
	+ Label: Product Information
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Provide details about your products, including features and benefits.
* **Contact Details (<id: 6>):**
	+ Label: Contact Details
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Enter your company's contact details, such as address and phone number.
* **Email (<id: 7>):**
	+ Label: Email
	+ Type: Email
	+ Purpose: Enter your company email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (<id: 8>):**
	+ Label: Phone
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Enter your company phone number for communication purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your selections before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
