# Development Priorities Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about project characteristics, team involvement, and stakeholder relationships to help project teams and stakeholders prioritize development projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully, and fill out the relevant fields as applicable.
2. For multi-select fields (e.g., "Stakeholders Involved", "Business Stakeholder", "Tech Stakeholder"), select all relevant options that apply.
3. For single-select fields (e.g., "Project Status", "Project Lead", "Customer"), select the most accurate option that applies to your project.
4. Enter text in the "Project Overview" field to provide a brief description of your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Overview** (`project_overview`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your project, including its purpose, goals, and objectives.
* **Project Scope** (`project_scope`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether your project is scoped to:
	+ Yes: The project scope is well-defined and includes all necessary tasks.
	+ No: The project scope is not fully defined or is subject to changes.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, optional): Identify the current status of your project:
	+ Active: The project is currently ongoing and progressing.
	+ Inactive: The project is paused or has been completed.
* **Project Team Size** (`project_team_size`, number, optional): Enter the size of your project team, including team members and their respective roles.
* **Stakeholders Involved** (`stakeholders_involved`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the involvement of the following stakeholders:
	+ Yes: The customer is involved in the project.
	+ No: The customer is not involved in the project.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, select_one, optional): Identify who is leading your project:
	+ Yes: A project lead is assigned and actively involved.
	+ No: There is no project lead assigned or involved.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, select_one, optional): Identify who is managing your project:
	+ Yes: A project manager is assigned and actively involved.
	+ No: There is no project manager assigned or involved.
* **Business Stakeholder** (`business_stakeholder`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the involvement of business stakeholders:
	+ Yes: Business stakeholders are involved in the project.
	+ No: Business stakeholders are not involved in the project.
* **Tech Stakeholder** (`tech_stakeholder`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the involvement of technical stakeholders:
	+ Yes: Technical stakeholders are involved in the project.
	+ No: Technical stakeholders are not involved in the project.
* **Customer** (`customer`, select_one, optional): Identify the customer for your project:
	+ Yes: The customer is involved in the project.
	+ No: The customer is not involved in the project.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when answering each field to ensure that project priorities are set effectively.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with project team members or stakeholders to clarify.
* This form is a tool to help project teams and stakeholders communicate effectively; use it to facilitate collaboration and project success.
