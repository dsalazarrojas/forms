# Expense Management Innovation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Management Innovation Registration form is designed to collect and register information about new expense management innovations or projects within an organization. It serves as a tool for tracking and organizing project details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the "Registration Form" section, which is used to provide a brief overview of your project.
2. In the "Additional Information" section, add any further details or comments related to your project.
3. In the "Team Information" section, list the team members or key contacts involved in your project.
4. If applicable, enter the expected budget for your project in the "Budget" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Registration Form
**Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required: false)
This is the main section where you provide a brief overview of your project.

### Additional Information
**Additional Information** (`registration_form_2`, text, required: false)
Use this field to add any additional comments or details about your project.

### Team Information
**Team Information** (`registration_form_3`, number, required: false)
List the team members or key contacts involved in your project.

### Budget
**Budget** (`registration_form_4`, number, required: false)
Enter the expected budget for your project if applicable.

## Tips
- Fill in the fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure proper project registration.
- Ensure that any additional information added is relevant and useful for project tracking and reporting.
- If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your project coordinator or relevant department for clarification.
