# Monthly Inventory Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly Inventory Assessment form is a tool used to track and record inventory movements and issues on a monthly basis. It helps the inventory management team to monitor and manage stock levels accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the month for which you are reporting inventory data (Page 1).
2. Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the stock (Fields 1-2).
3. Select the current stock count (Field 4).
4. Determine if there are any stock incoming or outgoing (Fields 5-6).
5. Enter any reason for stock movement (Field 7).
6. Record the quantity of stock moving (Field 8).
7. Identify the type of location where the stock is being moved (Field 9).
8. Document any issues with stock (Fields 10-11).
9. Specify the stock location (Field 12).
10. Enter any location notes (Field 13).
11. Record the stock status (Field 14).
12. Enter the date and time of the stock entry (Fields 15-16).
13. Add any stock notes (Field 17).
14. Enter contact email and phone number (Fields 18-19).
15. Record the issue date and time (Fields 20-21).
16. Specify any follow-up actions (Field 22).
17. Set a follow-up date (Field 23).
18. Specify who will follow up (Field 24).
19. Add any additional comments (Field 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User (1)**: Enter your name to identify yourself as the person responsible for the stock.
* **Location (2)**: Enter the location where the stock is located.
* **Month (3)**: Select the month for which you are reporting inventory data.
* **Stock Count (4)**: Enter the current stock count.
* **Stock Incoming (5)**: Select if there is any stock incoming.
* **Stock Outgoing (6)**: Select if there is any stock outgoing.
* **Reason (7)**: Enter the reason for stock movement.
* **Quantity (8)**: Record the quantity of stock moving.
* **Location Type (9)**: Select the type of location where the stock is being moved.
* **Issue (10)**: Document any issues with stock.
* **Issue Details (11)**: Enter details about the issue.
* **Stock Location (12)**: Specify the stock location.
* **Location Notes (13)**: Enter any location notes.
* **Stock Status (14)**: Record the stock status.
* **Stock Date (15)**: Enter the date of the stock entry.
* **Stock Time (16)**: Enter the time of the stock entry.
* **Stock Note (17)**: Add any stock notes.
* **Email (18)**: Enter your email for contact purposes.
* **Phone (19)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Issue Date (20)**: Enter the date of the issue.
* **Issue Time (21)**: Enter the time of the issue.
* **Follow Up (22)**: Specify any follow-up actions.
* **Follow Up Date (23)**: Set a follow-up date.
* **Follow Up By (24)**: Specify who will follow up.
* **Comments (25)**: Add any additional comments.
