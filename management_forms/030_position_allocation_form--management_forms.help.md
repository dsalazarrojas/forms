# Position Allocation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Position Allocation Form is used to collect and manage information about job positions within the organization. It helps to ensure that all necessary details are captured and recorded accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "New Form" button to create a new form.
2. Select the department for the position from the options provided.
3. Enter the position title.
4. Enter the effective start date of the position.
5. Enter the number of prophets (if applicable).
6. Select the manager's name (if applicable).
7. Enter the effective end date of the position (if applicable).
8. Enter the reason for the change (if applicable).
9. Review and submit the form.
