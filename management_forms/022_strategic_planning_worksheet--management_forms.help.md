# Strategic Planning Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strategic Planning Worksheet is a tool for organizations to create a comprehensive strategic plan. It helps in outlining and defining the company's goals, vision, mission, and objectives, and aids in tracking progress and setting target outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the company information section with relevant details about your organization.
2. Describe your company's goals and vision in the "Goals" and "Vision" sections.
3. Define your company's mission and objectives in the "Mission" and "Objectives" sections.
4. Identify the key performance indicators (KPIs) that measure progress in the "Key Performance Indicators" section.
5. Set a date for progress tracking and review in the "Review Period" and "Review Frequency" sections.
6. Define the target and actual outcomes in the "Target Outcome" and "Actual Outcome" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Information (1)** (`Company Name`, text, required): This field is used to input the name of your organization.
* **Goals (2)** (`What are your company's goals?`, text, required): This field is used to describe the goals of your organization. Be as specific as possible when defining what your organization wants to achieve.
* **Company Name (3)** (`Company Name`, text, required): This field is used to reiterate the name of your organization.
* **Vision (4)** (`Vision`, text, required): This field is used to describe the long-term vision of your organization.
* **Mission (5)** (`Mission`, text, required): This field is used to define the purpose and goals of your organization.
* **Objectives (6)** (`Objectives`, text, required): This field is used to describe the objectives or goals of your organization in a specific timeframe.
* **Key Performance Indicators (7)** (`Key Performance Indicators`, text, required): This field is used to identify the performance metrics that measure progress towards the goals and objectives defined earlier.
* **Progress Tracking (8)** (`Progress Tracking`, text, required): This field is used to track progress towards the goals and objectives.
* **Target Outcome (9)** (`Target Outcome`, text, required): This field is used to describe the expected outcome or result of your organization's efforts.
* **Actual Outcome (10)** (`Actual Outcome`, text, required): This field is used to report on the actual outcome or result of your organization's efforts.
* **Review Period (11)** (`Review Period`, date, optional): This field is used to set a date for progress review.
* **Review Frequency (12)** (`Review Frequency`, text, optional): This field is used to define how often progress reviews should be conducted.
* **Target Date (13)** (`Target Date`, date, optional): This field is used to set a target date for completion or achievement.

## Tips
- Be specific when defining goals, vision, and objectives.
- Use relevant and measurable key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Track progress regularly to stay on track.
- Review progress against targets to make adjustments as needed.
