# Departmental Budget Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request budget support for departmental projects. It is designed to gather necessary information to assess and process your request in a timely and accurate manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide a clear and concise description of your project in the "Project Description" field.
2. Enter the amount of funding requested for your project in the "Funding Request" field.
3. If applicable, attach supporting documents to substantiate your request in the "Supporting Documents" field.
4. Select the "Project Lead" responsible for the project from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Department Request** (`department_request_details`, text, optional): 
  A brief description of the departmental project, explaining why you are requesting support.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): 
  A few sentences describing the project, highlighting its key objectives and outcomes.
* **Funding Request** (`funding_request_amount`, number, optional): 
  The total amount of funding requested for the project, including any specific allocations or allocations.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): 
  The date on which you are submitting this request. Please note that submission dates may have specific deadlines.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_multiple, optional): 
  Attach relevant documents, such as project plans, proposals, or budget breakdowns, to support your request.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, select_one, optional): 
  Select the individual or team member primarily responsible for the project's management and implementation.
