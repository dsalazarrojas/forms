# Work Life Balance Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Work Life Balance Acknowledgment Form is designed to collect feedback from employees on the company's Work Life Balance (WLB) policies. Completing this form will help the company understand employee perspectives and experiences with these policies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please select the options that best represent your experiences with the company's WLB policies. If you are unsure or have not reviewed the policies, select "Not Sure" or "False" as applicable.

1. **Work Life Balance Policies** (`work_life_balance_policies`, text, not required): Describe your understanding of the company's WLB policies.
2. **Have reviewed policies** (`have_reviewed_policies`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have reviewed the company's WLB policies. You can choose from:
	* I
	* False
	* Not Sure
3. **Rate satisfaction** (`rate_policies_satisfaction`, select_multiple, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the company's WLB policies. You can choose from:
	* Very Good
	* Good
	* Neutral
	* Not Good
	* Very Not Good
4. **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required): Share any comments or suggestions you have about the company's WLB policies.
5. **Have reviewed policy changes** (`have_reviewed_policy_changes`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have reviewed any changes to the company's WLB policies. You can choose from:
	* True
	* False

6. **Rate policy changes** (`rate_policy_changes_satisfaction`, select_multiple, not required): Rate your satisfaction with the changes made to the company's WLB policies. You can choose from:
