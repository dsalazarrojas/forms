# Duty Sheet For Missed Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track services that have not been completed as scheduled. It helps management to keep track of missed services and ensures that follow-up actions are taken to resolve the issue. This form is meant to be filled out by staff members when a service is missed and is used to document the reason for the missed service and the follow-up actions taken.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the **missed service** field with a brief description of the missed service.
2. Enter the **date** when the service was scheduled but not completed.
3. Type out the **reason** for the missed service, such as a reason provided by the staff member, the supervisor, or any other relevant information.
4. Choose the **staff member** who was supposed to perform the missed service.
5. Choose the **supervisor** who is responsible for the missed service.
6. Enter any **notes** about the situation, such as additional information or details.
7. Select the **assigned to** field to specify who will be responsible for following up on the missed service.
8. Enter the **follow-up date** and **follow-up time** when the next follow-up action is scheduled.
9. Enter the **follow-up contact** information, such as the phone number or email address of the person to contact.
10. Select the **follow-up status** to indicate the current status of the follow-up action.
11. Enter any **follow-up notes** about the situation.
12. Enter the **next follow-up action** to be taken.
13. Enter the **next follow-up contact** information.
14. Select the **next follow-up status** to indicate the status of the next follow-up action.
15. Enter the **next follow-up date** and **next follow-up time** when the next follow-up action is scheduled.
16. Enter any additional **next follow-up notes** about the situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **missed service** (`missed_service`, number, required): Enter a brief description of the missed service.
* **date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date when the service was scheduled but not completed.
* **reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Type out the reason for the missed service.
* **staff member** (`staff_member`, text, optional): Choose the staff member who was supposed to perform the missed service.
* **supervisor** (`supervisor`, text, optional): Choose the supervisor who is responsible for the missed service.
* **notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or details about the situation.
