# sustainability_policy_review_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for a policy review. A policy review is a process where a manager or department head reviews and assesses a policy to ensure it aligns with the company's goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Request details** field with a brief description of the policy you are requesting to be reviewed.
2. Select the appropriate **Policy review status** from the dropdown list to indicate the current status of the policy.
3. Choose the **Priority** of the policy review request, if applicable.
4. If applicable, provide the name of the person who requested the policy review in the **Requested by** field.
5. Enter the **Review date** and **Review time** for the policy review, if applicable.
6. Add any supporting **Documents** related to the policy request.
7. Provide any additional **Comments** about the policy review request.
8. If applicable, enter the information for **Policy update**.
9. Finally, select the **Policy areas** (e.g. Sustainability, Financial, Environmental, Operational, Strategic) that the policy review request is related to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request details**: A brief description of the policy you are requesting to be reviewed.
* **Policy review status**: The current status of the policy (Requested for review, In review, Approved, Rejected).
* **Priority**: The level of importance of the policy review request (Low, Medium, High).
* **Requested by**: The name of the person who requested the policy review.
* **Review date**: The date when the policy review is scheduled to take place.
* **Review time**: The time when the policy review is scheduled to take place.
* **Documents**: Any supporting documents related to the policy request.
* **Comments**: Additional information or notes about the policy review request.
* **Policy update**: Information about any updates to the policy.
* **Policy areas**: The areas of the company that the policy review request is related to (Sustainability, Financial, Environmental, Operational, Strategic).
* **Review request**: Additional information or notes about the review request.
