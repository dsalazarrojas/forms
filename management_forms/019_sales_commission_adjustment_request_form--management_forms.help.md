# Sales Commission Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request adjustments to sales commission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user name in the "User" field.
2. Provide a brief explanation for the adjustment in the "Reason" field.
3. Enter the amount of the adjustment in the "Amount" field.
4. Enter the date of the adjustment in the "Date" field.
5. If necessary, attach supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents" field.
6. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager" field.
7. Enter your sales representative's name in the "Sales Rep" field.
8. Select the type of adjustment you are requesting ("Increase" or "Decrease") in the "Amount Type" field.
9. Enter the actual value of the adjustment in the "Amount Value" field.
10. Optionally, enter any additional information about the reason in the "Reason Value" field.
11. Optionally, enter any additional information about the supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents Value" field.
12. Enter any additional information about your manager's feedback in the "Manager Value" field.
13. Enter any additional information about the sales representative's feedback in the "Sales Rep Value" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User**: Your name as a sales representative.
  * A required field where you should enter your full name as a sales representative.
* **Reason**: Briefly explain the reason for the commission adjustment.
  * This field is optional, but it is recommended to provide a clear explanation for the adjustment request.
* **Amount**: The amount of the adjustment.
  * A required field where you should enter the actual amount of the adjustment.
* **Date**: The date of the adjustment.
  * An optional field where you can enter the date of the adjustment.
* **Supporting Documents**: Any additional documents that support your adjustment request.
  * An optional field where you can attach relevant documents.
* **Manager**: Your manager's name.
  * An optional field where you can enter your manager's name.
* **Sales Rep**: Your sales representative's name.
  * An optional field where you can enter your sales representative's name.
* **Amount Type**: The type of adjustment you are requesting ("Increase" or "Decrease").
  * A required field where you should select the type of adjustment.
* **Amount Value**: The actual value of the adjustment.
  * A required field where you should enter the actual value of the adjustment.
* **Reason Value**: Any additional information about the reason.
  * An optional field where you can enter any additional information about the reason.
* **Supporting Documents Value**: Any additional information about the supporting documents.
  * An optional field where you can enter any additional information about the supporting documents.
* **Manager Value**: Any additional information about your manager's feedback.
  * An optional field where you can enter any additional information about your manager's feedback.
* **Sales Rep Value**: Any additional information about the sales representative's feedback.
  * An optional field where you can enter any additional information about the sales representative's feedback.
