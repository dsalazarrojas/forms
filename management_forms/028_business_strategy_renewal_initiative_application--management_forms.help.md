# Business Strategy Renewal Initiative Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for submitting business strategy renewal initiatives to senior management. It requires applicants to provide detailed information about their proposals, including the initiative's title, description, strategic alignment, and expected outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
2.  Type your current role or title within the company.
3.  Enter the name of the department you belong to.
4.  Enter your company's name as it appears on official documents.
5.  Enter the date of submission in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
6.  Enter the title of the strategy renewal initiative.
7.  Write a detailed description of the proposal.
8.  Briefly describe how the initiative aligns with the company's strategy.
9.  Describe the current state of affairs related to the proposal.
10.  Specify what you want to achieve through this proposal.
11.  Define how you plan to measure success.
12.  Provide a timeline for the initiative, including key milestones and the completion date.
13.  Enter the total budget required for the initiative.
14.  List the people, tools, or assets needed for the initiative.
15.  Identify any stakeholders that need to be involved in the process.
16.  Enumerate the expected benefits of the proposal.
17.  List potential challenges or risks associated with the proposal.
18.  Describe how you will address these risks.
19.  If you have any additional information, enter it here.
20.  Make a recommendation to senior management regarding approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Role or Title (2)**: Type your current role or title within the company.
* **Department (3)**: Enter the name of the department you belong to.
* **Company Name (4)**: Enter your company's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Application Date (5)**: Enter the date of submission in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Initiative Title (6)**: Enter the title of the strategy renewal initiative.
* **Initiative Description (7)**: Write a detailed description of the proposal.
* **Strategic Alignment (8)**: Briefly describe how the initiative aligns with the company's strategy.
* **Current Situation (9)**: Describe the current state of affairs related to the proposal.
* **Desired Outcome (10)**: Specify what you want to achieve through this proposal.
* **Success Metrics (11)**: Define how you plan to measure success.
* **Proposed Timeline (12)**: Provide a timeline for the initiative, including key milestones and the completion date.
* **Budget Required (13)**: Enter the total budget required for the initiative.
* **Resource Requirements (14)**: List the people, tools, or assets needed for the initiative.
* **Stakeholders Involved (15)** (Optional): Identify any stakeholders that need to be involved in the process.
* **Expected Benefits (16)**: Enumerate the expected benefits of the proposal.
* **Risks Identified (17)** (Optional): List potential challenges or risks associated with the proposal.
* **Risk Mitigation Strategies (18)** (Optional): Describe how you will address these risks.
* **Internal Recommendation (19)** (Optional): Make a recommendation to senior management regarding approval.
* **Additional Notes (20)** (Optional): Enter any additional information you have about the proposal.
