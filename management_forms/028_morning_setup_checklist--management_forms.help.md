# Morning Setup Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help teams quickly report on their morning setup status, including any issues or goals they've encountered, their readiness status, and other important details. This information helps team leaders and members stay informed and work efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the status for each section, such as "Morning Not Ready" or "Morning Ready".
2. Answer any text fields, such as "morning tasks" or "notes".
3. For select_multiple fields, choose one or more options from the list provided.
4. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Morning setup checklist** (`morning_setup_checklist_page`): Enter a brief description of your morning setup, if necessary.
  * A text field to provide a brief description of your morning setup.
  * Required: No
- **Morning tasks** (`morning_tasks`): Select all tasks completed during the morning.
  * A select_multiple field to choose one or more tasks from the list.
  * Required: No
  * Examples: "Tasks completed: Yes, No"
- **Issues and goals** (`issues_and_goals`): Enter any issues or goals encountered during the morning.
  * A text field to provide details about any issues or goals.
  * Required: No
- **Readiness status** (`readiness_status`): Select your overall morning readiness status.
  * A select_one field to choose one option from the list.
  * Required: No
  * Examples: "Morning Not Ready", "Morning Ready", "On Break"
- **Shift handover issues** (`shift_handover_issues`): Enter any issues or concerns with the shift handover.
  * A text field to provide details about any issues or concerns.
  * Required: No
- **Team readiness** (`team_readiness`): Select your team's overall readiness status.
  * A select_one field to choose one option from the list.
  * Required: No
  * Examples: "Team Not Ready", "Team Ready"
- **Morning comments** (`morning_comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes.
  * A text field to provide any additional comments or notes.
  * Required: No
- **Issues and goals 2** (`issues_and_goals_2`): Enter any additional issues or goals.
  * A text field to provide details about any additional issues or goals.
  * Required: No
- **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments.
  * A text field to provide any additional notes or comments.
  * Required: No
- **Morning Routine Issues** (`morning_routine_issues`): Select all issues encountered during the morning routine.
  * A select_multiple field to choose one or more options from the list.
  * Required: No
  * Examples: "Yes, No"
- **Morning Routine Goals** (`morning_routine_goals`): Select all goals achieved during the morning routine.
  * A select_multiple field to choose one or more options from the list.
  * Required: No
  * Examples: "Yes, No"
- **Morning Routine Comments** (`morning_routine_comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the morning routine.
  * A text field to provide any additional comments or notes about the morning routine.
  * Required: No
- **Morning Status** (`morning_status`): Select the overall status of the morning.
  * A select_one field to choose one option from the list.
  * Required: No
  * Examples: "Active", "Inactive"
- **Morning Status 2** (`morning_status_2`): Select the overall status of the morning.
  * A select_one field to choose one option from the list.
  * Required: No
  * Examples: "Active", "Inactive"
