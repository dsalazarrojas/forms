## Business Performance Modeling Request Form - Help Guide

### Purpose

This form is used by employees to request business performance modeling support from the business analytics team. It helps us understand the business objectives, performance metrics, and other requirements to provide accurate and timely analysis.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your request date to track when this form was submitted.
2. Provide your full name as the requester.
3. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you for further discussions.
5. Enter your company name to identify the organization.
6. Specify the department or division related to your request.
7. Clearly state the purpose of your business performance modeling request.
8. List your business objectives.
9. Specify the current performance metrics you are tracking.
10. Identify the key variables you want to focus on.
11. Indicate whether you have enough data available for the analysis.
12. List the documents you can provide to support your request.
13. If applicable, mention any budget constraints.
14. Specify when you expect results from the analysis (a specific date).
15. Choose the desired complexity level of the model (Simple, Moderate, Complex, or Very Complex).
16. Identify the key stakeholders who need to be involved in the analysis.
17. Describe the expected outcomes from the analysis.
18. If applicable, provide any additional requirements or special requests.
19. Enter the name and contact information of the best person to contact for any questions.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Request Date** (`request_date`, date): Date of this request.
*   **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, text): Full name of the requester.
*   **Contact Email** (`requester_email`, email): Email address of the requester for communication purposes.
*   **Contact Phone** (`requester_phone`, text): Phone number of the requester for further discussions.
*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, text): Organization name.
*   **Department/Division** (`department_name`, text): Department or division related to the request.
*   **Purpose of Modeling** (`modeling_purpose`, text): What is the purpose of the business performance modeling request?
*   **Business Objectives** (`business_objectives`, text): What are the business objectives.
*   **Current Performance Metrics** (`current_metrics`, text): What performance metrics are you tracking.
*   **Key Variables** (`key_variables`, text): What variables matter most.
*   **Data Availability** (`data_availability`, select_one): Indicate the availability of data.
*   **Available Documents** (`available_documents`, text): What documents can be provided to support the request.
*   **Budget Constraints** (`budget_constraints`, text): If applicable, mention any budget limitations.
*   **Timeline Requirements** (`timeline_requirements`, date): When do you expect results from the analysis?
*   **Complexity Level Needed** (`complexity_level`, select_one): Choose the desired complexity level of the model.
*   **Stakeholders** (`stakeholders`, text): List the key stakeholders who need to be involved.
*   **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text): Describe the expected outcomes from the analysis.
*   **Additional Requirements** (`additional_requirements`, text): If applicable, provide any additional requirements or special requests.
*   **Best Contact for Questions** (`contact_for_questions`, text): Enter the name and contact information of the best person to contact for questions.

### Tips

*   Fill out this form carefully to ensure your request is thoroughly understood.
*   Please be as specific as possible when providing information.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, use the contact information provided above.
*   We'll get back to you promptly with further questions or any additional requirements.
*   Thank you for using this form to communicate your business performance modeling request!
