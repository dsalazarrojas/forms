# Daily Team Huddle Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Team Huddle Report Form is a tool for teams to report their progress, address concerns, and set priorities for the day. This form helps maintain open communication, ensures everyone is on the same page, and provides a clear picture of the team's overall status.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the team name and date fields, which are required to identify the team and the reporting date.
2. Select the team lead's email address from the provided options to ensure that they are informed of any updates or concerns.
3. Choose whether team members are present or not by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' for the team members field.
4. Select the highest priority for the day from the provided options, which can help focus the team's efforts.
5. Enter any action items for team members to take after the meeting, which can help ensure tasks are completed.
6. Provide next steps for team members to take after the meeting, which can help maintain momentum.
7. If desired, add any additional notes to provide further context or information.
8. Enter the team lead's email address to ensure they are informed of any updates or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name (text)**: Enter the name of your team to help identify the team's reporting status.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date you are submitting the report, which helps track progress over time.
* **Team Lead (text)**: Enter the email address of your team lead, which will receive updates and concerns.
* **Team Members (select_multiple)**: Select 'Yes' for team members who are present and 'No' for those who are not.
* **Highest Priority (select_one)**: Select the highest priority for the team's efforts on that day.
* **Next Steps (text)**: Enter the next steps team members should take after the meeting, which helps maintain momentum.
* **Notes (text)**: Add any additional context or information to further clarify the team's status.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the team lead's email address to ensure they receive updates and concerns.
* **Phone Number (text)**: Enter a contact number for the team lead, which can be used to communicate with the team lead.

## Tips
* Make sure to update the form daily to maintain open communication and clear priorities.
* Review and update the form before and after the meeting to ensure accurate reporting.
* Use the 'Next Steps' field to provide actionable tasks for team members to complete.
