# Medical Device Maintenance Record Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record the maintenance history of a medical device. It captures essential information related to the service, repair, and maintenance of the device. Completing this form ensures that accurate and up-to-date records are kept for future reference and for regulatory compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief description of the service, repair, or maintenance performed on the medical device.
2. Select the type of service (Service, Repair, or Calibration) from the options provided.
3. Enter the date of the service, using the date format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Enter the serial number of the medical device.
5. Describe the location where the medical device is used or stored.
6. Enter the next scheduled service date, if applicable.
7. Provide a detailed description of the service, including any relevant notes.
8. Sign and identify the technician who performed the service.
9. Enter the department responsible for the medical device, if applicable.
10. Add any additional notes, if necessary.
11. Record the current condition of the equipment.
12. Enter the cost of the service.
13. Enter the name of the person or department who will be responsible for future services.
14. Enter the contact information for the department or person responsible for the medical device.
15. Enter the phone number of the department or person responsible for the medical device.
16. Enter the fax number of the department or person responsible for the medical device.
17. Enter the email address of the department or person responsible for the medical device.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service History**: A brief description of the service, repair, or maintenance performed on the medical device.
* **Medical Device**: The name of the medical device being serviced.
* **Device Serial Number**: The serial number of the medical device, used for identification and tracking.
* **Equipment Location**: The department, ward, or location where the medical device is used or stored.
* **Service Type**: The type of service performed on the medical device (Service, Repair, or Calibration).
* **Service Date**: The date the service was performed, using the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Next Service Date**: The date of the next scheduled service, if applicable.
* **Service Description**: A detailed description of the service, including any relevant notes.
* **Technician Name**: The name of the technician who performed the service.
* **Technician Signature**: The signature of the technician who performed the service.
* **Department**: The department responsible for the medical device, if applicable.
* **Notes**: Additional notes or comments about the service.
* **Equipment Condition**: A description of the current condition of the equipment.
* **Service Cost**: The cost of the service performed.
* **Service By**: The name of the person or department responsible for future services.
* **Phone**: The contact phone number of the department or person responsible for the medical device.
* **Fax**: The contact fax number of the department or person responsible for the medical device.
* **Email**: The contact email address of the department or person responsible for the medical device.
* **Contact Person**: The name of the person or contact for the department or medical device.
* **Department Phone**: The contact phone number of the department or person responsible for the medical device.
* **Department Fax**: The contact fax number of the department or person responsible for the medical device.
* **Department Email**: The contact email address of the department or person responsible for the medical device.
