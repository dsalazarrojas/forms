# key_issuance_log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to log the issuance and return of keys, such as keys to a building or a vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by reading through the form carefully to ensure you understand what information is required and what each field is used for.
- Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, paying attention to the required fields.
- If you are unsure about any information, please ask for clarification from your supervisor or relevant personnel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Note): A brief introduction or description of the key issuance or return.
* **Key Type** (Text): Enter the type of key being issued or returned (e.g. vehicle key, building key, etc.).
* **Key Description** (Text): A brief description of the key being issued or returned.
* **Date Issued** (Date): The date the key was issued to the person or people listed in the "Issued To" field.
* **Issued To** (Select Multiple): Select the person or people that the key was issued to. This field can have multiple selections.
* **Returned By** (Select One): Select the person or people that returned the key. This field is required.
* **Returned Date** (Date): The date the key was returned. This field is optional.
* **Remarks** (Text): Any additional comments or information regarding the key issuance and return.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are populated with accurate and thorough information.
- If the "Returned Date" field is used, ensure it is the correct date the key was returned.
- If unsure about any information, please ask for clarification from your supervisor or relevant personnel.
