# Warehouse Staff ID Reissue Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Staff ID Reissue Request Form is used for Warehouse staff members to request a reissue of their Warehouse ID.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your Employee ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
2. In the "Reason for Reissue" field, enter the reason why you are requesting a reissue of your Warehouse ID.
3. Choose how you would like to be contacted in the "How would you like to be contacted" field.
4. Select the department that you belong to from the "Department" field.
5. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager's Name" field.
6. Enter your manager's email address in the "Manager's Email" field.
7. Enter your manager's phone number in the "Manager's Phone" field.
8. Select the date and time of your request from the "Date of Request" and "Time of Request" fields.
9. Select how your request was submitted from the "Submitted by" field.
10. Select the status of your request from the "Status" field.
11. Enter any additional remarks in the "Remarks" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee ID (1)
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, **Required**): Enter your Employee ID number.
### Reason for Reissue (2)
* **Reason for Reissue** (`reason_for_reissue`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the reason why you are requesting a reissue of your Warehouse ID.
### How would you like to be contacted (3)
* **How would you like to be contacted** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email or Phone).
### Department (5)
* **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select the department that you belong to.
### Manager's Name (6)
* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your manager's name.
### Manager's Email (7)
* **Manager's Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, **Required**): Enter your manager's email address.
### Manager's Phone (8)
* **Manager's Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your manager's phone number.
### Date of Request (9)
* **Date of Request** (`date_of_request`, `date`, **Required**): Select the date of your request.
### Time of Request (10)
* **Time of Request** (`time_of_request`, `time`, **Required**): Select the time of your request.
### Submitted by (11)
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select how your request was submitted (Warehouse Staff, Operations, or HR).
### Status (12)
* **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the status of your request (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
### Remarks (13)
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, `note`, **Optional**): Enter any additional remarks.
### Warehouse Staff ID Reissue Request Form (14)
* **Warehouse Staff ID Reissue Request Form** (`warehouse_staff_id_reissue_request_form_id`, `text`, **Not Required**): This field is automatically generated and not editable.
