# Product Lifecycle Management Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to manage product lifecycle stages and track changes. It captures essential information about product developments, updates, or retirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product name and description.
2. Select the lifecycle stage change (New Product Request, Product Update, or Product Retire).
3. Enter the target date and time if necessary.
4. Choose the approver for the request.
5. Select the status (Active or Inactive) if necessary.
6. Add any comments if needed.
7. Review your filled form to ensure all required fields are completed (Life Cycle Stage and Approver).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (required: false): Enter the name of the product you are requesting or updating.
* **Description** (required: false): Enter a brief description of the product.
* **Lifecycle Stage** (required: true): Select the lifecycle stage change (New Product Request, Product Update, or Product Retire).
* **Target Date** (required: false): Enter the target date for the product release or update.
* **Target Time** (required: false): Enter the target time for the product release or update.
* **Approver** (required: true): Choose the approver for the request.
* **Status** (required: false): Select the status (Active or Inactive) if necessary.
* **Comments** (required: false): Add any comments or notes about the product.
* **Comments** (required: false) - This is actually the **Comments** field which is a text field allowing to enter any comments or notes about the product.
