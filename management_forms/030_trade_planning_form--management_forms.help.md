# Trade Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help traders, brokers, and analysts plan and analyze trades. It collects essential information to create a comprehensive trade plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **trade** field with the type of trade you want to plan, e.g., stock, forex, or commodity.
2. Enter the **target** field with your expected profit or loss.
3. Enter the **stop loss** field with your maximum acceptable loss.
4. Enter the **size** field with the amount of capital you are willing to risk.
5. Enter the **rationale** field with a brief explanation of your trade strategy.
6. Enter the **discipline** field with any additional relevant information about your trade.
7. Enter the **review** field with any notes or comments about your trade.
8. Finally, enter the **data-driven decision** field with any relevant data or statistics that influenced your trade decision.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trade**: Enter the type of trade you want to plan, e.g., stock, forex, or commodity. This field helps you specify the type of trade.
* **Target**: Enter your expected profit or loss for the trade. This field helps you define your trading goals.
* **Stop Loss**: Enter your maximum acceptable loss for the trade. This field is crucial for risk management.
* **Size**: Enter the amount of capital you are willing to risk in the trade. This field helps you manage your position size.
* **Rationale**: Enter a brief explanation of your trade strategy. This field helps you document your thought process.
* **Discipline**: Enter any additional relevant information about your trade. This field is for any extra comments or notes.
* **Review**: Enter any notes or comments about your trade. This field is for future reference.
* **Data-Driven Decision**: Enter any relevant data or statistics that influenced your trade decision. This field is for data-driven traders.
