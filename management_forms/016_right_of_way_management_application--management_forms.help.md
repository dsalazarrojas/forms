# right_of_way_management_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Right of Way Management Application form is designed to collect relevant information for the management and processing of right of way applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your Name, Email, and Location fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
* Provide a clear and concise Purpose statement that explains the reason for the right of way application.
* Select the Start Date and End Date for the application, ensuring they are correct and consistent.
* Attach any relevant Documents that support your application.
* Finally, sign the User Signature field with your genuine signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`user_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, **Required**): Provide your official email address for communication purposes.
* **Location** (`location`, text, **Required**): Enter your location or the location related to the right of way application.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, **Required**): Clearly state the reason or purpose of the right of way application.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, **Required**): Select the start date of the right of way application.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, **Required**): Select the end date of the right of way application.
* **Documents** (`documents`, text, **Required**): Attach any relevant documents that support your application.
* **User Signature** (`user_signature`, text, **Required**): Sign the form with your genuine signature, indicating your agreement and consent to the application.
