# Inventory Management Trial Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering company and segment information for inventory management trials. Please ensure you are using this form for its intended purpose and not duplicating efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the segment leads relevant to your company from the provided options.
2. Enter the schedule trial date if you need to schedule a trial.
3. Enter the trial start and end dates if applicable.
4. Enter the company name and software name in the provided text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Segment Leads** (`segment_leads`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the leads that are relevant to your company from the provided options.
* **Schedule Trial** (`schedule_trial`, `date`, optional): If you need to schedule a trial, enter the date and time in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Trial Start Date** (`trial_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of the trial if it has a specific start date.
* **Trial End Date** (`trial_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of the trial if it has a specific end date.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Software** (`software`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the software you are using for inventory management.

## Tips
* Double-check that you have selected the correct segment leads for your company.
* Ensure the schedule trial, trial start, and trial end dates are accurate if applicable.
* Enter your company and software name correctly to avoid any errors.
