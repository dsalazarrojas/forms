# Daily Team Huddle Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Team Huddle Checklist is a simple form designed to help teams stay organized and on track. It's meant to be filled out daily, covering essential activities such as attendance, progress updates, and follow-up tasks.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form section by section**, starting with the "Standup Notes" section.
2. For each section, answer the questions truthfully and concisely.
3. **Make sure to fill out all required fields**, which are marked as "required" in the form.
4. **Use the options provided** for select fields, and select only one option that best answers the question.
5. **Save and submit the form** when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Standup Notes** (`1`): A text field for team members to write down their standup notes.
	+ This section is for brief notes from each team member about their current work and progress.
* **Attendance** (`2`): A select field for team members to choose their attendance status.
	+ Options: On Time, Late, Absent, Work from Home
* **Progress Updates** (`3`): A text field for team members to provide progress updates.
	+ This section is for team members to share any updates on their progress and accomplishments.
* **Blockers** (`4`): A text field for team members to list blockers or issues.
	+ This section is for team members to identify any obstacles or challenges they're facing.
* **Next Steps** (`5`): A text field for team members to outline next steps.
	+ This section is for team members to plan and schedule next actions.
* **Team Members** (`6`): A select-multiple field for team members to choose their attendance status.
	+ Options: John, Jane, Bob, Alice
* **Team Leads** (`7`): A select-multiple field for team leads to choose members for follow-up activities.
	+ Options: John, Jane, Bob
* **Action Items** (`8`): A text field for team members to list action items.
	+ This section is for team members to track and document actions.
* **Next Actions** (`9`): A text field for team members to outline next actions.
	+ This section is for team members to plan and schedule next actions.
* **Next Deadline** (`10`): A date field for team members to schedule next deadlines.
	+ This section is for team members to track and schedule upcoming deadlines.
* **Next Review** (`11`): A date field for team members to schedule next reviews.

* **Follow Up Reminders** (`14`): A number field for team leads to schedule follow-up reminders.
