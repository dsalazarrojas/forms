# Retail Store Closing Procedures Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by retail store employees to record and document the closing procedures at the end of each day.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each field explanation below to understand what information is required.
2. Complete the form by answering each question based on your experience and knowledge of the store's closing procedures.
3. Make sure to complete all the required fields (indicated by asterisk *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **End of Day Tasks** (`end_of_day_tasks`, text, optional): This field is intended to capture any specific tasks that need to be completed at the end of the day. Describe any tasks that were not completed as part of the closing procedures.
* **Closing Routines** (`closing_routines`, text, optional): This field is meant to record any recurring tasks that are part of the store's closing routines. Describe any tasks that are typically done at the end of each day.
* **Incidents Documented** (`incidents_documented`, select_one, required): This field is designed to capture any incidents that occurred during the day and should be documented. Select one of the options:
	+ Cash handling: Cash is counted and accounted for
	+ Security: All doors and gates are locked
	+ Security: All lights are turned off
	+ Security: All keys are returned to the safe
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, optional): This field is intended to capture any additional information about the incident that occurred during the day. Describe the incident in detail.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): This field is for recording the date the form is completed.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): This field is for recording the time the form is completed.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, text, optional): This field is intended to capture the location of the store where the form is being completed.

Note: The form is not required to be completed in a specific order, but it is recommended to complete it in a sequential manner to ensure accuracy and consistency.
