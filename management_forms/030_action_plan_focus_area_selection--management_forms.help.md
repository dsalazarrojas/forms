# Action Plan Focus Area Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form guides employees in selecting the primary and secondary focus areas for their action plans, defining specific objectives, and creating a comprehensive plan for achieving their performance goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in your personal information:
	* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
	* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee identification number.
	* **Department**: Enter the department where you work.
2. Select your **Primary Focus Area** from the list of options provided.
3. For each focus area, answer the following questions:
	* **Specific Objectives**: Describe what specific objectives you aim to achieve in the selected focus area.
	* **Current State Assessment**: Describe your current situation in the focus area.
	* **Desired State Outcome**: Describe what success looks like in the focus area.
	* **Action Steps**: List specific steps you will take to achieve your objectives.
	* **Target Completion Date**: Enter the date by which you expect to achieve your objectives.
4. If applicable, select an **Secondary Focus Area** from the list of options provided.
5. In the **Required Resources** field, list any resources you need to accomplish your action plan.
6. In the **Success Metrics** field, describe how you will measure success in achieving your objectives.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, text, required): Enter your employee identification number.
* **Department** (Department, text, required): Enter the department where you work.
* **Manager Name** (Manager Name, text, required): Enter the name of your direct manager.
* **Primary Focus Area** (Select One, required): Select the main area of focus for your action plan.
	+ Options: "Performance Improvement", "Skill Development", "Leadership Growth", "Process Optimization", "Team Collaboration", "Customer Relations", "Innovation", "Other"
* **Secondary Focus Area** (Select One, optional): Select an additional area of focus (if applicable).
	+ Options: "Performance Improvement", "Skill Development", "Leadership Growth", "Process Optimization", "Team Collaboration", "Customer Relations", "Innovation", "None"
* **Specific Objectives** (text, required): Describe what specific objectives you aim to achieve in the selected focus area.
* **Current State Assessment** (text, required): Describe your current situation in the focus area.
* **Desired State Outcome** (text, required): Describe what success looks like in the focus area.
* **Action Steps** (text, required): List specific steps you will take to achieve your objectives.
* **Target Completion Date** (Date, required): Enter the date by which you expect to achieve your objectives.
* **Required Resources** (text, optional): List any resources you need to accomplish your action plan.
* **Success Metrics** (text, optional): Describe how you will measure success in achieving your objectives.
