# Workforce Planning Matrix Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Planning Matrix Form is a tool for HR or management teams to collect information on departmental priorities, next steps, and resource needs. It helps in planning and allocating resources to meet business objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the department(s) that this form applies to.
2. Indicate whether you have staffing priorities in place or not.
3. Identify the next step(s) for your department.
4. Specify the document(s) related to your department's needs.
5. Enumerate the needs of your department.
6. Select whether your department needs resources.
7. Choose the next step(s) your department requires.
8. Select whether resources are available for your department's needs.
9. Prioritize the needs of your department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Departments** (`departments`): Enter the department(s) this form applies to.
* **Staffing Priorities** (`staffing_priorities`): Check 'Yes' if you have staffing priorities in place, 'No' otherwise.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`): Describe the next step(s) for your department.
* **Document** (`document`): Specify the document(s) related to your department's needs.
* **Needs** (`needs`): Enumerate the needs of your department.
* **Department** (`department`): Check 'Yes' if your department needs resources, 'No' otherwise.
* **Next Step** (`next_step`): Choose the next step(s) your department requires.
* **Needs Department** (`needs_department`): Select 'Yes' for the needs of your department.
* **Needs Priorities** (`needs_priorities`): Select 'Yes' for the priorities of your department's needs.
* **Next Steps Department** (`next_steps_department`): Select 'Yes' for the next step(s) your department requires.
* **Priorities** (`priorities`): Prioritize the needs of your department.
