# quarterly_schedule_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The quarterly schedule submission form is used to provide quarterly schedule information for employees and departments, allowing management to review and track progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary authorization to access this form.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information in the required fields.
3. Select the relevant quarters for which you are submitting schedule information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Data** (`Employee Data`, `text`, required): This field is used to enter your employee data, including any relevant details or notes about your employee's schedule.
* **Quarter Plans** (`Quarter Plans`, `number`, not required): This field is used to select the 4 quarters for which you are submitting schedule information. Please select 4 quarters to ensure complete coverage of your employee's schedule.
* **Weekly Schedules** (`Weekly Schedules`, `date`, not required): This field is used to enter the specific dates of your weekly schedules. Please format the date in the standard `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
* **Risk Notes** (`Risk Notes`, `text`, not required): This field is used to enter any risk-related notes or comments about your employee's schedule.
* **Department Planning** (`Department Planning`, `text`, not required): This field is used to enter any relevant planning information about your department's schedule.

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough when entering information in the form.
- If unsure about any field, please reach out to the designated authority for clarification.
- Regularly review and update your submitted schedule information to ensure it reflects any changes. 

Note: The form is designed for management use, so please ensure all information is properly formatted and submitted.
