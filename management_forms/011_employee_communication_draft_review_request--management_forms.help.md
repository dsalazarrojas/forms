# Employee Communication Draft Review Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Communication Draft Review Request form is used to submit a draft of an internal or external communication, such as a memo, newsletter, or presentation, for review by a manager.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief description of the employee communication draft in the "Employee Communication Draft" field.
2. Provide any additional context that may be relevant to the draft in the "draft_context" field.
3. Set the deadline for the review in the "Deadline" field (if applicable).
4. Enter any comments or feedback from the manager in the "Manager Comments" field (if applicable).
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (if applicable).
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (if applicable).
7. Click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Communication Draft**: 
  A brief description of the employee communication draft, such as a memo, newsletter, or presentation.
* **draft_context**: 
  Any additional context that may be relevant to the draft, such as the purpose of the communication or the intended audience.
* **Deadline**: 
  The date and time by which the manager is expected to review and provide feedback on the draft.
* **Manager Comments**: 
  Any comments or feedback from the manager, such as suggestions for improvement or questions.
* **Email**: 
  Your email address, which will be used for communication with you.
* **Phone**: 
  Your phone number, which will be used for communication with you.
* **Submit**: 
  Click this button to send the form and request a review of the draft.
