# digital-process-governance-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information for digital process governance applications. It aims to collect relevant details about the objectives, documentation, consent, and review processes involved in implementing a digital process, ensuring transparency and accountability throughout the lifecycle of the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the designated fields.
2. Provide accurate and clear information about the digital process.
3. Fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction and Objectives** (`1`, `title`, required): This field is intended to introduce the project's objectives and goals. Provide a brief description of the project's purpose and its expected outcomes.
* **Documentation** (`2`, `documentation`, required): Here, you are asked to provide details about the documentation required for the project, such as reports, manuals, or other supporting materials.
* **Consent** (`3`, `consent`, required): In this field, you are expected to describe the consent process, including how it will be obtained, from whom, and what it will be used for.
* **Additional Objectives** (`4`, `additional_objectives`, required): This field is for additional objectives that are not already mentioned in the initial introduction. Be sure to explain how these objectives contribute to the overall project goals.
* **More Objectives** (`5`, `more_objectives`, required): Similar to the previous field, provide further information about the project's objectives and their importance.
* **Documentation Details** (`6`, `documentation_details`, required): Here, you can provide more specifics about the documentation needed for the project, such as the format, frequency, or distribution methods.
* **Consent Details** (`7`, `consent_details`, required): Describe the details of the consent process, including the methods used to obtain consent and how it will be stored or handled.
* **Review and Approval** (`8`, `review_and_approval`, required): In this field, you are asked to outline the review and approval process, including who will be involved and the criteria for approval.
* **Additional Information** (`9`, `additional_information`, required): This field is for any extra details or context that may be necessary for the project's success.
* **Objective Details** (`10`, `objective_details`, required): Provide a detailed description of the project's objective, including any relevant background or context.
* **Consent Type** (`11`, `consent_type`, required): Specify the type of consent required for the project, such as explicit, implicit, or informed consent.
* **Review Details** (`12`, `review_details`, required): Outline the details of the review process, including the criteria for review and the expected outcomes.
