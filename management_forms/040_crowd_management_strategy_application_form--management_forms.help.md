# Crowd Management Strategy Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crowd Management Strategy Application Form is a tool for event organizers and venue owners to collect information about their crowd management strategies. This form will help you provide essential details about your event, crowd, and submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Event Details section with your event's name, date, and time.
2. Provide information about the crowd attending your event, including their expected number and demographics.
3. Enter the name of your venue.
4. Add your contact name and email address so we can get in touch with you.
5. Choose the type of submission you are making (Event Details or Crowd Details).
6. Enter the name and title of the reviewer who will be reviewing your submission.
7. Enter the date when the reviewer will be reviewing your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter the name and date of your event, including the date and time.
* **Crowd Details** (`crowd_details`, `text`, required): Describe the type of crowd expected to attend your event, including their number and demographics.
* **Venue Name** (`venue_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your venue.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the contact person for your event.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Submission** (`submission`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose between "Event Details" and "Crowd Details" to indicate what you are submitting.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person reviewing your submission.
* **Reviewer Title** (`reviewer_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the person reviewing your submission.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you want the reviewer to review your submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your submission before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, feel free to ask our support team for help.
