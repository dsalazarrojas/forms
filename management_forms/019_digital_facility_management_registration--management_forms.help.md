# Digital Facility Management Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about digital facilities, including site details, owner, location, services, contacts, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, follow the instructions below for each field:
- **Site Information**: Enter a brief description of the site.
- **Site Owner**: Enter the name of the site's owner.
- **Location**: Enter the site's location.
- **Services**: Select "Yes" or "No" for the services offered.
- **Services Details**: Enter additional information about the services offered.
- **Contacts**: Enter the site's contact information.
- **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the site.
- **Facilities**: Select "Yes" or "No" for the facilities offered.
- **Email**: Enter the site's email address.
- **Phone**: Enter the site's phone number.
- **Website**: Enter the site's website URL.
- **Facebook**: Enter the site's Facebook URL.
- **Twitter**: Enter the site's Twitter URL.
- **Instagram**: Enter the site's Instagram URL.
- **YouTube**: Enter the site's YouTube URL.
- **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about the site.
- **Form Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
- **Form Submitted Date**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
- **Form Updated Date**: Enter the date the form was last updated.
- **Form Submitter**: Enter the name of the person who last updated the form.
- **Comments**: Enter any comments about the form.
- **Attachments**: Enter any attachments related to the form.
- **Form Status**: Select "Active" or "Inactive" for the form status.
