# Responsibility And Authority Matrix Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Responsibility And Authority Matrix Form is designed to document and clarify the responsibilities and authorities within a project's scope, particularly when handoffs occur. It helps teams to understand their duties and deadlines and facilitates effective handoffs between parties.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the project to which this form relates.
2. Enter the name of the team working on the project.
3. Identify the party responsible for each task or activity.
4. Choose the authority level of each party.
5. Indicate if the party is the owner of the task.
6. Mark whether the party reports directly to another party or to multiple parties.
7. Select the roles involved in the handoff (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
8. Enter the date when the handoff was completed or is expected to be completed.
9. Select the status of the handoff (e.g., "Active" or "Inactive").
10. (Optional) Enter the date when the handoff was started or is ongoing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **project_id (1)**: Enter the project number or ID.
* **team_name (2)**: Enter the name of the team working on the project.
* **responsible_party (3)**: Identify the party responsible for each task or activity. This is the person or team responsible for performing the task.
* **authority_level (4)**: Choose the authority level of each party:
	+ High: The party has full authority and decision-making power.
	+ Low: The party has limited authority or is responsible for a minor task.
* **owner (5)**: Indicate if the party is the owner of the task:
	+ Yes: The party is the owner and responsible for the task.
	+ No: The party is not the owner, but still responsible for the task.
* **reporting_line (6)**: Mark whether the party reports directly to another party or to multiple parties:
	+ Yes: The party reports directly to one or multiple parties.
	+ No: The party does not report directly to anyone.
* **role (7)**: Select the roles involved in the handoff:
	+ Yes: The party is involved in the handoff.
	+ No: The party is not involved in the handoff.
* **handoff_date (8)**: Enter the date when the handoff was completed or is expected to be completed.
* **handoff_status (9)**: Select the status of the handoff:
	+ Active: The handoff is currently active and ongoing.
	+ Inactive: The handoff is completed or inactive.
* **handoff_date_completed (10)**: Enter the date when the handoff was completed.
* **handoff_date_ongoing (11)**: Enter the date when the handoff was started or is ongoing (optional).
