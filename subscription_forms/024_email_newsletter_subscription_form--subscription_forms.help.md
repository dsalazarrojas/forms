# email_newsletter_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The email newsletter subscription form is used to gather contact information and preferences from users who are interested in receiving email newsletters from our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address you'd like to use for the newsletter.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, required): Re-enter the email address you'd like to use for the confirmation.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, required): Re-enter your phone number for verification.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): If you have any message for us, enter it here.
* **Subscribe** (`subscribe`, select_one, required): Select "True" to confirm your subscription to the email newsletter.
