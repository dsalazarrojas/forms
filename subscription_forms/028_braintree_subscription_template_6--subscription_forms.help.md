# Braintree Subscription Template 6 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to subscribe to a Braintree plan, selecting a subscription plan, billing frequency, and providing necessary billing information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling out the Customer Information section**, which includes:
	* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for account and billing purposes.
2. **Choose your Subscription Plan**:
	* Select one of the available plans: Basic, Standard, Premium, Professional, or Enterprise.
3. **Select your Billing Frequency**:
	* Choose how often you are billed: Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annual, Annual, or Custom.
4. **Complete the Billing Information section**:
	* **Billing Address**: Enter your street address for billing purposes.
	* **City**: Enter your city name.
	* **State and ZIP Code**: Enter your state and postal code.
5. **Review the Subscription Terms**:
	* Confirm that you agree to the subscription terms.
6. **Confirm your subscription**:
	* Check the box to indicate that you agree to the subscription terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email Address**: For account and billing purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Optional contact information.
* **Select Subscription Plan**: Choose one of the available plans.
* **Plan Description**: Description of what is included in the plan.
* **Billing Frequency**: How often you are billed.
* **Monthly Cost**: Price per month.
* **Features Included**: Select plan features.
* **Number of Users**: Optional number of users allowed on the plan.
* **Billing Address**: Street address for billing purposes.
* **City**: City name.
* **State and ZIP Code**: State and postal code.
* **Auto Renewal**: Automatically renew subscription.
* **Cancel Anytime**: You can cancel your subscription.
* **I agree to subscription terms**: Confirm that you agree to the subscription terms.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
