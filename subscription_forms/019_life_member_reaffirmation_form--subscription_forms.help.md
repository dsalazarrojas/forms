# Life Member Reaffirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The life member reaffirmation form is used to update and verify the contact and job title information of life members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your current information and the new information if applicable.
2. Provide the current job title and company you are with.
3. If you are leaving the company, enter the date and time you are leaving.
4. If you are returning to the company, enter the reason for returning.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes about your status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your records.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your records.
* **Email**: Enter your current email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your current phone number.
* **Company**: Enter the name of your current company.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
* **Years with Company**: Enter the number of years you have been with the company.
* **Reason for Change**: If you are leaving the company, enter the reason why.
* **New Job Title**: Enter your new job title if you are switching roles.
* **New Company**: Enter the name of your new company if you are switching companies.
* **New Email**: Enter your new email address if it has changed.
* **New Phone**: Enter your new phone number if it has changed.
* **Date of Change**: Enter the date you will be leaving or the date you returned.
* **Time of Change**: Enter the time you will be leaving or the time you returned.
* **Reason for Leaving**: If you are leaving the company, enter the reason why.
* **Reason for Returning**: If you are returning to the company, enter the reason why.
* **Job Title**: Enter your new job title if it has changed.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your status.

## Tips
* Be sure to update your information accurately to ensure that the organization has the most up-to-date contact information.
* If you are leaving the company, please provide a clear reason for leaving to help the organization understand your decision.
* If you are returning to the company, please provide a clear reason for returning to help the organization understand your decision.
