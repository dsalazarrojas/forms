# Coworking Space Subscription Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users who wish to subscribe to a coworking space. The form will help us understand your workspace preferences, user details, subscription details, payment information, and subscription frequency and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select a workspace from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your user details in the text field.
3. Provide your subscription details in the text field.
4. Enter your payment information in the text field.
5. Select the start date of your subscription.
6. Select the end date of your subscription.
7. Choose the frequency of your subscription from the dropdown menu.
8. Select the payment terms from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Select a Workspace
* **Workspace Selection (Select a Workspace)**: This field allows you to choose the workspace you wish to subscribe to. Please select the workspace that best suits your needs.

### 2. User Details
* **User Details**: This field is where you can enter your information, such as name, email, and other contact details. Please fill in your details accurately to ensure we can contact you properly.

### 3. Subscription Details
* **Subscription Details**: This field is where you can provide additional information about your subscription, such as any specific needs or requirements you have. Please be as detailed as possible to help us understand your needs.

### 4. Payment Info
* **Payment Info**: This field is where you can enter your payment information, such as credit card details or bank account information. Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.

### 5. Start Date
* **Start Date**: This field allows you to select the start date of your subscription. Please choose a date that works best for you and your team.

### 6. End Date
* **End Date**: This field allows you to select the end date of your subscription. Please choose a date that works best for you and your team.

### 7. Frequency
* **Frequency**: This field allows you to choose how often you would like your subscription to recur. Please select the frequency that best suits your needs.

### 8. Payment Terms
* **Payment Terms**: This field allows you to choose how you would like to pay for your subscription. Please select the payment terms that best fit your needs.
