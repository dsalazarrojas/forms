# snail_mailing_list_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The snail_mailing_list_request form is used to request subscription to a snail mailing list. It allows users to provide their name, email address, phone number, and other relevant information to receive regular updates and information from the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field. Make sure to use a valid email address that you check regularly.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field, if you would like to be contacted by phone.
4. Select your preferred subscription options from the "interested_in" field. You can choose to be subscribed to one or more of the following:
	* Snail
	* Postcard
	* Newsletter
	* Email Newsletter
	* Snail and Postcard
	* Snail and Newsletter
	* Snail and Newsletter and Postcard
	* Snail Email and Postcard
	* Snail and Email
	* Snail and Newsletter and Email
	* Snail and Email and Postcard
	* Snail Newsletter
5. Choose how often you would like to receive updates in the "how_often" field. You can select from the following options:
	* Once a year
	* Twice a year
	* Three to four times a year
	* Five to six times a year
	* Daily
	* Weekly
	* Monthly
	* Quarterly
	* Annually
6. Enter any additional notes or information in the "notes" field, if you have any.
7. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name**: Your name.
* **email**: Your email address. Please make sure to use a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **phone**: Your phone number, if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **interested_in**: Choose one or more of the options to be subscribed to.
* **how_often**: Choose how often you would like to receive updates.
* **notes**: Any additional notes or information you would like to provide.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy before submitting.
* Use the correct formatting for your phone number.
* Select only what you are interested in to avoid unnecessary mailings.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact us at [contact email/phone number].
