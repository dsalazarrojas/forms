# Mini Subscribe Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect subscription information from users for a platform or service. To ensure accuracy, please fill out the form carefully and follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To subscribe, simply fill out the form with your name and email address.
2. Confirm your email address by typing it again in the confirmation email field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **subscribe_name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on our platform.
* **subscribe_email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address where we will send updates and notifications.
* **confirm_email** (`confirm_email`, text, required: false): Re-enter your email address to confirm that it is correct.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your email address for errors before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues with submitting the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
