# Newspaper Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to provide their subscription information, including their name, contact details, and subscription preferences, to be used by the newspaper's administration or management team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
5. Enter your street address in the "Street address" field.
6. Enter the city where you are from in the "City" field.
7. Select your state from the list provided in the "State" field.
8. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
9. Select your preferred subscription frequency (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually) in the "Subscription frequency" field.
10. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can enter them in the "Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address.
- **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Street address** (`street_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your street address.
- **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city where you are from.
- **State** (`state`, select_one, required/optional): Select your state from the list provided.
- **Zip** (`zip`, text, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
- **Subscription frequency** (`subscription_frequency`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your preferred subscription frequency (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually).
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments (optional). 

## Tips
- Please ensure that you fill in all required fields to avoid incomplete submissions.
- If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please contact the newspaper's administration team for assistance.
