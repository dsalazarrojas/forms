# Customer Experience Platform Subscription Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Experience Platform Subscription Inquiry form is designed to collect information from customers about their experience on the Customer Experience Platform. This form gathers demographic information and subscription details to better understand customer needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Company (if applicable).
4. Enter your Email address.
5. Enter your Phone number (if applicable).
6. Select the subscription Plan you are interested in (Plan A, B, or C).
7. Choose a date for the rollout (if applicable).
8. Select a time for the rollout (if applicable).
9. Choose the frequency of follow-up communication (7, 14, or 30 days).
10. Add any additional comments or questions (if applicable).
11. Enter the name and email of the Sales Representative handling your inquiry (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`customer_experience_platform_subscription_inquiry_1`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`customer_experience_platform_subscription_inquiry_2`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Company
* **Company** (`customer_experience_platform_subscription_inquiry_3`, text, optional): If you are contacting us on behalf of a company, enter the company name here.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

### Plan
* **Plan** (`customer_experience_platform_subscription_inquiry_6`, select_one, required): Select the subscription plan you are interested in (Plan A, B, or C).

### Rollout Date
* **Rollout Date** (`customer_experience_platform_subscription_inquiry_7`, date, optional): Choose a date for the rollout (if applicable).

### Rollout Time
* **Rollout Time** (`customer_experience_platform_subscription_inquiry_8`, time, optional): Select a time for the rollout (if applicable).

### Follow Up
* **Follow Up** (`customer_experience_platform_subscription_inquiry_9`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the frequency of follow-up communication (7, 14, or 30 days).

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`customer_experience_platform_subscription_inquiry_10`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have (if applicable).

### Sales Rep
* **Sales Rep** (`customer_experience_platform_subscription_inquiry_11`, text, optional): Enter the name of the sales representative handling your inquiry (if applicable).

### Sales Rep Email
* **Sales Rep Email** (`customer_experience_platform_subscription_inquiry_12`, email, optional): Enter the email of the sales representative handling your inquiry (if applicable).

Note: Make sure to fill in all required fields to complete and submit the form successfully.
