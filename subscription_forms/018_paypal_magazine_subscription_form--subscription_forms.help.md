# paypal_magazine_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for subscribing to a magazine. Please fill out the form to provide your personal and subscription details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the desired subscription duration in the "Subscription Duration" field. You can choose one or more options.
5. Select your preferred subscription frequency in the "Subscription Frequency" field.
6. Choose a payment method in the "Payment Method" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Full Name (text)
*   **Full Name**: A field to enter your full name.

### email (email)
*   **email**: A field to enter your email address.

### Phone Number (text)
*   **Phone Number**: A field to enter your phone number.

### Subscription Duration (select_multiple)
*   **Subscription Duration**: Select one or more options for the subscription duration. You can choose from the available options.

### Subscription Frequency (select_one)
*   **Subscription Frequency**: Select a single option for your preferred subscription frequency.

### Payment Method (select_one)
*   **Payment Method**: Choose a payment method for your subscription.
