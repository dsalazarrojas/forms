# Travel Agency Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Agency Subscription Form is designed for travel agency clients to provide their contact information and travel preferences. This will help us understand who you are and how we can assist you in planning your trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address.
2. Enter your Phone number for us to contact you.
3. Provide your Address for us to send any travel-related documents.
4. Enter your desired Travel Date.
5. Choose your preferred Travel Time.
6. Create a Password for your account (optional).
7. Confirm your Password to ensure accuracy.
8. Please double-check all fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide your address so we can send any travel-related documents to you.
* **Password** (`password`, text, optional): Create a password for your account (if you wish to have an account with us).
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, text, optional): Confirm the password you created to ensure accuracy.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your trip.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, time, optional): Choose the time of your trip.
 
## Tips
- Please make sure to double-check all fields before submitting the form to avoid errors.
- If you need help or have any questions, feel free to contact us.
- This form is for subscription purposes only and will not be used for any other purpose.
