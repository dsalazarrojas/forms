# Cultural Program Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in subscribing to a cultural program. By filling out this form, you will be able to select your preferred subscription options and provide any additional notes or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your preferred **Phone** type (e.g., mobile, landline, or no preference).
3. Choose one of the provided **Select One** options (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
4. Select multiple options from the **Select Multiple** field (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3).
5. Enter the **Date** of your subscription (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
6. Enter the **Time** of your subscription (in 24-hour format).
7. Add any additional **Note** about your subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name as it appears on your official ID or personal documents.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name as it appears on your official ID or personal documents.
* **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (text): Select your preferred phone type (e.g., mobile, landline, or no preference).
* **Select One** (select_one): Choose one of the provided options for your subscription type.
* **Select Multiple** (select_multiple): Select multiple options for your subscription type (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3).
* **Date** (date): Enter the date of your subscription in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Time** (time): Enter the time of your subscription in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Note** (note): Add any additional notes or comments about your subscription.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate smooth communication and subscription processing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us using the email address provided on the form.
* You can always unsubscribe from the cultural program by contacting us with your subscription details.
