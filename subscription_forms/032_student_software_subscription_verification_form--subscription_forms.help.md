# Student Software Subscription Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to verify student information and subscription status for students who have registered for software access.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Ensure that the student's subscription status is verified correctly.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Verification Form** (1, text, required/optional):
	+ This is a general notes field where you can enter any additional comments or information about the student's subscription.
* **Student Information** (2, text, required/optional):
	+ Enter the student's name and student ID (or other relevant details) to identify them accurately.
* **Student Email** (3, email, required/optional):
	+ Enter the student's email address to contact them or send verification notifications.
* **Verification Status** (4, select_one, required/optional):
	+ Select the verification status of the student's subscription from the options: Pending, Approved, or Denied.
* **Verification Date** (5, date, required/optional):
	+ Enter the date of the verification action taken or expected completion date.

## Tips

* Double-check the student's subscription status and verification details before submitting the form.
* Make sure to keep the student's information up-to-date and accurate for future reference.
