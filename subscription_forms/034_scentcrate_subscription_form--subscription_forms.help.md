The Scentscrate Subscription Form is used by customers to subscribe or opt out of our subscription services.

To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Decide if you want to be subscribed to our subscription services ("subscription_type").
2. Fill in your name ("name").
3. Enter a valid email address ("email").
4. Enter a phone number, if available ("phone").
5. If you have any specific questions or requests, write them down in the "questions" field ("questions").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **subscription_type** (required/optional): Check this box if you wish to be subscribed to our subscription services.
* **name** (text, required/optional): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our communication with you.
* **email** (email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and communication from us.
* **phone** (text, required/optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can contact you if needed.
* **questions** (text, required/optional): If you have any questions or requests, write them down in this field for us to address.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address is accurate and active to receive timely updates and communication.
* If you have any issues with your subscription, you can contact us using the provided phone number.
* For any questions or concerns, please use the "questions" field to reach out to us directly. 

You can use this form to manage your subscription to our services. We will use the information you provide to keep you updated on our latest news, promotions, and important announcements. 

Note: This form is for customer subscription management only. It is not intended for other uses, such as customer support or feedback. If you have any other questions or concerns, please use our other support channels.
