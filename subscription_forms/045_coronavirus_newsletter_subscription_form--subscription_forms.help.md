# coronavirus_newsletter_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to subscribe to a newsletter about the coronavirus pandemic. It collects your name, email, and preferences to provide relevant and targeted information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**.
2. Enter your valid **Email** address.
3. Select how you **Hear About Us** by choosing one of the options: Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, Online Search, or Referral.
4. Select **Multiple Channels** you'd like to receive information from (check all that apply): Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, Online Search, or Referral.
5. Leave a **Note** if you have anything you'd like to mention.
6. If applicable, select **Select One** option to confirm: Yes, No, or Other.
7. If applicable, select **Select Multiple Options** to choose multiple options: Yes, No, or Other.
8. You can also enter a date and a time for any specific purpose.
9. Leave a **Note 2** if you have any further comments.
10. Select **Select One Option 2** to confirm: Yes, No, or Other.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name for us to know who you are.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for us to contact you.
- **How did you hear about us?** (`select_one_source`, select_one, required): Choose how you found out about our newsletter.
  • Facebook
  • Twitter
  • Instagram
  • Online Search
  • Referral
- **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple_channels`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the channels you'd like to receive information from:
  • Facebook
  • Twitter
  • Instagram
  • Online Search
  • Referral
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Leave a note if you have any special requests or preferences.
- **Select One** (`select_one_option`, select_one, optional): Select one option to confirm: Yes, No, or Other.
  • Yes
  • No
  • Other
- **Select Multiple Option** (`select_multiple_option`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options to choose multiple options: Yes, No, or Other.
  • Yes
  • No
  • Other
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter a date for any specific purpose.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter a time for any specific purpose.
- **Note 2** (`note_2`, note, optional): Leave another note if you have any further comments.
- **Select One Option 2** (`select_one_option_2`, select_one, optional): Select one option to confirm: Yes, No, or Other.
  • Yes

## Tips
- Make sure to enter a valid email address.
- Be cautious when selecting multiple options, as some might imply different purposes.
- You can leave notes if you need to provide additional context.
- Selecting dates and times are not required but can be helpful for specific purposes.
