# Green Power Subscription Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Green Power Subscription Consent Form is designed to be completed by customers who wish to sign up for Green Power services. This form is used to collect the necessary information to provide personalized services to customers and to ensure that they have agreed to the terms and conditions of Green Power's services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your customer details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2.  Select the plan that best suits your needs from the list of available plans.
3.  Provide your household information, including your name and any other relevant details.
4.  Provide your address and contact information, including your email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect your customer details, including your name and any other relevant information. Please provide your name and any other relevant details that you would like to share with us.
* **Plan Selections** (`plan_selections`, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to select the plan that best suits your needs. You can choose from the following plans: Green Power Plan, Regular Power Plan, and Hybrid Power Plan.
* **Household Info** (`household_info`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect your household information, including your household name and any other relevant details.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required: false): This field is a one-time consent form where you need to indicate that you agree to the terms and conditions of Green Power's services.
* **Household Name** (`household_name`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect the name of your household, if applicable.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect the city where you live.
* **State** (`state`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect the state where you live.
* **ZIP** (`zip`, number, required: false): This field is used to collect your zip code.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This field is used to collect your email address.
