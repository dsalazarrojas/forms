# Document Scanning Subscription Cancellation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Document Scanning Subscription Cancellation Form is used to formally cancel a subscription to our Document Scanning service. This form is typically completed by customers and staff members involved in the cancellation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before filling it out to ensure you understand the purpose of each section.
2. Fill out all required fields marked as required.
3. If a field is optional, you can leave it blank.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Please enter your name and contact information in this field. This is an optional field but helps us to reach out to you in case of any further questions or concerns.
* **Subscription Status** (`subscription_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of your subscription: Cancel or Cancelled (for staff members), or Active (for customers).
* **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or multiple reasons for cancelling your subscription. Please be honest and clear about your reasons.
* **Document Type** (`document_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of document(s) you were subscribed to cancel. Please choose all that apply.
* **Date of Cancellation** (`date_of_cancellation`, date, optional): Enter the date you want to cancel your subscription. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, optional): Please sign your name to confirm cancellation. This is optional but recommended.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name. This is an optional field.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. This is an optional field.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number. This is an optional field.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your cancellation. This is an optional field.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, text, optional): Enter any further comments or questions. This is an optional field.
* **Staff Note** (`staff_note`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the cancellation. This is an optional field.
* **Staff Message** (`staff_message`, text, optional): Enter any further comments or questions from the staff. This is an optional field.
* **Cancellation Confirmation** (`cancellation_confirmation`, date, optional): Enter the date you confirm the cancellation.
* **Confirmation Message** (`confirmation_message`, text, optional): Enter any additional confirmation comments. This is an optional field.
* **Confirmation Signature** (`confirmation_signature`, text, optional): Please sign your name to confirm cancellation. This is optional but recommended.
* **Cancellation Date** (`cancellation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of cancellation.
* **Cancellation Reason** (`cancellation_reason`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or multiple reasons for cancelling your subscription. Please be honest and clear about your reasons.
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`, text, optional): Please sign your name to confirm cancellation. This is optional but recommended.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, optional): Enter your name. This is an optional field.
* **Staff Email** (`staff_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. This is an optional field.
* **Staff Phone** (`staff_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number. This is an optional field.

Please note that fields marked as "required" must be completed before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
