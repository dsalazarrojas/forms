# WordPress Website Maintenance Subscription Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect subscription information from users who want to subscribe to a WordPress website maintenance service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Website Details section with your website's information, including the name and URL of your website.
2. Provide your billing information in the Billing Info section, including your name, email address, and payment method.
3. Read and agree to the Payment Terms, which outline the subscription pricing and payment schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website Details**
	+ (`<name>`, `text`, required: false): Fill in your website's name and URL in this section.
* **Billing Info**
	+ (`<name>`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and billing information in this section, including your email address and payment method.
* **Payment Terms**
	+ (`<label>`, `text`, required: false): Read and accept the Payment Terms, which outline the subscription pricing and payment schedule.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Review the Payment Terms carefully before agreeing to them.
