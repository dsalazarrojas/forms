# Subscription Tracker Worksheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a tool to track and document recurring subscriptions. It will help you gather information on services that require regular renewals, such as software or service subscriptions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Select the appropriate options for "Recurring Services", "Renewal Timelines", "Subscription Spend", and "Subscription Frequency" fields.
3. Add any relevant notes to the "Notes" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if desired.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recurring Services** (`recurring_services`, text, required): Please describe the recurring service, such as software or service subscriptions.
* **Renewal Timelines** (`renewal_timelines`, select_one, required): Select the frequency at which the subscription renews. Options are: **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, **Yearly**.
* **Subscription Spend** (`subscription_spend`, number, required): Enter the total spend for the subscription.
* **Teams Involved** (`teams`, text, optional): List the teams involved in the subscription (if desired).
* **Subscription Frequency** (`subscription_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Check the frequencies at which this subscription occurs. Options are: **Yes**, **No**.
* **Renewal Status** (`renewal_status`, select_multiple, required): Check the current renewal status. Options are: **Active**, **Inactive**.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the subscription.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up or notifications (if desired).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up or notifications (if desired).

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Be thorough in completing the "Notes" field for any additional comments or information about the subscription.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to [team/department] for assistance.
