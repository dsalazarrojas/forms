# Exotic Fruit Subscription Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a subscription service that delivers exotic fruit to customers. It helps us understand your preferences for the type of fruit, delivery frequency, and payment method you would like to use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the title field with a brief description of your subscription preferences.
2. Select the type of exotic fruit category you would like to receive.
3. Enter your delivery address in the format of "Street Address, City, State, ZIP".
4. Choose a delivery date and time that suits your schedule.
5. Select your preferred payment method and frequency.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
7. Optionally, add any notes or special instructions for delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exotic Fruit Subscription Signup** (`title`, Text, Required): Enter a brief description of your subscription preferences.
* **category** (`category`, Select One, Required): Select the type of exotic fruit you would like to receive, such as Apple, Berries, or Oranges.
* **delivery address** (`delivery_address`, Text, Required): Enter your delivery address in the format of "Street Address, City, State, ZIP".
* **delivery date** (`delivery_date`, Date, Required): Select a date for your delivery.
* **payment method** (`payment_method`, Select One, Optional): Choose a payment method, such as Credit Card or PayPal.
* **payment amount** (`payment_amount`, Number, Required): Enter the amount you are willing to pay for your subscription.
* **payment frequency** (`payment_frequency`, Select One, Optional): Select how often you want to pay, such as one time or recurring.
* **delivery notes** (`delivery_notes`, Text, Optional): Add any special instructions or notes for your delivery.
* **delivery time** (`delivery_time`, Time, Optional): Select a time for your delivery.
* **delivery frequency** (`delivery_frequency`, Select One, Optional): Select how often you want to receive your delivery, such as weekly or monthly.
* **email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **note** (`note`, Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **form id** (`form_id`, Text, Optional): This field is not used and can be ignored.
