# Subscription Service Growth Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the growth and performance of a subscription service. It helps in tracking and analyzing key metrics such as user engagement, revenue, and subscription status. The purpose of this form is to provide a comprehensive view of the subscription service's growth and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **User Email**: Enter the email address of the user who is subscribed to the service.
2. **Subscription Plan**: Select the subscription plan that the user is currently subscribed to.
3. **Start Date**: Enter the date when the user started their subscription.
4. **End Date**: Enter the date when the user ended their subscription (if applicable).
5. **Start Time**: Enter the time when the user started their subscription.
6. **End Time**: Enter the time when the user ended their subscription (if applicable).
7. **User Name**: Enter the name of the user.
8. **User Organization**: Enter the organization that the user belongs to.
9. **Subscription Status**: Select the current status of the user's subscription (Active or Inactive).
10. **Subscription Service**: Enter the name of the subscription service.
11. **Engagement Rate**: Enter the engagement rate of the user (e.g., the number of times they interacted with the service).
12. **Subscription Service Provider**: Select whether the user is subscribed to the service provided by the subscription service.
13. **Total Users**: Enter the total number of users subscribed to the service.
14. **Total Revenue**: Enter the total revenue generated by the service.
15. **Average Revenue per User**: Enter the average revenue per user.
16. **Growth Rate**: Enter the growth rate of the service.
17. **Churn Rate**: Enter the churn rate of the service.
18. **Revenue Growth Rate**: Enter the revenue growth rate of the service.
19. **Retention Rate**: Enter the retention rate of the service.
20. **Average Revenue per User Growth**: Enter the average revenue per user growth.
21. **Revenue per User**: Enter the revenue per user.
22. **Revenue per User Growth**: Enter the revenue per user growth.
23. **Average Revenue per User per Period**: Enter the average revenue per user per period.
24. **Total Revenue Growth**: Enter the total revenue growth.
25. **Subscription Plan Change**: Select whether the user's subscription plan changed during the period.
