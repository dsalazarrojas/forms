# Magazine Subscription With Paysafe Payment Gateway - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for magazine subscriptions with a Paysafe payment gateway. It ensures that customers can easily select their payment option and provide relevant details for their subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete this form to provide your magazine subscription information and select your payment option.
- Select the correct payment gateway (Paysafe) to proceed.
- Enter your magazine title.
- Choose your start and end dates for the subscription.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Paysafe Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" if you want to use the Paysafe payment gateway for your subscription.
  - This field determines whether you want to use the Paysafe payment gateway for your magazine subscription.
  - If you select "Yes", you will be able to proceed with your subscription using the Paysafe payment gateway.
  - If you select "No", you will not be able to use the Paysafe payment gateway for your subscription.
- **Magazine Title** (`magazine_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the title of the magazine you are subscribing to.
  - This field is where you provide the title of the magazine you want to subscribe to.
  - Please ensure that the title is correctly spelled and formatted.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Choose the start date of your subscription.
  - This field is where you select the date when you want your subscription to start.
  - Use the date picker or calendar to ensure accurate date entry.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Choose the end date of your subscription.
  - This field is where you select the date when you want your subscription to end.
  - Use the date picker or calendar to ensure accurate date entry.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
- If you have any issues with your subscription, contact our support team.
