# Budget Friendly Clothing Subscription Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you sign up for our clothing subscription service, where you can receive regular shipments of clothes that fit your style and budget.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Subscriber name**, which is the name that will be associated with your subscription account.
2. Enter your **Contact email**, which will be used for subscription communications and updates.
3. Select your **Clothing size**, which is the size that you prefer to receive in your subscription boxes.
4. If you have any specific **Style preferences**, you can enter them here. This information will help us tailor the clothes we send you to your taste.
5. Choose your **Delivery frequency**, which is how often you want to receive your subscription boxes (Monthly, Quarterly, or Seasonally).
6. Set your **Budget per box**, which is the maximum amount you are willing to spend on each delivery.
7. Enter your **Shipping address**, where we will send your subscription boxes.
8. Select your preferred **Payment method** (Credit card, PayPal, or Bank transfer).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subscriber name (Subscriber name)**: This is your name that will be associated with your subscription account. Please enter your full name.
* **Contact email (Contact email)**: This is the email we will use to communicate with you about your subscription, including updates on new shipments and subscription details.
* **Clothing size (Clothing size)**: Select your preferred clothing size from the list below. This will help us send you clothes that fit you well.
	+ XS
	+ S
	+ M
	+ L
	+ XL
* **Style preferences (Style preferences)**: If you have any specific clothing style or color preferences, enter them here. We will use this information to tailor our shipments to your taste.
* **Delivery frequency (Delivery frequency)**: Choose how often you want to receive your subscription boxes. Options include:
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Seasonally
* **Budget per box (Budget per box)**: Set the maximum amount you are willing to spend on each delivery. This will help us adjust the clothes we send you to fit your budget.
* **Shipping address (Shipping address)**: Enter the address where you want us to send your subscription boxes.
* **Payment method (Payment method)**: Select your preferred payment method for your subscription:
	+ Credit card
	+ PayPal
	+ Bank transfer
