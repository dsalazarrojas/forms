Custom Accessories Subscription Form - Help Guide
======================================

### Purpose
This form is for users to subscribe to custom accessories services.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your Email address to receive updates and notifications.
3. Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
4. Select your preferred Subscription Service (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
5. Choose your Subscription Type (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
6. Select your preferred Payment Method (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
7. Enter your Delivery Date and Delivery Time for the subscription.
8. Add any additional Notes about your subscription.
9. If necessary, enter any Custom Fields as per your requirement.
10. Enter your First Name (alternative) and Last Name (alternative) if applicable.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Subscription Service** (`subscription_service`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you want to subscribe to our custom accessories services.
* **Subscription Type** (`subscription_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple subscription types as per your need.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your subscription delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Enter your preferred delivery time.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you have a preferred payment method.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your subscription.
* **Custom Fields** (`custom_fields`, text, optional): Enter any additional information as per your requirement.
* **First Name Alt** (`first_name_alt`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID (alternative).
* **Last Name Alt** (`last_name_alt`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID (alternative).

### Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information to receive timely updates and notifications.
- If you have any issues with your subscription, please contact us through your provided Phone number or Email address.
