# video_streaming_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The video_streaming_subscription_form is a user-facing form designed to collect necessary information for creating new video streaming subscriptions. This form helps customers create a subscription by providing relevant details, such as subscription plans, payment methods, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a subscription plan that suits your needs.
2. Choose the term of your subscription (one or multiple options).
3. Provide your payment details (not required but recommended for successful subscription).
4. Select your preferred payment method (not required but recommended for successful subscription).
5. Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
6. Optionally, enter your phone number for further contact and communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subscription Plan**: Select the plan that best fits your needs from the available options. This will determine the features and content you have access to.
* **Subscription Term**: Choose whether you want a one-time subscription or select multiple terms for a longer subscription period.
* **Payment Details**: Enter your payment information if you're ready to proceed with your subscription. This is not required, but it's necessary for a successful subscription.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you prefer (e.g., credit card, PayPal).
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive notifications, updates, and important information about your subscription.
* **Phone**: Optionally, enter your phone number for further contact and communication. This is not required but recommended for a smoother subscription experience.
