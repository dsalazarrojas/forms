# kids_activity_box_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect subscription information from customers who want to sign up for a kids activity box service. Fill out this form to start or modify your subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Enter a valid email address.
4. Select your preferred payment method (credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer).
5. Choose how often you want to be charged (weekly, bi-monthly, or monthly).
6. Enter the start date of your subscription.
7. Enter the end date of your subscription (optional).
8. Enter your address line 1 (optional).
9. Enter your address line 2 (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Email Hint** (`email_hint`, text, optional): Enter a valid email address (optional).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you want to pay for your subscription. Options include credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how often you want to be charged. Options include weekly, bi-monthly, or monthly.
* **Subscription Start** (`subscription_start`, date, required): Enter the date you want to start your subscription.
* **Subscription End** (`subscription_end`, date, optional): Enter the date you want to end your subscription (if you want to cancel or modify your subscription).
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, optional): Enter your address line 1 (optional).
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, text, optional): Enter your address line 2 (optional).
