# Club Newsletter Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Club Newsletter Subscription Form is designed to help you subscribe to your favorite club's newsletter, which will keep you up-to-date with the latest events, news, and updates from your club. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your details as required.
2. Select your club of interest.
3. Choose your preferred communication method and frequency.
4. Confirm your subscription preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (subscriber_name, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (subscriber_last_name, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents. (Note: This is an optional field and not required for subscription)
* **Email Address** (subscriber_email, email, required): Enter the email address where you want to receive the newsletter.
* **Subscription Date** (subscription_date, date, required): Select the start date of your subscription. (Note: This is used to track when you started receiving our newsletter)
* **Which Club Newsletter** (club_name, select_one, required): Choose the club you are interested in.
* **Content Interests** (interests, select_multiple, required): Select the content topics that interest you the most.
* **Newsletter Frequency Preference** (frequency, select_one, required): Choose how often you'd like to receive updates from us.
* **Are You a Club Member** (member_status, select_one, required): Confirm your membership status with us.
* **Member ID** (member_id, text, optional): If you are a member, please enter your member ID. (Note: This field is not required for subscription)
* **Communication Preference** (communication_preference, select_one, required): Choose how you prefer to stay informed about club news.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, optional): If you want to receive text or phone updates, enter your phone number. (Note: This is an optional field)
* **Notify About Special Announcements** (special_announcements, select_one, optional): Choose if you want to be notified about important club news. (Note: This field is optional)
* **Language Preference** (language_preference, select_one, optional): Select your preferred language for communication. (Note: This field is optional)
* **I Agree to Privacy Policy** (privacy_agreement, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to our privacy policy.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you enter your email address correctly.
- If you select "As Needed Only" for newsletter frequency, we'll send updates only when there's something important to share.
- If you select "Not Sure" for membership status, we'll contact you to clarify your membership details. 

This help guide aims to provide a clear and concise understanding of the form's purpose and each field's explanation, while highlighting potential points of confusion to be addressed.
