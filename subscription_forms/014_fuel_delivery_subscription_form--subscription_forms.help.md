# Fuel Delivery Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fuel Delivery Subscription Form is used to collect information for fuel delivery subscriptions. This form helps customers provide their personal details, delivery preferences, and subscription settings for fuel delivery services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details:
	- First Name
	- Last Name
	- Email
	- Phone
2. Select your delivery preferences:
	- Choose a delivery date
	- Choose a delivery time
3. Choose your subscription settings:
	- Select the frequency of your subscription (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly)
	- Choose the type of subscription you want (Regular, On Demand, or One Time)
4. Add any additional notes if needed

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for your delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Choose a time for your delivery.
* **Subscription Frequency** (`subscription_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you want to receive deliveries (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Subscription Type** (`subscription_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of subscription you want (Regular, On Demand, or One Time).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Choose a start date for your subscription.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Choose an end date for your subscription, if applicable.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or instructions for your delivery.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information in the form to ensure smooth delivery services.
* Double-check your delivery preferences to avoid any confusion or mistakes.
* Contact customer support if you have any questions or need help with your subscription.
