# Publication Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect subscription information from users for a publication. It includes selecting a subscription plan, payment method, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your desired subscription plan from the options provided.
2. Choose your preferred payment method.
3. Enter your email address.
4. If you wish to provide your phone number, enter it in the corresponding field.
5. If you have a preferred subscription date, enter it in the corresponding field.
6. If you are making a one-time payment, enter the payment amount. Note that this field is only required if you select a Free Trial plan.
7. Select your payment frequency (Monthly, Annually, or Bi-Annually).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subscription Information**: This field is where you enter the details of your subscription. Please enter your subscription information in this field.
* **Select Plan**: This field is where you choose your desired subscription plan. Select from the options provided: Monthly Plan, Annual Plan, or Free Trial.
* **Payment Method**: This field is where you choose your preferred payment method. Select from the options provided: Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Email Address**: This field is where you enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: This field is optional, and you can enter your phone number if you wish to provide it.
* **Subscription Date**: This field is optional, and you can enter your preferred subscription date.
* **Payment Amount**: This field is only required if you select a Free Trial plan. Enter the payment amount for your trial period.
* **Payment Frequency**: This field is where you select your payment frequency. Choose from the options provided: Monthly, Annually, or Bi-Annually.
