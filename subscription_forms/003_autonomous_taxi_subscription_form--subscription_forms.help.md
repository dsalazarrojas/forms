# Autonomous Taxi Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who wish to sign up for a subscription to our autonomous taxi services.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your personal details: 
    - **Full Name** (Text Field): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents
    - **Email Address** (Email Field): Enter the email address where you want to receive invoices and other important updates
    - **Phone Number** (Text Field): Enter your phone number for account verification
    - **Home Address** (Text Field): Enter your primary pickup location
*   Choose your subscription plan: 
    - **Select Your Plan** (Select One Field): Choose between the Basic, Premium, Executive, or Pay-As-You-Go plans
*   Select your preferred vehicle type: 
    - **Preferred Vehicle Model** (Select One Field): Choose between Sedan, Luxury SUV, or Compact EV
*   Set your commute preferences: 
    - **Preferred Morning Commute Time** (Time Field): Set your regular pickup window
*   Set your in-car temperature settings: 
    - **Default In-Car Temperature** (Number Field): Set your preferred in-car temperature in Celsius
*   Choose your payment method: 
    - **Preferred Payment Method** (Select One Field): Choose between Credit/Debit Card, PayPal Account, Direct Bank Transfer, or Digital Wallet
*   Agree to subscription terms: 
    - **Agree to Subscription Terms** (Select One Field): Confirm that you have read and agree to our terms of service
