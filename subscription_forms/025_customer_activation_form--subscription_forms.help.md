# customer_activation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer_activation_form is a basic form designed to collect essential information from customers for activation purposes. It consists of four fields: First Name, Last Name, Company, and Email. This form is likely used for new customer onboarding or subscription activation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the customer_activation_form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. If applicable, enter your company name in the Company field.
4. Enter your Email address in the Email field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your surname.
* **Company** (Text): If you represent a company, enter its name.
* **Email** (Email): Enter a valid email address.
