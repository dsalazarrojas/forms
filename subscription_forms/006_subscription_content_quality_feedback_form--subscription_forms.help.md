This guide is intended to help users understand the purpose and usage of the Subscription Content Quality Feedback Form. The form is designed to collect user feedback on the quality of content provided by the organization. To fill out the form, please ensure that the fields are relevant and that you're not providing duplicate feedback. If you've already submitted feedback, please do not resubmit. The form's purpose is to gather information about the user's experience and suggestions for improvement. This guide will walk you through each field to ensure a smooth and accurate completion of the form.

## Subscription Content Quality Feedback Form - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the options for the "What was the quality of the content provided" field to indicate your satisfaction with the content quality.
2. Choose all that apply for the "How satisfied were you with the content" field to express your level of satisfaction.
3. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the "Do you have any recommendations for improvement" field.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, type them in the "Do you have any additional feedback about the content" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "What is your email" field.
6. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "What is your phone number" field.
7. Select your date of birth from the calendar in the "What is your date of birth" field.
8. Choose your time of day from the time input field in the "What is your time of day" field.
9. Select all that apply for the "What type of content would you like to see more of" field to indicate your preferences.
10. Select all that apply for the "How often would you like to see content" field to express your content frequency preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **What was the quality of the content provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor)**
  This field is used to rate the quality of the content provided by the organization. Please select one of the options that best describes your experience.

- **How satisfied were you with the content (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied)**
  This field is used to measure your level of satisfaction with the content. Choose all that apply to express your feelings.

- **Do you have any recommendations for improvement** (text)
  This field is used to collect any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the content. Type as much or as little as you like.

- **Do you have any additional feedback about the content** (note)
  This field is used to collect any additional comments or thoughts you have about the content. Type as much or as little as you like.

- **What is your email (email)**
  This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter a valid email address.

- **What is your phone number (phone number)**
  This field is used to collect your phone number. If you don't have a phone number, leave this field blank.

- **What is your date of birth (date)**
  This field is used to collect your date of birth. Select a date from the calendar.

- **What is your time of day (time)**
  This field is used to collect your time of day. Choose a time from the time input field.

- **What type of content would you like to see more of (Article, Video, Podcast, Social Media Post, Email Newsletter)**
  This field is used to gather your preferences for content types. Select all that apply.

- **How often would you like to see content (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, Never)**
  This field is used to express your content frequency preferences. Select all that apply.
