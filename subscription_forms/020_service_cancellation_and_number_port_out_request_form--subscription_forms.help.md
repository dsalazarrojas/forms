# Service Cancellation And Number Port Out Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Service Cancellation And Number Port Out Request Form is a form designed for customers to request service cancellation and number portability. It collects user information, service details, and request reasons for cancellation and number porting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form to ensure you understand its purpose and agree to use it for service cancellation and number portability requests.
2.  Enter your first name.
3.  Enter your last name.
4.  Enter your customer contact number.
5.  Select the service number you wish to cancel.
6.  Select the type of account you have.
7.  Select the reason for cancellation.
8.  Enter the effective date of cancellation or port-out.
9.  Select the reason for port-out request.
10.  Select the reason for change of account type (if applicable).
11.  Select the reason for change of service provider (if applicable).
12.  Confirm that you have read and agreed to the customer agreement.
13.  Select the submission option for your request.
