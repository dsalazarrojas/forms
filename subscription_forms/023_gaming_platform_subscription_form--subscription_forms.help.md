# gaming_platform_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gaming_platform_subscription_form is used to collect subscription information from users, including payment details and billing information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, Subscription Plan, State, and Country.
2. Choose your preferred Payment Method and Subscription Plan from the options provided.
3. Enter your payment details, including the Payment Amount.
4. Provide your billing information, including City and Zip.
5. Confirm your Phone Number.
6. Review and accept the User Agreement.
7. If necessary, enter additional information for Payment Frequency and CVV.
8. Finally, submit the form to complete your subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, required): Choose your payment method (card or prophets).
* **Payment Details** (`Payment Details`, text, required): Enter your payment details, including the payment amount.
* **Subscription Plan** (`Subscription Plan`, select_multiple, required): Choose your subscription plan (monthly, yearly, or other).
* **City** (`City`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`State`, select_one, required): Choose your state (CA, NY, or other).
* **Zip** (`Zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (`Country`, select_one, required): Choose your country (US or other).
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Confirm Phone Number** (`Confirm Phone Number`, text, required): Confirm your phone number for accuracy.
* **User Agreement** (`User Agreement`, note, required): Review and accept the user agreement to continue.
* **Expiration Date** (`Expiration Date`, date, optional): Enter your expiration date, if applicable.
* **CVV** (`CVV`, text, optional): Enter your CVV for payment verification.
* **Payment Terms** (`Payment Terms`, text, optional): Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Frequency** (`Payment Frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your payment frequency (weekly, monthly, or other).
* **Card Number** (`Card Number`, text, optional): Enter your card number for payment verification.
* **Security Code** (`Security Code`, text, optional): Enter your security code for payment verification.
* **CVV2** (`CVV2`, text, optional): Enter your CVV2 for payment verification.
* **Payment Method2** (`Payment Method2`, select_one, optional): Choose your second payment method (card or prophets).
* **Expiration Date2** (`Expiration Date2`, date, optional): Enter your second expiration date, if applicable.
* **CVV3** (`CVV3`, text, optional): Enter your CVV3 for payment verification.

## Tips

* Ensure accurate and up-to-date billing information to avoid any payment issues.
* Review your subscription plan and payment terms carefully before confirming.
* If you encounter any issues during the submission process, contact our support team for assistance.
