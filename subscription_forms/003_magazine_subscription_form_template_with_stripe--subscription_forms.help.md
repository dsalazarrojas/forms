# Magazine Subscription Form Template With Stripe - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information and payment details for magazine subscription management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please fill out the fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subscription Form** (`magazine_subscription_form`, text, optional): This is the main title of the form. Enter the title of your magazine or publication.
* **Billing Information** (`billing_information`, text, optional): Enter the billing information of the user, including their name, address, and contact details.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): Enter the billing address of the user, including their street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Billing Email** (`billing_email`, email, optional): Enter the billing email address of the user.
* **Billing Phone** (`billing_phone`, text, optional): Enter the billing phone number of the user.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the payment method you want to use (Credit Card or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter the payment details, including the card number, expiration date, and CVV.
* **Payment Method Details** (`payment_method_details`, text, optional): Enter additional payment method details, if required.
* **Subscription Period** (`subscription_period`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the subscription period you want to offer (Monthly or Annually).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount for the selected subscription period.
* **Subscription Status** (`subscription_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the subscription status you want to apply (Active or Inactive).

## Tips
- Ensure that the form is filled out accurately and completely to avoid any issues with subscription processing.
- Verify the user's billing information and payment details before proceeding with the subscription.
- If you're unsure about any field or option, contact the support team for assistance.
