# Seasonal Product Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a product's subscription settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the product in the Product Name field.
2. Choose the subscription duration and frequency from the drop-down menus.
3. Select the subscription type (if applicable).
4. Enter the customer's email address in the Customer Email field.
5. Enter the customer's phone number in the Customer Phone field (if necessary).
6. Provide any additional payment information in the Payment Info field (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the product being subscribed.
* **Subscription Duration** (`subscription_duration`, number, **optional**): Choose the duration of the subscription (e.g., 1, 2, 3 months).
* **Subscription Frequency** (`subscription_frequency`, select_one, **optional**): Choose the frequency of the subscription (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
* **Subscription Type** (`subscription_type`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select one or more subscription types (if applicable).
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, **optional**): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, **optional**): Enter the customer's phone number (if necessary).
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, **optional**): Enter any additional payment information (if required).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate subscription settings.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact the support team for guidance.
* This form is for feedback and information collection purposes only; it's not a formal subscription application.
