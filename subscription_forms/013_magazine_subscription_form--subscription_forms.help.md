# Magazine Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The magazine subscription form is used for subscribers to sign up for a magazine subscription. This form collects basic contact information and subscription details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your name, phone number, and email address in the text fields marked "First Name", "Last Name" and "Email" respectively.
* Select your desired subscription plan from the dropdown menu marked "Plan".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field. This field is optional but recommended for our records.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field. This field is optional but recommended for our records.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in this field. We will use this to send you subscription updates and reminders. This field is optional but recommended for our records.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in this field. This field is optional but recommended for our records.
* **Plan**: Select your desired subscription plan from the dropdown menu:
	+ **Monthly**: A monthly subscription that renews automatically each month.
	+ **Yearly**: A yearly subscription that renews automatically each year.
	+ **Lifeline**: A special subscription plan with a one-time payment option.
* **Sign Up**: This field is not a form field, but rather a call to action to submit your subscription details and complete the sign up process.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly, as this is how we will contact you with subscription updates and reminders.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact our support team.
* If you are already a subscriber, you will not be able to sign up again with the same email address within a certain time frame.
