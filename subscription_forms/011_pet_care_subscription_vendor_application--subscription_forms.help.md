# pet_care_subscription_vendor_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for pet care subscription vendors. The purpose of this form is to gather details about the vendor's services, pricing, contact information, and agreement terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all the services you offer.
2. Enter your pricing information.
3. Provide your contact information.
4. Agree to the vendor's agreement terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Basic Info** (`basic_info`, text, optional): Enter your basic information, including your name and business name (if applicable).
* **Services** (`vendor_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select all services you offer. This may include:
	+ Grooming
	+ Walking
	+ Training
	+ Boarding
	+ Other services
* **Pricing Info** (`pricing_info`, text, optional): Enter your pricing information, including rates and any other relevant details.
* **Pricing** (`pricing`, number, optional): Enter the pricing for your services.
* **Vendor Contact** (`vendor_contact`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_docs`, select_one, optional): Please select if you have any documents to upload.
