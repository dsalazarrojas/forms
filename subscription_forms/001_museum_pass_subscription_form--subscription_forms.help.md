# Museum Pass Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Museum Pass Subscription Form is a tool for individuals to sign up for a museum pass subscription. The form collects contact information and subscription preferences to process a user's subscription request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the User Details section with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select your preferred Pass Type (Standard, Premium, or Basic) and Subscription Period (Month, Month-to-Month, or Year) in the Select Pass Type and Subscription Period section.
3. Enter your Start Date in the Subscription Start Date section.
4. Provide your Phone Number in the Phone Number section.
5. Enter your Email address in the Email section.
6. Add any additional Note if needed in the Note section.
7. Choose your Payment Method (Pay Online, Pay By Mail, or Pay In Person) in the Payment Method section.
8. If you selected Pay Online, enter your Online Payment Details in the Online Payment Details section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details**: Enter your name, as it will be displayed on your pass.
* **Select Pass Type**: Choose the type of pass you'd like to subscribe for (Standard, Premium, or Basic).
* **Subscription Period**: Select how long you'd like to subscribe for (Month, Month-to-Month, or Year).
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you'd like your subscription to start.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Note**: Add any additional information or note that you'd like to include with your subscription request.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you'd like to pay for your subscription (Pay Online, Pay By Mail, or Pay In Person).
* **Online Payment Details**: If you selected Pay Online, enter any additional information required for online payment.
