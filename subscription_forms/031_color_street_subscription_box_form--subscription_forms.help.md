# Color Street Subscription Box Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your subscription preferences and help Color Street tailor its subscription box service to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Review each field carefully and provide as much information as possible.
3. If you have any questions or need clarification on a particular field, feel free to reach out to Color Street's support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. \*\* Name (sub_customer_name)
Your name will be used to personalize your subscription experience.
- Type: text
- Required: true

### 2. \*\* Email Address (sub_email)
Provide your email address to receive important updates and notifications about your subscription.
- Type: email
- Required: true

### 3. -- Box Customization -- (section_box_preferences)
This section is where you can provide us with your thoughts on what you'd like to see in your subscription box.
- Type: note
- Required: false

### 4. \*\* How often would you like a subscription box? (sub_box_frequency)
Choose how often you'd like to receive a subscription box:
- Options: Monthly, Every 2 Months, Quarterly (Every 3 months)
- Type: select_one
- Required: true

### 5. \*\* Which styles should we prioritize? (sub_preferred_styles)
Select the styles you're most interested in:
- Type: select_multiple
- Required: true
- Options: New Releases, Best Sellers, Mystery Picks, Seasonal/Holiday, Tools and Care (Nail files, etc.)

### 6. \*\* Favorite colors for your box (sub_color_favorites)
Feel free to share your favorite colors with us.
- Type: text
- Required: false

### 7. \*\* Preferred price point for the box (sub_box_budget)
Choose your preferred price point:
- Options: $25 - $35 (2-3 sets + goodies), $40 - $55 (4-5 sets + premium goodies), $60+ (Ultimate fan box)
- Type: select_one
- Required: true

### 8. -- Your Ideas -- (section_sub_feedback)
This section is where you can provide any suggestions or ideas you have for your subscription box experience.
- Type: note
- Required: false

### 9. \*\* What "extras" would you love to see in the box? (sub_add_on_ideas)
Feel free to share any additional items you'd like to see in your subscription box.
- Type: text
- Required: false

### 10. \*\* Do you already have a Color Street Stylist? (sub_current_stylist)
Let us know if you already have a Color Street Stylist.
- Type: select_one
- Required: true

### 11. \*\* If yes, what is their name? (sub_stylist_name)
If you have a Color Street Stylist, provide their name.
- Type: text
- Required: false

### 12. \*\* On a scale of 1-10, how likely are you to subscribe? (sub_interest_level)
Rate how likely you are to subscribe on a scale of 1-10.
- Type: number
- Required: true

### 13. \*\* Can I contact you when the subscription box launches? (sub_launch_notification)
Let us know if you'd like to be contacted when the subscription box launches.
- Type: select_one
- Required: true

### 14. -- Anything else you want to share? -- (sub_final_thoughts)
This is your last chance to share any final thoughts or comments.
- Type: text
- Required: false
