# Free Month Subscription Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Free Month Subscription Request form is used to collect information from users who wish to request a free month subscription. It helps to identify users' interests and confirm their subscription request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Request Details field with a brief description of your request.
2. Select your interest in the services offered by checking the corresponding checkboxes. Note that some users may not be interested in any services.
3. Confirm your subscription request by selecting the correct option from the dropdown list.
4. Provide additional notes if needed.
5. If your subscription is for business purposes, enter your company name.
6. Finally, indicate whether your subscription is for personal or business use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Details (1)**: A short description of your request, e.g., "I would like to request a free month subscription for my new business."
* **Services (2)**: Check the services you are interested in. You can select one or multiple services, or none if you are not interested in any.
* **Confirm Subscription Request (3)**: Confirm your subscription request by selecting the option that applies to you.
* **Additional Notes (4)**: Provide any additional information that may be helpful for us to understand your request.
* **Company Name (if applicable) (5)**: If your subscription is for business purposes, enter your company name.
* **Business or Personal (6)**: Indicate whether your subscription is for personal or business use.
