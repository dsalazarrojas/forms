# Subscription Service Value Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Subscription Service Value Feedback Form is a tool designed to collect valuable insights from customers about their experience with our subscription service. This form helps us to understand how our customers perceive the value of our service, identify areas that need improvement, and make data-driven decisions to enhance the overall customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please carefully read the instructions and guidelines below.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Select the options that best describe your experience with our subscription service.
2. Provide numerical values as required for fields such as satisfaction score, service value score, and billing frequency.
3. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "What can we improve?" and "Additional comments" sections.
4. Fill in your contact information (email and phone number) if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions.
5. Answer the questions related to your subscription status, payment status, and billing method.
6. Select the options that best describe your experience with our contact method, resolution, and communication channel.
7. Provide any additional comments on resolution and subscription length.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subscription Service** (`subscription_form`, Select Multiple, **Required: No**): Choose 'Yes' if you believe our subscription service offers good value to you.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_score`, Number, **Required: No**): Rate your satisfaction with our service (scale: 1-10).
* **Service Value** (`service_value`, Select One, **Required: No**): Choose 'Yes' if you believe our service provides value to you.
* **Service Value Score** (`service_value_score`, Number, **Required: No**): Rate the value provided by our service (scale: 1-10).
* **What can we improve?** (`feedback`, Text, **Required: No**): Share your thoughts on what we can do better.
* **What plan are you currently subscribed to?** (`plan`, Select One, **Required: No**): Choose the plan you are currently subscribed to.
* **How frequently do you use our service?** (`feedback_frequency`, Number, **Required: No**): Rate the frequency of your usage (scale: 1-10).
* **Are you satisfied with your subscription?** (`retention`, Select One, **Required: No**): Choose 'Yes' if you are satisfied with your subscription.
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, Note, **Required: No**): Share any additional thoughts or feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, **Required: No**): Provide your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, **Required: No**): Provide your phone number so we can contact you for follow-up questions.
* **How did you contact us?** (`contact_method`, Select One, **Required: No**): Choose the method you used to contact us.
* **Who contacted you?** (`contact_source`, Select One, **Required: No**): Choose who contacted you.
* **What channel was used?** (`channel`, Select One, **Required: No**): Choose the channel used for communication.
* **Did we resolve the issue?** (`resolution`, Select One, **Required: No**): Choose 'Yes' if the issue was resolved.
* **How long did it take to resolve?** (`resolution_time`, Number, **Required: No**): Rate the time it took to resolve the issue (scale: 1-10).
* **Additional comments on resolution** (`comments_resolution`, Note, **Required: No**): Share any additional thoughts on the resolution.
* **Length of Subscription** (`subscription_length`, Number, **Required: No**): Rate the length of your subscription (scale: 1-10).
* **Status of Subscription** (`subscription_status`, Select One, **Required: No**): Choose the status of your subscription.
* **Type of Subscription** (`subscription_type`, Select One, **Required: No**): Choose the type of subscription.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, Select One, **Required: No**): Choose the status of your payment.
* **Billing Method** (`billing_method`, Select One, **Required: No**): Choose the billing method.
* **Billing Frequency** (`billing_frequency`, Number, **Required: No**): Rate the billing frequency (scale: 1-10).
* **Billing Status** (`billing_status`, Select One, **Required: No**): Choose the status of your billing.
* **Additional comments** (`feedback_comments`, Note, **Required: No**): Share any additional thoughts or feedback.

### Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Be as detailed as possible in your answers to provide valuable feedback.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please describe them in the "What can we improve?" and "Additional comments" sections.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us at [insert contact information].
