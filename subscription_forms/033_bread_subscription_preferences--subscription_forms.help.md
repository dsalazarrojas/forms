# Bread Subscription Preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for your bread subscription preferences. Please fill out this form to ensure that we deliver your preferred bread on time and in the right quantity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the personal information section (fields 1-3) with your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose your preferred subscription plan (field 4) from the options provided.
3. Select your favorite bread types (field 5) by choosing multiple options.
4. Choose your delivery frequency (field 6), preferred delivery day (field 7), and delivery time window (field 8).
5. Enter your delivery address (fields 9-12) accurately.
6. Specify your slice preference (field 14) and any dietary restrictions (field 15).
7. Add any add-on items (field 16) you would like to receive with your subscription.
8. Choose your payment method (field 17).
9. Set the start date of your subscription (field 18).
10. Specify your vacation hold preferences (field 19).
11. Accept the subscription terms (field 20).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subscriber Full Name** (field 1): Your full legal name.
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (field 2): For subscription communications.
	+ This will be used for order updates, delivery notifications, and special promotions.
* **Phone Number** (field 3): Contact number for delivery.
	+ We will use this number to contact you regarding delivery updates or issues.
* **Subscription Plan** (field 4): Choose your subscription tier.
	+ Select from Basic, Standard, Premium, Family, or Custom Plan.
* **Bread Preferences** (field 5): Select your favorite bread types.
	+ Choose multiple options from Sourdough, Whole Wheat, White Bread, Rye, Multigrain, Baguette, Ciabatta, Focaccia, Brioche, and Challah.
* **Delivery Frequency** (field 6): How often should we deliver.
	+ Choose from Twice Per Week, Once Per Week, Every Other Week, or Monthly.
* **Preferred Delivery Day** (field 7): Which day works best.
	+ Select from Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, or Saturday.
* **Delivery Time Window** (field 8): Preferred time for delivery.
	+ Choose from Morning 7 AM to 10 AM, Mid-Morning 10 AM to 12 PM, Afternoon 12 PM to 3 PM, or Evening 3 PM to 6 PM.
* **Delivery Address Line 1** (field 9): Street address.
	+ Enter your street address as it appears on official documents.
* **Delivery Address Line 2** (field 10): Apartment, suite, unit.
	+ Enter your apartment or unit number if applicable.
* **City** (field 11): City.
	+ Enter the name of the city where your delivery will be made.
* **ZIP Code** (field 12): ZIP or Postal Code.
	+ Enter the numeric code for your area.
* **Delivery Instructions** (field 13): Any special instructions for delivery.
	+ If you have any special instructions, enter them here.
* **Slice Preference** (field 14): How you want your bread sliced.
	+ Choose from Unsliced, Thin Sliced, Medium Sliced, Thick Sliced, or Half Loaves.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (field 15): Any allergies or dietary needs.
	+ If you have any dietary restrictions, select from None, Gluten Sensitivity, Celiac Disease, Dairy-Free, Vegan, or Nut Allergy.
* **Add-On Items** (field 16): Would you like to add any extras.
	+ Choose from Butter, Jam or Preserves, Honey, Olive Oil, Bread Sticks, or Rolls.
* **Payment Method** (field 17): How will you pay.
	+ Choose from Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Subscription Start Date** (field 18): When should your subscription begin.
	+ Enter the date you want your subscription to start.
* **Vacation Hold Preferences** (field 19): How should we handle vacations.
	+ Choose from Skip with Notice, Continue Delivery, or Donate to Charity.
* **Subscription Terms** (field 20): Do you accept our subscription terms.
	+ If you do not accept the terms, please do not complete this form.
