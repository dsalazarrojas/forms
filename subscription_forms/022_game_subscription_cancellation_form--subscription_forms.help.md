# Game Subscription Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you cancel your game subscription. Please fill out the form with the required information to ensure a smooth cancellation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your game details.
2. Share your customer information.
3. Select a reason for cancellation.
4. If needed, provide more details about the reason you selected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Game Details (Game Details)
* **Game Details** (`game_details`, text, optional): Please enter your game's title or the name of the game you want to cancel.

### Customer Info (Customer Info)
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.

### Cancellation Reason (Cancellation Reason)
* **Cancellation Reason** (`cancellation_reason`, select_one, optional): Choose a reason for cancelling your game subscription. Options include:
	+ Game Not Fun
	+ Too Expensive
	+ Other (for any other reason)

### Cancellation Reason Other (Cancellation Reason Other)
* **Cancellation Reason Other** (`cancellation_reason_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" as the cancellation reason, please provide more details about your reason.

### Comments (Comments)
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them here.
