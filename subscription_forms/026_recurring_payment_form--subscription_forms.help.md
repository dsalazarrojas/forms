# Recurring Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recurring Payment Form is designed for users to input their payment details and preferences for recurring transactions such as subscription plans or invoices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have filled out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your payment details in the relevant fields.
3. Select your preferred payment method (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
4. Specify the payment frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
5. Choose the status of your payment (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
6. Add any additional notes if required.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input (1)**: This field is an open input for users to enter any additional information not covered by the other fields.
* **Payment Details (2)**: Enter a description or notes about the payment, such as the invoice number or payment plan name.
* **Payment Method (3)**: Choose how you want to make the payment. Options include:
	+ Stripe: An online payment method that processes payments directly.
	+ PayPal: An online payment method that uses PayPal to process payments.
	+ Bank Transfer: A traditional bank transfer method for payment.
* **Payment Date (4)**: Enter the date you want to make the payment.
* **Payment Amount (5)**: Enter the amount you want to pay.
* **Payment Frequency (6)**: Choose how often you want to make the payment. Options include:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Annually
* **Payment Status (7)**: Select the status of your payment. Options include:
	+ Active: Payment is active and ongoing.
	+ Inactive: Payment is inactive or on hold.
	+ Pending: Payment is pending review or confirmation.
* **Note (8)**: Enter any additional notes or information about the payment.
* **Email (9)**: Enter your email address for contact or notification purposes.
* **Phone (10)**: Enter your phone number (if required).
* **Assigned Tool (11)**: This field appears to be intended for internal use or assignment by a tool or system. Enter any relevant information if required.
