# Unlimited Internet Plan Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers to register for an unlimited internet plan, providing details about their subscription and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide unlimited internet plan details**: Fill in the "Unlimited Internet Plan Details" section with your chosen plan.
2. **Enter customer information**: In the "Customer Info" section, enter your name and relevant details.
3. **Choose a plan option**: Select the plan that suits your needs from the "Plan Choice" dropdown menu.
4. **Specify installation preferences**: Select where you'd like your installation to take place from the "Installation Preferences" option.
5. **Add contact information**: In the "Customer Info" section, enter your email address and phone number.
6. **Enter address**: Provide your address in the "Address" field.
7. **Add additional details**: If you have any additional information, please add it in the "Additional Details" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Unlimited Internet Plan Details (text)**: Enter a brief description of the unlimited internet plan you're interested in.
* **Customer Info (text)**: Provide your name and relevant customer details.
* **Plan Choice (select)**: Choose between "Basic Plan", "Medium Plan", and "Advanced Plan" that suits your needs.
* **Installation Preferences (select)**: Select "Install at Home", "Install at Work", or "Install at Both" for your installation location.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address associated with your customer info.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address (text)**: Enter your complete address for installation purposes.
* **Additional Details (note)**: Provide any additional details you'd like to share with us.
