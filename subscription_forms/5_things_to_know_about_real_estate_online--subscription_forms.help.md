# 5K Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to sign up for a 5K run event. It collects necessary information for event management, participant placement, and waiver purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button after filling out all required fields. Note that some fields are optional and can be filled out or skipped.
2. Ensure you complete all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*) in the label.
3. Review your information for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Full name
* `Full name`: Enter your complete name, including both first and last name.

### Email address
* `Email address`: Enter your email address for confirmation and bib information.

### Phone number
* `Phone number`: Enter your phone number for last-minute updates and event communication.

### Date of birth
* `Date of birth`: Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.

### Shirt size
* `Shirt size`: Choose your preferred shirt size for the event.

### Running experience level
* `Running experience level`: Select your level of running experience to help place you in the correct wave.

### I agree to event terms and waiver
* `I agree to event terms and waiver`: This field is required for participant liability.

### Optional donation amount
* `Optional donation amount`: Enter a numeric amount if you'd like to make an optional donation.

### Additional comments or accessibility needs
* `Additional comments or accessibility needs`: Describe any accommodations required for a smooth and enjoyable event experience.

## Tips
* Be honest when selecting your running experience level, as it affects your wave placement.
* Ensure your email address is correct for confirmation and bib information.
* If you have any specific needs or requirements, please include them in the "Additional comments or accessibility needs" field.
