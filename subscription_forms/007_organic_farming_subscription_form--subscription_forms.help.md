# organic_farming_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The organic farming subscription form is a tool for farmers to sign up for subscription plans for organic farming services. This form collects information about the farmer's contact details, farm name, subscription preferences, and other relevant data. By using this form, farmers can easily sign up for a subscription that suits their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the organic farming subscription form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred subscription plan from the "Subscription Plan" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the name of your farm in the "Farm Name" field.
7. Choose the start date for your subscription in the "Start Date" field.
8. Select the end date for your subscription in the "End Date" field.
9. Choose the frequency of your subscription from the "Frequency" dropdown menu.
10. Enter any additional notes about your subscription in the "Notes" field (optional).
11. Enter the name of the tool assigned to you in the "Assigned Tool" field.
12. Enter a unique ID for this form in the "Form ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
  * This field is required and should contain your first name.
- **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
  * This field is required and should contain your last name.
- **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
  * This field is required and should be in the format of a valid email address.
- **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
  * This field is required and should contain your phone number.
- **Subscription Plan** (select_multiple, required): Select your preferred subscription plan.
  * Choose one or more of the available options (Organic Farm prophets, Organic Farm monthly, Organic Farm weekly).
- **Farm Name** (text, required): Enter your farm name.
  * This field is required and should contain the name of your farm.
- **Start Date** (date, required): Choose the start date for your subscription.
  * This field is required and should contain a valid date.
- **End Date** (date, required): Choose the end date for your subscription.
  * This field is required and should contain a valid date.
- **Frequency** (select_one, required): Choose the frequency of your subscription.
  * Select one of the available options (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
- **Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your subscription.
  * This field is optional and should contain any additional information you'd like to share.
- **Assigned Tool** (text, required): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you.
  * This field is required and should contain the name of the tool assigned to you.
- **Form ID** (number, required): Enter a unique ID for this form.
  * This field is required and should contain a unique number.
