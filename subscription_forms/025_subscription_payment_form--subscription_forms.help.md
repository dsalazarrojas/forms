# Subscription Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for subscription payments, such as payment method, subscription type, and other relevant details. It is meant to be used for various subscription services, including online memberships, one-time payments, and recurring payments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Payment Method**: Select one of the available payment methods: Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
2. **Subscription Type**: Choose the type of subscription you need: Monthly, Yearly, or One Time. Note that this field is not required.
3. **Subscription Start Date**: Enter the start date of your subscription. This field is not required.
4. **Subscription End Date**: Enter the end date of your subscription. This field is not required.
5. **Card Number**: If you have chosen Credit Card as your payment method, enter your card number here. This field is not required.
6. **Card Expiration**: If you have chosen Credit Card as your payment method, select your card expiration date from the options provided. This field is not required.
7. **Card CVC**: If you have chosen Credit Card as your payment method, enter your card security code (CVC) here. This field is not required.
8. **Email**: Enter your email address. This field is not required.
9. **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the available payment methods to complete your subscription payment.
* **Subscription Type** (`Subscription Type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of subscription you require, such as Monthly, Yearly, or One Time.
* **Subscription Start Date** (`Subscription Start Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of your subscription if required.
* **Subscription End Date** (`Subscription End Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of your subscription if required.
* **Card Number** (`Card Number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your card number if you have chosen Credit Card as your payment method.
* **Card Expiration** (`Card Expiration`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your card expiration date if you have chosen Credit Card as your payment method.
* **Card CVC** (`Card CVC`, `text`, required: false): Enter your card security code (CVC) if you have chosen Credit Card as your payment method.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for subscription-related communications.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for subscription-related communications.
