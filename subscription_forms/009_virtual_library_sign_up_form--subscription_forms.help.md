# Virtual Library Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects demographic and reading preference information from users to create a user profile for the virtual library service.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Virtual Library Sign Up Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone number" field.
5. Enter your street address in the "street" field.
6. Enter your city in the "city" field.
7. Enter your state in the "state" field.
8. Enter your zip code in the "zip code" field.
9. Select your preferred book genres from the "book genres" field.
10. Select your reading speed from the "reading speed" field (number of pages per day).
11. Select your reading frequency from the "reading frequency" field (how often you read).
12. Select your preferred reading format from the "reading format" field (type of format you prefer).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Street** (text, required/optional): Enter your street address.
* **City** (text, required/optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (text, required/optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip Code** (number, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Book Genres** (select multiple, required/optional): Select the types of books you are interested in reading. Options: Action, Adventure, Science, Fantasy, Romance.
* **Reading Speed** (number, required/optional): Select the number of pages you can read per day.
* **Reading Frequency** (select one, required/optional): Select how often you read. Options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Never.
* **Reading Format** (select one, required/optional): Select the type of format you prefer. Options: Book, E-book, Audiobook, Digital, Print.

Please note that all fields are optional, but your "Reading Frequency" and "Reading Format" fields are required to be filled out.
