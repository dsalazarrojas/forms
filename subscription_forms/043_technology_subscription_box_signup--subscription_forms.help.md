# Technology Subscription Box Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather information for a Technology Subscription Box Signup. Please follow these steps to complete the form successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your subscription frequency from the "subscription_signups" field. Choose from options like "Monthly", "Quarterly", or "Annually".
2. Select your plan from the "plan_selections" field. Options include "Monthly - 30 day free trial", "Monthly", "Quarterly", or "Annually".
3. Choose your delivery preferences from the "delivery_preferences" field. Select one or more options, such as "Home", "Office", or "Other".
4. Enter your delivery address in the "delivery_address" field.
5. Enter your city in the "delivery_city" field.
6. Enter your zip/postal code in the "delivery_zip" field.
7. Select your payment type from the "payment_type" field. Options include "Credit Card", "Bank Transfer", or "Other".
8. Enter your card number in the "card_number" field.
9. Enter your expiry date in the "expiry_date" field.
10. Select your payment method from the "payment_method" field. Choose from options like "Stripe" or "PayPal".
11. Enter your card security code in the "card_cvv" field.
12. Enter your card zip code in the "card_zip" field.
13. Select the duration of your plan from the "plan_duration" field. Choose from "1-Month", "3-Month", or "6-Month".
14. Select the frequency of your plan from the "plan_frequency" field. Select from options like "Daily", "Weekly", or "Monthly".
15. Enter your payment method details in the "payment_method_details" field.
16. Enter your payment method ID in the "payment_method_id" field.
17. Enter the total order amount in the "order_total" field.
18. Enter the shipping cost in the "shipping_cost" field.
19. Enter the tax rate in the "tax_rate" field.
20. Enter the shipping rate in the "shipping_rate" field.
21. Add any additional notes about your subscription in the "notes" field.
22. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
23. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
24. Enter your name in the "name" field.
25. Enter your company name in the "company" field.
