# Email Subscription Incentive - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Email Subscription Incentive form is designed to capture user's information for an email subscription service. It is intended for users who want to subscribe to the service and receive rewards.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Select Your Incentive Reward
* **incentive_reward**: Select the reward you are interested in receiving as an incentive for subscribing to the email service. This can be any of the options: "Reward 1", "Reward 2", or "Reward 3".

### Step 2: Enter Your Email Address
* **email_address**: Enter your valid email address where you would like to receive emails from us. Please ensure it is formatted as: `your.name@example.com`.

### Step 3: Confirm Your Email Address
* **confirm_email**: Confirm that the email address you entered is correct. Please select "Yes" if it's accurate.

### Step 4: Provide Your Name
* **first_name**: Enter your first name as it should appear in the email communications.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name as it should appear in the email communications.

### Step 5: Select Your Incentive Type
* **incentive_type**: Select the type of incentive you are interested in. This can be any of the options: "Incentive 1", "Incentive 2", or "Incentive 3".

### Step 6: Opt-in for Emails
* **email_optin**: Confirm that you would like to receive emails from us. Please select "Yes" if it's accurate.

### Step 7: Select Email Frequency
* **email_frequency**: Select how frequently you would like to receive emails from us. This can be "Once", "Daily", or "Weekly".

### Step 8: Set Email Frequency Details
* **email_frequency_frequency**: Select the frequency of your email preferences. This can be "Monthly", "Weekly", or "Daily".
* **email_frequency_type**: Select the type of email frequency you prefer. This can be "Once", "Continuous", or "Periodically".
* **email_frequency_interval**: If you selected a periodic frequency, enter the interval (e.g., "weekly" or "monthly").
* **email_frequency_unit**: Enter a unit of time for your email frequency preferences (e.g., "hours", "days", etc.).

### Step 8: Set Email Incentive Details
* **incentive_amount**: Set a monetary amount as an incentive for your subscription.
* **signup_bonus**: Set an amount as a bonus for signing up.
* **email_frequency_frequency**: Confirm your email frequency preferences.
* **email_frequency_type**: Confirm your email frequency type preferences.
* **email_frequency_interval**: Confirm your email frequency interval preferences.
* **email_frequency_unit**: Confirm your email frequency unit preferences.

### Step 9: Confirm Your Opt-in
* **confirm_optin**: Confirm that you would like to receive emails from us. Please select "Yes" if it's accurate.

### Step 10: Review and Submit
* Please review all the information entered carefully before submitting the form. If everything looks good, click "Submit" to complete your subscription.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact us using the contact form.
* We will use the collected data to tailor email communication to your preferences.
* You can unsubscribe from the email service at any time.
