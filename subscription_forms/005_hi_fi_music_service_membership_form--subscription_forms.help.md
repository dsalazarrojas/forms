## Hi Fi Music Service Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form collects information from users who are interested in becoming members of our Hi Fi Music Service. The data collected here will be used to onboard users into our service and provide them with the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the required fields (marked as "required") carefully.
2. Use the provided hints for guidance on any field you're unsure about.
3. Ensure that all the provided information is accurate.
4. Once you've filled out all the required fields, click the submit button to submit your membership application.
