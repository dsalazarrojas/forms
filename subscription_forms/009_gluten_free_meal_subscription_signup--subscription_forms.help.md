# Gluten Free Meal Subscription Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gluten Free Meal Subscription Signup form is designed to collect user information for a gluten-free meal subscription service. This form is part of a larger system that handles meal ordering and subscription management. It is intended for users who have been invited to subscribe to the gluten-free meal service and have not already subscribed or been removed from the subscription list.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Information in the "User Information" page.
2. Provide your Subscription Info in the "Subscription Info" page.
3. Choose your preferred Delivery Method in the "Delivery Method" page.
4. Select your preferred Payment Frequency in the "Payment Frequency" page.
5. If necessary, add any Special Instructions in the "Special Instructions" page.
6. Review and agree to the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Agreement in the respective pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Information (page 1)
* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information to help us get in touch with you.
### 2. Subscription Info (page 1)
* **Subscription Info** (`subscription_info`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about your subscription preferences, such as dietary restrictions or special requests.
### 3. Delivery Method (page 2)
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to receive your meals, whether Home, Pickup, or Delivery.
### 4. Payment Frequency (page 2)
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how often you would like to receive your meals, whether Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
### 5. Special Instructions (page 3)
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `note`, optional): If you have any special requests or instructions for your meal subscription, please provide them here.
### 6. Terms & Conditions (page 4)
* **Terms & Conditions** (`terms_conditions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Review and agree to our Terms & Conditions.
### 7. Privacy Agreement (page 4)
* **Privacy Agreement** (`privacy_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Review and agree to our Privacy Agreement. 

Note: If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
