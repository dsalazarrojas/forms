# Marketing strategy subscription form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your marketing strategy and preferences to help our marketing team better understand your needs and provide tailored solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the marketing strategy that best describes your current approach (Marketing strategy).
2. Choose the marketing channels you are using or interested in (Marketing channels).
3. Enter your marketing budget (if applicable).
4. Indicate whether you have a specific target audience in mind (Target audience).
5. Select the marketing strategy subscription you are looking for (Marketing strategy subscription).
6. Choose the marketing preferences that align with your needs (Marketing preferences).
7. Enter your email address (Email).
8. Enter your phone number (Phone number).
9. Provide any additional marketing-related needs or preferences you have (Marketing needs).
10. Add any additional notes or comments (Notes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Marketing Strategy** (<type>: `select_multiple`): Please select one or more of the options that best describe your current marketing strategy.
	+ Good: You have a well-planned and executed marketing strategy.
	+ Fair: Your marketing strategy is somewhat developed but may need refinement.
* **Marketing Channels** (<type>: `select_one`): Choose the marketing channels you are using or interested in.
	+ Yes: You are currently using or interested in using this channel.
	+ No: You are not using or interested in using this channel.
* **Marketing Budget** (<type>: `number`): Enter your marketing budget if you have one. If you don't have a specific budget, you can leave this field blank.
* **Target Audience** (<type>: `select_multiple`): Indicate whether you have a specific target audience in mind.
	+ Yes: You have a defined target audience.
	+ No: You don't have a specific target audience.
* **Marketing Strategy Subscription** (<type>: `select_one`): Select the marketing strategy subscription you are looking for.
	+ Good: A comprehensive marketing strategy that aligns with your needs.
	+ Fair: A basic marketing strategy that meets your minimum requirements.
* **Marketing Preferences** (<type>: `select_multiple`): Choose the marketing preferences that align with your needs.
	+ Yes: You prefer to use this marketing preference.
	+ No: You do not prefer to use this marketing preference.
* **Email** (<type>: `email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (<type>: `text`): Enter your phone number.
* **Marketing Needs** (<type>: `text`): Provide any additional marketing-related needs or preferences you have.
* **Notes** (<type>: `note`): Add any additional comments or notes about your marketing strategy and preferences.
