# nature_conservancy_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The nature_conservancy_subscription_form is a form designed to collect subscriber's information for Nature Conservancy's newsletter and updates management. Fill out this form to sign up for our newsletter and stay up-to-date on the latest environmental news and initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Enter your Phone number (optional).
4. Enter your Street Address (optional).
5. Enter your City (optional).
6. Select your State (Yes or No).
7. Enter your Zip code (optional).
8. Choose your Newsletter preference (Yes or No).
9. Enter your Donation amount (optional).
10. Provide any additional Notes (optional).
11. Review and agree to our Terms and Conditions.
12. Click the Submit button to submit your subscription.
13. If you are already subscribed, click Unsubscribe to cancel your subscription.
14. If you are not subscribed, click Subscribe to sign up for our newsletter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our newsletter.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address to receive our newsletter and updates.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like us to contact you.
* **Street Address** (Street Address, text, optional): Enter your street address for our records.
* **City** (City, text, optional): Enter your city to help us personalize our updates.
* **State** (State, select_one, optional): Choose whether you'd like us to use your location to personalize our updates.
* **Zip** (Zip, number, optional): Enter your zip code to help us personalize our updates.
* **Newsletter** (Newsletter, select_multiple, required): Choose whether you'd like to receive our newsletter and updates.
* **Donation** (Donation, number, optional): Enter any donation amount if you'd like to support our work.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional information or comments.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Terms and Conditions, text, required): Review and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, required): Click to submit your subscription.
* **Unsubscribe** (Unsubscribe, text, optional): Click to cancel your subscription if you're already subscribed.
* **Subscribe** (Subscribe, text, required): Click to sign up for our newsletter if you're not already subscribed.
