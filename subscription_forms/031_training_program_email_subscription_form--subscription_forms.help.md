# Training Program Email Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who are interested in our training programs. It helps us to better understand their needs and preferences, which enables us to provide them with tailored services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Email address in the required fields.
2. Choose the training program that interests you the most.
3. Select all applicable answers for how you learned about the training program (e.g., Yes, No).
4. Optionally, fill in your Location, Job Title, Department, Phone, Job Level, Industry, Job Function, Job Abbreviation, Other Source, and Email Source fields, if applicable.
5. Choose whether you are a member of any social network (Yes/No).
6. Provide any additional comments you may have.
7. Select your preferred certification type (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please fill in your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Please fill in your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your work or personal email address.
* **Training Program** (`program_interest`, `select_one`, required): Choose the training program that interests you the most.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide your location, please fill it in here.
* **How do you know about the training program?** (`how_learned`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the ways you learned about the training program (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Date Joined** (`date_joined`, `date`, optional): If you have an exact date you joined, please fill it in (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): If you have any comments or additional information, please type them here.
* **Email Type** (`email_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your email type (e.g., work, personal).
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide your company name, please fill it in here.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Please fill in your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide your department name, please fill it in here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide your phone number, please fill it in here.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your job level (e.g., High, Low).
* **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide your industry, please fill it in here.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide your job function, please fill it in here.
* **Job Abbreviation** (`job_abbreviation`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide your job abbreviation, please fill it in here.
* **How did you hear about the training program?** (`how_knew`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide more information about how you heard about the training program, please fill it in here.
* **How did you hear about the training program? (other)** (`how_knew_other`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide more information about other sources, please fill it in here.
* **Other Source** (`other_source`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide more information about other sources, please fill it in here.
* **Email Source** (`email_source`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide more information about email sources, please fill it in here.
