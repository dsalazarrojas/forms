# Subscription Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Subscription Renewal Form is designed to facilitate the renewal of your subscription to a service or product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Select your preferred **Subscription Plan** from the provided options.
6.  Choose a **Renewal Date** for your subscription renewal.
7.  Add any **Comments** regarding your subscription renewal.
8.  Select your agreement option from the provided choices in the "**Agreement**" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
*   **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
*   **Email** (email, optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your subscription.
*   **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Subscription Plan** (select_multiple, required): Select the subscription plan you want to renew, allowing you to continue enjoying the service or product.
*   **Renewal Date** (date, optional): Choose a date for your subscription renewal.
*   **Comments** (note, optional): Add any relevant comments or notes about your subscription renewal.
*   **Agreement** (select_one, required): Select your agreement to the terms of the renewal, ensuring your subscription is successfully renewed.
