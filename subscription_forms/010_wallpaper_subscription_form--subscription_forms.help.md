# Wallpaper Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to manage their subscription preferences for a wallpaper service. The purpose of this form is to collect information about the user's subscription preferences, such as their full name, email address, phone number, and subscription frequency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Choose your subscription frequency from the "Subscription Frequency" field.
5. Select a start and end date for your subscription.
6. Choose how often you want to receive new wallpapers.
7. Select the subscription type that suits your needs.
8. Confirm your subscription details.
