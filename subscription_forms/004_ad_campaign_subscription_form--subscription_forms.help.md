# Ad Campaign Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ad Campaign Subscription Form is designed to collect information from potential subscribers who are interested in learning more about our advertising services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is where we will send campaign updates.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone Number" field. This is optional.
4. If you are representing a company, enter your company name in the "Company Name" field. This is optional.
5. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field. This is optional.
6. Select the industry that best describes your work or business in the "Industry" field.
7. Choose the types of campaigns that interest you in the "Which campaigns are you interested in?" field.
8. Select your estimated monthly marketing budget in the "Estimated Monthly Marketing Budget" field.
9. If you have a primary target audience in mind, enter it in the "Primary Target Audience" field. This is optional.
10. Select the marketing goals that are most important to you in the "Primary Marketing Goals" field.
11. Choose the platforms where you would like to advertise in the "Preferred Advertising Platforms" field. This is optional.
12. Select how often you would like to receive campaign communications in the "How often would you like campaign communications?" field.
13. Choose your level of advertising experience in the "Your advertising experience level" field. This is optional.
14. If you are currently running any ad campaigns, select "True" in the "Are you currently running any ad campaigns?" field.
15. If you have any previous campaign performance results, enter them in the "Previous campaign performance" field. This is optional.
16. Choose how you heard about us in the "How did you hear about us?" field. This is optional.
17. If you have any additional information or questions, enter them in the "Additional information or questions" field. This is optional.
18. Select "I agree" to our terms and conditions in the "I agree to the terms and conditions" field.
19. Finally, select whether you would like to subscribe to our marketing newsletter in the "Subscribe to our marketing newsletter" field. This is optional.

* **Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your email address where we will send campaign updates. Please ensure this is a valid email address.
* **Phone Number (3)**: If you have a phone number, enter it here. This is optional.
* **Company Name (4)**: If you are representing a company, enter its name here. This is optional.
* **Job Title (5)**: If you have a job title, enter it here. This is optional.
* **Industry (6)**: Select the industry that best describes your work or business.
* **Which campaigns are you interested in? (7)**: Choose the types of campaigns that interest you. You can select multiple options.
* **Estimated Monthly Marketing Budget (8)**: Select your estimated monthly marketing budget.
* **Primary Target Audience (9)**: If you have a primary target audience, enter it here. This is optional.
* **Primary Marketing Goals (10)**: Select the marketing goals that are most important to you. You can select multiple options.
* **Preferred Advertising Platforms (11)**: Choose the platforms where you would like to advertise. You can select multiple options. This is optional.
* **How often would you like campaign communications? (12)**: Select how often you would like to receive campaign communications.
* **Your advertising experience level (13)**: Choose your level of advertising experience. This is optional.
* **Are you currently running any ad campaigns? (14)**: Select "True" if you are currently running ad campaigns.
* **Previous campaign performance (15)**: If you have any previous campaign performance results, enter them here. This is optional.
* **How did you hear about us? (16)**: Choose how you heard about us. This is optional.
* **Additional information or questions (17)**: If you have any additional information or questions, enter them here. This is optional.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions (18)**: Select "I agree" to our terms and conditions.
* **Subscribe to our marketing newsletter (19)**: Select whether you would like to subscribe to our marketing newsletter. This is optional.
