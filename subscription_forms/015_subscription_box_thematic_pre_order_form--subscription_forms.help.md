# subscription_box_thematic_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to provide their subscription box theme pre-order information. It collects their customer details, shipping details, order details, payment details, and any additional notes they'd like to include.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your shipping details, such as your address and contact information.
3. Enter your order details, including the theme you want to pre-order and any other specifics.
4. Enter your payment details, such as your method of payment and any relevant payment information.
5. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can include them in the notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, **required**): This field is for you to input your name and any other relevant customer information. This is the first step in providing your pre-order information.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, **required**): This field is where you input your shipping information, including your address and any other contact details. This ensures that we can ship your subscription box to the correct address.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, **required**): In this field, you input the specifics of your order, including the theme you want to pre-order. This helps us understand your order accurately.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, **required**): This field is where you input your payment information, including your payment method and any relevant details. This is a secure field, and we ensure that your information is protected.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, **optional**): This is an optional field where you can include any additional notes or comments about your pre-order. This could be anything from special requests to product preferences.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, **optional**): This is the final step. Once you've filled out all the required fields, click submit to complete your pre-order and we'll take care of the rest.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting, as we'll use this to process your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us using the information provided in this help guide.
* We'll contact you once we've processed your pre-order and are ready to ship your subscription box.
