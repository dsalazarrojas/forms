# trading_card_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The trading_card_subscription_form is designed to collect information from users for subscription purposes. This form is divided into multiple pages, each with different fields to gather specific information from the user.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your subscription preferences from the first page.
2. On the second page, select whether you would like to collect information about you, your phone number, or your address.
3. Enter your personal details, including your name and email address on the third page.
4. On the fourth page, enter your address details, including city, state, and zip code.
5. On the fifth page, select whether you would like to receive information from us and in which country you would like to receive it.
6. Finally, review and submit your subscription information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **subscribe_preferences** (Select Multiple, required: no): Select whether you would like to subscribe to our trading card service. This will determine what information we will collect from you.
* **collecting_preferences** (Select One, required: no): Select whether you would like to collect information about yourself, your phone number, or your address.
* **payment_information** (Select Multiple, required: no): Select whether you would like to pay for your subscription in advance or at the end of each month.
* **first_name** (Text, required: no): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (Text, required: no): Enter your last name.
* **email** (Email, required: no): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (Text, required: no): Enter your phone number.
* **address** (Text, required: no): Enter your address.
* **city** (Text, required: no): Enter your city.
* **state** (Select One, required: no): Select whether you would like to receive information from us in the state where you live.
* **zip** (Text, required: no): Enter your zip code.
* **country** (Select One, required: no): Select whether you would like to receive information from us in your country.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have selected your subscription preferences correctly, as this will determine what information we will collect from you.
* Make sure to enter your personal details accurately, as these will be used to contact you.
* Review your information carefully before submitting your subscription form.
