# Vitamin Subscription Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who want to cancel their vitamin subscription. Please fill out this form if you wish to end your subscription to our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
4. If you have any attachments or additional information to support your cancellation request, you may upload them separately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first name`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your first name as it appears on your account or identification.
* **Last name** (`last name`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your last name as it appears on your account or identification.
* **Reason for cancellation** (`reason`, `text`, required: false): Please select a reason for cancelling your subscription from the list (not provided in this form) or type a valid reason for your cancellation.
* **Account ID** (`account_id`, `text`, required: false): If you are unsure about your account ID, please refer to your account information or contact our support team.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please provide your email address as it is associated with your account.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number as it is associated with your account.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional feedback or comments regarding your cancellation, please provide them here.
