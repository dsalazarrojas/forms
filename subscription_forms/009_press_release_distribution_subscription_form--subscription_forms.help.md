# press_release_distribution_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The press_release_distribution_subscription_form is a form used to collect information from users who wish to subscribe to press release distribution services. This form is used to gather details such as user name, organization, contact email, phone number, press release topic, press release frequency, and confirmation of subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the user name field with your name as it should appear in our records.
2. Enter your organization's name in the organization field.
3. Provide your contact email address in the contact email field.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the phone number field (optional).
5. Select the press release topics you are interested in from the press release topic field.
6. Choose the frequency at which you would like to receive press releases from the press release frequency field.
7. Read and confirm that you will be contacted to confirm your subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name as it should appear in our records.
- **Organization** (2, text, required): Enter your organization's name.
- **Contact Email** (3, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
- **Phone Number** (4, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
- **Press Release Topic** (5, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of the following topics: Technology, Finance, Healthcare, Marketing, Media, News, Politics, Science, Sports, Transportation, Entertainment.
- **Press Release Frequency** (6, select_one, required): Choose the frequency at which you would like to receive press releases: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly.
- **Confirmation** (7, note, required): Read and confirm that you will be contacted to confirm your subscription.
