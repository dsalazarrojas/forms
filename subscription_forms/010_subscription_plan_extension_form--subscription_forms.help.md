# Subscription Plan Extension Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Subscription Plan Extension Form is used to submit a request to extend a subscription plan for a customer. It is designed to collect information from the customer to facilitate the process of extending their subscription plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the "Customer Information" page to fill out the form with your personal information.
2. On the second page, select your current subscription plan from the list.
3. On the same page, select your desired subscription plan from the list.
4. On the "Extension Request Details" page, provide any additional information you would like to include with your request.
5. If requested, sign the "Customer Signature" page with your signature.
6. On the "Confirm Contact Information" page, confirm your contact information to ensure it is accurate.
7. Finally, click "Confirm" to submit the form to your account representative for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (Page 1)**
  - **Title (text)**: This field is for the title to be filled out by the customer.
* **Current Subscription Plan (Page 2)**
  - **Current Subscription Plan (select_one)**: Select your current subscription plan from the list.
* **Desired Subscription Plan (Page 2)**
  - **Desired Subscription Plan (select_one)**: Select the subscription plan you would like to extend to.
* **Extension Request Details (Page 3)**
  - **Extension Request Details (text)**: Provide any additional information you would like to include with your request.
* **Customer Signature (Page 4)**
  - **Customer Signature (text)**: If requested, sign this field with your signature.
* **Confirm Contact Information (Page 5)**
  - **Confirm Contact Information (text)**: Confirm your contact information to ensure it is accurate. 
* **Confirm (Page 5)**
  - **Confirm (text)**: This field is used to submit the form to your account representative for review and approval.
