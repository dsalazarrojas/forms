# vehicle_maintenance_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The vehicle_maintenance_subscription_form is designed to provide vehicle owners with a convenient way to subscribe to maintenance services for their vehicle. This form is intended to be used by vehicle owners who want to schedule and manage their vehicle maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information in the `customer_info` field.
2. Add your contact details, including phone number and email address in the `contact_details` field.
3. Select your preferred services, such as oil changes, tire rotations, and tire inspections in the `services_preferences` field.
4. Provide additional vehicle information, such as your vehicle's make and model, in the `vehicle_info` field.
5. Enter any customer preferences or notes in the `customer_preferences` field.
6. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
7. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
8. Enter a note or message in the `note` field (optional).
9. Select the service frequency in the `service_frequency` field.
10. Enter the date for the service in the `service_date` field.
11. Select your preferred payment method, such as online payment or cash, in the `payment_method` field.
12. Confirm your subscription in the `confirm_subscription` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_info** (text, required: false): Enter your customer name and any other relevant information.
* **contact_details** (text, required: false): Add your contact details, including phone number and email address.
* **services_preferences** (select multiple, required: false): Select the services you want to subscribe to, such as oil changes, tire rotations, and tire inspections.
* **vehicle_info** (text, required: false): Provide additional vehicle information, such as your vehicle's make and model.
* **customer_preferences** (text, required: false): Enter any customer preferences or notes.
* **email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **note** (note, required: false): Enter a note or message (optional).
* **service_frequency** (number, required: false): Select the frequency of the services, such as weekly, bi-weekly, etc.
* **service_date** (date, required: false): Enter the date for the service.
* **payment_method** (select one, required: false): Select your preferred payment method, such as online payment or cash.
* **confirm_subscription** (select one, required: false): Confirm your subscription by selecting 'Yes'.
