# Utility Auto Pay Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended for customers to enroll in the utility auto pay program. By filling out this form, customers can set up their account for automated billing and payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your first name, last name, email address, and phone number to identify yourself as a customer.
2. Select the billing service provider and provide their address.
3. Choose the start and end dates for the service.
4. Indicate whether you are a new or existing customer and if you have already signed up for the service.
5. Confirm your service type, frequency, and payment status.
6. Select your preferred method of authorization.
7. Add any additional notes or comments for the customer or service provider.
8. Check the terms and conditions box to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the program's terms.
9. Finally, review and submit the form for verification and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`customer_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`customer_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Billing Service Provider** (`billing_service_provider_name`, text, required: false): Select the billing service provider you are working with.
* **Service Provider Address** (`billing_service_provider_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the billing service provider.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Choose the start date for the service.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Choose the end date for the service.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select one, required: false): Choose whether you are a new or existing customer.
* **Service Frequency** (`service_frequency`, select one, required: false): Choose how frequently you would like to receive services.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select one, required: false): Choose how frequently you would like to make payments.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select one, required: false): Indicate your current payment status.
* **Authorization Method** (`authorization_method`, select one, required: false): Choose the method of authorization for the service.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, note, required: false): Add any notes or comments for the customer.
* **Service Provider Signature** (`billing_service_provider_signature`, note, required: false): Add any notes or comments for the service provider.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required: false): Check the box to acknowledge and agree to the program's terms and conditions.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Service Start Date** (`service_start_date`, date, required: false): Choose the start date for the service.
