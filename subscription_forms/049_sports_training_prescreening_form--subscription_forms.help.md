# Sports Training Prescreening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Training Prescreening Form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to participate in sports training programs. This form helps to identify potential medical risks and ensures that participants are aware of their emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select "Emergency Contact" for the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field if you have a contact person who should be notified in case of an emergency.
6. For the "Medical History" field, select all conditions that apply to you.
7. If you have a doctor, enter their Name, Phone number, Email address, and Facility information in the "Doctor Name", "Doctor Phone", "Doctor Email", and "Doctor Facility" fields respectively.
8. Enter any additional notes about your doctor in the "Doctor Notes" field.
9. Enter the Name, Phone number, and Email address of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name", "Emergency Contact Phone", and "Emergency Contact Email" fields.
10. Enter a description of your emergency contact relationship with the person you entered in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
11. Enter your emergency contact's address in the "Emergency Contact Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (`5`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Select "Emergency Contact" if you have a contact person who should be notified in case of an emergency.
* **Medical History** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all conditions that apply to you.
* **Doctor Name** (`8`, `text`, required): Enter your doctor's name.
* **Doctor Phone** (`9`, `text`, required): Enter your doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Email** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter your doctor's email address.
* **Doctor Facility** (`11`, `text`, required): Enter your doctor's facility information.
* **Doctor Notes** (`12`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about your doctor.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`13`, `text`, required): Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`14`, `text`, required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`16`, `text`, required): Enter a description of your emergency contact relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`17`, `text`, required): Enter your emergency contact's address.
