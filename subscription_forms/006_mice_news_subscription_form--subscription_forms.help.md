# MICE News Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MICE News Subscription Form is designed to collect information from users to deliver news and event updates tailored to their interests. To use this form, follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred category for subscription (News or Events) from the "Subscriber Information" field.
2. Enter your Email Address in the correct format (e.g., user@example.com).
3. Enter your Phone Number in the required format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
4. Choose your Topic Preferences, selecting both News and Events if you want to be informed on both.
5. Confirm your consent by selecting the "True" option.
6. If you have additional information you'd like to provide, you can enter it in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Subscriber Information** (`subscriber_information`, select_multiple, required): Select the category you want to subscribe to (News or Events) to receive updates.
- **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., user@example.com).
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the required format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
- **Topic Preferences** (`topic_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select both News and Events if you want to be informed on both.
- **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Confirm your consent by selecting "True".
- **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill out all required fields (Subscriber Information, Email Address, Phone Number, Topic Preferences, and Consent).
- Use the correct format for your email address and phone number.
- If you're unsure about a field, review the "How To Complete This Form" section.
- Double-check your selections before submitting the form to avoid errors.
- For any issues or questions, contact our support team.
