# Simple Email Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Email Form is a basic form designed for users to send emails to the form owner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your email address in the Email Address field.
2. Type your name in the Name field.
3. Write your message in the Message field.
4. Confirm your email address in the Confirm Email field.
5. Click the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email Address** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address to be able to receive a response or further communication from the form owner.
* **Name** (`text`, required: false): Provide your name to help the form owner better understand your identity and respond accordingly.
* **Message** (`text`, required: false): Write a brief description of your purpose or reason for sending the email.
* **Confirm Email** (`email`, required: false): Confirm your email address to help the form owner ensure that it is correct and to prevent typos.
