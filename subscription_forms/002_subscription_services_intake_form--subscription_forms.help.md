# Subscription Services Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Subscription Services Intake Form is a tool used to collect information from clients about their subscription services needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Subscription Services Intake Form" to open it.
2. Enter your client's name in the "Client Name" field.
3. Select the business type from the options in the "Business Type" field.
4. Choose all the services you need from the options in the "Services Needed" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Select a start date for your subscription from the calendar.
8. Select an end date for your subscription from the calendar.
9. Enter any additional comments or information in the "Notes" field.
10. Select a contact person from the options in the "Assigned Contact" field.
11. Choose the review status of your subscription from the options in the "Review Status" field.
12. Enter the date of the review in the "Review Date" field.
13. Enter a comment about the review in the "Review Comment" field.
14. Enter the name of the person who reviewed your subscription in the "Reviewed By" field.
15. Enter the name of the person who created your subscription in the "Created By" field.
16. Enter the date when your subscription was created in the "Created At" field.
17. Enter the date when your subscription was updated in the "Updated At" field.
18. Select the status of your subscription from the options in the "Status" field.
19. Enter any information in the "Custom Field 1", "Custom Field 2", "Custom Field 3", "Custom Field 4", "Custom Field 5", and "Custom Field 6" fields as required.

* **Subscription Services Intake** (Subscription Services Intake Form, text, required/optional): This is the name of the form.

* **End Date** (End Date, date, required/optional): Select an end date for your subscription from the calendar.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Assigned Contact** (Assigned Contact, select_multiple, required/optional): Select a contact person.
* **Review Status** (Review Status, select_one, required): Choose the review status of your subscription.
* **Review Date** (Review Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Comment** (Review Comment, text, required/optional): Enter a comment about the review.
* **Reviewed By** (Reviewed By, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who reviewed your subscription.
* **Created By** (Created By, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who created your subscription.
* **Created At** (Created At, date, required/optional): Enter the date when your subscription was created.
* **Updated At** (Updated At, date, required/optional): Enter the date when your subscription was updated.
