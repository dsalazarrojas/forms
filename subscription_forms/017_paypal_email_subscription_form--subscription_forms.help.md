# paypal_email_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect subscription information for an email-based service offered by PayPal. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a successful subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully, making sure to input all required information.
- Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
- Click on the "Subscribe" button to submit your subscription request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your identification.
- **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email**: Your primary email address.
- **Phone Number**: Your primary phone number.
- **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
- **Confirm Phone Number**: Confirm your phone number to ensure accuracy.
- **Accept Terms**: Agree or disagree with the terms and conditions of the subscription.
- **Subscription Frequency**: Select one or more subscription frequencies that you are interested in (Weekly, Monthly, Yearly, Daily, Bi-Weekly, Quarterly, Semi-Monthly).
- **Confirm Subscription Frequency**: Confirm your selected subscription frequency.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your email and phone number for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns while filling out the form, please reach out to support at contact information below.
- Contact: form@gic.mx
