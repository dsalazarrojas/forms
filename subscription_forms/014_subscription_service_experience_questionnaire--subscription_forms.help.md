# Subscription Service Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather customer feedback and insights about your experience with our subscription service. Your input will help us understand how we can improve and tailor our service to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select the most relevant answer for each field.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (Customer Information, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide your name and contact information so we can follow up with you for any further discussions.
* **Which month do you subscribe to our service?** (question1, select_one, required: false): Please select the month you subscribe to our service. This will help us understand which services you are using when.
* **What service do you subscribe to?** (question2, select_one, required: false): Select the specific service you are subscribed to. This will help us understand your specific needs and preferences.
* **What is the primary reason for your subscription?** (question3, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the primary reason why you subscribed to our service. This will help us understand your motivations and expectations.
* **How often do you use our service?** (question4, select_one, required: false): Please select how often you use our service. This will help us understand your usage patterns.
* **What time of day do you typically use our service?** (question5, time, required: false): Enter the time of day you usually use our service. This will help us understand your peak usage hours.
* **When was your first subscription?** (question6, date, required: false): Enter the date you first subscribed to our service. This will help us understand your subscription history.
* **Have you ever changed your subscription service?** (question7, select_one, required: false): If you have changed your subscription service, select yes. If not, select no.
* **How likely are you to recommend our service to friends?** (question8, number, required: false): Please rate how likely you are to recommend our service to friends. The scale is from 1 (not likely at all) to 5 (very likely).
* **How satisfied are you with our service?** (question9, number, required: false): Please rate your level of satisfaction with our service. The scale is from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **What would you like to see changed or improved in our service?** (question10, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the areas you'd like to see changed or improved in our service.
* **Customer Comments** (customer_comment, note, required: false): This is an open-ended field for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Have you used other services similar to ours?** (question12, select_one, required: false): If you have used other services similar to ours, select yes. If not, select no.
* **Do you have any suggestions for our service?** (question13, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the suggestions you have for our service.
