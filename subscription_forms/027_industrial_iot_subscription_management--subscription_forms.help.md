# Industrial IoT Subscription Management - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Industrial IoT Subscription Management form is used to manage industrial IoT subscriptions for customers. It collects information about the subscription plan, account details, onboarding status, and any account changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Industrial or Service option for the "Industrial" field.
2. Choose a plan for your subscription from the "Plan" field. Options include 1 Month, 3 Months, and 6 Months.
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. List any account changes in the "Account Changes" field.
7. Select the onboarding method for your subscription from the "Onboarding" field. Options include 1 Month, 3 Months, and 6 Months.
8. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field.
9. Confirm your phone number in the "Confirm Phone" field.
10. Confirm your onboarding plan in the "Confirm Plan" field.
11. Enter the date and time of onboarding in the "Onboarded At" and "Onboarded At Time" fields.
12. Select the onboarding method used (e.g., Email, Phone, or Both) in the "Onboarded How" field.
13. Enter any notes or onboarding schedule in the "Notes" field.
14. Enter the onboarding notes text in the "Notes Text" field.
15. Enter the onboarding method used text in the "Onboarded How Text" field.
16. Enter the onboarding date and time text in the "Onboarded At Datetime Text" field.
17. Enter the onboarding date text in the "Onboarded At Date" field.
18. Enter the onboarding time text in the "Onboarded At Time Text" field.
19. Enter the onboarding datetime text in the "Onboarded At Datetime Text" field.
20. Enter the onboarding datetime text 2 in the "Onboarded At Datetime Text 2" field.
