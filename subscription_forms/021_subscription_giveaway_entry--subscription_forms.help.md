# Subscription Giveaway Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Subscription Giveaway Entry" form is for customers to enter themselves into the subscription giveaway. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and consistently.
2. Ensure that your email doesn't match any previous entry in the past 30 days.
3. Select the appropriate plan and channel for your giveaway entry.
4. Agree to the terms and conditions of the giveaway.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field. This field is not required to be filled out but it is helpful for our team to address you properly.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field. This field is not required to be filled out but it is helpful for our team to address you properly.
* **Email**: Enter your email in this format: example@example.com. The email should be a valid address.
* **Plan**: Select the plan you want to participate with. You can choose Basic, Premium, or Enterprise plan.
* **Channel**: Choose the channel where you'd like to be contacted if you win the subscription. You can select from Facebook, Twitter, or Instagram.
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions**: Agree or disagree to the terms and conditions of the giveaway. You can only select I Agree, Do Not Agree, or I Am Not Sure.

## Tips
* Ensure that the email you enter is valid and not duplicated in the past 30 days.
* Fill out the form accurately and consistently.
* Be honest in selecting the plan and channel you want.
