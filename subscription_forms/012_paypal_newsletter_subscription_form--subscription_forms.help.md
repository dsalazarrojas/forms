# paypal_newsletter_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to help users subscribe to a newsletter offered by PayPal. It allows users to provide their personal details and subscription preferences, ensuring a smooth subscription process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Choose your preferred format (Digital, Print, or Both) in the Format Preference field.
4. Select your desired subscription duration (One Month, Three Months, Six Months, or One Year) in the Subscription Duration field.
5. Choose your subscription frequency (Monthly, Bi-Monthly, Quarterly, or Bi-Quarterly) in the Subscription Frequency field.
6. Select your subscription type (Digital Subscription, Print Subscription, or Both Subscription) in the Subscription Type field.
7. Review your selections carefully and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name for the purpose of addressing you in our newsletter.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name for the purpose of addressing you in our newsletter.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication and subscription management.
* **Format Preference** (`preferred_format`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred format of subscription (Digital, Print, or Both).
* **Subscription Duration** (`subscription_duration`, select_multiple, required): Select your desired subscription duration (One Month, Three Months, Six Months, or One Year).
* **Subscription Frequency** (`subscription_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose your subscription frequency (Monthly, Bi-Monthly, Quarterly, or Bi-Quarterly).
* **Subscription Type** (`subscription_type`, select_multiple, required): Select your subscription type (Digital Subscription, Print Subscription, or Both Subscription).

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy.
* Choose carefully from the subscription options to ensure you receive the most suitable subscription package for you.
* If you're unsure about any option, feel free to contact us for assistance.
