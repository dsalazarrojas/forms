# Streaming Channel Subscription Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Streaming Channel Subscription Management Form is designed for users to manage their streaming channel subscriptions. This form provides a platform for users to update their subscription preferences, change their current plan, and receive notifications as per their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully to ensure accurate completion.
2. For each field, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section for a detailed understanding of each field's purpose and requirements.
3. Select or enter the relevant information in each field.
4. Click "Submit" to save your updated subscription settings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Plan Change** (`plan_change`, select_one, required: false):
  This field asks if you want to change your current plan. Select 'Yes' if you wish to change your plan, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Notification Choices** (`notification_choices`, select_multiple, required: false):
  This field allows you to choose how you want to receive notifications from the streaming channel. Select one or more options as per your preference.
* **Subscription Preferences** (`subscription_preferences`, note, required: false):
  In this text field, you can type any additional preferences or notes about your subscription.
* **Subscription Plan** (`subscription_plan`, text, required: false):
  Here, you can enter your desired subscription plan (e.g., monthly, yearly, etc.).
* **Subscription Status** (`subscription_status`, date, required: false):
  In this field, you can select the date when your subscription is expected to end or start.

## Tips
* Double-check each field to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section for clarification.
* Take your time to complete the form carefully, as incorrect information may affect your subscription settings.
