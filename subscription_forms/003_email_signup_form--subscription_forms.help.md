# email_signup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for email subscription, allowing users to subscribe to newsletters, leads, or campaigns by providing their email address, name, and preferred type of subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., [name@domain.com](mailto:name@domain.com)) and your name as it is written on your identification.
2. On the second page, select the type of subscription you are interested in from the options provided.
3. Read through the terms of service and agree to them by scrolling to the end of the "Terms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your valid email address in the correct format (e.g., [name@domain.com](mailto:name@domain.com)) in this text field.
* **Name** (`name`, required/optional): Enter your actual name as it appears on your identification documents, such as a driver's license or passport.
* **Company** (`company`, optional/optional): If you are representing a company, enter the name of the company here. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Type** (`type`, required/optional): Select one of the three options: Newsletter, Leads, or Campaigns, to indicate the type of subscription you are interested in.
* **Terms** (`terms`, required/optional): Read through the terms of service and agree to them by scrolling to the end of this field.
