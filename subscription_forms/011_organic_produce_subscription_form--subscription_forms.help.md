# Organic Produce Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organic Produce Subscription Form is designed to gather information from customers who want to subscribe to our organic produce delivery service. This form collects essential details such as delivery frequency, preferred produce types, and contact information to provide a personalized experience for the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Subscription Form" button to start filling out the form.
2. Choose your preferred delivery frequency from the options provided (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
3. Select the produce types you would like to receive (Fruits, Vegetables, Leafy Greens, Berries, or Herbs).
4. Enter your preferred start date for your subscription.
5. Enter your preferred end date for your subscription (if applicable).
6. Provide your contact number.
7. Enter your contact email address.
8. Add any additional notes about your subscription preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subscription Form** (`subscription_form`, text, optional): This is the main section where you can enter your subscription details. Please fill out this section with accurate and complete information.
* **Delivery Frequency** (`delivery_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred delivery frequency from the options provided (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
	+ This will determine how often you receive your organic produce delivery.
* **Produce Types** (`produce_types`, select_multiple, optional): Select the produce types you would like to receive (Fruits, Vegetables, Leafy Greens, Berries, or Herbs).
	+ This will help us tailor your produce selection to your preferences.
* **Subscription Start Date** (`subscription_start_date`, date, optional): Enter your preferred start date for your subscription.
	+ This will be the date we start delivering your organic produce.
* **Subscription End Date** (`subscription_end_date`, date, optional): Enter your preferred end date for your subscription (if applicable).
	+ This will be the date we stop delivering your organic produce.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Provide your contact number so we can reach you if needed.
	+ This is the best phone number to contact you with any questions or issues.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address so we can reach you if needed.
	+ This is the best email address to contact you with any questions or issues.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your subscription preferences.
	+ This is a good place to mention any specific requests or preferences you have.
