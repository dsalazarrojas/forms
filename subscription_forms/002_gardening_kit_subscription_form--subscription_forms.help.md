# gardening_kit_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gardening_kit_subscription_form is designed to collect information from customers who want to subscribe to a gardening kit, which typically includes a series of product shipments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the kit you wish to subscribe to by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' for each option.
2. Enter your shipping address in the 'Shipping Address' field.
3. Enter your shipping postal code in the 'Shipping Postal Code' field.
4. Provide your name in the 'Name Of Shipper' field.
5. Enter your email address in the 'Email' field.
6. Enter your phone number in the 'Phone Number' field.
7. Choose the start date for your subscription in the 'Start Date' field.
8. Select your preferred shipping frequency from the options provided in the 'Shipping Frequency' field.
9. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can enter them in the 'Notes' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subscribe To Kit**: 
  * Label: Subscribe To Kit
  * Type: Select multiple
  * This field asks if you wish to subscribe to any kits, selecting multiple options will add corresponding kits to the subscription.

* **Shipping Address**: 
  * Label: Shipping Address
  * Type: Text
  * Enter your shipping address for the kit delivery.

* **Shipping Postal Code**: 
  * Label: Shipping Postal Code
  * Type: Text
  * Enter your postal code for shipping purposes.

* **Name Of Shipper**: 
  * Label: Name Of Shipper
  * Type: Text
  * Enter your name for delivery purposes.

* **Email**: 
  * Label: Email
  * Type: Email
  * Enter your email address for communication purposes.

* **Phone Number**: 
  * Label: Phone Number
  * Type: Text
  * Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

* **Start Date**: 
  * Label: Start Date
  * Type: Date
  * Enter the start date for your subscription.

* **Shipping Frequency**: 
  * Label: Shipping Frequency
  * Type: Select one
  * Choose how frequently you wish to receive your kit shipments.

* **Notes**: 
  * Label: Notes
  * Type: Note
  * Enter any additional notes or comments for your subscription.
