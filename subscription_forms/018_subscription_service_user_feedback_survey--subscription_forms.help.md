# Subscription Service User Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback as a subscriber to our services. It's a chance for us to understand your experience and preferences. Please take a few minutes to share your thoughts with us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field as required or optional fields as you see fit. You'll need to fill out the form on the first page. The second page will only appear if you select a service in the "Service used" field and if the field is not optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (First name, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last name** (Last name, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted by us for follow-up feedback.
* **Phone number** (Phone number, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to share it with us.
* **Service used** (Service used, select_one, optional): Select which service you currently use: Monthly Payment, One-time payment, or Both.
* **Rating** (Rating, number, optional): Rate your experience on a scale from 1-5.
* **Feedback** (Feedback, text, optional): Share your thoughts about your experience with our services.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted by us for follow-up feedback. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to complete the form.
* If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up feedback, please provide your email address.
