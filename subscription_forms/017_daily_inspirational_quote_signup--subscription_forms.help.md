# Daily Inspirational Quote Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Inspirational Quote Signup form is designed to collect user information for receiving daily inspirational quotes. Users can customize their quote preferences, including time zone, quote frequency, and quote type.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields: 
   - First Name
   - Email Address
   - Time Zone
   - Quote Frequency
   - Quote Type
2. Optional fields are:
   - Last Name
   - Phone Number
   - Quote Topic (you can select "Other" if your topic is not listed)
   - Time Zone Auto-Set Preferences (if you want to auto-set your time zone)
3. For Quote Topic, select one of the provided options or choose "Other" and enter your preferred topic.
4. For Time Zone Auto-Set Preferences, select "True" if you want to auto-set your time zone, and enter your reason for selecting this option.
5. Review and submit the form.
