# Chargify Subscription - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Chargify Subscription" form is designed to sign up customers for recurring subscriptions. This form collects necessary information for subscription management, including customer details, payment information, and subscription preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  The form is divided into sections for easy navigation. Each section focuses on a specific aspect of the subscription process.
2.  Fill in your **customer name** and **customer email** for billing and account communications. These fields are required.
3.  Select the **plan selected** that suits your needs from the available options.
4.  Choose your **billing cycle** (e.g., monthly or annually) to decide when you want to be billed.
5.  Input your **payment info**. This may include a reference token or the last four digits of a credit card number.
6.  If you have a **coupon code**, you can input it here.
7.  If you're signing up on behalf of a business, you can input your **company name**.
8.  Confirm your consent to **agreed recurring billing** by selecting the appropriate option.
9.  Finally, select the **start date** for your subscription activation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **-- Subscription Sign Up --**: Title of the form, indicating its purpose.
*   *   **customer name** (`customer_name`): Full name for billing purposes. Required.
*   *   **customer email** (`customer_email`): Required for billing and account communications.
*   *   **plan selected** (`plan_selected`): Choose a subscription plan. Required.
*   *   **billing cycle** (`billing_cycle`): Select how often to bill. Required.
*   *   **payment info** (`payment_info`): Input payment information. Required.
*   *   **coupon code** (`coupon_code`): Input a discount code if you have one. Optional.
*   *   **company name** (`company_name`): Optional business account name.
*   *   **agree to recurring billing** (`agree_recurring_billing`): Consent to recurring charges. Required.
*   *   **start date** (`start_date`): Select the subscription activation date. Optional.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill in all required fields accurately for a smooth subscription process.
- Use a valid email for billing and account communications.
- If you have issues with payment, contact the billing department.
