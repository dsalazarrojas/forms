# Academic Journal Subscription Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users interested in requesting a quote for academic journal subscriptions. It helps us understand their specific needs and requirements for accessing journal content. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure we provide an accurate quote for your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through each field carefully and answer all questions thoroughly.
- Fill out the form completely, but do not worry if you're unsure about some fields. Our team will review your request and help clarify any doubts.
- Make sure to provide your contact information so we can send you the quote and communicate with you efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name** (2): Your full name as the person requesting the quote.
* **Email Address** (3): Your professional email address where we can send the quote and follow up with you.
* **Institution** (4): The university, college, or organization where you work or are affiliated with.
* **Department** (5): The academic department or division within the institution.
* **Position/Title** (6): Your role or position within the institution.
* **Journal Title** (7): The full name of the journal you're interested in subscribing to.
* **Publisher Name** (8): The name of the journal's publisher. (This field is optional, but it's helpful if you know the publisher's name.)
* **Subscription Type** (9): Select the type of subscription you're interested in:
	+ Individual subscription
	+ Institutional subscription
	+ Multi-user license
	+ Site license
	+ Consortium license
	+ Other
* **Access Requirements** (10): The number of people who need access to the journal.
* **Coverage Requirements** (11): Specify the time period of articles you need access to (e.g., current volume, past 5 years, etc.).
* **Budget Range** (12): Your approximate budget for this subscription.
* **Intended Users** (13): Indicate who will be using this journal (e.g., faculty, students, researchers, etc.).
* **Expected Usage Frequency** (14): Describe how often you anticipate using the journal:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Occasionally
	+ For specific research projects
* **Special Features Needed** (15): If you need any specific features, such as alerts, RSS feeds, or API access, mention them here.
* **Current Alternatives** (16): If you're currently using alternative resources, select one of the options:
	+ Yes, but looking for a better option
	+ Yes, considering an upgrade
	+ No, this is our first choice
	+ Evaluating multiple options
* **Decision Timeline** (17): Specify when you need to make a decision about this subscription (e.g., by a specific date).
