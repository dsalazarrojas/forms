# Newsletter Subscription Preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect your preferences for receiving newsletters on various topics. It helps our newsletter service tailor its content and delivery schedule to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose how often you'd like to receive newsletters (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly) in the "How often would you like to receive news letters" field.
2. Select the topics you're interested in by choosing one or more options from the "what news letter topics are you interested in" field (Tech, Sports, Entertainment, Business).
3. Provide your email address in the "email address" field.
4. Enter your name in the "name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### How often would you like to receive news letters
* **How often would you like to receive news letters** (`newsletter_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how often you'd like to receive newsletters. Select one or more options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly.
### what news letter topics are you interested in
* **what news letter topics are you interested in** (`newsletter_topics`, select_one, optional): Select the topics that interest you. Choose one option: Tech, Sports, Entertainment, Business.
### email address
* **email address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Provide your email address where we can send you newsletters.
### name
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name for our records.
