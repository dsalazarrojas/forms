# Gardening Supply Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers who want to subscribe to the gardening supply service, allowing them to choose their preferred delivery frequency and type of subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **customer name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **email address** in the format "your_email@example.com".
3. Enter your **phone number** in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX".
4. Choose the **subscription start date** for when you'd like to start receiving your gardening supply deliveries.
5. Choose the **subscription end date** for when you'd like your subscription to end.
6. Select how often you'd like your **deliveries to be delivered**:
	* Every week
	* Every 2 weeks
	* Every 3 weeks
	* Every month
7. If you have any specific notes or comments about your delivery frequency, you can enter them in the **delivery frequency note** field.
8. Choose the **type of subscription** you'd like:
	* One-time
	* Monthly
	* Bi-monthly
	* Quarterly
9. Select the **payment method** you'd like to use for your subscription:
	* Cash
	* PayPal
	* Credit card
	* Bank transfer
10. If you have any specific notes or comments about your payment method, you can enter them in the **payment method note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **email**: Enter your email address in the format "your_email@example.com". This will be used for communication and account management purposes.
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX".
* **subscription_start_date**: Choose the start date for your subscription. This is the date when you'd like to start receiving your gardening supply deliveries.
* **subscription_end_date**: Choose the end date for your subscription. If you don't select an end date, your subscription will continue indefinitely.
* **delivery_frequency**: Choose how often you'd like to receive your gardening supply deliveries.
* **delivery_frequency_note**: If you have any specific notes or comments about your delivery frequency, you can enter them here.
* **subscription_type**: Choose the type of subscription you'd like:
	+ One-time: A single delivery of gardening supplies.
	+ Monthly: A monthly delivery of gardening supplies.
	+ Bi-monthly: A bi-monthly delivery of gardening supplies.
	+ Quarterly: A quarterly delivery of gardening supplies.
* **payment_method**: Choose the payment method you'd like to use for your subscription:
	+ Cash: Pay for your subscription with cash upon delivery.
	+ PayPal: Pay for your subscription using PayPal.
	+ Credit card: Pay for your subscription using your credit card.
	+ Bank transfer: Pay for your subscription using a bank transfer.
* **payment_method_note**: If you have any specific notes or comments about your payment method, you can enter them here.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer support team.
* Review the provided options carefully and choose the one that best suits your needs.
