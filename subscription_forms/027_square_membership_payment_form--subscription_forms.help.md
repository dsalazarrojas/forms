# Square Membership Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for subscription-based membership payments through Square. It is intended for use in situations where users are signing up for a membership program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your ID or documentation.
3. Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications.
4. Enter your phone number for further communication.
5. Enter your card details, including card number, expiration month, and expiration year.
6. Enter your CVV (Card Verification Value) for added security.
7. Enter the payment amount you wish to pay.
8. Select the membership ID you have been assigned or choose from the available options.
9. Review your entries carefully and select the 'Submit' option to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, required/optional): Select your preferred payment method from the available options.
* **Name** (name, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or documentation.
* **Email** (email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Card Number** (card_number, number, required/optional): Enter your card number for payment processing.
* **Exp. Month** (exp_month, select_one, required/optional): Select the expiration month of your card.
* **Exp. Year** (exp_year, select_one, required/optional): Select the expiration year of your card.
* **CVV** (cvv, select_one, required/optional): Enter your CVV for added security.
* **Payment Amount** (payment_amount, number, required/optional): Enter the amount you wish to pay.
* **Membership Id** (membership_id, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the membership ID you have been assigned or choose from the available options.
* **Submit** (submit, select_one, required/optional): Review your entries carefully and select the 'Submit' option to complete the form.

## Tips
* Ensure you select the correct payment method and membership ID to avoid any issues with payment processing.
* Enter your name, email, and phone number accurately to ensure smooth communication.
* Enter your card details carefully, including card number, expiration month, and year, to avoid any payment processing issues.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
