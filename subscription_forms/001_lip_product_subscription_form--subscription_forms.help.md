# Lip Product Subscription Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers interested in subscribing to a lip product. The form is designed to gather essential details such as name, contact information, and subscription preferences to facilitate a smooth subscription process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email in the "Email" field. Please make sure it's a valid email address.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your desired subscription plan (Monthly, Quarterly, or Bi-Annually) in the "Subscription Plan" field.
6. If applicable, choose a specific Lip Product from the list.
7. Choose your desired Order Frequency (if available) in the "Order Frequency" field.
8. If you have any additional information or requests, type them in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as per your preference.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as per your preference.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Subscription Plan** (`subscription_plan`, select_one, required: false): Select your desired subscription plan (Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-Annually).
* **Subscription Date** (`subscription_date`, date, required: false): This field is not currently applicable.
* **Lip Product** (`lip_product`, text, required: true): Select a specific lip product you are interested in.
* **Order Frequency** (`order_frequency`, time, required: false): Choose how often you'd like to receive your subscription (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Share any additional information or requests you have.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any comments or feedback you have.
