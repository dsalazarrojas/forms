# r_d_program_subscription_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The r_d_program_subscription_form is used to collect information from users for internal subscription purposes. This form is intended for users within a specific organization or business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the **first_name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **last_name** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field.
5. Select your subscription date in the **date** field.
6. Choose your subscription time in the **time** field.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the **note** field.
8. Enter your organization or department in the **organization** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name**: Enter your first name. This is a required field.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name. This is a required field.
* **email**: Enter your email address. This is a required field.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number. This is an optional field.
* **date**: Select the date of your subscription. This is an optional field.
* **time**: Choose the time of your subscription. This is an optional field.
* **note**: Add any additional comments or notes. This is an optional field.
* **organization**: Enter your organization or department. This is an optional field.
