# Streaming Service Membership Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enroll a subscriber to a streaming service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form by providing the required information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subscriber Name (1)**: Enter the name of the subscriber.
* **Email (3)**: Enter the subscriber's email address.
* **Plan Selection (4)**: Select the desired plan from the list.
* **Plan Details (5)**: Provide any additional details about the selected plan.
* **Subscription Plan (6)**: Confirm the selected plan.
* **Payment Method (7)**: Select the payment method for the subscription.
* **Payment Method Details (8)**: Provide any additional details about the payment method.
* **Billing Cycle (9)**: Confirm the billing cycle.
* **Billing Date (10)**: Enter the date of the billing cycle.
* **Payment Method Type (11)**: Confirm the type of payment method selected.
* **Card Number (12)**: Enter the card number for the payment method.
* **Card Expiration (13)**: Enter the expiration date of the card.
* **CVV (14)**: Enter the CVV (Card Verification Value) for the card.
* **Subscriber Phone (15)**: Enter the subscriber's phone number.
* **Subscriber Address (16)**: Enter the subscriber's address.
* **Subscriber City (17)**: Enter the subscriber's city.
* **Subscriber State (18)**: Enter the subscriber's state or region.
* **Subscriber Country (19)**: Enter the subscriber's country.
* **Subscriber Zip (20)**: Enter the subscriber's zip code.
* **Form End (21)**: Confirm that you have completed the form.
* **Confirm Email (22)**: Confirm the email address of the subscriber.
* **Confirm Payment Method (23)**: Confirm the payment method selected.
* **Confirm Payment Method Type (24)**: Confirm the type of payment method selected.
* **Confirm Plan Selection (25)**: Confirm the selected plan.
