# Gardening Service Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gardening Service Subscription Form is a tool used to collect essential information from clients for our gardening services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the subscription form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Enter your address for delivery and service scheduling.
4. Specify your start date for our services to begin.
5. If applicable, enter your end date for our services to conclude.
6. Add additional service details for our staff to prepare accordingly.
7. Finally, select the type of service you are interested in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required*): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required*): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required*): Enter your email address where we can contact you for updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required*): Enter your phone number where we can contact you for scheduling and updates.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required*): Enter your address for our staff to visit and provide our services.
* **Start date** (`start_date`, `date`, required*): Enter the date when you would like our services to begin.
* **End date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): If you have a specific end date, enter it here. Otherwise, we will continue to service you until further notice.
* **Service details** (`service_details`, `text`, required*): Provide any additional information about the service you require, such as specific plants or services needed.
* **Service type** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required*): Select the type of service you are interested in from the available options.
