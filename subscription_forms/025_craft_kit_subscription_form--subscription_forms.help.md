**craft_kit_subscription_form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The **craft_kit_subscription_form** is a comprehensive form designed to capture essential details for your craft kit subscription. This form ensures that you provide accurate and relevant information for your subscription to be successfully processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the **craft_kit_subscription_form**, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** (page 1, field 1).
2. Provide your **Last Name** (page 1, field 2).
3. Enter your **Email** (page 1, field 3).
4. Confirm your **Email** (page 1, field 4).
5. Enter your **Phone Number** (page 2, field 5).
6. Confirm your **Phone Number** (page 2, field 6).
7. Enter your **Address Line 1** (page 3, field 7).
8. Enter your **Address Line 2** (page 3, field 8).
9. Provide your **City** (page 3, field 9).
10. Provide your **State/Province** (page 3, field 10).
11. Enter your **Postal Code** (page 3, field 11).
12. Select your preferred **Payment Method** (page 4, field 12).
13. Choose your preferred **Payment Cycle** (page 4, field 13).
14. Choose your preferred **Subscription Cycle** (page 4, field 14).
15. Select your preferred **Payment Frequency** (page 5, field 17).
16. Choose your **Subscription Status** (page 5, field 18).
17. Enter the **Payment Amount** (page 5, field 19).
18. Choose your **Payment Interval** (page 5, field 20).
19. Add any additional **Notes** (page 6, field 21).
20. Confirm your **Email Subscription** (page 6, field 22).
21. Confirm your **Phone Subscription** (page 6, field 23).
22. Review and sign the **Billing Agreement** (page 7, field 24).
23. Review and acknowledge the **Terms and Conditions** (page 7, field 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (page 1, field 1) (`text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (page 1, field 2) (`text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (page 1, field 3) (`email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Confirm Email** (page 1, field 4) (`email`, required): Re-enter your email address to confirm it.
* **Phone Number** (page 2, field 5) (`text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Phone Number** (page 2, field 6) (`text`, required): Re-enter your phone number to confirm it.
* **Address Line 1** (page 3, field 7) (`text`, required): Enter your address line 1.
* **Address Line 2** (page 3, field 8) (`text`, required): Enter your address line 2.
* **City** (page 3, field 9) (`text`, required): Enter your city.
* **State/Province** (page 3, field 10) (`text`, required): Enter your state or province.
* **Postal Code** (page 3, field 11) (`text`, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Payment Method** (page 4, field 12) (`select_one`, required): Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Check).
* **Payment Cycle** (page 4, field 13) (`select_one`, required): Choose your preferred payment cycle (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Subscription Cycle** (page 4, field 14) (`select_one`, required): Choose your preferred subscription cycle (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Payment Frequency** (page 5, field 17) (`select_one`, required): Select your preferred payment frequency (Every 1 Month, Every 3 Months, Every 6 Months, or Every 12 Months).
* **Subscription Status** (page 5, field 18) (`select_one`, required): Choose your subscription status (Active or Inactive).
* **Payment Amount** (page 5, field 19) (`number`, required): Enter your payment amount.
* **Payment Interval** (page 5, field 20) (`select_one`, required): Choose your preferred payment interval (Once, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Notes** (page 6, field 21) (`note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email Subscription Confirmation** (page 6, field 22) (`text`, optional): Confirm your email subscription.
* **Phone Subscription Confirmation** (page 6, field 23) (`text`, optional): Confirm your phone subscription.
* **Billing Agreement** (page 7, field 24) (`text`, required): Review and sign the billing agreement.
* **Terms and Conditions** (page 7, field 25) (`text`, required): Review and acknowledge the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and completely.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
