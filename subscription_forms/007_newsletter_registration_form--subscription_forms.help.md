# Newsletter Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users interested in subscribing to a newsletter. By filling out this form, users can register for the newsletter and receive regular updates on various topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required fields:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Confirm Email
	* Subscribe ( select 'Yes' to subscribe to the newsletter)
2. Optionally, provide a Message if you want to communicate with us.
3. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the international format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, text, required): Re-enter your email address to confirm it is correct.
* **Subscribe** (`subscribe`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' to subscribe to the newsletter and receive regular updates.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): If you have any comments or questions, provide them here.
