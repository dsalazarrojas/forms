# Chargify Magazine Subscription - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect subscription details and billing information from users who wish to subscribe to the Chargify Magazine. Please note that the form is specific to the Chargify Magazine subscription process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **subscriber name** in the field labeled "subscriber name".
2. Provide your **delivery address** in the field labeled "delivery address", including the street address, city, and postal code.
3. Enter your **email address** in the field labeled "email", which will be used for digital editions and billing purposes.
4. Select your chosen **subscription tier** from the options provided (Basic, Standard, or Premium).
5. Choose your desired **billing frequency** from the options provided (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual).
6. Enter your preferred **payment method** in the field labeled "payment method".
7. If you have a **promo code**, you can enter it in the field labeled "promo code".
8. Review and confirm that you **agree to terms** by selecting the correct option.
9. If applicable, select your desired **start date** for your subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subscriber Name**: Enter your full name for delivery purposes.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter your street address, city, and postal code for delivery purposes.
* **Email**: Enter the email address you wish to use for digital editions and billing purposes.
* **Subscription Tier**: Select the subscription level that suits your needs (Basic, Standard, or Premium).
* **Billing Frequency**: Choose the billing interval that works best for you (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual).
* **Payment Method**: Enter your preferred payment method, which will be used for billing purposes.
* **Promo Code**: Enter any promotional code you have, if applicable, to receive discounts or special offers.
* **Agree to Terms**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the subscription terms and conditions.
* **Start Date**: If you have a preferred start date for your subscription, enter it here. Note that this field is optional.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to avoid any delays or issues with your subscription.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
