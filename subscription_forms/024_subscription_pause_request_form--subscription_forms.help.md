# Subscription Pause Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Subscription Pause Request Form is designed to allow customers to request a temporary pause for their subscription services. This form helps ensure that all valid reasons for pausing a subscription are properly documented to prevent abuse and maintain a fair and transparent process for requesting pauses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly to ensure that your request is properly assessed.
2. Ensure that you have a valid reason for requesting a pause, such as testing, moving, or financial difficulties.
3. Select the correct subscription type from the options provided.
4. Enter the pause reason and provide any additional notes if needed.
5. If applicable, select the pause duration and start and end dates for the pause.
6. Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication and verification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subscription Type**: Select the type of subscription you have with us, such as Monthly, Annually, or Bi-Annually. This helps us understand which services you are requesting to pause.
* **Pause Reason**: Choose one or more valid reasons for pausing your subscription, such as Testing, Moving, or Financial. This is a critical field that helps us assess your request and ensure you have a legitimate reason for pausing your subscription.
* **Pause Reason Note**: Provide additional details about your pause reason, such as any context or specifics that might help us understand your situation. This field is optional but recommended for providing clarity.
* **Pause Duration**: Select the duration of the pause you are requesting. If applicable, enter a specific date for the start and end of the pause.
* **Pause Start Date** and **Pause End Date**: Enter the dates for the pause start and end, if applicable.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication and verification purposes. We may reach out to you with questions or to discuss your request.

## Tips
* Be honest and transparent in your submission.
* Ensure that you have a valid reason for requesting a pause.
* Keep the form up to date with any changes to your pause reason or duration.
* We may contact you for further clarification or to confirm your request.
* Your subscription pause request will be reviewed and processed in accordance with our company's policies and procedures.
