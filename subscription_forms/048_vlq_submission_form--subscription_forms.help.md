# VLQ Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The VLQ Submission Form is designed to collect data for a VLQ program or assessment. This form gathers personal details, participation information, and agreements from participants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as such (e.g., participant_name, participant_email, participant_phone, participant_reason_for_participation, participant_vlq_level, participant_vlq_group, participant_vlq_comments, and participant_vlq_files).
2. For fields with options, select the relevant choice that best describes the participant's information.
3. If a field is not required, you may leave it blank or provide additional information if necessary.
4. Double-check your submission before finalizing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_name** (Required, text): Enter the participant's name.
* **participantEmail** (Required, email): Enter the participant's email address.
* **participantPhone** (Required, text): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **participantDateOfBirth** (Optional, date): Enter the participant's date of birth, if applicable.
* **participantDateOfDeath** (Optional, date): Enter the participant's date of death, if applicable.
* **participantVlqStartDate** (Optional, date): Enter the participant's start date in the VLQ program, if applicable.
* **participantVlqEndDate** (Optional, date): Enter the participant's end date in the VLQ program, if applicable.
* **participantReasonForParticipation** (Required, text): Describe the reason for the participant's participation in the VLQ program.
* **participantVlqLevel** (Required, select_one): Select the participant's level of participation.
* **participantVlqGroup** (Required, select_one): Select the participant's group.
* **participantVlqComments** (Required, text): Provide comments about the participant's experience in the VLQ program.
* **participantVlqFiles** (Required, select_multiple): Select any files related to the participant's participation.
* **participantOtherInfo** (Optional, text): Enter any other information about the participant.
* **participantOtherInfoOther** (Optional, text): Enter additional information about the participant, if necessary.
* **participantOtherFiles** (Optional, select_multiple): Select any other files related to the participant.
* **participantOtherFilesOther** (Optional, select_multiple): Select additional files related to the participant, if necessary.
* **participantVlqAgreement** (Required, select_one): Confirm the participant's agreement to participate in the VLQ program.
* **participantVlqAgreementOther** (Optional, select_one): Confirm the participant's agreement to other terms, if applicable.
* **participantVlqAgreementOtherInfo** (Optional, text): Provide additional information about the participant's agreement, if necessary.
* **participantVlqAgreementOtherInfoOther** (Optional, text): Enter additional information about the participant's agreement, if necessary.
* **participantVlqAgreementOtherOther** (Optional, text): Enter additional information about the participant's agreement, if necessary.
* **participantVlqAgreementOtherOtherInfo** (Optional, text): Enter additional information about the participant's agreement, if necessary.
* **participantVlqAgreementOtherOtherInfoOther** (Optional, text): Enter additional information about the participant's agreement, if necessary.

## Tips

* Please fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
* Use clear and concise language when providing information.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult the participant or the relevant documentation.
* Double-check your submission before finalizing.
