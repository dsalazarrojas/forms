# Online Subscription Sample Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for a sample order for an online subscription service. It is for testing and demo purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Customer Details` section with your name and contact information.
2. In the `Order Details` section, describe the type of order you would like to place.
3. Select the `Order Options` you would like to include in your order.
4. Enter your `Email Address` in the format `name@example.com`.
5. Add your `Phone Number` for our records.
6. If you have any additional comments, please type them in the `Order Comments` section.
7. If you have any special requests, please add them in the `Customer Notes` section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details (Text)** (1, Required: No): Enter your name and contact information in this section.
* **Order Details (Text)** (2, Required: No): Describe the type of order you would like to place, e.g., "Monthly subscription to Premium Plan".
* **Order Options (Select Multiple)** (3, Required: No): Select the options you would like to include in your order, e.g., "Delivery to home" and "Gift wrapping".
* **Email Address (Email)** (4, Required: No): Enter your email address in the format `name@example.com`.
* **Phone Number (Text)** (5, Required: No): Enter your phone number for our records.
* **Order Comments (Note)** (6, Required: No): If you have any comments or additional information, please type them in this section.
* **Customer Notes (Note)** (7, Required: No): If you have any special requests or notes, please add them here.
