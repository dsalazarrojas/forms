# virtual-office-subscription-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for businesses and individuals seeking to establish a virtual office presence. It's used to collect subscription information for a virtual office service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the text field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the format of a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Company Name** in the text field.
5. Select your **Business Type** from the dropdown menu.
6. Select your **Service Plan** from the list of options. You can select multiple plans.
7. Enter your **Start Date** in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
8. Enter your **End Date** in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
9. Select your **Time Zone** from the dropdown menu.
10. Select your **Payment Plan** from the list of options. You can select multiple plans.

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.

* **Service Plan** (`service_plan`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more service plans.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of your subscription in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of your subscription in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, select_one, required: false): Select your time zone.
* **Payment Plan** (`payment_plan`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more payment plans.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address for **Email**.
* Select only the service and payment plans that apply to your virtual office subscription.
* Ensure that the start and end dates are in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
