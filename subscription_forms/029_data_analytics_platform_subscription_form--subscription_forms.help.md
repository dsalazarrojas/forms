# Data Analytics Platform Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for subscribing to the Data Analytics Platform. It requires some basic user information and subscription details to complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2.  Enter your Email address in the Email field. Make sure to enter a valid email address.
3.  Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
4.  Select the subscription Plan you are interested in. You can choose from Free, Premium, and Enterprise plans.
5.  Choose your preferred Payment Method. You can select one or more methods (credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer).
6.  Select the Subscription Period for your subscription. You can choose from Month-to-Month, Annual, or Bi-Annual.
7.  Enter any additional Notes about your subscription in the Notes field.
8.  Read and accept the Agreement by checking the Agreement checkbox (you need to acknowledge that you understand the subscription agreement).
9.  Confirm that you have understood and accepted the Agreement by selecting "Yes" next to the Agreement Acknowledgement field.
10. Enter any specific Payment Terms in the Payment Terms field.
11. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click "Submit" to complete your subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Enter your first name here.
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name here.
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Plan**: Choose a subscription plan that suits your needs: Free, Premium, or Enterprise.
*   **Payment Method**: Select one or more payment methods: credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer.
*   **Subscription Period**: Choose the frequency of your subscription: Month-to-Month, Annual, or Bi-Annual.
*   **Notes**: Enter any additional information about your subscription here.
*   **Agreement**: Review and accept the terms and conditions of the subscription by checking this checkbox.
*   **Agreement Acknowledgement**: Confirm that you have understood and accepted the agreement by selecting "Yes".
*   **Payment Terms**: Enter any specific payment terms or conditions here.
