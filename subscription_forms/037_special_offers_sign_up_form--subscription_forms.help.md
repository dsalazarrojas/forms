# special_offers_sign_up_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user's information and preferences for potential future interactions with the system. It's intended to gather details such as name, email, phone, and preferences for special offers and newsletter integration. By filling out this form, users can sign up for special offers and stay updated on the latest news and promotions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your E-mail address in the E-mail field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Choose your preference for Special Offers by selecting "Yes" or "No".
5. Select whether you want to integrate with our Newsletter by choosing "Yes" or "No".
6. Read and agree to our Terms and Conditions.
7. Click the Sign Up button to complete the registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Special Offers** (`special_offers`, select_one, required/optional): Choose "Yes" to receive special offers and promotions.
* **Newsletter Integration** (`newsletter_integration`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" to integrate with our newsletter system.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required/optional): Please read and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Sign Up** (`sign_up_button`, text, required/optional): Click this button to complete the registration and submit your information.
