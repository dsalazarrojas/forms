# home_cleaning_service_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "home_cleaning_service_subscription_form" is a tool for users to subscribe to a home cleaning service. It collects relevant information for service providers to tailor their services to individual needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **customer_name** field. This is the name of the person subscribing to the service.
2. Choose your **subscription_plan** by selecting one or more options from the dropdown menu. This will inform the service provider of your cleaning needs.
3. Select your **preferred_schedule** for the cleaning service to be provided. This will help the service provider determine the best schedule for your needs.
4. Enter the **start_date** for when you would like the cleaning service to begin.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the person subscribing to the service.
* **subscription_plan** (select multiple, required: false): Choose one or more options from the dropdown menu to inform the service provider of your cleaning needs. Options are: 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **preferred_schedule** (date, required: false): Select a date for the cleaning service to be provided.
* **start_date** (date, required: false): Enter the date when you would like the cleaning service to begin.

## Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult with the service provider for guidance.
- Ensure all fields are completed to the best of your abilities, as incomplete information may lead to delays or errors.
