# legal_support_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The legal_support_subscription_form is used to collect user information for legal support purposes. It is part of the onboarding process for new users and is meant to be filled out by the user prior to the activation of their account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your User Information in the "User Information" field.
3. Enter your Contact Details in the "Contact Details" field.
4. Enter your Subscription Details in the "Subscription Details" field.
5. Enter your Billing Information in the "Billing Information" field.
6. Enter your Payment Information in the "Payment Information" field.
7. Review and accept the agreement in the "Agreement" field.
8. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
9. Confirm your information by selecting "True" in the "Confirm" field.
10. Enter any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you regarding your subscription.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your subscription (this field is not required but is recommended).
* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant user information such as name, username, or other details.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any contact details such as address or other contact information.
* **Subscription Details** (`subscription_details`, `text`, optional): Enter details about your subscription such as subscription type or other relevant information.
* **Billing Information** (`billing_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your billing information such as billing address or other relevant details.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your payment information such as payment method or other relevant details.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `text`, optional): Review and accept the agreement terms regarding your subscription.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your subscription.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm your information by selecting "True".
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your subscription.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have entered accurate and up-to-date information.
* Please review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
