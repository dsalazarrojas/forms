# paypal_meal_kit_delivery_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in subscribing to a meal kit delivery service. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details from customers to facilitate a smooth subscription process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field. Please ensure it is a valid 10-digit number.
5. Select your preferred Meal Frequency from the options provided.
6. Choose your preferred Payment Method from the available options.
7. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
8. Enter your City in the "City" field.
9. Enter your State in the "State" field.
10. Enter your Zip Code in the "Zip Code" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you use to receive communications from us.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter a valid 10-digit phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Meal Frequency** (`meal_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how often you would like to receive meal kit deliveries:
	+ Meal Frequency Daily
	+ Meal Frequency 2-3 days
	+ Meal Frequency Weekly
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the payment method you prefer:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Apple Pay
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your full address where meal kits can be delivered.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you would like to receive meal kits.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where you would like to receive meal kits.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter the zip code where you would like to receive meal kits.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled correctly to avoid any issues with your subscription.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
