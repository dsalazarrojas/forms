# Digital Health App Subscription Cancellation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide users through the process of cancelling their subscription with the digital health app. Please follow the instructions below to complete the cancellation request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), to provide necessary information for the cancellation process.
2. Review the fields below to ensure you have completed all required fields before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, \*): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, \*): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address associated with your subscription.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason`, note, \*): Enter the reason for your subscription cancellation.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, \*): Select one of the two options to confirm cancellation.
* **Date of Cancellation** (`date_of_cancellation`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to cancel your subscription. 
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, select_multiple, \*): Select one of the two options to confirm your cancellation request.
* **Cancellation Details** (`cancellation_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your cancellation.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback about your cancellation.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Enter your signature for verification purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Verify that your email address is valid and up-to-date.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
