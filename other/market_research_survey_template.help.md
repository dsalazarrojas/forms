# market_research_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on your experience with the market research survey. Your input will help us understand what works well and what can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer the question "How satisfied are you with your experience completing this Market Research Survey?" by selecting one of the options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
2. Choose all the aspects that influenced your overall opinion of this survey from the list: Question clarity, Survey length, Topic relevance, Design/layout, Ease of completion, or Technical performance.
3. Answer the question "Would you recommend participating in a similar market research survey to others?" by selecting True, False, or Maybe.
4. If you have any suggestions or improvements for the survey, type them in the text box "What would you improve or change about this Market Research Survey?"
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback, type them in the text box "Do you have any additional comments or feedback about this survey?"

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with your experience completing this Market Research Survey?** (`Very satisfied`, `text`, required):
	+ This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with the survey.
* **Which aspects most influenced your opinion of this survey?** (`options`, `multiple`, required):
	+ Select all that apply from the list: Question clarity, Survey length, Topic relevance, Design/layout, Ease of completion, or Technical performance.
* **Would you recommend participating in a similar market research survey to others?** (`True`, `false`, `Maybe`, required):
	+ Please answer with your honest opinion.
* **What would you improve or change about this Market Research Survey?** (`text`, optional):
	+ If you have any suggestions or improvements, type them in here.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback about this survey?** (`text`, optional):
	+ Share any additional thoughts or concerns you have about the survey.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and provide as much detail as possible in your feedback.
- Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
