# Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey is designed to collect information from end-users on their climate policy awareness, attitudes, and participation. This survey aims to gather valuable insights to improve climate policy initiatives for youth engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Provide honest and accurate responses to each question.
3. Complete all required fields (if any).
4. Submit your form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Climate policy awareness** (`climate_policy_awareness`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your current level of awareness about climate policy.
* **Attitudes towards climate policy** (`climate_policy_attitudes`, text, optional): Here, you can express your thoughts and feelings about climate policy.
* **Climate policy participation** (`climate_policy_participation`, text, optional): Share any experiences or involvement you have had with climate policy-related activities or initiatives.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Please choose one category that best describes your current affiliation or role:
	+ NGOs (Non-Governmental Organizations)
	+ Schools
	+ Advocacy groups
* **Climate policy knowledge** (`climate_policy_knowledge`, text, optional): If you have any notable knowledge or experience related to climate policy, please describe it here.
* **Climate policy solutions** (`climate_policy_solutions`, text, optional): Share any ideas or suggestions you have for effective climate policy solutions.
* **Government role in climate policy** (`climate_policy_government_role`, text, optional): Describe your understanding of the government's role in climate policy.
* **Climate policy participation history** (`climate_policy_participation_history`, text, optional): Share any past experiences or involvement you have had with climate policy-related activities.
* **Climate policy application** (`climate_policy_application`, text, optional): If you have applied climate policy principles in your work or personal life, please describe how and why.
* **Confidence in applying climate policy principles** (`confidence`, number, optional): If applicable, rate your confidence level in applying climate policy principles on a scale of 1-10.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* Your responses will help improve climate policy initiatives for youth engagement, so your input is valuable!
