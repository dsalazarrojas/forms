# happiness_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The happiness_survey_template form is designed to help understand overall happiness levels and the factors that contribute to it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your understanding.
2. For the "Overall, how would you rate your current level of happiness?" question, select one of the five options provided.
3. For the "How often do you engage in activities that bring you joy?" question, select one of the four options provided.
4. In the "Which of the following contribute most to your happiness?" question, select all that apply from the listed options.
5. For the "What is one thing you would change to improve your overall happiness?" question, enter a brief written response.
6. In the "Do you have any additional thoughts on well-being or happiness?" question, enter any thoughts or comments you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall, how would you rate your current level of happiness?** (`Very happy`, `Somewhat happy`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat unhappy`, `Very unhappy`): This question asks you to rate your current level of happiness on a scale of very happy to very unhappy.
* **How often do you engage in activities that bring you joy?** (`Daily`, `Weekly`, `Monthly`, `Rarely`, `Never`): This question inquires about how frequently you participate in activities that bring you happiness and joy.
* **Which of the following contribute most to your happiness? (select all that apply)** (`Family/Friends`, `Work/Career`, `Hobbies`, `Health`, `Personal growth`, `Financial stability`): This question asks you to choose all the factors that contribute most to your happiness from the provided list.
* **What is one thing you would change to improve your overall happiness?**: This question invites you to think of one thing that could improve your happiness and describe it briefly.
* **Do you have any additional thoughts on well-being or happiness?**: This question allows you to share any additional thoughts or comments you may have about well-being or happiness.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and to the best of your understanding.
* You can select multiple options for the "Which of the following contribute most to your happiness?" question, as all that apply.
* You can type freely in the "What is one thing you would change to improve your overall happiness?" question, with no character limit.
* You can enter as many thoughts or comments as you like in the "Do you have any additional thoughts on well-being or happiness?" question.
