# cpap_intolerance_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cpap_intolerance_assessment_form is used to collect information about a patient's cpap usage and any associated intolerance issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name.
2. Enter the details of your cpap usage.
3. Indicate your tolerance or intolerance frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Cpap Usage** (`cpap_usage`, `text`, required: false): Enter details about your cpap usage.
* **Intolerance Frequency** (`intolrence_frequency`, `text`, required: false): Indicate how frequently you experience cpap-related intolerance issues.
