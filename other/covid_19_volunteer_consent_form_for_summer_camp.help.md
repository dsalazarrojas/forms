# untitled_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for summer camps, including details about the event, individual contact information, and terms and agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the details about the summer camp in the **Summer Camp Details** field.
2. Provide your **Name** in the text field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the field.
4. Choose either your email or phone contact option from the **Email or Phone** field.
5. Enter your **Address** in the text field.
6. Select the **Volunteer Role** you are assigned or have a interest in.
7. If necessary, choose any or all of the terms and agreements from the **Terms and Agreement** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Summer Camp Details (Page 1)**: Enter a brief description of the summer camp event or program.
* **Name (Page 1)**: Enter your full name.
* **Email (Page 1)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Email or Phone (Page 1)**: Select either your email or phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address (Page 1)**: Enter your mailing address.
* **E-Signature (Page 1)**: Note: This field is not necessary given it's an optional field, but enter your name and date if required for your organization. 
* **Date Signed (Page 1)**: Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Date Completed (Page 1)**: Enter the date when you complete this form.
* **Volunteer Role (Page 1)**: Choose your role in the summer camp.
* **Terms and Agreement (Page 1)**: Select any or all of the terms and agreements that apply to you.
