# demographic_survey_questionnaire_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The demographic survey questionnaire template is designed to collect demographic information from respondents to better understand their characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your age group from the options listed.
2. Choose your gender identity from the available options.
3. Select your highest level of education completed.
4. Choose your current employment status.
5. Select the continent that best describes your primary residence.
6. Choose your approximate annual household income from the listed options.
7. Select all the racial/ethnic backgrounds that apply to you.
8. Optionally, provide any additional demographic information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **What is your age group?**
Age Group (Under 18, 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55+)

* This question helps us understand the age distribution of our audience.

### **How do you describe your gender identity?**
Gender Identity (Woman, Man, Non-binary, Prefer not to say, Other)

* This question is to help us understand the diversity of our audience's gender identities.

### **What is your highest level of education completed?**
Education Level (High School, Associate's Degree, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, Doctorate, Other)

* This question will help us understand the educational background of our audience.

### **What is your current employment status?**
Employment Status (Employed Full-time, Employed Part-time, Self-employed, Student, Unemployed, Retired)

* This question helps us understand the employment status of our audience.

### **Which continent best describes your primary residence?**
Primary Residence (Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, South America, Oceania)

* This question helps us understand the geographic distribution of our audience.

### **What is your approximate annual household income?**
Annual Household Income (Under $25, $0, $25,000 - $49,999, $50,000 - $74,999, $75,000 - $99,999, $100,000+ or Prefer not to say)

* This question helps us understand the income level of our audience.

### **Which of the following best describes your racial/ethnic background? (Select all that apply)**
Racial/Ethnic Background (White, Black or African American, Asian, Hispanic or Latino, Indigenous Peoples, Other)

* This question helps us understand the racial/ethnic diversity of our audience.

### **Do you have any additional demographic information you'd like to share?**
Additional Demographic Information

* This question is an open-ended field for you to provide any additional demographic information you'd like to share with us. Please use this field to provide details that are not listed in the previous questions.
