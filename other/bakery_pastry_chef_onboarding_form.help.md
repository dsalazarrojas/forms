# Untitled Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about new hires for HR or administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please provide accurate and clear information in each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can contact the relevant department or team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Details**: This field is for entering general information about the new hire.
* **Name**: Enter the full name of the new hire.
* **Email**: Enter the new hire's email address.
* **Address**: Enter the new hire's address.
* **Phone**: Enter the new hire's phone number.
* **First Day**: Choose the first day of work for the new hire.
* **Job Title**: Select the job title of the new hire.
* **Position**: Select the position of the new hire.
* **Experience**: Enter the number of years of experience the new hire has.
* **Availability**: Enter the new hire's availability in a 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00-17:00).
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and phone number of the new hire's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Address**: Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **References**: This field is for entering notes about the new hire's references.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the new hire.
* **Notes**: This field is for entering additional notes about the new hire.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Reviewed By**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the new hire.
* **Status**: Select the status of the review (Approved, Rejected, Pending, In Progress).
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the new hire.
* **Onboarding Completed**: Select whether the onboarding process is completed or not.
* **First Day Notes**: Enter any notes about the new hire's first day.
* **Last Day Notes**: Enter any notes about the new hire's last day.
* **Next Steps**: Enter any next steps or actions to be taken for the new hire.
