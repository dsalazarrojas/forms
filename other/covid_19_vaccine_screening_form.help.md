# COVID 19 Vaccine Screening Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide explains the purpose, completion, and each field of the COVID-19 Vaccine Screening Form.

## How To Complete This Form

Before completing this form, please make sure to gather all necessary information and answer each question accurately. If you're unsure about any field, refer to this help guide for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Vaccine Type (Page 1)**
* **Vaccine Type** (`vaccine_type`, select_one): Select the type of vaccine you have received.
  - Option 1, Option 2, Option 3

### 2. **Symptoms (Page 1)**
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text): List all symptoms you have experienced, such as fever, cough, or shortness of breath.

### 3. **Vaccination Status (Page 1)**
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one): Indicate your current vaccination status.
  - Option 4, Option 5, Option 6

### 4. **Employee ID (Page 2)**
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number): Enter your employee ID number, as assigned by HR.

### 5. **Department (Page 2)**
* **Department** (`department`, text): Type your department name, as indicated on your ID badge.

### 6. **Job Title (Page 2)**
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text): Type your job title, as indicated on your ID badge.

### 7. **Manager Name (Page 2)**
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text): Type your manager's name.

### 8. **Manager Email (Page 2)**
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email): Enter your manager's email address.

### 9. **Manager Phone (Page 2)**
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text): Type your manager's phone number.

### 10. **Employee Email (Page 2)**
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email): Enter your own email address.

### 11. **Employee Phone (Page 2)**
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text): Type your own phone number.

### 12. **Medical Condition (Page 3)**
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text): List any medical conditions you have.

### 13. **Medical Notes (Page 3)**
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, note): Enter any additional medical notes or information.

### 14. **Emergency Contact (Page 3)**
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text): List your emergency contact's name and contact information.

### 15. **Relationship (Page 3)**
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one): Indicate your relationship with the emergency contact.
  - Option 7, Option 8, Option 9

### 16. **Emergency Contact Address (Page 3)**
* **Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text): Type the emergency contact's address.

### 17. **Emergency Contact Phone (Page 3)**
* **Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text): Type the emergency contact's phone number.

### 18. **Emergency Contact Email (Page 3)**
* **Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email): Enter the emergency contact's email address.

### 19. **Medical Release (Page 3)**
* **Medical Release** (`medical_release`, text): Sign a statement indicating that you've read and understand the medical release.

### 20. **Date Signed (Page 3)**
* **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, date): Date and time when you signed the form.

### 21. **Time Signed (Page 3)**
* **Time Signed** (`signature_time`, time): Time when you signed the form.

### 22. **Signature (Page 3)**
* **Signature** (`signature`, text): Sign your name to confirm that you've completed the form accurately and truthfully.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when completing each field.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to this help guide for clarification.
* Ensure that you have all necessary information before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before signing.
