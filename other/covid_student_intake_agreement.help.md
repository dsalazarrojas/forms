# covid_student_intake_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "covid_student_intake_agreement" form collects student information and consent for sharing health data, travel history, and other relevant details to support public health measures and student safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Fill in your student information (page 1).
2. Provide your contact information and relationship with the emergency contact (pages 2-4).
3. Indicate your health status, symptoms, travel history, and testing status (pages 5-7).
4. Choose who you consent to share information with (pages 8-13).
5. Provide details about your emergency contact (pages 14-15).
6. Enter your healthcare provider and contact information (pages 16-17).
7. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (Page 1)**
	+ Enter your name, student ID, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Information (Page 2)**
	+ Enter your name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Health Status (Page 3)**
	+ Select "Healthy", "Unhealthy", or "Asymptomatic" to indicate your current health status.
* **Symptoms (Page 4)**
	+ Describe any COVID-19 symptoms you're currently experiencing.
* **Travel History (Page 5)**
	+ Enter your travel history, including destinations and dates.
* **Testing Status (Page 6)**
	+ Select "Tested Negative", "Tested Positive", or "Not Tested" to indicate your testing status.
* **Quarantine Status (Page 7)**
	+ Select "In Quarantine", "Not In Quarantine", or "N/A" to indicate your quarantine status.
* **Travel Restrictions (Page 8)**
	+ Describe any travel restrictions you have, such as border closures or flight bans.
* **Contact Tracing (Page 9)**
	+ Describe any recent contacts that may be relevant to contact tracing efforts.
* **Consent to Share Info (Page 10)**
	+ Choose who you consent to share your information with, such as school administrators, parents, or healthcare providers.
* **Consent to Track Location (Page 11)**
	+ Select "True" or "False" to indicate whether you consent to location tracking.
* **Consent to Share Health Info (Page 12)**
	+ Select "True" or "False" to indicate whether you consent to sharing health information.
* **Consent to Use Camera (Page 13)**
	+ Select "True" or "False" to indicate whether you consent to using a camera for health-related purposes.
* **Consent to Share Health Data (Page 14)**
	+ Select "True" or "False" to indicate whether you consent to sharing health data.
* **Consent to Share Location Info (Page 15)**
	+ Select "True" or "False" to indicate whether you consent to sharing location information.
* **Consent to Share Medical History (Page 16)**
	+ Select "True" or "False" to indicate whether you consent to sharing medical history.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 17)**
	+ Enter your emergency contact's name, phone number, and address.
* **Healthcare Provider (Page 18)**
	+ Enter your healthcare provider's name and phone number.
* **Healthcare Provider Address (Page 19)**
	+ Enter your healthcare provider's address.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (Page 20)**
	+ Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email (Page 21)**
	+ Enter your emergency contact's email.
* **Emergency Contact Notes (Page 22)**
	+ Enter any additional notes about your emergency contact.
* **Healthcare Provider Phone (Page 23)**
	+ Enter your healthcare provider's phone number.
* **Healthcare Provider Address (Page 24)**
	+ Enter your healthcare provider's address.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to ensure your health and safety.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider or school administrator for guidance.
- Review your completed form carefully before submitting it.
