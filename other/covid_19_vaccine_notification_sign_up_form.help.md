# covid-19-vaccine-notification-sign-up-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to sign up for the Covid-19 vaccination program. It collects basic contact information and preferences for vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field.
5. Enter the Time of vaccination in the Time field.
6. Provide any Additional Information in the Additional Information field.
7. Select one option from the Select One dropdown menu.
8. Select multiple options from the Select Multiple dropdown menu.
9. Select one date from the Select One Date dropdown menu.
10. Select multiple dates from the Select Multiple Dates dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your full first name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used as the primary contact method for vaccination updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for vaccination scheduling.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time**: Enter the time you are available for vaccination.
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your vaccination needs.
* **Select One**: Select one option from the available choices. This field is optional.
* **Select Multiple**: Select multiple options from the available choices. This field is optional.
* **Select One Date**: Select one date from the available choices. This field is optional.
* **Select Multiple Dates**: Select multiple dates from the available choices. This field is optional.
* **Additional Information (Optional)**: Enter any additional information you feel is relevant to your vaccination needs.
