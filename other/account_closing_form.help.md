# account_closing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers for account closure.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Fill out each field carefully and accurately**.
- **Save and submit the form when completed**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Account Type** (`account_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of account you are closing.
* **Bank Phone** (`bank_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your bank's phone number (optional).
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the customer requested to close their account.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required: false): Enter the reason for account closure.
* **User Notes** (`user_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the customer's email address (optional).

Note: Please fill out the required fields (Account Type and Bank Phone) if applicable. The other fields are optional and can be filled out at your discretion.
