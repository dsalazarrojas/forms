# skin_care_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your daily skin care habits and preferences, helping skin care professionals, businesses, or individuals better understand your needs and behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "How satisfied are you with your current skin care routine?" by selecting one of the options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
2. Indicate how often you perform a dedicated skin care routine by choosing one of the options: Daily (morning & night), Daily (once a day), A few times a week, Weekly, or Rarely/Never.
3. Select all the types of skin care products you regularly use from the list (Cleanser, Toner, Serum, Moisturizer, Sunscreen, Exfoliant, Face Mask).
4. Choose all the skin concerns you are currently experiencing from the list (Acne/Breakouts, Dryness, Oiliness, Sensitivity, Anti-aging, Uneven tone, Redness).
5. Indicate where you typically get your skin care information or product recommendations from the list (Dermatologist, Social media, Friends/Family, Beauty blogs, Retail staff, Magazines/TV).
6. Provide one thing you would like to improve about your skin or skin care routine in the text field.
7. Share any other comments or suggestions regarding skin care in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with your current skin care routine?** (Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied): This question helps us understand your level of satisfaction with your current skin care routine.
* **How often do you perform a dedicated skin care routine?** (Daily (morning & night), Daily (once a day), A few times a week, Weekly, Rarely/Never): This question assesses how frequently you practice a dedicated skin care routine.
* **Which types of skin care products do you regularly use?** (Cleanser, Toner, Serum, Moisturizer, Sunscreen, Exfoliant, Face Mask): Please select all the types of skin care products you use regularly to help us understand your product usage habits.
* **What are your primary skin concerns?** (Acne/Breakouts, Dryness, Oiliness, Sensitivity, Anti-aging, Uneven tone, Redness): This question helps us identify the skin concerns you are currently experiencing.
* **Where do you typically get your skin care information or product recommendations?** (Dermatologist, Social media, Friends/Family, Beauty blogs, Retail staff, Magazines/TV): Please select all the sources where you usually get skin care information or product recommendations.
* **What is one thing you would like to improve about your skin or skin care routine?** (Text field): Share your thoughts on what you would like to improve about your skin or skin care routine.
* **Do you have any other comments or suggestions regarding skin care?** (Text field): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you have about skin care.
