# COVID Comfortability - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your comfort level in the context of the COVID-19 pandemic. It is meant to collect information about your recent symptoms and experiences related to the pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly, and try to complete the form as thoroughly as possible.
2. Each question has a specific label and options, as described below.
3. You can submit your completed form and get access to resources and support related to your comfort level.
