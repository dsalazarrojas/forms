# user_persona_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights to understand our users' roles, goals, and needs to build comprehensive user personas. It aims to collect information on your primary role or occupation, motivation for engaging with digital tools or services, frequency of interaction, common challenges, and desired features in a digital product or service. This data will help us better understand your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your primary role or occupation from the options provided.
2. Choose your primary motivation for engaging with digital tools or services.
3. Indicate how often you interact with digital tools or services related to your work or studies.
4. Select all the challenges you frequently encounter when trying to achieve your goals.
5. Choose the features that are most important to you in a digital product or service.
6. Provide one significant improvement or change that would help you achieve your goals more effectively.
7. Share any additional information about your needs, workflows, or expectations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your primary role or occupation?**: Choose the option that best describes your job or study-related activities.
  - **Options**: Student, Professional, Manager, Entrepreneur, Other
  - **Type**: single
- **What is your primary motivation for engaging with digital tools or services?**: Select the reason that drives your usage of digital tools or services.
  - **Options**: Improve efficiency, Learn new skills, Connect with others, Solve specific problems, Stay informed
  - **Type**: single
- **How often do you interact with digital tools or services related to your work/study?**: Indicate the frequency of your interaction with digital tools or services.
  - **Options**: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, Never
  - **Type**: single
- **Which of the following challenges do you frequently encounter?**: Select all the challenges that you commonly face when trying to achieve your goals.
  - **Options**: Lack of time, Complex software, Insufficient resources, Finding relevant information, Technical difficulties
  - **Type**: multiple
- **What features are most important to you in a digital product or service?**: Choose all the features that matter most to you.
  - **Options**: Ease of use, Robust functionality, Integrations, Strong customer support, Customization
  - **Type**: multiple
- **What is one significant improvement or change that would help you achieve your goals more effectively?**: Describe one major improvement or change that would enhance your productivity or effectiveness.
  - **Type**: text
- **Anything else you'd like to share?**: Provide any additional information about your needs, workflows, or expectations.
  - **Type**: text
