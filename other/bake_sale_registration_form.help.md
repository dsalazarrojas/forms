# untitled_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for registering bake sale information, including volunteer hours, bake sale details, and submission status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Enter your name in the **Volunteers Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter the number of volunteers in the **Volunteers Count** field.
5. Describe the **Bake Sale Details**.
6. Record the volunteer hours worked in the **Volunteer Hours Worked** field.
7. Provide any additional volunteer information in the **Additional Volunteer Info** field.
8. Record the volunteer hours notes in the **Volunteer Hours Notes** field.
9. Enter the date of volunteer hours in the **Volunteer Hours Date** field.
10. Record the form submission date in the **Form Submission Date** field.
11. Record the bakery hours worked in the **Bakery Hours Worked** field.
12. Provide any additional baker information in the **Additional Baker Info** field.
13. Record the baker hours notes in the **Baker Hours Notes** field.
14. Enter the date of baker hours in the **Baker Hours Date** field.
15. Set the **Submission Status** to either Approved, Pending, or Declined.
16. Record the submission notes in the **Submission Notes** field.
17. Set the **Submission Date** and **Submission Time** fields if required.
18. Describe the **Bake Sale Registration Form Description** field.
19. Enter the name of the form submitters in the **Form Submitted By** field.
20. Enter the date the form was submitted by in the **Form Submitted On** field.
21. Enter the name of the baker who submitted the form in the **Form Submitted By Baker** field.
22. Enter the date the baker submitted the form in the **Form Submitted On Baker** field.
