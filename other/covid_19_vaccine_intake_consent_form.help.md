# covid-19-vaccine-intake-consent-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "covid-19-vaccine-intake-consent-form" is a survey used to collect information and consent for COVID-19 vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the form carefully and ensure you understand each question and the information required.
2.  Complete all mandatory fields (marked as required) with your details.
3.  For fields that are optional, provide any additional information you feel is relevant.
4.  Once you have completed all fields, review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Consent** (Consent, text, required): Please provide your consent for COVID-19 vaccination. This is a critical field that indicates your agreement to receive the vaccine.
*   **patient-info** (patient-info, text, required): Enter your personal details as prompted on this field.
*   **immunized-info** (immunized-info, text, required): Enter any previous immunization history as prompted.
*   **vaccine-info** (vaccine-info, text, required): Enter details about the vaccine you are receiving.
*   **Date** (Date, date, required): This field is for the date you are consenting to receive the vaccine. Please enter the date in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
*   **Time** (Time, time, required): This field is for the time at which you are consenting to receive the vaccine. Please enter the time in the correct format (HH:MM).
*   **vaccine-dose** (vaccine-dose, number, required): Enter the dose number of the vaccine you are receiving.
*   **vaccine-type** (vaccine-type, select_one, required): Please choose from the list of available vaccine types (Pfizer or Moderna).
*   **vaccine-manufacturer** (vaccine-manufacturer, text, required): Enter details about the manufacturer of the vaccine.
*   **side-effects** (side-effects, text, required): List any side effects you experience after receiving the vaccine.
*   **medical-conditions** (medical-conditions, select_multiple, required): Choose any medical conditions that apply to you (High Blood Pressure, Diabetes, Asthma, or other).
*   **allergies** (allergies, text, required): If you have any allergies, list them here.
*   **other-medical-conditions** (other-medical-conditions, text, required): If you have any other medical conditions not listed above, describe them here.
*   **emergency-contact-info** (emergency-contact-info, text, required): Enter the emergency contact person's information.
*   **contact-name** (contact-name, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
*   **contact-email** (contact-email, email, required): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
*   **signature** (signature, note, required): Provide your digital signature to confirm that you have read and understand the form.
