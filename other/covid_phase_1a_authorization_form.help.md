# untitled form 48264 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to support COVID-19 Phase 1A applicants in submitting their authorization for vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Form Name" field with the name of your form.
2. Enter a unique "Form ID" number.
3. Provide a brief "Description" of the form.
4. Add a "Form Title" that will appear on the form.
5. Enter a "Description" of the form.
6. Answer the question "Have Your Form In Seconds" with your expected response time.
7. Enter "Let Your Covid 19 Phase 1a Applicants Submit Their Authorization For Vaccination Immediately" with your expected response time.
8. Add a note "Copy this Covid Phase 1a Authorization Form Template to Your Jotform Account and Start Receiving Your Requests Right Away".
9. Enter your "Email Address" to receive notifications.
10. Enter your "Phone Number" to receive calls.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Name** (`form_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your form.
- **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): Enter a unique ID number for your form.
- **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your form.
- **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a title that will appear on your form.
- **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of your form.
- **Have Your Form In Seconds** (`have_your_form_in_seconds`, text, optional): Answer the question about expected response time.
- **Let Your Covid 19 Phase 1a Applicants Submit Their Authorization For Vaccination Immediately** (`let_your_covid_19_phase_1a_applicants_submit_their_authorization_for_vaccination_immediately`, text, optional): Enter your expected response time.
- **Copy this Covid Phase 1a Authorization Form Template to Your Jotform Account and Start Receiving Your Requests Right Away** (`copy_this_covid_phase_1a_authorization_form_template_to_your_jotform_account_and_start_receiving_your_requests_right_away`, text, optional): Add a note about copying the template to your Jotform account.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive notifications.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to receive calls.
