# Untitled Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for babysitting or childcare arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. You can navigate between pages using the navigation controls provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **babysitting_release** (Text, Optional): This field is for entering any relevant information about the babysitting or childcare arrangement.
* **parent_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the parent's name.
* **parent_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the parent's address.
* **babysitter_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the babysitter's name.
* **date_of_birth** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of birth of the child(ren) being cared for.
* **time_of_dropoff** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of dropoff (pick-up) for the child(ren).
* **date_of_pickup** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of pickup for the child(ren).
* **babysitting_details** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about the babysitting or childcare arrangement.
* **emergency_contact** (Text, Optional): Enter any emergency contact information for the child(ren).
* **emergency_contact_info** (Text, Optional): Enter further details about the emergency contact.
* **medical_conditions** (Select One, Optional): Select 'Yes' if the child has any medical conditions.
* **allergies** (Text, Optional): Enter any allergies the child has.
* **medical_equipment** (Text, Optional): Enter any medical equipment the child requires.
* **emergency_medical_info** (Text, Optional): Enter any emergency medical information.
* **medical_instructions** (Text, Optional): Enter any specific medical instructions for the child's care.
* **medication** (Text, Optional): Enter any medication the child is taking.
* **contact_preference** (Select One, Optional): Select 'Yes' if you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **contact_number** (Phone, Optional): Enter a phone number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
