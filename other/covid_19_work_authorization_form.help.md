# COVID 19 Work Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Work Authorization Form is a simple text-based form designed to gather information about an employee's exposure to COVID-19. It is intended to be used by employees who have been exposed to COVID-19 and need to report their status to their healthcare provider and HR department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Patient Name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Provide a brief reason for decline in the "Reason for Decline" field, if applicable.
3. Enter the name of your healthcare provider in the "Healthcare Provider" field.
4. Enter the date you were declined for work in the "Date Declined" field.
5. Sign the form by providing your signature in the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Patient Name (text)** (Patient Name, Text, Required)
The patient name is the name of the person who has been exposed to COVID-19. Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.

### **Reason for Decline (text)** (Reason for Decline, Text, Optional)
This field is for providing a brief explanation for why you are declining work. If you are not declining work, leave this field blank.

### **Healthcare Provider (text)** (Healthcare Provider, Text, Required)
Enter the name of your healthcare provider who has confirmed your exposure to COVID-19.

### **Date Declined (date)** (Date Declined, Date, Required)
Enter the date you were declined for work in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### **Signature (text)** (Signature, Text, Required)
Please sign this form by providing your signature. This will confirm that you have been exposed to COVID-19 and have reported it to your healthcare provider.
