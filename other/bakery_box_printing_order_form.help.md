# Bakery Box Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bakery Box Printing Order Form is meant to collect information from customers for a custom printing order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **customer_info** field with your name and contact information.
2. In the **order_details** field, provide a brief description of the order.
3. In the **order_items** field, select the specific box type that you would like to order (Box 1, Box 2, or Box 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_info** (Text, Required): Enter your name and contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **order_details** (Text, Not Required): Describe your order in detail, including any specific instructions or requests you may have.
* **order_items** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the type of box you would like to order (Box 1, Box 2, or Box 3). 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you have any special requests or instructions, include them in the **order_details** field.
* If you need any assistance with your order, contact us and we'll be happy to help.
