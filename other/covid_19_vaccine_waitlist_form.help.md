# covid 19 Vaccine Waitlist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Vaccine Waitlist Form is a tool used to collect information from patients waiting for vaccination. Completing this form helps the vaccination center to efficiently manage the waitlist and provide timely updates and notifications to the patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the First Name and Last Name fields with the patient's name as it appears on their government-issued ID or passport.
2. Enter the patient's email address to ensure they receive updates and notifications from the vaccination center.
3. Enter a valid phone number for the patient or their caregiver/contact.
4. Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* First Name (`First Name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's first name.
### Last Name
* Last Name (`Last Name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's last name.
### Email
* Email (`Email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address to receive updates and notifications from the vaccination center.
### Phone
* Phone (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number or their caregiver/contact's phone number for updates and contact.
### Date of Birth
* Date of Birth (`Date of Birth`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled correctly to avoid delays in processing the waitlist.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the vaccination center for assistance.
* Do not submit duplicate entries for the same person.
