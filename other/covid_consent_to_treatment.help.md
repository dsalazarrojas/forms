# COVID Consent To Treatment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients before administering treatment related to COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the "Patient Information" form (Page 1).
- Then, provide detailed information about your medical history on "Medical History" page (Page 2).
- After that, indicate your consent to receive treatment on "Consent To Treatment" form (Page 3).
- Lastly, select the date you signed this consent form on "Consent Date" page (Page 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (patient_info)** (`text`, required: false)
  This section is where you provide basic information about yourself, such as your name, address, and contact details. Be sure to fill this out accurately and truthfully.

* **Medical History (medical_history)** (`text`, required: false)
  Please provide detailed information about your medical history, including any pre-existing conditions, allergies, or previous medical treatments. This will help us better understand your health status and provide you with the best care possible.

* **Consent To Treatment (consent)** (`text`, required: false)
  In this section, please indicate your willingness to receive treatment related to COVID-19. Please carefully review the information provided and ask questions if you are unsure about anything.

* **Consent Date (consent_date)** (`date`, required: false)
  Please select the date you signed this consent form. This will serve as proof of your agreement to receive treatment and will be used for record-keeping purposes.

## Tips
- Make sure to review all the information carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
- Be sure to sign this form before leaving the treatment facility.
