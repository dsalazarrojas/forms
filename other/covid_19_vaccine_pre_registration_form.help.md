# untitled_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "untitled_form" form is designed to collect and store patient information for follow-up purposes in a hospital setting. This form helps healthcare professionals keep track of patient details, including their medical history, vaccinations, and follow-up appointments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. Provide the patient's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the patient's contact person's name and phone number in the "Contact Person" and "Contact Number" fields.
6. Enter the hospital's name in the "Hospital Name" field.
7. Enter the patient's address in the "Address" field.
8. Enter the patient's contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
9. Enter the patient's contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
10. Select the patient's gender from the "Gender" dropdown menu.
11. Enter the patient's age in the "Age" field.
12. Enter the patient's weight in the "Weight" field.
13. Enter the patient's height in the "Height" field.
14. Enter the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" text area.
15. Select any relevant medical conditions the patient has from the "Medical Condition" dropdown menu.
16. Indicate whether the patient has been vaccinated or not by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Vaccine Status" field.
17. Enter the patient's vaccine dose information in the "Vaccine Dose" field.
18. Enter the date and time of vaccination in the "Vaccine Date" and "Vaccine Time" fields.
19. Enter the follow-up date and time in the "Follow-up Date" and "Follow-up Time" fields.
20. Enter the follow-up location in the "Follow-up Location" field.
21. Enter the contact person's name and phone number in the "Follow-up Contact" fields.
22. Add any additional follow-up notes in the "Follow-up Note" text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (Required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
  - **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - **Email** (Optional): Enter the patient's email address for contact purposes.
  - **Phone** (Optional): Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
  - **Contact Person** (Optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the patient.
  - **Contact Number** (Optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
  - **Hospital Name** (Optional): Enter the name of the hospital where the patient is being treated.
  - **Address** (Optional): Enter the patient's address for contact purposes.
  - **Contact Email** (Optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
  - **Contact Phone** (Optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
  - **Gender** (Required): Select the patient's gender from the dropdown menu.
  - **Age** (Optional): Enter the patient's age in years.
  - **Weight** (Optional): Enter the patient's weight in kilograms.
  - **Height** (Optional): Enter the patient's height in meters.
  - **Medical History** (Optional): Enter the patient's medical history, including any relevant information.
  - **Medical Condition** (Optional): Select any relevant medical conditions the patient has from the dropdown menu.
  - **Vaccine Status** (Required): Indicate whether the patient has received vaccinations or not.
  - **Vaccine Dose** (Optional): Enter the patient's vaccination dose information.
  - **Vaccine Date** (Optional): Enter the date of vaccination in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - **Vaccine Time** (Optional): Enter the time of vaccination in the format HH:MM.
  - **Follow-up Date** (Optional): Enter the follow-up date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - **Follow-up Time** (Optional): Enter the follow-up time in the format HH:MM.
  - **Follow-up Location** (Optional): Enter the follow-up location.
  - **Follow-up Contact** (Optional): Enter the contact person's name and phone number.
  - **Follow-up Note** (Optional): Add any additional follow-up notes.
