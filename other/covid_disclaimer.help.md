# COVID Disclaimer - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Disclaimer form is designed to collect personal and medical information from users regarding their vaccination status. This form helps to ensure that users have been vaccinated, are aware of the risks associated with COVID-19, and are informed about the importance of maintaining social distancing and following COVID-19 protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field. This is your personal name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field. This is your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the third field. This is your email address for correspondence and updates.
4. Confirm your **Email Address** in the fourth field. Type the same email address you entered in the previous field to ensure it's accurate.
5. Check the box to **Agree** to the terms and conditions of the form. This includes your confirmation that you have been fully vaccinated, received one dose, or not vaccinated at all.
6. Optionally, you can enter the date of your last **Date** update in the eighth field. If you choose to fill out this field, please enter the date in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
7. Optionally, you can enter the time of your last **Time** update in the ninth field. If you choose to fill out this field, please enter the time in the 12-hour format (AM/PM) and the correct minutes (00-59).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your personal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for correspondence and updates.
* **Confirm Email Address**: Type the same email address you entered in the previous field to ensure it's accurate.
* **Agree**:
	+ **I have been fully vaccinated**: You have received both doses of the vaccine and are fully protected.
	+ **I have received one dose**: You have received one dose of the vaccine, but not the second dose.
	+ **I have received no vaccination**: You have not received any vaccination for COVID-19.
* **Date**: Optionally, enter the date of your last update in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Time**: Optionally, enter the time of your last update in the 12-hour format (AM/PM) and the correct minutes (00-59).
