# COVID Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect basic contact and availability information from individuals interested in volunteering for COVID-19 related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Volunteer Name**.
2. Enter your **Email** to allow us to contact you.
3. Provide your **Phone** number so we can reach you.
4. Enter your **Date Of Birth**.
5. Select your **Availability Start** time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Volunteer Name**: This is the name of the individual registering as a volunteer.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address to which we can send updates and communication.
  - Type: Email
  - Required: Yes
- **Phone**: Provide your phone number so we can contact you.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
- **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
  - Type: Date
  - Required: No
- **Availability Start**: Select your earliest availability to volunteer.
  - Type: Time
  - Required: No

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to help us process your volunteer status efficiently.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
- We appreciate your willingness to volunteer and contribute to the community's COVID-19 response.
