# untitled form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect financial data for assessment and planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Company Name" field with the name of the company or individual being assessed.
2. Enter the current value of each asset and liability in the respective fields.
3. Enter the increase or decrease in the value of each asset and liability in the respective "Increase" or "Decrease" fields.
4. Enter the date and time of the assessment.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
6. Enter the contact information in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
7. Enter any general information in the "General" field.
8. Enter the value of each asset in the "Asset 1 Value" to "Asset 6 Value" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the company or individual being assessed.
* **Asset** (`assets`, `number`, required: false): Enter the current value of each asset.
* **Liability** (`liabilities`, `number`, required: false): Enter the current value of each liability.
* **Net Worth** (`net_worth`, `number`, required: false): Enter the net worth of the company or individual.
* **Asset Increase** (`asset_increase`, `number`, required: false): Enter the increase in the value of each asset.
* **Liability Increase** (`liability_increase`, `number`, required: false): Enter the increase in the value of each liability.
* **Asset Decrease** (`asset_decrease`, `number`, required: false): Enter the decrease in the value of each asset.
* **Liability Decrease** (`liability_decrease`, `number`, required: false): Enter the decrease in the value of each liability.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the assessment.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the contact email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact phone number.
* **General** (`general`, `text`, required: false): Enter any general information.
* **Asset 1** (`asset1`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the first asset.
* **Asset 1 Value** (`asset1_value`, `number`, required: false): Enter the value of the first asset.
* **Asset 2** (`asset2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the second asset.
* **Asset 2 Value** (`asset2_value`, `number`, required: false): Enter the value of the second asset.
* **Asset 3** (`asset3`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the third asset.
* **Asset 3 Value** (`asset3_value`, `number`, required: false): Enter the value of the third asset.
* **Asset 4** (`asset4`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the fourth asset.
* **Asset 4 Value** (`asset4_value`, `number`, required: false): Enter the value of the fourth asset.
* **Asset 5** (`asset5`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the fifth asset.
* **Asset 5 Value** (`asset5_value`, `number`, required: false): Enter the value of the fifth asset.
* **Asset 6** (`asset6`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the sixth asset.
* **Asset 6 Value** (`asset6_value`, `number`, required: false): Enter the value of the sixth asset.
