# untitled form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and professional information from individuals for employment or onboarding purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read and understand the form's purpose.
2. Fill out each field accurately and truthfully.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submission.
4. Double-check for any specific requirements or guidelines mentioned within the form or by your representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
- **Date of Birth** (`2`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
- **Address** (`3`, `text`, optional): Enter your current address for contact purposes.
- **Contact Number** (`4`, `phone`, required): Enter your primary contact number for communication.
- **Email** (`5`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address for communication.
- **Background Details** (`6`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your educational and professional background.
- **Education Level** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Select your highest level of education attained (High School, Diploma, Bachelor, Master).
- **Employment Status** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your current employment status (Working, Unemployed, Retired, Student).
- **Emergency Contact Details** (`9`, `select_multiple`, optional): Specify if you have emergency contacts and select all applicable choices.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`10`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Choose the relationship you share with your emergency contact (Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Spouse, Other).
- **Emergency Contact Number** (`12`, `phone`, optional): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Email** (`13`, `email`, optional): Enter the email of your emergency contact person.
- **Employment History** (`14`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your past employment history.
- **Previous Employers** (`15`, `text`, optional): List your previous employers.
- **Work Experience** (`16`, `text`, optional): Describe your work experience, including job titles and years of service.
- **Job Titles** (`17`, `text`, optional): List your job titles, if applicable.
- **Years of Experience** (`18`, `integer`, optional): Specify the number of years you have been working in your profession.
- **Previous Workplace** (`19`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your previous workplace(s).
- **Emergency Contact Relationship Status** (`20`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your relationship with your emergency contact person (Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Spouse, Other).
- **Employment Status Description** (`21`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional details about your employment status.
- **Job Title** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter your current job title.
- **Job Occupation** (`23`, `text`, optional): Enter your current job occupation.
- **Job Industry** (`24`, `text`, optional): Enter the industry you work in.

Note: The order of fields and their labels might be subject to change based on the specific use-case.
