# COVID Safe Plan Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Safe Plan Checklist is designed to help you assess your office's preparedness and compliance with COVID-19 guidelines. This form guides you through various aspects of a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions as honestly and accurately as possible. Each question is a checklist style, where you select the options that apply to your office situation. Please make sure to answer all questions in the "Office Cleanliness", "Hand Hygiene", "Social Distance", "Travel Restrictions", "Contact Tracing", "Emergency Contact", "Medical Facilities", "Reporting", "Training", and "Emergency Procedure" sections. You can select multiple options for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Office Cleanliness** (`office_cleanliness`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if the office has measures in place to ensure cleanliness, such as regular cleaning, disinfecting, and trash disposal.
* **Hand Hygiene** (`hand_hygiene`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how hand hygiene is promoted in the office, such as providing hand sanitizer, soap, and encouraging frequent washing.
* **Social Distance** (`social_distance`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check if social distancing measures are observed, such as limiting meetings, using masks, and maintaining personal space.
* **Travel Restrictions** (`travel_restrictions`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if travel restrictions are in place, such as limiting non-essential travel, providing alternative transportation, or requiring travel approval.
* **Contact Tracing** (`contact_tracing`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check if contact tracing measures are taken, such as keeping records of staff and visitors, monitoring for symptoms, and following up with those affected.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, required/optional): Identify the emergency contact person or procedure in place in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Facilities** (`medical_facilities`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check if medical facilities such as medical supplies, first aid kits, and emergency medical procedures are available.
* **Reporting** (`reporting`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate how the office handles reporting of COVID-19 cases, such as through a designated channel, regular meetings, or incident reporting.
* **Training** (`training`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check if training on COVID-19 safety and protocols is provided to staff, including information on symptoms, prevention, and response measures.
* **Emergency Procedure** (`emergency_procedure`, select_multiple, required/optional): Outline the office's emergency procedure in case of a COVID-19 outbreak, including evacuation plans, emergency response teams, and protocols for isolating affected individuals.
