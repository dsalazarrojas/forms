# baking_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users about their baking preferences and attendance plans for a baking workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your preferred type of baking by choosing from the options provided.
2.  Provide your contact information, which will be kept confidential and used only to communicate with you about the workshop.
3.  Choose a date for the workshop you would like to attend.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **What is your favorite type of baking?** (`workshop_info`, Select Multiple, required: false)
    This field asks you to select which type of baking you are interested in or enjoy the most. Select all that apply.
*  **contact details** (`contact_info`, Note, required: false)
    Please enter your contact information, including your name, email, and any other relevant details. This will help us reach out to you about the workshop.
*  **What date would you like to attend?** (`workshop_date`, Date, required: false)
    Choose a date for the workshop you would be interested in attending.

## Tips
*   This form is not mandatory, so you can fill it out at your convenience.
*   If you need to cancel or change your attendance plans, please contact us directly.
*   We'll use this information to tailor the workshop content to meet your needs and preferences.
