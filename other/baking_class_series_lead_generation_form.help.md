# untitled_form_1 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a workshop, including the interest level, qualification, schedule, data collection methods, contact information, and workshop details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and questions carefully.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.
4. If you need help with any section, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Workshop Interest
* **Workshop Interest** (`workshop_interest`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide a brief statement about your interest in the workshop. Try to be as specific as possible in your answer.

### Lead Qualify
* **Lead Qualify** (`lead_qualify`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to indicate whether you would like to be contacted about the workshop. If you choose 'Yes', you will be contacted by our team to discuss further.

### Schedule Plan
* **Schedule Plan** (`schedule_plan`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the days and times that you are available to attend the workshop. Be sure to choose all that apply.

### Data Collection
* **Data Collection** (`data_collection`, note, optional): This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional information about your data collection needs or preferences.

### Contact Information
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, phone, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

### Workshop Details
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, optional): If you know the date of the workshop you are interested in, you can provide it here.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, optional): If you know the time of the workshop you are interested in, you can provide it here.
