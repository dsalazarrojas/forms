# Untitled Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey template used to collect information about unsold items. It is part of the "SurveyTemplates" category.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter information about unsold items in the "unsold_items" text field.
2. Input the date information in the "unsold_item_date" date field.
3. Select a product from the options in the "unsold_item_product" selection field.
4. Enter the quantity of the unsold item in the "unsold_item_quantity" number field.
5. Input the price of the unsold item in the "unsold_item_price" number field.
6. Enter the weight of the unsold item in the "unsold_item_weight" number field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **unsold_items** (`text`, required: false): Enter information about unsold items in this text field.
* **Date** (`date`, required: false): Input the date information. This field is typically used for tracking historical data.
* **Product** (`select_one`, required: false): Select one of the available products from this dropdown menu.
* **Quantity** (`number`, required: false): Enter the quantity of the unsold item.
* **Price** (`number`, required: false): Input the price of the unsold item.
* **Weight** (`number`, required: false): Enter the weight of the unsold item.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all relevant fields to provide accurate information.
- If a product selection is not available, you may need to add it to the available options.
- The date field uses the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- The quantity, price, and weight fields accept numbers as input.
