# Untitled Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in donating plasma for the COVID-19 pandemic. It is intended to be completed by individuals, not medical professionals or institutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the fields provided, making sure to include your contact information, medical history, and consent to donate.
2. Select the correct options for your blood type and medical condition.
3. Ensure your contact method and privacy agreement are accurate.
4. Review all fields carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **COVID Plasma Donation Form** (`donation_form`, `text`, optional): This is the title of the form, which you should not fill out as it is a static field.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1999).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `select_one`, optional): Select "No Condition" if you do not have any medical conditions, or select "Condition 1" or "Condition 2" if applicable.
* **Blood Type** (`blood_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all blood types that apply to you (A, B, AB, or O).
* **Hospital Location** (`hospital_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the hospital where you can be reached for further instructions or questions.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, optional): Briefly describe any relevant medical history, including any medical conditions, allergies, or medications you are taking.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your medical provider, if applicable.
* **Consent to Donation** (`consent_to_donation`, `select_one`, optional): Select "True" to indicate your consent to plasma donation.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Mail).
* **Privacy Agreement** (`privacy_agreement`, `select_one`, optional): Select "True" to agree to the terms of the privacy agreement.
* **HIPAA Compliance** (`hipaa_compliance`, `select_one`, optional): Select "True" to indicate your understanding of HIPAA compliance.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): This field should not be filled out.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, `text`, optional): This field should not be filled out.

## Tips
Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and honestly.
