# Untitled Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients regarding their COVID-19 vaccination experience. It is intended to be used by healthcare professionals to gather essential data for monitoring and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in all the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the relevant vaccine type from the options provided for the vaccine type field.
3. Choose all applicable options for the vaccination site from the options provided.
4. Enter the date and time of vaccination in the required format.
5. Read and agree to the consents provided at the end of the form.
