# covid_19_vaccine_planning_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "covid_19_vaccine_planning_survey" form is designed to collect data about a facility's preparedness and ability to handle COVID-19 vaccines. This form will help in identifying the strengths and weaknesses of the facility in managing and storing vaccines during a pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each question carefully to ensure you have the necessary information to provide accurate answers.
2.  Select the correct options for each question based on the information provided.
3.  Provide the required and optional information accurately.
4.  Double-check for any typos or errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, optional): This field is a title for your facility, which will be displayed on the form.
* **Which type of facility?** (`facility_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of facility that best describes your facility.
* **What type of equipment or tools do you have in the facility?** (`vaccination_equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all equipment or tools available at your facility.
* **What are the storage conditions in the facility?** (`vaccine_storage_conditions`, select_one, optional): Please select the storage conditions in the facility.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, optional): This field is for the facility's address.
* **Facility City** (`facility_city`, text, optional): This field is for the facility's city.
* **Facility Country** (`facility_country`, text, optional): This field is for the facility's country.
* **Has a Pandemic Vaccine Plan?** (`has_pandemic_vaccine_plan`, select_one, optional): Please select if your facility has a pandemic vaccine plan.
* **Have the necessary equipment to store vaccines?** (`has_equipment_to_store_vaccines`, select_one, optional): Please select if your facility has the necessary equipment to store vaccines.
* **Facility Capacity** (`facility_capacity`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the facility's capacity.
* **Total Vaccines to Store** (`total_vaccines_to_store`, number, optional): This field is for the total number of vaccines that need to be stored.
* **Store Vaccine Capacity** (`store_vaccine_capacity`, number, optional): This field is for the capacity of the vaccine storage in the facility.
* **Store Vaccine Capacity Notes** (`store_vaccine_capacity_notes`, note, optional): You can provide any notes or remarks about the facility's vaccine storage capacity.
* **Facility Pandemic Capacity** (`facility_pandemic_capacity`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the facility's capacity to handle pandemics.
* **Pandemics** (`pandemics`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the types of pandemics your facility can handle.
* **Pandemics Stored Vaccines** (`pandemics_stored_vaccines`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the vaccines stored for pandemics in your facility.
* **Facility Pandemic Equipment** (`facility_pandemic_equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the equipment available at your facility for pandemics.
* **Facility Pandemic Storage** (`facility_pandemic_storage`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the storage methods and facilities for pandemics in your facility.
* **Facility Pandemic Plan** (`facility_pandemic_plan`, select_one, optional): Please select if your facility has a pandemic plan.
* **Pandemics Stored Vaccines** (`pandemics_stored_vaccines`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of vaccines stored for pandemics in your facility.
* **Vaccination Program** (`vaccination_program`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the program your facility participates in.
* **Vaccination Vaccine** (`vaccination_vaccine`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the type of vaccine your facility uses.
* **Pandemics Vaccines Stored** (`pandemics_vaccines_stored`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the vaccines stored for pandemics in your facility.
* **Pandemics Stored Vaccines Notes** (`pandemics_stored_vaccines_notes`, note, optional): You can provide any notes or remarks about the facility's storage of vaccines for pandemics.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Please enter the submitter's name and other relevant details. This will serve as an acknowledgment of the completed survey for your facility. 

**Tips**

1. Be accurate in your answers.
2. Check for errors before submitting.
3. If you need help, contact us.
4. If you have completed a similar form previously, it is likely that this form's data is not required. Please check with your facility manager.
5. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to complete this form.
