# untitled_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who are interested in getting vaccinated. It will help us understand your vaccination interests and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form to start filling it out.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided for each field.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vaccine Interest Form** (`vaccine_interest_form`, `text`, required/optional): This field is where you can express your interest in getting vaccinated. Please type your answer in the space provided.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Please enter your email address here. This will help us get in touch with you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your phone number here.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Are you interested in getting vaccinated?** (`vaccine_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you are interested in getting vaccinated, 'No' otherwise.
* **Vaccination Site** (`vaccination_site`, `text`, required/optional): If you answered 'Yes' to the previous question, please let us know where you would like to get vaccinated.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, required/optional): If you answered 'Yes' to the previous question, please provide the name of your doctor.
* **Doctor's Phone Number** (`doctor_phone`, `text`, required/optional): If you answered 'Yes' to the previous question, please provide your doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor's Email** (`doctor_email`, `text`, required/optional): If you answered 'Yes' to the previous question, please provide your doctor's email address.
* **Message** (`message`, `note`, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, you can write them here.
