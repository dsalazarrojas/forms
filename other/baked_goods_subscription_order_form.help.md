# baked_goods_subscription_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "baked_goods_subscription_order_form" is a template for processing subscription orders for baked goods. This form helps the bakery team collect customer information, delivery address, subscription information, order items, payment details, and confirmation from the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to the bakery team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, *): This section is for providing your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, *): Please fill in your delivery address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Subscription Information** (`subscription_info`, text, *): This is where you provide details about your subscription, such as the type of subscription (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.) and any special requests.
* **Order Items** (`order_items`, text, *): List the items you would like to order, including the quantity and any special requests.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, *): Provide payment details, including your payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, text, *): This is a verification section to confirm that you have filled out the form accurately and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the bakery team for assistance.
* This form is for subscription orders only; if you would like to place a one-time order, please use the regular order form.
