# Untitled Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Untitled Form" is a general-purpose form used to collect user information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your preferred "Order Type" from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the date for the event or occurrence.
3. Enter the time for the event or occurrence.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about the event or occurrence.
5. Enter your name.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your address.
9. Enter the text to submit or cancel the form.
10. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Type** (`Order Type`, select one): Select the type of order you are submitting for (e.g., order 1, order 2, or order 3).
* **Date** (`Date`, date): Enter the date of the event or occurrence (mm/dd/yyyy format is recommended).
* **Time** (`Time`, time): Enter the time of the event or occurrence (12-hour or 24-hour format is accepted).
* **Note** (`Note`, note): Add any additional notes or comments about the event or occurrence.
* **Name** (`Name`, text): Enter your full name.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`Email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`Address`, text): Enter your address.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text): Enter the text to submit or cancel the form.
* **Cancel** (`Cancel`, text): Enter the text to cancel the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your submission is complete and accurate.
* If you need to submit multiple orders, use the "Order Type" dropdown menu to select the correct order type for each submission.
