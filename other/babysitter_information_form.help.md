# Untitled Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about a babysitter's profile. It's likely used for caregivers who offer childcare services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form and a new page will load.
2. The form has 10 fields across 1 page, each with a label and a corresponding input field.
3. Fill out each field with the required information.
4. Once completed, click the "Submit" button to save the form data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Babysitter Information** (`Babysitter Information`, text, optional): Enter a brief description about yourself as a caregiver.
* **Years of experience** (`Years of experience`, integer, optional): Please enter the number of years you have experience as a babysitter.
* **References** (`References`, select multiple, optional): If applicable, select the "Yes" option for any relevant information you have.
* **Emergency contact** (`Emergency contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and relationship of the emergency contact.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, phone, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, optional): Enter your street address.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, select one, optional): Select "Yes" if you're available for childcare duties and "No" otherwise.
* **Rate** (`Rate`, decimal, optional): Enter your hourly rate for childcare services.
* **Payment** (`Payment`, select one, optional): Select "Yes" if you accept payment through this platform and "No" otherwise.
