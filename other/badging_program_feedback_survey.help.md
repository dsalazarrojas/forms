# badging_program_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "badging_program_feedback_survey" form is used to collect feedback from trainees on their experience with the badging program. It aims to gather information on different aspects of the program, such as training quality, content, and organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer it honestly.
2. For rating questions, select the rating that best reflects your opinion.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, type them in the corresponding text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Badging Program Feedback** (id: 1): Describe your overall experience with the badging program.
- **Training Satisfaction** (id: 2): Rate your satisfaction with the training provided.
- **Content** (id: 3): Rate your satisfaction with the content of the training.
- **Improvement Suggestion** (id: 4): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
- **Organizing Committee** (id: 5): Rate your opinion on how well the organizing committee performed.
- **Organizers** (id: 6): Rate your opinion on each organizer.
- **Email** (id: 7): Enter your email for any additional comments or suggestions.
- **Phone** (id: 8): Enter your phone number for any additional comments or suggestions.
- **Date** (id: 9): Enter the date you submitted this form.
- **Time** (id: 10): Enter the time you submitted this form.
- **Note** (id: 11): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
- **Submission** (id: 12): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
- **Submission Date** (id: 13): Enter the date you submitted this form.
- **Satisfaction** (id: 14): Enter your overall satisfaction with the training.
- **Benefits** (id: 15): Enter any benefits you gained from the training.
- **Suggestion** (id: 16): Enter any suggestions for improvement.
- **Rating Benefits** (id: 17): Rate your opinion on each benefit.
- **Improvement** (id: 18): Rate your opinion on each improvement.
- **Value** (id: 19): Rate your opinion on the value of the training.
- **Comments** (id: 20): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
- **Submission Comments Date** (id: 21): Enter the date you submitted your comments.
- **Submission Time** (id: 22): Enter the time you submitted your comments.
- **Submission Note** (id: 23): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
- **Quality of Training** (id: 24): Rate your opinion on the quality of the training.
- **Value of Training** (id: 25): Rate your opinion on the value of the training.
