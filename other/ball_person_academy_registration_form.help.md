# untitled_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a participant registration form for a sports-related event. It is designed to collect information about participants, including their name, contact details, and registration time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Form Name** in the text field at the top of the page. This is an optional field, and it seems it's meant for the administrator to identify the form.
2. Enter your **Participant Name** in the text field below. This is the name of the person registering for the event.
3. Select the sport you are participating in from the dropdown menu in the **Sport** field. Choose one of the available options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4.
4. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the date field. This is the date you were born.
5. Enter the time you registered for the event in the **Time of Registration** field. This is the time you submitted the form.
6. Enter your **Phone** number in the text field. This is your contact phone number.
7. Enter your **Email** address in the email field. This is your contact email address.
8. Enter your **Participant Number** in the number field. This is a unique identifier for the participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Name**: Enter a brief name for this registration instance, if needed.
* **Participant Name**: The name of the person registering for the event.
* **Sport**: Select the sport you are participating in.
* **Date of Birth**: The date of your birth.
* **Time of Registration**: Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Participant Number**: Enter a unique identifier for the participant.
