# Untitled Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information from performers regarding their availability and scheduling needs. It helps staff or administrators to coordinate and manage performances more efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form and understand what information is required for each field.
* Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions or approvals before submitting the form.
* Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performer Scheduling** (`performer_scheduling`, text, optional): A brief description of the performer's schedule and availability.
* **Equipment Needs** (`equipment_needs`, text, optional): A list of equipment or resources the performer may require for the performance.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Any additional comments or feedback from the performer.
* **Backline Scheduling** (`backline_scheduling`, select_one, optional): Select one of the available options for backline scheduling.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Time Slots** (`time_slots`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple time slots that work for the performer.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Available Dates** (`available_dates`, date, optional): The date(s) the performer is available to work.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, time, optional): The time of day the performer is available.
* **Performer Contact** (`performer_contact`, text, optional): A contact person or point of contact for the performer.
* **Performer Phone** (`performer_phone`, text, optional): The performer's phone number.
* **Performer Email** (`performer_email`, email, optional): The performer's email address.

## Tips

* Please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant staff or administrator.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
