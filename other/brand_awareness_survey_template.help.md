# brand_awareness_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brand Awareness Survey is a tool used to gauge the level of recognition and understanding of our brand among the public. It is designed to gather information on the familiarity, sources of information, and overall impression of the brand, as well as suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select all applicable sources from the list in question 2.
3. Answer questions 3 and 4 with a brief description or phrase.
4. Be honest and objective in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Question 1: How familiar are you with our brand?
* **How familiar are you with our brand?** (`Very familiar`, `Somewhat familiar`, `Heard of it`, `Not familiar`, `Unknown`)
	+ This question measures the level of personal recognition of our brand.

### Question 2: Where have you seen or heard about our brand? (Select all that apply)
* **Where have you seen or heard about our brand?** (`Social media`, `News/articles`, `Advertising`, `Word-of-mouth`, `Our website`, `Events`, `Other`)
	+ This question identifies the sources of information about our brand.

### Question 3: What is your overall impression of our brand?
* **What is your overall impression of our brand?** (`Very positive`, `Positive`, `Neutral`, `Negative`, `Very negative`)
	+ This question gauges the sentiment or attitude towards our brand.

### Question 4: What comes to mind when you think of our brand?
* **What comes to mind when you think of our brand?** (text field)
	+ This question seeks to understand any thoughts, associations, or memories that people have when they think of our brand.

### Question 5: What could we do to improve awareness or perception of our brand?
* **What could we do to improve awareness or perception of our brand?** (text field)
	+ This question encourages respondents to provide suggestions on how we can enhance our brand's image and recognition.

## Tips
* Be honest and objective in your responses.
* Provide specific examples or details if possible.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the text fields.
