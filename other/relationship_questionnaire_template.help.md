# Relationship Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information on personal relationships, focusing on aspects such as satisfaction, connection frequency, important qualities, and effort put into relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the question "How satisfied are you with your closest relationships?" by selecting one of the five satisfaction levels (Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
2.  Answer the question "How often do you intentionally connect with important people in your life?" by selecting one of the five frequency options (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, Never).
3.  For the question "Which qualities are most important in a healthy relationship?" select all that apply from the six qualities (Trust, Communication, Support, Honesty, Respect, Shared interests).
4.  Answer the question "What is one area you would like to improve in your current relationships?" by providing a free-text response.
5.  Answer the question "How much effort do you feel you put into maintaining your relationships?" by selecting one of the five effort levels (A great deal, A fair amount, Some, Not much, Very little).
6.  For the question "Do you have any additional comments or thoughts on relationships?" provide a free-text response.
