# Babysitter Medical Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Babysitter Medical Release Form is a legal document that allows parents or guardians to grant permission for their child to receive medical treatment in their absence. It's essential to complete this form to ensure that the babysitter is aware of any medical conditions or allergies that may affect their care of your child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the Babysitter's Name in the "Babysitter Name" field.
2. Enter your child's name in the "Parent Name" field.
3. Provide any medical information in the "Medical Information" field.
4. Enter contact information for emergency situations in the "Emergency Contact" field.
5. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (if desired).
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (if desired).
7. Check "Yes" in the "Medical Condition" field if your child has any medical conditions.
8. If your child takes any medication, enter the type and dosage in the "Medication" field.
9. If your child has any allergies, enter the type and severity in the "Allergy" field.
10. If your child has any medical procedures, enter the type and details in the "Medical Procedure" field.
11. Enter your health insurance information in the "Health Insurance" field (if desired).
12. Enter the name and contact information of the emergency medical authority in the "Emergency Medical Authority" field (if desired).
13. Enter the name of your child's medical provider in the "Medical Provider" field (if desired).
14. Provide any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.
15. Enter any emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact Info" field (if desired).
16. Enter the name of the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field (if desired).
17. Select the relationship of the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field (if desired).
18. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have provided accurate information.
19. Enter the date and time of completion in the "Date" and "Time" fields (if desired).
20. Enter the address of the location where the care will be provided in the "Address" field (if desired).
21. Enter the zip code of the location in the "Zip" field (if desired).
22. Enter the city of the location in the "City" field (if desired).
23. Enter the state of the location in the "State" field (if desired).
24. Enter the country of the location in the "Country" field (if desired).

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian.

* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): If your child takes any medication, enter the type and dosage here.
* **Allergy** (`allergy`, text, optional): If your child has any allergies, enter the type and severity here.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, text, optional): If your child has any medical procedures, enter the type and details here.
* **Health Insurance** (`insurance`, text, optional): Enter your health insurance information (if desired).
* **Emergency Medical Authority** (`emergency_medical_authority`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the emergency medical authority (if desired).
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of your child's medical provider (if desired).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical history about your child.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any emergency contact information (if desired).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact (if desired).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Select the relationship of the emergency contact (if desired).
