# untitled_form_80883 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects patient information for vaccine administration and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure that you have permission from the patient before collecting their data.
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vaccine Type** (`vaccine_type`, text, optional): This field collects the type of vaccine administered to the patient.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): This field collects any medical conditions or allergies that may be relevant to the patient's treatment.
* **Medical Conditions List** (`medical_conditions_list`, text, optional): This field is used to provide additional medical conditions or allergies not listed in the main Medical Conditions field.
* **Contraindication List** (`contraindication_list`, text, optional): This field is used to list any contraindications for the vaccine, such as previous reactions or sensitivities.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, optional): This field collects the patient's age.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): This field is used to note any allergies or sensitivities the patient may have.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This field collects any relevant medical history or previous medical conditions.
* **Contraindications** (`contraindications`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to select any contraindications for the vaccine.
* **Contrainications Other** (`contraindications_other`, text, optional): This field is used to provide additional information for contraindications not listed in the main Contraindications field.
* **Contrainication Reason** (`contraindication_reason`, text, optional): This field is used to provide reason for any contraindication selected in the main Contraindications field.
* **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`, note, optional): This field is used to collect patient consent for data collection.
* **Medical Practitioner Name** (`medical_practitioner_name`, text, optional): This field collects the name of the medical practitioner administering the vaccine.
* **Medical Practitioner Title** (`medical_practitioner_title`, text, optional): This field collects the title of the medical practitioner administering the vaccine.
* **Date of Screening** (`date_of_screening`, date, optional): This field collects the date of the patient screening or assessment.
* **Date of Vaccination** (`date_of_vaccination`, date, optional): This field collects the date of the vaccine administration.
* **Vaccine Dose** (`vaccine_dose`, number, optional): This field collects the number of vaccine doses administered to the patient.
* **Vaccine Type** (`vaccine_type`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select the type of vaccine administered to the patient.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field collects any relevant contact information for the patient.
* **Follow-up Date** (`followup_date`, date, optional): This field collects the date for follow-up appointments.
* **Vaccine Administered** (`vaccine_administered`, time, optional): This field collects the time of vaccine administration.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, text, optional): This field is used to track the patient's status after vaccination.
* **Vaccine Batch** (`vaccine_batch`, number, optional): This field collects the batch number of the vaccine administered.
* **Medical Practitioner** (`medical_practitioner`, text, optional): This field collects any additional information about the medical practitioner administering the vaccine.
* **Vaccination Site** (`vaccination_site`, text, optional): This field collects the location where the vaccine was administered.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to select the status of follow-up appointments.

## Tips
When filling out this form, please ensure that you are using it for its intended purpose and that the information collected is accurate and relevant to the patient's needs. If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please consult the medical practitioner or patient care team for guidance.
