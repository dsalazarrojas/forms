# untitled_form_10284 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and insights from parents about their child's education during the home schooling period. Your input is valuable and will help create a better understanding of what parents are going through during this time.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Please answer each question truthfully and as thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the correct type for each question (text or number).
3. Enter your response in the designated fields.
4. Submit your answers when you've completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Back To School Parent Survey** (`back_to_school_parent_survey`, text, required: false): This is an introductory section where you can express your thoughts and feelings about the home schooling period. Please provide a brief description of your experiences and concerns.
* **What did parents learn about their child's education during the home schooling period** (`what_did_parents_learn`, text, required: false): Describe what you, as a parent, learned about your child's education during the home schooling period.
* **Parents' support for child's education** (`parents_support`, number, required: false): Indicate the level of support you, as a parent, provided to your child's education during this time. You can enter a number from 1-10, where 1 means "not much" and 10 means "maximum support".
* **Child's Age** (`child_s_age`, number, required: false): Enter your child's current age.
* **What were parents' challenges** (`what_were_parents_challenges`, text, required: false): Describe any challenges you faced as a parent during the home schooling period.
* **What will parents' expectations for school reopening be** (`what_will_parents_expectations`, text, required: false): Share your expectations about your child's future school experience. What do you hope to see or experience when they return to school?
