# covid-19-viewer-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about COVID-19 from users. The data collected will be used to better understand the impact of COVID-19 on users and to improve the overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, click on the "Submit" button to begin the survey.
2. The form will be divided into sections, each with its own set of questions or fields. Please fill out each section completely and accurately.
3. If you have any feedback or comments, please use the provided text fields to express your thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **demographics** (demographics_page, text, required/optional): This field is where you can input your demographic information, such as your age, occupation, and education level.
* **feedback** (feedback_page, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide any feedback or comments about your experience with COVID-19.
* **questions** (questions_page, select_multiple, required): Please select all the options that apply to you from the list of questions.
* **comments** (comments_page, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments about your experience with COVID-19.
* **contact** (contact_page, text, required/optional): This field is where you can input your contact information, such as your email address and phone number.
* **email** (email_page, email, required/optional): This field is where you can input your email address.
* **questions** (questions_page, select_multiple, required): Please select all the options that apply to you from the list of questions provided on this page.
 
Note: The questions_page field is repeated to accommodate the multiple question options. Please ensure to select all the options that apply to you.
 
Tips:
* Please ensure that you are filling out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask the administrator for clarification.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
