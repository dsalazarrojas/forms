# product_research_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users to understand their perceptions and areas for improvement regarding the product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "How satisfied are you with our product?" by selecting one of the five options provided.
2. For the question "Which aspects most influenced your opinion of our product?", select all that apply.
3. Answer the question "Would you recommend this product to others?" by selecting one option.
4. Provide your thoughts on what you would improve or change about our product in the text box.
5. If you have any additional features you would like to see added or enhanced, please provide them in the text box.
6. Answer the question "Overall, how valuable is this product to you?" by selecting one of the five options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How satisfied are you with our product?** (`Very satisfied`, `single`, required): How satisfied are you with our product? Select one of the five options provided.
- **Which aspects most influenced your opinion of our product?** (`Design`, `Price`, `Performance`, `Features`, `Support`, `Ease of use`, `Reliability`, multiple, required): This question is meant to help us understand which aspects of our product most influenced your opinion.
  * Design: The design of the product, including its aesthetics and layout.
  * Price: The price of the product and its value for the money.
  * Performance: The product's functionality and performance.
  * Features: The features included in the product.
  * Support: The quality and availability of customer support.
  * Ease of use: How easy or difficult it is to use the product.
  * Reliability: The product's reliability and consistency.
  Select all that apply.
- **Would you recommend this product to others?** (`true`, `false`, `Maybe`, single, required): This question is meant to gauge your willingness to recommend our product to others.
- **What would you improve or change about our product?** (text, required): This question is an open-ended question meant to help us understand areas for improvement.
- **Are there any specific features you'd like to see added or enhanced?** (text, required): This question is meant to gather suggestions for new features or improvements you would like to see.
- **Overall, how valuable is this product to you?** (`Very valuable`, `Somewhat valuable`, `Neutral`, `Not very valuable`, `Not valuable at all`, single, required): This question is meant to gauge your overall opinion on the product's value to you.
