# Songwriting Collaboration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in collaborating on songwriting projects. It is meant to be shared publicly to facilitate potential collaborations between artists.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill in your contact details, including your name, contact info, and email address.
3. Select one or more genres you are interested in collaborating on, and list the instruments you are proficient in.
4. Provide a brief bio or description of your musical experience and interests.
5. Indicate your specific collaboration interests or goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact info** (text, required): This field is where you will provide your contact details. Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **email** (email, optional): This field is for your email address. You can enter your personal or professional email address.
* **phone number** (text, optional): This field is for your phone number. This can be your personal or work phone number.
* **bio** (text, optional): This field is for a brief description of your musical experience and interests. Be as concise as possible.
* **genre** (select_one, optional): This field allows you to select one or more genres you are interested in collaborating on. Select all that apply.
* **instruments** (select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select the instruments you are proficient in. Select all that apply.
* **collaboration interest** (text, optional): This field is for you to describe your specific collaboration interests or goals. Be as specific as possible.

## Tips

* Please make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* Use a clear and concise format for your bio and collaboration interest fields.
* If you have any specific requirements or requests for the collaboration (e.g. specific genre, instrumentation, or project requirements), please indicate them in the collaboration interest field.
