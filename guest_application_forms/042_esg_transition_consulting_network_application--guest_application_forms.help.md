# ESG Transition Consulting Network Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The ESG Transition Consulting Network Application is a form designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining the ESG Transition Consulting Network. This form helps us understand your background, expertise, and interests to determine the best fit for collaboration and knowledge-sharing opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form with accurate and complete information**. Make sure to provide the required fields.
2. **Review each field carefully** before submitting the form.
3. **Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **ESG Transition Consulting Network Application Form** (`application_form`, text, required): This is the main form section where you can provide a brief introduction about yourself and your interest in joining the ESG Transition Consulting Network.
* **Experience** (`experience`, date, optional): If you have any relevant experience in the ESG Transition, please provide the dates of your experience.
* **Expertise Areas** (`expertise_areas`, select multiple, optional): Select all the areas where you have expertise, such as ESG Transition, ESG Impact, Sustainability, and Energy.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Upload your resume as a PDF to showcase your qualifications and experience. Please make sure to upload a valid PDF file.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted: by Email, Phone, or LinkedIn.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **LinkedIn** (`linkedin`, text, optional): Enter your LinkedIn profile URL where you can be contacted.
* **LinkedIn** (no label, text, optional): This field is not visible in the form, but it is the same as the `linkedin` field. 

Please note that all fields are optional, but it is recommended to fill out as much information as possible to help us understand your background and interests.
