# Digital Performance Exhibition Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for artists to submit applications for a digital performance exhibition. It is designed to gather necessary information about the artist's performance, including details about the artist's name, proposal, media links, performance title, performance description, and performance image.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information and attach any necessary media links. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Details**: This field is for you to provide your artist name and any other relevant details about yourself as an artist.
* **Proposal**: In this field, please share a brief proposal outlining your performance concept, goals, and expectations.
* **Media Links**: Attach any relevant media links that support your performance, such as videos or images.
* **Performance Title**: Enter a title for your performance that best describes it.
* **Performance Description**: Write a brief description of your performance, including details about the concept, duration, and any notable features.
* **Performance Image**: Attach an image that represents your performance and helps the organizers understand it better.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your application is complete.
* Attach clear and relevant media links to support your performance.
* Be concise and clear in your proposal and description.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
