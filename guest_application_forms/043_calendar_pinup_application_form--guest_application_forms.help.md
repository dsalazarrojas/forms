# Calendar Pinup Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from potential calendar pinups for the year(s) specified. The form includes questions about personal and professional information, as well as preferences and availability for shoots.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, stage name (if different), and email address.
2. Provide your phone number and date of birth. Please note that you must be 18 or older to be eligible for this opportunity.
3. Confirm your age by selecting "Yes, I am 18 or older" in the Age Verification section.
4. Enter your full mailing address.
5. Select your preferred calendar year.
6. If you have any modeling experience, describe it in the Modeling Experience section.
7. If you have any previous pinup experience, select the corresponding option.
8. If you have a portfolio, provide the link to it.
9. If you have social media accounts, list them in the Social Media Handles section.
10. If you have any photos to submit, please prepare them according to the Photo Submission section.
11. Select your availability for shoots from the options provided.
12. Finally, provide any additional comments or information you would like us to know.
