# Guest Blogger Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Blogger Application Form is used to collect information from potential guests who want to contribute to the blog as influencers. This form helps the blog administrators evaluate the applicants' suitability for guest blogging opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Fill In This Form" field with your introduction or message.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the type of influencer you are (e.g. Fashion, Beauty, Sports, etc.) by choosing one option from the "What Type Of Influencer Are You?" dropdown menu.
5. Provide the URL of your blog in the "Blog Name" field.
6. Enter your blog's website address in the "Blog URL" field.
7. Select your social media links (e.g. Twitter, Instagram, TikTok, etc.) by choosing one or multiple options from the "Social Media Links" dropdown menu.
8. Add any additional information about yourself in the "Additional Information" field (optional).
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Email**: Provide your email address.
* **What Type Of Influencer Are You?**: Select one option from the dropdown menu:
	+ Fashion
	+ Beauty
	+ Sports
	+ Gaming
	+ Food
	+ Travel
* **Blog Name**: Enter your blog's name (optional).
* **Blog URL**: Enter your blog's website address (optional).
* **Social Media Links**: Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu:
	+ Twitter
	+ Instagram
	+ TikTok
* **Additional Information**: Add any additional information about yourself (optional).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).

## Tips
- Please ensure that you fill out all the required fields accurately to increase your chances of getting approved as a guest blogger.
- If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, feel free to contact our team for assistance.
