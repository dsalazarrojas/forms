# Eco Exhibit Design Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Exhibit Design Application Form is used to collect information from applicants who wish to participate in the Eco Exhibit Design project. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to submit your proposal and any additional information required for the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your contact information in the **Contact Information** section.
2.  Fill out the **Name** field with your full name.
3.  Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
4.  Provide your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5.  Enter the title of your proposal in the **Proposal Title** field.
6.  Provide a brief description of your proposal in the **Description** field.
7.  Select the type of exhibit you are proposing in the **Exhibit Type** field.
8.  Enter the date you would like to submit your proposal in the **Submission Date** field.
9.  Enter the time you would like to submit your proposal in the **Submission Time** field.
10.  Enter any additional notes or comments in the **Notes** field.
11.  Select any applicable options for **Assigned** field.
11.  Enter the name of the person submitting the proposal in the **Submitter** field.
12.  Enter the date you submitted your proposal in the **Submit Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Contact Information**: Enter your name, phone number, and email address to ensure we can get in touch with you if needed.
*   **Name**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed on the proposal.
*   **Email**: Enter the email address to which you can be contacted.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
*   **Proposal Title**: Enter a brief title of your proposal.
*   **Description**: Provide a brief description of your proposal.
*   **Exhibit Type**: Select the type of exhibit you are proposing from the available options.
*   **Submission Date**: Enter the date you would like to submit your proposal.
*   **Submission Time**: Enter the time you would like to submit your proposal.
*   **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your proposal.
*   **Assigned**: Select any applicable options for the assigned team members.
*   **Submitter**: Enter the name of the person submitting the proposal.
*   **Submit Date**: Enter the date of submission of your proposal.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely before submission.
*   Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
*   Review your proposal before submission to ensure it meets the project requirements.
