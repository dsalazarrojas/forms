# Podcast Guest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect podcast guest information for potential interviews or features on the podcast.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the application. This is the title of the podcast episode in which you would like to be featured. This field is optional and only necessary for organization purposes.
2. Select a category for your application. Choose 'Guest Application Forms' if you are applying as a general guest, or 'Other' if your category does not fit into the default category.
3. Enter a brief description about yourself, including your experience, expertise, and qualifications. This will give the podcast host an idea of who you are and what makes you a good fit for the podcast.
4. If you have a logo associated with your brand or identity, please include it here. This will be displayed on the podcast episode's artwork.
5. Please upload any files or information that you believe the host needs to consider your application. This will help the host to get to know you better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title (1)** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for the application.
* **Category (2)** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select a category for your application, either 'Guest Application Forms' or 'Other'.
* **Description (3)** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description about yourself, including your experience, expertise, and qualifications.
* **Logo (4)** (`logo`, text, optional): If you have a logo associated with your brand or identity, please include it here.
* **Form File (5)** (`form_file`, text, optional): Please upload any files or information that you believe the host needs to consider your application.
* **Custom Fields (6)** (`custom_fields`, note, optional): This is a note field for any custom information required by the podcast host. Please enter any additional details about yourself that the host has requested.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the podcast host or email support for clarification.
* Once you have submitted your application, you will not be able to submit the same form again. If you wish to reapply, please contact the podcast host or email support.
