# Guest Blog Posting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Guest Blog Posting Form is designed for guest bloggers to submit their blog posts to the site. This form collects various information about the blog post, including the guest blogger's details, blog post details, and submission information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields, which include:
	* Name
	* Email
	* Blog Description
	* Blog Categories
	* Blog Topics
	* Submission Date
	* Submission Time
	* Submission Status
	* Submitted By
	* Submitter's Name
	* Submitter's Email
	* Submission URL
2. Add additional information about your blog post, such as:
	* Blog Image
	* Blog Link
	* Blog Tags
	* Submitter's Comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Blogger Information** (`guest_blogger_info`): A field for the guest blogger's contact information.
* **Name** (`name`): A field for the guest blogger's name.
* **Email** (`email`): A field for the guest blogger's email.
* **Website** (`website`): A field for the guest blogger's website.
* **Blog Description** (`blog_description`): A field for the guest blogger's blog description.
* **Blog Image** (`blog_image`): A field for the guest blogger's blog image.
* **Blog Link** (`blog_link`): A field for the guest blogger's blog link.
* **Blog Categories** (`blog_categories`): A select_multiple field for the guest blogger to select multiple categories for their blog post.
* **Blog Topics** (`blog_topics`): A select_multiple field for the guest blogger to select multiple topics for their blog post.
* **Blog Tags** (`blog_tags`): A select_multiple field for the guest blogger to select multiple tags for their blog post.
* **Submission** (`submission`): A field for the guest blogger to provide their submission information.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`): A field for the guest blogger to confirm their submission.
* **Contact** (`contact`): A field for the guest blogger's contact information.
* **Blog Type** (`blog_type`): A select_one field for the guest blogger to select the type of their blog post.
* **Blog Format** (`blog_format`): A select_one field for the guest blogger to select the format of their blog post.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): A field for the guest blogger to enter the submission date of their blog post.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`): A field for the guest blogger to enter the submission time of their blog post.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`): A select_one field for the guest blogger to select the status of their submission.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): A field for the guest blogger to enter who submitted the blog post.
* **Submitter's Name** (`submitter_name`): A field for the guest blogger's submitter's name.
* **Submitter's Email** (`submitter_email`): A field for the guest blogger's submitter's email.
* **Submitter's Phone** (`submitter_phone`): A field for the guest blogger's submitter's phone number.
* **Submission IP** (`submission_ip`): A field for the guest blogger's submission IP address.
* **Submission URL** (`submission_url`): A field for the guest blogger's submission URL.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`): A field for the guest blogger's submitter comments.
* **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`): A field for the guest blogger's submission notes.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a successful submission.
* Use the select_multiple fields to select multiple categories, topics, and tags for your blog post.
* Use the select_one fields to select the type and format of your blog post.
* Enter accurate information in the submission date, submission time, and submission status fields.
* Double-check your submission information before submitting the form.
