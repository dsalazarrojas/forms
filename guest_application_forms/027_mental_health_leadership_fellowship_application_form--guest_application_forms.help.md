# Mental Health Leadership Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Leadership Fellowship Application Form is a comprehensive application form designed to collect essential information from applicants for the Mental Health Leadership Fellowship program. This form is intended to gather data that will be reviewed by the selection committee for the fellowship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring that each field is filled out accurately and thoroughly.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you are providing the necessary information.
3. Once you have filled out the form, click on the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information (1)**:
  * Type: Text field
  * Optional: Yes
  * This field is intended for you to provide your name, title (if applicable), and any other relevant details about yourself.

* **Personal Details (2)**:
  * Type: Text field
  * Optional: Yes
  * This field is intended for you to provide any relevant personal details that will help us get to know you better.

* **Work Experience (3)**:
  * Type: Text field
  * Optional: Yes
  * This field is intended for you to describe your work experience, including any relevant job titles, company names, and job responsibilities.

* **Education (4)**:
  * Type: Text field
  * Optional: Yes
  * This field is intended for you to provide information about your educational background, including degree earned and institution attended.

* **Skills (5)**:
  * Type: Text field
  * Optional: Yes
  * This field is intended for you to list any skills you possess that are relevant to the fellowship program.

* **Mental Health Background (6)**:
  * Type: Text field
  * Optional: Yes
  * This field is intended for you to describe your mental health background, including any relevant training or experience.

* **Goals (7)**:
  * Type: Text field
  * Optional: Yes
  * This field is intended for you to outline your professional goals, including what you hope to achieve through the fellowship program.

* **References (8)**:
  * Type: Text field
  * Optional: Yes
  * This field is intended for you to provide the names and contact information of two professional references.

* **Submit (9)**:
  * Type: Text field
  * Optional: No
  * This field is the final step in submitting your completed application.

* **Submit Button (10)**:
  * Type: Button
  * Optional: No
  * Click on this button to finalize and submit your completed application.

## Tips
Please ensure that you provide accurate and thorough information in each field. Applications that are incomplete or contain inaccuracies may be disqualified.
