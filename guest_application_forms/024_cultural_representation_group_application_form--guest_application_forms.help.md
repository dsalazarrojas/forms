> # Cultural Representation Group Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who wish to join a Cultural Representation Group. It is intended to gather relevant details about the applicant, which will be reviewed and considered by group administrators for membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your organization's name, if applicable.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select one option from the "Select one" field.
6. Select multiple options from the "Select multiple" field.
7. Enter a date if applicable.
8. Enter a time if applicable.
9. Add any additional notes.
10. Indicate when you submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization** (text, optional): Enter your organization's name, if applicable.
* **Name** (text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Select one** (select_one, optional): Select one option from the dropdown list.
* **Select multiple** (select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options from the dropdown list.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter a date if applicable.
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter a time if applicable.
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Submitted** (date, optional): Indicate when you submitted the form.
