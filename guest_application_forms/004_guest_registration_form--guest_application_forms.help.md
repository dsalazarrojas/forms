# Guest Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Guest Registration Form is a simple form used to collect information from guests when they visit our facility. This form helps us gather essential details about each guest, ensuring we provide the best possible experience during their stay.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select the date of your visit using the calendar input.
6. Choose the time of your visit using the clock input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first name** (Text, Required/Optional: Not Required): Please enter your first name, which is typically the name given to you at birth.
* **last name** (Text, Required/Optional: Not Required): Enter your last name, which is your family name or surname.
* **email** (Email, Required/Optional: Not Required): Provide a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **phone** (Text, Required/Optional: Not Required): Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **date of visit** (Date, Required/Optional: Not Required): Select the date of your visit from the provided calendar.
* **time of visit** (Time, Required/Optional: Not Required): Choose the time of your visit from the clock input.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure we can provide you with the best possible experience.
* If you have any special requests or preferences, please let us know in the "special requests" field (not included in this form but assumed to be relevant).
