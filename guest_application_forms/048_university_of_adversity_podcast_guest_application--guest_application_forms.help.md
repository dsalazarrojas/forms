### Form: University Of Adversity Podcast Guest Application
### Purpose
The University Of Adversity Podcast Guest Application form is designed to collect information from potential guests for the podcast. This form helps us to find the best fit for our episodes and ensures a smooth guest experience.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information. The process is straightforward, and you'll need to provide us with your basic contact details and any additional information you'd like to share.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
#### User Info
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, not required): This field is not required but provides a brief introduction or description of who you are and what you do.

#### Full Name
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, not required): Enter your full name here.

#### Email
* **Email** (`email`, text, not required): Your email address where we can contact you.

#### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Provide your phone number so we can reach you easily.

#### Social LinkedIn URL
* **Social LinkedIn URL** (`social_linkedin_url`, text, not required): If you have a LinkedIn profile, share your URL here.

### Tips
- Please note that all fields are optional, but filling them in can help us better understand your background and find the right fit for our episodes.
- Make sure to proofread your application before submitting to avoid any errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
- You can also submit your application via email to [insert email], and we will manually create a form for you.
