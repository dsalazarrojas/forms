# ประก prophets Creative Residency Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "ประก prophets Creative Residency Program Application Form" is designed to collect information from artists applying to the "ประก prophets Creative Residency Program". This form is meant to assess an artist's eligibility for the residency program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your artist statement in the "Artist Statement" field.
2. Provide your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields respectively.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Describe your project details in the "Project Details" field.
5. Outline your project proposals in the "Project Proposals" field.
6. Select the residency requirements that fit your project needs from the "Residency Requirements", "Residency Duration", "Residency Location", and "Residency Dates" fields.
7. Choose your residency application fee payment method in the "Residency Application Fee Payment Method" field.
8. Select the residency application fee refund policy that applies to your situation in the "Residency Application Fee Refund Policy" field.
9. Finally, submit your application by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Statement** (Artist Statement, text, required: false): This is a field where you can express your artistic vision, goals, and motivations for applying to the residency program. Please provide a clear and concise statement that showcases your artistic abilities and creativity.
* **Name** (Name, text, required: false): This field is for your name. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): This field is for your email address. Please enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): This field is for your phone number. Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Project Details** (Project Details, text, required: false): This is a field where you can describe your project, including its theme, medium, and goals. Please provide a clear and concise description of your project.
* **Project Proposals** (Project Proposals, text, required: false): This field is where you can outline your project proposals, including its timeline, location, and expected outcomes. Please provide a detailed plan for your project.
* **Residency Requirements** (Residency Requirements, select one, required: false): Please select the residency requirements that fit your project needs. You can choose from Residency Duration, Residency Location, and Residency Dates.
* **Residency Duration** (Residency Duration, select one, required: false): Please select the duration of your desired residency. You can choose from 1 month, 2 months, 3 months, 4 months, or 5 months.
* **Residency Location** (Residency Location, select one, required: false): Please select the location where you would like to reside. You can choose from Location 1, Location 2, or Location 3.
* **Residency Dates** (Residency Dates, select one, required: false): Please select the dates you would like to reside. You can choose from January 1 - December 31, January 1 - January 31, February 1 - March 31, March 1 - May 31, April 1 - June 31, May 1 - June 31, June 1 - August 31, July 1 - August 31, August 1 - September 30, September 1 - November 30, October 1 - December 31, September 1 - October 31, November 1 - December 31.
* **Residency Application Fee** (Residency Application Fee, number, required: false): Please enter the residency application fee you are willing to pay.
* **Residency Application Fee Included** (Residency Application Fee Included, select one, required: false): Please select if the residency application fee is included in your application or not.
* **Residency Application Fee Payment Method** (Residency Application Fee Payment Method, select multiple, required: false): Please select the payment method for your residency application fee. You can choose from Bank Transfer, Cash, or Check.
* **Residency Application Fee Refund Policy** (Residency Application Fee Refund Policy, select one, required: false): Please select the residency application fee refund policy that applies to your situation. You can choose from Full Refund, Partial Refund, or No Refund.
* **Residency Application Fee Refund Granting** (Residency Application Fee Refund Granting, select one, required: false): Please select when the residency application fee refund will be granted. You can choose from On Request or Automatically.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, required: false): Please submit your application by clicking this button.
