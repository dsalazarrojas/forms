# University of Adversity Podcast Guest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University of Adversity Podcast Guest Form is designed to collect information from potential guests to be considered for podcast episodes. Please fill out this form to be considered for a guest spot on the podcast.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Scroll down to the first page of the form and click on the "guest_form" field.
2. Type a brief description of your availability and suitability for the podcast in this field.
3. Fill out the "guest_name" field with your name.
4. Enter your "guest_email" field with your valid email address.
5. If applicable, fill out the "guest_phone" field with your phone number.
6. If applicable, fill out the "guest_website" field with your website or social media handle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **guest_form** (`guest_form`, text, optional): A brief description of your availability and suitability for the podcast. Please provide any relevant information about your background, expertise, or interests that would make you a good fit for the podcast.
* **guest_name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): Your name as the potential guest. Please ensure that your name is spelled correctly.
* **guest_email** (`guest_email`, email, optional): A valid email address where you can be reached for further communication. Please ensure that your email address is accurate and not a spam trap.
* **guest_phone** (`guest_phone`, text, optional): Your phone number, if you wish to be contacted via phone call. Please ensure that your phone number is accurate and not a spam or fake number.
* **guest_website** (`guest_website`, text, optional): Your website or social media handle. This can be a link to your personal or professional website, or your social media profile. Please ensure that the link is accurate and not a spam or phishing site.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the podcast team.
* We will review your application and be in touch with you shortly if we're interested in featuring you on the podcast.
