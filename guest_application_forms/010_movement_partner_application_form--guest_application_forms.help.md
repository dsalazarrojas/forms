# Movement Partner Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Movement Partner Application Form is a tool for users to apply to become a movement partner, allowing them to collaborate with us and contribute to our mission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information, including your email and phone number.
2. Select your motivations for becoming a movement partner.
3. Choose the areas of interest for you to be involved in as a movement partner.
4. Provide any collaboration requests you may have.
5. Submit your review date, review time, and any review comments.
6. Select your preferred review approval status and review actions.
7. Fill out your contact information, including your email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **user_inputs** (Text, Optional): Fill out this field with your personal details.
• **partner_inputs** (Text, Optional): Describe any relevant experience or skills you have that make you suitable for the movement partner role.
• **motivations** (Select One, Optional): Choose the reasons why you are interested in becoming a movement partner. Options include:
  • Option 1
  • Option 2
  • Option 3
• **areas_of_interest** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the areas you are interested in being involved in as a movement partner. Options include:
  • Option 1
  • Option 2
  • Option 3
• **collaboration_requests** (Text, Optional): If you have any specific collaboration requests, describe them here.
• **review_date** (Date, Optional): Select your preferred review date.
• **review_time** (Time, Optional): Select your preferred review time.
• **review_comments** (Note, Optional): Provide any additional comments or information for the reviewer.
• **review_approvals** (Select One, Optional): Choose your preferred review approval status. Options include:
  • Option 1
  • Option 2
• **review_status** (Select One, Optional): Choose your preferred review status. Options include:
  • Option 1
  • Option 2
• **review_actions** (Select One, Optional): Choose your preferred review actions. Options include:
  • Option 1
  • Option 2
• **email** (Email, Required): Fill out your email address.
• **phone** (Text, Optional): Fill out your phone number.
• **submission_date** (Date, Optional): Select your submission date.
