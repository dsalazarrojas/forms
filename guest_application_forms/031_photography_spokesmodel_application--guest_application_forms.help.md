# photography_spokesmodel_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photography spokesmodel application form is used to collect information from potential candidates for collaborations or projects. This form is designed to gather essential details about the candidate's experience, availability, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your basic contact information in the "User Input" section.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
4. Upload or provide the URL of your portfolio in the "photo_url" field.
5. Write a brief bio about yourself in the "bio" field.
6. Describe your experience in the "experience" field.
7. Enter your portfolio type (e.g., Website, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, or Other) in the "portfolio_type" field.
8. Provide the URL of your portfolio in the "portfolio_url" field.
9. Enter your location in the "location" field.
10. Enter your availability in the "availability" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: Enter your basic contact information, such as your name and any other relevant details.
* **email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **photo_url**: Upload or provide a URL for your portfolio or website.
* **bio**: Write a brief bio about your experience and background.
* **experience**: Describe your experience in the field of photography.
* **portfolio**: Describe your portfolio or provide a URL for it.
* **portfolio_type**: Select the type of portfolio you have (e.g., Website, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, or Other).
* **portfolio_url**: Provide the URL of your portfolio.
* **location**: Enter your location or the location you are available to work in.
* **availability**: Describe your availability and scheduling preferences.
* **rate**: Enter your rate or pricing structure.
* **rate_2**: Enter your rate or pricing structure for another type of service (if applicable).
* **available_rate**: Enter the rate for your availability or scheduling (if different).
* **available_rate_2**: Enter the rate for your second availability or scheduling (if different).
* **rate_type**: Select the type of services you offer (e.g., hourly, daily, or package-based).
* **rate_type_2**: Select the type of services you offer for another type of service (if applicable).
* **rate_type_3**: Select the type of services you offer for a third type of service (if applicable).
* **payment_terms**: Describe your payment terms or structure.
* **payment_terms_2**: Describe your payment terms or structure for another type of service (if applicable).
* **availability**: Describe your overall availability and scheduling preferences.
* **availability_2**: Describe your second overall availability and scheduling preferences (if applicable).
