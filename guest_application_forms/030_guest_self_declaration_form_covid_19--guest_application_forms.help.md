# Guest Self Declaration Form COVID 19 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Self Declaration Form COVID-19 is a mandatory form designed to collect essential information from guests for the purpose of verifying their health status, travel history, and other relevant details in compliance with COVID-19 guidelines and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Carefully read the instructions and questions on each page.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Enter your first name, last name, and email address in the corresponding fields.
4. Provide your date of arrival and contact phone number.
5. Indicate whether you have any travel documents and if you traveled by any mode of transport.
6. Select whether you have had any recent travel history or symptoms of COVID-19.
7. Enter your country of origin.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your given name as per your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Your surname as per your identification documents.
* **Email**: A valid email address where you can be contacted for further information or updates.
* **Date of Arrival**: The date you arrived at our location (airport, hotel, etc.).
* **Contact Phone**: Your contact phone number where you can be reached in case of emergencies or further information.
* **Address**: Your address where you can be contacted or sent further information.
* **Travel Documents**: Indicate if you have any travel documents such as a passport or visa.
* **Mode of Transport**: Indicate how you traveled to our location (airplane, bus, etc.).
* **Travel History**: Indicate whether you have traveled recently or have any symptoms of COVID-19.
* **Country**: Your country of origin.

## Tips

* Please be truthful and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, ask our staff for assistance.
* This form is a mandatory requirement for your stay at our location.
