# Community Housing Alliance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Housing Alliance Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals or families applying for housing through the alliance. This form will be used to determine eligibility and facilitate the placement process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal information section, which includes your full legal name as it appears on your ID, date of birth, contact email, and phone number.
2. Provide your current address, including the street, city, state, and postal code.
3. Describe your housing needs and preferences.
4. If applicable, indicate your primary income source (e.g., employment, benefits, or other).
5. Consent to share your application data with partners for placement purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant full name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your ID.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for notifications and follow-up purposes.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your mobile or landline number.
* **Household size** (`household_size`, number, required): Enter the number of people in your household.
* **Current address** (`current_address`, text, required): Enter your current address, including the street, city, state, and postal code.
* **Description of housing needs** (`housing_needs`, text, required): Describe your housing needs and preferences.
* **Primary income source** (`income_source`, text, optional): Indicate your primary income source (e.g., employment, benefits, or other).
* **Consent to share** (`consent_to_share`, select_one, required): Select "True" to agree to share your application data with partners for placement purposes.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have filled out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your contact email and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
