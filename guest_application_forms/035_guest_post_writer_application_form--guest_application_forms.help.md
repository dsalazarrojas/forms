# Guest Post Writer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from potential guest writers applying to write on our blog or website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form categories to ensure you're applying to the correct section.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Provide a clear and concise message explaining why you're interested in writing for our blog or website.
4. Attach any relevant writing samples or examples of your work.

* A descriptive title helps us understand your application.

* This helps us understand your online presence and reputation.

### 6. **Required Info** (`form_required_info`): Select the information you're willing to share with us.

### 7. **Name** (`name`): Enter your full name.
