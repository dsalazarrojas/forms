# Guest Contributor Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect demographic information from individuals interested in contributing to our platform as guests. It will help our team assess their eligibility and suitability for further information or services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form carefully, ensuring all required fields (marked as required) have a valid response before proceeding.
- If you experience any issues while submitting, please contact our administration team for assistance.
- Make sure to fill out the form only once. Re-submission is not required if you update any fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`first_name`)
*   **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.

### Last Name (`last_name`)
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.

### Email (`email`)
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you have access to and can receive notifications from us.

### Phone (`phone`)
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number, including any country code if applicable.

### Website (`website`)
*   **Website**: Enter a website or landing page that represents your work or identity.

### Message (`message`)
*   **Message**: Briefly describe your interest in contributing as a guest to our platform.

### Category (`category`)
*   **Category**: Select the category that best describes your area of expertise:
    *   Category One
    *   Category Two
    *   Category Three

### Other Category (`category_other`)
*   **Other Category**: If the category that best describes your expertise is not listed, provide a description of it.

### Accept (`accept`)
*   **Accept**: Select this option to confirm you understand and agree to our terms of use.
    *   Accept
    *   Decline

### Submit
*   **Submit**: Once all required fields are filled out, click this button to submit your application.
