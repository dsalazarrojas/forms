# Film Critic Accreditation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential film critics to determine their eligibility for accreditation to film-related events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the 'Media Details' section to provide general information about yourself and your media type.
2. In the 'Accreditation Information' section, provide detailed information about your experience, review frequency, and event specifics.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Media Details**: 
  + **Type**: Select the type of media you work with (e.g. Film, Event).
  + **Roles**: Choose the roles you have experience with (e.g. Film Critic, Event Manager, Curator).
  + **Experience (Years)**: Enter the number of years you have experience in the film industry.
  + **Review Frequency**: Indicate how often you review (e.g. Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
  + **Review Platforms**: Select the platforms where you publish your reviews (e.g. YouTube, Vimeo, Vimeo Live).
  + **Review Keywords**: Enter any keywords related to your reviews.
  + **Portfolio Link**: Enter a link to your portfolio, if applicable.
  + **Portfolio**: Choose the types of content you produce (e.g. Review, Feature, Interview).
  + **Email**: Enter your contact email.
  + **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
  + **Address**: Enter your physical address.
  + **Bio**: Enter a short description about yourself.

* **Accreditation Information**: 
  + **Event Type**: Indicate the type of events you have experience with (e.g. Film Festival, Cinema Event, Film Industry Event).
  + **Event Dates**: Enter the dates of the events you have experience with.
  + **Event Description**: Describe the events you have experience with.
  + **Event Locations**: Enter the locations of the events you have experience with.
  + **Experience Years**: Enter the number of years you have experience in the film industry.
  + **Experience Frequency**: Indicate how often you review (e.g. Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
  + **Review Experience Years**: Enter the number of years you have experience reviewing.
  + **Review Experience Frequency**: Indicate how often you review (e.g. Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).

## Tips

* Double-check your information for accuracy to avoid any issues with your application.
* Ensure you provide all required information to increase your chances of accreditation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
