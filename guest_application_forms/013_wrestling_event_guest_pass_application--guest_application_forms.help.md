# Wrestling Event Guest Pass Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from guests attending a wrestling event. It helps the event organizers verify attendee details and ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone at the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **General Information** page with any relevant details about the event or the guest (if applicable).
2. Enter your valid **Email** address to receive communication from the event organizers.
3. Provide your **Affiliation** to help the event organizers understand your connection to the event.
4. Select the **Event** type that best describes the purpose of your visit (e.g., Event, Club, Promotion).
5. Read and agree to the **Rules Acknowledgment 2** to ensure you understand the event's rules and guidelines.
6. Optionally, provide your **Phone Number**, **Address**, **Emergency Contact**, and **Additional Notes** if required or if you want to provide more information.
7. If you need emergency services, select the services you require (e.g., **First aid**, **Emergency services**, **Security**) in the **Emergency Services** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This page is for any additional information about the event or guest that the event organizers may need to know.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive important updates, reminders, and information about the event.
* **Affiliation**: Provide your affiliation or connection to the event, such as being a competitor, spectator, or sponsor.
* **Event Name**: Optionally provide the name of the event you will be attending.
* **Dates of Attendance**: Enter the dates you will be attending the event, if different from the event date.
* **Rules Acknowledgment**: Read and agree to the event's rules and guidelines to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by the event organizers or event staff.
* **Address**: Optionally provide your home or office address for contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of someone who should be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information you think is relevant to your attendance.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you will be attending (e.g., Event, Club, Promotion).
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you will be attending.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event you will be attending.
* **Emergency Services**: Select the emergency services you require during the event (e.g., First aid, Emergency services, Security).

## Tips
* Double-check your email address is accurate and valid.
* Ensure you provide accurate information about your affiliation and event details.
* Review the event's rules and guidelines before agreeing to them.
* Use the **Emergency Services** field only if you truly require emergency services during the event.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the event organizers.
