# documentary_screening_series_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from documentary filmmakers for a screening series.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the title of your documentary.
2. Provide details about your film, including any relevant information about the project.
3. Enter information about the screener, including any relevant details.
4. List any assets required for the screening, such as equipment or personnel.
5. Enter the email address where you would like to receive follow-up information.
6. Click "Submit" to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, required: false): Enter the title of your documentary.
* **Film Details** (`film_details`, required: false): Provide details about your film, including any relevant information about the project.
* **Screener Details** (`screener_details`, required: false): Enter information about the screener, including any relevant details.
* **Assets** (`assets`, required: false): List any assets required for the screening, such as equipment or personnel.
* **Submit** (`submit`, required: false): Click "Submit" to send your application.
* **Email To** (`email_to`, required: false): Enter the email address where you would like to receive follow-up information.
