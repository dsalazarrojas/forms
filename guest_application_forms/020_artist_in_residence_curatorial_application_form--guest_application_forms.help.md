# Artist In Residence Curatorial Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for artists to apply for an artist-in-residence program. We will use the information provided to evaluate your eligibility and suitability for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Make sure to answer all questions thoroughly and clearly.
3. Attach a portfolio or work samples as described in the "Portfolio or Work Samples" field.
4. Submit the form once you have reviewed and double-checked all the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (Required): Please enter your name as it is spelled and formatted on any official documents or records.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your primary email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your primary phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Artist Website or Portfolio URL** (Optional): If you have a professional website or online portfolio, please provide the URL.
* **Primary Art Medium or Discipline** (Required): Select the primary medium or discipline you work with (e.g. Painting, Sculpture, Photography, etc.).
* **Years of Professional Experience** (Required): Enter the number of years you have been working professionally as an artist.
* **Education Background** (Optional): Provide information about your education background, including degrees and institutions.
* **Previous Artist-in-Residence Programs** (Optional): If you have participated in previous artist-in-residence programs, please describe your experience.
* **Curatorial or Exhibition Experience** (Optional): Describe any curatorial or exhibition experience you have.
* **Preferred Residency Duration - Months** (Required): Enter the number of months you are seeking for the residency.
* **Proposed Project or Exhibition Concept** (Required): Describe your project or exhibition concept in 200-300 words.
* **Project Goals and Outcomes** (Required): Explain what you hope to accomplish and achieve through this residency.
* **Community Engagement Plans** (Optional): Describe how you plan to involve the community in your project or exhibition.
* **Facilities and Resources Needed** (Required): List any facilities or resources you need to complete your project.
* **Estimated Project Budget** (Optional): Enter an estimated budget for your project.
* **Plans for Public Presentation** (Required): Describe your plans for presenting your project or exhibition to the public.
* **References or Recommenders** (Optional): List any references or recommenders who can vouch for your artistic abilities and qualifications.
* **Portfolio or Work Samples Attached** (Required): Select 'Yes' if you have attached a portfolio or work samples, or 'No' if you have not.
* **Additional Artist Statement** (Optional): Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your application.
