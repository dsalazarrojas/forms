# Property Condition Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document the condition of a property. It is intended for inspectors to report on the status of a property, including its overall condition, any damage, and recommended next actions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the property address.
2. Select the property type (residential, commercial, or mixed use).
3. Enter the date of the inspection.
4. Enter the date of the previous inspection (if applicable).
5. Enter the inspector's name.
6. Enter the inspector's license number.
7. Describe the property condition, including any notable issues or defects.
8. Rate the property condition and provide justification for your rating.
9. Describe any property damage and its severity.
10. Identify any defects in the property.
11. Provide a URL for photos or videos of the property (if applicable).
12. Update the status of the property condition report.
13. Enter the date the report was completed.
14. Enter the due date for the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property being inspected.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of property (residential, commercial, or mixed use).
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`, date, required): Enter the date the inspection was conducted.
* **Date of Previous Inspection** (`date_of_previous_inspection`, date, optional): Enter the date of the previous inspection (if applicable).
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Inspector License Number** (`inspector_license_number`, text, optional): Enter your inspector license number.
* **Property Condition Notes** (`property_condition_notes`, text, optional): Describe the overall condition of the property.
* **Photos** (`photos`, note, optional): Upload any relevant photos of the property.
* **Videos** (`videos`, note, optional): Upload any relevant videos of the property.
* **Previous Inspection Results** (`previous_inspection_results`, text, optional): Describe any previous inspection results or reports.
* **Next Actions** (`next_actions`, text, optional): Describe any recommended next actions or steps.
* **Property Condition** (`property_condition`, text, optional): Describe the current condition of the property.
* **Property Condition Rating** (`property_condition_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the property condition (0-5).
* **Property Condition Rating Justification** (`property_condition_rating_justification`, text, optional): Provide justification for your rating.
* **Property Condition Recommendations** (`property_condition_recommendations`, text, optional): Describe any recommended actions or steps.
* **Property Damage** (`property_damage`, text, optional): Describe any property damage or issues.
* **Property Damage Rating** (`property_damage_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the severity of the property damage (1-5).
* **Property Damage Justification** (`property_damage_justification`, text, optional): Provide justification for your rating.
* **Property Defects** (`property_defects`, text, optional): Describe any property defects or issues.
* **Photos URL** (`photos_url`, text, optional): Enter a URL for any photos of the property.
* **Videos URL** (`videos_url`, text, optional): Enter a URL for any videos of the property.
* **Property Condition Report Status** (`property_condition_report_status`, select_one, optional): Update the status of the property condition report (Draft, In Progress, Complete).
* **Property Condition Report Date** (`property_condition_report_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the report was completed.
* **Property Condition Report Due Date** (`property_condition_report_due_date`, date, optional): Enter the due date for the report.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the person or organization assigned to the report.
* **Property Condition Rating Justification** (`property_condition_rating_justification`, text, optional): Provide additional context or explanation for your rating.
