# Home Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about a home assessment. It is intended to be filled out by a user who is inspecting or evaluating a home in some way.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Home Address" field with the address of the home being assessed.
2. Answer the "Condition" question with a "Yes" or "No", depending on the condition of the home.
3. If necessary, add a note about the condition in the "Condition Note" field.
4. List any equipment present in the home in the "Equipment List" field.
5. Add any additional notes about the assessment in the "Notes" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you want to be contacted for further information.
7. Fill in the "Date" field with the date of the assessment.
8. Fill in the "Time" field with the time of the assessment.
9. Sign your name in the "Signature" field, if required.
10. Upload any supporting documents or files in the "Uploaded Documents" field.
11. Enter the username of the person who submitted this form in the "Username" field, if required.
