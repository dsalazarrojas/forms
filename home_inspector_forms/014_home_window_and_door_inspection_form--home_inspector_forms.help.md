# Home Window And Door Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Window And Door Inspection Form is designed to collect information about the condition of windows and doors in a property. This form will help the home inspector gather relevant data to inform the homeowner or property owner about any necessary repairs or replacements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name and title in the "Inspector Name" field. This is the name of the person conducting the inspection.
2. Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field. This is the address of the property being inspected.
3. Select the type of property in the "Property Type" field. This will help the inspector understand the property's characteristics.
4. Choose the type of windows from the "Windows Type" dropdown menu. This will help the inspector understand the type of windows installed.
5. Choose the type of doors from the "Doors Type" dropdown menu. This will help the inspector understand the type of doors installed.
6. Enter the date of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field. This is the date the inspection was conducted.
7. Enter the time of the inspection in the "Inspection Time" field. This is the time the inspection was conducted.
8. Describe the condition of the windows in the "Window Condition" field. This will help the inspector identify any potential issues.
9. Describe the condition of the doors in the "Door Condition" field. This will help the inspector identify any potential issues.
10. Enter any general notes about the inspection in the "General Notes" field. This can include any observations or comments about the property.
11. Enter any comments about the inspection in the "Comments" field. This can include any additional information or feedback about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required): Enter your name and title as the inspector conducting the survey.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property being inspected.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, text, required): Choose the type of property (e.g. single family, condominium, etc.)
* **Windows Type** (`windows_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of windows installed (e.g. Single Hung, Double Hung, Casement, etc.)
* **Doors Type** (`doors_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of doors installed (e.g. Hinge, Slider, etc.)
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Window Condition** (`window_condition`, text, required): Describe the condition of the windows.
* **Door Condition** (`door_condition`, text, optional): Describe the condition of the doors.
* **General Notes** (`general_notes`, note, optional): Enter any general notes about the inspection.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about the inspection.
