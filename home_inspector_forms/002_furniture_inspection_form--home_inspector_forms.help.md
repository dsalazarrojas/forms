# Furniture Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Furniture Inspection Form is used to collect information about the condition and quality of inspected furniture items. This form is intended to be used internally and is crucial for assessing the condition and quality of the inspected furniture.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on the "Seller Information" page.
2.  Fill in the seller's name and other relevant details.
3.  Move on to the next page.
4.  Select the condition of the inspected furniture.
5.  Choose the material of the inspected furniture item (Wood, Metal, Fabric, or Other).
6.  Enter the price and date received for the inspected furniture item.
7.  Provide the location where the inspected furniture was found.
8.  Describe the inspected item in the "Description" field.
9.  Sign off on the inspection with your signature.
10. Enter your contact information and any additional comments in the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seller Information (`seller_info`, text, required):** Enter the seller's name and other relevant details about the inspected furniture item.
* **Condition (`condition`, text, required):** Describe the condition of the inspected furniture item (e.g., new, used, damaged).
* **Material (`material`, select_one, required):** Choose the type of material used to make the inspected furniture item (Wood, Metal, Fabric, or Other).
* **Price and Date Received (`price_info`, text, required):** Enter the price of the inspected furniture item and the date it was received.
* **Location (`location`, text, required):** Specify the location where the inspected furniture was found.
* **Description (`description`, text, required):** Describe the inspected furniture item in detail.
* **Signature (`signature`, text, required):** Sign off on the inspection to confirm.
* **Contact Information (`contact_info`, text, required):** Enter your contact information for any follow-up queries.
* **Comments (`comments`, text, required):** Enter any additional comments or remarks regarding the inspected furniture item.
* **Date Inspected (`date_inspected`, date, required):** Enter the date when the inspection was conducted.
* **Time Inspected (`time_inspected`, time, required):** Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Other Information (`other_info`, text, optional):** Enter any other relevant information about the inspected furniture item.
* **None 1 (`none`, text, required):** This field is not intended for end-users, please do not fill this.
* **None 2 (`none_2`, select_one, required):** Choose whether the inspected item is Yes or No.
* **None 3 (`none_3`, select_multiple, optional):** This is not intended for end-users, please do not select any option here.
* **None 4 (`none_4`, time, optional):** This field is not intended for end-users, please do not fill this.
* **None 5 (`none_5`, number, optional):** This field is not intended for end-users, please do not fill this.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately for a successful inspection.
* If unsure about any field, refer to this help guide for clarity.
* If you have any further questions, please contact the relevant department for assistance.
