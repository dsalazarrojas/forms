# Home Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Safety Checklist form is a tool to help you identify potential safety hazards in your home and gather comments or special notes on each item.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  In the "Room List" section, list the rooms in your home that you are concerned about safety hazards. Select all the rooms that apply.
2.  In the "Safety Items" section, select all the safety items that apply to each room listed above.
3.  In the "Comments" section, enter any comments or special notes about the safety hazards you have identified.
4.  In the "Safety Items Comments" section, enter any comments or special notes about the safety items you have selected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Safety Checklist** (`home_safety_checklist`, text, optional): Enter a brief title or description of your home safety check list.
* **Room List** (`room_list`, select_multiple, optional): List each room in your home that you are concerned about safety hazards, such as Basement, Bedroom, Bathroom, Den, etc.
* **Safety Items** (`safety_items`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the safety items that apply to each room listed above, such as Electrical, Flooring, Handrails, etc.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or special notes about the safety hazards you have identified.
* **Safety Items Comments** (`safety_items_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or special notes about the safety items you have selected.
