# lawn_mower_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to ensure the lawn mower's engine is in good working condition and that there is sufficient fuel level for optimal use.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply navigate through each page (Introduction, Engine Condition, and Fuel Level) and answer the questions accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Introduction**
(engine_condition, text, required: false)

This page provides a brief overview of the lawn mower inspection process and explains what to expect on the next page.

### **Engine Condition**
(engine_condition, text, required: false)

Please describe the current condition of the lawn mower's engine. Consider factors such as:

* Leaks or oil spills
* Rust or corrosion on the engine block or cylinder head
* Evidence of overheating or excessive wear
* Any other notable issues

### **Fuel Level**
(fuel_level, number, required: false)

Please indicate the current fuel level of the lawn mower. Use the following measurements:

* Low (0-1/4 full tank)
* Medium (1/4-1/2 full tank)
* Full (above 1/2 full tank)

Choose the correct level to ensure sufficient fuel for optimal operation.

## Tips

* Make sure to inspect the mower thoroughly and check for any other issues not covered on this form.
* Use a consistent method of measurement for the fuel level.
* If you're unsure about any aspect of the inspection, consult a more experienced colleague or a professional mechanic.
