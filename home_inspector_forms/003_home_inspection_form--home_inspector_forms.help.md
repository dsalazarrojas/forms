# Home Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for conducting home inspections, gathering information about the property's address, date of birth of occupants, phone number, email, inspection type, inspector's name, inspection results, inspection date, agent's name, inspector's license, and other relevant details. The goal is to collect comprehensive data to aid in assessing the property's condition and identifying potential issues.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields: "inspection type" and "inspection date" are marked as required. Ensure you select the correct inspection type from the options provided and enter the correct inspection date.
2.  Enter the property's address in the "address field".
3.  Enter the date of birth of the property occupants.
4.  Enter the phone number and email of the property contact person.
5.  Enter the inspection results in the "inspection results" field.
6.  Enter the name of the inspector and any other relevant details.
7.  Enter any additional information regarding the inspection in the "inspection results" field.
8.  Enter the agent's name (if applicable).
9.  Enter any information about the inspector's license (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **address_field** (text, required/optional):
    Enter the property's address where the inspection will take place. This could be a single-family home, a commercial building, or an industrial facility.
*   **date of birth** (date, required/optional):
    Enter the date of birth of the property occupants, if applicable.
*   **phone_number** (text, required/optional):
    Enter the phone number of the property contact person.
*   **email** (email, required/optional):
    Enter the email address of the property contact person.
*   **inspection type** (select_one, required):
    Select the type of inspection being conducted. This could be a residential inspection, commercial inspection, or industrial inspection.
*   **inspector name** (text, required/optional):
    Enter your name as the inspector conducting the inspection.
*   **inspection results** (text, required/optional):
    Provide detailed results of the inspection, including any issues found and recommendations for improvement.
*   **inspection date** (date, required):
    Enter the date of the inspection, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **agent name** (text, required/optional):
    Enter the name of the real estate agent or agent's representative (if applicable).
*   **inspector license** (text, required/optional):
    Provide any information about your inspector's license, if required.
*   **other options** (text, required/optional):
    Select "other (please specify)" if none of the other options fit the inspection type.
