# Standing Order Mandate Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Standing Order Mandate Form is used to define a standing order for automatic payments or transactions with your bank account. This form is used to authorise your bank to make regular payments or transactions from your account, and to specify the details of the mandate, including the amount, frequency, and duration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your account information, including your account number and account type.
2. Define the mandate details, including the amount, frequency, and duration of the automatic payments or transactions.
3. Specify the type of mandate you wish to establish.
4. Add any additional standing order details, if necessary.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required: false)
This is the title of the form, which should not be filled in. Please ignore this field.

*   **Account Number** (`account_number`, `number`, required: false)
    This is your bank account number, which is required to complete a standing order mandate.
*   **Account Type** (`account_type`, `select_one`, required: false)
    Please select the type of account you wish to use for the standing order mandate. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
*   **Bank Name** (`bank_name`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter the name of your bank or financial institution.
*   **Mandate Amount** (`mandate_amount`, `number`, required: false)
    Specify the amount you wish to transfer or pay out regularly.
*   **Mandate Frequency** (`mandate_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false)
    Select how often you wish to make the automatic payments or transactions. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
*   **Mandate Start Date** (`mandate_start_date`, `date`, required: false)
    Enter the date when you wish the standing order mandate to start.
*   **Mandate End Date** (`mandate_end_date`, `date`, required: false)
    Enter the date when you wish the standing order mandate to end.
*   **Bank Account Holder** (`bank_account_holder`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter your name as the account holder.
*   **Mandate Type** (`mandate_type`, `select_one`, required: false)
    Please select the type of mandate you wish to establish. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
*   **Standing Order Details** (`standing_order_details`, `text`, required: false)
    Add any additional details about the standing order mandate, if necessary.
