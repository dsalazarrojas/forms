# Financial Services Career Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information about your qualifications, experience, and skills for potential positions within our banking services department.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the form in the order of the pages presented.
2. For each field, ensure you have the necessary information ready before proceeding.
3. Be as thorough as possible when providing information, as it will be used to evaluate your suitability for the role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Candidate Details
* **Candidate Details** (id: 1, text, required: false): This field is for providing your basic contact information.

* **Resume Upload** (id: 2, text, required: true): Please upload your resume in PDF or docx format. Ensure it is up-to-date and accurately represents your experience.

* **Work History** (id: 3, text, required: false): This field is for describing your previous work experience. Be as detailed as possible, including job title, company, dates of employment, and job responsibilities.

* **Education** (id: 4, text, required: false): List your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.

* **Experience** (id: 5, text, required: false): Describe your relevant experience, including job title, company, dates of employment, and job responsibilities.

* **References** (id: 6, text, required: false): Provide information about your professional references, including name, job title, and contact information.

* **Skills** (id: 7, select_multiple, required: true): Choose all the relevant skills you possess from the list provided. This will help us understand your qualifications for the role.

* **Certifications** (id: 8, text, required: false): If applicable, list any relevant certifications you hold.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out completely.
* Be honest and accurate when providing information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
