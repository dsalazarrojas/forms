# Premium Credit Card Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Premium Credit Card Application Form is used to collect information for a premium credit card application. This form is used to gather relevant details from the user to determine their eligibility and process their application.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal and employment details.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
3. Submit the form when you have finished filling it out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents, such as your passport or driver's license.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`3`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Home Address** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your home address as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact Phone** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`6`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Social Security Number** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter your social security number as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employment Status** (`8`, `text`, required): Select your current employment status from the options provided.
* **Annual Income** (`9`, `number`, required): Enter your annual income.
* **Employment Length** (`10`, `number`, required): Enter the length of your current employment.
* **Credit Score** (`11`, `number`, required): Select your credit score from the options provided.
* **Employment Type** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Select your employment type from the options provided.
* **Job Title** (`13`, `text`, required): Enter your job title.
* **Work Address** (`14`, `text`, required): Enter your work address as it appears on your official documents.
* **Credit Limit** (`15`, `number`, required): Select your desired credit limit from the options provided.
* **Credit Card Type** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of credit card you are interested in from the options provided.
* **Bank Name** (`17`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the bank you are applying to.
* **Branch Name** (`18`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the branch you are applying to.
* **Account Number** (`19`, `text`, required): Enter your account number.
* **Routing Number** (`20`, `text`, required): Enter your routing number.
* **CVV** (`21`, `text`, required): Enter your CVV (Card Verification Value) number.
* **Expiration Date** (`22`, `date`, required): Enter your credit card expiration date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **CVV Type** (`23`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of CVV you are interested in from the options provided.
* **Bank Branch** (`24`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the bank branch you are applying to.
* **Submit** (`25`, `select_one`, required): Select the option to submit your application.

Note: Some fields may be optional, but all required fields must be completed accurately and truthfully for the application process to proceed. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the bank for assistance.
