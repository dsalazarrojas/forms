# Loan Guarantee Participation Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for individuals who want to participate in a loan guarantee program. It's designed to gather necessary information to evaluate your eligibility and assist in the loan guarantee process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully, ensuring you've filled out all required fields.
2. Enter your information accurately and truthfully.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **application_form** (Page 1, text): Enter a brief description of your loan guarantee participation application.
* **guarantor_info** (Page 2, text): Provide information about yourself as the guarantor, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
* **applicant_name** (Page 3, text): Enter your name as the applicant for the loan guarantee participation.
* **loan_amount** (Page 4, number): Enter the total loan amount you're applying for.
* **loan_period** (Page 5, number): Enter the loan period or duration you're applying for.
* **guarantor** (Page 6, select_one): Choose from the list to select your guarantor (Mr. John, Mrs. Jane, Dr. Jim).
* **guarantee_amount** (Page 7, number): Enter the amount you're willing to guarantee for the loan.
* **collateral** (Page 8, text): Describe any collateral you're offering for the loan.
* **guarantor_address** (Page 9, text): Enter your address as the guarantor.
* **guarantor_phone** (Page 10, text): Enter your phone number as the guarantor.
* **guarantor_email** (Page 11, email): Enter your email address as the guarantor.
* **bank_branch** (Page 12, text): Enter the name of your bank branch.
* **loan_term** (Page 13, time): Enter the loan term or duration you're applying for.
* **collateral_value** (Page 14, number): Enter the value of the collateral you're offering.
* **loan_interest_rate** (Page 15, text): Enter the interest rate of the loan.
* **loan_fees** (Page 16, number): Enter any loan fees associated with the loan.
* **loan_security** (Page 17, note): Enter any loan security information (e.g., collateral, insurance, etc.).
* **notes** (Page 18, note): Enter any additional notes or comments about your loan guarantee participation application.

Note: Please ensure you've reviewed all fields carefully and entered accurate information to facilitate a smooth evaluation process.
