# Loan Restructuring Approval Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loan Restructuring Approval Application form is used to apply for modifications to your existing loan terms. This form is designed to gather all necessary information to review and approve your loan restructuring request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your loan details such as Current Outstanding Balance, Loan Amount, and Restructured Amount.
2. Specify the new interest rate and term for the restructured loan.
3. Provide the reason for loan restructuring.
4. Add any additional comments or notes as needed.
5. Specify the approved date and by whom, if applicable.
6. Select the loan status and review date, if applicable.
7. Add any comments or notes as needed.
8. Select the reviewed by and comments reviewed, if applicable.
9. Finally, provide your contact information (Email, Phone, Address, and Zip).
10. Review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Loan Restructuring Form** (`loan_restructuring_form`): A text field where you can enter any additional comments or notes regarding your loan restructuring request.
* **Date Restructured** (`date_restructured`): The date on which the loan was restructured. This is a required field, but please note that it cannot be modified once submitted.
* **Current Outstanding Balance** (`current_outstanding_balance`): Enter the current outstanding balance on your loan. This is a required field to understand your loan's current state.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`): Enter the original loan amount.
* **Restructured Amount** (`restructured_amount`): Enter the new loan amount after the restructuring.
* **Current Interest Rate** (`current_interest_rate`): Enter the current interest rate of your loan.
* **Restructured Interest Rate** (`restructured_interest_rate`): Enter the new interest rate for the restructured loan.
* **Restructured Term** (`restructured_term`): Enter the new term for the restructured loan.
* **Reason for Restructuring** (`reason_for_restructuring`): Provide a clear and concise reason for loan restructuring.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or notes regarding your loan restructuring request.
* **Date Approved** (`date_approved`): The date on which the loan was approved, if applicable.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`): The person or team that approved the loan restructuring request, if applicable.
* **Comments** (`comments_approved`): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the approved loan restructuring.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`): The date on which the loan restructuring request was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): The person or team that submitted the loan restructuring request.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes regarding your loan restructuring request.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your contact address.
* **Zip** (`zip`): Enter your contact zip code.
* **Loan Status** (`loan_status`): Select the current status of your loan.
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`): Add any additional comments or notes regarding your loan.
* **Date Review** (`date_review`): The date on which the loan was reviewed, if applicable.
* **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`): The person or team that reviewed the loan restructuring request, if applicable.
* **Comments Reviewed** (`comments_reviewed`): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the reviewed loan.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your address.
* **Zip** (`zip`): Enter your zip code.
