# Digital Mortgage Platform Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for the digital mortgage platform. It will help us understand your needs and preferences to provide a personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details in the **Applicant Details** section.
2. Provide your contact information in the **Contact Info** section.
3. Enter your loan amount and select your **Collateral Type** and **Collateral Value**.
4. Select your **Property Status** from the available options.
5. Choose your **Mortgage Term**.
6. Select your **Property Type** from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details** (Page 1): This section is used to collect your personal details, such as your name and address. Please enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Info** (Page 1): This section is used to collect your contact information, such as your email and phone number. Please enter your contact information accurately.
* **Loan Amount** (Page 1): This field is used to enter the amount you are looking to borrow. Please enter a numerical value.
* **Collateral Type** (Page 1): This field is used to select whether you have collateral or not. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.
* **Collateral Value** (Page 1): This field is used to enter the value of your collateral. Please enter a numerical value.
* **Property Status** (Page 2): This field is used to select the status of your property. Please select 'Active' or 'Inactive' from the dropdown menu.
* **Mortgage Term** (Page 2): This field is used to select the term of your mortgage. Please note that this field is currently not functional and will be filled out by the platform.
* **Property Type** (Page 2): This field is used to select the type of property you are applying for. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.
