# Banking Leads Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in banking services, allowing the bank to tailor their services to meet the needs and preferences of potential customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
2. Ensure that you enter accurate and relevant information for each field.
3. Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure that everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`lead_full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`lead_email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`lead_phone`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`lead_address`, text, required): Enter your street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Product Interest** (`interest_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all the banking products you are interested in (e.g., Checking Account, Savings Account).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Choose your current employment status (e.g., Employed Full-Time, Employed Part-Time).
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your current or previous employer.
* **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, select_one, required): Select your annual gross income range.
* **Current Bank** (`current_bank`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your current bank.
* **Reason for Switching** (`reason_for_switch`, select_one, optional): If applicable, choose the reason you are considering switching banks (e.g., Better Rates, Better Service).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would prefer to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone Call).
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`, select_one, optional): If applicable, choose a preferred time to be contacted.
* **Decision Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, required): Select when you plan to decide on a banking solution.
* **Existing Relationship** (`existing_relationship`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have an existing relationship with the bank.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Choose how you heard about the bank (e.g., Online Search, Social Media).
* **Additional Services Interest** (`additional_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional banking services you are interested in (e.g., Online Banking, Mobile Banking).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any specific requirements or requests you have.
* **Consent to Contact** (`consent_to_contact`, select_one, required): Indicate your preference to be contacted by the bank.
* **Privacy Policy Acceptance** (`privacy_policy_acceptance`, select_one, required): Accept the bank's privacy policy.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): This field is automatically filled in upon form submission.
