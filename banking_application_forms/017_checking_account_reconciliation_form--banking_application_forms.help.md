# Checking Account Reconciliation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the reconciliation of checking account balances between a financial institution's internal ledger and the bank statement. It guides the user through three steps: Step 1 - Bank Statement Balance, Step 2 - Book Balance, and Step 3 - Final Reconciliation.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Bank Statement Balance
- **Financial Institution Name**: Enter the name of the financial institution that issued the bank statement.
- **Statement Date**: Enter the date of the bank statement, using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Account Name / Reference**: Enter the account name or reference, e.g. Main Operating Account.
- **Ending Balance shown on Statement ($)**: Enter the balance as reported on the bank statement.
- **Total Outstanding Deposits ($)**: Enter deposits not yet shown on the statement, if any.
- **Total Outstanding Checks / Withdrawals ($)**: Enter checks or withdrawals not yet cleared, if any.
- **Adjusted Bank Balance ($)**: Calculate the adjusted balance by adding outstanding deposits and subtracting outstanding checks or withdrawals from the ending balance.

### Step 2: Book Balance
- **Current General Ledger / Book Balance ($)**: Enter the current balance on the internal ledger as of the statement date.
- **Unrecorded Bank Fees / Interest ($)**: Enter fees or interest not accounted for on the internal ledger, if any.
- **Adjusted Book Balance ($)**: Calculate the adjusted balance by subtracting unrecorded fees or interest from the current book balance.

### Step 3: Final Reconciliation
- **Difference Amount ($)**: Calculate the difference between the adjusted bank balance and the adjusted book balance.
- **Reconciliation Status**: Select the status of the reconciliation: Fully Reconciled (Balanced), Unbalanced (Discrepancy), or Pending Investigation.
- **Explanation of Discrepancies**: Enter any discrepancies or comments, if applicable.
- **Reconciled By (Name)**: Enter the person responsible for the reconciliation.
- **Date of Review**: Enter the date of the review or audit.
- **Reviewer Acknowledgment**: Select "Reviewed and Approved" or "Other" to acknowledge the review.
