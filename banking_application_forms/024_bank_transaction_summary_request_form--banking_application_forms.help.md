# Bank Transaction Summary Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a summary of your bank transactions within a specific time period. The summary can be tailored to include various types of transactions, formats, and delivery methods to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required information, including your name, email, phone number, mailing address, account number, and purpose of request.
2. Select the correct account type and transaction period.
3. Choose the types of transactions to include in the summary.
4. Select the format and delivery method for the summary.
5. Confirm that you are aware of any applicable fees.
6. Confirm that you authorize this request.
7. Enter the date of your request.
8. Add any additional notes or requirements if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Account Holder Full Name** (`account_holder_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your account.
* **Email Address** (`account_holder_email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`account_holder_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`account_holder_address`): Enter your street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`): Enter your full account number.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`): Select the type of account you hold (Personal Checking, Personal Savings, Business Checking, etc.).
* **Transaction Period Start** (`transaction_period_start`): Enter the start date of the transaction period you want to summary.
* **Transaction Period End** (`transaction_period_end`): Enter the end date of the transaction period you want to summary.
* **Summary Type** (`summary_type`): Select the type of summary you need (All Transactions, Deposits Only, Withdrawals Only, etc.).
* **Transaction Categories** (`transaction_categories`): Select the categories of transactions you want to include (Income, Bills and Utilities, etc.).
* **Format Preference** (`format_preference`): Select how you want the summary to be formatted (PDF, Excel, CSV, etc.).
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`): Select how the summary should be delivered to you (Secure Email, Online Banking, etc.).
* **Include Running Balance** (`include_balance`): Select whether to include the balance after each transaction.
* **Include Transaction Categories** (`include_categorization`): Select whether to auto-categorize transactions.
* **Minimum Amount Filter** (`minimum_amount_filter`): Enter the minimum amount for transactions to be included.
* **Purpose of Request** (`purpose_of_request`): Select the reason for your request (Budget Planning, Tax Preparation, etc.).
* **Third Party Access** (`third_party_access`): Select whether to authorize third-party access.
* **Third Party Name** (`third_party_name`): Enter the name of the third party to be authorized.
* **Third Party Email** (`third_party_email`): Enter the email address of the third party to be authorized.
* **Fee Acknowledgment** (`fee_acknowledgment`): Confirm that you are aware of any applicable fees.
* **Authorization** (`authorization`): Confirm that you authorize this request.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`): Enter the date of your request.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Enter any additional notes or requirements if needed.
