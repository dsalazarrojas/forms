# Housing Investment Loan Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Housing Investment Loan Application Form is designed to gather essential information from applicants to evaluate their creditworthiness and loan eligibility for housing investment loans.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section accurately and comprehensively, taking into account that required fields are marked as "required".
2. Ensure you provide all necessary information to support your loan application.
3. Review each field for its specific purpose and corresponding explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, required: false): Provide your name and contact details to help us reach out to you.
* **Property Information** (`property_info`, text, required: false): Enter your property address, city, state, postal code, and country for us to understand your property's location.
* **Financial Information** (`financial_info`, text, required: false): Please provide your employment status, income, and years of employment for us to understand your financial capacity.
* **Loan Details** (`loan_details`, text, required: false): State the purpose of your loan, the loan amount requested, and other relevant loan details.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the loan amount you are seeking.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, select_one, required: false): Choose the loan term that suits your needs (3 years, 5 years, 10 years, 15 years, 20 years).
* **Loan Type** (`loan_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of loan you are interested in (Primary Residence, Investment, Other).
* **Employment Status** (`applicant_employment_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose how you are employed (Full-time, Part-time, Self-Employed).
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of property you are seeking a loan for (Single Family, Duplex, Townhouse, Condo).
* **Loan Purpose** (`loan_purpose`, select_multiple, required: false): State the purpose of your loan (Home Improvement, Debt Consolidation, Other).
* **Applicant Income** (`applicant_income`, number, required: false): Enter your income for us to understand your financial capacity.
* **Applicant Employer** (`applicant_employer`, text, required: false): Enter your employer's name.
* **Job Title** (`applicant_job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Years of Employment** (`applicant_years_of_employment`, number, required: false): Enter the years you have been employed.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required: false): Enter your property address.
* **Property City** (`property_city`, text, required: false): Enter your property city.
* **Property State** (`property_state`, text, required: false): Enter your property state.
* **Property Postal Code** (`property_postal_code`, text, required: false): Enter your property's postal code.
* **Property Country** (`property_country`, text, required: false): Enter your property country.
* **Property Purchase Price** (`property_purchase_price`, number, required: false): Enter the purchase price of your property.
* **Property Purchase Date** (`property_purchase_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you purchased your property.
* **Property Value** (`property_value`, number, required: false): Enter your property's value.
* **Loan Amount Requested** (`loan_amount_requested`, number, required: false): Enter the loan amount you are seeking.
* **Loan Interest Rate** (`loan_interest_rate`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the interest rate that suits your needs (6.5%, 7.5%, 8.5%, 9.5%, 10.5%).
* **Loan Term Years** (`loan_term_years`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the loan term that suits your needs (3 years, 5 years, 10 years, 15 years, 20 years).
