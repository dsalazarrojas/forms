# High Interest Savings Account Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for a High Interest Savings Account. The goal is to provide a straightforward and efficient way to gather necessary details for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill out the form in the provided fields.
3. Submit your application once you've filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Details
**Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.

### Employment Status
**Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required: false):
  * Option 1: Select this option if you are currently employed.
  * Option 2: Select this option if you are self-employed.
  * Option 3: Select this option if you are a student or have a fixed-term contract.
  
### Deposit Information
**Deposit Information** (`deposit_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the amount you plan to deposit into your High Interest Savings Account.

### Contact Information
**Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you with updates on your application.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* Double-check your application before submission to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
