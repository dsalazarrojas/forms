# Boat Financing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boat Financing Application Form is designed to collect essential information from applicants seeking boat financing. This form helps our loan officers evaluate the applicant's creditworthiness and determine the loan eligibility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all required documents and information before starting the application process.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and completely, paying attention to the required fields marked as "true".
3.  Enter the correct email address and phone number for communication purposes.
4.  If you're employed, select "Employed" as your employment status. If self-employed or retired, select the corresponding options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name** (`Applicant name`, required/true): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email address** (`Email address`, required/true): Enter your contact email address for loan updates.
* **Phone number** (`Phone number`, required/true): Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Loan amount requested** (`Loan amount requested`, required/true): Enter the numeric value of the loan you're seeking.
* **Down payment amount** (`Down payment amount`, optional/false): Enter the numeric value of the down payment (if applicable).
* **Vessel make and model** (`Vessel make and model`, required/true): Enter the manufacturer and model of the boat you're financing.
* **Vessel year** (`Vessel year`, optional/false): Enter the year of manufacture of the boat.
* **Employment status** (`Employment status`, required/true): Select "Employed" if you're currently employed, "Self employed" if you're self-employed, "Unemployed" if you're not working, or "Retired" if you're retired.
* **Annual income** (`Annual income`, required/true): Enter your gross annual income as a decimal value.
