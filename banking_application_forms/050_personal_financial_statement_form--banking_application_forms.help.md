# Personal Financial Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal financial information from individuals, including income, assets, liabilities, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Personal Financial Statement Form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have provided accurate and complete information.
2. Enter your income information in the "Income" field on page 1.
3. List your assets in the "Assets" field on page 1.
4. List your liabilities in the "Liabilities" field on page 1.
5. For each income source, select all applicable options from the "Income Sources" dropdown on page 2.
6. Choose the type of asset(s) you own from the "Asset Types" dropdown on page 2.
7. Enter the total value of your assets in the "Asset Value" field on page 2.
8. Select the type of liability(ies) you have from the "Liability Types" dropdown on page 3.
9. Enter the total value of your liabilities in the "Liability Value" field on page 3.
10. Enter the date of the financial statement on page 4.
11. If necessary, select the time of the financial statement on page 4.
12. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Income** (Page 1): Enter your total annual income from all sources, including salary, investments, and other regular income.
* **Assets** (Page 1): List all your assets, including cash, properties, stocks, and other valuables.
* **Liabilities** (Page 1): List all your liabilities, including credit cards, loans, and other debts.
* **Income Sources** (Page 2): Select all the sources of your income, such as alimony, rental income, or interest income. You can select multiple sources.
* **Asset Types** (Page 2): Choose the type(s) of asset(s) you own, such as cash, property, or stock. You can select only one type.
* **Asset Value** (Page 2): Enter the total value of your assets.
* **Liability Types** (Page 3): Choose the type(s) of liability(ies) you have, such as credit card, loan, or alimony. You can select only one type.
* **Liability Value** (Page 3): Enter the total value of your liabilities.
* **Date** (Page 4): Enter the date of the financial statement.
* **Time** (Page 4): If necessary, select the time of the financial statement.
* **Note** (Page 5): Add any additional comments or notes about your financial situation.
