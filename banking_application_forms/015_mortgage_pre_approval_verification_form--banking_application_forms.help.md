# Mortgage Pre Approval Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify your pre-approval status for a mortgage application. Fill out this form to provide necessary information about your personal details, loan specifics, income, employment, and credit history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Personal Information**: Fill in your personal details in the first page of the form, including any co-borrower information if applicable.
2. **Loan Information**: Provide details about your loan, such as the loan amount and loan type.
3. **Income Information**: Enter your annual income, which will help determine your pre-approval status.
4. **Employment Information**: Enter the length of your employment in years.
5. **Credit Information**: Select your credit score and credit history from the provided options.
6. **Income Proof**: Attach relevant documents to prove your income.
7. **Social Security Number**: Enter your Social Security number to verify your identity.
8. **Finalize**: Review your information and submit the form to complete the pre-approval process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, not required)
This section is where you can enter your personal details.

*   **Co Borrower Info** (`co_borrower_info`, select_one, not required):
    Select 'Co Applicant 1' if you have a co-applicant.
    Select 'Co Applicant 2' if you have a co-applicant, but they are not a primary applicant.
    Select 'No Co Applicant' if you are the sole applicant.
