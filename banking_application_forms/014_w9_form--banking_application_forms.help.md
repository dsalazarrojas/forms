# W9 Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report certain types of payments to the US Internal Revenue Service (IRS) to report payments made to non-employees or independent contractors. It is used in a banking context to report payments made to customers, such as interest paid on accounts or other types of payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the customer's name.
2. Enter the customer's address.
3. Enter the customer's city.
4. Enter the customer's state.
5. Enter the customer's zip code.
6. Enter the bank name.
7. Enter the routing number.
8. Enter the account number.
9. Enter the date the account was opened.
10. Enter the amount of the payment.
11. Select the account type (Checking, Savings, Money Market, or Other).
12. Select the bank type (Commercial, Investment, or Savings).
13. Enter the employee ID (if applicable).
14. Enter the bank's phone number.
15. Enter the bank's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their account.
- **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required): Enter the customer's address, including the street address.
- **City** (`customer_city`, text, required): Enter the customer's city.
- **State** (`customer_state`, text, required): Enter the customer's state.
- **Zip** (`customer_zip`, text, required): Enter the customer's zip code.
- **Bank Name** (`bank_name`, text, required): Enter the bank's name as it appears on the customer's account.
- **Routing Number** (`routing_number`, text, required): Enter the bank's routing number.
- **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, required): Enter the customer's account number.
- **Date Opened** (`date_opened`, date, required): Enter the date the account was opened.
- **Amount** (`amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the payment.
- **Account Type** (`account_type`, select one, required): Select the type of account (Checking, Savings, Money Market, or Other).
- **Bank Type** (`bank_type`, select one, required): Select the type of bank (Commercial, Investment, or Savings).
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter the employee ID if applicable.
- **Bank Phone** (`bank_phone`, text, optional): Enter the bank's phone number.
- **Bank Email** (`bank_email`, email, optional): Enter the bank's email address.
