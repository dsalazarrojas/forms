# Investment Portfolio Opening Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from investors who wish to open an investment portfolio. The form aims to understand the investor's details, risk profiles, and account data, which will be used to tailor a suitable investment strategy for their needs. Before proceeding, ensure that you are not duplicating an existing investment portfolio and that this form is relevant to your current situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Answer each section accurately and thoroughly, providing the requested details.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
4. Once you have completed the form, review it for accuracy before submission.
