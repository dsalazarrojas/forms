# Business Loan Prequalification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Loan Prequalification Form is used to gather essential information about your business and its owner to help lenders evaluate your creditworthiness and determine whether you can be considered for a loan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling out the business name, contact name, and contact email fields. These fields are required and should be filled out accurately.
2. Next, enter the requested amount you wish to prequalify for. This is the amount you are looking to borrow.
3. If applicable, provide an approximate yearly revenue for your business.
4. If known, estimate your business's credit score.
5. Enter the number of years your business has been operating.
6. Describe how you intend to use the funds in the "Intended use of funds" field.
7. Finally, indicate whether you agree to undergo a credit check.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact for prequalification purposes.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for prequalification results.
* **Requested Amount** (`requested_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you wish to prequalify for.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, number, optional): If known, provide an approximate yearly revenue for your business.
* **Credit Score Estimate** (`credit_score_estimate`, number, optional): If known, provide an approximate credit score for your business.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, optional): Enter the number of years your business has been operating.
* **Intended Use of Funds** (`intended_use`, text, required): Describe how you intend to use the funds.
* **Agree to Credit Check** (`agree_credit_check`, select_one, required): Check "True" to agree to undergo a credit check for prequalification.
