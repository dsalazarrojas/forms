# Loan Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loan Estimate Form is a tool used to calculate loan estimates for personal and commercial loans. It is designed to help you understand the potential costs and terms of a loan, including loan amounts, interest rates, and repayment schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Make sure to choose the correct loan type from the options provided.
3. Enter the loan amount, interest rate, and payment frequency as applicable.
4. Select the payment schedule type and frequency as applicable.
5. Enter any additional information as necessary.
6. Click on the "Submit" button to generate the loan estimate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **loan_term** (Number): Enter the loan term in years.
* **loan_amount** (Number): Enter the loan amount.
* **payment_frequency** (Select One): Choose how often you would like to make payments. Options include 6, 12, 24, and 36 months.
* **monthly_payment** (Number): Enter the monthly payment amount (calculated automatically).
* **total_interest** (Number): This field will display the total interest paid over the loan term (calculated automatically).
* **interest_rate** (Number): Enter the interest rate (annual percentage rate).
* **loan_date** (Date): Enter the loan date.
* **maturity_date** (Date): Enter the maturity date.
* **amortization_schedule** (Time): Enter the amortization schedule (calculated automatically).
* **payment_type** (Select Multiple): Choose the payment type from monthly, annual, quarterly, and biannually.
* **payment_frequency_type** (Select Multiple): Choose the payment frequency type from Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, and Option 4.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **note** (Note): Enter any additional comments or notes about the loan.
* **loan_term_frequency** (Select One): Choose how often you would like to make loan payments. Options include monthly, quarterly, biannually, and annually.
* **loan_type** (Select Multiple): Choose the loan type from personal, commercial, construction, and home equity.
* **loan_amount_frequency** (Select Multiple): Choose the loan amount frequency from fixed, variable, and variable plus fees.
* **loan_amount_frequency_type** (Select Multiple): Choose the loan amount frequency type from daily, weekly, monthly, biweekly, quarterly, biannually, and annually.
* **loan_date_type** (Select Multiple): Choose the loan date type from date of birth, date of death, date of mortgage, and date of sale.
* **payment_schedule_type** (Select Multiple): Choose the payment schedule type from linear, non-linear, balloon, and deferred.
* **payment_type_frequency** (Select Multiple): Choose the payment type frequency from lump sum, level, variable, and fixed.
* **loan_status** (Select Multiple): Choose the loan status from active, inactive, and paid.
