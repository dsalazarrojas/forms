# Investment Limit Increase Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request an increase in the investment limit for an existing investment account. The purpose of this form is to gather relevant information from the client to assess their eligibility for a higher investment limit.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and details ready before starting the form.
2. Scroll down to the "Client Details" page, enter your name, and any other relevant contact information (if applicable).
3. On the "Justifications" page, explain in detail why you are requesting an investment limit increase and provide any relevant supporting documents or evidence.
4. On the "Investment Amount" page, enter the specific amount of the investment limit increase you are requesting.
5. In the "Contact Details" page, provide your contact information and any other relevant contact details (if applicable).
6. Finally, on the "Broker Details" page, provide any relevant information about your broker or financial advisor (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (Text, Optional): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information to help us reach you for further clarification or follow-up questions.
* **Justifications** (Text, Optional): Please provide a clear and concise explanation for why you are requesting an investment limit increase. Be sure to provide any relevant supporting documents or evidence to support your claim.
* **Investment Amount** (Number, Required): Enter the specific amount of the investment limit increase you are requesting.
* **Contact Details** (Text, Optional): Provide your contact information and any other relevant contact details to help us reach you for further clarification or follow-up questions.
* **Broker Details** (Text, Optional): If you have a broker or financial advisor, provide their contact information and any other relevant details about their role in your investment portfolio.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are accurate and complete to the best of your ability.
* If you need any help or clarification on any of the fields, feel free to contact us.
* This form should only be used to request an investment limit increase, and not for other purposes such as account opening or maintenance.
