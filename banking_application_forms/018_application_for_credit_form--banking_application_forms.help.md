# Application For Credit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Application For Credit Form" is designed to collect essential information from applicants seeking credit. It helps our institution assess the applicant's creditworthiness and gather relevant details for a loan application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full legal name in the "Applicant name" field.
2. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field.
3. Enter your Social Security Number (SSN) last 4 digits in the "SSN last 4 digits" field for verification purposes.
4. Enter your current residential address in the "Residential address" field.
5. Select your employment status from the options provided in the "Employment status" field.
6. Enter your annual gross income in the "Annual income" field.
7. Indicate the loan amount you are requesting in the "Loan amount requested" field.
8. If applicable, describe the purpose of the loan in the "Purpose of loan" field.
9. Provide your best contact phone number in the "Contact phone" field.
10. Confirm your consent for a credit check in the "Consent for credit check" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your ID.
* **Date of birth**: Provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **SSN last 4 digits**: Enter your Social Security Number (SSN) last 4 digits for verification purposes.
* **Residential address**: Enter your current residential address, including street, city, and postal code.
* **Employment status**: Select your current employment status from the provided options.
* **Annual income**: Enter your gross annual income in local currency.
* **Loan amount requested**: Indicate the loan amount you are requesting.
* **Purpose of loan**: Describe the intended use of the loan (if applicable).
* **Contact phone**: Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Consent for credit check**: Confirm your permission for a credit check.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our institution's customer support team.
* This form is designed to be submitted online, please ensure you are in a secure environment to protect your personal data.
