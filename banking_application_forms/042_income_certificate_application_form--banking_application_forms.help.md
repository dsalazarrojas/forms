# Income Certificate Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect income certificate application information from applicants. It helps to gather details about the applicant's income, employment status, tax information, and bank accounts. The form is used to streamline the process of verifying income and employment information for application purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Applicant Information section with your personal details.
2. Provide income details in the Income Details section.
3. Select your employment status and fill out the Employment Details section.
4. Add any employment history in the Employment History section.
5. Enter your tax information in the Tax Information section.
6. List your bank accounts in the Bank Accounts section.
7. Finally, provide any additional income verification information in the Income Verification section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Information
• **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, `text`, required/optional): This is the first section of the form where you need to provide your personal details such as name, date of birth, and contact information. Please fill this section accurately to ensure your identity is verified correctly.

### Income Details
• **Income Details** (`income_details`, `text`, required/optional): In this section, provide details about your income from various sources, such as salary, investments, and other sources. Be as specific as possible to help with verification.

### Employment Status
• **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `text`, required/optional): Check your current employment status (employed, self-employed, unemployed, etc.) to fill out this section.

### Employment Details
• **Employment Details** (`employment_details`, `text`, required/optional): If you are employed, provide details about your job, work hours, and work location to help with verification.

### Employment History
• **Employment History** (`employment_history`, `text`, required/optional): List any previous employment history, including job positions, duration, and reasons for leaving to help with verification.

### Tax Information
• **Tax Information** (`tax_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide details about your tax filing status (filing as individual or partnership), tax ID number, and tax year to help with income verification.

### Bank Accounts
• **Bank Accounts** (`bank_accounts`, `text`, required/optional): List all your bank accounts, including account numbers and routing numbers, to help with income verification.

### Income Verification
• **Income Verification** (`income_verification`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional information to support your income, such as pay stubs, bank statements, or other relevant documentation.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all sections accurately and completely.
- Use a clear and legible handwriting.
- Double-check the form for any mistakes or errors before submission.
- Contact the form owner or administrator if you have any questions or concerns.
