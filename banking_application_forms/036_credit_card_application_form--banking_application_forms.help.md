# credit_card_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from applicants for a credit card application. The fields in this form are designed to help the bank evaluate the applicant's eligibility for a credit card.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form header section with your name and surname.
2. Enter your birth date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Provide your current address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Select your employment status.
7. Choose your employment type (if employed).
8. Enter the number of years you have been employed.
9. Enter your annual income.
10. Choose your credit score.
11. Select your credit card provider.
12. Choose your account type.
13. Enter your account provider (if applicable).
14. Enter your credit limit.
15. Enter your credit rate.
16. Enter your account number (if applicable).
17. Enter your CVV (if applicable).
18. Enter your PIN (if applicable).
19. Provide your signature.
20. Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, text, optional): This field is a placeholder for the form title, which will be filled out by the bank or system.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Surname** (`surname`, text, optional): Please enter your surname as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter your birth date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current employment status.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_multiple, optional): If employed, select all applicable employment types.
* **Employment Years** (`employment_years`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been employed.
* **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, number, optional): Enter your annual income.
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, select_one, optional): Choose your credit score.
* **Credit Card Provider** (`credit_card_provider`, select_one, optional): Select your credit card provider.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, select_one, optional): Choose your account type.
* **Account Provider** (`account_provider`, text, optional): Enter your account provider (if applicable).
* **Credit Limit** (`credit_limit`, number, optional): Enter your credit limit.
* **Credit Rate** (`credit_rate`, number, optional): Enter your credit rate.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, optional): Enter your account number (if applicable).
* **CVV** (`cvv`, text, optional): Enter your CVV (if applicable).
* **PIN** (`pin`, text, optional): Enter your PIN (if applicable).
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Provide your signature.
* **Submit** (`form_submit_btn`, text, optional): Click to submit your application.
* **Cancel** (`form_cancel_btn`, text, optional): Click to cancel your application.
* **Reset** (`form_reset_btn`, text, optional): Click to reset the form.
* **Error Message** (`error_message`, note, optional): This field is for display of error messages, if any.
