# Tax Preparer Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Preparer Agreement Form is a crucial document that requires specific information about the tax preparer, client, and tax return details. This form serves as an agreement between the tax preparer and client, outlining the terms and conditions of tax preparation services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information as a tax preparer.
2. Enter your client's details, including their name and contact information.
3. Specify the tax service details, if applicable.
4. Review the tax return details to ensure accuracy.
5. Sign the agreement form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, required): A brief description of the agreement between the tax preparer and client.
- **Client** (`client`, text, required): Enter your client's name and contact information.
- **Tax Preparer** (`tax preparer`, text, required): Enter your tax preparer name and contact information.
- **Service Details** (`service details`, text, optional): Provide details about the tax services offered.
- **Agreement Terms** (`agreement terms`, text, required): Specify any terms and conditions of the agreement.
- **Tax Return** (`tax return`, number, required): Enter the tax return number or ID.
- **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign the agreement form to confirm understanding and agreement.
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form Notes** (`tax preparer agreement form notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form Phone** (`tax preparer agreement form phone`, text, required): Enter your tax preparer phone number.
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form Email** (`tax preparer agreement form email`, email, required): Enter your tax preparer email address.
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form Address** (`tax preparer agreement form address`, text, required): Enter your tax preparer address.
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form Postal Code** (`tax preparer agreement form post al code`, text, required): Enter your tax preparer postal code.
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form City** (`tax preparer agreement form city`, text, required): Enter your tax preparer city.
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form Province** (`tax preparer agreement form province`, text, required): Enter your tax preparer province.
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form Employment Status** (`tax preparer agreement form employment status`, select_one, required): Select your employment status as a tax preparer (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, etc.).
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form Income Range** (`tax preparer agreement form income range`, select_multiple, required): Select your income range (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form Income Range 2** (`tax preparer agreement form income range 2`, select_multiple, optional): Select your income range (optional).
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form Employment Type** (`tax preparer agreement form employment type`, select_one, required): Select your employment type as a tax preparer (e.g., Business, Personal, Self).
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form Business Name** (`tax preparer agreement form business name`, text, optional): Enter your business name (if applicable).
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form Business Registration Number** (`tax preparer agreement form business registration number`, text, optional): Enter your business registration number (if applicable).
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form Business Phone** (`tax preparer agreement form business phone`, text, optional): Enter your business phone number (if applicable).
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form Business Email** (`tax preparer agreement form business email`, email, optional): Enter your business email address (if applicable).
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form Business Tax Filing** (`tax preparer agreement form business tax filing`, text, optional): Enter your business tax filing information (if applicable).
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form Business Tax Filing 2** (`tax preparer agreement form business tax filing 2`, text, optional): Enter your business tax filing information (if applicable).
- **Tax Preparer Agreement Form Notes** (`tax preparer agreement form notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
