# Fast Track Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Fast Track Application Form" is designed to collect essential information from individuals who wish to take advantage of the expedited loan or application process. This form aims to speed up the process for those who need immediate attention or have special requirements that make traditional processes inconvenient.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out all required fields**: Indicate that you have filled out all required fields to ensure a smoother and faster processing time. Required fields are marked as "required" in the form.
2. **Enter accurate information**: Ensure that the details you provide are accurate and up-to-date.
3. **Review and submit**: Once you have filled out all the fields, review your information for accuracy and click on the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false)
Your first name is requested to help us identify you.

### 2. **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false)
Your last name is requested to help us identify you.

### 3. **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
Please enter your email address where we can reach you for updates or further instructions.

### 4. **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false)
Your phone number will help us contact you efficiently.

### 5. **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required: false)
Select your current employment status from the provided options.

### 6. **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, number, required: false)
Please enter your current credit score.

### 7. **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required: false)
Enter the amount you are applying for.

### 8. **Loan Terms** (`loan_terms`, select_one, required: false)
Select the loan terms you are interested in from the provided options.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions or approvals before submitting the form.
- The form is designed to be completed as quickly and efficiently as possible, so please fill out all required fields and provide accurate information to speed up the process.
