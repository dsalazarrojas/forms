# credit_repair_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The credit repair consultation form is designed to help individuals seeking advice or guidance on improving their credit scores. This form collects personal and financial information that will be reviewed by a credit repair consultant or advisor. The purpose of this form is to provide a structured approach to gathering necessary details for offering personalized advice and strategies for credit improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form effectively:
1.  Please fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Phone number to identify yourself.
2.  Provide a valid Email address to facilitate communication.
3.  Enter your Date of Birth as it appears on your identification documents.
4.  Enter your current Address for contact and verification purposes.
5.  Select your Account Type to help the consultant understand your current financial status.
6.  Enter your Account Number for verification purposes.
7.  If applicable, enter your current Credit Score and Credit Limit for a better understanding of your credit situation.
8.  If you've had recent credits or loans, provide information on their utilization (Credit Utilization).
9.  Specify your Employment Status and Income for a clearer picture of your financial situation.
10. Enter your income and Debt Ratio for more detailed analysis.
11. Provide a history of your credits or loans (Credit History) if you wish to be advised on handling past issues.
12. If applicable, enter your Account Balance for a clearer picture of your financial situation.
13. Indicate whether you have a Credit Recommender.
14. If you have multiple accounts, specify their types and ages for a more comprehensive analysis.
15. Select the types of accounts you have for more precise advice.

- **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter a valid phone number for communication.
- **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address for future communication.

- **Address** (Text, Required): Enter your current address for verification and contact purposes.
- **Account Type** (Select One, Required): Select the type of account you have (Account A, Account B, etc.).
- **Account Number** (Text, Required): Enter your account number for verification purposes.
- **Credit Score** (Number, Optional): If you wish to know your current credit score for the consultant to advise on it.
- **Credit Limit** (Number, Optional): Enter your current credit limit for a better understanding of your credit situation.
- **Credit Utilization** (Number, Optional): Enter the utilization of your credits or loans if you've had any.
- **Employment Status** (Text, Optional): Specify your employment status for a clearer picture of your financial situation.
- **Income** (Number, Optional): Enter your income for a more detailed analysis.
- **Debt Ratio** (Number, Optional): Enter your debt ratio for the consultant to understand your financial situation better.
- **Credit History** (Text, Optional): Provide a history of your credits or loans for advice on handling past issues.
- **Account Balance** (Text, Optional): Enter your account balance for a clearer picture of your financial situation.
- **Credit Recommender** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if you have a credit recommender.
- **Account Age** (Number, Optional): Enter the age of your account(s) for a more comprehensive analysis.
