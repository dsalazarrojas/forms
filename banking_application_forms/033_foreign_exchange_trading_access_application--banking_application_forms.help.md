# Foreign Exchange Trading Access Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants who wish to access foreign exchange trading services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in the input fields, providing detailed and accurate information about yourself.
- Select the relevant options for "Experience Details", "Trading Platforms", "Risk Level", and "Account Type" based on your actual experience and preferences.
- Ensure you enter correct and up-to-date contact information in the "Contact Details" field.
- Once you have filled out all the input fields, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself that you feel are necessary to provide in order to be considered for foreign exchange trading access.
* **Experience Details** (`experience_details`, text, optional): Describe your experience in the foreign exchange trading field, including any relevant qualifications or certifications you may have.
* **Trading Platforms** (`trading_platforms`, select_multiple, optional): Select the foreign exchange trading platforms you have experience with or are familiar with, such as Alpha Vantage, Interactive Brokers, OANDA, or Saxo Brokerage.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_one, optional): Indicate your risk level, which can be Low, Moderate, or High, depending on your investment strategy and risk tolerance.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, select_one, optional): Specify the type of account you wish to open, such as Personal, Business, or Institutional.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your up-to-date contact information, including your email address and/or phone number.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to complete the form and submit your application.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or the form administrators for assistance.
