# Broker Accreditation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Broker Accreditation Form is a crucial document for licensed brokers seeking to work with our organization. This form helps us verify your credentials, experience, and accuracy of information. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth accreditation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Personal Information**: 
   - **Full Legal Name**: Your complete legal name as per your identification documents.
   - **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
   - **Social Security Number**: Your Social Security Number for identification purposes.
   - **Email Address**: Your primary email address.
   - **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
   - **Home Address**: Your current residential address.
2. **Professional Information**:
   - **Broker License Number**: Your current broker license number.
   - **Licensing State**: The state where your license is issued.
   - **License Expiration Date**: When your current license expires.
   - **Current Firm Name**: Name of the firm you are affiliated with.
   - **Firm Address**: Address of your firm.
   - **Years of Experience**: The number of years you've been a licensed broker.
3. **Accreditation Information**:
   - **Accreditation Category**: Select from: Residential Broker, Commercial Broker, Investment Broker, Property Manager, Real Estate Agent, Mortgage Broker, Other.
   - **Accreditation Level**: Select from: Standard Accreditation, Senior Broker Accreditation, Master Broker Accreditation, Premium Partner, Other.
4. **Employment History**:
   - **Current Position**: Your current job title.
   - **Duration at Current Firm**: Select from: Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, 10+ years.
   - **Previous Firms**: List any previous firms if applicable.
5. **Certifications and Training**:
   - **Relevant Certifications**: Any relevant certifications or training.
   - **Continuing Education Credits**: Number of CE credits completed this period.
6. **Compliance Information**:
   - **Disciplinary History**: Select from: No disciplinary actions, Yes disciplinary action taken.
   - **Disciplinary Details**: Provide details if yes.
   - **Bankruptcy History**: Select from: False, True.
7. **Supporting Documents**:
   - **Documents Submitted**: Select from: License Copy, Photo ID, Proof of Insurance, Employment Verification, Certification Documents.
8. **Declaration**:
   - **Accuracy Statement**: Check to certify accuracy of information provided.
   - **Date**: Date of application.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- Ensure you have all necessary documents and information before completing the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
- Please note that this form is not for new brokers or those without experience; it's for those seeking higher levels of accreditation.
