# Credit Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Credit Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals applying for a credit or loan from a financial institution. This form helps the applicant provide necessary details about themselves, their employment, income, financial history, and other relevant information to facilitate the application process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information in the first field, including your name, contact details, etc.
2. Provide your address in the second field.
3. Fill in your employment details in the third field.
4. Enter your income information in the fourth field.
5. Share your credit score in the fifth field.
6. List your financial institutions in the sixth field.
7. Add any relevant account information in the seventh field.
8. Review and agree to the loan terms in the eighth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (1)**: Enter your personal details, including your name, email, and phone number. This information will help us contact you regarding your application.
* **Address (2)**: Provide your address to help us verify your identity and location.
* **Employment (3)**: Fill in your employment details, including your job title, company name, and length of employment.
* **Income (4)**: Enter your annual income to help us understand your financial capabilities.
* **Credit Score (5)**: Share your credit score to help us assess your creditworthiness.
* **Financial Institutions (6)**: List the financial institutions you are currently working with.
* **Account Information (7)**: Add any relevant account information, such as loan accounts or credit cards.
* **Loan Terms (8)**: Review and agree to the loan terms, which will be displayed in a separate section.
