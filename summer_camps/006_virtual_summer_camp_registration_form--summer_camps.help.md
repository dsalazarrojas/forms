# Virtual Summer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register children for virtual summer camps. Please fill out this form to provide necessary information for their registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your child's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill out your child's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your child's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your child's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your child's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Check the "Parent Permission" field to indicate if you are the parent or guardian giving permission for your child's registration.
7. If necessary, enter your name and contact information in the "Emergency Contact Name", "Emergency Contact Phone", and "Emergency Contact Email" fields.
8. Fill out the name of the summer camp your child will be attending in the "Summer Camp Name" field.
9. Enter the start and end dates of the summer camp in the "Summer Camp Start Date" and "Summer Camp End Date" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your child's last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your child's email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your child's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Parent Permission** (select_one, required): Check "Yes" if you are the parent or guardian giving permission for your child's registration.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Summer Camp Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the summer camp your child will be attending.
* **Summer Camp Start Date** (date, required): Enter the start date of the summer camp.
* **Summer Camp End Date** (date, required): Enter the end date of the summer camp.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and completely.
* If you are the parent or guardian giving permission, check the "Parent Permission" field.
* If you are filling out this form for multiple children, fill out a separate form for each child. 
* If you are filling out this form for multiple camps, fill out a separate form for each camp. 
* If you are unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.
