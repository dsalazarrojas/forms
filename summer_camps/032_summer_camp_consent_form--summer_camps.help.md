# Summer Camp Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Summer Camp Consent Form is used for parents or guardians to provide consent and vital information for their child's participation in summer camp activities. This form helps ensure that the camper's health and well-being are considered while they are away from home.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of summer camp activities your child will participate in.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the summer camp.
3. Choose the activities your child will be participating in.
4. Provide contact information for yourself and your emergency contact.
5. List any medical conditions your child has and any allergies they are aware of.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Select the type of summer camp your child will be attending, such as Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4.
* **Date Start**: Enter the start date of the summer camp in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Time Start**: Enter the start time of the summer camp in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Date End**: Enter the end date of the summer camp in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Time End**: Enter the end time of the summer camp in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Camp Activities**: Select the activities your child will participate in, such as Activity 1, Activity 2, Activity 3, Activity 4, or Activity 5.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as the contact person for your child.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Guardian Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Guardian Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and relationship of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Info**: Provide any additional information about the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions your child has.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies your child has.
* **Consent**: Sign and date this section to confirm your consent for your child to participate in summer camp activities.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your child's participation in summer camp.
* **Signature Date**: (Optional) Enter the date you sign the form.
* **Signature**: (Optional) Sign your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Camper ID**: (Optional) Enter a unique ID for your child.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: (Optional) Provide the relationship between the emergency contact person and your child.
* **Emergency Contact Info**: (Optional) Provide any additional information about the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Info**: (Optional) Provide any additional medical information about your child.
* **Insurance Card**: (Optional) Attach a copy of your child's insurance card.
* **Other Info**: (Optional) Provide any other relevant information about your child.
