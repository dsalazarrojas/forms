# Health And Consent Form For Summer Camp - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health and Consent Form for Summer Camp is a crucial document that helps us ensure the health and well-being of our campers. It collects information about the camper's medical history, any health issues they may have, and obtains consent for potential treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Summer Camp Info: This section is used to provide basic information about the summer camp the camper is attending.
2. Provide Staff Info: Enter the name and contact information of the staff member who will be in charge of the camper during the summer camp.
3. Enter Camper Info: Enter the name, date of birth, and contact information of the camper.
4. Report Health Issues: If the camper has any medical conditions, please describe them in this section.
5. Treatment Consent: Indicate whether you give your consent for treatment of any medical issues that may arise during the summer camp.
6. Medical History: Enter any medical history or allergies the camper may have.
7. Emergency Contact: Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
8. Parent Consent: Indicate whether you give your consent for your child to participate in activities that may involve risk.
9. Additional Info: Enter any additional information that is relevant to the camper's health and well-being.
