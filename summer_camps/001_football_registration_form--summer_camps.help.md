# Football Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Football Registration Form is designed to collect information from players registering for a football camp. This form is used to gather essential details about each player, including their name, contact information, and specific team and player attributes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the required information and the purpose of each field.
2. Enter your personal details as prompted, including your player name, email address, phone number, and age.
3. If you have a team, enter your team's name and the player's jersey number.
4. Indicate whether you are a yes or no answer regarding your team status.
5. Enter the emergency contact's name and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Player Email**: Enter your email address that you use to communicate with your team or coach.
* **Player Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Player Age**: Enter your age to ensure we have accurate information for your camp experience.
* **Team Name**: If you are part of a team, enter the name of your team.
* **Position**: Answer 'yes' or 'no' to indicate whether you are on a team or not.
* **Jersey Number**: Enter your jersey number if you are part of a team.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to have contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person you would like to have contact in case of an emergency.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [your contact email or phone number].
* This form is for personal use only and should not be shared or used for commercial purposes.
