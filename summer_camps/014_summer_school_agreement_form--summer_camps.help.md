# summer_school_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from students attending summer camps for administrative and emergency contact purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
Follow the steps below to complete this form:

1. Enter your summer camps name in the "summer_camps_terms" field.
2. Provide your student's name in the "student_name" field.
3. Enter your name as a parent/guardian in the "parent_name" field.
4. Enter your student's date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
5. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
7. Enter your address in the "address" field.
8. Identify your emergency contact in the "emergency_contact" field.
9. If applicable, enter the emergency contact's name, phone number, email, and relationship in the subsequent fields.
10. If necessary, provide any relevant medical conditions, allergies, medical equipment, and medication information.
11. Finally, confirm your consent by selecting the appropriate options in the "consent_1", "consent_2", "consent_3", and "consent_4" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### summer_camps_terms
* **summer_camps_terms** (`summer_camps_terms`, text): Enter the name of the summer camp your student will be attending.
### student_name
* **student_name** (`student_name`, text): Enter your student's name.
### parent_name
* **parent_name** (`parent_name`, text): Enter your name as a parent/guardian.
### date_of_birth
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, date): Enter your student's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
### contact_number
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, text): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
### email
* **email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address for emergency contact.
### address
* **address** (`address`, text): Enter your address for emergency contact.
### emergency_contact
* **emergency_contact** (`emergency_contact`, text): Identify your emergency contact.
### emergency_contact_name
* **emergency_contact** (`emergency_contact`, text): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
### emergency_contact_phone
* **emergency_contact_phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
### emergency_contact_email
* **emergency_contact_email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
### relationship
* **relationship** (`relationship`, text): Enter the relationship of your emergency contact.
### emergency_contact_notes
* **emergency_contact_notes** (`emergency_contact_notes`, text): Enter any additional information about your emergency contact.
### medical_conditions
* **medical_conditions** (`medical_conditions`, note): Provide any relevant medical conditions your student may have.
### allergies
* **allergies** (`allergies`, text): List any allergies your student may have.
### medical_equipment
* **medical_equipment** (`medical_equipment`, text): List any medical equipment your student may have.
### medical_notes
* **medical_notes** (`medical_notes`, text): Enter any relevant medical notes.
### consent_1
* **consent_1** (`consent_1`, select_one): Confirm that you have read and understand the terms of the summer camp.
### consent_2
* **consent_2** (`consent_2`, select_one): Confirm that you are authorizing your student to participate in the summer camp.
### consent_3
* **consent_3** (`consent_3`, select_multiple): Indicate any medical conditions your student has that require attention during the summer camp.
### consent_4
* **consent_4** (`consent_4`, select_one): Confirm that you will inform the camp about any changes to your student's medical conditions.
### medical_special
* **medical_special** (`medical_special`, select_one): Select the medical speciality of your student's conditions.
### medical_condition_medication
* **medical_condition_medication** (`medical_condition_medication`, text): Enter any medication related to your student's conditions.
### emergency_procedure
* **emergency_procedure** (`emergency_procedure`, text): Enter any emergency procedures for your student's conditions.
### medical_special_notes
* **medical_special_notes** (`medical_special_notes`, text): Enter any special notes about your student's conditions.
