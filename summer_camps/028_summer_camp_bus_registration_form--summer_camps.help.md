# Summer Camp Bus Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your child for summer camp bus services. It helps our drivers know your child's details and pickup/drop-off points.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your child's parent name
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter your phone number
4. Enter your child's name
5. Enter your child's date of birth (in the format: month/day/year)
6. Enter your child's school name
7. Select your child's bus route
8. Select your child's bus stop
9. If you have any special instructions or requests, enter them in the "Special Instructions" field
10. Sign electronically, indicating that you have read and agreed to the terms
11. Enter the date and time of your electronic signature
12. Enter your IP address
13. Enter your device type
14. Enter the browser you are using
15. Enter your operating system
16. Enter the version of your browser
17. Enter your time zone
18. Select your time zone offset
19. Select your preferred language
20. Select your country
21. Enter your city
22. Enter your postal code (zip code)
23. Enter the name of your host (this field might be optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name**: Enter your name, as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Student Name**: Enter your child's name.
* **Student DOB**: Enter your child's date of birth (in the format: month/day/year).
* **School**: Enter your child's school name.
* **Bus Route**: Select the bus route that your child will be taking.
* **Bus Stop**: Select the bus stop where your child will be picked up or dropped off.
* **Special Instructions**: If you have any special instructions or requests, enter them in this field.
* **Parent E-Signature**: Sign electronically, indicating that you have read and agreed to the terms.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your electronic signature.
* **IP Address**: Enter your IP address.
* **Device**: Enter the type of device you are using to sign this form.
* **Browser**: Enter the browser you are using.
* **OS**: Enter your operating system.
* **Version**: Enter the version of your browser.
* **Time Zone**: Enter your time zone.
* **Time Zone Offset**: Select your time zone offset.
* **Language**: Select your preferred language.
* **Country**: Select your country.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **Postal Code**: Enter your postal code (zip code).
* **Host**: Enter the name of your host.
* **Notes**: If you have any additional information or comments, enter them in this field.
* **URL**: Enter any URL or reference to relevant information.

Note: The form fields that are marked as "required" are mandatory fields that must be filled out to proceed with the submission.
