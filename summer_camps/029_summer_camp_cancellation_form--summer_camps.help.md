# summer_camp_cancellation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The summer_camp_cancellation_form is a form used to request the cancellation of a summer camp. It is designed to collect necessary information from both parents and camp staff to facilitate a smooth cancellation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Parent Request (Page 1)
   - Enter the parent's request to cancel the summer camp. This is a text field where the parent can provide a brief explanation for their request.
2. Cancellation Date (Page 1)
   - Enter the date on which the camp will be cancelled.
3. Reason (Page 1)
   - Enter a brief explanation for the cancellation reason, if applicable.
4. Enter the Staff Response (Page 1)
   - Enter a brief response from the camp staff regarding the cancellation.
5. Enter the Parent Response (Page 1)
   - Enter a brief response from the parent.
6. Enter the Parent Confirmation (Page 1)
   - Enter the parent's confirmation of the cancellation.
7. Enter the Staff Confirmation (Page 2)
   - Enter the staff member's confirmation of the cancellation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Request** (`Parent Request`, `text`, optional)
  - This is a text field where the parent can provide a brief explanation for their request to cancel the summer camp.
* **Cancellation Date** (`Cancellation Date`, `date`, required)
  - Enter the date on which the camp will be cancelled.
* **Reason** (`Reason`, `text`, optional)
  - Enter a brief explanation for the cancellation reason, if applicable.
* **Staff Response** (`Staff Response`, `text`, optional)
  - Enter a brief response from the camp staff regarding the cancellation.
* **Parent Response** (`Parent Response`, `text`, optional)
  - Enter a brief response from the parent.
* **Parent Confirmation** (`Parent Confirmation`, `text`, optional)
  - Enter the parent's confirmation of the cancellation.
* **Staff Confirmation** (`Staff Confirmation`, `text`, optional)
  - Enter the staff member's confirmation of the cancellation.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are using this form for its intended purpose and not for general inquiries or other purposes.
* If you need to cancel a summer camp, please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly.
* The form should only be used in the event of a camp cancellation.
* If you are not sure about any field, please contact the form creator for clarification.
