# Summer Camp Survey Form For Parents - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Survey Form For Parents is a tool for parents to provide feedback and ratings about the summer camp experience for their children. This will help the camp administrators to understand your opinions and make informed decisions to improve the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and thorough in your responses.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the text box at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you know about our summer camp?** (`summer_camp_survey_form_for_parents_question_1`, text, optional): This question is an open-ended question where you can describe anything you knew about our summer camp before your child attended it. Please keep it brief.
* **How do you rate our camp's quality in terms of the program?** (`summer_camp_survey_form_for_parents_question_2`, number, optional): This question asks you to rate the quality of our summer camp program on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **How would you rate our camp's communication about your child's daily activities and schedule?** (`summer_camp_survey_form_for_parents_question_3`, number, optional): This question asks you to rate the communication about your child's daily activities and schedule on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Do you believe our camp's social and emotional growth is one of the priorities?** (`summer_camp_survey_form_for_parents_question_4`, select_one, optional): This question asks you to select one of the options regarding the importance of social and emotional growth at our summer camp.
* **How likely are you to recommend our summer camp to other parents?** (`summer_camp_survey_form_for_parents_question_5`, select_one, optional): This question asks you to select one of the options regarding how likely you are to recommend our summer camp to other parents.
