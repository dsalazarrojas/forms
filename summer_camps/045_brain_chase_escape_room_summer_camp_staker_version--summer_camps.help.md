# Brain Chase Escape Room Summer Camp - Staker Version - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect registration information for the Brain Chase Escape Room Summer Camp - Staker Version. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and safe experience for the camper.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form in sections, starting from the first page.
2.  Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
3.  Review each page to ensure you have filled out all required fields before proceeding.
4.  Use the hints provided for guidance on each field.
5.  Save your progress and come back later if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Camper First Name** (`camper_first_name`, text, required): Enter the camper's first name.
*   **Camper Last Name** (`camper_last_name`, text, required): Enter the camper's last name.
*   **Camper Date of Birth** (`camper_dob`, date, required): Enter the camper's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Camper Age** (`camper_age`, number, required): Enter the camper's age at the start of the camp.
*   **Camper Gender** (`camper_gender`, select_one, required): Select the camper's gender.
*   **Parent First Name** (`parent_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as a parent or guardian.
*   **Parent Last Name** (`parent_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as a parent or guardian.
*   **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for camp communications.
*   **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter your primary contact number.
*   **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter your home address.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Enter your relationship to the emergency contact.
*   **Camp Session** (`camp_session`, select_one, required): Select the camp session the camper will attend.
*   **Camp Experience** (`camp_experience`, select_one, required): Select the camper's experience level with the camp.
*   **Grade Completed** (`grade_completed`, select_one, required): Select the grade level the camper has finished.
*   **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school the camper attends.
*   **Camper Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, required): Select the camper's interests.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): List any medical conditions the camper has.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): List any allergies the camper has.
*   **Medications** (`medications`, text, required): List any medications the camper is taking.
*   **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, required): Enter any dietary restrictions the camper has.
*   **Behavioral Needs** (`behavioral_needs`, text, optional): Enter any special considerations for the camper.
*   **Swimming Ability** (`swimming_ability`, select_one, required): Select the camper's swimming ability.
*   **Authorized Pickup Persons** (`pickup_authorization`, text, required): Enter names of authorized pickup persons.
*   **Medical Treatment Authorization** (`medical_treatment_authorization`, select_one, required): Enter authorization for emergency medical treatment.
*   **Photo and Video Consent** (`photo_video_consent`, select_one, required): Select your consent for photos and videos.
*   **Liability Waiver** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, required): Select your understanding of the risks and liability.
*   **Camp Rules Agreement** (`camp_rules`, select_one, required): Select your agreement to follow the camp rules.
*   **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, required): Select the T-Shirt size.
*   **Camp Fee** (`camp_fee`, text, required): Enter the fee amount.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the method of payment.
*   **Sibling Discount** (`sibling_discount`, select_one, optional): If you are eligible for a sibling discount, select it.
*   **Early Bird Discount** (`early_bird_discount`, select_one, optional): Select if you registered by the early bird deadline.
*   **How Did You Hear About Camp** (`how_heard_about_camp`, select_one, optional): Select how you heard about the camp.
*   **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required): Enter your full name as a parent or guardian.
*   **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of registration.

## Tips

*   Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   Save your progress and come back later if needed.
*   Review each page carefully to avoid missing any required fields.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the camp organizers.
