# Pre Camp Health Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Camp Health Screening Form is a crucial document for all individuals attending the summer camp. This form is used to collect vital information that ensures the health and safety of all attendees. Please review this guide carefully to understand the purpose of each section and complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review all sections carefully before completing the form.
2. Provide honest and accurate information for each field.
3. If you are unsure about any field, contact the camp staff for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Info Form
* **General Info Form** (`general_info_form`, text, not required): This section is used to gather basic information about you, such as your name, contact information, and other relevant details.

### Contact Tracing
* **Contact Tracing** (`contact_tracing`, select_one, required): This field is used to determine if you have been in contact with anyone with COVID-19 or any other communicable disease. Please select 'Yes' if you have been in contact with someone or 'No' if you have not.

### Symptoms
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, not required): If you have any symptoms of illness, please describe them in detail in this field.

### Test Results
* **Test Results** (`test_results`, text, not required): If you have undergone any tests or screenings for infectious diseases, please provide the results in this field.

### Vaccination Status
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, text, not required): Please indicate your vaccination status, including any vaccinations you have received or are scheduled to receive.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, not required): Identify your emergency contact person, including their name, contact information, and relationship to you.

### Travel
* **Travel** (`travel`, text, not required): Describe any recent travel history, including dates and destinations.

### Medical Info
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text, not required): Provide any medical information that may be relevant to your health and well-being at the camp, such as allergies, medications, or medical conditions.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the camp staff for guidance.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the camp staff.
