# Summer Camp Medical Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Summer Camp Medical Form is designed to collect vital information about a summer camp participant's health, medical history, and emergency contact details. This form will help the summer camp staff to take necessary precautions and provide proper care and support for each participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the General Info section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. In the Health Conditions section, list any health conditions or allergies you have, such as diabetes, asthma, or food allergies.
3. In the Medications section, list any medications you are currently taking.
4. In the Vaccinations section, list any vaccinations you have received.
5. In the Emergency Contact section, provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
6. In the Signature section, sign and date the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
7. In the Medical History section, provide any relevant medical history, such as previous injuries or surgeries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`General Info`, text, required/optional): This section is used to collect your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Health Conditions** (`Health Conditions`, text, required/optional): List any health conditions or allergies you have, such as diabetes, asthma, or food allergies.
* **Medications** (`Medications`, text, required/optional): List any medications you are currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Vaccinations** (`Vaccinations`, text, required/optional): List any vaccinations you have received, such as flu shots or tetanus shots.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, text, required/optional): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, text, required/optional): Sign and date the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, required/optional): Provide any relevant medical history, such as previous injuries or surgeries.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your doctor or healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is confidential and will only be used for the purpose of providing better care and support for you at the summer camp.
* Please return the completed form to the summer camp staff before arriving at the camp.
