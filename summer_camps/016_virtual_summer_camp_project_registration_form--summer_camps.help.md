# Virtual Summer Camp Project Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Summer Camp Project Registration Form is used to register a project for a virtual summer camp. This form helps the camp's administration collect necessary information from participants, including their personal details, contact information, and state of residence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out the required fields (marked as **Required**) with accurate and up-to-date information.
- Make sure to fill out all the fields on each page.
- Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your valid email address that the camp can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (Required): Enter your phone number that the camp can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Address** (Optional): Enter your address, if you'd like to provide it.
* **Country** (Required): Select your country of residence from the list.
* **State** (Required): Select all the states you are interested in participating in the virtual summer camp. You can select multiple states.
* **Zip** (Required): Enter your zip code.
*   Please note that some fields, like "State", allow you to select multiple options, while others require a single entry.
