# online_summer_camp_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and manage online summer camp applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Fill in the Required Information

* Enter the name of your summer camp in the **Summer Camp Name** field.
* Provide the name of the contact person in the **Contact Person** field.
* Enter your email address in the **Email** field. Please make sure to use a valid email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
* Provide your full address in the **Address** field.

### Step 2: Select the Camp Details

* Choose the type of summer camp you are applying for in the **Summer Camp Type** field. You can select one of the following options: Nature, Adventure, Water, Arts, Sports, or Other.
* Write a brief description of your summer camp in the **Description** field.

### Step 3: Assign Tools and Equipment

* Select one or more tools or equipment that you will be using for your summer camp from the **Assigned Tool** field. You can choose one or more of the following options: chatjimmy, chatjanie, or other.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer Camp Name (text, required)**: This is the name of your summer camp.
* **Contact Person (text, required)**: This is the name of the person who will be in contact with us.
* **Email (email, required)**: This is the email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone (text, required)**: This is your phone number.
* **Address (text, required)**: This is your full address.
* **Dates (date, required)**: Please enter the dates of your summer camp.
* **Summer Camp Type (select_one, required)**: Choose the type of summer camp you are applying for.
* **Description (text, required)**: Write a brief description of your summer camp.
* **Assigned Tool (select_multiple, required)**: Select one or more tools or equipment that you will be using for your summer camp.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at [support email].
