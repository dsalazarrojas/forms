# Summer Camp Staff Training Assessment Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the performance of summer camp staff after a training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Training** (Text): Record any comments or feedback about the training session.
* **Training Goals** (Text): Evaluate how well the training goals were met.
* **Staff Readiness** (Number): Assess how ready the staff is to perform after the training.
* **Performance Standards** (Select one): Evaluate how well the staff's performance meets the standards.
* **Evaluation Date** (Date): Record the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (Time): Record the time of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Notes** (Note): Record any additional comments or feedback about the evaluation.
* **Staff Performance** (Select multiple): Evaluate the staff's performance.
* **Evaluation Frequency** (Select one): Evaluate how often the staff is evaluated.
* **Evaluation Method** (Select one): Evaluate the method used for the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Scale** (Select one): Evaluate the scale used for the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Comments** (Text): Record any additional comments or feedback about the evaluation.
