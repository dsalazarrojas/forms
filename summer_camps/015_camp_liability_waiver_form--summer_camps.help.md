# Camp Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a legal release of liability for your camper, allowing them to participate in the summer camp activities. It's a requirement for registration and ensures that you, as the parent or guardian, understand the risks associated with the camp program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to understand its purpose and requirements.
2. Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
3. Read and sign the form before submitting it to the camp.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Camper First Name** (`camper_first_name`, text, required): Enter your camper's first name.
* **Camper Last Name** (`camper_last_name`, text, required): Enter your camper's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your camper's date of birth.
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): Enter your full name and title (e.g., Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr.).
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter your home address, including street address and city or town.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city or town where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code for your residence.
* **Camp Session** (`camp_session`, select_one, required): Select the session your camper will be attending.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person authorized to pick up your camper in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Primary Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your camper's primary physician.
* **Primary Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of your camper's primary physician.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): If you have any insurance provider, enter their name.
* **Insurance Policy Number** (`insurance_policy_number`, text, optional): If you have any insurance provider, enter their policy number.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): List any known medical conditions that affect your camper.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): List any known allergies your camper has, including food, insect, and medication allergies.
* **Acknowledgement of Activity Risks** (`activity_risks_acknowledged`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to acknowledge that you understand camp activities involve inherent risks.
* **Assumption of Risk** (`assumption_of_risk`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to voluntarily assume all risks associated with camp activities.
* **Release of Liability** (`release_of_liability`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to release the camp from liability for injuries.
* **Medical Treatment Authorization** (`medical_treatment_authorization`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to authorize emergency medical treatment for your camper.
* **Transportation Authorization** (`transportation_authorization`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to authorize camp transportation if needed.
* **Photo and Video Release** (`photo_video_release`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to allow the camp to use photos and videos of your camper.
* **Code of Conduct Agreement** (`code_of_conduct_agreement`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that your camper agrees to follow all camp rules.
* **Authorized Pickup Persons** (`pickup_authorization`, text, required): List all individuals authorized to pick up your camper.
* **I Confirm I Am the Legal Guardian** (`legal_guardian_confirmation`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm you are the legal guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required): Enter your full name as a signature.
* **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of a witness (if applicable).
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of a witness (if applicable).

## Tips
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to the camp.
- Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any questions, contact the camp staff for clarification.
- Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
