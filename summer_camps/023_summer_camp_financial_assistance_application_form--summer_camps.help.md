# Summer Camp Financial Assistance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is to collect information from applicants for financial assistance for summer camps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer all questions carefully and accurately.
2.  Provide information for all family members who will be attending the summer camp.
3.  Ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`): This is the section where you will provide your personal details.
* **Household Income** (`household_income`): Please enter your household's annual income.
* **Household Size** (`household_size`): Enter the number of people living in your household.
* **Family Member 1 Name** (`family_member_1_name`): Enter the name of the first family member who will be attending the summer camp.
* **Family Member 1 Relationship** (`family_member_1_relationship`): Choose the relationship between you and the family member (e.g. Mother or Father, Other, Grandparent, Guardian).
* **Family Member 2 Name** (`family_member_2_name`): Enter the name of the second family member who will be attending the summer camp.
* **Family Member 2 Relationship** (`family_member_2_relationship`): Choose the relationship between you and the family member (e.g. Mother or Father, Other, Grandparent, Guardian).
* **Household Zip** (`household_zip`): Enter your household zip code.
* **Camp Info** (`camp_info`): Enter any relevant information about the summer camp (e.g., location, dates).
* **Camp Name** (`camp_name`): Enter the name of the summer camp.
* **Camp Address** (`camp_address`): Enter the address of the summer camp.
* **Camp Dates** (`camp_dates`): Enter the dates of the summer camp.
* **Camp Fees** (`camp_fees`): Enter the total camp fees.
* **Camper Info** (`camper_info`): Enter any relevant information about the camper.
* **Camper Name** (`camper_name`): Enter the name of the camper.
* **Camper Age** (`camper_age`): Enter the age of the camper.
* **Camper Gender** (`camper_gender`): Choose the gender of the camper (Male, Female, Other).
* **Camper Ethnicity** (`camper_ethnicity`): Choose the ethnicity of the camper (`Yes', 'No`).
* **Camper Disability** (`camper_disability`): Choose if the camper has a disability (`Yes', 'No`).
* **Applicant Signature** (`applicant_signature`): Sign and date this form to confirm your identity.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`): If the camper is a minor, sign and date this form to confirm your guardianship.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the current date.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the name and relationship of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`): Choose the relationship between the emergency contact person (Mother or Father, Other, Grandparent, Guardian).
