# Summer Day Camp Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register your child for the summer day camp program. Please submit this form to enroll your child in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your child's **First Name** in the text field labeled "first_name".
2. Fill in your child's **Last Name** in the text field labeled "last_name".
3. Fill in your **Parent/Guardian Name** in the text field labeled "parent_name".
4. Enter your **Email Address** in the email field labeled "email".
5. Fill in your **Phone Number** in the text field labeled "phone".
6. Fill in your **Address** in the text field labeled "address".
7. Fill in your **City** in the text field labeled "city".
8. Fill in your **State** in the text field labeled "state".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your child's first name to identify them in our records.
- **Last Name**: Enter your child's last name to identify them in our records.
- **Parent/Guardian Name**: Enter your name as the parent/guardian of the child.
- **Email Address**: Enter your email address for us to contact you about your child's registration and any important updates.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you about your child.
- **Address**: Enter your address for us to contact you about your child's registration and any important updates.
- **City**: Enter your city where you reside.
- **State**: Enter your state of residence.
