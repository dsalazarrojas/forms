# Camp Knock Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your child and your relationship with them. It will help us understand your consent for various camp activities, such as medical treatment, transportation, and photo release. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure we can provide the best possible experience for your child.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, making sure to answer all the required fields.
2. Use the provided hints for guidance on what information is needed.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper First Name (required)**: Enter your child's first name as it is written on their identification documents.
* **Camper Last Name (required)**: Enter your child's last name as it is written on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (required)**: Enter your child's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 02/12/2000).
* **Gender (required)**: Select your child's gender from the provided options.
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name (required)**: Enter your full name as a parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email (required)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone (required)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Home Address (required)**: Enter your home address in the format of "123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345".
* **City (required)**: Enter the city or town where you reside.
* **State (required)**: Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **ZIP Code (required)**: Enter the postal code of your residence.
* **Emergency Contact Name (required)**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (required)**: Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (required)**: Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions (required)**: List any medical conditions your child has, including allergies, illnesses, or disabilities.
* **Allergies (required)**: List any food, insect, or medication allergies your child has.
* **Current Medications (optional)**: If your child takes any medications, list them with dosages.
* **Primary Physician Name (required)**: Enter the name of your child's primary physician.
* **Physician Phone (required)**: Enter the contact phone number of the physician.
* **Insurance Provider (optional)**: If your child has insurance, enter the name of the insurance provider.
* **Insurance Policy Number (optional)**: If your child has insurance, enter the policy number.
* **Consent for Camp Activities (required)**: Give your consent for your child to participate in various camp activities, such as sports and crafts.
* **Photo and Video Release (required)**: Give your consent for your child's photos to be used by the camp.
* **Consent for Transportation (required)**: Give your consent for your child to be transported to and from camp activities.
* **Consent for Medical Treatment (required)**: Give your consent for your child to receive medical treatment if needed.
* **Consent for Sunscreen Application (required)**: Give your consent for camp staff to apply sunscreen to your child.
* **Code of Conduct Agreement (required)**: Agree that your child will follow the camp rules.
* **Liability Waiver Acknowledgement (required)**: Acknowledge that you understand the liability waiver.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature (required)**: Sign your name as a parent or guardian.
* **Date Signed (required)**: Date and time of signature.
