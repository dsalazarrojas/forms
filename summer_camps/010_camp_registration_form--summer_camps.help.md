# Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians to register their child for a summer camp experience. This form helps ensure that the camp has all necessary details to provide a safe and enjoyable experience for the child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your child's Camper Information:
	* Enter your child's First Name
	* Enter your child's Last Name
	* Choose their Grade Entering in Fall
	* Enter their Date of Birth
	* Choose their Age
2. Provide your Parent or Guardian Information:
	* Enter your first name
	* Enter your last name
	* Enter your Phone Number
	* Enter your Email Address
	* Enter your Street Address
	* Enter your City
	* Enter your State
	* Enter your Zip Code
3. Choose your Camp Session and Options:
	* Select which week you would like to attend
	* Choose whether you would like to enroll your child in Full Day or Half Day program
4. Provide Health and Safety Information:
	* If your child has any allergies, enter them
	* If your child has any medical conditions, enter them
	* If your child is taking any medications, enter them
5. Set up Emergency Contact Information:
	* Enter the name of the emergency contact
	* Enter the phone number of the emergency contact

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your child's first name as it appears on their birth certificate or government-issued ID.
* **Last Name**: Enter your child's last name as it appears on their birth certificate or government-issued ID.
* **Grade Entering in Fall**: Choose your child's grade level that they will be entering in the fall.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your child's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Age**: Choose your child's age as of the start of the camp session.
* **Gender**: Choose your child's gender.
* **Parent First Name**: Enter your first name as the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Last Name**: Enter your last name as the parent or guardian.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address where you live.
* **City**: Enter the city where you live.
* **State**: Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code.
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address where you live. (Note: this field was duplicated and is intended to be the same as "Parent Street Address" above, but will be removed in production)
* **Which week would you like to attend**: Choose the week you would like to attend from the list provided.
* **Full Day or Half Day**: Choose whether you would like to enroll your child in the full day or half day program.
* **Does your child have any allergies**: Enter any allergies your child may have.
* **Any medical conditions we should know about**: Enter any medical conditions your child may have.
* **Current medications being taken**: Enter any medications your child is taking.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
