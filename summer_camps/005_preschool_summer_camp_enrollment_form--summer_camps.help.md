# Preschool Summer Camp Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Summer Camp Enrollment Form is used to collect information for children's summer camp enrollment. This form requires parents to provide essential details about themselves and their child to ensure a smooth and enjoyable summer experience.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out the form completely and correctly to ensure that your child's enrollment is processed smoothly.
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding to the next page.
- If you need any assistance, do not hesitate to contact the camp administration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, note, not required): This is a static header that explains the purpose of the form and the camp.
* **Parent Name** (`name`, text, not required): Please enter your name as the parent/guardian of the child.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for any updates or concerns.
* **Parent Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Provide your phone number so we can reach out for any urgent matters.
* **Birth Date** (`birthdate`, date, not required): Enter the birthdate of your child. This will help us to ensure their eligibility for our program.
* **Camp Name** (`camp_name`, select_one, not required): Please choose the camp that your child is interested in enrolling.
* **Session** (`camp_session`, select_one, not required): Select the preferred session your child wants to join.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, not required): This field is optional and can be left blank.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, not required): Enter your child's name.
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, date, not required): Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, not required): This field is optional and can be left blank.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, not required): This field is optional and can be left blank.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately to avoid any processing delays or errors. If you have any doubts or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
