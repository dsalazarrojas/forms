# Parental Consent And Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain the parental consent and signature for your child to participate in a summer camp. It's a mandatory requirement for all camp participants and helps the camp organizers keep track of your child's information and ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Review and Fill Out the Form

- Start by carefully reviewing the form and ensure you have all the necessary information to complete it accurately.
- Fill out the form with your child's name, your name, and your email address.
- Make sure to sign the form with your signature.

### Step 2: Add Date and Signatures

- Provide the date when you gave your consent for your child to participate in the summer camp.
- Sign the form to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
- Your signature will serve as proof that you have given your consent and are aware of your child's participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parental Consent** (`parental_consent`): Provide a brief written consent from a parent or guardian that their child can participate in the summer camp. This is a one-time statement of your consent.
* **Name of Parent** (`parental_name`): Enter your name as the parent or guardian providing consent for your child.
* **Name of Child** (`child_name`): Enter your child's name to ensure accurate documentation and identification.
* **Email** (`email`): Provide your email address for contact purposes, in case of emergencies or communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes, in case of emergencies or communication.
* **Signature** (`signature_of_parent`): Sign this line to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the summer camp.
* **Date of Consent** (`date_of_consent`): Enter the date when you gave your consent for your child to participate in the summer camp.
* **Date of Signature** (`date_of_signature`): This is the date you signed the form, indicating when you gave your consent.
* **Signature of Parent** (`signature_of_parent`): Sign here to confirm your identity and consent.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete the form accurately and legibly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the summer camp organizers.
* The form will be kept on file for internal documentation purposes only.
