# Summer Camp Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Scholarship Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants for a summer camp scholarship. This form is used to gather essential details about you, your child/ward, and your family situation to help us evaluate your eligibility and provide support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Please start by providing your contact information, including your name, address, and phone number. This will help us reach you with any additional questions or concerns.
2. Proceed to provide details about the applicant, including their name, age, and any relevant medical or disability information.
3. Continue to the demographic section, where you'll provide information about your family's income, occupation, and education level.
4. Please provide any supporting documents or references if requested.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1:** 
  * **Page 1**: This is the first page of the application form. Please start by providing your contact information, including your name, address, and phone number.
  * **Page 2-20:** These pages contain demographic information, such as family income, occupation, and education level. Please answer these questions accurately and completely.
  * **Page 21:** 
    + **Name**: Please enter your child/ward's name.
    + **Age**: Enter the applicant's age.
    + **Disability/Medical Information**: If the applicant has any medical or disability issues, please provide details here.
  * **Page 22-25:** 
    + **Name**: Please enter your contact name (in case of emergencies).
    + **Address**: Enter your contact address.
    + **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
    + **Additional Comments**: If you have any other comments or concerns, please add them here.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Make sure to review and edit your responses before submitting.
* If you need any help or clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
