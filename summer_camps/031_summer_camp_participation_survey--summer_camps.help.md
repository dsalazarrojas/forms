# Summer Camp Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about summer camp participation from users. The data collected will help us better understand the demographics of summer camp participants and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you understand what information is being collected.
2. Select your answer to "Summer Camp Participation" on page 1.
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field on page 2.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field on page 2.
5. Enter your age in the "Age" field on page 2, which should be a numerical value.
6. Select the date you participated in the "Date" field on page 3, using the YYYY-MM-DD format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Summer Camp Participation** (Select Multiple):
	+ Please select whether you participated in summer camp, with options "Yes" and "No".
* **Name** (Text):
	+ Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email** (Email):
	+ Enter your valid email address.
* **Age** (Number):
	+ Enter your age as a numerical value.
* **Date** (Date):
	+ Select the date you participated in summer camp, using the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1995-01-15). 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure the data is useful to us.
* If you have any issues or need help, please contact us.
* If you participate in summer camp multiple times, you will need to fill out the form multiple times. However, for the purpose of this form, please only fill out the form once.
