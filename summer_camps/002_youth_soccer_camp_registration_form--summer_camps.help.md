# Youth Soccer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from parents or legal guardians to register their child or ward for a youth soccer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to include all required fields (indicated by a asterisk \*).
3. Review each field for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Athlete Information** (id: 1, required: false)
    *   Enter your child's name and any other relevant details.
*   **Parents Guardian Information** (id: 2, required: false)
    *   Provide your name and contact information as the parent or legal guardian.
*   **Contact Info** (id: 3, required: false)
    *   Enter your contact information, including email, phone number, and address.
*   **Agreement and Consent** (id: 4, required: false)
    *   Read and agree to the camp's terms and conditions.
*   **Payment Information** (id: 5, required: false)
    *   Enter payment details, including credit card information or other payment methods.
*   **Athlete Information 2** (id: 6, required: false)
    *   Provide any additional athlete information as requested.

## Tips
*   Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
*   Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
*   Review your answers carefully before submission to avoid errors.
