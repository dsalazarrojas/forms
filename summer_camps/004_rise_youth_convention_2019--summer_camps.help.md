Rise Youth Convention 2019 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for the Rise Youth Convention 2019. It includes fields for selecting the convention type, choosing a t-shirt size, and providing additional information such as parental release and contact details.
## How To Complete This Form
This form has multiple pages, each with various fields. To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. On the first page, you will be asked to select the convention type (Full Convention, One-Day Convention, or Conference).
2. On the second page, choose your preferred t-shirt size (Small, Medium, or Large).
3. On the third page, select the price range you are willing to pay ( $0-$20, $21-$40, or Other).
4. On the fourth page, please review and sign the parental release form, if applicable.
5. On the fifth page, enter your parents' email address (if you are a minor).
6. On the sixth page, enter your church leaders' email address.
7. On the final page, add any additional notes or comments you would like to share with the convention staff.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Convention Type (id: 2)**: This field allows you to select the type of convention you would like to attend (Full Convention, One-Day Convention, or Conference).
* **T-Shirt Size (id: 3)**: Select the t-shirt size you would like to order. Please note that you can choose one or more sizes if you would like.
* **Price Range (id: 4)**: Choose the price range you are willing to pay for the convention. Please select from the provided options or enter "Other" if you have a different budget in mind.
* **Parental Release Form (id: 5)**: If you are a minor, please review and sign this form to grant permission for your parents or guardians to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Parents Email (id: 6)**: If you are a minor, please enter your parents' email address so we can contact them if necessary.
* **Church Leaders Email (id: 7)**: Enter the email address of your church leaders or contact person so we can keep them informed about your convention experience.
* **Notes (id: 8)**: Add any additional comments or information you would like to share with the convention staff.
