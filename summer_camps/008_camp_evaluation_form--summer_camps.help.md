# Camp Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from parents or guardians about their child's experience at a summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the required information for each field.
2. Select the relevant options for each field based on your experience.
3. Provide additional comments or suggestions in the text fields.
4. Submit the form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Name** (`camper_name`, text, required): Full name of the camper attending the camp.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Name of the parent or guardian completing the evaluation.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Contact email for follow-up questions or communications.
* **Camp Session Attended** (`camp_session`, select_one, required): Select the session attended by the camper (e.g., Session 1 - Week 1, Session 2 - Week 2, etc.).
* **Overall Satisfaction with Camp** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall experience with the camp (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Rate whether the camp provided good value for the money spent (Excellent value, Good value, Fair value, Poor value, Very poor value).
* **Staff Friendliness and Professionalism** (`staff_friendliness`, select_one, required): Rate the friendliness and professionalism of the camp staff (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Counselor Quality** (`counselor_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the camp counselors (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Variety of Activities Offered** (`activity_variety`, select_one, required): Rate the variety of activities provided by the camp (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Favorite Activity** (`favorite_activity`, select_one, required): Select the favorite activity of the camper (e.g., Sports and games, Arts and crafts, etc.).
* **Least Favorite Activity** (`least_favorite_activity`, text, not required): Suggest an activity that could be improved (optional).
* **Facility Cleanliness** (`facility_cleanliness`, select_one, required): Rate the cleanliness of the camp facilities (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Safety Measures** (`safety_measures`, select_one, required): Rate the safety measures in place at the camp (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Quality of Camp Communication** (`communication_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of communication from the camp staff (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **How Much Did Your Camper Enjoy Camp** (`camper_enjoyment`, select_one, required): Rate how much the camper enjoyed the camp experience (Loved it, Enjoyed it a lot, Enjoyed it somewhat, Did not enjoy much, Did not enjoy at all).
* **Social Experience** (`social_experience`, select_one, required): Rate whether the camper made friends at the camp (Made many friends, Made a few friends, Made one friend, Did not make friends, Already had friends attending).
* **How Likely Are You to Return Next Year** (`return_likelihood`, select_one, required): Rate the likelihood of returning to the camp next year (Definitely will return, Very likely to return, Somewhat likely, Unlikely to return, Definitely will not return).
* **Would You Recommend This Camp to Others** (`recommend_to_others`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would recommend the camp to others (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably no, Definitely no).
* **What Improvements Would You Suggest** (`improvements_suggested`, text, not required): Suggest any improvements or changes to the camp.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Provide any additional feedback or comments.
