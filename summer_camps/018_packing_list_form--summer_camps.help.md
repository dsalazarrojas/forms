# Packing List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Packing List Form" is designed to help us prepare for your summer camp experience. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary information to ensure your stay is comfortable and enjoyable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your general information, including your phone number and email.
2. Specify your clothing and medication needs.
3. Provide your camp date and arrival time.
4. Answer questions about your medical conditions, dietary requirements, and transportation needs.
5. Share any additional information or concerns you may have.
6. Let us know if you have any questions or concerns about the camp.
7. Finally, let us know if you need any additional forms or documentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`General Information`, text, required/optional): This section is where you can enter your general contact information, such as your phone number and email.
* **What type of clothing do you want to pack?** (`packing_clothing`, text, required/optional): Please specify the type of clothing you would like to pack, such as casual or formal.
* **Do you have any medications that you need to pack?** (`medications`, select_one, required/optional): If you have any medications that you need to bring, please select "Yes" and provide more details.
* **List any special equipment you need to bring?** (`equipment`, text, required/optional): If you have any special equipment that you need to bring, such as a wheelchair or medical device, please list them here.
* **What is your camp date?** (`camp_date`, date, required/optional): Please enter the date of your camp in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **What time do you need to arrive at camp?** (`arrival_time`, time, required/optional): Please enter the time you need to arrive at the camp in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **What is your email?** (`email`, email, required/optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Do you have any medical conditions that we need to know about?** (`medical_conditions`, text, required/optional): If you have any medical conditions that we should be aware of, please list them here.
* **Do you have any dietary requirements?** (`dietary_requirements`, select_multiple, required/optional): If you have any dietary requirements, please select the relevant options.
* **Is there anything else you want to tell us about yourself?** (`additional_info`, text, required/optional): Please share any additional comments or concerns you may have.
* **Do you have any questions or concerns about camp?** (`questions_concerns`, text, required/optional): If you have any questions or concerns, please let us know here.
* **Do you need any help with transportation?** (`transportation`, select_multiple, required/optional): If you need help with transportation, please select the relevant options.
* **Is there anything else you want to share?** (`more_info`, text, required/optional): Please share any additional information you may have.
* **Is there anything else you want to say about your child?** (`child_info`, text, required/optional): If you have any comments or concerns about your child, please share them here.
* **Are you a first-time camper?** (`first_time_camper`, radio, required/optional): If this is your first time at the camp, please select "Yes".
* **Do you want to join a buddy program?** (`buddy_program`, checkbox, required/optional): If you're interested in joining a buddy program, please select one of the options.
* **Do you have any comments or concerns about your child's behavior?** (`behavioral_comments`, text, required/optional): If you have any comments or concerns about your child's behavior, please share them here.
* **Do you need any additional forms or documentation?** (`additional_forms`, text, required/optional): If you need any additional forms or documentation, please specify here.
* **Is there anything else you want to tell us?** (`final_comments`, text, required/optional): Please share any final thoughts or comments here.
