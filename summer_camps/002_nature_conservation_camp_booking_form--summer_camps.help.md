# Nature Conservation Camp Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Nature Conservation Camp Booking Form is used to collect information for booking purposes for participants of the nature conservation camp.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as prompted. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.
2. Make sure to fill in accurate and complete information to ensure that your booking is processed smoothly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Booking Details

* **Booking Details** (`booking_details`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of your booking request.

### Name

* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.

### School Name

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your school.

### Contact Person

* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): If you are booking on behalf of a school or organization, enter the name of the contact person.

### Email

* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.

### Phone

* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for quick contact.

### Booking Date

* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to book the conservation camp.

### Accommodation Requirements

* **Accommodation Requirements** (`accommodation_requirements`, text, optional): Describe any specific accommodation requirements you have.

### Number of Participants

* **Number of Participants** (`number_of_participants`, number, optional): Enter the number of participants joining you.

### Participant Type

* **Participant Type** (`select_one_participant_type`, select one, optional): Select whether you are a:
	+ Teacher
	+ Participant

### Programs

* **Programs** (`select_multiple_programs`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more programs you wish to join:
	+ Conservation
	+ Education
	+ Research

### Note

* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or requests.

### Booking Details 2

* **Booking Details 2** (`booking_details_2`, text, optional): If you need to provide any further details, enter them here.
