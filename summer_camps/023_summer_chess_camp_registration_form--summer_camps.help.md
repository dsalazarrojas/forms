# Summer Chess Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the summer chess camp. It collects information from both the participant and parent to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have all the necessary information for the participant and parent before starting.
*   Fill out the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
*   Enter the parent's name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
*   Select the participant's experience level:
    *   Choose "Beginner" for a new chess player.
    *   Choose "Intermediate" for those with some experience.
    *   Choose "Advanced" for experienced players.
*   Enter any medical information, including allergies, medical conditions, and special needs, in the "Participant Medical" field.
*   Enter any dietary restrictions or preferences.
*   Select the participant's T-shirt size and color.
*   Choose the payment method:
    *   Select "Credit Card" for credit card payments.
    *   Select "Debit Card" for debit card payments.
    *   Select "Bank Transfer" for bank transfer payments.
*   Enter any additional information or notes in the "Participant Additional Info" field.
*   Sign and date the form for both the participant and parent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name** (text, required):
    *   Enter the name of the participant registering for the summer chess camp.
*   **Parent Name** (text, required):
    *   Enter the name of the participant's parent or guardian.
*   **Contact Email** (email, required):
    *   Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
*   **Contact Phone** (text, required):
    *   Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
*   **Participant Age** (number, required):
    *   Select the age level of the participant:
        +  Choose "Beginner" for a new chess player.
        +  Choose "Intermediate" for those with some experience.
        +  Choose "Advanced" for experienced players.
*   **Participant Experience** (select one, required):
    *   Select the experience level of the participant:
        +  "Beginner"

    *   Enter any medical information or allergies.

*   **Participant Food Allergies** (text, required):
