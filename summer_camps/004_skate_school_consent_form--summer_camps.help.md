# Skate School Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skate School Consent Form is used to gather information from participants and their parents/guardians about their consent to join a skate school program. This form is essential for ensuring that participants are aware of the risks and requirements associated with skateboarding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter the participant's email address in the "Participant Email" field.
3. Enter the participant's phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
4. Enter the parent/guardian's name in the "Parent/Guardian Name" field.
5. Enter the parent/guardian's phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
6. Enter the participant's address in the "Participant Address" field.
7. Read the Skate School Participation Agreement section and check the box to confirm that you agree to the terms.
8. If necessary, enter any medical information in the "Medical Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (Participant Name)**: Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Email (Participant Email)**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone (Participant Phone)**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Parent/Guardian Name (Parent/Guardian Name)**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone (Parent Phone)**: Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Participant Address (Participant Address)**: Enter the participant's address.
* **Skate School Participation Agreement (Skate School Participation Agreement)**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the skate school program.
* **Medical Info (Medical Info)**: If necessary, enter any medical information about the participant.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and carefully.
* Ensure that you are filling out this form on behalf of the participant.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the skate school staff for assistance.
