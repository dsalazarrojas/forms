# Boy Scout Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track attendance at Boy Scout events or meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the date of the meeting or event on the "Meeting date" field.
2. Fill in the full name of the Scout on the "Scout name" field.
3. If known, enter the assigned troop number in the "Troop number" field.
4. Mark the Scout's attendance status (Present, Absent, or Excused) on the "Attendance status" field.
5. If applicable, enter the time the Scout arrived on the "Arrival time" field.
6. If applicable, enter the time the Scout departed on the "Departure time" field.
7. Add any special notes about the Scout's attendance on the "Notes" field.
8. Finally, enter your initials on the "Leader initials" field as a record of your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting date** (Meeting date, date, required): Enter the date of the meeting or event.
* **Scout name** (Scout name, text, required): Fill in the full name of the Scout.
* **Troop number** (Troop number, text, optional): If known, enter the assigned troop number.
* **Attendance status** (Attendance status, select_one, required): Mark the Scout's attendance status (Present, Absent, or Excused).
* **Arrival time** (Arrival time, time, optional): If applicable, enter the time the Scout arrived.
* **Departure time** (Departure time, time, optional): If applicable, enter the time the Scout departed.
* **Notes** (Notes, text, optional): Add any special information about the Scout's attendance.
* **Leader initials** (Leader initials, text, required): Enter your initials as a record of your signature.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately to ensure correct tracking of attendance.
* Use the "Troop number" field only if you know it.
* Use the "Arrival time" and "Departure time" fields only if the Scout arrived or departed at a specific time.
* Use the "Notes" field for any special circumstances about the Scout's attendance.
* Remember to sign with your initials on the "Leader initials" field.
