## Medical Employment Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects essential information for employee medical employment needs, ensuring a smooth and compliant hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure you complete the form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information to help us verify your identity.
2. Provide details of your emergency contact, including their name, email, and phone number.
3. Share information about your medical insurance coverage, including the name of the policyholder and policy number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Contact Information** (`medical_employment_information_form_1`, text, required): This field is for your personal details, such as your name, email, and phone number. Please ensure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to enable us to contact you effectively.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`medical_employment_information_form_2`, text, required): In case of an emergency, please provide the contact information of a person we can reach out to. This could be a family member, friend, or colleague.
* **Medical Insurance Information** (`medical_employment_information_form_3`, text, required): Please provide details about your medical insurance coverage, including the name of the policyholder (you or your employer) and policy number, which is usually found on your insurance card or policy document.
