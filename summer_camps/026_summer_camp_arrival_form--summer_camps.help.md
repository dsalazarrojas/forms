# Summer Camp Arrival Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Arrival Form is a tool used by camp administrators to collect information from parents or guardians of children attending summer camp. This form ensures that the camp has accurate and up-to-date information about each camper, including their arrival details, medical information, and special requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Time of arrival field with the exact date and time of when your child will arrive at the camp.
2. Select the Transportation method that best describes how your child will be arriving at the camp (e.g., Car, Bus, Train, Plane, or Other).
3. Choose the Pick up location where you would like the camp staff to pick up your child (e.g., Campsite entrance, Campsite parking, or Other).
4. Enter your Contact number so that we can reach you in case of any emergencies.
5. Fill in the Camper name field with your child's name.
6. Enter your child's age in the Camper age field.
7. Select your child's Camper gender.
8. Enter any relevant medical information about your child in the Camper medical info field.
9. If there are any special requests or requirements for your child, enter them in the Camper special requests field.
10. If your child has any allergies, enter them in the Camper allergies field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Time of arrival**: The exact date and time your child will arrive at the camp.
*  **Transportation method**: The mode of transportation your child will be using to arrive at the camp.
* **Pick up location**: Where the camp staff will pick up your child.
*  **Contact number**: A phone number or email where the camp staff can reach you in case of emergencies.
* **Camper name**: Your child's name.
* **Camper age**: Your child's age.
* **Camper gender**: Your child's gender.
* **Camper medical info**: Any medical conditions or allergies your child has.
* **Camper special requests**: Any special requests or requirements your child needs.
* **Camper allergies**: Any allergies your child has.
