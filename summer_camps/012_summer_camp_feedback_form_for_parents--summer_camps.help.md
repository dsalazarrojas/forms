# Summer Camp Feedback Form For Parents - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect feedback from parents about their child's experience at the summer camps. The goal is to gather information that will help us improve the camps for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the summer camp(s) your child attended from the list.
2. Provide your feedback about your child's experience at the camp.
3. Enter your child's ID (unique identifier given by the camp).
4. Enter your ID as a parent or guardian (unique identifier given by the camp).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **summer_camps**: Select one or more of the summer camps your child attended from the list below:
	+ Camp One
	+ Camp Two
	+ Camp Three
* **feedback**: Provide a written feedback about your child's experience at the summer camp. This will help us to know what we can improve for future sessions.
* **camper_id**: Enter the unique ID given to your child by the summer camp. This will help us to track your child's attendance and feedback.
* **parent_id**: Enter your unique ID given by the camp. This is required to ensure that you are the correct parent or guardian providing feedback.
