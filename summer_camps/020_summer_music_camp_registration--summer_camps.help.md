# Summer Music Camp Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Music Camp Registration form is designed to collect information from registrants for music camp planning and organization purposes. This form is likely intended for music enthusiasts or camp administrators to gather relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. Provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
3. Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registrant Info (Registrant Info)**: A text field where you can enter your name, email, or other relevant details.
* **Music Experience (Music Experience)**: A multiple-select field where you can choose your music experience (e.g., Guitar, Piano, Violin).
* **Camp Date (Camp Date)**: A date field where you can select the camp date you are interested in attending.
* **Scholarship (Scholarship)**: A select-one field where you can indicate if you are eligible for a scholarship (True or False).
* **Payment (Payment)**: A numerical field where you can enter your payment details.
* **Camp Name (Camp Name)**: A text field where you can enter the name of the music camp you are attending.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your payment details for accuracy to avoid any issues.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the camp administrators for clarification.
