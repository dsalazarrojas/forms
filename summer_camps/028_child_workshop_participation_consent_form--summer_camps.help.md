# Child Workshop Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your child's participation in a workshop, including their personal details, workshop information, and consent for various purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the child's personal details, including their first and last name, date of birth, age, and gender.
2. Provide the workshop information, including the workshop name, dates, and location.
3. Answer questions about your child's medical and dietary needs, including any allergies or restrictions.
4. Answer questions about transportation and meal requirements.
5. List any medical conditions or current medications your child may need to take during the workshop.
6. Provide an emergency contact person's name, phone number, and relationship to the child.
7. Indicate your consent for photography and video recording of your child during the workshop.
8. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have reviewed and understand the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consent Date**: The date on which you are providing consent for your child's participation in the workshop.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name**: The legal first name of the parent or guardian providing consent.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name**: The legal last name of the parent or guardian providing consent.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: The primary email address for contact purposes.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: The primary phone number for contact purposes.
* **Child First Name**: The legal first name of the child.
* **Child Last Name**: The legal last name of the child.
* **Child Date of Birth**: The child's date of birth.
* **Child Age**: The child's current age in years.
* **Child Gender**: The child's gender.
* **Workshop Name**: The name of the workshop your child is participating in.
* **Workshop Dates**: The dates when the workshop will be held.
* **Workshop Location**: The location where the workshop will be held.
* **Workshop Type**: The type of workshop your child is participating in.
* **Workshop Schedule**: The schedule of the workshop (full day or half day).
* **Transportation Needed**: Indicate whether your child needs transportation to or from the workshop.
* **Meal Requirements**: Indicate whether your child requires meals or snacks during the workshop.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: List any dietary restrictions or allergies your child has.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions your child has.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications your child is currently taking.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations**: Indicate whether your child requires any special accommodations.
* **Behavioral Considerations**: List any behavioral considerations staff should be aware of.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: The full name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: The phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: The relationship of the emergency contact person to the child.
* **Authorized Pickup Persons**: List the names of authorized persons to pick up your child.
* **Photo and Video Consent**: Indicate whether you consent to your child being photographed or filmed during the workshop.
* **Medical Treatment Consent**: Indicate whether you consent to emergency medical treatment for your child during the workshop.
* **Workshop Fee Payment**: Indicate how you will pay the workshop fee.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional information you would like to share.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Type your full name as the parent or guardian.
* **Signature Date**: The date of your electronic signature.

Please note that some fields may not be applicable to your situation.
