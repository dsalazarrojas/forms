# Camp Ministries Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from the reference provider about a potential volunteer or employee applicant for various camp ministries. The form is to be completed by someone who knows the applicant, such as a pastor, employer, teacher, or colleague, and is meant to provide an evaluation of the applicant's character, work ethic, and other relevant qualities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please enter the applicant's name in the "Applicant Name" field.
2.  Enter the applicant's contact email in the "Applicant Email" field.
3.  Enter the applicant's phone number in the "Applicant Phone" field.
4.  Select the position for which the applicant is applying from the "Position Applying For" dropdown menu.
5.  Enter the reference provider's name in the "Reference Name" field.
6.  Enter the reference provider's title or position in the "Reference Title or Position" field.
7.  Enter the reference provider's organization in the "Reference Organization" field.
8.  Enter the reference provider's contact email in the "Reference Email" field.
9.  Enter the reference provider's phone number in the "Reference Phone" field.
10. Select the relationship between the reference provider and the applicant from the "Relationship to Applicant" dropdown menu.
11.  Select how long the reference provider has known the applicant from the "How Long Have You Known the Applicant" dropdown menu.
12.  Rate the applicant's character, work ethic, reliability, and spiritual maturity from the corresponding dropdown menus.
13.  Describe the applicant's strengths and areas for growth in the "Strengths" and "Areas for Growth" fields.
14.  Enter any concerns or red flags about the applicant in the "Concerns or Red Flags" field.
15.  Check "Yes" or "No" to recommend the applicant for camp ministry in the "Would You Recommend This Applicant for Camp Ministry" field.
16.  Enter any additional comments about the applicant in the "Additional Comments" field.
17.  Sign and date the form by entering your full name and the date in the "Reference Signature" and "Date Signed" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the volunteer or employee applicant.
* **Applicant Email** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email of the applicant.
* **Applicant Phone** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the applicant.
* **Position Applying For** (`position_applying_for`, select_one, required): Select the role the applicant is applying for.
* **Reference Name** (`reference_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the reference provider.
* **Reference Title or Position** (`reference_title`, text, required): Enter the reference provider's title or position.
* **Reference Organization** (`reference_organization`, text, required): Enter the reference provider's organization.
* **Reference Email** (`reference_email`, email, required): Enter the reference provider's contact email.
* **Reference Phone** (`reference_phone`, text, required): Enter the reference provider's phone number.
* **Relationship to Applicant** (`relationship_to_applicant`, select_one, required): Select how the reference provider knows the applicant.
* **How Long Have You Known the Applicant** (`relationship_duration`, select_one, required): Select how long the reference provider has known the applicant.
* **How Would You Rate the Applicant Character** (`applicant_character_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's character from 1 to 5.
* **How Would You Rate Their Work Ethic** (`applicant_work_ethic`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's work ethic from 1 to 5.
* **How Reliable Is the Applicant** (`applicant_reliability`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's reliability.
* **How Would You Rate Their Spiritual Maturity** (`applicant_spiritual_maturity`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's spiritual maturity from 1 to 5.
* **How Well Does the Applicant Work with Others** (`applicant_teamwork_skills`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's teamwork skills.
* **How Well Does the Applicant Interact with Youth** (`applicant_youth_interaction`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's interaction with young people.
* **What Are the Applicant Greatest Strengths** (`applicant_strengths`, text, required): Describe the applicant's strengths.
* **What Areas Could the Applicant Improve** (`applicant_weaknesses`, text, optional): Describe the areas where the applicant could improve.
* **Do You Have Any Concerns About the Applicant** (`concerns_or_red_flags`, text, optional): Enter any concerns or red flags about the applicant.
* **Would You Recommend This Applicant for Camp Ministry** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Check "Yes" or "No" to recommend the applicant for camp ministry.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the applicant.
* **Reference Signature** (`reference_signature`, text, required): Enter your full name as the reference provider.
* **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you sign the form.
