# Camp Volunteer Staff Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by reference providers to provide their opinion on the character and work style of volunteer applicants. Your input will help us make informed decisions about who we bring to our camps.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and honestly, and make sure to answer all required fields.
- If you've filled out this form for an applicant before, you may not need to fill it out again if you're providing an update or additional reference.
- Make sure to use the format and options provided for each field, as this will help us process your response more efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Volunteer Applicant Name**: This is the name of the person applying to be a volunteer at our camp. Please type their full name.
  - **Type**: Text
  - **Required**: Yes
- **Date of Birth**: This is the applicant's date of birth.
  - **Type**: Date
  - **Required**: Yes
- **Position Applied For**: This is the position the applicant is applying for. Please select the correct option from the list.
  - **Type**: Text
  - **Required**: Yes
- **Date of Application**: This is the date the applicant submitted their application.
  - **Type**: Date
  - **Required**: Yes
- **Reference Provider Name**: This is your name as the reference provider.
  - **Type**: Text
  - **Required**: Yes
- **Title or Position**: This is your title or position at your organization or company.
  - **Type**: Text
  - **Required**: Yes
- **Organization or Company**: This is the name of your organization or company.
  - **Type**: Text
  - **Required**: Yes
- **Phone Number**: This is your phone number.
  - **Type**: Text
  - **Required**: Yes
- **Email Address**: This is your email address.
  - **Type**: Email
  - **Required**: Yes
- **How do you know the applicant**: This is how you know the applicant, as a direct supervisor, colleague, teacher, community leader, coach, or other professional.
  - **Type**: Select One
  - **Required**: Yes
- **How long have you known the applicant**: This is how long you've known the applicant.
  - **Type**: Select One
  - **Required**: Yes
- **Reliability and Punctuality**: This is your assessment of the applicant's reliability and punctuality.
  - **Type**: Select One
  - **Required**: Yes
- **Ability to work with children**: This is your assessment of the applicant's ability to work with children.
  - **Type**: Select One
  - **Required**: Yes
- **Communication and Interpersonal skills**: This is your assessment of the applicant's communication and interpersonal skills.
  - **Type**: Select One
  - **Required**: Yes
- **Teamwork ability**: This is your assessment of the applicant's ability to work as part of a team.
  - **Type**: Select One
  - **Required**: Yes
- **Problem-solving and critical thinking**: This is your assessment of the applicant's problem-solving and critical thinking skills.
  - **Type**: Select One
  - **Required**: Yes
- **Character and personal conduct**: This is your assessment of the applicant's character and personal conduct.
  - **Type**: Select One
  - **Required**: Yes
- **Safety awareness and attention to detail**: This is your assessment of the applicant's safety awareness and attention to detail.
  - **Type**: Select One
  - **Required**: Yes
- **Overall Recommendation**: This is your overall recommendation for the applicant.
  - **Type**: Select One
  - **Required**: Yes
- **Key Strengths**: This is an optional field for you to list the applicant's key strengths.
  - **Type**: Text
  - **Required**: No
- **Any Concerns or Additional Comments**: This is an optional field for you to provide any concerns or additional comments about the applicant.
  - **Type**: Text
  - **Required**: No

## Tips
- Please answer all required fields.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- If you're unsure about an answer, please don't hesitate to contact us.
