# Sports Training Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a liability waiver for participants in sports training camps, aiming to gather essential information for emergency contact, medical conditions, and authorization. It's crucial to complete this form accurately and honestly to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Summer Camp Details**: This section is not required, but providing summer camp details can help us better understand your camp experience. If you wish to include this information, please fill in the provided text fields.
2. **Emergency Contact**: Please enter the name, phone number, and relationship of the emergency contact person who will be available to receive updates and take care of you during the camp.
3. **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the camp, please describe them in this text field.
4. **Medical Allergies**: List any allergies you have in this text field.
5. **Emergency Medical Authorization**: Indicate if you authorize us to take medical action on your behalf in case of an emergency.
6. **Parent Signature**: As a parent/guardian, please sign to confirm you have read and agreed to the waiver. Select 'I have read and understand the above waiver and agree to it.' from the dropdown menu.
7. **Coach Signature**: If applicable, sign to confirm the coach has read and agreed to the waiver. Select 'I have read and understand the above waiver and agree to it.' from the dropdown menu.
8. **Medical Release**: Indicate whether a medical release is required for participation.
9. **Facility Release**: Indicate whether a facility release is required for participation.
10. **Name of Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
11. **Phone of Emergency Contact**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
12. **Relationship of Emergency Contact**: Describe the relationship between you and the emergency contact person.
13. **Address of Emergency Contact**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
14. **Emergency Contact Method**: Specify how you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency (e.g., phone, email, or both).
15. **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility where you will be participating.
16. **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility.
17. **Facility City**: Enter the city of the facility.
18. **Facility State**: Enter the state of the facility.
19. **Facility Zip**: Enter the zip code of the facility.
20. **Facility Phone**: Enter the phone number of the facility.
21. **Facility Email**: Enter the email of the facility.
22. **Participant Age**: Enter your age.
23. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
24. **Participant Gender**: Select your gender.
