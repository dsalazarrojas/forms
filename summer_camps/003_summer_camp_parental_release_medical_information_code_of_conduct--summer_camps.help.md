# summer_camp_parental_release_medical_information_code_of_conduct - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for parents or guardians to provide medical information and acknowledge the terms and conditions of the summer camp for their child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the general information about your child.
2. Fill in your child's name and date of birth.
3. Provide your child's address.
4. Enter your name and contact information.
5. Provide any relevant medical history for your child.
6. Answer the consent questions, including agreeing to the terms and conditions.
7. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`Student Information`): This field is for general information about your child, such as their name and address.
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`): Enter your child's name here.
* **Date Of Birth** (`Date Of Birth`): Enter your child's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Address** (`Address`): Enter your child's address, including street address and city.
* **Parent Name** (`Parent Name`): Enter your name, as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Parent Phone** (`Parent Phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Parent Email** (`Parent Email`): Enter your email address.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`): Describe any relevant medical conditions or concerns for your child, such as allergies or medical conditions that may impact their ability to participate in camp activities.
* **Consent Term 1** (`Consent Term 1`): Answer "True" or "False" to indicate whether you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the form.
* **Consent Term 2** (`Consent Term 2`): Answer "True" or "False" to indicate whether you agree to all the terms and conditions.
	+ If you answer "No" to the first question, please answer the medical condition questions that follow.
	+ If you have any medical conditions that may impact your child's ability to participate in camp activities, describe them below.
	+ If you have any allergies or medical conditions that may impact the ability of any camp staff to care for your child, describe them below.
	+ If your child has ever had any medical tests or examinations, describe them below.
	+ If your child has ever had any surgeries, describe them below.
	+ If your child has ever had any medical conditions that may impact their ability to participate in camp activities, describe them below.
* **Terms and Conditions Read** (`Terms and Conditions Read`): Answer "True" or "False" to indicate whether you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions Agree** (`Terms and Conditions Agree`): Answer "True" or "False" to indicate whether you agree to all the terms and conditions.
* **Parent Signature** (`Parent Signature`): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
* **Date Signed** (`Date Signed`): Enter the date you signed the form.

Note: Some fields require a "True" or "False" answer, while others are for descriptive text or dates.
