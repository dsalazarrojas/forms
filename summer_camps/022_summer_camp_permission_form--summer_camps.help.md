# Summer Camp Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Permission Form is designed to collect necessary contact information and permissions from parents or guardians for their child's participation in a summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Parent First Name field with your name as the parent or guardian.
2. Enter your Parent Last Name in the next field.
3. Provide your Email Address where you can be reached for communication.
4. Enter your Phone Number for emergency contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent First Name (Text)**: Enter your first name as the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Last Name (Text)**: Enter your last name as the parent or guardian.
* **Email Address (Email)**: This is where you can be reached for any important communication related to the summer camp.
* **Phone Number (Text)**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in your correct contact information accurately to ensure you receive any important updates or messages from the summer camp.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the camp administration for assistance.
