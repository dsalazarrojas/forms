# Summer Camp Parental Release Medical Information Code of Conduct - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect parental release of medical information and code of conduct for a student attending summer camp. It ensures that parents are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the summer camp, as well as provide necessary medical information to ensure the student's safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form, which should be "summer_camp_parental_release_medical_information_code_of_conduct".
2. Answer the "category" question by selecting "Yes" or "No" depending on whether the student is attending summer camp.
3. Provide a brief description of the student's information in the "description" field.
4. Enter the student's information, including student ID, in the "student_information" field.
5. Provide the parents' information in the "parents_information" field.
6. Answer the "medical_information" question by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each relevant medical condition.
7. Enter the student's health history in the "health_history" field.
8. Select "I confirm that all of the above information is true" in the "consent" field to confirm that the information is correct.
9. Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "terms_and_conditions" field, although this field is not editable.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" depending on whether you have signed the form in the "signature" field.
11. Choose the date you are signing the form from the "date" field.
12. Select the time you are signing the form from the "time" field.
13. If necessary, specify the assigned tool or device for the student in the "assigned_tool" field.
14. Enter the form ID in the "form_id" field, although this is not necessary for the form's purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the form, which should be "summer_camp_parental_release_medical_information_code_of_conduct".
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the student is attending summer camp.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the student's information.
* **Student Information** (`student_information`, text, optional): Enter the student's information, including student ID.
* **Parents Information** (`parents_information`, text, optional): Enter the parents' information.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_information`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" for each relevant medical condition.
* **Health History** (`health_history`, text, optional): Enter the student's health history.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Select "I confirm that all of the above information is true" to confirm that the information is correct.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, optional): This field is not editable and should be read and agreed to by the parent.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have signed the form.
* **Date** (`date`, select_one, optional): Choose the date you are signing the form.
* **Time** (`time`, select_one, optional): Choose the time you are signing the form.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): If necessary, specify the assigned tool or device for the student.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter the form ID, although this is not necessary for the form's purpose.
