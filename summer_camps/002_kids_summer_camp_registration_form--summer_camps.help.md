# Kids Summer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians to register their child for a summer camp program. The form is used to gather demographic details, medical history, and consent for the child's participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information as a parent or guardian in the **Personal Info** field.
2. Enter your contact information in the **Contact Info** field.
3. Share your child's background information in the **Child Background** field.
4. Disclose any medical information relevant to your child's health and well-being in the **Medical Info** field.
5. Indicate whether you grant authorization for your child's participation in the **Authorization** field.
6. Confirm your consent for your child's participation in the **Consent** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Info (ID 1)
* **Personal Info** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant personal details.

### Parent Info (ID 2)
* **Parent Info** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name, contact number, and email for communication purposes.

### Contact Info (ID 3)
* **Contact Info** (`text`, required: false): Enter your preferred contact method, such as phone number, email, or address.

### Child Background (ID 4)
* **Child Background** (`text`, required: false): Describe your child's background, including allergies, medical conditions, or any other relevant information.

### Medical Info (ID 5)
* **Medical Info** (`text`, required: false): Disclose any medical history, allergies, or health conditions that may affect your child's participation in the summer camp.

### Authorization (ID 6)
* **Authorization** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether you grant authorization for your child's participation in the summer camp program, including activities, trips, or excursions.

### Consent (ID 7)
* **Consent** (`select_one`, required: false): Confirm your consent for your child's participation in the summer camp program.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the summer camp staff or administration.
* This form is a requirement for your child's participation in the summer camp program.
