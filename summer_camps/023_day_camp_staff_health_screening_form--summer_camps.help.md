# Day Camp Staff Health Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the Day Camp Staff Health Screening Form, ensuring you complete it accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, start at the first page (**Medical History**) and follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History**: This page gathers general information about the staff member's medical history.
* **Staff Information**: Provide basic staff information, such as name and contact details.
* **Medical History Form**: This page requests more detailed medical history information.
* **Allergies**: Select any known allergies from the dropdown list.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you are aware of.
* **Staff Medical Clearance**: Choose whether the staff member has been cleared for medical reasons.
* **Medical History Form 2**: Another page for medical history information.
* **Vaccination**: List any past vaccinations.
* **Emergency Contact Information**: Provide emergency contact information for the staff member.
* **Emergency Contact Information Form 2**: A duplicate of the previous page, but no further explanation is needed.
* **Medical Record Request**: Choose whether to request medical records.
* **Medication**: Select any ongoing medication from the dropdown list.
* **Medical History Form 3**: Another page for medical history information.
* **Staff Certification**: List any certifications you hold.
* **Staff Credibility**: Choose the staff member's level of credibility.

## Tips

- Please ensure you complete the form accurately and truthfully.
- If you are unsure about any information, consult with your healthcare provider or other relevant medical professionals.
- Ensure you have permission from the staff member before completing this form.
- This form is designed to ensure staff members are cleared for medical reasons before starting work at the day camp.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your supervisor or HR department.
