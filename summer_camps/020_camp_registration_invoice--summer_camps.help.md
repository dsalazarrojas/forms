# Camp Registration Invoice - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Registration Invoice is a document that summarizes the registration details and payment information for a summer camp. It is used to communicate the total amount due for the camp session, including any applicable discounts, fees, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Invoice Number** to auto-generate or enter a custom number.
2. Choose the **Invoice Date**.
3. Select the **Payment Due Date** for the invoice.
4. Fill in the **Camper First Name** and **Camper Last Name**.
5. Enter the **Camper Date of Birth**.
6. Enter the **Parent or Guardian Full Name**.
7. Enter the **Parent or Guardian Email**.
8. Enter the **Parent or Guardian Phone**.
9. Enter the **Home Address**.
10. Select the **City**.
11. Select the **State**.
12. Enter the **ZIP Code**.
13. Choose the **Camp Session Selection** from the options.
14. Enter the **Base Registration Fee**.
15. Select the **Before Camp Care** and **After Camp Care** options, if needed.
16. Select the **Transportation Service** option.
17. Choose any **Camp Merchandise** options.
18. Select any applicable **Sibling Discount** and **Early Bird Discount** options.
19. Select the **Financial Aid or Scholarship** option, if applicable.
20. Calculate the **Subtotal**.
21. Calculate the **Total Discounts**.
22. Enter the **Total Amount Due**.
23. Choose the **Payment Method**.
24. Select the **Payment Status**.
25. Enter the **Deposit Amount Paid**, if applicable.
26. Calculate the **Balance Due**.
27. Enter any **Special Requests or Notes**.
28. Accept the **Terms and Conditions**, by selecting "Yes - I accept".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Invoice Number**: Enter a unique identifier for the invoice.
* **Invoice Date**: The date the invoice is issued.
* **Payment Due Date**: The date payment is due for the invoice.
* **Camper First Name**: First name of the camper.
* **Camper Last Name**: Last name of the camper.
* **Camper Date of Birth**: Date of birth of the camper.
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name**: Full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Contact email of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Contact phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Home Address**: The camper's home address.
* **City**: City or town of the camper's residence.
* **State**: State or province of the camper's residence.
* **ZIP Code**: Postal code of the camper's residence.
* **Camp Session Selection**: Choose the summer camp session the camper is registering for.
* **Base Registration Fee**: Standard camp fee.
* **Before Camp Care** and **After Camp Care**: Select the early drop-off and late pickup services needed.
* **Transportation Service**: Choose the bus transportation service option.
* **Camp Merchandise**: Select any optional items to purchase.
* **Sibling Discount**: Apply a discount if the camper has a sibling registered.
* **Early Bird Discount**: Apply a discount if registered early.
* **Financial Aid or Scholarship**: Select if financial aid or scholarship is applicable.
* **Subtotal**: Calculate the sum of all items.
* **Total Discounts**: Calculate the sum of all discounts applied.
* **Total Amount Due**: Calculate the final amount due.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method.
* **Payment Status**: Select the current payment status.
* **Deposit Amount Paid**: Enter the amount paid as a deposit, if applicable.
* **Balance Due**: Calculate the remaining amount due.
* **Special Requests or Notes**: Enter any additional information or requests.
* **Terms and Conditions Acceptance**: Accept the camp terms and conditions by selecting "Yes - I accept".
