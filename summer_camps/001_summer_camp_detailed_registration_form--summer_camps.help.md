# Summer Camp Detailed Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for staff members to register participants for a summer camp program. The purpose of this form is to gather information necessary for the participant's safety and well-being during the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter the participant's full name.
* **Participant Email**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Date of Birth**: Enter the participant's date of birth.
* **Participant Address**: Enter the participant's address.
* **Participant Postal Code**: Enter the participant's postal code.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Participant Special Needs**: Enter any special needs that the participant has.
* **Participant Medical Notes**: Enter any medical notes that are relevant to the participant.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Enter the relationship between the emergency contact and the participant.
* **Participant Medical Allergies**: Enter any medical allergies that the participant has.
* **Participant Medical Conditions**: Enter any medical conditions that the participant has.
* **Participant Medical Treatment**: Enter any medical treatment that the participant is undergoing.
* **Participant Medical Prescription**: Enter any medical prescription that the participant has.
* **Participant Medical Medication**: Enter any medical medication that the participant is taking.
* **Participant Medical Doctor**: Enter the participant's medical doctor's name.
* **Participant Medical Phone**: Enter the participant's medical doctor's phone number.
* **Participant Medical Email**: Enter the participant's medical doctor's email address.
* **Staff Name**: Enter the staff member's name.
* **Staff Email**: Enter the staff member's email address.
* **Staff Phone**: Enter the staff member's phone number.
* **Staff Position**: Choose the staff member's position (Director, Assistant Director, Counselor).
* **Staff Special Needs**: Enter any special needs that the staff member has.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to the staff member.

Note: The staff member's information is required for the participant's safety and well-being during the camp.
