# mid_session_summer_camp_evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mid_session_summer_camp_evaluation form is used to collect feedback from summer camp attendees after the mid-session evaluation. This feedback will help us to improve the camp experience.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form as follows:

1. **Summer Camp Attendant Feedback** (`summer_camp_attendant_feedback`, text, optional)
	* This field is for any additional feedback you may have about your experience at the summer camp.
2. **Name** (`event_name`, text, optional)
	* Please enter your name to associate your feedback with your identity.
3. **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, select one, optional)
	* Please select the evaluation period you are referring to (Week 1, Week 2, Week 3).
	* This will help us track your feedback across different periods.
4. **Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional)
	* Please enter the time of day you filled out this form.
5. **Rating (Cabin 1)** (`rating_cabin`, select one, optional)
	* Please rate your experience at the cabin (Excellent, Good, Neutral, Bad, Poor).
6. **Rating (Food)** (`rating_food`, select one, optional)
	* Please rate your experience with the food (Excellent, Good, Neutral, Bad, Poor).
7. **Rating (Activities)** (`rating_activities`, select one, optional)
	* Please rate your experience with the activities (Excellent, Good, Neutral, Bad, Poor).
8. **Rating (Facilitators)** (`rating_facilitators`, select one, optional)
	* Please rate your experience with the facilitators (Excellent, Good, Neutral, Bad, Poor).
9. **Rating (Environment)** (`rating_environment`, select one, optional)
	* Please rate your experience with the environment (Excellent, Good, Neutral, Bad, Poor).
10. **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional)
	* Please provide any suggestions you may have to improve the summer camp experience.
11. **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
	* Please enter your email address to allow us to contact you.
12. **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
	* Please enter your phone number to allow us to contact you.
13. **Submit** (`submit`, note, not required)
	* Please click this button to submit your feedback.

## Tips
- Please fill out all the required fields (if any).
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- If you have any suggestions, please enter them in the **Suggestions** field.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by summer camp attendees, and it is not necessary to fill out the form if you are not an attendee.
