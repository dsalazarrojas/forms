# Cheer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cheer Camp Registration Form" is used to register participants for the Cheer Camp. This form collects information needed to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for the participant. 

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant's first name and last name in the "First name" and "Last name" fields respectively.
2. Select the "Age group" that the participant belongs to.
3. Select the participant's "Cheerleading experience level".
4. Enter the participant's "Date of birth".
5. Fill out the "Parent or Guardian name" field with the name of the participant's parent or guardian.
6. Select the relationship of the parent or guardian to the participant in the "Relationship to participant" field.
7. Enter the parent or guardian's email address and phone number.
8. Choose the preferred "Camp session".
9. Select the skills that the participant would like to focus on during the camp, such as "Stunts" or "Tumbling".
10. Indicate whether the participant has athletic shoes or needs to purchase them.
11. If applicable, enter any medical conditions that the participant has.
12. Select any allergies that the participant has.
13. Describe the allergies in the "Allergy details" field.
14. Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
15. Accept or decline the camp's safety waiver and liability release.
16. Select the camp shirt size.
17. Enter the registration fee.
18. Enter any additional information or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (Participant First name, text, required): Enter the first name of the participant.
* **Last name** (Participant Last name, text, required): Enter the last name of the participant.
* **Date of birth** (Participant Date of birth, date, required): Enter the participant's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Age group** (Age group, select_one, required): Select the age group that the participant belongs to.
* **Cheerleading experience level** (Cheerleading experience level, select_one, required): Select the participant's cheerleading experience level.
* **Parent or Guardian name** (Parent or Guardian name, text, required): Enter the name of the participant's parent or guardian.
* **Relationship to participant** (Relationship to participant, select_one, required): Select the relationship of the participant to their parent or guardian.
* **Parent email address** (Parent email address, email, required): Enter the email address of the participant's parent or guardian.
* **Parent phone number** (Parent phone number, text, required): Enter the phone number of the participant's parent or guardian.
* **Preferred camp session** (Preferred camp session, select_one, required): Choose the preferred camp session for the participant.
* **Skills focus** (Skills focus, select_multiple, required): Select all the skills that the participant would like to focus on during the camp.
* **Athletic shoes** (Athletic shoes, select_one, required): Indicate whether the participant has athletic shoes or needs to purchase them.
* **Medical conditions** (Medical conditions, text, required): If applicable, enter any medical conditions that the participant has.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, select_one, required): Select any allergies that the participant has.
* **Allergy details** (Allergy details, text, required): If applicable, describe the participant's allergies.
* **Emergency contact name** (Emergency contact name, text, required): Enter the name of an emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact phone** (Emergency contact phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Safety waiver and liability release** (Safety waiver and liability release, select_one, required): Accept or decline the camp's safety waiver and liability release.
* **Camp shirt size** (Camp shirt size, select_one, required): Select the camp shirt size.
* **Registration fee** (Registration fee, number, required): Enter the registration fee.
* **Additional information or special requests** (Additional information or special requests, text, required): Enter any additional information or special requests.
