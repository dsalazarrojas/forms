# Summer Camp Facilities Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your summer camp's rental and event details so that our team can review and process your request for summer camp facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your summer camp's rental and event details.
2. Provide your contact information so we can follow up with you.
3. Click "submit" to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Summer Camp Facilities Request** (`summer_camp_facilities_request`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the facilities you need for your summer camp.
* **Camp Rental Info** (`camp_rental_info`, text, optional): Provide details about the rental of your summer camp, including the dates and times you need the facilities.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Describe the event or activity you plan to have at your summer camp.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Leave your name, email address, and phone number so we can follow up with you.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click to send your request to our team.
* **Form Submit Confirmation** (`form_submit_confirmation`, text, optional): After submitting your request, you will see this message if your submission was successful.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form to ensure that our team understands your summer camp's needs.
* If you have any attachments or documents to support your request, please attach them to your email or provide a link to them.
* Our team will review and process your request within [insert timeframe].
