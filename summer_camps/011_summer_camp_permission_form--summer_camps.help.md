# Summer Camp Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from parents or guardians of children enrolled in our summer camp. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure we can contact you in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through the form to understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Sign and date the "Parent Signature" field and the "Emergency Contact Signature" field.
4. Ensure all necessary fields are filled out completely before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Information** (`summer_camp_permission_form`, text, required): Please enter your name and contact information as the parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the person you have designated as the emergency contact for your child.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, text, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, number, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your child's name.
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, date, required): Enter your child's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Permission Granted** (`permission_granted`, text, required): Please indicate that you grant permission for your child to attend the summer camp.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, required): Enter your child's address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person you have designated as the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, number, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, required): Select the relationship between the emergency contact and your child.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, required): Sign and date the "Parent Signature" field to confirm your identity and grant permission for your child to attend the summer camp.
* **Date** (`parent_date`, date, required): Date the "Parent Signature" field.
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (`emergency_contact_signature`, note, required): Sign and date the "Emergency Contact Signature" field.
* **Emergency Contact Signature Date** (`emergency_contact_date`, date, required): Date the "Emergency Contact Signature" field.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out before submission.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification.
* This form will be kept confidential and used for emergency contact purposes only.
* Please do not submit this form if your child is not enrolled in the summer camp or if you are not a parent or guardian of a child enrolled in the summer camp.
