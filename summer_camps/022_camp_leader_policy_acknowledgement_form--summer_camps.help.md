# Camp Leader Policy Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain acknowledgment from camp leaders that they have reviewed and agreed to certain policies. The policies outlined in this form are essential for maintaining a safe and efficient working environment within the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your **Camp Leader Name** (Field 1) with your full name as it appears on your records.
2. Enter your **Email Address** (Field 2) for official communications and record-keeping purposes.
3. Select the **Policy Review Date** (Field 3) when you last reviewed the policies.
4. List the **Policies Acknowledged** (Field 4) with the relevant policies you have reviewed.
5. Indicate whether you have completed **Required Training** (Field 5) or not.
6. Sign with your full name as **Electronic Signature** (Field 6).
7. If applicable, enter your **Supervisor's Name** (Field 7) for verification.
8. Add any additional **Acknowledgment Notes** (Field 8) if necessary.
9. (Optional) Select the **Effective Date** (Field 9) when the acknowledgment becomes effective.
10. If required, indicate whether renewal of this acknowledgment is needed **Annually** (Field 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Camp Leader Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your records.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
  - Hint: Full name of the camp leader
- **Email Address**: Enter your email address for official communications and record-keeping purposes.
  - Type: email
  - Required: true
  - Hint: For official communications and records
- **Policy Review Date**: Select the date you last reviewed the policies.
  - Type: date
  - Required: true
  - Hint: Date the policies were reviewed
- **Policies Acknowledged**: List the policies you have reviewed and agreed to.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
  - Hint: List of key policies the leader acknowledges
- **Training Completed**: Indicate whether you have completed required training.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: true
  - Hint: Has required training been completed
- **Electronic Signature**: Sign with your full name as a digital signature.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
  - Hint: Type your full name as signature
- **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor for verification purposes.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
  - Hint: Name of supervisor verifying acknowledgement
- **Acknowledgment Notes**: Add any additional notes or clarification regarding the acknowledgment.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
  - Hint: Any additional notes or clarifications
- **Effective Date**: Select the date the acknowledgment becomes effective (optional).
  - Type: date
  - Required: false
  - Hint: Date the acknowledgement becomes effective
- **Renewal Required**: Indicate whether renewal acknowledgment is required annually.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: false
  - Hint: Whether renewal acknowledgement is required annually

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have reviewed the policies before submitting this form.
- If you have questions or concerns about any policy, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
- Make sure to keep your email address up-to-date for official communications.
