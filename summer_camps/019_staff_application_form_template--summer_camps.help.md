# staff_application_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for staff members to provide their basic information and experience when applying for a position at a summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Basic Info" section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. In the "Work Experience" section, describe your previous work experience, especially any relevant experience you have working with children or in similar roles.
3. In the "References" section, list the names and contact information of two people who can vouch for your skills and character.
4. The "Job Applicant Info" section is where you can provide more information about the position you're applying for and your qualifications.
5. In the "Education" section, list your educational background and any relevant training or certifications.
6. The "Availability" section is where you can indicate your availability to work during the summer season.
7. In the "Skills" section, list any skills you have that may be relevant to the position.
8. The "Contact Details" section is where you can provide more contact information and any other relevant details.
9. Finally, in the "Uploaded Files" section, you can attach any supporting documents or files that you'd like to include with your application.
