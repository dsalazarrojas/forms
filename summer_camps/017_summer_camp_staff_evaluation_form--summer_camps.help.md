# Summer Camp Staff Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Staff Evaluation Form is a self-assessment tool designed to help staff members reflect on their experience and performance at a summer camp. It allows staff members to evaluate their own strengths and areas for improvement, providing valuable feedback to camp administrators.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully and answer all relevant questions.
2. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
3. Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff** (`staff`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your position at the summer camp (e.g., staff member, camp director, counselor).
* **Camp** (`camp`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the summer camp where you worked.
* **Counselor** (`counselor`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter "counselor" if this is your primary role at the camp (if unsure, leave blank).
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: `false`): Rate your overall performance at the camp, using a numerical value from 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: `false`): Share any additional thoughts, suggestions, or feedback about your experience at the camp.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Take care to complete all fields, but do not feel obligated to fill out any field that does not apply to you.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, contact your camp administrator.
* Submit the form by the end of the camp season to ensure timely feedback.
