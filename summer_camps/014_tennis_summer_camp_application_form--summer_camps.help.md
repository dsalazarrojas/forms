# Tennis Summer Camp Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tennis Summer Camp Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all participants. This form gathers personal and contact details, medical information, and emergency contact data to help the camp staff prepare and manage the camp activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately and efficiently, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your application data, including your name, contact email, contact phone number (if applicable), and other required fields.
2. Provide details about your level of tennis experience, your goals for attending the camp, and any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your participation.
3. Enter emergency contact information, including name, phone number, email, and other relevant details.
4. If applicable, provide additional medical information, such as medications or special needs.
5. Enter your guardian's information, including name, phone number, email, and address (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, **required**): Enter your primary email address for communication and updates.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, **optional**): If you have a phone number, please enter it for camp staff to reach you.
- **Applicant Address** (`applicant_address`, text, **required**): Enter your current address for camp purposes.
- **Age** (`age`, number, **required**): Enter your age to ensure we can accommodate your needs.
- **Level of Tennis Experience** (`level_of_tennis_experience`, select_one, **required**): Choose your level of tennis experience from High or Low.
- **Tennis Goals** (`tennis_goals`, text, **required**): Describe your goals for attending the camp, such as improving skills or having fun.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, **required**): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, **required**): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, **optional**): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation, please describe them here.
- **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, **optional**): If you have any allergies or sensitivities, please list them here.
- **Medications** (`medications`, text, **optional**): If you are taking any medications, please list them here.
- **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, text, **optional**): If you have any special needs, please describe them here.
- **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of your guardian or responsible adult.
- **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, **required**): Enter the phone number of your guardian.
- **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, email, **required**): Enter the email of your guardian.
- **Guardian Address** (`guardian_address`, text, **optional**): Enter your guardian's address, if different from your own.
- **Guardian Work Phone** (`guardian_work_phone`, text, **optional**): If your guardian has a work phone, please enter it here.
- **Guardian Home Phone** (`guardian_home_phone`, text, **optional**): If your guardian has a home phone, please enter it here.
- **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, **required**): Enter your student ID or equivalent identifier.
- **School Name** (`school_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of your school.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **required**): Enter your date of birth to ensure we can verify your age.
- **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, note, **optional**): If you have any further medical information, please describe it here.
