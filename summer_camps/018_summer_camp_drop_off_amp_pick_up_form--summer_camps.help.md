# Summer Camp Drop Off & Pick Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a camper's drop off and pick up details for a summer camp. It is designed to ensure a safe and smooth experience for your child while at the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and submit it before the camper's arrival at the summer camp.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Info** (`parent_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information about yourself, such as your name, contact number, and address.
* **Drop Off Info** (`drop_off_info`, `text`, required/optional): Here, you can provide the drop off details, including the time and date of drop off, and any other relevant information.
* **Camp Info** (`camp_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for any additional information about the camp, such as the camp's name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Pick Up Info** (`pick_up_info`, `text`, required/optional): In this field, you can provide the pick up details, including the time and date of pick up, and any other relevant information.
* **Camper Info** (`camper_info`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of your child, including their name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, `text`, required/optional): If your child has any special needs or requirements, please describe them here.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required/optional): List any medical conditions your child may have that could impact their camp experience.
* **Medical Administrators** (`medical_administrators`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the name and contact information of your child's medical administrators.
* **Camper Medical Info** (`camper_medical_info`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your child's medical history, including any allergies or medical conditions.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, `text`, required/optional): List any emergency contacts, including their name, phone number, and relationship to your child.
* **Camper Medical Insurance** (`camper_medical_insurance`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your child's medical insurance information, including the insurance provider and plan.
* **Camper Medical Insurance Card** (`camper_medical_insurance_card`, `text`, required/optional): Upload your child's medical insurance card or provide a photocopy.
* **Camper Medical Insurance Provider** (`camper_medical_insurance_provider`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the name of your child's medical insurance provider.
* **Camper Medical Insurance Plan** (`camper_medical_insurance_plan`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your child's medical insurance plan, including any specific coverage or requirements.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any medical procedures your child has undergone or is currently undergoing.
* **Medical Procedure Details** (`medical_procedure_details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional details about the medical procedure.
* **Medication** (`medication`, `text`, required/optional): If your child takes any medication, list it here, including the dosage and frequency.
* **Medication Admin** (`medication_admin`, `text`, required/optional): Describe how the medication is administered, such as orally or via injection.
* **Medication Info** (`medication_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional information about your child's medication.
* **Camper T-Shirt Size** (`camper_tshirt_size`, `text`, required/optional): Specify your child's T-shirt size.
* **Camper T-Shirt Name** (`camper_tshirt_name`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the name of your child's T-shirt.
* **Camper Shoes** (`camper_shoes`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your child's shoes, including the type and brand.
* **Camper Extra Info** (`camper_extra_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional information about your child that is not covered in the other fields.
* **Camper Allergies** (`camper_allergies`, `text`, required/optional): If your child has any allergies, list them here.
* **Camper Other Info** (`camper_other_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any other information about your child that is not covered in the other fields.
