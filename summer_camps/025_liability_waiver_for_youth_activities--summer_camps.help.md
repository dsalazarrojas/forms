# Liability Waiver For Youth Activities - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather essential contact and medical information from parents or guardians of children participating in youth activities, such as summer camps or sports programs. It also includes a section for the child to sign a liability waiver, indicating that they understand and accept the risks involved in the activity.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Guardian Consent**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the guardian or parent has given their consent for the child to participate in the activity.
2. **Emergency Contacts**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the guardian or parent has listed emergency contact information.
3. **Signatures**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the guardian or parent has signed the waiver.
4. **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of an emergency contact in case of an emergency.
5. **Guardian Phone**: Enter the phone number of the guardian or parent.
6. **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions that the child has.
7. **Allergies**: List any allergies that the child has.
8. **Medical Treatment**: List any medical treatment or medication that the child is currently taking.
9. **Emergency Medical Treatment**: List any emergency medical treatment that the child may require.
10. **Waiver Text**: Enter any additional information or comments about the child's health or participation in the activity.
11. **Waiver Date**: Enter the date the waiver was signed.
12. **Waiver Signatures**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the child has signed the waiver.

## Tips

* Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
