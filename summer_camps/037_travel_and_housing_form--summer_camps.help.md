# Travel And Housing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a summer camp, specifically for travel and housing arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields according to the guidelines and instructions provided.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **summer_camp_travel_information** (summer_camp_travel_information_form, text, optional): This field is for any additional information about the camper's travel plans.
* **Housing Information** (housing_information_form, select_one, optional): Please select 'Yes' if the camper will be staying in the camp's housing, or 'No' if they will be staying elsewhere.
* **Parent Information** (parent_info_form, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about the parent or guardian's contact details.
* **Camper Details** (camper_details_form, text, optional): This field is for any additional information about the camper.
* **Camper Photo** (camper_photo_form, text, optional): Upload a photo of the camper.
* **Camper Documentation** (camper_documentation_form, text, optional): Provide any necessary documentation for the camper.
* **Camper Signature** (camper_signature_form, text, optional): Sign this field to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Guardian Details** (guardian_details_form, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the guardian.
* **Guardian Information** (guardian_info_form, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the guardian.
* **Guardian Photo** (guardian_photo_form, text, optional): Upload a photo of the guardian.
* **Guardian Documentation** (guardian_documentation_form, text, optional): Provide any necessary documentation for the guardian.
* **Guardian Signature** (guardian_signature_form, text, optional): Sign this field to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Travel Details** (travel_details_form, text, optional): This field is for any additional information about the travel arrangements.
* **Travel Agency** (travel_agency_form, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the travel agency.
* **Travel Documentation** (travel_documentation_form, text, optional): Provide any necessary documentation for the travel.
* **Travel Photo** (travel_photo_form, text, optional): Upload a photo of the travel arrangements.
* **Travel Signature** (travel_signature_form, text, optional): Sign this field to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Other Travel Details** (other_travel_details_form, text, optional): This field is for any other information about the travel arrangements.
* **Other Travel Agency** (other_travel_agency_form, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the other travel agency.
* **Other Travel Documentation** (other_travel_documentation_form, text, optional): Provide any necessary documentation for the other travel.
* **Other Travel Photo** (other_travel_photo_form, text, optional): Upload a photo of the other travel arrangements.
* **Other Travel Signature** (other_travel_signature_form, text, optional): Sign this field to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact_form, text, optional): Provide the emergency contact name and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (emergency_contact_number_form, text, optional): Provide the emergency contact phone number.
* **Submit** (submit_form, text, optional): Click this button to submit the form.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
* Contact the summer camp administrators if you have any questions or concerns about the form.
