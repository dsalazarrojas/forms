# Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for each camper at the summer camp. Please follow this guide to understand what to expect and how to complete the form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form carefully**: Double-check the accuracy of all entered data.
2. **Complete each section**: Make sure to enter all required information before proceeding to the next section.
3. **Submit once complete**: Click the submit button when all fields are filled and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Camper First Name** (`camper_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the camper.
- **Camper Last Name** (`camper_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the camper.
- **Camper Nickname** (`camper_nickname`, text, optional): Enter an optional nickname for the camper.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the birth date of the camper.
- **Age at Start of Camp** (`age_at_camp`, number, required): Enter the age of the camper at the start of the camp session.
- **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select the camper's gender.
- **Grade Completed This Year** (`grade_completed`, select_one, required): Select the grade the camper has completed this year.
- **Parent or Guardian 1 Full Name** (`parent_guardian_1_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the parent or guardian 1.
- **Parent or Guardian 1 Email** (`parent_guardian_1_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the parent or guardian 1.
- **Parent or Guardian 1 Phone** (`parent_guardian_1_phone`, text, required): Enter the primary phone number of the parent or guardian 1.
- **Parent or Guardian 2 Full Name** (`parent_guardian_2_name`, text, optional): Enter the full name of the parent or guardian 2 (if applicable).
- **Parent or Guardian 2 Phone** (`parent_guardian_2_phone`, text, optional): Enter the secondary phone number of the parent or guardian 2 (if applicable).
- **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter the camper's home address.
- **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city or town of the camper's residence.
- **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of the camper's residence.
- **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code of the camper's residence.
- **Emergency Contact 1 Name** (`emergency_contact_1_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact 1.
- **Emergency Contact 1 Phone** (`emergency_contact_1_phone`, text, required): Enter the primary phone number of the emergency contact 1.
- **Emergency Contact 1 Relationship** (`emergency_contact_1_relationship`, text, required): Enter the relationship of the camper to the emergency contact 1.
- **Authorized Pick-Up Persons** (`authorized_pickup_persons`, text, required): List all individuals authorized to pick up the camper.
- **Medical Conditions or Health Concerns** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): List any medical conditions or health concerns of the camper.
- **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): Include any food, insect, or medication allergies of the camper.
- **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): List any current medications the camper takes, with dosages and schedule.
- **Primary Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the camper's primary physician.
- **Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, text, required): Enter the primary phone number of the camper's physician.
- **Medical Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the camper's medical insurance provider (if applicable).
- **Insurance Policy Number** (`insurance_policy_number`, text, optional): Enter the policy or member ID of the camper's insurance provider (if applicable).
- **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable dietary restrictions for the camper.
- **Swimming Ability** (`swimming_ability`, select_one, required): Select the camper's swimming ability level.
- **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, select_one, required): Indicate if the camper has any special needs.
- **Special Needs Description** (`special_needs_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of any special needs if applicable.
- **Camp Session Selection** (`camp_session`, select_one, required): Choose the desired camp session.
- **Before Camp Care Needed** (`before_camp_care`, select_one, optional): Indicate if before-care or early drop-off services are required.
- **After Camp Care Needed** (`after_camp_care`, select_one, optional): Indicate if after-care or late pickup services are required.
- **Transportation Needed** (`transportation_needed`, select_one, required): Select the transportation options (morning only, afternoon only, or both) for the camper.
- **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, required): Select the size of the camp t-shirt for the camper.
- **Photo and Video Release** (`photo_release`, select_one, required): Indicate if permission is given to use photos and videos of the camper.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method for the camp session.
- **How Did You Hear About This Camp** (`how_heard_about_camp`, select_multiple, optional): Select all sources from which you heard about this camp.
