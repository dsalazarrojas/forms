# Remote Artist Camp Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Artist Camp Booking Form is designed to collect information from artists and camps for remote artist camps. This form helps the organization gather data on various aspects of the camps and artists, such as dates, discipline, preferences, and artist and camp contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the dates for the camp by selecting multiple options from the drop-down menu.
2. Select the discipline (or type) of art you will be participating in at the camp.
3. Indicate your preferences for the camp by selecting multiple options.
4. Provide participant data, including contact information.
5. Choose whether the artist or camp contact will be responsible for the camp.
6. Select the artist's preferences for the camp.
7. Choose the camp's preferences for the camp.
8. Indicate the participant's preferences for the camp.
9. Select other preferences for the camp.
10. Confirm the camp booking details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Booking Form** (`artist_booking_form`): This field is the main section of the form where you will provide basic information about the artist and the camp.
* **Dates** (`dates`): Select one or multiple dates for the camp from the drop-down menu.
* **Discipline** (`discipline`): Choose the type of art or discipline you will be participating in at the camp.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`): Indicate your preferences for the camp, such as artist preferences, camp preferences, participant preferences, other preferences, and camp booking details.
* **Participant Data** (`participant_data`): Provide contact information of participants who will be attending the camp.
* **Artist Contact** (`artist_contact`): Choose whether the artist or camp contact will be responsible for the camp.
* **Camp Contact** (`camp_contact`): Choose whether the camp contact will be responsible for the camp.
* **Artist Preferences** (`artist_preferences`): Select the artist's preferences for the camp.
* **Camp Preferences** (`camp_preferences`): Choose the camp's preferences for the camp.
* **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`): Indicate the participant's preferences for the camp.
* **Other Preferences** (`other_preferences`): Select other preferences for the camp.
* **Camp Booking Details** (`camp_booking_details`): Confirm the camp booking details.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`): Choose the payment details for the camp.
* **Artist Camps** (`artist_camps`): Choose whether you will be participating in an artist camp.
* **Camp Type** (`camp_type`): Choose the type of camp you will be participating in.
