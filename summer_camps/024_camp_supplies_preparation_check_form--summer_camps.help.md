# Camp Supplies Preparation Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Camp Supplies Preparation Check Form" is a critical document used to ensure that campers are well-prepared for their stay at the summer camp. This form helps parents or guardians double-check what their child has packed and what they need to purchase or borrow, reducing the risk of accidents or inconveniences during the camp stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the camper's name and name of the parent or guardian.
2. Select the camp session the camper will be attending.
3. Check the status of each item, selecting "Packed and ready" if the camper has all the necessary items, or "Need to purchase" or "Need to borrow from camp" if they do not.
4. Confirm that all prohibited items have been removed.
5. Provide any additional information about the camper's preparation.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Name** (`camper_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the camper, as it should appear on their identification documents.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the parent or guardian completing the form.
* **Camp Session** (`camp_session`, select_one, required): Select the camp session the camper will be attending.
* **Sleeping Bag** (`sleeping_bag`, select_one, required): Select whether the camper has a packed and ready sleeping bag or needs to purchase or borrow one from the camp.
* **Sleeping Pad or Mattress** (`sleeping_pad`, select_one, required): Select whether the camper has a packed and ready sleeping pad or mattress or needs to purchase or borrow one from the camp.
* **Pillow** (`pillow`, select_one, required): Select whether the camper has a packed and ready pillow or needs to purchase one.
* **Sheets and Blanket** (`sheets_blanket`, select_one, required): Select whether the camper has sheets and a blanket, if using camp cots. Otherwise, select "Not needed".
* **Towels** (`towels`, select_one, required): Select the number of towels the camper has packed.
* **Change of Clothes** (`clothing_quantity`, select_one, required): Select the number of outfits the camper has packed for the week.
* **Underwear and Socks** (`underwear_socks`, select_one, required): Select whether the camper has packed enough underwear and socks or needs to pack more.
* **Pajamas** (`pajamas`, select_one, required): Select whether the camper has packed a set of pajamas or needs to pack one.
* **Swimwear** (`swimwear`, select_one, required): Select the number of swimsuits the camper has packed.
* **Footwear** (`footwear`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of footwear the camper has packed, including sneakers or athletic shoes, sandals with straps, water shoes, rain boots, or flip flops for showering.
* **Rain Gear** (`rain_gear`, select_one, required): Select whether the camper has packed a rain jacket or poncho.
* **Toiletries** (`toiletries`, select_multiple, required): Select the personal hygiene items the camper has packed, including toothbrush and toothpaste, soap and shampoo, deodorant, hairbrush or comb, sunscreen, insect repellent, lip balm, and any other items.
* **Flashlight with Extra Batteries** (`flashlight`, select_one, required): Select whether the camper has a packed and ready flashlight or needs to purchase one.
* **Reusable Water Bottle** (`water_bottle`, select_one, required): Select whether the camper has a packed and ready water bottle or needs to purchase one.
* **Backpack or Daypack** (`backpack_daypack`, select_one, required): Select whether the camper has a packed and ready backpack or daypack or needs to purchase one.
* **Laundry Bag** (`laundry_bag`, select_one, required): Select whether the camper has a packed and ready laundry bag or needs to pack one.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_one, required): Select whether the camper has packed their medications in original containers or not.
* **Spending Money for Camp Store** (`spending_money`, select_one, optional): Select whether the camper has packed spending money or will provide it later.
* **Prohibited Items Not Packed** (`prohibited_items_check`, select_one, required): Confirm that all prohibited items have been removed.
* **Luggage or Duffel Bag** (`luggage`, select_one, required): Select whether the camper has a packed and labeled luggage or duffel bag.
* **All Items Labeled with Camper Name** (`labeled_items`, select_one, required): Select whether all items are labeled with the camper's name for identification.
* **Additional Items Packed** (`additional_items`, text, optional): Enter any additional items not listed in the form.
* **Any Concerns About Preparation** (`preparation_concerns`, text, optional): Describe any concerns or issues with the camper's preparation.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required): Enter your full name as the parent or guardian.
* **Date Completed** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you completed this form.

By following these steps and filling out the form, you can ensure that your child has everything they need for a safe and enjoyable summer camp experience.
