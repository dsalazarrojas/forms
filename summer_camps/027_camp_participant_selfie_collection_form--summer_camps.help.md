# Camp Participant Selfie Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect selfies and relevant metadata of camp participants for the camp's records and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

### General Instructions

1. Please fill out the form with the required information and submit.
2. If a field is not applicable, please select "Unknown" or leave it blank.
3. Please make sure to provide a brief description of the photo content.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Enter the participant's **Participant Name** in the first field.
2. Enter the **Date Photo Taken** in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Enter the **Name of Person Uploading Photo**, which can be either a camper or staff member.
4. Select whether you have the **Consent for Photo Use**.
5. If applicable, provide a **Photo Description** of the photo content.
6. If applicable, select the **Location Where Photo Was Taken**.
7. If applicable, provide the name of the **Photographer Name**.
8. If applicable, enter any **Usage Limitations** for the photo.
9. (Optional) Select whether you contacted the **Parent/Guardian** about the photo.
10. (Optional) Add any **Additional Notes** about the photo submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: <label> (Required) Enter the name of the camper who is in the selfie.
* **Date Photo Taken**: <label> (Required, Format: mm/dd/yyyy) Enter the date when the selfie was taken.
* **Name of Person Uploading Photo**: <label> (Required) Enter the name of the person uploading the photo (camper or staff member).
* **Consent for Photo Use**: <label> (Required) Select whether you have the consent to use the photo in camp communications.
* **Photo Description**: <label> (Optional) Brief description of the photo content.
* **Location Where Photo Was Taken**: <label> (Optional) Enter the camp area or activity where the photo was taken.
* **Photographer Name**: <label> (Optional) Enter the name of the person who took the photo.
* **Usage Limitations**: <label> (Optional) Enter any restrictions on how the photo may be used.
* **Parent Contacted**: <label> (Optional) Select whether you contacted the parent or guardian about the photo.
* **Additional Notes**: <label> (Optional) Enter any other context for the photo submission.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the camp staff for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
