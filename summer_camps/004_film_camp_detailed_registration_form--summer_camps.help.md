# film_camp_detailed_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "film_camp_detailed_registration_form" is designed to collect detailed information from participants for a summer film camp. This form is meant to gather essential data for camp organizers to better understand participants' needs, preferences, and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the participant before starting.
2. Enter the participant's basic information, such as name and contact details.
3. Provide additional information about the participant's preferences and additional details.
4. Enter any additional information that doesn't fit in the previous fields.
5. Verify your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant info** (`participant_info`, text, optional):
  This is the main section where you can provide a brief overview of the participant.
* **Contact info** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
  Enter the participant's contact details, such as address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Participant preferences** (`participant_preferences`, text, optional):
  Describe the participant's preferences, likes, and dislikes.
* **Additional info** (`additional_info`, text, optional):
  Enter any additional information that doesn't fit in the previous fields.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, number, optional):
  Enter a unique participant ID, if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
  Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
  Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional):
  Enter the participant's date of birth.
