# Camp Staff Expression Of Interest - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help us understand your background, interests, and qualifications to work at the summer camp. This information will be used to process your application and determine the best fit for you among our various staff roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each section as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields.
3. Select all relevant options that apply to you.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city or town you reside in.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province you reside in.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Type of Interest** (`interest_type`, select_one, required): Select which type of staff role you are interested in:
	+ Volunteer staff member
	+ Paid staff member
	+ Internship
	+ Ministry partner
	+ Activity specialist
	+ Support staff
* **Roles You Are Interested In** (`roles_interested_in`, select_multiple, required): Select all roles you are interested in:
	+ Camp counselor
	+ Activity leader
	+ Lifeguard
	+ Worship leader
	+ Sports coach
	+ Arts and crafts
	+ Nature guide
	+ Kitchen staff
	+ Maintenance
	+ Administration
	+ Medical staff
	+ Other
* **Your Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required): Select all sessions you are available to work:
	+ Session 1 only
	+ Session 2 only
	+ Session 3 only
	+ Session 4 only
	+ Full summer
	+ Weekends only
	+ Flexible
* **Previous Volunteer Experience** (`previous_volunteer_experience`, select_one, required): Select your experience level:
	+ Yes - with youth
	+ Yes - with adults
	+ Yes - at a camp
	+ No - first time
* **Describe Your Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience_details`, text, optional): Provide details about your volunteer experience.
* **Skills and Talents** (`skills_and_talents`, select_multiple, required): Select all skills and talents you can offer:
	+ Youth mentoring
	+ Sports coaching
	+ Music or worship
	+ Arts and crafts
	+ Outdoor skills
	+ First aid or medical
	+ Cooking
	+ Maintenance or repair
	+ Photography
	+ Technology
	+ Administration
	+ Counseling
	+ Other
* **Relevant Certifications** (`certifications`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant certifications:
	+ None
	+ CPR
	+ First aid
	+ Lifeguard
	+ Water safety
	+ Outdoor leadership
	+ Youth ministry
	+ Teaching
	+ Coaching
	+ Other
* **Why Are You Interested in Serving at This Camp** (`why_interested`, text, required): Explain why you are interested in serving at this camp.
* **Tell Us About Your Spiritual Background** (`spiritual_background`, text, optional): Share your spiritual background, if you feel comfortable doing so.
* **Preferred Age Group to Work With** (`age_group_preference`, select_multiple, required): Select the age group you prefer to work with:
	+ 5-7 years
	+ 8-10 years
	+ 11-13 years
	+ 14-16 years
	+ 17-18 years
	+ No preference
* **Are You Comfortable with Overnight Duties** (`comfortable_with_overnight`, select_one, required): Select your comfort level:
	+ Yes - fully comfortable
	+ Somewhat comfortable
	+ No - day only
* **Do You Prefer Working in a Team or Independently** (`team_or_individual`, select_one, required): Select your preference:
	+ Prefer team
	+ Prefer independent
	+ Comfortable with both
* **Can You Provide References** (`references_available`, select_one, required): Select your reference availability:
	+ Yes - 3 references
	+ Yes - 2 references
	+ Yes - 1 reference
	+ False
* **Are You Willing to Undergo a Background Check** (`background_check_willing`, select_one, required): Select your willingness:
	+ Yes - willing
	+ False
* **How Did You Hear About This Camp** (`how_heard_about_camp`, select_multiple, optional): Select how you heard about this camp:
	+ Church referral
	+ Friend or family
	+ Social media
	+ Website
	+ Previous volunteer
	+ Community event
	+ Other
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you signed the form.

Please review the form carefully and answer each question as accurately as possible. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
