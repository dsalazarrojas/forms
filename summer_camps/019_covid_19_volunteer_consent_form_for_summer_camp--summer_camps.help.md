# COVID 19 Volunteer Consent Form For Summer Camp - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from volunteers about their COVID-19 vaccination status, medical history, and understanding of the risks associated with volunteering at the summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Volunteer Full Name**, which is required.
2. Provide your **Date of Birth** in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Choose the **Summer Camp Name** you will be volunteering at.
4. Indicate the **Camp Session or Dates** you will be volunteering.
5. Enter your **Email Address**, which is required.
6. Enter your **Phone Number**, which is required.
7. Provide your **Emergency Contact Name**, which is required.
8. Enter your **Emergency Contact Phone Number**, which is required.
9. Choose your **COVID-19 Vaccination Status**, which is required.
10. If you are **Fully vaccinated**, **Partially vaccinated**, or **Unvaccinated**, choose the corresponding option.
11. If you are **Partially vaccinated**, enter the **Vaccine Type Received**.
12. If you are **Partially vaccinated**, you may enter the **Date of Last Vaccination**.
13. Choose whether you **Currently Have COVID-19 Symptoms**.
14. Choose whether you have had **Recent Exposure to COVID-19** within the past 14 days.
15. Choose whether you have had a **Previous COVID-19 Infection**.
16. Choose whether you **Acknowledge the risks of COVID-19 transmission**.
17. Choose whether you **Agree to follow camp COVID-19 safety protocols**.
18. Choose whether you **Waive liability for COVID-19 exposure**.
19. Sign your name as the **Volunteer Signature**.
20. Enter the **Date Signed**, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be recorded.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Summer Camp Name**: Choose the summer camp you will be volunteering at.
* **Camp Session or Dates**: Specify the dates you will be volunteering.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status**: Choose your COVID-19 vaccination status.
*   *Fully vaccinated*: You have completed the full series of COVID-19 vaccinations.
*   *Partially vaccinated*: You have received at least one COVID-19 vaccine dose.
*   *Unvaccinated*: You have not been vaccinated against COVID-19.
*   *Prefer not to answer*: You do not want to disclose your vaccination status.
* **Vaccine Type Received**: If partially vaccinated, enter the type of vaccine you received (e.g., Pfizer, Moderna, Johnson & Johnson).
* **Date of Last Vaccination**: If partially vaccinated, enter the date of your last vaccination.
* **Currently Have COVID-19 Symptoms**: Choose yes if you currently have COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Recent Exposure to COVID-19**: Choose yes if you have had recent exposure to COVID-19 within the past 14 days.
* **Previous COVID-19 Infection**: Choose yes if you have had a previous COVID-19 infection.
* **Acknowledge the risks of COVID-19 transmission**: Choose yes if you acknowledge the risks of COVID-19 transmission.
* **Agree to follow camp COVID-19 safety protocols**: Choose yes if you agree to follow the camp's COVID-19 safety protocols.
* **Waive liability for COVID-19 exposure**: Choose yes if you waive liability for COVID-19 exposure.
* **Volunteer Signature**: Sign your name as a volunteer.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you sign this form in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please contact the camp administration.
* Please provide complete and accurate information.
* Please sign the form with your full name as electronic signature.
* Please review this form carefully before submitting it.
