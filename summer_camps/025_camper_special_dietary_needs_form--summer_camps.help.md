# Camper Special Dietary Needs Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camper Special Dietary Needs Form is used to gather information about a camper's dietary restrictions, allergies, and preferences to ensure their safety and well-being during the camp experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Camper Information**: This section is used to gather basic information about the camper.
    *   Enter the camper's **Full Name** in the provided text field.
    *   Provide the **Date of Birth** in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
    *   Enter the camper's **Age**.
    *   Select the **Camp Session Attending** from the available options.
2.  **Parent or Guardian Information**: This section is used to gather information from the parent or guardian.
    *   Enter the **Parent or Guardian Name** in the provided text field.
    *   Enter the **Email Address** of the parent or guardian.
    *   Enter the **Phone Number** of the parent or guardian.
3.  **Dietary Restrictions**: This section is used to identify the camper's dietary restrictions.
    *   Select `Yes` if the camper has dietary restrictions.
    *   If yes, select all the types of dietary restrictions that apply from the provided options (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut Allergy, Shellfish Allergy, Kosher, Halal, Other).
    *   Provide a brief description of the dietary restriction in the **Describe the dietary restriction** field.
4.  **Food Allergies**: This section is used to identify the camper's food allergies.
    *   Select `Yes` if the camper has food allergies.
    *   If yes, select all the allergens that apply from the provided options (Peanuts, Tree Nuts, Milk, Eggs, Fish, Shellfish, Soy, Wheat, Sesame, Other).
    *   Select the **Allergy Severity** from the provided options (Mild, Moderate, Severe, Life-threatening).
    *   Provide any **Allergy Symptoms or Reactions** experienced by the camper.
    *   Select `True` or `False` if the camper has an **Epinephrine auto-injector**.
5.  **Food Preferences and Likes/Dislikes**: This section is used to gather information about the camper's food preferences.
    *   Enter the **Favorite foods** of the camper.
    *   Enter the **Foods that your child dislikes**.
    *   Provide any **foods that need special preparation**.
6.  **Acknowledgment**: This section is used to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
    *   Select **Yes, I confirm** to confirm the accuracy of the provided information.
    *   If you are unsure about any of the information, please contact the camp staff before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Full Name**: Enter the camper's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
*   **Date of Birth**: Provide the camper's date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
*   **Age**: Enter the camper's age.
*   **Camp Session Attending**: Select the camp session the camper will be attending.
*   **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
*   **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
*   **Does your child have dietary restrictions**: Select `Yes` if the camper has dietary restrictions.
*   **Type of Dietary Restriction**: Select all the types of dietary restrictions that apply.
*   **Describe the dietary restriction**: Provide a brief description of the dietary restriction.
*   **Does your child have food allergies**: Select `Yes` if the camper has food allergies.
*   **Allergens**: Select all the allergens that apply.
*   **Allergy Severity**: Select the severity of the allergy.
*   **Allergy Symptoms or Reactions**: Provide any allergy symptoms or reactions experienced by the camper.
*   **Does child have Epinephrine auto-injector**: Select `True` or `False` if the camper has an Epinephrine auto-injector.
*   **Favorite foods**: Enter the camper's favorite foods.
*   **Foods that your child dislikes**: Enter the foods that the camper dislikes.
*   **Foods that need special preparation**: Provide any foods that need special preparation.
*   **Accuracy Confirmation**: Select **Yes, I confirm** to confirm the accuracy of the provided information.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you are unsure about any of the information, please contact the camp staff before proceeding.
*   This form is used to gather information about the camper's dietary needs; however, it is the parent or guardian's responsibility to inform the camp staff of any changes to the camper's needs during the camp experience.
