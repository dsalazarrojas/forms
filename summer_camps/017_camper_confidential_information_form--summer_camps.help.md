# Camper Confidential Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Camper Confidential Information Form is a document that captures important personal and health information from campers and their parents or guardians. It helps us ensure the camper's well-being and safety during their stay at the camp.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are filling out the form for the correct camper.
2. Read each field carefully and fill out the form accurately.
3. Provide the camper's personal information, including their name, date of birth, age, and gender.
4. Provide the parent or guardian's information, including their name, phone number, email address, and address.
5. If applicable, provide information about any allergies, medical conditions, or current medications.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`camper_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the camper's first name.
* **Last Name** (`camper_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the camper's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`camper_date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter the camper's date of birth.
* **Age** (`camper_age`, `number`, required): Enter the camper's age.
* **Gender** (`camper_gender`, `select_one`, optional): Select the camper's gender (Male, Female, Non-binary, or Prefer not to say).
* **Parent or Guardian First Name** (`parent_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's first name.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name** (`parent_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's last name.
* **Relationship to Camper** (`parent_relationship`, `text`, required): Describe the relationship between the parent or guardian and the camper.
* **Phone Number** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Email Address** (`parent_email`, `email`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Street Address** (`parent_address`, `text`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's street address.
* **City** (`parent_city`, `text`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's city.
* **State** (`parent_state`, `text`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's state.
* **Zip Code** (`parent_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's zip code.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): List any allergies the camper has.
* **Allergy Severity** (`allergy_severity`, `select_one`, required): Describe the severity of the camper's allergies (Mild, Moderate, Severe, or Life-threatening).
* **Current Medications and Dosages** (`medications`, `text`, optional): List any medications the camper is currently taking, along with their dosages.
* **Medical Conditions or Health Concerns** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): List any medical conditions or health concerns the camper may have.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Relationship to Camper** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Describe the relationship between the emergency contact and the camper.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* Ensure that the information is up-to-date and relevant.
* If you are unsure about any information, contact the camp administrator for guidance.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out.
