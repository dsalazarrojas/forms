# Summer Camp Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be filled out by parents/guardians of children attending summer camp. It is used to provide important information about the child, including medical conditions, allergies, and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out this form once per child**: This form is designed to be completed once per child, so please make sure to fill it out completely and accurately for each child you are registering.
2. **Provide accurate information**: Please enter the child's correct name, date of birth, and school information.
3. **List medical conditions and allergies**: If the child has any medical conditions or allergies, please list them in the designated fields.
4. **Select emergency contact options**: Choose the correct options for the child's emergency contact information, including the relationship to the child.
5. **Review and sign agreements**: Carefully read through the agreements and select the correct options for each.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent/Guardian Name** (parent_name): Enter your name as the parent/guardian of the child.
* **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact): Select 'Yes' if you want to provide additional emergency contact information.
* **Relationship to Child** (emergency_contact_relationship): Enter your relationship to the child if you selected 'Yes' for emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (emergency_contact_phone): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (emergency_contact_address): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (medical_conditions): List any medical conditions the child has, such as diabetes or asthma.
* **Allergies** (allergies): List any allergies the child has, such as peanut or gluten intolerance.
* **Medical Supervision** (medical_supervision): Select 'Yes' if the child requires medical supervision.
* **Medications** (medications): List any medications the child is taking.
* **Medical Notes** (medical_notes): Enter any additional medical notes.
* **Camper Name** (camper_name): Enter the child's name.
* **Date of Birth** (camper_date_of_birth): Enter the child's date of birth.
* **School** (camper_school): Enter the child's school information.
* **Camper Signature** (camper_parent_signature): Have the child sign the form to indicate agreement.
* **Date** (camper_parent_date): Enter the date the form is being signed.
* **Camper Relationship** (camper_parent_relationship): Enter your relationship to the child.
* **Agreement 1-8** (camper_agreement_1-8): Carefully read through each agreement and select the correct options for each.
