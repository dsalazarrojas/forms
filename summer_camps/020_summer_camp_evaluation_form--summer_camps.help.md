# summer_camp_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from participants about their experiences at the summer camp. This feedback will help us improve and provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your thoughts and answers. Make sure to provide as much information as possible to give us a clear understanding of your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What were your overall impressions of this summer camp?** (`introduction`, `text`, required: false)
	* Write a brief description of your overall experience at the summer camp.
- **How would you rate the quality of this program?** (`how_would_you_rate_the_quality_of_this_program`, `select_one`, required: false)
	* Choose one of the options: Good or Poor.
- **How satisfied are you with this summer camp?** (`participant_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false)
	* Enter a number between 1 and 10 to rate your level of satisfaction.
- **Participant Satisfaction Scale** (`participant_satisfaction_scale`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	* Choose one or more options: Satisfied or Dissatisfied.
- **What suggestions do you have for future summer camps?** (`recommendations`, `text`, required: false)
	* Provide any suggestions or improvements you think we should make for future summer camps.
- **How can we contact you to follow up?** (`contact_information`, `email`, required: false)
	* Enter your email address so we can contact you.
- **Who is the person in charge of this summer camp?** (`camp_director`, `text`, required: false)
	* Write the name of the person in charge of the summer camp.
- **What is the location of this summer camp?** (`camp_location`, `note`, required: false)
	* Write a brief description of the location of the summer camp.
- **On what date did you attend this summer camp?** (`date_attended`, `date`, required: false)
	* Enter the date you attended the summer camp.
- **How many days did you spend at this summer camp?** (`time_spent`, `number`, required: false)
	* Enter the number of days you spent at the summer camp.
- **How many hours did you spend at this summer camp?** (`time_spent_hours`, `number`, required: false)
	* Enter the number of hours you spent at the summer camp.
- **How would you rate the quality of the staff?** (`staff_quality`, `number`, required: false)
	* Enter a number between 1 and 10 to rate the quality of the staff.
- **What type of summer program did you participate in?** (`program_type`, `select_one`, required: false)
	* Choose one of the options: Yes or No.
- **What suggestions do you have for our summer camp staff?** (`staff_suggestion`, `text`, required: false)
	* Provide any suggestions or improvements you think our staff should make.
- **Did a parent chaperone attend with you?** (`parent_permission`, `select_one`, required: false)
	* Choose one of the options: Yes or No.
- **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false)
	* Write any additional thoughts or comments you have.
- **What suggestions do you have for future summer camps?** (`camp_suggestion`, `text`, required: false)
	* Provide any suggestions or improvements you think we should make.
- **Was the staff present and attentive?** (`staff_attendance`, `select_one`, required: false)
	* Choose one of the options: Yes or No.
- **How many times did you attend this summer camp?** (`time_attendance`, `number`, required: false)
	* Enter the number of times you attended this summer camp.
- **How would you rate the overall satisfaction of the program?** (`program_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false)
	* Enter a number between 1 and 10 to rate your level of satisfaction.
- **What was the most enjoyable part of the summer camp?** (`camp_experience`, `text`, required: false)
	* Write a description of the most enjoyable part of the summer camp.
- **What was the most impressive part of the summer camp?** (`camp_impression`, `text`, required: false)
	* Write a description of the most impressive part of the summer camp.
- **Would you recommend this summer camp to others?** (`camp_recommendation`, `select_one`, required: false)
	* Choose one of the options: Yes or No.
- **Do you have any additional feedback for parents?** (`parent_feedback`, `text`, required: false)
	* Provide any additional feedback for parents.
- **How can we contact you to follow up?** (`follow_up`, `email`, required: false)
	* Enter your email address so we can contact you.
