# Summer Camp Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Enrollment Form is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians about their child's enrollment in our summer camp program. This form helps us to verify the child's details, ensure proper communication, and provide a smooth experience for both parents/guardians and the summer camp team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the child's **Child Information** (field 1) to describe the child you are enrolling.
2. Provide the child's **Name** (field 2) to ensure we have accurate records.
3. Enter the child's **Birthdate** (field 3) to record their age and birth details.
4. Add information about the **Emergency Contact** (field 4), including the name and contact details of the parent or guardian.
5. Enter your **Parent Name** (field 5), which will be used as a reference for communication.
6. Add your **Parent Email** (field 6), so we can keep you updated on summer camp events.
7. Enter your **Parent Phone** (field 7), for easy contact in case of emergencies.
8. If applicable, **Medical Record** (field 8) is an option to upload or describe any medical records or conditions that may affect the child.
9. Select **Payment Status** (field 9) to confirm whether a payment is involved in this enrollment.
10. Add any relevant **Notes** (field 10) about your child's needs or requirements.
11. Confirm that the information provided is correct by selecting **Confirm** (field 11).
12. Finally, review and **Submit** (field 12) your form to complete the enrollment process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Child Information (1)**: A text field to provide a brief description of the child.
* **Name (2)**: A text field to enter the child's name accurately.
* **Birthdate (3)**: A date field to record the child's birthdate.
* **Emergency Contact (4)**: A text field for the parent or guardian's name, followed by their contact details.
* **Parent Name (5)**: A text field for the parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent Email (6)**: An email field for the parent or guardian's contact email.
* **Parent Phone (7)**: A text field for the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Medical Record (8)**: A selection field to indicate whether a medical record or conditions are present.
* **Payment (9)**: A selection field to confirm payment status.
* **Notes (10)**: A text field for any relevant notes or requirements.
* **Confirm (11)**: A selection field to verify that the information is accurate.
* **Submit (12)**: A selection field to finalize the enrollment.
