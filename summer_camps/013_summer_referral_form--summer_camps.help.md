# Summer Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Referral Form is designed to receive referrals of summer programs or camps that we might consider partnering with. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill out your first name and last name as they appear on your business card or contact information. 
2. Enter your email address and phone number where you can be reached.
3. Choose the type of program you are referring (summer camp, school, or community organization). 
4. Provide the name of the program you are referring. 
5. Enter the start and end dates of the program. 
6. Enter the location of the program. 
7. If you are referring a program, enter the name of the person or organization making the referral. 
8. If you are referring a program, enter your referral number. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (4): Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Program Type** (5): Choose the type of program you are referring:
	+ Summer Camp
	+ School
	+ Community Organization
* **Program Name** (6): Enter the name of the program you are referring.
* **Program Location** (7): Enter the location of the program.
* **Start Date** (8): Enter the start date of the program (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **End Date** (9): Enter the end date of the program (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Referral Name** (10): Enter the name of the person or organization making the referral. 
* **Referral Number** (11): If you are referring a program, enter your referral number. 

## Tips
Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete. 
Do not submit duplicate submissions for the same program. 
If you are experiencing issues with the form, contact [support email].
