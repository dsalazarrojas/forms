# Girl Scout Troop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track attendance for Girl Scout troop meetings. It captures information about the date of the meeting, the number of scouts and leaders present, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Troop Meeting Date** in the format `MM/DD/YYYY` to indicate the date of the meeting.
2. Enter the **Leader Name** of the troop, which should match the name of the person submitting the form.
3. Enter the **Number Of Scouts** attending the meeting.
4. List the **Scout Names** of the scouts who attended the meeting.
5. Check **Scout Attendance** for each scout to indicate whether they were present or not.
6. Enter the **Total Attendance** for the meeting.
7. Enter the **Total Scouts Attending** and **Total Scouts Missing** for the meeting.
8. Enter the **Number of Leaders** attending the meeting.
9. List the **Leaders Names** of the leaders who attended the meeting.
10. Check **Leader Attendance** for each leader to indicate whether they were present or not.
11. Enter the **Total Leaders Attending**, **Total Leaders Missing**, and **Total Participating Leaders** for the meeting.
12. Enter the **Total Participating Scouts**, **Total Participating Scouts Attending**, and **Total Participating Scouts Missing** for the meeting.
13. Enter the **Total Participating Leaders**, **Total Leaders Participating**, and **Total Leaders Missing** for the meeting.
14. Enter the **Total Scouts Participating Attending** and **Total Scouts Participating Missing** for the meeting.
15. Enter the **Form Submissions** count for the meeting, which should be set to 1 for single submissions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Troop Meeting Date**: The date of the troop meeting. Enter in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Leader Name**: The name of the person submitting the form, which should match the name of the troop leader.
* **Number Of Scouts**: The number of scouts attending the meeting.
* **Scout Names**: List the names of all scouts attending the meeting. Separate each name with a comma.
* **Scout Attendance**: Check `Yes` for scouts who attended, `No` for scouts who did not attend.
* **Total Attendance**: Enter the total number of attendees at the meeting.
* **Total Scouts Attending**: Enter the number of scouts who attended the meeting.
* **Total Scouts Missing**: Enter the number of scouts who were not present at the meeting.
* **Number of Leaders**: The number of leaders attending the meeting.
* **Leaders Names**: List the names of all leaders attending the meeting. Separate each name with a comma.
* **Leader Attendance**: Check `Yes` for leaders who attended, `No` for leaders who did not attend.
* **Total Leaders Attending**: Enter the number of leaders who attended the meeting.
* **Total Leaders Missing**: Enter the number of leaders who were not present at the meeting.
* **Total Participating Leaders**: Enter the number of leaders who participated in the meeting.
* **Total Participating Scouts**: Enter the number of scouts who participated in the meeting.
* **Total Participating Scouts Attending**: Enter the number of participating scouts who attended the meeting.
* **Total Participating Scouts Missing**: Enter the number of participating scouts who were not present at the meeting.
* **Total Participating Leaders**: Enter the number of leaders who participated in the meeting.
* **Total Leaders Participating**: Enter the number of leaders who participated in the meeting.
* **Total Leaders Missing**: Enter the number of leaders who did not participate in the meeting.
* **Total Scouts Participating Attending**: Enter the number of participating scouts who attended the meeting.
* **Total Scouts Participating Missing**: Enter the number of participating scouts who were not present at the meeting.
* **Form Submissions**: Enter 1 for single submissions, 0 for no submissions.
