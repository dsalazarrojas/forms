# swim_camp_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect contact and medical information of the swimmer for summer camp registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Registration Information page (Page 1).
2. Enter the Swimmer Information (Page 2).
3. Provide contact and medical details for the swimmer, including parent contact information and emergency contact details (Pages 3-5).
4. Enter the swimmer's age and address (Pages 6-7).
5. Confirm the submission details (Page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Registration Information (Page 1)
* **Camper Name** (`camper_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the swimmer as it appears on their official document (e.g. passport, ID card).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the swimmer's date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information for the swimmer.

### Swimmer Information (Page 2)
* **Swimmer Information** (`swimmer_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the swimmer.
* **Swimmer Age** (`swimmer_age`, number, required): Enter the swimmer's age.
* **Camper Address** (`camper_address`, text, optional): Enter the swimmer's address.
* **Camper City** (`camper_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the swimmer resides.
* **Camper State** (`camper_state`, text, optional): Enter the state where the swimmer resides.
* **Camper Zip** (`camper_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code where the swimmer resides.
* **Camper Phone** (`camper_phone`, text, optional): Enter the swimmer's phone number.

### Parent Information (Page 3)
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the parent/guardian.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the parent/guardian.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Information** (`emergency_info`, text, optional): Enter any emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional emergency contact information.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, text, optional): Enter any medical information.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Enter any medical conditions the swimmer has.
* **Medical Allergies** (`medical_allergies`, text, optional): Enter any medical allergies the swimmer has.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional medical notes.

### Submission Information (Page 8)
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the submission.
* **Submission Information** (`submission_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional submission information.
* **Registration Fees** (`registration_fees`, text, optional): Enter the registration fees for the swimmer.
* **Camper Fee** (`camper_fee`, text, optional): Enter the camper fee for the swimmer.
* **Submission Information** (`submission_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional submission information.
