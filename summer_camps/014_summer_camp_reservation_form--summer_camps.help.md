# Summer Camp Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for summer camp reservations. Please fill out the form to create a camper profile and provide payment details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. If you are unsure about any field, contact the camp organization for clarification.
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer Camp Name**: Enter the name of the summer camp your child is attending.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date your child's summer camp starts.
* **End Date**: Enter the date your child's summer camp ends.
* **Camper Name**: Enter your child's name.
* **Parent Name**: Enter your name or the name of the parent/guardian making the reservation.
* **Phone**: Enter a contact phone number.
* **Email**: Enter a contact email address.
* **Camper Grade**: Enter your child's grade level (e.g., 1st, 2nd, etc.).
* **Camper Birthday**: Enter your child's birthday.
* **Camper Gender**: Select your child's gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **Camper Age**: Enter your child's age.
* **Camp Hours**: Select the camp hours your child will attend (8am-4pm, 4pm-8pm, 8am-8pm).
* **Camp Duration**: Select the duration of the camp your child will attend (1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month, 2 months).
* **Camp Type**: Select the type of activities your child will participate in (Arts and Crafts, Music and Movement, STEM Education, Outdoor Adventures).
* **Camp Fees**: Enter the total camp fees (not applicable if payment method is selected).
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment (Check, Credit Card, PayPal).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your child's needs or requirements.
* **Camper Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your child's needs or requirements.

## Tips

* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the camp organization.
* Review the camp organization's policies and procedures for any specific requirements or restrictions.
