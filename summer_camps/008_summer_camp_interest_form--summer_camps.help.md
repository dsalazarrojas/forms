# Summer Camp Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Interest Form is designed to collect information about individuals or organizations interested in attending the summer camp event. The data collected will help us anticipate the number of people who may register for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. If you're interested in attending the event, select how much you anticipate your summer camp event being from the "event_anticipation" field.
4. If you plan to register for the event, enter the number of people who will be registering in the "register_count" field.
5. You can add any additional information in the "phone" field, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### summer_camp_interest_form
* **summer_camp_interest** (`summer_camp_interest`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your interest in the summer camp event.
### event_anticipation
* **How much is your summer camp event being anticipated?** (`event_anticipation`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options to indicate how much you anticipate your summer camp event:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
### register_count
* **How many people would register?** (`register_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of people who plan to register for the event.
### name
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
### email
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
### phone
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter any additional phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form only once per person or organization.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will use the data collected from this form to gauge interest and anticipate the number of people who will be attending the event.
