# Summer Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Program Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from students for the summer program. This form is used by program administrators to verify student details and schedule. Please ensure that you fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid any issues with your program registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Double-check the information you provide before submitting the form.
3. Review the form for any required fields (marked as required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of your school. This will help the program administrators verify your school affiliation.
* **Student First Name** (`student_first_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student Last Name** (`student_last_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, Text, Optional): If you are under 18, please enter your parent's name and contact information. This is for emergency contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address. This is the preferred contact method for the program administrators.
* **Program Title** (`program_title`, Text, Optional): Enter the title of the program you are registering for.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, Optional): Select the start date of your program.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, Date, Optional): Select the end date of your program.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, Time, Optional): Select the start time of your program. If your program runs across multiple days, please enter the start time for each day.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, Time, Optional): Select the end time of your program. If your program runs across multiple days, please enter the end time for each day.
* **Duration** (`duration`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of hours your program will run.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields (marked as required).
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors.
* If you are experiencing any issues with the form, please contact the program administrators for assistance.
