# Summer Camper Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from summer campers. It's a required form to be filled out by campers themselves or on their behalf by a parent/guardian.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Be accurate and honest when filling out the form.
3. If you're unsure about any field, you can contact the camp administration for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Summer Camper Registration** (1): This is the main title of the form.
* **First Name** (2): Please enter your first name or the first name of the camper you're registering.
* **Last Name** (3): Please enter your last name or the last name of the camper you're registering.
* **Email** (4): Please enter a valid email address for the camper or your contact information.
* **Phone Number** (5): Please enter a valid phone number for the camper or your contact information.
* **Birthdate** (6): Please enter the camper's birthdate.
* **Medical Notes** (7): Please provide any medical notes or conditions that might affect the camper's stay.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (8): Please enter the name of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (9): Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (10): Please enter the email of the emergency contact.
* **Guardian Name** (11): If the camper is under 18, please enter the name of their parent or guardian.
* **Guardian Phone** (12): If the camper is under 18, please enter the phone number of their parent or guardian.
* **Guardian Email** (13): If the camper is under 18, please enter the email of their parent or guardian.
* **Camper Attendance** (14): Please choose 'True' if the camper will attend the camp and 'False' if not.
* **Camper Medical Notes** (15): Please provide any additional medical notes or conditions that might affect the camper's stay.
* **Medical Conditions** (16): Please select any relevant medical conditions that might affect the camper's stay.
* **Camper Food Restrictions** (17): Please select any dietary restrictions or preferences the camper has.
* **Camper Language** (18): Please select the language the camper speaks or prefers.
* **Camper English Level** (19): Please choose the camper's English level.
* **Camper Camp Preferences** (20): Please select any preferences or interests the camper has for activities.
* **Camper Additional Comments** (21): Please provide any additional comments or information about the camper.
* **Parent Signature** (22): Please sign as the parent or guardian of the camper.
* **Parent Contact Info** (23): Please enter your contact information as the parent or guardian.
* **Admin Comments** (24): Not applicable to campers, only for camp staff to fill out.
* **Admin Signature** (25): Not applicable to campers, only for camp staff to fill out.
