# Cub Scout Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record attendance and activities of Cub Scouts during summer camps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
3. Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
4. Choose whether you are attending the summer camp on the specified Date.
5. Indicate whether you will be participating in Activities, such as swimming, sports, or crafts.
6. If you are an adult leader, do not answer the Achievements question.
7. Enter any notable Achievements or accomplishments you want to share.
8. Add any additional Comments about your camp experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
### 2. Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
### 3. Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
### 4. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
### 5. Date
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Choose the date you are attending the summer camp.
### 6. Attendance
* **Attendance** (`attendance`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you are attending the summer camp.
### 7. Activities
* **Activities** (`activities`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate which activities you will be participating in, such as swimming, sports, or crafts.
### 8. Achievements
* **Achievements** (`achievements`, `text`, optional): If you are an adult leader, do not answer this question. Otherwise, enter any notable Achievements or accomplishments you want to share.
### 9. Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments about your camp experience.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask a camp staff member for assistance.
