# Camp Admission Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect comprehensive information about the camper, including personal details, medical history, and other relevant data for their safety and well-being during the camp stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the camper's first name in the "Camper First Name" field.
2. Enter the camper's last name in the "Camper Last Name" field.
3. Provide the camper's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Select the camper's gender from the options provided.
5. Fill in the camper's home address in the "Home Address" field.
6. Enter the city in the "City" field.
7. Enter the state in the "State or Province" field.
8. Enter the camper's ZIP or postal code in the "ZIP or Postal Code" field.
9. Enter the full name of the parent or guardian in the "Parent or Guardian 1 Full Name" field.
10. Enter the contact phone number of the parent or guardian in the "Parent or Guardian 1 Phone Number" field.
11. Enter the email address of the parent or guardian in the "Parent or Guardian 1 Email" field.
12. If applicable, enter the full name of the second parent or guardian in the "Parent or Guardian 2 Full Name" field.
13. If applicable, enter the contact phone number of the second parent or guardian in the "Parent or Guardian 2 Phone Number" field.
14. Enter the name of the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
15. Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
16. Select the relationship with the emergency contact from the options provided.
17. If applicable, enter the name of the medical insurance provider in the "Medical Insurance Provider" field.
18. If applicable, enter the insurance policy number in the "Insurance Policy Number" field.
19. List all known allergies in the "Known Allergies" field.
20. List any medical conditions in the "Medical Conditions" field.
21. List all current medications with dosages in the "Current Medications" field.
22. Enter the name of the primary physician in the "Primary Physician Name" field.
23. Enter the contact phone number of the primary physician in the "Physician Phone Number" field.
24. Select any dietary restrictions from the options provided.
25. Select the swimming ability level from the options provided.
26. Select if the camper has any special needs from the options provided.
27. If applicable, describe any special needs in the "Special Needs Details" field.
28. Select if transportation is needed for the camper.
29. Select the T-shirt size for the camper from the options provided.
30. Select all the ways you heard about this camp from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Camper First Name**: Enter the first name of the camper.
* **Camper Last Name**: Enter the last name of the camper.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the birth date of the camper.
* **Camper Gender**: Select the gender of the camper.
* **Home Address**: Enter the full street address of the camper's residence.
* **City**: Enter the city or town of the camper's residence.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province of the camper's residence.
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter the camper's ZIP or postal code.
* **Parent or Guardian 1 Full Name**: Enter the full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian 1 Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian 1 Email**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian 2 Full Name**: If applicable, enter the full name of the second parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian 2 Phone Number**: If applicable, enter the phone number of the second parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Medical Insurance Provider**: If applicable, enter the name of the medical insurance provider.
* **Insurance Policy Number**: If applicable, enter the insurance policy number.
* **Known Allergies**: List all known allergies.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions.
* **Current Medications**: List all current medications with dosages.
* **Primary Physician Name**: Enter the name of the primary physician.
* **Physician Phone Number**: Enter the contact phone number of the primary physician.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select any dietary restrictions.
* **Swimming Ability Level**: Select the swimming ability level.
* **Does the Camper have any Special Needs**: Select if the camper has any special needs.
* **Special Needs Details**: If applicable, describe any special needs.
* **Does Transportation Need to be Arranged**: Select if transportation is needed for the camper.
* **T-Shirt Size**: Select the T-shirt size for the camper.
* **How Did You Hear About This Camp**: Select all the ways you heard about this camp.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and honestly.
* If any field is not applicable, mark the "N/A" option.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your camp counselor for guidance.
* Make sure to review your entries before submitting the form for accuracy and completeness.
