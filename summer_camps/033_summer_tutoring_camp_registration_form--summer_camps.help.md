# Summer Tutoring Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for students to register for a summer tutoring camp, providing essential information about themselves and their parents. This form should only be completed once per student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Student Information section, providing details about the student.
2. Enter the parent's information in the Parents section.
3. Select the student's interests in the Student Interests section.
4. Enter the parent's contact information, including email, phone number, and address.
5. Provide payment details in the Payment Details section.
6. Choose the payment method.
7. Click the Submit button to complete the registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information (Student Information)** (`Student Information`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's first and last name, as well as any other identifying information you would like to provide.
* **Parent Information (Parents)** (`Parents`, `text`, optional): Enter the parent's name and any other contact details.
* **Student Interests (Student Interests)** (`Student Interests`, `select_one`, optional): Select all the student's interests to help us tailor the experience to their needs.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Email (Email)** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Address (Address)** (`Address`, `text`, optional): Enter the parent's address.
* **Payment Details (Payment Details)** (`Payment Details`, `text`, optional): Enter all payment details, including amount and method.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the payment method from the options provided.
	+ Options: Payment Option 1, Payment Option 2
* **Submit (Submit)** (`Submit`, `text`, optional): Click this button to complete the registration and submit your application.
