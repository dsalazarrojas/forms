# Camp Staff Expression Of Interest - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Staff Expression Of Interest form is used by staff members to apply for various roles at the summer camp. It helps the management team to assess the qualifications and suitability of applicants for specific roles and dates.

## How To Complete This Form

Please fill out the form carefully, providing as much relevant information as possible. Ensure that you complete all the required fields to increase your chances of being considered for a role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (1, text): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (2, email): Provide your preferred contact email address.
* **Role of Interest** (3, select_multiple): Choose which roles you are interested in applying for (Counselor, Activity Leader, Kitchen Staff, Volunteer Coordinator).
* **Availability Dates** (4, text): List the dates you are available to work during the summer camp season.
* **Relevant Experience** (5, text): Briefly describe any relevant experience you have working with youth or in a similar role.
* **Training Certificates** (6, text): List any relevant training certificates or certifications you have.
* **References** (7, text): Provide contact details for two professional references.
* **Motivation Statement** (8, text): Explain in 50 words or less why you want to work at the summer camp.
* **Consent for Background Check** (9, select_one): Choose 'True' to consent to background checks if required by the camp.
* **Additional Notes** (10, text): Provide any additional information that you think would be relevant for the management team to consider.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Ensure that you provide all required fields to increase your chances of being considered for a role.
* Attach any supporting documents or references as attachments where possible.
