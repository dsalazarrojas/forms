# Podcasts For Children ( And Animation Fans) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects metadata for a podcast targeting children and animation fans. It helps in categorizing and filtering existing podcasts or generating new podcast entries.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant category for your podcast (e.g., summer camps, camps).
2. Enter a brief description of your podcast.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form ID** (1, number, required/optional: false): A unique identifier for the form, which is not required but helps in tracking and managing multiple entries.
* **Category** (2, select_multiple, required/optional: false): Choose the category your podcast belongs to, such as summer camps or camps. Select multiple categories if your podcast covers multiple topics.
* **Description** (3, text, required/optional: false): Enter a brief summary of your podcast, including its focus and content.

## Tips
- Make sure to choose the most relevant categories for your podcast.
- Keep the description concise and to the point to help users quickly understand your podcast's content.
