# spring-break-day-camp-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for the spring break day camp, a program designed for campers to have fun and learn during their spring break.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the pages of the form, following the instructions on each page.
2. Ensure you fill out all required fields (marked as `required: true`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Camper Info
* **Camper Info** (`Camper Info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your camper's name, age, and any other relevant information about them.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number, so we can reach you if needed.

### Payment Info
* **Payment Info** (`Payment Info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your payment method and information, including any relevant payment details.

### File Uploads
* **File Uploads** (`File Uploads`, `text`, required: false): You can upload any relevant files, such as health certificates, identification documents, or proof of payment.

### Registration Info
* **Registration Info** (`Registration Info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter any additional registration details, such as any medical conditions or allergies.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We will review your submitted form and contact you with any further instructions or concerns.
