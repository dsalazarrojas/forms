# Summer Camp Box Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from customers who want to order summer camps. It is used to process orders and deliver summer camps to the correct address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the delivery address field with the address where the summer camp will be delivered.
2. Enter the name of the participant who will be attending the summer camp.
3. Enter the street address.
4. Enter the city.
5. Enter the zip code.
6. Enter any additional information or comments in the content field.
7. Enter the contact email of the participant or customer.
8. Enter the contact phone number.
9. Select the date of the summer camp.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Enter the address where the summer camp will be delivered.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the participant who will be attending the summer camp.
* **Street** (`street`, text, required): Enter the street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code.
* **Content** (`content`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or comments.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email of the participant or customer.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date of the summer camp.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid issues with delivery or customer service.
* Enter the correct contact information to ensure that we can contact you if there are any issues with your order.
* If you have any additional information or comments, enter them in the content field.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure that it is accurate and complete.
