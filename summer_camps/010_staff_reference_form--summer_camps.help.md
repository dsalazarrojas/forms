# Staff Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Reference Form is a tool used to collect and document information about staff members' contact details and professional background for easy reference in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the basic contact information:
	* **Staff Name** (`staff_reference_form_name`, text, required): Enter the staff member's name.
	* **Phone** (`staff_reference_form_phone`, number, required): Enter the staff member's phone number.
	* **Email** (`staff_reference_form_email`, email, required): Enter the staff member's email address.
2. Add any additional relevant details:
	* **Date of Birth** (`staff_reference_form_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the staff member's date of birth (if applicable).
	* **Education** (`staff_reference_form_education`, text, optional): List any relevant education or training the staff member has received.
	* **Work History** (`staff_reference_form_work_history`, text, optional): Describe the staff member's previous work experience.
	* **Reference** (`staff_reference_form_reference`, text, optional): Provide any relevant references or recommendations about the staff member.
	* **Relationship** (`staff_reference_form_relationship`, text, optional): Specify the nature of the staff member's relationship to the organization or team (e.g., employee, contractor, volunteer).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Name** (`staff_reference_form_name`, text, required): This field is the primary identifier for the staff member. Enter their full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Phone** (`staff_reference_form_phone`, number, required): Enter the staff member's primary phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`staff_reference_form_email`, email, required): Enter the staff member's professional email address for communication and contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`staff_reference_form_date_of_birth`, date, optional): This field is for recording the staff member's date of birth, which is useful for age-related purposes or to verify identification documents.
* **Education** (`staff_reference_form_education`, text, optional): Describe any relevant education or training the staff member has received, including degrees earned, institutions attended, or certifications held.
* **Work History** (`staff_reference_form_work_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the staff member's previous work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
* **Reference** (`staff_reference_form_reference`, text, optional): Enter any relevant references or recommendations about the staff member, such as character references or performance reviews.
* **Relationship** (`staff_reference_form_relationship`, text, optional): Specify the nature of the staff member's relationship to the organization or team, including their role, position, or status.
