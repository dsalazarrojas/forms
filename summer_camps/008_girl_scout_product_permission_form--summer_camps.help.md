# Girl Scout Product Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the fields of the Girl Scout Product Permission Form, which is used to manage the sale of Girl Scout products such as cookies, candy, and calendars.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, click on the "Girl Scout Product Permission Form" on the main menu.
2. Select the product(s) you are requesting permission to sell from the "Products" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the Girl Scout's name and contact information for the "Name", "Contact Number", and "Email" fields.
4. Enter the quantity and price of the product you are requesting permission to sell.
5. Add any additional notes or comments for the product in the "Notes" field.
6. Select the person assigned to handle the sale from the "Assigned To" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Girl Scout Name (Page 1)**: This field is where you should enter the name of the Girl Scout who is selling the product.
* **Contact Number (Page 1)**: This field is where you enter the contact number of the Girl Scout who is selling the product. This is used for communication and contact purposes.
* **Email (Page 1)**: This field is where you enter the email address of the Girl Scout who is selling the product.
* **Products (Page 1)**: This is a dropdown menu where you select the type of product you are requesting permission to sell. Select one or multiple products from the list, depending on your needs.
* **Quantity (Page 2)**: This field is where you enter the number of products you are requesting permission to sell.
* **Price (Page 2)**: This field is where you enter the price of the product you are requesting permission to sell.
* **Notes (Page 2)**: This field is for any additional comments or notes you may have about the product or sale.
* **Assigned To (Page 3)**: This is a dropdown menu where you select the person assigned to handle the sale. Choose from John, Jane, or Bob.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Double-check your selection in the "Products" field to ensure you are only requesting permission for the correct product(s).
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your Girl Scout leader or manager.
