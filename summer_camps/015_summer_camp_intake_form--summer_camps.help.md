# Summer Camp Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Intake Form is used to collect essential details about campers and their parents' information. This form is designed to ensure that each camper has a fun and safe experience at the summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the camper information section with the necessary details.
2. Provide the parent's information in the corresponding section.
3. Review and submit the form to ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Camper Information (`camper_info`, text, not required):** Enter the camper's name, age, and any relevant medical information or allergies.
* **Parent Information (`parent_info`, text, not required):** Enter the parent's name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Camper Details (`camper_details`, text, not required):** Enter any additional comments or details about the camper's experience and preferences for the camp.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to contact us for clarification.
* If you have any special needs or requirements, please provide them in the Camper Details section.
* You can review and edit the form before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
