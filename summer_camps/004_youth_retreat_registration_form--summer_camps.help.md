# youth-retreat-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from participants who are attending the summer camp program. This information will be used to ensure the participant's safety and well-being during the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Participant Details section with the participant's name, age, and contact information.
2. In the Emergency Contact section, provide the name and contact information of the person who will be available to contact in case of an emergency.
3. Select the participant's dietary preferences from the list of options.
4. Choose the activities that the participant is interested in or able to participate in.
5. Indicate if the participant has any special needs or medical conditions that require attention.
6. Check if the participant has a relationship with the person who will be available to contact in case of an emergency.
7. Select the participant's medical needs and if they have a special needs physician.
8. Provide the contact information for the participant's physician (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details (Page 1)**: This section is used to collect the basic information about the participant, including their name, age, and contact details.
	+ **Name**: Please enter the participant's full name.
	+ **Age**: Enter the participant's age.
	+ **Contact Information**: Provide the participant's home phone number and email address.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 2)**: This section is used to collect the contact information of the person who will be available to contact in case of an emergency.
	+ **Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
	+ **Relationship**: Check if the participant has a relationship with the emergency contact person.
	+ **Contact Information**: Provide the emergency contact person's phone number and email address.
* **Dietary Preferences (Page 3)**: This section is used to select the participant's dietary preferences.
	+ **Diet**: Choose from the options Apple, Banana, or Orange.
* **Participant Activities (Page 4)**: This section is used to select the activities that the participant is interested in or able to participate in.
	+ **Activities**: Choose from the options Hiking, Swimming, or Arts and Crafts.
* **Participant Special Needs (Page 5)**: This section is used to indicate if the participant has any special needs or medical conditions.
	+ **Special Needs**: Check if the participant has any special needs or medical conditions.
* **Emergency Contact Info (Page 6)**: This section is used to select if the participant has a relationship with the emergency contact person.
	+ **Relationship**: Check if the participant has a relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Participant Medication (Page 7)**: This section is used to select if the participant takes any medication.
	+ **Medication**: Check if the participant takes any medication.
* **Participant Physician (Page 8)**: This section is used to select if the participant has a physician.
	+ **Physician**: Check if the participant has a physician.
* **Notes for Staff (Page 9)**: This section is used to provide any additional notes for staff.
	+ **Notes**: Enter any additional information that may be helpful for the staff.
* **Physician Note (Page 10)**: This section is used to provide any notes from the participant's physician.
	+ **Note**: Enter any information from the participant's physician.
* **Participant Medical Need (Page 11)**: This section is used to select if the participant has any medical needs.
	+ **Medical Need**: Check if the participant has any medical needs.
* **Participant Special Needs Physician (Page 12)**: This section is used to select if the participant has a special needs physician.
	+ **Special Needs Physician**: Check if the participant has a special needs physician.
* **Physician Contact Info (Page 13)**: This section is used to select if the participant has any contact information for their physician.
	+ **Physician Contact Info**: Check if the participant has any contact information for their physician.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all the information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If the participant has any special needs or medical conditions, please provide as much information as possible.
* If the participant has a relationship with the emergency contact person, please make sure to provide their contact information.
* If the participant has a physician, please provide their contact information.
