# Summer Camp Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the discharge process of a camper, providing important information for the next stage of their summer camp journey or for their return home.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the camper's file readily available for reference.
2. Choose the correct date for the camper's discharge.
3. Select the reason for the camper's discharge.
4. Add any additional notes if necessary.
5. Choose whether the camper's assigned counselor is 'Yes' or 'No'.
6. Have the camper sign and date the form to confirm their discharge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Camper Discharge Date (`camper_discharge_date`)**: Enter the date when the camper will be discharged from the camp.
* **Reason for Discharge (`reason_for_discharge`)**: Select a valid reason for the camper's discharge. This field is used to document the cause for the camper's departure.
* **Notes (`notes`)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the camper's discharge, such as their next destination or any other relevant information.
* **Assigned Counselor Name (`assigned_counselor_name`)**: Indicate whether the camper's assigned counselor is 'Yes' or 'No'. If you select 'Yes', the counselor will be responsible for the camper's post-discharge care.
* **Camper Signature (`camper_signature`)**: Have the camper sign and date the form to confirm their discharge from the camp.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date before submitting the form.
* Review the camper's file before filling out this form to avoid any confusion or duplication of efforts.
* If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to reach out to the camp director or other team members for clarification.
