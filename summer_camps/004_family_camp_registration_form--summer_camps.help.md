This is a form for registering a camper for the summer camp program. It is intended for parents or guardians to provide information about the camper's medical history, emergency contact information, and previous camping experiences. 

This form is not required for all campers, but it is recommended for campers who have specific medical needs or allergies. It will help our camp staff better understand the camper's situation and ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for them.

The fields in this form are designed to be completed by the camper's parent or guardian, so we'll focus on explaining how to complete it correctly.

### Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather relevant information about the camper, including their medical history, emergency contact information, and previous camping experiences. This information will help our camp staff provide the best care and support for the camper during the summer camp program.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the Camper Information section with the camper's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. In the Medical History section, provide any medical conditions, allergies, or special needs that the camper has.
3. In the Emergency Contact section, provide the name and contact information of the person who will be responsible for the camper in case of an emergency.
4. In the Previous Camping Activities section, select all the activities the camper has participated in previously.
5. In the Questions/Comments section, provide any additional information or comments that you think would be helpful for our camp staff to know.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Information**: This is the first section of the form, where you can enter the camper's name, contact information, and any other relevant details. Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Medical History**: This section is where you can provide any medical conditions, allergies, or special needs that the camper has. Please make sure to be as detailed as possible to ensure we can provide the best care for the camper.
* **Emergency Contact**: This section is where you can provide the name and contact information of the person who will be responsible for the camper in case of an emergency. Please make sure to include any relevant contact information, such as phone numbers and addresses.
* **Previous Camping Activities**: This section is where you can select all the activities the camper has participated in previously. Please select all the activities that apply to the camper's experience and skills.
* **Questions/Comments**: This section is where you can provide any additional information or comments that you think would be helpful for our camp staff to know. Please feel free to share any relevant details that will help us support the camper during the summer camp program.

### Tips
* Please double-check that all the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your cooperation and look forward to having your camper join us this summer!
