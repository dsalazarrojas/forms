# Camp Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a liability waiver that must be completed by parents or guardians of camp participants to acknowledge and agree to certain terms and conditions before their child can participate in our summer camp program. The form is a legal document that protects the camp from potential liabilities and ensures that parents or guardians understand and agree to the camp's rules and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's name, parent/guardian's name, and emergency contact phone number.
2. If necessary, provide any relevant medical conditions or allergies in the "Medical Conditions" field.
3. Carefully read the liability waiver text and indicate your agreement by typing your full name as the electronic signature.
4. If you wish to give consent for your child to be photographed or videographed, select 'True' for "Photo Release Consent".
5. Indicate your permission for the camp staff to seek medical treatment in case of an emergency by selecting 'True' for "Emergency Medical Consent".
6. If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name**: (`participant_name`, text, required): The full name of the camper or participant.
- **Parent or Guardian Name**: (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): The name of the adult providing consent.
- **Emergency Contact Phone**: (`emergency_contact`, text, required): The phone number for emergencies.
- **Medical Conditions**: (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
- **Liability Waiver Text**: (`waiver_text`, note, required): A detailed text explaining the risks and liabilities associated with camp activities.
- **Signature Name**: (`consent_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as electronic signature.
- **Date Signed**: (`date_signed`, date, required): The date the waiver is signed.
- **Photo Release Consent**: (`photo_release`, select_one, optional): Consent to use images for camp promotion.
	+ Select 'True' to give consent for photography and videography.
	+ Select 'False' to decline.
- **Emergency Medical Consent**: (`emergency_medical_consent`, select_one, required): Permission to seek medical treatment in case of an emergency.
	+ Select 'True' to give consent.
	+ Select 'False' to decline.
- **Additional Comments**: (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other relevant information.
