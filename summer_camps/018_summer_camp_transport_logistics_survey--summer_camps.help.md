# Summer Camp Transport Logistics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the transport needs and logistics of summer camps. It helps streamline the process of planning and organizing transportation for the camps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all applicable transport needs from the options provided in the 'Transport Needs' page.
2. Choose the pick-up locations that are relevant to your transport needs.
3. Select your preferred pick-up time from the time options provided.
4. If you have any special requirements, describe them in the 'Special Requirements' page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transport Needs** (`transport_needs`, text, optional): This field is where you should briefly describe your transport needs. Please be as specific as possible to help the organizers plan your transport effectively.
* **Pick-up locations** (`pickup_locations`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the locations from which you would like to be picked up. You can choose more than one location if needed.
* **Preferred pick-up time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Select your preferred pick-up time from the options provided. This will help the organizers schedule the transport at a time that suits you best.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, note, optional): If you have any special requirements for the transport, such as mobility issues or equipment needs, describe them here. This will help the organizers ensure that all your needs are met.
