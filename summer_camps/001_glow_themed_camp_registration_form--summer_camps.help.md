# Glow Themed Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who wish to join the Glow Themed Camp. Please read each field explanation carefully to ensure you provide accurate details for your camp experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure that you have read and understand the camp's rules and regulations.
2. Select the correct participant details.
3. Fill in the required contact information for your camp staff to get in touch with you.
4. Choose your preferences for the camp activities (morning, evening, or both).
5. Indicate your participant preferences (fun, social, or adventure).
6. Add any additional notes that our staff needs to know about you.
7. Provide your email address for communication purposes.
8. Enter your phone number so that our staff can reach out to you.
9. Enter your date of birth for age verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details (1)**: This is the section for your basic details, such as name, age, or any other relevant information about you.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Contact Info (2)**: Provide us your contact details so that we can get in touch with you.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Preferences (3)**: Select your preferred time for camp activities:
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: no
	+ Options: Day, Evening, Both
* **Participant Preferences (4)**: Indicate your preferences for camp activities.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: no
	+ Options: Fun, Social, Adventure
* **Notes (5)**: Add any additional information that you would like our staff to know about you.
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: no
* **Email (6)**: This is where you input your email address for communication purposes.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: no
* **Phone (7)**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Date of Birth (8)**: This is your date of birth for age verification purposes.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: no

Please note that all fields are optional, but providing accurate information will ensure that we can cater to your needs during the camp. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to our staff.
