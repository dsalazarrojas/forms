# Summer Apprenticeship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for individuals to apply for summer apprenticeships. It will collect information about your qualifications and suitability for the apprenticeship.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Make sure to provide the required information for each section.
- If a field is marked as "required", you must fill it out.
- If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: 
  - **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: 
  - **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email**: 
  - **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your valid email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: 
  - **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number. This can be a home, work or mobile number.
* **Category**: 
  - **Category** (`category`, `text`, required: false): Enter the category of the field you are applying for (e.g., Software Development).
* **Job Type**: 
  - **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of job you are interested in (e.g., Software Development, Engineering, Data Science).
* **Education**: 
  - **Education** (`education`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant education or qualifications you have.
* **Skills**: 
  - **Skills** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any relevant skills you have (e.g., Programming, Web Development, Data Science).
* **Experience**: 
  - **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant work experience you have.
* **Availability**: 
  - **Availability** (`availability`, `text`, required: false): Enter your expected availability for the apprenticeship.
* **Expected Start Date**: 
  - **Expected Start Date** (`expected_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you expect to start the apprenticeship.
* **Expected End Date**: 
  - **Expected End Date** (`expected_end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you expect to end the apprenticeship.
* **Hours Per Week**: 
  - **Hours Per Week** (`hours_per_week`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select how many hours per week you are available to work (e.g., Part-time, Full-time, Remote).
* **Location**: 
  - **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location you are interested in working from.
* **Tools**: 
  - **Tools** (`tools`, `text`, required: false): Enter any tools you are familiar with.
* **About Yourself**: 
  - **About Yourself** (`about_self`, `note`, required: false): Enter a brief introduction about yourself.
* **References**: 
  - **References** (`references`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant references.
* **Why Appretice**: 
  - **Why Appretice** (`why_apprentice`, `text`, required: false): Enter your motivation for applying for this apprenticeship.
