# leadership-camp-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The leadership camp registration form is designed to collect personal and medical information from participants. This information will be used to create a participant roster and for camp preparation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and email address.
2. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Describe your educational background and any relevant experience.
4. Share your medical history, if applicable.
5. Enter your family information, including any relevant details about your family members.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your personal email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Educational Background** (`educational_background`, `text`, required: false): Describe your educational background and any relevant experience.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Share any medical conditions or allergies that may impact your camp experience.
* **Family Information** (`family_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant details about your family members, such as ages or special needs.
