# marketing-material-printing-request-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request for printing of marketing materials such as brochures, flyers, and posters. It gathers necessary information for the production process, ensuring accuracy in communication between the marketing team and the production team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Enter the print specs in the "Print Specs" field. This is where you can provide detailed information about the print job, including paper size, ink colors, and other relevant details.
3. Enter the quantities of each item you want to print in the "Quantities" field.
4. Select a deadline for the production process in the "Deadline" field. This is the date by which you want the marketing materials to be printed and delivered.
5. Upload the artwork files for the print job in the "Artwork Uploads" field. This is where you can upload design files, images, or other relevant files.
6. Add any production notes or instructions in the "Production Notes" field. This is where you can provide additional information about the print job, such as any special requirements or considerations.
7. Enter the agency contact information in the "Agency Contact" field. This is where you can provide contact details for the person responsible for the print job.
8. Enter the client contact information in the "Client Contact" field. This is where you can provide contact details for the client or customer.
9. Finally, provide a brief summary of the order in the "Order Summary" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Print Specs**: Enter the print specs for the marketing materials, including paper size, ink colors, and other relevant details.
* **Quantities**: Enter the number of units you want to print.
* **Deadline**: Select the date by which you want the marketing materials to be printed and delivered.
* **Artwork Uploads**: Upload design files, images, or other relevant files for the print job.
* **Production Notes**: Add any production notes or instructions for the print job.
* **Agency Contact**: Enter the contact information for the person responsible for the print job.
* **Client Contact**: Enter the contact information for the client or customer.
* **Order Summary**: Provide a brief summary of the order, including any relevant details.
