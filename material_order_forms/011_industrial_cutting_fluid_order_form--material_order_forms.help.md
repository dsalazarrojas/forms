# Industrial Cutting Fluid Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Industrial Cutting Fluid Order Form is a tool used to collect information related to industrial cutting fluid orders. This form aims to gather all necessary details to process orders accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are using the correct form for your intended purpose.
2. Select one or more products from the "Product selection" section.
3. Provide delivery details, such as whether or not delivery is required.
4. Enter contact information and any relevant order comments.
5. Select a delivery date.
6. Choose the product information (Product A, B, or C).
7. Enter customer order details.
8. Provide delivery instructions if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product selection** (Select one or more, product_selection, required: false): Select the product(s) you wish to order from the options available.
* **Delivery details** (Select one or more, delivery_details, required: false): Select whether delivery is required or not.
* **Contact info** (Text, contact_info, required: false): Enter your contact information to ensure smooth communication.
* **Order comments** (Note, order_comments, required: false): Add any comments regarding your order.
* **Delivery date** (Date, delivery_date, required: false): Specify the desired delivery date for your order.
* **Product info** (Select one, product_info, required: false): Choose the product information (Product A, B, or C) corresponding to your order.
* **Customer order** (Text, customer_order, required: false): Enter your customer order details.
* **Delivery instructions** (Note, delivery_instructions, required: false): Provide any specific delivery instructions if necessary.
