# Gym Signboard Replacement Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to place a new or replacement gym signboard order. Please fill in the necessary information to ensure a smooth order process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of order you are placing (New Gym Signboard or Replacement Gym Signboard).
2. Provide a brief description of your order in the "order_details" field.
3. Enter your name and phone number (optional but recommended for efficient communication).
4. If you have an email address, please enter it to ensure our team can contact you regarding your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order_type** (New Gym Signboard, Replacement Gym Signboard): This field determines the type of signboard order you are placing. Select the correct option to ensure our team knows what to do with your order.
* **order_details** (note): A brief description of your order. This can be anything from "new signboard for gym A" to "replace existing signboard with new design". 
* **Name** (text): Your name and contact details for our team to reach out to you.
* **Phone Number** (text): Your phone number for our team to contact you about your order (optional but recommended for efficient communication).
* **Email** (email): Your email address for our team to contact you about your order (optional but recommended for efficient communication).
* **Quantity** (number): The quantity of signboards you are ordering. This field is not required but can help our team prepare for your order.
