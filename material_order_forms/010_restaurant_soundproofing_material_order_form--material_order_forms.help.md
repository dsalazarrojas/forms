# restaurant_soundproofing_material_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for soundproofing material orders. It is intended for customers who have been properly assigned to a sales representative and have agreed to place an order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information in the **Customer Info** field.
2. Provide order details in the **Order Details** field.
3. Select your **Delivery Options** (Yes/No).
4. Choose your **Payment Method** (Yes/No).
5. Enter the **Total Cost** of your order.
6. Select a **Date** for delivery.
7. Choose a **Time** for delivery.
8. Add any additional **Customer Note** about your order.
9. Enter your **Email** address for contact.
10. Provide your **Phone** number for contact.
11. Select a **Delivery Time** in hours.
12. Choose a **Delivery Date** for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: This is where you enter your name, company name (if applicable), and other identifying information.
* **Order Details**: This field is for you to provide specific details about the materials you need for soundproofing. Be as clear and concise as possible so that our team can process your order accurately.
* **Delivery Options**: Here, you can select whether you want us to deliver the materials or whether you will pick them up at our warehouse.
* **Payment Method**: If you have already been invoiced for your order, select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No" to be invoiced later.
* **Total Cost**: This is where you enter the total cost of your order.
* **Date**: This field is for selecting a specific date for delivery.
* **Time**: This is where you select a specific time for delivery.
* **Customer Note**: This is an optional field where you can add any additional comments or instructions for your order.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you directly.
* **Delivery Time**: This field is for selecting the delivery time in hours.
* **Delivery Date**: Choose a specific date for delivery.
* **Phone**: This field is not required but it is a good idea to provide it in case we need to contact you directly.
* **Phone**: We do not use this field for any purpose other than contact.
* This field is a duplicate of **Phone** field above.
