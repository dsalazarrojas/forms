# sustainable_steel_raw_material_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place orders for sustainable steel raw materials. It captures essential details related to the delivery and customer information to ensure accurate and efficient processing of the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the delivery details, including the delivery date and time, as accurately as possible.
* Provide the delivery address, including the street address and any additional details about the delivery location.
* Enter your contact information, including phone number and email, for efficient communication.
* Enter the customer's name(s) and phone number(s) for delivery and order purposes.
* Add any additional delivery notes, such as special instructions or specific delivery requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`): Enter a brief description of the delivery details.
* **Delivery Details 1** (`delivery_details_1`): Enter any additional delivery details, if necessary.
* **Delivery Details 2** (`delivery_details_2`): Enter more delivery details, if needed.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`): Enter the street address where the delivery will be made.
* **Delivery Address 1** (`delivery_address_1`): Enter any additional delivery address information, if required.
* **Delivery Address 2** (`delivery_address_2`): Enter any other delivery address details, if necessary.
* **Delivery Details 3** (`delivery_details_3`): Enter any additional delivery details, if needed.
* **Delivery Details 4** (`delivery_details_4`): Enter more delivery details, if necessary.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`): Enter the date of the delivery in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Delivery Date 1** (`delivery_date_1`): Enter the date of the delivery, if it differs from the previous field.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`): Enter the time of the delivery in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Delivery Time 1** (`delivery_time_1`): Enter the time of the delivery, if it differs from the previous field.
* **Delivery Note** (`delivery_note`): Enter any delivery notes or instructions.
* **Delivery Email** (`delivery_email`): Enter your email address for delivery updates.
* **Delivery Phone** (`delivery_phone`): Enter your phone number for delivery updates.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter the name(s) of the customer(s) receiving the delivery.
* **Customer Name 1** (`customer_name_1`): Enter any additional customer name information, if required.
