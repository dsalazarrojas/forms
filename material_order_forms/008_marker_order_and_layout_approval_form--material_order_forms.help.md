# Marker Order And Layout Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marker Order And Layout Approval Form is a submission form used by customers to request markers with specific layout and order requirements. This form is designed to gather information necessary for production staff to accurately process and fulfill the customer's order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your order and layout request in the **Order and layout request** field.
2. Choose whether you have a **Marker order** requirement.
3. Choose whether you have a **Material order** requirement.
4. Enter any specific **Layout request** information.
5. Sign and date the form with your **Customer signature**.
6. Enter the **Date of submission** when you submitted the form.
7. Enter the time the form was **Submitted at**.
8. Leave any additional **Customer comments** you would like production staff to be aware of.
9. Confirm **Customer approval** of the order.
10. Optionally, select if you **Rejection**.
11. If rejected, enter the reason for **Approval reason**.
12. Enter any **Approval comments**.
13. Choose whether you **Approve** the layout.
14. Enter any **Customer request** information.
15. Enter any **Customer layout** information.
16. If necessary, enter your **Marker ID**, **Marker name**, **Marker number**, and **Order number**.
17. Enter your **Customer name**, **Customer email**, and **Customer phone**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order and layout request**: Enter a brief description of your marker order and layout requirements.
* **Marker order**: Check if you have specific marker order requirements.
* **Material order**: Choose if you have material requirements for the marker order.
* **Layout request**: Enter any specific layout requirements for the marker.
* **Customer signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your submission.
* **Date of submission**: Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
* **Time of submission**: Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Customer comments**: Leave any additional comments or information for production staff.
* **Customer approval**: Confirm or reject the order.
* **Approval reason**: Enter the reason for rejection.
* **Approval comments**: Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Layout approval**: Choose whether you approve the layout.
* **Customer request**: Enter any additional information for the customer request.
* **Customer layout**: Enter any additional information for the customer layout.
* **Marker ID**: Enter your marker ID if necessary.
* **Marker name**: Enter your marker name if necessary.
* **Marker number**: Enter your marker number if necessary.
* **Order number**: Enter your order number if necessary.
* **Customer name**: Enter your name.
* **Customer email**: Enter your email.
* **Customer phone**: Enter your phone number.
