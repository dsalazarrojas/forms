# Menu Redesign Material Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect input on menu redesigns for a company's menu.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the menu information on the first page.
*   Select the material type (Redesign or Material Order) on the second page.
*   Select multiple material types on the third page.
*   Enter the quantity and unit price on the fourth and fifth pages.
*   Enter the total price on the sixth page.
*   Enter the order date and time on the seventh and eighth pages.
*   Enter any notes about the order on the ninth page.
*   Enter your email address on the tenth page.
*   Enter your phone number on the eleventh page.
*   Enter the address on the twelfth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Menu Information**: Enter the information about the menu being redesigned.
*   **Material Type**: Select whether this is a Redesign or Material Order.
*   **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the material being ordered.
*   **Unit Price**: Enter the unit price of the material.
*   **Total Price**: The total price is calculated based on the quantity and unit price.
*   **Date**: Enter the date for the order.
*   **Time**: Enter the time for the order.
*   **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the order.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Address**: Enter the address where the material will be delivered.
