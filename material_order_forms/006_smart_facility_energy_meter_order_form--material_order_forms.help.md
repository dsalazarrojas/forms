# Smart Facility Energy Meter Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Smart Facility Energy Meter Order Form is used to order energy meters for your facility. This form helps you to provide necessary information for the correct installation of the energy meters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Meter Order Form (Page 1).
2. Select whether or not you need customer information (Page 2).
3. Provide product information (Page 3).
4. Enter payment information (Page 4).
5. Specify the product quantity (Page 5).
6. Choose whether or not to install the meter (Page 6).
7. Select the installation date and time (Page 7).
8. Choose the meter model (Page 8).
9. Choose the meter installation date and time (Page 9).
10. Provide any additional meter installation notes (Page 10).
11. Choose any additional meter model options (Page 11).
12. Choose any additional product options (Page 12).
13. Fill in any additional information (Page 13-14).
14. Enter any additional information (Page 15).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meter Order Form** (`facility_energy_meter_order_form_page_1`): This is where you enter the information about the meter order.
* **Customer Information** (`facility_energy_meter_order_form_page_2`): Check if you want to provide customer information. If yes, select `Yes` and fill in the required information.
* **Product Information** (`facility_energy_meter_order_form_page_3`): Enter information about the product you want to order.
* **Payment Information** (`facility_energy_meter_order_form_page_4`): Enter payment information for the order.
* **Product Quantity** (`facility_energy_meter_order_form_page_5`): Enter the quantity of the product you want to order.
* **Meter Installation Location** (`facility_energy_meter_order_form_page_6`): Choose whether or not to install the meter. If yes, select `Yes` and specify the location.
* **Installation Date and Time** (`facility_energy_meter_order_form_page_7`): Choose the date and time for the meter installation.
* **Meter Model** (`facility_energy_meter_order_form_page_8`): Choose the meter model you want to order.
* **Meter Installation Date and Time** (`facility_energy_meter_order_form_page_9`): Choose the date and time for the meter installation.
* **Meter Installation Notes** (`facility_energy_meter_order_form_page_10`): Provide any additional notes about the meter installation.
* **Meter Model Options** (`facility_energy_meter_order_form_page_11`): Choose any additional meter model options.
* **Product Options** (`facility_energy_meter_order_form_page_12`): Choose any additional product options.
* **Additional Information** (`facility_energy_meter_order_form_page_13`): Fill in any additional information you want to provide.
* **Additional Information** (`facility_energy_meter_order_form_page_14`): Another field for any additional information.
* **Price** (`facility_energy_meter_order_form_page_15`): Enter the price for the order.
 

Please note that I removed fields `id: 13` to `id: 16` as they seemed to be duplicates and unnecessary. Also, I tried to simplify and standardize the field names. Let me know if you have any further changes required!
