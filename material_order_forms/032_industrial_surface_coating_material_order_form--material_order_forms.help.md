# Industrial Surface Coating Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to submit orders for industrial surface coating materials. Please fill out the required fields to ensure timely and accurate fulfillment of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one of the three material order categories: Material Order Forms, Coating Forms, or Other Forms.
2. Provide a brief description of your order in the text field below.
3. Choose the correct date for your order.
4. Enter your email address so we can contact you with order updates.
5. Enter your phone number for easy communication.
6. Add any additional notes or instructions for our team.
7. Select the categories of material orders you would like to place. This will help us fulfill your order correctly.
8. Confirm your order by selecting the "Submit" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: This field is not currently required, but it allows you to title your order for our reference.
* **Material Order Category**: This is a selection of three categories to help us understand the type of material order you are placing. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ Material Order Forms
	+ Coating Forms
	+ Other Forms
* **Description**: Please provide a brief description of your order to help us understand what you need.
* **Date**: Choose the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or instructions for our team.
* **Material Order Forms**: This is a selection of categories that will help us fulfill your order correctly. Please choose all applicable options.
* **Submit**: Confirm your order by selecting the "Submit" option.

## Tips
- Please ensure you select the correct material order category and options to avoid delays in order fulfillment.
- Double-check your information before submitting your form to ensure accuracy.
