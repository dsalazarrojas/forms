# Factory Soundproof Panel Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Factory Soundproof Panel Order Form is used to collect information necessary for the production and installation of soundproof panels for a client. This form is used to gather details about the client's delivery address, installation date and time, panel type, size, color, and other relevant information that will aid in the creation of the panels.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Complete each field with the correct information.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Name**: Enter the name of the delivery address.
	+ A short description of the delivery address, e.g., "Client's Building, Floor 3, Room 100".
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the full address of the delivery location.
	+ Include the street number and name, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you would like the delivery to occur on.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY, e.g., 01/15/2023.
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the time you would like the delivery to occur on.
	+ Format: HH:MM AM/PM, e.g., 02:00 PM.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client or person responsible for the order.
* **Client Contact**: Enter the contact information for the client or person responsible for the order.
	+ Include the name, phone number, and email address.
* **Client Notes**: Enter any additional notes or instructions from the client.
* **Panel Type**: Select the type of panel you require.
	+ Select from options 1, 2, or 3.
* **Panel Size**: Select the size of the panel you require.
	+ Select multiple options from 1, 2, 3, and 4.
* **Panel Color**: Enter the color of the panel you require.
* **Panel Quantity**: Enter the number of panels you require.
* **Installation Date**: Enter the date of installation.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY, e.g., 01/15/2023.
* **Installation Time**: Enter the time of installation.
	+ Format: HH:MM AM/PM, e.g., 02:00 PM.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or instructions.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of the order.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY, e.g., 01/01/2023.
* **Order Comments**: Enter any additional comments or instructions.

Note: Ensure you complete all required fields to avoid delays in production and installation.
