# Factory Insulation Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Insulation Material Order Form is a form used to collect necessary information for the material order process. Please complete the form accurately to enable efficient processing of your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your User Id to identify your account.
2. Select the Material Id to specify the type of material you are ordering.
3. Enter the Quantity Ordered to indicate how many units you need.
4. Choose the Order Date for when you would like the order to be processed.
5. Select the Delivery Date for when you would like to receive the materials.
6. Provide the Contact Name and Contact Phone for communication purposes.
7. Enter the Contact Email to receive notifications and updates on your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Id** (`user_id`, number, Optional): Enter your unique user ID to identify your account.
* **Material Id** (`material_id`, number, Optional): Choose the material ID to specify the type of material you are ordering.
* **Quantity Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`, number, Optional): Enter the number of units you need.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, Optional): Choose the date you would like the order to be processed.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, Optional): Select the date you would like to receive the materials.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, Optional): Provide your name for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address to receive notifications and updates on your order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your User Id is correctly filled out to avoid delays in processing your order.
* If you need assistance with the order process, contact the factory support team.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* You can always contact the factory for clarification on any field requirements or questions.
