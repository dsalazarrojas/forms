# promotional-material-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from customers for ordering promotional materials such as brochures, posters, t-shirts, hats, pins, stickers, water bottles, and mugs. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer information fields (customer name, contact person, contact title, contact email, and contact phone) with your details. 
2. Select the type of material you are ordering from the "Material Type" dropdown menu. 
3. Enter the quantity of the material you are ordering. 
4. If you have any special instructions or requests, you can enter them in the "Special Instructions" field. 
5. If you need to provide additional shipping instructions, you can enter them in the "Shipping Instructions" field. 
6. Finally, if you have any comments or feedback, you can enter them in the "Comments" field. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **promotional-material-order-form** (1): This is the form header, which cannot be edited. 
* **customer-information** (2): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself or your organization. 
* **contact-person** (3): Enter the name of the person to contact about your order. 
* **contact-title** (4): Enter the title of the contact person (e.g., Marketing Manager). 
* **contact-email** (5): Enter the email address of the contact person. 
* **contact-phone** (6): Enter the phone number of the contact person. 
* **material-type** (7): Select the type of promotional material you are ordering from the dropdown menu. 
* **quantity** (8): Enter the number of materials you are ordering. 
* **special-instructions** (9): If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, enter them here. 
* **shipping-instructions** (10): If you need to provide additional shipping instructions, enter them here. 
* **comments** (11): Enter any comments or feedback you have about your order. 
* **form-footer** (12): This is the form footer, which cannot be edited. 

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the relevant fields to ensure that your order is processed correctly. 
* If you have any questions or issues with your order, contact the contact person's email or phone number. 
* If you have any special instructions or requests, be sure to enter them in the "Special Instructions" field so that they can be taken into account.
