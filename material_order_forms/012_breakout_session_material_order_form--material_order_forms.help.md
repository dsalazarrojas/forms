# Breakout Session Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the materials needed for breakout sessions at events. This includes printed handouts, audio visual equipment, and other supplies required for the session. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the event in the "Event Name" field.
2. Provide the title of the breakout session in the "Breakout Session Title" field.
3. Select the date and time of the session in the "Session Date and Time" field.
4. Enter your name and email in the "Facilitator Name", "Facilitator Email" fields respectively.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Facilitator Phone Number" field (optional).
6. Estimate the number of participants attending the session in the "Expected Number of Participants" field.
7. Enter the location of the session in the "Session Location/Room" field.
8. Choose the type of session (e.g., Workshop, Panel discussion, Presentation with Q&A, etc.) in the "Session Format" field.
9. Select all the material categories needed for the session in the "Material Categories Needed" field.
10. Describe the printed materials needed in the "Handout Materials Description" field (optional).
11. Estimate the number of handout sets needed in the "Number of Handout Sets" field (optional).
12. List any interactive materials needed for participant engagement in the "Interactive Materials Needed" field (optional).
13. Select any audio visual equipment required for the session in the "Audio Visual Equipment Needed" field (optional).
14. Specify any special technology setup requirements in the "Technology Setup Requirements" field (optional).
15. List any additional supplies needed in the "Additional Supplies List" field (optional).
16. Enter the budget available for materials in the "Budget Available" field (optional).
17. Select the required delivery date for the materials in the "Required Delivery Date" field.
18. Enter any special requests or instructions in the "Special Requests or Instructions" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name (1)**: Name of the conference or meeting.
* **Breakout Session Title (2)**: Title of your breakout session.
* **Session Date and Time (3)**: When is your session scheduled.
* **Facilitator Name (4)**: Your full name.
* **Facilitator Email (5)**: Your contact email.
* **Facilitator Phone Number (6)**: Your contact phone number (optional).
* **Expected Number of Participants (7)**: Estimate the number of people who will attend your session.
* **Session Location/Room (8)**: Where will the session be held.
* **Session Format (9)**: Type of session (e.g., Workshop, Panel discussion, Presentation with Q&A, etc.).
* **Material Categories Needed (10)**: Select all material types you need (e.g., Printed handouts, Workbooks or notebooks, etc.).
* **Handout Materials Description (11)**: Describe printed materials needed (optional).
* **Number of Handout Sets (12)**: Estimate the number of copies needed (optional).
* **Interactive Materials Needed (13)**: List any materials for participant engagement (optional).
* **Audio Visual Equipment Needed (14)**: Select any audio visual equipment required for the session (e.g., Projector, Screen or whiteboard, Microphone, etc.).
* **Technology Setup Requirements (15)**: Specify any special technology needs (optional).
* **Additional Supplies List (16)**: List any other materials you need (optional).
* **Budget Available (17)**: Enter your budget for materials (optional).
* **Required Delivery Date (18)**: When you need the materials.
* **Special Requests or Instructions (19)**: Enter any special requests or instructions.
