# Material Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to submit a material requisition request for work-related resources. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your order number, if you have one.
2. Select the date you need the material by, if it's not the current date.
3. Choose the equipment or supplies you need from the available options.
4. Specify the quantity of each item required.
5. Select the supplier for the requested materials.
6. Add any comments or notes about the requisition.
7. Enter your email address for contact.
8. Fill in your phone number and fax number (if applicable).
9. Select who is requisitioning the material (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Material Requisition Form Label**: This field is a brief description of this form.
* **Order Number**: If you have a specific order number, enter it here. This can help the procurement team quickly identify your request.
* **Order Date**: Select the date you need the materials by. If the date is not specified, the default is usually the current date.
* **Equipment/Supplies Requested**: Select the equipment or supplies you need from the provided options. Be specific about what you need, as it will help the procurement team make accurate decisions.
* **Quantity Requested**: Enter the quantity of each item you need. This will help the procurement team know how many of each item to procure.
* **Supplier**: Choose the supplier that will provide the materials or equipment you need. Be sure to select the one that can meet your requirements.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or notes about the requisition, if needed.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so the procurement team can contact you for any questions or updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so the procurement team can contact you.
* **Fax**: Enter your fax number (if applicable).
* **Requisitioned By**: Select who is requisitioning the material. This is usually the employee who needs the material for their work task.
