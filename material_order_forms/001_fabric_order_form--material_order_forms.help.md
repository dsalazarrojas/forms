# Fabric Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fabric Order Form is a tool used to gather information about fabric orders, including customer details, order details, and fabric specifics. This form helps to streamline the ordering process and ensure accurate communication between customers and our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information, including name and other relevant details.
2. Provide order details, including the type of fabric, quantity, width, and length.
3. Select the fabric type (cotton, polyester, or wool).
4. Choose the desired fabric color(s).
5. Specify the fabric weight and unit of measurement (pounds, kilograms, or ounces).
6. Select the payment method and fabric price.
7. Add any special instructions or comments.
8. Select the shipping address (home, work, or other).
9. Complete the form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info (`customer_info`, text, Optional)**: Enter the customer's name and any other relevant information.
* **Order Details (`order_details`, text, Optional)**: Describe the order you're placing, including any specific instructions or details.
* **Fabric Type (`fabric_type`, select_one, Optional)**: Choose the type of fabric you need (cotton, polyester, or wool).
* **Fabric Color (`fabric_color`, select_multiple, Optional)**: Select the color(s) you want for your fabric (red, blue, or green).
* **Fabric Width (`fabric_width`, number, Optional)**: Enter the width of the fabric you require.
* **Fabric Length (`fabric_length`, number, Optional)**: Enter the length of the fabric you require.
* **Fabric Thickness (`fabric_thickness`, number, Optional)**: Enter the thickness of the fabric you require.
* **Special Instructions (`special_instructions`, text, Optional)**: Add any special requests or notes about your order.
* **Shipping Address (`shipping_address`, select_one, Optional)**: Choose where the fabric will be shipped to (home, work, or other).
* **Fabric Weight (`fabric_weight`, number, Optional)**: Enter the weight of the fabric.
* **Fabric Weight Unit (`fabric_weight_unit`, select_one, Optional)**: Select the unit of measurement for the fabric weight (pounds, kilograms, or ounces).
* **Fabric Weight Value (`fabric_weight_value`, number, Optional)**: Enter the fabric weight value.
* **Fabric Cost (`fabric_cost`, number, Optional)**: Enter the cost of the fabric.
* **Fabric Price (`fabric_price`, number, Optional)**: Enter the price of the fabric.
* **Fabric Total (`fabric_total`, number, Optional)**: Calculate the total cost of the order.
* **Fabric Notes (`fabric_notes`, text, Optional)**: Add any notes or comments about the fabric.
* **Customer Notes (`customer_notes`, text, Optional)**: Add any additional notes or comments from the customer.
* **Order Comments (`order_comments`, text, Optional)**: Add any comments or notes about the order.
* **Payment Method (`payment_method`, select_one, Optional)**: Choose the payment method (bank transfer or credit card).
* **Order Status (`order_status`, select_one, Optional)**: Select the status of the order (pending, confirmed, or cancelled).
* **Order Notes (`order_notes`, text, Optional)**: Add any notes or comments about the order.
* **Order ID (`order_id`, text, Optional)**: Enter the order ID.
* **Assigned To (`assigned_to`, select_one, Optional)**: Choose who the order is assigned to (chatjimmy or another user).
* **Created At (`created_at`, date, Optional)**: Display the date the order was created.
* **Updated At (`updated_at`, date, Optional)**: Display the date the order was last updated.
