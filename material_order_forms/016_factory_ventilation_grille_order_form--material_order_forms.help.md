# Factory Ventilation Grille Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Factory Ventilation Grille Order Form" is used to collect and record order details for ventilation grilles. It ensures that accurate information is gathered and stored for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for each field.
2. Select the grille specifications that best match your order requirements.
3. Enter the quantity of the grille you're ordering.
4. Choose the desired color for the grille.
5. Select the material for the grille.
6. Choose the shape of the grille.
7. Enter the delivery details.
8. Choose a delivery date and time.
9. Enter the delivery address.
10. Select the payment method.
11. Choose the payment terms.
12. Enter the payment total.
13. Enter any additional delivery instructions.
14. Enter delivery notes.
15. Select the order status.
16. Enter the customer's name.
17. Enter the customer's email address.
18. Enter the customer's phone number.
19. Enter any customer notes.
20. Enter the company name.
21. Enter the company email address.
22. Enter the company phone number.
23. Enter any company notes.
24. Assign the order to a specific employee.
