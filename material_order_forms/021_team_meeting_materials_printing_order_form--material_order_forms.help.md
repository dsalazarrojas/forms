# Team Meeting Materials Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Meeting Materials Printing Order Form is used to collect information about team meeting materials printing orders. It is meant to be filled out by meeting coordinators or event planners to gather the necessary details for printing team meeting materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the meeting title in the "Meeting Title" field.
2. Enter the meeting date in the "Meeting Date" field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. Enter the meeting time in the "Meeting Time" field in the format `HH:MM`.
4. Enter the meeting location in the "Meeting Location" field.
5. Select the files needed for the meeting from the "Files Needed" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the quantity of each file needed in the "File Quantities" field.
7. Enter any additional delivery details in the "Delivery Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Title** (`meeting_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the meeting.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the meeting in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the meeting in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Files Needed** (`meeting_files`, select_multiple, optional): Select the files needed for the meeting.
* **File Quantities** (`file_quantities`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of each file needed.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional delivery details.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate printing of meeting materials.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your meeting coordinator or event planner for clarification.
* The form is meant to be filled out once per meeting, so please do not reuse old forms for new meetings.
