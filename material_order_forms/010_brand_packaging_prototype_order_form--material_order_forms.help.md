# Brand Packaging Prototype Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order custom packaging prototypes for your product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your company name, contact person, and contact email address to ensure we can communicate with you effectively.
2. Select the type of product you need packaging for (e.g., food, cosmetics, electronics).
3. Choose the packaging type (e.g., box, bottle, pouch).
4. Specify the quantity of prototypes you need.
5. Select your preferred packaging material (e.g., cardboard, plastic, glass).
6. Enter the package dimensions (length, width, height) in inches or centimeters.
7. If necessary, specify any specific colors or finishes needed.
8. Select any additional printing requirements (e.g., logo, text, barcode).
9. Check if you have any special features (e.g., window cutout, embossing).
10. Confirm if your design files are ready or if you need design help.
11. Enter the required delivery date.
12. Enter the shipping address where the prototypes will be delivered.
13. Estimate your budget range for the project.
14. Finally, add any additional specifications or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (1, text, required): Enter the legal name of your company.
* **Contact Person Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for this order.
* **Contact Email** (3, email, required): Enter the email address for order communications.
* **Contact Phone** (4, text, required): Enter the phone number for order questions.
* **Product Type** (5, select_one, required): Select the type of product you need packaging for.
* **Packaging Type** (6, select_one, required): Select the type of packaging you need.
* **Prototype Quantity** (7, select_one, required): Specify how many prototypes you need.
* **Material Preference** (8, select_one, required): Select your preferred packaging material.
* **Package Length** (9, number, required): Enter the length of the package in inches or centimeters.
* **Package Width** (10, number, required): Enter the width of the package in inches or centimeters.
* **Package Height** (11, number, required): Enter the height of the package in inches or centimeters.
* **Color Requirements** (12, text, optional): Enter any specific colors or finishes needed.
* **Printing Requirements** (13, select_multiple, required): Select any additional printing requirements.
* **Special Features** (14, select_multiple, optional): Select any special features you need.
* **Design Files Ready** (15, select_one, required): Confirm if your design files are ready or if you need design help.
* **Deadline Date** (16, date, required): Enter the required delivery date.
* **Shipping Address** (17, text, required): Enter the full delivery address.
* **Budget Estimate** (18, select_one, required): Estimate your budget range for the project.
* **Additional Specifications** (19, text, optional): Enter any additional specifications or notes.
