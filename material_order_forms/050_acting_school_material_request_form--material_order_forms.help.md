# Acting School Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Acting School Material Request Form is designed to help students and teachers request materials for their classes, performances, or projects. It's a quick and easy way to submit requests for essential materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Student or Teacher Name field with the name of the person making the request.
2. Enter the Class Name where the material is needed.
3. Select the Request Type from the options provided (Script, Prop, Costume Item, Technical Equipment, or Reference Material).
4. Describe the material you need in the Item Description field.
5. Specify the quantity of material needed.
6. Choose a Date Needed By for delivery or pickup.
7. Select the Urgency Level of your request (Normal or High - Needed for imminent performance).
8. If applicable, provide Context for the request in the Scene or Project Reference field.
9. Choose how you will receive the materials (In-class Pickup, Office Collection, or Digital Download).
10. The Department Head Approval Signature and Request Date fields are optional and intended for internal use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student or Teacher Name**: Fill in the name of the person making the request.
* **Class Name**: Enter the name of the class where the material is needed.
* **Request Type**: Choose the category of material you need (Script, Prop, Costume Item, Technical Equipment, or Reference Material).
* **Item Description**: Describe the material you need. This helps us understand your request better.
* **Quantity Needed**: Specify the amount of material you need.
* **Date Needed By**: Choose a date when you need the material.
* **Urgency Level**: Select if the material is needed immediately (High - Needed for imminent performance) or not (Normal).
* **Scene or Project Reference**: If applicable, provide context for your request.
* **Pickup or Delivery**: Choose how you will receive the materials (In-class Pickup, Office Collection, or Digital Download).
* **Department Head Approval Signature**: This is for internal use only.
* **Request Date**: The date of submission of the request.

## Tips
- Make sure to submit only one request at a time to avoid confusion.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the department head or support team for clarification.
- Be as specific as possible with your requests to help us fulfill them efficiently. 

This guide is designed to help you complete the Acting School Material Request Form with ease, ensuring that your requests are processed quickly and accurately.
