# Factory Emergency Exit Signage Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Emergency Exit Signage Order Form is used to order emergency exit signage for the factory. This form is intended for factory staff to provide information about the signage required for emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the factory name in the "Factory Name" field.
2. Provide the name and contact information of the contact person responsible for the emergency exit signage.
3. Enter the phone number of the contact person.
4. Enter the email address of the contact person.
5. Specify the location where the signage is required.
6. Enter the quantity of signage needed.
7. Select the material type of the signage (Sign or Exit Sign).
8. Choose the size of the signage (Standard, Large, or Extra Large).
9. Select the color of the signage (Red, Blue, or Green).
10. Enter the date the signage is required by.
11. Enter the time the signage is required by.
12. Provide any additional comments or instructions about the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Factory Name**: Enter the name of the factory where the emergency exit signage is required.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person who will be contact for this order.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Location**: Enter the specific location within the factory where the signage is required.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of signage required.
* **Material Type**: Select the type of signage required (Sign or Exit Sign).
* **Size**: Choose the size of the signage (Standard, Large, or Extra Large).
* **Color**: Select the color of the signage (Red, Blue, or Green).
* **Date**: Enter the date the signage is required by.
* **Time**: Enter the time the signage is required by.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or instructions about the order.
* **Form Submitted By**: Enter your name or identifier as the person submitting the form.
