# Fireproof Flooring Tile Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fireproof Flooring Tile Order Form is used by customers to place an order for fireproof flooring tiles after speaking with our sales team.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete each section of the form in order.
- Start with "Customer Details" and enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
- In "Tile Selections", choose your preferred tile options, including whether you want them.
- Select a delivery date and time that works for you in "Delivery Date" and "Delivery Time".
- Enter the desired delivery location in "Delivery Location".
- Enter the expected "Order Total" to ensure accurate pricing.
- Choose your preferred "Payment Method" (if yes, we'll contact you with payment instructions).
- Add any "Customer Notes" you'd like to share with our team.
- Enter your "Customer Email" and "Customer Phone" so we can contact you with updates.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details (1)**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Delivery Address (2)**: Enter the address where the tiles should be delivered.
* **Tile Selections (3)**: Choose from the available tile options by selecting the "Yes" option next to each tile type you'd like.
* **Delivery Date (4)**: Select a date for the delivery of your tiles.
* **Delivery Time (5)**: Select a time for the delivery of your tiles.
* **Delivery Location (6)**: Enter the specific location where the tiles should be delivered, such as a room or area.
* **Order Total (7)**: Enter the expected total cost of your order, including any applicable discounts or promotions.
* **Payment Method (8)**: Choose "Yes" to allow us to contact you with payment instructions; choose "No" if you'd like to take care of payment separately.
* **Customer Notes (9)**: Add any additional comments or notes to help our team with your order.
* **Customer Email (10)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and information.
* **Customer Phone (11)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any questions or concerns.

## Tips

* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
