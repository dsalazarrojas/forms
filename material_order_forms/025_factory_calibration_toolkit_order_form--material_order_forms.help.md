# Factory Calibration Toolkit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Calibration Toolkit Order Form is a tool used to place orders for calibration tools. It is designed to be simple and easy to use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the type of tool you are ordering. This will help us ensure that we have the correct type of tool in stock.
2. Enter the name of the tool you are ordering.
3. If you have a specific calibration date in mind for the tool, please enter it.
4. Specify the quantity of the tool you need.
5. Provide your contact information so we can reach out to you if needed.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
7. Finally, confirm the total cost of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tool Type** (`tool_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of tool you are ordering from the options provided.
* **Tool Name** (`tool_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the tool you are ordering.
* **Calibration Date** (`calibration_date`, date, required: false): If you have a specific calibration date in mind, please enter it here.
* **Quantity Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`, number, required: false): Specify the quantity of the tool you need.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the customer or person placing the order.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required: false): Confirm the total cost of your order.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure that we can fulfill your order correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* This form is intended for factory employees only, so please do not share it with anyone outside of the factory.
