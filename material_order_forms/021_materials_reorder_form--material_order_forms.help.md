# Materials Reorder Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Materials Reorder Form is used to reorder materials, such as Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3, for a specific customer. This form is designed to help customers request new materials for their needs, ensuring that the necessary items are reordered on time and in the correct quantity.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the correct customer details**: Enter the customer's details in the "customer_details" field. This field is used to identify the customer and their associated materials.
2. **Select additional materials**: Choose the additional materials that the customer requires by selecting from the options in the "additional_materials" field. This is a drop-down menu with multiple levels (Level 1, Level 2, Level 3).
3. **Select exam booklet levels**: Select the exam booklet levels required for the customer by choosing multiple options in the "exam_booklet_levels" field.
4. **Enter customer phone number**: If the customer has a phone number, enter it in the "customer_phone" field. This is an optional field.
5. **Enter customer email**: If the customer has an email address, enter it in the "customer_email" field. This is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Customer Details
* **customer_details** (text, required/optional): Enter the customer's name and any other relevant details to identify the customer.

### Additional Materials
* **additional_materials** (select_one, required/optional): Select the additional materials required for the customer. Choose from Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3 options.

### Exam Booklet Levels
* **exam_booklet_levels** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select multiple exam booklet levels required for the customer. Choose from Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3 options.

### Customer Phone
* **customer_phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number, if available.

### Customer Email
* **customer_email** (email, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address, if available.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have the correct customer details and materials selected to avoid any delays in the reorder process.
* If you are unsure of any customer details or materials, please contact the relevant personnel for assistance.
* The form will be reviewed and processed by the relevant staff member after submission.
