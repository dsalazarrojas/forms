# Mining Explosives Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mining Explosives Requisition Form is used to submit a request for mining explosives, which are essential tools for mining operations. This form is typically used by requestors to provide the necessary information for the requisitioning process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in all the required fields, which are marked as "Required" in the field description.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
3. Review the form before submission to ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Info** (`request_info`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the request, including the purpose and any other relevant details.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are submitting the request.
* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the person submitting the request.
* **Requestor Department** (`requestor_department`, text, optional): Enter the department or team you are representing.
* **Requestor Phone** (`requestor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Requestor Email** (`requestor_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Requestor Location** (`requestor_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the mining explosives will be used.
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, note, optional): Provide any additional details or notes about the request.

## Tips
- Be sure to review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are filled out.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the designated authority for clarification.
- This form is intended to be used for legitimate requests only; unauthorized requests will not be processed.
