# Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Order Form is a tool used to order materials from our inventory. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that we can process your order correctly and deliver the materials as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out your contact information, including your phone number and email address, in the "contact_info" field.
* Choose the date you'd like to order materials in the "date_ordered" field.
* Select the materials you'd like to order in the "select_materials" field.
* If you have any special requests or instructions, enter them in the "special_requests" field.
* Choose the delivery details, such as time, in the "delivery_details" field.
* Enter the date you'd like to receive your materials in the "delivery_date" field.
* If you have any special instructions for the delivery, enter them in the "special_instructions" field.
* Fill out your contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
* Enter your contact email address in the "contact_email" field.
* Indicate whether you've been assigned to someone else to handle the order in the "assigned" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **contact_info (Number)**: Enter your phone number and any other contact information.
* **date_ordered (Date)**: Choose the date you'd like to order materials.
* **select_materials (Select Multiple)**: Select the materials you'd like to order.
* **special_requests (Text)**: If you have any special requests or instructions, enter them here.
* **delivery_details (Time)**: Choose the delivery details, such as time.
* **delivery_date (Date)**: Choose the date you'd like to receive your materials.
* **special_instructions (Text)**: If you have any special instructions for delivery, enter them here.
* **contact_phone (Text)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **contact_email (Email)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **assigned (Text)**: Indicate whether you've been assigned to someone else to handle the order.
