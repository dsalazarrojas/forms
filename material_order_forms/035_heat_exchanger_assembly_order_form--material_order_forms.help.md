# Heat Exchanger Assembly Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is for customers to provide necessary information about their heat exchanger assembly orders. Please complete it carefully to ensure your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please fill out all the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
* For fields without options, type in your answer directly.
* For fields with options, select one or multiple options from the provided list.
* If a field is left blank, your order may be delayed or not fulfilled accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **contact_number**: Enter your contact number where you can be reached for further questions or updates.
* **order_details**: Provide a brief description of your order, including any specific requirements or instructions.
* **order_date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **heat_exchanger_assembly**: Select the type of heat exchanger assembly you would like to order (Heat Exchanger Assembly 1, Heat Exchanger Assembly 2, or Heat Exchanger Assembly 3).
* **material_type**: Choose one or multiple materials you would like to use for the assembly (Material 1, Material 2, or Material 3).
* **quantity**: Enter the quantity of the selected material.
* **comments**: If you have any additional comments or instructions, please provide them here.
* **email**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates about your order.
* **form_10**: Select one or multiple options to provide additional information about your order (Select Option 1, Select Option 2, or Select Option 3).
* **time**: If applicable, enter a specific time for your order pickup or delivery.

Note: If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to contact our support team. We are here to help you.
