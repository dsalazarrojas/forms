# Robotics Calibration Stand Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Robotics Calibration Stand Order Form is a form designed to collect and manage orders for robot calibration. It helps ensure that the required materials and specifications are accurately documented, making it an essential tool for streamlining communication between users and the production team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Calibration Request Form** with any additional details regarding the calibration request.
2. In the **Select Materials** field, choose the correct material for the calibration.
3. Enter the **Quantity** of the materials needed.
4. Fill in the **Date of Receipt** to track when the materials are received.
5. Provide any **Order Details** relevant to the calibration.
6. Select the **Contact Person** responsible for the order.
7. Enter the **Email** and **Phone Number** of the contact person.
8. Choose the **Delivery Date** and **Delivery Time** for the order.
9. Fill in the **Shipping Address** where the materials will be delivered.
10. Specify the **Payment Terms** for the order.
11. Enter any **Special Instructions** relevant to the order.
12. Assign the **Assigned To** person or team responsible for the order.
13. Specify the **Assigned Tool** required for the calibration.
14. Enter the **Customer PO Number** and **Internal PO Number** for the order.
15. Fill in the **Purchase Order** details.
16. Select the **Order Status** of the order (e.g., New, In Progress, Shipped, Completed, Cancelled, Returned).
17. Add any **Notes** regarding the order.
18. Fill in the **Created By** and **Modified By** fields with the names of the users involved.
19. Finally, enter the **Created At** and **Modified At** dates to track the order's history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Calibration Request Form** (`calibration_request_form`, text, not required): 
  This field allows you to enter any additional details regarding the calibration request. It's a text area where you can provide a brief description of the calibration needed.
* **Select Materials** (`material_selection`, select_one, not required):
  Choose the correct material for the calibration.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, not required): 
  Enter the quantity of materials needed for the calibration.
* **Date of Receipt** (`date_of_receipt`, date, not required): 
  Track when the materials are received.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, not required): 
  Provide any additional details relevant to the calibration.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, not required): 
  Specify the contact person responsible for the order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required):
  Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, not required): 
  Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, not required): 
  Choose the delivery date for the order.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, not required): 
  Choose the delivery time for the order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, not required): 
  Enter the shipping address where the materials will be delivered.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, not required):
  Specify the payment terms for the order.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, not required): 
  Enter any special instructions relevant to the order.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, not required): 
  Assign the person or team responsible for the order.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, not required): 
  Specify the assigned tool required for the calibration.
* **Customer PO Number** (`customer_po_number`, text, not required): 
  Enter the customer PO number.
* **Internal PO Number** (`internal_po_number`, text, not required): 
  Enter the internal PO number.
* **Purchase Order** (`purchase_order`, text, not required): 
  Fill in the purchase order details.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, not required):
  Choose the status of the order (e.g., New, In Progress, Shipped, Completed, Cancelled, Returned).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, not required): 
  Add any notes regarding the order.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, not required): 
  Fill in the name of the user who created the order.
* **Modified By** (`modified_by`, text, not required): 
  Fill in the name of the user who modified the order.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, not required): 
  Enter the date when the order was created.
* **Modified At** (`modified_at`, date, not required): 
  Enter the date when the order was last modified.
