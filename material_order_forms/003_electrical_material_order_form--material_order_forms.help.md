# electrical material order form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The electrical material order form is a tool used to collect information from customers and suppliers for electrical material orders. It's designed to provide a structured way to gather details about the customer, supplier, and products being ordered, making it easier to manage and process orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the customer information section, which includes fields for customer details.
2. Move on to the supplier information section, where you'll provide the supplier's details.
3. List the product information for each product being ordered in the product information sections.
4. Finally, add your signature in the customer and supplier signature fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information** (1): Provide your contact information, including name, email, and any other relevant details.
- **Supplier Information** (2): Enter your business details, such as company name, address, and contact information.
- **Product 1 Information** (3): Describe the product being ordered, including its details and quantity.
- **Product 2 Information** (4): Describe the product being ordered, including its details and quantity.
- **Product 3 Information** (5): Describe the product being ordered, including its details and quantity.
- **Customer Signature** (6): Sign and date your order.
- **Supplier Signature** (7): Sign and date your acknowledgement of the order.
