# Café Loyalty Program Materials Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for ordering loyalty program materials (e.g., loyalty cards, brochures, flyers, posters, banners) for a café. Fill out the form to request materials that meet your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide café information in the first section.
2. Specify materials order details, including quantities and design preferences.
3. Confirm delivery information, including date and method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Café Name** (`cafe_name`): Enter the name of your café.
* **Contact Person** (`cafe_contact_person`): Enter the name of the person to contact for further information.
* **Phone Number** (`cafe_phone`): Enter your café's phone number for easy contact.
* **Email Address** (`cafe_email`): Enter your café's email address for quick communication.
* **Café Address** (`cafe_address`): Enter your café's address for delivery purposes.
* **Quantity of Loyalty Cards Needed** (`loyalty_cards_qty`): Specify the number of loyalty cards required.
* **Card Design Preference** (`card_design`): Choose from:
	+ Standard Design
	+ Custom Design (upload)
	+ Digital Card
* **Upload Custom Design File** (`custom_design_upload`): If you prefer a custom design, upload a PDF or image file here.
* **Quantity of Marketing Brochures** (`brochures_qty`): Specify the number of brochures needed.
* **Quantity of Promotional Flyers** (`flyers_qty`): Specify the number of flyers needed.
* **Quantity of Posters** (`posters_qty`): Specify the number of posters needed.
* **Quantity of Banners** (`banner_qty`): Specify the number of banners needed.
* **Loyalty Program Structure** (`loyalty_rewards`): Describe your loyalty program's structure and rules.
* **Include Reward Tiers?** (`reward_tiers`): Indicate if you want to include reward tiers in your loyalty program.
* **Include Café Logo?** (`logo_inclusion`): Choose if you want to include your café's logo on the materials.
* **Desired Delivery Date** (`desired_delivery_date`): Select a specific date for desired delivery.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`): Choose from:
	+ Shipping
	+ Local Pickup
	+ Courier
* **Estimated Delivery Cost** (`delivery_cost`): Enter the estimated delivery cost (if applicable).

Note: Make sure to review and fill out all required fields (marked as `required`) accurately to ensure smooth order processing. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
