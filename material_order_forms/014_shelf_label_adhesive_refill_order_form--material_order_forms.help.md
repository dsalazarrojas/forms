# Shelf Label Adhesive Refill Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Shelf Label Adhesive Refill Order Form is designed to collect information for the refill of shelf labels. This form is intended for users who need to reorder adhesive for their shelf labels. Please ensure you are using this form for its intended purpose and not to duplicate work. If you are unsure, please review the form carefully before submitting it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "User Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter the name of your company in the "Company" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name to help us identify you in case we need to contact you about your order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can send you order updates and instructions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for any reason.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company to help us process your order correctly.

## Tips
- Please double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
- If you are having trouble filling out any field, contact your support team for assistance.

This guide assumes you have a basic understanding of email and text fields, so we do not need to elaborate on them in more detail.
