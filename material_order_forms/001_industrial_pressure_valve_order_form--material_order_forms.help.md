# Industrial Pressure Valve Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information to create an order for industrial pressure valves. To ensure accurate and timely processing of your order, please fill out the form completely and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter the necessary information in each field according to the field-by-field explanation section.
2. Review your submission carefully before submitting.
3. If you encounter any issues, don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Manufacturer Name
*   **Manufacturer Name** (`manufacturer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the manufacturer of the industrial pressure valve you wish to purchase.

### Contact Information
*   **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your order.
*   **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding your order.

### Product Details
*   **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the product you wish to purchase.
*   **Product Type** (`product_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of valve you are interested in purchasing from the available options (Valve Type A, Valve Type B, or Valve Type C).
*   **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity of the product you wish to purchase.
*   **Price** (`price`, `number`, required): Enter the price of the product.

### Shipping Information
*   **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping address so we can ensure correct delivery.
*   **City** (`shipping_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city for your shipping address.
*   **State** (`shipping_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state for your shipping address.
*   **Zip** (`shipping_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the zip code for your shipping address.
*   **Country** (`shipping_country`, `text`, required): Enter the country for your shipping address.

### Additional Information
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): If you know the date you wish to ship by, please enter it here.

## Tips
*   Please double-check your submission for accuracy before submitting.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
*   If you need assistance with filling out this form, contact us for support.
