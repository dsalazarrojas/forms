## industrial_cable_tray_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The industrial cable tray order form is used to collect information for ordering industrial cable trays. Fill out this form to provide the required details for your order. The form is divided into several sections, and each section has a specific set of fields that need to be completed. Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to avoid any delays or issues with your order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll down to the first page of the form and select "Cable Tray Details" from the options.
2. Fill out the "Cable Type" field by selecting the type of cable you need from the dropdown menu.
3. In the "Cable Size" field, enter the length and/or diameter of the cable you require.
4. Enter the quantity of cable you need in the "Quantity" field.
5. Select the desired "Cable Finish" from the dropdown menu.
6. Choose the "Cable Material" from the dropdown menu.
7. In the "Shipping Address" field, enter your shipping address.
8. Fill out the "Customer Name" field with your name.
9. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
11. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
12. In the "Comments" field, enter any additional information or comments about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cable Tray Details** (`cable_tray_details`, text, required/optional): This is the first section of the form. It's a text field where you can enter any additional information about the cable tray.
* **Cable Type** (`cable_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of cable you need from the dropdown menu.
* **Cable Size** (`cable_size`, number, required/optional): Enter the length and/or diameter of the cable you require.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of cable you need.
* **Cable Finish** (`cable_finish`, select_one, required/optional): Select the desired finish for your cable.
* **Cable Material** (`cable_material`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the material for your cable.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required/optional): Enter your company name.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your order.

## Tips

* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
