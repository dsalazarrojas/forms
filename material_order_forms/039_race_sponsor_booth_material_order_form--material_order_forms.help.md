# Race Sponsor Booth Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Race Sponsor Booth Material Order Form is designed to collect information related to material orders for sponsors' booths at race events. This form helps gather essential details about events, materials to be ordered, and delivery information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review and fill in the Event Details page.
2. On the Select Booth Materials page, choose the materials you need for your booth.
3. On the Delivery Information page, enter your contact details and delivery information.
4. Review and confirm your event details on the Share Event Details page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (page 1):
	+ **Event Details**: This page collects general information about the event, such as date, time, and location.
* **Select Booth Materials** (page 2):
	+ **Select Booth Materials**: Choose the materials you need for your booth from the available options (Tote Bag, Bottle, T-shirt, Hoodie).
* **Delivery Information** (page 3):
	+ **Delivery Information**: Enter your contact name, email, phone number, and delivery information.
* **Share Event Details** (page 4):
	+ **Share Event Details**: Enter a brief description of your event for the sponsor to confirm the details.
* **Event Details 2-7** (pages 5-10):
	+ These pages collect additional event information, such as event date, time, location, and type.
* **Event Date 2-3** (pages 11-12):
	+ **Event Date**: Enter the date of your event.
* **Event Time 2-3** (pages 13-14):
	+ **Event Time**: Enter the start time of your event.
* **Event Location 2-3** (pages 15-16):
	+ **Event Location**: Enter the location of your event.
* **Event Type 2-7** (pages 17-22):
	+ **Select Event Type**: Choose the type of event you are hosting.
