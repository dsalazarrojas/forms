# Machinery Lubrication Tube Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for ordering machinery lubrication tubes. Please use this form to provide details about your order, and we will process it accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Tube Size** field with the size of the lubrication tube you require.
2. Enter the **Quantity** of tubes you need to order.
3. Select a **Delivery Date** for your order.
4. Provide your **Delivery Address** where the order will be shipped to.
5. Choose the **Contact Person** responsible for this order (if applicable).
6. If applicable, fill in the **Total Cost** of the order.
7. Add any additional **Notes** regarding your order.
8. Provide your **Email** address for communication purposes.
9. Enter your **Phone** number for contact and delivery confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tube Size (Number)**: Enter the size of the machinery lubrication tube you need to order.
* **Quantity (Number)**: Indicate how many tubes of the specified size you need.
* **Delivery Date (Date)**: Choose a date for the desired delivery of your order.
* **Delivery Address (Text)**: Enter the address where the order will be shipped to.
* **Contact Person (Select One)**: Select the person responsible for this order from the provided list.
* **Total Cost (Number)**: If applicable, fill in the total cost of your order for accurate pricing.
* **Notes (Note)**: Add any additional comments or notes related to your order.
* **Email (Email)**: Provide your email address for communication and order updates.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number for contact and delivery confirmation purposes.
