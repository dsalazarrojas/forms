# Safety Data Sheet Binder Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to order a binder or a collection of safety data sheets. Please fill out the form with your contact and shipping details to process your order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have a clear and legible handwriting.
* Fill out each field with the required information. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) next to the label.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Safety Data Sheet Binder Order Form *
This field is for writing or typing the name of the safety data sheet binder or collection you are ordering.
### 2. Contact Details *
This field is for providing your contact information, such as your name and address.
### 3. Shipping Details *
This field is for providing shipping details, such as the shipping method and any special instructions.
### 4. Contact Name
This field is for providing your name as the person ordering the safety data sheet binder or collection.
### 5. Contact Email
This field is for providing your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your contact and shipping details for accuracy.
* If you need to make any changes or updates, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We will respond to your order request as soon as possible after receiving your completed form.
