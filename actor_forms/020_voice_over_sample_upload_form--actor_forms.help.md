# Voice Over Sample Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from voice over talent for sample uploads.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill out the first page with the basic information about the submission.
2. If necessary, go back and forth between pages to fill out additional information.
3. When you have completed all the required information, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Voice-over Type** (`6`, `select_one`): Select the type of voice over you are submitting, such as Commercial, Narration, etc.
* **Talent Name** (`4`, `text`): Enter the name of the talent submitting the voice over.
* **Talent Email** (`5`, `email`): Enter the email address of the talent submitting the voice over.
* **Demo Reel Length** (`7`, `text`): Enter the length of the demo reel being submitted.
* **Upload Demo Reel** (`8`, `text`): Upload the demo reel for evaluation.
