# Kid Audition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents or guardians to provide information about their child's audition details such as name, contact information, and performance history in order to facilitate the audition process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field, marked as "required."
2. Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for your child's audition details.
3. If you're unsure about any field, you can ask your local contact person for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (required): Enter your child's full name.
- **Email** (required): Enter your child's email address.
- **Phone** (required): Enter your child's phone number.
- **Parent Name** (required): Enter your own name.
- **Audition Date** (required): Enter the date of the audition.
- **Audition Time** (required): Enter the time of the audition.
- **Performance Experience** (required): Describe your child's performance experience, including any relevant training or experience.
- **Role** (optional): Select 'Yes' if your child is auditioning for a role, 'No' otherwise.
- **Performance Type** (optional): Select all types that apply to your child's performance experience.
- **Schedule Audition** (required): Enter the date of the scheduled audition.
- **Audition Time** (required): Enter the time of the scheduled audition.
- **Audition Note** (required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the audition.
- **Additional Info** (optional): Provide any additional information about your child that's relevant to the audition.
- **Performance Location** (required): Enter the location of the performance.
- **Parent Permission** (optional): Select 'Yes' if you give permission for your child's participation.
- **Schedule Audition Repeat** (optional): Enter the date and time of the repeated audition (if applicable).
- **Audition Time Repeat** (optional): Enter the time of the repeated audition (if applicable).
- **Performance Schedule Audition Repeat** (optional): Enter the location of the repeated performance (if applicable).
- **Audition Note Repeat** (optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the repeated audition.
- **Additional Info Repeat** (optional): Provide any additional information about the repeated audition.
- **Parent Permission Repeat** (optional): Select 'Yes' if you give permission for your child's participation in the repeated audition.
- **Additional Fields** (optional): Enter any additional information about the audition that doesn't fit into other fields.

Note: This form is used for both regular and repeated auditions.
