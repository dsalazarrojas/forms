# Theater Cast Media Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from the cast for media recording, use, and sharing in theater performances. It ensures that the cast's rights and interests are respected and protected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the media type (Image, Video, or Audio) that corresponds to the type of media you wish to record or use.
2. Indicate your consent to the media recording, use, and sharing (Yes, No).
3. Provide the name of the person who is the guardian or parent (Guardian's Name).
4. Enter the guardian's email address (Guardian's Email).
5. Enter the guardian's phone number (Guardian's Phone).
6. Enter the name of the performer (Performer's Name).
7. Enter the performer's email address (Performer's Email).
8. Enter the performer's phone number (Performer's Phone).
9. Enter the name of the school the performance is for (School).
10. Enter the teacher's name (Teacher).
11. Select which category the performance belongs to (Student, Parent, Parent or Other).
12. Enter any other relevant details about the performance (Other Details).
13. Enter the date and time of the performance (Date and Time).
14. Enter the rehearsal dates (Rehearsal Dates).
15. Enter the show dates (Show Dates).
16. Enter any production notes (Production Notes).
17. Indicate your consent to media permission (Media Permission).
18. Select whether the performer or guardian is giving consent (Performer/Guardian).
19. Finally, click "Submit" to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form**: The main form section, where you input your consent and related information.
* **Media Type**: Select the type of media you wish to record or use (Image, Video, or Audio).
* **Consent**: Indicate your willingness to be recorded, use, and share your performance (Yes, No).
* **Guardian's Name**: Enter the name of the person who is the guardian or parent.
* **Guardian's Email**: Enter the guardian's email address.
* **Guardian's Phone**: Enter the guardian's phone number.
* **Performer's Name**: Enter your name as the performer.
* **Performer's Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Performer's Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **School**: Enter the name of the school where the performance is for.
* **Teacher**: Enter the name of the teacher.
* **Category**: Select which category the performance belongs to (Student, Parent, or Other).
* **Other Details**: Enter any other relevant details about the performance.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the performance (Format: 24h).
* **Time**: Enter the time of the performance (Format: 24h).
* **Rehearsal Dates**: Enter the rehearsal dates.
* **Show Dates**: Enter the show dates.
* **Production Notes**: Enter any production notes.
* **Media Permission**: Indicate your consent to media permission (Yes, No).
* **Performer/Guardian**: Select whether the performer or guardian is giving consent.
* **Form ID**: A unique identifier for the form.
* **Form Version**: The version of the form.
* **Submit**: Click to complete and submit the form.
* **Cancel**: Click to cancel and exit the form.
