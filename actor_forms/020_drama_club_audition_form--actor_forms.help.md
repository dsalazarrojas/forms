# drama_club_audition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "drama_club_audition_form" is a form designed for actors who are interested in auditioning for the drama club. It is meant to gather essential information that will help the drama club's staff review and contact the actors for further consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your age in the "Age" field.
3. Provide your contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Choose your performance experience from the "Performance Experience" field.
6. Select your availability date in the "Availability Date" field.
7. Enter your availability time in the "Availability Time" field.
8. Choose one or more drama types you are interested in (e.g. Drama, Comedy, Musical) in the "Drama Type" field.
9. If you have a conflict with your availability, enter the conflict date and time in the "Conflict Date" and "Conflict Time" fields.
10. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
11. Choose who submitted the form (e.g. chatjimmy, johndoe, janedoe, or other) in the "Submitted By" field.
12. Finally, select the submission date and time in the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Age** (`Age`, number, required): Enter your age in years.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Performance Experience** (`Performance Experience`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ High School Drama
	+ Community Theater
	+ College/University Theater
	+ Professional Theater Company
* **Availability Date** (`Availability Date`, date, required): Select the date(s) when you are available to audition.
* **Availability Time** (`Availability Time`, time, required): Enter the time of day when you are available to audition.
* **Drama Type** (`Drama Type`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Drama
	+ Comedy
	+ Musical
* **Conflict Date** (`Conflict Date`, date, optional): If you have a conflict with your availability, enter the date you are not available.
* **Conflict Time** (`Conflict Time`, time, optional): If you have a conflict with your availability, enter the time you are not available.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your audition.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, select_one, required): Select who submitted the form:
	+ chatjimmy
	+ johndoe
	+ janedoe
	+ other
* **Submission Date** (`Submission Date`, date, required): Select the date you submitted the form.
* **Submission Time** (`Submission Time`, time, required): Select the time you submitted the form.
