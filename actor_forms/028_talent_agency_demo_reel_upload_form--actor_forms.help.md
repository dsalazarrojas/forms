# Talent Agency Demo Reel Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Agency Demo Reel Upload Form is designed to collect necessary information from users for a talent agency to review and consider for potential collaboration or representation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form fields with the required information.
2. Select the correct options for the "Talent Type" field, if applicable.
3. Attach the demo video file as specified in the "Demo Video File" field.
4. Click the "Upload" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Demo Reel** (`demo_reel`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of your demo reel.
* **Talent Inquiry** (`talent_inquiry`, text, not required): Enter any additional information about your inquiry or interest in the talent agency.
* **Casting Director** (`casting_director`, text, not required): Enter the name of the casting director you are working with or would like to work with.
* **Talent Agent** (`talent_agent`, text, not required): Enter your talent agent's name and contact information.
* **Talent Type** (`talent_type`, select multiple, not required): Select all applicable talent types to be considered for representation.
	+ Options: `Yes` or `No`
* **Demo Link** (`demo_link`, text, not required): Enter a link to your demo reel or a YouTube link to a relevant video.
* **Demo Video File** (`demo_file`, text, not required): Attach a video file (.mp4 or .mp3) of your demo reel.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Website** (`website`, text, not required): Enter your website URL.
* **Upload** (`upload_button`, text, not required): Attach your demo reel video file. This field is only accessible after you have attached your video file in the "Demo Video File" field.
* **Upload** (`upload_button`, text, not required): This field seems to be duplicated and will be ignored. Please only use the "Upload" field above to attach your video file.
