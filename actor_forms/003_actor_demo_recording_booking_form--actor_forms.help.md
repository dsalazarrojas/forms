# Actor Demo Recording Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Actor Demo Recording Booking Form is designed to collect necessary information for booking a demo recording session with an actor. This form ensures that the booking details are complete and accurate, and that the client has all the necessary information for the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

* Provide your professional name as required.
* Enter your contact email for booking confirmation and file sharing.
* Choose a preferred date and time for the session.
* Select the type of demo recording you need.
* Provide the session length in minutes.
* Specify how you plan to pay for the session (e.g. credit card, invoice, or cash).
* If you have any additional comments or instructions, use this field to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Actor full name**: This field requires your professional name, which will be used for credits.
	+ Example: John Doe
* **Contact email**: This field is required for booking confirmation and file sharing.
	+ Example: johndoe@example.com
* **Preferred date and time**: Provide two or more options for the session date and time.
	+ Example: 2024-03-12 08:00 or 2024-03-13 10:00
* **Recording type**: Choose the type of demo recording you need.
	+ Options: Voiceover demo, On camera monologue, Scene compilation
* **Session length minutes**: Specify the duration of the session in minutes.
	+ Example: 30, 60, or 90
* **Materials to bring**: If you need to bring any materials (e.g. scripts, wardrobe, props), list them here.
	+ Example: Scripts, costumes, props
* **Special instructions for engineer**: If you have any specific instructions or technical notes, enter them here.
	+ Example: Please use a specific microphone or lighting setup.
* **Payment method**: Choose how you plan to pay for the session.
	+ Options: Credit card, Invoice, Cash on session
* **Additional comments**: Enter any other information that you'd like to share with the engineer.
	+ Example: Please let me know if there are any issues during the session.
