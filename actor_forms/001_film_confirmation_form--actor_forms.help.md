# Film Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Film Confirmation Form is used to confirm details about a film for our records. It helps us gather accurate and up-to-date information about your film.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the First Name, Last Name, and Email fields with your personal details.
2. Choose a category for your film from the Category field (Film, Theater, or Screenplay).
3. Enter a brief description of your work in the Description field.
4. Add a URL to the File URL field if you'd like to share additional resources or documents related to your film.
5. Set a Submission Date for your work.
6. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send us your confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field. 
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field. 
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your film or account. 
* **Phone**: Enter a phone number associated with your film or project. 
* **Category**: Select one of the three options: Film, Theater, or Screenplay. 
* **Title of Work**: Enter a brief title of your work. 
* **Description**: Enter a short description of your film. 
* **File URL**: If you have additional resources or documents related to your film, add a URL here. 
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you're submitting your work for confirmation.
