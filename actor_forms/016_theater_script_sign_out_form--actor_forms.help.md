This form should not be duplicated or used for any other purpose. This form will capture the necessary information for tracking and recording the script sign-out process.

# Theater Script Sign Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theater Script Sign Out Form is used to document the sign out process of a theater or a specific script. This form is designed to collect information from the actor or production staff member signing out, ensuring a smooth and accurate process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the `actor_name` field.
2. Enter your Actor ID number in the `actor_id` field.
3. Enter the script ID number in the `script_id` field.
4. Select a date for the `sign_out_date` field using the date picker.
5. Select a time for the `sign_out_time` field using the time picker.
6. Enter a brief reason for sign out in the `reason_for_sign_out` field.
7. If you have any additional notes, enter them in the `note` field.
8. Enter your name in the `sign_out_by` field.
9. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
10. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Actor Name (`actor_name`)**: Enter your name as it appears on your actor contract.
- **Actor ID (`actor_id`)**: Enter your Actor ID number as per your contract.
- **Script ID (`script_id`)**: Enter the ID number of the script you are signing out from.
- **Sign Out Date (`sign_out_date`)**: Select the date you are signing out using the date picker.
- **Sign Out Time (`sign_out_time`)**: Select the time you are signing out using the time picker.
- **Reason for Sign Out (`reason_for_sign_out`)**: Enter a brief reason for signing out.
- **Note (`note`)**: Enter any additional notes or comments related to your sign out.
- **Sign Out By (`sign_out_by`)**: Enter your name for official sign out documentation.
- **Email (`email`)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone (`phone`)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
