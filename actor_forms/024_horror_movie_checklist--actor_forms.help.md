# Horror-Movie-Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your horror movie watching experience. Fill out the form to track your favorite horror movies, including the title, time of last viewing, and with whom you watched it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the horror movie you want to record.
2. Select whether you have watched the movie.
3. Optionally, enter the date you last watched the movie.
4. Enter the time of last viewing.
5. Enter any notes or comments about your experience.
6. Select who watched the movie with you.
7. Optionally, select whether you've watched the movie again.
8. If you've watched it again, enter the date and time of the re-watch.
9. Select who watched the movie with you again.
10. Review your entries to make sure they are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Movie Title** (`title`): Enter the title of the horror movie you're recording.
  - This field is required to be filled out.
- **Watched** (`watched`): Select whether you have watched the movie or not.
  - Select 'Yes' if you've watched the movie, 'No' otherwise.
- **When** (`watched_when`): Enter the date you last watched the movie.
  - This field is optional.
- **At** (`watched_at`): Enter the time of last viewing.
  - This field is optional.
- **Time** (`watched_at_time`): Enter the time of last viewing in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
  - This field is required.
- **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments about your experience.
  - This field is optional.
- **Watched By** (`watched_by`): Select who watched the movie with you.
  - Select from the options: Me, Family, Friend, Group.
  - This field is optional.
- **Watched With** (`watched_with`): Select with whom you watched the movie.
  - Select from the options: Alone, Friend, Family, Group.
  - This field is optional.
- **Watched Again** (`watched_again`): Select whether you've watched the movie again.
  - Select 'Yes' if you've watched it again, 'No' otherwise.
  - This field is optional.
- **When Again** (`watched_when_again`): Enter the date you last re-watched the movie.
  - This field is optional.
- **At Again** (`watched_at_again`): Enter the time of last re-watch in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
  - This field is optional.
- **Watched By** (`watched_by_again`): Select who watched the movie with you again.
  - Select from the options: Me, Family, Friend, Group.
  - This field is optional.

## Tips
