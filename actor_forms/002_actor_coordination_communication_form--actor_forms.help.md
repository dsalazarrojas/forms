## Actor Coordination Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate communication and coordination between actors, production teams, and coordinators for project execution. It gathers essential information about actors' availability, communication preferences, and production-related needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in the `Production name` field with the actual name of the project or production.
2.  Enter your assigned `Role name` (e.g., character or role assigned to you).
3.  Fill in your `Actor name` (your full professional name).
4.  List your available `Availability windows` (days and times you are available).
5.  Select your preferred `Communication preference` (Email, Phone, Text message, or another option).
6.  If necessary, fill in your `Emergency contact name` and `Emergency contact phone number`.
7.  If applicable, note your `Wardrobe requirements` and `Travel and accommodation needs`.
8.  Add any additional `Notes for production` that you think the production team should be aware of.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Production name** (`production_name`, Text, Required): This field is where you enter the actual name of the project or production. It helps the production team understand the context and relevance of your participation.
*   **Role name** (`role_name`, Text, Required): Enter your assigned role or character in the production. This is crucial for the production team to coordinate your tasks and responsibilities.
*   **Actor name** (`actor_name`, Text, Required): Fill in your full professional name. This is essential for the production team to record and address any correspondence with you properly.
*   **Availability windows** (`availability_windows`, Text, Required): List your available days and times for the production. Be as specific as possible so that the production team can plan accordingly.
*   **Communication preference** (`communication_preference`, Select One, Required): Choose how you prefer to be contacted (Email, Phone, Text message, or other). This ensures the production team contacts you in your preferred method.
*   **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact`, Text, Optional): If necessary, provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact. This is for production team's emergency records.
*   **Wardrobe requirements** (`wardrobe_requirements`, Text, Optional): If you have specific wardrobe needs or requirements, note them here. This helps the production team prepare and accommodate your needs.
*   **Travel and accommodation needs** (`travel_needs`, Select One, Optional): Indicate if you need travel or lodging arrangements. Choose from None, Local commute, or Overnight stay required to help the production team plan accordingly.
*   **Notes for production** (`notes_for_production`, Text, Optional): Add any additional information that you think the production team should be aware of. This could be anything from your schedule conflicts to your specific talents or skills.

## Tips
*   Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to avoid confusion.
*   Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the production team or coordinator for guidance.
