# Musical Audition Self Tape Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a self-tape audition for a musical production. Please fill out the required information to submit your audition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each field with the required information, and click "Submit" at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performer Details**: This field is for general information about yourself as a performer.
* **Performer Name**: Please enter your full name as you would like it to be listed in the production.
* **Performer Email**: Please enter your email address where we can contact you for further information.
* **Self Tape**: Please upload your self-tape video file (max 10MB) that showcases your performance.
* **Material**: Please specify the material you are auditioning for (e.g., musical, play, etc.).
* **Audition Details**: Please provide any additional information about your audition, such as the character you are auditioning for.
* **Role Sought**: Please indicate which role you are auditioning for by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Contact Person**: If you are submitting for a specific contact person, please enter their name here.
* **Contact Email**: If you are submitting for a specific contact person, please enter their email address here.
* **Audition Date**: If you are aware of the audition date, please enter it here.
* **Audition Time**: If you are aware of the audition time, please enter it here.
* **Audition Location**: If you are aware of the audition location, please enter it here.
* **Production Name**: Please enter the name of the production you are auditioning for.
* **Self Tape Link**: If you have a link to your self-tape, please enter it here.
* **Submitter**: This field is for internal use only and is not required to be filled out by the performer.
* **Submitter Note**: This field is for internal use only and is not required to be filled out by the performer.
* **Submitter Email**: This field is for internal use only and is not required to be filled out by the performer.
* **Submitter Phone**: This field is for internal use only and is not required to be filled out by the performer.
* **Submitter Agree**: This field is for the performer to agree to the terms and conditions of the production.
* **Submitter Agree Text**: This field is for the performer to agree to the terms and conditions of the production in writing.
* **Submitter Agree Hint**: This field is for internal use only and is not required to be filled out by the performer.
* **Submitter Agree Note**: This field is for internal use only and is not required to be filled out by the performer.
* **Submitter Agree Date**: This field is for internal use only and is not required to be filled out by the performer.
* **Submitter Agree Time**: This field is for internal use only and is not required to be filled out by the performer.
* **Submitter Agree Location**: This field is for internal use only and is not required to be filled out by the performer.
