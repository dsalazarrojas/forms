# Audition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide the production team with information about your acting experience, skills, and qualifications for a specific audition or casting call.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your Audition Date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
2. Enter the Production Title for which you are auditioning.
3. Fill out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Date of Birth, Height (feet and inches), Eye Color, Hair Color, and Acting Experience Level.
4. Optionally, you can fill out the fields: Ethnicity, Special Skills and Talents, Previous Acting Credits, Acting Training and Education, Willing to Relocate if Needed, Union Membership Status, Headshot and Resume Provided, and Additional Information.
5. Choose your availability for filming in the text field.
6. Finally, select whether or not you have submitted your headshot and resume, and any additional information you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Audition Date (1)**: Date and time of your audition.
	+ Format: YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Production Title (2)**: Name of the film or show auditioning for.
* **First Name (3)**: Your first name.
* **Last Name (4)**: Your last name.
* **Email Address (5)**: Your email contact.
* **Phone Number (6)**: Your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth (8)**: Your birth date.
* **Height (9)**: Your height in feet and inches.
* **Eye Color (11)**: Your natural eye color.
* **Hair Color (12)**: Your natural hair color.
* **Acting Experience Level (16)**: Select your experience level.
* **Availability for Filming (22)**: When you would be available to work.
* **Headshot and Resume Provided (21)**: Whether or not you have submitted your materials.
* **Additional Information (23)**: Any other information you would like to provide.
 
Note: Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
