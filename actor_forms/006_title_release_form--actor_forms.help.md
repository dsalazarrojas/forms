# Title Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Title Release Form" is used for releasing vehicle titles from the seller's name to the buyer's name. This form is typically used in dealership settings for vehicle transfers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety according to the information provided.
2. Make sure all required fields are filled out.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Owner**: This field is where you enter the current owner's name of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle**: Enter the make and model of the vehicle being transferred.
* **Date of Transfer**: Enter the date the vehicle was transferred to the new owner.
* **Transfer Amount**: Enter the amount of money involved in the transfer.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment used for the transfer (cash, check, bank transfer).
* **Payment Terms**: Enter any additional terms of the payment.
* **Witness**: Enter the name of a witness who can attest to the transfer.
* **Date of Beneficial Interest**: Enter the date the buyer had a beneficial interest in the vehicle.
* **Date of Sale**: Enter the date the vehicle was sold.
* **Seller**: Enter the name of the seller of the vehicle.
* **Buyer**: Enter the name of the buyer of the vehicle.
* **Witness Signature**: This field is for the witness to sign and date the form.
* **Notary Signature**: This field is for a notary to sign and date the form.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g. Toyota, Ford, etc.).
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle (e.g. Camry, Mustang, etc.).
* **Engine Number**: Enter the engine number of the vehicle (if applicable).

Note: Some fields may be required or have specific requirements depending on the context and jurisdiction. Be sure to review and follow any relevant laws and regulations.
