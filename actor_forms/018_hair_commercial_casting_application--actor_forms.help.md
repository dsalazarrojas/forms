# Hair Commercial Casting Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information from actors for commercial casting purposes. Please use this form to submit your application for a hair commercial casting opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with the requested information. Start by entering your name, email, and phone number. Then, provide a brief description of your experience in the relevant field, including the number of years you have worked. Finally, select your availability for the casting opportunity and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your experience in the relevant field, including the number of years you have worked.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `time`, required/optional): Select the date and time range you are available for the casting opportunity.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required/optional): Click to submit your application for review.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
