# Actor Coaching Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Actor Coaching Session Booking Form is designed to help actors book a coaching session that suits their needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), such as your full name, email address, and session type.
2. Provide your preferred coach from the dropdown menu, if you have a specific preference.
3. Enter your preferred dates for the session, providing 2-3 options.
4. Briefly describe what you want to achieve in the session and any specific goals or focus areas.
5. Select your acting experience level.
6. If necessary, provide any special requirements or accessibility needs.
7. Add any additional notes or information for the coach.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full name**: * (Required): Enter your legal or professional name.
- **Email address**: * (Required): Enter your email address for session confirmations and materials.
- **Phone number**: (Optional): Enter your phone number for scheduling updates.
- **Preferred coach**: (Optional): Select the coach you prefer from the dropdown menu.
- **Session type**: * (Required): Choose the session format you prefer.
	+ One on one: A private session with one coach.
	+ Group workshop: A group session with multiple coaches.
	+ Online coaching: A session conducted online.
- **Preferred dates**: * (Required): Enter 2-3 date options for the session.
- **Goals and focus areas**: * (Required): Briefly describe what you want to achieve in the session.
- **Acting experience level**: * (Required): Select your acting experience level from the dropdown menu.
	+ Beginner: Little or no experience.
	+ Intermediate: Some experience with occasional performances.
	+ Professional: Significant experience with regular performances.
- **Special requirements**: (Optional): Enter any special requirements or accessibility needs, such as mobility issues.
- **Additional notes**: (Optional): Add any other information for the coach.

Remember to review your entries carefully before submitting the form to ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
