# theater_audition_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record the evaluation of an actor's performance in a theatrical audition. The form collects information on the actor, the audition date and time, the role performed, and the director's opinion on their performance. It's used to provide an evaluation and feedback to the actor after the audition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the name of the actor being evaluated.
3. Provide the date and time of the audition.
4. Select the role performed during the audition.
5. Describe the scene(s) performed (e.g., monologue, scene from a play).
6. Indicate whether or not the director was impressed by the performance (Yes or No).
7. Enter any comments or feedback you may have on the actor's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Actor**: Enter the name of the person being auditioned.
* **Audition Date**: Enter the date of the audition in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the audition in the format `HH:MM:SS` (24-hour format).
* **Role**: Enter the role or part the actor was auditioning for.
* **Scene**: Describe the scene or monologue performed by the actor.
* **Performance**: Indicate whether or not the director was impressed by the actor's performance. Select "Yes" if the performance was impressive, "No" otherwise.
* **Director**: Enter the name of the director who evaluated the performance.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional feedback or comments on the actor's performance.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use clear and concise language when entering comments or feedback.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, refer back to the form's purpose and instructions above.
