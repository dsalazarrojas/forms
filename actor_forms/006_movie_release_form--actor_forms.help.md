# Movie Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Movie Release Form is a document used to record the release details of a movie, including the title, date of birth, occupation, contact information, release date, production company, production manager, and other relevant details. This form ensures that all necessary information is accurately captured and documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the movie title.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Select your occupation.
4. Provide your contact email and phone number (if applicable).
5. Enter the release date.
6. Enter your production company information (name and manager, if applicable).
7. Sign the release form by providing your signature and acknowledgment.
8. Date and time of signing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Movie Title** (1): Enter the title of the movie being released.
* **Date of Birth** (2): Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Occupation** (3): Select your occupation from the available options.
* **Email** (4): Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Phone** (5): Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Release Date** (6): Enter the date the movie is being released.
* **Production Company** (7): Enter your production company's name.
* **Production Manager** (8): Enter the name of your production manager.
* **Sign** (9): Provide your signature to confirm the information provided.
* **Acknowledgment** (10): Sign and date the acknowledgment to confirm you've read and understood the form.
* **Date** (11): The date and time of signing.
* **Company Name** (12): Enter your production company's name.

## Tips
- Please ensure you complete all required fields.
- Double-check your information before signing the form.
- Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
