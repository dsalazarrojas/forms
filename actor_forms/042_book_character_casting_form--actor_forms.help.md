# Book Character Casting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from actors who are interested in auditioning for book character roles. It gathers essential details such as actor name, contact email, role interest, age range, headshot link, availability, and experience notes. The form is intended for book character casting directors to review and consider potential actors for their roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your **Actor name** in the "Actor name" field. This is your stage or legal name.
2. Provide your **Contact email** in the "Contact email" field. This email address will be used for casting notifications.
3. Indicate the **Role interested in** by entering the character name or description in the "Role interested in" field.
4. If applicable, enter your **Age range** in the "Age range" field. This is optional but helps casting directors filter and match you with roles more suited to your appearance.
5. If you have a **Headshot link**, please provide it in the "Headshot link" field. This can be a URL to a portfolio or website showcasing your work.
6. Specify your **Availability** for rehearsal and recording dates and times in the "Availability" field.
7. If you have relevant **Experience notes**, enter them in the "Experience notes" field. This can include past roles or acting training.
8. Finally, indicate your **Audition location preference** in the "Audition location preference" field:
	* Choose "In person" if you're available to attend in-person auditions.
	* Choose "Virtual" if you're available to audition via video or online platforms.
	* Choose "Either" if you're open to both in-person and virtual auditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Actor name** (Actor name, text, required): Enter your stage or legal name.
* **Contact email** (Contact email, email, required): Provide an email address for casting notifications.
* **Role interested in** (Role interested in, text, required): Specify the character name or description you're interested in auditioning for.
* **Age range** (Age range, text, optional): Optional field to indicate your age or appearance range.
* **Headshot link** (Headshot link, text, required): Provide a URL to a portfolio or website showcasing your work.
* **Availability** (Availability, text, required): Specify your availability for rehearsal and recording dates and times.
* **Experience notes** (Experience notes, text, optional): Enter any past roles or acting training that might be beneficial for casting directors.
* **Audition location preference** (Audition location preference, select_one, required): Choose your preferred audition location:
	+ In person
	+ Virtual
	+ Either
