**Actor Status Survey - Help Guide**

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from actors about their current status, 
interests, and training needs. The data collected will help casting directors, 
agents, and other industry professionals better understand the actor's qualifications 
and find suitable opportunities for them.

**How To Complete This Form**

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the basic information at the top of the form.
2. Answer each question thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Select the correct options for each field.
4. Review and submit the form once you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it should be used for professional purposes.
* **Union membership** (`Union membership`, select_one, true): Select your current union membership status. Options are: None, SAG AFTRA, Equity, Other.
* **Current availability** (`Current availability`, text, true): Provide an open date and notice period, if any, where you are currently available for work. Be specific and include any restrictions or limitations.
* **Recent roles and credits** (`Recent roles and credits`, text, false): Briefly summarize your recent work and credits, including films, television shows, commercials, and theater productions.
* **Training and education** (`Training and education`, text, false): List any relevant workshops, degrees, or certifications you have completed.
* **Representation status** (`Representation status`, text, false): If you have an agent or manager, provide their contact information. If not, select "None".
* **Interest in audition calls and projects** (`Interest in audition calls and projects`, select_multiple, false): Select all the types of projects you are interested in, such as commercials, film, television, theater, or voiceover.
* **Areas where you seek coaching** (`Areas where you seek coaching`, text, false): Specify the acting techniques or skills you need improvement, such as on-camera coaching or voice training.
* **Additional comments** (`Additional comments`, note, false): Provide any other relevant information for casting directors or agents, such as your experience, skills, or availability.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Double-check your spelling and grammar for errors.
* Use clear and concise language in your answers.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.
