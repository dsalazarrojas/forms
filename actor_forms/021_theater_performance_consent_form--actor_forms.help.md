# Theater Performance Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theater Performance Consent Form is a document intended to collect necessary information from participants and their guardians about their involvement in a theater performance. This information helps us understand who will be participating and ensures we meet any necessary requirements for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's name in the "participant_name" field.
2. Enter the guardian's name in the "guardian_name" field.
3. Select the relationship between the participant and the guardian in the "guardian_relationship" field.
4. Enter the participant's age in the "participant_age" field.
5. Select the guardian's consent in the "guardian_consent" field.
6. Select the participant's consent in the "participant_consent" field.
7. Enter any additional details about the performance in the "performance_details" field.
8. (Optional) Enter any additional contact information for the participant in the "contact_information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Name
• **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant who will be attending the theater performance. This is an optional field.

### Guardian Name
• **Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the guardian or contact person for the participant. This is an optional field.

### Guardian Relationship
• **Guardian Relationship**: Select the relationship between the participant and the guardian. Options are:
	+ Parent
	+ Guardian
	+ Other

### Participant Age
• **Participant Age**: Enter the age of the participant. This is a required field.

### Guardian Consent
• **Guardian Consent**: Select the guardian's level of consent for the participant's involvement in the performance. Options are:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'

### Participant Consent
• **Participant Consent**: Select the participant's level of consent for their involvement in the performance. Options are:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'

### Performance Details
• **Performance Details**: Enter any additional details about the performance, such as the performance type, date, or time.

### Contact Information
• **Contact Information**: Enter any additional contact information for the participant, such as their address or phone number. This is an optional field.

This guide will assist users in understanding the form's purpose and how to fill it out correctly, ensuring they can submit the required information efficiently.
