## Form Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals apply to become voice acting talent at a talent agency. The form gathers information about the applicant's experience, contact details, and voice type to assess their suitability for voice acting work. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to increase your chances of being considered for voice acting opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing a clear and concise answer to the question on the "Main Page" section.
2.  Move on to the "Experience" section, where you should describe your past experience in voice acting, including any relevant training or certifications.
3.  On the "Contact" page, enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
4.  In the "Voice Type" section, select the type of voice you have, such as a narrator, news anchor, or character voice.
5.  Finally, submit your application by clicking the "Submit" button on the "Submission" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Main Page** (`title`, `text`, not required): This is the first section of the form where you can briefly introduce yourself. Please provide your name, a short bio, or a statement about your interest in voice acting.
*   **Experience** (`experience_page`, `text`, not required): This section is where you should describe your past experience in voice acting, including any relevant training or certifications. Be specific about your roles, productions, or companies you've worked with.
*   **Contact** (`contact_page`, `text`, not required): Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number. This is how the agency will reach out to you for further discussion or to schedule auditions.
*   **Voice Type** (`voice_type`, `text`, not required): Select the type of voice you have, such as a narrator, news anchor, or character voice. This will help the agency understand your range and suitability for different types of voice acting work.
*   **Submission** (`submission_page`, `text`, not required): This is the final section where you submit your application. Make sure to review your answers before submitting to ensure they accurately reflect your skills and experience.
