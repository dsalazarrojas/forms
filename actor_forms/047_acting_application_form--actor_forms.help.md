# Acting Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Acting Application Form is a tool used by actors to submit their applications for auditions, casting calls, or other acting opportunities. This form helps casting directors, agents, or other stakeholders quickly and easily gather relevant information about the actor, making it easier to consider them for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about yourself ready, including your full name, email address, phone number, and any other details requested.
2. Fill in your Full Name, which should match the name you would like to be known by in the industry.
3. Enter your Email Address, which will be used for communication and updates about casting calls and auditions.
4. Provide your Phone Number, which will be used for callbacks, interviews, or other communication.
5. Select your Gender from the provided options to indicate your identity.
6. Enter your Age, which should be your actual age in years.
7. If you're comfortable sharing, enter your Height in Feet and Inches, which will help casting directors envision how you might fit into the role.
8. If you're comfortable sharing, enter your Weight in LBS, which can be useful for fitting into costumes or roles with specific physical requirements.
9. Select your Hair Color and Eye Color from the provided options, as they can be relevant to character choices or role requirements.
10. If you have special skills, such as singing, dancing, or accents, list them here to showcase your versatility.
11. Select your Union Status to indicate whether you're a member of SAG-AFTRA, Equity, or non-union.
12. Briefly describe your Acting Experience Summary, highlighting your background and accomplishments.
13. For the Availability section, indicate when you're available to work, selecting all days of the week and any evening availability.
14. If you have agency representation, list them here.
15. Finally, review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name, as you would like to be known in the industry.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for callbacks, interviews, or other communication.
* **Gender**: Select your identity as Male, Female, Non-binary, or Other.
* **Age**: Enter your actual age in years.
* **Height in Feet and Inches**: Enter your height in feet and inches if you're comfortable sharing (e.g., 5'10").
* **Weight in LBS**: Enter your weight in pounds if you're comfortable sharing (e.g., 150 lbs).
* **Hair Color**: Select all applicable hair colors you have (e.g., Black, Brown, Blonde, Red, Grey).
* **Eye Color**: Select all applicable eye colors you have (e.g., Brown, Blue, Green, Hazel, Grey).
* **Special Skills**: List any special skills you have, such as singing, dancing, or accents.
* **Union Status**: Select your union status as SAG-AFTRA, Equity, or non-union.
* **Acting Experience Summary**: Briefly describe your background and accomplishments.
* **Availability**: Select all days of the week you're available to work and any evening availability.
* **Agency Representation**: If you have agency representation, list them here.
* **Agency Representation (if any)**: Enter any agency information if you have representation.
* **Availability Header**: This is a placeholder header for the availability section.
* **Availability Days**: Select all days you're available to work.
* **Evenings**: Select if you're available to work in the evenings.
* **On-call**: Select if you're available for on-call shifts.
