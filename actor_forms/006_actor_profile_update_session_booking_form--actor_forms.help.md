# Actor Profile Update Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Actor Profile Update Session Booking Form is used to gather information from an actor for an upcoming photo shoot or session. This form is used to schedule and coordinate the session details with the actor and ensure that all necessary information is captured for a smooth and successful session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the actor's name and email address, which is required for communication and delivery of session materials.
2. Select the purpose of the session from the available options (Headshot session, Reel update, Bio and resume update, or Other).
3. Enter the preferred dates and times for the session. You can provide multiple options.
4. If desired, provide brief descriptions of the wardrobe choices you would like to bring to the session.
5. Specify the length of the session in minutes (e.g., 30, 60, or 90 minutes).
6. Optionally, select any additional services you would like to use during the session (Hair styling, Makeup, Photo retouching, or Video editing).
7. If you have any mobility or sensory accommodations that you need, please describe them here.
8. Lastly, provide any special notes or creative direction you would like to share with the photographer or coach, such as specific lighting or poses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Actor name** (`actor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full professional name of the actor.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address that will be used for communication and delivery of session materials.
* **Session purpose** (`session_purpose`, `select_one`, required): Select the purpose of the session from the available options (Headshot session, Reel update, Bio and resume update, or Other).
* **Preferred dates and times** (`preferred_dates`, `text`, required): Enter the preferred dates and times for the session.
* **Wardrobe choices** (`wardrobe_choices`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the wardrobe choices you would like to bring to the session.
* **Length of session** (`length_of_session`, `number`, required): Specify the length of the session in minutes (e.g., 30, 60, or 90 minutes).
* **Additional services** (`additional_services`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional services you would like to use during the session (Hair styling, Makeup, Photo retouching, or Video editing).
* **Accessibility needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): If you have any mobility or sensory accommodations, please describe them here.
* **Special notes** (`special_notes`, `note`, optional): Provide any special notes or creative direction you would like to share with the photographer or coach.
