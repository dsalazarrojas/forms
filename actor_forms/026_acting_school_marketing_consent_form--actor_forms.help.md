# Acting School Marketing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Acting School Marketing Consent Form is a document that allows acting school students to provide their consent for the use of their photographs and videos for marketing purposes. This form helps the school to promote students' work and image while ensuring they respect students' rights and boundaries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as a student.
2. Provide your unique student ID number.
3. Enter your contact email address for communication.
4. Enter your contact phone number for communication.
5. Select the acting program you are enrolled in.
6. Check the boxes for the consents you are willing to give for marketing purposes:
	* Consent to Use of Photographs and Videos
	* Consent to Social Media Promotion
	* Consent to Website Display
	* Consent to Print Materials
7. If you consent, select all permitted uses for your image/work.
8. Specify any restrictions or specifications for the use of your image/work.
9. Choose the duration of your consent for marketing purposes.
10. Confirm that you understand you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as a student.
* **Student ID**: Enter your unique student ID number.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Program Enrolled In**: Select the acting program you are enrolled in.
* **Consent to Use of Photographs and Videos**: Check if you consent to the use of your photographs and videos for marketing purposes.
* **Consent to Social Media Promotion**: Check if you consent to your image/work being featured on social media platforms.
* **Consent to Website Display**: Check if you consent to your image/work being displayed on the school website.
* **Consent to Print Materials**: Check if you consent to your image/work being used in brochures, catalogs, and other print materials.
* **Permitted Uses**: Select all permitted uses for your image/work:
	+ Promotional materials
	+ Website
	+ Social media
	+ Newsletters
	+ Student showcases
	+ Alumni publications
	+ Advertising
* **Any Restrictions or Specifications**: Specify any restrictions or specifications for the use of your image/work.
* **Duration of Consent**: Choose the duration of your consent for marketing purposes:
	+ Duration of studies
	+ 1 year
	+ 2 years
	+ 5 years
	+ Perpetual
	+ Until withdrawn
* **Rights to Withdraw Consent**: Confirm that you understand you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.
