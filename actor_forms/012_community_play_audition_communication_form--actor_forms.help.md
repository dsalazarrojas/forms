# Community Play Audition Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from actors who want to register for a community theater play audition. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure you're considered for the role(s) you're interested in.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information, including your name, age group, email, phone number, and the roles you're interested in. 
2. Indicate whether you'd accept any role in the production. 
3. Provide a brief summary of your previous acting experience.
4. Select your preferred audition time slot. 
5. If you have any known conflicts or unavailability for rehearsals, list them.
6. Provide your height information for costuming purposes. 
7. Confirm if you have a physical headshot and resume to bring to the audition. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Community Theater Audition Registration --**: This is a note to introduce the form and provide context.
* **Actor Full Name**: Enter your full name, as it will appear in the play's cast list. 
* **Age Group**: Select your age group: Child (under 13), Teen (13-17), Adult (18-64), or Senior (65+). This helps us plan rehearsals and accommodations. 
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address so we can contact you about the audition and any upcoming rehearsals. 
* **Phone Number**: Provide your phone number, and we'll use it to contact you with audition details and updates. 
* **Specific Role(s) You are Auditioning For**: Enter the role(s) you'd like to audition for. If you're not familiar with the play, please ask for the role descriptions. 
* **Would you accept any role in the production?**: Choose 'True' if you're willing to take on any role, 'False' otherwise. This helps us plan rehearsals and casting decisions. 
* **Brief Summary of Previous Acting Experience**: If you have experience, provide a brief description of your background. This will help us understand your skills and suitability for the roles. 
* **Special Skills**: Select any applicable skills you have, such as singing, dancing, or playing an instrument. 
* **Other Special Skills - please specify**: If you have any other skills not listed, describe them briefly. 
* **Preferred Audition Time Slot**: Choose your preferred time slot for the audition. All slots are available on Saturday, 10th of October. 
* **Rehearsal Conflicts**: If you have any known conflicts or unavailability for rehearsals, list them here. 
* **Height**: Provide your height for costuming purposes. 
* **Do you have a physical headshot and resume to bring?**: Confirm if you have a physical headshot and resume to bring to the audition. 
* **-- Audition Requirements --**: This is a note to remind you to prepare a 1-minute contemporary monologue, 16 bars of a showtune for singers, and other specific requirements. 
* **Today's Date**: Enter the current date to complete the form accurately. 

Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure your registration is valid.
