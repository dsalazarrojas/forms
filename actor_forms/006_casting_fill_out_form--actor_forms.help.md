# Casting Fill Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Casting Fill Out Form is designed to collect information from applicants for casting and auditioning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your resume.
2. Provide your email address for audition notifications.
3. Enter your mobile phone number (optional).
4. Describe the role or character you are applying for.
5. Provide a link to your online portfolio or headshot.
6. Enter your height in centimeters (numeric value).
7. List your relevant skills and training (e.g., dance, dialects, stunts, musical instruments).
8. Indicate your availability for rehearsals and shoots (in a format similar to "Monday to Thursday, 10am-6pm").
9. If represented by an agent, provide their contact details (agency name and phone number).
10. Confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your resume.
* **Contact email**: Enter your email address for audition notifications.
* **Contact phone**: Enter your mobile phone number (optional).
* **Role applied for**: Describe the role or character you are applying for.
* **Headshot or portfolio link**: Provide a link to your online portfolio or headshot.
* **Height in cm**: Enter your height in centimeters as a numeric value.
* **Skills and training**: List your relevant skills and training (e.g., dance, dialects, stunts, musical instruments).
* **Availability windows**: Indicate your availability for rehearsals and shoots.
* **Agent contact details**: If represented by an agent, provide their agency name and phone number.
* **Declarations and consent**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
