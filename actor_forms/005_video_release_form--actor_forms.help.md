# Video Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for the release of video recordings. It is a necessary step in the process for users to provide their information for video releases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
5. Enter your City in the "City" field.
6. Enter your State in the "State" field.
7. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Describe the purpose of the video release in the "Purpose" field.
9. If applicable, enter the date you signed the form in the "Date Signed" field.
10. If applicable, enter the time you signed the form in the "Time Signed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for video release purposes.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city name.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state name.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, optional): Describe the purpose of the video release.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Enter the date you signed the form, if applicable.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, optional): Enter the time you signed the form, if applicable.
