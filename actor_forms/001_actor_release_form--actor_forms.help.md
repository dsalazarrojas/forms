# Actor Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Actor Release Form is used to document the release of an actor's or performer's rights to a production or project. This form ensures that the production company has the necessary rights to use the talent's work, while also providing the talent with a clear understanding of their obligations and compensation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the full name of the talent or performer.
2. Provide their address, including city, state, and zip code.
3. Enter the title of the production or project.
4. Enter the name of the production company.
5. Briefly describe the role or performance in the production.
6. Enter the total compensation or consideration received by the talent.
7. Enter the date when the performance took place.
8. Specify the location of the performance.
9. Choose the media formats to which the talent grants rights.
10. Indicate whether the talent grants perpetual rights to the footage.
11. Sign and date the form to confirm the agreement.
12. Sign the form with your legal name as the talent.
13. If a witness is present, sign and date the form as the witness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Talent**: Enter the legal name of the actor or performer. This field is required.
* **Address**: Enter the current residential address of the talent. This field is optional.
* **City State Zip Code**: Enter the city, state, and zip code of the talent's address. This field is optional.
* **Title of Production**: Enter the name of the film, TV show, commercial, or project. This field is required.
* **Production Company Name**: Enter the legal name of the production company. This field is required.
* **Description of Work Performed**: Briefly describe the role or performance in the production. This field is required.
* **Compensation Amount**: Enter the total payment or consideration received by the talent. This field is optional.
* **Date of Work**: Enter the date when the performance took place. This field is required.
* **Location of Work**: Enter the location where the performance took place. This field is optional.
* **Media Rights Granted**: Choose all media formats to which the talent grants rights. This field is required.
* **Grant Perpetual Rights**: Indicate whether the talent grants perpetual rights to the footage. This field is required.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date when this agreement is signed. This field is required.
* **Talent Signature**: Sign with your legal name as the talent. This field is required.
* **Witness Signature**: If a witness is present, sign with your legal name as the witness. This field is optional.
* **Witness Name**: If a witness is present, enter their printed name. This field is optional.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before signing to ensure you understand the terms of your agreement.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the production company or your representative.
* Ensure that the witness signs and dates the form if they are present.
