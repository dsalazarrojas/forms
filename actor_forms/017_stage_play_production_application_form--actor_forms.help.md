# stage-play-production-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect application information from actors, dancers, and singers for a stage play production.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Actor Information in the "Actor Information" field.
2. Provide details about the play you are applying for in the "Play Information" field.
3. Specify your availability for the production in the "Availability" field.
4. Select your relevant skills from the "Skills" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Add your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Choose your preferred role in the "Role Preference" field.
8. Upload your resume in the "Resume" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Actor Information
* **Actor Information** (`actor_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description about yourself as an actor.

### Play Information
* **Play Information** (`play_info`, text, optional): Provide a description of the play you are applying for, including the title and director's name if available.

### Availability
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, optional): Specify your availability for the production, including dates and times when you are available to work.

### Skills
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Actor: You are an actor, and you have experience in acting roles.
	+ Dancer: You are a dancer, and you have experience in dancing roles.
	+ Singer: You are a singer, and you have experience in singing roles.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.

### Role Preference
* **Role Preference** (`role_preference`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Lead Role: You prefer to be cast in a lead role.
	+ Supporting Role: You prefer to be cast in a supporting role.
	+ Ensemble Role: You prefer to be cast in an ensemble role.

### Resume
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Upload your resume for us to review.
