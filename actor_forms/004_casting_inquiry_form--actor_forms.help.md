# Casting Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in casting inquiries for various productions. It helps us understand your qualifications, interests, and availability to determine the suitability for potential roles.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have a clear understanding of your qualifications and availability before filling out the form.
2. If you are inquiring about a specific role, please provide detailed information about it.
3. Fill out the form accurately and completely, making sure to provide all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Full Name): Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (contact_email): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Acting Age Range** (acting_age_range): Select the age range you can realistically portray in your acting roles.
* **Type of Production Interest** (interest_type): Select all types of productions you are interested in (e.g., Feature Film, Short Film, Television Series, Commercial, Voice Over, Theater).
* **Are you inquiring about a specific role?** (specific_role_query): Answer "Yes" if you are inquiring about a specific role, and provide detailed information in the **Specific Role Name** field.
* **Specific Role Name** (role_name): If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, provide the name of the specific role you are inquiring about.
* **Portfolio or Website Link** (portfolio_link): Share a link to your professional portfolio or website to showcase your work.
* **Instagram or Professional Social Handle** (social_media_handle): Share your social media handle if you have one.
* **General Availability** (work_availability): Describe your schedule for the next 3 months, including days and time slots you are available.
* **Willingness to Travel** (travel_willingness): Select where you are willing to travel for productions (Locally, Nationally, Internationally).
* **Any additional questions or information** (additional_comments): Provide any further questions or information you think we should know.
* **How did you find out about our agency?** (reference_source): Optional field to share how you found out about our agency (Casting Site, Social Media, Referral, Industry Event).
