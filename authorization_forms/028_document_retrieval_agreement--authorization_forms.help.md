# Document Retrieval Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Document Retrieval Agreement form is used to obtain an agreement from end-users for document retrieval. It is essential to review the form carefully before completing it to ensure that you understand the purpose and requirements of the form.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and ensure you understand all the fields and requirements.
- Fill in the required fields accurately and truthfully.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the relevant authorities or seek guidance from the form administrator.
- Ensure that all the fields are filled correctly, as incorrect information may lead to delays or additional procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Organization Name
• (organization_name, text, required/optional): 
Enter the name of the organization requesting document retrieval.

### Document Type
• (document_type, select_one, required): 
Choose the type of document being requested. This could be Type A, Type B, or Type C.

### Document ID
• (document_id, text, required): 
Enter the ID number of the document to be retrieved.

### Date Submitted
• (date_submitted, date, required): 
Enter the date the document was submitted.

### Submitted By
• (submitted_by, text, required): 
Enter your name as the person submitting the document request.

### Request Reason (optional)
• (request_reason, text, optional): 
If applicable, enter the reason for requesting the document.

### Organization Contact
• (organization_contact, text, optional): 
Enter the contact person's name, phone number, and email for the organization.

### Contact Email
• (contact_email, email, required): 
Enter the email address of the contact person for the organization.

### Contact Phone
• (contact_phone, text, optional): 
Enter the phone number of the contact person for the organization.

### Document Retained
• (document_retained, note, optional): 
Enter any additional information about the document to be retained.

### Document Return
• (document_return, select_one, required): 
Choose whether the document will be returned to the sender or not.

### Notes (optional)
• (notes, note, optional): 
Enter any additional comments or notes about the document retrieval request.

### Document Retrieval Fee
• (document_retrieval_fee, number, optional): 
Enter any additional fees associated with the document retrieval process, if applicable.

### Agreement Signed
• (agreement_signed, select_one, required): 
Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the document retrieval process.
Note: This field should be marked as 'True' after reading and agreeing to the terms and conditions.

### Tips
- Double-check all the fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed correctly.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact the form administrator or relevant authorities.
- Review the form carefully to avoid any potential issues or delays.
