# Health Insurance Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to release your health insurance records to a specific healthcare provider or insurance company. You will need to provide identifying information, such as your name, date of birth, and address, as well as specify the reason for releasing your records and the date and time of this authorization.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2.  Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3.  If applicable, enter the name of the healthcare provider or insurance company you wish to release your records to.
4.  Check if you have any healthcare records to release ("Yes" or "No").
5.  If "Yes" is selected for the above field, enter the ID number of your current insurance provider.
6.  Briefly describe your reason for releasing your records.
7.  Select the type of authorization you wish to grant, such as HIPAA, State Law, or Court Order.
8.  Enter the date this authorization expires, if applicable.
9.  Sign and date this form to confirm your release of records.
10. Enter your email address.
11. Enter your phone number.
12. Enter the phone number of the healthcare provider or insurance company.
13. Enter the email address of the healthcare provider or insurance company.
14. Enter the address of the insurance company.
15. Sign this form to confirm the release of your records by the healthcare provider or insurance company.
16. Select the organizations you wish to release your records to, from a list of options.
17. Include any additional notes or comments, if necessary.
18. Sign this form to confirm your release of records.
19. Enter the date and time this form was filled out.
20. Enter the address of the healthcare provider.
21. Enter your home or work address.
22. Enter the fax number of the healthcare provider.
23. Select your state of residence.
24. Enter the license number of the healthcare provider, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Healthcare Provider Name**: If you are releasing records to a specific healthcare provider, enter their name here.
* **Have Healthcare Records to Release**: Check if you have healthcare records to release.
* **Insurance Provider ID**: If you have healthcare records to release, enter the ID number of your current insurance provider.
* **Reason for Release**: Briefly describe your reason for releasing your records.
* **Authorization Type**: Select the type of authorization you wish to grant, such as HIPAA or State Law.
* **Authorization Expiration Date**: If applicable, enter the date this authorization expires.
* **Signature**: Sign and date this form to confirm your release of records.
* **Patient Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Healthcare Provider Phone**: Enter the phone number of the healthcare provider or insurance company.
* **Healthcare Provider Email**: Enter the email address of the healthcare provider or insurance company.
* **Insurance Company Address**: Enter the address of the insurance company.
* **Insurance Provider Signature**: Sign this form to confirm the release of your records by the healthcare provider or insurance company.
* **Release To**: Select the organizations you wish to release your records to.
* **Notes**: Include any additional notes or comments, if necessary.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign this form to confirm your release of records.
* **Date**: Enter the date and time this form was filled out.
* **Healthcare Provider Address**: Enter the address of the healthcare provider.
* **Patient Address**: Enter your home or work address.
* **Healthcare Provider Fax**: Enter the fax number of the healthcare provider.
* **State**: Select your state of residence.
* **Healthcare Provider License Number**: Enter the license number of the healthcare provider, if applicable.
