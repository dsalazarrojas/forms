# Collaborative Practice Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document collaborative practice agreements between healthcare providers. It captures essential details about the agreement, including provider names, scope of practice, and termination conditions. This form ensures that all parties involved are aware of their responsibilities and obligations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Provider name** field with the name of the practitioner providing services.
2. Enter the **Supervising provider** name in the second field, if applicable.
3. Select the **Effective date** in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
4. Describe the **Scope of practice** services covered under the agreement.
5. Summarize the **Responsibilities of parties** in a few sentences.
6. Optionally, add **Termination conditions** if applicable.
7. List the **Signatures required** for the agreement.
8. Enter your **Contact email** for future correspondence.
9. Add any **Additional terms** if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Provider name**: The name of the practitioner providing services.
* **Supervising provider**: The name of the supervising practitioner, if applicable.
* **Effective date**: The date the agreement takes effect, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Scope of practice**: A description of the services covered under the agreement.
* **Responsibilities of parties**: A brief summary of the key responsibilities of each party involved.
* **Termination conditions**: Conditions for terminating the agreement, if applicable.
* **Signatures required**: A list of parties required to sign the agreement.
* **Contact email**: Email address for future correspondence and updates.
* **Additional terms**: Any special clauses or conditions not mentioned in the previous fields.

Note: This form is intended to be a general guide and may require adaptation or modifications to suit specific use cases or regulatory requirements.
