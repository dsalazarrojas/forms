# Joint Assignment Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to facilitate the assignment of tasks between parties, ensuring that all individuals involved are aware of their responsibilities and obligations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Assignor**: Type the name of the individual or group assigning the task.
2. **Assignee**: Type the name of the individual or group accepting the assignment.
3. **Effective Date**: Enter the date on which the assignment becomes effective.
4. **Start Date**: Enter the date on which the assignment is set to start.
5. **End Date**: Enter the date on which the assignment is set to end.
6. **Task**: Briefly describe the task being assigned.
7. **Rights**: Provide any additional information about the rights or permissions granted to the assignee.
8. **Signature Date**: This field should be filled in by the assignor, indicating when their signature was made.
9. **Assignor Signature**: This field should be filled in by the assignor, indicating their approval of the assignment.
10. **Assignee Signature**: This field should be filled in by the assignee, indicating their understanding and acceptance of the assignment.
11. **Consent**: Check this box to confirm that the assignment has been acknowledged and accepted by the assignee.
12. **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or information relevant to the assignment.
13. **Assigned Tool**: If necessary, specify which tool or resource is being assigned.
14. **Assigned User**: Select the user(s) to whom the task is assigned.
