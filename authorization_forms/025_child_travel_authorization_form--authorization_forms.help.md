# Child Travel Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Travel Authorization Form is used to collect information about the child, the accompanying adult, and travel details. This form ensures that necessary permissions are obtained from the child's parent or guardian before allowing travel.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  The first section of the form asks for the child's information, including their first name, last name, date of birth, and any medical conditions or allergies.
2.  The second section of the form asks for the parent or guardian's information, including their name, address, phone number, and email.
3.  The third section of the form asks for the accompanying adult's information, including their name, phone number, email, and relationship to the child.
4.  The fourth section of the form asks for travel details, including the destination city, state or country, departure date, return date, and travel purpose.
5.  The last section of the form asks for additional information, including the travel method, medical insurance provider, and emergency contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Authorization Date**: The date on which travel authorization was given.
*   **Parent or Guardian First Name**: Legal first name of parent or guardian authorizing travel.
*   **Parent or Guardian Last Name**: Legal last name of parent or guardian authorizing travel.
*   **Parent or Guardian Email**: Primary email address for contact.
*   **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Primary phone number for contact.
*   **Parent or Guardian Address**: Full street address.
*   **Parent or Guardian ID Type**: Type of identification provided.
*   **Parent or Guardian ID Number**: Identification number.
*   **Child First Name**: Legal first name of the child.
*   **Child Last Name**: Legal last name of the child.
*   **Child Date of Birth**: The child's birth date.
*   **Child Passport Number**: Passport number if traveling internationally.
*   **Child Passport Expiry Date**: Passport expiration date if applicable.
*   **Accompanying Adult First Name**: Legal first name of adult supervising travel.
*   **Accompanying Adult Last Name**: Legal last name of adult supervising travel.
*   **Accompanying Adult Relationship**: Relationship of accompanying adult to the child.
*   **Accompanying Adult Phone**: Phone number for accompanying adult.
*   **Accompanying Adult Email**: Email address for accompanying adult.
*   **Travel Destination City**: City of destination.
*   **Travel Destination State or Country**: State or country of destination.
*   **Travel Departure Date**: Date of departure.
*   **Travel Return Date**: Date of return.
*   **Travel Purpose**: Purpose of the travel.
*   **Travel Method**: How the child will be traveling.
*   **Emergency Contact Name**: Full name of emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone**: Phone number for emergency contact.
*   **Medical Insurance Provider**: Name of medical insurance provider.
*   **Medical Insurance Policy Number**: Policy or member number.
*   **Child Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions or allergies.
*   **Child Current Medications**: List any current medications.
*   **Medical Treatment Authorization**: Authorization for emergency medical treatment during travel.
*   **Additional Authorizations**: Any additional authorizations or restrictions.
*   **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Type your full name as your electronic signature.
*   **Signature Date**: Date of electronic signature.
*   **Notary Required**: Is notarization required for this authorization?
*   **Emergency Contact Name**: Full name of emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone**: Phone number for emergency contact.
