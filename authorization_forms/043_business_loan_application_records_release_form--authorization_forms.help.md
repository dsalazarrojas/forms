# Business Loan Application Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from a loan applicant to grant authorization for the release of their business loan application records. It is a requirement for institutions requesting records from the loan applicant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the loan applicant.
2. Enter the name of the institution requesting the records.
3. Specify which records you are requesting to be released.
4. Provide the reason for releasing the records.
5. Enter the date the authorization expires (if applicable).
6. Enter your contact email for confirmation of record release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the loan applicant.
* **Lender name** (`lender_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the institution requesting the records.
* **Records to release** (`records_to_release`, `text`, required): Specify which records you are requesting to be released, e.g., loan application, credit report, or other relevant documents.
* **Release purpose** (`release_purpose`, `text`, required): Provide a clear reason for releasing the records, e.g., for loan review, audit, or other business purposes.
* **Expiration date** (`expiration_date`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date the authorization expires.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `text`, required): Enter your contact email for confirmation of record release.
* **Signature instructions** (`signature_instructions`, `text`, optional): Follow the provided instructions for providing a valid signature, e.g., sign with a pen or electronic signature.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure the authorization process is completed correctly.
- Ensure that the records you are requesting to be released are relevant and necessary for the intended purpose.
- Keep the expiration date in mind, if applicable, to avoid any delays in the authorization process.
