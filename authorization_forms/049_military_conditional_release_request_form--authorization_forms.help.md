# Military Conditional Release Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a conditional release from military service. A conditional release allows individuals to temporarily leave their military duties for specific reasons such as education, family emergencies, or other personal circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form with your information, including the release date and time, the reason for release, and the supervisor's signature.
2. Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
3. Submit the form to your unit administrator for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Unit Administrator** (`unit_administrator`, text, optional): This field is for the individual requesting the conditional release to enter their unit administrator's name.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to be released from military service.
* **Release Time** (`release_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to be released from military service.
* **Reason for Release** (`reason_for_release`, text, optional): Provide a clear and concise explanation for your request. This will help your unit administrator understand your circumstances and ensure a smooth transition.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, text, optional): Your supervisor's signature is required to approve your request. Please ensure they have signed this field before submitting the form.
* **Unit Administrator Signature** (`unit_administrator_signature`, text, optional): This field should be signed by the unit administrator after reviewing your request.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): This field is for the unit administrator to indicate when they have reviewed your request.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, optional): This field is for the unit administrator to indicate when they have reviewed your request.
* **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, text, optional): The reviewer's signature is required to acknowledge their review of your request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If your unit administrator has any comments or suggestions, please add them here.
* **Approved** (`approved`, select_one, optional): Select "Approved" or "Denied" to indicate the outcome of your request.
