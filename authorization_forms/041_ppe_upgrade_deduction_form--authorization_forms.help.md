# ppe_upgrade_deduction_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PPE Upgrade Deduction Form is used to track and manage employee upgrades for Personal Protective Equipment (PPE). This form is required for all employee upgrades, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and reviewed for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the **Employee Name** field.
2. Enter the employee's job title in the **Job Title** field.
3. Enter the employee's ID number in the **Employee ID** field.
4. Select the employee's department from the **Department** field.
5. Enter the manager's name in the **Manager's Name** field (optional).
6. Enter the manager's email in the **Manager's Email** field (optional).
7. Enter the date of the upgrade in the **Date of Upgrade** field (optional).
8. Enter the cost amount of the upgrade in the **Cost Amount** field.
9. Select the authorized person from the **Authorized By** field.
10. Enter any comments or notes in the **Comments** field (optional).
11. Select the submission date in the **Submission Date** field (optional).
12. Select the person submitting the form from the **Submitted By** field.
13. Select the type of PPE being upgraded in the **PPE Type** field.
14. Enter the reason for the upgrade in the **Reason for Upgrade** field (optional).
15. Enter any additional notes in the **Notes** field (optional).
16. Select the manager's approval status in the **Manager's Approval** field.
17. Select the person approving the upgrade in the **Approved By** field.
18. Select the department to which the upgrade was submitted in the **Submitted To** field.
19. Select the approval date in the **Approval Date** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee requesting the upgrade.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the employee requesting the upgrade.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the ID number of the employee requesting the upgrade.
* **Department**: Select the department of the employee requesting the upgrade.
* **Manager's Name**: Enter the name of the employee's manager (optional).
* **Manager's Email**: Enter the email of the employee's manager (optional).
* **Date of Upgrade**: Enter the date the upgrade was completed (optional).
* **Cost Amount**: Enter the cost of the upgrade.
* **Authorized By**: Select the person authorizing the upgrade.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the upgrade (optional).
* **Submission Date**: Select the date the form was submitted (optional).
* **Submitted By**: Select the person submitting the form.
* **PPE Type**: Select the type of PPE being upgraded.
* **Reason for Upgrade**: Enter the reason for the upgrade (optional).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the upgrade (optional).
* **Manager's Approval**: Select the manager's approval status.
* **Approved By**: Select the person approving the upgrade.
* **Submitted To**: Select the department to which the upgrade was submitted.
* **Approval Date**: Select the date the upgrade was approved (optional).
