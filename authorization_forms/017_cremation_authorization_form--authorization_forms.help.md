# Cremation Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to authorize the cremation of an individual who has passed away. It is completed by a representative of the deceased, typically a family member.

## How To Complete This Form

### General Information

*   To complete this form, follow these steps:
    1.  Review the form carefully to understand the questions and answers.
    2.  Fill out the form with the requested information, which includes:
        *   The family name of the deceased.
        *   The address of the deceased.
        *   The contact number of the representative.
        *   Whether the representative is authorized for the cremation.
        *   The relationship between the representative and the deceased.
        *   The date of death.
        *   The time of death.
        *   Whether certain cremation services are desired.

### Specific Fields

*   **Family Name**: Enter the name of the deceased's family, as listed in the deceased's identification documents.
*   **Address**: Enter the address where the deceased was living at the time of death.
*   **Contact Number**: Enter a phone number or other contact information where the representative can be reached.
*   **Authorized for Cremation**: Indicate whether the representative is authorized to make decisions on behalf of the deceased for the purposes of cremation.
*   **Relationship to Deceased**: Select all relationships that apply to the representative's connection to the deceased. This could include:
    *   Yes (representative is the spouse)
    *   Yes (representative is the parent)
    *   Yes (representative is the child)
    *   Yes (representative is the sibling)
*   **Date of Death**: Enter the date of the deceased's passing.
*   **Time of Death**: Enter the time of the deceased's passing.
*   **Cremation Services**: Select all services desired for the cremation, which could include:
    *   Yes (cremation is desired)
    *   Yes (cremation plot or interment in a cemetery is desired)
    *   Yes (cremation niche or columbarium is desired)

## Tips

*   Please make sure to complete the form as accurately as possible, ensuring the correct information is provided for the deceased's final arrangements.
*   If you are unsure about any information, contact the relevant authorities or funeral service representatives for clarification.
*   This form is a requirement for cremation procedures. Failure to complete it will delay the process.
*   If you need assistance with completing this form, do not hesitate to reach out to the funeral service or relevant authorities for guidance.
