# Property Modernization Program Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from property owners to facilitate participation in the Property Modernization Program. It's essential to provide accurate and complete answers to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by providing basic owner details, including property address, city, and state.
2. Specify property information, such as type and size.
3. Answer questions about the property's status and condition.
4. If you are a property owner, please sign the consent section to confirm your participation in the program.
5. If you are not the property owner, please ensure you have the necessary permissions to represent the owner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Details**: This is the section for property owners to provide their personal details, including their name, phone number, and email.
* **Property Information**: Here, you'll provide basic property information, such as address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Property Type**: Choose the type of property you own, such as Single Family, Multi Family, or Townhouse.
* **Property Size**: Enter the size of your property.
* **Property Status**: Specify the current status of your property (Active or Sold).
* **Owner Signature**: Please sign this section to confirm your consent to participate in the Property Modernization Program.
* **Property Photo ID**: If you have a photo ID of your property, please provide the ID number.
* **Property Address**: Enter your property's address.
* **Property City**: Enter the city of your property.
* **Property State**: Choose the state where your property is located.
* **Property Zip**: Enter your property's zip code.
* **Property Type**: Choose the type of property you own.
* **Property SQFT**: Enter the square footage of your property.
* **Property Year Built**: Enter the year your property was built.
* **Property Floor Plan**: Describe the floor plan of your property.
* **Property Bed/Bath**: Enter the number of bedrooms and bathrooms in your property.
* **Property Lot Size**: Enter the size of your property's lot.
* **Property Views**: Describe the views from your property.
* **Property Neighborhood**: Choose the neighborhood where your property is located.
* **Property Status**: Specify the current status of your property (Active or Sold).
* **Property Notes**: This is an optional section to provide any additional notes about your property.

Tips: Please ensure you answer all the required fields and provide accurate information to avoid delays or issues with your application. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the program administrators for assistance.
