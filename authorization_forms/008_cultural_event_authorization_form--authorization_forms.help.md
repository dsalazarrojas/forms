# Cultural Event Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Event Authorization Form is designed to help event organizers request authorization for cultural events that align with our organization's standards and guidelines. Please use this form to provide detailed information about your event, ensuring a smooth approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `Event Details` page with the essential information about your event.
2. On the `Event Organizer Info` page, provide details about the person or team leading the event.
3. In `Approval Details`, describe the approval process and the reasons for your event's cultural significance.
4. Attach any relevant documents or supporting materials in the `Attachments` field.
5. Add any additional information you think would be helpful for us to know.
6. List the event participants and their roles.
7. Finally, select the `Submit` button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Details**: A page where you can enter the essential details about your event, such as date, time, location, and description.
- **Event Organizer Info**: This is the contact information of the person or team leading your event.
- **Approval Details**: Explain the approval process for your event, highlighting its cultural significance.
- **Attachments**: Upload any relevant documents or images that support your event's authorization.
- **Additional Info**: Share any extra information that may be helpful for a smooth review process.
- **Event Participants**: List all the people involved in your event, including their roles.
- **Approval Status**: This field is only for internal use; you will see the approval status of your event once it's been reviewed and approved.

# Tips
* Please make sure to provide as much detail as possible to ensure a smooth and efficient review process.
* If you're unsure about any information, feel free to contact us for clarification.
* Make sure to proofread your submission carefully before hitting the `Submit` button.
* You can attach documents and images to support your event's authorization.
* This form is for cultural events only; if you're planning a different type of event, please use a different form.
* Check with us first if you have any questions or concerns about this form or its usage.
