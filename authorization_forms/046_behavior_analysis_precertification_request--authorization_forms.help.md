# Behavior Analysis Precertification Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information necessary for the behavior analysis precertification process. It gathers data about the patient, provider, and services requested to ensure that the precertification process is completed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Information**: Provide accurate and up-to-date information about the patient.
	* Fill in the patient's name, date of birth, and insurance member ID.
2. **Provider Information**: Enter the requesting provider's name, NPI number, tax ID, and contact phone number.
3. **Service Details**: Select the correct service type and hours requested per week for the patient's needs.
	+ Choose the requested service type from the list provided.
4. **Attestation**: Confirm that the requested services are medically necessary.
	* Select the attestation option that best reflects your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Insurance Member ID**: Enter the patient's insurance member ID number.
* **Primary Diagnosis Code (ICD-10)**: Enter the primary diagnosis code for the patient (e.g., F84.0 Autism Spectrum Disorder).
* **Requesting Provider Name**: Enter the name of the provider requesting the services.
* **Provider NPI Number**: Enter the NPI number of the provider.
* **Facility Tax ID (TIN)**: Enter the tax ID of the facility.
* **Provider Phone Number**: Enter the contact phone number of the provider.
* **Requested Service Type**: Choose the service type that best meets the patient's needs.
	+ Assessment / Reassessment
	+ Direct Therapy (1-on-1)
	+ Supervision / Case Management
	+ Caregiver Training
	+ Social Skills Group
* **Total Hours Requested Per Week**: Enter the total hours requested per week for the services.
* **Requested Start Date**: Enter the date the services are requested to start.
* **Brief Summary of Clinical Necessity**: Provide a brief summary of the clinical necessity of the requested services.
* **Have You Attached the Required Clinical Assessment?**: Confirm that you have attached the required clinical assessment.
* **I Certify That the Requested Services Are Medically Necessary**: Confirm that the requested services are medically necessary.
* **Date of Request**: Enter the date the request was submitted.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
