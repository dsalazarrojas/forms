# Expense Dispute Resolution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist employees in resolving disputes related to expenses. It allows employees to provide details about the dispute, including their name, the details of the dispute, and any supporting documents. The form also captures the resolution outcome and any comments or actions taken.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct employee name from the dropdown list to associate the dispute with the correct employee.
2. Provide a detailed description of the dispute in the "Dispute Details" field.
3. Select any supporting documents relevant to the dispute.
4. Enter the date of the dispute.
5. Enter the amount of the dispute.
6. Enter the business name related to the dispute.
7. Enter your contact email address.
8. Enter your contact phone number.
9. Enter the manager's name.
10. Enter the manager's email address.
11. Enter the manager's phone number.
12. Select the resolution outcome for the dispute.
13. Provide any comments or actions taken.
14. Enter the date and time of submission.
15. Select who submitted the form.
16. Enter the IP address of submission.
17. Enter the user agent of submission.
18. Enter the platform of submission.
19. Enter the browser of submission.
20. Enter the time of last update.
21. Enter the time of creation.
22. Enter the time of deletion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, required/false): Select your name from the dropdown list to associate the dispute with you.
* **Dispute Details** (`dispute_details`, required/false): Provide a detailed description of the issue.
* **Dispute Date** (`dispute_date`, required/false): Enter the date of the dispute.
* **Dispute Amount** (`dispute_amount`, required/false): Enter the amount of the dispute.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, required/false): Select any supporting documents relevant to the dispute.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, required/false): Enter the name of the business related to the dispute.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, required/false): Enter your email address for contact.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, required/false): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, required/false): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, required/false): Enter the email address of your manager.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, required/false): Enter the phone number of your manager.
* **Resolution Outcome** (`resolution_outcome`, required/false): Select the outcome of the resolution.
* **Comments** (`comments`, required/false): Provide any comments or actions taken.
* **Date Of Submission** (`date_of_submission`, required/false): Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Time Of Submission** (`time_of_submission`, required/false): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, required/false): Select who submitted the form.
* **Submission IP Address** (`submission_ip_address`, required/false): Enter the IP address of submission.
* **Submission User Agent** (`submission_user_agent`, required/false): Enter the user agent of submission.
* **Submission User Platform** (`submission_user_platform`, required/false): Enter the platform of submission.
* **Submission User Browser** (`submission_user_browser`, required/false): Enter the browser of submission.
* **Time Of Last Update** (`time_of_last_update`, required/false): Enter the time of last update.
* **Time Of Creation** (`time_of_creation`, required/false): Enter the time of creation.
* **Time Of Deletion** (`time_of_deletion`, required/false): Enter the time of deletion.

# Tips

* Be sure to provide detailed descriptions for each field as needed.
* Ensure that you're filling out the form correctly to avoid delays in resolving the dispute.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
