# Authorization To Collect Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the authorization for collecting information from individuals, ensuring transparency and accountability in data collection practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Authorization Date**, which is the date you are granting authorization for data collection.
2. Enter your **Individual Full Name**, which is the name of the person whose data will be collected.
3. Enter your **Email Address**, which is your contact information for data collection purposes.
4. If applicable, enter your **Phone Number**, which is your contact phone number for data collection purposes.
5. Enter your **Date of Birth**, which is your birth date.
6. Enter the **Organization Collecting Data**, which is the name of the organization collecting your data.
7. Select the **Organization Type**, which is the type of organization collecting your data (e.g., Healthcare Provider, Financial Institution, etc.).
8. Enter the **Purpose of Data Collection**, which is a brief description of why your data is being collected.
9. Select the **Purpose Category**, which is the category under which the data collection purpose falls (e.g., Service Delivery, Research, etc.).
10. Select the **Types of Data to Be Collected**, which are the types of data being collected (e.g., Personal Information, Contact Information, etc.).
11. Select the **Data Sources**, which are the sources from which data will be collected (e.g., Directly from You, Third-Party Services, etc.).
12. Describe how **Data Will Be Used**, which is the intended use of your data.
13. Select whether your data will be **Shared with Third Parties**, which determines who will have access to your data.
14. If applicable, enter **Third Party Details**, which are the names of parties that will receive your data.
15. Select the **Data Retention Period**, which is how long your data will be kept.
16. Select the **Data Security Measures**, which are the protections used to safeguard your data (e.g., Encryption, Access Controls, etc.).
17. Confirm that you have **Reviewed the Privacy Policy**, which ensures you understand how your data will be used.
18. Indicate how well you **Understand Privacy Practices**, which is your level of understanding about data use.
19. Confirm whether you are subject to **GDPR or Similar Compliance**, which determines if you have data protection rights.
20. Acknowledge your **Data Rights**, which ensures you understand your rights as a data subject.
21. Confirm that you have the **Ability to Withdraw Consent**, which is your right to withdraw consent at any time.
22. Indicate whether your **Consent is Voluntary**, which ensures you are not coerced into providing data.
23. Confirm that you **Authorize Data Collection**, which is your permission to collect your data.
24. Provide any additional **Notes or Questions**, which are extra information or queries you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Authorization Date** (Date of this authorization): The date you are authorizing data collection.
* **Individual Full Name** (Name of person whose data will be collected): Enter your name in full.
* **Email Address** (Your email contact): Enter your email address for data collection purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Your contact phone): Optional field for entering your phone number for data collection purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (Your birth date): Enter your date of birth.
* **Organization Collecting Data** (Name of organization): Enter the name of the organization collecting your data.
* **Organization Type** (What type of organization): Select the type of organization collecting your data (e.g., Healthcare Provider, Financial Institution, etc.).
* **Purpose of Data Collection** (Why is data being collected): Briefly describe why your data is being collected.
* **Purpose Category** (What category does purpose fall under): Select the category under which the data collection purpose falls (e.g., Service Delivery, Research, etc.).
* **Types of Data to Be Collected** (Select all data types): Select all the types of data being collected (e.g., Personal Information, Contact Information, etc.).
* **Data Sources** (Where will data come from): Select the sources from which data will be collected (e.g., Directly from You, Third-Party Services, etc.).
* **Data Will Be Used** (Describe intended use): Describe how your data will be used.
* **Will Data Be Shared with Third Parties** (Yes, No, or Depends on circumstances): Determine whether your data will be shared with others.
* **Third Party Details** (Which parties will receive data): If applicable, enter the names of parties that will receive your data.
* **Data Retention Period** (How long will data be kept): Select how long your data will be kept.
* **Data Security Measures** (What protections will be used): Select the protections used to safeguard your data (e.g., Encryption, Access Controls, etc.).
* **Reviewed Privacy Policy** (Have you reviewed privacy policy): Confirm that you have reviewed the privacy policy.
* **Understand Privacy Practices** (How well do you understand): Indicate how well you understand data use.
* **GDPR or Similar Compliance** (Do you have data protection rights): Confirm whether you are subject to GDPR or similar laws.
* **Data Rights** (Acknowledge your data rights): Acknowledge your understanding of your data rights.
* **Ability to Withdraw Consent** (Can you withdraw consent): Confirm that you can withdraw consent at any time.
* **Voluntary Consent** (Is your consent voluntary): Indicate whether your consent is voluntary.
* **Authorize Data Collection** (Give permission): Confirm that you authorize data collection.
* **Additional Notes or Questions** (Any other information): Provide any additional information or queries you may have.
