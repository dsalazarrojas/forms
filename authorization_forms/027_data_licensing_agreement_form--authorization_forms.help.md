# data_licensing_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from data providers about their data usage and licensing agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Choose the correct data provider from the select_one field.
2.  Select the organization or research team associated with the data.
3.  Enter the permitted use of the data.
4.  Add a note for signatures if necessary.
5.  Confirm the terms of agreement on a specific date.
6.  Sign the form to confirm agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Data Provider** (`dataProvider`, select_one, required: false): Choose the correct data provider for the data you are providing. This will help us understand the origin of the data.
* **Organization** (`organization`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the organization or research team associated with the data. If there is no specific organization or team, choose "Other" and provide a note if necessary.
* **Permitted Use** (`permittedUse`, text, required: false): Describe the permitted use of the data. Be as specific as possible about what the data will be used for.
* **Signatures** (`signatures`, note, required: false): Add a note for signatures if necessary. This is an optional field for additional information.
* **Terms Acceptance** (`termsAcceptance`, date, required: false): Confirm the terms of agreement on a specific date. Please enter the date you accept the terms.
* **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, note, required: false): Add a note for acknowledgement if necessary. This is an optional field for additional information.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`termsAndConditions`, text, required: false): Describe the terms and conditions of the agreement.
* **Signature Date** (`signatureDate`, date, required: false): Enter the date you signed the agreement.
* **Signature Time** (`signatureTime`, time, required: false): Enter the time you signed the agreement.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Sign the agreement by typing your name and contact information.
* **Data License Agreement** (`dataLicenseAgreement`, note, required: false): Add a note for the license agreement if necessary. This is an optional field for additional information.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you need help or have questions, contact form@gic.mx.
