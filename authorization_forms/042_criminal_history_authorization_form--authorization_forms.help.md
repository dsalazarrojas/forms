# Criminal History Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect authorization to access or conduct background checks related to an individual's criminal history. It is intended to be used for background checks, security screenings, or other purposes where access to an individual's criminal history is required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the authorization form you are using (e.g., "Authorization Forms").
2. Provide a brief description of why this form is being used.
3. Enter your full name.
4. Enter your date of birth.
5. Enter your address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Select the type of form this is for. For example, "Authorization Forms".
* **Description**: Provide a brief explanation of why this form is needed. This will help others understand the purpose of the form.
* **Name**: Enter your full name, as it will be used to identify you.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Address**: Enter your current address, including your street address, city, state, and zip code.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure the form is accurate and complete.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask someone for guidance or clarification.
