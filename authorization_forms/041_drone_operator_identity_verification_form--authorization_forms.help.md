# Drone Operator Identity Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to verify the identity of drone operators by collecting and validating their personal information, drone details, and verification status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in your personal details**: Enter your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Operator ID accurately and completely.
2. **Select your drone information**: Choose your Drone Type, and enter your Drone License Number and Drone Registration Number (if applicable).
3. **Verify your license**: Select your Issue Authority, License Status, and Verification Status.
4. **Sign and date**: Sign the form with your Signature, Signature Date, and Signature Time.
5. **Verification**: Select the Verification By, and enter the Verification Authority (if applicable).
6. **Finalize**: Review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (required): Enter your phone number.
* **Operator ID** (required): Enter your operator ID.
* **Drone Type** (required): Select the type of drone you operate (A, B, or C).
* **Drone License Number** (optional): Enter your drone license number (if applicable).
* **Drone Registration Number** (required): Enter your drone registration number.
* **Issue Date** (optional): Enter the date of issue (if applicable).
* **Expiration Date** (optional): Enter the expiration date of issue (if applicable).
* **Issue Number** (required): Enter the issue number.
* **Issue Authority** (optional): Enter the issue authority (if applicable).
* **Drone Type** (required): Select the type of drone you operate (A, B, or C).
* **License Status** (required): Select the status of your license (A, B, or C).
* **Verification Status** (required): Select the verification status.
* **Notes** (optional): Enter any additional information or comments.
* **Additional Notes** (optional): Enter any additional information or comments.
* **Verification Date** (optional): Enter the date of verification.
* **Verification By** (required): Enter the person or authority verifying your identity.
* **Verification Authority** (required): Select the authority verifying your identity (A, B, or C).
* **Signature** (required): Sign the form to confirm your identity.
* **Signature Date** (required): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Signature Time** (required): Enter the time you signed the form.
* **Verification By Title** (optional): Enter the title of the person verifying your identity (if applicable).
