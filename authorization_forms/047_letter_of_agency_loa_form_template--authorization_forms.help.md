# Letter Of Agency (LOA) Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Letter Of Agency (LOA) Form Template is used to create and manage letters of agency. These documents authorize one party to act on behalf of another in a business or legal context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are using this form for its intended purpose and that the information you are providing is accurate and up-to-date.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form ID (1)
* **Form ID** (`number`, `required`): This is a unique identifier for the form. Please fill in the provided number.

### Form Title (2)
* **Form Title** (`text`, `required`): Enter the title of the form, a brief and descriptive name for the letter of agency.

### Form Description (3)
* **Form Description** (`text`, `optional`): Provide a detailed description of the form, if needed.

### Category (4)
* **Category** (`select multiple`, `required`): Choose one or multiple categories for the form, such as "Authorization Forms" or "Other".

### Form Version (5)
* **Form Version** (`number`, `required`): Enter the version number of the form, which will be incremented automatically.

### Form Category (6)
* **Form Category** (`select one`, `required`): Choose the category for the form, "Letter Of Agency (LOA)" or "Other".

### Form Status (7)
* **Form Status** (`select one`, `required`): Choose the status of the form, "Draft" or "Published".

### Form Author (8)
* **Form Author** (`text`, `optional`): Enter the name of the person who created the form, if applicable.

### Created At (9)
* **Created At** (`date`, `optional`): Enter the date the form was created.

### Updated At (10)
* **Updated At** (`date`, `optional`): Enter the date the form was last updated.

## Tips
- Review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
- If you need to make changes to the form, make sure to update the Form Version and Form Status accordingly.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
