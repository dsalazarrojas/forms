# Energy Plan Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for energy plan releases, enabling customers to request and track the status of their energy plans.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Customer Name** field with your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your **Customer Email** in the provided field.
3. Choose a date and time for your **Request Date** and **Request Time** when you would like to release your energy plan.
4. Provide a brief description of the **Reason for Request** in the field below.
5. Sign your name in the **Customer Signature** field to confirm your request.
6. If applicable, specify who authorized the release in the **Authorized By** field.
7. Enter the date and time of authorization in the **Authorized Date** and **Authorized Time** fields, respectively.
8. Provide any additional **Customer Notes** that you think are relevant to the energy plan release.
9. Select the **Customer Approval** option that best describes your approval status.
10. If your request was not approved, provide a brief description of the reason in the **Customer Rejection Reason** field.
11. Enter any additional **Internal Review Notes** that are relevant to the energy plan release.
12. Select the **Internal Approval** option that best describes the internal approval status.
13. Enter any comments or notes that are relevant to the energy plan release in the **Comments** field.
14. Enter the date and time when the customer approved the energy plan in the **Customer Approval Date** and **Customer Approval Time** fields, respectively.
