# Business Audit Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Audit Authorization Form is used to authorize an audit of a business, providing information about the business being audited, the audit itself, and the scope of the audit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by completing the client information section, which includes the business's legal name and full address.
2. Next, complete the representative's information, including their name, title, and email address.
3. Select the type of audit to be conducted, such as financial or operational.
4. Specify the audit period and start date.
5. Choose how long the authorization is valid, such as until audit completion or for a set period.
6. Finally, sign and date the form, indicating that you have authorized the audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (Required)**: This section provides information about the business being audited. Please enter the business's legal name and full address.
* **Business Name (Required)**: Enter the legal name of the business.
* **Business Address (Required)**: Enter the full address of the business.
* **Business Phone (Required)**: Enter the contact number of the business.
* **Business Email (Required)**: Enter the contact email of the business.
* **Tax ID or EIN (Required)**: Enter the Federal tax identification number or Employer Identification Number (EIN) of the business.
* **Authorized Representative (Optional)**: This section is for the person authorizing the audit. Please provide information about who is authorizing the audit.
* **Representative Name (Required)**: Enter the legal name of the representative.
* **Representative Title (Required)**: Enter the title or position of the representative.
* **Representative Email (Required)**: Enter the email address of the representative.
* **Representative Phone (Required)**: Enter the contact number of the representative.
* **Audit Type (Required)**: Select the type of audit to be conducted, such as financial or operational.
* **Audit Period (Required)**: Specify the time period to be audited.
* **Audit Start Date (Required)**: Enter the date when the audit will begin.
* **Estimated Completion (Optional)**: Estimate when the audit is expected to end.
* **Auditor Information (Optional)**: This section is for the person or firm conducting the audit. Please provide information about who will conduct the audit.
* **Auditor Firm Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the auditing firm.
* **Lead Auditor Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the primary auditor.
* **Auditor Email (Required)**: Enter the contact email of the auditor.
* **Authorization Scope (Optional)**: This section is for any additional information about the scope of the audit.
* **Records Access Authorized (Required)**: Choose whether to grant access to the business's records.
* **Accessible Records (Required)**: Select which records can be accessed, such as financial statements or tax returns.
* **Employee Interviews Authorized (Required)**: Choose whether to allow auditors to interview employees.
* **Site Access Authorized (Required)**: Choose whether to grant access to the business's facilities.
* **Confidentiality Agreement (Required)**: Choose whether the auditor must maintain confidentiality.
* **Authorization Valid Period (Required)**: Choose how long the authorization is valid, such as until audit completion.
* **Special Conditions or Restrictions (Optional)**: Enter any additional conditions or restrictions on the authorization.
* **Authorized Signature (Required)**: Sign the form to indicate that you have authorized the audit.
* **Signature Date (Required)**: Date and sign the form to indicate that you have authorized the audit.
* **Witness Name (Optional)**: This section is for an optional witness to the signature.
* **Witness Signature Date (Optional)**: Date and sign the form to indicate that you have witnessed the signature.
