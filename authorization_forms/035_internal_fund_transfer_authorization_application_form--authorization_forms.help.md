# Internal Fund Transfer Authorization Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request authorization for internal fund transfers within the organization. It helps management to track and approve or deny transfer requests based on the submitted criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for 'Transfer Type' to indicate the type of transfer being requested.
2. For 'Source Account' and 'Destination Account', select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the accounts involved in the transfer.
3. Enter the date and time of the transfer in the 'Date' and 'Time' fields, respectively.
4. Enter the amount to be transferred in the 'Amount' field.
5. Provide a clear reason for the transfer in the 'Reason for Transfer' field.
6. Select the 'Authorization Level' (High or Low) to determine the level of approval required.
7. Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' in 'Approval Status' to indicate the current status of the approval process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transfer Type**: Select whether this is a transfer from one account to another within the organization.
* **Source Account**: Select the account from which funds are being transferred.
* **Destination Account**: Select the account to which funds are being transferred.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the transfer.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the transfer.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount of funds to be transferred.
* **Reason for Transfer**: Provide a clear reason for the transfer, including any relevant details.
* **Authorization Level**: Select High or Low to determine the level of approval required.
* **Approval Status**: Indicate whether the approval process is active or inactive.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have filled out all required fields correctly to avoid delays in processing your request.
* Review each field carefully to provide accurate and relevant information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the appropriate authority for clarification.
