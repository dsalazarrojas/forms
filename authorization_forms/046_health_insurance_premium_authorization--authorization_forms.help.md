# Health Insurance Premium Authorization - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to authorize health insurance premium payments for an individual or entity. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that the insurance provider has the necessary permissions to collect premium payments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each field based on your current situation.
2. Review each page carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	* This field is used to determine if the applicant is authorized to provide information on their own behalf or if they have permission from someone else.
### 2. **Policy Details** (`policy_details`, `number`, required: false)
	* This field is used to input the policy number for the insurance policy you are authorizing.
### 3. **Payment Permission** (`payment_permission`, `select_one`, required: false)
	* Select "Yes" if you are authorizing payment of your premiums, "No" otherwise.
### 4. **Premium Amount** (`premium_amount`, `number`, required: false)
	* This field is used to input the premium amount to be authorized.
### 5. **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_one`, required: false)
	* Select "Yes" if you agree to the payment terms, "No" otherwise.
### 6. **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, `date`, required: false)
	* This field is used to input the date when you are authorizing payment of your premium.
### 7. **Authorization Time** (`authorization_time`, `time`, required: false)
	* This field is used to input the time when you are authorizing payment of your premium.
### 8. **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, `text`, required: false)
	* This field is used to input the policy number for the insurance policy you are authorizing.
### 9. **Premium Frequency** (`premium_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	* Select "Yes" if you are authorizing payment of premiums for the policy holder, "No" otherwise.
### 10. **Premium Cycle** (`premium_cycle`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	* Select "Yes" if you are authorizing payment of premiums for the policy holder, "No" otherwise.
### 11. **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false)
	* Select "Yes" if you are authorizing payment of premiums using the selected method, "No" otherwise.

## Tips
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your insurance provider for clarification.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to authorize premium payments for the policy holder.
