# Credit Report Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and employment information, and authorization to share the credit report with various parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, date of birth, and social security number.
2. Provide your address and contact information, including phone and email.
3. Enter your employment information, such as job title and employer address.
4. Select the reason for your request.
5. Sign the form by adding your digital signature.
6. Choose who you want to authorize to share your credit report with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **John Doe** (name): Your full name as you would like to be referenced.
* **Address** (Address): Your home address.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth): Your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Social Security Number** (social_security_number): Your social security number.
* **Phone** (phone): Your phone number.
* **Email** (email): Your email address.
* **Employer** (employer): Your current or most recent employer's name.
* **Job Title** (job_title): Your job title.
* **Employer Address** (employer_address): Your employer's address.
* **Reason for Request** (reason_for_request): A brief description of why you're requesting this credit report.
* **Signature Date** (signature_date): The date you sign this form.
* **Signature** (signature): Your digital signature.
* **Authorized to Share** (authorized_to_share): Choose who you want to authorize to share your credit report with.
* **Authorized to Share With** (authorized_to_share_with): Select who you want to share your credit report with.
* **Authorized to Share With Employer** (authorized_to_share_with_employer): Choose whether you want to share your credit report with your employer.
* **Authorized to Share With Landlord** (authorized_to_share_with_landlord): Choose whether you want to share your credit report with your landlord.
* **Authorized to Share With Lender** (authorized_to_share_with_lender): Choose whether you want to share your credit report with your lender.
* **Authorized to Share With Other** (authorized_to_share_with_other): Choose whether you want to share your credit report with other parties not mentioned above.

Tips: Please double-check your information and ensure you're authorizing the correct parties to access your credit report.
