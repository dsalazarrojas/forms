# Production Environment Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to grant access to the production environment, a restricted area where critical infrastructure and sensitive information are stored. Users must be authorized to access this environment to perform specific tasks or requests.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:


1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have a clear understanding of the required information and procedures.
2. Complete the Access Request Form (page 1) by selecting "Yes" or "No" as required.
3. Enter your User Name and Email address in the corresponding fields (pages 2 and 3).
4. Select the relevant options for Access Granted By and Date/Time of access request (pages 2 and 3).
5. Review and confirm your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Access Request Form** (`access_request_form`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you need access to the production environment, otherwise select "No".
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your official email address.
* **Access Granted By** (`access_granted_by`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you have permission to access the production environment and "No" otherwise.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you need access to the production environment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the specific time you need access to the production environment.
* **Production Environment Access Form 2** (`production_environment_access_form_page_2`, select_one, required/optional): This field is not applicable for this form version.
* **Access Request Form 2** (`production_environment_access_form_page_3`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you need access to the production environment.

Note: This form is subject to change based on company policies and procedures. Please refer to your local policies for the most up-to-date information.


# Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure that you have selected the correct options for each field.
* Do not select "Yes" for Access Granted By unless you have actual permission to access the production environment.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your local policies or contact your supervisor for clarification.
