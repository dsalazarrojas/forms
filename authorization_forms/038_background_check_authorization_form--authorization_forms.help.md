# Background Check Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request background checks on potential or current employees. It collects information about the applicant's personal details, employment history, and authorization for background investigations.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
- Make sure to sign and date the form after completing it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Legal Name** (1): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your official documents, including first, middle, and last name.
* **Other Names Used** (2): List any other names you have used, such as maiden name or aliases.
* **Date of Birth** (3): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Social Security Number** (4): Enter your social security number in the XXX-XX-XXXX format.
* **Current Address** (5): Enter your current street address.
* **City** (6): Enter the city of your current residence.
* **State** (7): Enter the state or province of your current residence.
* **ZIP Code** (8): Enter the postal code of your current residence.
* **Years at Address** (9): Select how long you have lived at your current address from the options provided.
* **Previous Address** (10): If you have lived less than 5 years at your current address, enter your previous address.
* **Email Address** (11): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (12): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Drivers License Number** (13): Enter your driver's license number.
* **Drivers License State** (14): Enter the state that issued your driver's license.
* **Position Applied For** (15): Enter the job title or purpose for which you are applying or are in this position.
* **Employer or Organization Name** (16): Enter the name of the company or organization requesting the background check.
* **Types of Checks Authorized** (17): Select all the types of background checks you authorize, such as criminal history, employment verification, etc.
* **Have You Been Convicted of a Crime** (18): Select 'True' if you have been convicted of a crime, excluding minor traffic violations. If yes, explain in the **Conviction Details** field.
* **Conviction Details** (19): If you selected 'True' in the previous field, explain the details of your conviction.
* **Authorization Statement** (20): Select 'Yes, I authorize' to express your consent for background investigations.
* **FCRA Rights Acknowledged** (21): Select 'Yes, acknowledged' to confirm you understand your rights under FCRA.
* **Request Copy of Report** (22): Select 'True' if you want a copy of the background check results.
* **Applicant Signature** (23): Sign your name digitally or type it out.
* **Date Signed** (24): Enter the date of your authorization.
* **Witness Signature** (optional): If required, sign and date this field.

Note: This guide is for information purposes only, and the actual requirements and procedures may vary depending on your organization's policies and procedures.
