# Bar Inspection Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bar Inspection Records Release Form is used to request access to bar inspection records. This form is designed to gather essential information from the requester to facilitate the efficient processing of their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2.  Select the correct format and method of records delivery that suits your needs.
3.  Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, **Required**): The date on which this records request was made.
* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, `text`, **Required**): Your full name as the requester.
* **Requester Title or Position** (`requester_title`, `text`, **Optional**): Your title or position, if applicable.
* **Requester Email Address** (`requester_email`, `email`, **Required**): Your email address where records will be sent.
* **Requester Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, `text`, **Required**): Your phone number for contact.
* **Organization Name** (`requester_organization`, `text`, **Optional**): Your company or organization name.
* **Bar or Establishment Name** (`establishment_name`, `text`, **Required**): The name of the bar or establishment for which records are being requested.
* **Establishment Address** (`establishment_address`, `text`, **Required**): The full address of the bar or establishment.
* **City** (`establishment_city`, `text`, **Required**): The city where the bar or establishment is located.
* **State** (`establishment_state`, `text`, **Required**): The state where the bar or establishment is located.
* **ZIP Code** (`establishment_zip`, `text`, **Required**): The zip code of the bar or establishment.
* **License Number (if known)** (`license_number`, `text`, **Optional**): The establishment license number, if known.
* **Reason for Records Request** (`request_reason`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose the reason for requesting the records (e.g., Property Purchase, Insurance Verification, etc.).
* **Detailed Reason Description** (`reason_description`, `text`, **Optional**): A brief description of the reason for the records request.
* **Records Requested** (`records_requested`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Choose the specific records you require (e.g., Health and Safety Inspections, Liquor License Records, etc.).
* **Records From Date** (`date_range_from`, `date`, **Optional**): The earliest date for which you require records.
* **Records To Date** (`date_range_to`, `date`, **Optional**): The most recent date for which you require records.
* **Preferred Format for Records** (`records_format`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose how you would like the records delivered (e.g., Electronic Copy, Physical Copy, etc.).
* **Authorized by Establishment Owner** (`authorization_by_owner`, `select_one`, **Required**): Confirm whether the owner of the establishment has authorized this request.
* **Establishment Owner Contact** (`owner_contact_info`, `text`, **Optional**): Name and phone number of the establishment owner.
* **I Understand Records May Have Associated Fees** (`fee_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, **Required**): Confirm that you understand that records may have associated fees.
* **I Agree to Keep Records Confidential** (`confidentiality_agreement`, `select_one`, **Required**): Confirm that you agree to keep the records confidential.
