# Parcel Recharge User Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parcel Recharge User Agreement is a form used by parcel delivery personnel to record and confirm details about the recharge process. It's a critical document that ensures the recharge process is completed correctly and any issues are addressed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
2. Review each field carefully before confirming.
3. Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.
4. Sign and confirm the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (1): Enter the first name of the person being recharged.
* **Last name** (2): Enter the last name of the person being recharged.
* **Email address** (3): Enter the email address of the person being recharged.
* **Contact number** (4): Enter the contact number of the person being recharged.
* **Recharge date** (5): Enter the date of the recharge process.
* **Recharge time** (6): Enter the time of the recharge process.
* **Delivery person** (7): Enter the name of the delivery person.
* **Confirm recharge** (8): Confirm whether the recharge process was successful.
* **Signature** (9): Add any additional comments or notes about the recharge process.
* **Confirm delivery** (10): Confirm whether the delivery was successful.
* **Delivery date** (11): Enter the date of the delivery.
* **Delivery time** (12): Enter the time of the delivery.
* **Confirm signature** (13): Confirm whether the signature was obtained.
* **Customer notes** (14): Add any additional comments or notes from the customer.
* **Delivery notes** (15): Add any additional comments or notes about the delivery process.
* **Confirmation signature** (16): Add the confirmation of the signature.
* **Confirmation date** (17): Enter the date of the confirmation.
* **Confirmation time** (18): Enter the time of the confirmation.
* **Confirm delivery person** (19): Confirm whether the delivery person was present.
* **Recharge details** (20): Add any additional comments or notes about the recharge process.
* **Delivery details** (21): Add any additional comments or notes about the delivery process.
* **Notes** (22): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Confirm notes** (23): Confirm whether the notes are accurate.
* **Confirm first name** (24): Confirm whether the first name is correct.
* **Confirm last name** (25): Confirm whether the last name is correct.

Please note that all fields are optional, but it's essential to fill out as many fields as possible to ensure accurate records and efficient communication.
