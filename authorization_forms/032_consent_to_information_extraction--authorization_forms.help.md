# Consent To Information Extraction - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your consent for the extraction of information from your data. This form is used by organizations to request your consent before extracting data from your records. Please review the following sections to understand how to complete the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Consent Date**: The date when you are providing your consent for the data extraction.
2. **Your Name**: Your full name as it appears on your documents.
3. **Email Address**: Your contact email address.
4. **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number (optional).
5. **Address**: Your mailing address (optional).
6. **Organization Name**: The name of the organization requesting the data extraction.
7. **Organization Contact**: The name of the contact person from the organization.
8. **Purpose of Information Extraction**: A brief description of why the organization needs to extract your data.
9. **Types of Information to Extract**: Select all the types of information that the organization needs to extract (Personal, Contact, Financial, Medical, etc.).
10. **Other Information Types**: If you selected "Other" in the previous field, please specify the type of information.
11. **Data Sources**: Where the information will be extracted from.
12. **Extraction Method**: How the information will be extracted (Automated Software, Manual Review, etc.).
13. **Estimated Data Volume**: The approximate amount of data to be extracted.
14. **Extraction Timeline**: When the data extraction will occur.
15. **Data Retention Period**: How long the extracted data will be kept.
16. **Data Storage Location**: Where the extracted data will be stored.
17. **Security Measures**: The measures taken to protect the extracted data (Encryption, Access Controls, etc.).
18. **Will Data Be Shared**: Will the extracted data be shared with others (Named Parties, Anonymous Parties, etc.).
19. **Third Party Details**: If data will be shared with others, list the parties involved.
20. **Where Will Data Be Processed**: The geographic location of processing.
21. **Legal Basis for Extraction**: The legal basis for the data extraction (Consent, Contract Performance, etc.).
22. **Your Rights Understood**: Do you understand your rights concerning the extracted data.
23. **Right to Withdraw**: Do you know you can withdraw your consent at any time.
24. **Withdrawal Process Explained**: Do you know how to withdraw your consent.
25. **Were Your Questions Answered**: Did you have an opportunity to ask questions about the data extraction.
26. **Your Consent Decision**: Do you consent to the data extraction.
27. **Your Signature**: Your full name as a signature.
28. **Organization Representative**: Name of the organization representative.
29. **Representative Signature**: Name of the organization representative as a signature.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are providing accurate information about yourself and your data.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the organization representative for clarification.
* This form is designed to ensure that your data rights are respected and that you are aware of the data extraction process.
