**C-Level Authorization Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The C-Level Authorization Form is a critical document that facilitates informed decision-making at the executive level. It ensures that projects and decisions align with company objectives and are properly authorized by the relevant executives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Begin by reviewing the Executive Authorization Details section (Page 1).
2.  Fill in the Name of Authorizing Executive field with the executive who will be authorizing this action.
3.  Select the Executive Role of the authorizing executive from the provided options (Page 2).
4.  Enter the Project or Decision Title that requires approval (Page 3).
5.  Indicate the Level of Strategic Alignment by selecting from the provided options (Page 4).
6.  Enter the Estimated Financial Impact of the project or decision (Page 5).
7.  Assess the Risk Level associated with this project or decision (Page 6).
8.  Provide a brief Risk Mitigation Strategy (Page 7).
9.  Enter the Proposed Implementation Timeline (Page 8).
10. Make an Authorization Decision (Page 9).
11. If applicable, enter Conditions for Approval (Page 10).
12. Select the Date of Authorization (Page 11).
13. Finally, confirm your Electronic Signature by selecting "Yes, I authorize this action" (Page 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Executive Authorization Details
- **-- Executive Authorization Details --** (Page 1): This section serves as a brief introduction to the authorization process and provides space for any executive-level comments or observations.
### Name of Authorizing Executive
- **Name of Authorizing Executive** (`executing_executive`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the executive who will be authorizing this action.
### Executive Role
- **Executive Role** (`executive_role`, `select_one`, required): Select the executive role that will be authorizing this action from the provided options (CEO, CFO, COO, CTO, CMO, or Other).
### Project or Decision Title
- **Project or Decision Title** (`project_proposal_title`, `text`, required): Enter the project or decision title that requires approval.
### Level of Strategic Alignment
- **Level of Strategic Alignment** (`strategic_alignment`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the level of strategic alignment by selecting from the provided options (High, Moderate, Low).
### Estimated Financial Impact
- **Estimated Financial Impact** (`budget_impact`, `number`, required): Enter the estimated financial impact of the project or decision in USD.
### Risk Level
- **Risk Level** (`risk_assessment`, `select_one`, required): Assess the risk level associated with this project or decision by selecting from the provided options (Low, Medium, High, Critical).
### Risk Mitigation Strategy
- **Risk Mitigation Strategy** (`risk_mitigation_plan`, `text`, required): Provide a brief summary of how risks will be managed.
### Proposed Implementation Timeline
- **Proposed Implementation Timeline** (`timeline_approval`, `text`, required): Enter the proposed implementation timeline for the project or decision.
### Authorization Decision
- **Authorization Decision** (`authorization_status`, `select_one`, required): Make an authorization decision by selecting from the provided options (Approved, Approved with Conditions, Denied, Further Review Required).
### Conditions for Approval
- **Conditions for Approval** (`conditional_requirements`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter any conditions that must be met for approval.
### Date of Authorization
- **Date of Authorization** (`authorization_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the authorization was made.
### Electronic Signature Confirmation
- **Electronic Signature Confirmation** (`digital_signature_notice`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your electronic signature by selecting "Yes, I authorize this action" or "Other" if you have a different approval method.

**Tips**
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant executive or departmental authority.
* The form is designed to facilitate informed decision-making, so please ensure that all sections are filled accurately and completely.
