# wealth_management_custodian_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect onboarding details and custodian agreements for wealth management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by entering your onboarding details on the first page.
2.  On the "Onboarding Details" page, fill in the following fields:
	*   **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
	*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
	*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
	*   **Address**: Enter your address, including the street address and postal code.
	*   **Email**: Enter your email address.
	*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
3.  On the next page, select the following fields:
	*   **Relationship Status**: Select your relationship status:
		+   Single
		+   Married
		+   Common Law
4.  **Employment Status**: Select your employment status:
		+   Employed
		+   Self-Employed
		+   Retired
5.  **Assets**: Select all assets you own:
		+   Cash
		+   Stock
		+   Mutual Funds
6.  **Beneficiary**: Select all beneficiaries:
		+   Self
		+   Spouse
		+   Trust
7.  **Custodian Agreement**: Select whether you agree or do not agree to the custodian agreement.
8.  Finally, add any **Additional Comments** regarding your onboarding details.
