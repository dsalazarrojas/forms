# Business Consent Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document an individual's consent for the processing and retention of their personal data. It is a legal requirement for data controllers and organizations to obtain explicit consent from individuals before collecting and processing their personal data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your personal information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the types of communications you consent to (email, phone, or marketing).
3. Choose how you consent to the processing of your personal data (yes or no).
4. Specify the retention period for your data (6 months, 1 year, 2 years, etc.).
5. Confirm your right to access and delete your data.
6. Indicate your authorization to provide this consent on behalf of your organization.
7. Enter your title or role in your organization (if applicable).
8. Sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, required): Your complete name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Your primary email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Your birth date.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, required): The name of your organization.
* **Consent Types** (`consent_types`, optional): Select all or specific types of communications you consent to (email, phone, marketing).
* **Email Communications Consent** (`email_consent`, required): You consent to receive email communications from us.
* **Phone Communications Consent** (`phone_consent`, required): You consent to receive phone calls or SMS from us.
* **Marketing Communications Consent** (`marketing_consent`, required): You consent to marketing and promotional materials from us.
* **Data Sharing Consent** (`data_sharing_consent`, required): You consent to sharing your information with partners.
* **Cookie and Tracking Consent** (`cookies_consent`, required): You consent to cookies and website tracking.
* **Data Processing Consent** (`personal_data_processing`, required): You consent to the processing of your personal data.
* **Data Retention Period** (`data_retention`, required): You specify the retention period for your data (6 months, 1 year, 2 years, etc.).
* **Right to Access Your Data** (`data_access`, required): You understand you can request access to your data.
* **Right to Delete Your Data** (`data_deletion`, required): You understand you can request deletion of your data.
* **Authorization** (`authorization_section`, optional): You confirm your authorization to provide this consent on behalf of your organization.
* **Authority** (`authority`, required): You confirm your authority to provide this consent.
* **Title or Role** (`authorization_title`, optional): If authorizing on behalf of your organization, enter your title or role.
* **Consent Effective Date** (`effective_date`, required): The date this consent becomes effective.
* **Signature** (`signature`, required): Type your name as signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, required): The date of your signature.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have read and understood the purpose and content of this form before filling it out.
* Double-check your personal data and consent options before submitting the form.
* If you are authorizing on behalf of an organization, ensure you have the necessary authority to do so.
* Please sign the form with your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* Date your signature with the current date.
