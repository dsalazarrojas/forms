# Firearms Training Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Firearms Training Release Form is a document used to record and verify the completion of firearms training sessions. This form ensures that participants have received the necessary training and have been properly authorized to handle firearms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant information fields, including name, address, phone number, and email.
2. Enter the details of the training session, including the date and time of the training, the instructor, and the location.
3. Sign the form to confirm that you have completed the training and are authorized to handle firearms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: This field is where you enter your name as a participant in the training session.
* **Participant Address**: Enter your physical address as a participant in the training session.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your contact phone number as a participant in the training session.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address as a participant in the training session.
* **Training Date**: Enter the date when you received the training.
* **Training Time**: Enter the time when you received the training.
* **Training Instructor**: Enter the name of the instructor who led the training session.
* **Training Location**: Enter the location where the training session took place.
* **Training Facility**: Enter the name of the facility where the training session occurred.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date you are releasing the participant to handle firearms.
* **Instructor Signature**: The instructor's signature confirms that you have completed the training and are authorized to handle firearms.
* **Participant Signature**: Your signature confirms that you have completed the training and are aware of your responsibilities and limitations as a firearms handler.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have filled out all required fields accurately and completely before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the training administration for assistance.
* Please note that this form is a requirement for all participants in the training program.
