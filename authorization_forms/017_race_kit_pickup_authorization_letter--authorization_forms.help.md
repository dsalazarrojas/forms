## Race Kit Pickup Authorization Letter - Help Guide

### Purpose

The Race Kit Pickup Authorization Letter form is used to obtain authorization for a Race Kit pickup. This form is typically used by athletes to grant permission for someone else to pick up their Race Kit on their behalf. The form includes event details, Race Kit pickup and return information, and signer details.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Select the Event Details from the list provided.
2. Enter the Event Name, Date of Event, and Time of Event.
3. Enter the Race Kit Details.
4. Enter the Pickup Location.
5. Enter the Race Kit Collection Date and Time.
6. Enter the Race Kit Return Location and Return Details.
7. Enter the Signer's Name, Date of Signature, Signature Type, and Signer's Signature.
8. Enter the Signer's contact information, including E-mail, Phone, Address, City, State, and Zip Code.
9. Review all entered information for accuracy.
10. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: Select the Event for which you are obtaining authorization.
	+ **Event Name**: Enter the name of the Event.
	+ **Date of Event**: Enter the date of the Event.
	+ **Time of Event**: Enter the time of the Event.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the Event.
* **Date of Event**: Enter the date of the Event.
* **Time of Event**: Enter the time of the Event.
* **Race Kit Details**: Enter a brief description of the Race Kit.
* **Pickup Location**: Enter the location where the Race Kit will be picked up.
* **Race Kit Collection Date**: Enter the date the Race Kit will be collected.
* **Race Kit Collection Time**: Enter the time the Race Kit will be collected.
* **Race Kit Return Location**: Enter the location where the Race Kit will be returned.
* **Return Details**: Enter any additional details about the return process.
* **Race Kit Signature**: Enter your signature confirming the Race Kit authorization.
* **Signer Name**: Enter the name of the person signing the authorization.
* **Date of Signature**: Enter the date the signature was made.
* **Race Kit Signature Type**: Select the type of signature (Hand Signed or E-Signed).
* **Race Kit Signer Signature**: Enter the signature of the person signing the authorization.
* **E-mail**: Enter the email of the person signing the authorization.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person signing the authorization.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the person signing the authorization.
* **City**: Enter the city of the person signing the authorization.
* **State**: Select the state where the person signing the authorization resides.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the person signing the authorization.
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

Note: Make sure to review all entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
