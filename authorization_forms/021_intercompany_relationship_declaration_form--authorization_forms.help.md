# Intercompany Relationship Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to declare an intercompany relationship between two or more companies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the form carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for related companies.
3. Choose the type of company (Company, Business, or Other) from the options.
4. If you have a relationship status other than "True" or "False", select "Other (please specify)" and provide a description.
5. If you have conflicts of interest, select "Yes" and provide details.
6. If you have conflicts of interest, but no details to provide, select "No".
7. Enter the date when the relationship started.
8. Enter the date when the relationship ended (if applicable).
9. Enter a description of the relationship.
10. Enter the name of the related company.
11. Enter the job title of the individual in the related company.
12. Enter the address of the related company.
13. Enter the contact number of the related company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **relatedCompany**: Select "Yes" if the company has a relationship with another company, "No" if not.
* **companyType**: Choose the type of company (Company, Business, or Other) that is related to this company.
* **relationshipStatus**: Select "True" if the relationship is active, "False" if not, or choose "Other (please specify)" if it's a different status.
* **relatedCompanyDescription**: Provide a description of the related company.
* **conflictOfInterest**: Select "Yes" if there is a conflict of interest, "No" if not.
* **conflictOfInterestDetails**: If you selected "Yes" for conflicts of interest, provide details about the conflict.
* **dateOfStart**: Enter the date when the relationship started.
* **endDate**: Enter the date when the relationship ended (if applicable).
* **description**: Enter a description of the relationship.
* **companyName**: Enter the name of the related company.
* **jobTitle**: Enter the job title of the individual in the related company.
* **companyAddress**: Enter the address of the related company.
* **contactNumber**: Enter the contact number of the related company.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all questions truthfully.
* If unsure about any question, ask for clarification.
* Double-check all information before submitting the form.
