# Vendor Banking Update Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Banking Update Consent Form is designed to collect essential information from vendors for banking updates. This form will help us understand and update your banking details accurately, ensuring efficient and secure financial transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your details for the following:
	* Vendor Details (Field 1)
	* Consent (Field 2)
	* Banking Update (Field 3)
	* Bank Name (Field 4)
2. Select your preferred banking update frequency:
	* Banking Update Date (Field 6)
	* Update Frequency (Field 7)
3. Choose your branch and account information:
	* Bank Branch (Field 8)
	* Account Number (Field 9)
	* Routing Number (Field 10)
	* Bank Address (Field 11)
4. Provide contact and communication information:
	* Contact Number (Field 12)
	* Email (Field 13)
	* Fax (Field 14)
	* Website (Field 15)
5. Confirm your prophet(s) selection:
	* Prophets (Field 5) and Confirm Prophets (Field 16)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Details (Field 1)**: Please enter your vendor information as per your vendor details.
* **Consent (Field 2)**: Provide your consent for banking updates.
* **Banking Update (Field 3)**: Describe the banking update you're submitting.
* **Bank Name (Field 4)**: Enter your bank name.
* **Prophets (Field 5)**: Select the prophet(s) you would like to include. (This field is for internal use; please only select the prophets relevant to your banking update.)
* **Banking Update Date (Field 6)**: Enter the date of your banking update.
* **Update Frequency (Field 7)**: Choose your preferred update frequency: Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Bank Branch (Field 8)**: Select your bank branch.
* **Account Number (Field 9)**: Enter your account number.
* **Routing Number (Field 10)**: Select your routing number.
* **Bank Address (Field 11)**: Enter your bank address.
* **Contact Number (Field 12)**: Enter your contact number.
* **Email (Field 13)**: Enter your email address.
* **Fax (Field 14)**: Enter your fax number.
* **Website (Field 15)**: Enter your website URL.
* **Confirm Prophets (Field 16)**: Confirm your selected prophet(s) to ensure accuracy.

## Tips
* Please ensure you've filled in all required fields.
* Review your form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your vendor representative for assistance.
