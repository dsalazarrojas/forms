# Attorney Substitution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the "Attorney Substitution Form" to ensure accurate and efficient submission.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all necessary information before starting the form.
2. Fill out the Case Information section with the case details:
   - **Case Name**: Enter the title of the case.
   - **Case Number**: Enter the court case reference number.
3. Select the **Case Type** from the provided options (e.g., Criminal, Civil, Family, etc.).
4. In the Current Attorney section:
   - **Current Attorney Name**: Enter the name of the withdrawing attorney.
   - **Bar Number**: Enter the withdrawing attorney's bar number.
   - **Attorney Phone**: Enter the withdrawing attorney's phone number.
   - **Attorney Email**: Enter the withdrawing attorney's email.
5. In the New Attorney section:
   - **Substitute Attorney Name**: Enter the name of the incoming attorney.
   - **New Attorney Bar**: Enter the new attorney's bar number.
   - **New Attorney Phone**: Enter the new attorney's phone number.
   - **New Attorney Email**: Enter the new attorney's email.
6. Select the **Reason for Substitution** from the provided options (e.g., Withdrawal, Client Request, Firm Change, etc.).
7. Enter the **Effective Date** of substitution.
8. In the Client Information section:
   - **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client or party.
   - Confirm **Client Consent to Substitution** (Client Consents, Client Does Not Consent, No Consent Required).
   - If required, enter the **Client Signature Date**.
9. Confirm the **Court Filing Date** for the substitution.
10. Add any additional **Additional Notes** if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Case Name**: Enter the title of the case.
* **Case Number**: Enter the court case reference number.
* **Case Type**: Select the nature of the case (e.g., Criminal, Civil, Family, etc.).
* **Current Attorney Name**: Enter the name of the withdrawing attorney.
* **Bar Number**: Enter the withdrawing attorney's bar number.
* **Attorney Phone**: Enter the withdrawing attorney's phone number.
* **Attorney Email**: Enter the withdrawing attorney's email.
* **Substitute Attorney Name**: Enter the name of the incoming attorney.
* **New Attorney Bar**: Enter the new attorney's bar number.
* **New Attorney Phone**: Enter the new attorney's phone number.
* **New Attorney Email**: Enter the new attorney's email.
* **Reason for Substitution**: Select the reason for the substitution (e.g., Withdrawal, Client Request, Firm Change, etc.).
* **Effective Date**: Enter the date the substitution takes effect.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client or party.
* **Client Consent to Substitution**: Confirm client consent (Client Consents, Client Does Not Consent, No Consent Required).
* **Client Signature Date**: Enter the date the client signed the authorization (if required).
* **Client Filing Date**: Enter the date the court files the substitution.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional relevant information.

**Tips**

- Please ensure all required fields are filled before submitting.
- Confirm with the client and withdrawing attorney before completing this form.
- Double-check all entered information for accuracy.
- Keep the form up to date with the latest information.
