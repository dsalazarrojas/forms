# Online Meeting Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Meeting Authorization Form is designed to gather essential information from participants before the meeting takes place. This form ensures that all attendees are aware of the meeting details and have agreed to the terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in.
3. Review the terms and conditions carefully and check the agreement box accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or ID.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your official business email address.

### Meeting Date
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the meeting.

### Meeting Time
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, `time`, required): Select the time of the meeting.

### Meeting Location
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, `text`, required): Enter the physical location of the meeting, if applicable.

### Participant Phone
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your direct phone number for emergency purposes.

### Participant Organization
* **Participant Organization** (`participant_organization`, `text`, required): Enter your organization name or affiliation.

### Meeting Duration
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, `text`, required): Estimate the duration of the meeting in minutes.

### Meeting Description
* **Meeting Description** (`meeting_description`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the purpose and objectives of the meeting.

### Terms and Conditions
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, required): Carefully read and acknowledge the terms and conditions.

### Agreement
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_one`, required): Check the box to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the meeting organizer or your supervisor for assistance.
- Review the terms and conditions carefully before agreeing to them.
