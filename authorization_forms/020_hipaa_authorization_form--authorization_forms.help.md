# hipaa_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HIPAA Authorization Form is used to obtain patient consent for the release of their medical records to a healthcare provider. This form is a crucial document that ensures the confidentiality and security of patient information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the healthcare provider to whom the records are to be released in the "Release to" field.
3. Enter the date when the patient's medical records can be released to the selected healthcare provider in the "Authorization Start Date" field.
4. Enter the date when the patient's medical records will no longer be released to the selected healthcare provider in the "Authorization End Date" field.
5. Choose the purpose for which the patient's records are being released in the "Purpose of Request" field.
6. Choose how the patient would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Fax, or Mail) in the "Patient Contact Method" field.
7. Sign the form electronically by clicking on the "Electronic Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the full name of the patient whose medical records are being accessed.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: Optional
  * Hint: Enter the patient's full name

* **Healthcare Provider**: Select the healthcare provider to whom the patient's medical records are being released.
  * Type: Select-one field
  * Required: Optional
  * Hint: Select a healthcare provider to receive medical information

* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
  * Type: Date field
  * Required: Optional
  * Hint: Enter the patient's date of birth

* **Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's medical record number.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: Optional
  * Hint: Enter the patient's medical record number

* **Release to**: Select the healthcare provider to receive medical information.
  * Type: Select-one field
  * Required: Optional
  * Hint: Select a healthcare provider to receive medical information

* **Authorization Start Date**: Enter the date when the patient's medical records can be released to the selected healthcare provider.
  * Type: Date field
  * Required: Optional
  * Hint: Enter the date when the patient's medical records can be released

* **Authorization End Date**: Enter the date when the patient's medical records will no longer be released to the selected healthcare provider.
  * Type: Date field
  * Required: Optional
  * Hint: Enter the date when the patient's medical records will no longer be released

* **Purpose of Request**: Choose the reason why the patient's medical records are being released.
  * Type: Select multiple field
  * Required: Optional
  * Hint: Select a reason for accessing the patient's medical records

* **Electronic Signature**: Sign the form electronically by clicking on the button.
  * Type: Select-one field
  * Required: Optional
  * Hint: Sign electronically with a mouse click

* **Signature Date**: The date on which the form is signed.
  * Type: Date field
  * Required: Optional
  * Hint: The date on which the form is signed

* **Signature**: The electronic signature of the patient.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: Optional
  * Hint: Enter the patient's electronic signature

* **Patient Contact Method**: Choose how the patient would like to be contacted.
  * Type: Select multiple field
  * Required: Optional
  * Hint: Choose a contact method for the patient

* **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: Optional
  * Hint: Enter the patient's phone number

* **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
  * Type: Email field
  * Required: Optional
  * Hint: Enter the patient's email address
