# Waste Management Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to release waste management records for a specific business or organization. If you are a representative of a business or organization with a waste management account, you are required to complete this form to indicate that you are authorized to release such records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the "Release Waste Management Records" option that applies to you.
2.  Enter your business name as it is registered with our organization.
3.  Enter your business address where we can send correspondence.
4.  Choose a date for the release of records.
5.  If your business has a phone number, enter it so we can contact you.
6.  If your business has an email address, enter it so we can contact you.
7.  Sign below to confirm that you are authorized to release waste management records for your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Release Waste Management Records** (`release_waste_management_records`, select_one, required): Select "True" to indicate that you are authorized to release waste management records for your business or organization.
*   **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your business name as it is registered with our organization.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your business address where we can send correspondence.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, required): Choose a date for the release of records.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If your business has a phone number, enter it so we can contact you.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If your business has an email address, enter it so we can contact you.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required): Sign below to confirm that you are authorized to release waste management records for your business.
