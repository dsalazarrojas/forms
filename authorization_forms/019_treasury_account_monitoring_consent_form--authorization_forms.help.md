# Treasury Account Monitoring Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Account Monitoring Consent Form is used to collect information from clients regarding their authorization to access and monitor their treasury accounts. This form ensures that clients understand and agree to the scope of their account monitoring, and provides a formal record of their consent.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the **Client Name** field.
2. Provide the client's address in the **Client Address** field.
3. Enter the client's phone number in the **Client Phone** field.
4. Select a date in the **Date** field to sign the consent agreement.
5. In the **Scope of Authorization** field, describe the specific accounts that you are authorized to monitor.
6. If applicable, select all representatives who have been authorized to access the client's accounts in the **Authorized Representatives** field.
7. In the **Client Consent** field, type a statement indicating that the client consents to the monitoring of their accounts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter the client's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's address where they can be contacted.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's phone number where they can be contacted.
* **Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, optional): Select a date to sign the consent agreement. This date will be used to record the client's consent.
* **Scope of Authorization** (`scope_of_authorization`, `text`, optional): Describe the specific accounts that you are authorized to monitor.
* **Authorized Representatives** (`authorized_representatives`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check all representatives who have been authorized to access the client's accounts.
* **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, `text`, optional): Type a statement indicating that the client consents to the monitoring of their accounts.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have obtained a signed consent from the client before proceeding with account monitoring.
- Review the scope of authorization carefully to ensure it aligns with your monitoring needs.
- If the client has multiple authorized representatives, ensure that all are listed in the **Authorized Representatives** field.
