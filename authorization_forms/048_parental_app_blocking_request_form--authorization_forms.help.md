# Parental App Blocking Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by parents or guardians to request blocking of specific applications on a child's device. Please use this form to provide the necessary information to help us process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of application you are requesting to block (e.g., Apple, Android, or None).
2.  Choose the reason why you are requesting to block the application (e.g., Inappropriate content, Excessive screen time, or Violates app terms).
3.  Enter your child's first name.
4.  Enter your child's last name.
5.  Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
6.  Enter your email address.
7.  Select the type of device your child uses (e.g., iOS, Android, or Windows).
8.  Enter your child's date of birth.
9.  Select the application(s) you are requesting to block (e.g., Fortnite, Minecraft, or None).
10.  (Optional) If you have a specific date by which you would like the application blocked, enter it in the "Block Until" field.
11.  Provide any additional notes or comments about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **App Block Request**: Select the type of application you are requesting to block. This will help us identify the correct application and process your request.
* **Block Reason**: Choose the reason why you are requesting to block the application. This will help us understand the context of your request.
* **Child First Name**: Enter your child's first name to help us identify them accurately.
* **Child Last Name**: Enter your child's last name to help us identify them accurately.
* **Parent Name**: Enter your name as the parent or guardian to confirm your identity.
* **Parent Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your request.
* **Device Type**: Select the type of device your child uses (e.g., iOS, Android, or Windows).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your child's date of birth to verify their age.
* **App Name**: Select the application(s) you are requesting to block. This will help us identify the correct application and process your request.
* **Block Until**: (Optional) If you have a specific date by which you would like the application blocked, enter it here.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **User Agreement**: This field is a confirmation of your understanding and agreement to the terms of the blocking request.
