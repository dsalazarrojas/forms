# COVID 19 Work Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from employees for COVID-19 work authorization purposes. It ensures that employees meet the necessary requirements and agree to work-related policies to minimize the risk of transmission and protect the well-being of everyone in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your employee details:
	* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
	* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee number or ID as assigned by your organization.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
2. Provide your contact details:
	* **Email Address**: Enter your work email address.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
3. Confirm your employment details:
	* **Department or Division**: Enter your department or division name.
	* **Position or Job Title**: Enter your job title.
4. Confirm your authorization status:
	* **Employment Type**: Select your employment type (Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, or Temporary).
5. Confirm your COVID-19 vaccination status:
	* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status**: Select your vaccination status (Fully vaccinated and boosted, Fully vaccinated, not boosted, Partially vaccinated, or Not vaccinated).
6. Confirm your work arrangement:
	* **Authorized Work Location**: Select where you are authorized to work (On-site only, Remote only, Hybrid arrangement, or Flexible arrangement).
7. Date of Authorization:
	* Enter the date you are providing this authorization.
8. Certification and Confirmation:
	* **I Certify That All Information is Accurate**: Confirm that the information provided is truthful.
	* **I Have Read and Understand This Authorization Form**: Confirm that you have read and understood the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee ID**: Your employee number or ID as assigned by your organization.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
* **Email Address**: Your work email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number.
* **Department or Division**: Your department or division name.
* **Position or Job Title**: Your job title.
* **Employment Type**: Your employment type (Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, or Temporary).
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status**: Select your vaccination status.
* **Authorized Work Location**: Where you are authorized to work.
* **I Am Eligible to Work On-Site**: Select whether you meet all work requirements.
* **I Have No COVID-19 Symptoms**: Select whether you have symptoms.
* **I Have Not Been Exposed to COVID-19**: Select whether you have been exposed.
* **I Am Not Under a Quarantine Order**: Select whether you are under a quarantine order.
* **I Agree to Follow Mask and Safety Policies**: Select whether you agree to work-related policies.
* **I Certify That All Information is Accurate**: Confirm that the information provided is truthful.
* **I Have Read and Understand This Authorization Form**: Confirm that you have read and understood the form.
