# music_licensing_authorization_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to seek authorization for music licensing, typically from music rights holders for commercial use of their music in various projects.

<thinking>
This form is intended to be used internally by music licensing teams to gather necessary information from clients or users who wish to use copyrighted music. The form is not designed for public submission and should only be used for official business purposes.</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information as best as you can.
2. Make sure to fill out all the fields accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review the music_licensing_agreement field carefully, as this is where you will select one or more of the licensing agreements that apply to your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **musician** (1): Enter the name of the musician or artist whose work is being licensed.
* **producer** (2): Enter the name of the producer or publisher associated with the music.
* **project_name** (3): Enter the name of the project that will be using the licensed music.
* **project_description** (4): Enter a brief description of the project that will be using the licensed music.
* **project_start_date** (5): Enter the start date of the project, if applicable.
* **project_end_date** (6): Enter the end date of the project, if applicable.
* **project_budget** (7): Enter the budget for the project, if applicable.
* **project_location** (8): Enter the location where the project will be recorded, if applicable.
* **project_contact** (9): Enter the contact information for the project, if applicable.
* **music_licensing_agreement** (10): Select one or more of the licensing agreements that apply to your project from the provided options.
* **music_licensing_terms** (11): Enter any specific terms or conditions that are relevant to the music licensing, if applicable.
* **music_licensee** (12): Enter the email address of the music license holder, if applicable.
* **music_license_email** (13): Enter the email address of the music license holder, if applicable.
