# Employee Travel Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request approval for employees to travel on business or official trips. It ensures that all travel plans align with company policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

### 1. Company

*   Fill in your company name, as it is required to identify your workplace.

### 2. Employee

*   Enter your employee name, as it is needed for employee identification.

### 3. Purpose

*   Describe the purpose of your trip, including the reason for travel, such as a conference, seminar, or audit/inspection.

### 4. Conference/Seminar

*   Select all the applicable options for the purpose of your trip, such as Conference, Seminar, or Audit/Inspection.

### 5. Travel Start Date

*   Enter the start date of your trip.
*   Format: MM/DD/YYYY

### 6. Travel End Date

*   Enter the end date of your trip.
*   Format: MM/DD/YYYY

### 7. Travel Duration

*   Enter the duration of your trip in days.

### 8. Location

*   Enter the location of your trip, including city, state, or region.

### 9. Travel Method

*   Select the mode of transportation you will be using, such as Air, Train, Car, or Other (Specify).
*   If Other (Specify) is selected, provide additional details in the Travel Details field.

### 10. Travel Details

*   Provide any additional details about your trip, such as flight numbers, meeting schedules, or accommodation information.

### 11. Approval Status

*   Select the current status of your travel request, such as Pending, Approved, or Denied.

### 12. Notes

*   Add any additional comments or explanations regarding your travel request.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your travel request is processed correctly.
*   Use the correct format for dates (MM/DD/YYYY) for fields 5 and 6.
*   If you select Other (Specify) for travel method, provide detailed information in the Travel Details field to facilitate processing of your request.
