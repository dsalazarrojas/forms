# ACH Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ACH Authorization Form is used to authorize electronic funds transfers between your bank and another financial institution. This form is essential for ensuring that your account holder information is accurately represented and that the authorization process is properly completed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for the account holder and bank.
2. Review the authorization details to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (2): Enter the full name of the account holder.
* **Email address** (3): Enter the account holder's primary email address.
* **Phone number** (4): Enter the account holder's phone number, including the country code if not from the US.
* **Bank name** (5): Enter the name of the bank or financial institution holding the account.
* **ACH routing number** (6): Enter the 9-digit ACH routing number without spaces or dashes.
* **Account number** (7): Enter the account number without spaces or dashes.
* **Account type** (8): Select the type of account (Checking, Savings, Business checking, or Other).
* **Authorization type** (10): Select the type of authorization (One-time debit or Ongoing recurring debit).
* **Authorized amount (USD)** (11): Enter the authorized amount in US dollars. Leave this field blank if the amount is open-ended.
* **Payment frequency** (12): Select the frequency of payments (Weekly, Biweekly, Semi-monthly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually) if applicable for recurring payments.
* **Effective date** (13): Enter the date when this authorization becomes effective.
* **Authorization start date** (14): Enter the date for recurring payments to start, or leave blank for immediate payments.
* **Authorization end date** (15): Enter the date when this authorization will end, or leave blank for indefinite authorization.
* **Remittance reference or invoice number** (16): Enter a reference or invoice number if applicable.
* **Authorized signatory name** (17): Enter the full name of the person authorizing the debit.
* **Signature method** (18): Select how the authorization is being provided (Electronic signature, Wet ink signature, or Phone authorization).
* **Authorization consent** (19): Select "I authorize" to confirm consent for ACH debits per the terms provided.
* **Additional comments or instructions** (22): Enter any additional comments or instructions for the bank or financial institution.
