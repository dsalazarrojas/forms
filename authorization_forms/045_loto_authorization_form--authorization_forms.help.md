# LOTO Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LOTO Authorization Form is a request form used to seek approval for maintenance operations from a higher authority. It gathers necessary information for maintenance authorization and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling in the facility information page (first page).
2. Enter the requestor's information.
3. Provide details about the equipment and tagout data.
4. Select the lockout information and reason.
5. Complete the maintenance details (lockout date, lockout time, tagout date, and tagout time).
6. Choose the maintenance type.
7. Enter the approval date and time.
8. Add any comments or comments as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page (Facility Information)**: Enter the facility name and address where the maintenance operation will take place.
* **Requestor Information**: Enter your name and title, along with your email address and phone number.
* **Equipment Information**: Provide details about the equipment undergoing maintenance.
* **Tagout Data**: Enter the necessary tagout data related to the equipment.
* **Lockout Information**: Select the lockout method to be used during the maintenance operation.
* **Lockout Reason**: Provide a brief reason for lockout.
* **Lockout Date**: Enter the date of the lockout.
* **Lockout Time**: Enter the time of the lockout.
* **Tagout Date**: Enter the date the equipment will be taken out of lockout.
* **Tagout Time**: Enter the time the equipment will be taken out of lockout.
* **Maintenance Type**: Select the type of maintenance to be performed.
* **Approval Date**: Enter the date when the approval was received.
* **Approval Time**: Enter the time when the approval was received.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or details as necessary.
* **Facility Comments**: Enter any comments related to the facility.
* **Requestor Comments**: Enter any comments from the requestor.
* **Maintenance Comments**: Enter any comments related to the maintenance operation.
* **Equipment Status**: Select the status of the equipment.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the information entered for accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant authority for clarification.
