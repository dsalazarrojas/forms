# Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) Authorization Form is used to authorize electronic funds transfers between accounts. This form ensures that all parties involved in the transfer have agreed on the terms of the transaction.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill in the details of the transaction.
3. Select the correct payment method.
4. Confirm the authorized amount.
5. Select the authorized by field.
6. Enter the account number and bank account number.
7. Enter the transfer amount and approval date and time.
8. Add any additional notes.
9. Enter the EFT reference number, transaction date and time, and transaction details.
10. Enter the authorization code, transfer type, and amount.
11. Select the status and authorization level of the transfer.
12. Enter the authorizer's name, title, email, phone number, and any additional remarks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) Authorization Form** (text): This field is the title of the form.
* **2. Date of Transaction** (date): Enter the date of the transaction.
* **3. Payment Method** (select multiple): Select the payment method used for the transaction.
* **4. Authorized Amount** (number): Enter the authorized amount for the transaction.
* **5. Authorized By** (select multiple): Select the authorized by field.
* **6. Account Number** (text): Enter the account number.
* **7. Bank Account Number** (text): Enter the bank account number.
* **8. Transfer Amount** (number): Enter the transfer amount.
* **9. Approval Date** (date): Enter the approval date of the transfer.
* **10. Approval Time** (time): Enter the approval time of the transfer.
* **11. Notes** (note): Enter any additional notes about the transaction.
* **12. EFT Reference Number** (text): Enter the EFT reference number.
* **13. EFT Transaction Date** (date): Enter the date of the EFT transaction.
* **14. EFT Transaction Time** (time): Enter the time of the EFT transaction.
* **15. EFT Transaction Details** (text): Enter any additional details about the EFT transaction.
* **16. EFT Authorization Code** (text): Enter the EFT authorization code.
* **17. EFT Transfer Type** (text): Select the type of EFT transfer.
* **18. EFT Amount** (number): Enter the EFT amount.
* **19. EFT Remarks** (text): Enter any additional remarks about the EFT transaction.
* **20. EFT Status** (select one): Select the status of the EFT transaction (Active or Inactive).
* **21. EFT Authorization Level** (select multiple): Select the authorization level of the EFT transfer.
* **22. EFT Authorizer Name** (text): Enter the name of the EFT authorizer.
* **23. EFT Authorizer Title** (text): Enter the title of the EFT authorizer.
* **24. EFT Authorizer Email** (email): Enter the email of the EFT authorizer.
* **25. EFT Authorizer Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the EFT authorizer.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Verify that the information is correct before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it for final approval.
