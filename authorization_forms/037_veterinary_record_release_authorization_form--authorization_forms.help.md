# Veterinary Record Release Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization for the release of veterinary records to a third-party individual or organization. It is a crucial document that ensures the pet owner's consent is obtained before releasing sensitive medical information. The form is typically used when a pet owner is unable to pick up their pet after a veterinary visit or when the pet needs to be transferred to another facility.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information (if you are the pet owner).
2. Select the authorized party (Client or Other (please specify)).
3. If you selected "Other (please specify)", enter the name of the authorized party.
4. Check the boxes for the conditions under which you are authorizing the release of your pet's records.
5. Enter any additional comments or details about the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Owner**: Please enter your name and contact information. This field is not required, but it's essential to have your contact details for communication purposes.
* **Authorized Party**: Choose between "Client" and "Other (please specify)" to indicate who you are authorizing to access your pet's records.
	+ If you select "Client", you are authorizing the veterinary clinic to release the records to the client directly.
	+ If you select "Other (please specify)", you will need to enter the name of the authorized party.
* **Authorized Name**: If you selected "Other (please specify)" for the authorized party, enter the name of the individual or organization you are authorizing.
* **Veterinary Clinic**: This field is optional, but if you are authorizing a clinic to release the records, enter their name or facility ID.
* **Reason for Transfer**: Enter a brief explanation for the reason behind the transfer of your pet's records.
* **Date of Transfer**: Enter the date when you would like the records to be transferred.
* **Time of Transfer**: Enter the time when you would like the records to be transferred.
* **Authorization**: Check the boxes for the specific conditions under which you are authorizing the release of your pet's records.
	+ Check "True" for each condition that applies to your pet's condition.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or details about the transfer, if necessary.

## Tips

* Make sure to carefully review the form and ensure you have provided accurate information before submitting it to the veterinary clinic.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, do not hesitate to ask the veterinary staff for clarification.
* This form is a one-time authorization, so ensure that the authorized party is aware that they are only authorized to access the records for the specified reason and date.
