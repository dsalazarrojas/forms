### monthly_payment_authorization_form - Help Guide

#### Purpose

This form is used for authorizing monthly payments for a specific service. 

#### How To Complete This Form

1. Select the monthly payment option that best describes the payment frequency for this service.
2. Choose the payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, or ACH).
3. Enter the amount of the recurring payment.
4. Enter the date of the payment.
5. Enter the end date of the payment.
6. Enter the authorized individual's name, email, phone number, and address.
7. Enter the authorized individual's title and email.
8. Enter the authorized individual's signature.
9. Enter the payment terms and the date of the payment terms.

#### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Monthly Payment Option** (`1`, `select_one`, required): Choose the monthly payment option that best describes the payment frequency for this service.
* **Member** (`2`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the member or client being authorized.
* **Service** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the service(s) being authorized for this payment.
* **Frequency** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Enter the frequency of the payment (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Amount** (`5`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of the recurring payment.
* **Date** (`6`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the payment.
* **End Date** (`7`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the payment.
* **Payment Method** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Choose the payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, or ACH).
* **Notes** (`9`, `note`, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the payment.
* **Email** (`10`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the authorized individual.
* **Phone** (`11`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the authorized individual.
* **Address** (`12`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the authorized individual.
* **City** (`13`, `text`, required): Enter the city of the authorized individual.
* **State** (`14`, `text`, required): Enter the state of the authorized individual.
* **Zip** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter the zip code of the authorized individual.
* **Country** (`16`, `text`, required): Enter the country of the authorized individual.
* **Authorization Date** (`17`, `date`, required): Enter the date when this authorization was given.
* **Authorized By** (`18`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who authorized this payment.
* **Authorized Title** (`19`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the person who authorized this payment.
* **Authorized Email** (`20`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the person who authorized this payment.
* **Authorized Phone** (`21`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the person who authorized this payment.
* **Authorized Signature** (`22`, `text`, required): Enter the signature of the person who authorized this payment.
* **Payment Terms** (`23`, `text`, required): Enter any special payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Terms Date** (`24`, `date`, required): Enter the date when these payment terms expire.
* **Submission ID** (`25`, `text`, required): Enter a unique ID for this payment authorization. 

Please note that all fields marked as required must be filled out for the form to be completed successfully.
