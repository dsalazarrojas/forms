# property_entry_keycard_activation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user consent and information for property entry keycard activation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your Last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select a date in the **Date Submitted** field.
6. Select a time in the **Time Submitted** field.
7. Check the box next to **I agree to the terms and conditions of this activation process** if you agree to the terms.
8. Enter any additional comments or remarks in the **Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`property_entry_keycard_activation_consent_form_first_name`, text, required): Your first name as per your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`property_entry_keycard_activation_consent_form_last_name`, text, required): Your last name as per your identification documents.
* **Email** (`property_entry_keycard_activation_consent_form_email`, email, required): Your email address to be used for communication about this property entry keycard activation.
* **Phone** (`property_entry_keycard_activation_consent_form_phone`, text, required): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions of this activation process** (`property_entry_keycard_activation_consent_form_agree`, select_one, required): Check this box to show you agree to the terms and conditions of this activation process.
* **Comments** (`property_entry_keycard_activation_consent_form_comments`, note, optional): Additional comments or remarks about this property entry keycard activation.
* **Date Submitted** (`property_entry_keycard_activation_consent_form_date_submitted`, date, required): The date you're submitting this form.
* **Time Submitted** (`property_entry_keycard_activation_consent_form_time_submitted`, time, required): The time you're submitting this form.
