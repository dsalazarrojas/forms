# Salary Adjustment Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Salary Adjustment Authorization Form is a template for requesting salary changes for an employee. This form helps guide HR or administrative personnel in collecting the necessary information for each request.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information for the employee, including their ID, salary, and other relevant details.
2. Fill out the request fields, including justifications and reasons for the salary change.
3. Select the approver and approver's contact information (name, email, and phone number).
4. Specify the salary change details, including the amount, frequency, and status.
5. Add any approval comments or notes as necessary.
6. Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **request salary change** (id: 1): A text field for briefly explaining the reason for the salary change request.
* **effective date change** (id: 2): A date field for specifying the date when the salary change should take effect.
* **justifications** (id: 3): A text field for explaining the reason or justification for the salary change request.
* **approver name** (id: 4): The name of the person authorizing the salary change, typically a manager or supervisor.
* **approver email** (id: 5): The email address of the approver for easy contact.
* **approver phone** (id: 6): The phone number of the approver for easy contact.
* **supervisor name** (id: 7): The name of the employee's supervisor or manager.
* **supervisor email** (id: 8): The email address of the supervisor for easy contact.
* **supervisor phone** (id: 9): The phone number of the supervisor for easy contact.
* **employee id** (id: 10): A unique identifier for the employee (e.g., employee ID number).
* **manager name** (id: 11): The name of the manager or team lead associated with the employee.
* **manager email** (id: 12): The email address of the manager for easy contact.
* **manager phone** (id: 13): The phone number of the manager for easy contact.
* **department** (id: 14): The department associated with the employee or team.
* **team** (id: 15): The team or group associated with the employee.
* **reason for adjustment** (id: 16): A text field for specifying the reason or justification for the salary change request.
* **salary change amount** (id: 17): The amount of the salary change.
* **approver** (id: 18): The name of the person authorizing the salary change (same as approver name).
* **reason for change** (id: 19): A text field for explaining the reason or justification for the salary change request.
* **employee salary** (id: 20): The current salary of the employee.
* **new salary** (id: 21): The new salary after the change.
* **salary change frequency** (id: 22): The frequency of the salary change (e.g., once, ongoing, semi-annual, quarterly, monthly).
* **salary change status** (id: 23): The status of the salary change request (pending, approved, denied, cancelled).
* **approval comments** (id: 24): A field for adding notes or comments from the approver.
* **notes** (id: 25): A field for adding additional comments or notes.
