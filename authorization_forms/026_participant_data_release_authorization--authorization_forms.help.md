# Participant Data Release Authorization - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Participant Data Release Authorization form is used to collect participant's consent for the release of their data to a third party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and any other personal details required.
2. Select the recipient to whom you consent to release your data.
3. Specify the purpose for which your data will be released.
4. Enter your email address for the recipient to contact you.
5. If you would like to be contacted, provide your phone number.
6. Select whether you have a relationship with the recipient (yes/no).
7. Provide a date for when you would like your data to be released (if applicable).
8. Select the release time (if applicable).
9. Confirm your consent to release your data (yes/no).
10. Add any additional comments or notes about the release of your data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_name** (text): Enter your name and any other personal details required.
* **recipient** (text): Select the person or organization to whom you consent to release your data to.
* **purpose** (text): Explain the reason for the data release, e.g., for research purposes only.
* **email** (email): Enter the email address of the recipient or a contact person.
* **participant_phone** (text): If you would like to be contacted, provide your phone number.
* **recipient_relationship** (select_multiple): Select whether you have a relationship with the recipient (yes/no).
* **release_date** (date): Provide a date for when you would like your data to be released (if applicable).
* **release_time** (time): Select the time for when you would like your data to be released (if applicable).
* **consent** (select_one): Confirm your consent to release your data (yes/no).
* **note** (note): Add any additional comments or notes about the release of your data.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have reviewed and understood the requirements for data release in your organization before completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask a data protection officer or administrator for guidance.
* Complete the form accurately and truthfully.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
