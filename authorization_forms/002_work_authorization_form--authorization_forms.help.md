# Work Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Work Authorization Form" is used to collect personal and work-related information from individuals seeking work authorization in a specific country.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Select your nationality from the dropdown list in the "Nationality" field.
5. Select the country where you are currently located from the dropdown list in the "Current Country" field.
6. If you have a visa, provide the details in the "Visa Details" field. This is optional.
7. Enter your work authorization number in the "Work Authorization Number" field.
8. Select the location where you will be working from the dropdown list in the "Work Location" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your nationality from the list (US, Canada, Mexico, UK, France).
* **Current Country** (`current_country`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the country where you are currently located from the list (US, Canada, Mexico, UK, France).
* **Visa Details** (`visa_details`, `text`, optional): If you have a visa, provide details about it.
* **Work Authorization Number** (`work_authorization_number`, `number`, required): Enter your work authorization number.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, `select_one`, required): Select the location where you will be working from the list (New York, Chicago, Los Angeles, Dallas, Houston).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth work authorization process.
* Double-check your selections and entries before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative for assistance.
