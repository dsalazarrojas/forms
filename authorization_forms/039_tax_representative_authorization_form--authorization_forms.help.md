# Tax Representative Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Representative Authorization Form is a document used to formalize the relationship between a taxpayer and their authorized representative. It ensures that essential information about the representative is captured in a standardized format for official record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your name as the taxpayer.
2.  Provide details about the tax representative, including their name, scope of work, start date, and end date.
3.  Specify your name, address, and phone number as the taxpayer.
4.  Provide your email address for official communication.
5.  Enter your representative's phone number and email address.
6.  Add any additional details about the representative, such as their address.
7.  Explain the reason for authorizing the representative.
8.  Specify the duration of the authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Tax Representative Name**: Enter the name of the tax representative who will be handling your tax matters.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
*   **Scope**: Specify the scope of work that the tax representative will be handling on your behalf.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
*   **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the tax representation agreement.
    *   Type: date
    *   Required: false
*   **End Date**: Enter the end date of the tax representation agreement.
    *   Type: date
    *   Required: false
*   **Taxpayer Name**: Enter your name as the taxpayer.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
*   **Taxpayer Address**: Enter your address.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
*   **Representative Phone**: Enter the representative's phone number.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
*   **Taxpayer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for official communication.
    *   Type: email
    *   Required: false
*   **Taxpayer Email**: Enter the representative's email address.
    *   Type: email
    *   Required: false
*   **Representative Address**: Enter the representative's address.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
*   **Reason for Authorization**: Explain why you are authorizing the tax representative.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
*   **Duration**: Specify the duration of the authorization.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
*   **Authorized By**: Enter the name of the person authorizing the tax representative.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
