# Financial Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Records Release Form is used to release financial records to a user or employee. This form ensures that users understand the implications of releasing sensitive financial records and that the release is authorized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "Release Type" field to indicate whether the financial records should be released or not.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the release period in the "Release Period Start" and "Release Period End" fields.
3. Provide your authorization and consent in the "Authorization" field.
4. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field (optional).
5. Sign and date the form with your "User Signature".
6. Enter the name of the authorized person or entity in the "Authorized By" field (optional).
7. Enter the date the form was approved in the "Date Approved" field.
8. Enter any additional approval codes or notes in the "Approval Code" and "Additional Notes" fields (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Release Type** (Release Type, Select One, Required): This field allows you to select whether the financial records should be released or not. Select "Yes" to release the records, and "No" to keep them confidential.
* **Release Period Start** (Release Period Start, Date, Required): Enter the start date of the release period. This date will determine when the financial records become available for access.
* **Release Period End** (Release Period End, Date, Required): Enter the end date of the release period. This date will determine when the financial records are no longer available for access.
* **Authorization** (Authorization, Text, Required): Enter your authorization to access and release the financial records.
* **Consent** (Consent, Note, Required): Provide your consent to release the financial records. This is a written acknowledgement that you understand the implications of releasing sensitive information.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, Text, Optional): Enter your contact information, such as email or phone number, for any follow-up or inquiries.
* **User Signature** (User Signature, Note, Required): Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement to release the financial records.
* **Authorized By** (Authorized By, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person or entity authorized to access and release the financial records (e.g., manager, HR representative).
* **Date Approved** (Date Approved, Date, Required): Enter the date the form was approved.
* **Approval Code** (Approval Code, Text, Optional): Enter any additional approval codes or notes.
* **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes, Text, Optional): Enter any additional information or notes about the release of financial records.
