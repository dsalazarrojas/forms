# Social Service Access Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Social Service Access Authorization Form, which is used to request access to social services on behalf of a client.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

*   Enter the client's permission in the **Client Permission** field. This field is required and should not be left blank.
*   Select the specific **Service Name** that the client requires access to. This field is required and should be selected from the options provided.
*   Enter the **Service Location** where the service will be provided. This field is not required but is recommended for accurate record-keeping.
*   Select the **Social Worker** responsible for the service. This field is required and should be selected from the options provided.
*   Enter the client's **Name**. This field is required and should be accurately filled out.
*   Enter the client's **Email**. This field is required and should be in a valid format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
*   Enter the client's **Phone Number**. This field is required and should be formatted as a phone number (e.g., `123-456-7890`).
*   Enter the **Social Service Organization** that will provide the service. This field is required and should be accurately filled out.
*   Sign your **Social Worker Signature** in the designated field. This field is required and should not be left blank.
*   Sign your **Client Signature** in the designated field. This field is required and should not be left blank.
*   Enter the **Authorization Date** when the service is authorized. This field is required and should be in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
*   Enter the **Authorization Time** when the service is authorized. This field is required and should be in the `HH:MM TT` format (e.g., `14:00 PM`).
*   Enter any **Comments** about the service authorization. This field is not required but is useful for record-keeping.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Permission**: A field for the client to indicate their permission for the social service.
*   **Service Name**: A field to select the specific social service the client requires.
*   **Service Location**: A field to enter the location where the service will be provided.
*   **Social Worker**: A field to select the social worker responsible for the service.
*   **Client Name**: A field to enter the client's name.
*   **Email**: A field to enter the client's email address.
*   **Phone**: A field to enter the client's phone number.
*   **Social Service Organization**: A field to enter the social service organization providing the service.
*   **Social Worker Signature**: A field for the social worker's signature.
*   **Client Signature**: A field for the client's signature.
*   **Authorization Date**: A field to enter the date of service authorization.
*   **Authorization Time**: A field to enter the time of service authorization.
*   **Comments**: A field for any additional comments about the service authorization.
*   **Email**: A field to enter the client's email address.
*   **Phone**: A field to enter the client's phone number.
