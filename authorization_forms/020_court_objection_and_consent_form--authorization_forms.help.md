# Court Objection And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Court Objection And Consent Form is a document used to gather essential details for a party's objection and consent in a court-related context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and contact number.
2. Provide the case reference number or unique identifier.
3. Write a brief statement explaining your position or objection in the **Written Statement** section.
4. If applicable, acknowledge any parties or individuals involved in the case in the **Acknowledgments** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, `required`): Fill in your name, email, and contact number.
- **Acknowledgments** (`Acknowledgments`, `text`, `false`): Acknowledge any parties or individuals involved in the case (if applicable).
- **Party Name** (`Party Name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your name.
- **Email** (`Email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address.
- **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, `number`, `required`): Enter your contact number.
- **Address** (`Address`, `text`, `required`): Enter your address.
- **Case Reference** (`Case Reference`, `text`, `required`): Enter the case reference number or unique identifier.
- **Written Statement** (`Written Statement`, `text`, `required`): Write a brief statement explaining your position or objection.
- **Contact Details 2** (`Contact Details 2`, `text`, `false`): This field is not required.
- **Acknowledgments 2** (`Acknowledgments 2`, `text`, `false`): This field is not required.
