# Return Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from customers who wish to return or exchange a purchased item. This form will help to initiate the return or exchange process, ensuring that the customer's concerns are addressed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
3. Double-check the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_info_form** (Text, required): This field is used to collect the customer's name and contact information. Please enter your full name and any other relevant contact information.
* **return_details_form** (Text, required): This field is used to provide a detailed description of the reason for the return or exchange. Be as clear and concise as possible to help us understand your concern.
* **shipping_details_form** (Text, required): This field is used to collect the shipping information related to the purchased item. This includes the order number, shipping address, and any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly to avoid any delays in the return or exchange process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* This form is intended to be used solely for the purpose of returning or exchanging a purchased item.
