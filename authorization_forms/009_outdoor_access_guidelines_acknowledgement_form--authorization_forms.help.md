# Outdoor Access Guidelines Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to ensure that users have read and understood the Outdoor Access Guidelines and are aware of their responsibilities while accessing outdoor areas. By filling out this form, users acknowledge their commitment to follow all applicable laws, regulations, and guidelines to maintain a safe and respectful environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the Outdoor Access Guidelines to ensure you understand the rules and responsibilities.
2.  Fill out each field carefully and accurately.
3.  Check that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
4.  Review the form carefully before submitting it to your supervisor/manager for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Outdoor Access Guidelines Acknowledgement** (Required): This is the introduction to the form, where you confirm that you have read and understood the Outdoor Access Guidelines.
* **What outdoor areas are you requesting access to?** (Required): Select all the outdoor areas you wish to access. Please be specific and accurate.
* **Date of Acknowledgement** (Optional): Enter the date you are submitting this form. If left blank, the date of submission will be considered as the current date.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your official email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **I acknowledge that I have read and understood the Outdoor Access Guidelines and I will comply with these guidelines.** (Required): Confirm that you have read and will abide by the guidelines.
* **I confirm that I am authorized to enter the outdoor areas I am requesting access to.** (Required): Acknowledge that you have the necessary permissions or clearance to access the outdoor areas.
* **I understand that failure to comply with the Outdoor Access Guidelines may result in denial of access.** (Required): Be aware that failing to follow the guidelines may result in restricted access.
* **I understand that this acknowledgement does not constitute a guarantee of access.** (Required): Recognize that this form does not guarantee your access to the outdoor areas.
* **I understand that this acknowledgement can be withdrawn at any time.** (Required): Understand that your access rights can be revoked at any point in time.
* **I understand that access to the outdoor areas is subject to change.** (Required): Acknowledge that access restrictions or permissions may be modified or revoked.
* **I will report any incidents or accidents to my supervisor/manager.** (Required): Commit to reporting any incidents or accidents to the relevant authorities.
* **I will comply with all applicable laws and regulations.** (Required): Confirm that you will adhere to all relevant laws and regulations.
* **I will respect the safety and well-being of all individuals.** (Required): Pledge to prioritize the safety and well-being of others while in the outdoor areas.
* **I will not bring any prohibited items into the outdoor areas.** (Required): Acknowledge that you will not bring any restricted or prohibited items into the outdoor areas.
* **I will not use electronic devices while in the outdoor areas.** (Required): Confirm that you will not use electronic devices in areas where they are prohibited.
* **I will not litter or tamper with the outdoor areas.** (Required): Pledge to maintain the cleanliness and integrity of the outdoor areas.
* **I will not enter the outdoor areas if I am feeling unwell.** (Required): Acknowledge that you will take care of your health and not access the outdoor areas if you are not feeling well.
* **I will not enter the outdoor areas if I am under the influence of alcohol or drugs.** (Required): Confirm that you will not access the outdoor areas while under the influence.
* **I will not enter the outdoor areas if I have a medical condition that may pose a risk to myself or others.** (Required): Acknowledge that you will not access the outdoor areas if you have a medical condition that may pose a risk.
* **I will not enter the outdoor areas if I am not authorized.** (Required): Confirm that you have the necessary permissions or clearance to access the outdoor areas.
* **I will not enter the outdoor areas if they are closed.** (Required): Acknowledge that you will respect closed areas and not attempt to access them.
* **I will not bring any pets into the outdoor areas.** (Required): Pledge to respect the outdoor areas' pet policy.
