# CRA Business Consent Authorization - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain business authorization from a representative who wishes to access your business information on the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure the representative is authorized to access your business information.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Select the level of authorization you wish to grant to the representative.
4. Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete by selecting "True" in the Declaration Signed section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Legal Name** (`business_legal_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your business as registered with the CRA.
* **CRA Business Number** (`business_number`, text, required): Enter your 9-digit CRA business number.
* **Business Physical Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter your business physical address.
* **City, Province, and Postal Code** (`city_province_postal`, text, required): Enter your city, province, and postal code.
* **Authorized Representative Name** (`representative_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the representative who is authorized to access your business information.
* **Representative Firm Name** (`representative_firm`, text, optional): If the representative is from a firm, enter the firm name. If not, leave blank.
* **RepID or GroupID** (`representative_id`, text, required): Enter the RepID or GroupID for the representative for CRA identifying purposes.
* **Level of Authorization** (`authorization_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of authorization you wish to grant to the representative, either Level 1 (View Only) or Level 2 (Update Access).
* **Authorized Program Accounts** (`program_accounts`, select_multiple, required): Select all accounts that you wish to authorize the representative to access.
* **Effective Date of Authorization** (`effective_date`, date, required): Enter the date from which the authorization is effective.
* **Expiry Date** (`expiry_date`, date, optional): Leave blank to indicate an indefinite authorization.
* **Declaration Signed** (`declaration_signed`, select_one, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete by selecting "True".
