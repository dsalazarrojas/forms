# sticker_usage_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sticker usage agreement form is a document used to manage user authorization to use company stickers. This form is essential for ensuring that users understand and agree to the conditions for sticker usage, approval, and renewal.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your basic information in the **User Information** section.
2. Provide details about your sticker usage in the **Usage Details** section.
3. Indicate if you need to place the sticker on a specific request in the **Sticker Placement Request** section.
4. Request approval for sticker usage in the **Approval Request** section.
5. Select the status of your approval in the **Approval Status** section.
6. Specify when you expect the sticker to be renewed in the **Renewal Request** section.
7. Choose the status of your renewal in the **Renewal Status** section.
8. Enter any relevant notes for the renewal in the **Renewal Notes** section.
9. Enter the date for the expected sticker renewal in the **Renewal Date** section.
10. Explain the reason for renewal in the **Reason for Renewal** section.
11. Describe the conditions for sticker renewal in the **Renewal Condition** section.
12. Select the status of your sticker in the **Sticker Status** section.
13. Enter the date for the expected sticker expiration in the **Expiration Date** section.
14. Enter the date for the approval in the **Approval Date** section.
15. Enter the person who approved the sticker usage in the **Approval By** section.
16. Explain the conditions for sticker approval in the **Approval Condition** section.
17. Describe the conditions for sticker usage in the **Usage Condition** section.
18. Enter any additional notes in the **Notes** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your basic information, such as name and contact details.
* **Usage Details** (`usage_details`, text, required/optional): Provide details about your sticker usage, such as where and when you plan to use the sticker.
* **Sticker Placement Request** (`sticker_placement_request`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if you need to place the sticker on a specific request.
* **Approval Request** (`approval_request`, select_one, required/optional): Request approval for sticker usage.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of your approval.
* **Renewal Request** (`renewal_request`, date, required/optional): Specify when you expect the sticker to be renewed.
* **Renewal Status** (`renewal_status`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the status of your renewal.
* **Renewal Notes** (`renewal_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant notes for renewal.
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date for the expected sticker renewal.
* **Reason for Renewal** (`reason_for_renewal`, text, required/optional): Explain the reason for renewal.
* **Renewal Condition** (`renewal_condition`, text, required/optional): Describe the conditions for sticker renewal.
* **Sticker Status** (`sticker_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of your sticker.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date for the expected sticker expiration.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date for the approval.
* **Approval By** (`approval_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the person who approved the sticker usage.
* **Approval Condition** (`approval_condition`, text, required/optional): Explain the conditions for sticker approval.
* **Usage Condition** (`usage_condition`, text, required/optional): Describe the conditions for sticker usage.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes.
