# Maintenance Device Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Device Tracking Consent Form is used to obtain user consent for tracking of their devices. It helps ensure that users have a clear understanding of what data is being collected and how it will be used.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your name in the **User Input** field. This is the name you want to be associated with your tracking records.
*   Enter your email address in the **User Email** field. This will be used to contact you regarding your tracking records.
*   Select your preferred **Tracking Method** from the available options. You can choose from the options provided in the dropdown list.
*   Select your preferred **Tracking Frequency**, which can be one or multiple options. You can choose from the options provided in the dropdown list.
*   Enter your preferred **Tracking Date** and **Tracking Time** for the tracking to occur. This will ensure that you are aware of when the tracking will take place.
*   Add any optional **Notes** you want to include in the form. This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional information.
*   Finally, submit the form by clicking the **Submit** button.
*   Select your consent for tracking by choosing from the **Consent** options provided in the dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User Input** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name as you want it to appear in your tracking records.
*   **User Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address as you want it to appear in your tracking records.
*   **Tracking Method** (`select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred method for tracking your device. Options include [Option 1, Option 2, Option 3].
*   **Tracking Frequency** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select your preferred frequency for tracking your device. Options include [Option 1, Option 2, Option 3].
*   **Tracking Date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date on which you want tracking to occur.
*   **Tracking Time** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time on which you want tracking to occur.
*   **Notes** (`note`, required: false): Add any additional information you want to include in your tracking records.
*   **Submit** (`text`, required: false): Click this button to submit your form and save your tracking preferences.
*   **Consent** (`select_one`, required: false): Select your consent for tracking by choosing one of the provided options. Options include [Option 1, Option 2, Option 3].
