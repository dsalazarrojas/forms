# Direct Deposit Account Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to select and verify the details of a direct deposit account for direct deposit purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are selecting the correct account details.
2. Select the relevant account type from the options provided (ACH, Direct Deposit, Wire Transfer, or Other).
3. Enter the bank account number and routing number for the selected account type.
4. Confirm whether the account holder is 'Yes' or 'No'.
5. Enter the account name and bank address if necessary.
6. Confirm the account type again from the options provided (ACH, Direct Deposit, Wire Transfer, or Other).
7. Enter the bank name and routing number for the selected account type, if different from the previous account.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Account Selection** (`account_selection`, select_multiple, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the account is to be used for direct deposit.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose from the options provided (ACH, Direct Deposit, Wire Transfer, or Other) to specify the type of account.
* **Bank Account Number** (`bank_account_number`, text, required: false): Enter the bank account number for the selected account type.
* **Routing Number** (`routing_number`, text, required: false): Enter the routing number for the selected account type.
* **Account Holder** (`account_holder`, select_multiple, required: false): Confirm whether the account holder is 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Account Name** (`account_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the account.
* **Bank Address** (`bank_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the bank.
* **Account Type 2** (`account_type_2`, select_one, required: false): Confirm the account type again from the options provided (ACH, Direct Deposit, Wire Transfer, or Other).
* **Bank Name** (`bank_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the bank.
* **Routing Number 2** (`routing_number_2`, text, required: false): Enter the routing number for the selected account type.

## Tips
* Double-check the account details for accuracy before submitting.
* Ensure that you have selected the correct account type and holder information.
* If you are unsure about any field, seek assistance from the support team.
* Review the form carefully to avoid duplication of effort and potential errors in processing.
