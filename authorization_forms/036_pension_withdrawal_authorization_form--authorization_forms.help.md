# Pension Withdrawal Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pension Withdrawal Authorization Form is used to request a pension withdrawal from your pension account. This form is to be completed by pension account holders who wish to withdraw their pension funds for a specific reason.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **User Information** page (page 1) with your personal details.
2. Proceed to the **Account Information** page (page 2) and enter your pension account details.
3. On the **Reason** page (page 3), select a reason for your pension withdrawal from the dropdown menu.
4. Provide more details about your reason on the **Reason Details** page (page 4).
5. Sign and date the **Signature** page (page 5) to authenticate the request.
6. Enter your contact information on the **Email** page (page 6) and your phone number on the **Phone** page (page 7).
7. Choose a withdrawal date on the **Withdrawal Date** page (page 8).
8. Enter the amount you wish to withdraw on the **Withdrawal Amount** page (page 9).
9. Select the reason for your withdrawal from the dropdown menu on the **Reason for Withdrawal** page (page 10).
10. On the **Authorization** page (page 11), select any necessary authorities to approve the withdrawal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (page 1)**:
	+ **User Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your pension account.
	+ **User ID**: Enter your pension account ID or member number.
* **Account Information (page 2)**:
	+ **Account Number**: Enter your pension account number.
	+ **Account Type**: Select your pension account type (e.g., lump sum, annuity, etc.).
* **Reason (page 3)**:
	+ Select a reason for your pension withdrawal from the dropdown menu.
* **Reason Details (page 4)**:
	+ Provide a brief explanation of your reason for the pension withdrawal.
* **Signature (page 5)**:
	+ Sign and date the form to authenticate the request.
* **Email (page 6)**:
	+ Enter your email address associated with your pension account.
* **Phone (page 7)**:
	+ Enter your contact phone number.
* **Withdrawal Date (page 8)**:
	+ Choose the date you wish to withdraw your pension funds.
* **Withdrawal Amount (page 9)**:
	+ Enter the amount you wish to withdraw from your pension account.
* **Reason for Withdrawal (page 10)**:
	+ Select the reason for your pension withdrawal from the dropdown menu.
* **Authorization (page 11)**:
	+ Select any necessary authorities to approve the withdrawal.
	+ Please note that this field is optional and depends on your pension plan.

## Tips

* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed correctly.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with your HR or pension administrator for clarification.
* Review and understand the purpose of each field before proceeding.
