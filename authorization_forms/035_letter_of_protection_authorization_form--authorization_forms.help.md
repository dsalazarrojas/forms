# Letter Of Protection Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Letter Of Protection Authorization Form is used to obtain authorization from patients for medical treatment or procedures. This form ensures that the healthcare provider has the necessary consent and information to perform the treatment or procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other required information in the relevant fields.
2. Select the authorized party from the options provided.
3. Enter any relevant insurance information, if applicable.
4. Enter the claim number, date of injury, and injury description in the relevant fields.
5. Select the authorization type from the options provided.
6. Enter any additional information or notes about the patient's visit or treatment.
7. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name, as it appears on their identification.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
- **Authorized Party**: Select the party that will be authorized to receive medical treatment or perform a procedure. This can be a family member, friend, or other authorized representative.
- **Insurance Provider**: If the patient has insurance, enter the name of their insurance provider.
- **Claim Number**: Enter the claim number, if applicable.
- **Date of Injury**: Enter the date of the injury or condition that requires treatment.
- **Injury Description**: Enter a brief description of the injury or condition.
- **Authorization Type**: Select the type of authorization required (e.g., surgery, medication, treatment).
- **Insurance Information**: If the patient has insurance, enter any additional information about their coverage or policies.
- **Medical Provider**: Enter the name and title of the healthcare provider performing the treatment.
- **Claim Status**: Select the status of the claim (e.g., pending, approved, denied).
- **Authorized By**: Enter the name of the person authorizing the treatment.
- **Date Signed**: Enter the date the form was signed.
- **Signature**: Sign and date the form to indicate consent.
- **Clinic**: Enter the name of the clinic or facility where the treatment will be performed.
- **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of the doctor performing the treatment.
- **Doctor Title**: Enter the title of the doctor performing the treatment.
- **Office Phone**: Enter the phone number of the healthcare provider's office.
- **Office Address**: Enter the address of the healthcare provider's office.
- **Fax**: Enter the fax number of the healthcare provider's office.
- **Email**: Enter the email address of the healthcare provider's office.
- **License Number**: Enter the license number of the healthcare provider.
- **Authorization Date**: Enter the date the authorization was given.
- **Authorization Expiration**: Enter the date the authorization will expire.
- **Notes**: Enter any additional information or notes about the patient's visit or treatment.
