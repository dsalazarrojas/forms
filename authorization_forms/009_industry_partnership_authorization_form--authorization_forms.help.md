This form is used to collect information about industry partnerships. It is intended for business partners to provide general information about themselves and their companies, as well as details about their contact information and agreement terms. The form is not meant to be a comprehensive business partnership agreement, but rather a way for the company to get to know the partner better and establish a foundation for potential collaboration. Please note that this form is not a legally binding document.

# industry_partnership_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about industry partnerships between the company and external business partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the General Information page with your company's name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your business partner's name and any other contact information on the Partner Information page.
3. On the Partner Information page, you can also specify the agreement terms that apply to your partnership, such as confidentiality or non-disclosure agreements.
4. Don't forget to include your email address so we can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (Text)**: This page is for you to provide a brief introduction about your company and any other relevant details.
* **Business Partner Name (Text)**: Please enter the name of your business partner or organization.
* **Partner Details (Text)**: Use this field to provide any additional details about your partner or organization.
* **Contact Info (Text)**: Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Email (Email)**: Please enter your email address. We will use this to communicate with you.
* **Agreement (Select One)**: Choose the type of agreement that applies to your partnership. You can select from the following options:
	+ Partner Agreement
	+ Confidentiality Agreement
	+ Non-Disclosure Agreement
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Partnership Terms (Text)**: Use this field to specify any specific terms of your partnership that are not covered by the selected agreement.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as accurately and completely as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
