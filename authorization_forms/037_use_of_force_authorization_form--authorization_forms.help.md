# Use Of Force Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Use Of Force Authorization Form" is used to document and authorize the use of force in various situations. It serves as a record of the incident and helps ensure that all necessary information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the User ID: This is your unique identification number assigned to you by your agency. If you do not know your User ID, please contact your supervisor or HR department.
2. Incident Date: Enter the date the incident occurred.
3. Time of Day: Enter the time the incident occurred.
4. Location: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
5. Force Used: Select the type of force used from the options provided.
6. Suspect Name: Enter the name of the suspect.
7. Suspect Address: Enter the address of the suspect.
8. Officer ID: Enter your unique identification number.
9. Supervisor Name: Enter your supervisor's name.
10. Supervisor Signature: This is your supervisor's electronic signature, which is used to approve the use of force.
11. Incident Number: Enter the incident number assigned to the case.
12. Form Submitted On: Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
13. Officer Notes: Enter any additional comments or notes about the incident.
14. Supervisor Notes: Enter any additional comments or notes from your supervisor.
15. Approved By: Select the person who approved the use of force from the options provided.
16. Approved Date: Enter the date the use of force was approved.
17. Force Authorization: Select the types of force authorization used from the options provided.
18. Officer Aware Of: Enter any information that you, as the officer, are aware of regarding the incident.
19. Supervisor Aware Of: Enter any information that your supervisor is aware of regarding the incident.
20. Incident Reported By: Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
21. Reported To: Enter the person or entity to whom the incident was reported.
22. Incident Type: Select the type of incident from the options provided.
23. Incident Details: Enter any additional details about the incident.
24. Officer Comments: Enter any comments or notes from you as the officer.
25. Supervisor Comments: Enter any comments or notes from your supervisor.
