# Decision Support Referral And Authorization Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Decision Support Referral And Authorization Request Form is used to request a referral and authorization from the support team, enabling efficient communication and ensuring that all parties are informed about the referral and its status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Info" section.
2. Select the support team's response to the referral in the "Support Team" section.
3. Enter the reason for the referral in the "Referral Reason" section.
4. Enter the date of the referral in the "Referral Date" section.
5. Select the status of the referral in the "Referral Status" section.
6. Enter the reviewer's name in the "Reviewer" section.
7. Select the support team's response to the referral in the "Support Team" section.
8. Enter the patient's email address in the "Prophets Email" section.
9. Enter the support team's phone number in the "Support Phone" section.
10. Enter the date of the review in the "Review Date" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_info** (optional): Enter the patient's relevant information, such as their name, date of birth, or medical history.
* **support_team** (required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the support team is involved.
* **referral_reason** (optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for the referral.
* **referral_date** (optional): Enter the date of the referral (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **referral_status** (optional): Select the current status of the referral (open, pending, closed).
* **reviewer** (optional): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **support_team** (required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the support team is involved.
* **prophets_email** (optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **support_phone** (optional): Enter the support team's phone number.
* **review_date** (optional): Enter the date of the review (MM/DD/YYYY).
