# Pharmaceutical Dispensing Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pharmaceutical Dispensing Authorization Form is used to collect information for the authorization of pharmaceutical dispensing. This form is used to verify the credentials of authorized prescribers and patients to ensure the legitimacy of the pharmaceutical supply chain and prevent misuse.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **patient_name** field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the **date_of_birth** field.
3. Select the authorized prescriber's ID from the **authorized_prescriber_id** field.
4. Enter the prescriber's name in the **prescriber_name** field.
5. Enter the prescriber's phone number in the **prescriber_phone** field.
6. Enter the prescriber's email address in the **prescriber_email** field.
7. Enter the prescriber's address in the **prescriber_address** field.
8. Enter the patient's address in the **patient_address** field.
9. Select the relevant medication(s) from the **medication_list** field.
10. Enter the date the authorization expires in the **authorization_expires** field.
11. Select the authorization status, either **Active** or **Inactive**, in the **authorization_status** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name** (text, required: false): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **date_of_birth** (date, required: false): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **authorization_date** (date, required: false): This field is likely a placeholder for the date the authorization was granted or last updated.
* **authorized_prescriber_id** (text, required: false): Enter the ID of the authorized prescriber.
* **prescriber_name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the authorized prescriber.
* **prescriber_phone** (text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the authorized prescriber.
* **prescriber_email** (email, required: false): Enter the email address of the authorized prescriber.
* **prescriber_address** (text, required: false): Enter the address of the authorized prescriber.
* **patient_address** (text, required: false): Enter the address of the patient.
* **medication_list** (select_multiple, required: false): Select the medication(s) to be dispensed using the options provided.
* **authorization_expires** (date, required: false): Enter the date the authorization expires.
* **authorization_status** (select_one, required: false): Select the status of the authorization, either **Active** or **Inactive**.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access and complete this form.
* Verify that the patient and prescriber information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review the medication list carefully to ensure the selected medications are authorized for dispensing.
* Enter the correct authorization expiration date to prevent unauthorized access.
* Select the correct authorization status to reflect the current state of the authorization.
