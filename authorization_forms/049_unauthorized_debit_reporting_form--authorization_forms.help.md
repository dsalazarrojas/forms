# Unauthorized Debit Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Unauthorized Debit Reporting Form is a form used to report unauthorized debits or suspicious transactions on an account. It is used by customers to provide information to the bank or financial institution to investigate and resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Ensure you are reporting an unauthorized transaction on your account.
3. Provide accurate and detailed information about the unauthorized transaction.
4. Answer questions about the account holder's authorized contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Account Number**: Enter the account number associated with the unauthorized transaction.
* **Account Type**: Select the type of account associated with the transaction (e.g., Checking, Savings, Credit).
* **Account Ownership**: Enter the ownership type of the account (e.g., Individual, Joint, Business).
* **Unauthorized Amount**: Enter the total amount of the unauthorized transaction.
* **Authorized Amount**: Enter the authorized amount for the account holder (leave blank if not applicable).
* **Unauthorized Date**: Enter the date of the unauthorized transaction.
* **Unauthorized Time**: Enter the time of the unauthorized transaction.
* **Unauthorized Description**: Provide a detailed description of the unauthorized transaction.
* **Unauthorized Recurring**: Indicate if the unauthorized transaction was a recurring or one-time transaction.
* **Suspicious Activity**: Indicate if you suspect any suspicious activity related to the account.
* **Authorized Description**: Provide a description of the authorized contact's relationship with the account holder.
* **Authorized Amount Other**: Enter any authorized amount that does not fit into the standard options.
* **Authorized Occupation**: Enter the authorized contact's occupation.
* **Account Phone**: Enter the authorized contact's phone number.
* **Authorized Address**: Enter the authorized contact's address.
* **Authorized Email**: Enter the authorized contact's email address.
* **Account Type Other**: Enter the type of account if it does not fit into the standard options.
* **Authorized Amount Other**: Enter any authorized amount that does not fit into the standard options.
* **Account Zip**: Enter the account's zip code.
* **Account Country**: Enter the account's country.
* **Account City**: Enter the account's city.
* **Account State**: Enter the account's state.
