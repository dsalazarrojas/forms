# do_not_resuscitate_dnr_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for obtaining authorization to do not resuscitate (DNR) a patient, ensuring their wishes are respected and rights are protected in the event of a medical emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Dnr Authorization` field with the patient's wishes regarding DNR.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the `healthcare provider` who will be responsible for the patient's care.
3. Enter the date when the DNR authorization will take effect (`Date Effective`).
4. Enter the date when the DNR authorization was issued (`Date Issued`).
5. Provide a brief explanation for the reason why the DNR authorization is requested (`Reason For Dnr`).
6. If applicable, enter the name and contact information of the `Legal Representative` who will be making decisions on the patient's behalf.
7. Have the patient sign the `Patient Signature` field to acknowledge their understanding and agreement with the authorization.
8. Have the `Legal Representative` sign the `Representative Signature` field to confirm their understanding and agreement with the authorization.
9. Finally, have the `Healthcare Provider` sign the `Healthcare Provider Signature` field to confirm their understanding and agreement with the authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dnr Authorization** (`dnr_authorization`, text, required): This field should be completed with the patient's wishes regarding DNR, which can be a specific set of instructions or any other relevant details.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the healthcare provider who will be responsible for the patient's care.
* **Date Effective** (`date_effective`, date, required): Enter the date when the DNR authorization will take effect.
* **Date Issued** (`date_issued`, date, required): Enter the date when the DNR authorization was issued.
* **Reason For Dnr** (`reason_for_dnr`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation for the reason why the DNR authorization is requested.
* **Legal Representative** (`legal_representative`, text, required): If applicable, enter the name and contact information of the Legal Representative who will be making decisions on the patient's behalf.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required): Have the patient sign this field to acknowledge their understanding and agreement with the authorization.
* **Representative Signature** (`representative_signature`, text, required): Have the Legal Representative sign this field to confirm their understanding and agreement with the authorization.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`healthcare_provider_signature`, text, required): Have the Healthcare Provider sign this field to confirm their understanding and agreement with the authorization.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the patient's wishes are respected and their rights are protected.
* Review the completed form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors.
* Store the form in a safe and secure location to prevent tampering or unauthorized changes.
