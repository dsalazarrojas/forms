# Travel Authorization Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request approval for travel on behalf of the employee. It ensures that all necessary procedures are followed and that the travel plans align with company policies.

## How To Complete This Form

### 1. Fill in your employee name and details
Fill in your full name as an employee in the "Employee Name" field.

### 2. Provide travel dates
Enter the date you plan to start and end your travel in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.

### 3. Specify travel details
Enter the destination and duration of your travel in the "Destination" and "Duration" fields, respectively. If you are traveling domestically or internationally, select the correct option in the "Mode" field.

### 4. Enter travel purpose
Explain the purpose of your travel in the "Purpose" field.

### 5. Submit your form
Click the submit button to send your form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as an employee.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you planned to start your travel.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to start your travel.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to end your travel.
* **Travel To** (`travel_to`, text, optional): Enter the city or location you are traveling to.
* **Destination** (`destination`, text, optional): Enter the city or location you are traveling to.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, optional): Enter the number of days for your travel.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, optional): Explain the reason for your travel.
* **Mode** (`mode`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether your travel is domestic or international.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to return from your travel.
* **Travel Document** (`travel_document`, text, optional): Attach any necessary travel documents, e.g. boarding passes, itineraries, etc.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your travel.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, optional): This field is automatically populated after approval or decline by the relevant authority.
* **Approval Comment** (`approval_comment`, text, optional): This field will be populated after approval or decline by the relevant authority.

## Tips

* Double-check your fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting your form.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or department head for clarification.
* This form is designed to guide you through the necessary steps for requesting travel approval. If you have any issues or questions, contact your supervisor or department head.
