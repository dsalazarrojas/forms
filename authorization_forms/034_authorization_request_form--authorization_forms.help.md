# Authorization Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request authorization for activities or procedures involving a minor or person under a parent/guardian's care. It ensures that the parent/guardian's understanding and consent are captured for each request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field carefully and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
3. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Date (1)**: Enter the date of the authorization request.
* **Type of Authorization Request (2)**: Select the type of activity or procedure being requested.
* **Subject Name (3)**: Enter the name of the minor or person under your care.
* **Subject Age or Date of Birth (4)**: Enter the age or date of birth of the subject.
* **Subject ID Number (5)**: Enter the subject's ID number if applicable.
* **Parent or Guardian Name (6)**: Enter your full name.
* **Relationship to Subject (7)**: Select your relationship to the subject, such as parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Address (8)**: Enter your current residential address.
* **Parent/Guardian Email (9)**: Enter your email contact information.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone (10)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Activity or Procedure Description (11)**: Provide a detailed description of the activity or procedure being requested.
* **Activity Date or Period (12)**: Enter the date or period when the activity or procedure will take place.
* **Activity Location (13)**: Enter the location where the activity or procedure will take place.
* **Duration of Activity (14)**: Enter the duration of the activity or procedure. (Optional)
* **Organization or Provider Name (15)**: Enter the name of the organization or provider involved.
* **Responsible Adult or Supervisor (16)**: Enter the name of the responsible adult or supervisor.
* **Risk and Liability Disclosure (17)**: Select whether you understand the risks and liability involved.
* **Special Instructions or Requirements (18)**: Enter any special needs or requirements for the activity or procedure.
* **Relevant Medical Conditions (19)**: Enter any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
* **Emergency Contact Name (20)**: Enter the name of an alternative contact if you are unavailable.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (21)**: Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Liability Waiver Agreement (22)**: Select whether you agree to the liability waiver.
* **Consent to Activity (23)**: Select whether you consent to the activity or procedure.
* **Duration of This Authorization (24)**: Select the duration of the authorization.
