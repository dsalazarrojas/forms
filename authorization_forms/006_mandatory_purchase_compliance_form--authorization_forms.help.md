# Mandatory Purchase Compliance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect mandatory purchase request information from requesters to ensure compliance with company policies and procedures. It gathers details about the purchase request, including the requester's information and the requesting manager's information. The form is intended to streamline the purchasing process and promote transparency and accountability.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
2. Enter the requester's details, such as name, email, phone number, and department.
3. Enter the requesting manager's details, including department and job title.
4. Select the correct category and type of purchase from the provided options.
5. Provide a brief description of the purchase and its current status.
6. Add any relevant notes about the purchase request.
7. Enter the submitting user's information, including department.
