# Digital Identification Card Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Identification Card Consent Form is a document that allows users to consent to the processing of their personal data for the purpose of issuing a digital identification card. It is an essential step in the process of creating a digital identification card, as it ensures that users are aware of and agree to the terms of their data handling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your user information (Page 1, user_info field).
2. Select your consent status from the options provided (Page 2, consent field).
3. If you wish to upload a photo, provide your photo information (Page 3, photo field).
4. Provide any additional details you'd like to share (Page 4, details field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (Page 1, user_info): Enter your name, email address, and other relevant details as prompted. This is an optional field.
* **Consent** (Page 2, user_consent): Select one of the options: "I agree", "I disagree", or "I decline". This field is not required.
* **Photo Upload** (Page 3, photo): Enter your photo information, including the photo itself. You can upload a photo from your computer or device. This field is not required.
* **Details** (Page 4, details): Provide any additional details you'd like to share about your identification card request. This field is not required.

## Tips
* Make sure to read and understand the terms of your data handling before making a decision.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
* Ensure that your photo is clear and legible for proper identification card processing.
