# Authority To Lodge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Authority to Lodge Form is used to grant authorized agents permission to act on behalf of the principal in specific matters. This form outlines the scope and limitations of the agent's authority, ensuring that both the principal and the agent understand their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your full name, address, phone number, and email address.
2. Select the relationship between you and the agent (e.g., family member, attorney, accountant, business partner).
3. Specify the type of authority you are granting to the agent (e.g., lodging applications, documents, permits).
4. Define the scope of authority, including any limitations or restrictions that apply.
5. Confirm that you have given your explicit consent to the agent acting on your behalf.
6. Specify the effective and expiry dates of the authority.
7. Choose the type of authority duration (e.g., indefinite, fixed period, single transaction).
8. Decide how the authority can be revoked (e.g., written notice, email notification, phone call).
9. Confirm your authorization for the agent to sign documents on your behalf.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, date, required): This is the date on which this authority begins.
* **Principal Full Name** (`principal_name`, text, required): Your full legal name.
* **Principal Address** (`principal_address`, text, required): Your residential address.
* **City** (`principal_city`, text, required): Your city of residence.
* **State or Province** (`principal_state`, text, required): Your state or province of residence.
* **Postal Code** (`principal_postal_code`, text, required): Your postal code.
* **Email Address** (`principal_email`, email, required): Your email contact information.
* **Phone Number** (`principal_phone`, text, required): Your phone contact number.
* **Agent Full Name** (`agent_name`, text, required): The name of the authorized agent.
* **Relationship to Principal** (`agent_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship between you and the agent (e.g., family member, attorney, accountant).
* **Agent Address** (`agent_address`, text, required): The agent's address.
* **Agent Phone** (`agent_phone`, text, required): The agent's contact phone number.
* **Agent Email** (`agent_email`, email, required): The agent's email address.
* **Type of Lodgement Authority** (`lodgement_type`, select_one, required): Specify the type of authority you are granting to the agent (e.g., applications, documents, permits).
* **Specific Application or Document** (`specific_application`, text, required): Specify what specifically the agent can lodge on your behalf.
* **Organization Where Lodging** (`organization_name`, text, required): The government agency or organization where the agent will be lodging on your behalf.
* **Scope of Authority** (`scope_of_authority`, select_one, required): Define the scope of authority, including any limitations or restrictions that apply.
* **Specific Limitations on Authority** (`authority_limitations`, text, optional): Specify any specific limitations on the authority.
* **Financial Authority Included** (`financial_authority`, select_one, required): Confirm whether the agent has full financial authority, authority for fees only, or no financial authority.
* **Agent Can Sign on Your Behalf** (`signature_authority`, select_one, required): Confirm whether the agent has authority to sign documents on your behalf.
* **Authority Effective Date** (`start_date`, date, required): The date on which this authority begins.
* **Authority End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): The date on which this authority expires.
* **Duration Type** (`duration_type`, select_one, required): Specify the type of authority duration (e.g., indefinite, fixed period, single transaction).
* **How Can Authority Be Revoked** (`revocation_procedure`, select_one, required): Decide how this authority can be revoked (e.g., written notice, email notification, phone call).
* **Principal Authorization and Consent** (`principal_authorization`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have given your explicit consent to the agent acting on your behalf.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you understand the scope and limitations of the agent's authority before granting it.
* Review the form carefully and ensure all fields are completed accurately.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of this form, please seek guidance from your authorized representative or a relevant authority figure.
