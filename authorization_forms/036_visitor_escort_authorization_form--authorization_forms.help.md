# Visitor Escort Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Visitor Escort Authorization Form" is used to authorize visitors to access certain areas of a facility. This form helps ensure that visitors are accompanied by authorized personnel, improving security and safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "First name", "Last name", and "E-mail" fields.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Select a date and time for the visit in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
4. Describe the purpose of your visit in the "Purpose" field.
5. Enter your escort's name and your relationship with them in the "Escort name" and "Relationship" fields.
6. Select the approval status of your request ("Approved", "Pending", or "Denied") in the "Approval status" field.
7. You can add any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field (if required).
8. Select the ID of the visitor you are authorizing access for in the "Visitor ID" field.
9. Select the ID of your escort in the "Escort ID" field.
10. Choose who created the form in the "Created by" field.
11. Select a date and time when the form was created in the "Created at" field.
12. (Optional) Choose who updated the form in the "Updated by" field.
13. (Optional) Select a date and time when the form was last updated in the "Updated at" field.
14. (Optional) Enter the name of the department or team your employee belongs to in the "Employee department" field.
15. (Optional) Enter the position held by the employee in the "Employee position" field.
16. (Optional) Select a date when the approval was granted in the "Approval date" field.
17. (Optional) Enter the name of the person who approved the request in the "Approved by" field.
18. (Optional) Select the time when the approval was granted in the "Approved at" field.
19. (Optional) Select the time when the approval was granted in the "Approval time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name (1, text, Required)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name (2, text, Required)**: Enter your last name.
* **E-mail (3, email, Required)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (4, text, Required)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date (5, date, Required)**: Select the date of your visit.
* **Time (6, time, Required)**: Select the time of your visit.
* **Purpose (7, text, Required)**: Describe the purpose of your visit.
* **Escort name (8, text, Required)**: Enter the name of your escort.
* **Relationship (9, text, Required)**: Describe your relationship with your escort.
* **Approval status (10, select_one, Required)**: Select "Approved", "Pending", or "Denied".
* **Comments (11, text, False)**: Add any additional comments or notes (if required).
* **Notes (12, text, False)**: Add any additional comments or notes (if required).
* **Visitor ID (13, number, Required)**: Select the ID of the visitor you are authorizing access for.
* **Escort ID (14, number, Required)**: Select the ID of your escort.
* **Created by (15, select_one, Required)**: Choose who created the form.
* **Created at (16, date, Required)**: Select a date and time when the form was created.
* **Updated by (17, select_one, False)**: (Optional) Choose who updated the form.
* **Updated at (18, date, False)**: (Optional) Select a date and time when the form was last updated.
* **Employee name (19, text, False)**: (Optional) Enter the name of the employee.
* **Employee department (20, text, False)**: (Optional) Enter the department or team of the employee.
* **Employee position (21, text, False)**: (Optional) Enter the position held by the employee.
* **Approval date (22, date, False)**: (Optional) Select a date when the approval was granted.
* **Approved by (23, text, False)**: (Optional) Enter the name of the person who approved the request.
* **Approved at (24, time, False)**: (Optional) Select the time when the approval was granted.
* **Approval time (25, time, False)**: (Optional) Select the time when the approval was granted.
