# Steel Erection Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization for steel erections on a construction project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Steel Erection Request**: This field is used to describe the steel erection request in detail. Please provide a brief description of the request, including the location and any relevant details.
2. **Request Date and Time**: Enter the date and time the request was made. This will help track the timeline of the authorization process.
3. **Supervisor Signature**: If you are a supervisor, please sign and date this field to indicate your review and approval of the request. Select "Yes" if you have reviewed and approved the request, or "No" if you need further information or clarification.
4. **Safety Approval**: Check the box next to "Yes" if you have reviewed the request from a safety perspective and approve it. If you are unsure or need further information, select "No".
5. **Site Supervisor Signature**: If you are the site supervisor, please sign and date this field to indicate your review and approval of the request, or select "No" if you are not responsible for this particular request.
6. **Safety Manager Signature**: If you are the safety manager, please sign and date this field to indicate your review and approval of the request, or select "No" if you are not responsible for this particular request.
7. **Contractor Name**: Enter the name of the contractor responsible for the steel erection work.
8. **Site Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the site supervisor responsible for the steel erection work.
9. **Safety Manager Name**: Enter the name of the safety manager responsible for the steel erection work.
10. **Project Manager Name**: Enter the name of the project manager responsible for the steel erection work.
11. **Date**: This field is used to enter the date the request was reviewed and approved.
12. **Date**: This field is used to enter the date the request was completed or resolved.

## Tips
* Make sure to clearly describe the steel erection request in the "Steel Erection Request" field.
* Double-check the "Request Date" and "Request Time" fields for accuracy.
* Ensure that all necessary parties have reviewed and approved the request by checking the "Supervisor Signature", "Safety Approval", "Site Supervisor Signature", and "Safety Manager Signature" fields.
