# out_of_network_coverage_exception_request - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to request an exception for a patient who is not covered by the insurance network. It is used when a patient's claim is denied due to lack of network coverage.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the patient is not covered by the network.
2. Enter the patient's name.
3. Enter the patient's phone number.
4. Enter the provider's name.
5. Enter the claim ID.
6. Select the start date of the coverage.
7. Select the end date of the coverage.
8. Enter the claimant's name.
9. Enter the claimant's phone number.
10. Select the type of claim.
11. Enter the reason for the claim.
12. Select the types of out-of-network claims.
13. Enter the claimant's address.
14. Enter the claimant's city.
15. Enter the claimant's state.
16. Enter the claimant's zip code.
17. Enter the claimant's email address.
18. Describe the claimant's relationship.
