# Delegation Of Online Services Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Delegation Of Online Services Form is used by delegates to request online services from the system. This form collects information about the delegate, services, and submission status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the title of the form, which is also the title of the form itself.
2. Enter the organization name, user name, and delegate email.
3. Select the services you are interested in.
4. Enter the delegate name, role, and phone number.
5. Provide a brief description of the services you are requesting.
6. Confirm your submission status and submission type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delegation Of Online Services Form** (`title`, text, required): Enter the title of the form, which is also the title of the form itself.
* **Organization** (`organization_title`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization you represent.
* **User** (`user_title`, text, required): Enter your name as the user.
* **Delegate Email** (`delegate_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as the delegate.
* **Select Services** (`service_select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Select the services you are interested in.
* **Service Select Multiple Options** (`service_select_multiple_options`, select_multiple, required): Select the options for the services you are interested in.
* **Selected Services** (`selected_services`, select_multiple, required): Select the services you are interested in (again).
* **Delegation Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date of delegation.
* **Expiration Date** (`consent_expiration_date`, date, required): Enter the expiration date of delegation.
* **Delegate Name** (`delegate_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the delegate.
* **Delegate Role** (`delegate_role`, text, required): Enter your role as the delegate.
* **Delegate Phone** (`delegate_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as the delegate.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the services you are requesting.
* **Services** (`services`, text, required): List the services you are requesting.
* **Delegate Notes** (`delegate_notes`, text, required): Provide any additional notes about the delegate.
* **Service Status** (`service_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the service you are requesting.
* **Service Status Options** (`service_status_options`, select_one, required): Select the options for the service status.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the submission.
* **Submission Status Options** (`submission_status_options`, select_one, required): Select the options for the submission status.
* **Submission Type** (`submission_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of submission.
* **Submission Type Options** (`submission_type_options`, select_one, required): Select the options for the submission type.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Select who submitted the form.
* **Submitted By Options** (`submitted_by_options`, select_one, required): Select the options for who submitted the form.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form was submitted.

## Tips

* Please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
* Make sure to select the correct services and options for each field.
* Double-check your submission status and submission type before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, contact your administrator for assistance.
