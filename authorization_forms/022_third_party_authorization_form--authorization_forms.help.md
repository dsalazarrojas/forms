**Third Party Authorization Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The Third Party Authorization Form is designed to collect information about a third party, allowing us to verify your authorization to act on their behalf.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about the third party.
2. Make sure to enter the correct information in each field, as indicated below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Third Party Name** (Third Party Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the third party, as it appears on their business card or letterhead.
* **Third Party Email** (Third Party Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the third party, if they have one.
* **Third Party Address** (Third Party Address, text, optional): Enter the address of the third party, including the street, city, province, and postal code.
* **Third Party City** (Third Party City, text, optional): Enter the city where the third party is located.
* **Third Party Province** (Third Party Province, text, optional): Enter the province or state where the third party is located.
* **Third Party Postal Code** (Third Party Postal Code, text, optional): Enter the postal code or zip code of the third party's location.
* **Third Party Phone** (Third Party Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the third party.
