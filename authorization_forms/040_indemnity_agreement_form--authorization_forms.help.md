# Indemnity Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Indemnity Agreement Form is used to document the terms and conditions of an indemnity agreement between two parties. This form helps ensure that both parties understand their responsibilities and liabilities in the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section of the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Fill in the transaction details, including the transaction date and time.
3. Identify the parties involved in the transaction (party A and party B).
4. Specify the responsibilities and liabilities of each party.
5. Determine the indemnity period and value.
6. Complete any additional fields as needed, such as legal coverage, signature, date signed, and contact information (email, phone number, fax number, address).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **transaction_details**: This field is used to enter a brief description of the transaction. Enter a clear and concise explanation of what was transacted.
* **party_a**: This field is used to enter the name of party A involved in the transaction. Enter the name of the party as it appears on their official documentation.
* **party_b**: This field is used to enter the name of party B involved in the transaction. Enter the name of the party as it appears on their official documentation.
* **responsibilities**: This field is used to specify the responsibilities of each party in the transaction. Enter a clear and concise explanation of what each party is responsible for.
* **liabilities**: This field is used to specify the liabilities of each party in the transaction. Enter a clear and concise explanation of what each party is liable for.
* **indemnity_period**: This field is used to specify the duration of the indemnity period. Enter the start and end dates of the indemnity period.
* **indemnity_value**: This field is used to specify the value of the indemnity coverage. Enter the monetary value of the coverage.
* **legal_coverage**: This field is used to specify any legal coverage or insurance policies in place.
* **transaction_date**: This field is used to enter the date of the transaction. Enter the date in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **transaction_time**: This field is used to enter the time of the transaction. Enter the time in the standard time format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **responsible_party**: This field is used to specify the party responsible for any liabilities or damages.
* **indemnity_agreement**: This field is used to enter the terms and conditions of the indemnity agreement.
* **form_signature**: This field is used for the signature of the party responsible for the transaction.
* **date_signed**: This field is used to enter the date the form was signed.
* **signature**: This field is used to enter the electronic signature of the party responsible for the transaction.
* **email_address**: This field is used to enter the email address of the party responsible for the transaction.
* **phone_number**: This field is used to enter the phone number of the party responsible for the transaction.
* **fax_number**: This field is used to enter the fax number of the party responsible for the transaction.
* **address_1**: This field is used to enter the primary address of the party responsible for the transaction.
* **address_2**: This field is used to enter the secondary address of the party responsible for the transaction.
* **city**: This field is used to enter the city of the party's location.
* **state**: This field is used to enter the state or region of the party's location.
* **zip**: This field is used to enter the zip code of the party's location.
* **country**: This field is used to enter the country of the party's location.
* **submit**: This field is used to submit the form and complete the transaction. Select "Submit" to complete the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure accurate information is entered.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the transaction details or contact the party responsible for the transaction.
* Complete the form as soon as possible to ensure timely completion of the indemnity agreement.
