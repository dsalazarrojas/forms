# 401(k) Plan Termination Distribution Request - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather essential information from employees who wish to initiate a 401(k) plan distribution due to termination of their employment. It guides the participant through the required steps and fields to accurately provide their details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form's instructions carefully before filling it out.
2.  Ensure that you have all necessary documents and information readily available.
3.  Fill in your personal details, such as name, social security number, date of birth, and email address.
4.  Provide accurate account information, including current account balance (USD) and employee ID number.
5.  Select your preferred distribution method and specify any additional information required.
6.  Confirm your eligibility for distributions and acknowledge potential early distribution penalties.
7.  Verify the accuracy of your beneficiary information.
8.  Sign and date the form, indicating that you certify the information provided.
9.  Add any additional comments or special instructions if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your account records.
*   **Social Security Number**: Enter your Social Security Number, last 4 digits only.
*   **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth.
*   **Email address**: Enter your current email address for important notifications.
*   **Phone number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
*   **Current mailing address**: Enter the address where you'd like to receive distribution documents.
*   **Employee ID number**: Enter your employee ID number.
*   **Date of initial plan participation**: Enter the date you first participated in the 401(k) plan.
*   **Current account balance (USD)**: Enter the balance as of the most recent statement.
*   **Preferred distribution method**: Select one of the available options (Lump sum cash payment, Rollover to another qualified plan, Rollover to IRA, Installment payments, Annuity purchase).
*   **Rollover destination (if applicable)**: Enter the name and address of the institution where you wish to rollover.
*   **Federal tax withholding percentage**: Enter the percentage, with a minimum of 20% for distributions over $200,000.
*   **State tax withholding**: Select whether you want to withhold state taxes, not withhold state taxes, or handle state taxes yourself.
*   **Eligible for distributions**: Confirm whether you are eligible for distributions under plan terms.
*   **Aware of potential early distribution penalties**: Confirm if you understand the potential penalties for early distributions.
*   **Beneficiary information is current**: Confirm if your beneficiary information is up-to-date on file with HR/Payroll department.
*   **Participant signature**: Sign the form, certifying the accuracy of the information provided.
*   **Date of signature**: Enter the date you sign the form.
*   **Additional comments or special instructions**: Enter any additional details that may be relevant for a smooth distribution process.
