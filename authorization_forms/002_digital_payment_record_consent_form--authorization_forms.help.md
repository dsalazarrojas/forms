# digital_payment_record_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for obtaining user consent for digital payment records. It gathers essential information to ensure a secure payment experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully before filling it out.
2. Enter your authorization details in the "authorization_details" field.
3. Provide your contact information in the "contact_details" field.
4. Choose your digital payment record consent type in the "digital_payment_record_consent" field.
5. Indicate your payment method in the "payment_method" field.
6. Add any additional comments or notes in the "additional_comments" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Authorization Details** (`authorization_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your authorization details, which are the credentials or approval codes required for digital payment processing.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, email, required/optional): Provide your email address so we can contact you about your digital payment record consent.
* **Digital Payment Record Consent** (`digital_payment_record_consent`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the provided options for your digital payment record consent.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether you will be paying digitally or not. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your digital payment record consent, if needed.
