# Background Screening Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain authorization from applicants for background screening services. It is used to collect necessary information for the screening process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in all the required fields (marked with *).
* Review and confirm that all information provided is accurate and complete.
* Sign the form to acknowledge that you have read and understand the information provided.
* Ensure that all relevant state-specific disclosures have been checked correctly.
* Review and acknowledge that you understand your electronic signature is legally binding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Applicant Full Name
* **Applicant Full Name**: Enter your full name as it is written on your identification documents.
* Required

### 2. Applicant Email Address
* **Applicant Email Address**: Enter your email address as it appears on your identification documents.
* Required

### 3. Screening Consent Statement
* **Screening Consent Statement**: Select the statement that best indicates your consent for the screening process. The options are:
	+ I give my authorization: You are authorizing the screening.
	+ Other: If other, please provide an explanation or reason for not authorizing the screening.
* Required

### 4. State Specific Rights
* **State Specific Rights**: If you reside in CA, MN, or OK, check here if you would like a copy of your report. This option is only applicable to the states mentioned.
* Required (only for residents in CA, MN, or OK)

### 5. FCRA Compliance Acknowledgement
* **FCRA Compliance Acknowledgement**: Acknowledge that you have read the Summary of Your Rights under the FCRA. The options are:
	+ I acknowledge: You are acknowledging that you have read the document.
	+ Other: If other, please provide an explanation or reason for not acknowledging.
* Required

### 6. Social Security Number for Verification
* **Social Security Number for Verification**: Enter your Social Security number as it appears on your identification documents.
* Required

### 7. Date of Birth (Identification Only)
* **Date of Birth (Identification Only)**: Enter your Date of Birth for identification purposes only.
* Required

### 8. Current Residential Address
* **Current Residential Address**: Enter your current residential address as it appears on your identification documents.
* Required

### 9. Digital Signature Acknowledgement
* **Digital Signature Acknowledgement**: Select the statement that best indicates your understanding of the electronic signature. The options are:
	+ I understand and agree: You are acknowledging that your electronic signature is legally binding.
	+ Other: If other, please provide an explanation or reason for not understanding.
* Required

### 10. Date of Authorization
* **Date of Authorization**: Enter the date on which you are authorizing the screening process.
* Required

### 11. Organization Requesting Screening
* **Organization Requesting Screening**: Enter the name of the organization requesting the screening.
* Optional

### 12. Authored Signature Date
* **Authored Signature Date**: This field is automatically filled out by the system.
* Not Applicable

Please note that some fields may have their own specific instructions within the form, so be sure to read each field carefully before submitting.
