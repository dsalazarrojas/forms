# Club Event Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Club Event Authorization Form is used to collect information about a club event, including its organizers, location, date, time, duration, expected attendees, event type, activities, and safety measures. This form is required for all club events and must be submitted for approval before the event can take place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information about the event organizer, including their name and contact email and phone number.
2. Enter the club name and its location.
3. Provide the event details, including its name, date, start time, duration, and expected number of attendees.
4. Select the type of event and describe the activities that will take place.
5. Indicate whether food and beverages will be served and whether alcohol will be involved.
6. Describe any potential safety concerns and the measures that will be taken to mitigate them.
7. Specify whether a liability waiver is needed.
8. Get approval from the club president by entering their name and approval date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer Name** (`organizer_name`, required): Enter the name of the person organizing this event.
* **Club Name** (`club_name`, required): Enter the name of the club that is hosting this event.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person for this event.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person for this event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, required): Enter the title of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, required): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event Duration - Hours** (`event_duration`, required): Enter the length of the event in hours.
* **Event Location - Venue** (`event_location`, required): Enter the location where the event will be held.
* **Expected Number of Attendees** (`expected_attendance`, required): Estimate the number of people expected to attend.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, required): Select the type of event (Social gathering, Educational workshop, Competition, Fundraiser, Meeting, Field trip, Other).
* **Description of Activities** (`activities_description`, required): Describe what will happen at this event.
* **Will food and beverages be served?** (`food_beverages`, required): Indicate whether food and beverages will be served.
* **Will alcohol be involved?** (`alcohol_involved`, required): Indicate whether alcohol will be involved in the event.
* **Potential Risks or Hazards** (`potential_risks`, required): Describe any safety concerns or hazards associated with this event.
* **Safety Measures in Place** (`safety_measures`, required): Describe the measures that will be taken to mitigate the potential risks or hazards.
* **Liability Waiver Needed?** (`liability_waiver`, required): Indicate whether a liability waiver is needed for this event.
* **Club President Name - Approval** (`club_president_name`, required): Enter the name of the club president approving this event.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, required): Enter the date when the approval was given.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, required): Select the status of the approval (Approved, Conditional approval, Requires modifications, Denied).
