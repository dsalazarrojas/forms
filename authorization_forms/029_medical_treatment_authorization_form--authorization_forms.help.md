# Medical Treatment Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for obtaining and documenting medical treatment authorizations. It captures essential details for medical treatments, including consent dates, subject information, medical provider information, and authorization details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand the required and optional fields.
2. Enter the Subject Name and Subject Contact information to identify the individual receiving medical treatment.
3. Fill in the Medical Provider and Medical Treatment fields with relevant details.
4. Obtain a valid Informed Consent, typically in the form of a signed document or electronic record.
5. Fill in the Medical Treatment Details field with specific information about the treatment.
6. If necessary, select an option for Authorized By.
7. Sign the form digitally to finalize the authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Treatment Authorization Form** (Medical treatment authorization form): This is the main form title for tracking medical treatment authorizations.
* **Consent Date** (Consent Date): Enter the date when the subject gave their informed consent for the medical treatment.
* **Subject Name** (Subject Name): Enter the name of the individual receiving medical treatment.
* **Subject Contact** (Subject Contact): Enter the contact information (e.g., phone number, email) for the subject.
* **Medical Provider** (Medical Provider): Enter the name of the medical provider administering the treatment.
* **Medical Treatment** (Medical Treatment): Enter a brief description or name of the medical treatment.
* **Informed Consent** (Informed Consent): Attach or paste a valid informed consent document or record for the subject.
* **Medical Treatment Details** (Medical Treatment Details): Add specific details about the medical treatment, such as dosage, duration, or frequency.
* **Signature** (Signature): Add a digital signature to finalize the authorization form.
* **Medical Treatment Authorized By** (Medical Treatment Authorized By): Select one of the options to indicate who authorized the medical treatment (e.g., parent, guardian, or individual).
* **Medical Treatment Authorized Date** (Medical Treatment Authorized Date): Enter the date when the medical treatment was authorized.
* **Medical Provider Signature** (Medical Provider Signature): Add a digital signature from the medical provider to confirm the treatment details.
* **Medical Provider Name** (Medical Provider Name): Enter the name of the medical provider.
* **Authorized By Title** (Authorized By Title): Enter the title of the person or entity that authorized the medical treatment (e.g., Parent, Guardian, or Healthcare Practitioner).
* **Authorized By** (Authorized By): Enter the name of the person or entity that authorized the medical treatment.
