# Pecuniary Interests Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a tool for employees to declare their pecuniary interests in a transparent and confidential manner. It helps to ensure that employees are aware of and disclose any potential conflicts of interest that may affect their work.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the **Option 1**, **Option 2**, or **Option 3** that best describes your pecuniary interests. You can choose one or multiple options.
2. Choose the **Period 1**, **Period 2**, or **Period 3** that applies to your declaration. You can only choose one period.
3. In the **Conflict of Interest Disclosures** field, please provide any relevant information about your pecuniary interests.
4. Indicate whether you have a **Pecuniary Interest Disclosure Declaration**. If yes, please specify.
5. Enter the **Date** of the declaration in the corresponding format (yyyy-mm-dd).
6. Enter the **Time** of the declaration in the 24-hour format (hh:mm am/pm).
7. In the **Employee Information** field, provide your name and contact details.
8. In the **Employee Position** field, describe your role and department within the organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3** (`pecuniary_interests_declaration_form`): Select the option(s) that best describes your pecuniary interests.
* **Period 1, Period 2, or Period 3** (`reporting_periods`): Choose the period that applies to your declaration.
* **Conflict of Interest Disclosures** (`conflict_of_interest_disclosures`): Provide any relevant information about your pecuniary interests.
* **Pecuniary Interest Disclosure Declaration** (`pecuniary_interest_disclosure_declaration`): Indicate whether you have a pecuniary interest disclosure declaration. If yes, please specify.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the declaration in the corresponding format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the declaration in the 24-hour format (hh:mm am/pm).
* **Employee Information** (`employee_information`): Enter your name and contact details.
* **Employee Position** (`employee_position`): Describe your role and department within the organization.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when describing your pecuniary interests and conflicts of interest.
* Make sure to choose the correct period and pecuniary interest declaration declaration options.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
