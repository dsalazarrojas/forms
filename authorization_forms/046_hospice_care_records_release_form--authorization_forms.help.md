# hospice_care_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to release patient records from a healthcare provider's care. 

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's social security number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, required/optional): The patient must sign this form to confirm their release of records.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`healthcare_provider_signature`, note, required/optional): The healthcare provider must sign this form to confirm release of records.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Reason for Release** (`reason_for_release`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable reasons for release.
* **Release To** (`release_to`, select_one, required/optional): Select the individual or entity authorized to receive the records.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Authorization to Access** (`authorization_to_access`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select whether the authorized individual or entity has access to the records.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the records are to be released.
* **Authorization Expiration Date** (`authorization_expiration_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the authorization expires.
* **Reason for Rejection** (`reason_for_rejection`, text, required/optional): Enter any reason for rejection of records.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Authorized Party** (`authorized_party`, select_one, required/optional): Select the authorized party.
* **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of authorization.
