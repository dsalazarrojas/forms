# payment_authorization_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for payment authorization to verify and validate payments made by customers or clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields to the best of your knowledge.
2. Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
3. Review each field carefully to prevent errors and ensure a smooth payment process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **title (1)**: Enter a brief title for the payment authorization form.
- **business_info (2)**: Provide information about the business or company making the payment.
- **contact_info (3)**: Enter details about the contact person or representative responsible for the payment.
- **payment_term (4)**: Specify the payment term or agreement relevant to the payment.
- **authorized_party (5)**: Identify the party or individual authorized to make the payment.
- **date_of_payment (6)**: Enter the date the payment is due or was made.
- **amount_of_payment (7)**: Enter the amount of the payment made or due.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field explanation above for guidance.
