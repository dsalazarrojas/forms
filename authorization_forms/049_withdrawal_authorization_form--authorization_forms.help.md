# withdrawal Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The **withdrawal Authorization Form** is a document used for authorizing a student's early departure from school due to unforeseen circumstances. This form is typically completed by the parent or guardian of the student and requires their signature of consent.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Parent or Guardian** section with your name and relationship to the student.
2. Select the **Relationship to Student** field to indicate your relationship to the student (e.g., Mother, Father, Grandparent, Guardian, or Other).
3. Enter the **Name**, **Student Date of Birth**, and **Student Contact Information** (email, phone, address) if applicable.
4. Provide a brief **Reason for Early Departure**.
5. Sign the **Parent or Guardian Signature** field to confirm your agreement.
6. If applicable, sign the **Teacher Signature** field to confirm the teacher's knowledge of the early departure.
7. Fill in the **School ID** and **School Name** fields, if applicable.
8. Verify the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent or Guardian**: Enter your name and relationship to the student.
* **Relationship to Student**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Mother
	+ Father
	+ Grandparent
	+ Guardian
	+ Other
* **Name**: Enter the student's full name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Student Contact Information**: Enter student email, phone number, and address if applicable.
* **Reason for Early Departure**: Enter a brief explanation for the student's early departure.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Provide your electronic signature to confirm your agreement.
* **Teacher Signature**: If applicable, provide a teacher's signature to confirm their knowledge of the early departure.
* **School ID**: Enter the school's identification number, if applicable.
* **School Name**: Enter the school's name, if applicable.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **Parent Contact Email**: Enter the parent contact email.
* **Parent Contact Phone**: Enter the parent contact phone number.
* **Student Address**: Enter the student's address.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the teacher's name.
* **Title**: Enter the teacher's title.
* **Email**: Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Signature 2**: Provide a teacher's signature to confirm their knowledge of the early departure.
* **Teacher**: Enter the teacher's name.
* **School Email**: Enter the school's email address.
