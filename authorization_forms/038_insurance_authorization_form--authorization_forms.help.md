# Insurance Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect authorization from a patient and their policyholder for medical treatment or services. It ensures that the patient and policyholder are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the treatment or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the patient's name, insurance company, and date of birth.
2. Enter the patient's address.
3. Fill in the policyholder's name, address, and phone number.
4. Enter the policyholder's email address.
5. Select the policyholder's relationship to the patient from the list provided.
6. Enter any additional relationship information if the policyholder relationship is "Other".
7. If necessary, provide any relevant medical information about the patient.
8. Both the patient and policyholder must sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (1): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Insurance Company** (2): Enter the name of the patient's insurance company.
* **Date Of Birth** (3): Enter the patient's date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Patient Address** (4): Enter the patient's home address.
* **Policyholder Name** (5): Enter the policyholder's full name.
* **Policyholder Address** (6): Enter the policyholder's home address.
* **Policyholder Phone** (9): Enter the policyholder's phone number.
* **Policyholder Email** (10): Enter the policyholder's email address.
* **Policyholder Relationship** (21): Select the policyholder's relationship to the patient from the list provided.
* **Policyholder Relationship (Other)** (22): If the policyholder's relationship is "Other", provide additional information about the relationship.
* **Patient Medical Condition** (23): If necessary, provide any relevant medical information about the patient.
* **Policyholder Agreement** (24): The policyholder must agree to the terms and conditions of the treatment or service.
* **Patient Agreement** (25): The patient must agree to the terms and conditions of the treatment or service.
* **Authorization Date** (7): Enter the date when the form is authorized.
* **Authorized By** (15): Enter the name of the person authorizing the form.
* **Authorized Title** (17): Enter the title of the person authorizing the form.
* **Authorized Phone** (18): Enter the phone number of the person authorizing the form.
* **Authorized Email** (19): Enter the email address of the person authorizing the form.
* **Authorized Signature** (20): The person authorizing the form must sign it.
* **Policyholder Signature** (14): The policyholder must sign the form.
* **Signature Date** (12): Enter the date when the form is signed.
* **Patient Signature** (13): The patient must sign the form.
