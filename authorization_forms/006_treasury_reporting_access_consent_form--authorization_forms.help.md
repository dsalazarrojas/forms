# treasury_reporting_access_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The treasury reporting access consent form is used to grant or deny reporting access to authorized personnel in the organization. It is a formal document that confirms or refuses reporting access, ensuring the integrity and confidentiality of sensitive reporting information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "User Info" section with your personal details.
2. Choose your reporting access level from the "Reporting Access" dropdown menu.
3. Select the reporting frequency from the "Reporting Frequency" dropdown menu (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
4. Enter your reporting date and time in the "Reporting Date" and "Reporting Time" fields, respectively.
5. Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field, if applicable.
6. Enter your email address and phone number in the respective fields.
7. Sign your name in the "Signature" field.
8. Confirm your reporting access in the "Confirm Reporting Access" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info (1)**: This section requires your personal details, such as your name and other identification information.
* **Reporting Access (2)**: Choose the level of reporting access you wish to grant or deny, such as Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3.
* **Reporting Frequency (3)**: Select the frequency at which you would like to report, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Reporting Date (4)**: Enter the date you would like to report, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Reporting Time (5)**: Enter the time you would like to report, in the format hh:mm AM/PM.
* **Notes (6)**: Enter any additional information you would like to include with your request.
* **Email (7)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for reporting purposes.
* **Phone Number (8)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for reporting purposes.
* **Signature (9)**: Sign your name to confirm your identity and consent.
* **Confirm Reporting Access (10)**: Choose whether you confirm or deny reporting access, Yes or No.
