# Work Location Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Work Location Authorization Form is used for requesting approval to use a work location for meetings, events, or other work-related purposes. It requires personal details, event details, and approval status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal details section with your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Enter your phone number and department information.
3. Provide your job title and manager's first and last name, email, and phone number.
4. Enter the start and end dates and times of the event, as well as the work location.
5. Enter the reason for the request and select the request type.
6. Choose the approval status and add any comments if necessary.
7. Select the approval date and time.
8. Choose who submitted the request (HR, Manager, or User) and when the request was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (Phone): Enter your phone number.
* **Department** (Department): Enter your department information.
* **Job Title** (Job Title): Enter your job title.
* **Manager First Name** (Manager First Name): Enter your manager's first name.
* **Manager Last Name** (Manager Last Name): Enter your manager's last name.
* **Manager Email** (Manager Email): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (Manager Phone): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Start Date** (Start Date): Enter the start date of the event.
* **End Date** (End Date): Enter the end date of the event.
* **Start Time** (Start Time): Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time** (End Time): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Work Location** (Work Location): Enter the work location.
* **Reason for request** (Reason for request): Enter the reason for the request.
* **Request Type** (Request Type): Select the type of request.
* **Approval Status** (Approval Status): Select the approval status.
* **Comments** (Comments): Add any comments if necessary.
* **Approval Date** (Approval Date): Enter the approval date.
* **Approval Time** (Approval Time): Enter the approval time.
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By): Choose who submitted the request (HR, Manager, or User).
* **Submitted Date** (Submitted Date): Enter the date the request was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (Submitted Time): Enter the time the request was submitted.
* **Submitted By Title** (Submitted By Title): Choose who submitted the request (CEO, Manager, Team, or User).

Please note that this form requires approval from a manager or other stakeholders before it can be submitted.
