# Frozen Semon Usage Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Frozen Semon Usage Agreement form is used to collect information for breeding purposes, such as details about the owner, recipient, and frozen semen usage. This form ensures accurate and efficient processing of relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section carefully, one at a time.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field according to your relevant information.
3. Enter accurate and clear information for all required fields.
4. Review your submission before finalizing it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Details** (1, text, required): Enter the details about the owner of the frozen semen, including their name and other relevant information.
* **Recipient Details** (2, text, required): Enter the details about the recipient of the frozen semen, including their name and other relevant information.
* **Breeding Details** (3, text, required): Enter details about the breeding process, including any relevant information about the frozen semen.
* **Breeding Schedule** (4, number, required): Select a number to represent the breeding schedule, which might be used to schedule the breeding process.
* **Collection Methods** (5, select_one, required): Select one of the available methods for collecting frozen semen, such as Frozen, Natural, or Artificial.
* **Breeding Location** (6, text, required): Enter the location where the breeding process will take place.
* **Breeding Date** (7, date, required): Enter the date of the breeding process.
* **Form Submission** (8, select_one, required): Select whether to Submit or Decline the form submission.
* **Form Submission Status** (9, text, required): Enter the status of your form submission.
* **Notes** (10, note, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the breeding process.
* **Email** (11, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (12, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can refer back to this guide for clarification.
- If you need further assistance, contact the relevant authorities or support team.
