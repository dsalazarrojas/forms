# Business Formation Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization for the formation of a new business entity.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Ensure that the form is signed by the authorizing party.
3. Date and sign the form to confirm authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Proposed Business Name**: Enter the name of the new business entity.
* **Type of Business Entity**: Select the type of business entity (e.g. Sole Proprietorship, General Partnership, etc.) that the new business will be.
* **Primary Business Purpose**: Describe the main activities and services of the business.
* **Proposed Principal Address**: Enter the main business location.
* **State of Incorporation**: Enter the state where the business will be incorporated.
* **Authorizing Party Name**: Enter the name of the person authorizing the formation of the business.
* **Title/Role**: Enter the title or position of the authorizing party.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the authorizing party.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the authorizing party.
* **Number of Owners**: Enter the number of owners of the business.
* **Owner Details**: Enter the names and ownership percentages of the owners.
* **Management Structure**: Describe how the business will be managed.
* **Fiscal Year End Date**: Enter the date when the fiscal year ends (if applicable).
* **EIN/Tax ID Application Status**: Select the status of EIN/Tax ID application.
* **Initial Capital Contribution**: Enter the total initial investment.
* **Sources of Capital**: Describe where the initial capital is coming from.
* **Articles of Organization prepared**: Select whether the formation documents are prepared.
* **I authorize the formation of this business entity**: Select whether you authorize the formation of the business.
* **Date of Authorization**: Enter the date when the form is signed.

Note: All fields marked as required must be completed in order to submit the form.
