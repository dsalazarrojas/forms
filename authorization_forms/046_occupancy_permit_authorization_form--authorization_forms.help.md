# Occupancy Permit Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information necessary to authorize the occupancy of a property. It requires the property owner's details, property address, and the date of inspection. The property inspector's approval is crucial to ensure that the property meets all the necessary standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Form Title** at the top of the page, which should be a brief description of the purpose of the form.
2. Provide the **Property Address** of the location you wish to authorize for occupancy.
3. Enter the **Property Owner Name** and **Property Owner Email** to confirm their identity.
4. If applicable, enter the **Date Moved In** when the occupant started inhabiting the property.
5. Provide the name of the **Property Inspector Name**, who conducted the inspection and gave approval.
6. Enter the **Property Inspection Date**, when the inspection took place.
7. Select the status of **Property Inspector Approval**, indicating whether the property meets all the necessary standards.
8. Sign the **Form Signature** to confirm that the property owner has reviewed and agreed to the terms.
9. Sign the **Property Inspector Signature** to confirm that they have inspected the property and approved it for occupancy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: A brief description of the form, which can be left blank or customized as needed.
* **Property Address**: Enter the exact address of the property you wish to authorize for occupancy. This is a required field to identify the property.
* **Property Owner Name**: Enter the name of the property owner, who is the person responsible for the property.
* **Property Owner Email**: Enter the email address of the property owner, for communication purposes.
* **Date Moved In**: If the property has been occupied, enter the date when the occupant started inhabiting the property. This is an optional field.
* **Property Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the person who conducted the inspection and gave approval.
* **Property Inspection Date**: Enter the date when the inspection took place.
* **Property Inspector Approval**: Select the status of approval from the dropdown menu. This field is required to confirm that the property meets all the necessary standards.
* **Form Signature**: The property owner must sign this field to confirm that they have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
* **Property Inspector Signature**: The property inspector must sign this field to confirm that they have inspected the property and approved it for occupancy.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the **Property Inspector Approval** status to ensure that it matches the inspection findings.
* Verify that the **Property Owner Signature** and **Property Inspector Signature** are genuine and valid.
* Keep the **Form Title** brief and descriptive to facilitate easy identification of the form's purpose.
