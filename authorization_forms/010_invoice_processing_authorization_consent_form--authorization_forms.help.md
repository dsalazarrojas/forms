# invoice processing authorization consent form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect authorization and consent from clients for invoice processing activities. 

It is used to ensure that the client understands and agrees to our invoice processing practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Authorization**: Type your name in this field to acknowledge that you understand and agree to our invoice processing authorization.
2. **Client Consent**: Select one of the options (Yes or No) to indicate whether you consent to our invoice processing practices.
3. **Email Address**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, which may be used to contact you regarding your invoices.
5. **Additional Notes (Optional)**: If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Authorization (Text)**: This field is required. Enter your name to acknowledge that you understand and agree to our invoice processing authorization.
* **Client Consent (Select One)**: This field is required. Select "Yes" if you consent to our invoice processing practices, or "No" if you do not.
* **Email Address (Email)**: This field is required. Enter a valid email address to allow us to communicate with you regarding your invoices.
* **Phone Number (Text)**: This field is required. Enter your phone number, which may be used to contact you regarding your invoices.
* **Additional Notes (Note)**: This field is optional. Enter any additional comments or requests you may have.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly. If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact us.
