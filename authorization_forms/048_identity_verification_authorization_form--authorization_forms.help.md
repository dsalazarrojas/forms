# identity_verification_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Identity Verification Authorization Form is used to verify the identity of clients and ensure they are authorized to access certain resources or systems. This form helps to confirm the client's information, their chosen verification method, and other necessary details to complete the authorization process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the client information field with the relevant details.
2. Select the verification method you would like to use to verify your identity.
3. (Optional) Upload a document to support your identity verification.
4. Confirm your identity with the client.
5. Confirm your identity with the user.
6. Upload your signature to complete the verification process.
7. Review the results of the verification process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (Page 1)** (`client_information`, text, required: false): Enter your client information, including any relevant details that will help us verify your identity.
* **Select Method (Page 2)** (`verification_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose the method you would like to use for identity verification (e.g., method 1, method 2, method 3).
* **Document Upload (Page 3)** (`document_upload`, note, required: false): (Optional) Upload a document to support your identity verification.
* **Confirm with Client (Page 4)** (`client_confirmation`, text, required: false): Confirm your identity with the client, ensuring we have their confirmation of your identity.
* **Confirm with User (Page 5)** (`user_confirmation`, text, required: false): Confirm your identity with the user, verifying that they acknowledge your identity.
* **Upload Signature (Page 6)** (`signature`, text, required: false): Upload your signature to complete the verification process.
* **Review Results (Page 7)** (`review_results`, note, required: false): Review the results of the verification process, ensuring everything is accurate and complete.
* **Submit (Page 8)** (`submit`, text, required: false): Submit the form once all necessary fields have been completed and reviewed.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and honestly.
* Select the most relevant verification method for your identity.
* Ensure that the client and user have confirmed your identity.
* Double-check your signature upload before submitting the form.
* Review the results carefully to ensure everything is complete and accurate.
