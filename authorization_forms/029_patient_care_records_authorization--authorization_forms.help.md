# patient_care_records_authorization - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient care records authorization form is used to grant access to a patient's care records for a specific purpose. This form ensures that the patient's data is not shared or used without their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Form Title** in the first field. This is the title of the authorization request.
2. Provide a brief description of the **Form Description** in the second field. This should explain the purpose of the authorization request.
3. Select the **Release Purpose** from the dropdown menu. Choose "For Research and Education", "For Treatment and Care", or "For Other" depending on the reason for accessing the patient's care records.
4. Enter the **Recipient Name** of the person or organization requesting access to the patient's care records.
5. If applicable, provide a brief explanation of the **Purpose of Use** in the fourth field.
6. If applicable, enter the **Duration of Use** in the time format (e.g., "10 months" or "1 year").
7. Optionally, provide **Contact Information** of the person or organization requesting access to the patient's care records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (text, optional): A brief title of the authorization request.
* **Form Description** (text, optional): A short description of the purpose of the authorization request.
* **Release Purpose** (select_one, required): Choose the reason for accessing the patient's care records (e.g., "For Research and Education", "For Treatment and Care", or "For Other").
* **Recipient Name** (text, optional): The name of the person or organization requesting access to the patient's care records.
* **Purpose of Use** (text, optional): A brief explanation of the reason for accessing the patient's care records.
* **Duration of Use** (time, optional): The length of time the patient's care records will be used (e.g., "10 months" or "1 year").
* **Contact Information** (text, optional): The contact information of the person or organization requesting access to the patient's care records.

## Tips
* Be sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure patient trust and confidentiality.
* Check if the form is already completed for the same patient and purpose before filling it out again.
* Make sure to keep the **Release Purpose** and **Contact Information** fields up-to-date and accurate.
