# Cultural Heritage Media Rights Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for requesting cultural heritage media rights, which is a type of permission to use or reproduce cultural artifacts, artworks, or other creative works that are protected by copyright or other intellectual property laws.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Enter your name and email address.
* Provide the name of the organization or institution you are representing.
* Enter the name of the work you are requesting rights for and its date.
* Briefly describe the work and your reason for requesting the rights.
* Enter the date you are requesting the rights.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter the name of the person submitting this request.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Organization**: If you are submitting this request on behalf of an organization, enter its name here. This field is optional.
* **Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the person who should be contacted for further communication.
* **Work Name**: Enter the name of the work or cultural artifact you are requesting rights for.
* **Work Date**: Enter the date of the work or cultural artifact.
* **Work Description**: Briefly describe the work or cultural artifact, including its creator or origin.
* **Request Reason**: Enter a brief explanation of why you are requesting the rights.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you are requesting the rights.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information for each field.
* Double-check your entries for correctness and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact us at form@gic.mx for clarification.
