# Utility Account Consolidation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about your utility accounts, which will be used for internal purposes by the utility company.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your First Name
2. Enter your Last Name
3. Enter your Email
4. Enter your Phone number
5. Enter your Address (line 1)
6. Enter your Address (line 2)
7. Enter your Address (line 3)
8. Enter your Date of birth
9. Select your Year of birth
10. Select your Month of birth
11. Select your Day of birth
12. Enter your Address (line 4)
13. Enter your Address (line 5)
14. Select which Utility account types you have (e.g., water, electricity, gas)
15. Select which other Utility account types you have (e.g., internet, cable)
