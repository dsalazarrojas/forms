# Paycheck Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paycheck Authorization Form is a document that grants permission for someone else to pick up a paycheck on behalf of an employee. This form is used to establish a secure and efficient process for paycheck pickup.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter First Name**: Enter the first name of the person picking up the paycheck in the `First name` field.
2. **Enter Last Name**: Enter the last name of the person picking up the paycheck in the `Last name` field.
3. **Select Date to Pick Up**: Select the date on which the paycheck is to be picked up in the `Date to pick up` field.
4. **Enter Employee ID**: Enter the Employee ID of the authorized employee in the `Employee ID` field.
5. **Select Relationship**: Select the relationship between the person picking up the paycheck and the employee from the available options in the `Relationship` field.
6. **Enter Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the authorized person in the `Phone` field.
7. **Enter Email**: Enter the email address of the authorized person in the `Email` field.
8. **Select Date of Authorization**: Select the date on which the authorization is effective in the `Date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`paycheck_authorization_form`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the first name of the person picking up the paycheck.
* **Last name** (`paycheck_authorization_form_first_name_hint`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter the last name of the person picking up the paycheck.
* **Date to pick up** (`paycheck_authorization_form_date_pick_up`, `date`, **Optional**): Select the date on which the paycheck is to be picked up.
* **Employee ID** (`paycheck_authorization_form_employee_id`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the Employee ID of the authorized employee.
* **Relationship** (`paycheck_authorization_form_relationship`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Select the relationship between the person picking up the paycheck and the employee from the available options.
	+ Authorized Spouse
	+ Authorized Parent
	+ Authorized Child
* **Phone** (`paycheck_authorization_form_phone`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter the phone number of the authorized person.
* **Email** (`paycheck_authorization_form_email`, `email`, **Optional**): Enter the email address of the authorized person.
* **Date** (`paycheck_authorization_form_date`, `date`, **Optional**): Select the date on which the authorization is effective.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.
* Note that some fields may be required or optional, depending on the specific situation.
* This form is used for the purpose of granting permission for someone else to pick up a paycheck, and any false or misleading information may lead to complications.
