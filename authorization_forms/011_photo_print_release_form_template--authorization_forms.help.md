# Photo Print Release Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Print Release Form Template is designed to be a straightforward way to collect necessary information for the print release of photographs from clients or individuals. This form will help ensure that you have their consent for the print release of their images, and it's easy to fill out and submit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Form Title**, which is a brief description of the form and your company name.
2. Enter your **Name**, which is your full name and contact information.
3. Provide the **Date** when the photographs were taken.
4. Specify the person who took the photographs, including their name and title (e.g., "John Doe, Photographer").
5. Enter the **Print Release Permission**, which is where you'll select the type of release you need: "Photo Release Form Template", "Authorization Forms", or "Photo Release Form Template - Other".
6. Add any additional **Notes** about the photographs or the shoot, if needed.
7. Check the box to confirm you have obtained the **Photo Release**.
8. Finally, add your **Print Release Permission**, which is the date when you plan to release the photographs for print.
9. Sign the **Client Agreement** with your client's signature, which is a requirement for the print release.
10. Add any **Form Notes**, which are additional comments or notes about the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: A brief description of the form and your company name.
* **Name**: Your full name and contact information.
* **Photo Taken**: Date when the photographs were taken.
* **Photo Taken By**: Name and title of the photographer who took the photographs.
* **Photo Taken At**: Time when the photographs were taken.
* **Client Agreement**: A requirement for the print release, which should be signed by the client.
* **Client Signature**: The client's signature confirming the agreement.
* **Form Notes**: Additional comments or notes about the form.
* **Photo Release**: A selection of the type of release you need: "Photo Release Form Template", "Authorization Forms", or "Photo Release Form Template - Other".
* **Print Release Permission**: Date when you plan to release the photographs for print.
* **Print Release Permission**: A required field where you'll specify the type of release needed.

Note: The form is designed to be flexible and adaptable, but please make sure to review the fields carefully before submitting the form.
