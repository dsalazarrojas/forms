# Device Access Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to company devices for employees. It ensures that employees understand and agree to the terms and conditions of device usage.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee information in the **employee_info** field. This will help us get your contact details and confirm your identity.
2. Select the type of device you need access to from the **device_type** dropdown menu. Choose from Laptop, Smartphone, or Tablet.
3. Indicate the level of access you need for the device by selecting the relevant options from the **access_levels** field. You can choose multiple levels of access, including Read, Write, and Admin.
4. Enter the date and time you would like the review to take place in the **review_date** and **review_time** fields. This will help us schedule the review at a time convenient for you.
5. Sign and date the **signature** field. This confirms that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of device usage.
6. Enter your signature and the current date in the **date** and **time** fields. These will be used for record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_info**: Enter your employee information here. This will help us get your contact details and confirm your identity.
* **device_type**: Select the type of device you need access to. This will help us determine what kind of device you require.
* **usage_agreements**: This is a note field where you can enter any additional agreements or notes about your device request.
* **access_levels**: Select the level(s) of access you need for the device. You can choose multiple levels of access, including Read, Write, and Admin.
* **review_date**: Enter the date you would like the review to take place. This will help us schedule the review at a time convenient for you.
* **review_time**: Enter the time you would like the review to take place. This will help us schedule the review at a time convenient for you.
* **signature**: Sign and date this field to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of device usage.
* **date**: Enter the current date to be used for record-keeping purposes.
* **time**: Enter the current time to be used for record-keeping purposes.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your device type and access levels before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or IT department for assistance.
* Once you have submitted the form, a review will be scheduled at the time specified in the **review_date** and **review_time** fields.
