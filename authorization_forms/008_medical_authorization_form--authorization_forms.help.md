# Medical Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about authorized medical providers and their authorization to provide medical care or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the authorized persons section by selecting "Yes" or "No" for the medical providers you are authorizing.
* Enter the medical condition or service being authorized in the text field.
* If you are an authorized medical provider, sign and date the form.
* Enter your name and address in the text fields.
* Provide your phone number and email address for contact purposes.
* Enter the date of authorization in the date field.
* If you are a medical provider, enter your license number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Authorized Persons** (`medical_authorized_persons`, Select Multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are authorized to provide medical care or services.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, Text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the medical condition or service being authorized.
* **Authorized Signature** (`medical_authorized_signature`, Note, required: false): Sign and date the form if you are an authorized medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Name** (`medical_provider_name`, Text, required: false): Enter your name as a medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Address** (`medical_provider_address`, Text, required: false): Enter your address as a medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Phone** (`medical_provider_phone`, Text, required: false): Enter your phone number as a medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Email** (`medical_provider_email`, Email, required: false): Enter your email address as a medical provider.
* **Medical Authorization Date** (`medical_authorization_date`, Date, required: false): Enter the date of authorization in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical Provider License Number** (`medical_provider_license_number`, Number, required: false): Enter your medical license number as a medical provider.

## Tips
* Be sure to enter accurate and complete information in all fields.
* If you are an authorized medical provider, make sure to sign and date the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification or seek guidance from a supervisor.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
