# Legal Document Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to authorize and track legal documents. It is used to provide a record of who has authorized a document, when it was authorized, and what its status is.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Client Name**, which is the name of the client or person associated with the document.
2. Choose the **Legal Document Type** from the options provided, which is the type of document being authorized.
3. Enter the **Document ID** if applicable, which is a unique identifier for the document.
4. Provide the **Purpose** of the document, which should be a brief description of why the document is required.
5. Enter the **Date Signed**, if applicable, which is the date the document was signed.
6. Enter the **Authorized By**, the person or entity authorizing the document.
7. Enter the **Date Authorized**, which is the date the document was authorized.
8. Enter the **Authorized By Email**, which is the email address of the person or entity authorizing the document.
9. Choose the **Document Status**, which indicates the current status of the document.
10. Add any **Notes** regarding the document, if necessary.
11. Enter the **Phone** number of the person or entity authorizing the document.
12. Enter the **Fax** number of the person or entity authorizing the document.
13. Enter the **Mail Address** of the person or entity authorizing the document.
14. Enter the **Street Address** of the person or entity authorizing the document.
15. Enter the **City** of the person or entity authorizing the document.
16. Enter the **State** of the person or entity authorizing the document.
17. Enter the **Zip** of the person or entity authorizing the document.
18. Enter the **Authorized By Phone** number of the person or entity authorizing the document.
19. If the document has a duplicate, enter the **Document ID 2**.
20. Enter the **Purpose 2**, which is an optional description of the document's purpose.
21. If the document has a duplicate, enter the **Date Signed 2**, which is the date the document was signed.
22. If the document has a duplicate, enter the **Authorized By 2**, which is the person or entity authorizing the document.
23. If the document has a duplicate, enter the **Date Authorized 2**, which is the date the document was authorized.
24. If the document has a duplicate, choose the **Document Status 2**, which indicates the current status of the document.
25. If the document has a duplicate, add any **Notes 2** regarding the document.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (1)**: Enter the name of the client associated with this document.
* **Legal Document Type (2)**: Choose the type of document being authorized.
* **Document ID (3)**: A unique identifier for the document, if applicable.
* **Purpose (4)**: Brief description of why the document is required.
* **Date Signed (5)**: Date the document was signed, if applicable.
* **Authorized By (6)**: Person or entity authorizing the document.
* **Date Authorized (7)**: Date the document was authorized.
* **Authorized By Email (8)**: Email address of the person or entity authorizing the document.
* **Document Status (9)**: Current status of the document.
* **Notes (10)**: Additional information or comments regarding the document.
* **Phone (11)**: Phone number of the person or entity authorizing the document.
* **Fax (12)**: Fax number of the person or entity authorizing the document.
* **Mail Address (13)**: Address of the person or entity authorizing the document.
* **Street Address (14)**: Street address of the person or entity authorizing the document.
* **City (15)**: City of the person or entity authorizing the document.
* **State (16)**: State of the person or entity authorizing the document.
* **Zip (17)**: Zip code of the person or entity authorizing the document.
* **Authorized By Phone (18)**: Phone number of the person or entity authorizing the document.
* **Document ID 2 (19)**: Unique identifier for the duplicate document.
* **Purpose 2 (20)**: Optional description of the document's purpose.
* **Date Signed 2 (21)**: Date the duplicate document was signed, if applicable.
* **Authorized By 2 (22)**: Person or entity authorizing the duplicate document.
* **Date Authorized 2 (23)**: Date the duplicate document was authorized.
* **Document Status 2 (24)**: Current status of the duplicate document.
* **Notes 2 (25)**: Additional information or comments regarding the duplicate document.
