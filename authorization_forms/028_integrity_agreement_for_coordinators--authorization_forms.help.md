# Integrity Agreement For Coordinators - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from coordinators about their name, title, organization, commitment, and acknowledgement. It is intended to establish a clear understanding of their role and responsibilities within the organization or project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and title in the respective fields.
2. Provide the name of the organization you are representing.
3. Describe your commitment to the project or organization.
4. Acknowledge the terms and conditions outlined in this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Name
* **Name** (required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents. This will be used to reference you within the project or organization.
### 2. Title
* **Title** (required): Enter your official job title or position within the organization or project.
### 3. Organization
* **Organization** (required): Enter the name of the organization you are representing. This could be your company, institution, or any other entity you are working on behalf of.
### 4. Commitment
* **Commitment** (required): Describe the specific commitment you are making to the project or organization. This should outline your responsibilities, expectations, and any other relevant details.
### 5. Acknowledgement
* **Acknowledgement** (required): Confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions outlined in this form. This is a critical section that ensures you are aware of your responsibilities and obligations.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure all fields are filled in, especially if marked as required.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, contact the administrators at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
