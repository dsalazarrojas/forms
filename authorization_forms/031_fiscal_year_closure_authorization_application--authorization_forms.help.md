# Fiscal Year Closure Authorization Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
The Fiscal Year Closure Authorization Application form is used to obtain authorization for the closing of the current fiscal year.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Field 1): This is your first name.
* **Last Name** (Field 2): This is your last name.
* **Employee ID** (Field 3): This is your employee ID, if required.
* **Department** (Field 4): This is your department, if required.
* **Manager ID** (Field 5): Select 'Yes' to authorize, 'No' to decline.
* **Date Requested** (Field 6): The date you are requesting the fiscal year closure.
* **Reason** (Field 7): The reason for requesting the fiscal year closure, if required.
* **Approver Comments** (Field 8): Additional comments or notes from the Approver, if required.
* **Approver Name** (Field 9): Select 'Yes' to authorize, 'No' to decline.
* **Approver Signature** (Field 10): If Approver Name is 'Yes', upload your signature as an image.
* **Approver Email** (Field 11): Not required, but can be filled if needed.
* **Approver Phone** (Field 12): Not required, but can be filled if needed.
* **Approver Title** (Field 13): Not required, but can be filled if needed.
* **Approver ID** (Field 14): Select 'Yes' to authorize, 'No' to decline.
* **Approver Date** (Field 15): Not required, but can be filled if needed.
* **Approver Notes** (Field 16): Not required, but can be filled if needed.
* **Approver Agreement** (Field 17): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate agreement.
* **Decline Reason** (Field 18): If Approver Agreement is 'No', enter the reason for decline.
* **Decline Notes** (Field 19): If Approver Agreement is 'No', enter additional notes.
* **Decline Comments** (Field 20): If Approver Agreement is 'No', enter additional comments.
* **Approver Signature Image** (Field 21): If Approver Agreement is 'Yes', upload your signature as an image.
* **Decline Reason Image** (Field 22): Not required, but can be filled if needed.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues with the authorization process.
* If you are unsure about any field, it's best to consult with your supervisor or HR department.
* Remember to review and submit the form carefully before finalizing it.
