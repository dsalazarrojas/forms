# Marketing Release Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Release Authorization Form is used to allow us to share your information with our marketing channels, such as social media, email campaigns, or other marketing activities. This form is optional, and you are not required to fill it out if you do not wish to participate in these activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Scroll down to the first page of the form, `marketing_release_authorization_form`.
2. In this section, simply type in your response to indicate whether or not you consent to share your information with our marketing channels. You can choose from one of the two options: "Do not let <assigned tool> use my data" or "Other".
3. If you select "Other", please provide a brief explanation in the `consent_term` section below.
4. In the `email` field, please enter your email address that you would like us to use for marketing purposes.
5. In the `phone` field, please enter your phone number that you would like us to use for marketing purposes.
6. Review and verify that your information is accurate before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **marketing_release_authorization_form** (`marketing_release_authorization_form`, text, optional): In this section, indicate whether or not you consent to share your information with our marketing channels. You can type in one of two options: "Do not let <assigned tool> use my data" or "Other". Please note that this form is optional, and you can simply leave this field blank if you do not wish to participate.
* **signature** (`signature`, select_one, optional): Please select whether or not you consent to share your information with our marketing channels. You can choose from one of two options: "Do not let <assigned tool> use my data" or "Other". If you select "Other", please provide a brief explanation in the `consent_term` field below.
* **consent_term** (`consent_term`, note, optional): If you select "Other" in the `signature` field, please provide a brief explanation of your reason for selecting this option in this section.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address that you would like us to use for marketing purposes.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number that you would like us to use for marketing purposes.
* **submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Review and verify that your information is accurate before clicking this button to submit the form.
