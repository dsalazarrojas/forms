# Direct Debit Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization from customers for direct debit payments. It collects information about the customer's bank account and payment preferences to facilitate direct transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name and account details in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the type of account (Checking, Savings, or Credit) and frequency (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly) of payments.
3. Choose the payment type (One-time, Recurring, or Scheduled).
4. Enter the start date and authorization amount for the direct debit payment.
5. Provide the bank's name, account number, and routing number for the customer's account.
6. Enter the customer's email and phone number for communication purposes.
7. Verify the entered information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, required): Enter the customer's name in this field.
* **Account Number** (account_number, text, required): Enter the customer's account number for direct debit purposes.
* **Routing Number** (routing_number, text, required): Enter the customer's routing number for direct debit purposes.
* **Bank Name** (bank_name, text, required): Enter the bank's name and branch.
* **Account Type** (account_type, select, optional): Select the type of account (Checking, Savings, or Credit) used for direct debit payments.
* **Start Date** (start_date, date, optional): Enter the start date of the direct debit payment.
* **Amount** (amount, number, optional): Enter the authorization amount for the direct debit payment.
* **Frequency** (frequency, select, optional): Choose the frequency of payments (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Payment Type** (payment_type, select, optional): Select the type of payment (One-time, Recurring, or Scheduled).
* **Bank Account** (bank_account, note, optional): Enter a note about the bank account used for direct debit purposes.
* **Customer Email** (customer_email, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (customer_phone, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Authorization Amount** (authorization_amount, number, optional): Enter the maximum amount authorized for direct debit payments.
* **Merchant Account** (merchant_account, text, optional): Enter the merchant account details (if applicable).
