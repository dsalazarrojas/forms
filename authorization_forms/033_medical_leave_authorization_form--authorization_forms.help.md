# medical leave authorization form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Leave Authorization Form is designed for employees to provide necessary information for a medical leave, which allows the employer to process the leave request and ensure a smooth return-to-work experience for the employee.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your name, reason for leave, and employee ID (if required).
2. Provide the dates when your medical leave will start and end.
3. Enter your physician's name and signature to confirm that the medical leave is required.
4. Enter the company name and any additional comments or details (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User** (User, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or employee database.
* **Reason** (Reason, text, optional): Explain the reason for your medical leave, e.g., illness, injury, or surgery.
* **Medical Certification** (Medical Certification, text, optional): Leave this field blank unless your doctor requires a specific medical certification.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, optional): Enter the date when your medical leave will begin.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, optional): Enter the date when you expect to return to work.
* **Physician Name** (Physician Name, text, optional): Enter your treating physician's name and title (e.g., MD, Dr.).
* **Physician Signature** (Physician Signature, text, optional): Have your physician sign this form to confirm the medical leave.
* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, optional): Enter your company's name or department name where you work.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, text, optional): Enter your employee ID, if required by your company or HR policy.

## Tips

- Please ensure you have your doctor's signature before submitting this form.
- If you have any supporting documentation or attachments, attach them as needed.
- Review your company's HR policies or consult with your HR representative for specific requirements and guidelines.
