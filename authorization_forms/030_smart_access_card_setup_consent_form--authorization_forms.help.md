# Smart Access Card Setup Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent and basic employee information for access setup purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select "Yes" or "No" to give your consent for access.
2.  If you select "Yes", please provide the reason for access in the text field below.
3.  Enter your signature to confirm your consent.
4.  Fill in your employee or visitor ID.
5.  Enter your name.
6.  Enter your title.
7.  Enter your division.
8.  Enter your department.
9.  Enter your building.
10. Enter your floor.
11. Enter your unit.
12. Enter your phone number.
13. Enter your email address.
14. Enter the start date of the access.
15. Enter the end date of the access.
16. Finally, sign the form to confirm your access.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Give Consent** (Select One):
	+ Select "Yes" to grant access or "No" to deny access.
* **Reason for Access** (Text):
	+ Please provide a brief explanation for why you need access.
* **Sign Here** (Note):
	+ Please sign this form to confirm your consent.
* **Employee or Visitor ID** (Text):
	+ Enter your employee or visitor ID number.
* **Name** (Text):
	+ Enter your name.
* **Title** (Text):
	+ Enter your title.
* **Division** (Text):
	+ Enter your division.
* **Department** (Text):
	+ Enter your department.
* **Building** (Text):
	+ Enter the building you are accessing.
* **Floor** (Text):
	+ Enter the floor you are accessing.
* **Unit** (Text):
	+ Enter the unit you are accessing.
* **Phone Number** (Text):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (Email):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Start Date** (Date):
	+ Enter the date you start accessing.
* **End Date** (Date):
	+ Enter the date you finish accessing.
* **Signature** (Note):
	+ Please sign this form to confirm your access.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide a valid reason for access if you select "Yes".
* Ensure your signature is legible and complete.
* Double-check your information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you need help, contact <form@gic.mx>.
