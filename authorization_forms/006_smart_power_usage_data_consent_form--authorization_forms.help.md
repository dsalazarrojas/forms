# Smart Power Usage Data Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Power Usage Data Consent Form is used to collect user consent for collecting and analyzing power usage data. This form informs users about the data collection process, its purpose, and the duration for which the data will be collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name or identifier in the "User" field.
2. Specify the duration for which you consent to collect and analyze your power usage data in the "Consent Duration" field.
3. Review and acknowledge each point in the "Acknowledgement" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User (text)**: Enter your name or identifier to be associated with the data collection.
* **Consent Duration (number)**: Specify the time period for which you consent to collect and analyze your power usage data.
* **Acknowledgement 1 (number)**: Acknowledge that you understand the data collection process.
* **Acknowledgement 2 (number)**: Acknowledge that you understand the purpose of the data collection.
* **Acknowledgement 3 (number)**: Acknowledge that you are aware of your rights to withdraw consent.
* **Acknowledgement 4 (number)**: Acknowledge that you understand how your data will be used.
* **Acknowledgement 5 (number)**: Acknowledge that you understand the data collection methods.
* **Acknowledgement 6 (number)**: Acknowledge that you understand the security measures to protect your data.
Note: The exact text for each acknowledgement may vary depending on the specific use case and jurisdiction. The provided explanations serve as a general guide.
