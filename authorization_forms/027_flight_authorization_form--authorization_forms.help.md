# Flight Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to request authorization for a flight. It is intended to gather information about the flight plan and any unusual circumstances that may affect the flight.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **flight_authorization_form** (text, **flight authorization form**, required): Enter a brief description of the flight plan, including the flight number, departure and arrival airports, and any other relevant details.
* **flight_plan** (text, **flight plan**, optional): Enter a detailed description of the flight plan, including any special instructions or considerations.
* **unusual_circumstances** (text, **unusual circumstances**, optional): If there are any exceptional or unplanned events that may affect the flight, such as weather conditions, aircraft issues, or air traffic control concerns, enter them here.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when filling out the flight authorization form to ensure accurate and efficient review and approval.
- Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals before completing this form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary information is included.
