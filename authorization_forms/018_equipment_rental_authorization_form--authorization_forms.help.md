# equipment_rental_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to authorize the rental of equipment. It is intended for use by authorized individuals or departments within an organization to manage equipment rentals within their premises.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Renter Information page with the necessary details about the person renting the equipment.
2. Provide the Equipment Details on the next page.
3. Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions.
4. Enter your contact information on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Renter Information** (`renter_info`, text, required: false): Enter the details of the person renting the equipment, including their name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`, text, required: false): Describe the equipment being rented, including its type and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your name, address, and phone number.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required: false): Read and agree to the rental terms and conditions by selecting this checkbox.
* **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant details as the person submitting this form.
* **Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please make sure to read and agree to the Terms and Conditions before submitting this form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant department or individual within your organization.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure it's accurate.
