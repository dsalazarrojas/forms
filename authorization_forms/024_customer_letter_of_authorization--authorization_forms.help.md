# Customer Letter Of Authorization - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Letter Of Authorization form is used to collect information about a customer for verification or identification purposes in various scenarios, such as in a business setting, a government agency, or a financial institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with the correct information about the customer.
2. Review and verify the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name (customer name)**: Enter the full name of the customer.
- **Customer Company (customer company)**: Enter the name of the company the customer is associated with.
- **Customer Title (customer title)**: Enter any relevant job title or position held by the customer.
- **Customer Address 1 (customer address 1)**: Enter the street address of the customer.
- **Customer Address 2 (customer address 2)**: Enter any additional address information if necessary.
- **Customer City (customer city)**: Enter the city where the customer is located.
- **Customer State (customer state)**: Enter the state where the customer is located.
- **Customer Post Code (customer post code)**: Enter the postal code or zip code of the customer's location.
- **Customer Email (customer email)**: Enter the customer's email address (must be in the correct format for email addresses).
- **Customer Phone (customer phone)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
## Tips
- Double-check for accuracy and completeness in the provided information.
- Make sure to format the email address correctly.
- Ensure the postal code entered is in the correct format (usually 5 digits in the US, or a similar format for other countries).
- If unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or department head for clarification.
