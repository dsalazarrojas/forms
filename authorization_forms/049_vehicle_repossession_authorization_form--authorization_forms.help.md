# Vehicle Repossession Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Repossession Authorization Form is a document that enables vehicle owners to provide necessary information about their vehicle and contact details for repossession purposes. This form is essential for the vehicle recovery process to proceed smoothly and securely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are not duplicating an existing form submission.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Enter the required information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Repossession Form** (`vehicle_repossession_form`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the reason for vehicle repossession.
* **Vehicle Form Data** (`vehicle_form_data`, text, required): Provide details about the vehicle being repossessed, including make, model, year, and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Form Data** (`contact_form_data`, text, required): Enter your name, address, and contact information for the repossession team to reach out to you.
* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`, text, not required): If applicable, provide an update on the vehicle's current status, such as "sold to a third party" or "impounded by another creditor".

## Tips
* Ensure you enter all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check for any errors or discrepancies before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the repossession team for assistance.
