# Claim Settlement Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Claim Settlement Authorization Form is a document used to formally grant authorization for claim settlement on behalf of the claimant. It is an important part of the claim process and ensures that the authorized representative's actions are legally binding and recognized by all parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please fill out the form completely and accurately with the provided information.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3.  If any field is unclear or requires further explanation, please contact the relevant authorities for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Policyholder name** (`policyholder_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the full name of the claimant as it appears on their policy.
*   **Claim number** (`claim_number`, `text`, required): Enter the reference ID number associated with the claim.
*   **Authorized representative** (`authorized_representative`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person or entity authorized to act on behalf of the claimant.
*   **Scope of authorization** (`authorization_scope`, `text`, required): Describe the specific powers granted for the settlement, including any limitations or restrictions.
*   **Settlement amount limit** (`settlement_amount_limit`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter the maximum authorized settlement amount. If this field is not relevant, leave it blank.
*   **Signature acknowledgement** (`signature`, `text`, required): This is a typed name accepted as a signature, acknowledging that the authorized representative has agreed to the terms of authorization.
*   **Authorization effective date** (`effective_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the authorization begins.
*   **Authorization expiry date** (`expiry_date`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date the authorization expires. If this field is not relevant, leave it blank.
*   **Witness name** (`witness_name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of an optional witness for the authorization. If this field is not relevant, leave it blank.
*   **Submission date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date this form was completed.

## Tips
- Always use the most up-to-date information available.
- Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If any issues arise during the claim process, please contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submission to avoid delays in the claim process.
