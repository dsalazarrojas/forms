# Privacy Settlement Payment Method Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update the payment method of a claimant in a privacy settlement.

## How To Complete This Form
To update the payment method of a claimant, follow these steps:

1. Verify the claimant's identification and gather their verification details.
2. Select the new payment method for the claimant.
3. Enter the claimant's payment method ID (if applicable).
4. Obtain the claimant's signature and date of signature.
5. Confirm the claimant's consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Verify Claimant** (`claimant_verification`, `note`, required): Verify the claimant's identification details.
* **Verification Details** (`verification_details`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the verification process or the claimant's identification details.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the new payment method for the claimant.
* **Payment Method ID** (`payment_method_id`, `select_multiple`, optional): If applicable, select the claimant's payment method ID.
* **Claimant Signature** (`claimant_signature`, `text`, required): Obtain the claimant's signature, which serves as proof of consent.
* **Claimant Consent** (`claimant_consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm the claimant's consent for the payment method update.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the claimant's consent.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `time`, optional): Enter the date and time of the claimant's signature.

## Tips
* Ensure that the claimant's payment method has not been updated recently and that this new update is necessary.
* Follow the required procedures and guidelines for updating payment methods.
* Verify the claimant's identification details to ensure accuracy and authenticity.
