# Accounting Record Use Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect and document the consent of clients or individuals for the use of their accounting records. The form ensures that clients are aware of how their records will be used and provides a record of their consent.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, including your name and contact details.
2. Describe the records you are willing to have used and the date ranges involved.
3. Specify the purpose for which you are authorizing the use of your records.
4. Indicate whether you are willing to share your records with third parties.
5. Describe any confidentiality measures that will be taken to protect your records.
6. If another person is granting consent on your behalf, please provide their name.
7. Sign the form electronically with your full name.
8. Enter the date your consent was granted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Provide the name of the individual or organization granting consent.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for consent confirmation.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Records to be used** (`records_to_be_used`, `text`, required): Describe the specific records and date ranges to be used.
* **Purpose of use** (`purpose_of_use`, `text`, required): Specify the reason for which your records will be used.
* **Duration of use** (`duration_of_use`, `text`, optional): Enter the time period during which your records will be used.
* **Third party sharing** (`third_party_sharing`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you are willing to share your records with third parties.
* **Confidentiality measures** (`confidentiality_measures`, `text`, optional): Describe any security or confidentiality measures that will be taken to protect your records.
* **Consent given by** (`consent_given_by`, `text`, required): If another person is granting consent on your behalf, provide their name.
* **Consent signature** (`consent_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form electronically with your full name.
* **Consent date** (`consent_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date your consent was granted.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any extra information regarding consent.
