# Parent Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Parent Authorization Form is a crucial document that allows parents or guardians to provide consent and information about their child's medical treatment. It is essential to fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure the best possible care for your child.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed before submission.
2.  Enter the patient's name as it is spelled and recorded on their official documents.
3.  Enter the parent or guardian’s name, address, and contact information for easy communication.
4.  Provide the child’s date of birth accurately.
5.  Describe the treatment your child will be undergoing, including the purpose and expected outcomes.
6.  Confirm or deny consent for the treatment by selecting one of the options provided.
7.  Sign the form to acknowledge you have read, understand, and agree to the treatment.
8.  Date and time of signing can be added for record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **patient_name** (Text, optional): Enter the patient's name as it is spelled and recorded on their official documents.
*   **parent_name** (Text, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's name for identification and contact purposes.
*   **date_of_birth** (Date, optional): Enter the child's date of birth accurately for medical purposes.
*   **guardian_contact_info** (Text, optional): Provide the parent or guardian’s contact information for easy communication.
*   **treatment_description** (Text, optional): Describe the treatment your child will be undergoing, including the purpose and expected outcomes.
*   **patient_dob** (Date, optional): Enter the child's date of birth accurately for medical purposes.
*   **consent** (Select One, optional): Confirm or deny consent for the treatment by selecting one of the options provided.
*   **signature** (Note, optional): Sign the form to acknowledge you have read, understand, and agree to the treatment.
*   **date** (Date, optional): Date of signing for record-keeping purposes.
*   **time** (Time, optional): Time of signing for record-keeping purposes.
*   **note** (Note, optional): Additional comments or notes about the treatment.
