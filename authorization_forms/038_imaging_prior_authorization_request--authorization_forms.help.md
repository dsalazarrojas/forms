# Imaging Prior Authorization Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the prior authorization process for medical imaging procedures. It collects relevant clinical information and supporting documentation to ensure a thorough review of the requested imaging.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the patient information section (fields 1-6) with the patient's details.
2. Provide clinical information (fields 7-10) about the imaging procedure, such as the medical necessity of the imaging.
3. Add any supporting documentation (fields 11-12) to justify the imaging procedure.
4. Choose the insurance provider (fields 23-24) and submitter (field 25) for the patient's coverage.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (1): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and medical record number.
* **Clinical Info** (2): Provide a brief description of the clinical condition or reason for the imaging procedure.
* **Provider Info** (3): Enter the provider's name, specialty, and contact information.
* **Insurance Info** (4): Enter the patient's insurance provider and policy number.
* **Imaging Info** (5): Describe the imaging procedure and the expected outcomes.
* **Medical Necessity** (6): Explain the medical necessity of the imaging procedure and how it aligns with the patient's diagnosis.
* **Clinical Review** (7): Describe the clinical review process and any relevant clinical notes.
* **Supporting Docs** (8): Attach any supporting documentation, such as lab results or medical records.
* **Review Date** (9): Enter the date of the clinical review.
* **Review Time** (10): Enter the time of the clinical review.
* **Submit Request** (11): Enter the submitter's name and date of submission.
* **Imaging Provider** (13): Enter the imaging provider's name and contact information.
* **Insurance Provider** (14): Enter the insurance provider's name and contact information.
* **Medical Record Locator** (15): Enter the medical record locator number.
* **Patient Name** (16): Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (17): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Medical Record Number** (18): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Medical Record Type** (19): Enter the type of medical record (e.g., hospital, clinic, etc.).
* **Patient Relationship** (20): Describe the patient's relationship with the submitter.
* **Clinical Review Status** (21): Enter the clinical review status (e.g., approved, denied, etc.).
* **Clinical Review Notes** (22): Enter any clinical review notes.
* **Insurance ID** (23): Enter the insurance ID number.
* **Insurance Plan** (24): Select the insurance plan (e.g., Blue Cross, Blue Shield, etc.).
* **Submitter Name** (25): Select the submitter's name (e.g., Jimmy, Jane, etc.).
