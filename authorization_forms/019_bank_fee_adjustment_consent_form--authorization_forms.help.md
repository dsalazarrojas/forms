# Bank Fee Adjustment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Bank Fee Adjustment Consent Form" is a document used to request adjustments to fees charged on your bank account. This form helps us understand your situation and the reason behind your request for an adjustment. By completing this form, you're providing us with the necessary information to review and potentially make changes to your fees.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

*   Review each field carefully, and enter the requested information in the corresponding fields.
*   Make sure to complete all required fields.
*   If you have any supporting documents, please attach them to the form.
*   Once you've filled out the form, please sign and date it.
*   Return the form to your bank representative for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Full Name** (Required)
    A field to enter your full name.
*   **Mailing Address** (Required)
    Enter your mailing address for notifications and correspondence.
*   **Email Address** (Required)
    Provide your email address for notifications and updates.
*   **Phone Number** (Required)
    Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Account Number** (Required)
    Enter your full account number for identification purposes.
*   **Account Type** (Required)
    Select the type of account you have (e.g., Personal Checking, Personal Savings, etc.).
*   **Fee Adjustment Type** (Required)
    Choose the type of fee adjustment you're requesting (e.g., Fee Waiver Request, Fee Reduction Request, etc.).
*   **Fee Category** (Required)
    Select the type of fee related to your request (e.g., Monthly Maintenance Fee, Overdraft Fee, etc.).
*   **Fee Amount** (Required)
    Enter the dollar amount of the fee you're contesting.
*   **Fee Date** (Required)
    Enter the date the fee was charged.
*   **Reason for Request** (Required)
    Select the reason for your fee adjustment request (e.g., Financial Hardship, Bank Error, etc.).
*   **Detailed Explanation** (Required)
    Provide a detailed explanation of your situation to support your request.
*   **Supporting Documents** (Optional)
    Select if you have supporting documents for your request.
*   **Previous Requests** (Required)
    Indicate if this is a repeat request or a first-time request.
*   **Customer Tenure** (Required)
    Select how long you've been a customer (e.g., less than 1 year, 1-3 years, etc.).
*   **Preferred Resolution** (Required)
    Choose the desired outcome for your request (e.g., Full Fee Waiver, Partial Fee Waiver, etc.).
*   **Contact Preference** (Required)
    Select how you'd like to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone Call, etc.).
*   **Best Time to Contact** (Optional)
    Choose a preferred time of day for contact.
*   **Authorization Statement** (Required)
    Confirm your authorization for this request.
*   **Terms Acknowledgment** (Required)
    Confirm you've read and understand the terms.
*   **Signature Date** (Required)
    Date and sign the form to confirm your consent.
*   **Additional Comments** (Optional)
    Enter any additional information or comments you'd like to share.

By completing this form, you're authorizing us to review and potentially make adjustments to your fees. Please review each field carefully and provide accurate information to help us assist you effectively.
