# Gift Return Authorization Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gift Return Authorization Contact Form is a document that allows customers to provide authorization for gift returns. It is used to collect relevant information from customers about their order details, customer preferences, and shipping details to facilitate the gift return process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the order details, including the order number and any other relevant information about the order.
2. Share your preferences about the return, such as what you would like to do with the gift (e.g., exchange, refund, or cancel the order).
3. Provide your shipping details, including your name, address, and any other relevant information about the shipping.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Details
* **Order Number** (required): Enter the number of the order you are requesting to return.
* **Order Date** (optional): Enter the date you received the order.
* **Order Description** (optional): Briefly describe the order or the product(s) being returned.

### Customer Preferences
* **Return Reason** (optional): Select the reason why you would like to return the gift (e.g., "I'd like to exchange it", "I'd like to refund it", etc.).
* **Return Action** (optional): Choose what you would like to do with the gift (e.g., exchange, refund, cancel the order, etc.).

### Shipping Details
* **Name** (required): Enter your name as it appears on your shipping label or as you would like it to appear on the return shipping label.
* **Address Line 1** (required): Enter your shipping address, including the street address or post office box number.
* **Address Line 2** (optional): Enter any additional address information, such as apartment or suite number.
* **City** (required): Enter the city where you are shipping the order.
* **State/Province** (required): Enter the state or province where you are shipping the order.
* **Postal/Zip Code** (required): Enter your postal or zip code for shipping.
* **Phone Number** (optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure efficient processing of your gift return request.
* If you have any additional comments or information about the return, please provide them in the space below the form.
* Once you have filled out this form, you will be contacted by our customer service team to confirm your return.
