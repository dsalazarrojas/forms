# Building Access Reconfiguration Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Building Access Reconfiguration Consent Form is used to request changes to access points, security levels, or zones within a building or facility. This form is a critical part of the reconfiguration process, as it ensures that all necessary information is collected and that the changes are properly coordinated and executed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authorization to submit this form.
2.  Enter your information in the required fields, including your name, job title, department, email address, and phone number.
3.  Provide a detailed description of the proposed changes, including the reason for the change and the affected parties.
4.  Specify the proposed start and end dates for the reconfiguration work.
5.  Indicate the expected level of disruption and the measures to minimize it.
6.  If applicable, provide information about external contractors involved and their names.
7.  Estimate the cost of the reconfiguration and specify the budget code.
8.  Describe the current configuration and proposed configuration.
9.  Indicate whether stakeholders have been notified and if consent has been given.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Requestor Name** (1): Please enter your full name as the requestor of this form.
*   **Job Title** (2): Enter your job title or position within the organization.
*   **Department** (3): Specify the department you belong to.
*   **Email Address** (4): Provide your contact email address.
*   **Phone Number** (5): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Request Date** (6): Enter the date of the request for reconfiguration.
*   **Building Name** (7): Enter the name of the building or facility.
*   **Building Address** (8): Enter the full address of the building or facility.
*   **Floor or Level** (9): Specify the specific floor or level to be reconfigured.
*   **Area Description** (10): Describe the area to be reconfigured.
*   **Reconfiguration Type** (11): Select the type of reconfiguration being proposed (e.g., Access Point Relocation, New Access Point, etc.).
*   **Current Configuration** (12): Describe the current configuration of the area to be reconfigured.
*   **Proposed Configuration** (13): Describe the proposed configuration of the area to be reconfigured.
*   **Reason for Change** (14): Specify the reason for the reconfiguration.
*   **Affected Parties** (15): Select the parties that will be impacted by the reconfiguration (e.g., Building Occupants, Security Personnel, etc.).
*   **Proposed Start Date** (16): Enter the proposed start date for the reconfiguration work.
*   **Expected Completion Date** (17): Enter the expected completion date for the reconfiguration work.
*   **Expected Disruption** (18): Indicate the expected level of disruption (e.g., No Disruption, Minimal, Moderate, etc.).
*   **Disruption Mitigation Plan** (19): Describe the plan to mitigate potential disruptions (if applicable).
*   **External Contractor Involved** (20): Indicate whether an external contractor will be involved in the reconfiguration (Yes/No).
*   **Contractor Name** (21): If an external contractor is involved, enter their name.
*   **Estimated Cost** (22): Enter the estimated cost of the reconfiguration.
*   **Budget Code** (23): Enter the budget code or cost center.
*   **Safety Measures** (24): Describe the safety measures to be taken during the reconfiguration.
*   **Stakeholder Notification** (25): Indicate whether stakeholders have been notified (Yes/No).
*   **Consent Acknowledgment** (26): Select whether you consent to the proposed reconfiguration (Yes/No).
*   **Authorization Level** (27): Select the authorization level for the reconfiguration (e.g., Self-Authorized, Manager Approved, etc.).
*   **Approving Authority Name** (28): If an approving authority is involved, enter their name.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
*   If you are unsure about any field or the process, please contact your supervisor or facility management team.
*   This form should not be used for duplicate or unnecessary reconfiguration requests.
*   Submission of this form does not guarantee approval or processing of the reconfiguration request.
*   The approval and processing of this form are internal processes and may take time to complete.
