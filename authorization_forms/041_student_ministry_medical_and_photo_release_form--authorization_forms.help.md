# Student Ministry Medical And Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect medical and photo release information for students participating in ministry events or activities. It helps to ensure their safety and well-being, and captures necessary permissions for event documentation or promotional materials.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and completely.
2. Select all applicable options that pertain to your medical details, emergency contacts, photo release, event details, and any additional notes.
3. Review and submit the form once finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Details** (`Medical Details`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide any necessary medical information for the student, such as medical conditions, allergies, or medications.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`Emergency Contacts`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all relevant emergency contacts, such as parents, guardians, or other contacts that the student would like to have notified in case of an emergency.
* **Photo Release** (`Photo Release`, select_one, required/optional): Select the photo release option that best describes the student's wishes, such as allowing their photo to be taken by parent or guardian, or by the ministry.
* **Event Details** (`Event Details`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all relevant event types, such as church events, camp events, or retreats, where the student will be participating.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, text, required/optional): Use this field to add any additional information or comments related to the student's medical details or photo release, such as any special instructions or requests.
