# Donor Recognition Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Recognition Authorization Form is used to collect information from donors about their preferences for recognition and acknowledgment of their donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions about donor recognition preferences.
2.  Provide your contact information.
3.  Indicate your consent to share your information and acknowledgment preferences.
4.  Add any additional comments or notes.
5.  Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Recognition** (id: 1, `required: false`)
    • Indicates whether you want to be recognized for your donation. 
* **Name** (id: 2, `type: text`, `required: false`)
    • Enter your name. Please enter your full name as you want to appear on recognition materials.
* **Relationship** (id: 3, `type: text`, `required: false`)
    • Enter your relationship to the donor (e.g., friend, family member, or colleague).
* **Contact Address** (id: 4, `type: text`, `required: false`)
    • Enter your address for communication purposes.
* **Email** (id: 5, `type: email`, `required: false`)
    • Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (id: 6, `type: text`, `required: false`)
    • Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Recognition Type** (id: 7, `required: false`)
    • Choose how you want to be recognized (e.g., publicly or privately).
* **Recognition Frequency** (id: 8, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`)
    • Choose how frequently you want to be recognized (e.g., once a year or never).
* **Recognition Medium** (id: 9, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`)
    • Choose the medium through which you want to be recognized (e.g., social media, email, or print).
* **Recognition Message** (id: 10, `type: text`, `required: false`)
    • Enter a message for the recognition.
* **Acknowledge Frequency** (id: 11, `required: false`)
    • Choose how frequently you want to be acknowledged (e.g., once a year or never).
* **Acknowledge Medium** (id: 12, `required: false`)
    • Choose the medium through which you want to be acknowledged (e.g., social media or email).
* **Acknowledge Message** (id: 13, `type: text`, `required: false`)
    • Enter a message for the acknowledgment.
* **Other Recognition Preferences** (id: 14, `type: text`, `required: false`)
    • Enter any additional preferences for recognition.
* **Other Recognition Message** (id: 15, `type: text`, `required: false`)
    • Enter a message for any additional recognition preferences.
* **Consent to Share** (id: 16, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`)
    • Choose who you consent to share your information with (e.g., social media or public records).
* **Consent to Email** (id: 17, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`)
    • Choose if you consent to being contacted via email.
* **Consent to Phone** (id: 18, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`)
    • Choose if you consent to being contacted via phone.
* **Consent to Social Media** (id: 19, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`)
    • Choose if you consent to being contacted via social media.
* **Consent to Print** (id: 20, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`)
    • Choose if you consent to being printed or shared in materials.
* **Consent to Other** (id: 21, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`)
    • Choose if you consent to being shared or printed in other materials.
* **Acknowledge Frequency Other** (id: 22, `type: select_one`, `required: false`)
    • Choose how frequently you want to be acknowledged (e.g., once a year or never) for other purposes.
* **Consent to Share Other** (id: 23, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`)
    • Choose who you consent to share your information with for other purposes.
* **Notes** (id: 24, `type: text`, `required: false`)
    • Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Additional Comments** (id: 25, `type: text`, `required: false`)
    • Enter any other comments or questions you may have.

Note: The fields marked as `required: false` are optional and can be filled in as needed.
