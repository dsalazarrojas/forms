# emergency_access_key_issue_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency_access_key_issue_request_form is used to report and resolve issues related to access keys. This form is essential for users to provide detailed information about the issue they're experiencing, allowing the support team to efficiently assist and address the matter.

## How To Complete This Form

### Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your issue is resolved promptly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (Category 1, Category 2, Category 3)**:
  Select all categories that apply to your issue.
* **Issue Description (Issue Description)**:
  Describe the problem you're experiencing with your access key.
* **Issue Status (Pending, Approved, Rejected)**:
  Select the current status of your issue from the options provided.
* **Issue Time (Issue Time)**:
  Enter the time the issue occurred.
* **Issue Date (Issue Date)**:
  Enter the date the issue occurred.
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)**:
  Enter a contact person's name and information for emergency purposes.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)**:
  Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
* **User ID (User ID)**:
  Enter your user ID for identification purposes.
* **Email (Email)**:
  Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Phone)**:
  Enter your phone number.

### Tips
- Ensure you fill out each section completely and accurately.
- Use the category selection to help the support team understand the nature of your issue.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult the support team for guidance.
