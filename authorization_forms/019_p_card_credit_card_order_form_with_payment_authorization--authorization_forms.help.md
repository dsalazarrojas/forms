**Purpose:**
This form is designed for users to complete a P-Card/Credit Card Order Form with Payment Authorization. It captures essential information for processing credit card transactions, including card type, number, expiration date, and security code. The form also collects personal identification details such as first name, last name, and email. This form is typically used for authorization or payment purposes, and it's essential to fill it out accurately to ensure smooth processing.

**How to Complete This Form:**

1.  Start by filling out the form title, which is self-explanatory and serves as the header for the form.
2.  Select the relevant category for this transaction from the Category dropdown menu. This helps to categorize the form according to the transaction type.
3.  Write a brief description of the transaction or order in the Description field. This helps in keeping a record of the transaction.
4.  Enter the P-Card Number in the P-Card Number field.
5.  Specify the type of credit card you are using in the Card Type field. You can choose from Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or Other.
6.  Enter the Card Number for the selected card type.
7.  Enter the Card Expiration date in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
8.  Enter the CVV/CVV code for the credit card.
9.  Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
10. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
11. Enter your email in the Email field.
12. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
13. Review all the fields and ensure all required fields are filled out correctly before submitting.

**Field-by-Field Explanation:**

*   **P-Card Number** (`p_card_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your P-Card number in this field. This is a unique identifier for your P-Card.
*   **Card Type** (`card_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of credit card you are using from the dropdown menu (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Other).
*   **Card Number** (`card_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the credit card number in the field.
*   **Card Expiration** (`card_expiration`, date, required/optional): Enter the credit card expiration date in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
*   **CVV/CVV** (`card_cvv`, text, required/optional): Enter the three-digit security code found on your credit card.
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit the form.

**Tips:**
Please ensure that all the required fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form. This includes choosing the correct Card Type, entering the correct P-Card Number, Card Number, Card Expiration, and CVV/CVV, and clicking on the Submit button.
