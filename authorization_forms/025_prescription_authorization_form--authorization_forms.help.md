# prescription_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prescription Authorization Form is used to obtain authorized person information and signature for prescription medication pickup in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information on page 1.
2. Select the relationship between the authorized person and the patient.
3. Choose the pickup option for the medication.
4. Enter any additional pickup information.
5. Sign and date the form as the authorized person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's full name, date of birth, and contact information (page 1). This field is not required but helps with communication and follow-up.
* **Authorized Person**: Enter the name of the person picking up the medication (page 2).
* **Relationship**: Select the relationship between the patient and the authorized person (e.g., "Patient," "Caregiver," or "Spouse"). This field is not required but helps with authorization verification.
* **Pickup Options**: Choose how the medication will be picked up (e.g., "In-person pickup," "Mail pickup," or "Delivery"). This field is not required but ensures correct medication distribution.
* **Pickup Info**: Enter any additional information related to the pickup (page 5). This field is not required but helps with clarification.
* **Authorized Person Signature**: Sign and date the form as the authorized person (page 6). This is a required field to ensure the person picking up the medication is authorized to do so.

## Tips
* Make sure to use this form for prescription medication pickup authorization only.
* Use the form for each patient individually, as each patient has unique needs.
* If you need to use this form for other purposes, please use a different form or modify this one accordingly.
