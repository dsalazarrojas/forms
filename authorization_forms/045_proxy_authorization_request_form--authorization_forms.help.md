# Proxy Authorization Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Proxy Authorization Request Form is used for a proxy to request authorization to access resources on behalf of a primary user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with the required information, making sure to provide as much detail as possible.
2.  Review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Proxy Authorization Request Form** (`proxy_authorization_request_form`, text, required): This is the main field where you can describe your request for proxy authorization.
- **Scope** (`scope`, text, required): Here you can specify the scope of the resources you're asking for access to.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Provide your contact details so we can reach out to you with any further questions or updates.
- **First Name** (`contact_details_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here.
- **Last Name** (`contact_details_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
- **Email** (`contact_details_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`contact_details_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Start Date** (`validity_start_date`, date, required): The start date for the authorization request.
- **End Date** (`validity_end_date`, date, required): The end date for the authorization request.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
- In case of any issues with your submission, please get in touch with us directly.
- Please do not submit duplicate requests.
