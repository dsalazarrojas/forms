# promotional_material_usage_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect contact and personal information from users who wish to access or utilize promotional materials. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in any of the required information that you feel necessary to proceed. 
2. Ensure that the entered information is accurate and up-to-date. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (text): Enter your full name, which will be used to identify you.
* **Contact Email** (email): Enter your contact email, which will be used to communicate with you about promotional material usage.
* **Business Address** (text): Enter your business address, if applicable.
* **Business Phone** (text): Enter your business phone number, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter your date of birth, which may be required for certain promotional material usage purposes (e.g., age verification).

## Tips
* Ensure that all entered information is accurate and up-to-date to prevent any potential issues with promotional material usage.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
