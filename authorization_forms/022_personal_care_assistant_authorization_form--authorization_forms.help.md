# Personal Care Assistant Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect authorization information from family members or caregivers to establish a relationship between the personal care assistant and the individual requiring care.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please read the form carefully to ensure you understand what is required.
- Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any question, please do not hesitate to ask the individual or their primary caregiver for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Authorization Form** (form_1): This section is a placeholder for information that should be provided by the individual or their family member. Please add a brief note or explanation here if needed.
* **Authorized Caregiver Information** (form_2): Please enter the name and contact information of the authorized caregiver.
* **Caregiver Name** (form_3): Please indicate if the caregiver is authorized to provide care. You can select "Yes" if they are, or "No" if they are not.
* **Relationship** (form_4): Please specify your relationship with the individual, such as family member, friend, or neighbor.
* **Date of Birth** (form_5): Please enter the individual's date of birth for verification purposes.
* **Contact Information** (form_6): Please indicate if you are willing to provide the caregiver's contact information. You can select "Yes" if you are, or "No" if you are not.
* **Caregiver Contact Number** (form_7): Please enter the authorized caregiver's contact number.
* **Caregiver Contact Email** (form_8): Please enter the authorized caregiver's email address.
* **Family Contact Name** (form_9): Please enter your name if you are the family contact.
* **Family Contact Email** (form_10): Please enter your email address if you are the family contact.
* **Family Contact Number** (form_11): Please enter your contact number.
* **Date** (form_12): Please indicate if you are available to provide care at any time. You can select "Yes" if you are, or "No" if you are not.
* **Time** (form_13): Please indicate if you are available to provide care at specific times. You can select "Yes" if you are, or "No" if you are not.
* **Reason for Authorization** (form_14): Please provide a brief explanation for why you are authorizing the caregiver.
* **Notes** (form_15): Please add any additional comments or notes about the caregiver or care arrangement.
