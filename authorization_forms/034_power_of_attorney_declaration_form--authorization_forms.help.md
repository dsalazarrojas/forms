# Power Of Attorney Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power of Attorney Declaration Form is a document used to establish the authority and powers granted to an agent to act on behalf of an individual or entity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the step-by-step guide below.

1. Fill in your legal name and address accurately to ensure that your identity is correctly represented.
2. Provide your date of birth to confirm your age and identity.
3. Enter your agent's name and address, ensuring they are aware of their responsibilities as an agent.
4. Enter your contact number to ensure the agent can get in touch with you.
5. Enter your email address for further communication with the agent.
6. Select the specific powers you are granting to the agent, e.g., banking, investments, business, etc.
7. Provide the authorization date when you are willing for the agent to take effect.
8. Enter the agent's contact number to facilitate communication between you and the agent.
9. Select the notarized by field to indicate which notary has authenticated this document.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Legal Name** (<code>legal_name</code>, text, required): Your legal name and address as it appears on official documents.
- **Legal Address** (<code>legal_address</code>, text, required): Your current legal address as it appears on official documents.
- **State Province** (<code>state_province</code>, text, required): The state or province you reside in, as it appears on official documents.
- **Date of Birth** (<code>date_of_birth</code>, date, required): Your date of birth to confirm your age and identity.
- **Agent Name** (<code>agent_name</code>, text, required): Name of the person or entity being appointed as your agent.
- **Agent Address** (<code>agent_address</code>, text, required): The address of the person or entity appointed as your agent.
- **Contact Number** (<code>contact_number</code>, text, required): Phone number of the person or entity appointed as your agent for communication.
- **email Address** (<code>email_address</code>, email, required): Email address of the person or entity appointed as your agent for further communication.
- **Agent Authorized To** (<code>agent_authorized_to</code>, select one, required): Powers granted to the agent, e.g., banking, investments, business, etc.
- **Authorization Date** (<code>authorization_date</code>, date, required): Date when you are authorizing the agent to act on your behalf.
- **Agent Contact Number** (<code>agent_contact_number</code>, text, required): The contact information of the person or entity appointed as your agent for communication.
- **Notarized By** (<code>notarized_by</code>, select one, required): Name of the Notary that authenticated this document.

## Tips

- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and true to the best of your knowledge.
- The form should be filled out in the presence of a notary or other authorized representative.
- This document may be subject to local and state regulations, which might require additional documentation or notarization. Check the relevant laws and regulations of your area before using this form.
- Ensure that the notary's name matches one of the options provided in the "notarized by" field.
- Once completed, this form must be notarized before it can be used as a legal document.
