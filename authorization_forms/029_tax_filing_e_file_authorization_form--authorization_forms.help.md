# Tax Filing E File Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to authorize electronic filing and capture client details and representative information for tax filing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the client's name in the **Client Name** field.
2. Fill in the client's address in the **Client Address** field (if applicable).
3. Select the E-file authorization status in the **E-File Authorization** field.
4. Enter the date of the client's signature in the **Signature Date** field.
5. Enter the date the representative signed the form in the **Date Signed** field (if applicable).
6. Fill in the authorized representative's name in the **Authorized Representative** field (if applicable).
7. Enter the authorized representative's title in the **Authorized Representative Title** field (if applicable).
8. Enter the authorized representative's phone number in the **Authorized Representative Phone** field (if applicable).
9. Enter the authorized representative's email address in the **Authorized Representative Email** field (if applicable).
10. Provide a signature from the client in the **Client Signature** field.
11. Provide a signature from the authorized representative in the **Authorized Representative Signature** field.
12. Add any additional comments or notes in the **Notes** field (if applicable).
13. Add any other comments or notes in the **Form Footer** field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Client Name (Client Name)**: Enter the name of the client.
*   **Client Address (Client Address)**: Enter the client's address.
*   **E-File Authorization (E-File Authorization)**: Indicate whether the client is authorized to E-file or not.
*   **Signature Date (Signature Date)**: Enter the date of the client's signature.
*   **Date Signed (Date Signed)**: Enter the date the representative signed the form.
*   **Authorized Representative (Authorized Representative)**: Enter the name of the authorized representative.
*   **Authorized Representative Title (Authorized Representative Title)**: Enter the title of the authorized representative.
*   **Authorized Representative Phone (Authorized Representative Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the authorized representative.
*   **Authorized Representative Email (Authorized Representative Email)**: Enter the email address of the authorized representative.
*   **Client Signature (Client Signature)**: Provide a signature from the client.
*   **Authorized Representative Signature (Authorized Representative Signature)**: Provide a signature from the authorized representative.
*   **Notes (Notes)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the client.
*   **Form Footer (Form Footer)**: Enter any other notes or comments about the form.
