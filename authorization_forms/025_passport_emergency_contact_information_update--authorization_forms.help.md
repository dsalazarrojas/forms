# Passport Emergency Contact Information Update - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Passport Emergency Contact Information Update" form allows you to provide and update emergency contact information for yourself or an authorized representative to contact in case of emergency while abroad.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and correctly. All fields are optional but recommended for accurate emergency contact.
2.  Make sure you are filling out the correct fields with the right information.
3.  Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Traveler's Contact Information** (`traveler_info`, text, required: false)
    This field allows the traveler or authorized representative to provide their contact information, such as name, phone number, and email address.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false)
    Provide the name and contact information of the emergency contact.
*   **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, text, required: false)
    Describe the relationship between the traveler and the emergency contact (e.g., parent, spouse, friend).
*   **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required: false)
    Enter the address of the emergency contact.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false)
    Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, required: false)
    Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
*   **Next of Kin** (`next_of_kin`, text, required: false)
    Provide the name of the next of kin.
*   **Contact Relationship Type** (`contact_relationship_type`, text, required: false)
    Describe the type of relationship between the traveler and the emergency contact.
*   **Contact Type** (`contact_type`, text, required: false)
    Specify the type of contact (e.g., family, friend, guardian).

## Tips

*   Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Make sure to provide the correct contact information for the emergency contact.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
