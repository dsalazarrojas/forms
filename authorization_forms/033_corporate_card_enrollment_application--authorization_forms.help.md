# Corporate Card Enrollment Application - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for corporate card enrollment, allowing employees to request a corporate credit card for business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all required information before proceeding.
2. Fill out each section thoroughly and accurately, as incomplete or incorrect information may delay the card enrollment process.
3. Select the correct options for each field, as specified in the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Employee ID**: This field is required to track your employee ID number.
  - Please enter your employee ID number as it appears on your employee ID card or in your HR system.

- **Name**: This field is required to know who is applying for the corporate card.
  - Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID card or HR records.

- **Email**: This field is required for communication purposes.
  - Please enter your work email address.

- **Department**: This field is required to determine the departmental approval process.
  - Select your department from the provided options to ensure correct approval processes.

- **Card Holder Name**: This field is required for card registration purposes.
  - Please enter the name of the card holder, which is you.

- **Card Type**: This field is required to determine the card type and limits.
  - Select the correct card type from the provided options to ensure correct card limits and approval processes.

- **Card Number**: This field is required for card registration purposes.
  - Please enter your corporate credit card number, which will be issued after approval.

- **Approver**: This field is required to determine the approver's identity.
  - Select the correct approver from the provided options to ensure correct approval processes.

- **Date of Approval**: This field is required to track the approval date.
  - Please select the date the approval was granted.

- **Comments**: This field is optional for any additional comments or notes.
  - If you have any additional information to provide, enter it in this field.

- **Submission**: These fields are not required but are for future use.
  - Please select 'Yes' if you agree to the submission terms and conditions.

Note: The fields marked as 'Submission' are not currently being used in the form but are present for potential future developments. Please fill out the remaining fields carefully, as they are crucial for the corporate card enrollment process.
