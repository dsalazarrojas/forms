# Smoke Alarm Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from building owners and occupants to verify the presence and maintenance of smoke alarms in their premises. This includes details such as owner information, address, and smoke alarm serial numbers. The form is essential for ensuring that fire safety regulations are met and to provide a record of smoke alarm certifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting "Owner Information" and filling in your details as requested.
2. Enter the address of the premises you are submitting for.
3. Choose a contact method (First Name, Last Name, Email, or Phone) to contact you.
4. Select your owner type from the options provided (Owner, Tenant, or Agent).
5. If applicable, enter the date and time of your visit.
6. Enter details about the smoke alarm, including its serial number, type, and model.
7. Add any additional smoke alarm information or notes as required.
8. Finally, add your signature and the agent's signature, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Information** (`owner_info`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information, such as your first and last name, email address, or phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the address of the premises you are submitting for.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (First Name, Last Name, Email, or Phone).
* **Owner Type** (`owner_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select your status as Owner, Tenant, or Agent.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Enter the date of your visit, if applicable.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): Enter the time of your visit, if applicable.
* **Smoke Alarm Information** (`smoke_alarm_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the smoke alarm.
* **Smoke Alarm Type** (`smoke_alarm_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of smoke alarm you have (Hardwired, Wireless, or Battery).
* **Smoke Alarm Serial Number** (`smoke_alarm_serial_number`, number, optional): Enter the serial number of the smoke alarm.
* **Smoke Alarm Model** (`smoke_alarm_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of the smoke alarm.
* **Smoke Alarm Description** (`smoke_alarm_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the smoke alarm.
* **Smoke Alarm Notes** (`smoke_alarm_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the smoke alarm.
* **Signature Information** (`signature_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your signature.
* **Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`, text, required): Sign with your name and date.
* **Agent Signature** (`agent_signature`, text, optional): If applicable, sign with the agent's name and date.
* **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, optional): Enter the agent's name.
* **Date of Visit Signature** (`date_of_visit_signature`, date, optional): Enter the date of the visit, if applicable.
* **Time of Visit Signature** (`time_of_visit_signature`, time, optional): Enter the time of the visit, if applicable.
* **Certify** (`certify`, text, required): Enter a statement of certification, typically "I certify that the information above is accurate and true."
* **Name** (`certify_name`, text, required): Enter your name for certification purposes.
* **Date** (`certify_date`, date, required): Enter the date of certification.
* **Signature** (`certify_signature`, text, required): Sign with your name.

Please note that all fields marked as required should be completed before submitting the form.
