# Contract Agreement Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contract Agreement Authorization Form is used to collect information about a contract, including the contract details, parties involved, and approval requirements. This form is typically used in a business setting to ensure that all necessary approvals and reviews are in place before proceeding with a contract.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the Requestor Name, Requestor Title, Department, and other relevant fields.
2. Select the type of contract from the Contract Type dropdown menu.
3. Enter the contract details, including Contract Value, Contract Duration, Start Date, and End Date.
4. Identify the Counterparty and select their type.
5. Determine the Authorization Level Required and select the relevant option.
6. Enter the Budget Code, Funding Source, and any other relevant information.
7. Confirm if Legal Review is required and the status of the Risk Assessment.
8. Add any additional comments or terms and conditions.
9. Sign and date the form with the Authorizing Manager's signature and name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Name (id: 1)** (`Requestor Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or department requesting the contract.
* **Requestor Title (id: 2)** (`Requestor Title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the person or department requesting the contract.
* **Department (id: 3)** (`Department`, text, required): Enter the department or team responsible for the contract.
* **Request Date (id: 4)** (`Request Date`, date, required): Enter the date the request was made.
* **Contract Type (id: 5)** (`Contract Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of contract from the dropdown menu (e.g., Service Agreement, Vendor Contract, etc.).
* **Contract Title (id: 6)** (`Contract Title`, text, required): Enter the name of the contract.
* **Contract Description (id: 7)** (`Contract Description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the contract.
* **Counterparty Name (id: 8)** (`Counterparty Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the counterparty (the other party involved in the contract).
* **Counterparty Type (id: 9)** (`Counterparty Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of counterparty from the dropdown menu (e.g., Corporation, LLC, etc.).
* **Contract Value (id: 10)** (`Contract Value`, number, required): Enter the total dollar amount of the contract.
* **Contract Duration (id: 11)** (`Contract Duration`, select_one, required): Select the length of the contract from the dropdown menu (e.g., Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, etc.).
* **Start Date (id: 12)** (`Start Date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **End Date (id: 13)** (`End Date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the contract, if applicable.
* **Authorization Level Required (id: 14)** (`Authorization Level Required`, select_one, required): Select the level of approval required for the contract (e.g., Department manager, Director, etc.).
* **Budget Code (id: 15)** (`Budget Code`, text, required): Enter the cost center or account responsible for the contract.
* **Funding Source (id: 16)** (`Funding Source`, text, required): Enter the source of the funding for the contract.
* **Legal Review Required (id: 17)** (`Legal Review Required`, select_one, required): Confirm if legal review is required for the contract.
* **Risk Assessment (id: 18)** (`Risk Assessment`, select_one, required): Select the risk level associated with the contract (e.g., Low risk, Medium risk, etc.).
* **Terms and Conditions (id: 19)** (`Terms and Conditions`, text, optional): Add any special terms and conditions related to the contract.
* **Attachments (id: 20)** (`Attachments`, text, optional): List any attached documents or files.
* **Authorizing Manager (id: 21)** (`Authorizing Manager`, text, required): Enter the name of the manager who authorized the contract.
* **Manager Signature (id: 22)** (`Manager Signature`, text, required): Enter the signature of the manager who authorized the contract.
* **Authorization Date (id: 23)** (`Authorization Date`, date, required): Enter the date the contract was authorized.
* **Additional Comments (id: 24)** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information related to the contract.
