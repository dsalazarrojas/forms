# Building Access Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Access Authorization Form is designed to collect information from requestors seeking access to the building. It is essential that all required fields are completed accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Requestor Name** field.
2. Enter your department name in the **Requestor Department** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **Email Address** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
5. Enter the date you are requesting access in the **Request Date** field.
6. Select the type of access you require in the **Access Type** field.
7. Enter the full name of the person seeking access in the **Person Seeking Access** field.
8. Enter the name of the building you wish to access in the **Building Name** field.
9. Enter the full address of the building in the **Building Address** field.
10. Select all areas requiring access in the **Areas Requiring Access** field.
11. Choose the level of access you require in the **Access Level** field.
12. Select the start date of your access in the **Access Start Date** field.
13. If necessary, enter the end date of your access in the **Access End Date** field.
14. Select the method of access you will use in the **Access Method** field.
15. Provide a justification for your access request in the **Justification for Access** field.
16. If applicable, enter the name and contact number of your emergency contact in the **Emergency Contact Name** and **Emergency Contact Phone** fields.
17. Confirm if this access has been approved by management in the **Authorization Approval** field.
18. If applicable, enter the name of the manager who approved your access in the **Approving Manager Name** field.
19. Confirm that you agree to the building's policies in the **Terms and Conditions Acknowledged** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself as the requestor.
* **Requestor Department** (2, text, required): Enter your department name to indicate which team or division you belong to.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Provide your contact email for further communication.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for emergency purposes.
* **Request Date** (5, date, required): Enter the date of your access request.
* **Access Type** (6, select_one, required): Choose the type of access you require (e.g., Employee, Contractor, Visitor, etc.).
* **Person Seeking Access** (7, text, required): Enter the full name of the person requiring access.
* **Building Name** (9, text, required): Enter the name of the building you wish to access.
* **Building Address** (10, text, required): Enter the full address of the building.
* **Areas Requiring Access** (11, select_multiple, required): Select all areas you require access to (e.g., Main Lobby, Office Floors, etc.).
* **Access Level** (12, select_one, required): Choose the level of access you require (e.g., Full Access - 24/7, Business Hours Only, etc.).
* **Access Start Date** (13, date, required): Enter the start date of your access.
* **Access End Date** (14, date, false): If necessary, enter the end date of your access.
* **Access Method** (15, select_one, required): Choose the method of access you will use (e.g., Key Card, Mobile Access, etc.).
* **Justification for Access** (16, text, required): Provide a reason for your access request.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (17, text, false): If applicable, enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (18, text, false): If applicable, enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
* **Authorization Approval** (19, select_one, required): Confirm if this access has been approved by management.
* **Approving Manager Name** (20, text, false): If applicable, enter the name of the manager who approved your access.
* **Terms and Conditions Acknowledged** (21, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the building's policies.
