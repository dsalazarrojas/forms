# Public Fund Access Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Fund Access Authorization Form is used to request access to public funds for an organization or individual. It is designed to gather essential information about the applicant, their authorized officer, access level, and contact details to facilitate a secure access process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the organization or individual seeking access.
2. Provide the authorized officer's name and contact details.
3. Choose the access level for the organization or individual.
4. Select the start and end dates of the access period.
5. Enter the purpose of the access request.
6. Enter the contact email and phone number of the individual or organization.
7. Indicate whether authorized representatives are allowed to act on behalf of the organization or individual.
8. Enter the signature and signature date of the authorized signer.
9. Enter the name of the authorized signer.
10. Enter the title of the authorized signer.
11. Enter the contact address, city, state, zip, and country of the individual or organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization or individual seeking access.
* **Authorized Officer**: Enter the name of the authorized officer.
* **Title**: Select 'Yes' if the officer holds a title.
* **Access Level**: Select the access level for the organization or individual (High, Low).
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the access period.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the access period.
* **Purpose**: Enter the reason for the access request.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the individual or organization.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the individual or organization.
* **Authorized Representatives**: Select 'Yes' if authorized representatives are allowed to act on behalf of the organization or individual.
* **Signature**: Enter your signature.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your signature.
* **Authorized Signer**: Enter the name of the authorized signer.
* **Signer Title**: Enter the title of the authorized signer.
* **Contact Address 1**: Enter the first line of the contact address.
* **Contact Address 2**: Enter the second line of the contact address.
* **Contact City**: Enter the city of the contact address.
* **Contact State**: Enter the state of the contact address.
* **Contact Zip**: Enter the zip code of the contact address.
* **Contact Country**: Enter the country of the contact address.

Note: All fields marked as optional are not required for submission, but may be required for processing or approval.
