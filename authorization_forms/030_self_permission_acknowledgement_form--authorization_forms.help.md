# Self Permission Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Permission Acknowledgement Form is a simple form designed to gather information from individuals who need to acknowledge their own permission for a specific purpose. This form is intended for internal use and does not require any additional fields that may be sensitive or confidential.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your first name
2. Provide your last name
3. Enter your date of birth (in the "date" field)
4. If applicable, enter your time of birth (in the "time" field, which is optional)
5. Enter your email address
6. If you have an organization or affiliation, provide it in the "organization" field
7. Select your role (optional)
8. Enter the date you are granting permission
9. Provide a reason for granting permission (this is a note field, so feel free to be as detailed as you need to be)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (Text, Required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **last_name** (Text, Required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **date** (Date, Required): Please enter your date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **time** (Time, Optional): If you wish to provide your time of birth, please enter it in the correct format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **email** (Email, Required): Please enter a valid email address that you regularly use.
* **organization** (Text, Optional): If you have an affiliation with an organization, please provide it here.
* **role** (Select One, Optional): Select your role in the organization you are affiliated with.
* **permission_date** (Date, Required): Please enter the date you are granting permission for.
* **reason** (Note, Required): Please provide a reason for granting permission. This is a free-form field, so feel free to be as detailed as you need to be.
