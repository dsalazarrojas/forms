# Equipment Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Authorization Form is a tool used for requesting access to or permission to use specific equipment within an organization. This form helps ensure that equipment usage is authorized and tracked correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the required information in each field, paying attention to the field-specific requirements and hints.
2.  Review the form before submission to ensure all fields have been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization or department requesting equipment authorization.
* **Equipment Requested** (`equipment_requested`, text, optional): Provide a clear description of the equipment being requested, including its name, model, and any relevant details.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of equipment being requested (e.g., General, Specialized, or Both).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the equipment usage is expected to begin.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the equipment usage is expected to end.
* **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the equipment, including its features and any specific requirements.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person or representative responsible for the equipment request.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person or representative.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person or representative.

## Tips
- Double-check that all fields have been completed accurately and thoroughly before submitting the form.
- Ensure that the equipment type is selected correctly to avoid delays in processing.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the hints provided or ask your supervisor for assistance.
