# Grooming Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grooming Release Form is used to document the details of a pet's grooming or service visit. It collects information about the pet, its owner, and the service provided. This form is essential for maintaining accurate records and ensuring the pet's owner is informed and aware of the service details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the pet owner's details in the `pet_owner` field.
2. Provide the pet's name in the `pet_name` field.
3. Select the pet type from the options provided in the `pet_type` field.
4. Enter the pet's breed in the `pet_breed` field.
5. Enter the service provider's details in the `service_provider` field.
6. Select the service date from the calendar.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the `signature` field (not required).
8. Enter the service time in the `service_time` field.
9. Enter the service address in the `service_address` field (not required).
10. Enter the service city in the `service_city` field (not required).
11. Enter the service state in the `service_state` field (not required).
12. Enter the service zip in the `service_zip` field (not required).
13. Enter the phone number of the service provider in the `phone` field (not required).
14. Enter the email of the service provider in the `email` field (not required).
15. Enter the fax number of the service provider in the `fax` field (not required).
16. Add any additional notes or comments in the `note` field (not required).
17. Select the release date from the calendar in the `release_date` field (not required).
18. Enter the release time in the `release_time` field (not required).
19. Add any additional notes or comments in the `release_note` field (not required).
20. Enter the name of the person releasing the pet in the `release_by` field (not required).
21. Enter the timeframe for the pet's release in the `release_timeframe` field (not required).
22. Enter the emergency contact person's name in the `emergency_contact` field (not required).
23. Enter the emergency contact person's phone number in the `emergency_phone` field (not required).
24. Add your signature in the `release_form_signature` field (not required).
25. Select the date of the release form from the calendar in the `release_form_date` field (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pet_owner**: Enter the name of the pet owner who is releasing the pet.
* **pet_name**: Enter the name of the pet being released.
* **pet_type**: Select the type of pet (e.g., dog, cat, etc.).
* **pet_breed**: Enter the breed of the pet (e.g., Labrador, Siamese, etc.).
* **service_provider**: Enter the name of the service provider (e.g., groomer, trainer, etc.).
* **service_date**: Select the date of the service provided.
* **service_time**: Enter the time of the service provided (not required).
* **service_address**: Enter the address of the service provider (not required).
* **service_city**: Enter the city of the service provider (not required).
* **service_state**: Enter the state of the service provider (not required).
* **service_zip**: Enter the zip code of the service provider (not required).
* **phone**: Enter the phone number of the service provider (not required).
* **email**: Enter the email of the service provider (not required).
* **fax**: Enter the fax number of the service provider (not required).
* **note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the service provided (not required).
* **release_date**: Select the date of the release from the calendar (not required).
* **release_time**: Enter the release time (not required).
* **release_note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the release (not required).
* **release_by**: Enter the name of the person releasing the pet (not required).
* **release_timeframe**: Enter the timeframe for the pet's release (not required).
* **emergency_contact**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person (not required).
* **emergency_phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person (not required).
* **release_form_signature**: Add your signature to the release form (not required).
* **release_form_date**: Select the date of the release form from the calendar (required).
* **signature**: Add any additional notes or comments about the release (not required).
