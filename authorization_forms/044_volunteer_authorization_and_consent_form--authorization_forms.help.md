# Volunteer Authorization And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from volunteers, including their contact details and authorization to work with the organization. It is essential to complete this form carefully to ensure that you are properly authorized and informed about your role and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and other contact information on page 1.
2. On page 2, select your contact address from the options provided.
3. On page 3, select your preferred method of contact (phone, email, or name).
4. On page 4, choose the type of volunteer work you will be doing (role, date, time, or location).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Volunteer Information
* **Volunteer Information** (`volunteer_information`, text, required): Enter your name and other relevant contact details to identify you as a volunteer.

### Page 2: Contact Address
* **Contact Address** (`address`, select_one, required): Select one option to provide your contact address:
	+ Select this option if you want to provide your street address
	+ Select this option if you want to provide your city
	+ Select this option if you want to provide your state

### Page 3: Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_one, required): Select one option to provide your preferred method of contact:
	+ Select this option to provide your name
	+ Select this option to provide your email
	+ Select this option to provide your phone number

### Page 4: Volunteer Info
* **Volunteer Info** (`volunteer_info`, select_one, required): Select one option to specify the type of volunteer work you will be doing:
	+ Select this option to specify your role
	+ Select this option to specify your date of service
	+ Select this option to specify the time of service
	+ Select this option to specify the location of service

## Tips
Please ensure that you complete all required fields accurately and truthfully. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification from authorized personnel before submitting the form.
