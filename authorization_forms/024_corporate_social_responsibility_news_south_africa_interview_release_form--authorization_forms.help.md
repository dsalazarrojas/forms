# corporate_social_responsibility_news_south_africa_interview_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential information from individuals who have participated in interviews for corporate social responsibility news articles in South Africa. The purpose of this form is to ensure that their personal experiences, remarks, and recollections are used for the intended purpose. It also helps to obtain their consent for using personal and document data. The form is intended for South African residents who have participated in interviews.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields with your personal information, including first name, last name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and interview details (date, time, location).
2. Provide your consent for using your personal experiences, photographs, and documents in the intended context.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
4. If you have any additional comments or notes, please provide them in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1) (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (2) (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your full last name.
* **Email Address** (3) (`Email Address`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (4) (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (5) (`Company`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, enter its name.
* **Job Title** (6) (`Job Title`, text, optional): If you have a job title, enter it.
* **Department** (7) (`Department`, text, optional): If you have a department, enter its name.
* **Date of Birth** (8) (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Interview Date** (9) (`Interview Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the interview.
* **Interview Time** (10) (`Interview Time`, time, required): Enter the time of the interview.
* **Interview Location** (11) (`Interview Location`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide the location of the interview.
* **Interview Materials** (12) (`Interview Materials`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide materials used during the interview.
* **Consent** (13) (`Consent`, select_one, required): Select one of the three options to grant consent for the use of personal experiences, photographs, and documents.
* **Agreement** (14) (`Agreement`, select_one, required): Select one of the two options to confirm your agreement.
* **Signature** (15) (`Signature`, note, optional): If you'd like to sign the form, please provide your signature.
* **Date** (16) (`Date`, date, required): Enter the date you sign the form.
* **Time** (17) (`Time`, time, required): Enter the time you sign the form.
* **IP Address** (18) (`IP Address`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your IP address.
* **Country** (19) (`Country`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your country.
* **City** (20) (`City`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your city.
* **State** (21) (`State`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your state or province.
* **Zip** (22) (`Zip`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your zip code or postal code.
* **Address** (23) (`Address`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your physical address.
* **Notes** (24) (`Notes`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide any additional comments or notes.
* **Agreement Date** (25) (`Agreement Date`, date, optional): If you'd like to provide the date of your agreement.
