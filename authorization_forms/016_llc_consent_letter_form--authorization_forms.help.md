# LLC Consent Letter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a letter of consent from an LLC. It is used to upload documents and ensure their legitimacy. The form is intended to be completed by LLC representatives or members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form title to briefly describe the purpose of the document.
2. Enter the name of your company.
3. Provide the name and position of the LLC member associated with the document.
4. Describe the action or purpose of the document.
5. Enter the date the document was uploaded.
6. Enter your phone number and fax number (if applicable).
7. Select the document link, if applicable.
8. Enter the name of the company representative who uploaded the document.
9. Enter the position of the company representative who uploaded the document.
10. Add any additional comments about the document.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Briefly describe the purpose of the document.
* **company_name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **member_name** (`member_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the LLC member associated with the document.
* **member_position** (`member_position`, text, optional): Enter the position or title of the LLC member.
* **action_description** (`action_description`, text, required): Describe the action or purpose of the document.
* **date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date the document was uploaded.
* **signature** (`signature`, text, required): Enter your signature.
* **company_address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your company.
* **member_address** (`member_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the LLC member.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **fax** (`fax`, text, optional): Enter your fax number.
* **document_link** (`document_link`, select_one, optional): Select the document link, if applicable.
* **uploaded_by** (`uploaded_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the company representative who uploaded the document.
* **uploaded_date** (`uploaded_date`, date, required): Enter the date the document was uploaded.
* **uploaded_time** (`uploaded_time`, time, optional): Enter the upload time in 24-hour format.
* **uploaded_file** (`uploaded_file`, text, optional): Upload a file, if applicable.
* **file_name** (`file_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the uploaded file.
* **file_size** (`file_size`, text, optional): Enter the size of the uploaded file.
* **uploaded_by_employees** (`uploaded_by_employees`, select_multiple, optional): Select all employees who uploaded documents.
* **company_representative** (`company_representative`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company representative.
* **signature2** (`signature2`, text, optional): Enter the name of the second signature, if applicable.
* **uploaded_by_representative** (`uploaded_by_representative`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company representative who uploaded this document.
* **uploaded_by_representative_position** (`uploaded_by_representative_position`, text, optional): Enter the position of the company representative who uploaded this document.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the document.
