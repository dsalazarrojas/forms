# Corporate Expense Disclosure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Expense Disclosure Consent Form is a mandatory form that requires employees to acknowledge their understanding of the corporate expense reporting policy. This form helps ensure that employees are aware of their responsibilities and obligations regarding expense reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the designated fields.
2. Select your department from the list provided.
3. Choose the frequency of your expense reports (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, or Annually).
4. Indicate your reporting manager's name and their email address.
5. Select the types of expenses you are responsible for reporting (Business Travel, Entertainment, or Meal and Incidentals).
6. Enter the amount of your expenses.
7. Provide a note regarding your consent to the corporate expense disclosure policy.
8. Sign and date your consent.
9. Have your reporting manager sign and date their acceptance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official personnel records.
* **Email**: Enter your official company email address.
* **Department**: Select your department or team name from the provided list.
* **Report Frequency**: Indicate the frequency of your expense reports, which is the same as your reporting cycle. 
* **Report Type**: Choose the type of expenses you are responsible for reporting (e.g., Business Travel, Entertainment, Meal and Incidentals).
* **Reporting Manager Name**: Enter the name of your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Email**: Enter the email address of your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Title**: Enter the title of your reporting manager.
* **Expense Type**: Select the types of expenses you are responsible for reporting.
* **Expense Amount**: Enter the total amount of your expenses.
* **Consent**: Provide a brief note acknowledging your understanding of the corporate expense disclosure policy.
* **Signature**: Sign your name, indicating your consent to the policy.
* **Signature Date**: Date your signature.
* **Reporting Manager Signature**: Have your reporting manager sign and date their acceptance.
* **Reporting Manager Signature Date**: Date your reporting manager's signature.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before signing.
* Ensure that you have understood and agreed to the corporate expense disclosure policy.
* Have your reporting manager review and sign the form as well.
* Retain a copy of this form for your records.
