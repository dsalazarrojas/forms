# Parental Authorization Form For Minors - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Authorization Form For Minors is a document that grants permission for a minor to receive medical treatment. It is designed for the minor's parent or guardian to acknowledge and confirm their consent to the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with accurate and complete information for the minor.
- Review and confirm the consent to the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent's Name** (`parent_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your name as the minor's parent or guardian.
* **Child's Name** (`child_name`, Text, Required): Enter the minor's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required): Provide the minor's date of birth.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, Select One, Optional): Choose how you are related to the minor (Mother, Father, Grandparent, Guardian).
* **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you're authorizing the treatment.
* **Treatment Details** (`treatment_details`, Text, Optional): Describe the reason for the treatment and any relevant medical history.
* **Signature** (`signature`, Text, Required): Sign this section to confirm your consent.
* **Parent Contact Info** (`parent_contact_info`, Text, Optional): Include any additional contact information for the minor's parent or guardian.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider Info** (`healthcare_provider_info`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional information about the healthcare provider.
* **Facility Info** (`facility_info`, Text, Optional): Include any details about the facility where the treatment will take place.
* **Consent** (`consent`, Select Multiple, Optional): Confirm or deny the consent for the treatment.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, Text, Required): Sign this section as the minor's parent or guardian to confirm your signature.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`healthcare_provider_signature`, Text, Required): Sign this section as the healthcare provider.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, Text, Optional): Describe the treatment plan and any relevant medical history.
* **Treatment Cost** (`treatment_cost`, Number, Optional): Enter the expected treatment cost.
* **Insurance Info** (`insurance_info`, Text, Optional): Provide any insurance information related to the treatment.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, Text, Optional): Include any payment information for the treatment.
* **Authorization Status** (`authorization_status`, Select One, Required): Choose the current authorization status (Active, Completed, Denied, Pending).
* **Authorization Date Status** (`authorization_date_status`, Select One, Required): Choose the status of the authorization date (Authorized, Denied, Pending).
* **Reason for Denial** (`reason_denied`, Text, Optional): If the authorization was denied, describe the reason.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Add any additional comments about the treatment.
* **Authorization Comments** (`authorization_comments`, Text, Optional): Include any comments or feedback about the authorization process.
* **Healthcare Provider Phone** (`healthcare_provider_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the healthcare provider's contact phone number.
* **Facility Phone** (`facility_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the contact phone number for the facility where the treatment will occur.
