# ประก Rdv Peds Authorization For Medical Treatment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting authorization from a patient's guardians or legal representatives for medical treatment or procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure you are authorized to request medical treatment on behalf of the patient.
2. Fill out all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Select the correct authorization type and period from the dropdown menus.
4. Provide your contact information for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (1)**: Enter the name of the patient requiring medical treatment.
* **Medical Condition (2)**: Describe the patient's medical condition that requires treatment.
* **Authorization Date (3)**: The date on which this authorization is being requested.
* **Authorization Type (4)**: Select the type of treatment or procedure being authorized:
	+ Authorization for treatment
	+ Authorization for surgery
	+ Authorization for other procedures
* **Authorization Period (5)**: Select the period of time for which the treatment is authorized:
	+ Authorization for 24 hours
	+ Authorization for 72 hours
	+ Authorization for other periods
* **Doctor Name (6)**: Enter the name of the doctor authorizing the treatment.
* **Medical Facility (7)**: Enter the name of the medical facility where the treatment will take place.
* **Phone (8)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes. (Optional)
* **Email (9)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes. (Optional)
* **Note (10)**: Enter any additional comments or information for the treating medical staff. (Optional)

## Tips
Please ensure the form is filled out accurately and completely to avoid delays in treatment. If you need assistance or have questions, contact your medical facility for guidance.
