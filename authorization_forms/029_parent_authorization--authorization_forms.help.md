# Parent Authorization - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization from the parent/guardian of a minor applicant, ensuring that we have their consent to process the application. This form is a crucial step in our process to verify the legitimacy of the application and maintain the trust of our minor applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete this form:

1. Enter the name of the applicant's parent/guardian in the `parent_name` field.
2. Provide the email address of the parent/guardian in the `parent_email` field to facilitate communication.
3. Enter the phone number of the parent/guardian in the `parent_phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### `parent_name`
* `parent_name` (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the parent/guardian of the applicant.
### `parent_email`
* `parent_email` (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the parent/guardian to ensure we can contact them for updates and communication.
### `parent_phone`
* `parent_phone` (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the parent/guardian for any urgent matters that may arise during the application process.
