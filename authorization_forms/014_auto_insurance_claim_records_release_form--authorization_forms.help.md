# Auto Insurance Claim Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization from the insured to release their personal and claim-related records to a specified entity or individual.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the following fields:
	* Full Legal Name: Enter the insured's name as it appears on their official documents.
	* Date of Birth: Enter the insured's date of birth.
	* Insurance Company Name: Enter the name of the insurance company holding the records.
	* Claim Number: Enter the reference number for the specific claim.
	* Date of Accident: Enter the date when the incident occurred.
	* Release Records To (Name): Enter the name of the recipient entity or individual to whom the records will be released.
	* Recipient Email: Enter the email address where the records will be sent.
	* Types of Records to Release: Select all categories of records that apply (e.g., Complete Claim File, Medical Records, etc.).
	* Authorization Expiration Date: Enter the date when this consent expires.
	* I understand my revocation rights: Confirm that you understand you may revoke this consent at any time in writing.
	* I agree to electronic release: Confirm that you agree to the digital transmission of the records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name** (Full Legal Name, text, required): Enter the insured's name as it appears on their official documents.
