# Hotel Booking Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Booking Appointment Form is designed for guests to book a room at a hotel or lodge. This form helps the hotel staff to collect necessary information about the guest's stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Check In" page to select the check-in date.
2. On the same page, select the "Check Out" date for the guest's stay.
3. Enter the guest's name in the "Guest Name" field.
4. Enter the guest's phone number in the "Guest Phone" field.
5. Select the number of guests and rooms required in the "Number of Guests" and "Number of Rooms" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the guest's email address in the "Email" field.
7. If the guest has any special requests, enter them in the "Special Requests" field.
8. Select the room type from the "Room Type" dropdown menu (e.g., Single, Double, Suite).
9. Select the amenities the guest would like to have in their room (e.g., TV, Minibar, Wi-Fi).
10. Indicate if the guest requires parking facilities by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Parking" field.
11. Choose whether the hotel has a smoking policy by selecting "Non-Smoking" or "Smoking".
12. Select whether the guest would like to have breakfast provided by choosing "Yes" or "No".
13. If the guest requires meals, select all the meal options they would like to have (e.g., Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner).
14. Leave any additional notes for the hotel staff in the "Notes" field.
15. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form and book the room.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check In** (`date`, required): Select the date the guest will check in.
* **Check Out** (`date`, required): Select the date the guest will check out.
* **Guest Name** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the guest.
* **Guest Phone** (`text`, required): Enter the guest's phone number.
* **Number of Guests** (`number`, required): Select the number of guests.
* **Number of Rooms** (`number`, required): Select the number of rooms.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter the guest's email address.
* **Arrival Date** (`date`, required): Select the date the guest will arrive.
* **Arrival Time** (`time`, required): Select the time the guest will arrive.
* **Special Requests** (`text`, required): Enter any special requests the guest may have.
* **Room Type** (`select_one`, required): Select the type of room the guest would like to have (e.g., Single, Double, Suite).
* **Room Amenities** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the amenities the guest would like to have in their room (e.g., TV, Minibar, Wi-Fi).
* **Parking** (`select_one`, required): Indicate if the guest requires parking facilities by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Smoking Policy** (`select_one`, required): Choose whether the hotel has a smoking policy by selecting "Non-Smoking" or "Smoking".
* **Breakfast** (`select_one`, required): Select whether the guest would like to have breakfast provided by choosing "Yes" or "No".
* **Meals** (`select_multiple`, required): If the guest requires meals, select all the meal options they would like to have (e.g., Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner).
* **Notes** (`note`, required): Leave any additional notes for the hotel staff.
