# Guest Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Guest Satisfaction Survey is a crucial tool for the hospitality establishment to understand its guests' experiences and gather feedback for improvement. By providing honest and complete answers, you will help the establishment provide better services and amenities for future guests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Carefully review each question to ensure you are answering the correct information.
2. Fill in the required information in the corresponding fields.
3. Make sure to answer all the questions accurately and truthfully.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Experience** (`guest_experience_label`, `text`, optional): Please describe your overall experience with the hospitality establishment, including any notable incidents or moments that left an impression on you.
* **Overall** (`overall_experience`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall experience with the establishment on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Event Satisfaction** (`event_satisfaction_label`, `select_one`, optional): Please choose how satisfied you were with your event, whether you were **Satisfied** or **Dissatisfied**.
* **Staff Satisfaction** (`staff_satisfaction_label`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the staff's attitude and behavior towards you during your stay. Choose **Satisfied** if the staff was attentive, friendly, and helpful, or **Dissatisfied** if they were not.
* **Communication Satisfaction** (`communication_satisfaction_label`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether the communication between you and the staff was clear and efficient. Choose **Satisfied** if the communication was good, or **Dissatisfied** if it was not.
* **Food/Drink Satisfaction** (`food_drink_satisfaction_label`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the food and drink services, whether you were **Satisfied** or **Dissatisfied**.
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness_label`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the cleanliness of the establishment, including the rooms, common areas, and amenities. Choose **Yes** if the place was clean, or **No** if it was not.
* **Environment** (`environment_label`, `select_multiple`, optional): Evaluate the ambiance and atmosphere of the establishment, whether it met your expectations or not. Choose **Yes** if the environment was good, or **No** if it was not.
* **Food Quality** (`food_quality_label`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the quality of the food served to you during your stay, whether it was **Good** or **Poor**.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality_label`, `select_multiple`, optional): Assess the service quality of the staff, whether it was **Good** or **Poor**.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when answering the questions.
* Do not leave any questions blank if you have an opinion or experience.
* Take your time to complete the form carefully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the establishment's management team for assistance.
* Your feedback is valuable and helps improve the establishment's services.
* Your responses will remain confidential and will only be used for internal purposes.
