# Hospitality AI Chatbot Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and data from users about their interaction with a hospitality AI chatbot. The data collected will help us improve the chatbot's performance and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, follow the instructions on each page.
*   Make sure to fill in all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
*   You can skip optional fields if you do not have the information or prefer not to provide it.
*   Be as detailed and accurate as possible when providing information.
*   If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in the space provided at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Hotel Details (hotel\_details)**: Please provide the name of the hotel you interacted with the chatbot. This will help us associate the feedback with the specific hotel.
    
*   **Rating (rating)**: Please provide your overall rating of your interaction with the chatbot, using the following options: Good, Fair.
    
*   **Rating Options (rating\_options)**: In addition to the overall rating, please also provide which specific aspects of the chatbot were Good, Fair, or Bad. You can select one or multiple options from the list.
    
*   **Comments (comments)**: Please provide any additional comments or feedback about your interaction with the chatbot. This will help us understand your thoughts and feelings.
    
*   **Phone Number (phone\_number)**: If you used the chatbot through phone, please provide your phone number. This will help us understand your interaction channel.
    
*   **Email Address (email\_address)**: If you interacted with the chatbot through email, please provide your email address. This will help us verify your identity and ensure that we contact you if necessary.
    
*   **Interaction Time (interaction\_time)**: Please provide the time of day when you interacted with the chatbot. This will help us understand your interaction timing preferences.
    
*   **Time of Interaction (time\_of\_interaction)**: Please provide the time of day you started interacting with the chatbot.
    
*   **Date of Interaction (date\_of\_interaction)**: Please provide the date of interaction.
    
*   **Chatbot Name (chatbot\_name)**: Please provide the name of the chatbot you interacted with.
    
*   **Chatbot Version (chatbot\_version)**: Please provide the version of the chatbot you used.
    
*   **Hotel ID (hotel\_id)**: This field is used to identify the hotel you interacted with. Please provide the hotel ID if you know it.
    
*   **Department Name (department\_name)**: If you interacted with a department, please provide the name of the department. This will help us associate the feedback with the department.
    
*   **User Gender (user\_gender)**: Please select your gender from the options: Male, Female, Other.
    
*   **User Location (user\_location)**: Please provide your location (city, country, etc.).
    
*   **User Language (user\_language)**: Please provide the language you used to interact with the chatbot. This will help us understand your language preferences.
    
*   **Interaction Type (interaction\_type)**: If you interacted with the chatbot through a particular channel, please select one or multiple options from the list: Phone, Chat, Email, Other.
    
*   **Interaction Channel (interaction\_channel)**: If you interacted with the chatbot through a particular channel, please select one or multiple options from the list: Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, Other.
    
*   **Additional Comments (additional\_comments)**: If you have any additional comments or feedback about your interaction with the chatbot, please provide them here.
