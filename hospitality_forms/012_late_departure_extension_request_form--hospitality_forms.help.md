# Late Departure Extension Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for requesting a late departure extension from your stay at our establishment. If you are unable to depart as originally planned, please fill out this form to request an extension.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button after filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form in the following order:
	* Page 1: Enter your late departure request.
	* Page 2: Select the new departure date and time.
	* Page 3: Provide details about your guest stay (e.g. room number, check-in date).
	* Page 4: Enter your contact information (e.g. email, phone number).
	* Page 5: Acknowledge our late departure policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Late Departure Request (late_departure_extension_request)** (`Text`, optional):
	+ Please provide a brief explanation for your late departure request.
* **Timing (timing)** (`Date`, optional):
	+ Select the new departure date and time.
* **Guest Details (guest_details)** (`Text`, optional):
	+ Please provide your room number, check-in date, and any other relevant details about your stay.
* **Contact Information (contact_details)** (`Text`, optional):
	+ Enter your email address and/or phone number so we can contact you about your request.
* **Policy Acknowledgment (policy_acknowledgment)** (`Note`, optional):
	+ Please read and agree to our late departure policy. This field is only for acknowledgement purposes and will not be printed on your submitted form.

## Tips
* If you are experiencing any issues or have questions, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Please make sure to fill out all relevant fields accurately to ensure your request is processed correctly.
