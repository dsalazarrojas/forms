# Bed And Breakfast Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for guests to provide feedback on their stay at a Bed and Breakfast establishment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter the dates of your stay, including check-in and check-out dates.
4. Specify the room number or name where you stayed.
5. Select the type of room you stayed in from the provided options.
6. Indicate the number of nights you stayed.
7. Specify the number of guests in your party.
8. Choose the purpose of your visit from the provided options.
9. Select your overall satisfaction with your stay.
10. Rate the cleanliness of your room and facilities.
11. Rate the comfort of your room.
12. Rate the friendliness of our staff.
13. Rate the helpfulness of our staff.
14. Rate the quality of our breakfast.
15. Rate the quality of the room amenities.
16. Rate the location of our establishment.
17. Rate the value for money of your stay.
18. Rate the quality of our WiFi.
19. (Optional) If you have a favorite feature about your stay, describe it in the "Favorite Feature" field.
20. (Optional) If you have any areas for improvement, describe them in the "Areas for Improvement" field.
21. Select how likely you are to recommend our Bed and Breakfast.
22. Indicate whether you plan to visit us again.
23. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`guest_email`, email, required): Please provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Date of Stay** (`stay_date`, date, required): Enter the date you arrived at our Bed and Breakfast.
* **Checkout Date** (`checkout_date`, date, required): Enter the date you checked out.
* **Room Number or Name** (`room_number`, text, required): Specify the room number or name where you stayed.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of room you stayed in, such as Standard, Deluxe, Suite, Family, etc.
* **Number of Nights** (`number_of_nights`, number, required): Enter the number of nights you stayed with us.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required): Enter the number of guests in your party.
* **Purpose of Visit** (`purpose_of_visit`, select_one, required): Select the reason for your visit, such as leisure, business, etc.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with your stay.
* **Cleanliness Rating** (`cleanliness_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the cleanliness of your room and facilities.
* **Comfort Rating** (`comfort_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the comfort of your room.
* **Staff Friendliness** (`staff_friendliness`, select_one, required): Rate the friendliness of our staff.
* **Staff Helpfulness** (`staff_helpfulness`, select_one, required): Rate the helpfulness of our staff.
* **Breakfast Quality** (`breakfast_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of our breakfast.
* **Room Amenities** (`room_amenities`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the room amenities.
* **Location Rating** (`location_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the location of our establishment.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Rate the value for money of your stay.
* **WiFi Quality** (`wifi_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of our WiFi.
* **Favorite Feature** (`favorite_feature`, text, optional): Describe your favorite feature about your stay (if applicable).
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_improvement`, text, optional): Describe areas where we could improve (if applicable).
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_recommend`, select_one, required): Select how likely you are to recommend our Bed and Breakfast.
* **Will You Return** (`will_return`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you plan to visit us again.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other feedback or comments you'd like to share.
