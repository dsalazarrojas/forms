# Guest Service Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Service Incident Report Form is a tool used by staff members to report incidents that occur within the hospitality sector. It helps to collect and track incident data, ensuring that the necessary steps are taken to address and resolve the issue effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the Incident Details section with a brief description of the incident.
2. Enter the Date and Time of the incident using the provided date and time fields.
3. Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details that may be helpful in understanding what happened.
4. If the guest's name is known, enter it in the Guest Name field.
5. Identify the cause of the incident, if known.
6. Describe the actions taken to address the issue.
7. Select the outcome of the incident (Success, Failed, or Other).
8. Enter any manager review comments, if applicable.
9. Schedule a follow-up date and time, if necessary.
10. Provide a brief description of the follow-up action taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details**: A brief description of the incident, including any relevant details that may be helpful in understanding what happened.
* **Date**: The date the incident occurred.
* **Time**: The time the incident occurred.
* **Date & Time**: A combined field for entering the date and time of the incident.
* **Incident Description**: A detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details that may be helpful in understanding what happened.
* **Guest Name**: The name of the guest involved in the incident, if known.
* **Incident Cause**: The reason or cause of the incident, if known.
* **Action Taken**: A description of the steps taken to address the issue.
* **Incident Outcome**: The outcome of the incident (Success, Failed, or Other).
* **Manager Review**: Any comments or feedback from the manager, if applicable.
* **Follow-up Date**: The date of the follow-up action.
* **Follow-up Time**: The time of the follow-up action.
* **Follow-up Date & Time**: A combined field for entering the follow-up date and time.
* **Follow-up Description**: A brief description of the follow-up action taken.
* **Assigned Manager Name**: The name of the manager assigned to handle the incident, if applicable.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed thoroughly and accurately.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure that all necessary information is included.
* If the incident is still ongoing, update the form regularly with new information.
* If the incident is resolved, ensure that the follow-up fields are completed, if applicable.
