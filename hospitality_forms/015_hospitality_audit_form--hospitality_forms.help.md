# hospitality_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hospitality_audit_form is a tool used to collect feedback on the services provided by a hospitality establishment. It is designed to be completed by customers or guests of the establishment, and the feedback will be used to help the establishment identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions to enhance their services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through the form questions and answer them honestly based on your experience.
2. Select the options that best match your experience for each field.
3. Provide additional comments if you have any further feedback or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief note about your experience at the hospitality establishment.
* **Service Quality**: Select the level of service quality you experienced:
	+ Excellent: The service was exceptional and met your expectations.
	+ Good: The service was satisfactory, but not exceptional.
	+ Fair: The service was average, but not impressive.
* **Staff Attitude**: Select the level of staff attitude you experienced:
	+ Friendly: The staff were welcoming and approachable.
	+ Helpful: The staff were helpful and responsive to your needs.
	+ Unhelpful: The staff were not helpful or unresponsive to your needs.
* **Communication**: Select the level of communication you experienced:
	+ Good: The staff communicated clearly and effectively.
	+ Fair: The staff communicated somewhat effectively, but not clearly.
	+ Poor: The staff did not communicate effectively.
* **Cleanliness**: Select the level of cleanliness you experienced:
	+ Excellent: The facilities were spotless and well-maintained.
	+ Good: The facilities were clean, but not spotless.
	+ Fair: The facilities were average, but not impressive.
* **Amenities**: Select the amenities that were available and met your expectations:
	+ Excellent: All amenities were available and exceeded your expectations.
	+ Good: Some amenities were available, but not all were impressive.
	+ Fair: Few amenities were available.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Select the level of overall satisfaction you experienced:
	+ Excellent: You were extremely satisfied with your experience.
	+ Good: You were somewhat satisfied, but not extremely satisfied.
	+ Fair: You were average, but not unsatisfied.
	+ Poor: You were not satisfied.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about your experience.
* **Staff Professionalism**: Select the level of staff professionalism you experienced:
	+ Very Professional: The staff were highly professional.
	+ Somewhat Professional: The staff were somewhat professional.
	+ Not Professional: The staff were not professional.
* **Staff Punctuality**: Select the level of staff punctuality you experienced:
	+ Always: The staff were always on time.
	+ Mostly: The staff were mostly on time.
	+ Sometimes: The staff were not always on time.
