# Reservation Information And Terms Acceptance - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect reservation details and obtain electronic terms agreement from guests. It's an essential step in the booking process, ensuring that you are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of your stay.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully before starting.
2. Answer all required fields (if any) and provide accurate information.
3. Review the terms and conditions, and select the applicable agreements.
4. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
5. Click on the "Submit" button to finalize your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Information (id: 1)**: Enter your reservation details such as dates, room type, and any special requests you may have.
* **Terms and Conditions (id: 2)**: Carefully read through the terms and conditions provided. This section outlines the rules and regulations of your stay.
* **Special Requests (id: 3)**: If you have any specific requests or needs for your stay, such as extra towels or late check-out, please specify them here.
* **Terms Agreement (id: 4)**: Select the terms and conditions that apply to your stay. Make sure to review the options carefully before making a selection.
* **Email (id: 5)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with important updates and information about your stay.
* **Submit (id: 6)**: Review all the information you have entered and click on this button to finalize your reservation.
