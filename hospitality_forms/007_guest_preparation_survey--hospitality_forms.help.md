# Guest Preparation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Guest Preparation Survey is a form used to collect information from guests arriving at a hospitality establishment. This information is used to prepare for their stay and ensure that the staff is informed and prepared to provide a good experience for the guests.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please complete each field with the relevant information.
- You can submit the form once you have filled all the required and optional fields.
- If you encounter any issues or have trouble accessing or completing the form, contact the hospitality staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Type**: Select the category that best describes your status, such as Guest, Staff, Vendor, or Other.
* **Arrival Date**: Enter the date of your arrival in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Arrival Time**: Enter the time of your arrival in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of guests arriving with you.
* **Accommodation Type**: Select the type of accommodation you will be staying at, such as Hotel, Resort, Motel, or Other.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Arrival Method**: Select how you will be arriving, such as By Car, By Plane, By Train, or Other.
* **Arrival Details**: Enter any additional details about your arrival, such as flight numbers or arrival instructions.
* **Arrival Notes**: Enter any important notes or messages you would like to be shared with the staff.
* **Special Requests**: Select any special requests you may have, such as Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Other.
* **Special Request Details**: Enter any additional details about your special requests.
* **Parking Details**: Enter any information about parking, such as vehicle type or parking location.
* **Preferred Language**: Select the language you would prefer to be communicated in, such as English, Spanish, French, or Chinese.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any additional information or instructions that the staff should be aware of.

Note: Please ensure that you fill in all the required fields before submitting the form.
