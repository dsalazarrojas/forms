# Hotel Amenties Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Amenities Access Form is a tool for guests to provide their personal and room information to access hotel amenities. This form helps hotel staff to efficiently manage guest requests and provide a better experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide your room number and floor number to ensure accurate room assignment.
3. Indicate your check-in and check-out times.
4. List any amenities you would like to access, such as extra towels or Wi-Fi.
5. If you have any special requests, such as late check-out or early check-in, please specify them in the "Special Requests" field.
6. Provide any additional comments or information that may be helpful for our staff to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Information**: Enter your name, phone number, and email.
* **Room Information**: Enter your room number and floor number.
* **Room Number**: Enter the actual room number assigned to you.
* **Room Floor**: Enter the floor number where your room is located.
* **Checkin Time**: Enter your check-in time.
* **Checkout Time**: Enter your check-out time.
* **Amenities Access**: Select all the amenities you would like to access, such as 'Wi-Fi', 'Extra Towels', etc.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional comments or details about your stay.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests, such as late check-out or early check-out.
* **Additional Requests**: Enter any additional comments or requests.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback you may have about your stay.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your room number and floor number are accurate to avoid delays in accessing amenities.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our staff.
* We appreciate your feedback and suggestions to help us improve our services!
