# Hospitality Front Desk Associate Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from applicants for the Hospitality Front Desk Associate position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to enter a valid email address that is checked regularly.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This is the number that we will use to contact you.
4. Enter your work phone number in the "Work Phone Number" field. If you do not have a work phone number, you can leave this field blank.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field. This can be a street address or a PO box.
6. Enter your city in the "City" field.
7. Enter your state in the "State" field.
8. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
9. Select your availability days of the week (Monday to Sunday) in the "Availability" field.
10. If you have any notes or comments about your availability, enter them in the "Availability Time" field.
11. If you have any additional notes or comments, enter them in the "Notes" fields (e.g., "Notes", "Notes 2", "Notes 3", etc.).
