# Short Term Rental Guest Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from short-term rental guests, helping properties to understand their experiences, likes, and dislikes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your guest information.
2. Enter the Property ID number if available.
3. Provide your arrival date.
4. Indicate the duration of your stay.
5. Rate your satisfaction with your stay.
6. Share any issues or problems you experienced during your stay.
7. Share any suggestions for improvement.
8. Add any comments about your stay.
9. Enter your contact information (email and phone number).
10. Choose the type of property (e.g., Vacation Rental, Serviced Apartment, Managed Property).
11. Select the type of stay (e.g., Vacation, Short-term, Long-term, Business).
12. Indicate the purpose of your stay (e.g., Leisure, Business, Work, Study).
13. Choose the type of room you stayed in (e.g., Standard Room, Suite, Apartment).
14. Select the amenities that were available in your room.
15. Rate the quality of staff interaction during your stay.
16. Rate the cleanliness of the property.
17. Rate the availability of amenities.
18. Provide feedback on the location of the property.
19. Rate your overall satisfaction with the property.
20. Indicate whether you would rebook at the same property.
21. Suggest whether you would recommend the property to others.
22. Add any additional comments about your stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Information** (`guest_info`, text, optional): This is where you can enter your name, email, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Property ID** (`property_id`, number, optional): If you have the Property ID number, please enter it here. This will help us to identify your stay.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you arrived at the property.
* **Stay Duration** (`stay_duration`, number, optional): Indicate how many days you stayed at the property.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with your stay on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **What Went Wrong** (`satisfaction_what_went_wrong`, text, optional): If there were any issues during your stay, please describe them here.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving the property.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your stay.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, optional): Enter your email address and/or phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Property Name** (`property_name`, text, optional): If you're familiar with the property name, please enter it here.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of property you stayed at (e.g., Vacation Rental, Serviced Apartment, Managed Property).
* **Stay Type** (`stay_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of stay you had (e.g., Vacation, Short-term, Long-term, Business).
* **Stay Purpose** (`stay_purpose`, select_one, optional): Indicate the purpose of your stay (e.g., Leisure, Business, Work, Study).
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of room you stayed in (e.g., Standard Room, Suite, Apartment).
* **Room Amenities** (`room_ammenities`, select_multiple, optional): Select the amenities that were available in your room.
* **Staff Interaction** (`staff_interaction`, number, optional): Rate the quality of staff interaction during your stay.
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, number, optional): Rate the cleanliness of the property.
* **Amenities** (`amenities`, number, optional): Rate the availability of amenities.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Provide feedback on the location of the property.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the property.
* **Would Rebook** (`would_rebook`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you would rebook at the same property.
* **Recommend** (`recommend`, select_multiple, optional): Suggest whether you would recommend the property to others.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your stay.
* **Property Contact** (`property_contact`, text, optional): Enter any contact information for the property.
