# Beach Resort Sunbed Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to reserve a sunbed at the beach resort for guests' use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

1. **Guest Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
2. **Room Number**: If you are staying at the resort, please provide your room number.
3. **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address so we can reach you for any updates.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for any last-minute questions or changes.
5. **Reservation Date**: Select the date for your sunbed reservation.
6. **Time Slot**: Choose your preferred time slot for the sunbed.
7. **Sunbed Type**: Select the type of sunbed you prefer (Standard, Premium Cabana, Beach Lounger, Daybed, or VIP Cabana).
8. **Number of Sunbeds**: Indicate how many sunbeds you require.
9. **Location Preference**: Choose your preferred location on the beach (Front Row - Waterfront, Second Row, Shaded Area, Pool Side, or No Preference).
10. **Umbrella Needed**: Check if you need an umbrella.
11. **Towel Service**: Indicate if you need towel service.
12. **Additional Services**: Select any additional services you require (Beverage Service, Food Service, Massage Service, Water Sports Equipment, or None).
13. **Special Occasion**: If you are celebrating a special occasion, select the type of occasion.
14. **Accessibility Needs**: If you have any accessibility needs, provide them in this field.
15. **Estimated Total Cost**: Please note the estimated total cost for your reservation.
16. **Payment Method**: Choose how you will pay for your reservation (Room Charge, Credit Card, Debit Card, or Cash).
17. **I Understand Cancellation Policy**: Check to acknowledge that you have read and agree to the cancellation policy.
