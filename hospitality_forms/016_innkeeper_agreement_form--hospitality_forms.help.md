# Innkeeper Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Innkeeper Agreement Form is a standardized document that helps innkeepers gather necessary information from their guests. This form serves as a contract between the inn and the guest, outlining expectations and requirements for the stay.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill out all the required fields.
*   Provide accurate and honest information about yourself as the guest.
*   Review the Terms carefully to ensure you understand the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General (1)**: Enter your name and contact information as the guest.
*   **Guest Details (2)**: Provide your address, email, and phone number to help us contact you during your stay.
*   **Property Details (3)**: If you have any specific property-related requests or concerns, please mention them here.
*   **Innkeeper Details (4)**: Share any specific details about your interaction with the innkeeper, such as any special requests or concerns.
*   **Terms (5)**: Carefully review and agree to our terms and conditions for your stay.
*   **Signature (6)**: Sign the agreement to confirm your understanding and acceptance of our terms.

## Tips

*   Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
*   Review the Terms section carefully to understand your responsibilities and rights as a guest.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask the innkeeper for clarification.

Note: The form's fields are not strictly necessary for functionality, so I've assumed that all are optional but still included a "Fill out all the required fields" instruction for clarity. If you want to make some fields required, please let me know.
