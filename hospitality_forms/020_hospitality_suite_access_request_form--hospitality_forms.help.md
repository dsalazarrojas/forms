# Hospitality Suite Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to the Hospitality Suite for guests and staff. It is a mandatory form to ensure that all visitors are aware of and comply with the rules and regulations of the suite.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out your guest details, including their name and any relevant contact information.
2. Enter the visit dates for which you are requesting access.
3. Specify the purpose of your visit.
4. Check the box to acknowledge that you have read and agree to the rules.
5. Enter any additional information or notes that are relevant to your visit.
6. If you are responsible for other guests, please specify their names in the "I understand I am responsible for all guests" field.
7. If you are responsible for other staff members, please specify their names in the "I understand I am responsible for all staff" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Details**: Please enter the name of the guest(s) visiting the Hospitality Suite. You can enter multiple guests, separated by commas.
	+ Label: Guest Details
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Visit Dates**: Enter the dates of your visit to the Hospitality Suite.
	+ Label: Visit Dates
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Purpose**: Enter a brief description of the purpose of your visit to the Hospitality Suite.
	+ Label: Purpose
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Rules Acknowledgment**: Check this box to confirm that you have read and agree to the rules and regulations of the Hospitality Suite.
	+ Label: Rules Acknowledgment
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: No
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication with you or your team.
	+ Label: Email
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: No
* **Phone**: Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
	+ Label: Phone
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Other (Please specify)**: If you have any additional information or a purpose that is not listed above, please enter it here.
	+ Label: Other (Please specify)
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Guest Notes**: Enter any additional information that is relevant to your visit.
	+ Label: Guest Notes
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Staff Notes**: Enter any additional information that is relevant to your team.
	+ Label: Staff Notes
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **I understand I am responsible for all guests**: Check the boxes of the guests you are responsible for during your visit.
	+ Label: I understand I am responsible for all guests
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: No
* **I understand I am responsible for all staff**: Check the boxes of the staff members you are responsible for during your visit.
	+ Label: I understand I am responsible for all staff
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: No

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to the Hospitality Suite staff for assistance.
