# travel_and_hotel_partnership_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel and hotel partnership inquiry form is designed to gather information from potential partners about their interest in collaborating with the company. This form helps the company to understand the partner's preferences and requirements, which aids in creating a successful partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Partnership Request that best describes your inquiry.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Enter your travel request information, including any specific options you have chosen.
4. Choose a travel date and time that suits your needs.
5. Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to include.
6. Select a reviewer for the partnership inquiry.
7. Choose the email notification options you'd like to receive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partnership Request** (`partnership_request`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the Partnership Request that best describes your inquiry.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address.
* **Travel Request Info** (`travel_request_info`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose any specific travel request options you have.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_request_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your travel request.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_request_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your travel request.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your partnership inquiry.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a reviewer for your partnership inquiry.
* **Email Notification** (`email_notification`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the email notification options you'd like to receive.

## Tips

* Please make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required information is provided.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the company for assistance.
* Make sure to choose the correct Partnership Request and Travel Date options to ensure that the company can process your inquiry efficiently.
