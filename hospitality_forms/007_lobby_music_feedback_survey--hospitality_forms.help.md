# Lobby Music Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Lobby Music Feedback Survey" form is designed to collect feedback on our lobby music, allowing us to understand user preferences and make informed decisions about our music selection and volume.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Read through the form carefully and understand what information we're looking for.
2.  Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), if any.
3.  Answer the questions honestly, providing as much detail as you feel necessary.
4.  Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback about our lobby music. You can type any thoughts or suggestions you may have.
*  **How would you rate our lobby music** (`rating_1`, number, optional): Rate our lobby music from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
*  **How would you rate the volume of our lobby music** (`rating_2`, number, optional): Rate the volume of our lobby music from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
*  **What music genre do you prefer** (`preference`, select_multiple, optional): Select all music genres that you enjoy listening to, e.g., "Jazz", "Classical", "Pop", etc.
*  **What music tempo do you prefer** (`preference_2`, select_one, optional): Select the tempo you prefer for our lobby music, e.g., "Fast", "Slow", "Medium".
*  **Any notes** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about our lobby music.
*  **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
*  **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips

*  Please answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as you feel necessary.
*  If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "User Input" field.
*  Make sure to double-check your answers before submitting the form.
