# Remote Housing Provider Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Housing Provider Registration form is used to collect information from individuals or companies that provide housing services for guests or customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form with the required information:
	* Enter your name in the "Name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
	* Enter your address in the "Address" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
	* Select your provider type from the "Provider Type" drop-down menu.
	* Enter your license number (if applicable).
2. If you have a written agreement, provide it in the "Agreement" field.
3. Enter the date you signed the agreement in the "Agreement Date" field.
4. If you have an end date for the agreement, enter it in the "End Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`text`, Required/Optional): Enter your name as it appears on your business license or registration.
- **Email** (`email`, Required/Optional): Enter your primary email address associated with your business or organization.
- **Address** (`text`, Required/Optional): Enter your business address as it appears on your business license or registration.
- **Phone** (`text`, Required/Optional): Enter your primary phone number associated with your business or organization.
- **Agreement** (`text`, Required/Optional): If you have a written agreement, upload it as a PDF or provide a link to a secure online version.
- **License Number** (`text`, Required/Optional): Enter your business license number (if applicable).
- **Provider Type** (`select_one`, Required/Optional): Select your provider type from the menu:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
- **Agreement Date** (`date`, Required/Optional): Enter the date you signed the agreement.
- **End Date** (`date`, Required/Optional): If you have an end date for the agreement, enter it here.

## Tips
- Make sure to use your business email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you.
- If you have any issues with the form, contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
