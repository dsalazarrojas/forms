# Beverage Menu Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beverage Menu Knowledge Quiz is a tool to evaluate your knowledge on beverages, their preparation, ingredients, and serving standards. This quiz is designed to assess your understanding of our menu offerings and your ability to provide excellent service.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the form carefully, answering all required questions.
2.  Make sure to choose the correct options for each question.
3.  Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge.
- **Staff Position** (`staff_role`, select_one, required): Select your role in the team, such as Server, Bartender, Host/Hostess, Manager, Back-of-house staff, or Other Hospitality.
- **Primary Work Shift** (`shift_type`, select_one, required): Choose your primary work shift, which can be Day shift, Evening shift, Night shift, or Mix of shifts.
- **Quiz Date** (`quiz_date`, date, required): Enter the date this quiz is being taken.
- **House Special Margarita** (`signature_drink1`, text, required): List the main ingredients in our House Special Margarita.
- **Seasonal Sangria Recipe** (`signature_drink2`, text, required): Describe the main components and garnish of our Seasonal Sangria.
- **Mojito Distinguishing Features** (`signature_drink3`, text, required): Explain what makes our Mojito different from competitors.
- **Best Wine Pairing for Fish** (`wine_pairing1`, select_one, required): Choose the best wine type to pair with fish dishes, such as Red wine, White wine, or Rosé wine.
- **Popular Red Wine** (`wine_pairing2`, text, required): Name our most popular red wine and its origin (brand and region).
- **Spirit in Old Fashioned** (`spirit_question`, text, required): Identify the spirit used in our traditional Old Fashioned cocktail.
- **Cosmopolitan Preparation** (`cocktail_ingredients`, text, required): List the ingredients and proportions to make our House Cosmopolitan.
- **Cocktail Technique** (`cocktail_technique`, select_one, required): Choose how to make our House Cosmopolitan, such as Shaken or Stirred.
- **Virgin Mojito Ingredients** (`non_alcoholic`, text, required): List the ingredients in our Virgin Mojito.
- **Number of Beers on Tap** (`beer_selection`, number, required): Enter the current number of beers available on tap.
- **Beer Types** (`beer_type`, text, required): Name three types of beer we serve and their styles.
- **Highest Alcohol Content** (`beer_alcohol`, text, required): Identify the beer with the highest alcohol content.
- **Specialty Coffee Preparation** (`specialty_coffee`, text, required): Describe how we prepare our specialty coffee drinks, such as espresso, cappuccino, latte.
- **Tea Varieties** (`tea_types`, text, required): List the types of tea available on our menu.
- **Fresh Juice Options** (`juice_drinks`, text, required): List the fresh juice options available.
- **Common Allergens** (`allergen_knowledge`, text, required): Identify common allergens found in our beverages, such as nuts, gluten, dairy, etc.
- **Recommended Serving Temperature for White Wine** (`recommended_serving`, text, required): Enter the recommended serving temperature for white wine in Fahrenheit.
- **Glass Type for Martini** (`glassware`, text, required): Choose the glass type used for Martinis.
- **Premium Beverage Upsell Strategy** (`upsell_strategy`, text, required): Explain your strategy for recommending premium beverages to customers.

Note that the explanations are based on the provided YAML fields and aim to provide a clear understanding of each field's purpose and required information.
