# hospitality_cultural_guide_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hospitality cultural guide referral form is used to gather information about guests who are being referred to our cultural guides. This form helps us to better understand the guest's background, preferences, and needs, which allows us to provide them with a more personalized and effective experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (applicant_name, email, phone, department, and job_title).
2. If applicable, fill in the optional fields (referral_name, referral_relationship, follow_up_date, follow_up_details, note, and additional_comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the guest you are referring.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the guest you are referring.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the guest you are referring.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department or team the guest is part of.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the guest you are referring.
* **Referral Name** (`referral_name`, text, optional): If you are referring the guest to yourself, enter your name.
* **Referral Relationship** (`referral_relationship`, text, optional): Briefly describe your relationship with the guest.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): If you plan to follow up with the guest after the referral, enter the date.
* **Follow-up Details** (`follow_up_details`, text, optional): If you plan to follow up with the guest after the referral, provide any additional details.
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the guest you are referring.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other comments about the guest you are referring.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Be as specific as possible when filling in the optional fields.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or the cultural guide team for assistance.
- If you have already submitted a referral, please do not submit another one unless the circumstances have significantly changed.
