# Hotel Meal Service Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Hotel Meal Service Registration form, which is used to register meal requests and preferences for hotel guests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete the form, simply select the "Guest Name" field and enter your name as you would like it to appear in your meal requests.
2. In the "Contact Number" field, enter your contact number so that our team can reach out to you for any further questions or concerns.
3. You can choose your preferred meal option from the "Meal Preference" dropdown menu.
4. If you have a specific time in mind for your meal service, you can select it using the "Schedule Service" and "Schedule Service Time" fields.
5. If you have any special requests or preferences, you can type them in the "Special Request" field.
6. You can also select the language you prefer to communicate in from the "Service Type" dropdown menu.
7. If you'd like to be assigned to a specific staff member, you can choose them from the "Assigned To" dropdown menu.
8. Please select the status of your meal request from the "Status" dropdown menu (e.g., In progress, Completed, Pending).
9. You can add any additional notes about your meal request in the "Note" field.
10. Finally, indicate whether you've received confirmation of your meal request from our team in the "Confirmation" dropdown menu.
