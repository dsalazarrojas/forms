# family_friendly_dinner_reservation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a family's visit to a restaurant, including meal preferences, guest count, arrival and departure dates and times, and any special requests or needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of meal you would like to have (Dinner, Brunch, or Lunch).
2. Enter the total number of guests in your party.
3. Choose the arrival and departure dates for your visit.
4. Select the arrival and departure times for your visit.
5. If you have any special requests or needs, such as a high chair or pacifier, select them from the list.
6. Enter your name, email, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
7. Confirm that you have submitted this form and if you would like to receive any messages or confirmations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Friendly Dinner** (`family_friendly_dinner_reservation_form`, Select Multiple): Choose the type of meal you would like to have (Dinner, Brunch, or Lunch).
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, Number): Enter the total number of guests in your party.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, Date): Select the date you plan to arrive at the restaurant.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, Time): Select the time you plan to arrive at the restaurant.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, Date): Select the date you plan to depart from the restaurant.
* **Departure Time** (`departure_time`, Time): Select the time you plan to depart from the restaurant.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, Select One): If you have any special requests or needs, such as a high chair or pacifier, select them from the list.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Number of Adults** (`number_of_adults`, Number): Enter the number of adults in your party.
* **Number of Children** (`number_of_children`, Number): Enter the number of children in your party.
* **Note** (`note`, Text): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Form Submission Confirmation** (`form_submission_confirmation`, Select Multiple): Confirm that you have submitted this form and if you would like to receive any messages or confirmations.
* **Other Form Submission Confirmation** (`other_form_submission_confirmation`, Select Multiple): Confirm that you have submitted this form and if you would like to receive any messages or confirmations.
* **Other Note** (`other_note`, Text): Enter any other notes or comments.
* **Special Note** (`special_note`, Text): Enter any special notes or comments.
* **Restaurant Confirmation** (`restaurant_confirmation`, Select Multiple): Confirm that you have been contacted by the restaurant.
* **Message to Restaurant** (`message_to_restaurant`, Text): Enter any messages or notes to the restaurant.
* **Send to Owners** (`send_to_owners`, Select Multiple): Choose if you would like to send a copy of this form to the restaurant owners.
* **Send to Waitlist** (`send_to_waitlist`, Select Multiple): Choose if you would like to send a copy of this form to the waitlist.
* **Restaurant Selection** (`restaurant_selection`, Select Multiple): Choose from the list of restaurants to have dinner at.
