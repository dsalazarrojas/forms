# Hospitality Seasonal Employment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from seasonal staff about their employment experience, allowing us to identify areas for improvement and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the feedback questions in the **Feedback Questions** field.
2. Provide any additional suggestions or comments in the **Suggestions** field.
3. Identify any improvement areas or needs in the **Improvement Areas** field.
4. Provide any comments about the form in the **Seasonal Employment Form Comments** field.
5. If you have any suggestions for the agency that worked with you, enter them in the **Seasonal Employment Agency Suggestions** field.
6. Enter any additional feedback notes in the **Seasonal Employment Feedback Notes** field.
7. Describe any improvement plans for the agency in the **Seasonal Employment Agency Improvement Plan** field.
8. Rate your satisfaction with the seasonal employment opportunity in the **Seasonal Employment Satisfaction** field.
9. If you have any recommendations for future seasonal employment opportunities, enter them in the **Seasonal Employment Recommendation** field.
10. Enter any additional comments about the form in the **Seasonal Employment Form Comments 2** field.
11. Record the date and time you completed this form in the **Seasonal Employment Form Completion Date and Time** field.
12. Record the date the form was created in the **Seasonal Employment Form Creation Date** field.
13. If any revisions were made to the form, record the revision date in the **Seasonal Employment Form Revision Date** field.
14. If any status updates were made, enter them in the **Seasonal Employment Form Status** field.
15. If you need to specify the agency for the second seasonal employment opportunity, enter it in the **Seasonal Employment Agency 2** field.
16. Specify the seasonal employment position you held in the **Seasonal Employment Position** field.
17. Enter the start date of your seasonal employment opportunity in the **Seasonal Employment Start Date** field.
18. Enter the end date of your seasonal employment opportunity in the **Seasonal Employment End Date** field.
19. If any further revisions were made to the form, record the date in the **Seasonal Employment Form Revision Date 2** field.
20. If you submitted the form, record the submission date in the **Seasonal Employment Form Submission Date** field.
21. If you are specifying another agency for the seasonal employment opportunity, enter the date in the **Seasonal Employment Agency 2** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seasonal Staff Feedback** (`seasonal_staff_feedback`, text, required): Provide your overall feedback about your seasonal employment experience.
* **Team** (`team`, select_one, required): Choose the team you worked with.
* **Seasonal Employment Agency** (`seasonal_employment_agency`, select_multiple, required): Select any agencies you worked with.
* **Feedback Questions** (`feedback_questions`, note, optional): Answer any questions or comments about your seasonal employment experience.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, note, optional): Provide any suggestions or comments about the form.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional): Identify any areas that need improvement or could be improved.
* **Seasonal Employment Form Comments** (`seasonal_employment_form_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments about the form.
* **Seasonal Employment Agency Suggestions** (`seasonal_employment_agency_suggestions`, text, optional): Enter any suggestions for the agency you worked with.
* **Seasonal Employment Feedback Notes** (`seasonal_employment_feedback_notes`, text, optional): Enter any feedback notes.
* **Seasonal Employment Agency Improvement Plan** (`seasonal_employment_agency_improvement_plan`, text, optional): Describe any improvement plans for the agency.
* **Seasonal Employment Satisfaction** (`seasonal_employment_satisfaction`, text, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the seasonal employment opportunity.
* **Seasonal Employment Recommendation** (`seasonal_employment_recommendation`, text, optional): Enter any recommendations for future seasonal employment opportunities.
* **Seasonal Employment Form Comments 2** (`seasonal_employment_form_comments_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the form.
* **Seasonal Employment Form Completion Date and Time** (`seasonal_employment_form_completion_date` and `seasonal_employment_form_completion_time`, date and time, optional): Record the date and time you completed this form.
* **Seasonal Employment Form Creation Date** (`seasonal_employment_form_creation_date`, date, optional): Record the date the form was created.
* **Seasonal Employment Form Revision Date** (`seasonal_employment_form_revision_date`, date, optional): If any revisions were made, record the date.
* **Seasonal Employment Form Status** (`seasonal_employment_form_status`, text, optional): Enter any status updates.
* **Seasonal Employment Agency 2** (`seasonal_employment_agency_2`, text, optional): If you need to specify the agency for the second seasonal employment opportunity, enter it here.
* **Seasonal Employment Position** (`seasonal_employment_position`, text, optional): Specify the seasonal employment position you held.
* **Seasonal Employment Start Date** (`seasonal_employment_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your seasonal employment opportunity.
* **Seasonal Employment End Date** (`seasonal_employment_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your seasonal employment opportunity.
* **Seasonal Employment Form Revision Date 2** (`seasonal_employment_form_revision_date_2`, date, optional): If any further revisions were made, record the date.
* **Seasonal Employment Form Submission Date** (`seasonal_employment_form_submission_date`, date, optional): If you submitted the form, record the submission date.
