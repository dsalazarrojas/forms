# Hotel Staff Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to onboard new employees into the hotel's staff. It collects essential information about new employees for internal management and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete the form, please ensure you have the following information handy:
    - Your employee name
    - Your job title
    - Your department
    - Your date of hire
    - Your date of birth
    - Your phone number
    - Your email
    - Emergency contact information (name, relation, phone number, email, address, city, state, zip, and country)
    - Manager information (name, email, job title, and phone number)

2.  Start by filling in your personal details:
    - Employee Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
    - Job Title: Enter your job title as assigned by the hotel management.
    - Department: Enter the department you are a part of.
    - Date Hired: Enter the date you started working at the hotel.
    - Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth.

3.  Next, fill in your contact information:
    - Phone Number: Enter your mobile number or other contact number you prefer to be contacted on.
    - Email: Enter your work or personal email address you use for communication.
    - Manager Name: Enter the name of your direct supervisor or manager.
    - Manager Email: Enter the email address of your manager.
    - Manager Job Title: Enter your manager's job title.
    - Manager Phone: Enter your manager's contact phone number.

4.  Then, provide your emergency contact information:
    - Emergency Contact Name: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
    - Emergency Contact Relation: Select the relation of the emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, etc.).
    - Emergency Contact Number: Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
    - Emergency Contact Email: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
    - Emergency Contact Address: Enter the address of your emergency contact.
    - Emergency Contact City: Enter the city of your emergency contact's address.
    - Emergency Contact State: Enter the state of your emergency contact's address.
    - Emergency Contact Zip: Enter the zip code of your emergency contact's address.
    - Emergency Contact Country: Enter the country of your emergency contact's address.

5.  Finally, provide any other information as per the instructions provided.
