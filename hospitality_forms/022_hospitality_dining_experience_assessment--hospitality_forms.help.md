# Hospitality Dining Experience Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us assess your overall dining experience at our establishment. Your feedback will help us improve our services and provide a better experience for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer the questions honestly, and try to provide as much detail as possible. You can select only one option for multiple-choice questions, unless otherwise specified. If you have any issues or questions, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction (1)**: Please rate your overall satisfaction with our service, from 1 to 5.
* **Quality Of Service (2)**: Please describe your experience of our service quality.
* **Food Quality (3)**: Please rate the quality of the food you had during your visit, from 1 to 5.
* **Ambiance (4)**: Please select if our ambiance was Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Recommend to a Friend (5)**: Please select if you would recommend our establishment to a friend.
* **Feedback (6)**: Please provide any additional feedback you have about your visit.
* **Contact Info (7)**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Phone Number (8)**: Please enter your phone number so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Visit Date (9)**: Please select the date you visited our establishment.
* **Visit Time (10)**: Please select the time you visited our establishment.
* **Helpfulness Of Staff (11)**: Please rate how helpful our staff was during your visit, from 1 to 5.
* **Cleanliness (12)**: Please rate the cleanliness of our establishment, from 1 to 5.
* **Food Cost (13)**: Please rate the value for money of the food, from 1 to 5.
* **Service Cost (14)**: Please rate the value for money of the service, from 1 to 5.
* **Overall Cost (15)**: Please rate the overall value for money of your visit, from 1 to 5.
* **Value For Money (16)**: Please rate if you felt that the value for money of our food and service was good, fair, or bad.
* **Will Recommend (17)**: Please select if you will recommend our establishment to a friend.
* **Will Return (18)**: Please select if you will return to our establishment.
* **Will Return Again (19)**: Please select if you will return to our establishment again.
* **Visit Frequency (20)**: Please select how frequently you visit our establishment.
* **Friendliness Of Staff (21)**: Please rate how friendly our staff was during your visit, from 1 to 5.
* **Parking Quality (22)**: Please select if our parking quality was Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Will Spend Money (23)**: Please select if you will spend money at our establishment.
* **Visit Reason (24)**: Please select the reason for your visit.
* **Recommend Staff (25)**: Please select if you will recommend our staff to a friend.
* **Visit Frequency Again (26)**: Please select how frequently you will visit our establishment again.
* **Visit Duration (27)**: Please select how long your visit was.
* **Staff Hired (28)**: Please select if our staff was helpful in hiring you.
* **Staff Hire Duration (29)**: Please select the duration of our staff hire.
* **Staff Hire Frequency (30)**: Please select how frequently our staff is hired.
* **Food Quality Score (31)**: Please rate the quality of the food, from 1 to 5.
* **Staff Quality Score (32)**: Please rate the quality of our staff, from 1 to 5.
* **Value Score (33)**: Please rate the overall value of your visit, from 1 to 5.
* **Cleanliness Score (34)**: Please rate the cleanliness of our establishment, from 1 to 5.
