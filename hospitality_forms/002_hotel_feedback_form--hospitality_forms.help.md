# Hotel Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from guests about their stay at the hotel. This form is designed to gather information on their overall satisfaction, room condition, services quality, food quality, and any other feedback or suggestions they might have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your guest name in the text field labeled "Guest Name".
2. Enter your email address in the email field.
3. Select the correct room number from the options provided.
4. Choose the type of room you stayed in from the options provided.
5. Select the condition of your room from the options provided.
6. Rate the quality of the services you experienced from the options provided.
7. Rate the quality of the food you had from the options provided.
8. Choose your overall satisfaction with the stay from the options provided.
9. If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, you can enter them in the text field labeled "Feedback".
9. If you have any further suggestions, you can enter them in the text field labeled "Suggestions".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (text, optional): Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you.
* **Room Number** (number, required): Select the number of the room where you stayed.
* **Room Type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of room where you stayed from the options provided.
* **Room Condition** (select_multiple, required): Select the condition of your room from the options provided.
* **Services Quality** (select_one, required): Rate the quality of the services you experienced from the options provided.
* **Food Quality** (select_multiple, required): Rate the quality of the food you had from the options provided.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (select_one, required): Choose your overall satisfaction with the stay from the options provided.
* **Feedback** (text, optional): If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, you can enter them in this text field.
* **Suggestions** (text, optional): If you have any further suggestions, you can enter them in this text field.
