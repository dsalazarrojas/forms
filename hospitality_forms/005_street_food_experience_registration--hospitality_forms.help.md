# Street Food Experience Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your street food experience. Please take a few minutes to fill it out accurately and honestly. The data will be used for marketing, feedback, or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Form Submission**: Enter a brief description of your experience with street food.
2. **Name**: Enter your full name as it should appear on any official documents or receipts.
3. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
5. **Address**: Enter your street food vendor contact address (if any).
6. **Dietary Restrictions**: Select all applicable options (e.g., Gluten Free, Vegetarian, Vegan, None).
7. **Experience Level**: Select your level of experience with street food (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
8. **Emergency Contact Information**: Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
9. **Consent**: Select whether you consent to be contacted for further information or research.
10. **Email**: Enter your email address.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Be honest in your answers to avoid any confusion or misidentification.
* Double-check your phone number and email for accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask a representative for clarification.

Note: The required fields are **Dietary Restrictions**, **Experience Level**, and **Consent**. If you're unsure about any other field, please consult a representative for assistance.
