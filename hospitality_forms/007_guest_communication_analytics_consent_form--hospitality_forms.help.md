# Guest Communication Analytics Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Communication Analytics Consent Form is designed to collect guest communication preferences and contact information for improving communication between guests and the hospitality team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information, including your phone number, email, and any other contact methods you prefer.
2. Choose how you would like to be contacted (SMS, Email, Phone, WhatsApp, or Other).
3. Check the box to indicate if you consent to being contacted.
4. Add any additional comments or notes you would like to share with the hospitality team.
5. Select who the form should be assigned to.
6. Add any additional notes if necessary.
7. Finally, review your completed form and submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Communication Analytics Form Data** (`guest_communication_analytics_form_data`): This is the main content area where you can enter your guest communication preferences and contact information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Enter your contact phone number, email address, or any other contact details you prefer.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`): Choose how you would like to be contacted (SMS, Email, Phone, WhatsApp, or Other).
* **Consent** (`consent`): Check the box to indicate if you consent to being contacted.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Add any additional comments or notes you would like to share with the hospitality team.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Enter any additional contact details you prefer.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Contact SMS** (`contact_sms`): Enter your SMS contact information.
* **Contact WhatsApp** (`contact_whatsapp`): Enter your WhatsApp contact information.
* **Other Contact Methods** (`other_contact_methods`): Enter any other contact methods you prefer.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Select who the form should be assigned to.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Add any additional notes if necessary.
* **Output File** (`output_file`): Enter the output file name.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Enter the assigned tool name.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`): Enter the form ID number.
* **Created By** (`created_by`): Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date the form was created.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time the form was created.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`): Enter the form version number.
* **Category** (`category`): Enter the category of the form.
* **Description** (`description`): Enter a brief description of the form.
* **Form ID** (`form_ids`): Enter the form ID number.
* **Output File** (`output_file`): Enter the output file name.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional notes if necessary.

This guide is designed to help you complete the Guest Communication Analytics Consent Form accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the hospitality team.
