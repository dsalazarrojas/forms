# Hospitality Language Proficiency Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about language proficiency in various languages and related details about a specific German occupation or other occupation. This data is intended for employment purposes or other related activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, please enter the desired title for this form submission.
2. Next, enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
4. Provide your phone number for further communication.
5. For each language listed, select your level of proficiency:
	* Basic: You have a basic understanding of the language, which may be useful for general communication.
	* Elementary: You have an elementary understanding of the language, which may be sufficient for simple tasks.
	* Intermediate: You have an intermediate level of understanding, which can be applied to more complex tasks.
	* Advanced: You have an advanced level of understanding, which can be applied to highly technical tasks.
	* Mastery: You have achieved a high level of expertise in the language, which can be applied to the most complex tasks.
6. If you have a background in a specific German occupation, please select the corresponding education level (e.g., High School, Vocational, Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctorate).
7. If you have a background in an "Other" occupation, please enter a brief description of your occupation and provide additional details in the "Other Detail" field.
8. Finally, review all information entered and submit the form.
