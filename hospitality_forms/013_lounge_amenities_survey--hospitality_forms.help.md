# lounge_amenities_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lounge amenities survey is a tool designed to gather feedback on the quality of lounge services to help identify areas of improvement and increase guest satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your response to "guest_info" based on the options provided.
2. Rate the quality of our food, drinks, and staff on a scale from 1-6, choosing the best fit from the options provided.
3. Choose the amenities that were available during your visit to the lounge.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
5. Enter your contact information (email, phone number, or other) for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **guest_info** (Select One, Required): Select the most accurate option that describes your status as a guest. This helps us understand who you are and tailor our services to your needs.
	+ 1: Tourist/Traveler
	+ 2: Resident
	+ 3: Local
	+ 4: Business Guest
	+ 5: Other
* **rating_scale** (Select Multiple, Required): Rate the quality of our amenities and services on a scale from 1-2 and 3-6. Choose the best fit from the options provided.
	+ 1-2: Poor, Not Satisfied
	+ 3-4: Average
	+ 5-6: Excellent, Satisfied
* **rating_food** (Number, Optional): Rate the quality of our food on a scale from 1-6.
* **rating_drinks** (Number, Optional): Rate the quality of our drinks on a scale from 1-6.
* **rating_staff** (Number, Optional): Rate the quality of our staff on a scale from 1-6.
* **feedback** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose from the options provided to describe your overall feedback on the lounge experience.
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
* **amenity_list** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the amenities that were available during your visit to the lounge.
	+ Wi-Fi
	+ Comfortable Seating
	+ Power Outlets
	+ Charging Station
	+ Food/Drink Service
* **additional_comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the lounge experience.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **contact_info** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **survey_code** (Text, Optional): Enter any survey code or reference number provided to you.
* **date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of your visit to the lounge.
* **time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of your visit to the lounge.
* **time_zone** (Time, Optional): Enter the time zone of your location.
* **location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location of your visit.
* **latitude** (Number, Optional): Enter the latitude of your location.
* **longitude** (Number, Optional): Enter the longitude of your location.
* **timestamp** (Date, Optional): Enter the timestamp of your survey completion.
* **time_zone_offset** (Number, Optional): Enter the time zone offset of your location.
* **time_zone_diff** (Text, Optional): Enter the time zone difference of your location.
* **survey_duration** (Number, Optional): Enter the duration of your visit to the lounge.
* **survey_start_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the start date of your visit to the lounge.
* **survey_start_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the start time of your visit to the lounge.
