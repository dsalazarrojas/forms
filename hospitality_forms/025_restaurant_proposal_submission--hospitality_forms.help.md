# Restaurant Proposal Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for restaurant operators or entrepreneurs to submit proposals for new restaurant concepts. It gathers basic information about the proposed restaurant, including its concept, business plan, and essential contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Submit your restaurant concept**: In the "Concept" field, briefly describe your restaurant's idea, including its unique selling points and target audience.
2. **Outline your business plan**: In the "Business Plan" field, provide a concise overview of your restaurant's operational and financial strategy.
3. **Share your contact details**: In the "Contact Details" field, provide your name, and any other relevant contact information (such as a phone number or email address).
4. **Name your chef**: In the "Chef Name" field, mention the name of your proposed head chef or the person who will be running the kitchen.
5. **Specify your restaurant type**: In the "Concept Type" field, select the type of cuisine your restaurant will offer (e.g., Italian, French, Chinese, American).
6. **Describe your seating capacity**: In the "Number of Seats" field, indicate the number of seats your restaurant will have.
7. **Choose your location**: In the "Location" field, specify the proposed location for your restaurant.
8. **Provide contact information**: In the "Email" field, provide your contact email address, and in the "Phone" field, enter your contact phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Concept**: A brief description of your restaurant's idea, including its unique selling points and target audience.
* **Business Plan**: A concise overview of your restaurant's operational and financial strategy.
* **Contact Details**: A field for providing your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Chef Name**: The name of your proposed head chef or the person who will be running the kitchen.
* **Concept Type**: The type of cuisine your restaurant will offer (e.g., Italian, French, Chinese, American).
* **Number of Seats**: The number of seats your restaurant will have.
* **Location**: A field to specify the proposed location for your restaurant.
* **Email**: Your contact email address.
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by the *required* tag next to each field).
* If a field has options, select the relevant one from the list.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification on a field, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date to help us review and evaluate your proposal effectively.
