# smart_hospitality_cluster_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Hospitality Cluster Registration Form is designed for hospitality businesses to join the Smart Hospitality Cluster initiative. This form helps the cluster administrators collect necessary information from businesses that are interested in joining the cluster.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by providing your form name. This will be the name of your business's registration form in the Smart Hospitality Cluster.
2.  Enter your email address. This is the contact email address of your business.
3.  Add a website link to your business's website, if you have one.
4.  Enter your company's name.
5.  Provide your business's address.
6.  Enter your zip or postal code.
7.  Add your phone number.
8.  Select your form's owners from the provided options.
9.  Choose the industry that best describes your business.
10. Provide a brief description of your business.
11. List the areas of interest for your business.
12. Enter the date you would like to join the Smart Hospitality Cluster.
13. Select the time you would be available to join.
14. Add any additional company details that you think would be helpful for the cluster.
15. Enter the areas of interest for your company.
16. Select the technology providers that your business uses.
17. Please do not enter any information in this field as it is not intended for user input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Name** (`title`, optional): Enter a name for your business's registration form in the Smart Hospitality Cluster.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your contact email address for your business.
* **Form Website** (`form_website`, optional): Enter a website link for your business.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, optional): Enter your business's name.
* **Address** (`address`, optional): Enter your business's address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, optional): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter your business's phone number.
* **Form Owners** (`form_owners`, required): Select the people who are the owners of your business.
*   This is a multiple-select field. You can select multiple options from the provided list.
* **Industry** (`industry`, required): Choose the industry that best describes your business.
*   This is a single-select field. Choose only one option that best fits your business.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, optional): Provide a brief description of your business.
* **Interest Areas** (`interest_areas`, optional): List areas of interest for your business.
* **Join Date** (`join_date`, optional): Enter the date you would like to join the Smart Hospitality Cluster.
* **Join Time** (`join_time`, optional): Select the time you would be available to join the cluster.
*   This field is not currently being used and should be ignored.
* **Company Details** (`company_details`, optional): Enter any additional company details that would be helpful for the cluster.
* **Company Interest Areas** (`company_interest_areas`, optional): List areas of interest for your company.
* **Tech Providers** (`tech_providers`, required): Select the technology providers that your business uses.
*   This is a multiple-select field. You can select multiple options from the provided list.
* **Form Image** (`form_image`, optional): This field is not intended for user input.

Please note that all required fields are marked as such. Ensure that these fields are filled out before submitting the form.
