# Guest Departure Notification - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Departure Notification form is used by hotel guests or their representatives to notify hotel management of their departure details. This includes whether they plan to depart or not, their room number, date of departure, and time of departure. The form helps hotel management keep track of guest departure information and prepare for check-out processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Guest Departure Notification form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Departure Details" field and choose whether you plan to depart or not.
2. If you plan to depart, enter your room number in the "Room Number" field.
3. Enter your departure date in the "Departure Date" field.
4. Enter your departure time in the "Departure Time" field.
5. If necessary, enter your check-out time in the "Check Out Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Departure Details** (`Departure Details`, select_multiple, required/optional):
	+ Choose whether you plan to depart or not. This will help hotel management know when you plan to check out.
* **Room Number** (`Room Number`, text, required/optional):
	+ Enter your room number so hotel management can prepare for your departure.
* **Departure Date** (`Departure Date`, date, required/optional):
	+ Enter the date you plan to check out of the hotel.
* **Departure Time** (`Departure Time`, time, required/optional):
	+ Enter the time you plan to leave the hotel.
* **Check Out Time** (`Check Out Time`, time, required/optional):
	+ If you're not planning to depart immediately, enter the time you plan to check out of the hotel.
