# Hospitality Service Partner Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Hospitality Service Partner Registration" form is designed for hospitality service partners to register their information with the organization. This form helps establish a partnership with the service provider, collects and verifies contact and location details, gathers information about the services offered, and confirms payment terms and agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the general information section with the service partner's basic details.
2. Enter the contact person's name and contact information.
3. Select the type of vendor service you offer (Hotel, Restaurant, Event Space, Bed And Breakfast, or Other).
4. Upload or attach any relevant documents required by the organization.
5. Describe the services you offer in the services field.
6. Enter the location of your service.
7. Provide your contact email address.
8. Enter your contact phone number.
9. Add your website (if applicable).
10. Specify your payment terms.
11. Confirm your agreement with the organization.
12. Add your signature for verification.
13. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This is the first section of the form where you'll enter your basic information.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for contacting you.
* **Vendor Details**: Select the type of vendor service you offer from the provided options: Hotel, Restaurant, Event Space, Bed And Breakfast, or Other.
* **Documents**: Upload or attach any relevant documents required by the organization.
* **Services**: Describe the services you offer in this field.
* **Location**: Enter the location of your service.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Website**: Add your website (if you have one).
* **Payment Terms**: Specify your payment terms with the organization.
* **Agreement**: Confirm your agreement with the organization.
* **Signature**: Add your signature for verification.
* **Submit**: Review and submit the form to complete your registration.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure you have all necessary documents and information before submitting the form.
- Review the agreement and payment terms carefully before confirming.
