# Guest Information And Preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Information And Preferences form is used by guests to provide their personal details and preferences for hospitality operations.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by filling out your name, including First name and Last name.
- Enter your Email for communication purposes.
- Provide your Phone number for easy contact.
- Enter the Room number you've been assigned.
- Choose your Arrival date.
- Choose your Arrival time.
- Choose your Departure date.
- Choose your Departure time.
- In the Special requests field, you can provide any specific requests you have during your stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
  - **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
  - **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
  - **Phone number** (text, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
  - **Room number** (text, required): Enter the room number you've been assigned.
  - **Arrival date** (date, required): Choose the date of your planned arrival.
  - **Arrival time** (time, required): Choose the time of your planned arrival.
  - **Departure date** (date, required): Choose the date of your planned departure.
  - **Departure time** (time, required): Choose the time of your planned departure.
  - **Special requests** (text, required): Enter any specific requests you have during your stay.
