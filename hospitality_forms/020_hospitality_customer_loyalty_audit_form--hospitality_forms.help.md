# Hospitality Customer Loyalty Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a hospitality customer loyalty program for auditing and analysis purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the **Property Name**, which is the name of the property where the loyalty program is implemented.
2. Enter the **Property Address**, including the street address, city, and state.
3. Select the **Property State** from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the **Property ZIP** code.
5. Select the **Program Start Date** and **Program End Date** for the loyalty program.
6. Enter the **Program Name**, which is the name of the loyalty program.
7. Select the **Property Type** of the program, which indicates whether it's active or inactive.
8. Select the **Program Status**, which indicates whether the program is active or inactive.
9. Enter any additional **Program Status Notes** if necessary.
10. Enter the **Form Submitter's Name**, which is the person submitting the form.
11. Enter the **Submission Date**, which is the date the form was submitted.
12. Enter the **Auditor's Name**, which is the person performing the audit.
13. Enter any **Audit Notes**, which are additional comments for the auditor.
14. Enter the **Reviewed By** name, which is the person reviewing the form.
15. Enter the **Review Date**, which is the date the form was reviewed.
16. Enter the **Property Manager's Name**, which is the manager of the property where the loyalty program is implemented (optional).
17. Enter the **Property Manager's Email** and **Property Manager's Phone** (optional).
18. Enter the **Customer Service Manager's Name**, which is the manager of customer service (optional).
19. Enter the **Customer Service Manager's Email** and **Customer Service Manager's Phone** (optional).
20. Enter any **Other Notes**, which are additional comments or information.
