# Hotel Response Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather detailed information from customers about their stay at a hotel, including their personal details and feedback about their experience. The data collected will be used for internal analysis and improvement of hotel services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's first and last name in the "Customer First Name" and "Customer Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Provide the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Enter the hotel ID where the customer stayed in the "Hotel ID" field.
4. Select the date the customer stayed at the hotel in the "Customer Stay Date" field.
5. Enter the reference number of the booking at the hotel in the "Booking Reference" field.
6. If you have any message for the customer, please enter it in the "Customer Message" field. This is optional.
7. If the hotel staff needs to respond to the customer, enter your message in the "Hotel Message" field. This is optional.
8. Select the reason for the customer's visit from the available options in the "What was the reason for your visit?" field.
9. Check any amenities that you think should be improved in the "Which of the following amenities would you like to see improved?" field.
10. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer First Name** (Customer First Name, text, required): Enter the first name of the customer.
* **Customer Last Name** (Customer Last Name, text, required): Enter the last name of the customer.
* **Customer Email** (Customer Email, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Hotel ID** (Hotel ID, number, required): Enter the ID of the hotel where the customer stayed.
* **Customer Stay Date** (Customer Stay Date, date, required): Select the date the customer stayed at the hotel.
* **Booking Reference** (Booking Reference, text, required): Enter the reference number of the booking at the hotel.
* **Customer Message** (Customer Message, note, optional): Enter any message for the customer.
* **Hotel Message** (Hotel Message, note, optional): Enter any message that the hotel staff needs to respond to the customer.
* **What was the reason for your visit?** (What was the reason for your visit?, select_one, required): Select the reason for the customer's visit from the available options.
* **Which of the following amenities would you like to see improved?** (Which of the following amenities would you like to see improved?, select_multiple, required): Check any amenities that you think should be improved.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, optional): Click to complete and submit the form.
