# Housekeeping Attendance Sheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Housekeeping Attendance Sheet is a form used to track employee attendance during their shifts. It helps with scheduling, payroll, and staff management by collecting essential information about employee presence, absences, and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Employee Name** in the first field.
2. Select your **Department** from the available options.
3. Choose your **Shift** from the options provided (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Night).
4. Enter the **Date** of your shift in the date format (e.g., MM/DD/YY).
5. If applicable, enter your **Start Time** and **End Time** for your shift.
6. Select your **Attendance** status from the options (e.g., Present, Absent, Late).
7. Provide a brief **Reason** for any absences or tardiness, if applicable.
8. Include any additional **Comments** about your shift or work experience.
9. Finally, indicate the **Assigned Tool** used during your shift, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or personnel records.
* **Department** (select_one, optional): Choose your department from the available options to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
* **Shift** (select_one, optional): Select the shift you worked during your scheduled time (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Night).
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of your shift in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Start Time** (time, optional): If applicable, enter your start time for the shift.
* **End Time** (time, optional): If applicable, enter your end time for the shift.
* **Attendance** (select_one, optional): Select your attendance status from the options (e.g., Present, Absent, Late).
* **Reason** (text, optional): Provide a brief reason for any absences or tardiness, if applicable.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Include any additional comments about your shift or work experience.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, optional): Indicate if you were assigned a specific tool during your shift.
