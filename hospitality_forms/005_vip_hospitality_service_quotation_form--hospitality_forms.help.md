# vip_hospitality_service_quotation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The vip_hospitality_service_quotation_form is a form used to collect information about a guest's stay or visit. This includes details such as the guest's contact person, phone number, and email. The form is likely used by hospitality staff to gather information and provide a personalized experience for the guest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the guest's contact person from the provided list.
2. Enter the guest's phone number.
3. Enter the guest's email address.
4. Type any additional message or comment for the guest.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select_one, required): Choose the name of the person who will be responsible for the guest's stay or visit.
* **Phone** (`phone`, number, optional): Enter the guest's phone number, including the country code if necessary.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the guest's email address.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Type any additional comments or special requests for the guest.
