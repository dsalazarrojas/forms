# Hospitality Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospitality Application Form is designed to collect information from job applicants in the hospitality industry. This form helps hiring managers and HR personnel to screen and select suitable candidates for a job position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
2. Provide details about your work experience in the hospitality industry.
3. Describe your education background and relevant skills.
4. Offer your job references (2 professional references).
5. Select your job position and availability for the interview.
6. Specify your salary requirements.
7. Choose the current status of your job application.
8. If you have any additional comments, provide them in the "Additional Comments" section.
9. Attach your resume(s) for further review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* E-mail Address (required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* Phone Number (required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* Emergency Contact (optional): Enter the name and contact information of a person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.

### Work Experience
* Work Experience (optional): Describe your work experience in the hospitality industry. This can include your job title, company, and dates of employment.

### Education Background
* Education Background (optional): Describe your education background, including the name of your school, degree, and dates of attendance.

### Personal Statement
* Personal Statement (optional): Briefly describe your relevant skills and experience that make you a strong candidate for the position.

### Job Reference
* Job Reference (optional): Provide 2 professional references. This can include your manager, coworker, or supervisor who can vouch for your skills and experience.

### Job Position
* Job Position (optional): Select the job position you are applying for, such as manager, assistant manager, or staff.

### Availability
* Availability (optional): Choose your availability for the interview, including the date and time.

### Salary Requirements
* Salary Requirements (optional): Specify your expected salary for the position.

### Job Application Status
* Job Application Status (optional): Choose the current status of your job application, such as under review, rejected, or selected for interview.

### Additional Comments
* Additional Comments (optional): Provide any additional information about yourself that you think would be helpful for the hiring manager to know.

### Resume
* Resume (optional): Attach your resume for further review.

Note: Some fields are optional, but providing accurate and comprehensive information will increase your chances of being considered for the position.
