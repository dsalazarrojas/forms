# Restaurant Internship Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to refer a potential candidate for a restaurant internship position. It helps us to track and evaluate candidates' referrals from our staff members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the necessary information. You can find the fields explained in detail below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Restaurant Name (Text, Optional)**: Please enter the name of the restaurant where the candidate has worked or will be working.
- **Location (Text, Optional)**: Enter the location where the candidate will be working, such as city and state.
- **Start Date (Date, Optional)**: Enter the start date of the candidate's internship or employment.
- **End Date (Date, Optional)**: Enter the end date of the candidate's internship or employment.
- **Employee Name (Text, Optional)**: Please enter the name of the employee who referred the candidate.
- **Job Title (Text, Optional)**: Enter the job title of the employee who referred the candidate.
- **Role (Select One, Optional)**: Choose the role of the person being referred:
	+ Intern: A candidate who is participating in a structured internship program.
	+ Host: A candidate who is hosting events, meetings, or other activities.
	+ Trainee: A candidate who is being trained for a specific role.
- **Hours Worked (Number, Optional)**: Enter the number of hours worked by the candidate.
- **Days Worked (Number, Optional)**: Enter the number of days worked by the candidate.
- **Hourly Rate (Number, Optional)**: Enter the candidate's hourly rate of pay.
- **Referral Reason (Text, Optional)**: Explain the reason for referring this candidate.
