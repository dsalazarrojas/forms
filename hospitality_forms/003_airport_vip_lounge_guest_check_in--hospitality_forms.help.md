# Airport VIP Lounge Guest Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about you and your travel plans to ensure you receive the best possible service during your stay in the lounge.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your Guest Full Name as it appears on your boarding pass.
2. Enter your Membership Number if you have one.
3. Provide your Email Address for receipt and confirmation purposes.
4. Enter your Phone Number in case we need to contact you during your stay.
5. Select the Airline Name of your operating carrier.
6. Enter your Flight Number.
7. Select your scheduled Flight Departure Time.
8. Enter your Boarding Pass Number for verification.
9. Choose the Travel Class of your booked service.
10. Select the Access Method you are using to enter the lounge.
11. (Optional) Enter the number of Additional Guests accompanying you.
12. (Optional) Enter the names of your Additional Guests.
13. Select the Lounge Location.
14. Select the Time of your Check-in.
15. (Optional) Select your Special Dietary Requirements if any.
16. (Optional) Select your Beverage Preferences.
17. (Optional) Select the Purpose of your Visit.
18. Opt-in to receive a brief feedback survey after your stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your boarding pass.
- **Membership Number** (text, optional): If you have a VIP lounge membership, enter your membership ID.
- **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address for receipt and confirmation purposes.
- **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your contact number in case we need to reach you during your stay.
- **Airline Name** (text, required): Select the airline operating your flight.
- **Flight Number** (text, required): Enter your departing flight number.
- **Flight Departure Time** (time, required): Select your scheduled flight departure time.
- **Boarding Pass Number** (text, required): Enter your boarding pass number for verification purposes.
- **Travel Class** (select_one, required): Select the class of service you booked.
- **Access Method** (select_one, required): Select how you are accessing the lounge.
- **Number of Guests** (number, optional): Enter the number of guests accompanying you.
- **Guest Names** (text, optional): Enter the names of your additional guests.
- **Lounge Location** (select_one, required): Select the lounge location you are visiting.
- **Check-in Time** (time, required): Select the time of your check-in.
- **Special Dietary Needs** (select_multiple, optional): Select any special dietary restrictions or requirements you have.
- **Beverage Preferences** (select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred beverages.
- **Purpose of Visit** (select_one, optional): Select the reason for your visit.
- **Receive Follow-up Survey** (select_one, optional): Opt-in to receive a brief feedback survey after your stay.
