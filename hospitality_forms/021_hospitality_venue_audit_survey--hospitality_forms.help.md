# Hospitality Venue Audit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience at hospitality venues such as hotels, restaurants, or bars. The purpose of this form is to help venues understand their strengths and weaknesses and make improvements to provide a better experience for their customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please choose the relevant option for each field and provide any relevant comments or notes. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly to help the venue understand your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_info`, select_multiple, optional): Please select one or more of the following options that apply to your experience at the venue:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Cleanliness Rating** (`cleanliness_rating`, number, optional): On a scale of 1-5, how would you rate the cleanliness of the venue? A score of 1 indicates poor cleanliness, while 5 indicates excellent cleanliness.
* **Safety Rating** (`safety_rating`, number, optional): On a scale of 1-5, how would you rate the safety of the venue? A score of 1 indicates poor safety, while 5 indicates excellent safety.
* **Staff Performance** (`staff_performance`, number, optional): How would you rate the performance of the venue's staff? A score of 1 indicates poor performance, while 5 indicates excellent performance.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): On a scale of 1-5, how would you rate the overall experience at the venue? A score of 1 indicates a poor experience, while 5 indicates an excellent experience.
* **Cleanliness Comments** (`cleanliness_comments`, note, optional): Please provide any comments on the cleanliness of the venue.
* **Safety Comments** (`safety_comments`, note, optional): Please provide any comments on the safety of the venue.
* **Staff Comments** (`staff_comments`, note, optional): Please provide any comments on the performance of the venue's staff.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback to help the venue understand your experience.
* Please only fill out this form for each visit to a venue, to avoid duplication of effort.
* If you have already filled out this form for the same venue in the past, your input will not be recorded again.
