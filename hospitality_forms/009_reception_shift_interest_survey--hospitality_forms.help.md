**Reception Shift Interest Survey - Help Guide**

### Purpose

The Reception Shift Interest Survey is used to collect information from staff members regarding their availability and preferences for receptions shifts. This includes details such as contact information, shift availability, start and end times, and preferred times and days.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information. Make sure to include all required fields.
2. Select the appropriate shift options for each available shift, if applicable.
3. Enter the correct start and end times for each shift.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments for managers or team leaders if necessary.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`staff_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Contact Email** (`staff_contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`staff_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Available Shift 1** (`staff_available_shift_1`, select_one, required): Select the shift you are available for.
* **Available Shift 2** (`staff_available_shift_2`, select_one, required): Select the second shift you are available for.
* **Available Shift 3** (`staff_available_shift_3`, select_one, required): Select the third shift you are available for.
* **Available Shift 4** (`staff_available_shift_4`, select_one, required): Select the fourth shift you are available for.
* **Start Time** (`staff_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time for the shift.
* **End Time** (`staff_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time for the shift.
* **Notes** (`staff_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager's Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager's Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager's Notes** (`manager_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes or comments for your manager.
* **Team Leader's Name** (`team_leader_name`, text, required): Enter your team leader's name.
* **Team Leader's Email** (`team_leader_email`, email, required): Enter your team leader's email address.
* **Team Leader's Phone** (`team_leader_phone`, text, required): Enter your team leader's phone number.
* **Team Leader's Notes** (`team_leader_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes or comments for your team leader.
* **Availability Notes** (`availability_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes or details on your availability.
* **Availability Shift** (`availability_shift`, select_one, required): Select the shift you are available for.
* **Availability Start Time** (`availability_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time for your availability.
* **Availability End Time** (`availability_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time for your availability.
* **Preferred Start Time** (`availability_preferred_start_time`, time, required): Enter your preferred start time.
* **Preferred End Time** (`availability_preferred_end_time`, time, required): Enter your preferred end time.
* **Preferred Day** (`availability_preferred_day`, select_one, required): Select your preferred day.
