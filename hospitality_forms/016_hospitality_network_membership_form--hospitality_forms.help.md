# Hospitality Network Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospitality Network Membership Form is designed to collect information from potential members, including their personal details, membership preferences, and payment information. This form is meant for both personal and business use cases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your basic user information in the "User Information" section.
2. In the "Membership Preferences" section, specify your membership preferences.
3. In the "Payment Information" section, enter your payment details.
4. Finally, add any additional information you'd like to share with us in the "Additional Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_information`, text, required: false): This section is for you to provide your basic user information, including your name and contact details.
* **Membership Preferences** (`membership_preferences`, text, required: false): Here, you can specify your membership preferences, such as the type of membership you're interested in or any specific services you'd like to access.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required: false): Please enter your payment information, including any relevant details about your payment method or preferences.
* **Additional Info** (`note`, text, required: false): This is an optional section for you to provide any additional information you'd like to share with us, such as special requests or comments.

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill in all required fields to the best of your ability.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
