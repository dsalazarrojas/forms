# resort_guest_special_request_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect guest preferences and special requests for their stay at the resort. This helps us understand your needs and ensure that your stay is comfortable and enjoyable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your guest preferences from the available options.
2. Choose your preferred arrival date.
3. Select your preferred language.
4. If you have any special requests, enter them in the text box.
5. Choose your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or Text).
6. Select your preferred contact time.
7. Choose how often you would like to be contacted (Daily, Twice a day, or Three times a day).
8. Select your preferred meal time (Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner).
9. Choose your preferred smoking area (Yes or No).
10. Select your preferred view (Sea View, Pool View, or Mountain View).
11. Choose your preferred activity (Beach, Pool, or Outdoor).
12. Select your preferred room type (King, Queen, or Twin).
13. Choose your preferred room location (Beachfront, Poolside, or Mountain).
14. Select your preferred bed type (King, Queen, or Twin).
15. Choose your preferred bathroom preferences (Yes or No).
16. Select your preferred meal preferences (Yes or No).
17. Choose your preferred transportation method (Private or Public).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Preferences** (Text): Enter any special preferences you have as a guest.
* **Arrival Date** (Text): Enter your expected arrival date.
* **Preferred Language** (Text): Select your preferred language for communication.
* **Special Requests** (Text): Enter any special requests you may have.
* **Contact Method** (Select One): How do you prefer to be contacted? (Phone, Email, or Text).
* **Preferred Contact Time** (Time): When do you prefer to be contacted? (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
* **Preferred Contact Frequency** (Select One): How often do you prefer to be contacted? (Daily, Twice a day, or Three times a day).
* **Preferred Meal Time** (Select One): At what time do you prefer to have meals? (Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner).
* **Preferred Smoking Area** (Select One): Do you prefer a smoking area during your stay? (Yes or No).
* **Preferred View** (Select One): Which view do you prefer during your stay? (Sea View, Pool View, or Mountain View).
* **Preferred Activity** (Select One): What type of activities do you prefer during your stay? (Beach, Pool, or Outdoor).
* **Preferred Room Type** (Select One): What type of room do you prefer during your stay? (King, Queen, or Twin).
* **Preferred Room Location** (Select One): Where do you prefer your room to be located? (Beachfront, Poolside, or Mountain).
* **Preferred Bed Type** (Select One): What type of bed do you prefer during your stay? (King, Queen, or Twin).
* **Preferred Bathroom Preferences** (Select Multiple): Do you prefer a bathroom with a specific feature? (e.g., tub, shower, or private bathroom) (Yes or No).
* **Preferred Meal Preferences** (Select Multiple): Do you have any meal preferences during your stay? (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free) (Yes or No).
* **Preferred Transportation** (Select One): How do you prefer to travel during your stay? (Private or Public).
