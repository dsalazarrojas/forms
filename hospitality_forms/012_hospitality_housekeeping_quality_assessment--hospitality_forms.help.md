# Hospitality Housekeeping Quality Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the quality of housekeeping services provided by a hotel or resort. It will help identify areas for improvement and ensure that the hotel meets the standards of the hospitality industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all the questions.
2. Answer the questions based on your personal experience and observations.
3. Be honest and objective in your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Assessment (1)**: This section is used to gather a general overview of your experience with the housekeeping services. Please answer this question based on your overall impression.
	+ **Housekeeping Services (2)**: This question asks if you received good service from the housekeeping staff. Please select "Yes" if the service was satisfactory, and "No" if not.
	+ **Room Cleanliness (3)**: This question asks about the cleanliness of your room. Please select all the areas that apply:
		- Cleanliness of the room
		- Cleanliness of the bathroom
		- Cleanliness of the common areas
		- Other (please specify)
	+ **Bathroom (4)**: This question asks about the quality of the bathroom. Please select all the areas that apply:
		- Cleanliness of the bathroom
		- Quality of the fixtures and amenities
		- Cleanliness of the surrounding area
		- Other (please specify)
	+ **Common Areas (5)**: This question asks about the cleanliness and maintenance of common areas such as the lobby, corridors, and public areas. Please select all the areas that apply:
		- Cleanliness of the common areas
		- Maintenance of the common areas
		- Other (please specify)
	+ **Linens (6)**: This question asks about the quality of the linens provided. Please provide your answer in the text field.
	+ **Laundry Services (7)**: This question asks about the laundry services provided. Please provide your answer in the text field.
	+ **Housekeeping Equipment (8)**: This question asks about the condition and quality of the housekeeping equipment. Please provide your answer in the text field.
	+ **Employee (9)**: This question asks about the friendliness and helpfulness of the employee. Please select "Yes" if the employee was friendly and helpful, and "No" if not.
	+ **Overall Experience (10)**: This question asks about your overall experience with the housekeeping services. Please provide your answer in the text field.
	+ **Follow Up (11)**: This question asks if you received a follow-up from the hotel staff after checking out. Please provide your answer in the text field.
	+ **Additional Comments (12)**: This question is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the housekeeping services.
	+ **Completed By (13)**: This question asks about the employee who completed the form.
	+ **Date Completed (14)**: This question asks about the date the form was completed.
	+ **Time Completed (15)**: This question asks about the time the form was completed.
	+ **Employee Name (16)**: This question asks about the name of the employee who completed the form.
	+ **Employee Position (17)**: This question asks about the position of the employee who completed the form.
	+ **Employee ID (18)**: This question asks about the ID of the employee who completed the form.

Note: The required fields are marked with an asterisk (*).
