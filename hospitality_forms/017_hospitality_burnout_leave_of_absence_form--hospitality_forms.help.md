# Hospitality Burnout Leave Of Absance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report a burnout-related leave of absence for employees in the hospitality industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details:
	* Enter your **Employee Name** in the "Employee Name" field.
	* Enter your **Email** in the "Employee Email" field.
	* Enter your **Phone** in the "Employee Phone" field.
2. Provide your leave of absence information:
	* Select your **start_date** by choosing a date.
	* Select your **end_date** by choosing a date.
	* Enter the **reason_for_leave** for your burnout leave.
3. Add any additional details:
	* If necessary, add any **Supporting Details** to help us understand your situation.
4. Confirm your manager's contact information:
	* Enter your **Manager Name**.
	* Enter your manager's **Email**.
	* Enter your manager's **Phone**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or in our company directory.
* **start_date** (required): Select the date you would like to start your burnout leave.
* **end_date** (required): Select the date you would like to end your burnout leave.
* **reason_for_leave** (required): Provide a brief description of why you are requesting burnout leave (e.g., "Due to burnout, I am unable to perform my job duties effectively").
* **Supporting Details** (optional): Add any additional information that might help us understand your situation and provide support.
* **return_date** (optional): If you know the date you will return to work, enter it here.
* **Employee Email** (required): Enter your work email address.
* **Employee Phone** (required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Manager Name** (required): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Email** (required): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (required): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Department** (required): Enter your department or team name.
* **Manager Title** (optional): If your manager has a title, enter it here (e.g., "Chef de Cuisine" or "Assistant Manager").
* **Leave Reason** (required): Provide a brief description of the reason for your burnout leave.
* **Manager Comment** (optional): If you have any additional comments or information for your manager, enter it here.
