# Sponsorship Alignment Reception RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Sponsorship Alignment Reception RSVP" form is designed to collect information from guests and contacts regarding their sponsorship alignment, reception preferences, and RSVP responses. This form is a crucial tool for managing reception details and ensuring that guests have a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Reception Date** field with the date of the reception or event.
2. Provide the **Guest Name** of the person attending the reception.
3. If applicable, specify the **Guest Title** of the individual.
4. Enter the **Guest Count** if you're attending with a group.
5. Select your **Dietary Needs** from the provided options (Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Omnivore).
6. Enter your **Contact Name**, which should be your name or the name of the person attending the reception.
7. Fill in your **Contact Phone** and **Contact Email** for easy communication.
8. Choose your **RSVP Type** (True or False) to indicate whether you plan to attend.
9. If you have a **Contact Title** (e.g., CEO, Manager) or other contact details (Company, Position, Address, City, State, ZIP, Country), fill them in for reference.
10. Use the **Additional Message** or **Additional Note** field if you want to share any other relevant information or ask questions about the reception.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reception Date**: The date of the reception or event.
* **Guest Name**: The name of the person attending the reception.
* **Guest Title**: A title or position of the guest, if applicable.
* **Guest Count**: The number of people attending with you.
* **Dietary Needs**: Select your dietary preferences from the options:
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Non-Vegetarian
	+ Omnivore
* **Contact Name**: Your name or the name of the person attending the reception.
* **Contact Phone**: Your or the contact's phone number.
* **Contact Email**: Your or the contact's email address.
* **RSVP Type**: Choose True or False to indicate whether you plan to attend.
* **Contact Title**: A title or position of the contact person.
* **Company**: The name of the contact's company.
* **Position**: The contact's position or role.
* **Address**: The contact's address.
* **City**: The city of the contact's address.
* **State**: The state of the contact's address.
* **ZIP**: The contact's ZIP code.
* **Country**: The country of the contact's address.
* **Additional Message**: Any additional information you'd like to share or questions you have.
* **Additional Note**: Any other notes or comments about the reception.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all required fields accurately to avoid errors.
* Double-check your RSVP response to confirm your attendance.
* Use the provided options for Dietary Needs to ensure your preferences are considered.
* Be clear and concise in your Additional Message or Additional Note fields to help with communication and planning.
