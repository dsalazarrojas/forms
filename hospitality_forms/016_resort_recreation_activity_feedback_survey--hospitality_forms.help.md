# Resort Recreation Activity Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather feedback about the resort's recreation activities. The data collected will help management to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions about future programming.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the option that best describes your start of participation in the resort's recreational activities.
2. Provide feedback on the programming improvement of the recreational activities you participated in.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the recreational activities you participated in.
4. Enter your email address to receive any follow-up correspondence.
5. Enter your phone number for contact.
6. Provide any additional comments about your experience.
7. Rate the frequency of the recreational activities you participated in.
8. Select the activities that interest you the most.
9. Provide feedback on the frequency of improvement for the recreational activities.
10. Rate the value of improvement for the recreational activities.
11. Select the areas of improvement for the recreational activities.
12. Rate the value of improvement for the improvement areas.
13. Provide comments on the improvement frequency for the recreational activities.
14. Select the interest for improvement areas.
15. Rate the value of interest for the improvement areas.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start** (`1`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your start of participation in the resort's recreational activities.
* **Recreation Activity Programming Improvement** (`2`, `text`, optional): Provide feedback on the programming improvement of the recreational activities you participated in.
* **Recreation Activity Satisfaction** (`3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the recreational activities you participated in. Select all the options that apply.
* **On-Site Experience** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about your experience.
* **Email Address** (`6`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive any follow-up correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Feedback** (`5`, `note`, required): Enter your feedback about the resort's recreation activities.
* **Activity Rating** (`9`, `number`, required): Rate the recreational activities you participated in using the provided number rating system.
* **Activity Rating Value** (`10`, `number`, required): Enter the value you rate for the activity rating.
* **Recreation Activity Frequency** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Select the frequency you participate in the resort's recreational activities.
* **Recreation Activity Interest** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the activities that interest you the most.
* **Recreation Activity Satisfaction Frequency** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about the satisfaction frequency.
* **Recreation Activity Satisfaction Interest** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about the satisfaction interest.
* **Recreation Activity Frequency Value** (`15`, `number`, required): Enter the value you rate for the frequency of recreational activities.
* **Recreation Activity Interest Value** (`16`, `number`, required): Enter the value you rate for the interest in recreational activities.
* **Recreation Activity Improvement** (`17`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the areas of improvement for the recreational activities.
* **Recreation Activity Improvement Value** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about the improvement value.
* **Recreation Activity Rating Improvement** (`19`, `select_one`, optional): Select the rating for the improvement of the recreational activities.
* **Recreation Activity Rating Improvement Value** (`20`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about the rating improvement value.
* **Recreation Activity Improvement Frequency** (`21`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about the improvement frequency.
* **Recreation Activity Improvement Interest** (`22`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the interest for improvement areas.
* **Recreation Activity Improvement Frequency Value** (`23`, `number`, required): Enter the value you rate for the improvement frequency.
* **Recreation Activity Improvement Interest Value** (`24`, `number`, required): Enter the value you rate for the improvement interest.
* **Recreation Activity Improvement Comment** (`25`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about the improvement areas.
