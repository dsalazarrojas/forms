> **Senior Hybrid Hospitality Operations Manager Application Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form is designed for interested candidates to apply for the Senior Hybrid Hospitality Operations Manager position. It will guide you through the required and optional fields to ensure a complete and accurate submission.
## How To Complete This Form
To submit a successful application, follow these steps:
1. Fill in all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Enter your job title.
3. Enter your first name and last name.
4. Select your date of birth (format: mm/dd/yy).
5. Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
6. If applicable, provide a 10-digit phone number for contact.
7. Enter your address (not required but recommended for internal communication).
8. Attach your resume.
9. Describe your relevant work experience in the provided space.
10. If applicable, list your professional references.
11. If applicable, provide your manager's name, phone number, and email address.
12. Enter your manager's name, job position, and company (if different from your current company).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title**: Enter your current or desired job title.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Select your date of birth in the mm/dd/yy format.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: If you have a 10-digit phone number, enter it for contact purposes.
* **Address**: Enter your current or preferred address (not required but recommended for internal communication).
* **Resume**: Attach your up-to-date resume.
* **Experience**: Describe your relevant work experience in the provided space.
* **References**: If you have professional references, list them in the provided space.
* **Manager Name**: If you have a manager, enter their name.
* **Manager Phone**: If you have a manager, enter their 10-digit phone number.
* **Manager Email**: If you have a manager, enter their valid email address.
* **Manager**: Describe your manager's position and company (if different from your current company).
* **Position**: Enter your manager's position (if different from your current job title).
* **Company**: Enter your manager's company (if different from your current company).
