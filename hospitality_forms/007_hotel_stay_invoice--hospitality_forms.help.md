# Hotel Stay Invoice - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Hotel Stay Invoice" form is used to create a comprehensive invoice for a guest's hotel stay, ensuring that all necessary details are captured for accurate billing and financial record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please ensure that you have all the required information before completing the form.
- Complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
- Use the provided instructions for each field for clarification if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Hotel Name
* **Hotel Name**: (`Hotel Name`, text, required) Enter the name of the hotel where the guest stayed.
### Guest Name
* **Guest Name**: (`Guest Name`, text, required) Enter the name of the guest for whom the invoice is being created.
### Room Service
* **Room Service**: (`Room Service`, select_one, required) Select whether room service was provided during the stay (True or False).
### Services Rendered
* **Services Rendered**: (`Services Rendered`, text, required) Describe the services provided to the guest, such as food, amenities, etc.
### Date Stayed
* **Date Stayed**: (`Date Stayed`, date, required) Enter the date of the guest's stay in the format MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY.
### Checkin Check-out
* **Checkin/Check-out**: (`Checkin Check-out`, time, required) Enter the check-in and check-out times in the format HH:MM AM/PM or HH:MM (24-hour format).
### Total Cost
* **Total Cost**: (`Total Cost`, number, required) Enter the total cost incurred during the stay.
### Payment Method
* **Payment Method**: (`Payment Method`, select_one, required) Select the payment method used by the guest (Credit Card, Pay Pal, Bank Transfer).
### Notes (Optional)
* **Notes**: (`Notes`, note, optional) Enter any additional comments or notes about the stay, if applicable.
