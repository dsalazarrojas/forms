# Guest Communication Portal Access Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for guests or visitors to request access to a guest communication portal, which is likely used by hospitality or accommodation services to manage guest interactions and requests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Guest Request** (Text Field): Enter a brief description of the purpose or reason for your visit.
2. **Guest Name** (Text Field): Enter your full name.
3. **Purpose** (Text Field): Describe the purpose of your visit.
4. **Contact Method** (Text Field): Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, etc.).
5. **Contact Method Details** (Text Field): Provide additional details about your preferred contact method.
6. **Access Request Start** (Date Field): Enter the start date of your requested access period.
7. **Access Request End** (Date Field): Enter the end date of your requested access period.
8. **Access Duration** (Number Field): Specify the number of days or hours you would like to access the portal.
9. **Access Granted By** (Select One Field): Choose the manager who will grant you access.
10. **Access Granted On** (Date Field): The system will automatically generate this field with the date and time access was granted.
11. **Reason Denied** (Text Field): If your request is denied, provide a reason for the denial.
12. **Notes** (Text Field): Add any additional notes or comments.
13. **Email** (Email Field): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
14. **Phone** (Text Field): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
15. **Contact** (Text Field): Enter your preferred contact method (e.g., phone, email, etc.).
16. **Address** (Text Field): Enter your physical address.
17. **City** (Text Field): Enter the city of your physical address.
18. **Zip** (Text Field): Enter your zip code.
19. **Country** (Text Field): Enter your country of residence.
20. **State** (Text Field): Enter your state or province of residence.
21. **Access Granted By Manager** (Select One Field): Select the manager who granted you access.
22. **Access Granted By Manager Name** (Text Field): Enter the name of the manager who granted you access.
23. **Access Granted By Manager Title** (Text Field): Enter the title of the manager who granted you access.
24. **Access Granted By Manager Department** (Text Field): Enter the department of the manager who granted you access.
25. **Access Granted By Manager Email** (Email Field): Enter the email address of the manager who granted you access.
