# Hotel Concierge Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Concierge Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from hotel guests on their concierge experience. It helps us to understand your experience and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Hotel Concierge Feedback Form" link or button.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are indicated by an asterisk (\*).
3. You can submit your feedback as much or as little information as you want, but please keep in mind that the more detailed feedback you provide, the more useful it will be for us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Hotel Concierge Feedback
* **Hotel Concierge Feedback (\*)**: This field is for you to describe your overall experience with the hotel's concierge services. Please provide a short description of your experience, including any positive or negative aspects of your interaction with our staff.

### Name
* **Name**: This field is for you to provide your name, which will be kept confidential and used only for internal purposes.

### Feedback
* **Feedback**: This field is for you to provide more detailed feedback on your experience with the hotel's concierge services. Please be as specific as possible and provide any suggestions or comments you have.

### Service Quality
* **Service Quality**: Here, you can select one of the options to indicate the quality of service you received from our concierge staff. Please choose the option that best describes your experience.

### Staff Professionalism
* **Staff Professionalism**: In this field, you can select all that apply to indicate the level of professionalism displayed by our concierge staff during your visit. Please choose all that apply.

### Communication Skills
* **Communication Skills**: This field is for you to provide feedback on the communication skills of our concierge staff, such as language skills or clarity of information provided.

### Time of Visit
* **Time of Visit**: This field is not currently used, but it's here for internal purposes.

### Date of Visit
* **Date of Visit**: This field is not currently used, but it's here for internal purposes.

### Email
* **Email**: If you would like to be contacted for any follow-up questions or to be kept informed of any updates, please provide your email address here.

### Phone
* **Phone**: This field is optional, but if you would like to be contacted directly, you can provide your phone number here.

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is not currently used, but it's here for internal purposes.

### Comments
* **Comments**: This field is for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

Tips:

* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your feedback.
* You can submit your feedback anonymously, and we will keep your information confidential.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please do not hesitate to share them with us.

Note: This form is for internal use only. Please do not use it for any other purpose other than providing feedback on your concierge experience.
