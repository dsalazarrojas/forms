# hotel_opening_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hotel opening release form is used to provide information about a hotel's opening status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Hotel Details" section with the hotel's name, address, and other relevant information.
2. If applicable, fill out the "Owner" section with the owner's name and contact information.
3. If applicable, fill out the "Manager" section with the manager's name and contact information.
4. In the "Manager Phone" field, enter the manager's phone number.
5. In the "Manager Email" field, enter the manager's email address.
6. In the "Manager Authorization" field, provide a confirmation of the manager's authorization to receive emails or notifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hotel Details** (`hotel_details`, `text`, required: false): Fill in the hotel's name, address, and other necessary details to ensure the form is complete and accurate.
* **Owner** (`owner`, `text`, required: false): If the hotel has an owner, fill in their name and contact information for reference purposes.
* **Manager** (`manager`, `text`, required: false): If the hotel has a manager, fill in their name and contact information for communication and notification purposes.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the manager's phone number for easy contact.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the manager's email address for notifications and communication.
* **Manager Authorization** (`manager_authorization`, `text`, required: false): Confirm the manager's authorization to receive emails or notifications from the system.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and correctly to avoid duplication and ensure the form is valid.
* If the form is not required, it's still essential to fill it out for completeness and reference purposes.
* Verify the manager's authorization before submitting the form.
* Review and submit the form carefully to avoid mistakes or errors.
