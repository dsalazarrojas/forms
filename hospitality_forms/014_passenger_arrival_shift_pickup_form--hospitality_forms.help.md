This form is used to collect information about passenger arrival details and preferences for a hotel or similar establishment. The form is designed to be completed by passengers or their representatives to provide essential information about their arrival time, contact details, and preferred time for pickup or shift information. The purpose of this form is to ensure smooth and efficient arrival processing for both the passenger and the hotel staff. This form will help the hotel staff to identify the arrival time, confirm the passenger's contact information, and determine the preferred time for pickup or shift assignment.

# Passenger Arrival Shift Pickup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information from passengers arriving at the hotel or similar establishment, ensuring a smooth and efficient arrival process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your arrival details in the "Arrival Details" field.
2.  Provide your arrival time in the "Arrival Time" field.
3.  Indicate if you require a passenger arrival shift in the "Arrival Shift" field.
4.  Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
5.  Specify if you require a pickup shift in the "Pickup Shift" field.
6.  Enter your preferred time for pickup or shift in the "Preferred Time" field.
7.  Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Arrival Details** (`arrival_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your arrival details, such as the flight number, airline, or any other relevant information.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, required/optional): Enter your expected arrival time, using the format HH:MM.
* **Arrival Shift** (`arrival_shift`, select multiple, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you require a passenger arrival shift, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Pickup Shift** (`pickup_shift`, select multiple, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you require a pickup shift, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required/optional): Enter your preferred time for pickup or shift, using the format HH:MM.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your arrival.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your arrival time is accurate to avoid any delays or miscommunications.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please mention them in the "Notes" field.
* This form will help our staff to process your arrival efficiently and ensure your needs are met.
