# Hospitality Blockchain Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering with the Hospitality Blockchain Network. It collects contact and organization information, use case, email and phone confirmation, and agreement to terms of service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information on page 1.
2. Provide your organization information on page 2.
3. Describe your use case on page 3.
4. Confirm your email address and phone number on page 4.
5. Agree to the terms of service on page 5.
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information (Page 1)
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.

### Organization Information (Page 2)
* **Organization Information**: Enter your organization's name, address, and any other relevant details.

### Use Case (Page 3)
* **Use Case**: Describe how you plan to use the Hospitality Blockchain Network. This will help us understand your needs and provide better support.

### Confirm Email (Page 4)
* **Confirm Email**: Enter your email address to confirm it with us. This will ensure we can contact you with important updates and information.

### Confirm Phone (Page 4)
* **Confirm Phone**: Enter your phone number to confirm it with us. This will help us reach out to you if needed.

### Terms of Service 1-3 (Page 5)
* **Terms of Service 1, 2, 3**: These are agreements that you must confirm before registering with the network. Please read and confirm your agreement to each term.

### Submit (Page 6)
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your registration and complete the process.
