# Hotel Referral Program Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a referral for a hotel stay. Please provide the following information to help us better understand your referral.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with as much detail as possible. Required fields are marked.
2. Review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referral Information** (1): A brief description of the referred person.
* **Referral Contact Information** (2): The contact information for the referred person.
* **Referral Relationship** (3): How do you know the referred person? (Select one or more from Friend, Colleague, Family Member)
* **Consent** (4): Has the referred person given you their consent to refer them? (Select one or more from Yes, No)
* **Referral Details** (5): Any additional information about the referred person.
* **Referral Date** (6): The date the referral was made.
* **Time Referral Made** (7): The time the referral was made.
* **Referral Amount** (8): The amount you'd like to receive for the referral. This field is for internal purposes only.
* **Form Submission** (9): A field for any additional comments or notes.
* **Referral Contact** (10): Has the referred person given you permission to contact them? (Select one or more from Yes, No)
* **Referral Email** (11): The email address of the referred person.
* **Referral Phone** (12): The phone number of the referred person.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
