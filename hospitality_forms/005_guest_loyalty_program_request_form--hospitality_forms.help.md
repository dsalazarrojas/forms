# Guest LoyalitY Program Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest LoyalitY Program Request Form is designed to gather essential information from guests about their preferences and requests for interacting with the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and preferred contact method.
2. If applicable, describe your communication preferences.
3. Provide your email address or phone number.
4. List any follow-up questions or requests you have.
5. Explain any specific requests or concerns you may have.
6. Select the team you would like your requests to be assigned to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Loyalty Request**: A brief description of your request for the Guest LoyalitY Program.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ In-app
	+ SMS
* **Communication Preferences**: If you have any specific communication preferences or restrictions, describe them here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Follow-up Questions**: List any follow-up questions or requests you have for the Guest LoyalitY Program:
	+ What are your favorite destinations?
	+ What is your preferred method of communication?
	+ Can we help you with something else?
* **Loyalty Program Requests**: Describe any specific requests or concerns you have about the Guest LoyalitY Program.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Assigned Team**: Select the team that you would like your requests to be assigned to:
	+ Hospitality
	+ Sales
	+ Marketing
