# Hostel Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from guests about their accommodation preferences, including their contact details, booking preferences, and accommodation details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Guest Info**. Enter your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Select your **Booking Preferences**. Choose an option that suits your needs.
3. Select your **Room Selection**. Choose an option that suits your needs.
4. Select your **Accommodation Type**. Choose an option that suits your needs.
5. Enter your **Accommodation Date**. Enter the date you would like to stay.
6. Enter your **Checkout Date**. Enter the date you would like to check out.
7. Enter your **Accommodation Price**. Enter the price you are willing to pay.
8. Enter your **Accommodation Address**. Enter your address.
9. Enter your **Accommodation Email**. Enter your email address.
10. Enter your **Accommodation Phone**. Enter your phone number.
11. Add any **Guest Notes**. Enter any additional notes or requests you have.
12. Enter your **Accommodation Name**. Enter the name of your accommodation.
13. Enter your **Accommodation Phone Number**. Enter your accommodation's phone number.
14. Enter your **Accommodation Website**. Enter your accommodation's website.
15. Enter your **Accommodation License Number**. Enter your accommodation's license number.
16. Enter your **Accommodation Vat Number**. Enter your accommodation's VAT number.
17. Enter your **Accommodation Country**. Enter your accommodation's country.
18. Enter your **Accommodation City**. Enter your accommodation's city.
19. Enter your **Accommodation Zip**. Enter your accommodation's zip or postal code.
20. Enter your **Accommodation State**. Enter your accommodation's state.
21. Enter your **Accommodation Postcode**. Enter your accommodation's postcode.
22. Enter your **Accommodation Country Code**. Enter your accommodation's country code.
23. Enter your **Accommodation Latitude**. Enter your accommodation's latitude.
24. Enter your **Accommodation Longitude**. Enter your accommodation's longitude.
25. Enter your **Accommodation Image**. Enter an image of your accommodation.
