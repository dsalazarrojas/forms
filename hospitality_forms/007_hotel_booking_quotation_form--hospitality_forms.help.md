Hotel Booking Quotation Form - Help Guide
======================================

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather essential information from guests for a hotel booking quotation. Fill out this form to provide the hotel with all relevant details about your stay, and they will send you a quotation for the requested period.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the desired dates for your stay.
2. Enter your guest details, including name, email, and phone number.
3. Specify any special requests or preferences you may have.
4. Choose the type of room(s) you require (Single, Double, Suite, or Other).
5. Indicate the number of rooms you need.
6. Select the rate type (Per Room Per Night, Per Person Per Night, or Other).
7. Specify the total number of nights you will be staying.
8. Choose any special services or amenities you would like to have (e.g., Breakfast, Dinner, Spa).
9. Enter any additional requests or preferences you may have.
10. Finally, specify your quote request (Breakfast, Dinner, Room Service, or Other).
11. Select any special services or amenities you would like to have during your stay.
12. Specify any additional requests or preferences you may have.
13. Enter a message for the quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **hotel_dates**: Select the dates for your stay to request a quote.
* **guest_details**: Enter your name, email, and phone number for communication with the hotel.
* **guest_name**: Enter your name.
* **guest_email**: Enter your email address.
* **guest_phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **special_requests**: Specify any special requests or preferences you may have.
* **room_type**: Choose the type of room you require (Single, Double, Suite, or Other).
* **room_quantity**: Indicate the number of rooms you need.
* **room_rate**: Enter the rate for the room.
* **arrival_date**: Select the arrival date for your stay.
* **departure_date**: Select the departure date for your stay.
* **room_rate_type**: Choose the rate type (Per Room Per Night, Per Person Per Night, or Other).
* **total_nights**: Specify the total number of nights you will be staying.
* **quote_request**: Choose any services or amenities you would like to have (e.g., Breakfast, Dinner, Room Service).
* **special_services**: Select any special services or amenities you would like to have (e.g., Spa, Gym, Wi-Fi).
* **additional_requests**: Specify any additional requests or preferences you may have.
* **quote_message**: Enter a message for the quote.
* **guest_count**: Indicate the number of guests in your party.
