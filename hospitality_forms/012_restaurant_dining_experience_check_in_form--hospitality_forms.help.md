# Restaurant Dining Experience Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the restaurant staff gather essential information about your dining experience, ensuring that you have a smooth and enjoyable meal. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form to help us tailor your experience to your preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that we can provide the best service.
- If you have any special requests or dietary restrictions, please select the options that apply to you.
- If you have any other special requests, please provide us with more information in the "Special Requests" field.
- Select your preferred dining experience from the "Dining Preferences" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Restaurant Name**: Please enter the name of the restaurant you are dining at, so we can look up your reservation.
- **Guest Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your reservation or identification.
- **Phone Number**: If you have any questions or need to make changes to your reservation, we may contact you at this number.
- **Email**: If you have any email address you'd like to be contacted at in case of an emergency, please enter it here.
- **Arrival Time**: Please let us know when you expect to arrive at the restaurant.
- **Departure Time**: Please let us know when you expect to leave the restaurant.
- **Reservation ID**: If you have a reservation ID, please enter it here to help us locate your reservation.
- **Table Number**: If you have a table number, please enter it here.
- **Special Requests**: Please select any of the following that apply to you:
  - Dietary Restrictions
  - Special Requests
  - Other (please specify in the space provided)
- **Dining Preferences**: Please select your preferred dining experience:
  - Casual
  - Formal
  - Informal
  - Other (please specify in the space provided)
- **Experience Rating**: Please rate your experience after dining with us:
  - Poor
  - Good
  - Great
  - Excellent

## Tips
- Please be as accurate and complete as possible when filling out this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask our staff.
- We will do our best to accommodate your requests and provide you with a memorable dining experience.
