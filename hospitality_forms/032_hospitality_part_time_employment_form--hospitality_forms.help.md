# Hospitality Part Time Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from applicants for part-time job positions in the hospitality industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
2. Select your employment status from the "Employment Status" field.
3. Choose your job title, job description, and job type from the respective fields.
4. Enter your work hours, shift frequency, and work location in the respective fields.
5. Provide your start and end dates if applicable.
6. Enter your salary expectations.
7. Specify your available days.
8. Select your job availability.
9. Enter your notice period.
10. Provide information about your work experience, education, and skills.
11. List any languages you are proficient in.
12. Attach a copy of your resume.
13. Provide contact information for your emergency contact.
14. Specify your emergency relationship with the contact.
15. Enter the contact's phone number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact details.
* **Employment Status** (`Employment Status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your employment status from the dropdown list.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Description** (`Job Description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Type** (`Job Type`, text, required/optional): Enter the type of job you are applying for.
* **Work Hours** (`Work Hours`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of hours you are willing to work.
* **Shift Frequency** (`Shift Frequency`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose how often you are willing to work shifts.
* **Work Location** (`Work Location`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the location where you are available to work.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to start working.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to end working.
* **Salary** (`Salary`, number, required/optional): Enter your expected salary.
* **Available Days** (`Available Days`, text, required/optional): Enter the days you are available to work.
* **Job Availability** (`Job Availability`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose when you are available to work.
* **Notice Period** (`Notice Period`, text, required/optional): Enter the notice period you require.
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, text, required/optional): Enter your relevant work experience.
* **Education** (`Education`, text, required/optional): Enter your educational background.
* **Skills** (`Skills`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant skills you possess.
* **Languages** (`Languages`, text, required/optional): List any languages you are proficient in.
* **Resume** (`Resume`, note, required/optional): Attach a copy of your resume.
* **References** (`References`, text, required/optional): Provide contact information for your references.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Relationship** (`Emergency Relationship`, text, required/optional): Specify the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`Emergency Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email** (`Emergency Email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email for your emergency contact.
