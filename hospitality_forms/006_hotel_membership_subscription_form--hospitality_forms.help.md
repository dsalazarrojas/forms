# Hotel Membership Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Hotel Membership Subscription Form is used to collect subscription information from users who wish to join the hotel's membership program. The form is used to gather the necessary details required for the hotel's membership team to process the subscription request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Select the type of membership you are interested in subscribing to (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* Choose a membership term (e.g., Month, Quarter, Year).
* Provide your name and contact information (Email, Contact Number).
* Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Stripe).
* Enter your payment details (CVV/CVV, Payment Amount).
* Confirm the status of your payment (Success or Failed).
* Optionally, provide additional details about your payment method and membership duration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Plan** (`membership_plan`): Choose the type of membership you are interested in subscribing to (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Membership Term** (`plan_term`): Select the term of your membership (e.g., Month, Quarter, Year).
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name for the membership subscription.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address associated with your membership subscription.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact number for the membership subscription.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Stripe).
* **CVV/CVV** (`payment_cvv`): Enter your CVV/CVV for the payment method.
* **Membership Start Date** (`membership_start_date`): Enter the start date of your membership subscription.
* **Membership End Date** (`membership_end_date`): Enter the end date of your membership subscription.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the payment amount for your membership subscription.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Confirm the status of your payment (Success or Failed).
* **Note** (`note`): Enter any additional notes about your membership subscription.
* **Payment Method (Optional)** (`payment_method_optional`): Select an alternative payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Stripe).
* **Name (Optional)** (`name_optional`): Enter an optional name for the membership subscription.
* **CVV/CVV (Optional)** (`payment_cvv_optional`): Enter your CVV/CVV for the payment method.
* **Note (Optional)** (`note_optional`): Enter any additional notes about your membership subscription.
* **Membership Duration** (`membership_duration`): Select the duration of your membership (Month, Quarter, Year).
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`): Select the type of membership (Individual or Corporate).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method_alt`): Select an alternative payment method (Bank Transfer or Stripe).
