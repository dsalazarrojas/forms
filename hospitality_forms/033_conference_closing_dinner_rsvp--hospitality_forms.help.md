# Conference Closing Dinner RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Conference Closing Dinner RSVP form helps us plan and prepare for the closing dinner event by collecting essential information from attendees, such as their name, organization, and meal preferences. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form accurately to ensure we can provide a great dining experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Conference Closing Dinner RSVP**: This is the title of the form, which you are already on.
2. **Attendee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or business card.
3. **Email Address**: Enter your contact email for any follow-up information or updates about the dinner.
4. **Organization**: If you work for a company or institution, please enter your organization name.
5. **Dinner Details**: This field is for any additional event information or context you'd like to share.
6. **Conference Name**: Enter the name of the conference you are attending.
7. **Dinner Date**: Select the date of the closing dinner.
8. **Dinner Time**: Select the start time of the dinner (if available).
9. **Dinner Venue**: Enter the location of the dinner venue.
10. **RSVP Response**: Choose whether you will be attending the dinner.
11. **Guest Information**: If you're bringing guests, type any additional context or information in this field.
12. **Number of Additional Guests**: If you're bringing guests, enter the number of additional attendees.
13. **Guest Names**: List the names of your additional guests.
14. **Meal Selection**: Choose your primary entree for the dinner.
15. **Primary Entree Choice**: Select a meal option for yourself.
16. **Guest Entree Choice**: If you're bringing guests, select a meal option for them.
17. **Dietary Requirements**: If you have any dietary restrictions, list them here.
18. **Beverage Preference**: Choose your drink preference for the dinner.
19. **Seating Preference**: Select your table arrangement preference.
20. **Special Occasions**: If this is a special occasion, such as an anniversary, please indicate it here.
21. **Transportation Needs**: If you need transportation to the venue, please indicate it here.
22. **Shuttle Service Needed**: If you require shuttle service, select this option.
23. **Accessibility Needs**: If you have any special accessibility requirements, list them here.
24. **Additional Comments**: If you have any other information to share about your dinner plans, please enter it here.
25. **RSVP Deadline Acknowledgment**: Choose when you understand and agree to the RSVP deadline.

Note: Please answer all questions accurately and truthfully to help us prepare for the dinner event. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
