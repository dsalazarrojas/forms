# Hospitality Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from attendees for the hospitality conference. This form is used to register attendees and ensure that we have the necessary details to provide the best experience for them during the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling out the registration form on the first page.
2. Provide your basic contact information, including your name and email address.
3. If you have any dietary restrictions, please indicate them on the "Dietary Restrictions" page.
4. On the "Registration Details" page, provide any additional information about your registration.
5. On the "Confirm Attendance" page, confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes" or "No".
6. Finally, review your form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on conference materials.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with conference updates and information.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with conference updates and information.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date you plan to attend the conference.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time you plan to attend the conference.
* **Select One** (`Select One`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the options provided.
* **Select Multiple** (`Select Multiple`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select multiple options from the list provided.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`Dietary Restrictions`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, please indicate them here.
* **Registration Details** (`Registration Details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional information about your registration.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional information that is not covered in the other fields.
* **Confirm Attendance** (`Confirm Attendance`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Payment Information** (`Payment Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any relevant payment information, such as credit card details or check number.
* **Confirm Attendance 2** (`Confirm Attendance 2`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes" or "No".
