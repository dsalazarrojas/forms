# Co Living Entrepreneur Space Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help Co Living understand your needs and preferences for your upcoming stay with us. It will help us provide you with the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each field as accurately as possible. If you need any clarification on a particular field, you can contact our staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your legal full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Please enter your contact phone number.
* **Company or Startup Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): If you are booking on behalf of a company or startup, please enter the name here.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, optional): If you have a company website, please enter the URL here.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, required): Please select the industry your company or startup belongs to from the list.
* **Company Stage** (`company_stage`, `select_one`, required): Please select the stage your company or startup is at from the list.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, `select_one`, required): Please select the number of people in your team from the list.
* **Space Type Needed** (`space_type`, `select_one`, required): Please select the type of space you need from the list.
* **Check-In Date** (`check_in_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date you would like to start your stay.
* **Check-Out Date** (`check_out_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date you would like to end your stay.
* **Booking Duration Type** (`duration_type`, `select_one`, required): Please select the type of booking you are making from the list.
* **Number of People** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, required): Please enter the number of people that will be using the space.
* **Amenities Needed** (`amenities_needed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select any amenities you require from the list.
* **Purpose of Visit** (`purpose_of_visit`, `select_one`, required): Please select the reason for your visit from the list.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, optional): If you have any special requirements or requests, please enter them here.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your estimated budget for this stay from the list.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard_about_us`, `select_one`, optional): Please select how you heard about Co Living from the list.
* **Have You Stayed Before** (`previous_stay`, `select_one`, required): Please select if you have booked with us previously.
* **Interested in Networking Events** (`networking_interest`, `select_one`, optional): If you are interested in attending community events, please select from the list.
* **I Accept Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, `select_one`, required): Please accept our house rules and policies by selecting "Yes I Accept".
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Please select your preferred method of payment from the list.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, please enter them here.

## Tips
Please answer each field accurately and completely. If you have any questions or need clarification on a particular field, please don't hesitate to contact our staff.
