# Senior Living Trial Stay Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Living Trial Stay Booking Form is used for booking a trial stay for senior living facilities. This form helps users to request a trial stay and provides the necessary information required for the facility to process the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. **Senior Living Trial Stay Booking Form**: This is the form title.
2. Fill out the **Guest Needs** field with a description of the needs of the guest staying in the facility.
3. In the **Contact Details** field, enter your name, phone number, and email address.
4. Select your preferred type of trial stay in the **Trial Stay Preferences** field.
5. Select the date and time for the trial stay in the **Trial Stay Request Date** and **Trial Stay Request Time** fields.
6. Add any additional information in the **Notes** field.
7. Select two preferred dates for the trial stay in the **Trial Stay Request Date 2** field.
8. Finally, select a date for follow-up on the trial stay request in the **Follow-up Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Needs**: This field is used to describe the needs of the guest staying in the facility.
* **Contact Details**: This field is used to provide contact information for the user or their representative.
* **Trial Stay Preferences**: This field is a dropdown menu where users can select their preferred type of trial stay.
* **Trial Stay Request Date**: This field is a date picker where users can select the date they would like to book the trial stay.
* **Trial Stay Request Time**: This field is a time field where users can select the time of day they would like to book the trial stay.
* **Notes**: This field is a text field where users can provide any additional information about their trial stay request.
* **Trial Stay Request Date 2**: This field is a date picker where users can select the date of their second preferred trial stay.
* **Follow-up Date**: This field is a date picker where users can select a date for follow-up on their trial stay request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the trial stay is booked successfully.
* Check the fields carefully before submitting the form to avoid any mistakes.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at contact information provided in the **Contact Details** field.
