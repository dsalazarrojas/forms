# Hotel Staff Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from hotel staff members when they are leaving the premises during their shift. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the department you are currently working in from the "Department" field.
2. Choose how you would like to be contacted after your shift from the "Contact Preferred" field.
3. Provide a brief explanation for your departure in the "Reason for Leaving" field.
4. Enter your date of departure in the "Date of Departure" field.
5. Enter your time of departure in the "Time of Departure" field.
6. If you have any comments or concerns, please enter them in the "Comment" field.
7. Sign and date your name in the "Staff Signature" field.
8. Sign your manager's name in the "Manager Signature" field, and include any comments or instructions in the "Manager Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Member**: A text field for the staff member's name.
 
* **Department**: A dropdown select field to choose the department you are working in.
 
* **Reason for Leaving**: A text field for a brief explanation of your reason for leaving.
 
* **Date of Departure**: A date field for the date you are leaving.
 
* **Time of Departure**: A time field for the time you are leaving.
 
* **Contact Preferred**: A dropdown select field to choose how you would like to be contacted after your shift.
 
* **Comment**: A text field for any additional comments or concerns.
 
* **Staff Signature**: A text field for the staff member's signature.
 
* **Manager Signature**: A text field for the manager's signature.
 
* **Manager Comments**: A text field for the manager's comments or instructions.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Check the "Department" field to ensure you are selecting the correct option.
- Double check the "Date of Departure" and "Time of Departure" fields for accuracy.
- Use the "Comment" field for any additional comments or concerns.
- Make sure both you and your manager sign the form.
