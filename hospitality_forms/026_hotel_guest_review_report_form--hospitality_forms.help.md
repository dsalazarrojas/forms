# Hotel Guest Review Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Guest Review Report Form is designed to gather feedback from hotel guests about their stay. This feedback will help hotel management identify areas for improvement and make necessary changes to provide a better experience for future guests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your checkin date to report the date you arrived at the hotel.
2. Enter your room number to provide specific details about your stay.
3. Rate your experience by selecting a number from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
4. Share your comments about your stay, including any praise or criticism.
5. If you have any contact information, such as an email or phone number, we would appreciate it in case we need to reach out to you for additional information.
6. Any suggestions you may have for improvement will also be greatly appreciated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hotel Checkin** (`hotel_checkin`, text, required/optional): Enter the date you arrived at the hotel.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, text, required/optional): Provide the specific room number where you stayed.
* **Date of Stay** (`date_of_stay`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your stay.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your experience from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Share any comments, praise, or criticism about your stay.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required/optional): Provide any suggestions for improvements.
