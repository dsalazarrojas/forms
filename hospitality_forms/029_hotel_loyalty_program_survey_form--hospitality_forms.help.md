# Hotel Loyalty Program Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect information about your experience and preferences related to our hotel's loyalty program, to help us better serve you and our other guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Carefully read each question to ensure you understand what's being asked.
* Select the relevant options for each question, or leave the optional ones blank.
* Submit the form when you're ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Are you a member of our hotel loyalty program** (`loyalty_program_membership`, select_one, optional):
	+ Answer 'Yes' if you're currently a member or have ever been a member.
	+ Answer 'No' if you're not a member or have never been a member.
* **What is your contact information** (`contact_information`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your email address, phone number, or any other contact method we might use to reach you.
	+ You can skip this question if you're not comfortable sharing your contact information.
* **On a scale of 0-10, how would you rate your overall score** (`feedback_score`, number, optional):
	+ Rate your overall experience on a scale of 0 (very poor) to 10 (excellent).
	+ You can skip this question if you're not comfortable sharing feedback.
* **What is your favorite hotel stay** (`favorite_hotel_stay`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose 'Yes' for stays you've enjoyed the most.
	+ You can select multiple stays if you've had multiple favorite stays.
* **What is your favorite hotel room type** (`favorite_hotel_room`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose 'Yes' for room types you've enjoyed the most.
	+ You can select multiple room types if you have multiple favorites.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere with your answers, as they will help us improve our services.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the text field provided.
* Thank you for your time and participation! Your feedback is valuable to us!
