# Cocktail Bar Booking Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cocktail Bar Booking Inquiry form is designed to help us understand your event details and preferences for a seamless experience. Please fill out this form to let us know about your event, and we'll take care of the rest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and provide as much information as possible. You can skip the optional fields if they don't apply to your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Full Name (Required)
* **Full Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name to help us personalize your booking experience.

### 2. Email Address (Required)
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for us to contact you regarding your event booking.

### 3. Phone Number (Required)
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Please provide your best contact number, and we'll call or text you if needed.

### 4. Company or Organization (Optional)
* **Company or Organization** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): If you're booking for a business or organization, enter the name here.

### 5. -- Contact Information -- (Section Title)
* This is a section title and not a field to be filled out.

### 6. Preferred Event Date (Required)
* **Preferred Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date for your event.

### 7. Event Time (Required)
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required): Select the start time for your event.

### 8. Type of Event (Required)
* **Type of Event** (`event_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of event you're hosting (Corporate Event, Wedding Reception, Birthday Party, etc.).

### 9. Estimated Event Duration - hours (Required)
* **Estimated Event Duration - hours** (`event_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the length of your event in hours.

### 10. Expected Number of Guests (Required)
* **Expected Number of Guests** (`expected_guests`, `number`, required): Approximate the number of guests attending.

### 11. -- Service Requirements -- (Section Title)
* This is a section title and not a field to be filled out.

### 12. Type of Service Needed (Required)
* **Type of Service Needed** (`service_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the types of services you require (Full Bar Service, Cocktail Menu Only, etc.).

### 13. Bar Location (Required)
* **Bar Location** (`bar_location`, `select_one`, required): Choose where the bar will be set up (On premises, Off premises, TBD).

### 14. Event Venue Address (Required)
* **Event Venue Address** (`venue_address`, `text`, required): Provide the full address or venue name.

### 15. -- Drink Preferences -- (Section Title)
* This is a section title and not a field to be filled out.

### 16. Preferred Drink Styles (Required)
* **Preferred Drink Styles** (`drink_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your preferred drink styles (Classic Cocktails, Modern Mixology, etc.).

### 17. Signature Cocktail Interest (Required)
* **Signature Cocktail Interest** (`signature_drink`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you'd like a signature cocktail for your event.

### 18. Dietary Restrictions or Allergies (Optional)
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please list them here.

### 19. -- Special Requirements -- (Section Title)
* This is a section title and not a field to be filled out.

### 20. Special Requests or Customizations (Optional)
* **Special Requests or Customizations** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): If you have any special requests or customizations, please describe them here.

### 21. Approximate Budget per Person (Optional)
* **Approximate Budget per Person** (`budget`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your approximate budget per person to help us with menu recommendations.

### 22. Additional Information (Optional)
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): If there's anything else we should know about your event, please let us know here.
