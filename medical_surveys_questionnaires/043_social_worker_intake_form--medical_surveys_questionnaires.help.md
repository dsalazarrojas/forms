# Social Worker Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Social Worker Intake Form is a tool used to collect information about a client's medical history, social support system, medical conditions, and other relevant details to provide effective support and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the client's information, including their name, address, phone number, and email (if applicable).
2. Answer the medical history questions, providing as much detail as possible about the client's medical conditions, treatments, and medications.
3. Identify the client's social support system, including their support networks, such as family, friends, or support groups.
4. Select the medical conditions that apply to the client.
5. Provide the date and time of birth for the client.
6. Enter the address and phone number of the emergency contact person.
7. Enter the medical record number and provider's information, if applicable.
8. Enter the social security number, if applicable.
9. Enter the date and time of the last service received, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **client-info** (Text, Required: False): A brief description of the client, including their background information.
* **medical-history** (Text, Required: False): A detailed account of the client's medical history, including any previous treatments or conditions.
* **social-support** (Select Multiple, Required: True): A list of the client's support networks, including family, friends, or support groups.
* **medication** (Text, Required: False): A list of the client's current medications, including dosage and frequency.
* **medical-condition** (Text, Required: False): A list of the client's current medical conditions.
* **medical-history-2** (Text, Required: False): Additional information about the client's medical history.
* **emergency-contact** (Text, Required: False): The name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **medical-condition-2** (Select One, Required: True): A list of the client's medical conditions.
* **date-of-birth** (Date, Required: False): The client's date of birth.
* **time-of-birth** (Time, Required: False): The client's time of birth.
* **address** (Text, Required: False): The client's address.
* **email** (Email, Required: False): The client's email address.
* **phone** (Text, Required: False): The client's phone number.
* **medical-record-number** (Text, Required: False): A reference number for the client's medical record.
* **medical-record-provider** (Text, Required: False): The name and contact information of the client's medical provider.
* **social-security-number** (Text, Required: False): The client's social security number.
* **date-of-service** (Date, Required: False): The date of the last service received.
* **time-of-service** (Time, Required: False): The time of the last service received.
