# Wound Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a comprehensive assessment tool used to evaluate a patient's wound. It gathers information on the wound's location, type, depth, length, width, and area, as well as the patient's condition, medical history, and treatment details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Wound Location**: Select the location of the wound from the provided options.
2. **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical record.
3. **Wound Type**: Select all applicable types of wounds that apply to the patient.
4. **Wound Depth**: Select the depth of the wound from the provided options.
5. **Wound Length**, **Wound Width**, and **Wound Area**: Enter the measurements of the wound in centimeters or inches, as applicable.
6. **Wound Surface Area**: Enter the surface area of the wound in square centimeters or square inches, as applicable.
7. **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history that may impact the wound's condition.
8. **Wound Description**: Provide a detailed description of the wound, including its appearance and any notable features.
9. **Wound Pain Level**: Select the level of pain the patient experiences due to the wound.
10. **Wound Pain Location**: Select where the patient experiences the wound pain.
11. **First Opening Date** and **First Treatment Date**: Enter the dates when the wound first opened and when it received its first treatment, respectively.
12. **Next Treatment Date**: Enter the date of the next scheduled treatment or follow-up appointment.
13. **Wound Closure Date**: Enter the date when the wound was closed or healed, if applicable.
14. **Closure Method**: Select the method used to close the wound, if applicable.
15. **Patient Status**: Select the current status of the patient, either Active or Inactive.
16. **Patient Contact**: Select whether the patient is contactable or not.
17. **Wound Complications**: Select all applicable complications that the patient experiences due to the wound.
18. **Wound Care**: Select whether the wound requires ongoing care or not.
19. **Wound Rehabilitation**: Select whether the wound requires rehabilitation or not.
20. **Wound Suture Type**: Select the type of suture used to close the wound, if applicable.
21. **Wound Infection Status**: Select the status of the wound infection, either Active or Inactive.
