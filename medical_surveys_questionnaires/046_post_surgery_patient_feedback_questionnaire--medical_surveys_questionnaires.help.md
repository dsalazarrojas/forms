# post_surgery_patient_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients after their surgery to help our team understand their experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form to make sure you understand each question.
2. Answer every question that applies to your experience with our facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Patient Satisfaction** (`patient_satisfaction`, number, required: false): Please rate your overall satisfaction with your experience at our facility, using the numbers 1-5.
* **2. Feedback on Clinician** (`feedback_on_clinician`, text, required: false): Describe any feedback you have about your clinician.
* **3. Follow-up Instructions Given** (`followup_instructions_given`, select_one, required: false): Check if you received follow-up instructions from our staff. If not, please select "not sure".
* **4. Follow-up Instructions Received** (`followup_instructions_received`, select_multiple, required: false): Check the instructions you received from our staff. Select all that apply.
* **5. Post-op Care Instructions** (`post_op_care_instructions`, text, required: false): Describe any post-operative care instructions given to you.
* **6. Post-op Care Instructions Received** (`post_op_care_instructions_received`, text, required: false): Describe any post-operative care instructions you received.
* **7. Pain Management** (`pain_management`, number, required: false): Please rate your pain management using the numbers 1-5.
* **8. Pain Management Given** (`pain_management_given`, text, required: false): Describe any pain management provided to you.
* **9. Pain Management Effectiveness** (`pain_management_effectiveness`, select_one, required: false): Rate the effectiveness of your pain management using the options "very good", "somewhat good", or "not good".
* **10. Follow-up Phone Number** (`followup_phone_number`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted by our staff for follow-up.
* **11. Follow-up Phone Time** (`follow_up_phone_time`, time, required: false): Please specify the best time for us to call you back.
* **12. Follow-up Phone Date** (`follow_up_phone_date`, date, required: false): Provide a date and time you would be available to be contacted by our staff.
