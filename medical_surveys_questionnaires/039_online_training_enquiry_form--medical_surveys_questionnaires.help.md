# online_training_enquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in online training and development programs. The collected information will be used to assign the right training tool for the customer's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the customer information section, which includes your name, email address, and other relevant details.
2. Provide your contact information, including your phone number and address.
3. Share your health and lifestyle details, such as any medical conditions or dietary restrictions.
4. Specify your expectations from the online training program.
5. Share your customer experience with our previous training programs (if any).
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
7. Select your preferred date and time for the training program.
8. Choose the tool that you would like to be assigned for the training program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (customer_info): Enter your name and other relevant details to help us understand your needs.
* **Contact Info** (contact_info): Provide your phone number and address so we can reach out to you.
* **Health and Lifestyle** (health_and_lifestyle): Share any medical conditions or dietary restrictions that may affect your training needs.
* **Expectations** (expectations): Be specific about what you want to achieve from the online training program.
* **Customer Experience** (customer_experience): If you've taken our training programs before, share your experience with us.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): Share any additional thoughts or feedback you may have about our training programs.
* **Preferred Date** (preferred_date): Select a date that works best for you.
* **Preferred Time** (preferred_time): Choose a time that suits you best.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool): Choose the tool that you think will be most beneficial for your training needs.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Be specific about your needs and expectations.
* Choose a preferred date and time that works best for you.
* Select a tool that aligns with your training needs.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
