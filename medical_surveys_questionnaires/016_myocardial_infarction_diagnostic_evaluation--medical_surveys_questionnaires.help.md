# myocardial_infarction_diagnostic_evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The myocardial infarction diagnostic evaluation form is a tool used by healthcare providers to assess a patient's risk of myocardial infarction (heart attack) based on their medical history, symptoms, and test results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including their medical history, symptoms, and medications.
2. Enter the results of any relevant laboratory tests, including blood work and cardiac function tests.
3. Include the findings from any electrocardiogram (ECG) tests.
4. Summarize any imaging studies that may be relevant to the patient's cardiac health.
5. Identify any cardiac risk factors that may be contributing to the patient's risk.
6. Assess the patient's overall outlook and make recommendations for further evaluation or treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required/optional): 
This section is for gathering information about the patient, including name, date of birth, and other identifying details.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): 
Please provide a brief summary of the patient's past medical history, including any previous heart conditions, surgeries, or other relevant medical conditions.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, required/optional): 
Describe any symptoms the patient is currently experiencing, including chest pain, shortness of breath, or other concerns.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required/optional): 
List any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Laboratory Results** (`laboratory_results`, `text`, required/optional): 
Enter the results of any laboratory tests, including blood counts, cardiac function tests, and other relevant lab work.
* **Electrocardiogram Results** (`electrocardiogram_results`, `text`, required/optional): 
Enter the results of any ECG tests, including tracings and interpretations.
* **Imaging Studies** (`imaging_studies`, `text`, required/optional): 
Summarize any imaging studies, including echocardiograms, angiograms, or other cardiac imaging studies.
* **Cardiac Risk Factors** (`cardiac_risk_factors`, `text`, required/optional): 
Identify any cardiac risk factors, including high blood pressure, high cholesterol, or smoking history.
* **Patient Outlook** (`patient_outlook`, `text`, required/optional): 
Assess the patient's overall cardiac risk level and make recommendations for further evaluation or treatment. 

Note: The fields are all optional, but providing as much information as possible will help in assessing the patient's risk level accurately.
