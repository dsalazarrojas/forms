# Injection Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Injection Therapy Intake Form is used to collect information about a patient's medical history, allergies, and injection therapy treatment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Please provide your personal information in the "Patient Information" section.
2.  Describe your medical history and any relevant information about your medical conditions, medications, and allergies.
3.  Specify the details about your injection therapy, including the injection site, frequency, duration, and dose.
4.  Select any applicable medical conditions, allergies, or medications that may affect your treatment.
5.  Confirm your consent for treatment and provide any additional information about your medical history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, Required/Optional): This section is used to collect your personal information, such as your name, date of birth, and contact details. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, Optional): Describe your medical history, including any previous injuries, illnesses, or medical conditions.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, Optional): Confirm your consent for treatment by checking the relevant options.
* **Injection Details** (`injection_details`, text, Optional): Specify the details about your injection therapy, including the injection site, frequency, duration, and dose.
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, select_multiple, Required): Select any medications you are allergic to.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, Required): Select any medical conditions you have.
* **Injection Procedure** (`injection_procedure`, text, Optional): Describe the details about your injection procedure.
* **Injection Site** (`injection_site`, text, Optional): Specify the injection site.
* **Injection Frequency** (`injection_frequency`, number, Optional): Specify the frequency of your injections.
* **Injection Duration** (`injection_duration`, number, Optional): Specify the duration of your injections.
* **Injection Dose** (`injection_dose`, number, Optional): Specify the dose of your injections.
* **Injection Frequency Unit** (`injection_frequency_unit`, select_one, Optional): Select the unit for your injection frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Other).
* **Injection Site Allergies** (`injection_site_allergies`, select_multiple, Required): Select any allergies related to your injection site.
* **Medical Conditions Allergies** (`medical_conditions_allergies`, select_multiple, Required): Select any medical conditions you are allergic to.
* **Medical History Allergies** (`medical_history_allergies`, select_multiple, Required): Select any allergies related to your medical history.
* **Consent Allergies** (`consent_allergies`, select_multiple, Required): Select any allergies related to your consent.
* **Medication Allergies 2** (`medication_allergies_2`, select_multiple, Required): Select any medications you are allergic to.
* **Medical Conditions 2** (`medical_conditions_2`, select_multiple, Optional): Select whether you have any medical conditions.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, select_multiple, Optional): Select whether you have any medical history.
* **Medication** (`medication`, select_multiple, Optional): Select whether you have any medications.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, Optional): Select whether you have any allergies.
* **Consent 2** (`consent_2`, select_multiple, Optional): Select whether you have given consent.
* **Medical History Other** (`medical_history_other`, text, Optional): Provide any additional information about your medical history.
* **Medical Conditions Other** (`medical_conditions_other`, text, Optional): Provide any additional information about your medical conditions.
* **Consent Other** (`consent_other`, text, Optional): Provide any additional information about your consent.
