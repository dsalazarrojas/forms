# Obsessive Compulsive Disorder (OCD) Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare providers assess the severity of symptoms and impact on daily life of individuals with Obsessive Compulsive Disorder (OCD).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability. For the multiple choice questions, select the option that best describes your current situation. For text fields, provide as much detail as possible. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): Please provide a brief overview of your current situation and symptoms.
* **Symptom Checklist** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer the following questions:
	+ Do you experience repetitive thoughts or mental images?
	+ Do you experience anxiety or fear of certain situations?
	+ Do you have intrusive thoughts or urges?
	+ Do you engage in compulsive behaviors?
* **Severity** (`3`, `number`, required: false): Please rate the severity of your symptoms on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being minimal and 10 being severe.
* **Impact on Relationships** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Describe how OCD has affected your relationships with family, friends, or romantic partners.
* **Impact on Occupation** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Describe how OCD has affected your ability to perform your job or daily activities.
* **Impact on Leisure Activities** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Describe how OCD has affected your ability to engage in leisure activities or hobbies.
* **Impact on Hobbies** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Describe how OCD has affected your ability to participate in hobbies or activities you enjoy.
* **Functional Impairment** (`8`, `number`, required: false): Please rate the level of functional impairment caused by your symptoms on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being minimal and 10 being severe.
* **Social Impact** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Describe how OCD has affected your social interactions or relationships.
* **Emotional Impact** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Describe how OCD has affected your emotional state or mood.
* **Physical Impact** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Describe any physical symptoms or experiences caused by your OCD, such as pain or fatigue.
* **Mental Impact** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Describe how OCD has affected your mental health or well-being.
* **Overall Impact** (`13`, `number`, required: false): Please rate the overall impact of OCD on your life on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being minimal and 10 being severe.
* **Functional Capacity** (`14`, `number`, required: false): Please rate the level of functional capacity caused by your symptoms on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being minimal and 10 being severe.
* **Self-Care Capacity** (`15`, `number`, required: false): Please rate the level of self-care capacity caused by your symptoms on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being minimal and 10 being severe.
* **Self-Care Support** (`16`, `number`, required: false): Please rate the level of self-care support you receive from others on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being minimal and 10 being extensive.
* **Self-Care Independence** (`17`, `number`, required: false): Please rate the level of self-care independence caused by your symptoms on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being minimal and 10 being severe.
* **Self-Care Habilitation** (`18`, `text`, required: false): Describe any self-care strategies or techniques you use to manage your symptoms.
* **Treatment Goals** (`19`, `text`, required: false): Describe your goals for treatment and what you hope to achieve.
* **Treatment Plan** (`20`, `text`, required: false): Describe any treatment plan or therapy you are currently undergoing.
* **Follow-up Plan** (`21`, `text`, required: false): Describe any follow-up appointments or check-ins you have with your healthcare provider.
* **Patient Involvement** (`22`, `number`, required: false): Please rate the level of your involvement in your treatment on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being minimal and 10 being extensive.
* **Provider Involvement** (`23`, `number`, required: false): Please rate the level of your healthcare provider's involvement in your treatment on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being minimal and 10 being extensive.
* **Family Involvement** (`24`, `number`, required: false): Please rate the level of your family's involvement in your treatment on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being minimal and 10 being extensive.
* **Other Involvement** (`25`, `number`, required: false): Please rate the level of other involvement in your treatment on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being minimal and 10 being extensive.

Note: This form is not intended for diagnosis, but rather as a tool for healthcare providers to gather information about the symptoms and effects of OCD on a person's life. Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
