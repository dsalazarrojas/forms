# COVID 19 Employee Health Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your health status, potential exposure to COVID-19, and any chronic conditions or medications you may be taking. The information collected will help us to better understand your health and potential risks in the workplace, and we will use it to take necessary precautions to keep you and your colleagues safe.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form completely and accurately, including all relevant information.
2. For each question, select the answer that best describes your current situation.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please select "Not sure" and provide a brief explanation in the "Current Medications" field.
4. If you have any medical conditions or concerns that are not listed, please include them in the "Current Medications" field.

## Tips

* If you experience any symptoms or concerns, please inform your supervisor or HR representative.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* This form is not a substitute for medical advice or diagnosis. If you need medical attention, please seek help from a qualified medical professional.
