# Stress Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your stress levels, symptoms, and coping mechanisms. It will ask you to rate your stress levels, identify any physical, emotional, and cognitive symptoms, and explore your sources of support and self-care practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and select the best answer that applies to you.
2. For questions with multiple options, choose all that apply.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses, as they will help us better understand your stress experience.
4. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you or that you are not comfortable answering.
