# Temporary Candidate COVID 19 Self Screening - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a candidate's risk of COVID-19 exposure, particularly regarding potential contact with infected individuals, symptoms of the virus, and recent travel history. The goal is to help identify candidates who may require further evaluation or testing to ensure a safe work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately, as your responses will help us understand your risk of exposure.
2. Select the correct options for each question from the provided lists.
3. Provide detailed answers where necessary, such as in the "Other Symptoms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Have you been in contact with someone diagnosed with COVID-19?** (`have_you_been_in_contact_with_someone_diagnosed_with_covid_19`, select_one, **required**):
	+ Choose "Yes" if you have been in close contact (within 6 feet) with someone diagnosed with COVID-19 within the last 14 days.
	+ Choose "No" if you have not been in close contact with someone diagnosed with COVID-19 within the last 14 days.
* **Temperature (F)** (`temporary_candidate_covid_19_self_screening_temperature`, number, **required**):
	+ Enter your current temperature in degrees Fahrenheit (°F).
* **Do you have any symptoms of COVID-19?** (`do_you_have_any_symptoms_of_covid_19`, select_multiple, **required**):
	+ Select all the symptoms you are experiencing from the list:
		- Yes, Fever
		- Yes, Cough
		- Yes, Shortness of Breath
	+ If you are not experiencing any symptoms, choose "None" from the list.
* **Other Symptoms** (`other_symptoms`, text, **optional**):
	+ If you are experiencing symptoms not listed, please describe them here.
* **Travel History?** (`travel_history`, select_one, **required**):
	+ Choose "Yes" if you have traveled recently (within the last 14 days).
	+ Choose "No" if you have not traveled recently (within the last 14 days).
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, select_one, **required**):
	+ Select the location where you traveled from the list:
		- Country
		- City
		- Airport
* **Days since travel** (`days_since_travel`, number, **required**):
	+ Enter the number of days since your last travel date.
* **Last Travel Location** (`last_travel_location`, text, **required**):
	+ Describe the location where you traveled.
* **Country of origin?** (`country_of_origin`, select_one, **required**):
	+ Choose "Yes" if you are from a country with a high-risk COVID-19 situation.
	+ Choose "No" if you are not from a high-risk country.
* **Last Travel City** (`last_travel_city`, select_one, **optional**):
	+ If you traveled to a city, select it from the list:
		- City
		- City 2
* **Airport Name?** (`airport_name`, select_one, **optional**):
	+ If you traveled through an airport, select it from the list:
		- Yes
		- No
* **Last Travel Airport** (`last_travel_airport`, select_one, **optional**):
	+ If you traveled through an airport, select it from the list:
		- Airport
		- Other

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering these questions to ensure a thorough assessment of your risk.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your HR representative for clarification.
