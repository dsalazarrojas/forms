# medication_effects_on_nutritional_status - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information on how medications may affect a patient's nutritional status. This includes gathering data on the patient's medical condition, list of medications, dietary restrictions, and other related factors. The collected data will help in better understanding the effects of medications on a patient's nutritional status.

## How To Complete This Form

*   This form is divided into two sections, **Medical Information** and **Nutritional Status**, and **Patient Information**. Please fill out the form in the following order:
    1.  Fill in the **patient_name**, **medical_condition**, **medication_list**, and **dietary_restrictions** in the **Medical Information** section.
    2.  In the **Patient Information** section, fill in **food_intake**, **water_intake**, **medication_side_effects**, **patient_weight** and **date_started_medication** and **date_stopped_medication** fields.
    3.  Finally, in the **Nutritional Status** section, select the **patient_nutritional_status** from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient for whom you are filling out the form.
*   **Medical Condition**: Select one or more medical conditions from the drop-down list.
*   **Medication List**: Select all medications taken by the patient.
*   **Dietary Restrictions**: Select any dietary restrictions or allergies the patient may have.
*   **Food Intake**: Enter the patient's food intake.
*   **Water Intake**: Enter the patient's water intake.
*   **Medication Side Effects**: Select any side effects of the medication the patient is taking.
*   **Patient Weight**: Enter the patient's weight.
*   **Date Started Medication**: Enter the date the patient started taking the medication.
*   **Date Stopped Medication**: Enter the date the patient stopped taking the medication.
*   **Patient Nutritional Status**: Select the patient's current nutritional status from the provided options.

This form is for collecting information on the effects of medication on a patient's nutritional status. Please fill it out carefully and accurately.
