# Autism Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Autism Survey is a collection of questions designed to gather information about community attitudes towards individuals with autism. This form aims to understand how often people interact with someone with autism, how comfortable they are in social settings, and their awareness of support services for autism. Your responses will contribute to improving social inclusion and accessibility for individuals with autism.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Read each question carefully and provide your honest answers.
2.  Select the most accurate response for each question from the provided options.
3.  Fill in any required fields as indicated by the "required" label in each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Contact Frequency** (id: 4, required: true)
Contact Frequency: How often do you interact with someone with autism?

*   This question asks about the frequency of your interactions with someone with autism. Please select the option that best describes your typical experience.

### 2. **Comfort Level Interacting** (id: 5, required: true)
Comfort Level Interacting: How comfortable are you interacting in social settings?

*   This question assesses your comfort level when interacting with someone with autism. Choose the response that best describes your feelings.

### 3. **Awareness of Support Services** (id: 6, required: false)
Awareness of Support Services: Are you aware of local support services for autism?

*   This question inquires about your knowledge of support services available for individuals with autism in your area. Select 'True' if you are aware, 'False' if you are not.

### 4. **Barriers to Inclusion** (id: 7, required: false)
Barriers to Inclusion: Describe any barriers you perceive for inclusion.

*   This open-ended question invites you to share any obstacles or challenges you experience or have experienced in interacting with individuals with autism.

### 5. **Suggestions for Community Support** (id: 8, required: false)
Suggestions for Community Support: Ideas to improve social inclusion and accessibility.

*   This question allows you to contribute your ideas and suggestions for enhancing social inclusion and accessibility for individuals with autism.

### 6. **Introduction Note** (id: 1, required: false)
-- Community Attitudes Survey --

*   This is an introductory note that provides context for the survey.

### 7. **Respondent Age** (id: 2, required: false)
Respondent Age: Age bracket of the respondent.

*   This question asks for your age group.

### 8. **Respondent Relation** (id: 3, required: false)
Respondent Relation: Relationship to someone with autism if applicable.

*   This question inquires about your connection to someone with autism if applicable.

By following this guide, you will be able to provide accurate and meaningful responses to the Autism Survey, contributing to a better understanding of community attitudes towards individuals with autism.
