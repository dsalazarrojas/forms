# new_shortened_covid_19_patient_questionnaire_and_consent_for_dental_treatment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from patients for dental treatment planning and execution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and honestly.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field (ID 2).
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (ID 3).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (ID 4).
5. Select the date of your scheduled appointment in the "Appointment Date" field (ID 5).
6. Select the time of your scheduled appointment in the "Appointment Time" field (ID 6).
7. Enter the address of our office in the "Office Address" field (ID 7).
8. Indicate whether you are required to wear a mask for your appointment by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Required to Wear a Mask" field (ID 8).
9. If you have any medical condition or allergies that may affect your treatment, enter the information in the "Mask Provision" field (ID 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (ID 2, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (ID 3, email, required): Enter your personal email address where you can be contacted.
- **Phone** (ID 4, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Appointment Date** (ID 5, date, required): Select the date of your scheduled appointment.
- **Appointment Time** (ID 6, time, required): Select the time of your scheduled appointment.
- **Office Address** (ID 7, text, required): Enter the address of our office.
- **Required to Wear a Mask** (ID 8, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you are required to wear a mask for your appointment. Select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
- **Mask Provision** (ID 9, text, optional): If you have any medical condition or allergy that affects your ability to wear a mask, enter the necessary information here.

## Tips
* Please complete the form accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask for assistance from our staff.
* Your information will be kept confidential and used solely for the purpose of treating your dental condition.
