# Patient Intake Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient information for medical assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and accurately to ensure that your patient's information is up-to-date and accurate.
2. You can fill out the form at your own pace, but please do not skip any required fields.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: This section is for collecting basic patient information, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Medical History**: Please describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, conditions, or surgeries.
* **Contact Details**: Provide your contact information, including phone number and email, so that we can get in touch with you for any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Medical Condition**: Describe the patient's current medical condition, if any.
* **Medication Use**: Indicate if the patient takes any medications, and if so, please list them.
* **Medical Condition Description**: Provide a detailed description of the patient's medical condition.
* **Medication List**: List the patient's current medications.
* **Allergies**: Describe any allergies the patient may have.
* **Medical History Details**: Provide any additional medical history information that may be relevant.
* **Patient Notes**: Add any notes or comments about the patient.
* **Contact Method**: Choose your preferred contact method: Phone, Email, or Text.
* **Emergency Contact**: Indicate if the patient has an emergency contact, and provide their name and relationship.
* **Contact Method 2**: Choose a second contact method, if applicable.
* **Medical Specialty**: Select the patient's medical specialty, such as Cardiology or Obstetrics & Gynecology.
* **Medical Specialty 2**: Select a second medical specialty, if applicable.
* **Contact Number**: Provide your contact number.
* **Email**: Provide your email address.
* **Patient ID**: Provide a unique patient ID, if applicable.
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of the patient's visit.
* **Time of Visit**: Enter the time of the patient's visit.
* **Medical Specialty 3**: Select a third medical specialty, if applicable.
* **Medical Specialty 4**: Select a fourth medical specialty, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Gender**: Choose the patient's gender.
* **Medical History Text**: Provide any additional medical history information that may be relevant.

Please note that some fields are required, and you must fill them out to complete the form. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask for assistance.
