# Pregnancy And Development Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on pregnancy and child development data. This includes details about the patient's pregnancy status, gestational age, prenatal care habits, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, first, select the patient's pregnancy status.
- Fill in the number of months pregnant the patient is.
- Enter the gestational age of the patient.
- Choose the patient's prenatal care habits.
- Provide any additional comments or experiences related to the pregnancy in the "Experiences" field.
- Select the healthcare team that has been managing the patient's care.
- Enter any prenatal test results for the patient.
- Identify any pregnancy complications the patient may have experienced.
- Indicate if the patient was hospitalized during pregnancy.
- Select the location where the patient delivered the baby.
- Enter the weight of the baby at birth.
- Enter the length of the baby at birth.
- Enter the date of the baby's birth.
- Select the method of delivery.
- Enter the weight of the infant.
- Enter the length of the infant.
- Enter the date of the infant's birth.
- Indicate if the patient is breastfeeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pregnancy Status** (`pregnancy_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the current status of the patient's pregnancy (Pregnant or Not Pregnant).
- **Months Pregnant** (`months_of_pregnancy`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of months pregnant the patient is.
- **Gestational Age** (`gestational_age`, `number`, optional): Enter the gestational age of the patient.
- **Prenatal Care Habits** (`prenatal_care_habits`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the patient's prenatal care habits (Regular prenatal care, Irregular prenatal care).
- **Experiences** (`experiences`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or experiences related to the pregnancy.
- **Healthcare Team** (`healthcare_team`, `select_one`, optional): Select the healthcare team managing the patient's care (Primary care physician, OB/GYN specialist, Midwife).
- **Prenatal Test Results** (`prenatal_test_results`, `text`, optional): Enter any prenatal test results for the patient.
- **Pregnancy Complications** (`pregnancy_complications`, `select_multiple`, optional): Identify any pregnancy complications the patient may have experienced (High blood pressure, Diabetes, Other (please specify)).
- **Hospitalization** (`hospitalization`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if the patient was hospitalized during pregnancy (True or False).
- **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, `select_one`, optional): Select the location where the patient delivered the baby (Hospital, Home, Clinic).
- **Birth Weight** (`birth_weight`, `number`, optional): Enter the weight of the baby at birth.
- **Birth Length** (`birth_length`, `number`, optional): Enter the length of the baby at birth.
- **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the baby's birth.
- **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select the method of delivery (Natural or C-section).
- **Infant Weight** (`infant_weight`, `number`, optional): Enter the weight of the infant.
- **Infant Length** (`infant_length`, `number`, optional): Enter the length of the infant.
- **Infant Birth Date** (`infant_birth_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the infant's birth.
- **Breastfeeding** (`breastfeeding`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate if the patient is breastfeeding (Started breastfeeding, Never breastfed, Currently breastfeeding).
