The "COVID Daily Health Screening Form For School" is designed to be used for the daily health screening of students in a school setting. This form is intended to help identify potential health risks and ensure a safe environment for all students and staff. The form is typically completed by students or authorized school personnel and is usually filled out at the start of each school day.

Before completing this form, please ensure that you have not filled it out previously for the current day. If you have already completed the form, you do not need to fill it out again. However, if you have experienced any new or worsening symptoms since the last screening, please update your responses accordingly.

## COVID Daily Health Screening Form For School - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID-19 Daily Health Screening Form is designed to quickly and easily identify potential health risks in students, enabling the school to take necessary precautions to keep the student and staff safe.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Complete all required fields before submitting the form.
3. For select_one questions, choose the response that best answers the question.
4. For temperature readings, enter the actual reading in Fahrenheit.
5. For date and time fields, enter the current date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your student ID or record.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`): Enter your student ID number if you have one. If you don't have a student ID number, enter 'Unknown' or leave this field blank.
* **Date of Screening** (`screening_date`): Enter the current date (e.g., 02/15/2022).
* **Temperature - 100.4F or Higher** (`fever_check`): Answer 'True' if you have a temperature of 100.4F or higher.
* **Temperature Reading** (`actual_temperature`): Enter your actual temperature reading in Fahrenheit.
* **New or Worsening Cough** (`cough`): Answer 'True' if you have a new or worsening cough.
* **Shortness of Breath or Difficulty Breathing** (`shortness_of_breath`): Answer 'True' if you experience shortness of breath or difficulty breathing.
* **Sore Throat** (`sore_throat`): Answer 'True' if you have a sore throat.
* **Extreme Fatigue or Lethargy** (`fatigue`): Answer 'True' if you experience extreme fatigue or lethargy.
* **Headache or Body Aches** (`headache`): Answer 'True' if you have a headache or body aches.
* **Loss of Taste or Smell** (`taste_smell`): Answer 'True' if you have lost your taste or smell.
* **Nausea, Vomiting, or Diarrhea** (`nausea_vomiting`): Answer 'True' if you experience nausea, vomiting, or diarrhea.
* **Known Contact with COVID-19 Case** (`covid_contact`): Answer 'True' if you have had contact with someone with COVID-19 within the past 14 days.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Provide the name of the person you came into contact with and the date of contact.
* **Prior COVID-19 Diagnosis** (`prior_diagnosis`): Answer 'True' if you have been diagnosed with COVID-19 within the past 3 months.
* **Clearance to Attend School** (`clearance_to_attend`): Answer 'Cleared - may attend' if your screening results allow you to attend school.
* **Health Screening Pass Issued** (`health_pass_issued`): Answer 'True' if you have been issued a health screening pass.
* **Screened By - Staff Name** (`screening_staff_name`): Enter the name of the staff member conducting the screening.
* **Time of Screening** (`screening_time`): Enter the time of the screening (e.g., 08:30 AM).
* **Additional Health Notes** (`additional_notes`): Add any additional health notes or concerns that you have.
