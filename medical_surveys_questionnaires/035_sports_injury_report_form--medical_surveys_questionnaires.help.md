## Sports Injury Report Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The "Sports Injury Report Form" is designed to collect essential information about sports-related injuries, enabling healthcare professionals and team management teams to monitor athlete health and performance.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the athlete's name in the "Athlete Name" field.
2.  Provide the date of the injury in the "Date of Injury" field using the date picker.
3.  Describe the injury in the "Description of Injury" field.
4.  Select the type of injury (if applicable) by choosing from the provided options in the "Injury Type" field.
5.  Enter the date of the training session where the injury occurred in the "Training Session" field.
6.  If applicable, select the competition level at which the injury occurred from the "Competition Level" field.
7.  Provide any medical attention received in the "Medical Attention" field.
8.  Update the athlete's status in the "Athlete Status" field.
9.  Document any treatment administered in the "Treatment" field.
10.  Schedule a follow-up appointment in the "Follow-up" field.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Athlete Name** (Required)
    Enter the name of the athlete who sustained the injury.
*   **Date of Injury** (Required)
    Select the date when the injury occurred using the date picker.
*   **Description of Injury** (Required)
    Provide a detailed account of the injury, including its severity and any other relevant details.
*   **Injury Type** (Optional)
    Select the type of injury that occurred, if known. Options include:
    \- Muscle Strain
    \- Ligament Strain
    \- Concussion
*   **Training Session** (Required)
    Select the date of the training session where the injury occurred.
*   **Competition Level** (Optional)
    If the injury occurred during a competition, select the competition level from the available options: National, Club, or Rec.
*   **Medical Attention** (Optional)
    Record any medical attention received due to the injury.
*   **Athlete Status** (Optional)
    Update the athlete's status after the injury. Options include:
    \- Injured
    \- Illness
*   **Treatment** (Optional)
    Document any treatment administered to the athlete.
*   **Follow-up** (Optional)
    Schedule a follow-up appointment to monitor the athlete's progress.
