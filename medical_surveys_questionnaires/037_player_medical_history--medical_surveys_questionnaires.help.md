# Player Medical History - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Medical History form is designed to collect personal and medical information from players. This data will be used to provide a safe and healthy environment for players to participate in sports and physical activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **player_name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **birth_date** in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Enter your **health_card_number**.
4. Provide your **emergency_contact_name** and **emergency_contact_phone** details.
5. List any **allergies** you have.
6. List any **illnesses** you currently have or have had in the past.
7. List any **medications** you are currently taking.
8. Describe any **previous_sports_injuries** you have experienced.
9. Indicate whether you have a **family_history_heart_disease** by selecting from the options provided.
10. Confirm if all your **all_organ_intact** are currently intact by selecting from the options provided.
11. Confirm if you have any paired **paired_organs** by selecting from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **player_name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **birth_date**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **health_card_number**: Provide your health insurance card number or any other unique identifying number.
* **emergency_contact_name**: Enter the name of the person you want to contact in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_contact_phone**: Enter the phone number of the person you want to contact in case of an emergency.
* **allergies**: List any allergies you have, including food, medication, or any other allergies.
* **illnesses**: List any current or past illnesses, including chronic conditions or injuries.
* **medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **previous_sports_injuries**: Describe any previous sports-related injuries you have experienced.
* **family_history_heart_disease**: Select whether you have a family history of heart disease from the options provided.
* **all_organ_intact**: Confirm if all your organs are currently intact by selecting from the options provided.
* **paired_organs**: Confirm if you have any paired organs by selecting from the options provided.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions as accurately and honestly as possible.
* If you have any doubts or concerns about any field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* Ensure that your emergency contact is available and reachable.

Note: The above guide is not intended to provide medical advice or diagnoses. The collected data will be used to provide a safe and healthy environment for players. If you have any medical concerns, please consult a healthcare professional.
