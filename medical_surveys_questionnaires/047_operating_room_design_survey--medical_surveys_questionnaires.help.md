# Operating Room Design Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the operating room's design, layout, equipment, space, and team involvement. It helps healthcare professionals to provide their opinions and suggestions on how to improve the operating room's usability and functionality.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the operating room's design, layout, equipment, space, and team involvement.
2. Select the relevant options for each section, and provide any additional comments or suggestions.
3. Fill in the required fields with your opinions and ratings.
4. Submit the form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Operating Room Layout** (`or_room_layout`): Select all the options that apply to the operating room's layout.
	+ Option 1: The layout is well-designed and functional.
	+ Option 2: The layout is somewhat functional, but could be improved.
	+ Option 3: The layout is not functional and needs improvement.
* **Operating Room Equipment** (`or_equipment`): Select all the options that apply to the operating room's equipment.
	+ Option 1: The equipment is well-maintained and functional.
	+ Option 2: The equipment is somewhat functional, but could be improved.
	+ Option 3: The equipment is not functional and needs improvement.
* **Layout Issues** (`or_layout_issues`): Select all the options that apply to the operating room's layout issues.
	+ Option 1: The layout is too small.
	+ Option 2: The layout is too large.
	+ Option 3: The layout is just right.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions on the operating room's design, layout, equipment, space, or team involvement.
* **Medical Team Involvement** (`medical_team_involvement`): This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Operating Room Equipment List** (`or_equipment_list`): Select all the options that apply to the operating room's equipment list.
	+ Option 1: Yes, the equipment is available and functional.
	+ Option 2: No, the equipment is not available or functional.
* **Operating Room Space** (`or_space`): Rate the operating room's space on a scale of 1-5.
* **Operating Room Furniture** (`or_furniture`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions on the operating room's furniture.
* **Operating Room Lighting** (`or_lighting`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions on the operating room's lighting.
* **Additional Photos** (`additional_photos`): Provide any additional photos or documentation to support your feedback.
* **Medical Specialties** (`medical_specialties`): Select all the specialties that apply to the operating room's use.
	+ Option 1: General Surgery
	+ Option 2: Cardiology
	+ Option 3: Neurosurgery
* **Operating Room Designer** (`or_designer`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions on the operating room's designer or architect.
* **Operating Room Location** (`or_location`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions on the operating room's location.
* **Operating Room Name** (`or_name`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions on the operating room's name.
* **Operating Room Description** (`or_description`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions on the operating room's description.
* **Operating Room Design Satisfaction** (`or_design_satisfaction`): Rate the operating room's design satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Operating Room Layout Satisfaction** (`or_layout_satisfaction`): Rate the operating room's layout satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Operating Room Equipment Satisfaction** (`or_equipment_satisfaction`): Rate the operating room's equipment satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Operating Room Space Satisfaction** (`or_space_satisfaction`): Rate the operating room's space satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Operating Room Furniture Satisfaction** (`or_furniture_satisfaction`): Rate the operating room's furniture satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Operating Room Lighting Satisfaction** (`or_lighting_satisfaction`): Rate the operating room's lighting satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`additional_comments2`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Operating Room Team Involvement** (`or_team_involvement`): This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Operating Room Survey Date** (`or_survey_date`): This field is not applicable for this form.

Note: Fields marked as "not applicable" are not included in this help guide as they are not relevant to the user's feedback.
