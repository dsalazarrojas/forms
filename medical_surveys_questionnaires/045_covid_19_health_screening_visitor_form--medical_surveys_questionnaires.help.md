# Covid 19 Health Screening Visitor Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Covid 19 Health Screening Visitor Form is a medical survey designed to identify visitors who may have been exposed to or infected with COVID-19. It helps medical professionals quickly assess visitors' medical history, symptoms, and travel history to reduce the risk of COVID-19 transmission within a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. If you have any symptoms or medical history that may be related to COVID-19, select the corresponding option from the dropdown menu.
3. If you do not have any symptoms or medical history that may be related to COVID-19, select "None of the above" or "No symptoms" from the dropdown menu.
4. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields for medical history and travel history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions. Provide as much detail as possible.
- **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_one`, required): Select any symptoms you have experienced in the past 14 days, including:
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Shortness of breath
	+ Sore throat
	+ Runny nose or congestion
	+ Muscle aches or pains
	+ Headache
	+ Fatigue
	+ Loss of taste
	+ Loss of smell
	+ Diarrhea
	+ Nausea or vomiting
	+ Chest pain or pressure
	+ Confusion
	+ Difficulty breathing
	+ Loss of appetite
	+ New or change in the pattern of cough
	+ New or change in sputum production
	+ New or change in skin
	+ New or change in mental status
	+ New or change in urinary output
	+ New or change in bowel function
	+ New or change in muscle weakness
	+ New or change in dizziness
	+ New or change in syncope
	+ New or change in seizures
	+ New or change in tremors
	+ New or change in ataxia
	+ New or change in incontinence
	+ New or change in hematemesis
	+ New or change in hematuria
	+ New or change in hemoptysis
	+ New or change in hematochezia
	+ New or change in melena
- **Contact Tracing** (`contact_tracing`, `text`, required): Provide any contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses, of people you have been in contact with in the past 14 days.
- **Travel History** (`travel_history`, `text`, required): Describe your recent travel history, including destinations and dates of travel.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough when completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, ask a medical professional for guidance.
* This form is designed to be completed by visitors to the healthcare setting.
