# Declaration Of Compliance Covid 19 Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a crucial document that provides a record of COVID-19 vaccination. It ensures that healthcare providers and authorities have accurate and up-to-date information on vaccine administration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the correct answers to each question based on your vaccination experience.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Ensure that all fields are filled out carefully, as this information will be used for official documentation and tracking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Have you received the Covid 19 vaccine?** (`Have_you_received_the_Covid_19_vaccine`, `select_multiple`, required): Answer this question with "Yes" or "No" based on your COVID-19 vaccination experience.
* **Last Name (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Date vaccinated (`date_vaccinated`, `date`, required): Provide the date you received the COVID-19 vaccine. Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **First Name (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address. This will be used for official communication.
* **Phone number (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your valid phone number. This will be used for contact purposes.
* **Has undergone test (`has_undergone_test`, `select_multiple`, required): Answer this question with "Yes" or "No" based on your experience with COVID-19 testing.
* **Vaccination dose (`vaccination_dose`, `number`, required): Enter the number of doses of the COVID-19 vaccine you have received.
* **Vaccination location (`vaccination_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where you received the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Vaccination center (`vaccination_center`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the vaccination center where you received the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Vaccination administered by (`vaccination_administered_by`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the healthcare professional who administered the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Vaccine Certificate (`vaccine_certificate`, `text`, required): Provide any existing vaccine certificate you have received.
* **Medical Certification (`medical_certification`, `text`, required): Provide any medical certification related to your COVID-19 vaccination experience.
* **Proof of inocularation (`proof_of_inoculation`, `text`, required): Provide any proof of inoculation related to your COVID-19 vaccination experience.
* **Vaccination Card (`vaccination_card`, `text`, required): Provide any vaccination card related to your COVID-19 vaccination experience.
* **Proof of Vaccination (`proof_of_vaccination`, `text`, required): Provide any proof of vaccination related to your COVID-19 vaccination experience.
* **Proof of COVID 19 (`proof_of_covid`, `text`, required): Provide any proof of COVID-19 vaccination experience.
* **Other Proof (`other_proof`, `text`, required): Provide any other proof related to your COVID-19 vaccination experience.
* **Proof of Inoculation 2 (`proof_of_inoculation_2`, `text`, required): Provide any other proof of inoculation related to your COVID-19 vaccination experience.
* **Other Proof 2 (`other_proof_2`, `text`, required): Provide any other proof related to your COVID-19 vaccination experience.
* **Proof of Vaccination 2 (`proof_of_vaccination_2`, `text`, required): Provide any other proof of vaccination related to your COVID-19 vaccination experience.
* **Proof of COVID 19 2 (`proof_of_covid_2`, `text`, required): Provide any other proof of COVID-19 vaccination experience.
* **Other Proof 3 (`other_proof_3`, `text`, required): Provide any other proof related to your COVID-19 vaccination experience.
* **Proof of Vaccination 3 (`proof_of_vaccination_3`, `text`, required): Provide any other proof of vaccination related to your COVID-19 vaccination experience.
* **Other Proof 4 (`other_proof_4`, `text`, required): Provide any other proof related to your COVID-19 vaccination experience.
