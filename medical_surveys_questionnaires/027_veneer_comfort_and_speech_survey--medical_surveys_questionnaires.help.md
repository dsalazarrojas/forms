# Veneer Comfort And Speech Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Veneer Comfort And Speech Survey is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experience with the treatment, specifically regarding comfort level, sensitivity, speech changes, overall satisfaction, and other related aspects. This survey aims to gather information that can help healthcare professionals improve the treatment process and provide better care for future patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read through each question carefully and answer the ones that apply to your experience with the treatment.
2. Select the answer that best matches your feelings or experiences for each question.
3. If you have any other comments or concerns, please use the space provided for notes.
4. Fill in your actual treatment duration and time in the respective fields.
5. Make sure to submit your answers accurately and honestly, as this will help us improve the treatment process.
