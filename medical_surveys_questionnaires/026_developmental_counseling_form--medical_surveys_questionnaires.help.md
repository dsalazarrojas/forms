# Developmental Counseling Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Developmental Counseling Form is a patient data collection form used to gather demographic and medical information from patients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are completing the form in a secure and private setting, and that the patient's identity is verified.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, using the following fields:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Date of Birth
	* Address
	* Phone Number
	* Email
	* Medical History
	* Family History
	* Mental Health History
	* Treatment Plan

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name 
**First Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it is commonly known.

### Last Name
**Last Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it is commonly known.

### Date of Birth
**Date of Birth** (`date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Address
**Address** (`text`, required: false): Enter your current address, including apartment or suite number, city, state, and zip code.

### Phone Number
**Phone Number** (`text`, required: false): Enter your primary phone number for communication.

### Email
**Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your primary email address.

### Medical History
**Medical History** (`text`, required: false): Provide a comprehensive medical history, including any allergies and past or current medical conditions.

### Family History
**Family History** (`text`, required: false): Describe your family's medical history, including any hereditary medical conditions.

### Mental Health History
**Mental Health History** (`text`, required: false): Describe any mental health conditions you may have, including therapy or treatment history.

### Treatment Plan
**Treatment Plan** (`text`, required: false): Describe any treatment plans or recommendations you have for your care.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you answer all fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them in the Medical History field.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact your healthcare provider for support.
