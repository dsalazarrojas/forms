# Dysphagia Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to assess a patient's swallowing difficulties and collect information on their medical history, symptoms, and experiences related to swallowing.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Choose the answers based on your experiences and feelings.
3. Review each question carefully before proceeding to ensure accuracy.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask a healthcare professional for assistance.

* **2. Patient Info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required): This field is for providing your name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **3. Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required): Answer "Yes" if you experience difficulty swallowing, and "No" if you do not.
