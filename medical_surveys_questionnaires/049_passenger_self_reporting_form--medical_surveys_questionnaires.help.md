# Passenger Self Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Passenger Self Reporting Form is a tool designed to help passengers report their health symptoms in a quick and efficient manner. This form collects information on the frequency and severity of various symptoms, allowing healthcare professionals to make informed decisions about the passenger's treatment and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding text fields.
2. Enter your Email address, which will be used for correspondence and follow-up.
3. Select the frequency and severity of each symptom from the options provided. Please answer "Yes" or "No" to the first question, and choose "mild", "moderate", or "severe" for the other symptoms.
4. Once you have completed all the fields, click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name is required to be completed.
* **Last Name**: Your last name is required to be completed.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for correspondence and follow-up. Please make sure to enter a valid email address.

* **Symptoms**: Select the frequency and severity of each symptom from the options provided:

	+ **Difficulty Breathing**: Select "mild", "moderate", or "severe" based on the severity of your difficulty breathing symptom.
	+ **Shortness of Breath**: Select "mild", "moderate", or "severe" based on the severity of your shortness of breath symptom.
	+ **Loss of Taste/Smell**: Select "mild", "moderate", or "severe" based on the severity of your loss of taste/ smell symptom.
	+ **Runny Eyes**: Select "mild", "moderate", or "severe" based on the severity of your runny eyes symptom.
	+ **Fatigue**: Select "mild", "moderate", or "severe" based on the severity of your fatigue symptom.
	+ **Headache**: Select "mild", "moderate", or "severe" based on the severity of your headache symptom.
	+ **Muscle Pain**: Select "mild", "moderate", or "severe" based on the severity of your muscle pain symptom.
	+ **Diarrhea**: Select "mild", "moderate", or "severe" based on the severity of your diarrhea symptom.
	+ **Nausea**: Select "mild", "moderate", or "severe" based on the severity of your nausea symptom.
	+ **Loss of Appetite**: Select "mild", "moderate", or "severe" based on the severity of your loss of appetite symptom.

* **Submitted By**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form, which will be used for correspondence and follow-up.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and accurately to help healthcare professionals assess your condition effectively.
* If you are unsure about the severity of a symptom, please choose the option that best describes your condition.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the healthcare professional assigned to you for further assistance.
* Make sure to submit the form only once, as repeating the form will not provide any additional information to the healthcare professionals.
