# medication_log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for tracking medication usage and related information for healthcare professionals to monitor treatment efficacy and potential risks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "medication_log" form.
2. Select a date for when you started taking the medication (medication_start_date).
3. Select a date for when you plan to stop taking the medication (medication_end_date).
4. Enter the dosage of the medication (medication_dose).
5. Choose how often you take the medication (medication_frequency).
6. Enter the units of the medication (dosage_units).
7. Select any side effects you are experiencing (medication_side_effects).
8. Enter any additional frequency information for taking the medication (medication_log_frequency).
9. Provide a reason for taking the medication (reason_for_use).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **medication_log_form**: A text field for entering the medication name or description.
- **medication_date**: Date field for selecting the start date of medication.
  This field should be filled with a date in a standard format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
- **medication_dose**: A field for entering the dosage amount of the medication.
  Enter a numerical value.
- **medication_frequency**: A field for selecting how often the medication is taken.
  Choose from standard units like "daily", "weekly", etc.
- **start_date**: A date field for selecting the start date of medication.
  This field should be filled with a date in a standard format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
- **end_date**: A date field for selecting the end date of medication.
  This field should be filled with a date in a standard format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
- **how_often**: A field for selecting the frequency of medication.
  Choose from options like "daily", "weekly", etc.
- **units**: A field for selecting the units of measurement for the medication dosage.
  Choose from standard units like "mg", "ml", etc.
- **side_effects**: A field for selecting any experienced side effects.
  Choose from provided options or add your own if not listed.
- **frequency**: A field for entering additional information about the medication's frequency.
  Enter text to describe how often you take the medication.
- **reason_for_use**: A field for entering the reason for taking the medication.
  Provide a brief description of why you are taking the medication.
- **medication_side_effects**: A field for selecting any experienced side effects.
  Choose from provided options or add your own if not listed.
  
# Tips
* Make sure to accurately enter all required fields to ensure the form is complete and useful for healthcare professionals.
* If a field is not required, feel free to add any relevant information that might be helpful.
* Double-check that you've filled out all fields before submitting the form.
