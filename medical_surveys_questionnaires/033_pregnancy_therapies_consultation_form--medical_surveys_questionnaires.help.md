# Pregnancy Therapies Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from pregnant clients to make informed decisions regarding their treatment and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Select the week of your pregnancy.
3. Provide your emergency contact's name and phone number (if applicable).
4. Describe your current state of health.
5. Consent to treatment by selecting "Yes" or "No".
6. Provide further details about your condition, if necessary.
7. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
8. Include your printed name, initials, and title (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Id** (`1`, number, required/optional): Enter your unique client ID, if applicable.
* **Client Name** (`2`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`3`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Week of Pregnancy** (`4`, number, required): Select the week of your pregnancy.
* **Phone** (`5`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`6`, text, required): Enter your physical address for communication and visit purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`7`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact, if you have one.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`8`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact, if you have one.
* **Current State of Health** (`9`, text, required): Describe your current state of health, including any medical conditions or concerns.
* **Consent to Treatment** (`10`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to consent to treatment or "No" if you decline.
* **Further Details** (`11`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your condition or concerns, if necessary.
* **I consent to the treatment terms and conditions** (`12`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to agree to the terms and conditions of treatment.
* **Date of Consent** (`13`, date, required): Date the form after selecting your consent.
* **Time of Consent** (`14`, time, optional): Enter the time of your consent, if desired.
* **Additional Comments** (`15`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions about your condition or treatment.
* **Signature** (`16`, text, required): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Date of Signature** (`17`, date, required): Date your signature after signing the form.
* **Initials** (`18`, text, optional): Enter your initials, if required by the healthcare team.
* **Printed Name** (`19`, text, optional): Enter your printed name, if required by the healthcare team.
* **Title** (`20`, text, optional): Enter your title (e.g., Mrs., Mr., Dr.).
* **Hospital Name** (`21`, text, optional): Enter the name of the hospital where you are receiving treatment.
* **Doctor Name** (`22`, text, optional): Enter the name of your doctor.
* **Department** (`23`, text, optional): Enter the department of the hospital where you are receiving treatment.
* **Medical Record Number** (`24`, text, optional): Enter your medical record number, if applicable.
* **Medical Record Date** (`25`, date, optional): Enter the date of your medical record, if applicable.
