# Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to assess an individual's awareness and perception of a foreign conflict. It is designed to gather information on various aspects of conflict, including awareness levels, sources of influence, and conflict types. Completing this form will help to provide valuable insights for further research and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select your responses based on your genuine understanding and experience.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please mark it as "No" or "Unknown" if applicable.
4. Fill in all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
5. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Awareness and Perception
* **Awareness and Perception** (awareness_and_perception, select_multiple, Optional):
	+ This question assesses your level of awareness and perception of a foreign conflict.
	+ Please select all the options that apply to you, if any.

### 2. Information Source
* **Information Source** (information_source, text, Required):
	+ This field asks about the primary source of information you have on the conflict.
	+ Please enter the source of your information (e.g., news article, expert opinion, personal experience).

### 3. Date of Data Collection (optional)
* **Data Collection** (data_collection, time, Optional):
	+ This question asks for the date when you last collected information on the conflict.
	+ If no specific date is available, you can leave this field blank.

### 4. Sources of Influence
* **Sources of Influence** (sources_of_influence, select_one, Optional):
	+ This question assesses the extent to which you have been influenced by external factors (e.g., media, social media, family, friends) in your understanding of the conflict.
	+ Select one option that best represents your experience.

### 5. Awareness Level
* **Awareness Level** (awareness_level, select_one, Optional):
	+ This question asks about your level of awareness on the conflict (High or Low).
	+ Please select the option that accurately reflects your understanding.

### 6. Conflict Type
* **Conflict Type** (conflict_type, select_one, Optional):
	+ This question asks if you are involved in the conflict or have any connections to it (Yes or No).
	+ Select one option that best represents your experience.

### 7. Additional Information (optional)
* **Additional Information** (sources, text, Optional):
	+ This field is for providing any additional information you feel is relevant to the conflict.
	+ Please enter any additional insights or comments about the conflict.
