# Patient Rehabilitation Progress Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Rehabilitation Progress Assessment form is a tool used to track the progress of patients undergoing physical therapy. It helps healthcare professionals to monitor the patient's recovery status, identify areas of improvement, and make informed decisions on their treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the date of the visit from the calendar or enter it manually in the "Visit Date" field.
3. Enter the name of the therapist responsible for the patient's care in the "Therapist Name" field.
4. Select the type of assessment being performed from the options listed in the "Assessment Type" field.
5. Indicate the frequency of the assessment from the options listed in the "Assessment Frequency" field.
6. Record the patient's pain score in the "Pain Score" field.
7. Enter any relevant information about the patient's functional ability in the "Functional Ability" field.
8. List any medication the patient is currently taking in the "Medication List" field.
9. Specify the treatment goal for the patient in the "Treatment Goal" field.
10. Record any goals set by the patient in the "Patient Goals" field.
11. Specify the goals set by the therapist for the patient in the "Therapist Goals" field.
12. Enter any progress notes or comments about the patient's condition in the "Progress Notes" field.
13. Provide any recommendations for the patient's care in the "Recommendations" field.
14. If applicable, enter the follow-up date in the "Follow-up Date" field.
15. Select the frequency of the follow-up from the options listed in the "Follow-up Frequency" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Visit Date**: Enter the date of the visit or select it from the calendar. The format is MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Therapist Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare professional responsible for the patient's care.
* **Assessment Type**: Select the type of assessment being performed, such as Initial, Follow-up, or Discharge.
* **Assessment Frequency**: Indicate the frequency of the assessment, such as Weekly, Bi-weekly, or Monthly.
* **Pain Score**: Record the patient's current pain level, usually on a scale of 1-10.
* **Functional Ability**: Provide information on the patient's current functional ability, including any limitations or restrictions.
* **Medication List**: List any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Treatment Goal**: Specify the goal of the treatment plan, such as "Reduce pain" or "Improve mobility".
* **Patient Goals**: Record any goals set by the patient, such as "Improve balance" or "Increase range of motion".
* **Therapist Goals**: Specify the goals set by the healthcare professional, such as "Improve strength" or "Reduce stress".
* **Progress Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the patient's progress, including any changes or concerns.
* **Recommendations**: Provide any recommendations for the patient's care, including exercises, activities, or lifestyle changes.
* **Follow-up Date**: If applicable, enter the date of the next follow-up visit.
* **Follow-up Frequency**: Select the frequency of the follow-up visit, such as Daily, Weekly, Bi-weekly, or Monthly.
