# Ear Infection Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the risk of ear infection in patients. It collects general information, symptoms, risk factors, medical history, ear infection history, and next steps to be taken by the healthcare provider. By completing this form, healthcare providers can quickly and accurately assess the risk of ear infection and provide appropriate care to patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please review the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the healthcare provider before completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)**: Enter any general information about the patient that you think may be relevant to their risk of ear infection.
	+ Example: "Age, sex, or other relevant medical history"
* **Symptoms (2)**: Select all the symptoms that the patient is experiencing, such as ear pain, fever, or difficulty hearing.
	+ Example: "Pain, Fever, Difficulty hearing"
* **Risk Factors (3)**: Choose the risk factors that apply to the patient, such as recent travel or allergies.
	+ Example: "Recent travel, Allergies, Other"
* **Medical History (4)**: Enter any relevant medical history, such as previous ear infections or surgeries.
	+ Example: "Previous ear infections, Surgeries"
* **Detailed Medical History (5)**: Enter any additional medical information that may be relevant to the patient's risk.
	+ Example: "Previous medical conditions, Medications"
* **Ear Infection History (6)**: Select any previous ear infection experiences.
	+ Example: "Previous ear infections, Ear infections with antibiotics"
* **Risk Assessment (7)**: Choose the level of risk for the patient's ear infection.
	+ Example: "Low, Moderate, High"
* **Next Steps (8)**: Choose the next steps to be taken by the healthcare provider, such as referral or appointment.
	+ Example: "Referral, Appointment"
