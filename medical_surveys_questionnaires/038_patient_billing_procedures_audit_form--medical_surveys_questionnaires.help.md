# Patient Billing Procedures Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Billing Procedures Audit Form is used to audit and review patient billing procedures to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the patient's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter the patient's address in the "Address" field.
5. Enter the name of the procedure performed in the "Procedure Name" field.
6. Enter the date of the procedure in the "Date of Procedure" field.
7. Enter the name of the practitioner who performed the procedure in the "Practitioner Name" field.
8. Enter the cost of the procedure in the "Procedure Cost" field.
9. If applicable, enter the name of the minimalism organization or representative in the "Minimalism Name" field.
10. If applicable, enter the contact information for the minimalism organization or representative in the "Minimalism Email" and "Minimalism Phone" fields.
11. Select the current status of the procedure from the "Procedure Status" dropdown menu.
12. Select the follow-up status from the "Follow-up Status" dropdown menu.
13. Enter the date of the follow-up in the "Follow-up Date" field.
14. Enter any additional comments about the follow-up in the "Follow-up Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address.
* **Procedure Name**: Enter the name of the procedure performed.
* **Date of Procedure**: Enter the date the procedure was performed.
* **Practitioner Name**: Enter the name of the practitioner who performed the procedure.
* **Procedure Cost**: Enter the cost of the procedure.
* **Minimalism Name**: Optional. Enter the name of the minimalism organization or representative.
* **Minimalism Email**: Optional. Enter the contact email of the minimalism organization or representative.
* **Minimalism Phone**: Optional. Enter the contact phone number of the minimalism organization or representative.
* **Procedure Status**: Select the current status of the procedure from the dropdown menu:
	+ Completed
	+ Pending
	+ Cancelled
* **Follow-up Status**: Select the follow-up status from the dropdown menu:
	+ Completed
	+ Pending
	+ Cancelled
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date of the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Comment**: Optional. Enter any additional comments about the follow-up.
