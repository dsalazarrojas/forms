# Hypertension Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on how healthcare providers communicate about hypertension to their patients. Completing this form will help us understand how effectively you receive information about your condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions honestly, based on your actual experience with the healthcare provider.
2. For each question, provide your genuine thoughts and feelings.
3. Do not provide any confidential information about your health.
4. Please answer all applicable questions, even if you are not satisfied with the current state of communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: How People Receive Information
* **Information About Hypertension** (`text`, required/optional): This question is about how often you receive updates or information about your blood pressure, including any explanations or discussions about your condition. Be honest and share your thoughts about the clarity and frequency of communication you receive from your healthcare provider.

### Page 2: Satisfaction Measures
* **Satisfaction Measures** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): In the past, how satisfied were you with the communication about your blood pressure? Select all that apply:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied

### Page 3: Suggestions for Improvement
* **Suggestions** (`note`, required/optional): Do you have any suggestions on how the communication about your blood pressure could be improved? Please share your thoughts in the space below.
