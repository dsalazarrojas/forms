# Eating Disorder Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about eating habits, symptoms, medical history, and other relevant factors related to eating disorders. The data collected will help healthcare professionals assess and treat patients with eating disorders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant pages that apply to your situation.
2. Enter your information in the corresponding fields, making sure to include all relevant details.
3. Review your answers carefully to ensure they accurately reflect your experiences and conditions.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information (page 1)
* **Patient Information (patient_info)**: Please provide general information about the patient, including their name, contact details, and any other relevant background information.

### Eating Habits (page 2)
* **Eating Habits (eating_habits)**: Describe your eating habits, including how often you eat, how much you eat, and any difficulties you experience during meals.

### Symptoms (page 3)
* **Symptoms (symptoms)**: List any physical or emotional symptoms you are experiencing related to eating disorders, such as feeling full, experiencing food cravings, or having difficulty sleeping.

### Medical History (page 4)
* **Medical History (medical_history)**: Provide a brief medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or treatments you have undergone.

### Eating Frequency (page 5)
* **Eating Frequency (eating_frequency)**: Indicate how often you eat per day, using the scale below:
	+ 1-2 times a day
	+ 3-4 times a day
	+ 5-6 times a day
	+ More than 6 times a day

### Meal Plan (page 6)
* **Meal Plan (meal_plan)**: Describe your typical meal plan, including what foods you eat, how much you eat, and when you eat.

### Weight Management (page 7)
* **Weight Management (weight_management)**: Discuss your methods of weight management, such as dieting, exercising, or using supplements.

### Emotional Triggers (page 8)
* **Emotional Triggers (emotional_triggers)**: Identify any emotional triggers that contribute to your eating habits, such as stress, anxiety, or boredom.

### Treatment History (page 9)
* **Treatment History (treatment_history)**: Describe any previous treatment for eating disorders, including the type of treatment and its outcome.

### Medication (page 10)
* **Medication (medication)**: List any medications you are currently taking or have taken for eating disorders.

### Family History (page 11)
* **Family History (family_history)**: Provide information about your family medical history, including any eating disorder conditions or medical conditions that may be relevant.

### Mental Health (page 12)
* **Mental Health (mental_health)**: Discuss any mental health conditions that may be contributing to or related to your eating habits.

### Social Support (page 13)
* **Social Support (social_support)**: Identify any social support systems you have in place, such as therapists, support groups, or loved ones.

### Physical Activity (page 14)
* **Physical Activity (physical_activity)**: Discuss your physical activity level and any relevant exercise or physical challenges.

### Medical Conditions (page 15)
* **Medical Conditions (medical_conditions)**: List any current or past medical conditions that may be contributing to or related to your eating habits.

### Mental Health Conditions (page 16)
* **Mental Health Conditions (mental_conditions)**: Identify any mental health conditions that may be contributing to or related to your eating habits.

### Treatment Plans (page 17)
* **Treatment Plans (treatment_plans)**: Describe any treatment plans you have in place, including therapy, counseling, or medication.

### Treatment Outcomes (page 18)
* **Treatment Outcomes (treatment_outcomes)**: Report any treatment outcomes, including successes or challenges.

### Follow-up (page 19)
* **Follow-up (follow_up)**: Schedule a follow-up appointment to review your progress and adjust your treatment plan as needed.

### Other Conditions (page 20)
* **Other Conditions (other_conditions)**: Provide any other relevant medical or mental health conditions you would like to mention.

Note: This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice. If you are experiencing any symptoms or concerns related to eating disorders, please consult a healthcare professional.
