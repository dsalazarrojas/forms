# Adult Physical Exam Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Adult Physical Exam Form is used to collect vital signs, medical history, and physical examination findings for adult patients. This form helps healthcare providers assess the patient's overall health status, identify potential medical issues, and guide further treatment or testing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review the form**: Make sure you understand the purpose and structure of the form.
2. **Gather necessary information**: Review the patient's medical history and current condition before completing the form.
3. **Complete each field**:
	* **Body Temperature - Fahrenheit**: Record the patient's body temperature in degrees Fahrenheit.
	* **Blood Pressure Systolic**: Record the patient's blood pressure in millimeters of mercury (mmHg).
	* **Blood Pressure Diastolic**: Record the patient's diastolic blood pressure in mmHg.
	* **Heart Rate - BPM**: Record the patient's heart rate in beats per minute (BPM).
	* **Body Weight - lbs**: Record the patient's weight in pounds (lbs).
	* **Head and Neck Exam**: Select whether the head and neck exam is **Normal** or **Abnormal**.
	* **Head and Neck Notes**: Describe any abnormalities noted during the head and neck exam.
	* **Cardiovascular Exam**: Select whether the cardiovascular exam is **Normal** or **Abnormal**.
	* **Cardiovascular Notes**: Describe any cardiovascular notes.
	* **Respiratory Exam**: Select whether the respiratory exam is **Normal** or **Abnormal**.
	* **Respiratory Notes**: Describe any respiratory notes.
	* **Abdominal Exam**: Select whether the abdominal exam is **Normal** or **Abnormal**.
	* **Abdominal Notes**: Describe any abdominal notes.
	* **Musculoskeletal Exam**: Select whether the musculoskeletal exam is **Normal** or **Abnormal**.
	* **Musculoskeletal Notes**: Describe any musculoskeletal notes.
	* **Primary Diagnosis or Finding**: Summarize your clinical assessment and diagnosis.
	* **Recommended Follow-Up Plan**: Provide any suggested lab tests or specialist referrals.
	* **Physician Name and Credentials**: Record your name and credentials.
