# Multiple Sclerosis Treatment Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experience with MS treatment. The collected data will help healthcare providers evaluate the effectiveness of different treatments and improve patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Select the most accurate answer for each question based on your experience with the treatment.
3. Provide additional comments or concerns about your experience in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your experience like with the treatment?** (`ms_treatment_experience`, `text`, required): Describe your overall experience with the treatment, including any positive and negative aspects.
* **How would you rate the effectiveness of the treatment?** (`ms_treatment_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate how effective you found the treatment to be, using the provided scale (Very Effective, Somewhat Effective, Neutral, Somewhat Ineffective, Not at All Effective).
* **Did you experience any side effects?** (`ms_side_effects`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all the side effects you experienced, using the provided options (None, Yes).
* **What was the outcome of your treatment experience?** (`ms_treatment_outcome`, `select_one`, required: false): Report the outcome of your treatment experience, using the provided options (Improved, No change, Worsened).
* **Additional comments or concerns about your treatment experience?** (`ms_additional_comments`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or concerns you have about your experience with the treatment.
* **What is your disease status?** (`patient_disease_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Report your current disease status, using the provided options (Active, Remission, Relapsing, Primary Progressive).
* **What was your treatment dose?** (`treatment_dose`, `select_one`, required: false): Report the dose of the treatment you received, using the provided options (Low, Moderate, High).
* **How long did you receive the treatment?** (`treatment_duration`, `date`, required: false): Report the length of time you received the treatment, using the date format (e.g., 2022-01-01 to 2022-01-31).
* **How often did you receive the treatment?** (`treatment_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you received the treatment, using the provided options (Once a day, Once a week, Once a month).
* **What unit of time did you receive the treatment?** (`treatment_duration_unit`, `select_one`, required: false): Report the unit of time used to measure the treatment duration, using the provided options (Days, Weeks, Months).

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide accurate information about your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them in the "Additional comments" field.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact the healthcare provider for guidance.
