# COVID 19 Viewer Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the COVID-19 pandemic from the perspective of viewers like you. Your participation is valuable in helping us understand how the pandemic is affecting your daily life and what you think about the government's response.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer each question carefully, taking into account your personal experiences and opinions.
2.  Select the best option that matches your views from the provided choices.
3.  Fill out all required fields, denoted by the \*asterisk symbol.
4.  You can select multiple options for questions that allow it, like "Select all that apply".
5.  For text fields, provide as much detail as you'd like, but keep it concise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Demographic Information --**: This section collects basic demographic information to help us understand the diversity of our respondents.
  * **Age Group**: Select your age category.
*   **Age Group**: Select your age category.
*   **Gender**: Select your gender.
*   **State or Region**: Enter your location (state or region).
*   **Education Level**: Select your highest level of education completed.
*   **-- COVID-19 Perception --**: This section explores your views on the pandemic.
  * **How Severe is the COVID-19 Pandemic**: Select how serious you think the pandemic is.
  * **How Much Has COVID-19 Affected Your Life**: Select how much the pandemic has affected you personally.
  * **How Serious Are COVID-19 Deaths**: Select how seriously you worry about COVID-19 deaths.
  * **How Adequate is Media Coverage**: Select how well you think the media reports on the pandemic.
*   **-- Government Response --**: This section gauges your views on government actions.
  *   **Effectiveness of Government Response**: Rate the government's effectiveness in handling the pandemic.
  *   **Opinion on Lockdown Measures**: Select your opinion on lockdowns.
  *   **Opinion on Mask Mandates**: Select your opinion on mask requirements.
  *   **Opinion on School Closures**: Select your opinion on school closures.
*   **-- Personal Behavior --**: This section looks at your practices to stay safe.
  *   **Mask Wearing Frequency**: Select how often you wear masks.
  *   **Social Distancing Practice**: Select your social distancing practices.
  *   **Activity Modifications**: Select how much you've changed your daily activities.
*   **-- Information Sources --**: This section identifies where you get your information.
  *   **Most Trusted Information Sources**: Select all sources you trust.
*   **Concern About Misinformation**: Select your level of concern about COVID-19 misinformation.
*   **-- Personal Impact Areas --**: This section is about areas affected by the pandemic.
  *   **Which Areas of Your Life Have Been Affected**: Select all areas affected.
*   **Additional Comments or Concerns**: If you have any other thoughts or concerns about the pandemic, please share.
*   **Most Trusted Information Sources**: Select all sources you trust.
