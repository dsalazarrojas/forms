# Endodontic Procedure Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Endodontic Procedure Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback from patients after an endodontic procedure. This form helps us understand your experience and satisfaction with the procedure, and any medical conditions you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page as accurately and completely as possible.
2. Make sure to fill out every page of the form.
3. If a field is not required, you can skip it, but we encourage you to provide as much information as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1
* **Page 1** (`page_1`, text, not required): Please start by filling out this page if you have any additional comments or concerns that you would like to share with us.

### Patient Information (Page 2)
* **Name** (`page_2_1`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Age** (`page_2_2`, number, required): Please enter your age.
* **Gender** (`page_2_3`, select_one, not required): Please select your gender.
* **Medical Condition** (`page_2_4`, text, required): Please describe any medical conditions you may have.

### Endodontic Procedure (Page 3)
* **Procedure Date** (`page_3_1`, date, required): Please enter the date of the procedure.
* **Procedure Time** (`page_3_2`, time, required): Please enter the time of the procedure.

### Feedback (Page 4)
* **Satisfaction Level** (`page_4_1`, select_multiple, required): Please select how satisfied you are with the procedure.
* **Additional Comments** (`page_4_2`, text, not required): Please provide any additional comments or concerns you may have.
* **Feedback** (`page_4`, text, required): Please provide your overall feedback about the procedure.

### Additional Information (Page 5)
* **Additional Comments** (`page_5_1`, text, not required): Please provide any additional comments or concerns you may have.

Tips:

* Please be as accurate and detailed as possible when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your dentist or healthcare provider for clarification.
* We value your feedback and appreciate your honesty.
