# Antibody And Antigen Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your medical history, vaccination status, and other relevant details to help healthcare professionals better understand your immune system and make informed decisions for your care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Participant ID, which is a unique identifier for you.
2. Choose the date you completed the survey.
3. Enter your age in years.
4. Select your gender from the options provided.
5. Choose any chronic conditions you have from the list of options.
6. Indicate your vaccination status, including whether you are fully vaccinated, partially vaccinated, or not vaccinated.
7. If you have received any vaccines, select the type(s) you received.
8. Enter the date of your most recent vaccination.
9. Indicate whether you have had a booster dose.
10. Report your history of previous infections, including confirmed and suspected cases.
11. Enter the date of your most recent infection, if known.
12. Select any symptoms you have experienced from the list of options.
13. Choose the severity of your symptoms.
14. Indicate whether you have been hospitalized due to your condition.
15. Report your testing history, including any PCR tests, rapid antigen tests, and antibody tests.
16. Select your antibody test results, if available.
17. Enter the level of antibodies in your blood, if quantified.
18. Report your antigen test results, if available.
19. Enter the date of your most recent antigen test.
20. Assess your exposure risk level based on your occupation and other factors.
21. Enter the number of people in your household.
22. Indicate whether you are currently experiencing symptoms.
23. Describe any current symptoms you are experiencing.
24. Report any immune-related medications you are taking.
25. Indicate your willingness to participate in future testing.
26. Provide any additional comments or information you think is relevant to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Participant ID (participant_id)
* Participant ID (`participant_id`, text, required): A unique identifier for you, which is used to track your progress and results.

### 2. Survey Date (survey_date)
* Survey Date (survey_date, date, required): The date you completed this survey.

### 3. Age (age)
* Age (age, number, required): Your age in years.

### 4. Gender (gender)
* Gender (gender, select_one, required): Select your gender from the provided options.

### 5. Medical History (medical_history)
* Medical History (medical_history, select_multiple, required): Choose any chronic conditions you have from the list of options.

### 6. Vaccination Status (vaccination_status)
* Vaccination Status (vaccination_status, select_one, required): Indicate your vaccination status.

### 7. Vaccine Type (vaccine_type)
* Vaccine Type (vaccine_type, select_multiple, optional): If you have been vaccinated, select the type(s) of vaccine you received.

### 8. Most Recent Vaccination Date (vaccination_date)
* Most Recent Vaccination Date (vaccination_date, date, optional): Enter the date of your most recent vaccination.

### 9. Booster Dose (booster_received)
* Booster Dose (booster_received, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have had a booster dose.

### 10. Previous Infection History (previous_infection)
* Previous Infection History (previous_infection, select_one, required): Report your history of previous infections.

### 11. Infection Date (infection_date)
* Infection Date (infection_date, date, optional): Enter the date of your most recent infection, if known.

### 12. Symptoms Experienced (symptoms_experienced)
* Symptoms Experienced (symptoms_experienced, select_multiple, required): Select any symptoms you are experiencing from the list of options.

### 13. Symptom Severity (symptom_severity)
* Symptom Severity (symptom_severity, select_one, required): Choose the severity of your symptoms.

### 14. Hospitalization Required (hospitalization)
* Hospitalization Required (hospitalization, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have been hospitalized due to your condition.

### 15. Testing History (testing_history)
* Testing History (testing_history, select_multiple, required): Report your testing history, including any PCR tests, rapid antigen tests, and antibody tests.

### 16. Antibody Test Results (antibody_test_results)
* Antibody Test Results (antibody_test_results, select_one, required): Select your antibody test results, if available.

### 17. Antibody Level (antibody_level)
* Antibody Level (antibody_level, text, optional): Enter the level of antibodies in your blood, if quantified.

### 18. Antigen Test Results (antigen_test_results)
* Antigen Test Results (antigen_test_results, select_one, required): Select your antigen test results, if available.

### 19. Most Recent Test Date (test_date)
* Most Recent Test Date (test_date, date, optional): Enter the date of your most recent test.

### 20. Exposure Risk Level (exposure_risk)
* Exposure Risk Level (exposure_risk, select_one, required): Assess your exposure risk level based on your occupation and other factors.

### 21. Household Size (household_size)
* Household Size (household_size, number, required): Enter the number of people in your household.

### 22. Current Symptoms (current_symptoms)
* Current Symptoms (current_symptoms, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are currently experiencing symptoms.

### 23. Current Symptoms List (current_symptoms_list)
* Current Symptoms List (current_symptoms_list, text, optional): Describe any current symptoms you are experiencing.

### 24. Medication Use (medication_use)
* Medication Use (medication_use, select_multiple, required): Report any immune-related medications you are taking.

### 25. Willingness for Future Testing (willingness_for_future_testing)
* Willingness for Future Testing (willingness_for_future_testing, select_one, required): Indicate your willingness to participate in future testing.

### 26. Additional Comments (additional_comments)
* Additional Comments (additional_comments, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information you think is relevant to your situation.

## Tips
Please answer all required fields truthfully and thoroughly. If you are unsure about any field, please select "Not sure" or "Unknown."
