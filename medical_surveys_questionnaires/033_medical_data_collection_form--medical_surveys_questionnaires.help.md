# Medical Data Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for collecting medical data from patients, allowing medical practitioners to gather essential information for accurate diagnosis and treatment. It helps to ensure that medical practitioners have all the required data to provide optimal care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Fill in the required fields**: Please fill in the fields marked as required (`Medical Practitioner`, `Medical Practitioner`, `Medical Date of Birth`, and `Medical Survey`).
2.  **Enter patient information**: Fill in the `Patient Name`, `Patient Address`, `Contact Number`, `Medical Email`, and `Medical Record ID` fields with accurate information.
3.  **Choose a medical practitioner**: Select the correct medical practitioner from the list (`Dr Smith` or `Dr Johnson`).
4.  **Provide medical history**: Enter any relevant medical history in the `Medical History` field.
5.  **Choose medical questions**: Select any applicable questions from the `Medical Survey` field.
6.  **Submit the form**: Once all required fields are filled, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name (1)**: Enter the patient's name.
    *   **Type:** `text`
    *   **Required**: No
*   **Patient Address (2)**: Enter the patient's home address.
    *   **Type:** `text`
    *   **Required**: No
*   **Contact Number (3)**: Enter the patient's contact number.
    *   **Type:** `text`
    *   **Required**: No
*   **Medical Practitioner (4)**: Select the medical practitioner treating the patient.
    *   **Type:** `select_one`
    *   **Required**: Yes
*   **Medical History (5)**: Enter any relevant medical history.
    *   **Type:** `note`
    *   **Required**: No
*   **Medical Record ID (6)**: Enter the patient's medical record ID.
    *   **Type:** `text`
    *   **Required**: No
*   **Medical Date of Birth (7)**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
    *   **Type:** `date`
    *   **Required**: No
*   **Medical Time of Birth (8)**: Enter the patient's time of birth.
    *   **Type:** `time`
    *   **Required**: No
*   **Medical Survey (9)**: Select any applicable questions from the medical survey.
    *   **Type:** `select_multiple`
    *   **Required**: No
*   **Medical Email (10)**: Enter the patient's email address.
    *   **Type:** `email`
    *   **Required**: No
*   **Medical Note (11)**: Enter any additional medical notes.
    *   **Type:** `note`
    *   **Required**: No
*   **Form Submit (12)**: Click to submit the form once all required fields are filled.
    *   **Type:** `text`
    *   **Required**: No

## Tips

*   Please fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
*   Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
*   Review the form before submission to avoid any errors.
*   If you have any issues while filling in the form, contact your medical practitioner for assistance.
