# elderly_mental_wellness_study_participation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The elderly mental wellness study participation form is designed to collect information on the wellbeing scales for elderly people.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Fill out each wellbeing scale with a number from 0 to 10, where 0 means the scale is very poor and 10 means the scale is excellent.
