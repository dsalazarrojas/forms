# Mental Wellbeing Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Wellbeing Assessment form is designed to assess the current state of your mental wellbeing. This form is to be completed by individuals to help them understand their own mental wellbeing and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the required questions to ensure the form is valid.
2. Please answer "Yes" or "No" to the questions with the "Yes/No" options.
3. Enter your answers in the text fields as instructed.
4. Select your answer from the options for the "Wellbeing Score" field.
5. Select all the relevant options for the "Wellbeing Plan" field, separated by commas.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mental Wellbeing Assessment Page 1**: This is the first page of the mental wellbeing assessment. Please provide your answers to this page to help you better understand your mental state.
	+ **Contact Details** (`contact_details_page_1`, `text`, required): Enter your name, email, and other contact details here. This information will help us to get in touch with you for further support if needed.
* **Mental Wellbeing Assessment Page 2**: This is the second page of the mental wellbeing assessment. Please provide your answers to this page to get a better understanding of your mental state.
	+ **Contact Details** (`contact_details_page_2`, `text`, not required): Enter your contact details here, if you feel comfortable sharing this information.
* **Mental Health** (`mental_health_page`, `select_one`, not required): Please select "Yes" if you have any mental health issues, or "No" if you do not.
* **Emotional State** (`emotional_state_page`, `text`, not required): Describe your current emotional state here. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Mental Wellbeing Assessment Page 3**: This is the third page of the mental wellbeing assessment. Please provide your answers to this page to get a better understanding of your mental state.
	+ **Wellbeing Assessment** (`wellbeing_assessment_page`, `select_one`, not required): Please select "Yes" if you have any wellbeing issues, or "No" if you do not.
* **Mental Wellbeing Assessment Page 4**: This is the fourth page of the mental wellbeing assessment. Please provide your answers to this page to get a better understanding of your mental state.
* **Wellbeing Score** (`wellbeing_score_page`, `number`, required): Enter a numerical value between 1 and 10 to indicate your current wellbeing score.
* **Wellbeing Plan** (`wellbeing_plan_page`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all the options that apply to your wellbeing plan. Please be specific and choose all the relevant options, separated by commas.
