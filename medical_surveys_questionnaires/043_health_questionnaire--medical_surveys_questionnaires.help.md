# Health Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health Questionnaire is designed to collect comprehensive information about a patient's health status, medical conditions, allergies, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each section carefully to ensure you have not previously completed it.
2.  Fill in the required information for each field accurately and truthfully.
3.  Use the options provided for select fields to select the relevant answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Health** (`general_health`, `text`, required: false): Describe your overall health status in the provided text field.
* **Current Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the medical conditions that apply to you from the provided options.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the allergy that best describes your situation from the provided options.
* **Current Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): List your current medications in the provided text field.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Provide your medical history in the provided text field.
* **Social History** (`social`, `text`, required: false): Share your social history in the provided text field.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your primary medical provider.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, `text`, required: false): Share your family history in the provided text field.
* **Patient History** (`patient_history`, `text`, required: false): Provide your patient history in the provided text field.

## Tips
* Please answer each question accurately and truthfully.
* Review each section carefully to ensure that you have not previously completed it.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask for help or clarification.
* If you need to provide additional information, please add it to the corresponding field or section.
