# Patient Health Questionnaire And Generalized Anxiety Disorder Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for healthcare professionals or researchers to collect patient health information and assess generalized anxiety disorder (GAD) symptoms.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Please review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Be as detailed as possible when providing information about your symptoms and experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Health Questionnaire** (Patient Health Questionnaire, text, required): This section is a general health questionnaire where you can provide any relevant information about your health status.
* **Generalized Anxiety Disorder Questionnaire** (Generalized Anxiety Disorder Questionnaire, text, required): This section is specifically designed to assess your symptoms of generalized anxiety disorder. Please answer the questions carefully and honestly, as this information will help your healthcare provider understand your condition better.
* **Medical Surveys & Questionnaires** (Medical Surveys & Questionnaires, text, required): This section is a free-form text field where you can provide any additional information or context about your medical history, treatment, or other relevant information that might be helpful for your healthcare provider.

## Tips

* Please be thorough and complete as much of the form as possible.
