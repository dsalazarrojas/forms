# Advanced Biotechnology Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your proficiency levels in various areas of biotechnology, identify challenges you face, and determine your interests in future collaboration opportunities. Your input will help create targeted training programs and resources for the biotech community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the professional profile section, which includes your name and organization/institution.
2. In the Expertise Self-Assessment section, rate your proficiency levels in bioprocessing, bioinformatics, and regulatory affairs.
3. In the Industry Challenges section, select the primary challenges you face in your work.
4. In the Research and Development section, describe your current projects' focus and specific research goals.
5. Finally, in the Program Feedback section, provide suggestions for future biotech training programs and indicate your interest in collaboration opportunities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Professional Full Name** (`professional_full_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Organization or Institution** (`organization_or_institution`, text, required):
	+ Enter the name of your organization or institution.
* **Years of Experience in Biotech** (`years_of_experience_in_biotech`, number, required):
	+ Enter the number of years you have worked in the biotechnology industry.
* **Proficiency in Bioprocessing** (`proficiency_in_bioprocessing`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate your level of expertise in bioprocessing (Advanced, Intermediate, Beginner, None).
* **Proficiency in Bioinformatics** (`proficiency_in_bioinformatics`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate your level of expertise in bioinformatics (Advanced, Intermediate, Beginner, None).
* **Proficiency in Regulatory Affairs** (`proficiency_in_regulatory_affairs`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate your level of expertise in regulatory affairs (Advanced, Intermediate, Beginner, None).
* **Primary Challenges Faced** (`primary_challenges_faced`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select the primary challenges you face in your work (Funding Constraints, Regulatory Hurdles, Technical Limitations, Data Management, Ethical Concerns).
* **Description of Biggest Technical Bottleneck** (`description_of_biggest_technical_bottleneck`, text, required):
	+ Provide detailed description of the biggest technical challenge you face.
* **Focus of Current Projects** (`focus_of_current_projects`, select_one, required):
	+ Select the focus of your current projects (Therapeutics, Diagnostics, Agricultural Biotech, Environmental Biotech, Industrial Biotech).
* **Specific Research Goals** (`specific_research_goals`, text, required):
	+ Describe the specific goals of your current research projects.
* **Suggestions for Future Biotech Training Programs** (`suggestions_for_future_biotech_training_programs`, text, required):
	+ Suggest future training programs and resources for the biotech community.
* **Interest in Collaboration Opportunities** (`interest_in_collaboration_opportunities`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate your interest level in collaboration opportunities (High, Moderate, Low).

Note: If you have any questions or concerns while filling this form, please reach out to the form administrators.
