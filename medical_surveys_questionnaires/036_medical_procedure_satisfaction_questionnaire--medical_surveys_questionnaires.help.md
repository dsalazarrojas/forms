# Medical Procedure Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experience with a medical procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Use the space provided for each question to write your thoughts and comments.
3. Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
4. If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, not required): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **How would you rate the pain management during the procedure?** (`2`, `number`, required): Rate your satisfaction with the pain management during the procedure on a scale of 1-5.
* **Staff Support** (`3`, `select_one`, not required): How would you rate the support provided by the staff during the procedure? Choose from the options Yes, No, or Other.
* **Facility** (`4`, `text`, not required): Please provide any comments about the facility where you received treatment.
* **How would you rate the patient communication during the procedure?** (`5`, `text`, not required): Rate your satisfaction with the patient communication during the procedure.
* **How satisfied are you with the outcome of the procedure?** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options Very satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.
* **Comments** (`7`, `text`, not required): Please provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (`8`, `select_one`, not required): Choose from the options Yes, No, or Other.
* **Date of Procedure** (`9`, `date`, not required): Please enter the date of the procedure in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time of Procedure** (`10`, `time`, not required): Please enter the time of the procedure in the format 12:00 AM.
* **Any additional notes?** (`11`, `text`, not required): Please provide any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Medical Professionals involved** (`12`, `select_multiple`, not required): Please choose all the medical professionals who were involved in your procedure.
* **Main point of contact** (`13`, `select_one`, not required): Please choose the main point of contact for your procedure.
* **Facility 2** (`14`, `text`, not required): Please provide any comments about the facility 2.
* **How would you rate the patient communication?** (`15`, `text`, not required): Rate your satisfaction with the patient communication.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`16`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options Very satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.
* **Pain Management** (`17`, `select_one`, not required): How would you rate the pain management during the procedure? Choose from the options Yes, No, or Other.
* **Overall Satisfaction 3** (`18`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options Very satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.

Note: This form has multiple pages. Please review all pages carefully before submitting the form.
