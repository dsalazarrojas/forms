## Weight Loss Program Participant Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from participants in our weight loss program. It is a crucial tool to help us understand your goals, medical history, and emergency contact information. By providing accurate and complete information, you will help us tailor the program to your needs and ensure a safe and effective experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields. These are required fields and should be filled out accurately.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the designated field. This is a required field and will be used for communication purposes.
3. Describe your **Weight Loss Goal** in the dedicated field.
4. Enter the date you **started your program** in the date field.
5. Enter your **Height** in the text field.
6. Enter your **Phone** number in the text field.
7. Describe your **Medical History** in the text field.
8. List your **medications** in the text field.
9. Provide information about your **Emergency Contact**, including their name, relationship, and phone number.
10. Enter any additional **Medical Conditions** you may have in the select multiple field.
11. List any **Medical Devices** you are currently using in the text field.
12. Describe any **Medical Equipment** you are currently using in the text field.
13. Enter information about your **Doctor**, including their name and phone number.
14. Enter the name of the **Medical Facility** where you are being treated.
15. Enter any **Medical Notes** about your condition or treatment in the text field.
16. Provide information about your **Emergency Contact**, including their name, relationship, and phone number.

## Tips

* Please fill out this form accurately and completely.
