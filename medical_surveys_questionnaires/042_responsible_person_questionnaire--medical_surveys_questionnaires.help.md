# Responsible Person Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Responsible Person Questionnaire is used to collect information about the individual who will be responsible for the medical care and treatment of a patient. This information is crucial for medical professionals to have accurate contact details and to ensure continuity of care.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Navigate to the Responsible Person Questionnaire form.
2.  Enter the required information in the form.
3.  Fill out each section as described in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section.
4.  Review your answers for accuracy.
5.  Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: This field collects the individual's first name as it appears on their identification documents. Enter the individual's first name in this text box.
*   **Last Name**: This field collects the individual’s last name as it appears on their identification documents. Enter the individual's last name in this text box.
*   **Email**: This field collects the individual's email address. This will be used as a contact point for medical updates and communication. Enter a valid email address.
*   **Phone**: This field collects the individual's phone number. This will be used as a contact point for medical updates and communication. Enter a valid phone number.
*   **Date of Birth**: This field collects the individual's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. Enter the individual’s date of birth in this format.
