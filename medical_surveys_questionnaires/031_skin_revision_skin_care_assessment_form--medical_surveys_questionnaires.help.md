# Skin Revision Skin Care Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your skin health and skin care needs, which will help our team to provide the best possible skin revision service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section of the form with the required information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask the skin revision team for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Health** (`skin_revision_general_health`, text, required): Please describe your current overall health status.
* **Medical History** (`skin_revision_medical_history`, text, optional): If you have any previous medical conditions, please describe them here.
* **Family History** (`skin_revision_family_history`, text, optional): If you have any family members with skin problems, please describe them here.
* **Skin Care Products** (`skin_revisionskin_care_products`, select one, required): Are you currently using any skin care products? Please select one of the options.
* **Skin Problems** (`skin_revisionskin_problems`, text, optional): If you experience any skin problems, please describe them here.
* **Consent** (`skin_revision_consent`, text, optional): Please sign and date this form to indicate that you understand and agree to the terms of the skin revision service.
* **Therapist** (`skin_revision_therapist`, select one, required): Please indicate whether the skin revision therapist is male or female.
* **Notes** (`skin_revision_notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional information or comments, please enter them here.
* **Email** (`skin_revision_email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`skin_revision_phone`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`skin_revision_date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth.
* **Skin Care Routine** (`skin_revision_skin_care_routine`, text, optional): If you have a specific skin care routine, please describe it here.
