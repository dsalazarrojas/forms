# Pediatric Patient Intake Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Patient Intake Survey is a form designed to collect information about a child's health history, medications, and other relevant details to provide the best care for the child during a visit to the doctor.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Be thorough and accurate when answering the questions.
3. If you're unsure about any question, ask a healthcare professional for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (1, text, required): Enter the child's first name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Last name** (2, text, required): Enter the child's last name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of birth** (3, date, required): Enter the child's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact number** (4, text, required): Enter the contact number of the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Medical history** (5, text, optional): Describe the child's medical history, including any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies.
* **Medications** (6, select multiple, optional): Check all the medications that the child is currently taking. Options include 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Insurance provider** (7, text, optional): Enter the child's insurance provider's name and contact information.
* **Emergency contact** (8, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person in case of an unexpected event.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any question, ask a healthcare professional for assistance.
* This form will help the healthcare professionals understand the child's medical history, medications, and any allergies or special needs, which will enable them to provide the best care for the child during the visit.
