## Purpose
The Functional Capacity Evaluation Form is a comprehensive tool designed to collect and document a patient's functional capacity, medical history, and physical abilities. It's used to assess a patient's overall condition and help healthcare providers develop an appropriate treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Provide detailed responses to each field, as this information will help healthcare providers make informed decisions about your care.
3. Review and verify the information provided before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (1. **patient_name**): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date** (2. **date**): Enter the date of the assessment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Therapist Name** (3. **therapist_name**): Enter the name of the healthcare provider performing the assessment.
* **Patient ID** (4. **patient_id**): Enter your unique patient ID number.
* **Medical Condition** (5. **medical_condition**): Describe your current medical condition, if applicable.
* **Functional Capacity** (6. **functional_capacity**): List any functional limitations you may have due to your medical condition.
* **Assessment Type** (7. **assessment_type**): Select the type of assessment being performed (e.g., Musculoskeletal, Cardiovascular, Neurological).
* **Functional Limitations** (8. **functional_limitations**): Describe any functional limitations you have due to your medical condition.
* **Physical Abilities** (9. **physical_abilities**): List your physical abilities and any limitations you may have.
* **Medical History** (10. **medical_history**): Provide a brief summary of your medical history, if applicable.
* **Treatment Plan** (11. **treatment_plan**): Describe your proposed treatment plan.
* **Follow-up Plan** (12. **follow_up_plan**): Outline any follow-up plans or appointments.
* **Patient Comments** (13. **patient_comments**): Enter any comments or concerns you may have.
* **Therapist Comments** (14. **therapist_comments**): Enter any comments or notes from your healthcare provider.
* **Assessment Completed** (15. **assessment_completed**): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Assessment Completed By** (16. **assessment_completed_by**): Enter the name of the healthcare provider completing the assessment.
* **Assessment Completed Time** (17. **assessment_completed_time**): Enter the time of the assessment in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Assessment Completed Date** (18. **assessment_completed_date**): Enter the date of the assessment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical Provider Name** (19. **medical_provider_name**): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Medical Provider Title** (20. **medical_provider_title**): Enter your healthcare provider's title or profession.
* **Assessment Completed Date and Time** (21. **assessment_completed_date_time**): Enter the date and time of the assessment in the format MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Patient Signature** (22. **patient_signature**): Sign your name to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Medical Signature** (23. **medical_signature**): Sign your healthcare provider's name to confirm their involvement.
* **Medical Title** (24. **medical_title**): Enter your healthcare provider's title or profession.
* **Medical Provider License Number** (25. **medical_provider_license_number**): Enter your healthcare provider's license number.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Double-check the date and time of the assessment to ensure accuracy.
* Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submission. 
* Ensure that the patient and healthcare provider signatures are authentic and accurate. 
* Store the completed form in a secure location, as required.
