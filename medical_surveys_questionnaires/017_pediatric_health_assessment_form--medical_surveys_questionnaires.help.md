# Pediatric Health Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Health Assessment Form is designed to collect essential information about a child's health and medical history. This form is used to gather details about the child's personal and medical information, as well as their educational and healthcare needs. The data collected will be used to create a comprehensive profile for the child's medical and educational records.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the child's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the child's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the child's date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
4. If applicable, enter the name of the person responsible for the child's care in the "Caregiver Name" field.
5. Enter the contact number of the child's caregiver in the "Contact Number" field.
6. Enter the child's email address in the "Email" field, if applicable.
7. Enter the child's address in the "Address" field.
8. Describe any medical history or previous medical conditions in the "Medical History" field.
9. List any known allergies or medical conditions the child has in the "Allergy" field.
10. Enter any other relevant medical conditions in the "Medical Condition" field.
11. Enter the name and contact information of the child's emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact" field.
12. Enter the contact information of the child's emergency contact, including phone number in the "Emergency Phone" field.
13. Enter the email address of the child's emergency contact in the "Emergency Email" field.
14. Enter the name of the child's doctor in the "Doctor Name" field.
15. Enter the contact number of the child's doctor in the "Doctor Phone" field.
16. Enter the email address of the child's doctor in the "Doctor Email" field.
17. Enter the name of the child's school in the "School Name" field.
18. Enter the child's grade level in the "School Grade" field.
19. Enter the name of the child's school teacher in the "School Teacher" field.
20. Enter the contact number of the child's school in the "School Phone" field.
21. Enter the email address of the child's school in the "School Email" field.
22. Enter the name of the child's pediatrician in the "Pediatrician Name" field.
23. Enter the contact number of the child's pediatrician in the "Pediatrician Phone" field.
24. Enter the email address of the child's pediatrician in the "Pediatrician Email" field.
25. If applicable, enter any other health care providers the child sees in the "Other Health Care" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the child's first name. This field is used to identify the child's name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the child's last name. This field is used to identify the child's surname.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter the child's date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Caregiver Name**: If you are not the child's parent or guardian, enter the name of the person responsible for the child's care.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the person responsible for the child's care. This is used to reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the child's caregiver, if applicable.
* **Address**: Enter the child's home address.
* **Medical History**: Describe any medical history or previous medical conditions the child has.
* **Allergy**: List any known allergies or medical conditions the child has.
* **Medical Condition**: Enter any other relevant medical conditions the child has.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the child's emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the contact number of the child's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email**: Enter the email address of the child's emergency contact.
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of the child's doctor.
* **Doctor Phone**: Enter the contact number of the child's doctor.
* **Doctor Email**: Enter the email address of the child's doctor.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the child's school.
* **School Grade**: Enter the child's grade level.
* **School Teacher**: Enter the name of the child's school teacher.
* **School Phone**: Enter the contact number of the child's school.
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of the child's school.
* **Pediatrician Name**: Enter the name of the child's pediatrician.
* **Pediatrician Phone**: Enter the contact number of the child's pediatrician.
* **Pediatrician Email**: Enter the email address of the child's pediatrician.
* **Other Health Care**: If applicable, enter any other health care providers the child sees.
