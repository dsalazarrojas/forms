# Patient Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Information form is designed to collect relevant data for patients' medical records. 

## How To Complete This Form
Please provide information for each field carefully to ensure accurate records are kept.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Patient**: This field is for patients who do not already have an existing record. Enter any unique identifier or alias you want this patient to have.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the patient's contact information such as email or phone number, if available.
* **Patient History**: This field is to capture any medical history the patient may have.
* **Medical History**: A field to select whether the patient has an existing medical history or if they are a new patient. Options are 'Existing Patient' and 'New Patient'. 
* **Other Medical History**: If the patient has medical history that does not fit into the provided options, use this field to describe it.
* **Next of Kin**: A field to provide information about the patient's next of kin or family contact.
* **Next of Kin Other**: If the next of kin is not listed, enter the details here.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter any emergency contact information such as phone number or email.
* **Emergency Contact Other**: If the emergency contact is not listed, enter the details here.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time of Birth**: Enter the patient's time of birth in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Medical Condition History**: This field is to select whether the patient has a medical condition or not. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Other Medical Condition History**: If the patient has a medical condition not listed, describe it here.
