# Erectile Dysfunction Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help assess and understand the severity and duration of erectile dysfunction (ED) in individuals. Please fill out this form to provide us with accurate information about your ED symptoms, medical history, and treatment goals. This will help our team create a personalized plan to address your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Please fill out each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Answer all required fields, even if you're unsure or uncomfortable with a particular question.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
