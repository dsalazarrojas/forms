# HIPAA Notice Of Privacy Practices & Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HIPAA Notice Of Privacy Practices & Acknowledgement Form is a document that informs individuals about their rights under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and requires their acknowledgment of understanding and compliance with its requirements.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the form title in the "Form Title" field.
2. Select the correct category for the form from the "Category" dropdown menu.
3. Provide a brief description of the form in the "Description" field.
4. Check the box next to "I acknowledge that I have received and understood this notice and that I will comply with its requirements." to confirm your understanding.
5. Select the correct form category from the "Form Category" dropdown menu.
6. Enter a unique form number in the "Form Number" field.
7. Enter the desired output file name in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter the title of this form, e.g., "HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices & Acknowledgement Form".
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the category for this form from the dropdown menu. Options include "Medical Surveys & Questionnaires" and "Other".
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of this form, e.g., "A document that informs individuals about their rights under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA).".
* **I acknowledge that I have received and understood this notice and that I will comply with its requirements.** (`acknowledgement`, checkbox, required): Check the box to confirm that you have received and understood the notice.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_multiple, required): Select the category for this form from the dropdown menu. Options include "Medical Surveys & Questionnaires", "Other", and "HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices".
* **Form Number** (`form_number`, number, required): Enter a unique number for this form, e.g., "001".
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required): Enter the name of the file where you want to save the output, e.g., "hipaa_notice.pdf".
