# Medical Research Transparency Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to understand individuals' perception of transparency in medical research. The survey aims to collect information on respondents' experience and expectations regarding transparency in medical research, including their participation in research, areas of medical interest, and the level of transparency they expect from medical institutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each question. For questions that allow multiple selections, you can choose as many options as apply to you.
2. Answer the questions based on your genuine experience and perception.
3. Ensure that you fill in all required fields, indicated by the "required" flag on each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Have you participated in any medical research? 
* **Have you participated in any medical research?**: This question is asking about your personal experience with medical research. Select "Yes" if you have participated in any medical research, and "No" otherwise.

### 2. Which medical area are you interested in?
* **Which medical area are you interested in?**: This question is asking about the areas of medicine you are interested in or have a strong interest in. Select the option that best describes your area of interest.

### 3. What level of transparency do you expect from medical research?
* **What level of transparency do you expect from medical research?**: This question is asking about your expectations regarding the level of transparency in medical research. Since this is a numerical question, please respond with a numerical value.

### 4. What level of transparency do you expect from medical research?
* **What level of transparency do you expect from medical research?**: This question is identical to question 3 and is intended for comparison purposes only. Please ignore this question and proceed to the next.

### 5. Which medical research institution is most transparent?
* **Which medical research institution is most transparent?**: This question is asking about your perception of medical research institutions. Select "Yes" if you believe the institution is transparent, and "No" otherwise.

### 6. What is the name of the medical research institution that has the best transparency?
* **What is the name of the medical research institution that has the best transparency?**: This question is asking for a specific institution you consider to be highly transparent. Please provide the name of that institution.

### 7. What is your score for transparency in medical research?
* **What is your score for transparency in medical research?**: This question is asking about your personal score for transparency in medical research. Please respond with a numerical value that reflects your perception of transparency in medical research.

### 8. What is your score for transparency in the medical research institution?
* **What is your score for transparency in the medical research institution?**: This question is asking about your score for transparency in a medical research institution. Please respond with a free-form text that reflects your perception of transparency in that institution. 

## Tips
* Please answer based on your genuine experience and perception.
* Take as much time as you need to complete the form, and do not hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need clarification on any of the questions.
* Your responses will help us better understand individual perceptions of transparency in medical research.
