# Sleep Disorder Diagnosis Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a person's sleep patterns, symptoms, and medical history to aid in the diagnosis and treatment of sleep disorders.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2.  Fill in the required fields, marked with an \*. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sleep Disorder Info** (sleep_disorder_info): A text field where you can describe your sleep disorder in your own words.
* **Sleep Pattern** (sleep_pattern): This is a field to describe your typical sleep pattern.
* **Sleep Quality** (sleep_quality): Select one or more of the options provided (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent) to describe the quality of your sleep.
* **Insomnia Days** (insomnia_days): Enter the number of days you experience insomnia.
* **Sleep Disorder History** (sleep_disorder_history): A field where you can describe your sleep disorder history.
* **Sleep Patterns** (sleep_patterns): Select one option to describe your sleep patterns (Normal, Abnormal, Very Abnormal).
* **Sleep Disorder Diagnosis** (sleep_disorder_diagnosis): Select one or more of the options provided (Sleep Apnea, Insomnia, Sleep Paralysis, Sleep Terrors) to describe your sleep disorder diagnosis.
* **Sleep Disorder Symptoms** (sleep_disorder_symptoms): Select one or more of the options provided (Sleepiness, Fatigue, Confusion, Mood Swings) to describe your sleep disorder symptoms.
* **Medical History** (medical_history): A field where you can describe your medical history.
* **Other Health Conditions** (other_health_conditions): A field where you can describe any other health conditions you have.
* **Medication Use** (medication_use): A field where you can describe your medication use.
* **Sleep Disorder Treatment** (sleep_disorder_treatment): A field where you can describe your current sleep disorder treatment.
* **Sleep Disorder Outlook** (sleep_disorder_outlook): Select one option to describe your outlook on your sleep disorder (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
* **Sleep Disorder Recommendations** (sleep_disorder_recommendations): A field where you can describe any recommendations you have for treating your sleep disorder.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough when filling out the form.
* Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any questions, please ask for clarification or assistance.
