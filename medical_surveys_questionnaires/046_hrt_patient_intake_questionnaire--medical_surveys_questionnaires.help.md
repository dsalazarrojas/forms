# HRT Patient Intake Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients for the purpose of creating a comprehensive profile.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields with your personal information as prompted.
2. Double-check your answers for accuracy.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask for help if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Name** (patient_name, text, required/optional)
* A field to input the patient's name.

### 2. **Medical Record Number** (medical_record_number, text, required/optional)
* A field to input the patient's medical record number.

### 3. **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required/optional)
* A field to input the patient's date of birth.

### 4. **Medical Condition** (medical_condition, text, required/optional)
* A field to input the patient's medical condition(s) for which they are being treated.

### 5. **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, required/optional)
* A field to input the patient's emergency contact phone number.

### 6. **Contact E-mail** (contact_email, email, required/optional)
* A field to input the patient's emergency contact email address.

### 7. **Medical Specialty** (medical_specialty, text, required/optional)
* A field to input the specialty of the treating physician.

### 8. **Current Medications** (current_medications, text, required/optional)
* A field to input the patient's current medications.

### 9. **Medical History** (medical_history, text, required/optional)
* A field to input the patient's medical history.
