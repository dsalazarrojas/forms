# Auditory System Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auditory System Assessment form is designed to gather information about your auditory system. This form will help the audiology team better understand your hearing concerns and create a personalized plan for your evaluation and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, as each section is important for a comprehensive evaluation.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*). These fields are essential for the audiology team to understand your concerns.
3. For each field, provide as much detail as possible, as this will help the team better understand your situation.
4. If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or assistance from the audiology team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Assessment Date \* 
* Assessment Date: This field is required and indicates the date of the evaluation. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 2. Patient Name \* 
* Patient Name: This field is required and represents your full name.

### 3. Date of Birth \* 
* Date of Birth: This field is required and indicates your birth date.

### 4. Email Address \* 
* Email Address: This field is required and should be a valid email address where the audiology team can contact you.

### 5. Phone Number \* 
* Phone Number: This field is required and should be a valid phone number where the audiology team can contact you.

### 6. Referred By 
* Referred By: This field is optional and represents who referred you to the audiology department.

### 7. Primary Hearing Concern 
* Primary Hearing Concern: This field is required and helps the audiology team understand the main reason for your visit.

### 8. Duration of Hearing Difficulty \* 
* Duration of Hearing Difficulty: This field is required and indicates how long you've experienced your hearing concern.

### 9. Side of Hearing Loss \* 
* Side of Hearing Loss: This field is required and represents where you notice the hearing problem.

### 10. Perceived Severity 
* Perceived Severity: This field is required and indicates how significant you perceive the hearing loss.

### 11. Difficulty Understanding Speech 
* Difficulty Understanding Speech: This field is required and represents how much trouble you have with conversation.

### 12. Difficulty in Noisy Environments 
* Difficulty in Noisy Environments: This field is required and indicates how much you struggle with background noise.

### 13. Tinnitus 
* Tinnitus: This field is required and represents whether you experience ringing or buzzing in your ears.

### 14. Type of Tinnitus 
* Type of Tinnitus: This field is optional and represents the type of sound you experience.

### 15. Vertigo or Dizziness Episodes 
* Vertigo or Dizziness Episodes: This field is required and indicates whether you experience spinning sensations.

### 16. Ear Drainage or Discharge 
* Ear Drainage or Discharge: This field is required and represents whether you experience fluid or discharge.

### 17. Recent Infections 
* Recent Infections: This field is required and indicates whether you've had recent ear infections.

### 18. Occupational Noise Exposure 
* Occupational Noise Exposure: This field is required and represents your work environment noise level.

### 19. Recreational Noise Exposure 
* Recreational Noise Exposure: This field is optional and represents your exposure to loud music, firearms, and other sources of noise.

### 20. Use Hearing Protection 
* Use Hearing Protection: This field is optional and represents whether you use hearing protection.

### 21. Family History of Hearing Loss 
* Family History of Hearing Loss: This field is required and represents whether family members have hearing loss.

### 22. Prior Hearing Test 
* Prior Hearing Test: This field is required and indicates whether you've had a previous hearing evaluation.

### 23. Prior Hearing Aid Experience 
* Prior Hearing Aid Experience: This field is required and represents whether you've worn hearing aids.

### 24. Relevant Medical Conditions 
* Relevant Medical Conditions: This field is optional and represents any relevant medical conditions that may affect your hearing.

### 25. Current Medications 
* Current Medications: This field is optional and represents any medications you're taking that may impact your hearing.
