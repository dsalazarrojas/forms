# Aviation Medical Hypertension Evaluation Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aviation Medical Hypertension Evaluation Worksheet is designed to collect patient information and data for the evaluation and management of hypertension. This form helps healthcare professionals assess the patient's medical history, current medications, and diagnostic studies to make an informed decision for FAA certification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your full name and date of birth.
2. Provide your examination date.
3. Enter the name of your Aviation Medical Examiner (AME).
4. Enter your blood pressure readings, if available.
5. Answer questions about your medical history, including any previous diagnoses of hypertension.
6. List any current medications and their dosages.
7. Report any side effects from your medications.
8. Select any diagnostic studies you have completed.
9. Enter your physician's assessment notes.
10. Select your FAA disposition recommendation.
11. Sign and date this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Full Name (id: 2)
* **Patient Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your medical records.

### Date of Birth (id: 3)
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.

### Date of Examination (id: 4)
* **Date of Examination** (date, required): Enter the date of your medical examination.

### Aviation Medical Examiner (AME) Name (id: 5)
* **Aviation Medical Examiner (AME) Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your examining physician.

### Blood Pressure - Reading 1 (id: 7)
* **Blood Pressure - Reading 1** (text, required): Enter your blood pressure reading in MM/DD format (e.g., 120/80).

### History of Hypertension (id: 9)
* **History of Hypertension** (select_one, required): Answer "Yes, Previously diagnosed" if you have been diagnosed with hypertension in the past.

### Medications Prescribed (id: 11)
* **Medications Prescribed** (text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking, including dosage information.

### Side Effects Reported (id: 12)
* **Side Effects Reported** (select_one, required): Report any side effects from your medications, selecting from: No side effects, Mild (dizziness, fatigue), Moderate (affecting daily life), or Severe (immediate attention needed).

### Diagnostic Studies Completed (id: 13)
* **Diagnostic Studies Completed** (select_multiple, optional): Select any studies you have completed, including Electrocardiogram (EKG), Echocardiogram, Renal Function Tests (Labs), Fasting Blood Sugar, or Lipid Profile.

### Physician Assessment Notes (id: 14)
* **Physician Assessment Notes** (text, required): Enter your physician's overall clinical impression.

### FAA Disposition Recommendation (id: 15)
* **FAA Disposition Recommendation** (select_one, required): Select your FAA disposition recommendation from: Issue Certificate, Defer to FAA Oklahoma City, or Deny Certificate.

### Patient Digital Signature (id: 16)
* **Patient Digital Signature** (text, required): Enter your full name as a signature.

### Examiner Digital Signature (id: 17)
* **Examiner Digital Signature** (text, required): Enter your full name as a digital signature.

## Tips

*   Please fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
*   Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
*   If you are unsure about any question, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
*   This form is for informational purposes only and should not be considered a substitute for professional medical advice.
*   The information gathered from this form will be used to determine your fitness for flight certification.
