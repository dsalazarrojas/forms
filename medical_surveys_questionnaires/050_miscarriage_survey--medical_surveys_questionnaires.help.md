# Miscarriage Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Miscarriage Survey is a collection of questions designed to gather information from medical staff about their experience and performance in performing a miscarriage operation. The goal of this survey is to collect data on their skills, experience, and performance in this delicate and critical procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. Choose the correct answer option for each question.
3. Fill in any text fields as prompted.
