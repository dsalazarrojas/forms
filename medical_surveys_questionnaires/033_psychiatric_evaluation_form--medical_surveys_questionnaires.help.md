# Psychiatric Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Psychiatric Evaluation Form is designed to collect information from people who know the patient, such as family members or caregivers, about their relationship and feelings towards the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions in the order they appear.
2. For questions with options, select all that apply.
3. If a question is not applicable, please leave it blank or indicate "N/A" in the answer field.
4. Please provide as much detail as possible in the answer fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How long have you known the patient?** (question_1, text, optional): Please describe how long you have known the patient, including the duration of your relationship in months or years.
* **What is your role in the patient's life?** (question_2, text, optional): Choose the role you play in the patient's life, such as family member, caregiver, or friend.
* **How do you feel about the patient?** (question_3, select_multiple, optional): Select all the feelings you experience towards the patient, such as Good, Bad, or Okay.
* **When did you start feeling this way?** (question_4, date, optional): Enter the date when you started feeling the way you do about the patient.
* **What is your specialty?** (question_5, select_one, optional): Choose the specialty you have regarding the patient, such as Psychiatrist, Therapist, or Other.)
