# COVID 19 Screening Questionnaire For Dental Patients - Help Guide
## Purpose
This screening form is used to evaluate patients for potential COVID-19 exposure or symptoms before their dental visit. Patients must answer all questions to the best of their ability, as their answers will help the healthcare provider determine the best course of action for their safety during the visit.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please fill out all required fields.
* Select the correct answer for each question, based on your current situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name**: The full name of the patient who will be receiving dental treatment.
* **Scheduled Dental Procedure**: Briefly describe the dental procedure you are scheduled for or have already visited for.
* **Date of Last Dental Visit**: Enter the date of your last dental visit.
* **Do you have a fever or have you felt hot or feverish in the last 24 hours?**: Answer this question based on your current symptoms. If you have a fever or feel hot or feverish, please answer 'True'.
* **Have you taken any fever-reducing medications today?**: If you have taken any medication to reduce your fever, please answer 'True'. If you have not taken any such medication, answer 'False'.
* **Have you noticed a new loss of taste or smell?**: Answer this question based on your current symptoms. If you have noticed a new loss of taste or smell, please answer 'True'.
* **Are you having any difficulty breathing or shortness of breath?**: Answer this question based on your current symptoms. If you are experiencing difficulty breathing or shortness of breath, please answer 'True'.
* **Have you been in close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case in 14 days?**: Answer this question based on your recent interactions. If you have been in contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case, please answer 'True'.
* **Describe any recent travel outside your local area**: If you have traveled outside your local area recently, please provide details about your trip.
* **Do you work in a healthcare setting?**: Answer this question about your profession. If you work in a healthcare setting, please answer 'True'.
* **I agree to use a pre-procedural antimicrobial mouthrinse**: Please answer this question by selecting 'True' or 'I agree' if you agree to use a pre-procedural antimicrobial mouthrinse.
* **I agree to follow all dental office safety protocols during my visit**: Please answer this question by selecting 'True' or 'I agree' if you agree to follow all dental office safety protocols during your visit.
* **I certify my answers are accurate to the best of my knowledge**: Please answer this question by selecting 'True' or 'False'. This statement confirms that your answers are accurate and based on your knowledge.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* If you are unsure about any question, please inform your healthcare provider.
* If you answer 'True' to any question, you may need to take additional steps to ensure your safety and the safety of others during your visit.
