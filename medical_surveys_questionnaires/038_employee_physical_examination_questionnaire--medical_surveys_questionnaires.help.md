# Employee Physical Examination Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Physical Examination Questionnaire is a form used to collect personal and medical information about employees. This information is necessary for the organization to ensure a safe and healthy work environment and to provide necessary support and accommodations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email address as they appear on your official government-issued ID or as registered with the organization.
3. Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
4. Provide your Date of Birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
5. Enter your Address as it appears on your official government-issued ID or as registered with the organization.
6. Select any known medical conditions from the provided options.
7. Enter the date of the last Physical Exam in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
8. Enter your Height in inches.
9. Enter your Weight in pounds.
10. If applicable, list any allergies you may have.
