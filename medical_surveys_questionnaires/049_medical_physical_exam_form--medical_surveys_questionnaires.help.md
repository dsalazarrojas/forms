# Medical Physical Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive information from patients or individuals for medical evaluation and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form sections: General Information, Patient Information, Medical History, Medications, Allergies, Past Medical History, Systems Review, and Physical Exam.
* Answer the questions truthfully and accurately according to your medical condition.
* Fill in all the required fields (indicated by a `*` symbol).
* If you are unsure about any question or section, consult with a medical professional for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): This section is used to collect general information such as your name, date of birth, or any other demographic details.
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional): This section is used to collect information about the patient, such as their age, sex, or contact details.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses or medical conditions.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, optional): Check the medications you are currently taking, including any prescription or over-the-counter medications.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional): Check any allergies you may have, including food, medication, or environmental allergens.
* **Past Medical History** (`past_medical`, text, optional): Describe any previous medical conditions or procedures you have undergone.
* **Systems Review** (`systems_review`, text, optional): Report any problems or issues with your body systems, such as cardiovascular, respiratory, or nervous systems.
* **Physical Exam** (`physical_exam`, text, optional): Describe your physical condition, including any injuries or abnormalities.

## Tips
* Always answer questions truthfully and accurately according to your medical condition.
* If you are unsure about any question or section, consult with a medical professional for guidance.
* This form is for medical evaluation and treatment purposes only; do not use it for any other purpose.
