# Water Soluble Vitamins Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Soluble Vitamins Assessment is a form designed to evaluate an individual's intake and experience with water-soluble vitamins, including their frequency of use, dosage, and potential side effects. This form is intended to gather information on the use and effects of water-soluble vitamins, which can help medical professionals make informed decisions about treatment and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category of use for water-soluble vitamins from the options provided.
2. Indicate how often you take water-soluble vitamins, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
3. Choose the dosage level of water-soluble vitamins you are taking, if applicable.
4. Specify the time of day you take water-soluble vitamins.
5. Describe your reason for using water-soluble vitamins.
6. List any side effects you have experienced, such as nausea, dizziness, or fatigue.
7. Indicate if you have experienced any related symptoms, such as muscle weakness or headaches.
8. Confirm if you use water-soluble vitamins supplements.
9. Provide any additional symptoms or experiences you have had with water-soluble vitamins.
10. Add any additional comments or information you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (Field 1):
	+ This field allows you to select the category of use for water-soluble vitamins.
	+ Please choose from the options: Vitamin supplements, Other.
* **Frequency** (Field 2):
	+ This field asks how often you take water-soluble vitamins.
	+ Select from: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Never.
* **Dosage** (Field 3):
	+ This field is for indicating the dosage level of water-soluble vitamins you are taking.
	+ Choose from: Less than 50 mg, 50-100 mg, 100-200 mg, More than 200 mg.
* **Time** (Field 4):
	+ This field asks at what time of day you take water-soluble vitamins.
	+ Please enter the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00, 12:00, 14:00).
* **Reason for use** (Field 6):
	+ This field is for describing your reason for using water-soluble vitamins.
	+ Please provide a short explanation.
* **Side effects** (Field 7):
	+ This field asks if you have experienced any side effects with water-soluble vitamins.
	+ Select all that apply: Nausea, Dizziness, Fatigue.
* **Related symptoms** (Field 8):
	+ This field is for indicating if you have experienced any related symptoms.
	+ Select all that apply: Fatigue, Muscle weakness, Headache.
* **Supplement use** (Field 9):
	+ This field asks if you use water-soluble vitamins supplements.
	+ Please confirm: Yes, No.
* **Additional symptoms** (Field 10):
	+ This field is for listing any additional symptoms or experiences you have had with water-soluble vitamins.
	+ Please provide a brief description.
* **Additional comments** (Field 11):
	+ This field is for adding any additional comments or information about your use of water-soluble vitamins.
	+ Please type freely.
