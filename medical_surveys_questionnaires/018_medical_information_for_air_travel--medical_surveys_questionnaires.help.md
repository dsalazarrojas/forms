# Medical Information For Air Travel - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical information from passengers for air travel purposes. It will help ensure your safety and well-being during flights. Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Passenger Name" field with your full name as it appears on your passport or ID.
2. Enter your "Date of Birth" in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
3. Select your "Nationality" from the list of options provided.
4. If you have any medical conditions, describe them in the "Medical Condition" field.
5. If necessary, provide additional details about your medical condition in the "Medical Condition Details" field.
6. Indicate which travel documents you have (e.g., passport, visa).
7. If required, select the relevant medical clearance options.
8. Provide your signature in the "Consent" field to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
9. Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger Name** (text): Enter your full name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
* **Nationality** (text): Select your nationality from the list of options provided.
* **Medical Condition** (text): If you have any medical conditions, describe them here.
* **Medical Condition Details** (text): If necessary, provide additional details about your medical condition.
* **Travel Documents** (select multiple): Indicate which travel documents you have (e.g., passport, visa).
* **Medical Clearance** (select multiple): If required, select the relevant medical clearance options.
* **Consent** (text): Provide your signature in the format "Signature: ____________________".
* **Emergency Contact** (text): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure your safety and well-being during flights.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the support team.
* Ensure that you have the necessary travel documents before embarking on your flight.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you have any medical conditions, please provide detailed information to ensure your safety during flights.
