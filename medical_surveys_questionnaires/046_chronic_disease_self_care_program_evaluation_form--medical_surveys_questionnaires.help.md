The Chronic Disease Self-Care Program Evaluation Form is used to assess the effectiveness of various chronic disease self-care programs. This form is essential for healthcare providers to understand the impact of these programs on participant outcomes, satisfaction, and feedback. Please note that this form is designed to be completed after participating in a program, and the answers provided should reflect on the participant's experiences and perceptions after completing the program.

Before starting this form, please check that you have not already submitted an evaluation form. If you have, please contact your program administrator or facilitator for further assistance.

This form consists of several sections, each addressing a specific aspect of the participant's experience. Completing this form will help us gather valuable information on your program's effectiveness, areas for improvement, and participant satisfaction.

Please complete this form thoroughly and accurately to provide the best insights for future program development and improvement.

**How To Complete This Form**

* Fill in your full name and email address (if available) to identify yourself.
* Select the program you participated in from the list.
* Choose the duration of your program participation.
* Indicate your completion status of the program.
* Select your primary chronic condition.
* Rate your self-care confidence before and after the program.
* Select all the lifestyle changes you made during the program.
* Rate the improvement in symptom management, medication adherence, and quality of life.
* Rate the overall satisfaction with the program content, facilitator, and materials.
* Identify the most and least helpful components of the program.
* Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
* Rate your overall satisfaction with the program and indicate if you would recommend it.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **1. Full Name:** Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **2. Email Address:** If you have an email address, please enter it for follow-up communication.
* **3. Participant ID:** If you have a participant ID, please enter it. This is for tracking purposes only.
* **4. Program Name:** Select the program you participated in from the list.
* **5. Program Duration:** Choose the duration of your program participation.
* **6. Completion Status:** Indicate if you completed the program as scheduled.
* **7. Chronic Condition:** Select your primary chronic condition.
* **8. Self-Care Confidence Before:** Rate your self-care confidence before participating in the program.
* **9. Self-Care Confidence After:** Rate your self-care confidence after participating in the program.
* **10. Symptom Management Improvement:** Rate the improvement in symptom management.
* **11. Medication Adherence Improvement:** Rate the improvement in medication adherence.
* **12. Lifestyle Changes Made:** Select all the lifestyle changes you made during the program.
* **13. Quality of Life Rating:** Rate your overall quality of life.
* **14. Quality of Life Change:** Rate the change in your quality of life.
* **15. Healthcare Utilization Change:** Rate the change in your healthcare utilization.
* **16. Program Content Rating:** Rate the program content.
* **17. Facilitator Rating:** Rate the facilitator's performance.
* **18. Materials Quality:** Rate the quality of program materials.
* **19. Most Helpful Component:** Describe the most helpful component of the program.
* **20. Least Helpful Component:** Describe the least helpful component of the program.
* **21. Overall Satisfaction:** Rate your overall satisfaction with the program.
* **22. Would You Recommend:** Indicate if you would recommend the program.
* **23. Continue Practices:** Indicate if you will continue the practices you learned.
* **24. Suggestions for Improvement:** Provide any suggestions for program improvement.
* **25. Additional Comments:** Share any additional comments or feedback.

**Tips**

* Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* Select all that apply for lifestyle changes.
* Rate your answers based on your personal experiences and feelings.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" section.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your program administrator or facilitator.

By completing this form, you will be contributing to the ongoing improvement and development of our chronic disease self-care programs. Thank you for your time and effort!
