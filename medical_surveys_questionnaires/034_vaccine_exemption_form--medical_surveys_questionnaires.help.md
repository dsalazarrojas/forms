# Vaccine Exemption Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vaccine Exemption Form is used to collect information for vaccine exemptions. You will need to provide details about the person exempting from vaccination and the exemption itself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
2. Provide the reason for the exemption in the "Reason for Exemption" field.
3. Select the medical condition that applies to the exemption from the "Medical Condition" field.
4. If required, provide the doctor's name and signoff, as well as their license number.
5. Provide the child's name (if applicable).
6. Enter the birthdate of the child (if applicable).
7. Provide the name of the vaccine that the exemption applies to.
8. Select the type of vaccine that the exemption applies to.
9. Enter the date of the exemption.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the person providing the exemption.
* **Reason for Exemption** (2, text, required): Provide a clear and concise explanation for the exemption.
* **Medical Condition** (3, select_one, optional): Select the medical condition that applies to the exemption.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Doctor Signoff** (4, text, optional): If required, provide the doctor's signoff.
* **Doctor Name** (5, text, required): Enter the name of the doctor providing the exemption.
* **Doctor License** (6, text, required): Enter the doctor's license number.
* **Child Name** (7, text, optional): Enter the name of the child for whom the exemption applies.
* **Birthdate** (8, date, optional): Enter the birthdate of the child (if applicable).
* **Vaccine Name** (9, text, optional): Provide the name of the vaccine that the exemption applies to.
* **Vaccine Type** (10, text, required): Select the type of vaccine that the exemption applies to.
* **Exemption Date** (11, date, required): Enter the date of the exemption.
