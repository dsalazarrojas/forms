# Dental Patient Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from patients on their experience with a dental practice. The information collected will help the practice to identify areas for improvement and provide better services to its patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field based on your experience.
3. Enter your email and phone number, if applicable, to help the practice contact you for further information or to follow up on your feedback.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): This is your name as recorded in our system.
* **Practice Name** (`practice_name`, text, optional): This is the name of the dental practice you visited.
* **Experienced with Dentist** (`experience_with_dentist`, select multiple, optional): Choose all of the dentists you have experienced with during your visit:
	+ Dentist A
	+ Dentist B
	+ Dentist C
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): The time of your appointment.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select one, optional): Rate your overall experience:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Schedule Convenience** (`schedule_convenience`, select one, optional): How convenient was your schedule for your appointment?
	+ Always
	+ Mostly
	+ Sometimes
	+ Never
* **Staff Friendly** (`office_staff_friendly`, select multiple, optional): How would you rate the staff's friendliness:
	+ Very
	+ Somewhat
	+ Not very
	+ Not at all
* **Dental Services Offered** (`dental_services_offered`, select multiple, optional): Rate your experience with the dental services offered:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select multiple, optional): Would you recommend this practice?
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Undecided
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you would like to be contacted by the practice for further information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted by the practice for further information.
* **Date of Submission** (`submitted`, date, optional): The date of submission of this form.
* **Practice Location** (`practice_location`, text, optional): The location of the dental practice you visited.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, optional): The name of the staff member you interacted with during your visit.
* **Practice Name** (`practice_name`, text, optional): The name of the dental practice you visited.
* **Dentist Name** (`dentist_name`, text, optional): The name of the dentist you visited.
* **Practice Address** (`practice_address`, text, optional): The address of the dental practice you visited.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): The date of your appointment.
* **Practice Telephone** (`practice_telephone`, text, optional): The telephone number of the dental practice you visited.
* **Practice Email** (`practice_email`, email, optional): The email address of the dental practice you visited.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`additional_comments_2`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them here.
* **Practice URL** (`practice_url`, text, optional): The URL of the dental practice you visited.
* **Practice Rating** (`practice_rating`, select one, optional): Rate your experience with the practice:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5
	+ 6
	+ 7
	+ 8
	+ 9
* **Practice Score** (`practice_score`, number, optional): A numerical score for your experience with the practice.

## Tips

* Please take your time to answer each question carefully and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact the practice for clarification.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please do not hesitate to share them.
