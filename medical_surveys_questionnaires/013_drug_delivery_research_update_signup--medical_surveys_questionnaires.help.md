# Drug Delivery Research Update Signup - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Drug Delivery Research Update Signup form is designed to allow researchers and organizations to sign up for updates on drug delivery research. This form helps us keep you informed about new developments, breakthroughs, and important updates in the field. Please fill out this form to receive timely and relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your category from the "category" field. This will help us tailor the updates to your specific needs and interests. You can choose from:
	* Research Team: If you are part of a research team, select this option.
	* Professional Organization: If you represent a professional organization, select this option.
	* Lab: If you are a lab, select this option.
2. Enter your description in the "description" field. This is an optional field for you to provide more information about your interest in drug delivery research. Please keep it brief.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This is the email address we will use to send you updates.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field. This is an optional field for you to provide your phone number for updates. However, please note that we will not use your phone number for any other purpose.
5. Confirm your email address by entering it again in the "confirm_email" field.
6. Confirm your phone number by entering it again in the "confirm_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select your category to help us tailor the updates to your specific needs and interests.
* **description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your interest in drug delivery research. This will help us get a better understanding of your requirements.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for updates on drug delivery research. We will use this email address to send you relevant and timely information.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for updates on drug delivery research. We will not use this number for any other purpose.
* **confirm_email** (`confirm_email`, text, optional): Confirm your email address to ensure we get it right.
* **confirm_phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, optional): Confirm your phone number to ensure we get it right.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter your email address correctly to receive updates.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* We will only use the information you provide for the purpose of sending you updates on drug delivery research.
