# Gym Health Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Health Questionnaire Form is designed to collect information about a person's health and medical history for the purposes of evaluating their suitability for a gym membership or participation in a fitness program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully to ensure you understand the questions and answers required.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately to the best of your ability.
3. Be as detailed as possible when providing information about your medical conditions, medications, and emergency contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (Note): This page provides a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.

### Page 1: Health Information
* **Height** (Number): Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (Number): Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Date of Birth** (Date): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.

### Medical History
* **Medical Conditions** (Text): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, or a heart condition, please list them here.
* **Medical Conditions Other** (Text): If you have any other medical conditions not listed above, please provide a detailed description.
* **Medication** (Text): If you are currently taking any medications, please list them here.
* **Allergies** (Text): If you have any allergies, please list them here.

### Emergency Contact Information
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text): Enter the name of the person you would like to have contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the person you would like to have contact in case of an emergency.

### Tips
* Please take your time and answer each question carefully to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions or have concerns about your health, please do not hesitate to contact the relevant medical professionals.
* This form is not a substitute for a comprehensive medical evaluation, and if you have any underlying medical conditions, please consult a medical professional before starting a new exercise program.
