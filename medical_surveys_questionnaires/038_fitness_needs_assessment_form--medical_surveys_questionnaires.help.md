# Fitness Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your personal and health-related needs to help you get the most out of our fitness program. It's essential to provide accurate and honest information to ensure that our trainers can tailor a program that suits your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the following sections as accurately as possible:
	* Personal information (pages 1-2)
	* Health status (pages 3-5)
	* Fitness goals (page 6)
	* Physical characteristics (pages 7-9)
2. Be sure to select the correct options for your medical conditions (page 11), medical history (page 12), allergies (page 13), surgeries (page 14), and implants (page 15).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal information (page 1: submitter_information)**: Enter your full name and any other relevant personal details.
* **Contact (page 2: contact_details)**: Provide your email address and phone number so we can contact you for further assistance.
* **Health (page 3: medical_details)**: Briefly describe your current health status, including any medical conditions, treatments, or concerns.
* **Fitness Goals (page 4: fitness_goals)**: Outline your short-term and long-term fitness goals.
* **Height (page 5: physical_details)**: Enter your height in inches.
* **Weight (page 6: weight)**: Enter your weight in pounds.
* **inches (page 7: height)**: Enter your height in inches.
* **Age (page 8: age)**: Enter your age.
* **Email (page 9: email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (page 10: phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Medical conditions (page 11: medical_conditions)**: Select any medical conditions that apply to you, such as diabetes or hypertension.
* **Medical history (page 12: medical_history)**: Select any medical conditions that have been previously diagnosed or treated.
* **Allergies (page 13: medical_allergies)**: Select any allergies you have.
* **Surgeries (page 14: medical_surgeries)**: Select any surgeries you have undergone.
* **Implants (page 15: medical_implants)**: Select any implants you have.

Please take the time to fill out this form accurately and honestly. The information provided will help our trainers create a personalized fitness program tailored to your needs.
