# Healthcare Policy Impact Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the level of awareness and impact of healthcare policy changes on individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question carefully and honestly.
- You can select multiple answers for questions that allow multiple selections.
- For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you need to answer the question.
- If you need help or have questions, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
## Page 1: Introduction
* **Introduction**: This page is a brief introduction to the survey, and you do not need to answer this question.
 
## Page 2: Awareness Level
* **What is your level of awareness about healthcare policy changes?**: This question is to gauge your awareness of recent healthcare policy updates. Select the level of awareness that best describes your understanding of these changes:
  * Very High
  * High
  * Medium
  * Low
  * Not at all

## Page 3: Impact Level
* **How do you think healthcare policy changes impact your life?**: This question is to understand how you perceive the impact of policy updates on your daily life:
  * Extremely
  * Somewhat
  * Not very much
  * Not at all

## Page 4: Policy Changes
* **Please list 3 recent healthcare policy changes that you are aware of**: This question is open-ended, please list three recent healthcare policy changes that you are aware of. Be as specific as possible.

## Page 5: Policy Concerns
* **Are there any policy changes that you are concerned about?**: This question is to gauge your concerns about healthcare policy updates. Select all that apply:
  * Yes
  * No

## Page 6: Policy Concerns Details
* **Please provide more details about your concerns**: This question is open-ended, please provide as much detail as you need to explain your concerns.

## Page 7: Healthcare Professionals
* **How often do you consult with healthcare professionals about policy changes?**: This question is to understand your interaction with healthcare professionals:
  * Always
  * Sometimes
  * Rarely
  * Never

## Page 8: Healthcare Professionals Details
* **Please provide more details about your interactions with healthcare professionals**: This question is open-ended, please provide as much detail as you need to explain your interactions.

## Page 9: Community Resources
* **How often do you use community resources for policy information?**: This question is to understand your use of community resources:
  * Always
  * Sometimes
  * Rarely
  * Never

## Page 10: Community Resources Details
* **Please provide more details about the resources you use**: This question is open-ended, please provide as much detail as you need to explain your use of resources.
