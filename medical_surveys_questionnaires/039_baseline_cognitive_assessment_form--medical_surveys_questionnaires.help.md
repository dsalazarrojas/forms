# Baseline Cognitive Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Baseline Cognitive Assessment Form is designed to collect relevant information about a patient's cognitive functions, medical history, and other essential details. It is intended to be completed by a clinician or healthcare professional during the initial assessment of a patient. This form helps to identify potential cognitive impairments, medical conditions, and other factors that may impact the patient's treatment and care. By providing accurate and comprehensive information, this form assists healthcare professionals in making informed decisions and tailoring interventions to the patient's specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a clear understanding of the patient's purpose for the assessment and the specific circumstances surrounding the evaluation.
2. Complete each section as instructed, using the provided guidance for each field.
3. Be thorough and honest when answering questions, and provide as much detail as necessary.
4. Verify the patient's understanding of the form's purpose and the questions asked.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name** (1): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of Birth** (2): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (3): Select the patient's gender from the provided options.
* **Email Address** (4): Enter the patient's email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (5): Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assessment Date** (6): Enter the date of the assessment, which should be today's date.
* **Clinician Name** (7): Enter your name as the administering clinician.
* **Education Level** (8): Select the patient's highest level of education completed.
* **Primary Language** (9): Select the patient's first language.
* **Handedness** (10): Select the patient's dominant hand.
* **Reason for Assessment** (11): Select the reason for the assessment from the provided options.
* **Relevant Medical History** (12): Enter any relevant medical conditions that may impact the patient's cognitive function.
* **Current Medications** (13): List all medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Sleep Quality Last Night** (14): Rate how well the patient slept last night.
* **Caffeine Intake Today** (15): Rate the amount of caffeine consumed by the patient today.
* **Current Mood Rating** (16): Rate the patient's current mood.
* **Current Pain Level** (17): Rate the patient's current pain level from 0 to 10.
* **Vision Corrected** (18): Indicate if the patient is wearing glasses or contacts.
* **Using Hearing Aid** (19): Indicate if the patient is using a hearing aid.
* **Test Environment** (20): Select where the test is administered.
* **Distractions Present** (21): Select the level of environmental distractions present.
* **Assistance Needed** (22): Indicate the level of assistance the patient needs.
* **Informed Consent** (23): Indicate if the patient consents to the assessment.
* **Understanding Confirmed** (24): Verify that the patient understands the form's purpose.
* **Patient Signature** (25): Have the patient sign their name.
* **Clinician Signature** (26): Sign your name as the administering clinician.
* **Assessment Completed** (27): Confirm that the assessment is finished.
* **Clinician Notes** (28): Enter any observations or notes regarding the assessment.
