# Workplace Safety And Concerns Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Safety And Concerns Form is a tool used to gather specific information about your safety and concerns at work. Please fill out this form to provide the most accurate and detailed answers to the questions it asks.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1. Review each question carefully and choose the most accurate options for your current situation.
2. For questions with multiple-choice options, select as many options as apply to your situation.
3. For questions that ask for a specific piece of information, provide as much detail as you can.
4. Make sure to answer all questions that are marked as "required" to ensure that your submission is complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This is a general introduction to the form. It explains the purpose and context of the questions that follow.
* **Employee Id** (`2`, `number`, required: false): This field is not currently used by this form, so you can ignore it.
* **Have you experienced any of the following symptoms?** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all the symptoms that you are experiencing. Symptoms include:
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Fatigue
* **What type of symptoms do you experience?** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of symptoms you are experiencing:
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Fatigue
	+ Other (please specify)
* **What symptoms are you experiencing?** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Provide as much detail as possible about the symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Have you had any contact with someone who has COVID-19?** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Select:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Name of contact** (`7`, `text`, required: false): If you answer "true" to the previous question, provide the name of the person you had contact with.
* **Do you feel safe at work?** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false): Select:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Concerns** (`9`, `text`, required: false): If you have any concerns that you would like to share, type them here.
* **Would you like to report anything else?** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all the things you would like to report:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Additional comments** (`11`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or information that you would like to share, type them here.
* **Do you want to be contacted for further assistance?** (`12`, `select_one`, required: false): Select:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Phone number to contact** (`13`, `text`, required: false): If you selected "true" to the previous question, provide your phone number.
* **Email address to contact** (`14`, `email`, required: false): If you selected "true" to the previous question, provide your email address.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough when answering each question.
* You can skip any questions that do not apply to your situation.
* If you are unsure about any question, try your best to answer it according to your understanding.
* This form is for your safety and well-being, so please take the time to complete it carefully.
