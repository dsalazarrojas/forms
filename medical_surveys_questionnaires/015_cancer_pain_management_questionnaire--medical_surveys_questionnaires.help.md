# Cancer Pain Management Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Cancer Pain Management Questionnaire is a comprehensive tool designed to assess and manage cancer patients' pain experiences. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose, how to complete it, and what each field means.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information** (`patient_info_section`, note, not required): This section is not required but provides a brief introduction to the form.
2. **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, required): Enter your unique patient ID from your healthcare provider.
4. **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Provide a contact number where you can be reached for further questions or updates.
5. **Date of Assessment** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are being assessed for your pain levels.
6. **Primary Diagnosis** (`primary_diagnosis`, text, required): Specify your specific type of cancer diagnosis.
7. **Current Treatment Stage** (`treatment_stage`, select_one, required): Choose the stage of your care journey:
	* **Newly Diagnosed**: Newly diagnosed with cancer.
	* **Active Treatment**: Currently undergoing treatment for cancer.
	* **Maintenance**: Maintaining cancer treatment.
	* **Remission**: Currently in remission.
	* **Palliative Care**: End-of-life care.
8. **Pain Assessment** (`pain_assessment_section`, note, not required): This section is not required but provides additional space for notes.
9. **Rate your pain at its worst** (`worst_pain_rating`, number, required): Rate your pain level from 0 (no pain) to 10 (worst pain).
10. **Rate your pain at its best** (`best_pain_rating`, number, required): Rate your pain level from 0 (no pain) to 10 (worst pain).
11. **Describe the pain sensation** (`pain_sensation_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply:
	* **Sharp**
	* **Dull**
	* **Burning**
	* **Aching**
	* **Shooting**
	* **Throbbing**
	* **Other**
12. **Location of the pain** (`pain_location`, text, required): List the areas of your body where you feel pain.
13. **How long has this pain been present** (`pain_duration`, select_one, required): Choose how long the pain has been present:
	* **Less than 1 week**
	* **1-4 weeks**
	* **1-6 months**
	* **Over 6 months**
14. **Pain Impact on Daily Life** (`life_impact_section`, note, not required): This section is not required but provides additional space for notes.
15. **Pain interference with sleep** (`sleep_interference`, select_one, required): Choose how pain affects your sleep:
	* **Not at all**
	* **Slightly**
	* **Moderately**
	* **Significantly**
	* **Completely**
16. **Pain interference with appetite** (`appetite_interference`, select_one, required): Choose how pain affects your appetite:
	* **Not at all**
	* **Slightly**
	* **Moderately**
	* **Significantly**
	* **Completely**
17. **Pain interference with mood** (`mood_interference`, select_one, required): Choose how pain affects your mood:
	* **Not at all**
	* **Slightly**
	* **Moderately**
	* **Significantly**
	* **Completely**
18. **Current medications for pain** (`current_medications`, text, not required): List your current medications for pain, including names and dosages if known.
19. **Side effects from current medications** (`medication_side_effects`, text, not required): Briefly describe any unpleasant effects from your current medications.
20. **Goals for pain management** (`management_goals`, text, not required): Specify your goals for pain management.
21. **Submission Date** (`final_submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submit this form.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when rating your pain levels.
* Choose the best category for the areas where you feel pain.
* Provide as much detail as possible for each field.
* If unsure about any field, ask for clarification or guidance.
