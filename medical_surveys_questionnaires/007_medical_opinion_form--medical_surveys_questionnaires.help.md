# medicalOpinionForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medicalOpinionForm is a tool designed to collect opinions and feedback from medical professionals, researchers, or patients on various medical topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the medicalOpinionForm, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions and ensure you have a good understanding of the medical context.
2. Click on the "Personal Information" tab to provide your contact information.
3. In the "Medical History" tab, describe your medical history, if any.
4. In the "Medical Details" tab, provide any additional medical information you wish to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Information
* **Personal Information (personalInformation**, text, required: false):** This field is for providing your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. Please ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information.

### Medical History
* **Medical History (medicalHistory, text, required: false):** This field is for describing your medical history, if any. Please provide any relevant medical conditions, treatments, or allergies you have.

### Medical Details
* **Medical Details (medicalDetails, text, required: false):** This field is for providing any additional medical information you wish to share. Please be as detailed as possible, but ensure your response is respectful and relevant to the medical topic.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Ensure your responses are respectful and adhere to medical ethics and protocols.
* If you need help or have questions, contact form@gic.mx.
