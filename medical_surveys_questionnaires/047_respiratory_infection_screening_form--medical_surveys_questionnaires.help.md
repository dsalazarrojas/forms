# Respiratory Infection Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to screen for respiratory infections in patients. It gathers information on patient demographics, symptoms, medical history, test results, and potential respiratory infection indicators. This form helps healthcare professionals to quickly and accurately assess patients' conditions and provide timely interventions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: "Respiratory Infection".
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the other fields.
3. Ensure all fields are correctly filled before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Respiratory Infection**
 (`respiratory_infection`, `text`, **required**): 
This field is for indicating whether the patient has a respiratory infection or not. Please provide a brief description of the patient's symptoms or test results related to respiratory infection. 
### 2. **Medical History**
 (`medical_history`, `text`, **optional**): 
This field is for recording the patient's medical history, including any past illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions. 
### 3. **Test Results**
 (`test_results`, `text`, **optional**): 
This field is for recording any relevant test results, such as lab test results or imaging studies. 
### 4. **Patient Symptoms**
 (`patient_symptoms`, `text`, **optional**): 
This field is for describing the patient's symptoms, including those related to respiratory infection. 
### 5. **Patient Info**
 (`patient_info`, `text`, **optional**): 
This field is for collecting patient demographics, including name, age, and contact information. 

## Tips
* Please fill in all fields correctly and accurately.
* If unsure about any field, consult a healthcare professional.
* This form is for screening purposes only and not for diagnosis.
* Please use this form as part of a comprehensive patient evaluation.
