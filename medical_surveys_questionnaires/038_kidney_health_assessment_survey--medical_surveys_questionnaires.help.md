# Kidney Health Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the health of kidney function and potential risks associated with kidney disease. It gathers information about the patient's medical condition, family history, lifestyle habits, and medical procedures. The survey helps healthcare professionals evaluate the patient's overall kidney health and identify potential risks or complications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the medical condition that best describes the patient's condition from the list of options in the "Medical Condition" field.
3. Provide any relevant information about the patient's medication usage in the "Medication" field.
4. Check all applicable family history items in the "Family History" field. This may include checking boxes for "Father", "Mother", "Siblings", or other family members.
5. Describe any symptoms the patient is experiencing in the "Symptoms" field.
6. Add any additional lifestyle details in the "Lifestyle Details" field.
7. Enter the date of the patient's last dialysis in the "Date of Last Dialysis" field.
8. Indicate how often the patient undergoes dialysis in the "Dialysis Frequency" field.
9. Report any recent blood tests in the "Blood Tests" field.
10. Select the type of medical procedure the patient has undergone in the "Medical Procedure" field.
11. Report the patient's kidney function in the "Kidney Function" field.
12. Check all applicable hypertension levels in the "Hypertension" field.
13. Provide any additional medical conditions in the "Other Conditions" field.
14. Indicate the patient's smoking status in the "Smoking Status" field.
15. Indicate the patient's alcohol consumption status in the "Alcohol Status" field.
16. Check all applicable exercise habits in the "Exercise Status" field.
17. Indicate the patient's diet status in the "Diet Status" field.
18. Check all applicable weight status options in the "Weight Status" field.
19. Indicate the patient's height status in the "Height Status" field.
20. Report the patient's height in the "Height" field.
21. Report the patient's weight in the "Weight" field.
22. Report the patient's waist circumference in the "Waist Circumference" field.
23. Report the patient's blood pressure in the "Blood Pressure" field.
24. Report the patient's blood sugar levels in the "Blood Sugar" field.
25. Add any additional medical notes in the "Other Medical Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Medical Condition** (select_one, required): Select the medical condition that best describes the patient's condition from the options provided:
	+ Diabetes Mellitus
	+ Chronic Kidney Disease
	+ Other Medical Condition
* **Medication** (text, optional): List any medications the patient is taking.
* **Family History** (select_multiple, required): Check all applicable family history items:
	+ Father
	+ Mother
	+ Siblings
* **Symptoms** (text, optional): Describe any symptoms the patient is experiencing.
* **Lifestyle Details** (note, optional): Add any additional lifestyle details that may be relevant to the patient's kidney health.
* **Date of Last Dialysis** (date, required): Enter the date of the patient's last dialysis.
* **Dialysis Frequency** (number, optional): Indicate how often the patient undergoes dialysis.
* **Blood Tests** (text, optional): Report any recent blood tests the patient has undergone.
* **Medical Procedure** (select_one, required): Select the type of medical procedure the patient has undergone:
	+ Hemoglobin
	+ Creatinine
	+ Urea
* **Kidney Function** (number, optional): Report the patient's kidney function.
* **Hypertension** (select_multiple, required): Check all applicable hypertension levels:
	+ Stage 1
	+ Stage 2
	+ Stage 3
* **Other Conditions** (text, optional): Report any additional medical conditions.
* **Smoking Status** (select_multiple, required): Indicate the patient's smoking status:
	+ Never
	+ Occasional
	+ Daily
* **Alcohol Status** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate the patient's alcohol consumption status:
	+ Never
	+ Occasional
	+ Daily
* **Exercise Status** (select_multiple, optional): Check all applicable exercise habits:
	+ Never
	+ Occasional
	+ Daily
* **Diet Status** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate the patient's diet status:
	+ Balanced
	+ Unbalanced
	+ Other
* **Weight Status** (select_multiple, optional): Check all applicable weight status options:
	+ Normal
	+ Overweight
	+ Obese
* **Height Status** (select_multiple, optional): Check all applicable height status options:
	+ Normal
	+ Underweight
	+ Abnormal
* **Height** (number, optional): Report the patient's height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (number, optional): Report the patient's weight in kilograms.
* **Waist Circumference** (number, optional): Report the patient's waist circumference in centimeters.
* **Blood Pressure** (number, optional): Report the patient's blood pressure.
* **Blood Sugar** (number, optional): Report the patient's blood sugar levels.
* **Other Medical Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional medical notes about the patient.
