# Medical Procedure Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Procedure Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback from patients or healthcare providers about their experiences with medical procedures. This feedback helps us improve the quality of care and services provided by the medical team.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by describing the procedure you underwent in the space provided in "What was the procedure for?"
- Then, rate your experience on a scale of 1-5 for each field, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
  - For "How would you rate the procedure?" think about the efficiency, effectiveness, and overall experience during the procedure.
  - For "How would you rate the medical team?" consider their communication, professionalism, and care during the procedure.
  - For "How would you rate communication during the procedure?" think about how well the medical team explained things to you, including instructions, risks, and next steps.
  - For "Overall satisfaction" rate your overall satisfaction with the procedure, taking into account all aspects of your experience.
- Finally, add any recommendations you may have for future improvements in the "Do you have any recommendations?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What was the procedure for?
* **What was the procedure for?** (`procedure_description`, text, required): A text field where you can describe the procedure you underwent, including any relevant details or context.
### How would you rate the procedure?
* **How would you rate the procedure?** (`procedure_rating`, number, required): Rate your experience with the procedure on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
### How would you rate the medical team?
* **How would you rate the medical team?** (`medical_team_rating`, number, required): Rate the professionalism, care, and communication of the medical team on a scale of 1-5.
### How would you rate communication during the procedure?
* **How would you rate communication during the procedure?** (`communication`, number, required): Rate the effectiveness of the communication during the procedure, including instructions, risks, and next steps.
### Overall satisfaction
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the procedure.
### Do you have any recommendations?
* **Do you have any recommendations?** (`recommendations`, text, required): Add any suggestions or comments you may have for future improvements to the procedure or services.
