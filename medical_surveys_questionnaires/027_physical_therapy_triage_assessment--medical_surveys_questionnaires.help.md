# Physical Therapy Triage Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Therapy Triage Assessment form is a tool used to gather essential information to help healthcare professionals assess patients' physical therapy needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about the patient in the "General Information" field.
2. Enter relevant patient information in the "Patient Info" field (optional).
3. Describe the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field (optional).
4. Assess the patient's condition using numbers or scores in the "Assessment" field (required).
5. Outline the proposed physical therapy plan in the "Physical Therapy Plan" field (optional).
6. Report the patient's expected outcome in the "Patient Outcome" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (required)** (`general_information`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's general information, such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Patient Info (optional)** (`patient_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional relevant information about the patient.
* **Medical History (optional)** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant conditions or treatments.
* **Assessment (required)** (`assessment`, `number`, required): Assess the patient's condition using numbers or scores to provide a quantitative measure of their physical function.
* **Physical Therapy Plan (optional)** (`physical_therapy_plan`, `text`, optional): Outline the proposed physical therapy plan, including exercises, stretches, and other recommended treatments.
* **Patient Outcome (optional)** (`patient_outcome`, `text`, optional): Report the expected outcome of the physical therapy plan, including anticipated improvements and challenges.
