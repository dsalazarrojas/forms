# Medical Equipment Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record information about the medical equipment, its condition, and its maintenance status. It helps healthcare professionals evaluate and monitor the equipment's compliance with safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Equipment Name in the text field.
2. Select the correct Equipment Type from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the Manufacturer of the equipment.
4. Record the Serial Number of the equipment.
5. Enter the Purchase Date of the equipment.
6. Enter the Purchase Price of the equipment.
7. Enter the Location where the equipment is currently stored or used.
8. Select the current Condition of the equipment from the dropdown list.
9. Select the Maintenance Status of the equipment from the dropdown list.
10. Choose the safety Standards that the equipment meets from the multiple selection list.
11. Enter your User Evaluation of the equipment (e.g., satisfactory, unsatisfactory).
12. Enter any additional Notes about the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the medical equipment being evaluated.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of medical equipment (e.g., surgical instrument, imaging equipment, etc.).
* **Manufacturer** (`manufacturer`, text, required): Enter the manufacturer of the medical equipment.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, required): Enter the serial number of the medical equipment.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required): Enter the date the medical equipment was purchased.
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, number, required): Enter the original purchase price of the medical equipment.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location where the medical equipment is currently stored or used.
* **Condition** (`condition`, select_one, required): Select the current condition of the medical equipment from the dropdown list (e.g., functional, non-functional, awaiting repair).
* **Maintenance Status** (`maintenance_status`, select_one, required): Select the current maintenance status of the medical equipment from the dropdown list (e.g., up to date, overdue, unknown).
* **Safety Standards Meets** (`safety_standards_meets`, select_multiple, required): Select which safety standards the medical equipment meets (e.g., ISO, FDA, etc.).
* **User Evaluation** (`user_evaluation`, text, required): Enter your evaluation of the medical equipment (e.g., satisfactory, unsatisfactory).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the medical equipment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments about the medical equipment.
