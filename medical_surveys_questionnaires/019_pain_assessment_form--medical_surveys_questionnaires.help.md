# Pain Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pain Assessment Form is a tool used to collect information about a patient's pain experience. It helps healthcare professionals assess the severity and frequency of a patient's pain, which is essential for providing effective treatment and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the body location where you are experiencing pain from the list below.
2. Rate the intensity of your pain for each body location using a scale of 0 to 10.
3. Indicate how frequently you feel pain using the options provided.
4. Report how long you feel pain using the options provided.
5. If you experience pain in any other locations, please select all relevant locations from the list.
6. Provide any additional information about your pain experience in the text field.
7. Finally, provide your name and the date you are submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Head** (`body_location`, required): Select the location where you are experiencing pain in your head.
* **Pain intensity in head** (`pain_intensity_head`, optional): Rate the intensity of your pain in your head using a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Pain intensity in face** (`pain_intensity_face`, optional): Rate the intensity of your pain in your face using a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Pain intensity in neck** (`pain_intensity_neck`, optional): Rate the intensity of your pain in your neck using a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Pain intensity in chest** (`pain_intensity_chest`, optional): Rate the intensity of your pain in your chest using a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Pain intensity in back** (`pain_intensity_back`, optional): Rate the intensity of your pain in your back using a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Pain intensity in abdomen** (`pain_intensity_abdomen`, optional): Rate the intensity of your pain in your abdomen using a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Pain intensity in pelvis** (`pain_intensity_pelvis`, optional): Rate the intensity of your pain in your pelvis using a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Pain intensity in genital** (`pain_intensity_genital`, optional): Rate the intensity of your pain in your genital area using a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Pain intensity in arms** (`pain_intensity_arms`, optional): Rate the intensity of your pain in your arms using a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Pain intensity in hips** (`pain_intensity_hips`, optional): Rate the intensity of your pain in your hips using a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Pain intensity in legs** (`pain_intensity_legs`, optional): Rate the intensity of your pain in your legs using a scale of 0 to 10.
* **How much pain do you feel?** (`pain_level`, required): Rate the overall intensity of your pain using a scale of 0 to 10.
* **How frequent is your pain?** (`pain_frequency`, required): Select the frequency of your pain using the options provided.
* **How long do you feel pain?** (`pain_level_duration`, required): Select the duration of your pain using the options provided.
* **Other location(s)** (`other_location`, optional): Select any other locations where you are experiencing pain.
* **Other** (`other`, optional): Provide any additional information about your pain experience.
* **Submitter** (`submitted_by`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Submission Date** (`submitted_date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting the form.
