# Preparticipation Physical Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preparticipation Physical Evaluation Form is a crucial document used by athletic teams, schools, and other organizations to assess the health and fitness of athletes before they begin practicing or competing. This form helps to identify potential health risks and ensure that athletes are ready for physical activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and thoroughly, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and date as the athlete.
2. Select the sport you are participating in (Baseball, Football, or Soccer).
3. Provide the date of your last medical exam, if applicable.
4. List any treatments or medical conditions you have received, if applicable.
5. List any medications you are taking, if applicable.
6. List any allergies, if applicable.
7. Describe any previous injuries, if applicable.
8. List any medical conditions you have, if applicable.
9. Describe your overall medical history, if applicable.
10. List your family medical history, if applicable.
11. Provide any relevant social media information, if applicable.
12. Answer the following questions:
	* Head
	* Neck
	* Arms
	* Chest
	* Back
	* Abdomen
	* Hips
	* Thighs
	* Knees
	* Feet
	* Hands
	* Fingers
	* Spine
	* Overall Body

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Name** (`athlete_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the athlete.
* **Sport** (`sport`, select one, optional): Please select the sport you are participating in (Baseball, Football, or Soccer).
* **Date of Last Medical Exam** (`date_of_last_medical_exam`, date, optional): If you have had a medical exam recently, please provide the date.
* **Date of Last Treatment** (`date_of_last_treatment`, date, optional): If you have received any medical treatment, please provide the date.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies you have.
* **Previous Injuries** (`previous_injuries`, text, optional): Describe any previous injuries.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions you have.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe your overall medical history.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, optional): List your family medical history.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, text, optional): Provide any relevant social media information.
* **Head** (`head`, text, optional): Describe any issues or concerns with your head.
* **Neck** (`neck`, text, optional): Describe any issues or concerns with your neck.
* **Arms** (`arms`, text, optional): Describe any issues or concerns with your arms.
* **Chest** (`chest`, text, optional): Describe any issues or concerns with your chest.
* **Back** (`back`, text, optional): Describe any issues or concerns with your back.
* **Abdomen** (`abdomen`, text, optional): Describe any issues or concerns with your abdomen.
* **Hips** (`hips`, text, optional): Describe any issues or concerns with your hips.
* **Thighs** (`thighs`, text, optional): Describe any issues or concerns with your thighs.
* **Knees** (`knees`, text, optional): Describe any issues or concerns with your knees.
* **Feet** (`feet`, text, optional): Describe any issues or concerns with your feet.
* **Hands** (`hands`, text, optional): Describe any issues or concerns with your hands.
* **Fingers** (`fingers`, text, optional): Describe any issues or concerns with your fingers.
* **Spine** (`spine`, text, optional): Describe any issues or concerns with your spine.
* **Overall Body** (`overall_body`, text, optional): Describe any issues or concerns with your overall body.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* If you have any medical questions or concerns, please consult with a healthcare professional before completing this form.
* Please ensure that the information you provide is up-to-date and accurate.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please discuss them with the athletic team or organization before completing this form.
