# toulouse_olfactory_assessment_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Toulouse Olfactory Assessment Questionnaire is designed to assess an individual's sense of smell, including their preferences, experiences, and related issues. This form is intended for medical professionals or researchers conducting surveys or questionnaires related to olfactory characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your demographics (question 2).
2. Answer the questions about your olfactory history (question 3).
3. Describe your olfactory symptoms (question 4).
4. Indicate the characteristics of your sense of smell (question 5).
5. Rate the impact of your olfactory experiences (question 6).
6. Select the preferential factors influencing your sense of smell (questions 7-15).
7. Provide additional information on any other preferential factors related to your sense of smell (questions 17, 20, and 23).
8. Provide any additional comments or justifications for your answers (questions 18, 21, 22, 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Demographics** (question 2): This is a general question to gather information about your identity, including your age, sex, and location (if applicable).
* **Olfactory History** (question 3): This question will ask about your past experiences with your sense of smell, such as any notable events or changes.
* **Olfactory Symptoms** (question 4): Here, you will be asked to select symptoms you have experienced related to your sense of smell (e.g., loss of smell, distorted sense of smell, or others).
* **Olfactory Characteristics** (question 5): Describe your typical sense of smell experiences, such as the intensity and range of odors you can detect.
* **Olfactory Impact** (question 6): Rate the severity or frequency of your olfactory symptoms or experiences.
* **Preferential Factors** (questions 7-15): Select the factors you find most important or influential in your sense of smell, such as pleasantness, intensity, etc.
* **Additional Preferential Factors** (questions 17, 20, and 23): These questions will ask for any other factors related to your sense of smell, which you may not have mentioned earlier.
* **Justifications and Comments** (questions 18, 21, 22, 25): Provide any explanations or comments for your answers, especially for questions related to "other" options.
