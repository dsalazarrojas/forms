# Hemophilia Treatment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information about your current treatment regimen, experiences, and medical history related to hemophilia or bleeding disorders. This information will help healthcare providers better understand your needs and develop effective treatment plans tailored to your individual needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Choose your current treatment regimen from the dropdown menu.
* Select the frequency of your bleeding episodes from the available options.
* Choose your level of satisfaction with your current treatment from the dropdown menu.
* Enter your bleeding history in date format if requested.
* Choose your treatment goals.
* Select any additional medical conditions you have.
* Identify who is part of your healthcare team.
* Select your preferences for treatment.
* Enter any other medical information you would like to provide.
* Provide any additional comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your current treatment regimen?**: This field is used to determine your current treatment plan for hemophilia or bleeding disorders. Please select the regimen that best describes your current treatment.
* **How often do you experience bleeding episodes?**: This field helps us understand the frequency of your bleeding episodes. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **How satisfied are you with your current treatment?**: This field is used to gauge your satisfaction level with your current treatment. Please select a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **How long have you been experiencing bleeding episodes?**: If you are willing, please enter the date of your first bleeding episode.
* **What are your treatment goals?**: This field helps us understand what you want to achieve with your treatment. Please select all that apply.
* **What medical conditions do you have?**: If you have any additional medical conditions, please select all that apply.
* **Who is part of your healthcare team?**: This field helps us understand who provides your care. Please select all that apply.
* **What are your preferences for treatment?**: This field is used to understand your preferences for treatment. Please select all that apply.
* **Other medical information**: If you have any additional medical information that is not listed, please enter it here.
* **Additional comments or questions**: If you have any additional comments or questions, please enter them here.

Note: The fields that are not required but may be helpful for your care are marked as optional. Please only fill out the required fields if you are able to do so.
