# Insect Bite Physical Exam Documentation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for medical professionals to document the physical examination of a patient who has sustained an insect bite. It is intended to gather relevant information about the patient's general information, bite description, symptoms, location of the bite, medical history, examination findings, and patient findings.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the patient's general information, including their demographic details and contact information.
2. Describe the patient's bite in detail, including the location, size, and appearance of the bite.
3. Document the patient's symptoms, including any pain, swelling, or other effects.
4. Record the location of the bite on the patient's body.
5. Review the patient's medical history, including any previous injuries or health conditions.
6. Document the patient's examination findings, including any notable observations or abnormalities.
7. Record the patient's findings, including any relevant notes or conclusions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is for gathering basic patient information, such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter a brief description of the patient's age, sex, and any other relevant demographic details.
* **Bite Description** (`bite_description`, `text`, required: false): Describe the location, size, and appearance of the bite in detail.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, required: false): Document any symptoms the patient is experiencing, such as pain, swelling, or itching.
* **Location of Bite** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Specify the exact location of the bite on the patient's body.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Record any relevant medical history, including previous injuries or health conditions that may be related to the bite.
* **Examination** (`examination`, `text`, required: false): Document any notable observations or abnormalities during the examination.
* **Patient Findings** (`patient_findings`, `text`, required: false): Record any relevant conclusions or notes from the examination and findings.
