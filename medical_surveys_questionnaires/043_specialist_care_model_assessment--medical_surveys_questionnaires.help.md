# Specialist Care Model Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Specialist Care Model Assessment is a form designed to collect feedback on the quality of specialist care in an organization. It helps to evaluate the current state of specialist care, identify areas for improvement, and make informed decisions for its development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best match your perception of the specialist care model.
3. If you select "Other" in any of the questions, please provide details in the text field below.
4. Fill in the dates and times in the format specified in the hint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think about the quality of specialist care in our organization?** (`staff_feedback`): Please share your thoughts on the overall quality of specialist care in our organization. Be honest and provide your feedback.
* **How does this compare to where we are now?** (`implementation_progress`): Compare the current state of specialist care to where it was in the past. Select the option that best reflects your perception.
* **What progress have we made so far?** (`progress_evaluation`): Select all the options that describe the progress made in specialist care. Be specific and provide examples if possible.
* **How can we improve our specialist care model?** (`evaluation_efforts`): Choose the option that best describes how we can improve specialist care. If you select "Other," please provide details in the text field below.
* **Other (please specify)** (`evaluation_efforts_other`): If you selected "Other" in the previous question, please provide details here.
* **Date of last evaluation?** (`date_of_last_evaluation`): Enter the date of the last evaluation in the format specified.
* **Next evaluation date?** (`next_evaluation_date`): Enter the next evaluation date in the format specified.
* **Next evaluation time?** (`next_evaluation_time`): Enter the next evaluation time in the 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00, 13:30, etc.).
* **Who is on our specialist care team?** (`specialist_care_team`): Select all the members of the specialist care team. Be specific and accurate.
* **Other members of specialist care team?** (`specialist_care_team_others`): If there are other members not listed, please provide details in the text field below.
* **Some details about the other members** (`specialist_care_team_others_details`): If you provided other members in the previous question, please provide some details about them.
* **Specialist Care Model** (`specialist_care_model`): Enter the name of the specialist care model if not already provided.
* **Some more details about the specialist care model** (`specialist_care_model_details`): If you have additional details about the specialist care model, please provide them here.
* **Overall satisfaction with specialist care** (`overall_satisfaction`): Select the option that best reflects your overall satisfaction with specialist care.
* **Next steps for improving specialist care** (`next_steps`): If you have any suggestions or ideas for improving specialist care, please enter them here.
* **Specialist Care Model Impact** (`specialist_care_model_impact`): Choose the option that best describes the impact of the specialist care model.
* **Some details about the specialist care model impact** (`specialist_care_model_impact_details`): If you selected "Other" in the previous question, please provide details about the impact of the specialist care model.
* **Evaluation time** (`evaluation_time`): Enter the duration of the evaluation in the format specified.
* **Evaluation frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`): Select the frequency of the evaluations.
* **Last evaluation date** (`last_evaluation_date`): Enter the date of the last evaluation in the format specified.
* **Next evaluation date** (`next_evaluation_date`): Enter the next evaluation date in the format specified.
* **Last evaluation time** (`last_evaluation_time`): Enter the last evaluation time in the 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00, 13:30, etc.).
* **Next evaluation time** (`next_evaluation_time`): Enter the next evaluation time in the 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00, 13:30, etc.).
* **Overall satisfaction 2** (`overall_satisfaction_2`): Select the option that best reflects your overall satisfaction with specialist care.
* **Some details about the specialist care model impact 2** (`specialist_care_model_impact_details_2`): If you selected "Other" in the previous question, please provide details about the impact of the specialist care model.
