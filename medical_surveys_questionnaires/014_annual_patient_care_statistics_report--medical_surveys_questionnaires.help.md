# Annual Patient Care Statistics Report - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect data on various metrics related to patient care, including patient visits, emergency department visits, inpatient admissions, average patient age, age distribution, gender distribution, patient outcomes, readmission rate, patient satisfaction score, hospital-acquired infections, adverse events, total staff, number of physicians, nursing staff, support staff, staff turnover rate, open positions, and annual operating expenses and revenue.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields ( Facility Name, Reporting Year, Report Submission Date, Total Patient Visits, Report Prepared By, Contact Email, Total Staff Members )
2. Fill in optional fields if applicable ( Emergency Department Visits, Inpatient Admissions, Average Patient Age, Age Distribution, Gender Distribution, Patient Outcomes, 30-day Readmission Rate, Patient Satisfaction Score, Hospital-acquired Infections, Adverse Events, Number of Physicians, Nursing Staff, Support Staff, Staff Turnover Rate, Open Positions, Annual Operating Expenses, Annual Revenue )

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name (1)**: Enter the name of the healthcare facility.
* **Facility ID Number (2)**: If your facility has an ID number, enter it here. (Optional)
* **Reporting Year (3)**: Enter the calendar year being reported.
* **Report Submission Date (4)**: Enter the date this report is being submitted.
* **Report Prepared By (5)**: Enter the name and title of the person submitting this report.
* **Contact Email (6)**: Enter a contact email for follow-up questions.
* **Total Patient Visits (7)**: Enter the annual number of patient visits.
* **Emergency Department Visits (8)**: Enter the number of emergency department visits (Optional)
* **Inpatient Admissions (9)**: Enter the number of inpatient admissions (Optional)
* **Average Patient Age (10)**: Enter the mean age of patients served.
* **Age Distribution (11)**: Enter an approximate breakdown by age group (Optional)
* **Gender Distribution (12)**: Enter a percentage or count by gender (Optional)
* **Patient Outcomes (13)**: Select the overall patient outcome rating (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Mixed, Below target)
* **30-day Readmission Rate (14)**: Enter the percentage of readmitted patients (Optional)
* **Patient Satisfaction Score (15)**: Enter the average satisfaction rating (Optional)
* **Hospital-acquired Infections (16)**: Enter the number of HAI incidents (Optional)
* **Adverse Events (17)**: Enter the number of adverse events (Optional)
* **Total Staff Members (18)**: Enter the total number of staff members (FTE or headcount).
* **Number of Physicians (19)**: Enter the number of attending and resident physicians (Optional)
* **Nursing Staff (20)**: Enter the number of RNs, LPNs, and nursing assistants (Optional)
* **Support Staff (21)**: Enter the total number of support personnel (Optional)
* **Staff Turnover Rate (22)**: Enter the annual turnover percentage (Optional)
* **Open Positions (23)**: Enter the number of unfilled positions (Optional)
* **Annual Operating Expenses (24)**: Enter the total facility expenses (Optional)
* **Annual Revenue (25)**: Enter the total facility revenue (Optional)

## Tips

* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If a field is marked as optional, only fill it out if applicable to your facility.
* Review all fields carefully to ensure completeness and accuracy.
