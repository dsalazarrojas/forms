# Clubfoot Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Clubfoot Assessment Survey form is designed to collect information about the patient's clubfoot condition, including demographic information, clubfoot details, and treatment status.

## How To Complete This Form

The form is divided into several sections, each of which should be completed according to the following instructions:

1. **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, medical record number, and date of birth.
2. **Assessment Details**: Enter the assessment date and the clinician's name.
3. **Clubfoot Status**: Answer the questions about the affected side, age at diagnosis, current treatment status, current pain level, and range of motion status.
4. **Mobility and Skin Condition**: Answer the questions about walking or mobility ability, gait pattern assessment, skin condition, and footwear fit and comfort.
5. **Complications and Recommendations**: Describe any complications or concerns and provide treatment plan notes for the next visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: This field is required. Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Patient ID**: This field is required. Enter the patient's medical record number as it appears on their medical record.
* **Assessment Date**: This field is required. Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Date of Birth**: This field is required. Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Clinician Name**: This field is required. Enter the name of the healthcare provider conducting the assessment.
* **Affected Side**: Select the affected side of the clubfoot condition:
	+ **Left Foot Only**
	+ **Right Foot Only**
	+ **Both Feet**
* **Age at Diagnosis**: This field is optional. Enter the patient's age in years or months at the time of diagnosis.
* **Current Treatment Status**: This field is required. Select the current treatment status:
	+ **Pre-Treatment**
	+ **In Progress**
	+ **Post-Surgical**
	+ **Completed**
	+ **Maintenance**
* **Current Pain Level**: This field is optional. Rate the patient's current pain level on a 0-10 scale.
* **Range of Motion Status**: This field is required. Select the patient's current range of motion status:
	+ **Severely Limited**
	+ **Limited**
	+ **Moderate**
	+ **Good**
	+ **Excellent**
* **Walking or Mobility Ability**: This field is required. Select the patient's current walking or mobility ability:
	+ **Non-Ambulatory**
	+ **Assisted Devices Required**
	+ **Limited Mobility**
	+ **Independent Walking**
	+ **Full Mobility**
* **Gait Pattern Assessment**: This field is optional. Describe how the patient walks.
* **Skin Condition**: This field is required. Select the patient's current skin condition:
	+ **Normal**
	+ **Mild Irritation**
	+ **Moderate Breakdown**
	+ **Severe Breakdown**
	+ **Other**
* **Footwear Fit and Comfort**: This field is optional. Select the patient's current footwear fit and comfort status:
	+ **Excellent Fit**
	+ **Good Fit**
	+ **Adequate Fit**
	+ **Poor Fit**
	+ **No Orthotic Device**
* **Complications or Concerns**: This field is optional. Describe any complications or concerns related to the clubfoot condition.
* **Recommendations for Next Visit**: This field is optional. Provide treatment plan notes for the next visit.
