# Holistic Health Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect your personal and medical information to help healthcare professionals provide you with optimal care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please fill out each field carefully and accurately. If you need help or have questions, please don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is for your personal contact and identity information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is for your emergency contact information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Please describe your past medical conditions, illnesses, or surgeries.
* **Health Concerns** (`health_concerns`, `text`, required: false): List any health concerns or issues you are currently experiencing.
* **Services Needed** (`services_needed`, `text`, required: false): Specify what services you require for your healthcare needs.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): Provide the name, phone number, and address of an emergency contact person.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, please provide your insurance provider and policy number.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required: false): List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required: false): List any current medical conditions or diagnoses.
* **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`, `text`, required: false): List any recent or upcoming medical tests or procedures.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe your family medical history, including any genetic predispositions or conditions.
* **Lifestyle** (`lifestyle`, `text`, required: false): Describe your daily habits, exercise routine, and eating habits.
* **Social History** (`social_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe your social environment, relationships, and any social challenges.
* **Review** (`review`, `text`, required: false): Please review your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information not listed above.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: false): Please sign and date this form as proof of completion.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `text`, required: false): Your digital signature as the patient.
* **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, `text`, required: false): Please attach or describe your medical records, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Your date of birth.
* **Date of Last Visit** (`date_of_last_visit`, `date`, required: false): The date of your last visit to a healthcare professional.
* **Time of Last Visit** (`time_of_last_visit`, `time`, required: false): The time of your last visit to a healthcare professional.
* **Date of Next Visit** (`date_of_next_visit`, `date`, required: false): The date of your next scheduled visit to a healthcare professional.
* **Time of Next Visit** (`time_of_next_visit`, `time`, required: false): The time of your next scheduled visit to a healthcare professional.

## Tips

Please fill out this form as accurately and thoroughly as possible. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
