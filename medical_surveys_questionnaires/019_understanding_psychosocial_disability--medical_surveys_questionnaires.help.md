# understanding-psychosocial-disability - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect information about the psychosocial disability from a mental health professional, specifically to identify the research area, objectives, methodology, and objectives of a study. This form will help to assess the understanding of psychosocial disability by providing the necessary details about the study design.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **what_is_psychosocial_disability** (`what_is_psychosocial_disability`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to describe in your own words what you understand psychosocial disability to be.
* **mental_health_professional** (`mental_health_professional`, text, required/optional): In this field, please provide the name and title of the mental health professional conducting the research or providing the information.
* **research_area** (`research_area`, text, required/optional): Describe the area of research or the specific topic of the study.
* **project_details** (`project_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional details about the project, such as the study's duration, location, or funding source.
* **research_objectives** (`research_objectives`, text, required/optional): Describe the main objectives or goals of the study.
* **research_methodology** (`research_methodology`, text, required/optional): Explain the methods used to conduct the research or study.
* **research_participants** (`research_participants`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select which of the following apply to the research participants:
	+ Option 1: Adults
	+ Option 2: Children
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify)
* **other_participants** (`other_participants`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Other" in the above option, please provide more information about the participants.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* Make sure to select the correct options for the research participants.
