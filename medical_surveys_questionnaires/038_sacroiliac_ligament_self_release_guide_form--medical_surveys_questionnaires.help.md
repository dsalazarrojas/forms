# sacroiliac_ligament_self_release_guide_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sacroiliac ligament self-release guide form is designed to guide individuals through the self-release technique for the sacroiliac ligament, a common issue in medical practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read and understand the instructions and procedures outlined in this guide.
2. Fill out all required fields carefully and accurately.
3. Ensure that you have read and understood each field's purpose and requirements before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`title`, text, not required): Enter the title of this form.
* **Category** (`category`, select one, not required): Select "Medical Surveys & Questionnaires" from the dropdown menu.
* **Description** (`description`, note, not required): Enter a brief description of this form.
* **Practitioner Feedback** (`practitioner_feedback`, text, not required): Enter any feedback from the practitioner.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, not required): Enter any feedback from the participant.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select one, not required): Select your desired follow-up option (Immediate, 2 weeks, 4 weeks, 6 weeks).
* **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, select multiple, not required): Check the boxes to indicate your understanding and ability to perform the self-release technique.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, not required): Sign and date this form as the participant.
* **Practitioner Signature** (`practitioner_signature`, text, not required): Sign and date this form as the practitioner.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Enter the date of this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Enter the time of this form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Other Phone** (`other_phone`, text, not required): Enter any other phone number.
* **Other Email** (`other_email`, email, not required): Enter any other email address.
* **Practitioner Name** (`practitioner_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as the practitioner.
* **Practitioner License** (`practitioner_license`, text, not required): Enter your practitioner's license number.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, not required): Enter your participant's date of birth.
* **Participant Sex** (`participant_sex`, select one, not required): Select your participant's sex (Male, Female, Other).
* **Participant Country** (`participant_country`, text, not required): Enter your participant's country of residence.
* **Participant State** (`participant_state`, text, not required): Enter your participant's state or province of residence.
* **Participant Postal Code** (`participant_postal_code`, text, not required): Enter your participant's postal code or zip code.
* **Practitioner Specialization** (`practitioner_specialization`, text, not required): Enter your practitioner's specialization or title.
* **Practitioner Address** (`practitioner_address`, text, not required): Enter your practitioner's address.
* **Practitioner Facility** (`practitioner_facility`, text, not required): Enter your practitioner's facility or clinic name.
* **Participant Facility** (`participant_facility`, text, not required): Enter your participant's facility or clinic name.
