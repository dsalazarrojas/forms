# Contact Lens Comfort Study Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Contact Lens Comfort Study Interest Form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in participating in a study related to contact lens comfort. The form will help you understand your suitability for the study and will aid us in planning the study visits accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Provide the required information for each field.
3. Don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or concerns while completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Study Information

* **Contact Lens Comfort Study**: A brief note indicating the study's purpose and what you can expect as a participant.

### 2. First Name

* **First Name**: Enter your first name for identification and record-keeping purposes.
* Required: Yes

### 3. Last Name

* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for identification and record-keeping purposes.
* Required: Yes

### 4. Date of Birth

* **Date of Birth**: Provide your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format (e.g., 02-15-1995).
* Required: Yes

### 5. Gender

* **Gender**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Non-binary
	+ Prefer not to say
* Required: Yes

### 6. Email Address

* **Email Address**: Enter your preferred contact email address.
* Required: Yes

### 7. Phone Number

* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* Required: Yes

### 8. Street Address

* **Street Address**: Enter your residential or work address.
* Required: Yes

### 9. City

* **City**: Enter the name of the city where you reside or work.
* Required: Yes

### 10. State

* **State**: Enter the name of the state where you reside.
* Required: Yes

### 11. ZIP Code

* **ZIP Code**: Enter the postal code of your residential or work address.
* Required: Yes

### 12. Do You Currently Wear Contact Lenses

* **Do You Currently Wear Contact Lenses**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes - Soft Lenses
	+ Yes - Rigid Gas Permeable
	+ Yes - Hybrid
	+ No - But I Have in the Past
	+ No - Never Worn
* Required: Yes

### 13. How Often Do You Wear Lenses

* **How Often Do You Wear Lenses**: Select one of the following options, if applicable:
	+ Daily
	+ Most Days
	+ Few Times a Week
	+ Occasionally
	+ Not Applicable
* Required: No

### 14. Do You Experience Comfort Issues

* **Do You Experience Comfort Issues**: Select all that apply:
	+ Dryness
	+ Irritation
	+ Redness
	+ Blurry Vision
	+ Lens Awareness
	+ End of Day Discomfort
	+ No Issues
* Required: Yes

### 15. Do You Have Any Eye Conditions

* **Do You Have Any Eye Conditions**: Select all that apply:
	+ Dry Eye Syndrome
	+ Astigmatism
	+ Presbyopia
	+ Glaucoma
	+ None
	+ Other
* Required: Yes

### 16. Do You Have Seasonal Allergies

* **Do You Have Seasonal Allergies**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes - Severe
	+ Yes - Moderate
	+ Yes - Mild
	+ False
* Required: Yes

### 17. Are You Taking Any Eye Medications

* **Are You Taking Any Eye Medications**: Select one of the following options:
	+ True
	+ False
* Required: Yes

### 18. Availability for Study Visits

* **Availability for Study Visits**: Select all that apply:
	+ Weekday Mornings
	+ Weekday Afternoons
	+ Weekday Evenings
	+ Weekends
	+ Flexible Schedule
* Required: Yes

### 19. Interest in Participating

* **Interest in Participating**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Very Interested
	+ Somewhat Interested
	+ Need More Information
	+ Not Interested
* Required: Yes

### 20. How Did You Hear About This Study

* **How Did You Hear About This Study**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Clinic Website
	+ Social Media
	+ Doctor Referral
	+ Friend or Family
	+ Advertisement
	+ Other
* Required: No

### 21. Additional Comments or Questions

* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Enter any additional information or questions you may have about the study.
* Required: No

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask.
* We value your time and commitment to the study, and we'll be in touch with you soon.
