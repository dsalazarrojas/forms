# self-health-assessment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a self-reported health assessment tool designed to collect information about your health concerns and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Self-Health Assessment Form Page 1
* **Self-Health Assessment Form Page 1** (`self_health_assessment_form_page_1`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form, which serves as a general health concern entry point. You can briefly describe your health concerns here, if any.
* **What is your health concern?** (`question_1`, text, optional): Describe your current health concern in a few sentences.
* **What is your health concern 2?** (`question_2`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options to indicate the status of your second health concern.
* **Question 3** (`question_3`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last health check-up or medical appointment.
* **Question 4** (`question_4`, time, optional): Enter the time of your last health check-up or medical appointment.
* **Question 5** (`question_5`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to indicate the status of your third health concern.
* **Question 6** (`question_6`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value for your current health score.
* **Question 7** (`question_7`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for your healthcare provider or medical contact.
* **Question 8** (`question_8`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information regarding your health status.
* **Question 9** (`question_9`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your health concern.
