# Insomnia Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insomnia Questionnaire is designed to gather information about a user's sleep patterns and habits. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all the questions truthfully and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can provide any relevant information about your sleep patterns or habits. 

- **Sleep Time**: This is a field where you can enter the number of hours you sleep each night. 

- **Difficulty Falling Asleep**: This question asks about how often you experience difficulty falling asleep. The options are:
    - Never
    - Almost every night
    - 1-2 nights a week
    - 3-5 nights a week
    - Every night

- **Wake-up Time**: This is a field where you can enter the time you typically wake up each morning. 

- **Sleep Disorder**: This question asks about any sleep disorders you may have. The options are:
    - Insomnia
    - Sleep Apnea
    - Sleep Movement Disorder
    - Restless Legs Syndrome
    - None (if no sleep disorders)

- **Anxiety/Fear**: This is a field where you can enter a number indicating your anxiety levels. 

- **Stress Level**: This question asks about how often you experience stress. The options are:
    - Never
    - Almost always
    - Some days
    - Most days

- **Physical Discomfort**: This question allows you to select any physical discomforts you experience at night. The options are:
    - Back pain
    - Headache
    - Cough
    - Muscle fatigue
    - Other (if not listed, please specify)

- **Medication**: This question asks about any medications you use for sleep. The options are:
    - Melatonin
    - Sleeping pill
    - Other (if not listed, please specify)

- **Medical Condition**: This question asks about any underlying medical conditions that may affect your sleep. The options are:
    - None
    - High Blood Pressure
    - Heart disease
    - Diabetes
    - Other (if not listed, please specify)

- **Smoking Status**: This question asks about your smoking history. The options are:
    - Never smoked
    - Currently a smoker
    - Former smoker

- **Doctor Visit**: This is a field where you can enter the date of your last doctor visit. 

- **Doctor Visit Note**: This is a note field where you can provide any comments about your doctor visit. 

- **Medical History**: This is a note field where you can enter any relevant medical history information. 

Note: Please review all fields before submitting the form to ensure accurate and complete information. 

This form has 12 sections. Please answer all required fields to ensure that your data is collected correctly. 

Please let us know if you need any further assistance.
