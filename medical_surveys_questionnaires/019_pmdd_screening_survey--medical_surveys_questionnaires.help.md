# PMDD Screening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PMDD Screening Survey is a questionnaire designed to assess symptoms associated with Pre-Menstrual Dysphoric Disorder (PMDD). It is intended to be completed by individuals who are concerned about their symptoms or are seeking guidance from a medical professional.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the PMDD Screening Survey, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select all that apply for questions 1 and 2.
3. Choose one option for question 3.
4. Enter your answers in the text fields for questions 4 and 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Have you ever experienced any of the following?** (`question_1`, `select_multiple`, optional)
This question asks about past experiences with symptoms that are commonly associated with PMDD. Please select all that apply from the list of options.
* **Select all that apply** (`question_2`, `select_multiple`, optional)
This question is similar to the previous one, asking about past experiences with different symptoms.
* **Answer 1** (`answer_1`, `text`, optional)
This is a text field for you to provide more information about your experiences with the selected symptoms.
* **Answer 2** (`answer_2`, `text`, optional)
This is another text field for you to provide more information about your experiences with the selected symptoms.
* **How often do you experience?** (`question_3`, `select_one`, optional)
This question asks about the frequency of your symptoms. Please select one of the available options.

## Tips
* Please answer all the questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you experience any of the symptoms frequently, it is essential to consult with a healthcare provider for further evaluation and consultation.
* This survey is not a substitute for medical advice or treatment.
* If you have any concerns or questions about the survey, feel free to contact form@gic.mx.
