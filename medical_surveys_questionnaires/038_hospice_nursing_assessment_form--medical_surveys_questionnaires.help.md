# hospice_nursing_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hospice nursing assessment form is a tool used to evaluate the needs of patients who require end-of-life care. It helps healthcare professionals assess the patient's medical condition, identify their care needs, and create a care plan that meets those needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the **Introduction** page, which is optional and serves as a general overview of the patient's information.
2.  Move on to the **patient_info** page, which requires information about the patient's general details.
3.  The **medical_condition** page is also required, where you will input the patient's medical condition in detail.
4.  In the **care_level** page, select the level of care the patient needs from the options provided.
5.  The **medication_allergies** and **medication_current_use** pages are optional and capture any allergies or current medications the patient has.
6.  Next, you will evaluate the patient's **pain_level** by selecting from the provided options.
7.  Move on to the **vital_signs** page, which is a required field for tracking vital signs.
8.  In the **medical_notes** page, you can add any additional medical notes about the patient.
9.  The **hospice_care_needed** page is another required field where you select whether the patient needs hospice care.
10.  You will then select the type of hospice care the patient requires from the options provided in the **hospice_care_type** page.
11.  In the **hospice_care_plan** page, create a comprehensive care plan for the patient.
12.  The **hospice_care_goal** page is optional and allows you to set goals for the patient's care.
13.  Evaluate the frequency of care the patient needs in the **hospice_care_frequency** page, where you select from the provided options.
14.  You will also need to specify the duration of the care in the **hospice_care_duration** page, which is optional.
15.  In the **hospice_care_frequency_2** page, select from the provided options how frequently the care is needed.
16.  The **hospice_care_frequency_3** page is optional and allows for additional information about the care frequency.
17.  In the **hospice_care_frequency_4** page, select the time frame for the care from the provided options.
18.  The **hospice_care_frequency_5** page is optional and allows for further detail about the care frequency.
19.  Lastly, in the **hospice_care_frequency_6**, **hospice_care_frequency_7**, **hospice_care_frequency_8**, **hospice_care_frequency_9**, and **hospice_care_frequency_10** pages, you will select or input details about the care frequency in various time frames.

    *   Whether the patient needs hospice care.

    *   The type of care the patient requires.

## Tips
- Ensure you complete all required fields before submitting the form.
- Use clear and concise language when providing information.
- Be thorough and accurate when assessing the patient's condition.
- Consult with other healthcare professionals if you are unsure about any information.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
- Use a pen or pencil to fill out the form to ensure accurate and legible writing.
