# Anxiety Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The anxiety questionnaire is a tool to help us better understand anxiety and its effects on individuals. It will help us gather information on various aspects of anxiety, including symptoms, severity, impact on daily life, and coping strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question based on your personal experiences and feelings.
2. Select the options that best describe your experiences.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to the hints provided for each question or ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant ID**: This is an optional identifier for tracking purposes. You can leave it blank if you don't have an ID.
* **Date**: The date you are completing this form.
* **Age**: Your age in years. Please enter a numerical value.
* **Gender**: Select the option that best describes your gender.
* **Occupation**: Select the option that best describes your current occupation.
* **How long have you experienced anxiety**: Select the option that best describes the duration of your anxiety symptoms.
* **How often do you feel anxious**: Select the option that best describes the frequency of your anxiety symptoms.
* **How severe is your anxiety**: Select the option that best describes the severity of your anxiety symptoms.
* **Physical Symptoms**: Select all the physical symptoms that apply to you.
* **Emotional Symptoms**: Select all the emotional symptoms that apply to you.
* **Common Anxiety Triggers**: Select all the triggers that commonly cause your anxiety.
* **Panic Attacks**: Select the option that best describes your experiences with panic attacks.
* **Avoidance Behaviors**: Select the option that best describes your avoidance behaviors due to anxiety.
* **Impact on Daily Life**: Select the option that best describes the impact of anxiety on your daily life.
* **Impact on Work or School**: Select the option that best describes the impact of anxiety on your work or school performance.
* **Impact on Relationships**: Select the option that best describes the impact of anxiety on your relationships.
* **Sleep Quality**: Select the option that best describes your sleep quality.
* **Coping Strategies**: Select all the strategies you use to cope with anxiety.
* **Sought Professional Help**: Select the option that best describes your experiences with seeking help for anxiety.
* **Type of Help Received**: Select all the types of help you have received for anxiety.
* **Current Medication**: Select the option that best describes your current medication status for anxiety.
* **Current Therapy**: Select the option that best describes your current therapy status.
* **Support System**: Select the option that best describes your support system.
* **Substance Use**: Select the option that best describes your substance use for coping with anxiety.
* **Family History**: Select the option that best describes your family history of anxiety.
* **Previous Diagnosis**: Select the option that best describes your previous diagnosis of anxiety.
* **Goals for Treatment**: Enter your goals for managing anxiety, if applicable.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share.

## Tips

* Please answer each question based on your personal experiences and feelings.
* Refer to the hints provided for each question for guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask for clarification or guidance.
* You can select multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections.
