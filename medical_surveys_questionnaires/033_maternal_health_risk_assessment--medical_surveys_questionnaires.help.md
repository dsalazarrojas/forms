# Maternal Health Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the risk level of a pregnant or postpartum patient's health. It gathers essential information from the patient and the healthcare provider to identify potential health risks and ensure the patient receives appropriate care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "General Information" page with the patient's general details.
2. Answer the "Maternal Experience" question by selecting the appropriate option from the dropdown menu.
3. For the "Medical Conditions" field, select all conditions that apply to the patient from the list provided.
4. For the "Risk Factors" field, select all risk factors that apply to the patient from the list provided.
5. Enter the patient's healthcare provider information in the "Health Provider Information" field.
6. Create a maternal care plan for the patient in the "Maternal Care Plan" field.
7. Enter the patient's risk score in the "Risk Score" field.
8. Schedule the next follow-up date for the patient in the "Next Follow-Up" field.
9. Enter any feedback from the healthcare provider in the "Provider Feedback" field.
10. Enter any feedback from the patient in the "Patient Feedback" field.
11. Enter the healthcare provider's name, title, and contact information in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (Page 1): This section collects basic patient information such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Maternal Experience** (Page 2): This question asks about the patient's current pregnancy status. Select the correct answer from the dropdown menu:
	+ Pregnant Now: The patient is currently pregnant.
	+ Previous Pregnancy: The patient has had previous pregnancies.
* **Medical Conditions** (Page 3): Check all conditions that apply to the patient from the list:
	+ Hypertension: High blood pressure.
	+ Diabetes: A condition affecting blood sugar levels.
	+ Asthma: A chronic condition affecting the respiratory system.
	+ Epilepsy: A neurological condition affecting seizures.
	+ Migraine: A condition affecting headaches and vision.
	+ Other (please specify): Select this option if none of the above conditions apply.
* **Risk Factors** (Page 4): Check all risk factors that apply to the patient from the list:
	+ Smoking: The patient smokes or has a history of smoking.
	+ Obesity: A condition of having excess body fat.
	+ Substance Abuse: A condition of substance use or addiction.
	+ History of Mental Illness: A condition of mental health issues.
	+ Other (please specify): Select this option if none of the above risk factors apply.
* **Health Provider Information** (Page 5): Enter the patient's healthcare provider information, such as name and contact details.
* **Maternal Care Plan** (Page 6): Create a personalized care plan for the patient, outlining the necessary steps and recommendations.
* **Risk Score** (Page 7): Enter the patient's risk score, which is calculated based on the patient's conditions and risk factors.
* **Next Follow-Up** (Page 8): Schedule the next follow-up date for the patient.
* **Provider Feedback** (Page 9): Enter any feedback or comments from the healthcare provider.
* **Patient Feedback** (Page 10): Enter any feedback or comments from the patient.
* **Provider Name** (Page 11): Enter the healthcare provider's name.
* **Provider Title** (Page 12): Enter the healthcare provider's title.
* **Provider Contact** (Page 13): Enter the healthcare provider's contact information.
