# Coronavirus Suspected Patient Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather crucial information from suspected patients of COVID-19 to aid healthcare professionals in making informed decisions for proper diagnosis and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant fields for each section of the form, as indicated by the required field status.
2. Fill in all the required fields with accurate and relevant information.

* **Symptoms** (`coronavirus_symptoms`, text, required):
	+ Please provide a detailed description of the patient's symptoms, including but not limited to fever, cough, difficulty breathing, or shortness of breath.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, required):
	+ Enter the patient's age to provide context for their medical history.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select all relevant contact information for the patient, such as Emergency Contact, Primary Care, or Insurance.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose one of the available medical conditions listed, such as Hypertension or Diabetes, to indicate the patient's condition.
* **Date Visited Doctor** (`date_visited_doctor`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date the patient visited the doctor to help track their medical history.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter the name of the patient's doctor for accurate record-keeping.
* **Doctor's Info** (`doctor_info`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional information about the patient's doctor.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous conditions or treatments.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional):
	+ If applicable, list any allergies the patient has.
* **Insurance** (`insurance`, text, optional):
	+ Provide information about the patient's insurance coverage.
* **Other Medical Condition** (`medical_condition_other`, text, optional):
	+ Describe any other medical conditions that may not be listed.
