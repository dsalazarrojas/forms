# Covid Contact Tracing Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to aid in the contact tracing process for individuals who may have been exposed to or infected with COVID-19. It collects vital information about the individual's exposure history, symptoms, and contact tracing status to help healthcare professionals track and manage potential outbreaks.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section of the form carefully and accurately.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each field.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed (indicated by a *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Please enter your full name and any relevant identification information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact details (phone number, email, etc.) to allow for follow-up communication.
* **Exposure Details** (`exposure_details`, text, optional): Describe the circumstances of your exposure to COVID-19 (e.g., location, duration).
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): If experiencing symptoms, describe them in as much detail as possible.
* **Contact Tracing** (`contact_tracing`, select one, optional): Indicate if you have been in contact with someone who has been confirmed to have COVID-19.
* **Follow up** (`follow_up`, select multiple, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, in-person) for follow-up information.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you were exposed to or infected with COVID-19.
* **Date of Exposure** (`date_of_exposure`, date, optional): Enter the date you were exposed to or infected with COVID-19.
* **Time of Exposure** (`time_of_exposure`, time, optional): Enter the time you were exposed to or infected with COVID-19.
* **Medical Organization Info** (`medical_org_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about your medical organization or facility.
* **Contact Tracing Status** (`contact_tracing_status`, select one, optional): Indicate your current contact tracing status (active or not active).
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select one, optional): Indicate the status of your follow-up appointment or contact (completed or not completed).
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your follow-up appointment or contact.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your follow-up appointment or contact.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your situation.
