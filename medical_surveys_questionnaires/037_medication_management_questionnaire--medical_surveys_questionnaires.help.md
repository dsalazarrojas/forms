# Medication Management Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information about a patient's medication management, including their medical history, allergies, and medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's ID in the "patient_id" field.
2. Provide the patient's name in the "name" field.
3. Enter the patient's address in the "address" field.
4. Answer the "medical_condition" field regarding the patient's current medical condition.
5. Select the appropriate answer for "dosages" regarding the patient's medication dosages.
6. If the patient has other dosages, please enter them in the "dosages_other" field.
7. Select the appropriate answer for "medications" regarding the patient's current medications.
8. If the patient has other medications, please enter them in the "medications_other" field.
9. Answer the "medical_staff" field regarding the patient's current medical staff.
10. If the patient has other medical staff, please enter them in the "medical_staff_other" field.
11. Select the appropriate answer for "allergies" regarding the patient's allergies.
12. If the patient has other allergies, please enter them in the "allergies_other" field.
13. Select the appropriate answer for "medical_history" regarding the patient's medical history.
14. If the patient has other medical history, please enter them in the "medical_history_other" field.
15. Select the appropriate answer for "medical_conditions" regarding the patient's medical conditions.
16. If the patient has other medical conditions, please enter them in the "medical_conditions_other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_id**: Please enter the patient's ID number as assigned by the healthcare provider.
* **name**: Please enter the patient's name.
* **address**: Please enter the patient's current address.
* **medical_condition**: Please answer whether the patient has a medical condition. If yes, select "Yes" and provide further information in the "medical_conditions_other" field if necessary.
* **dosages**: Please select whether the patient has a dosage for their medications. If yes, select "Yes" and provide further information in the "dosages_other" field if necessary.
* **dosages_other**: If the patient has other dosages, please enter them here.
* **medications**: Please select whether the patient is taking medications. If yes, select "Yes" and provide further information in the "medications_other" field if necessary.
* **medications_other**: If the patient has other medications, please enter them here.
* **medical_staff**: Please answer whether the patient has a medical staff. If yes, select "Yes" and provide further information in the "medical_staff_other" field if necessary.
* **medical_staff_other**: If the patient has other medical staff, please enter them here.
* **allergies**: Please select whether the patient has allergies. If yes, select "Yes" and provide further information in the "allergies_other" field if necessary.
* **allergies_other**: If the patient has other allergies, please enter them here.
* **medical_history**: Please select whether the patient has medical history. If yes, select "Yes" and provide further information in the "medical_history_other" field if necessary.
* **medical_history_other**: If the patient has other medical history, please enter them here.
* **medical_conditions**: Please select whether the patient has medical conditions. If yes, select "Yes" and provide further information in the "medical_conditions_other" field if necessary.
* **medical_conditions_other**: If the patient has other medical conditions, please enter them here.
