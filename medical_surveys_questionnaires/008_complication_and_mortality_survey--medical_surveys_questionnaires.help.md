# Complication And Mortality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Complication And Mortality Survey is a tool used to record and analyze data on complications and mortality events in a healthcare facility. The data collected through this form is used to identify patterns and trends in patient outcomes and improve care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you are filling out a new case that has not been reviewed or has a similar outcome.
2. Enter the required information accurately and thoroughly.
3. Select the correct options from the drop-down menus, and make sure to select all relevant options for multiple-select fields.
4. Enter dates and other information in the correct format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey ID Number** (1): Enter a unique identifier for this survey.
* **Facility Name** (2): Enter the name of the healthcare facility where the patient was treated.
* **Department** (3): Select the department where the patient was treated.
* **Reporting Physician** (4): Enter the name of the physician who completed this survey.
* **Patient ID** (5): Enter the patient's identification number.
* **Patient Age** (6): Enter the patient's age in years.
* **Patient Gender** (7): Select the patient's gender.
* **Admission Date** (8): Enter the date the patient was admitted.
* **Outcome Date** (9): Enter the date of the complication or mortality event.
* **Primary Diagnosis** (10): Enter the main diagnosis on admission.
* **Comorbidities** (11): Select all existing conditions that may have contributed to the complication or event.
* **Outcome Type** (12): Select the type of outcome.
* **Complication Category** (13): If applicable, select the complication type.
* **Severity Rating** (14): Rate the severity of the complication or event.
* **Contributing Factors** (15): Select all factors that may have contributed to the complication or event.
* **Interventions Performed** (16): Describe any interventions taken in response to the complication or event.
* **Length of Stay** (17): Enter the total days the patient was in the facility.
* **Readmission Within 30 Days** (18): Select if the patient was readmitted within 30 days.
* **Root Cause Analysis Required** (19): Select if a root cause analysis is needed.
* **Lessons Learned** (20): Describe any lessons learned or improvements identified.
* **Preventive Measures** (21): What measures could prevent similar outcomes.
* **Case Review Status** (22): Select the current status of case review.

Note: Fields with asterisks (*) after the field name are required fields.
