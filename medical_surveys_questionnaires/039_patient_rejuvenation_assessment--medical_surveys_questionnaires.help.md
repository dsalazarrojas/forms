# Patient Rejuvenation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Rejuvenation Assessment form is a tool used to collect information about a patient's rejuvenation needs and status. This form helps healthcare providers understand the patient's goals, history, and concerns to provide effective rejuvenation services.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant pages (sections) to fill out, as each section is optional.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as such in the form.
3. Provide accurate and detailed information in each field.
4. Review and submit the form for review and further action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Section 1
* **Section 1 (patient_rejuvenation_assessment_page)**: This is the first section of the form. Please complete this section as it is required.
* **Patient Goals (patient_goals)**: Select all the goals that apply to the patient's rejuvenation needs. This field is required.
* **Patient History (patient_history)**: Provide any relevant information about the patient's history, if applicable.
* **Patient Concerns (patient_concerns)**: List any concerns or issues the patient has regarding their rejuvenation needs.
* **Rejuvenation Plan (rejuvenation_plan)**: Outline the plan for the patient's rejuvenation.
* **Assistance Needed (assistance_needed)**: If the patient needs assistance, select the relevant option. This field is optional.
* **Next Steps (next_steps)**: Describe the next steps for the patient's rejuvenation.
* **Provider ID (provider_id)**: Enter your unique ID as a healthcare provider.
* **Provider Notes (provider_notes)**: Add any notes about the patient's care or concerns.
* **Provider Name (provider_name)**: Enter your name as a healthcare provider.
* **Provider Title (provider_title)**: Enter your title as a healthcare provider.
* **Provider Email (provider_email)**: Enter your email as a healthcare provider.

### Section 2
* **Follow up (follow_up)**: Enter the date for the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow up Time (follow_up_time)**: Enter the time for the follow-up appointment.
* **Assistance Needed (assistant_needed)**: If the patient needs assistance, select the relevant option. This field is optional.
* **Follow Up Frequency (follow_up_frequency)**: Select how frequently you will follow up with the patient. This field is optional.
* **Next Follow Up Date (next_follow_up_date)**: Enter the date of the next follow-up appointment.
* **Next Follow Up Time (next_follow_up_time)**: Enter the time of the next follow-up appointment.
* **Contact Number (contact_number)**: Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Email Address (email_address)**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Next Contact Date (next_contact_date)**: Enter the date of the next contact.
* **Next Contact Time (next_contact_time)**: Enter the time of the next contact.
* **Rejuvenation Notes (rejuvenation_notes)**: Add any additional notes about the patient's rejuvenation.
* **Patient Status (patient_status)**: Select the current status of the patient.
* **Patient Notes (patient_notes)**: Add any notes about the patient's status.
* **Provider ID (provider_id)**: Enter your unique ID as a healthcare provider.
* **Provider Name (provider_name)**: Enter your name as a healthcare provider.
* **Provider Title (provider_title)**: Enter your title as a healthcare provider.
* **Provider Email (provider_email)**: Enter your email as a healthcare provider.

Note: Please review each section carefully and provide accurate information to ensure the best possible outcomes for the patient's rejuvenation.
