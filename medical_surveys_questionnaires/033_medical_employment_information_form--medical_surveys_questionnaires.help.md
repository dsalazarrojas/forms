# Medical Employment Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Employment Information Form is designed to collect essential information about an employee's medical status, insurance, and contact details. This information helps the employer to provide necessary support and benefits to the employee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select your relevant medical information from the options provided in each section.
2.  Enter your emergency contact information, including their phone number and email address.
3.  Provide your job title, years of service, and employment status.
4.  Enter your company's name.
5.  Select your medical insurance provider.
6.  Enter your emergency contact's phone number and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information**: Select your relevant medical information from the options provided.
* **Medical Insurance**: Select your medical insurance provider from the options provided.
* **Emergency Contact**: Select your emergency contact from the options provided.
* **Employment Info**: Select your employment information from the options provided.
* **Years of Service**: Enter your years of service with the employer.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title with the company.
* **Employment Status**: Select your employment status from the options provided.
* **Company**: Enter your company's name.
* **Medical Insurance Provider**: Select your medical insurance provider from the options provided.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter your emergency contact's email address.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your HR representative or designated support person.
* This form is a required part of your employment process, so please complete it carefully and thoroughly.
