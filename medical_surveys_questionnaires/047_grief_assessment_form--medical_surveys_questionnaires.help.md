# # Grief Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals assess their reactions to a loss. It is used to gather information about their experiences and perceptions of their loss, and to help researchers better understand the effects of loss on individuals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
2. Make sure to answer all the questions, as they are all important for accurate assessment.
3. If you need more time to complete a question, you can skip to the next page and come back later.
4. You can also come back to previous pages as many times as you need to review and complete previous sections.
5. When you have completed all the sections, review your responses to ensure that they accurately reflect your experiences and feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Physical Reactions** (Physical Reactions, text, optional): This section asks about how you have physically reacted to your loss, such as changes in your appetite, sleep patterns, or physical pain.
* **Cognitive Reactions** (Cognitive Reactions, text, optional): This section asks about how you have cognitively reacted to your loss, such as changes in your mood, concentration, or memory.
* **Emotional Reactions** (Emotional Reactions, text, optional): This section asks about how you have emotionally reacted to your loss, such as feelings of sadness, guilt, or anger.
* **Behavioral Reactions** (Behavioral Reactions, text, optional): This section asks about how you have behaviorally reacted to your loss, such as changes in your daily routine or social interactions.
* **Thoughts** (Thoughts, text, optional): This section asks about any thoughts or reflections you may have about your loss.
* **How They Consider** (How They Consider, text, optional): This section asks about how you consider your loss, such as what it means to you or how it affects your daily life.
* **Physical** (Physical, text, required): This section asks you to rate how strongly you have physically reacted to your loss, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Cognitive** (Cognitive, text, required): This section asks you to rate how strongly you have cognitively reacted to your loss, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Emotional** (Emotional, text, required): This section asks you to rate how strongly you have emotionally reacted to your loss, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Behavioral** (Behavioral, text, required): This section asks you to rate how strongly you have behaviorally reacted to your loss, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall** (Overall, text, required): This section asks you to rate how strongly you have reacted to your loss in general, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Grief** (Grief, text, required): This section asks you to rate how strongly you have experienced grief, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Trauma** (Trauma, text, required): This section asks you to rate how strongly you have experienced trauma, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Loss** (Loss, text, required): This section asks you to rate how strongly you have experienced loss, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Other** (Other, text, required): This section asks you to rate how strongly you have experienced other reactions to your loss, on a scale of 1-5.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
* If you need more time to complete a question, you can skip to the next page and come back later.
* You can also come back to previous pages as many times as you need to review and complete previous sections.
* Please note that your responses will be used for research purposes, and will not be used for personal benefit.
