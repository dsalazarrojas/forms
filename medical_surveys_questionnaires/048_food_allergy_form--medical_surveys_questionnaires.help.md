# Food Allergy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from summer camp attendees about their food allergies and medical conditions to ensure a safe environment for everyone at the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Make sure to answer all required fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, not required): This is a brief overview of the form and its purpose. Please review it carefully before proceeding.
  - **Do you have any food allergies?** (`has_food_allergy`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select 'Yes' if you have any food allergies.
  - **If yes, which food items do you have a food allergy to?** (`food_items`, `select_multiple`, not required): If you have food allergies, select all the food items that apply to you.
  - **What is the severity of your food allergy?** (`allergy_severity`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select the severity of your food allergy: None, Mild, Moderate, Severe.
  - **Do you have any other allergies besides food allergies?** (`additional_allergies`, `text`, not required): If you have any other allergies, please specify them here.
  - **Who would you like to contact in case of an emergency?** (`emergency_contacts`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select who you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
  - **What is the emergency contact number?** (`emergency_number`, `text`, not required): Please provide the contact number of your emergency contact.
  - **How would you like to treat your food allergy?** (`allergy_treatment`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select how you would like to treat your food allergy: EpiPen, Epipen, Epipen II, Other.
  - **Are there any food restrictions due to your allergy?** (`food_restriction`, `select_multiple`, not required): If you have food allergies, please select any food restrictions that apply to you.
  - **Do you have any other medical conditions?** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, not required): If you have any other medical conditions, please specify them here.
  - **Name of your doctor** (`doctor`, `text`, required): Please provide the name of your doctor.
  - **Contact information of your doctor** (`doctor_2`, `text`, required): Please provide the contact information of your doctor.
  - **Your medical history** (`medical_history`, `text`, not required): If you have any medical history, please provide it here.
  - **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): If you have any comments or concerns, please provide them here.
  - **Submission date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Please provide the date you are submitting this form.
  - **Additional food restrictions** (`food_restrictions`, `text`, not required): If you have any additional food restrictions, please specify them here.
  - **Other medical conditions** (`other_medical_conditions`, `text`, not required): If you have any other medical conditions, please specify them here.
