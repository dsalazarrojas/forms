# Pranic Healing Clinic Session Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record detailed information about pranic healing clinic sessions, including session specifics, client data, and practitioner data. The form helps to streamline the process of recording and maintaining accurate session records.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Session ID, which is used to identify the unique identifier for the session.
2. Enter the Practitioner ID, which is the identifier for the practitioner performing the session.
3. Enter the Client ID, which is the identifier for the client receiving the session.
4. Choose the Session Date for the appointment.
5. Enter the Session Time for the start of the session.
6. Select the Session Duration, which is the length of the session.
7. Select the Session Status from the provided options, indicating the status of the session.
8. Enter the Practitioner Name, which is the name of the practitioner performing the session.
9. Enter the Client Name, which is the name of the client receiving the session.
10. Select the Client Gender from the options provided.
11. Choose the Age Range of the client.
12. Enter the Medical Record Number, which is the unique identifier for the client's medical record.
13. Enter the Session Location, which is the location where the session was held.
14. Enter Session Notes, which can be used to record additional comments or observations about the session.
15. Enter the Medical Condition, which is the specific health issue addressed during the session.
16. Enter the Symptoms, which are the specific issues the client reported during the session.
17. Enter the Treatment Plan, which outlines the steps taken during the session.
18. Select the Follow-up Reminders, which are reminders for future follow-up appointments.
19. Select the Follow-up Frequency, which is the schedule for future follow-up appointments.
20. Enter the Client Signature, which is the client's acknowledgement of the session details.
21. Enter the Practitioner Signature, which is the practitioner's verification of the session details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session ID** (1, Number, Optional): Enter a unique identifier for the session.
- **Practitioner ID** (2, Number, Optional): Enter the practitioner's identifier.
- **Client ID** (3, Number, Optional): Enter the client's identifier.
- **Session Date** (4, Date, Optional): Select the date of the session.
- **Session Time** (5, Time, Optional): Select the start time of the session.
- **Session Duration** (6, Number, Optional): Enter the length of the session.
- **Session Status** (7, Select One, Optional): Choose the status of the session from the options provided.
- **Practitioner Name** (8, Text, Optional): Enter the practitioner's name.
- **Client Name** (9, Text, Optional): Enter the client's name.
- **Client Gender** (10, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the client's gender from the options provided.
- **Age Range** (11, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the age range of the client.
- **Medical Record Number** (12, Text, Optional): Enter the client's medical record number.
- **Session Location** (13, Text, Optional): Enter the location of the session.
- **Session Notes** (14, Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the session.
- **Medical Condition** (15, Text, Optional): Enter the client's medical condition.
- **Symptoms** (16, Text, Optional): Enter the client's symptoms.
- **Treatment Plan** (17, Note, Optional): Enter the treatment plan for the client.
- **Follow-up Reminders** (18, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the frequency of follow-up reminders.
- **Follow-up Frequency** (19, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the schedule for future follow-up appointments.
- **Client Signature** (20, Text, Optional): Enter the client's signature to acknowledge the session details.
- **Practitioner Signature** (21, Text, Optional): Enter the practitioner's signature to verify the session details.
