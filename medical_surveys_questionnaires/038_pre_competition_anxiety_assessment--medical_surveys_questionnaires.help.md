# pre-competition-anxiety-assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "pre-competition-anxiety-assessment" form is used to assess a user's anxiety levels before a competition or challenging event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For text fields, type your answer in the space provided.
3. For numerical fields, select the number that best describes your current anxiety level.
4. For select_one, select one option from the list.
5. For select_multiple, select all applicable options.
6. For date and time fields, select the correct date and time from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you feel right now**: Please type a brief description of your current emotional state.
* **How do you feel right now**: This field is a duplicate of "How do you feel right now" and is intended for internal validation purposes.
* **What are your goals**: Describe your goals or objectives for the competition or event.
* **pre-competition-anxiety-assessment-3**: Rate your current anxiety level on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being very low and 10 being very high.
* **competition-level**: Rate your level of competitiveness on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being not competitive at all and 10 being highly competitive.
* **stress_level_6**: Describe any stressors or factors that are contributing to your anxiety level.
* **7**: Select the time of day when you feel most anxious.
* **8**: Select the time of day when you feel most relaxed or calm.
* **9**: Rate your current energy level on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being very low and 10 being very high.
* **11**: Select one option that best describes your current physical sensations.
* **email**: Enter your email address to receive any follow-up questions or results from the survey.
* **13**: Select all options that describe your current thoughts or self-statements.
* **14**: Select all options that describe your current physical sensations.
* **15**: Select all options that are true for you.
* **16**: Select the date of the competition or event.
* **17**: Select the time of the competition or event.
* **18**: Select the date of the last time you experienced anxiety similar to this.
* **19**: Select the time of day when you usually feel anxious.
* **20**: Select the time of day when you usually feel relaxed or calm.
* **21**: Select the date of the next competition or event.
* **22**: Select the time of the next competition or event.
* **23**: Select the date of the last time you experienced anxiety similar to this.
* **24**: Select the time of day when you usually feel anxious.
* **25**: Select one option that best describes your current self-statement.
