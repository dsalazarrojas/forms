# Infection Control KAP Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Infection Control KAP Survey is a tool to help assess the knowledge and practices of healthcare workers regarding infection control in a facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer based on your current role and responsibilities in the facility.
2. Answer all questions truthfully, even if you are unsure of the answer.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or infection control officer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a non-mandatory introductory section that provides context for the survey. Please read it carefully and continue with the survey.
* **What is your role in the facility?**: This question helps us understand your position and responsibilities in the facility. Please select the option that best describes your role.
* **What is the approximate number of patients you care for on a daily basis?**: This question helps us understand your workload and the potential risks associated with it. Please answer with a numerical value.
* **How often do you review the current infection prevention and control guidelines?**: This question helps us understand the frequency of your reviews. Please select all that apply.
* **How confident are you in your current knowledge of infection prevention and control?**: This question helps us understand your self-assessment of your knowledge. Please answer with a numerical value.
* **What method would you use to clean and disinfect surfaces in a patient room?**: This question helps us understand your practices. Please select the option that best describes your method.
* **When was your last update on infection prevention and control?**: This question helps us understand the currency of your knowledge. Please answer with a date (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
