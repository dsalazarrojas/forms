# Medical Simulation Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback about the effectiveness of medical simulation training sessions. It captures information about the session's purpose, location, and participants' evaluations to monitor and improve the training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Go to the form and click on the "Start" button to begin.
*   Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
*   For questions with multiple options, select the one that best fits your experience.
*   If a question does not apply to you, select "N/A" or explain why in the comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What was the training session about?**
    (`training_session`, `text`, required/optional): This question asks you to describe the topic or purpose of the training session you attended. Be as specific as possible to provide a clear understanding of the session's objectives.
*   **Did you take the medical survey?**
    (`medical_survey_results`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks you to confirm whether you took the medical survey during the session. Select "Yes" if you did, and "No" if you didn't.
*   **What type of simulation were you trained on?**
    (`simulation_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks you to identify the type of simulation you were trained on. Select the option that best matches your experience.
*   **What was the date of the training session?**
    (`training_date`, `date`, required/optional): This question asks for the date of the training session. Enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **What time did the training take place?**
    (`training_time`, `time`, required/optional): This question asks for the time of the training session. Enter the time in the format 12:00 PM or 13:00.
*   **Where was the training held?**
    (`training_location`, `text`, required/optional): This question asks for the location where the training took place.
*   **What were the results of the medical survey?**
    (`medical_survey_results_2`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks you to confirm whether the medical survey indicated a "Yes" or "No" response.
*   **How would you evaluate the training outcome?**
    (`evaluation_outcome`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks for your evaluation of the training's outcome. Select the option that best reflects your opinion.
*   **Were any comments provided for the training session?**
    (`evaluation_comments`, `text`, required/optional): This question is an open-ended text field for you to provide any comments or feedback on the training session.
*   **What tool was assigned to you for the training?**
    (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks you to select the tool that was assigned to you during the training session.
*   **Evaluation score out of 5**
    (`evaluation_score`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks you to evaluate the training session on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
*   **How would you evaluate the training outcome of the session?**
    (`training_outcome`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks you to evaluate the outcome of the training session.
*   **Were any comments made on the medical survey?**

*   **Did the medical survey indicate a yes or no response?**
