# Veterinary EMR - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary EMR form is designed to collect information about a client's visit to a veterinary clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Answer the questions about the client's details: Do you work for the veterinary group? (Page 1)
2. Enter the appointment time and date for the visit.
3. Provide information about the client and their pet: client_name, pet_name, pet_species, pet_breed, pet_age, client_phone, client_email.
4. Provide information about the physical examination: physical_exam_findings, treatment_sheet_findings.
5. Provide any medical history, allergies, and medications for the pet.
6. Enter the client's contact information: client_address, client_city, client_state, client_zip.
7. Enter the veterinarian's information: veterinarian_name, veterinarian_title, veterinarian_phone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do you work for the veterinary group?**: Check this box to indicate whether you work for the veterinary group.
* **appointment_time**: Enter the time of the appointment in the format `HH:MM` (e.g., 14:30).
* **appointment_date**: Enter the date of the appointment in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., 2022-02-15).
* **client_name**: Enter the client's full name.
* **pet_name**: Enter the pet's name.
* **pet_species**: Enter the type of pet (e.g., dog, cat, etc.).
* **pet_breed**: Enter the pet's breed (e.g., Labrador, Persian cat, etc.).
* **pet_age**: Enter the pet's age.
* **client_phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **client_email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **physical_exam_findings**: Enter the findings of the physical examination.
* **treatment_sheet_findings**: Enter the findings of the treatment sheet.
* **medical_history**: Enter any medical history for the pet.
* **allergies**: Enter any allergies the pet has.
* **medication**: Enter any medications the pet is currently taking.
* **medical_condition**: Enter any medical conditions the pet has.
* **client_address**: Enter the client's address.
* **client_city**: Enter the city where the client resides.
* **client_state**: Enter the state where the client resides.
* **client_zip**: Enter the client's zip code.
* **veterinary_group**: Enter any additional information about the veterinary group you work for (if applicable).
* **veterinary_license**: Enter your veterinary license number.
* **veterinarian_name**: Enter your name as a veterinarian.
* **veterinarian_title**: Enter your title as a veterinarian.
* **veterinarian_phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
