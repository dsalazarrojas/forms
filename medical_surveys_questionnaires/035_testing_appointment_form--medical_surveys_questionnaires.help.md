# Testing Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and plan a medical testing appointment. It captures relevant details such as patient information, appointment date and time, and doctor's availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter the suspected disease or condition in the "What do you suspect?" field.
2. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient name" field.
3. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Patient phone" field.
4. Select the date for the appointment in the "Appointment date" field.
5. Select the time for the appointment in the "Appointment time" field.
6. Enter the expected duration of the appointment in the "Appointment duration" field.
7. If the doctor is available, select "Yes" in the "Doctor name" field.
8. If the doctor's department is relevant, select "Yes" in the "Doctor department" field.
9. If you have any additional notes, enter them in the "Notes" field.
10. If the patient's email address is available, enter it in the "Email" field.
11. Check the patient's condition in the "Patient condition" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What do you suspect?** (`What do you suspect?`, text, optional): Enter the suspected disease or condition.
- **Patient name** (`Patient name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name.
- **Patient phone** (`Patient phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
- **Appointment date** (`Appointment date`, date, optional): Select the date for the appointment.
- **Appointment time** (`Appointment time`, time, optional): Select the time for the appointment.
- **Appointment duration** (`Appointment duration`, number, optional): Enter the expected duration of the appointment.
- **Doctor name** (`Doctor name`, select_one, optional): Check if the doctor is available.
- **Doctor department** (`Doctor department`, select_one, optional): Check if the doctor's department is relevant.
- **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes.
- **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
- **Patient condition** (`Patient condition`, select_one, optional): Check the patient's condition.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any) before submitting the form.
- Double-check the appointment date and time to ensure it's accurate.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask a medical professional for assistance.
