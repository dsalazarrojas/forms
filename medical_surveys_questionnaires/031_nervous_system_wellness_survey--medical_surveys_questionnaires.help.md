# Nervous System Wellness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Nervous System Wellness Survey is designed to assess an individual's nervous system wellness, focusing on stress management, physical and mental symptoms, social interactions, stress frequency, self-care practices, emotional support, and coping mechanisms. This tool helps healthcare professionals evaluate and understand the patient's current status, allowing for personalized recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each page carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. Select all applicable options for each field from the provided list.
3. Answer all required fields. Fields with a red asterisk * are required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stress Management** (stress-management, select_multiple, *): This section assesses your ability to manage stress. Select all the options that apply to you:
  * Anxiety
  * Burnout
  * Depression
  * Insomnia
  * Trauma
* **Physical Symptoms** (physical-symptoms, select_one, *): Choose one physical symptom you have experienced:
  * Fatigue
  * Headaches
  * Muscle aches
  * Nausea
  * Dizziness
* **Mental Symptoms** (mental-symptoms, select_one, *): Choose one mental symptom you have experienced:
  * Mood changes
  * Irritability
  * Memory loss
  * Concentration problems
* **Social Interactions** (social-interactions, number, *): Rate your social interactions:
  * This question is not applicable in this example, as the options list is empty. However, you can still rate your social interactions based on your personal experience.
* **Stress Frequency** (stress-frequency, select_one, *): Select how often you experience stress:
  * High
  * Medium
  * Low
* **Self-Care Practices** (self-care-practices, select_multiple, *): Select all self-care practices you regularly engage in:
  * Meditation
  * Exercise
  * Journaling
  * Mindfulness
* **Emotional Support** (emotional-support, select_multiple, *): Select all sources of emotional support you have:
  * Friends
  * Family
  * Therapist
  * Online support
* **Coping Mechanisms** (coping-mechanisms, select_multiple, *): Select all coping mechanisms you use:
  * Social media
  * Exercise
  * Hobbies
  * Relaxation
