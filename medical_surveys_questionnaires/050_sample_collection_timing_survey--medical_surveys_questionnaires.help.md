# Sample Collection Timing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about sample collection timing from participants. It will be used to collect data on when and where samples were collected, which will help us better understand timing patterns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the page that you would like to complete, which represents different sections of the survey.
2. For each question, provide your response in the required format (date, text, email, etc.).
3. Be thorough and accurate in your responses, as they will be used for analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Sample Collection Timing Survey Form

* **sample collection timing survey form** (optional): This is an introductory page that describes the survey and its purpose. You can read it to understand what the survey is about, but you do not need to fill in any information here.

### Page 2: Collection Date

* **Collection Date** (optional): This page asks you to enter the date when the sample collection took place. Please select a date in the provided format to provide accurate information.

### Page 3: Sample Location

* **Sample Location** (optional): Please enter the location where the sample was collected, such as a laboratory, field site, or any other relevant site.

### Page 4: Sample Time

* **Sample Time** (optional): Enter the time when the sample collection occurred. This will help us understand timing patterns. You can use 24-hour time format (e.g., 14:30).

### Page 5: Participant Name

* **Participant Name** (optional): If you are the participant who collected the sample, please enter your name as it appears on your identification.

### Page 6: Participant Email

* **Participant Email** (optional): If you have an email associated with your participant ID, please enter it here to help us contact you for further questions or clarification.
