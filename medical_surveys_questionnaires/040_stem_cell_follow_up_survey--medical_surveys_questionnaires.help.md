# Stem Cell Follow Up Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on the status of patients who have undergone stem cell treatment, providing medical professionals with data for research, treatment evaluation, or patient monitoring purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field, ensuring accuracy and thoroughness.
2. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Follow Up" field.
3. Submit the form by filling out the "Submission" field with your date of completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (Patient Name, text, required): Enter the patient's name as per the medical record.
- **Patient Email** (Patient Email, email, required): Enter the patient's email address (if available).
- **Health Status** (Health Status, select_one, required): Select the current health status of the patient:
	+ Excellent: Excellent health with minimal symptoms.
	+ Fair: Fair health with noticeable symptoms but manageable treatment.
	+ Poor: Poor health with significant symptoms and potential complications.
- **Treatment Dates** (Treatment Dates, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable dates for the patient's treatment:
	+ Date of Transplant: Date of the transplant.
	+ Transplant Date: Date of the transplant (alternative to "Date of Transplant").
	+ Last Treatment Date: Date of the last treatment.
	+ Treatment Start Date: Date treatment started.
	+ Treatment End Date: Date treatment ended.
- **Side Effects** (Side Effects, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable side effects experienced by the patient:
	+ Hair Loss: Hair loss or thinning.
	+ Skin Rash: Skin rash or irritation.
	+ Fatigue: Fatigue or tiredness.
- **Treatment Status** (Treatment Status, text, required): Provide a brief update on the patient's treatment status (e.g., ongoing, completed, discontinued).
- **Follow Up** (Follow Up, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for the patient's follow-up.
- **Submission** (Submission, date, required): Enter the date of completion of the survey.
