# prophets_health_assessment_for_chronic_conditions_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Prophets Health Assessment for Chronic Conditions Form** is a tool used to gather information about patients with chronic conditions. This form helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's medical status, symptoms, medications, medical history, lifestyle, and interactions with medical professionals.

## How To Complete This Form

### To start, fill in the required fields:

- **Conditions**: Enter a description of your chronic condition(s) (e.g., diabetes, hypertension, etc.)
- **Medical Professionals**: Select the medical professionals you interact with (e.g., doctor, nurse, etc.)
- **Patient Status**: Choose your current status (Active or Inactive)
- **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking

### For the optional fields:

- **Symptoms**: Describe any symptoms you are currently experiencing
- **Lifestyle**: Share information about your daily routine and habits
- **Follow-up Frequency**: Provide the frequency of your follow-up appointments

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conditions**: This field is where you will enter a description of your chronic condition(s). For example, "I have diabetes and hypertension."
* **Symptoms**: Describe any symptoms you are currently experiencing, such as pain, fatigue, etc.
* **Medical Professionals**: Select all medical professionals you interact with, such as doctors, nurses, etc.
* **Patient Status**: Choose your current status: Active (you are currently under treatment) or Inactive (you are not currently under treatment)
* **Medical History**: Please list any medical history you have, including previous diagnoses and surgeries.
* **Medications**: List all medications you are currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Lifestyle**: Share information about your daily routine and habits, such as diet, exercise, etc.
* **Follow-up Frequency**: Provide the frequency of your follow-up appointments (e.g., monthly, quarterly, etc.)
* **Medical Professionals**: Select all medical professionals you interact with, such as doctors, nurses, etc.
