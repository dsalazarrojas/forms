# Dermal Filler Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dermal Filler Consultation Form is designed to collect information from patients for a dermal filler consultation. This form is used to gather details about the patient's medical history, skin concerns, and personal characteristics to help medical professionals provide accurate and effective treatment recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring that all required fields are completed accurately.
2. Provide truthful and comprehensive information about your medical history, skin concerns, and personal details.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are completed correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please provide your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your valid email address where you can be contacted for further information or to receive consultation results.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, required): Describe any significant medical conditions, allergies, or previous medical procedures you have had.
* **Skin Concerns** (`skin_concerns`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following skin concerns:
	+ Acne
	+ Age spots
	+ Fine lines
	+ Hyper-pigmentation
	+ Redness
	+ Skin laxity
	+ Wrinkles
	+ Sun damage
* **Medical Practice** (`medical_practice`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all medical practices that you have visited or consulted:
	+ Dermatologist
	+ Medical spa
	+ Plastic surgeon
	+ Skincare clinic
	+ Other
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following medical licenses:
	+ Doctor
	+ Nurse
	+ Medical student
	+ Other
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all allergies or sensitivities you have:
	+ Medication
	+ Food
	+ Environmental
	+ Other
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following medical conditions:
	+ Diabetes
	+ Hypertension
	+ High Cholesterol
	+ Other
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required): List all medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical History Notes** (`medical_history_notes`, `note`, required): Provide any additional medical history or notes that are not listed above.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following skin types:
	+ Combination
	+ Dry
	+ Normal
	+ Oily
	+ Sensitive
* **Skin Tone** (`skin_tone`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following skin tones:
	+ Fair
	+ Medium
	+ Olive
	+ Tan
	+ Tanned
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Enter your age.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Birthplace** (`birthplace`, `text`, required): Enter your birthplace.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your current address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, `select_one`, required): Select if you have medical insurance:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following blood pressure levels:
	+ Low
	+ Normal
	+ High
* **Height/Weight** (`height_weight`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one of the following height/weight status:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Medical History Photos** (`medical_history_photo_urls`, `note`, required): Upload any relevant medical history photos.
* **Medical History Documentation** (`medical_history_documentation`, `note`, required): Upload any relevant medical history documentation.
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, `select_one`, required): Select if you have read and agreed to the consent form:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name above your date of signature.

Please review this form carefully and ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
