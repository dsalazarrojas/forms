# Online Training Consultation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your goals, motivations, and health considerations for the online training consultation. This information will be used to create an individualized training plan tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question thoroughly and accurately. Make sure to:
* Select one of the options for each multiple-choice question.
* Enter your text responses in the space provided for each text field.
* Answer every question, as indicated by the "required" label next to each field.
* Don't worry about the length of your text responses, but provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What is the primary reason for the online training consultation?
* **Primary Reason for Training** (`primary_reason_for_training`, `select_one`, required): This question helps us understand your motivation for seeking online training. Please select the primary reason that best describes your goal.

### What are your short-term goals for the training?
* **Short-term Goals** (`short_term_goals`, `text`, required): Describe your immediate objectives for the training. What do you hope to achieve in the short-term?

### What are your long-term goals for the training?
* **Long-term Goals** (`long_term_goals`, `text`, required): Describe your long-term objectives for the training. What do you hope to achieve in the long-term?

### How will you achieve your short-term and long-term goals?
* **How will you Achieve your Goals** (`achieve_goals`, `text`, required): Explain how you plan to reach your short-term and long-term objectives.

### Have you received any medical clearance for the training?
* **Medical Clearance** (`medical_clearance`, `select_multiple`, required): This question is about whether you have received medical clearance or not. Please select "True" if you have received clearance, "False" if you have not.

### What are your health details (e.g., medications, allergies, injuries, etc.)?
* **Health Details** (`health_details`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant health details that might impact your training. This might include any medications, allergies, or injuries you're currently dealing with.

### What is your current progress report?
* **Progress Report** (`progress_report`, `text`, required): Share your current status or progress towards your goals. This will help us understand your current situation and tailor the training accordingly.

Please fill out this form carefully and thoroughly, and remember to answer every question. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
