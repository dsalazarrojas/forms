# PCOS Health Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PCOS Health Assessment Questionnaire is a comprehensive tool designed to help assess the severity and impact of polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) symptoms on a patient's health. This form gathers information about the patient's demographic details, menstrual history, medical history, and other relevant factors to better understand their condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the demographic details (questions 1-2) and medical history (questions 3-5) as accurately as possible.
3. Answer the menstrual history questions (questions 6-8) based on your menstrual cycle characteristics.
4. Indicate the severity of your menstrual cramps (question 9) and the frequency of heavy bleeding days (question 10).
5. Select the correct severity of your menstrual symptoms (question 12).
6. Provide any relevant medical history, surgery history, hospitalization history, blood pressure, blood glucose, thyroid medication, thyroid history, and other relevant information (questions 13-19).
7. If applicable, provide information about your fertility history and family members' health (questions 21-24).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (question 1):
	+ A brief description of your PCOS experience or concerns.
* **Demographic Info** (question 2):
	+ Your age, height, weight, and other demographic details that may be relevant to your PCOS assessment.
* **Menstrual Info** (question 3):
	+ Your menstrual cycle characteristics, including frequency, flow, and cyclicity.
* **Symptom Frequency** (question 4):
	+ The frequency of your PCOS symptoms, such as acne, hirsutism, or other related symptoms.
* **Medication Info** (question 5):
	+ Any medication you're taking or have taken for your PCOS symptoms or related conditions.
* **Menstrual Olig** (question 6):
	+ The number of days between your menstrual cycles.
* **Menstrual Flow** (question 7):
	+ A description of your menstrual flow, including heaviness and duration.
* **Menstrual Cyclicity** (question 8):
	+ The length of your menstrual cycle, measured in days.
* **Menstrual Cramps** (question 9):
	+ The severity of your menstrual cramps, using the options provided.
* **Menstrual Duration** (question 10):
	+ The number of days you experience heavy bleeding during your cycle.
* **Menstrual Severity** (question 11):
	+ The severity of your menstrual symptoms, using the options provided.
* **Medical History** (question 12):
	+ Any relevant medical history, including conditions or treatments you've had.
* **Surgery History** (question 13):
	+ Any surgical procedures you've had related to your PCOS or other health conditions.
* **Hospitalization History** (question 14):
	+ Any hospitalizations related to your PCOS or other health conditions.
* **Blood Pressure** (question 15):
	+ Your blood pressure reading, if applicable.
* **Blood Glucose** (question 16):
	+ Your blood glucose reading, if applicable.
* **Thyroid Medication** (question 17):
	+ Any thyroid medication you're taking or have taken.
* **Thyroid History** (question 18):
	+ Any thyroid conditions or treatments you've had.
* **Menstrual History** (question 19):
	+ A description of your menstrual history, including any irregularities or conditions.
* **Fertility History** (question 20):
	+ Any fertility-related conditions or treatments you've had.
* **Family Members** (question 21):
	+ A list of family members with PCOS or related conditions.
* **Family Member 1** (question 22):
	+ Information about your first family member with PCOS or related conditions.
* **Family Member 2** (question 23):
	+ Information about your second family member with PCOS or related conditions.
* **Family Member 3** (question 24):
	+ Information about your third family member with PCOS or related conditions.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any question, consult with a medical professional for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you've completed it accurately and thoroughly.

This guide should provide a clear understanding of the form's purpose, how to complete it, and what each field means. If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact a medical professional for guidance.
