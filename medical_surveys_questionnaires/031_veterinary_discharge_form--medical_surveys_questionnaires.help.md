# Veterinary Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Discharge Form is used to document the details of a pet's discharge from a veterinary facility. This form ensures that all necessary information is recorded for the pet's owner and for the veterinary staff's reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter the pet's name in the "Pet Name" field.
* Enter the owner's name in the "Owner Name" field.
* If applicable, enter the owner's phone number in the "Owner Phone" field.
* Enter the date of discharge in the "Discharge Date" field.
* Enter the date and time of discharge in the "Discharge Date-Time" field (if applicable).
* Write down any specific instructions for the owner and the pet in the "Discharge Instructions" field.
* Describe the pet's current condition in the "Pet Condition" field.
* If applicable, enter any follow-up treatment instructions in the "Follow-up Treatment" field.
* If applicable, enter any medication that was given to the pet in the "Medication Given" field.
* Enter the instructions for administering medication in the "Medication Instructions" field.
* If applicable, enter any medical history for the pet in the "Medical History" field.
* List any allergies the pet has in the "Allergies" field.
* Enter any special instructions for the owner in the "Special Instructions" field.
* Enter the name of the veterinary staff member who is responsible for the pet's care in the "Veterinary Staff" field (if applicable).
* Enter any additional comments or notes from the veterinary staff in the "Veterinary Notes" field (if applicable).
* Enter a discharge note from the veterinary staff in the "Discharge Note" field.
* If applicable, enter the contact information for follow-up in the "Follow-up Contact" field.
* Enter the name of the person responsible for follow-up contact in the "Follow-up Contact Person" field (if applicable).
* Enter any comments or notes for follow-up contact in the "Follow-up Comment" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Name** (text, required): The name of the pet.
* **Owner Name** (text, required): The name of the owner of the pet.
* **Owner Phone** (text, optional): The phone number of the owner.
* **Discharge Date** (date, required): The date of discharge.
* **Next Visit** (date, optional): The date and time of the next scheduled visit (if applicable).
* **Discharge Instructions** (text, required): Specific instructions for the owner and the pet.
* **Pet Condition** (text, required): The current condition of the pet.
* **Follow-up Treatment** (text, optional): Any follow-up treatment instructions.
* **Medication Given** (text, required): The medication given to the pet.
* **Medication Instructions** (text, required): Instructions for administering medication.
* **Medical History** (text, optional): The medical history of the pet.
* **Allergies** (text, required): Any allergies the pet has.
* **Special Instructions** (text, required): Any special instructions for the owner.
* **Veterinary Staff** (text, optional): The name of the veterinary staff member responsible for the pet's care.
* **Veterinary Notes** (text, optional): Notes from the veterinary staff.
* **Discharge Note** (text, required): A note from the veterinary staff.
* **Follow-up Contact** (text, required): Contact information for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Contact Person** (text, optional): The name of the person responsible for follow-up contact.
* **Follow-up Comment** (text, optional): Comments or notes for follow-up contact.
* **Discharge Date-Time** (time, optional): The date and time of discharge.
* **Veterinary Notes** (text, optional): Additional comments or notes from the veterinary staff.
