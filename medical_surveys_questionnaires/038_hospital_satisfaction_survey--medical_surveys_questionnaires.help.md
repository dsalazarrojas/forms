# Hospital Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides a step-by-step explanation on how to fill the Hospital Satisfaction Survey form, which is used to gather feedback from patients about their experiences with the hospital staff, clinics, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the survey questions and select the answers that best describe your experience.
2. Provide honest and accurate feedback about your visit to the hospital.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields as indicated in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with the quality of hospital staff?** (`staff_satisfaction`, select_one, required)
	+ This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the quality of care provided by the hospital staff during your visit.
* **How satisfied are you with the clinics?** (`clinics_satisfaction`, select_one, required)
	+ This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the clinics you visited during your stay at the hospital.
* **What is your overall satisfaction score for the patient?** (`patient_satisfaction`, select_one, required)
	+ This question asks for your rating of your overall satisfaction with the hospital.
* **How many clinics have you visited in the hospital recently?** (`visited_clinics`, number, required)
	+ Please enter the number of clinics you have visited in the hospital recently.
* **What is the date of your last visit?** (`last_visit_date`, date, required)
	+ Please enter the date of your last visit to the hospital in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **What is the time of your last visit?** (`last_visit_time`, time, required)
	+ Please enter the time of your last visit to the hospital in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, required)
	+ Please enter your email address as it appears on your hospital records.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required)
	+ Please enter your phone number as it appears on your hospital records.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required)
	+ Please provide any additional comments or feedback about your visit to the hospital.
* **Hospital Satisfaction Score** (`hospital_score`, number, required)
	+ Please enter a number between 1 and 5 to rate your overall satisfaction with the hospital's services.
