# Glaucoma And Vision Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's glaucoma and vision screening history to inform diagnosis and treatment decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Review each page of the form carefully to ensure you answer every question accurately.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided for each page.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, even if you are unsure or don't know the information.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure or don't know the information, please note it in the corresponding field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
* This form is designed to be completed in a clinical setting, so please do not submit it online or use it for general public use.
