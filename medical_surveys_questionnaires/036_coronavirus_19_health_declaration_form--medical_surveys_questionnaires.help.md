# coronavirus_19_health_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an individual's health status and potential exposure to COVID-19 for public health surveillance and outbreak tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient identifier (ID number or other unique identifier) in the "Patient Identifier" field.
2. If you have not been assigned an ID number, provide a brief note in the "Patient Identifier Note" field.
3. Answer all the required questions, starting with "Have you experienced a fever in the past 2 weeks?" and "Have you experienced a cough in the past 2 weeks?". These questions are essential for public health tracking.
4. Provide any additional information as required, such as your travel history, contact history, and exposure history.
5. Answer the optional questions, including "Do you have any underlying medical conditions?", "Are you taking any medication?", and "Have you had a previous test for COVID-19?".
6. Indicate your symptoms experienced in the past 2 weeks and any reported symptoms.
7. Check if you have been hospitalized and provide your emergency contact information.
8. Complete the travel-related fields, including "Travel destination", "Travel date", "Return date", and "Travel duration".
9. Select your travel mode and provide your contact number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Identifier** (1, number, required): Enter your patient identifier (ID number or other unique identifier).
* **Medical Organization** (2, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical organization that assigned you an ID number.
* **Have you experienced a fever in the past 2 weeks?** (3, select_one, required): Answer with Option 1 or Option 2.
* **Have you experienced a cough in the past 2 weeks?** (4, select_one, required): Answer with Option 1 or Option 2.
* **Travel history to affected area** (5, text, optional): Describe your travel history to affected areas.
* **Contact history with confirmed coronavirus-19 case** (6, text, optional): Describe any contact history with confirmed COVID-19 cases.
* **Exposure history to confirmed coronavirus-19 case** (7, text, optional): Describe any exposure history to confirmed COVID-19 cases.
* **Do you have any underlying medical condition?** (8, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply.
* **Are you taking any medication?** (9, select_one, optional): Answer with Option 1 or Option 2.
* **Have you had a previous test for coronavirus-19?** (10, select_one, optional): Answer with Option 1 or Option 2.
* **Symptoms experienced in past 2 weeks** (11, select_multiple, optional): Indicate any symptoms you have experienced.
* **Symptoms reported** (12, select_multiple, optional): Indicate any reported symptoms.
* **Have you been hospitalized?** (13, select_one, optional): Answer with Option 1 or Option 2.
* **Emergency Contact** (14, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Patient Identifier Note** (15, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your patient identifier.
* **Exposure Risk Level** (16, select_one, required): Select your exposure risk level.
* **Travel destination** (17, text, optional): Enter your travel destination.
* **Travel date** (18, date, required): Enter the date of your travel.
* **Return date** (19, date, required): Enter the date of your return.
* **Travel duration** (20, number, optional): Enter the duration of your travel.
* **Travel country** (21, text, required): Enter the country of your travel.
* **Travel mode** (22, select_one, required): Select your travel mode.
* **Contact number** (23, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact email** (24, email, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Patient Identifier Note** (25, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your patient identifier.

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields carefully and truthfully to ensure accurate public health surveillance and outbreak tracking.
* If you are unsure about any question or field, please contact the medical organization that assigned you an ID number or the emergency contact person listed.
* If you have any additional information or clarification for any question or field, please provide it in the "Patient Identifier Note" field.
