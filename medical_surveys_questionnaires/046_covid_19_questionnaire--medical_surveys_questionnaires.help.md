# COVID 19 Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to collect information about your COVID-19 status, household dynamics, and support needs during the pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Respond to each question honestly and accurately.
2. Answer all required questions.
3. For questions with multiple options, select the one that best applies to you.
4. If you have any difficulty understanding a question or need assistance, please contact us.
