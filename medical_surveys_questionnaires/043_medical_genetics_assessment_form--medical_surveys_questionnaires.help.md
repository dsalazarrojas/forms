# Medical Genetics Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Genetics Assessment Form is a tool for healthcare professionals to collect information from patients or families about their genetic history, test results, and other relevant data. This form is designed to aid in the assessment and management of genetic conditions and traits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form instructions carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and scope of the assessment.
2. Complete each section of the form thoroughly and accurately.
3. Answer all required fields and provide as much detail as possible for optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient History**: (<name>, <type>: text) - Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous diagnoses, treatments, and relevant medical conditions.
* **Family History**: (<name>, <type>: text) - Provide information about the patient's family history, including any known genetic conditions, medical history, or other relevant data.
* **Consent**: (<name>, <type>: select_one) - Indicate whether the patient agrees to share genetic test results with other family members. Options include:
	+ Patient agrees to share genetic test results with other family members
	+ Patient does not agree to share genetic test results with other family members
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Genetic Test Results**: (<name>, <type>: note) - Record any relevant genetic test results, such as DNA sequencing or genetic mutation data.
* **Patient Notes**: (<name>, <type>: text) - Add any additional notes or comments about the patient's condition or medical history.
* **Family Tree**: (<name>, <type>: text) - Describe the patient's family tree, including relationships and relevant medical history.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have obtained informed consent from the patient before completing this form.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- Consult with a healthcare professional if you are unsure about any aspect of the form or its use.
