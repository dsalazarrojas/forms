## Constipation Chief Complaint Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Constipation Chief Complaint Intake Form is designed to collect essential information about your constipation-related complaints and symptoms. This form helps our healthcare team understand your condition and provides the necessary details to provide you with the best care possible.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow the instructions below to complete the form accurately and thoroughly:

1. Enter your Patient Full Name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter your Date of Birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. Select your correct Gender Identity.
4. Enter your contact phone number so we can reach you for appointments and follow-ups.
5. Enter your email address for appointment confirmations and follow-ups.
6. Describe how long you have experienced constipation symptoms.
7. Indicate how often you have bowel movements.
8. Indicate the typical consistency of your stool.
9. Answer if you experience straining during bowel movements.
10. Select all associated symptoms you are experiencing.
11. Rate your pain level from 0 to 10.
12. If you wish, describe your typical daily diet.
13. Indicate your daily fluid intake.
14. Select how active you are on a typical day.
15. If applicable, list all your current medications and supplements.
16. If applicable, mention any relevant medical history.
17. If applicable, describe any previous treatments you have tried.
18. Indicate the urgency level of your condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender identity from the options provided.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your best phone number for us to reach you.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for appointment confirmations and follow-ups.
* **How Long Have You Experienced Constipation** (`symptom_duration`, select_one, required): Select how long you have been experiencing constipation symptoms.
* **Bowel Movement Frequency** (`bowel_movement_frequency`, select_one, required): Indicate how often you have bowel movements.
* **Stool Consistency** (`stool_consistency`, select_one, required): Describe the typical consistency of your stool.
* **Do You Experience Straining** (`straining_required`, select_one, required): Answer if you experience straining during bowel movements.
* **Associated Symptoms** (`associated_symptoms`, select_multiple, required): Select all symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Pain Level** (`pain_level`, number, required): Rate your pain level from 0 to 10.
* **Dietary Habits** (`dietary_habits`, text, optional): If you wish, describe your typical daily diet.
* **Daily Fluid Intake** (`fluid_intake`, select_one, required): Indicate your daily fluid intake.
* **Physical Activity Level** (`physical_activity`, select_one, required): Select how active you are on a typical day.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): If applicable, list all your current medications and supplements.
* **Relevant Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): If applicable, mention any conditions like diabetes, thyroid issues, IBS.
* **Previous Treatments Tried** (`previous_treatments`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any previous treatments you have tried.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Indicate the urgency level of your condition.

**Tips**

* Please answer each question accurately and thoroughly to ensure we provide the best care possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact our office.
* This form will be kept confidential and secure in accordance with HIPAA regulations.
