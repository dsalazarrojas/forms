# sepsis_assessment_for_pregnant_women - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess sepsis in pregnant women, a severe and potentially life-threatening condition that requires immediate attention. It is intended to gather essential information about a pregnant woman's condition to identify potential risk factors and signs of sepsis.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that the patient's condition meets the inclusion criteria for sepsis.
2. Fill out the form with the patient's information, including:
	* Date of Admission (page 1)
	* Patient ID (page 1)
	* Provider ID (page 1)
	* Body Temperature (page 2)
	* Respiratory Rate (page 2)
	* Pulse Oximetry (page 2)
	* Blood Pressure (page 3)
	* Vital Signs Respiratory Rate (page 3)
	* Vital Signs Pulse Oximetry (page 3)
	* Vital Signs Body Temperature (page 3)
	* White Count (page 3)
	* Sepsis Definition (page 4)
	* Organ Damage (page 4)
	* Mechanism (page 4)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of Admission** (1): Enter the date of the patient's admission to the hospital.
* **Patient ID** (2): Enter the patient's identification number or other unique identifier.
* **Provider ID** (3): Enter the healthcare provider's identification number or other unique identifier.
* **Body Temperature** (4): Enter the patient's body temperature in degrees Celsius (°C) or Fahrenheit (°F).
* **Respiratory Rate** (5): Enter the patient's respiratory rate in breaths per minute.
* **Pulse Oximetry** (6): Enter the patient's oxygen saturation level as measured by pulse oximetry, expressed as a percentage.
* **Blood Pressure** (7): Enter the patient's blood pressure in millimeters of mercury (mmHg) or other unit of measurement.
* **Vital Signs Respiratory Rate** (8): Enter the patient's respiratory rate in breaths per minute.
* **Vital Signs Pulse Oximetry** (9): Enter the patient's oxygen saturation level as measured by pulse oximetry, expressed as a percentage.
* **Vital Signs Body Temperature** (10): Enter the patient's body temperature in degrees Celsius (°C) or Fahrenheit (°F).
* **White Count** (11): Enter the patient's white count in thousands per cubic millimeter (thousands/mm³).
* **Sepsis Definition** (12): Describe the patient's condition in relation to sepsis, such as "suspected sepsis" or "sepsis confirmed."
* **Organ Damage** (13): Describe any organ damage or dysfunction associated with the patient's condition.
* **Mechanism** (14): Describe the potential mechanism or cause of the patient's condition.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to provide the best possible assessment of the patient's condition.
