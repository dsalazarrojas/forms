# orthopaedic_surgery_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on a patient's experience with orthopaedic surgery, allowing healthcare providers to evaluate and improve the quality of care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Please answer all the questions honestly and provide detailed responses where possible.
2. Be as specific as you can when describing your experience.
3. If you need help or clarification on any question, do not hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is this survey for?** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is an introductory question that explains the purpose and relevance of the survey. Please read this carefully and ensure you understand the context before proceeding.
* **Patient Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): This field asks for the patient's name, which will be used to identify your experience.
* **Surgery Date** (`3`, `date`, required: false): This field asks for the date of your surgery, which helps healthcare providers track the timing of your experience.
* **Surgery Time** (`4`, `time`, required: false): This field asks for the time of your surgery, which can help providers understand the schedule and workflow of their services.
* **Surgeon Name** (`5`, `text`, required: false): This field asks for the name of the surgeon who performed your surgery, which can help providers understand their individual performance.
* **Overall Experience** (`6`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to rate your overall experience with the surgery, using your own words.
* **Staff Communication** (`7`, `text`, required: false): This field asks you to describe how well the staff communicated with you throughout your experience.
* **Pain Management** (`8`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to rate your experience with pain management during and after your surgery.
* **Anesthesia** (`9`, `text`, required: false): This field asks for your experience with anesthesia, including any complications or concerns you may have had.
* **Recovery Room** (`10`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to describe your experience in the recovery room, including any comfort, pain, or other concerns.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`11`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to rate your overall satisfaction with the surgery and recovery experience.
* **Comments** (`12`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Hospital Environment** (`13`, `text`, required: false): This field asks for your thoughts on the hospital environment, including cleanliness, organization, and other factors.
* **Nurse Attendance** (`14`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to rate your experience with nurse attendance, including how often and how effective they checked on you.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses, as they will help healthcare providers improve the quality of care.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please do not hesitate to share them in the "Comments" field.
* If you have already completed this form, please do not submit duplicate responses.
