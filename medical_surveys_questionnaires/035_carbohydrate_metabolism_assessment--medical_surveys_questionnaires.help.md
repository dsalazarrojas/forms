# Carbohydrate Metabolism Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Carbohydrate Metabolism Assessment form is designed to collect data on clients' carbohydrate intake and metabolism to help identify potential areas of concern and provide personalized recommendations for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your personal information, including your name, date of birth, email, and phone number.
2. Answer the questions in the following sections:
   * Client Information (fields 2-5)
   * Health History (fields 6-8)
   * Carbohydrate Intake (fields 11-13)
   * Blood Sugar Symptoms (fields 16-19)
   * Activity and Lifestyle (fields 22-25)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your name** (Field 2, text, required): Enter your full name for reference purposes.
* **Date of birth** (Field 3, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email address** (Field 4, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone number** (Field 5, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Current medical conditions** (Field 7, text, optional): List any medical conditions you have, such as diabetes, pre-diabetes, or thyroid issues.
* **Current medications** (Field 8, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Family history of diabetes or metabolic issues?** (Field 9, select_one, optional): Answer whether your family has a history of diabetes or metabolic issues.
* **Details of family history** (Field 10, text, optional): Provide more information about your family history.
* **Estimate your daily carbohydrate intake** (Field 12, number, optional): Enter your estimated daily carb intake in grams.
* **Primary carbohydrate sources (select all that apply)** (Field 13, select_multiple, optional): Choose the primary sources of your carbohydrate intake.
* **How often do you consume refined carbohydrates?** (Field 14, select_one, required): Select how often you consume refined carbs.
* **Estimate daily fiber intake** (Field 15, number, optional): Enter your estimated daily fiber intake in grams.
* **Do you experience fatigue or low energy?** (Field 17, select_one, required): Answer how frequently you experience fatigue or low energy.
* **When do you experience energy dips?** (Field 18, select_multiple, optional): Choose when you experience energy dips.
* **Sugar or carb cravings frequency** (Field 19, select_one, required): Select how frequently you have sugar or carb cravings.
* **When do cravings typically occur?** (Field 20, text, optional): Describe when your cravings typically occur.
* **Mood swings or irritability related to eating?** (Field 21, select_one, optional): Answer whether you experience mood swings or irritability related to eating.
* **Exercise frequency per week** (Field 23, number, optional): Enter your exercise frequency per week.
* **Average exercise duration** (Field 24, number, optional): Enter your average exercise duration in minutes.
* **Sleep quality assessment** (Field 25, select_one, optional): Select your sleep quality assessment.

Note: The fields marked as "required" must be completed to submit the form.
