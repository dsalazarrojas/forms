# Human Albumin Usage Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the usage of albumin in different departments, clinics, and labs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. In the "Form Title" field, enter a brief title for your survey.
2. In the "Description" field, provide a short description of your survey.
3. In the "Category" field, select the appropriate category for your survey (e.g., "Medical Surveys & Questionnaires").
4. In the "Albumin Use" field, select the areas where albumin is used (e.g., "Across Departments", "Clinics", "Labs").
5. In the "Frequency" field, select how often albumin is used (e.g., "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly", or "Never").
6. In the "Staff Feedback" field, provide any feedback or comments from staff members related to albumin usage.
7. In the "Comments" field, provide any additional comments or feedback you have.
8. In the "Output File" field, select the format you want the output to be in (e.g., "PDF", "CSV", or "Excel").
9. In the "Assigned Tool" field, indicate if there's a specific tool assigned for this use case, and enter the name or description of the tool if provided.
