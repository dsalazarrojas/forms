# Vivitrol Enrollment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vivitrol Enrollment Survey is designed to collect essential information from patients enrolling in the Vivitrol program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient details section with your personal information.
2. Provide any relevant medical history, including allergies and medical conditions.
3. Confirm your consent status.
4. Enter the date and frequency of your planned follow-up appointments.
5. Provide details about your treatment plan.
6. Enter the start and end dates of your treatment plan.
7. Identify your insurance provider.
8. Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
9. Add any additional information that may be helpful for your healthcare provider.
10. Leave a note for your healthcare provider with any relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Patient Details**: This is the patient's demographic information, including name, address, and contact information.
* **2. Medical History**: Please provide any medical history, including allergies and medical conditions, that may impact your treatment.
* **3. Consent**: Confirm that you have given your consent to participate in the Vivitrol program.
* **4. Follow Up Date**: Enter the date of your planned follow-up appointment.
* **5. Follow Up Provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who will be following up with you.
* **6. Treatment Plan Details**: Provide detailed information about your treatment plan.
* **7. Treatment Plan Frequency**: Specify the frequency of your treatment plan, such as how often you will receive treatment.
* **8. Treatment Plan Start Date**: Enter the start date of your treatment plan.
* **9. Treatment Plan End Date**: Enter the end date of your treatment plan.
* **10. Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **11. Patient Phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **12. Additional Info**: Add any additional information that may be helpful for your healthcare provider.
* **13. Notes**: Leave a note for your healthcare provider with any relevant details.
