# Female BioTe Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential health information from female patients, including their medical history, current health status, and other personal details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please provide the required information accurately and thoroughly.
2.  Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3.  Use the provided options for select fields to ensure correct choices.
4.  If a field is not required, you can leave it blank or provide additional information if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required): Enter your birth date in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your current age.
*   **Weight** (`weight`, number, required): Enter your weight in kilograms or pounds.
*   **Height** (`height`, number, required): Enter your height in centimeters or inches.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for secure communication.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Describe your current medical conditions, if any.
*   **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): List your current medications, if any.
*   **Health History** (`health_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief overview of your health history, if any.
*   **Health Insurance Provider** (`health_insurance_provider`, select_one, required): Choose your health insurance provider from the provided options.
*   **Primary Care Physician** (`primary_care_physician`, select_one, required): Choose your primary care physician from the provided options.
*   **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, number, optional): Enter your blood pressure reading.
*   **Body Mass Index (BMI)** (`body_mass_index`, number, optional): Calculate and enter your BMI.
*   **Blood Glucose** (`blood_glucose`, number, optional): Enter your blood glucose reading.
*   **Blood Lipid** (`blood_lipid`, number, optional): Enter your blood lipid reading.
*   **Medical Insurance Status** (`medical_insurance_status`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your current medical insurance status.
*   **Medical History Date** (`medical_history_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last medical history check-up.
*   **Medical History Details** (`medical_history_details`, text, optional): Describe your medical history, if any.
*   **Menstrual Cycle** (`menstrual_cycle`, select_one, optional): Indicate your menstrual cycle type.
*   **Medical Condition Status** (`medical_condition_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate your current medical condition status.
