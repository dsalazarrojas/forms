# Male BioTe Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Male BioTe Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information from male patients about their personal and medical characteristics. This form is used to gather demographic and medical data that may be relevant to treatment suitability or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through the form to understand what information is being collected.
2. Fill in your name.
3. Enter your age.
4. Answer the medical conditions questions (Yes/No).
5. List any medications you are currently taking.
6. List any past medications you have taken.
7. Provide a brief description of your surgery history.
8. Answer the family history question (Yes/No).
9. List any medical conditions or allergies you have.
10. Submit the form once you have filled in all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Age** (Number, Required): Enter your age.
* **Medical Conditions** (Yes/No, Optional): Answer 'Yes' if you have any medical conditions such as diabetes, high blood pressure, or other medical issues. Answer 'No' otherwise.
* **Medications** (Yes/No, Optional): Answer 'Yes' if you are currently taking any medications. Answer 'No' otherwise.
* **Current Medications** (Select Multiple, Optional): If you answered 'Yes' to the previous question, list any current medications you are taking.
* **Past Medications** (Select Multiple, Optional): If you have a medical history, list any medications you have taken in the past.
* **Surgery History** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of any surgeries you have undergone.
* **Family History** (Yes/No, Optional): Answer 'Yes' if you have a family history of medical conditions. Answer 'No' otherwise.
* **Medical History** (Yes/No, Optional): Answer 'Yes' if you have any other medical history. Answer 'No' otherwise.
* **Allergies** (Yes/No, Optional): Answer 'Yes' if you have any allergies. Answer 'No' otherwise.
