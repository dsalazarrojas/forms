# Public Perception Of Health AI Tools Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information on the public perception of health AI tools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the form in the "Public Perception Of Health AI Tools Survey" field.
2. Choose the category for the form from the options provided ("Medical Surveys & Questionnaires" or "Other").
3. Provide a brief description of the form in the "description" field (optional).
4. Choose a form category from the options provided ("Medical Surveys & Questionnaires" or "Other").
5. Enter any tags for the form in the "tags" field (optional).
6. Enter the version of the form in the "version" field (optional).
7. Enter the author of the form in the "author" field (optional).
8. Provide the date the form was created in the "created_at" field (optional).
9. Provide a unique form ID in the "form_id" field (optional).
10. Choose the status of the form from the options provided ("active", "inactive", or "draft").
11. Choose the assigned tool from the options provided ("chatjimmy" or "other").
12. Enter the output file name in the "output_file" field (optional).
13. Enter the output file path in the "output_file_path" field (optional).
14. Enter the name of the assigned tool in the "assigned_tool_name" field (optional).
15. Enter the email of the assigned tool in the "assigned_tool_email" field.
16. Provide any additional notes in the "notes" field (optional).
17. Provide additional notes in the "Form Notes 2" field (optional).
18. Provide additional notes in the "Form Notes 3" field (optional).
19. Provide any additional notes for the assigned tool in the "assigned_tool_notes" field (optional).
20. Provide additional notes for the assigned tool in the "Assigned Tool Notes 2" field (optional).
21. Provide additional notes for the assigned tool in the "Assigned Tool Notes 3" field (optional).
22. Enter any additional description for the assigned tool in the "assigned_tool_description" field (optional).
