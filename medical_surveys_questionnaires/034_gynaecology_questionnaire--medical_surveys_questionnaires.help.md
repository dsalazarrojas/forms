# gynaecology_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gynaecology questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about patients related to gynaecology. It's intended to be used by healthcare professionals for research, treatment planning, or patient follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully to ensure you understand the purpose of each section.
2. Complete each field according to the instructions provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can provide context or background information about the patient.
* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's relevant medical information here. This field is for free-text input.
* **Contact Details**: Indicate whether the patient has contact details (e.g., phone number, email) that you need to follow up with them. Select 'Yes' or 'No' from the provided options.
* **Medical History**: Choose whether the patient has any medical history related to gynaecology. Select 'Yes' or 'No' from the provided options.
* **Physical Examination**: Indicate whether the patient has undergone a physical examination related to gynaecology. Select 'Yes' or 'No' from the provided options.
* **Diagnosis**: Enter any relevant diagnosis or results from medical tests here. This field is for free-text input.
* **Medication**: Enter any medication the patient is currently taking here. This field is for free-text input.
* **Medical History 2**: Choose whether the patient has any additional medical history related to gynaecology. Select 'Yes' or 'No' from the provided options.
* **Medical Test Results**: Indicate whether the patient has undergone any medical tests related to gynaecology. Select 'Yes' or 'No' from the provided options.
* **Medical Procedure**: Enter any relevant medical procedures the patient has undergone here. This field is for free-text input.
* **Patient Instructions**: Enter any additional information or instructions for the patient here. This field is for free-text input.
* **Gynaecology Questions**: Choose whether the patient has any specific gynaecology-related questions. Select 'Yes' or 'No' from the provided options.

## Tips
- Be thorough when completing the patient information section.
- Clearly indicate which medical procedures or tests the patient has undergone.
- Enter any relevant medical history or diagnosis in the dedicated fields.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
