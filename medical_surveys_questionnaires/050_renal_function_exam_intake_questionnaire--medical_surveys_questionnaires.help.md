# Renal Function Exam Intake Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a renal function exam. Please complete this form accurately to ensure a comprehensive understanding of your medical history and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out each section completely and accurately.
- Ensure that you have filled out all required fields (indicated by a *).
- Use correct formatting and syntax for date fields (DD/MM/YYYY).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First name**: Please provide your first name.
  *Type: Text, Required*

- **Last name**: Please provide your last name.
  *Type: Text, Required*

- **Date of birth**: Please provide your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
  *Type: Date, Required*

- **Medical record number**: Please provide your medical record number.
  *Type: Text, Required*

- **Contact phone number**: Please provide your contact phone number.
  *Type: Text, Required*

- **Contact email**: Please provide your contact email address.
  *Type: Email, Required*

- **Address**: Please provide your address.
  *Type: Text, Required*

- **Medical history**: Please provide a concise description of your medical history.
  *Type: Note, Required*

- **Allergies**: Please indicate if you have any allergies. If yes, specify what they are.
  *Type: Select One, Required*

- **Medications**: Please list any medications you are currently taking. If none, select "No".
  *Type: Select Multiple, Required*

- **Goals for visit**: Please state your goals for the visit.
  *Type: Text, Required*

- **Medical condition**: Please indicate if you have any medical conditions.
  *Type: Select One, Required*

- **Other medical condition**: Please specify any other medical conditions, if applicable.
  *Type: Text, Required*

- **Question**: Please add any questions you may have or concerns you want to share with your healthcare provider.
  *Type: Note, Required*

- **Additional comments**: Please provide any additional comments or information.
  *Type: Text, Required*

- **Insurance provider**: Please provide your insurance provider.
  *Type: Text, Required*

- **Insurance number**: Please provide your insurance number.
  *Type: Text, Required*

- **Insurance dentist**: Please provide your insurance dentist (if applicable).
  *Type: Text, Required*

- **Emergency contact**: Please provide your emergency contact information.
  *Type: Text, Required*

- **Contact relationship**: Please indicate if you would like your emergency contact to be notified of any issues.
  *Type: Select One, Required*

- **Blood test**: Please provide a description of any recent blood tests you have undergone.
  *Type: Text, Required*

- **Blood test date**: Please provide the date of your recent blood test.
  *Type: Date, Required*

- **Blood test results**: Please indicate if you have the results of your recent blood test.
  *Type: Select Multiple, Required*

- **Kidney function test date**: Please provide the date of your recent kidney function test.
  *Type: Date, Required*

- **Kidney function test results**: Please indicate if you have the results of your recent kidney function test.
  *Type: Select Multiple, Required*

## Tips
- Double-check that you have filled out all required fields.
- Ensure that you have provided accurate and complete information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your healthcare provider.
