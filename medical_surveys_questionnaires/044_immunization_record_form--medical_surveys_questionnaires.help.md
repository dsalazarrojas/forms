# immunization_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The immunization record form is used to collect and record immunization information for patients in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant medical surveys questionnaire from the available options on the first page.
2. Enter the patient's information, including any relevant details about their medical history, vaccination schedule, and dosage information.
3. Record the dosing schedule and time for each vaccine administered.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments if required.
5. Verify the form ID and submission information for accurate tracking and reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Surveys Questionnaire**: Select the relevant medical surveys questionnaire from the available options, which include Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3. This field is not required.
* **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's relevant information, such as their name, contact details, or any other relevant medical history. This field is not required.
* **Vaccine Details**: Select multiple vaccines administered to the patient, including Vaccine 1, Vaccine 2, and Vaccine 3. This field is not required, but it is essential to ensure accurate and comprehensive recording of vaccination details.
* **Dosing Schedule**: Record the dosing schedule for each vaccine administered, including the date and time of each dose. This field is not required.
* **Dosing Time**: Record the time of each vaccine dose, taking into account the patient's schedule and medical needs. This field is not required.
* **Dosage Info**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the dosing and vaccination process, if required. This field is not required.
* **Submission Info**: Include any necessary information about the submission, such as the submission method or date. This field is not required.
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for this form submission, for accurate tracking and reference. This field is not required.
