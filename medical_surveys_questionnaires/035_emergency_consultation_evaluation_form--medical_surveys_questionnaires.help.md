# emergency consultation evaluation form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency consultation evaluation form is used to gather information and evaluate the needs of patients in emergency situations, enabling clinicians to quickly and effectively assess and manage patients' emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are in the correct context for using this form. This form is intended for emergency situations only.
2. Carefully read and understand each field's purpose before completing the form.
3. Fill in the form accurately and completely.
4. Review and sign the form before submitting it to the clinician.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): Provide basic information about the patient, including their name, contact details, and any other relevant background information.
* **Emergency Consultation** (`emergency_consultation`, text, required: false): Record the details of the emergency consultation, including the nature of the emergency and any relevant medical history.
* **Assessment Data** (`assessment_data`, text, required: false): Enter any additional assessment data relevant to the emergency consultation.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's medical history, including any past conditions, allergies, or previous treatments.
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any medications the patient is currently taking, including their names and dosages.
* **Medication Dosage** (`medication_dosage`, text, required: false): Enter the dosage information for the selected medications.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, required: false): Outline the treatment plan for the patient, including any necessary steps or follow-up appointments.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, required: false): The patient must sign this section to indicate they have been informed and agree to the treatment plan.
* **Physician Signature** (`patient_signature2`, note, required: false): The clinician must sign this section to confirm they have discussed and agreed upon the treatment plan with the patient.
* **Emergency Consultation 2** (`emergency_consultation2`, text, required: false): This section can be used to document any additional information or details about the emergency consultation.
