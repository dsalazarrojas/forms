# Medical Support Coordinator Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Support Coordinator Survey is designed to collect feedback from patients and their families who have interacted with our Medical Support Team. The survey aims to gauge their experience and perceptions of our team's performance, helping us to identify areas for improvement and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each page of the survey, starting from the first page.
2. For each question, select the option that best reflects your experience or perception.
3. Be as specific as possible in your responses.
4. If a question is not applicable, select "Not applicable" and provide a brief explanation in the "Additional Comments" field, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1 - What was your experience with our Medical Support Team?
* **What was your experience with our Medical Support Team** (`medical_support_coordinator_survey_form`, text, required): Share your detailed response to this question, providing specific examples or anecdotes that highlight your experience.
* **How sensitive is your issue** (`medical_support_team_member_sensitivity`, number, required): Indicate how sensitive you feel about your issue, with options ranging from "Not sensitive at all" to "Very sensitive".
* **How long did you have to wait for your call to be answered** (`medical_support_team_member_response_time`, time, optional): If applicable, provide the duration you had to wait for your call to be answered.
* **How would you rate the quality of our call** (`call_quality`, select_multiple, required): Rate the quality of our call, with options ranging from "Very poor" to "Excellent".
* **How confident are you that our support team had the necessary knowledge** (`medical_support_team_member_knowledge`, select_one, required): Indicate your level of confidence in our support team's knowledge, with options ranging from "Very confident" to "Not confident at all".
* **How would you rate the communication effectiveness of our support team member** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_multiple, required): Rate the communication effectiveness of our support team member, with options ranging from "Very poor" to "Excellent".
* **How quickly would you like to be contacted for follow-up on your issue** (`follow_up_contact`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your preferred frequency of follow-up contact, with options ranging from "Always" to "Never".
* **How confident are you that we took your medical needs into account** (`medical_support_team_member_care_plan`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your level of confidence in our team's consideration of your medical needs, with options ranging from "Very confident" to "Not confident at all".
* **Would you like to receive a follow-up call from us?** (`follow_up_call`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your preference for follow-up calls, with options ranging from "True" to "False".
* **How likely are you to recommend our services to a friend or family member?** (`medical_support_team_member_care_plan`, number, required): Rate your likelihood of recommending our services, on a scale of 1-5.

### Page 2-3

* **How would you rate the knowledge of our support team member?** (`medical_support_team_member_knowledge`, select_multiple, required): Rate the knowledge of our support team member, with options ranging from "Very knowledgeable" to "Not confident at all".
* **How satisfied are you with the support team member?** (`medical_support_team_member_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your level of satisfaction with the support team member, with options ranging from "Very satisfied" to "Not satisfied at all".
* **Would you like to receive follow-up surveys?** (`follow_up_survey`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your preference for follow-up surveys, with options ranging from "True" to "False".
* **How would you like to be contacted for follow-up?** (`follow_up_contact_details`, select_multiple, required): Select the preferred method of follow-up contact, with options ranging from "Email" to "Text message".

### Page 3-4

* **How long did you have to wait for your call to be answered?** (`medical_support_member_response_time`, time, optional): If applicable, provide the duration you had to wait for your call to be answered.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with our care team?** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our care team, on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (`contact_details`, select_multiple, required): Select the preferred method of contact, with options ranging from "Email" to "Text message".
* **How often would you like to be contacted?** (`contact_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your preferred frequency of contact, with options ranging from "Always" to "Never".
* **How sensitive is your issue to contact frequency?** (`medical_support_team_member_sensitivity`, select_multiple, required): Indicate the sensitivity of your issue to contact frequency, with options ranging from "Very sensitive" to "Not sensitive at all".
* **How satisfied are you with your call?** (`medical_support_team_member_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required): Rate your level of satisfaction with your call, with options ranging from "Very satisfied" to "Not satisfied at all".

## Tips

* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* Be as specific as possible in your responses.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "Additional Comments" field.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback! Your input is invaluable to us.
