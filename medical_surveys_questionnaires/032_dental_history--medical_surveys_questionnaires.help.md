# Dental History - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dental History" form is designed to collect information about a patient's medical history. This information will be used for the patient's medical care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field using the date picker.
3. Enter the patient's address in the "Address" field.
4. In the "Medical History" field, enter any relevant medical history that could affect the patient's dental care.
5. For the "Allergy" field, select any allergies the patient has.
6. For the "Medical Conditions" field, select any medical conditions the patient has.
7. For the "Medsications" field, select any current medications the patient is taking.
8. In the "Family History" field, enter any relevant information about the patient's family medical history.
9. For the "Dental History" field, enter any relevant information about the patient's dental history.
10. In the "Dental Procedures" field, select any upcoming or previous dental procedures the patient has undergone.
11. In the "Next Visit" field, select the date of the patient's next visit.
12. In the "Medical Record Number" field, confirm the patient's medical record number.
13. In the "Physician" field, enter the patient's physician's name.
14. In the "Medical Provider" field, enter the patient's medical provider's name.
15. In the "Emergency Contact" field, enter the emergency contact person's name and phone number.
16. In the "Medical Insurance" field, enter the patient's medical insurance information.
17. In the "Insurance Card Number" field, enter the patient's insurance card number.
18. In the "Emergency Contact Number" field, enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
19. In the "Dental Insurance" field, enter the patient's dental insurance information.
20. In the "Medical Provider Phone Number" field, enter the patient's medical provider's phone number.
21. In the "Emergency Contact Phone Number" field, enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
22. In the "Medical Provider Email" field, enter the patient's medical provider's email address.
23. In the "Patient Notes" field, enter any additional notes about the patient.
24. In the "Next Procedure" field, enter any information about the patient's next procedure.
25. In the "Patient Image" field, enter any relevant patient images or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (Text, Required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Select the patient's date of birth.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Medical History** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant medical history that could affect the patient's dental care.
* **Allergy** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select any allergies the patient has.
* **Medical Conditions** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select any medical conditions the patient has.
* **Medsications** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select any current medications the patient is taking.
* **Family History** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant information about the patient's family medical history.
* **Dental History** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant information about the patient's dental history.
* **Dental Procedures** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select any upcoming or previous dental procedures the patient has undergone.
* **Next Visit** (Date, Optional): Select the date of the patient's next visit.
* **Medical Record Number** (Select One, Required): Confirm the patient's medical record number.
* **Physician** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's physician's name.
* **Medical Provider** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's medical provider's name.
* **Emergency Contact** (Text, Optional): Enter the emergency contact person's name and phone number.
* **Medical Insurance** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's medical insurance information.
* **Insurance Card Number** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's insurance card number.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (Text, Optional): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Dental Insurance** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's dental insurance information.
* **Medical Provider Phone Number** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's medical provider's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (Text, Optional): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Medical Provider Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the patient's medical provider's email address.
* **Patient Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes about the patient.
* **Next Procedure** (Text, Optional): Enter any information about the patient's next procedure.
* **Patient Image** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant patient images or information.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about a field, check with the patient or their medical provider.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the medical team for assistance.
