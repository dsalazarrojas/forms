# ICH Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ICH Risk Assessment Form is designed to help healthcare professionals assess the risk associated with a patient's condition. This form collects various types of information about the patient's medical history, symptoms, and outcomes to evaluate and categorize their risk level.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide accurate information about the patient.
2. Enter the relevant information for each field, using the following fields to guide your input:
	* **Patient Information:** Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other relevant demographic information.
	* **Medical History:** Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or surgeries.
	* **Ictus Characteristics:** Provide details about the patient's Ictus characteristics, such as symptoms and duration.
	* **Neuro Imaging:** Describe any neuroimaging findings, including results and interpretations.
	* **Laboratory Findings:** Enter any laboratory results, including test dates and values.
	* **Clinical Evaluation:** Summarize the patient's clinical evaluation, including any relevant diagnoses or treatments.
	* **Ictus Outcomes:** Describe the patient's Ictus outcomes, including any complications or sequelae.
	* **Follow-up:** Record any follow-up information, including appointments or test dates.
	* **Patient Status:** Update the patient's status, including any changes in condition or treatment.
	* **Risk Score:** Enter the patient's risk score, as calculated from the above fields.
	* **Risk Category:** Select the patient's risk category (Low, Moderate, High, or Critical) based on the risk score.
