# pre-health-screening-questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect pre-health screening information from patients, which is crucial for healthcare professionals to assess their health status.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Enter your patient information in the **Patient Information** field on **Page 1**.
- Provide your contact information in the **Contact Information** field on **Page 1**.
- Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses or surgeries, in the **Medical Info** field on **Page 1**.
- Continue on **Page 2** and describe any additional medical history or concerns in the **Medical Info 2** field.
- List any allergies or adverse reactions you may have in the **Allergies** field on **Page 2**.
- Describe any medications you are currently taking in the **Medications** field on **Page 2**.
- On **Page 3**, describe any other medical history or concerns.
- On **Page 4**, specify any medical conditions you have been diagnosed with.
- On **Page 5**, list any additional medical conditions or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (Page 1)**: Enter your name, date of birth, and other identifying information.
* **Contact Information (Page 1)**: Enter your address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Medical Info (Page 1)**: Describe any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical procedures you have undergone.
* **Medical Info 2 (Page 2)**: Describe any additional medical history or concerns.
* **Allergies (Page 2)**: List any allergies or adverse reactions you may have, selecting from the options provided.
* **Medications (Page 2)**: List any medications you are currently taking, selecting from the options provided.
* **Medical History 3 (Page 3)**: Describe any other medical history or concerns.
* **Medical Condition (Page 4)**: Specify any medical conditions you have been diagnosed with.
* **Medical Condition 2 (Page 5)**: List any additional medical conditions or concerns.
