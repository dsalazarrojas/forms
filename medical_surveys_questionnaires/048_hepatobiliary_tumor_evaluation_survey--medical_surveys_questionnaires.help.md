# Hepatobiliary Tumor Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hepatobiliary Tumor Evaluation Survey is designed to collect information about a patient's medical history, contact details, and other relevant data to help healthcare professionals evaluate hepatobiliary tumors. This survey is used to gather accurate and comprehensive information about the patient's condition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, select the patient's medical specialty from the list provided.
2. Enter the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, in the designated text field.
3. Provide the patient's contact information, including their phone number and email address.
4. Enter the date of the last medical follow-up, if applicable.
5. If necessary, add additional medical history notes in the designated text fields.
