# Herbalife Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from Herbalife customers to help us better understand their goals, preferences, and experiences with our products. The data collected will be used to improve our customer service, product development, and overall customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Answer the overall goals question with a brief description of your current goals or objectives.
4. Rate your fitness level, where 1 is "low" and 5 is "high".
5. Describe your health goals in a few sentences.
6. Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Goals (<overall_goals>):** This question asks you to briefly describe your current goals or objectives. Please provide a clear and concise answer that reflects your current aspirations and motivations.
* **Fitness Level (<fitness_level>):** This question helps us understand your current fitness level. Please rate your fitness level on a scale from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Name (<name>):** This question asks for your name, which will be used for contact purposes.
* **Email (<email>):** This question asks for your email address, which will be used for communication and account management.
* **Phone (<phone>):** This question asks for your phone number, which will be used for communication and account management.
* **Comments (<comments>):** This question allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with Herbalife products.
