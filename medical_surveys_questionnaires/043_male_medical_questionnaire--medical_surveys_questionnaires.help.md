# Male Medical Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Male Medical Questionnaire is a comprehensive form created to gather crucial health information from male patients. This form serves as a tool for healthcare professionals to collect data and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields on each page, marked as "required".
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (patient_demographics, text, required)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., driver's license, passport).
* **Contact Information (patient_contact_info, text, required)**: Provide your contact details, including your address and phone number.
* **Medical History (medical_history, text, optional)**: Describe any previous medical conditions or health issues you've experienced.
* **Allergies (allergies, select_one, optional)**: Select any allergies you have.
* **Family Medical History (family_medical_history, select_one, optional)**: Select any family medical conditions or health issues.
* **Mental Health Issues (mental_health_issues, select_multiple, optional)**: Select any mental health issues you've experienced.
* **Mental Health Treatment History (mental_health_treatment_history, text, optional)**: Describe any mental health treatments you've received.
* **Medical Conditions (medical_conditions, text, optional)**: List any current medical conditions you're experiencing.
* **Surgical History (surgical_history, text, optional)**: Describe any past surgeries you've undergone.
* **Medical Insurance (medical_insurance, text, optional)**: Provide your medical insurance information.
* **Active Medical Conditions (active_medical_conditions, text, optional)**: List any active medical conditions you're experiencing.

Please note that some fields are marked as "optional", which means you can skip them if you don't have the information or if the field does not apply to you. Ensure you answer each required field truthfully and thoroughly to help healthcare professionals provide accurate and effective care.
