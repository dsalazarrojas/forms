# Premature Ejaculation Training Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who are participating in or seeking Premature Ejaculation Training. The goal is to collect data on pre-program intake details, goals, medical history, and other relevant factors that may affect their progress in the program.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* For multi-select questions (e.g., select_multiple, select_one), please select all applicable options.
* For text fields (e.g., Medical History, Medications), list all relevant information.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or guidance.
