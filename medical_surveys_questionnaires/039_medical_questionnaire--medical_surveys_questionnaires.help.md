# Medical Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Questionnaire form is designed to collect medical information from patients or individuals. This form is used to gather details about a person's health, medical history, and other relevant medical information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* Select the relevant options from the provided select_one and select_multiple fields.
* Enter any additional information in the text fields.
* Ensure that you have completed all the fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age** (`Age`, `number`, required: false): Enter your age.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required: false): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant medical history, such as previous surgeries, illnesses, or allergies.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any allergies you have from the provided options.
* **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, `text`, required: false): Describe any current or previous medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Condition Date** (`Medical Condition Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you first experienced the medical condition or the date of your last medical check-up.
* **Medication** (`Medication`, `text`, required: false): Enter any medication you are currently taking.
* **Medical Specialist** (`Medical Specialist`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of medical specialist you see (e.g. doctor, nurse practitioner, etc.).
* **Medical Record** (`Medical Record`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information about your medical record, such as your medical ID number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person you would like to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`Emergency Contact Relationship`, `text`, required: false): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g. spouse, parent, etc.).
