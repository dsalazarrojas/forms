# IHUK COVID 19 Athlete/Coach Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information about your COVID-19 status, including vaccination history, contact with confirmed cases, symptoms, travel history, medical attention, and other relevant details. This information is essential for the assessment and management of COVID-19 cases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to select all relevant options that apply to your situation.
3. If you are unsure about any question, ask for clarification or guidance from the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Have you had COVID-19 vaccination?** (`have_you_had_covid_19_vaccination`): Answer 'True' if you have received a COVID-19 vaccination, 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you had contact with confirmed COVID-19 case?** (`contact`): Select 'True' if you have had contact with someone who has tested positive for COVID-19, 'False' otherwise.
* **Are you experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms?** (`symptoms`): In the text field, describe any symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Have you traveled outside your country in the last 14 days?** (`travel`): Select 'True' if you have traveled outside your country in the last 14 days, 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you had any COVID-19 medical attention in the last 30 days?** (`medical_attention`): Select 'True' if you have received any medical attention related to COVID-19 in the last 30 days, 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you been hospitalized during COVID-19 pandemic?** (`hospitalization`): Select 'True' if you have been hospitalized due to COVID-19, 'False' otherwise.
* **Are you currently recovering from COVID-19?** (`recovery`): Select 'True' if you are currently recovering from COVID-19, 'False' otherwise.
* **Submit** (`form_submit`): Select a date and time to submit your form.
* **Additional comments** (`note_section`): In this text field, you can provide any additional comments or information that you think are relevant to your COVID-19 status.
* **Email address** (`email`): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Phone number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Do you have any medical conditions that might be relevant to your COVID-19 status?** (`medical_condition`): In the text field, describe any medical conditions you have that might impact your COVID-19 status.
* **Travel history in the last 14 days** (`travel_history`): In the text field, describe any travel you have done in the last 14 days that might be relevant to your COVID-19 status.
* **Have you been contact traced?** (`contact_trace`): Select 'True' if you have been contact traced, 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you been tested for COVID-19?** (`testing_status`): Select 'True' if you have been tested for COVID-19, 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you received any vaccinations?** (`vaccination_status`): Select 'True' if you have received any vaccinations, 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you received any medical attention?** (`medical_attention`): Select 'True' if you have received any medical attention, 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you been hospitalized?** (`hospitalization_status`): Select 'True' if you have been hospitalized, 'False' otherwise.
* **Are you currently recovering?** (`recovery_status`): Select 'True' if you are currently recovering, 'False' otherwise.
* **Do you have any medical conditions?** (`medical_condition_status`): In the text field, describe any medical conditions you have.
* **Have you received any medical attention?** (`medical_attention_status`): Select 'True' if you have received any medical attention, 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you received any vaccination?** (`vaccination_status`): Select 'True' if you have received any vaccination, 'False' otherwise.
* **End of form** (`form_end`): Select this option to complete the form.
