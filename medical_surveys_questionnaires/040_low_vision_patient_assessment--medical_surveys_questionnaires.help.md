# Low Vision Patient Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Low Vision Patient Assessment form is a comprehensive survey designed to collect information about patients with low vision conditions. It helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's medical history, visual goals, and specific aspects of their vision condition, enabling them to provide personalized care and support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Name and Last Name**: Fill in your full name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Address**: Enter your complete address, including address line 1 and 2, city, and zip code.
3. **Phone Number and Email**: Provide your phone number and email address for communication purposes.
4. **Medical Condition**: Describe your current medical condition, if any.
5. **Date and Time of Last Visit**: If you have visited a healthcare professional recently, enter the date and time of your last visit.
6. **Reason for Visit**: Briefly explain why you visited a healthcare professional.
7. **Vision Difficulty**: Describe your current vision difficulty, if any.
8. **Visual Goals**: Specify your visual goals or what you would like to achieve through your treatment.
9. **Patient Goals**: Share your personal goals related to your vision condition.
10. **Other Medical Condition**: If you have any other medical condition, please mention it here.
11. **Other Medical Condition (Other)**: If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, provide more information about your other medical condition.
12. **Medical History**: Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses or surgeries.
13. **Medication Allergies**: List any medication allergies you may have.
14. **Allergies**: If you have any allergies other than medication allergies, list them here.
15. **Medical Equipment**: If you use any assistive medical equipment, such as hearing aids or walkers, list them here.
16. **Eye Conditions**: Indicate if you have any eye conditions, such as cataracts or glaucoma.
17. **Assistive Technology**: If you use any assistive technology, such as screen readers or magnifying glasses, list them here.
18. **Medical Specialties**: If you have visited a healthcare professional for a specific medical specialty, such as eye doctors or optometrists, list them here.

**Tips**

- Be as accurate and thorough as possible when filling out the form.
- If you're unsure about any question, ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
- If you have any additional comments or concerns, please share them in the "Medical History" or "Other Medical Condition" fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns while completing the form, feel free to contact your healthcare professional for assistance.
