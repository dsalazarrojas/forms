# Patient Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Health Questionnaire is a form used by healthcare professionals to collect information about a patient's personal details, medical history, family history, lifestyle habits, presenting complaints, additional information, allergies, medications, and medical conditions. This information is used to provide personalized care and treatment to each patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by reviewing the form and understanding the different sections and questions.
2.  Fill in your personal details, medical history, family history, lifestyle habits, presenting complaints, additional information, allergies, medications, and medical conditions accurately and thoroughly.
3.  Review each section carefully before moving on to the next.
4.  Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Personal details
*   **Personal details**: (`personal_details`, `text`, required: false): This section is for you to provide your personal information such as name, address, and contact details.
### 2. Medical History
*   **Medical History**: (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your past medical history, including any previous illnesses or medical conditions you have had.
### 3. Medical History Condition
*   **Medical History Condition**: (`medical_history_condition`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if you have a medical condition or not.
### 4. Family History
*   **Family History**: (`family_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe any medical conditions or illnesses that affect your family members.
### 5. Lifestyle Habits
*   **Lifestyle Habits**: (`lifestyle_habits`, `text`, required: false): Share any habits or activities that may impact your health.
### 6. Presenting Complaints
*   **Presenting Complaints**: (`presenting_complaints`, `text`, required: false): List any current health issues or concerns you are experiencing.
### 7. Additional Information
*   **Additional Information**: (`additional_info`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or information that may be helpful for your healthcare professional.
### 8. Allergies
*   **Allergies**: (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the box if you have any allergies.
### 9. Medications
*   **Medications**: (`medications`, `text`, required: false): List any medications or supplements you are currently taking.
### 10. Medical Conditions
*   **Medical Conditions**: (`medical_conditions`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate any medical conditions you may have, such as diabetes or high blood pressure.

Note: Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly, and if you have any doubts or questions, please ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
