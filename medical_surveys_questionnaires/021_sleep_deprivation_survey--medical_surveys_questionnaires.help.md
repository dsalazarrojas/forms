# Sleep Deprivation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your sleep habits and behaviors, including your sleep schedule, use of sleep aids, and lifestyle factors that may impact your sleep. Please answer the questions honestly to help us better understand your sleep patterns and identify potential areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them truthfully.
2. For time-based questions, use the format `HH:MM`.
3. For select_one questions, choose only one of the provided options.
4. For select_multiple questions, select all applicable options.
5. For number questions, enter a whole number.
6. Click "Submit" when you have completed all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What time do you go to bed?**: Enter the time you go to bed in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Do you work night shifts?**: This question asks about your work schedule. Answer `True` if you work night shifts, and `False` otherwise.
* **On average, how many hours of sleep do you get at night?**: Enter the number of hours you typically sleep per night.
* **What time do you wake up?**: Enter the time you wake up in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Do you take naps?**: Answer `True` if you take naps, and `False` otherwise.
* **On average, how many hours do you take naps for?**: Enter the number of hours you typically take naps for.
* **Do you use any sleep aids?**: Answer `True` if you use sleep aids, and `False` otherwise.
* **On average, how many hours do you use sleep aids for?**: Enter the number of hours you typically use sleep aids for.
* **Do you take any medications for sleep?**: Answer `True` if you take medications for sleep, and `False` otherwise.
* **On average, how many hours do you take medications for sleep?**: Enter the number of hours you typically take medications for sleep.
* **Do you drink caffeine?**: Answer `True` if you drink caffeine, and `False` otherwise.
* **On average, how many cups of caffeine do you consume?**: Enter the number of cups of caffeine you typically consume.
* **Do you smoke?**: Answer `True` if you smoke, and `False` otherwise.
* **How long have you been experiencing sleep issues?**: Select the time frame you have been experiencing sleep issues from the provided options.
* **Do you take naps during the day?**: Answer `True` if you take naps, and `False` otherwise.
* **What time do you wake up to go to work/school?**: Enter the time you wake up to go to work or school in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Do you feel tired when waking up?**: Answer `True` if you feel tired when waking up, and `False` otherwise.
* **Do you use any white noise apps?**: Answer `True` if you use white noise apps, and `False` otherwise.
* **On average, how many hours do you use white noise apps?**: Enter the number of hours you typically use white noise apps for.
* **Do you use any blue light?**: Answer `True` if you use blue light, and `False` otherwise.
* **On average, how many hours do you use blue light?**: Enter the number of hours you typically use blue light for.
* **Do you exercise regularly?**: Answer `True` if you exercise regularly, and `False` otherwise.
* **On average, how often do you exercise?**: Select the frequency of your exercise from the provided options.
* **Do you use any earplugs/ earbuds?**: Answer `True` if you use earplugs or earbuds, and `False` otherwise.
* **On average, how many hours do you use earplugs/ earbuds?**: Enter the number of hours you typically use earplugs or earbuds for.

Note: This guide is intended to help you understand the form's purpose and questions. Please answer the questions truthfully to provide accurate information.
