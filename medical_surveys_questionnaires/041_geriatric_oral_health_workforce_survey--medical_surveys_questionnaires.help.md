# Geriatric Oral Health Workforce Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information on the geriatric oral health workforce and their needs. This survey will help us understand the challenges and barriers faced by the geriatric care workforce in providing oral health care to elderly patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review each page and select the relevant information that applies to you.
2. For each field, provide the most accurate and complete answer possible.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Geriatric Care** (`geriatric_care`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks about the types of care you provide to geriatric patients.
- **Oral Health Barriers** (`oral_health_barriers`, text, optional): Describe the oral health barriers you face while providing care to geriatric patients.
- **Workforce Needs** (`workforce_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Select the specific workforce needs that you consider most important for providing geriatric oral health care. These may include dental hygienists, hygienist assistants, or other support staff.
- **Barriers to Care** (`barriers_to_care`, text, optional): List the barriers you face in providing geriatric oral health care.
- **Geriatric Oral Health** (`geriatric_oral_health`, select_one, optional): Rate the importance of geriatric oral health care on a scale of Very important, Somewhat important, or Not important.
- **Geriatric Care Organizations** (`geriatric_care_organizations`, select_one, optional): Select the type of geriatric care organization you work with, such as home health care or assisted living.
- **Geriatric Care Setting** (`geriatric_care_setting`, select_one, optional): Select the setting where you provide geriatric care, such as a clinic or nursing home.
- **Geriatric Care Provider** (`geriatric_care_provider`, select_one, optional): Select the type of geriatric care provider you are, such as a dentist or hygienist.
- **Geriatric Oral Health Issues** (`geriatric_oral_health_issues`, text, optional): Describe the oral health issues you commonly face in geriatric care.
- **Geriatric Oral Health Barriers** (`geriatric_oral_health_barriers`, text, optional): Describe the oral health barriers you face while providing care to geriatric patients.
- **Geriatric Oral Health Needs** (`geriatric_oral_health_needs`, text, optional): Describe the oral health needs of geriatric patients.

Tips:
- Please be as specific and detailed as possible when answering each question.
- If you have any concerns or questions, contact us.
- We appreciate your time and input in helping us better understand the geriatric oral health workforce.
