# Patient Empowerment In Digital Health Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information on patients' experiences and opinions about digital health tools and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as you are comfortable sharing.
3. If you need help or have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Demographics** (text, required: false): This field is where you can provide your name, age, and any other demographic information that you feel is relevant to your digital health experience.
* **Digital Health Tools** (select_one, required: false): Please select the digital health tools that you use or have used in the past. You can choose one or more options:
	+ Health apps
	+ Telehealth platforms
	+ Health portals
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Digital Tool Experience** (text, required: false): Describe your experience with the digital health tools you selected in the previous field. What do you like or dislike about them?
* **Confidence Levels** (select_multiple, required: false): Indicate your level of confidence in the digital health tools you use. Please select all that apply:
	+ Very Low
	+ Somewhat Low
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat High
	+ High
* **Barriers** (text, required: false): If you have any barriers or challenges when using digital health tools, please describe them in this field.
* **Comments** (note, required: false): If you have any additional comments or suggestions about digital health tools or services, please share them here.
* **Phone Number** (text, required: false): If you would like to be contacted by healthcare providers for follow-up questions or to discuss your experience, please provide your phone number.
* **Email** (email, required: false): If you would like to be contacted by email, please provide your email address.

## Tips

* Please keep your responses honest and as detailed as you are comfortable sharing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Your input is valuable in helping healthcare providers improve digital health services for all patients.
