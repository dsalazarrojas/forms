# Shingles Vaccination Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Shingles Vaccination Assessment Form is a survey designed to assess patient information related to shingles vaccination. This form gathers general information, medical history, allergy information, vaccine information, patient consent, medical condition, contact details, family history, medical professional's information, and medication details. The goal is to collect comprehensive information to make informed decisions regarding shingles vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the General Information section with your basic details.
2. In the Medical History section, provide any relevant medical history that may affect your vaccination.
3. If you have any allergies, fill out the Allergy Information section.
4. In the Vaccine Information section, provide any relevant information about your vaccination history.
5. Review and acknowledge your patient consent.
6. If you have any medical conditions, please list them in the Medical Condition section.
7. Provide contact details in the Contact Details section.
8. List any family medical history in the Family History section.
9. If you have a medical professional, fill out the Medical Professional section.
10. In the Medication section, list any medications you are taking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (General Information, text, required/optional): This section is for your basic contact information such as name, address, and phone number.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, required/optional): Please provide any relevant medical history that may affect your vaccination, such as previous surgeries, illnesses, or medical conditions.
* **Allergy Information** (Allergy Information, text, required/optional): If you have any allergies, please list them here.
* **Vaccine Information** (Vaccine Information, text, required/optional): Provide any relevant information about your vaccination history, such as doses received or any adverse reactions.
* **Patient Consent** (Patient Consent, note, required/optional): Review and acknowledge your patient consent form.
* **Medical Condition** (Medical Condition, text, required/optional): If you have any medical conditions, please list them here.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, required/optional): Provide contact details, such as name, address, and phone number.
* **Family History** (Family History, text, required/optional): If you have any family medical history, please list it here.
* **Medical Professional** (Medical Professional, text, required/optional): If you have a medical professional, fill out their information here.
* **Medication** (Medication, text, required/optional): List any medications you are taking.

## Tips
- Be thorough when filling out the form.
- If unsure about any information, do not hesitate to contact your healthcare provider.
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting.
