# Depression Screening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Depression Screening Survey is designed to assess an individual's mental health status, including general mood, sleep quality, energy level, physical symptoms, and suicidal tendency. It is a critical tool for identifying potential depressive episodes and providing early intervention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the option that best describes your current situation.
3. For text fields, provide a clear and concise description of your experience.
4. If you have any medical history or previous mental health conditions, please provide detailed information in the relevant fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Mood** (`general_mood`, text, required: false): Describe your current general mood, such as feeling sad, happy, anxious, or neutral.
- **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, text, required: false): Report your sleep quality, including any difficulties sleeping or excessive sleepiness.
- **Energy Level** (`energy_level`, select_one, required: false):
  * option 1: Very high
  * option 2: High
  * option 3: Medium
- **Physical Symptoms** (`physical_symptoms`, text, required: false): Describe any physical symptoms you experience, such as headaches, fatigue, or loss of appetite.
- **Emotional Symptoms** (`emotional_symptoms`, text, required: false): Describe any emotional symptoms you experience, such as crying, irritability, or loss of interest in activities.
- **Treatment Status** (`treatment_status`, select_multiple, required: false):
  * option 1: Not currently receiving treatment
  * option 2: Undergoing treatment
  * option 3: Completed treatment
- **Social Activities** (`social_activities`, text, required: false): Report your engagement in social activities, such as work, school, or social events.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Provide any past medical history, including mental health conditions or treatments.
- **Suicidal Thoughts** (`suicidal_thoughts`, text, required: false): Report any suicidal thoughts or plans you may have.
- **Depression Screening** (`depression_screening`, text, required: false): Report any symptoms of depression, such as feelings of worthlessness, hopelessness, or guilt.
- **Mental Status** (`mental_status`, text, required: false): Describe your current mental status, including any emotional or psychological distress.
- **Medication Status** (`medication_status`, text, required: false): Report any current medication or treatment for mental health conditions.
- **Family History** (`family_history`, text, required: false): Report any family history of mental health conditions.
- **Personal History** (`personal_history`, text, required: false): Provide any relevant personal history, including mental health conditions or traumatic events.
- **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required: false): Report any current medical conditions that may affect mental health.
- **Mental Health** (`mental_health`, text, required: false): Describe your current mental health status, including any symptoms or concerns.
- **Mental Health Care** (`mental_health_care`, text, required: false): Report any current mental health care services or therapies you are receiving.
- **Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment`, text, required: false): Report any current medical treatments or interventions.
- **Mental Therapy** (`mental_therapy`, text, required: false): Report any current mental health therapies or interventions.
- **Mental Therapy Outcome** (`mental_therapy_outcome`, text, required: false): Describe the results or outcome of any current mental health therapies or interventions.
- **Depression Level** (`depression_level`, number, required: false): Report your current level of depression, on a scale from 1-10.
- **Mental Status Level** (`mental_status_level`, number, required: false): Report your current mental status level, on a scale from 1-10.
- **Suicidal Tendency** (`suicidal_tendency`, text, required: false): Report any suicidal thoughts or plans you may have.
- **Mental Health Level** (`mental_health_level`, text, required: false): Describe your current mental health status, including any symptoms or concerns.
- **Depression Score** (`depression_score`, number, required: false): Report your current depression score, calculated based on your responses to the previous questions.
