# Medical Symptoms Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients for medical purposes. It is intended to be filled out by patients or their representatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. If you are unsure about any information, please consult with a healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or conditions.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): List the patient's current symptoms, including their description and duration.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one, required): Select the primary medical condition or disease the patient is experiencing.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, optional): Describe the patient's current treatment plan, including medications, therapies, or other interventions.
* **Medical Professional** (`medical_professional`, select_multiple, optional): Select any medical professionals involved in the patient's care.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, select_one, required): Select the contact details for the medical provider or facility.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, number, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the patient.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Record the date of the patient's visit.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): Record the time of the patient's visit.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the patient's doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Doctor Contact** (`doctor_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, select_one, optional): Select the type of medical record to be kept for the patient.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, text, optional): Enter the healthcare provider's medical license information.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, text, optional): Enter the patient's medical insurance information.
* **Medical Insurance ID** (`medical_insurance_id`, text, optional): Enter the patient's medical insurance ID number.
* **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`, select_one, required): Select the medical specialty of the healthcare provider.
* **Medical Department** (`medical_department`, text, optional): Enter the department or specialty of the healthcare provider.
* **Medical Clinical** (`medical_clinical`, text, optional): Enter any additional medical clinical information.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email for the patient or healthcare provider.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number for the patient or healthcare provider.
* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`, select_one, required): Select the patient's gender.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, optional): Enter the patient's age.
* **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth.

Note: This guide is intended to be a general guide and may not cover every possible scenario or situation. If you are unsure about any information or field, please consult with a healthcare professional.
