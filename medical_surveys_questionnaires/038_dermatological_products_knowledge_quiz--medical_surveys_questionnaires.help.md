* **Skin Types** (`skin_types`, `select_multiple`): Select one or more of the following skin types that you think you have.
* **Skin Concerns** (`skin_concerns`, `select_one`): Choose one primary concern from the list.
* **Skin Products** (`skin_products`, `select_multiple`): Check the skin products you are familiar with.
* **Dermatological Questions** (`dermatology_questions`, `time`): Answer the dermatological question in the text input field.
* **Skincare Expert Contact Information** (`skincare_expert_contact`, `email`): Provide the email of a skincare expert you would like to contact.
* **Relationship with Skincare Expert** (`relationship_with_skincare_expert`, `text`): Explain your relationship with the skincare expert.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`): Add any additional comments about your skin or experience with dermatological products.
* **Comments2** (`comments2`, `text`): Additional comments.
