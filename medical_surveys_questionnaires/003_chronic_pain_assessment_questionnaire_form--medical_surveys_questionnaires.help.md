# Chronic Pain Assessment Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chronic Pain Assessment Questionnaire Form is a tool used to gather information about a patient's chronic pain experience. This form helps healthcare providers understand the patient's pain, its severity, and its impact on daily life. The collected data will aid in developing a comprehensive treatment plan to alleviate the patient's pain.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the patient's personal details: name, date of birth, and patient ID or medical record number (if applicable) on page 1.
2. Move on to page 2 and select the primary location of the patient's pain from the options provided.
3. On page 3, describe the onset of the pain and its duration in years.
4. On page 4, select all that apply regarding the known cause of the pain.
5. On page 5, rate the current, typical, and worst pain levels using the 0-10 scale.
6. Select all the characteristics of the pain from the options provided.
7. Choose the pattern of the pain from the options.
8. On page 6, indicate the degree of functional limitation and impact on work or employment.
9. Select the impact on sleep quality.
10. On page 7, select all daily activities affected by the pain.
11. On page 8, list current pain medications and select all types of treatment currently being used.
12. Finally, rate the overall effectiveness of the current treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (2): Please enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (3): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Patient ID or Medical Record Number** (4): If applicable, enter the patient's ID or medical record number for reference.
* **Assessment Date** (5): Enter the date of the assessment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Primary Location of Pain** (7): Select the primary location of the patient's pain from the provided options.
* **When did your pain first start** (8): Describe the onset of the pain in detail, including the date or approximate date.
* **Duration of Chronic Pain (Years)** (9): Enter the duration of the patient's chronic pain in years.
* **Known Cause of Pain** (10): Select all known causes of the patient's pain from the provided options.
* **Current Pain Level (0-10 scale)** (12): Rate the current pain level using the 0-10 scale.
* **Average Pain Level (0-10 scale)** (13): Rate the average pain level over time using the 0-10 scale.
* **Worst Pain Level in Past Week (0-10 scale)** (14): Rate the worst pain level experienced in the past week using the 0-10 scale.
* **Pain Characteristics** (15): Select all characteristics of the pain from the provided options.
* **Pain Pattern** (16): Choose the pattern of the patient's pain from the options provided.
* **Degree of Functional Limitation** (18): Select the degree of functional limitation caused by the pain.
* **Impact on Work or Employment** (19): Select the impact of the pain on work or employment.
* **Impact on Sleep Quality** (20): Select the impact of the pain on sleep quality.
* **Daily Activities Affected** (21): Select all daily activities affected by the pain.
* **List Current Pain Medications** (23): List all current pain medications, including medication names and dosages if possible.
* **Types of Treatment Currently Using** (24): Select all types of treatment currently being used.
* **Overall Effectiveness of Current Treatment** (25): Rate the overall effectiveness of the current treatment using the provided options.

## Tips
- Be accurate when describing the onset and duration of the pain.
- Rate the pain levels honestly using the 0-10 scale.
- Select all that apply when describing pain characteristics, daily activities affected, and treatment types used.
- List current pain medications in detail, including medication names and dosages if possible.
- Rate the overall effectiveness of the current treatment truthfully.
