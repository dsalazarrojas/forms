# Chronic Pain Management Strategies Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect data from individuals who experience chronic pain to understand their pain management strategies, experiences, and challenges. The purpose of this survey is to gather information that can help inform the development of effective pain management programs and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start with demographics**: Answer the age and gender questions to provide basic information about yourself.
2. **Describe your pain experience**: Answer the type of chronic pain condition and how long you have had pain.
3. **Choose a pain severity level**: Rate your current pain severity on a 0-10 scale.
4. **Select all used management strategies**: Choose all the strategies you are currently using to manage your pain (select all that apply).
5. **Choose the most helpful strategy**: Select the most helpful pain management strategy for you.
6. **Medication management**: Answer questions about your current medication use, satisfaction, and side effects.
7. **Barriers and challenges**: Select all the barriers or challenges that you face in managing your pain (select all that apply).
8. **Quality of life impact**: Choose how much pain affects your quality of life.
9. **Emotional impact**: Select all the emotional impacts that you experience due to your chronic pain (select all that apply).
10. **Work and daily activities**: Rate your ability to work or perform daily tasks.
11. **Support and resources**: Answer questions about your current support and resources for pain management.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age (2)**: Please enter your age.
* **Gender (3)**: Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Type of Chronic Pain Condition (5)**: Choose the type of chronic pain condition you experience.
* **How long have you had chronic pain (6)**: Select how long you have had chronic pain.
* **Current Pain Severity (7)**: Rate your current pain severity on a 0-10 scale.
* **Pain Management Strategies Currently Using (9)**: Select all the strategies you are currently using to manage your pain.
* **Which strategy is MOST helpful for you (10)**: Select the most helpful pain management strategy for you.
* **Medication Satisfaction (12)**: Rate your satisfaction with your current medication.
* **Experience Side Effects from Pain Medications (13)**: Rate the side effects you experience from your pain medications.
* **Barriers to Effective Pain Management (15)**: Select all the barriers or challenges you face in managing your pain.
* **How much does pain affect your quality of life (17)**: Choose how much pain affects your quality of life.
* **Emotional Impact of Chronic Pain (18)**: Select all the emotional impacts that you experience due to your chronic pain.
* **Ability to Work or Perform Daily Tasks (19)**: Rate your ability to work or perform daily tasks.
* **Are you currently working with a pain management specialist (21)**: Answer yes or no to this question.
* **Types of Support You Need (22)**: Select all the types of support you need for pain management.
* **Would you be interested in a pain management program (23)**: Rate your interest in a pain management program.
