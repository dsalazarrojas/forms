# EMDR Session Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients after an EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) session. It aims to help therapists understand the client's experience, identify areas for improvement, and provide valuable insights for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Session ID to identify the session you are providing feedback for.
2. Enter your Client Name, which should match the name of the client who attended the session.
3. Select the Session Date and Session Time of the session you are providing feedback for.
4. Choose your feedback type: Positive, Neutral, or Negative Feedback.
5. Rate your experience using the rating scale: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.
6. Enter any additional Comments about your experience.
7. Enter the Therapist Name who facilitated the session.
8. Add Session Notes to provide more information about the session.
9. Enter Feedback Notes to share any specific feedback or suggestions.
10. Select Save, Cancel, or Save and Submit as your preferred action.
11. Enter your contact information, including Email, Phone, Address, City, State, and Zip Code.
12. Add any final Notes about your experience.
13. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
