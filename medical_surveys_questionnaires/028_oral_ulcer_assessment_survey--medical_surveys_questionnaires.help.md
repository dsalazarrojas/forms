# Oral Ulcer Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals assess and manage oral ulcers in patients. The form gathers information on patient details, symptoms, pain level, duration, location, frequency, treatment options, follow-up frequency, and other relevant medical history and allergies. The information collected will aid in providing personalized care and treatment plans for patients with oral ulcers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your personal details as requested.
2. Describe your current symptoms and pain level.
3. Report the duration of the current ulcer.
4. Specify the location of the current ulcer.
5. Indicate the frequency of ulcers, if applicable.
6. Choose any relevant treatment options.
7. Mention the follow-up frequency.
8. Provide any additional notes about your condition.
9. Share your medical history, if applicable.
10. Report any medication side effects.
11. List your current medications.
12. Mention any other medical conditions you are experiencing.
13. Indicate any allergies you have.
14. Provide the name and contact information of your medical professional.
15. Share your medical history, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Details** (`patient_details`, text, required: false): Please enter your name, age, and any other relevant identification information.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required: false): Describe the current symptoms you are experiencing, such as soreness, swelling, or pain.
* **Pain Level** (`pain_level`, number, required: false): Rate your current pain level using a number value (e.g., 1-10).
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, required: false): Indicate the number of days or weeks the ulcer has been present.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Describe the exact location of the ulcer in your mouth or gums.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, text, required: false): Indicate how often you experience ulcers, if they are recurring.
* **Treatment Options** (`treatment_options`, text, required: false): Choose any relevant treatment options you have tried or are considering.
* **Follow Up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, text, required: false): Indicate the frequency of follow-up visits or check-ups you have scheduled.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or details about your condition.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Share any relevant medical history, such as previous illnesses or surgeries.
* **Medication Side Effects** (`medication_side_effects`, text, required: false): Report any side effects from medications you are taking.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, required: false): List your current medications.
* **Other Medical Conditions** (`other_medical_conditions`, text, required: false): Mention any other medical conditions you are experiencing.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required: false): List any allergies you have.
* **Medical Professional** (`medical_professional`, text, required: false): Provide the name and contact information of your current medical professional.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, text, required: false): Share any additional medical history or information.
* **Other Medical Conditions** (`other_medical_conditions`, text, required: false): Repeat of the field above, likely a typo in the original form definition.
