# sports_medicine_practice_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports medicine practice quiz is a questionnaire designed for medical professionals to collect information about an individual's background and preferences for training. This form helps healthcare providers assess the patient's condition and provide personalized advice.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the instructions carefully and ensure you understand the purpose of the form.
2. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Select the options that best fit your condition.
4. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (1): This section is a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **What is your name** (2): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **What is your age** (3): Enter your age in years.
* **What is your height** (4): Enter your height in centimeters.
* **What is your weight** (5): Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Select your favorite sports** (6): Choose one or more sports that you enjoy participating in.
* **Select your preferred training location** (7): Choose where you prefer to train (indoor, outdoor, gym, home, etc.).
* **Select your preferred time of day for training** (8): Choose your preferred time of day for training (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
* **Select your preferred training frequency** (9): Choose how frequently you want to train (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **What is your injury history?** (10): Briefly describe any injuries or medical conditions you have experienced.
* **What is your preferred contact method?** (11): Please enter your preferred method of contact (e.g., phone, email, text).
* **What is your phone number?** (12): Enter your phone number.
* **Select your preferred language** (13): Choose the language you prefer to communicate in.
* **What is your emergency contact person?** (14): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.
* **What is your emergency contact information?** (15): Enter the contact information of your emergency contact person.
* **What is your medical history?** (16): Briefly describe any medical conditions you have.
* **What is your current medication?** (17): List any current medications you are taking.
* **Select your preferred doctor** (18): Choose the doctor you prefer to consult.
* **Select your preferred medical condition** (19): Choose the medical condition you are concerned about.
* **Select your preferred sport** (20): Choose the sport you prefer to train for.
* **Select your preferred sport for training** (21): Choose the sport you want to train for.
* **Select your preferred training style** (22): Choose the intensity level you prefer for your training (high, low, moderate).
* **What is your preferred training duration?** (23): Enter how long you prefer to train in hours.
* **What is your training frequency?** (24): Enter how frequently you want to train per week.
* **What is your preferred training schedule?** (25): Enter how often you want to train per week.
