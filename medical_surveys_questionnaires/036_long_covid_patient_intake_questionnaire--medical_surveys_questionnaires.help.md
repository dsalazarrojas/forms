# Long COVID Patient Intake Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients about their experiences with Long COVID. It helps healthcare providers better understand the symptoms and impact of Long COVID on patients' daily lives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start on the first page, which is the introduction page. This page provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
2. On the second page, please provide a brief history of your Long COVID symptoms. This will help healthcare providers understand the duration of your symptoms.
3. On the following pages, you will be asked to rate the impact of each symptom on your daily life. Please rate the impact using the scale:
	* Very Low
	* Low
	* Moderate
	* High
	* Extremely High

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**
	* This is the first page of the form. It explains the purpose and structure of the form.
* **Long COVID History**
	* Please provide a brief history of your Long COVID symptoms, including when they started and how long you've been experiencing them.
* **Symptom Impact**
	* Rate the impact of each symptom on your daily life using the scale above. For example, if you experience:
		+ **Very Low**: You experience the symptom occasionally, but it doesn't affect your daily life much.
		+ **Low**: The symptom affects you some of the time, but you can still perform daily activities.
		+ **Moderate**: The symptom affects you regularly, and it impacts your daily activities.
		+ **High**: The symptom affects you most of the time, and it significantly impacts your daily activities.
		+ **Extremely High**: The symptom affects you all the time, and it severely impacts your daily activities.
