# Patient Extensive Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect detailed information about a patient's medical condition, contact details, and personal history to provide a comprehensive overview of their health status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling out the form from top to bottom, page by page.**
2. **Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.**
3. **If you are unsure about any question, ask for help or clarification.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Condition**: Please describe your current medical condition, including any symptoms, illnesses, or health issues you are experiencing. This information will help our medical team understand your current health status.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number, which can be a phone number or email address, to enable us to get in touch with you in case of any urgent matters.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to help us understand your age and medical history.
* **Address**: Enter your current address to help us with any necessary visits or follow-ups.
* **Medical History**: Describe any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or health-related events that may be relevant to your current condition.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have, including any medications or substances that cause adverse reactions.
* **Social Security Number**: Enter your Social Security Number, which is a required field to ensure accurate identification.
* **Emergency Contact**: Name one person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Family History**: Describe any family medical history, including any conditions or surgeries that may be relevant to your current health status.
* **Medical Insurance**: Provide information about your current medical insurance, including any relevant policy details.
* **Medication List**: List any medications you are currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Surgical History**: Describe any previous surgeries or surgical procedures you have undergone.
* **Additional Comments**: If there's anything else you'd like to add or mention regarding your health status, please use this field to note it.

**Tips**:
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any question, ask for help or clarification.
* This form is a comprehensive overview of your health status, so please take your time and provide as much detail as possible.
