# Attachment Style Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Attachment Style Quiz is a self-assessment tool designed to help you understand your attachment style in relationships. It consists of 10 scenarios, each describing a common interaction or feeling in a romantic relationship. By choosing the response that best describes your typical behavior, you'll gain insight into your attachment tendencies and how they might impact your relationships.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read each scenario carefully and choose the response that best describes your typical behavior.
2.  Answer all questions in a way that reflects your genuine feelings and experiences.
3.  Be honest and genuine in your responses, as this will provide the most accurate assessment.
4.  Select the response that best fits each scenario, even if it's not the most socially desirable option.
5.  Answer as many questions as you feel comfortable with, and feel free to skip any that make you uncomfortable.
6.  Once you've answered all questions, review and reflect on your responses to gain a deeper understanding of your attachment style.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Your first name is a required field and will be used to identify you in the survey results.
*   **Last Name**: Your last name is also a required field and will be used to identify you in the survey results.
*   **Email Address**: This is a required field to receive the survey results. Please enter a valid email address.
*   **Age Group**: Choose the age group that best describes you.
*   **Quiz Instructions**: Read the instructions carefully before proceeding with the quiz.
*   **Scenario 1**: Your partner mentions they need some space. How do you feel?
    *   Anxious and worried: You feel uneasy and concerned about your partner's needs.
    *   Relieved and independent: You feel relieved and happy that your partner needs space.
    *   Confused but understanding: You're not sure what to do, but you try to understand your partner's needs.
    *   Hurt and rejected: You feel hurt and rejected by your partner's need for space.
*   **Scenario 2**: During a conflict with your partner, what do you usually do?
    *   Seek reassurance and closeness: You try to resolve conflicts by getting physically or emotionally close to your partner.
    *   Withdraw and distance yourself: You distance yourself from your partner during conflicts.
    *   Try to understand their perspective: You make an effort to see things from your partner's point of view.
    *   Suppress your emotions: You try to hide or repress your emotions during conflicts.
*   **Scenario 3**: When your partner expresses emotion, what do you feel?
    *   Emotionally connected: You feel a strong emotional connection with your partner.
    *   Uncomfortable or overwhelmed: You feel uneasy or overwhelmed by your partner's emotions.
    *   Empathetic and supportive: You offer support and understanding to your partner.
    *   Detached: You feel disconnected from your partner's emotions.
*   **Scenario 4**: What's your biggest fear in relationships?
    *   Being abandoned: You fear being left or abandoned by your partner.
    *   Losing independence: You fear losing your freedom or autonomy in the relationship.
    *   Emotional vulnerability: You fear being emotionally open with your partner.
    *   Conflict: You fear disagreements or conflicts in the relationship.
*   **Scenario 5**: When your partner is upset with you, what do you do?
    *   Panic and try to fix things immediately: You quickly try to resolve the issue.
    *   Become defensive or distant: You become defensive or create emotional distance.
    *   Listen and address their concerns: You actively listen to and try to understand your partner's concerns.
    *   Minimize the issue: You downplay or ignore the issue.
*   **Scenario 6**: What's your comfort level with asking for help?
    *   High - I ask often: You frequently ask for help or support.
    *   Low - I prefer independence: You prefer to handle things on your own.
    *   Moderate - depends on the situation: You ask for help sometimes, but not always.
    *   Very Low - I rarely ask: You rarely ask for help or support.
*   **Scenario 7**: When you're apart from your partner, what do you feel?
    *   Anxious or lonely: You feel anxious or lonely when separated from your partner.
    *   Comfortable and independent: You feel comfortable and independent when apart.
    *   Neutral: You have mixed feelings.
    *   Relieved: You feel relieved when apart.
*   **Scenario 8**: What's your approach to emotional intimacy?
    *   I crave deep connection: You seek intense emotional closeness.
    *   I prefer surface-level interactions: You prefer casual, surface-level interactions.
    *   I am balanced: You find a balance between emotional closeness and independence.
    *   I feel uncertain: You're unsure about your approach to emotional intimacy.
*   **Scenario 9**: How do you react when receiving criticism from your partner?
    *   Feel devastated and question the relationship: You feel threatened and question the relationship.
    *   Feel dismissive or angry: You become defensive or dismissive.
    *   Listen thoughtfully and reflect: You listen carefully and reflect on your partner's criticism.
    *   Shut down emotionally: You emotionally shut down or withdraw.
*   **Scenario 10**: How do you see yourself in relationships?
    *   Needing constant reassurance: You need frequent reassurance or validation.
    *   Self-sufficient and independent: You're confident and independent.
    *   Secure and balanced: You're emotionally secure and balanced in relationships.
    *   Confused about your needs: You're unsure about your needs or desires.
*   **Current Relationship Status**: Describe your current relationship status.
*   **How Satisfied Are You with Your Current Relationship?**: Rate your satisfaction with your current relationship.
*   **Would You Be Interested in Therapy or Coaching?**: Based on your attachment style, would you be interested in therapy or coaching?
*   **How Would You Describe Your Childhood Experiences with Caregivers?**: Describe your experiences with your caregivers in childhood.
*   **What Areas Would You Like to Improve in Your Relationships?**: Describe areas you'd like to improve in your relationships.
*   **Do You Notice Attachment Patterns in Your Family?**: Describe any patterns you've observed in your family.
*   **Additional Insights**: Share any additional insights or thoughts about your attachment style.
*   **May We Send You Personalized Insights?**: Would you like to receive personalized insights based on your answers?

## Tips

*   Be honest and genuine in your responses.
*   Take your time to reflect on each scenario before choosing your response.
*   Don't worry about what others might think; focus on your own experiences and feelings.
*   If you're unsure about a question, choose the response that best fits your behavior.
