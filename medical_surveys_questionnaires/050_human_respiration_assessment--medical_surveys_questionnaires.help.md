# Human Respiration Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Human Respiration Assessment form is designed to collect information about your respiratory health and related factors. This form will help healthcare professionals understand your overall respiratory status and provide necessary guidance for treatment or further evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and accurately, as they are crucial for a thorough assessment.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for questions that only require a simple response.
3. Enter numerical values for questions that require numerical data.
4. Provide detailed descriptions for open-ended questions like "medication_use", "previous_conditions", "work_environment", "smoke_exposure", and "smoke_exercise".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respiration Symptoms (`respiration_symptoms`):**
	+ Please choose all the symptoms that apply to you.
* **Risk Factors (`risk_factors`):**
	+ Enter any risk factors that may impact your respiratory health.
* **Smoking History (`smoking_history`):**
	+ Choose "Yes" if you have a smoking history.
* **Exercise History (`exercise_history`):**
	+ Enter the number of times you exercise per week.
* **Medication Use (`medication_use`):**
	+ Describe any medications you are currently taking.
* **Respiratory Risk (`respiratory_risk`):**
	+ Enter your estimated respiratory risk level (e.g., 1-5).
* **Previous Conditions (`previous_conditions`):**
	+ Describe any previous conditions that may impact your respiratory health.
* **Allergies (`allergies`):**
	+ Describe any allergies you have.
* **Family History (`family_history`):**
	+ Choose "Yes" if you have a family history of respiratory issues.
* **Work Environment (`work_environment`):**
	+ Describe your work environment and potential exposures.
* **Height (`height`):**
	+ Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight (`weight`):**
	+ Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Age (`age`):**
	+ Enter your age in years.
* **Respiratory Rate (`respiratory_rate`):**
	+ Enter your current respiratory rate.
* **Respiratory Rate Unit (`respiratory_rate_unit`):**
	+ Choose "Good" or "Fair" for your current respiratory rate.
* **Smoking Frequency (`smoking_frequency`):**
	+ Describe how often you smoke.
* **Smoke (`smoke`):**
	+ Choose "Yes" if you smoke.
* **Smoke Unit (`smoke_unit`):**
	+ Choose "Yes" if you smoke.
* **Smoke Duration (`smoke_duration`):**
	+ Describe how long you have smoked.
* **Smoke Amount (`smoke_amount`):**
	+ Describe your smoking amount per day.
* **Smoke Frequency (`smoke_frequency`):**
	+ Describe how often you smoke.
* **Smoke History (`smoke_history`):**
	+ Choose "Yes" if you have a smoking history.
* **Smoke Exercise (`smoke_exercise`):**
	+ Describe how exercise affects your smoking.
* **Smoke Exposure (`smoke_exposure`):**
	+ Describe your exposure to smoke in your environment.
