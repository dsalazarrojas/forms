# Beauty Salon COVID 19 Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client information and ensure that they understand the risks associated with COVID-19 in salon settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your stylist or salon staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (2): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Date of Birth** (3): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1999-02-01).
* **Email Address** (4): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (5): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (6): Enter your address.
* **Appointment Date** (8): Enter the date of your scheduled appointment.
* **Type of Service** (9): Select the type of service you are having (Hair, Makeup, Nails, Waxing, Skincare, or Other).
* **Have You Tested Positive for COVID-19** (11): Choose 'Yes' if you have tested positive for COVID-19.
* **If Yes - When** (12): If you have tested positive for COVID-19, enter the date you tested positive.
* **Recent COVID-19 Symptoms** (13): If you have been experiencing any symptoms related to COVID-19 in the last 14 days, enter them here (e.g., fever, cough, fatigue, loss of taste or smell).
* **Close Contact with COVID-19 Positive Person** (14): Choose 'Yes' if you have been in close contact with someone who has tested positive for COVID-19.
* **Recent Travel to High-Risk Areas** (16): Choose 'Yes' if you have traveled to any high-risk areas in the last 14 days.
* **Do You Work in Healthcare or High-Risk Setting** (17): Choose 'Yes' if you work in healthcare or a high-risk setting.
* **Underlying Health Conditions** (19): If you have any health conditions such as diabetes, heart disease, or immunocompromised, enter them here.
* **Are You 65 Years or Older** (20): Choose 'Yes' if you are 65 years or older.
* **I Understand the Risks of COVID-19 in Salon Settings** (22): Choose 'Yes' if you understand the risks of COVID-19 in salon settings.
* **I Acknowledge All Precautions Explained** (23): Choose 'Yes' to acknowledge that you understand all the precautions taken by the salon.
* **I Accept the Risk and Assume Full Responsibility** (24): Choose 'Yes' to accept the risk and take full responsibility.
* **I Hold the Salon Harmless from Any COVID-19 Related Claims** (25): Choose 'Yes' to hold the salon harmless from any COVID-19 related claims.
* **Client Signature** (26): Type your name to confirm you have read and understood this waiver.
* **Date** (27): Enter the date you sign this waiver.

Please review and sign this form carefully before your appointment. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your stylist or salon staff for assistance.
