# Knowledge Assessment For Seniors - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess your knowledge about healthy aging, including staying hydrated, managing blood pressure, and other related topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction page, which provides a brief overview of the form's purpose.
2. On the demographics page, you can provide any identifying information you feel comfortable sharing. This information will be used to help us better understand your background.
3. On the knowledge page, select all the correct answers from the list of multiple-choice questions. This will help us evaluate your knowledge about healthy aging.
4. On the confidence page, rate your confidence level in answering the previous questions.
5. Finally, on the conclusion page, you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `Note`, required): This page provides a brief overview of the form's purpose and instructions.
* **Demographics** (`Demographics`, `Text`, optional): Please provide any identifying information you feel comfortable sharing, such as your name, age, or contact information.
* **Healthy Ageing Knowledge** (`Healthy Ageing Knowledge`, `Select Multiple`, required): Select all the correct answers from the list of multiple-choice questions. For example:
	+ What are some of the key components of a healthy aging plan?
		- Answer 1: Exercise regularly
		- Answer 2: Manage stress
		- Answer 3: Get enough sleep
* **Confidence** (`Confidence`, `Select Multiple`, required): Rate your confidence level in answering the previous questions. For example:
	+ How would you rate your confidence level?
		- Answer 1: High
		- Answer 2: Medium
		- Answer 3: Low
* **Conclusion** (`Conclusion`, `Note`, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the form.
