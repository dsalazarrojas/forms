# HIPAA Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HIPAA Medical History Form is used to collect essential medical information from patients to improve the quality of care they receive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact details page with the patient's name, address, and contact information.
2. On the symptoms page, list any symptoms the patient is experiencing.
3. On the medications page, list any medications the patient is currently taking.
4. On the allergies page, list any allergies the patient has.
5. On the drug use page, provide information on any substances the patient uses.
6. On the family history page, provide details on any family medical history.
7. On the medical history page, list any significant medical events or conditions.
8. On the contact preferences page, indicate how the patient prefers to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or mail).
9. On the emergency contact page, provide the name, phone number, and relationship of the emergency contact person.
10. Finally, sign the form to confirm that you have accurately completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required/optional): This page asks for the patient's name, address, and contact information.

### Symptoms
* **Symptoms** (`Symptoms`, `text`, required/optional): This page is used to list any symptoms the patient is experiencing.

### Medications
* **Medications** (`Medications`, `text`, required/optional): This page lists any medications the patient is currently taking.

### Allergies
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, `text`, required/optional): This page lists any allergies the patient has.

### Drug Use
* **Drug Use** (`Drug Use`, `text`, required/optional): This page provides information on any substances the patient uses.

### Family History
* **Family History** (`Family History`, `text`, required/optional): This page provides details on any family medical history.

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, required/optional): This page lists any significant medical events or conditions.

### Contact Preferences
* **Contact Preferences** (`Contact Preferences`, `text`, required/optional): This page indicates how the patient prefers to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or mail).

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, required/optional): This page asks for the name, phone number, and relationship of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Phone
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, `text`, required/optional): This page asks for the phone number of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Relationship
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select from the list to indicate the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Address
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`Emergency Contact Address`, `text`, required/optional): This page asks for the address of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Notes
* **Emergency Contact Notes** (`Emergency Contact Notes`, `note`, required/optional): This page is for any additional information about the emergency contact person.

### Patient Signature
* **Patient Signature** (`Patient Signature`, `text`, required/optional): This page requires the patient's signature to confirm that the form has been accurately completed.

### Healthcare Provider Signature
* **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`Healthcare Provider Signature`, `text`, required/optional): This page requires the healthcare provider's signature to confirm that the form has been accurately completed.
