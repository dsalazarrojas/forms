# Mental Health Metrics Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Metrics Monitoring Form is designed to monitor and track mental health progress over time. It's a tool for healthcare professionals to gather information on patients' mental health metrics, including their current mental health status, treatment goals, symptoms, and follow-up details. This form aims to provide a comprehensive snapshot of a patient's mental health situation, enabling healthcare professionals to better understand the patient's needs and provide tailored care.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully to ensure you understand what each field is asking.
2. Answer each field to the best of your ability.
3. Be thorough and honest in your answers, as this information helps healthcare professionals understand your mental health situation better.
4. If you're unsure about any question, you can ask your healthcare professional for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief note about the purpose of this form and what you can expect.
	+ This section provides context for the following questions. Please feel free to add any relevant information or details about your mental health journey.
* **Demographics**: 
	+ Please provide your basic information for our records. This includes any details about yourself such as your name, age, or contact information (if desired).
* **Mental Health Status**: 
	+ Select one of the options that best describes your current mental health status:
		+ Anxiety
		+ Bipolar Disorder
		+ Depressive Disorder
		+ Eating Disorder
		+ Post Traumatic Stress Disorder
* **Treatment Goals**: 
	+ Select all the goals you aim to achieve through treatment:
		+ Mood regulation
		+ Anxiety reduction
		+ Sleep enhancement
		+ Energy level improvement
* **Symptoms**: 
	+ Please specify any current mental health symptoms you're experiencing:
		+ Anxiety
		+ Concentration problems
		+ Fatigue
		+ Insomnia
* **Wellness Scale**: 
	+ (Note: This field might not be applicable for this form)
* **Treatment Outcome**: 
	+ Select the option that best describes your current treatment outcome:
		+ Satisfied
		+ Somewhat Satisfied
		+ Neutral
		+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
		+ Dissatisfied
* **Follow-up**: 
	+ Enter the date of your next follow-up appointment or check-in.
* **Mental Health Professional**: 
	+ If you're seeing a mental health professional, please provide their name.
* **Mental Health Professional Phone**: 
	+ If you're seeing a mental health professional, please provide their phone number.
* **Mental Health Professional Email**: 
	+ If you're seeing a mental health professional, please provide their email address.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your answers.
- If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
- This form is for your benefit and helps healthcare professionals provide better care for you.
