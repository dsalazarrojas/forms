## Recently Contacted List - Help Guide
### Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about patients who have recently been in contact with your organization. It helps healthcare professionals keep track of patients' contact history and maintain accurate records. This form is intended for medical professionals to use as a reference tool for their practice.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **Recently Contacted List Form**: This is the introductory page with a brief message. Please fill out this form to provide information about patients you have recently contacted.
2. **Name**: Enter the name of the patient you have recently been in contact with.
3. **Last Contacted**: Select the date of your last contact with the patient using the calendar input field.
4. **Patient ID**: Enter the patient's ID number (if applicable).

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: The patient's name as provided during contact.
* **Last Contacted**: The date of the last time you were in contact with the patient. Use the calendar input field to select the date.
* **Patient ID**: A unique identifier for the patient. This is usually a number assigned to the patient by your organization or a medical record system.
