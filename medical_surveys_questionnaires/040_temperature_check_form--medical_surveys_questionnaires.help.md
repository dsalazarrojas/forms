# Temperature Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Temperature Check Form is used to gather information about a patient's current temperature and exposure to a possible illness or infection. This form helps track and manage patient data in a medical setting.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the patient's current temperature using the numerical input field.
2.  Enter the duration of exposure to the possible illness or infection in the numerical field.
3.  Provide the location where the patient was exposed to the illness or infection in the text field.
4.  Describe the symptoms experienced by the patient in the text field.
5.  Enter the temperature during exposure to the illness or infection in the numerical field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Temperature**: Enter the patient's current body temperature. Please ensure this value is accurate.
*   **Duration of exposure**: Record the length of time the patient spent exposed to the possible illness or infection. Use hours for this field.
*   **Location of exposure**: Enter the place where the patient was exposed to the possible illness or infection. Be specific for accurate tracking.
*   **Symptoms**: Provide an account of the symptoms experienced by the patient, if any.
*   **Temperature during exposure**: Enter the patient's temperature during the exposure. This is useful for tracking changes in body temperature over time. 

## Tips

*   Be as accurate as possible when filling out this form.
*   Ensure you understand the patient's condition before submitting the form.
*   Use this form only for patients meeting the specific criteria mentioned earlier. 

This help guide is designed to support users in filling out the form correctly, while avoiding potential misuse. 

(Note: The fields are all optional, so users must ensure that they are providing accurate and complete information, even if some fields are empty.)
