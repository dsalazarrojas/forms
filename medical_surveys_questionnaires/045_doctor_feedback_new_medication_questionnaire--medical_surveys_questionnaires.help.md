# Doctor Feedback: New Medication Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect valuable feedback from doctors about a new medication. The goal is to gather information about the medication's effectiveness, patient outcomes, and overall satisfaction. Your input will help us improve the medication and provide better care for patients.

## How To Complete This Form

- Read through each field carefully and answer all questions that apply.
- If a field is not applicable or doesn't apply to your experience, please indicate this by selecting "N/A" or "None" if possible.
- Use the provided examples or instructions for each field as a reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Introduction** (ID 1): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully to understand the purpose and context of the form.
  - **What is the name of the new medication?** (ID 2): Enter the name of the new medication that you have experience with.
  - **What is the dosage?** (ID 3): Enter the dosage of the medication that you have administered or prescribed.
  - **What are the outcome results?** (ID 4): Select the outcome of the medication:
    * Positive: The patient experienced a significant improvement in their condition.
    * Negative: The patient experienced a significant worsening of their condition.
    * Neutral: The patient did not experience a significant change in their condition.
  - **What are the side effects?** (ID 5): Select all side effects that you observed in the patient:
    * Dizziness
    * Fatigue
    * Nausea
    * Headache
    * Other (please specify)
  - **Please provide your comments** (ID 6): Share any additional comments or observations about the medication.
  - **How satisfied are you?** (ID 7): Rate your overall satisfaction with the medication:
    * Very Satisfied
    * Somewhat Satisfied
    * Neutral
    * Somewhat Dissatisfied
    * Very Dissatisfied
  - **How comparative is it to other medications?** (ID 8): Rate how the new medication compares to other medications:
    * Much Better
    * A Little Better
    * No Different
    * A Little Worse
    * Much Worse
