# Spa Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Consultation Form is designed to collect information from patients for a thorough understanding of their needs and medical history. This form is used to gather essential details to ensure we can tailor our services to your specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Spa Consultation Form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name and Last Name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your Age in the "Age" field.
3. Enter the date and time of your visit in the "Date of Visit" and "Time of Visit" fields, respectively.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Provide your contact phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. If you have any medical conditions, please describe them in the "Medical Conditions" field.
7. If you have any allergies, select one or more from the list in the "Allergies" field.
8. Choose your preferred treatment options from the list in the "Preferred Treatment" field.
9. Select the staff member you prefer to work with from the list in the "Preferred Staff" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your last name here.
* **Age** (`number`, required: false): Enter your age.
* **Date of Visit** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time of Visit** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time of your visit.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Provide your phone number.
* **Medical Conditions** (`note`, required: false): Describe any medical conditions you may have.
* **Allergies** (`select_one`, required: false): Select one or more from the list if you have any allergies.
* **Peanut Allergy** (option in `Allergies`, required: false): Select if you have a peanut allergy.
* **Latex Allergy** (option in `Allergies`, required: false): Select if you have a latex allergy.
* **Other (specify)** (option in `Allergies`, required: false): Select if you have other allergies and specify them in the "Medical Conditions" field.
* **Facials** (option in `Preferred Treatment`, required: false): Select if you prefer facials.
* **Massage** (option in `Preferred Treatment`, required: false): Select if you prefer a massage.
* **Manicures** (option in `Preferred Treatment`, required: false): Select if you prefer a manicure.
* **Pedicures** (option in `Preferred Treatment`, required: false): Select if you prefer a pedicure.
* **Esthetician** (option in `Preferred Staff`, required: false): Select if you prefer to work with an esthetician.
* **Therapist** (option in `Preferred Staff`, required: false): Select if you prefer to work with a therapist.
* **Trainer** (option in `Preferred Staff`, required: false): Select if you prefer to work with a trainer.
* **Hydrapist** (option in `Preferred Staff`, required: false): Select if you prefer to work with a hydrapist.
