# Business Health Check Up Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a business's health and performance. It's designed to be completed by business owners or administrators to assess their business's strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2.  Go through each section one by one, answering questions and filling out fields accordingly.
3.  Use the provided hints as guidance for each field.
4.  Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "Required" in the field explanation).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. <code>Business Name</code> (label: Business Name, type: text, required: true)
Please enter your business's name exactly as it is written on your business cards, letterhead, or other official documents.

### 2. <code>Industry Sector</code> (label: Industry Sector, type: text, required: true)
Enter the main industry your business operates in, e.g. Healthcare, Retail, Technology.

### 3. <code>Financial Health Rating</code> (label: Financial Health Rating, type: number, required: true)
Rate your business's financial health on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 indicates poor financial health and 5 indicates excellent financial health.

### 4. <code>Market Position Rating</code> (label: Market Position Rating, type: number, required: true)
Rate your business's position in the market on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 indicates weak position and 5 indicates strong position.

### 5. <code>Innovation Capability</code> (label: Innovation Capability, type: select_one, required: true)
Choose your business's level of innovation capability:
*   <code>High (Industry leader)</code>
*   <code>Moderate (Follower)</code>
*   <code>Low (Lagging behind)</code>
*   <code>Minimal (No current innovation)</code>

### 6. <code>Customer Satisfaction Level</code> (label: Customer Satisfaction Level, type: select_one, required: true)
Choose your business's level of customer satisfaction based on recent feedback or reviews:
*   <code>Excellent</code>
*   <code>Good</code>
*   <code>Fair</code>
*   <code>Poor</code>

### 7. <code>Employee Retention Rate</code> (label: Employee Retention Rate, type: select_one, required: true)
Choose your business's employee retention rate:
*   <code>Over 90%</code>
*   <code>75% - 90%</code>
*   <code>50% - 74%</code>
*   <code>Below 50%</code>

### 8. <code>Next Review Date</code> (label: Next Review Date, type: date, required: true)
Enter the date you plan to review these metrics again.

### 9. <code>Identified Top Strength</code> (label: Identified Top Strength, type: text, required: true)
Enter the single best thing about your business.

### 10. <code>Identified Top Weakness</code> (label: Identified Top Weakness, type: text, required: true)
Enter the primary area needing improvement.

### 11. <code>Immediate Growth Opportunity</code> (label: Immediate Growth Opportunity, type: text, required: false)
Enter a specific path for expansion or improvement, if applicable.

### 12. <code>Action Plan for Improvement</code> (label: Action Plan for Improvement, type: text, required: false)
Enter a brief description of the next steps to address weaknesses, if applicable.

Note: This guide is intended to be used as a reference for users, and is subject to change as the form's functionality and requirements may evolve. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out.
