# Beauty Salon Allergy Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beauty Salon Allergy Waiver Form is a required document that must be completed by all clients before receiving any services at the salon. It collects information about the client's allergies, medical conditions, and consent for treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
6. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
7. List any known allergies you have in the "Known Allergies" field.
8. List any product allergies or sensitivities you have in the "Product Allergies or Sensitivities" field.
9. Choose the severity of your allergic reactions in the "Severity of Allergic Reactions" field.
10. If you have had any previous reactions to beauty treatments, list them in the "Previous Reactions to Beauty Treatments" field.
11. If you are taking any current medications that may interact with the treatment, list them in the "Current Medications That May Interact" field.
12. If you have any skin conditions, list them in the "Current Skin Conditions" field.
13. If you have recently received any skin treatments, list them in the "Recent Skin Treatments or Procedures" field.
14. If you agree to perform a patch test, select "Yes" in the "I Agree to Perform a Patch Test" field.
15. If you are willing to wait 48 hours for patch test results, select "Yes" in the "I Am Willing to Wait 48 Hours for Patch Test Results" field.
16. Confirm that you understand the risks of allergic reactions by selecting "Yes" in the "I Understand the Risks of Allergic Reactions" field.
17. Confirm that you accept responsibility for allergic reactions by selecting "Yes" in the "I Accept Responsibility for Allergic Reactions" field.
18. Select "Yes" in the "I Release the Salon from Liability" field to confirm that you release the salon from liability.
19. Sign your name in the "Client Signature" field to confirm that you have read and understand the waiver form.
20. Enter the date in the "Date of Waiver" field to confirm the date of the waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Full Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your active email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, text, required): Enter the name of a contact person we can call in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Known Allergies** (Known Allergies, text, required): List any known allergies you have, such as peanut or shellfish allergy.
* **Product Allergies or Sensitivities** (Product Allergies or Sensitivities, text, required): List any product allergies or sensitivities you have, such as hair dyes, chemicals, or fragrances.
* **Severity of Allergic Reactions** (Severity of Allergic Reactions, select_one, required): Select the severity of your allergic reactions, which can be Mild, Moderate, Severe, or Severe - Anaphylaxis.
* **Previous Reactions to Beauty Treatments** (Previous Reactions to Beauty Treatments, text, optional): If you have had any previous reactions to beauty treatments, list them here.
* **Current Medications That May Interact** (Current Medications That May Interact, text, optional): List any current medications that may interact with the treatment.
* **Skin Type** (Skin Type, select_one, required): Select your skin type, which can be Sensitive, Normal, Oily, Dry, or Combination.
* **Current Skin Conditions** (Current Skin Conditions, text, optional): List any current skin conditions you have, such as eczema or psoriasis.
* **Recent Skin Treatments or Procedures** (Recent Skin Treatments or Procedures, text, optional): List any recent skin treatments or procedures you have had.
* **I Agree to Perform a Patch Test** (I Agree to Perform a Patch Test, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you agree to perform a patch test.
* **I Am Willing to Wait 48 Hours for Patch Test Results** (I Am Willing to Wait 48 Hours for Patch Test Results, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you are willing to wait 48 hours for patch test results.
* **I Understand the Risks of Allergic Reactions** (I Understand the Risks of Allergic Reactions, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you understand the risks of allergic reactions.
* **I Accept Responsibility for Allergic Reactions** (I Accept Responsibility for Allergic Reactions, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you accept responsibility for allergic reactions.
* **I Release the Salon from Liability** (I Release the Salon from Liability, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you release the salon from liability.
* **Client Signature** (Client Signature, text, required): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understand the waiver form.
* **Date of Waiver** (Date of Waiver, date, required): Enter the date you signed the waiver form.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete this form carefully and accurately to avoid any potential risks or complications.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your beauty therapist for guidance.
* Please answer all questions truthfully to ensure a safe and successful treatment experience.
