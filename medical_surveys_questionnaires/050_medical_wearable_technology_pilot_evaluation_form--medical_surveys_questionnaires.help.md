# Medical Wearable Technology Pilot Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather feedback from medical professionals on the usability and effectiveness of a wearable technology device. It is an important part of the pilot program, helping to shape future development and improvement of the technology.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read through each page carefully and fill out the form one page at a time.
2.  Answer each question according to your genuine experience with the device.
3.  For numerical fields, please provide a numerical value.
4.  For text fields, provide a brief, clear, and concise answer.
5.  Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pilot Program Background** (`pilot_program_background`): A text field where you can provide any relevant background information about the pilot program.
* **Device Usability** (`device_usability`): A numerical field where you can rate your experience with the device's usability, with higher values indicating better usability.
* **Medical Clinician Feedback** (`medical_clinician_feedback`): A selection field where you can choose how satisfied you are with the device. The options are:
	+ Highly satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Highly dissatisfied
* **Device Benefits** (`device_benefits`): A multiple selection field where you can choose the benefits you have experienced with the device. The options are:
	+ Easy to use
	+ Improves health outcomes
	+ Enhances patient engagement
	+ Others (please specify)
	+ None
* **User Experience** (`user_experience`): A text field where you can provide any comments or notes about your experience with the device.
* **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_facing`): A text field where you can list any challenges you faced while using the device.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): A text field where you can provide any additional information that you feel is important for us to know.
* **Email** (`email`): An email address field where you can input your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): A text field where you can input your phone number.
* **Medical Professional Name** (`medical_professional_name`): A text field where you can input your name.
* **Institution** (`institution`): A text field where you can input the institution where you work.
* **Date** (`date`): A date field where you can input the date you used the device.
* **Time** (`time`): A time field where you can input the time you used the device.
* **Note** (`note`): A text field where you can input any notes you have.
* **Form Filled By** (`form_filled_by`): A multiple selection field where you can choose who filled out this form. The options are:
	+ Participant
	+ Researcher
	+ Institutional Review Board (IRB)
* **Device Manufacturer** (`device_manufacturer`): A text field where you can input the device manufacturer.
* **Device Model** (`device_model`): A text field where you can input the device model.
* **Device Version** (`device_version`): A text field where you can input the device version.
* **Device Serial Number** (`device_serial_number`): A text field where you can input the device serial number.
* **Device Software Version** (`device_software_version`): A text field where you can input the device software version.
* **Pilot Group** (`pilot_group`): A text field where you can input the pilot group.
* **Pilot Location** (`pilot_location`): A text field where you can input the pilot location.
* **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`): A text field where you can input your medical specialty.
* **Study Duration** (`study_duration`): A text field where you can input the duration of the study.
* **Form Filled By Name** (`form_filled_by_name`): A text field where you can input your name.
