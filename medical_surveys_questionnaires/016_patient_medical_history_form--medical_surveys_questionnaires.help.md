# Patient Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Medical History Form is designed to collect a patient's medical history information for non-medical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a thorough understanding of the form's purpose and relevance.
2. Complete each field accurately and honestly to provide valuable information.
3. Review each field's explanation below for specific guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Info (1)
* **Name (text)**: This field is used to collect your name.
* **Age (text)**: This field is used to collect your age.
* **Gender (text)**: This field is used to collect your gender.

### Weight (2)
* **Weight (text)**: This field is used to collect your weight.

### Allergies (3)
* **Allergies (select multiple)**: This field allows you to select one or more allergies you have.

### Illnesses (4)
* **Illnesses (text)**: This field is used to collect information about your medical history.
* Note: Please list each illness separately.

### Operations (5)
* **Operations (select one)**: This field is used to select one operation you have undergone.

### Healthy Habits (6)
* **Healthy Habits (text)**: This field is used to collect information about healthy habits you have.
* Note: You can list multiple healthy habits.

### Unhealthy Habits (7)
* **Unhealthy Habits (text)**: This field is used to collect information about unhealthy habits you have.
* Note: You can list multiple unhealthy habits.

### Integration (8)
* **Integration (note)**: This field is a note field for any additional integration information.
* Note: This field is not mandatory.

### System Integration (9)
* **System Integration (text)**: This field is used to collect information about system integration.
* Note: This field is not mandatory.

Please note that the provided fields might not be comprehensive, and you might need to add or remove fields based on your specific needs.
