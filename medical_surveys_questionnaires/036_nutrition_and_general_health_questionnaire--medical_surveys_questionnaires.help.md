# Nutrition And General Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's nutrition and general health status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have completed all required fields.
2. Enter your responses in the designated fields.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Client Onboarding
• **Client Onboarding** (`Client Onboarding`, `number`, **optional**): This field is used to record the patient's onboarding process with our healthcare team. You can enter a numerical value to represent the stage of onboarding completed.
### Page 2: Health History
• **Health History** (`health_history`, `text`, **optional**): Describe your medical history in this field. Please be as detailed as possible, including any previous surgeries, illnesses, or allergies.
### Page 3: Nutrition Habits
• **Nutrition Habits** (`nutrition_habits`, `text`, **optional**): Share your eating habits, dietary preferences, and any notable changes in your nutrition habits.
### Page 4: Health Risks
• **Health Risks** (`health_risks`, `select_one`, **optional**): Indicate any health risks you are concerned about, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, or high cholesterol.
### Page 5: Medical Conditions
• **Medical Conditions** (`health_history_2`, `text`, **optional**): List any medical conditions you have been diagnosed with, including any current or past conditions.
### Page 6: Care Plans
• **Care Plans** (`care_plans`, `note`, **optional**): Describe any care plans you have in place, such as medication schedules or follow-up appointments.
### Page 7: Medical Tests
• **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select any medical tests you have undergone, such as blood tests, urine tests, or imaging studies.
### Page 8: Medications
• **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, **optional**): List any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
### Page 9: Allergies
• **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, **optional**): Share any known allergies or adverse reactions to specific substances.
