# Art Based Prejudice Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect responses to a given artwork that may or may not convey bias or prejudice. It will help us understand how people perceive and respond to such art.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form as completely as possible, providing as much detail as you can.
2. Be honest and think carefully about your responses.
3. If you're unsure about a question, choose the best answer you can or select 'Unsure'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant ID** (Text): Enter your anonymous or coded identifier to ensure your data is linked to your responses.
* **Evaluation Date** (Date): Record the date you are evaluating this artwork.
* **Age Group** (Select One): Choose your age group to help us understand demographics.
	+ Under 18
	+ 18-25
	+ 26-35
	+ 36-50
	+ 51-65
	+ 65+
* **Educational Background** (Select One): Choose your level of education.
	+ High school or less
	+ Some college
	+ College graduate
	+ Graduate degree
	+ Prefer not to say
* **Description of Artwork Presented** (Text): Describe what the artwork depicts.
* **Primary Subject of Artwork** (Text): Identify the primary subject of the artwork.
* **First Impression of the Artwork** (Select One): How did you initially feel about the artwork?
	+ Very positive
	+ Positive
	+ Neutral
	+ Negative
	+ Very negative
* **Emotional Response** (Text): Describe your emotional response to the artwork.
* **Immediate Associations or Thoughts** (Text): List any immediate thoughts or associations you have about the artwork.
* **Do you recognize any stereotypes in the artwork?** (Select One): Do you think the artwork conveys any stereotypes?
	+ Yes - clear stereotypes
	+ Yes - some stereotypes
	+ Neutral
	+ No - avoids stereotypes
* **If yes, describe the stereotypes** (Text): If you answered "Yes", list the stereotypes you recognize.
* **Does this artwork challenge your personal perspectives?** (Select One): How does the artwork affect your views?
	+ Yes - significantly
	+ Yes - somewhat
	+ Neutral
	+ No - reinforces my views
* **How aware are you of your own biases?** (Select One): How much do you know about your own biases?
	+ Very aware
	+ Somewhat aware
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat unaware
	+ Very unaware
* **What message do you think the artist intended?** (Text): What do you think the artist was trying to convey?
* **Does this artwork comment on prejudice?** (Select One): How does the artwork relate to prejudice?
	+ Yes - directly
	+ Yes - indirectly
	+ Neutral
	+ False
	+ Unsure
* **How does this artwork relate to your experiences with prejudice?** (Text): Describe how the artwork connects to your personal experiences with prejudice.
* **Likelihood of discussing this artwork with others** (Select One): How likely are you to share this artwork with others?
	+ Very likely
	+ Somewhat likely
	+ Neutral
	+ Unlikely
	+ Very unlikely
* **Has your perspective shifted after viewing this artwork?** (Select One): Has the artwork changed your views?
	+ Significantly shifted
	+ Somewhat shifted
	+ No change
	+ Unsure
* **Additional Reflections or Comments** (Text): Any additional thoughts or comments about the artwork?
