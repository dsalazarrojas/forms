# Dental Office Covid 19 Prescreening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a pre-screening tool to help our office assess the risk level of patients and determine the next steps for their appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each field accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact our office.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (id: 1, Contact Information, text, required: false): Please enter your name, phone number, and email address for us to contact you.
* **Patient Status** (id: 2, Patient Status, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether you are a new or current patient to our office. If you are a current patient, please provide your patient ID number.
* **Medical Condition** (id: 3, Medical Condition, text, required: false): Describe any medical conditions that you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
* **Travel History** (id: 4, Travel History, date, required: false): Enter the dates of your last trip or travel.
* **Symptoms** (id: 5, Symptoms, number, required: false): If you are experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms, please enter the number of symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Contact Trace** (id: 6, Contact Trace, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if you have been in contact with anyone who has tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
* **Vaccination Status** (id: 7, Vaccination Status, select_one, required: false): Indicate your vaccination status, if applicable.
* **Vaccination Received** (id: 8, Vaccination Received, date, required: false): If you have been vaccinated, please enter the date of your last vaccination.
* **Next Action** (id: 9, Next Action, text, required: false): Describe your planned next action, such as a visit to the dentist or a follow-up appointment.

## Tips
Please answer each field accurately and thoroughly, and do not hesitate to contact our office if you have any questions or concerns. The information provided will help us to assess your risk level and determine the next steps for your appointment.
