# Early Pregnancy Symptom And Concern Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about your early pregnancy symptoms and concerns. Please complete this form to help your medical provider understand your current health status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly, but do not worry about spelling mistakes.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask your medical provider for clarification.
4. Please provide your medical provider's contact information to ensure they can reach out to you for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, optional): This section is a brief introduction about yourself and your pregnancy. Please provide your name, your doctor's name (if you have one), and any other relevant information.
* **Early Pregnancy Symptoms** (`early_pregnancy_symptoms`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check all that apply to any of the listed symptoms you are experiencing during your pregnancy. These symptoms include:
	+ Morning sickness
	+ Fatigue
	+ Dizziness
	+ Back pain
	+ Headaches
* **Concerns** (`concerns`, `text`, optional): If you have any concerns or questions about your pregnancy, please list them here.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them here.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `select_one`, optional): If you are taking any medications, please select the one that best matches your current medication.
	+ None
	+ Morning sickness medication
	+ Folic acid
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Last Pregnancy Test Results** (`last_pregnancy_test_results`, `select_one`, optional): If you have had any previous pregnancy tests, please select the result:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Unknown/Waiting
* **Next Follow Up** (`next_follow_up`, `date`, optional): Please provide the date of your next appointment or checkup with your medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Name** (`medical_provider_name`, `text`, optional): Please provide your medical provider's name.
* **Medical Provider Phone** (`medical_provider_phone`, `text`, optional): Please provide your medical provider's phone number.
* **Medical Provider Email** (`medical_provider_email`, `email`, optional): Please provide your medical provider's email address.
