# Outpatient Care Service Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outpatient Care Service Assessment Form is used to evaluate the quality of service provided by the care team and gather feedback from both the patient and the staff to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select one of the options for each field.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the text fields.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: Please select "Yes" if the patient's visit was for an outpatient care service, and "No" otherwise.
* **patient_id**: This field is used to record the patient's ID number for identification purposes.
* **patient_name**: Please enter the patient's name.
* **date_of_service**: Enter the date of the patient's visit.
* **time_of_service**: Enter the time of the patient's visit.
* **care_team_name**: Enter the name of the care team member(s) who provided the service.
* **care_team_role**: Enter the role(s) of the care team member(s) who provided the service.
* **service_type**: Select "Yes" if the service was provided as requested, and "No" otherwise.
* **reason_for_visit**: Select "Yes" if the visit was for a reason that was previously discussed with the patient, and "No" otherwise.
* **overall_satisfaction**: Select how satisfied you are with the service, with "Satisfied" being the highest rating and "Dissatisfied" being the lowest.
* **service_quality**: Select the quality of the service provided, with "Good" being the highest rating and "Poor" being the lowest.
* **communication_effectiveness**: Select "Yes" if the communication was effective, and "No" otherwise.
* **recommendation**: Select "Yes" if you would recommend this service to others, and "No" otherwise.
* **comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the service.
* **additional_comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the service.
* **patient_comments**: Provide any comments or feedback from the patient.
* **staff_comments**: Provide any comments or feedback from the care team member.
* **follow_up_suggestion**: Suggest any follow-up actions or recommendations for improvement.
