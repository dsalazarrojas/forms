# Nutritional Protocol Recommendations Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutritional Protocol Recommendations Form is designed to collect information about patients' medical conditions, nutritional protocols, and other relevant health status details. This form is used by medical professionals to gather data for clinical or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate medical survey or questionnaire from the list.
2. Provide the patient's name, contact number, and email address (if available).
3. Select any applicable medical specialities, medical conditions, and medical diagnoses.
4. Indicate whether the patient takes medication or not.
5. Provide any additional nutrition protocol or medical notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Surveys & Questionnaires**: Select one or multiple medical surveys or questionnaires that apply to the patient. This field helps determine which set of questions to ask the patient based on their medical history.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address (if available).
* **Age**: Enter the patient's age.
* **Medical Specialty**: Select the relevant medical specialty (e.g., Cardio, Neuro, Endo) related to the patient's condition.
* **Medical Condition**: Select any applicable medical conditions (e.g., Hypertension, Diabetes, Hypothyroidism) related to the patient.
* **Medication Use**: Indicate whether the patient takes medication or not.
* **Nutrition Protocol**: Provide any additional nutrition protocol or recommendations.
* **Medical Supplement**: Select the medical supplement (e.g., Probiotics, Vitamine) related to the patient.
* **Food Recommendation**: Provide any food recommendations.
* **Follow Up**: Schedule a follow-up appointment or date for the patient.
* **Next Checkup**: Schedule the next checkup date.
* **Medical Note**: Enter any additional medical notes or observations.
* **Medical Diagnosis 1** to **Medical Diagnosis 9**: Enter any additional medical diagnoses (if necessary).
