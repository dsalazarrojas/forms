# Patient Recovery Outcome Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide healthcare professionals with a comprehensive assessment of a patient's recovery outcome after a medical treatment or surgery. It helps in understanding the patient's current condition, recovery progress, and expectations, and informs decisions for their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and as accurately as possible.
2. Choose the appropriate option for each field from the provided list.
3. Provide any additional comments or concerns you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### How are you feeling today
*   **How are you feeling today** (`patient_recovery_outcome_assessment_form_1`, text, optional): This field is used to describe your current emotional state or well-being. Please provide a brief description of how you are feeling today.

### Pain Level
*   **Pain Level** (`pain_level_1`, number, optional): This field is used to rate your current pain level. Please choose a number from the scale: 1 - 5.

### Patient Recover Progress
*   **Patient Recover Progress** (`patient_recover_progress_1`, select_one, optional): Please choose one of the following options to describe your current recovery progress:
    *   1 - Excellent
    *   2 - Good
    *   3 - Slightly Difficult
    *   4 - Moderately Difficult
    *   5 - Very Difficult

### Recovery Timeframe
*   **Recovery Timeframe** (`recovery_timeframe_1`, time, optional): This field is used to estimate the timeframe for your recovery. Please provide a date or a specific time in the format: YYYY-MM-DD or DD MMM YYYY.

### Recovery Expectation
*   **Recovery Expectation** (`recovery_expectation_1`, select_multiple, optional): Please choose one or more of the following options to describe your recovery expectation:
    *   1 - Complete Recovery
    *   2 - Partial Recovery
    *   3 - No Recovery
    *   4 - Improvement

### Follow Up Frequency
*   **Follow Up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency_1`, select_one, optional): Please choose one of the following options for follow-up frequency:
    *   1 - Daily
    *   2 - Weekly
    *   3 - Monthly

### Additional Comments
*   **Additional Comments** (`patient_comments_1`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or concerns you may have.

### Medical Team Comments
*   **Medical Team Comments** (`medical_team_comments_1`, text, optional): This field is intended for healthcare professionals to add any comments or notes about your condition.

### Follow Up Date
*   **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date_1`, date, optional): Please provide the date of the next follow-up appointment.

### Follow Up Frequency 2
*   **Follow Up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency_2`, select_one, optional): Please choose one of the following options for follow-up frequency:
    *   1 - Daily
    *   2 - Weekly
    *   3 - Monthly

### Additional Comments 2
*   **Additional Comments** (`patient_comments_1`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or concerns you may have.

Note: Fields marked as optional are not required to be filled. Please fill them out if applicable to provide a more comprehensive assessment.
