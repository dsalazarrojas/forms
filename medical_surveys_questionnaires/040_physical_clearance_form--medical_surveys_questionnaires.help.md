# physical_clearance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The physical_clearance_form is used to collect information on a patient's medical condition, history, deployment eligibility, and medical clearance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to provide us with the necessary information about your medical condition, history, and suitability for certain procedures or activities.
2. Ensure that you complete all required fields to ensure a smooth review process.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please contact your healthcare provider for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): Describe your current medical condition or any recent medical issues you have experienced.
  Please provide a clear and concise description of your medical condition, including any symptoms, treatments, or recent diagnoses.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Outline your medical history, including any previous medical conditions, treatments, or allergies.
  Be as detailed as possible, including any relevant surgeries, hospitalizations, or ongoing treatments.
- **Deployment Eligibility** (`deployment_eligibility`, number, required): Indicate if you are currently deployed or have been deployed in the past.
  Please choose 'yes' if you are currently or have been deployed in the military, or 'no' otherwise.
- **Medical Clearance** (`medical_clearance`, text, required): Provide any relevant medical clearance information, such as a recent medical clearance letter.
  Attach a copy of the letter or provide a description of the clearance status.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your healthcare provider has the necessary information.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your healthcare provider for guidance.
- Ensure that you have reviewed and understand the purpose and relevance of this form before submitting it.
