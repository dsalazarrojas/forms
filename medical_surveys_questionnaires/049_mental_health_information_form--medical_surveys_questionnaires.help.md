# mental_health_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect mental health information from individuals or organizations for guidance and support services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the appropriate medical survey category.
2. Choose the type of mental health survey you are seeking (Mental Health Assessment or Medical Survey).
3. Enter your contact information (first and last name, email address, and phone number).
4. Provide details about mental health issues you are facing.
5. Specify any mental health supports you are currently receiving.
6. Choose your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or In-Person).
7. If applicable, specify the mental health professional or facility you are working with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Survey Category**: Select the correct category for your mental health needs. This will help us understand the context of your concerns.
* **Medical Survey Type**: Choose between Mental Health Assessment and Medical Survey to specify the type of support you require.
* **Contact First Name**: Enter your first name as we would like to know who to contact you with.
* **Contact Last Name**: Enter your last name as we would like to know who to contact you with.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can reach out to you with information and support.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information or support.
* **Mental Health Issues**: Describe the mental health issues you are currently facing, and we will do our best to provide guidance and support.
* **Mental Health Supports**: Specify any mental health supports you are currently receiving, such as Support Groups, Self Help Groups, Individual Therapy, or Other.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or In-Person).
* **Mental Health Professional**: If you are currently working with a mental health professional, please specify their name or contact information.
* **Mental Health Facility**: If you are receiving services from a mental health facility, please specify its name or contact information.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough when filling out this form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
- We will use the information you provide to offer guidance and support.
- If you are in immediate crisis, please seek help from a crisis hotline or mental health professional.
