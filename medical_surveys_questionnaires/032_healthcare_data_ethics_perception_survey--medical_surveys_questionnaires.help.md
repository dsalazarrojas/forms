# Healthcare Data Ethics Perception Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Healthcare Data Ethics Perception Survey is designed to collect feedback from healthcare professionals and individuals about their perception of data ethics in healthcare settings. This survey aims to gather information on various aspects of data ethics, including survey participation, demographic information, medical history, data privacy, medical provider information, and healthcare settings. By completing this form, you will contribute to our understanding of data ethics in healthcare and help us improve the way we handle sensitive patient data.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer the questions as accurately as possible, based on your actual experience and knowledge.
2.  Select one or multiple options for each question, depending on the question type.
3.  If a question allows for free text entry (e.g., "Other"), provide a clear and concise description of your answer.
