# Cardiovascular Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cardiovascular Knowledge Assessment form is designed to evaluate an individual's understanding of various aspects of heart health, including normal blood pressure ranges, common heart attack symptoms, and benefits of regular aerobic exercise. This form aims to educate respondents on their current knowledge and preferences for learning about heart health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your full name in the "Respondent Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Select the correct normal blood pressure range from the options provided in the "What is considered a normal blood pressure range?" field.
4. Choose all that apply from the list of common heart attack symptoms in the "Which of the following are common heart attack symptoms?" field.
5. Select the primary benefit of regular aerobic exercise from the options provided in the "What is a primary benefit of regular aerobic exercise?" field.
6. Choose all that apply from the list of risk factors for atherosclerosis in the "Which factors increase the risk of atherosclerosis?" field.
7. Rate your self-assessed knowledge level about heart health from 1 to 5 in the "Rate your self-assessed heart health knowledge" field.
8. Select all that apply from the list of topics you would like to learn more about in the "Which topics would you like to learn more about?" field.
9. Choose your preferred method for learning about heart health from the options provided in the "Preferred method for learning about heart health" field.
10. If you have any specific questions about heart health, type them in the "Do you have any specific questions about heart health?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name_full`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **What is considered a normal blood pressure range?** (`normal_blood_pressure_range`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options for normal blood pressure ranges.
* **Which of the following are common heart attack symptoms?** (`heart_attack_symptoms_common`, select_multiple, required): Choose all that apply from the list of common heart attack symptoms.
* **What is a primary benefit of regular aerobic exercise?** (`aerobic_exercise_benefits_cardio`, select_one, required): Select the primary benefit of regular aerobic exercise.
* **Which factors increase the risk of atherosclerosis?** (`atherosclerosis_risk_factors`, select_multiple, required): Choose all that apply from the list of risk factors for atherosclerosis.
* **Rate your self-assessed heart health knowledge** (`self_assessed_knowledge_level`, number, required): Rate your self-assessed knowledge level about heart health from 1 to 5.
* **Which topics would you like to learn more about?** (`interests_topics_cardio`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the list of topics you would like to learn more about.
* **Preferred method for learning about heart health** (`preferred_learning_method_style`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred method for learning about heart health.
* **Do you have any specific questions about heart health?** (`additional_comments_questions`, text, required): If you have any specific questions about heart health, type them in this field.

Note: Some fields are required, indicated by `required: true` in the YAML file. Please ensure that these fields are filled out accurately to provide a complete assessment of your cardiovascular knowledge.
