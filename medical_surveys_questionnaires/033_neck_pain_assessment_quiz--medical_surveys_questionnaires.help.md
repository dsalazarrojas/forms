# Neck Pain Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neck Pain Assessment Quiz is a comprehensive form designed to assess the level of neck pain in patients. This quiz is used to gather information about the patient's neck pain experience, including the duration, location, activities causing pain, symptoms, and treatment options. The collected data will help healthcare professionals make informed decisions about patient care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Neck Pain Assessment Quiz, follow these steps:

1.  Read the introduction and understand the purpose of the quiz.
2.  Select the level of pain you are experiencing from the options provided.
3.  Choose the duration of your pain from the given options.
4.  Select the location of your pain from the options provided.
5.  Mark the activities that cause you pain from the given list.
6.  Choose the symptoms you are experiencing from the options provided.
7.  Select the treatment options you have considered or undergone from the provided list.
8.  Choose the medical condition(s) that you are experiencing from the given list.
9.  Enter your name and email address for identification purposes.
10. Enter your phone number for contact information.
11. Choose the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Neck Pain Level** (`neck_pain_level`): This field asks for the level of pain you are experiencing, with options for Severe, Moderate, Mild, or None.
* **Pain Duration** (`pain_duration`): Select the duration of your pain from Less than 24 hours, 24 hours - 3 days, 3 days - 1 week, or More than 1 week.
* **Pain Location** (`pain_location`): Choose the location of your pain from Cervical spine, Thoracic spine, Shoulder, Elbow, Wrist, or Other.
* **Activities** (`activities`): Mark the activities that cause you pain from Sedentary, Light, Moderate, Heavy, or Very Heavy.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`): Select the symptoms you are experiencing from Headache, Fatigue, Dizziness, Numbness, Tingling, Weakness, Pain, Loss of sensation, or Other.
* **Treatment** (`treatment`): Choose the treatment options you have considered or undergone from Physical therapy, Medication, Surgery, Chiropractic, Massage, or Other.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`): Select the medical condition(s) you are experiencing from Musculoskeletal, Neurological, Cardiovascular, Psychological, or Other.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date of submission (optional).
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`): Enter the time of submission (optional).
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`): Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`): Enter the person submitting the form on your behalf (optional).

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and accurately to provide the most helpful information for your healthcare professional.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, choose the one that best fits your situation.
* You can skip any fields that are not applicable to your situation.
* If you have any medical concerns or questions, please discuss them with your healthcare professional.
