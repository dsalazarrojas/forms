# Surrogate Matching Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a Surrogate Matching Questionnaire. It is intended for individuals who are seeking to become a surrogate, and the information collected will be used to match them with potential intended parents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions accurately and honestly.
2. Make sure to complete each field completely, as missing information may delay or affect the matching process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Background Form** (`1`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your basic demographic information, such as your name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Motivation Form** (`2`, `text`, required): Please describe your motivation for becoming a surrogate, including your reasons for wanting to help a family, your experience with pregnancy and childcare, and any previous surrogacy experience.
* **Preference Form** (`3`, `text`, required): In this field, please describe your preferences for a surrogacy, including the type of surrogacy (e.g., gestational, traditional), the number of children you are willing to carry, and any specific requirements (e.g., geographic location).
* **Medical History Form** (`4`, `text`, required): Please provide a detailed medical history, including any previous pregnancies, surgeries, and medical conditions that may affect your ability to carry a pregnancy.
* **Medical Background** (`5`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your medical background information, including any previous medical conditions, allergies, and medications.
* **Medical History** (`6`, `text`, required): Please describe your medical history, including any previous pregnancies, surgeries, and medical conditions.
* **Medical Form** (`7`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect any additional medical information that may be relevant to the surrogacy process.
* **Personal Form** (`8`, `text`, required): Please provide information about yourself, including your education, employment, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Contact Form** (`9`, `text`, required): In this field, please provide contact information for yourself and any emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Form** (`10`, `text`, required): Please list your emergency contact information, including their relationship to you and their contact details.
* **Social Media** (`11`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect information about your social media presence, including your social media handles.
* **Employment** (`12`, `text`, required): Please describe your employment status, including your occupation, job title, and any relevant work experience.
* **Education** (`13`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your educational background information, including your highest level of education and any relevant academic achievements.
* **Financial** (`14`, `text`, required): Please provide information about your financial situation, including your income, expenses, and any financial commitments.
* **Insurance** (`15`, `text`, required): In this field, please describe your insurance coverage, including any health, life, and disability insurance.
* **Health** (`16`, `text`, required): Please provide information about your health, including any medical conditions, allergies, and medications.
* **Medical Clinics** (`17`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect information about any medical clinics or hospitals you have visited in the past.
* **Fertility Clinics** (`18`, `text`, required): Please describe any previous experiences with fertility clinics or treatments.
* **Medical Expenses Form** (`19`, `text`, required): Please provide information about any medical expenses you have incurred in the past, including any hospital bills or medical debts.
* **Medical Expenses Details** (`20`, `text`, required): In this field, please describe any medical expenses or procedures you have undergone.
* **Legal** (`21`, `text`, required): Please provide information about any legal matters, including any court cases or legal disputes.
* **Surrogate Matching Process** (`22`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect information about your willingness to participate in the surrogacy matching process.
* **Surrogate Matching Commitment** (`23`, `text`, required): Please describe your level of commitment to the surrogacy matching process, including your willingness to follow any guidelines or rules.
* **Intended Parent** (`24`, `select_one`, required): Please select the intended parent(s) you are working with, including their age, location, and relationship status.
* **Intended Parent 2** (`25`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to collect information about the second intended parent, if applicable.
