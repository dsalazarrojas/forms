# Intravenous Vitamin C Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant medical information for patients undergoing intravenous vitamin C therapy.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review and fill out the form carefully.
2. Provide accurate and honest answers to each field.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, date of birth, and any other identifying information as required.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or treatments you've had.
* **Appointment Preferences** (`appointment_preferences`, text, optional): Share your preferred appointment date, time, or location.
* **IV Vitamin C Therapy** (`iv_c_therapy`, select_one, optional): Choose the option that best suits your treatment needs. Options:
	+ IV Vitamin C Therapy
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Health Details** (`health_details`, text, optional): Share any relevant health details or medical conditions you're experiencing.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): Select any conditions you're currently experiencing. Options:
	+ High Blood Pressure
	+ Diabetes
	+ Heart Conditions
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Medication Use** (`medication_use`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you use any medications. Options:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional): Check any allergies you have. Options:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Select your preferred appointment time.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, optional): Enter your medical provider's name and contact information.

Please note that this form is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Consult with your healthcare provider for personalized guidance.
