# Patient Review Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Review Form is designed to gather crucial information from patients for the medical department to perform thorough reviews and assessments. This form is only intended for patients who have been referred to the medical department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information, marked as "required" in the form.
2. For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. Enter accurate and relevant information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the medical conditions that apply to the patient. Options include Arthritis, Asthma, Cancer, Diabetes, Heart Conditions, Hypertension, Kidney Conditions, Liver Conditions, Lung Conditions, and Other.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, optional): Enter any relevant medical history or information that is not covered in the other fields.
* **Doctor Referral** (`doctor_referral`, select_one, optional): Select the doctor who referred the patient. Options include Dr. Smith, Dr. Lee, Dr. Thompson, and Dr. Johnson.
* **Patient Review** (`patient_review`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or reviews from the patient.
