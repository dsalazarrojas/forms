# Mental Health Journal Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Journal Template is a self-reporting tool designed to help individuals with mental health conditions track their daily experiences, emotions, and progress. This template is meant to be used in conjunction with the guidance of a mental health professional.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out this template consistently, ideally at the same time every day.
2. Answer each question as accurately and honestly as possible.
3. Be as detailed as you can be when answering questions that have a text or note box.
4. If you're unsure about any question, please consult with your mental health professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Daily Mood** (`1`, `number`, required: false): Rate your current mood on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the worst and 10 is the best.
* **Emotions** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any emotions you're experiencing today, such as Happy, Anxious, Angry, Sad, or Frustrated.
* **Thoughts** (`3`, `note`, required: false): Describe any thoughts or concerns you're having today.
* **Physical Pain** (`4`, `number`, required: false): Rate any physical pain you're experiencing, from 1 to 10.
* **Medication** (`5`, `text`, required: false): If you're taking any medication, please list the name, dosage, and frequency.
* **Social Support** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Identify who you have for social support, such as Family, Friends, Partner, or Alone.
* **Sleep** (`7`, `number`, required: false): Rate the quality of your sleep, from 1 to 10.
* **Nutrition** (`8`, `number`, required: false): Rate your nutrition, from 1 to 10, considering healthy eating habits.
* **Exercise** (`9`, `number`, required: false): Rate your physical activity level, from 1 to 10.
* **Mental Health Professional** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Confirm if you're currently seeing a mental health professional.
* **Mental Health Notes** (`11`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your mental health.
* **Mental Health Progress** (`12`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your mental health progress, from Progress, No Progress, Unknown, or Stopped Making Progress.

## Tips
* Consult with your mental health professional before making any significant changes or decisions based on this template.
* Use this template as a tool to track your progress, but don't rely solely on it for diagnosis or treatment.
* Remember that consistency is key, so try to fill out this template at the same time every day.
