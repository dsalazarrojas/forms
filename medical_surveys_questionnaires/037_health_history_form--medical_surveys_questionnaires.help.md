# Health History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health History Form is designed to collect comprehensive information about a patient's health condition, medical history, and other relevant details. This information will help healthcare professionals assess and manage your health effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and fill in the required information for each field.
2.  Ensure you understand each field's purpose before providing answers.
3.  If a field is marked as required, please fill it out carefully, as it is essential for accurate medical assessment.
4.  If a field has options, select the most suitable option that accurately describes your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information (Field 1)
*   **General Information** (`general_info`, text, Optional): This section is used to capture basic information about you, such as your name, contact details, and other relevant identification data.

### Medical History (Field 2)
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all medical conditions, allergies, or treatments you have experienced. Please be as detailed as possible.

### Sufferings (Field 3)
*   **Sufferings** (`sufferings`, text, Optional): Describe any sufferings or health issues you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.

### Family Information (Field 4)
*   **Family Information** (`family_info`, select_one, Optional): If you have any family members with medical conditions, select the corresponding option from the list.

### Drawing Board (Field 5)
*   **Drawing Board** (`drawing_board`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all medical procedures or treatments you have undergone or are currently undergoing.

### Habits (Field 6)
*   **Habits** (`habits`, text, Optional): Describe your daily habits, such as smoking or drinking, that may be relevant to your health.

### Pain Location (Field 7)
*   **Pain Location** (`pain_location`, select_one, Optional): Indicate the area of your body where you experience pain, if any.

### General Comments (Field 8)
*   **General Comments** (`general_comments`, text, Optional): Share any additional information about your health or medical history that you think is relevant to your care.

## Tips
*   Please fill out this form carefully and accurately, as the information will be used for medical assessment and treatment.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, refer to this guide for clarification.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask a medical professional for guidance.
