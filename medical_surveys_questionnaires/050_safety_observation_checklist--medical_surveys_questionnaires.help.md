# Safety Observation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Safety Observation Checklist is a form used to document patient safety observations in a hospital setting. It helps gather information on patient handling, observation methods, physical exams, vital signs, medication administration, and patient condition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant observation methods for the patient (Direct Observation, Review of Record, Both)
2. Note any relevant patient information (Patient Information)
3. Indicate whether a physical exam was conducted (Conducted, Not Conducted, Not Applicable)
4. Record the patient's vital signs (Vital Signs)
5. Check the status of medication administration (Medication Administered, False, Not Applicable)
6. Indicate the trend of the patient's vital signs (Vital Signs Trend)
7. Check the status of medication refilled (Medication Refilled)
8. Record the patient's medication status (Medication Status)
9. Select the patient's current condition (Patient Condition)
10. Select the patient's current risks (Patient Risks)
11. Check the progress of patient care (Patient Care)
12. Indicate the patient's status (Patient Status)
13. Record the trend of the patient's condition (Patient Condition Trend)
14. Indicate the trend of medication administration (Medication Administered Trend)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Handling**: A brief description of the patient's handling during observation.
* **Observation Method**: Select the methods used to observe the patient (Direct Observation, Review of Record, Both).
* **Patient Information**: A note field for any relevant patient information.
* **Physical Exam**: Indicate whether a physical exam was conducted (Conducted, Not Conducted, Not Applicable).
* **Vital Signs**: Record the patient's vital signs (e.g., heart rate, blood pressure, etc.).
* **Medication Administered**: Check the status of medication administration (Administered, False, Not Applicable).
* **Vital Signs Trend**: Record the trend of the patient's vital signs over time.
* **Medication Refilled**: Check the status of medication refilled (True, False, Not Applicable).
* **Medication Status**: Record the patient's medication status (e.g., administered, not administered, etc.).
* **Patient Condition**: Select the patient's current condition (Stable, Unstable, Not Applicable).
* **Patient Risks**: Select the patient's current risks (Stable, Unstable, Not Applicable).
* **Patient Care**: Check the progress of patient care (In Progress, Complete, Not Started).
* **Patient Status**: Select the patient's current status (Stable, Unstable, Not Applicable).
* **Patient Condition Trend**: Record the trend of the patient's condition over time.
* **Medication Administered Trend**: Record the trend of medication administration over time.

Note: Fields with "Not Applicable" options indicate that the information is not relevant or available for the patient.
