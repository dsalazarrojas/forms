# Local Anesthesia Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Use the options provided for each question to select your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Local Anesthesia Feedback Survey** (`form_start`, text, not required): This is the introduction page of the survey.
* **How would you rate your overall comfort with the local anesthesia?** (`question_1`, select_one, not required):
	+ Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What side effects did you experience during or after the procedure?** (`question_2`, note, not required): Please describe any side effects you experienced, such as numbness, tingling, or pain.
* **Were any of the side effects severe?** (`question_3`, select_one, not required):
	+ Select True if the side effects were severe, False if they were not.
* **How would you rate the pain or discomfort during the procedure?** (`question_4`, select_one, not required):
	+ Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Did you experience any numbness or tingling in your tongue or lips?** (`question_5`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ Select any or all of the options: Numbness, Tingling, or Neither.
* **Were you given sufficient time to relax before the procedure?** (`question_6`, select_one, required):
	+ Select True if you were given enough time, False if not.
* **Were you given information about what to expect during or after the procedure?** (`question_7`, select_one, not required):
	+ Select True if you received information, False if not.
* **Were there any other comments or suggestions you would like to share with us?** (`question_8`, note, not required): Please use this field to provide any additional feedback or suggestions.
* **What was your experience overall?** (`question_9`, select_one, not required):
	+ Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How likely are you to recommend this clinic or practice to a friend or family member?** (`question_10`, select_one, not required):
	+ Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How likely are you to recommend this clinic or practice to a friend or family member?** (`question_11`, select_one, not required): This question is identical to question 10.
* **Do you have any other comments or suggestions about your experience at this clinic or practice?** (`question_12`, note, not required): Please use this field to provide any additional feedback.
* **End of Survey** (`form_end`, text, not required): This is the final page of the survey.
