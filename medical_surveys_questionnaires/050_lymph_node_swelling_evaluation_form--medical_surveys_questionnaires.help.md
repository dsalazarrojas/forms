# Lymph Node Swelling Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals evaluate the characteristics of a patient's lymph node swelling. It is intended to collect information about the patient's medical history, current symptoms, and relevant medical treatments. The goal is to provide a comprehensive assessment of the swelling, which can help in making informed decisions regarding diagnosis and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the instructions and questions carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Enter your responses in the text fields provided.
4. Please note that this form is for evaluation purposes only and is not intended for diagnosis or treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for providing general information about the patient, such as name, date of birth, and medical record number.
	+ Please enter any relevant details about the patient's medical history, including previous treatments or conditions.
* **Lymph Node Details** (`lymph_node_details`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the characteristics of the lymph node swelling, including its size, shape, color, and any other notable features.
	+ Be as detailed as possible to provide a comprehensive understanding of the swelling.
* **Swelling Characteristics** (`swelling_characteristics`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the symptoms associated with the swelling, such as pain, redness, or tenderness.
	+ Provide information about the duration and progression of the swelling.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional):
	+ Provide a brief overview of the patient's medical history, including any previous surgeries, illnesses, or allergies.
	+ This will help in understanding the patient's overall health context.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional):
	+ List any medications the patient is currently taking or has taken in the past.
	+ Include dosages and frequencies.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional):
	+ List any known allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
	+ Include the type of substance or substance(s) the patient is allergic to.

## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, please consult with the patient or their healthcare provider.
* This form is for evaluation purposes only and should not be used for diagnosis or treatment.
