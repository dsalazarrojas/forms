# Cardiovascular Health Outcomes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cardiovascular Health Outcomes Survey is a form designed to collect data on participants' cardiovascular health outcomes. The survey aims to gather information on participant demographics, health history, lifestyle habits, and symptoms experienced over the past 30 days.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form to start the survey.
2. Enter your assigned study ID in the "Participant ID" field.
3. Provide your current age in the "Current Age" field.
4. Select your gender from the options provided in the "Gender" field.
5. Answer the questions in the subsequent sections, providing the required information for each section.
6. Enter your average daily physical activity in minutes in the "Average Daily Physical Activity (minutes)" field.
7. Report your daily fruit and vegetable intake in servings in the "Daily Fruit and Vegetable Intake (servings)" field.
8. Indicate your current smoking status in the "Current Smoking Status" field.
9. Report the frequency of your chest pain or pressure over the past 30 days in the "Frequency of Chest Pain or Pressure" field.
10. Report the frequency of your shortness of breath over the past 30 days in the "Frequency of Shortness of Breath" field.
11. Report the number of cardiac hospitalizations you've experienced over the past year in the "Number of Cardiac Hospitalizations (Past Year)" field.
12. Rate your overall health-related quality of life on a scale of 1-10 in the "Overall Health-Related Quality of Life Rating" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant ID (Required)** (`participant_id_reference`, `text`): Enter your assigned study ID to associate your responses with your identity.
* **Current Age (Required)** (`age_years`, `number`): Enter your current age in years.
* **Gender (Required)** (`gender_identity`, `select_one`): Select one of the following options: Male, Female, Non-binary, or Prefer not to say.
* **Have you been diagnosed with hypertension? (Required)** (`hypertension_diagnosis_status`, `select_one`): Select one of the following options: True, False, or Not Sure.
* **Average Daily Physical Activity (minutes) (Required)** (`daily_physical_activity_minutes`, `number`): Report your average daily physical activity in minutes.
* **Daily Fruit and Vegetable Intake (servings) (Required)** (`fruit_vegetable_intake_servings`, `number`): Report your daily fruit and vegetable intake in servings.
* **Current Smoking Status (Required)** (`smoking_status_current`, `select_one`): Select one of the following options: Never Smoked, Former Smoker (Quit >1 year ago), Recent Quitter (Quit <1 year ago), or Current Smoker.
* **Frequency of Chest Pain or Pressure (Required)** (`chest_pain_frequency_level`, `select_one`): Select one of the following options: Never, Rarely (1-2 times), Sometimes (Weekly), or Often (Daily).
* **Frequency of Shortness of Breath (Required)** (`shortness_of_breath_frequency_level`, `select_one`): Select one of the following options: Never, Rarely, Sometimes, or Often.
* **Number of Cardiac Hospitalizations (Past Year) (Required)** (`recent_hospitalizations_count`, `number`): Report the number of cardiac hospitalizations you've experienced over the past year.
* **Overall Health-Related Quality of Life Rating (Required)** (`quality_of_life_rating_overall`, `number`): Rate your overall health-related quality of life on a scale of 1-10.
