# inflammatory_response_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals assess the client's health status, medical history, and treatment history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the provided fields.
2. Provide accurate information about your symptoms, medical history, and treatment history.
3. Indicate whether you have any medical conditions, allergies, or supplements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Symptom History** (`symptom_history`): Describe your current symptoms, including any pain or discomfort you are experiencing.
* **Pain Locations** (`pain_locations`): List the areas where you experience pain or discomfort.
* **Pain Duration** (`pain_duration`): Estimate how long you have been experiencing pain or discomfort.
* **Medication History** (`medication_history`): Provide information about any medications you are currently taking.
* **Health Context** (`health_context`): Briefly describe your current health status.
* **Wellness Check** (`wellness_check`): Describe your overall health and wellness.
* **Client Informed** (`client_informed`): Indicate whether you have been informed about your health status by a healthcare provider.
* **Client Informed Other** (`client_informed_other`): If yes, describe what you were told.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`): Indicate whether you have a healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider Other** (`healthcare_provider_other`): If yes, describe your healthcare provider.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`): Indicate whether you have any allergies.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`): Indicate whether you have any medical conditions.
* **Medical Condition Other** (`medical_condition_other`): If yes, describe your medical condition.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Indicate whether you have a medical history.
* **Medical History Other** (`medical_history_other`): If yes, describe your medical history.
* **Treatment History** (`treatment_history`): Provide information about your treatment history.
* **Treatment History Other** (`treatment_history_other`): If yes, describe your treatment history.
* **Current Vitamins** (`current_vitamins`): Indicate whether you are currently taking any vitamins.
* **Past Vitamins** (`past_vitamins`): Indicate whether you have taken any vitamins in the past.
* **Past Vitamins Other** (`past_vitamins_other`): If yes, describe any vitamins you have taken.
* **Current Supplements** (`current_supplements`): Indicate whether you are currently taking any supplements.
* **Past Supplements** (`past_supplements`): Indicate whether you have taken any supplements in the past.
* **Past Supplements Other** (`past_supplements_other`): If yes, describe any supplements you have taken.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): Indicate whether you have any medical conditions.
* **Medical Conditions Other** (`medical_conditions_other`): If yes, describe any medical conditions you have.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`): Indicate whether you have any allergies.
