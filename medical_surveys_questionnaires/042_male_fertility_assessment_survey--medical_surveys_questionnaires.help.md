# Male Fertility Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a male patient's lifestyle, medical history, and fertility tests to assess their fertility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information as a patient.
2. Answer questions about your lifestyle, including any habits or practices that may affect your fertility.
3. Describe any medical conditions or history that may impact your fertility.
4. List any fertility tests or exams you have undergone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`Patient Info`, text, required: false): This is the contact information section of the form. Please provide your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
* **Lifestyle** (`lifestyle`, text, required: false): This section asks about your lifestyle habits, such as diet, exercise, or smoking history. Be as detailed as possible about any habits or practices that might affect your fertility.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Please describe any medical conditions, allergies, or surgeries that may impact your fertility.
* **Fertility Tests** (`fertility_tests`, text, required: false): List any fertility tests or exams you have undergone, such as sperm analysis or semen analysis.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your answers to ensure an accurate assessment of your fertility.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to ask for clarification.
- This form is for informational purposes only and not intended for medical diagnosis or treatment.
- If you're concerned about your fertility, please consult a healthcare professional for personalized advice.
