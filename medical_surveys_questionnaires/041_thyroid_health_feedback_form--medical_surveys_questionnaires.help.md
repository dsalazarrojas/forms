# thyroid_health_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from individuals regarding their thyroid health. It is essential to understand that this form is intended for this specific purpose and should only be used for collecting thyroid health feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2. For questions with a 'Yes' or 'No' option, select the most accurate response.
3. For questions with a select_multiple option, choose all that apply.
4. For the thyroid_health_frequency field, enter the frequency at which your doctor takes your blood pressure.
5. Enter any additional information or comments in the thyroid_health_other field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Thyroid Health Form**: This is the main section of the form. Please review the questions carefully and answer truthfully.
* **Thyroid Health Questions**: Select all that apply regarding your thyroid health questions.
* **Thyroid Health Symptoms**: Select all that apply regarding your thyroid health symptoms.
* **Thyroid Health Doctor**: Check if you have consulted a doctor for your thyroid health.
* **Thyroid Health Clinic**: Check if you have visited a thyroid health clinic.
* **Thyroid Health Program**: Select all that apply regarding thyroid health programs.
* **Thyroid Health Frequency**: Enter the frequency at which your doctor takes your blood pressure.
* **Thyroid Health Medicine**: Check if you are taking medicine for your thyroid health.
* **Thyroid Health Other**: Enter any additional information or comments regarding your thyroid health.
* **Thyroid Health Support**: Select all that apply regarding thyroid health support.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you answer each question truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, it's okay to ask for clarification or guidance.
* If you have any additional information or comments, please enter them in the thyroid_health_other field.
