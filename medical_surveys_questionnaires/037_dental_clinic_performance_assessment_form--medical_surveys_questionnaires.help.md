# Dental Clinic Performance Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for patients to evaluate their experiences with a dental clinic's performance across different aspects such as staff attitudes, service quality, waiting times, facilities, overall experience, staff professionalism, and communication. By completing this form, you can provide constructive feedback to the clinic, helping them improve their service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the questions and select an option that best describes your experience with the dental clinic.
2. Ensure you answer all questions that you feel are relevant to your experience.
3. You can submit your feedback anonymously.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (Page 1)**: A brief note about the form's purpose and how your answers will help the clinic.
* **Staff Attitudes (Page 2)**: How would you rate the friendliness of the staff?
	+ Excellent: The staff were extremely welcoming and friendly.
	+ Very good: The staff were generally friendly.
	+ Average: The staff were neutral.
	+ Poor: The staff were unfriendly.
* **Service Quality (Page 3)**: How would you rate the quality of service you received?
	+ Excellent: The service was of high standard.
	+ Very good: The service was generally good.
	+ Average: The service was okay.
	+ Poor: The service was bad.
* **Waiting Times (Page 4)**: How long did you wait before receiving treatment?
	+ Less than 15 minutes: Very short wait time.
	+ 15-30 minutes: Short wait time.
	+ 30-60 minutes: Moderate wait time.
	+ More than 60 minutes: Long wait time.
* **Facilities (Page 5)**: How would you rate the cleanliness and maintenance of the clinic's facilities?
	+ Well-maintained: Facilities were well-maintained and clean.
	+ Clean: Facilities were clean but not necessarily well-maintained.
	+ Not well-maintained: Facilities were not well-maintained and dirty.
* **Overall Experience (Page 6)**: How would you rate your overall experience at the clinic?
	+ Excellent: Extremely satisfied with the service.
	+ Very good: Generally satisfied with the service.
	+ Average: Satisfied with the service.
	+ Poor: Unsatisfied with the service.
* **Staff Professionalism (Page 7)**: How would you rate the professionalism of the staff?
	+ Highly professional: Staff were highly professional.
	+ Somewhat professional: Staff were somewhat professional.
	+ Not professional at all: Staff were not professional.
* **Communication (Page 8)**: How would you rate the communication from the staff?
	+ Excellent: Clear and efficient communication.
	+ Very good: Good communication.
	+ Average: Neutral communication.
	+ Poor: Poor communication.

## Tips

* Be honest and provide constructive feedback.
* Answer all questions that apply to your experience.
* If you have specific feedback or suggestions, please include them in the space provided.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact the clinic directly.
