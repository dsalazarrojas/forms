# cross_sector_health_collaboration_readiness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the readiness of a healthcare sector to collaborate with other sectors. It collects information on various aspects of collaboration, including sector and service selection, patient outcomes, collaboration readiness, submission tracking, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully to ensure that the information collected is useful for decision-making and planning purposes.
2. Ensure you are filling out the form for the correct sector and purpose.
3. If you are unsure about any field or its meaning, please ask your administrator or supervisor for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of the sector being evaluated.
* **Sector Health Collaboration** (`sector_health_collaboration`, text, optional): Describe the current state of health collaboration within the sector.
* **Healthcare Sector** (`healthcare_sector`, select one, optional): Choose the healthcare sector related to this assessment:
	+ Sector 1
	+ Sector 2
	+ Sector 3
* **Healthcare Services** (`healthcare_services`, select multiple, optional): Select the healthcare services currently available in the sector:
	+ Service 1
	+ Service 2
	+ Service 3
	+ Service 4
* **Patient Outcomes** (`patient_outcomes`, number, optional): Enter a number to represent the patient outcomes achieved by the sector.
* **Collaboration Readiness** (`collaboration_readiness`, time, optional): Enter the time required for the sector to be ready for collaboration.
* **Submission Tracking** (`submission_tracking`, date, optional): Select the date for submission tracking:
	+ Date of submission
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the sector's collaboration readiness.
