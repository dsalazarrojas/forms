# Caregiver Role Strain Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to understand your perception of the strain you experience in your caregiving role. It will take approximately 10-15 minutes to complete and should be filled out on your own.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form on your own device or in a quiet, private space where you can focus on your answers without distractions.
2. Answer each question honestly, using the scales provided for questions 4-18. For example, if you strongly agree with a statement, select the corresponding option.
3. For questions 23 and 24, you may provide any relevant information that you feel is helpful to us.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the space provided for question 25.
