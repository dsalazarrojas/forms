# Patient Centered Medical Home Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Centered Medical Home Survey is a form designed to collect demographic and medical history information from patients. This information can be used to improve the patient-centered medical home experience and provide better care for patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

1. Select the **Medical Surveys & Questionnaires** category that best describes the purpose of the survey.
2. Provide a brief description of the survey in the **description** field.
3. Choose the output file format from the **output_file** field.
4. Select the tool assigned to the survey from the **assigned_tool** field.
5. Enter the patient's name in the **patient_name** field.
6. Provide any additional comments or notes in the **survey_comments** field.
7. Enter the patient's email address in the **patient_email** field.
8. Enter the patient's phone number in the **patient_phone** field.
9. Enter the patient's date of birth in the **patient_date_of_birth** field.
10. Select the patient's gender from the **patient_gender** field.
11. Select the patient's race from the **patient_race** field.
12. Select the patient's ethnicity from the **patient_ethnicity** field.
13. Select the languages the patient is proficient in from the **patient_language** field.
14. Provide any additional comments or notes in the **comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Centered Medical Home Survey** (`patient_centered_medical_home_survey`): This field is the title of the survey.
* **Medical Surveys & Questionnaires** (`survey_category`): Select the category that best describes the purpose of the survey.
	+ Medical Surveys & Questionnaires: For surveys related to medical topics.
	+ Healthcare Surveys: For surveys related to healthcare services.
	+ Others: For other purposes not related to medical or healthcare.
* **Description** (`description`): A brief description of the survey.
* **Output File** (`survey_output_file`): Choose the file format to output the survey results in (e.g., CSV, PDF, Excel).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Select the tool assigned to the survey.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Comments** (`survey_comments`): Provide any additional comments or notes about the survey.
* **Email** (`patient_email`): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone** (`patient_phone`): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (`patient_gender`): Select the patient's gender.
* **Race** (`patient_race`): Select the patient's race.
* **Ethnicity** (`patient_ethnicity`): Select the patient's ethnicity.
* **Language** (`patient_language`): Select the languages the patient is proficient in.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any additional comments or notes about the patient.
