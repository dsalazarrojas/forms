# Upper Respiratory Infection Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Upper Respiratory Infection Assessment form is used to evaluate an individual's risk of having an upper respiratory infection, based on their travel history, recent contacts, and other relevant factors. This form helps healthcare professionals gather essential information to determine the likelihood of transmission and provide guidance on how to mitigate the risk.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Step 1:** Answer all questions honestly to the best of your ability.
- **Step 2:** Select all applicable options for each question.
- **Step 3:** Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all questions honestly and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any question, ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
- The information provided will help healthcare professionals determine the risk of transmission and provide guidance on how to mitigate it.
