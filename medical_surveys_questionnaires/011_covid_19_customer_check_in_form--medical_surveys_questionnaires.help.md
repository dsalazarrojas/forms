# Covid 19 Customer Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

> <thinking>This form is used to collect relevant information about patients who are being vaccinated against Covid-19. This includes identifying patient details, vaccination history, and medical information. By providing accurate and detailed responses, healthcare providers can better understand and cater to the needs of their patients. This form helps ensure that patients receive the best possible care and attention from healthcare professionals.

</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Patient ID to identify yourself as a patient.
2. Record your Date of Vaccination.
3. Indicate if the vaccination took place at a specific Vaccination Site.
4. Enter your Patient Name.
5. Provide your Age.
6. Select your Sex.
7. Enter the name of your Medical Office.
8. Identify your Vaccination Provider.
9. Add any Medical Notes that are relevant to your care.
10. Enter your Phone Number.
11. Enter your Email Address.
12. Provide your Medical History.
13. List any Medications you are allergic to.
14. Record your next scheduled visit date.
15. Enter the name and details of your Medical Insurance Provider.
16. Record your Family History.
17. Provide the contact details of an Emergency Contact.
18. Enter the Emergency Phone Number.
19. Enter the Emergency Email Address.
20. Add any additional Patient Notes.
21. Record any Medical Notes.
22. Enter the dosage and details of your vaccination.
23. Verify the correctness of your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient ID** (`1`, `number`, required): Enter a unique number to identify yourself as a patient.
* **Date Of Vaccination** (`2`, `date`, required): Enter the date you received the vaccination.
* **Vaccination Site** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the vaccination took place at a specific site.
* **Patient Name** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Age** (`5`, `number`, required): Enter your age at the time of vaccination.
* **Sex** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your sex.
* **Medical Office** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the name and address of your medical office.
* **Vaccination Provider** (`8`, `text`, required): Enter the name and details of your vaccination provider.
* **Medical Notes** (`9`, `note`, required): Add any relevant medical notes or comments.
* **Phone Number** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`11`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Medical History** (`12`, `text`, required): Provide your medical history and any relevant health information.
* **Medication Allergies** (`13`, `text`, required): List any medications you are allergic to.
* **Next Visit Date** (`14`, `date`, required): Record your next scheduled visit date.
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter the name and details of your medical insurance provider.
* **Family History** (`16`, `text`, required): Record any relevant family medical history.
* **Emergency Contact** (`17`, `text`, required): Provide the name and contact details of an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`18`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email** (`19`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Patient Notes** (`20`, `note`, required): Add any additional patient notes or comments.
* **Medical Notes** (`21`, `note`, required): Add any additional medical notes or comments.
* **Vaccination Dose** (`22`, `text`, required): Enter the details of your vaccination dose.
* **Vaccination Dosage** (`23`, `text`, required): Enter the dosage and details of your vaccination.

Note: All fields marked as required must be completed before submitting the form.
