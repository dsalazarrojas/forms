# Fat Cavitation Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fat Cavitation Client Intake Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect client information for fat cavitation procedures. The form is intended to provide a complete and accurate picture of the client's condition, medical history, and contact information to ensure proper treatment and client satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided all necessary information.
2. Complete each field with accurate and truthful information.
3. Ensure that all fields marked as "required" are completed before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fat Cavitation Procedures** (fatcavitationprocedures, text, required): This field is intended for the client to describe the specific fat cavitation procedure they are seeking or undergoing.
* **Fat Cavitation Procedures 2** (fatcavitationprocedures2, text, required): This field is a continuation of the fat cavitation procedure described in the previous field.
* **Contact Informations** (contactinformations, text, required): Provide your contact information, including your name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Phone Call Information** (phonecallinformation, text, required): Please provide your phone number and any other relevant contact information.
* **Fat Cavitation Procedures 3** (fatcavitationprocedures3, text, required): This field is intended for any additional fat cavitation procedures or details.
* **Medical History** (medical_history, text, required): Describe your medical history, including any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or treatments.
* **Medical History 2** (medical_history_2, text, required): This field is a continuation of your medical history.
* **Fat Cavitation Procedures 4** (fatcavitationprocedures4, text, required): This field is intended for any additional fat cavitation procedures or details.
* **Medical History 3** (medical_history_3, text, required): This field is a continuation of your medical history.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed before proceeding.
* Review the form carefully to avoid any errors or omissions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the medical team for assistance.
