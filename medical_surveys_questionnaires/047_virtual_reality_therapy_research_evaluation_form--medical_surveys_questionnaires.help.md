# Virtual Reality Therapy Research Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to evaluate Virtual Reality (VR) therapy research. It allows researchers and clinicians to collect structured data for studies on VR therapy. This form helps to gather information about the category, description, and output file associated with the research.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the category for your form from the "Category" field. The options are "Medical Surveys & Questionnaires" and "Other". Choose the category that best fits your form's purpose.
2. Provide a brief description of your form in the "Description" field. This will help others understand the context and content of your form.
3. If applicable, enter the output file associated with your form in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (Category, select_one, required: false)**: Select the category for your form from the provided options. This helps in organizing and categorizing forms for easier searching and filtering.
* **Description (Description, text, required: false)**: Enter a brief description of your form, which will help others understand the context and content of your form.
* **Output File (Output File, text, required: false)**: If applicable, enter the output file associated with your form. This can be a helpful reference for future use and collaboration.

## Tips

* Make sure to choose a category that accurately represents your form's purpose.
* Keep your description concise and clear, providing enough information for others to understand the form's context.
* If you have any issues or questions about the form, contact your research team or support staff for assistance.
