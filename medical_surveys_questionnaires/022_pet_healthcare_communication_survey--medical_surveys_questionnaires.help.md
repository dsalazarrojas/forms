# Pet Healthcare Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, "Pet Healthcare Communication Survey", is a tool to collect information about the pet's healthcare situation from the pet owner. It gathers data to facilitate effective communication between the pet owner and the veterinary team.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Answer the following sections one by one, starting from "Pet Ownership"

*   Fill in the text field with a brief description of your pet's ownership status (e.g., "owned by me" or "shared by me and others").

### Step 2: Answer the following sections one by one, starting from "Medical History"

*   Describe your pet's medical history, including any previous health issues or surgeries. Provide as much detail as possible, but keep the text concise and clear.

### Step 3: Answer the following sections one by one, starting from "Current Pet Health"

*   Enter the current state of your pet's health as a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being "poor" and 5 being "excellent".

### Step 4: Select your preferred veterinary team from the list

*   Choose one of the available options for the veterinary team you would like to work with.

### Step 5: Enter your contact details

*   Enter your email address or any other contact method you would like us to use to get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Pet Ownership (pet_ownership)** (text, optional): Briefly describe your pet's ownership status.
*   **Medical History (medical_history)** (text, optional): Describe your pet's medical history, including any previous health issues or surgeries.
*   **Current Pet Health (pet_health)** (number, optional): Enter the current state of your pet's health as a number from 1 to 5.
*   **Veterinary Team (veterinary_team)** (select one, optional): Choose one of the available options for the veterinary team you would like to work with.
*   **Contact Details (contact_details)** (email, optional): Enter your email address or any other contact method you would like us to use to get in touch with you.
