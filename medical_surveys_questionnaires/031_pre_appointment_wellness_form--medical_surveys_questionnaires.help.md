# Pre Appointment Wellness Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather medical information from patients before an appointment, helping healthcare professionals make informed decisions about their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions accurately and honestly to ensure the healthcare professional has a complete understanding of your medical history and current condition.
2. Be thorough in providing information about your symptoms and medical history, including any exposure risks and travel history.
3. If you are unsure about any questions or have concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare professional for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Patient Information
Patient information: 
  * **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
  * **Date of Birth** (Text, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  * **Contact Information** (Text, Required): Provide your address, phone number, and email address.

### 2. Medical History
Medical History: 
  * **Current Medications** (Text, Required): List all medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
  * **Previous Medical Conditions** (Text, Required): Describe any previous medical conditions, including surgeries, allergies, or medical procedures.

### 3. Symptoms
Symptoms: 
  * **Current Symptoms** (Text, Required): Describe any current symptoms you are experiencing, including pain, fatigue, or other discomforts.
  * **When Did Symptoms Start** (Text, Required): Estimate when your symptoms began, including approximate dates or times.

### 4. Exposure Risk
Exposure Risk: 
  * **Recent Travel** (Text, Required): Describe any recent travel, including destinations, dates, and duration.
  * **Contact with High-Risk Individuals** (Text, Required): List any high-risk individuals you have come into contact with, such as those with contagious illnesses.

### 5. Travel History
Travel History: 
  * **Travel Details** (Text, Required): Describe any travel history, including destinations, dates, and duration.
  * **Country of Residence** (Text, Required): Enter the country where you currently reside or have resided previously.
