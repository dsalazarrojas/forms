# Final Assessment For T Cell Expansion - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Final Assessment For T Cell Expansion form is designed to collect information from medical professionals or scientists regarding T Cell Expansion assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the title of the assessment in the "Final Assessment For T Cell Expansion" field.
2. Provide the patient's identifier in the "patient_identifier" field.
3. Enter the number of T cells expanded (in millions) in the "tcell_expansion_count" field.
4. Enter the viability of T cells as a percentage (x10^6) in the "tcell_viability" field.
5. Describe the methods used for T Cell Expansion in the "tcell_methods" field.
6. Enter any observations related to T Cell Expansion in the "tcell_observations" field.
7. Select the method used for T Cell Expansion from the options provided in the "method_used" field.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field (optional).
9. Enter the patient ID number in the "patient_id" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Final Assessment For T Cell Expansion** (`form_title`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the title of the assessment, which should be descriptive and relevant to the experiment or observation.
* **patient_identifier** (`patient_identifier`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the patient's unique identifier or code for tracking purposes.
* **Number of T cells expanded (x10^6)** (`tcell_expansion_count`, `number`, required/optional: false): Enter the number of T cells expanded during the experiment, in millions.
* **Viability as a % (x10^6)** (`tcell_viability`, `number`, required/optional: false): Enter the viability of T cells as a percentage, in millions.
* **tcell_methods** (`tcell_methods`, `text`, required/optional: false): Describe the methods used for T Cell Expansion, including any relevant techniques or protocols.
* **tcell_observations** (`tcell_observations`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter any observations or comments related to T Cell Expansion, including any notable results or findings.
* **method_used** (`method_used`, `select_one`, required/true): Select the method used for T Cell Expansion from the provided options: Cell Culture, Flow Cytometry, or ELISA.
* **notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the assessment.
* **patient_id** (`patient_id`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the patient ID number for tracking purposes (optional).
