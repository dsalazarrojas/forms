# Anticoagulation Discussion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anticoagulation Discussion Survey is a medical survey designed to evaluate the quality of anticoagulation care and communication between healthcare providers and patients. The survey aims to assess patient satisfaction, understanding, and concerns, ultimately helping healthcare providers refine their approach to anticoagulation care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your medical record number in the Patient ID field.
2. Select the correct date of completion from the calendar.
3. Choose the correct role from the "Who is completing this survey" dropdown.
4. Choose the correct age group from the "Age Group" dropdown.
5. Select the type of anticoagulant medication you are taking from the "Type of Anticoagulant" dropdown.
6. Choose the duration of your anticoagulation therapy from the "Duration of Therapy" dropdown.
7. Select the reason for your anticoagulation therapy from the "Reason for Anticoagulation" dropdown.
8. Rate the quality of your discussion with the healthcare provider from the "Quality of Discussion with Provider" dropdown.
9. Select all the topics you discussed with your healthcare provider from the "Topics Discussed" dropdown.
10. Rate your understanding of the medication from the "Understanding of Medication" dropdown.
11. Report whether your concerns were addressed by the healthcare provider from the "Were Your Concerns Addressed" dropdown.
12. Indicate your level of understanding of bleeding risks from the "Understanding of Bleeding Risk" dropdown.
13. Report your knowledge of warning signs from the "Knowledge of Warning Signs" dropdown.
14. Indicate your understanding of your monitoring schedule from the "Monitoring Schedule Understanding" dropdown.
15. Report how often you take your medication as prescribed from the "Medication Adherence" dropdown.
16. Select any barriers to adherence from the "Barriers to Adherence" dropdown.
17. List any side effects you have experienced from the "Side Effects Experienced" dropdown.
18. Report whether you told your healthcare provider about the side effects from the "Were Side Effects Reported" dropdown.
19. Rate the responsiveness of your healthcare provider from the "Provider Responsiveness" dropdown.
20. Indicate the frequency of follow-up appointments with your healthcare provider from the "Follow-up Appointment Frequency" dropdown.
21. Rate your overall satisfaction with care from the "Satisfaction with Care" dropdown.
22. Indicate whether you would recommend your healthcare provider to others from the "Would You Recommend Your Provider" dropdown.
23. Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share in the "Additional Information Needed" field.
24. Offer suggestions for improvement in the "Suggestions for Improvement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient ID (1)**: Enter your medical record number to identify yourself in the system.
* **Survey Date (2)**: Select the date you are completing this survey.
* **Who is Completing this Survey (3)**: Choose your role as a patient, family member, or caregiver.
* **Age Group (4)**: Select your age range.
* **Type of Anticoagulant (5)**: Choose the medication you are taking.
* **Duration of Therapy (6)**: Select how long you have been on anticoagulation.
* **Reason for Anticoagulation (7)**: Choose the reason you are taking anticoagulation medication.
* **Quality of Discussion with Provider (8)**: Rate how well you understand the discussion with your healthcare provider.
* **Topics Discussed (9)**: Select all the topics you covered with your healthcare provider.
* **Understanding of Medication (10)**: Rate how well you understand the anticoagulation medication.
* **Were Your Concerns Addressed (11)**: Indicate whether your concerns were addressed by your healthcare provider.
* **Understanding of Bleeding Risk (12)**: Rate how well you understand the risks of bleeding associated with anticoagulation.
* **Knowledge of Warning Signs (13)**: Report whether you know when to seek help.
* **Monitoring Schedule Understanding (14)**: Indicate your understanding of your monitoring schedule.
* **Medication Adherence (15)**: Report how often you take your medication as prescribed.
* **Barriers to Adherence (16)**: Select any obstacles that make it difficult for you to adhere to your medication regimen.
* **Side Effects Experienced (17)**: List any side effects you have experienced.
* **Were Side Effects Reported (18)**: Report whether you informed your healthcare provider about the side effects.
* **Provider Responsiveness (19)**: Rate how responsive your healthcare provider is.
* **Follow-up Appointment Frequency (20)**: Indicate the frequency of follow-up appointments with your healthcare provider.
* **Satisfaction with Care (21)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with care.
* **Would You Recommend Your Provider (22)**: Indicate whether you would recommend your healthcare provider to others.
* **Additional Information Needed (23)**: Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share.
* **Suggestions for Improvement (24)**: Suggest ways to improve anticoagulation care.

## Tips
- Please complete the form honestly and thoroughly.
- Select the options that best describe your experiences and perceptions.
- If you are unsure about any question, try to choose the option that best fits your situation.
