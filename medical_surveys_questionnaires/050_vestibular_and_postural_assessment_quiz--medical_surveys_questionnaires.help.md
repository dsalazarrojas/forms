# vestibular_and_postural_assessment_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Vestibular and Postural Assessment Quiz, a form designed to help clinicians evaluate patients' vestibular and postural systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, choosing the correct option for each field.
2. If a field is not required, you can skip it or leave it blank.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Balance Screening** (Balance Screening, select_multiple)
	* Select all applicable options for the patient's balance screening status.

### 2. **Dizziness Question** (Dizziness Question, text)
	* Describe your best effort to understand the nature of the dizziness or balance issues you are experiencing.

### 3. **Posture Check** (Posture Check, select_one)
	* Indicate whether the patient's posture is normal or abnormal.

### 4. **Symptoms Duration** (Symptoms Duration, time)
	* Enter the duration of the patient's vestibular symptoms, if applicable.

### 5. **Postural Deficits** (Postural Deficits, text)
	* Describe any postural deficits or symptoms you are experiencing.

### 6. **Treatment Plan** (Treatment Plan, note)
	* Provide any relevant medical history or treatment plans for the patient's postural system.

### 7. **Notes** (Notes, note)
	* Add any additional information or comments about the patient's vestibular and postural system.

### 8. **Vestibular Assessment** (Vestibular Assessment, select_one)
	* Indicate the patient's vestibular system assessment status.

### 9. **Balance Assessment** (Balance Assessment, select_one)
	* Indicate the patient's balance assessment status.

### 10. **Postural Assessment** (Postural Assessment, select_one)
	* Indicate the patient's postural assessment status.

### 11. **Overall Posture Score** (Overall Posture Score, number)
	* Enter a numerical value to indicate the patient's overall postural score.

### 12. **Overall Balance Score** (Overall Balance Score, number)
	* Enter a numerical value to indicate the patient's overall balance score.

### 13. **Vestibular Symptoms** (Vestibular Symptoms, text)
	* Describe any vestibular symptoms you are experiencing.

### 14. **Postural Symptoms** (Postural Symptoms, text)
	* Describe any postural symptoms you are experiencing.

### 15. **Postural Deficit Impact** (Postural Deficit Impact, number)
	* Enter a numerical value to indicate the impact of the patient's postural deficits on their daily life.

This guide is designed to help you complete the Vestibular and Postural Assessment Quiz. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the healthcare provider for further assistance.
