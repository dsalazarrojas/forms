# Egg Donor Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Egg Donor Screening Questionnaire is designed to collect personal and medical information from potential egg donors to help the medical team evaluate their suitability for the egg donation process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please provide accurate and truthful information about yourself. Please fill in all required fields. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Contact Information

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter your current address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.

### Page 2: Medical History

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or other health issues you have had in the past or are currently experiencing.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): List any allergies you have.
* **Medical Family History** (`medical_family_history`, text, required): Provide information about any medical conditions that run in your family.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, required): Describe any recent or upcoming travel plans.

### Page 3: Personal Information

* **Surgical History** (`surgical_history`, text, required): Describe any past surgeries you have had.
* **Height in Inches** (`height_in_inches`, number, required): Enter your height in inches.
* **Weight in Pounds** (`weight_in_pounds`, number, required): Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Body Mass Index** (`body_mass_index`, number, required): Enter your Body Mass Index (BMI).
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, select_one, optional): Select your marital status (Single, Married).
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, optional): Select your highest education level achieved (High School, Associate's, Bachelor's).

### Page 4: Personal Background

* **Family Background** (`family_background`, text, required): Provide information about your family, including any siblings, children, or parents.
* **Employment History** (`employment_history`, text, required): Describe your work history and any relevant work experience.
* **Travel Documentation** (`travel_documentation`, text, required): Provide any relevant travel documents or visas.
* **Family Plan** (`family_plan`, text, required): Describe any family plans you have.

### Page 5: Additional Information

* **Children** (`children`, text, required): List any children you have or are planning to have.
* **Siblings** (`siblings`, text, required): List any siblings you have.
* **Parents** (`parents`, text, required): Provide information about your parents.
* **Height in Feet and Inches** (`height_in_feet_and_inches`, text, optional): Enter your height in feet and inches (if you prefer this format).
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, optional): Select your highest education level achieved (High School, Associate's, Bachelor's).

Tips: Please double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
