# End Of Life Care Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the quality of end-of-life care communication between patients and healthcare staff. It helps to understand the effectiveness of communication, staff performance, and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the facility where the patient is receiving care in the "Facility Name" field.
3. Complete the sections for each of the 25 questions, using the provided options or scales to indicate your level of agreement or preference.
4. Review your responses to ensure that you're providing accurate and thoughtful input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it is commonly known.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the facility where the patient is receiving care.
* **Date Of Death** (`date_of_death`, date, required): Enter the date of the patient's death.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, number, required): On a scale of 1-5, rate the effectiveness of communication with the patient.
* **Communication Frequency** (`communication_frequency`, select_one, required): How often would you like to be updated on the patient's condition? Choose from:
	+ Never
	+ Rarely
	+ Sometimes
	+ Often
	+ Always
* **Communication Method** (`communication_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose the methods you prefer for communication with the patient. Select all that apply:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ In-Person
	+ Text Message
* **Communication Quality** (`communication_quality`, number, required): On a scale of 1-5, rate the quality of communication with the patient.
* **Pain Management** (`pain_management`, number, required): On a scale of 1-5, rate the effectiveness of pain management for the patient.
* **Emotional Support** (`emotional_support`, number, required): On a scale of 1-5, rate the level of emotional support provided to the patient.
* **Spiritual Support** (`spiritual_support`, number, required): On a scale of 1-5, rate the level of spiritual support provided to the patient.
* **Practical Support** (`practical_support`, number, required): On a scale of 1-5, rate the level of practical support provided to the patient.
* **Spiritual Support Services** (`spiritual_support_services`, select_multiple, required): Choose the spiritual support services you've provided to the patient. Select all that apply:
	+ Prayer
	+ Meditation
	+ Visitation
* **Pain Management Services** (`pain_management_services`, select_multiple, required): Choose the pain management services you've provided to the patient. Select all that apply:
	+ Medication Management
	+ Interventions
	+ Therapy
* **Emotional Support Services** (`emotional_support_services`, select_multiple, required): Choose the emotional support services you've provided to the patient. Select all that apply:
	+ Counseling
	+ Family Support
	+ Peer Support
* **Practical Support Services** (`practical_support_services`, select_multiple, required): Choose the practical support services you've provided to the patient. Select all that apply:
	+ Home Care
	+ Transportation
	+ Meal Delivery
* **Staff Respect** (`staff_respect`, number, required): On a scale of 1-5, rate how the staff treated the patient with respect.
* **Staff Professionalism** (`staff_professionalism`, number, required): On a scale of 1-5, rate the staff's professionalism.
* **Facilities** (`facilities`, number, required): On a scale of 1-5, rate the facilities available to the patient.
* **Communication Style** (`communication_style`, select_one, required): Choose the style of communication you prefer with the patient:
	+ Direct
	+ Indirect
	+ Family Involvement
	+ Patient Involvement
* **Communication Frequency 2** (`communication_frequency_2`, select_one, required): How often would you like to be updated on the patient's condition? Choose from:
	+ Never
	+ Rarely
	+ Sometimes
	+ Often
	+ Always
* **Communication Method 2** (`communication_method_2`, select_multiple, required): Choose the methods you prefer for communication with the patient. Select all that apply:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ In-Person
	+ Text Message
* **Communication Quality 2** (`communication_quality_2`, number, required): On a scale of 1-5, rate the quality of communication with the patient.
* **Pain Management 2** (`pain_management_2`, number, required): On a scale of 1-5, rate the effectiveness of pain management for the patient.
* **Emotional Support Services 2** (`emotional_support_services_2`, select_multiple, required): Choose the emotional support services you've provided to the patient. Select all that apply:
	+ Counseling
	+ Family Support
	+ Peer Support

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Take the time to reflect on your experiences with the patient.
* If you're unsure about any question, try to answer based on the patient's needs and your best judgment.
* This survey is a tool for improvement, so your input will help make a positive impact on patient care.
