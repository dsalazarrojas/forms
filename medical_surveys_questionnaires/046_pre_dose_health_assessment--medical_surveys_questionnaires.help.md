# pre_dose_health_assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The pre_dose_health_assessment form is designed to collect information about a patient's health status before administering a dose of a specific medication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Allergies** (1, text, required): Please list any known allergies the patient has.
* **Recent Symptoms** (2, text, required): Describe any recent symptoms the patient is experiencing.
* **Medical Condition** (3, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the patient has any medical conditions.
* **Medications** (4, select_multiple, required): List all the medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Consent** (5, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the patient has given their consent for the dose.
* **Dosing Consent** (6, note, not required): This field is for note-taking purposes.
* **Note** (7, note, not required): This field is for any additional notes about the patient.
* **Contact Number** (8, text, not required): Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Goal** (9, text, required): Describe the patient's treatment goal.
* **Follow-up Date** (10, date, required): Enter the date of the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Time** (11, time, required): Enter the time of the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Location** (12, text, required): Enter the location of the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Instrument** (13, text, required): Describe the instrument to be used for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Notes** (14, note, not required): This field is for any additional notes about the follow-up.
* **Medical Survey** (15, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the patient has agreed to participate in a medical survey.
* **Survey** (16, select_multiple, required): List all the surveys the patient has agreed to participate in.
* **Consent Date** (17, date, required): Enter the date the patient gave their consent.
* **Consent Time** (18, time, required): Enter the time the patient gave their consent.
* **Consent Location** (19, text, required): Enter the location where the patient gave their consent.
* **Consent Instrument** (20, text, required): Describe the instrument used for consent.
* **Consent Notes** (21, note, not required): This field is for any additional notes about the consent.
* **Contact Email** (22, email, not required): Enter the patient's contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (23, text, not required): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Contact Name** (24, text, not required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Contact Address** (25, text, not required): Enter the patient's address.
