# Hospital Energy Efficiency Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from hospital staff about their awareness and perceptions of energy use in the hospital. It helps to identify areas of improvement and inform hospital energy management strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your abilities.
2. Provide your suggestions for energy efficiency improvements.
3. Enter your contact information (email, phone number, and preferred date and time for a follow-up discussion).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **What is the purpose of this survey** (staff_survey)
This field is for you to describe the purpose of this survey. Please provide a brief explanation in a few sentences.

### 2. **Which energy use is most important to you** (energy_use)
Choose the energy use that is most important to you from the options provided:

* Option 1
* Option 2
* Option 3

### 3. **What is your current awareness level of energy use in the hospital** (awareness_level)
Please describe your current level of awareness about energy use in the hospital.

### 4. **What suggestions do you have for energy efficiency improvement** (suggestions)
Use this field to provide any suggestions you have for improving energy efficiency in the hospital.

### 5. **Email**
Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up discussions.

### 6. **Phone**
Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you for follow-up discussions.

### 7. **Date**
Enter the date you would prefer to have a follow-up discussion about this survey.

### 8. **Time**
Enter the time you would prefer to have a follow-up discussion about this survey.

### 9. **Are there any required fields** (required_fields)
Select all the required fields that you are familiar with. Please note that this information is for internal reference only and won't be visible to other users.

### 10. **Hospital Name** (hospital_name)
Enter the name of your hospital to help us understand which hospital you are representing.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
* If you have any further questions or clarifications, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Your input will help inform hospital energy management strategies and contribute to a more sustainable future.
