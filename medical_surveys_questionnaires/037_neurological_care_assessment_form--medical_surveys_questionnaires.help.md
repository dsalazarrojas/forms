# Neurological Care Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neurological Care Assessment Form is designed to gather information from patients about their neurological health and medical history to help healthcare professionals assess their condition and develop an appropriate treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability, providing as much information as possible.
2. For questions with a text field, please provide a brief but detailed answer.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask a healthcare professional for assistance.
4. Make sure to answer questions accurately and honestly, as the information will be used to inform your treatment plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your main complaint** (`neurological_care_assessment_form_1`, text, Required): Please describe your main reason for visiting the neurologist or coming to this assessment.
* **When did your symptoms start** (`neurological_care_assessment_form_2`, date, Required): Please select the date when you first experienced your symptoms.
* **What are your symptoms** (`neurological_care_assessment_form_3`, text, Required): Please describe your symptoms in as much detail as possible.
* **When are you taking your medication** (`neurological_care_assessment_form_4`, time, Required): Please select the time of day when you take your medication.
* **Do you smoke** (`neurological_care_assessment_form_5`, select_one, Required): Please select either "False" or "True" to indicate if you smoke.
* **What is your medication** (`neurological_care_assessment_form_6`, text, Required): Please provide the name and dosage of your current medication.
* **When was your last medication change** (`neurological_care_assessment_form_7`, date, Required): Please select the date when you last changed your medication.
* **What are your medical conditions** (`neurological_care_assessment_form_8`, text, Required): Please list any pre-existing medical conditions you have.
* **How long have you had your symptoms** (`neurological_care_assessment_form_9`, number, Required): Please select the number of days or weeks you have had your symptoms.

Please note that this form will be reviewed by a healthcare professional to assess your neurological condition and develop a personalized treatment plan. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
