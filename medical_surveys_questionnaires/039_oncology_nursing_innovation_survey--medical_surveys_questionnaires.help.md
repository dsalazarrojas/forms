# Oncology Nursing Innovation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is for users who need to complete the Oncology Nursing Innovation Survey to provide feedback on oncology nursing innovation practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "New Practices" text field to provide a brief description of any new practices you have adopted or plan to adopt in your oncology nursing work.
2. Choose "Yes" or "No" in the "New Technologies" dropdown menu to indicate whether you have adopted or plan to adopt new technologies.
3. In the "Barriers" text field, list any barriers you have faced or expect to face when implementing new oncology nursing practices or technologies.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" multiple times in the "Support Needs" dropdown menu to indicate which support needs you have or will have when implementing new oncology nursing practices or technologies.
5. In the "Innovation Barriers" text field, describe any innovation barriers you have faced or expect to face when implementing new oncology nursing practices or technologies.
6. In the "Project Characteristics" text field, provide a brief description of any project characteristics you have adopted or are planning to adopt in your oncology nursing work.
7. In the "New Practices 2" text field, describe any new practices you have adopted or plan to adopt in your oncology nursing work.
8. In the "Cancer Centers" text field, list any cancer centers you have worked with or plan to work with.
9. In the "Research Projects" text field, provide a brief description of any research projects you have or plan to participate in.
10. In the "Support Needs 2" text field, list any support needs you have or expect to have when implementing new oncology nursing practices or technologies.
11. In the "Innovation Barriers 2" text field, describe any innovation barriers you have faced or expect to face when implementing new oncology nursing practices or technologies.
