# Post Surgery Physiotherapy Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients after a surgery to evaluate their mobility and pain levels, and to track their overall status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "First Visit" page with any relevant information about your initial visit with the physiotherapist.
2. Enter your patient information in the "Patient Information" page.
3. Complete the "General Information" page with any additional details about your visit.
4. On the "Mobility Level" page, select the level of mobility you are experiencing after the surgery (e.g. 1-6, with 1 being very limited and 6 being completely unrestricted).
5. On the "Pain Level" page, select the level of pain you are experiencing after the surgery (e.g. 1-6, with 1 being very high and 6 being very low).
6. On the "Mobility Status", "Pain Status", "Mobility and Pain Level" and "Overall Status" pages, select the best option that describes your current status.
7. Finally, enter any additional notes about your visit in the "Notes" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Visit**: Enter the date of your first visit with the physiotherapist.
* **Patient Information**: Enter any relevant patient information, such as name, contact number, and medical history.
* **General Information**: Enter any additional information about your visit, such as the type of surgery you had, the date of the surgery, and any other relevant details.
* **Mobility Level**: Select the level of mobility you are experiencing after the surgery, with 1 being very limited and 6 being completely unrestricted.
* **Pain Level**: Select the level of pain you are experiencing after the surgery, with 1 being very high and 6 being very low.
* **Mobility Status**: Select the best option that describes your current mobility status.
* **Pain Status**: Select the best option that describes your current pain status.
* **Mobility and Pain Level**: Select the best option that describes your current mobility and pain levels.
* **Overall Status**: Select the best option that describes your overall status.
* **Mobility Level 1-6**: Select the level of mobility you are experiencing for each specific area (e.g. mobility in the legs, hips, etc.).
* **Pain Level 1-6**: Select the level of pain you are experiencing for each specific area (e.g. pain in the legs, hips, etc.).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your visit.
* **Date First Visited**: Enter the date of your first visit with the physiotherapist.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out each page thoroughly and accurately.
* If you are experiencing any unusual symptoms or concerns, be sure to note them in the "Notes" page.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your physiotherapist for clarification.
