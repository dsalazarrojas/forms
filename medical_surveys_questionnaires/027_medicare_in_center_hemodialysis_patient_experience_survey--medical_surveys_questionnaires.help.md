# medicare_in_center_hemodialysis_patient_experience_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experience with hemodialysis services at the hospital. It aims to gather information on the quality of care, staff interaction, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before starting to ensure you have enough time to complete it.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Use the provided options for multiple-choice questions and provide a free-form text response when asked.
4. Submit the form once you have completed it.
