# Patient Weight And Height Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Weight And Height Log form is used to record and track patient weight and height measurements, along with other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate weight unit (e.g., lb, kg, oz, st) from the "Weight Unit" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the patient's weight in the "Weight" field.
3. Select the appropriate height unit (e.g., cm, m, in, ft) from the "Height Unit" field.
4. Enter the patient's height in the "Height" field.
5. (Optional) Enter the date the measurement was taken in the "Date" field.
6. (Optional) Enter any additional comments or observations in the "Comments" field.
7. (Optional) Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Weight (Weight)**: Enter the patient's weight measurement.
* **Height (Height)**: Enter the patient's height measurement.
* **Date (Date)**: Enter the date the measurement was taken (optional). This field is used to record the date the weight and height measurements were taken.
* **Weight Unit (Weight Unit)**: Select the unit of measurement for the patient's weight (e.g., lb, kg, oz, st).
* **Height Unit (Height Unit)**: Select the unit of measurement for the patient's height (e.g., cm, m, in, ft).
* **Comments (Comments)**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the patient's weight and height.
* **Date Of Birth (Date Of Birth)**: Enter the patient's date of birth (optional).
