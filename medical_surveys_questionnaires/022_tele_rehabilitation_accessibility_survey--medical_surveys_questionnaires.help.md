# Tele Rehabilitation Accessibility Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the patient's and their caregiver's contact details and preferences for tele-rehabilitation services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please provide your patient information in the first field.
2. Select one of the preferred contact methods from the list in the second field.
3. If applicable, select any access barriers that may affect your tele-rehabilitation experience in the third field.
4. Provide any additional tele-rehabilitation information in the fourth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering the patient's name, contact information, and other relevant details. Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, select_one, required): Select one of the following contact methods:
	+ **Email**: We will contact you via email for future updates and communications.
	+ **Phone**: We will call you for further discussions or appointments.
	+ **Other**: Please specify the contact method (e.g., SMS, WhatsApp, etc.).
* **Access Barriers** (`access_barriers`, select_multiple, required/optional): If you experience any access barriers that may affect your tele-rehabilitation experience, please select them from the list. Examples include:
	+ **Transportation**: Limited mobility or transportation options.
	+ **Internet**: Limited internet access or connectivity issues.
	+ **Other**: Please specify the barrier (e.g., hearing or vision impairment, etc.).
* **Tele-Rehab Information** (`tele_rehab_info`, note, required/optional): This field is for providing any additional information about your tele-rehab experience or preferences. You may use this space to specify your needs or concerns.

## Tips
Remember that all fields are required unless otherwise specified. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us for further assistance.
