# SARC F Sarcopenia Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information on potential symptoms of sarcopenia to aid in early detection and proper care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly to provide a clear picture of your symptoms and experiences.
2. For the "What is your current level of physical activity?" question, choose the best description that applies to you.
3. In the "What is the best time to contact you?" question, simply type your preferred time of day.
4. For the "Do you experience difficulty with any of the following?" question, select all the symptoms that you experience.
5. In the "Summary" section, provide a brief overview of your experiences and symptoms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`title`, `text`, not required): This is a brief welcome message explaining the purpose and importance of the form. Please read it carefully to understand the context.
* **What is your current level of physical activity?** (`question_1`, `select_one`, required): This question is used to assess your physical activity level. Choose from the options:
	+ Very active
	+ Moderately active
	+ Less active
	+ Unable to perform activities
* **What is the best time to contact you?** (`question_2`, `text`, required): This is a simple question to ensure we can contact you at your preferred time.
* **Do you experience difficulty with any of the following?** (`question_2_1`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the symptoms that you experience:
	+ Fatigue
	+ Muscle weakness
	+ Difficulty swallowing
	+ Difficulty walking
	+ Dizziness or drowsiness
	+ Difficulty speaking
	+ Memory loss
	+ Sleep disturbances
	+ Decreased appetite
	+ Poor concentration
	+ Difficulty with daily activities
* **Summary** (`summary`, `text`, not required): This is a space for you to provide a brief summary of your experiences and symptoms.
