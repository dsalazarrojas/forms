# Sleeping Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to collect information about sleep issues, sleep duration, age, and other symptoms related to sleep disorders. Please answer the questions accurately and completely to provide the best possible information for your healthcare provider.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question in the space provided, following the guidelines and instructions for each field.
2. If you have any issues or concerns related to sleep, please select "Insomnia" or "Narcolepsy" if applicable.
3. If you have any other sleep-related issues not listed, please describe them in the "Other" fields.
4. Please be as detailed as possible when answering the questions, as this information will help your healthcare provider understand your sleep patterns and provide effective care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sleep Issue** (`sleep_issue`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe any sleep issues you are experiencing.
* **Sleep Duration** (`sleep_duration`, `number`, required: false): Report your typical sleep duration in hours.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required: false): Provide your age.
* **Sleep Hours** (`sleep_hours`, `number`, required: false): Report the number of hours you sleep each day.
* **Insomnia** (`insomnia`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Insomnia
	+ Other
* **Narcolepsy** (`narcolepsy`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Narcolepsy
	+ Other
* **Other1**, **Other2**, ..., **Other13** (`other1`, `text`, required: false): If you have any other sleep-related issues not listed, describe them in these fields. 

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
* This information will help your healthcare provider understand your sleep patterns and provide effective care.
