# Needle Aspirate Treatment Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Needle Aspirate Treatment Plan form is a tool used to collect information about patients undergoing needle aspiration procedures. The form helps healthcare professionals gather crucial data for effective treatment planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Patient Info" page and fill in the necessary details about the patient.
2. Proceed to the "Procedure Details" page and provide information about the procedure.
3. Finally, move to the "Follow Up Plans" page and document the plans for follow-up care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Info (patient_info)** 
* A text field to collect patient information. Enter the patient's name, date of birth, medical record number, or any other relevant details.

### 2. **Procedure Details (procedure_details)**
* A text field to document the specifics of the needle aspiration procedure, including the location, date, and any notable findings or complications.

### 3. **Follow Up Plans (follow_up_plans)**
* A text field to outline the plans for follow-up care, including appointment schedules, medication instructions, or any other necessary next steps for the patient's recovery.

## Tips
* Ensure to enter accurate and complete information to facilitate efficient treatment planning.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with a healthcare professional or the patient's medical team.
* Save the form regularly to avoid losing unsaved data.
