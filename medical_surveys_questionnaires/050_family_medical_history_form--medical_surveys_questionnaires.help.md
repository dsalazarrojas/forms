# Family Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Medical History Form is designed to collect detailed information about a patient's medical history, relationships, and other relevant health conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the family member being reported in the "Family Member Name" field.
3. Select the relationship between the patient and the family member in the "Relationship" field.
4. Report any family history of diseases in the "Family History of Diseases" field.
5. Provide details about any diseases the patient has been diagnosed with, including frequency, symptoms, and treatment outcomes.
6. Enter any other health conditions or notes about the patient's medical history in the "Other Health Conditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the patient.
* **Family Member Name** (`family_member_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the family member being reported.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, text, required): Select the relationship between the patient and the family member (e.g. mother, father, brother, etc.).
* **Family History of Diseases** (`family_history_diseases`, text, optional): Report any family history of diseases.
* **Disease 1** (`disease_1`, text, optional): Report details about the first disease the patient has been diagnosed with.
* **Disease 2** (`disease_2`, text, optional): Report details about the second disease the patient has been diagnosed with.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history_diseases`, text, optional): Report any other medical history.
* **Doctor Surname** (`doctor_s_surname`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's surname.
* **Doctor Given Names** (`doctor_s_given_names`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's given names.
* **Other Health Conditions** (`medical_history_other`, text, optional): Report any other health conditions.
* **Other Notes** (`medical_history_other_notes`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant notes about the patient's medical history.
* **Frequency** (`medical_history_disease_1_frequency`, text, optional): Report the frequency of the first disease.
* **Symptoms** (`medical_history_disease_1_symptoms`, text, optional): Report the symptoms of the first disease.
* **Treatment** (`medical_history_disease_1_treatment`, text, optional): Report the treatment of the first disease.
* **Outcome** (`medical_history_disease_1_outcome`, text, optional): Report the outcome of the first disease.
* **Medical History Disease 2 Frequency** (`medical_history_disease_2_frequency`, text, optional): Report the frequency of the second disease.
* **Medical History Disease 2 Symptoms** (`medical_history_disease_2_symptoms`, text, optional): Report the symptoms of the second disease.
* **Medical History Disease 2 Treatment** (`medical_history_disease_2_treatment`, text, optional): Report the treatment of the second disease.
* **Medical History Disease 2 Outcome** (`medical_history_disease_2_outcome`, text, optional): Report the outcome of the second disease.
* **Medical History Disease 3 Frequency** (`medical_history_disease_3_frequency`, text, optional): Report the frequency of the third disease.
* **Medical History Disease 3 Symptoms** (`medical_history_disease_3_symptoms`, text, optional): Report the symptoms of the third disease.
* **Medical History Disease 3 Treatment** (`medical_history_disease_3_treatment`, text, optional): Report the treatment of the third disease.
* **Medical History Disease 3 Outcome** (`medical_history_disease_3_outcome`, text, optional): Report the outcome of the third disease.
* **Medical History Other Notes 2** (`medical_history_other_notes_2`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant notes about the patient's medical history.
* **Medical History Other Conditions** (`medical_history_other_conditions`, text, optional): Report any other health conditions.
* **Medical History Other Conditions 2** (`medical_history_other_conditions_2`, text, optional): Report any other health conditions.

Note: This form does not collect sensitive or identifying information, and all responses will be treated confidentially. Please ensure that you have the patient's consent before completing this form.
