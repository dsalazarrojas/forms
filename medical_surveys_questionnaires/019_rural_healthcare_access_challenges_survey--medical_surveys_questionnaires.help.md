# Rural Healthcare Access Challenges Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the challenges faced by individuals in rural areas when accessing healthcare services. It helps to identify the issues and barriers that prevent rural populations from receiving necessary healthcare services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the instructions carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
* Fill out the form in a quiet and distraction-free environment.
* Ensure you have all necessary information and documents before completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. rural_healthcare_access_barriers
* **Rural Healthcare Access Barriers** (Text, required): This field is for you to describe the specific challenges or barriers you face in accessing healthcare services in your rural area. Please be as detailed and honest as possible.

### 2. service_availability
* **Service Availability** (Select One, required): Choose one of the following options to indicate how easily accessible healthcare services are in your area:
	+ Option 1: Very easy to access
	+ Option 2: Somewhat easy to access
	+ Option 3: Difficult to access

### 3. population_characteristics
* **Population Characteristics** (Text, optional): If you would like to provide additional information about the population in your area, this field is for you to describe their characteristics, such as age, income, or education level.

### 4. community_resources
* **Community Resources** (Text, optional): If you would like to mention any community resources that are available to help overcome the access barriers, such as community clinics or support groups, this field is for you to describe them.

### 5. service_benefits
* **Service Benefits** (Text, optional): If you would like to describe the benefits you receive from the healthcare services that are accessible in your area, this field is for you to describe them.

### 6. data_source
* **Data Source** (Email, required): Please provide your email address for us to contact you for follow-up questions or to share the results of this survey with you.

### 7. program_planning
* **Program Planning** (Time, required): Please provide a specific time for the program to be implemented or planned for.

### 8. research_reporting
* **Research Reporting** (Select Multiple, required): Choose all the options that apply to how you would like to receive research reports:
	+ Option 1: Email
	+ Option 2: Phone
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify)

### 9. other
* **Other** (Note, optional): If you have any other comments or suggestions, this field is for you to provide them.

### 10. comments
* **Comments** (Text, optional): If you would like to add any additional comments, this field is for you to do so.

### 11. submit
* **Submit** (Select One, required): Please choose how you would like to submit this form:
	+ Option 1: Online
	+ Option 2: In-person

### 12. email
* **Email** (Email, required): Please enter your email address for us to contact you.

### 13. name
* **Name** (Text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.

### 14. organization
* **Organization** (Text, optional): If you are representing an organization, please enter its name.

### 15. contact_number
* **Contact Number** (Text, required): Please enter your contact number for us to reach you.

### 16. address
* **Address** (Text, optional): If you would like to provide your address, this field is for you to do so.

### 17. city
* **City** (Text, optional): If you would like to provide your city, this field is for you to do so.

### 18. state
* **State** (Text, optional): If you would like to provide your state, this field is for you to do so.

### 19. zip
* **Zip** (Text, required): Please enter your zip code.

### 20. country
* **Country** (Text, required): Please enter your country of residence.

### 21. latitude
* **Latitude** (Number, optional): If you would like to provide your latitude, this field is for you to do so.

### 22. longitude
* **Longitude** (Number, optional): If you would like to provide your longitude, this field is for you to do so.

### 23. elevation
* **Elevation** (Text, optional): If you would like to provide the elevation of your area, this field is for you to do so.

### 24. submit_button
* **Submit** (Select One, required): Please choose how you would like to submit this form:
	+ Option 1: Online
	+ Option 2: In-person

### 25. submit
* **Submit** (Select One, required): This is the final step to submit your survey.

## Tips
* Please take your time to answer each question carefully and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask for help or clarification.
* Thank you for participating in this survey and helping us improve healthcare access in rural areas.
