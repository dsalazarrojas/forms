# Home Healthcare Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Healthcare Report Form is a tool for healthcare professionals to document essential information about patient visits, enabling them to track and analyze patient data for better care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the date of the visit in the "Date of Visit" field.
3. If necessary, describe the patient's primary complaint in the "Primary Complaint" field.
4. Indicate the patient's status in the "Patient Status" field by selecting either "Active" or "Inactive."
5. For patients who have visited, check "Yes" for "Visit Type" if it applies.
6. Describe the patient's medical condition in the "Medical Condition" field.
7. Provide any medication information for the patient in the "Medication" field (if applicable).
8. Enter the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field.
9. If applicable, enter the number of family members cared for by the patient in the "Family Members Cared For" field.
10. Enter the duration of the visit in the "Visit Duration" field (if applicable).
11. Include any relevant patient notes in the "Patient Notes" field.
12. Record any additional staff notes in the "Staff Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (Patient Name, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Visit** (Date of Visit, date, required): Enter the date of the patient's visit using the standard date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Primary Complaint** (Primary Complaint, text, optional): Describe the reason for the patient's visit, if applicable.
* **Patient Status** (Patient Status, select_one, optional): Select "Active" if the patient is currently under treatment or "Inactive" if they are not.
* **Visit Type** (Visit Type, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" if the patient has visited the healthcare provider.
* **Medical Condition** (Medical Condition, text, required): Describe the patient's current medical condition or diagnosis.
* **Medication** (Medication, text, optional): Enter any medication the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, required): Enter the patient's complete medical history, including past treatments and medical conditions.
* **Family Members Cared For** (Family Members Cared For, number, optional): Enter the number of family members cared for by the patient.
* **Visit Duration** (Visit Duration, time, optional): Enter the duration of the visit in hours and minutes.
* **Patient Notes** (Patient Notes, note, required): Include any important notes or comments about the patient's visit.
* **Staff Notes** (Staff Notes, text, required): Record any notes or comments from healthcare staff about the patient's visit.
