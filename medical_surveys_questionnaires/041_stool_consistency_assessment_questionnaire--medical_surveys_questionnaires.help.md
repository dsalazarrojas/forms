# Stool Consistency Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

This Stool Consistency Assessment Questionnaire is a tool designed to gather information about an individual's bowel movements, including frequency, consistency, color, odors, shape, size, texture, and associated symptoms. This questionnaire aims to assess the consistency and quality of stool, as well as any related symptoms or discomforts experienced during bowel movements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your actual experiences and observations.
2. Choose the options that best describe your situation.
3. Fill out the form completely, but do not worry if you are unsure about any question.
4. Submit the form to complete the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This section is for any additional information or context you'd like to provide about your bowel movements or symptoms.
* **Bowel movements frequency** (`question_1`): How often do you experience bowel movements in a 24-hour period? Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Stool consistency** (`question_2`): How would you describe the consistency of your stool? Choose one of the following options:
	+ Liquid: Very liquid or watery stool.
	+ Soft: Soft or loose stool.
	+ Medium: Normal consistency, not too hard or too soft.
	+ Hard: Hard or dry stool.
	+ Very hard: Extremely hard or dry stool.
* **Stool color** (`question_3`): What is the color of your stool? Choose all that apply:
	+ Brown: Normal brown color.
	+ Green: Green or greenish color.
	+ Black: Black or dark brown color.
	+ Red: Red or reddish color.
	+ Blood-stained: Presence of blood in the stool.
	+ White: White or pale yellow color.
* **Stool odors** (`question_4`): What is the odor of your stool? Choose all that apply:
	+ Strong: Strong, pungent odor.
	+ Weak: Mild odor.
	+ None: No odor.
	+ Abnormal: Other unusual or unpleasant odor.
* **Stool mucus amount** (`question_5`): How much mucus is present in your stool? Choose all that apply:
	+ Small amount: A small amount of mucus.
	+ Moderate amount: A moderate amount of mucus.
	+ Large amount: A large amount of mucus.
	+ None: No mucus.
* **Stool shape** (`question_6`): What is the shape of your stool? Choose one of the following:
	+ Normal shape: The normal or expected shape of stool.
	+ Sausage shape: A sausage-like shape.
	+ Ball or pellet: A ball or pellet shape.
	+ Amorphous shape: No shape or irregular shape.
* **Stool size** (`question_7`): How large is your stool? (Note: This question may not be directly related to the stool consistency)
* **Stool texture** (`question_8`): If you have any specific thoughts about the texture of your stool, describe them in this field.
* **Abdominal pain after bowel movements** (`question_9`): How often do you experience abdominal pain after bowel movements?
	+ None: No pain.
	+ Mild: Mild pain.
	+ Moderate: Moderate pain.
	+ Severe: Severe pain.
* **Diarrhea symptoms** (`question_10`): Are you experiencing any symptoms of diarrhea?
	+ None: No symptoms.
	+ Mild: Mild symptoms.
	+ Moderate: Moderate symptoms.
	+ Severe: Severe symptoms.
* **Stool passage duration** (`question_11`): How long does it take for you to pass stool?
* **How often do you experience bowel movements?** (`question_12`): How often do you experience bowel movements in a 24-hour period? Choose one of the following:
	+ Less than once a day: Less than once a day.
	+ Once a day: Once a day.
	+ 2 to 3 times a day: 2 to 3 times a day.
	+ More than 3 times a day: More than 3 times a day.
* **Stool color when passing gas?** (`question_13`): What color is your stool when passing gas?
	+ None: No color.
	+ Brown: Brown color.
	+ Red: Red color.
	+ Green: Green color.
* **Do you use fiber supplements?** (`question_14`): Do you take any fiber supplements?
	+ True: Yes, you take fiber supplements.
	+ False: No, you do not take fiber supplements.
* **Do you experience abdominal cramps after bowel movements?** (`question_15`): Do you experience abdominal cramps after bowel movements?
	+ True: Yes, you experience cramps.
	+ False: No, you do not experience cramps.
* **Have you been experiencing any other symptoms?** (`question_16`): Are you experiencing any other symptoms? Choose all that apply:
	+ None: No symptoms.
	+ Abdominal pain: Abdominal pain.
	+ Bloating: Bloating or discomfort.
	+ Nausea: Nausea or feeling sick.
	+ Vomiting: Vomiting or throwing up.
	+ Diarrhea: Diarrhea or loose stools.
	+ Abdominal cramps: Abdominal cramps.
	+ Blood in stool: Blood in stool.
	+ Mucus in stool: Mucus in stool.
	+ Other: Other symptoms not listed.
