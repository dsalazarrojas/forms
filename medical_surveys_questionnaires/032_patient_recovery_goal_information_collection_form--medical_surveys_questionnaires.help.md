# Patient Recovery Goal Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a patient's recovery goals and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by selecting the patient ID and provider from the available options.
2.  Fill in the primary and secondary recovery goals as per the patient's condition.
3.  Briefly describe the recovery planning and steps that will be taken.
4.  Enter the expected recovery date.
5.  Enter the expected recovery time.
6.  Describe the expected outcome of the patient's recovery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the patient's ID as per the hospital's ID system.
*   **Provider** (`provider_id`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the healthcare provider who will be treating the patient.
*   **Primary Recovery Goal** (`recovery_goal_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter the primary goal of the patient's recovery (e.g., surgery, physical therapy).
*   **Secondary Recovery Goal** (`recovery_goal_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the secondary goal of the patient's recovery.
*   **Recovery Planning** (`recovery_planning`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the steps and plans for the patient's recovery.
*   **Recovery Date** (`recovery_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the expected date of the patient's recovery.
*   **Recovery Time** (`recovery_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the expected time of the patient's recovery.
*   **Expected Outcome** (`expected_outcome`, `text`, required: false): Describe the expected outcome of the patient's recovery.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out this form accurately and truthfully.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the healthcare provider.
*   This form should only be filled out for patients who have been admitted to or are receiving care from the healthcare facility.
