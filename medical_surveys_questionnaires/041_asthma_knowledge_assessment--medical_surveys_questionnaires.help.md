# Asthma Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to evaluate the participant's knowledge and understanding of asthma, a chronic respiratory disease characterized by inflammation and constriction of the airways. The assessment covers various aspects of asthma, including its definition, triggers, symptoms, management, and prevention strategies. The form is designed to gauge the participant's confidence in their knowledge and identify areas where they may need more information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields as they appear on each page. These are marked with a red asterisk (\*) in the label. The form has two pages in total.
2.  Answer each question honestly to the best of your ability.
3.  Use the provided options to choose the correct answer for each question.
4.  If you have any additional comments or questions about asthma, use the "Additional Comments or Questions" field at the end of the form.
5.  Once you have completed all questions, review your answers for accuracy and completeness.
6.  Finally, submit the form for review.
