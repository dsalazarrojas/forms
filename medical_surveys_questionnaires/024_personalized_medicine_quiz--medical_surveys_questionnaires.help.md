# Personalized Medicine Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Personalized Medicine Quiz is designed to collect demographic and medical information from individuals to better understand their medical needs and preferences. This information will help healthcare providers tailor their care to meet your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, but you do not need to complete all the fields if you do not have information to provide.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address that you can access.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This can be your personal or work phone number.
6. Enter your birthdate in the format mm/dd/yyyy in the "Birthdate" field.
7. If applicable, enter any medical conditions you have in the "Medical Condition" field.
8. If applicable, enter any treatments you are currently undergoing in the "Treatment" field.
9. If applicable, enter any medications you are currently taking in the "Medication" field.
10. If applicable, enter any dosage information in the "Dosage" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Your first name, as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Your last name, as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): A valid email address that you can access.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Your personal or work phone number.
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, date, required: false): Your birthdate in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required: false): Any medical conditions you have, if applicable.
* **Treatment** (`treatment`, text, required: false): Any treatments you are currently undergoing, if applicable.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, required: false): Any medications you are currently taking, if applicable.
* **Dosage** (`dosage`, text, required: false): Any dosage information for your medications, if applicable.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): A brief description of your medical history, if applicable.

## Tips

* Please ensure your email address is valid and accessible.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your healthcare provider for guidance.
* Please provide as much information as possible, but do not feel obligated to provide sensitive information if you are not comfortable sharing it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider for assistance.
