# Dental Care Knowledge Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct answer option for each question from the provided options.
2. Fill in the text fields (e.g., "What is your job title?", "What is your phone number?").
3. Enter a date if required for the "How old are you?" question.
4. Ensure all required questions are completed before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Job Title** (`What is your job title?`, text, required): Enter your current job title in the dental care industry.
- **Role in the Dental Field** (`Which of the following best describes your role in the dental field?`, select multiple, required): Choose all the roles that apply to you, such as Dentist, Dental Hygienist, or Dental Assistant.
- **Years of Experience** (`How many years of experience do you have in the field?`, number, optional): If you have experience in the dental care industry, enter the number of years.
- **Highest Completed Educational Level** (`What is your highest completed educational level in dental school?`, select one, required): Choose the highest level of education completed from the options provided.
- **Age** (`How old are you?`, date, required): Enter your current age.
- **Highest Education Level** (`Which of the following describes your highest level of education completed?`, select one, required): Choose your highest completed level of education from the options provided.
- **Phone Number** (`What is your phone number?`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Email Address** (`What is your email address?`, email, optional): Enter your professional email address.
- **Address** (`What is your address?`, text, optional): Enter your physical address.
- **Current Dental License** (`Do you have a current dental license?`, select one, required): Choose "Yes" if you have a current dental license.
- **License Number** (`If yes, what is your license number?`, text, optional): If you have a current dental license, enter the license number.
- **Current Dental Certification** (`Do you have a current dental certification?`, select one, required): Choose "Yes" if you have a current dental certification.
- **Certification Number** (`If yes, what is your certification number?`, text, optional): If you have a current dental certification, enter the certification number.
- **Malpractice Insurance** (`Do you have any malpractice insurance?`, select one, required): Choose "Yes" if you have malpractice insurance.
- **Malpractice Insurance Number** (`If yes, what is your malpractice insurance number?`, text, optional): If you have malpractice insurance, enter the insurance number.
- **Malpractice Claims** (`Do you have any malpractice claims pending?`, select one, required): Choose "Yes" if you have a pending malpractice claim.
- **Claims Status** (`If yes, what is the status of the claim?`, text, optional): If you have a pending malpractice claim, enter the current status.
- **Malpractice Settlements** (`Do you have any malpractice settlements?`, select one, required): Choose "Yes" if you have a malpractice settlement.
- **Claims Status** (`If yes, what is the status of the claim?`, text, optional): If you have a malpractice settlement, enter the current status.
- **Malpractice Suits Filed** (`Do you have any malpractice suits filed against you?`, select one, required): Choose "Yes" if you have a malpractice suit filed against you.
- **Suit Status** (`If yes, what is the status of the suit?`, text, optional): If you have a malpractice suit filed against you, enter the current status.
- **Malpractice Lawsuits Pending** (`Do you have any malpractice lawsuits pending?`, select one, required): Choose "Yes" if you have a malpractice lawsuit pending.
- **Lawsuit Status** (`If yes, what is the status of the lawsuit?`, text, optional): If you have a malpractice lawsuit pending, enter the current status.
- **Malpractice Judgments** (`Do you have any malpractice judgments against you?`, select one, required): Choose "Yes" if you have a malpractice judgment against you.
- **Judgment Status** (`If yes, what is the status of the judgment?`, text, optional): If you have a malpractice judgment against you, enter the current status.
