# Voice Tremor Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voice Tremor Assessment Form is designed to gather information about a patient's symptoms and medical history related to tremors. This form is intended to be used by healthcare professionals to assess the severity and impact of tremors on a patient's daily life. Please ensure that you are completing this form for the correct purpose and do not use it for other medical assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability. If you are unsure about any question, please provide as much information as you can.
2. Complete all the required fields (indicated by *).
3. Use any additional space provided in the form to provide further information if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Symptom History**: *Required: false* (Not required)
	+ This field is a text box where you can provide any relevant medical history or symptoms you are experiencing related to tremors.
* **Tremor Severity**: *Required: false* (Not required)
	+ This field is a number field where you can rate the severity of your tremors from 1 to 10. 1 represents mild and 10 represents severe.
* **Daily Impact**: *Required: false* (Not required)
	+ This field has two options: 'Yes' and 'No'. Choose 'Yes' if you experience any daily impact related to your tremors, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Voice Samples**: *Required: false* (Not required)
	+ This is an optional field for additional comments or voice samples related to your tremors.
* **Medical History**: *Required: false* (Not required)
	+ This field is another text box where you can provide any relevant medical history or symptoms you are experiencing.

Note that all these fields are not required, but please do provide the necessary information for an accurate assessment.
