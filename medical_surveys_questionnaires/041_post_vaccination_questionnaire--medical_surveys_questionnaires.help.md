# Post-Vaccination Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post-Vaccination Questionnaire is a form used to collect information from patients after they have received a vaccine. This information helps healthcare professionals to assess the effectiveness and safety of the vaccination process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of vaccine administered from the drop-down menu.
2. Enter the date and time of vaccination.
3. Select any common side effects experienced from the list.
4. Enter any additional comments or information.
5. Fill in the relevant medical information, including doctor's name, patient's name, patient's date of birth, and medical license numbers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vaccine Administered** (`vaccine_administered`, select_one, required: false): This field asks you to select the type of vaccine you received. Choose the correct option from the drop-down menu.
* **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you received the vaccine.
* **Vaccination Time** (`vaccination_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you received the vaccine.
* **Common Side Effects** (`side_effects`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any common side effects you experienced after vaccination.
* **Medication Takes** (`medication_takes`, text, required: false): Enter any medication you are taking.
* **Follow up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, text, required: false): Enter any follow-up contact information.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, text, required: false): Enter any consent information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Enter your medical history.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any allergies you have.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any medical conditions you have.
* **Medical History Details** (`medical_history_details`, text, required: false): Enter your medical history details.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required: false): Enter your doctor's name.
* **Patient's Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Patient's DOB** (`patient_dob`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **Patient's Gender** (`patient_gender`, select_one, required: false): Select your gender.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, text, required: false): Enter your medical license number.
* **Patient's Address** (`patient_address`, text, required: false): Enter your address.
* **Patient's Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Patient's Email** (`patient_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email.
* **Medical License Number State** (`medical_license_number_state`, select_one, required: false): Select your state.
* **Doctor's Address** (`doctor_address`, text, required: false): Enter your doctor's address.
* **Doctor's Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor's License Number** (`doctor_license_number`, text, required: false): Enter your doctor's license number.

## Tips
Please fill in this form accurately and completely. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your healthcare provider.
