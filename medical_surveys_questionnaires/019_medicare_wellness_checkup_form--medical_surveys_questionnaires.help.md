# medicare wellness checkup form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medicare Wellness Checkup Form is designed to gather information about your medical history, health status, and other relevant factors to help your healthcare provider assess your overall well-being. This form is a helpful tool for you to provide accurate and comprehensive information to inform your care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions in detail, as your healthcare provider will use this information to make informed decisions about your care.
2. For fields with select options, choose the best fit for your situation.
3. Be honest and accurate with your answers, as they will help ensure your healthcare provider receives a true representation of your condition.
4. If you are unsure about a particular question, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your medical history**:
	+ This field asks for a brief description of your medical history, including any past illnesses, injuries, or medical procedures.
* **What is your current health condition**:
	+ This field is for you to describe your current health status, including any ongoing health issues or concerns.
* **What is your family medical history**:
	+ This field is for you to report any medical issues or conditions in your family members, including parents, siblings, or children.
* **What is your social history**:
	+ This field is for you to describe any significant life events or experiences that may impact your health, such as job changes, moves, or relationships.
* **What are your current medications**:
	+ Choose from the list of options to select all medications you are currently taking.
* **What are your current allergies**:
	+ This field is for you to report any allergies or sensitivities you may have.
* **What are your current vaccinations**:
	+ This field is for you to report any vaccinations you have received.
* **What are your health goals**:
	+ This field is for you to describe any health goals you have, such as weight loss or exercise plans.
* **What is your current diet**:
	+ This field is for you to describe your current diet, including any dietary restrictions or preferences.
* **What is your physical activity level**:
	+ This field is for you to report your level of physical activity, with options for sedentary, light, moderate, or strenuous.
* **What is your current sleep quality**:
	+ This field is for you to describe your sleep quality, including any difficulties or concerns.
* **What is your current stress level**:
	+ Choose from the list of options to select all stress levels that apply to you.
* **What is your current mood level**:
	+ Choose from the list of options to select your current mood level.
* **What is your current energy level**:
	+ This field is for you to report your current energy level, with options for high, low, or average.
* **What is your current pain level**:
	+ This field is for you to report your current pain level, with options for 0-10.
* **What is your current bowel movement**:
	+ This field is for you to report your current bowel movement frequency.
* **What is your current urinary frequency**:
	+ This field is for you to report your current urinary frequency.
* **What is your current bowel frequency**:
	+ This field is for you to report your current bowel frequency.
* **What is your current vital signs**:
	+ This field is for you to report any vital signs, such as blood pressure or temperature.
* **What is your current symptoms**:
	+ This field is for you to report any current symptoms or concerns.
* **What is your family history of medical issues**:
	+ This field is for you to report any medical issues or conditions in your family members.
* **What is your current immune system**:
	+ This field is for you to report your current immune system status.
* **What is your current mental health**:
	+ This field is for you to report your current mental health status.
* **What is your current social support**:
	+ This field is for you to report any social support systems you have in place.
