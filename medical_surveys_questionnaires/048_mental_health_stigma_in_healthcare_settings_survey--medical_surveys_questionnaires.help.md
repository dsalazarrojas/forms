# mental_health_stigma_in_healthcare_settings_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey aims to gather information about your experiences and attitudes towards stigma in healthcare settings. Your responses will help healthcare institutions better understand the challenges professionals like you face and work towards creating a more supportive environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all questions carefully, considering your current role and experience in healthcare.
2. Select the appropriate options for each question, and type in any additional comments when necessary.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Experience of Stigma** (`experienced_stigma`, `select_one`, **Required**): This question asks you to describe whether you have experienced stigma in your healthcare setting. Please select "True" if you have, and "False" if you haven't.
* **Attitudes towards Stigma** (`attitudes_towards_stigma`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Indicate your level of agreement or disagreement with the statement regarding stigma in healthcare settings.
* **Comfort Level** (`comfort_level`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate your comfort level with discussing mental health stigma in your workplace.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, **Required**): Share any suggestions or ideas you have to address mental health stigma in healthcare settings.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **Optional**): If you have any other thoughts or comments, please share them here.
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic_information`, `number`, **Required**): Enter your age.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **Optional**): Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions).
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your name.
* **Institution** (`institution`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the type of healthcare institution you work for (Hospital, Clinic, or Other).
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the department you work in.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your job title.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, **Required**): Enter your years of experience in healthcare.
* **Education Level** (`education`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select your education level (High School Diploma or Equivalent, Bachelor's, Master's, Doctoral, or Other).
* **Shift Worked** (`shift_worked`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Indicate the shifts you've worked in your current role.
* **Days per Week** (`days_per_week`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the number of days you work per week.
* **Work Shift Start Time** (`work_shift_start_time`, `time`, **Required**): Enter the time you start your shift.
* **Work Shift End Time** (`work_shift_end_time`, `time`, **Required**): Enter the time you end your shift.
* **Days Off per Week** (`days_off_per_week`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the number of days off you have per week.
* **Work Type** (`work_type`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the type of work you do (Clinical or Non-Clinical).
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select your current work status (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, or Temporary).
* **Job Title Change** (`job_title_change`, `select_one`, **Required**): Indicate whether you've had a job title change in the past 2 years.
* **Reason for Job Title Change** (`reason_for_job_title_change`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the reason for your job title change.
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, `text`, **Optional**): If you have any other comments or thoughts, please share them here.
