# Renal Transplant Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Renal Transplant Management Survey is a comprehensive form used to gather and track information on patients who have undergone renal transplants. This form helps healthcare providers monitor patient progress and make informed decisions regarding patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Fill out the form for each patient** who has undergone a renal transplant.
2.  **Enter the patient's name** in the `patient_name` field.
3.  **Enter the patient's birthday** in the `patient_birthday` field.
4.  **Enter the date of the transplant surgery** in the `surgery_date` field.
5.  **Enter the date of the transplant** in the `transplant_date` field.
6.  **Select the transplant doctor** from the list in the `transplant_doctor` field.
7.  **Select the transplant location** from the list in the `transplant_location` field.
8.  **Select the follow-up doctor** from the list in the `follow_up_doctor` field.
9.  **Select the follow-up location** from the list in the `follow_up_location` field.
10.  **Enter the transplant dosage** in the `transplant_dosage` field.
11.  **Enter the follow-up frequency** in the `follow_up_frequency` field.
12.  **Enter the follow-up duration** in the `follow_up_duration` field.
13.  **Select any medication side effects** from the list in the `medication_side_effects` field.
14.  **Select the medication tolerability** from the list in the `medication_tolerability` field.
15.  **Enter the medication dosage** in the `medication_dosage` field.
16.  **Enter any patient comments** in the `patient_comments` field.
17.  **Enter any follow-up comments** in the `follow_up_comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **patient_name** (Text, Required): This field is for entering the patient's name.
*  **patient_birthday** (Number, Required): This field is for entering the patient's birthday.
*  **surgery_date** (Date, Required): This field is for entering the date of the transplant surgery.
*  **transplant_date** (Date, Required): This field is for entering the date of the transplant.
*  **transplant_doctor** (Select One, Required): This field is for selecting the transplant doctor.
*  **transplant_location** (Select Multiple, Required): This field is for selecting the transplant location.
*  **follow_up_doctor** (Select One, Required): This field is for selecting the follow-up doctor.
*  **follow_up_location** (Select Multiple, Required): This field is for selecting the follow-up location.
*  **transplant_dosage** (Number, Required): This field is for entering the transplant dosage.
*  **follow_up_frequency** (Number, Required): This field is for entering the follow-up frequency.
*  **follow_up_duration** (Time, Required): This field is for entering the follow-up duration.
*  **medication_side_effects** (Select Multiple, Required): This field is for selecting any medication side effects.
*  **medication_tolerability** (Select Multiple, Required): This field is for selecting the medication tolerability.
*  **medication_dosage** (Number, Required): This field is for entering the medication dosage.
*  **patient_comments** (Note, Optional): This field is for entering any comments from the patient.
*  **follow_up_comments** (Note, Optional): This field is for entering any comments from the follow-up doctor or patient.
