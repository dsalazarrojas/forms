# Health Challenges Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Challenges Survey is designed to collect information from individuals about their current health challenges. This data will help healthcare professionals understand the prevalence of specific health challenges, their impact, and how to provide better support to those affected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **User Input** (Page 1)
	* This page provides a blank space for you to input any other health challenges you are facing.
2.  **What health challenges are you currently facing** (Page 2)
	* Please select all the health challenges that you are currently facing from the list below.
	* Arthritis
	* Diabetes
	* Hypertension
	* Other (please specify)
3.  **Do you have any of the following medical conditions** (Page 3)
	* Select all the medical conditions you have from the list below.
	* Arthritis
	* Diabetes
	* Hypertension
	* Other (please specify)
4.  **Please describe your health challenges** (Page 4)
	* Please provide a detailed description of your health challenges in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **User Input** (`user_input`, `note`, optional)
	+ This is a blank space for you to input any other health challenges you are facing.
*  **What health challenges are you currently facing** (`health_challenges`, `text`, optional)
	+ Please select all the health challenges that you are currently facing from the list below.
*  **Do you have any of the following medical conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Select all the medical conditions you have from the list below.
*  **Please describe your health challenges** (`health_challenges_description`, `text`, optional)
	+ Please provide a detailed description of your health challenges in this space.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please select "Other (please specify)" and provide a brief description.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
