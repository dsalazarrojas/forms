# Acanthamoeba Infection Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about Acanthamoeba keratitis, a rare but serious eye infection. The form is intended to help healthcare professionals assess the severity of the condition and provide appropriate treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and answer all questions accurately.
2. Answer all questions in the relevant section before moving on to the next one.
3. If you are unsure about any question, ask for clarification before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient full name** (`patient_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Date of birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone number** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of assessment** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your current visit.
* **Referring provider name (if applicable)** (`referring_provider`, text, optional): If you were referred by a provider, enter their name.
* **Do you wear contact lenses** (`contact_lens_wearer`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - currently", "Yes - previously but not currently", or "No - never worn contact lenses".
* **Type of contact lenses** (`contact_lens_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of contact lenses you use (Daily disposable, Extended wear (weekly or monthly), Rigid gas permeable, Cosmetic or decorative, Not applicable).
* **How do you typically clean your contact lenses** (`lens_hygiene`, select_one, required): Select how you typically clean your contact lenses (Multipurpose solution, Hydrogen peroxide system, Saline solution only, Tap water (not recommended), Do not clean - daily disposable, Not applicable).
* **Have you exposed your contact lenses to water** (`water_exposure`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - frequently", "Yes - occasionally", or "No - never".
* **Which eye is affected** (`affected_eye`, select_one, required): Select "Left eye", "Right eye", or "Both eyes".
* **Current symptoms** (`symptoms_present`, select_multiple, required): Select all symptoms you are currently experiencing (Severe eye pain, Redness, Blurred vision, Light sensitivity, Excessive tearing, Foreign body sensation, Discharge).
* **When did symptoms first begin** (`symptom_onset`, date, required): Enter the date your symptoms first began.
* **Rate your current pain level** (`pain_severity`, number, required): Rate your current pain level on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being minimal and 10 being the worst pain imaginable.
* **Have you received prior treatment** (`previous_treatment`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - prescribed medication", "Yes - over the counter drops", or "False".
* **If you received treatment, please describe what was prescribed** (`treatment_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the treatment you received.
* **Any relevant medical history** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical history that may be helpful for the clinical team.
* **Additional information** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information that may be helpful for the clinical team.

## Tips
* Answer all questions accurately and thoroughly to ensure a proper evaluation.
* If you are unsure about any question, ask for clarification before proceeding. 
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all questions have been answered.
