# health-condition-experience-submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user experiences with specific health conditions. This information will help researchers and support organizations better understand health conditions and provide valuable insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
5. Describe your health condition in the "Condition Name" field.
6. Provide a detailed description of your condition experience in the "Condition Description" field (optional).
7. Decide on your sharing preferences for researchers and support organizations by selecting "Share with researchers and support organizations", "Consent to share with researchers", or "Do not share" in the "Share with researchers and support organizations" field.
8. Decide on your consent for educational use by selecting "Consent for educational use" or "No consent" in the "Consent for educational use" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you use regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Condition Name** (`condition_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your health condition (e.g., diabetes, hypertension, etc.).
* **Condition Description** (`condition_description`, note, optional): Provide a detailed description of your condition experience (e.g., symptoms, effects, treatment, etc.).
* **Share with researchers and support organizations** (`consent_to_share`, select_one, required): Decide if you want to share your data with researchers and support organizations. You can select "Consent to share with researchers", "Consent to share with support organizations", or "Do not share".
* **Consent for educational use** (`consent_to_educational_use`, select_one, required): Decide if you consent to use your data for educational purposes. You can select "Consent for educational use" or "No consent".
