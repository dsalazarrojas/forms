# Anxiety Course Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants who have completed an anxiety course. It aims to help the course organizers understand what worked well and what areas need improvement, ensuring a better learning experience for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field as accurately and honestly as possible.
2. Start with the participant information at the top (1-3 fields).
3. Provide details about the course attended (4-5 fields).
4. Answer questions about your overall satisfaction and experience (6-11 fields).
5. Finally, add any additional comments or suggestions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your registration or identity card.
* **Name of anxiety course attended**: Enter the name of the course you completed.
* **Course completion date**: Type the date you finished the course.
* **Instructor name**: Enter the name of the course instructor.
* **Overall satisfaction with the course**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the course using the scale provided.
* **Was course content relevant to your needs?**: Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Did the course meet its stated learning objectives?**: Select the option that best fits your experience.
* **Did you acquire practical tools or techniques?**: Choose how many practical tools or techniques you gained from the course.
* **Are the tools you acquired applicable to your life?**: Select how well the tools are applicable to your situation.
* **How would you rate the instructor's knowledge?**: Choose the option that best describes their expertise.
* **How well did the instructor communicate?**: Rate the quality of their communication.
* **Was the course well organized?**: Select the option that best describes the course structure.
* **Was the course pacing appropriate?**: Choose the pace of the course that suited you best.
* **Quality of course materials**: Rate the quality of the resources provided.
* **How were group dynamics and interaction?**: Rate the interaction with other participants.
* **Was there sufficient practice or hands-on work?**: Select the amount of practice opportunities you had.
* **Has your confidence in managing anxiety improved?**: Choose how confident you feel about managing anxiety now.
* **How likely are you to recommend this course?**: Select how likely you are to recommend the course to others.
* **What improvements would you suggest?**: Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the course.
* **What additional topics would be helpful?**: Suggest any topics you believe should be included in future courses.
* **Additional comments or feedback**: Share any other thoughts or ideas you have about the course.
