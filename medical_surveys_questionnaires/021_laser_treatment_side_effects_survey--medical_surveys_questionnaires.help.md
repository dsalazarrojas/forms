# Laser Treatment Side Effects Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from patients about their experiences with laser treatments. It helps gather data on the side effects and reactions people have after undergoing laser treatment, which can be used to improve treatment effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient information, including your name and any relevant medical history.
2. Provide your contact details if you would like to be contacted for follow-up purposes.
3. Share information about the treatment you underwent, including any pre-treatment medications.
4. List any symptoms or reactions you experienced after treatment.
5. Provide a list of medications you took before and after treatment.
6. Describe any side effects or reactions you experienced after treatment.
7. Optionally, add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
8. Confirm your email address for further contact.
9. Indicate whether you can be reached by phone for follow-up.
10. Confirm that you are submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required): Please provide your name, date of birth, and any other relevant medical history.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted for follow-up purposes, provide your contact details here.
* **Treatment Information** (`treatment_information`, text, optional): Share information about the treatment you underwent, including any relevant dates and procedures.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): List any symptoms or reactions you experienced after treatment.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required): Please list all medications taken within 2 weeks prior to treatment.
* **Side Effects** (`side_effects`, text, optional): Describe any side effects or reactions you experienced after treatment.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, text, optional): Optionally, add any follow-up information or comments.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, email, required): Please confirm your email address for further contact.
* **Phone Confirmation** (`phone_confirmation`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you can be reached by phone for follow-up.
* **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`, note, required): Confirm that you are submitting this form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your symptoms and side effects.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can ask for assistance.
