# COVID 19 Employee Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Employee Survey is designed to collect information about employees' experiences, concerns, and perceptions during the COVID-19 pandemic in the workplace. This survey aims to help management and HR teams understand the needs and well-being of employees during this challenging time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and honest information.
2. Select the options that best fit your experiences and perceptions.
3. Provide additional comments or concerns in the "Additional Comments or Concerns" field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (1): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee ID** (2): Enter your employee ID or badge number.
* **Department** (3): Specify your department or team name.
* **Survey Date** (4): Select the date you are filling out this survey.
* **-- Work and Safety Concerns --**
*  **How safe do you feel in the workplace?** (6): Choose one of the options:
	+ Very safe
	+ Safe
	+ Somewhat safe
	+ Not safe
*  **Main workplace safety concerns** (7): Select all that apply:
	+ Insufficient PPE
	+ Poor ventilation
	+ Overcrowding
	+ Lack of social distancing
	+ Inadequate cleaning
	+ Lack of testing
	+ Other (please specify)
* **-- Health and Impact --**
*  **How has COVID-19 impacted you personally?** (9): Describe your personal experiences with the pandemic.
*  **Ability to perform job duties** (10): Choose one of the options:
	+ No impact
	+ Minimal impact
	+ Moderate impact
	+ Significant impact
*  **Work arrangement preference** (11): Choose one of the options:
	+ On-site only
	+ Remote only
	+ Hybrid
	+ Flexible arrangement
* **-- Mental Health --**
*  **Current stress level** (13): Choose one of the options:
	+ Not stressed
	+ Mildly stressed
	+ Moderately stressed
	+ Highly stressed
	+ Extremely stressed
*  **Need mental health support** (14): Choose one of the options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **-- Company Measures --**
*  **Aware of company COVID-19 measures** (16): Choose one of the options:
	+ Very aware
	+ Aware
	+ Somewhat aware
	+ Not aware
*  **Effectiveness of company measures** (17): Choose one of the options:
	+ Very effective
	+ Effective
	+ Somewhat effective
	+ Ineffective
*  **Suggestions for improvement** (18): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the company's response to the pandemic.
* **-- Vaccination and Testing --**
*  **Vaccination status** (20): Choose one of the options:
	+ Fully vaccinated with booster
	+ Fully vaccinated without booster
	+ Partially vaccinated
	+ Not vaccinated
	+ Prefer not to disclose
*  **Access to COVID-19 testing** (21): Choose one of the options:
	+ Easy access
	+ Accessible
	+ Difficult
	+ No access
* **-- Overall Feedback --**
*  **Rate company support during pandemic** (23): Choose one of the options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
*  **Additional comments or concerns** (24): If you have any comments, concerns, or suggestions, please include them here.
*  **May we contact you for follow-up?** (25): Choose one of the options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
