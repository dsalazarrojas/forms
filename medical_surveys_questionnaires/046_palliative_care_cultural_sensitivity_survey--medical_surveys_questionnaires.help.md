# Palliative Care Cultural Sensitivity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Palliative Care Cultural Sensitivity Survey is designed to help care teams understand the cultural needs and preferences of patients receiving palliative care. This survey aims to collect information about patients' backgrounds, care teams, challenges, and suggestions to improve cultural sensitivity in care settings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Please read the instructions and questions carefully before starting.
* For **patient_background**, please provide a brief description of the patient's background, including their cultural identity, language, and any other relevant details.
* For **care_team**, please select all the teams that are involved in the patient's care.
* For **challenges**, please select the challenges the patient faces in receiving care.
* For **suggestions**, please provide any suggestions or comments you have for improving cultural sensitivity in care settings.
* For **comments**, please share any additional comments or questions you have about the patient's care.
* For **email**, please enter your email address for contact purposes, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Background (patient_background)**: This field is for providing a brief description of the patient's background, including their cultural identity, language, and any other relevant details.
* **Care Team (care_team)**: This field allows you to select all the teams that are involved in the patient's care. Please choose all that apply.
* **Challenges (challenges)**: This field is for selecting the challenges the patient faces in receiving care.
* **Suggestions (suggestions)**: This field is for providing any suggestions or comments you have for improving cultural sensitivity in care settings.
* **Comments (comments)**: This field is for sharing any additional comments or questions you have about the patient's care.
* **Email (email)**: This field is for entering your email address for contact purposes, if desired.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly to ensure accurate data collection.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us for assistance.
* This survey is designed to improve cultural sensitivity in care settings. Your participation is invaluable to us!
