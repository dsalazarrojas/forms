# First Aid Treatment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about patients who have received first aid treatment. It is intended for medical professionals to gather data on the medical conditions of patients, the treatment provided, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the patient's name, age, and contact number.
3. Select the appropriate medical condition from the list.
4. Enter any medications given to the patient.
5. List the medical teams involved in the patient's care.
6. Enter any additional comments.
7. Select the appropriate first aid treatment from the list.
8. Enter the patient's address, city, state, zip, and country.
9. Select the appropriate first aid treatment from the list.
10. Enter the medical provider's name.
11. Enter the patient's zip, state, and country.
12. Select the appropriate first aid treatment from the list.
13. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
14. Review the form for errors before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, required): Enter the patient's age.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one, required): Select the patient's medical condition.
* **Medical Condition 2** (`medical_condition_2`, select_one, required): Select the patient's second medical condition.
* **First Aid Treatment** (`first_aid_treatment_form_3`, select_multiple, required): Select the first aid treatment provided.
* **Medication Given** (`medication_given`, text, required): Enter any medications given to the patient.
* **Patient Contact Number** (`patient_contact_number`, text, required): Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Medical Team Involved** (`medical_team`, select_multiple, required): List the medical teams involved in the patient's care.
* **Additional Comments** (`first_aid_treatment_form_6`, text, required): Enter any additional comments.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`first_aid_treatment_form_7`, text, required): Enter any additional comments 2.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time in hh:mm AM/PM format.
* **First Aid Treatment 2** (`first_aid_treatment_form_4`, select_one, required): Select the appropriate first aid treatment 2.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, required): Enter the medical provider's name.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required): Enter the patient's address.
* **Patient City** (`patient_city`, text, required): Enter the patient's city.
* **Patient State** (`patient_state`, text, required): Enter the patient's state.
* **Patient Zip** (`patient_zip`, text, required): Enter the patient's zip.
* **Patient Country** (`patient_country`, text, required): Enter the patient's country.
* **First Aid Treatment 3** (`first_aid_treatment_form_8`, select_one, required): Select the appropriate first aid treatment 3.
* **First Aid Treatment 4** (`first_aid_treatment_form_10`, select_multiple, required): Select the first aid treatment 4.
