# Blood Donor Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Blood Donor Consent Form is a comprehensive document that ensures blood donors understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the donation process. It covers various aspects of the donation, including donor information, consent to donate, procedure understanding, risks, benefits, and data usage.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read the form carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. Fill in your personal details, such as your full name, date of birth, contact email and phone number.
3. Indicate your consent to donate, including the type of donation (whole blood, platelets, or plasma).
4. Confirm that you understand the donation procedure and potential risks and benefits.
5. Provide your consent for blood testing and data usage.
6. Choose how you would like to be contacted with your test results (email, phone, mail, or patient portal).
7. Confirm that you consent to data being stored and used for research purposes.
8. Indicate whether you are willing to be contacted for future donations.
9. Confirm that this donation is voluntary.
10. Answer any questions that have been asked.
11. Sign the form to confirm your consent.
12. Have a staff member witness your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name** (Donor Name, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Consent** (Date of Consent, date, required): Enter the date on which you are giving your consent to donate.
* **Donation Type Consent** (Donation Type Consent, select_one, required): Select the type of donation you are consenting to (whole blood, platelets only, plasma only, or any component needed).
* **Procedure Understanding** (Procedure Understanding, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the donation procedure.
* **Risks Understanding** (Risks Understanding, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the potential risks of donation.
* **Potential Risks** (Potential Risks, note, required): If you have any questions or concerns about potential risks, write them here.
* **Benefits Understanding** (Benefits Understanding, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the potential benefits of donation.
* **Health Screening Consent** (Health Screening Consent, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to a health screening.
* **Blood Testing Consent** (Blood Testing Consent, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to blood testing.
* **Tests Performed** (Tests Performed, note, required): List the types of tests that will be performed.
* **Results Notification** (Results Notification, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted with your test results (email, phone, mail, or patient portal).
* **Data Usage Consent** (Data Usage Consent, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to data usage for research purposes.
* **Future Contact Consent** (Future Contact Consent, select_one, required): Choose whether you are willing to be contacted for future donations.
* **Research Use Consent** (Research Use Consent, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to anonymized research use.
* **Voluntary Statement** (Voluntary Statement, select_one, required): Confirm that this donation is voluntary.
* **Questions Answered** (Questions Answered, select_one, required): Confirm that all questions have been answered.
* **Donor Signature** (Donor Signature, text, required): Enter your name as a signature.
* **Witness Name** (Witness Name, text, required): Enter the name of the staff member who witnessed your signature.
* **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes, text, required): Enter any additional comments or questions.
