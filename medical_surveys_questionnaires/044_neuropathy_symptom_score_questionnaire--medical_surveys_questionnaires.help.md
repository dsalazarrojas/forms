# Neuropathy Symptom Score Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to collect detailed information about neuropathy symptoms. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question truthfully based on your current symptoms.
2. Fill out every field marked as required.
3. For each section, provide as much detail as possible about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pain History** (`pain_history`, text, optional): Describe any pain experienced, including the location, severity, and duration.
* **Pain History 1** (`pain_history_1`, text, optional): Describe any additional pain experienced.
* **Burning Sensations History** (`burning_sensations_history`, text, optional): Describe any burning sensations experienced.
* **Pain** (`pain`, number, optional): Rate the intensity of pain on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is no pain and 10 is the worst pain imaginable.
* **Numbness** (`numbness`, number, optional): Rate the degree of numbness on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is no numbness and 10 is complete numbness.
* **Weakness** (`weakness`, number, optional): Rate the degree of weakness on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is no weakness and 10 is complete weakness.
* **Giddiness** (`giddiness`, number, optional): Rate the degree of giddiness on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is no giddiness and 10 is complete giddiness.
* **Giddiness 1** (`giddiness_1`, number, optional): Rate the degree of giddiness on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is no giddiness and 10 is complete giddiness.
* **Pain History 2** (`pain_history_2`, text, optional): Describe any additional pain experienced.

## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please describe your experience in as much detail as possible.
* Do not hesitate to ask for help if you need it.
