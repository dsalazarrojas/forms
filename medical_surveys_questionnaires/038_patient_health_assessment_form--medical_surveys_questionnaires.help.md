# Patient Health Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's medical history, including their personal details, medical conditions, and allergies. The purpose of this form is to gather essential data that will help healthcare professionals assess the patient's health status and provide effective care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
3. Review and submit the form to ensure that all the information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name. This field is for your identification purposes only.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name. This field is for your identification purposes only.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Please describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical conditions you have experienced.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): If you are taking any medications, please list them in this field.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, or allergies, please list them in this field.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): If you have any allergies, please list them in this field.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Please provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
