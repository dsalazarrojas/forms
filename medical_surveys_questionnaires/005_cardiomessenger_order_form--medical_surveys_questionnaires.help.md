# CardioMessenger Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CardioMessenger Order Form is designed to collect necessary information from clinicians and physicians for processing and shipping CardioMessenger devices. The goal is to gather accurate and complete data for efficient order management.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your **Physician Full Name** (field 2) to identify yourself.
2.  Enter the **Clinic or Practice Name** (field 3) where you are affiliated with.
3.  Provide the **Clinic Mailing Address** (field 4), including the street address, city, state, and zip code.
4.  Enter the **Clinic Contact Phone Number** (field 5) for efficient communication.
5.  If applicable, include the **Patient Reference ID** (field 7) for your internal records.
6.  Select the **Device Model Requested** (field 8) from the available options (CardioMessenger Smart, CardioMessenger II, or CardioMessenger Pro).
7.  Enter the **Quantity Ordered** (field 9) accurately.
8.  Choose the preferred **Shipping Method** (field 10) from the available options (Standard Ground, Expedited, or Overnight Priority).
9.  If billing directly, provide the **Insurance Provider Information** (field 13) with the carrier name and group number.
10.  Enter the **Billing Contact Email** (field 12) for billing purposes.
11.  Optionally, add **Additional Instructions or Comments** (field 14) for specific delivery requests.
12.  Confirm your order by selecting **I authorize the order of the specified medical devices** (field 15).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Physician Full Name** (field 2): A required field to identify the physician filling out the form.
*   **Clinic or Practice Name** (field 3): Essential for billing and shipping purposes.
*   **Clinic Mailing Address** (field 4): Include the full address for delivery and billing purposes.
*   **Clinic Contact Phone Number** (field 5): A required field for efficient communication.
*   **Patient Reference ID** (field 7): Optional, but helpful for internal records.
*   **Device Model Requested** (field 8): Choose from available options for the specific device needed.
*   **Quantity Ordered** (field 9): Enter the number of devices ordered accurately.
*   **Shipping Method Preference** (field 10): Select from available options for delivery speed.
*   **Insurance Provider Information** (field 13): If billing directly, provide the carrier name and group number.
*   **Billing Contact Email** (field 12): Required for billing purposes.
*   **Additional Instructions or Comments** (field 14): Optional, but helpful for specific delivery requests.
*   **I authorize the order of the specified medical devices** (field 15): Confirm the order with this selection.
