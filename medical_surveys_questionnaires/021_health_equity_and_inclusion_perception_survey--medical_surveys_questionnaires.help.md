# Health Equity And Inclusion Perception Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health Equity And Inclusion Perception Survey is designed to collect feedback from patients on their experiences with healthcare services, focusing on various aspects of equity and inclusion.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and provide your honest thoughts and opinions.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as you are comfortable sharing.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clinic Experience** (Clinic Experience, text, required/optional): Please share your thoughts on your overall experience at the clinic.
* **Provider Interaction** (Provider Interaction, text, required/optional): Describe your interactions with healthcare providers, including any positive or negative experiences.
* **Staff Diversity** (Staff Diversity, text, required/optional): Share your observations on the diversity of the clinic staff, including any notable characteristics or differences you've noticed.
* **Facility Environment** (Facility Environment, text, required/optional): Describe your surroundings during your visits to the clinic, including any notable features or concerns.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (Communication Effectiveness, text, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction with the communication style and information provided by healthcare providers and staff.
* **Staff Training** (Staff Training, text, required/optional): Share your thoughts on the staff's level of training and expertise in providing patient-centered care.
* **Patient Involvement** (Patient Involvement, text, required/optional): Describe how involved you feel in your care decisions and the extent to which your opinions and values are taken into account.
* **Patient Care** (Patient Care, text, required/optional): Share your overall satisfaction with the care provided by the clinic, including any notable strengths or weaknesses.
* **Provider Culture** (Provider Culture, text, required/optional): Describe the values and behaviors of the healthcare providers you've interacted with, including any notable characteristics or concerns.

## Tips

* Be honest and open in your responses.
* Provide specific examples whenever possible.
* If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "Additional Comments" field.
