# Microdermabrasion Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients before performing microdermabrasion treatment. It is designed to ensure a safe and effective treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the client information section with the client's details.
2. Answer the medical history questions truthfully.
3. Provide any necessary information about allergies, medical conditions, and medications.
4. If necessary, provide additional medical history information.
5. Provide your contact information.
6. Select the appointment date and time for the treatment.
7. Provide the client's statement and your practitioner statement (if required).
8. Sign the form to acknowledge the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Surveys & Questionnaires (1)**: This is an optional section to gather additional medical survey or questionnaire information, if necessary.
* **Microdermabrasion Practice (2)**: Briefly describe your experience with microdermabrasion treatment. This can include the type of equipment used, the number of treatments performed, and your level of experience.
* **Client Information (3)**: This section is for providing the client's basic contact information, including phone number and address. It will help us stay in touch with the client.
* **Medical History (4)**: Indicate if the client has any medical history, such as previous surgeries, medical conditions, or allergies.
* **Allergies (5)**: Check if the client has any allergies that may affect the treatment.
* **Medical Conditions (6)**: Check if the client has any medical conditions that may affect the treatment.
* **Medications (7)**: List any medications the client is currently taking that may affect the treatment.
* **Medical History Additional (8)**: Provide any additional medical history information that may be relevant to the treatment.
* **Client Name (9)**: Provide the client's full name.
* **Date of Birth (10)**: Enter the client's date of birth.
* **Contact Details (11)**: This section is for providing the client's contact information, including email.
* **Email (12)**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone (13)**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Address (14)**: Enter the client's address.
* **Medical Specialist (15)**: This section is for providing information about the medical specialist involved in the treatment.
* **Appointment Date (16)**: Select the date of the appointment.
* **Appointment Time (17)**: Select the time of the appointment.
* **Next Appointment (18)**: Select the date for the next appointment.
* **Medical Notes (19)**: Provide any notes about the client's medical conditions or treatment.
* **Signature (20)**: Sign the form to acknowledge the agreement.
* **Signature Date (21)**: Enter the date of the signature.
* **Signature Time (22)**: Enter the time of the signature.
* **Client Statement (23)**: This section is for the client to provide a statement.
* **Practitioner Statement (24)**: This section is for the practitioner to provide a statement.
* **Consent (25)**: Check if the client agrees to the treatment and terms of service.
