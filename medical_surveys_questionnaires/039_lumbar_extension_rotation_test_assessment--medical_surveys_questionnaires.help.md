# Lumbar Extension Rotation Test Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to aid healthcare professionals in assessing a patient's lumbar extension rotation test results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient's basic information (patient ID, name, date of birth, etc.)
2. Provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history.
3. Record any notable clinical findings related to the patient's lumbar region.
4. Describe the patient's lumbar range of motion.
5. Perform the lumbar extension rotation test and record the results.
6. Summarize the assessment of the patient's lumbar spine.
7. Provide a treatment plan for the patient's lumbar condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_id`, `name`, `date_of_birth`): Enter the patient's unique identifier, name, and date of birth.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Briefly describe the patient's medical history, including any conditions or treatments relevant to their lumbar region.
* **Clinical Findings** (`clinical_findings`): Record any notable clinical findings related to the patient's lumbar region, such as pain, stiffness, or limited mobility.
* **Lumbar Range of Motion** (`lumbar_range_of_motion`): Describe the patient's lumbar range of motion, including any limitations or restrictions.
* **Lumbar Extension Rotation Test** (`lumbar_extension_rotation_test`): Perform the lumbar extension rotation test and record the results, including any limitations or restrictions.
* **Assessment** (`assessment`): Summarize the assessment of the patient's lumbar spine, including any findings, diagnoses, or recommendations.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`): Provide a treatment plan for the patient's lumbar condition, including any recommended exercises, stretches, or medical interventions.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have a good understanding of the lumbar extension rotation test and its implications before completing this form.
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when recording the patient's information and test results.
* Consult with a qualified healthcare professional if you are unsure about any aspect of this form or the lumbar extension rotation test.
