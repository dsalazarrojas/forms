# Chronic Illness Social Support Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Chronic Illness Social Support Survey is a collection of questions designed to gather information about your social support network and experiences with emotional and practical support. This survey will help healthcare professionals better understand your needs and provide more effective support and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. For select-one and select_multiple questions, choose the answer that best describes your experience.
3. For select_one questions with "Very Satisfied" and "Very Dissatisfied" options, these refer to extreme levels of satisfaction or dissatisfaction.
4. For the "What types of support do you wish you had more of?" question, select all that apply.
5. If you are not willing to participate in a follow-up interview, please answer "False" to the "Would you be willing to participate in a follow-up interview?" question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Age**: Please enter your current age.
* **How long have you lived with your primary chronic condition?**: Please select the time frame that best describes your experience with your condition.
* **Who is your primary source of day-to-day support?**: Please select the person or group that provides you with the most support.
* **Rate satisfaction with EMOTIONAL support received**: Please rate how satisfied you are with the emotional support you receive.
* **Rate satisfaction with PRACTICAL support received**: Please rate how satisfied you are with the practical support you receive.
* **How often do you interact with people who support you?**: Please select the frequency of your interactions with your support network.
* **How often do you feel socially isolated due to your illness?**: Please select the frequency of your feelings of social isolation.
* **What types of support do you wish you had more of?**: Please select all that apply to the types of support you wish you had more of.
* **What are the main barriers to receiving support?**: Please select all that apply to the barriers you face in receiving support.
* **How helpful are online support groups/communities for you?**: Please select how helpful you find online support groups and communities.
* **Is there anything else you want to share about your support needs?**: Please provide any additional comments or thoughts about your support needs.
* **Would you be willing to participate in a follow-up interview?**: Please answer "True" if you are willing to participate in a follow-up interview and "False" otherwise.
* **Email Address (if willing to follow up)**: If you are willing to participate in a follow-up interview, please provide your email address to be contacted.
