# Implanted Stimulator MRI Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to walk you through the Implanted Stimulator MRI Checklist form. This form is used to gather information regarding your implanted stimulator devices, which will be used to help in the evaluation and treatment of your condition.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through the form carefully and fill out each section accurately and completely.
- If a section does not apply to you, simply leave it blank.
- Ensure that each section is filled out only once, and not duplicated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details** (`patient_details`, `text`, required: false)
  This section is for entering your personal details. Please enter your name, and any other identifying information that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Implanted Stimulator Info** (`implanted_stimulator_info`, `text`, required: false)
  This section is for entering information about your implanted stimulator, such as its location and model. This will help in the evaluation and treatment of your condition.
* **Scan Details** (`scan_details`, `text`, required: false)
  This section is for entering details about your MRI scan, such as the date and time it was performed.
* **Patient Authorization** (`patient_authorization`, `text`, required: false)
  This section is for obtaining authorization from the patient to proceed with your treatment.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required: false)
  This section is for entering your name, which will be used for identification purposes.
* **Implanted Stimulator Type** (`implanted_stimulator_type`, `select_one`, required: false)
  This section is for selecting whether you have an implanted stimulator or not.
* **MR Number** (`mr_number`, `text`, required: false)
  This section is for entering your MR number, which will be used to identify your scan.
* **Scan Date** (`scan_date`, `date`, required: false)
  This section is for entering the date of your MRI scan.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false)
  This section is for entering the time of your MRI scan.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, `number`, required: false)
  This section is for entering your age, which will be used in the evaluation and treatment of your condition.
* **Implanted Stimulator Location** (`implanted_stimulator_location`, `text`, required: false)
  This section is for entering the location of your implanted stimulator.
* **Implanted Stimulator Model** (`implanted_stimulator_model`, `text`, required: false)
  This section is for entering the model of your implanted stimulator.
* **MR Date** (`mr_date`, `date`, required: false)
  This section is for entering the date of your MRI scan.
* **Radiologist Name** (`radiologist_name`, `text`, required: false)
  This section is for entering the name of the radiologist who performed your MRI scan.
* **Radiologist License** (`radiologist_license`, `text`, required: false)
  This section is for entering the license of the radiologist who performed your MRI scan.
* **Radiologist License Number** (`radiologist_license_number`, `text`, required: false)
  This section is for entering the license number of the radiologist who performed your MRI scan.
* **Other Stimulators** (`other_stimulators`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
  This section is for selecting whether you have other stimulators or not.
* **Other Stimulator Info** (`other_stimulator_info`, `text`, required: false)
  This section is for entering information about your other stimulators.
* **Other Stimulator Type** (`other_stimulator_type`, `select_one`, required: false)
  This section is for selecting the type of other stimulator you have.
* **Other Stimulator Location** (`other_stimulator_location`, `text`, required: false)
  This section is for entering the location of your other stimulator.
* **Other Stimulator Model** (`other_stimulator_model`, `text`, required: false)
  This section is for entering the model of your other stimulator.
* **Implanted Stimulator Condition** (`implanted_stimulator_condition`, `select_one`, required: false)
  This section is for selecting the condition of your implanted stimulator.
* **Implanted Stimulator Status** (`implanted_stimulator_status`, `select_one`, required: false)
  This section is for selecting the status of your implanted stimulator.
* **Implanted Stimulator Comment** (`implanted_stimulator_comment`, `text`, required: false)
  This section is for entering any additional comments about your implanted stimulator.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the radiologist or your healthcare provider.
- Ensure that you fill out each section accurately and completely.
- If a section does not apply to you, simply leave it blank.
