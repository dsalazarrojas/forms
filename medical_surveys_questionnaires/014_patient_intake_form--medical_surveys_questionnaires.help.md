# Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Intake Form is used to collect essential information about a patient during the initial consultation or intake process.

## How To Complete This Form
*   Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about the patient.
*   Ensure you do not duplicate information from existing records.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the patient or the patient's authorized representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **patient_name** (`patient_name`, text, required): This field is used to collect the patient's full name.
*   **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, required): This field is used to collect the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
*   **medical_record_number** (`medical_record_number`, text, required): This field is used to collect the patient's medical record number, if applicable.
*   **patient_address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the patient's address, if applicable.
*   **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): This field is used to collect the patient's date of birth.
*   **medical_history** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the patient's medical history, if applicable.

## Tips
*   Please review the form carefully and ensure you have filled in all required fields.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please consult with the patient or the patient's authorized representative.
