# neuro_rehabilitation_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a patient's rehabilitation progress and goals, enabling healthcare professionals to assess their overall well-being and provide personalized treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the patient from the list.
2. Fill out the form information, including medical history, rehabilitation goals, physical therapy, occupational therapy, cognitive therapy, recreational activities, and social interactions.
3. Rate your overall progress and select the frequency of progress tracking.
4. Set specific goals for your rehabilitation.
5. Rate your emotions, pain levels, or energy levels in the self-assessment section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Information**: This is the introductory page of the form, where you can review the form's purpose and instructions.
* **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's name, ID, or any other relevant details.
* **Patient Info 2**: Select a patient from the list provided.
* **Medical History**: Enter any significant medical history or conditions that affect your rehabilitation.
* **Rehabilitation Goals**: Enter your specific goals for rehabilitation, such as regaining mobility or improving cognitive function.
* **Physical Therapy**: Enter details about your physical therapy sessions, including dates, times, and outcomes.
* **Occupational Therapy**: Enter details about your occupational therapy sessions, including dates, times, and outcomes.
* **Cognitive Therapy**: Enter details about your cognitive therapy sessions, including dates, times, and outcomes.
* **Recreational Activities**: Enter details about your recreational activities, such as hobbies or sports, that aid in your rehabilitation.
* **Social Interactions**: Enter details about your social interactions, including family support or group therapy sessions.
* **Neurological Status**: Enter your current neurological status, including any symptoms or conditions affecting your rehabilitation.
* **Overall Progress**: Rate your overall progress on a scale, indicating how well you're doing in your rehabilitation.
* **Progress Tracking**: Select the frequency of progress tracking, such as weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.
* **Goal Setting**: Enter specific goals for your rehabilitation, such as achieving a certain level of mobility or improving cognitive function.
* **Self-Assessment**: Rate your emotions, pain levels, or energy levels on a scale, indicating how you're feeling during your rehabilitation.
