# pediatric_croup_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pediatric croup assessment form is a tool for collecting information for the diagnosis and treatment of pediatric croup. This form is designed to gather relevant data on patient medical history, respiratory findings, and other related factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with as much detail as possible. Start by filling out the Patient Info section with the patient's basic contact information and medical history. Then, move on to the Medical History section to provide any relevant medical history. 

Next, fill out the Respiratory Findings section to report any respiratory symptoms or conditions. This includes information about the patient's respiratory system such as coughing, wheezing, or other respiratory issues. 

After that, fill out the Assessment Data section to provide any other relevant medical data. This may include lab results, medications, or other medical findings.

Finally, complete the Treatment, Medications, and Allergy List sections to provide information about any medications the patient is currently taking, any allergies the patient has, and any treatments or therapies the patient may be undergoing.
