# Healthcare Professional Citizenship Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the Healthcare Professional Citizenship Survey form, which aims to assess your involvement and commitment to quality improvement and healthcare leadership development in your professional role. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to help us understand your current practices and future plans.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and select the best response for each section.
2. Complete each section in order from 1 to 6.
3. Fill in any additional information as required in the text fields.
4. Submit your completed form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Healthcare Professional Citizenship Survey Form** (`healthcare_professional_citizenship_survey_form_section`): This is the main form section where you'll provide information on your current practices and plans.
- **Which of the following activities does your organization currently support?** (`healthcare_professional_citizenship_survey_form_section_1`): This question lists activities related to healthcare leadership development and quality improvement. Select all the activities your organization currently supports.
	+ Options: Healthcare leadership development, Continuing medical education, Quality improvement, Safety, Other (please specify)
- **Other (please specify)** (`healthcare_professional_citizenship_survey_form_section_1_1`): If your organization supports an activity not listed above, provide a brief description in this text field.
- **Healthcare Professional Citizenship Survey Form Section 1 1 1** (`healthcare_professional_citizenship_survey_form_section_1_1_1`): This section is currently unused. Please skip it.
- **How often do you participate in the following activities?** (`healthcare_professional_citizenship_survey_form_section_2`): This question assesses your participation frequency in various activities.
	+ Options: Never, Less than once a month, About once a month, Several times a month, Every 2 weeks, Every week, More than once a week, Several times a week, Daily
- **Quality improvement** (`healthcare_professional_citizenship_survey_form_section_3`): This question asks about your level of involvement in quality improvement activities.
	+ Options: Participate in quality improvement projects, Lead quality improvement projects, Develop quality improvement tools, Other (please specify)
- **Healthcare Professional Citizenship Survey Form Section 3 1** (`healthcare_professional_citizenship_survey_form_section_3_1`): This section is currently unused. Please skip it.
- **How likely is it that the following activities will happen in the next 6 months?** (`healthcare_professional_citizenship_survey_form_section_4`): This question assesses the likelihood of various activities happening in the next 6 months.
	+ Options: Not Likely, Slightly Likely, Moderately Likely, Very Likely, Extremely Likely
- **Healthcare Professional Citizenship Survey Form Section 4 1** (`healthcare_professional_citizenship_survey_form_section_4_1`): This section is currently unused. Please skip it.
- **How many hours do you spend on community activities in a week?** (`healthcare_professional_citizenship_survey_form_section_5`): This question asks about your time commitment to community activities.
	+ Options: [Enter a numerical value]
- **What do you think is the impact of quality improvement on your workplace?** (`healthcare_professional_citizenship_survey_form_section_6`): Provide a brief description of your thoughts on the impact of quality improvement on your workplace in this text field.
