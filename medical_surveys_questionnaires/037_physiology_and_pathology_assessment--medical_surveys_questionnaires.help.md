# Physiology And Pathology Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document information about a patient's physiological and pathological assessments, which is crucial for healthcare professionals to make informed decisions about patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's unique identifier (Patient Id) in the corresponding field.
2. Describe the patient's medical history in the text field (Medical History).
3. List the patient's symptoms in the text field (Symptoms).
4. Enter any relevant medical records or information in the text field (Medical Record).
5. Answer the question about whether a comprehensive assessment form was completed (Assessment Form).
6. Answer the question about whether a second assessment form was completed (Assessment Form 2).
7. Enter the patient's date of birth in the date field (Date of Birth).
8. Enter the date of the visit in the date field (Date of Visit).
9. Enter the time of the visit in the time field (Time of Visit).
10. Add any additional notes or comments in the text field (Note).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Id** (1, number, required: false): A unique identifier for the patient.
* **Medical History** (2, text, required: false): A description of the patient's medical history.
* **Symptoms** (3, text, required: false): A list of the patient's symptoms.
* **Medical Record** (4, text, required: false): Any relevant medical records or information.
* **Assessment Form** (5, select_one, required: false): Answer whether a comprehensive assessment form was completed.
* **Assessment Form 2** (6, select_multiple, required: false): Answer whether a second assessment form was completed.
* **Date of Birth** (7, date, required: false): The patient's date of birth.
* **Date of Visit** (8, date, required: false): The date of the visit.
* **Time of Visit** (9, time, required: false): The time of the visit.
* **Note** (10, text, required: false): Any additional notes or comments.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check the patient's information for consistency and correctness.
* Use this form for its intended purpose only.
