# Nicotine Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an individual's nicotine usage habits, survey questions, and relevant details about their smoking history.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Enter your responses in the spaces provided.
3. Ensure that you fill out all required fields, if any.
4. Once you have completed the form, submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: Enter your personal details in this field. Please provide your full name, email address, and any other relevant information as prompted.
* **Nicotine Use Habits**: Describe your nicotine use habits, including how often you use it, how much you use it, and any specific circumstances surrounding your use.
* **Survey Questions**: Select the questions that apply to your situation from the list provided.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Smoker Status**: Indicate whether you have ever used nicotine by selecting 'True', 'False', or 'Never Tried'.
* **Years Smoking**: Enter the number of years you have been using nicotine.
* **Quit Reason**: Provide a reason for why you quit using nicotine.
* **Quit Year**: Enter the year you quit using nicotine.
* **Relapse Frequency**: Indicate whether you have relapsed by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
* **Relapse Frequency Details**: Provide any additional details about your relapse experiences.
* **Quit Support**: Select any support systems you used to quit nicotine, such as family, friends, or a support group.
* **Quit Methods**: Select any methods you used to quit nicotine, such as counseling, medication, or a support group.
* **Quit Success**: Indicate whether you were successful in quitting nicotine by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
* **Quit Success Reason**: Provide a reason for why you were successful in quitting nicotine.
* **Relapse Success**: Indicate whether you have relapsed again by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
* **Relapse Success Reason**: Provide a reason for why you were able to relapse.
* **Medical Conditions**: Select any medical conditions you experience that may be related to nicotine use.
* **Quit Date**: Enter the date you quit using nicotine.
* **Smoker Status 2**: Indicate whether you have ever used nicotine by selecting 'True', 'False', or 'Never Tried'.
* **Years Smoking 2**: Enter the number of years you have been using nicotine (for those who are currently or have previously used).
* **Quit Reason 2**: Provide a reason for why you quit using nicotine the second time.
* **Quit Year 2**: Enter the year you quit using nicotine the second time.
* **Relapse Frequency 2**: Indicate whether you have relapsed by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
* **Relapse Frequency Details 2**: Provide any additional details about your second relapse experiences.
* **Quit Support 2**: Select any support systems you used to quit nicotine the second time.
* **Quit Methods 2**: Select any methods you used to quit nicotine the second time.
* **Smoker Status 2**: Indicate whether you have ever used nicotine by selecting 'True', 'False', or 'Never Tried'. 

(Note: Please ensure that you fill out all fields accurately and truthfully, as this information will be used to help understand nicotine usage habits.)
