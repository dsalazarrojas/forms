# Medical Cannabis Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for patients to share their medical details, contact information, medical history, and consents for medical cannabis treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review each page and fill out the required information accurately and completely.
2. Please answer each question carefully and honestly.
3. If you need help or have any questions, do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Patient Details** (`patient_details`)
  * **Patient Name**, **type: text**, required: false**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. **Contact Information** (`contact_info`)
  * **Contact Information, **type: text**, required: false**: Please enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.

### 3. **Medical History** (`medical_history`)
  * **Medical History, **type: text**, required: false**: Please provide a brief description of your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or treatments.

### 4. **Medical History Context** (`medical_history_context`)
  * **Medical History Context, **type: text**, required: false**: Please provide additional context about your medical history, if applicable.

### 5. **Consents** (`medical_consents`)
  * **Consents, **type: text**, required: false**: Please read and agree to our consents.

### 6. **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`)
  * **Patient Signature, **type: text**, required: false**: Please sign this form to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.

### 7. **Medical Consent Date** (`medical_consent_date`)
  * **Medical Consent Date, **type: date, required: false**: Please enter the date you have read and agreed to our consents.

### 8. **Medical Cannabis Use** (`medical_cannabis_use`)
  * **Medical Cannabis Use, **type: text, required: false**: Please indicate your reason for using medical cannabis.

## Tips
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
- Please review each section carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have already filled out a similar form from our organization, please do not fill out this form again.
