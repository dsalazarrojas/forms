# Perinatal And Postnatal Mental Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your mental health and well-being as a pregnant woman or new mother. The goal is to understand your concerns, experiences, and support systems related to mental health during and after pregnancy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and choose the most accurate answer for you.
2. Use the multiple-choice options when available.
3. Enter any additional information in the text fields as needed.
4. Submit the form when you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maternal mental health issues during pregnancy** (`maternal_mental_health_issues_during_pregnancy`, Text, Required): Please describe any mental health concerns or issues you experienced during your pregnancy.
* **Maternal mental health issues after pregnancy** (`maternal_mental_health_issues_after_pregnancy`, Text, Required): Please describe any mental health concerns or issues you experienced after your pregnancy.
* **Mental health history** (`mental_health_history`, Text, Required): Please describe any mental health concerns or issues you have experienced in the past.
* **Mental health history of family members** (`mental_health_history_of_family_members`, Text, Required): Please describe any mental health concerns or issues your family members have experienced.
* **Mental health history of childhood** (`mental_health_history_of_childhood`, Text, Required): Please describe any mental health concerns or issues you experienced during your childhood.
* **Maternal mental health support** (`maternal_mental_health_support`, Text, Required): Please describe any support systems you have in place for your mental health.
* **Postnatal support services** (`postnatal_support_services`, Select one, Required): Please select one of the support options:
	+ Support group
	+ Individual counseling
	+ Online support
* **Other postnatal support services** (`other_postnatal_support_services`, Text, Optional): If you have used other support services, please describe them.
* **Maternal support services** (`maternal_support_services`, Select multiple, Required): Please select all applicable support options:
	+ Support group
	+ Individual counseling
	+ Online support
	+ Peer support
* **Maternal support services other** (`maternal_support_services_other`, Text, Optional): If you have used other support services, please describe them.
* **Mental health resources** (`mental_health_resources`, Select multiple, Required): Please select all applicable resource options:
	+ Self-care guide
	+ Mental health provider list
	+ Online resources
	+ Hotline
* **Mental health resources other** (`mental_health_resources_other`, Text, Optional): If you have accessed other resources, please describe them.
* **Mental health resources use** (`mental_health_resources_use`, Select one, Required): Please select whether you have used mental health resources:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Maternal mental health concerns** (`maternal_mental_health_concerns`, Select multiple, Required): Please select all applicable concerns:
	+ Anxiety
	+ Depression
	+ Panic disorder
	+ Post-traumatic stress disorder
	+ Substance abuse
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Mental health concerns other** (`mental_health_concerns_other`, Text, Optional): If you have any other mental health concerns, please describe them.
* **Maternal antenatal pregnancy support** (`maternal_antenatal_pregnancy_support`, Select multiple, Required): Please select all applicable support options:
	+ Support group
	+ Individual counseling
	+ Online support
	+ Peer support
* **Maternal pregnancy support other** (`maternal_pregnancy_support_other`, Text, Optional): If you have used other support services, please describe them.
* **Mental health pregnancy support** (`mental_health_pregnancy_support`, Select one, Required): Please select whether you have mental health support during pregnancy:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Antenatal pregnancy support** (`antenatal_pregnancy_support`, Select multiple, Required): Please select all applicable support options:
