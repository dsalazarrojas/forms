# Cardiology ROI Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cardiology ROI Assessment form is designed to evaluate the return on investment (ROI) of a cardiology department or institution. This form helps in identifying the financial benefits and quality improvements achieved through various procedures and metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the name of the institution or clinic where the evaluation is being done.
2. Enter the name of the evaluator or assessor responsible for the assessment.
3. Specify the evaluation period (e.g., a fiscal year or quarter).
4. Enter the total number of procedures performed during the evaluation period.
5. Enter the average cost per procedure.
6. Calculate the outcome improvement percentage compared to standard clinical benchmarks.
7. Calculate the complication rate reduction percentage compared to baseline.
8. Select the level of staff productivity gain observed.
9. Estimate the total net financial savings or gain (USD).
10. Rate the value-based care alignment, where 1 represents poorly aligned and 5 represents perfectly aligned.
11. Provide a conclusion and any additional notes about the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Institutional Evaluation Information**: This section is a header for the evaluation information.
* **Institution or Clinic Name** (`institution_clinic_name`, required, Text): Enter the name of the institution or clinic where the evaluation is being done.
* **Evaluator/Assessor Name** (`evaluator_assessor_name`, required, Text): Enter the name of the person responsible for the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period_dates`, required, Text): Enter the time period for which the evaluation is being done (e.g., a fiscal year or quarter).
* **Total Procedure Volume** (`procedure_volume_total`, required, Number): Enter the total number of procedures done during the evaluation period.
* **Average Cost per Procedure** (`average_cost_per_procedure`, required, Number): Enter the average cost per procedure, including materials and labor.
* **Outcome Improvement Percentage** (`outcome_improvement_percent`, required, Number): Enter the improvement in outcomes measured by standard clinical benchmarks.
* **Complication Rate Reduction** (`complication_rate_reduction`, required, Number): Enter the reduction in complications compared to baseline.
* **Staff Productivity Gain** (`staff_productivity_gain_rating`, required, Select): Choose the level of staff productivity gain observed, which can be:
	+ Significant Gain (>20%)
	+ Moderate Gain (10-20%)
	+ Slight Gain (0-10%)
	+ No Change
* **Estimated Financial ROI** (`financial_roi_estimate`, required, Number): Enter the total net financial savings or gain (USD) resulting from the evaluation period.
* **Value-Based Care Alignment** (`value_based_care_alignment`, required, Number): Rate the value-based care alignment, where 1 represents poorly aligned and 5 represents perfectly aligned.
* **Assessment Conclusion and Notes** (`assessment_conclusion_notes`, optional, Text): Enter a summary of the ROI analysis.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use standard clinical benchmarks for measuring outcome improvement.
* Be realistic when estimating financial ROI, considering all costs and gains.
* Align your care with value-based care principles for optimal results.
* Document your assessment conclusions and notes for future reference.
