# At Home Vaccination Outreach Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals for at-home vaccination purposes. It helps healthcare providers assess individual needs and preferences for vaccination services. Please take a few minutes to complete this form accurately to ensure your data is up-to-date and relevant.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you answer all questions correctly.
3. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1

* **First Name** (`respondent_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`respondent_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your birth date in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age range.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.

### Page 2

* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, required): Enter your full address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city you reside in.
* **State/Province** (`state_province`, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter your country of residence.

### Page 3

* **Current Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, required): Select your current vaccination status.
* **Interested In Getting Vaccinated For** (`interested_vaccines`, select_multiple, required): Select all vaccines you are interested in getting vaccinated for.
* **Vaccine Hesitation Concerns** (`vaccine_hesitation`, select_multiple, false): Select any vaccine hesitation concerns.
* **Current Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, false): Enter any health conditions you have.
* **Known Allergies** (`allergies`, text, false): Enter any medication or ingredient allergies.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, false): Enter any medications you are taking.

### Page 4

* **Pregnancy Status** (`pregnancy_status`, select_one, required): If applicable, select your pregnancy status.
* **Current Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, false): Enter your doctor or clinic name.
* **Insurance Status** (`insurance_status`, select_one, required): Select your insurance status.
* **Preferred Vaccination Location** (`preferred_vaccination_location`, select_one, required): Select where you prefer to get vaccinated.
* **Language Preference** (`language_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred language for communication.
* **How Did You Hear About This Vaccination Outreach** (`vaccination_information_source`, select_one, required): Select the source of your information about this vaccination outreach.
* **Additional Questions or Information** (`additional_information`, text, false): Enter any other relevant details.

### Page 5

* **Form Submission Date** (`form_submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you completed this form.

Note: This form is designed for internal use and will not be shared with anyone without your consent. All answers are kept confidential and for statistical purposes only. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
