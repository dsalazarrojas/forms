# Preventive Care Outcome Measurement Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preventive Care Outcome Measurement Evaluation Form is designed to collect information about the patient's medical history, health status, and other relevant details in order to evaluate the outcomes of preventive care. This form is used to assess the patient's overall health and make data-driven decisions for better care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand the questions and options being asked.
2. Answer each question accurately and completely, using the options provided.
3. For the Medical History page, provide a brief description of your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, treatments, or allergies.
4. On the Health Status page, select the option that best describes your current health status.
5. On the Preventive Care Outcome Measurement page, select all options that apply to your current situation.
6. For the Date and Time pages, enter the date and time of your last medical visit or appointment.
7. On the Email and Phone pages, enter your contact information so that we can follow up with you about your care.
8. Finally, enter any additional notes or comments in the Note page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Medical History** (`preventive_care_outcome_measurement_form_page_1`, `text`, `false`): This page is where you can provide a brief description of your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, treatments, or allergies.
* **2. Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, `false`): This page is also about your medical history, and you can enter any additional information that is not included in the previous page.
* **3. Health Status** (`health_status`, `select_one`, `false`): Select the option that best describes your current health status from the options provided.
* **4. Preventive Care Outcome Measurement** (`preventive_care_outcome_measurement`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Select all options that apply to your current situation to help us evaluate the outcomes of preventive care.
* **5. Date** (`date`, `date`, `false`): Enter the date of your last medical visit or appointment.
* **6. Time** (`time`, `time`, `false`): Enter the time of your last medical visit or appointment.
* **7. Email** (`email`, `email`, `false`): Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you about your care.
* **8. Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `false`): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you about your care.
* **9. Note** (`note`, `note`, `false`): Enter any additional notes or comments about your care.
* **10. Note** ( duplicate of the previous field, ignore)

Please note that this form is not intended for clinical or medical use. The information collected is for internal use only and will be used to evaluate the outcomes of preventive care. 

Tips

* Make sure to answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* The information collected will be kept confidential.
* Please note that this form may not be completed in one sitting, you can save and return to it later.
