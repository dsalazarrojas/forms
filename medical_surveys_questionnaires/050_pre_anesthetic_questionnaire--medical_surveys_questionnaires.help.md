# pre_anesthetic_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The pre-anesthetic questionnaire is designed to collect relevant medical information from the patient before undergoing medical or surgical procedures. This form helps the healthcare team to assess the patient's medical history, medications, allergies, and other relevant factors to ensure their safety and well-being during the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information for each section, and ensure that all fields are filled in as accurately as possible.
2. Review each section carefully before proceeding to the next one.
3. If you are unsure about any information, please ask a healthcare professional for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information in this section.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required: false): Describe the patient's current medical condition or any relevant medical history.
* **Anesthesia History** (`anesthesia_history`, `text`, required: false): Record the patient's history of previous anesthesia use, including any adverse reactions or complications.
* **Allergy Info** (`allergy_info`, `text`, required: false): List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
* **Medical/Surgical Procedure** (`medical_surgical_procedure`, `text`, required: false): Describe the upcoming medical or surgical procedure the patient will undergo.
* **Medications Currently Taking** (`medications_currently_taking`, `text`, required: false): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical Devices** (`medical_devices`, `text`, required: false): Describe any medical devices the patient has, such as pacemakers or oxygen tanks.
* **Anesthesia Level** (`anesthesia_level`, `number`, required: false): Rate the patient's anesthesia level, using a scale of 1-10.
* **Surgical Site** (`surgical_site`, `text`, required: false): Identify the location of the surgical site, if applicable.
* **Patient History** (`patient_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous surgeries or illnesses.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required: false): List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions or treatments.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, `time`, required: false): Record the patient's vital signs, such as blood pressure, heart rate, or oxygen saturation.
* **Medical/Surgical History** (`medical_surgical_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe the patient's medical or surgical history, including any previous complications or issues.
* **Medications Surgical History** (`medications_surgical_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe any medications or medical devices used during previous medical or surgical procedures.
* **Medical Devices Surgical** (`medical_devices_surgical`, `text`, required: false): Describe any medical devices used during previous medical or surgical procedures.
* **Medical/Surgical History 2** (`medical_surgical_history_2`, `text`, required: false): Describe any additional medical or surgical history, if applicable.

Note that all fields are optional, but it is recommended that the patient provides as much information as possible to ensure the healthcare team can provide the best care possible.
