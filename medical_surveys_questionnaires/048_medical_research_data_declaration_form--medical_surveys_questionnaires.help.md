# Medical Research Data Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Research Data Declaration Form is a tool used to collect medical information from participants in a research study. This form helps researchers gather essential data about the participant's medical history, contact details, medical conditions, medical specialties, medications, allergies, emergency contacts, health insurance, and medical record number. This information is crucial for the researchers to understand the participant's health status and ensure accurate data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions provided for each field carefully.
2. Provide accurate and detailed information for each field as requested.
3. Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
4. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information
**Participant Information (ID: 1)** (`Participant Information`, text, required: false)
This field is where you will enter your name and any other identifying information about yourself as a participant.

### Contact Details
**Contact Details (ID: 2)** (`Contact Details`, text, required: false)
Please enter your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email.

### Medical History
**Medical History (ID: 3)** (`Medical History`, text, required: false)
Provide a detailed description of your medical history, including any past medical conditions or treatments you have received.

### Medical Condition
**Medical Condition (ID: 4)** (`Medical Condition`, text, required: false)
Describe any medical conditions you currently have or have had in the past.

### Medical Specialty
**Medical Specialty (ID: 5)** (`Medical Specialty`, select_one, required: false)
Select the medical specialty that best describes your current medical condition.

*   Cardiovascular specialist
*   Neurologist
*   Pulmonologist

### Medications
**Medications (ID: 6)** (`Medications`, note, required: false)
List the name of any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.

### Allergies
**Allergies (ID: 7)** (`Allergies`, text, required: false)
Describe any allergies you have.

### Emergency Contacts
**Emergency Contacts (ID: 8)** (`Emergency Contacts`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select 'Yes' if you have emergency contacts who should be notified in case of an emergency, or 'No' otherwise.

### Health Insurance
**Health Insurance (ID: 9)** (`Health Insurance`, select_one, required: false)
Select your current health insurance status.

*   Private
*   Public
*   None

### Medical Record Number
**Medical Record Number (ID: 10)** (`Medical Record Number`, text, required: false)
Please enter your medical record number if you have one.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
*   Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact your research coordinator for clarification.
