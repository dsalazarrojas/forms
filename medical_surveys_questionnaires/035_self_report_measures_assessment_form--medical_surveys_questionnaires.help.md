# Self Report Measures Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Self Report Measures Assessment Form is a survey that gathers information to help you better understand your research, project, or program's needs and goals. It's used for various purposes, such as evaluating performance, monitoring progress, or collecting feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields to provide essential details about your survey.
2. Use the provided hints to help guide you through each field.
3. Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the purpose of this survey?**: Describe the main goal of your survey. What information do you want to collect? This field is essential for understanding the focus of your survey.
* **Who is the author of this survey?**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for creating this survey. This information helps identify the creator and contact them if needed.
* **What is the name of your survey?**: Provide a unique and descriptive name for your survey. This name will help you and others identify it.
* **What is a brief description of your survey?**: Give a short summary of your survey's purpose, including any relevant context or background information.
* **How often will this survey be administered?**: Select how frequently you plan to administer this survey, whether Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
* **How long will this survey take to complete?**: Estimate the time required to complete this survey.
* **What instruments will you use to administer this survey?**: List the tools or methods you'll use to collect data.
* **What is the scope of this survey?**: Describe the range of topics or areas covered in your survey.
* **What version is this survey?**: If this is a revised version, indicate the version number.
* **When is this survey due? (Date)**: Enter the date by which you want the survey to be completed.
* **At what time will this survey be due?**: Enter the time by which you want the survey to be completed.
* **Please provide more details about the purpose of this survey**: Explain the survey's purpose in more detail.
* **Please provide more details about the author of this survey**: Add any additional information about the author of this survey.
* **Please provide more details about the instruments used in this survey**: Elaborate on the tools or methods used to collect data.
* **Please provide more details about the scope of this survey**: Describe the range of topics or areas covered in more detail.

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough when filling out this form.
* Double-check that all required fields are filled out.
* Make sure to save your progress or submit the form as a draft if you need to stop and continue later.
