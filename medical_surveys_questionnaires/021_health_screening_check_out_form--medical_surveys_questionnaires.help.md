# Health Screening Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential medical information from patients to facilitate a quick and accurate health assessment. It is meant for patients who are being discharged from a medical facility or for general health screening purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your medical information is up-to-date and reliable.
2. Ensure that you have not previously completed this form or have been directed to do so by a medical professional.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please consult a medical professional for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): This field is for your full name as recorded in our medical records.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): This field is for your date of birth.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Describe your medical history, including any pre-existing medical conditions, surgeries, or treatments.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies you have, including medications, food items, or environmental factors.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, required): Describe your family history, including any known medical conditions or genetic predispositions that may affect your health.
* **Social History** (`social_history`, text, optional): Share any relevant social history, such as substance abuse, mental health conditions, or significant life events.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, text, required): Record your current vital signs, including blood pressure, temperature, pulse, and any other relevant measurements.
* **Medical Status** (`medical_status`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options to describe your current medical status:
	+ Normal: You are in good health with no known medical conditions.
	+ Stable: You have a stable condition, but may experience minor symptoms.
	+ Serious: You are experiencing significant medical symptoms or complications.
	+ Critical: You are in a critical condition requiring immediate medical attention.

## Tips
- Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure reliable medical information.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult a medical professional for guidance.
- This form is intended for general health screening purposes and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical care.
