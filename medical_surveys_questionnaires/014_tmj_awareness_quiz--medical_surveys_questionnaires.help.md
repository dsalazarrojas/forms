# TMJ Awareness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess your level of awareness about Temporomandibular Joint (TMJ) related issues, including symptoms and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question or field carefully and answer accordingly.
2. For question 5 (Diagnosis), select all applicable answers that match your TMJ experiences.
3. For questions 6-8, select the answer that best describes your experience or condition.
4. For question 9 (Do you experience any other symptoms?), feel free to provide any additional information that may be relevant.
5. Finally, write your conclusion in question 10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Diagnosis** (`id: 5`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: true`): Select all applicable answers that describe your Temporomandibular Joint (TMJ) experiences. This field helps us understand your level of awareness about TMJ related issues.
* **Have you ever experienced TMJ pain?** (`id: 6`, `type: select_one`, `required: true`): Answer this question with either "Yes" or "No". If you have any TMJ pain, proceed with the next question.
* **How long have you experienced TMJ pain?** (`id: 7`, `type: select_one`, `required: true`): Select the duration of your TMJ experiences. This will help us understand the duration of your symptoms.
* **How much pain do you experience in your TMJ?** (`id: 8`, `type: select_one`, `required: true`): Select the level of pain you experience in your TMJ. This will help us understand the severity of your symptoms.
* **Do you experience any other symptoms?** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, `required: false`): If you experience any other symptoms related to your TMJ, feel free to describe them here.
* **Conclusion** (`id: 10`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Write a brief conclusion about your experiences and any additional information you'd like to share.

## Tips
Remember to answer all questions honestly and accurately. If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
