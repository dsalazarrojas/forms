# Athlete Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Athlete Medical History Form is a comprehensive document that collects essential information about an athlete's medical history. This form is designed to ensure the athlete's safety and well-being during participation in sports activities. It covers various aspects of the athlete's medical history, including personal information, medical history, injury history, family medical history, and declaration of accuracy. Please note that this form is a confidential and legally binding document.

<thinking>Before proceeding, please ensure this form is relevant to your situation and you understand that the information provided will be used for medical and administrative purposes only. If you have any concerns or questions, please contact the relevant authority or medical professional.</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Complete the personal information section (fields 1-5)
*   Provide emergency contact information (fields 6-9)
*   Disclose past and present medical history (fields 10-15)
*   Report sports-specific injury history (fields 16-20)
*   Share family medical history (fields 21-23)
*   Declare accuracy and sign the form (fields 24-27)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **-- Athlete Personal Information --** (`patient_info_header`)
	+ A brief header for the personal information section.
*   **Athlete Full Name** (`athlete_name`)
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (`athlete_dob`)
	+ Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Gender** (`athlete_gender`)
	+ Select your gender (Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say).
*   **Primary Sport** (`primary_sport`)
	+ Enter the primary sport you participate in (e.g., football, basketball, swimming).
*   **-- Emergency Contact Information --** (`emergency_contact_header`)
	+ A brief header for the emergency contact section.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`)
	+ Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`)
	+ Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
*   **Relationship to Athlete** (`contact_relationship`)
	+ Enter your relationship to the athlete (e.g., Parent, Spouse, Guardian).
*   **-- Past and Present Medical History --** (`medical_history_header`)
	+ A brief header for the medical history section.
*   **Known Allergies** (`known_allergies`)
	+ List any known allergies (food, medicine, environment).
*   **Current Medications and Supplements** (`current_medications`)
	+ List any current medications or supplements you are taking, including dosages.
*   **Chronic Medical Conditions** (`chronic_conditions`)
	+ Select all conditions that apply (Asthma, Diabetes, Heart Murmur, Epilepsy, etc.).
*   **Previous Surgeries and Procedures** (`past_surgeries`)
	+ List any previous surgeries or procedures, including approximate dates.
*   **Hospitalizations in the Last 2 Years** (`recent_hospitalizations`)
	+ Provide reasons and dates of hospitalizations within the last 2 years.
*   **-- Sports-Specific Injury History --** (`sports_injury_header`)
	+ A brief header for the injury history section.
*   **Have you ever sustained a concussion?** (`concession_history`)
	+ Select Yes or No.
*   **If yes, how many concussions?** (`concession_count`)
	+ Enter the number of concussions (0 if none).
*   **Previous Bone or Joint Injuries** (`bone_joint_injuries`)
	+ List any previous bone or joint injuries (fractures, dislocations, etc.).
*   **Previous Ligament or Tendon Injuries** (`ligament_tendon_injuries`)
	+ List any previous ligament or tendon injuries (ACL, sprains, tears, etc.).
*   **-- Family Medical History --** (`family_history_header`)
	+ A brief header for the family medical history section.
*   **Family history of sudden cardiac death under 50?** (`family_cardiac_death`)
	+ Select True, False, or Unknown.
*   **Family history of heart disease or high blood pressure?** (`family_heart_disease`)
	+ Select True, False, or Unknown.
*   **-- Declaration and Authorization --** (`authorization_header`)
	+ A brief header for the declaration and authorization section.
*   **I certify that the information provided is accurate and complete** (`accuracy_certification`)
	+ Select I certify or Other.
*   **Electronic Signature of Athlete or Guardian** (`legal_signature`)
	+ Enter your full name.
*   **Date of Completion** (`signature_date`)
	+ Enter the date of completion in MM/DD/YYYY format.
