# Medical Device Usability Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback about the usability of medical devices from users who have experience with them. Your input is important in helping us improve the design of these devices for better patient outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and answer them as honestly as possible.
- Provide as much detail as you can in your responses, especially in the open-ended questions.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Submit your form when you have completed all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Required: False): Please feel free to read this brief introduction and ask questions before proceeding with the form.
* **device_description** (Required: False): Describe the specific medical device you are evaluating, including its name or model number.
* **user_experience** (Required: False): Tell us about your experience with this medical device, including how often you use it and in what capacity.
* **user_familiarity** (Required: False): Rate your level of familiarity with this medical device. You can choose one of the following options:
	+ Very familiar
	+ Somewhat familiar
	+ Not at all
* **rating** (Required: False): Provide a numerical rating of your experience with the medical device (e.g., 1-5).
* **comment** (Required: False): Leave any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the medical device.
* **contact_name** (Required: False): If you'd like to be contacted to follow up on your feedback, please provide your name and contact information.
* **contact_email** (Required: False): If you'd like to be contacted to follow up on your feedback, please provide your email address.
* **contact_phone** (Required: False): If you'd like to be contacted to follow up on your feedback, please provide your phone number.
* **submission_comments** (Required: False): Leave any additional comments or feedback about this form or the medical device you've evaluated.

Note: Required fields are not actually marked as required in the form, but it's recommended to fill out all fields for a complete evaluation.
