# Fundic Height Measurement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fundic Height Measurement Survey is designed to collect height measurements from patients for future reference and tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Enter the mother's name in the "mothers_name" field (if applicable).
3. Measure and enter the patient's height in centimeters in the "height_in_centimeters" field.
4. Measure and enter the patient's height in inches in the "height_in_inches" field.
5. Enter the date of the last exam in the "date_of_last_exam" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name**: Enter the full name of the patient. This will be used to identify the patient in the database.
* **mothers_name**: If the patient is a minor, enter the name of the mother or guardian. This field is not required but is helpful for tracking contact information.
* **height_in_centimeters**: Measure the patient's height in centimeters using a growth chart or stadiometer. Enter the measurement here.
* **height_in_inches**: Measure the patient's height in inches using a growth chart or stadiometer. Enter the measurement here. 
* **date_of_last_exam**: Enter the date of the last exam or measurement in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This will help track the timeline of measurements. 

## Tips
* Make sure to measure the patient's height accurately using a stadiometer or growth chart.
* If the patient is a minor, ensure you have obtained parental consent before completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any measurement, consult with a medical professional. 
* Please note that this form is for information purposes only and should not be used for medical diagnosis or treatment.
