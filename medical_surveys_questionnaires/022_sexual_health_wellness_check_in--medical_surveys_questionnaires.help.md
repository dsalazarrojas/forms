# Sexual Health Wellness Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on an individual's sexual health and wellness. It will help healthcare providers understand an individual's sexual identity, activity levels, pain management, mental health, substance use, relationship status, and follow-up appointment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and answer as accurately as possible.
2. Select the options that best apply to your situation from each select_one and select_multiple fields.
3. If you're unsure about any question, do not hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider for guidance.
4. Fill in the date and time you prefer for your follow-up appointment in the 'Follow Up' and 'Follow Up Date' fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This field is a text box where you can write any additional information or comments about your current state of sexual health and wellness.
* **Gender Identity**: Select the option that best describes your gender identity from Male, Female, Other, or Prefer Not to Say.
* **Age**: Enter your current age.
* **Sexual Orientation**: Select all options that apply from Gay, Lesbian, Bisexual, Heterosexual, Asexual, Aromantic, Pansexual, Polysex, Other, or Prefer Not to Say.
* **Sexual Activity**: Select all options that apply from Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **Pain**: Select all options that apply from True, False.
* **Mental Health**: Select True or False.
* **Alcohol Use**: Select all options that apply from Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **Drug Use**: Select all options that apply from Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **Relationship Status**: Select the option that best describes your current relationship status from Single, In a Relationship, Married, In a Partnership, or Prefer Not to Say.
* **Follow Up**: Enter your preferred date and time for your follow-up appointment in this field.
* **Follow Up Date**: Select the time of your preferred follow-up appointment.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history information in this text box.
