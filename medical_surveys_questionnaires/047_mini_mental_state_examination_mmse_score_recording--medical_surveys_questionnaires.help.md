# Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) Score Recording - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) Score Recording form is used to evaluate a patient's mental state by assessing various cognitive functions. The form is essential for clinicians to quickly and efficiently assess patients' mental state.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Goals** (`goalsprak`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the patient's goals or objectives.
2. **Attention** (`attention`, select_multiple, required): Select the level of distractibility, disorganization, confusion, or disorientation the patient is experiencing.
3. **Orientation** (`orientation`, select_one, required): Choose the correct orientation of the patient, which could be "Correct", "Incorrect", or "Not sure".
4. **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the patient's current time, in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
5. **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 02/16/2024).
6. **Time of day** (`timeofday`, time, required): Enter the time of day in 24-hour format, similar to step 4.
7. **Language** (`language`, select_multiple, required): Select the languages the patient understands or speaks, which could be "English", "Spanish", or "Other".
8. **Calculation** (`calculation`, select_one, required): Choose the range of calculation skills the patient possesses, which could be "1-5", "6-11", "12-17", or "18-24".
9. **Sum** (`sum`, select_one, required): Choose the sum of scores for the patient, which could be 5 or 10.
10. **Proportions score** (`proportions_score`, select_multiple, required): Select the proportions score for the patient, which could be "0-1", "2-3", or "4-5".
11. **Orientation score** (`orientation_score`, select_one, required): Choose the orientation score for the patient, which could be "1", "2", or "3".</thinking>
