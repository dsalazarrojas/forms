# wellness_consultation_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your medical history, lifestyle, and other relevant factors to help healthcare professionals assess your overall well-being and provide personalized recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide accurate information about your medical history, lifestyle, and other relevant factors.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible, but be honest and concise in your answers.
3. If you are unsure about any information, feel free to ask for clarification or advice from a healthcare professional.
