# Community Health Literacy Level Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Health Literacy Level Survey is a tool used to measure an individual's ability to understand and navigate health information and services. This survey assesses various aspects of health literacy, including confidence in finding health information, understanding prescription instructions, and barriers to accessing care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer based on your own experiences and perceptions.
2. Select the best response for each question from the provided options.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) next to the label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Age (2)** (`Age`, `number`, required/optional): This question asks for your age in years. Please enter your age as a number.
* **Highest Education Level (3)** (`Highest education level`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about the highest level of education you have completed. Please select the option that best fits your education level.
* **Confidence Finding Health Information (4)** (`Confidence finding health information`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your confidence in finding health information. Please select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **Ability to Understand Prescriptions (5)** (`Ability to understand prescription instructions`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your ability to understand instructions on prescriptions. Please select the option that best fits your experience.
* **Frequency of Healthcare Visits (6)** (`Frequency of healthcare visits`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about your frequency of healthcare visits in the past 12 months. Please select the option that best describes your frequency.
* **Preferred Health Information Format (7)** (`Preferred health information format`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about your preferred formats for health information. Please select all the formats that you find most helpful.
* **Barriers to Accessing Care (8)** (`Barriers to accessing care`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about any barriers you experience in accessing care. Please select all the barriers that apply to you.
* **Suggestions for Improvement (9)** (`Suggestions for improving health information`, `text`, optional): This question is an open-ended space for you to provide any suggestions on how to improve health information.
* **Contact for Follow-up (10)** (`Contact email for follow up`, `email`, optional): This question is optional and asks for an email address to contact you for follow-up purposes.

Note: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*) and need to be completed to complete the survey.
