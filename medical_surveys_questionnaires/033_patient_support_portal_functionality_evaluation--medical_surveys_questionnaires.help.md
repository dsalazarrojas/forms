This form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from patients on the usability and functionality of the Patient Support Portal. It allows patients to rate their satisfaction with the portal, describe any issues or bugs they experienced, and provide improvement ideas. It also asks for their contact information and preferred method of communication with support staff. The goal of this form is to help the development team understand the patient experience and make data-driven decisions for the portal's future development.

The form is intended for patients who are registered users of the portal, and it is assumed that they have a basic understanding of the portal's features and functionality.

**Patient Support Portal Functionality Evaluation - Help Guide**

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from patients on their experience with the Patient Support Portal. Your input will help us improve the portal and provide a better experience for all patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide your honest feedback.
2. For each question, please answer as accurately and completely as possible.
3. If you have any issues or improvement ideas, please describe them in the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Portal Usage** (`portal_usage`, `text`, optional): Describe how you have used the Patient Support Portal in the past week/month.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `text`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the portal.
* **Issues** (`issues`, `text`, optional): If you experienced any issues or bugs while using the portal, describe them here.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions on how to improve the portal, please describe them here.
* **Patient Portal Features** (`patient_portal_features`, `select_one`, optional): Check all the features that you use on the portal.
	+ Patient Portal Features
	+ Portal Navigation
	+ Secure Login
	+ Contact Support
	+ Patient Portal Access
* **Patient Portal Satisfaction** (`patient_portal_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the following features.
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Issue Severity** (`issue_severity`, `select_one`, optional): If you have experienced any issues, choose the severity level.
	+ Critical
	+ Minor
* **Patient Portal Ideas** (`patient_portal_ideas`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions for new features or improvements to the portal, please describe them here.
* **Portal Issues** (`portal_issues`, `text`, optional): If you have experienced any issues with the portal, describe them here.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, `text`, optional): If you have experienced any issues, please describe them in detail here.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional feedback or comments, please describe them here.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Please provide your contact information so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you would like to be contacted via email, please provide your email address.
* **Support Contact** (`support_contact`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you would prefer to be contacted by support staff.
	+ Primary
	+ Secondary
	+ Tertiary
* **Support Contact Type** (`support_contact_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you would prefer to be contacted by support staff.
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ In-person

Please note that all fields are optional, and you can skip any questions that do not apply to you. Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
