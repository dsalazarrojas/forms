# procedure_side_effects_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about side effects experienced during a medical procedure. It will help healthcare professionals to identify potential issues and provide better care for patients. If you've already completed this form or a similar form, you may not need to do so again.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Review the form carefully** and answer each question accurately to provide the best possible information to healthcare professionals.
2. **Answer each question according to your experience** during the medical procedure.
3. **Submit the form** once you've completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **How was your procedure performed**
This field is a free-text field where you can describe how the procedure was performed. Please provide a brief and accurate account of what happened during the procedure.

*   **How was your procedure performed**: Please provide a detailed description of what happened during the procedure. This will help healthcare professionals to understand the context of your experience.

### 2. **What was the medical condition**
This field is a multiple-choice field where you can select one or more conditions that apply to your experience.
*   **What was the medical condition**: Check all conditions that apply to your experience. This will help healthcare professionals to understand the underlying health issue.

### 3. **What symptoms did you experience**
This field is a multiple-choice field where you can select one or more symptoms that you experienced.
*   **What symptoms did you experience**: Check all symptoms that apply to your experience. This will help healthcare professionals to identify potential issues.

### 4. **How long did your symptoms last**
This field is a numeric field where you can enter the duration of your symptoms in minutes.
*   **How long did your symptoms last**: Enter the exact duration of your symptoms in minutes. This will help healthcare professionals to assess the severity of your experience.

### 5. **How severe were your symptoms**
This field is a single-choice field where you can select the severity of your symptoms.
*   **How severe were your symptoms**: Select the severity of your symptoms from the options provided. This will help healthcare professionals to understand the intensity of your experience.

### 6. **Time of symptoms**
This field is a time field where you can enter the exact time of your symptoms.
*   **Time of symptoms**: Enter the exact time of your symptoms. This will help healthcare professionals to identify any patterns or correlations.

### 7. **Do you have any comments or concerns**
This field is a note field where you can provide any additional comments or concerns.
*   **Do you have any comments or concerns**: Please provide any additional comments or concerns you have. This will help healthcare professionals to provide better care for you.

### 8. **What medical conditions have you had in the past**
This field is a multiple-choice field where you can select one or more medical conditions you've had in the past.
*   **What medical conditions have you had in the past**: Check all conditions that apply to your experience. This will help healthcare professionals to identify potential underlying health issues.

### 9. **Medications taken**
This field is a multiple-choice field where you can select one or more medications you've taken.
*   **Medications taken**: Check all medications that apply to your experience. This will help healthcare professionals to identify potential interactions or allergies.

### 10. **Other medical info**
This field is a note field where you can provide any additional medical information.
*   **Other medical info**: Please provide any additional medical information that may be relevant to your experience. This will help healthcare professionals to provide better care for you.
