# public_health_data_ethics_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "public_health_data_ethics_survey" form is a tool to gather information about public health data concerns, priorities, attitudes, and related policies. This form aims to understand the perspectives and values of individuals in the public health sector, particularly regarding data collection, anonymization, sharing, and use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and respond honestly to the best of your abilities.
2. Fill in your responses in the provided text fields.
3. Make sure to complete all fields, as indicated by the "required" status in each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **public_health_data_concerns** (1, text, required): This field is for you to list your major concerns related to public health data. Please provide a clear and concise description of your concerns.
* **public_health_data_priorities** (2, text, required): In this field, specify the aspects of public health data that you consider most important to address. Be as specific as possible.
* **public_health_data_attitudes** (3, text, required): Share your attitude towards public health data collection, sharing, and use. This could range from positive to negative or neutral perspectives.
* **data_collection_methodologies** (4, text, required): Describe the methods you believe are most effective for collecting public health data. This could be through surveys, interviews, or other data collection methods.
* **data_anonymization_methods** (5, text, required): Suggest the methods for anonymizing public health data that you think are most appropriate. This could involve data encryption, data masking, or other anonymization techniques.
* **data_sharing_policies** (6, text, required): Define the policies you think are essential for sharing public health data. For example, you could discuss open-access, restricted access, or specific permissions required.
* **data_use_cases** (7, text, required): Describe how you envision public health data being used. This could be for research, policy-making, or public awareness.
* **data_anonymization_levels** (8, text, required): Specify the level of anonymization you believe is required for public health data. This could range from minimal to maximum anonymization.

## Tips
