# prescriber_survey_substance_use_disorder - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the substance use disorder survey from a healthcare provider. It will help us understand the experiences and observations of healthcare providers working with patients who may have a substance use disorder.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question on each page carefully and thoroughly.
2. Please note that some fields are optional, but it's recommended that you fill out all fields if possible.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **prescriber_survey** (`prescriber_survey`, text, optional): Describe your interaction with the patient, including any notable events or discussions that may be relevant to their substance use disorder.
* **barrier** (`barrier`, text, optional): Describe any barriers you encountered while interacting with the patient, such as language or cultural issues.
* **education** (`education`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you provided education to the patient about substance use disorder, or 'No' if not.
* **mat_use** (`mat_use`, number, optional): Report the patient's reported history of material use (e.g. number of times they used substances).
* **barrier_education** (`barrier_education`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply if you had any barriers to providing education to the patient about substance use disorder.
* **providers** (`providers`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you referred the patient to other healthcare providers for substance use disorder treatment, or 'No' if not.
* **interest** (`interest`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have interest in referring the patient to substance use disorder treatment, or 'No' if not.
* **education_need** (`education_need`, text, optional): Describe any education or support the patient may need regarding substance use disorder.
* **provider_info** (`provider_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your interaction with the patient and any relevant details about your practice or organization.
* **note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or observations you have about the patient's substance use disorder.

## Tips
- If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact us for assistance.
- Please fill out this form carefully and thoroughly to ensure we receive accurate information.
- If you have any additional comments or observations, please use the "note" field to add them.
