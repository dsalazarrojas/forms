# Breast Imaging History Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about a patient's breast imaging history for the purpose of providing accurate and effective breast imaging examinations. The survey helps us understand your medical background, previous breast experiences, and any relevant health conditions. Please take a few minutes to answer these questions honestly and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully, providing accurate and complete information.
2. Use the options provided for select questions, as they are designed to help guide your response.
3. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. Enter dates in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
5. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask the technologist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Full Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Referring Physician**: Enter the name of your current or previous doctor.
*   **Reason for Exam**: Select the reason for your current examination from the provided options:
    +   Routine Screening
    +   Diagnostic Follow-Up
    +   Lump or Mass
    +   Pain or Discomfort
    +   Nipple Discharge
    +   Skin Changes
    +   Family History
    +   Other
*   **Reason Details**: Please elaborate on your reason for examination.
*   **Previous Mammograms**: Select your mammogram history:
    +   Yes, Multiple
    +   Yes, One or Two
    +   No, Never
*   **Last Mammogram Date**: Enter the date of your last mammogram.
*   **Last Mammogram Location**: Enter the location where your last mammogram was performed.
*   **Previous Breast Imaging**: Select all previous breast imaging you have had:
    +   None
    +   Breast Ultrasound
    +   Breast MRI
    +   Breast CT
    +   Biopsy
    +   Other
*   **Current Breast Symptoms**: Select all current breast symptoms you are experiencing:
    +   Lump or Thickening
    +   Pain or Tenderness
    +   Nipple Discharge
    +   Skin Dimpling
    +   Redness or Rash
    +   Nipple Inversion
    +   Swelling
*   **Symptom Location**: Select the location of your symptoms:
    +   Right Breast
    +   Left Breast
    +   Both Breasts
    +   Not Applicable
*   **Family History of Breast Cancer**: Select your family history of breast cancer:
    +   Yes, First-Degree Relative
    +   Yes, Second-Degree Relative
    +   False
    +   Unknown
*   **Family History Details**: Please provide details about your family history.
*   **Personal History of Breast Cancer**: Select if you have had breast cancer:
    +   True
    +   False
*   **Breast Surgery History**: Select your breast surgery history:
    +   Yes, Augmentation
    +   Yes, Reduction
    +   Yes, Lumpectomy
    +   Yes, Mastectomy
    +   Yes, Biopsy Only
    +   No Breast Surgery
*   **Hormone Therapy**: Select if you are currently on hormone therapy:
    +   Yes, Current
    +   Yes, Past
    +   Never
*   **Pregnancy Status**: Select your pregnancy status:
    +   Not Pregnant, Not Breastfeeding
    +   Currently Pregnant
    +   Currently Breastfeeding
    +   Recently Pregnant
*   **Breast Density Knowledge**: Select if you are aware of your breast density:
    +   Yes, Dense
    +   Yes, Not Dense
    +   No, Not Sure
*   **Additional Health Conditions**: Please list any other relevant health conditions.
*   **Questions or Concerns**: If you have any questions or concerns, please list them.
*   **Patient Signature**: Sign your name, including your full name.
*   **Signature Date**: Enter the date you sign this form.
