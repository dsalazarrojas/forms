# dyspnea-assessment-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "dyspnea-assessment-survey" form is designed to collect information on patients' symptoms, medical conditions, and medical history to help healthcare professionals better understand their condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Survey Title** to provide a brief description of the survey.
2. Provide the **Patient Name**, ensuring it matches the patient's identification.
3. Select the relevant **Medical Conditions** affecting the patient.
4. Specify the **Duration of Symptoms** as requested.
5. Choose how often the **Frequency of Symptoms** occur.
6. Answer the **Shortness of Breath Questions** (note-taking section).
7. Answer the **Triggers** questions (note-taking section).
8. Provide the **Medical History**, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title (1)**: A brief description of the survey to help identify the assessment.
* **Patient Name (2)**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Medical Conditions (3)**: Choose all relevant medical conditions affecting the patient, such as COPD, Cardiovascular, or Respiratory.
* **Duration of Symptoms (4)**: Record the length of time the patient has been experiencing the symptoms.
* **Frequency of Symptoms (5)**: Indicate how often the symptoms occur, such as constantly, occasionally, or rarely.
* **Shortness of Breath Questions (6)**: Take notes on the questions related to shortness of breath, providing a detailed description of the patient's experience.
* **Triggers (7)**: Note any triggers that may be contributing to the patient's symptoms.
* **Medical History (8)**: Document the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical procedures, allergies, or previous conditions.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
* Ensure the patient's information is up-to-date and accurate.
* Review the form carefully to ensure all necessary information is provided.
* If unsure about any information, consult with the healthcare team for clarification.
