# Patient Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for screening patients and gathering information about their medical history and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and select all relevant medical conditions, allergies, and medical histories that apply to the patient.
2. For each field, choose "None" if the condition or history does not apply to the patient.
3. Enter any additional medical history information in the text fields (Medical History 1-5).
4. Ensure that each field is accurately and thoroughly completed to provide comprehensive information about the patient's medical status.
