# Medical Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Information Form collects basic medical information from patients or individuals to help medical professionals understand their background, medical history, and current condition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Patient Name** in the first field. If you're a patient, enter your own name, and if you're providing information about a patient, enter their name.
2. In the **Medical Issue** field, describe the reason for your visit or the medical issue you're currently dealing with. This could be a condition you're experiencing, a symptom, or a diagnosis.
3. The **Symptoms** field is for listing any symptoms you're experiencing. You can enter as many as you need to accurately describe your condition.
4. **Medical History** is a field for you to provide any relevant medical history, such as previous illnesses, surgeries, or allergies.
5. Select all that apply for **Diabetes Control**. If you have diabetes, choose from the options listed:
	* Type 1 Diabetes
	* Type 2 Diabetes
	* Gestational Diabetes
	* Pre-Diabetic (you're not diabetic but have a higher risk)
	* Unknown (you're unsure or not diagnosed)
6. In **Medication** field, list any medications you're currently taking or have taken in the past.
7. Enter your doctor's name in the **Doctor Name** field if you're currently seeing a physician.
8. Select a date in the **Date of Birth** field to provide your date of birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (text, required: false):
	* Enter your name or the patient's name you're providing information about.
* **Medical Issue** (text, required: false):
	* Describe the reason for your visit or the medical issue you're currently dealing with.
* **Symptoms** (text, required: false):
	* List any symptoms you're experiencing.
* **Medical History** (text, required: false):
	* Provide any relevant medical history, such as previous illnesses, surgeries, or allergies.
* **Diabetes Control** (select multiple, required: false):
	* Select all that apply to describe your diabetes status.
* **Medication** (text, required: false):
	* List any medications you're currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Doctor Name** (text, required: false):
	* Enter your doctor's name if you're currently seeing a physician.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required: false):
	* Select a date to provide your date of birth.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, try to provide the best answer you can.
* If you're providing information about a patient, be sure to have their consent before completing this form.
