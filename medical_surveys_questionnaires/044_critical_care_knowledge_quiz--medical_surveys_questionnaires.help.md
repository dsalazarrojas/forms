# Critical Care Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge in critical care. It includes questions about sepsis, ventilation, critical care skills, and other relevant topics. The form is meant to be completed by healthcare professionals, including doctors, nurses, and medical residents, to gauge their understanding of critical care principles and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the correct answers to each question. Most questions are multiple-choice, and you may select multiple answers if applicable. For text questions, type your answer in the provided field.
