# Ischemic Heart Disease Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to educate individuals about ischemic heart disease, its symptoms, risk factors, and associated medical procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Choose the most applicable options for each question from the provided choices.
3. Be sure to review and verify your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Heart Disease Symptoms** (`heart_disease_symptoms`, text, required=False):
This field asks about your experiences with heart disease symptoms. Please describe any symptoms you have experienced, such as chest pain, shortness of breath, or palpitations.
* **Heart Disease Risk Factors** (`heart_disease_risk_factors`, text, required=False):
List any factors that increase your risk of developing heart disease, such as family history, smoking, or high blood pressure.
* **Heart Disease Type** (`select_one_heart_disease_type`, select_one, required=False):
Select the type of heart disease you are most familiar with or suspect you may have:
	+ Myocardial Infarction
	+ Cardiac Arrest
	+ Heart Failure
* **Heart Disease Treatments** (`heart_disease_treatments`, text, required=False):
 Describe any medical treatments you have received for heart disease, such as medication, surgery, or lifestyle changes.
* **Heart Disease Medical Procedures** (`select_multiple_heart_disease_medical_procedures`, select_multiple, required=False):
Select any medical procedures you have undergone or are familiar with:
	+ Angioplasty
	+ Angiography
	+ Angiectomy
	+ Balloon Angioplasty
	+ Cardiac Ablation
	+ Cardiac Angioplasty
	+ Cardiac Angiography
	+ Catheter Angioplasty
	+ Coronary Angioplasty
	+ Electrophysiology Studies
	+ Electrophysiology Study
	+ Laser Angioplasty
	+ Laser Atherectomy
	+ Minimally Invasive Cardiac Surgery
	+ Percutaneous Angioplasty
	+ Percutaneous Cardiac Angioplasty
	+ Percutaneous Cardiac Angiography
	+ Stenting
	+ Vaso-Occlusive Angioplasty
	+ Vaso-Occlusive Angioplasty Balloon
	+ Vaso-Occlusive Angioplasty Catheter
	+ Vaso-Occlusive Angioplasty Laser
	+ Vaso-Occlusive Angioplasty Stent
* **How often do you experience these symptoms?** (`heart_disease_symptoms_frequency`, select_multiple, required=False):
Rate how often you experience heart disease symptoms:
	+ Often
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Emergency Status** (`select_one_heart_disease_emergency_calling_status`, select_one, required=False):
Indicate your emergency status:
	+ Emergency
	+ Not in Emergency
* **Emergency Response** (`heart_disease_emergency_response`, text, required=False):
 Describe your emergency response, if any.
* **Safety Net Status** (`heart_disease_safety_net_status`, text, required=False):
 Describe your safety net status, if any.
* **Medical History** (`heart_disease_medical_history`, text, required=False):
 Describe your medical history, if any.
* **Family History** (`heart_disease_family_history`, text, required=False):
 Describe your family history, if any.
* **Lifestyle Habits** (`heart_disease_lifestyle_habits`, text, required=False):
 Describe your lifestyle habits, if any.

## Tips

* Please answer each question accurately and honestly.
* This form is for educational purposes only and should not be considered a substitute for professional medical advice.
* If you have any concerns about your heart health, please consult a medical professional.
