# Microdermabrasion Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Microdermabrasion Medical History Form is used to collect information from patients about their medical history before undergoing a microdermabrasion procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions on the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. If you have any medical history that you don't understand or are unsure about, please discuss it with your healthcare provider or doctor.
3. If you are unsure about what any of the questions mean, please ask your healthcare provider or doctor for clarification.
4. Please provide as much detail as possible in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical History Question**: This is the first question on the form, where you will be asked to provide any relevant medical history information.
	+ Please provide any relevant medical history information, such as previous surgeries, illnesses, or medical conditions.
* **Medication Allergies**: This question asks about any medications you are allergic to.
	+ Please list any medications you are allergic to, including the name of the medication and any relevant symptoms.
* **Allergies**: This question asks about any other allergies you may have.
	+ Please list any other allergies you may have, including any relevant symptoms.
* **Medical History Info 1-22**: These questions ask about specific medical history information, such as medical conditions, illnesses, or treatments.
	+ Please answer these questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible, and provide as much detail as possible.
	+ If you are unsure about what any of these questions mean, please ask your healthcare provider or doctor for clarification.

## Tips
* Please take your time to answer the questions thoroughly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your healthcare provider or doctor for clarification.
* Please provide as much detail as possible in your responses.
* If you have any medical history or allergies that are not listed, please include them in the "Medical History Question" field.
