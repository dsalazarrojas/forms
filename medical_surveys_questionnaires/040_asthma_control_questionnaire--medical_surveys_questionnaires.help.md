# Asthma Control Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Asthma Control Questionnaire is a comprehensive tool for healthcare professionals to collect patient information related to their asthma control and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Patient Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Patient ID Number** in the second field.
3. Select the **Date of this check-in** in the third field.
4. Enter the name of your **Healthcare Provider** in the fourth field.
5. Choose your **Age Group** from the drop-down menu in the fifth field.
6. If you were diagnosed with asthma, enter the **Asthma Diagnosis Date** in the sixth field.
7. Select your **Current Asthma Severity** from the drop-down menu in the seventh field.
8. For the past week, select the number of **Daytime Symptoms** you have experienced in the eighth field.
9. Choose how many **Nighttime Awakening from Asthma** you have experienced in the ninth field.
10. Indicate if asthma has **Activity Limitation** for you in the tenth field.
11. Select how often you experienced **Shortness of Breath** in the eleventh field.
12. For the past week, select the number of **Wheezing Episodes** you have experienced in the twelfth field.
13. Choose how many **Coughing Episodes** you have experienced in the thirteenth field.
14. Select how often you have used your **Rescue Inhaler** in the fourteenth field.
15. If you have visited the emergency department or hospital in the past month, select **Yes** in the fifteenth field and enter the number of **Emergency Visits** you have made in the sixteenth field.
16. Choose your medication adherence level by selecting **Always**, **Usually**, **Sometimes**, **Rarely**, or **Never** in the seventeenth field.
17. Select **Yes** if you are taking **Controller Medication** and **No** otherwise.
18. If you are experiencing **Medication Side Effects**, select **Yes** and describe the side effects in the twentieth field.
19. Choose the **Identified Asthma Triggers** that affect you in the twenty-first field (select multiple options).
20. Select how well you are **Successfully Avoiding Triggers** by choosing from the drop-down menu in the twenty-second field.
21. Select **Yes** if you have a written **Asthma Action Plan** from your doctor and **No** otherwise.
22. Choose your **Overall Asthma Control** from the drop-down menu in the twenty-fourth field.
23. Enter any **Additional Comments or Concerns** you have in the twenty-fifth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Patient ID Number**: Enter your medical record number or any other identification number assigned to you.
* **Questionnaire Date**: Select the date of this check-in.
* **Healthcare Provider**: Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Age Group**: Select your age group from the options provided (Child 0-4, Child 5-11, Adolescent 12-17, Adult 18-64, Older Adult 65+).
* **Asthma Diagnosis Date**: If you were diagnosed with asthma, enter the date of your diagnosis.
* **Current Asthma Severity**: Select your current asthma severity level (Mild Intermittent, Mild Persistent, Moderate Persistent, Severe Persistent).
* **Daytime Symptoms (past week)**: Select the number of days in the past week you experienced asthma symptoms during the daytime.
* **Nighttime Awakening from Asthma (past week)**: Select the number of nights in the past week you woke up with asthma symptoms.
* **Activity Limitation (past week)**: Indicate if your asthma has limited your activities during the past week.
* **Shortness of Breath (past week)**: Select how often you experienced shortness of breath in the past week.
* **Wheezing Episodes (past week)**: Select the number of wheezing episodes you experienced in the past week.
* **Coughing Episodes (past week)**: Select the number of asthma-related coughing episodes you experienced in the past week.
* **Rescue Inhaler Use (past week)**: Select how often you have used your rescue inhaler in the past week.
* **Emergency Department or Hospital Visits (past month)**: If you have visited the emergency department or hospital in the past month, select 'Yes' and enter the number of visits.
* **Emergency Visits**: If applicable, enter the number of emergency visits you have made in the past month.
* **Medication Adherence**: Select your medication adherence level (Always, Usually, Sometimes, Rarely, Never).
* **Taking Controller Medication**: Select if you are taking controller medication (Yes, No).
* **Medication Side Effects**: Select if you are experiencing side effects (Yes, No). If you select 'Yes', describe the side effects.
* **Identified Asthma Triggers**: Select all the triggers that affect you (Allergens, Respiratory infections, Exercise, Cold air, Air pollution, Strong odors, Stress, Other).
* **Successfully Avoiding Triggers**: Select how well you are able to avoid the triggers you have selected.
* **Have Asthma Action Plan**: Select if you have a written plan from your doctor (Yes, No).
* **Overall Asthma Control (patient perception)**: Select your perception of how well controlled your asthma is (Very well controlled, Well controlled, Partially controlled, Poorly controlled).
* **Additional Comments or Concerns**: Enter any other information or concerns you have regarding your asthma.
