# Measles Exposure Monitoring Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to monitor and track exposure to measles among individuals. It helps healthcare professionals quickly identify those at risk and take necessary precautions to prevent the spread of the disease.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1.  Ensure you have read and understood the purpose of this form and the information it seeks.
2.  Fill out the form completely, providing truthful answers to all questions.
3.  Ensure the answers accurately reflect your exposure status and any relevant travel history.
4.  Submit your completed form for review and follow-up actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is an introduction page that explains the purpose and importance of the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Contact Information**: 
    *   `Contact Information` (`Contact Information`, `text`, required): This field is where you provide your personal contact information so our team can reach out to you for further instructions or follow-up.
*   **Exposure Information**: 
    *   `Exposure Information` (`Exposure Information`, `select_multiple`, required): Answer "Yes" if you have been exposed to measles or "No" if you haven't. If you've been exposed, indicate the source of exposure (e.g., person-to-person, infected animal, travel).
*   **Symptoms**: 
    *   `Symptoms` (`Symptoms`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" if you are experiencing any symptoms of measles or "No" if you are symptom-free.
*   **Vaccination Status**: 
    *   `Vaccination Status` (`Vaccination Status`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current vaccination status: "Active" if you are vaccinated, "Inactive" if you are not vaccinated.
*   **Travel History**: 
    *   `Travel History` (`Travel History`, `select_multiple`, required): Answer "Yes" if you have traveled to any areas where measles is prevalent or "No" if you haven't.
*   **Additional Information**: 
    *   `Additional Information` (`Additional Information`, `text`, optional): If you have any further details or comments regarding your exposure, please provide them here.
*   **Consent**: 
    *   `Consent` (`Consent`, `select_one`, required): Please indicate that you have given consent to share your information and be contacted for further instructions.
*   **Review**: 
    *   `Review` (`Review`, `text`, optional): Review your submitted information and ensure it is accurate before submitting.
*   **Submit**: 
    *   `Submit` (`Submit`, `select_one`, optional): Once you have reviewed your information, click "Yes" to submit it for review and follow-up.
