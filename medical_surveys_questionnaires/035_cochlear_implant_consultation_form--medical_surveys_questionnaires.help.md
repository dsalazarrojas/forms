# Cochlear Implant Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cochlear Implant Consultation Form is designed to gather information from patients about their hearing loss, medical background, and lifestyle in order to determine their suitability for a cochlear implant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your name, date of birth, and email address.
2. Provide your hearing loss history, including the age of onset, type, and whether it is bilateral or unilateral.
3. Share your medical history, including any chronic conditions, current medications, and previous surgeries.
4. Describe your motivations and expectations for seeking a cochlear implant.
5. Discuss how your hearing loss impacts your daily life and what you hope to achieve with a cochlear implant.
6. Indicate your level of support from family and friends.
7. Provide information about your employment status and communication needs.
8. Select a preferred consultation date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Primary Language** (`Primary Language`, `text`, optional): If not English, please select your primary language.
* **Age of Hearing Loss Onset** (`Age of Hearing Loss Onset`, `number`, required): Enter the age when you first experienced hearing loss.
* **Type of Hearing Loss** (`Type of Hearing Loss`, `select_one`, required): Select from:
	+ Congenital
	+ Progressive
	+ Sudden
	+ Acquired
	+ Unknown
* **Is hearing loss bilateral?** (`Is hearing loss bilateral?`, `select_one`, required): Select from:
	+ Yes, both ears
	+ No, one ear only
	+ Not sure
* **Have you used hearing aids?** (`Have you used hearing aids?`, `select_one`, required): Select from:
	+ Never
	+ Tried in past
	+ Currently using
* **Benefit from Hearing Aids** (`Benefit from Hearing Aids`, `select_one`, optional): Select from:
	+ Very helpful
	+ Somewhat helpful
	+ Not helpful
	+ Not applicable
* **Chronic Medical Conditions** (`Chronic Medical Conditions`, `text`, optional): List any ongoing health issues.
* **Current Medications** (`Current Medications`, `text`, optional): List all medications you are currently taking.
* **Previous Ear Surgery** (`Previous Ear Surgery`, `text`, optional): Enter any prior ear procedures.
* **Known Allergies** (`Known Allergies`, `text`, optional): List any medication or material allergies.
* **Why are you interested in a cochlear implant?** (`Why are you interested in a cochlear implant?`, `text`, required): Describe your motivations for seeking a cochlear implant.
* **What are your expectations for improvement?** (`What are your expectations for improvement?`, `text`, required): What do you hope to achieve with a cochlear implant.
* **How does hearing loss impact your daily life?** (`How does hearing loss impact your daily life?`, `text`, required): Describe how your hearing loss affects your daily activities.
* **Family Support for Decision** (`Family Support for Decision`, `select_one`, required): Select from:
	+ Strong support
	+ Some support
	+ Neutral
	+ Some opposition
	+ Strong opposition
* **Employment Status** (`Employment Status`, `select_one`, required): Select from:
	+ Employed full-time
	+ Employed part-time
	+ Unemployed
	+ Retired
	+ Student
	+ Other
* **Communication Needs** (`Communication Needs`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Conversation
	+ Phone use
	+ Music enjoyment
	+ Professional needs
	+ Social interaction
	+ Safety awareness
* **Preferred Consultation Date** (`Preferred Consultation Date`, `date`, optional): Select a date and time for your consultation.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, `text`, optional): Provide any other information that may be relevant to your consultation.
