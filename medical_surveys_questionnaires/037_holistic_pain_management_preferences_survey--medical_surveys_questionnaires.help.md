# Holistic Pain Management Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about your preferences for managing pain. The survey consists of six questions, which are divided into sections for easier completion.

## How To Complete This Form

- Read each question carefully and select your answers based on your current situation.
- You can complete this form on your own without assistance.
- If you need help, contact your healthcare professional for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pain Levels** (`High`, `Low`): Please select all the levels that currently apply to you. If you are experiencing a mix of both high and low pain levels, choose both options.
* **Current Treatments** (`Yes`, `No`): Are you currently using any pain management treatments? Select `Yes` if you are, and `No` if you are not.
* **Therapies of Interest** (`Yes`, `No`): Which pain management therapies are you interested in? Select `Yes` for each therapy that interests you, and `No` for those that do not.
* **Pain Management Strategies** (`Good`, `Fair`): How effective are the pain management strategies you are currently using? Select `Good` if they are effective, `Fair` if they are somewhat effective, and leave blank if you are unsure.
* **Pain Impact** (`Yes`, `No`): Does pain have a significant impact on your daily life? Select `Yes` if it does, and `No` if it does not.
* **Medical Concerns** (`Yes`, `No`): Do you have any medical concerns that affect your pain management? Select `Yes` if you do, and `No` if you do not.

## Tips

- Answer honestly and accurately to ensure that your input is helpful to your healthcare professional.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your healthcare professional for guidance.
- This survey is intended to help healthcare professionals understand your preferences for managing pain, so provide as much detail as you can.
