# Medical Device Follow Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and follow-up information from patients who have used medical devices. The collected data will be used to improve the design, functionality, and safety of medical devices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly, providing as much detail as possible about your experience with the medical device.
2. Select the relevant device type(s) from the list.
3. Provide your device model number.
4. Please provide your contact information, so we can get in touch with you if needed.
5. If you are a healthcare provider, please provide your contact information.
6. Describe any issues you experienced with the device.
7. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please include them in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Device Follow Up Form Page 1 Label** (`medical_device_follow_up_form_page_1_label`, `note`, required/optional): This page is the introduction to the form, and it explains the purpose and importance of providing feedback.
* **Patient Feedback** (`patient_feedback_label`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe your overall experience with the medical device. Be as detailed as possible, including any positive or negative aspects of your experience.
* **Device Type** (`device_type_label`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the type of medical device you used from the list. This information will help us understand which devices are most commonly associated with issues or concerns.
* **Device Model Number** (`device_model_label`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide the exact model number of the medical device you used.
* **Medical Device Follow Up Form Page 2 Label** (`medical_device_follow_up_form_page_2_label`, `note`, required/optional): This page is for additional information or comments.
* **Patient Contact Information** (`patient_contact_info_label`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Healthcare Provider Contact Information** (`healthcare_provider_contact_info_label`, `text`, required/optional): If you are a healthcare provider, please provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Healthcare Provider Comments** (`healthcare_provider_comments_label`, `note`, required/optional): This is an optional field for healthcare providers to provide any additional comments or information.
* **Device Issue** (`device_issue_label`, `select_one`, required/optional): Describe the specific issue you experienced with the device.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments_label`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please include them here.
