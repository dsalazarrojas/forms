# new_patient_medical_history_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_patient_medical_history_form is a comprehensive form to collect essential information about new patients, including their contact details, medical history, and consent. This form is crucial in ensuring that you have a complete understanding of the patient's situation, which will help you provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the patient's basic information in the "Patient Info" field.
2. In the "Medical History" field, describe the patient's medical history, including any allergies, previous medical conditions, or treatments.
3. In the "Consent" field, select "None" if the patient does not have any medical conditions or allergies, or "Other" if the patient has a medical condition or allergy that is not listed.
4. Enter the patient's contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
5. If necessary, complete the "Medical Survey" field with any relevant information.
6. Answer the "Medical Survey Questions" field with any relevant information.
7. In the "Terms and Conditions" field, enter any relevant details.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Additional Notes" field.
9. If applicable, enter the name of the contact person and their information in the "Contact Person" and "Contact Person Info" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (1)**: Enter the patient's full name, date of birth, and any other essential contact information.
* **Medical History (2)**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any allergies, previous medical conditions, or treatments.
* **Consent (3)**: Select "None" if the patient does not have any medical conditions or allergies, or "Other" if the patient has a medical condition or allergy that is not listed. Please note that this field is optional.
* **Contact Info (4)**: Enter the patient's contact information, including their address, phone number, and email.
* **Medical Survey (5)**: Enter any relevant information about the patient's medical history or condition.
* **Medical Survey Questions (6)**: Answer any relevant questions about the patient's medical history or condition.
* **Terms and Conditions (7)**: Enter any relevant details about the patient's treatment or care.
* **Additional Notes (8)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the patient's case.
* **Contact Person (9)**: Enter the name of the contact person, if applicable.
* **Contact Person Info (10)**: Enter any relevant information about the contact person, if applicable.
