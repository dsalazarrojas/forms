# Repair And Inflammation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for healthcare professionals to assess a patient's current condition and make informed decisions. It is not intended for personal or non-medical use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the patient's information in the `Patient Information` field.
2. Select the patient's current pain level from the `Pain Level` dropdown menu.
3. Describe the patient's inflammatory symptoms in the `Inflammatory Symptoms` field.
4. Note the patient's healing progress in the `Healing Progress` field.
5. Enter the date of the patient's last visit in the `Date Of Last Visit` field.
6. Enter the time of the patient's last visit in the `Time Of Last Visit` field.
7. Enter the patient's phone number in the `Phone Number` field.
8. Enter the patient's email address in the `Email Address` field.
9. Describe the patient's medical history in the `Medical History` field.
10. Enter any additional comments in the `Additional Comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (`patient_info`)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Pain Level (`pain_level`)**: Select the number that best describes the patient's current pain level, where:
	+ 1: Mild pain
	+ 2: Moderate pain
	+ 3: Severe pain
	+ 4: Extreme pain
	+ 5: No pain
* **Inflammatory Symptoms (`inflammatory_symptoms`)**: Describe any symptoms the patient is experiencing, such as swelling, redness, or warmth.
* **Healing Progress (`healing_progress`)**: Note the patient's progress towards recovery, such as improved symptoms or reduced pain.
* **Date Of Last Visit (`date_of_last_visit`)**: Enter the date of the patient's last visit to a healthcare provider.
* **Time Of Last Visit (`time_of_last_visit`)**: Enter the time of the patient's last visit to a healthcare provider.
* **Phone Number (`phone_number`)**: Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address (`email_address`)**: Enter the patient's email address for contact purposes.
* **Medical History (`medical_history`)**: Describe the patient's relevant medical history, including any previous conditions or treatments.
* **Additional Comments (`additional_comments`)**: Enter any additional comments or information about the patient's condition.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to help healthcare professionals make informed decisions.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with a healthcare professional for guidance.
* Please do not enter sensitive or confidential information in any of the fields.
