# Eye Exam Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eye Exam Form Template is a medical form designed to collect essential information from patients undergoing an eye exam. This form aims to gather details about patient demographics, medical history, medications, visual acuity, and other relevant medical information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling in the patient's information on **Patient Information** (page 1).
2. Move on to the **Medical History** section and provide any relevant medical information.
3. List the patient's current **Meditations** (page 3).
4. Record the patient's **Visual Acuity** (page 4).
5. Document the **Eye Exam Results** (page 5).
6. Note the **Next Steps** to be taken (page 6).
7. Choose the **Medical Procedure** recommended (page 7).
8. Set a **Follow Up** appointment date (page 8).
9. Add any **Doctor Notes** (page 9).
10. Record the patient's **Patient Comments** (page 10).
11. Finally, include any **Doctor Comments** (page 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (<name>, text, required: false): Fill in the patient's full name and demographic details as instructed.
* **Medical History** (<name>, text, required: false): Report any relevant medical history or conditions.
* **Meditations** (<name>, select multiple, required: false): List the patient's current medications (e.g., Aspirin, Ibuprofen, Paracetamol, Captopprazol).
* **Visual Acuity** (<name>, number, required: false): Record the patient's visual acuity using a numerical value.
* **Eye Exam Results** (<name>, text, required: false): Document the results of the eye exam.
* **Next Steps** (<name>, note, required: false): Note any recommended next steps or actions for the patient.
* **Medical Procedure** (<name>, select one, required: false): Choose the recommended medical procedure (e.g., Eye Exam, Referral to Specialist, Surgery).
* **Follow Up** (<name>, date, required: false): Set a follow-up appointment date.
* **Doctor Notes** (<name>, text, required: false): Add any additional doctor notes or comments.
* **Patient Comments** (<name>, text, required: false): Record any comments or concerns from the patient.
* **Doctor Comments** (<name>, text, required: false): Include any comments or observations from the doctor.

## Tips
* Ensure that you complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure all required and optional fields are filled.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the medical staff or patient's doctor for clarification.
