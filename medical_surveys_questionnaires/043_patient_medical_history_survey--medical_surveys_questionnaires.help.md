# Patient Medical History Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Medical History Survey is a form designed to gather information about a patient's medical history, including current medical conditions, medications, allergies, and family medical history. This data can help healthcare providers make informed decisions about patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. **Medical History**
	* Enter a brief description of your current medical conditions, including any chronic illnesses, injuries, or surgeries.
2. **Current Medications**
	* Select all medications you are currently taking, including prescription medications, vitamins, and supplements.
3. **Allergies**
	* Select any allergies you have, including food, environmental, or medication allergies.
4. **Family History**
	* Enter a brief description of your family medical history, including any chronic illnesses or conditions that affect your relatives.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): A brief description of your current medical conditions, including any chronic illnesses, injuries, or surgeries.

### Current Medications
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all medications you are currently taking, including prescription medications, vitamins, and supplements.
	+ Options: Medication A, Medication B, Medication C

### Allergies
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select any allergies you have, including food, environmental, or medication allergies.
	+ Options: Allergy A, Allergy B

### Family History
* **Family History** (`family_history`, `text`, required/optional): A brief description of your family medical history, including any chronic illnesses or conditions that affect your relatives.

## Tips
* Ensure you have the patient's consent before collecting their medical history information.
* Review and update this form regularly to reflect any changes in the patient's medical history.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the patient or their healthcare provider.
