# long_term_medication_safety_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The long-term medication safety evaluation form is designed to collect information about a patient's experiences with their medication, enabling healthcare professionals to assess the effectiveness and safety of the treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
2. Ensure that you're providing up-to-date information.
3. If you're unsure about any question, contact your healthcare provider for guidance.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medication Safety** (1, text, required): This field is for you to describe your experiences with the medication, including any concerns or issues you have encountered.
* **Side Effects** (2, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options to describe any side effects you've experienced:
	+ Option 1: Mild side effects, such as dizziness or nausea.
	+ Option 2: Moderate side effects, such as pain or fatigue.
	+ Option 3: Severe side effects, such as allergic reactions or organ damage.
* **Adherence Patterns** (3, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply to describe your adherence patterns with the medication:
	+ Option 1: Taking the medication as prescribed.
	+ Option 2: Missing doses or taking less frequently than prescribed.
* **Overall Experience** (4, time, optional): If you'd like to provide more specific information about your overall experience with the medication, feel free to enter a date and time.
* **Patient Feedback** (5, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the medication.
* **Medication Side Effects** (6, number, optional): If you've experienced any side effects, please report their frequency (e.g., 1-3 times a week).
* **Medication Dosage** (7, number, optional): If you've had to adjust your medication dosage, please report the new dosage.
* **Medical Team Feedback** (8, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options to describe the feedback from your medical team:
	+ Option 1: Positive feedback, such as improvement in condition.
	+ Option 2: Negative feedback, such as need for adjustment.
* **Additional Comments** (9, note, optional): Share any additional thoughts or concerns you have about your medication or treatment.
* **Patient Info** (10, text, optional): If you'd like to provide more information about yourself, such as your age or medical history, feel free to do so.
* **Email** (11, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Phone** (12, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Date** (13, date, optional): If you'd like to report when you started taking the medication.
* **Time** (14, time, optional): If you'd like to report the time of day you take your medication.
* **Medical Team Comments** (15, note, optional): Share any comments or concerns you have about your medical team's feedback.
* **Patient Name** (16, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your name or nickname for reference.

Please ensure that you've answered all required questions and provided accurate information before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider.
