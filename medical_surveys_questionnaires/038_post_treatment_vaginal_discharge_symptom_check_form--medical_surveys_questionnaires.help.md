# post-treatment-vaginal-discharge-symptom-check-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's symptoms after treatment, helping healthcare professionals track and manage post-treatment follow-ups.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the symptoms the patient is currently experiencing from the list provided.
2.  Indicate how many days have passed since the treatment.
3.  Choose how often the symptoms have been present.
4.  Rate the severity of the symptoms.
5.  Enter the date and time for the follow-up appointment.
6.  Provide the phone number and method of follow-up (email or phone).
7.  Enter any additional comments or remarks.
8.  Confirm the follow-up status.
9.  Indicate whether the patient will need follow-up care.
10. Enter any remarks for the next follow-up appointment.
11. Enter any additional notes.
