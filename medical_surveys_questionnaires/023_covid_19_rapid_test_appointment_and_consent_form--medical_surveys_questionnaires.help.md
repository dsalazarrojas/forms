# COVID 19 Rapid Test Appointment And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "COVID 19 Rapid Test Appointment And Consent Form" is a crucial document that requires patients to provide their demographic information, select an appointment date and time, and provide consent for the testing process. This form is used to ensure that patients are aware of the test's purpose, the collection of their specimens, and the sharing of their results with public health authorities if necessary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Patient Full Name**, which is required to identify you as a patient.
2. Provide your **Date of Birth**, which is also required for identification purposes.
3. Enter your **Mobile Phone Number**, which will be used for appointment confirmation via SMS.
4. Enter your **Email Address**, which will be used to communicate with you about your test results and appointment.
5. Select your preferred **Appointment Date and Time** for the test. Please note that you will be contacted for confirmation via SMS.
6. Choose the **Reason for Test**, which can be for Symptoms, Travel, Exposure, Work/School Requirement, or Screening.
7. Answer **Are you currently experiencing any symptoms?** with either "True" or "False". This is essential for the healthcare professionals to assess your condition.
8. Read the **Informed Consent** section carefully before agreeing to the terms.
9. Confirm that you **consent to the collection of a specimen for COVID-19 testing**.
10. Confirm that you **consent to your results being shared with public health authorities as required by law**.
11. Acknowledge that you are **responsible for any fees not covered by insurance**.
12. Read and agree to the **HIPAA Privacy Policy**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name (1)**: This is the name of the person being tested.
* **Date of Birth (2)**: This is the date of birth of the person being tested.
* **Mobile Phone Number (3)**: This number will be used to confirm your appointment via SMS.
* **Email Address (4)**: This is the address where you will receive communication about your test results and appointment.
* **Appointment Selection (5)**: This is where you select your preferred appointment date and time.
* **Preferred Appointment Date (6)**: This is the date you have selected for the test.
* **Preferred Appointment Time (7)**: This is the time you have selected for the test.
* **Reason for Test (8)**: This is why you are requesting the COVID-19 test.
* **Are you currently experiencing any symptoms? (9)**: This is a question to assess your current health status.
* **Informed Consent (10)**: This is a section to read before agreeing to the testing process.
* **Testing Consent (11)**: This is where you confirm your consent for specimen collection.
* **Result Sharing Consent (12)**: This is where you confirm your consent to share your results with public health authorities.
* **Fee Acknowledgement (13)**: This is where you acknowledge your responsibility for any fees not covered by insurance.
* **Privacy Policy (14)**: This is where you agree to the HIPAA privacy policy.
