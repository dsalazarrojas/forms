# COVID 19 Booster Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Booster Vaccine Consent Form is a document used to gather information about patients' medical history, vaccine information, and consent for vaccination. This form is essential for healthcare providers to make informed decisions about vaccination and ensure patient safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Read each question carefully and answer according to your medical history and vaccine information.
2.  For questions with multiple options, select the correct response that best describes your situation.
3.  Enter your full name and contact information accurately.
4.  Review each section carefully to ensure you have provided all necessary information.
5.  Sign the form to indicate your consent for vaccination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Patient Name (1) 
**Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name.

### 2. Date of Birth (2)
**Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM-DD-YYYY.

### 3. Gender (3)
**Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the following options:
*   Male
*   Female
*   Other
*   Prefer Not to Say

### 4. Email Address (4)
**Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your contact email address for records.

### 5. Phone Number (5)
**Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your best contact number.

### 6. Street Address (6)
**Street Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your complete street address.

### 7. City (7)
**City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city of your residence.

### 8. State (8)
**State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province where you reside.

### 9. ZIP Code (9)
**ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your postal code.

### 10. Emergency Contact Name (10)
**Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.

### 11. Emergency Contact Phone (11)
**Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.

### 12. Vaccine Information (12)
**Vaccine Information** (`vaccine_information`, note, optional): Read this section carefully and take note of any important information about the vaccine.

### 13. Understand Vaccine Type (13)
**I Understand Vaccine Type** (`understand_vaccine_type`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
*   Yes I Understand
*   False

### 14. Understand Benefits (14)
**I Understand Benefits** (`understand_benefits`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
*   Yes I Understand
*   False

### 15. Understand Risks (15)
**I Understand Risks** (`understand_risks`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
*   Yes I Understand
*   False

### 16. Common Side Effects (16)
**Common Side Effects Acknowledgment** (`common_side_effects`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
*   Yes I Understand
*   False

### 17. Rare Side Effects (17)
**Rare Side Effects Acknowledgment** (`rare_side_effects`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
*   Yes I Understand
*   False

### 18. Medical History Review (18)
**Medical History Review** (`medical_history`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
*   Reviewed with provider
*   Self-reported
*   Not reviewed

### 19. Allergies (19)
**Severe Allergies** (`allergies`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
*   No known severe allergies
*   Yes - Vaccine components
*   Yes - Medications
*   Yes - Other

### 20. Allergy Details (20)
**Allergy Details** (`allergy_details`, text, optional): Enter details about your severe allergies.

### 21. Previous COVID-19 Vaccine (21)
**Previous COVID-19 Vaccine** (`previous_covid_vaccine`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
*   Yes - Completed series
*   Yes - Partially vaccinated
*   False

### 22. Previous Vaccine Reaction (22)
**Previous Vaccine Reaction** (`previous_vaccine_reaction`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
*   False
*   Yes - Mild
*   Yes - Severe

### 23. Current Medications (23)
**Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): List all your current medications.

### 24. Medical Conditions (24)
**Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Report any chronic conditions.

### 25. Pregnancy Status (25)
**Pregnancy or Breastfeeding** (`pregnancy_status`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
*   Not pregnant or breastfeeding
*   Pregnant
*   Breastfeeding
*   Trying to conceive
*   Not applicable

### 26. Immunocompromised Status (26)
**Immunocompromised Status** (`immunocompromised`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
*   False
*   True
*   Unsure

### 27. Bleeding Disorder (27)
**Bleeding Disorder** (`bleeding_disorder`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
*   False
*   True

### 28. Current Illness (28)
**Current Illness** (`current_illness`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
*   No - Feeling well
*   Yes - Mild illness
*   Yes - Moderate to severe

### 29. Voluntary Consent (29)
**Voluntary Consent** (`voluntary_consent`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
*   Yes I Consent
*   False

### 30. Opportunity for Questions (30)
**Opportunity for Questions** (`opportunity_for_questions`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
*   Yes - All answered
*   Yes - Most answered
*   No - Still have questions

### 31. Data Sharing Consent (31)
**Data Sharing Consent** (`data_sharing_consent`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
*   Yes I Consent
*   False

### 32. Vaccine Registry Registration (32)
**Vaccine Registry Registration** (`vaxtrack_registration`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
*   True
*   False

### 33. Patient Signature (33)
**Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required): Type your full name for signature.

### 34. Parent or Guardian Signature (34)
**Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_guardian_signature`, text, optional): If under 18, enter your parent or guardian's signature.

### 35. Witness Name (35)
**Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your witness.

### 36. Witness Signature (36)
**Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, text, optional): Enter your witness's signature.

### 37. Healthcare Provider Name (37)
**Healthcare Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.

### 38. Provider Signature (38)
**Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, text, optional): Enter your healthcare provider's signature.

### 39. Date Signed (39)
**Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date you signed this form.

### 40. Vaccine Lot Number (40)
**Vaccine Lot Number** (`vaccine_lot_number`, text, optional): Enter the vaccine lot number for your vaccination.

### 41. Vaccination Site (41)
**Vaccination Site** (`vaccination_site`, text, optional): Enter the vaccination site.

## Tips
*   Please review each section carefully before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any question, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
*   Complete the form honestly and accurately.
*   Sign and date the form when you have completed it.
*   Give the form to your healthcare provider for review and vaccination.
