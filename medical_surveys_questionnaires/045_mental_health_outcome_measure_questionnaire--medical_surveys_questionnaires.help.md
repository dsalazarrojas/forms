# Mental Health Outcome Measure Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a mental health outcome measure questionnaire used to assess an individual's mental health status across various conditions. It is designed to help healthcare professionals gather information for mental health evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If you are unsure about any question, consult with a healthcare professional.
3. Complete each section according to the instructions provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Condition Description** (id: 2, label: medical condition description, required: false, type: text): Please describe your current medical condition or situation that is affecting your mental health.
* **Mental Health Status** (id: 3, label: mental health status, required: false, type: select_one):
	+ Select the option that best describes your current mental health status:
		- Anxiety
		- Depression
		- Bipolar
		- Schizophrenia
		- ADHD
		- Autism
		- OCD
		- Personality Disorder
* **Anxiety Score** (id: 4, label: anxiety score, required: false, type: number): Please enter a score between 0 and 10 to indicate your current anxiety level.

* **Bipolar Score** (id: 6, label: bipolar score, required: false, type: number): Please enter a score between 0 and 10 to indicate your current bipolar level.
* **Schizophrenia Score** (id: 7, label: schizophrenia score, required: false, type: number): Please enter a score between 0 and 10 to indicate your current schizophrenia level.
* **ADHD Score** (id: 8, label: adhd score, required: false, type: number): Please enter a score between 0 and 10 to indicate your current ADHD level.
* **Autism Score** (id: 9, label: autism score, required: false, type: number): Please enter a score between 0 and 10 to indicate your current autism level.
* **OCD Score** (id: 10, label: ocd score, required: false, type: number): Please enter a score between 0 and 10 to indicate your current OCD level.

## Tips

* If you are unsure about any question, consult with a healthcare professional.
