# COVID 19 Exposure Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you understand and acknowledge your potential exposure to COVID-19 and your responsibilities in monitoring and reporting symptoms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Please review each section carefully and answer all the required questions.
2.  If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact your healthcare provider.
3.  Complete the form honestly and accurately to ensure that you and your healthcare provider can take the necessary steps to protect your health and the health of others.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required)
Enter the date on which you are signing this agreement.

*   Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YY.

### 2. **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required)
Enter your full legal name.

*   Please enter your name exactly as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.

### 3. **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required)
Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.

### 4. **Patient ID/Medical Record Number** (`patient_id`, text, optional)
Enter your patient ID or medical record number if applicable.

### 5. **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required)
Enter your email address for contact.

*   Please ensure that it is accurate and active.

### 6. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required)
Enter your phone number for contact.

*   Please ensure that it is accurate and active.

### 7. **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider`, text, required)
Enter the name of your healthcare provider.

*   If you do not have a healthcare provider, please contact your local health department for assistance.

### 8. **Type of COVID-19 Exposure** (`exposure_type`, select_one, required)
Choose the type of COVID-19 exposure you experienced.

*   Healthcare worker exposure
*   Household exposure
*   Community exposure
*   Workplace exposure
*   Travel-related exposure
*   Unknown exposure
*   Other

### 9. **Date of Exposure** (`exposure_date`, date, optional)
Enter the date on which the exposure occurred.

*   If you are unsure, leave this field blank and provide the date when you have more information.

### 10. **Details of Exposure** (`exposure_details`, text, required)
Describe the exposure situation.

*   Please be as detailed as possible to help your healthcare provider understand the situation.

### 11. **Duration of Exposure** (`duration_exposure`, text, optional)
Enter the duration of the exposure.

*   If you are unsure, leave this field blank and provide the duration when you have more information.

### 12. **Location of Exposure** (`exposure_location`, text, optional)
Enter the location where the exposure occurred.

*   If you are unsure, leave this field blank and provide the location when you have more information.

### 13. **Number of Individuals Involved** (`individuals_involved`, number, optional)
Enter the number of individuals you were exposed to.

*   If you are unsure, leave this field blank and provide the number when you have more information.

### 14. **Personal Protective Equipment Worn** (`ppe_worn`, text, optional)
Enter the personal protective equipment worn during the exposure.

*   If you do not know, leave this field blank and provide the information when you have more.

### 15. **I Acknowledge the Risk of COVID-19 Exposure** (`exposure_acknowledgment`, select_one, required)
Acknowledge that you understand the risk of COVID-19 exposure.

*   I acknowledge
*   I do not acknowledge

### 16. **I Understand COVID-19 Symptoms** (`symptoms_understanding`, select_one, required)
Do you understand the symptoms of COVID-19?

*   Yes, I understand
*   No, I do not understand
*   Have questions

### 17. **I Agree to Monitor Myself for Symptoms** (`monitoring_agreement`, select_one, required)
Agree to monitor yourself for symptoms.

*   I agree
*   I do not agree
*   Have questions

### 18. **I Understand Quarantine/Isolation Requirements** (`quarantine_understanding`, select_one, required)
Understand the quarantine/isolation requirements.

*   Yes, I understand
*   No, I do not understand
*   Have questions

### 19. **I Agree to Be Tested if Symptoms Develop** (`testing_agreement`, select_one, required)
Agree to be tested if symptoms develop.

*   I agree
*   I do not agree
*   Conditional agreement

### 20. **I Will Report Symptoms Immediately** (`reporting_agreement`, select_one, required)
Agree to report symptoms immediately.

*   I agree
*   I do not agree
*   Have questions

### 21. **I Consent to Contact Tracing** (`contact_tracing`, select_one, optional)
Agree to contact tracing.

*   I consent
*   I do not consent
*   Have questions

### 22. **I Understand Privacy Protections** (`confidentiality_understanding`, select_one, required)
Understand the confidentiality protections.

*   Yes, I understand
*   No, I do not understand
*   Have questions

### 23. **I Release the Healthcare Provider from Liability** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, required)
Agree to release the healthcare provider from liability.

*   I agree
*   I do not agree
*   Have questions

### 24. **I Provide Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, select_one, required)
Agree to provide informed consent.

*   I consent
*   I do not consent
*   Need clarification

### 25. **Signature Date** (`agreement_date_signature`, date, required)
Enter the date you are signing this agreement.

*   Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YY.

Please review this form carefully and answer all the questions honestly and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider before submitting this form.
