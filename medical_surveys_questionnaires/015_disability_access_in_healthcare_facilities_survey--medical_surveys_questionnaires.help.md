# Disability Access In Healthcare Facilities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experiences with accessibility in healthcare facilities. Your feedback is essential to help us improve the accessibility of healthcare facilities and ensure that all patients, regardless of their abilities, can have equal access to healthcare.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as they appear below. Please ensure that you have visited or will be visiting a healthcare facility in the past or upcoming 6 months.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`General Info`, `text`, optional): Please provide a brief description of your experience with the healthcare facility's accessibility.
* **Patient Info** (`Patient Info`, `text`, optional): If you are completing this form on behalf of another patient, please enter their name and date of birth.
* **Accessibility Barriers** (`Accessibility Barriers`, `text`, optional): Describe any barriers you experienced while visiting or accessing the healthcare facility.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`Suggestions for Improvement`, `text`, optional): Share your suggestions on how the healthcare facility can improve their accessibility.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`Healthcare Provider`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the healthcare providers that you experienced or interacted with during your visit.
* **Healthcare Providers** (`Healthcare Providers`, `select_one`, optional): Select the primary healthcare provider that you interacted with during your visit.
* **Healthcare Provider Info** (`Healthcare Provider Info`, `text`, optional): Provide additional information about your healthcare provider interaction, if needed.
* **Accessibility Barriers Question** (`Accessibility Barriers Question`, `text`, optional): Answer this question to help us understand your experience with accessibility barriers in healthcare facilities.
* **Suggestions for Improvement Question** (`Suggestions for Improvement Question`, `text`, optional): Share your suggestions for improvement, if you have any.
* **Date of Visit** (`Date of Visit`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you visited the healthcare facility.
* **Time of Visit** (`Time of Visit`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of visit for the healthcare facility.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, optional): Provide your contact information so that we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted about your feedback.

Note: Fields marked as required are essential for completing the form.
