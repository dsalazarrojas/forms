# Nursing Home Enquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Nursing Home Enquiry Form is a tool used to gather information from potential customers or existing customers who want to contact or visit a nursing home. This form helps our staff understand their needs and preferences, which aids in providing the best possible care and service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the second field.
3. Provide your Phone Number in the third field.
4. Choose the best time to contact you in the fourth field. You can select from the available time slots or enter a custom time.
5. Select the Type of Enquiry that best describes your purpose for visiting or contacting the nursing home. You can choose from New enquiry, Follow-up enquiry, or Other.
6. Enter your message or comments in the last field, describing your reasons for visiting or contacting the nursing home.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (<name>, <type>, required: false)
The First Name field is where you enter your given name, as used by most people. This is the first field on the form and is not mandatory. Please fill this in for the best results.

### Last Name (<name>, <type>, required: false)
The Last Name field is for your surname or family name. Like the First Name field, this is also not required but helps us understand your full name for our records.

### Phone Number (<name>, <type>, required: false)
Enter your contact phone number in this field so we can get in touch with you easily.

### Best Time to Contact (<name>, <type>, required: false)
Select the time of day that suits you best for us to contact you. You can choose from the available times or enter a custom time.

### Type of Enquiry (<name>, <type>, required: false)
Choose the reason you are visiting or contacting the nursing home. This helps us prepare for your visit or enquiry.

### Customer's Message (<name>, <type>, required: false)
Enter your message or comments here, so we can tailor our services to your needs.

# Tips
* Please fill in all required fields for the best possible service.
* Be as detailed as possible in the message field for accurate preparation.
* If you have any specific requests or needs, mention them in your message.
