# Mental Health Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's mental health status. It includes questions about their feeling scale, sleep quality, energy level, stress level, anxiety level, mood, and mental health provider. The purpose of this form is to gather data that can be used to understand the mental health of an individual and provide support where necessary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing honest and accurate information about your mental health status.
2. For questions with scale or selection options, choose the option that best represents your current state.
3. If you need more space to provide additional information or comments, use the 'note' field to type your thoughts.
4. If you don't have a preferred contact method, you can leave the 'email' and 'phone' fields blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **mental_health_survey** (1): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or details about your mental health status.
* **feeling_scale** (2): This question asks you to rate your current feeling scale. Please choose the option that best represents your current emotional state.
* **sleep_quality** (3): This question asks about your sleep quality. Please select the option that best describes your sleep quality over the past week.
* **energy_level** (4): This question asks you to choose the energy levels that best describe your current energy levels. You can select one or multiple options.
* **stress_level** (5): This question asks you to rate your stress level. Please choose the option that best describes your current stress level.
* **anxiety_level** (6): This question asks you to rate your anxiety level. Please choose the option that best describes your current anxiety level.
* **mood** (7): This question asks you to choose the option that best describes your current mood.
* **mental_health_provider** (8): This question asks you to choose the mental health provider you are currently working with.
* **note** (9): This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional comments or notes about your mental health status.
* **email** (10): This is an optional email field where you can provide your email address if you want to be contacted.
* **phone** (11): This is an optional phone field where you can provide your phone number if you want to be contacted.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
* If you need more space to provide additional information, use the 'note' field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the mental health provider you selected.
