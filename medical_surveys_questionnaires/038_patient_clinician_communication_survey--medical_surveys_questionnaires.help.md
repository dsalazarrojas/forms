# Patient Clinician Communication Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Clinician Communication Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from patients about their last visit with a clinician. This survey aims to evaluate the quality of communication between the patient and the clinician, as well as other aspects of the visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all the relevant options that apply to your last visit.
2. Provide a brief description in the text fields where required.
3. Answer all questions marked as "required".
4. Skip the fields that are not applicable to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you rate the quality of your last visit** (1): Rate the quality of your last visit from 1 to 5.
* **How would you rate the clinician's communication** (2): Rate the clinician's communication from 1 to 5.
* **Which clinician was your provider** (3): Select all the clinicians who were involved in your last visit.
* **What was the main issue you came to visit for** (4): Select all the reasons you came to visit for (e.g., appointment, follow-up, etc.).
* **How long was your wait time** (5): Enter the length of time you waited before seeing the clinician.
* **What was the time of your last visit** (6): Enter the time of your last visit.
* **What was the date of your last visit** (7): Enter the date of your last visit.
* **What were the main issues discussed during your last visit** (8): Select all the issues discussed during your last visit.
* **What were the outcomes discussed during your last visit** (9): Select all the outcomes discussed during your last visit.
* **What did your provider explain to you during your last visit** (10): Provide a brief description of what your provider explained to you.
* **Comments** (11): Provide any additional comments about your last visit.
* **Email Address** (12): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Phone Number** (13): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Fax Number** (14): Enter your fax number (if applicable).
* **What were the next steps planned during your last visit** (15): Provide a brief description of the next steps planned.
* **Did your provider follow up with you after your last visit** (16): Select yes or no.
* **What did your provider ask you to do during your last visit** (17): Select all the tasks your provider asked you to do.
* **What was the cause for your last visit** (18): Select all the reasons for your last visit.
* **What was the outcome of your last visit** (19): Select all the outcomes of your last visit.
* **Did your provider check for any other conditions** (20): Select yes or no.
* **How long did you wait in the wait room** (21): Enter the length of time you waited in the wait room.
* **Did you have to wait in the wait room before your visit** (22): Select yes or no.
* **What did your provider explain about your condition** (23): Provide a brief description of what your provider explained.
* **What did your provider explain to you about your test results** (24): Provide a brief description of what your provider explained.
* **Provider** (25): Select all the clinicians who were involved in your last visit.
