# Pre Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Screening Form is designed to gather employment information from applicants for medical screening purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please provide the following information:
- Enter the applicant's First Name in the "First Name" field.
- Enter the applicant's Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
- Enter the applicant's Phone number in the "Phone" field.
- Select the type of address the applicant prefers to use for contact: "Home Address", "Company Address", or "Mailing Address".
- Indicate if the applicant is a job applicant. Select one of the options provided.
- Indicate the applicant's employment status: "Employed", "Unemployed", or "Retired".
- Select the type of employment: this field is currently optional and does not have a description.
- Enter the applicant's Job Title.
- Enter the dates of the applicant's employment, if applicable.
- Select the type of work experience the applicant has, if applicable.
- Select the types of job duties the applicant has, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`contact_information_1`, text, required): Enter the applicant's First Name.
- **Last Name** (`contact_information_2`, text, required): Enter the applicant's Last Name.
- **Phone** (`contact_information_3`, number, required): Enter the applicant's Phone number.
- **Address** (`address`, select_one, required): Select the type of address the applicant prefers to use for contact: "Home Address", "Company Address", or "Mailing Address".
- **Job Applicant** (`job_applicant`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if the applicant is a job applicant.
- **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Indicate the applicant's employment status: "Employed", "Unemployed", or "Retired".
- **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, text, required): Enter the type of employment. (This field is currently optional and does not have a description.)
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the applicant's Job Title.
- **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, time, required): Enter the applicant's work experience. (This is a date and time field. Enter in the MM/DD/YY format.)
- **Job Duties** (`job_duties_1`, select_one, required): Select the types of job duties the applicant has.
- **Job Duties 2** (`job_duties_2`, select_one, required): Select the types of job duties the applicant has.
- **Note** (`note`, note, required): Enter any additional information about the applicant.
- **Address 2** (`contact_information_4`, text, required): Enter the applicant's second address.
- **Contact Information 2** (`contact_information_5`, text, required): Enter the applicant's second contact information.
- **Contact Information 3** (`contact_information_6`, text, required): Enter the applicant's third contact information.
- **Job Duties 3** (`job_duties_3`, select_one, required): Select the types of job duties the applicant has.
- **Contact Information 4** (`contact_information_7`, text, required): Enter the applicant's fourth contact information.
- **Contact Information 5** (`contact_information_8`, text, required): Enter the applicant's fifth contact information.

Note: The fields with "required: false" are optional and do not need to be filled in order to complete the form.
