# surgical_technology_clinical_slot_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The surgical technology clinical slot survey is a form designed to collect information from students about their preferences and availability for clinical rotations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred options for student preferences, such as clinical rotations and settings.
2. Provide your availability for clinical rotations.
3. Choose your preferred rotations.
4. Enter your student ID number.
5. Provide your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Add any additional comments about your preferences or availability.
8. Click the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Preferences** (Student Preferences, note, optional): This section allows you to provide information about your preferences for clinical rotations, such as your preferred settings and rotations.
- **Availability** (Availability, date, optional): Enter your availability for clinical rotations. This will help the organizers schedule rotations accordingly.
- **Rotation Preferences** (Rotation Preferences, select multiple, optional): Choose your preferred clinical rotations from the options provided.
- **Clinical Rotation** (Clinical Rotation, select one, optional): Select one of the clinical rotations that you prefer.
- **Student ID** (Student ID, number, optional): Enter your student ID number. This is a required field for the system to track your information.
- **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address. This is useful for the organizers to contact you with further information.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number. This is optional but helps the organizers to contact you with any updates.
- **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information that you think is relevant to your preferences or availability.
- **Submit** (Submit, text, optional): Click this button to submit your form once you have completed all the required fields.
