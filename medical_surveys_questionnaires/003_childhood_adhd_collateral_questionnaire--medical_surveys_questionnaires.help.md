# Childhood ADHD Collateral Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Childhood ADHD Collateral Questionnaire is a form used to gather information about a child's behavior and symptoms related to Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD). The form is used to support a diagnosis and treatment plan for the child.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Respondent Information**: Please fill in your personal contact information and specify your relationship to the child (Parent, Guardian, Teacher, School Counselor, Other Caregiver).
2. **Child Information**: Enter the child's name and age in years.
3. **Observed Period**: Rate the length of time you have observed the child's behavior and symptoms.
4. **Attention and Concentration**: Rate the child's difficulty sustaining attention in tasks or activities, completing assigned tasks without supervision, and other relevant behaviors.
5. **Hyperactivity and Impulsivity**: Rate the child's tendency to exhibit impulsive behaviors such as fidgeting, interrupting, excessive talking, and blurting out answers.
6. **Overall Impression**: Provide an overall assessment of the child's behavior and symptoms.
7. **Additional Concerns**: If you have any additional concerns or observations about the child's behavior and symptoms, please enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name**: Your full name as the person completing this questionnaire.
* **Respondent Relationship**: Specify your relationship to the child.
* **Respondent Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Child Name**: Enter the child's full name.
* **Child Age**: Enter the child's age in years.
* **Observed Period**: Rate the length of time you have observed the child's behavior and symptoms (Less than 3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, More than 2 years).
* **Difficulty Sustaining Attention**: Rate on scale 1 (not at all) to 5 (very often) how often the child has difficulty sustaining attention in tasks or activities.
* **Fails to Complete Tasks**: Rate on scale 1 (not at all) to 5 (very often) how often the child fails to complete assigned tasks without supervision.
* **Listening Skills**: Rate on scale 1 (not at all) to 5 (very often) how often the child appears not to listen when spoken to directly.
* **Organizing Skills**: Rate on scale 1 (not at all) to 5 (very often) how often the child has difficulty organizing tasks and activities.
* **Forgetfulness**: Rate on scale 1 (not at all) to 5 (very often) how often the child is forgetful in daily activities.
* **Fidgeting**: Rate on scale 1 (not at all) to 5 (very often) how often the child fidgets with hands, feet, or squirms in seat.
* **Leaving Seat**: Rate on scale 1 (not at all) to 5 (very often) how often the child leaves their seat when expected to remain seated.
* **Excessive Talking**: Rate on scale 1 (not at all) to 5 (very often) how often the child talks excessively.
* **Impulsive Answers**: Rate on scale 1 (not at all) to 5 (very often) how often the child blurt's out answers before questions are finished.
* **Turn-taking**: Rate on scale 1 (not at all) to 5 (very often) how often the child has difficulty waiting turns in activities.
* **Interrupting**: Rate on scale 1 (not at all) to 5 (very often) how often the child interrupts or intrudes on others.
* **Overall Severity**: Rate the overall severity of observed symptoms (None, Mild, Moderate, Severe).
* **Additional Concerns**: Provide any additional concerns or observations about the child's behavior and symptoms. 

## Tips

* Please answer questions based on your observations and experiences with the child.
* If you are unsure or lack information, rate "Not Applicable" or enter "None" for symptoms you have not observed.
* Please be as specific as possible when describing the child's behavior and symptoms.
