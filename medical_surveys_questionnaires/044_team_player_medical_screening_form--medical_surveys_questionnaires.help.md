# Team Player Medical Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Player Medical Screening Form is a crucial tool used to collect essential medical information from team players to ensure their health and safety while participating in team activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the required information.
2. Fill in the Team Player Information page with your details.
3. Provide Emergency Contact Information, including your emergency contact's phone number and relation.
4. Select your online consent status, indicating whether you have a valid medical insurance or if you need to specify other insurance details.
5. If required, provide additional medical history information on the Medical History Information page.
6. Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Player Information**: (`team_player_medical_screening_form_page` , text, required/optional): This section asks for your basic information, including your name, contact details, and other relevant details. Please fill in your correct name and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Information**: (`team_player_medical_screening_form_page_2` , text, required/optional): Provide the phone number and relation of your emergency contact. This is crucial in case of an incident.
* **Medical History Information**: (`team_player_medical_screening_form_page_3` , text, required/optional): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, please provide details on the Medical History Information page. This helps us understand your health risks better.
* **Online Consent**: (`team_player_medical_screening_form_page_4` , select_one, required): Select the status of your online insurance:
	+ I do not have a valid medical insurance
	+ I have a valid medical insurance
	+ I have a valid medical insurance with a copay
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Additional Information** (`team_player_medical_screening_form_page_5` , text, required/optional): If you need to add any additional information, please use this section to provide details.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone` , text, required/optional): Provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation` , text, required): Specify your relation with your emergency contact.
