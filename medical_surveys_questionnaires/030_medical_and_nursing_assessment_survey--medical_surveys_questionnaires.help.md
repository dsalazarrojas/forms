# Medical And Nursing Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's medical history, allergies, and condition to provide a thorough assessment and treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Provide any contact information in the "contact_info" field, if necessary.
3. Describe the patient's medical history in the "medical_history" field.
4. Indicate whether the patient has any allergies in the "allergies" field. Select all applicable options (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
5. Indicate whether the patient has a medical condition in the "medical_condition" field. Select all applicable options (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
6. If the patient has a medical condition, describe the type of condition in the "medical_condition_type" field.
7. If applicable, list any medications the patient is currently taking in the "medication" field.
8. Describe the patient's surgical history in the "surgical_history" field.
9. Click "submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name** (text, required/optional): Enter the patient's full name.
* **contact_info** (text, required/optional): Enter any relevant contact information, such as phone number or email.
* **medical_history** (text, required/optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, treatments, or surgeries.
* **allergies** (select multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether the patient has any allergies. Select "Yes" or "No" for each applicable condition.
* **medical_condition** (select one, required/optional): Indicate whether the patient has a medical condition. Select "Yes" or "No".
* **medical_condition_type** (text, required/optional): If the patient has a medical condition, describe the type of condition.
* **medication** (text, required/optional): If applicable, list any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **surgical_history** (text, required/optional): Describe the patient's surgical history, including any relevant procedures or complications.
* **surgical_history** is likely a duplicate field, as it is not required, and its label is not clearly defined. Consider removing or re-labeling this field.
