# COVID 19 2020 Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details (Full Name, Date of Birth, Email Address, Phone Number, and Physical Address).
2.  Choose your availability and volunteer options.
3.  Provide any relevant healthcare credentials.
4.  Specify your language spoken (if applicable).
5.  Describe any past experience with volunteering.
6.  Share your motivation for volunteering.
7.  Disclose your current health status.
8.  Indicate if you've been tested for COVID-19.
9.  Specify if you are willing to submit to a background check.
10.  Provide an emergency contact's name and contact information.
11.  Choose a date for the registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Full Name (id: 1) 
Full Name (`volunteer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification.

### Date of Birth (id: 2)
Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your birthdate as MM/DD/YYYY.

### Email Address (id: 3)
Email Address (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address where we can contact you.

### Phone Number (id: 4)
Phone Number (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your primary contact number.

### Street Address (id: 5)
Street Address (`street_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your physical address where we can reach you.

### City (id: 6)
City (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter your city of residence.

### State/Province (id: 7)
State/Province (`state`, text, required/optional): Enter your state or province of residence.

### Postal Code (id: 8)
Postal Code (`postal_code`, text, required/optional): Enter your postal code of residence.

### Type of Volunteer Service You Can Provide (id: 9)
Type of Volunteer Service You Can Provide (`volunteer_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the areas where you can volunteer.

### Do you have a medical license or healthcare credentials? (id: 10)
Do you have a medical license or healthcare credentials (`medical_license`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' if you have medical credentials.

### If yes, specify your credentials (id: 11)
If yes, specify your credentials (`medical_credentials`, text, required/optional): Enter your medical credentials.

### Availability for Volunteer Work (id: 12)
Availability for Volunteer Work (`availability`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the days and times you are available.

### Hours Per Week You Can Commit (id: 13)
Hours Per Week You Can Commit (`hours_per_week`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of hours you can commit per week.

### Do you have reliable transportation? (id: 14)
Do you have reliable transportation (`transportation`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes', 'No', or 'Sometimes' to indicate your transportation situation.

### Languages Spoken (id: 15)
Languages Spoken (`language_spoken`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the languages you can speak.

### Previous Volunteer Experience (id: 16)
Previous Volunteer Experience (`previous_volunteer_experience`, text, required/optional): Describe any past volunteering work you've done.

### What motivates you to volunteer? (id: 17)
What motivates you to volunteer? (`motivation`, text, required/optional): Tell us why you want to volunteer.

### Current Health Status (id: 18)
Current Health Status (`health_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select your current health status.

### Have you been tested for COVID-19? (id: 19)
Have you been tested for COVID-19? (`covid_test`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether you've been tested or not.

### Are you willing to submit to a background check? (id: 20)
Are you willing to submit to a background check? (`background_check`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes', 'No', or 'Have Questions'.

### Emergency Contact Name (id: 21)
Emergency Contact Name (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Phone (id: 22)
Emergency Contact Phone (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.

### Registration Date (id: 23)
Registration Date (`registration_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you register.

# Tips
* Please be honest when filling out this form.
* Make sure to answer all required questions.
* If you have any issues filling out this form, please contact us for assistance.
