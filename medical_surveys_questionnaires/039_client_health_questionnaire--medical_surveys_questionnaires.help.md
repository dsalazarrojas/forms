# Client Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Health Questionnaire is designed to collect comprehensive medical information from clients, including their demographics, medical history, and lifestyle habits. This information will be used to help healthcare providers make informed decisions and provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details, including your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your date of birth and gender.
3. Answer questions about your medical history, including any chronic conditions you may have, current medications, and allergies.
4. Answer questions about your lifestyle habits, including your exercise habits, diet, and sleep patterns.
5. Confirm that all information provided is accurate and truthful.
