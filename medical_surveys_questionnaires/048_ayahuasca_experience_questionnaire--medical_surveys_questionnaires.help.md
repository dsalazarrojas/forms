# Ayahuasca Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experience with Ayahuasca, a plant-based psychedelic substance used in traditional healing and spiritual practices. The questionnaire aims to gather data on the effects of Ayahuasca, including emotional, physical, and visual sensations, as well as your overall experience and willingness to participate in future ceremonies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out your demographic information, including your full name, date of birth, and email address. This information will help us understand your background and demographic profile.
2. Describe your intentions and goals for the ceremony, and rate your overall experience, including its intensity and difficulty.
3. Select all sensations and emotions you experienced during the ceremony, and briefly describe the most significant takeaway or insight you gained from your experience.
4. Indicate your current emotional state and whether you plan to participate in future Ayahuasca ceremonies.
5. Provide any additional comments or observations about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name, as seen on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`dob_aya`, date, **required**): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Email Address** (`email_address_aya`, email, **required**): Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you for future research opportunities.
* **Date of Ceremony** (`ceremony_date_aya`, date, **required**): Enter the date of your Ayahuasca ceremony.
* **Location of Ceremony** (`ceremony_location_aya`, text, **required**): Enter the location where you took the Ayahuasca ceremony.
* **Facilitator or Shaman Name** (`facilitator_name_aya`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the facilitator or shaman who led your ceremony.
* **Intent for the Ceremony** (`ceremony_intent_text`, text, **required**): Briefly describe your primary motivation for taking the Ayahuasca ceremony.
* **Overall Experience Rating** (`overall_experience_rating`, number, **required**): Rate your overall experience from 1 (Challenging) to 10 (Transformative).
* **Intensity of Visual Experience** (`intensity_visual_rating`, number, **required**): Rate the intensity of your visual experience from 1 to 10.
* **Physical Sensations** (`physical_sensations_aya`, select multiple, **required**): Select all the physical sensations you experienced during the ceremony.
	+ Options:
		- Purging (Vomiting/Diarrhea)
		- Nausea and Body Discomfort
		- Rapid Temperature Changes
		- Muscle Shaking or Tremors
		- Deep Physical Euphoria
		- Altered Sense of Time
* **Emotional Experience** (`emotional_experience_aya`, select multiple, **required**): Select all the emotions you experienced during the ceremony.
	+ Options:
		- Profound Peace and Serenity
		- Overwhelming Joy and Gratitude
		- Confronting Fear and Anxiety
		- Intense Sadness and Grief
		- Profound Gratitude and Connection
		- Temporary Confusion
* **Key Insight or Realization** (`key_insight_aya`, text, **required**): Briefly describe the most significant takeaway or insight you gained from your experience.
* **Perceived Difficulty of the Experience** (`difficulty_experience_aya`, select one, **required**): Rate the perceived difficulty of your experience from 1 (Easy) to 5 (Extremely Difficult/Intense).
* **Current State of Mind** (`current_mindset_aya`, select one, **required**): Select how you feel right now.
	+ Options:
		- Calm and Peaceful
		- Anxious and Questioning
		- Energized and Inspired
		- Reflective and Quiet
		- Confused and Disoriented
* **Post-Ceremony Integration Needs** (`integration_needs_aya`, select multiple, **required**): Select all the integration needs you have after the ceremony.
	+ Options:
		- Therapeutic Support/Psychotherapy
		- Group Discussion/Sharing Circles
		- Time in Nature
		- Continued Solitude and Reflection
		- Journaling and Artistic Expression
* **Would you participate in a ceremony again?** (`participation_future_aya`, select one, **required**): Indicate your willingness to participate in future Ayahuasca ceremonies.
* **Final Comments or Observations** (`final_comments_aya`, text, **optional**): Provide any additional comments or observations about your experience.
