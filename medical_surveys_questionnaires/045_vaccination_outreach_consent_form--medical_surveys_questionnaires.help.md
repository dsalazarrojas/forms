# vaccination_outreach_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vaccination_outreach_consent_form is a medical form designed to collect user consent and other relevant information for vaccinations. It helps in tracking vaccination schedules, sites, and statuses, which can aid in the effective management and administration of vaccination programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review the form's fields and instructions to understand what information is required and optional.
2. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible to ensure that your vaccination information is up-to-date.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below for clarification.

* **Consent to Vaccination** (id: 1, label: Consent to Vaccination, required: true)
	+ This field asks if you consent to vaccination. Please select "Yes" to indicate your agreement.
* **Vaccination Information** (id: 2, label: Vaccination Information, required: false)
	+ This field is for providing additional details about your vaccination, such as the type or dosage.
* **Vaccination Date** (id: 3, label: Vaccination Date, required: false)
	+ Select the date of your next vaccination if you know it already.
* **Contact Information** (id: 4, label: Contact Information, required: false)
	+ Provide your contact details so we can reach out to you for further communication.
* **Vaccination Site** (id: 5, label: Vaccination Site, required: false)
	+ Choose "Yes" if you'll be receiving the vaccination at a specific site, or "No" otherwise.
* **Next Vaccination** (id: 6, label: Next Vaccination, required: false)
	+ Select the date of your next vaccination if you know it already.
* **Vaccination Status** (id: 7, label: Vaccination Status, required: false)
	+ Choose "Active" if you're currently vaccinated, or "Inactive" if not.
* **Vaccination Type** (id: 8, label: Vaccination Type, required: false)
	+ Select "Yes" if this vaccination applies to you, or "No" otherwise.
* **Vaccination Dose** (id: 9, label: Vaccination Dose, required: false)
	+ Choose "Yes" if you've received a vaccination dose, or "No" otherwise.

## Tips
