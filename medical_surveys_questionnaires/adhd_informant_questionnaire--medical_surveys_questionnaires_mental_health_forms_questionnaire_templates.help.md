# ADHD Informant Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ADHD Informant Questionnaire is a form designed to gather standardized and structured information about a child's behavior and development. It is intended for use by professionals in the field of psychology, education, or healthcare who require data about a child's condition. The form provides a framework to collect data from the informant (such as a parent, teacher, or guardian) to help in the assessment and diagnosis of Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD).

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Start by filling out the informant's section**:
    a. Enter your full legal name in the `Full name` field.
    b. Select your role relative to the child from the `Your role` dropdown menu (e.g., Parent, Teacher, Sibling, Friend, or Other).
    c. If applicable, provide your phone number and email address for follow-up contact.
2.  **Provide the child's information**:
    a. Enter the child's full legal name in the `Patient's full name` field.
    b. Enter the child's date of birth in the `Date of birth` field, using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
    c. Enter the child's age in the `Age` field.
    d. Select the child's gender from the `Gender` dropdown menu.
3.  **Describe the observational setting**:
    a. Select the primary setting where the child's behaviors were most frequently observed from the `Primary setting observed` dropdown menu (e.g., Home, School, Community, or Other).
    b. Enter the date of the observation in the `Date of observation` field, using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
4.  **Record behavioral observations**:
    a. Select all the symptoms that you have observed from the child's behavior, such as `Inattention symptoms`, `Hyperactivity symptoms`, and `Impulsivity symptoms`.
5.  **Rate the frequency of behaviors**:
    a. Choose the frequency of the child's `Inattentive`, `Hyperactive`, and `Impulsive` behaviors from the rating scale provided.
6.  **Additional context**:
    a. Enter the average hours of sleep per night in the `Average hours of sleep per night` field.
    b. Enter the average hours of screen time per day in the `Average hours of screen time per day` field.
    c. Provide any additional comments or observations in the `Additional comments or observations` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Informant's Information**:
    *   **Full name**: Enter your full legal name.
    *   **Your role**: Select the option that best describes your relationship with the child.
    *   **Phone number**: Enter your phone number for follow-up.
    *   **Email address**: Enter your email address for follow-up.
*   **Patient's Information**:
    *   **Patient's full name**: Enter the child's full legal name.
    *   **Date of birth**: Enter the child's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
    *   **Age**: Enter the child's age.
    *   **Gender**: Select the child's gender from the dropdown menu.
*   **Observational Setting**:
    *   **Primary setting observed**: Select the primary setting where the child's behaviors were most frequently observed.
    *   **Date of observation**: Enter the date of the observation in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Behavioral Observations**:
    *   **Inattention symptoms**: Select all the symptoms that you have observed from the child's behavior, such as difficulty sustaining attention, forgetfulness, etc.
    *   **Hyperactivity symptoms**: Select all the symptoms that you have observed from the child's behavior, such as excessive talking, restlessness, etc.
    *   **Impulsivity symptoms**: Select all the symptoms that you have observed from the child's behavior, such as blurting out answers, etc.
*   **Rating Scales**:
    *   **Frequency of Inattentive behaviors**: Choose the frequency of the child's inattentive behaviors from the rating scale.
    *   **Frequency of Hyperactive behaviors**: Choose the frequency of the child's hyperactive behaviors from the rating scale.
    *   **Frequency of Impulsive behaviors**: Choose the frequency of the child's impulsive behaviors from the rating scale.
*   **Additional Context**:
    *   **Average hours of sleep per night**: Enter the average hours of sleep per night.
    *   **Average hours of screen time per day**: Enter the average hours of screen time per day.
    *   **Additional comments or observations**: Provide any further details that may help in the assessment.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
*   Select the options that best describe the child's behavior and situation.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the child's parent or guardian.
*   The data collected from this form is for the child's benefit and will help in understanding their behavior and development.
*   The information will be kept confidential and will not be shared without the child's parent or guardian's consent.
