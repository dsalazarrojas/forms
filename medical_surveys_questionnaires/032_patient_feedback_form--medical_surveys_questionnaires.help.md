# Patient Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Feedback Form is used to gather feedback and information from patients after their visit to a healthcare provider or clinic. It helps to assess patient satisfaction and identify areas for improvement in care quality.

## How To Complete This Form

1. First, please ensure you are on a page (by checking the page ID).
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field (page 1).
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field (page 2).
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (page 3). Note: This is a required field for communication purposes.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (page 4).
6. In the "Feedback" field (page 5), please provide any comments or suggestions you have about your visit. This is an optional field, but your feedback is highly valued.
7. Select the "Overall Experience" rating (page 6) from the available options to rate your experience on a scale.
8. Choose your preferred method of contact from the "Preferred Contact Method" (page 11) options, such as "Phone", "Email", or "In Person".
9. Select the time of day you'd prefer to be contacted from the "Preferred Contact Time" (page 12) options.
10. If applicable, indicate whether you have any medical history or conditions (page 13) that may be relevant to your care.
11. If applicable, enter your visit frequency (page 14) to help us better schedule appointments.
12. If applicable, enter your doctor's name (page 15), department, clinic name, and location (pages 15-17) for our records.
13. If applicable, enter any medical conditions (page 18) you'd like to share with us.
14. If applicable, describe any medication side effects (page 19) you're experiencing.
15. If applicable, provide more details about your medical history (page 20).
16. If applicable, schedule a follow-up visit (page 21) with us.
17. Finally, add any additional comments or thoughts in the "Additional Comments" (page 25) field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (page 1)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (page 2)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (page 3)**: Enter your email address, which is necessary for us to contact you.
* **Phone (page 4)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Feedback (page 5)**: Add any comments or suggestions about your visit.
* **Overall Experience (page 6)**: Rate your experience on a scale.
* **Preferred Contact Method (page 11)**: Choose how you'd like us to contact you.
* **Preferred Contact Time (page 12)**: Select the time you'd prefer to be contacted.
* **Medical History (page 13)**: Indicate if you have any medical history or conditions.
* **Visit Frequency (page 14)**: Enter how often you visit us.
* **Doctor Name (page 15)**: Enter the name of your doctor.
* **Doctor Department (page 16)**: Enter the department of your doctor.
* **Clinic Name (page 17)**: Enter the name of the clinic where you visited.
* **Clinic Location (page 18)**: Enter the location of the clinic where you visited.
* **Medical Condition (page 19)**: Enter any medical conditions you'd like to share.
* **Symptoms (page 20)**: Describe any symptoms you're experiencing.
* **Treatment Plan (page 21)**: Enter any details about your treatment plan.
* **Medication Side Effects (page 22)**: Describe any side effects you're experiencing from medications.
* **Medical History Details (page 23)**: Provide more details about your medical history.
* **Follow-Up Visit (page 24)**: Schedule a follow-up visit with us.
* **Additional Comments (page 25)**: Add any additional comments or thoughts.
