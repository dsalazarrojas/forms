# health_savings_account_eligibility_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's eligibility for a Health Savings Account (HSA). The purpose of this form is to determine if an individual meets the necessary criteria to open an HSA account.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing General Information, including name and date of birth.
2. Next, answer questions about your HSA Eligibility, Age Group, HSA Type, Income Range, and Coverage.
3. Move on to Spouse Status and Family Status, indicating your relationship status and family details.
4. Provide information about your HSA Contributions, HSA Benefits, and Tax Filing Status.
5. Answer questions about Income Verification, Medical Status, and Employer Sponsored.
6. Indicate the number of dependents you have.
7. Finally, provide information about your HSA Investment, HSA Contribution, HSA Deduction, and HSA Coverage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (Page 1):
	+ **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **HSA Eligibility** (Page 2):
	+ **HSA Eligibility**: Select the correct option that best describes your current employment status.
* **Age Group** (Page 3):
	+ **Age Group**: Select all that apply, indicating your age range.
* **HSA Type** (Page 4):
	+ **HSA Type**: Choose the type of HSA account you are interested in opening.
* **Income Range** (Page 5):
	+ **Income Range**: Enter your annual income in numerical format.
* **Coverage** (Page 6):
	+ **Coverage**: Select all that apply, indicating your coverage type.
* **Spouse Status** (Page 7):
	+ **Spouse Status**: Select the status of your spouse.
* **Family Status** (Page 8):
	+ **Family Status**: Select all that apply, indicating your family details.
* **HSA Contributions** (Page 9):
	+ **HSA Contributions**: Select all that apply, indicating your contribution frequency.
* **HSA Benefits** (Page 10):
	+ **HSA Benefits**: Select all that apply, indicating your benefits selection.
* **Tax Filing Status** (Page 11):
	+ **Tax Filing Status**: Select your current tax filing status.
* **Income Verification** (Page 12):
	+ **Income Verification**: Select all that apply, indicating your income verification method.
* **Medical Status** (Page 13):
	+ **Medical Status**: Select the status of your medical coverage.
* **Employer Sponsored** (Page 14):
	+ **Employer Sponsored**: Select the status of your employer-sponsored HSA.
* **Number of Dependents** (Page 15):
	+ **Number of Dependents**: Enter the number of dependents you have.
* **HSA Investment** (Page 16):
	+ **HSA Investment**: Enter your HSA investment amount.
* **HSA Contribution** (Page 17):
	+ **HSA Contribution**: Enter your HSA contribution amount.
* **HSA Deduction** (Page 18):
	+ **HSA Deduction**: Enter your HSA deduction amount.
* **HSA Coverage** (Page 19):
	+ **HSA Coverage**: Enter your HSA coverage amount.
* **Notes** (Page 20):
	+ **Notes**: Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your HSA eligibility.
* **Signature** (Page 21):
	+ **Signature**: Sign and date this document to indicate your understanding of the HSA eligibility criteria.
* **Date** (Page 22):
	+ **Date**: Enter the date you complete this form.
* **Email** (Page 23):
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (Page 24):
	+ **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Medical Group** (Page 25):
	+ **Medical Group**: Select all that apply, indicating your medical group affiliation.
