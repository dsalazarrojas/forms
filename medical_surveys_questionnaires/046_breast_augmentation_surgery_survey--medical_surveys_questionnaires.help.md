# Breast Augmentation Surgery Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from patients who have undergone breast augmentation surgery. Your input will help us better understand the outcomes of this type of procedure and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please answer each question honestly and thoroughly. Make sure to include any relevant details and experiences you had during and after the surgery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Full Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Please enter your full legal name.
* **Age** (`patient_age`, number, required): Please enter your current age.
* **Email Address** (`patient_email`, email, required): This will be used for follow-up communications. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Surgery Date** (`surgery_date`, date, required): When was your surgery performed? Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Surgeon Name** (`surgeon_name`, text, required): What is the name of your surgeon?
* **Implant Type** (`implant_type`, select_one, required): What type of implant did you receive? Please select one of the following options: Saline, Silicone, Gummy Bear, or Not Sure.
* **Implant Placement** (`implant_placement`, select_one, optional): Where were the implants placed? Please select one of the following options: Subglandular, Submuscular, Dual Plane, or Not Sure. If not sure, simply leave this field blank.
* **Incision Location** (`incision_location`, select_one, optional): Where was the incision made? Please select one of the following options: Inframammary, Periareolar, Transaxillary, Transumbilical, or Not Sure. If not sure, simply leave this field blank.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the results of the surgery. Please select one of the following options: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Appearance Rating** (`appearance_rating`, select_one, required): Please rate the appearance of your results. Please select one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **How Natural Do Implants Feel** (`feel_natural`, select_one, required): How natural do your implants feel? Please select one of the following options: Very Natural, Mostly Natural, Somewhat Natural, Not Very Natural, or Not Natural at All.
* **Size Expectations Met** (`size_expectations`, select_one, required): Were your size expectations met? Please select one of the following options: Exactly as Expected, Close to Expected, Somewhat Different, Very Different.
* **Recovery Experience** (`recovery_experience`, select_one, required): How was your recovery experience? Please select one of the following options: Easier Than Expected, As Expected, Harder Than Expected, Much Harder Than Expected.
* **Complications Experienced** (`complications`, select_multiple, required): What complications, if any, did you experience? Please select one or more of the following options: None, Infection, Capsular Contracture, Implant Rupture, Scarring Issues, Sensitivity Changes, Pain, Asymmetry, Other.
* **Current Pain Level** (`pain_level_current`, select_one, required): What is your current pain level? Please select one of the following options: No Pain, Mild, Moderate, Severe, Very Severe.
* **Would You Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Would you recommend this surgery to others? Please select one of the following options: Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, Definitely No.
* **Would You Have It Again** (`would_repeat`, select_one, required): Would you have the surgery again? Please select one of the following options: Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, Definitely No.
* **Emotional Impact** (`emotional_impact`, select_one, required): How did the surgery affect you emotionally? Please select one of the following options: Very Positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, Very Negative.
* **Confidence Change** (`confidence_change`, select_one, required): How did your confidence change after the surgery? Please select one of the following options: Significantly Increased, Somewhat Increased, No Change, Somewhat Decreased, Significantly Decreased.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Do you have any additional comments or experiences you'd like to share?
