# Emergency Department Patient Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency department patient satisfaction survey is designed to collect feedback from patients and their families about their experiences in the emergency department. The information gathered will help the hospital administration identify areas of improvement and make necessary changes to provide better patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer based on your genuine experience in the emergency department.
2. For each field, select the option that best describes your experience.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Experience** (Page 1): This field asks you to rate your overall experience in the emergency department. Please select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".
* **Staff Attitude** (Page 1): This field assesses the attitude and behavior of the emergency department staff. Please rate your experience with staff attitude on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".
* **Quality of Care** (Page 1): This field evaluates the quality of care provided by the emergency department staff. Please rate your experience with the quality of care on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".
* **Wait Time** (Page 1): This field measures the time you spent waiting in the emergency department. Please rate your experience with wait time on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".
* **Pain Management** (Page 1): This field assesses the management of pain and discomfort during your visit. Please rate your experience with pain management on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".
* **Cleanliness of Room and Equipment** (Page 1): This field evaluates the cleanliness and maintenance of the emergency department rooms and equipment. Please rate your experience with the cleanliness of rooms and equipment on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".
* **Communication** (Page 1): This field assesses the communication between you and the emergency department staff. Please rate your experience with communication on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".
* **Would you recommend this hospital to a friend or family member?** (Page 2): This field asks if you would recommend the hospital to a friend or family member. Please select one of the options:
	+ Would you definitely recommend this hospital
	+ Would you probably recommend this hospital
	+ Would you probably not recommend this hospital
	+ Would you definitely not recommend this hospital
* **Comments** (Page 2): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience in the emergency department. Please use this field to provide any feedback or suggestions that are not covered in the previous fields.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* Please rate your experience based on your genuine feelings and observations.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Comments" field.
* The information collected will be kept confidential and will only be used to improve patient care and experience.
