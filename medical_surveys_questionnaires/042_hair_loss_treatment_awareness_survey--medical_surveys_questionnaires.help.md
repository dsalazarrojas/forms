# hair_loss_treatment_awareness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hair loss treatment awareness survey aims to gather information about individuals' experiences, perceptions, and intentions regarding hair loss treatment. This survey will help to:

- Understand the extent of hair loss among the population.
- Investigate awareness levels regarding available hair loss treatment options.
- Gather information about the factors influencing decision-making processes when planning hair loss treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Choose the best option that reflects your feelings and experiences.
3. Enter any additional information when prompted.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (1): This is a text field where you can provide any relevant information about your experiences with hair loss.
- **How long have you been experiencing hair loss** (2): Please enter the length of time you've been dealing with hair loss.
- **Awareness of hair loss treatment** (3): Choose an option that best describes your level of awareness regarding hair loss treatment.
- **Hair loss treatment options considered** (4): Select all the options you've considered or are considering for hair loss treatment.
- **Awareness of hair loss treatment options** (5): Choose an option that best describes your level of awareness regarding hair loss treatment options.
- **Hair loss treatment experiences** (6): Enter the date of your last hair loss treatment experience, if any.
- **Hair loss treatment satisfaction** (7): Provide a brief description of your satisfaction level with hair loss treatment, if applicable.
- **Awareness of hair loss treatment satisfaction** (8): Choose an option that best describes your level of awareness regarding hair loss treatment satisfaction.
- **Hair loss treatment planning** (9): Choose an option that describes your intention to plan hair loss treatment.
- **Reasons for planning hair loss treatment** (10): Select all the reasons you're considering or considered hair loss treatment, except for 'other' which can be explained in the next field.
- **Other reasons for hair loss treatment planning** (11): Enter any additional reasons not listed in the options above.
- **Sources for hair loss treatment planning** (12): Select all the sources you've used or plan to use for hair loss treatment information.
- **Other sources for hair loss treatment planning** (13): Enter any additional sources not listed in the options above.
- **Awareness of hair loss treatment planning** (14): Choose an option that best describes your level of awareness regarding hair loss treatment planning.
- **Awareness of hair loss treatment influence** (15): Select all the factors that influence your decision on hair loss treatment, except for 'other' which can be explained in the next field.
- **Awareness of hair loss treatment influence** (16): Choose an option that best describes your level of awareness regarding the factors that influence hair loss treatment decision-making.
- **Sources of hair loss treatment influence** (17): Select all the sources that influence your decision on hair loss treatment, except for 'other' which can be explained in the next field.
- **Other sources of hair loss treatment influence** (18): Enter any additional sources not listed in the options above.
- **Details about hair loss treatment influence** (19): Enter any additional information about the sources that influence your hair loss treatment decision-making.
- **Perceptions of hair loss** (20): Select all the options that best describe your perceptions about hair loss.
- **Awareness of hair loss treatment perceptions** (21): Choose an option that best describes your level of awareness regarding hair loss treatment perceptions.
- **Details about hair loss perceptions** (22): Enter any additional information about your perceptions of hair loss.
- **Awareness importance of hair loss treatment** (23): Select all the options that best describe your level of awareness about the importance of hair loss treatment, except for 'other' which can be explained in the next field.
- **Details about hair loss importance** (24): Enter any additional information not listed in the options above.
- **Other factors importance** (25): Enter any additional factors that influence your hair loss treatment decision-making.

Please note that you can skip any field that does not apply to you.
