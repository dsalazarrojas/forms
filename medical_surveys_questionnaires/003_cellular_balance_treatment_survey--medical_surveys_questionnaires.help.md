# Cellular Balance Treatment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients who have received cellular balance treatments, such as Ionic Detox, Microcurrent Therapy, PEMF (Pulsed Electromagnetic Field), and Bio-Resonance. The form aims to gather information about the patient's experience, comfort level, and overall satisfaction with the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the treatment date**: Choose the date you received the treatment by clicking on the date field.
2. **Select the type of treatment**: Choose the type of cellular balance treatment you received (e.g., Ionic Detox, Microcurrent Therapy, etc.).
3. **Rate your comfort level**: Rate how comfortable you were during the procedure on a scale from Very Comfortable to Very Uncomfortable.
4. **Rate your energy level after the session**: Rate how you feel your energy level is after the session on a scale from Significantly Higher to Significantly Lower.
5. **Select any post-treatment effects**: Choose all that apply from the list of options (e.g., Increased focus, Better sleep, etc.).
6. **Rate practitioner explanation**: Rate how well the practitioner explained the procedure to you on a scale from Very well to Not very well.
7. **Rate likelihood to recommend**: Rate how likely you are to recommend this treatment to others on a scale from Extremely likely to Extremely unlikely.
8. **Add additional feedback or suggestions**: Leave any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or medical record.
* **Treatment Date**: Select the date you received the treatment by clicking on the calendar icon.
* **Treatment Type**: Choose the type of cellular balance treatment you received.
* **Comfort Level During Session**: Rate how comfortable you were during the procedure on a scale from Very Comfortable to Very Uncomfortable.
* **Energy Level After Session**: Rate how you feel your energy level is after the session on a scale from Significantly Higher to Significantly Lower.
* **Post-Treatment Effects**: Select all that apply from the list of options.
* **Practitioner Explanation Clarity**: Rate how well the practitioner explained the procedure to you on a scale from Very well to Not very well.
* **Likelihood to Recommend**: Rate how likely you are to recommend this treatment to others on a scale from Extremely likely to Extremely unlikely.
* **Additional Comments**: Leave any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Your Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up regarding your feedback (optional).
