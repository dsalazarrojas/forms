# Medical Excuse Note Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from medical providers to create a medical excuse note for a patient. This form is intended for use in medical settings, such as clinics or hospitals, to document the reason for an absence or leave of a patient. By providing the necessary information, medical providers can create a clear and concise medical excuse note that can be used by the patient to inform their employer or academic institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own field that requires specific information. Please make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure that the medical excuse note is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Enter the name of the patient who is requesting the medical excuse note.
* **User Email**: Enter the email address of the patient who is requesting the medical excuse note. This field is optional, but it will help us contact the patient if we need more information.
* **Medical Provider**: Enter the name of the medical provider who is issuing the excuse note.
* **Reason**: Enter the reason for the patient's absence or leave. Be as specific as possible, including the diagnosis and any relevant medical information.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the patient's absence or leave.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the patient's absence or leave.
* **Doctor's Note**: Enter a brief note from the medical provider explaining the reason for the patient's absence or leave.
* **Clinic Name**: Enter the name of the clinic or hospital where the medical provider practices.
* **Authorization Date**: Enter the date the medical provider authorized the patient's absence or leave.
* **Patient ID**: Enter the patient's identification number or other unique identifier.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure that the medical excuse note is complete and accurate. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the patient or their medical provider for clarification.
