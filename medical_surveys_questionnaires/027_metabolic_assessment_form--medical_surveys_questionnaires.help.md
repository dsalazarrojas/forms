# Metabolic Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metabolic Assessment Form is a tool used to gather data about a client's physical characteristics and activity level. This information helps healthcare professionals and researchers estimate the client's resting metabolic rate (RMR) and daily energy expenditure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Client Information (Client Info, text, Optional)**: Enter the client's basic information, such as their name and other identifying details.
2. **Weight (weight, text, Optional)**: Enter the client's weight in kilograms (kg) or pounds (lb).
3. **Height (height, number, Optional)**: Enter the client's height in centimeters (cm) or meters (m).
4. **Age (age, number, Optional)**: Enter the client's age.
5. **Activity Level (activity_level, select_one, Optional)**: Select the client's level of physical activity:
	* Sedentary (e.g., sitting most of the day)
	* Lightly Active (e.g., light physical activity or exercise a few times a week)
	* Moderately Active (e.g., regular exercise or physical activity most days)
	* Very Active (e.g., intense physical activity or exercise multiple times a day)
	* Extremely Active (e.g., extremely intense or physically demanding activity)
6. **Resting Metabolic Rate (resting_metabolic_rate, number, Optional)**: Enter the client's resting metabolic rate (RMR) in kcal/day.
7. **Daily Energy Expenditure (daily_energy_expenditure, number, Optional)**: Enter the client's daily energy expenditure (DEE) in kcal/day.
8. **Body Fat Percentage (body_fat_percentage, number, Optional)**: Enter the client's body fat percentage.
9. **Client Data (client_data, text, Optional)**: Enter any additional information about the client, such as medical history or relevant health conditions.
10. **Notes (notes, note, Optional)**: Enter any relevant notes or comments about the client's assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (Client Info)**: Enter the client's basic information to help healthcare professionals and researchers identify the client accurately.
* **Weight**: Enter the client's weight to calculate the resting metabolic rate (RMR).
* **Height**: Enter the client's height to calculate the RMR.
* **Age**: Enter the client's age to calculate the RMR and daily energy expenditure (DEE).
* **Activity Level**: Choose the client's level of physical activity to estimate their RMR and DEE.
* **Resting Metabolic Rate (RMR)**: Enter the client's RMR to calculate their energy needs.
* **Daily Energy Expenditure (DEE)**: Enter the client's DEE to understand their energy needs.
* **Body Fat Percentage**: Enter the client's body fat percentage to calculate their RMR and DEE.
* **Client Data**: Enter any additional information about the client to provide context for their assessment.
* **Notes**: Enter any relevant notes or comments about the client's assessment.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Enter values in the correct units (e.g., kg for weight and height, kcal/day for RMR and DEE).
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the client or the relevant healthcare professional.
* This form should be completed by healthcare professionals or researchers with the client's consent and under their guidance.
