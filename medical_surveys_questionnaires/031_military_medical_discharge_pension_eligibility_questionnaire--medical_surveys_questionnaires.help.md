# Military Medical Discharge Pension Eligibility Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help military personnel or veterans determine their eligibility for a medical discharge pension. It collects necessary information to assess your qualifications for such benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email address" field.
3. Select your military service branch from the "Service branch" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your service start date in the "Service start date" field.
5. Enter your service end date in the "Service end date" field.
6. Enter your discharge date in the "Discharge date" field.
7. Select the reason for your discharge from the "Reason for discharge" dropdown menu.
8. Indicate your service connected status in the "Service connected status" dropdown menu.
9. If applicable, enter your disability rating in the "Disability rating" field.
10. If applicable, enter your service connected rating in the "Service connected rating" field.
11. If applicable, provide any additional information about your military medical discharge pension eligibility in the "Military Medical Discharge Pension Eligibility" field.
12. Finally, indicate your veteran status in the "Veteran status" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official military documents.
* **Email address** (`Email address`, `text`, optional): Provide your email address where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Service branch** (`Service branch`, `select_one`, optional): Select the branch of the military you served in (US Army, US Navy, US Air Force, US Marine Corps, US Coast Guard).
* **Service start date** (`Service start date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you started your military service.
* **Service end date** (`Service end date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you ended your military service.
* **Discharge date** (`Discharge date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you received your medical discharge from the military.
* **Reason for discharge** (`Reason for discharge`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the reason for your discharge (Dishonorable, General (Other), Medical, Other, Retired, Separation).
* **Service connected status** (`Service connected status`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate your service connected status (True, False).
* **Disability rating** (`Disability rating`, `text`, optional): If you have a disability rating, enter it here.
* **Service connected rating** (`Service connected rating`, `text`, optional): If you have a service connected rating, enter it here.
* **Military Medical Discharge Pension Eligibility** (`Military Medical Discharge Pension Eligibility`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide any additional information about your military medical discharge pension eligibility.
* **Veteran status** (`Veteran status`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate your veteran status (True, False).
