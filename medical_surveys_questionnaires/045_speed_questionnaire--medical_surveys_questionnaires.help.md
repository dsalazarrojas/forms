# Speed Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speed Questionnaire is a patient-facing form designed to collect information about their medical history, symptoms, and treatment plans. This information is used by healthcare professionals to better understand the patient's condition and provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and fill out all required fields.
2. Enter your patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
3. Provide your patient's age in the "Patient Age" field.
4. Select your patient's gender from the "Gender" dropdown menu.
5. Enter your patient's Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. If applicable, specify your patient's other gender in the "Other Gender" field.
7. Describe your patient's occupation in the "Occupation" field.
8. List any symptoms you are experiencing in the "Symptoms" field.
9. Share any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.
10. Select any eye problems you are experiencing in the "Eye Problems" field.
11. Provide a brief summary of your treatment plan in the "Treatment Plan" field.
12. If necessary, describe any eye diseases you are experiencing in the "Eye Disease" field.
13. List any medications you are taking in the "Medications" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Name
• **Patient Name** (required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Patient Age
• **Patient Age** (required/optional): Enter your age in years.

### Gender
• **Gender** (required): Select "Male", "Female", or "Other" from the dropdown menu to indicate your gender.

### Date of Birth
• **Date of Birth** (required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

### Other Gender
• **Other Gender** (optional): If your gender is not listed in the dropdown menu, describe your gender here.

### Occupation
• **Occupation** (optional): Describe your occupation or job role.

### Symptoms
• **Symptoms** (optional): List any symptoms you are experiencing, such as eye strain or vision disturbance.

### Medical History
• **Medical History** (optional): Share any relevant medical history, including surgeries, allergies, or medical conditions.

### Eye Problems
• **Eye Problems** (required/optional): Select all eye problems that apply, such as dry eyes or vision disturbance.

### Treatment Plan
• **Treatment Plan** (optional): Provide a brief summary of your treatment plan, including any medications or therapies you are taking.

### Eye Disease
• **Eye Disease** (optional): If you have a diagnosed eye disease, describe it here.

### Medications
• **Medications** (optional): List any medications you are taking for your eyes or medical conditions.
