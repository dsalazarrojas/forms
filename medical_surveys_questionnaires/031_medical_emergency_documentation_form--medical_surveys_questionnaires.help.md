# Medical Emergency Documentation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Emergency Documentation Form is a comprehensive tool for collecting information about medical emergencies. This form helps medical professionals gather essential details about the incident, patient, and witness statements to provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions for each section:

1. **Emergency Details**: Please provide a brief description of the medical emergency, including the location, time, and any relevant details.
2. **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's name, age, and any relevant medical history.
3. **Witness Info**: Provide contact information for witnesses, including names, phone numbers, and addresses.
4. **Medical History**: Describe any previous medical conditions or treatments that may be relevant to the incident.
5. **Incident Info**: Provide details about the incident, including the date and time it occurred.
6. **Treatment Details**: Outline any medical services or treatments provided to the patient.
7. **Witness Statements**: Include any witness statements or testimonies about the incident.
8. **Medical Emergency Services**: Describe any medical emergency services provided, such as CPR or defibrillation.
9. **Other Info**: Provide any additional information that may be relevant to the patient's care.
10. **Witness List**: Select up to 5 witnesses who were present during the incident.
11. **Witness Name**: Enter the name of each witness selected in the previous step.
12. **Medical History 1-2**: Provide detailed medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, treatments, or medications.
13. **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
14. **Incident Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
