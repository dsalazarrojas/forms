# viral-infection-assessment-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the severity of a viral infection and gather information about the patient's symptoms, exposure history, medical history, treatment, and follow-up care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's first name.
2. **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's last name.
3. **Exposure** (`exposure_2`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the list of exposure options.
	* Family Member
	* Healthcare Worker
	* Public Place
	* School/Work
4. **Symptoms** (`symptoms_3`, note, required): List the symptoms you are experiencing.
5. **Health History** (`health_history_4`, select_one, required): Select the health history that applies to you.
	* Vaccinated
	* Previously Infected
6. **Screening Date** (`screening_5`, date, required): Enter the date of the screening.
7. **Test Results** (`test_results_6`, select_one, required): Select the test results.
	* Negative
	* Positive
8. **Treatment** (`treatment_7`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the list of treatment options.
	* Antibiotics
	* Antiviral
	* Antimalarial
	* Antifungal
9. **Follow up** (`follow_up_8`, date, optional): Enter the date of the follow-up.
10. **Medical History** (`medical_history_9`, select_one, required): Select the medical history that applies to you.
	* None
	* Allergies
	* Chronic Conditions
11. **Next Steps** (`next_steps_10`, text, optional): Describe the next steps you want your healthcare provider to take.
12. **Provider Info** (`provider_info_11`, note, optional): Provide more information about your healthcare provider.
13. **Contact Info** (`contact_info_12`, text, optional): Provide your contact information.
14. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments_13`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments.
15. **Medical Facility** (`medical_facility_14`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the list of medical facility options.
	* Hospital
	* Clinic
	* Doctor
	* Urgent Care
16. **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider_15`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the list of medical provider options.
	* Doctor
	* NP
	* PA
	* RN

## Tips
* Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is for the patient's information only, and will not be shared with anyone without their consent.
