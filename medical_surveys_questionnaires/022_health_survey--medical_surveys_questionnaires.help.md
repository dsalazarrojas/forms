# Health Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Survey form is designed to collect information about a patient's health condition and medical history. This form is used to gather general information about the patient's health status, medical history, and any relevant medical conditions or allergies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first text field, "Health Survey", with a brief description of the patient's health condition.
2. In the second text field, "medical anamnesis", describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, medical procedures, or allergies.
3. In the third text field, "medical survey description", provide a detailed description of the patient's current health status, including any symptoms or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Medical anamnesis
• **medical anamnesis** (text): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, medical procedures, or allergies.

### Medical survey description
• **medical survey description** (text): Provide a detailed description of the patient's current health status, including any symptoms or concerns.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to provide the most helpful information.
* If you have any questions or concerns while filling out the form, please contact form@gic.mx.
* This form can be filled out multiple times, but only the current submission will be saved.
* The form is designed to be used for general information collection and not for medical diagnosis or treatment.
