# COPD Patient Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COPD Patient Assessment Form is a questionnaire designed to gather essential information about a patient's chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) symptoms, medical history, and treatment goals. This form helps healthcare providers assess the patient's condition, monitor progress, and tailor treatment plans accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Enter your full legal name.
3. Fill in the correct date of birth, medical record number (if applicable), and assessment date.
4. Indicate your current smoking status.
5. Describe your current medications for COPD.
6. Answer questions about your symptoms, including dyspnea at rest and with activity, and number of exacerbations in the past 12 months.
7. List any comorbid conditions you may have.
8. Report your FEV1 value and test date (if applicable).
9. Rate your quality of life on a scale of 1-10.
10. State your treatment goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (1, text, required): Please enter your full legal name.
* **Date of Birth** (2, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical Record Number** (3, text, optional): If you have a medical record number, enter it here.
* **Assessment Date** (4, date, required): Enter the date this assessment was conducted.
* **Primary Physician** (5, text, required): Name of your treating physician.
* **COPD Diagnosis Date** (6, date, optional): Enter the date you were first diagnosed with COPD.
* **Current COPD Classification** (7, select_one, required): Indicate your current level of COPD severity:
	+ GOLD Stage 1 - Mild
	+ GOLD Stage 2 - Moderate
	+ GOLD Stage 3 - Severe
	+ GOLD Stage 4 - Very Severe
	+ Unknown
* **Current Smoking Status** (8, select_one, required): Indicate your current smoking status:
	+ Current Smoker
	+ Former Smoker
	+ Never Smoked
	+ Unknown
* **Pack-Years of Smoking** (9, number, optional): If you're a smoker, multiply the number of years smoked by the number of cigarettes smoked per day and divide by 20.
* **Dyspnea at Rest - Modified Rankin Scale** (10, select_one, required): Rate your shortness of breath at rest:
	+ 0 - No dyspnea
	+ 1 - Mild dyspnea
	+ 2 - Moderate dyspnea
	+ 3 - Severe dyspnea
	+ 4 - Very severe dyspnea
* **Dyspnea with Normal Activity** (11, select_one, required): Describe your shortness of breath during typical daily activities:
	+ No limitation
	+ Slight limitation
	+ Moderate limitation
	+ Severe limitation
	+ Unable to perform activities
* **Number of Exacerbations in Past 12 Months** (12, number, optional): Indicate the number of times your symptoms have worsened significantly in the past 12 months.
* **Hospitalizations for COPD in Past 12 Months** (13, number, optional): If you've been hospitalized for COPD in the past 12 months, indicate the number of times.
* **Current COPD Medications** (14, text, optional): List your current medications for COPD.
* **Medication Adherence** (15, select_one, optional): Rate your adherence to your COPD medications:
	+ Excellent - take all doses
	+ Good - miss occasional doses
	+ Fair - miss several doses per week
	+ Poor - rarely take as prescribed
* **Comorbid Conditions** (16, select_multiple, optional): Check any comorbid conditions you have:
	+ Hypertension
	+ Diabetes Mellitus
	+ Coronary Artery Disease
	+ Heart Failure
	+ Atrial Fibrillation
	+ Osteoporosis
	+ Anxiety
	+ Depression
	+ Other
* **FEV1 Value** (17, number, optional): Enter your latest Forced Expiratory Volume in 1 second percentage.
* **FEV1 Test Date** (18, date, optional): Enter the date of your last spirometry test.
* **Physical Functional Limitations** (19, text, optional): Describe any physical limitations you experience.
* **How would you rate your quality of life?** (20, select_one, optional): Rate your quality of life on a scale of 1-10:
	+ 1 - Poor
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5 - Moderate
	+ 6
	+ 7
	+ 8
	+ 9
	+ 10 - Excellent
* **Patient Treatment Goals** (21, text, optional): Describe the main goals of your treatment.
