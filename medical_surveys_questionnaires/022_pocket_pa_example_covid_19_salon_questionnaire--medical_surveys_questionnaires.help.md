# POCKET_PA_EXAMPLE_Covid_19_Salon_Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a client's Covid-19 experience, including their medical history, symptoms, test results, and treatment plans. This data will help healthcare professionals assess and respond to the client's situation effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the relevant answers to each question based on your experience with Covid-19.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
4. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you think would be helpful for the healthcare professionals to know.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Please describe any medical conditions or allergies you have that may be relevant to your Covid-19 experience.
* **Covid 19 Test Results** (`covid_19_test_results`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the result of your Covid-19 test:
	+ Covid-19 Positive
	+ Covid-19 Negative
	+ Unknown
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the symptoms you have experienced during your Covid-19 experience:
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Fatigue
	+ Shortness of breath
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): If you have any contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses, that you would like to share with the healthcare professionals, please include them here.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required: false): Please describe any medical conditions or allergies that may be relevant to your Covid-19 experience.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, `text`, required: false): This is another section for you to describe any additional medical history that may be relevant to your Covid-19 experience.
* **Covid 19 Treatment** (`covid_19_treatment`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any treatments you have received or are taking for your Covid-19:
	+ Antibiotics
	+ Antiviral
	+ Antihistamines
* **Follow-up Appointments** (`follow_up_appointments`, `date`, required: false): If you have any follow-up appointments scheduled, please select the dates.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information about your next steps, such as any further medical appointments or tests you plan to do.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions thoroughly and truthfully to ensure that the healthcare professionals can provide you with the best possible care.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification.
* Make sure to keep the information up-to-date and accurate.
* If you need help or have questions, please contact the healthcare professionals.
