# Telemedicine Encounter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telemedicine Encounter Form is a digital form used to collect and record information about patients' medical histories, symptoms, diagnoses, and treatment plans during remote consultations. It helps healthcare providers to assess the patient's condition and create a comprehensive treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the "First Page" tab, which is the introduction page.
2. Click on the "Patient Info" tab, where you can enter the patient's information.
3. Move on to the "Medical History" tab, where you can record the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses or conditions.
4. In the "Symptoms" tab, select all the symptoms that the patient is experiencing.
5. In the "Diagnosis" tab, choose the diagnosis that best matches the patient's condition.
6. In the "Treatment Plan" tab, outline the recommended treatment plan for the patient.
7. Finally, schedule a follow-up appointment in the "Follow Up" tab.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page**: This is the introduction page of the form.
* **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant identifying information.
* **Medical History**: Record the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or conditions.
* **Symptoms**: Select all the symptoms the patient is experiencing, such as headaches, fever, or pain.
* **Diagnosis**: Choose the diagnosis that best matches the patient's condition, based on the symptoms and medical history.
* **Treatment Plan**: Outline the recommended treatment plan for the patient, including any medications, therapies, or lifestyle changes.
* **Follow Up**: Schedule a follow-up appointment for the patient, including the date, time, and any specific instructions or reminders.
