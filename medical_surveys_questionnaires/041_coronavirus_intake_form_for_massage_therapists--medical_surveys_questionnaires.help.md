# Coronavirus Intake Form For Massage Therapists - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from massage therapists about their medical history, potential exposure risks, and adherence to public health measures.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:
1. Answer each question truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. Provide detailed information in the spaces provided for each section.
3. Be honest and accurate when describing your medical history and exposure risks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (required): Please enter your name and contact information, so we can get in touch with you if we need further details.
* **Medical History** (required): Share your full medical history, including any pre-existing conditions, allergies, or previous illnesses. This will help us understand your overall health status.
* **True** (optional): This field is not applicable for this guide. It seems like an error in the original schema. Please disregard this field.
* **Massage History** (required): Describe your experience with massage, including your level of experience, training, and certifications. This will help us understand your professional background and potential exposure risks.
* **Contact Tracing** (optional): If you've been in contact with someone who has been diagnosed with COVID-19, please provide details about that encounter.
* **Exposure History** (required): List any potential exposure risks you've had, such as traveling, being in crowded areas, or interacting with someone who has been diagnosed with COVID-19.
* **True 2** (optional): Another duplicate field, please disregard this field.
* **False 2** (optional): Also another duplicate field, disregard this field.
* **None** (optional): If you have no relevant medical history, please let us know.
* **Self-Quarantine** (optional): Have you been instructed to self-quarantine due to potential exposure? Please answer yes or no.
* **Isolation** (optional): Have you been instructed to isolate due to exposure or illness? Please answer yes or no.
* **1** (optional): Please disregard this field as it seems to be an error in the original schema.
* **2** (optional): Another duplicate field, please disregard this field.
* **None 2** (optional): This field is also a duplicate, disregard it.
* **Self Quarantine 2** (optional): Please provide the date you started and ended self-quarantine if applicable.
* **Isolation 2** (optional): Provide the date you started and ended isolation if applicable.
