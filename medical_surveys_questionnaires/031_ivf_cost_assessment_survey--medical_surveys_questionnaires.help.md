# IVF Cost Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IVF Cost Assessment Survey is designed to help individuals or couples assess the costs associated with in-vitro fertilization (IVF) treatments. This form will guide you through a series of questions to help you understand your financial situation and potential costs involved in IVF treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Enter your answers in the designated fields, using the options provided.
3. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: `false`): This is a brief introduction to the form and its purpose. Please read through it carefully to understand what the form is designed to accomplish.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required: `false`): Please describe your current medical condition related to fertility. Be as detailed as possible in your explanation.
* **Insurance Coverage** (`insurance_coverage`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Please select all that apply from the options below:
	+ Yes
	+ No
This field is to determine if you have insurance coverage for IVF treatments.
* **Treatment Interests** (`treatment_interests`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Please select all that apply from the options below:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
This field is to understand which treatment options you are interested in.
* **Financial Situation** (`financial_situation`, `text`, required: `false`): Please describe your current financial situation in detail. This will help you understand how IVF costs may impact your finances.
* **Treatment Options** (`treatment_options`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Please select all that apply from the options below:
	+ Yes
	+ No
This field is to determine which treatment options you are considering.
* **Estimated Costs** (`estimated_costs`, `number`, required: `false`): Please provide estimated costs for IVF treatments. This will help you understand the financial implications of IVF.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: `false`): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please share them here.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: `false`): Please enter any tools or resources assigned to you by a healthcare professional.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, required: `false`): Please enter a unique identifier for this form.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, required: `false`): Please enter the output file name.

## Tips
- Please complete the form carefully and accurately to get the most out of it.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
- Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
