# Premenstrual Symptom Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Premenstrual Symptom Screening Questionnaire is a self-administered questionnaire designed to assess the severity of physical, emotional, and psychological symptoms experienced by women of reproductive age during the premenstrual phase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
*   Answer each question according to your personal experiences in the premenstrual phase.
*   For questions 2 and 3, indicate the number of days you have experienced your symptoms.
*   For questions 4, 5, and 7, select the answer that best describes your level of experience with each symptom.
*   For question 6, select all that apply from the list of options.
*   Be honest and accurate in your responses, as this will help provide a clear picture of your symptoms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **How many days have you experienced these symptoms?** (`question1`, `number`, required/optional): This question is asking about the number of days you have experienced symptoms. Please provide the actual number of days.
*   **What is the duration of your symptoms (days)?** (`question2`, `number`, required/optional): This question is asking about the total duration of your symptoms. Please provide the actual number of days.
*   **Do you experience physical symptoms?** (`question3`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question is asking about the level of physical symptoms you experience. Please select one of the following options:
    *   Mild: Your symptoms are mild and you can perform your daily activities without much discomfort.
    *   Moderate: Your symptoms are moderate and may impact your daily activities, but you can still perform them.
    *   Severe: Your symptoms are severe and significantly impact your daily activities.
*   **Do you experience emotional symptoms?** (`question4`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question is asking about the level of emotional symptoms you experience. Please select one of the following options:
    *   Mild: Your symptoms are mild and you can still perform your daily activities without much emotional distress.
    *   Moderate: Your symptoms are moderate and may impact your daily activities, but you can still perform them.
    *   Severe: Your symptoms are severe and significantly impact your daily activities.
*   **Do you experience anxiety?** (`question5`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question is asking about the emotional symptoms you experience. Please select all that apply from the following options:
    *   Anxiety
    *   Fear
    *   Anger
    *   Racing thoughts
    *   Restlessness
*   **Do you experience depression?** (`question6`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question is asking about the emotional symptoms you experience. Please select all that apply from the following options:
    *   Depression
    *   Sadness
    *   Hopelessness
    *   Lethargy
*   **Do you experience fatigue?** (`question7`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question is asking about the emotional symptoms you experience. Please select all that apply from the following options:
    *   Fatigue
    *   Loss of energy
    *   Tiredness
    *   Lethargy
