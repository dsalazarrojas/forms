# Walking Aids Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a knowledge quiz to test your understanding of walking aids, their characteristics, and how healthcare professionals should provide care for patients using these devices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer based on your knowledge.
2. For select multiple and select one fields, choose the correct option from the list.
3. Fill in the text fields with your answers.
4. Make sure to complete all required fields.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.
