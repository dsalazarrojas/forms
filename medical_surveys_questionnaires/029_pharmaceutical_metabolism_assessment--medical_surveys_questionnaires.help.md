# Pharmaceutical Metabolism Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pharmaceutical Metabolism Assessment form is used to collect information about a patient's medical history, current treatment, and health status to help medical professionals assess their pharmaceutical metabolism.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the survey_start page.
2. Enter the **Drug Name**, which should be the name of the medication the patient is currently taking.
3. Enter the **Dosage Dose**, which is the amount of medication the patient is taking.
4. Enter the **Frequency** of administration, which can be daily, weekly, or monthly.
5. Enter the **Duration** of treatment, which can be a specific date or length of time.
6. Enter the **Symptoms**, any side effects the patient is experiencing.
7. Enter the **Drug Side Effects**, any additional information about the medication's side effects.
8. Enter the **Medical Condition**, the condition being treated with the medication.
9. Enter the **Current Treatment**, any other treatments or therapies the patient is undergoing.
10. Select the **Allergies**, if any, from the options provided.
11. Enter the **Medical History**, any relevant medical history or conditions.
12. Enter the **Other Medication**, if any, to report any other medications the patient is taking.
13. Enter the **Family History**, any relevant family medical history.
14. Enter the **Lab Tests**, any lab tests the patient has undergone.
15. Enter the **Doctor Name**, the name of the prescribing doctor.
16. Enter the **Hospital Number**, the patient's hospital ID number.
17. Enter the **Date of Last Visit**, the date of the patient's last visit.
18. Enter the **Blood Pressure**, the patient's current blood pressure reading.
19. Enter the **Height**, the patient's current height.
20. Enter the **Weight**, the patient's current weight.
21. Enter the **Smoking History**, any relevant smoking history.
22. Enter the **Exercise Frequency**, any exercise habits.
23. Enter the **Alcohol Consumption**, any relevant alcohol consumption habits.
24. Enter the **Family History**, any relevant family medical history.
25. Enter any **Doctor Comments**, any additional comments from the prescribing doctor.

* **survey_start**: The starting page of the form, used to signal the beginning of the assessment.

* **Frequency**: Enter how often the medication is administered.
* **Duration**: Enter the length of time the patient has been taking the medication.
* **Symptoms**: Enter any symptoms experienced as a result of the medication.
* **Drug Side Effects**: Enter any additional information about the medication's side effects.
