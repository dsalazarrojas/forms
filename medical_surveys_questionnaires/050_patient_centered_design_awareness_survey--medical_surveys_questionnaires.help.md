# Patient Centered Design Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to test users' understanding of patient-centered design principles, including its definition, components, goals, and application in daily work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them honestly.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all the applicable ones.
3. For the "Additional Comments" field, provide any additional information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you know about patient centered design principles** (`page_one`, `text`, required: false): This question asks about your general understanding of patient-centered design principles.
* **Which of the following statements are true about patient centered design** (`page_two`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the statements that accurately describe patient-centered design.
	+ Patient-centered design emphasizes the patient's needs and preferences
	+ Patient-centered design is a design approach that prioritizes the needs of the healthcare organization
	+ Patient-centered design is the current state of the art in healthcare
* **How often do you use patient centered design principles in your daily work** (`page_three`, `number`, required: true): Answer on a scale of 1-5 how often you apply patient-centered design in your work.
* **What is the main goal of using patient centered design in healthcare** (`page_four`, `text`, required: false): Describe the primary purpose of patient-centered design in healthcare.
* **Which of the following are key components of patient centered design** (`page_five`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the components that you believe are essential to patient-centered design.
	+ Patient-centered approach
	+ Collaboration between patients and healthcare providers
	+ Involvement of family members
	+ Use of technology
* **How would you rate your current level of patient centered design knowledge** (`page_six`, `number`, required: false): Answer on a scale of 1-5 how well-versed you are about patient-centered design.
* **Additional Comments** (`page_seven`, `note`, required: false): Share any additional insights or thoughts about patient-centered design.
* **Time Spent on this Survey** (`page_eight`, `time`, required: false): If required, indicate the time you spent on this survey.
