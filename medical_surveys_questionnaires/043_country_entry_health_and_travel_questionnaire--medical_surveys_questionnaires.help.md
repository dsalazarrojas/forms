# Country Entry Health And Travel Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a person's country of origin, travel history, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your country of origin in the "Country of Origin" field.
2. Describe your travel history in the "Travel History" field.
3. Enter the date of your last travel in the "Travel Date" field.
4. Select whether you have traveled recently in the "Travel Reason" field.
5. Indicate whether you have traveled recently in the "Travel Type" field.
6. If you have traveled recently, enter the duration of your trip in the "Travel Duration" field (if applicable).
7. If you have traveled recently, indicate how often you travel in the "Travel Frequency" field (if applicable).
8. If you have not traveled in the past 2 years, answer "No" in the "Travel Past 2 Years" field.
9. Indicate your vaccination status in the "Vaccination Status" field.
10. If you have any medical conditions, describe them in the "Medical Condition" field.
11. If you have any health issues, describe them in the "Health Issue" field.
12. If you have any medical documentation, enter it in the "Medical Documentation" field.
13. If you have experienced any travel accidents, describe them in the "Travel Accident" field.
14. If you have experienced any travel accidents, describe them in the "Travel Accident Description" field.
15. If you have any medical conditions since birth, describe them in the "Medical Conditions Since Birth" field.
16. If you have any contact information, answer "Yes" in the "Contact Information" field.
17. Enter your name in the "Contact Name" field.
18. Enter your email address in the "Contact Email" field.
19. Enter your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
20. Enter your organization's name in the "Contact Organization" field.
21. Enter your address in the "Contact Address" field.
22. Enter your title in the "Contact Title" field (if applicable).
23. Enter your department in the "Contact Department" field (if applicable).
24. Enter your extension in the "Contact Extension" field (if applicable).
25. Enter your fax number in the "Contact Fax" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Country of Origin** (`country_of_origin`, text, required): Enter the country of your origin.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, required): Describe your travel history.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your last travel.
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`, text, required): Select whether you have traveled recently.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have traveled recently.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, text, optional): If you have traveled recently, enter the duration of your trip.
* **Travel Frequency** (`travel_frequency`, text, optional): If you have traveled recently, indicate how often you travel.
* **Travel Past 2 Years** (`travel_past_2_years`, text, optional): If you have not traveled in the past 2 years, answer "No".
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate your vaccination status.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, describe them.
* **Health Issue** (`health_issue`, text, optional): If you have any health issues, describe them.
* **Medical Documentation** (`medical_documentation`, text, optional): If you have any medical documentation, enter it.
* **Travel Accident** (`travel_accident`, text, optional): If you have experienced any travel accidents, describe them.
* **Travel Accident Description** (`travel_accident_description`, text, optional): If you have experienced any travel accidents, describe them.
* **Medical Conditions Since Birth** (`medical_conditions_since_birth`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions since birth, describe them.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, select_multiple, required): If you have any contact information, answer "Yes".
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Organization** (`contact_organization`, text, required): Enter your organization's name.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, optional): Enter your title (if applicable).
* **Contact Department** (`contact_department`, text, optional): Enter your department (if applicable).
* **Contact Extension** (`contact_extension`, text, optional): Enter your extension (if applicable).
* **Contact Fax** (`contact_fax`, text, optional): Enter your fax number (if applicable).
