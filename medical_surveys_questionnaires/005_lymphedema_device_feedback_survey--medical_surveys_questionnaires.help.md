# lymphedema_device_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather your feedback and experience with your lymphedema device. Your input will help us understand how the device meets your needs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions on this form honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the answer that best matches your experience with the device.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text box provided.
4. Double-check that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What do you think about your device? (required)
* **What do you think about your device?** (text): This question is an open-ended question to get your overall impression of the device. Please describe your experience with the device in your own words.

### 2. How satisfied are you with your device? (optional)
* **How satisfied are you with your device?** (select_one: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Very Satisfied, Not At All Satisfied): This question helps us understand how satisfied you are with the device. Select one of the options that best matches your experience.

### 3. How often do you use your device? (optional)
* **How often do you use your device?** (select_multiple: Daily, Several times a week, A few times a month, Rarely): This question helps us understand how frequently you use the device. Select all that apply.

### 4. What do you think could be improved on your device? (optional)
* **What do you think could be improved on your device?** (text): This question is for you to suggest any improvements or changes you would like to see on the device. Your input will help us make the device more effective and user-friendly.

### 5. What is your contact information? (required)
* **What is your contact information?** (text): Please provide your contact information so we can get back to you with any follow-up questions or to thank you for your feedback.

### 6. Do you have any additional comments? (optional)
* **Do you have any additional comments?** (text): This is an optional text box for you to add any additional thoughts, suggestions, or comments about your experience with the device.

### 7. Email (required)
* **Email** (email): Please enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.

### 8. Submit (optional)
* **Submit** (text): Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.
