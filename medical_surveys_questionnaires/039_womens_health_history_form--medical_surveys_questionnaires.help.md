# Womens Health History Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a patient's medical history, including their personal details, medical conditions, and contact information. Please answer the questions accurately to help our healthcare providers better understand their health status and provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your name** (Patient Name): This is the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
2. **Enter your date of birth** (Date Of Birth): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
3. **Select your medical condition(s)** (Medical Condition): Choose one or more of the following medical conditions that apply to you:
	* Asthma: A chronic respiratory disease that inflames the airways and can cause shortness of breath and coughing.
	* Diabetes: A metabolic disorder that affects the way the body regulates blood sugar levels.
	* Cancer: A group of diseases characterized by the uncontrolled growth of cells in the body.
4. **Describe your medical history** (Medical History): Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical procedures.
5. **List your allergies** (Allergies): Choose one or more of the following allergens that apply to you:
	* Asthma: If you have an allergy to asthma medication or other substances that trigger asthma symptoms.
	* Peanuts: If you are allergic to peanuts or have a severe allergic reaction to peanuts.
	* Penicillin: If you are allergic to penicillin or have a severe allergic reaction to penicillin.
6. **Select your medical procedure(s)** (Medical Procedure): Choose one or more of the following medical procedures that apply to you:
	* Surgery: If you have had or are scheduled to have surgery.
	* Medication: If you take regular medication for a medical condition.
	* Consultation: If you have had or are scheduled to have a medical consultation.
7. **Provide alternative medical procedure details** (Medical Procedure Other): If none of the above options apply, please describe your medical procedure.
8. **Enter your email address** (Patient Email): Enter your email address if you have one.
9. **Enter your phone number** (Patient Phone): Enter your phone number if you have one.
10. **Enter your address** (Patient Address): Enter your home or work address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical Condition**: Select one or more medical conditions that apply to you.
* **Medical History**: Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical procedures.
* **Allergies**: Choose one or more allergens that apply to you.
* **Medical Procedure**: Select one or more medical procedures that apply to you.
* **Medical Procedure Other**: If none of the above options apply, please describe your medical procedure.
* **Patient Email**: Enter your email address if you have one.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Patient Address**: Enter your home or work address.

## Tips
* Please answer all fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with a healthcare professional.
* If you have any other medical conditions or procedures not listed, please describe them in the "Medical Procedure Other" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
