# Medication Review Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medication Review Checklist is a form designed to gather information about a patient's medication history, including their current medications, potential side effects, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please review the form carefully and enter the patient's information in the corresponding fields.
- You can select from the provided options or type your answer in the text fields.
- Ensure that the form is complete and accurate before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Medication History** (`patient_medication_history`): This field is used to enter a brief description of the patient's current medication history. Please provide a concise and accurate account of the patient's medication regimen, including the names of the medications, dosages, and frequencies.
* **Medication Side Effects** (`medication_side_effects`): This field is used to report any potential side effects or adverse reactions the patient has experienced with their medications. Select 'Yes' if the patient has experienced any side effects, or type in any additional information if desired.
* **Allergy Information** (`allergy_info`): This field asks whether the patient has any known allergies or sensitivities to medications. Select 'Yes' if the patient has any allergies, or type 'No' if they do not have any known allergies.
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`): This field is used to report any additional medications the patient is taking that are not listed in the patient's medical history. Select 'Yes' if the patient is taking additional medications, or type 'No' if they are not taking any additional medications.
* **Dosing Information** (`dosing_info`): This field is used to enter additional information about the patient's dosing regimen, such as frequency, duration, or any other relevant details. Please provide a brief and concise description.
* **Medication Dosage** (`medication_dosage`): This field is used to enter the specific dosage of the medication the patient is taking. Please enter the dosage in the format specified (e.g. "X mg" or "X mg/kg/day").
* **Medication Frequency** (`medication_frequency`): This field is used to enter the frequency of the medication the patient is taking, such as daily, weekly, or monthly. Please specify the exact frequency in the format specified.
* **Medication Duration** (`medication_duration`): This field is used to enter the duration of the medication the patient is taking, such as the start and end dates or the total duration. Please enter the duration in the format specified.
* **Medication Route** (`medication_route`): This field is used to enter the specific route of administration for the medication, such as orally, intravenously, or topically. Please specify the exact route in the format specified.
* **Medication Form** (`medication_form`): This field is used to enter the specific form of the medication, such as tablet, capsule, or injection. Please specify the exact form in the format specified.
* **Medication Strength** (`medication_strength`): This field is used to enter the strength of the medication, such as "X mg/ml" or "X mcg/mL". Please enter the strength in the format specified.
* **Medication Other** (`medication_other`): This field is used to enter any additional information about the medication that does not fit in the other fields. Please provide a brief and concise description.
* **Other** (`patient_medication_other_info`): This field is used to enter any other relevant information about the patient's medication history that does not fit in the other fields. Please provide a brief and concise description.

Note that some fields are optional, and the form can be completed as needed. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
