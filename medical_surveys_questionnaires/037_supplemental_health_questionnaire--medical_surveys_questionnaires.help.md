# Supplemental Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Supplemental Health Questionnaire** is designed to gather essential information about a patient's health status, travel history, and medical conditions to aid healthcare professionals in assessing and managing patient care. This form is typically used for patients who require attention from medical staff, such as those with recent travel history, symptoms, or medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient's name in the **patient_name** field.
2. If applicable, enter your patient's phone number in the **patient_phone** field.
3. Select the medical conditions that apply to your patient from the list provided in the **medical_condition** field. You can select multiple conditions if necessary.
4. If your patient has a medical condition not listed, please enter the condition in the **other_medical_condition** field.
5. Enter the date the symptoms started in the **date_of_symptoms** field.
6. If your patient has traveled recently, indicate the countries they have visited and the date of travel in the **travel_countries** and **symptoms_start_date** fields, respectively.
7. If your patient has taken a temperature, enter it in the **patient_temperature** field.
8. Indicate whether your patient has been hospitalized or admitted to intensive care in the **patient_hospitalization** field.
9. If your patient has had contact with someone who is sick, select the relevant option from the **patient_contact** field.
10. Describe any symptoms your patient is experiencing in the **patient_symptoms** field.
11. If your patient has undergone any tests, select the relevant option from the **patient_test_results** field.
12. Enter any treatment received by your patient in the **patient_treatment** field.
13. If your patient has traveled internationally, enter the countries visited in the **travel_countries** field.
14. Select your patient's vaccination status from the **patient_vaccination_status** field.
15. If your patient's test results are not among the listed options, enter the results in the **patient_test_results_other** field.
16. Enter any additional medical conditions your patient may have in the **other_medical_conditions** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name** (Required): Please enter your patient's name.
* **patient_phone** (Optional): Enter your patient's phone number for contact purposes.
* **medical_condition** (Required): Select all medical conditions that apply to your patient from the list. Examples: COVID, Fever, Cough, etc.
* **other_medical_condition** (Optional): If a condition is not listed, enter it here.
* **date_of_symptoms** (Required): Enter the date when your patient's symptoms started.
* **symptoms_start_date** (Optional): Enter the date of travel if your patient has symptoms.
* **patient_travel** (Required): Indicate whether your patient has traveled recently.
* **travel_countries** (Optional): If your patient has traveled, list the countries visited.
* **patient_temperature** (Optional): Enter your patient's temperature if measured.
* **patient_hospitalization** (Required): Indicate whether your patient has been hospitalized or admitted to intensive care.
* **patient_contact** (Optional): Select the type of contact your patient has had with someone who is sick.
* **patient_symptoms** (Optional): Describe your patient's symptoms.
* **patient_test_results** (Optional): Select the type of test your patient has undergone.
* **patient_treatment** (Optional): Enter the treatment received by your patient.
* **patient_travel_history** (Optional): Describe your patient's travel history.
* **patient_vaccination_status** (Optional): Select your patient's vaccination status.
* **patient_test_results_other** (Optional): If test results are not listed, enter here.
* **other_medical_conditions** (Optional): List any additional medical conditions your patient may have.
* **patient_medical_condition_1 to 7** (Optional): List any additional medical conditions your patient may have.
