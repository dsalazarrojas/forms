# Behavior Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Behavior Observation Form is a tool used to document and report on student behaviors observed during lessons or activities. It helps teachers and educators identify behaviors that require support, provide recommendations for improvement, and maintain accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the student's name under "Student Name".
2. Enter the observer's name and role in the "Observer Name / Role" field.
3. Select the date of the observation from the calendar.
4. Enter the start and end times of the observation.
5. Describe the classroom setting or activity being observed.
6. Select all positive behaviors observed under "Positive / On-task Behaviors Observed".
7. Select all target behaviors that require improvement under "Behaviors Targeted for Improvement".
8. Enter the approximate frequency of the target behaviors.
9. Rate the intensity of the target behaviors using the scale provided.
10. If necessary, describe the antecedent (trigger) and consequence (response) of the target behaviors.
11. Provide recommended strategies for home or school support under "Recommended strategies for home/school support".
12. Indicate if a follow-up meeting with parents is required.
13. Sign and print your name in the "Observer Signature (Print Name)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (Required): Enter the name of the student being observed.
* **Observer Name / Role** (Required): Enter your name and role (e.g., Ms. Smith - Classroom Teacher).
* **Date of Observation** (Required): Select the date of the observation from the calendar.
* **Start Time** (Required): Enter the start time of the observation.
* **End Time** (Required): Enter the end time of the observation.
* **Classroom Setting / Activity during observation** (Required): Describe the classroom setting or activity being observed (e.g., Math Lesson, Recess, Small Group).
* **Positive / On-task Behaviors Observed** (Optional): Select all positive behaviors observed during the session (e.g., followed directions promptly, remained in seat, participated in discussion).
* **Behaviors Targeted for Improvement** (Optional): Select all target behaviors that require improvement (e.g., out of seat without permission, talking out of turn).
* **Frequency of Targeted Behavior** (Required): Enter the approximate number of times the target behavior occurred.
* **Intensity of Behavior** (Required): Rate the intensity of the target behavior on the provided scale (1-5).
* **Antecedent (What happened right before the behavior?)** (Optional): Provide a brief description of what led to the target behavior.
* **Consequence (How did the teacher/observer respond?)** (Optional): Describe how you responded to the target behavior.
* **Recommended strategies for home/school support** (Optional): Provide strategies for parents or teachers to support the student at home or school.
* **Is a follow-up meeting with parents required?** (Required): Indicate if a follow-up meeting with parents is necessary.
* **Observer Signature (Print Name)** (Required): Sign and print your name.
