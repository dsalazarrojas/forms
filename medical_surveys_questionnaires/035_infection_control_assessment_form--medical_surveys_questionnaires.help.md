# Infection Control Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare providers assess their practice level and identify areas for improvement in infection control. It includes questions about practice level, patient care, staff care, patient outcomes, infection control measures, procedures, safety protocols, patient care continuity, and evaluation and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question based on your current practice level and protocols.
2. Select all relevant measures and procedures from the list.
3. Provide details and explanations for each answer, if necessary.
4. Be honest and accurate in your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Practice Level** (`practice_level`, text, optional): 
    This question asks you to describe the level of practice you have in place for infection control in your facility or department.
* **Infection Control Practice** (`infection_control_practice`, text, optional): 
    Describe your current infection control practice, including any measures and procedures in place to prevent the spread of infection.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, select_one, optional):
    Please select the level of risk factors associated with your practice:
    * High: High risk factors are present and need immediate attention.
    * Medium: Medium risk factors are present but can be managed.
    * Low: Low risk factors are minimal or none.
* **Patient Care** (`patient_care`, text, optional): 
    Describe your current patient care protocols and procedures in place to prevent the spread of infection.
* **Staff Care** (`staff_care`, text, optional): 
    Describe your current staff care protocols and procedures in place to prevent the spread of infection.
* **Patient Outcomes** (`patient_outcomes`, text, optional): 
    Describe the outcomes and results of your patient care protocols and procedures.
* **Infection Control Measures** (`infection_control_measures`, select_multiple, optional):
    Please select all relevant measures you have in place to prevent the spread of infection:
    * Hand hygiene: Hand hygiene is a critical measure to prevent the spread of infection.
    * Personal Protective Equipment (PPE): PPE is essential for preventing the spread of infection.
    * Isolation procedures: Isolation procedures are necessary to prevent the spread of infection.
    * Environmental cleaning: Environmental cleaning is crucial for preventing the spread of infection.
* **Infection Control Procedures** (`infection_control_procedures`, text, optional): 
    Describe your current infection control procedures in place to prevent the spread of infection.
* **Safety Protocols** (`safety_protocols`, text, optional): 
    Describe your current safety protocols in place to prevent the spread of infection.
* **Patient Care Continuity** (`patient_care_continuity`, text, optional): 
    Describe your current patient care continuity protocols in place to prevent the spread of infection.
* **Evaluation and Improvement** (`evaluation_and_improvement`, select_one, optional):
    Please select how frequently you evaluate and improve your infection control practices:
    * Continuously: Continuous evaluation and improvement.
    * As needed: Evaluation and improvement only when necessary.
    * Rarely: Rare evaluation and improvement.
* **Staff Care Continuity** (`staff_care_continuity`, text, optional): 
    Describe your current staff care continuity protocols in place to prevent the spread of infection.
* **Evaluation and Improvement** (`evaluation_improvement`, text, optional): 
    Describe your current evaluation and improvement procedures in place to prevent the spread of infection.
* **Evaluation and Improvement Frequency** (`evaluation_improvement_frequency`, text, optional): 
    Describe how frequently you evaluate and improve your infection control practices.
* **Evaluation and Improvement Method** (`evaluation_improvement_method`, text, optional): 
    Describe how you evaluate and improve your infection control practices.
* **Evaluation and Improvement Results** (`evaluation_improvement_results`, text, optional): 
    Describe the results of your evaluation and improvement procedures.
