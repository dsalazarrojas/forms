**Head To Toe Assessment Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose

The Head To Toe Assessment Form is a medical questionnaire designed to gather essential information from patients about their personal and medical history, medical conditions, and contact information for their healthcare provider. This form is used to assess the patient's overall health status and provide healthcare providers with the necessary information for proper care and diagnosis.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Answer each question thoroughly and accurately**. Please provide as much detail as possible to help your healthcare provider understand your situation better.
2. **Fill in your name, date of birth, and contact information**. This is necessary for the healthcare provider to communicate with you and provide you with the best possible care.
3. **List your current medical conditions, medications, and allergies**. This information will help your healthcare provider to understand your overall health status.
4. **Assess your pain level and activity level**. Please select the options that best describe your current condition.
5. **Provide your healthcare provider's contact information**. This includes their name, phone number, email, and address.
6. **Provide any additional information about your healthcare provider**. This can include their specialty, medical license number, and NPIN (National Provider Identifier).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **what_is_your_name**:
	+ (Required) Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **what_is_your_date_of_birth**:
	+ (Optional) Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **what_is_your_contact_information**:
	+ (Required) Enter your home address, phone number, and email address.
* **what_is_your_medical_history**:
	+ (Optional) Describe your medical history, including any significant health events or conditions.
* **what_is_your_medical_condition**:
	+ (Optional) List any current or chronic medical conditions you have.
* **what_is_your_pain_level**:
	+ (Required) Select the option that best describes your current pain level:
		- Option 1: None
		- Option 2: Mild
		- Option 3: Moderate
* **what_is_your_pain_level_other**:
	+ (Optional) If your pain level is not listed in the options above, describe your current pain level.
* **what_is_your_current_medications**:
	+ (Required) List all your current medications, including dosages and frequencies.
* **what_is_your_allergies**:
	+ (Optional) List any known allergies or sensitivities.
* **what_is_your_family_history**:
	+ (Optional) Provide information about your family medical history.
* **what_is_your_physical_activity_level**:
	+ (Required) Select the options that best describe your current activity level:
		- Option 1: None
		- Option 2: Limited
		- Option 3: Moderate
* **what_is_your_socioeconomic_status**:
	+ (Optional) Provide information about your socioeconomic status.
* **what_is_your_medical_care_provider_name**:
	+ (Required) Enter your healthcare provider's name.
* **what_is_your_medical_care_provider_phone**:
	+ (Optional) Enter your healthcare provider's phone number.

	+ (Optional) Enter your healthcare provider's email address.
* **what_is_your_medical_care_provider_address**:
	+ (Optional) Enter your healthcare provider's address.
* **what_is_your_medical_care_provider_license**:
	+ (Optional) Enter your healthcare provider's medical license number.
* **what_is_your_medical_care_provider_npi**:
	+ (Optional) Enter your healthcare provider's NPIN (National Provider Identifier).
* **what_is_your_medical_care_provider_facility**:
	+ (Optional) Enter your healthcare provider's facility or practice.
* **what_is_your_medical_care_provider_specialty**:
	+ (Optional) Enter your healthcare provider's specialty.

	+ (Optional) Enter any additional notes about your healthcare provider.

	+ (Optional) Enter how you found your healthcare provider.
