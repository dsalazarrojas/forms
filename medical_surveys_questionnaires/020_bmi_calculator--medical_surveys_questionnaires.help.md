# BMI Calculator - Help Guide

## Purpose
The BMI Calculator is designed to provide a simple and efficient way to calculate and track patient weight status, helping healthcare providers make informed decisions about patient care.

## How To Complete This Form

To use the BMI Calculator, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Provide the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. Enter the patient's current age in the "Age" field (if applicable).
4. Select the patient's gender from the "Gender" field.
5. Choose the unit of measurement for height and weight from the "Measurement Unit Preference" field.
6. Enter the patient's current height in the selected unit of measurement.
7. Enter the patient's current weight in the selected unit of measurement.
8. The system will calculate the patient's BMI based on the provided height and weight.
9. Optionally, select the patient's BMI category from the calculated results.
10. Provide any additional health goals or objectives the patient has.
11. Select the patient's current activity level from the provided options.
12. Describe the patient's typical dietary habits.
13. Report any relevant medical conditions the patient has.
14. List any current medications the patient is taking.
15. If applicable, provide previous weight and date of recording for comparison.
16. Enter any additional notes or recommendations from the healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (Text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Age** (Number, optional): Enter the patient's current age (if known).
* **Gender** (Select One, optional): Select the patient's gender.
* **Measurement Unit Preference** (Select One, required): Choose whether to use Metric (kg, cm) or US (lbs, inches) units for height and weight.
* **Current Height** (Number, required): Enter the patient's current height in the selected unit of measurement.
* **Current Weight** (Number, required): Enter the patient's current weight in the selected unit of measurement.
* **Calculated BMI** (Number, optional): The system will calculate the patient's BMI.
* **BMI Category** (Text, optional): The system will determine the patient's BMI category.
* **Health Goals** (Text, optional): Enter any health objectives the patient has.
* **Current Activity Level** (Select One, optional): Select the patient's current activity level.
* **Dietary Habits** (Text, optional): Describe the patient's typical dietary habits.
* **Medical Conditions** (Text, optional): Report any relevant medical conditions the patient has.
* **Current Medications** (Text, optional): List any current medications the patient is taking.
* **Previous Weight** (Number, optional): Provide previous weight (if known).
* **Weight Change Timeline** (Date, optional): Enter the date of recording of previous weight (if known).
* **Provider Notes or Recommendations** (Text, optional): Enter any clinical observations or recommendations from the healthcare provider.
