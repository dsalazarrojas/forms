# Cardiologists Event Log Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from cardiologists who have attended an event, to evaluate the quality of session content and event organization, and to gather suggestions for future events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Cardiologists Event Log Survey" form.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Complete each field according to the explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or contact information.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Session Title** (`session_title_evaluated`, text, required): Enter the name of the session you are evaluating.
* **Rate the session content quality** (`session_content_rating`, number, required): Rate the session content on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Relevancy to your clinical practice** (`clinical_relevancy`, select_one, required): Select the one that best applies to your clinical practice:
	+ Highly Relevant
	+ Somewhat Relevant
	+ Not Very Relevant
	+ Not Relevant At All
* **Key Takeaways from this session** (`key_takeaways`, text, optional): Briefly describe what you learned from the session.
* **Suggestions for future cardiology topics** (`future_topic_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide suggestions for future cardiology topics.
* **Overall event satisfaction** (`overall_event_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Rate the event organization and logistics** (`organization_rating`, number, required): Rate the event organization and logistics on a scale from 1 (Poorly Managed) to 5 (Perfectly Managed).
* **May we contact you for more feedback?** (`permission_to_follow_up`, select_one, required): Select your preference:
	+ Yes, I am open to it
	+ No, please do not contact me
