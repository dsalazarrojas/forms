# Mental Health Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Survey is a comprehensive tool used to gather information about an individual's mental health status. This form helps identify areas that require attention and support, enabling the development of a tailored mental health plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Select the options that best describe your current mental health status for each question.
3. Ensure you answer all required questions, indicated by an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This section provides an overview of the survey and its purpose.
* **Mood Assessment**: Please select all the emotions or feelings you are currently experiencing. (Select all that apply)
* **Mental Health History**: Describe your mental health history, including any previous mental health issues or concerns.
* **Mental Health Concerns**: Select 'Yes' if you have mental health concerns or 'No' otherwise.
* **Goals Assessment**: Select all the goals you are currently working towards for your mental health. (Select all that apply)
* **Mental Health Goals Assessment**: Please select all the areas you are currently focusing on for your mental health. (Select all that apply)
* **Mental Health Symptoms**: Select all the symptoms you are experiencing. (Select all that apply)
* **Mental Health Support System**: Describe your current support system, including family, friends, or professionals.
* **Social Supports**: Select all the social supports you have, such as family, friends, or community connections. (Select all that apply)
* **Self-Care Plan**: Select 'Yes' if you have a self-care plan or 'No' otherwise.
* **Mental Health Assessment**: Select 'Yes' if you have received a mental health assessment or 'No' otherwise.
* **Mental Health Plan**: Select 'Yes' if you have a mental health plan or 'No' otherwise.
* **Mental Health Outcome**: Select 'Yes' if you have a positive mental health outcome or 'No' otherwise.
* **Mental Health Recommendations**: Describe any mental health recommendations or resources you have received.
* **Mental Health Follow-up**: Describe any follow-up appointments or sessions you have scheduled.
* **Mental Health Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your mental health.
* **Mental Health Signature**: Select 'Yes' if you have signed the mental health plan or 'No' otherwise.
* **Mental Health Survey Date**: Record the date you completed this survey.
* **Mental Health Survey Time**: Record the time you completed this survey.
* **Mental Health Survey Completed**: Select 'Yes' if you have completed the survey or 'No' otherwise.
