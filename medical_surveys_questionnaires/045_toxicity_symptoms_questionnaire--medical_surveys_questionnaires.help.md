# Toxicity Symptoms Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's symptoms related to toxicity exposure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for help or seek guidance from a healthcare professional.
3. Complete the form in its entirety, as missing or incomplete information may affect the accuracy of the data collection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exposure Location** (`exposure_location`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the location where you were exposed to the toxic substance.
* **Symptom Duration** (`symptoms_duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of time you have been experiencing symptoms.
* **Symptom Frequency** (`symptom_frequency`, `text`, required: false): Describe how often you are experiencing symptoms.
* **Symptom Severity** (`symptom_severity`, `number`, required: false): Rate the severity of your symptoms on a scale of 1-10.
* **Toxic Exposure** (`toxic_exposure`, `text`, required: false): Describe the circumstances surrounding your exposure to the toxic substance.
* **Symptom Description** (`symptoms_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your symptoms.
* **Exposure Route** (`exposure_route`, `text`, required: false): Describe the route of exposure (e.g., ingestion, inhalation, skin contact).
* **Exposure Date** (`exposure_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the exposure.
* **Exposure Time** (`exposure_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the exposure.
* **Exposure Duration** (`exposure_duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the duration of exposure.
* **Symptoms Onset** (`symptoms_onset`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when your symptoms first appeared.
* **Symptoms Recovery** (`symptoms_recovery`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when your symptoms recovered or are expected to recover.
* **Healthcare Facility** (`healthcare_facility`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all healthcare facilities that you have visited for treatment.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all healthcare providers that have treated you.
* **Symptoms Treated** (`symptoms_treated`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the symptoms that have been treated.
* **Treatment Administered** (`treatment_administered`, `text`, required: false): Describe any treatments you have received for your symptoms.
* **Treatment Outcome** (`treatment_outcome`, `text`, required: false): Describe the outcome of your treatment.
* **Follow-up** (`follow-up`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the reason for follow-up (e.g., routine checkup, additional treatment).
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow-up_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the follow-up visit.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow-up_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the follow-up visit.
* **Follow-up Facility** (`follow-up_facility`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the healthcare facility where the follow-up visit occurred.
* **Follow-up Provider** (`follow-up_provider`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the healthcare provider who conducted the follow-up visit.
* **Follow-up Outcome** (`follow-up_outcome`, `text`, required: false): Describe the outcome of the follow-up visit.
* **Follow-up Note** (`follow-up_note`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional notes from the follow-up visit.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as the submitter of this form.
