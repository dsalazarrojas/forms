# Pet Healthcare Financing Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about pet owners' current methods of paying for pet healthcare services and their interest in exploring new payment options.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select all the methods you currently use to pay for your pet healthcare services on page 1.
2. On page 2, choose how often you pay for your pet healthcare services.
3. On page 3, select all the methods you currently use to finance your pet healthcare services (this is a required field).
4. On page 4, select all the factors that influence your payment plan choice.
5. On page 5, choose how interested you are in exploring new payment options.
6. On page 6, indicate if you are currently using any new or alternative methods to pay for your pet healthcare services.
7. On page 7, type any new or alternative payment options you would be interested in learning about.
8. On page 8, provide any additional comments or suggestions you have regarding our payment options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current method of paying for pet healthcare services?** (`pet_healthcare_financing_interest_survey_1`, select_multiple, required: false)
    This field asks you to select all the methods you currently use to pay for your pet's healthcare services. You can choose more than one option.
* **How often do you currently pay for pet healthcare services?** (`pet_healthcare_financing_interest_survey_2`, select_one, required: false)
    This field asks you to choose how often you pay for your pet's healthcare services. Options include Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, and Never.
* **How do you currently finance your pet healthcare services?** (`pet_healthcare_financing_interest_survey_3`, select_multiple, required: true)
    This field is a required field that asks you to select all the methods you currently use to finance your pet's healthcare services. Options include Pet Insurance, Self-Financing, Pet Care Credit, Financing through credit card, and Financing through bank loan.
* **What are the main factors that influence your payment plan choice?** (`pet_healthcare_financing_interest_survey_4`, select_multiple, required: true)
    This field asks you to select all the factors that influence your payment plan choice. Options include Cost, Quality of service, Convenience, Payment terms, and Other (please specify).
* **How interested are you in exploring new payment options?** (`pet_healthcare_financing_interest_survey_5`, select_one, required: false)
    This field asks you to choose how interested you are in exploring new payment options. Options include Very interested, Somewhat interested, Not very interested, and Not at all interested.
* **Are you currently using any new or alternative methods to pay for your pet healthcare services?** (`pet_healthcare_financing_interest_survey_6`, select_multiple, required: true)
    This field asks you to indicate if you are currently using any new or alternative methods to pay for your pet's healthcare services. Options include True, False, and Unsure.
* **What new or alternative payment options would you be interested in learning about?** (`pet_healthcare_financing_interest_survey_7`, text, required: false)
    This field is a text field where you can type any new or alternative payment options you would be interested in learning about.
* **Do you have any other comments or suggestions regarding our payment options?** (`pet_healthcare_financing_interest_survey_8`, note, required: false)
    This field is a note field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you have regarding our payment options.
