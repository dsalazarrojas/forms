# orthodontic_intake_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **orthodontic_intake_form_template** is a form designed to gather necessary patient information for orthodontic procedures. It is essential for ensuring accurate data collection and efficient patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient information fields (page 1)
2. Provide medical history (page 2)
3. Select medications currently being taken (page 3)
4. Choose the medical procedure (page 4)
5. Provide details on the orthodontic procedure (page 5)
6. Enter date of patient onboarding (page 6)
7. Select insurance coverage (page 7)
8. Provide emergency contact information (page 8)
9. Enter phone number (page 9)
10. Provide medical history 2 (page 10)
11. Select the medical procedure 2 (page 11)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, **required**): Enter your full name, date of birth, and any other relevant identification information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, **optional**): Describe any medical conditions or concerns you have that may impact your treatment.
* **Meds** (`medications`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, select_one, **optional**): Choose the medical procedure you are seeking.
* **Orthodontic Procedure** (`orthodontic_procedure`, text, **optional**): Describe the specific orthodontic procedure you are seeking.
* **Patient Onboarding** (`patient_onboarding`, date, **optional**): Enter the date of your initial appointment or onboarding.
* **Insurance Coverage** (`insurance_coverage`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select your insurance provider for coverage.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, email, **optional**): Enter the contact information of a family member or emergency contact.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **optional**): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, text, **optional**): Describe any recent medical history or concerns.
* **Medical Procedure 2** (`medical_procedure_2`, select_one, **optional**): Choose a medical procedure from the options provided.
* **Tips**:
	+ Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
	+ Double-check your answers for accuracy before submission.

Note: Fields marked as optional can be skipped or left blank, but are still important for providing complete and accurate information.
