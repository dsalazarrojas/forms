# Locate Medical Professionals Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Locate Medical Professionals Form" is designed to help us identify and categorize medical professionals based on their preferences and characteristics. This information is used for internal purposes to better understand our user base and improve services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out each page carefully, making sure to answer all required fields.
2. Be honest with your answers, as they will be used to tailor our services to your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Preferences
* **User Preferences** (`user_preferences`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information or notes about yourself that you'd like to share. Be as detailed as you'd like, but only enter text that is relevant to your professional identity as a medical professional.

### 2. Specialty
* **Specialty** (`specialty`, select_one, optional): Please select "Yes" if you specialize in a particular area of medicine, and "No" if you do not. This field helps us understand your areas of expertise.

### 3. Location
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, optional): Select all locations where you are actively practicing or working as a medical professional. This helps us understand your reach and coverage area.

### 4. Language
* **Language** (`language`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all languages you are proficient in, as a medical professional. This helps us identify your linguistic capabilities.

### 5. Availability
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you are available to work with patients, and "No" if you are not. This field helps us understand your current work status.

### 6. Experience Years
* **Experience Years** (`experience_years`, number, optional): Please enter the number of years you have been practicing as a medical professional. This helps us gauge your level of experience.

### 7. Work Days
* **Work Days** (`work_days`, select_multiple, optional): Select all days you are available to work with patients. This helps us understand your availability and schedule.

### 8. Work Hours
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all hours you are available to work with patients. This helps us understand your work hours and schedule.

### 9. Patient Referrals
* **Patient Referrals** (`patient_referrals`, select_multiple, optional): Select all sources where you receive patient referrals (e.g., hospitals, clinics, word of mouth). This helps us understand how you gain patient referrals.
