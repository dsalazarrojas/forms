# health_coverage_qualification_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the user's health coverage qualification. It includes questions about their medical condition, family history, health insurance status, and medical provider information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them as accurately as possible.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please consult with your healthcare provider or contact your HR/ Benefits department.
3. Fill out the form completely and accurately, as this information will be used to process your health coverage qualification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_info** (contact_info, text, Optional): This field is for you to provide your contact information, such as your name, address, and phone number.
* **medical_condition** (medical_condition, select_one, Optional): Please select "Yes" if you have any medical condition that may affect your health coverage qualification.
* **family_history** (family_history, select_multiple, Optional): Check the boxes for any family members with medical conditions that may affect your health coverage qualification.
* **medication** (medication, text, Optional): This field is for you to provide any ongoing medication information.
* **medical_test_results** (medical_test_results, select_multiple, Optional): Please select "Yes" if you have undergone any medical tests related to your health coverage qualification.
* **health_insurance_provider** (health_insurance_provider, text, Optional): This field is for you to provide your health insurance provider's information, such as their name and address.
* **health_insurance_status** (health_insurance_status, select_one, Optional): Please select "Active" if you currently have active health insurance.
* **health_insurance_type** (health_insurance_type, select_multiple, Optional): Check the boxes for any health insurance types you have.
* **medical_record_number** (medical_record_number, select_multiple, Optional): Please select "Yes" if you have a medical record number.
* **medical_record_date_of_issue** (medical_record_date_of_issue, date, Optional): This field is for you to provide the date your medical record was issued.
* **medical_record_expiration_date** (medical_record_expiration_date, date, Optional): This field is for you to provide the expiration date of your medical record.
* **medical_record_provider_name** (medical_record_provider_name, text, Optional): This field is for you to provide your medical record provider's name.
* **medical_record_provider_address** (medical_record_provider_address, text, Optional): This field is for you to provide your medical record provider's address.
* **medical_record_providing_organization** (medical_record_providing_organization, text, Optional): This field is for you to provide the organization that issued your medical record.
* **medical_record_provider_specialty** (medical_record_provider_specialty, select_multiple, Optional): Please select "Yes" for any medical record provider specialties.
* **medical_record_provider_license_number** (medical_record_provider_license_number, select_multiple, Optional): Please select "Yes" for any medical record provider license numbers.
* **health_care_plan_id** (health_care_plan_id, number, Optional): This field is for you to provide your health care plan ID.
* **health_care_plan_status** (health_care_plan_status, select_one, Optional): Please select "Active" if you currently have active health care plan.
* **health_care_plan_start_date** (health_care_plan_start_date, date, Optional): This field is for you to provide the start date of your health care plan.
* **health_care_plan_end_date** (health_care_plan_end_date, date, Optional): This field is for you to provide the end date of your health care plan.
* **health_care_plan_provider_name** (health_care_plan_provider_name, text, Optional): This field is for you to provide your health care plan provider's name.
* **health_care_plan_provider_address** (health_care_plan_provider_address, text, Optional): This field is for you to provide your health care plan provider's address.
* **health_care_plan_provider_specialty** (health_care_plan_provider_specialty, select_multiple, Optional): Please select "Yes" for any health care plan provider specialties.
* **health_care_plan_provider_license_number** (health_care_plan_provider_license_number, select_multiple, Optional): Please select "Yes" for any health care plan provider license numbers.
