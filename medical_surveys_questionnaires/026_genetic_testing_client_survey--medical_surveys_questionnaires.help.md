# Genetic Testing Client Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients about their experiences with genetic testing services. This information will help us understand how we can improve the quality of our services and better meet the needs of our clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions based on your personal experience with genetic testing services.
2. For select multiple and select one questions, choose the options that best describe your experience.
3. Use the text fields to provide any additional feedback or comments.
4. Once you've completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Testing Experience (#1)**: Describe your experience with genetic testing services, including any relevant details.
* **Communication Clarity (#2)**: Select the option that best describes how clear the communication was during the testing process.
* **Satisfaction (#3)**: Describe your overall satisfaction with the genetic testing services.
* **Feedback (#4)**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Testing Process (#5)**: Describe your experience with the testing process, including any relevant details.
* **Testing Preparation (#6)**: Describe your experience with the preparation for the genetic testing process, including any relevant details.
* **Genetic Testing Services (#7)**: Select the types of genetic testing services you received.
* **Testing Communication (#8)**: Select the option that best describes how clear the communication was before the testing process.
* **Testing Clarity (#9)**: Select the options that best describe how clear the testing process was.
* **Client Service (#10)**: Select the option that best describes your experience with the client service.
* **Testing Satisfaction (#11)**: Select the option that best describes your overall satisfaction with the testing process.
* **Testing Clarity (#12)**: Describe your experience with how clear the testing process was.
* **Genetic Testing Client Survey (#13)**: This is a note field for any additional comments.
* **Additional Feedback (#14)**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions based on your personal experience.
* Be as specific as possible when describing your experience.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please provide them in the text fields.
