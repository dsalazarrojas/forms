# public_health_crisis_preparedness_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations regarding their crisis preparedness and response plans during public health emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question with a brief text response, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Please be as thorough as possible, considering each section carefully.
3. Do not hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Crisis Preparedness
* **Crisis Preparedness** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your organization's or individual's crisis preparedness plan, including any procedures, protocols, or systems in place.
* **Emergency Communication Plan** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Outline your emergency communication plan, including who will be in charge, how you will contact team members, and any other relevant details.
* **Response Plan** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your response plan, including how you will respond to emergencies, what resources you will use, and any other relevant details.

### Response Team
* **Contact** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): List the contact information for your response team, including names, phone numbers, and email addresses.
* **Relationship** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the relationship between your response team and emergency services or other relevant authorities.
* **Emergency Notification** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Outline how you will notify your response team and other relevant authorities in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Information** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional contact information for your response team, such as phone numbers or email addresses.

### Location Information
* **Location** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the location where you are responding to the emergency.
* **Status** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Outline the status of your response, including any details about injuries, casualties, or other relevant information.
* **Status Description** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of your response status, including any challenges or concerns you are facing.
* **Location Coordinates** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the exact coordinates of the location where you are responding to the emergency.

### Response Team
* **Response Team** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): List the names and contact information of your response team members.
* **Team Location** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the location of your response team, if applicable.
* **Team Status** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Outline the status of your response team, including any details about team members or equipment.
* **Team Status Description** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of your response team's status.
* **Notification Method** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Outline how you will notify your response team in case of an emergency.
* **Notification Time** (`16`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the estimated time it will take for your response team to arrive at the emergency location.

### Public Health Messages
* **Public Health Message** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Outline any public health messages you will disseminate to the public or other relevant parties.
* **Public Health Message** (`24`, `text`, required/optional): Provide additional public health messages, if necessary.

### Response
* **Response Location** (`20`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the location where your response team is responding to the emergency.
* **Response Status** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): Outline the status of your response, including any details about injuries, casualties, or other relevant information.
* **Response Status Update** (`22`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of your response status, including any updates or changes.
* **Response Time** (`25`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the estimated time for your response team to arrive at the emergency location.

### Team Response Status
* **Team Response Status** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): Outline the status of your response team, including any details about team members or equipment.
* **Team Response Status Update** (`not present in the original YAML, I've included this field for completeness`)
