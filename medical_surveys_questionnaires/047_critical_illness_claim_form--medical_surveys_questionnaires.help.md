# Critical Illness Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about critical illnesses, which may lead to a claim. Please answer the questions carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and select the correct answers.
2. Enter the required information in the designated fields.
3. Use the available space for note fields to add any additional information if necessary.
4. Review the form before submitting it for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Illness Type (Illness Type)
*   **Illness Type** (`Select One`, `Yes/No`): Please indicate if the claim is related to a critical illness.
*   **Condition Date** (`Date`, `Condition Date`): The date the condition occurred.
*   **Provider Name** (`Text`, `Provider Name`): The name of the healthcare provider who treated you for the condition.
*   **Provider ID** (`Number`, `Provider ID`): The identification number of the healthcare provider.
*   **Patient Name** (`Text`, `Patient Name`): The name of the patient affected by the condition.
*   **Patient Relationship** (`Text`, `Patient Relationship`): The relationship between the patient and the healthcare provider (e.g., patient, family member, etc.).
*   **Claim Status** (`Select One`, `Active/Inactive`): The status of the claim (active or inactive).
*   **Claim Description** (`Text`, `Claim Description`): A brief description of the claim.
*   **Provider Address** (`Text`, `Provider Address`): The address of the healthcare provider.
*   **Patient Email** (`Email`, `Patient Email`): The email address of the patient.
*   **Patient Phone** (`Text`, `Patient Phone`): The phone number of the patient.
*   **Claim Date** (`Date`, `Claim Date`): The date the claim was made.
*   **Claim Amount** (`Number`, `Claim Amount`): The amount claimed for the condition.
*   **Medical Record** (`Text`, `Medical Record`): Any relevant medical records related to the condition.
*   **Medical History** (`Note`, `Medical History`): A detailed medical history of the patient.
*   **Policy Number** (`Text`, `Policy Number`): The policy number related to the claim.
*   **Policy Provider** (`Text`, `Policy Provider`): The name of the policy provider.
*   **Policy Provider ID** (`Text`, `Policy Provider ID`): The ID of the policy provider.
*   **Policy Efficacy Date** (`Date`, `Policy Efficacy Date`): The date the policy became effective.
*   **Policy Expiration Date** (`Date`, `Policy Expiration Date`): The date the policy expires.
*   **Provider License** (`Select One`, `Yes/No`): Whether the healthcare provider holds a license.
*   **Policy Group** (`Text`, `Policy Group`): The group the policy belongs to.
*   **Policy Type** (`Text`, `Policy Type`): The type of policy.
*   **Claim Reason** (`Text`, `Claim Reason`): The reason for the claim.
*   **Claim Details** (`Note`, `Claim Details`): A detailed description of the claim.

This form is intended to be used for critical illness claims, which are typically medical events that require significant treatment or recovery time. The form will help users to report such incidents and gather relevant information for potential claims. To complete this form, please ensure you have all necessary medical records and contact information before starting.

Please review the form carefully and enter the required information in the designated fields. Use the available space for note fields to add any additional information if necessary. Review the form before submitting it for accuracy and completeness.

Note: This help guide is for general guidance only and may not cover all specific business rules or regulations. If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact your supervisor or relevant authorities for clarification.
