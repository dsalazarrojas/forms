# COVID 19 Vaccine Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the COVID-19 Vaccine Survey, a questionnaire designed to understand your knowledge, attitudes, and intentions regarding COVID-19 vaccines.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Ensure you fill in all required fields marked with a *asterisk*.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
4. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you like.
5. Click on the 'Submit' button when you have completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Respondent Information --**: Please provide your details to help us understand your context.
	+ **First Name**: Your first name.
	+ **Last Name**: Your last name.
	+ **Age Group**: Select your age category.
	+ **Gender**: Select your gender identity.
	+ **Email Address**: Your email address (optional).
* **-- Vaccine Awareness --**: This section inquires about your knowledge and understanding of COVID-19 vaccines.
	+ **How Much Do You Know About COVID-19 Vaccines**: Select your level of awareness.
* **Where Do You Get Vaccine Information**: Select all sources you use to gather information.
* **-- Trust and Confidence --**: This section assesses your trust and confidence in COVID-19 vaccines.
	+ **How Much Do You Trust COVID-19 Vaccines**: Select your level of trust.
* **I Believe COVID-19 Vaccines Are Effective**: Select your belief in vaccine effectiveness.
* **I Believe COVID-19 Vaccines Are Safe**: Select your belief in vaccine safety.
* **-- Vaccination Intent --**: This section explores your intention to get vaccinated.
	+ **Do You Plan to Get the COVID-19 Vaccine**: Select your intention.
* **If Hesitant, What Are Your Main Concerns**: Select all concerns that apply.
* **What Would Help You Decide to Get Vaccinated**: Select all factors that would influence your decision.
* **How Serious Is COVID-19 as a Health Threat**: Select your perception of the threat.
* **How Much Would Vaccination Impact Your Life**: Select your expected impact on your life.
* **Do You Have Underlying Health Conditions**: Select 'True' if you have any conditions that may impact your vaccination decision.
* **How Exposed Are You to COVID-19**: Select your level of exposure risk.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback**: Provide any other thoughts about COVID-19 vaccines.
* **May We Contact You for Follow-Up Survey**: Select 'Yes' to be contacted for follow-up surveys.
* **-- Thank You --**: A message of appreciation for completing the survey.
