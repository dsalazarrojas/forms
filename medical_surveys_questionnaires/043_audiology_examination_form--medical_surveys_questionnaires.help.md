# Audiology Examination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and results for an audiology examination. It includes patient information, chief complaint, and examination results.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your patient's information:
	* **Patient Full Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
	* **Referral Source**: Enter the source of the referral, if applicable.
2. Describe the patient's primary concern:
	* **Primary Concern**: Select the main issue the patient is experiencing (e.g., Hearing Loss, Tinnitus, etc.).
3. Specify which ear(s) to be examined:
	* **Ear(s) to be Examined**: Select the ear(s) to be examined (Left Ear, Right Ear, or Both Ears).
4. Record the examination results:
	* **Visual Otoscopy Notes**: Describe the external canal and eardrum appearance.
	* **Pure Tone Audiometry Summary**: Record thresholds for 250Hz-8kHz.
	* **Speech Reception Threshold (SRT)**: Record in dB for L and R.
5. Determine the next steps for the patient:
	* **Clinical Recommendations**: Select the next steps for the patient (e.g., Hearing Aid Evaluation, ENT Specialist Referral, etc.). 
6. Sign the form:
	* **Examining Audiologist (Type Name)**: Sign the form with your name and credentials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name** (Required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Referral Source** (Optional): Enter the source of the referral, if applicable.
* **Primary Concern** (Required): Select the main issue the patient is experiencing (e.g., Hearing Loss, Tinnitus, etc.).
* **Ear(s) to be Examined** (Required): Select the ear(s) to be examined (Left Ear, Right Ear, or Both Ears).
* **Visual Otoscopy Notes** (Optional): Describe the external canal and eardrum appearance.
* **Pure Tone Audiometry Summary** (Optional): Record thresholds for 250Hz-8kHz.
* **Speech Reception Threshold (SRT)** (Optional): Record in dB for L and R.
* **Tympanometry Result Type** (Optional): Select the type of tympanometry result (Type A, Type B, Type C, etc.).
* **Clinical Recommendations** (Required): Select the next steps for the patient (e.g., Hearing Aid Evaluation, ENT Specialist Referral, etc.).
* **Detailed Examiner Notes** (Optional): Describe any additional notes from the examination.
* **Examining Audiologist (Type Name)** (Required): Sign the form with your name and credentials.
