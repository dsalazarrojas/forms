# Covid 19 Daily Status Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to track the daily status of patients in a Covid 19 vaccination program.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form for each patient as instructed below. 
2.  Make sure to enter accurate information about each patient. 
3.  Review the form before submitting to ensure you have filled it out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Id** (`patient_id`, Number, Required): Enter the patient's ID number to identify them in the system.
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the patient's name for easy reference.
*   **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, Select, Required): Choose whether the patient is Fully Vaccinated, Partially Vaccinated, or Not Vaccinated.
*   **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select any symptoms the patient is experiencing.
*   **Temperature** (`temperature`, Number, Optional): Enter the patient's body temperature.
*   **Date Last Vaccination** (`date_last_vaccination`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the patient's last vaccination.
*   **Time Of Last Vaccination** (`time_of_last_vaccination`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the patient's last vaccination.
*   **Date Status Update** (`date_status_update`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the status update.
*   **Time Status Update** (`time_status_update`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the status update.

## Tips

*   Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
*   Review the form before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
*   If you are unsure about any field, refer to your training or ask for guidance.
