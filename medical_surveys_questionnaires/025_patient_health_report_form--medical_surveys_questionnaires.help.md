# Patient Health Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect detailed information about a patient's health report from their healthcare provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Enter your unique patient ID in the "Patient Id" field.
*   Provide a detailed description of your medical history in the "Medical History" field.
*   Indicate whether you have any active medications by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Medications" field.
*   Report your current vital signs, such as blood pressure, heart rate, and temperature, in the "Vital Signs" field.
*   List any allergies you have by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option in the "Allergies" field.
*   Describe any medical conditions you currently have in the "Medical Conditions" field.
*   Report any medical surgeries you have undergone in the past by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option in the "Medical Surgeries" field.
*   Provide any additional medical procedures that are not listed in the "Medical Procedures" field.
*   Describe any other medical history that is not included in the "Medical History" field.
*   Report any family medical history by describing it in the "Family History" field.
*   Indicate whether any family members have any active medications by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option in the "Patient Family Medications" field.
*   Report any medical conditions that have been diagnosed in your family by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option in the "Patient Medical Conditions" field.
*   Report any surgeries that have been undergone by family members by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option in the "Patient Surgeries" field.
*   Provide any additional medical procedures that have been performed on family members in the "Patient Procedures" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Id** (`Patient Id`, number, required): Enter your unique patient ID.
*   **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your medical history.
*   **Medications** (`Medications`, select one, required): Indicate whether you have any active medications by selecting "Yes" or "No".
*   **Vital Signs** (`Vital Signs`, text, required): Report your current vital signs, such as blood pressure, heart rate, and temperature.
*   **Allergies** (`Allergies`, select multiple, required): List any allergies you have by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, text, required): Describe any medical conditions you currently have.
*   **Medical Surgeries** (`Medical Surgeries`, select multiple, required): Report any medical surgeries you have undergone by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option.
*   **Medical Procedures** (`Medical Procedures`, text, required): Provide any additional medical procedures that are not listed.
*   **Other Medical History** (`Other Medical History`, text, required): Describe any other medical history that is not included in the "Medical History" field.
*   **Family History** (`Family History`, text, required): Report any family medical history.
*   **Patient Family Medications** (`Patient Family Medications`, select multiple, required): Indicate whether any family members have any active medications by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option.
*   **Patient Medical Conditions** (`Patient Medical Conditions`, select multiple, required): Report any medical conditions that have been diagnosed in your family by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option.
*   **Patient Surgeries** (`Patient Surgeries`, select multiple, required): Report any surgeries that have been undergone by family members by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option.
*   **Patient Procedures** (`Patient Procedures`, text, required): Provide any additional medical procedures that have been performed on family members.
