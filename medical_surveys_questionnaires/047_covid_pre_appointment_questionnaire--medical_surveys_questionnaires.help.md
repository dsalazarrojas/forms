# Covid Pre Appointment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a pre-appointment questionnaire designed to collect information from patients before their doctor's visit. The form helps the doctor gather essential details about the patient's contact information, medical history, and other relevant details to better understand their needs and prepare for the visit.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your abilities.
2. Fill out all required fields, which are marked as "required".
3. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
4. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Select the date of your appointment in the "Appointment date" field.
8. Choose the time of your appointment from the available options in the "Available time slots" field.
9. Enter any relevant medical history in the "Medical history" field.
10. List any allergies in the "Allergies" field.
11. List any medications you are currently taking in the "Medications" field.
12. Describe any medical conditions you are experiencing in the "Medical conditions" field.
13. Provide the contact information of your emergency contact in the "Emergency contact" field.
14. Enter your contact number in the "Contact number" field.
15. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
16. Enter any notes you would like to share with the doctor in the "Notes" field.
17. Confirm your consent to share this information with the doctor by selecting "True" in the "Consent" field.
18. Confirm that you have signed the form by selecting "True" in the "Confirm consent" field.
19. Sign and date the form by entering your signature in the "Signature" field.
20. Select the doctor's name from the list in the "Doctor's name" field.
21. Select the doctor's specialty from the list in the "Doctor specialty" field.
22. Select the doctor's phone number from the list in the "Doctor's phone" field.
23. Enter the doctor's email address in the "Doctor's email" field.
24. Enter the doctor's address in the "Doctor's address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Appointment date** (5, date, required): Select the date of your appointment.
* **Appointment time** (6, time, required): Choose the time of your appointment from the available options.
* **Doctor specialty** (7, select_one, required): Select the doctor's specialty.
* **Reason for visit** (8, text, required): Explain why you are visiting the doctor.
* **Available time slots** (9, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more time slots for your appointment.
* **Medical history** (10, text, required): Describe your medical history, including any previous conditions or treatments.
* **Allergies** (11, text, required): List any allergies you have.
* **Medications** (12, text, required): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical conditions** (13, text, required): Describe any medical conditions you are experiencing.
* **Emergency contact** (14, text, required): Provide the contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Contact number** (15, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Address** (16, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Notes** (17, text, required): Share any notes or information with the doctor.
* **Consent** (18, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to share this information with the doctor.
* **Confirm consent** (19, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the consent statement.
* **Signature** (20, date, required): Sign and date the form.
* **Doctor's name** (21, select_multiple, required): Select the doctor's name from the list.
* **Doctor specialty 2** (22, select_multiple, required): Select the doctor's specialty from the list.
* **Doctor's phone** (23, text, required): Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor's email** (24, email, required): Enter the doctor's email address.
* **Doctor's address** (25, text, required): Enter the doctor's address.
