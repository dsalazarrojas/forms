# Medical Device Configuration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about medical devices, their configurations, and user experiences. It will help identify trends and patterns in medical device usage and configuration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the form completely and honestly.
2. Select the correct medical device type from the list.
3. Rate the usability of the medical device.
4. Provide any additional comments or recommendations.
5. Enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.
6. Provide manufacturer information if applicable.
7. Select the medical specialties that apply to your work or expertise.
8. Fill in any patient care information relevant to your device use.
9. Specify the location where you use the medical device.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`): This field is for you to provide your contact information, such as your name, title, and organization.
* **Device Type** (`device_type`): Choose the type of medical device you are using, from the dropdown list: Medical Device, Diagnostic Equipment, or Surgical Equipment.
* **Configuration** (`configuration`): Describe the configuration of your medical device, including any custom settings or modifications.
* **Usability** (`usability`): Rate the usability of your medical device, from Very High to Very Low.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`): Share any recommendations or suggestions for improvement of your medical device.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments about your device or experience.
* **Manufacturer Information** (`manufacturer_info`): If you are representing a manufacturer, please provide their contact information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter your personal or business contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`): Specify the date and time for any follow-up communication.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`): Select the time slot for follow-up communication.
* **Medical Specialties** (`medical_specialties`): Choose the medical specialties that apply to your work or expertise.
* **Patient Care** (`patient_care`): Describe any patient care information relevant to your device use.
* **Device Location** (`device_location`): Specify the location where you use the medical device.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Use concrete and specific language when filling in the fields.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
