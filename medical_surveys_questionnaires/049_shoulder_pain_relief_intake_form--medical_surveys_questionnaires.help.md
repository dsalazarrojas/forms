# Shoulder Pain Relief Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from patients about their shoulder pain, medical history, and current treatment plan. This data will be used to create a personalized treatment plan to help alleviate their shoulder pain.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your personal and medical information, including your name, contact details, and medical history.
2. Answer the questions about your shoulder pain, including your current pain level and how it affects you.
3. Provide information about your current treatment plan and any past experiences with treatments.
4. Review and sign the consent form to acknowledge that you have read and understand the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (Patient Information, text, required): Please fill in your name, email, and phone number so we can contact you.
* **Shoulder Pain Information** (Shoulder Pain Information, text, required): Describe your current shoulder pain, including the location, severity, and duration.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, required): Share your past medical experiences, including any relevant surgeries, allergies, or previous injuries.
* **Shoulder Pain Scales** (Shoulder Pain Scales, text, required): Answer questions about your current pain levels using the provided scales.
* **Shoulder Pain Impact** (Shoulder Pain Impact, text, required): Describe how your shoulder pain affects your daily activities and quality of life.
* **Treatment Plan** (Treatment Plan, text, required): Share your current treatment plan, including any medications, exercises, or therapies you are currently undergoing.
* **Consent** (Consent, text, required): Read and sign this form to acknowledge that you have understood and agreed to the provided information.
