# scoliosis_quality_of_life_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the quality of life of individuals with scoliosis, a condition that can cause pain and discomfort. It will help you assess your functional ability and report on your symptoms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Select the options that best describe your situation.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your responses.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can briefly describe any background information about your scoliosis condition or any relevant medical history.
* **scoliosis_history**: Describe your scoliosis history, including the age you were diagnosed, the type of treatment you received, and any notable milestones or events related to your condition.
* **symptoms**: Select all the symptoms that you are currently experiencing, including:
	+ Back pain
	+ Pain in back of neck
	+ Pain in neck
	+ Pain in arm
	+ Pain in hand
	+ Pain in leg
	+ Pain in hip
	+ Pain in foot
	+ Other (please specify)
* **functional_ability**: Select the option that best describes your current functional ability:
	+ Unable to perform most activities of daily living
	+ Unable to walk or stand for long periods
	+ Difficulty walking or standing for more than 10 minutes
	+ Difficulty walking or standing for more than 1 minute
	+ Limited to sitting or lying for more than 30 minutes
	+ Normal

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Select all applicable symptoms and functional abilities to ensure an accurate assessment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please seek medical attention.
