# Oxygen Concentrator Usage Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback about your experiences with oxygen concentrators. Your responses will help us understand how these devices are being used and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question based on your experiences with the oxygen concentrator.
2. If you're unsure about any question or have no experience with the device, please select "None" or provide a brief explanation.
3. Complete each field by selecting the appropriate option or entering the required information.
4. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **oxygen_concentrator_usage** (1): Describe your general experience with the oxygen concentrator, including how often you use it and any notable issues you've encountered.
  * This field is where you can provide a brief description of your overall experience with the oxygen concentrator.

* **maintenance_habits** (2): Indicate how frequently you perform maintenance on the device.
  * Select the frequency of maintenance you perform on the oxygen concentrator, such as "Daily", "Weekly", or "Monthly".

* **issue_encountered** (3): Identify any issues you've encountered with the device.
  * Select the types of issues you've experienced, such as "Technical issues with equipment", "User error", or "Other".

* **satisfaction_with_device** (4): Rate your satisfaction with the device.
  * Enter a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is "Very dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied".

* **satisfaction_with_support** (5): Rate your satisfaction with the support you've received for the device.
  * Enter a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is "Very dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied".

* **device_use** (6): Describe how often you use the device.
  * Select how often you use the device, such as "1-6 hours", "6-12 hours", or "More than 12 hours".

* **maintenance_frequency** (7): Indicate how frequently you perform maintenance on the device.
  * Select the frequency of maintenance you perform on the device, such as "Daily", "Weekly", or "Monthly".

* **issue_description** (8): Provide a brief description of any issues you've encountered with the device.
  * If you selected an issue in the "issue_encountered" field, please provide a brief description of the issue.

* **patient_dose_response** (9): Enter the patient's dose response to the device.
  * This field is for the patient's response to the oxygen concentrator, not for the user. If not applicable, please enter "N/A".

* **patient_weight_response** (10): Enter the patient's weight response to the device.
  * This field is for the patient's response to the oxygen concentrator, not for the user. If not applicable, please enter "N/A".

* **oxygen_flow_rate** (11): Enter the oxygen flow rate of the device.
  * This field is for the device's oxygen flow rate, not for the user. If not applicable, please enter "N/A".

* **device_condition** (12): Describe the condition of the device.
  * Select the condition of the device, such as "New", "Used", or "Refurbished".

* **service_provider** (13): Identify the service provider for the device.
  * Select the service provider, such as "Healthcare Professional", "Manufacturer", or "Supplier".

* **followup_frequency** (14): Indicate how frequently you follow up with the device's user.
  * Select the frequency of follow-up, such as "Once a day", "Once a week", or "Once a month".

* **followup_method** (15): Identify the method of follow-up with the device's user.
  * Select the method of follow-up, such as "Phone", "Email", or "In Person".

* **contact_person** (16): Enter the contact person's name.
  * Enter the name of the person responsible for contact or communication.

* **contact_phone** (17): Enter the contact person's phone number.
  * Enter the phone number of the contact person.

* **followup_note** (18): Enter any notes about follow-up with the device's user.
  * Enter any additional comments or notes about the follow-up process.

* **issues_encountered** (19): Identify any issues you've encountered with the device.
  * Select the types of issues you've experienced, such as "Technical issues with equipment", "User error", or "Other".

* **satisfaction_with_oxygen** (20): Rate your satisfaction with the oxygen supply from the device.
  * Enter a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is "Very dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied".

* **maintenance_habits** (21): Indicate how frequently you perform maintenance on the device.
  * Select the frequency of maintenance you perform on the device, such as "Daily", "Weekly", or "Monthly".

* **device_location** (22): Identify where the device is located.
  * Select the location of the device, such as "Clinic", "Home", or "Other".

* **issue_description** (23): Provide a brief description of any issues you've encountered with the device.
  * If you selected an issue in the "issues_encountered" field, please provide a brief description of the issue.

* **patient_name** (24): Enter the patient's name.
  * Enter the name of the patient using the device.

* **patient_email** (25): Enter the patient's email.
  * Enter the patient's email address.

## Tips

- Be honest and accurate when answering these questions.
- If you're unsure about any question, please select "None" or provide a brief explanation.
- You can refer to any relevant documentation or records when answering these questions.
- If you need further assistance, please contact us for clarification or support.
