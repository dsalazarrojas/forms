# Rehabilitation Outcomes Monitoring Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to track the rehabilitation outcomes, medical information, medication adherence, and follow-up schedules for patients. It is a crucial tool for healthcare professionals to assess the effectiveness of treatments and make informed decisions about patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Begin by selecting the "General Info" page and enter the patient's general information, including any relevant medical information, medication adherence, and treatment outcomes.
2. Next, move on to the "Medical Info" page and provide any additional medical information that is relevant to the patient's condition.
3. On the "Medication Adherence" page, select the relevant medication adherence options.
4. On the "Treatment Outcomes" page, select the best treatment outcome for the patient.
5. On the "Patient Feedback" page, select all that apply regarding the patient's feedback.
6. On the "Additional Comments" page, provide any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition.
7. On the "Medical Survey" page, select all that apply regarding the medical survey.
8. On the "Follow-up Scheduled" page, select one of the follow-up scheduled options.
9. On the "Follow-up Completed" page, select one of the follow-up completed options.
10. On the "Medical Survey 1", "Medical Survey 2", and "Medical Survey 3" pages, select all that apply regarding the medical survey.
11. On the "Additional Comments 1", "Additional Comments 2", "Additional Comments 3", "Additional Comments 4", "Additional Comments 5", and "Additional Comments 6" pages, provide any additional comments or notes.
12. On the "Follow-up Scheduled 1", "Follow-up Scheduled 2", and "Follow-up Scheduled 3" pages, select one of the follow-up scheduled options.
13. On the "Follow-up Completed 1", "Follow-up Completed 2", and "Follow-up Completed 3" pages, select one of the follow-up completed options.
