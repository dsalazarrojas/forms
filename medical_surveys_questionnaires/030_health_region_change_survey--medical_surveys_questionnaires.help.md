# Health Region Change Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who have transferred to a different region for their medical care or services. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve the region transfer process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to provide detailed explanations for the required fields.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What region have you moved to?** (`region_transfered`, text, required): This field is used to record the name of the region you have transferred to.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction with the region transfer?** (`satisfaction_with_region_transfer`, number, required): Please rate your satisfaction with the region transfer on a scale of 1-10.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction with the previous region?** (`satisfaction_with_previous_region`, number, required): Please rate your satisfaction with the previous region on a scale of 1-10.
* **Why did you transfer to this region?** (`reason_for_region_transfer`, text, required): This field is used to record the reasons for transferring to the new region.
* **What was your expected outcome of this region transfer?** (`expected_outcome`, text, required): Describe the outcome you expected from the region transfer.
* **What was your actual outcome of this region transfer?** (`actual_outcome`, text, required): Describe the actual outcome you experienced from the region transfer.
* **What improvement opportunities do you see for the region transfer process?** (`improvement_opportunities`, text, required): This field is used to record any suggestions or ideas for improvement for the region transfer process.
* **Do you have any additional comments about your region transfer experience?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional thoughts or concerns, please share them here.
