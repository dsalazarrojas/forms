# Mood Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mood Tracking Form is a tool used to monitor and track patients' mental and physical health. By filling out this form, patients can help healthcare professionals understand their current well-being, which can inform treatment plans and improve patient care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your current mood level from the options listed.
2. Choose your current energy level from the options listed.
3. Select your current motivation level from the options listed.
4. Choose your current anxiety level from the options listed.
5. Choose your current sleep quality from the options listed.
6. Select your current hunger level from the options listed.
7. Choose how often you engage in social interaction from the options listed.
8. Add any additional comments or notes about your current mood and situation.
9. Enter your patient's email address.
10. Enter your patient's phone number.
11. Select a date for the form to be completed on.
12. Select a time for the form to be completed on.
13. Enter any existing medical conditions you have.
14. List any medications you are currently taking.
15. Select your primary care physician from the options listed.
16. Enter any medical history information.
17. List any allergies you have.
18. Select the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency from the options listed.
19. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
20. Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
21. Enter any additional medical history information.
22. Enter a unique ID for the form.
23. Enter the tool assigned to you by your healthcare provider.
24. Enter the version of the form you are using.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mood** (1) - Choose your current mood level: Very Sad, Somewhat Sad, Neither Sad nor Happy, Somewhat Happy, Very Happy.
* **Energy Level** (2) - Choose your current energy level: Extremely Low, Low, Neutral, High, Extremely High.
* **Motivation Level** (3) - Select your current motivation level: Extremely Low, Low, Neutral, High, Extremely High.
* **Anxiety Level** (4) - Choose your current anxiety level: Extremely High, High, Moderate, Low, Extremely Low.
* **Sleep Quality** (5) - Choose your current sleep quality: Very Poor, Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent.
* **Appetite** (6) - Choose your current hunger level: Not Hungry, A little Hungry, Hungry, Very Hungry.
* **Social Interaction** (7) - Choose how often you engage in social interaction: Not at all, A little, Some, Almost Every Day, Every Day.
* **Mood Tracker Note** (8) - Add any additional comments about your current mood and situation.
* **Email** (9) - Enter your patient's email address.
* **Phone** (10) - Enter your patient's phone number.
* **Date** (11) - Select a date for the form to be completed on.
* **Time** (12) - Select a time for the form to be completed on.
* **Medical Conditions** (13) - Enter any existing medical conditions you have.
* **Medication** (14) - List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Doctor** (15) - Select your primary care physician: Dr Smith, Dr Johnson, Dr Wilson.
* **Medical History** (16) - Enter any medical history information.
* **Allergies** (17) - List any allergies you have.
* **Emergency Contact** (18) - Select the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency: Mother, Father, Brother, Friend, Other.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (19) - Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (20) - Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Medical History Note** (21) - Enter any additional medical history information.
* **Mood Tracker Form ID** (22) - Enter a unique ID for the form.
* **Assigned Tool** (23) - Enter the tool assigned to you by your healthcare provider.
* **Form ID** (24) - Enter the version of the form you are using.
* **Form Version** (25) - Enter the version of the form you are using.

## Tips
* Please answer each field honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* This form is a tool to help healthcare professionals better understand your current well-being. Your participation is crucial in ensuring your healthcare provider receives accurate and useful information.
