## neurotoxic_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The neurotoxic_questionnaire is designed to collect information from users about their medical history and contact details to provide guidance and support for their specific situation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Birth Date** in the "Birth Date" field. (Format: MM/DD/YYYY)
5. Provide a brief **Medical History** in the "Medical History" field.
6. Select one of the following options for **Contact Method**:
	* Phone
	* Email
	* Other
7. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
8. Enter your **Contact Number** in the "Contact Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that is not subject to change.
* **Birth Date**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical History**: Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or sensitivities.
* **Contact Method**: Select the method by which you would like to be contacted. Options include:
	+ Phone: We will contact you by phone.
	+ Email: We will contact you by email.
	+ Other: Please specify the method you prefer.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Contact Number**: Enter a contact number for us to reach you.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can best assist you.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to contact our support team.
