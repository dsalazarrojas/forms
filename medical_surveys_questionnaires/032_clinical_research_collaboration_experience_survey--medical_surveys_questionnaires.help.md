# Clinical Research Collaboration Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback from clinical research collaborators on their experiences with a specific collaboration. It assesses various aspects of the collaboration, such as communication, goal alignment, data sharing, and overall satisfaction, to help improve future collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and title in the "Respondent Name" field.
2.  Select your professional role in the collaboration from the dropdown menu in the "Your Professional Role in this Collaboration" field.
3.  If you are collaborating with an organization, enter the name of that organization in the "Primary Partner Organization Name" field.
4.  Identify the type of your partner organization (Academic, Industry, Healthcare Provider, Non-Profit) in the "Partner Organization Type" field.
5.  Specify the duration of your collaboration in the "Duration of Collaboration to Date" field.
6.  For each of the remaining sections, rate your experience with the collaboration:
	*   Rate the clarity of communication with your partner(s) on a scale of 1-5.
	*   Rate the alignment of research goals between you and your partner(s) on a scale of 1-5.
	*   Rate the efficiency of research data sharing processes on a scale of 1-5.
7.  Provide your overall satisfaction with the collaboration experience on a scale of 1-5.
8.  In the "What were the most successful aspects of this partnership?" field, describe the most positive aspects of your collaboration.
9.  In the "What were the biggest hurdles or frustrations during the project?" field, describe any significant challenges you faced.
10.  Indicate whether you would collaborate with this partner again on future studies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Respondent Name** (text): This is a field to enter your name as the respondent of this survey.
*   **Your Professional Role in this Collaboration** (select_one): Choose your role in this collaboration from the given options (Lead Investigator, Study Coordinator, Data Analyst, Clinical Partner).
*   **Primary Partner Organization Name** (text): If you are collaborating with an organization, enter the name of that organization here.
*   **Partner Organization Type** (select_one): Identify the type of your partner organization (Academic, Industry, Healthcare Provider, Non-Profit).
*   **Duration of Collaboration to Date** (select_one): Choose the duration of your collaboration from the given options (Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, Over 3 years).
*   **Rate the clarity of communication with partners** (select_one): Rate your experience with the clarity of communication with your partner(s) on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Rate the alignment of research goals between parties** (select_one): Rate your experience with the alignment of research goals between you and your partner(s) on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Rate the efficiency of research data sharing processes** (select_one): Rate your experience with the efficiency of research data sharing processes on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Overall satisfaction with the collaboration experience** (select_one): Rate your overall satisfaction with the collaboration experience on a scale of 1-5.
*   **What were the most successful aspects of this partnership?** (text): Describe the most positive aspects of your collaboration.
*   **What were the biggest hurdles or frustrations during the project?** (text): Describe any significant challenges you faced.
*   **Would you collaborate with this partner again on future studies?** (select_one): Indicate whether you would collaborate with this partner again.
