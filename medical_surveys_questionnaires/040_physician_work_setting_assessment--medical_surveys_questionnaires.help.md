# Physician Work Setting Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Physician Work Setting Assessment" form is designed to gather information about your work setting and experiences as a physician. This assessment will help us understand your work environment and identify areas where improvements can be made to support your well-being and work performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question carefully, providing the most accurate information about your work setting and experiences.
2. Choose the most suitable options for each question, taking into account your specific work situation.
3. If you select "Other" as an option, please provide a brief explanation in the text field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction (Text)
* **Introduction**: This field is a brief introduction to the form. Please use it to provide a general overview of your work setting and experiences.

*   **Work Setting (Select One)**: This question asks you to select the type of work setting you are in. Please choose the option that best describes your work setting:
    *   Primary Care: General medical practice or primary care setting.
    *   Specialty Clinic: A specialized clinic, such as a hospital or clinic with a specific focus.
    *   Hospital: A hospital setting, such as a general or specialized hospital.
    *   Other: If your work setting is not listed above, please specify.

*   **If other, please specify (Text)**: If you selected "Other" as your work setting, please provide a brief description of your work setting.

*   **Patient Flow (Select One)**: This question asks you to select the level of patient flow in your work setting. Please choose the option that best describes your experience:
    *   Low: You have a relatively low volume of patients.
    *   Medium: You have a moderate volume of patients.
    *   High: You have a high volume of patients.

*   **Physician Workload (Select Multiple)**: This question asks you to select the level of your workload. Please choose the options that best describe your experience:
    *   Light: Your workload is relatively light.
    *   Moderate: Your workload is moderate.
    *   Heavy: Your workload is heavy.

*   **Communication (Select One)**: This question asks you to select the quality of communication in your work setting. Please choose the option that best describes your experience:
    *   Good: Communication is clear and effective.
    *   Fair: Communication is somewhat clear, but sometimes there are issues.
    *   Bad: Communication is often poor or unclear.

*   **Team Size (Select Multiple)**: This question asks you to select the size of your team. Please choose the options that best describe your experience:
    *   Small: Your team is small, typically 1-2 colleagues.
    *   Medium: Your team is medium-sized, typically 3-5 colleagues.
    *   Large: Your team is large, typically 6 or more colleagues.

*   **Physician Work Environment (Select One)**: This question asks you to select the quality of your work environment. Please choose the option that best describes your experience:
    *   Excellent: Your work environment is comfortable and supportive.
    *   Good: Your work environment is generally good, but with some issues.
    *   Fair: Your work environment is somewhat challenging or uncomfortable.
    *   Bad: Your work environment is often poor or challenging.

*   **Burnout (Select One)**: This question asks you to select the level of burnout you experience. Please choose the option that best describes your experience:
    *   High: You experience burnout symptoms frequently.
    *   Medium: You experience burnout symptoms occasionally.
    *   Low: You rarely experience burnout symptoms.

*   **Burnout Reasons (Select Multiple)**: This question asks you to select the reasons for burnout. Please choose the options that best describe your experience:
    *   High work hours: You have high work hours or long working hours.
    *   High patient demand: You have high patient demand or a heavy patient load.
    *   High expectations: You have high expectations from yourself or others.
    *   Other: If burnout is not caused by the above reasons, please select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.

*   **Burnout Reasons Other (Text)**: If you selected "Other" as a reason for burnout, please provide a brief explanation.

*   **Burnout Prevention (Select One)**: This question asks you to select the level of burnout prevention in your work setting. Please choose the option that best describes your experience:
    *   High: Burnout prevention is effective and supported.
    *   Medium: Burnout prevention is somewhat effective, but could be improved.
    *   Low: Burnout prevention is not effective.

*   **Burnout Prevention Strategies (Select Multiple)**: This question asks you to select the burnout prevention strategies used in your work setting. Please choose the options that best describe your experience:
    *   Time management: Time management is used to prevent burnout.
    *   Self-care: Self-care activities are encouraged or provided.
    *   Support from colleagues: Support from colleagues is available.
    *   Other: If other burnout prevention strategies are used, please select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.

*   **Burnout Prevention Strategies Other (Text)**: If you selected "Other" as a burnout prevention strategy, please provide a brief explanation.

*   **Burnout Prevention Strategies Other 2 (Text)**: If you need to provide additional information about burnout prevention strategies not listed above, please use this field.
