# sleep_study_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sleep Study Questionnaire is designed to collect information on patients' sleep patterns, habits, and medical conditions to help healthcare providers better understand and treat sleep-related issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

*   Ensure you have read and understood the purpose and instructions for this form.
*   Answer each question carefully and to the best of your ability.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Id** (1, Number, Optional): Enter your unique identifier number assigned by your healthcare provider.
* **Sleep Duration** (2, Number, Optional): Record the number of hours you sleep each night.
* **Sleep Quality** (3, Select One, Required): Choose how you would rate your sleep quality from the following options:
    + Sleepy
    + Light
    + Restless
    + Refreshed
* **Bed Time** (4, Time, Optional): Record the time you go to bed each night.
* **Wake Up Time** (5, Time, Optional): Record the time you wake up each morning.
* **Bed Time Concentration** (6, Number, Optional): Record your level of concentration at bedtime on a scale of 1-10.
* **Wake Up Feel** (7, Select One, Optional): Choose how you feel when waking up from the following options:
    + Good
    + Bad
    + Fair
* **Notes** (8, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your sleep or medical condition.
* **Sleep Stages** (9, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the sleep stages you experience during your sleep from the following options:
    + REM
    + NREM
    + Stage 1
    + Stage 2
    + Stage 3
    + Stage 4
* **Sleep Disorder** (10, Select One, Required): Choose whether you have a sleep disorder:
    + Yes
    + No
* **Sleep Disorder Description** (11, Text, Optional): Add any additional information about your sleep disorder.
* **Medication** (12, Text, Optional): Record any medication you are taking for sleep.
* **Medical Condition** (13, Select Multiple, Required): Choose whether you have any medical conditions from the following options:
    + Yes
    + No
* **Medication Status** (14, Select One, Optional): Choose whether you are taking medication for your medical condition:
    + True
    + False
* **Sleep Schedule** (15, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how frequently you follow a sleep schedule from the following options:
    + Yes
    + No
* **Sleep Schedule Frequency** (16, Select One, Optional): Choose how frequently you follow a sleep schedule:
    + Once a day
    + Several times a week
    + Several times a month
    + Several times a year
* **Sleep Duration Frequency** (17, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how frequently you experience long sleep durations from the following options:
    + Yes
    + No
* **Sleep Stage Duration** (18, Number, Optional): Record the average duration of your sleep stages.
* **Sleep Stage Frequency** (19, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how frequently you experience different sleep stages from the following options:
    + Yes
    + No
* **Sleep Stage Duration Frequency** (20, Select One, Optional): Choose how frequently you experience long sleep stages:
    + Once
    + Several times
    + Continuous
* **Medical Condition Status** (21, Select One, Optional): Choose the status of your medical condition:
    + Active
    + Inactive
* **Medical Condition Description** (22, Text, Optional): Add any additional information about your medical condition.
* **Sleep Stages Duration** (23, Number, Optional): Record the average duration of your sleep stages.
* **Sleep Stages Frequency** (24, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how frequently you experience different sleep stages from the following options:
    + Yes
    + No
* **Sleep Quality Duration** (25, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how frequently you experience good sleep quality from the following options:
    + Good
    + Poor
