# Surgery Recovery Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the recovery status of patients after undergoing surgery. It collects information on their pain level, medication side effects, vital signs, and surgical site. This data is used to monitor their progress and provide better care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select your response to each question** from the provided options.
2. Make sure to answer all required questions, marked with a **(*)**.
3. If you are currently taking any medications, list them in the "Current Medications" field.
4. If you experience any side effects from your medications, describe them in the "Medication Side Effects" field.
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" to each question in the "Vital Signs" fields as applicable.
6. Select the location of your pain from the options provided.
7. Select your vital signs (e.g., blood pressure, temperature) as applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How did you feel today?** (`surgery_recovery`, select_one, required: false): How would you rate your overall feeling today? Choose from Very Good, Good, Neutral, Bad.
* **Pain level** (`pain_level_1`, select_one, required: false): How would you rate your current pain level? Choose from None, Mild, Moderate, Severe.
* **Surgery type** (`surgery_type`, select_one, required: false): What type of surgery did you undergo? Choose from General Surgery, Cardiovascular Surgery, Orthopedic Surgery, Plastic Surgery.
* **Are you currently taking any medications?** (`current_medications_1`, note, required: false): List each medication you are currently taking.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all applicable vital signs.
* **Vital Sign Values** (`vital_sign_values_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the values for each vital sign.
* **Medication Side Effects** (`medication_side_effects_1`, text, required: false): Describe any side effects from your medications.
* **Surgical Site** (`surgical_site_1`, select_one, required: false): Choose the site of your surgery.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medication_list_1`, select_multiple, required: false): List each of your current medications.
* **Pain Location** (`pain_location_1`, select_one, required: false): Where is your pain located? Choose from Face, Back, Stomach, Throat.
* **Vital Signs 2** (`vital_signs_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all applicable vital signs.
* **Pain Location 2** (`pain_location_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose the location of your pain.
* **Medication Side Effects 2** (`medication_side_effects_2`, text, required: false): Describe any side effects from your medications.
* **Surgical Site 2** (`surgical_site_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose the site of your surgery.
* **Vital Signs 3** (`vital_signs_3`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all applicable vital signs.
* **Medication Side Effects 3** (`medication_side_effects_3`, text, required: false): Describe any side effects from your medications.
* **Surgical Site 3** (`surgical_site_3`, select_one, required: false): Choose the site of your surgery.
* **Surgical Site 4** (`surgical_site_4`, select_one, required: false): Choose the site of your surgery.
* **Medication Side Effects 4** (`medication_side_effects_4`, text, required: false): Describe any side effects from your medications.
