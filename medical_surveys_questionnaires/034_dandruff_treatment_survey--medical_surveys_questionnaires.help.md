# Dandruff Treatment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's dandruff symptoms, treatment history, and treatment effectiveness. It will help healthcare professionals understand the patient's condition and tailor their treatment plan accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Please select all that apply for the options that best describe your symptoms or treatment history.
3. If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dandruff Symptoms** (`treatment_symptoms`): Describe your dandruff symptoms, such as itching, flaking, or redness.
* **Treatment History** (`treatment_history`): Please select all the treatments you have tried for your dandruff in the past, such as coal tar, shampoo, or anthralen.
* **Previous Therapies** (`previous_therapies`): Select the treatment you have tried in the past that is not listed, and describe it in the "Other (please specify)" field.
* **Treatment Effectiveness** (`treatment_effectiveness`): Please rate how effective your current treatment has been, on a scale of 1-10.
* **How often do dandruff symptoms occur** (`dandruff_frequency`): Please select the frequency of your dandruff symptoms, such as daily, weekly, or rarely.
* **How severe are dandruff symptoms** (`dandruff_severity`): Please rate the severity of your dandruff symptoms, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Dandruff Prevention** (`dandruff_prevention`): Please describe any hair care products you use to help prevent dandruff.
* **Dandruff Medication** (`dandruff_medication`): If you are currently taking medication for your dandruff, please select "True".
* **Hair care products used** (`hair_care_products`): Please list any hair care products you use to help manage your dandruff.
* **Treatment outcome** (`treatment_outcome`): Please provide any additional comments or information about your treatment outcome.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Please provide any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Contact number** (`contact_number`): Please provide your contact number, so we can reach out to you for follow-up.
* **Contact email** (`email`): Please provide your email address, so we can contact you for follow-up.
* **Do you want to be contacted for follow-up?** (`follow_up`): Please select "True" if you want to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): This field is not used in the current version of the form.
