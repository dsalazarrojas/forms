# Pharmacology Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pharmacology Knowledge Assessment form is designed to evaluate the knowledge and understanding of learners in pharmacology. This form aims to gather responses from learners about their pharmacology knowledge, whether it's yes or no. It also includes sections for learner details, review responses, educator comments, and additional text fields for further information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Select your answer**: Select one or both of the options for each question.
2.  Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each question based on your knowledge and understanding.
3.  Optionally, provide your learner details if applicable.
4.  If you have comments or feedback regarding your learning experience, type them in the 'Comments' text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **pharmacology_knowledge_assessment_form**: This question asks about your knowledge assessment of pharmacology. Select 'Yes' if you have the necessary knowledge, 'No' otherwise.
*   **Learner Details**: Please provide any relevant learner details if you have any information to add.
*   **Review Responses**: You can add your responses for review here. If you need more time, please let us know and we will extend the response time.
*   **Comments**: You can type any comments or feedback here.

## Tips

*   Please be honest and thorough when answering the questions based on your knowledge and understanding.
*   Take as much time as you need to complete this form, and let us know if you have any issues.
*   If you have any comments or feedback, please do not hesitate to type it in the Comments field.
*   Review and verify your answers before submitting them.
