# Nutrition Coaching Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutrition Coaching Intake Form is used to collect information from patients for medical coaching purposes. This form helps medical professionals gather relevant information to provide personalized coaching and guidance to patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your medical professional for clarification.
3. Please complete all required fields.
4. If you have any medical conditions or medications that are not listed, please use the "Other" field to describe them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Nutrition Coaching Form 1
* **Nutrition Coaching Form 1**: This is the first page of the form. Please complete this page with your basic information.

* **Medical History**: Please describe any medical conditions or health issues you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.

  - Other
