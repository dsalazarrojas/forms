# safe_sleep_post_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the "safe_sleep_post_survey" form, which is designed to help you share your sleep experiences and practices with your healthcare team.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, review the questions carefully and answer them based on your sleep habits and experiences.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. For each question, select the option that best describes your sleep environment, habits, or experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required/optional): A brief introduction to your sleep story, including any notable events or experiences that may impact your sleep.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, required): Choose your age group from the given options (0-1 year, 1-2 years, 2-3 years).
* **Sleep Environment** (`sleep_environment`, `text`, optional): Describe your sleep environment, including the location, noise level, and any other factors that influence your sleep.
* **Sleep Practices** (`sleep_practices`, `text`, optional): Share your sleep habits, including any routines, rituals, or activities that help you fall asleep or stay asleep.
* **Sleep Disorder** (`sleep_disorder`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of sleep disorder you may be experiencing (None, Sleep Terrors, Night Terror, Sleepwalking, Sleepwalking Disorder).
* **Wake Up Time** (`wake_up_time`, `time`, optional): If applicable, report the time you typically wake up.
* **Sleep Duration** (`sleep_duration`, `number`, optional): If applicable, report the average time you spend sleeping each night.
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, `select_one`, required): Choose the quality of your sleep (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Sleep Location** (`sleep_location`, `text`, optional): Describe where you sleep, including any specific locations or settings.
* **Sleep Partners** (`sleep_partners`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the number of sleep partners you usually sleep with (Alone, With one other, With two or more).
* **Safe Sleep Practices** (`safe_sleep_practices`, `text`, optional): Share any safe sleep practices or routines you follow to promote a good night's sleep.
