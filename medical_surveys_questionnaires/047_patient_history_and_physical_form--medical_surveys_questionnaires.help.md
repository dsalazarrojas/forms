# Patient History And Physical Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient History and Physical Form is a medical form used for gathering comprehensive information about a patient's medical history and current status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each page carefully and answer all questions according to the provided guidelines.
2. For each field, provide accurate and detailed information as much as possible.
3. Ensure that the required fields are completed before proceeding to the next page.
4. Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient History** (1): A text field where you can provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history.
* **Allergies List** (2): Select one or more of the options to indicate if the patient has allergies. This field is optional.
	+ "Yes" indicates the patient has allergies, "No" indicates they do not have allergies.
* **Medical History** (3): A text field where you can provide detailed information about the patient's medical history.
* **Medications** (4): Select one or more of the options to indicate if the patient is taking any medications. This field is optional.
	+ "Yes" indicates the patient is taking medications, "No" indicates they are not taking medications.
* **Surgical History** (5): Select one or more of the options to indicate if the patient has had any surgeries. This field is optional.
	+ "Yes" indicates the patient has had surgeries, "No" indicates they have not had surgeries.
* **Chief Complaint** (6): A text field where you can describe the patient's chief complaint or reason for visit.
* **Social History** (7): A text field where you can provide details about the patient's social history.
* **Family History** (8): A text field where you can provide details about the patient's family history.
* **Medical/Surgical Notes** (9): A text field where you can provide any additional medical notes or surgical details.
* **Review Status** (10): Select one of the options to indicate the review status of the patient. This field is optional.
	+ "Active" indicates the patient is currently active, "Inactive" indicates they are not active.
* **Date of Last Examination** (11): A date field where you can provide the date of the last examination.
* **Patient History And Physical Form Submitted By** (12): A text field where you can provide the name of the healthcare provider who submitted the form.

## Tips
- Always review the form carefully before submitting it.
- If you're unsure about any field, please consult with a healthcare professional.
- Ensure that you complete all required fields before submitting the form.
