# Pediatric Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Therapy Intake Form is designed to gather information about the patient's medical history, therapy history, and other relevant medical information to better understand their needs for therapy. This form is mandatory for all patients seeking pediatric therapy services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **General Information**: Fill out the general information page with your child's basic details.
2. **Medical History**: Provide a detailed history of your child's medical conditions, including any allergies or medications they are taking.
3. **Therapy History**: Indicate if your child has had any previous therapy sessions or treatments.
4. **Medical Contact**: If your child has a medical contact person, enter their information in the Medical Contact field.
5. **Emergency Contact**: Indicate if you have designated an emergency contact person.
6. **Allergies and Medications**: List any allergies or medications your child is taking.
7. **Family History**: Enter any relevant medical history about your child's family members.
8. **Additional Medical Conditions**: Describe any additional medical conditions your child may have.
9. **Allergy Information**: Provide any additional information about your child's allergies.
10. **Emergency Contact Information**: Enter any relevant contact information for your child's emergency contact person.
