# Blood Sugar Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's risk for blood sugar issues. It's essential to complete this form carefully and truthfully to get an accurate assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Fill out the demographic information (full name, date of birth).
3. Provide accurate information about your medical history, lifestyle habits, and health conditions.
4. Select the correct options for each field based on your experiences and circumstances.
5. Review the risk category and recommendations provided at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (2, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (4, select_one, required): Select your gender as Male, Female, Other, or Prefer Not to Say.
* **Height** (5, text, required): Enter your height in feet and inches (e.g., 5'9" = 5 feet 9 inches).
* **Weight** (6, number, required): Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Family History of Diabetes** (7, select_one, required): Select Yes Parent or Sibling, Yes Other Relative, False, or Not Sure.
* **Physical Activity Level** (8, select_one, required): Select Very Active 5 plus days, Moderately Active 3-4 days, Lightly Active 1-2 days, or Sedentary.
* **Diet Quality** (9, select_one, required): Select Very Healthy, Mostly Healthy, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Sugary Drink Consumption** (10, select_one, required): Select Daily, Several Times Weekly, Once Weekly, Rarely, or Never.
* **Processed Food Intake** (11, select_one, required): Select Most Meals, Daily, Several Times Weekly, Occasionally, or Rarely.
* **Previous High Blood Sugar Reading** (12, select_one, required): Select True, False, or Not Sure.
* **History of Gestational Diabetes** (13, select_one, required): Select True, False, or Not Applicable (if female).
* **PcOS History** (14, select_one, required): Select True, False, or Not Applicable (if female).
* **High Blood Pressure** (15, select_one, required): Select True, False, or Not Sure.
* **High Cholesterol** (16, select_one, required): Select True, False, or Not Sure.
* **Smoking Status** (17, select_one, required): Select Current Smoker, Former Smoker, or Never Smoked.
* **Sleep Quality** (18, select_one, required): Select Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Stress Level** (19, select_one, required): Select Low, Moderate, High, or Very High.
* **Current Medications** (20, text, optional): List any current medications.
* **Other Medical Conditions** (21, text, optional): List any other health issues.
* **Risk Category** (22, select_one, required): Select Low Risk, Moderate Risk, High Risk, or Very High Risk.
* **Recommendation** (23, select_one, required): Select Maintain Healthy Lifestyle, Lifestyle Changes Recommended, Consult Healthcare Provider, Testing Recommended.
* **Additional Notes** (24, text, optional): Enter any other information.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering each field.
* Review the risk category and recommendations carefully.
* Discuss any concerns or questions with a healthcare provider.
* Follow the recommended course of action to maintain a healthy lifestyle or address any health concerns.
