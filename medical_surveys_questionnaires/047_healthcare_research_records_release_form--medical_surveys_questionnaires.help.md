# Healthcare Research Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Healthcare Research Records Release Form" is designed to facilitate the release of patient records for healthcare research purposes. This form helps researchers obtain necessary permissions from patients to access their medical records and conduct research without any legal or moral implications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring you provide accurate information.
2. If you have any questions, please contact <contact_info>.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Records Release Form**
	+ <name> (`research_records_release_form_page_1`, text, optional)
	+ This is a free-text field for any additional notes or comments you have about the form.
* **Release Purpose** (`release_purpose`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose the purpose for which you want to release the patient's records. Options are "Research" and "Talent Acquisition".
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional)
	+ Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Name Of Researcher** (`name_of_researcher`, text, optional)
	+ Provide your name and any relevant title or designation.
* **Affiliation** (`affiliation`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the name of your institution or organization.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your contact information (email or phone number) for any follow-up inquiries.
* **Research Institution** (`research_institution`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the name of the institution conducting the research.
* **Purpose Statement** (`purpose_statement`, text, optional)
	+ Describe the purpose of your research, including what you aim to accomplish.
* **Research Funding Source** (`research_funding_source`, text, optional)
	+ If applicable, indicate the source of your research funding.
* **Research Funding Source Amount** (`research_funding_source_amount`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the amount of funding provided for your research.

## Tips

* Ensure you have the necessary permissions from patients or their legal representatives before submitting this form.
* Verify that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you need help or have questions, contact <contact_info>.
