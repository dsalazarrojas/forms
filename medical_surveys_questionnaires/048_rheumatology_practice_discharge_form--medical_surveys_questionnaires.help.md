# Rheumatology Practice Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rheumatology Practice Discharge Form is used to document the details of a patient's discharge from rheumatology care, ensuring a smooth transition and accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Patient Name** in the field provided.
2. Provide the **Patient Date of Birth** to accurately identify the patient.
3. Specify the **condition** being treated.
4. List any **medications** currently being taken by the patient.
5. Write a **Treatment Plan** for the patient's continued care.
6. List any **Medications** that the patient will continue taking or new ones prescribed.
7. Select the **Discharge Status**, indicating the patient's current status (e.g., discharged, transferred, etc.).
8. Schedule a **Follow-up Date** for the patient's next appointment.
9. Specify the **Follow-up Time** for the next appointment.
10. Enter the name of the **Staff Member Name** who handled the discharge process.
11. Enter the **Staff Member Designation** (e.g., doctor, nurse, etc.).
12. Write any **Next Steps**, detailing the next actions required for the patient.
13. Enter the name of the **Submitted By**, who initiated the discharge process.
14. Enter the **Submitted By Designation** (e.g., doctor, nurse, etc.).
15. Specify the **Patient Goals**, detailing any specific objectives for the patient's recovery.
16. Enter any additional **Patient Goals Other** that are not listed.
17. Describe any **Patient Goals Other Description** for the added goal.
18. Enter the date and time the form was **Submitted On**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical records.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format (month/day/year).
* **Condition**: Describe the specific rheumatology condition being treated.
* **Medication**: List any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Treatment Plan**: Outline the recommended treatment and care steps for the patient's continued recovery.
* **Medications List**: Select any medications the patient will continue taking after discharge or those prescribed during treatment.
* **Discharge Status**: Choose the patient's current status (e.g., discharged, transferred, etc.).
* **Follow-up Date**: Schedule the date for the patient's next appointment.
* **Follow-up Time**: Specify the time for the next appointment.
* **Staff Member Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare professional handling the discharge process.
* **Staff Member Designation**: Enter the designation of the healthcare professional (e.g., doctor, nurse, etc.).
* **Next Steps**: Detail any next actions required for the patient's recovery.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the healthcare professional who initiated the discharge process.
* **Submitted By Designation**: Enter the designation of the healthcare professional (e.g., doctor, nurse, etc.).
* **Patient Goals**: Specify the patient's specific objectives for their recovery.
* **Patient Goals Other**: Enter any additional patient goals not listed.
* **Patient Goals Other Description**: Describe any additional patient goals.
* **Submitted On**: Enter the date and time the form was submitted.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by healthcare professionals, ensuring accurate and consistent data entry for patient records.
