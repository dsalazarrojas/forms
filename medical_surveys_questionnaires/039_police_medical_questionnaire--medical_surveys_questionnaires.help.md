# Police Medical Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect sensitive personal and medical information from individuals. It is essential to fill this form out carefully and truthfully to ensure accurate representation and proper treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start by reading the form carefully and understanding the purpose of each question.
* Fill out each field with the requested information.
* Ensure that each question is answered accurately and truthfully.
* Review the form for completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Medical Survey
* **Medical Survey** (`medical_survey`, text, not required): This is a free-text field for any pre-existing medical conditions or concerns the individual wishes to report.
* **Personal History** (`personal_history`, text, not required): This field is for the individual to provide any relevant personal history or circumstances they think would be helpful for the officer filling out the form.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, not required): This field is for the individual to provide any pre-existing medical history they think is relevant.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, not required): This field is for the individual to report any pre-existing medical conditions or concerns.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, not required): This field is for the individual to report any prescribed medications they are currently taking.
* **Physical Examination** (`physical_examination`, text, not required): This field is for the individual to provide details of any recent or ongoing physical examinations or medical procedures they have undergone.
* **Mental Health** (`mental_health`, text, not required): This field is for the individual to report any pre-existing mental health concerns or conditions.

### Page 2: Police Medical History
* **Police Medical History** (`police_medical_history`, text, not required): This field is for the individual to provide any relevant police medical history or concerns.

### Tips
* If unsure about any question, ask for clarification or assistance.
* If the individual has difficulty filling out the form, provide support and guidance.
* Ensure the form is filled out carefully and truthfully to ensure accurate representation.
* Review the form for completeness before submission.
