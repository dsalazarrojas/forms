# Hernia And Varicose Vein Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hernia And Varicose Vein Assessment form is used to gather crucial information about a patient's hernia and varicose vein condition. This information is essential for medical professionals to assess the severity of the condition and provide the best possible treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the patient's medical history and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2.  For each field, provide accurate and complete information based on the patient's condition.
3.  Make sure to check the boxes or select the relevant options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (text, not required): Please provide a brief introduction or description of the patient's condition.
* **Symptoms** (select multiple, required): Check all the boxes that apply to the patient's symptoms, such as experiencing pain or discomfort in their abdomen or groin.
* **Pain Location** (text, required): Describe the location of the patient's pain or discomfort, e.g., lower abdomen, upper abdomen, groin, or other (please specify).
* **Pain Duration** (text, not required): Estimate the duration of the patient's pain or discomfort, e.g., hours, days, weeks, or months.
* **Varicose Vein History** (text, required): Provide information about the patient's varicose vein history, including any previous treatments or surgeries.
* **Family History** (text, not required): Provide information about the patient's family history of hernia or varicose veins.
* **Pain Characteristics** (select one, required): Select the option that best describes the patient's pain or discomfort, such as sharp, dull, or burning.
* **Medical History** (text, required): Provide a detailed medical history, including any previous surgeries, medical conditions, or allergies.
* **Treatment Goals** (text, required): Describe the patient's treatment goals, such as relief from pain or improvement in overall health.
* **Current Medication** (text, not required): List the patient's current medication, including any prescription or non-prescription medications.
* **Allergies** (select multiple, required): Check all the boxes that apply to the patient's allergies, such as latex or medications.
* **Previous Surgeries** (text, not required): Provide information about the patient's previous surgeries, including any relevant medical details.
* **Medical Conditions** (text, required): Provide information about the patient's current medical conditions, including any previous diagnoses or treatments.
* **Consent** (select one, required): Select the option that best describes the patient's consent, such as yes or no.
* **Patient Name** (text, required): Provide the patient's name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Time** (time, required): Enter the time of the assessment.
