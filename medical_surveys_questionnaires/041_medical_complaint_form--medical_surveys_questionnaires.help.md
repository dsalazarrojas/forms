# Medical Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Complaint Form is a tool for patients to report their medical complaints or concerns to healthcare professionals. It is a crucial step in the medical process, as it helps healthcare providers understand the patient's symptoms, medical history, and current conditions. This form should only be completed by the patient or their authorized representative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Carefully read the form and ensure you understand its purpose and the information required.
2. Enter your name and age in the respective fields.
3. Select the medical complaint type from the dropdown list that best describes your symptoms or concerns.
4. Check any other symptoms or complaints that apply to your condition.
5. Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes, if applicable.
6. Review and submit the completed form.
