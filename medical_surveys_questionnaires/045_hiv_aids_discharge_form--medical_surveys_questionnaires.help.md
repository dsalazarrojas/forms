# HIV/AIDS Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the discharge information of patients with HIV/AIDS. It is essential for medical professionals to accurately capture the patient's details for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the HIV/AIDS Discharge Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's **Patient Name** in the first field.
2. Provide the patient's **Contact Number** in the second field.
3. Fill in the **Medical Record Number** field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical records.
* **Contact Number** (text, required): Provide the patient's contact number, which can be a phone number, email, or any other contact information.
* **Medical Record Number** (text, optional): Enter the patient's medical record number if you have access to it.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the patient's contact number.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the patient's medical team or hospital administration for clarification.
- This form is intended to be a reference tool for future medical care; ensure that all fields are filled accurately to avoid any confusion.
