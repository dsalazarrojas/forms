# Men's Hormonal Health Lab Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by medical professionals to document and review lab results for patients undergoing hormone-related tests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
2. Ensure that you have obtained the necessary patient consent to collect and use their medical information.
3. Use the form to document the results of the lab review, including any relevant comments or notes.
4. Update the form regularly to reflect any changes in the patient's status or follow-up information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Men's Hormonal Health Lab Review** (<type:text>): A brief description of the lab review process and its purpose.
* **Medical Specialist Name** (<type:text>): The name of the medical specialist performing the review.
* **Lab Review Date** (<type:date>): The date the lab review was performed.
* **Lab Review Time** (<type:time>): The time the lab review was performed.
* **Lab Review Comments** (<type:text>): Any additional comments or notes from the medical specialist.
* **Lab Review Results** (<type:text>): The results of the lab review, including any relevant findings or recommendations.
* **Medical Specialist Comments** (<type:text>): Any additional comments or notes from the medical specialist.
* **Patient Medical Record Number** (<type:text>): The patient's medical record number.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (<type:date>): The patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Weight** (<type:text>): The patient's weight.
* **Patient Height** (<type:text>): The patient's height.
* **Patient Gender** (<type:select_one>): The patient's gender (male, female, or other).
* **Lab Review Follow-up Comments** (<type:text>): Any additional comments or notes for follow-up information.
* **Lab Review Follow-up Status** (<type:select_one>): The status of the lab review follow-up (pending, completed, cancelled, declined, rescheduled, or on-hold).
* **Lab Review Follow-up Date** (<type:date>): The date of the lab review follow-up.
* **Lab Review Follow-up Time** (<type:time>): The time of the lab review follow-up.
* **Medical Specialist Email** (<type:email>): The medical specialist's email address.
* **Lab Review Comments** (<type:text>): Any additional comments or notes from the medical specialist for the lab review follow-up.
* **Lab Review Follow-up Comments** (<type:text>): Any additional comments or notes for the lab review follow-up.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when completing the form to ensure that the patient's information is up-to-date and accurate.
* Use clear and concise language when entering comments or notes.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and consents to collect and use the patient's medical information.
* Regularly review and update the form to reflect any changes in the patient's status or follow-up information.
