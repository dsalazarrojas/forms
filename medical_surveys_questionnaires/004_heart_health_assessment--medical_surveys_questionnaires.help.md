# Heart Health Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this Heart Health Assessment form is to gather information about an individual's heart health risk factors and habits. This data will help healthcare professionals understand the individual's risk level for heart disease and provide personalized recommendations for improving heart health.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the correct answers for each section based on your personal experiences and habits.
2.  Fill in the required fields, which are denoted with an asterisk (\*).
3.  Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
4.  Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Introduction** (`Introduction`, Note, Optional):
    This section is a brief introduction to the form and explains its purpose.
*  **Heart Health Risk Factors** (`Heart failure`, `High blood pressure`, `Diabetes`, `Smoking`, `Family history of heart disease`, Select multiple, Optional):
    Answer the questions based on your personal experiences and habits.
    Select all the heart health risk factors that apply to you.
*  **Medical History** (`True`, `False`, Select one, Optional):
    Answer `True` if you have a medical condition, `False` otherwise.
*  **Medications** (`text`, Optional):
    List any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
*  **Smoking History** (`Current smoker`, `Former smoker`, `Never smoked`, Select multiple, Optional):
    Answer based on your smoking habits.
*  **Alcohol Use** (`Never`, `Occasionally`, `Frequently`, Select one, Optional):
    Answer based on your drinking habits.
*  **Physical Activity** (`text`, Optional):
    Describe your physical activity level in the past 6 months or 1 year.
*  **Height** (`number`, Optional):
    Enter your height in feet and inches.
*  **Family History** (`First degree relative`, `Second degree relative`, `Third degree relative`, Select multiple, Optional):
    Select all family members who have heart disease.
*  **Blood Pressure** (`number`, Optional):
    Enter your blood pressure reading.
*  **Heart Disease Family History** (`First degree relative`, `Second degree relative`, `Third degree relative`, Select multiple, Optional):
    Select all family members who have heart disease.
*  **Date of Last Baby Born** (`date`, Optional):
    Enter the date of the last baby born.
*  **Date of Last Hospitalization** (`date`, Optional):
    Enter the date of your last hospitalization.
*  **Date of Last Medications** (`date`, Optional):
    Enter the date of your last medications.
*  **Date of Last Smoking** (`date`, Optional):
    Enter the date of your last smoking.
*  **Date of Last Alcohol Use** (`date`, Optional):
    Enter the date of your last alcohol use.
*  **Physical Activity Last 6 Months** (`text`, Optional):
    Describe your physical activity level in the past 6 months.
*  **Physical Activity Last 1 Year** (`text`, Optional):
    Describe your physical activity level in the past 1 year.
*  **Height in Feet** (`number`, Optional):
    Enter your height in feet.
*  **Height in Inches** (`number`, Optional):
    Enter your height in inches.
*  **Weight** (`number`, Optional):
    Enter your weight.
*  **BMI** (`number`, Optional):
    Enter your body mass index.
*  **Blood Glucose** (`number`, Optional):
    Enter your blood glucose reading.

Note: Please review this form carefully before submitting it, and answer all fields truthfully and accurately. The information gathered from this form will be used for general assessment and education purposes only.
