## Plastic Surgery Patient Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Plastic Surgery Patient Form is designed to collect essential information about the patient, including medical history, contact details, and personal information. This data will help medical professionals assess the patient's suitability for plastic surgery procedures and create a personalized treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information**: This section collects general information about the patient. Please fill in your name, date of birth, and address in the respective fields.
2. **Contact Information**: Provide your contact number and any additional contact details if available.
3. **Medical History**: Answer the medical history questions honestly and thoroughly. If you have undergone previous surgeries, please specify their details in the "Previous Surgeries" field. If you have any medical conditions, please choose from the options provided (Diabetes, Blood Pressure, Heart Conditions, or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Previous Surgeries** (`4`, `number`, required: `false`): This field is used to collect information about any previous surgeries you may have had. Please provide the number of previous surgeries and a brief description of each, if necessary.
* **Allergies** (`5`, `number`, required: `false`): This field is used to collect information about any allergies you may have. Please provide the number of allergies and a brief description of each, if necessary.
* **Medical Conditions** (`6`, `select_one`, required: `false`): This field is used to collect information about any medical conditions you may have. Please select one or more from the options provided (Diabetes, Blood Pressure, Heart Conditions, or Other).

* **Date of Birth** (`8`, `date`, required: `false`): This field is used to collect your date of birth, which will be used to verify your age for the surgery.
* **Contact Number** (`9`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is used to collect your contact number, which will be used to communicate with you about your surgery.
* **Address** (`10`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is used to collect your address, which will be used to communicate with you about your surgery.
