# patient_task_observation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about patient tasks, observations, or assessments. It helps healthcare providers track and monitor patient progress, identify potential issues, and provide targeted interventions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each field according to your knowledge and observation of the patient's task performance.
2. Select the relevant options for task frequency, task location, and task duration.
3. Optionally, add any additional notes or contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Task Performance 1** (`patient_task_performance_1`, `text`, required): Please describe the patient's performance in the task.
* **Patient Task Performance 2** (`patient_task_performance_2`, `text`, required): Please describe the patient's performance in the second task.
* **Task Frequency** (`task_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **Task Location** (`task_location`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Clinic
	+ Home
	+ Other
* **Task Duration** (`task_duration`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Less than 15 minutes
	+ 15-30 minutes
	+ 30-60 minutes
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the patient's task performance.
* **Contact** (`email_contact`, `email`, optional): Enter the patient's email address for future communication.
* **Phone** (`phone_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's phone number for future communication.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer each field according to your knowledge and observation of the patient's task performance.
- Be as specific as possible when describing the patient's performance.
- If you are unsure about any of the options, choose the one that best fits your situation.
