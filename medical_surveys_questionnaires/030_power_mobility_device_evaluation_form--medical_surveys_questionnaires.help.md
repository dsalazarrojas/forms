# Power Mobility Device Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Power Mobility Device Evaluation Form is a tool designed to gather data on the effectiveness and usability of Power Mobility Devices. It's meant to provide valuable insights for further research and development in the field of assistive technologies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Applicant Information section with your name, address, and contact details.
2. Provide the contact information of up to eight medical professionals who have worked with you or assessed your mobility needs.
3. Enter the names of up to ten medical professionals who have provided input on your mobility device usage and effectiveness.
