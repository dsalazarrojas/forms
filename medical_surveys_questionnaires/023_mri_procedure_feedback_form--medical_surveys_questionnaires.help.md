# MRI Procedure Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients after an MRI procedure. It helps us understand your experience and satisfaction with our service. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our procedures and better serve you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the correct options for each field.
3. Provide clear and concise feedback in the "Comments" field.
4. Submit the form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: Please provide your personal details such as name, email, and phone number.
* **MRI Procedure**: Please select the type of MRI procedure you underwent.
* **Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with our service by selecting one or more of the options.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions about your experience.
* **Name**: Please provide your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Medical Department**: Enter the name of the medical department that handled your procedure.
* **Doctor's Name**: Provide the name of the doctor who performed the procedure.
* **Appointment Date**: Enter the date of your appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: Enter the time of your appointment.
* **Procedure Type**: Select whether you underwent a yes or no MRI procedure.
* **Feedback Score**: Rate your satisfaction with our service by selecting one or more scores.
* **Feedback Text**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Rating**: Enter a numerical rating of your satisfaction.
* **Staff Name**: Enter the name of the staff member who handled your procedure.
* **Department**: Enter the name of the department where you received the procedure.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your appointment.
* **Time**: Enter the time of your appointment.
* **Medical Condition**: Provide any medical conditions related to your procedure.
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history related to your procedure.
* **Medication**: Enter any medication you took before or after the procedure.
* **Patient Age**: Enter your age.
