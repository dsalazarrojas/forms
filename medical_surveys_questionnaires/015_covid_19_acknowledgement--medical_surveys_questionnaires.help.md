# Covid 19 Acknowledgement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for patients to acknowledge that they have been informed and understood about the risks and precautions related to COVID-19, as well as their own medical conditions and the medical staff's protocols for handling their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the instructions and guidance provided on each page carefully.
* Answer the questions and provide accurate information about yourself.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Acknowledgement** (`patient_acknowledgement`, note, required/optional): This is an open-ended field for you to write your acknowledgement about the risks and precautions related to COVID-19. Please take this opportunity to confirm that you have understood and will comply with the necessary safety measures and protocols.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it is written on your identification documents or patient records.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your home address or the address where you can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Patient Contact Number** (`patient_contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number or other contact information where you can be reached for updates or concerns about your care.
