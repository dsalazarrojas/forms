# Health Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect general information, medical history, and physical examination details from patients to assess their health status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the "General Info" field with any relevant general information about the patient.
2. Answer the "Questions" section with any relevant medical history or concerns.
3. For the "Physical Exam" section, select one of the options (Good, Fair, or Poor) to describe the patient's physical condition.
4. Fill in the "Medical History" fields with any additional medical history or concerns the patient may have. Please be as specific as possible when filling in these fields.
