# health_examination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about a patient's health and medical history during a clinical examination or consultation. It includes questions about patient demographics, medical history, medications, allergies, medical conditions, vital signs, and information about the medical practitioner performing the examination.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. Be honest and thorough when providing information about the patient's medical history, medications, allergies, and medical conditions.
3. If you are unsure about any of the questions or need clarification, ask the patient or their guardian for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`page: 1`, `id: 1`): This section is for demographic information about the patient, such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Medical History** (`page: 1`, `id: 2`): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical conditions.
* **Medications** (`page: 1`, `id: 3`): List all the medications the patient is currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Allergies** (`page: 1`, `id: 4`): List any allergies the patient has, including severity and treatment.
* **Medical Conditions** (`page: 1`, `id: 5`): Describe any current medical conditions the patient has, such as diabetes, hypertension, or asthma.
* **Vital Signs** (`page: 1`, `id: 6`): Record the patient's vital signs, including temperature, pulse, blood pressure, and respiratory rate.
* **Medical History (2)** (`page: 1`, `id: 7`): This is a duplicate of the medical history section, please only fill this out if necessary.
* **Medical Practitioner** (`page: 1`, `id: 8`): This section is for the medical practitioner's information, including name and title.
* **Medical Practitioner Info** (`page: 1`, `id: 9`): This section is for additional information about the medical practitioner, such as their qualifications or contact details.
* **Medical Practitioner Specialties** (`page: 1`, `id: 10`): List the medical practitioner's specialties, such as pediatrics or cardiology.
* **Medical Practitioner Experience** (`page: 1`, `id: 11`): Record the medical practitioner's experience in years.

## Tips

* Double-check the patient's information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If the patient has any unusual or complex medical conditions, consult with the medical practitioner for guidance.
* Ensure the form is signed and dated by the medical practitioner before storing it in the patient's file.
* This form should only be used in a clinical or healthcare setting. If you are unsure, consult with your organization's policy and procedures.
