# data_analytics_in_medicine_research_evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback and opinions from medical professionals and researchers on the use of data analytics in medicine research. Its main objective is to collect information on the outcomes of medical studies, the challenges faced by researchers, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a short description of your background and expertise in the field of medicine research. Please provide a brief introduction to help us understand your perspective.
* **Medical Studies Outcomes**: Please select one of the options to indicate whether you have experienced positive or negative outcomes from using data analytics in medicine research.
* **Data Analytics Challenges**: Please select one of the options to indicate whether you have faced any challenges while using data analytics in medicine research.
* **Improvement Suggestions**: Please select all that apply to suggest potential improvements for data analytics in medicine research.
* **Hospitals**: How many hospitals have you involved in your research?
* **Universities**: How many universities have you collaborated with on your research?
* **Research Teams**: How many research teams have you worked with on your research?
* **Time to Complete**: How many hours or days do you take to complete a typical data analytics project in medicine research?

**Tips**

* Take your time to answer each question carefully.
* If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the "Additional Comments" section.
