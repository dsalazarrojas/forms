# Body Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Body Assessment Form is a collection of questions designed to gather information about a patient's personal and medical history, including demographic details, medical conditions, and skincare habits. This form will help our team understand your needs and concerns, allowing us to provide personalized guidance and recommendations tailored to your skin.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill in all required fields.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, select all applicable options.
3. For text fields, provide clear and concise answers.
4. Do not leave any page uncompleted.
5. Ensure you have authorization and acceptance of the terms and conditions at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Date of Birth** (2, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Height - Feet** (5, number, required): Enter your height in feet.
* **Height - Inches** (6, number, required): Enter your height in inches.
* **Weight in Pounds** (7, number, required): Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Gender** (8, select_one, required): Select your gender.
* **Skin Type** (9, select_one, required): Select your skin type.
* **Current Skin Condition** (10, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable skin conditions you currently experience.
* **Known Skin Allergies** (11, text, optional): If you have any known skin allergies, enter them here.
* **Relevant Medical Conditions** (12, text, optional): If you have any relevant medical conditions, enter them here.
* **Current Medications** (13, text, optional): If you are currently taking any medications, enter them here.
* **Areas of Body Concern** (14, select_multiple, required): Select all areas of your body you are concerned about.
* **What are your skincare goals** (15, select_multiple, required): Select all your skincare goals.
* **Average Daily Sun Exposure** (16, select_one, required): Select your average daily sun exposure.
* **Do you regularly use sunscreen** (17, select_one, required): Select your sunscreen usage.
* **Current Skincare Routine** (18, text, optional): Describe your current skincare routine.
* **Sensitive to Any Skincare Products** (19, text, optional): If you are sensitive to any skincare products, enter them here.
* **Lifestyle Factors Affecting Skin** (20, text, optional): If you think any lifestyle factors affect your skin, enter them here.
* **Prior Skin Treatments or Procedures** (21, text, optional): If you have had any skin treatments or procedures, enter them here.
* **What results do you hope to achieve** (22, text, optional): Describe the results you hope to achieve from this assessment and consultation.
* **I authorize this assessment and consultation** (23, select_one, required): Select to confirm you authorize this assessment and consultation.
* **I accept the terms and conditions** (24, select_one, required): Select to confirm you accept the terms and conditions.

Note: Please make sure to read and understand the terms and conditions before selecting "I Accept".
