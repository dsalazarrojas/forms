# Health Information Sharing Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Information Sharing Preferences Survey is designed to collect your health information sharing preferences to ensure that your healthcare provider has a clear understanding of your preferences for sharing your personal health information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions according to your preferences.
2. For each question, choose the option that best represents your preference.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Preferences** (`1`, `text`, optional): This field is used to capture any general preferences you have for sharing your health information. Please use this space to provide any relevant details or instructions you want to share with your healthcare provider.
* **Sharing With** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks if you want to share your health information with others. Select "Yes" if you want to share with someone, otherwise select "No".
* **Sharing For** (`3`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks with whom you want to share your health information. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Data Collection** (`4`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about your preferences for data collection. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Consent** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks if you consent to sharing your health information. Select "Yes" if you give your consent, otherwise select "No".
* **Preferences** (`6`, `note`, optional): This is a text field for you to provide any additional preferences or comments.
* **Sharing With Hospital** (`7`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks with whom you want to share your health information at the hospital. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Sharing With Family** (`8`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks with whom you want to share your health information with your family. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Sharing With Provider** (`9`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks with whom you want to share your health information with your provider. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Sharing For Treatment** (`10`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks for what purpose you want to share your health information for treatment. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Sharing For Research** (`11`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks for what purpose you want to share your health information for research. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Sharing For Billing** (`12`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks for what purpose you want to share your health information for billing. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Sharing For Coverage** (`13`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks for what purpose you want to share your health information for coverage. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Data Retention** (`14`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about your preferences for data retention. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Contact Preferences** (`15`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about your contact preferences. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Contact Method** (`16`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks how you want to be contacted. Select "Yes" if you want to be contacted through the selected method, otherwise select "No".
* **Sharing With Others** (`17`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks with whom you want to share your health information with others. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Sharing For Others** (`18`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks for what purpose you want to share your health information with others. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Sharing With Hospitals** (`19`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks with whom you want to share your health information with hospitals. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Sharing With Insurance** (`20`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks with whom you want to share your health information with insurance companies. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Sharing With Emergency** (`21`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks with whom you want to share your health information with emergency services. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Sharing With Brokers** (`22`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks with whom you want to share your health information with brokers. Select all the options that apply to you.

## Tips

* Please take your time to review each question carefully and select the options that best reflect your preferences.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider.
* This form is designed to be completed by patients, and it's essential to answer all questions truthfully to ensure that your healthcare provider has accurate information about your preferences.
