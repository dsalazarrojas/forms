# 2025 FASXA Surgery Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 2025 FASXA Surgery Report Form is designed to collect comprehensive information about a surgical procedure performed on an animal. This form helps medical professionals and researchers document key details of the surgery, including owner and patient information, surgical and anesthesia details, and postoperative status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the full legal name of the owner or primary contact person on page 1, section 2.
2. Provide the owner's phone number, including the country code if necessary, on page 1, section 3.
3. Enter the owner's email address for post-operative updates on page 1, section 4.
4. Enter the animal's name or identifier on page 1, section 5.
5. Select the species of the animal on page 1, section 6.
6. Enter the breed or strain of the animal if known on page 1, section 7.
7. Record the animal's date of birth or approximate date of birth on page 1, section 8.
8. Enter the animal's age in years on page 1, section 9.
9. On page 2, select the date of surgery on section 11.
10. Enter the primary surgical procedure performed on page 2, section 12.
11. Enter the name of the surgeon or operator on page 2, section 13.
12. Choose the type of anesthesia used during the surgery on page 2, section 14.
13. Select any intraoperative anesthesia complications that occurred on page 2, section 15.
14. Enter the estimated amount of intraoperative blood loss in milliliters on page 2, section 16.
15. List any intraoperative medications and doses administered on page 2, section 17.
16. Select the postoperative status on page 2, section 18.
17. Enter any analgesics administered during recovery on page 2, section 19.
18. Enter the IACUC protocol or study ID if applicable on page 2, section 20.
19. Provide any surgeon notes and deviations from protocol on page 2, section 21.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner / Primary contact full name**: The full legal name of the owner or primary contact person.
* **Owner phone number**: The phone number of the owner or primary contact person, including the country code if necessary.
* **Owner email address**: The email address of the owner or primary contact person for post-operative updates.
* **Animal / patient name or identifier**: The name or identifier of the animal (e.g., cage/study ID).
* **Species**: The species of the animal (e.g., Dog, Cat, Rabbit, Rodent, Other).
* **Breed or strain**: The breed or strain of the animal if known (e.g., Labrador, Golden Retriever, Cavia).
* **Date of birth / approximate DOB**: The date of birth or approximate date of birth of the animal.
* **Age (years)**: The age of the animal in years (round down to the nearest whole number).
* **Date of surgery**: The date of the surgical procedure.
* **Primary surgical procedure**: A detailed description of the surgical procedure performed (e.g., ovariohysterectomy, exploratory laparotomy).
* **Surgeon / operator name**: The name of the surgeon or operator, including initials and role if part of a team.
* **Type of anesthesia used**: The type of anesthesia used during the surgery (e.g., General anesthesia, Local anesthesia, Sedation only, Regional block).
* **Intraoperative anesthesia complications**: Select all that apply from the list of potential complications (e.g., Hypotension, Bradycardia, Hypoventilation, Allergic reaction, Prolonged recovery).
* **Estimated intraoperative blood loss (mL)**: The estimated volume of blood lost during the surgery in milliliters.
* **Intraoperative medications and doses**: A list of medications and their doses administered during the surgery.
* **Postoperative status**: The status of the animal at the conclusion of the surgery (e.g., Uneventful recovery, Minor complications expected, Major complications anticipated, Euthanasia performed).
* **Analgesics administered (name, dose, route, time)**: A list of analgesics administered during recovery, including name, dose, route, and time.
* **IACUC protocol or study ID**: The IACUC protocol or study ID if applicable.
* **Surgeon operative notes and deviations from protocol**: Any notes from the surgeon about the procedure, including any deviations from the protocol.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you have permission from the owner or primary contact person to collect and share their contact information.
- If you're unsure about any field or require clarification, please reach out to the relevant authorities or medical professionals.
