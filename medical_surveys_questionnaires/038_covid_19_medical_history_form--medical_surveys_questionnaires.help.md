# COVID 19 Medical History Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical history information from patients, including personal details, medical conditions, medications, allergies, and COVID-19 history. This information is essential for healthcare professionals to provide accurate and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details, including your full name and date of birth.
2. List any chronic medical conditions you have, such as diabetes or hypertension.
3. Describe any recent surgeries you have had in the past 6 months.
4. Indicate whether you have been hospitalized in the past year, and if so, provide the reason for hospitalization.
5. List all your current medications and dosages.
6. Indicate whether you have drug allergies, and if so, list the drugs that cause allergies.
7. List any other allergies you may have (e.g., latex, food, environmental).
8. Answer questions about your COVID-19 history, including whether you have had a prior infection, the date of infection, and the severity of the illness.
9. Indicate whether you have received COVID-19 vaccines and list the dates of vaccination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`Patient Name`, `Text`, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `Date`, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `Text`, Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `Email`, Required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Chronic Medical Conditions** (`Chronic Conditions`, `Select Multiple`, Optional): Select all chronic medical conditions you have, such as diabetes or hypertension.
* **Describe any other conditions** (`Describe any other conditions`, `Text`, Optional): Provide a brief description of any other medical conditions you have that are not listed above.
* **Recent surgeries in past 6 months** (`Recent surgeries in past 6 months`, `Select One`, Required): Indicate whether you have had a recent surgery in the past 6 months.
* **Describe surgeries** (`Describe Surgeries`, `Text`, Optional): Describe any recent surgeries you have had, including the dates and reasons for the surgeries.
* **Hospitalizations in past year** (`Hospitalizations in past year`, `Select One`, Required): Indicate whether you have been hospitalized in the past year.
* **Reason for hospitalization** (`Reason for hospitalization`, `Text`, Optional): Describe the reason for hospitalization.
* **List all current medications** (`List all current medications`, `Text`, Required): List all your current medications and their dosages.
* **Drug Allergies** (`Drug Allergies`, `Select One`, Required): Indicate whether you have drug allergies.
* **List drug allergies and reactions** (`List drug allergies and reactions`, `Text`, Optional): List the drugs that cause allergies and describe any reactions.
* **Other allergies** (`Other Allergies`, `Text`, Optional): List any other allergies you have, such as latex, food, or environmental allergies.
* **COVID-19 History** (`COVID-19 History`, `Note`, Required): Read and understand the questions about COVID-19 history before proceeding.
* **Prior COVID-19 infection** (`Prior COVID-19 infection`, `Select One`, Required): Indicate whether you have had a prior COVID-19 infection.
* **Date of infection** (`Date of infection`, `Date`, Optional): Enter the date of your prior COVID-19 infection if you have had one.
* **Severity of illness** (`Severity of illness`, `Select One`, Optional): Describe the severity of your prior COVID-19 illness, such as asymptomatic, mild, or severe.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination History** (`COVID-19 Vaccination History`, `Note`, Required): Read and understand the questions about COVID-19 vaccination history before proceeding.
* **COVID-19 vaccines received** (`COVID-19 vaccines received`, `Select Multiple`, Required): Select all COVID-19 vaccines you have received.
* **Dates of vaccine doses** (`Dates of vaccine doses`, `Text`, Optional): Describe the dates of your COVID-19 vaccine doses if you have received any.

## Tips

Please ensure that all fields marked as "Required" are completed accurately and thoroughly. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
