# Covid 19 Antigen Testing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document patient information related to Covid-19 antigen testing. This includes medical history, symptoms, test results, and contact information to aid in the management and tracking of patient records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient information section, including medical history, with as much detail as possible.
2. Report any relevant symptoms you are experiencing.
3. Indicate your test results and the date and time of the test.
4. Confirm whether you have received medical certification.
5. Specify your doctor's contact information and address.
6. Confirm whether you have been certified by your doctor.
7. Provide any additional test information, such as antigen testing results and locations.
8. Complete the form with any other required information as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (1)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your full name, age, and any other relevant information about you as a patient.
* **Medical Info (2)** (`text`, required): Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any pre-existing conditions or allergies.
* **Vaccine Info (3)** (`text`, required): Report any recent vaccination history, including dates and types of vaccines administered.
* **Test Result (4)** (`text`, required/optional): Report the result of your Covid-19 antigen test.
* **Test Date (5)** (`date`, required): Enter the date of the test.
* **Test Time (6)** (`time`, required): Enter the time of the test.
* **Test Location (7)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate whether the test was performed at a hospital, clinic, or other location.
* **Patient Symptoms (8)** (`text`, required): Report any symptoms you are experiencing, such as fever or shortness of breath.
* **Medical Cert (9)** (`select_one`, required): Confirm whether you have received medical certification.
* **Patient Phone (10)** (`select_one`, required): Confirm your contact phone number with the healthcare system.
* **Patient Email (11)** (`select_one`, required): Confirm your email address with the healthcare system.
* **Doctor Info (12)** (`text`, required): Enter your doctor's full name and any other relevant information.
* **Doctor Phone (13)** (`select_one`, required): Confirm your doctor's contact phone number.
* **Doctor Email (14)** (`select_one`, required): Confirm your doctor's email address.
* **Patient Address (15)** (`text`, optional): Enter your home address if you choose to provide it.
* **Doctor Address (16)** (`text`, optional): Enter your doctor's address if they wish to share it.
* **Antigen Testing Info (17)** (`note`, required): Enter any relevant information about your antigen testing, including the location and date.
* **Antigen Testing Result (18)** (`select_multiple`, required): Report whether you have received a positive or negative antigen test result.

## Tips
* Ensure that you complete all required fields and answer questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Double-check the accuracy of your information to avoid errors.
* Ask for help if you need clarification on any section or field.
