# medication_adherence_motivation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's motivation, adherence level, and concerns related to taking medication for blood pressure, cholesterol, or diabetes management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the type of medication you are taking (page 2).
* Answer the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
* Provide details on your reasons for taking the medication (page 3).
* Choose your adherence level to the medication (page 4).
* Share your motivation for taking the medication (page 5).
* List any concerns you have about taking the medication (page 6).
* Describe any benefits you have experienced while taking the medication (page 7).
* If you have faced any challenges while taking the medication, describe them (page 8).
* Finally, provide any additional comments you would like to share about your medication experience (page 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What medication** (`medication_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of medication you are taking, such as "Blood pressure medication", "Cholesterol medication", or "Diabetes medication".
* **Why are you taking this medication** (`why_taking`, text, required: false): Explain why you are taking this medication. This is your opportunity to provide detailed information about your medical condition and how the medication helps you manage it.
* **Adherence level** (`adherence_level`, select_one, required: false): Indicate how well you adhere to taking your medication. Choose one of the options: "Always", "Most of the time", "Sometimes", "Rarely", or "Never".
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, text, required: false): Share what motivates you to take your medication regularly. This could be to manage your health, maintain energy, or for other reasons.
* **Concerns** (`concerns`, text, required: false): If you have any concerns or hesitations about taking your medication, list them here. This will help identify potential issues that need attention from healthcare professionals.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, text, required: false): If you face any challenges while taking your medication, describe them here. This could include side effects, cost, or other difficulties.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or insights you would like to share about your medication experience.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing thorough and honest answers will help us better understand your medication habits and experiences.
