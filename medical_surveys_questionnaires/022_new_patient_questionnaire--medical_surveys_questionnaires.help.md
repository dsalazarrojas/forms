# new_patient_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about new patients, including their medical history, allergies, medications, medical conditions, and contact information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information in each field. If a field is marked as "required", you must provide a valid answer. If a field is marked as "optional", you can skip it if you don't have the necessary information.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **patient_info** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant personal details.
- **medical_history** (2, text, required/optional): Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or treatments you have undergone.
- **allergies** (3, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the boxes that apply to your allergies, such as "Yes" for "Allergic to penicillin" or "No" for "Not allergic to any medications".
- **medications** (4, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the boxes that apply to your medications, such as "Yes" for "Take medication for high blood pressure" or "No" for "No medication".
- **medical_conditions** (5, text, required/optional): Describe any medical conditions you are currently experiencing, such as "Diabetes" or "Asthma".
- **medical_procedure** (6, text, required/optional): Describe any recent medical procedures or surgeries you have undergone.
- **contact_info** (7, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
- **emergency_contact** (8, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with a medical professional before completing the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us at [insert contact information].
* Please note that this form is for general purposes only, and it is not a substitute for regular medical care. If you have any symptoms or conditions that require immediate attention, please seek help from a medical professional.
