# Disability Benefits Medical Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Disability Benefits Medical Questionnaire is a form used to collect detailed information about the claimant's medical condition and history. This information is used to support a disability benefits claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your medical condition's name in the "Medical Condition Name" field (Page 1).
2. Provide a brief description of your medical condition in the "Medical Condition Description" field (Page 1).
3. Enter the date you were diagnosed with your medical condition (Page 2).
4. Enter your doctor's name and contact information (Page 2-3).
5. Provide your medical history (Page 3).
6. Enter the date you became disabled (Page 4).
7. Enter the name of your current medical provider (Page 4).
8. Provide your treatment plan (Page 5).
9. Attach any relevant medical reports (Page 5).
10. Enter information about your disability benefits claim (Page 6-7).
11. Enter your address, city, state, postal code, country, and phone number (Page 6-7).
12. Enter your email address (Page 7).
13. Indicate your relationship to the claimant (Page 8).
14. Enter your claimant's date of birth, sex, height, and weight (Page 8-9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Condition Name**: Enter the name of your medical condition, e.g., 'Hypertension' or 'Knee Injury'.
* **Medical Condition Description**: Briefly describe your medical condition in this field.
* **Date Diagnosed**: Enter the date you were diagnosed with your medical condition.
* **Doctor's Name**: Enter the name of your current doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Doctor's Contact Info**: Enter your doctor's contact information, including their phone number and email address.
* **Doctor's Contact Number**: Repeat the phone number of your doctor's contact information (if necessary).
* **Medical History**: Describe any relevant medical history that may be important to your claim.
* **Date of Disability**: Enter the date you became disabled due to your medical condition.
* **Medical Provider**: Enter the name of your current medical provider, if different from the previous answer.
* **Treatment Plan**: Describe your treatment plan and any ongoing care you are receiving.
* **Medical Report**: Attach any relevant medical reports that support your claim.
* **Disability Benefit Claimant**: Enter your information as the claimant, ensuring accuracy.
* **Disability Benefit Type**: Indicate whether you are seeking a Permanent, Temporary, or Short-Term Disability Benefit.
* **Claimant's Address**: Enter your address.
* **Claimant's City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **Claimant's State**: Enter the state where you reside.
* **Claimant's Postal Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Claimant's Country**: Enter your country of residence.
* **Claimant's Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Claimant's Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Claimant's Relationship**: Indicate your relationship to the claimant (e.g., self, parent, spouse).
* **Claimant's Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Claimant's Sex**: Enter your sex.
* **Claimant's Height**: Enter your height in centimeters (cm).
* **Claimant's Weight**: Enter your weight in kilograms (kg).

This guide helps ensure that you understand what each field requires, ensuring accurate and complete filling out of the form.
