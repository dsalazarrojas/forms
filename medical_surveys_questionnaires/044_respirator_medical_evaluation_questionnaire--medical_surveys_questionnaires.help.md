# Respirator Medical Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Respirator Medical Evaluation Questionnaire is designed to gather information about your medical history, current health status, and respirator use. This will help healthcare professionals understand your medical needs and provide proper care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in a clear and concise manner, answering each question to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that you answer all required fields, marked with a red asterisk (*).
3. If you need help with any questions or have a medical condition you are unsure about, please consult your healthcare provider.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure that they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Please describe your medical history, including any previous surgeries, hospitalizations, or medical conditions.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_one, required: false): Check if you have any of the following medical conditions:
	+ None
	+ True (yes)
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, required: false): List any medications you are currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required: false): Check any allergies you have:
	+ Penicillin
	+ Aspirin
	+ Other
* **Surgical History** (`medical_surgical_history`, text, required: false): Describe any previous surgeries or medical procedures you have undergone.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, required: false): Describe your family medical history, including any inherited medical conditions.
* **Smoking History** (`smoking_history`, text, required: false): Describe your smoking history, including the duration and frequency of smoking.
* **Alcohol History** (`alcohol_history`, text, required: false): Describe your alcohol use, including the frequency and amount consumed.
* **Respirator Use** (`respirator_use`, select_multiple, required: false): Check how often you use a respirator:
	+ Never
	+ Occasionally
	+ Frequently
* **Date Last Worn** (`date_last_worn`, date, required: false): If you have used a respirator, please enter the date it was last worn.
* **Last Worn Duration** (`last_worn_duration`, text, required: false): Describe how long you have used a respirator.
* **Breathing Treatment** (`breathing_treatment`, select_one, required: false): Check how your breathing has been treated:
	+ Normal
	+ Using a respirator
	+ Other
* **Oxygen Level** (`oxygen_level`, text, required: false): Describe your current oxygen level, if known.
* **Prosthesis** (`prosthesis`, select_multiple, required: false): If you have a prosthesis, check the following:
	+ Left Leg
	+ Right Leg
	+ Other
* **Type of Respirator** (`respirator_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Check the type of respirator you use:
	+ Ventilator
	+ Non-invasive positive pressure ventilation
	+ Other
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, text, required: false): Please describe any additional medical history or conditions that are not listed above.

**Tips**

* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, please consult your healthcare provider.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
