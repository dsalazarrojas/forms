# Chemotherapy Patient Outcome Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chemotherapy Patient Outcome Survey is designed to help healthcare providers understand the effectiveness of treatment and identify areas for improvement in patient care. By providing your feedback, you contribute to better patient outcomes and care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each field according to your honest and accurate experiences.
2. If a field asks for a specific date, use the calendar or date picker to select the correct date.
3. If a field asks for selection options, choose the one that best matches your situation.
4. For text fields, provide a brief but clear answer.
5. If you're unsure about any field, seek guidance from your healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient ID**: This field is required to identify you as the patient.
* **Survey date**: This field records the date of this survey completion.
* **Age group**: This field asks for your age group to understand demographics.
* **Type of cancer diagnosed**: This field allows you to report the type of cancer you've been diagnosed with.
* **How long have you been receiving chemotherapy?**: This field helps healthcare providers track the duration of your treatment.
* **Number of chemotherapy cycles completed**: This field reports the number of treatment cycles you've undergone.
* **Overall health status since starting treatment**: This field helps healthcare providers track changes in your overall health.
* **Physical side effects experienced**: This field allows you to report any physical side effects you've experienced.
* **Severity of side effects**: This field helps healthcare providers understand the impact of side effects on your well-being.
* **Emotional impact of treatment**: This field reports the emotional toll of treatment on you.
* **Have you received mental health support?**: This field helps healthcare providers track your access to mental health support.
* **Current quality of life rating**: This field allows you to rate your current quality of life.
* **Current pain level**: This field reports your current pain level.
* **How well are you following your medication regimen?**: This field helps healthcare providers track your adherence to treatment.
* **Have you missed any treatment appointments?**: This field reports any missed appointments.
* **Satisfaction with oncology care**: This field allows you to rate your satisfaction with care.
* **Support system adequacy**: This field helps healthcare providers understand your support network's effectiveness.
* **Would you choose the same treatment again?**: This field reports your willingness to continue with treatment.
* **Additional concerns or comments**: This field is for any further comments or concerns you may have.
