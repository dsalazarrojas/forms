# Hospice Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospice Transfer Form is used to transfer patients between hospices, ensuring continuity of care by capturing necessary details for a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Gather all the required information for the patient transfer.
2.  Fill in the patient's name.
3.  Enter the patient's date of birth (dob).
4.  Provide the current provider name.
5.  Enter the name of the provider the patient is being transferred to.
6.  Enter the date of the transfer.
7.  Enter the time of the transfer.
8.  Provide a reason for the transfer.
9.  Enter the location of the transfer.
10.  Describe the patient's current condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name**: (`patient_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's name.
*   **Patient Date of Birth (dob)**: (`patient_dob`, `date`, required: false): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **Current Provider Name**: (`current_provider_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the current provider.
*   **Destination Provider Name**: (`destination_provider_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the provider the patient is being transferred to.
*   **Transfer Date**: (`transfer_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the transfer.
*   **Transfer Time**: (`transfer_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the transfer in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
*   **Reason for Transfer**: (`reason_for_transfer`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the reason for the transfer.
*   **Transfer Location**: (`transfer_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the transfer.
*   **Patient Condition**: (`patient_condition`, `text`, required: false): Describe the patient's current condition.
*   **Contact Number**: (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact number for the patient.

**Tips**

*   Always ensure that you have the necessary approvals and reviews before submitting this form.
*   Double-check the patient's details to avoid errors.
*   If any fields are marked as "required," ensure they are filled in accurately and completely.
*   Review and update the form regularly to guarantee up-to-date information.
