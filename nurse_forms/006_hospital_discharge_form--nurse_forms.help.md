# prophets_hospital_discharge_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The prophets_hospital_discharge_form is a document used to record discharge information for patients. It includes details about the patient's hospital stay, medical treatment, and follow-up care instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's personal details, such as name, patient ID, and contact information.
2. Provide the dates of admission and discharge.
3. Specify the discharge reason and status.
4. Document any allergies or medical history.
5. Outline the follow-up care plan, including the follow-up doctor and location, and the next appointment date.
6. Enter any additional information or instructions for the patient's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Admission Date** (2, date, required): Enter the date the patient was admitted to the hospital.
* **Discharge Date** (3, date, required): Enter the date the patient is being discharged.
* **Patient ID** (4, text, required): Enter the patient's ID number.
* **Discharging Nurse** (5, text): Enter the name of the nurse responsible for discharging the patient.
* **Discharging Doctor** (6, text): Enter the name of the doctor responsible for discharging the patient.
* **Room Number** (7, number, required): Enter the room number where the patient is staying.
* **Diagnosis** (8, text): Enter any relevant medical diagnosis or condition.
* **Medication** (9, text): Enter any prescribed medication or treatment.
* **Instructions** (10, text): Enter any specific instructions for the patient's care.
* **Contact Number** (11, text): Enter a contact number for the patient or emergency contact.
* **Insurance Provider** (12, text): Enter the patient's insurance provider.
* **Insurance Number** (13, text): Enter the patient's insurance number.
* **Medical History** (14, note, required): Enter any relevant medical history or conditions.
* **Allergies** (15, text): Enter any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
* **Discharge Reason** (16, text): Enter the reason for the patient's discharge.
* **Discharge Status** (17, select_one, required): Choose the discharge status (Surgical, Medical, Discharge Against Medical Advice).
* **Follow-up Care** (18, select_multiple, required): Choose the follow-up care plan (Yes, No, Unknown).
* **Follow-up Doctor** (19, text): Enter the name of the doctor for follow-up care.
* **Follow-up Location** (20, text): Enter the location for follow-up care.
* **Next Appointment** (21, date): Enter the date of the next appointment.
* **Discharge Instructions** (22, note): Enter any specific discharge instructions for the patient.
* **Emergency Contact** (23, text): Enter an emergency contact name and contact number.
* **Emergency Phone Number** (24, text): Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Additional Information** (25, note): Enter any additional information or notes.
