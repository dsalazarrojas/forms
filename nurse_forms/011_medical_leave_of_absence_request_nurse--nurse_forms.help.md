# Medical Leave Of Absence Request (Nurse) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a medical leave of absence for a nurse. It gathers information about the start and end dates of the leave, the reason for the leave, and other supporting details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the start date of the leave.
2. Select the end date of the leave.
3. Enter the reason for taking the medical leave.
4. Attach any relevant medical certificates or documents.
5. Enter the date you plan to return to work.
6. Provide the name of your healthcare provider.
7. Enter your employee ID number.
8. Indicate if the manager's approval is required.
9. Enter any comments from the manager.
10. Enter the manager's name and email address.
11. Enter the manager's phone number (optional).
12. Select the approver for the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **start_date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which you wish to begin your medical leave.
* **end_date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which you expect to return to work.
* **reason_for_leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required/optional): Describe the reason for your medical leave (e.g., surgery, illness, family emergency).
* **medical_certificate** (`medical_certificate`, text, required/optional): Attach any relevant medical certificates or documents.
* **return_to_work_date** (`return_to_work_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **medical_provider** (`medical_provider`, text, required/optional): Provide the name of your healthcare provider.
* **employee_id** (`employee_id`, text, required/optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **manager_approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required/optional): Check Yes if your manager's approval is required.
* **manager_comment** (`manager_comment`, note, required/optional): Enter any comments or feedback from your manager.
* **manager_name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **manager_email** (`manager_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **manager_phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's phone number (optional).
* **approver** (`approver`, text, required/optional): Select the person responsible for approving your request.

Note: Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully, and provide any necessary supporting documentation to facilitate the approval process.
