# Healthcare Nursing Skills Employment Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and evaluate a nurse's employment status and experience in the healthcare industry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please fill in the required information in the corresponding fields.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information.
3. For fields with multiple options, select the applicable option that best describes your situation.
4. For fields with dates, enter the dates in the format specified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Job Title** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Years of Experience** (`5`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have been working as a nurse.
* **Medical School Graduation** (`6`, `date`, required): Enter the date you graduated from medical school.
* **License Number** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter your nurse practitioner license number.
* **Hospital Name** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the hospital where you are currently working or have worked.
* **Department** (`9`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the department you work in.
* **Shift** (`10`, `time`, optional): If applicable, enter your shift schedule.
* **Medical Board Certification** (`11`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you have medical board certification.
* **Work Experience** (`12`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional work experience that you think is relevant to your employment status.
* **Medical School Name** (`13`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your medical school.
* **Supervisor Name** (`14`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Evaluation Form** (`15`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select whether you have completed a previous evaluation form.
* **Evaluation Score** (`16`, `number`, optional): Enter the score from your previous evaluation.
* **Comments** (`17`, `text`, optional): Provide any comments about your employment status or experience.
* **Submitted By** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On** (`19`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Completed By** (`20`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who completed the form.
* **Completed On** (`21`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Reviewed By** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who reviewed the form.
* **Reviewed On** (`23`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the form was reviewed.
* **Status** (`24`, `select_one`, optional): Select the current status of your employment.
* **Notes** (`25`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your employment status.
