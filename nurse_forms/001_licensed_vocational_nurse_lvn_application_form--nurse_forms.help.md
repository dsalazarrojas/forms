# LVN_Application_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The LVN_Application_Form is a mandatory form designed to collect information about Licensed Vocational Nurses (LVNs) for internal record-keeping and verification purposes. This information will be used to maintain accurate and up-to-date records of LVNs in the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Name in the required format (e.g. "John Doe").
2. Enter your Address, which is your current address where you can be reached for work-related matters.
3. Provide your Email address, which will be used for internal communication and notifications.
4. Enter your Phone number, which is the best contact number for work-related matters.
5. Briefly describe your Education background, including the name of your nursing school and degree earned.
6. Enter the name of your nursing school where you graduated.
7. Provide the year you graduated from your nursing school.
8. Select the date you graduated from your nursing school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter your current address where you can be reached for work-related matters.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false)
	+ This is the email address where you can be reached for internal communication and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter your primary phone number for work-related matters.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Briefly describe your education background, including the name of your nursing school and degree earned.
* **Nursing School Name** (`nursing_school_name`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of your nursing school where you graduated.
* **Nursing Degree** (`nursing_degree`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Specify the degree earned from your nursing school (e.g. ADN, BSN, etc.).
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, `date`, required: false)
	+ Select the date you graduated from your nursing school.
 
## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any issues while filling out this form, please contact [contact person] for assistance.
* This form must be submitted in order to be included in the internal records.
