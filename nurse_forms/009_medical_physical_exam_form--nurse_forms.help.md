# Medical Physical Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Physical Exam Form is designed to collect information from patients during a medical physical examination. This form is used to gather details about the patient's past medical history, chief complaint, and current medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the patient's past medical history in the "Past Medical History" field.
3. Describe the patient's main symptom or complaint in the "Main Symptom" field.
4. Select the area of the body that needs to be examined from the "Physical Exam" field.
5. Record any vital signs, such as blood pressure, pulse, and body temperature, in the "Vital Signs" field.
6. Indicate whether there is a positive family history or not in the "Family History" field.
7. Provide a brief description of the diagnosis and treatment plan in the "Diagnosis" and "Treatment Plan" fields, respectively.
8. Indicate whether the patient is currently taking any medications in the "Medication Use" field.
9. Provide any next steps or follow-up instructions in the "Next Steps" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Please fill in the patient's full name.
* **Past Medical History** (`Past Medical History`, text, required): Describe the patient's past medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or allergies.
* **Main Symptom** (`Main Symptom`, text, required): Describe the patient's main symptom or complaint.
* **Physical Exam** (`Physical Exam`, select_one, required): Select the area of the body that needs to be examined (e.g., Head and neck, Face and neck, Back, Abdomen, Pelvis).
* **Vital Signs** (`Vital Signs`, select_multiple, required): Record any vital signs, such as blood pressure, pulse, respiratory rate, body temperature, oxygen saturation, heart rate, and body mass index.
* **Family History** (`Family History`, select_one, required): Indicate whether there is a positive family history or not.
* **Diagnosis** (`Diagnosis`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the diagnosis.
* **Treatment Plan** (`Treatment Plan`, text, required): Describe the treatment plan, including any medications, lifestyle changes, or follow-up appointments.
* **Medication Use** (`Medication Use`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the patient is currently taking any medications.
* **Next Steps** (`Next Steps`, note, required): Provide any next steps or follow-up instructions.

Note: The fields marked as "required" must be filled in by the user.
