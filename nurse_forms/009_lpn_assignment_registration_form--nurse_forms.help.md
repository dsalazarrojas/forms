# LPN Assignment Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The LPN Assignment Registration Form is a tool for LPNs to register for shifts at a healthcare facility. This form helps us understand your availability and preferences for different shifts, allowing us to better match you with assignments that fit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through the form carefully and make sure you have an understanding of each field.
2. Select the shifts you are available for on the first page.
3. Provide your contact information on the second page.
4. Select your start and end times for the shifts you are interested in.
5. Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
6. Review your registration to ensure everything is accurate.
7. Select "Yes" to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page:**
	+ **Yes** / **No** (`first_shift`, select_multiple): Indicate your preference for the first shift. Select "Yes" if you are available for this shift.
	+ **Yes** / **No** (`second_shift`, select_multiple): Indicate your preference for the second shift. Select "Yes" if you are available for this shift.
	+ **Yes** / **No** (`third_shift`, select_multiple): Indicate your preference for the third shift. Select "Yes" if you are available for this shift.
	+ **Yes** / **No** (`fourth_shift`, select_multiple): Indicate your preference for the fourth shift. Select "Yes" if you are available for this shift.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text): Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date): Select the dates you are available.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time): Select the start time for your shift.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time): Select the end time for your shift.
* **First Shift** (`first_shift`, select_one): Select your first shift preference.
* **Second Shift** (`second_shift`, select_one): Select your second shift preference.
* **Third Shift** (`third_shift`, select_one): Select your third shift preference.
* **Fourth Shift** (`fourth_shift`, select_one): Select your fourth shift preference.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, select_one): Select "Yes" to confirm your registration.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate information for your contact details.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please enter them in the "Notes" field.
* Review your registration carefully before confirming.
