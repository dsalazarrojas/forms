# School Health Screening Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Health Screening Agreement is a form used to collect and record essential student health information from parents or guardians. This information will be used by school administrators to provide a safe and supportive environment for students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your child's name in the "Enter Name" field.
2. Provide a valid contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. If applicable, provide any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.
5. List any allergies in the "Allergies" field.
6. Enter any medication or treatment information in the "Medication List" field.
7. Specify any medical conditions in the "Medical Condition" field.
8. Add any relevant medical notes or comments in the "Medical Note" field.
9. Enter the name of your child's school in the "School Name" field.
10. Provide the school's email address in the "School Email" field.
11. Enter the school's phone number in the "School Phone" field.
12. Enter the school's address in the "School Address" field.
13. Identify the school contact person in the "School Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Name**: Enter the name of the child you are submitting the information for.
* **Contact Number**: A valid contact number where you can be reached for any further information or clarification.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Medical History**: If applicable, describe any relevant medical history or health issues that might affect your child's well-being in school.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities your child may have.
* **Medication List**: Enter any medications or treatments your child is currently taking.
* **Medical Condition**: Specify any medical conditions or health issues your child has.
* **Medical Note**: Add any relevant medical notes or comments.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your child's school.
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of your child's school.
* **School Phone**: Enter the phone number of your child's school.
* **School Address**: Enter the address of your child's school.
* **School Contact**: Identify the contact person at your child's school who can be reached for further information.

By following these steps and completing this form accurately, you will be providing essential health information for your child, ensuring their well-being and safety in school. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your child's school administration.
