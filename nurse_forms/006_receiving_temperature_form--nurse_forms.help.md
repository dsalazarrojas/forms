# receiving_temperature_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for recording information about a shipment that has been received by a company or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the shipment ID, if available.
2. Select the date of receipt using the calendar or date picker.
3. Enter the received temperature.
4. Choose the unit of measurement for the temperature (Celsius, Fahrenheit, or Kelvin).
5. Add any additional notes about the shipment, if needed.
6. Provide the contact email, if available.
7. Enter the contact phone number, if available.
8. Enter the shipper's name.
9. Enter the shipper's company.
10. Enter the shipper's address, if available.
11. Provide the name of the person who received the shipment.
12. Enter the contact information for the shipper.
13. Add any additional remarks about the shipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ship From (`shipment_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique ID for the shipment, if available.**
* **Date Received (`date_received`, date, optional): Select the date of receipt using the calendar or date picker.**
* **Received Temperature (`temperature`, number, optional): Enter the temperature of the shipment.**
* **Unit of Measurement (`temperature_unit`, select_one, optional): Choose the unit of measurement for the temperature (Celsius, Fahrenheit, or Kelvin).**
* **Notes (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the shipment, if needed.**
* **Contact Email (`email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email for the shipper, if available.**
* **Contact Phone (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number for the shipper, if available.**
* **Shipper Name (`shipper_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the shipper.**
* **Shipper Company (`shipper_company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company associated with the shipment.**
* **Shipper Address (`shipper_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where the shipment came from, if available.**
* **Received By (`received_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who received the shipment.**
* **Shipper Contact (`shipper_contact`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information for the shipper.**
* **Remarks (`remarks`, note, optional): Add any additional remarks about the shipment, if needed.**

## Tips
* Use the unit of measurement that is most commonly used in your industry or region.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the contact information.
* Use the notes field to record any specific conditions or issues with the shipment.
* Save the form before leaving the page to avoid losing your work.
