# Patient Safety Check Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Safety Check Form is used to collect essential information about a patient's medical history, allergies, and potential safety concerns. This information is crucial for medical staff to provide appropriate care and prevent potential risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in each section carefully and accurately.
2. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the patient or their healthcare provider.
3. Ensure that the information is up-to-date and accurate.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Allergies** (page 1): Please list any known allergies the patient has.
* **Diseases** (page 2): Select any known medical conditions the patient has, such as Hypertension or Diabetes.
* **Surgeries** (page 2): Indicate if the patient has undergone any surgeries.
* **Safety Questions** (page 3): Answer any relevant safety questions about the patient's health.
* **Comments** (page 4): Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's health.
* **Medical Notes** (page 4): Add any relevant medical notes or information about the patient.
* **Emergency Contact** (page 5): Provide contact information for the patient's emergency contact.
* **Medical History** (page 5): Add any relevant medical history information about the patient.
* **Contact Info** (page 6): Provide contact information for the patient's emergency contact.
* **Allergies Info** (page 6): Add any additional information about the patient's allergies.
* **Diseases Info** (page 7): Add any additional information about the patient's diseases.
* **Surgeries Info** (page 7): Add any additional information about the patient's surgeries.
* **Dental Record** (page 8): Add any relevant dental information about the patient.
* **Medical Record** (page 8): Add any relevant medical information about the patient.
* **Eyesight** (page 9): Add any relevant information about the patient's eyesight.
* **Heart Surgery** (page 9): Add any relevant information about the patient's heart surgery.
* **Lungs History** (page 10): Add any relevant information about the patient's lungs history.
* **Dental Condition** (page 10): Add any relevant dental information about the patient.
* **Hair History** (page 11): Add any relevant hair information about the patient.
* **Skin History** (page 11): Add any relevant skin information about the patient.
* **Hair Color** (page 12): Add any relevant hair color information about the patient.
* **Height** (page 12): Measure the patient's height in centimeters or inches.
* **Weight** (page 13): Measure the patient's weight in kilograms or pounds.
* **Blood Type** (page 13): Enter the patient's blood type.
* **Blood Pressure** (page 14): Measure the patient's blood pressure in millimeters of mercury (mmHg) or milliliters of mercury (mL/Hg).
