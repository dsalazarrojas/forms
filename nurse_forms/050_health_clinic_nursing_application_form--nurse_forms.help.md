# Health Clinic Nursing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Clinic Nursing Application Form is designed to gather information from nurses who want to apply for work at the Health Clinic. Completing this form will help us to evaluate your qualifications, experience, and availability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Candidate Name** in the "Candidate Name" field.
2. Enter your **Contact Phone** number in the "Contact Phone" field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Describe your **Qualifications** in the "Qualifications" field.
5. Enter your **Address** in the "Address" field.
6. Provide your **Availability** in the "Availability" field.
7. Describe your **Experience** in the "Experience" field.
8. Enter your **Education** in the "Education" field.
9. List your **Skills** in the "Skills" field.
10. Provide your **Availability 2** in the "Availability 2" field.
11. List your **References** in the "References" field.
12. If you have a **Medical License**, please provide it in the "Medical License" field.
13. Describe your **Work Experience** in the "Work Experience" field.
14. List your **Certifications** in the "Certifications" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact Phone** (required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Qualifications** (optional): Describe your relevant qualifications, such as certifications, training, or experience.
* **Address** (optional): Enter your home or work address, as relevant to your availability.
* **Availability** (optional): Describe your availability, including days and hours you are available to work.
* **Experience** (optional): Describe your relevant experience in the field of nursing.
* **Education** (optional): Enter your educational background, including degrees or diplomas.
* **Skills** (optional): List your relevant skills, such as languages, software, or tools.
* **Availability 2** (optional): Provide any additional availability information.
* **References** (optional): Provide the names and contact information of professional references.
* **Medical License** (optional): If you have a medical license, provide it in this field.
* **Work Experience** (optional): Describe your previous work experience in the field of nursing.
* **Certifications** (optional): List any relevant certifications, such as ACLS or CPR.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your application carefully and respond to you shortly.
