Emergency Medicine Residency Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Medicine Residency Evaluation Form is a tool used by medical professionals to assess the performance of medical residents from emergency medical services.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the **Medical Residency** field (id: 1) with the resident's name.
2. In the **Medical Services** field (id: 2), describe the specific services the resident was evaluated on.
3. The **Medical Residency Evaluation** field (id: 3) is a free-text field where you can provide detailed feedback on the resident's performance.
4. In the **Feedback** field (id: 4), describe any positive or negative aspects of the resident's performance, being specific about any issues you've seen and what they're doing well.
5. Fill in the **Resident** field (id: 5) with the resident's name if you wish to keep a record of their evaluations.
6. Enter the **Date of Evaluation** field (id: 6) if the evaluation was done on a specific date.
7. In the **Evaluated By** field (id: 7), enter your name or the name of the person who performed the evaluation.
8. Enter the **Evaluation Date** field (id: 8) if the evaluation was completed on a specific date.
9. In the **Evaluated Title** field (id: 9), enter your title or position if you wish to keep a record.
10. The **Emergency Medicine Residency Evaluation Form ID** field (id: 10) is a unique identifier for this form.
11. Select the **Category** field (id: 11) as "Nurse Forms" to indicate that this form is related to nursing evaluations.
12. In the **Description** field (id: 12), enter a brief description of the form, which is already filled in for your convenience.
13. Finally, enter your name in the **Assigned Tool** field (id: 13) if you are using a specific tool to perform the evaluation.
