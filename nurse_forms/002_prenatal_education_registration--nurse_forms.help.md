# Prenatal Education Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prenatal Education Registration form is designed to collect information from expectant mothers to provide them with relevant prenatal education. This form helps us understand their needs and preferences to tailor our education and support services to their requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "Expectant Mothers Info" by selecting "Yes" or "No" to indicate your status.
2. Provide your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Expectant Mothers Info (expectant_mothers_info)**: Indicates whether you are an expectant mother or not.
* **Name (name)**: Enter your full name to help us personalize our education and support services.
* **Date Of Birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your valid email address to stay updated on our education and support services.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any further assistance or updates.
* **Medical Info (medical_info)**: This is a text field for you to provide any relevant medical information that you think would be helpful for us to know.
