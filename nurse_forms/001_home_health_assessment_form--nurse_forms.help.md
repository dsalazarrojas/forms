# Home Health Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients for home health assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter the patient's **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Enter the **Date of Service**, which is the date you first saw the patient for this visit.
4. Enter the **Time of Service**, which is the time you first saw the patient for this visit.
5. Enter the patient's **Medical Record Number**, which is a unique identifier for each patient's medical record.
6. Enter the patient's **Physician Name**, the doctor who will be treating the patient.
7. Enter the patient's **Medical History**, which includes any relevant medical conditions, allergies, and medications.
8. Enter the patient's **Allergies**, if any.
9. Enter the patient's **Medications**, if any.
10. Enter the patient's **Medical Condition**, which is the specific condition being treated.
11. Enter the patient's **Treatment Plan**, which outlines the steps to be taken for their treatment.
12. Enter the patient's **Follow Up**, which is the next step in their care plan.
13. Enter the next **Next Step**, which is the next action to be taken in the patient's care plan (optional).
14. Enter any additional **Medical History** details, up to 12 fields (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your full first name as it is written on your ID or driver's license. 
* **Last Name**: Enter your full last name as it is written on your ID or driver's license.
* **Date of Service**: Enter the date of the visit using the format `MM/DD/YYYY` (e.g., 01/01/2024).
* **Time of Service**: Enter the time of the visit using the 12-hour clock format (e.g., 08:00).
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter your unique medical record number.
* **Physician Name**: Enter the name of the doctor who will be treating you.
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or medications.
* **Allergies**: Enter any allergies you have.
* **Medications**: Enter any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Condition**: Enter the specific medical condition being treated.
* **Treatment Plan**: Enter the steps to be taken for your treatment.
* **Follow Up**: Enter the next step in your care plan.
* **Next Step**: Enter the next action to be taken in your care plan (optional).
* **Medical History 2-12**: Enter any additional medical history details, up to 12 fields.
