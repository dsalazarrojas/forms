# Covid 19 Daily Status Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Covid 19 Daily Status Update Form is designed to gather information on patients' COVID-19 status, helping healthcare professionals track and manage the spread of the virus.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form questions carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Select the correct status for each field based on the patient's current condition.
3. Enter any relevant comments or notes in the "symptoms" field if the patient is experiencing any symptoms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, select_one, required: false): 
  This field indicates the patient's current COVID-19 status. Select "Positive" if the patient has tested positive, "Negative" if they have tested negative, or "Unknown" if their status is not yet determined.

* **Vaccine Status** (`vaccine_status`, select_one, required: false): 
  This field shows the patient's current vaccine status. Choose "Fully Vaccinated" if they have completed the recommended vaccination series, "Partially Vaccinated" if they have received some but not all recommended vaccines, or "Not Vaccinated" if they have not received any vaccines.

* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required: false): 
  Describe any symptoms the patient is currently experiencing, such as fever, cough, or fatigue.

* **Temperature** (`temperature`, select_one, required: false): 
  Indicate the patient's current body temperature. Choose "Above 37.5" if their temperature is higher than 37.5°C, "Normal (37.5C)" if it is within the normal range, or "Below 37.5" if it is lower than 37.5°C.

## Tips

* Be precise when selecting the patient's status and vaccine status to ensure accurate tracking and care.
* Provide detailed descriptions of any symptoms for better analysis and treatment.
* Ensure the patient's temperature reading is accurate to aid in medical decision-making.
