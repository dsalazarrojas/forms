# Discharge Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a patient's discharge from the hospital. It is meant to ensure that all necessary details are captured before a patient is released from the hospital, including the date and reason for discharge, as well as contact information for the patient's next destination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Patient Info" tab to enter the patient's basic information.
2. In the "Discharge Date" field, enter the date the patient is to be discharged from the hospital.
3. In the "Discharge Status" field, enter the patient's status after discharge, such as "Home", "Transfer to Rehab", etc.
4. In the "Discharge Reason" field, enter a brief description of the reason the patient is being discharged.
5. In the "Discharge Location" field, enter the address or location where the patient will be discharged to.
6. In the "Discharge Contact" field, enter the name and contact information of the person or organization the patient will be transferred to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's name and any other relevant information.
* **Discharge Date**: Enter the date the patient is to be discharged from the hospital.
* **Discharge Status**: Enter the patient's status after discharge, such as "Home", "Transfer to Rehab", etc.
* **Discharge Reason**: Enter a brief description of the reason the patient is being discharged.
* **Discharge Location**: Enter the address or location where the patient will be discharged to.
* **Discharge Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the person or organization the patient will be transferred to.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the information entered in each field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the relevant hospital staff for guidance.
* The information collected on this form will be used to ensure a smooth and safe discharge process for the patient.
