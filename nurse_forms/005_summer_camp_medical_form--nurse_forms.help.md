# summer_camp_medical_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The summer camp medical form is designed to collect essential medical information from campers to ensure their safety and well-being during their stay at the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your medical history, including any allergies or medical conditions you may have.
2. List any medications you are currently taking.
3. Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and emergency contact's information.
4. Share any relevant medical history details.
5. Report your vaccination record.
6. Indicate any medical conditions you have, if applicable.
7. Provide your health insurance information, if applicable.
8. Specify the emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Please write a brief summary of your medical history, including any allergies or medical conditions you have.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies you have, including the allergen and the severity of the reaction.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking, including the name, dosage, and frequency.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Provide your name, phone number, and emergency contact's name and phone number.
* **Medical History Details** (`medical_history_details`, text, optional): Share any additional details about your medical history, if applicable.
* **Vaccination Record** (`vaccination_record`, text, optional): Report your up-to-date vaccination record, including any vaccinations you have received and when.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Indicate any medical conditions you have, if applicable.
* **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, text, optional): Provide your health insurance information, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Specify the emergency contact's name and phone number.
* **Medical Authorization** (`medical_authorization`, text, optional): If you have given permission for the camp staff to share your medical information with someone, please specify their name and relationship to you.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please consult a medical professional.
* If you have any further medical information or updates, please let the camp staff know.
* Please make sure to update this form if your medical information changes during your stay at the camp.
