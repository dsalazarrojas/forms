# hospice_care_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "hospice_care_checklist" form is designed to collect information related to patient care and services provided by a hospice. This form helps assess the needs and conditions of patients receiving hospice care, ensuring accurate and complete data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. If applicable, provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter your Social Security number in the "Social Security Number" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Share your medical history in the "Medical History" field.
8. List your current medications in the "Medications" field.
9. Select your current pain management options from the dropdown menu in the "Pain Management" field.
10. Indicate whether you receive emotional support from others in the "Emotional Support" field.
11. Indicate whether you receive spiritual support from others in the "Spiritual Support" field.
12. Select any applicable medical conditions from the dropdown menu in the "Medical Conditions" field.
13. Enter the name of your caregiver in the "Caregiver Name" field.
14. Select the relationship between you and your caregiver from the dropdown menu in the "Relationship to Caregiver" field.
15. Enter any notes regarding your care plan in the "Care Plan" field.
16. If applicable, enter your discharge date in the "Discharge Date" field.
17. Select the type of hospice care you are receiving from the dropdown menu in the "Hospice Type" field.
18. Enter the location of your hospice care in the "Hospice Location" field.
19. Enter the location where you will be discharged from the "Discharge Location" field.
20. Indicate whether you have a hospice service agreement from the "Hospice Service Agreement" field.
21. Enter the start date of your hospice services in the "Hospice Service Start" field.
22. Enter the end date of your hospice services in the "Hospice Service End" field.
23. Enter the duration of your hospice services in the "Hospice Service Duration" field.
24. Select your current hospice service status from the dropdown menu in the "Hospice Service Status" field.
25. Enter any additional notes regarding your hospice services in the "Hospice Service Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your given name, such as John.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your surname, such as Doe.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): If applicable, enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Social Security Number** (number, optional): Enter your Social Security number if applicable.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your current address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Medical History** (text, optional): Share any relevant medical history or conditions.
* **Medications** (text, optional): List any current medications you are taking.
* **Pain Management** (select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ None
	+ Medication
	+ Palliative
	+ Other
* **Emotional Support** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you receive emotional support from others, such as friends or family:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unknown
* **Spiritual Support** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you receive spiritual support from others, such as religious leaders or support groups:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unknown
* **Medical Conditions** (select_multiple, optional): Select any applicable medical conditions from the dropdown menu:
	+ Cancer
	+ Heart Failure
	+ Alzheimer's
	+ Other
* **Caregiver Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of your caregiver.
* **Relationship to Caregiver** (select_multiple, optional): Select the relationship between you and your caregiver from the dropdown menu:
	+ Spouse
	+ Child
	+ Other Family Member
	+ Other
* **Care Plan** (note, optional): Enter any notes regarding your care plan.
* **Discharge Date** (date, optional): If applicable, enter your discharge date.
* **Hospice Type** (select_multiple, optional): Select the type of hospice care you are receiving from the dropdown menu:
	+ General Inpatient
	+ Palliative Care
	+ Respite Care
	+ Other
* **Hospice Location** (text, optional): Enter the location of your hospice care.
* **Discharge Location** (text, optional): Enter the location where you will be discharged.
* **Hospice Service Agreement** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you have a hospice service agreement:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unknown
* **Hospice Service Start** (date, optional): Enter the start date of your hospice services.
* **Hospice Service End** (date, optional): Enter the end date of your hospice services.
* **Hospice Service Duration** (number, optional): Enter the duration of your hospice services.
* **Hospice Service Status** (select_one, optional): Select your current hospice service status from the dropdown menu:
	+ Active
	+ Discharged
	+ Other
* **Hospice Service Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes regarding your hospice services.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult your healthcare provider or hospice care team.
