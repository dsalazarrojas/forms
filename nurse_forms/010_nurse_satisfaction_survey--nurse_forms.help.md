# Nurse Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nurse Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather feedback from nurses about their experiences and satisfaction with various aspects of their work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and choose the best response based on your personal experience.
2. Answer all questions that apply to you.
3. Use the options provided for each question to select your response.
4. If a question does not apply, leave it blank.
5. Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nurse Satisfaction Survey**: This is the title of the form, which is used to identify the purpose of the survey.
* **Work Schedules** (`work_schedules`, select_one, optional):
	+ Always on-time: You are always able to start and finish your shifts on time.
	+ Often: You are mostly able to start and finish your shifts on time.
	+ Rarely: You are sometimes unable to start and finish your shifts on time.
	+ Never: You are never able to start and finish your shifts on time.
* **Nurse Workload** (`nurse_workload_`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Light: Your workload is manageable and you have enough time to complete tasks.
	+ Medium: Your workload is moderate and you sometimes have to prioritize tasks.
	+ Heavy: Your workload is high and you often have to work overtime.
* **Communication with Colleagues** (`communication_with_colleagues`, text, optional):
	+ Please describe your experiences with communication with your colleagues.
* **Communication with Supervisors** (`communication_with_supervisors`, text, optional):
	+ Please describe your experiences with communication with your supervisors.
* **Patient Satisfaction** (`patient_satisfaction`, select_one, optional):
	+ High: You are highly satisfied with patient satisfaction.
	+ Medium: You are somewhat satisfied with patient satisfaction.
	+ Low: You are not satisfied with patient satisfaction.
* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Safe: You feel safe in your work environment.
	+ Comfortable: You feel comfortable in your work environment.
	+ Neither safe nor comfortable: You do not feel safe or comfortable in your work environment.
* **Support from Colleagues** (`support_from_colleagues`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Available: You receive support from your colleagues.
	+ Not available: You do not receive support from your colleagues.
* **Support from Supervisors** (`support_from_supervisors`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Available: You receive support from your supervisors.
	+ Not available: You do not receive support from your supervisors.
* **Training and Development** (`training_and_development`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Available: You have access to training and development opportunities.
	+ Not available: You do not have access to training and development opportunities.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, optional):
	+ High: You are highly satisfied with your job.
	+ Medium: You are somewhat satisfied with your job.
	+ Low: You are not satisfied with your job.
* **Turnover Concerns** (`turnover_concerns`, select_one, optional):
	+ Concerned: You are concerned about turnover.
	+ Not concerned: You are not concerned about turnover.

Please note that this form is designed to be completed by nurses, and your responses will be kept confidential and anonymous.
