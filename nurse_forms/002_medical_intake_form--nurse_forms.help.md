# Medical Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect a patient's medical history, including their current symptoms, past surgeries, genetic history, medical record number, allergies, past medications, family history, past illnesses, mental health history, and medical conditions. This information will be used to assess the patient's overall health and create a comprehensive medical record.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the label and instruction for each field carefully.
2. Ask the patient to provide as much information as possible about their medical history.
3. Enter the patient's information into the corresponding fields.
4. If you're unsure about any field, please seek guidance from a supervisor or medical professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Enter the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or medical conditions.
* **Past Surgeries** (`past_surgeries`, select_multiple, required): Select all the surgeries the patient has had, including the date and description of each surgery.
* **Genetic History** (`genetic_history`, text, optional): Record any genetic history or conditions that may be relevant to the patient's health.
* **Current Symptoms** (`current_symptoms`, text, optional): Describe the patient's current symptoms, including any pain, discomfort, or concerns they are experiencing.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, number, optional): Enter the patient's medical record number, if available.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): Record any allergies the patient has, including the type of allergy and any known triggers.
* **Past Medications** (`past_medications`, text, optional): List any past medications the patient has taken, including the name, dosage, and frequency of each medication.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, optional): Record any family history of medical conditions, including any known genetic risks.
* **Medical History Family** (`medical_history_family`, text, optional): Describe any family medical history, including any conditions that may be relevant to the patient's health.
* **Past Illnesses** (`past_illnesses`, text, optional): Record any past illnesses, including the date, symptoms, and treatment received.
* **Mental Health History** (`mental_health_history`, text, optional): Discuss any mental health concerns or diagnoses the patient has, including any treatment or counseling they've received.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Describe any current or past medical conditions, including any ongoing treatments or medications.
* **Medical Condition Other** (`medical_condition_other`, text, optional): Record any additional medical conditions not listed above.
* **Medical Condition Other Details** (`medical_condition_other_details`, text, optional): Provide more information about the medical condition, if needed.
* **Date Last Visit** (`date_last_visit`, date, optional): Record the date of the patient's last medical visit, if known.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before completing it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Seek guidance from a supervisor or medical professional if you're unsure about any field.
* Be sure to save the form and share it with the patient's healthcare team, as needed.
