# Student Allergy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Allergy Registration Form is designed to collect information about students' allergies, medications, or medical conditions to provide essential health and safety information for the school or institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Begin by selecting the type of allergy, medication, or medical condition you need to register.
2. Enter your first name and last name as they appear on your official identification documents.
3. Provide a brief description of your allergy, medication, or medical condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Allergy Registration** (`student_allergy_registration_form`, text, not required): This is the main title of the form.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Allergy Type** (`allergy_type`, select_one, not required): Choose from the options Allergy, Medication, or Medical Condition to classify your entry.
* **Allergy Description** (`allergy_description`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of your allergy, medication, or medical condition.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all relevant fields truthfully and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your school nurse or health administrator for help.
* This form will be kept confidential and stored securely for your protection and health record keeping.
