# COVID 19 Student Online Health Screening Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect health-related information from students, which will be reviewed by school authorities to determine their eligibility to attend school.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form thoroughly and accurately, providing truthful information about your health status.
2. Answer each question truthfully, and if unsure, select "Unknown" or "Not applicable" when necessary.
3. Ensure that you provide your parent or guardian's contact information (name, email, and phone number) for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, text, required): Provide your unique student identification number or ID card number.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select your current grade level:
	+ Elementary
	+ Middle School
	+ High School
	+ College
* **Screening Date** (`screening_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **-- Health Screening Questions --** (`health_section`, note, required): This section will help guide you through the subsequent questions.
* **Do you have a fever or high temperature?** (`fever_symptoms`, select_one, required): Answer:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Temperature Reading if Applicable** (`temperature_reading`, number, optional): If you answered "True" above, enter your current temperature in Fahrenheit.
* **Do you have a persistent cough?** (`cough_symptoms`, select_one, required): Answer:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Do you have difficulty breathing?** (`breathing_difficulty`, select_one, required): Answer:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Have you experienced loss of taste or smell?** (`loss_taste_smell`, select_one, required): Answer:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Are you experiencing unusual fatigue or weakness?** (`fatigue`, select_one, required): Answer:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Do you have a headache or body aches?** (`headache_body_ache`, select_one, optional): Answer:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Do you have nausea, vomiting, or diarrhea?** (`nausea_vomiting`, select_one, optional): Answer:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Have you been exposed to someone with COVID-19?** (`covid_exposure`, select_one, required): Answer:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
	+ Unknown
* **Have you tested positive for COVID-19?** (`covid_diagnosis`, select_one, required): Answer:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
	+ Currently awaiting results
* **If yes, when was the positive test?** (`diagnosis_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the positive test.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccinated_status`, select_one, required): Select your vaccination status:
	+ Fully vaccinated
	+ Partially vaccinated
	+ Not vaccinated
	+ Unknown
* **Additional Health Notes or Symptoms** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any other health concerns or symptoms not listed above.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone Number** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter your parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Student Clearance Status** (`clearance_status`, select_one, optional): This field will be completed by the school nurse or healthcare professional.

## Tips

* Please answer truthfully and accurately to ensure a safe and healthy learning environment.
* If unsure or unclear about any question, select "Unknown" or "Not Applicable" when possible.
* Review each question carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact your school nurse or healthcare professional.
