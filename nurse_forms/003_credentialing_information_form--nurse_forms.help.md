# Credentialing Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and verify a healthcare provider's education, training, and qualifications to ensure their credentials are up-to-date and compliant with medical group requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Please ensure you have not already submitted this information in the past year.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately, using your best judgment.
3. If you have completed post-graduate training in excess of 8 years, please complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Provider Education**
* **provider_education** (`provider_education`, text, required/optional): Please enter your medical school and degree earned (e.g., MD, DO, etc.).

### **Provider Training**
* **provider_training** (`provider_training`, text, required/optional): Please enter your post-graduate training (e.g., residency, fellowship, etc.).

### **Provider License**
* **provider_license** (`provider_license`, text, required/optional): Please enter your current medical license number and issuing state.

### **Other Qualifications**
* **other_qualifications** (`other_qualifications`, text, required/optional): Please enter any additional certifications, fellowships, or other specialized qualifications you have earned.

### **Medical Group**
* **medical_group** (`medical_group`, select_one, required): Please select your medical group from the list:
	+ American Board of Surgery
	+ American Osteopathic Academy of Orthodontics
	+ American Osteopathic College of Surgeons
	+ Other (if not listed, please select "Other" and enter your group name in the text field below)
* **medical_group_other** (`medical_group_other`, text, required): If you selected "Other", please enter the name of your medical group.

## Tips
* Please ensure your answers are accurate and thorough to avoid delays in processing your application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
