# Home Health Agency Compliance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information on client data, agency operations, and visit details from home health agencies for compliance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the `Client Name` field.
2. Provide the agency location in the `Agency Location` field.
3. Enter the agency name in the `Agency Name` field.
4. Enter the nurse's name in the `Nurse Name` field.
5. Select the client's relationship with the agency from the `Client Relationship` dropdown list.
6. Enter the date of the visit in the `Visit Date` field.
7. Enter the time of the visit in the `Visit Time` field.
8. Select the visit frequency from the `Visit Frequency` dropdown list.
9. Enter the length of the visit in the `Visit Length` field.
10. Enter any additional notes for the client in the `Client Notes` field.
11. Sign and date the form using the `Nurse Signature` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (client_name, text, required: false): Enter the client's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Agency Location** (agency_location, text, required: false): Enter the address of the agency's location.
* **Agency Name** (agency_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the agency.
* **Nurse Name** (nurse_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the nurse visiting the client.
* **Client Relationship** (client_relationship, select_one, required: false): Select the type of relationship the client has with the agency (e.g., patient, family member, etc.).
* **Visit Date** (visit_date, date, required: false): Enter the date of the visit in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Visit Time** (visit_time, time, required: false): Enter the time of the visit in the format hh:mm a.
* **Visit Frequency** (visit_frequency, select_one, required: false): Select the frequency of visits (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
* **Visit Length** (visit_length, text, required: false): Enter the length of the visit (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour, etc.).
* **Client Notes** (client_notes, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the client.
* **Nurse Signature** (nurse_signature, text, required: false): Sign and date the form to indicate completion.

# Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of the information provided before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the agency administration for clarification.
