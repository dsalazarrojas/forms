# COVID 19 Campus Visit Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather information about your health status, purpose of visit, and other relevant details to ensure the campus community's safety and health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required information accurately and honestly. Make sure to answer all questions carefully, as this will help screener personnel make informed decisions about your access to campus.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your visit to campus.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, optional): If you're unsure of the visit time, please leave this field blank.
* **Visitor Name** (`visitor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Phone Number** (`visitor_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in case of emergencies or follow-up questions.
* **Email Address** (`visitor_email`, email, optional): If you have an email address associated with your visit, enter it here.
* **Purpose of Visit** (`visit_purpose`, select one, required): Select one of the following options for the purpose of your visit:
	+ Student: Visiting as a student
	+ Employee: Visiting as an employee
	+ Contractor: Visiting for work-related purposes
	+ Visitor: Visiting for a different reason
	+ Delivery: Delivering items or packages
	+ Other: Specify your purpose in the text field
* **Sponsoring Employee or Department** (`employee_sponsor`, text, optional): If you're visiting with an employee, enter their name and department.
* **Building(s) You Will Access** (`building_access`, text, optional): If you have specific buildings you'll be visiting, enter them here.
* **Temperature Reading** (`temperature_check`, number, optional): If you have a thermometer, please take and record your current body temperature. If you don't have access to a thermometer, leave this field blank.
* **Do you have a fever over 100.4 degrees F?** (`fever_present`, select one, required): Answer yes or no to whether you have a fever above 100.4 degrees F.
* **Do you have a new or worsening cough?** (`cough`, select one, required): Answer yes, no, or not sure to whether you have a new or worsening cough.
* **Do you have shortness of breath?** (`shortness_of_breath`, select one, required): Answer yes, no, or not sure to whether you have shortness of breath.
* **Have you lost sense of taste or smell?** (`loss_taste_smell`, select one, required): Answer yes, no, or not sure to whether you have lost your sense of taste or smell.
* **Are you experiencing unusual fatigue?** (`fatigue`, select one, optional): Answer yes or no to whether you're experiencing unusual fatigue.
* **Do you have a sore throat?** (`sore_throat`, select one, optional): Answer yes, no, or not sure to whether you have a sore throat.
* **Do you have body aches?** (`body_aches`, select one, optional): Answer yes, no, or not sure to whether you have body aches.
* **Do you have a headache?** (`headache`, select one, optional): Answer yes, no, or not sure to whether you have a headache.
* **Do you have nausea or vomiting?** (`nausea_vomiting`, select one, optional): Answer yes, no, or not sure to whether you have nausea or vomiting.
* **Do you have diarrhea?** (`diarrhea`, select one, optional): Answer yes, no, or not sure to whether you have diarrhea.
* **Have you been exposed to someone with COVID-19?** (`covid_exposure`, select one, required): Answer yes, no, or prefer not to say to whether you've been exposed to someone with COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
* **Have you tested positive for COVID-19?** (`covid_diagnosis`, select one, required): Answer yes, no, or prefer not to say to whether you've tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 10 days.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select one, optional): Answer fully vaccinated, partially vaccinated, not vaccinated, or prefer not to say.
* **Will you wear a mask during your visit?** (`mask_requirement`, select one, optional): Answer yes, no, or not sure to whether you'll wear a mask during your visit.
* **Screening Result** (`screening_result`, select one, required): Answer cleared to enter, screening needed with nurse, denied access, or pending.
* **Nurse Notes or Comments** (`nurse_notes`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please share them here.
* **Screener Name** (`screener_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the screener conducting this form.
