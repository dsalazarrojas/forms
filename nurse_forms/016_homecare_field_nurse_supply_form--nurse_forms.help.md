# Homecare Field Nurse Supply Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homecare Field Nurse Supply Form is a tool used to collect information about patients' homecare needs. It is intended to be completed by field nurses who provide homecare services to patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options on the first page that apply to the patient you are working with.
2. Enter the patient's name and date of birth in the corresponding fields.
3. Check the box indicating whether or not the patient has any medical supplies.
4. Enter the name of the agency providing the services in the "Agency Name" field.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm that it has been completed accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Select Multiple Options** (form_page_1)
This page allows you to select the options that apply to the patient's homecare needs. Please choose the options that best describe the patient's situation.

*   Select the options that are true for the patient:
    *   Yes
    *   No

### **Patient Name** (patient_name)
Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification card or medical record.

### **Date of Birth (DOB)** (patient_dob)
Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### **Medical Supplies** (medical_supplies)
Check the box if the patient has any medical supplies.

*   Yes
*   No

### **Agency Name** (agency_name)
Enter the name of the agency providing the homecare services to the patient.

### **Nurse's Signature** (nurse_signature)
Sign and date the form to confirm that it has been completed accurately and thoroughly.
