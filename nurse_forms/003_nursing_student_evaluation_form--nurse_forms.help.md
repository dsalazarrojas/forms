# Nursing Student Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nursing Student Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from students about their experiences and perceptions of the nursing program. This form aims to collect both qualitative and quantitative data to help improve the quality of instruction and support services provided to nursing students. By completing this form, students will be able to share their thoughts, opinions, and suggestions on various aspects of the program, which will inform future improvements and development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Read through each page carefully, and make sure you are on the correct page before starting to complete the form.
*   For each field, provide as much detail as possible to ensure a comprehensive understanding of your experiences and opinions.
*   Be honest and thorough in your answers, as they will help shape the program's future development.
*   Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction**: A brief introduction to the form, explaining what the form is for and what is expected from you. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
*   **Instructor Rating**: Rate your instructor's teaching effectiveness using a number scale (1-5). This will help us understand how well your instructor is meeting your learning needs.
*   **Class Rating**: Rate the overall quality of the course using a number scale (1-5). This will help us gauge your satisfaction with the course content and structure.
*   **Instructor Feedback**: Provide written feedback about your instructor's teaching style, methodology, and effectiveness. Be specific about what worked well and what didn't.
*   **Class Feedback**: Provide written feedback about the course content, structure, and overall organization. Be specific about what worked well and what didn't.
*   **Suggestions**: Share any suggestions or recommendations you have for improving the program or course. Be as specific as possible with your ideas, and make sure they are feasible and relevant.
*   **Submit**: Once you have completed all fields, click "Submit" to submit your evaluation form. Your feedback will be reviewed and used to inform future improvements and development.
