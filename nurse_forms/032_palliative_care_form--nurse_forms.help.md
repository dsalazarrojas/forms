# Palliative Care Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on a patient's care and medical history to provide comprehensive care and support.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Patient Information

Please provide the patient's basic information, including their name, contact details, and any other relevant details.

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's name, address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.

### Step 2: Symptoms

Choose the symptoms the patient is experiencing from the list below.

* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the symptoms that apply from the list:
	+ Seizures
	+ Confusion
	+ Pain
	+ Loss of Appetite
	+ Difficulty Breathing
	+ Fatigue
	+ Loss of Speech
	+ Nausea

### Step 3: Care Plan

Please provide any relevant information about the patient's care plan, including medication and treatment details.

* **Care Plan** (`care_plan`, `note`, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of the patient's care plan.

### Step 4: Medication Allergies

List any medication allergies the patient has.

* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the medication the patient is allergic to:
	+ Aspirin
	+ Ibuprofen
	+ Amoxicillin
	+ Asthma Inhaler

### Step 5: Patient Family Information

Please provide any additional information about the patient's family members or caregivers.

* **Patient Family Information** (`patient_family_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about the patient's family or caregivers.

### Step 6: Medical History

Please provide any relevant medical history for the patient.

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any relevant medical history for the patient.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to provide the best possible care for the patient.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with medical professionals before completing the form.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are completed and accurate.
