# Home Care Timesheet Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Home Care Timesheet Form is a tool used to record hours worked by home care staff, providing a clear and detailed record of patient care hours.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field. This is the primary field for identifying the patient being visited.
2. Enter the start and end times of your visit in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
3. Choose the date of your visit from the calendar provided.
4. Enter the name of the staff member providing care in the "Staff Name" field.
5. Add any relevant notes or communications in the "Notes" field.
6. If necessary, enter the patient's phone number and email address in the "Phone Number" and "Email" fields, respectively.
7. For "Select One", choose the appropriate option from the dropdown menu.
8. For "Select Multiple", choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
9. For "Select Multiple 2", choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
10. Select the date and time of your visit in the "Date and Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient you visited. This field is required for accurate record-keeping.
* **Start Time**: Enter the time you started your visit. This field is required for billing and record-keeping purposes.
* **End Time**: Enter the time you ended your visit. This field is required for accurate record-keeping.
* **Date**: Select the date of your visit from the provided calendar. This field is required for accurate record-keeping.
* **Staff Name**: Enter the name of the staff member who provided care. This field is required for staff management and billing purposes.
* **Notes**: Add any relevant notes or communications about the patient or visit. This field is optional, but recommended for providing context and clarity.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number, if applicable. This field is optional.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address, if applicable. This field is optional.
* **Select One**: Choose the appropriate option from the dropdown menu. This field is optional and intended for specific scenarios or tasks.
* **Select Multiple**: Choose multiple options from the dropdown menu. This field is optional and intended for multiple scenarios or tasks.
* **Select Multiple 2**: Choose multiple options from the dropdown menu. This field is optional and intended for multiple scenarios or tasks.
* **Date and Time**: Select the date and time of your visit. This field is optional, but recommended for accurate record-keeping.

## Tips

* Ensure that you are not duplicating an existing entry.
* Double-check the accuracy of your entries, especially for dates and times.
* Use the "Select One" and "Select Multiple" fields for specific scenarios or tasks, as intended.
* Use the "Notes" field to provide context and clarity for your entries.
