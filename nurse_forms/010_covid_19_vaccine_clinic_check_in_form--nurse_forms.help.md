# COVID 19 Vaccine Clinic Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for patients to check in at the clinic for a COVID-19 vaccination. It collects personal information, appointment details, insurance status, and vaccination specifics to ensure a smooth and efficient vaccination process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in your personal information**: Enter your first and last name, date of birth, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. **Enter appointment details**: Enter the scheduled appointment time and confirmation number if available.
3. **Provide insurance information**: Indicate your insurance status and provide the name of your insurance provider and policy number (if applicable).
4. **Specify vaccination details**: Choose whether this is your first COVID-19 vaccine dose and select your preferred vaccine type if choices are available.
5. **Complete the health screening**: Answer the questions about your current health status and allergies.
6. **Enter emergency contact information**: Provide the name and phone number of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
7. **Confirm the accuracy of your information**: Review and confirm that all provided information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Appointment Time**: Enter the time of your scheduled appointment.
* **Do You Have an Appointment Confirmation Number**: Enter your confirmation number if available.
* **Is This Your First Time at This Clinic**: Indicate if this is your first visit to the clinic.
* **Insurance Status**: Indicate your current insurance status.
* **Insurance Provider Name**: Enter the name of your insurance company (if applicable).
* **Policy or Member ID Number**: Enter your policy number (if applicable).
* **Is This Your First COVID-19 Vaccine Dose**: Indicate if this is your first COVID-19 vaccine dose.
* **Previous Vaccine Type (if Not First Dose)**: Select the type of vaccine from the previous dose.
* **Vaccine Type Preference Today**: Select your preferred vaccine type if choices are available.
* **Do You Currently Have a Fever or Illness**: Answer the question about your current health status.
* **Do You Have Severe Allergies to Vaccine Components**: Answer the question about severe allergies.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **I Confirm All Information Is Accurate**: Review and confirm that all provided information is correct.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask a staff member for assistance.
* If you experience any issues or have questions during the check-in process, do not hesitate to reach out to a staff member for support.
