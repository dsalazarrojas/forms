# Patient Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to collect essential information from patients to ensure they receive personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the patient's name, address, and contact information in the "Patient Information" section (fields 1 and 2).
2. Answer the medical history questions (fields 3-6) by selecting "Yes" or "No" and providing any additional information as needed.
3. If the patient is taking medications, provide the details in field 3 (Is the patient taking any medications?).
4. Select the patient's age range (field 19) to ensure we have the correct medical guidelines for their care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. What is the patient's name?
*   Enter the patient's full name.

### 2. What is the patient's contact info?
*   Enter the patient's address.

### 3. Is the patient taking any medications?
*   Select "Yes" if the patient is taking any medications, and provide details in field 4 (What is the patient's medical history?).

### 4. What is the patient's medical history?
*   Describe the patient's medical history.

### 5. Additional medical history info?
*   Enter any additional details about the patient's medical history.

### 6. What are the patient's medical history info?
*   Enter any medical history information.

### 7. Is the patient's contact info public?
*   Select "Yes" if you want to share the patient's contact information publicly, otherwise select "No".

### 8. Who is the patient's emergency contact?
*   Enter the emergency contact name.

### 9. What is the patient's age?
*   Enter the patient's age (0-100 years).

### 10. What is the patient's medical conditions info?
*   Enter any medical conditions information.

### 11. Additional medical conditions info?
*   Enter any additional medical conditions information.

### 12. What is the patient's medical info?
*   Enter any medical information.

### 13. Additional medical info?
*   Enter any additional medical information.

### 14. What are the patient's contact preferences?
*   Select all that apply from the options.

### 15. Contact preferences info?
*   Enter any contact preferences information.

### 16. What are the patient's medical conditions?
*   Select all that apply from the options.

### 17. Medical history info?
*   Enter any medical history information.

### 18. Medical conditions additional info?
*   Enter any medical conditions additional information.

### 19. What is the patient's age range?
*   Select from the age range options.

### 20. What is the patient's contact info (again)?
*   Enter the patient's address.

Please make sure to read each field's label carefully and enter your answers truthfully to help us provide the best care for the patient.
