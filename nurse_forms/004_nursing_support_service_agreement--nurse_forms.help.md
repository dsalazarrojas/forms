# Nursing Support Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients regarding their nursing support service requests and agreements. It will aid healthcare providers in understanding the client's needs and requirements, ensuring that the services are provided accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. On the first page, "Client Info", provide the required information about yourself as the client.
	* Enter your contact details, including your contact number and email.
	* Select the services you need from the provided options.
2. On the second page, "Schedule Requests", choose a suitable time slot for your appointment.
3. On the third page, "Services Accepted/Denied", indicate which services you would like to accept or deny.
	* Select the services you accept and provide a comment if necessary.
	* Select the services you deny and provide a reason or comment if necessary.
4. On the fourth page, "Client Signature", sign the client signature field.
	* Enter the date and your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info** (`client_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, contact number, and contact email.
* **Services Needed** (`services_needed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the services you require from the provided options.
* **Schedule Requests** (`schedule_requests`, `select_one`, required): Choose a suitable time slot for your appointment.
* **Schedule Requests**
	+ 8:00 AM - 12:00 PM: Select this option if you prefer morning appointments.
	+ 1:00 PM - 3:00 PM: Select this option if you prefer afternoon appointments.
	+ 2:00 PM - 6:00 PM: Select this option if you prefer evening appointments.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, optional): This field is for your digital signature. If you are filling this out in person, sign it as per the instructions provided.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, etc.).
* **Services Accepted** (`services_accepted`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the services you would like to accept.
* **Services Denied** (`services_denied`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the services you would like to deny and provide a reason or comment if necessary.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please enter them here.
* **Services Denied Reason** (`services_denied_reason`, `text`, optional): Provide a reason for denying the services selected.
* **Services Denied Comment** (`services_denied_comment`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comment you would like to provide for the denied services.
* **Services Accepted Comment** (`services_accepted_comment`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comment you would like to provide for the accepted services.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or information you would like to provide.
