# CNA Shift Restoration Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CNA Shift Restoration Request form is designed to gather information for employees who are seeking restoration of their shift or position after being off work due to various reasons. This form is a crucial tool for HR and management to process requests and assess the appropriateness of the employee's restoration based on the provided information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full Name**, which is your official name as listed in our personnel records.
2. Enter your **Employee ID** for identification purposes.
3. Select your **Unit or Department**, which is where you are currently or were assigned to work.
4. Choose your **Current Employment Status**, which reflects your current working status, such as Active, Temporary Leave, or Suspended.
5. Enter the **Original Shift End Date**, which is the date you were originally scheduled to end your shift or position.
6. Enter the **Requested Shift Restoration Date**, which is the date you would like to be restored to your shift or position.
7. Choose the **Reason for Shift Restoration Request**, which should be one of the provided options.
8. Select the **Requested Shift Type**, which reflects the type of shift you are requesting to be restored to.
9. Enter the **Requested Hours per Week**, which should be an accurate representation of your expected working hours.
10. If applicable, please select the **Supporting documentation** you are providing, such as medical clearance, training certificates, or supervisor recommendations.
11. Enter the **Supervisor Name and Recommendation**, which should be a confirmation or recommendation from your supervisor.
12. If necessary, please provide any **Conditions or Restrictions** for your restoration request.
13. Enter the **HR Contact Name**, who will be responsible for processing your request.
14. Enter the **Date of Request Submission**, which is the date you are submitting this form.
15. Finally, confirm that the information provided is accurate with the **Employee Acknowledgment**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your official name as listed in our personnel records.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID for identification purposes.
* **Unit or Department**: Select the unit or department where you are currently or were assigned to work.
* **Current Employment Status**: Choose the status that reflects your current working status.
* **Original Shift End Date**: Enter the date you were originally scheduled to end your shift or position.
* **Requested Shift Restoration Date**: Enter the date you would like to be restored to your shift or position.
* **Reason for Shift Restoration Request**: Choose the reason for your restoration request from the provided options.
* **Requested Shift Type**: Select the type of shift you are requesting to be restored to.
* **Requested Hours per Week**: Enter an accurate representation of your expected working hours.
* **Supporting documentation**: If applicable, select the documentation you are providing to support your request.
* **Supervisor Name and Recommendation**: Enter your supervisor's name and recommendation.
* **Any conditions or restrictions**: If necessary, please provide any conditions or restrictions for your restoration request.
* **HR Contact Name**: Enter the name of the HR representative who will process your request.
* **Date of Request Submission**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Employee Acknowledgment**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
