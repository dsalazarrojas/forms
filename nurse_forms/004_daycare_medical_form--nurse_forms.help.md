# Daycare Medical Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for daycare centers or childcare facilities to collect medical information and consent from parents or guardians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic information as a parent or guardian.
2. Share your child's health information, including allergies, medications, and immunizations.
3. Select whether you give consent for medical records and submissions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Info**: Enter your name, contact information, or any other relevant details about yourself.
  (Note: This field is not required.)
- **Health Info**: Describe your child's medical history, including allergies, medications, and immunizations.
  (Note: This field is not required, but it's crucial for our facility to ensure your child's safety.)
- **Medical Records**: Choose the medical records you'd like to share with our facility. You can select multiple options.
  (Options: Allergies, Medications, Immunizations)
- **Medical Files**: Attach any additional medical files or documents you'd like us to have on record.
  (Note: This field is not required.)
- **HIPAA Compliancy**: Confirm whether you consent to our facility storing and handling your child's medical records.
  (Options: Yes, No)
- **Secure Submissions**: Confirm whether you consent to receiving secure and encrypted submission responses.
  (Options: Yes, No)

## Tips
- Please answer each question thoroughly and accurately to ensure your child's safety and our facility's compliance.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask our staff or contact us at form@gic.mx.
- We appreciate your cooperation in keeping your child's medical records up-to-date and secure.
