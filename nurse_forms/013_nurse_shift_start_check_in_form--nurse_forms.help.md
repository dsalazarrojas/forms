# nurse_shift_start_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The nurse_shift_start_check_in_form is a form used by nurses to report their start time, location, and other relevant details at the beginning of their shift. It is a personal responsibility of each nurse to complete this form accurately and on time to ensure accurate shift reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form to begin.
2. Fill in your shift start time in the **start_time** field.
3. Enter your **shift_id**.
4. Enter your **nurse_id** (employee ID).
5. Select your **shift_location** from the available options.
6. Select whether a **team_lead_nurse** is available.
7. Enter your **nurse_name**.
8. Enter your **nurse_role**.
9. Indicate whether you are **check_in** (arrived for shift) or **check_out** (leaving early).
10. If necessary, provide a reason for your **reason_for_absence**.
11. Enter any relevant **shift_notes**.
12. Finally, indicate who **submitted_by** (you) and record the time of submission in **submission_time**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **start_time** (`start_time`, required/optional: required): Enter the time you are starting your shift.
* **shift_id** (`shift_id`, required/optional: false): Enter the ID of your shift to ensure accurate tracking.
* **nurse_id** (`nurse_id`, required/optional: false): Enter your unique employee ID.
* **shift_location** (`shift_location`, required/optional: false): Select the location where you are working from the available options.
* **team_lead_nurse** (`team_lead_nurse`, required/optional: false): Select whether you have a team lead nurse available.
* **nurse_name** (`nurse_name`, required/optional: false): Enter your name.
* **nurse_role** (`nurse_role`, required/optional: false): Enter your role or position.
* **check_in** (`check_in`, required/optional: false): Indicate that you are arriving for your shift.
* **check_out** (`check_out`, required/optional: false): Indicate that you are leaving early.
* **reason_for_absence** (`reason_for_absence`, required/optional: false): If leaving early, enter a brief explanation for your absence.
* **shift_notes** (`shift_notes`, required/optional: false): Add any relevant notes or comments about your shift.
* **submitted_by** (`submitted_by`, required/optional: false): Ensure that you are submitting this form.
* **submission_time** (`submission_time`, required/optional: false): Record the time of submission.
