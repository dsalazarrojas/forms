# CNA Scope Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CNA Scope Assessment Form is designed to evaluate the competency and skills of a Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA) in areas such as clinical skills, safety protocols, and overall competency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Rate each skill area as "Competent", "Needs Improvement", "Not Competent", or "Not Observed".
3. Provide a brief description in the "Areas for Improvement" and "Training Recommendations" fields if necessary.
4. Sign and date the form after completing all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **CNA Name** (`cna_name`, text, Required): Enter the full name of the CNA.
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, Required): Enter the staff identification number of the CNA.
- **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, Required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
- **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, Required): Enter the name of the healthcare facility.
- **Unit or Department** (`unit_department`, text, Required): Enter the unit or department where the CNA is employed.
- **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, Required): Enter the name of the supervisor or evaluator.
- **Assessor Title** (`assessor_title`, text, Required): Enter the title of the supervisor or evaluator.
- **CNA Certification Status** (`certification_status`, select_one, Required): Select the current status of the CNA's certification:
	+ Active and Current
	+ Pending Renewal
	+ Expired
	+ In Training
- **State of Certification** (`certification_state`, text, Required): Enter the state issuing the CNA's certification.
- **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, Required): Select the number of years the CNA has worked:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 4-7 years
	+ 8-15 years
	+ More than 15 years
- **Vital Signs Measurement** (`vital_signs_competency`, select_one, Required): Rate the CNA's ability to measure vital signs:
	+ Competent
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Not Competent
	+ Not Observed
- **Activities of Daily Living Assistance** (`adl_assistance`, select_one, Required): Rate the CNA's ability to provide assistance with daily living activities:
	+ Competent
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Not Competent
	+ Not Observed
- **Mobility and Transfer Assistance** (`mobility_assistance`, select_one, Required): Rate the CNA's ability to assist with patient movement and positioning:
	+ Competent
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Not Competent
	+ Not Observed
- **Infection Control Practices** (`infection_control`, select_one, Required): Rate the CNA's knowledge of hand hygiene and PPE protocols:
	+ Competent
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Not Competent
	+ Not Observed
- **Documentation Skills** (`documentation_skills`, select_one, Required): Rate the CNA's ability to document patient information:
	+ Competent
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Not Competent
	+ Not Observed
- **Communication with Patients** (`communication_skills`, select_one, Required): Rate the CNA's patient interaction skills:
	+ Competent
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Not Competent
	+ Not Observed
- **Patient Safety Protocols** (`patient_safety_protocols`, select_one, Required): Rate the CNA's knowledge of fall prevention and safety checks:
	+ Competent
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Not Competent
	+ Not Observed
- **Emergency Procedures Knowledge** (`emergency_procedures`, select_one, Required): Rate the CNA's knowledge of emergency procedures:
	+ Competent
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Not Competent
	+ Not Observed
- **Understanding of Scope of Practice** (`scope_understanding`, select_one, Required): Rate the CNA's understanding of their scope of practice:
	+ Excellent Understanding
	+ Good Understanding
	+ Fair Understanding
	+ Poor Understanding
- **Overall Competency Rating** (`overall_competency`, select_one, Required): Rate the CNA's overall competency:
	+ Fully Competent
	+ Competent with Minor Guidance
	+ Needs Additional Training
	+ Not Competent - Remediation Required
- **Identified Strengths** (`strengths`, text, Optional): Describe the CNA's areas of excellence.
- **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, Required): Describe the skills the CNA needs to develop.
- **Training Recommendations** (`training_recommendations`, text, Optional): Suggest training or continuing education the CNA needs.
- **Reassessment Needed** (`reassess_needed`, select_one, Required): Determine if a reassessment is needed:
	+ Yes within 30 days
	+ Yes within 90 days
	+ No reassessment needed
- **CNA Comments** (`cna_comments`, text, Optional): Enter your comments about the CNA's performance.
- **CNA Signature** (`cna_signature`, text, Required): Sign your name for official certification.
- **Assessor Signature** (`assessor_signature`, text, Required): Sign your name as the supervisor or evaluator for official certification.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out thoroughly and accurately.
- The form is not intended for use as a formal evaluation tool by third-party agencies or for billing purposes.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
