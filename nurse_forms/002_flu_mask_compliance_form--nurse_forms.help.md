# Flu Mask Compliance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's flu vaccination status and mask compliance in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
2. Select one of the options under "Has The Flu" to indicate your current flu status.
3. Choose "True" or "False" under "Wear A Mask" to indicate if you are currently wearing a mask.
4. Indicate whether you wear a vaccination tag under "Wear Vaccination Tag".
5. Enter the date of your last flu vaccination under "Flu Vaccination Date", if applicable.
6. Select "True" or "False" under "Last Flu Vaccination Status" to indicate the status of your last vaccination.
7. Enter the date of your last flu vaccination under "Last Flu Vaccination Date", if applicable.
8. Repeat the above steps for "Flu Vaccination Date 2", "Last Flu Vaccination Status 2", "Last Flu Vaccination Date 2", "Flu Vaccination Date 3", "Last Flu Vaccination Status 3", "Last Flu Vaccination Date 3", "Last Flu Vaccination Status 4", "Last Flu Vaccination Date 4", "Last Flu Vaccination Status 5", "Last Flu Vaccination Date 5", "Last Flu Vaccination Status 6", and "Last Flu Vaccination Date 6".
