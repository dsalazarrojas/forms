# Daycare Allergy Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a child's allergies, including a brief summary of allergies and any relevant medical information or schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in the form carefully and accurately**. If you're unsure about any information, please ask for help from a healthcare professional.
2. **Provide all necessary details**. Make sure to fill in all fields, even if you're not sure or don't know the answer.
3. **Select all relevant allergies** that apply to the child. You can select multiple allergies using the "Allergies" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Name**: The name of the child you're filling this form for.
* **Allergies**: A list of allergies that the child may have. Select all relevant allergies from the list below:
	+ Eczema
	+ Peanut
	+ Tree nut
	+ Fish
	+ Shellfish
	+ Milk
	+ Soy
	+ Wheat
	+ Egg
	+ Sesame
* **Date of Last Dose**: The date of the last dose of medication the child received for their allergies (MM/DD/YYYY, e.g., 02/15/2024).
* **Next Dose Schedule**: A note about the next dose schedule of the child's allergy medication.
* **Notes**: Any additional information or instructions for the healthcare provider.

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to a healthcare professional.
* Remember to update this form as necessary to reflect any changes in the child's allergy status.
