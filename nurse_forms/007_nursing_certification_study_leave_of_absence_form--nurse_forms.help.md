# Nursing Certification Study Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for nurses to request study leave for their certification courses. Please use this form to request a study leave from your supervisor or HR department for a specified period, and provide details about your certification type, study duration, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the correct certification type for your study leave.
3. Enter your start and end dates for the study leave period.
4. Provide a valid reason for your request.
5. Specify your study location.
6. Choose the approver for your request.
7. Enter your contact information (email, phone number, and name).
8. Submit the form to complete your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Information (Text)** (`request_info`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your request.
* **Certification Type (Select Multiple)** (`certification_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable certification types for your study leave (e.g., NCLEX-RN, NCLEX-ATR, NCPD-PN, AANP, CNS).
* **Start Date (Date)** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to start your study leave.
* **End Date (Date)** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to end your study leave.
* **Reason for Leave (Text)** (`reason_for_leave`, `text`, required): Provide a valid reason for your study leave.
* **Study Location (Text)** (`study_location`, `text`, required): Specify the location where you will be studying.
* **Approvals (Select One)** (`approvals`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the approver options (Team Lead, Nurse Manager, HR, Supervisor).
* **Notes (Text)** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Provide additional information or comments about your request.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone (Text)** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Certification Course (Select One)** (`certification_course`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are taking a certification course.
* **Study Duration (Number)** (`study_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the number of days for your study leave period.
* **Estimated Return Date (Date)** (`estimated_return_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you expect to return to work.
* **Actual Return Date (Date)** (`actual_return_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the actual date you returned to work, if different from estimated.
* **Days Absence (Number)** (`days_absence`, `number`, required): Enter the number of days you will be absent from work.
* **Study Status (Select One)** (`study_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the status of your study leave (Pending, Active, Completed, Withdrawn).
* **Route To (Select One)** (`route_to`, `select_one`, required): Choose the route to send your approval request (HR, Manager, Team Lead).
* **Notes for Approver (Text)** (`notes_for_approver`, `text`, optional): Provide additional information or comments for the approver.
* **Approver Name (Text)** (`approver_name`, `text`, required): Enter the approver's name.
* **Approver Email (Email)** (`approver_email`, `email`, required): Enter the approver's email address.
* **Approver Phone (Text)** (`approver_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the approver's phone number.
* **Approver Comments (Text)** (`approver_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide additional comments or feedback from the approver.
* **Form Submitter (Text)** (`form_submitter`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Phone (Text)** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Route To Email (Text)** (`route_to_email`, `text`, required): Enter the email address to send your approval request.

Tips: Ensure you fill out the form completely and accurately. If you have any doubts or concerns, contact your supervisor or HR department for assistance.
