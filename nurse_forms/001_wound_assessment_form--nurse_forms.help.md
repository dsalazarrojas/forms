# Wound Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wound Assessment Form is a tool used by healthcare professionals to collect patient information and wound details for assessment and treatment planning. This form helps gather relevant data to provide effective care and treatment for patients with wounds.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information**
	* Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information in the Patient Information section.
2. **Wound Information**
	* Provide details about the wound, including its location, size, depth, and any other relevant characteristics.
3. **Assessment Result**
	* Record the assessment results, such as the wound's stage and any notable findings.
4. **Treatment Plan**
	* Outline the treatment plan, including any medications, dressings, or other interventions recommended for the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information**
	* **Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical records.
	* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
	* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number and email address.
- **Wound Information**
	* **Wound Location** (`wound_location`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the location of the wound, such as the affected area of the body.
	* **Wound Size** (`wound_size`, `text`, required/optional): Measure and report the size of the wound in centimeters or inches.
	* **Wound Depth** (`wound_depth`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the depth of the wound, including whether it is superficial or deep.
- **Assessment Result**
	* **Assessment Result** (`assessment_result`, `text`, required/optional): Record the results of the assessment, including the wound's stage and any notable findings.
- **Treatment Plan**
	* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, `text`, required/optional): Outline the recommended treatment plan, including any medications, dressings, or other interventions.

## Tips
- Ensure accuracy and up-to-date information is provided for the patient's contact details.
- Use standard medical terminology for wound classification and description.
- Review and update the treatment plan regularly to ensure it is relevant and effective.
