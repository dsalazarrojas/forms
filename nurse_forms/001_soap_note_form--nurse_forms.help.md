# Soap Note Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soap Note Form is designed to document a patient's medical information, including their name, medical provider's name, date and time of visit, patient condition, medical provider type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) next to the label.
2. Enter the patient's name in the `patient_name` field.
3. Enter the patient's medical provider's name in the `provider_name` field.
4. Choose the date of the visit in the `date` field.
5. Choose the time of the visit in the `time` field.
6. Select the patient's condition from the options provided in the `patient_condition` field.
7. Select the type of medical provider from the options provided in the `medical_provider` field.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes from the medical provider in the `medical_provider_notes` field.
9. If necessary, select the patient's status from the options provided in the `patient_status` field.
10. Enter any additional comments or notes from the medical provider in the `medical_provider_notes_2` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name**: The patient's name. Enter the patient's full name.
* **provider_name**: The name of the medical provider (e.g., nurse, doctor).
* **date**: Date of visit. This field type is specifically designed to handle date inputs (mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyyy).
* **time**: Time of visit. This field type is specifically designed to handle time inputs (HH:MM, 12:00, or 3:30 PM).
* **patient_condition**: Patient's medical condition. Select from the provided options.
* **medical_provider**: Type of medical provider. Select from the provided options (Nurse, Physician, or Other).
* **medical_provider_notes**: Additional comments or notes from the medical provider.
* **patient_status**: The patient's status. Select from the provided options (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
* **medical_provider_notes_2**: Additional comments or notes from the medical provider.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields (\*) before submitting the form.
- Use the provided field types (date, time, select_one, etc.) for accurate inputs.
- If you are unsure about any field or need further assistance, contact us at form@gic.mx.
