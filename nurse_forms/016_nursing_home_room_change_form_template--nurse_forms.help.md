# Nursing Home Room Change Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document a change in a resident's room in a nursing home. It captures information about the resident's current and new room, the reason for the move, and the status of the move.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter a unique ID number for the form**: Enter a unique ID number to identify the form. This field is not required.
2. **Enter the resident's name**: Enter the name of the resident being transferred.
3. **Indicate if the resident is currently in the old room**: Select "Yes" if the resident is currently occupying the old room, and "No" otherwise.
4. **Select the new room for the resident**: Choose the new room that the resident will be transferred to.
5. **Explain the reason for the move**: Enter a brief explanation for the reason why the resident is being transferred to a new room.
6. **Enter the date and time of the move**: Enter the date and time when the resident will be moved to the new room.
7. **Indicate if the move has been approved**: Select "Yes" if the move has been approved, and "No" otherwise.
8. **Enter the date and time when the move was approved**: Enter the date and time when the move was approved.
9. **Indicate if the move has been completed**: Select "Yes" if the move has been completed, and "No" otherwise.
10. **Enter the date and time when the move was completed**: Enter the date and time when the move was completed.
11. **Enter your name and email**: Enter your name and email address if you are the person submitting and completing the form.
12. **Indicate who approved the move**: Select "Yes" if the move was approved by the facility's management, and "No" otherwise.
13. **Enter the date and time when the move was approved**: Enter the date and time when the move was approved.
14. **Indicate who completed the move**: Select "Yes" if you are the person who completed the move, and "No" otherwise.
15. **Enter the date and time when the move was completed**: Enter the date and time when the move was completed.
