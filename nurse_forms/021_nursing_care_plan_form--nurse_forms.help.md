# nursing_care_plan_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The nursing care plan form is used to document the care plan for a patient, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and recorded accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2.  Select the patient's date of birth from the calendar in the "Patient Date of Birth" field.
3.  If applicable, list the patient's medical condition(s) in the "Medical Condition" field.
4.  Select the medications the patient is currently taking from the "Medications" field.
5.  List any allergies the patient has in the "Allergies" field.
6.  Select the patient's vital signs from the "Vital Signs" field.
7.  Enter the planned nursing care in the "Nursing Care Planned" field.
8.  If applicable, enter any care that was delivered in the "Nursing Care Delivered" field.
9.  Add any additional notes in the "Note" field.
10. Enter the patient's phone number and email (if applicable) in the respective fields.
11. Select the patient's family contact from the "Patient Family Member" field.
12. Enter the contact number for the patient's emergency contact in the "Contact Number" field.
13. Select whether an emergency contact exists ("Yes" or "No") in the "Emergency Contact" field.
14. Enter the name and number of the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" and "Emergency Contact Number" fields, if applicable.
15. Enter the name and title of the nurse responsible for the patient's care in the "Nurse Name" and "Nurse Title" fields.
16. Select the date and time of the care in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
17. If applicable, enter the name and specialty of the doctor who prescribed the medication in the "Doctor Name" and "Doctor Specialty" fields.
18. Enter any additional information about the patient's condition in the "Patient Condition" field.
19. Enter the patient's room number in the "Patient Room" field.
20. Enter the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field.
