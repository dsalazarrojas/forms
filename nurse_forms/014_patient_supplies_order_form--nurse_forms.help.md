# patient-supplies-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for ordering patient supplies for medical purposes. It is essential to fill in accurate information to ensure the order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Double-check your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (1): Enter the patient's basic details, such as address, contact information, etc. This will help identify the patient and ensure the order is directed to the correct location.
* **Patient Name** (2): Enter the patient's full name. Please ensure it is spelled correctly.
* **Medical Supply Information** (3): This field is used to enter any other information about the medical supply, if applicable.
* **Medical Supply Name** (4): Enter the name of the medical supply to be ordered.
* **Quantity** (5): Enter the quantity of the medical supply needed.
* **Unit Price** (6): Enter the unit price of the medical supply.
* **Total Cost** (7): This field is auto-calculated based on the quantity and unit price. Do not enter any value in this field as it will be calculated by the system.
* **Costs** (8): Choose the correct option to select the type of costs associated with the medical supply.
* **Costs Notes** (9): Provide any additional information regarding the costs of the medical supply.
* **Materials** (10): Enter any additional materials required for the medical supply.
* **Material Price** (11): Enter the unit price of the materials.
* **Material Costs** (12): This field is auto-calculated based on the quantity and unit price of the materials. Do not enter any value in this field as it will be calculated by the system.
* **Material Notes** (13): Provide any additional information regarding the materials required for the medical supply.
* **Medical Supply Notes** (14): Enter any additional notes regarding the medical supply ordered.
* **Patient Contacts** (15): Enter any contact information of the patient for future reference.
* **Medical Staff Contacts** (16): Enter any contact information of the medical staff involved in the order.
