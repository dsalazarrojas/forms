# Nurses Welfare Member's Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nurses Welfare Member's Registration Form is designed to collect information from new members wishing to join the Nurses Welfare organization. This form is meant for individuals who are interested in becoming part of our community and receiving its benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your accurate and up-to-date information. Make sure to check the "Required" fields as they are essential for processing your membership application. The form is divided into sections with clear instructions and labels to guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents. This field is required for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents. This field is required for identification purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Provide your date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy). This field is required for age verification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your identification documents. This field is required for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number for communication with the organization. This field is optional but recommended for ease of contact.
* **Membership Category** (`membership_category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the category that best describes your type of membership. Please choose one of the available options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your medical license number if applicable. This field is optional but recommended for verification purposes.
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select your current membership status. Please choose one of the available options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Address** (`address`, note, required/optional): Enter your address for communication and verification purposes. This field is optional but recommended for accurate address verification. You can provide a note or a brief description of your address if you prefer.

## Tips
Please double-check your information before submission. Ensure that your email address and phone number are up-to-date and accurate. If you have any issues or questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
