# Nursing Preceptor Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Nurse Preceptor Evaluation Form" is designed to assess the effectiveness of preceptors in providing adequate support to new nurses. This form helps in identifying areas of improvement and providing valuable feedback to preceptors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the relevant options for each question.
2. Fill in the date of evaluation.
3. Provide notes on the new nurse's care and preceptor's performance, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **New Nurse Evaluation** (`new_nurse_evaluation`): This question assesses whether the new nurse was effective in providing care to patients. Select 'Yes' if the new nurse performed well, and 'No' if they did not.
- **Does the preceptor provide adequate support for the new nurse?** (`preceptor_support`): This question checks if the preceptor offered sufficient guidance and support to the new nurse. Choose 'True' if the preceptor was supportive, and 'False' otherwise.
- **Date of Evaluation** (`evaluation_date`): Enter the date when this evaluation took place.
- **Effectiveness** (`preceptor_effectiveness`): Assess the preceptor's performance on a scale of Very Effective, Somewhat Effective, or Not Effective.
- **New Nurse's Care** (`new_nurse_care`): Evaluate the new nurse's care, selecting 'Safe', 'Needs Improvement', or 'Needs a Lot of Improvement'.
- **Evaluation Notes** (`evaluation_notes`): Add any additional notes regarding the new nurse's care and preceptor's performance.
- **Preceptor Suggestions** (`preceptor_suggestions`): Provide feedback to the preceptor on areas of improvement.
- **Preceptor Performance Comments** (`preceptor_performance_comments`): Write a summary of the preceptor's strengths and areas for improvement.

## Tips

* Ensure you complete all required fields accurately.
* Provide specific examples to support your evaluation.
* Review and update this form regularly to track progress and identify areas that need improvement.
