# first_aid_incident_report_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The First Aid Incident Report Form is used to document incidents requiring first aid treatment within a hospital or healthcare setting. It captures critical information about the patient, incident circumstances, and first aid response.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the patient's identity confirmed, if necessary.
2. Enter the patient's name in the **patient name** field.
3. Enter the location where the incident occurred in the **incident location** field.
4. Enter the date and time of the incident in the **incident date** and **incident time** fields, respectively.
5. Describe the first aid treatment provided in the **first aid treatment** field.
6. If applicable, enter a brief description of the patient's medical condition in the **medical condition** field.
7. Provide a detailed description of the injury in the **injury description** field.
8. Enter the name of the person providing first aid in the **first aider name** field.
9. Enter the phone number and email of the person providing first aid in the **first aider phone** and **first aider email** fields, respectively.
10. If there were witnesses, enter their name in the **witness 1 name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient name**: Enter the name of the patient who received first aid.
* **incident location**: Enter the exact location where the incident occurred.
* **incident date**: Enter the date of the incident using the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **incident time**: Enter the time of the incident using the 24-hour clock format "HH:MM".
* **first aid treatment**: Describe the first aid treatment provided to the patient, including any medications administered or procedures performed.
* **medical condition**: If applicable, describe the medical condition that may have contributed to the incident.
* **injury description**: Provide a detailed description of the patient's injury.
* **first aider name**: Enter the name of the person providing first aid.
* **first aider phone**: Enter the phone number of the person providing first aid.
* **first aider email**: Enter the email of the person providing first aid.
* **witness 1 name**: If there were witnesses, enter their name(s) in this field.
