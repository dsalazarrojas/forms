# medication_administration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of medication administration for patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the medication in the first field.
2. Enter the dosage and administration instructions in the second field.
3. Indicate whether the medication was administered or not by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the third field.
4. If the patient experienced any adverse reactions or has comments about the administration process, enter them in the fourth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **medication_administration_form_1** (text, required): Enter the name of the medication being administered.
* **medication_administration_form_2** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate that the medication was administered and "No" to indicate that it was not.
* **medication_administration_form_3** (select_multiple, required): Select all options that apply to any adverse reactions or comments about the administration process.
* **medication_administration_form_4** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the administration process.
