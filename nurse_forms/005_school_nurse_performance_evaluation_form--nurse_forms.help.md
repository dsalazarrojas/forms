# School Nurse Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the performance of school nurses in our district, with the goal of promoting professional growth and development. We strive to create a supportive and inclusive environment for all our nurses, and your feedback will help us achieve this goal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Please select the evaluation period start date for the evaluation cycle.
* Choose how you would rate your performance during this evaluation period.
* Enter the end date of the evaluation period.
* Provide your name as it appears on your identification.
* Type the name of the school where you were evaluated.
* Enter the school district where you provided service.
* Enter the school address where you were evaluated.
* Select the staff members you were evaluated by, and rate their performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Evaluation Period Start Date (`form_1`)
* This field is required: `false`
* This field is a date input field where you can select the start date of the evaluation period.
* Please enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### Staff Evaluation (`form_2`)
* This field is optional: `false`
* This field is a select-one input field where you can choose one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Unsatisfactory
* This field is used to evaluate your overall performance during this evaluation period.

### Evaluation Period End Date (`form_3`)
* This field is required: `false`
* This field is a date input field where you can select the end date of the evaluation period.
* Please enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### Nurse Name (`form_4`)
* This field is optional: `false`
* This field is a text input field where you can enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* Please enter your name in the format `Last Name, First Name`.

### School Name (`form_5`)
* This field is optional: `false`
* This field is a text input field where you can enter the name of the school where you were evaluated.
* Please enter the school name as it appears on your official records.

### School District (`form_6`)
* This field is optional: `false`
* This field is a text input field where you can enter the school district where you provided service.
* Please enter the school district as it appears on your official records.

### School Address (`form_7`)
* This field is optional: `false`
* This field is a text input field where you can enter the address of the school where you were evaluated.
* Please enter the address in the format `Street Number, Street Name, City, State, ZIP`.

### Staff Evaluation (`form_8`)
* This field is optional: `false`
* This field is a select_multiple input field where you can choose multiple staff members you were evaluated by, and rate their performance.
* Each selected option will be listed with a corresponding rating. Please choose all the staff members you were evaluated by.
