# Flu Vaccine Requirement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Flu Vaccine Requirement Form is used to gather information about the patient's flu vaccine requirement status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the question about whether you have received the flu vaccine.
2. If you have received the flu vaccine, enter the date and time of administration.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments about your flu vaccine status.
4. Review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy.
