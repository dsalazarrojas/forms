### COVID 19 Post Acute Care Facility Transfer Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is used to transfer patients with COVID-19 from one facility to another.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the patient's current condition and transfer requirements before filling out the form.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
3.  Double-check the form for errors before submitting it.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Transferring Facility Name** (`Transferring Facility Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the facility where the patient is currently located.
*   **Receiving Facility Name** (`Receiving Facility Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the facility where the patient will be transferred to.
*   **Patient Full Name** (`Patient Full Name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
*   **Patient Date of Birth** (`Patient Date of Birth`, `date`, required): Enter the patient's date of birth as recorded on their identification documents.
*   **COVID-19 Status** (`COVID-19 Status`, `select_one`, required): Select the patient's COVID-19 status from the options provided.
*   **Date of Most Recent COVID-19 Test** (`Date of Most Recent COVID-19 Test`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the patient's most recent COVID-19 test, if applicable.
*   **Most Recent Test Result** (`Most Recent Test Result`, `select_one`, optional): Select the result of the patient's most recent COVID-19 test, if applicable.
*   **Current Isolation Precautions** (`Current Isolation Precautions`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the isolation precautions currently in place for the patient.
*   **Respiratory Support Required** (`Respiratory Support Required`, `select_one`, required): Select the respiratory support required for the patient.
*   **Current Temperature (F)** (`Current Temperature (F)`, `number`, required): Enter the patient's current temperature in degrees Fahrenheit.
*   **Oxygen Saturation (%)** (`Oxygen Saturation (%)`, `number`, required): Enter the patient's current oxygen saturation level as a percentage.
*   **Transferring Nurse Name** (`Transferring Nurse Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the healthcare professional completing the form.
*   **Contact Phone Number** (`Contact Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter the contact phone number of the healthcare professional completing the form.

### Tips

*   Always double-check the form for errors before submitting it.
*   Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you are unsure about any information, contact the relevant healthcare team for assistance.
*   The patient's privacy and confidentiality should be respected at all times.
