# Covid 19 Vaccine Resident Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is used to collect information about residents who have received the COVID-19 vaccine, including their medical condition, contact information, vaccine type, and follow-up schedules.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select a resident from the list of residents who have received the vaccine.
2. Fill out the form with the required and optional information for the selected resident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **resident info** (Text, Optional)
	+ This field is used to enter the resident's information, such as their name, address, and other identifying details.
* **has_medical_condition** (Select One, Optional)
	+ This field indicates whether the resident has a medical condition that may require special attention.
* **contact method** (Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ This field allows you to select the method of contact used to reach the resident, such as phone, email, or messaging.
* **contact number** (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter the resident's contact number in this field.
* **email** (Email, Optional)
	+ Enter the resident's email address in this field.
* **vaccine status** (Select One, Optional)
	+ This field indicates the current status of the resident's vaccine, such as Active or Inactive.
* **date of birth** (Date, Optional)
	+ Enter the resident's date of birth in this field.
* **date of administration** (Date, Optional)
	+ Enter the date the resident received the vaccine in this field.
* **vaccination site(s)** (Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ This field allows you to select the site where the vaccine was administered, such as a hospital or clinic.
* **vaccination time(s)** (Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ This field allows you to select the time the vaccine was administered, such as morning or afternoon.
* **medical history** (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter any relevant medical history for the resident in this field.
* **vaccine type** (Select One, Optional)
	+ This field indicates the type of vaccine administered to the resident.
* **vaccine dose** (Number, Optional)
	+ Enter the number of vaccine doses administered to the resident.
* **vaccine date** (Date, Optional)
	+ Enter the date of the last vaccine administration in this field.
* **vaccination status** (Select One, Optional)
	+ This field indicates the current status of the resident's vaccination, such as Active or Inactive.
* **vaccination time** (Time, Optional)
	+ Enter the time of the last vaccine administration in this field.
* **vaccine dosages** (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter any additional dosages information for the resident in this field.
* **vaccine administering doctor** (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter the name of the doctor who administered the vaccine in this field.
* **vaccine administering nurse** (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter the name of the nurse who administered the vaccine in this field.
* **medical staff** (Select One, Optional)
	+ This field indicates whether the resident requires medical staff to be present during follow-up vaccinations.
* **vaccine side effects** (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter any side effects experienced by the resident after the vaccine in this field.
* **follow up schedules** (Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ This field allows you to select the follow-up schedule for the resident, such as weekly or monthly.
* **follow up notes** (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter any follow-up notes for the resident in this field.
* **vaccine administered** (Select One, Optional)
	+ This field indicates whether the vaccine was successfully administered to the resident.
* **resident status** (Select One, Optional)
	+ This field indicates the resident's current status, such as Active or Inactive.
