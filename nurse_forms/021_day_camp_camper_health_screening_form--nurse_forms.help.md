# Day Camp Camper Health Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Day Camp Camper Health Screening Form is designed to gather essential health information from campers before their participation in a day camp program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and answer all questions relevant to your child's health status.
* Provide accurate and honest information about your child's medical history, conditions, and allergies.
* Ensure you provide all necessary contact information in case of an emergency.
* Double-check your answers to ensure completeness and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Day Camp Camper Health Screening Form** (`title`): This is the title of the form, which is not a field to be completed.
* **Camper Details** (`camper_details`): Provide a brief description of your child's health status, including any relevant medical history or conditions.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`): Select all medical conditions that apply to your child, such as fever, rash or skin condition, cough or sore throat, and others.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Describe any previous medical conditions or illnesses that your child has experienced.
* **Camper Medical Info** (`camper_medical_info`): List any medical conditions or illnesses that your child has.
* **Allergies/Medications** (`medication_allergies`): Select all allergies and medications that apply to your child, including "Allergies" and "Medications" options, and specify "Other" if necessary.
* **Camper Medication Info** (`camper_medication_info`): List any medications that your child is currently taking.
* **Camper Health Risk Factors** (`camper_health_risk_factors`): Select all potential health risk factors that apply to your child, including history of recent illness, family history of illness, previous surgery or injury, and others.
* **Camper Travel Info** (`camper_travel_info`): Provide any relevant travel history or information about your child's travel plans.
* **Camper Contact Info** (`camper_contact_info`): Provide your contact information in case of an emergency.
* **Camper Emergency Info** (`camper_emergency_info`): List any emergency contact information for your child.
* **Camper Health Screening** (`camper_health_screening`): Indicate whether your child is cleared for participation in the day camp program or if further evaluation is required.
* **Camper Screening Status** (`camper_screening_status`): Indicate the current status of your child's health screening, whether complete, incomplete, or unknown.
* **Camper Signature** (`camper_signature`): A required field for the parent/guardian to sign, confirming that they have truthfully completed the form.

## Tips

* Ensure you answer all required and relevant questions to provide a comprehensive health screening for your child.
* If unsure about any medical condition or allergy, consult with your healthcare provider before submitting this form.
* Keep your answers accurate and up-to-date to ensure your child's safety and well-being during the camp experience.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid missed sections or incomplete information.
