# IV Access Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IV Access Assessment Form is used to record and track the details of IV access, including the site location, type, and insertion depth, as well as any complications that may have occurred during or after the IV access procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Patient ID** number in the designated field. This number should match the patient's identification number in the hospital's electronic medical record.
2. Enter the **Assessment Date** and **Assessment Time** for the IV access procedure. This will help track when the procedure was performed.
3. Enter the **Patient Name** as it appears on the patient's identification card or as known by the healthcare provider.
4. Identify the **Site Location** where the IV access was performed.
5. Select the type of **IV Access Type** from the options provided, which includes Central Line, Peripheral Line, or Peripherally Inserted Central Catheter.
6. Describe the **IV Access Site Characteristic**, which may include vein type, site description, or ulcers/irritation.
7. Record the **Insertion Depth** of the IV access.
8. Document the **Blood Flow Rate** as measured during the procedure.
9. List any **Complications** that occurred during or after the IV access procedure, which may include bleeding, allergic reaction, or infection.
10. Record the **Medication Administered**, if any, during the procedure.
11. Identify the **Medication Route** and **Medication Dosage** administered.
12. Document the **Medication Frequency** of administration.
13. Record any **Medication Side Effects** observed.
14. Describe the **Patient Monitoring** and **Patient Monitoring Frequency**.
15. Enter the **Removal Date** and **Removal Time**, when the IV access is removed.
16. Explain the **Removal Reason**.
17. Document any **Follow-up** actions, such as further monitoring or follow-up appointments.
18. Add any additional **Comments**, if necessary.
19. Finally, record the name of the healthcare provider who **Submitted** the form and the date it was **Submitted On**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **IV Access Site**: Enter the location where the IV access was performed, such as a vein in the arm or a central line in the neck.
* **Patient ID**: Enter the patient's identification number to ensure that the form is not duplicated.
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date the IV access procedure was performed.
* **Assessment Time**: Enter the time the IV access procedure was performed.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification card or as known by the healthcare provider.
* **Site Location**: Describe the location of the IV access site.
* **IV Access Type**: Select the type of IV access procedure performed.
* **IV Access Site Characteristic**: Describe the IV access site characteristics.
* **Insertion Depth**: Record the depth of insertion of the IV catheter.
* **Blood Flow Rate**: Record the rate of blood flow during the IV procedure.
* **Complications**: List any complications that occurred during or after the IV access procedure.
* **Medication Administered**: Record any medications administered during the IV access procedure.
* **Medication Route**: Identify the route of medication administration.
* **Medication Dosage**: Record the amount of medication administered.
* **Medication Frequency**: Document the frequency of medication administration.
* **Medication Side Effects**: Record any side effects observed after medication administration.
* **Patient Monitoring**: Describe the type of patient monitoring performed.
* **Patient Monitoring Frequency**: Record the frequency of patient monitoring.
* **Removal Date**: Enter the date the IV access is removed.
* **Removal Time**: Enter the time the IV access is removed.
* **Removal Reason**: Explain the reason for removal.
* **Follow-up**: Document any follow-up actions.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments, if necessary.
* **Submitted By**: Record the name of the healthcare provider who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On**: Record the date the form was submitted.

## Tips
* Double-check the patient's identification number to ensure that the form is not duplicated.
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use clear and concise language when describing the IV access site characteristics and complications.
* Document any medication administered and its route and dosage.
* Record the removal reason and date accurately.
* Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.
