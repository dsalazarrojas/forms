# COVID 19 Patient Waitlist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients who are being placed on a waitlist for COVID-19 care. The form helps medical staff to efficiently manage the waitlist and ensure that patients are contacted when an appointment becomes available.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and quickly, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's full name as they want to be identified on the waitlist.
2.  Enter the patient's preferred phone number for communication. We will use this number to contact you when an appointment becomes available.
3.  Enter the patient's email address for additional contact options.
4.  Choose a preferred date and time for the appointment. Select a date within the next 30 days for availability.
5.  Indicate the reason for your visit. Please select the most relevant option.
6.  Choose a priority level for your appointment based on medical urgency or frontline status.
7.  If you have an insurance provider, please enter the name or number for our records.
8.  If there's any additional information or special requests, please provide it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Full Name (Required)**: Enter the patient's full name as they want to be identified on the waitlist.
* **Preferred Phone Number (Required)**: Enter the patient's preferred phone number for communication. We will use this number to contact you when an appointment becomes available.
* **Email Address (Required)**: Enter the patient's email address for additional contact options.
* **Preferred Date for Appointment (Required)**: Choose a date within the next 30 days for availability. Try to pick the date closest to your available time frame.
* **Preferred Time Range (Required)**: Select your preferred time of day for the appointment. Please note that availability may vary based on the selected date.
* **Reason for Visit (Required)**: Indicate the reason for your visit. Please select the most relevant option.
* **Priority Level (Required)**: Choose a priority level for your appointment based on medical urgency or frontline status. If your condition is critical, please contact us directly.
* **Insurance Provider (Required)**: Enter your insurance provider's name or number for our records. If you are uninsured, enter 'N/A'.
* **Additional Notes or Special Requests (Optional)**: If you have any additional information or special requests, please provide them here. This will help us better understand your needs and schedule your appointment accordingly.
* **I understand that being on the waitlist does not guarantee an appointment**: This is a confirmation that you understand the terms of being on the waitlist. Please select 'I agree' to confirm.
