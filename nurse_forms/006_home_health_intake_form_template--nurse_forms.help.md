# Home Health Intake Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for collecting patient information in a home health setting. It is used to gather demographic data, medical history, and other relevant information about the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the patient's first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter the patient's last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Select the date of birth from the calendar.
4. In the **Medical History** field, enter a brief description of the patient's medical history.
5. In the **Medication Allergies** field, select any medications the patient is allergic to. This field can be selected multiple times.
6. Select the patient's medical condition(s) from the **Medical Conditions** field. This field can be selected once.
7. Select the party responsible for providing care, such as Clinician, Caregiver, Family Member, or Other.
8. In the **Home Health Aide License** field, select the status of the home health aide's license.
9. Enter the patient intake form number, if applicable.
10. Sign the **Nurse Signature** field with your name and contact information.
11. Date the form with the current date in the **Date Completed** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Select the patient's date of birth from the calendar.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, optional): Enter a brief description of the patient's medical history.
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, select_multiple, required): Select any medications the patient is allergic to. This field can be selected multiple times.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's medical condition(s). This field can be selected once.
* **Primary Responsible Party** (`primary_responsible_party`, select_one, optional): Select the party responsible for providing care, such as Clinician, Caregiver, Family Member, or Other.
* **Home Health Aide License** (`home_health_aide_license`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the home health aide's license, such as Active, Expired, or None.
* **Patient Intake Form Number** (`patient_intake_form`, number, optional): Enter the patient intake form number, if applicable.
* **Nurse Signature** (`nurse_signature`, text, optional): Sign the form with your name and contact information.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Date the form with the current date.

## Tips

* Ensure that the patient understands the purpose and scope of the form before completing it.
* Complete the form in a private and comfortable setting.
* Enter accurate and complete information.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure it is complete and accurate.
