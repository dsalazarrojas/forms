# Hand Hygiene Education Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your opinions and experiences related to hand hygiene practices and education. Your input will help us better understand the current state of hand hygiene practices and the effectiveness of hand hygiene education.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer the questions based on your experiences and opinions.
2. For text fields, provide detailed answers in the space provided.
3. For select_one questions, choose one option that best reflects your answer.
4. For select_multiple questions, select all options that apply.
5. For number fields, enter the numbers as prompted.
6. Review your answers before submitting the form.

* **Introduction** (Text): A brief introduction to your current hand hygiene practices.
* **hand_hygiene_practices** (Text): Describe your current hand hygiene practices.
* **hand_hygiene_practices_frequency** (Number): How often do you practice hand hygiene?
* **hand_hygiene_practices_duration** (Number): How long do you spend on hand hygiene practices?
* **hand_hygiene_practices_effectiveness** (Select One): How effective do you think hand hygiene practices are?
* **hand_hygiene_practices_effectiveness_rank** (Select Multiple): What are some effective hand hygiene practices?
* **hand_hygiene_education** (Text): Describe your experiences with hand hygiene education.
* **hand_hygiene_education_frequency** (Number): How often do you receive hand hygiene education?
* **hand_hygiene_education_duration** (Number): How long do you spend on hand hygiene education?
* **hand_hygiene_education_effectiveness** (Select One): How effective do you think hand hygiene education is?
* **hand_hygiene_education_effectiveness_rank** (Select Multiple): What are some effective hand hygiene education practices?
* **hand_hygiene_practices_skill** (Number): How skilled do you feel about hand hygiene practices?
* **hand_hygiene_education_skill** (Number): How skilled do you feel about hand hygiene education?
* **hand_hygiene_practices_knowledge** (Text): Describe your knowledge about hand hygiene practices.
* **hand_hygiene_education_knowledge** (Text): Describe your knowledge about hand hygiene education.
* **hand_hygiene_practices_challenge** (Text): What challenges do you face in hand hygiene practices?
* **hand_hygiene_education_challenge** (Text): What challenges do you face in hand hygiene education?
