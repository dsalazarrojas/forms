# Nursing Family Event Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report and request leave of absence due to a family event. The purpose of this form is to inform team members and supervisors about the leave of absence, allowing them to adjust schedules and plans accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of event that is taking place (e.g., Family Event, Other Event).
2. Provide a brief description of the event.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the leave.
4. Enter the reason for leaving.
5. Enter the expected start and end times of the leave.
6. List the team members affected by the leave.
7. Add any comments or additional information about the leave.
8. Select the approval status (e.g., Approved, Denied, Pending).
9. Provide the approver's signature, name, role, email, and phone number.
10. Select the approver's role and department.
11. Enter any additional approver notes.
12. Set the submission date and time.
13. Sign and date the approver's signature.
14. Enter the form submitter's name and title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header**: This is the title of the form and provides a brief introduction to the form.
* **event_type**: Select the type of family event that is taking place (e.g., birth, death, wedding).
* **Event Details**: Provide a brief description of the event.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the leave.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the leave.
* **Reason for Leaving**: Enter a brief explanation for the reason of leaving.
* **Expected Start Time**: Enter the expected start time of the leave.
* **Expected End Time**: Enter the expected end time of the leave.
* **Team Members Affected**: List the team members who will be affected by the leave.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the leave.
* **Approval Status**: Select the approval status of the leave (e.g., Approved, Denied, Pending).
* **Approver's Signature**: Sign and date the approver's signature.
* **Approver Name**: Enter the approver's name.
* **Approver Role**: Select the approver's role (e.g., Supervisor, Manager, Director).
* **Approver Email**: Enter the approver's email address.
* **Approver Phone**: Enter the approver's phone number.
* **Approver Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the approver.
* **Submission Date**: Set the submission date and time.
* **Approver Signature Date**: Sign and date the approver's signature.
* **Form Submitter**: Enter the form submitter's name.
* **Approver Title**: Enter the approver's title.
* **Approver Department**: Enter the approver's department.
* **Designation**: Select the approver's designation (e.g., Manager, Team Lead, Director).
* **Other (Specify)**: If the approver's designation is not listed, enter a specific designation.
* **Submission Status**: Select the submission status (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected, In Progress).
