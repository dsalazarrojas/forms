# Nurse Practitioner Preceptor Commitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the preceptorship program for a nurse practitioner, including rotation details, student information, and preceptor's involvement. The form helps track and manage the progress of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the rotation details: Provide information about the rotation, including the length, start and end dates, and any notes.
2. Enter student information: Provide the student's name, NPI, and rotation location.
3. Identify the rotation supervisor and preceptor: Enter their names and any other relevant details.
4. Select the rotation status: Choose from the options provided (Active, Completed, Cancelled).
5. Add any additional notes: Use this field to provide any further comments or updates about the rotation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rotation Details (1)**: This field is where you can enter information about the rotation, including its length, start and end dates. This information will help track the progress of the program.
* **Rotation Length (2)**: Enter the length of the rotation, in terms of months or other unit of time.
* **Rotation Start Date (3)**: Enter the start date of the rotation.
* **Rotation End Date (4)**: Enter the end date of the rotation.
* **Student Details (5)**: This field is where you can enter information about the student, including their name and any other relevant details.
* **Student Name (6)**: Enter the name of the student.
* **Student NPI (7)**: Enter the student's National Provider Identifier (NPI).
* **Rotation Location (8)**: Enter the location of the rotation site.
* **Rotation Site (9)**: Enter the site where the rotation will take place.
* **Rotation Supervisor (10)**: Enter the name of the rotation supervisor.
* **Rotation Preceptor (11)**: Enter the name of the preceptor.
* **Rotation Status (12)**: Choose from the options provided (Active, Completed, Cancelled) to indicate the current status of the rotation.
* **Rotation Notes (13)**: Use this field to enter any additional comments or updates about the rotation.
* **Rotation Agreement (14)**: This field is currently not utilized as a field, but it could potentially be used for entering a date of agreement related to the rotation.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about the rotation to ensure smooth communication and progress tracking.
* Review and update the rotation status regularly to reflect any changes.
* Use the notes field to capture any additional comments or updates about the rotation.
