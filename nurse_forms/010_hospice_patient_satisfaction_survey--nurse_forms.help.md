# Hospice Patient Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospice Patient Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect feedback from patients who have received care from our hospice program. This survey aims to evaluate the quality of care, communication, and overall experience of our patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, please fill out the form with your personal information, including your name and contact number.
2.  Rate the quality of care you received from our hospice team using the scale provided (1-5).
3.  Rate the quality of communication from our hospice team using the scale provided (1-5).
4.  If you have experienced any concerns or issues with your care team, please select the relevant option.
5.  If you have experienced any concerns or issues with the services provided, select the relevant option.
6.  If you would recommend our hospice program to other patients, select the relevant option.
7.  Add any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
8.  If you would like to send your survey results to your email, select the relevant option.
9.  Choose the email address you want to send your survey to.
10.  Select the attachment option to include any supporting documentation with your email.
11.  If you would like to reply to the email, select the relevant option.
12.  Choose the attachment option if you need to include any supporting documentation with your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number for follow-up or communication purposes.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication and follow-up.
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select the date you received care from our hospice program.
*   **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Select the time you received care from our hospice program.
*   **Hospice ID** (`hospice_id`, `text`, required): Enter the ID number assigned to you by our hospice program.
*   **How would you rate the quality of care you received from our hospice team?** (`question_1`, `select_multiple`, required): Rate the quality of care on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
*   **How would you rate the quality of communication from our hospice team?** (`question_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Rate the quality of communication on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
*   **Have you experienced any concerns or issues with your care team?** (`question_3`, `select_multiple`, required): Select yes or no if you experienced any concerns or issues with your care team.
*   **Have you experienced any concerns or issues with the services provided?** (`question_4`, `select_multiple`, required): Select yes or no if you experienced any concerns or issues with the services provided.
*   **Would you recommend this hospice program to other patients?** (`question_5`, `select_one`, required): Select yes or no if you would recommend our hospice program to other patients.
*   **Additional comments** (`note`, `note`, required): Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
*   **Send to email** (`email_sent`, `select_one`, required): Select yes or no if you want to send your survey results to your email.
*   **Email address** (`email_address`, `text`, required): Enter the email address you want to send your survey to.
*   **Sender** (`sender`, `text`, required): Enter the sender's name or email address.
*   **Cc** (`cc`, `text`, required): Enter any email addresses to include in the Cc field.
*   **Bcc** (`bcc`, `text`, required): Enter any email addresses to include in the Bcc field.
*   **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, required): Enter the subject of the email you want to send.
*   **Body** (`body`, `text`, required): Enter the body of the email you want to send.
*   **Send** (`send`, `select_one`, required): Select yes or no if you want to send your email.
*   **Attachment** (`attachment`, `select_one`, required): Select yes or no if you want to include an attachment with your email.
*   **Reply** (`reply`, `select_one`, required): Select yes or no if you want to reply to the email.
*   **Clear** (`clear`, `select_one`, required): Select yes or no if you want to clear the form and start again.

Note: The required fields are marked as required. Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
