# Hospice Discharge Summary Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Hospice Discharge Summary Form is designed to provide a detailed overview of a patient's hospice care, including medical conditions, vital signs, treatment outcomes, and other important information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's basic contact information, including name, date of birth, and address.
2. **Medical Conditions**: Describe the patient's current medical conditions, including any relevant diagnoses, symptoms, or treatments.
3. **Vital Signs**: Record the patient's current vital signs, such as weight, height, blood pressure, and other relevant metrics.
4. **Treatment Outcomes**: Describe the patient's treatment outcomes, including any successes or challenges.
5. **Hospice Care**: Select the type of hospice care the patient received, from the options provided.
6. **Physician Info**: Enter the name and contact information of the attending physician or healthcare provider.
7. **Discharge Date**: Enter the date of the patient's discharge from the hospice care program.
8. **Time of Discharge**: Record the time of the patient's discharge.
9. **Discharge Reason**: Describe the reason for the patient's discharge from the program.
10. **Hospice ID**: Select the ID number(s) associated with the patient's hospice care.
11. **Patient Status**: Select the patient's status, from the options provided.
12. **Medication**: Enter any medications the patient is currently taking or was prescribed.
13. **Patient Family Member**: Enter any relevant information about the patient's family member(s).
14. **Hospice Care Plan**: Enter any additional information about the patient's care plan.
