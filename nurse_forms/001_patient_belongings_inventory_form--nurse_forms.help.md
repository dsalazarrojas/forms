# Patient Belongings Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track patient belongings, including descriptions, types, and locations. It helps maintain accurate and up-to-date records of patient property for efficient organization and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Select the patient you are reporting on**: Enter the patient's ID number to associate the form with the correct patient.
2. **Enter patient's name**: Provide the patient's name to ensure accurate identification.
3. **Describe the belongings**: Enter a brief description of the patient's belongings.
4. **Specify the type of belongings**: Choose the category of belongings from the provided options.
5. **Select the location**: Choose the room where the patient's belongings were found.
6. **Record the discovery date and time**: Enter the date and time when the belongings were discovered.
7. **Add any additional notes**: Provide any relevant comments or observations about the patient's belongings.
8. **Enter email address**: Enter the patient's email address for future contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_id** (Number, Optional): Enter the patient's ID number associated with their medical records.
* **patient_name** (Text, Required): Provide the patient's name for accurate identification.
* **belongings_description** (Text, Optional): Briefly describe the patient's belongings in this field.
* **belongings_type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the category of belongings from the options provided.
* **patient_room** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the rooms where the patient's belongings were found.
* **date_found** (Date, Optional): Record the date when the belongings were discovered.
* **time_found** (Time, Optional): Record the time when the belongings were discovered.
* **note** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the patient's belongings.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter the patient's email address for future contact.
