# Mpox Vaccine Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mpox Vaccine Sign Up Form is a collection of fields that gather information from individuals to schedule appointments for the Mpox vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use a valid email address that you check regularly.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
6. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
7. Select your city in the "City" field.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "State" field to indicate whether you have a medical record ID or not.
9. Enter your medical record ID in the "Medical Record ID" field, if applicable.
10. Select the type of vaccine you are signing up for in the "Vaccine Type" field.
11. Enter the date of your next appointment in the "Next Appointment Date" field in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
12. Select the time of your appointment in the "Appointment Time" field in the format 12:00 AM - 11:59 PM.
13. Select your preferred time slots for the appointment in the "Preferred Time Slots" field.
14. Provide any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter your date of birth in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Address** (text): Enter your address.
* **City** (text): Enter your city.
* **State** (select_one): Select "Yes" if you have a medical record ID, otherwise select "No".
* **Zip Code** (number): Enter your zip code.
* **Medical Record ID** (text): Enter your medical record ID, if applicable.
* **Vaccine Type** (select_multiple): Select the type of vaccine you are signing up for.
* **Next Appointment Date** (date): Enter the date of your next appointment in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Appointment Time** (time): Select the time of your appointment in the format 12:00 AM - 11:59 PM.
* **Preferred Time Slots** (select_multiple): Select your preferred time slots for the appointment.
* **Medical History** (note): Provide any relevant medical history.

Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as "required" in the form definition.
