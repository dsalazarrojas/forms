# Infant Daily Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by healthcare professionals to document and report on the daily activities and events related to an infant's care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the intake date and infant ID number.
2. Record all feedings with their corresponding times and quantities.
3. Enter all feeding times with a brief description.
4. Report any medication administered or medical conditions requiring attention.
5. Record the infant's vital signs and any observations or notes.
6. Enter any diaper changes, sleep events, and developmental stage (if applicable).
7. Document any medical conditions, action plans, and staff responsibilities.
8. Include any additional notes or comments.
9. Complete the form by selecting the submitter, submit date, and reviewer.
10. Review and sign off on the form by selecting the reviewer and review date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intake Date** (`intake_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of the infant's intake.
* **Infant ID** (`infant_id`, Number, Required): Enter the unique identifier of the infant.
* **Feeding Schedule** (`feeding_schedule`, Text, Required): Enter all feedings with time and quantity.
* **Feeding Times** (`feeding_times`, Text, Required): Enter all feeding times.
* **Feeding Amount** (`feeding_amount`, Number, Required): Enter feeding amount.
* **Medication** (`medication`, Text, Required): Enter any medication administered.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, Text, Required): Enter the infant's vital signs.
* **Diaper Changing** (`diaper_changing`, Text, Required): Enter any diaper changes.
* **Sleep Schedule** (`sleep_schedule`, Text, Required): Enter all sleep events with time and duration.
* **Developmental Stage** (`developmental_stage`, Select One, Required): Select the infant's developmental stage.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, Text, Required): Enter any medical conditions.
* **Observations** (`observations`, Note, Required): Enter any observations or notes.
* **Action Plan** (`action_plan`, Text, Required): Document any action plans for the infant's care.
* **Staff Responsibility** (`staff_responsibility`, Select Multiple, Required): Select any staff responsibilities for the infant's care.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Text, Required): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Signature** (`signature`, Date, Required): Enter the date of the signature.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, Select One, Required): Select the person submitting the form.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, Select One, Required): Select the person submitting the form.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, Select One, Required): Select the person reviewing the form.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of review.
* **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, Select One, Required): Select the person reviewing the form.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly, and that any errors or discrepancies are addressed before submitting the form. If you are unsure about any field or process, please refer to your healthcare professional or contact the relevant authority for guidance.
