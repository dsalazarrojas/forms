# Student Medical Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical information about students, including their medical history, allergies, medications, contact information, emergency contact, medical conditions, medical treatment, medical procedure, and student signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you have the necessary information to fill it out.
2. Fill out the form using the provided fields, taking care to select relevant options and provide accurate information.
3. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and clearance to complete this form.
4. Review your completed form for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Medical Information** (`student_medical_information_form`, text, required: false): This field is for providing a brief description of the student's medical condition or history.
	+ This field should be completed with a clear and concise description of the student's medical condition or history.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): This field is for providing a detailed account of the student's medical history.
	+ This field should be completed with a detailed and accurate account of the student's medical history.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for listing the student's allergies.
	+ You can select multiple options from the dropdown menu to indicate the student's allergies.
* **Medical Assessments** (`medical_assessments`, date, required: false): This field is for recording the student's medical assessments or appointments.
	+ You should enter the date of the medical assessment or appointment.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for listing the student's medications.
	+ You can select multiple options from the dropdown menu to indicate the student's medications.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, email, required: false): This field is for providing the student's contact information.
	+ You should enter the student's email address.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for listing the student's emergency contact information.
	+ You can select multiple options from the dropdown menu to indicate the student's emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_one, required: false): This field is for selecting the student's medical conditions.
	+ You should select one option from the dropdown menu that best describes the student's medical condition.
* **Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment`, note, required: false): This field is for providing any additional medical treatment or procedures.
	+ You can enter any additional medical treatment or procedures in this field.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, time, required: false): This field is for recording the duration of the student's medical procedure.
	+ You should enter the duration of the student's medical procedure.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, select_one, required: false): This field is for obtaining the student's signature.
	+ You should select one option from the dropdown menu to indicate whether the student has signed and reviewed the form.

## Tips

* Please review each field carefully and ensure you have the necessary information to fill it out.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and clearance to complete this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant authorities or experts.
* Please be accurate and honest when completing this form.
