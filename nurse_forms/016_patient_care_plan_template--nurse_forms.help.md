# Patient Care Plan Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Care Plan Template is designed to help healthcare professionals create a comprehensive care plan for each patient. This form is intended to gather essential information about the patient's medical conditions, care needs, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the patient's medical records and history to ensure that all relevant information is captured.
2.  Fill out the form with the patient's information, including medical conditions, care needs, and other relevant details.
3.  Use the form as a starting point for creating a comprehensive care plan that takes into account the patient's individual needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Care Plan Template** (`patient_care_plan_template`, `text`, required): This field is the main section of the form where healthcare professionals can write a comprehensive care plan for the patient.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's medical conditions, including any allergies, medications, and medical history.
*   **Care Needs** (`care_needs`, `note`, required): This field is for healthcare professionals to outline the patient's care needs, including any specific requirements or concerns.
*   **Medical Conditions 0-6** (`medical_conditions_0`, `text`, required): These fields are for listing the patient's medical conditions, with each field representing a separate condition.

Tips:
*   Make sure to review the patient's medical records and history before filling out the form.
*   Use the form as a starting point for creating a comprehensive care plan that takes into account the patient's individual needs.
*   Consult with other healthcare professionals, including doctors and specialists, to ensure that the care plan is accurate and effective.
*   Keep the care plan up-to-date and revise as needed based on changes in the patient's condition.
