# Nasal Flu Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from patients for the administration of the Nasal Flu Vaccine. The form ensures that patients understand the risks and benefits associated with the vaccine and have given their consent to receive it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete this form by providing the required information in each field. If a field is optional, you can skip it.
2. Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure that you have provided accurate and complete information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask a healthcare professional for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, optional): This field is used to gather patient information such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the consent options that apply to you.
* **Vaccine Details** (`vaccine_details`, select_one, optional): Select the vaccine details that apply to your condition.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This field is used to document any medical history that may affect your vaccination. Please provide any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or medications.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Please sign this form to confirm that you have reviewed and understood the information provided.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Please enter the date on which you signed this form.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, optional): Please enter the time on which you signed this form.
* **Nurse Information** (`nurse_information`, text, optional): This field is used to document the name and contact details of the nurse who administered the vaccine.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, optional): Please sign this form to confirm that you have received the vaccine and understand the consent.
* **Nurse Signature** (`nurse_signature`, note, optional): Please sign this form to confirm that you have administered the vaccine to the patient and have reviewed the consent.
* **Printed By** (`printed_by`, text, optional): This field is used to document the name and title of the person who printed the form.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before signing it.
* Make sure to keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please ask a healthcare professional for assistance.
