# Pre Camp Health Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Camp Health Screening Form is designed to collect health information from campers before their arrival at the camp. This form helps the camp staff assess the camper's health status and take necessary precautions to ensure their safety and well-being during the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Camper Info section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Select any symptoms you are currently experiencing from the Symptoms section.
3. Answer the Travel History question with a "True" or "False" depending on whether you have traveled recently.
4. Answer the Contact History question with a "True" or "False" depending on whether you have had contact with anyone with a contagious illness.
5. Enter the date of your last health check-up or when you first experienced any current symptoms in the Health Status section.
6. You may add any additional notes in the Note section, if necessary.
7. Optionally, provide your email address and phone number in the Email and Phone sections, which will be used for contact purposes only.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Info**: This section is for you to provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details that we need to know about you.
* **Symptoms**: Please select all the symptoms you are currently experiencing from the list provided. If you are not feeling well, it is essential to let us know so we can provide appropriate care.
* **Travel History**: Answer "True" if you have traveled recently, which may affect your risk of exposure to illness. If you have not traveled recently, answer "False".
* **Contact History**: Answer "True" if you have had contact with anyone with a contagious illness, such as COVID-19. If you have not had contact with anyone with a contagious illness, answer "False".
* **Health Status**: Enter the date of your last health check-up or when you first experienced any current symptoms. This will help us keep track of your health status.
* **Note**: This is an optional section where you can add any additional notes about your health or any concerns you have.
* **Email**: If you would like us to contact you via email, please provide your email address. We will use it only for camp purposes.
* **Phone**: If you would like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number. We will use it only for camp purposes.
