# osce_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess a student's performance in a clinical setting. It provides a way for the evaluator to give feedback and rate the student's clinical skills, communication skills, problem-solving abilities, and other competencies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the student's performance during the clinical rotation.
2. Complete each field in the form according to your observations and reviews.
3. Ensure that you have answered all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): A brief introduction to the form. Please provide a short statement of your observations and the purpose of the form.
* **Student ID** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Patient Condition** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Describe the patient's condition as observed during the clinical rotation.
* **Clinical Skills** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the area where you observed the student to be proficient in, such as:
	+ Airway
	+ Breath sounds
	+ Vital signs
	+ Patient assessment
	+ Pharmacological knowledge
	+ Pharmacological application
	+ Basic physical examination
	+ Advanced physical examination
	+ Diseases
	+ Pharmacological management
* **Patient Care Plan** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Describe the care plan you observed the student to be using.
* **Communication Skills** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Assess the student's communication skills, such as their ability to explain complex medical concepts to patients.
* **Problem Solving** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Describe the student's problem-solving abilities, such as their ability to identify and address patient care issues.
* **Assessment** (`8`, `time`, required: false): Estimate the time spent assessing the student's performance.
* **Total Score** (`9`, `number`, required: false): Enter a total score for the student's performance. This field is optional.
* **Evaluator Rating** (`10`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the student's performance as:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Evaluator Comments** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the student's performance.
* **Evaluator Name** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and title as the evaluator.
* **Evaluator Department** (`13`, `text`, required: false): Enter your department as the evaluator.

## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible in your answers.
* Ensure that you have answered all required fields.
* Review and revise your answers before submitting the form.
* This form is meant to be used by clinical evaluators and assessors who have observed or reviewed the student's work.
