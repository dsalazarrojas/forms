# Patient Handover Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Handover Form is designed to gather essential information about a patient for transfer between healthcare professionals. This form helps ensure that the patient's condition, treatment, and medical history are accurately handed over for the best possible care.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Enter the medical staff member's name in the **Medical Staff Name** field.
3. Provide the time of transfer in the **Transfer Time** field.
4. Enter the date of transfer in the **Transfer Date** field.
5. Briefly describe the patient's current condition, any ongoing medication, allergies, and other relevant medical history in the corresponding fields.
6. Document any vital signs (e.g., temperature, blood pressure, oxygen saturation) in the **Vital Signs** field.
7. Fill in any other medical history information relevant to the patient's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Medical Staff Name** (`medical_staff_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare professional transferring the patient.
* **Transfer Time** (`transfer_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of transfer.
* **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of transfer.
* **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`, text, optional): Briefly describe the patient's current medical condition.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): List any medication the patient is currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): Document any allergies the patient has.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, text, optional): Record the patient's vital signs (e.g., temperature, blood pressure).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical history information.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, text, optional): Enter the patient's current status (e.g., stable, unstable).

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- Verify that the form is signed and dated by the transferring healthcare professional.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please consult with your supervisor or medical director.
- If you have any questions or suggestions for improving this form, please contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
