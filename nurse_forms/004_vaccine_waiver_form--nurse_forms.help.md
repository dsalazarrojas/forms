# Vaccine Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a patient's waiver of a vaccine, ensuring that they have provided informed consent and that the doctor has taken necessary steps to assess risks associated with the vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2.  Enter the patient's address in the **Patient Address** field.
3.  Enter the doctor's name in the **Doctor Name** field.
4.  Provide the date of the patient's refusal in the **Date of Refusal** field.
5.  Enter the reason for the patient's refusal in the **Reason for Refusal** field.
6.  Enter the patient's contact information in the **Contact Info** field.
7.  Sign and date the form with the doctor's signature in the **Doctor Signature** field.
8.  Sign and date the form with the patient's signature in the **Patient Signature** field.
9.  Select the patient's level of awareness of the consequences of the refusal in the **Patient Aware of Consequences** field.
10.  If applicable, select whether the doctor conducted a risk assessment in the **Doctor Conducted Risk Assessment** field.
11.  If applicable, select whether the doctor conducted a medical history in the **Doctor Conducted Medical History** field.
12.  Select whether the patient understands the consequences of their refusal in the **Patient Understands Consequences** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Patient Name** (required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
*  **Patient Address** (required): Enter the patient's address or contact information.
*  **Doctor Name** (required): Enter your full name as a medical professional.
*  **Date of Refusal** (required): Enter the date the patient refused the vaccine.
*  **Reason for Refusal** (required): Enter the reasons why the patient refused the vaccine.
*  **Contact Info** (required): Enter the patient's contact information, such as phone number or email, in case of any questions or concerns.
*  **Doctor Signature** (required): Sign and date the form to confirm that you, as the doctor, have spoken with the patient and understand their refusal.
*  **Patient Signature** (required): Sign and date the form to confirm that the patient understands their refusal and the consequences.
*  **Date of Signature** (required): Enter the date you signed the form with the patient.
*  **Patient Aware of Consequences** (required): Select whether the patient is fully aware, somewhat aware, or not aware of the consequences of their refusal.
*  **Doctor Conducted Risk Assessment** (optional): If the risk assessment was conducted, select "Yes" to indicate that you, as the doctor, have assessed potential risks associated with the vaccine.
*  **Doctor Conducted Medical History** (optional): If a medical history was conducted, select "Yes" to indicate that you, as the doctor, have reviewed the patient's medical history.
*  **Patient Understands Consequences** (required): Select whether the patient understands the consequences of their refusal.
