# Nurses Direct Home Visit E Slip - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Nurses Direct Home Visit E Slip" form is a crucial document that provides essential information about patients undergoing a nurse's direct home visit. It's used to gather details such as contact information, medical history, and allergies to ensure effective care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Review and complete each field as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification or medical records.
- **Patient Dob** (`patient_dob`, date, optional): Record the patient's date of birth (if applicable).
- **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number for communication purposes.
- **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Provide the patient's email address for contact and communication.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Summarize the patient's medical history, including previous conditions, treatments, and surgeries.
- **Medication List** (`medication_list`, text, optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
- **Allergy List** (`allergy_list`, text, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has, including severity and treatment.

## Tips
- Be thorough but concise when completing each field.
- If you're unsure about any information, consult with the patient or their medical records.
- Update the form as necessary to reflect any changes in patient information.
