# Mpox Vaccine Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from patients for the Mpox vaccine registration process. It's intended to gather essential details about the patient's user information, appointment schedule, contact details, medical history, and vaccine administration specifics.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your personal details in the "User Information" section.
2. Enter your appointment schedule in the "Appointment Information" section.
3. Fill in your contact details in the "Contact Information" section.
4. Share your medical history in the "Medical Information" section.
5. List any medical conditions you are aware of in the "Medical Conditions" section.
6. Provide details about the vaccine you are scheduled to receive in the "Vaccine Details" section.
7. Indicate your preferences for appointment scheduling in the "Appointment Preferences" section.
8. Sign and agree to the terms and conditions in the "Agreement" section.
9. Add any additional comments or questions in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (Page 1): Enter your name, email, and other relevant personal details. This section is not required but will help us get in touch with you.
* **Appointment Information** (Page 2): Provide your appointment schedule, including date, time, and location.
* **Contact Information** (Page 3): Fill in your contact details, such as phone numbers and addresses.
* **Medical Information** (Page 4): Share your medical history, including allergies, medications, and previous medical conditions.
* **Medical Conditions** (Page 5): List any existing medical conditions that may affect your treatment.
* **Vaccine Details** (Page 6): Enter the specifics of the vaccine you are scheduled to receive, including dosage and administration instructions.
* **Appointment Preferences** (Page 7): Indicate your preferences for appointment scheduling, such as preferred dates and times.
* **Agreement** (Page 8): Sign and agree to the terms and conditions of the vaccine administration.
* **Agreement Details** (Page 9): Provide any additional details about your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 10): Add any extra comments or questions you may have.
* **Submit** (Button): Once you've completed all sections, click this button to submit your form.

* **Delete Draft** (Button): Delete your saved draft.
* **View Draft** (Button): View your saved draft.
* **Back To Start** (Button): Return to the beginning of the form.
* **Save and Print** (Button): Save and print your completed form.
* **Back** (Button): Return to the previous page.
* **Close** (Button): Close the form without saving changes.
* **Save** (Button): Save your completed form without submitting it.
* **Cancel** (Button): Cancel your form and start again from the beginning.
