# Nurse Practitioner Practice Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture the details and agreements of a nurse practitioner's practice. It's meant to be a helpful tool for the nurse to document their practice, agreements, and collaboration details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page. You will be asked for information on collaboration details, signature, nurse practice agreement, effective date, and collaboration start date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Collaboration Details** (`collaboration_details`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide details about your collaboration. Please type in any relevant information about your practice, including any agreements or terms that you've discussed with others.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): This is a field for your signature. Please sign this form to indicate that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions of this practice agreement.
* **Nurse Practice Agreement** (`nurse_practice_agreement`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can describe your practice agreement. Be specific about what you agree to do and not do.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, optional): This field is for the start and end dates of your practice. Please select or enter the dates when your practice becomes effective.
* **Collaboration Start Date** (`collaboration_start_date`, date, optional): This field is for the start date of your collaboration. Enter the date when your collaboration begins.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and agree to the terms and conditions of this practice agreement.
* Enter accurate and clear information in each field.
* Sign and date the form once you have reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions.
