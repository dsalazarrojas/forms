# Covid 19 Mandatory Daily Health Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Mandatory Daily Health Screening Questionnaire is a form designed to gather essential information from patients about their health status, travel history, and potential exposure to COVID-19. This form is used to screen for symptoms and potential risks associated with COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Start by providing your **patient_name**, which is your personal name.
2.  Select the date of the screening from the calendar provided for **screening_date**.
3.  Answer the questions regarding your current symptoms:
   *   If you have a fever, select **Fever**. If you don't have a fever, select **None** for **have_fever**.
   *   If you have a cough, select **Cough** for **have_cough**. If you don't have a cough, select **None**.
   *   If you experience **Shortness of breath**, select **Shortness of breath** for **have_shortness_of_breath**. If you don't have shortness of breath, select **None**.
   *   If you have **Diarrhea**, select **Diarrhea** for **have_diarrhea**. If you don't have diarrhea, select **None**.
   *   If you have **Nausea**, select **Nausea** for **have_nausea**. If you don't have nausea, select **None**.
4.  Provide your travel history for **travel_history**. This is where you report any recent travel or international travel.
5.  Provide your exposure history for **exposure_history**. This is where you report any potential exposure to COVID-19, such as close contact with an infected individual or sharing utensils with an infected individual.
6.  Select any symptoms you are currently experiencing for **have_symptoms**. These include Fever, Cough, Shortness of breath, Diarrhea, and Nausea.
7.  If you have been experiencing symptoms, provide the start and end dates for **symptoms_start_date** and **symptoms_end_date**, respectively.
8.  Enter the name of your healthcare provider for **healthcare_provider_name**.
9.  Enter the department of your healthcare provider for **healthcare_provider_department**.
10. Enter your healthcare provider's ID for **healthcare_provider_id**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **patient_name**: Your full personal name.
*   **screening_date**: Select the date of the screening from the calendar provided. This is when you will be completing the form.
*   **have_fever**: Report if you have a fever or not. A fever is a high temperature.
*   **have_cough**: Report if you have a cough or not. This includes any coughing or respiratory symptoms.
*   **have_shortness_of_breath**: Report if you experience shortness of breath or not.
*   **have_diarrhea**: Report if you have diarrhea or not.
*   **have_nausea**: Report if you experience nausea or vomiting or not.
*   **travel_history**: Report any recent travel or international travel.
*   **exposure_history**: Report any potential exposure to COVID-19, such as close contact with an infected individual or sharing utensils with an infected individual.
*   **have_symptoms**: Select any symptoms you are currently experiencing, which include Fever, Cough, Shortness of breath, Diarrhea, and Nausea.
*   **symptoms_start_date**: If you have symptoms, report the start date of the symptoms.
*   **symptoms_end_date**: If you have symptoms, report the end date of the symptoms.
*   **healthcare_provider_name**: Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
*   **healthcare_provider_department**: Enter the department of your healthcare provider.
*   **healthcare_provider_id**: Enter your healthcare provider's ID.
