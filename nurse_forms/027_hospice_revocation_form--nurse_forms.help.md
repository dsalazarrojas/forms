# Hospice Revocation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to revoke a patient's hospice care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the **Date Of Birth** field. This should be entered in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY) to ensure accurate sorting and searching.
3. Enter the **Medical Certificate Number**, which is an identifier provided by the certifying physician or the state.
4. Enter the **Facility ID**, which is the unique identifier for the facility where the patient is receiving care.
5. Enter the date the hospice care should be revoked in the **Revocation Date** field.
6. Enter a brief explanation for revoking the patient's hospice care in the **Reason For Revocation** field.
7. The practitioner should sign and date the form in the **Practitioner Signature** field.
8. The patient should sign the form in the **Patient Signature** field, indicating their understanding and agreement with the form.
9. Select the correct option from the drop-down menu in the **Submit** field to complete the form.
10. Enter the name of the facility in the **Facility Name** field.
11. Enter the phone number of the facility in the **Facility Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient whose hospice care is to be revoked.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Medical Certificate Number**: Enter the identifying number provided by the certifying physician or the state.
* **Facility ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the facility where the patient is receiving care.
* **Revocation Date**: Enter the date the hospice care should be revoked.
* **Reason For Revocation**: Briefly explain the reason for revoking the patient's hospice care.
* **Practitioner Name**: The name of the healthcare practitioner signing the form.
* **Practitioner Signature**: Sign and date the form, indicating your approval of the patient's revocation.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign the form, indicating your understanding and agreement with the revocation of your hospice care.
* **Submit**: Select **Revokes the hospice of the patient** to complete the form.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility where you are submitting the form from.
* **Facility Phone**: Enter the phone number of the facility submitting the form.

## Tips
* Be sure to review the patient's medical history and current care plan before completing this form.
* Ensure that you have discussed the revocation with the patient and their family or caregivers.
* Complete the form in its entirety before submitting it to the patient's healthcare provider.
