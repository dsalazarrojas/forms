# Nursing Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals applying to participate in a nursing program. It captures personal details, contact information, education history, professional certifications, a personal statement, and references.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Personal Details section with your relevant personal information.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Information section.
3. Describe your education history in the Education History section.
4. Select your relevant professional certifications in the Professional Certifications section.
5. Write a clear and concise personal statement in the Personal Statement section.
6. List your references in the References section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, `text`, required): Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Enter your address, email, and phone number to ensure we can contact you about your application.
* **Education History** (`education_history`, `text`, required): Describe your educational background, including institutions attended and degrees earned.
* **Professional Certifications** (`professional_certifications`, `select_one`, required): Select one or more relevant certifications you hold, such as Certified LPN or Certified RN.
* **Personal Statement** (`personal_statement`, `text`, required): Write a clear and concise statement outlining your motivation for participating in the nursing program and your qualifications.
* **References** (`references`, `select_multiple`, required): List two or three professional references, providing their name, job title, and contact information.
