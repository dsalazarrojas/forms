# Nurse Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Nurse Onboarding Form is a crucial step in the onboarding process for new nurses. It helps us collect vital information about each nurse, ensuring they're equipped to provide the best possible care to our patients.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Share your Credentials or Qualifications in the "Credentials" field.
6. Read and confirm your understanding of our organization's policies and guidelines in the "Acknowledgement" field.
7. Choose to verify or not verify your Credentials in the "Verify Credentials" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Credentials**: Share any relevant credentials, qualifications, or certifications you have.
* **Acknowledgement**: Read and confirm your understanding of our organization's policies and guidelines.
* **Verify Credentials**: Choose "Verified" to confirm you've provided accurate Credentials or "Not Verified" if you're unsure or have not provided any Credentials.

## Tips

* Double-check your details before submitting the form.
* Ensure your Credentials are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you need any clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
