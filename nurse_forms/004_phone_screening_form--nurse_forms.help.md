# phone_screening_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The phone screening form is designed to collect essential information about candidates or individuals, including contact details, work history, and emergency contacts. This form helps in the initial screening process to gather relevant details before further evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with the required information, making sure to provide accurate and up-to-date details.
2. Review each field carefully and provide any supporting documentation if necessary.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Info** (`candidate_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the candidate or individual being screened.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide the candidate's contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, optional): Mention the candidate's availability for work, such as their schedule or time zone.
* **Availability Time** (`availability_time`, text, optional): Specify the candidate's availability time, if applicable.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): List any relevant skills or qualifications the candidate possesses.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, text, optional): Specify the relationship between the candidate and the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, spouse, etc.).
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical information about the candidate.
* **Work History** (`work_history`, text, optional): Describe the candidate's work history, including previous job titles, dates of employment, and relevant experience.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure a smooth and efficient screening process.
* Use clear and concise language when providing information.
* If you're unsure about any field, reach out to the form administrator for guidance.
