# CNA Unit Completion Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used by nurses (CNAs) to report their completion of a unit shift, including the number of patients assigned, tasks completed, and any incidents or accidents that occurred during the shift. The form is completed by the nurse at the end of their shift and submitted to their supervisor for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter your CNA Name**: Type your name as it appears on your ID badge.
2. **Enter your Employee ID**: Type your Employee ID number as assigned by your organization.
3. **Enter Shift Date**: Select the date of your shift from the calendar.
4. **Select Shift Time Period**: Choose the time period of your shift from the options provided (e.g. Day Shift 6am-2pm).
5. **Select Unit or Department Assigned**: Choose the unit or department where you worked during your shift from the options provided.
6. **Enter Number of Patients Assigned**: Type the number of patients assigned to you during your shift.
7. **Select Patient Care Tasks Completed**: Choose all tasks that apply to the patients you cared for (e.g. vital signs monitoring, patient hygiene assistance).
8. **Select Any Incidents or Accidents Occurred During Shift**: Choose 'Yes' if any incidents or accidents occurred during your shift.
9. **Describe Incident**: If you answered yes to the previous question, provide a brief description of the incident or accident.
10. **Report Incident to Supervisor**: Indicate if you reported the incident to your supervisor.
11. **Enter Equipment or Facility Issues Encountered**: If any equipment or facility issues occurred during your shift, describe them in the provided text field.
12. **Select Staffing Levels Were Adequate**: Choose how you rate the staffing levels during your shift (e.g. Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
13. **Select Shift Completion Status**: Choose how you completed your shift (e.g. Completed full shift, Left early - authorized, Left early - emergency, Incomplete).
14. **Enter Supervisor Name**: Type the name of your supervisor who reviewed and approved this form.
15. **Enter Supervisor Review Date**: Type the date your supervisor reviewed and approved this form.
16. **Enter Additional Notes or Feedback**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, enter them in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **CNA Name**: Your name as it appears on your ID badge.
* **Employee ID**: Your Employee ID number as assigned by your organization.
* **Shift Date**: The date of your shift.
* **Shift Time Period**: The time period of your shift.
* **Unit or Department Assigned**: The unit or department where you worked during your shift.
* **Number of Patients Assigned**: The number of patients assigned to you during your shift.
* **Patient Care Tasks Completed**: The tasks you completed for the patients you cared for (e.g. vital signs monitoring, patient hygiene assistance).
* **Any Incidents or Accidents Occurred During Shift**: If any incidents or accidents occurred during your shift.
* **Incident Description**: A brief description of the incident or accident.
* **Incident Reported to Supervisor**: If you reported the incident to your supervisor.
* **Equipment or Facility Issues Encountered**: Any equipment or facility issues that occurred during your shift.
* **Staffing Levels Were Adequate**: How you rate the staffing levels during your shift.
* **Shift Completion Status**: How you completed your shift.
* **Supervisor Name**: The name of your supervisor who reviewed and approved this form.
* **Supervisor Review Date**: The date your supervisor reviewed and approved this form.
* **Additional Notes or Feedback**: Any additional comments or feedback you have.

Note: This form is for reporting unit shifts completed by CNAs.
