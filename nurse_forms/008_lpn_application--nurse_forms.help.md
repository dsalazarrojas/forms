# LPN Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN) applicants for potential employment or other purposes in a healthcare setting. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to help us review your application efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure a smooth and efficient application process, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Address Line 1**, **Address Line 2**, **City**, **State**, and **Zip Code** sections with your personal details.
2. Enter your **Phone Number** and **Email** for contact purposes.
3. If you have a **Nursing License Number**, please provide it. This information will help us verify your credentials.
4. Share your **Years of Experience** in the nursing field.
5. Select your **Nursing License State** from the dropdown menu.
6. Specify your **Availability** for potential work shifts (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Daytime, Nighttime).
7. Choose your **Shift Preferences** from the options provided.
8. In case of an emergency, provide your **Emergency Contact Name**, **Emergency Contact Phone Number**, and your relationship with this contact.
9. Enter your **Date of Birth**, **Graduation Date**, and share your **Education** background.
10. If you have relevant **Clinical Experience**, please describe it briefly.
11. If applicable, share your **Years of Clinical Experience**.
12. For two professional references, provide their **Name** and **Phone Number**.
