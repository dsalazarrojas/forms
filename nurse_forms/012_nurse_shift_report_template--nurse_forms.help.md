# Nurse Shift Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nurse Shift Report Template is designed to record key information about patient care during a nurse's shift. It is used to track the duration of care provided, medications administered, vital signs, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Shift Start Time** field to report the time when your shift began.
2. Enter the **Shift End Time** field to report the time when your shift ended.
3. Enter the **Shift Date** field to report the date of the shift.
4. Enter the number of **Worked Hours** to report the duration of your shift.
5. Enter the **Patient ID** field to record the patient's ID number.
6. Enter the **Vital Signs** field to report any notable vital signs, such as heart rate, blood pressure, or temperature.
7. Enter the **Medications Administered** field to list any medications given to the patient.
8. Enter the **Medications Refused** field to list any medications refused by the patient.
9. Enter the **Notes** field to provide any additional notes or comments about the patient's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shift Start Time**: Enter the time when your shift started.
* **Shift End Time**: Enter the time when your shift ended.
* **Shift Date**: Enter the date of the shift.
* **Worked Hours**: Enter the total number of hours you worked during the shift.
* **Patient ID**: Enter the patient's ID number, if applicable.
* **Vital Signs**: Enter any notable vital signs, such as heart rate, blood pressure, or temperature.
* **Medications Administered**: List any medications given to the patient during the shift.
* **Medications Refused**: List any medications refused by the patient during the shift.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the patient's care.
