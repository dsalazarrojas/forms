# Nursing Report Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nursing Report Submission form is a data collection tool for nurses to record important information about a patient's condition. This form ensures consistency and accuracy in patient care planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the patient's information, such as their name, date of birth, and identification number.
2. Report your observations about the patient, including any notable findings or concerns.
3. Record the patient's vital signs, such as heart rate, blood pressure, and temperature.
4. List any medications the patient is currently taking.
5. Describe your nursing interventions, including any treatments or procedures you've performed.
6. Provide details about your shift, including the patient's status and any notable events.
7. Add any additional comments or information about the patient's condition.
8. Review your submissions for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and identification number to help us identify them accurately.
* **Patient Observation** (`patient_observation`, text, optional): Report any observations or notable findings about the patient's condition.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, text, optional): Record the patient's vital signs, such as heart rate, blood pressure, and temperature.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, optional): Select all medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Nursing Interventions** (`nursing_interventions`, text, optional): Describe any nursing interventions you've performed, such as treatments or procedures.
* **Shift Details** (`shift_details`, text, optional): Provide details about your shift, including the patient's status and any notable events.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's current status, such as "stable" or "unstable."
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the patient's condition.
