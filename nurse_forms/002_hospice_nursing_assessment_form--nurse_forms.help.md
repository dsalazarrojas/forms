## Tips
* Ensure accurate and up-to-date patient information is used when completing this form.
* Assess the patient's condition carefully and accurately.
* Verify that no duplicate assessments have been completed within the past week.
* Review the patient's medical history carefully before completing this form.
* Use the form's fields to document the patient's medical conditions, medications, and care goals.
* Summarize the patient's care plan clearly and concisely.
* Document any changes or updates to the patient's care plan.
* Schedule follow-up appointments as necessary to review the patient's care.
