# Immunization Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on patient's immunization records. It is essential to have accurate and up-to-date immunization records for patient care and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's data in the "Patient Data" field.
2. Provide information on the type of vaccination(s) administered in the "Vaccination Info" field.
3. Enter the date of vaccination in the "Vaccination Date" field.
4. Provide any relevant medical information about the patient's doctor in the "Doctor Info" field.
5. If applicable, provide any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.
6. Add any additional medical notes in the "Medical Notes" field.
7. Indicate if the patient has any medication allergies in the "Medication Allergies" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
8. Finally, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Submit" field to confirm the completion of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Data**: This field is used to enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant demographic information.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Vaccination Info**: This field is used to enter the type of vaccination(s) administered, such as flu shot or MMR.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Vaccination Date**: This field is used to enter the date of vaccination.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: no
* **Doctor Info**: This field is used to enter the doctor's name and any other relevant medical information.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Medical History**: This field is used to enter any relevant medical history that may be related to the vaccination.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Medical Notes**: This field is used to enter any additional medical notes or comments.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Medication Allergies**: This field is used to indicate if the patient has any medication allergies.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: no
* **Submit**: This field is used to confirm the completion of the form.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: no

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the patient or medical staff before completing the form.
