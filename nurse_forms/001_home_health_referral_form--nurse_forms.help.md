# Home Health Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to refer patients to external home health agencies for care and services. It captures important information about the patient, including name, medical records, and reason for referral.

## How To Complete This Form

Please follow the steps below to complete the form accurately.

1. Fill in the required fields as indicated with an asterisk (*) mark.
2. For non-mandatory fields, enter information only if it's available.
3. Ensure all entered information is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): The patient's full name as it appears on their medical record or identification document.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`): The patient's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Referral Agency Name** (`referral_agency_name`): The name of the home health agency or organization being referred to.
* **Date of Referral** (`referral_date`): The date the referral is being made, in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Reason for Referral** (`referral_reason`): A brief description of the reason for the referral, including the patient's medical condition or need for home health services.
* **Health Condition** (`health_condition`): A brief description of the patient's health condition or medical status.
* **Primary Caregiver Name** (`primary_caregiver_name`): The name of the primary caregiver or contact person for the patient.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`): The patient's phone number for contact and communication.
* **Agency Phone** (`referral_agency_phone`): The phone number of the home health agency or organization being referred to.
* **Medical Record ID** (`medical_record_id`): A unique identifier for the patient's medical records.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`): The patient's medical record number, if available.

## Tips

* Ensure all entered information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Double-check the form before submission to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification from the healthcare team.
* This form is for internal use only; please do not share it with external parties.
* If you need to refer multiple patients, complete a new form for each patient.
