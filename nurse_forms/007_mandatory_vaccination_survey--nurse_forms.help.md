# mandatory_vaccination_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mandatory Vaccination Survey form is used to collect and record important information about a patient's vaccination history, status, and related details. This form helps healthcare professionals ensure that patients receive the necessary vaccinations to prevent the spread of diseases and maintain public health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Survey**: Provide a brief description of the patient's current health status.
2. **Vaccination History**: Record the patient's vaccination history, including dates and types of vaccinations received.
3. **Vaccination Status**: Select the current vaccination status of the patient (Fully Vaccinated, Partially Vaccinated, or Not Vaccinated).
4. **Vaccination Date**: Enter the date of the last vaccination received.
5. **Vaccination Dose**: Record any additional vaccination dose information if applicable.
6. **Vaccination Notes**: Provide any additional notes about the patient's vaccination history or status.
7. **Vaccination Doctor**: Enter the name of the doctor who administered the vaccination.
8. **Next Vaccination**: If applicable, schedule the next vaccination date.
9. **Vaccination Reminders**: Select whether or not to send reminders for the next vaccination.
10. **Survey Complete**: Confirm if the survey is completed.
11. **Survey Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the survey.
12. **Survey Review**: Select who will review the survey (Nurse, Doctor, or Both).
13. **Survey Signature**: The patient's signature confirming they have completed the survey.
14. **Survey Review Date**: Enter the date the survey is reviewed.
15. **Patient Signature**: The patient's signature confirming they have read and understood the survey.
16. **Survey Completed By**: Enter the name of the healthcare professional who completed the survey.
17. **Survey Completed Date**: Enter the date and time the survey was completed.
18. **Vaccination Type**: Select the type of vaccination (Hepatitis A, Hepatitis B, or Hepatitis C).
