# quarantine_meal_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Quarantine Meal Order Form is designed to help healthcare staff provide nutritious meals to patients during their quarantine period. This form helps to gather essential information about the patient's meal preferences, dietary restrictions, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your meal preferences**: Choose the type of food you would like to eat during your quarantine period.
2. **Select your preferred fruit**: Choose your favorite fruit from the available options (Apple, Banana, or Orange).
3. **Select any dietary restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions (vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free), select the corresponding option.
4. **Provide your phone number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any further assistance.
5. **Enter your email address**: Provide your email address so we can contact you via email.
6. **Select any additional dietary restrictions**: If you have any additional dietary restrictions, select the corresponding option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you eat**: This field is where you can briefly describe what you would like to eat during your quarantine period.
* **What do you drink**: This field is not used in this form. Please enter "N/A" if you have no preference.
* **What do you prefer**: This field is used to select your favorite fruit from the options provided.
	+ **Apple**: Select this option if you have a preference for apples.
	+ **Banana**: Select this option if you have a preference for bananas.
	+ **Orange**: Select this option if you have a preference for oranges.
* **What is your preference**: This field is used to select your additional dietary preferences. Select one of the options provided.
* **What is your dietary restrictions**: This field is used to select any dietary restrictions you may have. Select one of the options provided.
* **What is your dietary restrictions 2**: This field is used to select any additional dietary restrictions you may have. Select one of the options provided.
* **What is your dietary restrictions 3**: This field is used to select any additional dietary restrictions you may have. Select one of the options provided.
* **What is your phone number**: This field is where you enter your phone number so we can contact you for any further assistance.
* **What is your email address**: This field is where you enter your email address so we can contact you via email.

## Tips

* Please make sure to select only one option for "What do you prefer" and "What is your preference" fields.
* Please select only one option for "What is your dietary restrictions", "What is your dietary restrictions 2", and "What is your dietary restrictions 3" fields.
* Please make sure to enter a valid phone number and email address.
