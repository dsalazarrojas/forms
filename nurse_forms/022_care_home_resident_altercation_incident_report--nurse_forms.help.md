# Care Home Resident Altercation Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Care Home Resident Altercation Incident Report** is used to document and track incidents that occur within a care home setting. This form is designed to gather factual information about the incident, including details about the incident, residents involved, witnesses, and any subsequent actions taken.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Select the date when the incident occurred in the "Incident Date" field.
3. Enter the approximate time of the incident in the "Incident Time" field, if applicable.
4. Enter the names of the resident(s) involved in the "Resident Involved" field.
5. List the names of any witnesses in the "Witness Names" field.
6. Provide the specific location of the incident in the "Location of Incident" field.
7. Write a detailed, objective account of what happened in the "Description of Event" field.
8. Describe the immediate actions taken in response to the incident, such as emergency interventions or medical treatment, in the "Immediate Actions Taken" field.
9. If applicable, list any injuries reported in the "Injuries Reported" field.
10. Outline the follow-up plan, including any further investigation, medical treatment, or other steps that will be taken, in the "Follow-up Plan" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date**: The date when the incident occurred. Please enter the date in a format that can be easily understood by care staff.
* **Incident Time**: The approximate time of the incident. This is optional, but helps to provide a more accurate timeline of events.
* **Resident Involved**: The name of the resident(s) who were involved in the incident. Please ensure that you accurately list all residents who were present at the time of the incident.
* **Witness Names**: A list of names of any staff or residents who witnessed the incident.
* **Location of Incident**: The specific room or area where the incident occurred.
* **Description of Event**: A detailed, objective account of what happened during the incident.
* **Immediate Actions Taken**: The immediate actions taken in response to the incident, including any emergency interventions or medical treatment.
* **Injuries Reported**: If applicable, describe any injuries observed during the incident.
* **Follow-up Plan**: A plan outlining any further investigation, medical treatment, or other steps that will be taken to address the incident and ensure resident safety and well-being.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly, as the information gathered will aid in the investigation and development of follow-up plans.
- If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, consult with care staff or management for guidance.
- The follow-up plan should be specific, actionable, and focused on ensuring resident safety and well-being.
