# ER Nurse Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for conducting interviews with nurses working in emergency rooms to gather information about their experience and responses to hypothetical scenarios. The data collected will be used to evaluate the candidate's qualifications and fit for a position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions and scenarios carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Enter your name and other identifying information in the "Candidate Details" field.
3. Answer the "Work Experience" question with the number of years you have been working as an ER nurse.
4. Choose one of the response options for each scenario in the "Scenario Responses" field.
5. If you have additional information that would be helpful to provide, enter it in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, text, required): This field is for entering your name and other identifying information. Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Work Experience** (`experience`, number, required): This field is for entering the number of years you have been working as an ER nurse.
* **Scenario Responses** (`scenario_responses`, select_one, required): This field presents several scenarios and asks you to choose one of the response options. Please choose the response that best matches your experience and qualifications.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): This field is for providing additional information that may be helpful to provide context or clarification. It is not required, but can be useful for providing further details. 

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, do not hesitate to ask for clarification or additional information.
* Review your responses carefully before submitting the form to ensure they accurately reflect your experience and qualifications.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the person administering the form for assistance.
