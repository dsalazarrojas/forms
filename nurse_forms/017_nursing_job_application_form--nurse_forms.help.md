# Nursing Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from candidates interested in applying for nursing jobs in a hospital or healthcare setting. It collects personal details, medical certifications, work experience, education, and contact information, as well as documents such as resumes, cover letters, and certifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including any relevant information about your medical certifications, work experience, and education.
2. Select your level of nursing experience and education from the provided options.
3. Enter your contact details and any relevant medical certification IDs.
4. Upload your resume, cover letter, and any necessary certifications.
5. Once you've completed all fields, submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Personal details
* **Personal details** (text): Enter your full name, address, phone number, and any other relevant personal details.

### 2. Medical certifications
* **Medical certifications** (select_multiple): Check the options to indicate if you have any medical certifications. You can select multiple certifications if applicable.

### 3. Nursing experience
* **Nursing experience** (select_one): Choose the option that best describes your level of nursing experience.

### 4. Education
* **Education** (select_one): Select the option that best describes your level of education.

### 5. Contact details
* **Contact details** (text): Enter your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.

### 6. Medical certification IDs
* **Medical certification IDs** (text): Enter any medical certification IDs you have obtained, separated by commas.

### 7. Resume
* **Upload resume** (text): Attach your resume to support your application.

### 8. Cover letter
* **Upload cover letter** (text): Attach your cover letter to support your application.

### 9. Certifications
* **Upload Certifications** (text): Attach any additional certifications that may be relevant to your application.

### 10. Submit
* **Submit** (text): Once you've completed all fields, click to submit your application.
