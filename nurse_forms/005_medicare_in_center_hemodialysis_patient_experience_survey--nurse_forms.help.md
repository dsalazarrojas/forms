# medicare_in_center_hemodialysis_patient_experience_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from patients who have undergone hemodialysis treatment in a medical center. The form aims to evaluate the patient's experience, satisfaction, and rating of the treatment and services provided.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page and answer all the questions carefully.
2. For numerical questions, such as "Hemodialysis Treatment Frequency", please choose the frequency that best describes your experience.
3. For categorical questions, such as "Hemodialysis Tech Rating", please select the rating that best reflects your experience.
4. For open-ended questions, such as "Comments", please provide your feedback in the space provided.
