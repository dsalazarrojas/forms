# proof_of_covid_19_vaccination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The proof of COVID-19 vaccination form is used to collect information about a patient's vaccination status, which can be used to verify the patient's vaccination details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient name and details.
2. Enter the name and address of the medical center where you received the vaccination.
3. Record the date and time of the vaccination.
4. Select the type of vaccine you received (e.g., Pfizer, Moderna, AstraZeneca).
5. Enter the dosage of the vaccine administered.
6. Specify the site where the vaccination was administered.
7. Enter your doctor's name.
8. Enter your doctor's medical license number.
9. Sign and date the form with your patient signature.
10. Sign and date the form with your doctor's signature.
11. Enter your age.
12. Select your gender.
13. Enter your contact number.
14. Enter your doctor's contact number.
15. Enter your medical record number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Medical Center Name** (`medical_center_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the medical center where you received the vaccination.
* **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you received the COVID-19 vaccination.
* **Vaccination Time** (`vaccination_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you received the COVID-19 vaccination.
* **Vaccine Type** (`vaccine_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of vaccine you received from the options: Pfizer, Moderna, AstraZeneca.
* **Dosage Administered** (`dosage_administered`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of vaccine administered to you.
* **Vaccination Site** (`vaccination_site`, `text`, required): Specify the site where the vaccine was administered.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, required): Enter your doctor's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license`, `text`, required): Enter your doctor's medical license number.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `note`, required): Sign and date the form with your patient signature.
* **Doctor's Signature** (`doctor_signature`, `note`, required): Sign and date the form with your doctor's signature.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, `number`, required): Enter your age.
* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender from the options: Male, Female, Other, Prefer not to say.
* **Patient Contact Number** (`patient_contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Doctor's Contact Number** (`doctor_contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your doctor's contact number.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, `number`, required): Enter your medical record number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurately filled out and signed before submitting the form.
* Make sure to keep a copy of the form for your personal records.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please consult your healthcare provider.
