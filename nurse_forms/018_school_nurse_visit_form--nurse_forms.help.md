# School Nurse Visit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Nurse Visit Form is a crucial tool for tracking and recording information about visits made by school nurses to students. It is used to provide accurate and consistent reporting of student health data, facilitate collaboration among school staff, and ensure that students receive quality care. This form should be used whenever a school nurse conducts a visit to a student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information and permissions to access this form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, paying attention to each field's requirements and options.
3. Save the form for future reference and to update visit records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student you visited.
* **School ID**: (`school_id`, number, required): Enter the school's identification number for the student you visited.
* **Visit Date**: (`visit_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the visit.
* **Visit Time**: (`visit_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the visit.
* **Student Grade**: (`student_grade`, select_one, required): Select the student's grade level from the provided options.
* **Reason for Visit**: (`reason_for_visit`, text, required): Describe the reason for the visit.
* **Visit Notes**: (`visit_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the visit.
* **Follow up Date**: (`follow_up_date`, date, required): Enter the date for the next follow-up visit.
* **Follow up Time**: (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Enter the time for the next follow-up visit.
* **Follow up Status**: (`follow_up_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the follow-up visit from the provided options.
* **School ID**: (`school_id`, select_one, required): Confirm whether the student is attending school.
* **School Name**: (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school where the visit took place.
* **School Address**: (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the school where the visit took place.
* **Contact Person**: (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Tips
* Be accurate and consistent when filling out the form to ensure that student data is up-to-date and reliable.
* Use the provided options for select fields to ensure that you are selecting the correct information.
* Save the form after completing it to update visit records and for future reference.
