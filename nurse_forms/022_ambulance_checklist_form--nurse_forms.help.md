# Ambulance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is designed to assist drivers and operators in completing the Ambulance Checklist Form. The form is used to check and verify the operational condition of their vehicle and its equipment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reviewing the general information section, which includes the checklist date and time.
2. Move on to the exterior vehicle check section, which covers the condition of the vehicle's exterior, lights and siren, tires, and fluid leaks.
3. Next, review the engine compartment section, which includes checks on the engine oil level, coolant level, and brake fluid level.
4. Proceed to the interior vehicle check section, which covers the condition of the dashboard, seatbelts, climate control, and patient compartment.
5. Check the safety equipment section, which includes fire extinguishers, first aid kits, and extrication equipment.
6. Review the communication equipment section, which covers radio equipment and mobile data terminals.
7. Move on to the fuel and mileage section, which includes the fuel level and odometer reading.
8. Complete the medical supplies section, which includes checks on bandages, dressings, IV supplies, and medications.
9. Finally, add any additional notes or comments and sign the form as completed by the driver or operator.
