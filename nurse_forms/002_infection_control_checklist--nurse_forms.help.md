# Infection Control Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Infection Control Checklist is a form used by nurses to document and track patient information related to infection control. This form helps healthcare providers to identify potential infection control issues and ensure prompt treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's identifier number.
2. Provide the patient's name.
3. Select the type of medical condition that the patient is experiencing (Bloodborne Infection, Urinary Tract Infection, or Surgical Site Infection).
4. Record the date the patient was last seen by the healthcare provider.
5. Record the date the patient was admitted to the healthcare facility (if applicable).
6. Enter the patient's date of birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Identifier (1)**: Enter the patient's unique identifier number assigned by the healthcare facility.
* **Patient Name (2)**: Enter the patient's full name, including first and last name.
* **Medical Condition (3)**: Select the type of infection that the patient is experiencing from the options provided.
* **Date Last Seen (4)**: Record the date and time the patient was last seen by the healthcare provider.
* **Date Admitted (5)**: Record the date and time the patient was admitted to the healthcare facility (if applicable).
* **Patient DoB (6)**: Enter the patient's date of birth.

## Tips
* Always ensure that you are using the correct patient identifier number.
* Verify the patient's name and date of birth with the patient or their family member before completing this form.
* If the patient has any medical conditions that are not listed in the options provided, please use the "Other" option and provide a brief description in the space provided.
