# new_patient_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new patient enrollment form is designed to collect comprehensive information from new patients, including their personal details, contact information, and medical history. This information is essential for patient care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about the patient.
2. Be sure to fill out each field carefully, as every piece of information is crucial for patient care.
3. If you're unsure about any field, ask your healthcare provider or the relevant department for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_information** (patient_info, text, not required): This field is for entering the patient's basic information, such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **contact_information** (contact_info, text, not required): Enter the patient's contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
* **emergency_contact** (emergency_contact, text, not required): List the names and relationships of emergency contacts, such as family members or next of kin.
* **medical_history** (medical_history, text, not required): Describe the patient's medical history, including previous illnesses, surgeries, and allergies.
* **emergency_contact_people** (emergency_contact_people, text, not required): Provide a list of emergency contacts and their relationships.
* **medical_history_conditions** (medical_history_conditions, text, not required): List the patient's medical conditions, such as diabetes, hypertension, etc.
* **allergies** (allergies, text, not required): List any allergies or sensitivities the patient may have.
* **medications** (medications, text, not required): Record any current medications or treatments the patient is taking.
* **medical_history_procedures** (medical_history_procedures, text, not required): Describe any previous medical procedures or surgeries the patient has undergone.
* **medical_history_surgeries** (medical_history_surgeries, text, not required): List any previous surgeries or surgical procedures.
* **medical_history_injuries** (medical_history_injuries, text, not required): Describe any previous injuries or accidents.
* **medical_history_surgeries_history** (medical_history_surgeries_history, text, not required): Record the history of any previous surgeries or surgical procedures.
* **medical_history_other** (medical_history_other, text, not required): Provide any additional medical history information not listed elsewhere.
* **emergency_contact_relationship** (emergency_contact_relationship, text, not required): Specify the nature of the emergency contact's relationship (e.g., mother, father, brother, etc.).
* **medical_history_family_history** (medical_history_family_history, text, not required): Describe the patient's family medical history, including any significant medical conditions or health issues.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure the best possible patient care.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask your healthcare provider or the relevant department for assistance.
- The collected data will be used exclusively for patient care and treatment purposes.
