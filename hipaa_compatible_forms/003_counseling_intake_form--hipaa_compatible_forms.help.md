# Counseling Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counseling Intake Form is a crucial document that helps us understand your background, medical history, and contact information. This form will be used to create a comprehensive profile of you and your needs, ensuring that we provide you with the best possible care and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in your **Client Name** in the designated box.
*   Enter your **Contact Info**, including any relevant contact methods such as phone numbers or email addresses.
*   If you have any **Insurance Info**, please provide it in the designated box.
*   If you have a **Medical History**, please provide it in the **Medical History** field.
*   For your **Emergency Contact**, please select the relationship from the options provided (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, etc.). Then, enter their **Phone Number** and provide any additional information in the **Other** field.
*   If you have an **Insurance Card ID**, please select it from the options provided.
*   If you have any other **Doctor Info**, please provide it in the **Doctor Info** field.
*   If you have a **Medical Record Number**, please enter it in the designated box.
*   If you have a **Doctor Name**, please enter it in the designated box.
*   If you have a **Phone Number** for your doctor, please enter it in the designated box.
*   If you have information about your **Medical Group**, please enter it in the designated box.
*   If you have a **Medical Provider**, please enter it in the designated box.
*   If you have information about your **Medical Insurance**, please enter it in the designated box.
*   If you have a **Medical Plan**, please enter it in the designated box.
*   If you have a **Medical Condition**, please enter it in the designated box.
*   If you have information about your **Medical Treatment**, please enter it in the designated box.
*   If you have a **Medical Prescription**, please enter it in the designated box.
*   If you have a **Medical Referral**, please enter it in the designated box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Contact Info**: Enter any contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses, that we can use to reach you.
*   **Insurance Info**: Enter any insurance information, such as your insurance card ID, if applicable.
*   **Medical History**: Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or treatments.
*   **Medical History Details**: Provide more detailed information about your medical history, if necessary.
*   **Emergency Contact**: Select the relationship of your emergency contact from the options provided (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, etc.). Then, enter their phone number and provide any additional information in the **Other** field.
*   **Emergency Contact Relation**: Select the relationship of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Relation Other**: If the relationship of your emergency contact is not listed, provide the relation in this field.
*   **Insurance Card ID**: Select your insurance card ID from the options provided.
*   **Doctor Info**: Provide any information about your doctor, such as their name or phone number.
*   **Medical Record Number**: Enter your medical record number, if applicable.
*   **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of your doctor.
*   **Phone**: Enter the phone number of your doctor.
*   **Medical Group**: Enter any information about your medical group.
*   **Medical Provider**: Enter any information about your medical provider.
*   **Medical Insurance**: Enter any information about your medical insurance.
*   **Medical Plan**: Enter any information about your medical plan.
*   **Medical Condition**: Enter any information about your medical condition.
*   **Medical Treatment**: Enter any information about your medical treatment.
*   **Medical Prescription**: Enter any information about your medical prescription.
*   **Medical Referral**: Enter any information about your medical referral.

## Tips
Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to help us better understand your needs and provide you with the best possible care.
