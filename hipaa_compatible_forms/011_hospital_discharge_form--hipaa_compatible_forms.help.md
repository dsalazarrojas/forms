# Hospital Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Discharge Form is a document used to collect information from patients as they are discharged from hospital care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the previous section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, date, required): Enter the date the patient is being discharged from the hospital.
*   **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, text, required): Enter the patient's identification number.
*   **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the attending physician.
*   **Discharge Time** (`discharge_time`, time, required): Enter the time of discharge in the "HH:MM" format.
*   **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): Enter a short description of the patient's medical condition.
*   **Discharge Status** (`discharge_status`, select_one, required): Select the discharge status of the patient from the options: "Active", "Recovered", or "Deceased".
*   **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of the patient's insurance provider.
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name.
*   **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the patient's contact person.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email of the patient's contact person.
*   **Next Appointment** (`next_appointment`, date, required): Enter the date of the next appointment in the "YYYY-MM-DD" format.
*   **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, text, optional): Enter any medication allergies the patient has.
*   **Discharge Instructions** (`discharge_instructions`, note, optional): Enter any additional discharge instructions.
*   **Signature of Discharging Provider** (`signature`, text, required): Sign the form with the signature of the discharging healthcare provider.
*   **Discharge Reason** (`discharge_reason`, text, required): Enter a short reason for discharging the patient.
*   **Discharge Location** (`discharge_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the patient will be discharging.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the contact address of the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the contact person.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email of the contact person.
*   **Insurance Claim** (`insurance_claim`, text, optional): Enter insurance claim details.
*   **Insurance Status** (`insurance_status`, select_one, required): Select the insurance status of the patient from the options: "Active", "Inactive", or "Denied".
*   **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the follow-up appointment in the "YYYY-MM-DD" format.
*   **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the follow-up appointment in the "HH:MM" format.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields.
*   Enter information in the correct format.
*   Check for spelling and grammar errors before submitting the form.
