# Medical Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from patients for medical consultations. It is meant to be filled out by patients prior to their appointment with a healthcare professional. The form's purpose is to streamline the consultation process, allowing healthcare providers to have a better understanding of the patient's medical history, chief complaints, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety.
2. Enter your name and date of birth as prompted.
3. Provide a detailed description of your chief complaints.
4. List any allergies or sensitivities you may have.
5. List any ongoing medical history.
6. Select the medical specialty under which you are seeking treatment.
7. Enter the name of your healthcare provider (if applicable).
8. Provide their email and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Medical Consultation Form**: This is the title of the form. It serves as a header and is not a field that requires filling out.
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): This is a free-form field for you to provide any additional contact information you feel is relevant to your consultation.
- **Chief Complaints** (`chief_complaints`): Describe your main symptoms or concerns that bring you to the consultation.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Provide a detailed account of any relevant medical history.
- **Allergies and Sensitivities** (`allergies`): List any allergies or sensitivities you may have.
- **Medications** (`medications`): List any medications you are currently taking.
- **Medical Specialties** (`medical_specialties`): Select one of the medical specialties listed that applies to your consultation (e.g., cardiology, dermatology, etc.).
- **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`): If you are seeing a specific doctor, enter their name here.
- **Doctor's Email** (`doctor_email`): If the doctor has a unique email, enter it here (if applicable).
- **Doctor's Phone** (`doctor_phone`): If the doctor has a unique phone number, enter it here (if applicable).
- **Consultation Notes** (`medical_consultation_notes`): Enter any additional information you think is important for your consultation.
