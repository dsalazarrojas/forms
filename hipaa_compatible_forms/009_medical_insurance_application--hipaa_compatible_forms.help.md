# Medical Insurance Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a medical insurance application designed for patients to provide their personal and health information to insurance providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the "Medical Insurance Application" page.
2.  Fill in the required information in the "Patient Information", "Contact Information", "Medical History", "Medical Insurance Details", and "Medical Insurance Application" fields.
3.  Make sure to fill in all the required fields as per the form instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Medical Insurance Application** (`medical_insurance_application`, text, optional): This field is used for the patient to apply for medical insurance. Please provide a brief description of your medical insurance application.
*   **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, optional): This field is used to gather patient's basic details such as name, date of birth, and contact information.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): This field is used to gather the contact information of the patient's primary contact person.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This field is used for the patient to provide a brief summary of their medical history.
*   **Medical Insurance Details** (`medical_insurance_details`, text, optional): This field is used for the patient to provide details about their existing medical insurance plans.

## Tips

*   Please fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
*   Please be as clear and concise as possible while filling the form.
*   In case of any doubts or issues, contact your medical insurance provider for further guidance.
