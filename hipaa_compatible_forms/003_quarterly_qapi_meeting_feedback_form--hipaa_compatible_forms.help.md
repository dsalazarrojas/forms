# quarterly_qapi_meeting_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The quarterly QAPI meeting feedback form is designed to gather feedback and information about meetings attended by employees. This form should be filled out by each attendee to ensure that we're using our time effectively and improving our processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have all the necessary information and documentation from the meeting.
*   Fill out the form completely and accurately.
*   Review each field carefully to ensure you're providing the right information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date of the meeting you attended.
*   **Meeting Attendees** (`meeting_attendees`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you attended the meeting by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Meeting Facilitator** (`meeting_facilitator`, select_one, optional): Identify who led the meeting by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Meeting Purpose** (`meeting_purpose`, text, optional): Briefly describe the meeting's purpose and objective.
*   **Feedback Question 1** (`feedback_question_1`, text, optional): Provide your thoughts and insights on what went well or what didn't go well during the meeting.
*   **Feedback Question 2** (`feedback_question_2`, text, optional): Share your suggestions or ideas on how we can improve future meetings or our process.
*   **Feedback Question 3** (`feedback_question_3`, text, optional): Summarize any takeaways or key points from the meeting that you'd like to highlight.
*   **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or information from the meeting that you think would be helpful to others.

Note: This form is designed to be a starting point for feedback, so feel free to add any other comments or insights that might be helpful to the team or organization.
