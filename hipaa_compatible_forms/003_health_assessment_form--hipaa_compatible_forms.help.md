# Health Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Assessment Form is designed to collect health-related information from users, including medical history, contact details, and other relevant health conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by entering your medical history in the **medical_history** field.
2. Provide your contact information in the **contact_info** field, if required.
3. Select any relevant medical procedures you have undergone in the **medical_procedures** field (optional).
4. Select any additional medical procedures (optional) from the **medical_procedures_2** field.
5. Select any other medical procedures (optional) from the **medical_procedures_3** field.
6. List any medications you are currently taking in the **medication_list** field (optional).
7. Select any allergies you have from the **allergies** field (optional).
8. List any other medical conditions you have (optional) in the **medical_condition_list**, **medical_condition_2**, **medical_condition_3**, **medical_condition_4**, **medical_condition_5**, **medical_condition_6**, **medical_condition_7**, **medical_condition_8**, **medical_condition_9**, and **medical_condition_10** fields.
9. If you have any other medical conditions not listed, please enter them in the **medical_condition_11** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your medical history.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, if required.
* **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant medical procedures you have undergone.
* **Medical Procedures 2** (`medical_procedures_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional medical procedures you have had.
* **Medical Procedures 3** (`medical_procedures_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select any other medical procedures you have had.
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_one, optional): Select any allergies you have.
* **Medical Condition List** (`medical_condition_list`, text, optional): List any other medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Condition 2** (`medical_condition_2`, text, optional): List any other medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Condition 3** (`medical_condition_3`, text, optional): List any other medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Condition 4** (`medical_condition_4`, text, optional): List any other medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Condition 5** (`medical_condition_5`, text, optional): List any other medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Condition 6** (`medical_condition_6`, text, optional): List any other medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Condition 7** (`medical_condition_7`, text, optional): List any other medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Condition 8** (`medical_condition_8`, text, optional): List any other medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Condition 9** (`medical_condition_9`, text, optional): List any other medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Condition 10** (`medical_condition_10`, text, optional): List any other medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Condition 11** (`medical_condition_11`, text, optional): List any other medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Condition 11-2** (`medical_condition_11_2`, text, optional): List any other medical conditions you have.

Note: The fields with numbers 11 and 11-2 are meant to be used for conditions that may not fit in the other fields.
