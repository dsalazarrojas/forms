# Patient Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Referral Form is used to collect information about a patient's visit to facilitate communication between healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Name**: Enter the patient's first name.
2. **Last Name**: Enter the patient's last name. This field is required.
3. **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of the doctor who referred the patient.
4. **Doctor Phone**: Enter the doctor's phone number in E. 123-456-7890 format.
5. **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
6. **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
7. **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of the patient's visit.
8. **Address**: Enter the patient's address.
9. **Referring Diagnosis**: Enter the diagnosis that led to the referral.
10. **Reason for Visit**: Enter the reason for the patient's visit.
11. **Patient Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the patient.
12. **Doctor Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the doctor.
13. **Referral Status**: Select the current status of the referral (New, In Progress, Completed).
14. **Referral Date**: Enter the date of the referral.
15. **Patient ID**: Enter the patient's ID number (if applicable).
16. **Patient Dob**: Enter the patient's date of birth (if not entered above).
17. **Sex**: Select the patient's sex (Male, Female, Other).
18. **Race**: Select the patient's race (White, Black or African American, Asian or Pacific Islander, Native American or Alaska Native, Other).
19. **Ethnicity**: Select the patient's ethnicity (Hispanic or Latino, American Indian or Alaska Native, Other).
20. **Language**: Select the languages spoken by the patient (English, Spanish, Other).
21. **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
22. **Doctor Email**: Enter the doctor's email address.
23. **Doctor Address**: Enter the doctor's address.
24. **Doctor Phone 2**: Enter the doctor's secondary phone number.
25. **Specialty**: Enter the doctor's specialty.
