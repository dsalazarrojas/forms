# Medication Error Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medication Error Report Form is used to report medication errors that occurred during medical treatment. This form helps to gather information about the patient, medication, and error that occurred.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
* Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Patient DOB" field.
* Enter the medication that was administered in the "Medication Administered" field.
* Enter the reason for the incorrect medication administered in the "Reason for incorrect Medication Administered" field.
* Enter the patient's ID in the "Patient ID" field.
* Enter the date the medication was administered in the "Medication Administered Date" field.
* Enter the time the medication was administered in the "Medication Administered Time" field.
* Enter the person reporting the error in the "Reported By" field.
* Enter the date the error was reported in the "Reported Date" field.
* Enter the time the error was reported in the "Reported Time" field.
* Enter the location where the error occurred in the "Incident Location" field.
* Enter a description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
* Enter the date of the incident in the "Incident Date" field.
* Enter the time of the incident in the "Incident Time" field.
* Enter the role of the person reporting the error in the "Reported By Role" field.
* Enter the medication that was administered to the patient in the "Medication Administered To" field.
* Enter the patient's weight in the "Patient Weight" field.
* Enter the dosage instructions for the medication administered to the patient in the "Medication Administered To Dosage Instructions" field.
* Enter the quantity of the medication administered to the patient in the "Medication Administered To Quantity" field.
* Enter the type of error that occurred in the "Error Type" field.
* Enter a description of the error that occurred in the "Error Description" field.
* Enter the severity of the error in the "Error Severity" field.
* Enter any action taken as a result of the error in the "Action Taken" field.
* Enter any additional comments about the incident in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Name (<patient_name>, text, required: false)
* Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.

### Patient DOB (<patient_dob>, date, required: false)
* Enter the patient's date of birth as it appears on their medical record.

### Medication Administered (<medication_administered>, text, required: false)
* Enter the name of the medication that was administered to the patient.

### Reason for incorrect Medication Administered (<incorrect_medication_administered_reason>, text, required: false)
* Enter the reason why the incorrect medication was administered to the patient.

### Patient ID (<patient_id>, text, required: false)
* Enter the patient's ID number as it appears on their medical record.

### Medication Administered Date (<medication_administered_date>, date, required: false)
* Enter the date the medication was administered to the patient.

### Medication Administered Time (<medication_administered_time>, time, required: false)
* Enter the time the medication was administered to the patient.

### Reported By (<reported_by>, text, required: false)
* Enter the name of the person reporting the error.

### Reported Date (<reported_date>, date, required: false)
* Enter the date the error was reported.

### Reported Time (<reported_time>, time, required: false)
* Enter the time the error was reported.

### Incident Location (<incident_location>, text, required: false)
* Enter the location where the error occurred.

### Incident Description (<incident_description>, text, required: false)
* Enter a description of the incident.

### Incident Date (<incident_date>, date, required: false)
* Enter the date of the incident.

### Incident Time (<incident_time>, time, required: false)
* Enter the time of the incident.

### Reported By Role (<reported_by_role>, text, required: false)
* Enter the role of the person reporting the error.

### Medication Administered To (<medication_administered_to>, text, required: false)
* Enter the name of the medication that was administered to the patient.

### Patient Weight (<patient_weight>, number, required: false)
* Enter the patient's weight in kilograms or pounds.

### Medication Administered To Dosage Instructions (<medication_administered_to_dosage_instructions>, text, required: false)
* Enter the dosage instructions for the medication administered to the patient.

### Medication Administered To Quantity (<medication_administered_to_quantity>, text, required: false)
* Enter the quantity of the medication administered to the patient.

### Error Type (<error_type>, text, required: false)
* Enter the type of error that occurred.

### Error Description (<error_description>, text, required: false)
* Enter a description of the error that occurred.

### Error Severity (<error_severity>, text, required: false)
* Enter the severity of the error.

### Action Taken (<action_taken>, text, required: false)
* Enter any action taken as a result of the error.

### Comments (<comments>, text, required: false)
* Enter any additional comments about the incident.
