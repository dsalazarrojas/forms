# Dental Treatment Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for dental treatment waiver purposes. It collects information from the patient to understand their medical history, allergy status, and other relevant details for safe and effective treatment. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately to provide necessary information about your medical history and allergy status.
2. Review each section of the form and enter the required information.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: Enter your name, date of birth, and time of visit. This section is optional but helps the dental practice understand your basic details.
* **Medical History**: Describe your medical history, including any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies. This helps the dentist identify potential risks and take necessary precautions.
* **Allergy Information**: Check 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have any known allergies. If 'Yes', list the allergies you are allergic to. This ensures your dentist takes extra precautions when treating you.
* **Medical Treatment Waiver**: If you have any medical conditions that may affect your treatment, describe them in this field. This helps the dentist understand your medical status.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that you have provided accurate and honest information. This is a required field.
* **Doctor Signature**: The dentist will sign this field after reviewing your completed form.
* **Date Signed**: The date the form was signed by you and the dentist.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format 'MM/DD/YYYY'.
* **Time of Visit**: Enter the time of your visit in 'HH:MM AM/PM' format.
* **Medical Practice**: Enter your medical practice name or ID, if applicable.
