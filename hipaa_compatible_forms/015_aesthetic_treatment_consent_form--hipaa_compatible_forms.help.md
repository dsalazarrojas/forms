# Aesthetic Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aesthetic Treatment Consent Form is designed to gather information from patients before undergoing aesthetic treatments. This form ensures that patients understand the treatment procedure, potential risks, and expected benefits. By signing this form, patients acknowledge their consent to receive the treatment and agree to the terms outlined.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name.
2. **Date of Birth**: Provide your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. **Address**: Enter your current residential address.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number.
5. **Email Address**: Enter your email address for appointment confirmations.
6. **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
7. **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
8. **Type of Aesthetic Treatment**: Select the specific aesthetic treatment you are consenting to (e.g., Botox Injection, Dermal Fillers, etc.).
9. **Treatment Area**: Choose the area of the body that will be treated (e.g., Face, Neck, etc.).
10. **Treatment Provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who will perform the treatment.
11. **Risks and Benefits**: Read the treatment risks and benefits section, then select if the risks have been explained to you.
12. **Common Risks Acknowledged**: If you understand that common risks may include bruising, swelling, redness, and temporary discomfort, select 'True'.
13. **Rare Risks Acknowledged**: If you understand that rare risks may include infection, scarring, allergic reaction, or unsatisfactory results, select 'True'.
14. **Benefits Understood**: If you understand the expected benefits of the treatment, select 'True'.
15. **Realistic Expectations**: If you have realistic expectations about the treatment outcomes, select 'True'.
16. **Current Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
17. **Allergies**: List any known allergies, especially to medications or cosmetic products.
18. **Previous Adverse Reactions**: If you have had any adverse reactions to aesthetic treatments before, select 'True' and provide details.
19. **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions that may affect the treatment, enter a description.
20. **Consent to Treatment**: Select 'True' to indicate your consent to receive the aesthetic treatment.
21. **Right to Ask Questions**: Select 'True' if you understand you have the right to ask questions about the procedure and have them answered.
22. **Right to Withdraw Consent**: Select 'True' if you understand you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time before the procedure begins.
23. **Photography Consent**: If you consent to before and after photographs for medical record purposes, select 'True'.
24. **Patient Signature**: Sign your name to indicate your consent.
25. **Date of Signature**: Enter the date when the consent is signed.
26. **Provider Signature**: Sign your name, indicating the healthcare provider's agreement to the terms.
