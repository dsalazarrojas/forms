# Prior Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from a patient's medical history and current medication list to ensure that the patient is eligible for a specific medication. It is a crucial step in the prior authorization process, which helps healthcare providers make informed decisions about a patient's treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's information: This includes the patient's name, date of birth, and contact details.
2.  Enter the physician's information: This includes the physician's name, contact details, and medical license number.
3.  Select relevant medical history: Choose the medical conditions or medications that apply to the patient's current situation.
4.  Select current medications: Choose the medications the patient is currently taking.
5.  Review and submit the form: Once you have completed all the necessary fields, review your entries and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required: false): This section is for entering the patient's name, date of birth, and contact details.
*   **Physician Information** (`physician_information`, text, required: false): This section is for entering the physician's name, contact details, and medical license number.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select multiple, required: false): This section is for selecting the medical conditions or medications that apply to the patient's current situation.
*   **Medication List** (`medication_list`, select multiple, required: false): This section is for selecting the medications the patient is currently taking.

## Tips

*   Please note that this form is designed to be completed by authorized healthcare professionals only.
*   Make sure to review the patient's information carefully before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any part of the form, please do not hesitate to contact your healthcare provider for guidance.
*   The form will not be completed if any of the conditions mentioned above are met.
