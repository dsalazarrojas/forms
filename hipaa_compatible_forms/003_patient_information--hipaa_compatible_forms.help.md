# Patient Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store patient information for various purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the "Patient Information" page.
2. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
3. Enter the patient's email address in the "patient_email" field.
4. Enter the patient's phone number in the "patient_phone" field.
5. Enter the patient's address in the "patient_address" field.
6. Enter the patient's social security number (if applicable) in the "patient_social_security_number" field.
7. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "patient_date_of_birth" field (in the format MM/DD/YY or DD MMM YYYY).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the form, if desired.
  - **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, not required): Enter the patient's name.
  - **Email** (`patient_email`, email, not required): Enter the patient's email address.
  - **Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, not required): Enter the patient's phone number.
  - **Address** (`patient_address`, text, not required): Enter the patient's address.
  - **Social Security Number** (`patient_social_security_number`, number, not required): Enter the patient's social security number, if applicable.
  - **Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, not required): Enter the patient's date of birth (in the format MM/DD/YY or DD MMM YYYY).
