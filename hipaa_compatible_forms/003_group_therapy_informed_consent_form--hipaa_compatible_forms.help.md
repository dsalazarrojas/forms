# Group Therapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect informed consent from clients prior to group therapy sessions. It outlines the services provided, risks, benefits, and consequences of the therapy, as well as the client's consent and signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. Select the correct answers for each field according to the options provided.
3. Sign the form to indicate your agreement to the terms of the therapy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Services Provided** (Page 1): This field is used to indicate whether the services provided are 'Yes' or 'No'. Please select 'Yes' if you are receiving group therapy, and 'No' if you are not.
* **Confidentiality** (Page 1): This field is not currently applicable, but it will be used to outline the level of confidentiality expected in the therapy process.
* **Group Therapy** (Page 1): Select the type of therapy you are receiving: Individual Therapy, Group Therapy, Marriage/Couples Therapy, or Family Therapy.
* **Services Provided Details** (Page 2): This field is where you will detail the services you will provide. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Risks** (Page 2): This field is used to identify potential risks associated with the therapy. Please select the risk that applies to you.
* **Benefits** (Page 2): This field is used to identify the benefits of the therapy. Please select the benefit that applies to you.
* **Services Provided What Will Happen** (Page 3): This field is used to outline what will happen during the therapy. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Services Provided What Will Not Happen** (Page 3): This field is used to outline what will not happen during the therapy. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Services Provided Consequences** (Page 3): This is where you will detail the consequences of the services you will provide. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Client Consent** (Page 4): This field is used to indicate your consent to the terms of the therapy. Please select the option that best describes your understanding.
* **Consent Form Signed** (Page 4): This field is not currently applicable, but it will be used to record the signature of the person who signed the form.
* **Signature** (Page 4): This is where you will sign the form to indicate your agreement to the terms of the therapy.
* **Date** (Page 5): Please enter the date you sign the form.
* **Date Time** (Page 5): Please enter the time you sign the form.
* **Therapist** (Page 5): This field is not currently applicable, but it will be used to record the name of the therapist.
* **Contact** (Page 5): This field is not currently applicable, but it will be used to record the contact information of the therapist.
* **Location** (Page 5): This field is not currently applicable, but it will be used to record the location of the therapy sessions.
* **Emergency Contact** (Page 5): This field is not currently applicable, but it will be used to record the emergency contact information.
* **Notes** (Page 5): Please enter any additional notes or comments that you want to add to the form.
* **Consent Form Reviewed By** (Page 6): This field is not currently applicable, but it will be used to record the name of the person who reviewed the form.
* **Consent Form Signed By** (Page 6): This field is not currently applicable, but it will be used to record the name of the person who signed the form.
* **Date Signed** (Page 6): Please enter the date you sign the form.
* **Time Signed** (Page 6): Please enter the time you sign the form.
* **Signature Image** (Page 6): This field is not currently applicable, but it will be used to record an image of your signature.
