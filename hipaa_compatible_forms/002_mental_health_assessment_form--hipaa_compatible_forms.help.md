# Mental Health Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help patients assess their mental health. It is a series of questions that gather information about a patient's well-being and experiences to help healthcare professionals better understand their mental state.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. If you're unsure about any question, do not worry, just fill out the best you can.
3. Be as accurate as possible when providing information about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (Text)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents. This will help healthcare professionals know who you are and what information is about you.
* **Age (Number)**: Enter your age. This will help healthcare professionals have a better understanding of your developmental stage and how you may be experiencing your mental health issues.
* **Gender (Select One)**: Choose your gender from the provided options: Female, Male, or Other. This will help healthcare professionals tailor their approach to your needs.
* **Medical History (Text)**: Describe any past medical conditions or experiences that may be affecting your mental health. This can include mental health issues, physical health conditions, or other experiences that impact your well-being.
* **Mental Health Assessment (Note)**: This is a free-text field for you to describe your current mental health situation and any concerns you may have. Please be as thorough as possible and include any details that may be relevant to your mental health.
