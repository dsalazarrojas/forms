# online-doctor-appointment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients for a doctor's online appointment. It gathers essential details for the medical professional to prepare for the appointment and provide quality service.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **patient-info**, including your name, contact information, and any relevant background details.
2.  Select a date and time for your appointment using the provided date and time fields.
3.  Describe your **medical-condition** to help the doctor understand your health situation better.
4.  Provide your **medical-history**, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or medical procedures you've undergone.
5.  List your **medication-list**, including any medications you're currently taking.
6.  Mention your **medical-allergies**, if any.
7.  Add any **appointment-notes** the doctor may find useful to know before your appointment.
8.  Enter your **doctor-info**, including the doctor's name, specialty, or other relevant details.
9.  Provide your **patient-contact** information, including phone number and email.
10. Provide **emergency-contact** information, including the name and phone number of a contact person in case of an emergency.
11. If applicable, select the **medical-guarantor** option to indicate if you have a guarantor for your appointment.
12. Enter the **appointment-time** and **appointment-date** for your scheduled appointment.
13. Confirm your **appointment** by selecting the "I confirm this appointment" option.
14. If you need to, you can also **cancel-appointment** by selecting the "I want to cancel this appointment" option.
15. Add any **additional-notes** the doctor may need to know for your appointment.
