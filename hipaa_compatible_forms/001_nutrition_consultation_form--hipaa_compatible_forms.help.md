# Nutrition Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutrition Consultation Form is designed to collect information from patients for nutrition consultation purposes. It's meant to be used by healthcare professionals to assess the nutritional needs and health status of patients, likely for dietary planning and treatment recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
2. **Medical History**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous conditions, surgeries, or allergies.
3. **Allergies**: List any allergies the patient has, including food or medication allergies.
4. **Current Medications**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
5. **Patient Concerns**: Describe any concerns or questions the patient has about their nutrition or health.
6. **Diet Restrictions**: Describe any dietary restrictions or preferences the patient has (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free).
7. **Food Preferences**: List any foods the patient likes or dislikes.
8. **Patient Goals**: Describe the patient's goals for their nutrition and health.
9. **Medical History Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the patient's medical history.
10. **Allergies Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the patient's allergies.
11. **Medications**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
12. **Patient Outcomes**: Describe the expected outcomes or results of the nutrition consultation.
13. **Next Steps**: Describe the next steps or actions to be taken after the consultation.
14. **Follow-up**: Schedule a follow-up appointment with the patient.
15. **Follow-up Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the follow-up appointment.
16. **Nutrition Plan**: Describe the proposed nutrition plan for the patient.
17. **Patient Status**: Describe the patient's current health status.
18. **Next Procedure**: Describe the next procedure or appointment with the patient.
19. **Next Procedure Date**: Schedule the next procedure or appointment date.
20. **Follow-up Date**: Schedule the follow-up appointment date.
21. **Next Follow-up**: Schedule the next follow-up appointment.
22. **Lab Tests**: List any lab tests or results that need to be performed or reviewed.
23. **Lab Tests Results**: Add any additional notes or comments about the lab tests or results.
24. **Lab Tests Dates**: List any dates related to lab tests or results.
25. **Lab Tests Results Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the lab tests or results.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when completing the form to ensure the best possible outcome for the patient.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you're unsure about any field or question, please consult with the patient or their healthcare provider.
