## Purpose
The medical_report_form is designed to collect medical information from patients for non-emergency or routine purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Review the form carefully and ensure you understand its purpose and requirements.
- Answer each field accurately and truthfully to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): This field is for entering basic patient information, such as name, contact details, or demographics.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select_one, required: false): Select the most relevant medical history from the provided options.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, required: false): List any ongoing or recent medications you are taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required: false): List any allergies you have.
* **Diagnosis** (`diagnosis`, select_one, required: false): Select the reason for your medical visit or condition.
* **Medical Report** (`medical_report`, text, required: false): Provide a brief medical report or summary of your current health status.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, required: false): Outline any recommended or ongoing treatment plans.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether you are the patient or another person signing this form.
* **Medical Report Form** (`medical_report_form`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that this form has been filled out accurately and truthfully.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether you are the patient signing this form.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, select_one, required: false): Confirm the medical provider's involvement in your care.
