# Hipaa Special Enrollment Compliance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hipaa Special Enrollment Compliance Checklist is used to track and verify employee enrollments, approvals, and revocations in the Hipaa program. It helps to ensure that all enrollments are properly documented and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the checklist carefully and ensure all sections are completed accurately.
2. Select the correct compliance status (In Compliance or Not in Compliance) for each enrollment.
3. Provide any comments for the compliance status.
4. Choose the approval status (Approved or Not Approved) for each enrollment.
5. Enter the employee's name and any relevant details.
6. Record the date and time of review and approval.
7. Attach any necessary files or documents.
8. Update the enrollment status (Active or Inactive) and provide any comments.
9. Select whether the enrollment has been revoked (Yes or No).
10. Record the date and time of the last enrollment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Compliance Status**: Choose whether the employee is currently in compliance with the program requirements or not.
* **Compliance Comments**: Provide any comments or reasons for the compliance status.
* **Approval Status**: Choose whether the enrollment is approved or not approved.
* **Employee**: Enter the name of the employee being enrolled.
* **Date Reviewed**: Record the date when the compliance was reviewed.
* **Time Reviewed**: Record the time when the compliance was reviewed.
* **Reviewer**: Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Approval Date**: Record the date when the approval was made.
* **Approval Time**: Record the time when the approval was made.
* **File Name**: Enter the name of the file attached.
* **File Attachment**: Attach any relevant files or documents.
* **Email Attachment**: Attach any relevant email attachments.
* **Phone Attachment**: Attach any relevant phone attachments.
* **Enrollment Status**: Choose whether the employee is currently active or inactive in the program.
* **Enrollment Comments**: Provide any comments or reasons for the enrollment status.
* **Reviewer Comments**: Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **Approval Revoker**: Enter the name of the person who can revoke the approval.
* **Date Enrolled**: Record the date when the employee was initially enrolled.
* **Time Enrolled**: Record the time when the employee was initially enrolled.
* **Enrolled By**: Enter the name of the person who enrolled the employee.
* **Enrollment Revoked**: Choose whether the enrollment has been revoked or not.
* **Employee Since**: Record the date since the employee has been in the program.
* **Last Enrolled Date**: Record the date of the last enrollment.
* **Last Enrolled Time**: Record the time of the last enrollment.

Please review the form carefully and ensure all sections are completed accurately to maintain compliance and avoid any potential issues.
