# Dietary Restrictions Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Dietary Restrictions Form is designed to collect information about a patient's allergies, dietary restrictions, medical conditions, and other relevant health information. This form helps us understand your needs and preferences to provide better care and support. Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure we can tailor our services to your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand what each field is asking for.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as `required`) with accurate and complete information.
3. For fields with options, select the most relevant choice that applies to you.
4. If you have any medical conditions or allergies that are not listed, please describe them in the `Medical Conditions Description` field.
5. Enter your height, weight, and age as accurately as possible.
6. Finally, review your completed form to ensure all fields are filled in correctly before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Allergies** (`patient_allergies`, text, optional):
	+ Please list any allergies you have, including food allergies or environmental allergies.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_one, required):
	+ Select "Allergies" if you have a food allergy.
	+ Select "Other (Please specify)" if you have a dietary restriction that is not listed.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional):
	+ If you have any medical conditions not listed, please describe them here.
* **Medications List** (`medication_list`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Indicate if you are currently taking any medications or supplements.
* **Dietary Lifestyle** (`dietary_lifestyle`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select "Vegetarian" if you follow a vegetarian diet.
	+ Select "Non-Vegetarian" if you follow a non-vegetarian diet.
	+ Select "Other (Please specify)" if you have a different dietary lifestyle.
* **Medical Conditions Description** (`medical_conditions_description`, text, optional):
	+ Briefly describe any medical conditions not listed.
* **Medical Specialist Referral** (`medical_specialist_referral`, text, optional):
	+ If you have been referred to a medical specialist, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Medical Test Results** (`medical_test_results`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the timeframe for when your last medical test was conducted.
* **Date Last Medical Test** (`date_last_medical_test`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of your last medical test.
* **Date Last Medical Check-up** (`date_last_medical_checkup`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of your last medical check-up.
* **Height (in feet)** (`height_feet`, number, optional):
	+ Enter your height in feet and inches.
* **Weight (lbs)** (`weight_lbs`, number, optional):
	+ Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional):
	+ Enter your age in years.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional):
	+ Enter your date of birth.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number.

Note: This form is for your convenience and accuracy, and we appreciate your cooperation. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
