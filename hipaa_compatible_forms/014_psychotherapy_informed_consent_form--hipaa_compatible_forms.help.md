# psychotherapy_informed_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from clients in a psychotherapy setting. It is designed for psychotherapists to use as a template to ensure that clients understand their rights, the therapeutic process, and what to expect during sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form header section to identify the form and its purpose.
2.  Provide the client's name.
3.  Enter the client's contact information (phone number, email, and physical address).
4.  Include the psychotherapist's information (name and any other relevant details).
5.  Instruct the client on the informed consent statement.
6.  Obtain the client's signature to confirm they understand and agree to the informed consent statement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header (1)**: Enter a brief description of the form and its purpose.
* **Client Name (2)**: Enter the client's full name as they would like to be addressed.
* **Contact Information (3)**: Enter the client's contact information (phone number, email, and physical address).
* **Client Contact Number (4)**: Enter the client's phone number where they can be reached.
* **Contact Email (5)**: Enter the client's email address where they can be reached.
* **Contact Address (6)**: Enter the client's physical address where they can be reached.
* **Therapist Information (7)**: Enter the psychotherapist's name and any other relevant details.
* **Informed Consent (8)**: This is a statement that explains the client's rights and what to expect from the therapeutic process. Read and understand this section carefully before signing.
* **Consent Signature (9)**: Obtain the client's signature to confirm they understand and agree to the informed consent statement.
* **Client Signature (10)**: Obtain the client's signature to confirm they have read and understand the informed consent statement.

## Tips
* Always read and explain the informed consent statement to the client before they sign.
* Ensure that the client understands their rights and what to expect from the therapeutic process.
* Store the completed form securely to ensure client confidentiality.
* Review and update the form as needed to ensure it remains relevant and compliant with industry standards.
