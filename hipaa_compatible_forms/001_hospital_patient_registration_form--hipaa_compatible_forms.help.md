# Hospital Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Patient Registration Form is a comprehensive document designed to collect patient information in a HIPAA-compliant manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Complete the Patient Information** page, providing your demographic details such as name, date of birth, and address.
2. **Provide Insurance Information**, if applicable.
3. **Enter Contact Details** such as phone number and email.
4. **List your Medical History**, including any medical conditions, medications, or allergies you may have.
5. **Indicate Medication & Allergy Status**, to ensure we have accurate records.
6. **List Emergency Contact Information**, for us to reach out in case of an emergency.
7. **Provide Medical Procedure Information**, if you've undergone any medical procedures.
8. **Specify Next of Kin Information**, if you have a person to contact on your behalf.
9. **Confirm your Medical History Date**, if applicable.
10. **List your Medications**, if you're taking any.
11. **Indicate Allergy Status**, for our medical staff to be aware.
12. **List Medical Procedures Date**, if you've undergone any medical procedures.
13. **Complete Contact Person Info**, if you have a person to contact.
14. **Submit the form**, once all pages are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1)**: Enter your demographic details such as name, date of birth, and address.
* **Insurance Information (2)**: If you have insurance, please provide the necessary information.
* **Contact Details (3)**: Provide your phone number and email for us to contact you.
* **Medical History (4)**: Describe any medical conditions, medications, or allergies you have.
* **Medication & Allergies (5)**: Indicate your current medication and allergy status.
* **Emergency Contact (6)**: List the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Procedures (7)**: Describe any medical procedures you've undergone.
* **Next of Kin (8)**: Specify the person to contact on your behalf.
* **Next of Kin Info (9)**: Provide more details about your next of kin.
* **Emergency Contact Person (10)**: Choose who is the primary or secondary contact person.
* **Medical Procedures List (11)**: List any medical procedures you've undergone.
* **Patient Signature (12)**: Please sign this form to confirm your information.
* **Doctor Signature (13)**: Our doctor will sign this form once you've been seen.
* **Medical History Date (14)**: Confirm the date of your medical history.
* **Medications (15)**: List any medications you're currently taking.
* **Allergies (16)**: Indicate any allergies you have.
* **Medical Procedure Date (17)**: Confirm the date of your medical procedures.
* **Contact Person Info (18)**: Provide more details about the contact person.
* **Patient Registration Form Submit (19)**: Confirm that you have completed the form accurately.

## Tips

- Ensure that each page is fully completed before proceeding to the next one.
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask us.
