# Medical Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from patients about their medical history, symptoms, and other relevant details. The purpose of this form is to ensure that the patient's medical history is accurately documented and to provide a comprehensive understanding of their health status.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields (indicated by a *) to the best of your ability.
2. For each field, provide as much information as possible.
3. If you are unsure about any field, you can either select "Other" or "Specify" and provide a brief explanation in the text field below.
4. Review each field carefully to ensure that you have provided all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Patient Info** (1, text, required): 
Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant identification information.

### **Medical History** (2, text, required): 
Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, treatments, or surgeries.

### **Past Surgeries** (3, select_multiple, required): 
Select any previous surgeries you have had, including "Heart Surgery". If you have had other surgeries, select "Other (Specify)" and provide a brief explanation.

### **Allergies** (4, select_multiple, required): 
Select any allergies you have, including "Penicillin". If you have other allergies, select "Other (Specify)" and provide a brief explanation.

### **Medical History Info** (5, select_one, required): 
Choose one of the options that best describes your medical history, including "Previous medical conditions" or "Previous medical treatments". If none of the options apply, select "Other (Specify)" and provide a brief explanation.

### **Family History** (6, text, required): 
Enter any relevant family medical history.

### **Medical Genetis** (7, text, required): 
Enter any relevant medical genetic information.

### **Medical Symptoms** (8, text, required): 
Describe any medical symptoms you are currently experiencing.

### **Medical Diagnosis** (9, text, required): 
Enter any known medical diagnosis or conditions.

### **Medical Notes** (10, note, required): 
Provide any additional medical notes or comments.

### **Phone Number** (11, text, required): 
Enter your phone number for contact.

### **Email** (12, email, required): 
Enter your email address for contact.

### **Medical Provider** (13, text, required): 
Enter the name of your current medical provider.

### **Medical Insurer** (14, select_one, required): 
Choose your medical insurance provider, including "Private" or "Medicaid". If you have other insurance, select "Other (Specify)" and provide a brief explanation.

### **Medical Purpose** (15, text, required): 
Describe the reason for your visit or the purpose of your medical treatment.

### **Medical History 2** (16, text, required): 
Describe any additional medical history that is not included in the previous fields.

### **Medical History 3** (17, text, required): 
Describe any further medical history that is not included in the previous fields.

### **Medical Notes 2** (18, note, required): 
Provide any additional medical notes or comments.

### **Medical Provider 2** (19, text, required): 
Enter the name of your previous medical provider.

### **Medical Notes 3** (20, note, required): 
Provide any additional medical notes or comments.

### **Medical Provider 3** (21, text, required): 
Enter the name of your current medical provider.

### **Medical Provider 4** (22, text, required): 
Enter the name of a secondary medical provider.

### **Medical Provider 5** (23, text, required): 
Enter the name of a tertiary medical provider.

### **Medical Notes 4** (24, note, required): 
Provide any additional medical notes or comments.

### **Medical Provider 6** (25, text, required): 
Enter the name of a medical provider.
