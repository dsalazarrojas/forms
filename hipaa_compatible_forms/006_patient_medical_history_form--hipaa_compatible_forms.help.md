# Patient Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document patients' medical history, including personal details, health habits, and medical information. It helps healthcare professionals to understand the patient's overall health status and provide appropriate care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the personal details section with your basic information, such as your name and contact details.
2. Enter your current weight.
3. Check the boxes to indicate if you have any allergies.
4. Provide any additional medical information in the notes section, if necessary.
5. Indicate your healthy and unhealthy habits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info** (personal_info, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Weight** (weight, number, required: true):
	+ Enter your current weight in kilograms (kg) or pounds (lbs).
* **Allergies** (allergies, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Check the box if you have any allergies (e.g., penicillin, latex, etc.).
* **Medical Info** (medical_info, note, required: false):
	+ Add any additional medical information not covered in the previous sections.
* **Healthy Habits** (healthy_habits, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Indicate your healthy habits, such as exercise or following a healthy diet.
* **Unhealthy Habits** (unhealthy_habits, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Check the box if you have any unhealthy habits, such as smoking or excessive eating.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you have any medical notes, try to keep them concise and clear.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
