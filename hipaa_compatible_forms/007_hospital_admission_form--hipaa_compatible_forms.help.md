# Hospital Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information for hospital admissions, ensuring that patients and medical staff have a comprehensive and accurate record of a patient's medical history, contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete the form, start by filling out the patient information section, which includes fields for the patient's name, contact information, and medical history.
2.  Review the medical history section and fill out any relevant medical conditions, allergies, and medications.
3.  In the medical procedure section, provide any relevant medical procedures or treatments the patient is undergoing or has undergone.
4.  In the billing information section, provide any relevant billing information, including insurance details and payment status.
5.  Finally, review the form carefully before signing and submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required): This section is for the patient's name, contact information, and other relevant details.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): This field is for the patient's contact information, including name, phone number, and address.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): This section is for any relevant medical history, including past illnesses, medical conditions, or allergies.
*   **Allergies** (`allergy_list`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any allergies the patient has from the provided options.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Provide the name and contact information for the patient's emergency contact person.
*   **Family History** (`family_history`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant family medical history.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required): List any relevant medical conditions the patient has.
*   **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
*   **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant medical procedures or treatments the patient is undergoing or has undergone.
*   **Surgical History** (`surgical_history`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant surgical history.
*   **Medical Certifications** (`medical_certifications`, `text`, required): List any relevant medical certifications or licenses.
*   **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`, `text`, required): List any medical tests or exams the patient has undergone.
*   **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant insurance information.
*   **Billing** (`billing_info`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant billing information.
*   **Medical Providers** (`medical_providers`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any relevant medical providers.
*   **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, `text`, required): Provide any additional relevant medical history.
*   **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `note`, required): Sign and date the form, indicating that the patient has reviewed and agreed to the information provided.
*   **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, `note`, required): Sign and date the form, indicating that the doctor has reviewed and agreed to the information provided.
*   **Hospital Information** (`hospital_info`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant information about the hospital stay.
*   **Emergency Room Information** (`emergency_room_info`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant information about the emergency room stay.
*   **Discharge Information** (`discharge_info`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant information about the patient's discharge.
*   **Billing Status** (`billing_status`, `text`, required): Indicate the current billing status.
*   **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant payment information.
*   **Insurance** (`insurance_payment`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant insurance information.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required): Add any additional relevant notes or comments about the patient's stay.
