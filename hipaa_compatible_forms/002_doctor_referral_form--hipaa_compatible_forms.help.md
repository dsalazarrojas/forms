# Doctor Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Doctor Referral Form is designed for healthcare professionals to share patient information and track referrals between care providers. This form helps maintain patient confidentiality and ensures accurate communication.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information**:
   - **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
   - **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date): Enter the patient's date of birth.
   - **Address** (`patient_address`, text): Enter the patient's address.
   - **Phone Number** (`patient_phone_number`, text): Enter the patient's phone number.
   - **Specialty** (`doctor_specialty`, select_one): Choose the specialty of the doctor or specialist the patient is being referred to (e.g., General Medicine, Surgery, Cardiology, etc.).

2. **Referral Details**:
   - **Referral Reason** (`patient_referral_reason`, text): Enter the reason for the referral.
   - **Medical Conditions** (`patient_medical_conditions`, text): List the patient's medical conditions.
   - **Medical History** (`patient_medical_history`, text): Provide the patient's medical history.
   - **Allergies** (`patient_allergies`, text): List the patient's allergies.
   - **Referral Comment** (`doctor_referral_comment`, text): Add any comments for the referring doctor.
   - **Referral Confirmation** (`doctor_referral_confirmation`, select_one): Confirm that the referral has been made (True or False).

3. **Agreements**:
   - **Confidentiality Agreement** (`doctor_confidentiality_agreement`, select_one): Confirm that you have a confidentiality agreement in place (True or False).
   - **Patient Confidentiality Agreement** (`patient_confidentiality_agreement`, select_one): Confirm the patient's confidentiality agreement (True or False).
   - **Emergency Contact** (`patient_emergencies_contact`, text): Provide the name and contact information of the patient's emergency contact.
   - **Next of Kin** (`patient_next_of_kin`, text): Enter the name and contact information of the patient's next of kin.
   - **Next of Kin Phone Number** (`patient_next_of_kin_phone_number`, text): Enter the phone number of the patient's next of kin.

4. **Completion and Status**:
   - **Completed By** (`form_completed_by`, text): Enter your name or initials after completing the form.
   - **Referral Status** (`doctor_referral_status`, select_one): Select the current status of the referral (Sent, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled, On Hold, Other).
   - **Patient Referral Status** (`patient_referral_status`, select_one): Select the patient's referral status (Sent, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled, On Hold, Other).
   - **Other Referral Status** (`patient_other_referral_status`, text): Enter any other status of the patient's referral.
   - **Doctor's Note** (`doctor_note`, text): Add any comments or notes from the referring doctor.
   - **Patient's Note** (`patient_note`, text): Add any comments or notes from the patient.
