# Sexually Transmitted Infection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a patient's sexually transmitted infections (STIs) symptoms and history, which will be used to inform diagnosis and treatment decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's details, including their name, age, and contact information.
2. Select the type of STI the patient is experiencing.
3. Report any symptoms the patient is experiencing, selecting all that apply.
4. Enter any relevant medical history related to the STI.
5. Report any test results related to the STI.
6. Indicate your confidence level in the diagnosis.
7. Report any next steps or follow-up care that will be needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details** (`patient_details`): Enter the patient's name, age, and contact information to ensure accurate identification and communication.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`): Select all the symptoms the patient is experiencing, such as fever, headache, fatigue, etc.
* **STI Type** (`sti_type`): Select the type of STI the patient is experiencing from the available options.
* **Other STI** (`sti_type_others`): If the STI type is not listed, enter a different STI type.
* **Symptom Duration** (`symptoms_duration`): Enter the duration of the patient's symptoms.
* **STI History** (`sti_history`): Enter any relevant medical history related to the STI.
* **Test Results** (`test_results`): Report any test results related to the STI, such as positive or negative.
* **STI Confidence Level** (`sti_confidence_level`): Indicate your confidence level in the diagnosis, selecting all that apply.
* **Symptoms 1-10** (`symptoms_1`-`symptoms_10`): Select all the symptoms for each corresponding symptom list.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`): Report any next steps or follow-up care that will be needed.
* **Test and Confirm** (`next_steps_1`): Select all the next steps or follow-up care that will be needed, such as getting tested and confirmed.
* **Follow-up Care** (`next_steps_2`): Report any follow-up care that will be needed.
* **Next Steps 3** (`next_steps_3`): Report any next steps or follow-up care that will be needed.
