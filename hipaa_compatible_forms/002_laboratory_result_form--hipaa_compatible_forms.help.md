# Laboratory Result Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by laboratory staff to document and report laboratory results for patients. It ensures that results are accurately attributed to patients and lab orders, and are easily accessible for medical staff to review and act upon.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the patient's **Patient ID** in the "Patient ID" field. This is a required field and ensures that the results are correctly attributed to the patient.
2. Enter the **Lab Order ID** in the "Lab Order ID" field. This is also a required field and helps to identify the specific lab order being reported.
3. Enter the **Result Date** in the "Result Date" field.
4. Enter the **Result Value** in the "Result Value" field. This is a numeric value, so please use numbers only.
5. Enter the **Test Name** in the "Test Name" field. This is a text field for the name of the test being performed.
6. Enter the **Test Result** in the "Test Result" field. This is a text field for the outcome of the test.
7. Enter the **Lab Name** (optional) in the "Lab Name" field. This field is not required and is primarily for lab staff to provide additional information.
8. Enter the **Physician Name** (optional) in the "Physician Name" field. This field is not required and is primarily for lab staff to indicate the reviewing physician.
9. Enter the **Patient Condition** in the "Patient Condition" field. This is a required field and helps to provide context for the laboratory result.
10. Enter any additional **Medical Note** in the "Medical Note" field. This is a required field and is intended for any additional comments or information related to the laboratory result.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient ID (Required)**: Enter the unique identifier for the patient being tested.
* **Lab Order ID (Required)**: Enter the unique identifier for the lab order being reported.
* **Result Date (Required)**: Enter the date on which the laboratory result was performed.
* **Result Value (Required)**: Enter the numeric value of the laboratory result.
* **Test Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the test being performed.
* **Test Result (Required)**: Enter the outcome of the test (e.g., positive, negative, normal, abnormal).
* **Lab Name (Optional)**: Enter the name of the laboratory where the test was performed. This field is not required.
* **Physician Name (Optional)**: Enter the name of the physician who ordered the lab order. This field is not required.
* **Patient Condition (Required)**: Enter any relevant medical condition or context for the laboratory result.
* **Medical Note (Required)**: Enter any additional comments or information related to the laboratory result.
