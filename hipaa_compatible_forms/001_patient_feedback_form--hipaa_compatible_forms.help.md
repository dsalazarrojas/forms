# Patient Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experience at the clinic. Your feedback is valuable to us and helps us make improvements to provide better care in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in all required fields, if any. Some fields are optional and can be left blank.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the quality of service provided to you today? (Patient Feedback)**
	+ Enter a brief description of your experience with our service.
	+ Be as specific as possible about what we could improve.
* **Who did you meet or interact with today? (Doctor, Nurse, Other)**
	+ Select the person you interacted with during your visit.
	+ If you didn't interact with anyone, select "Other" and provide more information in the "Additional Comments" field.
* **What do you think could be improved about your visit today? (Feedback)**
	+ Enter any comments or suggestions you have about your visit.
	+ Be specific about what we could do to improve your experience.
* **Rate your overall experience with our clinic? (Rating)**
	+ Select one or more of the following ratings:
		- 1: Poor
		- 2: Fair
		- 3: Good
		- 4: Very Good
		- 5: Excellent
* **When did you visit our clinic? (Date)**
	+ Enter the date of your visit in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **At what time did you visit our clinic? (Time)**
	+ Enter the time of your visit in the correct format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Do you have any additional comments or concerns? (Additional Comments)**
	+ Enter any further comments or concerns you have about your visit.
* **Do you have an e-mail address you'd like us to use for correspondence? (Email)**
	+ Enter your email address if you have one.
* **Do you have a phone number where we can contact you? (Phone)**
	+ Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **What would you like us to do next to improve your experience? (Next Steps)**
	+ Enter any suggestions or ideas you have for how we can improve your experience.

## Tips

* Please be honest and specific in your feedback.
* We value your input and will use it to improve our services.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
