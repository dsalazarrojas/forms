# Diet Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diet Consultation Form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking diet consultation services. Please take a few minutes to read through this guide to understand each field's requirements and complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number, and Diet Goals sections.
2. Provide your Medical Condition information.
3. Enter your Height and Weight values.
4. Select one option from the Select One field.
5. Choose one or multiple options from the Select Multiple field.
6. Fill in your Date Of Birth and Time information.
7. Enter a Note (optional).
8. Assign a user to this form (optional).
