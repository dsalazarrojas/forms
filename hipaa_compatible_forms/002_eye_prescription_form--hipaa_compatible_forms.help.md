# Eye Prescription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eye Prescription Form is a medical form designed to collect information about a patient's eye prescription. This form is used to diagnose and treat eye conditions, and the data collected will be used to generate a prescription for the patient's eyes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the options for "Patient Information" to indicate whether the patient has any relevant medical conditions.
2. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Select one of the options for "Prescription Type" to indicate the type of prescription the patient needs.
5. Enter any relevant information about the patient's eye condition in the "Eye Condition" field.
6. Add any additional notes about the patient's prescription in the "Eye Prescription Note" field.
7. Select one of the options for "Refraction Type" to indicate the patient's refraction type.
8. Enter the prescription number in the "Prescription Number" field.
9. Sign the form with the eye doctor's signature in the "Eye Doctor's Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (Yes, No):
	+ This field indicates whether the patient has any relevant medical conditions related to their eyes.
* **Patient Name** (text):
	+ Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (date):
	+ Enter the patient's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Prescription Type** (select_one):
	+ Select one of the options:
		+ Eye Specialist: Prescription for an eye specialist.
		+ Contact Lens: Prescription for contact lenses.
		+ Glasses: Prescription for glasses.
* **Eye Condition** (text):
	+ Enter any relevant information about the patient's eye condition.
* **Eye Prescription Note** (note):
	+ Add any additional notes about the patient's prescription.
* **Prescription Date** (date):
	+ Enter the date of the prescription in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Refraction Type** (select_one):
	+ Select one of the options:
		+ Near-sighted: Patient is near-sighted.
		+ Far-sighted: Patient is far-sighted.
		+ Astigmatism: Patient has astigmatism.
* **Prescription Number** (number):
	+ Enter the prescription number.
* **Eye Doctor's Signature** (text):
	+ Sign the form with the eye doctor's signature.
