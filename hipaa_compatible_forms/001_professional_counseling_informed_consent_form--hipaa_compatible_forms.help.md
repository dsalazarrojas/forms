# Professional Counseling Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from clients before starting professional counseling sessions. It helps establish a clear understanding of the counseling process, risks, and limitations involved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions and provide necessary information accurately and truthfully.
2. Select the relevant emergency contacts and confirm your agreement with the counselor.
3. Date and time of consent should be completed with the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Information** (`client_information`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide your basic information such as name, contact details, or any other relevant details as requested on the form.
*   **Risks and Limitations** (`risks_and_limitations`, `note`, required: `false`): Briefly explain the potential risks and limitations of the counseling process.
*   **Confidentiality** (`confidentiality`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe the confidentiality policy of the counseling sessions.
*   **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): List the emergency contact details of individuals who should be informed in case of an emergency.
*   **Client Agreement** (`client_agreement`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Confirm your agreement with the terms and conditions of the counseling service.
*   **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date you consent to participate in the counseling sessions.
*   **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, `time`, required: `false`): Enter the time you consent to participate in the counseling sessions.
*   **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required: `false`): Sign with your name, indicating your consent.
*   **Counselor Signature** (`counselor_signature`, `text`, required: `false`): Sign with your name, confirming your agreement with the terms.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: `false`): Add any additional notes or comments related to your consent.

## Tips

- Ensure you understand all the fields before completing this form.
- Review and sign only after you're certain about the information and the terms.
- Discuss any questions or concerns with the counselor before signing.
