# Teletherapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients prior to therapy sessions. The form is designed to ensure that patients understand and agree to the terms of their treatment, including obtaining informed consent and consent to share information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the **patient_name** field.
2. Enter your name as the therapist in the **therapist_name** field.
3. Enter the date and time of your therapy session in the **date_of_service** and **time_of_service** fields, respectively.
4. Select the option that best describes your informed consent status in the **consent_to_treatment** field.
5. Check the boxes corresponding to the information you are willing to share in the **consent_to_share_info** field.
6. Enter your signature in the **e_signature** field.
7. Enter your phone number, email address, and physical address in the **phone_number**, **email**, and **address** fields, respectively.
8. Enter your date of birth in the **date_of_birth** field.
9. Enter any relevant medical insurance information in the **patient_medical_insurance** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name**: Enter your name as the patient.
* **therapist_name**: Enter the name of the therapist you are working with.
* **date_of_service**: Enter the date of your therapy session in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **time_of_service**: Enter the time of your therapy session in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **consent_to_treatment**: Check one of the following options:
	+ Informed consent was obtained
	+ No
	+ Other (please specify)
* **consent_to_share_info**: Check the boxes for the types of information you are willing to share, or select **Other (please specify)** and enter your answer in the provided field.
* **e_signature**: Enter your electronic signature.
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **address**: Enter your physical address.
* **date_of_birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **patient_medical_insurance**: Enter any relevant medical insurance information.
