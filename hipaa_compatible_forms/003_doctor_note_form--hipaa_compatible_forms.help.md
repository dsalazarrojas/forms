# Doctor Note Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doctor Note Form is a tool for healthcare providers to document patient information and medical interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **date of visit** field with the date of the patient's visit.
2. Enter the **patient's name** in the corresponding field.
3. Provide your **doctor's name** for accurate identification.
4. Describe the **medical condition** being treated or addressed during the visit.
5. Sign your **doctor's signature** to authenticate the document.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **date_of_visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, required/optional: true): Enter the date of the patient's visit in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **patient_name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required/optional: true): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical record.
* **doctor_name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your own name as the healthcare provider.
* **medical_condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required/optional: true): Describe the medical condition being treated or addressed during the visit.
* **doctor_signature** (`doctor_signature`, `text`, required/optional: false): Sign your name as the healthcare provider to authenticate the document.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure the patient's medical history and treatment are properly recorded.
* Double-check your entries for correct spelling, grammar, and formatting before submitting the form.
* This form is for internal use by healthcare providers only, and its contents should only be shared with the patient with their explicit consent.
* Consult with a medical professional if you are unsure about any information or have concerns about the patient's care.
