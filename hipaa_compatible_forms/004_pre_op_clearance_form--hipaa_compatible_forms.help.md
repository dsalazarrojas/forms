# Pre Op Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Op Clearance Form is used to collect and record important medical information before a patient undergoes surgery or other invasive procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Information" section.
2. Enter the patient's contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Provide details about the patient's medical history in the "Medical Information" section.
4. Provide information about the patient's upcoming surgery or procedure in the "Surgical Information" section.
5. Specify the type of anesthesia the patient will be receiving in the "Anesthesia Information" section.
6. Report any allergies or sensitivities in the "Allergies" section.
7. List any medications the patient is currently taking in the "Medications" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information (2)**: Enter the patient's contact details, such as phone number and address.
* **Medical Information (3)**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any pre-existing conditions or illnesses.
* **Surgical Information (4)**: Provide details about the upcoming surgery or procedure, including the expected date and time.
* **Anesthesia Information (5)**: Specify the type of anesthesia the patient will be receiving.
* **Medical History (6)**: Provide any additional medical information that may be relevant to the patient's care.
* **Allergies (7)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the patient has any allergies or sensitivities.
* **Medications (8)**: List any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
