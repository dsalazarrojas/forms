# Eye Exam Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Eye Exam Form Template" is designed to gather patient information for eye examination purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2.  Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3.  Enter the examination date in the "Examination Date" field.
4.  Enter the doctor's name in the "Doctor Name" field.
5.  Select the patient's gender from the "Gender" dropdown menu.
6.  Enter the patient's age in the "Age" field.
7.  Enter any relevant medical condition in the "Medical Condition" field.
8.  Enter the patient's address in the "Address" field.
9.  Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
10. Enter the patient's email address in the "Email" field.
11. Enter any notes about the examination results in the "Examination Results" field.
12. Enter any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
13. Enter any prescribed medication in the "Medication" field.
14. Enter the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name.
*   **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth.
*   **Examination Date** (`examination_date`, date, optional): Enter the examination date.
*   **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's name.
*   **Gender** (`patient_gender`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's gender.
    *   **Male**: Select if the patient is male.
    *   **Female**: Select if the patient is female.
    *   **Other**: Select if the patient's gender is other.
*   **Age** (`patient_age`, number, optional): Enter the patient's age.
*   **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical condition.
*   **Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
*   **Phone Number** (`patient_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
*   **Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
*   **Examination Results** (`examination_result`, note, optional): Enter notes about the examination results.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments.
*   **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): Enter any prescribed medication.
*   **Medical History** (`patient_medical_history`, text, optional): Enter the patient's medical history.
