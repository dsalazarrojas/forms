# mental_health_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the mental health intake form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each field according to the instructions and explanations provided below.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
3. If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please ask your healthcare provider or contact our support team for assistance.
