# health_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "health_survey" form is a collection of questions designed to gather general information, medical history, and other relevant details about an individual's health and medical conditions. This form aims to provide a comprehensive overview of an individual's health status, which can be used by healthcare professionals or insurance providers to better understand their health and provide suitable care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Ensure you fill in all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk).
3. Select the correct options for each question based on your current status.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`1`): Provide your general information, including any relevant details about your medical status.
* **Medical History** (`2`): If you have any medical history, such as previous injuries or illnesses, please select "Yes" to indicate its existence. 
* **Medications** (`3`): List all the medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`4`): If you have any allergies, please select "Yes" to indicate their existence.
* **Contact Info** (`5`): Provide your contact information, including your date of birth.
* **Medical Conditions** (`6`): If you have any medical conditions, such as diabetes or high blood pressure, please select "Yes" to indicate their existence.
* **Medications 1** (`7`): List any medications you are taking for your medical conditions.
* **Medical History 1** (`8`): If you have any medical history related to your medical conditions, please list the date.
* **Contact Info 1** (`9`): Provide your contact information for any medical conditions or history you have listed.
* **Medical Conditions 1** (`10`): If you have any medical conditions related to your medical conditions, please select "Yes" to indicate their existence.
* **Medications 2** (`11`): List any medications you are taking for your medical conditions.
* **Medical History 2** (`12`): If you have any medical history related to your medical conditions, please list the date.
* **Contact Info 2** (`13`): Provide your contact information for any medical conditions or history you have listed.

## Tips
* Always answer each question truthfully and completely.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
