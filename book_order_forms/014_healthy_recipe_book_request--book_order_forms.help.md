# Healthy Recipe Book Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Healthy Recipe Book Request" form is designed to collect your preferences for a custom-made book with healthy recipes. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that your book is created according to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your First Name (field 1)
- Enter your Last Name (field 2)
- Provide your Email (field 3)
- Enter your Phone Number (field 4)
- Select the book format from the options for each field (fields 5, 6, 9, 10)
- Choose your dietary preferences (field 8)
- Select the desired format for each of the additional formats (fields 7 and 12)
- Enter any additional notes or comments (field 13)
- Optionally, submit a text description for the book (field 14)
- Confirm that you do not want to submit the form (field 15)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name to help us create the book with your preferred title.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name to help us create the book with your preferred title.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your book.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number for any further contact.
- **Format** (`format`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the primary book format (16x6, A4, Letter).
- **Format 1** (`format_1`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select up to 2 formats for the book (16x6, A4, Letter).
- **Format 2** (`format_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select up to 2 formats for the book (16x6, A4, Letter).
- **Diet** (`diet`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your dietary preferences (Vegetarian, Vegan, Omnivore).
- **Format 3** (`format_3`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the desired format for the third option (16x6, A4, Letter).
- **Format 4** (`format_4`, `select_one`, optional): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you want a specific format for the fourth option.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the desired date for the book creation.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the desired time for the book creation.
- **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your book preferences.
- **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Enter a text description for your book.
- **No Submission** (`no_submission_message`, `text`, optional): Confirm that you do not want to submit the form (this field should be avoided if possible).
