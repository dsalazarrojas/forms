# Poetry Book Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is used to collect user information and book orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information in the **User Information** field.
2. List the books you would like to order in the **Book List** field.
3. Enter your payment information in the **Payment Info** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (User Information, text, required: false)**: Enter your name, email, or other relevant user details.
* **Book List (Book List, text, required: false)**: List the titles or ISBNs of the books you would like to order. You can enter multiple books separated by commas or on separate lines.
* **Payment Info (Payment Info, text, required: false)**: Enter your payment method and details, such as credit card number, expiration date, and security code.

## Tips
* Please enter accurate and complete information to ensure smooth processing of your order.
* If you have any questions or issues with your order, contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.
