# Hostess Rewards Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hostess Rewards Form is used to claim rewards for purchasing books in bulk. This form helps customers provide the necessary information to redeem their rewards.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Book Order Form** (text, required): This field is for writing the title of the book you are ordering.
* **Event Date** (date, optional): If you are ordering for an event, enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (time, optional): If you are ordering for an event, enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (text, optional): If you are ordering for an event, enter the location of the event.
* **Book Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the book you are ordering.
* **Book Author** (text, optional): Enter the author of the book you are ordering.
* **Order Quantity** (number, optional): Enter the number of books you are ordering.
* **Total Value** (number, optional): If you are claiming a reward, enter the total value of the order.
* **Reward Type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of reward you are claiming. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **Reward Value** (select_multiple, optional): Select the value of the reward you are claiming. Options include:
	+ Option 5
	+ Option 6
	+ Option 7
	+ Option 8

**Tips**

* Please ensure you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the rewards program.
* Double-check your information for accuracy to ensure a smooth rewards redemption process.
