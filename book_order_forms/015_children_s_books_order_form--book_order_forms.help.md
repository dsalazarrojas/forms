# Children's Books Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for customers to order children's books. It captures essential information for the order, including customer contact details, shipping address, and book preferences. It also includes options for special gifts and add-ons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**. These are required fields.
2. If applicable, enter your organization or school name.
3. Fill in your **Street Address**, **City**, **State or Province**, and **Postal Code**. These are also required fields.
4. Enter your **Country**.
5. Choose the books you would like to order. Select the title and ISBN number of each book.
6. For each book, enter the quantity you would like to order.
7. Choose if you would like to **Add special gifts or bookmarks**.
8. If you selected yes, choose the gift items to include in the order.
9. Answer how often the recipient reads.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Your phone number.
* **Organization or School** (`organization`, `text`, optional): If you are ordering for a school or organization.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): The street address where the order should be shipped.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): The city where the order should be shipped.
* **State or Province** (`state`, `text`, required): The state or province where the order should be shipped.
* **Postal Code** (`zip`, `text`, required): The postal code where the order should be shipped.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): The country where the order should be shipped.
* **Book Title** (`book_one_title`, `text`, optional): The title of the first book.
* **ISBN Number** (`book_one_isbn`, `text`, optional): The ISBN number of the first book.
* **Quantity** (`book_one_qty`, `number`, optional): The quantity of the first book.
* **Book Two Title** (`book_two_title`, `text`, optional): The title of the second book.
* **Book Two Isbn** (`book_two_isbn`, `text`, optional): The ISBN number of the second book.
* **Book Two Qty** (`book_two_qty`, `number`, optional): The quantity of the second book.
* **Add special gifts or bookmarks** (`include_gifts`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you would like to add special gifts or bookmarks.
* **Gift items to include** (`gift_items`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the gift items to include if you selected yes.
* **How often does the recipient read** (`reading_habits`, `select_one`, optional): Answer how often the recipient reads.

Note: Some fields are optional, but they are necessary to ensure accurate and efficient processing of your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you selected to add special gifts or bookmarks, choose carefully to ensure the gift items are relevant to the recipient's reading habits.
* If you have any questions or need help with your order, contact us at [support email or contact information].
