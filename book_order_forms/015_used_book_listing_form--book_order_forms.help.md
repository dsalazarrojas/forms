# Used Book Listing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a detailed listing for used books. It will help users to accurately showcase their books, attracting more customers.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the instructions and guidelines provided above.
- Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Used Book Listing Form** (`Used Book Listing Form`, text, required: false)
- This is the title of the book listing.
- A brief and descriptive title should be provided to help users quickly identify the book.

### 2. **Book Title** (`book_title`, text, required: false)
- Enter the title of the book.
- Make sure to enter the correct title, including any subtitles or series information.

### 3. **Book Author** (`book_author`, text, required: false)
- Enter the author's name.
- Use the correct spelling and format as it appears on the book cover or title page.

### 4. **Book ISBN** (`book_isbn`, text, required: false)
- Enter the International Standard Book Number (ISBN).
- This helps customers verify the book's edition and accuracy.

### 5. **Price** (`book_price`, number, required: false)
- Enter the price of the book in the selected currency.
- Make sure to include any taxes, handling fees, or shipping costs.

### 6. **Condition** (`condition`, select_one, required: false)
- Choose the condition of the book from the options provided: Excellent, Good, or Fair.
- Assess the book's condition accurately to provide honest and reliable information.

### 7. **Condition Notes** (`condition_notes`, text, required: false)
- Provide additional comments or notes about the book's condition.
- This helps customers understand the book's condition better.

### 8. **Book Copyright Year** (`book_copyright_year`, number, required: false)
- Enter the year the book was published.
- This helps customers determine the book's age and relevance.

### 9. **Book Publisher** (`book_publisher`, text, required: false)
- Enter the book's publisher name.
- Use the correct spelling and format as it appears on the book cover or title page.

### 10. **Year of Publication** (`book_year_of_publication`, date, required: false)
- Enter the year the book was published.
- Use the correct date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### 11. **Year of Publication End** (`book_year_of_publication_end`, date, required: false)
- Enter the year the book's publication ended.
- This helps customers understand the book's relevance and availability.

### 12. **Year of Publication Start** (`book_year_of_publication_start`, date, required: false)
- Enter the year the book's publication started.
- This helps customers understand the book's relevance and availability.

### 13. **Used Book Image** (`used_book_image`, text, required: false)
- Enter the URL or file path of the book's image.
- Use a clear and high-quality image to showcase the book.

### 14. **Book Cover Image** (`book_cover_image`, text, required: false)
- Enter the URL or file path of the book's cover image.
- Use a clear and high-quality image to showcase the book's cover.

### 15. **Other Book Image** (`book_image_other`, text, required: false)
- Enter the URL or file path of any other relevant book image.
- Use a clear and high-quality image to showcase the book's other content.

### 16. **Is E-Book** (`book_is_ebook`, select_one, required: false)
- Choose whether the book is an E-book or not.
- This helps customers determine the book's format and availability.

### 17. **Is Audiobook** (`book_is_audiobook`, select_one, required: false)
- Choose whether the book is an audiobook or not.
- This helps customers determine the book's format and availability.

### 18. **Is Textbook** (`book_is_textbook`, select_one, required: false)
- Choose whether the book is a textbook or not.
- This helps customers determine the book's format and availability.

### 19. **Price Currency** (`book_price_currency`, select_multiple, required: false)
- Select the currency of the book's price.
- This ensures customers understand the price correctly.

### 20. **Other Price Currency** (`book_price_currency_other`, text, required: false)
- Enter any other currency for the book's price.
- Use the correct abbreviation or symbol for the currency.

### 21. **Shipping Cost** (`book_shipping_cost`, number, required: false)
- Enter the shipping cost for the book.
- Make sure to include any additional fees or taxes.

### 22. **Handling Fee** (`book_handling_fee`, number, required: false)
- Enter the handling fee for the book.
- Make sure to include any additional fees or taxes.

### 23. **Other Fees** (`book_other_fees`, text, required: false)
- Enter any other fees associated with the book.
- Use a clear and descriptive format.

### 24. **Description** (`book_description`, text, required: false)
- Enter a brief and accurate description of the book.
- This helps customers understand the book's content and condition.

### 25. **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false)
- Enter your contact details, including email or phone number.
- This helps customers contact you for more information or purchase the book.
