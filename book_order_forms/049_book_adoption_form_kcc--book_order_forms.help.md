# Book Adoption Form - KCC - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Book Adoption Form is a necessary step in the book adoption process for KCC.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure that all fields are filled in completely.
2. Select the relevant options for each field.
3. Provide accurate information for the book adoption.
4. Review the submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Date** (submission_date, date, required): The date for submission of this form.
* **Instructor Name** (instructor_name, text, required): Enter your name as the instructor.
* **Instructor Email** (instructor_email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Department** (department, text, required): Enter your department name.
* **Course Number** (course_number, text, required): Enter the course number for the course you are adopting the book for.
* **Course Title** (course_title, text, required): Enter the title of the course you are adopting the book for.
* **Section Number** (section_number, text, required): Enter the section number of the course you are adopting the book for.
* **Term of Adoption** (term, select_one, required): Choose the term of adoption.
* **Estimated Enrollment** (enrollment_estimate, number, required): Enter the estimated number of students in the course.
* **Current Textbook ISBN** (current_textbook_isbn, text, optional): Enter the ISBN of the current textbook if applicable.
* **Current Textbook Title** (current_textbook_title, text, optional): Enter the title of the current textbook if applicable.
* **Requested Textbook ISBN** (requested_textbook_isbn, text, required): Enter the ISBN of the requested textbook.
* **Requested Textbook Title** (requested_textbook_title, text, required): Enter the title of the requested textbook.
* **Textbook Author** (requested_textbook_author, text, required): Enter the author of the requested textbook.
* **Textbook Edition** (requested_textbook_edition, text, required): Enter the edition of the requested textbook.
* **Textbook Requirement Status** (textbook_requirement_status, select_one, required): Select the textbook requirement status.
* **Alternative Formats Available** (alternative_formats, select_multiple, optional): Select the alternative formats available for the book.
* **Additional Materials** (additional_materials, text, optional): Enter additional required materials such as solution manuals or lab manuals.
* **Materials on Course Reserve** (course_reserves, select_one, required): Choose whether materials will be placed on course reserve.
* **Using Open Educational Resources** (open_educational_resources, select_one, required): Choose whether to use open educational resources.
* **Rental Option Preference** (rental_option_preference, select_one, required): Choose whether to support the rental option.
* **Comments About Prior Adoption** (prior_adoption_comments, text, optional): Enter comments about why the book adoption is being changed or continued.
* **Cost Considerations** (cost_considerations, text, optional): Enter affordability concerns.
* **I Confirm the Above Information is Accurate** (confirmation_accuracy, select_one, required): Confirm that the information is accurate.
* **Submission Method** (submission_method_confirmation, select_one, required): Choose the submission method.
