# personal_invoice_the_don - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for users to provide personal details for book delivery or customer service purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **User name** in the field provided.
2. Enter your **Email address** in the field provided.
3. Fill in your **Street address** in the field provided.
4. Enter your **City** in the field provided.
5. Fill in your **State** in the field provided.
6. Enter your **Zip code** in the number field provided.
7. Fill in your **Phone number** in the field provided.
8. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format 'mm/dd/yyyy'.
9. Enter your **Time of Birth** in 24-hour format ('HH:MM' or 'HH:MM:SS').
10. Fill in your **E-sign** (likely a signature, but this is a weak field label).
11. Fill in your address lines as follows:
	* **Address Line 1**: This is the street address.
	* **Address Line 2**: This is an optional additional line for address details.
	* ... (Continue with **Address Line 3** to **Address Line 10** for more address lines, but these are not required)
12. Fill in your **Zip code** in the field (this is the same as step 6, consider removing for consistency).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be written on the book.
* **Email**: Enter the email address associated with your account.
* **Street address**: This is the main address where you would like to receive your book.
* **City**: Enter the city where your address is located.
* **State**: Enter the state or province where your address is located.
* **Zip code**: Enter the zip code or postal code of your address.
* **Phone number**: Enter your contact phone number for any follow-up questions.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in 'mm/dd/yyyy' format.
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth in 24-hour format ('HH:MM' or 'HH:MM:SS').
* **E-sign**: This might be a signature or a unique identifier, but its purpose is unclear, so proceed with caution.
* **Address Line 1 - 10**: These are additional address lines for any further details you want to provide about your address.
* **Zip code**: This is redundant and the same as step 6, consider removing for consistency.
