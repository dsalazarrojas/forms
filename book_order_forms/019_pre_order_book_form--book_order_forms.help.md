# Pre Order Book Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Order Book Form is a tool used to collect information from customers who wish to pre-order a book. This form is designed to gather essential details such as customer contact information, order details, and shipping information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
	* Enter your name in the customer_name field.
	* Enter your email address in the customer_email field.
	* Enter your phone number in the customer_phone field.
	* Enter the title of the book you wish to pre-order in the book_title field.
	* Enter the quantity of books you wish to pre-order in the quantity_ordered field.
	* Enter the price of the book in the book_price field.
	* Enter your shipping address in the shipping_address field.
	* Enter the city of your shipping address in the shipping_city field.
	* Enter the province of your shipping address in the shipping_province field.
	* Enter your postal code in the shipping_postal_code field.
	* If you have any additional comments, enter them in the customer_comment field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Your name is required for us to process your pre-order.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address, which we will use to contact you about your order.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Your phone number will help us contact you if we need to confirm any details.
- **Book Title** (`book_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the book you wish to pre-order.
- **Quantity Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`, number, required): Enter the number of books you wish to pre-order.
- **Book Price** (`book_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the book you wish to pre-order.
- **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter the address where you would like your book to be shipped.
- **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, required): Enter the city where you would like your book to be shipped.
- **Shipping Province** (`shipping_province`, text, required): Enter the province where you would like your book to be shipped.
- **Shipping Postal Code** (`shipping_postal_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code where you would like your book to be shipped.
- **Customer Comment** (`customer_comment`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to enter them here. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that you enter all required fields.
- Double-check that your email address is correct to ensure timely communication.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.
