# Book Purchase Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm the details of a book purchase, including customer information, order details, and payment status. It is essential to ensure that all information is accurate to prevent errors in the order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide all required information, marked with an asterisk (*), and ensure it is accurate.
2. Review the order details, including book title, author, format, quantity, and price.
3. Select the shipping method and estimated delivery date.
4. Choose the payment method and confirm the payment status.
5. Optionally, add a gift message or special instructions.
6. Confirm that the order details are correct by selecting "Yes I confirm" and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (1)** (Text, Required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address (2)** (Email, Required): We will send confirmation and other important updates to this email address.
* **Phone Number (3)** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for order inquiries.
* **Order Number (4)** (Text, Required): Enter the order reference number provided to you.
* **Order Date (5)** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the order.
* **Book Title (6)** (Text, Required): Enter the full title of the book.
* **Book Author (7)** (Text, Required): Enter the author of the book.
* **Book ISBN (8)** (Text, Optional): If available, enter the ISBN number of the book.
* **Book Format (9)** (Select, Required): Choose the format of the book (Hardcover, Paperback, eBook, Audiobook, or Deluxe Edition).
* **Quantity (10)** (Number, Required): Enter the number of copies you purchased.
* **Price Per Book (11)** (Number, Required): Enter the price per book.
* **Subtotal (12)** (Number, Required): The total cost before tax and shipping.
* **Tax Amount (13)** (Number, Required): The tax applied to the order.
* **Shipping Cost (14)** (Number, Required): The shipping charges.
* **Total Amount (15)** (Number, Required): The final total cost.
* **Shipping Address (16)** (Text, Required): Enter the full delivery address.
* **City (17)** (Text, Required): Enter your city.
* **State (18)** (Text, Required): Enter your state or province.
* **Postal Code (19)** (Text, Required): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Country (20)** (Select, Required): Choose the country of delivery.
* **Shipping Method (21)** (Select, Required): Choose the shipping method (Standard 5-7 days, Express 2-3 days, Overnight, or International).
* **Estimated Delivery Date (22)** (Date, Required): Choose the estimated delivery date.
* **Payment Method (23)** (Select, Required): Choose the payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Apple Pay, or Google Pay).
* **Payment Status (24)** (Select, Required): Confirm the current payment status (Paid, Pending, or Processing).
* **Gift Message (25)** (Text, Optional): If you want to add a gift message, enter it here.
* **Special Instructions (26)** (Text, Optional): Add any special instructions for delivery.
* **I Confirm This Order Is Correct (27)** (Select, Required): Confirm that all details are correct.
* **I Agree to Terms and Conditions (28)** (Select, Required): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.
