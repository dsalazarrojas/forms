# Afterpay Book Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Afterpay Book Order Form" is a form designed to collect information for a book order, including customer details, book specifics, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer name** in the "Customer name" field. This is the name that will be associated with the order.
2. Enter your **Email address** in the "Customer email" field. This will receive order confirmation and updates.
3. Enter your **Billing address** in the "Billing address" field. This should include your street, city, and postal code.
4. Select the **Book title** and enter the **Quantity** of copies you would like to order.
5. Choose your preferred **Format**: Physical, Paperback, or eBook.
6. Select whether you would like to use **Afterpay** for payment, splitting the payment into installments.
7. If necessary, provide **Delivery instructions** for the courier, such as specific delivery times or instructions.
8. If you have any **Special requests**, such as gift wrapping or personalization, enter them in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer name**: Your full name as it should appear on the order record.
* **Customer email**: The email address that will receive order confirmation and updates.
* **Billing address**: The address where the book will be shipped, including street, city, and postal code.
* **Book title**: The title of the book you would like to order.
* **Quantity**: The number of copies you would like to order.
* **Preferred format**: Choose between Physical, Paperback, or eBook for the book format.
* **Use Afterpay**: Select to split the payment into installments using Afterpay.
* **Delivery instructions**: Optional directions for the courier, such as delivery times or specific instructions.
* **Special requests**: Optional details for gift wrapping or personalization.
