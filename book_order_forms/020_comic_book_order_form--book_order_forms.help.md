# Comic Book Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Comic Book Order Form is designed for customers to place comic book orders with the option to choose from various comic book titles, payment methods, and shipping preferences. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information:
	* Provide your full name in the **Customer Name** field.
	* Enter your email address in the **Email Address** field.
	* Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
	* Enter your shipping address in the **Shipping Address** field.
2. Select your comic book preferences:
	* Choose your favorite **Marvel New Release** from the list.
	* Choose your favorite **DC New Release** from the list.
	* Choose your favorite **Image Comics New Release** from the list.
3. Select your comic book issues:
	* Choose the number of **Spider-Man Issues** you'd like to purchase (0-5).
	* Choose the number of **Batman Issues** you'd like to purchase (0-5).
	* Choose the number of **X-Men Issues** you'd like to purchase (0-5).
	* Choose the number of **Avengers Issues** you'd like to purchase (0-5).
4. Select your graphic novels and manga:
	* Choose up to 3 **Graphic Novels** you'd like to purchase (e.g. Watchmen, Dark Knight Returns).
	* Choose up to 3 **Manga Volumes** you'd like to purchase (e.g. One Piece, Naruto).
5. Choose your subscription plan:
	* Select from **No Subscription**, **Basic (3 comics/month)**, **Standard (5 comics/month)**, **Premium (10 comics/month)**, or **Unlimited Digital**.
6. Choose your payment method:
	* Select your preferred **Payment Method** (e.g. Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay).
7. Choose your shipping method:
	* Select your preferred **Shipping Method** (e.g. Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, Overnight Shipping, Store Pickup).
8. Choose a bag and board service (if desired):
	* Select **Yes Please** for protective covering for comics.
9. Enter any special instructions:
	* Enter any special requests or comments in the **Special Instructions** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for customer support and order inquiries.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your complete mailing address for delivery.
* **Marvel New Release**: Choose the latest Marvel comic book title.
* **DC New Release**: Choose the latest DC comic book title.
* **Image Comics New Release**: Choose the latest Image comic book title.
* **Spider-Man Issues**: Choose the number of Spider-Man issues you'd like to purchase (0-5).
* **Batman Issues**: Choose the number of Batman issues you'd like to purchase (0-5).
* **X-Men Issues**: Choose the number of X-Men issues you'd like to purchase (0-5).
* **Avengers Issues**: Choose the number of Avengers issues you'd like to purchase (0-5).
* **Graphic Novel Selection**: Choose up to 3 graphic novels you'd like to purchase.
* **Manga Volumes**: Choose up to 3 manga volumes you'd like to purchase.
* **Subscription Plan**: Choose a subscription plan that suits your needs.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Shipping Method**: Choose your preferred shipping method.
* **Bag and Board Service**: Choose whether you'd like a protective covering for your comics.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special requests or comments for your order.
* **Subtotal**: Review your order subtotal.
* **Shipping Cost**: Review your shipping cost.
* **Total Amount**: Review your final order total.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are filled accurately to avoid order issues.
* Select a subscription plan that suits your reading preferences.
* Check your shipping cost and ensure it's within your budget.
* Enter any special instructions to ensure a smooth order fulfillment process.
