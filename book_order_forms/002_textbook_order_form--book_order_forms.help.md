# textbook_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers to place a textbook order. It is meant to gather details such as the price of the book, the student's photo (if applicable), and their email and phone number for communication and order confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the price of the book in the **price** field.
2. Provide any additional information about the student's photo in the **photo** field.
3. Enter a brief description of the textbook in the **textbook_description** field.
4. Select whether payment will be made **Yes** or **No** in the **payment_method** field.
5. Enter any applicable **coupon_code** for a discount.
6. Add any **order_notes** for the customer.
7. Enter the customer's **customer_email** address.
8. Enter the customer's **customer_phone** number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Price** (Number, Required)
The price of the book being ordered.
### **Photo** (Text, Optional)
Any additional information about the student's photo, such as its size or resolution.
### **Textbook Description** (Text, Required)
A brief description of the textbook being ordered, including the title and author.
### **Payment Method** (Select One, Required)
Select **Yes** to indicate that payment will be made, or **No** to indicate otherwise.
### **Coupon Code** (Text, Optional)
Any applicable coupon code for a discount on the textbook.
### **Order Notes** (Note, Optional)
Any additional notes or comments about the order.
### **Customer Email** (Email, Required)
The customer's email address for communication and order confirmation.
### **Customer Phone** (Text, Required)
The customer's phone number for contact and order confirmation.
### **Tips**

* Make sure to enter the correct email and phone number for the customer.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact support for assistance.
* This form is meant to simplify the ordering process, so please fill it out clearly and accurately.
