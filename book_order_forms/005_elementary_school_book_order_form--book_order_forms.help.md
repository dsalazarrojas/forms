# Elementary School Book Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order books for your school's students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Enter the name of your school.
2.  Enter a valid email address for your school (this is not required but is an optional field).
3.  Choose the option you want for "Do you want to receive a book list for students who do not meet the required grade level?"
4.  Select the grade level for "What grade level do you want the books to be tailored to?"
5.  Choose a date to receive the books.
6.  Enter a note for the order if you want to.
7.  Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your school.
- **school_name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the school that the books are for.
- **school_email** (Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address for your school. This is an optional field.
- **form_4** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose "Yes" if you want to receive a book list for students who do not meet the required grade level.
- **form_5** (Select One, Not Required): Select the grade level you want the books to be tailored to.
- **form_6** (Select One, Not Required): This field is currently not used and can be ignored.
- **form_7** (Date, Required): Choose a date to receive the books.
- **form_8** (Time, Not Required): This field is currently not used and can be ignored.
- **note** (Note, Not Required): Enter a note for the order if you want to.
- **form_10** (Text, Not Required): This field is currently not used and can be ignored.
- **school_phone** (Text, Not Required): This field is currently not used and can be ignored.
