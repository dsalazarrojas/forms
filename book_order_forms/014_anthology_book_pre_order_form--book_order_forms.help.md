# Anthology Book Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect pre-order details from customers who want to purchase an anthology book. The form will ask for customer contact information, shipping details, pre-order quantity, edition preference, payment method, and special instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all necessary information before submitting the form.
* Fill out the form completely, but only the required fields are necessary.
* Enter your contact email and name in the "Contact email" and "Customer name" fields.
* Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping address" field.
* Select the number of copies to pre-order in the "Quantity to pre order" field.
* Select your preferred edition and payment method.
* If you have any special instructions or gift messaging, enter them in the "Special instructions for order" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name for billing and shipping purposes.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address that will be used for order confirmation.
* **Shipping address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter your street address, city, state, and postal code.
* **Quantity to pre order** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of copies you want to pre-order.
* **Edition preference** (`edition_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred edition (Hardcover, Paperback, eBook).
* **Preferred payment method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method (Credit card, PayPal, Invoice).
* **Special instructions for order** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or gift messaging for your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email and shipping address for accuracy.
* If you select Invoice as your payment method, please contact us for further instructions.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at [support email].
