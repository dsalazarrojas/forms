# Contact I Reviews - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information and inquiries related to book reviews and SEO services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Select the topic of your inquiry from the list of options.
3. Provide details about your message in the message box.
4. (Optional) If you are inquiring about a specific book, fill in the book title, author name, and ISBN number.
5. Select the priority level of your inquiry (Normal or Urgent).
6. Finally, select the date of your inquiry and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`user_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (`user_email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
* **Book Title** (`book_title`, Text, Optional): If you are inquiring about a specific book, type its title here.
* **Author Name** (`author_name`, Text, Optional): If you are inquiring about a specific book, type the author's name here.
* **ISBN Number** (`isbn_number`, Text, Optional): If you are inquiring about a specific book, type its ISBN number here.
* **Inquiry Topic** (`inquiry_topic`, Select One, Required): Select the topic of your inquiry. Options include: Privacy Policy, Review Request, SEO Services, General Question.
* **Message Details** (`message_details`, Text, Required): Provide details about your message.
* **Priority** (`priority_level`, Select One, Optional): Select the priority level of your inquiry. Options include: Normal, Urgent.
* **Date of Inquiry** (`contact_date`, Date, Required): Select the date of your inquiry.
* **Book Review and SEO Inquiry** (`review_inquiry_header`, Note, Optional): This field is not a field you need to fill in, but rather a heading to guide you through the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are inquiring about a specific book, make sure to fill in the book title, author name, and ISBN number for accurate reference.
* If you select the "Urgent" priority level, we will respond to your inquiry as soon as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, feel free to contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
