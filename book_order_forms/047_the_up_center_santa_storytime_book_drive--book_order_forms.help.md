# The Up Center "Santa Storytime" Book Drive! - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "The Up Center "Santa Storytime" Book Drive!" form is designed to collect information from donors and recipients of books for an internal book drive event. This form helps to gather details about the book drive, such as book titles, quantities, and types, as well as contact information from donors and recipients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the "Book Drive Information" details in the first field.
2. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Provide the title of the book(s) you'd like to donate in the "Book Title" field.
7. Enter the quantity of books you'd like to donate in the "Quantity" field.
8. Select the type of book(s) you're donating (Book, Audio Book, or E-Book) from the "Book Type" dropdown menu.
9. If you're a donor, enter your name in the "Donor Name" field.
10. Provide your address in the "Address" field.
11. If you're using a gift card, enter the gift card number in the "Gift Card Number" field.
12. Select the type of gift card you're using (Visa, Mastercard, or Amex) from the "Card Type" dropdown menu.
13. Enter any additional message or comments in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Book Drive Information (1)**: Enter details about the book drive event.
* **First Name (2)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (3)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (4)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (5)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Book Title (6)**: Enter the title of the book(s) you'd like to donate.
* **Quantity (7)**: Enter the number of books you'd like to donate.
* **Book Type (8)**: Select the type of book(s) you're donating (Book, Audio Book, or E-Book).
* **Donor Name (9)**: If you're a donor, enter your name.
* **Address (10)**: Enter your address.
* **Gift Card Number (11)**: If you're using a gift card, enter the gift card number.
* **Card Type (12)**: Select the type of gift card you're using (Visa, Mastercard, or Amex).
* **Message (13)**: Enter any additional message or comments.
