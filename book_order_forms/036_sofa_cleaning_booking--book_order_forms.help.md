# sofa_cleaning_booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide helps you understand the sofa cleaning form. Please review each field to ensure you provide accurate information about your sofa cleaning needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each field and fill in your answers accordingly. You may skip fields that do not apply to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **benefits** (`benefits`, text, optional): Please describe the benefits you are looking for when cleaning your sofa (e.g., "to get rid of stains" or "to make it look new").
* **equipment** (`equipment`, text, optional): Mention any equipment you use for sofa cleaning (e.g., vacuum cleaner, brush).
* **disinfectant_chemicals** (`disinfectant_chemicals`, select multiple, optional): Select all disinfectant chemicals you use for cleaning your sofa (e.g., "Yes" if you use them, "No" otherwise).
* **vacuuming_machine** (`vacuuming_machine`, select one, optional): Choose the type of machine you use for vacuuming your sofa (e.g., "Disinfectant Chemicals", "Vacuuming Machine", or "Buffing Machine").
* **chemical_spraying** (`chemical_spraying`, text, optional): Describe your use of chemical spraying for cleaning your sofa (e.g., "often" or "rarely").
* **leather_sofas** (`leather_sofas`, select one, optional): Indicate if your sofa is made of leather (e.g., "True" if it is, "False" otherwise).
* **stain_removal** (`stain_removal`, select multiple, optional): Select all stain removal methods you use (e.g., "Yes" if you use them, "No" otherwise).
* **embedded_dust_mites** (`embedded_dust_mites`, select one, optional): Check if you suspect embedded dust mites in your sofa (e.g., "True" if you do, "False" otherwise).
* **fresh_look** (`fresh_look`, select one, optional): Indicate if you want a "fresh look" after cleaning your sofa (e.g., "True" if you do, "False" otherwise).
* **food_particles** (`food_particles`, select one, optional): Check if you are concerned about food particles on your sofa (e.g., "True" if you are, "False" otherwise).

## Tips
Please ensure you provide accurate and honest answers to help us better understand your sofa cleaning needs. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
