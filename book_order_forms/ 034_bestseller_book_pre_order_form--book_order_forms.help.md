# Bestseller Book Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bestseller Book Pre Order Form is designed for customers to express their interest in purchasing specific books from the Bestseller series. This form will help us better understand your requirements and preferences for these books.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select your preferred book (Product A, Product B, Product C) from the Primary selection field.
3. If you wish to provide a quantity, enter a whole number in the Quantity or rating field.
4. Read and agree to the terms of service provided in the Agreement field.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes in the Final comments or notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (2, `text`, **required**): Enter your legal full name.
* **Email address** (3, `email`, **required**): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone number** (4, `text`, **optional**): Include the country code if you are outside the local region.
* **Date** (5, `date`, **required**): Select the relevant date for the pre-order.
* **Primary selection** (6, `select_one`, **required**): Select the book you wish to pre-order from the given options.
* **Quantity or rating** (7, `number`, **optional**): Provide a whole number for the quantity you wish to purchase or the rating you wish to give.
* **Agreement** (9, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select all that apply from the given options.
* **Estimated value** (10, `number`, **optional**): Use the decimal format if necessary.
* **Preferred time** (11, `time`, **optional**): Enter a time if the scheduling is required.
* **Final comments or notes** (12, `text`, **optional**): Provide any final comments or remarks about the pre-order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately for a smooth processing of your pre-order.
* Be precise when providing any additional comments or notes to ensure we can best fulfill your requirements.
* If you experience any issues with the form, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* This form is only accessible when the relevant products are available, so please refer to the product listing for more information on availability.
