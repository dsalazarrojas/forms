# School Book Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information and order details for school book orders. It is meant to be a simple, user-friendly process for users to submit their orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information in the "User Information" field.
2. Enter your order details in the "Order Information" field.
3. Specify the book information in the "Book Information" field.
4. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Information" field.
5. Click "Submit" to confirm your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required: false): Please enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
- **Order Information** (`order_info`, text, required: false): Provide details about the order, such as the type of books needed and any special requests.
- **Book Information** (`book_info`, text, required: false): Specify the books you wish to order, including titles, authors, and quantities.
- **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required: false): Enter your payment method and details, including credit card numbers and expiration dates.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): This is the final step to confirm your order. Please review your information before submitting.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- Ensure all required fields are completed, even if they are not marked as required.
- If you experience any issues or have questions, contact the form creator for assistance.
