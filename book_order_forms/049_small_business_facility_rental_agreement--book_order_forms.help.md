# small_business_facility_rental_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for small businesses to request a rental agreement for a facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the business location in the **Business Location** field.
2. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the rental in the **Name** field.
3. Select the contact person from the list in the **Contact Person** field.
4. Enter the email address of the contact person in the **Email** field.
5. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the **Phone** field.
6. Choose the type of facility you are interested in renting by selecting the **Facility Type** option (e.g. Conference, Training, Event).
7. Select the size of the facility you need by choosing the **Facility Size** options (e.g. Small, Medium, Large).
8. Choose the start time for your event by selecting the **Start Time** option (e.g. Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
9. Choose the end time for your event by selecting the **End Time** option (e.g. 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours).
10. Enter the duration of your event in the **Duration** field.
11. Enter any notes or comments about your rental request in the **Notes** field.
12. Enter any comments or additional information about your rental request in the **Comments** field.
13. Select the rental agreement terms you are interested in by choosing the **Rental Agreement** options (e.g. Month-to-Month, Short-Term, Long-Term).
14. Enter the contact information of the person or company responsible for the rental in the **Contact Info** field.
15. Enter a description of the facility you are interested in renting in the **Facility** field.
16. Select the rental terms you are interested in by choosing the **Rental Terms** options (e.g. Month-to-Month, Short-Term, Long-Term).
17. Select the payment method you plan to use for the rental in the **Payment Method** options (e.g. Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Check).
18. Enter the total cost of the rental in the **Total Cost** field.
19. Select the payment status of the rental by choosing the **Payment Status** option (e.g. Paid, Not Paid, Pending).
20. Select the status of the facility by choosing the **Facility Status** options (e.g. Available, Not Available, Pending).
21. Enter any additional comments or information in the **Report** field (optional).
