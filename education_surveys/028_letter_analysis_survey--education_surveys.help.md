# Letter Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Letter Analysis Survey is used to collect feedback and ratings about a letter in the education context. It is designed to help instructors or educators evaluate the clarity, tone, and effectiveness of a letter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are using this form for the intended purpose and not for personal or other non-educational purposes.
2. Review the letter carefully and consider your thoughts on its clarity, tone, and effectiveness.
3. Enter your ratings for Clarity, Tone, and Effectiveness on the scale provided.
4. Provide any comments or feedback in the Comments field if you feel it's necessary.
5. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) so we can reach out to you with any follow-up questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Letter Analysis** (`letter_analysis_survey_form` , text, required/optional): This field allows you to enter the title of the letter you are evaluating.
* **Clarity** (`clarity_rating`, number, optional): Rate the clarity of the letter on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Tone** (`tone_rating`, number, optional): Rate the tone of the letter on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Effectiveness** (`effectiveness_rating`, number, optional): Rate the effectiveness of the letter on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the letter.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any follow-up questions.
* **File** (`file`, text, optional): If you have any attachment or additional information related to the letter, you can upload it here.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing ratings and comments.
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for support.
