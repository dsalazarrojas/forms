# educational_leadership_culture_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather valuable insights from educational leaders in an institution to help improve the school culture, leadership, and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each page carefully and ensure you have an understanding of the questions.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Be specific with your responses, and provide examples whenever possible.
