# islamic_teachings_and_modernity_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and opinions on Islamic teachings and modernity. This information will help us understand the community's views on how Islamic teachings relate to modern issues and trends.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by reviewing the introduction page, where you can provide any necessary context or background information about your thoughts on Islamic teachings and modernity.
2. On the demographics page, select your choice of demographic information about yourself.
3. Move on to the faith and contemporary issues section, where you can share your thoughts on how you think Islamic teachings relate to modern issues.
4. In the contemporary lifestyle section, you can describe how you live your daily life in relation to Islamic teachings.
5. Next, select the options that best describe your views on faith and modernity in society.
6. On the trends across audiences section, describe any trends you've noticed in different communities regarding Islamic teachings.
7. In the survey data collection section, you can share any additional comments or feedback you have about the survey itself.
8. Finally, provide your email address so we can contact you, and optionally, your phone number.

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any background information or context about your thoughts on Islamic teachings and modernity.

* **Trends Across Audiences** (`6`, `text`, optional): Describe any trends you've noticed in different communities regarding Islamic teachings.
* **Survey Data Collection** (`7`, `text`, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback you have about the survey itself.
* **Comments** (`8`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback you have about the survey.

Note: Please ensure that you fill out only the fields that apply to you.
