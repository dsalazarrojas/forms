# Chemistry Knowledge Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess your knowledge and interests in the field of chemistry. It will help us understand your background and preferences, and provide insights that will be useful for educational or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer based on your actual experience and knowledge.
2. For multiple choice questions, select one option that best describes your current level of understanding or interest.
3. For fields with a "Select all that apply" option, choose all the options that apply to you.
4. Be honest and accurate in your answers, as they will help us understand your needs and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **Your role** (`respondent_role`, select_one, required): Please select one of the following options to describe your role: Student, Educator, Professional, Parent, or General public.
* **Highest education level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Please select one of the following options to describe your highest education level: High school, Associate degree, Bachelor degree, Master degree, Doctorate, or Other.
* **Years of chemistry experience** (`chemistry_experience`, select_one, required): Please select one of the following options to describe your level of experience: None, Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 4-7 years, or More than 7 years.
* **How interested are you in chemistry?** (`chemistry_interest`, select_one, required): Please rate your level of interest from 1 (Not interested) to 5 (Very interested).
* **Rate your overall chemistry knowledge** (`knowledge_self_assessment`, select_one, required): Please rate your current level of knowledge from Beginner to Expert.
* **Understanding of atomic structure** (`atomic_structure_knowledge`, select_one, required): Please select one of the following options to describe your understanding: Very poor, Poor, Average, Good, or Excellent.
* **Understanding of chemical bonding** (`bonding_knowledge`, select_one, required): Please select one of the following options to describe your understanding: Very poor, Poor, Average, Good, or Excellent.
* **Understanding of the periodic table** (`periodic_table_knowledge`, select_one, required): Please select one of the following options to describe your understanding: Very poor, Poor, Average, Good, or Excellent.
* **Understanding of chemical reactions** (`reaction_knowledge`, select_one, required): Please select one of the following options to describe your understanding: Very poor, Poor, Average, Good, or Excellent.
* **Understanding of acids and bases** (`acids_bases_knowledge`, select_one, required): Please select one of the following options to describe your understanding: Very poor, Poor, Average, Good, or Excellent.
* **Most effective learning methods for you** (`learning_methods`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Most challenging chemistry topics** (`challenging_topics`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Most interesting applications of chemistry** (`application_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Confidence in solving chemistry problems** (`confidence_problem_solving`, select_one, required): Please select one of the following options to describe your confidence level: Very unconfident, Unconfident, Neutral, Confident, or Very confident.
* **Resources that would help your chemistry learning** (`resource_access`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Areas where you want to improve** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional): Please describe the areas where you would like to improve.
* **Does your career involve chemistry?** (`career_chemistry`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the following options: Yes, directly, Yes, indirectly, False, or Planning to.

## Tips
* Answer honestly and accurately to help us better understand your needs and preferences.
* If you're unsure about any question, choose the option that best describes your current level of knowledge or experience.
* Take your time to read and answer each question carefully.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
