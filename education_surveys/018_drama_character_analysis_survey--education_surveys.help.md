# Drama Character Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you analyze a drama character's traits, motivations, and relevance to the theme of a piece of drama. It is intended for academic or educational purposes, such as studying drama or literary analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all applicable characters from the list of options on page 1.
2. For the character you have selected, provide a description of their motivation in the "character motivation" field.
3. In the "character development" field, describe the character's development throughout the story.
4. In the "theme relevance" field, describe how the character relates to the overall theme of the piece.
5. In the "group critique" field, provide any additional comments or insights about the character's strengths, weaknesses, or relevance to the theme.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drama Character Analysis Survey Page 1** (`drama_character_analysis_survey_page_1`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ This page lists all the characters in the drama piece. Select all characters that you want to analyze.
* **Character Motivation** (`character_motivation`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Describe the motivation or driving force behind the character's actions in the story.
* **Character Development** (`character_development`, note, required: false):
	+ Describe how the character changes or evolves throughout the story. This can include their growth, transformation, or decline.
* **Theme Relevance** (`theme_relevance`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe how the character relates to the overall theme of the piece. How do their actions, motivations, or development reflect or contrast with the theme?
* **Group Critique** (`group_critique`, note, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or insights about the character's strengths, weaknesses, or relevance to the theme.
