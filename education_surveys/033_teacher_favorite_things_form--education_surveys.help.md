# Teacher Favorite Things Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Favorite Things Form is designed to collect information about a teacher's personal preferences and interests. This data can be used for various purposes, such as understanding their strengths, weaknesses, and motivations in the educational or professional context.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Complete each field accurately and truthfully to provide the best insights about your preferences.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or administrators for clarification.
4. Ensure you do not duplicate any information that already exists in your profile or other relevant databases.
5. Once you've completed the form, review it carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Favorite Subject** (`favorite_subject`, `text`, required: false): Please indicate your favorite subject from the options provided. This could be a subject you enjoy teaching, learning, or both.
* **Favorite Subject (2)** (`favorite_subject_2`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is similar to the previous one, but please select only one option that best represents your favorite subject.
* **Favorite Food** (`favorite_food`, `text`, required: false): Please list your favorite food or cuisine. You can type in the name of a specific dish, ingredient, or type of cuisine.
* **Favorite Hobbies** (`favorite_hobby`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the hobbies that apply to you. This could include any leisure activities you enjoy during your free time.
* **Favorite Book** (`favorite_book`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of your favorite book. This could be a book you've read, a favorite author, or a book that has had a significant impact on you.
* **Favorite Activities** (`favorite_activity`, `text`, required: false): Describe your favorite activities or hobbies that you enjoy doing in your free time.
* **Favorite Color** (`favorite_color`, `text`, required: false): Please select your favorite color. This could be a color associated with a favorite memory or a color that represents you well.
* **Favorite Movie** (`favorite_movie`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of your favorite movie. This could be a film that you've watched, a favorite director, or a movie that has had a significant impact on you.
* **Favorite Game** (`favorite_game`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of your favorite game or sport. This could be a game you play, a sport you enjoy watching, or a game that you're skilled at.
* **Favorite Places** (`favorite_place`, `text`, required: false): Please describe your favorite places, either local or around the world.
* **Favorite Animal** (`favorite_animal`, `text`, required: false): Please select your favorite animal. This could be a pet, a wild animal, or an animal that represents you well.
* **More About You** (`more_about_you`, `text`, required: false): If you have any other information you'd like to add about yourself, please do so here.
* **Favorite Food 2** (`favorite_food_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the food preferences that apply to you.
* **Favorite Music** (`favorite_music`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of your favorite music genre or artist.
* **Favorite Music Genres** (`favorite_music_genres`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the music genres that you enjoy.
* **Favorite Sports** (`favorite_sport`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of your favorite sport.
* **Favorite Game 2** (`favorite_game_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your favorite game or sport.
* **Submit** (`form_submit`, `text`, required: false): Review and submit your completed form.

## Tips
1. Be honest and accurate when completing the form.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting.
3. If you need any help or clarification, don't hesitate to contact the relevant authorities or administrators.
4. Ensure you don't duplicate any information that already exists in your profile or other relevant databases.
