# Academic Innovation Climate Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your experience with innovation in your institution. Your input will help us understand how we can improve our support for innovation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all fields, but only if you can answer them.
2. For select fields, choose the option that best describes your experience.
3. For text fields, provide a brief description (up to 2 paragraphs).
4. For email fields, enter your institution email address if you want to receive follow-up discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Role in institution** (`role`, select_one): Select the option that best describes you (Student, Faculty, Staff, Administrator).
* **Perception of institutional support for innovation** (`perception_of_innovation`, select_one): Rate how supportive your institution is. Choose from Very supportive, Supportive, Neutral, Unsoppportive.
* **Availability of resources for innovation** (`resources_availability`, select_one): Rate how accessible resources are. Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Main barriers to innovation** (`barriers`, select_multiple): Select all that apply (Funding, Time, Institutional inertia, Lack of training).
* **Examples of successful innovation** (`successful_examples`, text): Describe up to two examples of successful innovation initiatives in your institution.
* **Suggestions to improve innovation climate** (`suggestions`, text): Provide specific policy or operational recommendations to improve innovation climate.
* **Willingness to join innovation initiatives** (`willingness_to_participate`, select_one): Indicate your willingness to participate (True, Maybe, False).
* **Contact email for follow up** (`contact_email`, email): Enter your institution email address if you want to receive follow-up discussion.

Note: Fields with no hint will be left blank, and if required, will be marked as required.
