# Back To School Parent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from parents or guardians to help us better understand their comfort level with remote learning, concerns about school reopening, and other needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's parent or guardian name in the `Parent or guardian name` field.
2. Select the grade level of the student in the `Student grade` field.
3. Rate your comfort level with remote learning in the `Comfort with remote learning` field.
4. If you have any concerns about school reopening, please enter them in the `Top concerns about reopening` field.
5. If you require any support from the school, select all that apply in the `Support needed from school` field.
6. Choose how you would like to receive updates from the school in the `Communication preference` field.
7. If you have any transportation needs, please enter them in the `Transportation needs` field.
8. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please enter them in the `Additional comments` field.
9. Enter the date you completed this survey in the `Survey completion date` field.
10. Finally, indicate whether you consent to be contacted by the school for further clarification in the `Consent to follow up` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent or guardian name**: Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the student.
  - **Type:** text, required: false
- **Student grade**: Select the grade level of your child.
  - **Type:** select_one, required: true
- **Comfort with remote learning**: Rate your comfort level with remote learning (Not comfortable, Somewhat comfortable, Comfortable, Very comfortable).
  - **Type:** select_one, required: false
- **Top concerns about reopening**: Enter any health, logistical, or transportation concerns you have about school reopening.
  - **Type:** text, required: false
- **Support needed from school**: Select all the support you need from the school (Academic support, Technology help, Counseling, Meal programs).
  - **Type:** select_multiple, required: false
- **Communication preference**: Choose how you prefer to receive updates from the school (Email, Phone, Text, School portal).
  - **Type:** select_one, required: false
- **Transportation needs**: Enter any transportation needs you have for your child.
  - **Type:** text, required: false
- **Additional comments**: Enter any additional comments or concerns you have for the school leadership.
  - **Type:** text, required: false
- **Survey completion date**: Enter the date you completed this survey.
  - **Type:** date, required: true
- **Consent to follow up**: Indicate whether you consent to be contacted by the school for further clarification.
  - **Type:** select_one, required: false
