# Nontraditional Learner Inclusion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nontraditional Learner Inclusion Survey is a tool to collect data on the experiences of non-traditional learners on campus. This survey will help us understand their experiences with campus climate, resources, and support services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
2. Select the options that best match your experiences.
3. Provide any additional comments or explanations if needed.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nontraditional Learner Inclusion Survey** (`1`, `text`, required): This is the title of the survey. Please do not need to answer this question, it's just a title.
* **barriers_faced** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required): Have you faced any barriers on campus? Yes or No.
* **barriers** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required): Which barriers have you faced on campus? Select all that apply.
* **campus_engagement** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Do you feel included and engaged on campus? Yes or No.
* **campus_climate** (`5`, `select_one`, required): How would you rate the campus climate? Yes or No.
* **campus_climate_comments** (`6`, `text`, required): Please provide any comments about the campus climate.
* **campus_resources** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): Are there adequate resources available to support your learning? Yes or No.
* **campus_resources_description** (`8`, `text`, required): Please describe the resources that are available to support your learning.
* **campus_resources_other** (`9`, `text`, required): If there are other resources that support your learning, please list them.
* **campus_safety** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Do you feel safe on campus? Yes or No.
* **campus_safety_comments** (`11`, `text`, required): Please provide any comments about campus safety.
* **campus_support_services** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required): Are there adequate support services available to support your learning? Yes or No.
* **campus_support_services_description** (`13`, `text`, required): Please describe the support services that are available to support your learning.
* **campus_support_services_other** (`14`, `text`, required): If there are other support services that support your learning, please list them.
* **campus_support_services_comments** (`15`, `text`, required): Please provide any comments about campus support services.
* **campus_culture** (`16`, `select_one`, required): Do you feel like you fit in with the campus culture? Yes or No.
* **campus_culture_comments** (`17`, `text`, required): Please provide any comments about campus culture.
* **campus_climate_diversity** (`18`, `select_multiple`, required): Do you feel that the campus is diverse and inclusive? Yes or No.
* **campus_climate_diversity_comments** (`19`, `text`, required): Please provide any comments about campus climate diversity.
* **faculty_support** (`20`, `select_one`, required): Do your professors support your learning goals? Yes or No.
* **faculty_support_comments** (`21`, `text`, required): Please provide any comments about faculty support.
* **departmental_support** (`22`, `select_one`, required): Do your professors in your department support your learning goals? Yes or No.
* **departmental_support_comments** (`23`, `text`, required): Please provide any comments about departmental support.
* **support_services_funding** (`24`, `select_multiple`, required): Are there adequate funding for support services? Yes or No.
* **support_services_funding_description** (`25`, `text`, required): Please describe the funding for support services.
