# Education Reform Assessment Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Education Reform Assessment Quiz is designed to gather information from schools and educational institutions about their assessment methods and practices. This information will be used to help identify areas for improvement and provide insights to support education reform efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information for each field as accurately as possible.
2. For select fields (e.g., School Type, School Subject Area, Assessment Method, etc.), choose the most suitable option from the list provided.
3. For text fields (e.g., Reform Ideas, Reform Barriers, etc.), provide brief and concise descriptions or examples.
4. For date and time fields (e.g., Date of Assessment, Time of Assessment), use the MM/DD/YYYY and HH:MM AM/PM format, respectively.
5. If you are not sure about a particular field, please provide the best effort you can or reach out to your supervisor or administrator for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Type** (select_one, required: false): Choose the type of school you are representing (Government, Public Charter, Private, or Special).
* **School Name** (text, required: false): Enter your school's name.
* **School Grade Level** (select_multiple, required: false): Select the grade levels your school serves (Elementary, Middle, High, or All).
* **School Subject Area** (select_one, required: false): Choose the subject area your school focuses on (Math, Science, English, or All).
* **Assessment Method** (select_multiple, required: false): Select the methods your school uses to assess student performance (Standardized, Project-Based, Portfolio-Based, or Other).
* **Assessment Frequency** (select_one, required: false): Choose how often your school assesses student performance (Regular, Irregular, As Needed, or Other).
* **Assessment Method Other** (text, required: false): If your school uses an other method not listed, please describe it briefly.
* **Date of Assessment** (date, required: false): Enter the date of the last assessment in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time of Assessment** (time, required: false): Enter the time of the last assessment in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Location of Assessment** (select_one, required: false): Choose the location where the assessment took place (School, District, State, or Region).
* **Stakeholders** (text, required: false): List all stakeholders involved in the assessment.
* **Stakeholder Count** (number, required: false): Enter the number of stakeholders involved in the assessment.
* **Stakeholder Role** (select_one, required: false): Choose the role of the stakeholders involved (Teacher, Administrator, Parent, or Other).
* **Stakeholder Opinions** (select_multiple, required: false): Select the opinions of stakeholders about the assessment (Positive, Negative, Neutral, or No Opinion).
* **Reform Ideas** (text, required: false): List reform ideas for your school or district.
* **Reform Barriers** (text, required: false): List barriers to implementing reform ideas.
* **Stakeholder Opinions on Reform** (select_multiple, required: false): Select the opinions of stakeholders on reform (Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Somewhat Disagree, or Strongly Disagree).

By following these guidelines, you will be able to provide accurate and useful information to support education reform efforts.
