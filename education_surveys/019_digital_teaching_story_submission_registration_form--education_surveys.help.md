# Digital Teaching Story Submission Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Teaching Story Submission Registration Form is used to collect personal anecdotes or stories from educators about their experiences, lessons learned, or teaching practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Please share your teaching story**: Type a personal anecdote or story about your teaching experience in the field on the first page.
2. **Select a Category**: Choose the category that best describes your submission: Academic, Non-Academic, or Other.
3. **Enter a Title**: Provide a brief title for your submission.
4. **Add a Description**: Write a brief description of your submission.
5. **Attach a File**: Upload a file related to your submission (e.g., document, image, or video).
6. **Provide Contact Information**: Enter your email address and/or phone number to enable us to contact you about your submission.
7. **Date and Time**: (Optional) If relevant, enter the date and time of your submission (e.g., a specific workshop or conference).
8. **Location**: (Optional) If relevant, enter the location of your submission (e.g., a specific school or institution).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Please share your teaching story (Submission Story)**: This is the main content of your submission. Type a personal anecdote or story about your teaching experience.
* **Category (Submission Category)**: Choose the category that best describes your submission: Academic, Non-Academic, or Other.
* **Title (Submission Title)**: Provide a brief title for your submission.
* **Description (Submission Description)**: Write a brief description of your submission.
* **Attachment (Submission File)**: Upload a file related to your submission (e.g., document, image, or video).
* **Email (Submission Email)**: Enter your email address to enable us to contact you about your submission.
* **Phone Number (Submission Phone)**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Date (Submission Date)**: Enter the date of your submission (if relevant).
* **Time (Submission Time)**: Enter the time of your submission (if relevant).
* **Location (Submission Location)**: Enter the location of your submission (if relevant).

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate and relevant information for your submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrators.
