# learning_motivation_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Learning Motivation Questionnaire is designed to collect information about the motivation levels of students or learners in an educational setting. This form is intended to help educators or researchers understand students' attitudes and preferences, and to gather comments on their submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Learning Motivation Questionnaire** (1): A brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
- **Motivation** (2): Please describe your current motivation levels, e.g., high, medium, low.
- **Preferences** (3): Please specify your preferences, e.g., learning style, preferred topics, or study habits.
- **Submission** (4): A text field for any additional comments about your submission.
- **Motivation Scale** (5): Select all that apply from the given options, e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
- **Submission Comments** (6): Please provide any feedback or comments on your submission.

## Tips
Please answer all fields honestly and to the best of your ability. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.
