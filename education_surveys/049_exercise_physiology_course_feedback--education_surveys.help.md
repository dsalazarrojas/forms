# Exercise Physiology Course Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the Exercise Physiology Course from students who have taken it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Rate the course on a scale of 0-5.
2. Choose how satisfied you are with the course overall.
3. Rate your instructor's performance.
4. Provide any additional comments on your experience with the course.
5. Indicate whether you would recommend the course to others.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rate the course** (0, 5): This field asks you to rate the course on a scale of 0-5, where 0 indicates a very poor course and 5 indicates an excellent course.
* **Overall satisfaction** (0, 5): This field asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the course, on a scale of 0-5.
* **Instructor's rating** (0, 5): This field allows you to rate your instructor's performance, on a scale of 0-5.
* **Feedback comments** (text): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or thoughts on your experience with the course.
* **Recommend this course** (select one): Choose whether you would highly recommend, somewhat recommend, or not recommend this course to others.
* **Email** (email): This field is for you to provide your email address, which will be used to contact you if the instructors need to follow up with you.
* **Phone number** (text): This field is for you to provide your phone number, which will be used to contact you if needed.
* **Prophets** (0, 5): This field seems to be an error in the original YAML schema and is likely a placeholder or a redundant field. Please ignore it.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide your genuine feedback.
* Please provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your comments.
* If you have any concerns or issues with the course, please mention them in the feedback comments field.
