# Digital Inclusion Barriers In Learning Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to identify potential barriers to digital inclusion in learning environments. It gathers information on various aspects of digital inclusion, including device issues, internet access, skills, and demographics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully, considering the options provided.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for questions that ask about your experiences or circumstances.
3. Choose a specific option or answer in the text fields for demographic questions.
4. If you are unsure or do not have information for a particular question, you can leave it blank.

* **device_issues** (3): Do you experience any difficulties with your device or computer? (Yes or No)
