# Global Health Equity Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Health Equity Awareness Survey is designed to collect demographic and health-related information from users, aiming to promote global health equity awareness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and answer all the questions truthfully.
2. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select your date of birth or the date you would like to participate in the survey.
5. Answer the questions regarding your demographics, health status, medical history, and other related fields according to your experiences and observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
*   **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, not required): This is a brief introduction to the survey and its purpose.

### Name
*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.

### Email
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address for contact and follow-up purposes.

### Date
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select your date of birth or the date you would like to participate in the survey.

### Gender
*   **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you identify as male, female, or other. Select 'No' if you do not identify as any of the above.

### Race
*   **Race** (`race`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the races or ethnicities you identify with.

### Ethnicity
*   **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the ethnicities you identify with.

### Level of Education
*   **Level of Education** (`education_level`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your level of education.

### Household Size
*   **Household Size** (`household_size`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of people living in your household.

### Household Income
*   **Household Income** (`household_income`, `select_one`, required): Choose your household's annual income.

### Occupation
*   **Occupation** (`occupation`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the occupations you have had in the past or currently hold.

### Education Field
*   **Education Field** (`education_field`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the fields of education you have studied or are interested in.

### Health Insurance
*   **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the types of health insurance you have or do not have.

### Health Care
*   **Health Care** (`health_care`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the types of health care you have or do not have.

### Access to Health Care
*   **Access to Health Care** (`access_to_health_care`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the types of health care you have access to.

### Health Status
*   **Health Status** (`health_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your current health status.

### Medical Condition
*   **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, not required): If you have a medical condition, describe it briefly.

### Symptoms
*   **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, not required): If you have any symptoms, describe them briefly.

### Medical History
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, not required): If you have a medical history, describe it briefly.

### Doctor
*   **Doctor** (`doctor`, `text`, not required): If you have a doctor, provide their name and contact information.

### Hospitalization
*   **Hospitalization** (`hospitalization`, `text`, not required): If you have been hospitalized, provide details about the hospitalization.

### Emergency Contact
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, not required): If you have an emergency contact, provide their name and contact information.

### Emergency Phone
*   **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, not required): If you have an emergency phone, provide the number.

### Address
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, not required): If you want to be contacted, provide your address.

### Submit
*   **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, not required): Click this button to submit your survey responses.

**Tips**

*   Please review the form carefully and answer all the questions truthfully.
*   If you need help with any question, contact our support team.
*   The information collected from this form will be used to promote global health equity awareness.
