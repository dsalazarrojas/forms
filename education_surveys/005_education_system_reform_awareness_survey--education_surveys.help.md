# Education System Reform Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education System Reform Awareness Survey is designed to gather information from respondents about their awareness and opinions on education system reform. The survey aims to collect data that can be used to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions about the education system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that you have read the introduction carefully and understand the purpose of the survey.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly, as your responses will help to inform education system reform.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please contact your administrator or supervisor for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Introduction, Note, Not Required): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before starting to answer the questions.
* **Question 1** (Question 1, Text, Not Required): Please enter your thoughts or comments about the current state of education system reform.
* **Question 2** (Question 2, Number, Not Required): Please rate your level of awareness about education system reform on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Question 3** (Question 3, Select One, Not Required): Do you think education system reform is necessary? 
* **Question 4** (Question 4, Time, Not Required): How long have you been aware of education system reform? 
* **Question 5** (Question 5, Number, Not Required): Please rate your level of interest in education system reform on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Question 6** (Question 6, Select Multiple, Not Required): What are your opinions about education system reform? Select all that apply.
* **Question 7** (Question 7, Date, Not Required): When did you first become aware of education system reform? 
* **Question 8** (Question 8, Email, Not Required): Please enter your email address to receive updates about education system reform. 
* **Question 9** (Question 9, Text, Not Required): Please enter any additional comments or suggestions about education system reform.
* **Question 10** (Question 10, Text, Not Required): Please enter any further comments or suggestions about education system reform.
* **Question 11** (Question 11, Select One, Not Required): Do you think education system reform is a priority? 
* **Question 12** (Question 12, Select Multiple, Not Required): What do you think are the most important issues in education system reform? Select all that apply.

Note: This help guide is intended to guide the respondent through the survey, but it is not exhaustive. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your administrator or supervisor.
