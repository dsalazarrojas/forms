# Educational Outreach Impact Study - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Outreach Impact Study is designed to gather data on the effectiveness of a particular activity in reaching its target audience. This data will be used to evaluate and improve future educational outreach programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please ensure you have actually engaged with the educational outreach program.
2. Select the correct answer to each question based on your experience.
3. Enter any relevant comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Participant Details**
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): This field is used to collect any additional information about the participant that may be relevant to the evaluation.

### **How would you rate the activity**
* **How would you rate the activity** (`activity_ratings`, select_one, optional): Rate the activity from 1 to 3 based on your experience. The options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### **What feedback do you have**
* **What feedback do you have** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the activity.

### **Email**
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. This will help us contact you if we need further information.

### **Phone**
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number. This will help us contact you if we need further information.

### **Date**
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you engaged with the educational outreach program.

### **Time**
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you engaged with the educational outreach program.

### **Additional Notes**
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the activity.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when providing feedback. Your input will help us improve future educational outreach programs.
