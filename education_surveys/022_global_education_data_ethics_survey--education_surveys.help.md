# Global Education Data Ethics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Education Data Ethics Survey is designed to collect feedback and opinions from users about the ethics of data in education. This survey aims to understand user perceptions and concerns about data ethics in education and gather information to improve the educational system.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please go through each page carefully and answer the questions to the best of your abilities.
- For each question, provide as much information as you feel necessary.
- You can choose one answer for most questions.
- Please note that some sections are not required, but providing your thoughts will help us better understand your opinions.
