# Design Thesis Review Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Design Thesis Review Survey is a form used to gather feedback from evaluators on a student's design thesis. The survey is designed to collect your thoughts on the overall quality and impact of the thesis, identify areas for improvement, and provide recommendations for future students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the provided instructions and guidelines for each section.
2.  Answer each question in the relevant section, providing as much detail as possible.
3.  Ensure that all required questions are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Overall Impression** (`design_thesis_review_survey_page_1`, text, optional): This section is where you can provide your overall thoughts on the thesis. Write your impressions of the thesis, including any positive or negative comments.
*   **What were the strengths of the thesis?** (`design_thesis_review_survey_page_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options to describe the strengths of the thesis:
    *   Excellent
    *   Good
    *   Fair
    *   Poor
*   **What were the areas for improvement in the thesis?** (`design_thesis_review_survey_page_3`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options to describe the areas of improvement for the thesis:
    *   Excellent
    *   Good
    *   Fair
    *   Poor
*   **Would you recommend this student to be awarded a degree?** (`design_thesis_review_survey_page_4`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options to provide a final assessment and recommendation:
    *   Yes
    *   No
*   **What are your thoughts on the project's overall structure and organization?** (`design_thesis_review_survey_page_5`, text, optional): Provide feedback on the structure and organization of the thesis, including any suggestions for improvement.
*   **What were your thoughts on the thesis's overall quality and impact?** (`design_thesis_review_survey_page_6`, text, optional): Write an overall assessment of the thesis, including any comments on its quality and impact.

## Tips

*   Please provide as much detail as possible for each question.
*   Ensure that all required questions are completed before submitting the form.
*   If you have already completed a survey for the same thesis, do not repeat the process.
