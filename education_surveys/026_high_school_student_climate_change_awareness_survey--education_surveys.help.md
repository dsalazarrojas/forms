# High School Student Climate Change Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High School Student Climate Change Awareness Survey is a tool designed to gauge high school students' understanding of climate change, their level of concern, and their willingness to take action on climate-related issues. This survey aims to provide insights into the students' knowledge, attitudes, and behaviors regarding climate change.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the options that best reflect your level of understanding of climate change for the question "Climate Change Knowledge".
2.  Choose the option that best describes your level of concern about climate change for the question "Climate Concern".
3.  If you have any thoughts on climate action, describe them in the "Climate Action" field.
4.  For "Climate Awareness Level", select the option that best describes your current level of knowledge about climate change.
5.  Choose the options that best reflect your knowledge in different areas related to climate change for the question "Climate Knowledge Area".
6.  If you have a plan to take action on climate change, describe it in the "Climate Action Plan" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Climate Change Knowledge**: Please select the level of knowledge about climate change that best describes your understanding. For "a bit", you have some basic knowledge or a general idea. "Not at all" indicates a lack of knowledge about climate change. "Somewhat" means you have some knowledge, but it might be limited. "Very much" indicates a more in-depth understanding.
*   **Climate Concern**: Select the option that best reflects your level of concern about climate change.
*   **Climate Action**: If you have thoughts on how to take action on climate change, provide a brief description of your ideas.
*   **Climate Awareness**: This field is optional. If you have thoughts on how to take action on climate change, provide a brief description of your ideas.
*   **Student Support**: Please select whether you think there is a need for student support related to climate change.
*   **Climate Influence**: Choose the options that best reflect how climate change influences your decisions and actions.
*   **Climate Emphasis**: This field is optional. Please select the level of emphasis on climate change education in your school.
*   **Climate Participation**: If you participate in climate-related activities or projects, select the options that best describe your level of participation.
*   **Climate Awareness Level**: Please select the level of knowledge about climate change that best describes your understanding.
*   **Climate Knowledge Area**: Choose the options that best reflect your knowledge of different areas related to climate change.
*   **Climate Action Plan**: This field is optional. If you have ideas for taking action on climate change, provide a brief description of your plan.

## Tips
*   Please take your time to answer each question carefully and honestly, as your responses will help inform education and policy-making efforts.
*   If you need help understanding any of the questions or fields, please don't hesitate to ask for assistance.
*   Your participation is greatly appreciated, and your responses will contribute to a better understanding of high school students' climate change awareness.
