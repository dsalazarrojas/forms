# Writing Questionnaire Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an individual's writing habits, goals, and progress. It is intended to assess their writing skills, frequency, and environment, providing valuable insights for education and research purposes. Please fill out this form only if your writing-related activities are relevant to your role or responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the text fields provided.
2. Select the appropriate option from the drop-down lists for multiple-choice questions.
3. You can add or remove options from the list as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Writing Questionnaire Form** (`1`, `text`, not required): This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional information about your writing habits, goals, or progress.
*   **Writing Goal** (`2`, `select_one`, not required): 
    *   **Yes**: You aim to write regularly or as a profession.
    *   **No**: You don't write regularly or as a profession.
- **Writing Frequency** (`3`, `text`, not required): Describe your writing frequency, e.g., daily, weekly, or sporadically.
*   **Writing Progress** (`4`, `select_multiple`, not required): 
    *   **Yes**: You've made progress with your writing goals.
    *   **No**: You haven't made progress with your writing goals.
*   **Writing Habits** (`5`, `text`, not required): Provide any details about your writing habits, e.g., writing style, tools, or environments.
*   **Writing Environment** (`6`, `text`, not required): Describe your writing environment, e.g., office, home, or outdoors.
*   **Review Support** (`7`, `select_one`, not required): 
    *   **Yes**: You seek feedback on your writing from peers or mentors.
    *   **No**: You don't seek feedback on your writing. 

## Tips
*   This form is for informational purposes only, and your responses will be kept confidential.
*   Please fill out this form accurately and truthfully to provide valuable insights.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification, don't hesitate to contact us.
