# Chess Opening Practice Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about players' habits and preferences when it comes to opening preparation in chess. The goal is to understand how players approach opening study, what tools they use, and what challenges they face. This data can help improve the overall chess playing experience for users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information. You can type in text fields, select from the available options in dropdown fields, and click on the options you'd like to choose in select_multiple fields. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Player name** (`Player name`, text, required)
Enter your name as it should be displayed in the survey results.

### 2. **Age** (`Age`, number, optional)
Enter your age to help us understand your background. This information is optional, but it can aid us in analyzing the data.

### 3. **Chess club or school** (`Chess club or school`, text, optional)
If you're affiliated with a chess club or school, please enter their name here. This information can help us understand if there's a network effect in opening preparation.

### 4. **Current Elo rating** (`Current Elo rating`, number, optional)
Enter your current Elo rating to help us understand your level of skill. This information is optional, but it can aid us in analyzing the data.

### 5. **Playing category** (`Playing category`, select_one, required)
Choose how you would categorize yourself as a chess player:

* Beginner (under 1000)
* Intermediate (1000-1600)
* Advanced (1600-2000)
* Expert (2000-2200)
* Master (over 2200)

### 6. **Years playing chess** (`Years playing chess`, select_one, required)
Choose how long you've been playing chess:

* Less than 1 year
* 1-2 years
* 3-5 years
* 6-10 years
* More than 10 years

### 7. **How often do you study openings?** (`How often do you study openings?`, select_one, required)
Choose how frequently you dedicate time to opening study:

* Daily
* Several times per week
* Once a week
* Few times per month
* Rarely
* Never

### 8. **Hours per week spent on opening study** (`Hours per week spent on opening study`, number, required)
Enter the number of hours you spend on opening study per week. This information will help us understand your dedication to the subject.

### 9. **Your favorite openings with White** (`Your favorite openings with White`, text, optional)
List your favorite openings with White here. This can help us understand your preferences and approaches.

### 10. **Your favorite openings with Black** (`Your favorite openings with Black`, text, optional)
List your favorite openings with Black here. This can help us understand your preferences and approaches.

### 11. **Resources used for opening study** (`Resources used for opening study`, select_multiple, required)
Select all the resources you use for opening study:

* Opening books
* Chess engines
* Online databases
* Coaching
* YouTube videos
* Mobile apps
* Newspaper/magazines
* Playing practice games

### 12. **Preferred opening style** (`Preferred opening style`, select_one, required)
Choose your preferred opening style:

* Aggressive and tactical
* Strategic and positional
* Solid and defensive
* Flexible and varied
* No preference

### 13. **Depth of opening preparation** (`Depth of opening preparation`, select_one, required)
Choose the depth of your opening preparation:

* Very shallow (first 5 moves)
* Shallow (first 10 moves)
* Moderate (first 15-20 moves)
* Deep (20+ moves prepared)
* Varies by opponent

### 14. **Has opening study improved your rating?** (`Has opening study improved your rating?`, select_one, required)
Choose how opening study has impacted your rating:

* Significantly improved
* Somewhat improved
* No change
* Slightly worse
* Significantly worse

### 15. **Biggest challenge in opening study** (`Biggest challenge in opening study`, select_one, required)
Choose the biggest challenge you face in opening study:

* Memorization
* Understanding principles
* Finding time to study
* Choosing what to study
* Applying knowledge in games
* Finding good resources
* Other

### 16. **Do you prefer memorization or understanding principles?** (`Do you prefer memorization or understanding principles?`, select_one, required)
Choose your approach to opening preparation:

* Pure memorization
* Mostly memorization
* Balanced approach
* Mostly principles
* Only principles

### 17. **Interest in opening coaching or instruction** (`Interest in opening coaching or instruction`, select_one, required)
Choose your level of interest in opening coaching or instruction:

* Very interested
* Somewhat interested
* Neutral
* Not very interested
* Not interested

### 18. **What improvements would help your opening study?** (`What improvements would help your opening study?`, text, optional)
Enter any suggestions you have on how we can help improve your opening study. This can be a specific tool or feature you'd like to see, or a general improvement you'd like to see made.

This form aims to gather comprehensive and detailed information about your opening preparation habits and preferences. Please take your time to fill in the required fields and provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields. Thank you for participating in this survey, and we hope to use this data to create better chess playing experiences for you and all users.
