# Campus Sustainability Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Campus Sustainability Feedback Survey is designed to gather your opinions and feedback on the campus's sustainability efforts. Your responses will help us understand your experiences, preferences, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the option that best describes your role on campus.
2. If you are a student, faculty, or staff member, please provide your campus affiliation (e.g., college, school, or office name).
3. Rate your level of familiarity with campus sustainability initiatives (Not aware, Somewhat aware, Moderately aware, Very aware).
4. Indicate your participation in campus sustainability activities over the past year (None, 1-2 events, 3-5 events, More than 5).
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with campus sustainability efforts (1 - Very dissatisfied, 2 - Somewhat dissatisfied, 3 - Neutral, 4 - Somewhat satisfied, 5 - Very satisfied).
6. Select all the barriers that prevent you from participating in sustainability programs (e.g., Lack of awareness, Scheduling conflicts, No incentives, Accessibility issues, Other).
7. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide specific ideas or feedback.
8. Comment on campus waste management and recycling systems.
9. If applicable, select the areas where you think the campus should focus on energy conservation (e.g., Lighting upgrades, Heating and cooling efficiency, Renewable energy adoption, Behavior change campaigns).
10. Please indicate if you would like to be contacted about sustainability initiatives (Yes/No).
11. If you selected "Yes" for follow-up, please provide your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Role**: This question helps us understand your relationship with the university. Please select the option that best describes your status (Student, Faculty, Staff, Visitor).
* **Campus Affiliation**: This field is only required if you are a student, faculty, or staff member. Please enter your college, school, or office name.
* **Sustainability Awareness Level**: This question helps us gauge your familiarity with campus sustainability initiatives. Please rate your awareness level (Not aware, Somewhat aware, Moderately aware, Very aware).
* **Participation in Sustainability Activities Past Year**: This question helps us understand your engagement with sustainability activities. Please indicate your participation level (None, 1-2 events, 3-5 events, More than 5).
* **Overall Satisfaction with Campus Sustainability Efforts**: This question helps us understand your satisfaction with current sustainability initiatives. Please rate your satisfaction level (1 - Very dissatisfied, 2 - Somewhat dissatisfied, 3 - Neutral, 4 - Somewhat satisfied, 5 - Very satisfied).
* **Barriers to Participating in Sustainability Programs**: Please select all the obstacles that prevent you from participating in sustainability programs (Lack of awareness, Scheduling conflicts, No incentives, Accessibility issues, Other).
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: If you have any ideas or feedback on how we can improve our sustainability initiatives, please provide specific suggestions.
* **Feedback on Waste Management and Recycling**: We want to hear your thoughts on our current waste management and recycling systems. Please comment on how we can improve them.
* **Energy Conservation Priorities**: If applicable, please select the areas where you think we should focus on energy conservation (Lighting upgrades, Heating and cooling efficiency, Renewable energy adoption, Behavior change campaigns).
* **Contact for Follow Up**: Please indicate if you would like to be contacted about sustainability initiatives (Yes/No).
* **Contact Email**: If you selected "Yes" for follow-up, please provide your email address so we can contact you with updates and news about sustainability initiatives on campus.

Please note that filling out this form is entirely optional, and your responses will be kept confidential. Your feedback is greatly appreciated, and it will help us create a more sustainable and environmentally friendly campus environment for everyone.
