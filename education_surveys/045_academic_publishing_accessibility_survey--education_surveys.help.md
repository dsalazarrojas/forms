# Academic Publishing Accessibility Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from academic publishing professionals about their experiences and perceptions on accessibility practices in academic publishing. The goal is to understand the current state of accessibility in the industry, identify areas for improvement, and provide insights to inform future initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your primary role in publishing from the options provided.
2. Rate your familiarity with accessibility standards.
3. Identify barriers to accessible publishing that apply to you.
4. Select tools or methods used to improve accessibility.
5. Rate the institutional support available for accessibility.
6. Indicate areas where training is most needed.
7. Provide examples of successful accessibility practices (if any).
8. Rate your willingness to adopt accessibility practices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your role** (`role`, required): Select your primary role in publishing, such as Author, Editor, Reviewer, Librarian, or Publisher.
* **Familiarity with accessibility standards** (`familiarity_with_accessibility`, required): Rate your level of familiarity with accessibility practices, from Very familiar to Not familiar.
* **Barriers to accessible publishing** (`barriers_to_accessibility`, optional): Identify barriers that apply to you, such as Lack of training, Costs and resources, Technical limitations, Publisher policies, or Lack of awareness.
* **Tools used to improve accessibility** (`tools_used_for_accessibility`, optional): Select tools or methods used to improve accessibility, such as PDF remediation tools, HTML publishing, Alt text for images, or Accessible templates.
* **Institutional support** (`institutional_support`, optional): Rate the level of institutional support available for accessibility, from Strong to None.
* **Training needs** (`training_needs`, optional): Indicate areas where training is most needed, such as Authoring accessible manuscripts, Producing accessible figures, Using remediation tools, or Understanding standards.
* **Examples of successful accessibility practices** (`success_examples`, optional): Describe any positive examples or case studies of successful accessibility practices.
* **Willingness to adopt accessibility practices** (`willingness_to_adopt_practices`, optional): Rate your willingness to change workflows to adopt accessibility practices, from Very willing to Unwilling.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Share any other thoughts or insights on publishing accessibility.
