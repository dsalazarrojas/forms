# Study App Setup Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Study App Setup Survey is a form designed to gather essential information from users to set up their study application preferences. This form helps us understand your study needs and requirements, allowing us to tailor the application to your needs and provide a better experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the page navigation buttons to switch between the different pages.
2. For each field, enter your relevant information. All fields are text-based and allow for free-form input.
3. Review each page carefully to ensure you have filled in all required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **study_app_setup_survey_page_1 (Text)** (`study_app_setup_survey_label_1`, Text, Optional)
This field is the first page of the survey. Enter any relevant information you'd like to provide about your study application.

### **study_app_setup_survey_page_2 (Text)** (`study_app_setup_survey_page_2`, Text, Optional)
This field is the second page of the survey. Enter any additional information about your study application.

### **study_app_setup_survey_page_3 (Text)** (`study_app_setup_survey_page_3`, Text, Optional)
This field is the third page of the survey. Enter any further information about your study application.

## Tips
* Be sure to review your input carefully before submitting the form.
* If you encounter any issues while filling out the form, don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
