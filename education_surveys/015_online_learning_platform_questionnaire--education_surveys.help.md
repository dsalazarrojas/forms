# online_learning_platform_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect feedback from users about their experience with online learning platform courses.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accurately and truthfully.
2. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), if applicable.
3. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
4. Submit the form at the end of the survey.
