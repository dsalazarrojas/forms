# University Distance Learning Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students on their experience with the University Distance Learning program. We would like to know about your online learning quality, communication, support services, and overall experience. Your input will help us improve our program and make it better for future students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the options that apply to your experience with the University Distance Learning program. 
2. If you have any other comments, suggestions, or experiences, feel free to add them in the text field provided.
3. Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **online_learning_quality**: 
  • Select all the aspects of online learning that you think are of high quality:
    + Quality of online content
    + Accessibility of online resources
    + Effectiveness of online communication
  • If you have any other comments about the quality of online learning, please specify in the text field.

* **communication**: 
  • Select the type of communication you experienced:
    + Regular emails and updates
    + Live online support
    + No support at all
  • If you have any other comments about communication, please specify in the text field.

* **support_services**: 
  • Select all the types of support services you utilized:
    + Support for course material understanding
    + Support for technical issues
    + Support for personal issues
  • If you have any other comments about support services, please specify in the text field.
