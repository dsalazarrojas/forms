# Science Center Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from visitors about their experience at the Science Center. Your input will help us better understand what we do well and what we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting, please note that all fields are optional and anonymous. You can answer as many or as few questions as you feel comfortable with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What exhibits do you enjoy most at our center?** (`exhibit_interest`, select_one, optional): Please select the exhibit that you enjoyed the most during your visit. This will help us understand what draws you to our exhibits.
* **What programs do you participate in?** (`program_experience`, select_multiple, optional): If you've participated in any of our programs or activities, please select which ones you enjoy the most. This will help us identify which programs are engaging for our visitors.
* **Overall experience at our center?** (`overall_experience`, select_one, optional): Please rate your overall experience at the Science Center. This will help us gauge our success in providing a enjoyable experience for you.
* **How often do you interact with our staff?** (`staff_interaction`, select_one, optional): We're interested in knowing how often you interact with our staff, whether it's for questions, assistance, or just a chat. This will help us understand how well we're serving our visitors.
* **Do you have any additional feedback?** (`feedback`, note, optional): If you have any comments, suggestions, or concerns that didn't fit into the previous questions, please type them in here. We value your thoughts and ideas!
* **What do you think of the exhibit quality?** (`exhibit_quality`, select_one, optional): Please rate the quality of our exhibits, from Good to Poor. This will help us understand how we're doing in terms of exhibit quality.
* **Have you any exhibit suggestions?** (`exhibit_suggestions`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any ideas on how we can improve our exhibits, please select from the options below. This will help us identify areas for improvement.
* **Have you any program suggestions?** (`program_suggestions`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any ideas on how we can improve our programs, please select from the options below. This will help us identify areas for improvement.
* **How do you rate the exhibits?** (`exhibit_rating`, select_one, optional): Please rate the overall quality of our exhibits, from Good to Fair.
* **How do you rate the programs?** (`program_rating`, select_one, optional): Please rate the quality of our programs, from Good to Fair.
* **Overall rating of our center?** (`overall_rating`, select_one, optional): Finally, please rate your overall experience at the Science Center, from Good to Fair.

By taking a few minutes to complete this form, you'll be contributing to the growth and improvement of the Science Center. Thank you for your time and input!
