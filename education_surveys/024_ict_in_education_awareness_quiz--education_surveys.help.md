# ict_in_education_awareness_quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information on respondents' awareness and perceptions of technology's role in education. It's essential to complete the form honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable insights for educators and policymakers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your genuine experiences and opinions.
2. Select the most appropriate option for each question, considering your role in the educational setting and your familiarity with technology.
3. Complete all questions, as they are all required to be answered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in the educational setting?** (`select_one`, required): Please select your position or category in the educational setting, such as Teacher, Student, Parent, or Staff.
* **How would you rate your level of awareness about the potential benefits of technology in education?** (`number`, required): Rate your level of awareness about the benefits of technology in education on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **What is the primary benefit of technology in education?** (`select_one`, required): Choose the most significant advantage technology offers in the educational context, such as Improved student engagement, Improved student assessment, Improved teacher feedback, or Better student outcomes.
* **What is the primary way technology can increase opportunities for students to work in groups?** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable ways technology can enhance group work, such as Better collaboration, More opportunities for students to work together, Improved teacher feedback, or Better student outcomes.
* **How often do you use technology in your daily work or study routine?** (`date`, required): Enter a date in the format (YYYY-MM-DD) representing how regularly you use technology in your daily activities.
* **What is your current level of satisfaction with technology in education?** (`number`, required): Rate your level of satisfaction with technology on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Do you think technology can help reduce costs in education?** (`select_one`, required): Choose whether you think technology has a positive, neutral, or no effect on reducing costs in education.
* **Have you ever received feedback from teachers on how to use technology effectively?** (`select_one`, required): Select whether you've received feedback from teachers on technology usage, such as Yes, always, Yes, sometimes, Yes, never, or No.
* **Do you think technology should be used in more areas of education, such as teaching?** (`select_one`, required): Choose whether you agree or disagree with using technology in more educational contexts.
* **How would you rate your level of agreement with the statement "Technology has improved the way I teach?"** (`number`, required): Rate your level of agreement on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Do you think technology can help increase opportunities for students to work in groups?** (`select_one`, required): Choose whether you think technology has a positive effect on group work, such as Yes, always, Yes, sometimes, Yes, never, or No).
