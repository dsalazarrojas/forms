# Peer Learning Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your motivation for engaging in peer learning activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the following pages:
	+ Page 1: This section requires your first name and last name.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any).
* If you're not sure about any field, you can try to fill it in as best as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required: false): Enter your student ID number if you have one.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required: false): Choose your gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **Age** (`age`, number, required: false): Enter your age.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all that apply:
	+ Social interaction
	+ Academic challenge
	+ Learning new things
	+ Other
* **Teacher's Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): Enter your teacher's name if you are a student.
* **Teacher's Email** (`teacher_email`, email, required: false): Enter your teacher's email address if you are a student.
* **Teacher's Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your teacher's phone number if you are a student.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required: false): Choose the department of your teacher (Mathematics, Science, Language Arts, Other).

## Tips

* If you're not sure about any field, you can try to fill it in as best as you can.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact your teacher or program administrator.
