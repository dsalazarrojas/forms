# Ancient Human Sites Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ancient Human Sites Survey is a form designed to collect information about ancient human sites, such as archaeological sites, historical monuments, or places of cultural significance. The form helps researchers and analysts to gather data about these sites, which can be used for further study and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:
1. Start by filling in the Site name field with the name of the site.
2. Provide the site's location, either with coordinates or the nearest town.
3. If possible, estimate the period in which the site was used or occupied.
4. Rate the site's condition, from 1 to 5, to help assess its preservation.
5. Add any research notes from your field team.
6. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions, enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site name (Required)**: Enter the name of the site, as it is known or referred to.
* **Site location (Required)**: Provide the exact coordinates or the name of the nearest town or city.
* **Estimated period**: If possible, estimate the period in which the site was used or occupied. For example, Neolithic, Bronze Age, etc.
* **Site condition rating (Optional)**: Rate the site's condition from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Research notes (Optional)**: Add any observations or notes from your field team.
* **Contact email (Optional)**: If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions, enter your email address.

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when filling in the site's name and location.
* If you're unsure about the site's period, try to research and provide the best estimate available.
* Rate the site's condition honestly, taking into account any factors that may affect its preservation.
* Use clear and concise language when adding research notes.
* Only enter your email address if you'd like to receive follow-up questions or updates.
