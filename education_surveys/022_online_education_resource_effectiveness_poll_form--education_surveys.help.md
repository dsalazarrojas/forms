# online_education_resource_effectiveness_poll_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online education resource effectiveness poll form is used to gather feedback on the effectiveness of online education resources. The form is designed to be completed by users to rate and provide comments on the resources they have used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the "Overall Rating" field and rate the resource from 1-5, with 1 being "Rarely" and 5 being "Almost Always".
2. In the "Comment" field, provide any additional comments or feedback you have on the resource.
3. In the "Resource Type" field, select all that apply from the dropdown list (Digital Tool, Online Course, or Offline Resource).
4. Select "Goals" that you think the resource has helped you achieve.
5. For "Prophets", leave blank if N/A as you do not have any.
6. Select the "Frequency of Use" field and select the frequency with which you have used the resource.
7. Select "Impact on Learning" and choose the option that best reflects your experience.
8. In the "Recommendations" field, provide any additional recommendations for others.
9. In the "Title 10" field, provide any additional comments or feedback you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating (1)** (`rating_scale`, required/true): Rate the resource's overall effectiveness on a scale of 1-5.
* **Education Resource Effectiveness** (`education_resource_effectiveness`, required/true): This field is not applicable as it is not used in the form.
* **Comment** (`comment`, required/false): Provide comments or feedback on the resource.
* **Resource Type** (`resource_type`, required/true): Select all that apply from the dropdown list (Digital Tool, Online Course, or Offline Resource).
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, required/false): Leave blank if N/A as you do not have any.
* **Frequency of Use** (`frequency`, required/true): Select the frequency with which you have used the resource (Rarely, Occasionally, Frequently, or Almost Always).
* **Goals** (`goals`, required/true): Select goals that you think the resource has helped you achieve.
* **Impact on Learning** (`impact_on_learning`, required/true): Select the option that best reflects your experience (Positive, Negative, or No Effect).
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, required/false): Provide any additional recommendations for others.
* **Title 10** (`title_10`, required/true): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have.

## Tips
- Make sure to rate the resource's overall effectiveness truthfully.
- Be as specific as possible when providing comments or feedback.
- Select all that apply from the "Resource Type" field.
- Select one frequency of use option from the dropdown list.
- Select one impact on learning option from the dropdown list.
- Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Recommendations" field.
- Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Title 10" field.
