# Economic Education Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Economic Education Feedback Survey is a tool used to collect feedback from end-users about the economic education programs they have attended. This survey helps educators and program directors evaluate and improve their offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide accurate and helpful feedback, please complete the form carefully and answer all the questions as thoroughly as possible. The form is divided into sections with clear labels, and each field is explained below. Please do not leave any field blank without providing feedback, as your input is essential for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the course you attended. This will help us identify the specific program you are providing feedback on.
* **Course Instructor** (`course_instructor`, text, optional): Provide the name of the course instructor who taught the course. This will help us assess the quality of instruction and identify areas for improvement.
* **Overall Rating** (`course_rating`, number, optional): Rate the overall quality of the course using a numerical rating from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the course. This will help us understand the strengths and weaknesses of the program and identify areas for improvement.

## Tips
* Be honest and genuine in your feedback.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your comments.
* If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* Your feedback is essential for us to improve our educational offerings, so please complete the form carefully and accurately.
