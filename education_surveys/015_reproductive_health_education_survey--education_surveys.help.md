# Reproductive Health Education Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect data on reproductive health education to understand the current state of education and awareness among participants on various reproductive health topics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please go through each page of the survey carefully, and make sure to answer all questions honestly and accurately.
2. You may skip questions that are not applicable to you.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the survey administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, note, required: false):
This page explains the purpose and importance of the survey.
* **Participant Demographics** (`demographics_page`, text, required: false):
 Please provide your age, gender, and any other relevant demographic information. This will help us understand your background and how it may relate to your reproductive health education.
* **Reproductive Health Page** (`reproductive_health_page`, select_one, required: false):
Please answer 'Yes' if you have received education on the following topics:
	+ Sex education
	+ STI/HIV prevention
	+ Family planning
	+ Fetal development
	+ Menstrual health
	+ Reproductive rights
	+ Maternal care
	+ Other (please specify):
* **Education Page** (`education_page`, select_multiple, required: false):
Please answer 'Yes' if you have received education on any of the following topics:
	+ Family planning methods
	+ STI/HIV prevention
	+ Safe sex practices
	+ Menstrual health
	+ Maternal care
	+ Other (please specify):
* **Survey End** (`survey_end_page`, note, required: false):
You have completed the survey. Thank you for your participation and time.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
- If you need help or clarification on any question, please contact the survey administrator.
- You may stop the survey at any time and revisit it later.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the survey administrator.
