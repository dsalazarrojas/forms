# Academic Recognition Fairness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Recognition Fairness Survey is designed to evaluate the perception and experience of fairness in academic recognition processes. It will help identify areas of improvement and provide insights into how recognition is distributed, decided, and perceived by different groups within the institution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose your role within the institution from the provided options.
2. Indicate how long you have been affiliated with the institution.
3. Select your department or college affiliation.
4. Evaluate the fairness of recognition processes using a scale from 1-5.
5. Provide feedback on transparency and equity in recognition criteria and processes.
6. Rate the representation of demographics in recognition decisions.
7. Assess the satisfaction with recognition outcomes.
8. Suggest improvements to enhance recognition fairness.
9. Leave any additional comments or thoughts about recognition fairness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Role in the Institution**
	+ (`respondent_role`, `select_one`, required)
	+ This question is about your primary role within the institution (e.g., Faculty member, Graduate student, Administrative staff).
* **Years at This Institution**
	+ (`years_at_institution`, `number`, required)
	+ Please indicate how long you have been affiliated with this institution.
* **Department or College Affiliation**
	+ (`department_college`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter your primary department or college affiliation.
* **Perception of Recognition Fairness**
	+ (`recognition_fairness_perception`, `number`, required)
	+ On a scale of 1-5, evaluate how fairly you believe recognition is distributed at your institution.
* **Transparency of Recognition Criteria**
	+ (`recognition_criteria_transparency`, `number`, required)
	+ On a scale of 1-5, rate the transparency of the recognition criteria.
* **Equity in Recognition Process**
	+ (`recognition_process_equity`, `number`, required)
	+ On a scale of 1-5, evaluate the perceived fairness of the recognition process.
* **Perception of Demographic Bias**
	+ (`demographic_bias_perception`, `number`, required)
	+ On a scale of 1-5, report your perception of demographic bias in recognition decisions.
* **Gender Representation in Recognition**
	+ (`gender_representation_in_recognition`, `number`, required)
	+ On a scale of 1-5, evaluate the representation of gender diversity in recognition.
* **Race/Ethnicity Representation in Recognition**
	+ (`race_ethnicity_representation_in_recognition`, `number`, required)
	+ On a scale of 1-5, assess the representation of racial/ethnic diversity in recognition.
* **Career Stage Representation in Recognition**
	+ (`career_stage_representation_in_recognition`, `number`, required)
	+ On a scale of 1-5, evaluate the representation of early-career scholars in recognition.
* **Awareness of Recognition Opportunities**
	+ (`recognition_opportunities_awareness`, `number`, required)
	+ On a scale of 1-5, report how well you think people know about recognition opportunities.
* **Fairness of Application Process**
	+ (`recognition_application_process`, `number`, required)
	+ On a scale of 1-5, evaluate the fairness of the application process for recognition.
* **Fairness of Decision-Making Process**
	+ (`recognition_decision_making_process`, `number`, required)
	+ On a scale of 1-5, evaluate the perceived fairness of the decision-making process for recognition.
* **Satisfaction with Recognition Outcomes**
	+ (`recognition_outcomes_satisfaction`, `number`, required)
	+ On a scale of 1-5, report your satisfaction with recognition outcomes.
* **Suggested Improvements**
	+ (`suggested_improvements`, `text`, optional)
	+ Provide any suggestions to enhance fairness in academic recognition at your institution.
* **Additional Comments**
	+ (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional)
	+ Share any additional thoughts about recognition fairness within your institution.

Please ensure that you complete the form carefully and truthfully to provide valuable insights into recognition fairness within your institution.
