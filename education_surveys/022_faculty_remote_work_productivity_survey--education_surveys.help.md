# Faculty Remote Work Productivity Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Faculty Remote Work Productivity Survey is designed to gather feedback from faculty members about their remote work experiences. This information will help us identify areas of success and areas for improvement in our current remote work arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

1. Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to fill out all fields marked as required.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best matches your response.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, please share them in the text box provided.
