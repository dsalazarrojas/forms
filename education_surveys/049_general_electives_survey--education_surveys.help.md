# General Electives Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about students' elective course selections. It will help the educational institution to gather data on student preferences and selections for electives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your student name.
2. Select your choices for each elective from the corresponding dropdown menus.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter the date and time of the form completion.
5. Add any additional notes or comments if necessary.
6. Enter your student ID.
7. Enter your school year.
