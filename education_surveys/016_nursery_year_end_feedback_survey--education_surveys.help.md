# Nursery Year End Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather feedback from you about your experience in our nursery school for the current year. The feedback will help us to better understand your needs and expectations, and improve our services for the next year. 

Please take a few minutes to answer these questions and provide your honest and sincere feedback. We value your opinion and it will greatly help us to make informed decisions about how to provide the best possible experience for you and your family next year.

## How To Complete This Form

- This form consists of eight questions, each representing a different aspect of your experience in our nursery school. 
- You can answer each question in the space provided, and you can also add any additional comments or suggestions you may have. 
- If you have any difficulty completing this form, you can contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall experience**: Please describe your overall experience in our nursery school this year. 
* **How would you rate our teaching?**: Rate our teaching staff based on your experience. The options are:
	+ Excellent: Our teaching staff exceeded your expectations.
	+ Good: Our teaching staff met your expectations.
	+ Fair: Our teaching staff was average, with some room for improvement.
	+ Poor: Our teaching staff fell short of your expectations.
* **How would you rate our teachers?**: Rate your experience with your child's teachers. The options are:
	+ Excellent: Your child's teacher exceeded your expectations.
	+ Good: Your child's teacher met your expectations.
	+ Fair: Your child's teacher was average, with some room for improvement.
	+ Poor: Your child's teacher fell short of your expectations.
* **How would you rate our care and safety?**: Rate our care and safety measures in the nursery school. The options are:
	+ Excellent: Our care and safety measures exceeded your expectations.
	+ Good: Our care and safety measures met your expectations.
	+ Fair: Our care and safety measures were average, with some room for improvement.
	+ Poor: Our care and safety measures fell short of your expectations.
* **What improvements can we make for the next year?**: Suggest any improvements you think we can make to better serve you and your family next year.
* **Email address**: Please enter your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Phone number**: Please enter your phone number so we can contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Additional comments**: Provide any other comments or suggestions you may have about your experience in our nursery school.

Please note that all fields are optional, but providing feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us to improve our services.
