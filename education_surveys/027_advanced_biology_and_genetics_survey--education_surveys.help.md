# Advanced Biology And Genetics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather your feedback and survey responses about your experience in the Advanced Biology and Genetics course. Your input will help us to better understand your understanding and engagement with the course material.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and make sure you answer them as honestly as possible.
2. For each question, select the response that best reflects your opinion.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Respondent name**: This is an optional field where you can enter your name, but it is not required.
	* Type: `text`
	* Required: `false`
	* Hint: Optional
- **Respondent name** is meant to allow you to provide a name, but it's not a requirement.
- **Academic level**: This question asks about your academic level, whether you are an undergraduate, graduate, postgraduate, or have a professional degree.
	* Type: `select_one`
	* Required: `true`
	* Options: Undergraduate, Graduate, Postgraduate, Professional
- **Comprehension of genetics topics**: Rate your understanding of the course material after completing the course.
	* Type: `select_one`
	* Required: `true`
	* Options: Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent
- **Lab experience quality**: Rate the quality of lab exercises and supervision in the course.
	* Type: `select_one`
	* Required: `true`
	* Options: Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent
- **Recommended resources**: If you have any recommendations for readings or tools that would help you in the future, please enter them here.
	* Type: `text`
	* Required: `false`
	* Hint: Suggest readings or tools
- **Future topics of interest**: Select all topics that you are interested in learning more about in the future.
	* Type: `select_multiple`
	* Required: `false`
	* Options: CRISPR and gene editing, Genomics data analysis, Molecular techniques, Population genetics
- **Overall satisfaction rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the course from 1 to 5.
	* Type: `select_one`
	* Required: `true`
	* Options: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5
- **Additional comments**: If you have any additional feedback or comments for the instructor, please enter them here.
	* Type: `text`
	* Required: `false`
	* Hint: Feedback for instructors
