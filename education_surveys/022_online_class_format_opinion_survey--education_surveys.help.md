# Online Class Format Opinion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Class Format Opinion Survey is designed to collect opinions from users about the online class format. This survey aims to gather feedback on their preferences, experiences, and challenges with online classes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your school or department name in the field marked "What is your school or department name".
2. Select your role in the field marked "What is your role".
3. Choose your preferred online class format from the options provided in the field marked "Which online class format do you like best".
4. If you selected "Other" as your preferred online class format, please specify in the field marked "Other (Please specify)".
5. Rate your experience with online classes by selecting a number in the field marked "How would you rate your experience with online classes".
6. Rate the difficulty level of the online class by selecting a number in the field marked "How would you rate the difficulty level of the online class".
7. In the field marked "Which challenges do you face when taking online classes", select all that apply from the options provided.
8. If you face other challenges not listed, please specify in the field marked "Other (Please specify)".
9. Finally, use the field marked "Comments" to provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your school or department name**: This is a required field where you should enter the name of your school or department.
- **What is your role**: This is a required field where you should select your role (e.g., student, instructor, staff).
- **Which online class format do you like best**: This is a required field where you should select your preferred online class format from the options provided. The options are:
	+ Online Video
	+ Online Audio
	+ Online Text
	+ Online Interactive
	+ Online Live
	+ Online Self-Paced
	+ Other (Please specify)
- **Other (Please specify)**: This is a text field where you should provide more information if you selected "Other" as your preferred online class format.
- **How would you rate your experience with online classes**: This is a required field where you should select a number to rate your experience with online classes.
- **How would you rate the difficulty level of the online class**: This is a required field where you should select a number to rate the difficulty level of the online class.
- **Which challenges do you face when taking online classes**: This is a required field where you should select all that apply from the options provided. The options are:
	+ Poor internet connection
	+ Difficulty in accessing course materials
	+ Difficulty in engaging with instructors and peers
	+ Difficulty in understanding course content
	+ Other (Please specify)
- **Other (Please specify)**: This is a text field where you should provide more information if you selected "Other" as one of your challenges.
- **Comments**: This is an optional field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Tips
- Please answer all required fields to ensure your responses are complete.
- Use numbers to rate your experience and difficulty level, where 1 means very easy/difficult and 10 means very hard/easy.
- Select all that apply for the challenges field to provide a comprehensive list of your challenges.
