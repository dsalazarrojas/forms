# Doctoral Program Monthly Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doctoral Program Monthly Check In form is designed to help you track your progress and stay on top of your doctoral studies. This form guides you in reviewing your monthly progress, noting challenges and achievements, and setting goals for the next month. It's a tool to help you stay organized and focused on your academic goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form fields as honestly and thoroughly as possible, providing detailed information about your progress, challenges, and goals.
2. Review each field carefully and make sure to explain your answers clearly.
3. Take your time to reflect on your progress and goals for the next month.
4. Submit the form by the end of each month.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Monthly Progress** (`Monthly Progress`, text, required/optional): Describe your progress on your doctoral program over the past month. This includes any accomplishments, new skills acquired, or notable events that have impacted your studies.
* **Progress Challenges** (`Progress Challenges`, text, required/optional): List any challenges you faced during the past month, such as difficulties in research, obstacles in finding resources, or other issues that hindered your progress.
* **Goals** (`Goals`, text, required/optional): Outline your goals for the next month, including any new skills you want to acquire, research topics you want to explore, or milestones you aim to achieve.
* **Monthly Challenges** (`Monthly Challenges`, text, required/optional): Describe any ongoing or upcoming challenges you anticipate facing in the next month, such as upcoming deadlines, conferences, or other events that may impact your progress.
* **Progress Notes** (`Progress Notes`, text, required/optional): Record any notable events, thoughts, or insights that have shaped your understanding or approach to your research topic.
* **Next Steps** (`Next Steps`, text, required/optional): Outline the concrete steps you plan to take in the next month to move forward with your research and meet your goals.
* **Goals Review** (`Goals Review`, text, required/optional): Review and refine your goals for the next month, ensuring they remain relevant and achievable.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback that can help you or your supervisor track your progress and adjust your goals.
* **Next Month** (`Next Month`, time, required/optional): Estimate the date you expect to submit your next monthly progress update, providing a timeline for tracking your progress over time.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when describing your progress, challenges, and goals.
* Use clear and concise language to ensure your responses are easy to read and understand.
* Reflect on your progress regularly, and use this form as a tool to adjust and refine your goals and approach as needed.
* Keep your goals SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound) to ensure you're making progress towards them.
