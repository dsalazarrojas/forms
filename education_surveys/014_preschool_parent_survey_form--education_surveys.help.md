# Preschool Parent Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Parent Survey Form is designed to collect your opinions and feedback about your child's experience at the preschool. It aims to understand your satisfaction with various aspects of our services and how we can improve to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by answering each question carefully and honestly to ensure your feedback is accurate and helpful.
- Make sure to fill out all the necessary fields, as indicated by the required fields.
- You can add any additional comments or suggestions you'd like to share with us in the text boxes provided.
- Once you've completed the form, review it for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Satisfaction** (required): Please describe your overall satisfaction with our preschool program, including your child's academic, social, and emotional growth.
- **Parent Communication** (required): Please share your thoughts on how well we have communicated with you about your child's daily activities, needs, and progress.
- **Parent Participation** (required): Share your level of engagement in your child's preschool education, including attending events, volunteer hours, or participating in school activities.
- **Parent Relationship** (required): Describe the quality of the relationship between you and our preschool staff, including their support and responsiveness to your concerns.
- **Satisfaction-Survey-Question-5** (text): Please answer the fifth question on the survey. 
- **Satisfaction-Survey-Question-6** (text): Please answer the sixth question on the survey. 
- **Satisfaction-Survey-Question-7** (text): Please answer the seventh question on the survey. 

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when answering each question to provide valuable feedback.
- If you have any additional thoughts or suggestions, please include them in the text boxes.
- Your feedback is confidential, and we value your input to make our preschool an even better place for your child.
