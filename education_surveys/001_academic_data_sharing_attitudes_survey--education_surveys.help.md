# Academic Data Sharing Attitudes Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on your attitudes and experiences with academic data sharing. Your responses will help us understand your willingness to share research data and identify potential barriers or challenges associated with data sharing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each field with the corresponding information. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the options that best match your answer. For text and email fields, provide your full name and institutional email address if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: Provide your full name to enable us to contact you if necessary.
* **Institution or affiliation**: Enter your university, research group, or organization name.
* **Contact email**: Provide your institutional or preferred email address where we can contact you.
* **Primary role**: Select the option that best describes your role in research (e.g., student, postdoctoral researcher, faculty member, etc.).
* **Disciplinary area**: This field is optional but helps us understand your main research area. You can leave this field blank if you don't want to disclose this information.
* **Have you shared research data previously**: Check the option that best describes your experience with sharing data (e.g., yes frequently, yes occasionally, no but plan to, no and do not plan to).
* **Willingness to share future data**: Rate your willingness to share data for future research on a 1 to 5 scale where 5 is very willing.
* **Major barriers to sharing data**: Select all that apply from the list of potential barriers.
* **Support that would increase data sharing**: Choose up to three options from the list that you think would make you more comfortable with sharing your data.
* **Types of data you are willing to share**: Select all that apply from the list of data types.
* **Preferred repositories or platforms**: If you have a preferred repository or platform where you share your data, please enter the name.
* **Additional comments or concerns**: Provide any further thoughts or concerns you may have about data sharing.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns about data sharing, use the "Additional comments or concerns" field to express them.
