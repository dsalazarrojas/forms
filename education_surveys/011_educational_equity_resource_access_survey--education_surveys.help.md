# Educational Equity Resource Access Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your experiences with accessing educational resources. Your responses will help us identify areas where we can improve resource accessibility for students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and student ID on pages 1 and 2, respectively.
2. Choose the resources you need access to on page 3.
3. Indicate whether you are able to access the resources you need on page 4.
4. If you are unable to access a resource, specify the time you are unable to access it on page 10.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback, type them on page 6.
6. If you have a specific resource in mind that you would like to access, describe it on page 7.
7. Provide your contact information on page 8.
8. Finally, if you have any further comments on your resource access experience, you can add them on page 9.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name?** (`student_info_1`, Text, Required): Please fill in your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **What is your student ID?** (`student_info_2`, Text, Required): Enter your student ID number as it appears on your student ID card.
* **What resources do you need access to?** (`resource_access_1`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose all the resources you need access to, such as Library materials, Online resources, or Other (please describe).
* **Are you able to access the resources you need?** (`resource_access_2`, Select One, Required): Indicate whether you are able to access the resources you need, Yes or No.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Type any additional comments or feedback about your resource access experience.
* **Do you have a specific resource you would like to access?** (`resource_request_1`, Text, Optional): Describe the specific resource you would like to access.
* **Please provide your contact information for follow-up.** (`contact_info_1`, Text, Optional): Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **What is your email address?** (`contact_info_2`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Are there any specific times when you are unable to access resources?** (`resource_access_3`, Date, Optional): Select a date when you are unable to access resources.
* **Are there any specific time of days when you are unable to access resources?** (`resource_access_4`, Time, Optional): Select a time of day when you are unable to access resources.
* **Please include any additional information about your resource access experience.** (`note_1`, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments about your resource access experience.
