# Father Son Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the relationship between a father and his son. It aims to gauge how well the father is involved in his son's learning process, the quality of their communication, and other aspects that affect their relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2.  Answer each question based on your experiences and observations.
3.  Be honest and provide specific examples when answering questions that ask for details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Contact** (`first_contact`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is used to describe the first time you had contact with your son. Please write a brief description of this event.
*   **Communication** (`communication`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is used to rate the quality of communication between you and your son. Please answer on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
*   **Father Involvement** (`father_involvement`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is used to rate how involved you are in your son's learning process. Please answer on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is not involved at all and 5 is very involved.
*   **Mother Involvement** (`mother_involvement`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is used to rate how involved your mother is in your son's learning process. Please answer on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is not involved at all and 5 is very involved.
*   **Parent Child Relation** (`parent_child_relation`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is used to describe the relationship between you and your son. Please write a brief description of your relationship.
*   **Learning Environment** (`learning_environment`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is used to describe the environment in which your son learns. Please write a brief description of the environment.
*   **Parent Child Conflict** (`parent_child_conflict`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is used to describe any conflicts that you have with your son. Please write a brief description of any conflicts.
*   **Learning Goals** (`learning_goals`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is used to describe the goals you have for your son's learning. Please write a brief description of your goals.
*   **Parent Child Closeness** (`parent_child_closeness`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is used to rate how close you feel to your son. Please answer on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is not close at all and 5 is very close.

## Tips

*   Please answer each question honestly and accurately.
*   If you are unsure about any question, please try to provide the best possible answer you can.
*   The more information you provide, the more helpful this survey will be to us.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
