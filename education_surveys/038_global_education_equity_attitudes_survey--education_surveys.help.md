# Global Education Equity Attitudes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Education Equity Attitudes Survey is a tool used to collect information about participants' attitudes towards education, covering various aspects such as institution types, educational fields, and demographics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the correct option for each question, ensuring that you are answering truthfully.
2.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3.  Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Education Survey Global** (1): This is the first question of the survey. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are participating in the survey.
* **Institution Type** (2): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the type of institution where you received your education.
* **Survey Participation Type** (3): Indicate whether you participated in the survey.
* **Participant ID** (4): Enter your participant ID.
* **Email** (5): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (6): You can enter your phone number.
* **Gender** (7): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your gender.
* **Education Level** (8): Select 'High' or 'Low' to indicate your level of education.
* **Education Field** (9): Indicate whether you are in a specific education field or not.
* **Highest Education Level** (10): Select 'High' or 'Low' to indicate the highest education level you have attained.
* **Highest Degree Awarded** (11): Enter the date of the highest degree you have received.
* **Year of Education Beginning** (12): Enter the year you started your education.
* **Year of Education Completion** (13): Enter the year you completed your education.
* **Years of Education Participation** (14): Enter the number of years you have participated in education.
* **Education Institution** (15): Enter the name of the institution where you received your education.
* **Education Institution URL** (16): Enter the URL of the institution where you received your education.
* **Education Program** (17): Indicate whether you are in an education program or not.
* **Education Program Other** (18): If you are in an education program, enter the name of the program.
* **Participant Notes** (19): Enter any additional comments or notes about your education.
* **Ethnicity** (20): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you belong to an ethnic group.
* **First Language** (21): Indicate whether your first language is 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Highest Level of Education Attained** (22): Select 'High' or 'Low' to indicate the highest level of education you have attained.
* **Highest Degree** (23): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have a highest degree.
* **Years of Education** (24): Enter the number of years you have participated in education.
* **Education Institution Country** (25): Enter the country where the institution is located.
