# Digital Resource Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Resource Usage Survey is designed to gather information about the types of digital resources you use in your educational activities. This will help us understand your needs and preferences, ensuring we provide you with the best possible resources to support your learning.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please go through each page of the form carefully.
2. For each field, provide the information that best describes your answer.
3. You can save your progress and continue later, if needed.
4. Submit the form when you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a brief overview of the form, please read it before proceeding.
* **Audience**: Describe the people or groups you are using these digital resources for, e.g., students, teachers, researchers.
* **Resource Type**: Select the type of digital resource you use (Books, Articles, Videos, or Websites).
* **Frequency of Use**: Indicate how frequently you use these digital resources, e.g., Daily, Weekly, Rarely.
* **Duration of Use**: Specify how long you typically spend using these digital resources, e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour, more than 2 hours.
* **Purpose of Use**: Choose all applicable reasons why you use these digital resources, e.g., Research, Training, Reference, Other (please specify).
* **Difficulty Level**: Select how challenging or easy you find these digital resources to use, e.g., Easy, Medium, Hard.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the digital resources you use.

Please note that this form is designed to gather your honest and accurate feedback. Thank you for your participation!
