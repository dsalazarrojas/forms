# Open Access Publishing Attitudes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the attitudes and experiences of researchers regarding open access publishing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select one of the provided options that best represent your opinion or experience.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What are your research goals** (`page_1`, text, optional): This question aims to understand the goals you have for your research. Please provide a brief description of your research goals.
* **What do you perceive as your top research priority** (`researcher_goals_3`, select_one, optional): This question asks about the priority you give to open access publishing in your research goals. You can select one of the provided options.
* **How important is open access publishing in your research** (`open_access_2`, number, optional): This question measures your level of importance for open access publishing in your research. Please select one of the provided options.
* **What is your primary research field** (`research_field_1`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks about your research field. Select all the fields that apply to your research.
* **Email address** (`email_address_1`, email, optional): This question asks for your email address, which will be used to contact you if needed.
* **How much time do you spend on OA publishing activities each month** (`time_1`, time, optional): This question measures the time you spend on open access publishing activities each month.
* **How do you currently use OA publishing** (`open_access_publishing_3`, select_one, optional): This question asks about your experience with open access publishing. Select one of the provided options.
* **How do you perceive the benefits of OA publishing** (`open_access_publishing_2`, select_one, optional): This question gauges your perception of the benefits of open access publishing. Select one of the provided options.
* **Have you published OA** (`open_access_publishing_4`, select_one, optional): This question asks if you have published open access. Select one of the provided options.
* **How satisfied are you with the OA publishing process** (`open_access_publishing_5`, select_one, optional): This question measures your level of satisfaction with the open access publishing process. Select one of the provided options.
* **What are the main barriers to OA publishing** (`open_access_publishing_6`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks about the barriers to open access publishing. Select all the provided options that apply to you.
* **What do you think is the main advantage of OA publishing** (`open_access_publishing_7`, select_multiple, optional): This question gauges your perception of the main advantage of open access publishing. Select all the provided options that apply to you.
* **Have you ever submitted a manuscript through a preprint server** (`open_access_publishing_8`, select_one, optional): This question asks about your experience with preprint servers. Select one of the provided options.
* **How much time do you usually spend on manuscript preparation** (`time_2`, number, optional): This question measures the time you usually spend on manuscript preparation.
* **What do you prefer** (`open_access_publishing_9`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks about your preference regarding preprint, peer-review, or other options.
* **How satisfied are you with OA publishing** (`open_access_publishing_10`, select_one, optional): This question measures your level of satisfaction with open access publishing. Select one of the provided options.
* **Additional comments** (`note_1`, note, optional): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about open access publishing.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions truthfully to provide valuable insights.
- If you are unsure about an option, select the one that best represents your opinion or experience.
- If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "Additional comments" field.
