# youth_risk_behavior_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The youth_risk_behavior_survey is a form designed to collect data on risk behaviors among students. This form is intended to gather information on various aspects of student life, including sports participation, substance use, driving habits, fighting, and bullying.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the introduction page (page 1) for general information about the survey.
2. Answer each question on the subsequent pages, selecting the response that best applies to your situation.
3. Make sure to answer all required questions, as indicated by the "required" label next to each question.
4. For optional questions, you may choose not to answer if you feel it's not relevant to your situation.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
