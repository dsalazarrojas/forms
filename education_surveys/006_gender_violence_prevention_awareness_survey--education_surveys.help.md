# Gender Violence Prevention Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to raise awareness and gather data on the prevalence and nature of gender-based violence or abuse within the target population.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form carefully, providing honest answers to all questions.
2.  Ensure that you answer all questions to the best of your ability.
3.  For questions that ask for a specific date or time, please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD` or `HH:MM:SS` respectively.
4.  For questions that ask for a frequency of occurrence (e.g., "How often have you experienced violence or abuse?"), please choose one of the options provided.
5.  For questions that ask for a number (e.g., "How many people have you known who have experienced violence or abuse?"), please enter the actual number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Information**: This section is for demographic information about you.
    *   **Contact Details**: This is where you can enter your contact information, such as your name, email, and phone number.
    *   **Email**: Please enter your email address.
    *   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Experiences with Violence or Abuse**: This section asks about your personal experiences with violence or abuse.
    *   **Have you ever experienced violence or abuse?**: Answer whether you have ever experienced violence or abuse. Select one of the options provided.
    *   **When did you first experience violence or abuse?**: Provide the date of your first experience with violence or abuse.
    *   **Have you ever known someone who has experienced violence or abuse?**: Answer if you have ever known someone who has experienced violence or abuse.
    *   **When did you experience the first instance of violence or abuse?**: Choose one of the provided options regarding when you first noticed the violence or abuse.
*   **Frequency of Experiences with Violence or Abuse**: This section asks about how often you have experienced or known someone with experienced violence or abuse.
    *   **Have you experienced more than one type of violence or abuse?**: Select all the types of violence or abuse you have experienced.
    *   **How long did the abuse or violence last?**: Enter the duration of the abuse or violence.
*   **Demographics and Additional Information**: This section provides information about your demographics and additional details.
    *   **How often have you experienced violence or abuse?**: Choose the frequency of your experiences with violence or abuse.
    *   **How old were you when it happened?**: Enter your age at the time of the violence or abuse.
    *   **How many people have you known who have experienced violence or abuse?**: Enter the number of people you know who have experienced violence or abuse.
*   **Additional Information**: You can provide additional information about your experiences with violence or abuse in this section.
*   **Consent and Second Consent**: You must consent to sharing your information, and confirm your consent to ensure that your data is used in line with this survey's purpose.
    *   **Second Consent**: Confirm your consent once again.
*   **Consent**: Confirm that you have read and understand the survey's purpose and that you are providing your information voluntarily.

## Tips

*   Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
*   If you need help or guidance on any of the questions, do not hesitate to ask.
*   Your responses will be kept confidential and used to inform educational programs and support services for individuals affected by gender-based violence or abuse.
