# Sociology Student Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sociology Student Feedback Survey is designed to gather your opinion and feedback on the sociology course you are taking. Your input will help us understand your experience and improve the course for future students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best represents your opinion.
3. For open-ended questions, provide a short description of your experience.
4. Make sure to submit all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your overall impression of the course?** (`overall_impression`, text, *): This question asks for a short description of your experience in the course. Please provide your honest opinion.
* **How satisfied are you with the course?** (`course_satisfaction`, number, *): This question asks how satisfied you are with the course. Please select the number that best represents your satisfaction.
* **Were you provided with enough resources and materials?** (`materials_provision`, select_multiple, *): This question asks about your experience with the resources and materials provided in the course. You can select one or more of the options that best represent your experience.
* **How well was the course structured?** (`course_structure`, select_multiple, *): This question asks about the organization and flow of the course content. You can select one or more of the options that best represent your opinion.
* **How would you rate the course?** (`course_rating`, select_one, *): This question asks you to rate the course overall. Please select the option that best represents your opinion.
* **How would you rate the instructor?** (`instructor_impression`, select_one, *): This question asks you to rate the instructor's performance. Please select the option that best represents your opinion.
* **How would you rate the instructor's teaching effectiveness?** (`instructor_effectiveness`, select_one, *): This question asks about the instructor's ability to teach and communicate the course material. Please select the option that best represents your opinion.
* **Do you have any comments or suggestions?** (`comments`, note, *): This question is an opportunity for you to share any thoughts or suggestions you have about the course. Please provide a short description of your feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, email, *): This question asks for your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Department** (`department`, text, *): This question asks about the department you are enrolled in, if any.
* **Instructor Rating** (`instructor_rating`, select_one, *): This question asks you to rate the instructor's performance. Please select the option that best represents your opinion.
* **Course Rating 2** (`course_rating_2`, select_one, *): This question asks you to rate the course overall, as a confirmation of your previous answer.
* **Instructor Rating 2** (`instructor_rating_2`, select_one, *): This question asks you to rate the instructor's performance, as a confirmation of your previous answer.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* Be as specific as possible when providing open-ended answers.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us.
* Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve the course and provide better student experience.
