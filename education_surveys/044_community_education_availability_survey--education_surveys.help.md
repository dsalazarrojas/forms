# Education Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your preferences and availability for education classes. Please provide your information accurately to help us tailor our services to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select your respondent type from the options provided.
2. Choose your topics of interest from the available options.
3. Select your preferred learning format (in person, online live, online self-paced, or hybrid).
4. If you have childcare needs during classes, mark whether you require support.
5. You can skip the preferred days and times fields if you don't have a preference.
6. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **1. Respondent type** (Respondent type, select_one, required)
* Select whether you are a resident educator, parent, or other.

### **2. Topics of interest** (Topics of interest, select_multiple, required)
* Choose all the learning topics that interest you from the list:
	+ Basic literacy
	+ Computer skills
	+ Job training
	+ Parenting classes
	+ Financial literacy

### **3. Preferred learning format** (Preferred learning format, select_one, required)
* Choose how you prefer to learn:
	+ In person
	+ Online live
	+ Online self-paced
	+ Hybrid

### **4. Preferred days** (Preferred days, text, optional)
* If you have a preference for days when you are available to attend, enter them here.

### **5. Preferred times** (Preferred times, select_one, optional)
* Choose your preferred times for classes:
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening

### **6. Childcare needs** (Childcare needs, select_one, optional)
* Mark whether you require childcare support:
	+ True
	+ False
