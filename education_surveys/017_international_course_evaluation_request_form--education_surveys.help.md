# International Course Evaluation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from international course participants on their overall satisfaction and experience with the course. The feedback will help course organizers improve their international course offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Choose your session rating from the options provided.
2.  Select your overall satisfaction level from the options provided.
3.  Indicate what topic(s) you found most valuable during the session.
4.  Answer if the course materials were helpful or not.
5.  Rate the course presentation as clear and engaging or not.
6.  Evaluate your instructor's performance.
7.  Choose how long you found the course to be.
8.  Provide any additional comments or feedback about the course.
9.  Enter your contact email and phone number for follow-up purposes.
