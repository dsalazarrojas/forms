# Advanced Degree Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advanced Degree Program Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from respondents about their experiences with an advanced degree program. The form helps us understand how well the program meets the needs of its students and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and read each section and question carefully.
2. Answer all required questions. If a question is not applicable to your experience, please indicate that it is not relevant.
3. Be as specific and honest as possible in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### -- Program Evaluation --
This is the introduction to the form. It explains that the form is for evaluating an advanced degree program.

### Respondent name
* **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required/optional): This field asks for your name, but you can leave it blank if you prefer to remain anonymous.

### Program name
* **Program name** (`program_name`, `text`, required/true): This field asks for the title of the program you are evaluating.

### Curriculum relevance to career goals
* **Curriculum relevance** (`curriculum_relevance`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate how well the program's curriculum meets your career goals. Choose from:
	+ Not relevant
	+ Somewhat relevant
	+ Relevant
	+ Highly relevant

### Faculty quality and engagement
* **Faculty quality** (`faculty_quality`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate the quality of the faculty, including teaching and support. Choose from:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent

### Research opportunities availability
* **Research opportunities** (`research_opportunities`, `select_one`, optional/true): Rate the availability and support for research opportunities. Choose from:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent

### Career services support quality
* **Career services support** (`career_services_support`, `select_one`, optional/true): Rate the quality of career services support. Choose from:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent

### Overall satisfaction
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program. Choose from:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5

### Suggestions for improvement
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional/true): Provide any suggestions or feedback you have for improving the program.

## Tips
* Be honest and specific in your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
