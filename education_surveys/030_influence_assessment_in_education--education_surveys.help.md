# Influence Assessment In Education - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Influence Assessment In Education form is a comprehensive survey designed to gather information from various stakeholders in the education sector, including teachers, principals, school administrators, parents, and students. It aims to collect data on their influence and perceptions on educational policies, procedures, and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the section that best corresponds to your role in the education sector:
	* Teachers: Teacher Survey
	* Principals: Principal Survey
	* School Administrators: School Admins Survey
	* Parents: Parents Survey
	* Students: Student Survey
	* Other Roles: Other Survey
2. Provide detailed and accurate answers to the questions in the selected section.
3. Be honest and specific when answering the questions.
4. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification or provide a brief explanation.
