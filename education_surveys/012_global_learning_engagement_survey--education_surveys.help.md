# Global Learning Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from users about their engagement with a presentation or program. Your responses will help us understand your feelings about the content, self-study, and overall design and user experience of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully about your experience with the program.
2. Choose the options that best reflect your feelings and thoughts about each question.
3. Make sure to complete all questions, but you can skip any questions that do not apply to you.
