# Academic Peer Feedback Culture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on peer feedback culture within an institution, including the frequency and perceived value of feedback, as well as barriers and suggestions for improvement. It aims to provide insights that can be used to enhance the culture of peer feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each page thoroughly, providing accurate and honest answers.
- Select the best option that reflects your role within the institution (Faculty, Staff, Student).
- Choose the frequency of peer feedback in your unit (Weekly, Monthly, Semesterly, Rarely).
- Rate the perceived value of peer feedback (Very valuable, Valuable, Neutral, Not valuable).
- Identify barriers to effective peer feedback (Select all that apply).
- Provide practical ideas for improving peer feedback culture (Text field, approximately 1-2 paragraphs).
- Indicate your willingness to participate in pilot programs (True, Maybe, False).
- If you agree to be contacted for follow-up, enter your email address in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Respondent Information --**: This is an informational page and does not affect your answers or the overall survey. It is an optional page to allow respondents to enter their name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Role --**: Select your official role within the institution (Faculty, Staff, Student) to categorize your responses.
* **Department or unit --**: If applicable, enter your department or unit name to help us understand the context.
* **Frequency of peer feedback in your unit --**: Rate how often peer feedback occurs in your unit. Choose one of the options: Weekly, Monthly, Semesterly, Rarely.
* **Perceived value of peer feedback --**: Rate how valuable you perceive peer feedback to be. Choose one of the options: Very valuable, Valuable, Neutral, Not valuable.
* **Main barriers to effective peer feedback --**: Identify any barriers that hinder effective peer feedback. Select all that apply from the options: Time constraints, Lack of training, Fear of conflict, Insufficient recognition.
* **Suggestions to improve peer feedback --**: Share practical ideas for enhancing peer feedback culture in the comments field.
* **Willingness to participate in pilot programs --**: Indicate your interest in participating in pilot programs to improve peer feedback.
* **Contact email for follow up --**: Leave your email address if you agree to be contacted for follow-up discussions or further questions.
