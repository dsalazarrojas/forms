# Peer Based Curriculum Evaluation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from reviewers for the peer-based curriculum evaluation process. It helps us to understand your preferences and needs for participating in the evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your institution's name and your name and contact information.
2.  Choose your preferred evaluation method and type.
3.  Select the frequency of your evaluations.
4.  Provide any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Institution Name** (Institution Name, text, optional): Enter the name of your institution.
* **Reviewer Name** (Reviewer Name, text, optional): Enter your name as the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email** (Reviewer Email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Reviewer Phone** (Reviewer Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Review Start Date** (Review Start Date, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to start reviewing.
* **Review End Date** (Review End Date, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to end reviewing.
* **Evaluation Method** (Evaluation Method, select_one, optional): Select one evaluation method:
	+ Self-evaluation
	+ Peer-evaluation
	+ Manager-evaluation
* **Other Evaluation Method** (Other Evaluation Method, text, optional): Enter any other evaluation method not listed above.
* **Evaluation Type** (Evaluation Type, select_multiple, optional): Select the type(s) of evaluation:
	+ Program
	+ Course
	+ Both
* **Other Evaluation Type** (Other Evaluation Type, text, optional): Enter any other evaluation type not listed above.
* **Department** (Department, text, optional): Enter the department you are evaluating.
* **Course Name** (Course Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the course you are evaluating.
* **Course Code** (Course Code, text, optional): Enter the course code.
* **Semester** (Semester, text, optional): Enter the semester you are evaluating.
* **Evaluation Start Date** (Evaluation Start Date, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to start evaluating.
* **Evaluation End Date** (Evaluation End Date, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to end evaluating.
* **Evaluation Start Time** (Evaluation Start Time, time, optional): Enter the start time of your evaluation.
* **Evaluation End Time** (Evaluation End Time, time, optional): Enter the end time of your evaluation.
* **Number of Evaluations** (Number of Evaluations, number, optional): Enter the number of evaluations you plan to perform.
* **Evaluation Frequency** (Evaluation Frequency, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of your evaluations:
	+ Once
	+ Twice
	+ Three times
* **Other Evaluation Frequency** (Other Evaluation Frequency, text, optional): Enter any other evaluation frequency not listed above.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your evaluation.
* **Assigned User** (Assigned User, text, optional): Enter the user assigned to you for evaluation.
* **Evaluation Status** (Evaluation Status, select_one, optional): Select the status of your evaluation:
	+ Not Started
	+ Completed
	+ Ongoing
* **Other Evaluation Status** (Other Evaluation Status, text, optional): Enter any other evaluation status not listed above.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out this form accurately and completely to avoid duplication of effort.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
