# School Withdrawal Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students who are withdrawing from a school. The purpose of this form is to understand their reasons for withdrawal, which can help the school administration to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the form carefully and answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
* For select multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
* For open-ended questions, provide detailed and clear responses.
* Take your time and ask for help if you need it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`personal_info`, text, not required):
	+ This field is for providing personal information about yourself, such as your name and any other relevant details.
* **Who Filled Out This Form** (`who_filled`, select_one, not required):
	+ Please select the category that best applies to you.
	+ Options: Student, Teacher, Parent, Other (Please Specify)
* **Grade** (`grade`, number, not required):
	+ Please enter your grade level (e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, etc.).
* **Thoughts** (`thoughts`, text, not required):
	+ This field is for providing any thoughts or comments you have about your experience at this school.
* **Grade Point** (`grade_point`, number, not required):
	+ Please enter your GPA or grade point average (e.g., 2.5, 3.5, etc.).
* **Grade Scale** (`grade_scale`, select_one, not required):
	+ Please select the grade scale that best reflects your current grade level.
	+ Options: A, B, C, D, F
* **Grade Note** (`grade_note`, text, not required):
	+ This field is for providing any additional notes or comments about your grade level.
* **Disappointments** (`disappointments`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ Please select all that apply to provide a comprehensive list of your disappointments while at this school.
	+ Options: School Workloads, Lack of Support, Too Busy, Other (Please Specify)
* **Why Are They Leaving** (`reason_leaving`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ Please select all that apply to provide a comprehensive list of reasons why you are leaving this school.
	+ Options: Lack of interest, Family Move, Health Reasons, Other (Please Specify)

## Tips

* Answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Take your time and ask for help if you need it.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the school administration.
