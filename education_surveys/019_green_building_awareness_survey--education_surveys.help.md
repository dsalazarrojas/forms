# Green Building Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Green Building Awareness Survey is designed to gauge users' awareness and experiences with green buildings, gathering information on their attitudes, knowledge, and experiences to inform the development of education and training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
4. Optionally, provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the survey and its purpose.
* **Contact**: Enter your email address.
* **Green Building Knowledge**: Indicate your level of knowledge about green buildings:
	+ High: You have a good understanding of green building concepts, materials, and practices.
	+ Medium: You have some knowledge, but could use more information or clarification.
	+ Low: You have limited or no knowledge about green buildings.
* **Attitudes Toward Sustainability**: Indicate your level of agreement with sustainable practices:
	+ Strongly Agree: You wholeheartedly support and apply sustainable practices.
	+ Somewhat Agree: You somewhat support and apply sustainable practices.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral or indifferent to sustainable practices.
	+ Somewhat Disagree: You somewhat disagree with sustainable practices.
	+ Strongly Disagree: You do not support or apply sustainable practices.
* **Experiences with Green Buildings**: Check all experiences you have had with green buildings:
	+ Yes: You have experienced or interacted with green buildings.
	+ No: You have not experienced or interacted with green buildings.
* **Sustainability Programs**: Indicate your level of engagement with sustainability programs:
	+ High: You are actively involved or have participated in sustainability programs.
	+ Medium: You have some involvement or participation in sustainability programs.
	+ Low: You have limited or no involvement in sustainability programs.
	+ None: You have no involvement in sustainability programs.
* **Education**: Check all education or training received on green buildings:
	+ Yes: You have received education or training on green buildings.
	+ No: You have not received education or training on green buildings.
* **Training**: Check all training or workshops attended on green buildings:
	+ Yes: You have attended training or workshops on green buildings.
	+ No: You have not attended training or workshops on green buildings.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback on the survey.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Comments**: Check if you are willing to be contacted for further feedback or discussion:
	+ Yes: You are willing to be contacted for further feedback or discussion.
	+ No: You are not willing to be contacted for further feedback or discussion.
