# E Learning Improvement Research Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The E Learning Improvement Research Consent Form is designed to collect information and consent from participants in an educational research study. This form is typically used to gather feedback, demographic information, or to ask for permission to use participant data.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and the questions asked.
2. Fill in your information accurately and honestly.
3. Make sure to ask any questions you have about the form or the research study.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1 (Page 1)**: This page provides a general note about the form and its purpose.
* **Participant Information (Page 2)**: Fill in your name, email address, and any other demographic information required.
* **Research Study (Page 3)**: Select the option that best describes the research study you are participating in.
* **Participant Data (Page 4)**: Choose the option that best describes your data preferences.
* **Consent (Page 5)**: Enter your email address to confirm your consent.
* **Participant Consent (Page 6)**: Date and sign your consent.
* **Contact Us (Page 7)**: If you have any questions or need to contact us, fill in your contact information.
* **Additional Comments (Page 8)**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the research study.
* **Participant Declaration (Page 9)**: Choose the option that best describes your declaration.
* **Participant Declaration 2 (Page 10)**: Select multiple options that best describe your declaration.

## Tips

* Please fill in the form accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
