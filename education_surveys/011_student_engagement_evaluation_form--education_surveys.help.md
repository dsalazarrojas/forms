# Student Engagement Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Engagement Evaluation Form is designed to assess the level of student engagement in a course. It collects information on individual students, such as their student ID, name, course ID, and course name, as well as their self-reported engagement level, learning goals, teaching method, and comments. This form is intended to help instructors and educators evaluate student engagement and identify areas for improvement in their teaching methods and course design.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information to fill out the form accurately.
2. Enter the student ID, which is a unique identifier for each student.
3. Enter the student's name.
4. Enter the course ID, which is a unique identifier for each course.
5. Enter the course name.
6. Choose the student's engagement level from the provided options (High, Medium, Low).
7. Write a brief description of the student's learning goals.
8. Describe the teaching method used in the course.
9. Enter any additional comments about the student's engagement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Id** (student_id, Number, Optional): Enter the student's unique identifier.
* **Student Name** (student_name, Text, Optional): Enter the student's full name.
* **Course Id** (course_id, Number, Optional): Enter the course's unique identifier.
* **Course Name** (course_name, Text, Optional): Enter the course's name.
* **Engagement Level** (engagement_level, Select One, Optional): Choose the student's engagement level from the options High, Medium, Low.
* **Learning Goals** (learning_goals, Text, Optional): Describe the student's learning goals.
* **Teaching Method** (teaching_method, Text, Optional): Describe the teaching method used in the course.
* **Student Comments** (student_comments, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments about the student's engagement.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when entering student information.
* Choose the correct engagement level based on your observation or assessment.
* Keep the learning goals description brief but informative.
* Describe the teaching method clearly and concisely.
* Use the comments section to provide any additional context or feedback.
