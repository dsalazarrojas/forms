# Student Stress Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Stress Survey is designed to help students reflect on their levels of stress, identify potential sources of stress, and provide insights into their overall well-being. This survey is meant to be a quick and easy way for students to report on their personal experiences and feelings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly, considering how often you feel stressed or what activities cause you stress.
2. Select all that apply for each question, even if it's just "Never" or "Not at all".
3. For question 6, "What activities cause you most stress?", select all activities that apply.
4. For question 7, "Do you have a family history of stress?", "Do you have a history of anxiety?", "Do you have a history of depression?", and "Do you have a history of trauma?", answer based on your personal family history.
5. For question 9, "Have you experienced bullying?", answer based on your personal experiences.
6. For question 10, "Are you satisfied with your school life?", answer based on your overall satisfaction with school.
7. For question 11, "Have you experienced physical symptoms of stress?", answer based on your personal experiences.
8. For question 12, "Do you feel lonely?", answer based on your personal feelings.
9. For question 13, "Do you have difficulty sleeping?", answer based on your personal experiences.
10. For question 14, "How satisfied are you with your school life?", answer based on your overall satisfaction with school.
11. For question 15, "Are you satisfied with your teachers?", answer based on your overall satisfaction with teachers.
12. For question 16, "Do you have a support system?", answer based on your personal feelings.
13. For question 17, "Do you have a stress management plan?", answer based on your personal experiences.
14. For question 18, "Do you know how to manage stress?", answer based on your personal experiences.
15. For question 19, "Do you have any other comments?", provide any additional comments or concerns you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Questions**: This section contains questions designed to help us understand your overall levels of stress.
* **How often do you feel stressed?**: Select all that apply to indicate how often you feel stressed. (Never, Sometimes, Often, Almost Always)
* **What activities cause you most stress?**: Select all activities that apply to you feeling stressed. (Watching TV, Playing sports, Hanging out with friends, Homework)
* **Do you exercise regularly?**: Select all that apply to indicate how often you exercise. (Every day, Several times a week, Rarely)
* **How would you describe your current level of stress?**: Select all that apply to indicate your current level of stress. (Not at all stressed, A little stressed, Somewhat stressed, Very stressed)
* **Summarize your feelings**: Please summarize your feelings in the space provided.
* **Stress Score**: Enter a number between 1 and 10 to indicate your current level of stress.
* **Comments**: Please provide any additional comments or concerns you'd like to share.
* **Do you have a family history of stress?**: Answer based on your personal family history. (True, False)
* **Do you have a history of anxiety?**: Answer based on your personal history. (True, False)
* **Do you have a history of depression?**: Answer based on your personal history. (True, False)
* **Do you have a history of trauma?**: Answer based on your personal history. (True, False)
* **Have you experienced bullying?**: Answer based on your personal experiences. (True, False)
* **Are you satisfied with your school life?**: Answer based on your overall satisfaction with school. (Not satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Satisfied)
* **Have you experienced physical symptoms of stress?**: Answer based on your personal experiences. (Headaches, Fatigue, Insomnia, Stomach issues)
* **Do you feel lonely?**: Answer based on your personal feelings. (Never, Somewhat, Often)
* **Do you have difficulty sleeping?**: Answer based on your personal experiences. (Never, Sometimes, Frequently)
* **How satisfied are you with your school life?**: Answer based on your overall satisfaction with school. (Not at all, A little, Somewhat, Very)
* **Are you satisfied with your teachers?**: Answer based on your overall satisfaction with teachers. (Not satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Satisfied)
* **Do you have a support system?**: Answer based on your personal feelings. (True, False)
* **Do you have a stress management plan?**: Answer based on your personal experiences. (True, False)
* **Do you know how to manage stress?**: Answer based on your personal experiences. (True, False)
* **Other comments**: Please provide any additional comments or concerns you'd like to share.
