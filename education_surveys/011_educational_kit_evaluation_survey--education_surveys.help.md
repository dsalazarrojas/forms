# Educational Kit Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the educational kit you have accessed. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to ensure the kits meet your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and answer each question based on your experience with the educational kit.
2. Please answer all the questions that apply to you.
3. Provide as much detail as possible for each question.
4. Make sure to rate the overall quality of the kit on the scale of 1-5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How useful is the learning kit?**: This question is designed to gauge your experience with the kit's effectiveness in achieving its intended purpose. Please provide a brief description of how the kit met your needs or expectations.
* **What is the quality of the content?**: This question assesses the accuracy, clarity, and relevance of the information provided in the kit. Please rate the content on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
* **How easy is it to use?**: This question evaluates how user-friendly the kit is in terms of its structure, design, and navigation. Please provide an honest assessment of how easy or difficult it was to use the kit.
* **Overall rating (1-5)**: This question asks you to rate the kit on a scale of 1-5 based on your overall experience. Please consider the quality of the content, ease of use, and overall effectiveness when rating the kit.
* **Any additional feedback?**: This question provides an opportunity for you to share any comments, suggestions, or concerns you may have about the kit. Please use this space to give us a more in-depth understanding of your experience.
* **Email**: This is an optional field for you to provide your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further follow-up or discussion about your feedback.
* **Phone**: This is an optional field for you to provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or clarification on your feedback.
* **Date**: This field is for you to provide the date you accessed the educational kit.
* **Time**: This field is optional, but if you provide it, we'll know when you accessed the kit and used it for a specific period.
* **Any additional notes?**: This question allows you to provide any further comments or thoughts that may not fit in the other questions.
