# Factors Influencing Academic Success - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the factors that influence academic success.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the options that apply to your academic experience.
2. For text input fields, provide a brief description or answer to the question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Habits** (`study_habits`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the habits that you regularly practice in your academic studies.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you feel motivated to learn and participate in academic activities.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, text, required/optional): Describe your strategies for managing your time effectively.
* **Peer Pressure** (`peer_pressure`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you feel pressure from your peers to perform well academically.
* **Self Efficacy** (`self_efficacy`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you feel confident in your ability to learn and perform well academically.
* **Family Support** (`family_support`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you receive support from your family members for your academic activities.
* **Parent Pressure** (`parent_pressure`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you feel pressure from your parents to perform well academically.
* **Teacher Support** (`teacher_support`, text, required/optional): Describe the level of support you receive from your teachers.
* **Student Support** (`student_support`, text, required/optional): Describe the level of support you receive from your peers or classmates.
* **Financial Resources** (`financial_resources`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you have sufficient financial resources to support your educational needs.
* **Social Environment** (`social_environment`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the level of social interaction you have with your classmates or peers.
* **Personal Interest** (`personal_interest`, text, required/optional): Describe your level of interest in your subject matter.
* **Academic Integrity** (`academic_integrity`, text, required/optional): Describe your level of academic integrity or honesty.
* **Time Commitment** (`time_commitment`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you are able to commit a sufficient amount of time to your studies.
* **Peer Relationships** (`peer_relationships`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the types of relationships you have with your peers.
* **Self Regulation** (`self_regulation`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you are able to regulate your emotions and behaviors effectively.
* **Student Engagement** (`student_engagement`, text, required/optional): Describe your level of engagement in your academic activities.
* **Parent Involvement** (`parent_involvement`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the types of involvement your parents have in your academic activities.
* **Teacher Support Quality** (`teacher_support_quality`, text, required/optional): Describe the quality of support you receive from your teachers.
* **Self Confidence** (`self_confidence`, text, required/optional): Describe your level of self-confidence or self-efficacy.
* **Peer Support** (`peer_support`, text, required/optional): Describe the level of support you receive from your peers.
* **Academic Self Confidence** (`academic_self_confidence`, text, required/optional): Describe your level of academic self-confidence.
* **Teacher Support Facts** (`teacher_support_facts`, text, required/optional): Describe the facts about your teacher support.
* ... (Note: The remaining fields are similar to the above, with the exception that they are text input fields and do not have specific labels or explanations.)

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the text input fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the administrator or instructor for guidance.
