# Survey On Full Day School Programs - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from participants on full day school programs. The goal is to gather data on your preferences and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Full Day School Programs**: Enter the name of the full day school program you are providing feedback on.
2. **Parent/Educator Role**: Select one or both of the following:
	* Parent: If you are a parent or guardian of a child participating in the program.
	* Educator: If you are an educator or teacher working with the program.
3. **Goals of the School Program**: Describe the goals of the full day school program.
4. **Outcomes of the School Program**: Describe the outcomes of the full day school program.
5. **School Days Frequency**: Select the frequency you think school days should be held:
	* Every day
	* 2 days a week
	* 3 days a week
6. **School Days Start Time**: Select the preferred start time:
	* 08:00-09:00
	* 09:00-10:00
	* 10:00-11:00
7. **School Days Duration**: Select the preferred duration:
	* 4 hours
	* 5 hours
	* 6 hours
8. **Survey Completion Time**: This field is for your reference only, please do not fill it in.
9. **Additional Comments**: If you have any other comments or suggestions, please add them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Day School Programs**: This field is where you can enter the name of the full day school program.
- **Parent/Educator Role**: This field allows you to select whether you are a parent or an educator. Selecting both options will not affect the survey's logic.
- **Goals of the School Program**: This field is for describing the goals of the full day school program. What are the main objectives of the program?
- **Outcomes of the School Program**: In this field, describe the outcomes of the full day school program. What benefits or results do you think the program should achieve?
- **School Days Frequency**: This field asks for your preference on how often school days should be held. Select the option that best suits your opinion.
- **School Days Start Time**: Select the preferred start time for school days.
- **School Days Duration**: Choose the duration of school days that you think is most suitable.
- **Survey Completion Time**: This field is for your reference only, please do not fill it in.
- **Additional Comments**: This field is for any extra comments or suggestions you may have. Please use it to provide any further feedback or ideas you may have.
