# study-guide-preparation-survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from learners about their preferences and requirements for a study guide. It helps educational institutions or instructors tailor their study materials and resources to meet the specific needs of their learners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred learning styles (e.g., Question 1, Question 2, Question 3) in the "learning-styles" field.
2. Choose your preferred output formats (e.g., Format 1, Format 2, Format 3) in the "preferred-outputs" field, if applicable.
3. Provide any additional challenges you're facing while studying in the "study-challenges" field.
4. Specify your learning style category (e.g., Category 1, Category 2, Category 3) in the "learning-style-categories" field.
5. Enter the duration of your study in the "study-duration" field, if applicable.
6. Provide any additional instructions or comments about your study preparation in the "additional-instructions" field, if necessary.
7. Enter the date you intend to submit your study guide in the "submit-date" field, if applicable.
8. Specify the time you plan to submit your study guide in the "submit-time" field, if applicable.
9. Enter your email address for follow-up communication in the "submit-email" field, if applicable.
10. Enter your phone number for follow-up communication in the "submit-phone" field, if applicable.
11. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "submit-notes" field, if necessary.
12. Enter any comments or suggestions in the "submit-comments" field, if applicable.
13. You may upload any supporting attachments relevant to your study guide preparation in the "submit-attachments" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Learning styles (learning_styles)**: Select one or more learning styles that best fit your learning preferences.
* **Preferred Outputs (preferred-outputs)**: Choose the format in which you would like to receive the study guide.
* **Study Challenges (study_challenges)**: Describe any challenges you're facing while studying.
* **Learning Style Categories (learning-style-categories)**: Select a category that best fits your learning style.
* **Study Duration (study_duration)**: If applicable, enter the duration of your study preparation.
* **Study Duration Range (study-duration-range)**: Enter the range of time you plan to study.
* **Additional Instructions (additional-instructions)**: Provide any additional instructions or comments about your study preparation.
* **Submit Date (submit_date)**: If applicable, enter the date you intend to submit your study guide.
* **Submit Time (submit_time)**: If applicable, enter the time you plan to submit your study guide.
* **Submit Email (submit_email)**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Submit Phone (submit_phone)**: Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Submit Notes (submit_notes)**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your study guide preparation.
* **Submit Comments (submit_comments)**: Enter any comments or suggestions about your study guide preparation.
* **Submit Attachments (submit_attachments)**: You may upload any supporting attachments relevant to your study guide preparation.
