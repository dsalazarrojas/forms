# Metals And Their Properties Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about respondents' knowledge and opinions on metals and their properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below for each page:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`page_1`, `text`, required: false): This is the first page where you will find a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Respondent Details** (`page_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select 'Yes' if you have knowledge of metals and their properties, or 'No' if you do not.
* **Metal Knowledge** (`page_3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): For this page, select all the metals you have knowledge of. Please be as specific as possible (e.g., 'Yes' to 'Aluminum' and 'Copper' means you have knowledge of both metals).
* **Opinions** (`page_4`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any opinions or thoughts you have about metals and their properties. This is a free-text field where you can express your views on the topic.
* **Additional Information** (`page_5`, `note`, required: false): This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional information you think is relevant to the survey.
* **Email** (`page_6`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive more information about the survey.
* **Phone** (`page_7`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive more information about the survey.
* **Confirmation** (`page_8`, `date`, required: false): This is where you can confirm the date you completed the survey. 

## Tips
- Please fill out the form honestly and accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact email or phone number].
