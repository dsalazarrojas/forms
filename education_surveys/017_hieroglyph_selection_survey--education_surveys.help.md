# Hieroglyph Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback and information about hieroglyphs from users. It's likely used in an educational setting or for research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer all the questions that apply to you.
2. For the `egyptian_symbols` field, select one of the options that best describes your understanding or experience with hieroglyphs.
3. In the `preferences` field, select all the options that apply to you.
4. For the `education` field, select the level of education you have completed.
5. Enter your date of birth in the `demographics` field.
6. For the `time_spent_on_hobby` field, select all the options that apply to you.
7. In the `age` field, select the age range that best fits you.
8. For the `income` field, select all the options that apply to you.
9. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
10. If you have any comments or feedback, use the `note` fields provided.
