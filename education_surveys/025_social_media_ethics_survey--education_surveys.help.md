# social_media_ethics_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on social media ethics, aiming to contribute to a more informed discussion on this topic and help identify areas of improvement in the industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and provide your thoughts and opinions on the given questions.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you need help with a particular question, feel free to ask and we'll do our best to assist.
