# Faculty Digital Skills Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Digital Skills Benchmark Survey is designed to measure the digital skills of faculty members in higher education institutions. It collects data about their experiences and perceptions regarding various digital tools and platforms. This information helps the institution identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions to support faculty members in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the relevant category for this survey (Education Surveys or Technology Surveys)
* Briefly describe the purpose and context of this survey
* Enter your Form ID 1 (a unique identifier, e.g., a number)
* Enter your Form ID 2 (a unique identifier, e.g., a number)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Category (<name>, <type>: select_one)
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes the nature of this survey:
	+ Education Surveys: Surveys about education-related topics
	+ Technology Surveys: Surveys about technology-related topics

### Description (<name>, <type>: text)
* **Description**: Provide a brief description about what this survey is about and the context in which it is being taken.

### Form ID 1 (<name>, <type>: number)
* **Form ID 1**: Enter a unique identifier for this survey, e.g., a number. This will help us to associate the survey with your response.

### Form ID 2 (<name>, <type>: number)
* **Form ID 2**: Enter another unique identifier for this survey, e.g., a number. This will help us to associate the survey with your response.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and to the best of your abilities.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to reach out to us.
* This survey will help us to better understand your needs and make informed decisions to support you.
