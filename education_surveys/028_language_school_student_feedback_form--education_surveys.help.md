# Language School Student Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Language School Student Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from students on their educational experience. This form is used to help the school improve its services and better meet the needs of its students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
2. Make sure you have reviewed the form's purpose and understood its goals.
3. Fill out the form in its entirety, including all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Content** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ This field is for you to provide comments or suggestions about the course content.
* **Instructor Feedback** (`2`, `select_one`, required: `true`):
	+ Choose `Yes` if you received helpful and clear feedback from your instructor, or `No` if you didn't.
* **Facilities** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Use this field to comment on the school's facilities, such as classroom environment, equipment, and other amenities.
* **Suggestions** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Share any suggestions you have for improving the school's services.
* **Comments** (`5`, `note`, required: `false`):
	+ Write any additional comments or thoughts you have about your educational experience.
* **Contact** (`6`, `email`, required: `false`):
	+ If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, provide your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`7`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, provide your phone number.
* **Course Evaluation** (`8`, `number`, required: `true`):
	+ Rate your course experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **Course Interest** (`9`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ Choose `Yes` if you're interested in taking more courses at the school, or `No` if you're not.
* **Instructor Interest** (`10`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ Choose `Yes` if you'd like to take more courses with your current instructor, or `No` if you're not.
* **Facilities Satisfaction** (`11`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ Rate your satisfaction with the school's facilities on a scale of 1-5.
* **Facilities Satisfaction - Happy** (`12`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ Rate your happiness with the school's facilities on a scale of 1-10.
* **Facilities Satisfaction - Neutral** (`13`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ Rate your neutrality with the school's facilities on a scale of 1-10.
* **Facilities Satisfaction - Unhappy** (`14`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ Rate your unhappiness with the school's facilities on a scale of 1-10.
* **Facilities Satisfaction - Very Unhappy** (`15`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ Rate your very unhappiness with the school's facilities on a scale of 1-10.
* **Facilities Satisfaction - None** (`16`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ Rate your lack of satisfaction with the school's facilities on a scale of 1-10.
* **Instructor Satisfaction** (`17`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ Rate your satisfaction with your instructor on a scale of 1-5.
* **Instructor Satisfaction - Happy** (`18`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ Rate your happiness with your instructor on a scale of 1-10.
* **Instructor Satisfaction - Neutral** (`19`, `number`, required: `false`):

* **Instructor Satisfaction - Unhappy** (`20`, `number`, required: `false`):

* **Instructor Satisfaction - Very Unhappy** (`21`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ Rate your very unhappiness with your instructor on a scale of 1-10.
* **Instructor Satisfaction - None** (`22`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ Rate your lack of satisfaction with your instructor on a scale of 1-10.
* **Student Interest** (`23`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ Choose `Yes` if you're interested in taking more courses at the school, or `No` if you're not.
* **Student Satisfaction** (`24`, `select_one`, required: `false`):

## Tips

* Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* Make sure you have reviewed the form's purpose and understood its goals.
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Comments" field.
* If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, provide your email address or phone number.
