# Institutional Sustainability Goals Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Institutional Sustainability Goals Awareness Survey form. The purpose of this form is to gather information from staff members on their awareness of the institution's sustainability goals. By completing this form, you will be helping us better understand the current state of sustainability practices and goals within our institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about yourself.
2. Share your feedback on the institution's sustainability practices.
3. Choose how you typically receive information about sustainability initiatives.
4. Provide your availability for follow-up discussions or questions.
5. Add any additional comments or feedback (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: Please enter your name, department, or role.
* **Staff Feedback**: Please rate your level of awareness about the institution's sustainability goals. A scale of 1-5 will be used, with 1 indicating "Low" and 5 indicating "High". 
* **Communication Channels**: Please select all the channels you typically use to receive information about sustainability initiatives (e.g., email, meeting, or company intranet).
* **Engagement**: If you have a follow-up meeting or discussion related to sustainability, what date would you be available?
* **Notes**: Please add any additional comments or feedback about the institution's sustainability practices or goals.
* **Email**: Please enter your work email address for any follow-up discussions or communications.
* **Phone**: Please enter your work phone number for any follow-up discussions or communications.
* **Follow-up**: Please indicate if you would be available for follow-up discussions or questions (Yes, No, or Other).
* **General Info Hint**: This field is intended for any additional information that might be relevant to your general background or context.
* **Staff Follow-up Hint**: If you answered "Yes" to the follow-up question, please provide any additional details on how you can be contacted for follow-up discussions.
* **Engagement Hint**: If you answered "Yes" to the follow-up question, please provide any specific dates or times you would be available for a follow-up discussion.
* **Communication Channels Hint**: If you answered "Yes" to the follow-up question, please provide any additional information on how you prefer to receive follow-up communications.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please don't hesitate to ask.
* Your input will help us better understand the institution's sustainability goals and practices.
