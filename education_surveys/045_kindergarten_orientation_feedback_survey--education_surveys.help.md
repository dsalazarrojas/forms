This form is intended to collect feedback from kindergarten students and parents after an orientation session. To ensure that the form is completed correctly, please review the following help guide.

## Purpose
The Kindergarten Orientation Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from attendees to evaluate the effectiveness of the orientation session. Your participation will help us improve the program and better serve the students and parents in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the corresponding page with the corresponding label.

1. **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the survey. Read it carefully before proceeding.
2. **How satisfied are you with the orientation?**: Select one of the options to indicate your level of satisfaction.
	* Extremely Satisfied: You were very pleased with the orientation.
	* Somewhat Satisfied: You were somewhat pleased with the orientation.
	* Somewhat Dissatisfied: You were somewhat dissatisfied with the orientation.
	* Extremely Dissatisfied: You were very dissatisfied with the orientation.
3. **Comments**: Share your thoughts and suggestions about the orientation session. This is an optional field, so feel free to write as much or as little as you want.
4. **How would you rate the orientation?**: Rate the orientation on a scale from 1 to 5.
5. **Do you have any suggestions?**: Share any suggestions or ideas you have about the orientation session. This is an optional field, so feel free to write as much or as little as you want.
6. **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
7. **Submit**: Click this button to submit your feedback.
