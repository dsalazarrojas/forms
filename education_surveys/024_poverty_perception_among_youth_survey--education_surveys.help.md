# poverty_perception_among_youth_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is part of the "education_surveys" category and is used to gather information on the economic well-being of the youth population. It aims to understand their perceptions on poverty and their demographic characteristics.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and fill out each section according to your demographic information.
2. Select the most accurate options for each field based on your personal experiences and circumstances.
3. Answer all questions honestly and completely.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Background**: This is a note field where you can provide any additional information about yourself that might be relevant to the survey.
* **demographic_info**: Please select your category: "Youth" or "Adult".
* **age_group**: Select your age group: "18-24", "25-34", "35-44", "45-54", "55-64", or "65+".
* **highest_level_of_education_attained**: Choose your highest level of education: "No education", "Primary education", "Secondary education", "Tertiary education".
* **monthly_income**: Enter your monthly income.
* **employment_status**: Select your current employment status: "Employed", "Unemployed", "Looking for work", "Retired".
* **monthly_income_frequency**: Choose how often you receive income: "Every day", "Weekly", "Monthly", "Less than once a month".
* **family_member**: Indicate if you have any family members: "Yes" or "No".
* **family_size**: Enter the number of family members you have.
* **household_type**: Select the type of household you belong to: "Family", "Alone", "With others".
* **family_members_income**: Enter the total income of your family members.
* **income_source**: Select the source of income for your family members: "Salary", "Business", "Investment", "Rent", "Other".
* **household_expenses**: Enter the total expenses of your household.
* **savings**: Enter your savings.
* **borrowing**: Enter the amount borrowed.
* **debt**: Enter the total debt.
* **asset**: Indicate if you own any assets: "House", "Car", "Other".
* **assets_value**: Enter the value of your assets.
* **assets_frequency**: Select how often you use your assets: "Yes, I have assets", "No, I have no assets".
* **assets_value_frequency**: Choose the value of your assets: "Less than 10000", "Between 10000-100000", "Between 100000-1000000", "More than 1000000".
* **assets_use**: Select how you use your assets: "To save money", "To buy assets", "Other".
* **assets_use_frequency**: Choose how often you use your assets: "Frequently", "Rarely", "Never".
* **assets_borrowed**: Indicate if you have ever borrowed assets: "Yes, I have borrowed", "No, I have not borrowed".
* **assets_borrowed_frequency**: Select how often you borrow assets: "Often", "Occasionally", "Rarely", "Never".
* **assets_borrowed_amount**: Enter the amount borrowed.

Note: Some fields are optional, but it is recommended to complete all fields to provide a comprehensive picture of your economic well-being.
