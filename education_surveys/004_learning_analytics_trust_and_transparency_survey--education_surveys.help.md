# Learning Analytics Trust And Transparency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from users about the learning analytics trust and transparency of educational data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Answer the questions about your trust levels, data transparency, and usage.
2. Provide information about your demographic background, including your education level and job function.
3. Share information about your experience with data, including how many years you've spent working with data and how many students, projects, and teams you're involved with.
4. Share your feedback or comments about the form itself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This field provides a space for you to write any comments or thoughts you have before proceeding with the form.
* **Do you trust the data (Always, Sometimes, Never)**: This question asks about your level of trust in the data provided. Please select one of the options that best reflects your feelings.
* **Do you think the data is transparent (Very transparent, Somewhat transparent, Not transparent)**: This question asks about your perception of data transparency. Please select one of the options that best reflects your feelings.
* **Do you think we use data appropriately (Completely, Somewhat, Not at all)**: This question asks about your opinion on how data is used. Please select one of the options that best reflects your feelings.
* **Any feedback**: This field is for you to provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about the form or the data.
* **Email**: Please provide your email address if you want to be contacted for further discussion or clarification.
* **Phone Number**: This field is for you to provide your phone number if you want to be contacted for further discussion or clarification.
* **Education background**: This question asks about your educational background, including the level of education you've completed.
* **Job function**: This question asks about your job function or role.
* **How many years experience with data in your job?**: This question asks about the number of years you've spent using data in your job.
* **How many students?**: This question asks about the number of students you're currently working with.
* **How many projects?**: This question asks about the number of projects you've worked on involving data.
* **How many teams?**: This question asks about the number of teams you're involved with.
* **How many work hours per week?**: This question asks about the number of work hours you have per week.
* **How many days per week do you use Jotform?**: This question asks about how often you use Jotform.

Please note that all fields are optional unless otherwise stated.
