# Invertebrate Classification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on invertebrates, their classification, habitats, and other essential characteristics. Fill out this form accurately to help document and conserve invertebrate species effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the appropriate invertebrate type(s) from the options provided.
2.  Choose the habitat(s) where the invertebrate is primarily found.
3.  Enter or select the correct body color(s) of the invertebrate.
4.  Provide the number of legs for the invertebrate.
5.  Specify if the invertebrate is winged.
6.  Enter the number of antennae for the invertebrate.
7.  Enter the average body size of the invertebrate.
8.  Enter the average height of the invertebrate.
9.  Enter the average length of the invertebrate.
10.  Enter the average weight of the invertebrate.
11.  Provide a description of the invertebrate's habitat.
12.  Select the conservation status of the invertebrate species.
13.  Enter any additional notes about the invertebrate species.
14.  Provide your email address for contact purposes.
15.  Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
16.  Enter the date of the observation or data collection.
17.  Enter the time of the observation or data collection.
18.  Select the time zone of the observation or data collection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Invertebrate Type** (`invertebrate_type`, required): Select all applicable invertebrate types, such as ants, spiders, bees, etc. This will help classify the species correctly.
*   **Habitat** (`habitat`, required): Choose the primary habitat where the invertebrate is found, such as grassland, forest, etc.
*   **Body Color** (`body_color`, optional): Enter the body color(s) of the invertebrate, if applicable.
*   **Legs Number** (`legs_number`, required): Enter the number of legs for the invertebrate.
*   **Winged** (`winged`, optional): Specify if the invertebrate is winged.
*   **Antennae Number** (`antennae_number`, optional): Enter the number of antennae for the invertebrate.
*   **Body Size** (`body_size`, optional): Enter the average body size of the invertebrate.
*   **Average Height** (`average_height`, optional): Enter the average height of the invertebrate.
*   **Average Length** (`average_length`, optional): Enter the average length of the invertebrate.
*   **Average Weight** (`average_weight`, optional): Enter the average weight of the invertebrate.
*   **Habitat Description** (`habitat_description`, optional): Provide a description of the invertebrate's habitat.
*   **Conservation Status** (`conservation_status`, optional): Select the conservation status of the invertebrate species.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the invertebrate species.
*   **Contact Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Date** (`date`, required): Enter the date of the observation or data collection.
*   **Time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of the observation or data collection.
*   **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, optional): Select the time zone of the observation or data collection.
