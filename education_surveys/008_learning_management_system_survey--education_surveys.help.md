# Learning Management System Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Learning Management System (LMS) Survey is a tool used to collect feedback from our users on the performance and usability of our platform. The feedback collected from this survey helps us understand areas for improvement and refine our platform's features and user interface.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Carefully read each field description and select the most appropriate answer based on your experience with our platform.
2. Answer each field based on your genuine experience and perception.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Other comments" field.
4. Review your answers before submitting the survey to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current relationship with our platform?**
	+ Select one that applies to you:
		- Current User: You are an active user of our platform.
		- Previous User: You have used our platform in the past but not currently.
		- New User: You are a new user of our platform.
* **How do you rate our platform's user interface?** (platform_rating)
	+ Rate the user interface from 1-10, where 1 is very poor and 10 is excellent.
* **How would you rate the overall user experience of our platform?** (user_experience)
	+ Rate the overall user experience from 1-10, where 1 is very poor and 10 is excellent.
* **What would you like to see improved in our platform?** (improvement_suggestions)
	+ Provide specific suggestions on how we can improve our platform.
* **How likely are you to recommend our platform to a friend or colleague?** (recommendation)
	+ Rate from 1-10, where 1 is very unlikely and 10 is very likely.
* **Are you experiencing any issues with our platform?** (issues)
	+ Select "True" if you are experiencing any issues, "False" otherwise.
* **How can we improve our platform for you?** (improvement_suggestions_2)
	+ Provide specific suggestions on how we can improve our platform for you.
* **On a scale of 1-10, how satisfied are you with our platform's performance?** (satisfaction_level)
	+ Rate from 1-10, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 10 is very satisfied.
* **What features would you like to see added to our platform?** (add_features)
	+ Provide specific features you would like to see added to our platform.
* **Are you aware that our platform provides a free trial?** (free_trial)
	+ Select "True" if you are aware of the free trial, "False" otherwise.
* **What is your status as a user of our platform?** (user_status)
	+ Select one of the following:
		- Current User: You are an active user of our platform.
		- Previous User: You have used our platform in the past but not currently.
		- Never Used: You have never used our platform.
* **Would you like to be contacted by our team?** (contact_us)
	+ Select "True" if you would like to be contacted, "False" otherwise.
* **Do you have any other comments or feedback about our platform?** (feedback)
	+ Provide your comments or feedback.
