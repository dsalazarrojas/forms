# Virtual Physics Professor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their virtual physics professors.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form fields as completely as possible, taking into account the requirements for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (Text)**: This field is used to collect your name and any other relevant information.
* **Physics Professor (Select One)**: Select one of the three available options (Professor A, B, or C) that corresponds to your virtual physics professor.
* **Teaching Methods (Select Multiple)**: Select all applicable methods (e.g., Online Lectures, Online Discussions, Video Lectures, and In-Person Lectures) that your virtual physics professor used.
* **Course Evaluation (Note)**: This field is used to add any comments or notes about the course.
* **Course Comments (Text)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the course.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your official email address.
* **Phone Number (Text)**: Enter your official phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (although none are required in this form).
* Enter your information accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
