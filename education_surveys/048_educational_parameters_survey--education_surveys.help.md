# Educational Parameters Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Parameters Survey is designed to gather information from users about their experiences with our school, department, and program. This feedback will help us understand how we can improve our services and provide better support to our students. Please ensure that you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out the form by providing the required information, which includes your school ID, department name, and email address.
- For optional fields, feel free to provide any additional feedback or comments you may have.
- Make sure to rate your experience with the school in question 1.
- Answer questions 2 and 3 in a descriptive manner, providing as much detail as possible.
- Use the space provided for question 3 to share any other comments or suggestions.
- For questions 4 and 5, enter the department and program names as they appear on your school ID.
- For question 6, select one of the options provided.
- Questions 7 and 8 are optional and open-ended.
- Question 9 is for contact information, so ensure you provide a valid email address.
- Question 10 is optional and for contact information purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School Id** (`1`, `number`, required): Enter your school ID as per the format specified in your school ID card.
  This field is used to identify your school and is crucial for your feedback to be correctly associated with your school.
- **School Name** (`2`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the school you are providing feedback on.
  This field is used to help us understand which school your feedback is about.
- **Department Name** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the department you are providing feedback on.
  This field is used to help us understand which department your feedback is about.
- **Program Name** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the program you are providing feedback on.
  This field is used to help us understand which program your feedback is about.
- **Course Name** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the course you are providing feedback on.
  This field is used to help us understand which course your feedback is about.
- **How would you rate your experience with our school?** (`6`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your experience with our school on a scale of Good or Fair.
  This field is used to help us understand how our school is performing in terms of your experience.
- **What could we improve in our programs?** (`7`, `text`, optional): Provide any suggestions or feedback you may have on how our programs can be improved.
  This field is used to help us understand areas where our programs can be improved.
- **Do you have any other comments or suggestions?** (`8`, `text`, optional): Provide any other comments or suggestions you may have.
  This field is used to gather any additional feedback you may have.
- **Email** (`9`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
  This field is used to contact you with any questions or follow-up discussions.
- **Phone** (`10`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
  This field is used to contact you for further discussion if needed.
