# Online Learning Platform Effectiveness Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is for users who want to provide their feedback and suggestions on how the online learning platform is meeting their needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please select the page that corresponds to your current page number.
* Answer each question thoroughly and honestly, providing specific examples and details about your experience.
* If you have any suggestions or comments that are not covered in the questions, feel free to provide them in the "Additional Comments" section at the end.
* Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Platform Effectiveness Report (Page 1)
* **Platform Effectiveness Report** (Required/Optional): This field is a note field where you can provide a brief description of your experience with the platform. Please keep it concise and to the point.

### User Satisfaction (Page 2)
* **User Satisfaction** (Required/Optional): This field is a text field where you can describe your overall satisfaction with the platform. Please answer in a few sentences, providing specific details about what you liked or disliked about the platform.

### Outcomes (Page 3)
* **Outcomes** (Required/Optional, Select Multiple): This field allows you to select which outcomes you achieved or expect to achieve through the platform. Please choose all that apply.

### Improvement Needs (Page 4)
* **Improvement Needs** (Required/Optional, Select One): This field allows you to indicate if you have any improvement needs or concerns with the platform. Please choose one that best describes your situation.

### Platform Effectiveness (Page 5)
* **Platform Effectiveness** (Required/Optional, Number): This field is a numerical field where you can rate the platform's effectiveness on a scale from 0 to 100. Please provide a specific number that reflects your assessment.

### Platform Effectiveness 1 (Page 5)
* **Platform Effectiveness 1** (Required/Optional, Text): This field is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback on the platform's effectiveness. Please keep it concise and focused on the specific page you are on.

### Additional Comments (Page 6)
* **Additional Comments** (Optional): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions that you may have that are not covered in the questions above. Please feel free to provide any feedback, ideas, or concerns you may have.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions that apply to your experience.
* Be as specific as possible when answering each question.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* If you have any technical issues or concerns, please report them to the platform administrators.
