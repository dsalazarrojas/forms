# Student Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students on their experience with the educational resources and services provided by the institution. The purpose of this form is to gather information that can be used to improve the quality of education and student life.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Answer the questions with the information relevant to your experience.
3. Make sure to complete all the fields as they are all important for us to understand your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_survey** (1, Text, Optional): This field is for students to write a brief title for their survey.
* **curriculum** (2, Text, Optional): Please enter your current or desired curriculum.
* **materials** (3, Text, Optional): Please specify any materials you have used or would like to use during your studies.
* **facilities** (4, Text, Optional): Describe your experience with the facilities provided by the institution.
* **facilities_rating** (7, Number, Optional): Rate your experience with the facilities (1-5).
* **facilities_friendly** (8, Number, Optional): Rate how friendly you found the facilities staff to be (1-5).
* **facilities_useful** (9, Number, Optional): Rate how useful you found the facilities to be (1-5).
* **facilities_equipment** (10, Text, Optional): List any facilities equipment that you found useful or not.
* **materials_rating** (11, Number, Optional): Rate your experience with the materials provided (1-5).
* **materials_friendly** (12, Number, Optional): Rate how friendly you found the materials staff to be (1-5).
* **materials_useful** (13, Number, Optional): Rate how useful you found the materials to be (1-5).
* **materials_equipment** (14, Text, Optional): List any materials that you found useful or not.
* **curriculum_rating** (15, Number, Optional): Rate your experience with the curriculum (1-5).
* **curriculum_friendly** (16, Number, Optional): Rate how friendly you found the curriculum staff to be (1-5).
* **curriculum_useful** (17, Number, Optional): Rate how useful you found the curriculum to be (1-5).
* **curriculum_equipment** (18, Text, Optional): List any curriculum materials that you found useful or not.
* **support_services_rating** (19, Number, Optional): Rate your experience with the support services provided (1-5).
* **support_services_friendly** (20, Number, Optional): Rate how friendly you found the support services staff to be (1-5).
* **support_services_useful** (21, Number, Optional): Rate how useful you found the support services to be (1-5).
* **support_services_equipment** (22, Text, Optional): List any support services that you found useful or not.
* **faculty_rating** (23, Number, Optional): Rate your experience with the faculty (1-5).
* **faculty_friendly** (24, Number, Optional): Rate how friendly you found the faculty to be (1-5).
* **faculty_useful** (25, Number, Optional): Rate how useful you found the faculty to be (1-5).
