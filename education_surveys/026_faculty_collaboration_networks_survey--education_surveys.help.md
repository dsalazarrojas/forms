# Faculty Collaboration Networks Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Collaboration Networks Survey is designed to collect feedback on the level of collaboration among faculty members in an institution. The survey aims to assess their willingness to collaborate with colleagues, the frequency of their collaboration, and their submission habits.
