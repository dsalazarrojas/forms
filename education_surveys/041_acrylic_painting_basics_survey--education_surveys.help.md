# Acrylic Painting Basics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data about your experience with acrylic painting.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out this form to provide information about your experience with acrylic painting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (text, optional): Enter your name to help instructors get in touch with you for the class.
* **Experience level** (select_one, required): Choose your level of experience with acrylic painting.
	+ Beginner: You are new to acrylic painting or have just started.
	+ Intermediate: You have some experience with acrylic painting and are looking to improve.
	+ Advanced: You are experienced with acrylic painting and want to refine your skills.
* **Preferred painting styles** (select_multiple, optional): Select all the painting styles you enjoy most, such as Realism, Abstract, or Impressionism.
* **Challenges faced** (text, optional): Describe the main challenges you faced while learning acrylics.
* **Helpful resources** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate which resources helped you learn most effectively, such as video tutorials or step-by-step guides.
* **Preferred class format** (select_one, required): Choose the best class format for you, such as in-person, online live, or on demand.
* **Materials availability** (select_one, required): Confirm if you have access to required materials for the class.
	+ True: You have access to required materials.
	+ False: You do not have access to required materials.
* **Class duration preference** (select_one, optional): Select your preferred class session duration, such as 1 hour, 2 hours, or a full day.
* **Topics of interest** (text, optional): Specify the topics you would like to cover in a class.
* **Willingness to share work for feedback** (select_one, optional): Indicate if you are willing to share your work for feedback from instructors.
	+ True: You are willing to share your work.
	+ False: You are not willing to share your work.
* **Additional comments** (text, optional): Add any other feedback to improve the class.

## Tips
Please fill out this form accurately and honestly to help us create a more effective class. If you have any questions, please contact us at [support email].
