# leisure_interest_and_barriers_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your leisure interests, potential barriers to participation, and motivation for engagement. The data collected will help us better understand your preferences and needs, and inform targeted interventions to support your leisure engagement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly to provide accurate and helpful information.
2. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you.
3. If you need help or clarification on any question, please don't hesitate to reach out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Demographics** (Demographics): This section is used to collect basic information about you. Please provide your details accurately.
* **Leisure_interests** (Leisure Interests, Select multiple): Select all the leisure activities that interest you from the list provided. This will help us understand what types of activities you enjoy.
* **Barriers** (Barriers, Text): Describe any potential obstacles that prevent you from participating in leisure activities. Be as specific as possible to help us identify areas to support you.
* **Participation** (Participation, Number): Indicate how often you participate in leisure activities on a scale of 0-10, where 0 means "never" and 10 means "always".
* **Motivation** (Motivation, Select one): Choose the reason that best motivates you to participate in leisure activities from the options provided. This will help us understand your motivation and engagement.

## Tips

- Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
- If you need help or clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out.
- Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
