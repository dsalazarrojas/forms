# Creative Practice Reflection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Practice Reflection Survey is designed to collect feedback and reflection from educators and project participants on the creative practice being conducted in the project. This form is meant to be completed after the project has been completed, and it's essential to provide a thorough and honest evaluation of the project's progress, goals, and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2.  If you're unsure about any of the fields, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for more details.
3.  Fill out the form thoroughly and honestly, providing as much information as you can.
4.  Save your work before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Project Feedback
*   **Project Feedback** (Required): This is a text field where you can provide any feedback, comments, or concerns about the project. Please write a brief but sincere reflection on how the project went, what you liked or disliked, and any suggestions for improvement.

### Artistic Goals
*   **Artistic Goals** (Optional): Select any artistic goals that apply to your work on this project. You can choose as many as are relevant.

### Project Insights
*   **Project Insights** (Optional): This is a text area for any additional insights, thoughts, or ideas that might not fit into any other field. You can write as much or as little as you like.

### Educator Goals
*   **Educator Goals** (Optional): If you're an educator, please describe your goals for the project. This might include what you hoped to achieve or what you'd like to see in future projects.

### Team Goals
*   **Team Goals** (Optional): Select the goals that apply to your team's involvement in the project. Choose as many as are relevant.

### Project Participants
*   **Project Participants** (Optional): Select as many participants who were involved in the project as you can. This might include yourself, colleagues, or others who contributed.

### Educator Participants
*   **Educator Participants** (Optional): If you're an educator, select the participants who were involved in your work on this project. This might include students, peers, or others who contributed.

### Date
*   **Date** (Optional): If you're unsure of the project's start and end dates, you can fill this field with the date you completed the form.

### Time
*   **Time** (Optional): If you're unsure of the project's start and end times, you can fill this field with the time you completed the form.

### Email
*   **Email** (Optional): If you have a preferred email address you'd like to be contacted at, you can fill this field.

### Phone
*   **Phone** (Optional): If you have a phone number you'd like to be contacted on, you can fill this field.

### Notes
*   **Notes** (Optional): This is a text area for any additional comments, thoughts, or ideas that might not fit into any other field.
