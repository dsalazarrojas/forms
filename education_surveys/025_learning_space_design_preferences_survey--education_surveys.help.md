# learning_space_design_preferences_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The learning space design preferences survey is designed to gather opinions and preferences from learners on the optimal setup of the learning space, focusing on technology integration and classroom design.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as they appear. You will find the questions divided into sections, and each section will be marked with a title. Follow the prompts and choose your preferences from the provided options.
