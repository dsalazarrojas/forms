# Higher Education Policy Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Higher Education Policy Perception Survey is designed to gather feedback and opinions from educators, administrators, and other stakeholders on their perception of higher education policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the sections that apply to you, and complete the corresponding questions to the best of your ability.
2. Please note that some questions may be optional.
3. Be as specific as possible when answering questions.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Education Survey Form 1** (`education_survey_form_1`, text, optional): This section is a free-form text field where you can provide any general feedback or comments about your perception of higher education policies.
* **What is your perception of current education policies?** (`education_policy_1`, text, optional): Please provide your general impression of the current policies governing higher education.
* **How effective do you rate the policies?** (`policy_effectiveness`, number, optional): On a scale of 1-5, rate how effective you think the current policies are.
* **What areas need improvement?** (`improvement_areas_1`, text, optional): Identify specific areas you think need improvement in the current policies.
* **Education Survey Form 2** (`education_survey_form_2`, text, optional): This section is a free-form text field where you can provide any general feedback or comments about your perception of higher education policies.
* ... (This pattern continues for all fields)

Note that the fields listed above are all text fields, except for the rating question "How effective do you rate the policies?" which is a number field.

Please ensure that you only complete this form once, and do not duplicate your responses.

If you have any questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.
