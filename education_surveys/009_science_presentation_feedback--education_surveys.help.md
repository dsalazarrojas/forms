# Science Presentation Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Science Presentation Feedback form is a tool used to collect feedback on science presentations. This feedback is essential for improving presentation strategies and providing valuable insights to presenters and educators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Science Presentation ID (if provided).
2. Enter your Evaluator ID (if provided).
3. Rate the presentation on a scale from 1 to 5.
4. Provide the date of the presentation.
5. Enter any feedback you have about the presentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Science Presentation ID (Science Presentation ID, number, optional)**: Enter the unique ID of the science presentation, if provided.
* **Evaluator ID (Evaluator ID, number, optional)**: Enter your evaluator ID, if provided.
* **Rating (Rating, number, optional)**: Rate the presentation on a scale from 1 to 5. A lower rating indicates areas that require improvement.
* **Evaluation Date (Evaluation Date, date, optional)**: Enter the date of the presentation.
* **Feedback (Feedback, text, optional)**: Provide any comments or suggestions you have about the presentation.

## Tips
* Please provide feedback only on those fields that are marked as required.
* Ensure that you rate the presentation fairly and provide constructive feedback.
* Keep your feedback concise and to the point.
