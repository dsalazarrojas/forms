# Persuasive Text Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Persuasive Text Evaluation Survey is designed to evaluate the strength and effectiveness of a given text, typically an argument or an article, based on several key factors. This survey will help you assess the persuasive power of the text and provide insights on how well it presents its argument and evidence. Your input will help us improve the quality of text presentation and provide more accurate assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read through the text of the argument provided in the "Text of the argument" field.
* Rate each section based on the provided scale:
	+ 1-2 for weak or non-existent
	+ 3-4 for moderate
	+ 5-6 for strong
* Complete all required and optional fields as prompted.
* Ensure that you have provided your name and email address for us to contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief summary of the text. You can read through it and take note of the main points or issues presented.
* **How strong is the main argument?** (`rating_scale_1`, `number`, required: false): Please rate the strength of the main argument based on the scale provided. A rating of 1-2 indicates a weak or non-existent argument, 3-4 for a moderate argument, and 5-6 for a strong argument.
* **How likely is the main argument to be accepted?** (`rating_scale_2`, `number`, required: false): Rate the likelihood of the main argument being accepted based on the scale provided.
* **How strong is the argument?** (`argument_adequacy`, `number`, required: false): This rate the overall strength of the argument presented.
* **How clear is the argument?** (`argument_clarity`, `number`, required: false): Rate the clarity of the argument.
* **How likely is the argument to be effective?** (`argument_effectiveness`, `number`, required: false): Rate the effectiveness of the argument.
* **How strong is the evidence?** (`evidence_quality`, `number`, required: false): Rate the strength of the evidence presented.
* **How strong is the counter argument?** (`counter_argument`, `number`, required: false): Rate the strength of the counter argument.
* **How effective is the counter argument?** (`counter_argument_effectiveness`, `number`, required: false): Rate the effectiveness of the counter argument.
* **How balanced are the main and counter arguments?** (`argument_counter_argument_balance`, `number`, required: false): Rate the balance between the main and counter arguments.
* **Text of the argument** (`text_evidence`, `text`, required: false): Please provide the text of the argument you are evaluating.
* **Your Name** (`author_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name so we can contact you if needed.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips

* Please rate each field based on your honest assessment of the text.
* You can take your time to review the text and provide your ratings.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Your ratings will help us improve the quality of text presentation and provide more accurate assessments.
