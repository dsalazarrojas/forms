# microfinance_awareness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess users' awareness and understanding of microfinance products and services. It is used to collect information that will help us better understand our users' knowledge and experience with microfinance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read the introduction page to understand the purpose and context of the form.
2. Answer all the questions on the "questions" page, selecting all the options that apply to you.
3. On the "answers" page, enter the number of options you selected on the previous page.
4. On the "comments" page, provide any additional information you think is relevant.
5. On the "email" and "phone" pages, enter your email address and phone number.
6. On the "demographic information" pages, enter any additional information you think is relevant.
7. Finally, click the "submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **introduction** (page 1): This page provides an overview of the form and its purpose.
* **questions** (page 2): Select all the options that apply to you from the list of questions. This will help us understand your level of awareness and experience with microfinance.
* **answers** (page 3): Enter the number of options you selected on the previous page.
* **comments** (page 4): Provide any additional information you think is relevant to your answers.
* **email** (page 5): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or surveys.
* **phone** (page 6): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **demographic_info1** (page 7): Enter any additional demographic information you think is relevant.
* **demographic_info2** (page 8): Enter any additional demographic information you think is relevant.
* **demographic_info3** (page 9): Enter any additional demographic information you think is relevant.
* **demographic_info4** (page 10): Enter any additional demographic information you think is relevant.
* **submit** (page 11): Click this button to complete and submit the form.
* **time** (page 12): This page is not currently implemented, so please skip it.

## Tips
- Make sure to select all the options that apply to you on the "questions" page.
- Enter accurate and truthful information on the "answers" page.
- Provide clear and concise comments on the "comments" page.
- Enter a valid email address on the "email" page.
- Enter a valid phone number on the "phone" page.
- Enter any additional demographic information you think is relevant on the "demographic_info1", "demographic_info2", "demographic_info3", and "demographic_info4" pages.
- Make sure to read the introduction page carefully before starting the form.
