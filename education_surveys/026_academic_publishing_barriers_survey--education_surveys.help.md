# Academic Publishing Barriers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Publishing Barriers Survey is a tool designed to gather information on various challenges academics face in publishing their research. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose, how to complete it, and explain each field in detail.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand each question.
2. Select your primary role in academia from the options provided.
3. Provide your primary field of study or research.
4. Answer questions regarding your research experience and number of publications.
5. Assess the level of challenge each barrier presents to your publishing process.
6. Select up to three major challenges you face in academic publishing.
7. Share your attitude towards open access publishing models.
8. Identify up to three solutions that would be most helpful in overcoming publishing barriers.
9. Leave any additional comments or thoughts about academic publishing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Academic Publishing Barriers Survey (1)** (`publishing_barriers_intro`, Note, Optional)
	+ This is a placeholder to introduce the purpose of the form and set expectations.
* **Your Role in Academia (2)** (`respondent_role`, Select One, Required)
	+ Please select your primary role in academia to help us understand your perspective on academic publishing.
* **Academic Discipline (3)** (`academic_discipline`, Text, Required)
	+ Enter your primary field of study or research to provide context for your responses.
* **Years of Research Experience (4)** (`years_research_experience`, Number, Required)
	+ Please provide the number of years you have research experience.
* **Number of Publications (5)** (`number_publications`, Number, Required)
	+ Provide an approximate number of peer-reviewed publications you have authored.
* **Financial Barriers (6)** (`publishing_barrier_financial`, Number, Required)
	+ Assess the level of challenge financial costs present in your publishing process (1-5 scale).
* **Time Constraint Barriers (7)** (`publishing_barrier_time_constraints`, Number, Required)
	+ Evaluate the level of challenge time constraints present in your publishing process (1-5 scale).
* **Language Barriers (8)** (`publishing_barrier_language`, Number, Required)
	+ Assess the level of challenge language barriers present in your publishing process (1-5 scale).
* **Access to Resources Barriers (9)** (`publishing_barrier_access_to_resources`, Number, Required)
	+ Evaluate the level of challenge access to resources present in your publishing process (1-5 scale).
* **Review Process Barriers (10)** (`publishing_barrier_review_process`, Number, Required)
	+ Assess the level of challenge the peer review process presents in your publishing process (1-5 scale).
* **Journal Selection Barriers (11)** (`publishing_barrier_journal_selection`, Number, Required)
	+ Evaluate the level of challenge difficulties in selecting journals present in your publishing process (1-5 scale).
* **Formatting Requirements Barriers (12)** (`publishing_barrier_formatting_requirements`, Number, Required)
	+ Assess the level of challenge complex formatting requirements present in your publishing process (1-5 scale).
* **Institutional Support Barriers (13)** (`publishing_barrier_institutional_support`, Number, Required)
	+ Evaluate the level of challenge lack of institutional support present in your publishing process (1-5 scale).
* **Major Publishing Challenges (14)** (`major_publishing_challenges`, Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ Select up to three major challenges you face in academic publishing.
* **Attitude Toward Open Access (15)** (`open_access_attitude`, Select One, Required)
	+ Share your attitude towards open access publishing models.
* **Important Solutions (16)** (`publishing_barrier_solutions_important`, Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ Identify up to three solutions that would be most helpful in overcoming publishing barriers.
* **Additional Comments (17)** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional)
	+ Leave any additional thoughts or comments about academic publishing.
