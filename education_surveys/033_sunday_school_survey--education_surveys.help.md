# sunday_school_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information and feedback from Sunday school attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name and any other requested information.
2. Select your class and subject of interest.
3. Share any comments, questions, or suggestions you may have.
4. Choose areas for improvement (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (id: 1)**: This is a non-required field meant to serve as an introduction to the form.
* **Name (id: 2)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your name tag.
* **Student Name (id: 3)**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed on your report card or other official documents.
* **Email (id: 4)**: If you have an email address, please provide it so that we can contact you with updates.
* **Phone (id: 5)**: Enter your contact phone number if you'd like us to reach out to you directly.
* **Age (id: 6)**: Please enter your age as of the current date.
* **Class (id: 7)**: Select your current class from the options A, B, or C.
* **Subject (id: 8)**: Enter the subject you're currently taking or interested in.
* **Comments (id: 9)**: Share any comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **Questions (id: 10)**: If you have any questions, please feel free to ask them.
* **Improvement (id: 11)**: Select one or more areas where we can improve our Sunday school program.
* **Other (id: 12)**: If there's anything else you'd like to share with us, please provide it in the space below.
* **Improvement options:** 
  * '1': Please choose option 1
  * '2': Please choose option 2
  * '3': Please choose option 3
  * Other: You can choose 'Other' to enter your own suggestion.
