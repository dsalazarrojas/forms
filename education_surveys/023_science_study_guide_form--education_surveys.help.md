# Science Study Guide Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Science Study Guide Form is a tool designed to collect information from students about their science study guides. This form is intended to gather essential details about a student's science study guide, and it's crucial to fill it out accurately to get the best results.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. In the "General Info" field, provide a brief description of your science study guide.
3. Enter your "Student Name" and "Student Email" in their respective fields.
4. Fill out the "Subject" field by selecting the correct option from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter the "Topic" of your science study guide.
6. Write a short "Description" of your science study guide.
7. Add any additional "Notes" about your submission.
8. For the "Submission Date" field, select the date you completed the form.
9. For the "Submission Time" field, select the time you completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, optional): A brief description of your science study guide.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, as the student submitting the form.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your science teacher.
* **Subject** (`subject`, select_one, optional): Select the subject of your science study guide (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Topic** (`topic`, text, optional): A brief description of the topic of your science study guide.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): A detailed description of your science study guide.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Any additional notes about your submission.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Select the date you completed the form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Select the time you completed the form.

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough in your submission.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to this help guide for clarification.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact your administrator for assistance.
