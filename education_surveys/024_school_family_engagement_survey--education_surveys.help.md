# School Family Engagement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Family Engagement Survey is designed to gather information about the importance and satisfaction of various aspects of school life. This survey is intended to help schools understand the needs and concerns of their families and make informed decisions about family engagement activities and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select the category of respondent from the "parents", "teachers", "school administrator", "staff" or "other" options.
2. Select the type of school from the "elementary", "middle", "high", "combined", "college", "university" or "other" options.
3. Indicate your level of experience and satisfaction with educational experiences by selecting from the "highly enjoyable", "somewhat enjoyable", "neutral", "somewhat dissatisfying" or "highly dissatisfying" options.
4. Rate the importance of educational experiences from the "very important", "somewhat important", "not important at all" options.
5. Indicate the level of influence on your school climate by selecting from the "very satisfying", "somewhat satisfying", "somewhat dissatisfying", or "not satisfying at all" options.
6. Rate the importance of relationships such as teacher-student, teacher-staff, teacher-parent, and school administrator-staff from the "very important", "somewhat important", "not important at all" options.
7. Indicate the level of teacher support and influence on your educational experience from the "very important", "somewhat important", "not important at all" options.
8. Rate the importance of parent participation and student participation from the "very important", "somewhat important", "not important at all" options.
9. Finally, rate the level of parent and student support from the "very important", "somewhat important", "not important at all" options.
