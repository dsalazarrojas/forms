# School Survey For Parents - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from parents about their involvement in their child's education, communication preferences, and any additional comments or notes they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your level of involvement in your child's education.
2. Choose how often you visit the school.
3. Select your preferred method of communication with teachers.
4. Enter your email address (required).
5. Enter your phone number (optional).
6. Add any additional comments or notes you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How Often Do You Visit The School** (Select One)
	+ Never
	+ Once a month
	+ A few times a month
	+ More than once a week
* **How Involved Are You In Your Child's Education** (Select Multiple)
	+ Very Involved
	+ Somewhat Involved
	+ Not Very Involved
	+ Not Involved At All
* **Last Date You Visited The School** (Date)
	+ This field is for recording the last time you visited the school.
* **How Do You Prefer To Communicate With Teachers** (Select One)
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Text Message
	+ In Person
* **Email** (Email)
	+ This field is for entering your email address.
* **Phone** (Text)
	+ This field is for entering your phone number.
* **Notes** (Note)
	+ This field is for adding any additional comments or notes you may have.
* **Comments** (Text)
	+ This field is for adding any additional comments or notes you may have.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please feel free to ask your school administration for clarification.
* All answers will be kept confidential.
* This form is designed to be completed by one parent per household. If you're unsure, please ask your school administration for guidance.
