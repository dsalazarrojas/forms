# Instrument Content Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about an instrument's content to ensure it is reliable and valid.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the instrument.
2. Choose the scale for the instrument.
3. Indicate whether the instrument has been tested.
4. Rate the instrument's content.
5. Rate individual items on the instrument.
6. Rate the frequency of the items on the instrument.
7. Choose the scale for rating items on the instrument.
8. Choose the scale for testing the instrument.
9. Choose the scale for rating the test.
10. Rate the test.
11. Rate the frequency of the test.
12. Choose the scale for rating the scale.
13. Choose the scale for testing the scale.
14. Rate the scale.
15. Choose the scale for items on the instrument.
16. Choose the scale for testing items on the instrument.
17. Rate items on the instrument.
18. Rate the frequency of items on the instrument.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **instrument_name** (Text Field, Optional): Enter the name of the instrument.
* **instrument_scale** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the scale for the instrument (e.g., Yes, No).
* **instrument_test** (Select One, Optional): Indicate whether the instrument has been tested (e.g., Yes, No).
* **instrument_rating** (Number, Optional): Rate the instrument's content.
* **rating_scale** (Select One, Optional): Choose the scale for rating the instrument (e.g., Good, Fair).
* **rating_test** (Select One, Optional): Choose the scale for testing the instrument (e.g., Yes, No).
* **rating_item** (Text Field, Optional): Rate individual items on the instrument.
* **rating_item** (Number, Optional): Rate individual items on the instrument.
* **rating_scale_frequency** (Select One, Optional): Choose the scale for rating the frequency of the instrument (e.g., Good, Fair).
* **test_name** (Text Field, Optional): Choose the scale for testing the instrument (e.g., Yes, No).
* **test_rating** (Number, Optional): Rate the test.
* **frequency_rating** (Number, Optional): Rate the frequency of the test.
* **rating_test_frequency** (Select One, Optional): Choose the scale for rating the frequency of the test (e.g., Good, Fair).
* **rating_scale_rating** (Select One, Optional): Choose the scale for rating the scale.
* **scale_name** (Text Field, Optional): Choose the scale for testing the scale (e.g., Yes, No).
* **scale_test** (Select One, Optional): Choose the scale for testing the scale (e.g., Yes, No).
* **scale_rating** (Number, Optional): Rate the scale.
* **item_name** (Text Field, Optional): Rate individual items on the instrument.
* **item_scale** (Select One, Optional): Choose the scale for items on the instrument (e.g., Yes, No).
* **item_test** (Select One, Optional): Choose the scale for testing items on the instrument (e.g., Yes, No).
* **item_rating** (Number, Optional): Rate individual items on the instrument.
* **item_frequency** (Text Field, Optional): Rate the frequency of items on the instrument.
* **frequency_item** (Text Field, Optional): Rate the frequency of items on the instrument.
* **scale_rating_frequency** (Select One, Optional): Choose the scale for rating the frequency of the instrument (e.g., Good, Fair).
