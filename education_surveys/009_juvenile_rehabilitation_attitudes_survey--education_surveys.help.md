# Juvenile Rehabilitation Attitudes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The juvenile rehabilitation attitudes survey form is designed to collect data on the attitudes and opinions of stakeholders towards juvenile rehabilitation. It is intended to gather feedback on the effectiveness of various aspects of the rehabilitation program, including facilities, programs, youth engagement, participation, satisfaction, and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and each question or field.
2. Answer the questions and fill in the fields as accurately and honestly as possible.
3. Select the options that best fit your opinion or answer to each question.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 2. what_do_you_think (Text Field)
* **what_do_you_think** (`what_do_you_think`, text, optional): This field is a text field where you can enter your thoughts or opinions on a related topic. Please provide a brief and concise answer that reflects your honest opinion.

### 3. rehab_facilities (Select Multiple)
* **rehab_facilities** (`rehab_facilities`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select multiple options regarding the effectiveness or satisfaction of juvenile rehabilitation facilities. Please choose all that apply from the available options.

### 4. rehab_programs (Select Multiple)
* **rehab_programs** (`rehab_programs`, select_multiple, optional): This field is similar to the previous one, asking about the effectiveness or satisfaction of juvenile rehabilitation programs. Choose all that apply from the available options.

### 5. youth_engagement (Select Multiple)
* **youth_engagement** (`youth_engagement`, select_multiple, optional): This field concerns the effectiveness or satisfaction of engaging young individuals in juvenile rehabilitation activities. Select all that apply.

### 6. rehab_participation (Select Multiple)
* **rehab_participation** (`rehab_participation`, select_multiple, optional): This field pertains to the participation of young individuals in juvenile rehabilitation activities. Select all that apply.

### 7. youth_satisfaction (Select Multiple)
* **youth_satisfaction** (`youth_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): This field is about the satisfaction of young individuals regarding juvenile rehabilitation. Choose all that apply.

### 8. rehab_outcome (Select Multiple)
* **rehab_outcome** (`rehab_outcome`, select_multiple, optional): This field concerns the outcome of juvenile rehabilitation. Select all that apply.

### 9. other_info (Date Field)
* **other_info** (`other_info`, date, optional): This field is a date field where you can enter any additional information not related to the other fields. Please use the correct date format.

### 10. other_info_time (Time Field)
* **other_info_time** (`other_info_time`, time, optional): This field is a time field where you can specify the time of day when you entered the other information. Please use 24-hour format.

Note: This form is not required, and users are free to choose which fields they wish to answer.
