# Historical Challenges Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information about the historical challenges you have faced in your academic or professional experience. This will help us improve educational resources and services to better support you and others in similar situations.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.**
- **For multiple-choice questions, select all applicable options.**
- **For open-ended questions, provide a brief description in the text field.**
- **Take your time and be as thorough as possible.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, required: false): This is a brief welcome note to introduce the survey. You can ignore this field as it does not affect your answers.
* **Historical Challenge** (`historical_challenge`, text, required: false): Describe a historical challenge you have faced. This can be from your academic or professional experience.
* **Cultural Organization** (`cultural_organization`, select_one, required: false): Please choose which of the following cultural organizations best describes the context of your historical challenge:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all roles you have played in your historical challenge:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Study Method** (`study_method`, text, required: false): Describe the method you used to study or overcome your historical challenge.
* **Years of Study** (`years_of_study`, number, required: false): Enter the number of years you spent studying or overcoming your historical challenge.
* **Experience** (`experience`, select_one, required: false): Please choose which of the following best describes your experience:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Study Field** (`study_field`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all fields related to your historical challenge:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Survey Frequency** (`survey_frequency`, time, required: false): Please enter the time period you have experienced your historical challenge.
* **Historical Event** (`historical_event`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all historical events you have experienced:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Historical Event Type** (`historical_event_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all historical event types you have experienced:
	+ 'Yes'

* **Survey Length** (`survey_length`, number, required: false): Enter the number of times you have experienced your historical challenge.
* **Historical Challenge Type** (`historical_challenge_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all historical challenge types you have experienced:
