# public_school_parent_student_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect contact and demographic information from parents and students, as well as their involvement in the school and their goals. It is essential for the school administration to gather this data to make informed decisions about parent-student interactions and school improvement initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information on the first page (Contact Information).
2. Provide information about yourself as a parent and your child (Parent/Student).
3. Add any additional details about your contact information (Contact Details).
4. Select your child's grade level (Student Grade Level).
5. Describe your level of involvement with your child's school life (Parent Involvement).
6. Indicate how often you attend school events (Parent Attendance).
7. Select from multiple options how often you are involved in school activities (Parent Participation).
8. Add any thoughts or comments about school improvement (School Improvement).
9. Finally, specify your goals for your child while at the school (Student Goals).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: Please enter your name, email, and phone number to enable us to contact you efficiently.
* **Parent/Student**: Please provide your name, your child's name, and the relationship between you and your child (e.g., mother, father, guardian).
* **Contact Details**: Add any additional contact details, such as your work address or any other relevant information.
* **Student Grade Level**: Select your child's grade level from the options provided (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
* **Parent Involvement**: Describe your level of involvement with your child's school life (Always, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never).
* **Parent Attendance**: Indicate how often you attend school events (Always, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never).
* **Parent Participation**: Choose how often you are involved in school activities (Always, Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never).
* **School Improvement**: Add any thoughts or comments you have about how the school can improve (Note field).
* **Student Goals**: Specify any goals you have for your child while at the school (Note field).

## Tips
* Please ensure that the form is filled out by both a parent and their child to prevent duplication of efforts.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy to ensure that your responses are clear and concise.
* If you have any difficulties or questions while filling out the form, feel free to contact us for assistance.
