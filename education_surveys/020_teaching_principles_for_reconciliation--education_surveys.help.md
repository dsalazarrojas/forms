# Teaching Principles For Reconciliation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences with reconciliation practices in the context of teaching. The form will help us understand your teaching approaches, educator role, years of experience, and other relevant details. The information gathered will be used to improve teaching principles for reconciliation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your educator role from the options provided.
2. Provide your years of experience.
3. Choose the reconciliation practices that work well for you.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the selected reconciliation practices.
5. Provide comments for your ratings.
6. Select the school programs and training programs that you have participated in.
7. Provide any other reconciliation practices that work well for you.
8. Add any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teaching Approaches**: Select all the reconciliation practices that you have used in your teaching.
  + **Reconciliation through dialogue**: This approach involves actively listening to and responding to students' concerns and ideas.
  + **Reconciliation through shared decision-making**: This approach involves working with students to make decisions that take into account their needs and concerns.
  + **Reconciliation through shared reflection**: This approach involves reflecting on and discussing your own and students' actions and decisions.
  + **Reconciliation through shared planning**: This approach involves planning and implementing activities and lessons that take into account students' needs and concerns.
  + **Reconciliation through other**: Select this option if you have used another approach not listed above.
* **Educator Role**: Select the role that best describes your position in the educational context.
  + **Teacher**: You are a classroom teacher.
  + **Trainer/Coach**: You are a trainer or coach.
  + **Consultant**: You are a consultant.
  + **Administrator**: You are an administrator.
* **Years of Experience**: Provide the number of years you have been working in education.
* **Reconciliation Reflection**: Provide a detailed reflection on the reconciliation practices that work well for you.
* **Ratings**: Select all the ratings that reflect your satisfaction with the selected reconciliation practices.
  + **1**: Not satisfied

  + **4**: Very satisfied
  + **5**: Extremely satisfied
