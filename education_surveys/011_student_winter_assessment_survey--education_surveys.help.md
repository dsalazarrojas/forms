# Student Winter Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students on their winter term experience. It will help the education department understand what worked well and what did not, and make improvements for future terms.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer the questions to the best of your ability. You can select one of the options for each question, and you can add any additional comments or thoughts in the free-text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How was your winter term experience?** (`student_winter_assessment_survey`, text, required): This is a free-text field where you can describe your overall experience in the winter term. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **How would you rate the support of your academic advisors?** (`academic_support`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate the support provided by your academic advisors. Choose one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate the effectiveness of the course materials provided?** (`course_materials`, number, optional): This question asks you to rate the effectiveness of the course materials on a scale of 1-5. You can enter any number between 1 and 5.
* **How would you rate the performance of your instructor?** (`instructor_performance`, number, required): This question asks you to rate the performance of your instructor on a scale of 1-5. You can enter any number between 1 and 5.
* **What was your overall experience in the winter term?** (`overall_experience`, note, optional): This is a free-text field where you can describe your overall experience in the winter term. Please be as detailed as possible.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can add them in the free-text field.
* If you did not participate in the winter term, please do not complete this form.
