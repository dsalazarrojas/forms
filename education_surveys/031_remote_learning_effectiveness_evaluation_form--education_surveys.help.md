# Remote Learning Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Learning Effectiveness Evaluation Form is designed to collect data on the effectiveness of remote learning programs. This form is intended for educators, administrators, or students to evaluate their experience and provide valuable feedback to improve the programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and ensure you understand the purpose and context of each section and field.
2. Complete the required fields, which are marked as such in the form.
3. Answer the multiple-choice questions truthfully based on your experience.
4. Input numerical values accurately and consistently with the unit of measurement.
5. Provide thoughtful and detailed feedback in the text fields.
6. Use the additional comments field to provide any further insights or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (Note)**: A short note section for you to provide any context or background information about the program.
* **Remote Learning Programs (Select One)**: Indicate if remote learning programs are available to you. Select 'Yes' if you have access to remote learning programs.
* **Program Length (Number)**: Enter the length of the remote learning program. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Educational Resources (Select Multiple)**: Choose all the resources that were available to you during the program. Select from the options 'Yes' for each resource you had access to.
* **Instructors (Select One)**: Indicate if the instructors were available and accessible during the program. Select 'Yes' if you had sufficient instructor support.
* **Course Content (Number)**: Evaluate the quality of the course content. Assign a numerical value between 1 and 10 to reflect your experience.
* **Feedback (Text)**: Share your experience and thoughts about the program in this text field. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Suggestions (Text)**: If you have any suggestions for improvement, input them here.
* **Additional Comments (Text)**: Use this field to add any further comments or insights about your experience.
* **Email (Email)**: If you want to be contacted for follow-up questions, input your email address.
* **Phone (Text)**: If you want to be contacted by phone for follow-up questions, input your phone number.
