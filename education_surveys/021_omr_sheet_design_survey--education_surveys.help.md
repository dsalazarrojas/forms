# OMR Sheet Design Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about OMR (Optical Mark Recognition) sheet design requirements. The purpose of this form is to help us better understand your needs and preferences for designing OMR sheets. We will use this information to improve our design process and create more effective OMR sheets for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Select the "OMR Sheet Options" page, which is used to enter general information about your OMR sheet.
3. On the "OMR Sheet Options" page, you can enter the number of questions you have on your OMR sheet by filling in the "How many questions do you have on your OMR sheet?" field.
4. For each question, enter the average size of your OMR sheet and the preferred font size and type for the question text.
5. Finally, review and submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### OMR Sheet Options (page 1)
* **OMR Sheet Options** (`page1`, text, required: false): Enter general information about your OMR sheet here. This field is used to provide context for the rest of the form.

### How many questions do you have on your OMR sheet? (page 2)
* **How many questions do you have on your OMR sheet?** (`question1`, number, required: false): Please enter the total number of questions on your OMR sheet.

### What is the average size of your OMR sheet?
* **What is the average size of your OMR sheet?** (`question2`, number, required: false): Enter the average size of your OMR sheet.

### What is the preferred font size for OMR question text?
* **What is the preferred font size for OMR question text?** (`question3`, select_one, required: false): Select the preferred font size for your OMR question text from the options:
	+ Aa
	+ B
	+ C
	+ D

### What is the preferred font type for OMR question text?
* **What is the preferred font type for OMR question text?** (`question4`, select_one, required: false): Select the preferred font type for your OMR question text from the options:
	+ Arial
	+ Comic Sans
	+ Impact
	+ Times New Roman
