# Student Registration Process Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Registration Process Evaluation form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experience with the registration process. This form helps identify areas for improvement and measures the quality of communication and support provided by the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the options provided for each question and select the one that best reflects your experience.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly to provide the most accurate feedback.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, as indicated by the required label next to each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. student_registration_process_evaluation (Select one of the options)
* **student-registration-process-evaluation** (`student_registration_process_evaluation`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select the best description of your experience with the registration process.

### 2. overall_experience (Note)
* **overall-experience** (`overall_experience`, `note`, required/optional): Describe your overall experience with the registration process. Please be as detailed as possible to help us improve.

### 3. communication (Select multiple)
* **communication** (`communication`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Rate the level of communication with our team. Select all that apply:
  * Very Good
  * Good
  * Fair
  * Poor
  * Very Poor

### 4. support (Number)
* **support** (`support`, `number`, required/optional): This field is not currently being used for anything. Please leave blank.

### 5. satisfaction (Select one)
* **satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required/optional): How would you rate your satisfaction with the overall experience?
  * Very Satisfied
  * Somewhat Satisfied
  * Not Satisfied

### 6. comments (Date)
* **comments** (`comments`, `date`, required/optional): This field is intended for any additional comments or feedback about your experience.

### 7. email (Email)
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address, if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.

### 8. phone_number (Text)
* **phone-number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): This field is not currently being used for anything. Please leave blank.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by students, so the language and tone should be adjusted to be clear and concise for their audience.
