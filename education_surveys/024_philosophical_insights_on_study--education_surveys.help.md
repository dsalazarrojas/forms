# philosophical_insights_on_study - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect philosophical insights or perspectives on studies, focusing on the study's purpose, personal growth aspects, and the date of the survey.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Answer the questions on the form fields. For "study_purpose" and "personal_growth", enter your thoughts or insights as text. For "survey_date", select a date from the calendar.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **study_purpose** (ID: 1, text, required: false): What is the purpose or objective of the study you are reflecting on?
* **personal_growth** (ID: 2, text, required: false): How has the study contributed to your personal growth or development?
* **survey_date** (ID: 3, date, required: false): When was the survey conducted or completed?

## Tips

* Be as specific and detailed as possible when answering the questions.
* If you need to modify or withdraw your responses, please contact our support team.
* This form is meant to collect qualitative feedback and insights, so feel free to express your thoughts and opinions without fear of judgment.
