# Latin America Historical Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals about their background and experience with Latin American history. It will help in creating a more accurate representation of potential participants for future surveys.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your role in the Latin America region from the options provided (Researcher, Educator, or Student).
2. Choose your level of interest in Latin American history (Low, Medium, or High).
3. Indicate your level of knowledge in Latin American history (Low, Medium, or High).
4. Select your level of interest in Latin American interests (Low, Medium, or High).
5. Check if you have taken a Latin American history survey before.
6. Choose how much time you have spent in Latin America (Less than 1 month, Between 1-2 months, Between 2-6 months, or More than 6 months).
7. Select how much time you have spent in Latin America in the last year (Less than 1 month, Between 1-2 months, Between 2-6 months, or More than 6 months).
8. Choose how much time you have spent in Latin America in the last 2 years (Less than 1 month, Between 1-2 months, Between 2-6 months, or More than 6 months).
9. Indicate your education level (Bachelor, Master, or Ph.D.).
10. Choose your level of Latin American language skills (None, Basic, or Fluent).
11. Select your preferred contact method for further surveys (Email, Social media, or Phone).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in the Latin America region?** (`survey_participation`, `select_multiple`): Select your role in the Latin American region, such as Researcher, Educator, or Student.
* **What is your level of interest in Latin American history?** (`latin_american_history_level`, `select_one`): Indicate your level of interest in Latin American history, from Low to High.
* **What is your level of knowledge in Latin American history?** (`knowledge_level`, `select_one`): Select your level of knowledge in Latin American history, from Low to High.
* **What is your level of interest in Latin American interests?** (`interests_level`, `select_one`): Indicate your level of interest in Latin American interests, from Low to High.
* **Have you taken a Latin American history survey before?** (`historical_survey`, `select_one`): Check if you have taken a Latin American history survey before.
* **How much time have you spent in Latin America?** (`time_spent`, `select_one`): Choose how much time you have spent in Latin America, from Less than 1 month to More than 6 months.
* **How much time have you spent in Latin America in the last year?** (`time_spent_other`, `select_one`): Select how much time you have spent in Latin America in the last year, from Less than 1 month to More than 6 months.
* **How much time have you spent in Latin America in the last 2 years?** (`time_spent_other_last_year`, `select_one`): Choose how much time you have spent in Latin America in the last 2 years, from Less than 1 month to More than 6 months.
* **What is your education level?** (`education_level`, `select_one`): Indicate your education level, such as Bachelor, Master, or Ph.D.
* **What is your level of Latin American language skills?** (`language_level`, `select_one`): Select your level of Latin American language skills, from None to Fluent.
* **What is your preferred contact method for further surveys?** (`contact_method`, `select_one`): Choose your preferred contact method for further surveys, such as Email, Social media, or Phone.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions as honestly and accurately as possible.
* If you are unsure about any question, select the option that best matches your situation.
* You can skip any question that you do not know or cannot answer.
* If you have any further questions, contact form@gic.mx.
