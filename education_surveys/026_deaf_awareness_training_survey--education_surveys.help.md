# Deaf Awareness Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Deaf Awareness Training Survey is designed to gather feedback from participants who have attended the Deaf Awareness Training course. This form will help us understand your experience, understanding, and satisfaction with the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer according to your experiences.
2. Please answer all questions that apply to you.
3. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments" field to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Section 1: Survey
* **Survey**: This section is the introductory page of the survey. It's not a question and doesn't require an answer.

### Section 1: Section 1
* **Section 1**: This section asks about your role in the organization and your experience with Deaf/HoH individuals.

* **What is your role in the organization?**: Please select one of the options that best describes your role:
	+ Deaf/HoH
	+ Hard of Hearing
	+ Other

* **What is your level of experience in working with Deaf/HoH individuals?**: Please select one of the options that best describes your experience:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced

* **Have you attended Deaf Awareness Training in the past 2 years?**: Please select one of the options:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Section 2: Section 2
* **Section 2**: This section asks about your expectations, hopes, and understanding.

* **What do you expect to gain from this training?**: Please answer in the text field what you hope to gain from this training.

* **What do you hope to take away from this training?**: Please answer in the text field what you hope to take away from this training.

* **How do you rate your understanding of Deaf/HoH individuals?**: Please select one of the options:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Very Good

### Section 3: Section 3
* **Section 3**: This section is not a question, but rather a space to provide any additional comments or information.

### Section 4: Section 4
* **Would you recommend this training to others?**: Please select one of the options:
	+ Yes
	+ No

* **What did you think of the course layout and content?**: Please answer in the text field any comments or suggestions you have about the course layout and content.

* **Would you like to be contacted for feedback?**: Please select one of the options:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Section 5: Section 5
* **Section 5**: This section is not a question, but rather a space to provide any additional comments or information.

* **Would you like to be contacted for feedback?**: Please select one of the options:
	+ Yes
	+ No

* **How long did you spend on the course?**: Please answer in the time field how many hours you spent on the course.

* **How many days did you spend on the course?**: Please answer in the number field how many days you spent on the course.

* **How would you rate the training?**: Please select one of the options:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Very Good

* **How long do you plan to use the skills gained from this course?**: This is not a question, but rather a field to provide any additional comments or information.

* **Name**: Please enter your name.

* **Comments**: Please enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the course.

* **Email**: Please enter your email address.

* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number.

* **Name**: Please re-enter your name. 

Please note that some fields are not required, but it's essential to fill out as much information as possible to help us understand your experience and satisfaction with the training. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
