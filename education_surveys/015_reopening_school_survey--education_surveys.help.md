# Reopening School Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from schools and educators about their plans for reopening. It helps administrators and decision-makers make informed decisions about school reopening policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these general steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Ensure you select the correct options for each field.
3. Provide accurate and up-to-date information about your school.
4. Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Parent Choice
* **Parent Choice** (`parent_choice`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks if the school plans to reopen or keep schools closed due to parent choice. Select one of the two options: "Yes" or "No".

### 2. Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): Enter your school's contact information, including name, email, and phone number.

### 3. Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your school's email address.

### 4. Phone Number
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your school's phone number.

### 5. Reopen School
* **Reopen School** (`reopen_school`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks if the school plans to reopen or keep schools closed. Select one of the two options: "Yes" or "No".

### 6. School ID
* **School ID** (`school_id`, number, required/optional): Enter your school's ID number.

### 7. Grade Levels
* **Grade Levels** (`grade_levels`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable grade levels that your school serves.

### 8. School Year
* **School Year** (`school_year`, date, required/optional): Enter the current school year.

### 9. School Days
* **School Days** (`school_days`, time, required/optional): Enter the number of school days.

### 10. School Hours
* **School Hours** (`school_hours`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of school hours.

### 11. School Start
* **School Start** (`school_start`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the school will start.

### 12. School End
* **School End** (`school_end`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the school will end.

### 13. School Duration
* **School Duration** (`school_duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of days the school will operate.

### 14. Survey Language
* **Survey Language** (`survey_language`, select_one, required/optional): Select the language of the survey. Choose "Yes" or "No".

### 15. Survey Version
* **Survey Version** (`survey_version`, text, required/optional): Enter the survey version number.

### 16. Survey ID
* **Survey ID** (`survey_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the survey ID number.

### 17. School Name
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your school's name.

### 18. School Address
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your school's address.

### 19. School City
* **School City** (`school_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city where your school is located.

### 20. School State
* **School State** (`school_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state where your school is located.

### 21. School Zip
* **School Zip** (`school_zip`, number, required/optional): Enter the zip code of your school.

### 22. School Country
* **School Country** (`school_country`, text, required/optional): Enter the country where your school is located.

### 23. School Region
* **School Region** (`school_region`, text, required/optional): Enter the school's region.

### 24. School Timezone
* **School Timezone** (`school_timezone`, text, required/optional): Enter the school's time zone.

### 25. School Coordinates
* **School Coordinates** (`school_coordinates`, text, required/optional): Enter the school's coordinates.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer to the instructions or ask your administrator for guidance.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions or approvals before submitting the form.
