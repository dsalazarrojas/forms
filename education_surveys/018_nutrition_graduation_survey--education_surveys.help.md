# Nutrition Graduation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from graduates about their experience in a nutrition program. This feedback will help us evaluate the effectiveness of our program and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page and take a moment to reflect on your experience in the nutrition program.
2. On the next page, evaluate the curriculum of the program by selecting one of the six options provided.
3. Choose the outcomes you believe you have achieved as a result of the program.
4. Provide your satisfaction feedback on the program by selecting one or more options.
5. Enter your suggestions for improvement, if any.
6. If you have any feedback about the tools used by you during the program, please provide that information.
7. Finally, enter your name and contact information so we can follow up with you.
8. If you would like to provide a graduation date and assigned tool, you can do so, but this is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This page is an introduction to the form and is meant to help you get familiar with the survey.
* **Curriculum Evaluation** (`curriculum_evaluation`, `number`, required: false): Please evaluate the effectiveness of the program's curriculum. Select the number from 1 to 6 that best represents your thoughts on this.
* **Outcomes** (`outcomes`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the outcomes you believe you have achieved as a result of the program. Select one option that best represents your experience.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select one or more options that represent your level of satisfaction with the program.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, required: false): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in the text field.
* **Program Evaluation** (`program_evaluation`, `text`, required: false): If you have any feedback about the program, please enter it in this field.
* **Graduate Name** (`graduate_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name as a graduate of the program.
* **Graduate Contact** (`graduate_contact`, `text`, required: false): If you wish to be contacted, please enter your contact information.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, `date`, required: false): If you want to provide the date you graduated, you can do so here.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): If you were assigned any specific tools during the program, please enter the name of the tool.
* **Output File Name** (`output_file_name`, `text`, required: false): If you have any suggestions for the name of the output file, please enter it here.
