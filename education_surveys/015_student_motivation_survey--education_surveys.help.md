# Student Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Motivation Survey is a tool used to assess a student's motivation levels, strengths, weaknesses, and needs. The survey is designed to collect information that can inform support and development initiatives for students.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, please enter the School ID of the student you are surveying.
2.  Enter the student's name in the Student Name field.
3.  Enter the student's age in the Student Age field.
4.  Enter the student's email address in the Student Email field.
5.  Select the student's grade level from the Student Grade dropdown menu.
6.  Indicate the student's gender in the Gender field. 
7.  Check all that apply for the student's motivations in the Motivation field.
8.  Enter any relevant strengths or positive qualities the student possesses in the Strengths field.
9.  Enter any areas where the student may need improvement in the Weaknesses field.
10.  Describe any needs or requirements the student has in the Needs field.
11.  Enter the date the survey was administered in the Survey Date field.
12.  Enter the time the survey was administered in the Survey Time field.
13.  Enter any additional comments or notes about the survey in the Survey Note field.
14.  Indicate if the student has a school counselor in the School Counselor field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **School ID** (number, not required): Enter the student's school ID number.
*   **Student Name** (text, not required): Enter the student's name.
*   **Student Age** (number, not required): Enter the student's age.
*   **Student Email** (email, not required): Enter the student's email address.
*   **Student Grade** (number, not required): Select the student's grade level.
*   **Gender** (select one, not required): Select the student's gender.
*   **Motivation** (select multiple, not required): Check all that apply for the student's motivations.
*   **Strengths** (text, not required): Enter any relevant strengths or positive qualities the student possesses.
*   **Weaknesses** (text, not required): Enter any areas where the student may need improvement.
*   **Needs** (text, not required): Describe any needs or requirements the student has.
*   **Survey Date** (date, not required): Enter the date the survey was administered.
*   **Survey Time** (time, not required): Enter the time the survey was administered.
*   **Survey Note** (note, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the survey.
*   **School Counselor** (select one, not required): Indicate if the student has a school counselor.

## Tips
*   Be as specific as possible when filling out the form, especially in fields like Strengths, Weaknesses, and Needs.
*   Ensure that you have permission from the student to administer this survey.
*   Review the survey for completeness before submitting it.
*   Use this information to support student development initiatives and improve student outcomes.
