# Writing Method Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information on writing methods and preferences from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select the options that best match your writing method and preferences.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **writing_method_selection_survey** (1): Enter a brief title for this survey.
- **writing_method_selection_survey_subheading** (2): Enter a brief subtitle for this survey.
- **question_1** (5): Select "Yes" if you prefer writing by hand, "No" otherwise.
- **question_2** (6): Select all that apply if you prefer writing on a computer, a tablet, or a smartphone.
- **question_3** (9): Enter the number of hours you dedicate to writing each week.
- **question_4** (10): Enter the time you usually spend on writing each day.
- **question_5** (11): Enter any additional comments or notes about your writing habits.
- **question_6** (12): Enter the date of your last written work.
- **contact_name** (14): Enter your name.
- **contact_email** (15): Enter your email address.
- **question_7** (16): Enter any additional comments or notes about your writing experience.
- **question_8** (19): Select "Yes" if you prefer writing in the morning, "No" otherwise.
- **question_9** (20): Select all that apply if you prefer writing on a specific day of the week or at a specific time.
- **question_10** (21): Enter any additional comments or notes about your writing routine.
- **question_11** (22): Enter any specific writing tools or resources you use.
- **question_12** (23): Enter any specific writing locations or settings you prefer.
- **question_13** (24): Enter any specific writing schedules or deadlines you adhere to.
- **question_14** (25): Enter any additional comments or notes about your writing experience.

Note: Please make sure to double-check your answers before submitting the survey, as the survey cannot be saved or retrieved for later completion.
