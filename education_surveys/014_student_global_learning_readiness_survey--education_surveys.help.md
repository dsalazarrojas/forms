# Student Global Learning Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand students' global learning goals, preferences, and readiness to be a global citizen.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please go through the sections one by one and answer the questions honestly.

- Fill in any personal information in the **General Information** section (page 1).
- Select all that apply in the **What are your Global Learning Goals?** and **What are your Global Learning Preferences?** sections (page 2).
- In the **Global Citizenship** section, select all that apply (pages 3-5).
- Answer the questions about **Global Leadership** and **Motivation to Change** (pages 6-11).
