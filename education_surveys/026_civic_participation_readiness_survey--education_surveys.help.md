# Civic Participation Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Civic Participation Readiness Survey is a tool used to measure community members' willingness and ability to engage in various civic activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the requested information, making sure to provide accurate and honest responses.
2. For each field, use the provided hints to guide your answers.
3. If you need more time or have questions, do not hesitate to reach out to the survey administrators for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name** (Respondent name, text, Optional): Your name, as you wish to be known in this survey.
* **Community affiliation** (Community affiliation, text, Optional): Please indicate your affiliation with any neighborhood organization, school, or workplace that you are a part of.
* **Perceived readiness to participate** (Perceived readiness to participate, number, Required): Rate your perceived level of readiness to participate in civic activities on a scale of 1 (not ready) to 5 (very ready).
* **Confidence in civic skills** (Confidence in civic skills, number, Optional): Rate your confidence in your public speaking, research, and organizing abilities on a scale of 1 (not confident) to 5 (very confident).
* **Interest in training opportunities** (Interest in training opportunities, select_multiple, Optional): Select any of the topics you would like to learn about, such as Organizing, Advocacy, Research methods, or Communication.
* **Resource barriers to participation** (Resource barriers to participation, text, Optional): If applicable, please list any resource barriers that prevent you from participating in civic activities, such as Time, Funding, or Transport.
* **Preferred support from organizations** (Preferred support from organizations, select_multiple, Optional): Select any of the supports you would like to receive from organizations, such as Funding, Training, Mentorship, or Materials.
* **Willingness to take leadership roles** (Willingness to take leadership roles, select_one, Required): Indicate your willingness to take on leadership roles in civic activities, choosing between 'True', 'Maybe', or 'False'.
* **Additional comments** (Additional comments, text, Optional): If you have any other feedback or thoughts, please share them in this field.
* **Submission date** (Submission date, date, Required): Enter the current date to confirm your submission. 

Note: Please ensure that you complete all required fields (marked as Required) and provide accurate information to support the survey's purpose.
