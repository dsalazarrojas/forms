# Special Education Teacher Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Special Education Teacher Feedback Form" is designed to collect feedback from students about their special education teachers. This feedback will help the school administration and teachers to identify areas of improvement and develop strategies for better support and educational experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
* For questions that allow multiple answers (e.g., "Teacher Feedback Score"), select all applicable options.
* Make sure to provide specific examples or instances that support your feedback.
* Be respectful and constructive in your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Info** (`teacher_info`, text, optional): Please provide your name and contact information so that we can contact you for any follow-up questions or clarifications.
* **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts about the teacher's teaching style, approach, and effectiveness.
* **Teacher Feedback** (`teacher_feedback`, text, optional): Provide feedback on the teacher's ability to meet the needs of students with special education requirements.
* **Teacher Performance** (`teacher_performance`, text, optional): Evaluate the teacher's performance in terms of teaching skills, lesson planning, and overall effectiveness.
* **Classroom Atmosphere** (`classroom_atmosphere`, text, optional): Comment on the classroom environment, including safety, organization, and respect for students' needs.
* **Teacher Support** (`teacher_support`, text, optional): Describe the level of support provided by the teacher, including guidance, encouragement, and accommodations.
* **Classroom Setup** (`classroom_setup`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on the classroom setup, including accessibility, comfort, and resources.
* **Teacher Training** (`teacher_training`, text, optional): Suggest areas where the teacher may need additional training or professional development.
* **Student Participation** (`student_participation`, text, optional): Evaluate the level of student participation and engagement in the classroom.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional thoughts, suggestions, or ideas you have for the teacher's improvement.
* **Teacher Suggestions** (`teacher_suggestions`, text, optional): Offer suggestions for the teacher's growth and development.
* **Teacher Evaluation** (`teacher_evaluation`, text, optional): Summarize your overall evaluation of the teacher's performance.
* **Teacher Recommendations** (`teacher_recommendations`, text, optional): Suggest areas where the teacher excels and can be recognized for their outstanding work.
* **Submission** (`submission`, date, optional): Record the date and time you submitted the feedback.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Record the time when you submitted the feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Share your phone number so that we can reach you if needed.
* **Teacher Rating** (`teacher_rating`, select one, optional): Rate the teacher's performance using one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Teacher Feedback Score** (`teacher_feedback_score`, select multiple, optional): Select all options that apply to your feedback score:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
