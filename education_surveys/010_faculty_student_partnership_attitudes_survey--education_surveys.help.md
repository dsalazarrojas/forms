# Faculty Student Partnership Attitudes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to measure the attitudes and perceptions of students and faculty members towards their partnership. Your input will help us better understand the effectiveness of our partnership and identify areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answers that best represent your opinions and experiences. You can choose one or multiple options for each question, depending on the type of question asked. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to provide a note in the corresponding text field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. How would you rate your overall satisfaction**: Select the level of satisfaction you feel towards our faculty-student partnership. You can choose from: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neither, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **2. How often do you feel that faculty members support you**: Choose how often you feel supported by faculty members. You can select from: Very often, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never.
* **3. How often do students feel that faculty members support them**: Select how often you believe students feel supported by faculty members. You can choose from: Very often, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never.
* **4. What activities promote faculty-student partnership**: Identify the activities that you believe promote a strong partnership between faculty and students. You can select multiple options.
	+ Collaborative projects
	+ Mentoring
	+ Teaching
	+ Community engagement
* **5. How important is it to faculty members**: Rate the importance of student-faculty partnership to faculty members. Please enter a number from 1-5, where 1 is not important and 5 is very important.
* **6. How important is it to students**: Rate the importance of student-faculty partnership to students. Please enter a number from 1-5, where 1 is not important and 5 is very important.
* **7. How would you rate communication between faculty and students**: Select the effectiveness of communication between faculty and students. You can choose from: Very effective, Somewhat effective, Neutral, Somewhat ineffective, or Very ineffective. (Note: This question is optional)
* **8. How do you feel about faculty-student partnership**: Provide a note about your feelings towards faculty-student partnership.
* **9. How do you feel about student-faculty partnership**: Select your level of agreement towards student-faculty partnership. You can choose from: Strongly agree, Somewhat agree, Neither agree nor disagree, Somewhat disagree, or Strongly disagree. (Note: This question is optional)
* **10. How satisfied are you with student-faculty partnership**: Select the level of satisfaction you feel towards student-faculty partnership. You can choose from: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neither, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied. (Note: This question is optional)
* **11. How fulfilling is student participation**: Select how fulfilling you find student participation in faculty-student partnership. You can choose from: Very fulfilling, Fulfilling, Neutral, Somewhat not fulfilling, or Not fulfilling.
* **12. How often do you participate in faculty-student partnership**: Select how often you participate in faculty-student partnership. You can choose from: Very often, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never. (Note: This question is optional)

Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide honest feedback. Your input will help us better understand the effectiveness of our partnership and identify areas for improvement.
