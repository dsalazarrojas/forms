# Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey is designed to gather information about respondents' awareness and perceptions of foreign conflicts, specifically regarding their sources of information, perception of conflict impact, and factors influencing conflict resolution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the options that best represent your public awareness of foreign conflicts.
2. Choose the sources of information you use to stay informed about foreign conflicts.
3. Indicate your perception of the impact of foreign conflicts on your daily life.
4. Select the factors that influence your perception of conflict impact.
5. Choose the options that best represent your perception of conflict prevention.
6. Indicate your perception of international community response to foreign conflicts.
7. Select the options that best represent your perception of global awareness.
8. Choose the options that best represent your perception of foreign policy.
9. Select the options that best represent your perception of education in conflict resolution.
10. Choose the options that best represent your perception of global conflict prevention.
11. Indicate your perception of local conflict resolution.
12. Select the options that best represent your perception of civil society involvement in conflict resolution.
13. Choose the options that best represent your perception of international leaders' involvement in conflict resolution.
14. Select the options that best represent other factors that influence conflict resolution.
15. Indicate your perception of conflict resolution.
