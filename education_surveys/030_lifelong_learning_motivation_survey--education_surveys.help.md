# Lifelong Learning Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your motivations for learning new skills and your preferences for learning environments. Your responses will help us understand how to better support and facilitate learning opportunities for you and others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the option that best describes your motivation for learning new skills in the "What motivates you to learn new skills?" section.
2. For each option selected, select all that apply in the corresponding "Answer" section.
3. In the "What are your preferences for learning?" section, select the option that best describes your learning style.
4. For each option selected, select all that apply in the corresponding "Answer" section.
5. You may optionally provide a description of your learning goals and experiences in the "Description" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lifelong Learning Motivation Survey** (Text, required): Enter a title for this survey.
* **What motivates you to learn new skills?** (`select_one`, required): This question asks about your motivation for learning new skills.
	+ **Motivated by personal interest**: You are motivated to learn new skills because you are interested in the subject matter.
	+ **Motivated by career goals**: You are motivated to learn new skills because you want to advance your career or improve your job prospects.
	+ **Motivated by external pressures**: You are motivated to learn new skills because of external factors, such as pressure from family or friends.
* **What motivates you to learn new skills? Answer** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply.
	+ **Personal interest**
	+ **Career goals**
	+ **External pressures**
* **What barriers prevent you from learning?** (`select_one`, required): This question asks about barriers to learning.
	+ **Limited time**: You don't have enough time to dedicate to learning.
	+ **Financial constraints**: You don't have the financial resources to support your learning.
	+ **Lack of support**: You don't have access to support, such as mentors or resources.
* **What barriers prevent you from learning? Answer** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply.
	+ **Work or family responsibilities**
	+ **Health issues**
	+ **Financial constraints**
* **What are your preferences for learning?** (`select_one`, required): This question asks about your learning style.
	+ **In-person instruction**: You prefer learning in a physical setting, such as a classroom or workshop.
	+ **Online learning**: You prefer learning online, through digital media.
	+ **Self-guided learning**: You prefer learning at your own pace, without direct instruction.
* **What are your preferences for learning? Answer** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply.
	+ **Collaborative learning**
	+ **Interacting with others**
	+ **Face-to-face interaction**
* **Description** (Text, optional): You may optionally provide a description of your learning goals and experiences.
* **Category** (Text, optional): Enter the category of this survey.
* **Version** (Number, optional): Enter the version number of this survey.
* **Author** (Text, optional): Enter the author of this survey.
* **Comments** (Text, optional): You may optionally provide comments about this survey.
* **Tags** (Text, optional): Enter tags for this survey.
* **Created at** (Date, optional): The date this survey was created.
* **Updated at** (Date, optional): The date this survey was last updated.
* **Published at** (Date, optional): The date this survey was published.
* **Status** (Text, optional): Enter the status of this survey.
* **Online learning** (select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply.
	+ **Accessibility of resources**
	+ **Flexibility of schedule**
	+ **Self-paced learning**
