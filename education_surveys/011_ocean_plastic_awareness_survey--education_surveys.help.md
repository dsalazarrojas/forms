# Ocean Plastic Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information on ocean plastic pollution awareness and attitudes among the general public.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your understanding.
2. Select the most appropriate option for multiple-choice questions.
3. Type your response in the text field for free-text questions.
4. Submit your answers to complete the survey.
