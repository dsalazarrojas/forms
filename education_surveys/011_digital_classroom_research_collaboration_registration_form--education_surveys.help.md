# digital_classroom_research_collaboration_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The digital classroom research collaboration registration form is designed to collect necessary information about digital research collaborations, enabling accurate tracking and analysis of project details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide collaboration details.
2. Enter researcher details.
3. Add collaborator details.
4. Choose the collaboration type.
5. Select the collaboration start and end dates (if applicable).
6. Input collaborator contact information (phone and email).
7. Add notes for the researcher and collaborator.
8. Select the collaboration and collaborator status.
9. Choose the assigned tool.
10. Select the collaborator location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Collaboration Details** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the collaboration.
* **Researcher Details** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your researcher details, including your role in the collaboration.
* **Collator Details** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Enter the collaborator's details, including their role in the collaboration.
* **Collaboration Type** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of collaboration (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Collaboration Start Date** (`5`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the collaboration.
* **Collaboration End Date** (`6`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the collaboration.
* **Collaborator Phone** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Enter the collaborator's phone number.
* **Collaborator Email** (`8`, `email`, required: false): Enter the collaborator's email address.
* **Collaborator Note** (`9`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about the collaborator.
* **Researcher Note** (`10`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about the researcher.
* **Collaboration Status** (`11`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the collaboration (e.g., Active, Inactive, or Completed).
* **Collaborator Status** (`12`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the collaborator (e.g., Active, Inactive, or Completed).
* **Assigned Tool** (`13`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the tool assigned to the collaboration.
* **Collaborator Location** (`14`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the collaborator's location (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

## Tips

* Ensure that you have all the necessary information before completing the form.
* Double-check your inputs for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your administrator.
