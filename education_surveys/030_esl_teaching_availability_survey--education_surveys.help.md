# ESL Teaching Availability Survey - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form

1. **Section 1**: Answer the following questions to indicate your availability and preferences.
2. **Section 2**: Provide your personal details and contact information.
3. **Submit**: Once you have completed all the sections, click the "Submit" button to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Section 1: Availability and Preferences**
  * **available_days**: Select all the days you are available to teach.
	+ Options: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday
* **start_date**: Enter the earliest date you are available to start teaching.
	+ Format: YYYY-MM-DD
* **end_date**: Enter the latest date you are available to end teaching.
	+ Format: YYYY-MM-DD
* **preferred_time**: Select your preferred teaching time.
	+ Format: 00:00-24:00
* **available_teachers**: Select all teachers you are available to teach.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **unavailable_teachers**: Select all teachers you are not available to teach.
	+ Options: Yes, No

* **Section 2: Personal Details**
  * **name**: Enter your full name.
	+ Format: Teacher Name
* **email**: Enter your email address.
	+ Format: email@domain.com
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
	+ Format: 123-456-7890
* **available_teachers_2**: Select all teachers you are available to teach.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **unavailable_teachers_2**: Select all teachers you are not available to teach.
	+ Options: Yes, No

* **Section 3: Additional Information**
  * **comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your availability.
* **name_2**: Enter any additional name information (if required).
* **name_3**: Enter any additional name information (if required).

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities.
* Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to submit your survey.
