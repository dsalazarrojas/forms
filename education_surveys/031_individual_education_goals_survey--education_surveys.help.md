# Individual Education Goals Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Individual Education Goals Survey is designed to gather information from parents or guardians about their child's educational goals, including whether the child receives special education services, demographic information, needs, and educational objectives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and the information being requested.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately, taking into account the child's current situation.
3. For each question, select the appropriate option from the list or enter the required information.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please contact the person who requested this form for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Education Survey (Page 1)

* **Education Survey (Education Survey, select_one, required: false)**:
	+ This question asks if your child receives special education services. Choose 'Yes' if your child receives special education services, and 'No' otherwise.

### Student Information (Page 2)

* **Student Information (Student Information, select_multiple, required: false)**:
	+ This section allows you to provide additional demographic information about your child. Select 'Yes' for each characteristic that applies to your child.

### Student Needs (Page 2)

* **Student Needs (Student Needs, select_multiple, required: false)**:
	+ This section asks about your child's needs. Select 'Yes' for each need that applies to your child.

### Educational Objectives (Page 3)

* **Educational Objectives (Educational Objectives, text, required: false)**:
	+ Describe your child's educational objectives and goals. This information will help us understand your child's learning aspirations and needs.

### Parent Involvement (Page 4)

* **Parent Involvement (Parent Involvement, text, required: false)**:
	+ Share any information about your involvement in your child's education, such as your role, communication preferences, and any relevant contact information.

## Tips

* Please answer each question truthfully and accurately, taking into account your child's current situation.
* If you are unsure about any question, please contact the person who requested this form for guidance.
* Please review the form carefully to ensure all required information is provided.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.
