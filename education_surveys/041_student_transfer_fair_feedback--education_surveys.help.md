# Student Transfer Fair Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students who attended the Student Transfer Fair. Your input will help us understand what worked well and what we can improve for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your Event Name and Date to help us identify the specific event you attended.
2. Indicate whether the event was In-Person or Virtual.
3. If you were able to attend, select whether you visited any colleges or met with representatives from colleges.
4. If you interacted with college representatives, select whether you had a positive experience.
5. Finally, please provide any additional feedback about your experience at the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you attended.
	* This is a text field where you should type the name of the event.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
	* This is a date field where you should type the date of the event.
- **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select whether the event was In-Person or Virtual.
	* This field helps us understand the format of the event you attended.
- **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event (e.g., campus building or online platform).
	* This is a text field where you should type the location of the event.
- **Colleges Attended** (`colleges_attended`, select_multiple, optional): Select the colleges or universities you visited during the event.
	* This field allows you to indicate which colleges you interacted with.
- **Colleges Met** (`colleges_met`, select_multiple, optional): Select the colleges or universities whose representatives you met with during the event.
	* This field helps us understand which colleges you had meaningful interactions with.
- **Event Experience** (`event_experience`, select_one, optional): Select whether you found the event helpful in meeting your goals or not.
	* This field helps us evaluate the overall effectiveness of the event in meeting your goals.
- **Event Presentation** (`event_presentation`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the event.
	* This is an open text field where you can type any comments or suggestions you have.
