# University Faculty Employment Workload Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Faculty Employment Workload Survey is designed to gather information about the faculty workload at your institution. This survey will help administrators understand how faculty members are spending their time and resources, and will provide valuable insights to inform decisions about resource allocation and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. For each question, select the best answer that reflects your current situation.
3. If you're unsure about any of the questions, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **University Faculty Employment Workload Survey**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about your workload that didn't fit into the other questions.
* **Teaching Workload**: This question is asking about how many students you are teaching per week. Please respond with the actual number of students you are teaching.
* **Research Workload**: This question is asking about the amount of time you spend on research activities per week. Please respond with the actual number of hours you spend on research activities.
* **Service Workload**: This question is asking about the amount of time you spend on service activities per week, such as committee work, departmental service, or other activities.
* **Institutional Support**: This question has four options:
	+ Option 1: I have access to all institutional support I need
	+ Option 2: I have limited access to institutional support
	+ Option 3: I have no access to institutional support
	+ Option 4: Other (please specify)
Please select the option that best describes your current situation.
* **Staffing Planning**: This question has four options:
	+ Option 1: I am well-staffed
	+ Option 2: I am under-staffed
	+ Option 3: My department has no control over staffing
	+ Option 4: Other (please specify)
Please select the option that best describes your current situation.
* **Trends and Benchmarking**: This question asks if you are aware of any trends or benchmarking activities within your department or institution.
* **Institutional Support Plan**: This question has two options:
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
Please select the option that best describes your current situation.
* **University Funds**: This question has four options:
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
	+ Option 3: Not applicable
	+ Option 4: Other (please specify)
Please select the option that best describes your current situation.
* **Departmental Funds**: This question has four options:
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
	+ Option 3: Not applicable
	+ Option 4: Other (please specify)
Please select the option that best describes your current situation.
* **Personal Funds**: This question has four options:
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
	+ Option 3: Not applicable
	+ Option 4: Other (please specify)
Please select the option that best describes your current situation.
* **Other Funds**: This question has four options:
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
	+ Option 3: Not applicable
	+ Option 4: Other (please specify)
Please select the option that best describes your current situation.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please refer back to the field-by-field explanation above.
* You can add any additional comments or information about your workload in the text field at the top of the form.
