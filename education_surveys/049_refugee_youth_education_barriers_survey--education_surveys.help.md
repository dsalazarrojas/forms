# Refugee Youth Education Barriers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refugee Youth Education Barriers Survey is designed to collect information about the education barriers faced by refugee youth. The survey aims to understand their experiences, challenges, and support needs in accessing education.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please answer each question carefully and honestly, as the information will be used for research purposes only.**
2. **You can complete the form in multiple sections, and you can skip any questions that you feel uncomfortable answering or do not know the answer to.**
3. **Be as specific as possible when answering each question, as this will help provide valuable insights.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Background Info (Page 1)**: This section is where you can provide some basic information about yourself, including your name, age, and nationality.
* **Family Background (Page 2)**: This section is about your family and household, including the number of people living with you, their ages, and their relationships with each other.
* **Education History (Page 3)**: This section is where you can describe your past educational experiences, including any formal education you may have had or are currently pursuing.
* **Education Barriers (Page 4)**: Here you can list any challenges you face in accessing education, such as language barriers, lack of resources, or difficulties with school administrators.
* **Support Needs (Page 5)**: This section is where you can identify what kind of support you need to access education, such as emotional support, financial assistance, or access to resources.
* **Refugee Background (Page 6)**: This section is about your background as a refugee, including how long you have been living as a refugee, your country of origin, and your current country of residence.
* **Family Safety (Page 7)**: This section is about the safety of your family, including any concerns or threats you may face in your daily life.
* **Education Support (Page 8)**: Here you can describe the support you receive or need to access education, such as language support, financial support, or emotional support.
* **Family Financial (Page 9)**: This section is about your family's financial situation, including any income, expenses, or financial struggles you may face.
* **Education Expectations (Page 10)**: This section is where you can describe your expectations for your education, such as your career goals, educational level, or desired educational outcomes.
* **Education Preferences (Page 11)**: Here you can list your preferences for education, such as language of instruction, learning style, or preferred educational path.
* **Education Goals (Page 12)**: This section is about your educational goals, including what you hope to achieve through education.
* **Family Relationships (Page 13)**: This section is about your relationships with family members, including their level of support and involvement in your education.
* **Education Attitudes (Page 14)**: Here you can describe your attitudes towards education, such as your motivation, interest, or enthusiasm for learning.
* **Education Barriers 2 (Page 15)**: This section is another opportunity to list any additional education barriers you face.
* **Education Barriers 3 (Page 16)**: Similar to the previous section, this is where you can list any further education barriers.
* **Family Structure (Page 17)**: This section is about your family structure, including the number of people living with you, their relationships with each other, and your role within the family.
* **Education Services (Page 18)**: Here you can describe the educational services you receive or need, such as language support, transportation, or counseling.
* **Education Outcomes (Page 19)**: This section is about your desired educational outcomes, including academic achievements, career goals, or personal growth.
* **Education Expectations 2 (Page 20)**: This section is similar to the previous one, but with another set of expectations for your education.
* **Refugee Identity (Page 21)**: This section is about your identity as a refugee, including your experiences, challenges, and feelings associated with being a refugee.
* **Family Mental Health (Page 22)**: This section is about the mental health of your family, including any concerns or struggles you may face.
* **Education Attitudes 2 (Page 23)**: Here you can describe your attitudes towards education, similar to the previous section.
* **Family Emotional Support (Page 24)**: This section is about the emotional support you receive or need from your family, such as encouragement, motivation, or emotional stability.
* **Education Support 2 (Page 25)**: This section is where you can describe the support you receive or need for education, similar to the previous section.

## Tips
* Please take your time to complete the form carefully and honestly.
* If you need assistance or have questions, feel free to reach out to the contact person mentioned above.
* The information collected will be used to support refugee youth education and improve their experiences.
