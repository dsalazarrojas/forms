# Education Policy Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is intended to help you complete the Education Policy Awareness Survey. The survey aims to gather information and opinions about various aspects of education policy.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the "Education Policy Awareness Survey" option from the dropdown menu.
2. Review each question carefully, taking your time to answer each section.
3. Respond to each question honestly, and provide as much detail as you need to answer the question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Education Policy Awareness Survey** (`education_policy_awareness_survey`): This is the title of the survey, which is meant to help you identify the form's purpose.
* **Education Policy Understanding** (`education_policy_understanding`): This question asks about your level of understanding of education policy. Please select the option that best represents your understanding:
	+ Fully understand
	+ Somewhat understand
	+ Don't understand
* **Impact of Education Policy on Learning** (`policy_impact`): This question asks about the impact of education policy on students' learning. Please select all that apply:
	+ Positive Impact
	+ Negative Impact
	+ Neutral Impact
* **Description of Policy Impact** (`policy_impact_description`): This question asks for a more detailed description of the impact of education policy on students' learning.
* **Student Engagement with Educational Policy** (`student_engagement`): This question asks you to estimate the time spent on engaging with educational policy.
* **Educator Engagement with Educational Policy** (`educator_engagement`): This question asks for your level of engagement with education policy as an educator.
* **Organizational Support of Educational Policy** (`organizational_support`): This question asks about your level of support for education policy within your organization:
	+ Strongly Support
	+ Somewhat Support
	+ Not at all
* **Education Policy Influence on Decisions** (`education_policy_influence`): This question asks about the influence of education policy on your decisions:
	+ Very Influential
	+ Somewhat Influential
	+ Not Influential
* **Effectiveness of Education Policy** (`policy_effectiveness`): This question asks about the effectiveness of education policy:
	+ Very Effective
	+ Somewhat Effective
	+ Not Effective
* **Sustaining Education Policy** (`policy_sustainability`): This question asks about the sustainability of education policy:
	+ Very Sustainable
	+ Somewhat Sustainable
	+ Not Sustainable
* **Areas for Improvement in Education Policy** (`policy_improvement`): This question asks for your suggestions on areas where education policy can be improved.
* **Description of Areas for Improvement** (`policy_improvement_description`): This question asks for a more detailed description of the areas where education policy can be improved.
* **Evaluation of Education Policy** (`policy_evaluation`): This question asks for your overall evaluation of education policy. 

## Tips

- Take your time to answer each question honestly and carefully.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the survey, please don't hesitate to reach out to your administrator.
- This survey is meant to be voluntary and anonymous, so feel free to answer as truthfully as you can.
