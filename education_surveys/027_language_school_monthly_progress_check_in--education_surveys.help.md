# Language School Monthly Progress Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help language school administrators track student progress in language programs on a monthly basis. It's used to gather information about student's language skills, progress notes, and any comments or feedback from students or teachers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the Student ID number.
*   Enter the Program Name of the student's language program.
*   Select the Language Skill(s) relevant to the student's progress.
*   Enter your Progress Notes about the student's progress.
*   Add any Student Feedback or comments from the student.
*   Enter your Teacher Notes about the student's progress.
*   Add any additional comments or notes about the student's progress in the Language School Monthly Progress Check In Comments section.
*   Enter the student's Email address.
*   Enter the student's Phone Number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Student ID (2)**: Enter the student's unique identifier number.
*   **Program Name (3)**: Enter the name of the student's language program.
*   **Progress Notes (4)**: Add any progress notes or comments about the student's progress.
*   **Date (5)**: Enter the date of the progress check-in.
*   **Language Skill (6)**: Select one or more language skills relevant to the student's progress (e.g., Reading, Writing, Speaking, Listening, Practicing).
*   **Student Feedback (7)**: Add any comments or feedback from the student.
*   **Teacher Notes (8)**: Add comments or notes about the student's progress from the teacher.
*   **Language School Monthly Progress Check In Comments (9)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the student's progress.
*   **Email (10)**: Enter the student's email address.
*   **Phone Number (11)**: Enter the student's phone number.

## Tips
*   Make sure to save your progress check-in form regularly to avoid losing your work.
*   Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
*   Use clear and concise language when entering your progress notes and comments.
*   Be sure to keep student feedback and comments confidential.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact your language school administrator.
