## Peer Learning Facilitation Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experience with peer learning facilitation. It is intended for facilitators, instructors, or educators who have led or participated in peer learning activities. The survey aims to gather feedback on the effectiveness of the peer learning model and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the survey questions carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in the required information for each question, and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
3. When selecting an option from the "effective_peer_learning_model" field, choose the option that best reflects your experience.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### session_id (1)
• **session_id** (number, optional): Enter the ID of the peer learning session you are reporting on.

### facilitation_experience (2)
• **facilitation_experience** (text, optional): Describe your experience with facilitating peer learning sessions. Try to be as specific as possible about what you found helpful or challenging.

### challenges_encountered (3)
• **challenges_encountered** (text, optional): List any challenges you encountered during the peer learning session. This can include technical issues, participant behavior, or other difficulties.

### suggestions_for_improvement (4)
• **suggestions_for_improvement** (text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving the peer learning model. Be as specific as possible about what you think would make the model more effective.

### effective_peer_learning_model (5)
• **effective_peer_learning_model** (select_one, optional): Select the option that best reflects your experience with the peer learning model. You can choose between "Effective" and "Not Effective".

### rating_of_effectiveness (6)
• **rating_of_effectiveness** (number, optional): Rate the effectiveness of the peer learning model, with higher numbers indicating a more effective experience.

### comments (7)
• **comments** (text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the peer learning model.

### facilitation_time (8)
• **facilitation_time** (time, optional): Estimate the total time spent facilitating the peer learning session.

## Tips
• Be honest and thorough in your responses to help us better understand your experience.
• If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, feel free to contact us.
• Your feedback is valuable to us and will help us improve the peer learning model.
