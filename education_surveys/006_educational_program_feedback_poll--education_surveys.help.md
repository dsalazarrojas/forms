# Educational Program Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for gathering feedback from participants on educational programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the rating question by selecting one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
2. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number, if you would like to be contacted for further discussion.
3. Provide feedback on your experience with the educational program. This will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve.
4. If applicable, report the length of the session you attended.
5. If you have any additional comments or concerns, you can enter them in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rating** (rating, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the educational program. Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Contact Info** (contact_info, text, optional): Enter your name, title, and department (if applicable) to allow us to follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Session Details** (session_details, text, optional): Describe the specific session you attended, including the date, time, and location.
* **Session Length** (session_length, number, optional): Report the length of the session you attended. Enter the number of hours or minutes you spent in the session.
* **Session Length Range** (session_length_range, number, optional): If you're unsure of the exact session length, enter an approximate range (e.g., 1-2 hours).
* **Feedback** (feedback, text, optional): Share any comments or suggestions you have about the educational program. This helps us improve future sessions.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email, email, optional): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you for further discussion.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you for further discussion.
