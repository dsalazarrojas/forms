# Cultural Diversity And Immigration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather information on cultural diversity and immigration. This form will help us understand the demographics, language proficiency, education level, years of residence in the USA, and opinions on the importance and challenges of cultural diversity and immigration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply select the appropriate options for each question or enter your responses in the text fields. Make sure to answer all the questions that are marked as required. If a question is not required, you may choose to answer it or leave it blank. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction

*   **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, not required): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.

### Demographic Information

*   **Demographic Information** (`demographic_info`, `select_one`, not required): Select your gender from the options provided:
    *   Male
    *   Female
    *   Other

### Ethnicity

*   **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose all the options that apply to your ethnicity:
    *   Yes
    *   No

### Country of Origin

*   **Country of Origin** (`country_of_origin`, `select_one`, not required): Select your country of origin from the options provided:
    *   USA
    *   Canada
    *   Other

### Language Spoken

*   **Language Spoken** (`language_spoken`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose all the languages you can speak:
    *   Yes
    *   No

### Education Level

*   **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, not required): Select your education level:
    *   Elementary
    *   High school
    *   College
    *   University

### Years in USA

*   **Years in USA** (`years_in_us`, `number`, not required): Enter the number of years you have been living in the USA.

### Language Spoken (Other)

*   **Language Spoken (Other)** (`language_spoken_other`, `text`, not required): If you can speak a language other than the ones listed, please enter it.

### How Long in USA

*   **How Long in USA** (`how_long_in_us`, `time`, not required): If you have lived in the USA for less than a year, choose 0. If you've lived more than one year but less than five years, choose 1-4. If you've lived more than 5 years, choose 5 or more.

### Most Important Thing

*   **Most Important Thing** (`what_do_you_think_is_most_important`, `text`, not required): Enter what you think is the most important aspect of cultural diversity and immigration.

### Most Challenging Aspects

*   **Most Challenging Aspect** (`what_do_you_believe_is_most_challenging`, `text`, not required): Enter what you believe is the most challenging aspect of cultural diversity and immigration.

### Most Important Aspects (Select)

*   **Most Important Aspects (Select)** (`what_do_you_believe_is_most_important`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose all the aspects you believe are most important:
    *   Very Important
    *   Not Important

### Agreement on Importance

*   **Agreement on Importance** (`do_you_agree`, `select_one`, not required): Select your level of agreement on the importance of cultural diversity and immigration:
    *   Strongly Agree
    *   Somewhat Agree
    *   Neither Agree nor Disagree
    *   Somewhat Disagree
    *   Strongly Disagree

### Comments

*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional comments you have.

### Additional Comments

*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional comments you have.
