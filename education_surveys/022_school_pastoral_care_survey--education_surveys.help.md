# School Pastoral Care Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and suggestions from students, parents, and staff members of a school on their experiences with the school's pastoral care services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant category (Student, Parent, or Staff) from the "Student, parent or staff" dropdown menu.
2. Fill out the form with your honest and constructive feedback.
3. Choose how you would like to submit your form (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
4. Select the relevant submission status (Option 1 or Option 2) for the form.
5. Enter your email address to receive a confirmation email with a unique reference number for your submission.
6. Enter your contact details to ensure that we can get back to you with any follow-up questions.
7. Optionally, enter the date of birth, time of completion, and phone number of the school.
8. Enter your name and student group/parent name if applicable.
9. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us and we will be happy to assist.
* You can submit feedback anonymously if you prefer.
* Please do not duplicate your submissions as this may cause confusion and affect our analysis.
