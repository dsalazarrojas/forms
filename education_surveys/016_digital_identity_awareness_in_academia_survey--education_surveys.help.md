# Digital Identity Awareness In Academia Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this survey is to understand your perceptions and attitudes towards digital identity and security within academic settings. Your responses will help inform the development of effective strategies for improving digital security awareness in academia.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read each question carefully and answer them according to your genuine thoughts and experiences.
2.  Provide detailed responses to open-ended questions (e.g., 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 10, 11, 12).
3.  For multiple-choice questions (e.g., 4, 8, 9, 12), select the most suitable option that best represents your response.
4.  You can provide an example or a story to support your response where relevant.
5.  If you're unsure or need clarification on any question, try to provide an honest response based on your current understanding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **introduction** (`1`, text, required: `false`): Please start by introducing yourself and providing a brief background on your academic or professional experience. 
    *   **What are you currently doing or have done academically or professionally?**

*   **digital_identity_meaning** (`2`, text, required: `false`): Think about how you interpret digital identity within an academic context. How does this concept relate to your personal or professional experiences? What does it mean to you?

*   **digital_security_importance** (`3`, text, required: `false`): Consider the significance of digital security in your academic or professional settings. How important is it to you, and why?

*   **digital_security_concerns** (`4`, select multiple, required: `false`): Have you ever experienced any digital security concerns while working or studying online? If so, how have you addressed these concerns?

*   **digital_identity_academic_purposes** (`5`, text, required: `false`): Think about the relationship between digital identity and academic purposes. In what ways do you think these concepts are interconnected?

*   **digital_signature** (`6`, text, required: `false`): How do you protect your digital signature in academic settings? What strategies do you use to maintain the security and authenticity of your digital identity?

*   **digital_security_concerns_online** (`7`, text, required: `false`): What common digital security concerns do you face while using online resources for academic purposes? Have you encountered any specific issues or challenges?

*   **digital_identity_critical** (`8`, radio, required: `false`): Do you believe digital identity is a critical aspect of online academic interactions? Why or why not?

*   **digital_signature_verification** (`9`, note, required: `false`): Have you ever had to verify your digital signature in a situation? If so, describe that experience.

*   **digital_security_improvement** (`10`, text, required: `false`): What do you think institutions and organizations can do to improve digital security awareness among academics?

*   **introduction_2** (`11`, text, required: `false`): What do you think are the implications of digital identity and security on academic integrity?

*   **digital_signature_concerns** (`12`, select one, required: `false`): Have you ever been unsure about the authenticity of a digital signature? If so, describe the situation.

## Tips

*   Be honest and genuine in your responses.
*   Take your time to read through each question carefully.
*   If you need clarification on any question, try to provide an honest response based on your current understanding.
*   Your responses will be kept confidential and used solely for the purpose of improving digital security awareness in academia.
