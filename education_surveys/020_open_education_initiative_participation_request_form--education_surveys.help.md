# open_education_initiative_participation_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in the open education initiative. It helps the program team understand who is interested in participating and how they can be contacted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form with your true and correct information.
2. Make sure to provide your username and email address correctly.
3. Select the correct participation method and education level that apply to you.
4. If you have selected "Other" for the participation method, please provide more information in the "participation_education_level_other" field.
5. You can leave the "comments" field blank if you do not have any additional comments or questions.
6. Fill out the "name" field with your real name.
7. Enter a valid email address in the "email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
9. If you are affiliated with an institution, you can provide it in the "institution" field.
10. If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the "question_1", "question_2", and "question_3" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **username** (text, required): Enter your username, which will be used to identify you in the initiative.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you about the initiative.
* **participation_method** (select_one, required): Select how you would like to participate in the initiative (Yes/No).
* **participation_education_level** (select_multiple, required): Select the education level(s) you are interested in (High/Low).
* **participation_education_level_other** (text, optional): If you selected "Other" for the participation method, provide more information about your education level.
* **comments** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have.
* **name** (text, required): Enter your real name.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address again for verification purposes.
* **phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **institution** (text, optional): If you are affiliated with an institution, enter it here.
* **question_1**, **question_2**, **question_3** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by one person at a time. Please do not fill out the form multiple times.
