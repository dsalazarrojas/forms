# Civic Education Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about civic education awareness among individuals. The goal is to collect data on respondents' self-assessed civic knowledge, interest in civic education, and preferred learning methods.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Answer the questions according to your personal experiences and interests.
* Select the options that best match your situation.
* Fill in the text fields with your thoughts and suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent age group**: Choose the age bracket that best describes you.
	+ Under 18
	+ 18 to 24
	+ 25 to 44
	+ 45 to 64
	+ 65 and over
* **Highest education level**: Select the highest level of education you have achieved.
	+ High school
	+ Some college
	+ Bachelor's degree
	+ Graduate degree
	+ Other
* **Self-assessed civic knowledge**: Rate your self-assessed civic knowledge from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **History of civic participation**: Describe your history of participation in civic activities such as voting, volunteering, and attending meetings.
* **Interest in civic education**: Select how interested you are in civic education opportunities.
	+ Very interested
	+ Somewhat interested
	+ Not interested
* **Preferred learning methods**: Select all the methods you prefer for learning about civic education.
	+ Workshops
	+ Online courses
	+ Printed materials
	+ Community events
* **Barriers to participation**: Describe any barriers that prevent you from participating in civic activities.
* **Recommendations to improve civic education**: Suggest any ideas you have to improve civic education.
* **Contact for follow up**: Provide an optional email or phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Submission date**: Use today's date to submit your form.
