## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's preferences for visual learning, gathering information about their learning style, preferred methods, and specific questions or concerns related to visual learning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each page with the required information. Start by providing general information on the first page, then move on to more specific questions and preferences. Please note that some fields are optional, but it is recommended to fill out as much information as possible.
