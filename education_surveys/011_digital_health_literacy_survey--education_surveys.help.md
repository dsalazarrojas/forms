# Digital Health Literacy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Health Literacy Survey is designed to evaluate your digital health literacy skills and habits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Select only one option for the sex field and one option for the health_literacy_score field.
4. Enter a date for the date of completion in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. Provide clear and concise responses in the comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any information or context related to your participation in the survey.
* **Participant Demographics** (`Participant Demographics`, `text`, optional): This field is not used in the survey; please leave it blank.
* **Age** (`Age`, `number`, optional): Enter your age in years.
* **Sex** (`Sex`, `select_one`, optional): Select your sex from the options provided:
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Other
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, optional): This field is not used in the survey; please leave it blank.
* **Health Literacy Questions** (`Health Literacy Questions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the digital health habits that you can perform:
	+ Can you access and use a computer?
	+ Can you send an email?
	+ Can you make an online payment?
	+ Can you read a blog?
	+ Can you use a smartphone?
	+ Can you use a tablet?
	+ Can you make a video call?
	+ Can you use a mobile banking app?
* **Health Literacy Skills** (`Health Literacy Skills`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your health literacy skills.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your survey experience.
* **Health Literacy Score** (`Health Literacy Score`, `select_one`, required): Select your self-assessed health literacy score:
	+ Low
	+ Moderate
	+ High
* **Date of Completion** (`Date of Completion`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you completed this survey in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Select only one option for each field that applies to you.
* Ensure that you complete all required fields.
