# University Alumni Feedback Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from university alumni to help us understand their experiences, satisfaction, and interests. Your input is valuable, and we appreciate your time to share your thoughts with us.

## How To Complete This Form
- To begin, navigate through the pages using the navigation bar at the top of the form.
- On each page, read the page title and the label next to each field to understand what information is required or optional.
- Fill out the form by providing answers to each field, following the instructions on each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### University Experiences
* **University Experiences** (Page 1)
	+ This field is for you to describe any experiences you had during your time at the university. Please provide a brief description of your experiences, including any notable events or accomplishments.

### Satisfaction Rating
* **Satisfaction Rating** (Page 2)
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the university on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".

### Areas of Interest
* **Areas of Interest** (Page 3, Select all that apply)
	+ Please select the areas of interest that are relevant to you:
		- **Alumni Relations**: How do you see the university supporting you and your network in your personal and professional development?
		- **Continuing Education**: Are you interested in continuing education opportunities, such as online courses or workshops?
		- **Career Development**: Do you require support with career development, such as career guidance or job search resources?

### Suggestions for Improvement
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (Page 4)
	+ This field is for you to provide any suggestions or ideas on how the university could improve its services and programs.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (Page 5)
	+ Please provide your email address so that we can reach out to you with any updates or follow-up on your feedback.

### Future Engagement
* **Future Engagement** (Page 6)
	+ If you would like to stay engaged with the university in the future, please let us know how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, social media).

### Comments
* **Comments** (Page 7)
	+ This field is for you to add any additional comments or thoughts about your experiences and feedback.

## Tips
- Please be as specific and detailed as possible when answering each question.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- Your feedback is confidential and will only be used to improve the university's services and programs.
