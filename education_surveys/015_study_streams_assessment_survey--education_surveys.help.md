# Study Streams Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and assessment on the study streams from users. The form aims to collect data on teaching methods, study formats, and feedback on the training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Study Streams Name** in the provided text field.
2. Select the **Teaching Method** used in the study streams by choosing one of the available options (Lecture, Online, or Offline).
3. Choose the **Study Formats** used (Online, Offline, or Hybrid).
4. For **Program Improvement**, enter the time it took for the program to improve.
5. Enter **Student Feedback 1**, **Student Feedback 2**, **Student Feedback 3**, and **Student Feedback 4** in the respective text fields.
6. Select the **School/University** where the study streams were conducted.
7. Choose the **Training Provider** (School, University, or Training).
8. Enter the **Date of completion** for the study streams.
9. Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** for the study streams.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Study Streams Name**: A text field to enter a brief description or name of the study streams.
	* This field is not required and can be left blank.
- **Teaching Method**: Select one option (Lecture, Online, or Offline) that best describes the teaching method used in the study streams.
	* This field is not required.
- **Study Formats**: Select one or multiple options (Online, Offline, or Hybrid) that best describe the study formats used in the study streams.
	* This field is not required.
- **Program Improvement**: A field to enter the time it took for the program to improve.
	* This field is not required.
- **Student Feedback 1, 2, 3, 4, 5**: Text fields to provide feedback on the training experience.
	* These fields are not required.
- **School/University**: Select one option (School or University) that best describes where the study streams were conducted.
	* This field is not required.
- **Training Provider**: Select one option (School, University, or Training) that best describes the training provider.
	* This field is not required.
- **Date of completion**: Enter the date the study streams were completed.
	* This field is not required.
- **Start Date** and **End Date**: Enter the start and end dates of the study streams.
	* These fields are not required.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and truthfully.
- If a field is not required, it's not necessary to complete it.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact the relevant stakeholders for clarification.

Note: The form fields are labeled with their respective names, labels, and types to help guide you through the process.
