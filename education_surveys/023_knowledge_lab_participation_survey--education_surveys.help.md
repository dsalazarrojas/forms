# Knowledge Lab Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather your feedback and opinions about your recent Knowledge Lab session. Your input will help us improve future sessions and provide a better experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Scroll through the form and read each question carefully.
2. Answer all or only the questions that apply to you.
3. Provide as much detail as you need to answer the questions accurately.
4. Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Attendance** (Session Attendance, text, optional): Please let us know if you attended a recent session. If yes, describe the date and time you attended, and if no, please explain why you didn't attend.
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (Rating, select_one, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session. Choose one of the following options:
	+ 1-2 (Not Satisfied)
	+ 3 (Neutral)
	+ 4-5 (Satisfied)
* **Improvement Suggestions** (Improvement Suggestions, text, optional): Share any suggestions or ideas you have to improve future sessions. Be as specific as possible.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted directly, provide your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted directly, provide your contact phone number.
* **Team Name** (Team Name, text, optional): If applicable, indicate the team you are part of.

## Tips

* If you have any feedback, suggestions, or questions, we're here to help!
* Please keep in mind that your input is greatly appreciated and will contribute to the improvement of our Knowledge Lab sessions.
