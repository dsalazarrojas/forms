# Academic Status Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Status Survey is designed to gather information about your academic experience at our institution. This form is used to gather feedback on various aspects of your program, including course quality, faculty support, and campus resources. Your input will help us understand your needs and concerns and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. For each field, select the most suitable option or provide a clear and concise answer.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student ID** (`Student ID`, text, required): Please enter your official student identification number.
* **Academic Program** (`Academic Program`, text, required): Select your current academic program or major.
* **Academic Year** (`Academic Year`, select_one, required): Select the year you are currently in (e.g., Freshman, Sophomore, etc.).
* **Enrollment Status** (`Enrollment Status`, select_one, required): Select your current enrollment status (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, etc.).
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (`Overall Satisfaction Rating`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with your academic experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Course Quality Rating** (`Course Quality Rating`, number, required): Rate the quality of courses in your program on a scale of 1-5.
* **Faculty Support Rating** (`Faculty Support Rating`, number, required): Rate the support provided by faculty members on a scale of 1-5.
* **Academic Advising Rating** (`Academic Advising Rating`, number, required): Rate the quality of academic advising on a scale of 1-5.
* **Campus Resources Rating** (`Campus Resources Rating`, number, required): Rate the availability and quality of campus resources on a scale of 1-5.
* **Career Services Rating** (`Career Services Rating`, number, required): Rate the effectiveness of career services on a scale of 1-5.
* **Academic Progress Satisfaction** (`Academic Progress Satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select your level of satisfaction with your academic progress (e.g., Very Satisfied, Satisfied, etc.).
* **Program Recommendation Likelihood** (`Program Recommendation Likelihood`, number, required): Rate your likelihood to recommend your program to others on a scale of 1-5.
* **Academic Challenges Faced** (`Academic Challenges Faced`, select_multiple, required): Select all the academic challenges you face (e.g., Course difficulty, Time management, etc.).
* **Support Services Utilization** (`Support Services Utilization`, select_one, required): Select whether you have utilized academic support services (e.g., Yes, regularly, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about your academic experience.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your instructor or academic advisor for guidance.
* Your input is invaluable in helping us improve our academic programs and services. Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
