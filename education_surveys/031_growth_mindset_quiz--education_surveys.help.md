# Growth Mindset Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Growth Mindset Quiz is a 25-question survey designed to measure an individual's growth mindset, self-awareness, and self-regulation skills. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction and familiarize yourself with the growth mindset concept.
2. Answer each question honestly, selecting one of the five options provided for each question.
3. Review your answers to ensure you have selected the correct level of self-awareness, self-regulation, and personal growth for each question.
4. Submit your completed form.
