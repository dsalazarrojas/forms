# Deadlock Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your understanding and concerns regarding deadlocks. It consists of eight pages, each with questions that will help us better comprehend your knowledge, experience, and concerns about deadlocks.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, select the category that best describes you (Student, Professional, or Researcher).
- Answer each question on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
- Save your progress and come back to the form later if you need to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What do you know about deadlocks?** (`intro_page`, `text`, not required): Please provide a brief description of what you know about deadlocks.
* **Which category best describes you?** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the three options: Student, Professional, or Researcher.
* **How much experience do you have with deadlocks?** (`experience_level`, `number`, not required): Provide a numerical value to indicate your level of experience with deadlocks. For example, 0-1 year, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, etc.
* **What level of knowledge do you have about deadlocks?** (`knowledge_level`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the three options: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **What symptom or symptoms are you experiencing?** (`deadlock_symptoms`, `text`, not required): Describe any symptoms or issues you are experiencing related to deadlocks.
* **What causes are you concerned about?** (`cause_concerns`, `text`, not required): List any causes or factors that concern you regarding deadlocks.
* **What effects are you concerned about?** (`effects_concerns`, `text`, not required): Describe any effects or consequences you are concerned about due to deadlocks.
* **What do you do to prevent deadlocks?** (`prevention_strategies`, `text`, not required): Share any strategies or actions you take to prevent deadlocks.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when answering each question.
- If you are unsure about a question, feel free to ask for clarification.
- You can fill out this form at your own pace and save your progress if needed.
