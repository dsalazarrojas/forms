# Teacher Autonomy And Creativity Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Teacher Autonomy And Creativity Survey" is designed to gather insights on teacher satisfaction, influence, and autonomy in educational settings. This survey aims to understand how teachers perceive their autonomy, creativity, and decision-making powers within their schools, as well as their satisfaction with various aspects of their work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your abilities.
2. Select the options that best match your experiences and perceptions.
3. For fields with select_one, select multiple, or number types, choose one or multiple options that fit your situation.
4. For fields with text or email types, provide a brief description or address as requested.
