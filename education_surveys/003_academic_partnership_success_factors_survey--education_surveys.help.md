# Academic Partnership Success Factors Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Partnership Success Factors Survey is designed to assess the effectiveness of academic-industry partnerships in achieving their goals and objectives. This form aims to identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights for strengthening partnerships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your organization type from the options provided under "Which type of organization do you represent".
2. Enter your full name in the "Your full name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Your email address" field.
4. Indicate how long the partnership has been active under "How long has the partnership been active".
5. Select all applicable activities under "What type of partnership activities are involved (select all that apply)".
6. Assess the effectiveness of communication between partner organizations under "How effective is the communication between partner organizations".
7. Evaluate the alignment of goals and objectives of both partners under "How well are the goals and objectives of both partners aligned".
8. Indicate your level of satisfaction with the sharing of resources under "How satisfied are you with the sharing of resources (funding, facilities, personnel) in this partnership".
9. Rate the extent to which desired outcomes of the partnership have been achieved under "To what extent have the desired outcomes of the partnership been achieved".
10. Select up to three important success factors for this partnership under "What do you consider the most important success factors for this partnership (select up to 3)".
11. Identify challenges faced in the partnership under "What challenges have you encountered in this partnership (select all that apply)".
12. Rate your overall satisfaction with the partnership under "Overall, how satisfied are you with this academic partnership".
13. Indicate your likelihood to continue this partnership under "How likely is your organization to continue this partnership".
14. Provide suggestions for improvement under "What suggestions do you have for improving this partnership".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which type of organization do you represent**: Select the type of organization you represent from the provided options (University or College, Industry Partner, Government Agency, Non-Profit Organization, Research Institute).
* **Your full name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Your email address**: Provide your email address to help us contact you for any further questions or clarification.
* **How long has the partnership been active**: Choose the duration of the partnership (Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 3-5 years, More than 5 years).
* **What type of partnership activities are involved (select all that apply)**: Select all activities that apply from the options (Joint research projects, Student internships or placements, Curriculum development, Technology transfer, Faculty exchange, Sponsored programs or grants).
* **How effective is the communication between partner organizations**: Rate the effectiveness of communication (Very ineffective, Somewhat ineffective, Neutral, Somewhat effective, Very effective).
* **How well are the goals and objectives of both partners aligned**: Rate the alignment of goals and objectives (Poorly aligned, Somewhat aligned, Moderately aligned, Well aligned, Perfectly aligned).
* **How satisfied are you with the sharing of resources (funding, facilities, personnel) in this partnership**: Rate your satisfaction level (Very dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, Very satisfied).
* **To what extent have the desired outcomes of the partnership been achieved**: Rate the extent to which desired outcomes have been achieved (Not at all, Minimally, Partially, Mostly, Fully).
* **What do you consider the most important success factors for this partnership (select up to 3)**: Select up to three success factors that are crucial for the partnership (Strong leadership commitment, Clear governance structure, Mutual trust and respect, Adequate funding, Shared vision and goals, Flexibility and adaptability).
* **What challenges have you encountered in this partnership (select all that apply)**: Select all challenges faced in the partnership (Misaligned expectations, Insufficient funding, Communication gaps, Bureaucratic delays, Cultural differences, Staff turnover, Intellectual property disputes).
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with this academic partnership**: Rate your overall satisfaction (Very dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, Very satisfied).
* **How likely is your organization to continue this partnership**: Indicate your likelihood to continue the partnership (Very unlikely, Unlikely, Neutral, Likely, Very likely).
* **What suggestions do you have for improving this partnership**: Provide specific suggestions for improving the partnership, if any.
