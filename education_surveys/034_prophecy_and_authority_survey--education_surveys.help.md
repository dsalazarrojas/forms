# Prophecy and Authority Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your beliefs, experiences, and views on prophecy and spiritual authority. The data collected will help us better understand your perspectives and inform our future initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. You can complete the form in multiple pages, and each page will have a set of questions related to a specific topic.
3. If you need help or have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Prophecy Beliefs (**prophecy_beliefs**)
* **Prophecy Beliefs** (`prophecy_beliefs`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your current beliefs about prophecy. Please provide a concise answer that best represents your views on the topic.

### Prophecy Experience (**prophecy_experience**)
* **Prophecy Experience** (`prophecy_experience`, text, optional): This field is for you to share your personal experiences related to prophecy. Please write a clear and concise description of your experiences.

### Authority Views (**authority_views**)
* **Authority Views** (`authority_views`, text, optional): This field is for you to express your views on authority. Please provide a clear and concise answer that best represents your perspective.

### Spiritual Practices (**spiritual_practices**)
* **Spiritual Practices** (`spiritual_practices`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your spiritual practices. Please provide a concise answer that best represents your current practices.

### Spiritual Authority (**spiritual_authority**)
* **Spiritual Authority** (`spiritual_authority`, select_one, optional): This field is for you to select the spiritual authority that resonates with you the most. Please choose one of the options provided.

### Faith Leader (**faith_leader**)
* **Faith Leader** (`faith_leader`, text, optional): This field is for you to mention a faith leader who influences your spiritual life. Please provide the name and title of the leader.

### Prophecy Scope (**prophecy_scope**)
* **Prophecy Scope** (`prophecy_scope`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for you to select the areas where you believe prophecy is applicable. Please choose one or more of the options provided.

### Survey Participants (**survey_participants**)
* **Survey Participants** (`survey_participants`, text, optional): This field is for you to share the number of people you have shared your prophecy and authority views with. Please provide a clear and concise answer.

### Personal Experiences (**personal_experiences**)
* **Personal Experiences** (`personal_experiences`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe any personal experiences related to prophecy. Please write a clear and concise description of your experiences.

### Spiritual Experiences (**spiritual_experiences**)
* **Spiritual Experiences** (`spiritual_experiences`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe any spiritual experiences related to prophecy. Please provide a clear and concise description of your experiences.

# Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any question, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Your responses will help us better understand your perspectives and inform our future initiatives.
