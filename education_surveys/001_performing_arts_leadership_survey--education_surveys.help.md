# performing-arts-leadership-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals in the performing arts leadership field, such as directors, producers, and administrators. It will be used to gather data for research and analysis purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each question and its relevance to your role and experience in the performing arts leadership field.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, Email, and any other required information accurately and completely.
3. Ensure that your Email is a valid and active email address, as it will be used for follow-up communication and surveys.
4. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. First Name
* **First Name** (Text, **Required**: No): Please enter your first name.
### 2. Last Name
* **Last Name** (Text, **Required**: No): Please enter your last name.
### 3. Email
* **Email** (Email, **Required**: No): Please enter a valid and active email address.
### 4. Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (Text, **Required**: No): If you have a phone number, please enter it. If not, you can leave this field blank.
### 5. Department
* **Department** (Text, **Required**: No): If applicable, enter the name of your department (e.g., Theater, Music, Dance).
### 6. Position
* **Position** (Text, **Required**: No): Please enter your role or job title within your department.
### 7. Institution
* **Institution** (Text, **Required**: No): If you are affiliated with an institution (e.g., university, school, company), please enter its name.
### 8. Degree
* **Degree** (Text, **Required**: No): If applicable, enter any degree(s) you have earned related to the performing arts.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [your contact email or contact information].
