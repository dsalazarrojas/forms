# Immigration Continuing Education Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on a continuing education session on immigration, ensuring that the content is relevant and meets the needs of attendees. This form helps to evaluate the session's goals, content, and format, as well as provide information about the speaker and attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the feedback questions carefully and answer each field according to the guidelines provided.
2. Select the relevant options for each question using the dropdown menus or radio buttons.
3. Enter any additional comments or observations in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **session_topics** (`session_topics`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the session topics covered.
* **session_outcomes** (`session_outcomes`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the session outcomes that apply:
	+ Session Goals and Objectives
	+ Session Content
	+ Session Materials
	+ Session Format
	+ Session Evaluation
* **speaker** (`speaker`, `select_one`, required): Select the title of the speaker:
	+ Speaker Name
	+ Presenter Name
	+ Instructor Name
	+ Trainer Name
	+ Coach Name
* **session_outcome** (`session_outcome`, `number`, required): Enter the number of session outcomes achieved.
* **training_providers** (`training_providers`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all relevant training providers:
	+ Provider Name 1
	+ Provider Name 2
	+ Provider Name 3
	+ Provider Name 4
	+ Provider Name 5
* **future_topic** (`future_topic`, `text`, required): Enter a future topic or session idea.
* **comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or observations.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (optional but recommended for follow-up contact).
* **session_evaluation** (`session_evaluation`, `select_one`, required): Select the overall evaluation of the session:
	+ Session Evaluation
	+ Other
* **date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the session.
* **submitted_by** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **submitted_by_name** (`submitted_by_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name (first and last name).
* **submitted_by_title** (`submitted_by_title`, `text`, required): Enter your title or position.
* **submitted_by_department** (`submitted_by_department`, `text`, required): Enter your department or office.
* **submitted_by_job_title** (`submitted_by_job_title`, `select_one`, required): Select your job title:
	+ Job Title 1
	+ Job Title 2
	+ Job Title 3
	+ Job Title 4
	+ Job Title 5
* **submitted_by_email** (`submitted_by_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **submitted_by_phone** (`submitted_by_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (optional but recommended for follow-up contact).
* **submitted_by_work** (`submitted_by_work`, `text`, required): Enter the place of work.
* **submitted_by_employer** (`submitted_by_employer`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your employer.
* **submitted_by_position** (`submitted_by_position`, `text`, required): Enter your position or role.
