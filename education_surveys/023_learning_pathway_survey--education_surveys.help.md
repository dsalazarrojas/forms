# Learning Pathway Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Learning Pathway Survey is designed to help us understand your current educational level, interests, and learning preferences. This survey will help us create personalized learning pathways for you, tailored to your needs and goals. Please answer the questions honestly, and we will use your input to create a more effective learning plan for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section as instructed. You can select one or multiple options for each question, depending on your answers. Don't worry, the required fields are marked as such. If you need help, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. What is your current educational level?
* **What is your current educational level?** (`form_title`, select_one): Please select your current educational level, which is the level of education you have completed or currently pursuing. You can choose from Elementary, High School, College, University, Post-Graduate, or Other (if you have any other level of education).

### 2. What subject(s) are you interested in learning more about?
* **What subject(s) are you interested in learning more about?** (`subject`, select_multiple): Select all the subjects you are interested in learning more about. You can choose from Math, Science, Language, History, Literature, Art, Music, Technology, or Other (please specify).

### 3. What are your goals for learning this subject(s)?
* **What are your goals for learning this subject(s)?** (`goals`, text): Write down your goals for learning the subject(s) you selected. This will help us understand what you hope to achieve through the learning process.

### 4. What is your current level of knowledge in this subject(s)?
* **What is your current level of knowledge in this subject(s)?** (`level_of_knowledge`, number): Estimate your current level of knowledge in the subject(s) you selected, on a scale from 0 to 100.

### 5. What motivates you to learn more about this subject(s)?
* **What motivates you to learn more about this subject(s)?** (`motivation`, text): Write down what motivates you to learn more about the subject(s) you selected. This will help us understand what drives your interest in learning.

### 6. What do you think is the most challenging part of the learning process?
* **What do you think is the most challenging part of the learning process?** (`challenging_part`, text): Share your thoughts on what you think is the most challenging part of the learning process for you.

### 7. How do you typically spend your free time?
* **How do you typically spend your free time?** (`free_time`, select_multiple): Select all the activities you typically do with your free time. You can choose from Reading, Watching videos, Exercising, Socializing, Playing games, or Other (please specify).

### 8. Do you have any prior experience with [subject]?
* **Do you have any prior experience with [subject]?** (`prior_experience`, checkbox): Check the box if you have any prior experience with the subject(s) you selected.

### 9. What is your learning style (e.g., reading, hands-on, lectures)?
* **What is your learning style (e.g., reading, hands-on, lectures)?** (`learning_style`, select_multiple): Select all the learning styles that suit you. You can choose from Reading, Hands-on, Lectures, Online courses, Workshops, or Other (please specify).

### 10. What do you think is the most important aspect of the learning process?
* **What do you think is the most important aspect of the learning process?** (`important_aspect`, text): Share your thoughts on what you think is the most important aspect of the learning process for you.

### 11. How do you prefer to learn new information (e.g., through books, videos, online courses, workshops)?
* **How do you prefer to learn new information (e.g., through books, videos, online courses, workshops)?** (`learning_method`, select_multiple): Select all the methods you prefer to learn new information. You can choose from Books, Videos, Online courses, Workshops, Seminars, or Other (please specify).

### 12. Is there anything that might hinder your learning (e.g., time constraints, motivation, resources)?
* **Is there anything that might hinder your learning (e.g., time constraints, motivation, resources)?** (`hinderances`, select_multiple): Select all the challenges that might hinder your learning. You can choose from Time constraints, Motivation, Resources, or Other (please specify).

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask.
* The more detailed and honest your answers, the more accurate your learning pathway will be.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey, and we will use your input to create a personalized learning plan for you.
