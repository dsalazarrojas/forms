# Language Roots And Structures Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Language Roots and Structures Survey is a form designed to collect information about language roots and structures. This form is used to gather data on various language features, such as language family tree, linguistic characteristics, language roots, and language structures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant answers to each question based on your knowledge of the language you are surveying. If you are unsure, try to make an educated guess.
2. Fill in the "languages" field with a specific language code or name.
3. For the "prophets" field, select "Yes" if you are aware of a prophet or leader associated with the language, or "No" if you are not.
4. Use the "additional_info" field to provide any additional information you think is relevant.
5. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the fields.
