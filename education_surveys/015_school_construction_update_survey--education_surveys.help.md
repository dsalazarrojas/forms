# School Construction Update Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides a step-by-step explanation of how to complete the School Construction Update Survey form. The form is designed to collect information about the status and details of school construction projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the status of your project from the options provided in the "What is the status of your school building project?" field.
2. Enter the project location in the "Project location (city, state, or region)" field, if required.
3. Enter the name of your school in the "School name" field, if required.
4. Enter the name of the building being constructed or updated in the "Building name" field, if required.
5. If applicable, select the start and end dates of the project in the "Project start date" and "Project end date" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the budget for the project, the school's budget, and any other relevant information related to the project's budget in the "Budget", "School budget", and optional fields.
7. Enter the name of the project manager in the "Project manager" field, if required.
8. Enter your contact email and phone number in the "Contact email" and "Contact phone" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the status of your school building project?** (`project_status`, select_one): This field asks for the current status of your school building project. Select one of the provided options to indicate whether the project is active, inactive, or if it's a different status.
* **Project location (city, state, or region)** (`project_location`, text): This field is used to specify the location of the project, which can be a city, state, or region. Enter the location to help us better understand the project's context.
* **School name** (`school_name`, text): This field is used to identify your school. If you're not sure what to enter, try to use the school's official name or any other name commonly used to refer to it.
* **Building name** (`building_name`, text): This field is used to specify the name of the building or facility being constructed or updated. Try to be as accurate as possible.
* **Project start date** (`project_start_date`, date): If you know the start date of the project, enter it here. This information can help us track project timelines.
* **Project end date** (`project_end_date`, date): If you know the end date of the project, enter it here. This information can help us track project timelines.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number): Enter the project's budget in the currency of your choice. If you need help with this field, refer to the next one for the school's budget.
* **School budget** (`school_budget`, number): Enter the school's budget related to the project. For example, if the project's budget is $1 million, you may want to enter the school's total annual budget here.
* **Project manager** (`project_manager`, text): This field is used to identify the person in charge of the project. If you're not sure who to enter, try to find the person's official email or phone number.
* **Contact email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any questions or updates related to your project.
* **Contact phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any questions or updates related to your project.
