# AI Augmented Assessment Perception Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback from respondents who have taken or observed the AI-augmented assessment. Your input will help us improve the assessment process and the AI system used.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter your full name in the "Full name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email address" field. Please note that your responses will be anonymized if you give consent.
* Select your primary role in the assessment process from the dropdown menu in the "Primary role in the assessment process" field.
* Enter the date you took or observed the AI-augmented assessment in the "Date of assessment experience" field.
* Enter the approximate duration of the assessment in minutes in the "Approximate assessment duration (minutes)" field.
* Select your overall satisfaction with the AI-augmented assessment from the dropdown menu in the "How satisfied are you with the AI augmentation overall?" field.
* Enter your overall rating of the AI-augmented assessment on a scale of 1 to 10 in the "Overall rating (1-10)" field.
* Select all the AI features that were applied during the assessment from the dropdown menu in the "Which AI features were applied in the assessment? (select all that apply)" field.
* Enter your perceived accuracy of the AI outputs on a scale of 0.0 to 1.0 in the "Perceived accuracy of AI outputs" field.
* Select your level of trust in the AI system to fairly evaluate responses from the dropdown menu in the "Trust in AI to fairly evaluate responses" field.
* Select all the concerns you have about the fairness or privacy of the AI system from the dropdown menu in the "What concerns (if any) do you have about fairness or privacy?" field. 
* Select all the technical issues you encountered during the assessment from the dropdown menu in the "Technical issues encountered (select all that apply)" field.
* Select whether you would be willing to use the AI-augmented assessment in the future from the dropdown menu in the "Would you be willing to use AI-augmented assessments in the future?" field.
* Enter any suggested improvements for the AI-augmented assessment in the "What would you improve or change about the AI-augmented assessment?" field. 
* Select whether you consent to the anonymized use of your responses in the "Consent to anonymized use of responses for product improvement" field.
* Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience in the "Additional comments or notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name (Full name, text, required):** Enter your full name, including your first and last name.
* **Email address (Email address, email, required):** Enter your email address. Please note that your responses will be anonymized if you give consent.
* **Primary role in the assessment process (select_one, required):** Select your primary role in the assessment process:
	+ Student
	+ Instructor
	+ HR Manager
	+ Assessment Designer
	+ Administrator
	+ Other
* **Date of assessment experience (Date, required):** Enter the date you took or observed the AI-augmented assessment.
* **Approximate assessment duration (minutes) (integer, optional):** Enter the approximate duration of the assessment in minutes.
* **How satisfied are you with the AI augmentation overall? (select_one, required):** Select your overall satisfaction with the AI-augmented assessment:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Overall rating (1-10) (integer, required):** Enter your overall rating of the AI-augmented assessment on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Which AI features were applied in the assessment? (select multiple, optional):** Select all the AI features that were applied during the assessment:
	+ Automated scoring
	+ Proctoring/monitoring
	+ Feedback generation
	+ Question generation
	+ Adaptive sequencing
	+ Cheating detection
	+ Other
* **Perceived accuracy of AI outputs (decimal, required):** Enter your perceived accuracy of the AI outputs on a scale of 0.0 to 1.0.
* **Trust in AI to fairly evaluate responses (select_one, required):** Select your level of trust in the AI system to fairly evaluate responses:
	+ High
	+ Moderate
	+ Low
* **What concerns (if any) do you have about fairness or privacy? (select multiple, optional):** Select all the concerns you have about the fairness or privacy of the AI system:
	+ Bias in scoring
	+ Privacy/data handling
	+ Lack of transparency
	+ False positives in cheating detection
	+ Accessibility concerns
	+ None
* **Technical issues encountered (select multiple, optional):** Select all the technical issues you encountered during the assessment:
	+ System crashes
	+ Slow loading or timeouts
	+ Incorrect or confusing feedback
	+ Login/authentication problems
	+ Browser compatibility issues
	+ None
* **Would you be willing to use AI-augmented assessments in the future? (select_one, required):** Select whether you would be willing to use the AI-augmented assessment in the future:
	+ Definitely
	+ Maybe with oversight
	+ False
* **What would you improve or change about the AI-augmented assessment? (text, optional):** Enter any suggested improvements or changes you would make to the AI-augmented assessment.
* **Consent to anonymized use of responses for product improvement (select_one, required):** Select whether you consent to the anonymized use of your responses:
	+ I consent to anonymized use of my responses
	+ I do not consent
* **Additional comments or notes (text, optional):** Enter any additional comments or notes about your experience.
