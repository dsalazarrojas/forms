# Academic Integrity Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on your experiences and perceptions regarding academic integrity at the university. Your responses will help the administration and support services better understand the needs and concerns of the academic community, enabling them to improve the overall integrity support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the option that best describes your role within the university.
2. Choose how familiar you are with the academic integrity policy.
3. Indicate whether you have received training on academic integrity within the past year.
4. Report any incidents of misconduct you have observed or witnessed.
5. Rate your confidence in reporting concerns about academic integrity.
6. Select any barriers you face when reporting misconduct (this can be more than one option).
7. Share your ideas on how to improve the university's academic integrity support services (optional).
8. Indicate your preference for completing anonymous surveys (optional).
9. Provide any other comments or feedback on academic integrity (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Role** (`role`, **select_one**): Select the option that best describes your role within the university. This will help us understand your perspective and experience with academic integrity.
* **Awareness of Academic Integrity Policy** (`awareness_of_policy`, **select_one**): Rate how familiar you are with the academic integrity policy. This will help us assess your knowledge and understanding of the policy.
* **Have You Received Integrity Training** (`training_received`, **select_one**): Indicate whether you have received training on academic integrity within the past year. This will help us understand the effectiveness of our training programs.
* **Observed Incidents of Misconduct** (`observed_incidents`, **select_one**): Report any incidents of misconduct you have observed or witnessed. This will help us identify potential issues and areas for improvement.
* **Confidence in Reporting Mechanisms** (`reporting_confidence`, **select_one**): Rate your confidence in reporting concerns about academic integrity. This will help us understand your comfort level with reporting mechanisms.
* **Barriers to Reporting Misconduct** (`barriers_to_reporting`, **select_multiple**): Select any barriers you face when reporting misconduct. This can include multiple options, as some individuals may face multiple challenges.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, **text**): Share your ideas on how to improve the university's academic integrity support services. This is an opportunity to provide constructive feedback.
* **Preference for Anonymous Surveys** (`anonymity_preference`, **select_one**): Indicate your preference for completing anonymous surveys. This will help us understand your comfort level with anonymous surveys.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, **text**): Provide any other comments or feedback on academic integrity. This is an open-ended field for any additional thoughts or suggestions.
