# Environmental Lifestyle Choices Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Lifestyle Choices Survey is designed to gather information about your environmental lifestyle choices and attitudes. Your responses will help us better understand how individuals like you live and behave in relation to the environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Review each page carefully before proceeding.
2. For each page, read the label and hint provided to understand what the questions are asking.
3. Select your preferred option from the provided list for each question.
4. Ensure you answer each question honestly, as your responses are confidential.
5. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.
