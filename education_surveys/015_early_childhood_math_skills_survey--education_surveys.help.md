# early_childhood_math_skills_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **early_childhood_math_skills_survey** form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's math skills, including their proficiency levels in various areas such as addition, subtraction, and counting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, providing accurate and honest information about your child's math skills.
2. Select the correct options for each select field, such as grade level, child's grade, math proficiency, and more.
3. Enter numerical values for fields that require numbers, such as addition score and subtraction score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, **required**): Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Age** (`parent_age`, number, **required**): Enter your age as the parent or guardian.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, **required**): Enter your child's name.
* **Child Age** (`child_age`, number, **required**): Enter your child's age.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, **required**): Select your child's grade level.
* **Child's Grade Level** (`child_grade`, select_one, **required**): Select the grade level that best describes your child.
* **Math Proficiency** (`math_proficiency`, select_multiple, **required**): Select your child's math proficiency level, multiple options can be selected.
* **Reading Proficiency** (`reading_proficiency`, select_multiple, **required**): Select your child's reading proficiency level, multiple options can be selected.
* **Writing Proficiency** (`writing_proficiency`, select_multiple, **required**): Select your child's writing proficiency level, multiple options can be selected.
* **Number Line** (`number_line`, note, **not required**): Enter any additional comments or observations about your child's number line skills.
* **Number Line Brief** (`number_line_brief`, note, **not required**): Enter any additional comments or observations about your child's number line skills.
* **Number Line Score** (`number_line_score`, number, **not required**): Enter a numerical value that describes your child's number line skills.
* **Addition/Subtraction** (`addition_subtrah`, select_multiple, **required**): Select the areas where your child struggles or excels in addition and subtraction.
* **Addition Score** (`addition_score`, number, **required**): Enter a numerical value that describes your child's addition skills.
* **Subtraction** (`subtraction_subtrah`, select_multiple, **required**): Select the areas where your child struggles or excels in subtraction.
* **Subtraction Score** (`subtraction_score`, number, **required**): Enter a numerical value that describes your child's subtraction skills.
* **Counting Up** (`counting_up`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select the areas where your child struggles or excels in counting up.
* **Counting Score** (`counting_score`, number, **required**): Enter a numerical value that describes your child's counting skills.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of your child's school.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of your child's teacher.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, **not required**): Enter the email address of your child's teacher (optional).
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, **not required**): Enter the phone number of your child's teacher (optional).
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, **not required**): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, **not required**): Enter your phone number (optional).

## Tips
- Please ensure that you answer all required fields.
- Select multiple options for select_multiple fields where applicable.
- Enter numerical values for fields that require numbers.
- If you have any additional comments or observations, use the note fields to provide them.
