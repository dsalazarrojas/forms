# surat_history_and_culture_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Surat History and Culture Survey" is a form designed to collect information about users' backgrounds, interests, and experiences related to Surat (a town or city) history and culture. This form will help researchers understand the community's engagement with local programs, educational background, and research interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out each section carefully**, making sure to provide accurate and complete information.
2. **Answer questions truthfully**. If you're unsure about a question, you can always reach out to the researcher or administrator.
3. **Be thorough**. Make sure you cover all the necessary information in each section.
4. **Save your progress** before submitting the form, in case you need to go back to a previous section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: This section collects general information about you, such as your name and contact details.
* **Demographic Data**: This section gathers demographic information about you, such as your education level, age, or profession. This will help researchers understand the diversity of the community.
* **Heritage Items**: In this section, you'll answer questions about your cultural engagement. Please select the items that apply to you from the given options. For example, if you're from Surat and have attended a local festival, select 'Yes'.
* **Cultural Engagement**: Similar to the previous section, you'll answer questions about your engagement with local cultural activities. Select 'Yes' if you've participated in any of the mentioned activities.
* **Research Interest**: This section allows you to share your research interests, if any. Please be specific about what topics you're interested in or what research projects you're currently involved with.
* **Educational Background**: If you've had formal education or training in a particular subject, please describe it briefly.
* **Local Programming**: If you've participated in any local programs or events, please describe them in this section.
* **Community Programming**: Similarly, if you've engaged with any community programs or activities, describe them here.
* **Research Education**: If you're currently involved in any research projects, please provide a brief description of them.

# Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the researcher or administrator.
* The form is designed to be completed once by each user, so you don't need to worry about saving your progress if you're not planning to come back to the form.
