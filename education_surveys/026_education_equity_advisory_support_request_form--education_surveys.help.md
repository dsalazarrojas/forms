# Education Equity Advisory Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Equity Advisory Support Request Form is designed to help school administrators, students, or parents request support or guidance from the education equity advisory team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your requested information.
2. Choose the correct support type and priority level for your request.
3. Provide your email address and phone number for the advisory team to contact you.
4. Enter the date and time you would like to be contacted.
5. Add any additional comments or notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name (1)**: Enter the name of the school that you are requesting support from.
* **School District (2)**: Enter the name of the school district that you are requesting support from.
* **Program Name (3)**: Enter the name of the specific program that you are requesting support for.
* **Support Type (4)**: Choose the type of support you are seeking from the equity advisory team. Options include:
	+ Equity Guidance
	+ Coaching
	+ Program Support
* **Priority Level (5)**: Choose the priority level of your request from Low, Medium, or High.
* **Email Address (6)**: Enter your email address so that the advisory team can contact you for further information or to discuss your request.
* **Phone Number (7)**: Enter your phone number so that the advisory team can contact you if needed.
* **Date Request (8)**: Enter the date you would like to be contacted or when you would like to request support.
* **Time Request (9)**: Enter the time you would like to be contacted.
* **Comment Request (10)**: Add any additional comments or notes to help the advisory team understand your request.
* **Submitted By (11)**: Choose who submitted the request from Administrator, Student, or Parent.
* **Comments (12)**: Add any additional comments or notes to help the advisory team understand your request.
* **Assigned Tool (13)**: Enter any specific tool or resource that you have used or would like to use to support your request.

**Tips**

* Please make sure to submit your request accurately and completely to help the advisory team better understand your needs.
* If you have already submitted a similar request, please do not resubmit it. Instead, contact the advisory team to discuss your existing request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the advisory team for assistance.
