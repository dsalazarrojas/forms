# Student Post Exam Feedback Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from students after completing an exam.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the **Student Name** field.
2. Enter your student ID in the **Student Id** field.
3. Select the subject of the exam you completed.
4. Enter the **Instructor Name** if applicable.
5. If you have any additional comments, enter them in the **Comments** field.
6. Select the date and time you completed the exam in the **Date Completed** field.
7. If you have any feedback on the quality of the exam, select a rating from the **Rating** field.
8. If you need any follow-up action, choose one or more options from the **Follow Up Action** field.
9. Enter the date and time for any follow-up action in the **Follow Up Date** field.
10. If you have any additional details about the follow-up action, enter them in the **Follow Up Details** field.
11. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
12. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Student Id**: Enter your student ID number.
* **Subject**: Select the subject of the exam you completed.
* **Instructor Name**: If you had an instructor for the exam, enter their name.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your experience with the exam.
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date and time you completed the exam.
* **Rating**: Select one of the four ratings options to indicate your feedback on the exam.
* **Follow Up Action**: If you need any follow-up action, select one or more of the options provided.
* **Follow Up Date**: Enter the date and time for any follow-up action.
* **Follow Up Details**: Enter any additional details about the follow-up action.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any issues or concerns with the exam, use the **Comments** field to provide feedback.
* If you need any follow-up action, select one or more options from the **Follow Up Action** field.
* Enter accurate dates and times for the **Date Completed**, **Follow Up Date**, and **Follow Up Time** fields.
