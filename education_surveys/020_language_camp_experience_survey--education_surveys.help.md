# Language Camp Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and experiences from participants after attending a language camp. Your input will help us improve and refine our service to better serve you and future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer all fields that apply to your experience. You can skip fields that don't apply.
- Be as detailed and honest as possible in your feedback comments.
- For select fields, choose the option that best describes your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Language Camp Experience** (`language_camp_survey_form_1`, text, optional): Briefly describe your overall experience at the language camp. What did you like or dislike about it?
- **Session Details** (`session_details`, select_one, optional): Which session type did you attend? Choose from:
  • Morning session
  • Afternoon session
  • Full day session
- **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions about your experience.
- **Session Rating** (`session_rating`, select_one, optional): How would you rate your session? Choose from:
  • Highly satisfying
  • Somewhat satisfying
  • Not at all satisfying
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Please provide your name for reference purposes.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get back to you with any questions or updates.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Provide your phone number so we can contact you for any follow-up questions or surveys.
- **Language Camp Rating** (`language_camp_rating`, select_one, optional): How would you rate your overall language camp experience? Choose from:
  • Highly satisfying
  • Somewhat satisfying
  • Neither satisfying nor unsatisfying

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all fields that apply to your experience.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any concerns or questions, contact us after filling out the form.
