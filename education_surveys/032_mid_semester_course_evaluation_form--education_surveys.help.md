# Mid Semester Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mid Semester Course Evaluation Form is a tool designed to collect feedback from students about their experiences and perceptions of the course, allowing instructors to make data-driven decisions to improve the course content, teaching methods, and overall learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please select the relevant semester for the course you are evaluating.
2.  Choose your response to the "Learning Experience" question, and provide a brief explanation.
3.  If you have any suggestions for course improvement, please provide them in the "Course Improvement" section.
4.  You can provide additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments" section.
5.  Please select your level of engagement for each of the questions, and provide explanations as needed.
6.  Enter your email and phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.
7.  Please fill out a separate instance of the form for each semester you want to provide feedback about.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Feedback**: Provide a brief description of your experience in the course.
*   *   *Learning Experience*: This question assesses how enjoyable or challenging you found the course.
   *   *Course Improvement*: Share your thoughts on what the instructor could do to improve the course content, teaching methods, or overall learning environment.
   *   *Additional Comments*: Provide any additional thoughts or suggestions you'd like to share with the instructor.
*   *Instructor Feedback*: Please provide feedback or comments for the instructor.
*   *Student Engagement*: Select your level of engagement with the course material and explain your reasoning.
*   *Email*: Enter your email address, and you will be contacted for further discussion.
*   *Phone*: Enter your phone number, and you will be contacted for further discussion.
*   *Student Feedback 2*: This is another chance to share your thoughts about the course.
*   *Instructor Feedback 2*: Provide feedback or comments for the instructor.
*   *Student Engagement 2*: Select your level of engagement with the course material and explain your reasoning.
*   *Course Improvement 2*: Share your thoughts on what the instructor could do to improve the course content, teaching methods, or overall learning environment.
*   *Additional Comments 2*: Provide any additional thoughts or suggestions you'd like to share with the instructor.

## Tips

*   Please answer these questions as honestly and accurately as possible.
*   Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your feedback.
*   If you have any suggestions or ideas for the instructor, share them in the "Course Improvement" or "Additional Comments" sections.
*   You can fill out this form multiple times, but please provide feedback for the most recent semester.
