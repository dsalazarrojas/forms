# School Virtual Learning Parent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide helps you navigate the "School Virtual Learning Parent Survey". The purpose of this survey is to collect your honest feedback about your child's virtual learning experience and preferences for communication with the school.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please take a few minutes to fill out this short survey. Your input will help us understand your thoughts and concerns.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any concerns or suggestions, feel free to elaborate in the text boxes provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Virtual Learning Parent Survey** (`title`, required: false): This is the title of the survey, and you've already started here!
* **How do you feel about your child's experience with virtual learning this past semester?** (`intro_text_1`, required: false, type: text): Please share your honest opinion about your child's experience with virtual learning this past semester. What do you like or dislike about the way the school is handling virtual learning?
* **If you have any concerns about your child's virtual learning experience, please provide details below.** (`intro_text_2`, required: false, type: text): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any concerns or worries you have about your child's virtual learning experience. Be as specific as possible so we can better understand your concerns.
* **Are you satisfied with our current communication methods?** (`intro_text_3`, required: false, type: text): Please rate your satisfaction level with the current communication methods used by the school. You can type "Yes" or "No" to answer this question.
* **What methods would you prefer for communication with your child's teacher or school administrator?** (`intro_text_4`, required: false, type: select_multiple): Select all the methods you prefer for communication with your child's teacher or school administrator. You can choose from "Yes" or "No" options.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false, type: email): This is where you can enter your email address if you want to be contacted by the school or if you have any questions or concerns.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required: false, type: text): This is where you can enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by the school or if you have any questions or concerns.
* **What support needs do you have that the school does not currently provide?** (`support_needs`, required: false, type: text): Please list the support needs you have that the school does not currently provide. This will help us identify areas where we can improve.

## Tips
- Be honest and open when providing your feedback.
- If you need help with any question, feel free to ask us.
- Your input is valuable in helping us improve the school's virtual learning program.
