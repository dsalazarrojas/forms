# Exponent Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather participant feedback and ratings about a course, allowing course providers to understand the strengths and weaknesses of their program and make data-driven decisions to improve future course offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Name** and **Participant Email Address** (if you want your name to appear on the report).
2. If you're not sure what to do, start with the **Participant Info** section and provide some background information about yourself as a participant.
3. Choose a rating for the overall quality of the course by selecting one of the options: **Excellent**, **Good**, **Fair**, **Poor**, or **Bad**.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions for improvement, use the **Suggestions for Improvement** text box.
5. Finally, enter your **Email Address** and **Phone Number** (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`): A text field where you can provide some information about yourself as a participant.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of the course?** (`program_rating`): Choose one of the options: **Excellent**, **Good**, **Fair**, **Poor**, or **Bad**.
* **How can we improve this course?** (`rating_comments`): If you have any comments or suggestions for improvement, please type them here.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your name as you'd like it to appear on the report.
* **Your Name (will appear on report)** (`participant_name_label`): This is your name as it will appear on the report, if you want it to match your actual name or use a nickname, you can type it here.
* **Participant Email Address** (`participant_email`): Enter your participant email address, this will be used for follow-up communication.
* **Participant Phone Number** (`participant_phone`): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`program_improvement`): If you have any comments or suggestions for improvement, please type them here.

## Tips
- Please be honest and provide constructive feedback to help course providers improve their programs.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can always ask for clarification.
- The form is meant to be completed by course participants, so please keep your responses respectful and professional.
