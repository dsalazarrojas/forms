# Homeless Youth Education Access Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the barriers and support needs of homeless youth in accessing education. The data collected will be used to inform the development of more effective support services for this population.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. If a question does not apply to your situation, please select "No" or leave the field blank.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please contact your support worker for guidance.
4. Please complete all required questions first before proceeding to optional sections.
