# Ecosystem And Food Chain Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ecosystem And Food Chain Survey is designed to gather information about your educational background and teaching experiences. Your input will help improve the quality of educational content in the domain of ecosystems and food chains.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the highest level of education you have completed from the options provided.
2. Indicate how familiar you are with ecosystems, choosing one of the four options.
3. Choose all the components of the food chain that you are familiar with.
4. Enter the amount of time you spend on activities related to ecosystems and food chains, if applicable.
5. Select any teaching practices you have used in the classroom.
6. Choose any materials you have used in teaching ecosystems and food chains.
7. Select any goals you have achieved in teaching ecosystems and food chains.
8. Indicate how long you would like the survey to take.
9. Choose how often you would like to take the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What Is Your Highest Level Of Education Completed (education_level)**: This question asks about your educational background. Please select the highest level of education you have completed (e.g., high school, bachelor's degree, etc.).
* **How Familiar Are You With Ecosystems (ecosystem_nowledge)**: This question assesses your level of familiarity with ecosystems. Please choose the option that best describes how well you know about ecosystems.
* **How Do You Understand The Food Chain (food_chain_concept)**: This question asks you to select all the components of the food chain that you are familiar with. Please choose from the options provided.
* **How Much Time Do You Spend On The Following Activities Per Month (survey_time)**: This question is optional and asks about the time you spend on activities related to ecosystems and food chains. Please enter the amount of time if you are familiar with this concept.
* **What Are Some Common Teaching Practices You Have Used In The Classroom (teaching_practices)**: This question asks you to select all the teaching practices you have used in the classroom for ecosystems and food chains. Please choose from the options provided.
* **What Are Some Common Teaching Materials You Have Used In The Classroom (teaching_materials)**: This question asks you to select all the materials you have used in teaching ecosystems and food chains. Please choose from the options provided.
* **What Are Some Common Goals Of Teaching (teaching_goals)**: This question asks you to select all the goals you have achieved in teaching ecosystems and food chains. Please choose from the options provided.
* **How Long Would You Like The Survey To Be (survey_length)**: This question asks you to select the length of time you would like the survey to take. Please choose from the options provided.
* **How Often Would You Like To Take The Survey (survey_frequency)**: This question asks you to select how often you would like to take the survey. Please choose from the options provided.
