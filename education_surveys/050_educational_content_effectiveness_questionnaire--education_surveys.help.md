# Educational Content Effectiveness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness of educational content, specifically the teaching materials provided to students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure that it is not a duplicate of any previous submission.
2. Complete the form with your feedback and comments.
3. Please answer all required fields, marked with asterisk (\*).
4. If you have any additional comments or notes, please use the "Note 1" and "Note 2" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Teaching Materials** (`teaching_materials`, `select_one`, \*): Select the most relevant teaching material that best meets your needs. This field is required.
- **Teaching Materials 2** (`teaching_materials_2`, `select_multiple`, \*): Select all the teaching materials that best meet your needs. This field is required.
- **Date of Last Use** (`teaching_materials_3`, `date`, \*): Please enter the date you last used the teaching materials. This field is required.
- **Time of Last Use** (`teaching_materials_4`, `time`, \*): Please enter the time you last used the teaching materials. This field is required.
- **Note 1** (`note_1`, `note`, \*): Add any additional comments or notes regarding your experience with the teaching materials. This field is required.
- **Note 2** (`note_2`, `note`, \*): Add any further comments or notes regarding your experience with the teaching materials. This field is required.
