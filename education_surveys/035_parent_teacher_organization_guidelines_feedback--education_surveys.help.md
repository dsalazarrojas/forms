# Parent Teacher Organization Guidelines Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from parents or guardians about the clarity and effectiveness of the parent-teacher organization guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your school name in the "School Name" field.
2. Provide your name as a teacher in the "Teacher Name" field.
3. Provide your feedback on the clarity of the guidelines in the "Feedback" field.
4. Rate the effectiveness of the guidelines on a scale from 1 to 5 in the "Clarity" field.
5. Rate the effectiveness of the guidelines on a scale from 1 to 5 in the "Effectiveness" field.
6. Indicate if you would like to be followed up on your feedback in the "Follow Up" field.
7. If you have any other feedback or suggestions, please provide them in the "Other Feedback" field.
8. If you have any additional comments, please provide them in the "Comments" field.
9. Enter your submission date and time in the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields.
10. If you have any further goals or suggestions, please provide them in the "Goals" field.
11. If you have any other suggestions, please provide them in the "Suggestions" field.
12. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
13. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Phone" field.
14. If you have any further suggestions, please provide them in the "Other Suggestions" field.
15. If you would like to provide your feedback on multiple dates, please do so in the "Submission Date 2", "Submission Date 3", "Submission Date 4", "Submission Date 5", and "Submission Date 6" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your school.
* **Teacher Name** (Text, Required): Provide your name as a teacher.
* **Feedback** (Text, Required): Provide your feedback on the parent-teacher organization guidelines.
* **Clarity** (Number, Required): Rate the clarity of the guidelines on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Effectiveness** (Number, Required): Rate the effectiveness of the guidelines on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Follow Up** (Select Multiple, Required): Indicate if you would like to be followed up on your feedback.
* **Other Feedback** (Text, Optional): If you have any other feedback or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): If you have any additional comments, please provide them here.
* **Submission Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you are submitting your feedback.
* **Submission Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time you are submitting your feedback.
* **Goals** (Text, Optional): If you have any further goals or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Suggestions** (Text, Optional): If you have any other suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it here.
* **Other Suggestions** (Text, Optional): If you have any further suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Submission Date 2**, **Submission Date 3**, ..., **Submission Date 5** (Date, Optional): If you would like to provide your feedback on multiple dates, please do so here.
* **Submission Time 2**, **Submission Time 3**, ..., **Submission Time 5** (Time, Optional): If you would like to provide your feedback on multiple dates, please do so here.
