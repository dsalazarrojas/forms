# researcher_type_identification_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to identify the type and characteristics of researchers in an education context.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select your researcher type from the options provided in the "researcher_type" field.
2. Enter your institution name in the "institution" field.
3. Select any applicable research interests from the "research_interests" field.
4. Enter your research goals in the "research_goals" field.
5. Select your primary research focus from the options provided in the "research_focus" field.
6. If applicable, describe your research habits in the "researcher_habits" field.
7. Select your primary research motivation from the options provided in the "research_motivations" field.
8. If applicable, describe your research experience in the "researcher_experience" field.
9. Enter the frequency of your research activities in the "research_frequency" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **researcher_type** (`researcher_type`, select_one, required): Select your primary role as a researcher within the institution. Options include "researcher1" and "Other".
* **institution** (`institution`, text, required): Enter the name of your institution.
* **research_interests** (`research_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all the applicable research interests that align with your work. Options include "interest1", "interest2", "interest3".
* **research_goals** (`research_goals`, text, required): Describe your current or long-term research goals.
* **research_focus** (`research_focus`, select_one, required): Select your primary research focus. Options include "focus1", "focus2", "focus3".
* **researcher_habits** (`researcher_habits`, text, required): Describe your research habits and any relevant details.
* **research_motivations** (`research_motivations`, select_one, required): Select your primary research motivation. Options include "motivation1", "motivation2", "motivation3".
* **researcher_experience** (`researcher_experience`, text, required): Describe your level of experience in research.
* **research_frequency** (`research_frequency`, number, required): Enter the frequency of your research activities.
