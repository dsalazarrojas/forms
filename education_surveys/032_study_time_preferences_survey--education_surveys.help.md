# Study Time Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your study preferences to help us understand your study habits and schedule. Your input will help us improve our educational experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully and answer according to your study habits.
2. For select_one and select_one options, choose the one that best fits your preference.
3. For checkbox options, choose all that apply.
4. For text input, provide a concise answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your preferred study frequency?** (`study_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Choose how often you prefer to study: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely.
* **How long do you prefer your morning study sessions to be?** (`morning_session_length`, number, required: false): Enter the number of hours you prefer to study in the morning.
* **What type of study sessions do you prefer?** (`study_session_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose between Self-Study, Group Study, or Online Course.
* **How many hours do you usually study in one sitting?** (`study_session_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the number of hours you usually spend studying in one session.
* **Do you usually study during the day or night?** (`study_time_of_day`, select_one, required: false): Choose between Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Do you have a preferred study schedule?** (`study_schedule`, checkbox, required: false): Check if you have a preferred study schedule.
* **How often do you usually study in the morning?** (`morning_study_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Choose how often you usually study in the morning: Every day, 2-3 times a week, 1-2 times a week, or Rarely.
* **What time of day do you usually study?** (`study_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you usually study in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **How long do you prefer to study in one session?** (`study_session_length`, number, required: false): Enter the number of hours you prefer to study in one session.
* **What is your preferred study location?** (`preferred_study_location`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your preferred study location.
