# Inclusive Curriculum Review Committee Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inclusive Curriculum Review Committee Registration form is a tool used to register individuals to the committee. The information collected will help to create a diverse and representative committee that can provide valuable feedback on the curriculum.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
1. Fill out the form with your name and email address.
2. Select your role(s) within the committee (e.g., Committee Member, Chair).
3. Choose your areas of expertise in mathematics, biology, and chemistry.
4. Confirm your consent to participate in the committee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name for registration purposes. 
* **Email**: Enter your email address to contact you regarding committee matters.
* **Role**: Select your role(s) within the committee (e.g., Committee Member, Chair). This will determine your level of participation and responsibilities.
* **Institution**: Enter your institution name for identification purposes.
* **Mathematics**: Select your areas of expertise in mathematics, such as Pure Mathematics, Applied Mathematics, or Mathematical Physics.
* **Biology**: Select your areas of expertise in biology, such as Botany, Zoology, or Genetics.
* **Chemistry**: Select your areas of expertise in chemistry, such as Inorganic Chemistry, Organic Chemistry, or Physical Chemistry.
* **Consent**: Confirm your consent to participate in the committee. Please select "None" if you do not wish to participate, or "Other" if you have other commitments.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email address is up-to-date and correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* If you are unable to complete this form, please contact us for assistance.
