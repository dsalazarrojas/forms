# Academic Recognition Systems Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to understand your experiences and opinions on the current academic recognition systems at this institution. Your feedback will help us identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the recognition process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your role at the institution from the options provided.
2. Choose the department or school you are affiliated with.
3. Indicate your level of awareness of the recognition programs available at the institution.
4. Answer questions about your personal experiences with academic recognition.
5. Select the types of recognition you value most.
6. Rate the fairness and transparency of the current recognition process.
7. Evaluate the clarity of the nomination or application process.
8. Rate the impact of academic recognition on your motivation to perform well.
9. Identify any areas you feel are underrepresented in current recognition programs.
10. Choose how frequently academic recognition should be awarded.
11. Describe the biggest challenge with the current recognition system, if any.
12. Rate your overall satisfaction with the academic recognition systems at this institution.
13. Provide any suggestions for improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your role at the institution** (`respondent_role`): This field asks for your position or affiliation within the institution. Please select the option that best represents your role.
* **Department or school** (`department`): Indicate the department or school you are most closely related to.
* **How aware are you of the recognition programs available at this institution** (`awareness_of_programs`): Choose how familiar you are with the recognition programs in place.
* **Have you personally received any form of academic recognition** (`recognition_received`): Select one of the options that best describes your experiences with recognition.
* **Which types of recognition do you value most** (`recognition_types_valued`): Select all the types of recognition that you value the most. Multiple answers are allowed.
* **How fair and transparent do you consider the current recognition process** (`fairness_rating`): Rate the fairness and transparency of the current recognition process.
* **How clear is the nomination or application process for academic recognition** (`nomination_process_clarity`): Choose how clear you consider the nomination or application process for academic recognition.
* **How much does academic recognition impact your motivation to perform well** (`impact_on_motivation`): Rate the impact of academic recognition on your motivation to perform well.
* **Which areas do you feel are underrepresented in current recognition programs** (`underrepresented_areas`): Select all the areas you feel are underrepresented in current recognition programs.
* **How frequently should academic recognition be awarded** (`frequency_preference`): Choose how frequently you think academic recognition should be awarded.
* **What is the biggest challenge with the current recognition system** (`improvement_challenges`): Describe the primary challenge you see with the current recognition system.
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with the academic recognition systems at this institution** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the current academic recognition systems.
* **What improvements would you suggest for academic recognition at this institution** (`suggestions`): Share your thoughts on what could be improved in the recognition process.
