# Educational Leadership Trust Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather data about the school's leadership and facilities from school stakeholders.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Survey** (`school_survey`, text, required): This is a short description of the school's current situation. Please provide a brief overview of the school's current state, including any challenges or concerns you have.
* **Demographic Info** (`demographic_info`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the options provided to describe your demographic information (e.g., teacher, administrator, etc.).
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): This is the name of the school.
* **Principal Name** (`principal_name`, text, optional): This is the name of the school principal.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): This is the address of the school.
* **School Zip** (`school_zip`, select_multiple, optional): Please select one or more of the options provided to describe the school's zip code(s).
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): This is the school's email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): This is the school's phone number.
* **School Principal Rating** (`school_principal_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the school's principal on a scale of 1-5.
* **School Teacher Rating** (`school_teacher_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the school's teachers on a scale of 1-5.
* **School Administrator Rating** (`school_administrator_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the school administrators on a scale of 1-5.
* **School Support Services Rating** (`school_support_services_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the school's support services on a scale of 1-5.
* **School Facilities Rating** (`school_facilities_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the school's facilities on a scale of 1-5.
* **School Environment Rating** (`school_environment_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the school's environment on a scale of 1-5.
* **School Community Rating** (`school_community_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the school's community on a scale of 1-5.
* **School Safety Rating** (`school_safety_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the school's safety on a scale of 1-5.
* **School Satisfaction Rating** (`school_satisfaction_rating`, number, optional): Please rate your satisfaction with the school on a scale of 1-5.
* **School Leadership Development** (`school_leadership_development_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the school's leadership development on a scale of 1-5.
* **School Leadership Effectiveness** (`school_leadership_effectiveness_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the school's leadership effectiveness on a scale of 1-5.
* **School Leadership Clarity** (`school_leadership_clarity_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the school's leadership clarity on a scale of 1-5.
* **School Leadership Inclusiveness** (`school_leadership_inclusiveness_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the school's leadership inclusiveness on a scale of 1-5.
* **School Leadership Supportive** (`school_leadership_supportive_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the school's leadership supportive on a scale of 1-5.
* **School Leadership Fairness** (`school_leadership_fairness_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the school's leadership fairness on a scale of 1-5.
* **School Leadership Integrity** (`school_leadership_integrity_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the school's leadership integrity on a scale of 1-5.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Please fill out this field with your name or ID.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have filled out the form truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities to clarify.
* Please rate each field based on your honest opinion.
* Do not leave any field blank.
* You may use a separate piece of paper to fill out this form if you prefer.
* Please submit the form once you have filled out all the fields.
