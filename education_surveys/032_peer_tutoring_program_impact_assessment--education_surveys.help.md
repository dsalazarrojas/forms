# Peer Tutoring Program Impact Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help program administrators and directors assess the impact of their peer tutoring programs. Your input will help us better understand the effectiveness of your program and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form once for each unique program.
- Review the form carefully and ensure accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to us for clarification.
- Make sure to provide relevant and specific information about your program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your peer tutoring program.
* **Program Description** (`program_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your peer tutoring program in 100-200 characters.
* **Number of Students Participating** (`number_students_participating`, number, optional): Enter the approximate number of students participating in your program.
* **Number of Tutors Available** (`number_tutors_available`, number, optional): Enter the number of tutors available to assist with your program.
* **Student Satisfaction** (`student_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your student satisfaction with the program on a scale of 1-5.
* **Program Satisfaction** (`program_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale of 1-5.
* **Program Improvement Areas** (`program_improvement_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the areas you'd like to improve on, such as:
	+ Improved student academic performance
	+ Improved student engagement
	+ Improved student retention
	+ Improved tutor availability
	+ Improved program satisfaction
	+ Improved student satisfaction
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Program Improvement Goals** (`program_improvement_goals`, select_one, optional): Select one goal you'd like to achieve with your program, such as:
	+ Increased student satisfaction
	+ Improved student engagement
	+ Improved tutor availability
	+ Improved academic achievement
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Share any additional feedback or suggestions about your program.
* **Contact Information** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Program Email** (`program_email`, email, optional): Enter your program's email address.
