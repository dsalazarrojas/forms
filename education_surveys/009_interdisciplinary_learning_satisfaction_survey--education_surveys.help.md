# Interdisciplinary Learning Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Interdisciplinary Learning Satisfaction Survey" is designed to collect feedback and ratings from users about a course they have taken. This form helps us understand the effectiveness and quality of the course, which informs our teaching practices and course development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the course title and description to provide a brief overview of the course.
2. Enter the instructor's name and contact information.
3. Rate the course using the scale provided.
4. Provide feedback about your experience with the course.
5. If you have additional notes or comments, please enter them in the "Additional notes" field.
6. If you have any further comments or ratings about another course, please use the additional sections at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the course title** (`course_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title of the course.
* **What is the course description** (`course_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the course.
* **What is the instructor name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the instructor for the course.
* **Rate the course** (`course_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the course using the scale: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What is your course rating?** (`course_rating_1`, select_multiple, required): Select all the ratings that apply to you.
* **What are your thoughts on the course?** (`feedback`, text, required): Share your thoughts and feedback about the course.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you completed the course.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you completed the course.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Additional notes** (`note`, note, optional): Share any additional notes or comments about the course.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, email, optional): Enter an email address for further communication.
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, text, optional): Enter a phone number for further communication.
* **Additional notes 2** (`note_2`, note, optional): Share any additional notes or comments about another course.

Note: This form is designed to be completed for each course separately. If you have feedback or comments about multiple courses, please use the additional sections at the end of the form.
