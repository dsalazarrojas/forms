# Triangle Measurement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from researchers or institutions on their projects and research goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your information as the submitter, including your name, email, phone number, and date.
2. Provide details about your research project, including its title, URL, and researcher's name, title, and institution's name, URL, and email.
3. If applicable, provide feedback and notes about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Measure Triangle 1**: Enter a numerical measurement for Triangle 1.
* **Measure Triangle 2**: Enter a numerical measurement for Triangle 2.
* **Measure Triangle 3**: Enter a numerical measurement for Triangle 3.
* **Measure Triangle 4**: Enter a numerical measurement for Triangle 4. 
* **Teacher Feedback**: Provide any feedback or comments for the teacher.
* **Research Goal**: Select the primary goal of your research (either "education" or "research").
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your full name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number as the submitter (optional).
* **Submitter Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments as the submitter (optional).
* **Submitter Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitter Time**: Enter the time of submission (optional).
* **Submitter Location**: Enter the location of submission (optional).
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher involved in the research.
* **Teacher Title**: Enter the title of the teacher involved in the research.
* **Research Institution**: Enter the name of the institution involved in the research.
* **Research Project**: Enter the title of the research project.
* **Research Project URL**: Enter the URL of the research project (optional).
* **Researcher Name**: Enter the name of the researcher involved in the research.
* **Researcher Title**: Enter the title of the researcher involved in the research.
* **Institution Name**: Enter the name of the institution involved in the research.
* **Institution URL**: Enter the URL of the institution involved in the research.
* **Institution Email**: Enter the email address of the institution involved in the research (optional).
* **Institution Phone**: Enter the phone number of the institution involved in the research (optional).
* **Institution Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the institution involved in the research (optional).

Please fill out the form accurately and completely, and do not alter or duplicate any of the fields.
