# Academic Research Visualization Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Research Visualization Request Form is used to collect information from researchers to provide visualization support for their projects. This form ensures that the research team has all necessary details to create effective visualizations that meet the researcher's needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, marked as `*required*` in the form.
2. Select the correct format of your dataset.
3. Choose the type of visualization you need and select all that apply.
4. Provide a brief description of your research project.
5. Specify who the intended audience is and when you need the visualization completed.
6. If you have any special requirements or design preferences, please include them in the additional notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Full Name** (`requester_name`, `text`, *required*): Please enter your full name as the requester.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`, `email`, *required*): Enter your email address to receive updates and information about your request.
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, `text`, *optional*): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Department or Research Group** (`department`, `text`, *required*): Enter the name of your department or research group.
* **Research Project Title** (`project_title`, `text`, *required*): Enter a clear and concise title for your research project.
* **Brief Description of the Research Project** (`project_description`, `text`, *required*): Provide a brief description of your research project, including enough detail to help the visualization team understand your data.
* **Type of Visualization Needed** (`visualization_type`, `select_multiple`, *required*): Choose all types of visualizations you need, such as charts and graphs, infographics, interactive dashboards, geographic maps, network diagrams, 3D models, and data animations.
* **What Format is Your Data Currently In?** (`data_format`, `select_one`, *required*): Select the format of your dataset, such as spreadsheet, database, statistical software output, or raw text.
* **Approximate Size of Your Dataset** (`data_size`, `select_one`, *required*): Estimate the size of your dataset, which will help us determine the complexity of the visualization.
* **Intended Audience** (`intended_audience`, `select_one`, *required*): Specify who the intended audience is, such as academic publication, conference presentation, grant proposal, internal review, public outreach, or classroom instruction.
* **Requested Completion Date** (`deadline`, `date`, *required*): Enter the date when you need the visualization completed, allowing at least two weeks for standard requests.
* **Priority Level** (`urgency_level`, `select_one`, *required*): Select the priority level of your request, which will guide our workload and timeline.
* **Any Special Requirements or Design Preferences** (`special_requirements`, `text`, *optional*): If you have any special requirements or design preferences, such as color schemes or branding guidelines, please specify them here.
* **Additional Notes or Instructions** (`additional_notes`, `text`, *optional*): If you have any additional comments or instructions, please include them here.
