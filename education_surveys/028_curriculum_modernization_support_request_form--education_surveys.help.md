# Curriculum Modernization Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for educators to request support for curriculum modernization initiatives. It's meant for specific, targeted requests for curriculum updates or changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Title` field with a concise and descriptive title for your request.
2. In the `Category` field, select the most appropriate category for your request from the provided options. This will help us understand the scope of your request.
3. In the `Description` field, provide a detailed description of the specific changes or updates you are requesting for your curriculum.
4. In the `Required Fields` field, select the fields that are necessary for your request to be processed.
5. In the `Submit` field, confirm that you are submitting your request.
6. If you have any additional contact information, enter your `Email` and `Phone` fields, but these are optional.
7. Finally, confirm whether your request includes any required fields (`Required`) and add any additional notes or information in the `Note` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`title`, text, required): Enter a concise and descriptive title for your request.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the most appropriate category for your request from the options provided.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the specific changes or updates you are requesting for your curriculum.
* **Required Fields** (`required_fields`, select_multiple, optional): Select the fields that are necessary for your request to be processed. This is a list of fields that should be included in your request.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you are submitting your request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Required** (`required`, select_one, optional): Confirm whether your request includes any required fields.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or information to your request.
