# Faculty Peer Resource Exchange Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Peer Resource Exchange Registration Form is designed for faculty members to sign up for the program. This will help create a network of peers who can support and learn from each other.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with your required information, marked with an asterisk (*).
2.  Choose your department and campus from the provided options.
3.  Select your role within your department (e.g., Admin, Director, Instructor, etc.).
4.  Ensure your email address is entered correctly, as this will be used for communication within the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your university email address, which will be used for program communication.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for any additional contact purposes.
*   **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Choose your department from the provided options.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title within your department.
*   **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, optional): Select your role within your department (e.g., Admin, Director, etc.).
*   **Campus** (`campus`, select_multiple, required): Choose your campus from the provided options.
*   **Faculty Number** (`faculty_number`, number, optional): If applicable, enter a faculty number for departmental tracking.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, optional): If you need to schedule a meeting, enter the preferred date.
*   **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If you need to schedule a meeting, enter the preferred time.
*   **Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or requests you have for the program.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter your email address correctly, as it will be used for program communication.
*   Choose only your actual department and campus affiliations.
*   Be honest about your role within your department to ensure accurate networking.
*   Use the "Date" and "Time" fields if you plan to schedule meetings or events.
*   Use the "Notes" field to add any additional comments or requests you have for the program.
