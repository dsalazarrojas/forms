# Education For Sustainability Attitudes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education For Sustainability Attitudes Survey is a form designed to collect feedback and perceptions from educators on their attitudes towards education for sustainability. This survey aims to gather information on how educators perceive the role of education in promoting sustainability and what challenges they face in teaching about sustainability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the roles that apply to you from the "What role do you currently play as an educator?" field.
2. Choose how you perceive the role of education for sustainability from the "How do you perceive the role of education for sustainable development?" field.
3. If you have any additional challenges you face when teaching about sustainability, type them in the "What are some current challenges you face when teaching about sustainability?" field.
4. If you have any additional comments, type them in the "Comments" field.
5. If you have another role as an educator, select it from the "Is there another role you play as an educator?" field.
6. If you have any comments about your other role, type them in the "Other role comments" field.
7. Add any additional comments you have in the "Additional comments" field.
8. Provide your email address in the "Email" field, if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive the results of the survey.
9. Finally, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you'd like to be contacted by phone for follow-up questions or to receive the results of the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What role do you currently play as an educator?**: Select all the roles that apply to you, such as Teacher, Researcher, Manager, Administrator, or Student.
* **How do you perceive the role of education for sustainable development?**: Choose how you perceive the role of education for sustainable development from the options: Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neutral, Somewhat Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
* **What are some current challenges you face when teaching about sustainability?**: Type any additional challenges you face when teaching about sustainability.
* **Comments**: Type any additional comments you have.
* **Is there another role you play as an educator?**: Select if you have another role as an educator.
* **Other role comments**: Type any comments about your other role.
* **Additional comments**: Add any additional comments you have.
* **Email**: Provide your email address, if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive the results of the survey.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted by phone for follow-up questions or to receive the results of the survey.

Note: The fields that are not required are not mandatory, but it would be helpful to fill them out if you have additional information you'd like to share.
