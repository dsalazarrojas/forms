# Student Concentration Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about how students rate their concentration in class and identify potential areas for improvement in their learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question carefully, as indicated below.
2. Select the answer that best matches your experience.
3. Be honest, as your input will help us better understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **How would you rate your ability to focus in class?** (id: 1)
* This question asks how you rate your ability to focus in class. Answer with a number rating, where:
	+ 1 is "Poor"
	+ 2 is "Fair"
	+ 3 is "Good"
	+ 4 is "Very Good"
	+ 5 is "Excellent"

### 2. **How would you rate your focus in class?** (id: 2)
* This question asks how focused you are in class. Answer with one of the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair

### 3. **What types of distractions do you experience in class?** (id: 3)
* This question asks if you experience any distractions in class. Answer by selecting all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 4. **How many hours do you typically study each day?** (id: 4)
* This question asks about your study habits. Answer with a number of hours.

### 5. **How often do you study outside of class?** (id: 5)
* This question asks about your study habits. Answer with one of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 6. **How do you manage your time?** (id: 6)
* This question asks about your time management. Please provide any relevant details.

### 7. **Email** (id: 7)
* This question is for your email address. If you'd like to be contacted for more information, please provide your email address.

### 8. **Phone** (id: 8)
* This question is for your phone number. If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please provide your number.

### 9. **Additional comments** (id: 9)
* This question is for any further comments or thoughts you'd like to share with us.
