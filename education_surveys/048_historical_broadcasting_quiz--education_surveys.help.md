# Historical Broadcasting Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to create a historical broadcasting quiz that can be used to educate and entertain users about historical events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and choose the answers that best apply.
2. Make sure to choose all that apply for questions that have multiple correct answers.
3. For questions that ask for a specific reason, choose the reason that best explains your answer.
4. For questions that ask for your name and contact information, please provide it accurately.

* **Historical Broadcasting** (`section_1`, `text`, required: false): This section will introduce the quiz and provide some background information about historical broadcasting.
* **How do you know the answer is correct?** (`question_1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking for the reason you know the answer is correct. Choose the reason that best applies to you.

* **What historical period do you like most?** (`question_4`, `select_one`, required: false): This question is asking for your favorite historical period.
