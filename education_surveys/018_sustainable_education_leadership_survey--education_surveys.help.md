# Sustainable Education Leadership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to understand the opinions and experiences of educators on sustainability in education. It's an essential tool for educational leaders to make informed decisions and develop effective strategies for promoting sustainability in educational settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and answer every question truthfully.
2.  For each question, select or enter the most accurate response based on your personal experiences and opinions.
3.  Ensure that you do not skip any required fields.
4.  Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **ratings and challenges** (Text, Optional): Please describe any challenges you face or have faced in promoting sustainability in your educational setting.
* **leadership perspectives** (Select One, Required): Do you consider sustainability when making decisions in your educational role? 
    - **Yes**: You believe sustainability is a key consideration in your decision-making process.
    - **No**: Sustainability is not a consideration in your decision-making process.
* **school and university** (Select Multiple, Required): Which of the following apply to your work? 
    - **Yes**: You have been involved in sustainability-focused initiatives or projects.
    - **No**: You have not been involved in sustainability-focused initiatives or projects.
* **sustainability focus** (Text, Optional): Please describe any sustainability-focused initiatives or projects you have been involved in or would like to be involved in.
* **ratings** (Number, Optional): Rate your level of agreement with the statement "Promoting sustainability in education is important."
*   1-5: Strongly disagree, Somewhat disagree, Neutral, Somewhat agree, Strongly agree
*   Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 5.
* **challenges** (Text, Optional): Describe any challenges you face or have faced in promoting sustainability in education.
* **leadership rating** (Number, Optional): Rate your overall leadership's commitment to promoting sustainability in education.
*   1-5: Strongly disagree, Somewhat disagree, Neutral, Somewhat agree, Strongly agree
*   Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 5.
*   **leadership perspective** (Text, Optional): Please describe your leader's perspective on promoting sustainability in education. 

Note: The numerical scale used in the "ratings" and "leadership rating" fields is based on the Likert scale, where 1 indicates strong disagreement and 5 indicates strong agreement.
