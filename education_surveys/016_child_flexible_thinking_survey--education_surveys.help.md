# Child Flexible Thinking Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Child Flexible Thinking Survey is designed to assess a child's ability to adapt to changes, problem-solve, and interact with their environment in a flexible and positive way.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read each question carefully and choose the best answer based on your observations of the child.
2.  Select the relevant option that best describes the child's behavior or attitude.
3.  Complete the additional observations section if you have any further comments or examples to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Child Name** (Child Name, text, required): Please enter the name of the child being surveyed.
*   **Date of Survey** (Survey Date, date, required): Please enter the date on which the survey was conducted.
*   **Reaction to Change in Routine** (Reaction to Change in Routine, select_one, required): Please choose an option that describes the child's response to unexpected changes in their routine.
    +   Adapts easily and stays calm
    +   Somewhat struggles but recovers
    +   Struggles significantly or melts down
*   **Ability to Try New Foods** (Ability to Try New Foods, select_one, required): Please choose an option that describes the child's level of food neophobia.
    +   Willing to try new things
    +   Needs significant encouragement
    +   Refuses to try new foods
*   **Response to Losing a Game** (Response to Losing a Game, select_one, required): Please choose an option that describes the child's sportsmanship level.
    +   Accepts loss calmly
    +   Gets frustrated or angry
    +   Refuses to continue playing
*   **Persistence when Stuck** (Persistence when Stuck, select_one, required): Please choose an option that describes the child's behavior when faced with difficult tasks.
    +   Tries a new way or strategy
    +   Asks for immediate help
    +   Gives up quickly
*   **Willingness to Share Toys** (Willingness to Share Toys, select_one, required): Please choose an option that describes the child's willingness to cooperate with their peers.
    +   Always shares willingly
    +   Usually shares with prompting
    +   Rarely shares
*   **Can child take turns without distress?** (Can child take turns without distress?, select_one, required): Please choose an option that describes the child's patience in group settings.
    +   Yes - Shows patience
    +   No - Struggles with waiting
*   **Additional Observations** (Additional observations, text, optional): Please share any additional comments or examples that may be helpful in understanding the child's flexible thinking.
*   **Parent or Teacher Email for Results** (Parent or Teacher Email for Results, email, required): Please enter your email address to receive the survey analysis. 

Note: The survey is designed to be completed by parents or teachers to assess the child's flexible thinking abilities. The answers are not meant to be subjective but based on observed behaviors and attitudes. The survey results will help in understanding the child's strengths and weaknesses in flexible thinking. 

This help guide is to provide a clear understanding of the survey purpose, instructions, and each field explanation to facilitate the completion process. The survey results can be used to identify areas of improvement and support the child's flexible thinking abilities. 

Please complete the form honestly and to the best of your abilities, and thank you for taking the time to assess the child's flexible thinking.
