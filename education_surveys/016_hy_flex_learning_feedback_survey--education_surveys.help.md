# hy_flex_learning_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the flexibility of a course, including its content, instructions, and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Go through each page and answer the questions truthfully and concisely.
2. For each field, provide as much information as you can.
3. If a field is not relevant to your experience, please skip it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Introduce yourself and briefly describe your experience with the course.
- **Course Experience** (`2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Have you had any course experience? (Yes/No)
- **Feedback on Course Content** (`3`, `text`, optional): What did you think about the course content? Was it relevant, clear, and engaging?
- **Feedback on Instructions** (`4`, `text`, optional): Were the instructions clear and helpful during the course? Did you find them easy to follow?
- **Feedback on Tutoring** (`5`, `text`, optional): How was the support from the tutors? Were they helpful and responsive?
- **Course Format** (`6`, `select_one`, optional): Did the course format (e.g., online, in-person, hybrid) meet your needs? (Yes/No)
- **Feedback on Support** (`7`, `text`, optional): How well was the support provided during the course? Was it timely and effective?
- **Overall Satisfaction** (`8`, `number`, optional): On a scale of 1-5, how satisfied are you with your course experience?
- **Suggestions** (`9`, `text`, optional): Do you have any suggestions for improving the course?
- **Comments** (`10`, `text`, optional): Is there anything else you'd like to share about your course experience?
- **Category** (`11`, `select_one`, optional): Do you think this course belongs to a specific category? (Yes/No)
- **Email** (`12`, `email`, optional): If you'd like to provide an email address, we can follow up with you about your feedback.
- **Phone** (`13`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide a phone number, we can contact you about your feedback.
- **Date** (`14`, `date`, optional): If you'd like to provide a date and time for when you took the course, we can use this information to better understand your experience.
- **Time** (`15`, `time`, optional): If you'd like to provide a specific time of day for when you took the course, we can use this information to better understand your experience.
- **Note** (`16`, `note`, optional): Is there anything else you'd like to mention about your course experience?
