# School System Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from school administrators, teachers, or visitors to the school. The data collected will help the school system improve its services and operations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information in the fields provided.
2. Make sure to enter the correct email address in the **Email** field.
3. If you have any feedback or comments, use the **Feedback** field to provide them.
4. Enter the correct date of your visit in the **Date Of Visit** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School System Survey**: This is a title field, please enter a descriptive name for the form.
* **School Name**: This field collects the name of the school.
* **School Address**: This field collects the address of the school.
* **Teacher Name**: This field collects the name of a teacher or staff member.
* **Feedback**: This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Email**: This field requires a valid email address. Please ensure it is correctly formatted.
* **Phone**: This field collects the phone number of the school or user.
* **Date Of Visit**: This field collects the date of your visit to the school. Please use the date format specified in your device's settings.
