# Student Inclusivity Advocacy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to help us understand your experiences and concerns regarding inclusivity and advocacy within our educational institution. Your responses will help us gather valuable insights to improve our services and create a more inclusive environment for all students.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully, and select the most accurate option for your response.
2. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you.
3. If you have any questions or concerns about a particular question, feel free to ask our support team for clarification.
4. Once you've completed the survey, click the submit button to send it to us.
