# Educational Technology Usability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather insights into your habits and preferences regarding educational technology, which will help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand the questions and the options provided.
2. Select the answer that best fits your experience or preference for each question.
3. Enter any additional comments or information in the "Additional comments" field.
4. Enter your email address (if desired).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educational Technology Usability Survey** (`introduction`, `text`, optional): This is a brief description of the survey purpose and introduction.
* **User Information** (`user_information`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself if you feel it is relevant to this survey.
* **How often do you use digital tools?** (`question_1`, `select_one`, optional): Answer how often you use digital tools, from "Never" to "Almost Always".
* **Which digital tools do you most frequently use in your work or studies?** (`question_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the digital tools you use most frequently, from "Learning Management System (LMS)" to "Other".
* **What is your preferred channel for communication with peers or teachers?** (`question_3`, `select_one`, optional): Answer how you prefer to communicate, from "Email" to "In-person".
* **Additional comments** (`note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you feel is important to share with us.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for any future reference or communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if desired.

# Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when selecting your answers.
* If unsure, choose the answer that best fits your experience.
* You can select more than one option for the question "Which digital tools do you most frequently use in your work or studies?".
* Enter any information you feel is relevant to this survey.
