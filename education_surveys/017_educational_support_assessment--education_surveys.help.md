# Educational Support Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Support Assessment form is designed to gather information from educators about students' challenges and support needs. This form helps educators identify areas where students require additional support, allowing for targeted interventions and improved student outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and understand their purpose.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's first name.
* **Student age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter the student's age.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Most significant challenge** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Choose the most significant challenge the student is facing, such as "lack of interest in the subject" or "difficulty keeping up with the pace."
* **Skills requiring support** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple skills where the student requires support, such as "time management," "organization skills," "communication skills," or "self-confidence."
* **Any other information** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any other relevant information about the student.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date the assessment is completed.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of day when the assessment is completed.
* **Primary goal of educational support** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Choose the primary goal of support, such as "improve grades" or "improve engagement."
* **Additional skills requiring support** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple skills that require support, such as "time management," "organization skills," "communication skills," or "self-confidence."
* **First language** (`first_language`, select_multiple, optional): Select the student's first language, such as "english," "chinese," "spanish," or "other."
* **Second language** (`second_language`, select_multiple, optional): Select the student's second language, such as "english," "chinese," "spanish," or "other."
* **Third language** (`third_language`, select_multiple, optional): Select the student's third language, such as "english," "chinese," "spanish," or "other."
