# MPH Concentration Change Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about students' concentration changes within the MPH program. Please fill out this form if you are changing your concentration and do not have any existing submission with the same student ID and date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your Student ID**: Please enter your Student ID number in the box provided.
2. **Select your new concentration**: Choose the new concentration you are requesting to change to from the dropdown menu.
3. **Provide a reason for the change**: Type a brief explanation for your reason for changing your concentration.
4. **Enter your advisor's name**: If you have an advisor, select their name from the dropdown menu. Otherwise, select "None" if you are not assigned to an advisor.
5. **Additional comments for your advisor**: If you have an advisor, type any additional comments or information you would like to share with them.
6. **Sign your name**: Please sign your name to confirm your submission.
7. **Date**: Select the date you are submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (1): Enter your Student ID number.
* **Concentration Requested** (2): Choose the new concentration you are requesting to change to.
* **Rationale** (3): Please provide a brief explanation for your reason for changing your concentration.
* **Advisor Name** (4): If you have an advisor, select their name from the dropdown menu. If you are not assigned to an advisor, select "None".
* **Advisor Comment** (5): If you have an advisor, type any additional comments or information you would like to share with them.
* **Student Signature** (6): Please sign your name to confirm your submission.
* **Date Submitted** (7): Select the date you are submitting this form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Check that your new concentration is correct before submitting.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the MPH administration team or your advisor.
