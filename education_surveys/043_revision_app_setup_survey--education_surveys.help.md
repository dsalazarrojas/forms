# Revision App Setup Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for the purpose of setting up a revision app. The form will help the development team tailor the app to meet the user's specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, follow these steps:
	* Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
	* For fields with options, select the one that best describes your needs.
	* If you have a specific requirement not listed in the options, use the "Other" field to provide more details.
	* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for specifying the subject of the revision app. Enter the subject name as it appears in your revision app.
* **Subject Label** (`subject_label`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for adding a label or a description for the subject of the revision app.
* **Device** (`device`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of device you will be using for the app. Options are:
	+ Android
	+ iPhone
	+ iPad
	+ Other (if your device is not listed)
* **Other** (`device_other`, `text`, required/optional): If you selected "Other" as your device type, provide more details about your device in this field.
* **Reminder Frequency** (`reminder_frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how often you want to receive reminders for the app:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **Reminder Frequency Other** (`reminder_frequency_other`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a specific reminder frequency not listed, provide more details about it in this field.
* **Feature Requests** (`feature_requests`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select any additional features you would like to see in the app:
	+ Reminder Notifications
	+ Smart Notifications
	+ Auto Save
	+ Auto Backup
* **Feature Requests Other** (`feature_requests_other`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a specific feature request not listed, provide more details about it in this field.
* **Subject Other** (`subject_other`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a specific subject not listed, provide more details about it in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the development team for assistance.
