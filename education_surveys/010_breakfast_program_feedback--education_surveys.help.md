# Breakfast Program Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Breakfast Program Feedback form is designed to gather information about your experiences with the breakfast program. This form helps us understand what you like or dislike about the program, and how we can improve it to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your role in the breakfast program (respondent_type) from the list provided.
2. Choose your age range from the available options.
3. Indicate how often you participate in the breakfast program (program_participation).
4. Rate the quality of the breakfast food (food_quality).
5. Choose the level of variety in breakfast options (food_variety).
6. Rate the taste of the food (food_taste).
7. Determine if the portion sizes are adequate (portion_size).
8. Select how the meal is presented (meal_presentation).
9. Choose if dietary needs are accommodated (dietary_accommodations).
10. List your favorite breakfast items (favorite_items).
11. List items you would like to see less of (least_favorite_items).
12. Suggest new breakfast items (new_items_suggestions).
13. Rate the service speed (service_speed).
14. Choose the level of friendliness from the staff (staff_friendliness).
15. Choose the level of convenience for the location (location_convenience).
16. Rate your overall satisfaction with the breakfast program (overall_satisfaction).
17. Provide suggestions for improvements (improvements).
18. Share any additional comments or feedback (additional_feedback).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Are you a** (`respondent_type`): Select your role in the breakfast program. This will help us understand your perspective as a participant.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`): Choose your age range to help us tailor the program to your needs.
* **How often do you participate in the breakfast program** (`program_participation`): Indicate how often you take part in the breakfast program.
* **How would you rate the quality of breakfast food** (`food_quality`): Rate the quality of the food from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **How would you rate the variety of breakfast options** (`food_variety`): Choose from 1 (Not enough variety) to 5 (Excellent variety).
* **How does the food taste** (`food_taste`): Rate the taste from 1 (Very unappetizing) to 5 (Very delicious).
* **Are portion sizes adequate** (`portion_size`): Rate the portion sizes from 1 (Too small) to 5 (Too large).
* **How is the meal presented** (`meal_presentation`): Rate the presentation from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Are dietary needs accommodated** (`dietary_accommodations`): Rate the accommodation of dietary needs from 1 (Not accommodated) to 5 (Excellently accommodated).
* **What are your favorite breakfast items** (`favorite_items`): List your preferred breakfast foods.
* **What items would you like to see less of** (`least_favorite_items`): List foods you do not enjoy.
* **What new breakfast items would you like to see** (`new_items_suggestions`): Suggest new breakfast foods you would like to see.
* **How long does it take to get breakfast** (`service_speed`): Rate the service speed from 1 (Too long) to 5 (Very quick).
* **How friendly is the breakfast program staff** (`staff_friendliness`): Rate the staff friendliness from 1 (Very unfriendly) to 5 (Very friendly).
* **Is the breakfast location convenient for you** (`location_convenience`): Rate the location convenience from 1 (Very inconvenient) to 5 (Very convenient).
* **Overall satisfaction with the breakfast program** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction from 1 (Very dissatisfied) to 5 (Very satisfied).
* **What improvements could be made** (`improvements`): Provide suggestions for improvements.
* **Any additional comments or feedback** (`additional_feedback`): Share any other thoughts or comments.
