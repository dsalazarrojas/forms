# Senior Seminar Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback about senior seminars. Your responses will help seminar organizers and facilitators improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and honestly.
2. Answer each question based on your experience with the senior seminar.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Seminar Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Feedback**: Briefly describe your experience with the seminar. This will help us understand what worked well and what could be improved.
* **Seminar Date**: Please enter the date of the seminar you attended.
* **Session Length**: If you have a specific idea about the ideal length of the seminar, please enter it here.
* **Seminar Topic**: Describe the topic of the seminar in a few words.
* **Session Leaders**: List the names of the people who led the seminar.
* **Seminar Facilitator**: If a facilitator was present, please list their name.
* **Seminar Notes**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please write them here.
* **Seminar Rating**: Rate your experience with the seminar from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Seminar Feedback**: If you have any specific feedback about the seminar, please write it here.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the seminar from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Would Attend Again**: If you would attend another seminar like this, please select "True", "False", or "Maybe". 

Please note that this form is not mandatory, and your feedback is greatly appreciated but not required.
