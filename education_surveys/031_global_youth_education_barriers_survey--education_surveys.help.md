# Global Youth Education Barriers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Youth Education Barriers Survey is designed to collect data on the experiences and challenges faced by students in accessing education. This form will help identify the specific obstacles that students face and gather information on the types of support systems in place to help them overcome these barriers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select your language from the options provided.
2. Select your country of residence from the options provided.
3. Select your gender from the options provided.
4. Select your education level from the options provided.
5. Select your field(s) of interest from the options provided.
6. Answer the questions regarding barriers to access to education, support necessary for education, obstacles to attending school, and other challenges you face.
7. Provide any additional information about your school participation, home environment, family background, social background, financial support, health status, personal status, family support, peer support, teacher support, government support, and community support.
8. Finally, provide any other comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Language** (`language`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field allows you to select your primary language. Please select the language you most frequently use or understand best.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required/optional): This field asks for your age. Please enter your age in years.
* **Country of Residence** (`country_of_residence`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the country where you currently reside.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field allows you to select your gender. Please select Male, Female, or Other (Please specify).
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your highest level of education achieved.
* **Field of Interest** (`education_field_of_interest`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the field(s) of interest to you.
* **Barriers to Access to Education** (`barriers_to_access_to_education`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe any barriers you face in accessing education.
* **Support Necessary for Education** (`support_necessary_for_education`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the types of support you require to access education.
* **Obstacles to Attending School** (`obstacles_to_attend_school`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any obstacles you face in attending school.
* **Obstacles to Access to Education** (`obstacles_to_access_to_education`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any obstacles you face in accessing education.
* **Education Support Needed** (`education_support_needed`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any education support you need.
* **School Participation** (`school_participation`, `date`, required/optional): If applicable, select the date of your school participation.
* **Home Environment** (`home_environment`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your home environment.
* **Family Background** (`family_background`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your family background.
* **Social Background** (`social_background`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your social background.
* **Financial Support** (`financial_support`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your financial support.
* **Health Status** (`health_status`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your health status.
* **Personal Status** (`personal_status`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your personal status.
* **Family Support** (`family_support`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your family support.
* **Peer Support** (`peer_support`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your peer support.
* **Teacher Support** (`teacher_support`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your teacher support.
* **Government Support** (`government_support`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your government support.
* **Community Support** (`community_support`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your community support.
* **Other** (`other`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any other information or comments you may have.
