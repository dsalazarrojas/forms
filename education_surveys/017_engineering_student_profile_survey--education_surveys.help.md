# Engineering Student Profile Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Engineering Student Profile Survey is designed to collect demographic and academic information from engineering students for research, program evaluation, or student outreach purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Review and submit the form once you've filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address (e.g., your university email).
* **Major**: Select one or more of the following majors: Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, Civil Engineering. This field is required.
* **Skills**: Describe your relevant skills, if any (e.g., programming languages, software proficiency, etc.).
* **Career Goals**: Describe your short-term and long-term career aspirations and objectives.
* **Academic Program**: Select the academic program you are currently enrolled in (e.g., Bachelor of Science in Engineering, Master of Science in Engineering). This field is required.
* **Department**: If you're part of a specific department at your university, provide the name of the department (e.g., Department of Mechanical Engineering).
* **Advisor**: If you have a research advisor, provide their name (e.g., John Doe, Jane Smith).

Note: If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact the form administrators at form@gic.mx.
