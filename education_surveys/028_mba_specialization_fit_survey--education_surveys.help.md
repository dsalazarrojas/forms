# MBA Specialization Fit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help admission officers understand your background, experience, and motivation for pursuing an MBA program. Your responses will be used to assess your suitability for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section as directed, providing accurate and detailed responses where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What is MBA Specialization Fit Survey** (`introduction`, `text`, optional): This is an introductory page to the form. Please provide a brief description of the form's purpose.
* **2. What is your background** (`background`, `text`, required): Describe your relevant work experience, education, or other relevant background that makes you a strong candidate for the program.
* **3. What is your work experience** (`experience`, `text`, optional): Describe your work experience, including any relevant experience related to your desired specialization.
* **4. What are your interests** (`interests`, `text`, optional): List your interests and hobbies that align with the program's focus.
* **5. What motivates you** (`motivation`, `text`, required): Describe what motivates you to pursue an MBA program, and how it aligns with your goals and aspirations.
* **6. What are your skills** (`skills`, `text`, optional): List your relevant skills and qualifications that make you a strong candidate for the program.
* **7. What specialization** (`specialization`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the available specializations that align with your interests and goals.
* **8. Where do you live** (`location`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all locations where you have lived or plan to live.
* **9. When do you plan to graduate** (`graduation_date`, `date`, optional): Enter your expected graduation date from the program.
* **10. What is your highest level of education completed** (`education`, `select_one`, required): Choose your highest level of education completed.
* **11. Have you had any work experience related to your desired specialization** (`previous_experience`, `select_multiple`, required): Answer whether you have had relevant work experience.
* **12. Which schools have you previously attended** (`previous_schools_attended`, `select_multiple`, optional): List any schools you have previously attended.
* **13. Which certifications or credentials do you have** (`previous_certifications`, `select_multiple`, optional): List any relevant certifications or credentials you have.
* **14. Do you have any additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your application.
* **15. What is your email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **16. What is your phone number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **17. What is your address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address.
* **18. What is your zip** (`zip`, `text`, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **19. What is your state** (`state`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your state of residence.
* **20. What is your country** (`country`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your country of residence.
* **21. What is your timezone** (`timezone`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your timezone.
* **22. What is your preferred language** (`language`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred language of communication.
* **23. What is your gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Choose your gender.
* **24. What is your date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **25. Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): This is the final step to submit your application.

Please take your time to carefully review each section and provide detailed responses where possible. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
