# Urbanization And Agriculture Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Urbanization And Agriculture Survey is a form designed to collect information on the effects of urbanization on agriculture in urban areas. This form is used to gather data on various aspects of urbanization, including its impact on agriculture, education, and local planning. The survey is intended to help understand the relationship between urbanization and agriculture and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and select the appropriate response from the options provided.
2. Make sure to answer all required questions, indicated by an asterisk (\*) after the question label.
3. For select multiple and select one questions, select as many or as few options as apply to your situation.
4. Use the note field to provide any additional information or comments that may be helpful in understanding your responses.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required questions to ensure accurate data collection.
* Select all options that apply to your situation for each question.
* If you are unsure about any question, please contact your supervisor or administrator for guidance.
* The survey is designed to be completed by one person per household. If multiple people need to complete the survey, please make sure to use a separate form for each person.
