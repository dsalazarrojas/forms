# Educational Institution History Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about educational institutions, specifically about students and staff. It is intended to track and analyze data about students and staff within educational institutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and provide accurate information about the educational institution.
2. Enter your student information (name, degree level, etc.) in the "Student Information" section.
3. Provide your staff information (department, position, etc.) in the "Staff Information" sections.
4. Enter attendance records for students and staff in the relevant sections.
5. Review your input carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (2)**: Enter your name and other relevant student information in this text field.
* **Degree Level (3)**: Choose the degree level that applies to you as a student (High or Low).
* **Staff Information (4)**: Enter your staff information in this text field.
* **Degree Level (5)**: Choose the degree level that applies to you as staff (High or Low).
* **Department (7)**: Check 'Yes' if you work in a department, otherwise check 'No'.
* **Position (8)**: Enter your staff position in this text field.
* **Institution Information (9)**: Enter the name of the institution you are affiliated with.
* **Student Attendance (10)**: Enter the date of your first day of attendance as a student.
* **Staff Attendance (11)**: Enter the date of your first day of attendance as a staff member.
* **Alumni Attendance (12)**: Enter the date of your last day of attendance as an alumnus/alumna.

## Tips
* Please enter accurate and complete information about your student and staff records.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can ask for help from the relevant authorities.
* Double-check your input before submitting the form.
* If you have completed this form previously, make sure to review your input for any changes or updates before resubmitting.
