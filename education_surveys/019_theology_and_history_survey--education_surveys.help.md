# Theology And History Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your background, interests, and preferences related to theology and history. The data collected will be used to gain insights into your understanding and engagement with various subjects.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each page carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2.  Ensure you provide the most accurate and relevant information possible.
3.  Complete each field according to your actual background, interests, and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Background**: This section provides a space for you to note any additional comments or information about your background that you feel is relevant to the questions that follow.
* **Demographic Info**: This field allows you to indicate whether you are a theology student, educator, or someone with a background in history.
* **Contact Info**: This section provides a space for you to indicate whether you have any contact information that you would like to share with us.
* **Theologies Studied**: This field allows you to indicate which theologies you have studied or are familiar with.
* **Favorite Subject**: This field allows you to indicate which subject you are most interested in, such as art, history, theology, or music.
* **Educational Background**: This field allows you to indicate your educational background related to history or theology.
* **Church Historic Event**: This field allows you to indicate whether you are familiar with a specific historic event in the church.
* **Historical Period**: This field allows you to indicate which historical period you are most interested in or familiar with.
* **Favorite Book**: This field allows you to indicate the book you are most interested in or have read recently.
* **Favorite Movie**: This field allows you to indicate the movie that you enjoyed most or learned something from.
* **Favorite Book Author**: This field allows you to indicate the author of your favorite book.
* **Favorite Movie Director**: This field allows you to indicate the director of your favorite movie.
* **Historical Period**: This field allows you to confirm your previous answer about the historical period.
* **Church Leadership**: This field allows you to indicate whether you are familiar with or have experience with church leadership.
* **Favorite Artist**: This field allows you to indicate the artist that you enjoy or admire most.
* **Favorite Composer**: This field allows you to indicate the composer that you enjoy or admire most.
* **Survey Completion**: This field allows you to confirm that you have completed the form and provide any further comments.

## Tips

* Be honest and truthful in your answers.
* Provide the most relevant and accurate information possible.
* You can provide additional comments or questions in the "Survey Background" section.
* If you have any further comments or questions after completing this form, you can provide them in the "Survey Completion" field.
