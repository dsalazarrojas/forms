# Virtual Hardware Tutor Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with the Virtual Hardware Tutor, a training or educational program on virtualization and hardware-related topics. The goal of this survey is to understand the users' satisfaction, learning impact, and any technical issues they encountered during the course.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the survey questions carefully and answer them accordingly.
2. Select the best response for each question, taking into account the options provided.
3. Fill in your email address in the "Email" field if you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions.
4. Provide your phone number, if you wish to be reached via phone.
5. If you wish to submit a specific date or time for your experience, enter it in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
6. Use the "Note" field to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
7. Finally, click the "Submit your response" button to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to our survey**: This is a brief introduction page that welcomes you to the survey and provides a brief overview of what to expect.
* **How satisfied are you with the course?**: This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the course. Select one of the options provided:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **What did you learn from the course?**: This question asks about your learning experience. Select all that apply:
	+ Learned a lot
	+ Learned a little
	+ Didn't learn much
	+ Didn't learn at all
* **Were there any technical issues during the course?**: This question asks about any technical difficulties you faced during the course. Select all that apply:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Not sure
* **Email**: If you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions, enter your email address here.
* **Phone**: If you wish to be reached via phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Date**: If you wish to specify a date for your experience, enter it here.
* **Time**: If you wish to specify a time for your experience, enter it here.
* **Note**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by individual users, and the fields are optional unless otherwise specified as required.
