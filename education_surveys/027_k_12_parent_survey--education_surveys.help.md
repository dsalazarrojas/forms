# K 12 Parent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from K-12 parents about their child's educational experience in the school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If a question does not apply to your situation, please indicate this and explain why.
3. If you have any additional feedback or comments, please use the "Any additional feedback you would like to share with the school" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your overall impression of this school?** (`classroom_experience`, text, required: false)
This question asks for your overall impression of the school, including any positive or negative experiences your child has had.
* **What is the level of communication between you and your child's teacher?** (`communication`, text, required: false)
This question is about how often you and your child's teacher communicate with each other, such as through emails, phone calls, or parent-teacher conferences.
* **What is the quality of the teacher's teaching style?** (`teacher_quality`, text, required: false)
This question is about how well the teacher engages your child and presents the material, and whether the teacher is able to meet your child's needs.
* **How satisfied are you with the level of learning your child has experienced?** (`student_learning`, number, required: false)
This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the amount of learning your child has achieved in the school.
* **How satisfied are you with the support your child receives from the teacher?** (`parent_support`, number, required: false)
This question is about how often you feel your child receives the support they need from the teacher.
* **How often do you have opportunities to volunteer or participate in your child's education?** (`parent_involvement`, text, required: false)
This question is about how frequently you are involved in your child's educational activities and events.
* **How often do you have opportunities to communicate with teachers or staff?** (`teacher_involvement`, text, required: false)
This question is about how often you are able to communicate with teachers or school staff.
* **Any additional feedback you would like to share with the school?** (`feedback`, note, required: false)
This is an open-ended question where you can provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your child's educational experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
Please enter your email address if you would like to be contacted by the school about your feedback.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, required: false)
Please enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by the school about your feedback.
* **How often do you think teachers are present for the whole class?** (`teacher_attendance`, text, required: false)
This question is about how often you think teachers attend classes regularly.
* **How often do you think teachers offer support to students?** (`teacher_support`, text, required: false)
This question is about how often you think teachers offer support to students.
* **How satisfied are you with the teacher's quality of teaching?** (`teacher_quality`, number, required: false)
This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the quality of teaching provided by your child's teacher.
