# Student Centered Learning Experiment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from educators and students about their participation in a Student Centered Learning Experiment. The goal of this form is to gather relevant details about each project, including project details, teacher and student information, contact information, and project objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields (indicated by a asterisk * symbol) and provide as much detail as possible for optional fields.
3. Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (Project Details, text, *): This is the main title of your project. Please provide a concise and descriptive name that accurately represents your project.
* **Teacher Info** (Teacher Info, text): Enter the name and contact information of the teacher in charge of the project.
* **Student Info** (Student Info, text): Enter the name and contact information of the student participating in the project.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text): Provide the contact information of the person responsible for the project, including name and email address.
* **Project Location** (Project Location, text): Enter the location where the project will take place (e.g., school, university, online).
* **Project Type** (Project Type, select_one): Select one of the options provided to indicate the type of project (e.g., Research, Experiment, Pilot Project).
* **School Year** (School Year, number): Enter the year the project will take place.
* **Project Description** (Project Description, text): Provide a brief description of the project, including its main objectives and goals.
* **Project Budget** (Project Budget, number): Enter the budget allocated for the project.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the project.
* **Email** (Email, email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (Phone, text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Project Start Date** (Project Start Date, date): Enter the date when the project will start.
* **Project End Date** (Project End Date, date): Enter the date when the project will end.
* **Project Hours** (Project Hours, time): Enter the total hours allocated for the project.
* **Project Goals** (Project Goals, text): Describe the goals of the project, including what it hopes to achieve.
* **Project Objectives** (Project Objectives, text): Describe the objectives of the project, including what it aims to accomplish.
* **Project Outcomes** (Project Outcomes, text): Describe the expected outcomes of the project.
* **Student Participation** (Student Participation, text): Describe the level of student participation in the project, including how students will be involved.
