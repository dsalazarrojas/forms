# Sustainable Fashion Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainable Fashion Awareness Survey is a research tool designed to gather information on individuals' attitudes and behaviors regarding sustainable fashion practices. Your participation will contribute to improving the industry's sustainability and customer understanding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the corresponding text or select fields.
2. Ensure you fill out all required fields (marked as *required*).
3. For select fields, choose the option that best represents your attitude or behavior.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, *required*): A brief introduction to this survey.
* **Sustainable Fashion Attitudes** (`sustainable_fashion_attitudes`, text, *required*): Describe your attitude towards sustainable fashion.
* **Fashion Consumption Habits** (`fashion_consumption_habits`, text, *required*): Share your habits related to sustainable fashion consumption.
* **Personal Importance of Sustainability** (`personal_importance_sustainability`, select_one, *required*): Choose how important sustainability is to you.
	+ *Sustainable fashion is very important to me* (Very important)
	+ *Sustainability is somewhat important to me* (Somewhat important)
	+ *Not at all important* (Not important)
* **Sustainable Practices** (`sustainability_practices`, select_multiple, *required*): Select how often you practice sustainable fashion.
	+ *Rarely* (Rarely)
	+ *Sometimes* (Sometimes)
	+ *Often* (Often)
	+ *Very often* (Very often)
* **Sustainable Lifestyle Change** (`sustainable_lifestyle_change`, time, *optional*): If you are willing to make a change in your lifestyle for the sake of sustainability, specify a timeframe (e.g., in months).
* **Fashion Brand Awareness** (`fashion_brand_awareness`, text, *optional*): Describe your awareness of sustainable fashion brands.
* **Sustainable Fashion Knowledge** (`sustainable_fashion_knowledge`, select_one, *optional*): Choose your level of knowledge about sustainable fashion.
	+ *High* (High)
	+ *Medium* (Medium)
	+ *Low* (Low)
* **Sustainability Influencers** (`sustainability_influencers`, select_one, *optional*): Choose how likely you are to follow sustainability influencers.
	+ *Very likely* (Very likely)
	+ *Somewhat likely* (Somewhat likely)
	+ *Not very likely* (Not very likely)
	+ *Not at all likely* (Not at all likely)
* **Email** (`email`, email, *required*): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, *required*): Your phone number.
* **Age** (`age`, number, *required*): Enter your age.
* **Income** (`income`, number, *required*): Enter your income.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, *required*): Enter your occupation.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, *required*): Choose your education level.
	+ *High school or below* (High school or below)
	+ *Some college or below* (Some college or below)
	+ *Bachelor's degree* (Bachelor's degree)
	+ *Master's degree* (Master's degree)
	+ *Doctoral degree or above* (Doctoral degree or above)
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, *required*): Choose your country of residence.
	+ *USA* (USA)
	+ *Canada* (Canada)
	+ *Mexico* (Mexico)
	+ *Other* (Other)
* **Region** (`region`, select_one, *required*): Choose your region of residence.
	+ *Northeast* (Northeast)
	+ *Midwest* (Midwest)
	+ *South* (South)
	+ *West* (West)
	+ *Other* (Other)
* **State** (`state`, select_one, *required*): Choose your state of residence (for USA and Canada).
	+ *Yes* (Yes)
	+ *No* (No)
* **City** (`city`, text, *required*): Enter your city of residence.
* **Address** (`address`, text, *required*): Enter your address.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, *required*): Enter your zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, *required*): Enter your date of birth.
* **How Did You Hear About This Survey?** (`how_did_you_hear_about_this_survey`, text, *optional*): Describe how you found out about this survey.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, *required*): Choose your consent to participate.
	+ *Yes, I consent to participate in this research study* (Yes, I consent to participate in this research study)
	+ *No, I do not consent to participate in this research study* (No, I do not consent to participate in this research study)
* **Survey End** (`survey_end`, select_one, *optional*): Indicate your completion status.
	+ *Complete* (Complete)
	+ *Partially complete* (Partially complete)

Please submit your completed survey to help us better understand sustainable fashion practices and attitudes. Your participation is greatly appreciated.

**Tips**:
- If you have trouble completing any field, please refer to the field explanations above.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- Thank you for your contribution to this sustainable fashion awareness survey!
