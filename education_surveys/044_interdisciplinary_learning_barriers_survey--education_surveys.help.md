# Interdisciplinary Learning Barriers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Interdisciplinary Learning Barriers Survey is designed to collect information on the obstacles students face when learning in an interdisciplinary context. This form is used to gather valuable insights to better understand these challenges and develop solutions to improve learning outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into multiple sections, each focusing on different aspects of interdisciplinary learning barriers.
2. Please select or input your answers based on your personal experiences and observations.
3. For questions with options, choose the option that best describes your answer.
4. For text fields, provide brief but detailed descriptions or answers.
5. Review each section carefully to ensure you've completed the form accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Section 1**: This section asks you to select one option that describes a common barrier you've encountered in an interdisciplinary learning setting.
	+ Option 1: Time constraints due to multiple assignments or projects.
	+ Option 2: Lack of clear instructions or guidelines.
	+ Option 3: Difficulty in understanding relationships between subjects.
* **Section 2**: In this section, you can select multiple options that describe the barriers you've faced in an interdisciplinary learning setting.
	+ Option 1: Difficulty in coordinating or scheduling with team members.
	+ Option 2: Limited access to resources or tools.
	+ Option 3: Insufficient feedback from instructors or peers.
* **Section 3**: Please input your date of experience in an interdisciplinary learning setting.
* **Section 4**: This section asks you to input the time you've dedicated to an interdisciplinary project or assignment.
* **Section 5**: Provide a brief note describing any additional insights or comments you have about your experiences.
* **Section 6**: Input your email address for any follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Section 7-23**: These sections are text fields for you to describe your experiences and observations in detail.

Note: Please ensure you've completed all sections accurately and thoroughly to provide valuable insights. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
