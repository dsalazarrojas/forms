# Classroom Observation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a classroom observation, focusing on various aspects of the teacher's performance, student behavior, and classroom environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, providing your name, role, and position or title as the observer.
2. Provide the name of the teacher being observed, the subject area of the class, and the grade level of the students.
3. Choose the correct date for the classroom observation.
4. Rate the classroom environment, student knowledge demonstration, student skills demonstration, student behavior, class management effectiveness, instructional quality, and overall impression using the scale provided.
5. Describe the teacher's strengths that you observed during the session.
6. Identify areas that need improvement.
7. Provide any additional feedback or comments about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Observer Name**: Your name as the observer conducting the assessment.
* **Observer Role**: Your position or title within the school or organization.
	+ School Administrator: A school administrator, such as a principal or vice principal.
	+ District Evaluator: An evaluator from the school district or regional office.
	+ External Reviewer: An external reviewer from a third-party organization.
	+ Peer Teacher: A teacher from the same school or institution.
	+ Accreditation Panel Member: A member of an accreditation panel.
	+ Other: If you don't see your role listed, choose "Other" and specify your position or title.
* **Teacher Observed**: The name of the teacher whose class was observed.
* **Class Subject**: The subject area of the class being observed.
* **Grade Level**: The grade level of the students being observed.
	+ Kindergarten: Students in kindergarten.
	+ Grade 1-2: Students in grades 1 and 2.
	+ Grade 3-5: Students in grades 3, 4, and 5.
	+ Grade 6-8: Students in grades 6, 7, and 8.
	+ Grade 9-10: Students in grades 9 and 10.
	+ Grade 11-12: Students in grades 11 and 12.
* **Observation Date**: The date of the classroom observation.
* **Classroom Environment Rating**: Rate the physical and emotional classroom environment as:
	+ Excellent - Very conducive to learning.
	+ Good - Generally positive environment.
	+ Fair - Adequate but needs improvement.
	+ Poor - Not conducive to learning.
* **Student Knowledge Demonstration**: Rate how well students demonstrated understanding of content as:
	+ Excellent - Strong understanding shown.
	+ Good - Adequate understanding.
	+ Fair - Limited understanding.
	+ Poor - Minimal understanding demonstrated.
* **Student Skills Demonstration**: Rate how well students applied skills during the lesson as:
	+ Excellent - Skills applied effectively.
	+ Good - Skills applied adequately.
	+ Fair - Skills application inconsistent.
	+ Poor - Limited skills application.
* **Student Behavior Rating**: Rate overall student behavior during the observation as:
	+ Excellent - Exemplary behavior throughout.
	+ Good - Generally appropriate behavior.
	+ Fair - Some behavioral issues.
	+ Poor - Significant behavioral concerns.
* **Class Management Effectiveness**: Rate the teacher's classroom management skills as:
	+ Excellent - Exceptional management.
	+ Good - Effective management.
	+ Fair - Management needs improvement.
	+ Poor - Ineffective management.
* **Instructional Quality**: Rate the overall quality of instruction as:
	+ Excellent - Outstanding instruction.
	+ Good - Solid instruction.
	+ Fair - Instruction needs improvement.
	+ Poor - Ineffective instruction.
* **Overall Impression**: Your overall impression of the class session as:
	+ Excellent - Exceeded expectations.
	+ Good - Met expectations.
	+ Fair - Partially met expectations.
	+ Poor - Did not meet expectations.
* **Strengths Observed**: Describe the teacher's strengths observed during the session.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Identify areas that need improvement.
* **Additional Feedback**: Provide any additional comments or recommendations.
* **Additional Feedback (Note: Optional)**: This field is not required; provide any additional feedback or comments that may be relevant.

## Tips
- Be as specific and concrete as possible when completing the form.
- Use the provided scale for ratings and assessments.
- Do not leave any fields blank without providing a reason or explanation.
- Provide thoughtful and constructive feedback in the "Strengths Observed" and "Areas for Improvement" fields.
- Use the "Additional Feedback" field to provide any additional comments or recommendations.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
