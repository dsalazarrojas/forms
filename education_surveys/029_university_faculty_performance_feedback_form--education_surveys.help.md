## Purpose
This form is a tool to help instructors improve their teaching performance and effectiveness.

1. Enter the `course_id` of the course for which you are providing feedback.
2. Enter the name of the instructor you are providing feedback about.
3. Enter the name of the course you are taking.

15. Rate the level of student support.

23. Rate your course satisfaction.
24. Rate your instructor satisfaction.
25. Rate the level of student participation in the course.
