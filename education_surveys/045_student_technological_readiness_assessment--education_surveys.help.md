# Student Technological Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Technological Readiness Assessment is a form designed to evaluate a student's technological readiness in a specific learning environment. It is used to assess their ability to use technology effectively and identify areas where they may need support or training.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question truthfully, based on your current technological capabilities and needs.
- Make sure to review each page carefully before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any question, please ask for help or clarification before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Device Access** (`device_access`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select all the devices you have access to, such as Mobile Phone, Desktop Computer, Laptop, Tablet, or Other. Please choose all the devices that apply to you.
* **Internet Reliability** (`internet_reliability`, number, required): This question asks you to rate the reliability of your internet connection, with options ranging from 0 (Very Slow) to 10 (Very Fast).
* **Digital Confidence** (`digital_confidence`, select_one, required): This question asks you to select your level of digital confidence, with options Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Age of Devices** (`device_age`, number, required): This question asks you to report the age of your devices, with the option to select a specific age in years.
* **Support Resources** (`support_resources`, note, required): This question is intended to be a free-text field where you can list any support resources you have access to, such as online tutorials or help desk.
* **Teacher Support** (`teacher_support`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select how much support you receive from your teachers, with options High, Medium, or Low.
* **Student Support** (`student_support`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select how much support you receive from other students, with options High, Medium, or Low.
* **Device Issues** (`device_issues`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select any issues you are experiencing with your devices, such as No Issues, Minor Issues, or Major Issues.
* **Online Learning Opportunities** (`online_learning`, select_one, required): This question asks you to select whether or not you have access to online learning opportunities.
* **Blended Learning Opportunities** (`blended_learning`, select_one, required): This question asks you to select whether or not you have access to blended learning opportunities.
* **Other Device Issues** (`other_device_issues`, note, required): This question is intended to be a free-text field where you can list any other issues you are experiencing with your devices.
* **Type of Device** (`device_type`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select the type of devices you use, with options Mobile Phone, Desktop Computer, Laptop, or Tablet.
* **Condition of Device** (`device_condition`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select the condition of your devices, with options New, Used, or Refurbished.
* **Internet Speed** (`internet_speed`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select the speed of your internet connection, with options Fast, Medium, or Slow.
* **Online Support** (`online_support`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select whether or not you have access to online support.
* **Technical Issues** (`technical_issues`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select any technical issues you are experiencing, such as Hardware Issues or Software Issues.
* **Network Issues** (`network_issues`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select any network issues you are experiencing, such as Connection Issues or Slow Speed.
* **Other Issues** (`other_issues`, note, required): This question is intended to be a free-text field where you can list any other issues you are experiencing.
* **Teacher Experience** (`teacher_experience`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select how much experience you have with your teachers, with options High, Medium, or Low.
* **Student Experience** (`student_experience`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select how much experience you have with your peers, with options High, Medium, or Low.
* **Teacher Training** (`teacher_training`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select how much training your teachers have received, with options High, Medium, or Low.
* **Teacher Support Training** (`teacher_support_training`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select how much support training your teachers have received, with options High, Medium, or Low.
* **Student Support Training** (`student_support_training`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select how much support training you have received, with options High, Medium, or Low.
* **Teacher Guidance** (`teacher_guidance`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select how much guidance you receive from your teachers, with options High, Medium, or Low.
* **Student Guidance** (`student_guidance`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select how much guidance you receive from your peers, with options High, Medium, or Low.
