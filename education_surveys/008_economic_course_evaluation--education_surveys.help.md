# Economic Course Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and evaluation from course stakeholders on the overall quality, content, and effectiveness of the economic course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Course Title**, which should be a brief description of the course.
2. Enter the **Course ID**, which is a unique identifier for the course.
3. Provide the **Instructor Name**, who taught the course.
4. Describe the **Course Outcome**, what did the course achieve?
5. Share your **Student Feedback**, any thoughts or comments about the course.
6. If applicable, provide a **Suggestion for Improvement**, any ideas you have to enhance the course.
7. Select the **Category**, where the course fits in the economic curriculum.
8. Finally, provide your **Submitted By** information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Title**: A brief description of the course. This field is optional and can be a maximum of 255 characters.
* **Course ID**: A unique identifier for the course, usually a number or alphanumeric code.
* **Instructor Name**: The person who taught the course.
* **Course Outcome**: Describe the outcome or result of the course, what did the course achieve? This field is optional.
* **Student Feedback**: Share your thoughts or comments about the course. This field is optional but provides valuable insights.
* **Suggestion for Improvement**: If you have any ideas on how to improve the course, share them here. This field is optional.
* **Category**: Select the category where the course fits in the economic curriculum. This field has two options: 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Submitted By**: This field is for administrative purposes and is not editable. Please do not fill out this field.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when describing the course title and course outcome.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance.
* The form is meant to be filled out by course evaluators, please ensure you have the necessary permissions or approvals to complete the form.
