# Rooftop Farming Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from individuals involved in rooftop farming to gain insights into the challenges and benefits of this innovative agricultural practice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability, providing detailed and concise responses.
2. Select the options that apply to your experience and practices.
3. If you have any other methods, challenges, or benefits that are not listed, please provide a brief description in the corresponding text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rooftop Farming Survey** (Page 1): This is the title of the form, which is used to identify the purpose and scope of the survey.
* **How long have you been rooftop farming?** (Page 2): Please respond with the number of years you have been involved in rooftop farming.
* **years** (Page 3): If you have been rooftop farming for less than 1 year, please enter 0.
* **What are the primary methods used for rooftop farming?** (Page 4): Select all the methods you use for rooftop farming, such as Rooftop farming, Community garden, Aquaponics, Hydroponics, and Aeroponics.
* **Other methods** (Page 5): If you use other methods not listed, please provide a brief description in this text field.
* **What are the biggest challenges you've faced?** (Page 6): Please provide a detailed description of the challenges you have experienced in rooftop farming.
* **Other challenges** (Page 7): If you have other challenges not listed, please provide a brief description in this text field.
* **What are the benefits of rooftop farming?** (Page 8): Select all the benefits you have experienced or observed, such as Improved soil quality, Increased crop yields, Reduced water usage, Improved air quality, and Increased food security.
* **Other benefits** (Page 9): If you have other benefits not listed, please provide a brief description in this text field.
* **Has your research on rooftop farming led to any new projects or initiatives?** (Page 10): Please respond with True or False, depending on whether your research has led to any new projects or initiatives.
* **Projects or initiatives** (Page 11): If you have any projects or initiatives, please provide a brief description in this text field.
* **Have you participated in any community programs related to rooftop farming?** (Page 12): Please respond with True or False, depending on whether you have participated in any community programs.
* **Other programs** (Page 13): If you have participated in other programs not listed, please provide a brief description in this text field.
* **Have you contributed to urban agriculture planning or policy making for your city or region?** (Page 14): Please respond with True or False, depending on whether you have contributed to urban agriculture planning or policy making.
* **Other contributions** (Page 15): If you have contributed to other initiatives not listed, please provide a brief description in this text field.
* **Is your data open to the public?** (Page 16): Please respond with True or False, depending on whether your data is open to the public.
* **Other data availability** (Page 17): If you have other data availability not listed, please provide a brief description in this text field.
* **Open license** (Page 18): Please select the open license you use, such as Creative Commons or Open Data Commons.
* **Other** (Page 19): If you use other open licenses not listed, please provide a brief description in this text field.
* **Comments** (Page 20): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in this text field.
* **Email** (Page 21): Please provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone number** (Page 22): If you have a phone number, please provide it for contact purposes.
* **Location** (Page 23): If you would like to share your location, please provide it in this text field.
* **Website** (Page 24): If you have a website, please provide the URL.
* **Attachments** (Page 25): If you have any attachments or supporting documents, please provide them in this text field.
