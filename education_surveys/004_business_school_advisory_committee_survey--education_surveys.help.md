# Business School Advisory Committee Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback from Business School Advisory Committee members on various aspects of the school.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Select the most appropriate option for each question.
3. Provide detailed answers when prompted in the text fields.
4. Review your responses carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required)
Your name as a member of the Business School Advisory Committee.

### 2. **Your Role on the Advisory Committee** (`committee_role`, `select_one`, required)
Select your primary role on the committee, such as Chair, Vice Chair, Member, Corporate Representative, or Alumni Representative.

### 3. **Organization or Company Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, optional)
If you represent an organization or company, enter it here. Otherwise, leave this field blank.

### 4. **How long have you been on this committee?** (`tenure_years`, `number`, optional)
Enter the number of years you've been on the committee.

### 5. **How familiar are you with our business programs?** (`program_familiarity`, `select_one`, required)
Rate your level of familiarity with our programs: Very Familiar, Familiar, Somewhat Familiar, Not Very Familiar, or Not at all Familiar.

### 6. **How relevant is our curriculum to current industry needs?** (`curriculum_relevance`, `select_one`, required)
Rate the relevance of our curriculum to current industry needs: Highly Relevant, Relevant, Neutral, Needs Improvement, or Outdated.

### 7. **How well-prepared are our graduates for industry roles?** (`student_readiness`, `select_one`, required)
Rate the preparation of our graduates for industry roles: Exceptionally prepared, Well prepared, Adequately prepared, Somewhat unprepared, or Not prepared.

### 8. **What are the main curriculum gaps?** (`curriculum_gaps`, `select_multiple`, required)
Select all the gaps in our curriculum that you believe need improvement: Technology and digital skills, Leadership skills, Communication skills, Data analytics, Entrepreneurship, Sustainability and ethics, Global business perspective, or No significant gaps.

### 9. **What skills should be prioritized in our programs?** (`skill_priorities`, `select_multiple`, required)
Select the top three skills that should be prioritized in our programs: Critical thinking, Problem solving, Teamwork and collaboration, Project management, Financial analysis, Strategic planning, Digital literacy, or Communication.

### 10. **Should we increase industry partnerships and internships?** (`industry_partnerships`, `select_one`, required)
Select your opinion on increasing industry partnerships and internships: Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Unsure, Probably No, or Definitely No.

### 11. **How would you rate the quality of instruction?** (`teaching_quality`, `select_one`, required)
Rate the quality of instruction: Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, or Poor.

### 12. **What feedback do you have for faculty development?** (`faculty_feedback`, `text`, optional)
Enter any feedback you have for the faculty development.

### 13. **How satisfied are you with graduate employment outcomes?** (`graduate_outcomes`, `select_one`, required)
Rate your satisfaction with graduate employment outcomes: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.

### 14. **What are our program strengths?** (`program_strengths`, `text`, optional)
Enter any strengths you see in our programs.

### 15. **What areas need improvement?** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, optional)
Enter any areas that need improvement in our programs.

### 16. **How effective is this advisory committee?** (`committee_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required)
Rate the effectiveness of the advisory committee: Very effective, Effective, Neutral, Ineffective, or Not effective.

### 17. **What is your top recommendation for the school?** (`recommendation_for_school`, `text`, optional)
Enter your top recommendation for the school.

### 18. **Will you continue involvement with the committee?** (`continued_involvement`, `select_one`, required)
Select your level of commitment to the committee: Yes, definitely, Probably yes, Unsure, Probably no, or False.
