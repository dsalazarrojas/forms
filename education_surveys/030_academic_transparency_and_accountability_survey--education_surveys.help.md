# Academic Transparency And Accountability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Transparency and Accountability Survey is designed to gather feedback from members of the institution on their experiences and perceptions of transparency and accountability within the institution. This survey aims to collect data on decision-making processes, communication, and leadership accountability.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct option for "Your role at the institution" from the dropdown menu. This will determine which questions you'll be asked to answer based on your affiliation with the institution.
2. Choose the duration of your time at the institution from the dropdown menu for "Years at this institution".
3. Select your answer to the following questions:
	* "How transparent do you find institutional decision-making processes?" (select one option from the dropdown menu)
	* "How easy is it to access information about institutional policies, budgets, and decisions?" (select one option from the dropdown menu)
	* "How effectively does the institution communicate important changes and decisions?" (select one option from the dropdown menu)
	* "How accountable do you believe institutional leadership is to the campus community?" (select one option from the dropdown menu)
	* "How fair are the processes for addressing complaints and grievances?" (select one option from the dropdown menu)
	* "Which areas need the most improvement in transparency?" (select all that apply from the dropdown menu)
	* "Do you feel you have adequate opportunities to provide input on institutional decisions?" (select one option from the dropdown menu)
	* "How much do you trust the institution to act in the best interest of its community?" (select one option from the dropdown menu)
	* "Overall, how satisfied are you with the institution's transparency and accountability?" (select one option from the dropdown menu)
4. If you have any suggestions for improving transparency and accountability, please provide them in the text box under "What specific steps could the institution take to improve transparency and accountability".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your role at the institution** (`respondent_role`, select_one, required): 
	+ Please select your current role within the institution, such as Student, Faculty, Staff, Administrator, or Alumni.
* **Department or unit** (`department`, text, required): 
	+ Enter the name of the department or unit you are affiliated with.
* **Years at this institution** (`years_at_institution`, select_one, required): 
	+ Select the duration of your time at the institution.
* **How transparent do you find institutional decision-making processes** (`transparency_decision_making`, select_one, required): 
	+ Choose how you perceive the level of transparency in decision-making processes, such as Not at all transparent, Slightly transparent, Moderately transparent, Very transparent, or Extremely transparent.
* **How easy is it to access information about institutional policies, budgets, and decisions** (`access_to_information`, select_one, required): 
	+ Select how easy or difficult you find it to access information, such as Very difficult, Difficult, Neutral, Easy, or Very easy.
* **How effectively does the institution communicate important changes and decisions** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, required): 
	+ Choose how effectively you think the institution communicates important changes and decisions, such as Very ineffectively, Ineffectively, Neutral, Effectively, or Very effectively.
* **How accountable do you believe institutional leadership is to the campus community** (`accountability_leadership`, select_one, required): 
	+ Select how accountable you think institutional leadership is to the campus community, such as Not at all accountable, Slightly accountable, Moderately accountable, Very accountable, or Extremely accountable.
* **How fair are the processes for addressing complaints and grievances** (`fairness_processes`, select_one, required): 
	+ Choose how fair you think the processes are for addressing complaints and grievances, such as Very unfair, Somewhat unfair, Neutral, Somewhat fair, or Very fair.
* **Which areas need the most improvement in transparency** (`areas_needing_improvement`, select_multiple, required): 
	+ Select all areas that you think need improvement in transparency, such as Budget allocation and spending, Hiring and promotion decisions, Academic policy changes, Student disciplinary processes, Strategic planning, or Resource distribution.
* **Do you feel you have adequate opportunities to provide input on institutional decisions** (`input_opportunities`, select_one, required): 
	+ Choose whether you feel you have ample, limited, or no meaningful opportunities to provide input, such as Yes - ample opportunities, Somewhat - limited opportunities, or No - no meaningful opportunities.
* **How much do you trust the institution to act in the best interest of its community** (`trust_level`, select_one, required): 
	+ Select how much trust you have in the institution, such as No trust at all, Low trust, Moderate trust, High trust, or Complete trust.
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with the institution's transparency and accountability** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): 
	+ Choose how satisfied you are with the institution's transparency and accountability, such as Very dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, or Very satisfied.
* **What specific steps could the institution take to improve transparency and accountability** (`suggestions`, text, optional): 
	+ Provide any suggestions you have for improving transparency and accountability within the institution.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately based on your experiences and perceptions.
* Select "Select all that apply" for the "Which areas need the most improvement in transparency" question to choose multiple options.
* Use the space provided under "What specific steps could the institution take to improve transparency and accountability" to provide detailed suggestions.
