# music_career_guidance_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music career guidance survey aims to collect data on music career goals and aspirations from respondents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully to understand the questions and options provided.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, or type your own response if none of the options match your answer.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
4. Provide honest and accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1, note, required: false): This is a note field where you can provide any additional comments or context about your music career journey. It is not required, but it can help us better understand your background.
* **Education Background** (2, select_one, required: false): Please select the level of education you have attained in music, such as Music School, College, or University.
* **Current Occupation** (3, select_multiple, required: false): If you are currently employed, please select all the occupations related to your current role. You can choose one or more from the options provided:
	+ Music Teacher
	+ Music Therapist
	+ Singer/Songwriter
* **Instrument** (4, select_one, required: false): Please select the instrument(s) you play or have experience playing. You can choose one or more from the options provided:
	+ Piano
	+ Guitar
	+ Violin
* **Years of Experience** (5, number, required: true): Please provide the number of years you have been working in the music industry or have experience with music.
* **Next Steps** (6, text, required: true): Please describe your next steps in your music career, such as seeking further education, gaining more experience, or achieving specific goals.
* **Music Career Goal** (7, select_one, required: true): Please select one or more of the career goals that best describe your aspirations. You can choose one or more from the options provided:
	+ Become a Music Teacher
	+ Become a Music Therapist
	+ Become an Artist
	+ Other
* **Music Career Goal Other** (8, text, required: false): If you selected "Other" for Music Career Goal, please provide a brief description of your alternative career goal.
* **Contact Details** (9, select_multiple, required: true): Please provide your contact details, such as phone number, email, or website. You can choose one or more from the options provided:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Website
* **Program Interest** (10, select_one, required: false): If applicable, please select the program that interests you, such as High School Program, College Program, or University Program.
* **Form Comments** (11, text, required: false): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about this form or your music career journey.
