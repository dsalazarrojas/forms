# Curriculum Impact On Values Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the impact of various values on the curriculum and to identify the most relevant and beneficial values for students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions honestly based on your experience and understanding.
2. Select all the values that you believe have a positive impact on the curriculum.
3. For each value, choose the rating that best describes your assessment.
4. Provide any additional comments or insights in the "Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief introduction to the survey.
* **Values to Assess**: 
	+ **Critical Thinking**: Helps students develop critical thinking skills through logical reasoning and analysis.
	+ **Creativity**: Promotes creative thinking and problem-solving skills.
	+ **Analytic Thinking**: Enhances students' ability to analyze and evaluate information.
	+ **Communication**: Develops students' ability to effectively express themselves.
	+ **Interpersonal**: Encourages students to work effectively with others.
	+ **Problem Solving**: Develops students' ability to solve problems.
	+ **Self-Management**: Helps students take control of their own learning.
	+ **Collaboration**: Fosters a culture of teamwork.
	+ **Emotional Intelligence**: Develops students' ability to understand and manage their emotions.
	+ **Resilience**: Helps students bounce back from failures.
* **Rating Scale**: 
	+ **1: None**: This value has no impact on the curriculum.
	+ **2: A little**: This value has a slight impact on the curriculum.
	+ **3: Some**: This value has a moderate impact on the curriculum.
	+ **4: Most**: This value has a significant impact on the curriculum.
	+ **5: Almost all the time**: This value has a major impact on the curriculum.
	+ **6: Always**: This value has a consistently major impact on the curriculum.
* **Comments**: Additional comments or insights about the survey.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and based on your actual experience.
* Do not select values that you think are good for the curriculum, but do not actually have a positive impact.
* Select only the values that you believe are actually beneficial for students.
