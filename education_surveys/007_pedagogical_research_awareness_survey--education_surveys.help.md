# Pedagogical Research Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand your level of awareness and engagement with pedagogical research, its theories, and its applications in education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

- Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
- Please select the options that best fit your current situation and understanding.
- You can provide additional comments or descriptions in the `note` fields, such as `page_3`, `page_7`, `page_9`, `page_13`, and `page_15`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome** (`first_page`, text, optional): This is the introduction page of the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Awareness Level** (`page_2`, select_one, required): Please select one of the options to indicate your current level of awareness about pedagogical research. The options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other
* **Research Project** (`page_3`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of a recent pedagogical research project you've participated in.
* **Theories Understanding** (`page_4`, select_one, required): Please select one of the options to indicate your current level of understanding of pedagogical theories. The options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other
* **Collaboration Level** (`page_5`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the options that apply to your level of collaboration with other researchers in pedagogical research. The options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other
* **Resource Access** (`page_6`, select_one, required): Please select one of the options to indicate how you usually access pedagogical research resources. The options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other
* **Favorite Resource** (`page_7`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the options that apply to your favorite pedagogical research resource. The options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other
* **Resource Evaluation** (`page_8`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the options that apply to how you usually evaluate the quality of pedagogical research. The options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other
* **Teaching Practice** (`page_9`, note, required): Provide an example of how you've applied pedagogical research in your teaching practice.
* **Support Level** (`page_10`, select_one, required): Please select one of the options to indicate your current level of support from your institution for pedagogical research. The options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other
* **Challenges** (`page_11`, select_one, required): Please select one of the options to indicate the main challenges you face in pedagogical research. The options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other
* **Outcome** (`page_12`, select_one, required): Please select one of the options to indicate the expected outcome of this survey. The options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other
* **Teaching Initiative** (`page_13`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of a pedagogical research initiative you'd like to propose or support.
* **Research Frequency** (`page_14`, select_one, required): Please select one of the options to indicate how frequently you conduct pedagogical research. The options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other
* **Conclusion** (`page_15`, text, optional): Thank you for participating in this survey!
