# Gardening Program Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your interest in various gardening programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of gardening program you are interested in from the "What type of gardening program are you interested in?" field.
2. Choose your availability for each of the following:
	* Time of year: Select your available time of year from the "What time of year are you available?" field.
	* Time of day: Select your available time of day from the "What time of day are you available?" field.
3. Enter your contact information in the "What contact information do you want to provide?" field.
4. Select the type(s) of learning you are interested in from the "What type of learning are you interested in?" field. You can select multiple options.
5. If you have any additional notes or comments, type them in the "Any additional notes?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What type of gardening program are you interested in?** (`Gardening Program Interest`, select_one): This field allows you to select the type of gardening program you are interested in, such as Gardening, Horticulture, Landscaping, Conservation, Environmental science, or Other.
* **What time of year are you available?** (`Time of Year`, select_one): This field allows you to select the time of year you are available to participate in a program. You can choose from Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter, or Year-round.
* **What time of day are you available?** (`Time of Day`, select_one): This field allows you to select the time of day you are available to participate in a program. You can choose from Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **What contact information do you want to provide?** (`Contact Information`, text): This field allows you to enter any contact information you would like to provide, such as your name, email address, or phone number.
* **What type of learning are you interested in?** (`Type of Learning`, select_multiple): This field allows you to select the type(s) of learning you are interested in, such as Classroom instruction, Online courses, Workshops, Video tutorials, or Other.
* **Any additional notes?** (`Additional Notes`, note): This field allows you to enter any additional notes or comments you have.
