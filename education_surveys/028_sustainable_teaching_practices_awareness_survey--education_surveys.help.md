# Sustainable Teaching Practices Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Sustainable Teaching Practices Awareness Survey aims to collect data on the awareness, knowledge, and adoption of sustainable teaching practices in educational institutions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read each section carefully and provide your honest answers.
* For text fields, please provide your answer in the space provided.
* You can refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for more details on each question.
* Please ensure that you complete all required fields (indicated by a *).
* If you have any difficulty or questions, please do not hesitate to contact form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Practices** (`current_practices`): Please describe your current practices that promote sustainable teaching methods.
* **Sustainability Knowledge** (`sustainability_knowledge`): How familiar are you with sustainable teaching practices? Please describe your understanding of these practices.
* **Future Adoption** (`future_adoption`): What are your plans for future adoption of sustainable teaching practices?
* **Barriers** (`barriers`): Are there any obstacles that prevent you from adopting sustainable teaching practices? Please describe them.
* **School Team** (`school_team`): How do you think your school can better support sustainable teaching practices?
* **School Practices** (`school_practices`): What sustainable teaching practices does your school currently have in place?
* **Educator Knowledge** (`educator_knowledge`): How do you think educators can better support sustainable teaching practices?
* **Current Practices (School)** (`current_practices_school`): What sustainable teaching practices does your school currently have in place?
* **Future Adoption (School)** (`future_adoption_school`): What are your plans for future adoption of sustainable teaching practices in your school?
* **Barriers (School)** (`barriers_school`): Are there any obstacles that prevent your school from adopting sustainable teaching practices? Please describe them.
* **Sustainability Knowledge (School)** (`sustainability_knowledge_school`): How familiar are you with sustainable teaching practices in your school? Please describe your understanding of these practices.
