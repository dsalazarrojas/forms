# Academic Freedom Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information about your perception of academic freedom within your institution. Your responses will help the institution understand your views on this topic and identify potential areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select your role within the institution from the options provided.
2. Rate your overall perception of academic freedom using the provided scale.
3. Indicate the clarity of the current policies on academic freedom.
4. If you have experienced any restrictions, describe them in the text field. (Optional)
5. Select all areas of concern related to academic freedom. (Optional)
6. Provide any practical suggestions to strengthen academic freedom through policies or actions.
7. Check your willingness to participate in further discussions on this topic.
8. If you're willing, please provide an email address so we can follow up with you.

# Academic Freedom Perception Survey - Help Guide

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Role in institution**: Select the option that best describes your position within the institution.
  * Student: You are a student in the institution.
  * Faculty: You are a faculty member.
  * Staff: You are a non-academic staff member.
  * Administrator: You are an administrator within the institution.
* **Perception of academic freedom**: Rate your overall perception of academic freedom within the institution using the provided scale.
  * Very high: You perceive academic freedom to be very high within the institution.
  * High: You perceive academic freedom to be relatively high within the institution.
  * Moderate: You perceive academic freedom to be moderate within the institution.
  * Low: You perceive academic freedom to be relatively low within the institution.
  * Very low: You perceive academic freedom to be very low within the institution.
* **Clarity of institutional policies on academic freedom**: Rate the clarity of the current policies on academic freedom.
  * Very clear: The policies are very clear and well-defined.
  * Clear: The policies are clear but may have some room for improvement.
  * Somewhat clear: The policies are somewhat clear but could be improved.
  * Unclear: The policies are not clear or well-defined.
* **Have you experienced restrictions**: Describe any incidents where you felt restricted in your academic freedom. (Optional)
* **Main areas of concern**: Select all areas related to academic freedom that you are concerned about.
  * Curriculum restrictions: Concerns related to course or curriculum restrictions.
  * Research restrictions: Concerns related to research or academic inquiry restrictions.
  * Speaking engagements: Concerns related to speaking engagements or freedom of expression.
  * Hiring and promotion: Concerns related to hiring and promotion practices.
* **Suggestions to strengthen academic freedom**: Provide any practical suggestions to strengthen academic freedom through policies or actions.
* **Willingness to participate in further discussions**: Indicate your willingness to engage in further discussions on this topic.
  * 'True': You are willing to participate in further discussions.
  * Maybe: You are unsure about participating in further discussions.
  * 'False': You are not willing to participate in further discussions.
* **Contact email for follow-up**: If you are willing to participate in further discussions, provide an email address so we can follow up with you. (Optional)
