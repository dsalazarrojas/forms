# Practice Questions Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Practice Questions Survey is designed to collect survey questions from students to improve the quality and relevance of the assessment questions. Your input is crucial in helping us create effective and challenging questions that cater to the needs of students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Survey Questions" field and type your answers in the text box provided.
2. Click on the "Student ID" field and enter your student ID number.
3. Enter the date of completion by clicking on the "Date" field. Please use the format DD/MM/YYYY.
4. Enter the time of completion by clicking on the "Time" field. Please use the format HH:MM AM/PM.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, text, optional): This field is where you provide the survey questions you have encountered during your practice sessions. Please type your answers in the text box provided.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): This field is for your student ID number. Please enter the correct number to help us associate your survey questions with your profile.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): This field is for the date of completion. Please use the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): This field is for the time of completion. Please use the format HH:MM AM/PM.

## Tips
- Ensure you are providing accurate and relevant survey questions.
- If you are experiencing technical difficulties, please contact the support team for assistance.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the survey process, please contact the support team.
