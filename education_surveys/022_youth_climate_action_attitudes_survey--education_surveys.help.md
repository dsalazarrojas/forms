# Youth Climate Action Attitudes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your attitudes and behaviors regarding climate action. Your responses will help researchers understand how young individuals perceive and act on climate change.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select your age group and gender from the provided options.
2. Choose your ethnicity from the list of options provided.
3. Select your highest level of education completed.
4. Indicate how often you use the internet.
5. Answer questions about your previous experience with climate-related events.
6. Pledge your commitment to climate action.
7. Choose your preferred sources of news.
8. Indicate your sources of information.
9. Answer questions about your family's involvement in climate action.
10. Specify how you usually get information about your school.
11. Answer questions about your school's climate action.
12. Express your opinion on how climate change affects your education.
13. Rate the importance of climate change in your opinion.
14. Answer questions about your opinion on climate change's importance.
15. Write a note on why you think climate change is important.
16. Write a note on why you think climate change is not important.
17. Write a note on why climate change is important if you think it is.
18. Write a note on why climate change is not important if you think it is.
19. Declare your commitment to climate action for the future.
20. Confirm whether you have a climate action plan in place.
