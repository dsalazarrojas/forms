# Educational Content Creator Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experiences and workflows as an educational content creator. Your input will help us better understand the challenges and opportunities in the content creation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the correct options for each question, using the provided answers as guidelines.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses, as this information will be used to improve the content creation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's your name?** (`title`, `text`, required): This is the first question in the form, where you will enter your name for identification purposes.
* **How long have you been creating educational content?** (`experience_years`, `number`, required): Please enter the number of years you have been creating educational content. This will help us understand your level of experience.
* **What type of content do you typically create?** (`content_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the types of content you typically create, such as Educational Videos, Podcasts, Blogs, or Courses.
* **What motivates you to create educational content?** (`motivation`, `text`, required): Please provide a brief explanation of what motivates you to create educational content.
* **Can you describe your experience with content creation tools?** (`tool_experience`, `text`, required): Share your experience with content creation tools, mentioning any specific software or platforms you use.
* **How do you typically share your content with others?** (`share_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the methods you use to share your content, such as Email, Social Media, or Website.
* **What are your favorite tools for content creation?** (`favorite_tools`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the tools you frequently use for content creation, like Google Slides, PowerPoint, or Canva.
* **How do you measure the effectiveness of your content?** (`effectiveness_measure`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the methods you use to measure the effectiveness of your content, such as Engagement, Completion Rate, or Satisfaction.
* **Can you walk us through your content creation process?** (`content_creation_process`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of your content creation process, from planning to delivery.
* **Have you used any of the following tools for content creation?** (`tools_used`, `select_multiple`, required): Check all the tools you have used for content creation, like Google Slides, PowerPoint, or Canva.
* **Have you created content for any of the following types of clients?** (`clients`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the types of clients you have created content for, such as Schools, Corporate, Non-profit, or Personal projects.
* **On a scale of 1-10, how satisfied are you with your current content creation workflow?** (`satisfaction_level`, `number`, required): Rate your satisfaction level with your current content creation workflow, on a scale of 1 to 10.

Please answer each question carefully and thoroughly, as your input will help us better understand the challenges and opportunities in the content creation process.
