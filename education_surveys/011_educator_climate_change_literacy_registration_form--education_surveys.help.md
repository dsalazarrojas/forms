# Educator Climate Change Literacy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from educators to improve our understanding of their climate change literacy and skills. By completing this form, you will be helping us to better understand the state of climate change education among educators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Educator Information in the first field.
2. Enter your Contact Information in the second field.
3. Add your Email Address to verify your identity.
4. Enter your Phone Number for contact purposes.
5. Select your Date of Birth for identification purposes.
6. Select one Category that best describes your role as an educator.
7. Choose multiple Subjects that you are knowledgeable about.
8. Add any additional Notes if you have any comments or additional information.
9. Click the Submit button to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educator Information**: This field is to collect your name, job title, and institution where you work.
* **Contact Information**: This field is to collect your address, city, state, and zip code. Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Email Address**: This field is to verify your identity and allow us to communicate with you effectively.
* **Phone Number**: Please provide your phone number for contact purposes. We may contact you for more information or clarification.
* **Date of Birth**: This field is to help us understand your age and identify you uniquely.
* **Category**: Choose the Category that best describes your role as an educator. You can select one of the following options: 
  - Climate Change Educator
  - Sustainability Educator
  - Other (please describe in the "Note" field)
* **Subjects**: Select multiple Subjects that you are knowledgeable about. You can choose one or more from the following options: 
  - Biology
  - Chemistry
  - Physics
  - Math
  - Other (please specify)
* **Note**: This field is for you to add any additional comments or information that may be relevant to your submission. You can describe your interests, experience, or any other information that you think will help us better understand your context.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to help us improve our services.
- If you are unable to submit the form, please contact us for assistance.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
