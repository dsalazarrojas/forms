# Survey Techniques Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Survey Techniques Assessment form is designed to collect feedback from participants and trainers about their experiences with survey techniques. This form helps us understand their preferences, pain points, and satisfaction levels regarding survey methods and formats. The information gathered will aid in improving the survey process and enhancing the overall quality of training services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred survey format and methods.
2. Choose how often you take surveys.
3. Select the tools you use for surveys.
4. Rate your satisfaction with survey length.
5. Identify the factors that affect your satisfaction with surveys (e.g., cost, time, convenience, accuracy, ease of use, and speed).
6. Rate your liking for survey methods and formats.
7. Provide additional comments or feedback about surveys.
8. Select your satisfaction level with the current survey services.
9. Provide additional comments or feedback about your experience with the survey services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (<code>participant_name</code>, <type>text</type>): Enter your name to ensure we can contact you for follow-up questions or clarifications.
* **Participant Email** (<code>participant_email</code>, <type>email</type>): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your survey responses.
* **Participant Phone** (<code>participant_phone</code>, <type>text</type>): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for further information.
* **Participant Survey Methods** (<code>participant_survey_methods</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>): Select all the methods you have used for surveys, such as Online, In-person, Hybrid, Other, or Not Applicable.
* **Participant Survey Frequency** (<code>participant_survey_frequency</code>, <type>select_one</type>): Choose how often you take surveys, such as Once a month, Every 2-3 months, Every 6-12 months, Less than once a year, More than once a year.
* **Participant Survey Format** (<code>participant_survey_format</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>): Select all the formats you have used for surveys, such as Structured Questionnaire, Unstructured Interview, Case Study, Focus Group Discussion, Other.
* **Participant Survey Length** (<code>participant_survey_length</code>, <type>number</type>): Rate the length of your surveys on a scale of 1-10.
* **Participant Survey Method** (<code>participant_survey_method</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>): Select all the tools you have used for surveys, such as SurveyMonkey, Qualtrics, Kwikly, Survey Method 2.
* **Participant Pain Point** (<code>participant_survey_pain_point</code>, <type>select_one</type>): Identify the factors that affect your satisfaction with surveys, such as Too long, Too many questions, Too frequent, Too complicated, Too short, Too infrequent.
* **Participant Value** (<code>participant_survey_value</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>): Select all the factors that affect your satisfaction with surveys, such as Cost, Time, Convenience, Accuracy, Ease of use, Speed.
* **Participant Important** (<code>participant_survey_important</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>): Select all the factors that are important to you when it comes to surveys, such as Ease of use, Speed, Convenience, Accuracy, Cost, Other.
* **Participant Liking** (<code>participant_survey_liking</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>): Rate your liking for survey methods and formats, such as Like, Hate, Neutral.
* **Participant Satisfaction** (<code>participant_survey_liking</code>, <type>select_one</type>): Rate your overall satisfaction with surveys, such as Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.

* **Trainer Email** (<code>trainer_email</code>, <type>email</type>): Enter the email address of the trainer who conducted the survey.

* **Trainer Survey Methods** (<code>trainer_survey_methods</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>): Select all the methods used by the trainer for surveys, such as Online, In-person, Hybrid, Other, Not Applicable.
* **Trainer Survey Frequency** (<code>trainer_survey_frequency</code>, <type>select_one</type>): Choose how often the trainer takes surveys, such as Once a month, Every 2-3 months, Every 6-12 months, Less than once a year, More than once a year.
* **Trainer Survey Format** (<code>trainer_survey_format</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>): Select all the formats used by the trainer for surveys, such as Structured Questionnaire, Unstructured Interview, Case Study, Focus Group Discussion, Other.
* **Trainer Survey Length** (<code>trainer_survey_length</code>, <type>number</type>): Rate the length of the surveys conducted by the trainer on a scale of 1-10.
* **Trainer Survey Method** (<code>trainer_survey_method</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>): Select all the tools used by the trainer for surveys, such as SurveyMonkey, Qualtrics, Kwikly, Survey Method 2.

* **Trainer Liking** (<code>trainer_survey_liking</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>): Rate the trainer's liking for survey methods and formats, such as Like, Hate, Neutral.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* You can provide additional comments or feedback in the space provided.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
