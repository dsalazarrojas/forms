# Equity In Education Research Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for researchers who wish to apply for funding or support for their research projects related to equity in education. Please provide detailed information about your research project, including your background, objectives, methodology, and budget.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety. Each field is mandatory, unless indicated otherwise.
2. Ensure that your contact information, institution information, and proposal information are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Choose the correct option for your proposal and submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Researcher Information**: Enter your name, title, and institutional affiliation.
* **Institution Information**: Enter your institution's name, address, website, and contact information.
* **Proposal Information**: Select the correct proposal option that best describes your research project.
* **Project Information**: Describe your research project, including its objectives and scope.
* **Research Question**: Clearly state the research question or problem you aim to address.
* **Methodology**: Describe the methods and procedures you will use to conduct your research.
* **Timeline**: Provide a detailed timeline for your project, including start and end dates.
* **Budget**: Enter your budget for the project, including any relevant expenses.
* **Contact Person**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your institution's contact information.
* **Institution Email**: Enter your institution's email address.
* **Institution Website**: Enter your institution's website URL.
* **Institution Address**: Enter your institution's address.
* **Institution Phone**: Enter your institution's phone number.
* **Institution Fax**: Enter your institution's fax number (if applicable).
* **Institution Phone Ext**: Enter any extension for your institution's phone number.
* **Institution Country**: Select your institution's country.
* **Institution State**: Select your institution's state (if applicable).
* **Institution City**: Enter your institution's city.
* **Institution Zip**: Enter your institution's zip code (if applicable).
* **Researcher Email**: Enter your personal email address.
* **Researcher Website**: Enter your personal website URL (if applicable).
* **Researcher Phone**: Enter your personal phone number (if applicable).
* **Submission**: Select all relevant submission options that apply to your research project.

Note: Please review each field carefully and ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
