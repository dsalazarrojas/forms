# Note Taking Methods And Memory Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather insights into an individual's note-taking habits and memory retention abilities. It will help us better understand how people take notes and remember information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Begin by marking the "Start of Survey" page.
2. Select the methods you use when taking notes, including Longhand writing, Audio recording, Mind mapping, Typing, or Other.
3. Answer how frequently you take notes, using a number.
4. Rate how well you retain information when taking notes, using the options: Excellent, Very Good, Neutral, Poor, or Very Poor.
5. Choose the methods that help you with memory retention, such as Mind maps, Flash cards, Mnemonics, Chunking, Self quizzing, or Other.
6. Select the difficulty you find in remembering information, using the options: Easy, Medium, or Hard.
7. Enter the amount of time you spend on remembering information, using a number.
8. Mark the "End of Survey" page to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start of Survey** (`survey_start`, `note`, required: false): This is the first page of the survey, where you will begin your responses.
* **What methods do you use when taking notes?** (`note_taking_methods`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the methods you use when taking notes, such as Longhand writing, Audio recording, Mind mapping, Typing, or Other.
* **How frequently do you take notes?** (`note_taking_frequency`, `number`, required: false): Answer how often you take notes, using a number.
* **How well do you think you retain information when taking notes?** (`memory_retention`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate how well you retain information when taking notes, using the options: Excellent, Very Good, Neutral, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Which methods help with memory retention?** (`memory_retention_methods`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the methods that help you with memory retention, such as Mind maps, Flash cards, Mnemonics, Chunking, Self quizzing, or Other.
* **What difficulty do you find in remembering information?** (`memory_retention_difficulty`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the difficulty you find in remembering information, using the options: Easy, Medium, or Hard.
* **How much time do you spend on remembering information?** (`memory_retention_time`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount of time you spend on remembering information, using a number.
* **End of Survey** (`survey_end`, `note`, required: false): This is the last page of the survey, where you will complete your responses.
