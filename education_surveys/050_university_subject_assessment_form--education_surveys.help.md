# University Subject Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and assessment from students about their university subject courses. Your input is crucial in helping us evaluate the effectiveness of the course and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Provide your honest assessment of the course, including what you liked and disliked about it.
3. If you have any suggestions or comments, please include them in the text boxes provided.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as `required` in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What's your assessment of the course?** (`course_assessment`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide your overall assessment of the course. Please write your thoughts about what you learned from the course, what you liked about it, and what you didn't like.
* **Who is your course instructor?** (`course_instructor`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select your instructor from the list provided.
* **What did you learn from your instructor?** (`instructor_feedback`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to describe what you learned from your instructor. Be specific about what you gained from their teaching.
* **How would you rate your experience with the course?** (`course_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate your experience with the course on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **What do you like about the course?** (`course_like`, text, required/optional): List the things you enjoyed about the course.
* **What do you think the course could improve on?** (`student_feedback`, text, required/optional): This is your chance to suggest changes or improvements that you think would benefit the course.
* **Email address to send feedback to** (`email_sent_to`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address if you would like to receive feedback on your submission.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the course you are assessing.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, time, required/optional): This field will be automatically filled out when you submit the form.
* **Student Feedback 2** (`student_feedback_2`, text, required/optional): This field is for any additional feedback you have.
* **What do you dislike about the course?** (`student_feedback_3`, text, required/optional): Describe the things you didn't enjoy about the course.
* **How would you evaluate the course?** (`course_evaluation`, number, required/optional): Rate your evaluation of the course on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Comments** (`assessment_comments`, text, required/optional): Share any thoughts or comments you have about the course.
* **Your Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for feedback purposes.
* **Student Feedback 3** (`student_feedback_3`, text, required/optional): This field is for any additional feedback you have.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* Be honest and specific in your feedback.
* Use the "Email address to send feedback to" field to receive updates on your feedback.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact the course administrators.
