# market_research_survey_for_study_spaces - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your study space preferences for market research.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your study area in the text box provided.
2. Enter your name for identification purposes.
3. Provide any feedback you might have about your experience with online study spaces.
4. Select the date you are most interested in participating in study spaces (note: this field is optional).
5. Choose the goal(s) that motivates you to participate in online study spaces (you can select more than one option).
6. Enter your email address for follow-up (if desired).
7. Enter your phone number (optional).
8. Select the time of day you are most likely to participate in online study spaces.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **study_area** (text, required): This is the physical location where you usually study, such as "library", "coffee shop", or "home office".
- **name** (text, required): Please enter your full name for identification purposes.
- **feedback** (text, required): Share any thoughts, opinions, or suggestions about your experience with online study spaces.
- **date** (date, optional): Choose a specific date that you are most interested in participating in an online study space (if you don't have a preferred date, just leave this field blank).
- **goals** (select_one, required): Select one or more of the following goals that motivate you to participate in online study spaces:
  • **goals**: This is an option, but its meaning is unclear without further context. Assuming it's a placeholder for other options that were removed from the form schema.
  • **goals1**: This is another option that motivates you to participate in online study spaces.
  • **goals2**: This is yet another option that motivates you to participate in online study spaces.
- **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up and to receive updates on new features.
- **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact (if desired).
- **time** (time, optional): Choose the time of day you are most likely to participate in an online study space (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).

## Tips
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can leave them blank.
- Be honest and provide as much detail as you can about your study space experiences and preferences.
- This form is designed to gather your opinions and preferences; your participation is greatly appreciated!
