# Student Basic Needs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Basic Needs Survey is designed to collect information from students about their basic needs, including financial aid, campus resources, health services, support services, housing, and transportation. This data will help administrators and faculty members understand student needs and make informed decisions to support students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select the most accurate option for your needs.
2. Fill in your Student ID number for identification purposes.
3. Choose your school year from the options provided.
4. Select your gender from the options provided.
5. Answer the questions about your current dining situation, financial aid, campus resources, health services, support services, housing, and transportation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This section is a note to welcome you to the survey and explain its purpose.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number to help us identify you in our records. This field is required.
* **Email**: This field is optional but recommended for future contact and communication. Please enter your email address if you have one.
* **School Year**: Select your current school year from the options provided: Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior.
* **Gender**: Choose your gender from the options provided: Male, Female, or Other.
* **Campus Dining Situation**: Select all that apply to describe your current dining situation:
	+ I am hungry
	+ I am not hungry
* **Financial Aid**: Choose how much financial aid you are receiving:
	+ I am receiving some financial aid
	+ I am receiving no financial aid
	+ I am receiving full financial aid
* **Campus Resources**: Select all that apply to describe your current needs:
	+ I do not need any support
	+ I need some support
	+ I need a lot of support
* **Campus Health Services**: Select all that apply to describe your current needs:
	+ I do not need any support
	+ I need some support
	+ I need a lot of support
* **Support Services**: Select all that apply to describe your current needs:
	+ I do not need any support
	+ I need some support
	+ I need a lot of support
* **Campus Housing**: Select all that apply to describe your current housing situation:
	+ I do not have a place to live
	+ I have a place to live
* **Transportation**: Select all that apply to describe your current transportation situation:
	+ I do not have a way to get home
	+ I have a way to get home

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question to ensure the survey results are reliable.
* If you need help or have questions, contact your support services or campus administrator.
* This survey should take only a few minutes to complete. If you have any concerns or questions, let us know, and we will address them promptly.
