# discipline_and_guidance_policy_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discipline and Guidance Policy Survey is used to assess the effectiveness and awareness of the discipline and guidance policy in your institution. This form will help you gather feedback from different stakeholders, including parents, staff, and students, regarding the policy's implementation, effectiveness, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin.
2. Select the relevant stakeholders for each question.
3. Answer each question based on your experience and knowledge.
4. Provide additional comments or suggestions in the "comments" field.
5. Click on the "Submit" button to finalize your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Note): This is a brief overview of the survey purpose and instructions.
* **awareness** (Select Multiple): Please select all the stakeholders that are aware of the discipline and guidance policy:
	+ School: School staff and administrators
	+ Parents: Parents and guardians
	+ Staff: Teachers, counselors, and other school staff
	+ Students: Students and student leaders
	+ Other: Other stakeholders
* **effectiveness** (Number): Please rate the policy's effectiveness on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not at all Effective" and 5 is "Very Effective".
* **improvement** (Text): Please provide ideas for improvement, if any.
* **policy_affect** (Select One): Please select the group that the discipline and guidance policy affects the most:
	+ School: School staff and administrators
	+ Parents: Parents and guardians
	+ Staff: Teachers, counselors, and other school staff
	+ Students: Students and student leaders
* **staff_response** (Select Multiple): Please select how the staff feels about the policy:
	+ Agree: Strongly agree with the policy
	+ Disagree: Disagree with the policy
	+ Undecided: Neither agree nor disagree
* **policy_effectiveness** (Select One): Please rate the policy's effectiveness:
	+ Very Effective
	+ Somewhat Effective
	+ Not very Effective
	+ Not at all Effective
* **survey_completed** (Number): Please rate how much you think you have completed the survey:
	+ (This field is not actually used in the survey)
* **policy_awareness** (Select Multiple): Please select how aware you are of the policy:
	+ Agree: Strongly agree with the policy
	+ Disagree: Disagree with the policy
	+ Undecided: Neither agree nor disagree
* **school_feedback** (Select Multiple): Please rate the policy's effectiveness for schools:
	+ Very Effective
	+ Somewhat Effective
	+ Not very Effective
	+ Not at all Effective
* **staff_feedback** (Select Multiple): Please rate the policy's effectiveness for staff:
	+ Very Effective
	+ Somewhat Effective
	+ Not very Effective
	+ Not at all Effective
* **student_feedback** (Select Multiple): Please rate the policy's effectiveness for students:
	+ Very Effective
	+ Somewhat Effective
	+ Not very Effective
	+ Not at all Effective
* **policy_improvement** (Text): Please provide your suggestions for improvement.
* **comments** (Text): Additional comments or suggestions.
