# University Laboratory Session Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience in a university laboratory session. It will help the university to evaluate the effectiveness of the session and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Session ID to ensure that you are evaluating the correct session.
2. Fill out the Instructor Name field with the name of the instructor who led the session.
3. Enter the Session Date and Session Time fields with the date and time of the session.
4. Rate the Overall Rating, Teaching Effectiveness, Materials and Equipment, Organization and Efficiency, and Interaction and Support fields using the number scale provided.
5. Provide any additional feedback or comments in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session ID (1)**: Enter the unique identifier for the session you attended.
* **Instructor Name (2)**: Enter the name of the instructor who led the session.
* **Session Date (3)**: Enter the date of the session in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Session Time (4)**: Enter the time of the session in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Overall Rating (5)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the session on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Teaching Effectiveness (6)**: Rate the effectiveness of the teaching staff on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Materials and Equipment (7)**: Rate the availability and quality of materials and equipment on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Organization and Efficiency (8)**: Rate the organization and efficiency of the session on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Interaction and Support (9)**: Rate the interaction and support provided by the staff on a scale from 1 to 5.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide specific feedback about your experience.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please include them in the comments section.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
