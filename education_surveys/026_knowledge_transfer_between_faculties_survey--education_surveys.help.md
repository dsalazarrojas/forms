# knowledge_transfer_between_faculties_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about knowledge transfer between faculties, which is essential for evaluating and improving collaboration and communication within academic institutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your experiences and opinions on the questions asked.
2. Be honest and thorough in your responses, as this will help ensure accurate data for analysis.
3. Please answer all questions, even if you are not sure or have no experience with a particular aspect.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Faculty Participation** (`faculty_participation`, select_one, not required): This question asks about your level of agreement or disagreement with the statement "I participate in knowledge transfer between faculties." Select the option that best reflects your experience or opinion.
* **Collaboration Barriers** (`collaboration_barriers`, text, not required): This question is open-ended and asks about any barriers or challenges you have faced while collaborating with other faculties. Please provide a brief description of your experiences.
* **Methods Used** (`methods_used`, text, not required): This question asks about the methods or approaches you have used or would like to use for knowledge transfer between faculties. Please describe your methods briefly.
* **Effective Cross-Faculty** (`effective_cross_faculty`, text, not required): This question is similar to "Collaboration Barriers" and asks about any obstacles or difficulties you have encountered in collaborating with other faculties. Describe your experiences briefly.
* **Knowledge Sharing Frequency** (`knowledge_sharing_frequency`, select_multiple, not required): This question asks how often you share knowledge with other faculties. Select all the options that apply to your experiences.
* **Collaboration Improvement** (`collaboration_improvement`, select_multiple, not required): This question asks about methods or approaches you would use to improve collaboration with other faculties. Select all the options that apply to your experiences.
* **Knowledge Transfer Channels** (`knowledge_transfer_channels`, select_multiple, not required): This question asks about the channels or methods you use to transfer knowledge between faculties. Select all the options that apply to your experiences.
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, not required): This question is open-ended and asks about your experiences with communication in knowledge transfer between faculties. Please provide a brief description of your experiences.
* **Time Commitment** (`time_commitment`, number, not required): This question asks about the time you typically dedicate to knowledge transfer with other faculties. Enter a numerical value that reflects your time commitment.
* **Resources Allocation** (`resources_allocation`, text, not required): This question is similar to "Collaboration Barriers" and asks about any resources or budget constraints you have faced in collaborating with other faculties. Describe your experiences briefly.
* **Support from Colleagues** (`support_from_colleagues`, select_one, not required): This question asks about the level of support you receive from your colleagues on knowledge transfer. Select the option that best reflects your experience or opinion.
* **Collaboration with Faculties** (`collaboration_with_faculties`, select_multiple, not required): This question asks about your level of collaboration with other faculties. Select all the options that apply to your experiences.
* **Collaboration with Department** (`collaboration_with_department`, select_multiple, not required): This question asks about your level of collaboration with your department. Select all the options that apply to your experiences.
* **Collaboration with Other Institutions** (`collaboration_with_other_institutions`, select_multiple, not required): This question asks about your level of collaboration with other institutions. Select all the options that apply to your experiences.
* **Knowledge Transfer Faculty Level** (`knowledge_transfer_faculty_level`, select_multiple, not required): This question asks about the level at which knowledge transfer occurs between faculties. Select all the options that apply to your experiences.
* **Communication Method Faculty Level** (`communication_method_faculty_level`, select_multiple, not required): This question asks about the level at which communication occurs between faculties. Select all the options that apply to your experiences.
* **Collaboration Method** (`collaboration_method`, text, not required): This question is open-ended and asks about your methods or approaches for collaboration with other faculties. Please provide a brief description of your experiences.
* **Collaboration Frequency** (`collaboration_frequency`, select_multiple, not required): This question asks about how often you collaborate with other faculties. Select all the options that apply to your experiences.
* **Collaboration Effectiveness** (`collaboration_effectiveness`, select_multiple, not required): This question asks about the effectiveness of your collaboration with other faculties. Select all the options that apply to your experiences.
