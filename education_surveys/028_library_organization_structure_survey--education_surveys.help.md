# Library Organization Structure Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps gather data about your library's organizational structure. Your responses will assist in better understanding the complexities of your library system and inform decisions about resource allocation and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for questions that require a simple response.
3. Enter numerical values where applicable.
4. Provide additional information in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. library_type (Select One)
*   **Library Type**: Choose the type of library you are representing: 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 2. library_department (Text)
*   **Library Department**: Enter the department or section of your library.

### 3. staff (Number)
*   **Library Staff**: Enter the number of staff members working in your library.

### 4. roles (Select Multiple)
*   **Library Roles**: Choose the roles available in your library: 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 5. role_manager (Select One)
*   **Role Manager**: Choose if there is a role manager in your library: 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 6. role_manager_role (Select Multiple)
*   **Role Manager Role**: Choose the role of your role manager: 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 7. management_structure (Text)
*   **Library Management Structure**: Describe your library's management structure.

### 8. manager_role (Select One)
*   **Manager Role**: Choose the role of your library manager: 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 9. library_administrative_structure (Select Multiple)
*   **Library Administrative Structure**: Choose the administrative structure of your library: 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 10. library_administrative_structure_other (Text)
*   **Library Administrative Structure Other**: If the above options do not apply, describe your library's structure.

### 11. library_administrative_structure_other_notes (Text)
*   **Library Administrative Structure Other Notes**: Provide more details about your library's structure.

### 12. library_collection (Select Multiple)
*   **Library Collection**: Choose the type of collection your library offers: 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 13. library_collection_other (Text)
*   **Library Collection Other**: If the above options do not apply, describe the types of collections your library offers.

### 14. library_collection_other_notes (Text)
*   **Library Collection Other Notes**: Provide more details about your collections.

### 15. library_technology (Select Multiple)
*   **Library Technology**: Choose the types of technology your library uses: 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 16. library_technology_other (Text)
*   **Library Technology Other**: If the above options do not apply, describe the technologies your library uses.

### 17. library_technology_other_notes (Text)
*   **Library Technology Other Notes**: Provide more details about your library's technology.

### 18. library_services (Select Multiple)
*   **Library Services**: Choose the services your library offers: 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 19. library_services_other (Text)
*   **Library Services Other**: If the above options do not apply, describe the services your library offers.

### 20. library_services_other_notes (Text)
*   **Library Services Other Notes**: Provide more details about your library services.

### 21. library_hours (Date)
*   **Library Hours**: Enter the hours during which your library operates.

### 22. library_hours_notes (Text)
*   **Library Hours Notes**: Provide more details about your library's hours.

### 23. library_funding (Number)
*   **Library Funding**: Enter the budget for your library.

### 24. library_survey_footer (Note)
*   **Survey Footer**: This is not a required field.
