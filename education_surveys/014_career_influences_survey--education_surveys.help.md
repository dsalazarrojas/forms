# Career Influences Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help gather information about how various factors have influenced individuals' career choices. The survey aims to explore the role of family, mentors, education, peers, social media, personal interests, financial considerations, job market, and societal factors in shaping career decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best describe your experiences and feelings.
3. Provide detailed descriptions for questions that require text-based answers.
4. Review and ensure that you have answered all required questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your full name to identify yourself in the survey.
* **Age Group**: Select the age group that best represents your current age.
* **Current Education Level**: Choose the level of education you currently possess or are pursuing.
* **Field of Study or Current Industry**: Describe the field of study or industry you are currently involved in or interested in.
* **Are You Satisfied with Your Career Choice?**: Select how satisfied you are with your current career choice.
* **How Much Did Family Influence Your Career Choice?**: Choose how much influence your family had on your career decision.
* **Family Career Expectations**: Describe what your family expected from you in terms of career choices.
* **Does Your Family Support Your Career Path?**: Select how supportive your family is of your career path.
* **Mentors or Role Models Influence**: Choose how much influence your mentors or role models had on your career.
* **If Yes - Who Were Your Key Mentors or Role Models?**: Describe the individuals who have had a significant impact on your career choice.
* **How Much Did Education Influence Your Career?**: Select how much influence your education had on your career decision.
* **Impact of Specific Teachers or Counselors**: Describe the specific teachers or counselors who had a significant impact on your career choice.
* **Did Internships or Work Experience Influence Your Career?**: Select how much influence internships or work experience had on your career choice.
* **Influence of Peers and Friends**: Choose how much influence your peers and friends had on your career decision.
* **Did Social Media or Online Resources Influence Your Career Choice?**: Select how much influence social media or online resources had on your career choice.
* **Did Your Personal Interests Drive Your Career Choice?**: Select how much your personal interests drove your career decision.
* **How Much Did Financial Considerations Influence You?**: Choose how much financial considerations influenced your career decision.
* **Job Market and Employment Trends Influence**: Select how much the job market and employment trends influenced your career choice.
* **Societal or Cultural Factors Influencing Your Choice**: Describe any societal or cultural factors that influenced your career choice.
* **Does Your Current Career Align with Your Original Influences?**: Select how well your current career matches your original career influences.
* **Do You Have Regrets About Your Career Choice?**: Select how much you regret your career choice.
* **Additional Insights About Career Influences**: Describe any additional insights or factors that influenced your career choice.

Please take your time to complete this form, and thank you for participating in this survey!
