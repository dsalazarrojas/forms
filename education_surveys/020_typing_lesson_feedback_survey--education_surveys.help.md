# Typing Lesson Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather feedback from students who have attended a typing lesson. The feedback will help us understand what worked well and what didn't, and make improvements to the lesson for future students.

## How To Complete This Form

Please answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly. You will be asked about your experience with the lesson, your satisfaction with the instructor and materials, and your likelihood of attending similar classes in the future.

* **What did you think of the lesson?** (lesson_feedback_1): Please share your overall impression of the lesson. This is a text field where you can provide a brief description or comment.

* **Your email address** (lesson_feedback_8): Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
