# public_awareness_of_human_traffic - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge public awareness of human trafficking. It will help in understanding how much people know about human trafficking, its prevention, and the role of various entities in preventing it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer it as accurately as possible.
2. You can skip any question you are not comfortable answering.
3. Provide your honest opinion, and do not worry about what you think others might think.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, you can reach out to the form administrators.
