# Polar Nature Of Water Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Polar Nature Of Water Survey is a form designed to collect users' subjective opinions and feedback about the polarities of water. It aims to understand how users perceive water's temperature, color, and other characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your email address in the Email field to ensure we can contact you if needed.
2. Select your answer for the Water Polarity Scale, which is a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the most negative and 5 is the most positive.
3. Choose one or more favorite colors from the Favorite Color field.
4. Select one or more temperatures from the Water Temperature field.
5. If you have any comments or feedback, please provide them in the Student Feedback field.
6. Finally, you can skip the remaining fields if you don't have a favorite food or science program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of yourself to help us understand your context and background.
* **Water Polarity Scale** (`water_polar_scale`, select_one, true): This is a 5-point scale where 1 is the most negative and 5 is the most positive. Select a number that best represents your perception of water's polarity.
* **Favorite Color** (`favorite_color`, select_multiple, false): Select one or more colors that you associate with water, such as Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, or Purple.
* **Water Temperature** (`water_temperature`, select_multiple, false): Choose one or more temperatures that you think water has, such as Cold, Medium, or Hot.
* **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, note, false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about water or the survey.
* **Email** (`email`, email, true): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, false): This field is not required, but if you have a phone number, you can fill it in for future contact.
* **Favorite Food** (`favorite_food`, select_multiple, false): Select one or more foods that you like, such as Pizza, Burgers, Tacos, Sushi, or Salad.
* **Science Programs** (`science_programs`, select_multiple, false): Choose one or more science programs that interest you, such as Chemistry, Physics, Biology, Geology, or Computer Science.
