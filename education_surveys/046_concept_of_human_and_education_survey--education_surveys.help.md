# Concept Of Human And Education Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect opinions and views on the role of education in human development. It aims to understand the respondent's perception of the importance and purpose of education, as well as their suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Read each question carefully and provide your honest opinions.
3. Select your response for each question from the provided options.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the text fields to provide more detailed information.
5. Click "Submit" to submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent affiliation** (`Respondent affiliation`, `select_one`, required): 
Select your affiliation: Student, Educator, Researcher, Practitioner, or Other.

* **Importance of education** (`Importance of education`, `select_one`, required):
Rate how essential education is for human development:
	+ Critical
	+ Important
	+ Somewhat important
	+ Not important

* **Primary purpose of education** (`Primary purpose of education`, `select_one`, required):
Select the main perceived purpose of education:
	+ Knowledge transfer
	+ Skill development
	+ Socialization
	+ Civic preparation

* **Challenges in education** (`Challenges in education`, `text`, optional): 
Describe the key barriers or challenges you observe in education.

* **Role of technology** (`Role of technology`, `text`, optional): 
Explain how technology should support education.

* **Suggestions for improvement** (`Suggestions for improvement`, `text`, optional): 
Share your policy or practice suggestions for education improvement.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
- You may skip fields marked as optional.
- If you need help or clarification, contact us at form@gic.mx.
