# Teaching Methods Quality Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teaching Methods Quality Assessment Form is used to gather feedback from students about their experiences and opinions on the teaching methods used in various courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of course you took from the options provided.
2. Enter the duration of the course if applicable.
3. Rate the teaching methods used in the course on a scale of 1-5.
4. Rate the instructor's teaching methods on a scale of 1-5.
5. Rate the course materials provided on a scale of 1-5.
6. Describe what you found most helpful in the course.
7. Provide any suggestions or comments about what could be improved in the course.
8. Rate the overall quality of the course on a scale of 1-5.
9. Select the format of the course from the options provided.
10. Indicate if there was an assessment or evaluation in the course.
11. If applicable, enter the number of hours per week you spent in the course.
12. Enter the number of weeks the course lasted.
13. Indicate if the course was free.
14. Indicate if the course was a requirement for your degree or certification.
15. Enter the start date of the course.
16. Enter the end date of the course.
17. Enter the name of your instructor.
18. Indicate if you would like to be contacted by the instructor in the future.
19. Select your grade in the course from the options provided.
20. Indicate if the course was a requirement for your degree or certification.
21. Enter any additional comments about this course.
22. Indicate if you would like to be contacted by the instructor in the future.
23. Select the category of the course as either work-related or personal development.
24. Rate the overall experience of the course on a scale of 1-5.
