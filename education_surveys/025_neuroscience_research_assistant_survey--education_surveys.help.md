# Neuroscience Research Assistant Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about candidates for neuroscience research assistant positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your candidate details, including your name and any other required information.
2. Select your academic status (Bachelor, Master, or Doctoral) from the list of options.
3. Choose all relevant skills and experiences you have as a research assistant.
4. Select all relevant research interests that align with the job requirements.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, `text`, required)
* This field is for providing your name and any other required information.
* Please ensure that your name is spelled correctly and any other information is accurate.

### **Academic Status** (`academic_status`, `select_one`, required)
* This field is for indicating your academic level:
	+ Bachelor: You have a Bachelor's degree.
	+ Master: You have a Master's degree.
	+ Doctoral: You have a Doctoral degree.
	+ Other: You have a different type of academic status.

### **Skills and Experience** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, optional)
* Select all relevant skills and experiences you have as a research assistant:
	+ Research Assistant: Experience as a research assistant.
	+ Lab Assistant: Experience as a lab assistant.
	+ Data Scientist: Data analysis and scientific background.
	+ Neuroscientist: Neuroscience research experience.
	+ Statistician: Statistical analysis experience.
	+ Other: Other relevant skills and experience.

### **Research Interests** (`interests`, `select_multiple`, optional)
* Select all relevant research interests that align with the job requirements:
	+ Clinical Trials: Research on clinical trials.
	+ Neuroscience: Research on neuroscience topics.
	+ Data Analysis: Data analysis in research.
	+ Machine Learning: Machine learning techniques in research.
	+ Other: Other research interests.

### **Submit** (`form_submit`, `text`, required)
* Click this button to send your response.

### **Cancel** (`form_cancel`, `text`, optional)
* If you need to cancel your response, click this button.
* Please note that this will not submit your response.
