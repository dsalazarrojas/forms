# Cultural Responsiveness In Teaching Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cultural Responsiveness In Teaching Survey is a tool designed to help educators and school administrators gauge their cultural responsiveness and identify areas for improvement. This survey is essential for creating a more inclusive and effective learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly to provide accurate feedback.
2. Review each field's description to understand what is being asked.
3. Submit your responses and review the results to identify areas of strength and weakness.
