# equity_in_digital_access_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Equity in Digital Access Survey" is designed to collect information about digital access experiences and challenges faced by schools, teachers, and students. This information will be used to inform policy and resource allocation decisions that promote digital equity and access.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the school location from the list provided.
2. Choose the devices that your school has access to from the list.
3. Enter the average internet connectivity speed (in Mbps) for your school.
4. Report any barriers to connectivity experienced by your school.
5. Rate the quality of devices available to students and teachers.
6. Indicate whether the school receives funding for digital resources.
7. Report on teacher training received for digital resources.
8. Report on community engagement with digital resources.
9. Identify barriers to device access faced by students.
10. Identify barriers to connectivity speed faced by students.
11. Report on teacher device access.
12. Rate the quality of internet available to students.
13. Report any other device access challenges or issues your school faces.
14. Report any other connectivity barriers your school faces.
15. Report any other teacher training challenges or issues your school faces.
16. Report any other community engagement challenges or issues your school faces.
17. Report any other device access barriers your school faces.
18. Report any other speed barriers your school faces.
19. Report any other internet quality challenges or issues your school faces.
20. Describe any other challenges or issues your school faces regarding equity in digital access.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Location**: (`1`, `select_one`, **required**: false)
  This question asks you to select the location of your school, which can be one of the following: Primary/Elementary School, Middle School, High School, Non-Profit Organization, Community Organization, Home Schooled, Public Library, or Other.

* **Device Access**: (`2`, `select_multiple`, **required**: false)
  Choose the devices that your school has access to: Desktop/Laptop/Computer, Tablet, Smartphone, or Other (please specify).

* **Internet Connectivity Speed**: (`3`, `number`, **required**: false)
  Please enter the average internet connectivity speed (in Mbps) for your school.

* **Time Spent Online**: (`4`, `text`, **required**: false)
  This field is intended to capture information about time spent online. Please enter a brief description of your school's online activity.

* **Barriers to Connectivity**: (`5`, `select_one`, **required**: false)
  If your school faces barriers to connectivity, select one: No issues, Slow internet speed, Cost, Limited internet access, or Other.

* **Device Quality**: (`6`, `select_one`, **required**: false)
  Rate the quality of devices available to students and teachers: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.

* **School Funding**: (`7`, `select_multiple`, **required**: false)
  Indicate whether your school receives funding for digital resources: 'Yes', 'No', or Unknown.

* **Teacher Training**: (`8`, `select_one`, **required**: false)
  Report whether your teachers receive training for digital resources: 'Yes', 'No', or Unknown.

* **Community Engagement**: (`9`, `select_one`, **required**: false)
  Report on community engagement with digital resources: 'Yes', 'No', or Unknown.

* **Device Access Barriers**: (`10`, `select_multiple`, **required**: false)
  Identify barriers to device access faced by students: Cost, Availability, or Other.

* **Connectivity Speed Barriers**: (`11`, `select_multiple`, **required**: false)
  Identify barriers to connectivity speed faced by students: Cost, Availability, or Other.

* **Teacher Device Access**: (`12`, `select_one`, **required**: false)
  Report on teacher device access: Always, Often, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never.

* **Student Device Access**: (`13`, `select_one`, **required**: false)
  Report on student device access: Always, Often, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never.

* **Internet Quality**: (`14`, `select_one`, **required**: false)
  Rate the quality of internet available to students: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.

* **Other Device Access**: (`15`, `text`, **required**: false)
  Report any other device access challenges or issues your school faces.

* **Other Connectivity Barriers**: (`16`, `text`, **required**: false)
  Report any other connectivity barriers your school faces.

* **Other Teacher Training**: (`17`, `text`, **required**: false)
  Report any other teacher training challenges or issues your school faces.

* **Other Community Engagement**: (`18`, `text`, **required**: false)
  Report any other community engagement challenges or issues your school faces.

* **Other Device Access Barriers**: (`19`, `text`, **required**: false)
  Report any other device access barriers your school faces.

* **Other Speed Barriers**: (`20`, `text`, **required**: false)
  Report any other speed barriers your school faces.

* **Other Internet Quality**: (`21`, `text`, **required**: false)
  Report any other internet quality challenges or issues your school faces.

* **Other**: (`22`, `text`, **required**: false)
  Describe any other challenges or issues your school faces regarding equity in digital access.
