# student_media_network_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and preferences from students regarding various media channels. It helps the media network to understand what types of content are most popular among students and what areas for improvement exist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your preferred media channel preferences.
2.  Provide your feedback and suggestions for improvement.
3.  Enter your email address or phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Heading** (`form_heading`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief heading or title for your feedback.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the media network on a scale of 1-5.
* **Favorite Radio Station** (`radio_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your favorite radio station from the options provided:
	+ WUJOY
	+ '89.9'
	+ WRFL
* **Newspaper Frequency** (`newspaper_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select how often you read newspapers:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Rarely
* **Newspaper Preference** (`newspaper_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred type of newspaper content:
	+ Local News
	+ National News
	+ Entertainment News
* **Digital Channel** (`digital_channel`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred digital channel:
	+ Facebook
	+ Instagram
	+ Twitter
* **Podcast Frequency** (`podcast_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select how often you listen to podcasts:
	+ Often
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
* **Podcast Preference** (`podcast_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred type of podcast content:
	+ Local Podcasts
	+ National Podcasts
	+ Entertainment Podcasts
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Share your feedback and suggestions for improvement.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional suggestions for the media network.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Email Confirmed** (`email_confirmed`, `email`, optional): Confirm your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Phone Confirmed** (`phone_confirmed`, `text`, optional): Confirm your phone number.
* **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`, `text`, optional): Confirm your form submission.
* **Page Break** (`page_break`, `note`, optional): This field is not visible on the form.
* **Thank You** (`thank_you`, `note`, optional): This field is not visible on the form.
* **Media Outlet** (`media_outlet`, `text`, optional): Enter your preferred media outlet.
* **Media Outlet Confirmed** (`media_outlet_confirmed`, `text`, optional): Confirm your media outlet.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `note`, optional): This field is not visible on the form, but it displays the submission status.
