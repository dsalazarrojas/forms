# Curriculum Innovation Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback on new curriculum initiatives from students, teachers, training providers, and schools. Your input will help us refine and improve our curriculum offerings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Please note that some fields are mandatory and must be completed to submit the form.
3. For fields with options, select all that apply or choose one option that best represents your response.
4. If you have additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
