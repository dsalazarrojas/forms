# Student Peer Collaboration Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Student Peer Collaboration Effectiveness Survey" is designed to gather feedback from students about the effectiveness of peer collaboration in their learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the Introduction carefully, which provides context and background information about the survey.
2. Fill out the fields as required or as you see fit, considering the options provided.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction
* A brief introduction that explains the purpose and relevance of the survey.

### 2. student-name
* **Student Name**: A text field where you can enter your name as a student.
* **Required:** No, it's optional but helps to identify who completed the survey.

### 3. group-name
* **Group Name**: A text field where you can enter your group name or project name.
* **Required:** No, it's optional but helps to identify the specific group you are referring to.

### 4. teacher-name
* **Teacher Name**: A text field where you can enter your teacher's name or name of the instructor who supervised the group project.
* **Required:** No, it's optional but helps to identify the specific teacher who supervised the project.

### 5. group-size
* **Group Size**: A numerical field where you can enter the number of students in your group.
* **Required:** No, but it helps to understand the dynamics of your group size.

### 6. communication
* **How would you rate the communication within your group?**:
	+ **Strongly Agree**: Indicate that communication was excellent in your group.
	+ **Neutral**: Indicate that communication was average in your group.
	+ **Somewhat**: Indicate that communication was somewhat effective in your group.
	+ **Limited**: Indicate that communication was limited in your group.
	+ **No Communication**: Indicate that there was no effective communication in your group.

### 7. collaboration
* **How effective was the collaboration within your group?**:
	+ **Strongly Agree**: Indicate that collaboration was excellent in your group.
	+ **Neutral**: Indicate that collaboration was average in your group.
	+ **Somewhat**: Indicate that collaboration was somewhat effective in your group.
	+ **Neither**: Indicate that collaboration was neither excellent nor poor.
	+ **Somewhat Disagree**: Indicate that collaboration was somewhat poor in your group.
	+ **Strongly Disagree**: Indicate that collaboration was poor in your group.

### 8. challenges
* **What were the main challenges faced by your group?**:
	+ **Time management**: Indicate that time management was a challenge.
	+ **Technical skills**: Indicate that technical skills were a challenge.
	+ **Communication**: Indicate that communication was a challenge.
	+ **Interpersonal skills**: Indicate that interpersonal skills were a challenge.
	+ **Other (please specify)**: Indicate any other challenges not listed.

### 9. teacher-support
* **How effective was the support provided by your teacher?**:
	+ **Strongly Agree**: Indicate that the teacher's support was excellent.
	+ **Neutral**: Indicate that the teacher's support was average.
	+ **Somewhat**: Indicate that the teacher's support was somewhat effective.
	+ **Neither**: Indicate that the teacher's support was neither excellent nor poor.
	+ **Somewhat Disagree**: Indicate that the teacher's support was somewhat poor.
	+ **Strongly Disagree**: Indicate that the teacher's support was poor.

### 10. student-feedback
* **What feedback would you like to provide about your experience with peer collaboration?**:
	+ **Strongly Agree**: Indicate that the student's feedback is positive.
	+ **Neutral**: Indicate that the student's feedback is average.
	+ **Somewhat Disagree**: Indicate that the student's feedback is somewhat negative.
	+ **Somewhat Agree**: Indicate that the student's feedback is somewhat positive.
	+ **Strongly Disagree**: Indicate that the student's feedback is strongly negative.

## Tips
* Answer each question truthfully and based on your experience with peer collaboration in your learning environment.
* Take as much time as you need to complete the form accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact your teacher or instructor.
* Your feedback is valuable and will help create a more effective learning environment.
