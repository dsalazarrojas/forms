# Hybrid Classroom Participation Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hybrid Classroom Participation Behavior Survey is designed to collect data on students' participation behavior in a hybrid classroom setting.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Select the page corresponding to your role: student or teacher.
2. Answer the questions based on your experience and knowledge.
3. Be honest and clear in your answers.
4. Review your responses carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID (2)** (`Student ID`, number, required: false): Enter your student ID number, if applicable.
* **name (3)** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or records.
* **school (4)** (`school`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your school.
* **Teacher Name (5)** (`Teacher Name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your teacher.
* **Teacher ID (7)** (`Teacher ID`, number, required: false): Enter your teacher's ID number, if applicable.
* **Teacher Email (8)** (`Teacher Email`, email, required: false): Enter your teacher's email address, if available.
* **School Email (9)** (`School Email`, email, required: false): Enter your school's email address, if available.
* **School Address (10)** (`School Address`, text, required: false): Enter your school's address.
* **Participation Frequency (11)** (`Participation Frequency`, select one, required: false): Select how often you participate in class:
	+ Daily
	+ Several times a week
	+ Weekly
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Participation Mode (12)** (`Participation Mode`, select multiple, required: false): Select the mode(s) in which you participate in class:
	+ In class
	+ Online
	+ Hybrid
	+ Other
* **Participation Time (13)** (`Participation Time`, time, required: false): Enter the average time you spend participating in class.
* **Challenges (14)** (`Challenges`, select multiple, required: false): Select any challenges you face while participating in class:
	+ Language barrier
	+ Technical issues
	+ Difficulty understanding
	+ Other
* **Feedback (15)** (`Feedback`, select multiple, required: false): Select your feedback about your experience:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Neutral
	+ Other
* **Participation Barriers (16)** (`Participation Barriers`, select multiple, required: false): Select any barriers you face while participating in class:
	+ Technical issues
	+ Language barrier
	+ Difficulty understanding
	+ Other
* **Suggestions (17)** (`Suggestions`, note, required: false): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the hybrid classroom experience.
* **Participation Rate (18)** (`Participation Rate`, number, required: false): Enter your participation rate (e.g., 1-5, where 1 is low and 5 is high).
* **Teacher Comments (19)** (`Teacher Comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments you have about your teacher's feedback.
* **Student Comments (20)** (`Student Comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments you have about the hybrid classroom experience.
* **Participation Rate Comments (21)** (`Participation Rate Comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments about your participation rate.
* **Teacher Survey Comments (22)** (`Teacher Survey Comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments you have about the teacher's survey.
* **Student Survey Comments (23)** (`Student Survey Comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments you have about the student survey.
* **Participation Rate Comments (24)** (`Participation Rate Comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments about your participation rate.
* **Final Comments (25)** (`Final Comments`, text, required: false): Enter any final comments or thoughts about the survey.
