# Academic Exchange Program Impact Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the impact of an Academic Exchange Program, gathering information about the experiences and outcomes of participants in this program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in your information as a respondent in the "Respondent Information" page.
*   Select your role during the exchange (Student, Faculty, or Staff) in the "Role during exchange" field.
*   Enter the name of your host institution in the "Exchange institution" field (if required).
*   Specify the duration of your exchange in weeks or months in the "Duration of exchange" field (required).
*   Describe any academic benefits you experienced in the "Academic benefits experienced" field.
*   Reflect on cultural and personal benefits you gained from the experience in the "Cultural and personal benefits" field.
*   Share any logistical or academic challenges you faced during the exchange in the "Main challenges faced" field.
*   Provide recommendations to help future participants in the "Recommendations to support future participants" field (required).
*   Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale of Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied in the "Overall satisfaction" field (required).
*   Finally, enter your contact email address in case we need to follow up with you (optional).

*   **Respondent Information** (Respondent Information):
    *   This section is for you to provide information about yourself as a respondent.
*   **Role during exchange** (Role during exchange):

*   **Exchange institution** (Exchange institution):
    *   Enter the name of your host institution, if required.
*   **Duration of exchange** (Duration of exchange):

*   **Recommendations to support future participants** (Recommendations to support future participants):
