# Civic Engagement In Youth Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from respondents about their civic engagement activities, interests, and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Respondent age** field with your age in years.
2.  If you attend school or a program, enter the name in the **School or institution** field.
3.  Select any civic activities you have participated in from the **Participation in civic activities** field.
4.  Indicate your interest in future civic engagement in the **Interest in future civic engagement** field.
5.  Describe any barriers you face for youth engagement in the **Barriers for youth engagement** field.
6.  Choose all preferred program types from the **Preferred program types** field.
7.  If you need any support services, enter your needs in the **Support services needed** field.
8.  Provide suggestions for increasing youth engagement in the **Suggestions for increasing youth engagement** field.
9.  If you are under 18, indicate your parent's consent in the **Parental consent note** field.
10. Enter the current date in the **Submission date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Respondent age**: Enter age in years.
*   **School or institution**: Enter the name of school or program.
*   **Participation in civic activities**: Select from the options Volunteering, Student government, Community service, and Advocacy campaigns.
*   **Interest in future civic engagement**: Select from the options True, Maybe, or False.
*   **Barriers for youth engagement**: Describe obstacles for youth participation.
*   **Preferred program types**: Select all that appeal: Workshops, Mentorship, Volunteer projects, and Internships.
*   **Support services needed**: Enter needs for support services: Transportation, childcare stipends, etc.
*   **Suggestions for increasing youth engagement**: Provide practical ideas for increasing youth engagement.
*   **Parental consent note**: Indicate parent's consent for minors.
*   **Submission date**: Enter today's date.
