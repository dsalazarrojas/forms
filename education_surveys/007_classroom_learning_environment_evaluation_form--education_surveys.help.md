# Classroom Learning Environment Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Classroom Learning Environment Evaluation Form is used to assess the physical space and atmosphere of a classroom, helping to identify areas that need improvement to create an optimal environment for teaching and learning.

## How To Complete This Form

### Introduction
Before completing this form, please make sure you have observed the classroom you are evaluating, either in person or through video recording or photos. This form is designed to gather information about the classroom environment, so please answer based on what you have observed.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

#### 1. Evaluator Name (Required)
Evaluators must enter their name and title to identify themselves and their affiliation.

#### 2. Date of Evaluation (Required)
Enter the date you evaluated the classroom.

#### 3. Teacher/Instructor Name (Required)
Enter the name of the teacher or instructor who teaches in this classroom.

#### 4. Physical Space - Layout and Organization (Required)
Rate how well the classroom is organized for learning by selecting an option from the following:
* Excellent: The classroom is well-organized and easy to navigate.
* Good: The classroom is generally well-organized, but some areas may be slightly cluttered or awkward.
* Fair: The classroom has some organization issues, but they are mostly minor.
* Poor: The classroom is severely disorganized or difficult to navigate.

#### 5. Accessibility for all Students (Required)
Rate how accessible the classroom is for all students by selecting an option from the following:
* Fully Accessible: The classroom is fully accessible for students with disabilities.
* Mostly Accessible: The classroom is mostly accessible, but some areas may be difficult.
* Limited Accessibility: The classroom has some accessibility issues.
* Not Accessible: The classroom is not accessible for students with disabilities.

#### 6. Availability and access to learning materials (Required)
Rate how easily students can access learning materials by selecting an option from the following:
* Abundant: Materials are easily accessible and readily available.
* Adequate: Materials are generally available, but some may be missing or hard to find.
* Minimal: Materials are limited or difficult to access.
* Insufficient: Materials are not available.

#### 7. Integration of Technology in the Environment (Required)
Rate how well technology is integrated into the classroom by selecting an option from the following:
* Highly Integrated: Technology is seamlessly integrated into lessons and activities.
* Moderately Integrated: Technology is moderately integrated, but some areas may be underutilized.
* Rarely Used: Technology is not well used or integrated.
* Not Available: No technology is available.

#### 8. Social-Emotional Climate (Required)
Rate how welcoming and safe the classroom feels by selecting an option from the following:
* Highly Positive: The classroom feels highly welcoming and safe.
* Positive: The classroom feels welcoming and safe, but some minor issues exist.
* Neutral: The classroom feels neither welcoming nor safe.
* Negative: The classroom feels unwelcoming or unsafe.

#### 9. Level of external or internal distractions (Required)
Rate how distracting the classroom environment is by selecting an option from the following:
* Very Low: The classroom has very few distractions.
* Low: The classroom has some minor distractions.
* Moderate: The classroom has noticeable distractions.
* High: The classroom is highly distracting.

#### 10. Lighting, Temperature, and Ventilation (Optional)
Rate the lighting, temperature, and ventilation in the classroom by selecting an option from the following:
* Comfortable: The climate is pleasant and comfortable.
* Adequate: The climate is generally acceptable, but some areas may be uncomfortable.
* Needs Improvement: The climate is uncomfortable or unpleasant.
* Not Applicable: The climate is not relevant to the classroom.

#### 11. Key Strengths of this Learning Environment (Required)
Enter the strengths you observed in the classroom environment.

#### 12. Recommended Improvements (Required)
Enter any recommended improvements you suggest for the classroom environment.

#### 13. Follow-Up Action (Optional)
Indicate if you recommend a follow-up discussion with the teacher.

#### 14. Suggested Next Review Date (Optional)
Enter a date for the next review of the classroom.

## Tips

* Answer based on what you observed, not what you think or feel.
* Be as specific as possible when describing strengths and weaknesses.
* Consider the impact on student learning and teaching when suggesting improvements.
