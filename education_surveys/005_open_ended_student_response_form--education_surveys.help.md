# Open Ended Student Response Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect open-ended responses from students, allowing them to provide detailed and subjective feedback, thoughts, or reflections. This form is intended for students to provide their honest and personal views on a particular topic or subject. The collected responses will be used to gain a deeper understanding of students' thoughts, opinions, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "student_name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "student_email" field. This is required for communication purposes.
3. Enter your phone number in the "student_phone" field (optional).
4. Write your reflection or comment in the "student_reflection" field.
5. Provide any suggestions or comments in the "student_suggestion" field (optional).
6. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Open Ended Response 1** (`open_ended_response_1`, text, required/optional): This field is an open-ended response where you can write your thoughts, reflections, or comments on the topic.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name for identification purposes.
- **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
- **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
- **Student Reflection** (`student_reflection`, text, required/optional): Write your reflection or comment on the topic.
- **Student Comment** (`student_comment`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comment or feedback on the topic.
- **Student Suggestion** (`student_suggestion`, text, required/optional): Suggest any improvement or changes you think would be beneficial.

## Tips

- Make sure to provide clear and concise responses in each field.
- Proofread your responses for accuracy and clarity before submitting.
- If you have any trouble filling out this form, contact us at [contact email/phone number].
- The submitted responses will be treated confidentially and used for internal review purposes only.
