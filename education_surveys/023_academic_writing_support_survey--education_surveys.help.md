# Academic Writing Support Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback on the writing support service you received. It will help us improve our service to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Select the option that best describes your experience with the service.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Suggested Resources" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Usage**: This section is not required, so you can skip it if you don't want to answer these questions.
* **Type of support received**: Please select the type of support you received, such as One-on-one Consultation, Writing Workshop, Online Guide or Resources, or Peer Group Review.
* **Approximate Date of Last Session**: If you remember the date of your last session, please enter it in the text field. This is not required, but it will help us understand when you received support.
* **Rate the expertise of the writing counselor**: Please rate the expertise of the counselor, using the options: Expert, Knowledgeable, Basic, or Inadequate.
* **How helpful was the feedback provided on your draft?**: Select how helpful you found the feedback provided on your draft, using the options: Extremely Helpful, Helpful, Somewhat Helpful, or Not Helpful.
* **Was the advice clear and easy to implement?**: Select how clear and easy to implement the advice provided was, using the options: Very Clear, Clear, Confusing, or Very Confusing.
* **Did the session improve your writing confidence?**: Select how much your writing confidence improved, using the options: Significantly, Slightly, No Change, or Too early to tell.
* **Did you feel the support improved your grades or results?**: Select whether you felt your grades or results improved, using the options: True, False, or Too early to tell.
* **Select areas where you improved most**: Choose all the areas where you improved, using the options: Grammar and Mechanics, Argument Structure, Citations and Formatting, Clarity of Expression, or any other options you select.
* **How easy was it to book an appointment?**: Select how easy or difficult it was to book an appointment, using the options: Very Easy, Easy, Neutral, or Difficult.
* **What additional resources should the writing center provide?**: If you have any suggestions for resources the writing center could provide, enter them in this text field.
* **Would you recommend this service to a peer?**: Select whether you would recommend this service to a peer, using the options: True or False.

Note: This form is intended to be completed by users who have had at least one session with the service, so please only answer the questions that apply to you.
