# Exam Package Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your preferences for an exam package. Your input will help us tailor the package to fit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each section of the form to access the fields
- Fill out each field according to the instructions provided
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by *) or provide a reason for not completing a required field

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are your exam goals** (`student_exam_goals`, text, optional): Please describe your exam goals and objectives. This will help us understand what you want to achieve with the exam package.
* **What type of exam package are you interested in** (`package_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the types of exam packages that interest you. You can choose one or more options.
* **What study format do you prefer** (`study_format`, select_one, optional): Choose the study format that best suits your needs. You can select one option.
* **What exam package do you prefer** (`package_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose the exam package that best suits your needs. You can select one option.
* **What is the desired exam duration** (`exam_duration`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours or days you want the exam to last.
* **What is the desired start time of the exam** (`exam_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the exam in the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 09:00, 12:00, etc.).
* **What is the desired end time of the exam** (`exam_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the exam in the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 09:00, 12:00, etc.).
* **Do you have any additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments you may have about the exam package.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, note, optional): Enter any contact information you'd like to share with us.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

Note: This form is for your convenience and will help us tailor the exam package to your needs. Please fill it out according to your preferences, and we'll take care of the rest.
