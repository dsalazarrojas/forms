# early_childhood_credential_pathways_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about early childhood educational settings, credentials, and survey completion status.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form in its entirety to ensure accurate and thorough submission.
*   Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
*   Review the form carefully to ensure that your answers are accurate.
