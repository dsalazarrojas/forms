# Holiday Math Skills Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Math Skills Survey is designed to help collect information on your math skills and experiences during holidays. It's essential to provide honest and detailed answers to help us improve math education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully:
1. Review each page carefully to ensure you're on the correct page.
2. Select only one option for each field where applicable.
3. Provide detailed answers for note-type fields.
4. Make sure to save your changes before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Holiday Math Skills Survey** (`holiday_math_skills_survey_form`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is the title of the form. It is not a required field, but it's recommended to fill it in to make it clear what you're about to survey about.
* **Math Practice During Holidays** (`math_practice_holidays`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all the math practices you've done during holidays.
* **Confidence In Math** (`confidence_in_math`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose your confidence level in math, from Option 1 to Option 3.
* **Math Challenges** (`math_challenges`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all the math challenges you've faced during holidays.
* **Comments** (`student_comments`, note, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your math experiences during holidays.
* **Comments** (`school_comments`, note, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your school experiences during holidays.
* **Comments** (`teacher_comments`, note, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your teacher's guidance during holidays.
* **Comments** (`holiday_math_skills_survey_form_comments`, note, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or thoughts about the survey itself.
* **Comments** (`holiday_math_skills_survey_form_comments_2`, note, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or thoughts about the survey itself.
* **Comments** (`holiday_math_skills_survey_form_comments_3`, note, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or thoughts about the survey itself.

Note: The required fields are not actually required, but it's recommended to fill them in for accurate and complete feedback.
