# language_function_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate language functions in students, covering various aspects of language proficiency and skills.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions according to your language function skills.
2. For each question, provide a clear and concise response that demonstrates your understanding of the language function being evaluated.
3. Be thorough and detailed in your answers, providing specific examples or examples whenever possible.
