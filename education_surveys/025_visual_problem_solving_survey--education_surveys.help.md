# Visual Problem Solving Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visual Problem Solving Survey is a form designed to collect information from users about their experience with visual problem-solving.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please select your response to the first question on the survey page.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
* Provide your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
* Select your response to any visual problems you have encountered in the "Visual Problems" field.
* Provide any additional notes you may have in the "Note" field.
* Once you have answered all the questions, click the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Page One (Select One)**: Please select one of the options provided.
* **user-info (Text Field)**: Enter your email address. (Please ensure you enter a valid email address.)
* **Visual Problems (Select Multiple)**: Select any visual problems you have encountered.
* **Email (Text Field)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (Text Field)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date Of Birth (Date Field)**: Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Of Response (Time Field)**: Select the time of your response.
* **Note (Text Field)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your experience with visual problem-solving.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill out all the fields as accurately as possible to provide reliable information for analysis.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the form administrators for assistance.
* Please note that this form does not collect sensitive information such as passwords or payment details.
* This form is designed to collect information only about your experience with visual problem-solving.
