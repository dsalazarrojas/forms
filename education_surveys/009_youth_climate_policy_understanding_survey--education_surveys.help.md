# Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gauge the level of awareness, attitudes, and understanding of the youth regarding climate policy and its various aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.
4. If you are unsure or do not have an opinion on a question, choose "No" or "Not sure".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: Please provide a brief introduction or background information about yourself (e.g., name, school, grade).
* **Awareness**: How aware are you about climate change? (Yes/No)
	+ This question asks about your level of awareness regarding climate change.
* **Attitudes**: What are your attitudes towards climate change? (Yes/No)
	+ This question asks about your feelings or opinions towards climate change.
* **Ideas**: What ideas do you have about climate policies? (Select all that apply) (Yes, No)
	+ This question asks about your ideas or suggestions for climate policies.
* **Policies**: What do you think about the following climate policies? (Select one) (Yes, No)
	+ This question asks about your opinions on specific climate policies.
* **NGO**: What do you think about NGOs (Non-Governmental Organizations) that work on climate change? (Text answer)
	+ This question asks about your thoughts or opinions on NGOs that work on climate change.
* **School**: What do you think about climate change in your school? (Text answer)
	+ This question asks about your experiences or thoughts on climate change in your school.
* **Advocacy**: What do you think about advocacy for climate change? (Text answer)
	+ This question asks about your opinions or suggestions on advocacy for climate change.
* **Group**: What do you think about working in a group to address climate change? (Text answer)
	+ This question asks about your thoughts or opinions on working in a group to address climate change.
* **Other**: Do you have any other thoughts or suggestions about climate change or climate policies? (Text answer)
	+ This question asks about any other thoughts or suggestions you have about climate change or climate policies.
* **Climate Change**: Do you think climate change is a serious issue? (Yes/No)
	+ This question asks about your level of concern about climate change.
* **Carbon Reduction**: What do you think about carbon reduction policies? (Select all that apply) (Yes, No)
	+ This question asks about your opinions on carbon reduction policies.
* **Energy Sources**: What do you think about the following energy sources? (Select all that apply) (Yes, No)
	+ This question asks about your opinions on different energy sources.
* **Transportation**: What do you think about transportation and climate change? (Select one) (Yes, No)
	+ This question asks about your opinions on transportation and climate change.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure or do not have an opinion on a question, choose "No" or "Not sure".
* This survey is designed to be completed by students and educators, and it should not be filled out by the same person more than once.
