# Budgeting and Saving Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your financial literacy, habits, and challenges. It will ask you a series of questions about your current savings and debt management practices, as well as your willingness to learn and improve your financial knowledge.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and select the answer that best describes your current situation.
2. For the **Age Group** question, select the age range that best fits your current age.
3. For the **Employment Status** question, choose the option that accurately describes your current employment situation.
4. For the **Annual Income Level** question, select the income range that best estimates your annual income.
5. Answer each question honestly, and select the option that best fits your financial habits and challenges.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Your full name will be used to identify your responses.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): This will be used for results delivery.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, required): Select the age range that best fits your current age.
	+ **Under 18**: You are under the age of 18.
	+ **18-24**: You are between 18 and 24 years old.
	+ **25-34**: You are between 25 and 34 years old.
	+ **35-44**: You are between 35 and 44 years old.
	+ **45-54**: You are between 45 and 54 years old.
	+ **55-64**: You are between 55 and 64 years old.
	+ **65 or Older**: You are 65 years old or older.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that accurately describes your current employment situation.
	+ **Employed Full-time**: You are employed full-time.
	+ **Employed Part-time**: You are employed part-time.
	+ **Self-employed**: You are self-employed.
	+ **Student**: You are a student.
	+ **Unemployed**: You are currently unemployed.
	+ **Retired**: You are retired.
	+ **Homemaker**: You are a homemaker (someone who stays home to care for a family).
* **Annual Income Level** (`income_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select the income range that best estimates your annual income.
	+ **Under $25,000**: Your annual income is under $25,000.
	+ **$25,000 - $49,999**: Your annual income is between $25,000 and $49,999.
	+ **$50,000 - $74,999**: Your annual income is between $50,000 and $74,999.
	+ **$75,000 - $99,999**: Your annual income is between $75,000 and $99,999.
	+ **$100,000 - $149,999**: Your annual income is between $100,000 and $149,999.
	+ **$150,000 or More**: Your annual income is $150,000 or more.
	+ **Prefer Not to Say**: You prefer not to disclose your income.
* **Question 1 - Do you currently have a written budget?** (`question_1`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your current situation.
	+ **Yes - I Follow It Regularly**: You have a written budget and follow it regularly.
	+ **Yes - But I Do Not Follow It Consistently**: You have a written budget, but you do not follow it consistently.
	+ **No - But I Have Tried Before**: You have tried to create a budget but have not yet created one.
	+ **No - Never Had One**: You have never had a written budget.
* **Question 2 - How much of your income do you save monthly?** (`question_2`, `select_one`, required): Be honest in your selection.
	+ **20 Percent or More**: You save 20% or more of your income.
	+ **10-19 Percent**: You save 10-19% of your income.
	+ **5-9 Percent**: You save 5-9% of your income.
	+ **1-4 Percent**: You save 1-4% of your income.
	+ **Nothing Currently**: You do not currently save any part of your income.
* **Question 3 - Do you have an emergency fund?** (`question_3`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your current situation.
	+ **Yes - 6 Months Expenses Covered**: You have an emergency fund that covers 6 months' expenses.
	+ **Yes - 3-6 Months Covered**: You have an emergency fund that covers 3-6 months' expenses.
	+ **Yes - Less Than 3 Months**: You have an emergency fund that covers less than 3 months' expenses.
	+ **No - But I Am Building One**: You have not yet created an emergency fund but are building one.
	+ **No - Not Started**: You have not started building an emergency fund.
* **Question 4 - How often do you track your expenses?** (`question_4`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your current habits.
	+ **Daily**: You track your expenses daily.
	+ **Weekly**: You track your expenses weekly.
	+ **Monthly**: You track your expenses monthly.
	+ **Rarely**: You rarely track your expenses.
	+ **Never**: You do not track your expenses.
* **Question 5 - Do you carry credit card debt?** (`question_5`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your current situation.
	+ **No - I Pay In Full Each Month**: You pay off your credit card balance in full each month.
	+ **Yes - Small Balance**: You carry a small credit card balance.
	+ **Yes - Moderate Balance**: You carry a moderate credit card balance.
	+ **Yes - Large Balance**: You carry a large credit card balance.
	+ **Prefer Not to Say**: You prefer not to disclose your credit card balance.
* **Question 6 - How confident are you in your financial knowledge?** (`question_6`, `select_one`, required): Rate your confidence level.
	+ **Very Confident**: You are very confident in your financial knowledge.
	+ **Somewhat Confident**: You are somewhat confident in your financial knowledge.
	+ **Neutral**: You are neutral about your financial knowledge.
	+ **Not Very Confident**: You are not very confident in your financial knowledge.
	+ **Not Confident at All**: You are not confident at all.
* **Question 7 - Do you set financial goals?** (`question_7`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your current situation.
	+ **Yes - And I Track Progress**: You set financial goals and track your progress.
	+ **Yes - But I Do Not Track Them**: You set financial goals but do not track them.
	+ **I Have Thought About It**: You have thought about setting financial goals but have not yet done so.
	+ **No - I Do Not Set Goals**: You do not set financial goals.
* **Question 8 - How often do you review your financial situation?** (`question_8`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your current habits.
	+ **Weekly**: You review your financial situation weekly.
	+ **Monthly**: You review your financial situation monthly.
	+ **Quarterly**: You review your financial situation quarterly.
	+ **Annually**: You review your financial situation annually.
	+ **Rarely or Never**: You rarely or never review your financial situation.
* **Question 9 - What is your biggest financial challenge?** (`question_9`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your current challenge.
	+ **Living Paycheck to Paycheck**: Your biggest challenge is living paycheck to paycheck.
	+ **High Debt Levels**: Your biggest challenge is high debt levels.
	+ **Irregular Income**: Your biggest challenge is irregular income.
	+ **Lack of Financial Knowledge**: Your biggest challenge is lack of financial knowledge.
	+ **Impulse Spending**: Your biggest challenge is impulse spending.
	+ **Saving for Retirement**: Your biggest challenge is saving for retirement.
	+ **Managing Multiple Accounts**: Your biggest challenge is managing multiple accounts.
* **Question 10 - Would you benefit from financial education?** (`question_10`, `select_one`, required): Be honest about your needs.
	+ **Yes - Definitely**: You would definitely benefit from financial education.
	+ **Probably Yes**: You probably would benefit from financial education.
	+ **Maybe**: You are unsure about your needs.
	+ **Probably Not**: You probably do not need financial education.
	+ **No - I Am Fine**: You feel that you do not need financial education.
* **Topics of Interest** (`learning_interests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the topics that you would like to learn about.
	+ **Budgeting Basics**: You are interested in learning about budgeting basics.
	+ **Saving Strategies**: You are interested in learning about saving strategies.
	+ **Debt Management**: You are interested in learning about debt management.
	+ **Investing Fundamentals**: You are interested in learning about investing fundamentals.
	+ **Retirement Planning**: You are interested in learning about retirement planning.
	+ **Tax Planning**: You are interested in learning about tax planning.
	+ **Estate Planning**: You are interested in learning about estate planning.
