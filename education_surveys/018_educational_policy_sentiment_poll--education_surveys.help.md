# Educational Policy Sentiment Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather opinions and sentiments from users about the educational policy of an institution or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and select your response from the available options.
2. For questions with multiple select options, choose all that apply.
3. Make sure to answer all required questions before submitting the form.
