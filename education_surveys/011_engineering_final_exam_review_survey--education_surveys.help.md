# Engineering Final Exam Review Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Engineering Final Exam Review Survey is designed to gather feedback from students about their experience in an engineering exam session. It is used to understand how effective the session was, identify areas for improvement, and gather contact information and instructor feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each section and its purpose.
* Provide honest and constructive feedback about your experience in the exam session.
* For each field, provide the requested information as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If a field is optional, you can skip it if you don't have any information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exam Session Questions** (`exam_session_questions`, text, optional): Please describe your experience with the exam session questions. What was challenging? What was unclear?
* **How effective was the session?** (`session_effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options to rate how effective the session was:
	+ Effective
	+ Not Effective
	+ Neutral
* **What can be improved for future sessions?** (`future_improvements`, text, optional): Suggest improvements or changes that can be made for future exam sessions.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Email to Contact** (`email_to_contact`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if necessary.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if necessary.
* **Review Session** (`review_session`, date, optional): Enter the date of the review session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the review session.
* **Instructor Feedback** (`instructor_feedback`, note, optional): If you have any comments or feedback about your instructor, please write them here.
* **Department Suggestions** (`department_suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for the department, please provide them here.

## Tips
* Be honest and constructive with your feedback.
* Provide clear and concise answers for each field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
