## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey type**: Select the type of survey you would like to complete. This will help us understand your preferences for the survey questions.
* **Other**: If you selected "Other" as the survey type, provide a brief description of your choice.
* **Dental graduate type**: Indicate your dental graduate type (e.g., DDS, BDS, DMD, or DDS and other). This will help us understand your background and qualifications.
* **Dental Specialty**: Choose your area of specialization in dentistry (e.g., General Dentistry, Special Care, General Surgery, or Periodontology).
* **Graduate degree level**: If applicable, select your graduate degree level from the provided options.
* **Practice Setting**: Choose your preferred practice setting (e.g., Private Practice, Public Health, Academia, Private & Public Health, or Other).
* **Years of Work Experience**: Enter your years of work experience in the field. This will help us understand your level of expertise.
* **Current Location**: Enter your current location. This will help us understand your geographical location and potential practice settings.
* **Graduate Program**: If applicable, describe your graduate program. This will provide context for your education and training.
* **Graduate Program Other**: If you answered "Other" to the graduate program question, provide additional details about your graduate program.
* **Graduate Program Other Other**: If you answered "Other" to the graduate program question, provide further details about your graduate program.
* **Graduate School**: If applicable, enter your graduate school. This will help us understand your educational background.
* **Graduate School Other**: If you answered "Other" to the graduate school question, provide additional details about your graduate school.
* **Graduate School Other Other**: If you answered "Other" to the graduate school question, provide further details about your graduate school.
* **Graduate School Other Other Other**: If you answered "Other" to the graduate school question, provide more details about your graduate school.
* **Graduate School Other Other Other Other**: If you answered "Other" to the graduate school question, provide even more details about your graduate school.
* **Practice Setting Other**: If you answered "Other" to the practice setting question, provide a brief description of your preferred practice setting.
* **Graduate Degree Level Other**: If you answered "Other" to the graduate degree level question, provide a brief description of your graduate degree level.
* **Years of Practical Training**: If applicable, enter your years of practical training. This will help us understand your level of hands-on experience.
* **Years of Practical Training Other**: If you answered "Other" to the years of practical training question, provide additional details about your practical training.
* **Graduation Date**: Enter your graduation date. This will help us understand your completion date of education and training.
* **Graduation Date Other**: If you answered "Other" to the graduation date question, provide additional details about your graduation date.
* **Graduation Date Other Other**: If you answered "Other" to the graduation date question, provide further details about your graduation date.
* **Submit**: Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the survey.
