# Student Wellbeing Research Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from students participating in the Student Wellbeing Research Network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your surname.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your phone number (optional).
4. Specify your survey name and description.
5. Indicate if you are part of the research network.
6. Fill in your university, programme, department, and faculty details.
7. Provide your country, state, and city details.
8. Fill in your level of study, field of study, and programme details.
9. Specify your year of education and graduation details (if applicable).
10. Confirm your current year of study.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Name** (Required): This is the name of the survey you are registering for. 
* **Survey Description** (Required): A brief description of the survey.
* **Research Network** (Required): Indicate if you are part of the research network.
* **Surname** (Required): Enter your surname as it appears on your identification document.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your university email address.
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Time of Birth** (Optional): Enter your time of birth (optional).
* **Organisation** (Required): Enter your university name.
* **Department** (Required): Enter your department name.
* **Level of Study** (Required): Select High (post-graduated or postdoc) or Low (undergraduate).
* **Field of Study** (Required): Enter your field of study.
* **Country** (Required): Enter your country.
* **State** (Required): Enter your state.
* **City** (Required): Enter your city.
* **University** (Required): Enter your university name.
* **Programme** (Required): Enter your programme name.
* **Institution** (Required): Enter your institution name.
* **Faculty** (Required): Enter your faculty name.
* **School** (Required): Enter your school name.
* **College** (Optional): Enter your college name (if applicable).
* **Year of Education** (Optional): Enter the year you started your education.
* **Year of Graduation** (Optional): Enter the year you graduated (if you have graduated).
* **Current Year** (Required): Enter your current year of study.
