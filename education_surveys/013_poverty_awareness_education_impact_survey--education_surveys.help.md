# Poverty Awareness Education Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Poverty Awareness Education Impact Survey is designed to collect information about the impact of a training session on participants' knowledge and behavior. The survey aims to gather data on various aspects of the training, including session quality, knowledge gain, behavior intent, and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out each page of the form carefully**. The form is divided into several pages, each with its own set of questions.
2. **Provide accurate and honest answers**. The information gathered from this form will be used to evaluate the effectiveness of the training session, so please answer truthfully.
3. **Make sure to complete all required fields**. The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Training Session Details
* **Training Session Details** (`training_session_details`, `note`, optional): This is a space for you to provide any additional information about the training session.

### Page 2: Demographics
* **Demographics** (`demographics`, `email`, optional): This field is not applicable to this form as there are no options provided. Please skip this field.

### Page 3: Training Attendance
* **Training Attendance** (`training_attendance`, `number`, optional): This field asks for the number of people who attended the training session. Please enter the actual number of attendees.

### Page 4: Knowledge Gain
* **Knowledge Gain** (`knowledge_gain`, `select_one`, optional): This field asks whether you gained knowledge as a result of the training session. Please select "Yes" if you gained new knowledge, and "No" otherwise.

### Page 5: Session Quality
* **Session Quality** (`session_quality`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field asks how you rate the quality of the training session. Please select one or more options that apply to you: "Good" or "Poor".

### Page 6: Behavior Intent
* **Behavior Intent** (`behavior_intent`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field asks whether the training session influenced your behavior in any way. Please select one or more options that apply to you: "Yes" or "No".

### Page 7: Evaluation
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, `date`, optional): This field is not applicable to this form as it has no options provided. Please skip this field.

### Page 8: Follow-up Support
* **Follow-up Support** (`follow_up_support`, `text`, optional): This is a space for you to provide any feedback or suggestions for future support.

### Page 9: Next Steps
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `note`, optional): This is a space for you to provide any information about the next steps you plan to take as a result of the training session.

### Page 10: Survey Completion Date
* **Survey Completion Date** (`survey_completion_date`, `time`, optional): This field asks for the date and time you completed this survey. Please enter the actual date and time you completed the survey.

Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as required. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out.
