# Data Driven Decision Making In Schools Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from schools in the education sector. The purpose of this form is to gather data on various aspects of schools, such as location, size, and type, to aid in data-driven decision making for schools. Please ensure you are completing this form as a representative of your school.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for each field as they apply to your school.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about your school.
3. Skip fields that are not applicable or do not pertain to your school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Location**: Enter the location of your school in this text field.
* **Name of School**: Enter the official name of your school in this text field.
* **Type of School**: Select all the types that apply to your school (e.g., Elementary, Middle, Secondary, High).
* **School Size**: Enter the approximate number of students in your school.
* **Number of Students**: Enter the exact number of students in your school.
* **School Year**: Select the year of your school (e.g., Elementary, Middle School, High School, Post-Secondary).
* **School Year Start**: Enter the start date of the current school year in this date field (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **School Year End**: Enter the end date of the current school year in this date field (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **School Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of your school in this text field.
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of your school in this email field.
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the contact person in this text field.
* **Contact Person Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the contact person in this text field.
* **Contact Person Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person in this email field.
* **Note**: Add any additional information or comments about your school in this text field.
* **School Address**: Enter the address of your school in this text field.
* **School City**: Enter the city where your school is located in this text field.
* **School Province**: Select all the provinces that apply to your school (e.g., Alberta, British Columbia, Ontario, Quebec, Saskatchewan).
* **School Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of your school in this text field.
* **Type of Survey**: Select the type of survey that this form represents (e.g., Form 1, Form 2, Form 3, Form 4).
* **Other Survey**: If none of the above options apply, describe the type of survey in this text field.
* **Time to Complete**: Estimate the time it takes to complete this form in this time field (HH:MM).
* **School Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the form in this text field.
* **School Comments 2**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the form in this text field.
* **School Comments 3**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the form in this text field.
* **School Comments 4**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the form in this text field.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the form administrator for clarification.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
