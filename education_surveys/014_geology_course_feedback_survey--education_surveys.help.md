# geology_course_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on a geology course. It's meant to help us understand your experience and opinions about the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review each page carefully and make sure you have completed all the fields on each page before proceeding.
* Answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
* Make sure to provide clear and concise answers in the text fields.
* Select all applicable options in the multiple choice fields.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Rating** (`course_rating`, number, optional): Rate the course on a scale of 1-4.
* **Instructor Evaluation** (`instructor_evaluation`, text, optional): Evaluate the instructor's performance and provide any specific comments or suggestions.
* **Student Suggestions** (`student_suggestions`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the suggestions that apply to you for improving the course.
* **Course Improvement** (`course_improvement`, text, optional): Suggest specific improvements for the course.
* **Instructor Improvement** (`instructor_improvement`, text, optional): Suggest specific improvements for the instructor.
* **Class Improvement** (`class_improvement`, text, optional): Suggest specific improvements for the class.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions not covered in the other fields.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the rating scale that best represents your experience with the course.
* **Course Rating 2** (`course_rating_2`, number, optional): Rate the course on a scale of 1-4.
* **Instructor Rating** (`instructor_rating`, number, optional): Rate the instructor on a scale of 1-4.
* **Course Suggestions** (`course_suggestions`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the suggestions that apply to you for improving the course.
* **Departmental Improvement** (`departmental_improvement`, text, optional): Suggest specific improvements for the department.
* **Course Improvement 2** (`course_improvement_2`, text, optional): Suggest specific improvements for the course.
* **Instructor Improvement 2** (`instructor_improvement_2`, text, optional): Suggest specific improvements for the instructor.
* **Class Improvement 2** (`class_improvement_2`, text, optional): Suggest specific improvements for the class.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`additional_comments_2`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions not covered in the other fields.

Note: The fields with "2" at the end are duplicates of the fields without the "2", and are intended to gather more detailed and specific feedback. You can choose to fill in any or all of these fields, depending on your preference.
