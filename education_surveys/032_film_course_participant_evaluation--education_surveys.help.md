# Film Course Participant Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluation from participants who have attended a film course session. The purpose of this form is to collect data on the participants' experience, satisfaction, and overall perception of the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide the date of the session attended.
3. Enter the start time of the session.
4. Select the duration of the session.
5. Choose the type of session attended (e.g., lecture, workshop, discussion).
6. Rate your overall satisfaction with the session (on a scale of 1-5).
7. Select multiple ratings for the session (e.g., quality, content, organization).
8. Provide any suggestions or comments about the session.
9. Enter your email address.
10. Enter your phone number (optional).
11. Add any additional notes about the session.
12. Select the course level (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
13. Select the session you attended.
14. Provide feedback about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: Film Course Participant Evaluation
	+ A label to identify the form's purpose.
* **Name**: Name
	+ A text field to collect the participant's name.
* **Session Date**: session_date
	+ A date field to collect the date of the session attended.
* **Session Start Time**: session_start_time
	+ A time field to collect the start time of the session.
* **Session Length**: session_length
	+ A number field to collect the duration of the session.
* **Session Type**: session_type
	+ A select field to choose the type of session attended.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: overall_satisfaction
	+ A number field to rate overall satisfaction with the session (on a scale of 1-5).
* **Rating**: rating
	+ A select field to choose multiple ratings for the session.
* **Suggestions**: suggestions
	+ A text field to provide any suggestions or comments about the session.
* **Email Address**: email
	+ An email field to collect the participant's email address.
* **Phone Number**: phone
	+ A text field to collect the participant's phone number (optional).
* **Additional Notes**: notes
	+ A text field to add any additional notes about the session.
* **Course Level**: course
	+ A select field to choose the course level.
* **Session**: session

* **Feedback**: feedback
	+ A text field to provide feedback about the session.
* **Course Participant**: course_participant
	+ A select field to choose the course participant status.
