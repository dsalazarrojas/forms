# Educator Retirement System Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and comments from educators about their experience with the Educator Retirement System. This form will help the system's administrators understand the educators' perceptions of the system's retirement benefits, communication, fairness, and submission agreements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*) after the label.
2. Answer all questions thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Choose one of the options for fields with multiple options.
4. For fields with text input, provide a clear and concise explanation.
5. For fields with date input, enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educator Retirement System Survey** (`survey_title`, text): *Enter a title for your feedback*
* **Retirement Benefits** (`retirement_benefits`, select_multiple): *Choose all that apply*
	+ Fair
	+ Unfair
	+ Undecided
* **Communication** (`communication`, text): *Describe your experience with communication from the system*
* **Fairness** (`fairness`, select_one): *Choose one of the options*
	+ Fair
	+ Unfair
	+ Undecided
* **Submission Agreement** (`submission_agreement`, note): *Read and agree to the submission agreement*
* **Submission Agreement Text** (`submission_agreement_text`, text): *Enter your name and other details as required*
* **Submission Agreement Email** (`submission_agreement_email`, email): *Enter your email address*
* **Submission Agreement Phone** (`submission_agreement_phone`, text): *Enter your phone number*
* **Submission Agreement Signature** (`submission_agreement_signature`, text): *Sign your name*
* **Submission Agreement Agreement Date** (`submission_agreement_agreement_date`, date): *Enter the date in `YYYY-MM-DD` format*
* **Submission Agreement Agreement Type** (`submission_agreement_agreement_type`, select_one): *Choose one of the options*
	+ Signature
	+ Acknowledgement
* **Submission Agreement Agreement Date Time** (`submission_agreement_agreement_date_time`, time): *Enter the time in `HH:MM AM/PM` format*

# Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please provide detailed explanations.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
