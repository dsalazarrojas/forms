# Literary Selections Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with a series of literary selections from an educational context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a brief title of your literary selections feedback (optional but recommended).
2. For each of the three literary selections, rate your experience using the rating fields (1, 2, and 3). 
   - A rating of 1 usually means you strongly disagree with the selection.
   - A rating of 2 means you somewhat disagree with the selection.
   - A rating of 3 means you are neutral or unsure about the selection.
3. In the comments fields, type any feedback or thoughts you have about each selection.
4. If necessary, attach any supporting files related to the selection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form-title`): A brief title to describe your feedback (optional, but recommended for context).
* **Rating** (`rating`): Rate your experience with the first literary selection using the scale:
  - 1: Strongly disagree
  - 2: Somewhat disagree
  - 3: Neutral/Undecided
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any feedback or thoughts you have about the first literary selection.
* **Rating Date** (`rating-date`): The date when you provided your rating for the first literary selection.
* **Comments Date** (`comments-date`): The date when you provided your comments for the first literary selection.
* **File 1** (`file-1`): Attach any supporting files related to the first literary selection.
* **Rating 2** (`rating-2`): Rate your experience with the second literary selection using the same scale as above.
* **Comments 2** (`comments-2`): Provide any feedback or thoughts you have about the second literary selection.
* **Rating 3** (`rating-3`): Rate your experience with the third literary selection using the same scale as above.
* **Comments 3** (`comments-3`): Provide any feedback or thoughts you have about the third literary selection.
* **File 2** (`file-2`): Attach any supporting files related to the second literary selection.
