# Postgraduate Program Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Postgraduate Program Selection Survey is a form designed to gather information from students about their preferences and interests to help identify suitable postgraduate programs. Please take a few minutes to complete this form, and it will help us understand your needs and provide you with guidance on the best program for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in all the required fields to ensure we have a complete picture of your needs.
2. For select multiple and select one fields, choose all the options that apply to you.
3. Enter your email address if you would like to receive any updates or information about our programs.
4. Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send your information to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (General Information, text, required): This section asks for a brief introduction about yourself, such as your name, program of interest, and any other relevant information you'd like to share with us.
* **Preferred Fields of Study** (Preferred Fields of Study, select multiple, required): Select all the fields of study that interest you or align with your goals, such as Business, Law, Engineering, or Computer Science. You can choose as many or as few as you'd like.
* **Student Interests** (Student Interests, select one, required): Select one option from below that best describes your main interest, such as "Research and Development", "Teaching and Education", "Business and Finance", or "Health and Wellness".
* **Study Modes** (Study Modes, select multiple, required): Select all the modes of study that you are interested in, such as Full-time, Part-time, Online, or Distance Learning.
* **Additional Information** (Additional Information, text, optional): If you have any other information that you think will help us understand your needs, please share it with us.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive any updates or information about our programs.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to receive any phone calls or messages about our programs.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, optional): Click this button to send your information to us and we will review your submission.
