# Class Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about their experience in the class. By providing honest and constructive feedback, you help instructors improve their teaching methods, course content, and overall student experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the scale for each question, where 1 represents "strongly disagree" and 5 represents "strongly agree".
2. Answer each question based on your personal experience and perceptions.
3. Fill out the required fields, and provide your name (instructor/teacher name) and semester/term.
4. You can provide additional feedback in the text fields "Strengths" and "Improvements".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (2)**: Enter your name as the student taking this course.
* **Student ID (3)**: Enter your student ID number (optional).
* **Class Name (4)**: Enter the name of the class you took.
* **Instructor or Teacher Name (5)**: Enter the name of the instructor or teacher for this class.
* **Semester or Term (6)**: Enter the semester or term you took this class.
* **The course content was clear and well-organized (8)**: Rate how clear and well-organized the course content was.
* **The instructor explained concepts clearly (9)**: Rate how well the instructor explained concepts.
* **The course content was relevant and useful (10)**: Rate how relevant and useful the course content was.
* **The pace of the course was appropriate (11)**: Rate the pace of the course.
* **The instructor used engaging teaching methods (13)**: Rate how engaging the instructor's teaching methods were.
* **The instructor encouraged student participation (14)**: Rate how the instructor encouraged student participation.
* **The instructor provided helpful feedback (15)**: Rate how helpful the instructor's feedback was.
* **The quality of course materials was adequate (17)**: Rate the quality of course materials.
* **Technology and resources were accessible (18)**: Rate the accessibility of technology and resources.
* **Assignments were clear and had clear expectations (20)**: Rate how clear and well-communicated the assignments were.
* **Grading and assessment methods were fair (21)**: Rate how fair the grading and assessment methods were.
* **The workload was manageable (22)**: Rate how manageable the workload was.
* **I achieved the learning objectives for this course (24)**: Rate how well you achieved the learning objectives for this course.
* **Overall, I was satisfied with this class (25)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the class.
* **I would recommend this class to other students (26)**: Rate how likely you are to recommend this class to other students.
* **What were the strongest aspects of this class (27)**: Provide any positive comments about the class.
* **What could be improved about this class (28)**: Provide any suggestions or areas for improvement.
* **Additional feedback for the instructor (29)**: Share any additional feedback or comments for the instructor.
