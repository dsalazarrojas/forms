# early_childhood_care_and_development_eccd_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The early childhood care and development ECCD checklist is a tool used to assess a child's development in various areas, including physical, cognitive, social-emotional, and language development. This form is designed to help parents or caregivers track the child's progress and identify areas where they may need extra support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. For each question, choose the correct option from the drop-down menu or enter a short text description.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields, denoted by "required: true".
4. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Physical Development**: This section assesses the child's physical development, including their gross motor skills. This is a multiple-choice question.
	+ *Motor Skills 1*, *Motor Skills 2*, or *Motor Skills 3*
* **Cognitive Development**: This section evaluates the child's cognitive abilities, such as problem-solving and self-regulation. This is a text field for the parent/caregiver to describe the child's abilities.
	+ *Cognitive Development 2*: A description of the child's problem-solving skills.
	+ *Cognitive Development 3*: A description of the child's self-regulation skills.
* **Social-Emotional Development**: This section assesses the child's social-emotional development, including their social skills and emotional intelligence. This is a multiple-choice question.
	+ *Social Skills 1*, *Social Skills 2*, or *Social Skills 3* for *Social Skills*
	+ *Emotional Intelligence 1*, *Emotional Intelligence 2* for *Emotional Intelligence*
* **Language Development**: This section evaluates the child's language development, including their ability to communicate effectively. This is a text field for the parent/caregiver to describe the child's language skills.
	+ *Language Development 2*: A description of the child's communication skills.
* **Motor Skills**: This section assesses the child's motor skills, including their ability to perform various physical tasks. This is a multiple-choice question.
	+ *Motor Skill 1*, *Motor Skill 2*, or *Motor Skill 3*
* **Problem Solving**: This section evaluates the child's problem-solving skills, such as their ability to identify and solve problems. This is a multiple-choice question.
	+ *Problem 1*, *Problem 2*, or *Problem 3*
* **Self-Regulation**: This section assesses the child's self-regulation skills, including their ability to manage their emotions and behaviors. This is a text field for the parent/caregiver to describe the child's self-regulation skills.
	+ *Self-Regulation 2*: A description of the child's self-regulation skills.
* **Social Skills**: This section evaluates the child's social skills, including their ability to interact with others. This is a multiple-choice question.
	+ *Social Skill 1*, *Social Skill 2*, or *Social Skill 3*
* **Emotional Intelligence**: This section assesses the child's emotional intelligence, including their ability to recognize and manage their emotions. This is a text field for the parent/caregiver to describe the child's emotional intelligence.
	+ *Emotional Intelligence 2*: A description of the child's emotional intelligence.
