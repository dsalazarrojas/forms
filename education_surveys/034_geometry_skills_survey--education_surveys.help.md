# Geometry Skills Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Geometry Skills Survey is a form designed to collect information on geometry skills and knowledge from students. This form will help us understand your geometry skills and knowledge, and will aid in providing better support and resources for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

- Read each question carefully and respond with your best answer.
- For select_multiple and select_one fields, choose the options that best apply to you.
- For text fields, type your answer in the required format.
- You can ask for help if you are unsure about any question or field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (`geometry_survey_1`, text, required/optional): Please type your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **What is your class** (`geometry_survey_2`, select_one, required/optional): This question asks about your current class (or level of study). Please select the option that best describes your current class, such as Geometry or Algebra.
* **What is your confidence level in geometry** (`geometry_survey_3`, number, required): This question is about how confident you are about your geometry skills. Please type a number between 0 and 10, where 0 means 'Not confident at all' and 10 means 'Completely confident'.
* **What is your favorite geometry topic** (`geometry_survey_4`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks which geometry topics you enjoy the most. Please select multiple topics that interest you the most, such as Triangles or Circles.
* **What challenges do you face with geometry** (`geometry_survey_5`, select_one, required): This question is about any challenges you are facing with geometry. Please select one option that best describes your challenge, such as Difficulty understanding concepts or Difficulty applying skills.
* **How many hours do you typically study for geometry each week** (`geometry_survey_6`, time, required/optional): This question is about how much time you spend studying geometry each week. Please type the time in a format such as '1-2 hours'.
* **How many times do you practice solving problems in geometry each week** (`geometry_survey_7`, select_multiple, required): This question asks how many times you practice solving geometry problems each week. Please select the option that best describes your practice routine.
* **How do you feel about the importance of geometry in math** (`geometry_survey_8`, select_one, required): This question is about the significance of geometry in math. Please select one option that best describes your opinion, such as 'Not important' or 'Extremely important'.
* **What topics do you want to learn more about in geometry** (`geometry_survey_9`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks which geometry topics you would like to learn more about. Please select multiple topics that interest you the most, such as Triangles or Angles.
* **What resources do you use to learn geometry** (`geometry_survey_10`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question is about how you learn geometry. Please select multiple resources that you use, such as Textbooks or Online resources.

Note: All fields are required unless specified as optional. If you need help or have questions, please don't hesitate to ask.
