# graduate-curriculum-relevance-questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about a graduate curriculum's relevance to education surveys. It helps determine if the curriculum is useful and applicable for educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by choosing the category that the graduate curriculum belongs to in the "What category does the graduate curriculum belong to?" field.
2.  In the "Describe the graduate curriculum's relevance to education surveys" field, provide a brief description of how the curriculum is related to education surveys.
3.  Select the category that the graduate curriculum relates to from the options provided in the "Which category does the graduate curriculum relate to?" field.
4.  Rate the graduate curriculum's relevance to education surveys on a scale from 1 to 10 in the "How would you rate the graduate curriculum's relevance to education surveys?" field.
5.  Finally, select whether you would recommend using the graduate curriculum for education surveys in the "Would you recommend using the graduate curriculum for education surveys?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What category does the graduate curriculum belong to?** (`curriculum_category`, select_one, required)
    Select the category that best describes the graduate curriculum from the provided options.

*   **Describe the graduate curriculum's relevance to education surveys** (`description`, text, required)
    Enter a brief description of how the graduate curriculum is related to education surveys.

*   **Which category does the graduate curriculum relate to?** (`curriculum_templates`, select_one, required)
    Select the category that the graduate curriculum is most closely related to from the provided options.

*   **How would you rate the graduate curriculum's relevance to education surveys?** (`curriculum_relevance_rating`, number, required)
    Rate the graduate curriculum's relevance on a scale from 1 to 10.

*   **Would you recommend using the graduate curriculum for education surveys?** (`recommendation`, select_multiple, required)
    Select whether you would recommend using the graduate curriculum for education surveys from the provided options.
