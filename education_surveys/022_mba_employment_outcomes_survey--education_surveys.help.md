# MBA Employment Outcomes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MBA Employment Outcomes Survey is a tool designed to collect data on the employment outcomes of our MBA graduates. This data will help us better understand their career paths and job satisfaction, informing our academic and career development programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the basic fields: Job Title, Company Name, Industry Sector, Employment Status, and Years of Experience.
2. Answer the "Why" fields for Job Satisfaction (1-4 fields) to provide more information on your job satisfaction.
3. If you have additional reasons for your job satisfaction not listed in the "Why" fields, fill out the "Other Job Satisfaction" fields (5-8 fields).
4. Finally, fill out the "Career Path" field to indicate your career progression.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (1, text, required): Enter your current or most recent job title.
* **Company Name** (2, text, required): Enter your current or most recent company name.
* **Industry Sector** (3, select_one, optional): Select the industry sector of your current or most recent job.
	+ Options: Financial Services, Health and Biotech, Technology, Arts and Entertainment, None
* **Employment Status** (4, select_multiple, optional): Select your current employment status.
	+ Options: Employed, Unemployed, Retired, None
* **Years of Experience** (5, number, optional): Enter the number of years of experience you have in your current or most recent job.
* **Job Satisfaction** (6, number, optional): Rate your level of job satisfaction on a scale of 1-10.
* **Why Job Satisfaction** (7, text, optional): Explain why you are satisfied or not satisfied with your job.
* **Career Path** (8, select_one, optional): Select your career path.
	+ Options: Consulting, Operations, Finance, None
* **Other Job Satisfaction** (9-17, text, optional): If you have additional reasons for your job satisfaction not listed above, fill out this field.
* **Other Job Satisfaction 2-17** (10-21, text, optional): Same as above.
 
Note: Please fill out all the "Other Job Satisfaction" fields consistently and concisely to provide us with a clear understanding of your job satisfaction. 

## Tips
- Be as detailed and concise as possible when filling out the form.
- If unsure about a field, please contact us and we'll be happy to assist.
- Please do not submit the form until you have filled out all the required fields.
