# Computer Lab Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users of computer labs on their experiences and perceptions of lab resources and services. Your input will help us improve the lab's facilities and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your role in the lab (Student, Staff, Faculty, or Visitor).
2. Choose how often you use the lab (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely).
3. Rate your satisfaction with the lab equipment (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied).
4. Indicate whether lab staff are available when you need them (Always, Often, Occasionally, or Never).
5. Select how easy it is to book or access the lab facilities (Very easy, Easy, Moderate, or Difficult).
6. (Optional) Suggest any improvements to the lab experience.
7. (Optional) Add any additional comments or feedback for lab management.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Lab Usage --
* Respondent role: Choose your role in the lab, such as Student, Staff, Faculty, or Visitor.
### Respondent role: This question helps us understand who uses the lab facilities.
### Frequency of use: How often do you use the lab? Select Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely.
### Satisfaction with equipment: Rate your satisfaction with the lab equipment. Select Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.
### Support availability: How often are lab staff available when you need them? Select Always, Often, Occasionally, or Never.
### Ease of access: How easy is it to book or access the lab facilities? Select Very easy, Easy, Moderate, or Difficult.
### Improvements suggested: Suggest any ideas to improve the lab experience.
### Additional comments: Add any additional comments or feedback for lab management.
