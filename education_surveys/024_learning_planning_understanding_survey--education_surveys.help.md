# learning_planning_understanding_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The learning_planning_understanding_survey form is designed to gather information from students for education surveys. This form is intended for students who have completed their academic program of study and have basic demographic information available.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email and Phone Number in the respective fields.
3. Specify your Learning Goals for the upcoming semester.
4. Choose your Education Level.
5. Select the Program of Study that you have completed.
6. Choose your Academic Institution (Yes/No).
7. Enter your Date of Birth.
8. (Optional) Enter your Time of Birth.
9. Fill in any additional Note if necessary.
10. Choose your Learning Planning Understanding (Agree/Disagree/Undecided).
11. Select whether you have Learning Outcomes (Yes/No).
12. Select whether you have Education Programs (Yes/No).
13. Select your Learning Resources (Online/Print/Face-to-face/Digital).
14. Choose your Feedback (Very satisfied/Satisfied/Neutral/Dissatisfied/Very dissatisfied).
15. Rate this survey from 1 to 5.
16. Fill in any Survey Comments if necessary.
17. Choose whether you have Demographic Information (Yes/No).
18. Enter your Student ID.
19. Enter your second Email (if necessary).
20. Enter your Phone Number 2 (if necessary).
21. Fill in your Learning Goals 2 (if necessary).
22. Choose your Learning Planning Understanding 2 (Strongly agree/Somewhat agree/Undecided/Somewhat disagree/Strongly disagree).
23. Select whether you have Education Programs 2 (Yes/No).
24. Select your Learning Resources 2 (Online/Print/Face-to-face/Digital).
25. (Optional) Enter any other information that you feel is necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: The email address you check most frequently.
* **Phone Number**: The phone number you prefer to use for contact.
* **Learning Goals**: Specify your top learning goals for the upcoming semester.
* **Education Level**: This is the highest level of education you have completed. Options: High School, Bachelor's, Master's, Doctoral.
* **Program of Study**: This is the program of study you have completed.
* **Academic Institution**: Have you completed your education at an institution? Options: Yes, No.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Time of Birth**: (Optional) Enter your time of birth (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Note**: (Optional) Enter any additional information that you feel is necessary.
* **Learning Planning Understanding**: How do you understand your learning goals? Options: Agree, Disagree, Undecided.
* **Learning Outcomes**: Do you have learning outcomes? Options: Yes, No.
* **Education Programs**: Do you have education programs? Options: Yes, No.
* **Learning Resources**: What types of learning resources do you use? Options: Online, Print, Face-to-face, Digital.
* **Feedback**: How satisfied are you with this form? Options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **Rating**: Rate this survey from 1 to 5.
* **Survey Comments**: (Optional) Enter any comments about this form.
* **Demographic Information**: Do you have demographic information available? Options: Yes, No.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number.
* **Email 2**: (Optional) Enter your second email address.
* **Phone Number 2**: (Optional) Enter your second phone number.
* **Learning Goals 2**: (Optional) Specify your top learning goals for the upcoming semester.
* **Learning Planning Understanding 2**: How do you understand your learning goals? Options: Strongly agree, Somewhat agree, Undecided, Somewhat disagree, Strongly disagree.
* **Education Programs 2**: Do you have education programs? Options: Yes, No.
* **Learning Resources 2**: What types of learning resources do you use? Options: Online, Print, Face-to-face, Digital.
* **Rating 2**: (Optional) Rate this form again from 1 to 5.
* **Feedback 2**: (Optional) How satisfied are you with this form again? Options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
* **Survey Comments 2**: (Optional) Enter any comments about this form.
* **Additional Information**: (Optional) Enter any other information that you feel is necessary.

Please review each field carefully and enter the information as required. If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the survey administrators for help.
