# AI Education Research Partnership Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the form fields required to request a partnership with our AI research team. Please fill out the form carefully, making sure to provide accurate and detailed information about your research project.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Principal investigator name** field with the name and contact information of the lead contact person for the project.
2. Provide your **Institution or organization** in the field next to it.
3. Enter your **Contact email** for partnership coordination.
4. Give a brief description of your **Project objectives** and expected outcomes.
5. If your project requires any specific **Resources**, select all the relevant options from the list.
6. Provide a **Proposed timeline** with major milestones and estimated completion dates.
7. Indicate your **Ethics approval status** (true, false, or pending).
8. Finally, add any **Additional comments** that might be useful for the reviewers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Principal investigator name**:
    * Type: Text
    * Required: Yes
    * Description: Enter the name and contact information of the lead contact person for the project.
* **Institution or organization**:
    * Type: Text
    * Required: Yes
    * Description: Affiliated university or organization.
* **Contact email**:
    * Type: Email
    * Required: Yes
    * Description: Email address for partnership coordination.
* **Project objectives**:
    * Type: Text
    * Required: Yes
    * Description: Brief description of the project's aims and expected outcomes.
* **Resources requested**:
    * Type: Select multiple
    * Required: No
    * Description: Select from the list to indicate which resources you need (Data access, Participant recruitment, Technical support, Funding).
* **Proposed timeline**:
    * Type: Text
    * Required: No
    * Description: Major milestones and estimated completion dates for the project.
* **Ethics approval status**:
    * Type: Select one
    * Required: Yes
    * Description: Has IRB or ethics approval been obtained?
* **Additional comments**:
    * Type: Text
    * Required: No
    * Description: Any other information useful to reviewers.
