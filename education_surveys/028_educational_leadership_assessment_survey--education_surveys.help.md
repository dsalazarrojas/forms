# educational_leadership_assessment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Leadership Assessment Survey is a tool for school administrators or HR personnel to evaluate a school leader's performance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your school's name in the "School Name" field.
2. Select the type of school you work at from the "School Type" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Leader Name" field.
4. Enter the number of years of experience you have working with the school leader in the "Years Experience" field.
5. Select the date of the assessment in the "Assessment Date" field.
6. Provide any additional evaluation comments in the "Evaluation Comments" field.
7. Choose the specific strengths and areas for improvement for the school leader in the "Recommendations and Actions" field. Multiple selections are allowed.
8. Enter the school leader's email address in the "Email" field (not required).
9. Enter any additional contact information (e.g. phone number) in the "Phone" field (not required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your school's name to provide context for the assessment.
* **School Type** (2, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of school you work at (Public School, Private School, or Charter School).
* **Leader Name** (3, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the school leader being assessed.
* **Years Experience** (4, number, required/optional): Enter the number of years you have working with the school leader.
* **Assessment Date** (5, date, required/optional): Select the date of the assessment.
* **Evaluation Comments** (6, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments about the school leader's performance.
* **Recommendations and Actions** (7, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more of the following:
	+ Improve Communication
	+ Increase Feedback
	+ Set Goals
* **Email** (8, email, required/optional): Enter the school leader's email address.
* **Phone** (9, text, required/optional): Enter any additional contact information for the school leader.
