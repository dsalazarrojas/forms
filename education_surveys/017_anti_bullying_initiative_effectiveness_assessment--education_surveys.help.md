# Anti Bullying Initiative Effectiveness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anti Bullying Initiative Effectiveness Assessment form is designed to gather data on the effectiveness of anti-bullying initiatives in schools. It helps to evaluate the impact of these initiatives on observed bullying incidents and gather suggestions for improvement.

4. Estimate the frequency of bullying incidents observed in your school since the anti-bullying program started:

	* (Optional text field)

	* (Optional text field)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent Role** (`respondent_role`): Select the category that best describes your role in the school.
* **School Level** (`school_level`): Choose the level of your school to help us understand the context of the program.
* **Observed Bullying Incidents Before** (`observed_incidents_before`): Estimate the frequency of bullying incidents observed in your school before the program started.
* **Observed Bullying Incidents After** (`observed_incidents_after`): Estimate the frequency of bullying incidents observed in your school since the program started.
* **Program Effectiveness** (`program_effectiveness`): Rate the overall effectiveness of the anti-bullying program.
* **Behavior Change Examples** (`behavior_change_examples`): Provide specific examples of behavior change observed in your school since the program started.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`): Share your suggestions for improving the anti-bullying program.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when estimating the frequency of bullying incidents.
* Provide specific examples of behavior change to help us understand the impact of the program.
* Share your suggestions for improvement to help us make the program more effective.
