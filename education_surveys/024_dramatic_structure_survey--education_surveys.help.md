# Dramatic Structure Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the "Dramatic Structure Survey" form, which is designed to gather information about a story or film's structure and composition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the purpose and explanation above.
2. Begin with the "intro" section, which is a brief note field for any general comments or observations about the story or film.
3. For each section, select one of the options provided (e.g., "Yes" or "No", "Linear" or "Non-linear") or enter your text in the appropriate field.
4. For sections that are not required, you can skip them or enter a brief explanation if you want to provide additional context.
5. Once you've completed all the sections, review your answers to ensure they accurately reflect the story or film's structure and composition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. intro
• **intro_label**: This is a brief note field for any general comments or observations about the story or film.

### 2. dramatic_structure
• **dramatic_structure_label**: Select one of the possible dramatic structures for the story or film (e.g., "Linear", "Non-linear", "Episodic").

### 3. scene
• **scene_label**: Select multiple possible scene types for the story or film (e.g., "Indoor", "Outdoor").

### 4. plot
• **plot_label**: Enter a brief synopsis of the story or film's plot (e.g., "The story revolves around a group of friends who go on a road trip").

### 5. character
• **character_label**: Select multiple possible character types for the story or film (e.g., "Protagonist", "Antagonist").

### 6. dialogue
• **dialogue_label**: Select one of the possible dialogue types for the story or film (e.g., "Internal", "External").

### 7. conflict
• **conflict_label**: Select one of the possible conflict types for the story or film (e.g., "Internal", "External").

### 8. resolution
• **resolution_label**: Select one of the possible resolution types for the story or film (e.g., "Happy", "Sad").

### 9. theme
• **theme_label**: Select multiple possible themes for the story or film (e.g., "Love", "Betrayal").

### 10. story_telling
• **story_telling_label**: Select multiple possible storytelling techniques for the story or film (e.g., "Suspense", "Humor").

### 11. narrative_device
• **narrative_device_label**: Select multiple possible narrative devices for the story or film (e.g., "Foreshadowing", "Symbolism").

### 12. feedback
• **feedback_label**: A note field for any additional comments or feedback about the story or film.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
