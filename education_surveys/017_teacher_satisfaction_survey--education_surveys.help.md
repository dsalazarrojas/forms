# Teacher Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your thoughts on your experiences and satisfaction with your school. Your input will help us understand what we do well and areas for improvement, enabling us to make informed decisions to enhance your work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in your contact information (First Name, Last Name, Email).
2. Answer each question honestly, choosing the responses that best fit your experiences and perspectives.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all applicable options that apply to your situation.
4. Use the open-ended fields to provide as much detail as you can, sharing any thoughts, concerns, or suggestions you have.
5. Review your responses carefully before submitting, ensuring you answer each question accurately and truthfully.
