# Ethical Use Of AI In Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Ethical Use of AI in Learning Survey.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Begin by reading the survey title. Please take a moment to understand the purpose and goals of this study.
2.  Choose the correct options for the demographic questions by selecting one or multiple options from the available choices. 
3.  Answer the select one questions by choosing one option.
4.  Answer the select multiple questions by choosing one or multiple options.
5.  Enter your comments and any other information about the survey topic.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Survey Title (1)** (`survey_title`, text, required): This is the title of the survey. Please enter your name or your organization.
*   **demographic information (3)** (`demographic_info`, select_one, required): This question asks you to select one of the two options. Please choose whether AI is used more frequently by you or others.
*   **what are the benefits of using AI in learning (5)** (`benefits`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the ways in which AI can be useful in learning.
*   **what are the concerns of using AI in learning (6)** (`concerns`, select_multiple, required):  Please select all the concerns you have about using AI in learning.
*   **How can AI be used responsibly in learning (7)** (`responsible_use`, select_one, required): Please choose how AI can be used responsibly.
*   **Survey Comment (25)** (`survey_comment`, text, optional): If you would like to share any other comments or information with us, please type it in here.
