# Human Rights Literacy Level Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the knowledge and attitudes of individuals towards human rights.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and select your response from the options provided.
2. Answer the questions in a way that reflects your honest opinion.
3. If you need help, do not hesitate to contact us.

* **Awareness of Human Rights** (`Awareness of Human Rights`, `select_one`, required: false): This question assesses your level of awareness about human rights. Choose the option that best reflects your current state of mind:
	+ Strongly Agree: You are very confident about human rights and their importance.
	+ Neutral: You have some knowledge, but are not entirely sure.
	+ Disagree: You do not believe human rights are important or applicable.
* **Understanding of Human Rights** (`Understanding of Human Rights`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question evaluates how well you understand human rights concepts. Select all options that apply to you:

Tips:
