**Library Weeding Strategy Survey - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data from library staff members about their weeding practices, which will help inform future weeding strategies and decision-making processes within the library.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Weeding Frequency**: Indicate how often your library weeds. This could be 'Yes' for frequently, or 'No' if rarely or never.
  - **Weeding Criterion**: Select the reason for weeding a book or item from the options provided (Remoteness, Overdueness, Condition, or Other).
  - **Challenges**: If you have faced any challenges during the weeding process, please specify them in the text field.
  - **Improvement Ideas**: Share your thoughts on how you would improve the weeding process if you could change one thing.
  - **Library Type**: Select the type of library you work for (Academic, Public, School, or Other).
  - **Number of Staff**: Report the number of staff members involved in the weeding process.
  - **Staff Experience**: Describe the level of experience your staff members have with weeding.
  - **Collection Size**: Provide information on the size of your library's collection.
  - **Weeding Criterion Frequency**: If applicable, report how frequently weeding is performed based on the selected weeding criterion.
  - **Decision Process**: Indicate whether your library follows a formal decision-making process for weeding (Yes or No).
  - **Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about the weeding process.
  - **Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up contact.
  - **Phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
  - **Date Weeded**: Report the date when weeding was last performed.
  - **Time Weeded**: Report the time when weeding was last performed.
  - **Location Weeded**: Describe the location where weeding was performed.
  - **Other Weeding Details**: If applicable, provide any additional details about the weeding process.
