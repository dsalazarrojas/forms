# Subject Representative Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from a subject representative about a specific subject. The feedback includes thoughts, concerns, and suggestions related to that subject.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the form by choosing "Education Surveys" from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter a brief description of the subject in the `form_description` field.
3. Choose one or more subject representatives from the list.
4. Select the type of feedback you want to provide: Concerns, Suggestions, or both.
5. Enter specific concerns in the text fields `concern_1`, `concern_2`, `concern_3`, `concern_4`, `concern_5`.
6. Enter specific suggestions in the text fields `suggestions_1`, `suggestions_2`, `suggestions_3`, `suggestions_4`, `suggestions_5`.
7. Choose the term and academic year for which you're providing feedback.
8. Enter a date for the submission.
9. Enter your contact information: email and phone number (optional).
10. Provide any additional comments in the `comments` field.
11. Finally, enter a subject line for any follow-up communication in the `email_subject` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief title of the subject.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Education Surveys" to ensure your feedback is categorized correctly.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, `note`, required: false): A brief description of the subject.
* **Subject Representative** (`subject_representative`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose one or more representatives of the subject.
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of feedback you want to provide: Concerns, Suggestions, or both.
