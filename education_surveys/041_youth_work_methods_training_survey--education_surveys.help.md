# Youth Work Methods Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Work Methods Training Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from participants after a youth work methods training session. By completing this form, you will help us better understand your experience with the training session and provide valuable insights for future improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter the title of the youth work training session that this form refers to.** 
   - This is an optional field, but it helps us understand the specific context of the session.
2. **Provide the date of the training session.** 
   - This field is also optional, but it helps us keep track of the sessions and their corresponding feedback.
3. **Enter your name.** 
   - This field is optional, but it's a good idea to provide your name so we can get back to you with any follow-up questions or results.
4. **Enter your organization or affiliation.** 
   - This field is optional, but it helps us understand your role in the training session.
5. **Enter your role in the training session.** 
   - This field is optional, but it helps us understand your perspective in the training session.
6. **Share any notes about the session.** 
   - This field is optional, but it's a good idea to provide any additional comments or thoughts about the session.
7. **Rate your satisfaction with the training session.** 
   - Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your level of satisfaction with the session.
8. **Share any suggestions for improving the youth work methods training program.** 
   - This field is optional, but it's a great opportunity to provide constructive feedback and help us improve the program.
9. **Enter your email address.** 
   - This field is optional, but it helps us get in touch with you for any follow-up questions or results.
10. **Select the category and description of this form.** 
    - These fields are not used for this form, but you can ignore them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the youth work training session that this form refers to?** (`form_title`, `text`, optional)
	+ This field is a free-text input field where you can provide a brief description of the training session.
* **Training Session Date** (`training_session_date`, `date`, optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter the date of the training session.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter your name.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter your organization or affiliation.
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter your role in the training session.
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, `note`, optional)
	+ This field is where you can provide any additional comments or thoughts about the session.
* **How satisfied were you with the training session?** (`session_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your level of satisfaction with the session.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improving the youth work methods training program?** (`suggestions`, `text`, optional)
	+ This field is where you can provide constructive feedback and suggestions for improving the program.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, `text`, not applicable)
	+ This field is not used for this form.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, `text`, not applicable)
	+ This field is not used for this form.
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, not applicable)
	+ This field is not used for this form.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, `text`, not applicable)
	+ This field is not used for this form.

## Tips

* Please fill in all the required fields (if any).
* Provide as much detail as possible to help us better understand your experience and feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out.
