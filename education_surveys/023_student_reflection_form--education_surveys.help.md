# Student Reflection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Reflection Form is designed to gather feedback from students on their learning experience. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and honestly to ensure that the education sector can better understand the students' needs and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Student Reflection Form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your Student ID.
2. Enter your name.
3. Select the school year you are currently in.
4. Choose your class name.
5. Select one or multiple subjects you are taking.
6. Answer the questions (Questions 1, 2, and 3).
7. Provide teacher feedback.
8. Enter your submission date and time.
9. Enter your school name, address, city, state, and zip code.
10. Enter your name and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Id**: Enter your unique student ID number.
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name.
* **School Year**: Select the current school year you are in (e.g., First Year, Second Year, Third Year).
* **Class Name**: Choose your class name (e.g., Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Calculus).
* **Subject Name**: Select one or multiple subjects you are taking (e.g., Math, Science, History, English).
* **Question 1**, **Question 2**, and **Question 3**: Answer these questions with your thoughts and reflections.
* **Teacher Feedback**: Provide feedback on your teacher's performance.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting the form.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time you are submitting the form.
* **School Name**: Enter your school's name.
* **School Address**: Enter your school's address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Submitted By Name**: Enter your name (in case of any queries or concerns).
* **Teacher Id**: Enter your teacher's ID number.
* **School Year Id**: Enter your school's ID number.
* **Submitted Date and Time**: Enter the date and time you are submitting the form.
* **Submitted At**: Enter the time you are submitting the form.
* **School**: Choose your school type (e.g., Public, Private, Charter).
* **Submitted By User**: Choose who you are submitting the form as (e.g., Teacher, Student, Administrator).
* **School Id**: Enter your school's ID number (not required).
* **Submitted By User**: You are required to choose who you are submitting the form as.

Please ensure you complete the form accurately and honestly to help the education sector better understand your needs and experiences. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authority for assistance.
