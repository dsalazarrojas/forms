# Educational Equity Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information about the educational experiences and challenges faced by students, teachers, and staff members in a particular school or district, focusing on barriers and opportunities related to educational equity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields:
    *   **Survey title**: Enter a brief title to describe the survey.
    *   **School name**: Enter the name of the school or district.
    *   **School start date**: Enter the date when the survey began.
    *   **Contact email**: Enter the email address to contact the school or district.
    *   **Contact phone**: Enter the phone number to contact the school or district.
    *   **Contact name**: Enter the name of the school or district representative.
2.  For each question, provide a brief description of the barrier or opportunity.
3.  Select the frequency of the barrier or opportunity.
4.  Select the impact of the barrier or opportunity.
5.  Enter any additional comments about the barrier or opportunity.
6.  Review and submit the form.
