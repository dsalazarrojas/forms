# School Communication Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from schools on their communication channels and strategies. It's meant to help schools reflect on their communication effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the correct options for each field. Make sure to fill out all required fields. If a field is optional, you can choose to fill it out or leave it blank. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Email** (`school_email`): Enter the email address of your school.
* **School Phone Number** (`school_phone_number`): Enter the phone number of your school.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`): Enter the email address of the parent.
* **Parent Phone Number** (`parent_phone_number`): Enter the phone number of the parent.
* **Staff Email** (`staff_email`): Enter the email address of your staff.
* **Staff Phone Number** (`staff_phone_number`): Enter the phone number of your staff.
* **School Website** (`school_website`): Enter the website URL of your school.
* **School Facebook** (`school_facebook`): Select the correct Facebook option for your school.
* **School Twitter** (`school_twitter`): Select all relevant social media platforms your school uses.
* **Parent Portal** (`parent_portal`): Select the correct option for your parent portal.
* **Staff Portal** (`staff_portal`): Select the correct option for your staff portal.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`): Select all relevant communication channels your school uses.
* **Most Effective Communication Channel** (`effective_communication_channels`): Select the communication channel you think is most effective.
