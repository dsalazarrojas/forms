# Faculty Collaboration Effectiveness Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Faculty Collaboration Effectiveness Index Survey is a form designed to gather feedback on faculty collaboration within the institution. This survey aims to understand the current state of collaboration among faculty members, identifying areas of strength and weakness to inform future initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into several sections, each addressing a different aspect of faculty collaboration.
2. Please complete each section thoroughly and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. You can skip sections that are not relevant to your experience or perspective.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Faculty Collaboration (Required)** (`faculty_collaboration`, `number`, required/required): This question assesses the overall effectiveness of faculty collaboration. Please provide a score between 1 and 5, with 1 being the least effective and 5 being the most effective.
* **Communication with Colleagues (Required)** (`communication_with_colleagues`, `select_multiple`, required/required): This section asks about your communication with colleagues. Please select all that apply from the options:
	+ Option 1: Regular meetings and discussions
	+ Option 2: Ad-hoc meetings and discussions as needed
	+ Option 3: Limited or no communication
* **Challenges (Required)** (`challenges`, `text`, required/required): Describe any challenges you have faced while working with colleagues.
* **Suggestions (Optional)** (`suggestions`, `text`, required/false): Provide any suggestions for improving faculty collaboration.
* **Email (Optional)** (`email`, `email`, required/false): If you have any suggestions or comments that are not suitable for other fields, please provide your email address so that we can follow up with you.
