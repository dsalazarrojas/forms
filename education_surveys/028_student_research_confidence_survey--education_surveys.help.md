# Student Research Confidence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Research Confidence Survey is a form designed to gather information from students about their confidence in conducting research. This form will help identify areas where students need additional support and allow for targeted support plans to be created.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the `student_name` field.
2. Rate your confidence in research skills using the `research_skills_rating` field. This field is a select-one field with ratings from 1-10.
3. Check the services you need help with in the `research_support_needs` field. This field is a select-multiple field with options for different types of research support.
4. If you need help with anything else, describe it in the `feedback` field. This field is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or information.
5. Enter your email address in the `email` field. This field is optional.
6. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field. This field is optional.
7. Enter your date of birth in the `date_of_birth` field. This field is optional and is in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
8. Enter the time of day you are best reached at in the `time_of_day` field. This field is optional.
9. Select your student major from the `student_major` field. This field is a select-one field with different major options.
10. Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 2. student_name
* **student_name** (`student_name`, text, optional): A field to fill in your name.

### 3. research_skills_rating
* **research_skills_rating** (`research_skills_rating`, select_one, optional): A field to rate your confidence in research skills, from 1-10.

### 4. research_support_needs
* **research_support_needs** (`research_support_needs`, select_multiple, true): A field to select the services you need help with, such as Information on research methods and skills, help with data analysis, and Guidance on literature review and academic writing.

### 5. research_support_needs_other
* **research_support_needs_other** (`research_support_needs_other`, text, optional): A field to describe any additional help you need that is not listed in the previous field.

### 6. feedback
* **feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): A field to provide any additional comments or information.

### 7. email
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): A field to enter your email address.

### 8. phone
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): A field to enter your phone number.

### 9. date_of_birth
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): A field to enter your date of birth, in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.

### 10. time_of_day
* **time_of_day** (`time_of_day`, time, optional): A field to enter the time of day you are best reached at.

### 11. student_major
* **student_major** (`student_major`, select_one, false): A field to select your student major from the available options.
