**Governance Theories Survey - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your familiarity with various governance theories, practices, and approaches in your organization. This survey aims to understand your current state of knowledge and practices related to governance, including leadership teams, research, and data collection. Your input will help us better understand your needs and inform our training and support programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2.  Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3.  You can select multiple options for select_multiple type fields.
4.  You can only select one option for select_one type fields.
5.  Use the notes field to provide any additional information you feel is relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Governance Theories**: This is the first question, and you can type in your thoughts about governance theories as you understand them.
* **Organizational Practices**: Similar to the first question, this is where you can provide your thoughts about organizational practices related to governance.
* **Familiarity**: This question asks about your level of familiarity with the options provided. If you are familiar with an option, simply select it. If not, select "No".
*  **Leadership Teams**: This question asks if you have a leadership team that is familiar with governance theories and practices. Select "Yes" if you do.
* **Consultants**: This question asks about your use of consultants in your organization. Select "Yes" if you have used or are using consultants.
* **Researchers**: This question is similar to the previous ones, asking about your organization's research team's familiarity with governance theories and practices.
* **Data Collection**: This question asks you to input a number to indicate how much data you collect on governance practices. You can type in a whole number.
* **Submission Tracking**: This is a free-form field where you can provide any additional information about how you track submissions in your organization.

## Tips

- If you're unsure about any of the questions or options, please feel free to reach out to your supervisor or support team for guidance.
- If you need more time to complete the form, do not worry and save your progress. You can always return to the form later.
- Your input is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our support and training programs!
