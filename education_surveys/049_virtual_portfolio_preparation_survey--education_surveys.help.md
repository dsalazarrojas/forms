# Virtual Portfolio Preparation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information from participants in a virtual portfolio preparation project.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, ensure that you have all the necessary information ready. If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to ask. Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly, as the data collected will be used for project evaluation and assessment. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, Required/Optional): Please enter your name as it would be used in official contexts (e.g., your university name, institutional ID, or other identifiers). 
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the institution you are associated with (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required/Optional): Enter your institutional email address, or a personal email address if you don't have one linked to your institution. 
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, Required/Optional): Choose one of the following roles:
	+ Academic
	+ Non-academic
* **Department** (`department`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the department you are associated with (if applicable).
* **Portfolio URL** (`portfolio_url`, text, Required/Optional): If you have a publicly accessible portfolio URL, enter it here. 
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, Required/Optional): Add any additional comments or information that might be helpful in evaluating your portfolio.
* **Date** (`date`, date, Required/Optional): Enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., 2022-01-01). 
* **Time** (`time`, time, Required/Optional): Enter the time in the format `HH:MM` (e.g., 12:00). 
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, select_multiple, Required/Optional): Check all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Portfolio Type** (`portfolio_type`, select_multiple, Required/Optional): Check all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Course** (`course`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the course you are associated with (if applicable).
* **Portfolio URL** (`portfolio_url`, text, Required/Optional): If you have a publicly accessible portfolio URL, enter it here. 
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, Required/Optional): Enter your name or identifier as the submitter of this form.

## Tips
- Ensure the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the form administrators.
- If you need to update your information, fill out the form again and note the changes you've made.
