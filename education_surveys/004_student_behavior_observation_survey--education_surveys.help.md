# Student Behavior Observation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Behavior Observation Survey is a tool for educators to report their observations of students' behavior during class or other school activities. This form helps gather information about student behavior and provides valuable insights for educators to support student learning and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form for each student observation.
* Answer all required fields.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
* Select the relevant options for student behaviors and observed behaviors.
* Use the date and time fields to record the date and time of the observation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Questions (Text, Optional):** This field is for any additional comments or questions you have about the student's behavior.
* **Student Behaviors (Select One, Optional):** Check the box next to the student behavior(s) you observed during this period.
* **Observed Behaviors (Select Multiple, Optional):** Check the box(es) next to the student behavior(s) you observed during this period.
* **Student Interactions (Text, Optional):** Provide any additional comments about how the student interacted with others or their environment.
* **Educator Comments (Text, Optional):** Share any notes or comments about the student's behavior or performance.
* **Date Observed (Date, Optional):** Record the date of the observation.
* **Time Observed (Time, Optional):** Record the time of the observation.
