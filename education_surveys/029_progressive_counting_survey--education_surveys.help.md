# Progressive Counting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Progressive Counting Survey is designed to assess an individual's progressive counting abilities and habits, including counting strategies, speed, accuracy, and self-assessment of counting skills. This form is intended for research or educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions on each page of the form in the order they are presented.
2. Read each question carefully and select the most accurate response for your counting habits and preferences.
3. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you or your counting habits.
4. If you have any questions or need help, please contact the survey administrator or researcher.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (Page 1)
	+ This page provides a brief introduction to the survey and asks you to confirm that you understand the purpose and guidelines.
* **How many fingers do you have?** (question_1)
	+ This question asks about your physical ability to count your own body parts.
* **Which of the following are true for you?** (question_2)
	+ This question presents multiple-choice options for your counting strategies, such as using fingers, counting out loud, or counting silently.
* **How often do you count slowly?** (question_3)
	+ This question inquires about your preferred counting pace.
* **When counting, what strategies do you use?** (question_4)
	+ This question asks about the methods you employ when counting, such as visualizing objects, using counting aids, or relying on intuition.
* **How would you rate your confidence in counting?** (question_5)
	+ This question measures your self-assessment of your counting abilities and confidence.
* **How would you rate your speed in counting?** (question_6)
	+ This question asks about your perceived speed in counting and comparison with others.
* **How would you rate your accuracy in counting?** (question_7)
	+ This question evaluates your self-assessment of your accuracy in counting.
* **Date** (question_8)
	+ This question asks for a specific date, such as the date when you last counted accurately or consistently.
* **Time** (question_9)
	+ This question asks for a specific time, such as the time you last counted quickly or with accuracy.
* **How many objects can you count correctly?** (question_10)
	+ This question inquires about your counting capacity and accuracy with a specific number of objects.
* **How many objects can you count with ease?** (question_11)
	+ This question asks about your counting ease and accuracy with a specific number of objects.
* **How many objects can you count quickly?** (question_12)
	+ This question measures your counting speed and accuracy with a specific number of objects.
* **How many objects can you count correctly under pressure?** (question_13)
	+ This question inquires about your counting accuracy and pressure tolerance with a specific number of objects.
* **How many objects can you count consistently?** (question_14)
	+ This question asks about your counting consistency and accuracy with a specific number of objects.
* **How many objects can you count with accuracy under distraction?** (question_15)
	+ This question measures your counting accuracy and focus under distraction with a specific number of objects.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your abilities.
* If you are unsure about a question, please choose the option that best represents your counting habits and preferences.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey. Your input will help us better understand your counting habits and preferences.
