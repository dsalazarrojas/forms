# Data Privacy Awareness In Schools Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about data privacy awareness and concerns from various stakeholders in schools, including students, parents, staff, and teachers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category that best describes your role in the school:
	* Student
	* Parent
	* Staff (Teacher, Administrator, etc.)
	* Teacher
2. For each question, choose the answer that best reflects your level of familiarity or concern:
	* Highly Familiar
	* Somewhat Familiar
	* Not Familiar
3. If you select "Not Familiar" for any of the risks, please specify the reason in the space provided.

	+ Highly Familiar: You have a good understanding of these risks.
	+ Somewhat Familiar: You have some knowledge, but may need more clarification or education.
	+ Not Familiar: You are not aware of these risks.
