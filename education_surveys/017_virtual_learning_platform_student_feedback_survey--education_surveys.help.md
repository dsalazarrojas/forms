# Virtual Learning Platform Student Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Learning Platform Student Feedback Survey is designed to collect your thoughts and opinions on your experience with the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the introduction carefully to understand the context and purpose of the form.
2. Answer the questions based on your honest experience with the platform.
3. You can answer as many or as few questions as you like, depending on your comfort level.
4. Be specific and provide as much detail as possible in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Experience**:
	* This question asks for your overall assessment of your experience with the platform, from Excellent to Bad.
* **Course Difficulty**:
	* This question asks for your rating of the difficulty level of the course, from Easy to Hard.
* **Interaction with Instructor**:
	* This question asks for your rating of your interaction with the instructor, from Excellent to Bad.
* **Course Content**:
	* This question asks for your rating of the quality of the course content, from Excellent to Bad.
* **Feedback**:
	* This question is for any specific feedback or suggestions you have for the platform.
* **Additional Comments**:
	* This question is for any additional comments or insights you may have about your experience with the platform.
* **Email**:
	* This question is for providing your email address if you wish to receive follow-up questions or updates.
* **Phone**:
	* This question is for providing your phone number if you wish to receive follow-up calls.
* **Date**:
	* This question is for timestamping your feedback, and is optional.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide specific feedback.
* Answer as many or as few questions as you like.
* Provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your email or phone number.
