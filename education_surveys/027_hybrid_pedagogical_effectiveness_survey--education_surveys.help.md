# Hybrid Pedagogical Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is a general data collection form, intended to gather information from respondents. It appears to be used for tracking and evaluation purposes.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title at the top of the page.
2. Select the category that best fits your response.
3. Enter a brief description of your response.
4. Provide your submission date and time.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
7. Add any additional notes.
8. Select all relevant options from the list of options provided.
9. Enter any evaluation questions or comments.
10. Add any final comments or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief title for the form, if desired.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category that best describes your response.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your response.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you are submitting the form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you are submitting the form.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Select Options** (`options`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all relevant options from the list provided.
* **Evaluation Questions** (`evaluation_questions`, `note`, required: false): Enter any evaluation questions or comments.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any final comments or observations.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* You can select multiple options from the list of options provided.
* If you have any evaluation questions or comments, please enter them in the "Evaluation Questions" field.
* If you have any final comments or observations, please enter them in the "Comments" field.
