# Social Movements And Their Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to collect information about social movements and their impact. It is intended to gather data on various aspects of social movements, including awareness, participation, impact, and demographic information. The collected data will be used for research or analysis purposes, likely to better understand social movements and their effects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select the correct options for each question based on your experience or knowledge.
3. Fill in the text fields with detailed and relevant information.
4. Ensure you have permission to provide the requested information and that you are providing accurate data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Awareness** (`awareness_section`, text, optional): This section is intended to assess your awareness of social movements. Please provide a brief description of your understanding of social movements.
* **Participation** (`participation_section`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to your participation in social movements. Please choose 'Yes' if you have participated in any social movements.
* **Impact** (`impact_section`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you believe social movements have had an impact on your life or community.
* **Awareness and Participation** (`awareness_participation`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your awareness and participation in social movements.
* **Participation Level** (`participation_level`, number, optional): If you have participated in social movements, please indicate the level of your participation on a scale of 1-10.
* **Awareness Level** (`awareness_level`, number, optional): If you have been aware of social movements, please indicate the level of your awareness on a scale of 1-10.
* **Impact Outcomes** (`impact_outcomes`, text, optional): Describe the outcomes or results of social movements in your experience or community.
* **Impact Outcomes Description** (`impact_outcomes_description`, text, optional): Provide a more detailed description of the impact outcomes of social movements.
* **Social Movement** (`social_movement`, select_one, optional): Choose the social movement that best describes your experience or association.
* **Social Movement Description** (`social_movement_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the social movement you chose.
* **Research Interest** (`research_interest`, select_multiple, optional): If you are interested in researching social movements, please choose 'Yes' if it applies.
* **Nonprofit Participant Information** (`nonprofit_participant_info`, select_one, optional): If you are a participant in a nonprofit, please choose 'Yes' to indicate your association.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you wish to be contacted for further information or research purposes, please provide your email address.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please provide your name if you wish to be credited or acknowledged for your input.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with an organization, please provide the name of the organization.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Describe your role within the organization you are affiliated with, if any.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): If you are part of a department within the organization, please provide the name of the department.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Provide the location of your workplace or affiliation, if applicable.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you wish to be contacted, please provide your phone number.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional): Provide any additional contact information or instructions.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): If you are tracking changes or progress, please provide the date of your participation or association.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If you are tracking changes or progress, please provide the time of your participation or association.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If you wish to provide demographic information, please provide your date of birth.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): If you wish to provide demographic information, please indicate your age.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Choose your gender from the provided options.
 
## Tips
* Please answer all questions carefully and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any question, please provide a brief explanation or note to clarify your response.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions to provide the requested information.
* If you wish to be contacted for further information or research, please provide accurate contact information.
