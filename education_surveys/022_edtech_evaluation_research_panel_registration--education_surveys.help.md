# edtech-evaluation-research-panel-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect metadata and settings for an educational research panel evaluation, allowing users to create, manage, and customize their evaluation forms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Category** field with the correct category for your form, which is "Education Surveys".
2. Enter a brief **Description** of your form, if necessary.
3. Select the correct **Output Format** for your form from the options provided (JSON, XML, CSV, or TXT).
4. Choose the desired **Submit Button Color** for your form, from the options provided (#0078f0, #ff0000, or #008000).
5. Enter the text to be displayed on the **Submit Button**, which will be shown to users.
6. Finally, click the submit button to save your form's metadata and settings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category (Education Surveys)**: Select the category for your form from the available options. This is a required field.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of your form. This is an optional field.
* **Output File**: Select the format in which you want your data to be exported (JSON, XML, CSV, or TXT).
* **Output Format**: Choose how your data will be exported and used by other tools or systems.
* **Submit Button Text**: Enter the text to be displayed on the submit button. This is a required field.
* **Submit Button Color**: Select the color of the submit button from the options provided. This is a required field.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is currently not used. Please disregard.
* **Output File**: This field is currently not used. Please disregard.
* **Creator**: Enter your name or username in this field.
* **Owner**: Enter your name or username in this field.
* **Version**: Enter the current version of your form in this field.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name or username in this field.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and correctly.
* Use the correct category for your form.
* Choose the right output format for your data.
* Customize the submit button text and color to match your form's branding and theme.
* Double-check your form's metadata and settings before submitting.
