# Impact Of Reading On Emotions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your reading habits and emotional state. Please answer these questions accurately to provide valuable insights into the impact of reading on your emotions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages and select the corresponding answers for each field. You can select multiple options for fields that ask for multiple responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Reading Habits
- **Reading Habits** (`reading_habits`, select_multiple, optional): Please select whether or not you engage in various reading activities (e.g., reading for pleasure, for work, or out of habit).

### 2. How Often Do You Read
- **How Often Do You Read** (`reading_frequency`, text, optional): Briefly describe how often you engage in reading activities (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, rarely).

### 3. Emotional State
- **Emotional State** (`emotional_state`, select_one, optional): Please select whether or not you experience a positive emotional state while reading (e.g., relaxed, happy, or sad).

### 4. How Would You Describe Yourself
- **How Would You Describe Yourself** (`emotional_state_2`, select_one, optional): Choose a word that best describes your emotional state while reading (e.g., calm, anxious, or content).

### 5. What Materials Do You Enjoy Reading
- **What Materials Do You Enjoy Reading** (`reading_materials`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of materials you enjoy reading (e.g., fiction, non-fiction, comics, or newspapers).

### 6. How Much Time Do You Spend Reading
- **How Much Time Do You Spend Reading** (`reading_time`, time, optional): Enter the amount of time you typically spend reading (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, etc.).

### 7. Where Do You Typically Read
- **Where Do You Typically Read** (`reading_location`, select_one, optional): Choose a location where you usually read (e.g., home, office, or outdoors).

### 8. How Often Do You Engage In Reading
- **How Often Do You Engage In Reading** (`reading_frequency_2`, text, optional): Briefly describe how often you engage in reading (e.g., frequently, occasionally, rarely, or never).

### 9. Reading Frequency 3
- **Reading Frequency 3** (`reading_frequency_3`, text, optional): Enter another description of how often you engage in reading (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, etc.).

### 10. Emotional State 3
- **Emotional State 3** (`emotional_state_3`, select_one, optional): Please select whether or not you experience a specific emotional state while reading (e.g., calm, excited, or bored).

### 11. Reading Frequency 4
- **Reading Frequency 4** (`reading_frequency_4`, text, optional): Enter another description of how often you engage in reading (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, etc.).

### 12. Emotional State 4
- **Emotional State 4** (`emotional_state_4`, select_one, optional): Choose a word that best describes your emotional state while reading (e.g., happy, anxious, or content).

## Tips
- Answer questions honestly to provide valuable insights.
- Select multiple options when required or if they apply to you.
- If a field is optional, feel free to skip it if you don't have an answer.
