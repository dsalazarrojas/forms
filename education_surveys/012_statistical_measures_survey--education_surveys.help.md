# Statistical Measures Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Statistical Measures Survey is a questionnaire designed to gather data from educators on their habits and self-assessment of their use of statistical measures in education. This data will be used to inform the development of new statistical measures and tools for educators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Answer the questions about your habits and self-assessment of your use of statistical measures in education.
2. Choose the correct answer for each question.
3. Enter your email and phone number to help us contact you for further clarification or to send you updates on new statistical measures and tools.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How Often Do Educators Adopt New Statistical Measures?** (`how_often_do_educators_adopt`, select_one, required):
	+ This question asks about your habits when it comes to adopting new statistical measures in education. Please select the answer that best describes your frequency of adoption.
* **How Familiar Do Educators Feel with Statistical Measures?** (`how_familiar_do_educators`, select_one, required):
	+ This question asks about your level of familiarity with statistical measures. Please select the answer that best describes your level of familiarity.
* **How Confident Do Educators Feel with Statistical Measures?** (`how_confident_do_educators`, select_one, required):
	+ This question asks about your level of confidence with statistical measures. Please select the answer that best describes your level of confidence.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required):
	+ Please enter your email address to help us contact you for further clarification or to send you updates on new statistical measures and tools.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required):
	+ Please enter your phone number to help us contact you for further clarification or to send you updates on new statistical measures and tools.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any comments or feedback, please use the "Comments" field to provide them.
* If you need help or have any questions, feel free to contact us at the email address or phone number you provided.
