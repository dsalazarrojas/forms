# Peer Tutoring Dynamics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool to collect feedback from peer tutors and learners about the peer tutoring services. It aims to gather information on the frequency of peer tutoring roles, the effectiveness of services, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read each question carefully and answer it honestly.
2. Choose the most relevant option for each select-one or select_multiple field.
3. Enter your comments and suggestions in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction (Text Field)**: This is a brief text field for you to provide any background information about your experience with peer tutoring services.
* **Peer Tutoring Roles Frequency (Select One)**: This field asks about the frequency of your involvement in different peer tutoring roles. Please select the most relevant option from the list.
	+ role1: This is the most frequent role you play in peer tutoring.
	+ role2: This is the second most frequent role you play in peer tutoring.
	+ role3: This is the third most frequent role you play in peer tutoring.
* **Frequency of Tutoring (Number Field)**: Enter the number of times you have participated in peer tutoring services.
* **Effectiveness of Services (Select Multiple)**: This field asks about the effectiveness of peer tutoring services. Please select all the relevant options from the list.
	+ Option 1: Peer tutoring services are very effective.
	+ Option 2: Peer tutoring services are somewhat effective.
	+ Option 3: Peer tutoring services are not very effective.
* **Challenges (Text Field)**: This is a brief text field for you to mention any challenges you have faced while using peer tutoring services.
* **Suggestions for Improvement (Text Field)**: This is a text field for you to provide any suggestions for improvement of peer tutoring services.
* **Comments (Text Field)**: This is a text field for you to provide any additional comments about your experience with peer tutoring services.
* **Email (Email Field)**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Phone (Text Field)**: Enter your phone number for any further questions or clarification.
* **Date Last Participated (Date Field)**: Enter the date of your last participation in peer tutoring services.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* You can skip any question if it's not relevant to your experience.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to us.
