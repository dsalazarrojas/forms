# Pedagogical Innovation Showcase Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pedagogical Innovation Showcase Consent Form collects user information and consent for the event. The form is designed to be completed by users who want to share their projects, ideas, and preferences with others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information on the first page: 
   - Enter your name
   - Optionally, select your teaching projects 
2. Move on to the next page to provide details on your project:
   - Enter a title for your project
   - Select the project lead
   - Select any relevant student work involved in your project
3. On the final page:
   - Enter your email address (for communication purposes)
   - Enter your phone number (if applicable)
   - Select a date and time for your project (if applicable)
   - Add any additional notes about your project
4. Finally, select your consent option to agree to share your project information

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_information`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Teaching Projects** (`teaching_projects`, select_one, optional): Select the projects you are involved in or have an interest in.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your project that summarizes it.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, select_one, optional): Select the lead person for your project.
* **Student Work** (`student_work`, select_multiple, optional): Select any student work related to your project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for your project (if applicable).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time for your project (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your project.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Select your consent option to agree to share your project information.
