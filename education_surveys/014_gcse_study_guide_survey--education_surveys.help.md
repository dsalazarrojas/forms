# GCSE Study Guide Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students who have used the GCSE Study Guide. Your input will help us improve the guide to better support future students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question based on your personal experience with the GCSE Study Guide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the purpose of this survey?**: This question is meant to understand how you used the guide and what you expected from it.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school that provided you with the guide.
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your student record.
* **Subject Area**: Please select the subject area that the guide was intended to cover.
* **Useful Features**: Select all the features from the guide that you found useful during your studies.
* **Not Useful Features**: Select all the features that you did not find useful during your studies.
* **Guide Title**: Enter a title that reflects the main topic or theme of the guide.
* **Helpfulness Score**: Enter a score between 1-5 that represents how helpful you found the guide during your studies.
* **Difficulty Level**: Enter a score between 1-5 that represents how difficult you found the guide to use.
* **Time Spent**: Enter the amount of time you spent using the guide.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Enter any suggestions you have for improving the guide.
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date you completed the guide.
* **Time Taken**: Enter the amount of time it took you to complete the guide.
* **Select Multiple Usefulness Score 2**: This is a duplicate of the Helpfulness Score question, please select a score between 1-5.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments you have about the guide.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have about the guide.
