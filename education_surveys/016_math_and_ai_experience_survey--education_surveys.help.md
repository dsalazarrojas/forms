# Math And AI Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Math And AI Experience Survey is designed to gather information about your math and AI experience. This includes assessing your math proficiency, AI tool usage, and your background in math education. Your input will help us understand your skills and how you apply AI in your work.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by reviewing each page of the survey.
- Answer each question according to your personal experience and knowledge.
- Be as accurate and detailed as possible.
- You may select multiple options for questions that allow for multiple answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Math Proficiency
 **Math Proficiency** (Number, Optional)
 This question is a self-assessment of your math skills. Rate your proficiency on a scale from 0 to 10, with 0 being basic understanding and 10 being advanced knowledge.

### 2. AI Tool Usage
 **AI Tool Usage** (Select Multiple, Optional)
 Select all AI tools you have used in your work or personal projects. Please be as specific as possible.

### 3. Integrating AI into Tasks
 **Integrating AI into tasks** (Note, Optional)
 This is an open-ended question where you can describe your experiences with integrating AI into your work or personal projects.

### 4. Math Education Background
 **Math education background** (Text, Optional)
 Provide a brief description of your educational background in math. This includes any degrees or courses you have taken related to math.

### 5. AI Proficiency
 **AI Proficiency** (Number, Optional)
 Rate your proficiency in AI on a scale of 0 to 10, with 0 being basic understanding and 10 being advanced knowledge.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your assessments.
- If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
- Your input is crucial in helping us understand your experience and skills. Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey.
