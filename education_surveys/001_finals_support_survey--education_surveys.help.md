# Finals Support Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from students about their experience with the support services provided during finals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any feedback or suggestions, feel free to include them in the comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your school.
* **Finals Support Survey** (`finals_support_survey`, text, optional): This field is automatically populated with the name of the survey you are completing. Please do not change it.
* **Resource prophets** (`resource_prophets`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes that apply to your experience with the resources available during finals support.
* **Resources available** (`resource_availability`, select_one, optional): Select the best answer to indicate the availability of resources during finals support.
* **Support Team** (`support_team`, note, optional): Please use this field to provide any feedback or comments about the support team that assisted you.
* **Stress prophets** (`stress_prophets`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes that apply to your experience with stress during finals support.
* **Improvement ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes that apply to your ideas for improving the finals support process.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with finals support.
* **Resources stress** (`resource_stress`, select_one, optional): Select the best answer to indicate the level of stress experienced with resources during finals support.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select the best answer to indicate your overall satisfaction with the finals support process.
* **Submission** (`submission`, select_one, optional): Select whether you submitted your finals support request or not.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further questions or discussions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further questions or discussions.
* **Resource quality** (`resource_quality_1`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes that apply to your experience with the quality of resources during finals support.
* **Resources available** (`resource_availability_2`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes that apply to your experience with the availability of resources during finals support.

## Tips
* Please be honest and truthful in your answers.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please reach out to the support team.
* Your feedback is valuable and will help us improve the finals support process.
