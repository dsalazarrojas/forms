# University Outreach Program Success Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Outreach Program Success Poll is designed to gather feedback from attendees of events organized by the University Outreach Program. Your input is crucial in helping us evaluate the success of our events and improve them in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and answer all the questions that apply to your experience.
2. Select the correct options for each question based on your actual experience.
3. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is this survey about?**
	+ This question is to ensure you are providing feedback on the correct event.
	+ Please describe the event you are providing feedback on.
* **Event Type**
	+ Select the type of event you attended (Conference, Workshop, Seminar, Webinar, or Other).
	+ This helps us understand the type of event and tailor future programs accordingly.
* **Event Date**
	+ Enter the date you attended the event.
	+ This helps us track the events and their impact on your experience.
* **Event Time**
	+ Enter the time you attended the event.
	+ This helps us schedule events more effectively in the future.
* **Number of attendees**
	+ Estimate the number of attendees you saw at the event.
	+ This helps us understand the event's reach and impact.
* **How satisfied are you with the event?**
	+ Select how satisfied you were with the event (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied).
	+ This helps us gauge the success of the event and identify areas for improvement.
* **Reasons**
