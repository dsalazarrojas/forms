# Individualized Education Plan Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Individualized Education Plan Survey is designed to collect information from educators and parents to help create tailored educational plans for students. This form helps gather feedback and input to support student success.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form instructions and questions carefully.
2. Ensure you answer all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk).
3. Provide honest and thorough responses.
4. If you're unsure about a question, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educator Feedback** (1): Share your thoughts and observations about the student's educational progress and needs.
* **Parent Input** (2): Share your input and concerns about the student's educational needs and goals.
* **Tailored Plans Effectiveness** (3): Select all the options that apply to how effective you think the tailored plans are in supporting the student's educational progress.
	+ Option 1: Very Effective
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Effective
	+ Option 3: Not Very Effective
* **Student Support** (4): Select the option that best describes the student's support needs.
	+ Option 1: High Support Needed
	+ Option 2: Medium Support Needed
	+ Option 3: Low Support Needed
* **Feedback Frequency** (5): Enter the frequency at which you would like to receive feedback from educators and staff. 

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the form creators or administrators.
- The information gathered will be used to create a comprehensive and tailored educational plan for the student.
