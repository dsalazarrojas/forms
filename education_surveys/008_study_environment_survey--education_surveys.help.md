# Study Environment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your study environment. The purpose of this form is to help us understand your experience and satisfaction with your campus and study settings. Your responses will help us identify areas of improvement and provide better services to you and your peers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information in the corresponding fields. Each question is designed to gather specific details about your study environment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Setting** (`survey_form_heading_1`, text, required): This field asks for a brief description of your study setting, which can be a classroom, library, or any other study space.
* **School Name** (`school_name_1`, text, required): Please enter the name of your school.
* **Campus Environment** (`campus_environment_1`, text, required): Describe the campus environment, including any notable features or issues.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating_1`, text, required): Rate your satisfaction level with your study environment on a scale of 1-5.
* **Study Settings** (`study_settings_1`, text, required): Describe your study settings, including the type of equipment and materials available.
* **Resources** (`resources_1`, text, required): List the resources available to you, such as computers, internet access, or library materials.
* **Support Services** (`support_services_1`, text, required): Describe any support services available, such as academic advisors or student organizations.
* **Student Population** (`student_population_1`, text, required): Describe the student population, including demographics and diversity.
* **Academic Support** (`academic_support_1`, text, required): Describe the level of academic support available, such as tutoring or academic advising.
* **Campus Facilities** (`campus_facilities_1`, text, required): List the campus facilities available, such as libraries, computer labs, or recreation centers.
* **Facilities Quality** (`facilities_quality_1`, text, required): Rate the quality of campus facilities on a scale of 1-5.
* **Campus Safety** (`campus_safety_1`, text, required): Describe any safety concerns or issues on campus.
* **Campus Culture** (`campus_culture_1`, text, required): Describe the campus culture, including diversity and inclusivity.
* **Student Culture** (`student_culture_1`, text, required): Describe the student culture, including student organizations and activities.
* **Campus Climate** (`campus_climate_1`, text, required): Describe the overall climate of the campus, including weather and environment.
* **Student Population Size** (`student_population_size_1`, text, required): Describe the size of the student population.
* **Student Population Growth** (`student_population_growth_1`, text, required): Describe the growth rate of the student population.
* **Support Services Rating** (`support_services_rating_1`, text, required): Rate the quality of support services on a scale of 1-5.
* **Facilities Quality Rating** (`facilities_quality_rating_1`, text, required): Rate the quality of facilities on a scale of 1-5.
* **Campus Safety Rating** (`campus_safety_rating_1`, text, required): Rate the safety of the campus on a scale of 1-5.
* **Campus Culture Rating** (`campus_culture_rating_1`, text, required): Rate the campus culture on a scale of 1-5.
* **Student Culture Rating** (`student_culture_rating_1`, text, required): Rate the student culture on a scale of 1-5.
* **Campus Climate Rating** (`campus_climate_rating_1`, text, required): Rate the campus climate on a scale of 1-5.
* **Student Population Size Rating** (`student_population_size_rating_1`, text, required): Rate the size of the student population on a scale of 1-5.
* **Student Population Growth Rating** (`student_population_growth_rating_1`, text, required): Rate the growth rate of the student population on a scale of 1-5.
