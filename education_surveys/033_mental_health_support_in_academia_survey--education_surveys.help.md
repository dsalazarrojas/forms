# Mental Health Support In Academia Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about mental health support services on campus and gather feedback on the current state of mental health services. The purpose of this form is to help administrators and support staff better understand the needs and concerns of students, faculty, and staff regarding mental health services on campus.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page to understand the purpose and context of the survey.
2. Answer the question about campus services to describe your experience with the mental health support services on campus.
3. Check the boxes to indicate whether you face any barriers to accessing mental health services.
4. Provide a number rating (e.g., 1-5) for your level of satisfaction with the mental health services on campus.
5. If you have additional comments, please provide them in the text field at the bottom of the page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campus Services** (`campus_services`, text, required/optional): Describe your experience with the mental health support services on campus, such as counseling services, therapy groups, or support hotlines. Please provide any details about your experience, positive or negative.
* **Barriers** (`barriers`, select_one, required/true): Check the box if you face any barriers to accessing mental health services on campus. You may select only one option.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, required/optional): Rate your level of satisfaction with the mental health services on campus, using a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied".
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions about the mental health services on campus, please provide them here.
