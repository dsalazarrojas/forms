# Scholarship Workshop Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scholarship Workshop Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback from participants who have attended a Scholarship Workshop. Your input will help us evaluate the effectiveness of the workshop and make necessary improvements for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your general information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Share your feedback about your experience with the workshop.
3. Rate your experience with the workshop using the provided ratings.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can add them in the comments section.
5. Enter your email address and phone number to allow us to contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (Page 1): This section is where you can provide your name, email, and phone number. Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* **Feedback** (Page 2): Share your honest feedback about your experience with the workshop. This will help us understand what worked well and what didn't.
* **Rating** (Page 2): Rate your experience using the provided ratings. This will give us an idea of your overall satisfaction.
* **Comments** (Page 3): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please add them here. This is an optional field, but your input is valuable.
* **Email** (Page 4): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (Page 5): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Name** (Page 6): Enter your name, as this will be used to identify you in our records.
* **Email 2** (Page 7): Enter your email address again, this is a duplicate field for verification purposes.
* **Name 2** (Page 8): Enter your name again, this is a duplicate field for verification purposes.
* **Rating 2** (Page 9): Rate your experience again using the provided ratings. This is an optional field.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be honest and provide constructive feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact us at [insert contact info].
