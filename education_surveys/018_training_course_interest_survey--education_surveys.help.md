# Training Course Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users about their interest in a training course. This includes their preferred category, topics, frequency of attendance, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully to understand the questions and options provided.
2. Select the category that best describes your interest.
3. Choose the topics you are interested in (you can select multiple options).
4. Indicate how often you would like to attend a training course.
5. Enter your preferred email address so we can contact you regarding the course.
6. Provide any additional comments about the course.
7. Fill in your preferred schedule and start and end dates for the course.
8. Choose your preferred start and end times for the course.
9. Specify the duration of the course in hours.
10. Indicate the number of participants you expect in the course.
11. Finally, provide any additional comments about the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page provides an overview of the training course.
* **What category are you interested in?** (`categories`, select_one): This question asks you to select the category that best describes your interest in the training course.
* **What topics are you interested in?** (`topics`, select_multiple): Choose the topics you are interested in and want to cover during the course.
* **How often would you like to attend a training course?** (`frequency`, select_one): Select how often you would like to attend the training course.
* **What is your preferred email address?** (`email`, email): Enter your preferred email address so we can contact you regarding the course.
* **Do you have any comments about the training?** (`comments`, text): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the course.
* **What is your schedule like?** (`schedule`, date): Fill in your preferred schedule for the course.
* **When would you like to start the course?** (`start_date`, date): Enter the start date for the course.
* **When would you like to end the course?** (`end_date`, date): Enter the end date for the course.
* **What time of day do you prefer to start the course?** (`start_time`, time): Choose your preferred start time for the course.
* **What time of day do you prefer to end the course?** (`end_time`, time): Choose your preferred end time for the course.
* **How long do you expect the course to last?** (`duration`, number): Specify the duration of the course in hours.
* **How many participants are you expecting in the course?** (`max_participants`, number): Indicate the number of participants you expect in the course.
* **Do you have any comments about the course?** (`comments_2`, text): Provide any additional comments about the course.
