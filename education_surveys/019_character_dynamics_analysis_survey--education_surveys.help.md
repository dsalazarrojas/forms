# Character Dynamics Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to analyze the dynamics between two characters in a given context, providing insights into their relationship's power balance, conflict level, and key interactions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Start by capturing the name of the first character** (`Character A Name`): Enter the name of the first character in the relationship.
2.  **Enter the name of the second character** (`Character B Name`): Enter the name of the second character in the relationship.
3.  **Select the primary relationship type** (`Relationship Type`): Choose the primary relationship type between the characters from the options provided (e.g., Friends, Family, Colleagues, Rivals, Romantic).
4.  **Describe the balance of power between characters** (`Power Balance`): Select the balance of power between the characters from the options provided (e.g., Symmetric, Asymmetric, Shifting).
5.  **Select the conflict level between characters** (`Conflict Level`): Choose the level of conflict between the characters from the options provided (e.g., Low, Moderate, High).
6.  **Describe pivotal scenes or moments** (`Key Interactions`): Provide a brief description of the most important moments that shape the relationship.
7.  **Choose the primary emotional tone of the relationship** (`Emotional Tone`): Select the emotional tone of the relationship from the options provided (e.g., Positive, Neutral, Negative).
8.  **Optional: Add additional notes or recommendations** (`Analyst Notes`): If you have any further insights, observations, or suggestions for the analysis, you can add them here.

*   **-- Character Dynamics Analysis --** (`Header`): This is a title section of the form that captures the relationship's context.

*   **Character A Name** (`Character A Name`): Enter the name of the first character in the relationship.
*   **Character B Name** (`Character B Name`): Enter the name of the second character in the relationship.
*   **Relationship Type** (`Relationship Type`): Choose the primary relationship type between the characters from the options provided.

*   **Emotional Tone** (`Emotional Tone`): Select the emotional tone of the relationship from the options provided.
*   **Analyst Notes** (`Analyst Notes`): Provide additional insights, observations, or suggestions for the analysis.
