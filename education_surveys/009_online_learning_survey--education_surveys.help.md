# Online Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information from users about their online learning experiences, allowing us to improve and refine our course offerings and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the type of course you've taken (Online Course, Offline Course, or Other).
* Enter the name of the course you've taken, if applicable.
* Rate your learning experience (1-5) to indicate how well you feel you learned from the course.
* Rate your instructor's performance (1-5) to provide feedback on their teaching quality.
* Provide any comments about your instructor's rating.
* Indicate whether the course materials were helpful or not.
* Select any technical support you received during the course (True or False).
* Provide any feedback or suggestions you may have.
* Enter your email address and phone number for follow-up contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Type**: Select the type of course you've taken. This can be Online Course, Offline Course, or Other.
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course you've completed, if applicable. This will help us understand which course you're referring to in your feedback.
* **Learning Experience**: Rate your learning experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent. This will help us gauge how well you felt you learned from the course.
* **Instructor Rating**: Rate your instructor's performance from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent. This will help us evaluate their teaching quality.
* **Instructor Rating Comment**: Provide any additional comments about your instructor's rating. This can include specific feedback or praise.
* **Course Materials**: Indicate whether the course materials were helpful or not. Select True if they were helpful, False if they weren't, or Not Sure if you're unsure.
* **Technical Support**: Select any technical support you received during the course. This can be True if you received assistance or False if you didn't.
* **Feedback**: Provide any general feedback or suggestions you may have about your learning experience.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further contact, if needed.

Note: The fields are marked as optional unless marked as "required" in the YAML definition, but it's essential to fill out as many fields as possible to provide a comprehensive and useful feedback.
