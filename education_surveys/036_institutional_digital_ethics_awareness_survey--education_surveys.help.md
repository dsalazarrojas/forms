# Institutional Digital Ethics Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Institutional Digital Ethics Awareness Survey is a tool for institutions to assess and improve their digital ethics policies and practices. This survey aims to help institutions understand their strengths and weaknesses in the area of digital ethics, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the most accurate options for each question.
3. If a question does not apply to your institution, please select "Not applicable" or "Other (please specify)".
4. Fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Is digital ethics training available within the institution?** (`has_digital_ethics_training`): This question asks whether your institution offers digital ethics training to its employees. Select "Yes" if your institution provides regular digital ethics training, or "No" if it does not.
* **How often is digital ethics training provided to employees within the institution?** (`digital_ethics_training_frequency`): This question asks about the frequency of digital ethics training provided by your institution. Select the option that best describes how often your institution provides training:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Rarely
* **How many hours of digital ethics training are provided to employees within the institution?** (`digital_ethics_training_hours`): This question asks about the number of hours of digital ethics training provided by your institution. Enter the actual number of hours.
* **Which of the following areas of digital ethics should be the top priority for the institution?** (`top_priority_areas`): This question asks you to select the most important digital ethics area for your institution. Select all applicable areas:
	+ Digital Security
	+ Data Protection
	+ User Authentication
	+ Digital Signature
	+ Data Breach
	+ Compliance
* **What is the primary goal of digital ethics training within the institution?** (`primary_digital_ethics_goal`): This question asks about the main objective of your institution's digital ethics training. Select the option that best describes your institution's goal:
	+ To increase user engagement
	+ To increase productivity
	+ To improve security
	+ To reduce costs
	+ To enhance customer satisfaction
	+ Other (please specify)
* **How does the institution communicate digital ethics policies to employees?** (`digital_ethics_policy_communication`): This question asks about your institution's method of communicating digital ethics policies. Select the option that best describes your institution's method:
	+ Email
	+ Newsletter
	+ Social Media
	+ Training sessions
	+ Other (please specify)
* **What is the main method used by the institution to ensure digital ethics practices are being followed?** (`digital_ethics_policies_implementation`): This question asks about your institution's method of enforcing digital ethics practices. Select the option that best describes your institution's method:
	+ Audits
	+ Monitoring
	+ Regular checklists
	+ Other (please specify)
* **How often are digital ethics policies reviewed within the institution?** (`digital_ethics_policies_revision`): This question asks about the frequency of reviewing digital ethics policies within your institution. Select the option that best describes your institution's frequency:
	+ Annually
	+ Quarterly
	+ Monthly
	+ Daily
	+ Rarely
* **Are there any digital ethics policies that are specific to a certain department within the institution?** (`departmental_digital_ethics_policies`): This question asks about whether your institution has digital ethics policies specific to certain departments. Select "Yes" if your institution has such policies.
* **Who is responsible for implementing and enforcing digital ethics policies within the institution?** (`responsible_person`): This question asks about your institution's responsibility for implementing and enforcing digital ethics policies. Select the option that best describes your institution's responsibility:
	+ Management
	+ Employees
	+ IT Department
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Are there any digital ethics related incidents that have occurred within the institution?** (`has_digital_ethics_incidents`): This question asks whether your institution has experienced any digital ethics related incidents. Select "Yes" if your institution has experienced such incidents.
* **Output File** (`output_file`): This question is for internal use only and should not be answered by end users.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): This question is for internal use only and should not be answered by end users.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`): This question is for internal use only and should not be answered by end users.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`): This question is for internal use only and should not be answered by end users.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select "Not applicable" or "Other (please specify)".
* This survey is intended to be used as a guide for your institution to improve its digital ethics policies and practices.
