# Career Services Participant Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback on your experience with our career services. Your input will help us understand what works well and where we can improve, enabling us to provide better support in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Your Name**: This field is for your full name, which we'll use to personalize the feedback process.
2. **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for any follow-up questions or discussions.
3. **Type of Career Service Received**: Choose the type of career service you received, such as career coaching, resume review, or interview preparation.
4. **Date of Service**: Enter the date when you received the service.
5. **Name of Career Counselor or Facilitator**: If you remember the name of the person who provided the service, please enter it here.
6. **Have You Used Career Services Before?**: Select how often you've used career services in the past.
7. **Overall Satisfaction with Service**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the service provided.
8. **Did Service Meet Your Expectations?**: Choose how well the service met your expectations.
9. **Knowledge and Expertise of Counselor**: Rate the counselor's knowledge and expertise.
10. **Communication and Clarity of Guidance**: Rate how clearly the counselor explained things.
11. **Personalization of Service to Your Needs**: Rate how well the service was tailored to your needs.
12. **Professional Demeanor of Provider**: Rate the counselor's professionalism and respectfulness.
13. **Use of Time in Session**: Rate how well the time was spent during the session.
14. **Quality and Usefulness of Materials/Tools Provided**: Rate the materials and tools provided.
15. **Did You Receive Actionable Guidance?**: Choose whether you received guidance that you could implement.
16. **Did Service Increase Your Career Confidence?**: Rate how much your career confidence increased after the service.
17. **Will This Service Help Your Career Progression?**: Rate how likely you think this service will help your career progression.
18. **Would You Recommend This Service to Others?**: Choose whether you would recommend this service to others.
19. **Specific Strengths of This Service**: Share what went well in your experience.
20. **Areas for Improvement**: Share what could be improved.
21. **Interest in Follow-up Services**: Choose whether you're interested in follow-up services.
22. **Additional Services You Would Like**: Suggest any other services you might find helpful.
23. **Additional Comments**: Share any other feedback or suggestions.
