# Inclusivity In Academic Policy Development Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from education institutions about their inclusive policies and procedures. This data will help us better understand and develop more effective policies for academic settings.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by providing your education institution's information in the "Education Institution Information" section.
2.  In the "Inclusive Policies" section, describe your institution's policies and procedures related to inclusivity.
3.  In the "Policy Development Process" section, explain how your institution develops and reviews its policies.
4.  In the "Contact Information" section, provide details about your institution's contact person, email, phone number, and department.
5.  Finally, review your submission and provide feedback in the "Submission and Feedback" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Institution Name (page_5)**: Please enter your education institution's full name.
* **Education Institution Details (page_5_1)**: Provide any additional details about your institution.
* **Inclusive (page_12)**: Describe your institution's inclusive policies and procedures.
* **Inclusive Policies (page_11)**: List your institution's specific policies related to inclusivity.
* **Policy Development (page_3)**: Explain how your institution develops and reviews its policies.
* **Policy 1 (page_11_1)**: Enter the name of your first policy.
* **Policy 2 (page_11_2)**: Enter the name of your second policy.
* **Policy 3 (page_11_3)**: Enter the name of your third policy.
* **Policy 4 (page_11_4)**: Enter the name of your fourth policy.
* **Contact Person (page_6_1)**: Enter the name of the person to contact for more information.
* **Email (page_6_2)**: Enter your institution's email address.
* **Phone Number (page_6_3)**: Enter your institution's phone number.
* **Department (page_6_4)**: Enter your institution's department.
* **Submit (page_4)**: Click this button to submit your institution's information.
* **Submit Feedback (page_5_2)**: Provide any feedback about the form or your experience.
* **Thank You (page_9)**: A message to thank you for your submission.
