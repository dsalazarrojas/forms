# Solar Exploration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solar Exploration Survey is designed to understand user preferences and behaviors related to solar exploration experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For select-type questions, choose one of the provided options that best describes your response.
3. For text-type questions, enter your answer in the space provided.
4. For numerical questions, enter a whole number as your response.
5. If a question is optional, you are not required to answer it, but please do so only if you have a relevant answer.
