# Anti Bullying Program Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anti Bullying Program Effectiveness Survey is a tool used to assess the effectiveness of the school's anti-bullying program. This survey is designed to collect feedback from various stakeholders, including teachers, students, staff, parents, and volunteers, to gauge the program's impact on bullying incidents and overall school climate. The data collected will help the school administration refine and improve the program to better support students and maintain a safe and inclusive environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer all required questions.
2. Select your role in the school (Teacher, Student, Staff, Parent, or Volunteer) in the "Respondent role" field.
3. Answer each question based on your perception and involvement with the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Respondent role
• **Respondent role** (Select One): 
  - Select the category that best describes your role in the school (Teacher, Student, Staff, Parent, Volunteer).

### Training Attendance
• **Attended training sessions** (Number): 
  - If you have attended any training sessions related to the anti-bullying program, enter the number of sessions you have attended.

### Perceived Impact on Incidents
• **Perceived impact on incidents** (Select One): 
  - Rate the change in bullying incident frequency since the program started:
    • Increased
    • No change
    • Decreased
    • Significantly decreased

### Effectiveness of Student Support Measures
• **Effectiveness of student support measures** (Select One): 
  - Rate the effectiveness of student support measures such as support groups and counseling:
    • Ineffective
    • Somewhat effective
    • Effective
    • Very effective

### Change in School Climate
• **Change in school climate** (Select One): 
  - Rate the overall change in school climate since the program started:
    • More negative
    • No change
    • More positive
    • Much more positive

### Suggestions for Improvement
• **Suggestions for program improvement** (Text): 
  - Provide any practical steps or resources that would help improve the anti-bullying program.

### Additional Comments
• **Additional comments** (Text): 
  - Share any other observations or facts that might be helpful to the program administrators.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and based on your actual experience with the program.
- If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
- If you have any other concerns or suggestions, please provide them in the "Additional comments" field.
