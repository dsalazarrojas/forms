# Multilingual Family Needs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Multilingual Family Needs Survey is a tool designed to gather information about families' language needs. This data will help us identify and address the needs of multilingual families in various socio-economic contexts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and truthfully to provide the most accurate information.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please select "Other" and provide a brief explanation in the "Other Education" field.
3. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
4. Please submit one form per family.
