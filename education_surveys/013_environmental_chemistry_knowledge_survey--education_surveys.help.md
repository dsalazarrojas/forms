# Environmental Chemistry Knowledge Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for educators who want to help us understand their knowledge and experiences in teaching environmental chemistry. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all that apply for each question.
2. Type your answers to the questions that ask for text responses.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields.
