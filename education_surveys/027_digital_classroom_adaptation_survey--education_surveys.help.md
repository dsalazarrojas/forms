# Digital Classroom Adaptation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from educators on their experiences and perceptions of digital classroom adaptation in their institutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form once for your school, and only if you haven't already done so in the past.
2. Enter your school's name in the "School Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Select the type of school your institution is (e.g., "Public", "Private", "Charter") in the "School Type" field.
5. Choose which department(s) you belong to in the "Department" field (optional).
6. If applicable, enter your name in the "Teacher Name" field.
7. For "Adaptation Level", select the level of digital classroom adaptation your school has achieved.
8. Select which digital tools your school uses (e.g., Google Meet, Zoom, Skype, Microsoft Teams) in the "Technology Used" field (optional).
9. Provide any additional comments on the digital adaptation in your school in the "Comment" field (optional).
10. If your school uses other technologies not listed, enter them in the "Other Technology" field (optional).
11. Finally, click "Submit" to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name**: Enter your school's name. This will help us identify which school is submitting the survey.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address. This will allow us to contact you with any follow-up questions or to share survey results.
* **Department**: Select the department(s) you belong to, such as Administration, Science, or Technology. This will help us understand how different departments are using digital technologies.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter your name if you are a teacher filling out this form. This is optional.
* **School Type**: Select the type of school you work at, such as Public, Private, or Charter. This will help us understand how different types of schools are using digital technologies.
* **Adaptation Level**: Select the level of digital classroom adaptation your school has achieved, such as Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced. This will help us understand where schools are in their digital adaptation journey.
* **Technology Used**: Select which digital tools your school is using, such as Google Meet, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams. This will help us understand which tools are being used in schools.
* **Comment**: Provide any additional comments or feedback on the digital adaptation in your school. This is optional.
* **Other Technology**: If your school is using other digital technologies not listed, enter them here. This is optional.
* **Submit**: Click "Submit" to submit your survey. This will send your survey to us, and we'll use it to improve digital classroom adaptation support for schools.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
* You can only submit this form once per school. If you've already submitted it, do not fill it out again.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please provide them in the "Comment" field.
