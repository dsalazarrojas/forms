# Learning Motivation During Hybrid Classes Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand your experience and motivation during hybrid classes, which combines online and offline learning environments. Your feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and tailor our teaching methods to better suit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
3. Fill in all the required fields (marked as *Required*).
4. Use the provided options as a guide to answer the questions.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
