# Student Voice Representation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to collect students' thoughts, opinions, and experiences regarding representation at their school. It is a crucial instrument for educators and administrators to understand students' perspectives and develop a more inclusive and representative school environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Student's First Name**, **Middle Initial** (if applicable), and **Student's Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your **Email** address for communication purposes.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** for any follow-up questions or discussions.
4. Select the **Grade Level** you are currently in.
5. Select the **Subject** you are taking.
6. Choose the **Representation Concerns** you have experienced or observed at your school, such as **Diversity**, **Bias**, or **Lack of Voice**.
7. Choose the **Participation Concerns** you have experienced or observed at your school, such as **Inclusive**, **Fair**, or **Safe**.
8. Select the **Participation Strategies** that you think can be used to promote more representation, such as **Encourage**, **Empower**, or **Engage**.
9. Select the **Representation Strategies** you think can be used to address concerns, such as **Increase**, **Decrease**, or **Maintain**.
10. In the **Any Additional Feedback** field, provide any extra comments or suggestions on how to improve representation at your school.
11. Enter the **Date**, **Time**, and **School Year** of this survey.
12. Enter your **Student ID**, **School ID**, **Teacher ID**, and **Department ID** (if applicable).
13. Finally, choose the **Student Group** you belong to, the **Student Language**, and the **School Type** you attend.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student's First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Middle Initial**: Enter your middle name initial (if applicable).
* **Student's Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Grade Level**: Select the level you are currently in (Grade 1-10).
* **Subject**: Enter the subject you are taking.
* **School Name**: Enter your school name.
* **Representation Concerns**: Select one or multiple concerns you have experienced or observed at your school, such as **Diversity**, **Bias**, or **Lack of Voice**.
* **Participation Concerns**: Select one or multiple concerns you have experienced or observed at your school, such as **Inclusive**, **Fair**, or **Safe**.
* **Participation Strategies**: Select one or multiple strategies you think can be used to promote more representation, such as **Encourage**, **Empower**, or **Engage**.
* **Representation Strategies**: Select one or multiple strategies you think can be used to address concerns, such as **Increase**, **Decrease**, or **Maintain**.
* **Any Additional Feedback**: Provide any extra comments or suggestions on how to improve representation at your school.
* **Date**: Enter the date you completed this survey.
* **Time**: Enter the time you completed this survey.
* **School Year**: Enter the school year you are currently in.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number (if applicable).
* **School ID**: Enter your school ID number (if applicable).
* **Teacher ID**: Enter your teacher's ID number (if applicable).
* **Department ID**: Enter your department ID number (if applicable).
* **Student Group**: Select the group you belong to, such as **Diverse**, **Monolingual**, or **Monocultural**.
* **Student Language**: Select the language you speak, such as **English**, **Spanish**, or **Chinese**.
* **School Type**: Select the type of school you attend, such as **Public**, **Private**, or **Community**.
