# Student Quiz Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Quiz Feedback Survey is a form used to gather feedback from students after a quiz has been administered. This feedback helps educators to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the learning experience for students. By providing honest and constructive feedback, students can help the instructors to refine their teaching methods and create a more engaging and effective learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by reading through each question carefully and answering as accurately as possible.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience with the quiz.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Feedback" field to provide more details.
4. Finally, click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Quiz Feedback Survey** (`introduction`, text, optional): This page welcomes you to the survey and explains its purpose. Please take a moment to read through it carefully.
* **Technical Issues** (`technical_issues`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate if you experienced any technical issues during the quiz. This could include problems with loading times, question display, or other technical difficulties.
* **Learning Objectives** (`learning_objectives`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the options that best describe your experience with the learning objectives of the quiz. This will help us understand how clear and achievable the objectives were for you.
* **Question Difficulty** (`question_difficulty`, number, optional): If you have an opinion on the difficulty level of the questions, please enter a number that reflects your experience. A higher number indicates a more difficult question.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate how you managed your time during the quiz. This will help us understand how well you were able to balance your time.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback about the quiz, please enter them here.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the options that best describe areas for improvement for the quiz. This could include clearer instructions, more feedback, or fewer questions.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to provide more details, please enter your contact information here.
* **Date of Birth** (`date`, date, optional): If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your date of birth.
