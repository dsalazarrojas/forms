# Music Ear Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Ear Training Survey is a form designed to gather information about your experience with music ear training, a practice used by musicians to improve their ability to recognize and reproduce music.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all the skills you have been using in your ear training practices from the "ear_training_skills" options.
2. Choose the methods you have been using for ear training from the "methods_used" options.
3. If you have no music theory background, select "no music theory".
4. If you have an aural skills background, choose the relevant option from "aural_skills_background" options.
5. If you have ideas for music-related apps, list them in the "app_ideas" options.
6. Answer the "challenges_faced" question with a text description.
7. If you have no ideas for music-related apps, select "no_app_ideas" and provide a text description.
