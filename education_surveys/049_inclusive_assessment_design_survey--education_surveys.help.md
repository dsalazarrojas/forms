# Inclusive Assessment Design Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inclusive Assessment Design Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from educators on the assessment design of inclusive practices in educational settings. It aims to collect information on the use and effectiveness of different strategies and methods used in inclusive assessments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Select the page with the relevant questions for you.
*   Answer the questions on each page according to your experience and understanding.
*   You can select multiple options for the "Educator Roles" and "How often do you use this strategy?" questions.
*   Enter your answer in the text field for the "Strategies" question.
*   Choose an option for the "Ratings" and "Feedback" questions.
*   You can provide feedback on the "Feedback" field.
*   Choose an option for the "How often do you use this strategy?" question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Educator Roles (Page 1: educator_roles)
*   **Educator Roles**: This question asks about the roles you have played as an educator, such as teacher, instructor, or researcher. Please select all that apply.

### Experience (Page 1: experience)
*   **Experience**: This question asks about your experience with designing and implementing assessments. Please select the option that best reflects your experience.

### Strategies (Page 1: strategies)
*   **Strategies**: This question asks about the strategies and methods you use in your assessments. Please provide a brief description of your approach in this field.

### Ratings (Page 1: ratings)
*   **Ratings**: This question asks you to rate the importance of different strategies and methods used in assessments on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is "Not Important" and 5 is "Very Important".

### Feedback (Page 1: feedback)
*   **Feedback**: This question allows you to provide any additional comments or suggestions about the assessment design process.

### How often do you use this strategy? (Page 2: feedback_2)
*   **How often do you use this strategy?**: This question asks about the frequency of using the strategies and methods in your assessments. Please select the option that best reflects your experience.

### How important is this strategy? (Page 2: ratings_2)
*   **How important is this strategy?**: This question asks you to rate the importance of different strategies and methods used in assessments on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is "Not Important" and 5 is "Very Important".

### How would you rate this strategy? (Page 2: ratings_3)
*   **How would you rate this strategy?**: This question asks you to rate the effectiveness of different strategies and methods used in assessments on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is "Not Effective" and 5 is "Very Effective".

Tips:

*   Please answer each question according to your experience and understanding.
*   You can select multiple options for some questions, but make sure to answer each question to the best of your ability.
*   If you have any suggestions or comments about the assessment design process, please use the "Feedback" field.
*   You can ask for help or clarification if you need assistance with any question.
*   Please answer honestly and accurately.
*   Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
