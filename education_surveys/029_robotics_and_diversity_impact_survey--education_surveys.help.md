# robotics_and_diversity_impact_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "robotics_and_diversity_impact_survey" form is designed to collect information on the impact of robotics on diversity. This form aims to gather insights and data from respondents on how robotics affects different demographics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the first page, "survey_start".
2. Proceed to the "demographics" page, where you will be asked to provide information on demographics.
3. Each demographic field on this page will ask for a specific type of demographic information.
4. Please fill out each demographic field accurately and honestly.
5. Finally, confirm your answers on the "survey_end" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **survey_start** (Text, Optional): This is the beginning of the form, where you will be introduced to the survey.
- **demographics** (Text, Optional): This field is part of a demographic section, where you will be asked to provide more information on demographics.
- **demographics_2** (Text, Optional): Similar to the previous field, this is another demographic section where you will be asked to provide additional information.
- **demographics_3** (Text, Optional): This field is also part of the demographic section, where you will be asked to provide more details on demographics.
- **demographics_4** (Text, Optional): This field is another demographic section where you will be asked to provide more information.
- **demographics_5** (Text, Optional): Another demographic field where you will be asked to provide specific information on demographics.
- **demographics_6** (Text, Optional): This is another demographic field where you will be asked to provide details on demographics.
- **demographics_7** (Text, Optional): Similar to the previous field, this is another demographic section where you will be asked to provide information on demographics.
- **demographics_8** (Text, Optional): This field is another part of the demographic section, where you will be asked to provide more details on demographics.
- **demographics_9** (Text, Optional): This field is another demographic section where you will be asked to provide information on demographics.
- **demographics_10** (Text, Optional): This is another demographic field where you will be asked to provide specific information on demographics.
- **survey_end** (Text, Optional): This is the end of the survey, where you will be asked to confirm your answers.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the demographic fields.
- Ensure that you are providing the correct information for each demographic field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
