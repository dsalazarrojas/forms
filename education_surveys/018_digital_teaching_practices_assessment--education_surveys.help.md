# Digital Teaching Practices Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on digital teaching practices in educational settings, including training programs, evaluation processes, and feedback mechanisms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. For each question, select the most appropriate answer based on your current practices. If the answer is not provided in the options, select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
2. Be as accurate as possible when answering each question.
3. Ensure you have the necessary permissions or approvals before answering questions about specific students or teachers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School District Survey** (`school_district_survey`, text, optional): This question is asking for a brief description of your school district's current state of digital teaching practices.
- **Teacher Survey** (`teacher_survey`, text, optional): This question is asking for a brief description of your school district's current state of teacher feedback and evaluation practices.
- **Training Programs** (`training_programs`, select_one, optional): This question asks about the type of training programs your school district offers to teachers. Options include:
  - Technology Integration
  - Professional Development
  - Curriculum Development
  - Data Literacy
  - Other
- **Challenges** (`challenges`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks about the challenges you face when implementing digital teaching practices. Options include:
  - Technology Integration
  - Professional Development
  - Curriculum Development
  - Data Literacy
  - Other
- **Professional Development Guidance** (`professional_development_guidance`, text, optional): This question asks for any guidance you have on professional development opportunities.
- **Training Programs** (`training_programs`, select_one, optional): Similar to the previous question, this question asks about training programs, but this time about specific teachers.
  - Technology Integration
  - Professional Development
  - Curriculum Development
  - Data Literacy
  - Other
- **Evaluation Process** (`evaluation_process`, text, optional): This question is asking for the process you use to evaluate teachers on their digital teaching practices.
- **Form Completion** (`form_completion`, select_one, optional): This question asks about how the form is completed. Options include:
  - Automated
  - Manual
  - Hybrid
  - No Form Completion
- **Data Analytics** (`data_analytics`, select_one, optional): This question asks about whether you use data analytics when evaluating teachers. Options include:
  - True
  - False
- **Survey Frequency** (`survey_frequency`, number, optional): This question asks about how often you conduct surveys. Enter a numerical value.
- **Survey Length** (`survey_length`, number, optional): This question asks about the length of each survey. Enter a numerical value.
- **Survey Method** (`survey_method`, select_one, optional): This question asks about how you conduct surveys. Options include:
  - Online
  - In-Person
  - Phone
  - Other
- **Survey Response Rate** (`survey_response_rate`, text, optional): This question asks for the response rate of your surveys.
- **Survey Participants** (`survey_participants`, number, optional): This question asks about how many participants you have in your surveys. Enter a numerical value.
- **Training Participants** (`training_participants`, number, optional): This question asks about how many teachers participate in training programs. Enter a numerical value.
- **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, number, optional): This question asks about the number of hours teachers receive for training. Enter a numerical value.
- **Training Cost** (`training_cost`, number, optional): This question asks about the cost of training programs. Enter a numerical value.
- **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, select_one, optional): This question asks about how often training is provided. Options include:
  - Monthly
  - Quarterly
  - Bi-Annual
  - Annual
- **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, select_one, optional): This question asks about how often feedback is given to teachers. Options include:
  - Monthly
  - Quarterly
  - Bi-Annual
  - Annual
- **Teacher Feedback Frequency** (`teacher_feedback_frequency`, select_one, optional): This question is similar to "Feedback Frequency," but specific to teacher feedback. Options include:
  - Monthly
  - Quarterly
  - Bi-Annual
  - Annual
- **Training Evaluation Frequency** (`training_evaluation_frequency`, select_one, optional): This question asks about how often training is evaluated. Options include:
  - Monthly
  - Quarterly
  - Bi-Annual
  - Annual
- **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, select_one, optional): This question is similar to "Training Evaluation Frequency," but for general evaluations. Options include:
  - Monthly
  - Quarterly
  - Bi-Annual
  - Annual
- **Teacher Evaluation Frequency** (`teacher_evaluation_frequency`, select_one, optional): This question is similar to "Evaluation Frequency," but specific to teacher evaluations. Options include:
  - Monthly
  - Quarterly
  - Bi-Annual
  - Annual
- **Teacher Survey Completion** (`teacher_survey_completion`, select_one, optional): This question asks about whether teachers complete surveys. Options include:
  - Yes
  - No

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact your administrator or educational institution.
* This form is designed to provide data for educational research and development; your participation is greatly appreciated.
