# Faculty Satisfaction Institutional Support Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from faculty members about their satisfaction with the institutional support provided by the institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction, which provides some background information about the form's purpose.
2. Select from the list of questions that best describe your experiences and feelings about the institutional support.
3. Answer the scale questions by providing a numerical value between 1 and 5, with 1 being "Not Satisfied" and 5 being "Satisfied".
4. Provide your numerical response for the scale questions in the scale response field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, optional): This is a brief overview of the form's purpose. It's a good idea to read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Questions** (`questions`, `select_multiple`, optional): This section asks about your experiences and feelings about the institutional support. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Scale Questions** (`scale_questions`, `number`, optional): These questions ask you to rate your satisfaction with the institutional support on a scale of 1 to 5. Answer each question separately, and your responses will be used to calculate your overall satisfaction score.
* **Scale Response** (`scale_response`, `number`, optional): This field is for you to provide your overall satisfaction score for the institutional support. Please answer with a numerical value between 1 and 5.

## Tips
* Answer the questions honestly and accurately, as your feedback will help the institution make informed decisions.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Questions" field to provide more details.
* Please answer all the questions and fields to ensure your feedback is complete and useful.
