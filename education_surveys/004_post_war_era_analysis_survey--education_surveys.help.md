# Post War Era Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about individuals who have completed their education during the post-war era (approximately 1945-1980). The data collected will be used for research purposes to analyze trends and patterns among individuals from this era.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully read through the form to understand each field and its purpose.
2. Enter your information accurately and completely.
3. Check your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **survey_id** (Number, optional): A unique identifier assigned to each participant. Please leave this field blank or use a random number.
* **participant_name** (Text, optional): Your name as it should appear on any official documents.
* **email** (Email, optional): Your email address. If you don't have an email address, please use a valid phone number or a note about why you don't have an email.
* **phone_number** (Text, optional): Your phone number or a note about why you don't have a phone number.
* **birth_year** (Number, optional): The year you were born.
* **gender** (Select One, optional): Select your gender (Male, Female, Other, Prefer not to say).
* **marital_status** (Select Multiple, optional): Select all that apply (Married, Single, Divorced, Widowed, Never Married).
* **education** (Select One, optional): Select your highest level of education (High School, Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, Ph.D.).
* **income** (Number, optional): Your annual income.
* **family_size** (Select One, optional): Select the size of your family (Less than 3, 3-5, 5-10, More than 10).
* **age** (Number, optional): Your age.
* **country_of_residence** (Text, optional): The country where you currently reside.
* **occupation** (Text, optional): Your occupation or job title.
* **highest_level_of_education** (Select One, optional): Select your highest level of education (High School, Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, Ph.D.).
* **years_of_education** (Number, optional): The number of years you have been educated.
* **post_high_school_education** (Select Multiple, optional): Select all that apply (Some College/Associate's degree, Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, Ph.D.).
* **years_lived_in_current_country** (Number, optional): The number of years you have lived in your current country.
* **years_spent_studying** (Number, optional): The number of years you have spent studying.
* **years_spent_working** (Number, optional): The number of years you have worked.
* **employment_status** (Select One, optional): Select your employment status (Full-time, Part-time, Unemployed, Retired, Other).
* **employment_status_other** (Text, optional): If other, describe your employment status.
* **years_in_current_job** (Number, optional): The number of years you have been in your current job.
* **years_in_current_education** (Number, optional): The number of years you have been in your current education.
* **employment_level** (Select One, optional): Select your employment level (Junior, Non-Executive, Executive).
* **note** (Text, optional): Any additional information or comments about yourself.

Please note that all fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help the researchers to get a better understanding of the post-war era trends and patterns.
