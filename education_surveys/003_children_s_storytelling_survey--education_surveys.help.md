# Children's Storytelling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Children's Storytelling Survey is a tool designed to gather information about children's preferences, habits, and experiences with storytelling. This survey is intended to help creators and educators develop more engaging and enjoyable stories for children.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Respond" button to start the survey.
2.  Fill out the form by answering each question carefully and accurately.
3.  You may skip questions that are not applicable to you.
4.  If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to share them in the "Any Other Comments or Suggestions" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Information** (`respondent_information`, `note`, optional): This section is for you to briefly tell us about yourself.
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, optional): This is an optional field where you can provide your name. You may remain anonymous if you prefer.
* **Age Group** (`respondent_age_group`, `select_one`, required): Please select your age group from the options provided.
* **School or Organization** (`school_or_organization`, `text`, optional): If you are responding on behalf of a school or organization, please provide the name here.
* **Reading Habits** (`reading_habits`, `note`, optional): Share with us about your reading preferences and habits.
* **How often do you read or are read to?** (`reading_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Please select how often you read or are read to from the options provided.
* **Favorite Story Genres** (`favorite_genres`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the genres you enjoy from the provided options.
* **What is your favorite book or story?** (`favorite_book`, `text`, optional): Share with us about your favorite book or story.
* **Storytelling Preferences** (`storytelling_preferences`, `note`, optional): Share with us how you prefer to experience stories.
* **Preferred Story Format** (`preferred_format`, `select_one`, required): Please select your preferred story format from the options provided.
* **Preferred Story Length** (`story_length_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred story length from the options.
* **What types of characters do you enjoy?** (`favorite_characters`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the character types you enjoy.
* **Engagement and Participation** (`engagement_feedback`, `note`, optional): Share your thoughts on storytelling activities.
* **Have you attended any storytelling events?** (`storytelling_events_attended`, `select_one`, required): Please select how often you have attended storytelling events.
* **What do you enjoy most about stories?** (`what_enjoys_most`, `text`, optional): Share with us what you enjoy most about stories.
* **What would make storytelling more enjoyable?** (`what_improves`, `text`, optional): Share your suggestions on how to make storytelling more enjoyable.
* **Library and Book Access** (`library_usage`, `note`, optional): Share with us about your access to books and libraries.
* **How often do you visit the library?** (`visits_library`, `select_one`, required): Please select how often you visit the library.
* **Approximately how many books are in your home?** (`books_at_home`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the number of books you have at home.
* **Would you like book recommendations?** (`recommendations`, `select_one`, optional): Share with us if you would like book recommendations.
* **Email for Book Recommendations** (`email_for_recommendations`, `email`, optional): If you prefer to receive email recommendations, provide your email address.
* **Any Other Comments or Suggestions** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, optional): Share with us any other comments or suggestions you may have.
