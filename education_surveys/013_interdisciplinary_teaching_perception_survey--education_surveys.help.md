# Interdisciplinary Teaching Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to provide an overview of the Interdisciplinary Teaching Perception Survey form. The purpose of this form is to collect information from educators on their perception of interdisciplinary teaching in their educational settings. The form is used to gather insights and opinions on the effectiveness, benefits, and challenges of interdisciplinary teaching.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the category that best suits your educational setting.
2. Provide a brief description of your educational setting.
3. Enter your email address for correspondence.
4. Select your department from the provided options.
5. Enter your job title.
6. Specify the number of years you have been in service.
7. Select your teaching level from the provided options.
8. List any departmentalization (if applicable).
9. Provide comments on benefits and challenges of interdisciplinary teaching (if any).
10. Provide comments on the effectiveness of interdisciplinary teaching (if any).
11. Enter any additional comments on the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`): A brief title for the form that should clearly indicate the purpose of the survey.
* **Category** (`category`): Please select the category that best suits your educational setting. This will help us understand the specific context of your response.
* **Description** (`description`): Please provide a brief description of your educational setting.
* **Comments** (`form_comments`): If you have any additional comments or observations, please feel free to share them.
* **Email** (`email`): Please enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): You may enter your contact number for easy communication.
* **Department** (`department`): Please select the department you belong to from the provided options.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Please enter your job title.
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`): Please specify the number of years you have been in service.
* **Teaching Level** (`teaching_level`): Select your teaching level from the provided options.
* **Departmentalization** (`departmentalization`): If applicable, list any departmentalization.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`): Provide comments on benefits of interdisciplinary teaching (if any).
* **Challenges** (`challenges`): Provide comments on challenges of interdisciplinary teaching (if any).
* **Effectiveness** (`effectiveness`): Provide comments on the effectiveness of interdisciplinary teaching (if any).
* **Additional Comments** (`form_comments`): You may enter any additional comments or observations.
* **Teaching Level Comments** (`teaching_level_comments`): You may enter comments on teaching level.
* **Department Comments** (`department_comments`): You may enter comments on departmentalization.
* **Email Comments** (`email_comments`): You may enter comments on email.
* **Contact Number Comments** (`contact_number_comments`): You may enter comments on contact number.
* **Years of Service Comments** (`years_of_service_comments`): You may enter comments on years of service.
* **Teaching Level 2** (`teaching_level_2`): Please enter comments on teaching level.
