# Virtual Learning Feedback Taskforce Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering individuals interested in participating in the Virtual Learning Feedback Taskforce. It will help us understand your background and interests for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete all the required fields.
2. Fill in the required information accurately.
3. Review and submit your form before sending it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Fill in your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Fill in your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required: false): Choose the role you are representing (Admin, User, Teacher).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Fill in your phone number for contact purposes. Please use the format of the country code followed by your phone number (e.g., +1 123-456-7890).
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the category that best describes your interest and participation in the Virtual Learning Feedback Taskforce (Virtual Learning, Virtual Event).

## Tips
* Ensure your email and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
* Be clear about your role and category for easy reference and communication.
* If you have any additional comments, fill in the comments section for further context.
* Please review your form before submission for any errors or inaccuracies.
