# Student Skills And Goals Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about students' skills, learning preferences, and goals to help students and educators create a supportive and effective learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form on your own or with the help of a teacher or administrator.
2. Please answer all required fields, but feel free to skip optional questions.
3. Choose your preferred format for receiving information and resources (Video, Podcast, Text format, or Other).
4. Share any additional comments or suggestions in the Skills Survey Note and Goals Note fields.
5. Submit the form to your instructor or administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Survey** (`student_survey`, text, required/optional): This field is a brief introduction to your thoughts about your own learning style and preferences. Please feel free to express yourself as much or as little as you feel comfortable sharing.
* **Learning Preferences** (`learning_preferences`, select_one, optional): Select the type of learning that you find most appealing: Self-directed learning, Collaborative learning, or Instructor-led learning.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, optional): Write down your goals for the upcoming school year, and any areas you're looking to improve or address.
* **Goal 1** (`skill_1`, text, optional): Describe your primary goal for the current school year.
* **Goal 2** (`skill_2`, text, optional): Describe your secondary goal for the current school year.
* **School Year Goals** (`school_year_goals`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the goals you have for the school year. Options include Academic achievement, Personal growth, Social skills, Career development, and Other.
* **Skills Survey** (`skills_survey`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the skills that you are proficient in or interested in developing. Options include Problem-solving, Communication, Teamwork, Time management, Leadership, and Other.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, select_one, optional): Select the school year you are currently in or plan to be in. Options include Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, Post-grad, and Other.
* **Preferred format** (`preferred_format`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to receive information and resources (Video, Podcast, Text format, or Other).
* **Skills and interests** (`skills_and_interests`, text, optional): List any skills or interests you have that are not on the Skills Survey list.
* **Skills Survey Note** (`skills_survey_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your skills and interests.
* **Goals Note** (`goals_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your goals.
* **Additional Tips**: Consider adding any additional comments or questions in the Skills Survey Note and Goals Note fields.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* You can choose not to answer any question you are not comfortable with.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to ask your instructor or administrator.
* This form is intended to be a helpful tool for creating a supportive learning environment. Your participation is greatly appreciated!
