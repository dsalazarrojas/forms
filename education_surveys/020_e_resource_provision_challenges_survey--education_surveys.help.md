# E Resource Provision Challenges Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand the challenges you face when provisioning eResources in your library. Your feedback will help us improve our services and better support your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions as accurately and honestly as possible.
2. Select the most appropriate option for each question, even if it's not a direct yes or no answer.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you can.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Do you have any additional comments?" field.
