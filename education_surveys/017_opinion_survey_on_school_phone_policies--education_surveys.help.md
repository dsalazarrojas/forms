# Opinion Survey On School Phone Policies - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect opinions and suggestions from the school community on the current phone policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the relevant role from the "Role" dropdown menu that best describes your position within the school.
2. Provide your opinion in the "What is your opinion?" text field.
3. If you have any suggestions, please type them in the "Do you have any suggestions?" text field.
4. Select your agreement level with the current phone policy from the "Do you agree with the current school phone policy?" dropdown menu.
5. Optionally, provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" text field.
6. Provide your contact email address in the "Email" text field if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or feedback.
7. If you have any other information you'd like to share, please type it in the "Other Information" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Select the role that best describes your position within the school.
* **What is your opinion?** (`opinion`, `text`, required): Share your honest opinion on the current school phone policies.
* **Do you have any suggestions?** (`suggestion`, `text`, optional): If you have any ideas on how to improve the current phone policies, please type them here.
* **Do you agree with the current school phone policy?** (`agreement`, `select_one`, optional): Select the level of your agreement with the current policy.
* **Comments** (`comment`, `text`, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback you have on the current policies.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or feedback, please provide your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Optionally, provide your phone number for further contact.
* **Other Information** (`other_info`, `text`, optional): Share any other relevant information you'd like to provide about the current policies.
