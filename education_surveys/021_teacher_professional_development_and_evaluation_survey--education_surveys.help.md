# Teacher Professional Development And Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback from teachers on the effectiveness of a training session they attended. The data collected will help us evaluate the quality of our professional development programs and make necessary improvements for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all the questions truthfully and concisely.
2. Fill in your name, the training session details, and rating questions.
3. Provide feedback on the topics covered, session effectiveness, and areas for improvement.
4. Add any additional comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, text, required): Enter the name of the training session you attended.
* **Session Start Date** (`session_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date the training session started.
* **Session End Date** (`session_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date the training session ended.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the trainer for this session.
* **Rating 1-2** (`rating_1`, select_one, required): Rate the session as 1-2, indicating low to moderate improvement.
* **Rating 3-4** (`rating_2`, select_one, required): Rate the session as 3-4, indicating moderate to high improvement.
* **Rating 5-6** (`rating_3`, select_one, required): Rate the session as 5-6, indicating high to very high improvement.
* **Rating 7-8** (`rating_4`, select_one, required): Rate the session as 7-8, indicating very high to extremely high improvement.
* **Rating 9-10** (`rating_5`, select_one, required): Rate the session as 9-10, indicating extremely high improvement.
* **What did you learn during the training session?** (`question_1`, text, required): Describe the key learnings from the session.
* **How can we improve the training session?** (`question_2`, text, required): Suggest areas where you feel we can improve the session.
* **What would you like to learn more about in future training sessions?** (`question_3`, text, required): Suggest topics you would like to have covered in future sessions.
* **Do you have any further comments?** (`question_4`, text, required): Add any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address to help us reach out to you for more information or clarification.
