# Digital Learning Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your thoughts, habits, and experiences related to digital learning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. You can select one or multiple options for each question, depending on the type of question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1, text, optional): This is the first page of the form, providing a brief introduction to the survey.
* **What are your thoughts on the importance of being prepared for digital learning?** (2, text, optional): Describe how you think being prepared for digital learning is important.
* **What are your thoughts on using technology to enhance learning?** (3, text, optional): Share your ideas on how technology can help with learning.
* **How often do you use a computer for learning?** (4, number, optional): Indicate how many hours you spend on a computer for learning each week.
* **How often do you use a computer for work?** (5, number, optional): Answer how many hours you spend on a computer for work each week.
* **How often do you use a computer for entertainment?** (6, number, optional): Tell us how many hours you spend on a computer for entertainment each week.
* **What type of devices do you use for learning?** (7, select_one, optional): Choose the devices you typically use for learning.
* **How many hours do you spend on a computer for learning each week?** (8, number, optional): Answer how many hours you spend on a computer for learning each week.
* **What do you think is the most important factor in being prepared for digital learning?** (9, text, optional): Reflect on what you think is crucial for being prepared for digital learning.
* **How important is it to have a reliable internet connection for learning?** (10, text, optional): Share your thoughts on the importance of having a reliable internet connection.
* **How often do you access the internet for learning?** (11, text, optional): Indicate how often you access the internet for learning.
* **What are some common challenges you face when using digital technologies for learning?** (12, text, optional): Describe any obstacles you experience when using digital tools.
* **What are some common benefits you have experienced from using digital technologies for learning?** (13, text, optional): Share the advantages you have experienced from using digital tools.
* **What are some common limitations you face when using digital technologies for learning?** (14, text, optional): Explain any difficulties you encounter when using digital tools.
* **Do you have a plan to improve your digital literacy?** (15, select_multiple, optional): Choose how confident you are that you can improve your digital literacy.
* **How confident are you that you can adapt to new technologies in the near future?** (16, select_one, optional): Select how confident you are in adapting to new technologies.
* **How many people in your household use digital devices?** (17, number, optional): Answer how many people in your household use digital devices.
* **What is your highest educational level?** (18, select_multiple, optional): Choose your highest level of education.
* **What type of devices do you own?** (19, select_multiple, optional): List the devices you own.
* **What is your preferred language when using a computer?** (20, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred language for computer use.
* **Do you think digital learning should be more prevalent in education?** (21, select_one, optional): Select your stance on digital learning in education.
* **What is your preferred way of learning new information?** (22, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred method of learning.
* **Have you used learning apps to help with your learning?** (23, select_multiple, optional): Answer if you have used learning apps.
* **Do you believe that using learning apps improves your learning experience?** (24, select_multiple, optional): Share your opinion on learning apps.
