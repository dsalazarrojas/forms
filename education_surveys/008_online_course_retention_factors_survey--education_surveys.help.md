# Online Course Retention Factors Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Course Retention Factors Survey is designed to gather information from students or faculty about factors that contribute to or affect their online course experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field below for a clear understanding of what's expected.
2. Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
3. Submit your responses when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Demographics**: Please select your category as either Student, Faculty, or Both (if applicable).
* **Enrollment Status**: Indicate your enrollment status as Enrolled or Not Enrolled.
* **Retention Factors**: Select all factors that apply regarding your online course retention experiences. You can choose up to 10 options.
* **Comments**: If you'd like to add any additional comments or factors not listed, please do so here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number.
* **Course Evaluation**: Record the date you completed the course evaluation.
* **Course Satisfaction**: Report the time it took to complete this form.
* **Survey Completed**: Please enter your completion statement for tracking purposes.
