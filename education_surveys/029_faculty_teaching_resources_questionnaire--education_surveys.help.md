# Faculty Teaching Resources Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from faculty members on their teaching resources needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions according to your actual teaching practices and preferences.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as possible.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the space provided at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Faculty Teaching Resources Questionnaire** (`faculty_teaching_resources_questionnaire`, text, optional): This is the title of the form.
* **Teaching Area** (`teaching_area`, text, optional): Please specify the area of teaching you are involved in (e.g., mathematics, language, history, etc.).
* **Teaching Method** (`teaching_method`, text, optional): Describe the teaching method you use (e.g., lecture, discussion, project-based, etc.).
* **Teaching Approach** (`teaching_approach`, text, optional): Explain the approach you take when teaching (e.g., student-centered, teacher-centered, problem-based, etc.).

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the space provided at the end of the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
