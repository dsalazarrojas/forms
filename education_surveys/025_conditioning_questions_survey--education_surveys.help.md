# Conditioning Questions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information about your background and experience with conditioning concepts. It will be used to assess your qualifications and suitability for a study or research project.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter your name in the Respondent name field if you wish to provide it. This is an optional field.
* Select your role or relationship to the subject from the options provided in the Role or relationship to subject field.
* Rate your understanding of conditioning concepts from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor in the Understanding of conditioning concepts field.
* If you have practical experience with conditioning, please enter the number of hours or sessions in the Practical experience with conditioning field.
* Choose all the methods you prefer for learning from the options in the Preferred learning methods field. This field allows multiple selections.
* Indicate your interest level in formal assessment or certification from the options in the Interest in assessment or certification field.
* If you need any resources to complete the project, please list them in the Resources needed field.
* In the Additional comments field, you can provide any other feedback or information you think is relevant to the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, Optional): This field is for you to enter your name if you wish to provide it.
* **Role or relationship to subject** (`role`, select_one, Required): Select your role or relationship to the subject, such as Student, Teacher, Researcher, or Parent.
* **Understanding of conditioning concepts** (`topic_understanding`, select_one, Required): Rate your understanding of conditioning concepts from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Practical experience with conditioning** (`practical_experience`, number, Optional): If you have practical experience with conditioning, enter the number of hours or sessions you have completed.
* **Preferred learning methods** (`preferred_learning_methods`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose all the methods you prefer for learning, such as Lectures, Hands on labs, Group discussion, or Online modules.
* **Interest in assessment or certification** (`assessment_interest`, select_one, Optional): Indicate your interest level in formal assessment or certification from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Resources needed** (`resource_needs`, text, Optional): If you need any resources to complete the project, list them here.
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, text, Optional): Provide any other feedback or information you think is relevant to the project.
