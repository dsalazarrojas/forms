# Personal Story And Language Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect personal stories and language experiences from individuals. It is meant to be used as a data collection tool for research or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Enter your personal story and language experiences in the designated fields.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Story** (`personal_story`, text, optional): Tell us about your personal story, including any relevant details that you feel are important.
* **Language Use** (`language_use`, text, optional): Describe how you use language in your daily life, including any languages you speak, read, write, or understand.
* **Multilingual Background** (`multilingual_background`, text, optional): Share your background in speaking multiple languages, including any languages you have learned or used throughout your life.
* **Language Experiences** (`language_experiences`, text, optional): Share any experiences you have had related to language, including any challenges or successes you have faced.
* **Language Skills** (`language_skills`, text, optional): Describe your language skills, including any proficiency levels you have achieved in specific languages.
* **Narratives** (`narratives`, text, optional): Share any personal narratives or stories that you feel are relevant to your language experiences.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, optional): Share your educational background, including any language-related courses or degrees you have completed.
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic_info`, text, optional): Provide demographic information about yourself, such as age, gender, or location.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any contact information you'd like to share with us, such as email or phone number.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click submit to send your form data to us.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Use the provided fields to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
