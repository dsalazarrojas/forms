# Algae Observation And Preparation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Algae Observation And Preparation Survey is designed to collect information on algae observations and preparations for scientific research or monitoring purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in sections, starting with your observer information and moving on to the observation details.
2. For each field, carefully read and understand the label and options before selecting an answer.
3. Take your time and ensure accuracy in your entries, as this information will be used for scientific research or monitoring.
4. If you are unsure about any field or option, refer to this guide for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Observer Name** (1): Enter your full name as the person conducting the observation.
* **Observer Email** (2): Enter your contact email for further communication or follow-up questions.
* **Institution** (3): Enter the name of the institution or school conducting the observation.
* **Observation Date** (4): Enter the date of the observation in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Observation Time** (5): Enter the time of the observation in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Location Name** (6): Enter the name of the observation site.
* **GPS Coordinates** (7): Enter the latitude and longitude of the location (if available).
* **Habitat Type** (8): Select the type of environment where the algae was observed (e.g., Freshwater pond, Freshwater lake, etc.).
* **Water Temperature** (9): Enter the temperature of the water in Celsius (if not available, leave blank).
* **pH Level** (10): Enter the pH level measurement (if not available, leave blank).
* **Water Clarity** (11): Select the visual clarity of the water (e.g., Clear, Slightly turbid, etc.).
* **Algae Color Observed** (12): Select all the colors that apply to the algae observed (e.g., Green, Blue-green, etc.).
* **Growth Form** (13): Select all the growth forms of the algae observed (e.g., Single cells, Colonies, etc.).
* **Algae Abundance** (14): Select the estimated coverage of the algae (e.g., None, Trace, etc.).
* **Sample Collected** (15): Indicate if a sample was collected during the observation.
* **Sample ID** (16): Enter the sample identifier (if a sample was collected).
* **Collection Method** (17): Select the method used to collect the sample (e.g., Plankton net, Water bottle, etc.).
* **Preservation Method** (18): Select the method used to preserve the sample (e.g., Fresh, Lugol solution, etc.).
* **Microscope Examination** (19): Indicate if a microscope was used during the observation.
* **Magnification Used** (20): Select the magnification level used (if applicable).
* **Genera Identified** (21): List the identified genera (if applicable).
* **Cell Characteristics** (22): Describe any notable cell features observed.
* **Flagella Present** (23): Indicate if flagella were observed.
* **Chloroplast Arrangement** (24): Describe the arrangement of chloroplasts (if applicable).
* **Associated Organisms** (25): Select other organisms observed with the algae (e.g., Bacteria, Protozoa, etc.).
* **Environmental Notes** (26): Record any relevant environmental notes (e.g., Weather, conditions, etc.).
* **Preparation Notes** (27): Record any notes on sample preparation (if applicable).
* **Photographs Taken** (28): Enter the number of photographs taken during the observation.
* **Additional Observations** (29): Record any additional notes or observations not covered by other fields.
