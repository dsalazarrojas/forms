# Module Reflection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on modules that you have taken, allowing us to improve and refine them for better learning experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions as honestly as possible, providing specific examples or details when possible.
2. Select one of the options in the "How satisfied" and "How likely" sections based on your genuine response.
3. Use the text fields to provide detailed feedback and suggestions for improvement.
4. Save your progress and come back to it later if you need more time to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What did you learn from this module?** (`module_reflection_survey`, text, optional): This question is to gather your thoughts on what you learned from the module.
* **What skills or outcomes did you gain from this module?** (`module_outcomes`, text, optional): Share with us what skills or outcomes you gained from the module.
* **How would you rate the trainer's performance?** (`trainer_performance`, number, optional): Rate the trainer's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the trainer's support?** (`trainer_support`, number, optional): Rate the trainer's support on a scale of 1-5.
* **Additional feedback about the module or trainer** (`feedback`, note, optional): Use this field to provide any additional feedback or suggestions.
* **What could be improved about this module?** (`module_improvement`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on what could be improved about the module.
* **How satisfied are you with the module?** (`module_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the module on a scale from Very dissatisfied to Very satisfied.
* **How likely are you to recommend this module to others?** (`module_satisfaction_2`, select_one, optional): Choose how likely you are to recommend the module on a scale from Very unlikely to Very likely.
* **How likely are you to take another module from the same trainer?** (`module_satisfaction_3`, select_one, optional): Choose how likely you are to take another module from the same trainer on a scale from Very unlikely to Very likely.
* **Module Satisfaction 4** (`module_satisfaction_4`, select_one, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the module on a scale from Very unlikely to Very likely.
* **How likely are you to take another module from this trainer?** (`module_satisfaction_5`, select_one, optional): Choose how likely you are to take another module from the same trainer on a scale from Very unlikely to Very likely.
* **Module Satisfaction 6** (`module_satisfaction_6`, select_one, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the module on a scale from Very unlikely to Very likely.
* **Module Satisfaction 7** (`module_satisfaction_7`, select_one, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the module on a scale from Very unlikely to Very likely.

## Tips
- Be specific and honest in your answers.
- Provide detailed feedback for the text fields.
- Choose the correct option that best reflects your genuine response for the select-one fields.
- Do not submit incomplete or duplicate submissions.
