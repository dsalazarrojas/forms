# Cross Cultural Learning Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback and evaluations from students or participants who have undergone a cross-cultural learning experience. This information will be used to improve the quality and effectiveness of these experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Participant ID to identify your unique experience.
2. If applicable, select the date you reflected on your experience.
3. Select your Country of Origin and Country of Current Study Abroad or Work.
4. Choose the type of program you participated in (Study Abroad, Exchange, International Project).
5. Select your primary language.
6. Enter the length of your stay.
7. Provide a reflection on your experience.
8. If necessary, fill in the Evaluation Form and comments section.
9. Answer whether you would recommend this experience.
10. Enter your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
11. If your supervisor is involved in your program, fill in their information (Name, Email, Phone).
12. Select the date of your program start and end.
13. Enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, number, required): Enter your unique participant ID, which is used to identify your experience.
* **Reflection Date** (`reflection_date`, date, optional): If you reflected on your experience, select the date you did so.
* **Country of Origin** (`country_origin`, text, optional): Select the country where you are from.
* **Country of Current Study Abroad or Work** (`country_current`, text, optional): Select the country where you are currently studying or working.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of program you participated in (Study Abroad, Exchange, International Project).
* **Primary Language** (`language`, select_one, optional): Select your primary language (English, Spanish, Mandarin, Other).
* **Length of Stay** (`duration`, number, required): Enter the length of your stay.
* **Reflection** (`reflection`, text, required): Provide a detailed reflection on your experience.
* **Evaluation Form** (`evaluation_form`, text, optional): If necessary, fill in any evaluation forms or comments.
* **Would you recommend this experience?** (`recommendation`, select_one, optional): Select whether you would recommend this experience (True, False, Unsure).
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): If necessary, select the start date of your program.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, optional): If necessary, select the end date of your program.
* **Participant Supervisor** (`participant_supervisor`, text, optional): If necessary, fill in your supervisor's information.
* **Participant Supervisor Email** (`participant_supervisor_email`, email, optional): If necessary, fill in your supervisor's email.
* **Participant Supervisor Phone** (`participant_supervisor_phone`, text, optional): If necessary, fill in your supervisor's phone number.
* **Participant Supervisor Program** (`participant_supervisor_program`, text, optional): If necessary, fill in your supervisor's program.
* **Participant Supervisor Department** (`participant_supervisor_department`, text, optional): If necessary, fill in your supervisor's department.
* **Participant Supervisor Job Title** (`participant_supervisor_job_title`, text, optional): If necessary, fill in your supervisor's job title.
* **Host Institution** (`host_institution`, text, optional): If necessary, fill in the host institution's information.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): If necessary, select the evaluation date.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): If necessary, fill in the program's name.
* **Program Description** (`program_description`, text, optional): If necessary, fill in the program's description.

Note: Fields marked as "optional" can be skipped if not applicable to your experience.
