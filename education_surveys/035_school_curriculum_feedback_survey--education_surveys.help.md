# School Curriculum Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Curriculum Feedback Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from parents, students, and teachers on the effectiveness and relevance of the school's curriculum. Please take a few minutes to review the form and provide your honest opinion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review each page and fill out the required fields.
* Select your category (Parent, Student, or Teacher) from the "Parent Information" dropdown menu.
* Fill out your name and email address.
* For each section, choose an option from the dropdown menu or type your answer in the text box.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments" field.
* Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information** (Select One)
	+ This field asks for your category (Parent, Student, or Teacher). Please select the correct option that best describes your relationship with the school.
* **Name** (Text)
	+ This is where you enter your name as it should be displayed on your feedback.
* **Email** (Email)
	+ This is where you enter your email address. We will use this to contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone** (Text)
	+ This is where you enter your phone number. We will use this to contact you if we need more information.
* **Curriculum Effectiveness** (Select One)
	+ Please select how effective you think the current school curriculum is. Choose from the following options:
		- Very effective
		- Somewhat effective
		- Neither effective nor ineffective
		- Not at all effective
* **Curriculum Relevance** (Select One)
	+ Please select how relevant you think the current school curriculum is to your needs. Choose from the following options:
		- Very relevant
		- Somewhat relevant
		- Neither relevant nor irrelevant
		- Not at all relevant
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (Text)
	+ If you have any suggestions or ideas for improving the school's curriculum, please enter them here.
* **Comments** (Note)
	+ If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please enter them here.
