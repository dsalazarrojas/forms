# Neurodiversity In Learning Environments Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the learning environment of schools, specifically focusing on how they support neurodiversity. It's essential to provide accurate and detailed information to help schools improve their services and support systems for students with neurodiversities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. For the "Category" field, select the primary category that best describes your school's learning environment.
3. For the "Learning Environment" field, select all the environments that apply to your school.
4. Enter your school's name in the "School Name" field.
5. Enter a detailed description of your school's learning environment in the "Description" field.
6. If desired, provide feedback in the "Feedback" field.
7. Enter the name of the person to contact in the "Contact Name" field.
8. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter a valid contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
10. If applicable, enter your school's website in the "School Website" field.
11. If applicable, enter your school's phone number in the "School Phone" field.
12. Select the assigned tool used by your school in the "Assigned Tool" field.
13. Enter the date in the "Date" field (in the format dd/mm/yyyy).
14. Enter the time in the "Time" field (in the format 12:30).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of your school, university, or institution.
* **Category** (2, select_one, required): Select the primary category that best describes your school's learning environment.
* **Description** (3, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your school's learning environment.
* **Learning Environment** (4, select_multiple, required): Select all the learning environments that apply to your school (Classroom, Online, Hybrid).
* **Feedback** (5, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your school's learning environment.
* **Contact Name** (6, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact.
* **Email** (7, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Contact Number** (8, text, required): Enter a valid contact number.
* **School Website** (9, text, optional): Enter your school's website (if applicable).
* **School Phone** (10, text, optional): Enter your school's phone number (if applicable).
* **Assigned Tool** (11, select_one, required): Select the assigned tool used by your school.
* **Date** (12, date, required): Enter the date in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Time** (13, time, required): Enter the time in the format 12:30.
* **Note** (14, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your school's learning environment.
