# Community Nutrition Education Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect feedback on community nutrition education awareness, focusing on understanding the respondents' perceptions and needs regarding healthy food options, their nutrition knowledge level, and interest in nutrition education programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select your age group from the options provided.
* Describe your typical daily meals.
* Rate your level of knowledge about nutrition.
* Assess your access to healthy food options in your area.
* Choose topics you are interested in learning more about through nutrition education programs.
* If you have any dietary restrictions, please mention them.
* Check any barriers to healthy eating that apply to you.
* Provide suggestions for topics or formats you would like to see in nutrition education programs.
* If you would like to be contacted for training opportunities, please provide your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Age group (Age group, select_one, true)**: This field asks for your age range to understand the demographics of the respondents.
* **Typical diet pattern (Typical diet pattern, text, false)**: Describe the types of food you usually eat or drink in a day.
* **Self-rated nutrition knowledge (Self rated nutrition knowledge, select_one, true)**: Rate your level of knowledge about nutrition to help us understand your current understanding.
* **Access to healthy food options (Access to healthy food options, select_one, true)**: Assess how easily you can access healthy food options in your area, such as farmers' markets or health food stores.
* **Interest in nutrition education programs (Interest in nutrition education programs, select_multiple, false)**: Choose topics you are interested in learning more about through nutrition education programs.
* **Dietary restrictions (Dietary restrictions, text, false)**: If you have any food restrictions or allergies, please mention them so we can understand your needs.
* **Barriers to healthy eating (Barriers to healthy eating, select_multiple, false)**: Check all the barriers that you face while trying to eat healthy.
* **Suggestions for nutrition programs (Suggestions for nutrition programs, text, false)**: Share your ideas for topics or formats you would like to see in nutrition education programs.
* **Contact email for training opportunities (Contact email for training opportunities, email, false)**: If you are interested in being contacted for training or educational opportunities, please provide your email address.
