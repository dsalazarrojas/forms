# Educational Content Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students on the educational content of a course. Your input will help course creators improve the quality of the course and provide a better learning experience for future students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and honestly.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Add any comments or suggestions you may have.
4. Provide your email and phone number for contact (optional).
5. Fill out the date and time you completed the course (optional).
6. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, note, required: false): This section is just a note to let you know that you're about to fill out the feedback form. Please read and understand the purpose of this form before proceeding.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the course you took.
* **What did you think of the course content** (`question_1`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the options that best describes your thoughts on the course content.
	+ option 1: It was excellent
	+ option 2: It was average
	+ option 3: It was poor
* **How would you rate the course content** (`question_2`, number, required: false): Rate the course content on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Was the course content relevant** (`question_3`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all the options that apply to your experience with the course content.
	+ option 1: Yes, it was very relevant
	+ option 2: Yes, it was somewhat relevant
	+ option 3: No, it was not relevant at all
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): If you have any comments or suggestions for the course, please add them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, required: false): Enter the date you completed the course.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, required: false): Enter the time you completed the course.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Click this button to send your feedback.
