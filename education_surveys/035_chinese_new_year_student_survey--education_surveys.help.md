# Chinese New Year Student Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students who have participated in lessons or workshops related to the Chinese New Year. It aims to measure the effectiveness of our educational content in promoting cultural understanding and knowledge among students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Student Name** field.
2. Select your **Class or Grade Level** from the provided options.
3. Rate your familiarity with the Chinese New Year in the **Prior Knowledge of Chinese New Year** select field.
4. Choose your favorite activity from the provided options in the **Favorite Activity from Lessons** field.
5. Answer **Yes** or **No** to whether the lessons improved your cultural understanding in the **Did the lessons improve cultural understanding** select field.
6. Provide any **Suggestions for Future Lessons** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name**: Enter your name as a student.
- **Class or Grade Level**: Select the level you are currently in (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd grade, High School, University, etc.).
- **Prior Knowledge of Chinese New Year**: Rate your familiarity with the Chinese New Year by selecting:
	+ **Very familiar**: You have a good understanding of the Chinese New Year.
	+ **Somewhat familiar**: You have some knowledge but need more clarification.
	+ **Slightly familiar**: You have a basic understanding.
	+ **Not familiar**: You have little to no knowledge.
- **Favorite Activity from Lessons**: Choose the activity that you enjoyed most from the provided options.
- **Did the lessons improve cultural understanding**: Answer with:
	+ **Yes**: The lessons increased your cultural understanding.
	+ **No**: The lessons did not increase your cultural understanding.
- **Suggestions for Future Lessons**: Provide any ideas you have for improving the lessons.
