# Alumni Choir Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alumni Choir Participation Survey is a form designed to gather information from alumni about their interest in joining the college choir. This information will help the choir organizers plan events and ensure that all participants have a positive experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your alumni email address in the "Alumni Email Address" field.
3. Select your graduation year in the "Graduation Year" field.
4. Choose your primary vocal range in the "Voice Part" field.
5. Select your availability for rehearsals in the "Rehearsal Availability" field.
6. If you want to be contacted through a specific method, select it in the "Preferred Communication Method" field.
7. If you have any previous choir experience, describe it in the "Previous Choir Experience" field (optional).
8. Choose your T-Shirt or robe size in the "T-Shirt or Robe Size" field.
9. If you have any dietary restrictions, select them in the "Dietary Restrictions for Events" field.
10. Finally, provide any additional comments or information in the "Anything else we should know?" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it should be displayed in the choir's records.
* **Alumni Email Address** (`alumni_email`, `email`, required): Enter your alumni email address so that the choir can contact you about events and rehearsals.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, `number`, required): Select the year you graduated from the college.
* **Voice Part** (`voice_part`, `select_one`, required): Choose your primary vocal range (Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass, or Baritone).
* **Rehearsal Availability** (`rehearsal_availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the times you are typically available for rehearsals (Monday Evenings, Wednesday Evenings, Friday Afternoons, Saturday Mornings, Sunday Afternoons).
* **Preferred Communication Method** (`communication_preference`, `select_one`, optional): If you want to be contacted through a specific method, select it (Email, Text Message, Phone Call, WhatsApp).
* **Previous Choir Experience** (`choir_experience`, `text`, optional): Describe any previous choir experience you have (solo or group).
* **T-Shirt or Robe Size** (`uniform_size`, `select_one`, required): Choose your T-Shirt or robe size (Small, Medium, Large, X-Large, XX-Large).
* **Dietary Restrictions for Events** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any dietary restrictions you have (None, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Nut Allergy).
* **Anything else we should know?** (`final_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information you think would be helpful for the choir organizers to know.
