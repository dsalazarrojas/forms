# Academic Curriculum Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the quality of an academic curriculum, identifying its strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name (if you're not a faculty member, this section is optional).
2. Enter your email address and institution name.
3. Select your role in the institution (student, faculty member, department head, etc.).
4. Choose the academic term being evaluated.
5. Rate the curriculum's quality for each aspect (relevance, learning outcomes, sequencing, workload balance, assessment methods, industry alignment, and practical components).
6. Provide open feedback about the curriculum.
7. If applicable, suggest additional courses or topics to add or remove.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Optional)** (`respondent_name`, `text`, `optional`): This field allows respondents to provide their full name, which is optional for non-faculty members.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address to ensure that the evaluation can be properly communicated.
* **Institution Name** (`institution`, `text`, `required`): This is the name of the institution being evaluated.
* **Department or Program** (`department`, `text`, `required`): Specify the department or program being reviewed.
* **Role** (`respondent_role`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your role in the institution (student, faculty member, department head, curriculum committee member, or external reviewer).
* **Academic Term** (`academic_term`, `text`, `required`): Choose the academic term being evaluated (e.g., Fall 2025, Spring 2026).
* **Relevance of Course Content to Program Objectives** (`course_content_relevance`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the curriculum's relevance to program objectives, from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Clarity of Stated Learning Outcomes** (`learning_outcomes_clarity`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the curriculum's clarity of stated learning outcomes.
* **Logical Sequencing of Courses within the Program** (`course_sequencing`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the logical sequencing of courses within the program.
* **Balance of Workload Across Courses** (`workload_balance`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the curriculum's balance of workload across courses.
* **Assessment Methods** (`assessment_methods`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the appropriateness of assessment methods.
* **Alignment of Curriculum with Industry or Professional Needs** (`industry_alignment`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the curriculum's alignment with industry or professional needs.
* **Adequacy of Practical or Experiential Learning Components** (`practical_component`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the adequacy of practical or experiential learning components.
* **Open Feedback** (`feedback_section`, `note`, `optional`): Provide any open comments or feedback about the curriculum.
* **Curriculum Strengths** (`curriculum_strengths`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the greatest strengths of the current curriculum.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`, `optional`): Suggest areas of the curriculum that need the most improvement.
* **Courses to Add** (`courses_to_add`, `text`, `optional`): Suggest any courses or topics that should be added to the curriculum.
* **Courses to Remove** (`courses_to_remove`, `text`, `optional`): Suggest any courses or topics that should be removed from the curriculum.
* **Additional Comments or Recommendations** (`additional_comments`, `text`, `optional`): Provide any additional comments or recommendations.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, especially if you're providing feedback.
* Be concise and specific when providing ratings and comments.
* Ensure that your responses are constructive and actionable, helping to improve the curriculum.
