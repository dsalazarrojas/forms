## Purpose
The Erasmus+ Consortium Interest Survey is a tool used by the Erasmus+ Consortium to gather information from its members about their interests and involvement with the consortium. This form is intended to help the consortium better understand its members' needs and interests, and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, make sure you have the necessary information ready. The form is divided into several sections, each with specific fields that you will need to fill out. Please follow the instructions below to complete the form accurately.

1. **What is your name?**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
2. **What is your role in the Erasmus+ Consortium?**: Briefly describe your role within the consortium.
3. **Which country do you represent?**: Choose the country where you are currently working or representing.
4. **What is your email address?**: Enter your official email address associated with your work within the consortium.
5. **Have you worked with the Erasmus+ program?**: Indicate whether you have previous experience with the program.
6. **How long have you been working with the Erasmus+ Consortium?**: Select the length of time you have been working with the consortium from the options provided.
7. **What is your current position within the Erasmus+ Consortium?**: Describe your current role within the consortium.
8. **What is your main area of work within the Erasmus+ Consortium?**: Select the areas that best describe your main responsibilities within the consortium.
9. **How many years have you been working in your current position?**: Enter the number of years you have been working in your current position.
10. **What is your main interest in the Erasmus+ Consortium?**: Provide a brief description of your main interest within the consortium.
11. **What are the top 3 benefits you gained from your involvement with the consortium?**: Select the top 3 benefits you believe you have gained from your involvement with the consortium.
12. **Do you have any feedback or suggestions for the Erasmus+ Consortium?**: Share any comments or suggestions you may have for the consortium.
13. **Additional comments**: If you have any additional thoughts or comments, please include them here.

* **Have you worked with the Erasmus+ program? (form_5)**: This field helps us understand your experience with the program. If you have previous experience, please select 'Yes', otherwise 'No'.
* **How long have you been working with the Erasmus+ Consortium? (form_6)**: This field is used to understand the length of time you have been working with the consortium. Please select the option that best fits your experience.
* **What is your current position within the Erasmus+ Consortium? (form_7)**: This field is used to understand your current role within the consortium. Please provide a brief description of your position.
* **What is your main area of work within the Erasmus+ Consortium? (form_8)**: This field is used to understand your main responsibilities within the consortium. Please select the options that best fit your role.
* **How many years have you been working in your current position? (form_9)**: This field is used to understand the duration of your time in your current position. Please enter the number of years accurately.
* **What is your main interest in the Erasmus+ Consortium? (form_10)**: This field is used to understand your main interest within the consortium. Please provide a brief description of your interest.
* **What are the top 3 benefits you gained from your involvement with the consortium? (form_11)**: This field is used to understand the benefits you gained from your involvement with the consortium. Please select the top 3 benefits you believe you gained.
