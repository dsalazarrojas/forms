# Educational Change Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Change Management Survey is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from educators on their experiences with the Educational Change Management (ECM) program. Your input will help us understand your needs and improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Read the introduction page.
2. Answer the questions about the initiative name and description.
3. Indicate whether you have a role in the ECM program.
4. Select all the departments that apply to you.
5. Provide your contact information.
6. Share any feedback or suggestions you have.
7. Add any additional comments if you feel there's something you'd like to highlight.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Initiative Name** (`initiative_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the initiative you are familiar with.
*   **Initiative Description** (`initiative_description`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of the initiative.
*   **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have a role in the Educational Change Management program. You can select either 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required): List all departments where you have a role in the ECM program.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Please provide your name, email, and/or phone number.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Share your thoughts on your experiences with the ECM program.
*   **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions you have on how to improve the program.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or ideas you have.
