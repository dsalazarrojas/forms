# religious_education_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about the religious education programs offered by your organization. It collects information on program feedback, satisfaction levels, and contact information to help us better understand your experiences and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the program feedback rating that best describes your experience (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
2. Choose the education level that best fits your current situation (Primary School, High School, College/University, Other).
3. Enter your satisfaction level as a number (0-10) to help us understand how well we met your expectations.
4. Add any additional comments you have about the program.
5. Provide your contact information (email) so we can get back to you.
6. Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Text).
7. Enter the date you attended the program or would like to receive more information (mm/dd/yyyy).
8. Indicate your level of interest in the program (Yes, No).
9. Recommend the program to friends and family (Yes, No).
10. Provide any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Feedback** (`program_feedback`): This field allows you to rate your overall experience with the program.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`): This field helps us understand your current education level, which can influence our services.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`): This field asks for your level of satisfaction with the program, with a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Comments** (`comments`): This field is optional, but if you have any feedback or suggestions, we'd love to hear them!
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Please enter your email address so we can get back to you.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Text).
* **Feedback Question** (`feedback_question`): This field asks you for the date you attended the program or would like to receive more information.
* **Program Interest** (`program_interest`): This field asks for your level of interest in the program (Yes, No).
* **Program Recommendation** (`program_recommendation`): Indicate whether you would recommend the program to friends and family.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): This field is optional, but if you have any further feedback or comments, please share them.
