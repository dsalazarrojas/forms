# Survey On Discovery Learning - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Survey On Discovery Learning" form is designed to collect data from educators about their use and opinions on Discovery Learning methods in their classrooms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form's questions and select the relevant options for each question.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
3. Provide feedback on your experience with the form at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Discovery Learning Method** (`discovery_learning_method`, `select_one`, **required: false**): 
Select the method that best describes how often you use Discovery Learning in your classroom.

*   Guided Discovery: You guide students through a series of steps to help them understand a concept.
*   Self-Directed Learning: Students are responsible for finding and applying their own learning materials and activities.

### 2. **How often do you use Discovery Learning in your classroom?** (`survey_on_discovery_learning_question`, `select_multiple`, **required: false**): 
Indicate how often you use Discovery Learning in your classroom.

*   Daily: You use Discovery Learning every day in your classroom.
*   Most Days: You use Discovery Learning most days in your classroom.
*   Some Days: You use Discovery Learning some days in your classroom.
*   Rarely: You rarely use Discovery Learning in your classroom.

### 3. **What do you see as the most significant benefit of using Discovery Learning in your classroom?** (`survey_on_discovery_learning_other_question`, `text`, **required: false**): 
Please write a brief description of what you see as the most significant benefit of using Discovery Learning in your classroom.

### 4. **How would you rate your experience with this form?** (`feedback_on_form`, `select_one`, **required: false**): 
Indicate your experience with this form.

*   Excellent: The form was easy to use and the questions were clear and relevant.
*   Good: The form was mostly easy to use, but some questions were unclear or confusing.
*   Fair: The form was somewhat difficult to use, and several questions were unclear or confusing.
*   Bad: The form was very difficult to use, and most questions were unclear or confusing.

### 5. **Other feedback** (`other_feedback`, `note`, **required: false**): 
If you have any other comments or suggestions about this form, please type them in the space below.
