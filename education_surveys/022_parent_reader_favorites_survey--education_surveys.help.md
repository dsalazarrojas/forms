# Parent Reader Favorites Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your child's reading preferences, favorite books, and reading habits to help us better understand how children are reading and what influences their reading habits.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
2. Please fill out the form once.
3. For questions that require a choice, select only one option that best fits your answer.
4. If you're unsure about any question, please ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **reader_survey** (`reader_survey`, `text`, required): Please provide a brief description or story about your child's reading habits.
* **parent_survey** (`parent_survey`, `text`, required): Share any additional comments or information about your child's reading habits or preferences.
* **favorite_books** (`favorite_books`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if your child enjoys reading books. 
*   - "Yes" if your child enjoys reading books
*   - "No" if your child does not enjoy reading books
* **reading_level** (`reading_level`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the level of reading your child typically reads at:
	+ 1: Pre-school level (picture books, simple stories)
	+ 2: Early reader level (simple stories with a few sentences per page)
	+ 3: Chapter book level (more complex stories with chapters)
	+ 4: Advanced reader level (chapter books with more complex content)
* **reading_frequency** (`reading_frequency`, `text`, optional): Describe how often your child reads.
* **favorite_character** (`favorite_character`, `text`, optional): Mention if there is a favorite character in your child's favorite book.
* **reading_location** (`reading_location`, `text`, optional): Indicate where your child usually reads.
* **reading_materials** (`reading_materials`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of reading materials your child enjoys:
	+ Books
	+ Audiobooks
	+ E-books
* **reading_device** (`reading_device`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the devices your child uses for reading:
	+ Tablet
	+ Phone
	+ E-reader
	+ Other (please specify)
* **reading_with** (`reading_with`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose where your child usually reads:
	+ Parents
	+ Siblings
	+ Friends
	+ Alone
* **family_reading_habits** (`family_reading_habits`, `text`, required): Describe any family reading habits or traditions that influence your child's reading habits.
* **reading_facts** (`reading_facts`, `text`, optional): Share any interesting facts or observations about your child's reading habits.
* **survey_date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): Please provide the date you completed this survey.
* **survey_time** (`survey_time`, `time`, optional): Mention the time you completed this survey.
* **contact_information** (`contact_information`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional contact information (e.g., phone number, email) that we can use to follow up.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter a phone number where we can reach you.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter an email address where we can reach you.
* **email_2** (`email_2`, `email`, optional): Enter an alternate email address where we can reach you.
* **favorite_website** (`favorite_website`, `text`, optional): Mention a favorite website that you or your child enjoy.
*   (Repeat this section 5 more times: `favorite_website_2`, `favorite_website_3`, `favorite_website_4`, `favorite_website_5`, `favorite_website_6`)
* **favorite_website_7** (`favorite_website_7`, `text`, optional): Mention a favorite website that you or your child enjoys.

## Tips
* Please fill out the survey accurately and truthfully.
* If you're unsure about any question, please ask for help.
* We appreciate your time and feedback about your child's reading habits.
