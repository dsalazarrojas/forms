# Global Teaching Innovation Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Teaching Innovation Index Survey is a tool designed to collect data on teaching innovation from educators. The form will help educators provide feedback on their teaching practices and experiences, allowing for a more accurate understanding of the frequency, types, and challenges of innovation in teaching.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the correct options for each question, taking note of the required fields.
3. If you have any issues or questions while completing the form, please contact the administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_question (Number)** (`first_question`, required/optional): This question asks you to provide a numerical answer, which can be a whole number or a decimal. Please enter the number carefully.
* **first_answer (Select Multiple)** (`first_answer`, optional): Please select one or more of the options that best describe your teaching practices. Select all that apply.
* **first_answer_2 (Select One)** (`first_answer_2`, optional): Choose one option that best describes your teaching experience.
* **first_question_2 (Date)** (`second_question`, optional): This question asks for a date in the format of MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/YY (e.g., 01/01/22). Please enter the date carefully.
* **first_question_3 (Time)** (`third_question`, optional): This question asks for a time in the format of HH:MM AM/PM or 24-hour clock (e.g., 01:30 PM or 13:30). Please enter the time carefully.
* **first_question_4 (Email)** (`fourth_question`, optional): This question asks for an email address, which should be in the format of name@domain.com. Please enter the email address carefully.
* **first_question_5 (Text)** (`fifth_question`, optional): This question allows you to enter a short description of your teaching practices or experiences.
* **first_question_6 (Text)** (`sixth_question`, optional): This question allows you to enter any additional comments or information about your teaching practices or experiences.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions carefully and accurately, as they will help us understand your teaching practices and experiences.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the administrator.
