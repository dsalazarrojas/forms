# International Exchange Program Impact Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the impact of an international exchange program on participating students and programs. It helps to gather information on the outcomes, experiences, and feedback from participants, enabling the program's administrators to assess its effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Provide detailed explanations and descriptions for each field, as they will be used to assess the program's impact.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol.
4. If you are unsure about any field or section, please ask for clarification or guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exchange Participant Information** (id: 1): This section is for providing basic information about the participant, such as name, contact details, and any other relevant details. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Student Info** (id: 2): This section is for providing information about the student participating in the exchange program. Please include relevant details about their experience, skills, and background.
* **What were the main outcomes of your exchange experience?** (id: 3): This section is for describing the main outcomes or results achieved through the exchange program. Please be specific and provide examples or evidence to support your answers.
* **What new skills did you acquire during your exchange experience?** (id: 4): This section is for listing the new skills, knowledge, or abilities gained through the exchange program. Please be specific and provide examples or evidence to support your answers.
* **Exchange Program Information** (id: 5): This section is for providing information about the exchange program, such as its duration, location, and structure.
* **What were the main outcomes of your exchange program?** (id: 6): This section is similar to id: 3, but specific to the exchange program.
