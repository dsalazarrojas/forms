# Curriculum Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Curriculum Survey is designed to gather feedback from learners about their experience with a given curriculum. This survey aims to identify strengths and weaknesses of the curriculum, gather suggestions for improvement, and assess overall satisfaction with the delivery of the curriculum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the survey questions and think about your experience with the curriculum.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Choose the correct response from the provided options for each question.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to add them in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1): This is a note section where you can provide any general comments or thoughts about the survey.
* **What was the most useful part of the curriculum?** (2): Share with us what you found most beneficial or useful during the curriculum.
* **What was the most difficult part of the curriculum?** (3): Describe the part of the curriculum that you struggled with or found challenging.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of the curriculum?** (4): Rate the quality of the curriculum using a number from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Was the curriculum delivered in a way that was clear and concise?** (5): Answer 'Yes' if you felt the curriculum was easy to understand, 'No' if you found it unclear, or 'Undecided' if you're not sure.
* **Was the curriculum delivered in a way that was relevant to your goals and objectives?** (6): Answer 'Yes' if you felt the curriculum aligned with your goals, 'No' if you didn't find it relevant, or 'Undecided' if you're not sure.
* **How would you rate the overall delivery of the curriculum?** (7): Rate the delivery of the curriculum using the options 'Excellent', 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Poor'.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement?** (8, 9): Share any specific ideas or suggestions you have for improving the curriculum in this text field. You can add as many suggestions as you like.
* **Was the curriculum delivered by a qualified instructor?** (10): Answer 'Yes' if you felt the instructor was qualified and effective, 'No' if you didn't, or 'Undecided' if you're not sure.
* **Was the curriculum delivered in a way that was engaging?** (11): Answer 'Yes' if you felt the curriculum was engaging and interesting, 'No' if you didn't, or 'Undecided' if you're not sure.
* **Would you recommend this curriculum to others?** (12): Answer 'Yes' if you would recommend the curriculum, 'No' if you wouldn't, or 'Undecided' if you're not sure.

Note: All questions are optional, and you can leave any question blank if you don't feel it's relevant to your experience.
