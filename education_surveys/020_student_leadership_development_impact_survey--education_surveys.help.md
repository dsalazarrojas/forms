# Student Leadership Development Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Leadership Development Impact Survey is designed to collect data on your experiences and perceptions of the leadership development programs at your school. Your feedback is essential for us to understand the effectiveness of these programs and make improvements for future students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all that apply to the best of your ability in each section.
2. For the "student_information" and "development_areas" sections, select as many options as apply to you.
3. Fill in the relevant fields with your school, program, and personal details as required.
4. Submit your form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_information** (Select Multiple): Please select all the student information that applies to you:
	+ Yes, I am currently enrolled at the school.
	+ No, I am not currently enrolled at the school.
* **leadership_areas** (Select One): Please select the leadership areas you are currently developing:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **program_outcomes** (Select One): Please select the program outcomes that you have achieved:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **development_areas** (Select Multiple): Please select all the development areas that you are currently developing:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **survey_questions** (Select Multiple): Please select all the survey questions that apply to you:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **additional_comments** (Note): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **email_address** (Text): Please enter your email address.
* **phone_number** (Text): Please enter your phone number.
* **school_name** (Text): Please enter the name of your school.
* **school_type** (Text): Please enter the type of your school.
* **program_name** (Text): Please enter the name of your program.
* **program_type** (Text): Please enter the type of your program.
* **department** (Text): Please enter the department of your school.
* **location** (Text): Please enter the location of your school.
* **survey_completed** (Text): Please acknowledge that you have completed the survey.
* **form_completed** (Text): Please acknowledge that you have completed the form.
* **form_title** (Text): Please enter the title of this form.
* **form_description** (Text): Please enter the description of this form.
* **form_category** (Text): Please enter the category of this form.
* **form_assigned** (Text): Please enter the person assigned to fill this form.
* **form_version** (Text): Please enter the version of this form.
* **date_submitted** (Date): The date when the form was submitted.
* **time_submitted** (Time): The time when the form was submitted. 

**Tips**
* Please fill in all the fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* Make sure to double-check your submissions before clicking the submit button.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact your school's leadership development team.
