# Officer Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an officer nomination from a voter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Make sure to enter the correct details for the nominee.
3. Provide a clear reason for the nomination.
4. Enter the nominee's qualifications.
5. Enter your contact information for confirmation and follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Form**: Enter a brief description of the reason why you are nominating this person.
* **VOTER NAME**: Enter your name as the nominator.
* **NOMINEE NAME**: Enter the name of the person you are nominating.
* **Reason for Nomination**: Provide a clear explanation for why you are nominating this person.
* **NOMINEE QUALIFICATIONS**: Briefly describe the qualifications or qualities that make this person fit for the role.
* **VOTER INFORMATION**: Enter your contact information for confirmation and follow-up purposes.
* **Name**: Enter your name as the voter.
* **Email**: Enter your email address as the voter.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number as the voter.

## Tips
* Make sure to proofread your entries for accuracy and clarity.
* Ensure that your nomination is based on your genuine opinion.
* If you are unable to complete the form, please contact <form@gic.mx> for assistance.
* Please follow the instructions for submission procedures in your organization's internal guidelines.
