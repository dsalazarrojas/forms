# Personal Assistant Time InOut Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Assistant Time InOut Form is designed to collect information about time worked or logged for the purpose of reporting and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Navigate to the Time In page (Page 1) and enter the date for the time you wish to log (or log the current date if you're not sure).
2. Enter the time you logged in (start time) in the Time In field.
3. Enter the time you logged out (end time) in the Time Out field.
4. For each subsequent page, repeat the process of entering the date and time in and out of the office or workspace.
