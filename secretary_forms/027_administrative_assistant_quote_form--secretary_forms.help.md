# Administrative Assistant Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Administrative Assistant Quote Form is designed to generate a quote for administrative services, providing a detailed estimate of the services required, estimated duration, hourly rate, and total amount. This form is essential for businesses or organizations seeking to outsource administrative tasks, ensuring accurate and transparent pricing for their clients.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in the basic client information:
    *   **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client requesting services.
    *   **Client Company/ Organization**: Enter the name of the client's company or organization.
    *   **Client Email**: Enter the email address for communication with the client.
    *   **Client Phone**: Optional - Enter the phone number for contact.
2.  Move on to the service details:
    *   **Quote Date**: Set the date when the quote is prepared.
    *   **Quote Valid Until**: Set the date until this quote is valid.
    *   **Type of Administrative Service**: Select the type of administrative service needed (e.g., General Administrative Support, Executive Assistance, etc.).
    *   **Service Description**: Provide a detailed description of the services required.
    *   **Estimated Duration**: Select the duration of the service (e.g., One-time, Daily, Weekly, etc.).
    *   **Hours per Day**: Optional - If the service is not one-time, specify the hours needed per day.
    *   **Days per Week**: Optional - If the service is not one-time, specify the days needed per week.
3.  Proceed to the billing details:
    *   **Hourly Rate**: Set the hourly rate for the service.
    *   **Estimated Total Hours**: Enter the estimated total number of hours needed.
    *   **Subtotal**: This field will automatically calculate the total amount before taxes and additional fees.
    *   **Tax Percentage**: If applicable, enter the tax percentage to be applied.
    *   **Tax Amount**: This field will automatically calculate the tax amount.
    *   **Additional Fees**: Optional - Enter any additional fees.
    *   **Total Amount**: This field automatically calculates the final total including all charges.
4.  Complete the form with the client and quote details:
    *   **Payment Terms**: Enter the terms for payment.
    *   **Special Conditions**: Optional - Enter any special conditions or requirements.
    *   **Quote Prepared By**: Enter the name of the person preparing the quote.
    *   **Quote Approved By**: Optional - Enter the name of the person approving the quote.
