# letter_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The letter request form is used to collect information to generate a formal letter for end users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the requested information. Start with the first field and work your way down. Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure the generated letter meets your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (First Name, Text, Required/Optional: False)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., driver's license).
* **Last Name (Last Name, Text, Required/Optional: False)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., driver's license).
* **Email (Email, Email, Required/Optional: False)**: Enter your contact email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone (Phone, Text, Required/Optional: False)**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Target Date (Target Date, Date, Required/Optional: False)**: Enter the date when you would like the letter to be issued.
* **Questions 1 (Questions 1, Text, Required/Optional: False)**: Enter any additional information that you would like to have included in the letter.
* **Questions 2 (Questions 2, Text, Required/Optional: False)**: Enter any additional information that you would like to have included in the letter.
* **Questions 3 (Questions 3, Text, Required/Optional: False)**: Enter any additional information that you would like to have included in the letter.
* **Questions 4 (Questions 4, Text, Required/Optional: False)**: Enter any additional information that you would like to have included in the letter.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in accurate and complete information to ensure the generated letter meets your needs.
- It is best to fill in all the fields for a more comprehensive letter.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the Secretary team for assistance.
