# office-assistant-job-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for applicants to provide their contact information, work experience, education, skills, and availability for office assistant positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your level of work experience, either "Resume" or "Available".
3. List your educational background in the "Education" field.
4. Choose your relevant skills from the "Skills" dropdown menu.
5. Enter your availability for the position, using the "Availability" and "Availability 2" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to provide your name, email address, and phone number. Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose how you would describe your level of work experience. Options are "Resume" and "Available".
* **Education** (`Education`, `text`, required: false): Describe your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Skills** (`Skills`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one or more relevant skills you have that align with the office assistant position. Options include "Communication", "Time Management", and "Organization".
* **Availability** (`Availability`, `time`, required: false): Enter your available work hours or shifts.
* **Availability 2** (`Availability 2`, `time`, required: false): Enter any additional availability information, such as flexible shifts or specific days off.
