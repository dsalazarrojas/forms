# Mail Forwarding Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Mail Forwarding Request Form is used to submit a request for mail forwarding services. It is intended for individuals who have moved to a new location and want to redirect their mail to their new address.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete details.
3. Submit the form to initiate the mail forwarding request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mail Forwarding Request** (`mail_forwarding_request_form`): Enter a brief description of your mail forwarding request, if any.
* **Recipient Address** (`recipient_address`): Enter the address where you want your mail to be forwarded to. Please make sure to enter it in the correct format (e.g., 123 Main Street, Anytown, USA, 12345).
* **Current Address** (`current_address`): Enter your current address, where the postal service will be able to contact you.
* **Forwarding Starting Date** (`forwarding_starting_date`): Select the date when you want the mail forwarding service to start. The date format is MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Forwarding Ending Date** (`forwarding_ending_date`): Select the date when you want the mail forwarding service to end. The date format is MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Mail Frequency** (`mail_frequency`): Select the frequency at which you want to receive mail at the recipient's address. Options are:
	+ Frequently: You want to receive mail at the recipient's address as regularly as possible.
	+ Occasionally: You only occasionally need to receive mail at the recipient's address.
	+ Rarely: You very rarely need to receive mail at the recipient's address.
	+ Never: You do not want to receive mail at the recipient's address.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to avoid delays in processing your mail forwarding request.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the postal service for assistance.
* You can also submit additional requests if you need to make changes to your mail forwarding settings.
