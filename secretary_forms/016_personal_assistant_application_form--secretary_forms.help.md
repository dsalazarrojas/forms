# Personal Assistant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personal Assistant Application Form is a tool used to gather information from potential employees about their background, skills, and availability to match them with suitable job postings for personal assistant positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This field requires a valid email format.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Provide a brief description of your work experience in the "Work Experience" field.
6. Mention your education background in the "Education" field.
7. Select your skills from the available options in the "Skills" field.
8. Choose your availability for work in the "Availability" field by selecting a date.
9. Select a start date for your work in the "Start Date" field.
10. Select an end date for your work in the "End Date" field.
11. Select a time slot for your availability in the "Availability Time" field.
12. Repeat the time slot selection for your second availability in the "Availability Time 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Work Experience** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of your work experience.
* **Education** (Text, Optional): Mention your education background.
* **Skills** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select your skills from the available options.
* **Availability** (Date, Optional): Choose your availability for work.
* **Start Date** (Date, Optional): Select a start date for your work.
* **End Date** (Date, Optional): Select an end date for your work.
* **Availability Time** (Time, Optional): Select a time slot for your availability.
* **Availability Time 2** (Time, Optional): Repeat the time slot selection for your second availability.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately.
- Be specific and concise while filling out the optional fields.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask a representative for clarification.
