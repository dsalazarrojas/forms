# fax_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to send a fax request to a specified fax number. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that the fax request is sent correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields.
2. Enter your contact details in the `contact_details` field.
3. Provide your email address in the `email` field.
4. Enter your phone number in the `phone_number` field.
5. Enter the fax number you want to send the fax to in the `fax_number` field.
6. Select the date and time you want to send the fax in the `date` and `time` fields.
7. Enter any additional message you want to include in the `message` field.
8. Select the type of contact you are (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3") in the `contact_type` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to be used for communication purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
- **Fax Number** (`fax_number`, number, required/optional): Enter the fax number you want to send the fax to.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you want to send the fax on.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Select the time you want to send the fax on.
- **Message** (`message`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional message to include in the fax.
- **Contact Type** (`contact_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of contact you are (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3").
