# Sales Discovery Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details about potential customers who have expressed interest in our sales team's services. It will help our sales representatives prioritize their follow-up interactions and improve conversion rates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you're filling out the form for a legitimate lead that has shown interest in our services.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with a *.
3. Select the correct options for fields with multiple options (e.g., company size, industry, sector, priority, status, and context).
4. Double-check that you've filled out all the necessary information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. *Lead Details* (text)
*   **Lead Details** (text): A brief description of the lead, including any relevant context or notes about their interest in our services.

*   *Company Name*: The name of the company the lead is from.

*   **Company Name** (text): Enter the name of the company the lead is from.

### 2. *Company Name* (text)
*   **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company the lead is from.

### 3. *Company Size* (select one)
*   **Company Size**: Select the size of the company (Small, Medium, or Large).

### 4. *Company Industry* (select multiple)
*   **Company Industry**: Select all the industries that apply to the company (Technology, Marketing, Finance).

### 5. *Company Sector* (select one)
*   **Company Sector**: Select the sector the company operates in (Software, Marketing, Finance).

### 6. *Company Address* (text)
*   **Company Address**: Enter the address of the company.

### 7. *Lead Priority* (select one)
*   **Lead Priority**: Select the level of priority (High, Medium, Low).

### 8. *Lead Status* (select one)
*   **Lead Status**: Select the status of the lead (New, Qualified, Disqualified).

### 9. *Lead Context* (select one)
*   **Lead Context**: Select the context of the lead (Sales, Marketing, Technical).

### 10. *Company Phone* (text)
*   **Company Phone**: Enter the phone number of the company.

### 11. *Company Website* (text)
*   **Company Website**: Enter the website of the company.

### 12. *Sales Rep* (text)
*   **Sales Rep**: Enter the name of the sales representative assigned to the lead.

Note: The form requires no other fields, but it's recommended to fill out all the fields for better information and follow-up. 

This help guide is designed to be a quick reference for filling out the form.
