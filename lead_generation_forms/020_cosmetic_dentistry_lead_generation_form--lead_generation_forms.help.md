# Cosmetic Dentistry Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to generate leads for a cosmetic dentistry business. The form collects information about the individual filling out the form, such as their first name, last name, email, phone number, address, date of birth, age, and preferred contact method. The form also includes a series of follow-up questions to gather more information about the individual's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the following fields:
	* First name
	* Last name
	* Email
	* Phone number
	* Address
	* Date of birth
	* Age
	* Preferred contact method (select as many options as apply)
	* Follow-up questions (answer each question as best as you can)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter your age.
* **Cosmetic Dentistry Service** (`cosmetic_dentistry_service`, select one, optional):
	+ Select "Yes" if you are interested in cosmetic dentistry services.
	+ Select "No" if you are not interested in cosmetic dentistry services.
* **Other Cosmetic Dentistry Services** (`other_cosmetic_dentistry_services`, text, optional): If you have other cosmetic dentistry services in mind, enter them here.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Select "Yes" if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
	+ Select "Yes" if you prefer to be contacted via email.
	+ Select "No" if you do not prefer to be contacted via any method.
* **Follow-up Questions** (`follow_up_question1` to `follow_up_question15`, text, optional): Answer each follow-up question as best as you can.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your interest in our services and look forward to following up with you!
