# Website Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from users for lead generation or data collection purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your User Input in the provided field.
2. Enter your Referral Code, if applicable.
3. Enter your Name and Email for identity verification.
4. Provide your Phone Number, Date of Birth, and Country for demographic information.
5. Select your State and Country Region for geographical information.
6. Confirm your Email and Phone Verification.
7. Enter any additional Notes, if necessary.
8. Select your Time Zone and Date of Birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant information about the user.
* **Referral Code** (`referral_code`, `text`, optional): Enter a referral code if you have been referred.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name for identity verification.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, email, optional): Enter your email address for verification.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, required): Select the country where you are located.
* **State** (`state`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your state of residence.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city where you are located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Email Verified** (`email_verified`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your email verification.
* **Phone Verified** (`phone_verified`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your phone verification.
* **Country Region** (`country_region`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the region where you are located.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant information about the prophet.
* **Time Reported** (`time_reported`, `time`, optional): Enter the time when you reported.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, `text`, optional): Enter the time zone where you are located.
* **Time Zoned** (`timezone`, `text`, optional): Enter the timezone where you are located.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time.
* **Date Reported** (`date_reported`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when you reported.
* **Date Reported** (`date_reported`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when you reported.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date.
