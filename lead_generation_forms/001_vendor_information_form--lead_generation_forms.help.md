# Vendor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Vendor Information Form" is a collection of fields that provide information about a vendor. It's likely used for business or administrative purposes, such as onboarding a new vendor or updating existing vendor details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information, indicated by a red asterisk (*), or the information you feel is necessary to provide. You can skip fields that are not required.
2. Ensure the contact email is in a proper email format (e.g., `user@example.com`).
3. Select a vendor type from the dropdown list.
4. Select multiple vendor status options if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Information** (1) (`vendor_information_form`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the vendor.
* **Vendor Address** (2) (`vendor_address`, text, required: false): Enter the vendor's address.
* **Contact Name** (3) (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Company Name** (4) (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company.
* **Contact Email** (5) (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person in a proper format (e.g., `user@example.com`).
* **Contact Phone** (6) (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone Extension** (7) (`contact_phone_extension`, text, required: false): Enter the phone extension of the contact person.
* **Vendor Type** (8) (`vendor_type`, select_one, required: false): Select a type from the dropdown list to categorize the vendor.
* **Vendor Status** (9) (`vendor_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple status options if applicable.
* **Vendor Notes** (10) (`vendor_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the vendor.
* **Vendor Verification Date** (11) (`vendor_verification_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the vendor was last verified.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled in correctly to provide accurate information.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer to your vendor agreement or contract for clarification.
* Double-check the vendor address and contact details for accuracy.
* If you're updating vendor information, ensure the new information is accurate and up-to-date.
