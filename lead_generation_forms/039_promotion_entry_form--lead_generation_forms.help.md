# Promotion Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect contact information and preferences from individuals interested in a promotion or offer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Contact Info** with your name and any relevant details.
2. Select your **Preferred Message Channel** (SMS, Email, Phone, or WhatsApp).
3. Choose how often you would like to receive messages (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
4. Agree to receive messages from us (or not).
5. Enter your **Email Address** for message delivery.
6. Enter your **Phone Number** for message delivery (if applicable).
7. Confirm your agreement to receive messages (by selecting "True" or "False").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info (1)**: Enter your name and any relevant details.
* **Message Preferred Channel (2)**: Select how you would like to receive messages (SMS, Email, Phone, or WhatsApp).
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address to receive messages.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number to receive messages (if applicable).
* **Message Channels (5)**: Select all the message channels you're willing to receive messages through (SMS, Email, Phone, or WhatsApp).
* **Message Frequency (6)**: Choose how often you'd like to receive messages (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Agreement (7)**: Confirm that you agree to receive messages from us.
* **Confirm (8)**: Confirm that you agree to receive messages from us.
* **Agreement 2-8 (9-18)**: These are confirmations of your agreements for receiving messages.
* **Confirm 2-9 (19-25)**: Confirm your agreements for receiving messages.
