# Communication Service Release Client Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Communication Service Release Client Interest Form is designed to collect information about your company's interest in our new communication service. This form helps us understand your requirements and preferences, allowing us to better serve your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
- Be honest about your current challenges and solutions.
- Indicate your level of interest in our new service.
- Provide any additional requirements or needs.
- Specify how you heard about our service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Interest Information**: This section is where you tell us about your interest in our new communication service.
  - **Submission Date**: The date of this form submission.
    - This field is required to track the timing of your interest.
  - **Company Information**: Provide details about your organization.
    - This is a note field where you can add any information you feel is relevant.
  - **Company Name**: The full legal name of your company.
    - This is a required field to ensure we understand your company identity.
  - **Company Size**: The number of employees in your company.
    - This field helps us understand your company's scale and potential needs.
  - **Industry**: The industry your company operates in.
    - Select the one that best describes your industry from the list.
  - **Company Website**: The URL of your company website.
    - This field is optional, but it helps us understand your online presence.
  - **Contact Information**: A note field for you to add any contact details you feel are relevant.
    - This is optional, but it helps us understand how to reach you.
  - **Contact Name**: The full name of the primary contact.
    - This is a required field to ensure we know who to contact.
  - **Contact Title**: The job title or position of the contact.
    - This field helps us understand their role in the company.
  - **Contact Email**: The email address of the primary contact.
    - This is a required field for communication purposes.
  - **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the primary contact.
    - This is a required field for quick contact.
  - **Current Solution**: The communication solution you currently use.
    - Select from the options to indicate your current setup.
  - **Current Challenges**: The challenges you face with your current solution.
    - Select all that apply from the list.
  - **Interest Level**: How interested are you in our new service?
    - Select the level that best describes your interest.
  - **Features of Interest**: Which features interest you the most.
    - Select all that apply from the list.
  - **Expected Users**: How many users would need the service?
    - Select the range that best describes your needs.
  - **Timeline**: When would you like to implement the service?
    - Select the timeline that works best for you.
  - **Budget Range**: What is your expected budget range for the service?
    - Select from the options to indicate your budget expectations.
  - **Decision Maker**: Are you the decision maker or just researching for someone else?
    - Select who you are to help us understand your role.
  - **Demo Interest**: Would you like a product demo?
    - Select from the options to indicate your interest in a demo.
  - **Pilot Interest**: Would you participate in a pilot program?
    - Select from the options to indicate your interest in a pilot.
  - **Additional Requirements**: Any specific requirements or needs you have.
    - This is an optional field for you to add any additional information.
  - **How Did You Hear About Us**: How did you learn about our service?
    - Select from the options to indicate how you found out about us.
  - **Additional Comments**: Any other comments or questions you have.
    - This is an optional field for you to add any further information.
