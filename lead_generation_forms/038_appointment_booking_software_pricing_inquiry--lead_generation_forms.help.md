# Appointment Booking Software Pricing Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users who want to inquire about the pricing of the Appointment Booking software. It collects contact information, business details, and specific requirements for the software to provide a more accurate pricing quote.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your contact information and business details.
2. Select your company's industry and size.
3. Indicate your current booking system status and the number of appointments you need to book per month.
4. Choose the key features you require from the software.
5. If applicable, provide your integration requirements and budget details.
6. Select your desired implementation timeline.
7. Provide any additional comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select your company's industry from the options provided.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Select your company's size from the options provided.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, optional): Enter your business address (if you choose to provide it).
* **Do you currently have a booking system?** (`current_booking_system`, select_one, required): Select whether you currently have a booking system and what your intentions are.
* **Approximately how many appointments per month?** (`monthly_appointments`, select_one, required): Select the approximate number of appointments you need to book per month.
* **Key features you need** (`key_features_needed`, select_multiple, required): Select all the key features you require from the software.
* **Integration requirements** (`integration_requirements`, text, optional): Describe any integration requirements you have for the software.
* **Monthly budget for software** (`monthly_budget`, select_one, optional): Select your monthly budget for the software.
* **When would you like to implement?** (`implementation_timeline`, select_one, required): Select your desired implementation timeline.
* **Additional questions or comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional questions or comments you have.
