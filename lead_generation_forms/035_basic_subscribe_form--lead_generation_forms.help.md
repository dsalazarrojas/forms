# Basic Subscribe Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to subscribe to a newsletter or other types of regular communications from an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the guide above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Choose your subscription type** (`subscription_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of subscription you are interested in.
* **Topics you are interested in** (`interest_topics`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the topics you are interested in.
* **Company name (optional)** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your company name if you wish.
* **Industry or profession** (`industry`, `text`, optional): Enter your industry or profession if you wish.
* **Phone number (optional)** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish.
* **How often would you like to hear from us?** (`frequency_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you would like to hear from us.
* **Preferred time to receive emails** (`send_time`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred time to receive emails.
* **I consent to receive communications from this organization** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Agree or disagree to receiving communications from this organization.
* **I agree to the privacy policy** (`privacy_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Agree to the organization's privacy policy.
* **Allow sharing my email with trusted partners** (`share_on_third_party`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you want to allow sharing of your email with trusted partners.
* **I confirm and want to subscribe** (`subscription_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your subscription by selecting "I confirm and want to subscribe".
