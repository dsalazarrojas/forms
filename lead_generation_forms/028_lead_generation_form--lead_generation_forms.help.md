# Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead Generation Form is designed to collect relevant information about potential leads for various projects, campaigns, or initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you're completing the form for the correct lead.
2. Fill in the required information, marked with a red asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`lead_generation_form_first_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`lead_generation_form_last_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`lead_generation_form_email`, `email`, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`lead_generation_form_phone`, `text`, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Location** (`lead_generation_form_location`, `text`, Optional): Enter your location or office address.
* **Company** (`lead_generation_form_company`, `text`, Optional): Enter your company name.
* **Position** (`lead_generation_form_position`, `text`, Optional): Enter your job position.
* **Department** (`lead_generation_form_department`, `text`, Optional): Enter your department in the company.
* **Job Title** (`lead_generation_form_job_title`, `text`, Optional): Enter your job title.
* **Annual Revenue** (`lead_generation_form_annual_revenue`, `number`, Optional): Enter your company's annual revenue.
* **Annual Budget** (`lead_generation_form_annual_budget`, `number`, Optional): Enter your company's annual budget.
* **Industry** (`lead_generation_form_industry`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the industry related to your company or project.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_generation_form_lead_source`, `select_multiple`, Required): Select all relevant lead sources, such as Social Media, Online Ad, or Referral.
* **Other Lead Source** (`lead_generation_form_lead_source_other`, `text`, Optional): If not listed, please specify other lead sources.
* **Agency Experience** (`lead_generation_form_agency_experience`, `text`, Optional): Describe your experience with agencies or service providers.
* **Agency Services** (`lead_generation_form_agency_services`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the agency services your company or project require.
* **Other Agency Services** (`lead_generation_form_agency_services_other`, `text`, Optional): If not listed, please specify other agency services.
* **Company Size** (`lead_generation_form_company_size`, `select_one`, Required): Select the size of your company.
* **Industry Experience** (`lead_generation_form_industry_experience`, `select_one`, Required): Choose your industry experience level.
* **Lead** (`lead_generation_form_lead`, `select_one`, Required): Select the lead you're representing.
* **Comments** (`lead_generation_form_comments`, `text`, Optional): Add any additional comments about the lead.
