# Retail Chatbot Integration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assist retail businesses in registering their integration with our chatbot system. The form collects essential information to enable seamless and secure integration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form fields carefully and ensure you have all the required information before proceeding.
2. Fill in the form with the requested information.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retail Chatbot Integration Registration** (`user_input_1`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your retail business or integration purpose.
* **Business Information** (`user_input_2`, text, required): Describe your business, including its name, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Email** (`email_input_1`, email, required): Enter the email address associated with your retail business or integration.
* **Phone** (`phone_input_1`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number for your retail business or integration contact.
* **Integration Type** (`integration_type_input_1`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you are integrating our chatbot with your retail business, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Integration Requirements** (`integration_requirements_input_1`, text, required): Describe any specific requirements or constraints for the integration.
* **Vendor or Agency** (`vendor_or_agency_input_1`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' if you are a vendor or agency, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name_input_1`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the integration.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email_input_1`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone_input_1`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number for the contact person.
* **Integration Status** (`integration_status_input_1`, select_one, required): Select 'Active' if the integration is active, or 'Inactive' if it is not.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments_input_1`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the integration.
