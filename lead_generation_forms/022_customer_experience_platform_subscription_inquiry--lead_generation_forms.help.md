# customer_experience_platform_subscription_inquiry - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential customers about their subscription inquiry for the Customer Experience Platform.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Form Header** (`form_header`, `text`, required): This is the title of the form, which is not editable.
2. **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter your company name as it is registered.
3. **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): If there is a specific person who will be the primary point of contact, please provide their name.
4. **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your company's email address where you would like to receive updates and notifications.
5. **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your company's phone number where you can be reached.
6. **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Provide your company's physical address.
7. **Subscription Plan** (`subscription_plan`, `select_one`, required): Select the subscription plan that best suits your business needs:
	* Free
	* Premium
	* Enterprise
8. **Rollout Details** (`rollout_details`, `date`, optional): If you have any specific rollout details, please provide them.
9. **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, optional): If you have a website for your company, provide the URL.
10. **Company Description** (`company_description`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your company and its services.
11. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): If you would like to be contacted directly, provide your personal email address.
12. **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): If you would like to be contacted directly, provide your personal phone number.
13. **Sales Email** (`sales_email`, `email`, optional): If you have a sales person who would like to be contacted, provide their email address.
14. **Sales Phone** (`sales_phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a sales person who would like to be contacted, provide their phone number.
15. **Support Email** (`support_email`, `email`, optional): If you have a support person who would like to be contacted, provide their email address.
16. **Support Phone** (`support_phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a support person who would like to be contacted, provide their phone number.
17. **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them here.
18. **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select the size of your company:
	* Small
	* Medium
	* Large
19. **Company Type** (`company_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of your company:
	* B2B
	* B2C
	* B2B+B2C
	* Other
20. **Industry** (`industry`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the industry that your company operates in:
	* Yes
	* No
21. **Location** (`location`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the location of your company:
	* Yes
	* No
22. **Job Title** (`job_title`, `select_one`, required): Select your job title:
	* Yes
	* No
23. **Role** (`role`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your role within the company.
24. **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, `select_one`, optional): Select where you found us:
	* Social Media
	* Referral
	* Online Ads
	* Other
25. **Follow-up Prospects** (`follow_up_prospects`, `date`, optional): If you would like to be followed up with, provide a date.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that we can provide you with the right information.
- If you are unsure about any field, please feel free to ask us.
- Make sure to check your email for updates and notifications.

This guide is meant to be a concrete and concrete explanation of the form. It is assumed that you are familiar with the fields and their meanings. If you are not, please ask the form creator.
