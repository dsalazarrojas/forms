# Lead Information And Promotion Code Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lead Information And Promotion Code Form is designed to collect data about leads and promotion codes for marketing and sales purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the correct options for lead source and promotion code from the drop-down menus.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name (<name>, text, required: false)
Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.

### Email (<email>, email, required: false)
Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.

### Phone (<phone>, text, required: false)
Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.

### Company (<company>, text, required: false)
Enter your company name as it appears on your business card or identification.

### Lead Source (<lead source>, select_one, required: false)
Select the source of the lead from the options provided:
* Yes
* No

### Promotion Code (<promotion code>, select_one, required: false)
Select the promotion code that applies to your lead from the options provided:
* code 1
* code 2
* code 3
* code 4
* code 5
* other
* none
* not provided
* custom 1
* custom 2
* custom 3
* custom 4

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and consistently to maintain data quality.
- If unsure about any field, refer to your business card or identification for clarification.
- If you need further assistance, contact the support team.
