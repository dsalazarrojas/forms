# Advertiser Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential advertisers to create an ad campaign that meets their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your business name, contact person name, title, email address, and phone number in the "Business Information" section.
2.  Select the type of ad you are interested in (e.g., Banner Ad, Text Ad, Image Ad, etc.).
3.  Choose your preferred banner size, placement, and campaign duration.
4.  Select your budget range for the ad campaign.
5.  Describe your target audience and their demographics.
6.  Provide a brief description of your product or service.
7.  Specify the action you want viewers to take.
8.  If applicable, upload your logo or ad image.
9.  If applicable, specify any special requirements for your advertisement.
10. Confirm that you are authorized to make this advertising purchase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Business Name** (`<Business Name>`, text, required): Enter your business or organization name.
*   **Contact Person Name** (`<Contact Person Name>`, text, required): Enter the name of the person making this purchase.
*   **Contact Person Title** (`<Contact Person Title>`, text, required): Enter the job title of the contact person.
*   **Email Address** (`<Email Address>`, email, required): Enter your business email for communication.
*   **Phone Number** (`<Phone Number>`, text, required): Enter your business phone number.
*   **Ad Type Requested** (`<Ad Type Requested>`, select_one, required): Select the type of ad you are interested in (e.g., Banner Ad, Text Ad, Image Ad, etc.).
*   **Banner Size Preference** (`<Banner Size Preference>`, select_one, required): If selecting banner ad, choose your preferred banner size.
*   **Placement Preference** (`<Placement Preference>`, text, required): Choose where you would like your ad to be placed.
*   **Campaign Duration** (`<Campaign Duration>`, select_one, required): Choose the length of your ad campaign.
*   **Budget Range** (`<Budget Range>`, select_one, required): Select your budget range for this advertising purchase.
*   **Target Demographics** (`<Target Demographics>`, text, required): Describe your target audience and their demographics.
*   **Product Service Description** (`<Product Service Description>`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your product or service.
*   **Call to Action** (`<Call to Action>`, text, required): Specify the action you want viewers to take.
*   **Logo or Image Upload** (`<Logo or Image Upload>`, text, required): If applicable, upload your logo or ad image.
*   **Special Requirements** (`<Special Requirements>`, text, required): If applicable, describe any special requirements for your ad.
*   **Purchase Authorization** (`<Purchase Authorization>`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are authorized to make this advertising purchase.
