# todays-mortgage-rates - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their interest in today's mortgage rates. Users can provide general information about themselves, their property address, and their mortgage preferences. The form is intended to be used by individuals seeking mortgage rates information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required and optional fields as applicable.
*   Review each field's explanation to ensure you understand what information is being requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **lead-form-info** (Text) (required: false)
This field is for general information about your inquiry. Please provide a brief description of your interest in today's mortgage rates.

### 2. **lead-form-properties** (Text) (required: false)
This field is for any additional information you'd like to share about your property.

### 3. **property-address** (Text) (required: false)
This field is for your property's address. Please include the full address, including street number and city.

### 4. **mortgage-preferences** (Select One) (required: false)
Please select one of the following options:
*   Option 1
*   Option 2
*   Option 3

### 5. **income** (Number) (required: false)
This field is for your income. Please enter a valid number.

### 6. **income-amount** (Number) (required: false)
This field is for your income amount. Please enter a valid number.

### 7. **phone-number** (Text) (required: false)
This field is for your phone number. Please enter your 10-digit phone number.

### 8. **email** (Email) (required: false)
This field is for your email address. Please enter a valid email address.

## Tips
*   Please fill in as much information as possible to help us better understand your inquiry.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
