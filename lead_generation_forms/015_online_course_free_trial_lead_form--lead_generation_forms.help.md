# Online Course Free Trial Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your interests and needs in our online courses. By filling out this form, you'll be eligible for a free trial of our online course, and we'll be in touch with you to follow up on your progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your valid Email Address.
4. Enter your Phone Number (optional).
5. Enter your Date of Birth (optional).
6. Select the course that interests you.
7. Select your preferred course language (select all that apply).
8. Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up (email or phone).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address that you check frequently. We will use this to contact you about your free trial.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Date of Birth (date)**: If you're interested in our courses for children or young adults, please enter your date of birth.
* **What course are you interested in? (select_one)**: Choose one course that interests you from the list:
	+ Art
	+ Business
	+ Computer Science
	+ Education
	+ Health
	+ History
	+ Math
	+ Science
	+ Social Science
	+ Technology
* **What language is your preferred course language? (select_multiple)**: Select all languages you are proficient in or prefer to learn:
	+ English
	+ French
	+ Spanish
	+ Chinese
	+ Japanese
	+ Korean
	+ German
	+ Portuguese
	+ Italian
	+ Dutch
* **How would you like to be contacted? (select_one)**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted for follow-up:
	+ Yes, email me for follow-up
	+ No follow-up
	+ Yes, phone me for follow-up

## Tips
- Be honest when filling out this form to ensure we can contact you correctly.
- If you're not interested in any of our courses, please don't fill out this form.
- This form is for lead generation purposes only, and your information will be handled accordingly.
