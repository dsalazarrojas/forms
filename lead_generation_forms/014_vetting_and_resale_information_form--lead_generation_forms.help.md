# vetting_and_resale_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for resale purposes. The goal is to gather details about the customer, their contact information, and product-related data for sales and customer interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide resale information about the product.
2. Enter customer details, including their contact information.
3. Add shipping and payment details.
4. Add any additional notes or comments.
5. Review and sign the terms and conditions.
6. Sign and date the sale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Resale Information** (`resale_information`, text, optional): Enter details about the resale, such as any relevant information or notes.
- **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter the customer's contact information, such as phone number and email.
- **Resale Address** (`resale_address`, text, optional): Enter the resale address, if applicable.
- **Product Information** (`product_info`, text, optional): Enter details about the product, including its description and condition.
- **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): Enter shipping details, such as delivery address and estimated delivery time.
- **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter payment details, such as payment method and amount.
- **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the sale.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the sale.
- **Resale Terms** (`resale_terms`, text, optional): Enter any resale terms or conditions specific to this sale.
- **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, optional): The customer must sign this form to confirm the sale.
- **Sales Person Signature** (`sales_person_signature`, text, optional): The sales person must sign this form to confirm the sale.
- **Sale Date** (`sale_date`, text, optional): Enter the date of the sale.
- **Sale Time** (`sale_time`, text, optional): Enter the time of the sale.
- **Resale Price** (`resale_price`, text, optional): Enter the price of the resale.
- **Resale Currency** (`resale_currency`, text, optional): Enter the currency of the resale.
- **Sale Value** (`sale_value`, text, optional): Enter the value of the sale.
- **Product Price** (`product_price`, text, optional): Enter the price of the product.
- **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the product.
- **Product Condition** (`product_condition`, text, optional): Enter the condition of the product.
- **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, optional): Upload an image of the product.
- **Product Video** (`product_video`, text, optional): Upload a video of the product.
- **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, text, optional): Enter the customer's ID.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, text, optional): Enter the customer's email.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields and review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Ensure that the customer's contact information and product details are accurate and complete.
- Attach a clear and legible signature to the form.
- Review the resale terms and conditions before signing the form.
- Use the correct currency and price for the sale.
- If you need any assistance, please contact form@gic.mx.
