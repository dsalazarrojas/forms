# Insurance Selling Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Selling Lead Form is designed to collect information from customers interested in purchasing insurance. This form helps us understand their preferences and needs to provide personalized assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully and thoroughly to ensure that we capture accurate information about you.
- Each section of the form is designed to collect specific details about your insurance preferences.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask, and we'll be happy to help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you for further assistance.
- **Phone**: Provide your phone number so we can reach out to you for more information.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
- **Day**: Enter the day of your birth.
- **Month**: Enter the month of your birth.
- **Year**: Enter the year of your birth.
- **Insurance Type**: Select the type of insurance you're interested in purchasing (Life, Health, Auto, or Home).
- **Insurance Purpose**: Choose the purpose of the insurance you're interested in (Medical, Financial, Retirement, or Other).
- **Agent**: Enter the name of your insurance agent, if you have one.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
- We'll use the information provided to contact you and assist you with your insurance needs.
