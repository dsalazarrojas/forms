# Social Media Post Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Post Request Form is used to request social media posts for internal stakeholders. This form captures information about the requestor, their manager, team, and any partner(s) involved in the request.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Make sure to check with your manager to see if a similar request has been submitted recently.
- Submit the form once you have filled it out completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is listed on your business card.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is listed on your business card.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your official phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company (if applicable).
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the name of your department (if applicable).
* **Position** (`position`, text, optional): Enter your position or job title (if applicable).
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your role (if applicable).
* **Manager** (`manager`, select_one, optional): Select whether you have a manager involved in this request.
* **Manager's Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter your manager's phone number (if applicable).
* **Manager's Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address (if applicable).
* **Team** (`team`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether your team is involved in this request.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, text, optional): Enter the names of team members involved (if applicable).
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, select_one, optional): Select whether your team lead is involved in this request.
* **Team Lead's Phone** (`team_lead_phone`, text, optional): Enter your team lead's phone number (if applicable).
* **Team Lead's Email** (`team_lead_email`, email, optional): Enter your team lead's email address (if applicable).
* **Partner** (`partner`, text, optional): Enter a partner's name (if applicable).
* **Partner's Phone** (`partner_phone`, text, optional): Enter a partner's phone number (if applicable).
* **Partner's Email** (`partner_email`, email, optional): Enter a partner's email address (if applicable).
* **Partner's Department** (`partner_department`, text, optional): Enter a partner's department (if applicable).
* **Partner's Title** (`partner_title`, text, optional): Enter a partner's title (if applicable).
* **Request** (`request`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your request.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date you need the social media post by.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, time, required): Enter the time you need the social media post by.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your request.
