# Smart Pricing Software Trial Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential customers who are interested in trialing our Smart Pricing Software. This form helps us understand their needs and preferences, which will be used to provide a tailored trial experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" field.
2. Type in the name of your company in the "Company" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure it is a valid email address.
4. Type in your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose your job title/role in the "Job Title/Company Role" field. You can select from the provided options.
6. Check the industries that apply to you in the "Industry" field. You can select multiple options.
7. Enter the number of employees in your company in the "Number of Employees" field.
8. Enter your location in the "Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): A field for entering your first name.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): A field for entering your company name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): A field for entering a valid email address. This will be used to communicate with you about your trial experience.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): A field for entering your phone number.
* **Job Title/Company Role** (`role`, select_one, required: false): A field to select your job title or company role. This will help us understand your position within your company.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all industries that apply to you. This will help us tailor your trial experience to your specific needs.
* **Number of Employees** (`company_size`, number, required: false): Enter the number of employees in your company.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): A field for entering your location.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter valid and accurate information.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will get back to you soon to discuss your trial experience.

Note: The form is not required for the application. It's just a helpful tool for us to understand your needs better.
