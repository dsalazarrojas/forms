# Advertising Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advertising Survey Form is a tool used to gather information about your advertising needs and preferences. It helps us understand your business goals, target audience, and current advertising strategies, which enables us to provide more effective support and services to help you achieve your marketing objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions truthfully and accurately to ensure we get a clear understanding of your needs.
2. Select the options that best match your business and advertising situation.
3. For text fields, provide detailed and descriptive answers.
4. For number fields, choose the rating that best reflects your opinion.
5. For multiple-choice fields, select all the options that apply to your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, select_one, required): Select your primary industry sector (e.g., Technology, Healthcare, Retail, etc.).
* **Annual Marketing Budget Range** (`marketing_budget_range`, select_one, required): Select your approximate annual marketing budget (e.g., Under $10,000, $10,000 - $50,000, etc.).
* **Primary Advertising Goals** (`primary_advertising_goals`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the advertising objectives that apply to your business (e.g., Brand awareness, Lead generation, Sales conversion, etc.).
* **Current Advertising Channels Used** (`current_channels_used`, select_multiple, required): Select all the channels you are currently using for advertising (e.g., Social media, Search engines, Television, etc.).
* **Satisfaction Rating with Current Advertising Efforts** (`satisfaction_rating`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with your current advertising performance (e.g., 1-5).
* **Biggest Advertising Challenge** (`biggest_challenge`, text, required): Describe your biggest challenge in advertising.
* **Target Audience Age Range** (`target_audience_age`, text, optional): Specify the primary age range of your target audience.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like us to contact you for follow-up (e.g., Email, Phone call, etc.).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your business email address for follow-up communication.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any other information about your advertising needs and preferences.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
* We will use the information gathered from this form to provide you with tailored support and services.
