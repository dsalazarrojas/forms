# Digital Culture Consulting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Culture Consulting Request Form is designed to collect information from users for digital culture consulting services. This form ensures that users provide necessary contact information and agree to the terms of the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill out the form in a new tab or window.
2. Provide contact information, including your name, company name, position, email, and phone number.
3. Select the desired option for the agreement term.
4. Enter any comments or questions you have in the comments field.
5. Select the desired date and time for the agreement term (if applicable).
6. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter your company name as it appears on your business cards or website.
* **Position**: Enter your current role within your company.
* **Email**: Enter your company email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your company phone number.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have about the service.
* **Agreement**: Choose one of the provided agreement options to confirm your acceptance of the service terms.
* **Agreement Date**: Enter the date you agree to the terms. (Note: This field is not required but provides a way to record the agreement date.)
* **Agreement Time**: Enter the time you agree to the terms. (Note: This field is not required but provides a way to record the agreement time.)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked with *) to ensure timely processing of your request.
* Enter accurate and valid contact information to ensure that we can reach you efficiently.
* Take a moment to read the agreement terms before selecting an option, as they may impact your use of the service.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the service, enter them in the comments field.
