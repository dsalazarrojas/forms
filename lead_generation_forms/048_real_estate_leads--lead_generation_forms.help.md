# Real Estate Leads - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and manage information about real estate leads, which are potential customers for a real estate agency or broker. The form helps to centralize and organize data about leads, making it easier to track and follow up with them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, start by filling out each page in order from top to bottom. Make sure to select the relevant options for each field, such as the lead source, lead type, and lead status. For fields that require text, enter the relevant information in the text box. For date and time fields, use the format specified. For fields that require selection, choose the relevant option from the dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead source**: Enter the source of the lead, such as a referral or an online advertisement.
* **Lead type**: Select whether the lead is a buyer or seller.
* **Lead category**: Select the category of the lead, such as residential or commercial.
* **Lead status**: Select the current status of the lead, such as new or converted.
* **Lead ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the lead.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company associated with the lead.
* **Agent**: Enter the name of the agent associated with the lead.
* **Lead Date**: Enter the date of the lead in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Lead Time**: Enter the time of the lead in the format HH:MM:SS AM/PM.
* **Lead Note**: Enter any additional notes about the lead.
* **Lead Email**: Enter the email address of the lead.
* **Lead Phone**: Enter the phone number of the lead.
* **Lead Company Address**: Enter the address of the lead company.
* **Agent Company Address**: Enter the address of the agent company.
* **Lead City**: Enter the city of the lead location.
* **Lead State**: Select the state where the lead is located.
* **Lead Zip**: Enter the postal code of the lead location.
* **Lead Country**: Select the country where the lead is located.
* **Agent Zip**: Enter the postal code of the agent company.
* **Lead Country Code**: Enter the country code for the lead location.
* **Lead Agent Phone**: Enter the phone number of the agent.
* **Agent Email**: Enter the email address of the agent.
* **Lead Lead Type**: Select whether the lead is a lead or not.
* **Lead Lead Category**: Select the category of the lead.
* **Lead Lead Source**: Enter the source of the lead.
* **Agent Zip**: Not required, but enter the agent's zip code for consistency.
* **Agent Lead Country Code**: Not required, but enter the agent's country code for consistency.
* **Agent Lead Phone**: Enter the phone number of the agent.

Note: Some fields may not be required, but it's always a good idea to fill them out for consistency and accuracy.
