# Off Grid Energy Solutions Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to inquire about off grid energy solutions for their homes or businesses. It helps energy companies understand users' energy needs and preferences to provide tailored solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
3. **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
4. **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
5. **Project Type**: Choose the type of project you are interested in:
	* Solar
	* Wind
	* Hydro
	* Geothermal
	* Other (please specify)
6. **System Size**: If applicable, enter the size of the energy system you are interested in.
7. **Location**: Choose the location of your project:
	* On Grid
	* Off Grid
	* On/Off Grid
8. **Energy Needs**: Briefly describe your energy needs and requirements.
9. **Description**: Provide a detailed description of your project and its goals.
10. **Budget**: Enter your estimated budget for the project.
11. **Date**: Enter the date you would like to start your project.
12. **Time**: Enter the time of day you would like to start your project.
13. **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your project.
14. **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: A text field to enter your last name.
* **Email**: A text field to enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter your contact phone number.
* **Project Type**: A select menu to choose the type of project you are interested in.
* **System Size**: A number field to enter the size of the energy system you are interested in, if applicable.
* **Location**: A select menu to choose the location of your project.
* **Energy Needs**: A text field to describe your energy needs and requirements.
* **Description**: A text field to provide a detailed description of your project and its goals.
* **Budget**: A number field to enter your estimated budget for the project.
* **Date**: A date field to enter the date you would like to start your project.
* **Time**: A time field to enter the time of day you would like to start your project.
* **Note**: A text field to add any additional notes or comments about your project.
* **Additional Info**: A text field to provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your project.
