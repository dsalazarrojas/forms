# MICE News Subscription Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals interested in receiving news updates about specific topics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subscriber Details**: Please enter your name and any other relevant contact information. This will help us understand who is interested in our news.
* **Topic Interests**: Please select the topics you are interested in by checking the boxes next to each option (e.g., "Yes" for "Marketing and Advertising"). This will help us tailor our news updates to your interests.
* **Email Consent**: Please choose how you would like to receive news updates on the selected topics. You can choose to receive news on one or multiple topics.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive news updates and notifications.
