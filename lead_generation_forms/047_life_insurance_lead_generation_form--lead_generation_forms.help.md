# Life Insurance Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Insurance Lead Generation Form is designed to collect information from potential customers about their life insurance needs and preferences. This information will be used to contact you for further follow-up and to provide personalized insurance quotes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
2. Indicate your interest level for different types of insurance coverage.
3. Choose how you would like to be contacted.
4. Enter your email address or phone number for us to contact you.
5. Fill out any additional information that you feel is relevant to your insurance needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): Enter a short description of yourself.
* **Age** (`customer_age`, number, required: false): Enter your age.
* **Insurance Coverage** (`insurance_coverage`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all the types of insurance coverage you are interested in (Health, Dental, Vision, Life, Disability).
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Mail).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Insurance Purpose** (`insurance_purpose`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all the types of insurance purposes that apply to you (Term Life, Whole Life, Variable Universal Life, Universal Life, Index).
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose where you heard about us (Facebook, Twitter, Google Ads, LinkedIn, Referral).
* **Insurance Agent** (`insurance_agent`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your insurance agent.
* **Insurance Agency** (`insurance_agency`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your insurance agency.
* **Customer Interest** (`customer_interest`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose how interested you are in life insurance (Very interested, Somewhat interested, Not interested).
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments about your insurance needs.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, required: false): Enter your signature.
* **Customer IP** (`customer_ip`, text, required: false): Enter your IP address.
* **Customer Device** (`customer_device`, text, required: false): Enter your device type.
* **Customer Browser** (`customer_browser`, text, required: false): Enter your browser type.
* **Customer OS** (`customer_os`, text, required: false): Enter your operating system.
* **Customer Country** (`customer_country`, text, required: false): Enter your country.
* **Customer Region** (`customer_region`, text, required: false): Enter your region.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, required: false): Enter your city.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, text, required: false): Enter your state.
* **Customer Zip** (`customer_zip`, text, required: false): Enter your zip code.
* **Customer Postal Code** (`customer_postal_code`, text, required: false): Enter your postal code.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required: false): Enter your address.
* **Customer Company** (`customer_company`, text, required: false): Enter your company name.
