# Career Mentorship Waitlist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Career Mentorship Waitlist form is used to connect individuals seeking mentorship with potential mentors who can offer guidance, advice, and support in their career development. By filling out this form, you'll be matched with mentors who share similar experiences and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you.
2. Select your preferred method of contact (Email, Phone, Text message, or No preference).
3. Fill in your current role or title, the industry you work in, and the number of years you've been in your profession.
4. Choose whether you're looking for a mentor, wanting to be a mentor, or both.
5. Select all areas you'd like to focus on for mentorship (e.g., Career advancement and promotion, Skill development, etc.).
6. If applicable, specify your ideal mentor profile, mentoring experience, and skills you can share.
7. Choose your preferred mentorship format (e.g., One-on-one meetings, Phone calls or video calls, etc.).
8. Indicate your availability in terms of the number of hours you can commit to mentorship each month.
9. Consent to the terms and conditions of the mentorship program and agree to receive communications about it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Name** (1): Enter your full name for contact purposes.
*   **Email Address** (2): Provide the email address we'll use to contact you.
*   **Phone Number** (3): Include your area code for easier contact.
*   **Preferred Method of Contact** (4): Select how you'd like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Text message, or No preference).
*   **Current Role or Title** (5): Enter your current job or position.
*   **Current Industry** (6): Specify the industry you're in.
*   **Years of Professional Experience** (7): Enter the number of years you've been in your profession.
*   **Are You Looking For a Mentor or to Be a Mentor** (8): Choose whether you're looking for a mentor, wanting to be a mentor, or both.
*   **Your Career Goals** (9): Describe what you want to achieve in your career.
*   **What Areas Do You Want Mentorship In?** (10): Select all areas you'd like to focus on (e.g., Career advancement and promotion, Skill development, etc.).
*   **Ideal Mentor Profile** (11): Specify the qualities or experience you're looking for in a mentor.
*   **If Mentoring Others - Your Mentoring Experience** (12): Describe any past mentoring experience you have.
*   **If Mentoring Others - Skills You Can Teach** (13): Specify the expertise you can share with mentees.
*   **If Mentoring Others - Preferred Mentee Level** (14): Choose the type of individuals you'd like to mentor (Students or early-career professionals, Mid-level professionals, etc.).
*   **How Much Time Can You Commit Monthly?** (15): Select the number of hours you can dedicate to mentorship each month.
*   **Preferred Mentorship Format** (16): Choose your preferred format for mentorship (e.g., One-on-one meetings, Phone calls or video calls, etc.).
*   **Your Location** (17): Enter your city and state or country (optional).
*   **Open to Virtual Mentorship?** (18): Indicate whether you're open to virtual mentorship or prefer in-person meetings.
*   **Specific Challenges You're Facing** (19): Describe any challenges you're currently facing in your career.
*   **Languages Spoken** (20): List any languages you're proficient in.
*   **LinkedIn Profile URL** (21): Provide your LinkedIn profile URL (optional).
*   **Additional Information** (22): Share any other relevant details about yourself.
*   **I Agree to the Mentorship Program Terms and Conditions** (23): Confirm you've read and agree to the terms and conditions.
*   **I Consent to Receive Communications About the Mentorship Program** (24): Agree to receive updates and information about the mentorship program.
