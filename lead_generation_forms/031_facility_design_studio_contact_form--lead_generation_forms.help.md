# Facility Design Studio Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Design Studio Contact Form is a tool designed for users to submit information about potential projects or inquiries to the design team. This form helps the team in generating new leads and facilitating the project generation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Begin by selecting the "Lead Generation" option from the dropdown menu for "lead_generation".
2.  Enter your name in the "name" field.
3.  Provide your email address in the "email" field, ensuring it is in the correct format.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5.  Specify the name of the facility in the "facility" field.
6.  Enter a brief description of your project or inquiry in the "project description" field (optional).
7.  Select the status of your project from the dropdown menu for "project status".
8.  Specify the source of your lead from the dropdown menu for "lead source".
9.  Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead Generation** (`lead_generation`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Lead Generation" if you are looking for a new project or "Lead Qualification" if you are looking to further qualify an existing lead.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name to be associated with the lead.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to be contacted regarding the project.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Facility** (`facility`, text, required): Enter the name of the facility you are contacting us about.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your project or inquiry.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the status of your project (New, In Progress, Complete).
* **Project Inquiries** (`project_inquiries`, text, required): Enter a description of your project or inquiry.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, select_one, required): Specify the source of your lead (Social Media, Email, Word of Mouth).
* **Lead Generation** (`lead_generation`, select_one, optional): Select whether you are looking for a new lead generation or lead qualification.
* **Project** (`project`, text, required): Enter a description of your project or inquiry.
* **Lead Generation** (`lead_generation`, select_one, optional): Select "Lead Generation" if you are looking to generate new leads or "Lead Qualification" if you are looking to further qualify an existing lead.
