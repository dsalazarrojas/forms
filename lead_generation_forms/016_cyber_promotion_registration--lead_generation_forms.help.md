# Cyber Promotion Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a cyber promotion. If you have already registered for a promotion, please do not submit this form again.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully before submitting it.
2. Make sure you have a legitimate business need for the promotion.
3. Fill out all required fields (marked as required) to ensure successful submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name**: This field is for your personal name.
* Please enter your first name as it appears on your ID or common usage.

### Last Name
* **Last Name**: This field is for your last name.
* Please enter your last name as it appears on your ID or common usage.

### Email
* **Email**: This field is for your work or personal email address.
* Please enter a valid email address that you regularly use.

### Phone
* **Phone**: This field is for your work or personal phone number.
* Please enter a valid phone number that you regularly use.

### Country
* **Country**: This field is for your country of residence.
* Select your country of residence from the list of available options.

### State
* **State**: This field is for your state or region of residence.
* Select your state or region from the list of available options.

### City
* **City**: This field is for your city or city of residence.
* Please enter the city where you live or work.

### Zip
* **Zip**: This field is for your zip code or postal code.
* Please enter a valid zip code that corresponds to your city or region.

### Message
* **Message**: This field is for any additional information you'd like to provide.
* Please keep your message brief and clear.

### Promotion
* **Promotion**: This field is for selecting which promotion to register for.
* Select all applicable promotions from the list of available options.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your submission carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the designated support team.
* If you have already registered for a promotion, please do not submit this form again.
