# Summer Camp Registration Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Registration Lead Form is designed to collect information for summer camp registration. It gathers details about the summer camp and allows for follow-up contacts and session follow-ups. This form is intended for individuals or organizations interested in registering for the summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and requirements.
2. Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, paying attention to any required fields.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer Camp Information (lead_form_1)** (`title`, `required`, `text`):
	+ This field is used to collect the name or title of the summer camp.
	+ Please provide a brief title for the summer camp.
* **Contact Follow Ups (contact_form_2)** (`label`, `hint`, `type`, `required`, `text`):
	+ This field is for collecting additional contact follow-up information.
	+ Although not strictly required, providing your contact information will help us get back to you for further communication.
* **Session Follow Up (session_form_3)** (`label`, `hint`, `type`, `required`, `text`):
	+ This field is for collecting session follow-up details.
	+ Please provide any additional session follow-up information as needed.

## Tips

* Ensure you complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us for further guidance.
