# Flooring Promotion Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flooring Promotion Registration form is used to collect information from customers about their flooring needs and preferences. This data will help sales teams or customer service representatives follow up with customers and track potential leads.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Enter your Promotion Details, including any relevant information about your flooring needs or preferences.
2.  Fill out your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
3.  Select your contact method for follow-up (Instore, Online, Phone, or Email).
4.  Indicate your current Lead Status (Pending, Qualified, or Converted).
5.  Enter your Lead Value, if applicable.
6.  Fill out your Customer Name, Phone Number, and Email Address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Promotion Details** (`promotion_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your flooring promotion needs or preferences.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, phone number, and email address to help us follow up with you.
* **Lead Status** (`lead_status`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Pending, Qualified, or Converted).
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (Instore, Online, Phone, or Email).
* **Lead Value** (`lead_value`, number, optional): Enter your lead value, if applicable.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for our records.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for our records.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for our records.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter your address for our records.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out as much information as possible to ensure we can follow up with you effectively.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask, and we'll do our best to assist you.
* If you need to update any information, please let us know, and we'll update your record accordingly.
