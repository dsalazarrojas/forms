# Unlimited Internet Plan Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Unlimited Internet Plan Registration form is designed to collect essential information from users to successfully register for the unlimited internet plan. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to help you provide accurate and complete details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your User Details, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2.  Choose a suitable plan from the options provided.
3.  Select your preferred installation location and date.
4.  Specify your preferred contact method.
5.  Confirm that you have read and agree to the Terms and Conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, required): Enter your name, which will be used to identify you as a registered user.
* **Choose a Plan** (`plan_choice`, select_one, required): Select one of the available plans that suits your needs. This will determine your internet plan's speed and other specifications.
* **Installation Preferences** (`installation_preferences`, text, optional): If you have any specific preferences for your internet installation, such as a preferred technician or time slot, please mention them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and confirmation purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number, which will be used for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your physical address, which will be used for installation and billing purposes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, please enter them here.
* **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, date, required): Select the date for your internet installation.
* **Installation Time** (`installation_time`, time, required): Select the preferred time for your internet installation.
* **Installation Location** (`installation_location`, select_multiple, required): Choose where you would like to have your internet installed - Home, Work, or Other.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred method of contact - Phone, Email, Text Messaging, or In Person.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or additional comments, please enter them here.
* **Confirm** (`confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the Terms and Conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the Terms and Conditions.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid any errors or incomplete submissions.
