# No Cost Solar Program. - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers interested in the No Cost Solar program. It will help us follow up with customers and provide them with more information about the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Enter your name in the **What is Your Name** field.
*   Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
*   Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
*   Select the method that describes how you found us in the **How Did You Find Us** field.
*   Choose the address fields that match your location in the **Address** field.
*   Enter your comments about the No Cost Solar program in the **No Cost Solar Program** field.
*   If you are interested in other No Cost Solar programs, enter them in the **No Cost Solar Program 2** field.
*   Enter your long-term savings expectations in the **Long Term Savings** field.
*   If you are interested in other long-term savings, enter them in the **Long Term Savings 2** field.
*   Enter any information about going solar in the **Going Solar** field.
*   Select the incentives that you are interested in from the **No Cost Solar Incentives** field.
*   Enter any additional comments or information about the No Cost Solar program in the **No Cost Solar Program 3** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **What is Your Name** (`form_1_start`, text, required: false): Enter your name for us to contact you.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
*   **How Did You Find Us** (`no_cost_solar_inquiry`, select_one, required: false): Select the method that describes how you found us:
    *   no cost solar Facebook
    *   no cost solar Instagram
    *   no cost solar email
    *   no cost solar YouTube
*   **Address** (`address`, select_multiple, required: false):
    *   Select your street address
    *   Select your city
    *   Select your state
    *   Select your zip code
*   **No Cost Solar Program** (`no_cost_solar_program`, text, required: false): Enter any comments about the No Cost Solar program.
*   **No Cost Solar Program 2** (`no_cost_solar_program_2`, text, required: false): If you are interested in other No Cost Solar programs, enter them here.
*   **Long Term Savings** (`long_term_savings`, text, required: false): Enter your long-term savings expectations.
*   **Long Term Savings 2** (`long_term_savings_2`, text, required: false): If you are interested in other long-term savings, enter them here.
*   **Going Solar** (`going_solar`, text, required: false): Enter any information about going solar.
*   **No Cost Solar Incentives** (`no_cost_solar_incentives`, select_multiple, required: false):
    *   Cash back incentives
    *   Tax credit
    *   Rebates
    *   No other
*   **No Cost Solar Program 3** (`no_cost_solar_program_3`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information about the No Cost Solar program.
