# Payment Processing Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a referral for payment processing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the referral source from the provided options.
2. Enter the partner or contact name, if applicable.
3. Enter the contact email and phone number, if applicable.
4. Choose the payment type (Credit Card, ACH, or Wire Transfer).
5. Enter the referral date.
6. Choose who submitted the referral (User or Partner).
7. Select the submission method (Web, Mobile, or Email).
8. Add any additional comments, if necessary.
9. Fill in the form ID, if applicable.
10. Select the submission method and time, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referral Source**: Choose the source of the referral from the options provided (Partner, Agency, Social Media, Email Marketing, or Other).
* **Partner Name**: Enter the name of the partner or contact person, if applicable.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person, if applicable.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person, if applicable.
* **Payment Type**: Choose the payment type from the options provided (Credit Card, ACH, or Wire Transfer).
* **Referral Date**: Enter the date the referral was received.
* **Submitted By**: Choose who submitted the referral from the options provided (User or Partner).
* **Submission Method**: Select the method through which the referral was submitted (Web, Mobile, or Email).
* **Submission Notes**: Add any additional comments about the submission, if necessary.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date the referral was submitted, if applicable.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time the referral was submitted, if applicable.
* **Form ID**: Enter the ID number for the form, if applicable.

Note: Fields with no options or hints are not relevant for explanation.
