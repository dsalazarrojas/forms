# School Event Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Event Lead Generation Form is a tool used to collect and store information about upcoming school events. This form helps to gather essential details about each event, making it easier to plan and coordinate activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event Name** in the text field provided.
2. Provide a brief **Event Details** in the text area.
3. Enter the **School Email** address for the event contact person.
4. Enter the **School Phone** number for the event contact person.
5. Select the **Event Date** of the event.
6. Choose the **Event Time** of the event.
7. Enter the **Event Location** where the event will take place.
8. Provide the **Event URL** if applicable.
9. Select the **Follow up Date** for communication (if not already set).
10. Choose the **Follow up Method** for communication (phone, email, or text).
11. Select the **Assigned Tool** for communication (chatjimmy or another tool).
12. Add any additional **Notes** about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required/optional): A brief title for the event.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, note, required/optional): A short description of the event.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required/optional): The contact email address of the school.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, required/optional): The contact phone number of the school.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): The date the event will take place.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/optional): The time the event will occur.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required/optional): The venue or location of the event.
* **Event URL** (`event_url`, text, required/optional): A URL to access event information or registration.
* **Follow up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, required/optional): The date to follow up on the event (if not already set).
* **Follow up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select_one, required/optional): Choose a method to follow up on the event (phone, email, or text).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the tool assigned for communication (chatjimmy or another tool).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Additional information or notes about the event.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of your submission before completing the form.
* If you need to follow up on an event, ensure you have selected the correct follow-up method and date.
* If you have already generated a lead for this event, do not complete the form again.
