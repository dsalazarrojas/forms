# join_the_solopreneur_waitlist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a simple sign-up form designed to capture basic information from potential customers who are interested in joining the Solopreneur waitlist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Join The Solopreneur Waitlist" button to access the form.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Select your preferred options from the dropdown menus where applicable.
4. Provide any additional information you'd like to include in the "Message" field.
5. Review your submission carefully before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, \*): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, \*): Enter a valid email address we can use to keep in touch with you.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you have a business or organization, please enter it here.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, optional): Select any or all of the roles that apply to you.
	+ 'Yes' and 'No' options are available for selection.
* **I agree to be contacted** (`agree`, select_one, \*): Check the box to indicate you are okay with us reaching out to you.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): If you have any additional information or comments, feel free to include them in this field.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to include your phone number, please enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure we can contact you effectively.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please include them in the "Message" field.
