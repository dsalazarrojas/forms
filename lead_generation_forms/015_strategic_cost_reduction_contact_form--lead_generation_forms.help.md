# Strategic Cost Reduction Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather contact information from individuals interested in exploring strategic cost reduction opportunities within our organization. Its purpose is to facilitate communication with our teams and allow us to provide tailored support and solutions to your cost reduction needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. Enter your **Name** in the text field provided.
3. Input your **Email** address to ensure we can respond to your inquiry.
4. Select the **Department** you are interested in working with.
5. Enter your **Job Title** to help us understand your role and context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name so we can address you personally.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Provide your email address for us to reply to your inquiry.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Select the department you are interested in exploring strategic cost reduction opportunities with.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title to help us understand your role and context.

## Tips
- Please ensure your email address is correctly formatted for a prompt response.
- If you are experiencing technical difficulties, please contact form@gic.mx for support.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, feel free to ask.
