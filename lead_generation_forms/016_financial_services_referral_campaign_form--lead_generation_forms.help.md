# Financial_Services_Referral_Campaign_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from customers for referral campaigns related to financial services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

1. Fill out the form carefully, making sure to provide accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field below for specific guidance on how to fill it out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact** (<name>Contact</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: true/false): Enter the name of the contact person you are referring.
* **Contact Details** (<name>contact_details</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: false): Briefly describe your relationship with the contact person.
* **Business Type** (<name>business</name>, <type>select_one</type>, required/optional: true): Select the type of business you are in:
	+ Financial Advisor
	+ Financial Planner
	+ Financial Manager
* **Referral Reason** (<name>referral_reason</name>, <type>select_multiple</type>, required/optional: true): Select the reasons for the referral:
	+ Financial Needs
	+ Financial Goals
	+ Financial Planning
	+ Other
* **Date of Birth** (<name>date_of_birth</name>, <type>date</type>, required/optional: false): Enter the date of birth of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Email** (<name>email</name>, <type>email</type>, required/optional: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (<name>phone</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Address** (<name>address</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: false): Enter the address of the contact person (if applicable).

## Tips
- Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information before submitting it.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the administrator for guidance.
