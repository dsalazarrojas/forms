# Marine Boat Show Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marine Boat Show Lead Generation Form is a tool to capture contact information and preferences of visitors for future follow-up and communication activities related to the show.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your preferred contact method from the "Contact Method" dropdown list.
5. Select your preferred follow-up contact methods from the "Follow up Method" dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name
• **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like to be contacted.

### Email
• **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your primary email address where you can be contacted.

### Phone
• **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your primary phone number where you can be contacted.

### Contact Method
• **Contact Method** (Select One, Required): Select your preferred contact method by choosing from Email, Phone, or SMS.

### Follow up Method
• **Follow up Method** (Select Multiple, Required): Select your preferred follow-up contact methods by choosing from Email, Phone, or SMS.
