# Sustainable Products Lead Generation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
<thinking>
This form is used for lead generation purposes, allowing you to collect information about potential leads for sustainable products. The form is designed to help you identify potential leads and tailor your follow-up approach to their specific needs and preferences.
</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Provide your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Enter your physical Address in the Address field.
5. If you're filling out this form in a professional context, provide your Company name in the Company Name field.
6. Indicate your Job Title and Department in the Job Title and Department fields.
7. Select the appropriate options for your Company Size and Industry from the dropdown menus.
8. If applicable, choose your Job Function from the dropdown menu.
9. Enter your annual Sales figures in the Annual Sales field.
10. Enter your annual Expenses figures in the Annual Expenses field.
11. Provide information on your Revenue Growth.
12. Choose one or multiple Lead Sources from the dropdown menu.
13. Indicate your Follow-up Preference for this form submission.
14. Select a preferred time for Contact (Time of Contact) and Preferred Contact Time.
15. Choose your preferred Day of the Week for Contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (id: 2)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your business card or introduction.
* **Last Name (id: 3)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your business card or introduction.
* **Email (id: 4)**: Enter your work email address or preferred contact email.
* **Phone (id: 5)**: Enter your work phone number or mobile phone number.
* **Address (id: 6)**: Enter your office or work location address.
* **Company (id: 7)**: Enter your company name if you're filling out this form in a professional context.
* **Job Title (id: 8)**: Enter your job title or position within the company.
* **Department (id: 9)**: Enter your department within the company (e.g., sales, marketing, etc.).
* **Company Name (id: 10)**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Job Function (id: 11)**: Select the job function that best describes your role within the company.
* **Company Size (id: 12)**: Choose the number of employees within your company.
* **Industry (id: 13)**: Select the industry your company operates in.
* **Annual Sales (id: 15)**: Enter your company's annual sales figures (optional).
* **Annual Expenses (id: 16)**: Enter your company's annual expenses figures (optional).
* **Revenue Growth (id: 17)**: Enter your company's revenue growth rate (optional).
* **Lead Source (id: 18)**: Select the sources from which you've heard about our sustainable products (e.g., Social Media, Trade Show, etc.).
* **Follow-up Preference (id: 19)**: Indicate your preferred time frame for follow-up contact.
* **Time of Contact (id: 20)**: Select a time of day you're most available to be contacted.
* **Preferred Contact Time (id: 21)**: Choose your preferred time for contact (e.g., morning, afternoon, etc.).
* **Preferred Day of Week (id: 22)**: Select a day of the week you're most available for contact.
* **Email Open Time (id: 23)**: Select a time of day when you usually check your email.
* **First Response Time (id: 24)**: Choose a time of day when you respond to new emails.
