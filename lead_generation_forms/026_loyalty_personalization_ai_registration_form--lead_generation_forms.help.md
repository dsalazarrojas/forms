# Loyalty Personalization AI Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your company's loyalty personalization needs and interests, in order to provide a custom-tailored solution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your company's details in the Company Details section.
2. Select your company's primary goal from the Goal dropdown menu.
3. Add any additional information you'd like to share about your company in the Additional Info section.
4. Indicate which SaaS provider you are using for your marketing automation.
5. Select the size of your company from the Company Size dropdown menu.
6. Optionally, you can add any notes about your company's needs or interests.
7. If you have any additional AI programs you are interested in, select them from the list.
8. Finally, confirm that you consent to have your data used for the purpose of registering for the Loyalty Personalization AI.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Details** (`company_details`, text, required): This section collects your company's basic details. Please fill out your company's name, and any other relevant information you'd like to share.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): This field is used to fill in your company's name, if it's not already included in the Company Details section.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): If you have a website for your company, please enter the URL here.
* **Goal** (`goal`, select_one, optional): Select your company's primary goal from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand your company's needs better.
* **AI Interest** (`ai_interest`, text, optional): If you have any specific AI interests or requirements, please list them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your company's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted, please enter your company's phone number.
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, optional): Please confirm that you consent to have your data used for the purpose of registering for the Loyalty Personalization AI.
* **AI Programs** (`ai_programs`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional AI programs you are interested in, in addition to the Loyalty Personalization AI.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): If you have any additional information about your company or your needs, please add it here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you'd like to add any notes about your company or your needs, please add them here.
* **SaaS Provider** (`saas_provider`, select_one, optional): Please select which SaaS provider you are using for your marketing automation.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Select the size of your company from the dropdown menu.
