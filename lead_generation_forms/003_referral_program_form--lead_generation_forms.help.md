# Referral Program Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients and referrals for the referral program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2.  Enter the client's email address in the "Email" field.
3.  Enter the client's client number (if applicable) in the "Client Number" field.
4.  Enter the referral's name in the "Referral Name" field.
5.  Enter the referral's email address in the "Referral Email" field.
6.  Enter the referral's client number (if applicable) in the "Referral Number" field.
7.  Enter any additional comments about the referral in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Client Name (client_name)**: A field to enter the client's name.
*   **Email (email)**: A field to enter the client's email address.
*   **Client Number (client_number)**: A field to enter the client's client number (if applicable).
*   **Referral Name (referral_name)**: A field to enter the referral's name.
*   **Referral Email (referral_email)**: A field to enter the referral's email address.
*   **Referral Number (referral_number)**: A field to enter the referral's client number (if applicable).
*   **Comments (comments)**: A field to enter any additional comments about the referral.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill in all required fields (though none are currently required).
*   Double-check the information you enter for accuracy and completeness.
*   Use the comments field to provide any additional context or information about the referral.
