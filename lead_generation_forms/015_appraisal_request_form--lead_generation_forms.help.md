# Appraisal Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Appraisal Request Form is a tool used by clients or their representatives to request an appraisal for a specific property. It collects essential information to help the appraiser understand the client's needs and provide an accurate appraisal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your full name and email address. These are required fields to ensure we can contact you about your appraisal request.
2. Enter your phone number, which will be used for further communication.
3. Specify the type of property (residential, commercial, industrial, agricultural, or mixed use) and its address.
4. Select the type of appraisal you need (full market, limited, desktop, insurance valuation, or tax assessment).
5. If urgent, choose the desired deadline for the appraisal (immediate, within one or two weeks, or flexible).
6. (Optional) Add any additional context or information about the appraisal's purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as the person or representative making the request.
* **Email Address** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and communication about your appraisal request.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number for further communication regarding your request.
* **Company or Organization** (`company`, Text, Optional): If you represent a company or organization, provide its name.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, Text, Required): Enter the exact address of the property for which you are requesting an appraisal.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, Select One, Required): Choose the type of property (residential, commercial, industrial, agricultural, or mixed use).
* **Current Use** (`property_use`, Text, Optional): Describe the current use of the property (e.g., occupied by tenants, vacant, under construction, etc.).
* **Type of Appraisal Needed** (`appraisal_type`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all applicable types of appraisals you require (full market, limited, desktop, insurance valuation, or tax assessment).
* **How Urgent is this Request** (`urgency`, Select One, Optional): Indicate how quickly you need the appraisal (immediate, within one or two weeks, or flexible).
* **Purpose of Appraisal** (`purpose`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional context about the appraisal's purpose.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_notes`, Text, Optional): Share any other relevant details about your request.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information, especially about the property address and type of appraisal needed.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, contact us for assistance.
* We will review your request and follow up with a confirmation and updates about your appraisal process.
