# Business Model Innovation Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help potential business partners provide information about themselves and their interests in collaborating with a business model innovation partner. The goal is to gather data that will aid in assessing their capabilities, expectations, and requirements for a successful partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal and company information as instructed.
2. Be as detailed as possible when answering the questions, especially when describing your value proposition and partnership proposal.
3. Select the options that best fit your company's characteristics and interests.
4. Review your submission before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or position in the company.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Select the number of employees in your company.
	+ 1 to 10
	+ 11 to 50
	+ 51 to 200
	+ 201 to 500
	+ 500 plus
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select your primary industry.
	+ Technology
	+ Healthcare
	+ Finance
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Retail
	+ Education
	+ Energy
	+ Professional services
	+ Other
* **Partnership Type Interested In** (`partnership_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of partnership you're interested in.
	+ Strategic alliance
	+ Joint venture
	+ Technology partnership
	+ Distribution partnership
	+ Co-development
	+ Licensing agreement
	+ Reseller agreement
	+ Other
* **Partnership Goals** (`partnership_goals`, select_multiple, required): Select the goals you hope to achieve through partnership.
	+ Market expansion
	+ Technology access
	+ Resource sharing
	+ Cost reduction
	+ Innovation acceleration
	+ Revenue growth
	+ Brand enhancement
	+ Risk sharing
* **Value Proposition** (`value_proposition`, text, required): Describe the value you bring to a partnership.
* **Target Markets** (`target_markets`, select_multiple, required): Select the markets you're targeting.
	+ North America
	+ Europe
	+ Asia Pacific
	+ Latin America
	+ Middle East
	+ Africa
	+ Global
* **Innovation Focus Areas** (`innovation_focus`, select_multiple, required): Select all areas of focus.
	+ Digital transformation
	+ Sustainability
	+ Customer experience
	+ Operational efficiency
	+ Product development
	+ Business model redesign
	+ Platform development
	+ Data analytics
* **Partnership Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, required): Select when you want to establish the partnership.
	+ Immediate
	+ Within 1 month
	+ Within 3 months
	+ Within 6 months
	+ Exploring options
* **Investment Capacity** (`investment_capacity`, select_one, optional): Select your investment capacity.
	+ Under $50K
	+ $50K to $100K
	+ $100K to $500K
	+ $500K to $1M
	+ Over $1M
	+ Non-financial resources
* **Decision Making Authority** (`decision_making_authority`, select_one, required): Select if you're the decision maker.
	+ Yes - final decision maker
	+ Yes - recommend to leadership
	+ No - influence decisions
	+ No - gathering information
* **Previous Partnership Experience** (`previous_partnerships`, select_one, required): Select your previous partnership experience.
	+ Yes - multiple successful
	+ Yes - some experience
	+ Yes - limited experience
	+ No - first partnership
* **Partnership Proposal Summary** (`proposal_summary`, text, required): Briefly describe your partnership proposal.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Select the preferred contact method.
	+ Email
	+ Phone call
	+ Video conference
	+ In-person meeting
* **Availability for Discussion** (`availability`, text, optional): Provide your availability for discussion.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any other relevant details.

Tips:

* Be honest and thorough when answering the questions.
* Make sure to review and double-check your submission before submitting.
