# customer_sign_up_quote_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `customer_sign_up_quote_request` form is designed to collect information for customers who are interested in our services. Please note that this form will only allow one submission per company name and contact number to prevent over-submission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information and quote request.
2. Review and ensure accuracy of all fields.
3. Submit the form for review and follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Sign Up** (`customer_sign_up`, text, required/optional): Enter your company or organization name.
* **Quote Request** (`quote_request`, text, required/optional): Describe your service request.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your company name for our records.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Provide your contact number so we can reach you for further inquiry.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Be as detailed as possible with your quote request.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact us directly for support.

Note: If a field is marked as "optional", it's not required but it's still beneficial for us to have it for further processing.
