# free_lead_generation_form_cleaning_coach_brand_builder - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help cleaning services businesses collect lead information from potential customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "what_is_your_name" field with your name.
2. Enter your email address in the "email_address" field.
3. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "phone_number" field.
4. Enter your business name in the "business_name" field.
5. Enter your business email address in the "business_email" field.
6. If your business has a phone number, please enter it in the "business_phone" field.
7. Select the lead source that best describes how you found our business in the "lead_source" field.
8. If you would like, you can also select "Home Cleaning" or "Office Cleaning" as options for the type of business you have in the "business_type" field.
9. In the "lead_message" field, enter a note about yourself or your business.
10. Select one of the three options in the "lead_message_2" field to indicate your interest in learning more about our services.
11. You may also enter additional notes about yourself or your business in the "lead_message_3" field.
12. Finally, enter any additional information you would like to share about your business in the "free_lead_generation_form_cleaning_coach_brand_builder_form_1", "free_lead_generation_form_cleaning_coach_brand_builder_form_2", and "free_lead_generation_form_cleaning_coach_brand_builder_form_3" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What Is Your Name** (text): Enter your name so we can get in touch with you.
* **Email Address** (email): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (text): If you have a phone number, enter it so we can reach you.
* **Business Name** (text): Enter the name of your business.
* **Email Address** (email): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (text): If your business has a phone number, enter it.
* **Lead Source** (text): Select the lead source that best describes how you found our business.
* **Business Type** (text): Select the type of business you have (e.g. "Home Cleaning" or "Office Cleaning").
* **Lead Message** (note): Enter any additional notes about yourself or your business.
* **Lead Message 2** (select one): Select one of the three options to indicate your interest in learning more about our services.
* **Lead Message 3** (note): Enter any additional notes about yourself or your business.
* **Free Lead Generation Form Cleaning Coach Brand Builder Form 1, 2, 3** (text): Enter any additional information you would like to share about your business.
