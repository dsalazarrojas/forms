# property-climate-consultant-contact-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for property owners to provide information about their properties and schedule a consultation with a climate consultant. The form aims to gather essential details to facilitate effective consultation and communication with the consultant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide your prophet info in the "prophets info" field.
3. Share your property details and info in the "property info" field.
4. Let us know your availability for consultation by selecting the relevant options.
5. Specify your consultation preferences and schedule a consultation date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Info (Property info)** (`Property info`, text, required): Please share any relevant information about your property.
* **Prophet Info (prophets info)** (`prophets info`, text, optional): This field is not mandatory, but if you want to share some info about prophets, you can do so.
* **Consultant Availability (consultant-availability)** (`Yes', 'No', select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' if you are available for consultation, 'No' otherwise.
* **Consultation Preferences (consultation-preferences)** (`Yes', 'No', select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted for the consultation, 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Schedule Consultation (schedule-consultation)** (`date`, date, required): Choose a date and time for your consultation.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to allow the consultant to contact you.
* **Phone (phone)** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is not mandatory, but if you want to provide your phone number for the consultant to reach you, you can do so.
* **Message (message)** (`message`, note, required): Share any important notes or comments with the consultant.
* **Property-Climate-Consultant (property-climate-consultant)** (`Yes', 'No', select_multiple, optional): This field is not mandatory, but if you want to indicate that you have a climate consultant associated with your property, you can select 'Yes'.
* **Property Owner (property-owner)** (`Yes', 'No', select_one, required): Confirm that you are the property owner.
* **Property Management Team (property-management-team)** (`Yes', 'No', select_one, required): Confirm if you have a property management team.
* **Property Details (property-details)** (`property_details`, text, required): Share details about your property.
* **Consultant Notes (consultant-notes)** (`property_climate_consultant_notes`, note, required): Leave any notes or comments for the consultant.
