# Seasonal Landscaping Services Lead Generation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for potential customers to provide their contact information and service needs to generate leads for seasonal landscaping services. It is designed to help our team get in touch with you and provide you with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields with your information. Make sure to select all relevant services and dates for the services you need. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can include them in the "Notes" field. After filling out the form, click "Submit" to send it to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead Generation Form** (`lead_generation_form`, text, optional): This field is not a field to fill in, but rather the title of this form. You can ignore it.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options: First Name, Last Name, Email, or Phone. This will help our team get in touch with you.
* **Service Needed** (`service_needed`, select_multiple, optional): Select all services you need from the options: Lawn Maintenance, Yard Work, Tree Trimming, or Garden Design. This will help us know what services you are interested in.
* **Service Dates** (`service_dates`, date, optional): Enter the dates you need our services. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, time, optional): Select the time you would like to be contacted for follow up. This can be any time of day.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options: Residential, Commercial, or Other. This will help us know the type of property you need services for.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your service needs or property.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Select "Submit" to send this form to our team, or select "Other" if you have alternative contact information.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, you can contact us at your preferred contact information.
* We will follow up with you shortly after you submit this form to discuss your service needs.
