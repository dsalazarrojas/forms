# Interest In Upcoming Demos - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interest In Upcoming Demos form is a way for users to express their interest in attending an upcoming demo, allowing us to gauge interest and collect relevant contact information from potential attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief request or comment about the demo you're interested in.
2. Fill out the required fields:
   - **first name**: Enter your first name.
   - **last name**: Enter your last name.
3. Enter your **Phone** (optional, but recommended for follow-up).
4. Enter your **Email** (optional, but recommended for communication).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Demo Request**: 
  * (Text input)
  * Description: A brief request or comment about the demo you're interested in.
  * This field is required.
  * Please type in the relevant information about the demo you're interested in attending.
- **first name**: 
  * (Text input)
  * Description: Enter your first name.
  * This field is required.
  * Please enter your real first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **last name**: 
  * (Text input)
  * Description: Enter your last name.
  * This field is required.
  * Please enter your real last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Phone**: 
  * (Text input)
  * Description: Your phone number.
  * This field is optional, but recommended for follow-up.
  * Please enter your phone number without any special characters.
- **Email**: 
  * (Email input)
  * Description: Your email address.
  * This field is optional, but recommended for communication.
  * Please enter a valid email address.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form only once per individual.
- Ensure the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
- We will use this information to send you updates and follow-up information about the demo.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact info].
