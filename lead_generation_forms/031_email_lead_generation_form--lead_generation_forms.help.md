# Email Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about job seekers and their preferences for new job opportunities in various industries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **First name**, **Last name**, and **Email** to get started.
2. Select your **Industry**, **Company size**, **Company type**, and **Job level** to indicate your job preferences.
3. Choose your **Job status**, **Job type**, and **Job role** to provide more details about your job search goals.
4. Select your **Job seniority**, **Job title level**, and **Job department** to narrow down your preferences.
5. Finally, review your answers carefully and submit the form to share your information with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name to help us understand who you are.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name to help us understand who you are.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Company**: If you're currently employed, enter your company name.
* **Job title**: Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Industry**: Select the industry you're interested in working in (Software, Finance, Marketing, Technology, or Healthcare).
* **Company size**: Select the size of your current or desired company (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **Company type**: Select the type of company you're interested in working in (not applicable in this form).
* **Job level**: Select the level of your current or desired job (Junior, Senior, Manager, Director, or CEO).
* **Job status**: Select your current or desired job status (Active, Inactive).
* **Job type**: Select the type of job you're interested in (Full time, Part time, Contract, or Freelance).
* **Job role**: Enter the role you're interested in or currently hold.
* **Job function**: Enter the function you're interested in or currently perform.
* **Job seniority**: Select the level of seniority you're interested in (Junior, Senior, Manager, Director, or CEO).
* **Job department**: Select the department you're interested in or currently work in (Sales, Marketing, Product, Engineering, or Finance).
* **Job title level**: Select the level of your desired job title (Entry level, Mid level, or Senior level).
* **Job seniority**: Select the level of seniority you're interested in (Junior, Senior, Manager, Director, or CEO).
* **Job department**: Select the department you're interested in or currently work in (Sales, Marketing, Product, Engineering, or Finance).

Please note that this form is only intended to be filled out once per person. If you've already completed this form, there's no need to do so again.
