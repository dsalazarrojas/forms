# Trade Show Lead Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for new leads generated during trade shows.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Event Name, which is the name of the trade show where the lead was generated.
2. Enter the Company Name, which is the name of the company that interacted with you at the trade show.
3. Enter the Job Title, which is the job title of the person you interacted with.
4. Enter the Email, which is the contact email of the person or company.
5. Enter the Phone number, which is the contact phone number of the person or company.
6. Enter the Company Name again, as this is the parent company (or the company that the person interacts with).
7. Enter the Event Date, which is the date of the trade show where the lead was generated.
8. Enter the Event Time, which is the start time of the trade show.
9. Enter the Assigned Tool, which is the tool or team responsible for handling this lead.
10. Enter any Note about the interaction or conversation that took place.
11. Enter the Lead Status, which indicates the progress of this lead.
12. Enter the Follow-up Date, which is the date to follow up with this lead.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (event_name, text, optional): The name of the trade show where the lead was generated.
* **Company Name** (company_name, text, optional): The name of the company that interacted with you at the trade show.
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, optional): The job title of the person you interacted with.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): The contact email of the person or company.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): The contact phone number of the person or company.
* **Company** (company, text, optional): The parent company (or the company that the person interacts with).
* **Event Date** (event_date, date, optional): The date of the trade show where the lead was generated.
* **Event Time** (event_time, time, optional): The start time of the trade show.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool, text, optional): The tool or team responsible for handling this lead.
* **Note** (note, note, optional): Any note about the interaction or conversation that took place.
* **Lead Status** (lead_status, text, optional): Indicates the progress of this lead.
* **Follow-up Date** (follow_up_date, date, optional): The date to follow up with this lead.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of your input to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult your team for guidance.
* This form is not intended for leads already handled via another form or manual process.
