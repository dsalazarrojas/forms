# Fancy Envelope Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fancy Envelope Contact Form is designed to collect detailed contact information and categorize leads or inquiries based on specific criteria. It's likely used for Business-to-Business (B2B) or Business-to-Consumer (B2C) lead generation or inquiry purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the following information:
   - **First name**: Your personal name.
   - **Last name**: Your surname.
   - **Company**: If you're representing a company, provide its name. Otherwise, leave this field empty.
   - **Email**: Your email address.
   - **Phone**: Your phone number.
   - **Message**: A brief description of your inquiry or question.
   - **Address**: Your physical address, if applicable.
   - **Website**: Your personal or company website, if available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (id: 1)
*   **First name** (text, required: false): Enter your personal first name.

### Last Name (id: 2)
*   **Last name** (text, required: true): Enter your personal last name.

### Company (id: 3)
*   **Company** (text, required: false): If you're representing a company, enter its name. Otherwise, leave this field empty.

### Email (id: 4)
*   **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address.

### Phone (id: 5)
*   **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number.

### Message (id: 6)
*   **Message** (text, required: true): Briefly describe your inquiry or question.

### Address (id: 7)
*   **Address** (text, required: false): If you'd like to provide your physical address, enter it here. Otherwise, leave it empty.

### Website (id: 8)
*   **Website** (text, required: false): If you have a personal or company website, enter the URL here. Otherwise, leave it empty.

### Job Title (id: 9)
*   **Job title** (text, required: false): Enter your job title or position, if applicable.

### Company Website (id: 10)
*   **Company website** (text, required: false): If you're representing a company, enter its website URL. Otherwise, leave this field empty.

### Lead Source (id: 11)
*   **Lead source** (select one, required: true): Select one of the lead sources provided:
    +   Lead Generation
    +   Web Scraping
    +   Referral

### Company Size (id: 12)
*   **Company size** (select multiple, required: true): Select all that apply:
    +   Large
    +   Medium
    +   Small
    +   Extra Small
