# Gardening Workshop Series Lead Generation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gardening Workshop Series Lead Generation form is used to collect information from potential attendees of the gardening workshop series.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to provide a valid email address.
3. Optionally, provide additional information such as workshop type, location, date, and time if you have it ready.
4. Provide any additional comments or workshop details if you have them.
5. Review your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Series Lead Generation** (<code>workshop_series_lead_generation_form</code>, <code>text</code>, <code>optional</code>): This field is not actually a field in the form, it's just the form title. However, it's a good starting point.
* **Name** (<code>name</code>, <code>text</code>, <code>optional</code>): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email** (<code>email</code>, <code>email</code>, <code>optional</code>): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (<code>phone_number</code>, <code>text</code>, <code>optional</code>): This is the phone number we can use to contact you.
* **Workshop Type** (<code>workshop_type</code>, <code>text</code>, <code>optional</code>): If you have any specific workshop type in mind, feel free to let us know.
* **Location** (<code>location</code>, <code>text</code>, <code>optional</code>): Where would you like to attend the workshop? Let us know the location.
* **Date** (<code>date</code>, <code>date</code>, <code>optional</code>): When would you like to attend the workshop? This can be in the future or past.
* **Time** (<code>time</code>, <code>time</code>, <code>optional</code>): If you have a specific time in mind, we will try our best to accommodate it.
* **Lead Source** (<code>lead_source</code>, <code>text</code>, <code>optional</code>): This is a good opportunity to let us know how you found us.
* **Comments** (<code>comments</code>, <code>note</code>, <code>optional</code>): Do you have any specific comments or workshop details you'd like to share?
* **Workshop Details** (<code>workshop_details</code>, <code>text</code>, <code>optional</code>): Feel free to provide any additional workshop details that might help us better understand your request.

## Tips
* Please make sure you're not duplicating a lead we already have.
* We will use your email address to contact you about this lead.
* If you have any issues while completing the form, do not hesitate to contact us.
