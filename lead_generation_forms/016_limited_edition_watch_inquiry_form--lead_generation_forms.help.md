# Limited Edition Watch Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users who are interested in purchasing limited edition watches.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information (name, email, phone number, and watch model).
2. Choose your preferred colors for the watch.
3. Add any additional notes or comments about your inquiry.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Watch Model** (`watch_model`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the limited edition watch model you are interested in from the provided options.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
- **Name** (`name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your name so we can contact you.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
  - Note: Make sure to enter a valid email address to ensure we can reach out to you about your inquiry.
- **Colors** (`colors`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select up to 3 colors that you prefer for your limited edition watch.
  - Note: You can choose from Black, White, or Green.
- **Additional Notes** (`note`, `note`, `required`): Add any additional comments or questions you have about the watch.
  - Note: This field is optional but helps us understand your specific needs.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, `optional`): Enter a specific date for your interest in the watch.
  - Note: This field is optional and only needed if you have a specific date in mind for the watch.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, `optional`): Enter a specific time for your interest in the watch.
  - Note: This field is optional and only needed if you have a specific time in mind for the watch.
