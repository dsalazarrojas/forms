# Customer Value Strategy Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to gather information about a customer's lead generation process. It helps our team understand their current strategies and goals, allowing us to provide more tailored support and services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Be sure to provide details about your lead generation process and goals.
3. Use the select_one and select_multiple options to select only the options that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Form** (`contact_form`, text, optional): This is a simple text box where you can provide any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **What is your company's current lead generation process?** (`lead_qualification`, text, optional): Describe your current lead generation process in a few sentences.
* **Can you describe your current lead generation process?** (`lead_qualification_details`, text, optional): Provide a more detailed description of your current lead generation process.
* **What is your lead generation process?** (`lead_generation_process`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options that best describes your lead generation process.
* **How often do you generate leads?** (`lead_qualification_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the frequencies that apply to your lead generation process (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **What lead method do you typically use?** (`lead_qualification_method`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options that best describes your lead method.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person filling out this form.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Company** (`contact_company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact company.
* **What is your lead generation goal?** (`lead_generation_goal`, text, optional): Briefly describe your lead generation goal.
* **What is your lead generation budget?** (`lead_generation_budget`, number, optional): Enter a number representing your lead generation budget.
* **What lead generation channels do you use?** (`lead_generation_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the channels that apply to your lead generation process (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **What measures do you use to evaluate lead generation?** (`lead_generation_measures`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the measures that apply to your lead generation process (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Date of lead generation** (`date_of_lead_generation`, date, optional): Enter the date of your lead generation activity.
* **Time of lead generation** (`date_of_lead_generation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your lead generation activity.
* **Status of lead generation** (`lead_generation_status`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options that best describes the current status of your lead generation process.
* **Contact Comment** (`contact_comment`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or questions you may have.
