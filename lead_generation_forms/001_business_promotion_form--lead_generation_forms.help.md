# Business Promotion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Promotion Form is designed to collect customer information and preferences for promotional purposes. This form helps us understand your shopping habits, interests, and how you heard about our business. Your input will aid us in providing better customer experiences and improve our marketing efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information: Provide your full name, email address, and phone number to help us reach out to you.
2. Share your business details: Enter the name of your store or business and its location to help us understand your context.
3. Select how you heard about us: Choose from Social Media, Friend or Family, Web Search, or Other to help us track our marketing efforts.
4. Let us know what you're interested in: Select all that apply from our product categories to help us tailor our promotions.
5. Tell us about your shopping habits: Choose your estimated shopping frequency to help us improve our services.
6. Add any additional feedback: Share your thoughts on how we can improve, if you'd like.
7. Join our newsletter (optional): Check Yes, subscribe me to receive updates on new products and promotions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your complete name for our records.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Your business or personal email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number with area code to help us reach you.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Provide the name of your store or business.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required): Enter your business location.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Provide the name of the city where your business is located.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, text, required): Enter the state or province where your business is located.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_postal_code`, text, required): Enter the ZIP or postal code of your business location.
* **How did you hear about us** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, required): Select from Social Media, Friend or Family, Web Search, or Other to help us track our marketing efforts.
* **What products are you interested in** (`interested_products`, select_multiple, required): Choose all that apply from our product categories to help us tailor our promotions.
* **How often do you shop with us** (`purchase_frequency`, select_one, required): Select your estimated shopping frequency to help us improve our services.
* **Additional Feedback or Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, optional): Share any thoughts on how we can improve.
* **Subscribe to our newsletter** (`subscribe_newsletter`, select_one, optional): Choose Yes, subscribe me to receive updates on new products and promotions.
