# Join A Regional Communication Group - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to join a regional communication group for networking and communication purposes. It helps the group administrators to manage membership and keep the group organized.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First name
2. Fill in your Last name
3. Enter your Email address
4. Enter your Phone number
5. Select your preferred Platform preferences (e.g. Amazon, LinkedIn, Facebook, Twitter)
6. Select your Preferred Regions (e.g. East, West, North, South)
7. Enter a Message (optional)
8. Review and acknowledge the Terms of Service

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Fill in your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last name
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Fill in your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Region
* **Region** (`region`, `text`, **optional**): If you want to join a specific regional group, fill in the name of your region (e.g. East, West, North, South). If left blank, we will try to match you to the best fitting regional group based on your location.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter a valid email address for communication and updates.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number for group administrators to contact you if needed.

### Platform preferences
* **Platform preferences** (`platform_preferences`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select which platforms you prefer to use for communication within the group. This will help us tailor our communication to your preferences.

### Preferred Regions
* **Preferred Regions** (`preferred_regions`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select your preferred region(s) to be part of the group. This will help us direct relevant communication and updates to you.

### Message
* **Message** (`message`, `note`, **optional**): If you have a specific message or introduction you'd like to share with the group, enter it here.

### Terms of Service
* **Terms of Service** (`terms_of_service`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Review and acknowledge that you have read and agree to the terms of service. Select "Yes" to proceed.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, **not required**): This button will submit your application once you've filled out all the required fields.
