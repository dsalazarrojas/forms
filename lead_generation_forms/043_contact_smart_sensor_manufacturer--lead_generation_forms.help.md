# Contact Smart Sensor Manufacturer - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request product information or technical support from the Smart Sensor Manufacturer. It is designed to provide the necessary details for the sales team to better understand your product interests and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Product interest" option that best describes your product inquiry.
2. Provide your company's name, name of the contact person, and business email address.
3. Enter a phone number (optional) for urgent communications.
4. Specify your product interests and technical requirements.
5. Estimate the quantity of units required (if applicable).
6. Choose your expected timeframe to purchase.
7. Add any additional notes or details to help the sales team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company name** (`Company name`, `text`, required): Enter your organization's name to help us understand who is requesting the information.
- **Contact name** (`Contact name`, `text`, required): Provide the name of the person responsible for the inquiry to enable us to contact them directly.
- **Email address** (`Email address`, `email`, required): Enter your business email address for technical follow-up and sales communication.
- **Phone number** (`Phone number`, `text`, optional): If you prefer to be contacted directly by phone, enter your phone number.
- **Product interest** (`Product interest`, `text`, required): Select one of the options to describe the sensor family or product you are interested in.
- **Technical requirements** (`Technical requirements`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your technical requirements or specifications.
- **Estimated quantity** (`Estimated quantity`, `number`, optional): Estimate the approximate number of units you will need.
- **Expected timeframe to purchase** (`Expected timeframe to purchase`, `select_one`, required): Choose when you plan to place an order:
	+ Immediately
	+ 1-3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ 6+ months
- **Additional notes** (`Additional notes`, `text`, optional): Share any other relevant details to help our sales team better understand your needs.
