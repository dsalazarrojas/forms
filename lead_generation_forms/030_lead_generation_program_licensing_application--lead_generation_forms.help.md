# Lead Generation Program Licensing Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead Generation Program Licensing Application form is designed for companies to apply for the Lead Generation Program Licensing Agreement. This program enables businesses to leverage lead generation strategies and tools to drive growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your company name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your business in the "Business Details" field.
3. Enter your business address in the "Business Address" field.
4. If you have any supporting documentation for your application, upload it in the "Supporting Documentation" field.
5. Enter the name of the contact person in the "Contact Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name (1)**: Enter the full name of your company as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Lead Generation Program Licensing Application (2)**: This field is for the name of the form, which is automatically filled in.
* **Business Details (3)**: Please provide a brief description of your business, including information about your products or services offered.
* **Business Address (4)**: Enter the physical address of your company, including the street, city, and country.
* **Supporting Documentation (5)**: If you have any supporting documentation for your application, such as financial statements or business licenses, upload it in this field.
* **Contact Name (6)**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for this application.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your application is considered for the program.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
