# Ebook Download Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ebook Download Form is used to collect information from users who want to download an e-book.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
4. Select your preferred e-book title.
5. Choose whether you agree to the terms and conditions, terms of service, and privacy policy.
6. Click the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ebook Download Form** (`ebook_download_form`): This is the title of the form.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name to associate it with the e-book download request.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address to receive the e-book download link.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number (if applicable) to help us contact you.
* **Ebook Title** (`ebook_title`): Select your preferred e-book title from the options provided.
* **Ebook Description** (`ebook_description`): This field is not required but can be used to provide additional information about the e-book.
* **Ebook Price** (`ebook_price`): This field is not required but can be used to provide the price of the e-book.
* **Download Link** (`download_link`): This field is not required but can be used to provide a download link to the e-book.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`): You must agree to the terms and conditions to proceed with the e-book download request.
* **Terms of Service** (`terms_of_service`): You must agree to the terms of service to proceed with the e-book download request.
* **Privacy Policy** (`privacy_policy`): You must agree to the privacy policy to proceed with the e-book download request.
* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`): Click this button to submit your e-book download request.
* **Form Cancel** (`form_cancel`): Not used in this form.
* **Form Reset** (`form_reset`): Not used in this form.
* **Form Help** (`form_help`): Not used in this form.
* **Ebook File** (`ebook_file`): This field is not required but can be used to provide additional information about the e-book.
* **Form Message** (`form_message`): This field is not required but can be used to provide additional information about the e-book download request.
* **Terms of Service** (`terms_of_service`): This field is not required but can be used to provide additional information about the terms of service.
* **Privacy Policy** (`privacy_policy`): This field is not required but can be used to provide additional information about the privacy policy.
* **Form Note** (`form_note`): Not used in this form.
* **Error Message** (`error_message`): Not used in this form.
* **Success Message** (`success_message`): Not used in this form.
* **Form Redirect** (`form_redirect`): Not used in this form.
* **Form Redirect URL** (`form_redirect_url`): Not used in this form.
* **Form Redirect Label** (`form_redirect_label`): Not used in this form.
* **Form Redirect URL2** (`form_redirect_url2`): Not used in this form.
* **Form Redirect Label3** (`form_redirect_label3`): Not used in this form.
