# Treadmill Product Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your fitness goals and preferences to provide you with the most suitable treadmill options. It will help us to understand your current fitness level, how you prefer to be contacted, and what type of service you're interested in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose your current fitness level by selecting an option from the dropdown list.
2. Select how you prefer to be contacted for further assistance.
3. Choose how you found us (e.g. social media, Google search, friend referral).
4. Select the type of service you are interested in.
5. If you have any specific questions or requests, you can provide more information in the text field "What type of service do you need?".
6. Provide your name, email, and phone number so we can contact you for further assistance.
7. If you have a business or studio, please provide its name.
