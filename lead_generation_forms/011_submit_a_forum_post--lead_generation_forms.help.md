# Submit A Forum Post - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user-generated content for a specific category, allowing users to engage in discussions and share their expertise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best represents the topic of your submission.
2. Add relevant tags to help others quickly identify the content's context.
3. If you have attachments or supporting documents, upload them using the "Attachments" field.
4. Optionally, indicate your consent to follow the submission rules by selecting "I Agree" or "I Disagree" from the "Rule Consent" field.
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit your post.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Forum Post** (field_1, text, optional):
	+ This field is the main body of your post, where you can describe your thoughts, ideas, or expertise.
	+ Enter your submission in plain text, and it will be displayed as is.
* **Category** (forum_category, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This field allows you to select one or multiple categories related to your submission.
	+ Choose the category that best represents the topic of your content.
* **Tags** (forum_tags, text, optional):
	+ Add relevant tags to help others quickly identify the context of your submission.
	+ Separate multiple tags with commas, and use a maximum of 5 tags.
* **Attachments** (file_uploads, text, optional):
	+ If you have supporting documents or files to attach to your submission, upload them here.
	+ Ensure files are in a format that can be read by the platform.
* **Rule Consent** (rule_consent, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate your consent to follow the submission rules by selecting "I Agree" or "I Disagree".
	+ Note that not following the rules may result in your submission being removed.
