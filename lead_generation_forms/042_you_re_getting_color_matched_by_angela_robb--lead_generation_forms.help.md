# You’re Getting Color Matched By Angela Robb - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the purpose of matching users with a suitable color match provided by Angela Robb.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Company in the "Company" field.
4. Enter your Job Title in the "Job Title" field.
5. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. This is the only required field.
6. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification or as you prefer it to be seen.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification or as you prefer it to be seen.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company you work for or the organization you represent.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your job or position within your organization.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address so that we can contact you about your color match.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact or follow up if needed.
