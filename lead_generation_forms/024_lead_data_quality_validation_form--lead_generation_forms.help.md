# lead-data-quality-validation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lead data quality validation form is a tool used to verify and improve the accuracy of lead contact information. This form is used to collect accurate and complete data from customers or users who are interested in generating leads for business development purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
2. Please ensure that the information you provide is correct and up-to-date.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **lead-generation-form** (1): 
    This is the main lead generation form. Please fill out this field accurately and completely.
* **contact-name** (2): 
    Please fill out this field with your name as it appears on official documents or business cards.
* **email** (3): 
    This is the email address associated with your business or organization. Please use the format "example@domain.com".
* **phone** (4): 
    Please fill out your primary phone number for verification purposes.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you're filling out this form accurately and completely, as incorrect or missing information can result in delays and inaccuracies in our lead generation process.
- If you have any questions or concerns about this form or the lead generation process, please do not hesitate to contact us.
