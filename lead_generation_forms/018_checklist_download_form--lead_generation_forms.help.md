# Checklist Download Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Checklist Download Form is used to collect contact information and preferences from users who want to download specific business checklists. This form helps us understand your needs and provide relevant resources to support your business goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information:
	* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is known to you.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is known to you.
	* **Business Email Address**: Enter the email address where you want to receive the selected checklist.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your business phone number (optional).
2. Select your company details:
	* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
	* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or role within the company.
3. Choose your industry and company size:
	* **Industry**: Select the category that best describes your business (e.g. Technology, Finance / Banking).
	* **Company Size**: Choose the number of employees in your company.
4. Select the checklist you want to download:
	* Choose the specific checklist that you are interested in downloading (e.g. Business Startup Essential Checklist).
5. Share your biggest business challenge:
	* Select one or more of the challenges that you are currently facing (e.g. Scaling Operations, Finding Talent).
6. Subscribe to our newsletter (optional):
	* If you want to receive our weekly business insights newsletter, select "Yes, Sign Me Up". Otherwise, choose "No, Just the Download".
7. Confirm your agreement to our Privacy Policy:
	* Select "I Agree" to confirm that you have read and accept our Privacy Policy. If you have concerns, select "Other".
