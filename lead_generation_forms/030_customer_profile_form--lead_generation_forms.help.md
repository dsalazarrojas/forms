# Customer Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects customer information to populate the customer database for targeted communication and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as `required` in the form).
2. Choose the correct options for the select fields (e.g., job title, role in the company, company size, industry).
3. Enter your first and last name, company name, email, phone number, address, city, state, and zip code accurately.
4. Choose whether you would like to connect on LinkedIn.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your business address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city name.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state/region name.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **What is your job title** (`customer_profile_form_3`, select_one, optional): Choose your job title (CEO, Manager, Employee, Other).
* **What is your role in the company** (`customer_profile_form_4`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the roles you hold in the company (Founder, Manager, Employee, Other).
* **What is the company size?** (`customer_profile_form_5`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the company size (1-9, 10-49, 50-99, 100-499, 500-999, 1000+).
* **What industry are you in?** (`customer_profile_form_6`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the industry your company operates in (Finance, Technology, Retail, Food and Beverage, Healthcare, Energy, Other).
* **Would you like to connect on LinkedIn?** (`customer_profile_form_17`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you would like to connect on LinkedIn (Yes, No).

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately.
- Choose the correct options for select fields based on your actual job title, role in the company, and industry.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the customer support team.
- If you would like to add or update information, please refer to the customer profile form again to make changes.
