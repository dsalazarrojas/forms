# Fitness Business Sales Readiness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help fitness businesses assess their sales readiness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your franchisee name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Enter your business name.
5. Enter your business location.
6. Select your business hours of operation.
7. Select your business type (e.g. gym, studio, online).
8. Enter your annual revenue.
9. Enter the name of your financial institution (e.g. bank).
10. Select your financial status (e.g. profitable, not profitable).
11. Select your financial investors.
12. Enter your business system (e.g. software, hardware).
13. Enter your hardware equipment.
14. Select your software and equipment.
15. Select your training programs and services.
16. Select your support options (e.g. online, phone).
17. Enter your owner's name.
18. Enter your owner's contact information.
19. Select your owner's experience level.
20. Select your owner's availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Franchisee Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the franchisee or business owner.
* **Email** (2, email, optional): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone** (3, text, optional): Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Name** (4, text, optional): Enter your business name.
* **Business Location** (5, text, optional): Enter your business location (street, city, state).
* **Business Hours** (6, select_one, optional): Select the hours of operation for your business.
* **Business Type** (7, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of business you have (e.g. gym, studio, online).
* **Annual Revenue** (8, number, optional): Enter your annual revenue.
* **Financial Institution** (9, text, optional): Enter the name of your financial institution (e.g. bank).
* **Financial Status** (10, select_one, optional): Select your financial status (e.g. profitable, not profitable).
* **Financial Investors** (11, select_multiple, optional): Select your financial investors.
* **System** (12, text, optional): Enter your business system (e.g. software, hardware).
* **Hardware** (13, text, optional): Enter your hardware equipment.
* **Software** (14, select_multiple, optional): Select your software and equipment.
* **Training** (15, select_multiple, optional): Select your training programs and services.
* **Support** (16, select_multiple, optional): Select your support options (e.g. online, phone).
* **Owner Name** (17, text, optional): Enter your owner's name.
* **Owner Contact Info** (18, text, optional): Enter your owner's contact information.
* **Owner Experience** (19, select_one, optional): Select your owner's experience level.
* **Owner Availability** (20, select_multiple, optional): Select your owner's availability.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields marked as required are completed before submitting the form. The information collected will help identify areas that need improvement to get your business sales ready.
