# Skincare New Customer Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare New Customer Consultation Form is designed to collect information from new customers to provide personalized skincare consultations and treatment plans. This form is meant to be used by customers to submit their details and preferences for a consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "New Customer" section with your name and any relevant information.
2. Provide your "Health Information" in the designated field.
3. Describe your "Skin Details" in the space provided.
4. Share your "Skincare Goals" in the space provided.
5. Specify your "Availability" for consultations and treatments.
6. Check the box indicating your consent to the terms and conditions.
7. Select the treatment plans that apply to your skin concerns.
8. Share any "Medical Conditions" you have that may affect your skincare routine.
9. List any medications you are currently taking.
10. Provide your "Contact Information" so we can reach out to you with follow-up reminders.
11. If applicable, sign the "Skincare Consultation Form Consent" to acknowledge our consultation terms.
12. Add any "Customer Notes" about your experience or concerns.
13. Include "Skincare Consultation Form Comments" about your preferences or concerns.
14. Add any "Treatment Plan Notes" for your consultation.
15. Specify your "Follow Up Treatment Date" and "Follow Up Time" for future appointments.
16. Add "Follow Up Treatment Notes" for your next appointment.
17. Set the "Next Follow Up Date" and "Next Follow Up Time" for future consultations.
18. Include "Next Follow Up Notes" for your next appointment.
19. Enter your "Email" address so we can send reminders and updates.
20. Provide your "Phone" number as a contact method.
21. Select whether you would like to receive follow-up reminders ("True" or "False").
22. Choose whether you would like to receive email reminders ("True" or "False").
23. Select whether you would like to receive appointment reminders ("True" or "False").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Customer (1)**: Enter your name to identify yourself as a new customer.
* **Health Information (2)**: Share any health concerns or medical conditions that may affect your skin.
* **Skin Details (3)**: Describe your skin type, concerns, or issues.
* **Skincare Goals (4)**: Outline your desired outcomes for your skincare routine.
* **Availability (5)**: Specify your availability for consultations and treatments.
* **Consent to Terms and Conditions (6)**: Check the box to acknowledge our consultation terms.
* **Treatment Plans (7)**: Select the plans that apply to your skin concerns.
* **Medical Conditions (8)**: List any medical conditions that may affect your skincare routine.
* **Medication (9)**: Share any medications you are currently taking.
* **Contact Information (10)**: Provide your contact details so we can reach out to you with updates.
* **Skincare Consultation Form Consent (11)**: Sign this if you agree to our consultation terms.
* **Customer Notes (12)**: Add any notes or comments about your experience or concerns.
* **Skincare Consultation Form Comments (13)**: Share your thoughts or concerns about your skin or consultation.
* **Treatment Plan Notes (14)**: Add any notes for your treatment plan.
* **Follow Up Treatment Date (15)**: Set your follow-up appointment date.
* **Follow Up Time (16)**: Set your follow-up appointment time.
* **Follow Up Treatment Notes (17)**: Add notes for your next appointment.
* **Next Follow Up Date (18)**: Set your next follow-up appointment date.
* **Next Follow Up Time (19)**: Set your next follow-up appointment time.
* **Next Follow Up Notes (20)**: Add notes for your next appointment.
* **Email (21)**: Enter your email address so we can send reminders and updates.
* **Phone (22)**: Provide your phone number as a contact method.
* **Follow Up Reminders (23)**: Select whether you would like to receive follow-up reminders.
* **Email Reminders (24)**: Choose whether you would like to receive email reminders.
* **Appointment Reminders (25)**: Select whether you would like to receive appointment reminders.

Note: This form is intended for customers seeking personalized skincare consultations and treatment plans.
