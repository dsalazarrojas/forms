# Lawn Care/Landscaping New Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from new clients for lawn care or landscaping services. It is designed to be completed by clients who are interested in scheduling services for their properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please provide your contact information (email and phone number) in the respective fields.
2. Select the services you are interested in (mowing, fertilizing, watering).
3. Choose a date for your scheduled appointment in the "Schedule" field.
4. If you have any special requests or notes, please enter them in the "Special Requests" field (this is optional).
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info** (Client Info, text, optional): This is a field for you to enter your name or any other identifying information. You do not need to complete this field if you want.
* **Select Services** (Select Services, select_one, true): Choose one or more services you are interested in:
	+ Mowing: lawn mowing services
	+ Fertilizing: lawn fertilizing services
	+ Watering: lawn watering services
* **Schedule** (Schedule, date, true): Choose a date for your scheduled appointment.
* **Special Requests** (Special Requests, note, false): If you have any special requests or notes, enter them here. This is an optional field.
* **Email** (Email, email, true): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, true): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (Address, text, true): Enter your address. 

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need to make changes to your submission, contact us at [insert contact info].

* Please let us know if you have any other services or areas of your lawn you would like us to focus on.
