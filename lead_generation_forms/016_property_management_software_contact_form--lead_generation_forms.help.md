# Property Management Software Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and details about the user's interests and preferences for Property Management Software. It's meant to help our team understand the user's needs and provide better support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or mail).
3. Select the features that interest you the most.
4. Provide a brief message with any additional information.
5. Specify the source where you heard about our software (website, social media, email marketing, or other).
6. If other, please specify the source.
7. Choose a follow-up method (email, phone, or mail).
8. Set a follow-up time.
9. Provide a brief follow-up note.
10. Fill in any custom fields if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Management Software Contact Form** (Title): This is the title of the form, indicating what the form is about.
* **Name** (`name`, Text, Optional): Your name.
* **Email** (`email`, Text, Optional): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, Text, Optional): Your company name or affiliation.
* **Location** (`location`, Text, Optional): The location associated with your contact.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, Text, Optional): How you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or mail).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, Select One, Optional): How you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or mail).
* **Feature 1** (`interest_in_feature_1`, Select Multiple, Optional): The features that interest you the most.
* **Feature 2** (`interest_in_feature_2`, Select Multiple, Optional): The features that interest you the most.
* **Feature Interests** (`feature_interests`, Select Multiple, Optional): The features that interest you the most.
* **Message** (`message`, Note, Optional): A brief message with any additional information.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, Select One, Optional): The source where you heard about our software (website, social media, email marketing, or other).
* **Other** (`lead_source_other`, Text, Optional): If other, please specify the source.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, Text, Optional): A brief follow-up note.
* **Follow Up Method** (`follow_up_method`, Select One, Optional): How you would like to be followed up (email, phone, or mail).
* **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, Date, Optional): The follow-up time.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, Date, Optional): The date you submitted this form.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, Email, Optional): Your email address (for follow-up purposes).
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, Text, Optional): Your phone number (for follow-up purposes).
* **Submitter Address** (`submitter_address`, Text, Optional): Your address (for follow-up purposes).
* **Follow Up Note** (`follow_up_note`, Note, Optional): A brief follow-up note.
* **Custom 1** (`custom_1`, Text, Optional): Custom field 1.
* **Custom 2** (`custom_2`, Text, Optional): Custom field 2.

Note: Please fill in only the required fields and provide any additional information as per your convenience.
