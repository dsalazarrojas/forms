# CRM Onboarding Consulting Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CRM Onboarding Consulting Intake Form is designed to gather essential information about your company to provide tailored CRM consulting services. By filling out this form, you will be helping our team understand your company's needs and prepare for a successful onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields (marked as **required**) thoroughly and accurately.
2. For fields with options, select all relevant choices that apply.
3. Use concrete numbers and specific details to fill out numerical fields (e.g., 10 users).
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company name**: This is the legal name of your company. Please enter the exact name as it appears on official documents.
* **Primary contact person**: This field should be filled out with the person who will be primarily responsible for project communications and decision-making.
* **Contact email**: This email address will be used for project-related communications. Please ensure it's a valid email address that can receive notifications and updates.
* **Current CRM platform**: If you have an existing CRM system in place, please specify the name of the platform. This helps us understand your current setup.
* **Primary goals for CRM implementation**: Select all relevant goals that align with your company's objectives (Lead management, Sales automation, Reporting and analytics, or Customer support). This helps us understand your key priorities.
* **Approximate number of CRM users**: Please provide an estimate of the number of users who will need access to the CRM system. This helps us plan for proper system configuration.
* **Desired implementation timeline**: Choose the timeframe for the desired completion date of the CRM implementation project. Please note that more complex projects may require longer timelines.
* **Additional technical or integration requirements**: If you have any special requirements or integrations in mind (e.g., APIs, data migrations, or customizations), please describe them in this field. This helps us prepare for potential technical challenges.
