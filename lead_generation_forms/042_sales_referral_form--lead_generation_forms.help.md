# Sales Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information about sales referrals, allowing the sales team to track the number of people informed about a sales opportunity and the means by which they were informed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the name of the form in the "Form Name" field.
* Enter your name in the "Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Select the sales person who referred you in the "Sales Person" field.
* Enter the number of people you informed about the sales opportunity in the "Number of People Informed" field.
* Enter the number of people who were informed about the opportunity at work in the "Number Informed at Work" field.
* Enter the number of people who were informed about the opportunity outside of work in the "Number Informed Outside of Work" field.
* Enter the number of people who were informed about the opportunity by email in the "Number Informed by Email" field.
* Enter the number of people who were informed about the opportunity by phone in the "Number Informed by Phone" field.
* Enter the follow-up date in the "Follow-up Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Name** (`form_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the form you are completing.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, not required): Enter your name as it is spelled on your business card or as you would like it to appear on the form.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number in the format of (123) 456-7890 or 123-456-7890.
* **Sales Person** (`sales_person`, `select_one`, not required): Select the sales person who referred you.
