# Content Download Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to download various types of content, and in return, you will provide the download link and any other information or services you may provide. By filling out this form, you will be able to choose which type of content you are interested in downloading and provide information on who you are and how we can contact you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as `required: true`.
2. Make sure to choose a valid option for each select fields.
3. Enter your email address to receive the download link.
4. Select the type of content you would like to download.
5. Answer any other relevant questions to help us provide you with the best experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, `required: true`): Your first name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, `required: true`): Your last name
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, `required: true`): We will send the download link here
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, `required: false`): Optional contact number
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, `required: false`): Name of your business
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, `required: false`): Your position or role
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, `required: true`): Choose the industry you are in
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, `required: true`): Choose the number of employees in your company
* **Content Type** (`content_type`, select_one, `required: true`): Choose the type of content you are interested in downloading
* **Content Title** (`content_title`, text, `required: true`): Title of the content
* **Download Purpose** (`download_purpose`, select_one, `required: true`): Choose the reason for downloading this content
* **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, text, `required: false`): What challenges are you trying to solve?
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, `required: true`): How did you find this content?
* **Interested in Consultation?** (`interested_in_consultation`, select_one, `required: true`): Would you like to speak with an expert?
* **Newsletter Subscription** (`newsletter_subscription`, select_one, `required: true`): Would you like to receive our newsletter?
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, `required: false`): How should we contact you?
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`, select_one, `required: false`): When is the best time to reach you?
* **Additional Interests** (`additional_interests`, select_multiple, `required: false`): What other topics interest you?
* **Privacy Policy Accepted** (`privacy_policy_accepted`, select_one, `required: true`): I accept the privacy policy
* **Download Date** (`download_date`, date, `required: true`): Date of download
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, `required: false`): Any other information you would like to share
