# tech_client_software_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "tech_client_software_inquiry_form" is a collection of questions to gather information from potential clients or customers about their software requirements and preferences. This form will help us understand their needs and provide better support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Company Name field with the name of your organization.
2. In the Software Inquiry field, describe the software you're interested in.
3. Enter your Email address so we can contact you back.
4. If available, provide your Phone number so we can reach out to you.
5. In the Tech Client Software Inquiry field, provide more details about your software requirements.
6. Choose the Best Time for a response by selecting a suitable time period.
7. In the Preferred Contact field, select the contact method you prefer:
	* Contact Person
	* Project Manager
	* IT Director
	* Other
8. Provide additional details about your software requirements in the Software Inquiry Details field.
9. For the Best Method field, select the method of contact you prefer:
	* In-person Meeting
	* Email
	* Call
	* Video Conference
	* Phone
