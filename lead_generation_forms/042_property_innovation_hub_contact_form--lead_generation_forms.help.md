# Property Innovation Hub Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for users to contact the Property Innovation Hub with inquiries or questions about properties. It helps the platform's team to understand your needs and provide relevant information or assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name.
2. Enter your Last name.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number (if you prefer to be contacted via phone).
5. Select the type of property you are inquiring about (Residential, Commercial, or Mixed).
6. Indicate the property status (Active, Inactive, or Under construction).
7. Choose the type of inquiry you have (General question, Request for information, or Request for proposal).
8. Write a Message with your question or comment.
9. Optionally, you can select an Assigned user to handle your inquiry.
10. Finally, provide the Submitted date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you prefer to be contacted via phone).
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of property you are inquiring about (Residential, Commercial, or Mixed).
* **Property Status** (`property_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate the property status (Active, Inactive, or Under construction).
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of inquiry you have (General question, Request for information, or Request for proposal).
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Write a brief message with your question or comment.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): Optionally, select an Assigned user to handle your inquiry.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you submit your inquiry.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you submit your inquiry.
