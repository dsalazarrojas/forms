# Interaction Details Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interaction Details Request Form is used to capture additional information from a company after an interaction has been pre-approved by a designated team member.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure the interaction has been pre-approved by a designated team member.
2. Fill in the company information section (Company Name, Company Email, Company URL, and Company Phone).
3. Provide a brief description of the interaction (Company Description).
4. Identify who requested the interaction (Requested by Name, Requested by Title, and Requested by Company).
5. Specify the date the interaction was requested (Requested by Date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (company_name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the company involved in the interaction.
* **Company Email** (company_email, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the company involved in the interaction.
* **Company URL** (company_url, text, required/optional): Enter the website URL of the company involved in the interaction.
* **Company Phone** (company_phone, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the company involved in the interaction.
* **Company Description** (company_description, note, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the interaction.
* **Requested by Name** (requested_by_name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who requested the interaction.
* **Requested by Title** (requested_by_title, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the person who requested the interaction.
* **Requested by Company** (requested_by_company, select_one, required/optional): Select the company that requested the interaction.
* **Requested by Date** (requested_by_date, date, required/optional): Enter the date the interaction was requested.

Note: If you're unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to a designated team member for assistance.
