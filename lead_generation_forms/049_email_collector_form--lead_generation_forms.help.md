# email_collector_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect email addresses from interested individuals, allowing you to reach out to them about a specific topic or industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply:

1. Enter your email address in the field below.
2. If you wish to, you can provide a brief description of yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address here. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Description** (`description`): Provide a brief description about yourself, if you wish. This field is optional.
 
## Tips
- Ensure you are entering a valid email address for the **Email** field.
- You can leave the **Description** field blank if you do not wish to provide additional information.
- If you need help or have questions, feel free to contact form@gic.mx.
