# Mortgage Calculator Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortgage Calculator Lead Form is designed to collect contact and loan information from users for the purpose of generating leads for a mortgage calculator or other related services. This form is meant to be used by potential customers who want to explore mortgage options and receive personalized quotes or further information from the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the appropriate payment type for your loan (Monthly, Bi-Weekly, or Weekly).
3. Enter your contact email address and phone number.
4. Provide your address and state.
5. Select your lead source (Yes or No).
6. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info**: This field is used to collect your contact information, including name, address, and phone number. This will help the company follow up with you for more information or to provide a quote.
* **Loan Amount**: This field is used to enter the amount you are looking to borrow for your mortgage. Please enter a numerical value.
* **Home Price**: This field is used to enter the price of the home you are looking to purchase. Please enter a numerical value.
* **Interest Rate**: This field is used to enter the interest rate of the loan. Please enter a numerical value.
* **Loan Term**: This field is used to enter the term of the loan. Please enter a numerical value.
* **Payment Type**: This field is used to select the payment type you are interested in (Monthly, Bi-Weekly, or Weekly).
* **Email**: This field is used to enter your email address, which will be used for follow up from the company.
* **Phone**: This field is used to enter your phone number, which will be used for follow up from the company.
* **Address**: This field is used to enter your address.
* **State**: This field is used to select your state.
* **City**: This field is used to enter your city.
* **Zip**: This field is used to enter your zip code.
* **Lead Source**: This field is used to select whether you are a lead from a specific source (Yes or No).

**Tips**:
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you need further assistance or have questions, contact the company using the provided contact information.
* The company will follow up with you to discuss your mortgage options and provide personalized quotes.
