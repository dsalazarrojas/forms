# Lead Generating Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead Generating Form is designed to capture information about leads generated, which are potential customers or prospects that have shown interest in a product or service. This form helps gather contact details and other relevant information about the generated leads.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead Generating Form Page 1** (Text): Enter a brief description of the lead generating form.
* **Lead Generating Form** (Select One): Please select the appropriate option for the lead generation form.
* **Lead Generating Form Page 2** (Text): Enter any additional information about the lead generating form.
* **Lead Generating Form Note** (Note): Add any relevant notes about the lead generating form.
* **Lead Generating Form Email** (Email): Enter the lead's email address.
* **Lead Generating Form Phone** (Text): Enter the lead's phone number.
* **Lead Generating Form Description** (Text): Enter a brief description of the lead.
* **Lead Generating Form Lead** (Number): Enter the lead's number (e.g., for ranking or priority).
* **Lead Generating Form Source** (Select Multiple): Please select the appropriate options for the lead generation form source.
* **Lead Generating Form Status** (Select One): Please select the status of the lead generating form.
* **Lead Generating Form Date** (Date): Enter the date of the lead generating form.

# Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required and optional fields accurately and comprehensively.
* Use the select options wisely to ensure the lead generating form is properly categorized.
* Double-check the lead's contact information for accuracy.
