# facebook_likes_and_twitter_followers - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic data about your social media presence, specifically about your Facebook likes and Twitter followers. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your business type from the list.
2. Enter your Facebook likes.
3. Enter your Twitter followers.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your name.
6. Enter a message.
7. Select whether you accept or not to the terms and conditions (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **is_your_business_seo** (`is_your_business_seo`, select_one, required): This field is used to determine if your business has a SEO.
* **facebook_likes** (`facebook_likes`, number, required): This field is used to enter your Facebook likes.
* **twitter_followers** (`twitter_followers`, number, required): This field is used to enter your Twitter followers.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): This field is used to enter your email address.
* **name** (`name`, text, required): This field is used to enter your name.
* **message** (`message`, text, required): This field is used to enter a message.
* **accept** (`accept`, select_one, required): This field is used to select whether you accept or not to the terms and conditions.
* **agree** (`agree`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select whether you agree or not to some terms or conditions.
* **agree2**, **agree3**, ..., **agree16**, **agree17**, **agree18**, **agree19** (`agree2`, ..., `agree19`, optional): These fields are used to select whether you agree or not to some terms or conditions.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is for demographic data collection purposes only, and the data will be used for research and marketing purposes.
