# Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The workshop attendance form is designed to collect information from attendees to help the workshop organizers with planning and communication. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure a smooth workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the workshop title in the provided text field.
2. Select the date of the workshop from the date picker.
3. Enter your email address in the format `name@domain.com`.
4. Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
5. If you have any other attendees with you, select the option "True" and enter their names in the field "Other attendees names".
6. Enter the contact phone number of the attendee.
7. Add any comments or special requests in the "Comments" field.
8. Enter the workshop location in the "Workshop Location" field.
9. Select the workshop status (Confirmed, Tentative, or Cancelled).
10. If other attendees are with you, enter their email addresses in the "Other attendee emails" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Title**: <label> (<type>: text, required: false): A brief title of the workshop to help the organizers keep track of it.
* **Workshops Date**: <label> (<type>: date, required: false): The date of the workshop. Use the date picker to select the correct date.
* **Email**: <label> (<type>: email, required: false): Enter your email address in the format `name@domain.com`.
* **Attendee Name**: <label> (<type>: text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Attendee Email**: <label> (<type>: email, required: false): Enter the email address of the attendee (if different from yours).
* **Do you have any other attendees with you?**: <label> (<type>: select_multiple, required: false): If yes, select "True" and enter other attendees' names and emails.
* **Attendee Phone**: <label> (<type>: text, required: false): Enter the contact phone number of the attendee.
* **Comments**: <label> (<type>: note, required: false): Add any comments or special requests here.
* **Workshop Location**: <label> (<type>: text, required: false): Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Workshop Status**: <label> (<type>: select_one, required: false): Select the status of the workshop (Confirmed, Tentative, or Cancelled).
* **Other attendees names**: <label> (<type>: text, required: false): If you selected "True" in the previous field, enter other attendees' names here.
* **Other attendee emails**: <label> (<type>: text, required: false): If you selected "True" in the previous field, enter other attendees' email addresses here.
