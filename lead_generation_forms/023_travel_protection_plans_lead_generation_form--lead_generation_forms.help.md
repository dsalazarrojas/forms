# Travel Protection Plans Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Protection Plans Lead Generation Form is used to gather information from potential customers interested in travel protection plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief introduction to your travel plans in the **travel_protection_plans_lead_generation_form_1_label** field.
2. Enter your contact information, including:
	* **contact_name_label** (required): Enter your name and contact details.
	* **lead_agent_label** (required): Enter your name.
3. Choose your lead source from the **lead_source_label** dropdown.
4. Select your travel type from the **travel_type_label** dropdown.
5. Choose a travel protection plan from the **travel_protection_plans_label** dropdown.
6. Enter your lead status from the **lead_status_label** dropdown.
7. Provide your customer's contact information, including:
	* **customer_phone_label** (optional): Enter your customer's phone number.
	* **email_label** (optional): Enter your customer's email address.
	* **customer_address_label** (optional): Enter your customer's address.
	* **customer_city_label** (optional): Enter your customer's city.
	* **customer_state_label** (optional): Enter your customer's state or province.
	* **customer_zip_label** (optional): Enter your customer's zip or postal code.
	* **customer_country_label** (optional): Enter your customer's country.
8. Enter your travel details, including:
	* **travel_start_date_label** (optional): Enter your travel start date.
	* **travel_end_date_label** (optional): Enter your travel end date.
	* **travel_duration_label** (optional): Enter the length of your travel in days.
	* **travel_cost_label** (optional): Enter the cost of your travel.
	* **travel_currency_label** (optional): Choose the currency of your travel cost.
9. Enter your travel insurance details, including:
	* **travel_insurer_label** (optional): Choose your travel insurer.
	* **travel_insurer_address_label** (optional): Enter your travel insurer's address.
	* **travel_insurer_phone_label** (optional): Enter your travel insurer's phone number.
	* **travel_insurer_email_label** (optional): Enter your travel insurer's email address.
	* **travel_insurer_website_label** (optional): Enter your travel insurer's website.
