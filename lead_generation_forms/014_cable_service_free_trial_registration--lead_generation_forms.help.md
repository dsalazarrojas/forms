# Cable Service Free Trial Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cable Service Free Trial Registration form is designed to gather information from customers interested in trying our cable service for free. This form will help us determine your eligibility and preferences for our services, and guide you through the sign-up process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your first and last name.
2. Enter your primary contact email address.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Enter your street address.
5. Specify your city and state or province.
6. Enter your ZIP code.
7. Check if you'd like to verify service availability at your address.
8. Select which trial package interests you (Basic, Standard, Premium, or Ultimate).
9. Choose if you'd like to add high-speed internet or home phone service.
10. If you have a current TV provider, select them from the list.
11. Rate your satisfaction with your current provider.
12. If you're looking for new service, select the reasons that apply.
13. Choose a preferred installation date (if applicable).
14. Select a preferred contact time for our team to reach you.
15. Confirm you understand and accept the trial terms.
16. Agree to receive marketing communications from us.
17. Tell us where you heard about our offer.
18. Answer any additional questions or make special requests (if needed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, **required**): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, **required**): Enter your best contact number where we can reach you.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, **required**): Enter your home address as it appears on your identification.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, **required**): Enter your city name as it appears on your identification.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, **required**): Enter your state or province as it appears on your identification.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, **required**): Enter your ZIP code as it appears on your identification.
* **Service Availability Check** (`service_availability_check`, `note`, **optional**): This field helps us verify service availability at your address. You can choose to skip this step.
* **Trial Package Interest** (`trial_package_interest`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose which package interests you: Basic, Standard, Premium, or Ultimate.
* **Internet Bundle Interest** (`internet_bundle`, `select_one`, **optional**): Check if you'd like to add high-speed internet service.
* **Home Phone Bundle** (`phone_bundle`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose if you'd like to add home phone service.
* **Current TV Provider** (`current_provider`, `select_one`, **optional**): If you have a current provider, select them from the list.
* **Satisfaction with Current Service** (`satisfaction_with_current`, `select_one`, **optional**): Rate your satisfaction with your current provider.
* **Reason for Considering Switch** (`reason_for_switching`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select the reasons you're considering new service (e.g., Better price, More channels, etc.).
* **Preferred Installation Date** (`preferred_installation_date`, `date`, **optional**): Choose a preferred date for our installation team to visit.
* **Preferred Contact Time** (`preferred_contact_time`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose a preferred time for our team to contact you.
* **Trial Terms Acceptance** (`trial_terms_acceptance`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm you understand and accept the trial terms.
* **Marketing Communications** (`marketing_consent`, `select_one`, **optional**): Agree to receive marketing communications from us.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, **optional**): Tell us where you learned about our offer.
* **Additional Questions** (`additional_questions`, `text`, **optional**): Answer any additional questions or make special requests if needed.
