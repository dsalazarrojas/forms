# hotel_sales_lead_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from potential leads in the hotel sales process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Contact Info" page.
2. Provide your contact information in the required fields, including the "Lead Name", "Lead Email", and "Lead Phone".
3. If your contact method is different from the options provided, select "Other" from the "Contact Type" field.
4. You may provide additional information, such as your job title, company, or other contact details, if necessary.
5. Click "Submit" to save your lead information.
