# Solar Energy Business Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solar Energy Business Inquiry form is designed to collect information from businesses interested in exploring opportunities related to solar energy. This form is intended for businesses that want to be contacted by the solar energy team for potential partnerships, collaborations, or other business-related opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your home address in the "Home Address" field. This is the physical address where your business is located or where you can be reached.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is where you can be contacted for further communication and follow-up.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This is how our team can contact you directly for more information or to discuss opportunities.
4. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field. This is your personal name, and we may use it to address you in our communication.
5. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field. This is your personal surname.
6. Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field. This is the name of your business.
7. Enter a brief description of your business in the "Business Description" field. Please provide a short description of your business, including what you do and any relevant information about your company.
8. Enter your website in the "Website" field. This is the URL of your company's website, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Address**: <u>Home Address</u> is where your business is located or where you can be reached. Please enter your physical address in this field.
* **Email**: <u>Email</u> is the address where you can be contacted for further communication and follow-up.
* **Phone Number**: <u>Phone Number</u> is how our team can contact you directly for more information or to discuss opportunities.
* **First Name**: <u>First Name</u> is your personal name and we may use it to address you in our communication.
* **Last Name**: <u>Last Name</u> is your personal surname.
* **Company Name**: <u>Company Name</u> is the name of your business.
* **Business Description**: <u>Business Description</u> is a brief description of your business, including what you do and any relevant information about your company.
* **Website**: <u>Website</u> is the URL of your company's website, if applicable.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that our team can best understand your business needs and opportunities.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us directly.
* We will review your form submission and follow up with you within a reasonable time frame.
