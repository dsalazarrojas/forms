# Tea Basket Giveaway Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tea Basket Giveaway Entry form collects necessary information from users to process their entry for a tea basket giveaway. This form is used to obtain essential details for accurate processing and to avoid duplication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Tea Basket Giveaway Entry form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the personal details section with your name and contact information.
2. Select your preferred tea basket brand.
3. Choose your delivery method and date and time.
4. Optionally, add a message if you have any special requests.
5. Agree to the terms and conditions and privacy policy.
6. Click the submit button to complete your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please fill in your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please fill in your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication and delivery purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact and delivery confirmation.
* **Tea Basket Size** (`tea_basket_size`, number, required): Select the size of tea basket you would like to receive.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter your shipping address for accurate delivery.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Please fill in your city name as it appears on your address.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state name as it appears on your address.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter a valid zip code for correct delivery.
* **Tea Basket Brand** (`tea_basket_brand`, select_one, required): Choose the brand of tea basket you would like to receive.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to receive your tea basket.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required): Choose the time slot for delivery confirmation.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Select the delivery method of your choice.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): If you have any special requests, please add it here.
* **Agree to Terms** (`agree_terms`, select_one, required): Select "I agree with the terms and conditions" to proceed.
* **Agree to Privacy** (`agree_privacy`, select_one, required): Select "I agree with the privacy policy" to proceed.
* **Submit Button** (`submit_button`, text, required): Click this button to complete your entry.
