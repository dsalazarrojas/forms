# Hemp Product Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hemp Product Referral Form is designed to collect information about customers who are interested in hemp products. This form helps to identify customers who may be potential leads for our business and allows us to tailor our communication and follow-up accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name and email address under "Customer Referral" and "Customer Email" fields, respectively.
2. Specify the product that interested the customer under "Product".
3. Choose the reason for the referral under "Referral Reason".
4. Select the type of entity the customer was referred to (Retailer, Clinic, or Wellness Brand) under "Clinic/Wellness Brand".
5. If necessary, provide additional details under "Note".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Referral** (`customer_referral`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer who referred this hemp product.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer who is interested in our hemp products.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the customer. Make sure to use a valid email format.
* **Product** (`product`, text, optional): Specify the specific hemp product that interested the customer.
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options "Interested", "Recommended", or "Other" to indicate the reason for the referral.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the customer (if applicable).
* **Clinic/Wellness Brand** (`clinic_wellness_brand`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from the options "Retailer", "Clinic", or "Wellness Brand" to indicate the type of entity the customer was referred to.
* **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): If you have a scheduled follow-up, enter the date here.
* **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): If you have a scheduled follow-up, enter the time here.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_one, optional): Choose the user to be assigned to follow up with the customer.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the referral.

## Tips
* Always ensure that the form is used for its intended purpose and not for other tasks.
* Double-check the information entered for accuracy.
* Use valid email formats for "Customer Email".
* Be cautious when choosing the "Assigned User" to ensure timely and effective follow-up.
* Use "Note" field to add context or reminders to the follow-up process.
