# Free Activation Guide Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register and collect information about free activation guides. It helps the organization to understand what guides are available and how they can be shared with users.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with the necessary information about the free activation guide you want to register.
2.  Make sure to fill out all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3.  If a field is optional, you can choose to fill it out or leave it blank, depending on your preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Lead Generation Form Fields**: This is the main section of the form where you can fill out information about the lead generation form fields.
*   **Name**: This field is used to enter the name of the activation guide.
*   **Email**: This field is used to enter the email address associated with the activation guide.
*   **Phone**: This field is used to enter the phone number associated with the activation guide.
*   **Company**: This field is used to enter the company associated with the activation guide.
*   **Job Title**: This field is used to enter the job title associated with the activation guide.
*   **Department**: This field is used to enter the department associated with the activation guide.
*   **Job Type**: This field is used to select the type of job that this activation guide is for. Choose from Marketing, Sales, Support, IT, or HR.
*   **Location**: This field is used to enter the location associated with the activation guide.
*   **Job Description**: This field is used to enter a brief description of the job associated with the activation guide.
*   **Job Status**: This field is used to select the status of the job associated with the activation guide. Choose from New, In Progress, Completed, On Hold, or Cancelled.
*   **Guide Type**: This field is used to select the type of guide you are registering. Choose from PDF, Video, Webinar, or Other.
*   **Guide Language**: This field is used to select the language of the guide you are registering. Choose from English, Spanish, French, German, or Chinese.
*   **Guide Frequency**: This field is used to select how often the guide is sent. Choose from Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-annually, or Annually.
*   **Guide Format**: This field is used to select the format of the guide you are registering. Choose from PDF, Video, Webinar, or Other.
*   **Send Frequency**: This field is used to select how often the guide is sent. Choose from Immediately, Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-annually, or Annually.

Please note that some fields are marked as "required", while others are optional. Please ensure to fill out all required fields to complete the form successfully.
