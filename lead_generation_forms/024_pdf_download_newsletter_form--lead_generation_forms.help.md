# pdf_download_newsletter_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user preferences for generating PDF newsletters. It is intended for users who want to receive newsletters and select which ones they are interested in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select the Newsletters you are interested in from the "Newsletter" field.
4. Confirm your selection by choosing "True" in the "Confirm" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Name** (First Name, text, required)
Enter your first name here. This will be used to address you in the generated PDF newsletter.

### **Email** (Email, email, required)
Enter your email address to receive the generated PDF newsletter.

### **Newsletter** (Newsletter, select_multiple, required)
Select the newsleters you are interested in downloading. You can choose one or more of the available options.

### **Confirm** (Confirm, select_one, required)
Confirm that you have selected the correct newsleters by choosing "True". This ensures that you are aware of your selection.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure you receive the generated PDF newsletter.
* Select all the newsleters you are interested in to receive a comprehensive PDF.
* Review your selection carefully before confirming to avoid mistakes.
