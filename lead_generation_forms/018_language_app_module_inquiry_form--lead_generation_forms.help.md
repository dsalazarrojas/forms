# language_app_module_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from language learners who are interested in learning a new language or improving their existing language skills. The form will ask for contact details, language preferences, and specific learning goals to better understand their needs and provide personalized support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your name in the "name" field.
* Provide your email address in the "email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
* Specify your location in the "location" field.
* Select your preferred language from the options in the "language" field.
* Choose your current level of proficiency in the language from the options in the "level" field.
* Select the language modules you are interested in from the options in the "module" field.
* Select the areas you would like to match with in the "match" field.
* Enter any additional method or context you have for learning the language in the "method" field.
* Write a brief description of your interests and goals in the "description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter your city or area where you are located.
* **Language** (`language`, select_one, required/optional): Select the language you are interested in learning or improving.
* **Level** (`level`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your current level of proficiency in the language.
* **Module** (`module`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the language modules you are interested in.
* **Match** (`match`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the areas you would like to match with.
* **Method** (`method`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional method or context you have for learning the language.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Write a brief description of your interests and goals in learning the language.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and detailed information to help us better understand your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
* We will be in touch with you after submitting the form to provide personalized support and guidance.
