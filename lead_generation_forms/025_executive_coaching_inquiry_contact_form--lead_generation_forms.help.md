# Executive Coaching Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Coaching Inquiry Contact Form is designed to gather information from job seekers who are interested in executive coaching, consulting, or speaking services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the first page.
2. Select your Inquiry Type (Coaching, Consulting, or Speaking).
3. If desired, add your Phone number, Company, Job Title, Location, Job Function, Department, Job Level, Years of Experience, Job Type, Industry, Desired Location, Job Status, and Job URL.
4. Enter your LinkedIn URL, Twitter URL, and Other Contact Information if you have any.
5. If you want to add any additional comments, you can do so in the Additional Comments field.
6. Finally, ensure that you've filled in the Job ID and Assigned Tool fields.
7. Review your form and ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address. This will be used to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: Coaching, Consulting, or Speaking.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): If you want to add any additional comments or information, you can do so here.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Please enter your company name if you're affiliated with one.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Please enter your job title.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Please enter your location or city.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, optional): Please enter your job function or role.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Please enter your department name.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, text, optional): Please enter your job level or seniority.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, optional): Please enter your years of experience.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, optional): Please enter your job type or industry.
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, optional): Please enter your industry or sector.
* **Desired Location** (`desired_location`, text, optional): Please enter your desired location or city.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, text, optional): Please enter your job status or current situation.
* **Job URL** (`job_url`, text, optional): Please enter the job URL or link to your job posting.
* **LinkedIn URL** (`linkedin_url`, text, optional): Please enter your LinkedIn URL.
* **Twitter URL** (`twitter_url`, text, optional): Please enter your Twitter URL.
* **Other Contact Information** (`other_contact_info`, text, optional): Please enter any other relevant contact information.
* **How did you find us?** (`how_did_you_find_us`, text, optional): Please tell us how you found us.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): If you want to add any additional comments or information, you can do so here.
* **Job ID** (`job_id`, text, required): Please enter the job ID or reference number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Please enter the assigned tool or software used for the inquiry.
