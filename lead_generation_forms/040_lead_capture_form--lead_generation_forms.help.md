# Lead Capture Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead Capture Form is a crucial tool for sales teams to collect relevant information about potential customers and leads. The form is designed to be easy to use and requires minimal information to fill out.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Lead Capture Form, follow these steps:

- Fill in your **First Name** in the designated field.
- Enter your **Last Name** in the designated field.
- Provide your **Email** address to help us get in touch with you.
- Enter your **Phone Number** for follow-up communications.
- Add any **Lead Information** in the space provided, if needed.
- Fill in your **Company** name for reference.
- Enter your **Job Title** to help us understand your role.
- Enter your **Job Position** for more detailed information about your position.
- Specify your **Department** or team you belong to.
- Enter the **Job Level** of your role for better understanding.
- Choose the **Job Function** that best describes your job role.
- Enter the **Location** where you are based or working.
- Specify the **Lead Source** where you heard about us (e.g., social media, event).
- Finally, choose the **Company Size** for better filtering and targeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, Required/Optional): Enter your first name to help us get a better understanding of who you are.
* **Last Name** (text, Required/Optional): Enter your last name to help us get a better understanding of who you are.
* **Email** (email, Required/Optional): Enter a valid email address for us to get in touch with you.
* **Phone Number** (text, Required/Optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up communications.
* **Lead Information** (note, Required/Optional): Add any additional information about yourself or your company (if needed).
* **Company** (text, Required/Optional): Enter your company name for reference.
* **Job Title** (text, Required/Optional): Enter the title of your job position.
* **Job Position** (text, Required/Optional): Enter more detailed information about your position.
* **Department** (text, Required/Optional): Enter the department or team you belong to.
* **Job Level** (text, Required/Optional): Enter the level of your job role for better filtering.
* **Job Function** (text, Required/Optional): Choose the job function that best describes your job role.
* **Location** (text, Required/Optional): Enter the location where you are based or working.
* **Lead Source** (text, Required/Optional): Specify where you heard about us (e.g., social media, event).
* **Company Size** (text, Required/Optional): Choose the size of your company for better filtering.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the required fields to ensure your lead information is complete and up-to-date.
- Be honest and accurate with the information you provide.
- If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us!
