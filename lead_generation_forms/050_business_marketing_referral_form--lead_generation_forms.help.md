# Business Marketing Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect referral information from business marketing teams. It helps the sales team to identify potential clients and follow up on referral leads.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the form with the required information about the referrer and the referred company.
2. Provide a brief description of the potential project or opportunity.
3. Estimate the value of the referral opportunity, if applicable.
4. Choose the urgency level for the sales team to contact the referral.
5. Add any additional notes about the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referrer name** (`referrer_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the person who is submitting the referral.
* **Referrer email** (`referrer_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email for the referrer.
* **Referred company** (`referred_company`, text, required): Enter the name of the business that was referred.
* **Referred contact** (`referred_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person at the referred company.
* **Referred email** (`referred_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email for the referred party.
* **Potential project** (`potential_project`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the potential project or opportunity.
* **Estimated value** (`estimated_value`, number, optional): Estimate the value of the referral opportunity, if known.
* **Urgency** (`urgency`, select_one, optional): Choose the urgency level for the sales team to contact the referral. Options are:
	+ Within 24 hours
	+ Within 3 days
	+ Within a week
	+ No rush
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any other information about the referral.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use a brief and clear description for the potential project or opportunity.
* If you're unsure about the estimated value, you can skip it.
* Choose the urgency level that best represents the sales team's required response time.
