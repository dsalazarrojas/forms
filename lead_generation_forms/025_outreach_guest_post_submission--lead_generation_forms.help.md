# Outreach Guest Post Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Outreach Guest Post Submission form, which is designed to help authors and writers submit their guest posts to be published on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your author information: Enter your name in the `author` field.
2. Enter your email address: Enter your email address in the `email` field.
3. Choose a subject: Select a subject that best describes your post in the `subject` field.
4. Enter your post title: Enter the title of your post in the `title` field.
5. Select a category: Choose the category that best fits your post from the `category` options (Marketing, SEO, Technology, Finance, or Sports).
6. Enter your post content: Enter the body of your post in the `content` field.
7. Add an image: Enter a URL or description of an image related to your post in the `image` field.
8. Write a brief author bio: Enter a brief description of yourself in the `author bio` field.
9. Add an author image: Enter a URL or description of an image for your author in the `author image` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **author** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name as the author of the post.
* **email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further questions or discussions.
* **subject** (`text`, required/optional): Select a subject that best describes your post to help us categorize it correctly.
* **title** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the title of your post to make it easily discoverable.
* **category** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose the category that best fits your post from the options provided.
* **subcategory** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a subcategory or tag to further describe your post.
* **content** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the body of your post, including text, links, or any other relevant information.
* **image** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a URL or description of an image related to your post to make it more engaging.
* **author_bio** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of yourself as the author, including your experience, expertise, or credentials.
* **author_image** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a URL or description of an image for your author to make it more personal and recognizable.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide high-quality and relevant content that aligns with our platform's standards.
* Use proper grammar and formatting for your post.
* If you're not sure about any field, please ask us for clarification or guidance.
* We'll review and moderate each submission before publication to ensure it meets our standards.
