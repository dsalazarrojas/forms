# Workforce Productivity Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential clients or customers interested in workforce productivity consulting services. It helps us understand your needs and preferences for consulting support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your name, organization, and email address.
2. Select your role within your organization.
3. Briefly describe your productivity challenges.
4. Choose a follow-up time for a meeting.
5. If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to type them in the "Challenges" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Form** (`contact_form`, text, not required): This is where you can type any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Please enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, not required): Type in your organization's name.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose your role within your organization from the options provided:
	+ Consultant
	+ Manager
	+ Director
	+ Executive
	+ Other
* **Productivity Challenges** (`challenges`, note, not required): Describe the specific productivity challenges you're facing.
* **Follow up** (`follow_up`, date, not required): Choose a time for a follow-up meeting.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): If you've selected a follow-up time, this field will automatically be populated with that time.
