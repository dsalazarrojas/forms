# Smart Returns Optimization Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Smart Returns Optimization Registration Form is a tool for customers to input their business details, return volumes, and return challenges related to their smart returns optimization journey. It helps our team understand their current state and provides a starting point for further analysis.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before submitting it.
2. Fill in the required fields, as marked with a red asterisk (\*).
3. Use the provided hints to guide your input.
4. Submit the form when you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Details** (Business Details, text, optional): Please input your business details such as company name, address, and contact information.
* **Return Volumes** (Return Volumes, number, optional): Please input your average return volumes per day/week/month.
* **Return Challenges** (Return Challenges, text, optional): Please describe any challenges you are facing with returns, such as high return rates, customer complaints, etc.

## Tips

* Please be accurate and up-to-date with your input.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your account manager.
* If you have already completed this form in the past, please do not resubmit it.
