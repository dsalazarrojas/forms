# music_festival_artist_lead_generation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music_festival_artist_lead_generation_form is a multi-page form for music festival organizers to collect artist lead generation information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please provide a brief description of the artist you would like to feature in the festival.
2.  Choose the category that best describes the artist (Artist Name, Artist Genre, Artist Website).
3.  Fill out the contact details for the artist or their representative.
4.  Proceed to the next page and provide any additional information or details as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Details** (Artist Name, Artist Genre, Artist Website): Please select the options that best describe the artist you are submitting.
    *   **Artist Name**: Enter the artist's name.
    *   **Artist Genre**: Select the genre of music the artist performs.
    *   **Artist Website**: Enter the artist's website.
* **Contact Details**: Please provide any additional contact information for the artist or their representative.
* **Additional Information**: This field is for any further information or details about the artist as prompted on each page.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before proceeding.
*   If you are unsure about any of the options, please refer to the artist's website or contact information for clarification.
*   You can save your progress and come back to the form later, but ensure that all required fields are filled out before submission.
