# Sweepstake Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and demographic information from users for sweepstakes or contest entry purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
6. Enter your street address in the "street" field.
7. Enter your city in the "city" field.
8. Enter your zip code in the "zip" field.
9. Enter your state in the "state" field.
10. Enter your country in the "country" field.
11. Enter the reason you'd like to enter the sweepstakes in the "entry_reason" field.
12. Enter the date and time of your form submission in the "entry_date" field.
13. Select your agreement to the terms and conditions.
14. (Optional) Enter an alternative entry date if your submission is not the first entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your given name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your surname as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your personal account.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, including area code and country code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Street** (`street`, `text`, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where your address is located.
* **Zip** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your 5-digit zip code as it appears on your postal address.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state where your address is located.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Enter the country where your address is located.
* **Why Did You Enter?** (`entry_reason`, `text`, required): Enter the reason you want to participate in this sweepstakes.
* **Entry Date** (`entry_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date and time of your form submission in MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM format.
* **Agree to Terms** (`agree`, `select_one`, required): Select "I agree to the terms and conditions" to confirm your acceptance.
* **Optional Entry Date** (`entry_date_optional`, `date`, optional): Enter an alternative entry date if your submission is not the first entry.

Note: Please ensure you fill out the required fields accurately to ensure your entry is valid.
