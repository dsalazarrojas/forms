# Art Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for an art workshop, which includes providing personal and workshop preferences, and committing to attend the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering the registration date.
2. Fill out the participant's name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
3. Select the age group and workshop to attend.
4. Choose a preferred workshop date and time.
5. Answer questions about experience level, previous workshops, goals for the workshop, and material needs.
6. Select a payment method and agree to the cancellation policy, liability waiver, and photo release.
7. Confirm that you have read and understand the workshop details.
8. Add any additional comments or information about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you plan to register for the workshop.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it should be on your registration and identification documents.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, required): Enter your home address where materials can be sent.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city name.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state or province name.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal or zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): If you want to provide your date of birth for any reason, it can be done here.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, optional): Select your age group.
* **Workshop Selection** (`workshop_selection`, `select_one`, required): Select which workshop you want to attend.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, required): Select a preferred workshop date.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, `select_one`, required): Select the preferred workshop time.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your experience level with art or the specific workshop.
* **Previous Workshops** (`previous_workshops`, `select_one`, required): Answer if you have attended previous workshops.
* **Goals for Workshop** (`goals_for_workshop`, `text`, required): Write what you hope to learn from the workshop.
* **Materials Included** (`materials_included`, `select_one`, required): Select if you need materials provided or not.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `select_one`, required): If you have special accessibility needs, select yes.
* **Accessibility Details** (`accessibility_details`, `text`, optional): Describe your accessibility needs.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have dietary restrictions, select them here.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
* **How Heard** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, optional): How did you hear about the workshop.
* **Referral Name** (`referral_name`, `text`, optional): If you were referred, enter the name of the person who referred you.
* **Workshop Cost** (`workshop_cost`, `number`, optional): Enter the cost of the workshop if you have already been informed.
* **Materials Cost** (`materials_cost`, `number`, optional): Enter the cost of materials if you have already been informed.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, `number`, optional): Enter the total amount due if you have already been informed.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method you will use.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy.
* **Liability Waiver** (`liability_waiver`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to the liability waiver.
* **Photo Release** (`photo_release`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you allow photos for promotional use.
* **Mailing List** (`mailing_list`, `select_one`, required): Decide if you want to join the mailing list.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any other information or comments.
