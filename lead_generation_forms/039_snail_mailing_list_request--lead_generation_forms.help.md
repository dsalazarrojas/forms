# snail_mailing_list_request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The snail_mailing_list_request form is used to request a snail mail address for a company. This form helps us gather information from users to create a mailing list for a snail mail campaign. Please ensure you are submitting a genuine request and not duplicating an existing request.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of form you want to submit (email or phone).
2. Fill in your name as it appears on the company records.
3. Enter a valid email address for the company.
4. Enter the company name as it appears on the company records.
5. Enter a valid phone number for the company.
6. Provide a message explaining why you need a snail mail address.
7. Enter a valid email address for the company (this field is for internal use).
8. Decide whether the company is willing to receive snail mail (check the "Snail Mail" field).
9. Select the tool to be assigned for the snail mail campaign (e.g., chatjimmy).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Type (`email`, `phone`, required):** Select the type of form you want to submit.
* **Name (`name`, text, required):** Enter your name as it appears on the company records.
* **Email (`email`, email, required):** Enter a valid email address for the company.
* **Company Name (`company_name`, text, required):** Enter the company name as it appears on the company records.
* **Phone Number (`phone_number`, number, required):** Enter a valid phone number for the company.
* **Message (`message`, text, required):** Provide a message explaining why you need a snail mail address.
* **Email Address (`email_address`, email, required):** Enter a valid email address for internal use.
* **Snail Mail (`snail_mail`, select_multiple, required):** Decide whether the company is willing to receive snail mail.
* **Assigned Tool (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required):** Select the tool to be assigned for the snail mail campaign (e.g., chatjimmy).

## Tips

* Please ensure you are submitting a genuine request and not duplicating an existing request.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant team for assistance.
