# Global Expansion Consulting Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from potential partners interested in global expansion consulting services. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure we can get in touch with you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Provide your Last Name.
3. Enter a valid Email address we can use to contact you.
4. Include a phone number we can reach you at.
5. If applicable, specify the name of the Company you're representing.
6. Define your Role within the Company.
7. Mention the Industry you're associated with.
8. Provide your Location (city or area).
9. Write a brief Message with any relevant details about your partnership interest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A field to input your first name for our records.
* **Last Name**: A field to input your last name for our records.
* **Email**: A field to input a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Phone**: A field to input a phone number we can reach you at.
* **Company (optional)**: If you're representing a company, please input its name for our reference.
* **Role (optional)**: Specify your role within the Company, e.g., CEO, Marketing Manager, etc.
* **Industry (optional)**: Mention the Industry your Company operates in, e.g., technology, finance, etc.
* **Location (optional)**: Provide your Location or the location where your Company is based.
* **Message (optional)**: A field to include any relevant information or details about your partnership interest. Be concise but clear, as this will help us assess our potential partnership opportunity.
