# Executive Insight Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to submit their contact information and message to the Executive Insight Consulting team. The team uses this information to respond to inquiries, provide support, and offer services to clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field. This is a required field if you want to be contacted by us.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field. This field is optional, but it helps us personalize our response.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field if you want us to respond to your inquiry. Please use a valid email address that you check regularly.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field. This field is optional, but it helps us contact you if you prefer to discuss over the phone.
5. Write your message in the "Message" field. This is a required field, as we need to know what you're inquiring about.
6. Enter your Company name in the "Company" field. This field is optional, but it helps us understand your context and provide more accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. First Name (required)
*   The "First Name" field is used to capture the first name of the person submitting the form. Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### 2. Last Name (optional)
*   The "Last Name" field is used to capture the last name of the person submitting the form. You may leave this field blank if you do not wish to provide it.

### 3. Email (required)
*   The "Email" field is used to capture the email address of the person submitting the form. Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly, as we will use this email to respond to your inquiry.

### 4. Phone (optional)
*   The "Phone" field is used to capture the phone number of the person submitting the form. You may leave this field blank if you do not wish to provide it.

### 5. Message (required)
*   The "Message" field is used to capture the message or question you have for the Executive Insight Consulting team. Please be as detailed as possible in your message, so we can better understand your inquiry.

### 6. Company (optional)
*   The "Company" field is used to capture the name of the company or organization you are representing. You may leave this field blank if you are not representing a company or organization.

## Tips
*   Please make sure to fill out all required fields (First Name, Email, and Message) to ensure we can effectively respond to your inquiry.
*   If you are a client or partner of Executive Insight Consulting, please include your account information or reference number in your message.
*   Our team will respond to your inquiry as soon as possible, usually within 24 to 48 hours. If you have not heard back from us, please check your email and phone again.
