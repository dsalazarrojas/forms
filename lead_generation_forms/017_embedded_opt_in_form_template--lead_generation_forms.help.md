# embedded_opt_in_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from visitors for embedded opt-in purposes. It will help you capture relevant data for lead generation and nurturing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the <strong>name</strong> field.
2. Enter your email address in the <strong>email</strong> field.
3. Provide your <strong>company</strong> name.
4. Enter your job title in the <strong>job_title</strong> field.
5. Enter your phone number in the <strong>phone</strong> field.
6. Choose whether you want to opt-in for our newsletter and marketing communications.
7. Select if you're interested in other specific opt-in options, if available.
8. Enter any additional information you'd like to share with us in the <strong>other_opt_in_options</strong> field.
9. Finally, confirm that the tool you were assigned by our organization is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: <i> (<name>)</i> - Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email**: <i> (<email>)</i> - Enter a valid email address to be contacted by us.
* **Company**: <i> (<company>)</i> - Enter your company or organization name.
* **Job Title**: <i> (<job_title>)</i> - Enter your job title or position.
* **Phone**: <i> (<phone>)</i> - Enter your phone number to be contacted.
* **Opt-in**: <i> (<opt_in>)</i> - Choose whether you want to opt-in for our newsletter and marketing communications.
* **Marketing Opt-in**: <i> (<marketing_opt_in>)</i> - Select if you're interested in marketing communications.
* **Other Opt-in**: <i> (<other_opt_in>)</i> - Choose if you're interested in other specific opt-in options, if available.
* **Other Opt-in Options**: <i> (<other_opt_in_options>)</i> - Enter any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **Assigned Tool**: <i> (<assigned_tool>)</i> - Confirm the tool you were assigned by our organization.
* **Created Date**: <i> (<created_at>)</i> - This is a system-generated field and will be filled in by the system.
*   **Select Multiple Options**: <i>(<select_multiple>)</i> - Select from the given options to choose your interest in different opt-in options.
