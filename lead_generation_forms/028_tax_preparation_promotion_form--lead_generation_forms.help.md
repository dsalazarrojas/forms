# Tax Preparation Promotion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Preparation Promotion Form is designed to help our team follow up with you to discuss your specific tax preparation needs and schedule a call to discuss further.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Fill out the form with your information and select the relevant options for each field.
* Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to help our team better understand your needs.
* Review each field carefully to ensure that you understand what is required and what is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tax Preparation Promotion Form**: This field is a text input field where you can enter a brief description of the form. Please don't enter anything here.
* **Your Name**: This field is for your name, which will be used to address you in our follow-up communication. Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email**: This field is for your email address, which will be used to contact you about your tax preparation needs. Please enter a valid email address that you check frequently.
* **Phone**: This field is for your phone number, which will be used to contact you about your tax preparation needs. Please enter a phone number that you check regularly.
* **What type of tax service are you looking for?**: This field is a select-one dropdown menu where you can choose the type of tax service you are interested in. Please select one of the options provided: Income Tax, Estate Tax, Tax Planning, or Other.
* **Schedule a call with our team**: This field is for scheduling a call with our team to discuss your tax preparation needs. Please enter a time in 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Uploads**: This field is not currently in use.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to help our team better understand your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Our team will follow up with you promptly after receiving your completed form.
