# sweepstakes_entry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to enter a sweepstakes contest by providing essential contact information and agreement to terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Provide your Company name.
6. Enter your street address.
7. Enter your City.
8. Enter your State.
9. Enter your Zip code.
10. Select your Country.
11. Choose how you heard about the contest.
12. Enter any Social Links you'd like to share.
13. You can optionally add any Comments about your participation in the sweepstakes.
14. Read and Agree to Terms and Conditions.
15. Select your agreement to the Privacy Pledge.
16. Choose your agreement to the Sweepstakes Rules.
17. Agree or disagree to the Terms of participation in the contest.
18. Enter the Date.
19. Enter the Time.

* **Company** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Address** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter your street address where you can be reached.
* **City** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you can be reached.
* **State** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter the state where you can be reached.
* **Zip** (`<name>`, `number`, required): Enter the zip code where you can be reached.
* **Country** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Select your country of residence.
