# Consent Of Media Release - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a consent form for media release, used to obtain permission from participants to use their likeness, voice, or image in media. The form is a legal document that confirms the participant's understanding and consent for the use of media in various contexts.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Date of Media Release**: Select the date when the media will be released or published.
2. **Participant Name**: Enter the full name of the person being captured in the media.
3. **Email Address**: Enter the contact email address of the participant or their representative.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter the contact phone number of the participant or their representative (optional).
5. **Address**: Enter the mailing address of the participant or their representative (optional).
6. **Date of Birth**: Select the participant's date of birth for age verification purposes.
7. **Is Participant a Minor**: Check if the participant is under 18 years of age.
8. **Parent or Guardian Name**: If the participant is a minor, enter the full name of their parent or guardian.
9. **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the contact email address of the participant's parent or guardian (optional).
10. **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the participant's parent or guardian (optional).
11. **Type of Media**: Select the type of media that will be used (e.g., photographs, video recording, audio recording, social media posts, website content, etc.).
12. **Event or Project Name**: Enter the name of the associated event or project.
13. **Event Date**: Select the date when the media was captured.
14. **Event Location**: Enter the location where the media was captured.
15. **Purpose of Media Use**: Select how the media will be used (e.g., marketing and advertising, social media, website, annual report, press release, internal communications, educational materials, fundraising, news media, etc.).
16. **Duration of Use**: Select the duration for which the media will be used (e.g., one-time use only, 1 year, 3 years, 5 years, indefinitely, until consent withdrawn).
17. **Geographic Scope**: Select where the media will be distributed (e.g., local only, regional, national, international, worldwide).
18. **Will You Receive Compensation**: Check if you will receive compensation for the media release.
19. **Compensation Details**: Describe the compensation if applicable.
20. **Do You Require Approval Before Use**: Check if you would like to review the media before publication.
21. **Name Attributions**: Select how you should be credited (e.g., full name, first name and last initial, first name only, anonymous, title or role).
22. **Any Restrictions on Use**: Enter any limitations on the use of the media.
23. **Right to Withdraw Understood**: Check if you understand that you can withdraw your consent.
24. **Terms and Conditions Understood**: Check if you understand all terms and conditions.
25. **Consent Given**: Select to confirm your consent to the media release.
26. **Participant Signature**: Type your full name as a signature.
27. **Parent or Guardian Signature**: If the participant is a minor, type the parent or guardian's full name as a signature (required if applicable).
28. **Organization Representative**: Enter the name of the organization representative.
29. **Representative Signature**: Type the representative's full name as a signature.

## Tips

* Make sure to read and understand all terms and conditions before signing the consent form.
* If you are a participant, you must sign the consent form to confirm your agreement.
* If you are a parent or guardian, you must sign the consent form if the participant is a minor.
* Ensure that the organization representative signs the consent form on behalf of the organization.
* Keep a copy of the signed consent form for your records.
