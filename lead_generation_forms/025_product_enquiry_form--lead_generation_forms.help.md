# Product Enquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from potential customers or partners interested in our products or services. Please fill it out to the best of your ability, and a member of our team will review and respond to your submission.

## How To Complete This Form
- Make sure you have all the necessary information before completing the form.
- Fill out each field as per the guidelines below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **title** (`product_enquiry_form_page_1`, text, required): 
Enter a brief title for your enquiry.
* **Name** (name, text, required): 
Please provide your name so we can address you correctly.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, required): 
Enter your 11-digit contact number for us to reach you.
* **Email** (email, email, required): 
Provide a valid email address so we can contact you.
* **Product Enquiry** (enquiry, text, required): 
Describe the product or service you're interested in, and we'll get in touch with you to discuss further.
* **Message** (message, note, required): 
Leave a clear message about your enquiry. This can help us better understand your needs and concerns.
* **Company** (company, text, required): 
Provide the name of your company for reference.
* **Department** (department, text, required): 
State the department or team you're representing.
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, required): 
Let us know your job title for a more personalized experience.
* **Company Website** (company_website, text, required): 
Share the URL of your company's website, if applicable.
* **Company Phone** (company_phone, text, required): 
Provide your company's contact phone number, also with 11 digits.
* **Company Email** (company_email, email, required): 
Add your company email address for communication.
* **Product Enquiry Form Page 2-13** (`product_enquiry_form_page_2`, `product_enquiry_form_page_3`, `product_enquiry_form_page_4`, `product_enquiry_form_page_5`, `product_enquiry_form_page_6`, `product_enquiry_form_page_7`, `product_enquiry_form_page_8`, `product_enquiry_form_page_9`, `product_enquiry_form_page_10`, `product_enquiry_form_page_11`, `product_enquiry_form_page_12`, `product_enquiry_form_page_13`, text, required):

* **Product Enquiry Form Page 14** (`product_enquiry_form_page_14`, text, required): 
Leave any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.
