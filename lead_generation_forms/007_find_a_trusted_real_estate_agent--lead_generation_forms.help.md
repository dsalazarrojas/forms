# find-a-trusted-real-estate-agent - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help find a trusted real estate agent for the lead. The form collects information about the lead such as their personal details, preferred contact method, and other relevant information that can be used to match them with the most suitable agent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select your preferred location from the "Preferred Location" field.
5. Choose the type of home you are interested in from the "Home Type" field (House, Condo, Townhouse, Apartment).
6. Enter your budget in the "Budget" field.
7. Select your desired moving date from the "Desired Moving Date" field.
8. Choose your available time for the consultation in the "Time Available" field.
9. Select the type of agent you prefer from the "Preferred Agent Type" field (Real Estate Agent, Property Manager, Broker).
10. Choose your preferred language from the "Preferred Language" field (English, French, Spanish, German, Chinese).
11. Confirm if you have a real estate license in the "Real Estate License" field.
12. Indicate the source of your lead in the "Lead Source" field (Online, Offline).
13. If you'd like, provide additional information about your lead in the "Marketing Source" field.
14. Select your preferred contact method from the "Preferred Contact Method" field (Phone, Email, Text).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Preferred Location**: Choose your preferred location from the list (e.g., New York City).
* **Home Type**: Select the type of home you are interested in (House, Condo, Townhouse, Apartment).
* **Budget**: Enter your budget for the home purchase.
* **Desired Moving Date**: Select your desired moving date (e.g., 1 month from now).
* **Time Available**: Choose the time available for the consultation (e.g., 9 AM).
* **Preferred Agent Type**: Select the type of agent you prefer (Real Estate Agent, Property Manager, Broker).
* **Preferred Language**: Choose your preferred language (English, French, Spanish, German, Chinese).
* **Real Estate License**: Confirm if you have a real estate license.
* **Lead Source**: Indicate the source of your lead (Online, Offline).
* **Marketing Source**: Provide additional information about your lead if needed.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, Text).
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about your lead if you'd like.
