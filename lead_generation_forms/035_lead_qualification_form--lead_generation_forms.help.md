# Lead Qualification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead Qualification Form is used to gather information about a lead's qualification status, industry, company, phone number, and other relevant details. It is used by authorized personnel to assess whether a lead is qualified and ready for the sales process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about the lead.
2.  Make sure that you have permission to modify the lead's status and other fields.
3.  Review and verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Lead Qualification Form** (`1`): This is the title of the form. You do not need to fill this in.
*   **Lead Name** (`2`): Enter the name of the lead.
*   **Email** (`3`): Enter the email address of the lead.
*   **Phone** (`4`): Enter the phone number of the lead.
*   **Company** (`5`): Enter the name of the lead's company.
*   **Industry** (`6`): Enter the lead's industry.
*   **Status** (`7`): Select one of the following options:
    +   **Qualified**: The lead is qualified and ready for the sales process.
    +   **Not Qualified**: The lead is not qualified.
    +   **Qualified but not yet ready**: The lead is partially qualified but not yet ready for the sales process.
*   **Reason for Ineligibility** (`8`): Select all that apply:
    +   **Sales Team**: This lead is not qualified because of the sales team's decision.
    +   **Not Ready**: The lead is not ready due to some other reason.
    +   **Not Qualified**: The lead is not qualified.
    +   **Qualified but not yet ready**: The lead is partially qualified but not yet ready.
*   **Assigned Sales Person** (`9`): Check this box if the lead has been assigned to a sales person.
*   **Sales Person Note** (`10`): Enter any notes about the sales person assigned to the lead.
*   **Status Updated** (`11`): Enter the date when the status was last updated.
*   **Reason for Status Update** (`12`): Enter the reason why the status was updated.
*   **Sales Person Update** (`13`): Enter any notes about the update.
*   **Lead Qualification Date** (`14`): Enter the date when the lead was last qualified.
*   **Next Step** (`15`): Check this box to indicate if this is the next step in the sales process.
*   **Lead Qualification Form ID** (`16`): You do not need to fill this in.

**Tips**

*   Make sure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
*   Review and verify the information before submitting.
*   Do not modify the lead's status without permission.
*   Do not fill out the form if the lead is already in the qualification process.
