# Retail Digital Loyalty Demo Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from businesses that are interested in participating in a retail digital loyalty demo. The goal is to gather essential details to facilitate the demo process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your business details.
2. Select your preferred demo dates.
3. Enter your contact person and contact email.
4. Provide your contact phone number.
5. Answer questions about your store type and demo dates.
6. Read and agree to the consent terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Details**: Enter your business name and other relevant details to help us understand your business.
* **Preferred Dates**: Select one or more dates for the demo. This will help us schedule the demo accordingly.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and title of the person we should contact for the demo.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Store Type**: Select the type of store you have (Yes or No).
* **Store Type Options (None)**: This field is not required. Do not worry about it.
* **Store Type Options (Multiple) (None)**: This field is also not required. Do not worry about it.
* **Demo Dates**: Enter the dates of the demo. This field is a date picker for easier date input.
* **Consent**: Read and agree to the consent terms. This is an important step before proceeding with the demo.
* **Consent Options (None)**: This field is not required. Do not worry about it.
