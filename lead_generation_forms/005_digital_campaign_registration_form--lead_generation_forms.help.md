# Digital Campaign Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Campaign Registration Form is designed to collect information from users interested in learning more about a digital campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields:

1. Provide your First Name.
2. Enter your Email.
3. Enter your Job Title.
4. Enter your Department.
5. Enter your Company.
6. Enter your Phone number.
7. Select the size of your Company.
8. Select your Job Function.
9. Select the tools assigned to you (you may select one or multiple tools).
10. Select the second tool assigned to you (you may select one or multiple tools).
11. Select the lead source of your campaign.
12. Select the status of your campaign.
13. Select the stage of your campaign.
14. Select the Lead Qualifier for your campaign.
15. Select the second Lead Qualifier for your campaign.
16. Select the third tool assigned to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name. This field is not required.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This field is not required, but it will be used to communicate with you about your digital campaign.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title. This field is not required.
* **Department**: Enter your department. This field is not required.
* **Company**: Enter your company. This field is not required.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This field is not required.
* **Company Size**: Select the size of your company from the options provided. This field is not required.
* **Job Function**: Select your job function from the options provided. This field is not required.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to you. You may select one or multiple tools. This field is not required.
* **Assigned Tool 2**: Select the second tool assigned to you. You may select one or multiple tools. This field is not required.
* **Lead Source**: Select the lead source of your campaign. This field is not required.
* **Lead Status**: Select the status of your campaign. This field is not required.
* **Lead Stage**: Select the stage of your campaign. This field is not required.
* **Lead Qualifier**: Select the lead qualifier for your campaign. This field is not required.
* **Prophets Tool**: Select the Prophets tool. This field is not required.
* **Lead Qualifier 2**: Select the second lead qualifier for your campaign. This field is not required.
* **Assigned Tool 3**: Select the third tool assigned to you. You may select one or multiple tools. This field is not required.

## Tips

* Double-check your selections to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
* If you're not sure whether you're eligible to fill out this form, please contact our support team for clarification.
