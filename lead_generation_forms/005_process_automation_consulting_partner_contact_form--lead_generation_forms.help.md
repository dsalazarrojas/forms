# process_automation_consulting_partner_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used by potential consulting partners to share their interests and requirements with our team. This information will be used to guide our team in following up with you and providing relevant information about our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information in the "User Info" section, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. In the "Contact Info" section, provide your email address, phone number, and any other contact information you wish to share.
3. In the "Company Info" section, provide information about your company, such as its name and address.
4. Select one or more interests that align with our services from the list provided.
5. In the "Requirements" section, provide any specific requirements or needs you have for our consulting services.
6. If you have any additional information to share, you can add notes in the "Notes" section.
7. Finally, select the name of the person you would like to receive follow-up information from.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info (Name, Text, Required: false)**: Please provide your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info (Text, Required: false)**: Provide your email address, phone number, and any other contact information you wish to share.
* **Company Info (Text, Required: false)**: Provide information about your company, such as its name and address.
* **Interests (Select One or More, Required: true)**: Select one or more interests that align with our services, such as Automation, Artificial Intelligence, Data Analytics, or Process Automation.
* **Requirements (Text, Required: false)**: In this section, provide any specific requirements or needs you have for our consulting services.
* **Notes (Note, Required: false)**: If you have any additional information to share, you can add it here.
* **Send To (Select One, Required: true)**: Select the name of the person you would like to receive follow-up information from.
* **Follow up (Number, Required: false)**: This field is not currently applicable as per the form's current configuration.
