# Home Improvement Customer Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Improvement Customer Inquiry Form is designed to gather information from customers about their home improvement projects, enabling the company's customer service team to better understand the customer's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of project you are inquiring about from the dropdown menu on **Page 1: What is your project about?**
2. Provide your name in **Page 2: What is your name?**
3. Specify your budget for the project in **Page 3: What is your budget for the project?** (Note: This field is a numerical value)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your project about?** (`home_improvement_customer_inquiry_form_page_1`, Select Multiple, Required: False): This field allows you to choose the type of project you are inquiring about. Please select one or more of the options that best describe your project.
  - Home Addition
  - Bathroom Remodel
  - Bedroom Addition
  - Basement Remodel
  - Kitchen Remodel
  - Roofing Project
- **What is your name?** (`home_improvement_customer_inquiry_form_page_2`, Text, Required: False): This field is for you to enter your name.
- **What is your budget for the project?** (`home_improvement_customer_inquiry_form_page_3`, Number, Required: False): This field is for you to specify your budget for the project. Please enter a numerical value.

## Tips
- Please make sure to provide accurate information about your project so that our customer service team can better understand your needs.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact our customer service team for assistance.
- Our goal is to provide you with the best possible service, and we appreciate your cooperation in providing us with clear and concise information about your project.
