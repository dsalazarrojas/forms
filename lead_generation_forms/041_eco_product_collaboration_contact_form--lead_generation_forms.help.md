# Eco Product Collaboration Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Eco Product Collaboration Contact Form is designed to collect contact information and collaboration details from potential collaborators for eco-friendly products. This form helps gather essential data to facilitate smooth communication and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Provide specific details about your collaboration inquiry, such as your interests and goals.
3. Write a brief message describing your collaboration inquiry.
4. If applicable, upload a relevant file to support your collaboration request.
5. Check the "Agreement" field to indicate your willingness to collaborate under the specified terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name and email address to ensure we can contact you about your collaboration inquiry.
* **Collaboration Details**: Describe your collaboration interests and goals to help us understand your needs better.
* **Message**: Write a brief message detailing your collaboration inquiry. This is your chance to share any additional information about your project or idea.
* **File Upload**: If you have a relevant file to share, such as a project document or a product image, upload it here. This can help us better understand your collaboration request.
* **Agreement**: Check this field to indicate your agreement to collaborate under the specified terms. This is an essential step to ensure we can move forward with your collaboration inquiry.
