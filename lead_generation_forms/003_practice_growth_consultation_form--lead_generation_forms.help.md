# Practice Growth Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Practice Growth Consultation Form is designed to collect information from leads (potential clients or customers) about their practice goals, consultation preferences, and growth status. This form helps identify potential leads and assess their practice growth opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as instructed. Make sure to answer all questions accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the lead's details are captured correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Details** (1): This is the section where you can add any additional information about the lead, such as their name, email, or phone number.
* **Practice Goals** (2): Describe the goals of the lead's practice, such as increasing revenue, improving customer satisfaction, or expanding services.
* **Consultation Date** (3): Select the date for the consultation, if scheduled or desired. This field is used to plan and coordinate the consultation process.
* **Consultant Information** (4): Provide your contact information as a consultant, including your name, email, and phone number. This allows the lead to contact you for further discussions.
* **Practice Information** (5): Describe the details of the practice, including the type of services offered, target market, and competition.
* **Lead Status** (6): Indicate the current status of the lead, whether they are a lead (interested) or no lead (not interested).
* **Consultation Schedule** (7): Select a date and time for the consultation, if scheduled.
* **Follow Up** (8): Add any additional information about the follow-up process, such as next steps or reminders.
* **Practice Growth Status** (9): Assess the growth status of the practice, using the options Good, Fair, or Bad. This helps identify areas for improvement.
