# Download Budget Wedding Ideas - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help users provide their budget information for a wedding and provide some additional details about their wedding. The form is used to gather essential information that will help us assist you better in planning your special day.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Start by filling in the "What is your budget for your wedding?" field with your estimated budget for your wedding. You can enter a numerical value for this field. 
*   If you are unsure about your budget or need help, please don't hesitate to ask for assistance. 
*   In the "What are your wedding details?" field, enter a brief description of your wedding. This can include details like number of guests, venue, and dress code.
*   In the "How do you like to be contacted?" field, select one or multiple ways that you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Mobile).
*   If you would like to receive planning information from us, select "True" for this option.
*   Once you have filled all the necessary information, click the "Submit" button to send your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **What is your budget for your wedding?** (`budget_wedding_ideas`, number, required/optional): Enter your estimated budget for your wedding.
*   **What are your wedding details?** (`wedding_details`, text, required/optional): Describe your wedding in a few words, including details like number of guests, venue, and dress code.
*   **How do you like to be contacted?** (`contact_details`, select_multiple, required): Select one or multiple ways that you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Mobile).
*   **Can we send you planning info?** (`planning_info`, select_one, required/optional): Select "True" if you would like to receive planning information from us.

## Tips
Please fill in as much information as you can to get the best help from us. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask. 

Note: This form does not collect any sensitive or personal data that could compromise your privacy. All information collected is strictly for wedding planning purposes only. 

We will review and get back to you soon after receiving your form submission. If you have any questions or need help with anything else, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
