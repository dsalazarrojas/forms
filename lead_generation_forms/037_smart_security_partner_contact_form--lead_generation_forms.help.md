# Smart Security Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Security Partner Contact Form is designed to collect contact information from potential smart security partners. This form helps our team identify and engage with partners who can assist in the development and implementation of the smart security system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand the information required.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for the Submission Time, Assigned To, Inquiry Type, and Follow Up fields.
4. You can skip the Notes field if it's not required or if you have nothing to add.
5. Enter a valid email address in the email field (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contact Information (Page 1)
* **First and Last Name** (`smart_security_partner_contact_form_page_1`, text, required): Fill in your name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Company Name** (`smart_security_partner_contact_form_page_2`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Address 1** (`smart_security_partner_contact_form_page_3`, text, required): Enter your work or business address.
* **Address 2** (`smart_security_partner_contact_form_page_4`, text, required): Enter your work or business address (if different from address 1).
* **Phone** (`smart_security_partner_contact_form_page_12`, text, optional): Enter a valid phone number (if you have one).
* **Email** (`smart_security_partner_contact_form_page_11`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address (if you have one).

### Company Details (Page 2)
* **Industry** (`smart_security_partner_contact_form_page_3`, text, required): Enter the industry your company operates in.
* **Website** (`smart_security_partner_contact_form_page_4`, text, optional): Enter your company's website (if you have one).
* **Social Media** (`smart_security_partner_contact_form_page_5`, text, optional): Enter your company's social media handles (if you have any).

### Inquiry Description (Page 3)
* **Inquiry Description** (`smart_security_partner_contact_form_page_3`, text, required): Describe your inquiry in a few sentences, explaining how your company can help with the smart security system.

### Submission Time (Page 5)
* **Submission Time** (`smart_security_partner_contact_form_page_5`, select_one, required): Select how long ago the inquiry was submitted:
	+ 1 hour ago
	+ 2-4 hours ago
	+ 5-24 hours ago
	+ More than 24 hours ago

### Assigned To (Page 6)
* **Assigned To** (`smart_security_partner_contact_form_page_6`, select_one, required): Select who is assigned to follow up with you:
	+ chatjimmy
	+ other

### Inquiry Type (Page 7)
* **Inquiry Type** (`smart_security_partner_contact_form_page_7`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of inquiry:
	+ New
	+ Existing

### Follow Up (Page 8)
* **Follow Up** (`smart_security_partner_contact_form_page_8`, select_multiple, required): Select how you would like to be followed up:
	+ Follow Up
	+ False

### Notes (Page 9)
* **Notes** (`smart_security_partner_contact_form_page_9`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your inquiry.

### Submission (Page 10)
* **Submission** (`smart_security_partner_contact_form_page_10`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your submission.
 
## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure we can follow up with you effectively.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, use the Notes field.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact our team for assistance.
