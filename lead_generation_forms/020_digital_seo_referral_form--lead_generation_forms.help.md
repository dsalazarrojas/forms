# digital_seo_referral_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from leads generated through various digital SEO referral channels. It helps us track and categorize leads by their source, status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the correct lead source from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose the relevant lead status.
4. Fill out the contact details and other information as needed.
5. If the lead is from a referral, provide additional referral details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Information** (`lead_information`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the lead.
* **Referral Details** (`referral_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the referral that may be helpful.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter the contact details of the person or organization related to the lead.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, select_one, required): Select where the lead originated from:
	+ Google
	+ Google Ads
	+ Facebook Ads
	+ Email
	+ Word of Mouth
	+ Social Media
	+ Referral
	+ Website
	+ Print Media
* **Lead Status** (`lead_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the lead:
	+ New
	+ Qualified
	+ Referred
	+ Converted
	+ Lost
* **Lead Type** (`lead_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of lead:
	+ New Lead
	+ Existing Client
	+ Hot Lead
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the person or organization related to the lead.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the person or organization related to the lead.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person or organization related to the lead.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or organization related to the lead.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company related to the lead.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the person or organization related to the lead.
* **Job Title (Other)** (`job_title_other`, text, optional): If the job title is not listed, enter the actual job title.
* **Lead Source Note** (`lead_source_note`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the lead source.
* **Referral Note** (`referral_note`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the referral.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* Use the correct lead source and status to help with categorization and follow-up.
* If you're unsure about any field, reach out to your team for clarification.
