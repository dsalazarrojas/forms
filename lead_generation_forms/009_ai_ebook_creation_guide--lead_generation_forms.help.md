# AI EBook Creation Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to guide users in creating a customized AI eBook based on their specific requirements. The form captures essential details such as the submitter's name, contact email, eBook topic, target audience, desired length, key points to cover, tone and style preferences, and additional resources or references.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the submitter's name and contact email address in the corresponding fields. This will help us follow up on your request and provide any necessary information.
2. Specify the main topic or theme of the eBook in the "Ebook topic" field.
3. Identify the target audience for the eBook, choosing from the options provided: Beginners, Intermediate, Advanced, or Executives.
4. Provide an approximate word count or page count for the desired eBook length.
5. List the key points to cover in the "Key points to cover" field, using bullet points for clarity.
6. Specify your tone and style preferences for the eBook, selecting from the options: Conversational, Formal, Technical, or Marketing.
7. If necessary, attach additional resources or references that support your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter name** (`submitter_name`, text, Optional): Enter your name and contact information for follow up purposes.
* **Contact email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter your email address for follow up and necessary information.
* **Ebook topic** (`ebook_topic`, text, Required): Describe the main topic or theme of the eBook.
* **Target audience** (`target_audience`, select_one, Required): Choose the primary audience and level of expertise for the eBook.
* **Desired length** (`desired_length`, number, Optional): Provide an approximate word count or page count for the desired eBook length.
* **Key points to cover** (`key_points`, text, Required): List the essential topics to include in the eBook, using bullet points for clarity.
* **Tone and style** (`tone_and_style`, select_one, Optional): Select the tone and style preferences for the eBook from the provided options.
* **Additional resources** (`additional_resources`, note, Optional): Attach any supporting links or attachments to your request.
