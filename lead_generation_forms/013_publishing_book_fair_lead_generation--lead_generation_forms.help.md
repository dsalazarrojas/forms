# Publishing Book Fair Lead Generation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Publishing Book Fair Lead Generation form is designed to gather information about potential leads for the book fair event. This form helps the event organizers to track leads and follow up with them before, during, and after the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "lead name" and "email" fields.
2. Select the event interests that apply to you.
3. If you are interested in attending other events, select "Yes" in the "Other event" field.
4. Choose "Yes" or "No" in the "Follow-up before the fair" field to indicate whether you would like to be contacted before the event.
5. Select the publishing fair date and time that you are interested in attending.
6. Choose the location of the publishing fair that you would like to attend (Online or Offline).
7. Enter the name of the person responsible for following up with you in the "Lead Owner" field.
8. Enter the Lead Owner's email address in the "Lead Owner's Email" field.
9. Enter any follow-up message you would like to be sent to you before the fair in the "Follow-up Message" field.
10. Finally, provide any additional notes that you would like to share with the event team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Publishing Book Fair Lead Generation**: This is the title of the form.
* **Lead name**: Enter your name to be associated with this lead.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you about the event.
* **Event interests**: Select the events that interest you, such as Books, Author Talks, Publishing News, and Industry Insights.
* **Other event**: If you are interested in attending other events, select "Yes".
* **Follow-up before the fair**: Choose "Yes" if you would like to be contacted before the fair to discuss your interests further.
* **Publishing fair date**: Select the date of the publishing fair that you are interested in attending.
* **Publishing fair time**: Select the time of the publishing fair that you are interested in attending.
* **Publishing fair location**: Choose whether you would like to attend the publishing fair online or offline.
* **Publishing fair organiser**: Enter the name of the person responsible for organizing the publishing fair.
* **Company**: Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Publishing fair URL**: Enter the URL of the publishing fair (if applicable).
* **Lead source**: Select where you heard about the lead (Website, Social Media, Print, or Other).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes you would like to share with the event team.
* **Lead Owner**: Enter the name of the person responsible for following up with you.
* **Lead Owner's Email**: Enter the email address of the Lead Owner.
* **Follow-up Message**: Enter any follow-up message you would like to be sent to you before the fair.
* **Lead Generation Form**: Enter any additional notes you would like to share with the event team.
