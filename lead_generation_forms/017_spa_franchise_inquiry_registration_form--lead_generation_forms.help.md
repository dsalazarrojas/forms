# Spa Franchise Inquiry Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from prospective franchisees to understand their interests and needs for franchising opportunities. It helps us assess their fit for our spa franchise model and guide the next steps in the process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page to make sure you understand what is expected of you.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all necessary information.
3. Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page** (`main_page`): This page welcomes you to the form and provides an overview of what to expect.
* **Contact Form** (`contact_form`): Please select all contact methods that apply to you. You can choose one or more options.
	+ Option 1: Phone
	+ Option 2: Email
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify in the `spa_franchise_inquiry_question_1` field)
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Enter your contact information, including name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter a valid email address associated with your contact information.
* **Website** (`website`): If you have a professional website, enter the URL.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`): Select your preferred social media channel to contact you.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`): If you have an existing business, enter its name.
* **Number of Franchised Locations** (`number_of_franchised_locations`): If applicable, enter the number of locations you have franchised under your brand.
* **Location Zip** (`location_zip`): Enter the zip code of your primary location.
* **Franchise Model** (`franchise_model`): Select the franchise model that best describes your business.
* **Franchise Fee** (`franchise_fee`): If applicable, enter the fee you are expecting for our franchise opportunity.
* **Franchise Territory** (`franchise_territory`): If you have a specific territory in mind, enter it.
