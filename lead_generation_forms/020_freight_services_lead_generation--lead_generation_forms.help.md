# Freight Services Lead Generation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to generate leads for freight services, which helps our business operations in managing customer inquiries and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the contact information section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. If you have a company, please provide it in the Company field.
3. On the second page, specify the shipment details:
   * Service Type: Choose the type of service you require (Less Than Truckload, Less Than Truckload & LTL, Full Truckload, Intermodal, Brokerage, Warehousing, or Other).
   * Origin and Destination details: Provide your city and state for both the origin and destination of the shipment.
   * Shipment Date and Time: Enter the desired shipment date and time.
   * Shipment Type: Choose the type of shipment you're making (Less Than Truckload, Less Than Truckload & LTL, Full Truckload, Intermodal, Brokerage, or Other).
   * Shipment Dimensions: Enter the weight, length, width, and height of the shipment.
   * Shipment Notes: Add any additional comments or instructions about the shipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name (if any).
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of freight service you need.
* **Origin City** (`origin_city`, text, optional): Enter the city for the shipment origin.
* **Origin State** (`origin_state`, text, optional): Enter the state for the shipment origin.
* **Destination City** (`destination_city`, text, optional): Enter the city for the shipment destination.
* **Destination State** (`destination_state`, text, optional): Enter the state for the shipment destination.
* **Shipment Date** (`shipment_date`, date, optional): Enter the desired shipment date.
* **Shipment Time** (`shipment_time`, time, optional): Enter the desired shipment time.
* **Shipment Type** (`shipment_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of shipment you're making.
* **Shipment Weight** (`shipment_weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the shipment.
* **Shipment Length** (`shipment_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the shipment.
* **Shipment Width** (`shipment_width`, number, optional): Enter the width of the shipment.
* **Shipment Height** (`shipment_height`, number, optional): Enter the height of the shipment.
* **Shipment Notes** (`shipment_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or instructions about the shipment.
