# Insurance Partner Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Partner Referral Form is used to gather information about potential customers who are referred to us by our partners.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the lead source from the options provided or select "Other" and provide a brief explanation of where the lead came from in the "Lead Source Other" field.
2. Enter the name of the partner who referred the customer.
3. Enter the partner's email address.
4. Enter the partner's phone number.
5. Add any additional notes about the partner or customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the lead source from the options provided or select "Other" and provide a brief explanation of where the lead came from.
* **Lead Source Other** (`lead_source_other`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief explanation of where the lead came from if you selected "Other".
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the partner who referred the customer.
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the partner's email address.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the partner's phone number.
* **Partner Notes** (`partner_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the partner or customer.
* **User Message** (`user_message`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the lead or opportunity.

## Tips
- Make sure you have permission from the partner to share their information.
- Only fill out this form for partners that you have an established relationship with.
- If you have already filled out this form for the same partner, please do not fill it out again.
