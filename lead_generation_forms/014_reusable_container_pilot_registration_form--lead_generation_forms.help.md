# Reusable Container Pilot Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants in the Reusable Container Pilot program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the participant details such as name, contact person, email, phone number, and organization.
2. Select your role in the pilot program from the options provided.
3. If you are registering for multiple locations, select the applicable locations.
4. Enter the pilot name and submission date and time.
5. Add any relevant notes about your registration.
6. Finally, select your participant type and status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: The name of the participant registering for the pilot program.
* **Contact Person**: The name of the person to contact for further information.
* **Email**: The email address of the participant or contact person.
* **Phone**: The phone number of the participant or contact person.
* **Organization**: The name of the organization or company represented by the participant.
* **Role**: The role of the participant in the pilot program (Participant, Team Member, or Team Lead).
* **Pilot Name**: The name of the pilot program being registered for.
* **Location**: The location(s) where the participant will be working on the pilot program (select multiple locations if applicable).
* **Submission Date**: The date of submission of the registration form.
* **Submission Time**: The time of submission of the registration form.
* **Note**: Any additional notes or comments about the registration.
* **Participant Type**: The type of participant (Type A, Type B, or Type C).
* **Participant Status**: The current status of the participant (Status A, Status B, or Status C).
