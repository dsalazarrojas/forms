# Lean Operations Strategy Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lean Operations Strategy Contact Form is a tool for collecting information from users who are interested in our services related to Lean operations strategy. This form helps us understand their needs and preferences, allowing our team to provide the best possible support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your contact information, including your Organization Name, Contact Name, Email, and Phone.
2. Specify your Job Title and the Department you belong to (if applicable).
3. Choose the type of support you are looking for:
	* Lean consulting for general consulting services
	* Process improvement for optimizing and improving processes
	* Strategy support for high-level strategy development and guidance
4. Write a message describing your inquiry and any specific needs or concerns you have.
5. Optionally, select the type of lead you are looking for:
	* Prophets (experts in specific areas of Lean operations)
	* Consultants (general Lean operations experts)
6. Select any relevant status of your lead:
	* New (initial inquiry)
	* In progress (ongoing project)
	* Closed (completed project)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (1): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Name** (2): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or correspondence.
* **Email** (3): Enter your professional email address.
* **Job Title** (4): Enter your job title within your organization (if applicable).
* **Company** (5): Enter your company name.
* **Department** (6): Enter your department within your organization (if applicable).
* **Phone** (7): Enter your phone number.
* **Request Type** (8): Choose one of the three options:
	+ Lean consulting (general consulting services)
	+ Process improvement (optimizing and improving processes)
	+ Strategy support (high-level strategy development and guidance)
* **Message** (9): Write a brief description of your inquiry, any specific needs or concerns you have, and any questions you'd like to ask.
* **Assigned Tool** (10): Enter any specific tool(s) you'd like to be assigned to your lead (if applicable).
* **Lead Type** (11): Choose one of the two options:
	+ Prophets (experts in specific areas of Lean operations)
	+ Consultants (general Lean operations experts)
* **Lead Status** (12): Choose any relevant status of your lead:
	+ New (initial inquiry)
	+ In progress (ongoing project)
	+ Closed (completed project)

## Tips
* Please fill out as much information as possible to ensure we can provide you with the best support and guidance.
* If you have any attachments or additional resources to support your request, you can upload them through the form or separately via email.
* Our team will review your form and get back to you promptly with next steps and any necessary follow-up questions.
