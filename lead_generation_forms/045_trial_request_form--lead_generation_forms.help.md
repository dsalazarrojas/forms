# Trial Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request a trial for a potential lead. It captures essential details about the lead, including their contact information, trial details, and lead generation preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the necessary fields with the lead's information, such as lead details, contact info, and preferred business type.
2. Select the trial start and end dates.
3. Choose the trial length and unit (e.g., days, weeks, or months).
4. Enter the lead's name and company name.
5. Provide a phone number and email address for the lead.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Generation Form**: This section is not visible to end-users. It's a placeholder for any internal information that may need to be filled out.
* **Lead Request Form**: This is the main form where you can enter the lead's details.
	+ **Lead Name**: Enter the name of the lead.
	+ **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company the lead represents.
* **Lead Contact Info**: Enter the lead's contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Business Type**: Select the type of business the lead represents (small, medium, or large).
* **Trial Start Date**: Select the start date of the trial.
* **Trial End Date**: Select the end date of the trial.
* **Trial Length**: Enter the length of the trial in the selected unit (days, weeks, or months).
* **Trial Length Unit**: Select the unit of the trial length (days, weeks, or months).
* **Lead Generation Form**: This section is not visible to end-users. It's a placeholder for any internal information that may need to be filled out.
* **Lead Details**: This section is not visible to end-users. It's a placeholder for any internal information that may need to be filled out.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the lead's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the lead's email address.
