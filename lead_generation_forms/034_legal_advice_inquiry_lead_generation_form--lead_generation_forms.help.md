# Legal Advice Inquiry Lead Generation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to generate leads for legal advice inquiries. It captures information from potential clients who are seeking legal guidance, allowing our team to follow up and provide relevant services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form only if you are requesting legal advice for a new inquiry.
2. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Describe your legal advice inquiry in the "Description" field.
6. Select the type of lead this is, such as "New", "Follow-up", or "Referral", in the "Lead Type" field.
7. Indicate the source of the lead, such as "Website", "Social Media", or "Word of Mouth", in the "Lead Source" field.
8. Choose the urgency level of your inquiry, such as "High", "Medium", or "Low", in the "Urgency Level" field.
9. Prioritize your lead by selecting "High", "Medium", or "Low" in the "Priority" field.
10. If applicable, select a follow-up date and time for your inquiry in the "Follow-up Date" and "Follow-up Time" fields.
11. Add any additional notes about your inquiry in the "Notes" field.
12. Finally, select the status of your lead, such as "New", "Qualified", "Qualified to Convert", "Qualified to Reject", "Qualified to Dismiss", or "Qualified to Follow-up", in the "Lead Status" field.
