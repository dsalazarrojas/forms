# Industrial IoT Integration Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Industrial IoT Integration Partner Contact Form is a tool used to gather information from potential partners interested in integrating their industrial IoT capabilities with our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of your company or organization in the "partner_inquiry" field.
2. Share your technical capabilities and how they align with our services in the "technical_capabilities" field.
3. Indicate your interest in potential collaboration leads by filling out the "collaboration_leads" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **partner_inquiry** (`partner_inquiry`, text, **required**): This field is for a brief description of your company or organization. Please provide a concise summary of your business or organization to help us understand your interest in our services.
* **technical_capabilities** (`technical_capabilities`, text, **required**): This field is for sharing your technical capabilities and how they align with our services. Be as detailed as possible about your technical strengths and how they can benefit our collaboration.
* **collaboration_leads** (`collaboration_leads`, text, **required**): This field is for indicating your interest in potential collaboration leads. Please specify any leads you are interested in pursuing and how our services can help you achieve your goals.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to provide the most accurate information.
- If you have any attachments or supporting documents, please attach them separately and mention their relevance to your submission.
- Our team will review your form submission and follow up with you to discuss further details.
