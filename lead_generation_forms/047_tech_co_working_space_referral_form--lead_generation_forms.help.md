# Tech Co Working Space Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tech Co Working Space Referral Form is designed to collect information from individuals who have a connection to the co-working space. This form helps us understand their preferences for follow-up contact and potential collaboration opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "What is your name" field.
2. Enter the name of the co-working space that you are referring.
3. Provide the name of the person you are referring (e.g., "John Doe").
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. Select your role in the co-working space (e.g., "Member", "Guest", "Team Lead").
6. Describe what you like about the co-working space in the "What do you like about this co working space?" field.
7. Indicate if you have already visited this co-working space ("True" or "False").
8. If you have visited this co-working space, select the date of your visit from the available options.
9. Choose whether you would like to be contacted for further follow-up ("True" or "False").
10. If you would like to be contacted, select a date and time for the follow-up.
11. Select a contact method (e.g., "Phone", "Email", "SMS").
12. Provide any additional notes about your referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Co Working Space Name**: Enter the name of the co-working space that you are referring.
* **Referral**: Enter the name of the person you are referring (e.g., "John Doe").
* **email**: Enter your email address to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Role**: Select your role in the co-working space (e.g., "Member", "Guest", "Team Lead").
* **What do you like about this co-working space?**: Describe what you like about the co-working space in a few sentences.
* **Have you already been to this co working space?**: Indicate whether you have already visited this co-working space ("True" or "False").
* **When did you visit this co working space**: Select the date of your visit from the available options.
* **Would you like to be contacted**: Choose whether you would like to be contacted for further follow-up ("True" or "False").
* **When would you like to be contacted**: Select a date and time for the follow-up.
* **At what time do you want to be contacted?**: Specify the time of day you would like to be contacted.
* **How would you like to be contacted?**: Select a contact method (e.g., "Phone", "Email", "SMS").
* **Do you have any other notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your referral.
