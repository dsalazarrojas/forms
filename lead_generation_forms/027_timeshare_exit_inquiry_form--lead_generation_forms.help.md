# Timeshare Exit Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who are inquiring about exiting their timeshare contracts. The collected data will be used to provide the customer with guidance and support in exiting their contract.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details in the text fields. These fields are for general information about your timeshare contract and your contact details.
2. Select the relevant options for your contract status, type, and property status from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter the contract date and value in the respective fields.
4. Select the exit date, if applicable.
5. Review your answers before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Generation Form**: (id: 1) This is a text field where you can enter a brief description of your lead generation inquiry.
* **Lead Details**: (id: 2) Enter your name, email address, and phone number in this text field.
* **Timeshare Contract**: (id: 3) Describe your current timeshare contract in this text field.
* **Timeshare Status**: (id: 4) Select the status of your timeshare contract from the dropdown menu. (e.g., Active, Inactive)
* **Contract Type**: (id: 5) Select whether your timeshare contract is 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Contract Status**: (id: 6) Select the status of your contract from the dropdown menu. (e.g., Active, Inactive)
* **Contract Date**: (id: 7) Enter the date of your contract in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contract Value**: (id: 8) Enter the value of your contract in numerical format.
* **Contact Name**: (id: 9) Enter your name in this text field.
* **Contact Email**: (id: 10) Enter your email address in this format: name@example.com.
* **Contact Phone**: (id: 11) Enter your phone number in this format: (123) 456-7890.
* **Property Type**: (id: 12) Select whether you have a 'Yes' or 'No' property.
* **Property Status**: (id: 13) Select the status of your property from the dropdown menu. (e.g., Active, Inactive)
* **Property Value**: (id: 14) Enter the value of your property in numerical format.
* **Exit Date**: (id: 15) Select the date of your exit from the calendar or enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for further assistance.
