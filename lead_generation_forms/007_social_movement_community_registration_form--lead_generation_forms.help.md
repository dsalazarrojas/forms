# Social Movement Community Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Movement Community Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining the movement. This form ensures that all registered individuals are over 18 years old and are not duplicates from previous registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Provide your **Email** in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the field labeled "Phone".
5. Briefly describe your **Occupation** in the field labeled "Occupation".
6. State your **Main Motivation** for joining the movement in the field labeled "Main Motivation".
7. Indicate whether you are a **Main Reporter** in the field labeled "Main Reporter". 
  If you are not a main reporter, leave this field blank.
8. Select your preferred **Contact Method** (Phone, Email, or Mail) in the field labeled "Contact Method".
9. Provide **Additional Info** about yourself in the field labeled "Additional Info".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you use and check regularly.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Occupation**: Briefly describe your current occupation or role.
* **Main Motivation**: Please state the reason why you want to join the Social Movement.
* **Main Reporter**: If you are a main reporter for the movement, select "True". Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Mail).
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information about yourself that you would like to share with the community.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
- If you are having trouble with any field, contact the movement administrators.
- If you have previously registered, do not attempt to register again.
