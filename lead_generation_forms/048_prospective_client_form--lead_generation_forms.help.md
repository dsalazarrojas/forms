# Prospective Client Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prospective Client Form is used to collect detailed information about a job opening from a prospective client. This form helps us understand the client's needs and provides a foundation for our internal team to analyze and respond to the client's inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the correct information about the job opening.
2. Provide detailed responses to the text fields, including the job description, job status, and other relevant information.
3. Select the correct options for the select fields, such as job level, company size, and industry.
4. Ensure you have filled in all required fields to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, required): Enter the name of the company that has the job opening.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person or the company's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person or the company's phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, optional): Enter the title of the job opening (e.g., Software Engineer, IT Manager).
* **Company** (`company`, required): Enter the name of the company.
* **Department** (`department`, optional): Enter the department or team responsible for the job opening (e.g., Sales, Marketing).
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, optional): Enter the function or role within the company (e.g., Software Development, Project Management).
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, required): Select the level of the job opening from the provided options (e.g., Software Engineer, IT Manager).
* **Job Level Other** (`other_job_level`, optional): If the job level is not listed, enter the correct level in this field.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, required): Select the size of the company from the provided options (e.g., 1-10, 11-50).
* **Industry** (`industry`, required): Select the industry of the company from the provided options (e.g., Finance, Marketing, Tech).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, optional): Enter the type of job opening (e.g., Full-time, Part-time).
* **Location** (`location`, required): Enter the location of the job opening.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, optional): Enter the status of the job opening (e.g., Open, Closed).
* **Job Status Other** (`other_job_status`, optional): If the job status is not listed, enter the correct status in this field.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, required): Enter a detailed description of the job opening.
* **Expected Salary** (`expected_salary`, optional): Enter the expected salary range for the job opening.
* **Salary Frequency** (`salary_frequency`, optional): Enter the frequency of the salary (e.g., Monthly, Annually).
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, optional): Enter the start date of the job opening.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, optional): Enter the end date of the job opening.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, optional): Enter the website of the company.
* **Job Title URL** (`job_title_url`, optional): Enter the URL of the job title.
* **LinkedIn** (`linked_in`, optional): Enter the LinkedIn profile of the contact person or the company's LinkedIn page.
* **Job Reference** (`job_reference`, optional): Enter any relevant job references or credentials.
* **Job Source** (`job_source`, optional): Enter the source of the job opening (e.g., LinkedIn, Company Website).
