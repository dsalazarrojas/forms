# Lead Preparer Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lead Preparer Agreement Form is a crucial document that outlines the agreement between the lead preparer and the client regarding the project's scope, timeline, and responsibilities. This form ensures a clear understanding of the project's details, reducing the risk of miscommunications and errors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Client**: Enter the name of the client.
2. **Project Start Date**: Enter the date when the project is expected to start.
3. **Project End Date**: Enter the date when the project is expected to be completed.
4. **Responsibilities**: Specify who will handle the project's responsibilities.
5. **Lead Preparer Agreement Form Acceptance**: Choose "I accept the lead preparer agreement form" to confirm your acceptance of the agreement.
6. **Lead Preparer Agreement Form Responsibilities**: Select the best option that describes how project responsibilities will be handled.
7. **Lead Preparer Agreement Form Timelines**: Choose the agreed upon timeline for starting and finishing the project.
8. **Lead Preparer Agreement Form Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments regarding the project.
9. **Lead Preparer Agreement Form Email**: Enter the email address of the client or project contact.
10. **Lead Preparer Agreement Form Phone**: Enter the phone number of the client or project contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client (text)**: Enter the name of the client for whom the project is being prepared.
*   **Project Start Date (date)**: Enter the expected start date of the project.
*   **Project End Date (date)**: Enter the expected completion date of the project.
*   **Responsibilities (text)**: Specify who will handle the project's responsibilities. This can be the lead preparer, client, or both.
*   **Lead Preparer Agreement Form Acceptance (select_one)**: Confirm your acceptance of the lead preparer agreement form.
*   **Lead Preparer Agreement Form Responsibilities (select_multiple)**: Choose how the project responsibilities will be handled among the lead preparer, client, or multiple parties.
*   **Lead Preparer Agreement Form Timelines (select_one)**: Select the agreed-upon timeline for the project.
*   **Lead Preparer Agreement Form Notes (text)**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding the project.
*   **Lead Preparer Agreement Form Email (email)**: Enter the email address of the client or project contact.
*   **Lead Preparer Agreement Form Phone (text)**: Enter the phone number of the client or project contact.

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully review and agree on the project's scope, timeline, and responsibilities with the client before filling out this form.
- Double-check the email and phone number fields for accuracy, ensuring they can be reached by the client or project contact.
- Use the "Notes" field for any additional comments or clarifications regarding the project. 
- Once you have filled out this form, save it to ensure both you and the client can refer back to it for future reference.
