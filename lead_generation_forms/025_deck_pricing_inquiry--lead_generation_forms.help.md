# Deck Pricing Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your project, including name, contact details, project size, deck type, desired start time, and preferred design style. The form helps our sales or customer service team understand your needs and provide accurate pricing information for your project.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Name** in the text field below. This will be the name of the person submitting the inquiry.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the correct format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`). This will be the contact email used to reach out to you for further information.
3. Enter your **Phone** number in the standard international format (e.g., `123-456-7890` or `1234567890`).
4. Select the **Project Size** of your project from the list:
	* Small: Small projects typically under 100 square feet.
	* Medium: Projects typically between 100-200 square feet.
	* Large: Projects typically above 200 square feet.
5. Choose the desired **Deck Type** from the list:
	* Deck only: A standard deck.
	* Deck + Pergola: A deck with a pergola.
	* Deck + Pergola + Roof: A deck with a pergola and a roof.
6. Select the desired **Timelines** for your project:
	* This field will allow you to choose a date or date and time. Please enter it in the format you are accustomed to (e.g., `YYYY-MM-DD` or `DD/MM/YY`).
7. Enter a **Project Description** if you have any additional details or specific requirements about your project.
8. Choose your **Preferred Design** style from the list:
	* Modern: A sleek and contemporary design.
	* Traditional: A classic and timeless design.
	* Rustic: A cozy and natural design.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name for contact and reference purposes.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for additional information or confirmation.
* **Project Size** (Select One, Required): Choose the size of your project to help us estimate the pricing accurately.
* **Deck Type** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the type of deck you are interested in for a detailed pricing breakdown.
* **Timelines** (Time, Required): Choose the start time for your project to ensure it fits into our schedules.
* **Project Description** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional details about your project.
* **Preferred Design** (Select One, Required): Select the design style that you envision for your deck project.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out correctly to help us provide an accurate pricing quote.
* If you have any specific requirements or requests, please include them in the **Project Description** field.
* A representative from our sales or customer service team will be in touch with you shortly to discuss your project and provide further information.
