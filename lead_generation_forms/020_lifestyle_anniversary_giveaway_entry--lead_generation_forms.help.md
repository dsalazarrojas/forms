# Lifestyle Anniversay Giveaway Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lifestyle Anniversay Giveaway Entry form is designed to collect information from individuals celebrating their anniversary. By filling out this form, you will be entered into a giveaway to receive a special prize related to anniversaries.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, ensuring all the required fields are filled in.
- Read and understand each field's purpose explained below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entry Form** (`Entry Form`, text, optional): Enter your anniversary wishes or a short message to express your enthusiasm for the giveaway.
* **Enter Information** (`Enter Information`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide more details about your anniversary, use this field to do so.
* **Anniversary Date** (`Anniversary Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your anniversary, in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Anniversary Year** (`Anniversary Year`, number, optional): Enter the year of your anniversary.
* **Anniversary Place** (`Anniversary Place`, text, optional): Describe the location where you celebrated your anniversary.
* **Prize Picked** (`Prize Picked`, select multiple, optional): Indicate if you've already picked a prize or not.
* **Entry Message** (`Entry Message`, text, optional): If you'd like to share a special message or thoughts about your anniversary, use this field.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you'd like to be known for the giveaway purposes.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive further instructions about the giveaway.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to receive further instructions about the giveaway.
* **Confirm Email** (`Confirm Email`, email, optional): Confirm your email address to ensure it's accurate.
* **Confirm Phone** (`Confirm Phone`, text, optional): Confirm your phone number to ensure it's accurate.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, select one, optional): Confirm that you have read and agree to the giveaway terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, note, optional): Click submit to complete and send your form submission.
* **Form Submission Data** (`Form Submission Data`, text, optional): This field is for internal use only and should be left blank.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
