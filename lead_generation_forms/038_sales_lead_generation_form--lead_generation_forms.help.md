# Sales Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Lead Generation Form is a tool designed to collect valuable contact information from potential customers, helping the sales team to identify and pursue new sales opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to provide a valid email address for each contact.
3. If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification from the relevant department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address for the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company the contact person is associated with.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the job title of the contact person.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Enter the department the contact person is in.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the company or contact person.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, required: false): Enter the job function of the contact person.
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, required: false): Enter the industry the company or contact person is in.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, required: false): Enter the website of the company the contact person is associated with.
