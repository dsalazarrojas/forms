# Art Fair Visitor Lead Generation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture contact information from potential art buyers and gather their preferences and interests to generate qualified leads for the art fair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (optional).
4. Select your city and state of residence in the "City - State" field (optional).
5. Indicate your interests in art styles by selecting all that apply in the "What styles of art are you most interested in?" field.
6. Choose your typical price range for art acquisitions in the "Typical price range of interest for acquisitions" field (optional).
7. If there is a specific artist you are interested in, enter their name in the "Is there a specific artist you are following?" field (optional).
8. Choose how you would like to be followed up with in the "How would you like us to follow up with you?" field.
9. If you are interested in an art consultation for your home or office, select "True" in the "Are you interested in an art consultation for your home or office?" field (optional).
10. Provide your feedback on the fair experience in the "Your thoughts on the Fair experience so far?" field (optional).
11. Specify how you found out about the fair in the "How did you find out about the Fair?" field (optional).
12. Finally, select a date of your visit to the fair in the "Date of Visit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Visitor Contact Details --**: This section captures your contact information.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **City - State**: Select your city and state of residence (optional).
* **-- Art Interest Profile --**: This section helps us understand your art interests.
* **What styles of art are you most interested in?**: Select all that apply (e.g., Contemporary - Modern, Abstract, etc.).
* **Typical price range of interest for acquisitions**: Choose your typical price range for art acquisitions (optional).
* **Is there a specific artist you are following?**: Enter the name of the artist you are interested in (optional).
* **How would you like us to follow up with you?**: Choose how you would like to be followed up with (e.g., Email me the fair catalog, etc.).
* **Are you interested in an art consultation for your home or office?**: Select "True" if you are interested in an art consultation (optional).
* **Your thoughts on the Fair experience so far?**: Provide your feedback on the fair experience (optional).
* **How did you find out about the Fair?**: Specify how you found out about the fair (optional).
* **Date of Visit**: Select the date of your visit to the fair.
