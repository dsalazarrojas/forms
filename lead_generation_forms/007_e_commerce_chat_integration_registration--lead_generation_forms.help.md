# e_commerce_chat_integration_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your online store's e-commerce integration with our chat system, providing necessary information about your store and its integration preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the online store name and the platform used for integration.
2. Fill out the payment information for your store.
3. Indicate which features are integrated into your chat.
4. Answer any additional questions or provide feedback about the chat integration.
5. If you have any other questions, provide them in the 'other_questions' field.
6. Optionally, share a message with us about your online store.
7. If you have any feedback about the chat integration, provide it in the 'feedback' field.
8. Decide if you would like to schedule a call back with us about your chat integration.
9. Provide any additional information about your online store, if needed.
10. Confirm that you have read and agreed to our terms and conditions by selecting 'I agree' in the 'confirm_terms_agreement' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What is the name of your online store?** (`online_store_info`, text, optional): Enter the name of your online store, which will be used to identify it in our integration process.
* **2. Which platform are you using for your e-commerce integration?** (`platform_info`, select_one, optional): Choose the platform you are using for your online store's integration (Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce).
* **3. What is the payment info for your e-commerce store?** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter the payment information for your online store.
* **4. Which features do you have integrated in your chat?** (`chat_feature_p`, select_multiple, optional): Select the features you have integrated into your chat (Yes or No).
* **5. What are your integration preferences?** (`integration_preferences`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your integration preferences.
* **6. Do you have any other questions about our e-commerce chat integration?** (`other_questions`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns, provide them here.
* **7. Would you like to share a message with us about your e-commerce store?** (`message`, text, optional): Share any additional information about your online store.
* **8. Do you have any feedback about our e-commerce chat integration?** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback about your chat integration experience.
* **9. Would you like to schedule a call back with us about your e-commerce chat integration?** (`schedule_call`, checkbox, optional): Decide if you would like to schedule a call back with us.
* **10. Is there anything else we should know about your e-commerce store?** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your online store.
* **11. Do you confirm that you have read and agree to our terms and conditions?** (`terms_agreement`, radio, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have read and agreed to our terms and conditions.
* **12. Confirm** (`confirm_terms_agreement`, checkbox, optional): Confirm that you have read and agreed to our terms and conditions.

Note: Please ensure that you fill out only the necessary fields and do not leave any required fields blank.
