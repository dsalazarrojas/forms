# Lead Conversion Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead Conversion Monitoring Form is a tool for tracking and monitoring the conversion status of generated leads. It helps in accurately tracking the lead source, status, and conversion rate, enabling better decision making and lead management.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow the steps outlined in the "How to Complete This Form" section of the help guide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter the first name of the lead.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter the last name of the lead.
* **Lead Source** (3, select_one, required): Select the source of the lead.
* **Other Lead Source** (4, text, optional): If the lead was generated from an "Other" source, enter the source here.
* **Company** (5, text, required): Enter the company name of the lead.
* **Job Title** (6, text, optional): Enter the job title of the lead.
* **Phone** (7, text, required): Enter the lead's phone number.
* **Email** (8, email, required): Enter the lead's email address.
* **Lead Status** (9, select_one, required): Select the status of the lead (Active or Inactive).
* **Goal Status** (10, select_multiple, required): Choose the current goal status of the lead (Active or Inactive).
* **Lead Creation Date** (11, date, required): Enter the date the lead was created.
* **Date of Birth** (12, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the lead.
* **Lead Update Date** (13, date, optional): Enter the date of the last lead update.
* **Lead Conversion Date** (14, date, optional): Enter the date of lead conversion (if applicable).
* **Converted** (15, text, optional): If a lead has converted, select the converted status.
* **Revenue** (16, number, optional): Enter the revenue associated with the lead (if applicable).
* **Cost** (17, number, optional): Enter the cost associated with the lead (if applicable).
* **Lead Category** (18, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the lead falls under a specific category.
* **Tags** (19, select_multiple, optional): Select any tags associated with the lead.
* **Owner** (20, select_one, optional): Select the lead's owner.
* **Notes** (21, note, optional): Enter any status notes for the lead.
* **Lead Tags** (22, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional tags associated with the lead.
* **Lead Owner** (23, select_one, optional): Select the lead's second owner.
* **Conversion Notes** (24, note, optional): Enter any conversion notes for the lead.
* **Lead Tags 2** (25, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional tags associated with the lead.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to ensure accurate tracking and assessment of lead conversion rates.
* If a field is not applicable, leave it blank or select the "None" or "Not Applicable" option.
* If you are unsure of any field's purpose or requirement, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
