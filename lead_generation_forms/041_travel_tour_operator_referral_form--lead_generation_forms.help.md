# Travel Tour Operator Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect referrals or nominations for travel tour operators that can be contacted for potential partnerships or collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the `First Name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
3. Provide the name of the travel tour operator company in the `Company` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address where you can be contacted regarding this referral.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the travel tour operator company you are referring.

## Tips
- If you are familiar with the company or individual you are referring, please ensure you are not duplicating a submission for the same company or individual.
- If the `Company` field is left blank, please make sure to include the relevant contact information in the `Email` field for the referral to be valid.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
