**Digital Growth Acceleration Request Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand the details of your project for digital growth acceleration. Please fill out as much information as possible to ensure we can better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields with as much information as you can provide. Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Project Title** (`project_title`, `text`, required)
* A brief and descriptive title of your project.
### 2. **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required)
* The name of the client this project is for.
### 3. **Company** (`company`, `text`, required)
* The name of the company associated with this project.
### 4. **Start Date** (`project_start_date`, `date`, required)
* The date when you would like to start the project, in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-03-15).
### 5. **Status** (`project_status`, `select_one`, required)
* Select the status of your project:
	+ `new`: A new project with no prior work
	+ `in progress`: An ongoing project with some prior work
	+ `completed`: A project that is already finished
### 6. **Budget Allocation** (`budget_allocation`, `number`, required)
* Enter your budget for this project.
### 7. **Manager** (`project_manager`, `text`, required)
* The name of the project manager.
### 8. **Team** (`team`, `select_multiple`, required)
* Select whether you have a team assigned to this project:
	+ `Yes`: The project has a team assigned
	+ `No`: The project does not have a team assigned
### 9. **Email** (`email`, `email`, required)
* Your email address.
### 10. **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required)
* Your phone number.
### 11. **Address** (`address`, `text`, required)
* Your project address.
### 12. **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional)
* Any additional notes about your project.
### 13. **File Upload** (`file_upload`, `text`, optional)
* Optional field for uploading any supporting documents.
### 14. **Website** (`website`, `text`, optional)
* The website associated with this project.
### 15. **Social Media** (`social_media`, `text`, optional)
* Any relevant social media channels for your project.
### 16. **LinkedIn** (`linkedin`, `text`, optional)
* Your LinkedIn profile link.
### 17. **Twitter** (`twitter`, `text`, optional)
* Your Twitter profile link.
### 18. **Instagram** (`instagram`, `text`, optional)
* Your Instagram profile link.
### 19. **Facebook** (`facebook`, `text`, optional)
* Your Facebook profile link.
### 20. **Other Social Media** (`other_social`, `text`, optional)
* Any other social media channels you'd like to share.
### 21. **LinkedIn Link** (`linkedin_link`, `text`, optional)
* Link to your LinkedIn profile or any other relevant LinkedIn content.
### 22. **Twitter Link** (`twitter_link`, `text`, optional)
* Link to your Twitter profile or any other relevant Twitter content.
### 23. **Instagram Link** (`instagram_link`, `text`, optional)
* Link to your Instagram profile or any other relevant Instagram content.
### 24. **Facebook Link** (`facebook_link`, `text`, optional)
* Link to your Facebook profile or any other relevant Facebook content.
### 25. **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, required)
* The person assigned to this project.

Please submit your completed form to help us better understand your project and provide you with the best possible service.
