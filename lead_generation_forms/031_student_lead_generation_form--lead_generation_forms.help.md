# Student Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students interested in our programs and services. It will help us understand their preferences and interests to better serve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the process.
2. Fill in the student's name in the "Student Name" field. This is the primary identifier for the student's contact information.
3. Enter the student's email address in the "Student Email" field. This is the primary communication channel for us to reach out to the student.
4. Select the program of interest from the "Program Of Interest" dropdown menu.
5. Fill in the student's phone number in the "Student Phone" field. This is an optional field for students who wish to be contacted via phone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Please enter the student's full name as it appears on their ID, driver's license, or passport.
* **Student Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the student's email address where we can contact them.
* **Program Of Interest** (`program`, select_one, required: false): Select the program that interests the student.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for students who wish to be contacted via phone.
