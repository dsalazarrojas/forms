# Real Estate Sales Lead Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Real Estate Sales Lead Form is designed to collect essential details from potential customers interested in real estate sales. This form helps in establishing initial contact and gathering vital information needed to provide tailored services.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, click on the "Get Started" button to initiate the form-filling process.
2.  Enter your name in the designated field as prompted.
3.  Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field, which will be used for future communication.
4.  Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field to stay updated on relevant real estate listings and services.
5.  In the "Business Name" field, enter any relevant business or affiliation information that may be helpful for our sales team to identify your needs.
6.  The "Address" field is where you should enter your physical address for us to better understand your location and provide relevant information.
7.  For the "City" field, enter the name of the city you reside in or are interested in.
8.  Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field for us to narrow down your location.
9.  Select your state from the provided options in the "State" field to further refine your location.
10.  Choose your country from the options in the "Country" field to ensure we understand your international or national location correctly.
11.  Finally, select all that apply from the "Lead Type" field according to your interest in real estate - whether it's a "Lead" for a purchase or a "Referral" for someone you know who is in the market.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Get Started**: This is the initial step to start filling out the form and initiate the process.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for future contact and communication from our team.
* **Email**: A valid email address is required for us to keep you updated on real estate listings and services.
* **Business Name**: Enter any business or affiliation name that may help our sales team better serve you.
* **Address**: Enter your physical location for us to provide relevant information and listings.
* **City**: Enter the name of the city where you reside or are interested in.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code to narrow down your location.
* **State**: Select your state from the options to refine your location.
* **Country**: Choose your country of origin or current location.
* **Lead Type**: Select all that apply - "Lead" if you're looking to purchase a property, or "Referral" if it's for someone you know who is in the market.
