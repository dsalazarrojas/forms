# Flat Six Engine Swap Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users interested in a flat six engine swap for their vehicle. This form helps us understand their goals, budget, and specific needs for the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the services of interest you are looking for (e.g., Engine Swap, Performance Tuning, etc.).
2. Choose the engine type that matches your vehicle (e.g., 3.0L V6, 4.0L V8, etc.).
3. Enter your vehicle's details, such as year, make, model, and trim.
4. Provide your budget range for the services.
5. Submit the form to express your interest and we'll get back to you with more information and options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Services of Interest
* **Services of Interest** (select_multiple, required): Choose the services you are interested in, such as Engine Swap, Performance Tuning, Custom Fabrication, Suspension and Alignment, or Electrical.

### 2. Engine Type
* **Engine Type** (select_one, required): Select the engine type that matches your vehicle from the options: 3.0L V6, 4.0L V8, 6.0L V8, or 8.0L V8.

### 3. Vehicle Details
* **Vehicle Year** (number, required): Enter your vehicle's year.
* **Vehicle Make** (text, optional): Enter your vehicle's make (e.g., Ford, Toyota, etc.).
* **Vehicle Model** (text, optional): Enter your vehicle's model (e.g., Mustang, Corolla, etc.).
* **Vehicle Trim** (text, optional): Enter your vehicle's trim (e.g., GT, Sport, etc.).

### 4. Budget Range
* **Budget Range** (number, optional): Enter your estimated budget for the services. This will help us understand your financial constraints.

### 5. Additional Information
* **Vehicle Swap Goals** (text, optional): Provide any additional information about your vehicle swap goals or specific needs.

### 6. Services of Interest (Repeat)
* **Services of Interest** (select_multiple, optional): Re-select the services of interest if you need to add or modify any after submitting your vehicle details.

# Tips
* Make sure to select the correct services of interest to ensure we can provide you with relevant information and options.
* Enter accurate vehicle details to ensure we can provide you with the best possible solutions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
