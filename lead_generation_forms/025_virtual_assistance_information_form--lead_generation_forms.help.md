# Virtual Assistance Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Assistance Information Form is designed to collect essential details about your client's virtual assistance needs. This form is used to gather specific information that will help us better understand their requirements, timelines, and evaluation expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Please fill in all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). The documents field is a required field.
2.  Select the evaluation level for your client's project. Choose from the available options: High, Medium, or Low.
3.  Ensure that all entered information is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details (\[client\_details\], text, optional\*):** Enter your client's name, organization, and any other relevant details in this text box. Please note that this field is optional but is useful for us to better understand your client's requirements.
* **Task Requirements (\[task\_requirements\], text, optional\*):** Describe the specific tasks or services your client needs help with. This field is also optional but helps us understand the scope of work.
* **Timelines (\[timelines\], text, optional\*):** Provide information about any specific deadlines or timelines related to your client's project. This field is optional but helps us plan and coordinate accordingly.
* **Documents (\[documents\], text, required\*):** Please upload any relevant documents or files related to your client's project. This is a required field.
* **Evaluation (\[evaluation\], select\_one, required\*):** Evaluate your client's project based on the provided options. Select High, Medium, or Low, depending on the complexity and urgency of the project.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all uploaded documents are relevant and related to your client's project.
* Review all entered information carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
