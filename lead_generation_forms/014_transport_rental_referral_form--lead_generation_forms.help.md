# Transport Rental Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transport Rental Referral Form is used to collect information from individuals or businesses that are referring others to the transport rental company. This form helps the company to track referrals and follow up with both the referrer and the referred individual/business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details in the First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Company fields.
2. Select the type of transport to be referred (Car, Truck, or Van).
3. Enter the details of the referral in the Trip Details field.
4. Enter the pickup and drop-off times and locations for the transport rental.
5. Enter the details of the individual or company being referred, including their position and contact information.
6. Confirm whether a follow-up is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Position** (`position`, text, required): Enter your position in the company.
* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the referral.
* **Transport Type** (`transport_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of transport to be referred (Car, Truck, or Van).
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, date, required): Enter the pickup date and time for the transport rental.
* **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, text, required): Enter the pickup location for the transport rental.
* **Drop-off Time** (`drop_off_time`, date, required): Enter the drop-off date and time for the transport rental.
* **Drop-off Location** (`drop_off_location`, text, required): Enter the drop-off location for the transport rental.
* **Referral** (`referral`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the referral.
* **Referred By** (`referred_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the individual or company being referred.
* **Referred Company** (`referred_company`, text, required): Enter the name of the company of the individual being referred.
* **Referred Phone** (`referred_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the individual or company being referred.
* **Referred Email** (`referred_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the individual or company being referred.
* **Referred Position** (`referred_position`, text, required): Enter the position of the individual being referred.
* **Referred Referral** (`referred_referral`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the referral from the individual or company being referred.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select_one, required): Confirm whether a follow-up is required.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the referral.
* **Notes 2** (`notes2`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the referral.
* **Notes 3** (`notes3`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the referral.
* **Notes 4** (`notes4`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the referral.
* **Notes 5** (`notes5`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the referral.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to complete this form on behalf of the individual or company being referred.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact the transport rental company for clarification.
* Remember to keep the notes fields for any additional information that may be helpful for the sales or customer service team to follow up on the referral.
