# Travel Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in booking travel packages, aiming to generate leads for the travel industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
3. Click the "submit" button to send your lead.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **lead_details (Text)** (`lead_details`, required/optional): This field is the main section for providing details about your travel lead.
* **membership_details (Text)** (`membership_details`, required): This field is for providing your membership details, which is a required field for further follow-up and sales processes.
* **submit (Text)** (`submit`, required): Click this button to submit your form and generate a lead.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your lead is generated successfully.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* If you need assistance or have questions about this form, feel free to contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
