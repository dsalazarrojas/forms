# Sustainability Transformation Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting information from potential leads who want to engage with our organization in sustainability projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: Project Location, Organization Size, and Lead Source.
2. Select the correct Project Location from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose the correct Organization Size and Lead Source from their respective dropdown menus.
4. Enter your details in the remaining fields as per your preference.
5. Submit the form once all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who will be the main point of contact for this lead.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of the sustainability project you are interested in.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, select_one, required): Choose where the project is located (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who will be managing the project.
* **Organization Size** (`organization_size`, select_one, required): Choose the size of your organization (e.g., Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large).
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, select_one, required): Choose how you got to know about this opportunity (e.g., Social Media, Referral, Other).
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select_multiple, optional): Choose when you would like to be followed up with (e.g., Yes, No).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form correctly and completely, as this will help us process your lead efficiently.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, feel free to contact us.
* You will only be contacted once for each unique lead submission.
