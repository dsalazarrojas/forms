# credit_consultation_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The credit consultation contact form is designed to provide potential customers with a way to reach out to us for credit consultation. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Make sure to provide your first name.
3. Make sure to provide your last name.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Enter your FICO score (this is an optional field).
7. Click the "submit" button to send the information to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): This field is where you can enter your first name. This will help us know who you are and what you're looking for.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): This field is for your last name. Enter the last name you are known by.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you directly.
* **FICO Score** (`fico_score`, `number`, optional): If you know your FICO score, enter it here. This will help us better understand your credit situation.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Clicking this button will send the information you've filled out to our team for review. 

## Tips
- Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
- Make sure your email address is correct so we can contact you back.
- If you have trouble submitting the form, try contacting our support team.
