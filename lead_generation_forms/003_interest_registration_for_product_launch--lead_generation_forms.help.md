# Interest Registration For Product Launch - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users interested in learning more about a product launch. By completing this form, you'll help us better understand your interests and needs, which will enable us to provide you with targeted information and potential opportunities. Please note that this form does not commit you to anything, and we'll use the information you provide to improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each field for accuracy before submitting.
3. Use the "Additional Comments" field to provide any relevant details or questions you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (lead_generation_form)** (`text`, required/optional: no): Enter your name and any other contact information you'd like to share with us. This will help us get in touch with you directly.
* **Lead Source (lead_source)** (`text`, required/optional: no): If you've been referred to us by someone or somewhere, please let us know so we can thank them for their support.
* **Product Launch Details (product_launch)** (`text`, required/optional: no): Provide any relevant information about the product launch you're interested in, such as its name, features, or benefits.
* **Other Lead Sources (lead_source_additional)** (`text`, required/optional: no): If you've been referred to us through a different source, please list it here.
* **Additional Comments (notes)** (`text`, required/optional: no): Do you have any questions, feedback, or additional information you'd like to share with us? This is your chance to have a voice.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and complete as possible when filling out the form.
* You can always reach out to us if you have any questions or need further clarification on any field.
* We'll review and follow up with you based on the information you provide, so keep an eye on your email for our response.
