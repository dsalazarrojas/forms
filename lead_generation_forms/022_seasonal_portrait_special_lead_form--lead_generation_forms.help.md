# Seasonal Portrait Special Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seasonal Portrait Special Lead Form is designed to collect information from customers interested in special lead generation services. This form allows customers to provide their name, contact method, and lead details, which will help our team generate a special lead for them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Choose your preferred contact method from the "Contact Method" dropdown menu:
	* Contact Form: Use this option if you prefer to contact us directly through this form.
	* Email: Enter your email address to receive notifications and updates.
	* Phone: Enter your phone number to receive calls from us.
	* Mail: Enter your physical mail address for postal notifications.
	* Other: Select this option if you have a different contact method not listed here.
3. Provide any additional details in the "Lead Details" field about your special lead generation requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`text`, required: false): Please enter your full name.
* **Contact Method** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose your preferred contact method for communication. Note: This field is not required, but we'll use the selected method to get in touch with you.
* **Lead Details** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about your lead generation requirements.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, don't hesitate to contact us.
