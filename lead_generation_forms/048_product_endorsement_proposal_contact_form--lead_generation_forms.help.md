# Product Endorsement Proposal Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and proposal details from users interested in endorsing a product. It is meant for internal use by product managers to review and contact potential end-users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in your contact details and proposal information, and click "Submit" when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Product Proposal
* **Product Proposal** (`product_proposal`, text, optional): This field is for users to briefly describe the product they are interested in. A clear and concise proposal will help us better understand your needs.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, date, optional): In this field, please enter your date of contact.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can keep in touch with you.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Add your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
*   **Proposal** (`proposal`, text, optional): This field is for you to elaborate on your product proposal, providing more details on why you're interested in the product.
*   **Proposal** (`proposal`, text, optional): If you want to add any other relevant information, you can do it here. This will help us understand your needs better.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Our team will review your proposal and get back to you soon.
