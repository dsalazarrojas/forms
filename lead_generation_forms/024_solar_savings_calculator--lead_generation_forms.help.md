# Solar Savings Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users estimate their potential solar savings. It collects information on the user's contact details and property specifics to provide a personalized estimate of their savings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter the size of your solar system in the "System Size" field.
5. Enter the cost of your solar system in the "Solar Cost" field.
6. Enter your estimated savings in the "Savings Estimate" field.
7. Select the type of solar panels you are interested in from the "Solar Panels" dropdown.
8. Select the type of roof you have from the "Roof Type" dropdown. You can select multiple options.
9. Enter the direction of your roof from the "Roof Direction" dropdown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name to help us better understand your preferences and provide more personalized estimates.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your email address to enable us to contact you with further questions or estimates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you for further discussion.
* **System Size** (`system_size`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the size of your solar system to help us estimate your potential savings.
* **Solar Cost** (`solar_cost`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the cost of your solar system to help us estimate your potential savings.
* **Savings Estimate** (`savings_estimate`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your estimated savings with a solar system.
* **Solar Panels** (`solar_panels`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of solar panels you are interested in:
	+ Monocrystalline
	+ Polycrystalline
	+ Thin Film
* **Roof Type** (`roof_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the type of roof you have:
	+ Flat Roof
	+ Gable Roof
	+ Hip Roof
	+ Dormer Roof
	+ Gambrel Roof
* **Roof Direction** (`roof_direction`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the direction of your roof:
	+ South
	+ East
	+ North
	+ West
	+ Other

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate information to ensure that you receive a correct estimate of your potential savings.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
