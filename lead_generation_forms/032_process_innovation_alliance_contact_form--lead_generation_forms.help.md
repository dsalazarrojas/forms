# Process Innovation Alliance Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Innovation Alliance Contact Form is designed to help the Process Innovation Alliance gather information from potential collaborators and lead seekers. By completing this form, you can express your interest in collaborating on a project or seeking leads for a project that aligns with our alliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your answer to "Lead Generation" by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.
2. If you selected "Yes" for Lead Generation, you can choose from the available options in the drop down menu for "Category" or enter a custom "Category" in the "Category" text field.
3. If you selected "Yes" for Lead Generation, enter your "Name" in the text field.
4. Enter your "Email" address in the text field.
5. Enter your "Phone" number in the text field.
6. Type your "Message" in the text field.
7. Select your answer to "Collaboration Request" by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.
8. If you selected "Yes" for Collaboration Request, you can enter your custom "Category" in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Lead Generation
• **Lead Generation**: Select whether you are interested in lead generation or not.
• **Category**: Choose from the available options or enter a custom category that aligns with your project.
• **Name**: Enter your name for contact purposes.
• **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
• **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
• **Message**: Type a brief message about your project or interests.
• **Collaboration Request**
• **Collaboration Request**: Select whether you are interested in collaboration or not.
• **Category**: Enter a custom category that aligns with your project.
• **Category**: Choose from the available options or enter a custom category that aligns with your project.
