# email-subscription-incentive - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "email-subscription-incentive" form is designed to collect contact information and preferences from users. This form is likely used for marketing or customer engagement purposes, such as tracking user interests or sending targeted emails.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field on page 1.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field on page 2.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field on page 3.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field on page 4.
5. Select a date in the "Date" field on page 5.
6. Select a time in the "Time" field on page 6.
7. Add any additional notes about yourself in the "Note" field on page 7.
8. Choose a country of interest from the "Country" field on page 8.
9. Select one option from the "Select One" field on page 9.
10. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field on page 10.
11. Repeat the last action (date) from page 5 in the duplicated "Date" field on page 11.
11. Finally, select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field on page 12.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required/Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Required/Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Required/Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Required/Optional): Enter your phone number. Please note that the "phone" input type is not specific for this field.
* **Date** (Required/Optional): Select a date.
* **Time** (Required/Optional): Select a time.
* **Note** (Required/Optional): Add any additional notes about yourself.
* **Country** (Required/Optional): Choose a country of interest.
* **Select One** (Required/Optional): Choose one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple** (Required/Optional): Select multiple options from the list.
* **Date** (Duplicate, Required/Optional): Repeat the selection of date.
* **Select Multiple** (Duplicate, Required/Optional): Select multiple options again.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have entered correct and complete information in each field.
* If you are unsure about a particular field, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
