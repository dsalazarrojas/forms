# Data Modernization Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential clients or prospects interested in data modernization consulting services. It helps the consulting team assess their needs and initiate a conversation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate options for each field, and make sure to fill in all required fields.
2. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
3. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead Generation Form** (`lead_generation_form`, text, optional): This field is not required and is used to provide a brief description of your current lead generation strategy or goals.
* **Data Modernization Goals** (`data_modernization_goals`, text, optional): Describe your data modernization goals, such as data quality improvement or data-driven decision-making.
* **Schedule Discovery Call** (`discovery_call`, number, optional): This field is not required, but it's used to schedule a discovery call with our team. Please enter a date and time in the format of 2023-12-31 13:45.
* **Data Modernization Experience** (`data_modernization_experience`, select_one, required): Choose your experience level with data modernization, from None to Lots.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more people you'd like to discuss this opportunity with.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you back.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easier communication.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of your company.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, text, optional): Enter the number of employees your company has.
