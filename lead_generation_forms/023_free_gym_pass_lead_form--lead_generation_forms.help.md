# Free Gym Pass Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential customers who are interested in obtaining a free gym pass. It is displayed on a public website or landing page and is used to filter potential leads.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. This field is required for us to contact you.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your Fitness Interest from the dropdown menu in the "Fitness Interest" field. You can choose one or multiple options.
6. Choose your preferred time slot in the "Preferred Time Slot" field. You can select multiple options.
7. If you have any additional comments or information you would like to share, enter them in the "Note" field (optional).
8. If you would like to be assigned a specific tool (chatjimmy) or have another option, select it in the "Assigned Tool" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This is the best way for us to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Fitness Interest**: Select one or multiple options that best describe your interest in fitness activities. This will help us understand your interests.
* **Preferred Time Slot**: Select one or multiple time slots that you are available to visit the gym.
* **Note**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or information you would like to share with us.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select "chatjimmy" if you would like to be assigned a specific tool, or choose "Other" if you have a different preference.
