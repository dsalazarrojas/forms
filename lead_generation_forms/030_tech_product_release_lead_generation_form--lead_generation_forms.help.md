# Tech Product Release Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather information about potential customers interested in new tech products, helping the development team understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Make sure to select the correct options for "Lead Segment" and "Lead Follow-up" to ensure proper categorization.
* Enter the correct Tech Product Release Date, as it affects the lead generation process.
* Select the correct Marketing Team and Product Manager for accurate follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Generation Form**: This is the main title of the form, and the form itself.
* **Lead Details**: Please fill in any additional comments or information about the lead here.
* **Lead Segment**: Choose all options that apply to the lead, such as "Yes" for interested in new products, or "No" for not interested in new products.
* **Lead Follow-up**: Choose all follow-up methods, such as "Yes" for phone calls, "No" for emails, or other follow-up options.
* **Tech Product Release Date**: Enter the date when the product is expected to be released.
* **Marketing Team**: Select the team that will handle the lead, such as Team A, Team B, or Team C.
* **Product Manager**: Select the product manager that will handle the lead, such as John Doe, Jane Doe, or Bob Smith.
* **Tech Product Name**: Enter the name of the tech product that the lead is interested in.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the product development team.
