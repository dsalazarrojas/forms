# Cross Brand Affiliate Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for new affiliates to sign up with the Cross Brand Affiliate Network. It requires some basic information to get started.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the requested information and submit it. Please ensure that all fields marked as required are filled out correctly.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cross Brand Affiliate Network Registration Form** (`title`, text, required): This is the title of the form and the name of the network. This field is not meant to be edited.
* **affiliate_name** (`affiliate_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your business cards or online profiles.
* **affiliate_email** (`affiliate_email`, email, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`affiliate_phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **affiliate_website** (`affiliate_website`, text, optional): If you have a website, enter its URL.
* **Facebook URL** (`affiliate_facebook`, text, optional): If you have a Facebook profile, enter its URL.
* **Twitter URL** (`affiliate_twitter`, text, optional): If you have a Twitter profile, enter its URL.
* **Instagram URL** (`affiliate_instagram`, text, optional): If you have an Instagram profile, enter its URL.

## Tips
Please ensure that you only submit this form once. If you have already submitted this form, do not attempt to resubmit it. If you need to make any changes to your registration, please contact our support team.
