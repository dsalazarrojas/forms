# painting_service_lead_capture_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The painting service lead capture form is used to collect information from customers interested in our painting services. This form helps us understand their needs and provides us with the necessary details to assist them in the next steps of the process.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, not required): Please provide a brief introduction about yourself or the company you represent. This will help us understand who you are and what you are interested in.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, not required): Describe the project you are looking to accomplish, including any relevant details such as size, scope, and location.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, number, not required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed. This will help us reach out to you if we need more information.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, not required): Provide your email address to enable us to contact you with updates or further questions. This will help us keep you informed about your lead.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, not required): If this is a large or complex project, please provide a detailed description of what you need from us. This will help us understand the scope of the project and ensure we can provide the best possible service.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask us and we will be happy to assist.
* Once you submit the form, you will receive a confirmation email from us with further instructions on what to do next.
