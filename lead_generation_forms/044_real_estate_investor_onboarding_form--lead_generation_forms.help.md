# Real Estate Investor Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential real estate investors for lead generation purposes. The goal is to capture relevant details about the investor's company, contact information, investment preferences, and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your company's name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Enter your name as the investor in the "Investor Name" field.
3. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
4. Add your email address in the "Email Address" field.
5. Enter your business address in the "Business Address" field.
6. Select your type as an investor in the "Investor Type" field.
7. Choose the purpose of your investment in the "Investment Purpose" field.
8. Select the type of property you are interested in investing in the "Property Type" field.
9. Enter the amount you are willing to invest in the "Investment Amount" field.
10. Indicate how often you plan to invest in the "Investment Frequency" field.
11. Choose the area you are interested in investing in the "Target Area" field.
12. Select the stage of investment you are interested in the "Investment Stage" field.
13. Select your investment focus areas in the "Investment Focus" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`text`, required: false): Your company's name.
* **Investor Name** (`text`, required: false): Your name as the investor.
* **Contact Number** (`text`, required: false): Your phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required: false): Your email address.
* **Business Address** (`text`, required: false): Your business address.
* **Investor Type** (`text`, required: false): Select your type as an investor (e.g., Individual, Company).
* **Investment Purpose** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the purpose of your investment (e.g., Real Estate, Real Estate Development, Real Estate Investment).
* **Property Type** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the type of property you are interested in investing in (e.g., Residential, Commercial, Land).
* **Investment Amount** (`number`, required: false): Enter the amount you are willing to invest.
* **Investment Frequency** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose how often you plan to invest (e.g., Once, Ongoing, Quarterly).
* **Target Area** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the area where you are interested in investing (e.g., Domestic, International, National).
* **Investment Stage** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose the stage of investment you are interested in (e.g., Seed, Series A, Series B).
* **Investment Focus** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select your investment focus areas (e.g., Technology, Real Estate, Healthcare).

## Tips
This form is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of your real estate investment preferences. Please fill out the form as accurately and completely as possible to ensure we can provide you with relevant information and opportunities.
