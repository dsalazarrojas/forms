# Predictive Reordering Pilot Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This is a registration form for companies interested in joining the Predictive Reordering pilot program. It helps us understand your company's details, goals, and needs to provide a tailored experience for you.
## How To Complete This Form

*   Complete this form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are filled.
*   Answer each question as accurately as possible, providing all necessary details.
*   If a field is marked as optional, fill it out only if it's relevant to your company's specifics.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **1. Company Details**: This page is for providing company-specific information such as name and website.
    *   **Company Name**: The name of your company.
    *   **Company URL**: The URL of your company's website.
*   **2. Goal Settings**: Here you can choose your lead generation goals.
    *   **What are your lead generation goals?**: Choose one or more of the goals that best fit your company's current needs.
*   **3. Join Program**: This is where you confirm your interest in joining the predictive reordering pilot program.
    *   **Are you interested in joining the predictive reordering pilot program?**: Answer with either 'True' or 'False' based on your company's interest.
*   **4. Contact Information**: Please provide your contact information to help us get in touch with you.
    *   **Contact Information**: A brief contact information for a company representative.
*   **5. Company Size**: This page is for your company's size and type.
    *   **Company Size**: Select the size of your company, either Small, Medium, or Large.
    *   **Company Type**: Enter any other type not listed that best fits your company's nature.
*   **6. Lead Generation**: This page is for setting up your lead generation frequency and channels.
    *   **Lead Generation**: Choose a channel for generating leads such as Email, Phone, or Social Media.
    *   **Lead Gen Frequency**: Set the frequency of your lead generation activities, either Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
*   **7. Other**: This is an optional page for any additional details you'd like to share.
    *   **Company Stage**: Enter your company's current stage such as growth stage or industry.
    *   **Company Size Other**: If your company size doesn't fit the provided options, please specify.
    *   **Lead Gen Channel Other**: If you use another lead generation channel not listed, specify it here.
    *   **Lead Gen Channel Other 2**: If you have another lead generation channel, please specify it here.
    *   **Company Size Other**: If your company size doesn't fit the provided options, specify it here.
*   **8. Notes**: This is an optional section for any additional comments or questions you might have.
    *   **Notes**: Any additional comments or questions that might help us better understand your needs.
## Tips

*   Ensure all required fields are filled out completely.
*   Be as specific and accurate as possible with your answers.
*   If you have any questions or need help, please contact us directly.
