# Franchise Drop Shipping Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Franchise Drop Shipping Inquiry form is a tool for us to better understand your business needs and provide more effective lead generation support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required information in the following fields:

1. **User Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or contact information.
2. **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you about your lead generation needs.
3. **Phone**: Enter your business phone number. This will be used for follow-up contact.
4. **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business.
5. **Business Description**: Briefly describe your business and its products or services.
6. **Industry**: Select the category that best describes your business (e.g. retail, health and wellness, etc.).
7. **Franchise Type**: Select the type of franchise your business is (e.g. fast food, grocery, etc.).
8. **Store Locations**: Select where your business is located (e.g. online, physical, both).
9. **Lead Source**: Select the source of your lead generation needs (e.g. Google, social media, etc.).
10. **Preferred Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g. phone call, email, etc.).
11. **Best Time to Contact**: Select a time of day when you are most available to be contacted.
12. **Business Email**: Enter your business email address.
13. **Business Phone**: Enter your business phone number.
14. **Business Fax**: Enter your business fax number.
15. **Business Address 1**: Enter your business address (line 1).
16. **Business Address 2**: Enter your business address (line 2).
17. **City**: Enter the city where your business is located.
18. **State**: Enter the state where your business is located.
19. **Zip**: Enter your business zip code.
20. **Website**: Enter your business website.
21. **Lead Manager**: Enter the name of your lead manager.
22. **Manager Email**: Enter your lead manager's email address.
23. **Manager Phone**: Enter your lead manager's phone number.
24. **Preferred Day and Time**: Select a day and time when you are most available to be contacted.
25. **Other Lead Source**: If none of the above options apply, please describe the source of your lead generation needs.
