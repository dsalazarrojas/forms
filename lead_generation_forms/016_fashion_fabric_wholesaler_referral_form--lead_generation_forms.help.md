# Fashion Fabric Wholesaler Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from fashion fabric wholesalers who are referring new customers to our brand. The form helps us understand the referral source, contact details, and preferences of the referrer, which enables us to follow up and establish a strong relationship with the new customer.

## How To Complete This Form

- Read through the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting.
- Fill out each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referral source** (`referral_source`, select_one, required: false): 
  Please select where you met our brand:
  - Designer
  - Brand
  - Manufacturer
  - Online
  - Social Media
  - Trade Show
  - Other
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): 
  Please enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: true): 
  Enter your company's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: true): 
  Enter your business phone number.
* **Type of Fabric** (`fabric_type`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Select the type of fabric you work with:
  - Cotton
  - Polyester
  - Synthetic
  - Other
* **Company details** (`company_details`, text, required: false): 
  Provide more information about your company.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): 
  Enter your company's location (City, State, Country).
* **How would you like to be followed up with?** (`follow_up_info`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Select how you would like to be followed up:
  - Phone
  - Email
  - Mail
* **Additional notes or message** (`message`, text, required: false): 
  Leave any additional notes or messages about this referral.

## Tips

- Please ensure the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
- You can select multiple options for "Type of Fabric" and "How would you like to be followed up with?" as per your preferences.
- You can add any additional information in the "Company details" and "Message" fields to help us understand your company and preferences better.
