# Therapeutic Treatment Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in seeking therapeutic treatment. It helps the treatment team understand your needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below for each field.
2.  Please answer all the fields, as some fields are required to ensure accurate processing.
3.  If you have any doubts or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): This is the first name of the person seeking treatment. Please enter your first name.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This is the email address of the person seeking treatment. If you have an email, please provide it.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This is the phone number of the person seeking treatment. If you have a phone number, please provide it.
*   **What kind of therapy do you need?** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the following options:
    *   **Physical Therapy**: This is for injuries or conditions that affect the body's movement or physical abilities.
    *   **Occupational Therapy**: This is for individuals who may need help with daily activities or tasks due to physical or mental limitations.
    *   **Speech Therapy**: This is for individuals who may need help with communication or language skills.
    *   **Chiropractic Therapy**: This is for individuals seeking chiropractic care for back pain or musculoskeletal issues.
*   **Which of the following apply?** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all that apply:
    *   **Injury**: You are seeking therapy due to an injury.
    *   **Pain Management**: You are experiencing pain and are seeking therapy to manage it.
    *   **Stress Management**: You are seeking therapy to manage stress or anxiety.
    *   **Wellness**: You are seeking therapy for overall wellness and balance.
*   **Date of Injury** (`date`, date, optional): If you are seeking therapy due to an injury, please provide the date of the injury.
*   **Time of Injury** (`time`, time, optional): If you are seeking therapy due to an injury, please provide the time of the injury.
*   **Note** (`note`, note, optional): This is a text box for you to provide any additional information about your condition or requirements. You may want to share your medical history, previous treatments, or specific goals you are seeking to achieve.

## Tips

*   Please answer honestly and accurately to ensure that the treatment team can best serve your needs.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
*   Remember to keep your answers concise and clear to facilitate quick processing of your information.
