# marine_corps_lead_generation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The marine_corps_lead_generation_form is a tool used by potential applicants to express their interest in joining the Marine Corps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal details section with your:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Phone number
	* Birth Date
	* Address

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that you have access to and regularly use. This will be used as a contact point for communication.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number to provide an easy way for us to contact you.
* **Birth Date** (Birth Date, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (Address, text, required: false): Enter your current address as it is written on your official identification documents.
* **Branch of Service** (Branch of Service, select_one, required: false): Check 'Yes' if you are interested in joining the Marine Corps. If not, you may select another branch of the military or 'No' if none apply.
* **Branch of Service** (Branch of Service, select_multiple, required: false): This option is for those who selected 'Yes' in the previous field, allowing you to specify which branch of the Marine Corps you are interested in (Marine Corps, Army, Navy, Air Force).
* **Lead Source** (Lead Source, text, required: false): Provide any information about how you came across this opportunity or why you are interested in joining the Marine Corps.
* **Recruiter Name** (Recruiter Name, text, required: false): If you were directed to this form by a recruiter, please enter their name here.

## Tips
* Make sure to be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out this form, feel free to contact us.
* This form is a non-binding expression of interest and does not commit you to any service obligations.
