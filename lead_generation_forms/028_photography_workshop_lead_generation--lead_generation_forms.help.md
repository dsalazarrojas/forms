# Photography Workshop Lead Generation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us generate leads for our photography workshop. It collects information from interested individuals, including their contact details, workshop preferences, and source of lead generation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first page with your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Select the workshops or classes you are interested in by choosing multiple options from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose your preferred schedule for the workshop by selecting one option from the dropdown menu.
4. If you found out about our workshop through a specific channel, select the corresponding option.
5. Add any additional comments or questions you may have in the "Additional Comments" field.
6. Finally, review the terms and conditions and agree to them by selecting "Yes" from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Capture Lead Information** (`photography_workshop_lead_generation_page`): This is the first page of the form where you can enter your contact information.
* **First Name** (`lead_generation_form_first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`lead_generation_form_last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`photography_workshop_lead_generation_email`): Enter your email address. This is a required field to ensure that we can contact you.
* **What Workshop(s) are you interested in?** (`photography_workshop_lead_generation_workshop_interest`): Select multiple options from the dropdown menu to indicate which workshops or classes you are interested in.
* **Preferred Schedule** (`photography_workshop_lead_generation_preferred_schedule`): Select your preferred schedule for the workshop from the dropdown menu.
* **How did you find out about our workshop?** (`photography_workshop_lead_generation_lead_status`): Select the source from which you learned about our workshop.
* **Additional Comments** (`photography_workshop_lead_generation_additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Agree to Terms** (`photography_workshop_lead_generation_agree_terms`): Select "Yes" from the dropdown menu to agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Agree to Privacy** (`photography_workshop_lead_generation_agree_privacy`): Select "Yes" from the dropdown menu to agree to our privacy policy.
* **Submit** (`photography_workshop_lead_generation_submit`): Click this button to submit your lead.
