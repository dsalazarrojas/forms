# EMail Opt In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EMail Opt In Form is a simple form designed to collect basic information from users who want to opt-in to receive email communications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the EMail Opt In Form, follow these steps:
1. Select one of the options for the field "Email Opt In Form".
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Choose whether to agree or disagree with the "Consent" field.
4. (Optional) Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
5. (Optional) Enter your department in the "Department" field.
6. (Optional) Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
7. (Optional) Enter your role in the "Role" field.
8. (Optional) Enter your name in the "Name" field.
9. (Optional) Enter a title in the "Title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email Opt In Form** (`form_name`, select_one, required): Choose between Form Name and Alternative Form Name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Agree or disagree to be added to the email list.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name (optional).
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department (optional).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title (optional).
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Enter your role (optional).
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name (optional).
* **Title** (`title`, text, optional): Enter a title (optional).
