# Lead Magnet Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead Magnet Checklist Form is designed to help you quickly and efficiently collect relevant information from customers. It's a comprehensive form that covers various aspects of lead generation and lead capture, making it an essential tool for sales and marketing teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields from the "Lead Generation Fields" dropdown menu.
2. Choose the lead magnet checklist items that apply to your situation.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if required.
4. Optionally, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the email lists that apply to your situation, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Checklist Form** (`lead_magnet_checklist_form`, text, optional): This section is a text field where you can enter any relevant information related to your lead generation needs.
* **Lead Generation Fields** (`lead_generation_fields`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the relevant fields you need to generate leads, such as Field 1, Field 2, or Field 3.
* **Lead Magnet Checklist** (`lead_magnet_checklist`, select_one, optional): Choose a specific lead magnet checklist item that applies to your situation, such as Checklist 1, Checklist 2, or Checklist 3.
* **Lead Capture Fields** (`lead_capture_fields`, number, optional): This is a numerical field where you can enter the number of leads you're looking to capture.
* **Lead Capture** (`lead_capture`, select_one, optional): Select a specific lead capture option, such as Capture 1, Capture 2, or Capture 3.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if required.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if required.
* **Email List** (`email_list`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the relevant email lists that apply to your situation, such as List 1, List 2, or List 3.
