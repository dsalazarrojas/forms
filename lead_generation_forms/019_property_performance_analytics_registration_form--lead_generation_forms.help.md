# Property Performance Analytics Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from property owners and managers for property performance analytics. The purpose of this form is to gather data on properties, including their details, lead sources, and analytics preferences. This information will help us understand how properties are performing and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each field.
3. Make sure to review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Details (Page 1)** (`property_details`, text, optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter any relevant details about the property, such as its address, size, or features.
* **Lead Details (Page 1)** (`lead_details`, text, optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter information about the leads associated with the property, including sources and conversion rates.
* **Contact Details (Page 1)** (`contact_details`, text, optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter your contact information, such as your name and email address.
* **Property Performance (Page 1)** (`property_performance`, text, optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter performance metrics for the property, such as its revenue or profit margins.
* **Analytics Preferences (Page 1)** (`analytics_preferences`, text, optional)
	+ This field is where you can indicate how you would like to receive analytics data for the property.
* **Review Details (Page 1)** (`review_details`, text, optional)
	+ This field is where you can provide any additional information about the property, such as its current status or future plans.
* **Upload Files (Page 1)** (`upload_files`, text, optional)
	+ This field is where you can upload any relevant files, such as documents or images, related to the property.
