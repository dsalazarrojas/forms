# Designer Entry Form OPEN Runway Video Series - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Designer Entry Form "OPEN Runway Video Series" is designed to collect information from designers who are interested in participating in an open call for video series submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill out the form with your information.
3. Ensure you have filled out all required fields (email is required).
4. Submit the form to initiate the submission process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Designer** (id: 1, type: text, required: false): 
This field is meant to be filled out with the name of the form designer.
* **Email** (id: 2, type: email, required: true): 
Please enter your valid email address. We will use this to get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (id: 3, type: text, required: false): 
You can enter your phone number, but it's optional.
* **Title** (id: 4, type: text, required: false): 
Enter a brief title for your design submission.
* **Description** (id: 5, type: text, required: false): 
Provide a short description of your design submission.

## Tips
