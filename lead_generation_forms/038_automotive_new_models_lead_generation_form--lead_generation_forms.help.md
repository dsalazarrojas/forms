# Automotive New Models Lead Generation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help our team understand your preferences and interests in our new models, allowing us to provide you with more personalized and relevant information and assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information, including your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address.
2. Select your interested models from the list provided.
3. Choose your vehicle type preference (e.g. Sedan, SUV, etc.).
4. Choose your budget range.
5. Select when you plan to purchase the vehicle.
6. Indicate if you have a vehicle to trade-in.
7. Let us know if you're interested in financing options.
8. Select your preferred contact method and best time to contact you.
9. Choose your preferred color, transmission type, and desired features.
10. Select how you heard about us and if you've visited our dealership.
11. Let us know if you're interested in scheduling a test drive.
12. Add any additional notes or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Interested in Which New Models** (`interested_models`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the new models you are interested in (you can choose multiple models).
* **Vehicle Type Preference** (`vehicle_type_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of vehicle you prefer (e.g. Sedan, SUV, etc.).
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required): Select your budget range for the new vehicle.
* **When Are You Planning to Purchase** (`purchase_timeline`, `select_one`, required): Choose when you plan to purchase the new vehicle (e.g. within 1 month, 3-6 months, etc.).
* **Do You Have a Vehicle to Trade-In** (`trade_in_available`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have a vehicle to trade-in.
* **Interested in Financing Options** (`financing_interest`, `select_one`, required): Let us know if you're interested in financing options for your new vehicle.
* **Preferred Color** (`color_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred color for the new vehicle.
* **Transmission Preference** (`transmission_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred transmission type.
* **Desired Features or Options** (`desired_features`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the desired features or options you want in your new vehicle (e.g. sunroof, leather interior, etc.).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred contact method (e.g. phone, email, etc.).
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_contact`, `select_one`, required): Choose the best time to contact you.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you heard about us (e.g. website, social media, etc.).
* **Have You Visited Our Dealership** (`dealership_visit`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you've visited our dealership.
* **Interested in Scheduling a Test Drive** (`test_drive_interest`, `select_one`, required): Let us know if you're interested in scheduling a test drive.
* **Additional Notes or Questions** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or questions you may have.
