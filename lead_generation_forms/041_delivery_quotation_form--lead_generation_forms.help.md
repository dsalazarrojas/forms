# Delivery Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from customers to create a delivery quotation for a specific product. This form is used to collect customer details and product information to provide a quote for delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the customer's name in the `Customer` field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the `Email` field. This is an optional field, but it's useful for communication purposes.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the `Phone` field.
4. Select the product you would like to deliver from the list of available products in the `Product` field.
5. Enter the quantity of the product you would like to deliver in the `Quantity` field.
6. Choose a date for delivery from the calendar in the `Delivery Date` field.
7. Select a time for delivery from the available options in the `Delivery Time` field.
8. Add any additional comments about the delivery in the `Comment` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer (Customer, text, required: false)**: Enter the customer's name.
- **Email (Email, email, required: false)**: Enter the customer's email address. This field is optional but helps with communication.
- **Phone (Phone, text, required: false)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
- **Product (Product, select_one, required: false)**: Select the product you would like to deliver from the list of available options.
- **Quantity (Quantity, number, required: false)**: Enter the quantity of the product you would like to deliver.
- **Delivery Date (Delivery Date, date, required: false)**: Choose a date for delivery from the calendar.
- **Delivery Time (Delivery Time, time, required: false)**: Select a time for delivery from the available options.
- **Comment (Comment, note, required: false)**: Add any additional comments about the delivery.
