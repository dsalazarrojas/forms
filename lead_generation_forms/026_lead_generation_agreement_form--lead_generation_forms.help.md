# lead-generation-agreement-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Lead Generation Agreement Form" is a tool used to collect essential information from users, which is then utilized by the sales team to initiate follow-up actions and provide relevant information about products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your Company name in the "Company" field.
3. If applicable, provide the Agreement Terms, which can include any specific conditions or details about your business.
4. Optionally, add any additional comments in the "Lead Generation Agreement Form Comments" field.
5. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
6. If you have a Phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
7. Provide the Date and Time of your availability in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
8. If necessary, provide any additional terms or conditions related to the Lead Generation Agreement Form in the "Lead Generation Agreement Form Terms" field.
9. Finally, select the status of the form from the dropdown menu in the "Lead Generation Agreement Form Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, Required): Your full First Name.
  This field captures your first name to help the sales team identify you and personalize their communication.
- **Last Name** (Text, Required): Your full Last Name.
  Your last name is required for accurate record keeping and communication.
- **Company** (Text, Required): Your Company name or name of your business.
  This field helps the sales team understand your affiliation and industry.
- **Agreement Terms** (Text, Optional): Any specific conditions or details about your business.
  This field allows you to provide any necessary information about your business.
- **Lead Generation Agreement Form Comments** (Text, Optional): Additional comments about this form.
  This field is for any additional information you might want to share with the sales team.
- **Email** (Email, Required): Your email address.
  This field is used to communicate with you effectively.
- **Phone** (Text, Optional): Your Phone number.
  If you have a phone number, please enter it for easier communication.
- **Date** (Date, Required): The Date of your availability.
  This field is used to schedule meetings or follow-up actions.
- **Time** (Time, Required): The Time of your availability.
  This field helps the sales team schedule meetings accordingly.
- **Lead Generation Agreement Form Terms** (Text, Optional): Additional terms or conditions related to the Lead Generation Agreement Form.
  This field is for any further details or conditions about your business.
- **Lead Generation Agreement Form Status** (Select One, Optional): The status of the form.
  This field helps the sales team track the progress of your form submission.
- **Lead Generation Agreement Form Comments** (Text, Optional): Any additional comments about this form.
  This field is for any further information or clarification about your submission.
