# strength_empire_consult_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "strength_empire_consult_form" is designed to gather contact information and goal data from users. Fill out this form to provide accurate and relevant details for our team to assist you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Contact Info" section with your contact details (email, phone number, etc.).
2. In the "Goal Object" field, describe your goal or what you want to achieve.
3. In the "Goal Scope" field, specify the scope of your goal (short-term or long-term).
4. If applicable, select the reasons you want to get started with your goal (personal, professional, financial, etc.).
5. Indicate how long you're willing to work on your goal (in number of days or weeks).
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Provide information about the lead source (if applicable).
9. Add any additional lead information (if applicable).
10. Leave any comments or additional information about your lead (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info (1)**: Enter your email address to help us contact you.
	+ Type: `email`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Goal Object (2)**: Describe your goal or what you want to achieve.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Goal Scope (3)**: Specify the scope of your goal (short-term or long-term).
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **How do you want to get started? (4)**: Select one or more reasons for getting started with your goal.
	+ Type: `select_multiple`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Why do you want to get started with this goal? (5)**: Select one or more reasons for getting started with your goal.
	+ Type: `select_multiple`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Goal Scope (6)**: Specify the scope of your goal.
	+ Type: `select_one`
	+ Required: `false`
* **How long are you willing to work on your goals? (7)**: Enter the number of days or weeks you're willing to work on your goal.
	+ Type: `number`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Email (8)**: Enter your email address.
	+ Type: `email`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Phone (9)**: Enter your phone number.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Lead Source (10)**: Provide information about the lead source (if applicable).
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Lead Info (11)**: Enter additional lead information (if applicable).
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **More Lead Info (12)**: Add any additional lead information (if applicable).
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Lead Comments (13)**: Leave comments or additional information about your lead (if applicable).
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`

## Tips
