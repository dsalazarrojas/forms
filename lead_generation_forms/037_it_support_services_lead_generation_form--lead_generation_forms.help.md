# IT Support Services Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and service details from users, helping to generate leads for IT support services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact form (page 1) by providing your contact information.
2. On the second page, fill out the service form (page 2) by describing the services you are interested in.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Lead Generation Form** (page 1):
  - **Contact Form** (contact_form, text, required):
    Fill out your name, email, and phone number for us to get in touch with you.
  - **Lead Generation Form** (lead_generation_form, text, required):
    Describe the services you are interested in, so we can understand your needs better.

- **Contact Form** (page 2):
  - **Contact Form** (contact_form, text, required):
    Fill out your name, email, and phone number for us to get in touch with you.
  - **Service Form** (service_form, text, required):
    Describe the services you are interested in, so we can understand your needs better.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to help us better understand your needs.
