# prospect_information_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The prospect_information_collection_form is a form designed to collect essential details about a prospect, including their name, contact information, company details, and job-related information. This information helps our sales team understand the prospect's needs, preferences, and pain points, enabling us to provide personalized services and build strong relationships.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Prospect Name** in the first field. This is a basic field to help us get started with your information.
2. Provide your **Email** address in the second field. This is your primary contact point.
3. Enter your **Phone** number in the third field. This ensures we can reach you for any follow-up discussions.
4. Fill in your **Company Name** in the fourth field. This helps us understand your business context.
5. Specify your **Job Title** in the fifth field. This is crucial for targeted communication.
6. Indicate your **Company Size** in the sixth field. Choose from Small, Medium, or Large to help us gauge your company's scale.
7. Select your **Company Type** in the seventh field. This helps us tailor our services according to your industry's needs.
8. Choose your **Industry** (or industries) from the eighth field. Select all relevant options to help us understand your business landscape.
9. Select your **Job Function** in the ninth field. This is key to our targeted communication and support.
10. Fill in your **Location** in the tenth field. This helps us understand your regional context.
11. If your **Job Title** doesn't match any of the listed options, type in your **Job Title Other** in the eleventh field.
12. If you have a website for your company, enter the URL in the **Company Website** field.
13. If your **Job Function** doesn't match any of the listed options, type in your **Job Function Other** in the twelfth field.
14. If you're in an industry not listed, specify your **Other Industry** in the thirteenth field.
15. If your **Job Function** is not listed, type in your **Other Job Function** in the fourteenth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prospect Name**: A field for your name, helping us initiate communication.
* **Email**: Your primary contact email address.
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number.
* **Company Name**: Your business name or name of your company.
* **Job Title**: Your job title or position.
* **Company Size**: Indicate your company size for targeted support.
* **Company Type**: Choose your company type from our options to help us tailor our services.
* **Industry**: Select all applicable industries related to your business.
* **Job Function**: Select your job function for targeted support and communication.
* **Location**: Your company's location or area of operation.
* **Job Title Other**: If your job title doesn't match the listed options, type it here.
* **Job Function Other**: If your job function doesn't match the listed options, type it here.
* **Other Industry**: If your industry is not listed, type it here.
* **Other Job Function**: If your job function is not listed, type it here.
* **Company Website**: The URL of your company's website.
* **Location**: Your business location or area of operation.
