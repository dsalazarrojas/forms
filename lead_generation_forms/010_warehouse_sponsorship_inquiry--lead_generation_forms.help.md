# Warehouse Sponsorship Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Sponsorship Inquiry form is a tool used to collect information for sponsoring a warehouse. It is essential to provide accurate details to increase the chances of your inquiry being successfully evaluated.

## How To Complete This Form
To successfully complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Category** field with the most relevant option from the dropdown list. The category will help us to direct your inquiry to the correct department.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the format `example@example.com` and your **Phone** number with the relevant option selected (Landline, Mobile, or Other).
3. Fill in the **Lead Owner** field with the name of the person responsible for the inquiry.
4. Provide your **Warehouse Address** with the full address including street name and number.
5. Select your **City** from the options provided or type it in if not listed.
6. Choose your **Country** from the dropdown list.
7. Select your **State** from the provided options or type it in if not listed.
8. Enter your **Postal Code** in the format `1234 AA1 1AA`.
9. Enter the desired **Lead Date** in the format `DD-MM-YYYY`.
10. If applicable, select the desired **Lead Time** in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
11. Optionally, add any additional **Note** about your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Warehouse Sponsorship Inquiry**: This is the title of the form, which will appear at the top of the page.
* **Category**: Choose the category that best describes your inquiry. The options provided are Lead Generation Forms and Other.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format `example@example.com`. This will be used as contact information.
* **Phone**: Select your phone type and enter your phone number. The options are Landline, Mobile, and Other.
* **Lead Owner**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the inquiry.
* **Warehouse Address**: Enter the full address of your warehouse, including the street name and number.
* **City**: Enter the city where your warehouse is located.
* **Country**: Choose your country from the dropdown list.
* **State**: Select your state from the provided options or type it in if not listed.
* **Postal Code**: Enter your postal code in the format `1234 AA1 1AA`.
* **Lead Date**: Enter the desired date for the lead in the format `DD-MM-YYYY`.
* **Lead Time**: If applicable, select the desired lead time in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Note**: Optionally, add any additional note about your inquiry.
