> # Confidential Business Profile Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Confidential Business Profile Registration Form is designed to collect information about your business to help us understand your investment strategies, interests, and preferences. This information will be used to provide you with relevant real estate leads and facilitate potential partnerships or collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose and requirements.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about your business.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, required/true): Enter the full legal name of your business.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, required/true): Enter the physical address of your business.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, required/true): Select the sector that best describes your business (e.g., Real Estate, Finance, Healthcare, Technology, Manufacturing, Other).
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, optional/true): If applicable, enter the number of years your business has been operating.
* **Primary Investment Strategy** (`investment_strategy`, required/true): Select the strategy that best describes your approach (e.g., Buy and Hold, Fix and Flip, Commercial Development, Wholesale, Land Acquisition).
* **Target Geographic Markets** (`target_markets`, required/true): List the cities or regions where you're interested in investing.
* **Typical Investment Capacity** (`investment_capacity`, required/true): Select the range of your typical investment capacity (e.g., Under $500k, $500k - $1M, $1M - $5M, $5M - $10M, Over $10M).
* **Preferred Property Types** (`preferred_property_types`, required/true): Select all the property types that you're interested in investing in (e.g., Single Family Residential, Multi-Family, Office, Retail, Industrial).
* **Primary Contact Person** (`contact_person_name`, required/true): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your business.
* **Professional Email Address** (`contact_email`, required/true): Enter a professional email address that can be used to receive real estate leads.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, required/true): Enter your business's contact phone number.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, required/true): Please review and agree to our confidentiality agreement.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed to prevent incomplete submissions.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
