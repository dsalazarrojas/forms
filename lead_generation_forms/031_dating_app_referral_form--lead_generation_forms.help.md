# Dating App Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect referrals for the dating app. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that the referral is valid.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your first name.
* Enter your last name.
* Enter your email address.
* Enter your phone number.
* Enter your friend's first name.
* Enter your friend's last name.
* Enter your friend's email address.
* Select how you met your friend (e.g., through friends, at a party, through social media, etc.).
* Enter the friend's reference code.
* Enter the friend's reason for referral.
* Enter the friend's referral source.
* Enter your company name.
* Enter your job title.
* (Optional) Enter your department.
* (Optional) Enter your location.
* (Optional) Select your job position.
* (Optional) Select your job level.
* (Optional) Select your job function.
* (Optional) Enter a brief description of the job.
* (Optional) Enter relevant job tags.
