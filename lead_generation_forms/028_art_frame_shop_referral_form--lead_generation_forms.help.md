# Art Frame Shop Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Frame Shop Referral Form is designed to collect information from existing customers who are willing to refer their friends and family members to our shop. By filling out this form, you will be providing us with essential details about the person being referred, such as their name, email, and framing requirements. This information will help us follow up with the referred person and potentially convert them into a new customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form.
2. Fill out each field with the required information.
3. Review your submission before submitting it to ensure everything is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### -- Referrer Information --
* **Your Full Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Your Relationship to the Business**: Choose how you are connected to the business:
	+ Existing Customer
	+ Interior Designer
	+ Artist
	+ Gallery Partner
	+ Employee
* **Your Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for any follow-up questions.
### -- Referral (New Lead) Information --
* **Name of the Person being Referred**: Enter the name of the person being referred.
* **Referral Email Address**: Enter the email address of the person being referred.
* **Referral Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person being referred.
### -- Framing Requirements --
* **What type of framing service are they interested in?**: Check all that apply:
	+ Custom Picture Framing
	+ Canvas Stretching
	+ Mirror Framing
	+ Memorabilia Shadowbox
	+ Conservation - Museum Grade
	+ Large Format Framing
* **Estimated Number of Items**: If you know how many items are being framed, enter that number here.
* **How soon are they looking to start the project?**: Choose how soon the project is expected to start:
	+ Immediately
	+ Within 1 month
	+ Just exploring options
* **Any additional notes about this referral?**: Enter any additional information you think would be helpful for us to know.
### -- Referral Reward --
* **Preferred Reward (If referral leads to a sale)**: Choose your preferred reward:
	+ Store Credit
	+ Discount on Next Order
	+ Gift Card
	+ No Reward Needed
### Referral Date
* **Referral Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
