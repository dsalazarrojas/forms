# real_estate_lead_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_lead_form is designed to collect lead information from potential customers in the real estate industry. The form captures customer contact details, preferred communication methods, and property type preferences to facilitate lead follow-up and provide personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the first two fields.
2. Provide your Email and Phone numbers in the subsequent fields.
3. Select your preferred contact method from the options provided.
4. Choose the type of property you are interested in (e.g., Single Family, Multi Family, etc.).
5. Select your desired Move-in Date.
6. Choose the source of your lead (e.g., Referral, Online Ad, etc.).
7. Indicate if you were referred to the agent through any other source.
8. If applicable, provide details about the property type you are interested in.
9. For each property type, describe the details in the corresponding text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number for communication.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`Preferred Contact Method`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Chat).
* **Property Type** (`Property Type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of property you are interested in (e.g., Single Family, Multi Family, etc.).
* **Desired Move-in Date** (`Desired Move-in Date`, date, optional): Select your preferred move-in date.
* **Lead Source** (`Lead Source`, select_one, optional): Choose the source of your lead (e.g., Referral, Online Ad, etc.).
* **Lead Source Submitted Through** (`Lead Source Submitted Through`, select_one, optional): If your lead was submitted through a different source, select it here.
* **Other Lead Source** (`Other Lead Source`, text, optional): If your lead was submitted through a different source, provide details about it.
* **Agent Preferred** (`Agent Preferred`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have a preferred agent for your lead.
* **Agent Preferred Reason** (`Agent Preferred Reason`, text, optional): Provide the reason for your preferred agent.
* **Agent Contact Method** (`Agent Contact Method`, select_one, optional): Choose the preferred method of contact for your agent.
* **Agent Preferred Language** (`Agent Preferred Language`, select_one, optional): Choose the preferred language for communication with your agent.
* **Agent Preferred Day of Week** (`Agent Preferred Day of Week`, select_one, optional): Choose the preferred day of the week for communication with your agent.
* **Agent Preferred Time of Day** (`Agent Preferred Time of Day`, select_one, optional): Choose the preferred time of day for communication with your agent.
* **Customer Preferred Day of Week** (`Customer Preferred Day of Week`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the preferred day(s) of the week for communication with your agent.
* **Customer Preferred Time of Day** (`Customer Preferred Time of Day`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the preferred time(s) of day for communication with your agent.
* **Property Type Details** (`Property Type Details`, text, optional): Provide additional details about the property type you are interested in.
* **Property Type Details 1-5** (`Property Type Details 1-5`, text, optional): Provide additional details about each property type you are interested in.

## Tips
* Use the form to capture essential customer information for lead follow-up.
* Ensure that you select the preferred communication methods and days of the week for both you and your agent.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, reach out to us directly.
