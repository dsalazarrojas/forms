# Property Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Property Inquiry Form is a tool used to collect information about a property from a user. It is used to gather details such as property type, number of bedrooms, number of bathrooms, square footage, property price, location, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the relevant information about the property you are inquiring about.
2. Select the type of property from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose the number of bedrooms and bathrooms you are inquiring about.
4. Enter the square footage of the property.
5. Enter the property price.
6. Enter the location of the property.
7. Enter your name, phone number, and email address (if you wish to be contacted for more information).
8. Enter any additional message you may have about the property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Inquiry** (`property_inquiry_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the property you are inquiring about.
* **What type of property do you want to inquire about?** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of property you are inquiring about (e.g. House, Apartment, Condo, etc.).
* **Number of Bedrooms** (`number_of_bedrooms`, select_one, optional): Select the number of bedrooms in the property.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (`number_of_bathrooms`, select_one, optional): Select the number of bathrooms in the property.
* **Square Footage** (`square_footage`, number, optional): Enter the square footage of the property.
* **Property Price** (`property_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the property.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the property.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Enter any additional message you may have about the property.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you are inquiring about a specific property, please make sure to provide all relevant information.
* If you have any questions or need clarification about the form, do not hesitate to contact us.
