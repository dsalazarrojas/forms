# Social Media Marketing Client Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from clients about their social media marketing needs, goals, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much relevant information as possible.
2. Review each question carefully and make sure to provide accurate and clear information.
3. If you need help or have any questions, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your business goal for lead generation?** (<lead_generation_goal, text, optional): This is an opportunity for you to describe your business goal, such as increasing sales, improving brand awareness, or promoting a specific product/service. Please provide a brief and clear description of your goal.
* **What is your target audience?** (<target_audience, text, optional): Describe your target audience in terms of demographics, interests, or behaviors. For example, you can specify age, location, or industry.
* **What marketing channels are you interested in?** (<marketing_channels, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the marketing channels you are interested in, such as Social Media, Email Marketing, or Content Marketing.
* **What social media platforms do you have a presence on?** (<social_media_platforms, select_multiple, optional): List all the social media platforms where you have an account, such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, or Twitter.
* **What is your marketing budget?** (<marketing_budget, number, optional): Estimate your marketing budget for the campaign, including any relevant details about budget allocation or limitations.
* **On what date would you like to start the campaign?** (<timeline, date, optional): Specify the desired start date for the campaign.
* **Which time zone do you operate in?** (<time_zone, select_one, optional): Select the time zone you operate in to ensure we can coordinate with you effectively.
* **What is your contact information?** (<contact_info, note, optional): Provide your contact information, including email, phone number, or any other relevant details.
