### Find A Local Dealer - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form helps users find local dealers for various products, such as automobiles, bicycles, electronics, furniture, home appliances, home decor, and industrial equipment. It is designed to be simple and straightforward, allowing users to input their necessary details to receive relevant information on local dealers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your user information in the "User Information" section.
*   Provide your location details in the "Location Information" section.
*   Select your product interests from the options in the "Product Interests" section.
*   Choose how you'd like to be contacted from the options in the "Contact Preference" section.
*   Select your preferred dealer search option from the options in the "Dealer Search" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information** (User Information, text, required/optional): This section is where you can enter your basic contact information such as name, email, and any other relevant details you want to share with the local dealers.
*   **Location Information** (Location Information, text, required/optional): Provide your location details such as city, state, or zip code to help local dealers find you more efficiently.
*   **Product Interests** (Product Interests, select multiple, required/optional): Choose the products you are interested in purchasing or dealing with, such as automobiles, bicycles, electronics, furniture, etc.
*   **Dealer Search** (Dealer Search, select one, required/optional): Choose how you'd like to find local dealers:
    -   Local Dealer: You'd like to find dealers who are local to your location.
    -   Nearby Dealer: You'd like to find local dealers, but also consider searching nationwide.
    -   National Dealer: You'd like to search nationwide.
*   **Contact Preference** (Contact Preference, select one, required/optional): Choose your preferred method of contact:
    -   Phone: You'd like to be contacted by phone.
    -   Email: You'd like to be contacted by email.
    -   Chat: You'd like to be contacted by in-app chat.
    -   In App: You'd like to be contacted through the app. 
*   **Tips**: Fill out this form carefully to ensure you get the most relevant results for your needs. If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can refer to this help guide for further clarification.
