# Lets_Get_To_Know_You_A_Little_Bit - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended to collect information about a person or business, including their contact details and preferences. The goal is to get a better understanding of who they are and what they do, so that [company/organization] can tailor their approach to connect with them.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as accurately and completely as possible. Make sure to review each field carefully to understand the information that will be gathered and how it will be used.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required: false): Enter your home address for the form. This will help [company/organization] understand your location.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name for the form.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name for the form.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the types of business that apply to you. Options include Home Based, Online, and any other options available.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, required: false): Describe your experience in this field. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your business or personal email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Website** (`website`, text, required: false): Enter your website URL.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, text, required: false): Enter your social media handle or URL.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select a time when you are most available to talk.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required: false): Enter a category that describes your business or work.
* **Tags** (`tags`, select_one, required: false): Choose one or more tags that apply to you. Options include Tag1, Tag2, and Tag3.
