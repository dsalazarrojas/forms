# product comparison lead form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information for product comparison leads. Users can input their name, email, phone number, and preferences regarding product 1 and product 2.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
	* First and last name.
2. Input your email address (we'll use this to communicate with you).
3. Provide your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).
4. For each product, choose either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your interest.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send your lead details to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (1): Enter your first name for our records.
* **Last name** (2): Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email** (3): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (4): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **product_1** (6): Choose 'Yes' if you are interested in product 1.
* **product_2** (7): Choose 'Yes' if you are interested in product 2.
* **product_3** (8): Choose 'Yes' if you are interested in product 3.
 
## Tips
* Be honest about your interests in product 1 and 2 to help us better understand your needs.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
