# solar_roof_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The solar_roof_survey_template form is designed to collect user information and data about their solar roof, with the goal of understanding their energy use and roof characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name in the First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Enter a valid email address in the Email field, if you wish to be contacted regarding your survey response.
3. Provide your phone number in the Phone Number field, if you would like to be contacted regarding your survey response.
4. Enter your home address in the Home Address field, if you would like to be contacted regarding your survey response.
5. Select the type of your roof in the Roof Type field. You have the following options:
	* Slate
	* Composite
	* Asphalt Shingle
	* Metal
	* Clay Tile
	* Wooden
6. Indicate your energy use in the Energy Use field. You can select one or more of the following options:
	* Low
	* Medium
	* High
	* Very High
7. Choose the size of your roof in the Roof Size field. You have the following options:
	* Small
	* Medium
	* Large
8. Indicate the age of your roof in the Roof Age field. You can select one or more of the following options:
	* New
	* Old
	* Very Old
9. Choose your energy efficiency in the Energy Efficiency field. You can select one or more of the following options:
	* High
	* Medium
	* Low
