# Email Capture Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Email Capture Form is used to gather information about a person's email preferences and details to help the company's marketing and customer service teams tailor their communication and provide a better service to the user.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Email Capture Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. If you have a MailChimp email address, select 'Yes' or 'No' in the MailChimp Email field.
4. Select your Email Provider from the list (if you have multiple providers, select all that apply).
5. You can provide a short description of why you are interested in being contacted.
6. If you would like to be contacted, enter your phone number in the Phone field.
7. Enter your availability in the Date field (MM/DD/YYYY) and Time (HH:MM).
8. If you would like to be contacted, select your country from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address here. This will be used to contact you.
* **MailChimp Email** (`mailchimp_email`, `select_one`, **optional**): If you have a MailChimp account, select 'Yes' to indicate you do. This will help us tailor our communication.
* **Email Provider** (`email_provider`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select all providers you have (e.g., Gmail, Outlook).
* **Description** (`email_capture_6`, `text`, **optional**): Provide a short description of why you are interested in being contacted.
* **Phone** (`email_capture_7`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted.
* **Date** (`email_capture_8`, `date`, **optional**): Enter your availability date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (`email_capture_9`, `time`, **optional**): Enter your availability time in the format HH:MM.
* **Country** (`email_capture_10`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select your country to help us understand your location.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you have a MailChimp email address, select 'Yes' to help us tailor our communication.
* If you would like to be contacted, provide your phone number and select your country.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us.
