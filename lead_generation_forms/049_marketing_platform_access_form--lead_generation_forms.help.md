# Marketing Platform Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Platform Access Form is a form designed to collect information from users for access to our marketing platform. This form helps us understand user interests and needs to provide tailored solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the form completely and honestly, ensuring you provide accurate information.
2.  Review each field carefully and ensure you understand its purpose before proceeding.
3.  Click on the 'Submit' button once you have finished filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for entering your name and contact information.
* **Access Level** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the level of access you require for our marketing platform: Basic, Advanced, or Full.
* **Marketing Platform** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose which marketing platforms you are interested in accessing.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`6`, `note`, required/optional): Leave any additional information or comments.
* **Assigned Tool** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the tool you are interested in using.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out.
* If you've submitted the form previously, please do not resubmit without consulting with us first.
