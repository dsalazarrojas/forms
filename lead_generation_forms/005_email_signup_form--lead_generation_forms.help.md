# email_signup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The email signup form is designed to collect contact information and preferences from users who want to sign up for a newsletter or subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the first field.
2. Provide your email address in the second field.
3. Select whether you'd like to be contacted by checking "I'd love to be contacted" field.
4. If applicable, enter your company name in the "Company" field.
5. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
6. Select your department from the list provided in the "Department" field.
7. Enter your job function in the "Job Function" field (if applicable).
8. Enter your location in the "Location" field (if applicable).
9. Enter your job level in the "Job Level" field (if applicable).
10. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (if applicable).
11. Provide your LinkedIn profile link in the "LinkedIn" field (if applicable).
12. Enter your website URL in the "Website" field (if applicable).
13. Select your industry from the list provided in the "Industry" field.
14. Choose your job type from the list provided in the "Job Type" field.
15. Select your job status from the list provided in the "Job Status" field.
16. Indicate whether you have a manager in the "Manager" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **email** (`2`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **I'd love to be contacted** (`3`, `select_one`, optional): Check this field if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Company** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Department** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your department from the list provided (e.g., Sales, Marketing, Product, Support).
* **Job Function** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter your job function (if applicable).
* **Location** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter your location (if applicable).
* **Job Level** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter your job level (if applicable).
* **Phone Number** (`10`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **LinkedIn** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter your LinkedIn profile link (if applicable).
* **Website** (`12`, `text`, optional): Enter your website URL (if applicable).
* **Industry** (`13`, `select_one`, optional): Select your industry from the list provided (e.g., Finance, Technology, Healthcare, Marketing, Other).
* **Job Type** (`14`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your job type from the list provided (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Freelance, Intern, Other).
* **Job Status** (`15`, `select_one`, optional): Select your job status from the list provided (e.g., Active, Inactive, On leave, On a leave of absence, Other).
* **Manager** (`16`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether you have a manager (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields (email, I'd love to be contacted) to ensure that we have a complete picture of your preferences.
* You can skip any section that doesn't apply to you.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
