# SDC Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information for generating and managing policy applications. It serves as a guide to help you fill out your application accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and make sure you have the required information ready.
2. Fill out the form page by page, starting from the first page with your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone.
3. Continue to the following pages to fill out the remaining fields such as Insurance Type, Policy Type, Date of Birth, Date of Application, and Date of Issue.
4. Provide the necessary details for Policy Number, Customer Name, Business Entity, and Employee Name.
5. Select the correct Business Entity Type and Employee Relationship from the options provided.
6. Enter the Policy Status, Policy Effective Date, and Policy Expiration Date.
7. Fill out the Policy Issuer fields with the necessary details.
8. Enter the Policy Holder Name and Policy Holder Phone (if applicable).
9. Review your form before submission to ensure that all necessary fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Insurance Type** (5, select_one, required): Choose the type of insurance you are applying for (Life, Health, Auto, Home, Other).
* **Policy Type** (6, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of policy you are applying for (Term, Whole, Annuity).
* **Date of Birth** (7, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Date of Application** (8, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this application.
* **Date of Issue** (9, date, required): Enter the date your policy is effective.
* **Policy Number** (10, text, required): Enter the number of your policy.
* **Customer Name** (11, text, required): Enter the name of the policy holder.
* **Business Entity** (12, text, required): Enter the name of the business entity.
* **Employee Name** (13, text, required): Enter the name of the employee if applicable.
* **Business Entity Type** (14, select_one, required): Choose the type of the business entity (Sole, Corp, LLC, DBA, Other).
* **Employee Relationship** (15, select_multiple, required): Choose the relationship between the policy holder and employee (Owner, Employee, Partner).
* **Policy Status** (16, select_one, required): Choose the status of your policy (Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Policy Effective Date** (17, date, required): Enter the effective date of your policy.
* **Policy Expiration Date** (18, date, required): Enter the expiration date of your policy.
* **Policy Issuer** (19, text, required): Enter the name of the policy issuer.
* **Policy Issuer Address** (20, text, required): Enter the address of the policy issuer.
* **Policy Issuer Phone** (21, text, required): Enter the phone number of the policy issuer.
* **Policy Issuer Email** (22, email, required): Enter the email address of the policy issuer.
* **Policy Issuer Notes** (23, text, required): Enter any additional notes or comments from the policy issuer.
* **Policy Holder Name** (24, text, required): Enter the name of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Phone** (25, text, required): Enter the phone number of the policy holder.
