# Startup Investor Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for potential investors to submit their contact information and inquire about investor opportunities in the startup sector. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that your inquiry is correctly routed to the relevant teams.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and title in the "investor_name" field.
2. Select the type of investor opportunity you are looking for (Early Stage, Late Stage, Growth Stage) in the "inquiry_type" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter any additional comments or questions you have in the "message" field.
6. Enter the name of the startup you are interested in in the "startup_name" field.
7. Enter the website of the startup you are interested in in the "website" field.
8. Enter the industry the startup operates in in the "industry" field.
9. Enter the location of the startup in the "location" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "investor_email" field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "investor_phone" field.
