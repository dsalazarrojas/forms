# Financial Optimization Program Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Optimization Program Contact Form is designed to allow users to contact the financial optimization program team to inquire about the program, express interest in it, or provide feedback. Fill out this form to get in touch with the team and receive guidance on the next steps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. If you're affiliated with a company, enter the name of the company in the "Company" field. (This is optional.)
6. If you have a specific "Role" or position within the company, enter that in the "Role" field. (This is optional.)
7. Select the "Program of Interest" that matches your goals and objectives.
8. Write a detailed "Message" to the team explaining your interest, questions, or feedback.
9. If you have a specific "Financial Goal" in mind, enter it in the field. (This is optional.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you use regularly. This will be used to contact you and potentially send you follow-up information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as it appears on your identification or device. This will help the team contact you if needed.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you're affiliated with a company, enter the name of the company.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): If you have a specific role or position within the company, enter it here.
* **Program of Interest** (`program_of_interest`, text, optional): Select the program that best matches your goals and objectives.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required): Write a detailed message to the team explaining your interest, questions, or feedback.
* **Financial Goal** (`financial_goal`, text, optional): If you have a specific financial goal in mind, enter it here.
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, optional): If you want to provide additional context, enter the industry you're in.
