# Schedule Your Exploratory Call - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information to schedule an exploratory call with a representative. Please fill out the form if you are interested in learning more about a service or solution and would like to discuss it with one of our representatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2.  Provide accurate and relevant information in each field.
3.  Review and submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Exploratory Call Details
* **Exploratory Call Details** (`exploratory_call_details`, `text`, optional):
    This field is intended to gather any additional information you would like to share with the representative about your exploratory call. Be as detailed as possible, as this information will help the representative better understand your needs and interests.

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional):
    Please enter your first name.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional):
    Enter your valid business email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional):
    Enter your phone number.

### Company
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional):
    Enter your company name.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional):
    Enter your job title or position.

### Role
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, optional):
    Enter your role or position within your company.

### Industry
* **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, optional):
    Enter your industry or field of work.

## Tips

*   Please ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting.
*   Be as detailed and honest as possible when providing information to get the most out of your exploratory call.
*   You will receive a notification once your form has been received and reviewed.
