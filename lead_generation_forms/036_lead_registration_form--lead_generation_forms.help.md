# Lead Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lead Registration Form is designed to collect information about partners and their contacts, helping our sales team categorize and follow up on potential leads.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Use the provided hints to guide you through the form.
3. Review the fields for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the partner you are registering.
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person. Please ensure the email address is valid and correctly formatted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person. Please use the international format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Lead Type** (`lead_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of lead you are registering (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the partner. Please ensure the email address is valid and correctly formatted.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the partner. Please use the international format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
