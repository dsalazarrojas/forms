# Technology Deployment Partner Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us identify potential partners for technology deployment and guide you through a series of questions to better understand your company's capabilities and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question accurately and completely.
2. Choose the correct options for the "tech_deployment_partners" field.
3. Select one area of interest for the "areas_of_interest" field.
4. Enter your company's regions of operation in the "regions" field.
5. Describe your company's partnership interests and goals in the "partner_interests" field.
6. Provide your contact name in the "contact_name" field.
7. Enter your company's email address in the "email" field.
8. Enter your company's phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **tech_deployment_partners**: Select all that apply:
	+ Yes: Are you interested in deploying technology solutions with us?
	+ No: Are you not interested in deploying technology solutions with us?
* **areas_of_interest**: Select one:
	+ partner inquiries: Are you interested in partnering with us to provide solutions for our customers?
	+ software vendor services: Are you interested in offering software solutions to our customers?
	+ it providers: Are you interested in providing IT services to our customers?
* **regions**: Enter the regions where your company operates.
* **partner_interests**: Describe your company's goals and interests for partnering with us.
* **contact_name**: Enter your name and title.
* **email**: Enter your company's email address.
* **phone**: Enter your company's phone number.
