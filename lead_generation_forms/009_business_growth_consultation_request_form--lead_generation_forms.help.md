This form is designed to collect information from businesses seeking a growth consultation service. The purpose of the form is to gather essential details about the company, their contact information, and their requirements for the consultation.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

### How To Complete This Form

1. **Request Details** (page 1): This is the introduction page of the form, and it's just a placeholder to help guide the user through the process.
2. **Company Name** (page 2):
	* Enter the legal name of your business.
3. **Contact Name** (page 2):
	* Enter the name of the person requesting the consultation.
4. **Contact Email** (page 2):
	* Enter the best email address for us to schedule the consultation with.
5. **Phone Number** (page 2):
	* Optionally enter your phone number for easier contact.
6. **Business Stage** (page 3):
	* Select the current stage of your business: *Startup*, *Growth*, *Established*, or *Scaling*.
7. **Consultation Goals** (page 3):
	* Describe the key objectives you hope to achieve during the consultation.
8. **Budget Range** (page 3):
	* Select an estimated budget for the consulting services: *Under 5k*, *5k to 20k*, *20k to 50k*, or *Over 50k*.
9. **Preferred Contact Method** (page 3):
	* Choose how you would like to be contacted: *Email*, *Phone*, or *Video Call*.
10. **Best Times to Contact** (page 3):
	* Provide your preferred availability windows for us to schedule the consultation.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the official name of your business.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person requesting the consultation.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the best email address for scheduling the consultation.
* **Phone Number**: Optionally enter your phone number for easier contact.
* **Business Stage**: Select the current stage of your business.
* **Consultation Goals**: Describe the key objectives for the consultation.
* **Budget Range**: Select an estimated budget for the consulting services.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted.
* **Best Times to Contact**: Provide your preferred availability windows for the consultation.

### Tips

* Please ensure you fill out all required fields to avoid delays in our response.
* You can adjust the budget range if your actual budget for the consultation changes.
* We'll use the provided contact information to schedule the consultation, so make sure it's accurate.
