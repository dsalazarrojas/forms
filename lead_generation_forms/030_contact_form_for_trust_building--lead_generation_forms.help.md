# Contact Form For Trust Building - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their contact and preferences for communication. It is intended to help us understand how they found out about our company and what aspects of our reputation built their trust. The form also serves as a means to gather more information from the user, such as their primary communication goal and preferred response method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose how you heard about us (e.g., referral from a friend, online search, etc.).
3. Select one or multiple aspects of our reputation that built your trust.
4. Indicate what you hope to achieve with this contact (e.g., information gathering, consultation, etc.).
5. Provide a brief message or inquiry.
6. Choose your preferred response method (e.g., email, phone call, etc.).
7. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Full Name (2)**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Your Email Address (3)**: Enter your primary email address for contact purposes.
* **Your Phone Number (4)**: Enter your mobile or landline number for contact.
* **How did you hear about us? (6)**: Select your primary source of information about our company.
* **Your Industry/Sector (7)**: Select one category that best describes your industry or sector.
* **What aspect of our reputation built your trust? (8)**: Select all that apply from the given options.
* **Primary Communication Goal (10)**: Choose what you hope to achieve with this contact.
* **Your Message or Inquiry (11)**: Provide more information about your inquiry or question.
* **Preferred Response Method (12)**: Choose how you prefer us to respond to your inquiry.
* **Submission Date (13)**: This is automatically generated today's date for record-keeping purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed to ensure accurate processing of your inquiry.
* If you have any attachments or additional files to provide with your message, please include them separately and provide details about them in the form's field "Your Message or Inquiry".
