# Fancy Envelope Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Fancy Envelope Contact Form" is a contact form used to collect information from users who want to reach out to us. Fill out this form to share your message, ask a question, or request more information.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the "Fancy Envelope Contact Form" correctly, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Type in your company or organization's name in the "Company/Organization" field, if you are contacting a business.
4. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
5. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Type out your message in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Company/Organization** (text, required): Enter the name of your company or organization, if you are contacting a business.
* **Job Title** (text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Message** (text, required): Type out your message or question.

## Tips
* Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you are contacting a personal contact, you can leave the "Company/Organization" field blank.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us through other means, such as phone or mail.
