# Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Questionnaire is a form used to gather essential information from clients to understand their business needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your Company Name, Job Title, and Phone Number in the fields provided.
3. Select your Company Size from the dropdown list to indicate the number of employees in your company.
4. Choose your Industry from the dropdown list to specify the industry your company operates in.
5. Briefly describe your primary business goals and main business challenges in the text fields provided.
6. State your specific needs and requirements for the solutions you are seeking.
7. Indicate your budget range for the solutions you are interested in implementing.
8. Select a suitable Project Timeline that aligns with your implementation schedule.
9. Confirm if you are the decision-maker for the project or if you share decision-making authority.
10. Choose how you heard about our company, and if applicable, provide any additional information about yourself or your company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your official email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your company phone number for communication.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your company name if you have one (not required for our internal use).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title in your company.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select the number of employees in your company.
	+ Options: 1-10 employees, 11-50 employees, 51-200 employees, 201-500 employees, 500+ employees
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, optional): Select the industry your company operates in.
	+ Options: Technology, Finance, Healthcare, Retail, Manufacturing, Consulting, Other
* **Primary Goals** (`primary_goals`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your primary business goals and objectives.
* **Current Challenges** (`current_challenges`, `text`, optional): Describe your main business challenges and obstacles.
* **Specific Needs** (`specific_needs`, `text`, required): State your specific needs and requirements for the solutions you are seeking.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, optional): Select the budget range for the solutions you are interested in implementing.
	+ Options: Under $10,000, $10,000 - $50,000, $50,000 - $100,000, $100,000 - $250,000, $250,000+
* **Project Timeline** (`timeline`, `select_one`, optional): Select a suitable project timeline that aligns with your implementation schedule.
	+ Options: Immediate - 1-2 weeks, Short-term - 1-3 months, Medium-term - 3-6 months, Long-term - 6+ months
* **Decision Authority** (`decision_authority`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm if you are the decision-maker or if you share decision-making authority.
	+ Options: True, False, Share decision-making
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`how_heard`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how you heard about our company.
	+ Options: Search engine, Social media, Referral, Advertisement, Other
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information you would like to share about yourself or your company.

Note: Some fields are marked as required or optional based on their purpose and the form's overall goals. Ensure you complete all required fields to help us better serve your needs.
