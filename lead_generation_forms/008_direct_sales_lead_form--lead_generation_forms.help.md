# Direct Sales Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Direct Sales Lead Form is used to collect and manage information about sales leads. This form is designed to be completed by sales teams, account managers, or other relevant stakeholders to record key details about leads.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you're providing accurate and relevant information.
2. Fill in the lead's name, email address, company, and other required fields.
3. Select the sales channel and priority level of the lead.
4. Add any additional tags or notes as needed.
5. Save and submit the form to update the lead's status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Direct Sales Lead Form**: This is the title of the form, which is not a field that needs to be completed. It's just a label to identify the form.
* **Lead Name**: Enter the name of the lead.
* **Email**: Enter the lead's email address.
* **Company**: Enter the lead's company name.
* **Sales Channel**: Select the channel through which the lead was generated (e.g., Sales, Marketing, Support, Other).
* **Sales Channel Other**: If the sales channel is Other, enter a brief description of the channel.
* **Source**: Enter any additional information about the lead's source.
* **Description**: Add a brief description of the lead.
* **Tags**: Select multiple tags to categorize the lead (e.g., Tag 1, Tag 2, Tag 3).
* **Tags 2**: Select multiple tags to further categorize the lead (e.g., Tag 4, Tag 5).
* **Status**: Select the status of the lead (e.g., Open, Converted, Lost, Follow up).
* **Status Other**: If the status is Other, enter a brief description of the status.
* **Priority**: Select the priority level of the lead (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
* **Priority Other**: If the priority is Other, enter a brief description of the priority.
* **Assigned to**: Select the person responsible for managing the lead.
* **Assigned to Other**: If the assigned person is Other, enter a brief description of the assigned person.
* **Tags 3**: Select multiple tags to further categorize the lead (e.g., Tag 6, Tag 7).
* **Date**: Enter the date of the lead.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the lead.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes about the lead.
* **File**: Upload any relevant files or documents related to the lead.
* **URL**: Enter a URL related to the lead.
* **Phone**: Enter the lead's phone number.
* **Email 2**: Enter the lead's secondary email address.
* **Email 3**: Enter the lead's tertiary email address.
 
Note: The form has multiple fields with "Other" options, which is intended to provide flexibility for users to enter custom information when the default options do not apply.
