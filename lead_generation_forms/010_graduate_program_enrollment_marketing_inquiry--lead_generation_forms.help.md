# Graduate Program Enrollment Marketing Inquiry - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from potential students interested in enrolling in a graduate program. It helps us understand their preferences and interests, allowing us to provide them with tailored information and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields to help us understand your interests and preferences.
2. Select the relevant options for each field to ensure we can assist you better.
3. If a field is not applicable to you, leave it blank.
4. Once you have completed the form, submit it to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the graduate program you are interested in.
* **Start Term** (`start_term`, select_one, optional): Choose the term when you would like to start the graduate program. Options: January, May, September.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of graduate program you are interested in. Options: Online, On-campus, Part-time.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): If you have a preferred name to be contacted by, enter it here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a preferred phone number to be contacted, enter it.
* **Are you interested in learning more about a specific campus or online?** (`campus_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the campus or online option that interests you. Options: Online, On-campus, Both.
* **How did you find out about us?** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, optional): Let us know how you found out about our graduate program. Options: Social Media, Online Search, Print Ad, Referral, Other.
* **What location would you like to be based in?** (`program_location`, select_one, optional): Choose the location where you would like to be based in. Options: United States, Canada, International.
* **How would you like to be contacted for follow-ups?** (`follow_up_method`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred method for follow-ups. Options: Phone, Email, Text Message, In-person.
* **What level of graduate studies are you interested in?** (`graduate_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select the level of graduate studies you are interested in. Options: Certificate, Associate, Bachelor, Master, Doctoral.
* **What is your level of English proficiency?** (`program_english_level`, select_one, optional): Choose your level of English proficiency. Options: Native English Speaker, Non-native English Speaker, English Language Learner.
* **Are you interested in a specific graduate program?** (`program_interested`, text, optional): If you have a specific program in mind, enter it here.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing your contact information.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will get back to you within [X] working days to discuss your interests and preferences.
