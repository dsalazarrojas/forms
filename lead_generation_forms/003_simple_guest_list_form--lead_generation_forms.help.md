# Simple Guest List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Guest List Form is intended for event planners or hosts to gather information from guests about their attendance status and any additional details they might find useful for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email address (if applicable).
4. If you have a phone number, enter it.
5. Enter your physical Address (street, apartment number, etc.)
6. Enter the City where you'll be attending the event.
7. Enter the State or province where you'll be attending the event.
8. Enter your Zip code (postal code).
9. Select whether you'll be attending the event as an individual or with a group of people (select 'Yes' or 'No').
10. Write any additional Comments about your attendance or any special requirements you might have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Email** (email, optional): If you have an email address associated with your account, enter it. This will help the event organizers contact you about event updates or details.
* **Phone** (text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it. This can help the event organizers reach you if needed.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your physical address, including the apartment number if applicable.
* **City** (text, optional): Enter the city where you'll be attending the event.
* **State** (text, optional): Enter the state or province where you'll be attending the event.
* **Zip** (text, optional): Enter your postal code.
* **Attendees** (select multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you'll be attending the event as an individual. Select 'No' if you'll be attending with a group of people. If you're unsure, please select 'Yes' and provide more details in the 'Comments' field.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Write any additional comments about your attendance or special requirements you might have. This is a good place to provide more details about your situation if you're attending with a group or if you have specific requests.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all required fields (First Name, Last Name, and Attendees).
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the 'Comments' field.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy, if you provide them.
* If you're attending with a group, please let the event organizers know in the 'Attendees' field.
* If you have any other questions or concerns, feel free to contact the event organizers for assistance.
