# win_a_free_training_session_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "win_a_free_training_session_entry_form" is a form designed for users to enter a giveaway and potentially receive a free training session. It is meant to collect users' personal details such as Name and Email to facilitate communication and identification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the form link to access the entry page.
2. Enter your name and email address in the required fields (Name and Email).
3. Optionally, enter a phone number for easier contact.
4. Choose your preferred time slot for a call-back or follow-up.
5. Click the submit button to complete your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for easier contact or follow-up.
* **Best Time to Call** (`best_time_to_call`, `time`, optional): Choose your preferred time slot for a call-back or follow-up in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00, 12:00, etc.).
* **Enter to Win** (`giveaway_entry`, `text`, optional): Enter your entry for the giveaway.
