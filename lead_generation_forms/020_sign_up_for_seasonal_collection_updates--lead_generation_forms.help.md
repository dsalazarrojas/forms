# Sign Up For Seasonal Collection Updates - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to sign up for seasonal collection updates from our brand. We collect information to tailor our updates according to your preferences and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your email address and name in the respective text fields.
* Select one or more seasonal interests you are interested in (e.g. Summer, Winter, Spring, Autumn).
* Select a seasonal purpose for your interest (e.g. Product Launch, Event, Collection Update, Special Offer).
* If required, enter your date of birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, false): Enter the email address you want to use for updates.
* **Name** (`name`, text, false): Enter your name for us to address you by.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, false): This field is not required but can be entered if you want to add a phone number for contact purposes.
* **Select Seasonal Interest** (`select_seasonal_interest`, select_multiple, false): Select one or more seasonal interests to receive updates about.
* **Select Seasonal Purpose** (`select_seasonal_purpose`, select_one, false): Choose a reason for selecting a seasonal interest.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, false): If required, enter your date of birth to help us tailor our updates according to your interests and preferences.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* If you select multiple seasonal interests, separate them with commas.
* If you have any issues while filling the form, contact us for assistance.
