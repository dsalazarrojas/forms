# Real Estate Agent Outreach Script - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Agent Outreach Script is a form designed to assist real estate agents in capturing information about potential leads. This form helps agents gather details about the leads, including their contact information, property preferences, and follow-up requirements.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Enter your name and email address in the respective fields.
3. Select the type of property you are looking to capture (Residential, Commercial, or Other).
4. Choose the location of the property (In the city, In the suburbs, or Other).
5. Enter the budget for the property.
6. Schedule a call with the lead (if applicable).
7. Schedule a meeting with the lead (if applicable).
8. Enter the meeting location (if applicable).
9. Select the source of the lead (Referral, Ad, Online Search, Social Media, or Other).
10. Add any follow-up instructions for the lead (if applicable).
11. Enter the follow-up method (e.g., phone call, email, etc.).
12. Select the type of lead (New, Returning, or Referral).
13. Determine the lead status (Qualified or Unqualified).
14. Add any additional notes about the lead.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **lead_capture** (Text, 1, Optional): Enter your name.
* **Name** (Text, 2, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **property_type** (Select One, 4, Optional): Choose the type of property you are looking to capture (Residential, Commercial, or Other).
* **property_location** (Select Multiple, 5, Optional): Select the location of the property (In the city, In the suburbs, or Other).
* **budget** (Number, 6, Optional): Enter the budget for the property.
* **call_schedule** (Time, 7, Optional): Schedule a call with the lead (in 24-hour format, e.g., 10:00 AM).
* **meeting_schedule** (Date, 8, Optional): Schedule a meeting with the lead (in date format, e.g., 2023-02-15).
* **meeting_location** (Text, 9, Optional): Enter the meeting location.
* **lead_source** (Select One, 10, Optional): Select the source of the lead (Referral, Ad, Online Search, Social Media, or Other).
* **follow_up** (Text, 11, Optional): Add any follow-up instructions for the lead.
* **follow_up_time** (Time, 12, Optional): Enter the follow-up method (e.g., phone call, email, etc.).
* **follow_up_method** (Text, 13, Optional): Enter the follow-up method (e.g., phone call, email, etc.).
* **lead_type** (Select One, 14, Optional): Select the type of lead (New, Returning, or Referral).
* **lead_status** (Select One, 15, Optional): Determine the lead status (Qualified or Unqualified).
* **notes** (Text, 16, Optional): Add any additional notes about the lead.
