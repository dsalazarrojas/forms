# sk_group_property_inquiry_form_new - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "sk_group_property_inquiry_form_new" form is designed to collect information about a property from a lead. It is intended for use in a business-to-business or business-to-consumer lead generation scenario.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the property address in the "Property Address" field.
2. Enter the name of the property owner or contact person in the "Name" field.
3. Enter the email address of the property owner or contact person in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the phone number of the property owner or contact person in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select the type of property from the "Property Type" dropdown menu (e.g. Apartment, House, Commercial).
6. Select any features of the property that apply from the "Property Features" dropdown menu (e.g. Air Conditioning, Elevator, Furnished).
7. Enter the price range of the property in the "Property Price Range" field.
8. Enter the price of the property in the "Property Price" field.
9. Enter the size of the property in the square footage (e.g. 1000, 2000, etc.) in the "Property Size" field.
10. Enter the number of bedrooms in the "Bedrooms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Address (text)**: Enter the address of the property.
* **Name (text)**: Enter the name of the property owner or contact person.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the email address of the property owner or contact person.
* **Phone Number (text)**: Enter the phone number of the property owner or contact person.
* **Property Type (select_one)**: Select the type of property from the dropdown menu (e.g. Apartment, House, Commercial).
* **Property Features (select_multiple)**: Select any applicable features of the property from the dropdown menu (e.g. Air Conditioning, Elevator, Furnished).
* **Property Price Range (number)**: Enter the price range of the property.
* **Property Price (number)**: Enter the price of the property.
* **Property Size (number)**: Enter the size of the property in square footage.
* **Bedrooms (number)**: Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have permission to provide information about the property before completing this form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the property owner or contact person for clarification.
