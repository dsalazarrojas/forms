# Training Program Email Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from users interested in our training programs, allowing us to provide them with updates and information about upcoming sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply:

1. Enter your email address in the "Enter Email" field.
2. Optionally, enter your name and organization, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your valid email address where you'd like to receive updates and information about our training sessions. Make sure to enter a email address that is not already subscribed to our training program updates.
* **Name** (`name`, optional): If you'd like to be contacted directly, please enter your name. This is optional, and your name will only be used to personalize our communication with you.
* **Organization** (`organization`, optional): If you are representing an organization and would like to be contacted regarding this form submission, please enter your organization's name. This is optional and not required.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address to ensure you receive our updates and information about our training sessions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* You can always unsubscribe from our updates at any time.
* Note that the information submitted in this form will not be shared with any third-party vendors or entities.
