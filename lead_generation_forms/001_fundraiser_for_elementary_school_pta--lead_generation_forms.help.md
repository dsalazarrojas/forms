# Fundraiser For Elementary School PTA - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to create a fundraiser for an Elementary School PTA event. It collects information about the fundraiser, including the target and actual amounts raised, the description, and other event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your fundraiser in the "Title" field.
2. In the "Fundraiser Form" field, enter a brief description of your fundraiser.
3. In the "Name" field, enter the name of the person or organization associated with the fundraiser.
4. In the "Organization" field, enter the name of the organization associated with the fundraiser.
5. Choose how you would like to share the fundraiser details using the "Share Via" field.
6. Enter the date of the fundraiser in the "Date" field.
7. Enter the time of the fundraiser in the "Time" field.
8. Add any notes about the fundraiser in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter the email contact for the fundraiser in the "Email" field.
10. Enter the phone number for the fundraiser in the "Phone" field.
11. Enter the address of the fundraiser in the "Address" field.
12. Enter the city of the fundraiser in the "City" field.
13. Enter the state of the fundraiser in the "State" field.
14. Enter the zip code of the fundraiser in the "Zip" field.
15. Enter the type of fundraiser in the "Fundraiser Type" field.
16. Enter the target amount of the fundraiser in the "Target Amount" field.
17. Enter the actual amount raised for the fundraiser in the "Actual Amount" field.
18. Enter a brief description of the fundraiser in the "Description" field.
19. Add any relevant tags to the fundraiser in the "Tags" field.
20. Select multiple tags for the fundraiser in the "Tags List" field.
21. Enter the number of times the fundraiser will repeat in the "Repeat" field.
22. Enter the unit of the repeat in the "Repeat Unit" field.
23. Enter the interval of the repeat in the "Repeat Interval" field.
24. Enter the duration of the repeat in the "Repeat Duration" field.
25. Enter the frequency of the repeat in the "Repeat Frequency" field.
26. Enter the tool assigned to the fundraiser in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`title`, `text`, required): The title of your fundraiser.
* **Fundraiser Form** (`fundraiser_form`, `text`, required): A brief description of your fundraiser.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): The name of the person or organization associated with the fundraiser.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required): The name of the organization associated with the fundraiser.
* **Share Via** (`share_via`, `select_one`, required): How you would like to share the fundraiser details (text, email, social media).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): The date of the fundraiser.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): The time of the fundraiser.
* **Notes** (`note`, `note`, optional): Any notes about the fundraiser.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): The email contact for the fundraiser.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): The phone number for the fundraiser.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): The address of the fundraiser.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): The city of the fundraiser.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, optional): The state of the fundraiser.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, optional): The zip code of the fundraiser.
* **Fundraiser Type** (`fundraiser_type`, `text`, optional): The type of fundraiser.
* **Target Amount** (`target_amount`, `number`, optional): The target amount of the fundraiser.
* **Actual Amount** (`actual_amount`, `number`, optional): The actual amount raised for the fundraiser.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): A brief description of the fundraiser.
* **Tags** (`tags`, `text`, optional): Any relevant tags for the fundraiser.
* **Tags List** (`tags_list`, `select_multiple`, optional): Multiple tags for the fundraiser.
* **Repeat** (`repeat`, `number`, optional): The number of times the fundraiser will repeat.
* **Repeat Unit** (`repeat_unit`, `text`, optional): The unit of the repeat (e.g., days, weeks).
* **Repeat Interval** (`repeat_interval`, `number`, optional): The interval of the repeat.
* **Repeat Duration** (`repeat_duration`, `number`, optional): The duration of the repeat.
* **Repeat Frequency** (`repeat_frequency`, `number`, optional): The frequency of the repeat.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): The tool assigned to the fundraiser.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
