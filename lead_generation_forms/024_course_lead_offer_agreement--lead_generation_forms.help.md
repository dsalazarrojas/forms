# course_leads_offer_agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the lead generation process for an offer, likely for a course or educational program. It gathers data on the lead details, terms of the offer, and agreements made by both the lead and partner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For fields with a "select_multiple" or "select" type, choose the applicable options from the provided list.
3. Enter timestamped information in the "signed_date", "signed_time", "signed_by", and "signed_at" fields as required.
