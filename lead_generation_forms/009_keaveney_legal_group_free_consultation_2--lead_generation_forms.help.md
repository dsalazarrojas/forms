# keaveney legal group free consultation 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from individuals who need legal advice or assistance from the Keaveney Legal Group. It will help us understand your needs and preferences for a free consultation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") completely and accurately.
2. For the "Phone number" field, please enter your phone number without any special characters or spaces.
3. For the "Email" field, please enter a valid email address.
4. In the "Description of the legal issue" field, please describe your legal concern in as much detail as possible.
5. Choose your preferred consultation method from the options provided.
6. If you have any additional comments, please include them in the "Additional comments" field.
7. Finally, click on "Submit" to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (Required): Enter your last name.
* **Phone number** (Required): Enter your phone number without any special characters or spaces.
* **Email** (Required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Description of the legal issue** (Required): Describe your legal concern in as much detail as possible.
* **Date of the consultation** (Required): Select a date for the consultation.
* **Time of the consultation** (Required): Select a time for the consultation.
* **Preferred consultation method** (Required): Choose how you would like to be consulted with.
* **Additional comments** (Optional): Include any additional comments you would like to include.

Please note that all fields marked as "Required" must be filled out completely and accurately to ensure your request is processed correctly.
