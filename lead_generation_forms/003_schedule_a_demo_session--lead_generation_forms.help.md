# Schedule A Demo Session - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a demo session, allowing sales team members to schedule and manage demo sessions efficiently. It helps gather relevant details about the demo, including the type, date, time, and other essential information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of demo you want to schedule (e.g., Discovery, Product Tour, or Sales Enablement).
2. Fill in the required fields (name, email, phone number, demo date, demo time, demo duration, and demo note).
3. Add any additional details (company, location, lead status, follow-up date, follow-up time, and follow-up note) as needed.
4. Submit the form to schedule the demo session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What type of demo** (demo_type): Choose the type of demo you want to schedule, such as Discovery, Product Tour, or Sales Enablement.
* **Name** (lead_name): Enter the name of the person participating in the demo.
* **Email** (email): Enter the email address of the person participating in the demo.
* **Phone** (phone): Enter the phone number of the person participating in the demo.
* **Demo Date** (demo_date): Select the date for the demo session in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Demo Time** (demo_time): Choose the time for the demo session in 12-hour format (e.g., 09:00 AM).
* **Demo Duration** (demo_duration): Enter the length of the demo session in minutes.
* **Demo Note** (demo_note): Add any additional notes or details about the demo session.
* **Lead Status** (lead_status): Select the current status of the lead, such as Not Qualified, Qualified, or Lead Added.
* **Follow-up Date** (follow_up_date): Select the date for the follow-up session in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Follow-up Time** (follow_up_time): Choose the time for the follow-up session in 12-hour format (e.g., 09:00 AM).
* **Follow-up Note** (follow_up_note): Add any additional notes or details about the follow-up session.
* **Company** (company): Enter the name of the company associated with the lead.
* **Location** (location): Enter the location of the demo session.
* **Lead Name** (lead_name): Enter the name of the lead or customer participating in the demo.
* **Lead Status** (lead_status): Select the current status of the lead, such as Not Qualified, Qualified, or Lead Added.
