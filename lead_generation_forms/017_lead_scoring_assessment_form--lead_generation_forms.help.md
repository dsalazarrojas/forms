# Lead Scoring Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information and evaluate the quality and priority of leads for sales teams to identify and prioritize the most valuable leads.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name and email address in the "Customer Name" and "Email" fields respectively.
2. If applicable, select the "Company" field to specify the customer's company.
3. If applicable, enter the customer's phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. In the "Lead Type" field, select all applicable options (e.g., "Yes" for a hot lead).
5. Enter the score in the "Score" field.
6. In the "Priority" field, select "Yes" if the lead is high priority.
7. If applicable, enter the date the lead was created in the "Date Created" field.
8. In the "Assigned To" field, enter the sales representative's name or ID.
9. In the "Description" field, enter a brief description of the lead.
10. In the "Lead Source" field, select the source of the lead (e.g., "Chat", "Email", etc.).
11. In the "Lead Status" field, select all applicable status options (e.g., "Active", "Inactive").



## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's company name if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Lead Type** (`lead_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable lead types (e.g., "Yes" for a hot lead).
* **Score** (`score`, `number`, required): Enter the lead score.
* **Priority** (`priority`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if the lead is high priority.
* **Date Created** (`date_created`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the lead was created if applicable.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, optional): Enter the sales representative's name or ID.
* **Description** (`description`, `note`, required): Enter a brief description of the lead.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, `select_one`, optional): Select the source of the lead (e.g., "Chat", "Email", etc.).
* **Lead Status** (`lead_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable status options (e.g., "Active", "Inactive").

## Tips

* Please ensure that the customer's name and email address are accurate and up-to-date.
* Make sure to select all applicable lead types and status options to ensure accurate lead scoring.
* The "Description" field should provide a brief but clear description of the lead.
* The "Date Created" field should be filled in if the lead was created manually.
