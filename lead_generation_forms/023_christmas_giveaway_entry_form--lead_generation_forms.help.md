# Christmas Giveaway Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and contact information from individuals interested in participating in a Christmas giveaway. The information collected will be used to determine eligibility, contactability, and communication preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* First Name: Enter your first name as it is spelled.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name as it is spelled.
	* Email Address: Enter your email address.
	* Phone Number: Enter your phone number.
	* Mailing Address: Enter your complete mailing address.
	* City: Enter the city where you live.
	* State or Province: Enter the state or province where you live.
	* ZIP or Postal Code: Enter your zip or postal code.
	* Country: Enter your country of residence.
2. Confirm your age: Check the box to confirm that you are 18 years or older.
3. Specify how you heard about the giveaway:
	* Choose from: Social media, Email newsletter, Website, Friend referral, Advertisement, or Other.
4. (Optional) If you were referred, enter the name of the person who referred you.
5. Choose how you would like to engage with us:
	* Select all that apply.
6. Confirm your communication preferences:
	* Choose to receive marketing emails or SMS updates.
7. (Optional) If available, choose your preferred prize.
8. Agree to the giveaway terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is spelled.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is spelled.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your complete mailing address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you live.
* **State or Province** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province where you live.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter your country of residence.
* **I confirm I am 18 years or older** (`age_confirmation`, select_one, required): Check the box to confirm that you are 18 years or older.
* **Citizenship Status** (`citizenship`, select_one, optional): Choose your citizenship status (Citizen, Permanent resident, Resident alien, Other).
* **How did you hear about this giveaway?** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Choose from Social media, Email newsletter, Website, Friend referral, Advertisement, Other.
* **Referred by (if applicable)** (`referred_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who referred you.
* **How would you like to engage with us?** (`engagement_options`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply.
* **Communication Preferences** (`preferences`, note, optional): This field is for note-taking purposes only.
* **I consent to receive marketing emails** (`email_consent`, select_one, required): Choose to receive marketing emails.
* **I consent to receive SMS updates** (`sms_consent`, select_one, optional): Choose to receive SMS updates.
* **Prize Preference (if choices available)** (`prize_preference`, text, optional): Choose your preferred prize.
* **I agree to the giveaway terms and conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): Check the box to agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information.
* If you are under 18 years old, you will not be eligible to participate.
* If you have already participated in this giveaway, do not submit duplicate entries.
* Our team will review and respond to your submission.
