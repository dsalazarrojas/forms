# Product Bundle Request Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in requesting a product bundle.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, Use Case, and Product Bundle.
2. If you are associated with a company, please provide your Company name.
3. If you have a specific phone number, please fill it in for our contact purposes.
4. If you have any additional information or description about your product bundle request, please use the Description field.
5. Click the submit button to send us your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please provide your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please provide your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): If you are associated with a company, please provide its name.
* **Use Case** (`use_case`, `text`, required): Describe the use case for your product bundle request.
* **Product Bundle** (`product_bundle`, `text`, required): Describe the product bundle you are interested in.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific phone number, please provide it for our contact purposes.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional information or description about your product bundle request, please use this field.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address for correctness before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [contact email/phone number].
