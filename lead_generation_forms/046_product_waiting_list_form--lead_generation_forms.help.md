# Product Waiting List Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Product Waiting List Form is used to record customer information and product details when a customer expresses interest in a product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field. This field is required for communication and updates.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field (optional). This helps sales representatives contact the customer more easily.
4. Enter the product ID in the "Product ID" field. This field is required for accurate product tracking.
5. Add any additional customer notes or comments in the "Customer Note" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, number, required): Enter the product ID number.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, note, optional): Add any additional customer notes or comments.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the customer's email address for accuracy.
* Ensure that the product ID is correct and up-to-date.
* If the customer requests more information about the product, add it in the customer note field.
* Save the form before submitting it to update the waiting list.
