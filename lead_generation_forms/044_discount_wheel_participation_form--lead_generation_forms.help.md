# Discount Wheel Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discount Wheel Participation Form is used to track participant involvement in promotions and discounts. It helps ensure accurate and efficient participation tracking.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Particpant Details**
To complete this form, fill out the following:
	* **Participant Details**: Enter the participant's information, such as their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
	* **Contact Details**: Provide the participant's contact information, like phone number and email.
	* **Conesent**: Check the box to indicate that you have received the participant's consent.
	* **Promotion**: Enter the promotion or discount details related to the participant.
	* **Participant ID** and **Entry ID**: Enter the participant's unique ID and the entry ID associated with their participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Enter the participant's name, address, and any other relevant details. This helps us understand who is participating.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide the participant's contact information, like phone number and email. This ensures we can reach them for any follow-up questions or updates.
- **Conesent** (`consent`, text, optional): Check the box to indicate that you have received the participant's consent. This ensures we can use their information for marketing or promotional purposes.
- **Promotion** (`promotion`, text, optional): Enter the promotion or discount details related to the participant. This helps us understand what offer they are participating in.
- **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, number, optional): Enter the participant's unique ID. This helps us track their participation efficiently.
- **Entry ID** (`entry_id`, number, optional): Enter the entry ID associated with the participant's participation. This ensures accurate tracking and reporting.

## Tips
- Please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly to ensure accurate participation tracking.
- Use a new instance of the form for each new participant.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
