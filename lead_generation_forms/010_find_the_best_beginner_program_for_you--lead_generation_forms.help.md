# Find The Best Beginner Program For You - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user preferences and goals to recommend suitable beginner programs. The form will ask about your goals, experience, available time, and preferred format to tailor the recommendations to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, select all your goals from the "What Are Your Goals" dropdown menu.
2.  Next, answer "Yes" or "No" to the "What Is Your Experience" question.
3.  For the "How Much Time Do You Have Per Week" field, enter a numerical value in hours.
4.  In the "Preferred Format" dropdown menu, choose all the formats you are interested in.
5.  Click "Submit" to receive your recommended program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What Are Your Goals**: This is a select_multiple field where you can choose one or more of the available options:
    -   Yes
    -   No
    The options represent different types of goals, such as personal development, career growth, or skill acquisition, which will help the system understand what you're aiming to achieve through a beginner program.
*   **What Is Your Experience**: This is a select_one field with two options:
    -   Yes
    -   No
    It will help to identify your current level of experience in a specific field, such as programming, data science, or design.
*   **How Much Time Do You Have Per Week**: This is a number field where you will input the number of hours you can dedicate to learning and practicing each week.
*   **Preferred Format**: This is a select_multiple field with options for different learning formats, such as:
    -   Yes
    -   No
    Choose all the formats you find suitable for your learning style and preferences.

## Tips
-   Please answer honestly about your goals and preferences to get the most accurate recommendations.
-   Select all the formats that suit your learning style, even if it's just one.
-   Enter a realistic number for the "How Much Time Do You Have Per Week" field to get a more precise fit.
-   If you've already completed the form, you will be directed to your previous results to avoid duplication of effort.
