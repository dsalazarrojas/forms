# Financial Inclusion Strategy Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential leads about their financial inclusion strategy, lead generation channels, and status. The collected data will help in assessing the strategy's objectives and impact.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number, Company)
2. Select your role within the organization (CEO, Manager, Staff)
3. Choose the department you belong to (Sales, Marketing, IT)
4. Provide any additional comments
5. Choose whether you have a financial inclusion strategy (Yes/No)
6. Select the lead generation channels you use (Website, Social Media, Email)
7. Choose the objectives of your financial inclusion strategy (open-ended)
8. Describe the impact of your financial inclusion strategy (open-ended)
9. State the status of your lead generation (Yes/No)
10. Choose the lead generation channel source (Website, Social Media, Email, Google, Facebook, LinkedIn)
11. Select other lead generation channel sources (if not listed above)
12. Provide a description of other lead generation channel sources (if not listed above)
13. Choose other financial inclusion objectives (if not listed above)
14. Describe the impact of other financial inclusion objectives (if not listed above)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Company**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Role**: Select your role within the organization (CEO, Manager, Staff).
* **Department**: Select the department you belong to (Sales, Marketing, IT).
* **Location**: Enter your company's location or office address.
* **Comment**: Enter any additional comments or information about your financial inclusion strategy.
* **Financial Inclusion Strategy**: Choose whether you have a financial inclusion strategy (Yes/No).
* **Lead Generation Channel**: Select the lead generation channels you use (Website, Social Media, Email).
* **Financial Inclusion Objectives**: Describe the objectives of your financial inclusion strategy.
* **Financial Inclusion Strategy Impact**: Describe the impact of your financial inclusion strategy.
* **Lead Generation Status**: State the status of your lead generation (Yes/No).
* **Lead Generation Channel Source**: Choose the lead generation channel source (Website, Social Media, Email, Google, Facebook, LinkedIn).
* **Other Lead Generation Channel Sources**: Select other lead generation channel sources (if not listed above).
* **Other Lead Generation Channel Sources Description**: Provide a description of other lead generation channel sources (if not listed above).
* **Other Financial Inclusion Objectives**: Choose other financial inclusion objectives (if not listed above).
* **Other Financial Inclusion Objectives Description**: Describe the impact of other financial inclusion objectives (if not listed above).
* **Lead Generation Status**: State the status of your lead generation (if other, specify).
* **Lead Generation Channel Source**: Choose the lead generation channel source (if other, specify).
* **Lead Generation Channel Source Other Description**: Provide a description of the lead generation channel source (if other, specify).
* **Financial Inclusion Objectives**: Describe the impact of your financial inclusion strategy (if other, specify).
* **Financial Inclusion Objectives Other Description**: Describe the impact of your financial inclusion objectives (if other, specify).
