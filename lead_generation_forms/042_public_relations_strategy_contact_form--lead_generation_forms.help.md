# public_relations_strategy_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who need to contact the public relations department for a specific project or to provide feedback. It's meant for inquirying about a particular topic or issue, not for general or sales-related inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Provide as much detail as you can about your inquiry or issue.
3. Make sure to include your contact information so we can get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info**: Please provide your name and any other identifying information.
	+ (text field)
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
	+ (email field)
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
	+ (text field)
* **Project Info**: Provide as much detail as you can about the project or issue you are inquiring about.
	+ (text field)
* **Contact Name**: If you are inquiring on behalf of someone else, please provide their name.
	+ (text field)
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person you are inquiring on behalf of.
	+ (email field)
* **Contact Phone**: If the person you are inquiring on behalf of would like to be contacted by phone, provide their phone number.
	+ (text field)
* **Message**: Provide a brief description of your inquiry or issue.
	+ (text field)
* **Company**: If you are inquiring on behalf of a company, please provide the name of the company.
	+ (text field)
* **Department**: If you are inquiring on behalf of a specific department within the company, provide its name.
	+ (text field)
* **Title**: Provide the title of the person inquiring on behalf of the company.
	+ (text field)
* **Address 1-8**: If you need to provide multiple address lines, enter them in the corresponding fields.
	+ (text field)
* **City**: Enter the city of the company's location.
	+ (text field)
* **State**: Enter the state of the company's location.
	+ (text field)
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the company's location.
	+ (text field)
* **Submit**: Once you have filled out all the required fields, click submit to send your inquiry.

Note: All fields are optional, but it's best to fill out as much information as possible to ensure we can efficiently address your inquiry.
