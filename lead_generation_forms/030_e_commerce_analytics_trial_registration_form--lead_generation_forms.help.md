# e_commerce_analytics_trial_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e Commerce Analytics Trial Registration Form is a user-facing form used to collect information from potential customers for an e-commerce analytics trial. This form is used to gather essential details from users to provide them with a personalized analytics trial experience. It is a crucial step in the registration process, and the information collected will be used to set up and configure the trial according to the user's preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the link to access the registration form.
2.  Select the "User Info" page and fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
3.  Proceed to the "Contact Info" page and fill in the required and optional fields.
4.  In the "E Commerce Analytics Trial Registration Form Info" page, select the relevant option for your business needs.
5.  Enter your Email address on the "Email" page.
6.  Enter your Phone number (if applicable) on the "Phone" page.
7.  Choose the Date and Time of the trial on the "Date" and "Time" pages respectively.
8.  Add any additional note on the "Note" page if needed.
9.  Review and submit the form once all fields have been filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, job title, or any identifying information that you would like to share with us.
* **Contact Info** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company address, department, or any other relevant contact information.
* **E Commerce Analytics Trial Registration Form Info** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of E Commerce Analytics Trial you are interested in (e.g., option 1, option 2, or option 3).
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address associated with your company or department.
* **Phone** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company phone number (if applicable).
* **Date** (`6`, `date`, required: false): Choose the preferred date for the trial.
* **Time** (`7`, `time`, required: false): Choose the preferred time for the trial.
* **Note** (`8`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your trial requirements.
