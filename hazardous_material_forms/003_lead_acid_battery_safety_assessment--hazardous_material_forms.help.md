# Lead Acid Battery Safety Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead Acid Battery Safety Assessment form is used to gather information on the safety aspects related to lead-acid batteries, ensuring compliance and identifying potential risks in a work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
2. Answer the questions according to your knowledge or available data.
3. Select relevant options for select-type fields as they apply to your situation.
4. Submit the form once you've completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Field 1: Safety Checklist
* **Safety Checklist** (`lead_acid_battery_safety_assessment_form`, text, optional)
	+ This field is a text box where you can provide any information regarding safety checklists relevant to lead-acid batteries.

### Field 2: Training Readiness
* **Training Readiness** (`lead_acid_battery_safety_training_form`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select all relevant options if the personnel have received training on lead-acid battery safety.
	+ Options:
		- Option 1
		- Option 2
		- Option 3

### Field 3: Storage Location
* **Storage Location** (`battery_storage_location`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select the location where lead-acid batteries are stored, such as a designated room or cabinet.

### Field 4: Battery Age
* **Battery Age** (`battery_age`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the age of the batteries in years for better maintenance and replacement planning.

### Field 5: Inspection Form
* **Inspection Form** (`lead_acid_battery_safety_inspection_form`, text, optional)
	+ Provide any inspection-related information, such as schedules or checklists used for battery safety inspections.

### Field 6: Training Records
* **Training Records** (`lead_acid_battery_safety_training_records`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select all relevant training records related to lead-acid battery safety, such as certifications or documentation.

### Field 7: Handling Guidelines
* **Handling Guidelines** (`lead_acid_battery_safety_handling_guidelines`, text, optional)
	+ Enter any guidelines or procedures for handling lead-acid batteries in your work environment.

### Field 8: Safety Equipment
* **Safety Equipment** (`lead_acid_battery_safety_equipment`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select the type of safety equipment used for handling or storing lead-acid batteries, such as gloves or protective clothing.

### Field 9: Incident Report
* **Incident Report** (`lead_acid_battery_safety_incident`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select all relevant incidents related to lead-acid battery safety, such as near misses or accidents.

### Field 10: Inspection Frequency
* **Inspection Frequency** (`lead_acid_battery_safety_inspection_frequency`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the frequency of inspections for lead-acid batteries, in number of inspections per year or other time period.
