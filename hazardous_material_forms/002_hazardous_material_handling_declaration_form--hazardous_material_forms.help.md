# Hazardous Material Handling Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare and report on the handling of hazardous materials by businesses. It's essential to fill it out accurately to ensure compliance with relevant regulations and ensure the safe handling of hazardous materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about your business.
2. Select the correct options for each field, such as the type of material being handled.
3. Be sure to sign and date the form to declare that the information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Fill in the name of your business as it appears on your license or registration.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person completing this form.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person completing this form.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person completing this form.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): If you're declaring materials on behalf of a customer, enter their name.
* **Material Type** (`material_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of material being handled. Options include Material Name, Chemical Name, and Hazard Statement.
* **Material Description** (`material_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the material being handled.
* **Physical State** (`physical_state`, text, optional): Describe the physical state of the material, such as solid, liquid, or gas.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of material being handled.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the material is being handled.
* **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the material was shipped.
* **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`, date, required): Enter the date this form is being submitted.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Sign and date this form to declare that the information is accurate.
* **Business License** (`business_license`, text, optional): Enter your business license number or registration.
* **Customer License** (`customer_license`, text, optional): If you're declaring materials on behalf of a customer, enter their license number or registration.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the person completing this form.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional shipping details, such as shipping method or carrier.
* **Handling Instructions** (`handling_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special handling instructions for the material.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special instructions for handling the material.
* **Certification** (`certification`, select_one, required): Choose whether your business is certified to handle hazardous materials.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the material.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or business submitting this form.

## Tips

* Be sure to review and update this form regularly to reflect changes in your business's hazardous material handling practices.
* If you're unsure about any field or question, contact your local authorities for guidance.
* Sign and date this form to declare that the information is accurate.
