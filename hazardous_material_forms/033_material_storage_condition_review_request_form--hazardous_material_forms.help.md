# Material Storage Condition Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to review the condition of materials stored in our facility. It is a mandatory step in the storage process to ensure that materials are handled and stored safely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the "Storage Location" field with the exact location where the material is stored.
2. Enter the "Condition Request Date" if you have a specific date for when you want the review to take place.
3. Choose the "Urgency Level" that best describes the condition of the material (Low, Medium, or High).
4. Select one or multiple "Priority" levels that apply to the material (High, Medium, or Low).
5. Choose any relevant "Supporting Documents" (if applicable).
6. Write any additional comments or notes in the "Review Request Comments" field (if applicable).
7. Assign the review to the correct person by selecting their name from the "Assigned To" field.
8. Finally, select a "Review Date" if this is a recurring review or if you want to schedule a review for a specific date in the future.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Material Storage Condition Request** (`material_storage_condition_request`): This field is a text field where you can describe the condition of the material you wish to review.
* **Storage Location** (`storage_location`): This is where the material is stored. Please enter the exact location, such as a bin number or a rack number.
* **Condition Request Date** (`condition_request_date`): If you have a specific date for when you want the review to take place, enter it here.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`): Select the urgency level that best describes the condition of the material. This will help us understand the importance of the review.
	+ Low: The material is in good condition and does not require immediate attention.
	+ Medium: The material is not in good condition and requires attention soon.
	+ High: The material is in poor condition and requires immediate attention.
* **Priority** (`priority`): Select one or multiple priority levels that apply to the material. This will help us determine how quickly the review should be completed.
	+ High: Review is urgent and should be completed as soon as possible.
	+ Medium: Review is important but not urgent.
	+ Low: Review is not time-sensitive.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`): If you have any relevant documents that support your request, select them here.
* **Review Request Comments** (`review_request_comments`): If you have any additional comments or notes about the review, enter them here.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Assign this review to the correct person by selecting their name from the dropdown list.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`): If this is a recurring review or if you want to schedule a review for a specific date in the future, select the date here.
