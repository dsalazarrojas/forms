# Hazardous Material Handling Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hazardous Material Handling Checklist Form is used to ensure that employees handling hazardous materials are following proper procedures and protocols to minimize risk.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name as it appears on your ID badge.
2. Enter your job title as it is assigned to you.
3. Enter the name of your supervisor who is overseeing your work.
4. Enter the date you handled the hazardous materials.
5. Enter the quantity of hazardous materials you handled.
6. Enter the location where you handled the hazardous materials.
7. Check all the personal protection equipment you used during handling (e.g. Respiratory protection, Gloves, etc.).
8. Answer yes or no if you followed the spill procedure, spill containment procedure, and spill clean up procedure.
9. Check all the personal protection equipment in good working condition you used during handling (e.g. Respiratory protection, Gloves, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (1)** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID badge.
* **Employee Job Title (2)** (`employee_job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as assigned by your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Name (3)** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your supervisor who oversees your work.
* **Date of Hazmat Handling (4)** (`date_of_hazmat_handling`, date, required): Enter the date you handled the hazardous materials.
* **Quantity of Hazmat Handled (5)** (`quantity_hazmat_handled`, number, required): Enter the quantity of hazardous materials you handled.
* **Location of Handling (6)** (`location_of_handling`, text, required): Enter the location where you handled the hazardous materials.
* **Personal Protection Equipment Used (7)** (`personal_protection_equipment_used`, select_multiple, required): This field asks you to select all the personal protection equipment you used during handling, such as Respiratory protection, Gloves, etc.
* **Spill Procedure Followed (8)** (`spill_procedure_followed`, select_one, required): Answer yes or no if you followed the spill procedure.
* **Spill Containment Procedure Followed (9)** (`spill_containment_procedure_followed`, select_one, required): Answer yes or no if you followed the spill containment procedure.
* **Spill Clean Up Procedure Followed (10)** (`spill_clean_up_procedure_followed`, select_one, required): Answer yes or no if you followed the spill clean up procedure.
* **Personal Protection Equipment in Good Working Condition (11)** (`personal_protection_equipment_in_good_working_condition`, select_multiple, required): This field asks you to select all the personal protection equipment in good working condition you used during handling, such as Respiratory protection, Gloves, etc.

Note: Please make sure to answer all questions accurately and truthfully to ensure a safe working environment.
