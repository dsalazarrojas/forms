# Virtual Career Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Career Fair Registration Form is used to register a company for a virtual career fair. This form is used to collect information about the company, the event details, and the type of job positions available. Please take the time to complete this form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your company name and address
* Select the event details (date and time)
* Indicate whether you will be bringing representatives or not
* Provide a description of your company and the job positions available
* Select the type of industry your company belongs to
* Select the type of job positions available
* Select the type of job level and function
* Provide any additional job requirements
* Select the size of your company
* Select the type of industry your company belongs to
* If you have any additional booth setup requirements, please list them
* Review and accept the terms and conditions

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Event Details**: Provide the date and time of the virtual career fair.
* **Company Representatives**: Indicate whether you will be bringing company representatives or not.
* **Company Address**: Enter the address of your company.
* **Company URL**: Enter the URL of your company website.
* **Company Description**: Provide a brief description of your company.
* **Company Type**: Indicate the type of company you have (e.g. software development, engineering, etc.).
* **Job Positions**: Provide a description of the job positions available at your company.
* **Industry**: Indicate the type of industry your company belongs to.
* **Job Type**: Indicate the type of job positions available at your company (e.g. full-time, part-time, internship, etc.).
* **Job Level**: Select the level of job position available (e.g. entry-level, mid-level, etc.).
* **Job Function**: Indicate the type of job function available (e.g. software development, sales, marketing, etc.).
* **Job Requirements**: Provide any additional requirements for the job positions.
* **Company Size**: Indicate the size of your company.
* **Industry Type**: Indicate the type of industry your company belongs to.
* **Booth Setup Requirements**: If you have any additional requirements for setting up your virtual booth, please list them.
* **Booth Setup Staff**: If you will be bringing staff to set up your virtual booth, please list them.
* **Additional Event Details**: If you have any additional event details, please provide them.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Review and accept the terms and conditions of the virtual career fair.

Note: The fields marked as required must be filled in, otherwise the registration will not be complete.
