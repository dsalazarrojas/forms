# Virtual Event Equipment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Event Equipment Request Form is used to request virtual event equipment for your events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event details, including the event name, start and end times.
2. List the equipment you need for your event.
3. Specify your delivery preferences.
4. Provide any additional comments or information that may be helpful for the equipment team to know.
5. Enter your contact information, including email, phone number, contact name, title, department, room number, building location, and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required):
	+ This field is where you will enter the name of your event, as well as the start and end times.
	+ Please make sure to include all required information.
* **Equipment Needs** (`equipment_needs`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select all the equipment you need for your event.
	+ Examples include Projector, Screen, Microphone, etc.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, text, required):
	+ Enter any delivery preferences or special instructions for the equipment delivery.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments or information that may be helpful for the equipment team to know.
* **Equipment Return** (`equipment_return`, text, optional):
	+ If you have equipment that is being returned, enter details about the equipment and when it is expected to be returned.
* **Contact Email** (`email_address`, email, required):
	+ Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your request.
* **Contact Phone** (`phone_number`, text, required):
	+ Enter your phone number so that we can contact you about your request.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter your name as the contact person for this request.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, required):
	+ Enter your title or position.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required):
	+ Enter your department or team name.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, text, required):
	+ Enter the room number where the event will take place.
* **Building Location** (`building_location`, text, required):
	+ Enter the building location where the event will take place.
* **Room Details** (`room_details`, text, required):
	+ Enter any additional details about the room, such as the room type or amenities.
* **IT Requirements** (`it_requirements`, text, required):
	+ Enter any IT requirements for your event, such as internet connectivity or power needs.
* **IT Notes** (`it_notes`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional IT notes or information.
* **AV Requirements** (`av_requirements`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any AV requirements for your event, such as sound or video needs.
* **AV Notes** (`av_notes`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional AV notes or information.
* **AV Equipment Needed** (`av_equipment`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select all the AV equipment you need for your event.
	+ Examples include Microphone, Speaker System, etc.
* **AV Notes 1** (`av_notes_1`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional AV notes or information.
* **AV Notes 2** (`av_notes_2`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional AV notes or information.
* **IT Notes 1** (`it_notes_1`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional IT notes or information.
* **IT Notes 2** (`it_notes_2`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional IT notes or information.
* **IT Equipment Needed** (`it_equipment`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select all the IT equipment you need for your event.
	+ Examples include Computer, Printer, etc.
* **AV Notes 3** (`av_notes_3`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional AV notes or information.
* **IT Notes 3** (`it_notes_3`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional IT notes or information.

Note: This guide assumes that the form will be completed by the event organizer or coordinator. If you are not the event organizer or coordinator, please do not complete this form.
