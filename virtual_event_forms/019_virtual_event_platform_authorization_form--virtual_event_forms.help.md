# virtual_event_platform_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for virtual events. It's used for registration and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the participant name field with your full name.
2. Provide your email address in the email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the phone number field (if applicable).
4. Enter your date of birth in the date of birth field (in the format YYYY-MM-DD).
5. Enter your time of birth in the time of birth field (in the format HH:MM:SS AM/PM).
6. Select your answer to the select one question.
7. Select your answers to the select multiple question.
8. Add any additional information in the note field if needed.
9. Select your answer to the select one other question.
10. Select your answers to the select multiple other question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for event updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for event updates and notifications (if you have one).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 1990-01-01).
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM:SS AM/PM (e.g., 08:00:00 AM).
* **Select One**: Select one of the options provided. This question is intended to gather information about your preferences or characteristics.
* **Select Multiple**: Select all that apply from the provided options.
* **Note**: Add any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to the event.
* **Select One Other**: Select one of the options provided. This question is intended to gather information about your preferences or characteristics.
* **Select Multiple Other**: Select all that apply from the provided options.
