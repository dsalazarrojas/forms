# Stripe Webinar Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect attendee's information for the webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: 
   * **Event name**: Enter the name of the webinar you are interested in.
   * **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and details about the webinar.

2. Fill out any additional fields you feel are relevant to your participation in the webinar:
   * **First name**: Your first name.
   * **Last name**: Your last name.
   * **Phone**: Your phone number for follow-up calls or reminders.
   * **Date**: The date of the webinar you would like to attend.
   * **Time**: The time of the webinar you would like to attend.
   * **Note**: Any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text, required: false): Your first name.
* **Last name** (text, required: false): Your last name.
* **Email** (email, required: true): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and details about the webinar.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Your phone number for follow-up calls or reminders.
* **Date** (date, required: false): The date of the webinar you would like to attend.
* **Time** (time, required: false): The time of the webinar you would like to attend.
* **Note** (note, required: false): Any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Event name** (text, required: true): The name of the event/webinar you are interested in.
