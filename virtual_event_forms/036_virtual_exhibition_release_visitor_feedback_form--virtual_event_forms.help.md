# Virtual Exhibition Release Visitor Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Exhibition Release Visitor Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from visitors to the virtual exhibition, enabling us to improve the user experience and overall exhibition quality.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Feedback" field (page 1) with your genuine thoughts about your experience. 
2. Choose your level of satisfaction with the exhibition by selecting one of the options (page 2). 
3. If you have any additional comments, type them in the "Comments" field (page 3). 
4. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field (page 4). 
5. Enter your email address (page 5) and phone number (page 6), if you're comfortable sharing them. 
6. Finally, enter your organization (page 7) if you're representing one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (`visitor_feedback`, text, required): Describe your experience with the virtual exhibition. 
* **How satisfied do you feel?** (`satisfaction_rating`, select_one, required): Choose one of the available options to rate your satisfaction. 
* **Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, required): Provide any additional thoughts or comments about your experience. 
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your name, organization, and address. 
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up. 
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number, if you're comfortable sharing it. 
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): If you're representing an organization, please enter your company or institution name. 

## Tips

- Be honest and detailed in your feedback to help us improve the virtual exhibition. 
- Ensure that your email address and phone number are accurate and up-to-date. 
- If you're representing an organization, please ensure that your contact information is correct and up-to-date.
