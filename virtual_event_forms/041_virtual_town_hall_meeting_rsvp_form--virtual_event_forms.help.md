# Virtual Town Hall Meeting RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Town Hall Meeting RSVP Form is a crucial tool for us to gauge the interest and attendance of our virtual town hall meeting. It helps us understand who is coming, their role, and any special requests they may have. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name accurately.
2. Enter a valid Email address for communication purposes.
3. Provide your Phone number for contact.
4. Select your Organization or affiliation.
5. Choose your role in the meeting (Administrator, Speaker, Participant, Moderator, or Organizer).
6. Indicate whether you will be Attending the meeting (True, False, or Maybe).
7. Enter the Date of the meeting you are RSVPing to.
8. Select the Time you expect to attend.
9. Choose the Timezone for the meeting.
10. Enter the Location of the meeting (if applicable).
11. Optionally, provide any Comments or Special Requests you may have.
12. Finally, submit your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name for registration purposes.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name for registration purposes.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Organization** (`Organization`, text, required): Enter your affiliation or organization name.
* **Role** (`Role`, select_one, required): Choose your role in the meeting (Administrator, Speaker, Participant, Moderator, or Organizer).
* **Attending** (`Attending`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you will be attending the meeting (True, False, or Maybe).
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the meeting you are RSVPing to.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required): Select the time you expect to attend.
* **Timezone** (`Timezone`, select_one, required): Choose the timezone for the meeting.
* **Location** (`Location`, text, required): Enter the location of the meeting (if applicable).
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): Optionally provide any comments or special requests.
* **Special Requests** (`Special Requests`, text, optional): Optionally provide any special requests.
* **RSVP** (`RSVP`, text, required): Confirm your RSVP.
* **Message** (`Message`, text, optional): Optionally enter a message to be shared with the group.
* **Message To** (`Message To`, text, optional): Optionally enter the name of the person you would like to send the message to.
* **Message From** (`Message From`, text, optional): Optionally enter the name of the person sending the message.
* **Subject** (`Subject`, text, optional): Optionally enter the subject of the message.
* **Cc** (`Cc`, text, optional): Optionally enter the email addresses to be CC'd on the message.
* **Bcc** (`Bcc`, text, optional): Optionally enter the email addresses to be BCC'd on the message.
* **Reply To** (`Reply To`, text, optional): Optionally enter the email address to reply to.
* **Reply Cc** (`Reply Cc`, text, optional): Optionally enter the email addresses to be CC'd on the reply.
* **Message Body** (`Message Body`, text, optional): Optionally enter the body of the message.
