# Virtual Conferencing Participant Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects participant information and consents for virtual conferencing events. It gathers necessary details such as name, email, phone number, event date, time, and location. The participant consents section asks multiple consents for various purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Enter your Participant Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents. This is the participant's identifying information.
2.  **Enter your Email**: Type your email address to ensure that we can contact you regarding the event.
3.  **Enter your Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you via phone.
4.  **Event Date**: Enter the date of the virtual conferencing event.
5.  **Event Time**: Enter the time of the virtual conferencing event.
6.  **Event Location**: Enter the location of the virtual conference, if applicable.
7.  **Event Description**: Briefly describe the event purpose and details if required.
8.  **Participation Consents**: Please confirm your participation by selecting "Yes" for the consents that apply to you:

    * Participant Consent 1: This is for general event participation.
    * Participant Consent 2: This is for sharing your email address with other participants.
    * Participant Consent 3: This is for sharing your phone number with other participants.
    * Participant Consent 4: This is for sharing your event location with other participants.
    * Participant Consent 5: This is for sharing your event description with other participants.
    * Participant Consent 6: This is for sharing your participant name with other participants.
    * Participant Consent 7: This is for sharing your event date with other participants.
    * Participant Consent 8: This is for sharing your event time with other participants.
    * Participant Consent 9: This is for sharing your participant consents with other participants.
    * Participant Consents 10-22: These are for future consents that are not specified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Your full name as it appears on official documents.
*  **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your email address for contact purposes.
*  **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Your phone number for emergency contact.
*  **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): The date of the virtual conferencing event.
*  **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): The time of the virtual conferencing event.
*  **Time Zone** (`timezone`, select_one, optional): Your time zone for time zone adjustments.
*  **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): The location of the virtual conference if applicable.
*  **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the event purpose and details if required.
*  **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, select_multiple, required): Confirm your participation consents for the following:
    *   General event participation.
    *   Sharing your email address with other participants.
    *   Sharing your phone number with other participants.
    *   Sharing your event location with other participants.
    *   Sharing your event description with other participants.
    *   Sharing your participant name with other participants.
    *   Sharing your event date with other participants.
    *   Sharing your event time with other participants.
    *   Sharing your participant consents with other participants.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you provide accurate information to avoid any issues during the event.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
