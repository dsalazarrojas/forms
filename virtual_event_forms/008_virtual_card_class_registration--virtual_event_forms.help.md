# Virtual Card Class Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Virtual Card Class Registration" form is designed to collect information from users who want to register for a virtual card class. This form gathers essential details such as name, contact information, class choice, and additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your valid Email address in the "Email" field.
3. Choose your preferred Class from the "Class Choice" dropdown menu.
4. Select your preferred Date and Time for the class.
5. Give your consent for the class by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "Consent" field.
6. Provide any Additional Info in the "Additional Info" field if necessary.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Fill in your Address in the "Address" field.
9. Enter your City in the "City" field.
10. Enter your Zip code in the "Zip" field.
11. Enter your State in the "State" field.
12. Enter your Country in the "Country" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Class Choice** (Select One, Required): Choose your preferred class from the options provided.
* **Date** (Date, Required): Select your preferred date for the class.
* **Time** (Time, Required): Select your preferred time for the class.
* **Consent** (Select Multiple, Optional): Give your consent for the class by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Address** (Text, Required): Enter your residential or office address.
* **City** (Text, Required): Enter the city of your address.
* **Zip** (Text, Required): Enter the zip code of your address.
* **State** (Text, Required): Enter the state of your address.
* **Country** (Text, Required): Enter the country of your address.
* **Additional Info 1-11** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information you want to provide about yourself.

Note that each of the "Additional Info" fields can be filled in if you feel it's necessary to provide more details about yourself.
