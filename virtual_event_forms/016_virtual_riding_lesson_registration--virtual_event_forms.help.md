# Virtual Riding Lesson Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Riding Lesson Registration form is designed to gather information from riders about their virtual riding lesson preferences and details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the `Riding School` field with the name of the riding school or instructor offering the virtual lesson.
2. Enter the `Trainer Name` of the instructor providing the virtual lesson.
3. Select the `Start Date` and `End Date` for the virtual lesson, keeping in mind that `start_date` is the actual date of the lesson and `preferred_date` is the rider's preferred date.
4. Choose the `Lesson Type` from the available options (Virtual Group, Private, or Semi-Private).
5. Select the `Riding Level` (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3) based on your riding experience.
6. Choose the `Lesson Duration` (30 minutes or 60 minutes) for the virtual lesson.
7. Select the `Rider Age` category (Child, Adult, or Senior).
8. Select the `Payment Method` (Cash or Credit/Debit).
9. Enter your `Email` address and `Phone` number for contact information.
10. If necessary, add any `Note` about the lesson or your preferences.
11. Confirm your `Phone` number and `Email` address for accuracy.
12. Click the `Submit` button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Riding School** (`riding_school`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the riding school or instructor offering the virtual lesson.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the instructor providing the virtual lesson.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, date, required/optional): Select the start time of the virtual lesson.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, date, required/optional): Select the end time of the virtual lesson.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Select the actual date of the virtual lesson.
* **Lesson Details** (`lesson_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the lesson or your preferences.
* **Rider Details** (`rider_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the rider.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required/optional): Select the preferred date for the virtual lesson.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required/optional): Select the preferred start time for the virtual lesson.
* **Riding Level** (`riding_level`, select_one, required): Select your riding level (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
* **Lesson Type** (`lesson_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of lesson (Virtual Group, Private, or Semi-Private).
* **Lesson Duration** (`lesson_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of the lesson (30 minutes or 60 minutes).
* **Rider Age** (`rider_age`, select_one, required): Select your age category (Child, Adult, or Senior).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method (Cash or Credit/Debit).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the lesson.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, required/optional): Confirm your phone number.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, required/optional): Confirm your email address.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click to submit the form.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, required/optional): Click to cancel the form submission.
* **Error Message** (`error_message`, text, required/optional): Display any error messages that may occur during the submission process.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, required/optional): Display the form ID.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, text, required/optional): Display the form category.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, required/optional): Display the form description.

## Tips
* Double-check the form fields for accuracy before submitting.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in.
* Review the form carefully before clicking the `Submit` button.
* If you encounter any errors during submission, check the `Error Message` field for guidance.
