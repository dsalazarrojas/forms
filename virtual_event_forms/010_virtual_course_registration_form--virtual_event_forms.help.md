# Virtual Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Course Registration Form is used to register students for virtual courses. This form is required to create a student account in the system and ensure the student is enrolled in the correct course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your last name in the field labeled "Last name".
3. Enter your course code in the field labeled "Course Code".
4. Provide the name of the course you wish to register for in the field labeled "Course Name".
5. Enter your student email address in the field labeled "Student Email".
6. Enter your student phone number in the field labeled "Student Phone".
7. Enter your student address in the field labeled "Student Address".
8. Provide any additional course details in the field labeled "Course Details".
9. Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided in the field labeled "Payment Method".
10. Read and agree to the terms of the agreement in the field labeled "Agreement".
11. Enter a message in the field labeled "Message" (optional).
12. Confirm your password in the field labeled "Confirm Password".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name here. This field is required to complete the registration process.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name here. This field is required to complete the registration process.
* **Course Code** (`course_code`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the course code here. This field is required to select the correct course for registration.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the course you wish to register for here. This field is required to select the correct course for registration.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your student email address here. This field is required to contact you with course information and updates.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your student phone number here. This field is optional and used for additional contact information.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your student address here. This field is optional and used for additional contact information.
* **Course Details** (`course_details`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional course details here. This field is optional and used to provide more information about the course.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided. This field is optional and allows you to select the payment method you wish to use for registration.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_one`, required/optional): Read and agree to the terms of the agreement. This field is required to complete the registration process.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a message here (optional). This field is optional and allows you to provide additional information about your registration.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm your password here. This field is required to complete the registration process, but is not actually used for password security purposes as this form does not appear to support password creation. 

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to complete the registration process.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the course administrator for assistance.
* This form is only intended for individual student use, please do not submit this form on behalf of another student.
