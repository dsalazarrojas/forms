# Digital Team Building Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register teams participating in the Digital Team Building Challenge. Fill out the form to provide information about your team's composition, challenge preferences, and schedule requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the number of members in your team.
2. Choose the type of challenge your team is participating in.
3. Enter the start date of the challenge (if applicable).
4. Enter the end date of the challenge (if applicable).
5. Select your preferred challenge type (if applicable).
6. Choose whether your team has a specific goal for the challenge.
7. Enter your preferred date for the challenge (if applicable).
8. Briefly describe your team's goal for the challenge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Members (select_multiple)** (`team_members`, multiple select options): Select the number of members in your team by choosing the corresponding option from the list.
* **Challenge Type (select_one)** (`challenge_type`, multiple select options): Choose the type of challenge your team is participating in by selecting one of the available options.
* **Start Date (date)** (`start_date`, date input): If you've selected a challenge type, enter the start date of the challenge. This field is only required if you've chosen a challenge type.
* **End Date (date)** (`end_date`, date input): If you've selected a challenge type, enter the end date of the challenge. This field is only required if you've chosen a challenge type.
* **Preferred Challenge Type (text)** (`preferred_challenge_type`, text input): If you have a preferred type of challenge, enter it here.
* **Team Goal (select_one)** (`team_goal`, multiple select options): Choose whether your team has a specific goal for the challenge by selecting one of the options.
* **Preferred Date (date)** (`preferred_date`, date input): If you've selected a challenge type, enter your preferred date for the challenge. This field is only required if you've chosen a challenge type.
* **Goal (text)** (`goal`, text input): Briefly describe your team's goal for the challenge.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct challenge type and dates if applicable.
* Enter your team's goal in a clear and concise manner.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact us for assistance.
