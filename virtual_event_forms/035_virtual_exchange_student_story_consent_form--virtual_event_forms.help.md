# Virtual Exchange Student Story Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about virtual exchange students and their media and story permissions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Student Name field with the student's legal name as it appears on their passport or government ID.
2. Enter the student's email address if they have one.
3. Check the "Media Type" field to indicate if you wish to share media (e.g., photos, videos) with the student's story. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
4. If you have checked "Media Type" as 'Yes', fill in the Media URL field with the URL or location where the media can be found.
5. Check the "Story Type" field to indicate the type of story the student wants to share. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
6. If you have checked "Story Type" as 'Yes', fill in the Story URL field with the URL or location where the story can be found.
7. Check the "Sharing Permission" field to indicate if you wish to share the student's story publicly. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
8. Fill in the Student ID field with the student's ID number.
9. Check the "Media Permission" field to indicate if you wish to share the media with the student's story. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
10. Read and agree to the terms of consent by filling in the "Consent" field with a signature.
11. Sign the "Signature" field with your own signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the student's legal name as it appears on their passport or government ID.
* **Email**: Enter the student's email address if they have one.
* **Media Type**: Check to indicate if you wish to share media (e.g., photos, videos) with the student's story. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Media URL**: Fill in the URL or location where the media can be found if you have checked "Media Type" as 'Yes'.
* **Story Type**: Check to indicate the type of story the student wants to share. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'. If you have checked "Story Type" as 'Yes', fill in the Story URL field.
* **Story URL**: Fill in the URL or location where the story can be found if you have checked "Story Type" as 'Yes'.
* **Sharing Permission**: Check to indicate if you wish to share the student's story publicly. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number.
* **Media Permission**: Check to indicate if you wish to share media with the student's story. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Consent**: Read and agree to the terms of consent by filling in the field with a signature.
* **Signature**: Sign the form with your own signature to confirm completion.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have the necessary approvals or permissions from your organization or institution to collect and share the information gathered from this form.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
