# Virtual Summer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from parents or guardians for their child(ren) to participate in a virtual summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your name and your child's name in the **User Input** field.
2. Provide your contact information in the **Parent Info** field.
3. Select one or more preferred summer camps from the **Camp Selections** field.
4. Choose your preferred dates and times for the selected summer camp in the **Dates and Times** field.
5. If you have any additional information to share with the summer camp staff, enter it in the **Additional Info** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input
**User Input** (`user_input`, text, required): Enter your name and your child's name. Please make sure to include your child's name and your own name as it appears on official documents (e.g., ID, passport, driver's license).

### Parent Info
**Parent Info** (`parent_info`, text, required): Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email address. This is how we will contact you regarding your child's registration and any questions you may have.

### Camp Selections
**Camp Selections** (`summer_camp_selections`, select multiple, required): Select one or more preferred summer camps from the list below:
* Summer Camp 1
* Summer Camp 2
* Summer Camp 3

### Dates and Times
**Dates and Times** (`dates_and_times`, time, required): Choose your preferred dates and times for the selected summer camp. Please select a start date and an end date for the camp, and also select the start and end times for each date.

### Additional Info
**Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, optional): If you have any additional information to share with the summer camp staff, enter it here. This can include any allergies, special needs, or other relevant information about your child.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We will contact you to confirm your registration and provide further instructions.
