# Virtual Show Replay Distribution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the consent of virtual event participants to share their name and email address with other attendees or the event organizers. Participants' responses will be reviewed and recorded separately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the option "Yes" or "No" to the question "Do you agree to the replay distribution".
2.  Enter your full name in the "Full name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Select "Yes" or "No" to the question "Verify email". This is to confirm that you own the email address you just entered.
5.  Select "I consent to have my name and email address recorded" or "I do not consent to have my name and email address recorded" in the "Do you consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Do you agree to the replay distribution** (`Yes/No`, required/optional): Please select "Yes" if you agree to have your name and email address shared with other attendees or the event organizers. Select "No" if you do not agree.
* **Full name** (`Full name`, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or as you would like to be referred to in the virtual event.
* **Email** (`Email`, required/optional): Enter the email address you used to register for the virtual event.
* **Verify email** (`Yes/No`, required/optional): Confirm that you own the email address you just entered by selecting "Yes".
* **Do you consent** (`I consent to have my name and email address recorded/I do not consent to have my name and email address recorded`, required/optional): Select "I consent to have my name and email address recorded" if you agree to have your information shared. Select "I do not consent to have my name and email address recorded" if you do not agree.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your full name and email address accurately.
* If you select "No" to the "Do you agree to the replay distribution" question, your information will not be shared.
* If you select "I do not consent to have my name and email address recorded", your information will not be shared.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
