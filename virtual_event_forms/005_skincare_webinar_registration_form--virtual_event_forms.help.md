# Skincare Webinar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from attendees registering for a skincare webinar. Please fill it out accurately to ensure a successful and informative experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select your professional background from the dropdown menu.
3. Answer "yes" or "no" to the confirmation question.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Segmentation (1)**: Select your professional background. Please choose from the options:
	+ **Beauty Professional**: You work in the beauty industry.
	+ **Wellness Professional**: You work in the wellness industry.
	+ **Other (please specify)**: You work in a different field. Provide more information in the **Additional Info** field.
* **Additional Info (2)**: If you have any additional information you'd like to share with us, please provide it here. This is optional.
* **Confirmation (3)**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the webinar. Please answer "yes" or "no" accordingly.
* **Form Submit (4)**: Double-check your answers and submit the form to complete your registration.
