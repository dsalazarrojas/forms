# Webinar Archive Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Webinar Archive Access Request form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Requester Details** field with any additional information you want to share about your request.
2. Select your intended use for the webinar archive from the **Intended Use** dropdown menu.
3. Read and agree to our terms of use by typing in the **Consent** field.
4. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Details (text)**: This field is where you can provide any additional information you want to share about your request.
	+ Please keep it concise and clear, and feel free to add any relevant details.
* **Intended Use (select_one)**: Select one of the three options for how you plan to use the webinar archive:
	+ Option 1: For personal use (e.g., for personal learning or research)
	+ Option 2: For company use (e.g., for company training or events)
	+ Option 3: For public sharing (e.g., on social media or blog)
* **Consent (note)**: This is a text field where you can indicate your agreement to our terms of use.
	+ Please type "I agree" to confirm your consent.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address where we can contact you about your request.
	+ Please ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
