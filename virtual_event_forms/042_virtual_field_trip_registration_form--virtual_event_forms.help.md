# Virtual Field Trip Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Virtual Field Trip Registration Form is used to collect information about virtual field trips for teachers and parents. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section carefully and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (User Input, text, not required): A space for users to provide any additional information or comments about the trip.
* **Trip Details** (Trip Details, text, required): A field for describing the trip, including any relevant details.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, required): The date the trip starts.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, not required): The date the trip ends. (Note: If not specified, it will be assumed that the trip ends on the same date it starts.)
* **Trip Name** (Trip Name, text, required): The name of the trip.
* **Teacher Name** (Teacher Name, text, required): The name of the teacher leading the trip.
* **Teacher Email** (Teacher Email, email, not required): The email address of the teacher.
* **Student List** (Student List, select multiple, required): A list of yes or no answers for students attending the trip.
* **Parent Signature** (Parent Signature, note, required): A space for parents to sign and date the form.
* **School ID** (School ID, select one, required): A yes or no answer about the school ID.
* **Teacher Signature** (Teacher Signature, select one, required): A yes or no answer about the teacher's signature.
* **Form Submitted By** (Form Submitted By, select one, required): A yes or no answer about who submitted the form.
* **Form Completed Date** (Form Completed Date, date, required): The date the form was completed.
* **Device Type** (Device Type, select one, required): A yes or no answer about the device type.
* **Student Names** (Student Names, text, required): A space for listing the names of students attending the trip.
* **Student Count** (Student Count, number, required): The number of students attending the trip.
* **Student Attendance** (Student Attendance, select multiple, required): A list of yes or no answers for student attendance.
* **Teacher Attendance** (Teacher Attendance, select multiple, required): A list of yes or no answers for teacher attendance.
* **Student Comments** (Student Comments, text, not required): A field for students to provide comments about the trip.
* **Teacher Comments** (Teacher Comments, text, not required): A field for teachers to provide comments about the trip.
* **Teacher Phone** (Teacher Phone, text, not required): The teacher's phone number.
* **Student Phone** (Student Phone, text, not required): The student's phone number.
* **Teacher Email List** (Teacher Email List, select multiple, not required): A list of yes or no answers about teacher email lists.
* **Parent Phone** (Parent Phone, text, not required): The parent's phone number.
* **Teacher Email Address** (Teacher Email Address, text, not required): The teacher's email address.

Please note that all fields with a "required" status are mandatory and must be filled out. Fields without a "required" status are optional and can be left blank if not applicable. 

This guide is designed to help you complete the Virtual Field Trip Registration Form accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
