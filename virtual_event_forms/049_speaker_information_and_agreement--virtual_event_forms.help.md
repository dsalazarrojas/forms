# Speaker Information And Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store speaker information, which will be displayed on event-related materials.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it should be displayed on event materials.
2. Enter your company or organization as it is relevant to your speaker role.
3. Provide a brief bio that describes your background and expertise.
4. Select your role in the event (Speaker, Organizer, Staff, or Guest).
5. Agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
6. If you wish to be contacted by the event organizers, enter your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it should be displayed on event materials.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address, if you wish to be contacted by the event organizers.
* **Speaker Bio** (`speaker_bio`, text, optional): Provide a brief bio that describes your background and expertise.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company or organization as it is relevant to your speaker role.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Select your role in the event (Speaker, Organizer, Staff, or Guest).
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, optional): Agree to the terms and conditions of the event by selecting both options.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address, if you wish to be contacted by the event organizers.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number, if you wish to be contacted by the event organizers.

## Tips

* Please keep your bio brief and concise.
* If you have any specific requests or requirements for your speaker role, please contact the event organizers.
* The event organizers will use this information to display on event materials.
