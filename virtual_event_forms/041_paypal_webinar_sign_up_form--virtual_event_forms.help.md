# PayPal Webinar Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user registration details for a webinar event hosted by PayPal. It is intended to gather essential information to facilitate your participation in the webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **first name** in the text field provided. This will be used to address you during the webinar.
2. Provide your **email address** in the format of `username@domain.com`. This is where you will receive important updates and reminders about the webinar.
3. Enter your **phone number** in the format of `###-#####-#####`. This is your contact number and will be used to contact you if needed.
4. Select the **webinar date** of your preferred attendance. Choose a date that works for you from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name to be addressed by name during the webinar.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Provide a valid email address where you will receive important updates and reminders about the webinar.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format of `###-#####-#####` for any necessary contact.
- **Date** (`webinar_date`, date, required): Select the date of the webinar you prefer to attend from the provided options.

## Tips
- Ensure you enter a valid email and phone number to receive important updates and reminders.
- Choose a date that works for you to avoid any schedule conflicts.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact us at your provided email or phone number.
- Review your details carefully before submitting to avoid mistakes.
- If you've already registered, please do not fill out this form again.
