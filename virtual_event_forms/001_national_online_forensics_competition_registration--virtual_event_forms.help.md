# National Online Forensics Competition Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather registration information for the National Online Forensics Competition. Please fill out this form with your accurate and complete details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with your accurate and complete details.
2. Select the appropriate event and team type for your team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter a unique email address associated with you. This will be used to verify your registration.
* **Team Name** (Team Name, text, required: false): Enter your team name as it appears on your uniform or ID card.
* **Event Name** (Event Name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the event you are participating in (e.g., "Forensic Science Fair").
* **Event Type** (Event Type, select one, required: false): Select the type of event you are participating in from the dropdown menu.
* **Team Type** (Team Type, select one, required: false): Select the type of team you are part of from the dropdown menu.
* **Coach First Name** (Coach First Name, text, required: false): Enter your coach's first name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Coach Last Name** (Coach Last Name, text, required: false): Enter your coach's last name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Coach Email** (Coach Email, email, required: false): Enter your coach's email address.
