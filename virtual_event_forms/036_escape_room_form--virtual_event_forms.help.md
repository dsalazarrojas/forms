# Escape Room Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Escape Room Form is designed to collect information from players participating in the escape room event. This information will help the escape room staff provide a better experience and support for each player.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each of the required fields (marked as "Required") as accurately as possible.
2. Enter your first and last name in the "first_name" and "last_name" fields respectively.
3. Enter a valid email address in the "email" field. This will be used for further communication with you regarding the event.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field. This will allow staff to contact you in case of emergencies or changes.
5. Select your player type in the "Player Type" field. If you do not see your type listed, please choose "Other" and provide a brief explanation in the "Player Type Other" field.
6. Enter your age in the "age" field. This will help staff ensure that the game is suitable for you.
7. Indicate your experience with escape rooms in the "Escape Room Experience" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address to receive further communication from us.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number to receive updates and emergency calls.
* **Player Type** (`player_type`, Select, Required): Select one of the options to indicate your type.
	+ Yes, I have played escape rooms before.
	+ No, this is my first time playing escape rooms.
* **Player Type Other** (`player_type_other`, Text, Optional): If you chose "Other" in the Player Type field, provide a brief explanation of why you chose it.
* **Age** (`age`, Number, Required): Enter your age to ensure the game is suitable for you.
* **Escape Room Experience** (`escape_room_experience`, Number, Required): Indicate your experience level with escape rooms.
	+ 1-2 hours
	+ 2-4 hours
	+ More than 4 hours

Please take a few minutes to review each of the fields carefully, and let us know if you have any questions.
