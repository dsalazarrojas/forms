# Virtual Assistant Exit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Assistant Exit Form is used to collect information from virtual assistants who are leaving the organization. This information is used to document the reasons for leaving and facilitate a smooth transition for both the virtual assistant and the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your company (if applicable) in the "Company" field.
4. Select your role in the "Role" field (Virtual Assistant or Client).
5. Enter your assistant ID (a numerical value).
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
8. Provide a reason for your departure in the "Reason for Ending" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Company** (Text, Required/Optional): If you were working with a specific company, enter its name here.
* **Role** (Select One, Required/Optional): Select either "Virtual Assistant" or "Client" to indicate your role.
* **Assistant ID** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter a numerical value representing your assistant ID.
* **Email** (Email, Required/Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Number** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Reason for Ending** (Text, Required/Optional): Provide a reason for your departure from the organization.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
- This form is only accessible to virtual assistants who are already part of the organization's system.
