# Virtual Education Fair Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees and staff members for the Virtual Education Fair.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all the required fields with accurate and correct information.
2. Review and confirm the information you've entered.
3. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event (if applicable).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Attendee Type** (`attendee_type`, select_one, optional): Choose your role in the event (Attendee, Staff, Parent, Other).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address (if applicable).
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your address (if applicable).
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter the country of your address (if applicable).
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of your address (if applicable).
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the event.
* **Confirm Terms** (`confirm_terms`, text, optional): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, text, optional): Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, optional): Confirm the phone number of the contact person.
* **Confirm Event** (`confirm_event`, text, optional): Confirm the event details.
