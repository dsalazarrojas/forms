# Webinar Registration For Leaders - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Webinar Registration For Leaders form is designed to collect registration information from webinar attendees, including their details, leader consent, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Indicate whether you have leader consent for the webinar.
3. Specify your goals for attending the webinar.
4. Provide details about the webinar you are interested in attending.
5. Choose the location of the webinar.
6. Confirm your leader consent for the webinar.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Attendee Details** (`webinar_registration_for_leaders_form`, text, optional)
This field is for you to fill in your personal details, including your name, email, and phone number.

### 2. **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, optional)
This field is for you to add any additional details about yourself, such as your job title or company.

### 3. **Leader Consent** (`leader_consent`, select_one, optional)
This field is for you to indicate whether you have leader consent for the webinar. Please choose 'Yes' if you have consent, and 'No' otherwise.

### 4. **Attendee Goals** (`attendee_goals`, note, optional)
This field is for you to specify your goals for attending the webinar. Please add any relevant details about why you are interested in attending the webinar.

### 5. **Webinar Details** (`webinar_details`, text, optional)
This field is for you to provide more details about the webinar you are interested in attending, such as the date, time, and topic.

### 6. **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional)
This field is for you to add any additional information about the event, such as the event type.

### 7. **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, optional)
This field is for you to fill in your email address so we can contact you about the webinar.

### 8. **Webinar Time** (`webinar_time`, time, optional)
This field is for you to specify the time you prefer to attend the webinar.

### 9. **Webinar Date** (`webinar_date`, date, optional)
This field is for you to specify the date you prefer to attend the webinar.

### 10. **Leader Consent** (`leader_consent_2`, select_one, optional)
This field is for you to confirm your leader consent for the webinar. Please choose 'Yes' if you have consent, and 'No' otherwise.

### 11. **Webinar Location** (`webinar_location`, select_multiple, optional)
This field is for you to choose the location of the webinar. Please select one or more locations from the list.

### 12. **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, optional)
This field is for you to fill in your name.

### 13. **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text, optional)
This field is for you to fill in your phone number.

### 14. **Attendee Notes** (`attendee_notes`, note, optional)
This field is for you to add any additional notes or comments about your attendance.

### 15. **Attendee Address** (`attendee_address`, text, optional)
This field is for you to fill in your address.

### 16. **Webinar Organiser** (`webinar_organiser`, text, optional)
This field is for you to fill in the name of the webinar organiser.

### 17. **Event Type** (`event_type`, text, optional)
This field is for you to fill in the type of event.

### 18. **Leader Consent** (`leader_consent_3`, select_one, optional)
This field is for you to confirm your leader consent for the webinar. Please choose 'Yes' if you have consent, and 'No' otherwise.

### 19. **Attendee Interests** (`attendee_interests`, text, optional)
This field is for you to specify your interests.

Note: All fields marked as optional can be left blank if not applicable to you.
