# virtual_library_sign_up_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the virtual_library_sign_up_form, a form designed to collect information from users who want to sign up for virtual library services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please fill in the required information.
2. Enter your name in the "User Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. If you wish, provide information about your reading habits in the "Reading Habits" field.
6. If you wish, share your book preferences in the "Book Preferences" field.
7. If you have a favorite genre, select it from the "Favorite Genre" dropdown list.
8. If you have a favorite author, enter their name in the "Favorite Author" field.
9. If you have a favorite book, enter its title in the "Favorite Book" field.
10. If you have any additional information, enter it in the "Other Info" field.
11. Finally, select "Yes" from the "Agree to Terms" dropdown list to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`User Name`, text, required): This is the field where you enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Reading Habits** (`Reading Habits`, text, optional): If you have a preferred way of reading, please let us know.
* **Book Preferences** (`Book Preferences`, text, optional): Share with us what type of books you enjoy.
* **Favorite Genre** (`Favorite Genre`, select_one, optional): Select the genre you enjoy reading the most.
* **Favorite Author** (`Favorite Author`, text, optional): Enter the name of your favorite author.
* **Favorite Book** (`Favorite Book`, text, optional): Enter the title of your favorite book.
* **Other Info** (`Other Info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **Agree to Terms** (`Agree to Terms`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to agree to our terms and conditions.

Note: Please be aware that some fields are required, while others are optional. Make sure to fill in all required fields and provide any additional information you think will help us get to know you better.
