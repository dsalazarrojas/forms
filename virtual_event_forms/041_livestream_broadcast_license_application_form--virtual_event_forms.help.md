# Livestream Broadcast License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Livestream Broadcast License Application Form is designed to collect applicant data for a livestream broadcast license application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Enter your name and contact details in the "Applicant Information" page.
2.  Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" page.
3.  Include the necessary details about your license in the "License Information" page.
4.  Specify the dates for your proposed broadcast in the "Dates" page.
5.  Add any additional information you would like to be considered in the "Additional Information" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information (1)**:
  * This is where you can enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
  * This is the starting point for the application process.
* **Contact Information (2)**:
  * Here, you can provide your contact details such as email address and phone number.
  * This ensures that we can reach out to you for any further questions or clarification.
* **License Information (3)**:
  * In this section, you can provide details about the license you are applying for, including the type of license and any other relevant information.
  * Please ensure that you include accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues during the application process.
* **Dates (4)**:
  * Please specify the dates for your proposed broadcast, including start and end dates.
  * This will help us to schedule the broadcast accordingly and ensure that it is broadcasted as planned.
* **Additional Information (5)**:
  * You can add any additional information you think is essential for your application.
  * This could include any supporting documents or evidence that you would like to submit with your application.
