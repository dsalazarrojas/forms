# Real Estate Virtual Tour Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Virtual Tour Registration Form is designed to collect information from real estate agents and clients who are interested in virtual tours of properties. This form helps us to keep a record of who has registered and when.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out all the required fields (marked as "required") completely and accurately.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3. If a field is optional, you may leave it blank or fill it out if you feel it is relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Name**
	* Label: real_estate
	* Type: text
	* Required: false
	* Explanation: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.

### 2. **Virtual Tour Registration**
	* Label: Virtual Tour Registration
	* Type: text
	* Required: false
	* Explanation: Enter a brief description of the virtual tour you are interested in.

### 3. **Email Address**
	* Label: Email Address
	* Type: email
	* Required: false
	* Explanation: Enter your email address where you can be reached.

### 4. **Availability**
	* Label: Availability
	* Type: select_one
	* Required: false
	* Explanation: Select the time slot you are available for the virtual tour.
	* Options: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, 5:00 PM - 9:00 PM, 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM, 12:00 PM - 5:00 PM

### 5. **Phone Number**
	* Label: Phone Number
	* Type: text
	* Required: false
	* Explanation: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

### 6. **Notes**
	* Label: Notes
	* Type: note
	* Required: false
	* Explanation: Add any additional information about your virtual tour preferences or concerns.

### 7. **Real Estate ID**
	* Label: Real Estate ID
	* Type: number
	* Required: false
	* Explanation: Enter your real estate ID number, if applicable.

### 8. **Real Estate Address**
	* Label: Real Estate Address
	* Type: text
	* Required: false
	* Explanation: Enter the address of the real estate property you are interested in.

## Tips
* Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure we can best serve you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will be in touch with you to confirm your virtual tour details.
