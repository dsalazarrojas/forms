# Virtual Webinar Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect the user's personal and contact information and their opinions on various topics for a virtual webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your information and answer the "Select Question" fields according to your preference.

* **Last Name** (2, text, optional): Enter your last name as you would like to be known during the webinar.
