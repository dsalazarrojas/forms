# Virtual Research Discussion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Research Discussion Consent Form is a tool used by researchers to obtain consent from participants in virtual research discussions. This form collects essential information from participants, including their signature, consent date and time, and permission levels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Participant Information**: Enter a short description of the participant, if necessary.
2. **Signature**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that the participant has given their consent for the research discussion.
3. **Participant Permissions**: Choose the permissions you want to grant to the participant. You can select 'View only', 'Edit', or 'Delete' depending on your needs.
4. **Consent Date**: Enter the date on which the participant gave their consent.
5. **Consent Time**: Enter the time on which the participant gave their consent.
6. **Researcher Information**: Enter a short description of the researcher or organization conducting the discussion, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Information**: A short description of the participant, if needed.
  * Required: No
  * Type: Text
  * Purpose: To provide a brief description of the participant.
* **Signature**: Indicate that the participant has given their consent for the research discussion.
  * Required: No
  * Type: Select One
  * Purpose: To confirm the participant's consent.
* **Participant Permissions**: Choose the level of access you want to grant to the participant.
  * Required: No
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Purpose: To define the permissions for the participant.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date on which the participant gave their consent.
  * Required: No
  * Type: Date
  * Purpose: To record the date of consent.
* **Consent Time**: Enter the time on which the participant gave their consent.
  * Required: No
  * Type: Time
  * Purpose: To record the time of consent.
* **Researcher Information**: A short description of the researcher or organization conducting the discussion, if necessary.
  * Required: No
  * Type: Text
  * Purpose: To provide information about the researcher or organization.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have the participant's consent before proceeding with the research discussion.
- Make sure to store the consent form securely to maintain participant confidentiality.
- Review the participant's permissions before granting them access to sensitive information.
