# Womens_Health_Tech_Forum_Registration_Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for the Womens Health Tech Forum. By filling out this form, attendees can indicate their session preferences, provide contact details, and specify their contact preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred session(s) by choosing from the available options under "Session_Preferences".
2. Provide your attendee details in the "Attendee_Details" field. This is an optional field, and you can enter any relevant information about yourself.
3. Enter your full name in the "Full_Name" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This is an email address that will be used to contact you.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Specify your contact preferences by selecting from the options under "Contact_Preferences". This can be "Online", "InPerson", or "Both".
7. Enter your contact name in the "Contact_Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session_Preferences** (Session A, Session B, Session C, required: false)
    This field allows you to select which sessions you are interested in attending. You can choose one or multiple sessions by selecting the corresponding checkboxes.
* **Attendee_Details** (Attendee_Details, type: note, required: false)
    This field is for any additional information you'd like to provide about yourself as an attendee. This can be a brief description of who you are and what you're interested in learning about.
* **Full_Name** (Full_Name, type: text, required: false)
    Enter your full name, as you would like to be known.
* **Email** (Email, type: email, required: false)
    Enter your email address that we can use to contact you about the event.
* **Phone** (Phone, type: text, required: false)
    Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you about the event.
* **Contact_Preferences** (Contact_Preferences, type: select_one, required: false)
    Specify how you would like to be contacted about the event. You can choose from "Online", "InPerson", or "Both".
* **Contact_Name** (Contact_Name, type: text, required: false)
    Enter the name of the contact person or organization you would like to be associated with.
