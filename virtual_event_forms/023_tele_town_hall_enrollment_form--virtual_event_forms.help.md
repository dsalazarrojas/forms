# Tele Town Hall Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tele Town Hall Enrollment Form is a tool for users to provide their personal and event preferences to the company to ensure an enjoyable and well-tailored event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose the date for the event from the "Event Date" field.
6. Select the time for the event from the "Event Time" field.
7. Enter the location of the event in the "Event Location" field.
8. Optionally, describe the event you are enrolling for in the "Event Description" field.
9. If assigned, enter the tool you will be using in the "Tool" field.
10. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date for the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time for the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, required/optional): Describe the event you are enrolling for (optional).
* **Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required/optional): If assigned, enter the tool you will be using.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
