# Virtual City Hall Meeting Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants in virtual city hall meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter your details**: Fill in your name, email, phone number, and participant count.
2. **Provide contact information**: Enter your contact details for communication.
3. **Choose your options**: If applicable, choose whether you are interested in participating.
4. **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to save your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participants Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it would be displayed on the meeting agenda.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required): Enter your email address and/or phone number for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like us to contact you.
* **Participant Count** (`participant_count`, `number`, required): Indicate how many people will participate with you.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your meeting.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, `date`, optional): Enter the date and time of your virtual meeting (if applicable).
* **Event End Time** (`event_end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the end time of your virtual meeting (if applicable).
* **Meeting Topic** (`meeting_topic`, `text`, optional): Enter the topic of your virtual meeting (if applicable).
* **Participant Interests** (`participant_interests`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate whether you are interested in participating.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are registering for the meeting.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): This will be populated automatically.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, optional): This will be populated automatically.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your submission status (Active, Inactive, or Cancelled).
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any comments for your meeting registration.
