# Virtual Convention Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Convention Registration Form is designed to collect information from attendees for virtual events or conferences. It helps us track and manage registrations, payment details, and event-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with your information.
2. Enter your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Provide payment details, including the payment method and amount.
4. Enter event-specific information, such as the convention name and dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required): This is the first section of the form where you can enter any relevant information about your registration.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number to help us contact you about the event.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required): This is where you can enter your payment method and amount for the convention.
* **Convention Details** (`convention_details`, text, required): Enter the name of the convention and the dates it will take place.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- Double-check your payment details to ensure they are correct.
- Review your filled form before submitting it to avoid any mistakes.
