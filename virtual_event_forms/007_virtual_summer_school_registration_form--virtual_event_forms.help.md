# Virtual Summer School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from students who want to attend the Virtual Summer School. It will be used to organize and track student enrollment and communication with schools participating in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form fields as instructed. All fields are optional, but please make sure to provide accurate and complete information.
2. Select the event(s) you would like to attend from the dropdown list under "Event Selection" and/or "Event Selection Multiple".
3. Enter your contact information and school details in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Enrollment** (`student_enrollment`, text, optional): This field is meant for students to indicate whether they are currently enrolled in a school.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your personal or student email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your personal or student phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please select the date of birth as per your passport or other government-issued ID.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select your preferred time zone for the event.
* **Event Selection** (`event_selection`, select_one, optional): Select the specific event you would like to attend (Summer School 1 or Summer School 2).
* **Event Selection Multiple** (`event_selection_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): If you would like to attend multiple events, select them here.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, please include them here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your registration.
* **School Branding** (`school_branding`, text, optional): This field is meant for school administrators to fill in their school's branding information.
* **School Logo** (`school_logo`, text, optional): Please upload your school's logo or name here.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter your school's physical address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Enter your school's phone number.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): Enter your school's email address.
* **School Note** (`school_note`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests related to your school, please include them here.

Note:
* Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask the event organizers for clarification.
* If you need to make changes to your registration, please contact the event organizers.
* This form will help us to better organize and communicate with you throughout the event.
* We appreciate your participation in this Virtual Summer School, and look forward to meeting you there!
