# Screen Share Session Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Screen Share Session Registration form is designed to collect information from users interested in or participating in screen share sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the "User Input" page with your name and any other relevant details.
2. In the "Session Topic" field, enter a brief description of the session topic that interests you.
3. Choose a scheduled time from the options provided in the "Scheduled Time" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "User Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Provide session notes in the "Session Notes" field, which will be sent to you and the session organizer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Session Topic**: Enter a brief description of the session topic that interests you.
* **Scheduled Time**: Choose a scheduled time that suits you.
* **User Email**: Enter your email address to receive session information and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for the session organizer to contact you.
* **Session Notes**: Provide any additional notes or instructions for the session organizer and yourself.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when filling out the form to avoid confusion and duplication of entries.
* Make sure to review and verify your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
