# Clubhouse Event Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants who wish to participate in Clubhouse events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out your basic contact information: *Your Full Name*, *Email Address*, *Phone Number*, and *Clubhouse Username or Handle*.
2. Select the *Event Name* you are applying for.
3. Choose the *Role You Are Applying For* (e.g., Speaker, Moderator, Panelist, Participant, Listener, or Other).
4. Describe your *Experience and Background* in the relevant fields.
5. Indicate your *Previous Speaking Experience* level (if applicable).
6. List your *Topic or Subject Expertise* areas.
7. Provide a *Key Message or Contribution* you would bring to the event.
8. If applicable, provide your *Social Media Reach*.
9. Specify your *Time Zone*.
10. Confirm your *Technical Readiness* for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name**: This is your full name as it appears on your personal documents.
* **Email Address**: This is the email address associated with your Clubhouse account or a valid email you'd like to use for communication.
* **Phone Number**: This is your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Clubhouse Username or Handle**: If you're an existing user, please enter your Clubhouse username or handle. If not, you can create a new account after submitting this form.
* **Event Name**: Select the name of the event you are applying for from the provided options.
* **Role You Are Applying For**: Choose the role you wish to take on in the event (e.g., Speaker, Moderator, Panelist, Participant, Listener, or Other).
* **Experience and Background**: Provide a detailed description of your relevant experience and background that aligns with the event's theme.
* **Previous Speaking Experience**: If you have public speaking experience, select the level of experience you have (No Experience, Some Experience, Moderate Experience, or Extensive Experience).
* **Topic or Subject Expertise**: List the areas of knowledge you are an expert in.
* **Key Message or Contribution**: Describe what you would bring to the event and what makes you a valuable participant.
* **Social Media Reach**: If applicable, provide your social media follower count or influence.
* **Time Zone**: Specify your time zone so that we can coordinate with you accordingly.
* **Technical Readiness**: Confirm your ability to handle the required audio and internet technical requirements for the event.
* **Application Date**: This is the date you are applying for. Please ensure this is accurate to prevent duplicate submissions.
