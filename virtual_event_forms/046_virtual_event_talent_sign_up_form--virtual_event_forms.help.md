# virtual_event_talent_sign_up_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The virtual event talent sign up form is designed for individuals to showcase their talent and interest in participating in virtual events. This form will help event organizers assess your talent and suitability for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you're eligible to participate in the event according to the specified criteria.
2.  Select the correct talent type that best describes your performance skillset.
3.  Provide detailed information about the length of your act.
4.  Describe your performance in a concise and clear manner.
5.  Add any additional details that you think would be helpful for the event organizers to know.
6.  Provide any additional information that you think would be relevant for the event organizers to consider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Talent Type** (`virtual_event_talent_sign_up_form_type`): Choose one of the three options: Musician, Dancer, or Comedian. Select the option that best represents your performance skillset.
* **Length of Act** (`virtual_event_talent_sign_up_form_length_of_act`): Please enter the exact length of your performance, in minutes. This will help event organizers schedule your act correctly.
* **Details of Performance** (`virtual_event_talent_sign_up_form_details_of_performance`): Provide a detailed description of your performance, including any specific skills you bring to the event.
* **Additional Details** (`virtual_event_talent_sign_up_form_additional_details`): If there's anything else you'd like to share with the event organizers, such as specific props or equipment you need, this is the place to do so.
* **Additional Notes** (`virtual_event_talent_sign_up_form_additional_notes`): Any additional information that would be helpful for event organizers to know, such as any special requirements or requests.
