# Online Donor Event Recording Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Online Donor Event Recording Consent Form is a digital form used to collect information about a virtual event, such as the event name, date, and time, as well as the contact details of the attendees, including their consent to be recorded and the method(s) of recording. This form is essential for documenting and tracking virtual events, ensuring that all necessary data is collected accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event Name** in the `Event Name` field. This field is not required but is useful for identifying the event.
2. Select a date for the **Event Date** in the `Event Date` field. This field is not required but helps in scheduling the event.
3. Choose the **Event Time** for the virtual event. This field is not required but is necessary for scheduling purposes.
4. Provide your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields. These fields are not required but are useful for identifying you as an attendee.
5. Enter your **Email** address in the `Email` field. This field is not required but is helpful for future communication.
6. Enter your **Phone** number in the `Phone` field. This field is not required but is useful for communication.
7. Select your **Recording Consent** preference in the `Recording Consent` field. This field is required to ensure you are aware of and agree to be recorded.
8. Choose the method(s) you consent to be **Recorded By**, selecting one or more options in the `Recording Method` field. This field is required to ensure accurate recording.
9. If you wish to specify who **Recorded By**, enter the name in the `Recorded By` field. This field is not required but is helpful for tracking.
10. Optionally, select a date for when you were **Recorded At** in the `Recorded At` field. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the virtual event (e.g., conference, webinar, or seminar).
* **Event Date**: Select a date for the virtual event. Format is MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time**: Choose the time of the virtual event. Format is 12:00 AM/HH:MM AM/PM.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Recording Consent**: Select your preference to be recorded during the virtual event:
	+ Option 1: I consent to be recorded.
	+ Option 2: Do not record me.
	+ Option 3: I am unsure.
* **Recording Method**: Select the method(s) you consent to be recorded:
	+ Option 1: Video and audio.
	+ Option 2: Audio only.
	+ Option 3: None.
* **Recorded By**: If you wish, specify the person or entity that recorded you.
* **Recorded At**: Optionally, select a date when you were recorded (format: MM/DD/YYYY).
