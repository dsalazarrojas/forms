# Song Queue Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows you to submit song requests to be added to the virtual event queue.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information. You will need to provide the following:
- **Song Title** (`song`, text, not required): Enter the title of the song you would like to add to the queue.
- **Artist** (`artist`, text, not required): If you know the artist of the song, you can enter it here.
- **Song Request** (`song_request`, text, not required): If you have a specific song request or reason for wanting the song in the queue, enter it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dedication** (`dedication`, text, not required): This is the dedication or message you'd like to send with the song when it's played.
* **Preferred Play Time** (`play_time`, time, not required): If you have a specific time you would prefer the song to be played, enter it here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates on your song request.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with updates on your song request.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Click this button to submit your song request. Make sure to review your submission before clicking submit, as you won't be able to edit it once it's submitted.

## Tips
- Please only submit unique song requests to avoid duplicates.
- If you have any issues with your submission, contact us at [contact information].
