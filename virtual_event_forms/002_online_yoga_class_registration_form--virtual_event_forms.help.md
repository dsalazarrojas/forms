# online_yoga_class_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users registering for an online yoga class. The form will collect user details and any additional comments or messages they want to share.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details as required.
2. Provide any additional registration information if necessary.
3. Write a message if you have any specific requests or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details**: Enter your name, email, and any other contact information you'd like to share. This field is not required but will help us get in touch with you.
* **Registration Details**: If you're interested in registering for a yoga class, please provide any necessary information here. You can include the class name, dates, or any other relevant details. This field is not required.
* **Message**: Is there anything else you'd like to tell us? Perhaps you have specific preferences or requests for your yoga practice? This field is not required, but it's a great opportunity to connect with us.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in your information accurately to avoid any issues with your registration.
* If you have any specific questions or need assistance with your registration, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
