# Webinar Content Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Webinar Content Release Form is used to gather information from attendees of a webinar, ensuring they have reviewed and acknowledged the webinar content and have provided any necessary contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked by *Required*.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Select your date of birth.
4. Select the time.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. For the "Select One" field, choose one option from the list.
7. For the "Select Multiple" field, choose multiple options from the list.
8. Add any notes you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Webinar Content Release Form** (`webinar_content_release_form`, text, Not Required): This is the form title.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Not Required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, Not Required): Select your date of birth.
* **Time** (`time`, time, Not Required): Choose your time of birth.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Not Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, Not Required): Choose one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, Not Required): Choose multiple options from the list.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, Not Required): Add any additional notes or comments.
