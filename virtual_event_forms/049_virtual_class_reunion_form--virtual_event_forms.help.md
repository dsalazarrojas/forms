# Virtual Class Reunion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for classmates to register their interest and provide some basic information for the virtual class reunion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill out the form fields with the required information.
3. Make sure to enter a valid email address for yourself.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **reunion_date** (`reunion_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the reunion, or choose not to fill this field if you don't have a specific date in mind.
* **classmates_name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the name of your city or town you currently reside in.
* **message** (`message`, text, optional): Share any message or comment with the class organizers or classmates.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that the reunion organizers can contact you.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for emergency contact or to discuss the reunion.
* **event_date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event if you have a specific date in mind.
* **event_time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event if you have a specific time in mind.
* **event_duration** (`event_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the event in hours.
* **event_location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event location.

Note: If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to contact the reunion organizers.
