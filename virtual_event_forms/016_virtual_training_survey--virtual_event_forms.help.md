# Virtual Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from participants in virtual training events. It helps us understand your experience, thoughts, and ratings about the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required".
2. Provide your name, email address, and participant interest in the corresponding fields.
3. Select your rating for the training event.
4. Add any additional comments or feedback you may have about the event.
5. Click "Submit" to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course you participated in. This field is a text entry field.
* **Course Description**: Add a brief description of the course if you'd like. This field is a text entry field.
* **Course Type**: Select the type of course you participated in from the options provided. This field is a dropdown selection with three options: Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant in the training event. This field is a text entry field.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address. This field is a text entry field with email validation to ensure it's a valid email address.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to contact you. This field is a text entry field.
* **Participant Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback you may have about the training event. This field is a text entry field.
* **Participant Rating**: Rate your experience with the training event from 0 to 100. This field is a numeric rating field.
* **Participant Feedback**: Add any further feedback or comments about the training event. This field is a text entry field.
* **Comments**: Add any general comments or feedback about the training event. This field is a text entry field.
* **Participant Interest**: Select any or all of the interests that apply to you from the provided options. This field is a multi-select field with three options: Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.
* **Participant Institution**: Enter your institution or affiliation if you'd like. This field is a text entry field. 

Note: Please ensure you fill in the required fields (marked as "required") to help us collect accurate and helpful feedback.
