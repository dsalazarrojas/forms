# Virtual Recruitment Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from job seekers interested in participating in a virtual recruitment fair. It is used to register for the fair and showcase your interest and qualifications for specific job openings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields respectively.
2. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field (optional).
3. Select your preferred language from the "Preferred Language" dropdown menu.
4. Choose your job category, sector, and type from the respective dropdown menus.
5. Enter your job reference and resume URL (if applicable).
6. Provide a brief message about yourself and your qualifications.
7. Click the submit button to register for the virtual recruitment fair.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`text`, `required`): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed at the virtual fair.
- **Email** (`email`, `required`): Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
- **Contact Number** (`text`, `optional`): Enter your phone number for follow-up communication (if you wish to be contacted).
- **Preferred Language** (`select_multiple`, `optional`): Choose your native language or the language you are most comfortable communicating in. This will help us understand your language preferences.
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ French
	+ German
	+ Portuguese
	+ Chinese
	+ Japanese
	+ Korean
	+ Arabic
	+ Hindi
- **Job Category** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the category of jobs you are interested in. This will help us direct you to the relevant fair sessions.
	+ IT
	+ Finance
	+ Marketing
	+ HR
	+ Engineering
	+ Logistics
	+ Sales
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Other
- **Job Sector** (`select_one`, `required`): Choose the sector of jobs you are interested in. This will help us understand your preferences.
	+ Public Sector
	+ Private Sector
	+ Non-Profit
	+ Start-up
- **Job Type** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the type of job you are looking for. This will help us direct you to the relevant fair sessions.
	+ Full Time
	+ Part Time
	+ Internship
	+ Freelance
- **Resume URL** (`text`, `optional`): If you have a publicly available resume, enter the URL so we can access it for review.
- **Job Reference** (`text`, `required`): Enter the job reference or code of the job you are interested in. This is usually provided by the employer or job listing.
- **Message** (`text`, `optional`): Write a brief message about yourself and your qualifications. This will help us understand your background and why you are a good fit for the job.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your registration is complete.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact us at form@gic.mx.
