# Speaker Registration And Material Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Speaker Registration And Material Upload" form is used to register speakers for virtual events and allow them to upload their presentation materials. This form is a crucial part of the event registration process, ensuring that speakers provide necessary details and content for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your speaker details in the "Speaker Details" page.
2. Upload your speaker image.
3. Provide event information and session details in the "Event Info" and "Session Info" pages.
4. Specify the session date and time.
5. Upload your speaker material.
6. Add your contact information, including email and phone number (optional).
7. Share your speaker experience and bio (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Speaker Details
* **Speaker Details** (text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself as a speaker.
### Speaker Image
* **Speaker Image** (text, optional): Upload a high-quality image of yourself to be displayed on the event platform.
### Event Info
* **Event Info** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the event or topic you are speaking about.
### Session Info
* **Session Info** (text, optional): Describe the session you will be leading or participating in.
### Session Date
* **Session Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the session you'll be speaking at.
### Session Time
* **Session Time** (time, optional): Enter the start and end time of your session.
### Speaker Material
* **Speaker Material** (text, optional): Upload your presentation or any supporting materials relevant to your session.
### Speaker File Upload
* **Speaker File Upload** (text, optional): This field is for uploading additional files related to your session.
### Speaker Email
* **Speaker Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
### Speaker Phone
* **Speaker Phone** (text, optional): Provide your phone number for easy contact.
### Speaker Experience
* **Speaker Experience** (note, optional): Share your experience in a note format.
