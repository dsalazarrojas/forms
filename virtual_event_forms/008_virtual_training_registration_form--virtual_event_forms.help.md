# Virtual Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for registering for virtual training sessions. It collects information about the attendee, their company, job title, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Provide your phone number, company name, and job title.
3. Select your job function and department.
4. Enter your location and country.
5. Choose your time zone.
6. Select the training session date and time.
7. Choose the duration of the training session.
8. Select the training language and level.
9. Choose the training topic.
10. Select your registration status (Active, Cancelled, or Completed).
11. If required, provide additional information for your registration status.
12. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (1, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (2, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (5, text, required: false): Enter your company name.
* **Job title** (6, text, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Job function** (7, text, required: false): Enter your job function.
* **Department** (8, text, required: false): Enter your department name.
* **Location** (9, text, required: false): Enter your location name.
* **Country** (10, text, required: false): Enter your country name.
* **Time zone** (11, time, required: false): Select your time zone.
* **Date of birth** (12, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Event date** (13, date, required: false): Select the date of the training session.
* **Event time** (14, time, required: false): Select the time of the training session.
* **Training length** (15, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the training session in minutes.
* **Training length unit** (16, text, required: false): Select the unit of the training length (e.g., hours, minutes).
* **Training language** (17, text, required: false): Select the language of the training session.
* **Training level** (18, text, required: false): Select the level of the training session.
* **Training topic** (19, text, required: false): Select the topic of the training session.
* **Training start time** (20, time, required: false): Select the start time of the training session.
* **Training end time** (21, time, required: false): Select the end time of the training session.
* **Registration status** (22, select_one, required: false): Select your registration status (Active, Cancelled, or Completed).
* **Registration status other** (23, text, required: false): If your registration status is not Active, Cancelled, or Completed, please provide a reason for your status.
* **Registration status reason** (24, text, required: false): Enter a detailed reason for your registration status.
* **Submit** (25, text, required: false): Click this button to submit your registration.
