# Atlas Corps Virtual Leadership Institute Directory & Communications Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Atlas Corps Virtual Leadership Institute Directory & Communications Form is a tool used to collect and showcase participant information for the Atlas Corps Virtual Leadership Institute. This form helps connect participants with one another, provides a platform for networking, and allows for efficient communication between participants and the institute.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Full Name** as you would like it to appear in the directory.
2. Enter your **Email Address**, your primary email for communications.
3. Enter your **Phone Number**, including the country code for international numbers.
4. Provide the **Organization Name** of your institution or company.
5. Enter your current **Job Title** or position.
6. Select your **Country of Residence** from the list provided.
7. Choose your local **Time Zone** for scheduling purposes.
8. If you have a **LinkedIn Profile URL**, enter it to provide a link to your profile.
9. Write a brief **Professional Biography** (max 150 words) of your professional background.
10. Select the **Leadership Development Interests** that you are interested in developing.
11. Choose your **Communication Preferences**, how you would like to receive updates (e.g., email newsletter, SMS notifications).
12. Decide on **Photo Consent**, whether you consent to having your photo included in the directory.
13. Indicate your **Networking Opt-In**, whether you are open to being contacted by other participants.
14. If necessary, enter any **Special Accommodations** you require.
15. Provide the **Emergency Contact Name** and **Phone Number** of the emergency contact person.
16. Finally, submit the form to complete the registration process.
