# Virtual Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for virtual workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields (indicated by asterisk \*).
2. Provide accurate and complete information in each field.
3. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Name** (workshop_name, text, Optional): This field is where you should enter the name of the virtual workshop you are interested in attending.
* **Participant Name** (participant_name, text, Optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents, such as your ID or passport.
* **Email** (email, email, Optional): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check for important updates and information.
* **Participant Phone Number** (participant_phone_number, text, Optional): Enter your contact number where you can be reached in case of emergencies or important updates.
* **How many participants do you plan to attend?** (workshop_participants, select_multiple, Optional): Select 'Yes' if you are attending with a group of people or 'No' if you are attending alone.
* **Participant Details** (participant_details, note, Optional): This is a text area where you can provide any additional information about yourself or your group that would be helpful for the workshop organizers.
