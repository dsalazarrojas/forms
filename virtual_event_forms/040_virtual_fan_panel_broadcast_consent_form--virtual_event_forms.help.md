# virtual_fan_panel_broadcast_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent from virtual event participants, ensuring they understand and agree to the event terms and conditions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the participant details section and ensure all information is accurate.
2. Read and understand the consent sections, and if you agree, sign and submit the form.
3. Enter your participant details, including name, email, and phone number (if required).
4. Submit the form once you have reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: This section is used to collect information about the participant, including their name, email, and phone number (if required). Please ensure all fields are accurate.
* **Consent 1**: This section is a statement where you will confirm your agreement to the virtual event terms and conditions. Please read carefully and sign if you agree.
* **Consent 2**: This section is an acknowledgment of your understanding of the virtual event terms and conditions. Please sign if you have read and understood.
* **E Signature 1**: This is the first electronic signature, confirming your agreement to the virtual event terms and conditions.
* **E Signature 2**: This is the second electronic signature, verifying your acknowledgment of the virtual event terms and conditions.
* **E Signature 3**: This is the third electronic signature, confirming your agreement to the virtual event terms and conditions.
* **E Signature 4**: This is the fourth electronic signature, verifying your acknowledgment of the virtual event terms and conditions.
* **E Signature 5**: This is the fifth electronic signature, confirming your agreement to the virtual event terms and conditions.
* **Participant Name**: This field is used to enter your name as a participant.
* **Participant Email**: This field is used to enter your email address as a participant.
* **Participant Phone**: This field is used to enter your phone number as a participant.
