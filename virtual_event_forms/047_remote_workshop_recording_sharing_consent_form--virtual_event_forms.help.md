# Remote Workshop Recording Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Workshop Recording Sharing Consent Form is designed to capture the participant's consent for sharing workshop recordings. This form ensures that participants are aware and agree to the sharing of their recordings and data with specific individuals or groups.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand what it's asking for.
2. For the first field, "Consent to share recordings," select "Consent to share recordings" if you agree to share your recordings with the workshop organizers and/or participants.
3. If you select "No Consent to share recordings," your recordings will not be shared with anyone.
4. In the "Participant Name" field, enter your name as it appears on your ID or identification document.
5. In the "Email" field, enter your email address that is associated with your workshop registration.
6. In the "Contact Number" field, enter your phone number or contact information.
7. In the "Workshop Title" field, enter the title of the workshop you participated in.
8. In the "Workshop Description" field, enter a brief description of the workshop you participated in.
9. In the "Recording URL" field, enter the URL of the recording if you have one.
10. For the "Sharing Consent" field, select "True" if you agree to share your recordings and data with the workshop organizers and/or participants. Select "False" if you do not agree.
11. In the "Sharing Consent Reason" field, provide a brief explanation for why you agree or do not agree to share your recordings and data.
12. In the "Participant Agreement" field, select all that apply. You agree to share your recordings and data with the workshop organizers, with the workshop participants, or neither.
13. In the "Recording Sharing Consent Notes" field, provide any additional notes or comments about your consent.

* **Consent to share recordings (id: 2)**: This field asks you to decide whether you agree to share your recordings or not. Select "Consent to share recordings" if you agree, or "No Consent to share recordings" if you do not.

* **Sharing Consent (id: 9)**: Select "True" if you agree to share your recordings and data with the workshop organizers and/or participants. Select "False" if you do not agree.
* **Sharing Consent Reason (id: 10)**: Provide a brief explanation for why you agree or do not agree to share your recordings and data.
* **Participant Agreement (id: 11)**: Select all that apply. You agree to share your recordings and data with the workshop organizers, with the workshop participants, or neither.
* **Recording Sharing Consent Notes (id: 12)**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your consent.
