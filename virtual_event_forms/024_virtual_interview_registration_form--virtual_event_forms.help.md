# Virtual Interview Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about job openings and candidates for virtual interviews. Please note that this form is intended for internal use only and is subject to change. To ensure accurate and efficient processing of your application, please fill out this form carefully and completely.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully read the instructions and guidelines for each field.
2. Fill out each field according to the guidelines and options provided.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: <label> (user_first_name, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Last Name**: <label> (user_last_name, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
* **Email**: <label> (email, text, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: <label> (phone, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Message**: <label> (user_message, text, optional): Enter a brief message about yourself, your experience, and why you're interested in this job.
* **Job Title**: <label> (job_title, text, optional): Enter the job title of the position you're applying for.
* **Job Type**: <label> (job_type, select_one, required): Choose your current job type from the options provided (Software Developer, Graphic Designer, Data Scientist, Marketing Manager).
* **Job Description**: <label> (job_description, text, optional): Describe your past experience with the skills and qualifications relevant to the job position.
* **Job Location**: <label> (job_location, text, optional): Enter the city or region where you're willing to work.
* **Job Requirements**: <label> (job_requirements, text, optional): List any specific requirements you need to complete the job, such as equipment or software.
* **Job Benefits**: <label> (job_benefits, text, optional): List any benefits you're willing to offer or require in the job.
* **Job Salary**: <label> (job_salary, text, optional): Enter the expected salary for the job.
* **Job Experience**: <label> (job_experience, select_multiple, required): Choose your level of experience from the options provided (Junior, Mid-level, Senior).
* **Job Status**: <label> (job_status, select_one, required): Choose your job status from the options provided (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance).
* **Job Education**: <label> (job_education, select_one, required): Choose your highest level of education from the options provided (High School Diploma, Bachelor's, Master's, Doctorate).
* **Job Skills**: <label> (job_skills, text, optional): List any relevant technical skills you possess.
* **Job Languages**: <label> (job_languages, text, optional): List any languages you're proficient in.
* **Resume**: <label> (resume, text, optional): Attach a link to your resume or a brief summary of your work experience.

**Tips**

* Double-check all entries for accuracy before submission.
* Use clear and concise language in your answers.
* Be prepared to discuss your qualifications and experience during the virtual interview.

Please note that this form is for internal use only and may be subject to change.
