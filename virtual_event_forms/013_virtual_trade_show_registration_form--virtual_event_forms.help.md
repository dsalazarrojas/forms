# Virtual Trade Show Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential attendees about their registration to the virtual trade show. Please provide accurate and relevant information to get the most out of the event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email correctly.
2. Enter your Phone number (if available).
3. Provide your Company name (if applicable).
4. Specify your Job Title and Job Role (if related to your work).
5. Once you've completed all the fields, review your information carefully and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for any updates and notifications from the event.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by the event organizers.
### Company
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name if you're representing a business or organization.
### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title if related to your work or profession.
### Job Role
* **Job Role** (`job_role`, text, optional): Enter your job role within your company or organization if applicable.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to use your official name and contact details.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers using the provided contact information.
* The event organizers will use this information to tailor the event experience to your needs.
