# Virtual Screening Replay Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Screening Replay Request Form is used to request a replay of a virtual screening event that has already taken place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Viewer Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. In the "Message" field, briefly explain the reason for your replay request.
5. Select one of the options in the "Select One" field.
6. Choose multiple options in the "Select Multiple" field.
7. Select a date in the "Date" field.
8. Enter the time in the "Time" field.
9. In the "Note" field, add any additional comments or information.
10. If required, select options for "Viewer Agreement", "Viewer Agreement 1", "Viewer Agreement 2", and "Viewer Agreement 3".
11. In the "Viewer Request" fields, enter any additional requests or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Viewer Name** (`viewer_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Briefly explain the reason for your replay request.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Select one of the available options.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time.
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information.
* **Viewer Agreement** (`viewer_agreement`, select_one, optional): If required, select one of the available options.
* **Viewer Agreement 1** (`viewer_agreement_1`, select_multiple, optional): If required, choose multiple options.
* **Viewer Agreement 2** (`viewer_agreement_2`, select_multiple, optional): If required, choose multiple options.
* **Viewer Agreement 3** (`viewer_agreement_3`, select_multiple, optional): If required, choose multiple options.
* **Viewer Request** (`viewer_request`, text, optional): Enter any additional requests or information.
* **Viewer Request 1-10** (`viewer_request_1-10`, text, optional): Enter any additional requests or information.
