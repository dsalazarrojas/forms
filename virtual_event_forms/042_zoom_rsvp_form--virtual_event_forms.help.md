# Zoom RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect your information for the virtual event. Please take a few minutes to fill it out accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form link provided to access the form.
2. Fill out your First Name and email address accurately.
3. Select your Time Zone.
4. Enter your Phone number (optional but recommended for event organizers to contact you).
5. Click the "Submit" button to send your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached. This is optional but recommended for event organizers to contact you.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, select_one, required): Select your Time Zone from the options provided. This will help event organizers schedule the event correctly.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the event organizer directly.
* Only submit this form once. If you need to make changes, contact the event organizer instead of resubmitting the form.
