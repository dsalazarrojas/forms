# Zoom Webinar Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Zoom Webinar Sign Up Form is a collection of demographic and logistical information from attendees to facilitate the webinar experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. For fields with dropdown options, select the relevant option that best matches your information.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, \*): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, \*): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, \*): Please enter your work email.
* **Company** (`company`, text,): Optional: If you are representing a company, enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text,): Optional: Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text,): Optional: Enter your department.
* **Role** (`role`, text,): Optional: Enter your role.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text,): Optional: Enter your job function.
* **Team** (`team`, text,): Optional: Enter your team.
* **Manager** (`manager`, text,): Optional: Enter your manager's name.
* **Report to** (`report_to`, text,): Optional: Enter the name of the person you report to.
* **Work Email** (`work_email`, email,): Optional: Enter your work email.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, text,): Optional: Enter your work phone number.
* **Join Time** (`join_time`, time, \*): Please select the time zone in which you will be joining the webinar.
* **Join Date** (`join_date`, date, \*): Please select the date you will be joining the webinar.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, select_one, \*): Select your time zone (PST, EST, CST, or MST).
* **Timezone Offset** (`timezone_offset`, number,): Optional: Enter your time zone offset.
* **Timezone Offset HH** (`timezone_offset_hh`, number,): Optional: Enter the hours part of your time zone offset.
* **Timezone Offset MM** (`timezone_offset_mm`, number,): Optional: Enter the minutes part of your time zone offset.
* **Timezone Offset SS** (`timezone_offset_ss`, number,): Optional: Enter the seconds part of your time zone offset.
* **Timezone Offset S** (`timezone_offset_s`, number,): Optional: Enter the seconds part of your time zone offset.
* **Timezone Offset AM/PM** (`timezone_offset_am_pm`, number,): Optional: Enter AM/PM part of your time zone offset.
* **Timezone Offset UTC** (`timezone_offset_utc`, number,): Optional: Enter your time zone offset in UTC.
* **Timezone Offset TZ** (`timezone_offset_tz`, text,): Optional: Enter your time zone offset in TZ.
* **Timezone Offset TZ Abbrev** (`timezone_offset_tz_abbrev`, text,): Optional: Enter your time zone offset in TZ abbrev.
