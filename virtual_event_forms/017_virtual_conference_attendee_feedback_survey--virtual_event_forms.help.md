# Virtual Conference Attendee Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from virtual conference attendees, aiming to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Select the correct options for the questions that require them.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the text fields.
4. Enter your contact information, if you would like to be contacted for follow-up discussions or actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Attendance** (`session_attendance`, `number`, required: false): Indicate how many sessions you attended during the conference.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how satisfied you are with the overall conference experience.
	+ .reportedly: Not applicable
	+ very satisfied: Very satisfied with the conference.
	+ fairly satisfied: Somewhat satisfied with the conference.
	+ somewhat satisfied: Neutral or average satisfaction.
	+ very unhappy: Very unhappy with the conference.
	+ not at all satisfied: Not satisfied at all.
* **Feedback on Sessions** (`feedback_on_sessions`, `note`, required: false): Provide any feedback on the sessions you attended.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, `text`, required: false): Suggest any improvements or ideas for future conferences.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email address, if you would like to be contacted for follow-up discussions or actions.
* **Session Ideas** (`session_ideas`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if you have any session ideas for future conferences.
	+ True: Yes, I have ideas for sessions.
	+ False: No ideas for sessions.
* **Session Attendance Idea** (`session_attendance_idea`, `text`, required: false): Provide any session ideas you have for future conferences.
* **Suggestion for Future Conferences** (`suggestion_for_future_conferences`, `text`, required: false): Suggest any improvements or ideas for future conferences.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Follow up** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if you would like to be contacted for follow-up discussions or actions.
	+ True: Yes, contact me.
	+ False: No, do not contact me.
* **Session Attendance Ideas** (`session_attendance_ideas`, `text`, required: false): Provide any session attendance ideas or suggestions.
* **Suggestion for Conferences** (`suggestion_for_conferences`, `text`, required: false): Suggest any improvements or ideas for future conferences.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any contact information, such as name and job title.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email address, if you would like to be contacted for follow-up discussions or actions.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, `text`, required: false): Enter any social media links.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, required: false): Enter your website URL.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: false): Enter your department name.
* **Company Employees** (`company_employees`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of employees in your company.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, `number`, required: false): Indicate the expected duration of sessions.
* **Session Start** (`session_start`, `number`, required: false): Indicate the expected start time of sessions.
* **Session End** (`session_end`, `number`, required: false): Indicate the expected end time of sessions.
