# Virtual Meeting Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Meeting Recording Consent Form is used to obtain explicit consent from participants in virtual meetings to be recorded. This form ensures that participants are aware of and agree to the recording of their virtual meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the **participant name** field.
2.  Enter your email in the **email** field.
3.  Choose your **participant type** from the dropdown menu.
4.  Select your consent from the **Recording Consent** dropdown menu.
5.  Enter the **meeting details** in the text box.
6.  Enter the **timezone** you will be participating from.
7.  Enter the **meeting topic**.
8.  Enter the **meeting description**.
9.  Optionally, enter the **meeting agenda** and **meeting location url** fields.
10.  Select the recording method in the **recording method** field.
11.  Optionally, enter the **meeting video url**, **meeting audio url**, and **meeting presentation** fields.
12.  Click the **Submit** button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (participant_name): Enter your full name as a participant in the virtual meeting.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address as a participant in the virtual meeting.
* **Participant Type** (participant_type): Choose your role in the virtual meeting from the dropdown menu:
	+ Speaker
	+ Panelist
	+ Audience
* **Recording Consent** (recording_consent): Select your consent to be recorded:
	+ I agree to be recorded
	+ I disagree to be recorded
* **Meeting Details** (meeting_details): Enter any additional comments or details about the meeting.
* **Timezone** (timezone): Enter the timezone you will be participating from.
* **Meeting Topic** (meeting_topic): Enter the topic of the virtual meeting.
* **Meeting Description** (meeting_description): Enter a brief description of the virtual meeting.
* **Meeting Agenda** (meeting_agenda): Optionally, enter the agenda of the virtual meeting.
* **Meeting Location URL** (meeting_location_url): Optionally, enter the URL of the virtual meeting location.
* **Recording Method** (meeting_recording_method): Select the method of recording:
	+ (optional field, not listed in dropdown menu)
* **Meeting Video URL** (meeting_video_url), **Meeting Audio URL** (meeting_audio_url), **Meeting Presentation** (meeting_presentation): These fields are optional and allow you to enter URLs or links related to the meeting.
* **Start Time**, **End Time** (meeting_start, meeting_end): These fields are optional and allow you to enter the start and end time of the virtual meeting.
* **Language** (language): Optionally, enter the language of the virtual meeting.
* **Meeting Agenda** (meeting_agenda), **Meeting Location** (meeting_location), **Meeting Topic** (meeting_topic): These fields are optional and allow you to enter additional details about the virtual meeting.
