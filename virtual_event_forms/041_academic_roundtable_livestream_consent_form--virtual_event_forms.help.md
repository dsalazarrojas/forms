# Academic Roundtable Livestream Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for participants in an academic roundtable livestream to provide their consent to various aspects of the event. It ensures that all participants understand and agree to the terms of their participation, including video and audio recordings, livestreaming, and distribution of recordings. The form is designed to be completed by each participant before the roundtable takes place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Identify your institution or organization affiliation in the "Institution" field.
4. Provide the title of the academic roundtable event in the "Roundtable Title" field.
5. Enter the date and time of the roundtable event in the "Roundtable Date" and "Roundtable Time" fields, respectively.
6. Answer the consent questions regarding participation, video recording, audio recording, livestreaming, distribution, and publication.
7. If you have any special requests or conditions, type them in the "Special Requests or Conditions" field.
8. Finally, confirm that you understand your withdrawal rights in the "Withdrawal Rights Acknowledged" field.
