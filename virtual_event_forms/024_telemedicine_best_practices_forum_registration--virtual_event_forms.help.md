# Telemedicine Best Practices Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the registration process for the Telemedicine Best Practices Forum. Please follow the instructions carefully to ensure a successful and accurate registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding text fields. Please make sure to use your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the email field. This is the address that will be used to communicate with you about the forum and any relevant updates.
3. Enter your **Phone** number in the text field, if you wish to be contacted by phone.
4. Select all the **Topic Interests** that apply to you from the dropdown menu. This will help us tailor the forum content and discussions to your preferences.
5. Click on the **Consent** field to agree to the terms and conditions of the forum. Please take a moment to read and understand the terms before selecting an option.
6. In the **Notes** field, you can add any additional information you would like to share with the forum administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name, as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ Required: No
	+ Type: Text
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname, as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ Required: No
	+ Type: Text
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you use and check regularly.
	+ Required: No
	+ Type: Email
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted by phone.
	+ Required: No
	+ Type: Text
* **Topic Interests**: Select all the topics that interest you from the dropdown menu.
	+ Required: No
	+ Type: Select Multiple
* **Consent**: Select the option that indicates you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the forum.
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Type: Select One
* **Notes**: Add any additional information you would like to share with the forum administrators.
	+ Required: No
	+ Type: Note
