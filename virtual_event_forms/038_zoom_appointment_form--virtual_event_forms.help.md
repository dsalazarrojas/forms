# Zoom Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zoom Appointment Form is designed for users to schedule appointments with providers for virtual events. This form will help you gather necessary information to facilitate a smooth and organized appointment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Select the appointment date from the calendar.
- Choose the provider from the list of options (Zoom or Other).
- If you are a participant, select the participant information (Participant 1 or Participant 2).
- Enter the start and end times for the appointment.
- If applicable, enter the Zoom URL and payment details.
- Specify the participant's role (Host or Participant) and status (Scheduled, Unavailable, Available, or Cancelled).
- Enter the participant's name and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Appointment Date**: Select the date of your scheduled appointment.
* **Provider Information (Provider)**: Choose from the available options for who will be providing the service (Zoom or Other).
* **Participant Information (Participant 1/Participant 2)**: Select this field if you are a participant in the appointment. This field will appear as a dropdown menu with options for the participants.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your appointment.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your appointment.
* **Zoom Url**: If you are using Zoom for your appointment, enter the Zoom URL here.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount for your appointment (if applicable).
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for your appointment (Pay Now, Pay Later, or Other).
* **Payment Status**: Select the payment status of your appointment (Paid, Unpaid, Pending, or Cancelled).
* **Participant Role**: Specify your role in the appointment (Host or Participant).
* **Appointment Status**: Choose the status of your appointment (Scheduled, Unavailable, Available, or Cancelled).
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address as a participant.

Note: The required fields are marked as "required" in the form description above.
