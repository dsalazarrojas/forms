## open_education_webinar_appearance_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect webinar details and user consents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field, which is required.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field, which is also required.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, which is required.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field, which is not required.
5. Choose your consent to record audio in the "Consent To Record Audio" field. You can select one of the three options provided.
6. Choose your consent to share audio with other organizations in the "Consent To Share Audio With Other Organizations" field. You can select one of the two options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your name to identify yourself.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Provide your email address so we can contact you regarding the webinar.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number for further contact.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your address if you want to provide it.
- **Consent To Record Audio** (`consent_to_record_audio`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose if you consent to record audio from the webinar.
- **Consent To Share Audio With Other Organizations** (`consent_to_share_audio_with_other_organizations`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose if you consent to share the recorded audio with other organizations.
