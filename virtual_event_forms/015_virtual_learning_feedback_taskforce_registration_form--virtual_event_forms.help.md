# Virtual Learning Feedback Taskforce Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Learning Feedback Taskforce Registration Form is designed for users who want to join the Virtual Learning Feedback Taskforce. It collects personal, contact, and other relevant details to facilitate communication and tailor the service offerings to meet the user's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form instructions and ensure you understand what each field is used for.
2. Enter your personal details (Page 1: Personal Details).
3. Provide your contact details (Page 2: Contact Details).
4. Select your preferred communication channel (Page 3: Preferred Communication Channel).
5. Choose your virtual environment and learning platform preferences (Page 4: Virtual Environment and Page 5: Virtual Learning Platform).
6. Enter any additional comments (Page 6: Additional Comments).
7. Sign the form with your name (Page 7: Signature).
8. Enter your contact email address (Page 8: Email) and phone number (Page 9: Phone).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details (1)**: This field is the first page where you will enter your name as it appears on your passport or government ID.
* **Contact Details (2)**: Enter your email address here. This will be used for further communication and updates.
* **Virtual Participants (3)**: Select all your roles in the virtual event: Virtual Instructor, Virtual Presenter, or Virtual Facilitator.
* **Preferred Communication Channel (4)**: Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Chat, or Video).
* **Date and Time (5)**: You can enter the date and time you would like to participate in the event.
* **Timezone (6)**: Choose your time zone (UTC+0, UTC-5, or UTC+8).
* **Timezone Offset (7)**: Enter the offset from your time zone.
* **Virtual Learning Goal (8)**: Describe your virtual learning goal in a few words.
* **Virtual Environment (9)**: Select where you would like to participate in the virtual event (On-site, Off-site, or Both).
* **Virtual Learning Platform (10)**: Choose the virtual learning platform you prefer (Zoom, Google Meet, or Skype).
* **Additional Comments (11)**: Enter any comments or preferences you might have.
* **Signature (12)**: Sign the form with your name and date.
* **Email (13)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone (14)**: Enter your phone number.
