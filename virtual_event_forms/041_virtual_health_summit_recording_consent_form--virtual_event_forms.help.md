# Virtual Health Summit Recording Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain attendees' consent for the recording and sharing of their sessions, images, and other content during the Virtual Health Summit. It is essential to complete this form to ensure a clear understanding of what is allowed to be recorded and shared.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and select the correct options for your consent.
2. Answer all required fields.
3. Provide accurate information where possible.
