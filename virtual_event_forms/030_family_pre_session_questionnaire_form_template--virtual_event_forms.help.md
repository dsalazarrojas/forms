# family_pre_session_questionnaire_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information and special needs from family members attending a session or workshop. It's essential to provide accurate and relevant details for the host to better understand the family's needs and plan accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your First Name in the text field marked "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the text field marked "Last Name" (optional).
3. Enter your email address in the text field marked "Email" (required for contact purposes).
4. Enter your Phone Number in the text field marked "Phone Number" (required).
5. Enter your Address in the text field marked "Address" (required).
6. Select how many family members are attending from the dropdown list marked "How many family members are attending?"
7. Indicate if you have any special needs from the dropdown list marked "Do you have any special needs?". You can select multiple options if applicable.
8. (Optional) Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact in the text fields marked "Emergency Contact" and "Emergency Contact Phone".
9. (Optional) If you have any special needs, you can elaborate on them in the text field marked "Special Needs" (not provided in this template).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address to enable communication with you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your physical address.
* **How many family members are attending?** (`family_members`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the number of family members attending the session or workshop.
* **Do you have any special needs?** (`special_needs`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have any special needs, select the applicable options. You can select multiple options if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Provide the name of an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide the phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, `text`, optional): (This field is not provided in this template, but you can provide any additional information about your special needs in this field if needed.)
