# Virtual Mental Wellness Session Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Virtual Mental Wellness Session Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with virtual mental wellness sessions. This form helps the organization to evaluate the quality and effectiveness of these sessions and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read through the form carefully and ensure you have participated in a virtual mental wellness session.
2.  Choose the answer that best describes your experience with the session.
3.  Fill out the form with the requested information.
4.  Ensure you have answered all required fields correctly.
5.  Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session** (`evaluation_session`, required): This field asks you to rate your overall experience with the session. Choose the answer that best describes how you felt about the session.
	+ Very good: The session was excellent, and I felt well-supported and informed.
	+ Good: The session was satisfactory, and I felt somewhat supported and informed.
	+ Neutral: The session was okay, and I didn't feel particularly supported or informed.
	+ Bad: The session was not satisfactory, and I didn't feel well-supported or informed.
	+ Terrible: The session was very disappointing, and I didn't feel supported or informed.
* **Mental Wellness** (`mental_wellness`, optional): This field is intended for additional feedback about your mental wellness.
* **Mental Wellness Level** (`mental_wellness_level`, optional): This field is a scale from 1 to 5 where 1 means low and 5 means high. Please select the level that best describes your current mental wellness.
* **Mental Wellness Comments** (`mental_wellness_comments`, optional): This field is intended for additional comments about your mental wellness.
* **Followup Session** (`followup_session`, optional): This field is intended to schedule a followup session with a professional.
* **Followup Session Date** (`followup_session_date`, optional): This field is intended for the date of the followup session. 

# Tips

* Please be honest in your answers, as this form is intended to help the organization improve its services.
* Ensure you have participated in a virtual mental wellness session before filling out this form.
* Fill out all required fields correctly.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please include them in the mental wellness comments field.
* If you would like to schedule a followup session, select a date in the followup session date field.
