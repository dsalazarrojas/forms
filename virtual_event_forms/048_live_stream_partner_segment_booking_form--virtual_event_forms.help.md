# Live Stream Partner Segment Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Live Stream Partner Segment Booking Form is used for partners or collaborators to book their live streams in a coordinated effort.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your **Goals** for the live stream.
2. Enter your **Contact Info** for communication and reference purposes.
3. Choose the **Stream Details** that align with your stream's requirements.
4. Select the **Segment Slots** that fit your schedule.
5. Choose the **Booking Details** that suit your stream's needs.
6. Enter the **Date and Time** for your stream's start time.
7. Add any **Additional Info** about your stream, if needed.
8. Select the **Partner Info**, **Streaming Platform**, and **Stream ID** for your stream's setup.
9. Enter the **Segment ID**, **Date**, and **Time** for your stream's timing.
10. Choose the **Timezone** for your stream's time offset.
11. Select the **Duration** for your stream's length.
12. Select the **Stream Status** and **Status** for your stream's status.
13. Enter the **Booking ID**, **Stream URL**, **Stream Type**, and **Stream Format** for your stream's metadata.
14. Select the **Segment Status**, **Language**, and **Segment URL** for your stream's content.
15. Finally, review all your selections before submitting the form.

* **Goals**: Select the objectives or purposes of your live stream.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your name, email, and other relevant contact information for communication and reference purposes. This will help us get in touch with you about your stream.
* **Stream Details**: Choose the specific stream you are booking, such as "Stream 1" or "Stream 2".
* **Segment Slots**: Select the time slots that fit your schedule for your live stream.
* **Booking Details**: Choose the details that suit your stream's needs, such as the number of viewers or technical requirements.
* **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time for your stream's start time.
* **Additional Info**: Add any additional information about your stream, such as the stream's description or tags.
* **Partner Info**: Select the partner or collaborator that will be handling your stream.
* **Streaming Platform**: Choose the platform where your stream will be hosted, such as "Platform 1" or "Platform 2".
* **Stream ID**: Enter a unique identifier for your stream.
* **Segment ID**: Enter a unique identifier for your segment.
* **Date**: Enter the date for your stream's start time.
* **Time**: Enter the time for your stream's start time.
* **Timezone**: Select the time offset for your stream's time.
* **Duration**: Select the length of your stream.
* **Stream Status**: Choose the status of your stream, such as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Status**: Select the status of your segment, such as "Available" or "Not Available".
* **Booking ID**: Enter a unique identifier for your booking.
* **Stream URL**: Enter the URL for your stream.
* **Stream Type**: Choose the type of your stream, such as "Video" or "Audio".
