<Veterinary Data Analytics Webinar Registration - Help Guide>
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a veterinary data analytics webinar. The collected data will be used to tailor the webinar content to the attendees' needs and goals. Please ensure that you fill out this form only once, as duplicate submissions will not be accepted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Click on the "Registration Form" page.
2. Select your role from the "Select your role" field. Please choose the option that best describes your profession or interest in the webinar.
3. If you have multiple roles, select all that apply in the "Multiple roles" field.
4. Enter your goals and objectives in the "What are your goals" field. This will help us understand your interests and tailor the content accordingly.
5. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used to communicate with you about the webinar.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This will be used as a contact number for follow-up questions or updates.
7. Enter your name in the "Name" field. This will be used for registration and identification purposes.
8. Read and accept or decline the "Consent" option. This is a crucial step to ensure that you are aware of and comply with our data protection policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form (1)**: This is the first page of the form where you will input your basic information.
	+ **Type:** `text`
	+ **Required:** No
* **Select your role (2)**: Choose the option that best describes your profession or interest in the webinar.
	+ **Type:** `select_one`
	+ **Required:** No
* **Multiple roles (3)**: If you have multiple roles, select all that apply.
	+ **Type:** `select_multiple`
	+ **Required:** No
* **What are your goals (4)**: Enter your goals and objectives for attending the webinar.
	+ **Type:** `text`
	+ **Required:** No
* **Consent (6)**: Read and accept or decline our data protection policies.
	+ **Type:** `select_one`
	+ **Required:** No
* **Email (8)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
	+ **Type:** `email`
	+ **Required:** No
* **Phone (9)**: Enter your phone number as a contact number.
	+ **Type:** `text`
	+ **Required:** No
* **Name (10)**: Enter your name for registration and identification purposes.
	+ **Type:** `text`
	+ **Required:** No
