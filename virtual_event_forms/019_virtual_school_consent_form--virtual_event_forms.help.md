# Virtual School Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect necessary information from students and their parents/guardians for virtual school purposes. This includes student and parent contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and email address in the fields provided.
2. Enter the parent's email address.
3. If you wish to provide, enter your phone number.
4. Double-check that all information is accurate and complete before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Virtual School Consent Form**: This is the title of the form, and it's just an indicator to help identify what this form is for. You can fill this field in with any information you like.
* **Student Name**: This field is for your name. Please enter your full name.
* **Student Email**: This field is for your email address. Please enter your email address in the format of `username@domain.com`.
* **Parent Email**: This field is for your parent/guardian's email address. If you're an adult, this field can be left blank.
* **Parent Phone**: This field is for your parent/guardian's phone number. If you're an adult, this field can be left blank.

## Tips

* Please make sure to double-check all the information you entered before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or issues while filling out the form, you can contact the virtual school staff for help.
