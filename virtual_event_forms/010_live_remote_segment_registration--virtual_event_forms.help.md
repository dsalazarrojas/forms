# Live Remote Segment Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Live Remote Segment Registration form is designed to collect information from users about their interest in attending or participating in a live event or webinar. This form will help you determine which segments you'd like to register for and your preferred time slot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the segments you're interested in by selecting all that apply from the "Segment Choice" field.
2. Select your preferred time slot from the "Time Slot" field.
3. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
6. Choose your start and end time for the event or webinar in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Segment Choice** (`Segment Choice`, select_multiple, required): Select all the segments you're interested in by choosing multiple options from the dropdown list.
* **Time Slot** (`Time Slot`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred time slot from the single option provided.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you'd like it to be displayed.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number (optional, but recommended for event organizers to contact you).
* **Start Time** (`Start Time`, time, optional): Choose your preferred start time for the event or webinar.
* **End Time** (`End Time`, time, optional): Choose your preferred end time for the event or webinar.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form honestly and accurately to ensure your registration is processed correctly.
* Double-check your segment and time slot choices before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the event organizers for assistance.
