# virtual_product_demo_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The virtual product demo registration form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in participating in a virtual product demo. This form helps to schedule the demo and provide the necessary information to the user.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first and last name in the **First Name** and **Last Name** fields, respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Confirm your email address by entering it again in the **Confirm Email** field.
4. Select a preferred **Available Time** for the demo.
5. Choose a **Product Demo** option from the dropdown list.
6. Select one or more **Schedule Appointment** options.
7. Add any **Notes** about the demo in the text area.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: This section is for entering your name and contact information.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email**: Re-enter your email address to confirm it.
* **Available Time**: Select a time slot for the demo.
* **Date**: Enter the date for the demo.
* **Product Demo**: Choose a product demo option from the list.
* **Schedule Appointment**: Select one or more time slots for the demo.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the demo in this text area.
## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure a smooth demo experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
