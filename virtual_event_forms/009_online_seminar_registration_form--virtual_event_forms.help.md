# online_seminar_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees of an online seminar. It helps us understand who is attending, what they want to learn, and how they plan to pay for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "User Information" section with your details as requested.
2. Provide information about the seminar you are interested in in the "Seminar Information" section.
3. Enter your registration details in the "Registration Information" section.
4. If you are making a payment, fill in the "Payment Information" section with your payment details.
5. If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your name, email address, and any other details as requested. This section is for your personal information.
* **Seminar Information** (`seminar_info`, `text`, optional): Describe the seminar you are interested in attending. Please provide the seminar title, date, and any other relevant details.
* **Registration Information** (`registration_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any registration details, such as your attendee ID or any specific requirements you have.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, optional): If you are paying for the seminar, enter your payment method, amount, and any other payment details as requested.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have about the seminar.
