# Town Hall Attendance Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about attendees' intentions to attend the event, ensuring that the event organizer has accurate and up-to-date information for planning and organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the "Are you sure to attend" question by selecting "True" if you are certain about your attendance, or "False" if you are not sure.
2. Enter your name in the text field provided.
3. Provide your contact information (email address or phone number) in the text field.
4. Select a date you are available to attend using the date picker.
5. Choose a preferred time of day using the time picker.
6. If you have any special requests or requirements, select the relevant option.
7. On a scale of 1-10, indicate your level of commitment to attending the event.
8. If you have any additional comments or information, type them in the text field.
9. Double-check your information by selecting the correct option for "Are you sure this is correct information?"

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Are you sure to attend** (`Are you sure to attend`, select_one, required): This question confirms whether you are certain about your attendance or not. Please select "True" if you are sure, or "False" if you are not sure.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Please provide your name so that we can identify you correctly.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required): Enter your email address or phone number so that we can get in touch with you with any updates or confirmations.
* **What date are you available to attend?** (`What date are you available to attend?`, date, required): Choose a date that you are available to attend the event using the date picker.
* **What time of day would you prefer to attend?** (`What time of day would you prefer to attend?`, time, required): Select a preferred time of day for your attendance using the time picker.
* **Special Requests** (`Special Requests`, checkbox, optional): If you have any special requirements or requests, please select one or more of the options provided.
* **Commitment Level** (`Commitment Level`, number, required): Indicate your level of commitment to attending the event on a scale of 1-10.
* **Additional Details** (`Additional Details`, text, optional): If you have any other comments or information you'd like to share, please type them in this field.
* **Are you sure this is correct information?** (`Are you sure this is correct information?`, checkbox, required): Double-check your information by selecting the correct option to confirm its accuracy.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and accurately to ensure that we have the best possible information for planning and organization.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form! Your input is crucial for making this event a success.
