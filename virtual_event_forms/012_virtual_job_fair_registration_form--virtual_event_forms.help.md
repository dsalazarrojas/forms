# Virtual Job Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to assist you in filling out the Virtual Job Fair Registration Form accurately and effectively. Please follow the instructions below to complete the form successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Virtual Job Fair Registration Form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is a required field, so please make sure to fill it out accurately.
2. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This field is also required and must be a valid email address.
3. If applicable, enter your **Job Title** in the "Job Title" field. Although this field is optional, it's helpful for recruiters to know your current or desired job title.
4. Enter your **Company** name in the "Company" field, if you have one. This field is optional but can be useful for networking purposes.
5. Write a brief description of your **Job Description** in the "Job Description" field. This field is optional but can help event organizers understand your job goals and expectations.
6. Select your preferred **Job Type** from the options provided in the "Job Type" field. This field is optional but will help event organizers understand your job preferences.
7. Enter your **Location**, if you have one, in the "Location" field. This field is optional but can be useful for recruiters to know your location.
8. Attach your **Resume** in the "Resume" field, if you have one. This field is optional but is recommended for a complete application.
9. Select the time slot you prefer for the **Job Fair Date** and **Time Slot** fields. These fields are optional but will help event organizers arrange the time slots accordingly.
10. Enter your preferred **Job Fair Time** in the "Job Fair Time" field, if you have one. This field is optional.
11. Enter the **Job Fair Location**, if you have one, in the "Job Fair Location" field. This field is optional.
12. Select your **Registration Status** from the options provided in the "Registration Status" field. This field is optional but will help event organizers track your status.
13. Enter the name of your **Company Representative**, if applicable, in the "Company Rep" field. This field is optional.
14. Enter your **Contact Number**, if you have one, in the "Contact Number" field. This field is optional.
15. Enter the name of the **Event Manager**, if applicable, in the "Event Manager" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to which you can be contacted about the event.
* **Job Title**: If you have an official job title, enter it here (e.g., Software Engineer).
* **Company**: If you have a company, enter its name here.
* **Job Description**: Briefly describe your job preferences and expectations from the event.
* **Job Type**: Select a job type that best describes your current or desired role (e.g., Software Engineer, Data Scientist, Product Manager).
* **Location**: Enter your current location or the location you are willing to work from.
* **Resume**: Attach a link to or upload your resume for event organizers to review.
* **Job Fair Date**: Select the date of the virtual job fair you wish to attend.
* **Time Slot**: Choose a time slot that suits your schedule.
* **Job Fair Time**: Enter the time of the job fair you wish to attend.
* **Job Fair Location**: Enter the location of the virtual job fair you wish to attend.
* **Registration Status**: Select your current status (Registered, Pending, Cancelled).
* **Company Rep**: If you represent a company, enter the name of your company representative.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number, if you have one.
* **Event Manager**: If you are an event manager, enter your name here.
