# Virtual Job Fair Employer Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to register as an employer for a virtual job fair. This form allows you to provide your company's information and job details to be showcased in the virtual job fair. Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about your company and job posting.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure the information provided is correct.
3. Submit the form once you have filled in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Registration**: This field is a text field where you can enter a brief description of your company or a short message to potential applicants.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the job posting or the contact person for your company.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person for your company.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person for your company.
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job posting.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of the job posting.
* **Job Type**: Enter the type of job (full-time, part-time, internship, etc.).
* **Job Location**: Enter the location where the job will be posted (city, state, etc.).
* **Job Start Date**: Enter the start date for the job posting.
* **Job End Date**: Enter the end date for the job posting.
* **Job Duration**: Enter the expected duration of the job posting.
* **Job Status**: Enter the status of the job posting (available, unavailable, etc.).
* **Job Category**: Enter the category of the job posting (engineering, sales, marketing, etc.).
* **Job Posted Date**: Enter the date the job was posted.
* **Job Posted Time**: Enter the time the job was posted.

## Tips

* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or need assistance, please contact your support team.
