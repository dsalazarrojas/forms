# Event Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Event Planning Survey form. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details about upcoming events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information about your event.
2. Make sure to review your input carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Planning Survey** (1, text, required/optional): This is the main title of the form. Please enter a brief description of your event.
* **Event Title** (2, text, required/optional): Enter the actual title of your event, e.g., "Company Meeting" or "Product Launch Event".
* **Event Start Date** (3, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your event (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Event End Date** (4, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your event (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Event Location** (5, text, required/optional): Enter the physical location of your event, e.g., "Conference Room" or "Online Platform".
* **Event Time** (6, time, required/optional): Enter the start time of your event in 12-hour format, e.g., "9:00 AM" or "3:00 PM".
* **Event Description** (7, text, required/optional): Briefly describe the purpose and details of your event.
* **Event Capacity** (8, number, required/optional): Enter the maximum number of attendees you expect for your event.
* **Event Organizer** (9, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person or organization hosting your event.
* **Event Sponsor** (10, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the name of the sponsor for your event.
* **Event Participants** (11, select multiple, required/optional): Choose whether your event is open to participants or not. Select "Yes" if your event is open to attendees, and "No" otherwise.
* **Contact Email** (12, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person to contact for event-related inquiries.
* **Contact Phone** (13, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person to contact for event-related inquiries.
* **Notes** (14, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your event.

Remember, this form is for planning purposes only. Please review the information carefully before submitting.
