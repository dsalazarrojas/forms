# Mechanical Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Mechanical Evaluation Form is used to gather information about a machine's mechanical condition and maintenance status. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your User Name in the "User Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter any relevant notes about the machine in the "Note" field (optional).
5. Enter the date and time of the evaluation in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
6. Enter your User ID in the "User ID" field.
7. Select the machine type in the "Machine Type" field.
8. Select the machine model in the "Machine Model" field.
9. Select the maintenance type in the "Maintenance Type" field.
10. Select the maintenance frequency in the "Maintenance Frequency" field.
11. Select the repair status in the "Repair Status" field.
12. Select the repair type in the "Repair Type" field.
13. Enter any comments about the machine's condition in the "Comments" field.
14. Enter the name of the mechanic performing the evaluation (if applicable) in the "Mechanic Name" field.
15. Enter the mechanic's ID (if applicable) in the "Mechanic ID" field.
16. Enter the mechanic's email (if applicable) in the "Mechanic Email" field.
17. Enter the mechanic's phone number (if applicable) in the "Mechanic Phone" field.
18. Enter the location of the machine in the "Machine Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **User Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the machine's condition.
- **Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Time**: Enter the time of the evaluation in the format `HH:MM`.
- **User ID**: Enter your unique identification number.
- **Machine Type**: Select the type of machine being evaluated (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
- **Machine Model**: Select the model of the machine being evaluated (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
- **Maintenance Type**: Select the type of maintenance required for the machine (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
- **Maintenance Frequency**: Select the frequency of maintenance required for the machine (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
- **Repair Status**: Select the status of the machine's repair (e.g., 'Active' or 'Inactive').
- **Repair Type**: Select the type of repair required for the machine (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
- **Comments**: Enter any comments about the machine's condition.
- **Mechanic Name**: Enter the name of the mechanic performing the evaluation (optional).
- **Mechanic ID**: Enter the ID of the mechanic performing the evaluation (optional).
- **Mechanic Email**: Enter the email of the mechanic performing the evaluation (optional).
- **Mechanic Phone**: Enter the phone number of the mechanic performing the evaluation (optional).
- **Machine Location**: Enter the location of the machine being evaluated.
