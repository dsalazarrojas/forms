# self_driving_car_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user feedback and information about self-driving cars.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read the form carefully and fill in all required fields.
2. On the first page, type your introduction or context about yourself in the text field.
3. On the second page, type your experience with self-driving cars in the text field.
4. On the third page, select your level of experience with self-driving cars from the options provided.
5. On the fourth page, select all the expectations you have about self-driving cars.
6. On the fifth page, type any additional feedback you have about your experience with self-driving cars.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **intro** (note, required: false): A field where you can write any introduction or context about yourself.
* **what_is_sd** (text, required: false): Describe your experience with self-driving cars.
* **driving_experience** (select_one, required: false): Choose your level of experience with self-driving cars.
	+ Option 1: None
	+ Option 2: Some experience
	+ Option 3: Extensive experience
* **driving_expectations** (select_multiple, required: false): Select all the expectations you have about self-driving cars.
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **feedback** (text, required: false): Type any additional feedback you have about your experience with self-driving cars.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your feedback is complete.
* Be as detailed as possible when answering the questions to provide valuable information.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact us.
