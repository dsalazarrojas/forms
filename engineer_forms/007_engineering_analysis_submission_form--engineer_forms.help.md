# Engineering Analysis Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Engineering Analysis Submission Form is a crucial document that enables submitters to provide essential information about their engineering analysis submissions. This form serves as a standardized template for collecting required details, which helps in streamlining the submission process and ensures that all necessary information is accurately recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the submittor's personal information, including their name, contact details, and job title.
2. Provide details about the document uploads, if any.
3. Describe the review context and any relevant details for the reviewers.
4. Specify the analysis package details.
5. Identify the project stakeholders.
6. Enter the submission date and time.
7. Confirm the engineering team members involved.
8. (Optional) Provide comments and any additional information.
9. Upload any relevant submission files.
10. Add any additional details about the submitter's company and position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Information (required)**: Provide a brief introduction about yourself and your role in the submission.
* **Document Uploads (required)**: Attach any relevant documents or files that support your analysis submission.
* **Review Context (optional)**: Describe any relevant details or context that will help the reviewers understand your submission.
* **Analysis Package (required)**: Enter a clear and concise description of your analysis package.
* **Project Stakeholders (required)**: List all individuals or groups involved in the project.
* **Submission Date (required)**: Enter the date and time of your submission.
* **Engineering Team Members (required)**: Confirm the names and roles of the engineering team members involved.
* **Consultants (optional)**: If any external consultants were involved, list their names and roles.
* **Submitter Comments (optional)**: Add any additional comments or information about your submission.
* **Engineering Analysis Submission (optional)**: Provide a brief description of your analysis submission.
* **Submitter Email (required)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Submitter Phone (required)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submitter Address (optional)**: Enter your work address or any other relevant contact information.
* **Submission Files (required)**: Upload any relevant files or documents that support your submission.
* **Submission Date-Time (required)**: Enter the date and time of your submission.
* **Submitter Signature (optional)**: Add your handwritten or digital signature to confirm your submission.
* **Submitter Job Number (optional)**: Enter the job number related to your submission.
* **Project Number (optional)**: Enter the project number for reference.
* **Submitter Client Name (optional)**: Enter the name of your client or sponsor.
* **Submitter Job Title (optional)**: Enter your job title for reference.
* **Submitter Company (optional)**: Enter your company name for reference.
* **Submitter Position (optional)**: Enter your position in the company for reference.
* **Submitter Department (optional)**: Enter your department for reference.
* **Submitter Date (optional)**: Enter the date of your submission.

Note: The optional fields are not required but are still important for additional context and information. Please fill in all required fields accurately and completely to ensure a smooth submission process.
