# geotechnical_knowledge_assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your geotechnical experience, education, skills, and other relevant details. This information will be used for assessment and certification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you answer all questions truthfully.
2. Enter your information in the corresponding fields.
3. If a field is marked as optional, you may leave it blank if you do not have relevant information.
4. Use the provided options for select fields, and enter your information in the correct format.
5. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **geotechnical_experience** (id: 1, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your geotechnical experience.
* **education_background** (id: 2, text, optional): Mention any relevant education background related to geotechnical field.
* **years_of_experience** (id: 3, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been working in the geotechnical field.
* **geotechnical_skills** (id: 4, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant geotechnical skills you have acquired.
* **technical_skills** (id: 5, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant technical skills you possess.
* **geotechnical_certifications** (id: 6, select_one, optional): Select any relevant geotechnical certifications you have.
* **geotechnical_tools** (id: 7, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant geotechnical tools you are familiar with.
* **geotechnical_languages** (id: 8, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant geotechnical languages you are proficient in.
* **geotechnical_certifications_2** (id: 9, select_one, optional): Select any additional geotechnical certifications you have.
* **geotechnical_certifications_3** (id: 10, select_one, optional): Select any additional geotechnical certifications you have.
* **date_of_birth** (id: 11, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **time_of_birth** (id: 12, time, optional): Enter your time of birth in 24-hour format.
* **email** (id: 13, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (id: 14, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **note** (id: 15, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **geotechnical_degree** (id: 16, select_multiple, optional): Select your relevant degree(s).
* **geotechnical_institution** (id: 17, select_multiple, optional): Select the relevant institution(s) you have studied at.
* **geotechnical_area_of_interest** (id: 18, select_multiple, optional): Select your areas of interest in geotechnical field.
* **geotechnical_achievement** (id: 19, select_multiple, optional): Select your relevant achievement(s) in geotechnical field.
* **geotechnical_contribution** (id: 20, text, optional): Enter your relevant contribution(s) to the geotechnical field.
* **geotechnical_recommendation** (id: 21, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant recommendation(s) you have.
* **geotechnical_reference** (id: 22, text, optional): Enter any relevant reference(s).
* **geotechnical_additional_information** (id: 23, text, optional): Enter any additional information you would like to share.
* **geotechnical_language_proficiency** (id: 24, text, optional): Enter your language proficiency level.
* **geotechnical_additional_comments** (id: 25, text, optional): Enter any additional comments you would like to make.
