# Renewable Energy Solar Engineer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Renewable Energy Solar Engineer Interview Form is designed to collect relevant information from solar energy system engineers for further review and analysis. This information is crucial for the development and improvement of solar energy systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Start by filling out the candidate details section, which includes the engineer's name, title, and contact information.
2.  In the experience section, provide a detailed account of your relevant experience in the field, including the number of projects you have worked on, your role in those projects, and any notable achievements or contributions.
3.  The project background section is where you will provide an overview of the projects you have worked on that are relevant to this form.
4.  If you have any additional information that you feel is important to share, you can provide it in the additional info section.
5.  If an assigned tool is required, you will select the tool that you used for this project.
6.  The output file section is used to upload a file that demonstrates your work, and it is optional.
7.  Finally, the category section is used to select the category of the project, which will help in organizing the data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, `text`, required): Provide your name, title, and contact information, as this will be the first point of contact with our team.
*   **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, required): Give a detailed account of your relevant experience in the field, including the number of projects you have worked on, your role, and any notable achievements or contributions.
*   **Project Background** (`project_background`, `text`, required): Provide an overview of the projects you have worked on that are relevant to this form.
*   **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional information that you feel is important to share, you can provide it here.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): Select the tool that you used for this project.
*   **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, optional): Upload a file that demonstrates your work, and it is optional.
*   **Category** (`category`, `text`, optional): Select the category of the project, which will help in organizing the data.
*   **Project Experience** (`project_experience`, `number`, optional): This field is not used in this form, and you can ignore it.
