# Manhole Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The manhole inspection form is designed to collect data on the condition, location, and other details of manholes inspected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the date of the inspection in the `date` field.
2. Enter the time of the inspection in the `time` field.
3. Enter the location of the manhole in the `location` field.
4. Enter the name of the inspector who performed the inspection in the `inspector` field.
5. Describe the condition of the manhole in the `condition` field.
6. Provide any comments or notes about the inspection in the `comments` field.
7. Attach any relevant images of the manhole in the `image` field.
8. Add any additional notes or remarks in the `notes` field.
9. If applicable, select the user responsible for the inspection from the `assigned_user` field.
10. Enter the user who created the form in the `created_by` field (this field is not applicable for user input and will be filled automatically).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of the inspection. The date should be in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time** (time, required/optional): Enter the time of the inspection. The time should be in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Location** (text, required/optional): Enter the location of the manhole.
* **Inspector** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the inspector who performed the inspection.
* **Condition** (text, required/optional): Describe the condition of the manhole.
* **Comments** (text, required/optional): Provide any comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Image** (text, required/optional): Attach any relevant images of the manhole.
* **Notes** (text, required/optional): Add any additional notes or remarks about the inspection.
* **Assigned User** (text, required/optional): If applicable, select the user responsible for the inspection.
* **Created By** (text, required/optional): This field will be filled automatically and is not applicable for user input.

# Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information about the manhole inspection.
* If an image is attached, ensure it is a clear and relevant picture of the manhole.
* If the manhole is in a critical or hazardous condition, indicate this in the `condition` field.
* If the inspection is part of a recurring or regular schedule, consider selecting the `assigned_user` field to track the responsible user.
