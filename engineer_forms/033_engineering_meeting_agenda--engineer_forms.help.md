# Engineering Meeting Agenda - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Engineering Meeting Agenda form is used to collect information about upcoming engineering meetings, including the meeting title, participants, agenda topics, action items, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the meeting title in the `Agenda Title` field.
2. List the participants in the `Participants List` field by selecting from the provided options or adding new participants as needed.
3. Select the agenda topics to be discussed from the `Agenda Topics` field.
4. Enter any action items that need to be addressed in the `Action Items` field.
5. Add any meeting notes in the `Meeting Notes` field.
6. Enter the meeting date in the `Meeting Date` field in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
7. Enter the meeting time in the `Meeting Time` field in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
8. Select the meeting location from the `Meeting Location` field.
9. Enter the assigned tool for the meeting in the `Assigned Tool` field.
10. Enter the assigned user responsible for the meeting in the `Assigned User` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agenda Title** (`title`, Required): Enter the title of the meeting, a concise and descriptive title that summarizes the meeting's purpose and objectives.
* **Participants List** (`participants`, Optional): List the participants in the meeting, selecting from the provided options or adding new participants as needed.
* **Agenda Topics** (`agenda_topics`, Optional): Select the agenda topics to be discussed during the meeting.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, Optional): Enter any action items that need to be addressed.
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`, Optional): Add any meeting notes or additional information that may be relevant.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, Optional): Enter the date of the meeting in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, Optional): Enter the time of the meeting in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Meeting Location** (`location`, Optional): Select the meeting location, either online or in-person.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, Optional): Enter the tool assigned for the meeting.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, Optional): Enter the user responsible for the meeting.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all required fields, but also take advantage of optional fields to provide more detailed information about the meeting.
* Use the provided options for participants and meeting location to keep the form concise and easy to complete.
* Proofread your form carefully to ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
