# Feedback Voltage Amplifier Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of a voltage amplifier, taking into account its input voltage rating, frequency rating, sensitivity, noise level, power consumption, temperature, and humidity. The input voltage rating assesses how the amplifier handles input voltage levels, while the frequency rating indicates its compatibility with different frequency ranges. Sensitivity evaluation helps determine how well the amplifier can operate at its intended operating temperature. Noise level evaluation helps understand unwanted noise or electrical interference. Power consumption is crucial as it affects energy efficiency. Temperature and humidity ratings assess the device's tolerance to environmental conditions. Optional fields allow users to provide their vote on the device's performance and remarks or comments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you understand the questions asked.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the voltage amplifier you are evaluating.
3. If you are unsure about any question, consult the user guide or contact the manufacturer.
4. Provide your vote on the device's performance in the "input_voltage_rating" field.
5. Optionally, provide remarks or comments about the device's performance in the "input_voltage_remark" field.
6. Submit the form to complete the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **input_voltage_rating** (`1`, `select_one`, required): This field asks for your rating (from 1 to 5) of the device's input voltage performance. Please select the rating based on your experience with the device.
* **input_frequency_rating** (`1`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is for selecting the frequency ratings of the device (from 1 to 5). You can select all applicable frequencies for the device.
* **input_voltage_sensitivity** (`number`, required): This field asks for the sensitivity (in dB) of the voltage amplifier.
* **input_voltage_noise** (`number`, required): This field asks for the noise level (in dB) of the voltage amplifier.
* **input_voltage_power** (`number`, required): This field asks for the power consumption (in watt) of the voltage amplifier.
* **input_voltage_temperature** (`1`, `select_one`, optional): This field is for selecting the temperature rating of the device (from 1 to 5 or Other if not listed).
* **input_voltage_humidity** (`1`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is for selecting the humidity ratings of the device (from 1 to 5 or Other if not listed).
* **input_voltage_remark** (`note`, optional): This field is for providing any remarks or comments about the device's performance.
* **input_v_votes_name** (`text`, optional): This field is for entering your name or alias for your vote.
* **input_v_voltage_amp_name** (`text`, optional): This field is for entering the name of the voltage amplifier being evaluated.
* **minimalist** (`1`, `select_one`, optional): This field is for selecting the design style of the device (from 1 to 5 or Other if not listed).
