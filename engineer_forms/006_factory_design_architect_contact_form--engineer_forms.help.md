# Factory Design Architect Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Design Architect Contact Form is designed to collect project-specific information from potential or current clients for factory design projects, such as inquiring about custom-built factories or expansions.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure that you successfully fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide project details, including any relevant information about the project you're inquiring about or collaborating on.
2. Enter your contact information so that the factory design architects can get in touch with you.
3. Upload any relevant files related to the project.
4. Ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date, as it will be used as the primary contact method.
5. Enter your phone number for further communication and clarification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Details (Project Details)** (`project_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide a brief description of the project you're inquiring about or collaborating on. Please provide any relevant information that will help the factory design architects understand your requirements.
- **Contact Info (Contact Info)** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide your contact information so that the factory design architects can get in touch with you. Please ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
- **File Uploads (File Uploads)** (`file_uploads`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to upload any relevant files related to the project. Please upload any files that will help the factory design architects understand your project requirements.
- **Email (Email)** (`email`, `email`, required: true): This field is used to provide a valid email address that the factory design architects can use for further communication and clarification. Please ensure that this field is accurate and up-to-date.
- **Phone Number (Phone Number)** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide your phone number for further communication and clarification purposes. Please ensure that your phone number is accurate and up-to-date.

## Tips
- Ensure that your email address and phone number are accurate and up-to-date to facilitate smooth communication.
- Upload any relevant files that will help the factory design architects understand your project requirements.
- Be concise and clear when providing project details to help the factory design architects understand your needs.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, do not hesitate to contact the factory design architects using the provided email address.
