# structural_engineer_review_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The structural engineer review application form is used to collect and evaluate information about a structural engineering project, gathering details from various stakeholders to facilitate a comprehensive review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the project details, including the project address and documents required for the review.
2. Select the structural design option that best describes the project's design.
3. Choose the building condition that accurately represents the project's state.
4. Enter the contact information for the property owner, including their email and phone number.
5. Enter the contact email and phone number of the reviewer.
6. Provide a review date and time for the project.
7. Select the review status that best represents the current state of the project.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes about the project.
9. Fill in the reviewer's contact information, including their name, email, phone number, company, and role.
10. Enter the project manager's contact information, including their name, email, phone number, and company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details**: A text field for entering project details. Please provide a brief description of the project.
* **Project Documents**: A text field for uploading or describing project documents. Please attach or describe any relevant documents for the review process.
* **Structural Design**: A dropdown menu with three options for selecting the project's structural design.
* **Building Condition**: A dropdown menu with three options for selecting the project's building condition.
* **Building Type**: A dropdown menu with three options for selecting the project's building type.
* **Project Address**: A text field for entering the project's address.
* **Property Owner**: A text field for entering the property owner's name and contact information.
* **Contact Email**: A text field for entering the reviewer's contact email.
* **Contact Phone**: A text field for entering the reviewer's phone number.
* **Additional Comments**: A text field for any additional comments or notes about the project.
* **Review Date**: A date field for setting the review date.
* **Review Time**: A time field for setting the review time.
* **Review Status**: A dropdown menu with three options for selecting the review status.
* **Reviewer Name**: A text field for entering the reviewer's name.
* **Reviewer Email**: A text field for entering the reviewer's email.
* **Reviewer Phone**: A text field for entering the reviewer's phone number.
* **Reviewer Company**: A text field for entering the reviewer's company.
* **Reviewer Role**: A text field for entering the reviewer's role.
* **Project Manager Name**: A text field for entering the project manager's name.
* **Project Manager Email**: A text field for entering the project manager's email.
* **Project Manager Phone**: A text field for entering the project manager's phone number.
* **Project Manager Company**: A text field for entering the project manager's company.
* **Structural Engineer Designer**: A text field for entering the structural engineer's name and company.
* **Structural Engineer Supervisor**: A text field for entering the structural engineer supervisor's name and company.

## Tips
* Ensure you complete the form accurately and thoroughly, including all required fields.
* Use the provided fields to provide as much detail as possible about the project.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, contact your structural engineer review coordinator.
