# Validation Engineer Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for the Validation Engineer position. It is used by the hiring team to review and assess candidate applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. If you have a professional email address, enter it in the **Email** field. (Note: This field is not required, but it's recommended for future communication.)
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field. (Note: This field is not required, but it's recommended for contact purposes.)
5. Enter your address in the **Address** field. (Note: This field is not required.)
6. Attach your resume in the **Resume** field.
7. Enter your current job title in the **Job Title** field.
8. If you have previous experience, enter the number of years you've worked as a Validation Engineer in the **Years of Experience** field.
9. If applicable, select the date you are available to start work in the **Available Start Date** field.
10. If you are available to move, select the relevant option (True/False/Maybe) in the **Available to Move** field.
11. If applicable, enter the desired salary range in the **Desired Salary** field.
12. If applicable, enter the notice period you expect to provide to your current employer in the **Notice Period** field.
13. If applicable, enter the date you expect to be available to end your current contract in the **Available End Date** field.
14. If applicable, provide a brief reason for leaving your current job in the **Reason for Leaving** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your professional email address for future communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your home or work address.
* **Resume** (`resume`, note, optional): Attach your up-to-date resume as a PDF or Word document.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you've worked as a Validation Engineer.
* **Available to Move** (`available_to_move`, select_multiple, optional): Select True, False, or Maybe, depending on your availability to move for the job location.
* **Desired Salary** (`desired_salary`, number, optional): Enter your desired salary range (e.g., $60,000 - $80,000).
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, date, optional): Select the date you are available to start work.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, number, optional): Enter the number of days or weeks you are required to give notice to your current employer.
* **Available End Date** (`available_end_date`, date, optional): Select the date you expect to be available to end your current contract.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`available_reason`, text, optional): Provide a brief reason for leaving your current job.
