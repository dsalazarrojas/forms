# Field Commissioning Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Commissioning Report Form is a tool for collecting data on field equipment commissioning activities. It is used to gather information about a specific piece of equipment, including its name, tag ID, measurement point, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Equipment Name** of the piece of equipment being commissioned in the "Equipment Name" field.
2.  Enter the **Equipment Tag ID** in the "Equipment Tag ID" field.
3.  Specify the **Measurement Point** where the measurement was taken in the "Measurement Point" field.
4.  Enter the date of the measurement in the "Measurement Date" field.
5.  Enter the time of the measurement in the "Measurement Time" field.
6.  Enter the **Measurement Value** obtained in the "Measurement Value" field.
7.  Select the **Measurement Unit** used in the "Measurement Unit" field.
8.  Enter any additional **Notes** about the measurement or commissioning process in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the equipment being commissioned.
*   **Equipment Tag ID** (`equipment_tag_id`, `text`, required): Enter the unique identifier for the equipment tag.
*   **Measurement Point** (`measurement_point`, `text`, required): Specify the location or point where the measurement was taken.
*   **Measurement Date** (`measurement_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the measurement.
*   **Measurement Time** (`measurement_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the measurement.
*   **Measurement Value** (`measurement_value`, `number`, required): Enter the measured value.
*   **Measurement Unit** (`measurement_unit`, `text`, required): Select the unit used for the measurement.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any comments or additional information about the measurement or commissioning process.

## Tips
*   Make sure to double-check the equipment name, tag ID, and measurement point for accuracy.
*   Ensure that the date and time of the measurement are correct.
*   Enter the correct measurement value and unit.
*   Keep the "Notes" field concise and focused on the commissioning process, measurement, or any issues encountered.
*   If any of the fields appear to be incomplete or invalid, review the form for errors before submitting it.
