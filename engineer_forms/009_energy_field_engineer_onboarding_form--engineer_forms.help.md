# Energy Field Engineer Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Field Engineer Onboarding Form is a comprehensive guide to help new hires get familiar with the company's safety procedures, onboarding process, and other essential information to ensure a smooth transition to their new role.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, please read through the form carefully and answer each section accordingly. The form is divided into several sections, each with a specific purpose. Please follow these steps:

*   Begin by filling out the New Hire Details section, providing your basic contact information and any other relevant details.
*   In the Safety Acknowledgments section, select the correct acknowledgments that apply to you.
*   The Onboarding Information section provides additional guidance on the onboarding process.
*   In the Emergency Contact section, please provide information about your emergency contacts.
*   The Contact Info section is for your primary contact details.
*   For Emergency Contact Information, please fill in your emergency contacts' information.
*   In the New Hire Form, provide any additional information that may be required by your supervisor or HR department.
*   Finally, fill out the Job Details section with any relevant job-specific information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **New Hire Details** (`new_hire_details`, text, required/optional): Please provide your basic contact information and any other relevant details about yourself.
*   **Safety Acknowledgments** (`safety_acknowledgments`, select_multiple, optional): Select the safety acknowledgments that apply to you. These are important for your safety and well-being in the workplace.
*   **Onboarding Information** (`onboarding_info`, text, optional): This section provides additional guidance on the onboarding process. Please read through it carefully.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Please provide information about your emergency contacts.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Fill in your primary contact details.
*   **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Fill in your emergency contacts' information.
*   **New Hire Form** (`new_hire_form`, text, optional): Please provide any additional information that may be required by your supervisor or HR department.
*   **Job Details** (`job_details`, text, optional): Fill out this section with any relevant job-specific information.
