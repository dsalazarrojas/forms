# Solar Panel Integration Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in integrating solar panels into their homes or properties. The form aims to gather essential details about the users, their properties, and their energy usage to provide a comprehensive assessment and potential recommendations for solar panel integration.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address in the "User Input" section.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
3. Enter your street address in the "Street" field.
4. Enter your locality in the "Locality" field.
5. Enter your postal code in the "Postal Code" field.
6. Provide information about your property in the "Property Details" field.
7. Describe your current energy usage in the "Energy Usage" field.
8. Enter details about the solar panels you have or plan to install in the "Solar Panel Details" field.
9. Set the date for the assessment in the "Assessment Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (Text, Optional): 
	+ Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **User Email** (Email, Optional): 
	+ Enter your email address to contact you regarding the assessment.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Optional): 
	+ Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Street** (Text, Optional): 
	+ Enter your street address.
* **Locality** (Text, Optional): 
	+ Enter your city or town where the property is located.
* **Postal Code** (Text, Optional): 
	+ Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **Property Details** (Text, Optional): 
	+ Provide a brief description of your property, including its size, shape, and any relevant features.
* **Energy Usage** (Text, Optional): 
	+ Describe your current energy usage, including the number of appliances, lighting, and devices you use.
* **Solar Panel Details** (Text, Optional): 
	+ Enter details about the solar panels you have or plan to install, including their size, type, and model.
* **Assessment Date** (Date, Optional): 
	+ Set the date for the assessment, which will help us determine the best course of action for your solar panel integration.

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when providing your details.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* This form will be used to provide a comprehensive assessment and potential recommendations for your solar panel integration.
