# Factory Floor Engineering Team Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Floor Engineering Team Contact Form is a tool to collect information from team members for request or issue resolution purposes. It helps the team to provide timely and efficient support for problems or requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the type of request or issue you are facing by choosing "Request" or "Issue" from the dropdown menu.
2. Provide a brief description of the problem or request.
3. Enter your name and contact information (phone number and email).
4. Choose a category for your request or issue (e.g., Electrical, Hydraulic, Pneumatic, or Steam).
5. Indicate the severity and priority of your request or issue (e.g., Low, Medium, or High).
6. Optionally, you can attach files or images to support your request or issue.
7. Once you have filled out all the necessary information, click the submit button to send your request or issue to the Factory Floor Engineering Team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Type**: Choose whether you are making a request (for a new feature or service) or reporting an issue (e.g., a problem or malfunction). 
* **Problem Statement**: Describe the problem or issue you are facing. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your full name or the name of the person responsible for contacting us.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number or the phone number of the person responsible for contacting us.
* **Email**: Enter your email address or the email address of the person responsible for contacting us.
* **Category**: Choose the relevant category for your request or issue (e.g., Electrical, Hydraulic, Pneumatic, or Steam).
* **Severity**: Indicate the severity level of your request or issue (e.g., Low, Medium, or High).
* **Priority**: Indicate the priority level of your request or issue (e.g., Low, Medium, or High).
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of your request or issue.
* **Attachments**: Attach files or images to support your request or issue, if necessary.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and clear information to help the team better understand your request or issue.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* The team will review your request or issue and get back to you as soon as possible.
