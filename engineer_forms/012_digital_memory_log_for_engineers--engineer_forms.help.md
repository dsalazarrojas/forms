# Digital Memory Log For Engineers - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a digital memory log for engineers to record and track significant events or incidents that need to be documented for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all the fields that are marked as required.
- Enter your First Name as it should be known to your colleagues and team leads.
- Enter your Email for contact purposes.
- In "Learning Notes," provide a brief description of what you learned or accomplished during the experience.
- In "Incident Notes," describe any incidents that occurred during the experience.
- Enter the Date and Time of the event.
- Specify the Location where the event took place.
- Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if this experience was managed by a team lead.
- Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you were assigned to this experience.
- In "Notes For Manager," add any additional comments or updates for your manager to review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it is known to your team and colleagues.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address for easy communication.
- **Learning Notes** (`learning_notes`, text, required): Describe what you learned or accomplished during the experience.
- **Incident Notes** (`incident_notes`, text, required): Report any incidents or issues that occurred during the experience.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Record the date of the event.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required): Record the time of the event.
- **Location** (`location`, text, required): Specify the location where the event took place.
- **Team Leads** (`team_leads`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if this experience was managed by a team lead. Select "Yes" if true.
- **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you were assigned to this experience. Select "Yes" if true.
- **Notes For Manager** (`notes_for_manager`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or updates for your manager to review.
