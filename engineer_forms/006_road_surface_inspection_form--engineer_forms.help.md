# road_surface_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for inspecting and documenting the condition of road surfaces. The collected data will be used for maintenance and inspection planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all necessary information and equipment before starting the inspection.
* Answer each field accurately and completely based on your observations.
* Take your time to review and verify the information before submitting the form.
* If you need to add any additional notes or comments, use the "Road Surface Condition Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Road Surface Condition** (`road_surface_condition`): Describe the current condition of the road surface.
* **Road Surface Type** (`road_surface_type`): Indicate if the road surface is:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Road Surface Material** (`road_surface_material`): Select all relevant types of material:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Road Surface Length** (`road_surface_length`): Enter the length of the road surface in meters.
* **Road Surface Width** (`road_surface_width`): Enter the width of the road surface in meters.
* **Road Surface Depth** (`road_surface_depth`): Enter the depth of the road surface in meters.
* **Road Surface Condition Notes** (`road_surface_condition_notes`): Add any additional notes or comments about the road surface condition.
* **Road Surface Inspector Signature** (`road_surface_inspector_signature`): Sign and date the form to confirm you've completed it.
* **Road Surface Inspection Date** (`road_surface_inspection_date`): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Road Surface Inspection Time** (`road_surface_inspection_time`): Enter the time of the inspection.

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough when completing each field.
* If unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or team lead for guidance.
* Double-check your work before submitting the form.
* Keep the form for your records and reference purposes.
