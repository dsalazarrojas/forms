# Bracket Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a bracket's condition and provide a comprehensive evaluation of its overall condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) as the label indicates they are required.
* For date fields, select the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* For time fields, select the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* For select_one fields, choose the corresponding option that best describes the condition or state of the bracket.
* For text fields, enter a brief description or explanation.
* For note fields, enter any additional information or comments that may be relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Inspection Information --**
	+ **Inspection Date**: The date when the inspection was conducted.
	+ **Inspection Time**: The time when the inspection was conducted.
	+ **Inspector Name**: The person conducting the inspection.
* **-- Bracket Information --**
	+ **Bracket ID or Serial Number**: A unique identifier for the bracket.
	+ **Bracket Type**: The type or classification of the bracket (e.g., Steel, Aluminum, Composite, etc.).
	+ **Installation Location**: The location where the bracket is installed.
	+ **Original Installation Date**: The date when the bracket was originally installed.
* **-- Visual Inspection --**
	+ **Overall Visual Condition**: A general assessment of the bracket's appearance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical).
	+ **Rust or Corrosion Present**: Whether any visible rust or corrosion is present (None, Minor, Moderate, Significant, Severe).
	+ **Visible Cracks or Damage**: Any structural cracks or damage (None, Minor hairline cracks, Small cracks, Large cracks, Breaks or failures).
* **-- Fasteners and Connections --**
	+ **Fasteners Condition**: The state of the fasteners (All tight and secure, Mostly secure, Many loose or corroded, Severely compromised, Failed).
* **-- Load and Stress Assessment --**
	+ **Current Load Rating**: The estimated safe load capacity of the bracket.
* **-- Recommendations --**
	+ **Action Required**: The necessary action to take (No action needed, Monitor and retest, Minor repairs needed, Major repairs required, Replace immediately).
* **Detailed Inspection Notes**: Any additional findings or observations about the bracket's condition.
