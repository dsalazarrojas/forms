# Civil Engineering Site Visit Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a crucial tool for ensuring that all necessary information is collected from civil engineers planning a site visit. It helps in minimizing duplication of requests and streamlining the process for travel and accommodation arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully, ensuring that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
- Provide your employee ID number and full name.
- Select your department and job title.
- Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
- Enter the site visit details, including the site name and address.
- Specify the start and end dates and times of the site visit.
- Choose the method of travel and whether overnight accommodation is required.
- If applicable, provide accommodation details.
- Enter the name and contact information of your direct supervisor.
- Choose the safety equipment needed for the site visit.
- Enter the estimated travel cost, if any.
- Add any additional comments or information that may be relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name (1)**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Employee ID Number (2)**: Enter your company employee ID number.
* **Department (3)**: Select your department, which helps identify your area of expertise.
* **Job Title (4)**: Enter your current job title.
* **Email Address (5)**: Enter your company email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number (6)**: Enter your best contact number during the leave.
* **Site Visit Location Name (7)**: Enter the name of the construction site or project.
* **Site Address (8)**: Enter the full address of the site visit location.
* **Leave Start Date (9)**: Enter the first day of your site visit.
* **Departure Time (10)**: Specify the time you will leave for the site visit.
* **Leave End Date (11)**: Enter the last day of your site visit.
* **Expected Return Time (12)**: Enter the time you expect to return.
* **Total Days Away (13)**: Enter the number of business days you will be away.
* **Purpose of Site Visit (14)**: Describe the objectives of your site visit.
* **Planned Activities (15)**: Select all activities that apply to your site visit.
* **Method of Travel (16)**: Choose how you will travel to the site.
* **Is Overnight Accommodation Required (17)**: Indicate whether you will need overnight lodging.
* **Accommodation Details (18)**: If you need lodging, enter the hotel name and address.
* **Supervisor Name (19)**: Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Backup Contact Person (20)**: Enter the name and contact information of who will cover your responsibilities.
* **Safety Equipment Needed (21)**: Select the required personal protective equipment (PPE) for the site visit.
* **Estimated Travel Cost (22)**: Enter the estimated travel cost, including fuel, lodging, and meals.
* **Additional Comments (23)**: Enter any other relevant information that may be helpful.
