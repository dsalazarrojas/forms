# Civil Drawings Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for submitting civil drawings to our team for review and approval. It is intended to gather necessary information about you and your project, as well as the drawings you are submitting. Completing this form accurately and thoroughly ensures that we can efficiently review and process your submission. Please note that submitting duplicate or incomplete information may lead to delays in the review process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details: Please enter your full name, company or organization name, primary contact email, and phone number.
2. Enter project details: Provide the official project name, project number or ID (if applicable), and project location.
3. Select project type: Choose the primary category of your project from the options provided.
4. Specify drawing types: Select all that apply from the list of drawing types provided.
5. Enter drawing details: Provide the total number of drawing sheets, primary drawing scale (if applicable), and format of the submitted files.
6. Confirm submission purpose: Choose the reason for submitting the drawings.
7. Confirm external consultants: If external consultants were involved, mark 'True' and provide their details.
8. Confirm expedited review: If expedited review is requested, mark 'True' and be aware that additional fees may apply.
9. Provide additional comments: If you have any other relevant information, please include it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Full Name**: Enter your first and last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Company or Organization**: Enter your company or firm name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Provide your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number.
* **Professional Engineer License Number**: If you hold a Professional Engineer license, enter the license number.
* **License State or Jurisdiction**: If applicable, enter the state or jurisdiction where your license is registered.
* **Project Name**: Enter the official project name or title.
* **Project Number or ID**: Enter the internal project reference number (if applicable).
* **Project Location**: Enter the address or location description of the project site.
* **Project Type**: Select the primary project category from the options provided.
* **Type of Drawings Submitted**: Select all that apply from the list of drawing types provided.
* **Number of Drawing Sheets**: Enter the total number of drawing sheets submitted.
* **Primary Drawing Scale**: If applicable, enter the most common scale used for the drawings.
* **Purpose of Submission**: Choose the reason for submitting the drawings.
* **Revision Number**: If applicable, enter the revision number of the submissions.
* **Previous Submission Date**: If this is a resubmission, enter the date of the previous submission.
* **Were External Consultants Involved**: If external consultants were involved, mark 'True' and provide their details.
* **Consultant Details**: If applicable, list the consultant firms and their roles.
* **Special Requirements or Notes**: If any special considerations for review, provide them here.
* **Is Expedited Review Requested**: If expedited review is requested, mark 'True' and be aware that additional fees may apply.
* **Drawing File Format**: Select the format of the submitted files.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any other relevant information, provide it here.
