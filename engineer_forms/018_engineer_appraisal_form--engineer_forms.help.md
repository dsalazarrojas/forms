# engineer_appraisal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from engineers about a particular project or proposal. It consists of a series of questions and an optional text field for additional comments or notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages by clicking on the "Next" button or use the "Submit" button to save your progress and continue later. The form is divided into several pages, each with one or more questions. For each question, select the corresponding answer from the dropdown menu, and for the text fields, type your response directly into the field. When finished, click "Submit" to send your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_question** (select_one, required: false): This question asks whether the project or proposal meets the expected standards. Choose "Yes" if you believe it meets the standards, or "No" if it does not.
* **answer_1_label** (text, required: false): This field allows you to provide a brief description of your answer to the previous question.
* **second_question** (select_one, required: false): This question asks whether the project or proposal meets the expected timelines. Choose "Yes" if it does, or "No" if it does not.
* **answer_2_label** (text, required: false): This field allows you to provide a brief description of your answer to the previous question.
* **third_question** (select_one, required: false): This question asks whether the project or proposal meets the expected budget. Choose "Yes" if it does, or "No" if it does not.
* **answer_3_label** (text, required: false): This field allows you to provide a brief description of your answer to the previous question.
* **notes** (note, required: false): This is an optional field for providing additional comments or notes about the project or proposal.
* **contact_email** (email, required: false): This is an optional field for providing your contact email address, in case we need to get back to you with further questions or concerns.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions to the best of your ability, and feel free to provide additional comments or notes if you have anything else you'd like to mention.
- You can save your progress and continue later by clicking on the "Submit" button.
- If you are experiencing any issues while filling out the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
