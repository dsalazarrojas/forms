# water_engineer_job_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a job application form for water engineers. Complete the form to apply for available positions in the field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your Email address in the **Email** field. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the **Phone Number** field.
5. Select your current Job Title from the available options in the **Job Title** field.
6. Enter your Job Start Date in the **Job Start Date** field. This is where you began working as a water engineer.
7. Enter your Years of Experience in the **Years of Experience** field. This will help us understand your level of expertise.
8. In the **Available to Start** field, explain when you're available to start a new position. Be as specific as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (Email, Required): A valid email address is required. This will be used for communication purposes.
- **Phone Number** (Text, Not Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Job Title** (Text, Not Required): Select your current job title from the available options.
- **Job Start Date** (Date, Not Required): Enter the date you began working as a water engineer.
- **Years of Experience** (Number, Not Required): Enter your level of experience as a water engineer.
- **Available to Start** (Text, Not Required): Explain when you're available to start a new position. Be specific.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your application is complete.
- Double-check your email address for accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask our HR department for clarification.
