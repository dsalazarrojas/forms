# Cloud Architect Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for individuals interested in applying for a Cloud Architect position at our organization. The form collects personal and professional information to assess your qualifications, experience, and motivation for the role. Please complete this form carefully, providing accurate and relevant information to help us get to know you better.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Personal Information**: This section is used to collect your basic contact details.
   - Fill out the `Full Name`, `Email Address`, and `Phone Number` fields accurately.
   - Enter your `Current Location` as per the city and country format (e.g., "New York, USA" or "London, UK").
2. **Availability and Notice Period**: This section is about your work status and notice period.
   - Choose your availability status: "Immediately available" or select a specific notice period.
3. **Professional Experience**: This section is to understand your work experience in cloud architecture.
   - Fill out the `Years of Experience in Cloud Architecture` accurately.
   - Enter your current job title and the name of your current company.
4. **Technical Skills**: This section is about your expertise in cloud platforms and technologies.
   - Select all cloud platforms you have experience with (e.g., AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, etc.).
   - Indicate your level of experience with each platform.
5. **Specific Expertise**: This section is to highlight your specific skills and knowledge.
   - Select your level of expertise in compliance and security, cloud cost optimization, and other relevant skills.
6. **Motivation and Expectations**:
   - In a few sentences, explain why you are interested in this position.
   - Provide a range for your salary expectation in USD.
7. **References**:
   - Select whether references are available upon request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as per your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Your active email address for correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Your primary contact number.
* **Current Location** (`current_location`, `text`, required): Your current location in city and country format.
* **Willig to relocate if required?** (`willing_to_relocate`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your willingness to relocate if required by the company.
* **Years of Experience in Cloud Architecture** (`years_experience`, `number`, required): Accurately report your years of experience in cloud architecture.
* **Current Job Title** (`current_role`, `text`, required): Enter your current job title accurately.
* **Current Company** (`current_company`, `text`, required): Enter your current company name accurately.
* **Notice Period Required** (`notice_period`, `select_one`, required): Select the period by which you are available to join.
* **Cloud Platform Experience** (`cloud_platforms`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all cloud platforms you have experience with.
* **Relevant Cloud Certifications** (`certification_level`, `select_multiple`, required): Select relevant cloud certifications you hold.
* **Types of architectures designed** (`architecture_experience`, `select_multiple`, required): Select types of architectures you have designed.
* **Programming languages experience** (`programming_languages`, `text`, optional): If you have experience, list your programming languages.
* **DevOps tools experience** (`devops_tools`, `text`, optional): If you have experience, list your DevOps tools.
* **Compliance and Security Expertise** (`compliance_security`, `select_one`, required): Select your expertise level in compliance and security.
* **Cloud Cost Optimization Expertise** (`cost_optimization`, `select_one`, required): Select your expertise level in cloud cost optimization.
* **Why are you interested in this position?** (`reason_applying`, `text`, required): In a few sentences, explain why you are interested in this position.
* **Salary Expectation Range** (`salary_expectation`, `text`, optional): Provide a range for your salary expectation in USD.
* **References available** (`references_available`, `select_one`, required): Select whether references are available upon request.

## Tips

- Please provide accurate and truthful information to ensure the best possible outcome.
- You can save your progress and come back later, or ask for help if you need it.
- This form is designed to be completed on a device with a keyboard for better typing ease.

By following these steps and explanations, you should be able to fill out the form correctly and provide the best possible application for the Cloud Architect position.
