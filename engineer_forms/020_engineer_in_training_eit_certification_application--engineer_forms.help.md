# Engineer In Training (EIT) Certification Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for engineers to apply for certification as an Engineer In Training (EIT). The purpose of this form is to gather information about the applicant, including their name, contact email, and contact phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Engineer In Training Application" page.
2. Fill out the required fields, including your name, email, and any other information you wish to provide.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Name** (required)
* The "Name" field is for your official name, as registered with your professional engineering institution.

*   **Email** (`email`, required)
    * This is the email address we will use to contact you.

*   **Phone** (required)
    * This is your contact phone number.
