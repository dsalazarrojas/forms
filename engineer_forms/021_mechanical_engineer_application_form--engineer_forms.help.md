# Mechanical Engineer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mechanical Engineer Application Form is designed to collect information from potential mechanical engineer applicants to assess their suitability for mechanical engineer positions within our company.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form only if you are a genuine applicant or if you have been specifically invited to do so by the hiring team.
- Ensure you have all the necessary information and documentation ready, such as education certificates, work experience, and relevant qualifications.
- For each field, provide accurate and truthful information to the best of your ability.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask the hiring team for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mechanical Engineer Applicant Info** (`mechanical_engineer_applicant_info_1`): Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details that we should know about you.
* **Mechanical Engineer Education** (`mechanical_engineer_education_1`): Select one or more relevant degree(s) in mechanical engineering or mechatronics engineering. If you have an education in a different field, please choose "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
* **Mechanical Engineer Experience** (`mechanical_engineer_experience_1`): Indicate whether you have relevant work experience in mechanical engineering. If yes, please specify the level of experience (Internship, Entry-Level, Mid-Level, or Senior-Level).
* **Mechanical Engineer Qualifications** (`mechanical_engineer_qualifications_1`): List any relevant certifications, skills, or courses related to mechanical engineering.
* **Mechanical Engineer Work History** (`mechanical_engineer_work_history_1`): Select one or more relevant work experiences from the list, and provide a brief description of each experience.
* **Mechanical Engineer References** (`mechanical_engineer_references_1`): Select one or more professional references and provide their contact information.
* **Mechanical Engineer Additional Info 1** (`mechanical_engineer_additional_info_1`): Provide any additional information that is not covered in the above fields, such as special skills or relevant projects you have worked on.
* **Mechanical Engineer Additional Info 2** (`mechanical_engineer_additional_info_2`): If you have any additional information that you think is relevant to your application, please select "Yes" or "No" and provide a brief explanation.

## Tips
- Please fill out this form carefully and accurately, as it will be reviewed and considered for your application.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the hiring team for clarification.
- We appreciate your honesty and transparency in providing truthful and up-to-date information.
