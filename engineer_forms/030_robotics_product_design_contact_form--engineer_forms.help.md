# Robotics Product Design Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow potential customers or clients to submit information about their project ideas and inquire about our product design services for robotics products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the project description in the "Project Inquiry" field, which is the field where you can describe your project idea.
2. Select the type of robotics product you are interested in from the "Application Type" dropdown menu. You can choose from Line Follower, Robot Arm, or Self Balancing.
3. In the "Requirements" field, list all the specific requirements you have for your project.
4. Fill in your contact information, including your email address in the "Contact Information" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. If you need to, you can attach a file related to your project in the "Upload File" field.
7. Finally, provide the date of the project in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Inquiry** (`project_inquiry`, `text`, required/optional): This is the field where you can describe your project idea in detail.
* **Application Type** (`application_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field allows you to select the type of robotics product you are interested in. Please choose from Line Follower, Robot Arm, or Self Balancing.
* **Requirements** (`requirements`, `note`, required/optional): In this field, list all the specific requirements you have for your project.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `email`, required/optional): Please enter your contact email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, `text`, required/optional): This field allows you to attach a file related to your project, if needed.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to provide the date of your project.
