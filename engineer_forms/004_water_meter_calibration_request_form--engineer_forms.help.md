# Water Meter Calibration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for water meter calibration requests. It's intended for water utility companies and their customers to submit calibration requests for meters installed on their properties. The form helps streamline the process, ensuring accuracy and efficiency in handling meter readings and calibrations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, following the guidelines in the Field-by-Field Explanation section.
2. Ensure you answer all required fields, denoted by a red asterisk (*) in the Field-by-Field Explanation section.
3. Submit your request using the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Water Meter Calibration Request** (`water_meter_calibration_request_form_page_1`, text, not required): This field allows you to describe the purpose of your request. Be clear and concise, as this will help us understand your needs.
* **Meter Type** (`water_meter_calibration_request_form_page_2`, select_one, not required): This field helps us understand the type of meter you need calibrated. Please select from the options:
	+ Calibrated Meter: This type of meter has already been calibrated.
	+ Non-calibrated Meter: This type of meter has not been calibrated.
* **Calibration Frequency** (`water_meter_calibration_request_form_page_3`, select_one, not required): This field asks how frequently the meter needs calibration. Please select from the options:
	+ 0-3 months: The meter needs calibration within three months.
	+ 6 months: The meter needs calibration within six months.
	+ 12 months: The meter needs calibration within twelve months.
* **Meter Calibration Date** (`water_meter_calibration_request_form_page_4`, date, not required): Enter the date when you last had the meter calibrated or when you think it needs calibration. This helps us track the history of your meter calibrations.
* **Meter Calibration Time** (`water_meter_calibration_request_form_page_5`, time, not required): Please enter the time when you last had the meter calibrated or when you think it needs calibration. This helps us schedule the calibration more accurately.
* **Contact Information** (`water_meter_calibration_request_form_page_6`, text, not required): This field is for you to provide any additional contact information, such as your contact name, phone number, or email.
* **Additional Information** (`water_meter_calibration_request_form_page_7`, text, not required): This field allows you to provide any additional details or context related to your meter calibration request.
* **Select Meter Type** (`water_meter_calibration_request_form_page_8`, select_multiple, not required): From the provided options, choose all the meters that require calibration.
* **Meter Calibration Type** (`water_meter_calibration_request_form_page_9`, select_one, not required): This field asks you to specify the type of calibration needed. Please select from the options:
	+ Calibration 1
	+ Calibration 2
* **Submit Request** (`water_meter_calibration_request_form_page_10`, select_one, required): Please select "Submit Request" to submit your calibration request.

## Tips

* Ensure you provide accurate and detailed information about your meter and its calibration needs.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before submitting your request.
* Our team will review your form submission and follow up with further instructions or clarification as needed.
