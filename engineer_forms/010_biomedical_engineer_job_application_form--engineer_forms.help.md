# Biomedical Engineer Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for biomedical engineers to apply for a job position. It collects relevant information about the applicant, including contact details, job specifics, and other necessary documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all required documents and information ready before proceeding.
2.  Complete all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3.  Double-check your contact email address and phone number for correct formatting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name to identify yourself in our records.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Provide the email address used for communication with us.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your mobile or daytime phone number for quick communication.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied_for`, text, required): Specify the job title or reference number you are applying for.
* **Desired Start Date** (`desired_start_date`, date, optional): Indicate when you are available to start in the format MM/DD/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Desired Salary** (`desired_salary`, number, optional): Enter your annual salary expectation in your local currency.
* **Eligibility to Work** (`eligibility_to_work`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you are eligible to work in the country, 'False' if not, or 'Need sponsorship' if you require sponsorship.
* **References** (`references`, text, required): Provide the names and contact details of two professional references.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Add any relevant information that may help your application.
* **Resume Upload** (`resume_upload_note`, note, required): If you are unable to upload a resume, provide a link to an online location where we can access it.

# Tips

* Make sure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy and formatting of your contact email address and phone number.
* If you have any issues with uploading your resume, contact us for alternative arrangements.
* Be clear and concise in your references and additional information sections to help us understand your background and fit for the position.
