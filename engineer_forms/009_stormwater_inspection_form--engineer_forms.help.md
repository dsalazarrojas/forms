# Stormwater Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often is the stormwater system inspected?**: Select the frequency of the inspection (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, etc.).
* **What system components are inspected?**: Choose the components that were inspected during the maintenance (Pump, Filter, Valve, etc.).
* **Any other notes on inspection?**: Add any additional comments or observations about the inspection.
* **Last inspection date**: Enter the date of the previous inspection.
* **Next inspection date**: Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection.
* **Inspector name**: Provide the name of the person performing the inspection.
* **Inspector phone**: Enter the phone number of the inspector.
* **Inspector email**: Enter the email address of the inspector.
* **Inspector location**: Enter the location where the inspection took place.

* **Comments**: Add any additional comments from the inspector.

* **Notes**: Add any additional notes on the inspection form.
* **Submitter name**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
