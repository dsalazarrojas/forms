# Industrial Energy Distribution Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the industrial energy distribution system's status. It's intended to be used by facility engineers or maintenance personnel to report on the energy distribution system's operational issues, energy sources, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before starting to ensure you understand what information is needed.
2. Complete the General Information page, if necessary, by providing basic details about the energy distribution system.
3. On the Energy Sources page, select the energy sources that apply to your facility. You can select multiple options for this field.
4. On the Operational Issues page, select the type of operational issue that best describes the current status of the energy distribution system.
5. On the Energy Distribution Details page, provide additional details about the energy distribution system. This includes selecting the type of energy distributed and providing a total energy distributed value.
6. If you have any operational issues, describe them in the Operational Issues Description field.
7. Finally, provide any additional comments or feedback in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, optional): This page is for basic information about the energy distribution system. If you don't have any updates, you can skip this page.
* **Energy Sources** (`energy_sources`, select_multiple, optional): Select all energy sources that apply to your facility. You can select multiple options for this field.
* **Total Energy Distributed** (`total_energy_distributed`, number, optional): Provide the total energy distributed value. Note that this field may be used for calculation or data analysis.
* **Operational Issues** (`operational_issues`, select_one, optional): Select the type of operational issue that best describes the current status of the energy distribution system.
* **Operational Issues Description** (`operational_issues_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of the operational issues, if any.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the energy distribution system.
* **Date Last Submission** (`date_last_submission`, date, optional): This field is not currently used but may be relevant for future updates.
* **Time Last Submission** (`time_last_submission`, time, optional): This field is not currently used but may be relevant for future updates.
* **Energy Sources Type** (`energy_sources_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of energy sources used by your facility. You can select multiple options for this field.
* **Total Energy Distributed Type** (`total_energy_distributed_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of total energy distributed. You can select multiple options for this field.
* **Total Energy Distributed Value** (`total_energy_distributed_value`, number, optional): Provide the total energy distributed value. Note that this field may be used for calculation or data analysis.
* **Energy Sources 1** (`energy_sources_1`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of energy sources used by your facility. You can select multiple options for this field.
