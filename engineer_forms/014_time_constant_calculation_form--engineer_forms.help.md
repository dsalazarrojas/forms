# Time Constant Calculation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Constant Calculation Form is a user-facing form designed to collect user information and allow users to calculate time constants. This form is used for internal purposes and will not be shared publicly. It will be reviewed internally to ensure accuracy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your user information on the first page.
2. Choose a specific date for the time constant calculation.
3. Select a calculation method for the time constant.
4. Enter result information on the next page.
5. Confirm your result by selecting an option.
6. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information (user_info)**: Enter your name, company, and any other relevant details that you want to associate with your calculated time constant.
- **Time Constant (time_constant)**: Choose a specific date for the time constant calculation. This will help us to calculate the time constant accurately.
- **Calculation Method (calculation)**: Select a calculation method that you want to use for the time constant calculation. This will ensure that the result is accurate and relevant.
- **Result Information (result_info)**: Enter result information on this page. This is where you will enter the details of your calculated time constant.
- **Review (review)**: Confirm your result by selecting one of the options. This is an important step to ensure that you are satisfied with the result.
- **Submit (submit)**: Click the "Submit" button to submit your form and get access to the calculated time constant. This is the final step in the form completion process.
