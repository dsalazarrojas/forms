# LED Retrofit Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help assess and document the risks associated with LED retrofit projects. It guides users in identifying and evaluating potential hazards and developing effective mitigation strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out this form for each LED retrofit project**. Ensure to provide accurate and detailed information for a thorough risk assessment.
2. **Provide the project details**:
	* Enter the project's title and location.
3. **Select the LED upgrade type**:
	* Choose from "LED Tube", "LED Troffer", or "Other" options.
4. **Identify risk factors**:
	* Select all relevant risk factors, such as Electrical, Fire, Water, or Structural hazards.
5. **Assess the risk level**:
	* Choose from "Low", "Medium", or "High" risk levels.
6. **Develop mitigation strategies**:
	* Enter a brief description of the measures to be taken to mitigate identified risks.
7. **Add any additional comments or notes**:
	* Enter any relevant details or comments related to the project.
8. **Save your progress and review as needed**:
	* Regularly review and update your risk assessment as project stages progress.
