# mechanical_engineer_job_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mechanical Engineer Job Application Form is designed to help mechanical engineers submit applications for job openings. This form collects information about the applicant's basic details, experience, qualifications, and job preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the required information before starting to fill in the form.
* Fill in the First Name field with your actual first name.
* Fill in the Last Name field with your actual last name.
* Enter your Email address for contact purposes.
* Enter your Phone number for easy contact.
* Attach a copy of your Resume for the hiring team to review.
* Provide a brief description of your experience in the Experience field.
* List your qualifications in the Qualifications field.
* Select the Mechanical Engineer Role that best suits your expertise in the Mechanical Engineer Role field.
* Enter your desired Job Title (if any).
* Provide the number of years of experience you have in the field.
* Mention your highest level of education achieved.
* Specify your preferred Job Type (full-time, part-time, or freelance).
* Indicate if you are Available to Relocate or not.
* Choose your preferred Notice Period.
* Select your preferred Available Start Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your actual first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your actual last name.

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, Optional): Enter your desired Job Title.

* **Education** (`education`, `text`, Optional): Mention your highest level of education achieved.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `text`, Optional): Choose your preferred Job Type (full-time, part-time, or freelance).
* **Desired Location** (`desired_location`, `text`, Optional): Specify your preferred location for the job.
