# Electronics Design Requirements Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document the key requirements and information for electronics design projects.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the previous section.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project, which will be used to identify and track the project.
* **Project Description**: Provide a brief description of the project to ensure everyone is on the same page.
* **Manufacturer Name**: Enter the name of the manufacturer or company producing the product.
* **Product Type**: Select the type of product being designed (e.g., Radio Button, Switch, LED).
* **Project Requirements**: Enter the specific requirements and goals for the project.
* **Design Team**: Choose the members of the design team involved in the project.
* **Manufacturing Team**: Select the members of the manufacturing team (if applicable).
* **Project Lead**: Choose the project lead (if applicable).
* **Due Date**: Set the date by which the project must be completed.
* **Submission Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes for the submission (optional).
## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "Required" in the form).
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* Double-check the due date to ensure it is realistic and achievable.
* Review and edit the submission notes for accuracy and completeness.
