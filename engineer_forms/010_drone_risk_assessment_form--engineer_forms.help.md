# Drone Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about drone risks, including risk level, category, and prevention measures, to ensure safety and compliance in drone operations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form's pages and fields to understand the overall structure and purpose.
2. Complete each page in the correct order, providing accurate information for each field.
3. Ensure that you do not duplicate the form for the same drone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drone Risk Assessment Form** (Page 1)
	+ This is the title of the form, which is self-explanatory.
* **Risk Level** (Page 1)
	+ Select one or multiple risk levels:
		- Low: minimal risk
		- Medium: moderate risk
		- High: high risk
* **Risk Category** (Page 1)
	+ Select one risk category:
		- Collision
		- Fire
		- Crash
		- Other (please specify)
* **Risk Description** (Page 1)
	+ Provide a brief description of the risk.
* **Risk Prevention Measures** (Page 1)
	+ Describe the measures taken to prevent or mitigate the risk.
* **Recommended Actions** (Page 1)
	+ Suggest any recommended actions for risk prevention or mitigation.
* **Drone Model** (Page 1)
	+ Select the model of the drone:
		- Mavic 2
		- Mavic Air
		- Spark
		- Other (please specify)
* **Drone Weight** (Page 1)
	+ Enter the weight of the drone in [units].
* **Drone Size** (Page 1)
	+ Select the size of the drone:
		- Small
		- Medium
		- Large
* **Drone Type** (Page 1)
	+ Select the type of drone:
		- Fixed Wing
		- Rotor
		- Hybrid
		- Other (please specify)
* **Battery Capacity** (Page 1)
	+ Select the battery capacity:
		- Low
		- Medium
		- High
* **Battery Type** (Page 1)
	+ Select the type of battery:
		- LiPo
		- NiMH
		- Other (please specify)
* **Battery Weight** (Page 1)
	+ Enter the weight of the battery in [units].
* **Drone Warranty** (Page 1)
	+ Select whether the drone is under warranty:
		- True
		- False
