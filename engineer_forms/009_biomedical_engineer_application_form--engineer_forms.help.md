# Biomedical Engineer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for applicants to the biomedical engineer position. It is used to gather accurate information about the applicant's contact and professional details, qualifications, and experience. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Start by filling out the required fields (indicated by *), and provide accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all fields marked as required are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1.  -- Applicant Details --
*   **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): This field is for your legal name.
*   **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): This field is for communication purposes. Please enter a valid email address that will receive updates about your application.
*   **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): This field is for your mobile or work phone number. Please ensure it is accurate for communication.
*   **Highest degree** (`highest_degree`, `text`, required): This field is for your highest degree earned.
*   **Years of experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, required): This field is for the number of years you have worked as a biomedical engineer.
*   **Resume or CV** (`resume_note`, `note`, required): Upload your resume or CV, or provide a link to it for review.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Double-check that all fields are accurate before submission.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the relevant personnel for help.
