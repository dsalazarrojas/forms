## Material Property Data Collection - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is designed to collect data on material properties and their related characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Go through each page**: The form is divided into 4 pages, each focusing on a specific aspect of material properties.
2.  **Fill out the required information**: Each page has a required field marked with an asterisk (\*). You must fill out the required fields to ensure the data collected is comprehensive and accurate.
3.  **Enter accurate and relevant information**: For each field, provide accurate and relevant information about the material property or characteristic being collected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Material Property Data Collection** (`material_property_data_collection_page_1`): This is the first page of the form where you will find a required field for collecting the material property data. Enter the relevant information about the material property you are collecting.
*   **Material Characteristics** (`material_characteristics_page_2`): This page is for collecting the material characteristics. Enter the characteristics related to the material property you are collecting data on.
*   **Material Sampling** (`material_sampling_page_3`): On this page, you will find a required field for collecting the material sampling data. Enter the accurate and relevant information about the sampling process.
*   **Material Testing** (`material_testing_page_4`): This page is for collecting the material testing information. Enter the relevant information about the testing process for the material.

## Tips
-   Always enter accurate and relevant information in each field.
-   Ensure you fill out all the required fields.
