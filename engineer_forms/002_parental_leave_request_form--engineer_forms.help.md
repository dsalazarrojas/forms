# Parental Leave Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is to request a parental leave, which is a type of leave that allows employees to take time off from work to care for their child. This form is designed to gather necessary information from the employee and their manager to process the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your employee name, job title, and department.
2. Provide the start and end dates of your requested leave, including the date you plan to start and end your leave.
3. Specify the reason for your leave and any additional information you feel is relevant.
4. Indicate your expected return date from work.
5. If applicable, provide your manager's approval status and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, **required**):
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your company ID badge.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, **required**):
	+ Enter your job title or position within the company.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, **required**):
	+ Enter your department or team you belong to.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, `date`, **required**):
	+ Enter the date you plan to start your leave in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, `date`, **required**):
	+ Enter the date you plan to end your leave in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, `text`, **required**):
	+ Briefly explain the reason for your leave, such as a family event or medical condition.
* **Expected Return Date** (`expected_return_date`, `date`, **required**):
	+ Enter the date you plan to return to work after your leave.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ Select 'Yes' if your manager has approved your leave request.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, **required**):
	+ Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, **required**):
	+ Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, **required**):
	+ Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Spouse Name** (`employee_name_spouse`, `text`, **required**):
	+ Enter your spouse's name.
* **Relationship Status** (`employee_relationship_status`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ Select 'Active' if you are currently married or in a domestic partnership.
* **Status** (`employee_status`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ Select 'Active' if you are currently employed by the company.
* **Job Title** (`employee_job_title`, `select_multiple`, **required**):
	+ Select all job titles that apply to you.
* **Department** (`employee_department`, `select_multiple`, **required**):
	+ Select all departments that apply to you.
* **Supervisor Name** (`employee_supervisor_name`, `text`, **required**):
	+ Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`employee_supervisor_phone`, `text`, **required**):
	+ Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Email** (`employee_supervisor_email`, `email`, **required**):
	+ Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_work_email`, `email`, **required**):
	+ Enter your work email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_work_phone`, `text`, **required**):
	+ Enter your work phone number.
* **Address** (`employee_work_address`, `text`, **required**):
	+ Enter your work address.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, `select_multiple`, **required**):
	+ Select all types of leave that apply to you.
* **Leave Start Time** (`leave_start_time`, `time`, **required**):
	+ Enter the time you plan to start your leave in the HH:MM format.
* **Leave End Time** (`leave_end_time`, `time`, **required**):
	+ Enter the time you plan to end your leave in the HH:MM format.

Note: The fields marked as **required** are mandatory and must be completed to process the leave request.
