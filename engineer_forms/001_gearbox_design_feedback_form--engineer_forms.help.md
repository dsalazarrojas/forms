# gearbox_design_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about gearbox design. It helps us understand your opinions, suggestions, and experiences with our gearboxes, which is essential for us to improve our design quality and user satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read the introduction to understand the context and purpose of the form.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the gearbox design on the rating scale provided.
3. If you have any comments or feedback, provide them in the text field.
4. If you have attached any relevant documents or files to support your feedback, upload them as an attachment.
5. Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
6. Enter your phone number for further communication (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a brief description of your experience with the gearbox design. Please provide a concise introduction that sets the context for your feedback.
* **Rating**: Please rate your overall satisfaction with the gearbox design using the scale provided.
* **Comment**: If you have any specific feedback or suggestions, provide them in this text field. This is your opportunity to tell us what you liked or disliked about the gearbox design.
* **Attachment**: If you have any relevant documents or files that support your feedback, upload them here.
* **Rating scale**: This is a dropdown menu with options 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest rating. Please select the rating that best reflects your overall satisfaction with the gearbox design.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone**: This is an optional field where you can enter your phone number for further communication. If you choose to provide your phone number, we will use it only for follow-up questions or discussions related to your feedback.
