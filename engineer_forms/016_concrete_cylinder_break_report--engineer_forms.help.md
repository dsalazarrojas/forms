# Concrete Cylinder Break Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Concrete Cylinder Break Report is a form used to document the results of a concrete cylinder test, which is a laboratory test to determine the compressive strength of concrete. This form is used to record the details of the test, including the laboratory technician's name, project reference, sample ID, test dates, specimen dimensions, load at failure, calculated compressive strength, type of break observed, and any remarks or observations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name as the laboratory technician conducting the test in the "Laboratory technician name" field.
2. Enter the project reference or job number associated with the test in the "Project reference" field.
3. Enter the unique sample ID for the concrete cylinder in the "Sample identifier" field.
4. Enter the date the concrete cylinder was cast in the "Cast date" field.
5. Enter the date the concrete cylinder was tested to failure in the "Break date" field.
6. Enter the dimensions of the concrete specimen (diameter and height) in millimeters in the "Specimen dimensions mm" field.
7. Enter the peak load recorded during the test in the "Load at failure kN" field.
8. Enter the calculated compressive strength of the concrete in MPa in the "Calculated compressive strength MPa" field.
9. Select the type of break observed during the test from the options provided in the "Type of break observed" field.
10. Enter any additional remarks or observations about the test in the "Remarks and observations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Laboratory technician name** (Required, Text): Enter your name as the person conducting the test.
* **Project reference** (Required, Text): Enter the project name or job number associated with the test.
* **Sample identifier** (Required, Text): Enter the unique sample ID for the concrete cylinder.
* **Cast date** (Required, Date): Enter the date the concrete cylinder was cast.
* **Break date** (Required, Date): Enter the date the concrete cylinder was tested to failure.
* **Specimen dimensions mm** (Required, Text): Enter the diameter and height of the specimen in millimeters.
* **Load at failure kN** (Required, Number): Enter the peak load recorded during the test.
* **Calculated compressive strength MPa** (Required, Number): Enter the calculated compressive strength of the concrete in MPa.
* **Type of break observed** (Optional, Select One): Select the type of break observed during the test (Typical, Shear, Split, or Other).
* **Remarks and observations** (Optional, Text): Enter any additional remarks or observations about the test.
