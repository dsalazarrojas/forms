# Electrical Termination Drawing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electrical Termination Drawing Request Form is designed to gather essential information for engineers and technicians to create accurate electrical termination drawings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields for each page, which are indicated by the `required: true` label.
2. For fields with type `email`, ensure the email address is in the correct format (e.g., `user@example.com`).
3. Enter your contact information accurately, as this will help the requesting engineer or technician get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Electrical Termination Drawing Request**: Enter a brief description of the electrical termination request.
* **Contact Information**:
	+ **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person requesting the termination drawing.
	+ **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person requesting the termination drawing.
	+ **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person requesting the termination drawing.
* **Termination Request Details**: Enter any additional details or comments about the termination request.
* **Termination Request Date**: Enter the date of the termination request (in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`).
* **Termination Request Time**: Enter the time of the termination request (in the format `HH:MM:SS`).
* **Location**: Enter the location where the electrical termination is required.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the termination request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely, as this will help the requesting engineer or technician provide the best possible service.
* If you have any questions or concerns while filling out the form, don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant department or team lead for guidance.
