# Shift Handoff Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect your preferences for shift handoffs, which is the process of passing information, tasks, or responsibilities from one person to another.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best fit your preferences for each question. Please note that all fields are optional, and you can select one or multiple options for some questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Shift (`shift_id`):
* Select the type of shift you are working on: None, Primary, Secondary, or Other.

### Handoff Method (`handoff_method`):
* Choose how you would like to receive the handoff: Voice, Video, Text, or Email.

### Shift Length (`shift_length`):
* You do not have to select an option for this field if you don't want to provide the shift length information.

### Team Name (`team_name`):
* Enter your team's name, if you want to associate your preferences with your team.

### How Often (`communication_frequency`):
* Indicate how often you would like to receive handoff information: Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly.

### Improvement Priorities (`improvement_priorities`):
* Select one or more areas where you would like to focus on improvements: Prophets, Shift Leader, Team Manager, Team Lead, or Other.

### Feedback Frequency (`feedback_frequency`):
* Choose how often you would like to receive feedback: Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly.

### Feedback Type (`feedback_type`):
* Choose how you would like to receive feedback: Verbal, Written, Email, or Other.

### Communication Type (`communication_type`):
* Select how you would like to communicate with others: Phone, Email.

### Improvement Areas (`improvement_areas`):
* Select one or more areas where you would like to focus on improvements: Prophets, Shift Leader, Team Manager, Team Lead, or Other.

### Submission Status (`submission_status`):
* This field is not used for feedback, it's just for tracking submission status.

Note: Some fields allow you to select multiple options, so don't be afraid to choose all that apply! If you have any questions or need help, please contact the form administrators.
