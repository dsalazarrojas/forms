# daily_operations_log_for_crews - Help Guide
## Purpose
The daily operations log for crews is a form used to record information about daily operations, including crew shifts, operational issues, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "daily_operations_log_for_crews" form.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are indicated by an asterisk (\*).
3. Select relevant options from the dropdown menus as needed.
4. Click "Submit" to save your entries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **crew_shift** (2): Enter the name of your crew or shift you are working on.
* **operational_notes** (3): Provide a brief description of any operational notes or comments about your work.
* **operational_issues** (4): Check all that apply if you encountered any operational issues during your shift. Options include "Yes" and "No".
* **work_activity** (5): Describe the work activity you were involved in during your shift.
* **site_shift** (6): Check "Yes" if you worked on a site or "No" if you worked remotely.
* **shift_start_time** (7): Enter the start time of your shift in the correct format (HH:MM or MM/DD/HH:MM).
