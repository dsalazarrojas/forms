# Retail Employee Schedule Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Employee Schedule Request Form is a tool for retail employees to request changes to their scheduled shifts. This form helps ensure that both the employee and manager are on the same page regarding shift changes and updates.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your employee name in the first field.
2.  Enter a brief description of the change you are requesting in the "Schedule Change Request" field.
3.  Select the date and time you would like your shift to start and end.
4.  Choose the department you will be working in for this shift.
5.  If necessary, add any additional notes about your shift in the "Shift Notes" field.
6.  Confirm that your employee ID is correct.
7.  Select the status of your shift (Open, Closed, or Cancelled).
8.  Indicate your availability for this shift (Available, Not Available).
9.  Indicate the shift's availability (Available, Not Available).
10.  Select the type of shift (Full Time, Part Time, or Overtime).
11.  Enter a brief description of your shift in the "Shift Description" field.
12.  Confirm with your manager that the shift changes have been made in the "Manager Confirmation" field.
13.  Confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the shift changes in the "Employee Confirmation" field.
14.  Enter any additional notes or comments about your shift changes in the "Schedule Update Note" fields.
14.  Finally, select a date and time for the shift request and click submit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name (1)**: Enter your name as it appears in your employee directory.
*   **Schedule Change Request (2)**: Briefly describe the change you are requesting to your shift schedule.
*   **Shift Start Date (3)**: Enter the date you would like your shift to start.
*   **Shift Start Time (4)**: Enter the time you would like your shift to start.
*   **Shift End Date (5)**: Enter the date you would like your shift to end.
*   **Shift End Time (6)**: Enter the time you would like your shift to end.
*   **Schedule Update (7)**: Describe the reason for your schedule change request.
*   **Department (8)**: Choose the department you will be working in for this shift.
*   **Shift Notes (9)**: Add any notes or comments about your shift.
*   **Employee ID (10)**: Confirm that your employee ID is correct.
*   **Shift Status (11)**: Select the status of your shift (Open, Closed, or Cancelled).
*   **Employee Availability (12)**: Indicate your availability for this shift (Available, Not Available).
*   **Shift Availability (13)**: Indicate the availability of this shift (Available, Not Available).
*   **Shift Type (14)**: Select the type of shift (Full Time, Part Time, or Overtime).
*   **Shift Description (15)**: Briefly describe your shift.
*   **Manager Confirmation (16)**: Confirm with your manager that the shift changes have been made.
*   **Shift Request Date (17)**: Enter the date of your shift request.
*   **Shift Request Time (18)**: Enter the time of your shift request.
*   **Employee Confirmation (19)**: Confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the shift changes.
*   **Schedule Update Note (20)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your shift changes.
*   **Schedule Update Date (21)**: Not applicable for this form.
*   **Schedule Update Time (22)**: Not applicable for this form.
*   **Employee Availability Note (23)**: Not applicable for this form.
*   **Shift Availability Note (24)**: Not applicable for this form.
*   **Notes (25)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your shift changes.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information in each field.
*   Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
*   Ensure that you have reviewed and agreed to the shift changes with your manager.
*   Enter any additional notes or comments about your shift changes, if necessary.
*   Submit the form to finalize your shift changes.
*   Follow up with your manager to confirm that the changes have been made.
