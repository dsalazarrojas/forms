# Night Shift Task Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Night Shift Task Audit Form is a tool used by supervisors to review and comment on tasks performed during night shifts. This form gathers essential information to assess the quality and accuracy of the tasks completed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your supervisor review comments in the "Supervisor Review Comments" field.
2. Provide the task name in the "Task Name" field.
3. Enter the date of the task completion in the "Date" field.
4. Input the start and end times of the task in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
5. Describe the activity or task in the "Activity Description" field.
6. If an incident occurred, provide a description in the "Incident Description" field and select the "Incident Type" from the available options.
7. Enter the "Reviewed By" field with the name of the person who reviewed the task.
8. Enter the date the task was reviewed in the "Reviewed On" field.
9. Enter supervisor comments in the "Supervisor Comments" field.
10. Select the task status from the available options in the "Task Status" field.
11. Indicate if the task was completed in the "Completed" field.
12. Enter the task ID in the "Task ID" field.
13. Enter task notes in the "Task Notes" field.
14. Enter the shift details, including the shift name, start time, and end time, in their respective fields.
15. Enter any additional notes about the task in the "Notes" field.
16. Enter the contact information, including email and phone number, in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively.
17. Enter supervisor, reviewer, and task type notes in their respective fields.
18. Enter the incident date in the "Incident Date" field.
