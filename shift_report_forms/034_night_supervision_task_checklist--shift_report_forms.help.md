# Night Supervision Task Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Night Supervision Task Checklist is a form used by night supervisors to document and verify the completion of tasks and incidents during night shifts. It's essential to accurately and thoroughly complete this form to ensure that all necessary information is recorded and reported.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by entering the shift start and end times** (`shift_start`, `shift_end`): Enter the start and end times of your night shift. These fields are required and should be entered in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
2. **Complete the task checklist** (`completed_tasks`): Check all tasks that were completed during your shift. Select 'Yes' for tasks that were done, and 'No' for tasks that were not completed.
3. **Report any incidents** (`incidents`): Check 'Yes' if any incidents occurred during your shift, and 'No' if none occurred.
4. **Sign and date the form** (`date`, `supervisor_name`, `supervisor_signature`): Enter your supervisor's name, the current date, and sign with the selected supervisor signature.
5. **Submit the form**: Once all fields are filled out, click 'Submit' to complete and save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Night supervision shift** (`night_supervision_shift`): Enter a brief description of your night shift.
* **Shift start** (`shift_start`): Enter the start time of your night shift in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Shift end** (`shift_end`): Enter the end time of your night shift in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Completed tasks** (`completed_tasks`): Select all tasks that were completed during your shift. This field is required.
* **Incidents** (`incidents`): Select 'Yes' if any incidents occurred during your shift, and 'No' if none occurred. This field is required.
* **Supervisor signature** (`supervisor_signature`): Check 'Yes' if you signed the form, 'No' otherwise. This field is required.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Supervisor name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor signature** (`supervisor_signature`): Check 'Yes' if you signed the form, 'No' otherwise. This field is required.
