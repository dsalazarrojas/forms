# healthcare_provider_shift_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare_provider_shift_report form is designed to collect information from healthcare providers about their shifts. This form can be used to capture details about the shifts, incidents, patient updates, handover notes, handover, patient name, patient ID, shift nurses, shift doctor, patient notes, and shift notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the shift details section, which includes fields for shift date, start time, end time, and shift type.
2. In the incidents section, describe any incidents that occurred during the shift.
3. In the patient updates section, provide any updates about the patients.
4. In the handover notes section, write any notes about the handover process.
5. In the handover section, select any relevant options.
6. In the patient name section, enter the patient's name.
7. In the patient ID section, enter the patient's ID.
8. In the shift nurses section, select any relevant options.
9. In the shift doctor section, select any relevant options.
10. In the patient notes section, provide any additional notes about the patient.
11. In the shift notes section, provide any additional notes about the shift.
