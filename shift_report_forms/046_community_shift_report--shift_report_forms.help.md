# Community Shift Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Shift Report form is used to gather information from shift workers to report their activities, issues, and other important details after their shifts. This form helps management or team leads to stay informed and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, and Employee ID (if applicable).
2. Provide your Email Address, which will be used for communication.
3. Enter your Position or Role.
4. Select the Shift Date when you worked.
5. Choose your Shift Type (Morning, Afternoon, Night, or Custom).
6. Enter your Shift Start and End Times.
7. Select the location where you worked.
8. Describe what happened during your shift in the Shift Activities field (optional).
9. List your main accomplishments in Tasks Completed.
10. Write a chronological summary of events in Activities Log.
11. If applicable, report any accidents, issues, or concerns in the Incidents section.
12. Provide a description of incidents, including actions taken.
13. Report if there were any issues during your shift.
14. Describe how you addressed any issues that arose.
15. Add any important handover notes for the next shift.
16. List any pending tasks for the next shift.
17. Share any important information to pass on to the next shift.
18. Report the status of equipment and supplies.
19. Mention any notable visitors or calls.
20. If applicable, record the number of people served, calls handled, requests completed, and other metrics.
21. Provide any recommendations for improvement.
22. Add any additional comments.
23. Finally, submit your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reporter Information**: This is your personal details section. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Employee ID (if applicable).
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID if you have one.
* **Email Address**: Provide your email address for communication.
* **Position or Role**: Enter your position or role.
* **Shift Date**: Select the date you worked.
* **Shift Type**: Choose your shift type (Morning, Afternoon, Night, or Custom).
* **Shift Start Time**: Enter the time you started your shift.
* **Shift End Time**: Enter the time you ended your shift.
* **Shift Location**: Enter the location where you worked.
* **Shift Activities**: Describe what happened during your shift (optional).
* **Tasks Completed**: List your main accomplishments during the shift.
* **Activities Log**: Write a chronological summary of events.
* **Were There Any Incidents**: Report if there were any accidents or issues during your shift.
* **Describe the Incidents**: Explain any incidents or issues that occurred and actions you took.
* **Were There Any Issues**: Report if there were any issues during your shift.
* **Describe the Issues**: Explain how you addressed any issues that arose.
* **Handover Notes**: Add any important handover notes for the next shift.
* **Pending Tasks**: List any tasks that are pending for the next shift.
* **Important Information to Pass On**: Share any important information to pass on to the next shift.
* **Equipment Status**: Report the status of equipment and supplies.
* **Supplies**: Mention any supplies that need restocking.
* **Staffing Notes**: Record any staffing issues or absences.
* **Notable Visitors or Calls**: Mention any notable visitors or calls during your shift.
* **Number of People Served**: Record the number of people you served if applicable.
* **Number of Calls Handled**: Record the number of calls you handled if applicable.
* **Number of Requests Completed**: Record the number of requests completed if applicable.
* **Recommendations for Improvement**: Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments.
* **Report Submission**: Select 'Yes - Submit Report' to finalize your submission.
