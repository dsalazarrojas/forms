# Shift Report Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shift Report Form Template is a tool used by employees to report their work hours, issues encountered during their shifts, and any actions taken to address these issues. This form helps supervisors and managers keep track of employee work hours, ensure accurate records, and maintain a smooth and efficient work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee ID, which is a required field and should be entered as a numerical value.
2. Enter your shift start and end times. These fields are optional but recommended for accurate record-keeping.
3. Report the duration of your shift, which is also an optional but recommended field.
4. Describe the work you did during your shift in the work description field, which is optional but helps supervisors and managers understand your activities.
5. If there were any issues or problems encountered during your shift, describe them in the Issues Encountered field.
6. If you took any actions to address these issues, report them in the Action Taken field.
7. Leave any additional comments or notes you'd like in the Notes field.
8. Click the "Submit" button to send your report and confirm that you have completed your shift report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (10, number, required): Enter your unique employee ID number to identify you as the reporter.
* **Employee Name** (1, text, optional): Enter your name for record-keeping purposes.
* **Shift Start Time** (2, time, optional): Enter the start time of your shift. This helps supervisors and managers keep track of your work hours.
* **Shift End Time** (3, time, optional): Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Shift Duration** (4, number, optional): Enter the duration of your shift for accurate record-keeping.
* **Work Description** (5, text, optional): Describe the work you did during your shift.
* **Issues Encountered** (6, text, optional): If you encountered any issues, describe them here.
* **Action Taken** (7, text, optional): If you took any actions to address issues, report them here.
* **Notes** (8, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to make about your shift.
* **Submit** (9, select, required): Click 'Yes' to confirm that you have completed your shift report and submit your form.
