# Supervisor In Shift Specific Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report on specific details about a shift, including start and end times, duration, notes, supervisors, employees, and location.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the shift start time in the `shift_start_time` field.
2. Enter the shift end time in the `shift_end_time` field.
3. Enter the duration of the shift in the `shift_duration` field.
4. Enter any notes or comments about the shift in the `shift_notes` field.
5. Indicate whether there were supervisors present during the shift in the `shift_supervisors` field.
6. Indicate whether employees worked during the shift in the `shift_employees` field.
7. Enter the location where the shift took place in the `shift_location` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, date, required): Enter the time the shift started.
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, time, required): Enter the time the shift ended.
* **Shift Duration** (`shift_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the shift in hours or minutes.
* **Shift Notes** (`shift_notes`, note, required): Enter any notes or comments about the shift.
* **Shift Supervisors** (`shift_supervisors`, select multiple, required): Check if supervisors were present during the shift.
* **Shift Employees** (`shift_employees`, select one, required): Check if employees worked during the shift.
* **Shift Location** (`shift_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the shift took place.

## Tips
* Be accurate when entering the start and end times to ensure that the shift duration is correct.
* If there were multiple supervisors or employees during the shift, separate each with a comma.
* If there were any issues or concerns during the shift, include them in the shift notes field.
