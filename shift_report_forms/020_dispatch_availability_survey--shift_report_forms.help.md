# Dispatch Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dispatch Availability Survey is used to collect data on the availability of dispatchers, including their preferred work time, scheduling constraints, and available days. This information is used to optimize dispatching and scheduling processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available options in each field to ensure you understand what they represent.
2. Select the available options that apply to your schedule and preferences.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Availability**: Choose whether you are available to work on a particular day or not. Options: Available, Not Available.
* **Preferred Work Time**: Select the time of day you prefer to work. Options: 06:00, 07:00, 08:00, 09:00, etc.
* **Scheduling Constraints**: Check the box next to any time slots that you are not available to work during. Options: Early Morning (06:00-08:00), Late Morning (08:00-12:00), Afternoon (12:00-17:00), etc.
* **Dispatchers I Report To**: Select which dispatchers you report to. Options: First, Second, Both, None.
* **Breaks**: Choose whether you take breaks or not. Options: True, False.
* **Available Days**: Select the days you are available to work. Options: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.
* **Available Times**: Select the times you are available to work. Options: 06:00, 07:00, 08:00, 09:00, etc.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your availability.
* **Available**: Choose whether you are available to work or not. Options: True, False.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your availability.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Review your answers carefully to ensure they accurately reflect your availability and preferences.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or manager.
