# Nursing Shift Handover Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a structured way for nurses to document and communicate the status of patients, vital signs, and other important information during the handover process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant "Status" field to indicate the current state of the handover (Complete or Incomplete).
2. Enter the "Nurse in Charge" field with the ID of the nurse who is currently in charge of the patient.
3. Provide any additional information in the "Note" field (optional).
4. Enter the "Nurse Outgoing ID" field with your own ID number for the handover record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Status**: Select the status of the handover, indicating whether it is complete or incomplete.
	+ Complete: The handover is complete and all necessary information has been transferred to the nurse taking over.
	+ Incomplete: The handover is not complete and further information needs to be added.
* **Nurse in Charge**: Enter the ID number of the nurse who is currently in charge of the patient.
* **Note**: Add any additional information or comments related to the handover, such as specific patient concerns or special instructions.
* **Nurse Outgoing ID**: Enter your own ID number to associate with the handover record.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before completing it to ensure all relevant information is included.
- Ensure that the "Status" field accurately reflects the current state of the handover.
- If the handover is incomplete, make sure to indicate this and provide a clear plan for what still needs to be done.
- Keep the "Note" field concise and focused on essential information only.
- Use your own ID number in the "Nurse Outgoing ID" field to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
