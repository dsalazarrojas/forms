# Time Clock Exception Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Clock Exception Form is used to report and adjust an employee's work hours due to exceptional circumstances, such as an unexpected absence or a need to start or end work early due to unforeseen events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Employee Name" field, as it appears on your ID or badge.
2. Select the date of the exception in the "Exception Date" field.
3. Enter the start and end times of the exception in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
4. Provide a brief reason for the exception in the "Reason" field.
5. Sign and date the form with your employee signature in the "Employee Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or badge.
* **Exception Date**: Select the date of the exception, using the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Start Time**: Enter the time you were initially scheduled to start work, in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
* **End Time**: Enter the time you were initially scheduled to end work, in 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00).
* **Reason**: Provide a brief explanation of the exceptional circumstances that led to your request.
* **Employee Signature**: Sign and date the form with your employee signature, using your full name.

## Tips
* Ensure you have discussed your request with your supervisor or HR representative before filling out this form.
* If you need to report a time-off, use the separate "Time-Off Request Form" for personal reasons, such as vacation days or sick leave.
