# retail_employee_daily_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The retail_employee_daily_check_in_form is a simple form designed to track employee check-in and check-out times, as well as any relevant notes or comments for retail employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct shift details in the "Retail Shift Details" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Employee Name" field.
3. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
4. Select your start time in the "Start Time" field.
5. Select your end time in the "End Time" field.
6. Choose your check-in status from the options provided in the "Check In Status" field.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. Enter the date of your shift in the "Date" field.
11. (Optional) Enter any comments or notes for your manager in the "Manager Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Retail Shift Details** (`retail_shift_details`, Text, Required): Enter the details of your shift, such as the store name, shift time, or any other relevant information.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID or company records.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Number, Optional): Enter your employee ID number, if applicable.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, Time, Optional): Select the time you started your shift.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, Time, Optional): Select the time you ended your shift.
* **Check In Status** (`check_in_status`, Select One, Optional): Choose how you are checking in (Checked In, Checked Out, or Late).
* **Note** (`note`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your shift.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address, if applicable.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of your shift.
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, Note, Optional): Enter any comments or notes for your manager to review.
