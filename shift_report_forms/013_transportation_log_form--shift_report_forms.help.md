# Transportation Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transportation Log Form is used to record and log transportation activities, such as tracking the start and end times of trips, routes, and vehicle conditions. This form helps transportation or delivery teams monitor and report on their activities, ensuring accurate and efficient operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the start time of the transportation activity.
2. Enter the end time of the transportation activity.
3. Indicate if the vehicle is on a route by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the `route_start` field.
4. Enter the vehicle's number if it is applicable.
5. Check the vehicle's condition by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the `vehicle_condition` field.
6. Enter the amount of fuel used, if applicable.
7. Enter the name of the driver who handled the transportation activity.
8. Select the delivery status as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
9. Enter the destination of the delivery, if applicable.
10. Provide any additional notes about the delivery activity.
11. Enter any notes about the vehicle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Time (start_time)**: The time when the transportation activity began. Enter the date in the correct format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **End Time (end_time)**: The time when the transportation activity ended. Enter the date in the correct format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Route Start (route_start)**: A checkbox to indicate if the vehicle is on a route. Select 'Yes' if the vehicle is on a route, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Vehicle Number (vehicle_number)**: The number of the vehicle used for the transportation activity. Enter the number if applicable.
* **Vehicle Condition (vehicle_condition)**: A checkbox to indicate the vehicle's condition. Select 'Yes' if the vehicle is in good condition, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Fuel Used (fuel_used)**: The amount of fuel used during the transportation activity. Enter a numerical value if applicable.
* **Driver Name (driver_name)**: The name of the driver who handled the transportation activity. Enter the name in the correct format (e.g., John Doe).
* **Delivery Status (delivery_status)**: A dropdown menu to select the delivery status as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Delivery Destination (delivery_destination)**: The destination of the delivery activity. Enter the location or address if applicable.
* **Delivery Notes (delivery_notes)**: Additional notes about the delivery activity. Enter any relevant details in this field.
* **Vehicle Notes (vehicle_notes)**: Any additional notes about the vehicle used for the transportation activity. Enter any relevant details in this field.
