# Weekly Shift Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's shift, including the start and end dates and times, hours worked, breaks, and pay rate. The form is used to accurately record and report employee work hours for payroll and scheduling purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the start date of the shift from the calendar.
2. Enter the start time of the shift in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 for 8:00 AM).
3. Select the end date of the shift from the calendar.
4. Enter the end time of the shift in 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00 for 5:00 PM).
5. Enter the total hours worked by the employee during the shift.
6. Enter the total minutes taken as breaks during the shift.
7. Enter the pay rate of the employee.
8. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when the shift began.
- **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time when the shift started in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 for 8:00 AM).
- **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when the shift ended.
- **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time when the shift ended in 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00 for 5:00 PM).
- **Employee Hours** (`employee_hours`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total hours worked by the employee during the shift.
- **Break Hours** (`break_hours`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total hours taken as breaks during the shift.
- **Break Minutes** (`break_minutes`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total minutes taken as breaks during the shift.
- **Pay Rate** (`pay_rate`, `number`, required: false): Enter the employee's pay rate per hour.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely to prevent errors in payroll and scheduling.
- If an employee has taken breaks, record the total minutes instead of hours to account for partial hours.
- If an employee's pay rate changes, update the form with the new rate to reflect accurate pay calculations.
- Save and submit the form to store the shift details for reference and future use.
