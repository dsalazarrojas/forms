# Duty Sheet For Missed Services - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Duty Sheet For Missed Services form is used to report instances where services were not provided as planned and to detail the steps that will be taken to rectify the situation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the staff member who missed the services.
2. Enter the date when the missed services occurred.
3. Provide a brief reason for the missed services.
4. Describe any follow-up actions that will be taken.
5. Enter the date and time when the follow-up actions will take place.
6. Enter any additional comments from the staff member.
7. Enter any comments from the supervisor.
8. Enter the name of the staff member to whom the missed services were assigned.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Missed Services**:
  * A brief description of the services that were missed.
* **Staff**:
  * Select the staff member who missed the services.
* **Date**:
  * Enter the date when the missed services occurred.
* **Reason**:
  * A brief explanation for why the services were missed.
* **Follow-up Actions**:
  * A description of the steps that will be taken to rectify the situation.
* **Follow-up Date**:
  * Enter the date and time when the follow-up actions will take place.
* **Follow-up Time**:
  * Enter the time when the follow-up actions will take place.
* **Supervisor Report**:
  * A summary of the situation and any actions taken.
* **Staff Comments**:
  * Any additional comments from the staff member.
* **Supervisor Comments**:
  * Any comments from the supervisor.
* **Assigned To**:
  * The name of the staff member to whom the missed services were assigned.
