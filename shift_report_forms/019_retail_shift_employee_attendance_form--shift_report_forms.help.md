# Retail Shift Employee Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Shift Employee Attendance Form is a tool used to record and track employee attendance during their shifts. It helps in maintaining accurate records of attendance, hours worked, and other relevant shift details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name and manager name in the respective fields.
2. Enter the attendance date.
3. Record your start and end times.
4. Report the hours you've worked and any overtime hours.
5. Indicate your employee status (Regular, Overtime, Vacation, or Sick Leave).
6. Select whether you worked on the shift or not (Yes or No).
7. Add any additional notes about your attendance.
8. Fill in the shift location and shift date.
9. Record the shift start and end times.
10. Enter the duration of the shift.
11. Select the type of shift you were on (Retail, Administrative, Training, or Meeting).
12. Add any personal notes about your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your direct supervisor or manager.
* **Location ID**: This is not a required field but enter it if you are filling out the form from a specific location.
* **Attendance Date**: This is the date on which you attended the shift.
* **Start Time**: Enter the time you started your shift.
* **End Time**: Enter the time you ended your shift.
* **Hours Worked**: Record the total number of hours you worked during the shift.
* **Overtime Hours**: If you worked more than your scheduled hours, record the extra hours here.
* **Employee Status**: Select your current status from the given options (Regular, Overtime, Vacation, or Sick Leave).
* **Shift ID**: Select 'Yes' if you attended the shift or 'No' if you did not.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your attendance.
* **Shift Location**: Enter the specific location where you attended the shift.
* **Shift Date**: This is the date of the shift you are reporting on.
* **Shift Start Time**: Enter the start time of the shift.
* **Shift End Time**: Enter the end time of the shift.
* **Shift Duration**: Record the duration of the shift.
* **Shift Type**: Select the type of shift you attended (Retail, Administrative, Training, or Meeting).
* **Employee Notes**: Add any personal comments or notes about your attendance.

## Tips

- Double-check the form for accuracy before submitting.
- Review the guidelines and procedures set by your manager or HR department.
- Fill out the form honestly and thoroughly.
- If unsure about any field or procedure, consult your manager or HR representative.
