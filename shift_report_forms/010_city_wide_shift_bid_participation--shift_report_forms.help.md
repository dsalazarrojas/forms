# City Wide Shift Bid Participation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "City Wide Shift Bid Participation" form is designed to collect employee information and preferences for shift bidding within the city's various departments. This form helps to streamline the shift bid process, ensuring that employees can accurately submit their preferences and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Page 1: Employee Information**
    *   Ensure that you have the following information ready:
        +   Employee ID
        +   Full Name
        +   Department
        +   Current Division or Station
        +   Position Title
        +   Email Address
    *   Enter your Employee ID, Full Name, and Department in the respective fields.
    *   Choose your Current Division or Station from the provided options.
    *   Enter your Position Title.
    *   Enter your Email Address.
2.  **Page 2: Shift Preferences**
    *   Rank your preferred shifts by selecting one of the options for First, Second, and Third Choice Shifts.
    *   For each shift preference, choose from the options:
        +   Morning - 6am-2pm
        +   Afternoon - 2pm-10pm
        +   Night - 10pm-6am
        +   Rotating
        +   Flexible
    *   If you have no preference, select 'No preference'
3.  **Page 3: Schedule Type and Weekend Availability**
    *   Choose your preferred schedule type:
        +   5 day work week
        +   4 day work week - 10 hour shifts
        +   4 day work week - 12 hour shifts
        +   No preference
    *   Select your weekend work availability:
        +   Willing to work weekends
        +   Prefer to avoid weekends
        +   No preference
4.  **Page 4: Special Requests and Constraints**
    *   Enter any special requests or constraints you may have, such as medical or family needs.
5.  **Page 5: Additional Information**
    *   List any dates you will be unavailable due to vacation, leave, or other reasons.

*   **First Choice Shift** (`shift_preference_1`, `select_one`, required): Select your first choice shift preference from the options.
*   **Second Choice Shift** (`shift_preference_2`, `select_one`, required): Select your second choice shift preference from the options.
*   **Third Choice Shift** (`shift_preference_3`, `select_one`, optional): Select your third choice shift preference from the options. (Only required if you have a specific preference)
*   **Location or Division Preferences** (`location_preferences`, `text`, optional): Enter any location or division preferences you may have.
*   **Preferred Schedule Type** (`schedule_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred schedule type from the options.
