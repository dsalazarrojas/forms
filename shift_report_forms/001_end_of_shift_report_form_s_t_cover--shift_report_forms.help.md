# End Of Shift Report Form S&T Cover - Help Guide
## Purpose
The End Of Shift Report Form S&T Cover is used to record employee work information, providing details such as shift summary, work periods, notes, and time-related information to help manage employee hours and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information, following all instructions and guidelines provided.
2. Select the correct work period from the options provided for the "Work Periods" field.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments about your shift in the "Notes" field.
4. Record your time worked, including shift start and end times, break start and end times, and overtime start and end times.
5. If applicable, indicate any late start or late end times.
6. Calculate your total work hours, including overtime and break hours, in the "Work Hours", "Overtime Hours", and "Break Duration" fields.
7. Enter your employee name and ID in the respective fields.
8. Enter your supervisor's name and ID in the respective fields.
9. Sign the "Employee Signature" and "Supervisor Signature" fields to confirm completion and review of the form.
10. Date and time-stamp the "Date Submitted" field with the date and time you completed the form.
