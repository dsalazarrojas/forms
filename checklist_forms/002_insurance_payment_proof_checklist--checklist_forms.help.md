# Insurance Payment Proof Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Payment Proof Checklist form is used to gather information about client payments related to insurance claims. This form is used to record the payment proof for clients who have made payments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, enter the client details in the "Client Details" field.
2. Enter the payment details in the "Payment Details" field.
3. Enter the details about the insurer in the "Insurer" field.
4. Enter the policy number related to the payment in the "Policy Number" field.
5. If the claim is active, enter the claim details in the "Claim Details" field.
6. Enter the date of the payment in the "Payment Proof Date" field.
7. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
8. Select the current claim status from the "Claim Status" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details**: Enter the client's name and any other relevant information.
* **Payment Details**: Enter the payment method and any other payment details.
* **Insurer**: Enter the name of the insurer who made the payment.
* **Policy Number**: Enter the policy number related to the payment.
* **Claim Details**: Enter the claim details, including the claim type and date.
* **Payment Proof Date**: Enter the date of the payment using the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount.
* **Claim Status**: Select the current status of the claim from the dropdown menu:
	+ Active: The claim is still active.
	+ Inactive: The claim is no longer active.
	+ Cancelled: The claim has been cancelled.
