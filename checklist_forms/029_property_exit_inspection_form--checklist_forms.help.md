# Property Exit Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Property Exit Inspection Form is used to document the condition of a property after an inspection. It captures essential details such as the names, contact information, and any relevant notes from the property inspector.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Ensure you are accessing this form to submit information after an inspection has been conducted.
2.  Review the form fields to understand what each one requires.
3.  Fill out the form accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **First name** (text, not required): Enter the first name of the person who will be contacted for this property.
*  **Last name** (text, not required): Enter the last name of the person who will be contacted for this property.
*  **Email** (email, not required): If you have an email address for the person who will be contacted, enter it here.
*  **Phone** (text, not required): If you have the phone number of the person who will be contacted, enter it here.
*  **Notes** (note, not required): Add any additional comments or observations that will help in tracking the property's condition after the inspection.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled out accurately to provide a complete record of the property's condition after inspection.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the relevant person who conducted the inspection.
- This form is not for general property inquiries; it's intended for inspection-related activities only.
- Do not fill out this form for properties you do not have permission to access.
