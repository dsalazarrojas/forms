# Commercial Humidifier Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide service technicians in performing regular maintenance checks on commercial humidifiers. The goal is to ensure that all necessary checks are performed and documented to maintain optimal performance and reliability.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name and contact information at the beginning of the form.
2. Record the date of maintenance performed.
3. Fill in the facility name and address.
4. Specify the humidifier's location within the facility.
5. Enter the humidifier model and serial number.
6. Complete the visual inspection section by checking the exterior condition, leaks, and corrosion.
7. Check the water system by evaluating the filter status, water level, drain line, and water quality.
8. Inspect electrical components, including connections, control panel, and sensors functionality.
9. Inspect mechanical components, including the fan motor, belt condition, and bearings.
10. Perform performance checks, including humidity output, noise level, and energy consumption.
11. Record the maintenance performed, including filter replacement, system cleaning, and parts replaced.
12. Enter the next maintenance due date and technician's notes and signature.

## Tips

* Always review the previous maintenance records before performing maintenance to ensure that this is not a duplicate entry.
* Check the humidifier's user manual for any specific maintenance requirements.
* If you're unsure about any aspect of the maintenance, do not hesitate to consult with the facility manager or previous servicing technician.
* Keep the form clean and organized as you work through it to ensure accuracy and clarity.
