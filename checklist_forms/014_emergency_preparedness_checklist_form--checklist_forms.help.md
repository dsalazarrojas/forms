# Emergency Preparedness Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool to help you prepare for emergencies by listing the items you have in your emergency kit and providing contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and fill out the required fields.
2. In the "Checklist_items" field, select all the items you have in your emergency kit by clicking on the checkboxes. You can select multiple items.
3. In the "Category" field, select the location where your emergency kit is located (Home, Office, Car, Outdoor, or Other).
4. Fill out the contact information fields, including "Contact_name", "Contact_phone", "Contact_email", and "Emergency_contact". This will help emergency services contact you in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional):
	+ This field is a brief introduction to the form. You can describe the purpose of the form and any other relevant information.
* **Checklist_items** (`checklist_items`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all the items you have in your emergency kit by clicking on the checkboxes. The available items are:
		+ First Aid Kit
		+ Flashlight
		+ Fire Extinguisher
		+ Battery
		+ Water
		+ Food
		+ Radio
		+ First Aid Manual
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional):
	+ This field is for any additional notes or comments you want to add to your emergency kit.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the location where your emergency kit is located:
		+ Home
		+ Office
		+ Car
		+ Outdoor
		+ Other
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the person who should be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of the person who should be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter an emergency phone number that can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email address of the person who should be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the person who should be contacted in case of an emergency.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all the items you have in your emergency kit.
* Be sure to fill out all the required fields.
* If you have any additional notes or comments, use the "Notes" field.
* If you have any further questions, contact your emergency services representative.
