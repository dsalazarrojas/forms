# Machine Guarding Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to aid in ensuring the safety and efficiency of machine guarding in a workplace. Machine guarding is a set of measures and procedures aimed at preventing or mitigating hazards associated with machinery, such as crushing, cutting, or shearing. This form will help you evaluate the effectiveness of your current machine guarding practices and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and fill in the corresponding fields with information about your machine guarding practices.
2. Ensure you have a thorough understanding of the questions and options before answering.
3. Enter your answers in a clear and concise manner.
4. Review your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.
