# Fire Extinguisher Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on fire extinguishers in a location, including their location, type, status, tag, last inspected date, and assigned person. This helps to maintain up-to-date records and ensures that fire safety information is accurate and accessible.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling in the **Fire Extenguisher Location** field. Enter the exact location of the fire extinguisher, such as "hallway" or "kitchen area".
2.  Next, select the **Fire Extinguisher Type**. This could be "Automatic", "Standpipe", or "Manual". If unsure, please research or consult with fire safety experts.
3.  In the **Fire Extinguisher Status** field, choose all that apply from the options "Functional", "Non-functional", and "Missing".
4.  In the **Fire Extinguisher Tag** field, enter any identifying tags or labels on the fire extinguisher.
5.  Enter the **Fire Extinguisher Last Inspected Date** by using the date picker. This is the date it was last inspected or maintained.
6.  In the **Assigned Person** field, enter the name of the person responsible for the fire extinguisher or the person who last inspected it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fire Extenguisher Location**: Enter the location of the fire extinguisher, such as "hallway" or "kitchen area".
* **Fire Extinguisher Type**: Select the type of fire extinguisher, which could be "Automatic", "Standpipe", or "Manual". 
* **Fire Extinguisher Status**: Choose all that apply from the options "Functional", "Non-functional", and "Missing". This field allows you to report the status of the fire extinguisher.
* **Fire Extinguisher Tag**: Enter any identifying tags or labels on the fire extinguisher.
* **Fire Extinguisher Last Inspected Date**: Use the date picker to enter the date it was last inspected or maintained. This is the date of the last inspection or maintenance.
* **Assigned Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the fire extinguisher or the person who last inspected it.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure up-to-date records.
* If unsure of any field, research or consult with fire safety experts.
* This form does not require any specific prior knowledge or experience.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
