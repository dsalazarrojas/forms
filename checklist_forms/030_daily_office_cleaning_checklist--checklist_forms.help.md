# Daily Office Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Office Cleaning Checklist is a form used to track and manage office cleaning tasks and schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "cleaning_items" field by selecting the items that need to be cleaned for the day.
2. Enter the "cleaning_frequency" in the text field to indicate how often the office cleaning is to be done.
3. Select the "office_hours" field to enter the time when the office is open.
4. Choose the "assigned_user" field to select the user assigned to perform the cleaning task.
5. Enter the "assigned_date" and "assigned_time" fields to schedule the cleaning task.
6. Select the "assigned_tool" field to choose the tool to be used for the cleaning task.
7. Enter any additional notes in the "notes" field for reference.
8. Ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **office_cleaning_checklist** (Text, Required/Optional): A field to enter a brief description of the cleaning schedule for the office.
* **cleaning_items** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the items that need to be cleaned, e.g., 'Yes' for dusting, 'No' for polishing, etc.
* **cleaning_frequency** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the frequency of office cleaning (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
* **office_hours** (Time, Required): Enter the time when the office is open.
* **assigned_user** (Select One, Required): Choose the user assigned to perform the cleaning task.
* **created_at** (Date, Required/Optional): Date when the form was created.
* **updated_at** (Date, Required/Optional): Date when the form was last updated.
* **completed_at** (Date, Required/Optional): Date when the form was completed.
* **assigned_tool** (Select One, Required): Choose the tool to be used for the cleaning task.
* **notes** (Note, Required/Optional): Enter any additional notes for reference.
* **email** (Email, Required/Optional): Enter the email address of the assigned user.
* **phone** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the phone number of the assigned user.
* **office_location** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the office location.
* **office_name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the office name.
* **office_type** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the type of office (e.g., commercial, residential, etc.).
* **office_hours_start** (Time, Required/Optional): Enter the start time of the office hours.
* **office_hours_end** (Time, Required/Optional): Enter the end time of the office hours.
* **office_hours** (Time, Required/Optional): Enter the office hours.
* **assigned_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date when the cleaning task is assigned.
* **assigned_time** (Time, Required/Optional): Enter the time when the cleaning task is assigned.
* **assigned_day** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the day when the cleaning task is assigned.
* **assigned_shift** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the assigned shift.
* **assigned_team** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the assigned team.
* **assigned_user_name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the assigned user.
* **assigned_note** (Note, Required/Optional): Enter any additional notes for reference.
