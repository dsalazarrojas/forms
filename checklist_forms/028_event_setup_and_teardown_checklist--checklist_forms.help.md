# Event Setup And Teardown Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Setup And Teardown Checklist is a comprehensive form designed to guide event organizers and participants through the essential steps for setting up and tearing down events. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose, completion instructions, and each field's explanation to ensure a clear understanding of its use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for your event's setup and teardown tasks.
2. Provide any general notes or comments regarding your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Setup** (`event_setup_page`, text, required/optional): This field is used to describe the event setup page where you can note any specific setup tasks or details required for your event.
* **Tasks (Event Setup)** (`event_setup_page_setup`, select_multiple, required): In this field, select all the setup tasks relevant to your event:
	+ **Setup**: Tasks related to setting up the venue, such as arranging tables and chairs.
	+ **Pre-Event**: Tasks to be completed before the event starts, like sending out invitations or preparing the stage.
* **Event Teardown** (`event_teardown_page`, text, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments related to the event teardown.
* **Tasks (Event Teardown)** (`event_teardown_page_teardown`, select_multiple, required): Choose the teardown tasks relevant to your event:
	+ **Pre-Event**: Tasks to be completed after the event is finished, like sending follow-up emails or storing event assets.
	+ **Cleanup**: Tasks for cleaning up the venue and resources after the event.
* **General Notes** (`general_notes_page`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your event setup and teardown process.
