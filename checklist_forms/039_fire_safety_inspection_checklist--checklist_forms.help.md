# fire_safety_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the results and actions taken during a fire safety inspection. It captures information about the inspection date, location, and findings, as well as the inspector's details and any notes or observations made during the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for each field based on your findings during the inspection.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
3. Provide thorough and detailed notes, if necessary, to support your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fire Safety Checklist** (`fire_safety_checklist`, select_multiple, required: false)
    * This field asks you to confirm if fire safety checks were performed or not.
* **Fire Safety Inspection Date** (`fire_safety_inspection_date`, date, required: false)
    * Record the date of the fire safety inspection.
* **Fire Safety Inspector Signature** (`fire_safety_inspector_signature`, note, required: false)
    * Write your name as the inspector.
* **Fire Safety Inspection Completed** (`fire_safety_inspection_completed`, select_one, required: false)
    * Confirm if the inspection is completed or not.
* **Fire Safety Inspection Notes** (`fire_safety_inspection_notes`, note, required: false)
    * Provide any relevant notes or observations made during the inspection.
* **Fire Safety Inspection Location** (`fire_safety_inspection_location`, text, required: false)
    * Enter the location where the inspection was conducted.
* **Fire Safety Inspector** (`fire_safety_inspector`, select_one, required: false)
    * Select the title or role of the inspector (e.g., Responsible Officer, Supervisor, Director).
* **Fire Safety Inspector Title** (`fire_safety_inspector_title`, select_multiple, required: false)
    * Confirm if the inspector is authorized or not.
* **Fire Safety Inspector Name** (`fire_safety_inspector_name`, text, required: false)
    * Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Fire Safety Inspector Phone** (`fire_safety_inspector_phone`, text, required: false)
    * Enter your phone number as the inspector.
* **Fire Safety Inspector Email** (`fire_safety_inspector_email`, email, required: false)
    * Enter your email address as the inspector.
* **Fire Safety Inspection Location** (`fire_safety_inspection_location`, text, required: false)
    * Enter the location of the fire safety inspection (e.g., building, floor, room).
* **Fire Safety Inspection Location Street** (`fire_safety_inspection_location_street`, text, required: false)
    * Enter the street address of the fire safety inspection location.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Verify the information with the relevant parties before finalizing the form.
* Avoid duplicate or outdated entries to maintain consistency and relevance.
