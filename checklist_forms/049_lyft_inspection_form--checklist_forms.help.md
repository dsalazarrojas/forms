# lyft_inspection_form_checklist_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a vehicle's inspection, including its make, year, model, and condition. It's used to track and update a vehicle's status and history.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the vehicle, including its make, year, model, and current status.
2. Fill out each field accurately and consistently with the actual vehicle conditions.
3. Select the correct options for each field based on the vehicle's characteristics and conditions.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, select_one):
	+ Please select the correct make of the vehicle from the provided options.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number):
	+ Enter a four-digit year for the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text):
	+ Enter the exact model of the vehicle, e.g., "Toyota Camry" or "Honda Civic".
* **Vehicle VIN** (`vehicle_vin`, text):
	+ Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle, a 17-digit code.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, select_multiple):
	+ Select all applicable colors of the vehicle, e.g., "Red", "Blue", "White".
* **Engine Number** (`engine_number`, text):
	+ Enter the engine number or ID of the vehicle.
* **Engine Type** (`engine_type`, select_one):
	+ Select the type of engine the vehicle has, e.g., "Gasoline", "Diesel", "Electric".
* **Transmission** (`transmission`, select_one):
	+ Select the type of transmission the vehicle has, e.g., "Automatic", "Manual", "Semi-Automatic".
* **Fuel Type** (`fuel_type`, select_one):
	+ Select the type of fuel the vehicle uses, e.g., "Gasoline", "Diesel", "Electric".
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, select_multiple):
	+ Select all applicable conditions of the vehicle, e.g., "Good", "Fair", "Poor".
* **Inspection Result** (`inspection_result`, select_multiple):
	+ Select all applicable results of the inspection, e.g., "Pass", "Fail", "Pending".
* **Notes** (`notes`, text):
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes about the vehicle's condition.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text):
	+ Enter your signature as the inspector or person filling out the form.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text):
	+ Enter your name as the inspector or person filling out the form.
* **Inspector Title** (`inspector_title`, text):
	+ Enter your title as the inspector or person filling out the form.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date):
	+ Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time):
	+ Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Mileage** (`mileage`, number):
	+ Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle ID** (`vehicle_id`, text):
	+ Enter a unique identifier for the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`, select_one):
	+ Select the current status of the vehicle, e.g., "Active", "Inactive".
* **Comments** (`comments`, text):
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes about the vehicle's status.
* **Vehicle Status Reason** (`vehicle_status_reason`, text):
	+ Enter the reason for the vehicle's current status.
* **Vehicle Status Comment** (`vehicle_status_comment`, text):
	+ Enter any additional comments about the vehicle's status.
* **Vehicle Status Notes** (`vehicle_status_notes`, text):
	+ Enter any additional notes about the vehicle's status.
* **Vehicle Status Image** (`vehicle_status_image`, text):
	+ Enter a relevant image or file URL about the vehicle's status.

Note: The fields marked as "select_one" or "select_multiple" require you to choose one or multiple options from the provided list. The fields marked as "text" or "number" require you to enter a free-form text or numerical value. The fields marked as "date" or "time" require you to enter a specific date or time in the correct format.
