# Scissor Lift Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document and track the pre-operation safety checks, inspections, and maintenance of scissor lifts. It is intended to ensure that the scissor lift is safe to operate and in compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select all pre-operation checks that have been completed for the scissor lift.
2. Enter the inspection date for the scissor lift.
3. Enter the location where the scissor lift is being operated.
4. Select the scissor lift number from the options provided.
5. Select all safety certifications that are required for the scissor lift.
6. Select all operational checks that have been completed for the scissor lift.
7. Indicate if the inspection has been completed.
8. Enter any additional notes about the scissor lift.
9. Enter the name of the assigned inspector.
10. Enter the name of the assigned tool.
11. Enter the location where the inspection was performed.
12. Enter the name of the site manager.
13. Enter the name of the site inspector.
14. Enter the make of the scissor lift.
15. Enter the model of the scissor lift.
16. Enter the year of the scissor lift.
17. Enter the capacity of the scissor lift.
18. Enter the height of the scissor lift.
19. Enter the weight of the scissor lift.
20. Enter the type of scissor lift.
21. Enter the condition of the scissor lift.
22. Enter the name of the site.
23. Enter the date of the last inspection.
24. Enter the name of the person who last inspected the scissor lift.
25. Enter the date of the next due inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre-Operation-Check** (`pre_operation_check`, select_multiple, required): This field is used to select all pre-operation checks that have been completed for the scissor lift. Pre-operation checks are essential to ensure that the scissor lift is safe to operate.
* **Inspection-Date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date of the inspection. If the scissor lift has never been inspected, please enter "None".
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the location where the scissor lift is being operated.
* **Scissor-Lift-Number** (`scissor_lift_number`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the scissor lift number from the options provided.
* **Safety-Certifications** (`safety_certifications`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to select all safety certifications that are required for the scissor lift. Please ensure that all certifications are up-to-date.
* **Operational-Check** (`operational_check`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to select all operational checks that have been completed for the scissor lift.
* **Inspection-Completed** (`inspection_completed`, select_one, optional): This field is used to indicate if the inspection has been completed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This field is used to enter any additional notes about the scissor lift.
* **Assigned-Inspector** (`assigned_inspector`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the assigned inspector.
* **Assigned-Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the assigned tool.
* **Inspection-Location** (`inspection_location`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the location where the inspection was performed.
* **Site-Manager** (`site_manager`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the site manager.
* **Site-Inspector** (`site_inspector`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the site inspector.
* **Scissor-Lift-Make** (`scissor_lift_make`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the make of the scissor lift.
* **Scissor-Lift-Model** (`scissor_lift_model`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the model of the scissor lift.
* **Scissor-Lift-Year** (`scissor_lift_year`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the year of the scissor lift.
* **Scissor-Lift-Capacity** (`scissor_lift_capacity`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the capacity of the scissor lift.
* **Scissor-Lift-Height** (`scissor_lift_height`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the height of the scissor lift.
* **Scissor-Lift-Weight** (`scissor_lift_weight`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the weight of the scissor lift.
* **Scissor-Lift-Type** (`scissor_lift_type`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the type of scissor lift.
* **Scissor-Lift-Condition** (`scissor_lift_condition`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the condition of the scissor lift.
* **Site-Name** (`site_name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the site.
* **Date-Last-Inspected** (`date_last_inspected`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the date of the last inspection.
* **Last-Inspected-By** (`last_inspected_by`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the person who last inspected the scissor lift.
* **Next-Due-Date** (`next_due_date`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date of the next due inspection.

Note:
This form is intended to be used by individuals involved in the operation and maintenance of scissor lifts to ensure that the scissor lift is safe to operate and in compliance with regulatory requirements. Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and consistently to ensure that the scissor lift is properly maintained and inspected.
