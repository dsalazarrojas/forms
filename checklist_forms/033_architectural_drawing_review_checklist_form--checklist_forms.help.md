# Architectural Drawing Review Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Architectural Drawing Review Checklist Form is designed to help reviewers assess and ensure that architectural drawings meet specific standards and requirements. This form helps to identify and document any issues or discrepancies found during the review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the project information (project name, project number, and drawing set name).
2. Confirm if the cover sheet and conventions are complete and accurate.
3. Check if the drawings meet building code requirements and are accessible design compliant.
4. Verify if all necessary dimensions are shown and if the dimensions are clear and legible.
5. Check if all necessary notes and specifications are included.
6. Evaluate if the drawings are coordinated with other disciplines (MEP, etc.).
7. Assess the overall quality of the drawings (excellent, good, fair, or poor).
8. Finally, determine if the drawings are recommended for approval or require revisions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (`project_name`): Enter the name of the project being reviewed.
* **Project Number** (`project_number`): Enter the reference code for the project.
* **Drawing Set Name** (`drawing_set_name`): Specify which set of drawings is being reviewed.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`): Enter the date of the review.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`): Enter the name of the person conducting the review.
* **Cover Sheet and Conventions** (`cover_sheet_complete`): Confirm if the cover sheet and conventions are complete and accurate.
* **Cover Sheet Complete** (`cover_sheet_complete`): Check if the cover sheet is complete and accurate.
* **Title Blocks Consistent** (`title_block_consistent`): Confirm if the title blocks are consistent throughout the drawings.
* **All Dimensions Shown** (`all_dimensions_present`): Check if all necessary dimensions are shown.
* **Dimensions Clear and Legible** (`dimensions_clear`): Verify if the dimensions are clear and legible.
* **All Notes and Specifications** (`notes_specifications`): Confirm if all necessary notes and specifications are included.
* **Coordinated with MEP Drawings** (`mep_coordination`): Check if the drawings are coordinated with MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing) drawings.
* **Coordinated with Structural Drawings** (`structural_coordination`): Check if the drawings are coordinated with structural drawings.
* **Overall Drawing Quality** (`overall_quality`): Assess the overall quality of the drawings (excellent, good, fair, or poor).
* **Recommended for Approval** (`review_approval`): Determine if the drawings are recommended for approval or require revisions.

## Tips
- Double-check the project information to ensure accuracy.
- Verify that all necessary dimensions and notes are included.
- Coordinate with other disciplines (MEP, etc.) to ensure accuracy.
- Assess the overall quality of the drawings to ensure they meet standards and requirements.
