# Daily Pre Operation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather critical information about the equipment, location, and pre-inspection items before starting a pre-operation activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have reviewed the form carefully and understand its purpose.
2. Select all equipment used during the operation from the "Equipment Used" field.
3. Provide the location where the operation will take place in the "Location Used" field.
4. For each pre-inspection item, select "Yes" if it requires attention, and "No" if it does not.
5. In the "Critical Item Issues" field, list any critical issues found during the pre-operation inspection.
6. In the "Pre-Inspection Items" field, list any additional pre-inspection items that need attention.
7. Enter your name as the operator in the "Operator Name" field.
8. Enter the name of the team leader in the "Team Leader Name" field.
9. Record the date and time the form was completed in the "Date Completed" and "Time Completed" fields.
10. Indicate if the form was completed or not by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Completed" field.
11. Indicate if the form was submitted or not by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Submitted" field.
12. Select the status of the equipment (Active or Inactive) in the "Equipment Status" field.
13. Select all additional pre-inspection items from the "Pre-Inspection Items 2" field.
14. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Completed" field to indicate if the form was completed or not.
15. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Submitted" field to indicate if the form was submitted or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Used (Select Multiple)**: Select all equipment used during the operation from the provided options.
* **Location Used (Text)**: Provide the location where the operation will take place.
* **Equipment Condition (Select One)**: Select the condition of the equipment (Yes or No).
* **Pre-Inspection Items (Text)**: List any pre-inspection items that need attention.
* **Critical Item Issues (Text)**: List any critical issues found during the pre-operation inspection.
* **Comments (Note)**: Provide any additional comments or notes.
* **Date Completed (Date)**: Record the date the form was completed.
* **Time Completed (Time)**: Record the time the form was completed.
* **Operator Name (Text)**: Enter your name as the operator.
* **Team Leader Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the team leader.
* **Form Completed By (Text)**: Enter the name of the person completing the form.
* **Completed (Select One)**: Indicate if the form was completed or not by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Submitted (Select One)**: Indicate if the form was submitted or not by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Pre-Inspection Items 2 (Select Multiple)**: Select all additional pre-inspection items.
* **Equipment Status (Select One)**: Select the status of the equipment (Active or Inactive).
* **Completed (Select One)**: Indicate if the form was completed or not by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Submitted (Select One)**: Indicate if the form was submitted or not by selecting "Yes" or "No".
