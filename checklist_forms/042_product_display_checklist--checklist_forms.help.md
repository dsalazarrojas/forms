# product_display_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about a product, including its store name, product name, type, category, subcategory, brand name, condition, price, date, time, note, email, phone number, follow-up date, and follow-up time. This information will help you provide accurate data for further processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the store name where the product is sold.
2. Enter the product name.
3. Select the product type from the given options.
4. Enter the category of the product.
5. Enter the subcategory of the product.
6. Enter the brand name of the product.
7. Select the condition of the product.
8. Enter the price of the product.
9. Enter the date the product was or will be available.
10. Enter the time the product was or will be available.
11. Enter any additional notes about the product.
12. Enter the email address associated with the product.
13. Enter the phone number associated with the product.
14. Enter the follow-up date for the product.
15. Enter the follow-up time for the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Name (store_name)**: Enter the name of the store where the product is sold.
* **Product Name (product_name)**: Enter the name of the product.
* **Product Type (product_type)**: Select the type of product from the given options.
* **Category (category)**: Enter the category of the product.
* **Subcategory (subcategory)**: Enter the subcategory of the product.
* **Brand Name (brand_name)**: Enter the brand name of the product.
* **Condition (condition)**: Select the condition of the product from the given options.
* **Price (price)**: Enter the price of the product.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date the product was or will be available.
* **Time (time)**: Enter the time the product was or will be available.
* **Note (note)**: Enter any additional notes about the product.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the email address associated with the product.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter the phone number associated with the product.
* **Follow-up Date (follow_up_date)**: Enter the follow-up date for the product.
* **Follow-up Time (follow_up_time)**: Enter the follow-up time for the product.

## Tips

* Double-check the product information to ensure accuracy.
* Be precise when entering dates and times.
* Use the provided options for product type and condition to make it easier to complete the form.
* If you need to add or remove any fields, please consult with the form designer or contact the support team.
