### New Year Financial Planning Checklist - Help Guide

### Purpose

The New Year Financial Planning Checklist is a tool to help users create a comprehensive financial plan for the upcoming year. It's designed to cover essential aspects of personal finance, including objectives, goals, income, expenses, financial institutions, risk management, and budgeting.

### How To Complete This Form

1. **Review each field carefully**, ensuring you've considered all aspects of your financial planning.
2. **Select "New Year Financial Planning Checklist" from the form options.**
3. **Complete the form by filling out each field.**

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Year Financial Planning Checklist**: This field is a text input for the user to title their financial plan. It's not a required field.
* **Objectives**: Select all applicable objectives for the upcoming year. Choose from: *objective 1*, *objective 2*, and *objective 3*. This field allows for multiple selections.
* **Goals**: Select the primary goal for the upcoming year. Choose from: *goal 1*, *goal 2*. This field allows for a single selection.
* **Income**: Enter your estimated annual income.
* **Expenses**: Enter your estimated annual expenses.
* **Financial Institutions**: Select all applicable financial institutions you'll be using. Choose from: *institution 1*, *institution 2*. This field allows for multiple selections.
* **Risk Management**: Select your primary risk management approach for the upcoming year. Choose from: *risk 1*, *risk 2*. This field allows for a single selection.
* **Budget**: Enter your estimated annual budget.

By following this guide, you'll be able to create a comprehensive financial plan for the upcoming year, tailored to your specific needs and goals.
