> # Groundwork Confirmation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Groundwork Confirmation Checklist is a general-purpose form designed to confirm the readiness of a site or location for various groundwork activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Enter your response to each question in the designated field.
3. Check the "required" fields, as they must be completed before proceeding.
4. Once you have filled out all the required fields, review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy.
5. Submit the form when you are confident that all tasks have been completed and the site is ready for the next stage of work.
