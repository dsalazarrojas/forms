# Flooring Installation Instructions Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form provides instructions on flooring installation projects, including the type of flooring, area, room selection, and installation details.

## How To Complete This Form
Follow the instructions above to complete this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Flooring Type**: This field determines the type of flooring being installed. Choose from the options provided.
* **Flooring Area**: Enter the total area of the flooring project in square feet.
* **Room Selection**: Select all rooms where the flooring will be installed.
* **Room Count**: Enter the total number of rooms with new flooring.
* **Flooring Length**: Enter the length of the flooring in feet.
* **Installation Type**: Choose the method of installation.
* **Start Date**: Enter the estimated start date of the project.
* **End Date**: Enter the estimated end date of the project.
* **Installer Name**: Enter the name of the installer or contractor.
* **Installer Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the installer.
* **Project Lead**: Enter the name of the project lead or contact person.
* **Site Contact**: Enter the name of the site contact person.
* **Site Phone**: Enter the phone number of the site contact person.
* **Site Email**: Enter the email address of the site contact person.
* **Installation Notes**: Add any additional installation notes or details for the project.
* **Completion Notes**: Add any completion notes or details for the project.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the installer has enough information for the project.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, contact the installer directly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it for accuracy and completeness.
