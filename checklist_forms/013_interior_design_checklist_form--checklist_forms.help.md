# interior_design_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the project's interior design preferences and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting a project title and entering its budget (if applicable).
2. Choose the preferred design style (e.g. Modern, Contemporary).
3. Select the color scheme that suits your project (e.g. Warm, Cool, Neutral).
4. Provide details about the room layout, such as the room's dimensions and furniture arrangement.
5. Enter your material preferences, such as the materials you would like to use or avoid.
6. Choose the space planning approach that suits your project (e.g. Open Plan, Closed Plan, Semi-Open).
7. Describe your design process, including any specific design goals or requirements.
8. Set a deadline or timeline for the project completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Preferences** (`project_preferences`, text, required: false): This field is optional, but entering a project title will help us understand the context and scope of the project.
* **project_budget** (`project_budget`, number, required: false): Enter the budget for the project. This will help us determine the resources and materials needed.
* **Design Goals** (`design_goals`, text, required: false): Describe the main goals and objectives of the design project.
* **Design Style** (`design_style`, select_one, required: false): Choose the design style that best fits your project's aesthetic and functionality needs (e.g. Modern, Contemporary).
* **Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more color schemes that you would like to use in the project (e.g. Warm, Cool, Neutral).
* **Room Layout** (`room_layout`, text, required: false): Describe the room's dimensions, layout, and furniture arrangement.
* **Material Preferences** (`material_preferences`, text, required: false): Enter any specific materials or finishes you would like to use or avoid.
* **Space Planning** (`space_planning`, select_one, required: false): Choose the space planning approach that suits your project (e.g. Open Plan, Closed Plan, Semi-Open).
* **Design Process** (`design_process`, text, required: false): Describe your approach to the design process, including any specific goals or requirements.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, date, required: false): Set a deadline or timeline for the project completion.

## Tips
* Make sure to be as specific as possible when answering each field to ensure that we can accurately understand your project's needs.
* If you have any additional information or requirements that are not captured in this form, please provide it in the comments section.
* This form is meant to be a starting point for the design process, and your input will help us create a tailored design solution that fits your project's unique needs.
