# School Bus Driver’s Daily Pre Trip Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report the status of a school bus vehicle before a trip, ensuring that it is in good working condition and safe for transportation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the driver’s details, including their name and vehicle license number.
2. Enter the current odometer reading of the vehicle.
3. Report the current fuel level of the vehicle.
4. Select the status of each vehicle component, such as brakes, steering, and tires.
5. Report any other issues or concerns with the vehicle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Bus Driver’s Details**: Enter your name and any other relevant details as required.
* **Vehicle License Number**: Enter the license number of the school bus vehicle you are driving.
* **Year**: Enter the current year of the vehicle.
* **Make**: Enter the make of the school bus vehicle.
* **Model**: Enter the model of the school bus vehicle.
* **Odometer Reading**: Enter the current odometer reading of the vehicle, which helps track mileage and maintenance.
* **Fuel Level**: Enter the current fuel level of the vehicle, which helps determine if refueling is necessary.
* **Driver Name**: Enter your name as the driver of the vehicle.
* **License Number**: Enter your driver's license number for identification purposes.
* **Yearly Maintenance Status**: Report the current status of the vehicle's yearly maintenance, which helps track and schedule maintenance.
* **Brakes Status**: Select the status of the brakes, which affects safety on the road:
	+ Good: Brakes are functioning properly.
	+ Fair: Brakes are functioning, but not at optimal condition.
	+ Poor: Brakes are not functioning and need attention.
* **Steering Status**: Select the status of the steering, which affects control and safety on the road:
	+ Good: Steering is functioning properly.
	+ Fair: Steering is functioning, but not at optimal condition.
	+ Poor: Steering is not functioning and needs attention.
* **Tires Status**: Select the status of the tires, which affects safety on the road:
	+ Good: Tires are in good condition and have enough tread.
	+ Fair: Tires are in fair condition, but have limited tread life left.
	+ Poor: Tires are in poor condition and need replacement.
* **Mirrors Status**: Select the status of the mirrors, which affects visibility and safety on the road:
	+ Good: Mirrors are functioning and in good condition.
	+ Fair: Mirrors are functioning, but not at optimal condition.
	+ Poor: Mirrors are not functioning and need attention.
* **Seatbelts Status**: Select the status of the seatbelts, which affects safety on the road:
	+ Good: Seatbelts are functioning properly and securely fastened.
	+ Fair: Seatbelts are functioning, but not at optimal condition.
	+ Poor: Seatbelts are not functioning and need attention.
* **Horn Status**: Select the status of the horn, which is essential for alerting other road users:
	+ Good: Horn is functioning properly and audibly signals its presence.
	+ Fair: Horn is functioning, but not at optimal condition.
	+ Poor: Horn is not functioning and needs attention.
* **Windshield Status**: Select the status of the windshield, which affects visibility on the road:
	+ Good: Windshield is free from cracks or damage.
	+ Fair: Windshield is in fair condition, but has minor cracks or damage.
	+ Poor: Windshield is damaged and affects visibility.
* **Fire Extinguisher Status**: Select the status of the fire extinguisher, which is essential in emergency situations:
	+ Good: Fire Extinguisher is functional and fully charged.
	+ Fair: Fire Extinguisher is functional, but low on charge.
	+ Poor: Fire Extinguisher is not functional and needs replacement or recharging.
* **Fire Alarm Status**: Select the status of the fire alarm, which alerts in case of emergency:
	+ Good: Fire Alarm is functioning properly and alerts in case of emergency.
	+ Fair: Fire Alarm is functioning, but not at optimal condition.
	+ Poor: Fire Alarm is not functioning and needs attention.
* **First Aid Kit Status**: Select the status of the first aid kit, which is essential in emergency situations:
	+ Good: First Aid Kit is available and fully stocked.
	+ Fair: First Aid Kit is available, but not fully stocked.
	+ Poor: First Aid Kit is not available and needs replenishment.
* **Safety Rods Status**: Select the status of the safety rods, which are used for safety and security:
	+ Good: Safety Rods are functional and securely fastened.
	+ Fair: Safety Rods are functional, but not at optimal condition.
	+ Poor: Safety Rods are not functional and need attention.
* **Reflective Device Status**: Select the status of the reflective device, which improves visibility:
	+ Good: Reflective Device is functional and visible from a distance.
	+ Fair: Reflective Device is functional, but not at optimal condition.
	+ Poor: Reflective Device is not functional and needs attention.
* **Fire Extinguisher Tag Status**: Select the status of the fire extinguisher tag, which indicates maintenance or expiration:
	+ Good: Fire Extinguisher Tag is updated and indicates no maintenance required.
	+ Fair: Fire Extinguisher Tag needs maintenance or update.
	+ Poor: Fire Extinguisher Tag is expired and needs replacement.
* **Fire Alarm Tag Status**: Select the status of the fire alarm tag, which indicates maintenance or expiration:
	+ Good: Fire Alarm Tag is updated and indicates no maintenance required.
	+ Fair: Fire Alarm Tag needs maintenance or update.
	+ Poor: Fire Alarm Tag is expired and needs replacement.
* **Other Issues**: Describe any other issues or concerns with the vehicle that were not covered in the previous fields.
