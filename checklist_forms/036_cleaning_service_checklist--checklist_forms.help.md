# Cleaning Service Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a cleaning service provided to a client. It helps track the details of the service, including the client's information, the address and date of the service, and a checklist of tasks completed during the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client's name and contact information in the `client_name` field.
2. Enter the address of the service in the `service_address` field.
3. Enter the date of the service in the `service_date` field.
4. Identify the type of service provided in the `service_type` field.
5. Review the living areas checklist and select the correct completion status for each task.
6. Review the kitchen checklist and select the correct completion status for each task.
7. Review the bathrooms checklist and select the correct completion status for each task.
8. Review the general tasks checklist and select the correct completion status for each task.
9. Enter the start and end times of the service in the `service_start_time` and `service_end_time` fields.
10. Enter the total time spent on the service in the `total_time_spent` field.
11. Rate your satisfaction with the service using the `client_satisfaction` field.
12. Provide any feedback in the `client_comments` field.
13. Sign the form electronically with your full name in the `client_signature` field.
14. Enter your full name for your electronic signature in the `cleaner_signature` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (client_name)**: Enter the name of the client.
* **Service Address (service_address)**: Enter the address where the service was provided.
* **Service Date (service_date)**: Enter the date of the service.
* **Cleaner Name (cleaner_name)**: Enter the name of the cleaner or team member.
* **Service Type (service_type)**: Select the type of service provided (e.g. Regular Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, etc.).
* **Living Room - All Surfaces Dusted (living_room_dusted)**: Check when the living room all surfaces are dusted.
* **Living Room - Floors Vacuumed or Swept (living_room_floors)**: Check when the living room floors are vacuumed or swept.
* **Dining Room - Cleaned (dining_room_cleaned)**: Check when the dining room is cleaned.
* **Bedrooms - Cleaned (bedrooms_cleaned)**: Check when the bedrooms are cleaned.
* **Beds Made (beds_made)**: Check when the beds are made.
* **Kitchen - Counters Cleaned (kitchen_counters)**: Check when the kitchen counters are cleaned.
* **Kitchen - Appliances Cleaned (Exterior) (kitchen_appliances)**: Check when the kitchen appliances are cleaned.
* **Kitchen - Sink Cleaned and Sanitized (kitchen_sink)**: Check when the kitchen sink is cleaned and sanitized.
* **Kitchen - Floors Swept and Mopped (kitchen_floors)**: Check when the kitchen floors are swept and mopped.
* **Kitchen - Cabinet Fronts Wiped (kitchen_cabinets)**: Check when the kitchen cabinet fronts are wiped.
* **Bathrooms - Toilets Cleaned and Disinfected (bathroom_toilets)**: Check when the bathrooms toilets are cleaned and disinfected.
* **Bathrooms - Showers or Tubs Cleaned (bathroom_showers)**: Check when the bathrooms showers or tubs are cleaned.
* **Bathrooms - Sinks Cleaned (bathroom_sinks)**: Check when the bathrooms sinks are cleaned.
* **Bathrooms - Mirrors Cleaned (bathroom_mirrors)**: Check when the bathrooms mirrors are cleaned.
* **Bathrooms - Floors Cleaned (bathroom_floors)**: Check when the bathrooms floors are cleaned.
* **Windows - Interior Glass Cleaned (windows_cleaned)**: Check when the windows are cleaned.
* **Baseboards - Dust and Wipe (baseboards_cleaned)**: Check when the baseboards are dusted and wiped.
* **Light Switches and Handles - Disinfected (light_switches)**: Check when the light switches and handles are disinfected.
* **Trash - All Bins Emptied (trash_removed)**: Check when the trash is emptied.
* **All Carpets - Vacuumed (all_floors_vacuumed)**: Check when all carpets are vacuumed.
* **All Hard Floors - Mopped (all_hard_floors)**: Check when all hard floors are mopped.
* **Service Start Time (service_start_time)**: Enter the time the service began.
* **Service End Time (service_end_time)**: Enter the time the service ended.
* **Total Time Spent (total_time_spent)**: Enter the total time spent on the service.
* **Client Satisfaction Rating (client_satisfaction)**: Rate your satisfaction with the service.
* **Client Comments (client_comments)**: Provide any feedback about the service.
* **Client Signature (client_signature)**: Sign the form electronically with your full name.
* **Cleaner Signature (cleaner_signature)**: Enter your full name for your electronic signature.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you complete each task accurately and truthfully.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are complete and accurate.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your cleaner or supervisor for guidance.
