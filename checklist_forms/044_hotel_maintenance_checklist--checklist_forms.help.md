# Hotel Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Maintenance Checklist is a tool used to track and record maintenance activities and schedules for hotel facilities and equipment. It helps ensure that regular maintenance tasks are performed and documented in a timely manner, minimizing the risk of equipment malfunctions and operational disruptions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of maintenance (yes or no) for each item on the list.
2. Choose the maintenance schedule (yes or no) for each item.
3. Enter the item being maintained, if applicable.
4. Select the due date for the maintenance.
5. Select the status of the maintenance (active or inactive).
6. Record any additional notes or comments, if applicable.
7. Update the tool status (active or inactive).
8. Update the date and time when the tool was last updated.
9. Record the last updated by name, if applicable.
10. Record the last updated by email, if applicable.
11. Record the last updated by phone number, if applicable.
12. Add any additional notes or comments for the maintenance item, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Type**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that the maintenance type is active, and 'No' to indicate it is inactive.
* **Maintenance Schedule**: Select multiple options to schedule the maintenance tasks.
* **Hotel Maintenance Item**: Enter a brief description of the item being maintained.
* **Due Date**: Enter the date by which the maintenance task should be completed.
* **Maintenance Status**: Select 'Active' to indicate that the maintenance is currently being performed, and 'Inactive' to indicate it has been completed or cancelled.
* **Maintenance Description**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activity.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the name and description of the tool or equipment being maintained.
* **Maintenance Item Status**: Select the status of the maintenance item, active or inactive.
* **Maintenance Item Priority**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that the maintenance item is high priority, and 'No' to indicate it is low priority.
* **Maintenance Item Status History**: Enter the history of the status changes for the maintenance item.
* **Maintenance Due**: Enter the date by which the maintenance task should be completed.
* **Maintenance Start**: Enter the date and time when the maintenance task began.
* **Maintenance End**: Enter the date and time when the maintenance task was completed.
* **Tool Status**: Select 'Active' to indicate that the tool is currently in use, and 'Inactive' to indicate it is not in use.
* **Tool Last Updated**: Record the date and time when the tool was last updated.
* **Tool Last Updated By**: Record the name of the person who last updated the tool.
* **Tool Last Updated By Email**: Record the email address of the person who last updated the tool.
* **Tool Last Updated By Phone**: Record the phone number of the person who last updated the tool.
* **Maintenance Item Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the maintenance item.
* **Maintenance Item Note Read Only**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the maintenance item that should not be edited.
* **Maintenance Item Note Last Updated**: Record the date and time when the note was last updated.
* **Maintenance Item Note Last Updated By**: Record the name of the person who last updated the note.
* **Maintenance Item Note Last Updated By Name**: Record the name of the person who last updated the note.
* **Maintenance Item Note Last Updated By Email**: Record the email address of the person who last updated the note.
* **Maintenance Item Note Last Updated By Phone**: Record the phone number of the person who last updated the note.

## Tips

* Ensure that the maintenance schedule is followed carefully to prevent operational disruptions.
* Update the tool status and last updated information regularly to ensure accuracy.
* Record accurate and clear notes and comments for each maintenance activity.
* Ensure that high-priority items are addressed promptly.
