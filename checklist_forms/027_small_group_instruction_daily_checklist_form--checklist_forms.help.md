# small_group_instruction_daily_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture daily information about small group instruction activities. It includes fields for small group names, dates, instructional activities, student participants, materials needed, teacher notes, and student participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the small group in the `Small Group Name` field.
2. Select the date of the instruction session from the calendar in the `Date` field.
3. Answer the question about whether the instructional activities took place by selecting one of the options in the `Instructional Activities` field.
4. Answer the question about whether students participated by selecting one of the options in the `Student Participants` field.
5. If necessary, select the materials that were needed for the instruction session by choosing one or multiple options in the `Materials Needed` field.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments about the session in the `Teacher Notes` field.
7. Answer the question about whether students participated by selecting one of the options in the `Student Participation` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Small Group Name** (group_name, text, required): Enter the name of the small group that received the instruction.
* **Date** (date, date, required): Select the date of the instruction session.
* **Instructional Activities** (instructional_activities, select_multiple, optional): Answer whether the activities were instructional by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Student Participants** (student_participants, select_multiple, optional): Answer whether students participated by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Materials Needed** (materials_needed, select_multiple, optional): If necessary, select the materials that were needed for the instruction session.
* **Lesson Plan** (lesson_plan, select_one, optional): Answer whether a lesson plan was used by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Teacher Notes** (teacher_notes, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the session.
* **Student Participation** (student_participation, select_multiple, optional): Answer whether students participated by selecting one of the options.
