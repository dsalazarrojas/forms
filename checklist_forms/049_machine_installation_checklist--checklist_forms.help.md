# Machine Installation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide individuals through the process of installing machines, ensuring that all necessary steps are taken to ensure a successful and safe installation. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by providing a site survey, describing the location where the machine will be installed.
2. Confirm that the site is prepared for installation, including any necessary preparations or modifications.
3. Confirm the arrival of the machinery and its condition upon delivery.
4. Describe the installation process, following all necessary safety protocols and procedures.
5. Check the electrical connectors and ensure they are properly secured and connected.
6. Perform a test and inspection to ensure the machine is working correctly and safely.
7. Document the installation and test results, including any issues or concerns.
8. Conduct a final check to ensure all steps have been completed and the machine is ready for use.

* **Machinery Arrival** (`machinery_arrival`, text, optional): This field is for confirming the arrival of the machinery and its condition upon delivery. Please describe the machinery and its contents.
* **Installation** (`installation`, text, required): This field is for describing the installation process, following all necessary safety protocols and procedures.
* **Electrical Connectors** (`electrical_connectors`, text, required): This field is for checking the electrical connectors and ensuring they are properly secured and connected.
* **Test and Inspect** (`test_and_inspect`, text, required): This field is for performing a test and inspection to ensure the machine is working correctly and safely.
* **Documentation** (`documentation`, text, optional): This field is for documenting the installation and test results, including any issues or concerns.
* **Final Check** (`final_check`, text, required): This field is for conducting a final check to ensure all steps have been completed and the machine is ready for use.
