The Server Health Checklist Form is used to assess the health of a server, gathering relevant information from IT professionals to help identify potential issues that may affect server performance, reliability, and overall health. This form aims to collect data on the current state of the server, including performance metrics, configuration, and maintenance history. By filling out this form, IT professionals can ensure that their servers are running smoothly and efficiently.

## Purpose

The Server Health Checklist Form is designed to:

*   Collect data on server performance and health
*   Identify potential issues and areas for improvement
*   Record maintenance and upkeep history

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form fields below and fill in the required information.
2.  For each field, provide as much detail as possible to help identify potential issues with your server.
3.  Use the provided fields to describe the current state of your server.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Assess Server Performance** (`it_pros_assess_server_performance`, text, optional): Describe the current performance of your server, including any issues or concerns you've noticed.
*   **Monitor Critical Parameters** (`monitor_critical_parameters`, select_one, optional):
    \*   High CPU Utilization: Indicate if your server is experiencing high CPU utilization.
    \*   Low Disk Space: Confirm if your server is running low on disk space.
    \*   High Memory Usage: Check if your server is experiencing high memory usage.
*   **Ensure Optimal Operational Health** (`ensure_optimal_operational_health`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last maintenance or check-up.
*   **Server Uptime** (`server_uptime`, time, optional): Record the server's current uptime.
*   **CPU Utilization Percentage** (`cpu_utilization_percentage`, number, optional): Enter the current CPU utilization percentage.
*   **Memory Usage Percentage** (`memory_usage_percentage`, number, optional): Enter the current memory usage percentage.
*   **Disk Space Percentage** (`disk_space_percentage`, number, optional): Enter the current disk space percentage.
*   **Network Uptime** (`network_uptime`, time, optional): Record the network uptime.
*   **System Logs** (`system_logs`, text, optional): Describe any system logs or messages that may indicate issues.
*   **IT Professional's Name** (`it_pros_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or title.
*   **IT Professional's Phone Number** (`it_pros_phone_number`, text, optional): Provide your contact phone number.
*   **IT Professional's Email** (`it_pros_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
*   **IT Professional's Comments** (`it_pros_comments`, text, optional): Record any additional comments or feedback.
*   **Date of Last Check** (`date_of_last_check`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last check or maintenance.
*   **Time of Last Check** (`time_of_last_check`, time, optional): Record the time of the last check or maintenance.
*   **Checked by** (`checked_by`, text, optional): Enter the person who last checked or maintained the server.
*   **IT Professional's Department** (`it_pros_department`, text, optional): Select your department.
*   **IT Professional's Title** (`it_pros_title`, select_one, optional):
    \*   IT Team Member
    \*   IT Team Leader
    \*   Senior IT Team Member
*   **IT Professional's Location** (`it_pros_location`, text, optional): Enter your location.
*   **IT Professional's Comments** (`it_pros_comments`, text, optional): Record any additional comments or feedback.
*   **Server Name** (`server_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your server.
*   **Server Model** (`server_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of your server.
*   **Server Version** (`server_version`, text, optional): Enter the version of your server software.
*   **Operating System** (`operating_system`, text, optional): Enter the operating system of your server.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Record any additional comments or feedback.

## Tips

*   Be as detailed and thorough as possible when filling out this form.
*   Use the provided fields to help identify potential issues and areas for improvement.
*   If unsure about any field, refer to your server documentation or seek guidance from a colleague.
