# Roof Maintenance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on the conditions of a roof's maintenance. It is meant to be filled out by users who have access to the roof and can provide an accurate assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1.  Go through the form page by page.
2.  For each field, choose the options that best describe the condition of your roof.
3.  Make sure to answer all fields to ensure a complete assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Roof Conditions** (`roof_conditions`, text, required: false): This field is for providing a general description of the roof's condition. Please provide a concise summary of the current state of the roof.
* **Roof Damage** (`roof_damage`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for identifying areas where the roof may be damaged. Select all options that apply to your roof.
	+ Damaged: There is visible damage to the roof such as cracks, breaks, or holes.
	+ Not Damaged: The roof has no visible damage.
* **Leaky Areas** (`leaky_areas`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for identifying areas where water may be leaking or entering the house. Select all options that apply to your roof.
	+ Leaky Gutters: Gutters are not functioning properly and water is not draining correctly.
	+ Leaky Siding: There is water entering the house through the siding.
	+ Leaky Windows: There is water entering the house through windows.
* **Inspect for Damage** (`inspect_for_damage`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for describing what was found during the inspection. Select all options that apply to your roof.
	+ Damaged: There is visible damage to the roof or building materials.
	+ Not Damaged: No damage was found during the inspection.
* **Repair Status** (`repair_status`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for tracking the status of any repairs. Select all options that apply to your roof.
	+ Done: All necessary repairs have been completed.
	+ In Progress: Repairs are currently being done.
	+ Not Done: No repairs have been started or completed yet.
* **Roof Cleanness** (`roof_cleanness`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for describing the overall cleanness of the roof. Select all options that apply to your roof.
	+ Very Clean: The roof is very clean and free of debris.
	+ Somewhat Clean: The roof has some minor debris, but is generally clean.
	+ Not Clean: The roof is heavily covered in debris or dirt.
* **Roof Condition Comments** (`roof_condition_comments`, text, required: false): This field is for providing any additional comments about the roof condition.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required: false): This is the submit button to submit your assessment.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all fields to provide a complete assessment.
- If you are unsure about any field, please use your best judgment or take your time to inspect the area thoroughly.
- This form is for general maintenance purposes only, and it's not meant to be used for official inspections or certifications.
