# Environmental Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an environmental inspection checklist, which is used to ensure compliance with safety and environmental regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "General Information" page, which includes fields such as the "Facility Name", "Facility Address", and "Inspection Date".
2. Provide the "Inspector Name" and any relevant "General Comments" about the inspection.
3. Identify any "Environmental Hazards" and "Safety Hazards" present during the inspection.
4. Select the "Facility Safety Standards" and provide "Emergency Contact" information.
5. Provide the "Action Planned" and "Action Taken" if applicable.
6. Enter the "Completed By" and "Date Completed" if the inspection is completed.
7. If the inspection is not completed, provide the "Follow Up" information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`General Information`, text, required):
    This field is used to provide general information about the facility being inspected, such as its name and address.
* **Building Address** (`building_address`, text, required):
    This field is used to enter the address of the building being inspected.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required):
    This field is used to enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required):
    This field is used to enter the name of the inspector.
* **Environmental Hazards** (`environmental_hazards`, select_multiple, required):
    This field allows you to identify any environmental hazards present during the inspection. Select all that apply.
* **Safety Hazards** (`safety_hazards`, select_multiple, required):
    This field allows you to identify any safety hazards present during the inspection. Select all that apply.
* **Facility Safety Standards** (`facility_safety_standards`, text, optional):
    This field is used to provide information on the facility's safety standards.
* **General Comments** (`general_comments`, text, required):
    This field is used to enter any relevant comments about the inspection.
* **Action Planned** (`action_planned`, text, optional):
    This field is used to enter any action planned as a result of the inspection.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, text, optional):
    This field is used to enter any action taken as a result of the inspection.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, text, optional):
    This field is used to enter any follow-up information.
* **Completed By** (`completed_by`, text, optional):
    This field is used to enter the name of the person who completed the inspection.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional):
    This field is used to enter the date the inspection was completed.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, optional):
    This field is used to enter the address of the facility.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, optional):
    This field is used to enter the name of the facility.
* **Building Type** (`building_type`, text, optional):
    This field is used to enter the type of building.
* **Number of Floors** (`number_of_floors`, number, optional):
    This field is used to enter the number of floors in the building.
* **Number of Tenants** (`number_of_tenants`, number, optional):
    This field is used to enter the number of tenants in the building.
* **Total Square Footage** (`total_square_footage`, number, optional):
    This field is used to enter the total square footage of the building.
* **Electrical Systems** (`electrical_systems`, select_one, optional):
    This field is used to select the electrical systems present in the building.
* **Plumbing Systems** (`plumbing_systems`, select_one, optional):
    This field is used to select the plumbing systems present in the building.
* **Fire Alarm System** (`fire_alarm_system`, select_one, optional):
    This field is used to select the fire alarm system present in the building.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required):
    This field is used to enter the emergency contact information.
* **Next Inspection Date** (`next_inspection_date`, date, optional):
    This field is used to enter the next inspection date.
* **Last Inspection Date** (`last_inspection_date`, date, optional):
    This field is used to enter the last inspection date.

## Tips
* Ensure you provide thorough and accurate information in all required fields.
* Use the "General Comments" field to provide any relevant information about the inspection.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult the relevant regulations or seek guidance from a qualified inspector.
