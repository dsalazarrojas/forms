# Medication List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medication List form is designed to collect information about a patient's current or past medications. This includes details such as patient name, a list of medications taken, dosage, and dosing frequency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name in the text field provided. This will be used to identify the patient in the medication list.
2. **Medications List**: Select all medications the patient is currently taking or has taken in the past from the multiple-choice list. You can select multiple medications.
3. **Dosage**: Enter the dosage information for each medication selected in the medications list.
4. **Dosing Frequency**: Enter the frequency at which the medication is taken, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: A text field where you can enter the patient's name for reference and identification.
* **Medications List**: A multiple-choice list of common medications where you can select all medications the patient is currently taking or has taken. This field is optional.
* **Dosage**: Enter the dosage amount or unit of measurement for each medication selected in the medications list.
* **Dosing Frequency**: Enter the frequency at which the medication is taken, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.

## Tips
* Double-check the medications list to ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.
* Enter dosage information for each medication listed.
* Be clear and concise when entering dosing frequency.
* If unsure about any medication or its usage, consult with the patient or a healthcare professional.
