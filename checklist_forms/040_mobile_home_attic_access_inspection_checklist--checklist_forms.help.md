# Mobile Home Attic Access Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide inspectors in conducting a thorough inspection of a mobile home's attic, covering essential aspects such as exterior access, floor plan, and insulation condition.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by reviewing the mobile home's exterior access and checking for any obstacles or barriers preventing access to the attic.
2.  Note the floor plan of the attic, including the number of stories.
3.  Report on the size of the attic, whether small, medium, or large.
4.  Record the type of insulation used in the attic, such as fiberglass, cellulose, or foam.
5.  Evaluate the condition of the insulation, whether satisfactory, unsatisfactory, or not evaluated.
6.  Check the thickness of the insulation and determine if it is adequate or inadequate.
7.  Inspect the electrical components in the attic, such as wiring, outlets, and switches, and report if they are adequate or unsatisfactory.
8.  Evaluate the condition of the electrical system, whether satisfactory, unsatisfactory, or not evaluated.
9.  Check for adequate or inadequate venting in the attic.
10.  Assess the condition of the roof, whether satisfactory or unsatisfactory.
11.  Determine if the attic is accessible or not.
12.  Record any additional notes about the attic inspection.
13.  Enter your name and title (if applicable).
14.  Sign and date your inspection report.
