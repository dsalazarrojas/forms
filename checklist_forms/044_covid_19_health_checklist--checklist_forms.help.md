# Covid 19 Health Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Covid-19 Health Checklist is a form designed to collect information from users on their health status and travel history related to COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: General Questions

* Answer the general questions in the first field.

### Step 2: Travel History

* Select your travel history in the second field.
* If you have answered "Yes" to this question, please describe the details of your travel history in the space provided.

### Step 3: Symptoms

* If you are experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms, describe them in the third field.

### Step 4: Contact Details

* Please provide your contact details, including name, phone number, and email in the fourth field.

### Step 5: Travel Status

* Select your current travel status in the fifth field.

### Step 6: Health Status

* Indicate your current health status in the sixth field.

### Step 7: Other Information

* Provide any other relevant information that may be helpful for our reference in the seventh field.

### Step 8: Submission Date

* Enter the date and time of submission in the eighth field.

### Step 9: Submitter Information

* Please provide your name, title, and organization as the submitter in the ninth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Questions**: Answer the following general questions to provide more information.
* **Travel History**: Select your travel history for the past [insert time frame].
* **Symptoms**: If you are experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms, describe them here.
* **Contact Details**: Please provide your contact details, including name, phone number, and email.
* **Travel Status**: Select your current travel status.
* **Health Status**: Indicate your current health status.
* **Other Information**: Provide any other relevant information that may be helpful for our reference.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Submitter Information**: Please provide your name, title, and organization as the submitter.
