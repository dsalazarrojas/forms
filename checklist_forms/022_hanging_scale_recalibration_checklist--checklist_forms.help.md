# Hanging Scale Recalibration Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hanging Scale Recalibration Checklist is used to collect and record essential information regarding the recalibration of hanging scales within the organization. This form helps maintain accurate records, ensuring that scales are recalibrated according to the set intervals and standards. Completing this form helps ensure that the scales are accurately calibrated and certified, ensuring accurate weighing and measurement results.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by entering the Warehouse ID (Field 1). This is a unique identifier for the warehouse where the scale is located.
- Next, enter the Scale Serial Number (Field 2), which is a unique identifier for the specific hanging scale being recalibrated.
- Then, fill in the Serial Number (Field 3), which is another identifier for the scale, often required for certification purposes.
- After that, select the Recalibration Date (Field 4) and Last Recalibration Date (Field 5) according to the actual recalibration dates.
- Enter the Recalibration Interval (Field 6), which specifies the time period between recalibrations, usually in months or years.
- If applicable, enter the Last Calibration Certificate (Field 7), which is often a record of the previous certification.
- Check the Calibration Status (Field 8), indicating whether the scale is currently active or inactive.
- Provide any additional information or comments in the Notes (Field 11), if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Warehouse Id (1, Number, Required: False)**: Enter the warehouse ID where the scale is located.
* **Scale Serial Number (2, Text, Required: False)**: A unique identifier for the scale, often required for certification purposes.
* **Serial Number (3, Text, Required: False)**: Another identifier for the scale, typically for certification purposes.
* **Recalibration Date (4, Date, Required: False)**: The date of the last recalibration.
* **Last Recalibration Date (5, Date, Required: False)**: The date of the previous recalibration.
* **Recalibration Interval (6, Text, Required: False)**: The time period between recalibrations.
* **Last Calibration Certificate (7, Text, Required: False)**: A record of the previous certification or calibration.
* **Calibration Status (8, Select One, Required: False)**: Check if the scale is Active or Inactive.
* **Calibration Result (9, Text, Required: False)**: Enter the outcome or results of the recalibration process.
* **Calibration Certificate (10, Text, Required: False)**: A record of the current certification or calibration.
* **Notes (11, Note, Required: False)**: Additional information or comments related to the recalibration process.
