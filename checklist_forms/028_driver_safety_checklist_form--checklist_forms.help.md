# Driver Safety Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Safety Checklist Form is designed to help individuals or organizations assess the safety condition of a vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide information about the vehicle's condition.
2. Answer all questions based on your observation or inspection.
3. Be honest and thorough in your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, note, required: false):
	+ This section is for general comments about the vehicle's overall condition.
	+ Please provide any notable issues or concerns you've observed.
* **Seat Belts** (`seat_belts`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Check the condition of the seat belts and select all that apply:
		- Yes: The seat belts are functional and in good condition.
		- No: The seat belts are not functional or are in poor condition.
* **Tire Condition** (`tire_condition`, text, required: false):
	+ Report the condition of the tires, including tread depth and wear.
* **Brakes** (`brakes`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe the condition of the brakes, including any issues or concerns.
* **Windshield Wipers** (`windshield_wipers`, text, required: false):
	+ Report the condition of the windshield wipers, including any damage or malfunction.
* **Parking Brakes** (`parking_brakes`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe the condition of the parking brakes, including any issues or concerns.
* **Steering** (`steering`, text, required: false):
	+ Report the condition of the steering, including any looseness or play.
* **Lights** (`lights`, text, required: false):
	+ Check the condition and function of all lights, including headlights and taillights.
* **Horn** (`horn`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe the condition and function of the horn, including any issues or concerns.
* **Tires** (`tires`, text, required: false):
	+ Report the condition and size of the tires.
* **Wheels** (`wheels`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe the condition of the wheels, including any damage or wear.
* **Body** (`body`, text, required: false):
	+ Report the condition of the vehicle's body, including any dents or scratches.
* **Suspension** (`suspension`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe the condition of the vehicle's suspension, including any issues or concerns.
* **Frame** (`frame`, text, required: false):
	+ Report the condition of the vehicle's frame, including any damage or rust.
* **Steering Gear** (`steering_gear`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe the condition of the vehicle's steering gear.
* **Steering Wheel** (`steering_wheel`, text, required: false):
	+ Report the condition of the steering wheel, including any damage or wear.

## Tips
- Be thorough in your assessment and check all sections carefully.
- If you're unsure about any aspect of the vehicle's condition, consult with a professional mechanic or expert.
