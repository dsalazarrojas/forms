# Quality Control Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Control Inspection Form is used by quality control teams to inspect and evaluate different aspects of a product or process. This form helps ensure that all required checks are completed and provides a comprehensive evaluation of the product's quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the sections and questions it contains.
2. Answer each section based on your evaluation of the product or process.
3. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the options that best match your evaluation.
4. For date and time fields, enter the actual date and time of the inspection.
5. Complete the form in a quiet and private area, free from distractions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Checklist (page_1)**:
	+ This section is a general overview of the product or process being inspected.
	+ Complete this section by checking the boxes for the aspects that you want to evaluate.
* **Document Management (page_2)**:
	+ This section is for selecting the type of document management system used.
	+ Choose the option that best describes the system used for document management.
* **Automotive (page_3)**:
	+ This section is for evaluating the automotive aspects of the product or process.
	+ Select all the options that apply to your evaluation.
* **Date (page_4)**:
	+ This section is for entering the date of the inspection.
	+ Enter the actual date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time (page_5)**:
	+ This section is for entering the time of the inspection.
	+ Enter the actual time of the inspection in the format HH:MM:SS.
