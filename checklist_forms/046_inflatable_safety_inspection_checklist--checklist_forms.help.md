# Inflatable Safety Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document safety information about inflatables used in events and activities. This form helps identify potential safety concerns and ensures compliance with relevant regulations and standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Event Information section with the event details, such as event name, date, and location.
2. Select the relevant Event Type from the options provided.
3. Provide details about the inflatable in the Inflatable Details section.
4. Document the inspection results in the Inspection Results section.
5. Choose relevant Safety Measures that were taken or needed.
6. Select the Action Taken in response to the inspection results.
7. Add any additional Notes if necessary.
8. Fill in the Contact Person and Contact Email fields with relevant contact information.
9. Enter the Contact Phone number for the person responsible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Information** (1, text, Required): Enter the name and details of the event where the inflatable was used.
  - Event name
  - Date of the event
  - Location of the event
  - Any other relevant details about the event

  - **Event Type** (2, select_one, Optional): Select the type of event where the inflatable was used (e.g. festival, party, corporate event).

  - **Inflatable Details** (3, text, Optional): Describe the inflatable in use, including its size, color, and any notable features.

  - **Inspection Results** (4, text, Optional): Document the outcome of the inspection, including any safety concerns found and their resolution.

  - **Safety Measures** (5, select_multiple, Optional): Select all relevant safety measures that should be taken or were taken to address any concerns:

    • Measure 1
    • Measure 2
    • Measure 3

  - **Action Taken** (6, select_one, Optional): Select the action taken in response to any safety concerns or measures:

    • Action 1
    • Action 2
    • Action 3

  - **Notes** (7, note, Optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the inspection.

  - **Contact Person** (8, text, Optional): Enter the name and job title of the person responsible for the inflatable's operation and maintenance.

  - **Contact Email** (9, email, Optional): Enter the contact email of the person responsible for the inflatable's operation and maintenance.

  - **Contact Phone** (10, text, Optional): Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for the inflatable's operation and maintenance.
