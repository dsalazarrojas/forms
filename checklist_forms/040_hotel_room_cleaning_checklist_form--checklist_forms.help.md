# Hotel Room Cleaning Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Room Cleaning Checklist Form is a document used by hotel staff to record the completion status of various cleaning tasks for each room. It's essential to ensure that all tasks are checked off to guarantee a thorough and high-quality cleaning service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand the tasks that need to be checked off for each room.
2. Check off each task as it's completed for the room.
3. Ensure all tasks are marked as complete before moving on to the next step.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check Bed Frame (1)**: Verify that the bed frame and surrounding area are clean and tidy.
* **Check Wardrobe (2)**: Inspect the closet and ensure that it's tidy and organized.
* **Check Nightstands (3)**: Examine the nightstands and ensure that they are free of clutter and clean.
* **Check Bathroom (4)**: Check the bathroom sink, toilet, shower, and any other bathroom facilities to ensure they are clean and sanitized.
* **Check Shower (5)**: Inspect the shower area for cleanliness and ensure that all soap, shampoo, and other products are properly stocked.
* **Check Sink (6)**: Examine the sink area, including the sink, countertops, and any other fixtures, to ensure they are clean and free of debris.
* **Check Toilet (7)**: Verify that the toilet, toilet seat, and surrounding area are clean and free of germs.
* **Check Floor (8)**: Inspect the floor for cleanliness and ensure it's free of dirt, dust, and any other debris.

## Tips
* Take your time to ensure that each task is completed accurately.
* Double-check each task before marking it as complete.
* If you're unsure about a particular task, consult with your supervisor or a colleague before marking it as complete.
