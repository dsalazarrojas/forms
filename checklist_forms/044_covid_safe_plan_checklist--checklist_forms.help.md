# COVID Safe Plan Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This COVID Safe Plan Checklist is designed to help facilities assess and improve their COVID-19 safety protocols. It is a comprehensive tool to identify gaps in your safety measures and ensure a safe environment for employees, visitors, and the community. The form is divided into several sections, including:

*   Facility Information
*   COVID-19 Safety Policies
*   Health Screening
*   Isolation and Quarantine
*   Cleaning and Sanitization
*   Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
*   Social Distancing
*   Overall Compliance

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each section carefully and ensure you have completed all required fields.
2.  Answer questions based on your current practices and procedures.
3.  Check all boxes for each question, even if you are unsure or have no policy or procedure in place.
4.  Be honest and accurate in your responses to ensure a comprehensive assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Facility Name** (`checklist_facility_name`): Enter the official name of your organization.
*   **Facility Type** (`checklist_facility_type`): Select the type of facility you are evaluating (e.g., Office, Healthcare facility, School, etc.).
*   **Inspector or Manager Name** (`checklist_inspector_name`): Enter the name of the person completing the checklist.
*   **Inspection Date** (`checklist_inspection_date`): Enter the date the checklist is completed.
*   **Written COVID-19 Safety Policy** (`checklist_policy_documented`): Check if a documented safety policy exists. If it does, it's likely that it's in place and implemented, unless it's marked as "Not in place."
*   **Employees Trained on Safety Protocol** (`checklist_employees_trained`): Select the number of employees trained on safety protocols.
*   **Daily Health Screening Implemented** (`checklist_daily_screening`): Check if daily health screening is in place.
*   **Symptom Reporting Procedure** (`checklist_symptom_reporting`): Select the process for reporting symptoms.
*   **Designated Isolation Area** (`checklist_isolation_area`): Check if a designated isolation area exists.
*   **Quarantine Protocol** (`checklist_quarantine_protocol`): Select the procedure for quarantine if needed.
*   **Regular Facility Cleaning Schedule** (`checklist_regular_cleaning`): Select the frequency of the cleaning schedule.
*   **Adequate Disinfection Supplies** (`checklist_disinfection_supplies`): Select the level of disinfection supplies available.
*   **High-Touch Surface Disinfection** (`checklist_high_touch_surfaces`): Select the frequency of disinfection of high-touch surfaces.
*   **Masks Available for Staff and Visitors** (`checklist_masks_available`): Select the availability of masks.
*   **Gloves Available** (`checklist_gloves_available`): Select the availability of gloves.
*   **Social Distancing Measures Implemented** (`checklist_distancing_implemented`): Select the level of social distancing implemented.
*   **Overall COVID-19 Safety Compliance** (`checklist_overall_compliance`): Select the overall facility compliance rating.
*   **Follow-Up Inspection Scheduled** (`checklist_follow_up_date`): Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection.

## Tips

*   This form is designed to be completed by facilities to assess their current COVID-19 safety protocols.
*   Please answer honestly and accurately to ensure a comprehensive assessment.
*   The goal is to identify gaps and areas for improvement.
*   Regular follow-up inspections are necessary to ensure continued compliance.
