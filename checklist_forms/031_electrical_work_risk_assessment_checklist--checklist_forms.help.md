# Electrical Work Risk Assessment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess and document the risks associated with electrical work. It helps to ensure that the work area is safe and free from hazards, and that the risk level for the project is accurately determined.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully and select the most accurate answer based on your assessment.
2. Complete all required fields. Some fields are marked as required, which means they must be filled out to complete the form.
3. If you are not sure of the answer to a question, use your best judgment or consult with a supervisor or safety expert.
4. Complete the form on a regular basis as recommended by your organization's safety protocols.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Is the electrical work area free from overhead hazards?
*   **Is the electrical work area free from overhead hazards?** (`electrical_work_risk_assessment_1`, checkbox, required): This question is asking if there are any overhead hazards such as exposed wiring, loose pipes, or other obstacles that could cause injury.
*   **Is the work area clear from debris and tripping hazards?** (`electrical_work_risk_assessment_2`, checkbox, required): This question is asking if the work area is clear from debris, such as cords, tools, or other obstructions, that could cause tripping or slipping.
*   **Are all electrical cables and wires properly secured and labeled?** (`electrical_work_risk_assessment_3`, select_one, required): This question is asking if all electrical cables and wires are properly secured to prevent them from becoming a tripping hazard, and if they are labeled correctly to identify their function.
*   **Is there a fire alarm system in place in the work area?** (`electrical_work_risk_assessment_4`, select_one, required): This question is asking if a fire alarm system is installed and functioning in the work area, which is essential for fire safety.
*   **Is there a risk assessment plan in place for the work area?** (`electrical_work_risk_assessment_5`, select_one, required): This question is asking if a risk assessment plan is created and followed in the work area, which helps to identify and mitigate risks.
*   **What is the highest risk level for the entire project?** (`electrical_work_risk_assessment_6`, select_one, required): This question is asking for the highest risk level for the entire project, which helps to prioritize safety measures.
*   **Risk Level** (`electrical_work_risk_assessment_7`, number, required): This question is asking for a numerical value that represents the highest risk level for the project.
*   **Assessment Date** (`electrical_work_risk_assessment_8`, date, required): This question is asking for the date when the assessment was done.
*   **Time of Assessment** (`electrical_work_risk_assessment_9`, time, required): This question is asking for the time when the assessment was done.

## Tips
*   Take your time to carefully complete each question, as they are essential for accurate risk assessment.
*   Review and update the form regularly to ensure that the work area remains safe.
*   Consult with a supervisor or safety expert if you are unsure of any of the questions.
