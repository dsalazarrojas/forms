# Sports Trading Card Rookie Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Trading Card Rookie Checklist form is designed to collect information about sports trading cards in a structured and organized way. The form is meant to be used by collectors, buyers, or sellers of sports trading cards to gather and store essential details about each card.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the sports trading card name in the field labeled "Sports Trading Card Name".
2. Enter the series of the card in the field labeled "Series".
3. Enter the player's position in the field labeled "Player Position".
4. Enter the rookie year of the card in the field labeled "Rookie Year".
5. Enter the grade of the card in the field labeled "Grade".
6. Select the condition of the card from the dropdown list in the field labeled "Condition".
7. Select the grade scale from the dropdown list in the field labeled "Grade Scale".
8. Enter the price of the card in the field labeled "Price" (optional).
9. Enter any additional notes about the card in the field labeled "Notes".
10. Enter the contact method to reach the seller or buyer in the field labeled "Contact Method".
11. Enter the card number in the field labeled "Card Number".
12. Enter the serial number of the card in the field labeled "Serial Number".
13. Enter the set number of the card in the field labeled "Set Number".
14. Enter the date the checklist was completed in the field labeled "Checklist Completed".
15. Enter the date the checklist was reviewed in the field labeled "Checklist Reviewed".
16. Enter the name of the shop where the card was purchased from in the field labeled "Shop Name".
17. Enter the name of the employee at the shop where the card was purchased from in the field labeled "Employee Name".
18. Enter the phone number of the shop or contact person in the field labeled "Phone Number".
19. Enter the email of the shop or contact person in the field labeled "Email".
20. Select the contact method to reach the seller or buyer in the field labeled "Contact Method 2".
21. Enter any additional notes about the card in the field labeled "Notes 2".
22. Enter the date the second checklist was completed in the field labeled "Checklist Completed 2".
23. Enter the date the second checklist was reviewed in the field labeled "Checklist Reviewed 2".
24. Enter the name of the shop where the second card was purchased from in the field labeled "Shop Name 2".
25. Enter the name of the employee at the shop where the second card was purchased from in the field labeled "Employee Name 2".
26. Select the contact method to reach the seller or buyer in the field labeled "Contact Method 2".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sports Trading Card Name**: Enter the official name of the sports trading card.
* **Series**: Enter the series of the card, such as the name of the set it comes from.
* **Player Position**: Enter the player's position on the field, such as quarterback or center.
* **Rookie Year**: Enter the year the player was a rookie.
* **Grade**: Enter a subjective score for the card's grade, such as 9/10.
* **Condition**: Select the condition of the card from the dropdown list, such as Near Mint or Centered.
* **Grade Scale**: Select the grade scale from the dropdown list, such as 1/10 or 1-10.
* **Price**: Enter the price of the card in USD (optional).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about the card, such as its rarity or provenance.
* **Contact Method**: Select the contact method to reach the seller or buyer, such as phone or email.
* **Card Number**: Enter the unique number of the card.
* **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the card.
* **Set Number**: Enter the number of the set the card comes from.
* **Checklist Completed**: Enter the date the checklist was completed.
* **Checklist Reviewed**: Enter the date the checklist was reviewed.
* **Shop Name**: Enter the name of the shop where the card was purchased from.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee at the shop where the card was purchased from.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the shop or contact person.
* **Email**: Enter the email of the shop or contact person.
* **Contact Method 2**: Select the contact method to reach the seller or buyer for the second card.
* **Notes 2**: Enter any additional information about the second card, such as its rarity or provenance.
* **Checklist Completed 2**: Enter the date the second checklist was completed.
* **Checklist Reviewed 2**: Enter the date the second checklist was reviewed.
* **Shop Name 2**: Enter the name of the shop where the second card was purchased from.
* **Employee Name 2**: Enter the name of the employee at the shop where the second card was purchased from.
* **Contact Method 2**: Select the contact method to reach the seller or buyer for the second card.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information about the card.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before completing the form.
* Use the dropdown lists to select the correct option for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact the seller or buyer for clarification.
