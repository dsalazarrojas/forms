# hockey_equipment_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hockey_equipment_checklist form is a tool for collecting and tracking player and game information. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the player's name in the "Player Name" field.
2. Enter the team's name in the "Team Name" field.
3. Choose the game date in the "Game Date" field.
4. Select the equipment needed for the game in the "Equipment Needed" field.
5. Enter the emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" fields.
6. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
7. List any additional equipment that needs to be provided for the game in the "Equipment List" field.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter the game time.
10. Enter the game location.
11. Enter the coach's name.
12. Enter the player's position.
13. Enter the player's number.
14. Enter the team color.
15. Enter the referee's name.
16. Enter the referee's phone number.
17. Select the game officials in the "Game Officials" field.
18. Enter any other game officials that need to be noted in the "Other Game Officials" field.
19. Enter the player's age.
20. Enter the player's height.
21. Enter the player's weight.
22. Enter the emergency contact name.
23. Enter the emergency contact phone number (if necessary).
24. Select the equipment that is provided for the game in the "Equipment Provided" field.
25. Select the game status.
26. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name** (1): Enter the name of the player.
* **Team Name** (2): Enter the name of the team.
* **Game Date** (3): Choose the date of the game.
* **Equipment Needed** (4): Select the equipment that the player needs for the game (multiple selections allowed).
* **Emergency Contact** (5): Enter a family member or emergency contact person and their phone number.
* **Phone Number** (6): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Equipment List** (7): List any additional equipment that needs to be provided for the game (optional).
* **Notes** (8): Enter any additional comments or notes about the player.
* **Game Time** (9): Enter the time of the game.
* **Game Location** (10): Enter the location of the game.
* **Coach Name** (11): Enter the name of the coach.
* **Player Position** (12): Enter the player's position.
* **Player Number** (13): Enter the player's number.
* **Team Color** (14): Enter the team color.
* **Referee Name** (15): Enter the name of the referee.
* **Referee Phone Number** (16): Enter the phone number of the referee.
* **Game Officials** (17): Select the game officials (multiple selections allowed).
* **Other Game Officials** (18): Enter any other game officials that need to be noted (optional).
* **Player Age** (19): Enter the player's age.
* **Player Height** (20): Enter the player's height.
* **Player Weight** (21): Enter the player's weight.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (22): Enter the name of the emergency contact person (optional).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (23): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person (optional).
* **Equipment Provided** (24): Select the equipment that is provided for the game (multiple selections allowed).
* **Game Status** (25): Select the status of the game.
* **Notes** (26): Enter any additional comments or notes about the game (optional).

## Tips
* Ensure that you have all the necessary information before filling out this form.
* Be accurate when entering the player's and game details.
* Use this form to update or add new player and game data, not to duplicate or create new records. 
* Review the form for any errors before submitting.
