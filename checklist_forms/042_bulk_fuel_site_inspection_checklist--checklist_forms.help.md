# Bulk Fuel Site Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a comprehensive checklist for conducting bulk fuel site inspections. It helps to identify potential safety and regulatory compliance issues at a fuel storage site.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Inspection Date** in the first field.
2.  Enter the **Site Location** in the second field.
3.  If applicable, enter the **Site ID Number** in the third field.
4.  Enter the **Inspector Name** in the fourth field.
5.  Enter the **Inspector ID or Certification** in the fifth field.
6.  Enter the **Site Manager or Operator Name** in the sixth field.
7.  Choose the condition of the **Tank** from the options provided.
8.  Check for **Signs of Corrosion or Rust** and select the appropriate level.
9.  Check for **Evidence of Leaks** and select the appropriate level.
10. Check if the **Secondary Containment System** is present and functional.
11. Choose the **Containment Area Inspection** condition.
12. Choose the condition of the **Piping** from the options provided.
13. Check for any **Piping Leaks or Damage** and select the appropriate level.
14. Check if the **Valve Operation and Seals** are functioning as expected.
15. Check the **Fuel Level Gauge Accuracy** and select the appropriate level.
16. Check the condition of the **Fuel Pump** from the options provided.
17. Check if the **Spill Kit** is present and accessible.
18. Check if the **Safety Signage** is complete and visible.
19. Check if the **Lighting** is adequate and functional.
20. Check the condition of the **Grounds and Drainage** from the options provided.
21. Conduct a **Fuel Quality Check** and select the appropriate level.
22. List any **Identified Deficiencies** found during the inspection.
23. List any **Corrective Actions Required** to address issues found.
24. Choose the overall **Compliance Status** from the options provided.
25. Enter the **Next Inspection Date** (if scheduled).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the inspection.
*   **Site Location** (`site_location`, text, required): Enter the address of the fuel site.
*   **Site ID Number** (`site_id`, text, optional): Enter the reference number if applicable.
*   **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
*   **Inspector ID or Certification** (`inspector_id`, text, optional): Enter the professional credentials of the inspector.
*   **Site Manager or Operator** (`site_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of the facility operator.
*   **Tank Condition** (`tank_condition`, select\_one, required): Choose the physical state of the tanks.
*   **Signs of Corrosion or Rust** (`tank_corrosion`, select\_one, required): Check for visible signs of corrosion or rust.
*   **Evidence of Leaks** (`tank_leaks`, select\_one, required): Check for visible fuel leaks.
*   **Secondary Containment System** (`secondary_containment`, select\_one, required): Check if the secondary containment system is present and functional.
*   **Containment Area Inspection** (`containment_inspection`, select\_one, required): Choose the integrity of the containment area.
*   **Piping Condition** (`piping_condition`, select\_one, required): Choose the physical state of the fuel transfer piping.
*   **Piping Leaks or Damage** (`piping_leaks`, select\_one, required): Check for any leaks or damage to the piping.
*   **Valve Operation and Seals** (`valve_operation`, select\_one, required): Check if the valve operation and seals are functioning correctly.
*   **Fuel Level Gauge Accuracy** (`gauge_accuracy`, select\_one, required): Check the accuracy of the tank level indicator.
*   **Fuel Pump Condition** (`pump_condition`, select\_one, required): Choose the condition of the dispensing equipment.
*   **Spill Kit Present and Accessible** (`spill_kit`, select\_one, required): Check if the spill kit is present and accessible.
*   **Safety Signage** (`safety_signage`, select\_one, required): Check if the warning signs are complete and visible.
*   **Lighting Adequate and Functional** (`lighting_inspection`, select\_one, required): Check if the area lighting is working correctly.
*   **Grounds and Drainage Condition** (`grounds_condition`, select\_one, required): Choose the maintenance status of the area.
*   **Fuel Quality Check** (`fuel_quality`, select\_one, required): Conduct a check for any visible contamination.
*   **Identified Deficiencies** (`deficiencies`, text, optional): List any issues found during the inspection.
*   **Corrective Actions Required** (`corrective_actions`, text, optional): List any necessary corrections or fixes.
*   **Overall Compliance Status** (`compliance_status`, select\_one, required): Choose the overall compliance status with regulations.
*   **Next Inspection Date** (`next_inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the scheduled next inspection date.
