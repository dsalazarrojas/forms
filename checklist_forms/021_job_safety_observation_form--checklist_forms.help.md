# Job Safety Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for recording observations on the job site to identify potential safety hazards and improve workplace safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about the safety conditions on the job site.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. Enter any additional comments or observations in the "Observation Notes" field.
4. If you need to record a specific date and time of the observation, use the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Safety Questions** (`job_safety_observation_form_1`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the safety conditions on the job site.
* **Do You Agree That Employees Are Wearing PPE?** (`safety_question_1`, select_one, optional): Indicate if employees are wearing personal protective equipment (PPE) on the job site.
	+ Select 'Yes' if most employees are wearing PPE.
	+ Select 'False' if few or no employees are wearing PPE.
	+ Select 'Not sure' if you are unsure or don't have enough information.
* **Are The Workstations Clean?** (`safety_question_2`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the cleanliness status of the workstations.
	+ Select 'True' if all workstations are clean and free from hazards.
	+ Select 'False' if any workstations are dirty, cluttered, or have hazards.
	+ Select 'Not applicable' if there are no workstations on the job site.
* **Observation Notes** (`observation_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the job site.
* **Date Of Observation** (`date_of_observation`, date, optional): Record the date of the observation.
* **Time Of Observation** (`time_of_observation`, time, optional): Record the time of the observation.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when answering the questions.
* Take note of any specific incidents or near-misses that occurred during the observation.
* Use the "Observation Notes" field to record any additional information that is not captured by the questions.
* Remember to save a copy of the completed form for your records.
