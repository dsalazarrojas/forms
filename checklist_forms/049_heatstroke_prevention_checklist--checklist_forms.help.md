# Heatstroke Prevention Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This help guide is designed to assist users in completing the Heatstroke Prevention Checklist form. The purpose of this form is to identify potential risks and take steps to prevent heatstroke.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the conditions that may lead to heat stroke (page 1).
2. Select the prevention steps that apply to you (page 2).
3. Identify the symptoms of heat stroke that you are experiencing (page 3).
4. Provide any additional prevention steps not listed (page 4).
5. Provide any additional prevention steps provided by your employer or coach (page 5).
6. Provide any additional prevention steps provided by organizers or event staff (page 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### page 1: conditions
* **heat_stroke_conditions** (`select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following conditions that may lead to heat stroke:
	+ Heat exhaustion: A milder form of heat-related illness that can progress to heatstroke if not treated promptly.
	+ Dehydration: A condition where the body loses more water and salts than it can replace, leading to dizziness, headaches, and other symptoms.
	+ Heatstroke: A severe and potentially life-threatening form of heat-related illness.

### page 2: prevention_steps
* **prevention_steps** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the prevention steps that apply to you:
	+ Drinking water regularly: Drink water regularly to stay hydrated.
	+ Taking regular breaks: Take regular breaks in a cool place to rest and rehydrate.
	+ Wearing lightweight clothing: Wear lightweight, light-colored, and loose-fitting clothing to keep cool.
	+ Wearing a hat and sunglasses: Wear a hat and sunglasses to protect your face, neck, and eyes from the sun.
	+ Staying in the shade: Stay in a cool, shaded area when possible.

### page 3: symptoms
* **heat_stroke_symptoms** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the symptoms you are experiencing:
	+ High or low blood pressure: A drop in blood pressure can be a sign of heatstroke.
	+ Other: If you are experiencing other symptoms not listed, describe them here.

### page 4: other_prevention_steps
* **other_prevention_steps** (`text`, required: false): If there are additional prevention steps you would like to take, describe them here.

### page 5: prevention_steps_employer
* **prevention_steps_employer** (`text`, required: false): If your employer has provided any additional prevention steps, describe them here.

### page 6: prevention_steps_coach
* **prevention_steps_coach** (`text`, required: false): If your coach has provided any additional prevention steps, describe them here.

### page 7: prevention_steps_organizers
* **prevention_steps_organizers** (`text`, required: false): If the event organizers or staff have provided any additional prevention steps, describe them here.

## Tips
* Always drink plenty of water before, during, and after physical activity.
* Take regular breaks to rest and rehydrate.
* Be aware of your body's signs of heat stroke, such as headaches, dizziness, and confusion.
* If you suspect someone is experiencing heat stroke, call for emergency assistance immediately.
