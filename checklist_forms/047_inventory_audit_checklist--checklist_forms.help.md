# Inventory Audit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Inventory Audit Checklist form is used to conduct an inventory audit, a systematic process to verify the accuracy and completeness of the inventory in a warehouse or stockroom.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting the form, ensure you have all necessary information and resources at hand. The form is divided into several sections, each addressing a specific part of the inventory audit. Follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Select the status of each item on the first page, indicating whether it is present or not.
2. Enter the date of the audit in the date field.
3. On the second page, select the correct items from the options provided, as applicable.
4. Add any additional remarks or comments regarding the audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Inventory Audit Checklist 1

* **Inventory Audit Checklist 1** (`inventory_audit_checklist_1`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if the item is present in the inventory, 'No' otherwise. This field helps to quickly identify missing items.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format to record the audit date.

### Page 2: (Page 1 details)

* **Item 1** (`inventory_audit_checklist_item_1`, select_one, required): Select the correct item from the options, which are likely products or materials in the inventory. Ensure you choose the correct item as per your audit.
* **Item 2** (`inventory_audit_checklist_item_2`, select_one, optional): Select the correct item from the options, which are likely products or materials in the inventory.
* **Item 3** (`inventory_audit_checklist_item_3`, select_one, optional): Select the correct item from the options, which are likely products or materials in the inventory.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks about the audit.

### Page 3: (Page 2 details)

* **Inventory Audit Checklist 2** (`inventory_audit_checklist_2`, select_multiple, optional): This field is similar to Inventory Audit Checklist 1 on Page 1, but for a different section or part of the inventory. Select 'Yes' if the item is present in the inventory, 'No' otherwise.
* **Inventory Audit Checklist 1** (`inventory_audit_checklist_1`, select_multiple, optional): This field is identical to the first field on Page 1 and is likely used for consistency or to ensure accuracy across the audit.

## Tips

- Ensure you have an accurate and up-to-date inventory list before starting the audit.
- Review each field carefully to avoid errors.
- Use the 'Remarks' field to capture any discrepancies or issues encountered during the audit.
- Store the completed form for future reference or for use in subsequent audits.
