# Litigation Checklist Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a general-purpose checklist template for litigation cases, designed to be used by users in various contexts, such as court proceedings or internal case management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the relevant options for the "Case Details" page.
2. Fill in the "Plaintiff" and "Defendant" fields, selecting the appropriate option from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose the "Case Status" by selecting the relevant option from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the addresses of the plaintiff and defendant in the "Plaintiff Address" and "Defendant Address" fields respectively.
5. Enter the date the case was opened in the "Date Opened" field.
6. Provide the case number in the "Case Number" field.
7. Choose the court in which the case is being heard in the "Court" field.
8. Select the type of case (civil or criminal) from the "Case Type" field.
9. Enter a brief description of the case in the "Case Description" field.
10. Fill in the fields for "Plaintiff Attorney", "Defendant Attorney", "Plaintiff Contact", and "Defendant Contact" with relevant information.
11. Select the relevant documents related to the case from the "Case Documents" field.
12. Enter any additional notes or comments about the case in the "Case Notes" field.
13. Specify the next steps to be taken in the "Next Steps" field.
14. Fill in the field for "Assigned User" with the relevant contact person.
15. Enter the date the case was assigned to the user in the "Assigned Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Plaintiff** (select_one): This field is used to select the party who is taking legal action, i.e., the plaintiff.
- **Defendant** (select_one): This field is used to select the party against whom the action is being taken, i.e., the defendant.
- **Case Status** (select_one): This field is used to select the current status of the case, such as open, closed, or pending.
- **Plaintiff Address** (text): This field is used to enter the address of the plaintiff.
- **Defendant Address** (text): This field is used to enter the address of the defendant.
- **Date Opened** (date): This field is used to enter the date the case was opened.
- **Case Number** (text): This field is used to enter the case number.
- **Court** (select_one): This field is used to select the court in which the case is being heard.
- **Case Type** (select_one): This field is used to select the type of case, i.e., civil or criminal.
- **Case Description** (text): This field is used to enter a brief description of the case.
- **Plaintiff Attorney** (text): This field is used to enter the name and contact information of the plaintiff's attorney.
- **Defendant Attorney** (text): This field is used to enter the name and contact information of the defendant's attorney.
- **Plaintiff Contact** (text): This field is used to enter the contact information of the plaintiff.
- **Defendant Contact** (text): This field is used to enter the contact information of the defendant.
- **Case Documents** (select_multiple): This field is used to select relevant documents related to the case.
- **Document 1**, **Document 2**, ..., **Document 5** (text): These fields are used to enter specific details about each selected document.
- **Case Notes** (text): This field is used to enter any additional notes or comments about the case.
- **Next Steps** (text): This field is used to specify the next steps to be taken in the case.
- **Assigned User** (text): This field is used to enter the name and contact information of the user assigned to the case.
- **Assigned Date** (time): This field is used to enter the date the case was assigned to the user.
