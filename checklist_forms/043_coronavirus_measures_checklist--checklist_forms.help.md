# Coronavirus Measures Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coronavirus Measures Checklist is a form designed to help you track your coronavirus measures and health status. The form will ask you questions about your employee status, travel, symptoms, temperature, test results, travel history, and symptoms history to identify potential risks and guide you towards taking the necessary actions.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_one, required): Select your current employee status:
	+ Active: You are currently an active employee with the company.
	+ Inactive: You are currently an inactive employee or on leave.
* **Travel** (`coronavirus_measures_checklist_form_2`, select_multiple, required): Check if you have recently traveled to any of the following countries or regions:
	+ Yes: You have traveled to one or more of the listed countries or regions.
	+ No: You have not traveled to any of the listed countries or regions.
* **Symptoms** (`coronavirus_measures_checklist_form_3`, select_one, required): Check if you have any of the following symptoms:
	+ Yes: You have symptoms.
	+ No: You do not have symptoms.
* **Temperature** (`coronavirus_measures_checklist_form_4`, select_one, required): Check your current temperature:
	+ Yes: Your temperature is elevated.
	+ No: Your temperature is normal.
* **Test Results** (`coronavirus_measures_checklist_form_5`, select_one, required): Check your recent test results:
	+ Yes: You have tested positive.
	+ No: You have not tested positive.
* **Recent Travel** (`coronavirus_measures_checklist_form_6`, select_multiple, not required): Check if you have recently traveled to any of the following countries or regions:
	+ Yes: You have traveled to one or more of the listed countries or regions.
	+ No: You have not traveled to any of the listed countries or regions.
* **Travel History** (`coronavirus_measures_checklist_form_7`, select_multiple, not required): Check if you have any travel history to the following countries or regions:
	+ Yes: You have traveled to one or more of the listed countries or regions.
	+ No: You have not traveled to any of the listed countries or regions.
* **Symptoms History** (`coronavirus_measures_checklist_form_8`, select_multiple, not required): Check if you have any symptoms history:
	+ Yes: You have symptoms.
	+ No: You do not have symptoms.
