# Road Surface Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Road Surface Inspection Form is used for assessing the condition and characteristics of a road surface.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:
* Carefully review the form questions and ensure you're answering them accurately.
* Complete each section thoroughly.
* If a field has a list of options (e.g., "Select One" or "Check all that Apply"), select the most suitable answer based on your observation.
* For fields with no options, enter your response in the provided text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Road Surface Inspection Form** (`road_surface_inspection_form_1`): Enter a brief description of the road surface inspection (e.g., "Road 123 on Main St").
* **Road Surface Type** (`road_surface_inspection_form_2`): Select the type of road surface:
	+ Asphalt: A type of road surface made from bitumen and aggregate.
	+ Concrete: A type of road surface made from cement, water, and aggregate.
	+ Brick: A type of road surface made from bricks or pavers.
	+ Gravel: A type of road surface made from small, loose stones.
	+ Dirt: A type of natural road surface.
* **Road Surface Condition** (`road_surface_inspection_form_3`): Assess the condition of the road surface:
	+ Poor: The road surface is in a poor state (e.g., cracked, uneven).
	+ Fair: The road surface is fair but could be better (e.g., some cracks or unevenness).
	+ Good: The road surface is generally in good condition.
	+ Excellent: The road surface is excellent, with minimal imperfections.
* **Road Surface Defects** (`road_surface_inspection_form_4`): Check all defects that apply:
	+ Potholes: Areas with potholes or holes in the road surface.
	+ Cracks: Cracks in the road surface.
	+ Bumps: Slight undulations or bumps in the road surface.
	+ Oil stains: Stains or residue from spills on the road surface.
	+ Slippery: Areas with a slippery or smooth surface.
	+ Uneven surface: Areas where the surface is uneven or irregular.
	+ Broken glass: Broken or shattered glass on the road surface.
* **Weather** (`road_surface_inspection_form_5`): If applicable, enter the date and time of the inspection.
* **Inspectors' Notes** (`road_surface_inspection_form_6`): Enter any additional comments or observations about the road surface inspection.
* **Road Surface Inspection Form Contact** (`road_surface_inspection_form_7`): Enter your contact information for further communication or follow-up.
