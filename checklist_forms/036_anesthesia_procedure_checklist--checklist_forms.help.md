# Anesthesia Procedure Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide healthcare professionals in preparing for an anesthesia procedure, ensuring that all necessary checks are performed before the procedure begins.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's hospital or chart number in the "Patient ID" field.
2. Select the scheduled procedure time in the "Procedure time" field.
3. Confirm that airway equipment is ready by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Airway equipment ready" field.
4. Confirm that medications are prepared by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Medications prepared" field.
5. Confirm that IV access is established by selecting "True" or "False" in the "IV access established" field.
6. If necessary, add any additional notes about the equipment in the "Equipment notes" field.
7. Enter the name of the staff member completing the checklist in the "Checklist completed by" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, text, required): Enter the patient's hospital or chart number.
* **Procedure time** (`procedure_time`, time, required): Select the scheduled procedure time.
* **Airway equipment ready** (`airway_equipment_ready`, select_one, required): Confirm that airway equipment is ready for the procedure. Select "True" to indicate it is ready or "False" to indicate it is not.
* **Medications prepared** (`medication_prepared`, select_one, required): Confirm that medications are prepared for the procedure. Select "True" to indicate they are prepared or "False" to indicate they are not.
* **IV access established** (`iv_access`, select_one, required): Confirm that IV access is established for the procedure. Select "True" to indicate it is established or "False" to indicate it is not.
* **Equipment notes** (`equipment_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the equipment being used during the procedure.
* **Checklist completed by** (`checklist_completed_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the staff member completing the checklist.
## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely before proceeding with the procedure.
* If any issues arise during the procedure, refer to the "Equipment notes" field for additional information about the equipment being used.
* This form should be kept confidential and stored according to hospital policies.
* The completed checklist should be stored in the patient's medical record for future reference.
