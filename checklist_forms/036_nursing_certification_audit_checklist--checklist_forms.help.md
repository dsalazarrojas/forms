# nursing certification audit checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The nursing certification audit checklist is a tool used to collect information about nursing certifications, ensuring they are up-to-date and accurate. This form helps to verify, update, or cancel certifications, which is essential for patient care and safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine the current status of your certification (Active or Inactive).
2. Select the type of certification you hold (Basic Life Support, Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support, or Advanced Cardiac Life Support).
3. Enter the date your certification was issued.
4. Enter the expiration date of your certification.
5. Enter your nurse ID number.
6. Select the source of your certification (State Nursing Board, American Heart Association, or American Medical Association).
7. Choose an action to take for your certification (Verify, Update, or Cancel).
8. Enter the date for any follow-up actions.
9. Enter the name of the nurse who holds the certification.
10. Enter any additional notes about your certification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Credential Audit** (`Credential Audit`, `select_one`): Choose "Yes" if your certification is current, or "No" if it's not.
* **Nurse ID** (`Nurse ID`, `text`): Enter your unique nurse ID number.
* **Cert Status** (`Cert Status`, `select_one`): Select "Active" if your certification is active, or "Inactive" if it's inactive.
* **Cert Type** (`Cert Type`, `select_multiple`): Choose the type of certification you hold (Basic Life Support, Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support, or Advanced Cardiac Life Support).
* **Cert Date Issue** (`Cert Date Issue`, `date`): Enter the date your certification was issued.
* **Cert Date Expiry** (`Cert Date Expiry`, `date`): Enter the date your certification expires.
* **Cert Number** (`Cert Number`, `text`): Enter your certification number.
* **Cert Source** (`Cert Source`, `select_one`): Select the source of your certification (State Nursing Board, American Heart Association, or American Medical Association).
* **Cert Action** (`Cert Action`, `select_one`): Choose an action for your certification (Verify, Update, or Cancel).
* **Cert Follow-up** (`Cert Follow-up`, `date`): Enter the date for any follow-up actions.
* **Nurse Name** (`Nurse Name`, `text`): Enter the name of the nurse who holds the certification.
* **Other Cert Type** (`Other Cert Type`, `text`): Enter any additional certification types not listed above.
* **Cert Date Issue Note** (`Cert Date Issue Note`, `note`): Enter any notes about the date your certification was issued.
* **Cert Date Expiry Note** (`Cert Date Expiry Note`, `note`): Enter any notes about the date your certification expires.
* **Other Note** (`Other Note`, `note`): Enter any other notes about your certification.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you select the correct type of certification and enter accurate dates for your certification.
* Make sure to enter your nurse ID number correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or administrator for guidance.
