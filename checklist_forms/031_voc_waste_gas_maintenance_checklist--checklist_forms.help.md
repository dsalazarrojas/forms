# voc_waste_gas_maintenance_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the maintenance status of facilities and equipment that handle waste gas to ensure regular inspections and maintenance are conducted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the facility information in the "Facility Information" field.
2. Select the status of the facility's equipment (Working or Not Working) in the "Equipment Status" field.
3. Select the status of the equipment (Online or Offline) in the "Equipment Status (2)" field.
4. Choose the maintenance schedule for the facility (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly) in the "Maintenance Schedule" field.
5. Select the inspection frequency for the facility (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly) in the "Inspection Frequency" field.
6. Enter the date of the next inspection in the "Next Inspection Due" field.
7. Enter any comments or notes about the facility in the "Comments" field.
8. Sign and date the form by entering your name and email address in the "Technician Signature" and "Technician Email" fields.
9. Sign and date the form again by entering your name and email address in the "Manager Signature" and "Manager Email" fields.
10. Enter the date and time of the last inspection in the "Last Inspection Date" and "Last Inspection Time" fields.
11. Enter the date and time the form was last updated in the "Last Updated Time" field.
12. Enter the user who last updated the form in the "Last Updated By" field.
13. Enter the date and time the form was last deleted in the "Last Deleted Time" field.
14. Enter the user who last deleted the form in the "Last Deleted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Information**: Enter the facility's name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Equipment Status**: Select the status of the facility's equipment (Working or Not Working).
* **Equipment Status (2)**: Select the status of the equipment (Online or Offline).
* **Maintenance Schedule**: Choose the maintenance schedule for the facility (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
* **Inspection Frequency**: Select the inspection frequency for the facility (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
* **Next Inspection Due**: Enter the date of the next inspection.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the facility.
* **Technician Signature**: Sign and date the form by entering your name and email address.
* **Technician Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Manager Signature**: Sign and date the form again by entering your name and email address.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Last Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the last inspection.
* **Last Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the last inspection.
* **Last Updated By**: Enter the user who last updated the form.
* **Last Updated Time**: Enter the time the form was last updated.
* **Last Deleted By**: Enter the user who last deleted the form.
* **Last Deleted Time**: Enter the time the form was last deleted.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information in the "Next Inspection Due" field.
* Ensure that the "Maintenance Schedule" and "Inspection Frequency" fields are consistent with each other.
* Keep the "Comments" field up to date with any issues or concerns related to the facility.
* Double-check the "Last Inspection Date" and "Last Inspection Time" fields for accuracy.
* Ensure that the form is updated regularly to reflect any changes in the facility's status.
