# Contingency Plan Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contingency Plan Checklist is designed to guide your organization in identifying and preparing for potential emergency scenarios. The form helps ensure that you have a solid foundation in place to respond to and recover from emergency situations. Fill out this form to document your organization's progress in addressing critical areas and prepare for any unexpected events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Contingency Plan Checklist, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the organization information section (fields 1-3).
2. For each critical function, select the status from the dropdown menu (fields 6-10).
3. Enter information in the logistics section (fields 12-13).
4. Fill out the verification and review section (fields 14-16).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business/Organization Name (2)** (`business_legal_name`, required): Fill in your organization's full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Primary Point of Contact (Emergency)** (`point_of_contact`, required): Enter the name and contact information of your primary point of contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (3)** (`contact_phone_emergency`, required): Enter the phone number where your emergency contact can be reached.
* **Essential staff roles and backups identified (6)** (`essential_staff_identified`, required): Select the status of identifying essential staff roles and their backups:
	+ **Completed**: All essential staff roles and backups are identified.
	+ **In Progress**: Essential staff roles and backups are being identified.
	+ **Not Started**: Essential staff roles and backups have not been identified.
* **Remote work infrastructure and access verified (7)** (`remote_work_ready`, required): Select the status of your remote work infrastructure and access:
	+ **Completed**: Remote work infrastructure and access are verified.
	+ **In Progress**: Remote work infrastructure and access are being verified.
	+ **Not Started**: Remote work infrastructure and access have not been verified.
* **Sanitization and hygiene protocols documented (8)** (`hygiene_protocols_set`, required): Select the status of sanitization and hygiene protocols:
	+ **Completed**: Sanitization and hygiene protocols are documented.
	+ **In Progress**: Sanitization and hygiene protocols are being documented.
	+ **Not Started**: Sanitization and hygiene protocols have not been documented.
* **Emergency communication tree (email/SMS) active (9)** (`communication_tree_active`, required): Select the status of your emergency communication tree:
	+ **Completed**: Emergency communication tree is active.
	+ **In Progress**: Emergency communication tree is being established.
	+ **Not Started**: Emergency communication tree has not been established.
* **Alternative suppliers for critical items identified (10)** (`supply_chain_backup`, required): Select the status of alternative suppliers for critical items:
	+ **Completed**: Alternative suppliers for critical items are identified.
	+ **In Progress**: Alternative suppliers for critical items are being identified.
	+ **Not Started**: Alternative suppliers for critical items have not been identified.
* **Emergency fund availability confirmed (12)** (`emergency_fund_status`, required): Select the status of emergency fund availability:
	+ **Yes - Fully Funded**: Emergency funds are fully available.
	+ **Yes - Partially Funded**: Emergency funds are partially available.
	+ **No - Requires Action**: Emergency funds are not available and require action.
* **Current PPE Stock Level (13)** (`personal_protective_equipment_stock`, optional): Enter the current stock level of personal protective equipment (PPE) in days.
* **Verification details (14)** (`verification_details`, optional): Note any important verification details or observations.
* **Date Checklist Completed (15)** (`review_date`, required): Enter the date the checklist was completed.
* **Verifier Name/Signature (16)** (`verifier_signature`, required): Sign and enter your name as the verifier.
