# Zone Compliance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist inspectors and auditors in monitoring and documenting facilities' and zones' compliance status within the facility. It captures key information regarding the zone's compliance status, corrective actions, contact persons, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

*   Begin by entering the Facility name in the text field.
*   Move on to the Zone area field and enter the specific area or zone within the facility you are inspecting.
*   Enter the Inspection date in the date field, ensuring it reflects the date of the inspection or assessment.
*   Select the Zone compliance status from the dropdown menu, which indicates whether the zone is reported, not reported, pending, or compliant.
*   In the Corrective actions field, provide any necessary corrective actions or recommendations for improvement.
*   Add any relevant Comments regarding the inspection or assessment results.
*   Enter the contact person responsible for addressing any issues raised during the inspection.
*   Add the Contact number for further communication and follow-up.
*   Enter the Email for the contact person in charge of addressing any issues raised during the inspection.
*   Finally, enter any additional Zone ID if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Facility name**: (`facility_name`, text, required) This field should be filled in with the exact name of the facility being inspected or assessed.
*   **Zone area**: (`zone_area`, text, required) This field is used to identify the specific area or zone within the facility being inspected.
*   **Inspection date**: (`inspection_date`, date, required) Enter the date of the inspection or assessment to ensure accurate timestamping of compliance status.
*   **Zone compliance status**: (`zone_compliance_status`, select one, required) Select one of the four options: Reported, Not reported, Pending, or Compliant, to accurately reflect the zone's compliance status.
*   **Corrective actions**: (`corrective_actions`, text, optional) Enter any necessary corrective actions or recommendations for improvement for the zone.
*   **Comments**: (`comments`, note, optional) Add any additional comments or insights from the inspection or assessment.
*   **Contact person**: (`contact_person`, text, optional) Enter the name of the person responsible for addressing any issues raised during the inspection.
*   **Contact number**: (`contact_number`, text, optional) Enter the phone number or extension of the contact person.
*   **Email**: (`email`, email, optional) Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Zone ID**: (`zone_id`, text, optional) Enter any additional zone or facility ID if required.

## Tips
*   Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
*   Use the correct format for date fields to avoid errors.
*   Double-check the contact information for accuracy.
*   Clearly state any corrective actions or recommendations in the `corrective_actions` field.
*   Provide clear and concise comments in the `comments` field.
*   Verify the compliance status selected in the `zone_compliance_status` field reflects the actual situation.
