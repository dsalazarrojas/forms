# Legal Documentation Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Legal Documentation Checklist Form is a tool used to collect and document specific details about legal documents. This form is necessary to ensure that all relevant information is gathered and organized for reference and compliance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Company Name** (Field 1) - This is the name of the company or entity associated with the legal document.
2. Enter the **Document Name and Version** (Field 2) - This is the title and version of the document being documented.
3. Select the **Document Type and Status** (Field 3) - Choose the type and status of the document from the provided options (Active, Inactive, True, or False).
4. Enter the **Date Created** (Field 4) - This is the date the document was created.
5. Enter the **Date Due** (Field 5) - This is the date the document is due to be reviewed or completed.
6. Enter the **Date Review** (Field 6) - This is the date the document was reviewed.
7. Enter the **Date Signed** (Field 7) - This is the date the document was signed (if applicable).
8. Enter the **Date Issued** (Field 8) - This is the date the document was issued (if applicable).
9. Enter the **Date Received** (Field 9) - This is the date the document was received (if applicable).
10. Enter the **Date Verified** (Field 10) - This is the date the document was verified (if applicable).
11. Enter the **Document Location** (Field 11) - This is the location where the document is stored or kept.
12. Enter the **Legal Entity** (Field 12) - This is the entity responsible for the document.
13. Enter the **Entity Location** (Field 13) - This is the location of the legal entity.
14. Enter the **Document URL** (Field 14) - This is a URL where the document can be accessed (if applicable).
15. Enter any relevant **Document Notes** (Field 15) - This is an optional field for adding any additional comments or notes about the document.
