# Pickup Truck Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pickup Truck Inspection Form is used to collect and store information about the condition and status of pickup trucks. This form helps to evaluate the overall condition, safety, and maintenance needs of the vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the year of the pickup truck from the list of options.
2. Choose the make of the vehicle from the options.
3. Enter the model of the vehicle (text input).
4. Check the engine number to ensure it matches the vehicle's documentation.
5. Check if the engine oil is at the required level.
6. Select all applicable safety features, such as 'Yes' for active safety stickers.
7. Evaluate the condition of the vehicle, including body damage, frame damage, suspension, steering, brakes wear, and tire wear.
8. Select the overall status of the vehicle, either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
9. Add any comments about the vehicle's condition.
10. Select the user assigned to inspect the vehicle.
11. Enter the date the inspection was assigned to.
12. Select the date the inspection was submitted.
13. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
14. Enter the title of the person who submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vehicle_year** (Number, Optional): The year of the pickup truck.
* **vehicle_make** (Select One, Optional): The make of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_model** (Text, Optional): The model of the vehicle.
* **engine_number** (Number, Optional): The engine number of the vehicle.
* **engine_oil** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check if the engine oil is at the required level.
* **transmission** (Select One, Optional): Check if the transmission is active.
* **brakes** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check if the brakes are functional.
* **tires** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check if the tires are in good condition.
* **safety_stickers** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check if the active safety stickers are present.
* **vehicle_condition** (Select One, Optional): Evaluate the overall condition of the vehicle.
* **overall_condition** (Select One, Optional): Evaluate the overall condition of the vehicle.
* **body_damage** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check if there is body damage on the vehicle.
* **frame_damage** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check if there is frame damage on the vehicle.
* **suspension** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check if the suspension is functional.
* **steering** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check if the steering is functional.
* **brakes_wear** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check the wear condition of the brakes.
* **tire_wear** (Select Multiple, Optional): Check the wear condition of the tires.
* **safety_stickers_status** (Select One, Optional): Check the status of the active safety stickers.
* **overall_status** (Select One, Optional): Evaluate the overall status of the vehicle.
* **comments** (Note, Optional): Add any comments about the vehicle's condition.
* **assigned_user** (Text, Optional): Select the user assigned to inspect the vehicle.
* **assigned_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the inspection was assigned to.
* **submitted_date** (Date, Optional): Select the date the inspection was submitted.
* **submitted_by** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **submitted_by_title** (Text, Optional): Enter the title of the person who submitted the form.
