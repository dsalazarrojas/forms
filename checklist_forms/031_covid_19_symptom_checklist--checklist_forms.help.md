# Covid 19 Symptom Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help diagnose or monitor health symptoms related to COVID-19. It's intended for individuals to report their current symptoms, which can aid healthcare professionals in making informed decisions about their health.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each symptom listed and select the ones you're experiencing.
2. Fill out the form completely, even if you're not experiencing all symptoms.
3. Answer each question honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
4. If you have trouble with any question, don't hesitate to contact your healthcare provider for assistance.
