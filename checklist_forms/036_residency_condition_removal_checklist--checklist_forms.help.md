# Residency Condition Removal Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Residency Condition Removal Checklist is a form designed to assist users in gathering and submitting necessary information and documents for residency removal. It is intended for use by immigration authorities, human resources departments, or housing departments to review and evaluate suitability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the Residency Requirements questions on page 1.
2. Upload the necessary supporting documents on page 2.
3. Sign the declaration on page 3.
4. Select the review option on page 4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Residency Requirements** (`confirm_residency_requirements`, text, required): Answer the questions on this page to confirm your residency requirements. This includes providing details about your current residency status and the reasons for requesting removal.
 
- **Upload Supporting Documents** (`upload_supporting_documents`, text, required): Upload any supporting documents that are required to evaluate your residency status. This may include proof of identity, proof of address, or other relevant documents.

- **Sign Declaration** (`sign_declaration`, text, required): Sign the declaration to confirm that the information provided is accurate and true. This is an important step in the residency removal process.

- **Submit for Review** (`submit_review_request`, select_one, required): Choose the review option that best suits your needs. Options include:
  - Review by Immigration: Select this option if you are seeking residency removal from an immigration authority.
  - Review by HR: Select this option if you are seeking residency removal from a human resources department.
  - Review by Housing: Select this option if you are seeking residency removal from a housing department.
