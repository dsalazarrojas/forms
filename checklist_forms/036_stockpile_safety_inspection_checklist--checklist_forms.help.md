# Stockpile Safety Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stockpile Safety Inspection Checklist is a form used to inspect stockpiles for safety and compliance. It helps stockpilers to systematically evaluate and address potential hazards, ensure compliance with regulations, and maintain accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the stockpile area, identifying any potential hazards or issues that require attention.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your findings.
3. Enter any additional comments or observations in the text fields.
4. Submit the form to ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stockpile Safety Inspection Checklist** (1, text, required): Enter a brief description of the stockpile safety inspection.
* **Stockpile Safety Inspection Notes** (2, note, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the stockpile safety inspection.
* **Hazards** (3, select_multiple, required): Select all hazards identified in the stockpile area.
	+ Option 1: List specific hazards found in the stockpile area.
	+ Option 2: List potential hazards that could potentially be present in the stockpile area.
	+ Option 3: List any previous hazards that have been resolved.
* **Corrective Actions** (4, select_one, required): Select the appropriate corrective action for the hazards identified.
	+ Option 1: Take immediate action to mitigate the hazard.
	+ Option 2: Schedule a follow-up inspection to review the hazard's status.
	+ Option 3: Refer to a higher authority for further guidance.
* **Stockpile Safety Inspection Date** (5, date, required): Enter the date of the stockpile safety inspection.
* **Stockpile Safety Inspection Time** (6, time, optional): Enter the time of the stockpile safety inspection.
* **Stockpile Safety Inspection Frequency** (7, number, required): Select how often the stockpile safety inspection should be performed.
	+ Option 1: Daily.
	+ Option 2: Weekly.
	+ Option 3: Monthly.
	+ Option 4: Quarterly.
* **Stockpile Safety Inspection Last Completed** (8, date, required): Enter the date of the last completed stockpile safety inspection.
* **Stockpile Safety Inspection Next** (9, date, required): Enter the date of the next scheduled stockpile safety inspection.
* **Stockpile Safety Inspection Completed By** (10, select_one, required): Select who completed the stockpile safety inspection.
	+ Option 1: Stockpiler.
	+ Option 2: Supervisor.
	+ Option 3: Third-party Inspector.
	+ Option 4: Other ( specify).
* **Stockpile Safety Inspection Status** (11, select_one, required): Select the status of the stockpile safety inspection.
	+ Option 1: In Progress.

	+ Option 4: Canceled.
* **Stockpile Safety Inspection Inspector Comments** (12, text, required): Enter any comments or observations made during the stockpile safety inspection.
* **Stockpile Safety Inspection Supervisor Comments** (13, text, required): Enter any comments or observations from the stockpile safety inspection supervisor.
* **Stockpile Safety Inspection Area** (14, select_multiple, required): Select the area(s) inspected during the stockpile safety inspection.
