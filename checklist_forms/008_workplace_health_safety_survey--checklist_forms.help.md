# Workplace Health Safety Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Health Safety Survey is designed to help identify and assess workplace health and safety hazards, risks, and measures to improve the work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer each question accurately.
2. Select the relevant options for each question from the provided list.
3. Provide detailed answers in the text fields.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

* **Work Environment Assessment** (`work_environment_assessment`): Select the type of work environment that best describes your workplace. 
	+ `option 1`: Office/ Administrative
	+ `option 2`: Manufacturing/Industrial
	+ `option 3`: Construction/Fieldwork
* **Hazards** (`hazards`): Select all hazards present in your workplace. 
	+ `option 1`: Slip, trip, or fall
	+ `option 2`: Chemicals or chemicals spills
	+ `option 3`: Electrical or fire hazards
* **Work Practice Assessment** (`work_practice_assessment`): Describe your work practices and habits.
* **Workplace Health Safety Risks** (`workplace_health_safety_risks`): Select the type of workplace health safety risk that you are concerned about. 
	+ `option 1`: Workplace violence
	+ `option 2`: Ergonomic hazards
	+ `option 3`: Noise or hearing damage
* **Workplace Health Safety Measures** (`workplace_health_safety_measures`): Describe any existing measures to address the hazards and risks.
* **Action Plan** (`action_plan`): Describe your plan to address the hazards and risks identified.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
