# Flooded Riser Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist in conducting a thorough inspection of a flooded riser. It captures essential details such as location, date, description, and next steps for maintenance or repair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these general steps:
- Select the location where the flooded riser was found.
- Enter the date when the inspection was completed.
- Provide a brief description of the flooded riser, including its location.
- Choose the next steps for the maintenance or repair.
- You may also add any inspection notes if necessary.
- Finally, choose whether the inspection is complete or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a note field meant to provide a brief description of the purpose and context of the form. Please fill it out to give a clear understanding of the inspection process.
* **Location**: Select the location where the flooded riser was found. This field is used to determine the specific site where the inspection took place.
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date when the inspection was completed. This date is crucial for tracking and referencing the inspection report.
* **Site Address**: Enter a description or address of the site where the flooded riser was inspected.
* **Description Completed**: Describe the condition or the state of the flooded riser. This can include details about its location, any visible signs of damage, and other relevant observations.
* **Riser Location**: Enter the location of the flooded riser within the inspected site. This can be in a specific room or area, such as a particular floor or wing.
* **Inspection Notes**: Add any additional notes about the inspection process or findings. This can include photos or other relevant documentation.
* **Photos Taken**: Indicate if photos of the flooded riser were taken during the inspection.
* **Next Steps**: Detail the next actions to be taken for the maintenance or repair of the flooded riser.
* **Completed**: Mark the inspection as complete or indicate that it requires further review or action.
