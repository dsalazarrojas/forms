# Pre Camp Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Camp Inspection Checklist is designed to ensure a safe and enjoyable camp experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and complete all the fields according to your observations.
2. Check the "required" fields as they are essential for the campsite's safety and functionality.
3. Use the "hint" provided as a guide for any additional information you may need.
4. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the campsite's management or the relevant authorities for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre Camp** (`pre_camp_inspection_checklist_pre_camp`, text, optional): This field is to be filled with the name of the campsite or location of the camp.
* **Water Sources** (`water_sources`, text, optional): List all the water sources available at the campsite, such as water tanks, wells, or water taps.
* **Firewood Areas** (`firewood_areas`, text, optional): Identify all areas where firewood is kept, such as firewood stores or storage areas.
* **Firewood Stores** (`firewood_stores`, text, optional): List all firewood stores or areas where firewood is kept.
* **Firewood Storage** (`firewood_storage`, text, optional): Identify the area or location where firewood is stored.
* **Cooking Areas** (`cooking_areas`, text, optional): List all the areas where cooking is done, such as kitchen or cooking stations.
* **Waste Management** (`waste_management`, text, optional): Identify all the measures in place for waste management, such as waste disposal or recycling facilities.
* **Waste Storage** (`waste_storage`, text, optional): List all areas or locations where waste is stored.
* **Campsites** (`campsites`, text, optional): List all the campsites or areas where people may set up tents or camps.
* **Emergency Procedures** (`emergency_procedures`, text, optional): List all emergency procedures in place, such as first aid kits, emergency phone numbers, etc.
* **Firstaid Kit** (`firstaidkit`, text, optional): Identify the location of the first aid kit.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the contact information of the emergency contact person or number.
* **Fire Prevention** (`fire_prevention`, text, optional): Identify all fire prevention measures in place, such as fire extinguishers, fire alarms, etc.
* **Firstaid Equipment** (`firstaid_equipment`, text, optional): List all first aid equipment available, such as bandages, antiseptic cream, etc.
* **Emergency Exits** (`emergency_exits`, text, optional): List all emergency exits or escape routes.
* **Fire Suppression** (`fire_suppression`, text, optional): Identify all fire suppression systems or measures in place, such as fire alarms, sprinklers, etc.
* **Emergency Equipment** (`emergency_equipment`, text, optional): List all emergency equipment available, such as first aid kits, emergency phones, etc.
* **Emergency Exit Lights** (`emergency_exit_lights`, text, optional): Identify all emergency exit lights or lighting in place.
* **Fire Extinguishers** (`fire_extinguishers`, text, optional): List all fire extinguishers available.
* **Firstaid Training** (`firstaid_training`, text, optional): Identify the first aid training provided to the camp staff or visitors.
* **Fire Suppression System** (`fire_suppression_system`, text, optional): Identify the fire suppression system in place.
* **Emergency Exit Signs** (`emergency_exit_signs`, text, optional): List all emergency exit signs or signs indicating emergency exits.
* **Fire Safety Equipment** (`fire_safety_equipment`, text, optional): List all fire safety equipment, such as fire alarms, fire extinguishers, etc.
* **Emergency Equipment Location** (`emergency_equipment_location`, text, optional): Identify the location of emergency equipment.
* **Emergency Exit Location** (`emergency_exit_location`, text, optional): Identify the location of emergency exits.

**Tips**

* Please review the form carefully before submission.
* Ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the campsite's management or the relevant authorities for guidance.
* The form should be completed according to your observations and experiences at the campsite.
