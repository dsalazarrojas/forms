# Arbitration Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and verify the completion of tasks related to arbitration cases.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the case information section, which includes:
	* Case Number: Enter the reference number for the case.
	* Case Title: Enter the name of the case.
	* Assigned Arbitrator Name: Enter the name of the assigned arbitrator.
2. Proceed to the documentation section and indicate the status of:
	* Initial Claim Filed: Whether the initial claim has been filed and served.
	* Response to Claim Filed: Whether the response to the claim has been filed.
	* Evidence Documentation Submitted: Whether evidence documentation has been submitted.
	* Witness Statements: Whether witness statements have been prepared (optional, but recommended).
3. Next, confirm the party preparation section:
	* All Parties Notified: Whether all parties have been notified of the hearing date.
	* Legal Representation Confirmed: Whether legal representation has been confirmed for each party.
4. Finally, complete the logistics section:
	* Pre-Hearing Conference: Whether a pre-hearing conference has been completed (optional).
	* All Required Disclosures Exchanged: Whether all required disclosures have been exchanged.
	* Hearing Venue Confirmed and Accessible: Whether the hearing venue has been confirmed and is accessible.
	* Technology Setup Tested (if virtual): Whether the technology setup has been tested for virtual hearings.
5. Complete the checklist by filling out the remaining fields:
	* Checklist Completion Date: Enter the date the form was completed.
	* Prepared By: Enter the name of the person who prepared the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Case Number**: Enter the reference number for the case.
* **Case Title**: Enter the name of the case.
* **Assigned Arbitrator Name**: Enter the name of the assigned arbitrator.
* **Initial Claim Filed**: Indicate whether the initial claim has been filed and served.
* **Response to Claim Filed**: Indicate whether the response to the claim has been filed.
* **Evidence Documentation Submitted**: Indicate whether evidence documentation has been submitted.
* **Witness Statements**: Indicate whether witness statements have been prepared (optional, but recommended).
* **All Parties Notified**: Confirm that all parties have been notified of the hearing date.
* **Legal Representation Confirmed**: Confirm that legal representation has been confirmed for each party.
* **Pre-Hearing Conference**: Indicate whether a pre-hearing conference has been completed (optional).
* **All Required Disclosures Exchanged**: Indicate whether all required disclosures have been exchanged.
* **Hearing Venue Confirmed and Accessible**: Indicate that the hearing venue has been confirmed and is accessible.
* **Technology Setup Tested**: If the hearing is virtual, indicate whether the technology setup has been tested.
* **Checklist Completion Date**: Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Prepared By**: Enter the name of the person who prepared the form.
