# Pre Delivery Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Delivery Inspection Form is a critical document that helps ensure the vehicle meets the customer's expectations and requirements. It is a comprehensive checklist that covers various aspects of the vehicle, including its condition, specifications, and details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Gather all necessary documentation and information about the vehicle, including its VIN, license plate number, and odometer reading.
2. Fill out the vehicle information section, including the vehicle's license plate number, VIN, and year model.
3. Specify the vehicle's engine type, fuel level, and transmission type.
4. Assess the vehicle's exterior and interior condition, selecting the appropriate options from the provided lists.
5. Provide information about the vehicle's mileage, weight, and year of manufacture.
6. Select the vehicle's color and make.
7. Enter the vehicle's year model (twice, as there are two fields for this information).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle License Plate Number** (`vehicle_license_plate`, required): Enter the vehicle's license plate number.
* **Vehicle VIN** (`vehicle_vin`, optional): Enter the vehicle's Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
* **Odometer Reading** (`odometer_reading`, required): Enter the current odometer reading.
* **Engine Type** (`engine_type`, required): Select the type of engine the vehicle is equipped with (Gasoline, Electric, or Hybrid).
* **Fuel Level** (`fuel_level`, required): Select the level of fuel in the vehicle (Full, Quarter, or Empty).
* **Exterior Condition** (`exterior_condition`, required): Select the exterior condition of the vehicle (Clean, Stains, or Dents).
* **Interior Condition** (`interior_condition`, required): Select the interior condition of the vehicle (Clean, Stains, or Odor).
* **Mileage** (`mileage`, required): Enter the vehicle's current mileage.
* **Year Model** (`year_model`, required): Enter the vehicle's year model.
* **Vehicle Weight** (`vehicle_weight`, required): Enter the vehicle's weight.
* **Transmission** (`transmission`, required): Select the type of transmission (Automatic or Manual).
* **Color** (`color`, required): Select the vehicle's color.
* **Make** (`make`, required): Enter the vehicle's make.
* **Model** (`model`, required): Enter the vehicle's model.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, required): Enter the vehicle's year of manufacture.
* **Vehicle Year 2** (`vehicle_year2`, optional): Enter an additional year of manufacture (if applicable).
* **Vehicle Year 3** (`vehicle_year3`, optional): Enter another additional year of manufacture (if applicable).
* **Vehicle Year 4** (`vehicle_year4`, optional): Enter a fourth year of manufacture (if applicable).
