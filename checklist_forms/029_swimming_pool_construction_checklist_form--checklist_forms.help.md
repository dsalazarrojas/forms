# swimming_pool_construction_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive checklist for swimming pool construction projects. It is used to document and verify the completion of critical tasks and inspections to ensure a safe and quality construction process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by reviewing the construction plan (page 3) and selecting the applicable plan (Plan A, Plan B, or Plan C).
2. Complete the electrical checklist (page 4) by selecting the items that apply to your project (Electrical System, Electrical Panel, and/or Electrical Outlet).
3. Complete the plumbing checklist (page 5) by selecting the items that apply to your project (Plumbing System, Plumbing Fixture, and/or Water Heater).
4. Complete the safety checklist (page 6) by selecting the items that apply to your project (Safety Equipment and/or Safety Sign).
5. Complete the inspection checklist (page 7) by selecting the items that apply to your project (Inspection Item 1, Inspection Item 2, and/or Inspection Item 3).
6. Complete the certification checklist (page 8) by selecting the items that apply to your project (Certification Item 1, Certification Item 2, and/or Certification Item 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### construction_checklist
* **Construction Checklist** (`construction_checklist`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used for additional notes or comments regarding the construction project.

### contractor_info
* **Contractor Information** (`contractor_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for entering any relevant information about the contractor, such as their name, contact number, or email.

### construction_plan_review
* **Construction Plan Review** (`construction_plan_review`, `select_one`, required): Select the applicable construction plan (Plan A, Plan B, or Plan C) to review and confirm.

### electrical_checklist
* **Electrical Checklist** (`electrical_checklist`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable items from the electrical checklist (Electrical System, Electrical Panel, and/or Electrical Outlet).

### plumbing_checklist
* **Plumbing Checklist** (`plumbing_checklist`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable items from the plumbing checklist (Plumbing System, Plumbing Fixture, and/or Water Heater).

### safety_checklist
* **Safety Checklist** (`safety_checklist`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable items from the safety checklist (Safety Equipment and/or Safety Sign).

### inspection_checklist
* **Inspection Checklist** (`inspection_checklist`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable items from the inspection checklist (Inspection Item 1, Inspection Item 2, and/or Inspection Item 3).

### certification_checklist
* **Certification Checklist** (`certification_checklist`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable items from the certification checklist (Certification Item 1, Certification Item 2, and/or Certification Item 3).

## Tips
* Make sure to thoroughly review and complete each section carefully to ensure accurate documentation.
* Use the form as a guide to help you stay organized and focused during the construction process.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any item, refer to your construction project plan or contact your contractor for guidance.
