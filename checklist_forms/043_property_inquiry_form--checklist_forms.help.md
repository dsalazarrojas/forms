# Property Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect property inquiry information from users. It is used to gather details about properties such as address, contact information, property type, price range, square footage, and year built.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the property type from the "Property" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your contact information, choosing one of the "Contact Method" options (Email, Phone, or Mail).
3. Enter your contact number and contact name.
4. Enter your contact email address.
5. Add any additional contact notes as needed.
6. Select the price range from the "Price Range" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the square footage of the property.
8. Enter the number of bedrooms and bathrooms.
9. Enter the year the property was built.
10. Select the year built range from the "Year Built Range" dropdown menu.
11. Enter the lot size of the property.
12. Enter the lot square footage.
13. Select the "Contact Method" for the second property from the same dropdown menu as before.
14. Enter the "Contact Number" and "Contact Name" for the second property.
15. Enter the "Contact Email" address for the second property.
16. Add any additional "Contact Notes" as needed.
17. Select the "Property" type for the second property from the "Property" dropdown menu.
18. Select the price range from the "Price Range" dropdown menu for the second property.
19. Enter the square footage of the second property.
20. Enter the number of bedrooms and bathrooms of the second property.
21. Enter the year built of the second property.
22. Select the "Year Built Range" for the second property.
23. Enter the lot size of the second property.
24. Enter the lot square footage of the second property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the property.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Mail).
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact name.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Note** (`Contact Note`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional contact notes as needed.
* **Property** (`Property`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the property type (Single Family, Multi Family, or Commercial).
* **Price** (`Price`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the price of the property.
* **Price Range** (`Price Range`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the price range of the property (Less than $1M, $1-5M, $5-10M, or Over $10M).
* **Sq Ft** (`Sq Ft`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the square footage of the property.
* **Bedrooms** (`Bedrooms`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
* **Bathrooms** (`Bathrooms`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of bathrooms in the property.
* **Year Built** (`Year Built`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the year the property was built.
* **Year Built Range** (`Year Built Range`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the year built range of the property (1900-1950, 1950-1969, 1970-1999, 2000-2019, or 2020+).
* **Lot Size** (`Lot Size`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the lot size of the property.
* **Lot Sq Ft** (`Lot Sq Ft`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the lot square footage of the property.
* **Contact Method Label 2** (`Contact Method Label 2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted for the second property (Email, Phone, or Mail).
* **Contact Number Label 2** (`Contact Number Label 2`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your contact number for the second property.
* **Contact Name Label 2** (`Contact Name Label 2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact name for the second property.
* **Contact Email Label 2** (`Contact Email Label 2`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your contact email address for the second property.
* **Contact Note Label 2** (`Contact Note Label 2`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional contact notes as needed for the second property.
* **Property Label 2** (`Property Label 2`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the property type for the second property (Single Family, Multi Family, or Commercial).
* **Price Label 2** (`Price Label 2`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the price of the second property.
* **Price Range Label 2** (`Price Range Label 2`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the price range of the second property (Less than $1M, $1-5M, $5-10M, or Over $10M).
* **Sqft Label 2** (`Sqft Label 2`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the square footage of the second property.
* **Bedrooms Label 2** (`Bedrooms Label 2`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of bedrooms in the second property.
* **Bathrooms Label 2** (`Bathrooms Label 2`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of bathrooms in the second property.
* **Year Built Label 2** (`Year Built Label 2`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the year the second property was built.
* **Year Built Range Label 2** (`Year Built Range Label 2`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the year built range of the second property (1900-1950, 1950-1969, 1970-1999, 2000-2019, or 2020+).
* **Lot Size Label 2** (`Lot Size Label 2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the lot size of the second property.
* **Lot Sq Ft Label 2** (`Lot Sq Ft Label 2`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the lot square footage of the second property.
