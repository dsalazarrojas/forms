# Patient Admission Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential patient information during the admission process. It is intended to be a comprehensive checklist that helps healthcare professionals gather accurate and up-to-date information about the patient's personal details, medical history, allergies, medical certificiations, family history, contact information, and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide accurate answers.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in.
3. If you are unsure about any question or field, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Details Form**: This section collects basic contact information about the patient, including their name, date of birth, and address. This information is essential for our records and future reference. (<a href='#personal_details_form'></a>)
- **Medical History Form**: In this section, please provide a brief description of the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medications. This helps us understand their health status and provide better care. (<a href='#medical_history_form'></a>)
- **Allergies Form**: If the patient has any allergies or sensitivities, please list them here. This includes food, medication, or other substances that may cause a reaction. (<a href='#allergies_form'></a>)
- **Medical Certifications Form**: Please provide any medical certifications or licenses held by the patient, such as driver's license or government-issued ID. (<a href='#medical_certifications_form'></a>)
- **Family History Form**: This section is about the patient's family medical history, including any known illnesses or conditions that may affect their health. Please provide details about their parents, siblings, or children. (<a href='#family_history_form'></a>)
- **Contact Info Form**: Please provide accurate contact information for the patient, including phone numbers, email, and address. This ensures that we can reach out to them for follow-up care or emergencies. (<a href='#contact_info_form'></a>)
- **Emergency Contact Form**: In case of an emergency, please provide the contact information for an authorized emergency contact person. This could be a family member, friend, or neighbor. (<a href='#emergency_contact_form'></a>)
- **Next Of Kin Form**: If the patient is not available, please provide contact information for their next of kin or legal guardian. This helps us ensure that the patient receives the best possible care. (<a href='#next_of_kin_form'></a>)

Note: Each field is labeled with its corresponding id from the YAML schema, to facilitate easy navigation.
