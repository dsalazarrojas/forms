# Checklist For Rare Items - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to create and manage records for rare items in your collection. This includes documenting item identification, provenance, and condition information to help ensure accurate and consistent cataloging and management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the required information for the selected rare item.
2. For each field, make sure to provide accurate and detailed information to ensure the item's record is up-to-date and consistent.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided all necessary information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Item Name / Title (2)**: This field is for the official title or name of the rare item. Please enter the exact name or title as it appears on the item or in the item's documentation.
* **Catalog / Inventory ID (3)**: This field is for the unique identifier for the item in your collection's catalog or inventory system. If you don't have an ID, please leave this field blank.
* **Item Category (4)**: Select the correct category for the rare item from the provided options (Manuscript / Book, Artifact / Object, Artwork, Specimen, Coin / Currency, or Other).
* **Rarity Level (5)**: Choose the correct rarity level for the item from the provided options (Common (Reference), Uncommon, Rare, Extremely Rare, or Unique).
* **Acquisition Source (8)**: This field is for the source of the item's acquisition (Donor, Auction House, or Vendor).
* **Acquisition Date Logged (7)**: Enter the date the item was acquired, or the date it was logged into the collection.
* **Inspector Name (16)**: This field is for the name of the person who inspected and verified the item's condition.
* **Condition Change Notes (17)**: Provide any notes on changes to the item's condition since its last inspection.
* **Verified (18)**: Confirm that the catalog entry has been reviewed and verified as accurate.
* **Estimated Valuation ($) (9)**: Enter the estimated value of the rare item, for insurance purposes.
* **Current Condition Grade (11)**: Select the current condition of the item from the provided options (Mint / Pristine, Excellent, Good, Fair (Stable), Poor (Fragile), or Needs Urgent Conservation).
* **Storage Environment (13)**: Choose the correct storage environment for the item from the provided options (Climate Controlled Vault, Standard Archive Room, Display Case (Public), or Off-site Storage).
* **Date of Last Inspection (15)**: Enter the date of the last inspection or assessment of the item's condition.
* **Notes on Condition Changes (17)**: Provide any additional notes on changes to the item's condition since its last inspection.
* **Last Inspection Date (15)**: Enter the date of the last inspection or assessment of the item's condition.
* **Storage Environment (13)**: Choose the correct storage environment for the item from the provided options (Climate Controlled Vault, Standard Archive Room, Display Case (Public), or Off-site Storage).

Note: This form is designed to be completed by experts, and some fields may require additional information or clarification beyond the provided explanations. If you are unsure about a field or require additional guidance, please refer to your institution's collection policies or consult with a relevant expert.
