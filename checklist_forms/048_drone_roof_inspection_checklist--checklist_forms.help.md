# Drone Roof Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report on the condition of a roof after a drone inspection. It is a checklist to ensure that all necessary information is gathered and recorded during the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each field based on your observation and findings during the inspection.
2. Choose the correct options from the provided list for fields like "roof_type", "drone_used", and "weather_condition".
3. Enter the required information for fields like "inspector_name", "inspector_company", and "date_inspected".
4. Add any additional comments or notes as needed in the "comments" field.
5. Sign and date the form to indicate completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Roof Condition** (`roof_condition`, text, required/optional): Report the overall condition of the roof, e.g., "excellent", "good", "fair", or "poor".
* **Roof Leaks** (`leaks`, select_one, required/optional): Check if there are any leaks in the roof. Choose from "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Damage Type** (`damage`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all types of damage visible on the roof. Choose from "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Photos Taken** (`photos_taken`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of photos taken during the inspection.
* **Date Inspected** (`date_inspected`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Time Inspected** (`time_inspected`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the inspector.
* **Inspector Company** (`inspector_company`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the inspector's company.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Inspector Email** (`email_inspector`, email, required/optional): Enter the email of the inspector.
* **Inspector Phone** (`phone_inspector`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the inspector.
* **Roof Size** (`roof_size`, number, required/optional): Enter the size of the roof.
* **Roof Type** (`roof_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of roof, e.g., "flat", "pitched", or "combination".
* **Inspector License** (`inspector_license`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all relevant licenses held by the inspector. Choose from "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Inspection Area** (`inspection_area`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all areas of the inspection. Choose from "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Weather Condition** (`weather_condition`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the weather condition of the inspection, e.g., "sunny", "rainy", or "cloudy".
* **Drone Used** (`drone_used`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of drone used, e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments about the inspection.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional): Sign and date the form to indicate completion.
* **Inspector Notes** (`inspector_notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes from the inspector.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign and date the form for the client.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes from the client.
* **Drone Type** (`drone_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of drone used, e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Flight Time** (`flight_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the flight time of the drone.
* **Form Completion** (`form_completion`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how the form was completed, e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".

## Tips

* Make sure to complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
* Review each field for relevance and importance.
* Sign and date the form to ensure completion.
* Keep the "additional notes" fields for any extra information that may not fit in the other fields.
* This form is designed to be completed by the inspector, but it may be useful for clients to provide feedback and additional information as well.
