# Vendor Due Diligence Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Due Diligence Checklist is a form used to collect information about a potential or existing vendor. This form is designed to gather essential details to help you evaluate and assess the vendor's qualifications and suitability for your organization's business needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the vendor from the available options.
2. Fill in the vendor's name, ensuring it's accurately spelled and formatted.
3. Enter the vendor's description, highlighting their key features and services.
4. Provide the vendor's address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
5. Enter the vendor's phone number, email address, and website.
6. Confirm the vendor's license information, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vendor_due_diligence_checklist** (`vendor due diligence checklist`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description or title summarizing the vendor's services or products.
* **category** (`category`, select one, required/optional): Choose a category that best describes the type of service or product the vendor offers.
* **vendor_name** (`vendor name`, text, required/optional): Enter the vendor's name, ensuring it's accurately spelled and formatted.
* **contact_person** (`contact person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and title of the person responsible for the vendor's account management or contact.
* **vendor_description** (`vendor description`, text, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of the vendor's services or products.
* **vendor_address** (`vendor address`, text, required/optional): Enter the vendor's physical address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **vendor_phone** (`vendor phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the vendor's phone number, including country code and extension if applicable.
* **vendor_email** (`vendor email`, email, required/optional): Enter the vendor's email address.
* **vendor_website** (`vendor website`, text, required/optional): Enter the vendor's website URL.
* **vendor_license** (`vendor license`, text, required/optional): Enter information about the vendor's licenses, certifications, or qualifications.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check the vendor's information for accuracy before submission.
* Verify the vendor's information with other sources, such as their website or third-party databases, for added validation.
* Keep in mind that this form is for internal use only, and the information collected will be used to evaluate the vendor's qualifications.
