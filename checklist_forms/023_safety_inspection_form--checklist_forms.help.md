# Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Inspection Form is used to collect data on the safety conditions and status of various areas within the facility, allowing for the tracking of issues, defects, and progress towards addressing them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the corresponding options for each field based on your observations.
2. Enter accurate information for each field, including dates and times if required.
3. Ensure that you fill out all required fields.

* This field is a text section for general notes or comments.

### first_question (select_one) (required: false)
