# Lifting Sling Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lifting Sling Inspection Checklist is used to record the inspection and maintenance of lifting slings. This form helps to identify any issues or defects in the sling and ensure that it is safe to use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Inspection Date** in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
2. Enter the **Site Address** where the inspection was performed.
3. Select the **Sling Type** from the options provided.
4. Select the **Sling Condition** from the available options. This will help identify if the sling is new, used, or worn.
5. Choose the **Hook Type** from the options provided.
6. Enter the **Load Capacity** if it is relevant to the inspection.
7. Enter the **Load Weight** if it is relevant to the inspection.
8. Enter the **Lift Height** if it is relevant to the inspection.
9. Enter the **Lift Distance** if it is relevant to the inspection.
10. Enter the **Lift Velocity** if it is relevant to the inspection.
11. Select the **Lift Direction** from the options provided.
12. Choose the **Sling Inspection Frequency** from the options provided.
13. Select the **Inspection Result** from the options provided.
14. Enter any **Inspection Comments** relevant to the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Site Address**: Enter the address where the inspection was performed.
* **Sling Type**: Select the type of sling being inspected (Lifting Sling, Picking Sling, or Rigging Sling).
* **Sling Condition**: Select the condition of the sling (New, Used, or Worn).
* **Hook Type**: Select the type of hook being used (Single Leg, Double Leg, or Adjustable).
* **Load Capacity**: Enter the maximum load capacity of the sling if it is relevant to the inspection.
* **Load Weight**: Enter the weight of the load being lifted if it is relevant to the inspection.
* **Lift Height**: Enter the height at which the load was lifted if it is relevant to the inspection.
* **Lift Distance**: Enter the distance over which the load was lifted if it is relevant to the inspection.
* **Lift Velocity**: Enter the velocity at which the load was lifted if it is relevant to the inspection.
* **Lift Direction**: Select the direction in which the load was lifted (Forward or Reverse).
* **Sling Inspection Frequency**: Select the frequency of inspections for the sling (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Inspection Result**: Select the outcome of the inspection (Passed, Failed, or Deferred).
* **Inspection Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations related to the inspection.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
- If a field is not relevant to the inspection, do not enter any information.
- Use clear and concise language in the **Inspection Comments** field.
- This form is intended to be used by authorized personnel only.
- If you are unsure about any aspect of the inspection, do not hesitate to seek guidance from a supervisor or expert.
