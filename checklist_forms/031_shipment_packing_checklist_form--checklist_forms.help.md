# shipment_packing_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for tracking and documenting shipping details, including package contents, shipping carriers, and other related information. It can be used by logistics or transportation companies, freight forwarders, or anyone involved in the shipping process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the shipping details, including any relevant information about the shipment.
2. Enter package contents, if applicable.
3. Provide the shipment address, if necessary.
4. Add any special handling instructions or additional information.
5. Enter any payment information, if required.
6. Select a shipping carrier, if applicable.
7. Add any relevant notes or comments about the shipment.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, required): Enter any relevant information about the shipment, such as the shipper's name and contact details.
* **Package Info** (`package_info`, text, required): List the items contained in the package, including any specific details about them.
* **Package Contents** (`package_contents`, text, required): Describe what's inside the package, including any fragile or high-value items.
* **Shipment Address** (`shipment_address`, text, required): Enter the delivery address, including any relevant information about the recipient or shipper.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required): Enter any payment information related to the shipment, such as payment method or amount.
* **Shipping Carrier** (`shipping_carrier`, text, required): Select the shipping carrier that will handle the shipment.
* **Special Handling Info** (`special_handling_info`, text, required): Enter any special handling instructions or additional information, such as customs forms or special care requirements.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the shipment, if required.
## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete documentation of the shipment.
* Double-check that you've included all necessary information, especially payment and shipment details.
* Save the completed form for future reference or to share with others involved in the shipping process.
