# Cleaning Service Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the terms and conditions of a cleaning service agreement between a service provider and client. It outlines the service provider's and client's responsibilities, pricing, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Service Provider Information (Pages 2-4)**: Enter the service provider's name, address, phone number, and email address.
2. **Client Information (Pages 5-7)**: Enter the client's name, address, phone number, and email address.
3. **Agreement Details (Pages 8-11)**: Select the agreement date, service start and end dates or duration, and service frequency.
4. **Pricing and Payment Terms (Pages 12-15)**: Enter the service cost, cost structure, accepted payment methods, and payment due date.
5. **Terms and Conditions (Pages 16-18)**: Review the late payment policy and cancellation notice requirements.
6. **Signatures and Acceptance (Pages 19-20)**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Provider Name (Page 2)**: Enter the full name of the service provider (e.g., "ABC Cleaning Service").
* **Provider Address (Page 3)**: Enter the service provider's business address (e.g., "123 Main St, Anytown USA").
* **Provider Phone Number (Page 3)**: Enter the service provider's contact phone number.
* **Provider Email (Page 4)**: Enter the service provider's email address.
* **License Number (Page 4)**: (Optional) Enter the service provider's business license or certification number, if applicable.
* **Client Name (Page 5)**: Enter the full name of the client (e.g., "John Doe").
* **Client Address (Page 6)**: Enter the client's residential or business address (e.g., "456 Elm St, Othertown USA").
* **Client Phone Number (Page 6)**: Enter the client's contact phone number.
* **Client Email (Page 7)**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Agreement Date (Page 8)**: Select the date the agreement is signed.
* **Service Start Date (Page 9)**: Select the date services will begin.
* **Service End Date or Duration (Page 10)**: Enter the duration or end date for the services.
* **Service Frequency (Page 11)**: Select how often the cleaning services will occur (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
* **Description of Services (Page 11)**: Enter a detailed list of services to be provided (e.g., "General cleaning, laundry, and window cleaning").
* **Service Cost (Page 12)**: Enter the price per service or monthly cost for the services.
* **Cost Structure (Page 12)**: Select how the cost is calculated (e.g., per visit, monthly, quarterly).
* **Accepted Payment Methods (Page 13)**: Select the payment methods accepted by the service provider (e.g., credit card, check, bank transfer).
* **Payment Due Date (Page 14)**: Select when payment is due (e.g., immediately after service, monthly invoice).
* **Late Payment Policy (Page 14)**: Enter the policy for late payments.
* **Cancellation Notice Required (Page 15)**: Enter the number of days notice required for cancellation.
* **Minimum Contract Duration (Page 15)**: Enter the minimum commitment period (optional).
* **Supplies Responsibility (Page 16)**: Select who is responsible for cleaning supplies (e.g., service provider, client).
* **Special Access Instructions (Page 17)**: Enter any special instructions for the service provider's access to the client's property.
* **Damage Liability Coverage (Page 17)**: Enter the policy for handling damages.
* **Insurance Required from Provider (Page 18)**: Select if the service provider is required to have insurance.
* **Both Parties Agree (Page 19)**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Agreement Version/ID (Page 20)**: Enter an internal reference number for the agreement.

**Tips**: Please review the entire form carefully to ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and completely. If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the service provider or consult with a representative.
