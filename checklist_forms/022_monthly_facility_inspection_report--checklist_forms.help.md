# Monthly Facility Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for submitting and documenting the monthly facility inspection report.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting the form, please ensure that you are authorized to access the facility you are inspecting, and that you have all necessary information and documentation. 

1. Enter the **facility ID** (field 1) - This is a unique identifier for the facility being inspected.
2. Select the **month ID** (field 2) - This is the month you are inspecting the facility.
3. Enter the **issue ID** (field 3) - This is the specific issue or item you are inspecting.
4. Enter any **notes** (field 4) - Additional comments or observations about the facility inspection.
5. Enter **facility notes** (field 5) - Any additional notes or comments about the facility.
6. Enter the **facility email** (field 6) - The email address of the facility.
7. Enter the **facility phone** (field 7) - The phone number of the facility.
8. Select the **assigned to** (field 8) - Who the report is assigned to. (If not applicable, leave blank)
9. Enter the **comments** (field 9) - Additional comments or feedback about the facility inspection.
10. Select the **facility inspection report date** (field 10) - The date of the facility inspection.
11. Select the **assigned tool** (field 11) - The tool used for the facility inspection.
12. Enter the **facility address** (field 12) - The physical address of the facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **facility ID** (field 1, text, required): Enter a unique identifier for the facility being inspected.
* **month ID** (field 2, number, required): Select the month you are inspecting the facility.
* **issue ID** (field 3, number, required): Enter the specific issue or item you are inspecting.
* **notes** (field 4, text, optional): Enter additional comments or observations about the facility inspection.
* **facility notes** (field 5, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the facility.
* **facility email** (field 6, email, optional): Enter the email address of the facility.
* **facility phone** (field 7, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the facility.
* **assigned to** (field 8, text, optional): Select who the report is assigned to. (If not applicable, leave blank)
* **comments** (field 9, text, optional): Enter additional comments or feedback about the facility inspection.
* **facility inspection report date** (field 10, date, required): Select the date of the facility inspection.
* **assigned tool** (field 11, select_one, required): Select the tool used for the facility inspection.
* **facility address** (field 12, text, optional): Enter the physical address of the facility.

## Tips

* Be accurate and clear when filling out this form.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are complete.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to this guide for clarification.
