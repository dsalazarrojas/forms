# Fire Hydrant Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Hydrant Inspection Form is used to collect information about fire hydrants in a specific area, such as their name, type, status, installation date, and last inspection date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the fire hydrant.
2. Select the type of the fire hydrant from the list provided.
3. Select the current status of the fire hydrant.
4. Enter the date the fire hydrant was installed.
5. If applicable, enter the date of the last inspection.
6. Add any notes or comments about the fire hydrant.
7. Enter the name of the inspector who conducted the inspection.
8. Enter the title of the inspector who conducted the inspection.
9. Enter the phone number of the inspector who conducted the inspection.
10. Enter the email of the inspector who conducted the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fire Hydrant Name**: The name of the fire hydrant, such as the location or building it is installed in.
* **Hydrant Type**: The type of fire hydrant, such as the model or make.
	+ Select the correct type from the provided list.
* **Hydrant Status**: The current status of the fire hydrant, such as functioning or not functioning.
	+ Select the correct status from the provided list.
* **Date Installed**: The date the fire hydrant was installed.
* **Last Inspected**: The date of the last inspection (optional).
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or information about the fire hydrant.
* **Inspector Comments**: Any comments or feedback from the inspector (optional).
* **Inspector Name**: The name of the inspector who conducted the inspection.
* **Inspector Title**: The title of the inspector who conducted the inspection.
* **Inspector Phone**: The phone number of the inspector who conducted the inspection.
* **Inspector Email**: The email of the inspector who conducted the inspection.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid any issues or discrepancies in the data.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or the asset owner for clarification.
* Please review all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Some fields are optional, and you can skip them if they are not applicable or relevant to the inspection.
