**Container Loading Procedure Checklist - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is used to record the container loading procedure in a systematic and accurate manner, ensuring that all relevant checks and tasks are completed before a container is loaded or unloaded.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each field carefully and accurately based on the actual events during the container loading process.
2. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of fields. Complete each section according to the labels and instructions provided.
3. Ensure that you complete all required fields before proceeding to the next section.
4. Review and confirm the accuracy of the information provided by selecting "I Agree" on the signature confirmation field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Container Identification Number** (`container_id`, `text`, required): Enter the unique identification number of the container being loaded or unloaded.
* **Loading Date** (`loading_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the loading or unloading process.
* **Loading Time** (`loading_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the loading or unloading process.
* **Stage 1 - Initial Inspection Completed?** (`stage_1_complete`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if the initial inspection was completed. Select "True" if it was completed, "False" otherwise.
* **Stage 2 - Pre-loading Checks Completed?** (`stage_2_complete`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if the pre-loading checks were completed. Select "True" if they were completed, "False" otherwise.
* **Stage 3 - Loading Process Completed?** (`stage_3_complete`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if the loading process was completed. Select "True" if it was completed, "False" otherwise.
* **Stage 4 - Final Verification Completed?** (`stage_4_complete`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if the final verification was completed. Select "True" if it was completed, "False" otherwise.
* **Security Seal Integrity?** (`security_seal_integrity`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if the security seal integrity was maintained during the loading or unloading process. Select "True" if it was maintained, "False" otherwise.
* **Adherence to Loading Plan?** (`loading_plan_adherence`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if the loading or unloading process adhered to the plan. Select "True" if it did, "False" otherwise.
* **Safety Equipment Used?** (`safety_equipment_used`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if safety equipment was used during the loading or unloading process. Select "True" if it was used, "False" otherwise.
* **Supervisor Full Name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the supervisor who signed off on the loading or unloading process.
* **Supervisor Identification Number** (`supervisor_id`, `text`, required): Enter the identification number of the supervisor who signed off on the loading or unloading process.
* **Loading Completed?** (`loading_completed`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if the loading or unloading process was completed. Select "True" if it was completed, "False" otherwise.
* **Any additional details or comments?** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional details or comments regarding the loading or unloading process.
* **I confirm that the information provided is accurate** (`signature_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Select "I Agree" to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Date of Sign-off** (`date_of_sign_off`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the sign-off by the supervisor.

Please review and complete the form carefully, ensuring that all required fields are filled out accurately. The supervisor's sign-off is a critical step in the process, confirming that the information provided is accurate.
