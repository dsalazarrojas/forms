# Pharmacy Weekend Work Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pharmacy Weekend Work Checklist is a form designed for pharmacy staff to document their weekend work, ensuring accurate and timely reporting of tasks and any issues that occurred.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the relevant checklists completed during your shift. You can choose one or multiple checklists, depending on the tasks completed.
2.  Choose the status of your current task, if applicable.
3.  Enter any notes related to your shift or tasks completed.
4.  Select the date and time you completed your work.
5.  Enter your contact information: email and phone number.
6.  If you need to contact you at work, enter your work address.
7.  Choose the task you have completed, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Weekend Work Checklist

*   **Weekend Work Checklist** (`pharmacy_weekend_work_checklist`, select_multiple): Select one or multiple checklists completed during your shift from the options provided. This field is optional.
*   **Task Status** (`task_status`, number): If you have tasks assigned to you, select your current status in the provided options. This field is optional.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, text): Enter any relevant notes about your shift or tasks completed. This field is optional.
*   **Date** (`date`, date): Enter the date you completed your work. Please use the format: YYYY-MM-DD. This field is optional.
*   **Time** (`time`, time): Enter the time you completed your work. Please use the format: HH:MM:SS. This field is optional.
*   **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your contact email. This field is optional.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text): Enter your contact phone number. This field is optional.
*   **Address** (`address`, text): Enter your work address, if you need to be contacted at work. This field is optional.
*   **Task Completed** (`task_completed`, select_one): If you have completed a task, select the corresponding option from the list. This field is optional.

**Tips**

*   Always complete the form as soon as possible after each shift.
*   Be accurate and thorough when completing the form.
*   If you have questions or concerns about the form, reach out to the support team.
