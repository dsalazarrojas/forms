# Tutoring Session Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help tutors evaluate and document the progress and outcomes of a tutoring session. It helps gather information on objectives achieved, materials used, session goals, session notes, dates and times, and session duration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the objectives of the session and select the relevant objectives achieved.
2. List the materials used during the session.
3. Identify the session goals and select the relevant goals.
4. Record any notable session notes.
5. Enter the session date.
6. Enter the session time.
7. Specify the session duration.
8. Select the session outcomes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Objectives** (`Objectives`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the objectives achieved during the session by choosing from the provided options ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Materials Needed** (`Materials Needed`, `text`, required: false): List the materials used during the session.
* **Session Goals** (`Session Goals`, `select_one`, required: false): Identify the session goals by selecting the relevant option ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Session Notes** (`Session Notes`, `note`, required: false): Record any notable or important information about the session.
* **Session Date** (`Session Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the session.
* **Session Time** (`Session Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the start and end time of the session.
* **Session Duration** (`Session Duration`, `number`, required: false): Specify the duration of the session in hours.
* **Session Outcomes** (`Session Outcomes`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the session outcomes by choosing from the provided options ('Yes' or 'No').

## Tips
* Clearly define the session objectives and goals before completing the form.
* Be concise when recording session notes.
* Ensure the session date and time are accurate.
* Specify the session duration in hours.
* Review the session outcomes carefully to ensure they align with the objectives and goals.
