# e_commerce_store_checklist_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The e_commerce_store_checklist_form is used to configure specific store settings for online selling.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow the instructions below to fill out this form:

1. Review each field carefully and make sure you understand what it represents before filling it out.
2. Fill out the form according to your store setup needs, and make sure to check or uncheck the items that apply to your store.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any) before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Setup** (`store_setup`, `text`, optional): This field is used to describe your store setup in detail. You can enter any relevant information about your store, such as its name, description, and type.
* **Product Setup** (`product_setup`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to determine if you have set up products in your store. Check "Yes" if you have products, and "No" if you don't have any products yet.
* **Pricing** (`pricing`, `number`, optional): This field is used to specify your store's pricing strategy. Enter a numerical value to specify your pricing structure, e.g., 10%, 20%, etc.
* **Payments** (`payments`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to specify your store's payment method. Check "Yes" if you have set up payments, and "No" if you don't have payment methods yet.
* **Shipping** (`shipping`, `time`, optional): This field is used to configure shipping details for your store. Enter the time your store is open for shipping, e.g., 08:00-17:00.
* **Taxes** (`taxes`, `text`, optional): This field is used to specify your store's tax rate. Enter the tax rate as a textual description, e.g., "GST: 5%."
* **Returns** (`returns`, `email`, optional): This field is used to specify your store's return policy email address. Enter the email address to which return requests will be sent.
* **Contact Support** (`contact_support`, `text`, optional): This field is used to specify the contact information for store support. Enter any relevant contact information such as phone number, address, or email.
* **Customer Support** (`customer_support`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to determine if your store has customer support. Check "Yes" if you have customer support, and "No" if you don't have any support services yet.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form according to your store's needs and setup.
* Double-check each field to ensure you are filling it out correctly.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact your store's support team or administrator for help.
