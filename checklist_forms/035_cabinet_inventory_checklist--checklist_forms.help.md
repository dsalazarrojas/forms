# Cabinet Inventory Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cabinet Inventory Checklist is a form used to track and manage inventory details for cabinets and their contents. It's designed to ensure accurate and up-to-date records of assets and their conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and access to the relevant sections.
2. Fill out the required fields, including Cabinet ID or Number, Assigned Department, Last Full Audit Date, Auditor Name, Stock Level Status, Lock and Security Status, Shelf Integrity, and Final Sign-off Date.
3. If applicable, fill out the sections for tracking content inventory, physical asset condition, and any discrepancies or issues found during the audit.
4. Enter any additional information, such as items requiring immediate reorder and estimated total value of contents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### -- Inventory Control Details --
* **Cabinet ID or Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the cabinet.
* **Assigned Department**: Select the department to which the cabinet is assigned.
* **Last Full Audit Date**: Record the date of the last full audit or inspection.
* **Auditor Name**: Enter the name of the person performing the audit.
* **Stock Level Status**: Select the current stock level status of the cabinet (Fully Stocked, Adequate, Low - Reorder Needed, Critically Low).
* **Lock and Security Status**: Select the status of the cabinet's lock and security features (Secure and Functional, Broken, No Lock Present).
* **Shelf Integrity**: Select the condition of the shelf (Pass, Fail).
* **Internal Lighting Status**: Select the status of the cabinet's internal lighting (Functional, Needs Bulb, No Lighting).
* **Final Sign-off Date**: Record the date of the final sign-off or completion of the audit.

### -- Content Inventory --
* **Were any expired items identified**: Select 'Yes - Removed' if expired items were found and removed.
* **Are all items clearly labeled**: Select 'True' if all items are clearly labeled.
* **Are safety manuals or SDS sheets present**: Select 'True' if safety manuals or SDS sheets are present for hazardous materials.
* **Items Requiring Immediate Reorder**: Enter a list of items that require immediate reorder.
* **Describe any inventory discrepancies**: Enter any discrepancies found during the audit for comparison to the master list.
* **Estimated Total Value of Contents**: Enter the estimated total value of the contents, if known.

Note: The 'Are all items clearly labeled' field is to verify that all items are properly labeled for easy identification and compliance with regulations. The 'Are safety manuals or SDS sheets present' field is to ensure that safety materials are readily available for handling hazardous materials. 

Please fill in this form according to the guidelines provided, and don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need further clarification.
