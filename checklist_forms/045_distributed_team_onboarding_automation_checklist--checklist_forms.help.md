# Distributed Team Onboarding Automation Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to standardize the onboarding process for new team members, ensuring that all necessary information and resources are provided to perform tasks effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each section carefully and ensure you have the necessary information before filling out the form.
2.  Enter the hire date and time for the new team member.
3.  Provide access details such as company email, phone number, and chat channels.
4.  Select the onboarding details that apply to the new team member (e.g., team lead introduction, project overview, team members introduction).
5.  Select the setup details that apply to the new team member (e.g., company email, phone number, team lead, project manager).
6.  Enter project information such as project description, goals, and status.
7.  Select team information such as team lead, team members, and project manager.
8.  List first week tasks for the new team member.
9.  Mark the onboarding completed status.
10. Enter notes and comments for each section as needed.
