# Nighttime Medication Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the medication administration of patients at night. It helps healthcare professionals ensure that patients receive their medications as prescribed and are safe.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the medications taken at night from the options provided.
2. Choose if the correct dosages were administered.
3. Check if the medications were given at the correct timing.
4. Note any side effects experienced by the patient.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes about the medication administration.
6. Identify if a caregiver was assigned to assist with medication administration.
7. Enter the contact information of the assigned caregiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **evening medications** (`evening_medications`, select multiple, required): Select all the medications that the patient took at night. Choose from the options:
	+ 'Yes' for medications that were taken.
	+ 'No' for medications that were not taken.
* **dosages** (`dosages`, select one, required): Choose if the correct dosages were administered.
	+ 'Yes' for correct dosages.
	+ 'No' for incorrect dosages.
* **timing** (`timing`, select one, required): Check if the medications were given at the correct timing.
	+ 'Yes' for correct timing.
	+ 'No' for incorrect timing.
* **side effects** (`side_effects`, text, optional): Type any side effects experienced by the patient.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the medication administration.
* **assigned caregiver** (`assigned_caregiver`, select one, required): Identify if a caregiver was assigned to assist with medication administration.
	+ 'Yes' for assigned caregiver.
	+ 'No' for no assigned caregiver.
* **caregiver contact** (`caregiver_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the assigned caregiver.
