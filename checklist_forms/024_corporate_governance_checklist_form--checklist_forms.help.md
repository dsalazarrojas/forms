# Corporate Governance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a company's corporate governance and compliance practices. The information collected will help the company's stakeholders, such as investors, partners, or regulatory bodies, to understand the company's commitment to good governance and compliance practices.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant pages and sections that apply to your company.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review your answers carefully to ensure they accurately reflect your company's practices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your company.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of your company.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact phone number of your company.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of your company.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and title of the contact person.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the role of the contact person (e.g. CEO/President, CFO, COO).
* **Date Founded** (`date_founded`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the company was founded.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of employees in your company.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, required: false): Enter the industry your company operates in.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, required: false): Enter the URL of your company's website.
* **Company Facebook** (`company_facebook`, `text`, required: false): Enter the URL of your company's Facebook page.
* **Governance and Compliance Information** (`page_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant information about your company's governance and compliance practices.
* **Code of Conduct** (`code_of_conduct`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the boxes that apply to your company's Code of Conduct (e.g. True, False).
* **Whistleblower Policy** (`whistleblower_policy`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the boxes that apply to your company's Whistleblower Policy (e.g. True, False).
* **Data Privacy Policy** (`data_privacy_policy`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the boxes that apply to your company's Data Privacy Policy (e.g. True, False).
* **Governance and Compliance** (`page_3`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant information about your company's governance and compliance practices.
* **Risk Management** (`risk_management`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the boxes that apply to your company's Risk Management practices (e.g. Regular, Periodic, Event-Driven).
* **Audit Committee** (`audit_committee`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the boxes that apply to your company's Audit Committee (e.g. True, False).
* **Compliance Committee** (`compliance_committee`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the boxes that apply to your company's Compliance Committee (e.g. True, False).
* **Compliance Program** (`compliance_program`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the boxes that apply to your company's Compliance Program (e.g. True, False).
* **Ethics Committee** (`ethics_committee`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the boxes that apply to your company's Ethics Committee (e.g. True, False).
* **Compliance and Reporting** (`page_4`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant information about your company's compliance and reporting practices.
* **Whistleblower Procedure** (`whistleblower_procedure`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant information about your company's Whistleblower Procedure.
* **Reporting Procedure** (`reporting_procedure`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant information about your company's Reporting Procedure.
