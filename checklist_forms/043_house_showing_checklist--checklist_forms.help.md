# House Showing Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The House Showing Checklist is a form designed to help gather information about a property during a showing. This form collects details about the property's condition, features, and contact information of the person showing the property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the property details in the "property_details" field.
2. Enter the address of the property in the "address" field.
3. Provide the contact information in the "contact_info" field.
4. Describe the property's condition in the "property_condition" field.
5. List the amenities in the "amenities" field.
6. Describe the safety features in the "safety_features" field.
7. Describe the electrical features in the "electrical_features" field.
8. List the plumbing features in the "plumbing_features" field.
9. Describe the water quality in the "water_quality" field.
10. Indicate if there is parking available in the "parking" field.
11. Describe the neighborhood in the "neighborhood" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **property_details** (text): Describe the property's details such as its size, number of bedrooms, number of bathrooms, etc.
* **address** (text): Enter the property's address.
* **contact_info** (text): Enter the name, phone number, and email of the person showing the property.
* **property_condition** (text): Describe the property's condition, including any needed repairs or issues.
* **amenities** (text): List the amenities of the property such as pool, gym, etc.
* **safety_features** (text): Describe the property's safety features such as smoke detectors, carbon monoxide detectors, etc.
* **electrical_features** (text): Describe the property's electrical features such as number of outlets, circuit breakers, etc.
* **plumbing_features** (text): Describe the property's plumbing features such as water pressure, fixtures, etc.
* **water_quality** (text): Describe the quality of the water in the property.
* **parking** (text): Indicate if parking is available, and describe the type of parking (street, garage, etc.).
* **neighborhood** (text): Describe the neighborhood, including local amenities and attractions.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* Make sure to include any relevant information about the property's condition and features.
* Double-check the contact information for accuracy.
