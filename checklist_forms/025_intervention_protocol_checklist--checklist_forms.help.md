# Intervention Protocol Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a structured checklist for documenting the intervention process, ensuring that all critical information is collected and recorded in a clear and concise manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out the date and start and end times of the intervention.
2. Enter the team members involved in the intervention.
3. Fill out the personal details of the individuals affected by the intervention (First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, Location).
4. Provide any additional notes about the intervention.
5. Review and sign the form to confirm that it is complete and accurate.
