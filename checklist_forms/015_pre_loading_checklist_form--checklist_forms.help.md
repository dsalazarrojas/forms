# Pre Loading Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Loading Checklist Form is designed to help users collect and record information about a preloading checklist event. This form is a crucial tool for ensuring accuracy and thoroughness in preparing the checklist for a specific event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event name in the **Event Name** field.
2. Indicate the loading status in the **Loading Status** field.
3. Confirm whether all items have been loaded or not in the **Confirm All Loaded** field by selecting one of the provided options.
4. Optionally, enter the date of loading in the **Date Loaded** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event for which you are preparing the preloading checklist.
* **Loading Status**: Choose whether the loading is complete, pending, or another status that best describes the current loading state.
* **Confirm All Loaded**: Choose 'True' if all items have been loaded, 'False' if they have not, or 'Not Applicable' if the concept of loading status does not apply to this event. This field allows you to verify that all items have been loaded accurately.
* **Confirm All Loaded**: Select all loaded items to confirm their presence or absence in the event. This field is used to verify that all loaded items have been accounted for.
* **Date Loaded**: Optionally enter the date when the loading was completed. This can be a useful reference for future events or audits.

## Tips
* Ensure that you accurately report the loading status and confirmation of loaded items to guarantee the integrity of the preloading checklist.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If the event loading status changes, please update this form accordingly to reflect the new status.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact your supervisor or support team for assistance.
