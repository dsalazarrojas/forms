# Divorce Mediation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients regarding their divorce mediation process. The information collected will be used to guide the mediation sessions and help reach a fair and satisfactory resolution for all parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions accurately and thoroughly.
2. Fill out all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*) next to the field label.
3. For select multiple fields, choose all relevant options that apply to your situation.
4. Use the text fields to provide detailed information as requested.
5. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Client Information (\*)
Provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.

*   **Client Information**: This is the first page of the form, where you'll provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
