# Campaign Launch Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to assist campaign teams in creating and managing checklists for new campaigns. It helps teams track and verify the completion of key tasks, assignments, and deadlines, ensuring a smooth and successful campaign launch.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Campaign Name** and select the **Launch Date** for your campaign.
2. Add each task that needs to be completed before the campaign launch, along with the **Assigned To** team member and the **Due Date** (if applicable).
3. Choose the **Status** of each task (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed).
4. Identify any dependencies that must be met before completing the task.
5. Select the **Verification** team member who will verify the task's completion.
6. Add any relevant **Notes** about the task.
7. Evaluate the overall **Readiness For Launch** status by selecting Ready, Not Ready, or Partial.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Campaign Name**: Enter the name of the campaign being launched, e.g., "Summer Sale 2023". This field is required to track which campaign this checklist belongs to.
* **Launch Date**: Schedule the launch date for your campaign. This field is required and ensures that all tasks are due before the campaign launch.
* **Checklist Item**: List each task that needs to be completed before the campaign launch. For example, "Create ad copy" or "Set up landing page". This field is required and helps teams stay organized.
* **Assigned To**: Name the team member responsible for completing the task. This field is required and ensures that tasks are assigned correctly.
* **Due Date**: If applicable, set a date by which the task must be completed. This field is optional and allows teams to schedule their work efficiently.
* **Status**: Choose the status of each task (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed). This field is required and helps team members visualize the campaign's progress.
* **Dependencies**: If a task relies on another task or condition, list those dependencies here. This field is optional but helps teams understand how tasks are interrelated.
* **Verification**: Name the team member who will verify the task's completion. This field is optional but ensures that tasks are checked for quality and accuracy.
* **Notes**: Add any relevant remarks or comments about the task. This field is optional and allows team members to communicate about task complexities or issues.
* **Readiness For Launch**: Evaluate the overall readiness of the campaign by selecting Ready, Not Ready, or Partial. This field is required and ensures that the team is aligned on the campaign's launch status.
