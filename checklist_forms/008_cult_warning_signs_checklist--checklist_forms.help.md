# Cult Warning Signs Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help identify potential warning signs that may indicate cultural or societal issues. It is meant to provide a structured and systematic approach to recognizing and addressing these signs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Select all that apply for each question.
3. Fill in the text fields with brief descriptions of your findings.
4. Submit the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Warning Signs** (`warning_signs`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for listing specific warning signs that you have observed or have reason to suspect. Please provide a brief description of each sign you have noticed.
* **Concer Level** (`concern_levels`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This section helps you categorize the level of concern for each sign. Please select all that apply from the available levels (Level 1-5).
* **Follow-up Support** (`follow_up_support`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing any follow-up information or support you think would be helpful in addressing the warning signs you have identified. Please provide a brief description of the support needed.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and thoroughly, as your input will help in better understanding and addressing potential cultural or societal issues.
* If you are unsure about a particular sign or concern level, please use your best judgment and provide a brief explanation.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
