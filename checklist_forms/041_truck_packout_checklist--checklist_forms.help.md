# Truck Packout Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Truck Packout Checklist is a comprehensive form designed to assess the status and condition of a truck before it's packed out or shipped. This form ensures that all critical areas are checked, reducing the risk of damage or issues during transportation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and select the relevant status for each field.
2. For each field, choose the option that best describes the condition or state of the corresponding truck component.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields, as indicated by the "required" label in each field.
4. If a field is not applicable or unknown, select "N/A" as the status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Status (1)**: Select the current status of the vehicle, whether it's "Ready", "Not Ready", or "N/A".
* **Engine Type (2)**: Choose the type of engine used in the truck, such as "Petrol" or "Diesel".
* **Fuel Level (3)**: Enter the current fuel level in the truck's tank, as a numerical value.
* **Oil Level (4)**: Enter the current oil level in the truck's engine, as a numerical value.
* **Tires (5)**: Select the condition of the truck's tires, whether they're "Good", "Bad", or "N/A".
* **Tread Depth (6)**: Enter the current tread depth of the truck's tires, as a numerical value.
* **Tire Air Pressure (7)**: Enter the current air pressure in the truck's tires, as a numerical value.
* **Tire Type (8)**: Choose the type of tires used in the truck, such as "Steel" or "Alu".
* **Tire Size (9)**: Select the size of the truck's tires, such as "18" or "20".
* **Wheel Type (10)**: Choose the type of wheels used in the truck, such as "Aluminum" or "Steel".
* **Wheel Size (11)**: Select the size of the truck's wheels, such as "Standard" or "Heavy Duty".
* **Brake Wear (12)**: Enter the current brake wear condition, as a numerical value.
* **Suspension (13)**: Select the condition of the truck's suspension, whether it's "Good", "Bad", or "N/A".
* **Brake Temperature (14)**: Enter the current brake temperature, as a numerical value.
* **Transmission Type (15)**: Choose the type of transmission used in the truck, such as "Automatic" or "Manual".
* **Transmission Fluid Level (16)**: Enter the current transmission fluid level, as a numerical value.
* **Differential Type (17)**: Choose the type of differential used in the truck, such as "Open" or "Closed".
* **Differential Fluid Level (18)**: Enter the current differential fluid level, as a numerical value.
* **Coolant Level (19)**: Enter the current coolant level in the truck's engine, as a numerical value.
* **Engine Type (20)**: Choose the type of engine used in the truck, such as "Petrol" or "Diesel".
* **Engine Temperature (21)**: Enter the current engine temperature, as a numerical value.
* **Battery Level (22)**: Enter the current battery level, as a numerical value.
* **Battery Type (23)**: Choose the type of battery used in the truck, such as "12V" or "24V".
* **Battery Size (24)**: Select the size of the battery used in the truck, such as "Small" or "Large".
* **Battery Age (25)**: Enter the age of the battery, as a numerical value.

## Tips
