# Pre Funding Mortgage Audit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Funding Mortgage Audit Checklist is a form used to validate and verify information related to a mortgage loan before it is approved and funded. This form helps to identify potential issues or risks associated with the loan, ensuring that the underwriter or auditor can review and address these concerns before moving forward.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you have all necessary documents and data.
2. Answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
3. Select the correct options for each field based on the information you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Lender Verification (2)** (Required, select_one): Verify the lender and their contact information.
- **Appraisal Status (3)** (Required, select_one): Choose the status of the appraisal, whether it's approved, disapproved, or pending.
- **Credit Report (4)** (Required, select_multiple): Select the current status of the credit report, whether it's clean, requires review, or other options as needed.
- **Loan Amount (5)** (Required, number): Enter the total loan amount.
- **Interest Rate (6)** (Required, number): Enter the interest rate of the loan.
- **Mortgage Term (7)** (Required, time): Enter the length of the mortgage in years.
- **Loan Type (8)** (Required, select_multiple): Select the type of loan, whether it's fixed rate or adjustable.
- **Escrow Status (9)** (Required, select_one): Choose the status of the escrow, whether it's yes or no.
- **Property Type (10)** (Required, select_multiple): Select the type of property, whether single family, condo, or multi-unit.
- **Risk Status (11)** (Required, select_one): Assess the risk level of the loan, whether none, moderate, or high.
- **Comments (12)** (Optional, note): Add any additional comments or notes about the loan.
- **Risk Comments (13)** (Optional, note): Add any additional comments or notes about the risk status.
- **Review Status (14)** (Required, select_one): Choose the status of the review, whether completed or not started.
- **File Uploaded (15)** (Required, select_one): Verify if the file has been uploaded.
- **Review Date (16)** (Required, date): Enter the date of the review.
- **Reviewer Notes (17)** (Optional, note): Add any notes from the reviewer.
- **Risk Level (18)** (Required, select_one): Assess the risk level of the loan, whether low, moderate, or high.
- **Audit Notes (19)** (Optional, note): Add any additional notes or comments about the audit.
- **Pre Funding Date (20)** (Required, date): Enter the date of pre-funding.
- **Review Comments (21)** (Optional, note): Add any additional comments about the review process.
