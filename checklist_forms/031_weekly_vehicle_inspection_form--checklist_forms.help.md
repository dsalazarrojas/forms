# weekly_vehicle_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The weekly vehicle inspection form is a tool to help users record and track the status of their company vehicles on a weekly basis. This form is designed to gather information on the vehicle's condition, mileage, and other relevant details for maintenance and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vehicle's year of manufacture.
2. Enter the vehicle's make (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.).
3. Enter the vehicle's model (e.g., Corolla, F-150, etc.).
4. Enter the vehicle's license plate number.
5. Select the current condition of the vehicle.
6. Enter the location of the vehicle inspection.
7. Enter the date of the inspection.
8. Enter any comments regarding the vehicle's condition.
9. Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
10. Enter the fuel level (e.g., full, half full, empty, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required: false): Enter the year the vehicle was manufactured.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required: false): Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.).
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required: false): Enter the model of the vehicle (e.g., Corolla, F-150, etc.).
* **License Plate** (`license_plate`, text, required: false): Enter the license plate number of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, number, required: false): Select the current condition of the vehicle using the available numeric options (e.g., 1 = Excellent, 2 = Fair, 3 = Poor).
* **Inspection Location** (`inspection_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the vehicle inspection.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Vehicle Condition Comments** (`vehicle_condition_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments regarding the vehicle's condition.
* **Vehicle Mileage** (`vehicle_mileage`, number, required: false): Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
* **Fuel Level** (`fuel_level`, number, required: false): Enter the current fuel level of the vehicle (e.g., full, half full, empty, etc.).
