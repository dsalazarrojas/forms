# Food Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Safety Checklist is a document used to track and record food safety information in a kitchen, food service, or retail environment. This form helps ensure that food safety guidelines are followed and that any incidents or concerns are documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand the questions and fields provided.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately to the best of your knowledge.
3. Fill in all required fields with the correct information.
4. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank, but ensure that you understand the implication of leaving it blank.
5. Review and edit your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Food Safety** (Page 1): This field is a text area where you can provide a brief description of the food safety concern or incident.
* **Food Safety 2** (Page 2): Another text field for providing more information about food safety.
* **Food Safety 3** (Page 3): This is a select one field where you choose one of the available options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Food Safety 4** (Page 4): A text field for recording information.
* **Food Safety 5** (Page 5): Another text field for recording information.
* **Food Safety 6** (Page 6): A text field where you can provide more details.
* **Food Safety 7** (Page 7): A select multiple field where you choose two or more options (Option 4, Option 5, Option 6).
* **Food Safety 8** (Page 8): A text field for recording more information.
* **Food Safety 9** (Page 9): A text field for further recording.
* **Food Safety 10** (Page 10): Another text field for recording more information.
* **Food Safety 11** (Page 11): A text field for recording more details.
* **Food Safety 12** (Page 12): A date field for recording a specific date.
* **Food Safety 13** (Page 13): A time field for recording a specific time.
* **Food Safety 14** (Page 14): A number field for recording a numerical value.
* **Food Safety 15** (Page 15): A select one field where you choose one of the available options (Option 7, Option 8, Option 9, Option 10).
* **Food Safety 16** (Page 16): A number field for recording a numerical value.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* Review and edit your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
