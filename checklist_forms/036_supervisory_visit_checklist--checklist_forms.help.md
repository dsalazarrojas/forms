# Supervisory Visit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supervisory Visit Checklist is used to document supervisory visits to sites, ensuring accurate and thorough documentation of key information about the visit, the site, and next follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the date of the site visit in the "Site Visit Date" field. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY, where MM is the month, DD is the day, and YYYY is the year.
2. In the "Site Visit Time" field, select the time of the visit.
3. Enter the name of the site in the "Site Name" field.
4. Provide the supervisor's name in the "Supervisor Name" field.
5. Enter the supervisor's phone number in the "Supervisor Phone" field.
6. Enter the supervisor's email address in the "Supervisor Email" field. Please ensure that this is a valid email address.
7. In the "Observations" field, please describe any observations or concerns noted during the site visit. This is an opportunity to capture any notable events or issues that arose during the visit.
8. In the "Site Visit Notes" field, record any additional notes about the site visit.
9. If necessary, enter the next follow-up action in the "Next Follow Up Action" field.
10. If applicable, select the next follow-up date in the "Next Follow Up Date" field.
11. Enter the site's location in the "Site Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Visit Date** (`site_visit_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the site visit, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Site Visit Time** (`site_visit_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time of the visit.
* **Site Name** (`site_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the site.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required/optional): Provide the supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the supervisor's email address. Please ensure that this is a valid email address.
* **Observations** (`observations`, text, required/optional): Describe any observations or concerns noted during the site visit.
* **Site Visit Notes** (`site_visit_notes`, text, required/optional): Record any additional notes about the site visit.
* **Next Follow Up Action** (`next_follow_up_action`, text, required/optional): If necessary, enter the next follow-up action.
* **Next Follow Up Date** (`next_follow_up_date`, date, required/optional): If applicable, select the next follow-up date.
* **Site Location** (`site_visit_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the site's location.
