# Summer Camp Admission Checklist Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is designed to gather essential details about a camper's admission to summer camp. It helps the summer camp administration office to quickly verify and process camper registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Checklist**: Fill out the general checklist with any necessary information about the camper's admission.
2. **Camp Name**: Enter the name of the summer camp the camper is registering for.
3. **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the camp.
4. **End Date**: Enter the end date of the camp.
5. **Camp Hours**: Enter the number of hours the camper will be at the camp.
6. **Camper Hours**: Enter the number of hours the camper will spend at the camp.
7. **Contact Information**:
	* **Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of the contact person.
	* **Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of the contact person.
	* **Contact E-mail**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
	* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
8. **Emergency Contact Information**:
	* **Emergency Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of the emergency contact person.
	* **Emergency Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of the emergency contact person.
	* **Relationship**: Describe the relationship between the camper and the emergency contact person.
9. **Medical Information**:
	* **Medical Conditions**: Enter any relevant medical conditions the camper has.
	* **Allergies**: List any allergies or medical conditions that require special attention.
	* **Medical Notes**: Enter any additional medical information.
10. **Payment and Registration**:
	* **Payment Method**: Select whether the payment is made in person or online.
	* **Payment Status**: Select the payment status (e.g., Paid or Pending).
11. **Camper Details**:
	* **Age**: Enter the age of the camper.
	* **Gender**: Select the gender of the camper.
	* **Diet**: Select the camper's diet type (e.g., Vegetarian or Non-Vegetarian).
	* **Medication**: Select whether the camper takes medication.
12. **Additional Notes**:
	* **Registration Date**: Enter the date of registration.
	* **Camp Address**: Enter the address of the summer camp.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Checklist**: A general checklist of camper information.
* **Camp Name**: The name of the summer camp the camper is registering for.

* **End Date**: The date when the camp ends.

* **Contact E-mail**: The email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the contact person.
* **Emergency Contact First Name**: The first name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name**: The last name of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship**: The relationship between the camper and the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions**: Any relevant medical conditions the camper has.
* **Allergies**: A list of any allergies or medical conditions that require special attention.
* **Medical Notes**: Additional medical information.
* **Payment Method**: The method of payment for the camper (e.g., in-person or online).

## Tips
* Double-check the camper's information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.
* Review the camper's medical conditions and allergies carefully to ensure their safety and well-being.
* Confirm the payment method and status to ensure smooth payment processing.
* Update the camper's information as necessary throughout the summer camp session.
