# residential_moving_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their residential move, including move date, type, and details about the move.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of move (Local or Long-Distance) to indicate the distance of the move.
2. Enter the move date if you know it.
3. Provide details about the move location (e.g., city, state, or region).
4. List the items you are moving (e.g., furniture, electronics, or pets).
5. Select the items you are moving (e.g., sofa, bed, table).
6. Indicate if you will be moving storage items (e.g., boxes, furniture).
7. List the electronics you are moving (e.g., TV, computer, phone).
8. Indicate if you will be moving a vehicle.
9. Indicate if you will be moving pets.
10. Add any additional move-related notes.
11. Enter your contact name.
12. Enter your contact email.
13. Enter your contact phone number.
14. If you have a secondary move date, enter it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Move Date**: Enter the date of the move if you know it.
* **Move Type**: Select the type of move (Local or Long-Distance) to indicate the distance of the move.
* **Move Location**: Enter the details about the move location (e.g., city, state, or region).
* **Moving Items**: List the items you are moving (e.g., furniture, electronics, or pets).
* **Move Furniture**: Select the items you are moving (e.g., sofa, bed, table).
* **Move Storage**: Indicate if you will be moving storage items (e.g., boxes, furniture).
* **Move Electronic**: List the electronics you are moving (e.g., TV, computer, phone).
* **Move Vehicle**: Indicate if you will be moving a vehicle.
* **Move Pets**: Indicate if you will be moving pets.
* **Move Notes**: Add any additional move-related notes.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your contact name.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Move Date 2**: Enter any secondary move date if applicable.
