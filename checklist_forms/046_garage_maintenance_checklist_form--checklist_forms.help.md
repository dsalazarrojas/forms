# Garage Maintenance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Garage Maintenance Checklist Form is a tool used to record and manage maintenance activities for vehicles. It allows users to select the type of vehicle, maintenance type, and schedule maintenance tasks such as oil changes, tire rotations, and fluid checks. The form also allows users to record contact information and assign tasks to specific contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of vehicle from the "Vehicle Type" field.
2. Enter the vehicle's make in the "Vehicle Make" field.
3. Enter the vehicle's model in the "Vehicle Model" field.
4. Select the maintenance type (e.g., oil change, tire rotation, etc.) in the "Maintenance Type" field.
5. Select the frequency of maintenance tasks (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.) in the "Maintenance Frequency" field.
6. Record the next maintenance date in the "Next Maintenance Date" field.
7. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter the contact's email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter the contact's phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. Enter the name of the assigned contact in the "Assigned Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one): Select the type of vehicle, e.g., car, truck, or trailer.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text): Enter the vehicle's make, e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text): Enter the vehicle's model, e.g., Corolla, F-150, etc.
* **Maintenance Type** (`maintenance_type`, select_one): Select the type of maintenance task, e.g., oil change, tire rotation, etc.
* **Maintenance Due Date** (`maintenance_due_date`, date): Enter the next maintenance date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency`, select_one): Select the frequency of maintenance tasks, e.g., daily, weekly, etc.
* **Next Maintenance Date** (`next_maintenance_date`, date): Enter the next maintenance date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Add any additional notes or comments about the maintenance task.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter the contact's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter the contact's phone number.
* **Assigned Contact** (`assigned_contact`, text): Enter the name of the assigned contact.

Tips:
* Please ensure that the vehicle type is selected correctly to ensure accurate maintenance scheduling.
* Make sure to enter the maintenance due date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* If a contact is assigned to perform the maintenance task, make sure to enter their contact information accurately.
