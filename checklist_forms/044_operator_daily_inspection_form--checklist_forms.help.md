# Operator Daily Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Operator Daily Inspection Form is designed to collect information about a vehicle's condition and status. It helps operators or mechanics perform a daily inspection, documenting key details about the vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant vehicle details, such as make, model, year, and color.
2. Enter the vehicle's inspection date and time.
3. Assess the vehicle's condition using the "Vehicle Condition" field (Good or Bad).
4. Provide any additional comments or observations in the "Inspection Report" field.
5. Enter the assigned operator's email address.
6. Select the vehicle's status (Operational or Non-Operational).
7. Enter any vehicle-specific comments or defects.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vehicle Details**: Enter the vehicle's make (e.g., Toyota, Ford).
- **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the inspection in the format `HH:MM`.
- **Vehicle Condition**: Select "Good" if the vehicle is in good condition, or "Bad" if it is not.
- **Inspection Report**: Provide any comments or observations about the vehicle's condition.
- **Assigned Operator**: Enter the email address of the operator performing the inspection.
- **Vehicle Status**: Select "Operational" if the vehicle is operational, or "Non-Operational" if it is not.
- **Vehicle Status History**: Provide any relevant history about the vehicle's status changes.
- **Vehicle Defect**: Enter any defects or issues found during the inspection.
- **Vehicle Remaks**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the vehicle.
- **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford).
- **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle.
- **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
- **Vehicle Color**: Select the vehicle's color (e.g., Red, Blue).
- **Vehicle Engine**: Enter the vehicle's engine type or specifications.
- **Vehicle Transmission**: Enter the vehicle's transmission type or specifications.
- **Operator Signature**: Enter your name and signature as the operator performing the inspection.
- **Vehicle Serial Number**: Enter the vehicle's serial number.
- **Vehicle License Plate**: Enter the vehicle's license plate number.
- **Vehicle VIN**: Enter the vehicle's Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
- **Vehicle Odometer**: Enter the vehicle's odometer reading.
- **Vehicle Registration**: Enter any relevant registration information.
- **Vehicle Type**: Select the vehicle type (e.g., Car, Truck).
- **Vehicle Defect**: Enter any defects or issues found during the inspection.
- **Vehicle Remaks**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the vehicle.
