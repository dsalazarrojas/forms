# policy_rollout_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The policy rollout checklist is a form used to track the implementation and status of policies within an organization. It helps policy owners and administrators to ensure that policies are properly communicated, understood, and implemented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form, which corresponds to a specific policy or section of policies.
2. For each field, provide the requested information or response as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### policy_rollout_checklist_1
* **policy_rollout_checklist_1** (`number`, optional): This field is intended to track the number of people who have been informed about the policy. Enter the number of individuals who have received information about the policy.

### policy_rollout_checklist_2
* **policy_rollout_checklist_2** (`text`, optional): Enter a brief description or note about the policy or the information provided to stakeholders.

### policy_rollout_checklist_3
* **policy_rollout_checklist_3** (`select_one`, optional): This field allows you to select whether the policy has been communicated to the relevant parties (`Yes` or `No`).

### policy_rollout_checklist_4
* **policy_rollout_checklist_4** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable options from the list, indicating which groups or teams have received the policy communication (`Yes` or `No`).

### policy_rollout_checklist_5
* **policy_rollout_checklist_5** (`date`, optional): This field is intended to track the date when the policy was communicated to stakeholders. Enter the date in a standard date format (yyyy-mm-dd).

### policy_rollout_checklist_6
* **policy_rollout_checklist_6** (`time`, optional): This field is intended to track the time when the policy was communicated to stakeholders. Enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).

### policy_rollout_checklist_7
* **policy_rollout_checklist_7** (`note`, optional): This field is for providing any additional notes or comments about the policy implementation and communication.

### policy_rollout_checklist_8
* **policy_rollout_checklist_8** (`email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person or group that received the policy communication.

### policy_rollout_checklist_9
* **policy_rollout_checklist_9** (`text`, optional): Enter any relevant comments or notes about the policy.

### policy_rollout_checklist_10
* **policy_rollout_checklist_10** (`text`, optional): Enter any final comments or notes about the policy implementation and communication.
