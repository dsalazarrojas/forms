# Special Education Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Education Checklist Form is a tool used to gather information about students who require special education support. It helps educators identify student needs, track progress, and document services provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the student's name and age, and select their parent/guardian relationship.
2. Enter the student's birth date and age.
3. Identify any disabilities the student may have.
4. List any services the student requires.
5. Select any accommodations the student needs.
6. List any services already provided to the student.
7. Enter the date of the last evaluation and the time of the last evaluation.
8. Indicate the evaluation status.
9. Identify the special education teacher(s) involved.
10. Record any parent concerns.
11. Add any comments from teachers.
12. Add any comments from the student.
13. Record any other comments.
14. Enter the follow-up date.
15. Enter the next evaluation date.
16. Enter the last evaluation date.
17. Enter the student's ID number.
18. List any additional ID numbers.
19. Enter the school name.
20. Enter the district name.
21. Enter the school address.
22. Enter your signature as the teacher.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Parent/Guilty Party** (`parent_guilty`, select_one, optional): Select the relationship of the parent/guardian (Parent, Guardian, or Other).
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, number, optional): Enter the student's age.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter the student's birth date.
* **Disability** (`disability`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any disabilities the student may have (Physical, Emotional/Behavioral, Speech/Communication, or Other).
* **Services Needed** (`services_needed`, select_multiple, optional): Select any services the student requires (Speech Therapy, Occupational Therapy, Physical Therapy, or Other).
* **Accommodations** (`accommodations`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any accommodations the student needs (Modified Curriculum, Extended Time, Assistive Technology, or Other).
* **Services Provided** (`services_provided`, select_multiple, optional): List any services already provided to the student (Speech Therapy, Occupational Therapy, Physical Therapy, or Other).
* **Date of Last Evaluation** (`date_of_last_evaluation`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last evaluation.
* **Time of Last Evaluation** (`time_of_last_evaluation`, time, optional): Enter the time of the last evaluation.
* **Evaluation Status** (`evaluation_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate the evaluation status (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
* **Special Education Teacher** (`special_education_teacher`, select_multiple, optional): Identify the special education teacher(s) involved (Teacher 1, Teacher 2, or Other).
* **Parent Concerns** (`parent_concerns`, text, optional): Record any parent concerns.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments from the teacher.
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments from the student.
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, text, optional): Record any other comments.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, date, optional): Enter the follow-up date.
* **Next Evaluation Date** (`next_evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the next evaluation date.
* **Last Evaluation Date** (`last_evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the last evaluation date.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, number, optional): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Student ID Number 2** (`student_id_number_2`, select_multiple, optional): List any additional ID numbers.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the school name.
* **District Name** (`district_name`, text, optional): Enter the district name.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter the school address.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, text, optional): Enter your signature as the teacher.

## Tips
When completing this form, please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly. This form is intended to be a thorough and accurate assessment of the student's special education needs and services.
