# Equipment Functional Test Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to perform a functional test of the equipment to ensure it's operating correctly and meeting the required standards for operation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the equipment's history, including maintenance records, warranty, and previous test results.
2. Answer each question carefully, taking into account the specific options provided.
3. Complete the form in its entirety, paying attention to the required fields.
4. If any issues or defects are identified, document them clearly and concisely.
5. Submit the completed form for review and evaluation.
