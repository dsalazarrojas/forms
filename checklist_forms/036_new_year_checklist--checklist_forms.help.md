# new_year_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_year_checklist form is designed to gather user feedback on their goals and aspirations for the upcoming year. It is intended for internal use within the organization to identify trends and patterns in the user's goals and aspirations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are filling out only the fields that apply to you.
2. Select the goals that you have for the upcoming year from each of the listed categories.
3. Fill out only the fields that you have actual goals in the corresponding area.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **New Year Check List** (<type>: select_multiple)
This field is a select multiple field that allows you to choose the goals that you have for the new year. The options for this field are "Yes" and "No". This field is required for internal use, but it's not a specific field and it seems that it is intended to confirm that you have goals in other fields that follow.

### 2. **Personal Goals** (<type>: select_one)
In this field, select "Yes" if you have personal goals for the upcoming year. Personal goals might include things like personal growth, self-improvement, or resolving personal issues.

### 3. **Career Goals** (<type>: select_one)
Select "Yes" if you have career goals for the upcoming year. Career goals might include promotions, new job opportunities, or advancing in your field.

### 4. **Financial Goals** (<type>: select_one)
If you have financial goals for the upcoming year, select "Yes" in this field. Financial goals might include paying off debt, saving for a down payment, or increasing your income.

### 5. **Health and Fitness** (<type>: select_one)
Select "Yes" if you have health and fitness goals for the upcoming year. Health and fitness goals might include exercising more, eating a healthier diet, or reducing stress.

### 6. **Relationships** (<type>: select_multiple)
In this field, select "Yes" for the relationships you have goals for the upcoming year. Selecting "Yes" here might mean you have goals related to romantic relationships, friendships, or family.

### 7. **Lifestyle Goals** (<type>: select_multiple)
Select "Yes" for the lifestyle goals you have for the upcoming year. Lifestyle goals might include travel, hobbies, or personal interests.

### 8. **Professional Goals** (<type>: select_multiple)
Select "Yes" if you have professional goals for the upcoming year. Professional goals might include career advancement, industry recognition, or learning a new skill.

### 9. **Travel Goals** (<type>: select_multiple)
Select "Yes" if you have travel goals for the upcoming year. Travel goals might include visiting a specific destination, taking a certain number of trips, or exploring new cultures.

### 10. **Miscellaneous** (<type>: select_multiple)
In this field, select "Yes" for any other goals you have for the upcoming year that don't fit into the other categories. This might include goals related to education, personal growth, or creative pursuits.
