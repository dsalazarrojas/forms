# IT Certification Exam Study Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a comprehensive study plan for an IT certification exam. It collects information about your preparation, review materials, time management, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the "Topics to Review" options that apply to you.
2. Enter your "Instructor Name" and ensure it is accurate.
3. Choose a "Submission Date" for your exam submission.
4. Enter the other fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Certification Exam Prep** (`certification_exam_prep`, text, optional)
This field is used to note any additional comments or notes about your certification exam preparation.

### 2. **Topics to Review** (`topics_to_review`, select_multiple, required)
Select all the topics you need to review for the exam. Choose from the available options.

### 3. **Review Materials** (`review_materials`, text, optional)
Enter any relevant information about the review materials you have or plan to use for the exam.

### 4. **Time Management** (`time_management`, select_one, optional)
Indicate how you manage your study time. Choose from the available options.

### 5. **Exam Questions** (`exam_questions`, text, optional)
Enter any questions or doubts you have about the exam.

### 6. **Study Schedule** (`study_schedule`, date, optional)
Select a date for your study schedule.

### 7. **Review Method** (`review_method`, text, optional)
Enter any details about your review method or approach.

### 8. **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of your instructor or contact person for the exam.

### 9. **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required)
Select the date for your exam submission.

Note: The form's required fields are marked as required. Ensure you complete those fields accurately to avoid any issues.
