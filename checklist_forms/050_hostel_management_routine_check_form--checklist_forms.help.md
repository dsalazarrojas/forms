Hostel Management Routine Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to check and report on the hostel's daily routine activities, including cleaning, maintenance, and shopping routines. It is a vital tool to ensure that the hostel is running smoothly and safely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Hostel Management Routine Check Form" link to access the form.
2.  Select the corresponding answers for each page, indicating whether each routine activity is being performed or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Cleaning Routines
* **Cleaning Routines** (`Cleaning Routines`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field requires you to select all the cleaning routines that are being performed daily. You can choose from the options 'Yes' or 'No'.
### Maintenance Routines
* **Maintenance Routines** (`Maintenance Routines`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field requires you to select all the maintenance routines that are being performed. You can choose from the options 'Yes' or 'No'.
### Shopping Routines
* **Shopping Routines** (`Shopping Routines`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field requires you to select all the shopping routines that are being performed. You can choose from the options 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer honestly and accurately to the best of your knowledge.
* If you are unsure about any activity, select 'No' to indicate that it is not being performed.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please report them to the hostel management team.
* This form should be completed daily to ensure continuous monitoring and evaluation of hostel activities.
