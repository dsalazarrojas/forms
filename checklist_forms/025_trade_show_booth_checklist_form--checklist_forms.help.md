## trade_show_booth_checklist_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The trade show booth checklist form is a tool to ensure that you have considered all the essential elements for a successful trade show booth. This form helps you to organize and track your preparations, making sure you don't forget anything important.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the "Booth Layout" page.
2. On this page, you can enter any additional information that is relevant to your booth layout.
3. Click "Next" to move on to the next page.
4. Select the "Booth Staffing" page.
5. Check all the items that apply to your staffing plan:
	* Staffing Plan
	* Staff Training
	* Uniforms
6. Click "Next" to move on to the next page.
7. On the "Equipment" page, select all the items that you will have at your booth:
	* LED Sign
	* Tabletop
	* Shelving Unit
	* Chair
	* Tablecloth
	* Display Stand
	* Whiteboard
	* Power Strip
8. Click "Next" to move on to the next page.
9. On the "Marketing Materials" page, select all the materials that you will use to promote your product:
	* Brochures
	* Posters
	* Flyers
	* Business Cards
10. Click "Next" to move on to the next page.
11. On the "Demo Materials" page, select all the materials that you will have for demonstrations:
	* Product Samples
	* Product Literature
	* Marketing Sheets
12. Click "Next" to move on to the next page.
13. On the "Booth Decor" page, select the decoration that fits your booth:
	* Tablecloth
	* Floor Covering

	* Decorative Wall
14. Click "Next" to move on to the next page.
15. On the "Networking" page, select all the items that you will use for networking:
	* Business Cards

16. Click "Next" to move on to the next page.
17. On the "Safety" page, enter any additional safety information:
18. Click "Next" to move on to the next page.

	* Power Strips

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booth Layout** (text): Enter any additional information about your booth layout.
* **Booth Staffing** (select multiple): Check the items that apply to your staffing plan:
	+ Staffing Plan: Plan and schedule your staff's duties and shifts.
	+ Staff Training: Provide training to your staff on the product and company.
	+ Uniforms: Decide if you will provide uniforms for your staff.
* **Equipment** (select one): Select the equipment that you will have at your booth:
	+ LED Sign: An electronic display that shows your company's logo or product information.
	+ Tabletop: A table or counter where you can display your product.
	+ Shelving Unit: A unit that you can use to display your product.
	+ Chair: A chair for your staff to sit on.
	+ Tablecloth: A cloth that you can place on your table.
	+ Display Stand: A stand to display your product.
	+ Whiteboard: A whiteboard for notes and drawings.
	+ Power Strip: A strip that supplies power to your equipment.
* **Marketing Materials** (select multiple): Select the materials that you will use to promote your product:
	+ Brochures: A leaflet that describes your product.
	+ Posters: A large poster that showcases your product.
	+ Flyers: A single sheet of paper that promotes your product.
	+ Business Cards: Cards with your contact information.
* **Demo Materials** (select multiple): Select the materials that you will have for demonstrations:
	+ Product Samples: Physical examples of your product.
	+ Product Literature: Information about your product.
	+ Marketing Sheets: Sheets with marketing information about your product.
* **Booth Decor** (select one): Select the decoration that fits your booth:
	+ Tablecloth: A cloth that you can place on your table.
	+ Floor Covering: A material that covers the floor.
	+ Planters: Plants that can be placed in your booth.
	+ Decorative Wall: A wall decoration that can be seen in your booth.
* **Networking** (select multiple): Select the items that you will use for networking:
	+ Business Cards: Cards with your contact information.
	+ Phone List: A list of phone numbers.
	+ Email List: A list of email addresses.
* **Safety** (text): Enter any additional safety information.
* **Electrical** (select one): Select the electrical equipment that you will use:
	+ Power Strips: A strip that supplies power to your equipment.
	+ Extension Cord: A cord that extends the power from a power strip.
* **Utilities** (select multiple): Select the utilities that you will need:
	+ Water: Water for your booth.
	+ Electricity: Electricity for your equipment.
	+ Internet: Internet connection for your booth.
