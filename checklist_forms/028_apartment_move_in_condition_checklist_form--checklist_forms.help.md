# Apartment Move In Condition Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Apartment Move In Condition Checklist Form is a tool used by landlords or property managers to assess the condition of an apartment when a tenant moves in. It helps to establish a baseline for the apartment's condition and serves as a reference for future maintenance and repairs.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form carefully and understand each section and its purpose.
2.  Answer the questions honestly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
3.  Make sure to complete all required fields.
4.  Sign and date the form to confirm your acknowledgment of the condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Move-in date**: The date you're moving into the apartment.
* **Tenant name**: Your name as the tenant.
* **Property address**: The address of the apartment, including the building number and any additional identifying information.
* **Unit or apartment number**: The specific number or identifier for your apartment.
* **Landlord or property manager name**: The name of the landlord or property manager responsible for the apartment.
*   **Living room overall condition**: Assess the overall condition of the living room. Choose from the options:
	+ Clean and good condition
	+ Good with minor marks
	+ Fair - some damage or cleanliness issues
	+ Poor - significant issues
*   **Living room details or issues**: If there are any specific issues or problems with the living room, describe them here.
*   **Kitchen overall condition**: Assess the overall condition of the kitchen. Choose from the options:
	+ Clean and good condition
	+ Good with minor marks
	+ Fair - some damage or cleanliness issues
	+ Poor - significant issues
*   **Kitchen appliances condition**: Describe the condition of the kitchen appliances.
*   **Bedrooms overall condition**: Assess the overall condition of the bedrooms. Choose from the options:
	+ Clean and good condition
	+ Good with minor marks
	+ Fair - some damage or cleanliness issues
	+ Poor - significant issues
*   **Bathrooms overall condition**: Assess the overall condition of the bathrooms. Choose from the options:
	+ Clean and good condition
	+ Good with minor marks
	+ Fair - some damage or cleanliness issues
	+ Poor - significant issues
*   **Flooring condition**: Assess the condition of the flooring. Choose from the options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
*   **Flooring issues if any**: If there are any issues with the flooring, describe them here.
*   **Walls and paint condition**: Assess the condition of the walls and paint. Choose from the options:
	+ Excellent - freshly painted
	+ Good
	+ Fair - some marks or damage
	+ Poor - needs repainting
*   **Doors and windows condition**: Assess the condition of the doors and windows. Choose from the options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair - minor damage
	+ Poor - significant damage
*   **Hardware and fixtures condition**: Assess the condition of the hardware and fixtures. Choose from the options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
*   **Plumbing condition**: Assess the condition of the plumbing. Choose from the options:
	+ All working properly
	+ Mostly working
	+ Some issues noted
	+ Multiple problems
*   **Plumbing issues if any**: If there are any issues with the plumbing, describe them here.
*   **Electrical condition**: Assess the condition of the electrical system. Choose from the options:
	+ All working properly
	+ Mostly working
	+ Some issues noted
	+ Multiple problems
*   **Electrical issues if any**: If there are any issues with the electrical system, describe them here.
*   **HVAC condition**: Assess the condition of the heating and cooling system. Choose from the options:
	+ Working well
	+ Mostly functional
	+ Some issues
	+ Not functioning
*   **HVAC issues if any**: If there are any issues with the heating and cooling system, describe them here.
*   **Appliances condition**: If there are any appliances included in the apartment, describe their condition.
*   **Overall cleanliness**: Assess the overall cleanliness of the apartment. Choose from the options:
	+ Excellent - spotless
	+ Good - minor cleaning
	+ Fair - moderate cleaning needed
	+ Poor - extensive cleaning needed
*   **Pest evidence**: If there's any evidence of pests or infestations, describe them here.
*   **Unusual or unpleasant odors**: If there are any unusual or unpleasant odors in the apartment, describe them here.
*   **Additional issues or damage noted**: If there are any other issues or damage not listed, describe them here.
*   **Move-in photos taken**: Confirm if photos were taken of the apartment at move-in.
*   **Tenant acknowledges condition as stated**: Confirm that you've acknowledged the condition as stated.
*   **Landlord/property manager signature date**: Sign and date the form to confirm the condition and acknowledge your understanding of it.
*   **Tenant signature date**: Sign and date the form to confirm your acknowledgment of the condition.
