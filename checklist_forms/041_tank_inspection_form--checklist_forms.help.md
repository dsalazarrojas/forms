# Tank Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tank Inspection Form is designed to collect comprehensive data for tank inspections, ensuring accurate and efficient record-keeping. This form is essential for industries and organizations that handle large-scale tanks, such as chemical or fuel storage facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the tank (Tanks, Chemicals, or Fuel) that the inspection is for.
2. Provide a brief description of the inspection, if necessary.
3. Enter the tank number for reference.
4. Enter your name and title as the inspector.
5. Choose the date and time of the inspection.
6. Specify the location of the tank inspection.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`Tanks`, `Chemicals`, `Fuel`): Select the type of tank being inspected.
* **Description**: A brief description of the inspection, if needed.
* **Tank Number**: The unique identification number for the tank.
* **Inspector**: Your name and title as the person performing the inspection.
* **Date**: The date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time**: The time of the inspection in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Location**: The location where the tank is situated.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or notes related to the inspection.

## Tips
* Fill in the form accurately and thoroughly for each tank inspection.
* Use this form for each unique inspection event.
* Keep a record of all tank inspections for easy reference and future reference.
* Ensure the form is completed by the authorized person performing the inspection.
