# Fee Payment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fee Payment Checklist form is used to track and document payments made to an individual or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Methods** (Select Multiple)
	+ Select one or more of the following: Cash, Check, Card, Bank transfer
* **Payment Dates** (Date)
	+ Enter the date of the payment
* **Fee Amounts** (Number)
	+ Enter the amount paid
* **Payment Notes** (Note)
	+ Enter any additional notes about the payment method
* **Paid By** (Select Multiple)
	+ Select one or more of the following: School, Club, Business
* **Paid Dates** (Date)
	+ Enter the date of the second payment
* **Payment Status** (Select One)
	+ Select the status of the payment: Paid, Unpaid, Partially paid
* **Payment Method Notes** (Note)
	+ Enter any additional notes about the payment status
* **Payment Method** (Select One)
	+ Select the payment method for the second payment
* **Amount** (Number)
	+ Enter the amount of the second payment
* **Status** (Select One)
	+ Select the status of the second payment: Paid, Unpaid, Partially paid
* **Fee** (Number)
	+ Enter the fee amount
* **Amount** (Number)
	+ Enter the amount of the second payment
* **Status** (Select One)
	+ Select the status of the second payment: Paid, Unpaid, Partially paid
* **Method2** (Select One)
	+ Select the payment method for the second payment
* **Amount2** (Number)
	+ Enter the amount of the second payment
* **Status2** (Select One)
	+ Select the status of the second payment: Paid, Unpaid, Partially paid
* **Fee2** (Number)
	+ Enter the fee amount
* **Payment Date** (Date)
	+ Enter the date of the payment
* **Fee Date** (Date)
	+ Enter the date of the fee payment
* **Amount** (Number)
	+ Enter the fee amount
* **Status** (Select One)
	+ Select the status of the second payment: Paid, Unpaid, Partially paid

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct payment method and status for each payment.
* Enter accurate dates and amounts for each payment.
* Use the notes fields to provide additional context or notes about each payment.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.
