# language_school_admission_checklist_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Language School Admission Checklist Form Template.

## How To Complete This Form
To start, please review the form instructions and ensure you've answered all the required fields. This template contains seven sections:

- Application Information
- Contact Information
- Language Proficiency
- Education Background
- Employment Status
- Language Study Plan
- Additional Comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Information (Page 1)** (`application_information`, `text`, required: false): This section is where you provide a brief overview of your application. Please provide a concise statement about your motivation for applying to the language school.
* **Contact Information (Page 1)** (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false): This section is where you provide your contact details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Language Proficiency (Page 1)** (`language_proficiency`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your language proficiency level: English, French, Spanish, German, Chinese, or Other.
* **Education Background (Page 1)** (`education_background`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate your education background by selecting one or more options: High School, College, University, or Other.
* **Employment Status (Page 1)** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your current employment status: Full-time, Part-time, Not employed, or Retired.
* **Language Study Plan (Page 1)** (`language_study_plan`, `text`, required: false): Describe your language study plan, including your goals and objectives.
* **Additional Comments (Page 1)** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Use this section to provide any additional information or comments about your application.

## Tips
- Please ensure you've filled out all the required fields.
- Be as concise as possible when answering the questions.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the language school's admissions office.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
