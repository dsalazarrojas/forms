# Real Estate Open House Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Open House Checklist form is designed for real estate agents and staff to collect and track information about open houses. This form helps to streamline communication with visitors and potential buyers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1.  Select the property type (Office, Residential, Commercial, or Other)
2.  Enter the address of the open house
3.  Choose the date of the open house in the format `yyyy-mm-dd`
4.  Select the time of the open house
5.  Specify the show duration
6.  Enter the agent's name, phone number, and email address
7.  Enter the visitor's name and number
8.  Add any additional notes or comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **real_estate_type** (Office, Residential, Commercial, or Other): Select the type of property being shown.
*  **real_estate_address**: Enter the address of the open house.
*  **real_estate_open_house_date**: Enter the date of the open house in the format `yyyy-mm-dd`.
*  **real_estate_open_house_time**: Select the time of the open house.
*  **real_estate_showtime**: Select the time slot for the open house. (Note: This field is optional.)
*  **real_estate_show_duration**: Specify the duration of the open house show.
*  **real_estate_agent_name**: Enter the name of the real estate agent or staff member.
*  **real_estate_agent_phone**: Enter the phone number of the real estate agent or staff member.
*  **real_estate_agent_email**: Enter the email address of the real estate agent or staff member.
*  **real_estate_visitor_name**: Enter the name of the visitor or potential buyer.
*  **real_estate_visitor_number**: Enter the visitor or potential buyer's number (if applicable).
*  **real_estate_note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the open house.
*  **real_estate_note_2**: Add any additional notes or comments about the open house (if needed).
*  **real_estate_showtime_am_pm**: Select the time of the day for the open house (AM or PM).
*  **real_estate_show_duration_2**: Specify the duration of the open house show (if needed).
*  **real_estate_open_house_date_2**: Enter the date of the open house (if needed).
*  **real_estate_open_house_time_2**: Select the time of the open house (if needed).
*  **real_estate_show_duration_3**: Specify the duration of the open house show (if needed).
*  **real_estate_open_house_date_3**: Enter the date of the open house (if needed).
*  **real_estate_open_house_time_3**: Select the time of the open house (if needed).
*  **real_estate_show_duration_4**: Specify the duration of the open house show (if needed).
*  **real_estate_open_house_date_4**: Enter the date of the open house (if needed).
*  **real_estate_open_house_time_4**: Select the time of the open house (if needed).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Use the correct format for dates and times.
- Double-check the information before submitting the form.
- Use the form only for open houses.
