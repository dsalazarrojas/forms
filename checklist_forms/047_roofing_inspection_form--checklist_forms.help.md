# Roofing Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Roofing Inspection Form is a tool used to assess the condition of various roofing components, including the roof, chimney, gutters, flashing, vents, and skylights. This form helps inspectors gather detailed information about the condition of these components, which can be used to identify potential issues and prioritize repairs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is being requested.
2. For each section, enter a brief description of the condition of the corresponding roofing component.
3. Use the available space provided to provide as much detail as possible.
4. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Checklist** (`inspect_roofing`, text, optional): This section is a general overview of the inspection process. Enter a brief description of your inspection process, including any notable findings or observations.
* **Roof Condition** (`roof_condition`, text, optional): Describe the condition of the roof, including any notable damage, leaks, or defects.
* **Chimney Condition** (`chimney_condition`, text, optional): Assess the condition of the chimney, including any cracks, gaps, or damage.
* **Gutter Condition** (`gutter_condition`, text, optional): Evaluate the condition of the gutters, including any blockages, rust, or sagging.
* **Flashing Condition** (`flashing_condition`, text, optional): Inspect the condition of the flashing, including any gaps, cracks, or damage.
* **Vent Condition** (`vent_condition`, text, optional): Examine the condition of the vents, including any damage, blockages, or leaks.
* **Skylight Condition** (`skylight_condition`, text, optional): Check the condition of the skylights, including any damage, cracks, or gaps.
* **Soffit Condition** (`soffit_condition`, text, optional): Assess the condition of the soffits, including any damage, warping, or rotting.

## Tips
* Ensure to provide detailed and accurate information about the condition of each roofing component.
* Use clear and concise language to explain any issues or concerns.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the inspection process, refer to the manufacturer's guidelines or consult with a professional.
