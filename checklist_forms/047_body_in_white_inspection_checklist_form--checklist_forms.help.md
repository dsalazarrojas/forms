# Body In White Inspection Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Body In White Inspection Checklist Form is used to evaluate the quality of the BIW production line, ensuring consistency and accuracy in the manufacturing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. For select_one fields, choose the option that best describes the condition of the inspected item.
3. For text fields, enter a brief description or comment.
4. Sign and date the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Inspection Date (`inspection_date`)
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.

### 2. Inspection Time (`inspection_time`)
* **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the inspection (optional).

### 3. Inspector Name (`inspector_name`)
* **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the inspector performing the inspection.

### 4. Inspector ID (`inspector_id`)
* **Inspector ID**: Enter the ID of the inspector performing the inspection (optional).

### 5. Vehicle VIN (`vehicle_vin`)
* **Vehicle VIN**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the inspected vehicle.

### 6. Vehicle Model (`vehicle_model`)
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model name of the inspected vehicle.

### 7. Production Line (`production_line`)
* **Production Line**: Enter the line number of the inspected vehicle.

### 8. Station Number (`station_number`)
* **Station Number**: Enter the inspection station number (optional).

### 9. Inspection Type (`inspection_type`)
* **Inspection Type**: Choose the type of inspection performed (e.g., Routine, First Article, Random Sample, Quality Audit, or Pre-Shipment).

### 10. Panel Gaps Consistent (`panel_gaps_consistent`)
* **Panel Gaps Consistent**: Check if the panel gaps are consistent and even.

### 11. Panel Gaps Within Specification (`panel_gaps_within_spec`)
* **Panel Gaps Within Specification**: Check if the panel gaps are within the specified tolerance.

### 12. Surface Quality (`surface_quality`)
* **Surface Quality**: Check for dents, scratches, or other surface defects.

### 13. Weld Quality (`weld_quality`)
* **Weld Quality**: Check the weld points for quality and consistency.

### 14. Weld Count Correct (`weld_count_correct`)
* **Weld Count Correct**: Verify the number of welds is correct.

### 15. Flange Quality (`flange_quality`)
* **Flange Quality**: Check the flange condition for quality and consistency.

### 16. Hem Quality (`hem_quality`)
* **Hem Quality**: Check the hem condition for quality and consistency.

### 17. Edge Condition (`edge_condition`)
* **Edge Condition**: Check for sharp edges or burrs.

### 18. Corrosion Protection (`corrosion_protection`)
* **Corrosion Protection**: Check the coating application for coverage and quality.

### 19. Adhesive Application (`adhesive_application`)
* **Adhesive Application**: Check the adhesive beads for quality and consistency.

### 20. Mounting Points (`mounting_points`)
* **Mounting Points**: Check the alignment and condition of mounting points.

### 21. Dimensional Accuracy (`dimensional_accuracy`)
* **Dimensional Accuracy**: Check if the inspected vehicle is within the specified tolerance.

### 22. Assembly Completeness (`assembly_completeness`)
* **Assembly Completeness**: Check if all parts are installed correctly.

### 23. Defects Found (`defects_found`)
* **Defects Found**: List any defects or issues found during the inspection.

### 24. Defect Photos (`defect_photos`)
* **Defect Photos**: Enter the photo file names for any defects found.

### 25. Corrective Action Required (`corrective_action_required`)
* **Corrective Action Required**: Check if any corrective action is needed.

### 26. Corrective Action Description (`corrective_action_description`)
* **Corrective Action Description**: Enter a description of the corrective action required.

### 27. Overall Result (`overall_result`)
* **Overall Inspection Result**: Finalize the inspection result (e.g., Pass, Pass with Minor Deficiencies, Fail - Reword Required, or Fail - Critical Issues).

### 28. Inspector Signature (`inspector_signature`)
* **Inspector Signature**: Sign and date the form with the inspector's name.

### 29. Supervisor Review (`supervisor_review`)
* **Supervisor Review**: Enter the supervisor's name if required.
