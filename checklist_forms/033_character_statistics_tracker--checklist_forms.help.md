# Character Statistics Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track character progress and statistics across multiple sessions in role-playing games or similar activities. The purpose of this form is to provide an easy and organized way to keep track of character data, including levels, experience points, health, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Campaign Title or Story Name**: Enter the title or name of the campaign or story your character is participating in.
2. **Player Full Name**: Enter your full name as a player.
3. **Character Full Name**: Enter your character's full name.
4. **Current Character Level**: Enter your character's current level as of this session.
5. **Total Experience Points (XP)**: Enter the cumulative XP earned by your character.
6. **XP Earned This Session**: Enter the amount of XP gained since the last update.
7. **Maximum Health (Hit Points)**: Enter your character's total health (hit points).
8. **Current Health (Hit Points)**: Enter the health remaining at the end of the session.
9. **Enemies Defeated This Session**: Enter the total count of enemies defeated during this session.
10. **Significant Achievement or Milestone**: Describe one major success or milestone achieved during this session.
11. **New Items Acquired This Session**: List 2-3 significant items acquired during this session.
12. **Gold and Currency Total Update**: Enter the cumulative wealth of your character.
13. **Confirmation of Stats Update**: Select "I Confirm and Log Session Stats" to confirm that the stats accurately reflect the session's progress.
14. **Date of Session Log**: Enter the date of the current session log submission.
15. **Additional Session Notes**: Enter any other relevant information or context about the session.
16. **Interest in Follow-up Campaign**: Select "Yes, please notify me" if you'd like to be notified about the next session.
