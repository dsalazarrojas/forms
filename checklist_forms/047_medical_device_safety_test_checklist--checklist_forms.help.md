# Medical Device Safety Test Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Medical Device Safety Test Checklist is a set of questions designed to evaluate the safety of medical devices. The form helps to identify potential hazards and risks associated with the device and ensures that it is safe for use in a clinical setting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and thoughtfully, considering the safety and functionality of the medical device.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the most accurate answer based on your assessment.
3. Enter your name and date to complete the form.
