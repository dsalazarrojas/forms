# home_finish_selections_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about finish selections for a project. It includes fields for flooring, paint, cabinetry, and other selections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about your project's finish selections.
2. Review each field and select the relevant options for your selections.
3. Complete each field according to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. home_finish_selections_checklist_page_1 (Page 1, text, **Required**)
This field is the first page of the form. It serves as a placeholder for the form's title and is not a field to be filled out by the user.

### 2. flooring_selections (Page 2, select_multiple, **Optional**)
Select all the flooring selections that apply to your project. You can choose from "Yes" and "No" options.

### 3. paint_selections (Page 3, select_one, **Required**)
Select one of the paint options that apply to your project. You can choose from "Yes" and "No" options.

### 4. cabinetry_selections (Page 4, select_multiple, **Optional**)
Select all the cabinetry selections that apply to your project. You can choose from "Yes" and "No" options.

### 5. other_finish_selections (Page 5, text, **Optional**)
Enter any other finish selections that are not listed in the previous fields. This is a free-form text field for any additional information.

### 6. other_finish_selections_hint (Page 6, text, **Optional**)
Enter a brief description or details about the other finish selections you selected in the previous field.

### 7. finish_selections_notes (Page 7, note, **Optional**)
Enter any additional notes about your finish selections. This is a free-form text field for any comments or explanations.

### 8. finish_selections_email (Page 8, email, **Optional**)
Enter the email address associated with your finish selections.

### 9. finish_selections_phone (Page 9, text, **Optional**)
Enter the phone number associated with your finish selections.

### 10. finish_selections_time (Page 10, time, **Optional**)
Enter the time allocated for your finish selections.

## Tips
* Ensure you select all relevant options for your project's finish selections.
* Use the "other_finish_selections" field for any additional information that is not listed in the previous fields.
* Enter accurate contact information in the "finish_selections_email" and "finish_selections_phone" fields if applicable.
