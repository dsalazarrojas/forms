# New Car Purchase Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a new car purchase from the user. The form helps in gathering essential details about the vehicle, its condition, pricing, and other relevant aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Vehicle Details** section, providing a brief description of the vehicle being purchased.
2. In the **Owner Details** section, enter the owner's information.
3. Choose the **Vehicle Condition** from the available options (New, Used, or Certified Pre-Owned).
4. In the **Vehicle Pricing** section, enter the vehicle's price.
5. In the **Delivery Details** section, provide any additional delivery instructions or information.
6. Check the **Vehicle Registration** box if the vehicle is registered.
7. In the **Vehicle Images** section, choose whether or not to upload vehicle images.
8. In the **Delivery Notes** section, add any additional delivery notes.
9. Select the **Vehicle Status** (Active or Inactive) to indicate the vehicle's status.
10. Date and time of form submission in the **Form Submitted** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Details**: Enter a brief description of the vehicle being purchased (required).
* **Owner Details**: Enter the owner's name and contact information (optional).
* **Vehicle Condition**: Choose from the available options (New, Used, or Certified Pre-Owned) (required).
* **Vehicle Pricing**: Enter the vehicle's price (required).
* **Delivery Details**: Provide any additional delivery instructions or information (optional).
* **Vehicle Registration**: Check this box if the vehicle is registered (required).
* **Vehicle Images**: Choose whether or not to upload vehicle images (required).
* **Delivery Notes**: Add any additional delivery notes (optional).
* **Vehicle Status**: Select the vehicle's status (Active or Inactive) (required).
* **Form Submitted**: Date and time of form submission (required).

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all required fields to ensure accurate data collection.
* Be specific when describing the vehicle's condition and status.
* Upload vehicle images if possible to provide more information about the vehicle.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date delivery notes if applicable.
* Double-check the form before submission to avoid errors.
