# Walkability Audit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Walkability Audit Checklist is a tool used to assess the walkability of a location, evaluating the ease of use or navigability of a pedestrian or pedestrian path in an area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field that apply to the location you are assessing.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible to help identify potential issues or obstacles.
3. Answer each question truthfully and accurately, based on your observations and experiences.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Streets**: This section asks you to select the streets or pathways that are relevant to the area being assessed. Select all streets or pathways that are relevant to the location.
* **Campuses**: This section asks you to select the campuses or educational institutions that are relevant to the area being assessed. Select all campuses or educational institutions that are relevant to the location.
* **Properties**: This section asks you to select the properties or buildings that are relevant to the area being assessed. Select all properties or buildings that are relevant to the location.
* **Comments**: This section is for any additional comments or observations you may have about the walkability of the area.
* **Walkability Observations 1-18**: These sections are for specific observations related to walkability issues, such as obstacles, hazards, or difficulties in the area.
* **Submit**: This is the final step, where you will submit your completed form. Please review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Tips
* Be as thorough and accurate as possible when completing the form.
* Provide as much detail as possible for each field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please select the "Don't Know" or "Not Applicable" option.
* If you have any additional comments or observations, use the "Comments" field to provide them.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
