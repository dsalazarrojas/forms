# Daily Team Huddle Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Team Huddle Checklist is a tool used to facilitate daily team discussions, promote transparency, and ensure everyone is on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your team member name in the **Team Member** field.
2. Choose your **Attendance** status from the available options (Attended, Absent, Late, On Vacation, or Working Remotely).
3. Share your **Stand-up Notes** in the provided field.
4. Identify any **Blockers** you are currently experiencing and select them from the options.
5. Provide details about the **Blockers** you have selected by typing in the **Blocker Details** field.
6. Specify your **Next Steps**, which are the actions you plan to take or tasks you will complete next.
7. Add any **Comments** you want to make about the previous day's discussions or upcoming challenges.
8. Record **Progress Updates** about any accomplishments or milestones achieved.
9. Select the date and time you are available for the daily huddle from the **Date** and **Time** fields.
10. Assign a task or issue to **Assigned To** by selecting a team member from the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member**: Enter your name so we can identify you as a team member contributing to the daily huddle.
* **Attendance**: Choose how you were present during the previous day's huddle (Attended, Absent, Late, On Vacation, or Working Remotely).
* **Stand-up Notes**: Share your notes from the previous day, including any key points or questions you want to discuss.
* **Blockers**: Select any blockers or obstacles you are facing, such as technical or non-technical issues.
* **Blocker Details**: Provide more details about the blockers you selected, so we can work together to overcome them.
* **Next Steps**: Specify the actions you plan to take or tasks you will complete next, so we can track progress.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or questions you have about the previous day's discussions or upcoming challenges.
* **Progress Updates**: Share any progress made on tasks or milestones since the previous day.
* **Date**: Select the date for which you are reporting your attendance and progress.
* **Time**: Choose the time you are available for the daily huddle.
* **Assigned To**: Select a team member to whom you would like to assign a task or issue for further action or resolution.
* **Assigned To**: This field will be used for tracking and assignment of tasks, so please select the right person for the job.
