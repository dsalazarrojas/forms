# Daily Closing Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Closing Checklist is a form used to track and document the completion of various daily procedures and activities at the end of the day.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the "End of Day Procedures" field, where you can choose whether you completed the end of day procedures or not.
2. In the "Confirm Tasks" field, select the tasks that were completed or not.
3. Choose "Confirm Close" to confirm that the day is closed.
4. In the "Comments" field, provide any additional comments or notes for the day.
5. Enter the shift start and end times in the "Shift" fields.
6. Enter the location in the "Location" field, and provide any additional notes in the "Location Notes" field.
7. Select the person assigned to handle any issues or concerns in the "Assigned To" field.
8. Enter the name of the assigned person in the "Assigned To Name" field.
9. Enter the title and email of the assigned person in the "Assigned To Title" and "Assigned To Email" fields, respectively.
10. Enter the phone number of the assigned person in the "Assigned To Phone" field.
11. Provide additional comments in the "Shift Notes" field.
12. Enter any additional location notes in the "Location Notes 2" field.
13. Set goals or targets for the day in the "Goals" field.
14. Enter specific goals or targets 1 through 8 in the "Goal 1" through "Goal 8" fields.
