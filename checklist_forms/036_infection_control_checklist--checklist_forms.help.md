# Infection Control Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Infection Control Checklist is a form designed to gather information about patient infection control measures and follow-up activities. This form helps healthcare professionals track and update patient infection status, staff training, and follow-up details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the patient's name.
*   Choose the current date and time of contact.
*   Enter the patient's phone number and email address (if available).
*   Enter the patient's address and the name of the department they are visiting.
*   Update the patient's condition and any infection control practices being implemented.
*   Select the status of the patient's infection (not infected or infected).
*   Choose the date and time for follow-up activities.
*   Enter the contact person's name and the location of follow-up activities.
*   Add any follow-up notes.
