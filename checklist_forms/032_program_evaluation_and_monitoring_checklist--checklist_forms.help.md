# Program Evaluation And Monitoring Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Program Evaluation And Monitoring Checklist is a form used to track and evaluate the success or progress of projects. It helps to assess project outcomes, progress, budget, team, and follow-up information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Project Outcome" field to choose the overall outcome of the project from the options "Successful", "Partially successful", and "Unsuccessful".
2. Enter the "Program Goals" field to describe the goals of the project.
3. Enter the "Project Progress" field to track the progress of the project.
4. Enter the "Project Scope" field to describe the scope of the project.
5. Enter the "Project Start Date" field to record the start date of the project.
6. Enter the "Project End Date" field to record the end date of the project.
7. Enter the "Project Start Location" field to record the location where the project started.
8. Enter the "Project End Location" field to record the location where the project ended.
9. Enter the "Project Budget" field to record the budget of the project.
10. Select the "Project Team" field to choose the team members involved in the project from the options "Program Director", "Project Manager", "Project Coordinator", and "Other".
11. Select the "Project Outcome Frequency" field to choose the frequency of project outcomes from the available options.
12. Select the "Project Outcome Status" field to choose the status of project outcomes from the options "In Progress", "Not Yet Started", and "Completed".
13. Enter the "Program Evaluated By" field to record the person who evaluated the project.
14. Enter the "Project Follow Up" field to record any follow-up information.
15. Select the "Follow Up Status" field to choose whether follow-up was done from the options "True" and "False".
16. Enter the "Project Outcome Frequency 2" field to record the frequency of project outcomes 2.
17. Select the "Project Outcome Status 2" field to choose the status of project outcomes 2 from the options "In Progress", "Not Yet Started", and "Completed".
18. Select the "Project Outcome 2" field to choose the outcome of project 2 from the options "Successful", "Partially successful", and "Unsuccessful".
19. Enter the "Follow Up Date" field to record the follow-up date.
20. Enter the "Follow Up Time" field to record the follow-up time.
21. Enter the "Follow Up Location" field to record the follow-up location.
22. Enter the "Follow Up Note" field to record any follow-up notes.
23. Enter the "Follow Up By" field to record the person who did the follow-up.
24. Enter the "Project Outcome Measures" field to record any outcome measures.
25. Enter the "Project Outcome Indicators" field to record any outcome indicators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Outcome** (`Project Outcome`, select_one, required): Choose the overall outcome of the project from the options "Successful", "Partially successful", and "Unsuccessful".
* **Program Goals** (`Program Goals`, text, optional): Describe the goals of the project.
* **Program Progress** (`Program Progress`, number, optional): Track the progress of the project.
* **Project Scope** (`Project Scope`, text, optional): Describe the scope of the project.
* **Project Start Date** (`Project Start Date`, date, optional): Record the start date of the project.
* **Project End Date** (`Project End Date`, date, optional): Record the end date of the project.
* **Project Start Location** (`Project Start Location`, text, optional): Record the location where the project started.
* **Project End Location** (`Project End Location`, text, optional): Record the location where the project ended.
* **Project Budget** (`Project Budget`, number, optional): Record the budget of the project.
* **Project Team** (`Project Team`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the team members involved in the project from the options "Program Director", "Project Manager", "Project Coordinator", and "Other".
* **Project Outcome Frequency** (`Project Outcome Frequency`, number, optional): Choose the frequency of project outcomes from the available options.
* **Project Outcome Status** (`Project Outcome Status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of project outcomes from the options "In Progress", "Not Yet Started", and "Completed".
* **Program Evaluated By** (`Program Evaluated By`, text, optional): Record the person who evaluated the project.
* **Project Follow Up** (`Project Follow Up`, note, optional): Record any follow-up information.
* **Follow Up Status** (`Follow Up Status`, select_one, optional): Choose whether follow-up was done from the options "True" and "False".
* **Project Outcome Frequency 2** (`Project Outcome Frequency 2`, number, optional): Record the frequency of project outcomes 2.
* **Project Outcome Status 2** (`Project Outcome Status 2`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of project outcomes 2 from the options "In Progress", "Not Yet Started", and "Completed".
* **Project Outcome 2** (`Project Outcome 2`, select_one, optional): Choose the outcome of project 2 from the options "Successful", "Partially successful", and "Unsuccessful".
* **Follow Up Date** (`Follow Up Date`, date, optional): Record the follow-up date.
* **Follow Up Time** (`Follow Up Time`, time, optional): Record the follow-up time.
* **Follow Up Location** (`Follow Up Location`, text, optional): Record the follow-up location.
* **Follow Up Note** (`Follow Up Note`, note, optional): Record any follow-up notes.
* **Follow Up By** (`Follow Up By`, text, optional): Record the person who did the follow-up.
* **Project Outcome Measures** (`Project Outcome Measures`, text, optional): Record any outcome measures.
* **Project Outcome Indicators** (`Project Outcome Indicators`, text, optional): Record any outcome indicators.

## Tips

* Please make sure to complete all the required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation above.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the project administrator.
