# visitor_management_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The visitor management checklist form is designed to collect essential information from visitors to ensure secure and efficient visitor management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. If a field is marked as required, it is essential to fill it out.
4. If you are unsure about any field, check the instructions or guidelines provided by the form administrators or support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page**: This page serves as the introduction to the form and provides a brief overview.
* **Contact Info**: Please provide your contact details accurately and truthfully.
	+ Type your contact information in this text field.
* **Photo Info**: Select either "Yes" or "No" depending on the nature of your visit.
* **ID Number**: Enter your ID number accurately.
* **Name**: Provide your name accurately and truthfully.
* **Date**: If required, select the date of your visit.
* **Time**: If required, select the time of your visit.
* **Purpose**: Briefly describe the reason for your visit.
* **Department**: Select the department you are visiting.
* **Contact**: If you have a contact person, provide their email address.
* **Phone**: Provide the phone number of your contact person.
* **Visitor Type**: Briefly describe the type of visitor you are (e.g., employee, contractor, etc.).
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests or requirements, please list them here.
* **Visitor Info**: Provide any additional information about your visit.
* **Signature**: This field is for the visitor's signature.
* **Additional Info**: If you have any additional information to provide, use this text field.
* **Photo**: If required, upload a photo of yourself.
* **ID Photo**: If required, upload a photo of your ID.
* **Document**: If required, provide a scanned copy of your ID or other required document.
* **ID Document**: If required, scan a copy of your ID or other required document.
* **Notes**: If you have any additional information to provide, use this text field.
* **Signature Date**: If required, select the date of your signature.
* **Signature Time**: If required, select the time of your signature.
* **Signature Status**: If required, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your signature has been verified.
* **Verified**: If required, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your signature has been verified.
