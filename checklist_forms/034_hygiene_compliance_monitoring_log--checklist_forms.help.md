This form is used to track and report on the status of hygiene compliance in a facility. This form is meant to be completed by supervisors or inspectors after visiting a specific area of the facility. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all areas of the facility are being maintained in a clean and safe state, and to track any issues that have been found and the actions taken to address them.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

## Purpose
The Hygiene Compliance Monitoring Log is used to track and report on the status of hygiene compliance in a facility. This form is meant to be completed by supervisors or inspectors after visiting a specific area of the facility.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the "Area Visited" page, selecting the area of the facility that was inspected.
2.  Record the time the area was visited using the "Time Visited" field. Note that this field is not required, but will help in tracking the schedule of visits.
3.  Check the "Issues Found" field to see if any issues were identified during the visit. If there were issues, describe them in the "Issue Description" field.
4.  Check the "Corrective Action" field to see if any action was taken to address the issues found. If so, describe the action in this field.
5.  Check the "Supervisor Verified" field to see if the action taken has been verified by a supervisor.
6.  Check the "Area Checked" field to see if the area has been checked for other potential issues.
7.  Check the "Retest Date" and "Retest Time" fields if a retest is needed.
8.  Check the "Retested By" field to see if the retest has been completed by someone.
