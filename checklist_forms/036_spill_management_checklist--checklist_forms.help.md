# Spill Management Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents or spills that occur within the organization, ensuring accurate documentation and prompt response.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the following fields with relevant information:

    *   **Incident ID** (`1`): Enter a unique identifier for the incident.
    *   **Date** (`2`): Enter the date the incident occurred.
    *   **Time** (`3`): Enter the time the incident occurred.
    *   **Spill Type** (`4`): Select the type of spill that occurred from the list:
        - IDLE SPILL
        - LEAK
        - RELEASE
        - SPILL
    *   **Location** (`5`): Enter the location where the spill occurred.
    *   **Affected Areas** (`6`): Select all areas affected by the spill.
        - Grounds
        - Offices
        - Workstations
    *   **Spill Volume** (`7`): Enter the volume of the spill.
    *   **Spill Duration** (`8`): Enter the duration of the spill.
    *   **Response Time** (`9`): Enter the time it took to respond to the spill.
    *   **Response Duration** (`10`): Enter the duration of the response.
    *   **Response Status** (`11`): Select the status of the response:
        - COMPLETED
        - ONGOING
        - NOT STARTED
    *   **Notes** (`12`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the incident.
    *   **Contact Name** (`13`): Enter the name of the contact person.
    *   **Contact Email** (`14`): Enter the email of the contact person.
    *   **Contact Phone** (`15`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
    *   **External Phone Number** (`16`): Enter the external phone number of the contact person.
    *   **External Phone Type** (`17`): Select the type of external phone number from the list:
        - Mobile
        - Work
        - Home
    *   **External Phone Extension** (`18`): Enter the external phone number extension.
    *   **External Phone Number** (`19`): Enter the external phone number (if different from above).
    *   **Submitted By** (`20`): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
    *   **Submitted Date** (`21`): Enter the date the form was submitted.
    *   **Submitted Time** (`22`): Enter the time the form was submitted.
    *   **Reviewer Name** (`23`): Enter the name of the reviewer.
    *   **Reviewer Email** (`24`): Enter the email of the reviewer.
    *   **Reviewer Phone** (`25`): Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
