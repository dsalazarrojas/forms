# Wholly Owned Subsidiary Incorporation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a wholly owned subsidiary for incorporation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents required for completion.
2. Fill in the relevant fields with the required information.
3. Review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Setup Tasks** (`setup_tasks`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional setup tasks you may have for the entity. If none, you can leave this field blank.
* **Entity Name** (`entity_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the entity.
* **Entity Address** (`entity_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the entity.
* **Entity Type** (`entity_type`, `text`, optional): Select the type of entity.
* **Legal Entity Type** (`legal_entity_type`, `text`, optional): Select the legal entity type.
* **Entity Status** (`entity_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the entity (Active or Inactive).
* **Status Date** (`status_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the entity's status.
* **Business Number** (`business_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the business number.
* **Principal Place of Business** (`principal_place_of_business`, `text`, optional): Enter the principal place of business.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the business address.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the business phone number.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the business email.
* **Entity License Number** (`entity_license_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the entity license number.
* **Entity Incorporation Date** (`entity_incorporation_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of entity incorporation.
* **Entity Status Description** (`entity_status_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the entity's status.
* **Entity Tax Filing Status** (`entity_tax_filing_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the tax filing status (Active or Inactive).
* **Entity Active Status** (`entity_active_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the active status (Active or Inactive).
* **Entity Operating Status** (`entity_operating_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the operating status (Good or Fair).
* **Entity Dissolved Status** (`entity_dissolved_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the dissolved status (Active or Inactive).
* **Entity Dissolved Date** (`entity_dissolved_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the entity was dissolved.
* **Entity Dissolved Reason** (`entity_dissolved_reason`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for entity dissolution.
* **Entity Dissolved Status Description** (`entity_dissolved_status_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the entity's dissolved status.
* **Entity Reinstated Status** (`entity_reinstated_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the reinstated status (Active or Inactive).
* **Entity Reinstated Date** (`entity_reinstated_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of entity reinstatement.
* **Entity Reinstated Reason** (`entity_reinstated_reason`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for entity reinstatement.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents before proceeding.
* Refer to the relevant documentation for any specific requirements or regulations.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please seek assistance from the relevant authorities.
* Review and submit the form carefully to avoid errors.
