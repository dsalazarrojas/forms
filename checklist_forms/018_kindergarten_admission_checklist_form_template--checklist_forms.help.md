# Kindergarten Admission Checklist Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a Kindergarten admission process, including contact, medical, and emergency contact information for a child who is applying for admission to the kindergarten.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, providing accurate and up-to-date information for each section.
2. Start with the student's personal information, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
3. Move on to the medical section, where you will provide any relevant medical information, such as allergies, medical conditions, and medical history.
4. Next, list any emergency contacts, including names, phone numbers, and relationship.
5. If applicable, provide any special needs information, including any special education or medical requirements.
6. Finally, sign and date the form, indicating that you have reviewed and acknowledged the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Kindergarten Admission Checklist**: This is the title of the form, and serves as a header for the rest of the form.
* **Student Name**: Please fill out the child's full name, as it appears on their birth certificate or ID.
* **Student DOB**: Enter the child's date of birth.
* **Contact Number**: Provide the best phone number to contact you at, including a primary and secondary number if applicable.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and updates.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: List the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Provide the contact number for the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Notes**: Enter any relevant medical information, including allergies, medical conditions, or special needs.
* **Allergy Information**: List any allergies or sensitivities that the child has.
* **Special Needs**: Provide any special education or medical needs that the child may have.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions that the child has.
* **Family History**: Enter any relevant family medical history, including any genetic conditions or medical conditions that may affect the child.
* **Parent Signature**: Sign and date this section, indicating that you have reviewed and acknowledged the information provided.
* **Doctor Sign Off**: Sign and date this section, indicating that a medical professional has reviewed and acknowledged the information provided.
* **Teacher Sign Off**: Sign and date this section, indicating that a teacher or educational professional has reviewed and acknowledged the information provided.
* **Other Medical Conditions**: List any other medical conditions that are not listed above.
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history, including any previous medical conditions or treatments.
* **Family Medicine**: Enter any relevant family medical history, including any genetic conditions or medical conditions that may affect the family.
* **Emergency Medical**: List any emergency medical procedures that may be required.
* **Emergency Contacts**: List any emergency contacts, including names, phone numbers, and relationship.
* **Special Needs Information**: Provide any special needs information, including any special education or medical requirements.
* **Teacher Notes**: Enter any notes or comments from a teacher or educational professional.
* **Doctor Prescription**: Enter any relevant medical prescriptions or treatments.
* **Doctor Signature**: Sign and date this section, indicating that a medical professional has reviewed and acknowledged the information provided.
* **Final Comments**: Enter any final comments or notes from a teacher or educational professional.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have completed the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
* Keep the form in a secure location, and only share it with authorized personnel.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure that the information remains current and accurate.
