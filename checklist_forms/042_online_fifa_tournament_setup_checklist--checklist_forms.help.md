# Online FIFA Tournament Setup Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your online FIFA tournament, including the name and start date of the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your tournament in the "tournament_name" field. This is the main title of your tournament.
2. If applicable, select the start date of your tournament using the calendar picker in the "start_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **tournament_name** (text, required): Enter the name of your online FIFA tournament. This will be the title of your tournament and will be displayed to participants.
### **start_date** (date, optional): Select the start date of your tournament. If you're unsure of the exact date, you can leave this field blank for now and come back to it later.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the tournament name for spelling and grammar accuracy.
* If you're unsure about the start date, it's better to be safe than sorry and leave it blank for now. You can always come back and update it later.
