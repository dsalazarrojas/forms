# Physical Security Supervisor Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from the physical security supervisor for a specific site. It helps to track and document the status of tasks, incidents, and physical security systems on the site.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For select fields, choose the relevant options that apply to your situation.
3. For date fields, enter the date in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
4. For text fields, enter a brief description or comment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **general_info** (Note, not required): This is a note field for any general information or comments about the site.
* **site_access** (\*: True): Indicate if the physical security system is in place and accessible.
* **physical_security_system** (Select multiple, not required): Choose all physical security systems that apply to your situation.
* **site_inspection** (\*: True): Enter the date of the last site inspection.
* **task_completion_status** (\*: Active/Inactive): Indicate the status of the task completion.
* **incident_reporting** (Select multiple, not required): Choose all incident reporting methods that apply.
* **incident_summary** (Text, not required): Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **recommendations** (Select multiple, not required): Choose all recommendations that apply.
* **task_description** (Select multiple, not required): Choose all task descriptions that apply.
* **task_status** (Select one, not required): Indicate the status of the task.
* **incident_description** (Select multiple, not required): Choose all incident descriptions that apply.
* **incident_priority** (Select one, not required): Indicate the priority of the incident.
* **incident_category** (Select multiple, not required): Choose all incident categories that apply.
* **task_due_date** (Date, not required): Enter the due date of the task.
* **task_responsible** (Select one, not required): Indicate the responsible person for the task.
* **incident_responsible** (Select one, not required): Indicate the responsible person for the incident.
* **task_completion_date** (Date, not required): Enter the completion date of the task.
* **incident_completion_date** (Date, not required): Enter the completion date of the incident.
* **incident_type** (Select one, not required): Indicate the type of incident.
* **incident_status** (Select one, not required): Indicate the status of the incident.
* **incident_comments** (Text, not required): Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **general_comments** (Text, not required): Enter a general comment about the site.
* **incident_id** (\*: True): Enter the unique incident ID.
* **site_id** (\*: True): Enter the site ID.
* **task_id** (\*: True): Enter the task ID.

Note: This form is intended to be filled out by physical security supervisors and is meant to provide information about the status of tasks, incidents, and physical security systems on the site. The fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required fields.
