# Preschool Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the availability and readiness of various materials and supplies for a preschool setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page to understand the different sections and their corresponding questions.
2. Select the relevant options for each page, indicating whether the corresponding item is available or not.
3. Ensure you answer each question honestly, as this will help in planning and budgeting for future needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School Year
* **school_year**: Select the current school year (`Yes` or `No`).

### Classroom Supplies
* **classroom_supplies**: Select the items you have available in the classroom for the preschoolers (`Yes` or `No`).

### Educational Materials
* **educational_materials**: Select the educational materials you have available in the classroom for the preschoolers (`Yes` or `No`).

### Art Supplies
* **art_supplies**: Select the art supplies you have available in the classroom for the preschoolers (`Yes` or `No`).

### Craft Supplies
* **craft_supplies**: Select the craft supplies you have available in the classroom for the preschoolers (`Yes` or `No`).

### Classroom Furniture
* **classroom_furniture**: Select the classroom furniture you have available in the classroom for the preschoolers (`Yes` or `No`).

### Technology Supplies
* **tech_supplies**: Select the technology supplies you have available in the classroom for the preschoolers (`Yes` or `No`).

### Safety Items
* **safety_items**: Select the safety items you have available in the classroom for the preschoolers (`Yes` or `No`).

## Tips
* Be honest about the availability of each item to ensure accurate planning and budgeting.
* Review the form regularly to track changes and updates in your classroom's inventory.
* Use this form as a tool for future planning and budgeting to ensure the preschoolers have the necessary materials and supplies for a safe and effective educational environment.
