# volunteer_safety_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Volunteer Safety Checklist" is a form used to gather information about a volunteer's safety-related details and emergency contact information. This form helps to identify potential risks or vulnerabilities that the volunteer may face and prepares for emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal contact information:
	* "First Name"
	* "Last Name"
2. Provide your email and phone number for emergency contacts:
	* "email"
	* "phone_number"
3. If you have any medical conditions, please list them:
	* "medical_conditions"
4. List any allergies or sensitivities you have:
	* "allergies"
5. Describe the safety protocols you follow:
	* "safety_protocols"
6. List any emergency procedures you have taken:
	* "emergency_procedures"
7. If you are visiting a medical facility, please provide the name of the facility:
	* "medical_facility"
8. Identify your nearest emergency contact:
	* "nearest_emergency_contact"
9. Provide the location of the nearest emergency contact:
	* "nearest_emergency_location"
10. If you need to contact someone in an emergency, please provide their phone number:
	* "emergency_phone"
11. List any medical supplies you have access to:
	* "medical_supplies"
12. Select the type of first aid kit you have:
	* "first_aid_kit"
13. If you have received first aid training, select the level:
	* "first_aid_training"
14. If you have oxygen equipment, select the type:
	* "oxygen_equipment"
15. Describe your emergency plan:
	* "emergency_plan"
16. List up to two emergency contacts:
	* "emergency_contact_name_1", "emergency_contact_phone_1", "emergency_contact_relationship_1"
	* "emergency_contact_name_2", "emergency_contact_phone_2", "emergency_contact_relationship_2"

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (**1. first_name**)
* A text field to input your first name.

### Last Name (**2. last_name**)
* A text field to input your last name.

### Email (**3. email**)
* A text field to input your email address.

### Phone Number (**4. phone_number**)
* A field to input your phone number.

### Emergency Contact Name (**5. emergency_contact_name**)
* A text field to input the name of your emergency contact.

### Relationship with Emergency Contact (**6. emergency_contact_relationship**)
* A text field to describe your relationship with the emergency contact.

### Medical Conditions (**7. medical_conditions**)
* A text field to list any medical conditions you have.

### Allergies (**8. allergies**)
* A text field to list any allergies or sensitivities you have.

### Safety Protocols (**9. safety_protocols**)
* A text field to describe the safety protocols you follow.

### Emergency Procedures (**10. emergency_procedures**)
* A text field to list any emergency procedures you have taken.

### Medical Facility (**11. medical_facility**)
* A text field to input the name of the medical facility you visit.

### Nearest Emergency Contact (**12. nearest_emergency_contact**)
* A text field to identify your nearest emergency contact.

### Nearest Emergency Location (**13. nearest_emergency_location**)
* A text field to input the location of your nearest emergency contact.

### Emergency Phone (**14. emergency_phone**)
* A text field to input the phone number of your nearest emergency contact.

### Medical Supplies (**15. medical_supplies**)
* A select multiple field to select the medical supplies you have access to:
	* AED
	* EpiPen
	* First Aid Kit
	* First Aid Training
	* CPR
	* Oxygen

### First Aid Kit (**16. first_aid_kit**)
* A select one field to select the type of first aid kit you have:
	* Basic
	* Advanced
	* Both

### First Aid Training (**17. first_aid_training**)
* A select one field to select the level of first aid training you have:
	* Basic
	* Advanced
	* Both

### Oxygen Equipment (**18. oxygen_equipment**)
* A select multiple field to select the type of oxygen equipment you have:
	* Portable
	* Inpatient

### Emergency Plan (**19. emergency_plan**)
* A text field to describe your emergency plan.

### First Contact Information (**20. emergency_contact_name_1**, **21. emergency_contact_phone_1**, **22. emergency_contact_relationship_1**)
* A set of text fields to input the information of your first emergency contact:
	* "emergency_contact_name_1"
	* "emergency_contact_phone_1"
	* "emergency_contact_relationship_1"

### Second Contact Information (**23. emergency_contact_name_2**, **24. emergency_contact_phone_2**, **25. emergency_contact_relationship_2**)
* A set of text fields to input the information of your second emergency contact:
	* "emergency_contact_name_2"
	* "emergency_contact_phone_2"
	* "emergency_contact_relationship_2"

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible in your answers to ensure accurate information.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your emergency contact or supervisor for guidance.
- If you have any questions about the form, please contact your supervisor or the form administrator.
