# rental_checklist_check_out_check_in - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the rental process of a piece of equipment, specifically for the check-out and check-in process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Check Out Date** in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
2. Enter the **Check Out Time** in the format hh:mm am/pm.
3. Enter the **Check In Date** in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Enter the **Check In Time** in the format hh:mm am/pm.
5. Describe the **Equipment Condition** in the text field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input Form** (`user_input_form`, text): Enter any additional information about the rental that you want to include.
* **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, date): Enter the date you will be taking the equipment out for rent in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Check Out Time** (`check_out_time`, time): Enter the time you will be taking the equipment out for rent in the format hh:mm am/pm.
* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, date): Enter the date you expect to return the equipment in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Check In Time** (`check_in_time`, time): Enter the time you expect to return the equipment in the format hh:mm am/pm.
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, text): Describe the condition of the equipment when you received it. This field is optional.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date): Enter the date you actually returned the equipment in the format mm/dd/yyyy. This field is optional and only visible if you have already checked out equipment on this form.
* **Return Time** (`return_time`, time): Enter the time you actually returned the equipment in the format hh:mm am/pm. This field is optional and only visible if you have already checked out equipment on this form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Use the correct formats for dates and times.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the rental staff for assistance.
