# First Responder Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The First Responder Checklist Form is designed to help first responders quickly assess the situation and gather essential information about victims of various injuries. This form is intended for use in emergency situations where first responders need to collect data about the victim's status, medical conditions, and response time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary permissions and clearance to access the victim.
2. Read the form carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible based on the information you have collected.
3. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing as much detail as possible about the victim's situation.
4. Submit the form to the designated authority for further evaluation and treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Initial Assessment (1)
* **Initial Assessment** (`initial_assessment`, `text`, optional): Use this field to describe the situation you initially encountered and any initial impressions or observations you made.

### Victim Status (2)
* **Victim Status** (`victim_status`, `select_one`, optional): Use this field to indicate the severity of the injury, selecting one of the following options:
	+ Critical
	+ Non-critical
	+ Dead

### Vital Signs (3)
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, `text`, optional): Use this field to note any vital signs you observed, such as pulse, breathing rate, or body temperature.

### Injury Description (4)
* **Injury Description** (`injury_description`, `text`, optional): Use this field to describe the injury or condition you observed in as much detail as possible.

### Medical Conditions (5)
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the medical conditions you suspect the victim may have, such as:
	+ Cardiovascular
	+ Pulmonary
	+ Neurological

### Response Time (6)
* **Response Time** (`response_time`, `time`, optional): Use this field to note the time it took for you to arrive on the scene or respond to the emergency.

### Patient Name (7)
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, optional): Use this field to record the name of the victim, if available.

### Medical Treatment (8)
* **Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment`, `text`, optional): Use this field to describe any medical treatment or actions taken on the scene.

# Tips
* Always use your professional judgment when assessing the situation and collecting information.
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Do not use this form as a substitute for professional medical evaluation.
* Consult with medical experts when in doubt or unsure about any information collected.
