# Business Sustainability Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate an organization's sustainability practices across various categories, including environmental, social, and governance. It aims to help identify areas where the organization is excelling and areas where improvement is needed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Organization Name, Department or Division, and Assessor Name fields with your company's information.
2. Select the date when this assessment is being conducted.
3. Enter your contact email for further communication.
4. Choose the status of your environmental practices (Fully Implemented, Mostly Implemented, Partially Implemented, Minimally Implemented, or Not Implemented).
5. Select the types of environmental practices you have implemented (Recycling Program, Composting, Waste Audit, Supplier Requirements, Employee Training, or None).
6. Choose how well you use renewable energy (Yes, fully, Yes, partially, Planning to implement, No plans currently, or Not applicable).
7. Select the measures you have implemented for water conservation (Low-flow fixtures, Rainwater harvesting, Water monitoring, Leak prevention, Landscape optimization, or None).
8. Answer the question about whether you have measured your carbon footprint (Yes, annually, Yes, occasionally, In progress, Not yet, or No plans).
9. Select the types of social responsibility programs you have (Community Service, Charitable Giving, Fair Labor Practices, Employee Wellness, Diversity and Inclusion, or None).
10. Choose the types of governance practices you have implemented (Code of Ethics, Board Oversight, Risk Management, Stakeholder Engagement, Transparency Reporting, or None).
11. Select the level of employee sustainability training (Comprehensive, Basic, Planned, Minimal, or None).
12. Answer if you have established specific sustainability goals (Yes, clearly defined, Yes, but vague, In development, or Not yet).
13. Identify the priority areas for improvement (enter any relevant information).

# Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Department or Division** (`department`, text, optional): Choose the department or division within your organization that is completing this assessment.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date when this assessment is being conducted.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person completing the assessment.
* **Assessor Email** (`assessor_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email of the person completing the assessment.
* **Environmental Practices Status** (`environmental_practices`, select_one, required): Choose how well your company's environmental practices are implemented (Fully Implemented, Mostly Implemented, Partially Implemented, Minimally Implemented, or Not Implemented).
* **Renewable Energy Usage** (`renewable_energy`, select_one, required): Choose how well you use renewable energy (Yes, fully, Yes, partially, Planning to implement, No plans currently, or Not applicable).
* **Waste Reduction Programs** (`waste_reduction`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of waste reduction programs you have implemented (Recycling Program, Composting, Waste Audit, Supplier Requirements, Employee Training, or None).
* **Water Conservation Measures** (`water_conservation`, select_multiple, required): Select the measures you have implemented for water conservation (Low-flow fixtures, Rainwater harvesting, Water monitoring, Leak prevention, Landscape optimization, or None).
* **Carbon Footprint Assessment** (`carbon_footprint`, select_one, required): Answer whether you have measured your carbon footprint (Yes, annually, Yes, occasionally, In progress, Not yet, or No plans).
* **Social Responsibility Programs** (`social_responsibility`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of social responsibility programs you have implemented (Community Service, Charitable Giving, Fair Labor Practices, Employee Wellness, Diversity and Inclusion, or None).
* **Governance Standards** (`governance_standards`, select_multiple, required): Choose the types of governance standards you have implemented (Code of Ethics, Board Oversight, Risk Management, Stakeholder Engagement, Transparency Reporting, or None).
* **Employee Sustainability Training** (`sustainability_training`, select_one, required): Choose the level of employee sustainability training (Comprehensive, Basic, Planned, Minimal, or None).
* **Established Sustainability Goals** (`sustainability_goals`, select_one, required): Answer whether you have established specific sustainability goals (Yes, clearly defined, Yes, but vague, In development, or Not yet).
* **Priority Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional): Identify the areas that need the most attention.
