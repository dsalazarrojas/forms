# Home Visit Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Visit Safety Checklist form is a tool used to identify and document potential safety hazards during home visits. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a safe and successful home visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct options for each field, taking into account the safety hazards present during the home visit.
3. Ensure that all fields are filled out completely before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Check the area
* Check the area: (`safety_issues`, text, required)
	+ This field is used to identify any potential safety issues or hazards found during the home visit.
	+ Please describe any safety concerns or risks identified during the visit.

### 2. Electrical Hazards
* Electrical Hazards: (`electrical_hazards`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select 'Yes' if any electrical hazards are present during the home visit.
	+ Examples of electrical hazards include exposed wiring, faulty appliances, or electrical panels.

### 3. Tripping Hazards
* Tripping Hazards: (`tripping_hazards`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select 'Yes' if any tripping hazards are present during the home visit.
	+ Examples of tripping hazards include loose rugs, uneven flooring, or obstacles on the floor.

### 4. Fire Hazards
* Fire Hazards: (`fire_hazards`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select 'Yes' if any fire hazards are present during the home visit.
	+ Examples of fire hazards include unattended cooking appliances, smoking materials, or combustible materials near open flames.

### 5. Sharp Objects
* Sharp Objects: (`sharp_objects`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select 'Yes' if any sharp objects are present during the home visit.
	+ Examples of sharp objects include knives, scissors, or broken glass.

### 6. Water Hazards
* Water Hazards: (`water_hazards`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select 'Yes' if any water hazards are present during the home visit.
	+ Examples of water hazards include leaks, slippery surfaces, or standing water.

### 7. Medical Hazards
* Medical Hazards: (`medical_hazards`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select 'Yes' if any medical hazards are present during the home visit.
	+ Examples of medical hazards include medications, medical equipment, or medical conditions that may pose a risk.

### 8. Structural Hazards
* Structural Hazards: (`structural_hazards`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select 'Yes' if any structural hazards are present during the home visit.
	+ Examples of structural hazards include unstable structures, falling objects, or hazardous materials.

### 9. Other
* Other: (`other_hazards`, text, optional)
	+ If other safety hazards are present during the home visit that are not listed above, describe them here.

### 10. Solutions
* Solutions: (`home_visit_safety_solutions`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select 'Yes' if solutions are needed to address the safety hazards identified during the home visit.
	+ Examples of solutions include implementing safety measures, providing guidance, or taking corrective action.

### 11. Actions
* Actions: (`follow_up_actions`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select 'Yes' if follow-up actions are needed to address the safety hazards identified during the home visit.
	+ Examples of follow-up actions include scheduling follow-up visits, providing additional support, or revisiting the area.

### 12. Status
* Status: (`home_visit_safety_status`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select 'Active' if the safety hazards are currently active and pose a risk.
	+ Select 'Inactive' if the safety hazards are currently inactive and do not pose a risk.

### 13. Inspector Name
* Inspector Name: (`safety_inspector_name`, text, required)
	+ Enter the name of the inspector who conducted the home visit.

### 14. Contact
* Contact: (`safety_inspector_contact`, text, required)
	+ Enter the contact information for the inspector who conducted the home visit.

### 15. Date
* Date: (`safety_inspector_date`, date, required)
	+ Enter the date the home visit was conducted.

### 16. Time
* Time: (`safety_inspector_time`, time, required)
	+ Enter the time the home visit was conducted.

### 17. Notes
* Notes: (`safety_notes`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select 'Yes' if any notes are available to provide additional information about the safety hazards.
	+ Examples of notes include observations, recommendations, or follow-up instructions.

### 18. Review Status
* Review Status: (`safety_review_status`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select 'Active' if the review is currently active and ongoing.
	+ Select 'Inactive' if the review is currently inactive and not ongoing.

### 19. Follow-up Review Notes
* Follow-up Review Notes: (`follow_up_review_notes`, text, optional)
	+ If additional information or notes are available to provide during the follow-up review, enter them here.

### 20. Follow-up Review Status
* Follow-up Review Status: (`follow_up_review_status`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select 'Active' if the follow-up review is currently active and ongoing.
	+ Select 'Inactive' if the follow-up review is currently inactive and not ongoing.

### 21. Safety Reviewed
* Safety Reviewed: (`home_visit_safety_reviewed`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select 'Yes' if the home visit has been safety reviewed.
	+ Examples of safety reviewed include completed reviews, ongoing reviews, or pending reviews.

### 22. Reviewed by
* Reviewed by: (`home_visit_safety_reviewed_by`, text, required)
	+ Enter the name of the person who reviewed the safety hazards.

### 23. Reviewed Date
* Reviewed Date: (`home_visit_safety_reviewed_date`, date, required)
	+ Enter the date the safety hazards were reviewed.

### 24. Reviewed Time
* Reviewed Time: (`home_visit_safety_reviewed_time`, time, required)
	+ Enter the time the safety hazards were reviewed.

### 25. Reviewed by Name
* Reviewed by Name: (`home_visit_safety_reviewed_by_name`, text, required)
	+ Enter the name of the person who reviewed the safety hazards.

This help guide is designed to ensure that the user understands the purpose and requirements of the Home Visit Safety Checklist form, and can complete it accurately and efficiently.
