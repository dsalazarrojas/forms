# Toilet Training Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help track the progress and milestones of a child's toilet training process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your child's parent or guardian's name in the "Parent Name" field.
2. Enter the date you started the child's toilet training in the "Date" field.
3. Fill in your child's name in the "Child Name" field.
4. Enter the date the child began using the toilet in the "Start Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name**: This field is where you enter your child's parent or guardian's name. This information is used to keep track of who is responsible for the child's toilet training.
* **Date**: This field is used to record the date you started the child's toilet training. This helps in tracking the progress and timeline of the child's training.
* **Child Name**: This field is for filling in your child's name. This information is used to identify the child and their progress in the toilet training process.
* **Start Date**: This field is where you enter the date the child began using the toilet. This is an important milestone in the child's toilet training journey.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in the form accurately and consistently.
* Use the same format for dates (e.g., DD/MM/YY or MM/DD/YY).
* If you need to make changes or corrections, use the "Edit" feature to update the form.
* Consult a pediatrician or healthcare professional for personalized guidance on toilet training.
