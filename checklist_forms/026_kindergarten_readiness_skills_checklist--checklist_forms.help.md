# Kindergarten Readiness Skills Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a child's readiness for kindergarten by evaluating their communication skills, math concepts, social behavior, emotional readiness, language and literacy, fine motor skills, and domain-specific skills.

## How To Complete This Form

* Answer each question in the best of your ability and according to your knowledge of the child's abilities.
* If a field is marked as required, it is essential to complete it.
* If a field is marked as optional, you may choose to provide information, but it is not mandatory.
* Make sure to complete each section thoroughly, as this will provide the most accurate picture of the child's abilities.
* If you are unsure about any question, please provide the best answer you can based on your knowledge of the child's abilities.
