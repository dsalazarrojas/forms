# Annual Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Inspection Form is used to document the results of regular annual inspections of various facilities, including buildings, vehicles, equipment, and properties. The form is designed to be used for multiple types of facilities and properties, and it includes fields for rating different systems such as electrical, plumbing, HVAC, and foundation. The form also includes fields for tracking corrective actions required after inspection and the deadline for completion of those actions. Additionally, the form includes fields for the inspector's signature, additional comments, and reinspection requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information as the inspector.
2. Select the type of inspection being performed (e.g. building, vehicle, equipment, property).
3. Fill in the inspection date and year.
4. Answer the questions regarding the overall condition of the facility and its different systems (e.g. electrical, plumbing, HVAC).
5. Rate the condition of each system.
6. Enter any issues or notes for each system.
7. Determine if any corrective actions are required.
8. Set a deadline for completion of the required actions.
9. Determine if reinspection is required.
10. Enter the reinspection date if scheduled.
11. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector Full Name**: This is the person conducting the inspection. Please enter your full name as the inspector. (type: text, required)
	+ This is the name of the person performing the inspection.
* **Email Address**: This is your contact email address. (type: email, required)
	+ Please enter your work email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: This is your best contact number. (type: text, optional)
	+ Enter your work or personal phone number for contact purposes.
* **Company Name**: This is the company or department inspecting. (type: text, required)
	+ Enter the name of the company or department performing the inspection.
* **Inspection Date**: This is the date of the inspection. (type: date, required)
	+ Enter the date the inspection was performed.
* **Inspection Year**: This is the year of the inspection. (type: select_one, required)
	+ Choose the correct year for the inspection from the list of options.
* **Inspection Type**: This is the type of inspection being performed. (type: select_one, required)
	+ Choose the type of facility or property being inspected from the list of options.
* **Property Address**: This is the address of the property being inspected. (type: text, required)
	+ Enter the full address of the property being inspected.
* **Property Owner**: This is the owner of the property being inspected. (type: text, optional)
	+ If the property has an owner, enter their name. Otherwise, leave blank.
* **Property Type**: This is the type of property being inspected. (type: select_one, required)
	+ Choose the type of property from the list of options.
* **Building Age**: This is the approximate age of the building. (type: text, optional)
	+ Enter the approximate age of the building in years.
* **Square Footage**: This is the total square footage of the property. (type: number, optional)
	+ Enter the total square footage of the property.
