# Hazard Identification Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this Hazard Identification Checklist is to identify potential hazards in the workplace or environment and document them for review and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and select the applicable options for each field. Make sure to review each page carefully and complete all required fields before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **product_type** (select_one, required: false): Select the type of product or material that is associated with the hazard you are identifying. This will help to narrow down the options for the next fields.
* **hazard_type** (select_multiple, required: false): Choose the types of hazards that are present. You can select multiple options that apply.
* **risk_level** (select_one, required: false): Rate the level of risk associated with the hazard. High or Low.
* **risk_factor** (select_multiple, required: false): Identify the factors that contribute to the risk level. Select all that apply.
* **hazard_description** (text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the hazard, including its nature and any relevant details.
* **risk_description** (text, required: false): Describe the potential risk associated with the hazard, including any consequences.
* **risk_likelihood** (select_one, required: false): Determine whether the risk is likely to occur. Yes or No.
* **risk_impact** (select_one, required: false): Determine whether the risk has a significant impact. Yes or No.
* **corrective_action** (text, required: false): Describe any corrective actions taken to mitigate or eliminate the hazard.
* **preventive_measures** (text, required: false): Describe any preventive measures taken to prevent the hazard from occurring in the future.
* **review_date** (date, required: false): Record the date when this hazard was reviewed for follow-up.
* **review_by** (select_one, required: false): Identify who reviewed this hazard. Yes or No.
* **assigned_to** (select_one, required: false): Determine whether this hazard has been assigned to someone for follow-up. Yes or No.

## Tips
When completing this form, please make sure to:

* Clearly describe each hazard and its associated risk.
* Rate the risk level and factors contributing to it.
* Provide relevant details for follow-up and review.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.
