# Property Onboarding Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Onboarding Analysis Form is a tool used to collect and analyze information about a property. This form is used to record details about a property, including its onboarding status, inspection results, and recommended repairs. The information collected will help us better understand the property's condition and assist in the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you are completing it for the correct property.
2. Enter all required information in the fields below, starting with **onboarding_info**.
3. Provide detailed information in the **property_details** field about the property's current state.
4. In the **onboarding_notes** field, provide any relevant comments or observations about the property.
5. Enter the results of the inspection in the **property_inspection** field.
6. Enter the results of the inspection in the **inspection_results** field.
7. In the **property_condition** field, describe the current state of the property.
8. If recommended repairs or maintenance are needed, enter them in the **recommended_repairs** field.
9. Enter any inspection results or findings in the **property_inspection_results** field.
10. Select the current onboarding status of the property from the dropdown menu in **onboarding_status**.
11. Enter any additional comments about the property in the **comments** field.
12. Enter the date of onboarding in the **onboarding_date** field.
13. Enter the time of onboarding in the **onboarding_time** field.
14. Enter the name of the inspector in the **onboarding_inspector** field.
15. Enter the owner's name in the **property_owner_name** field.
16. Enter the owner's phone number in the **property_owner_phone** field.
17. Enter the owner's email address in the **property_owner_email** field.
18. Enter the address of the property in the **property_address** field.
19. Enter the city of the property in the **property_city** field.
20. Enter the state of the property in the **property_state** field.
21. Enter the zip code of the property in the **property_zip** field.
22. Enter the country of the property in the **property_country** field.
23. Enter the state or province of the property in the **property_state_province** field.
24. Enter the postal code of the property in the **property_postal_code** field.
25. Enter the street address of the property in the **property_street** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **onboarding_info** (text, required): Enter a brief description of the property's onboarding status.
* **property_details** (text, required): Enter detailed information about the property's current state.
* **onboarding_notes** (note, required): Provide any relevant comments or observations about the property.
* **property_inspection** (text, required): Enter the results of the inspection.
* **inspection_results** (text, required): Enter any additional inspection results or findings.
* **property_condition** (text, required): Describe the current state of the property.
* **recommended_repairs** (text, required): Enter any recommended repairs or maintenance needed.
* **property_inspection_results** (text, required): Enter any additional inspection results or findings.
* **onboarding_status** (select multiple, required): Select the current onboarding status of the property from the dropdown menu.
* **comments** (text, required): Enter any additional comments about the property.
* **onboarding_date** (date, required): Enter the date of onboarding.
* **onboarding_time** (time, required): Enter the time of onboarding.
* **onboarding_inspector** (text, required): Enter the name of the inspector.
* **property_owner_name** (text, required): Enter the owner's name.
* **property_owner_phone** (text, required): Enter the owner's phone number.
* **property_owner_email** (email, required): Enter the owner's email address.
* **property_address** (text, required): Enter the address of the property.
* **property_city** (text, required): Enter the city of the property.
* **property_state** (text, required): Enter the state of the property.
* **property_zip** (text, required): Enter the zip code of the property.
* **property_country** (text, required): Enter the country of the property.
* **property_state_province** (text, required): Enter the state or province of the property.
* **property_postal_code** (text, required): Enter the postal code of the property.
* **property_street** (text, required): Enter the street address of the property.
