# Veterinary Inventory Audit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Inventory Audit Checklist is a form designed to help veterinary staff conduct a thorough inventory audit of the clinic's stock and equipment. The goal of this form is to gather information about the current state of the inventory and identify any potential issues that require attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and select the corresponding option for each question.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes where necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stock Check** (`stock_check`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Check the current stock levels in the clinic. Select "Yes" if the stock levels are sufficient, or "No" if they are not.
* **Equipment Check** (`equipment_check`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Review the current state of equipment in the clinic. Select "Yes" if the equipment is in good working order, or "No" if it is not.
* **Safety Observations** (`safety_observations`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Identify any safety-related observations or concerns in the clinic. Select "Yes" if there are safety-related issues, or "No" if there are none.
* **Clinic Stock** (`clinic_stock`, Number, Required: False): Enter the total value of the stock in the clinic.
* **Stock Value** (`stock_value`, Number, Required: False): Enter the value of the specific stock item you are checking.
* **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`, Select One, Required: False): Determine the status of the equipment in the clinic. Select from the options:
	+ Available: The equipment is available and in working order.
	+ Maintenance: The equipment is in maintenance mode.
	+ Out of Order: The equipment is not working properly.
	+ Out of Service: The equipment is not available for use due to maintenance or repair.
	+ Pending: The equipment's status is pending maintenance or repair.
	+ Replaced: The equipment has been replaced.
	+ Replaced with: The equipment has been replaced with a new one.
	+ Spare: The equipment is a spare or backup piece.
	+ Unknown: The equipment's status is unknown.
* **Equipment Last Checked** (`equipment_last_checked`, Date, Required: False): Enter the date the equipment was last inspected.
* **Equipment Next Check Due** (`equipment_next_check_due`, Date, Required: False): Enter the date the equipment is due for its next inspection.
* **Safety Notes** (`safety_notes`, Note, Required: False): Provide any safety-related notes or comments.
* **Safety Risk** (`safety_risk`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Identify any safety-related risks in the clinic. Select "Yes" if there are safety-related risks, or "No" if there are none.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when completing the form.
* Use the options available to provide clear and concise answers.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
* This form is a tool to help identify potential issues and areas for improvement. Use the information gathered to make informed decisions about inventory management and equipment upkeep.
