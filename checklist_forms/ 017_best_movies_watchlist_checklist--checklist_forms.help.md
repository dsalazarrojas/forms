# Best Movies Watchlist Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Movies Watchlist Checklist is designed to collect information from users about their favorite movies and preferences. This form helps users share their thoughts on movies and provides additional context for users who want to discuss or analyze these preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**, which is used to identify you as a user.
2. Enter a valid **Email Address** for contact purposes.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** (optional) if you are outside the local region.
4. Select a relevant **Date** for the purpose of your movie preference.
5. Choose your **Primary Selection** from the available options.
6. Enter a **Quantity or Rating** for the movie (optional).
7. Provide any **Additional Details** about the movie.
8. Select the relevant **Agreement** options that apply.
9. Enter the **Estimated Value** of the movie (optional).
10. Enter your **Preferred Time** for scheduling (optional).
11. Leave any **Final Comments or Notes** if you need to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Please enter your legal full name.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: This is an optional field for users outside the local region. Please include your country code if necessary.
* **Date**: Select a relevant date for the purpose of your movie preference.
* **Primary Selection**: Choose the option that best applies to your movie preference.
* **Quantity or Rating**: This field is optional. If you want to provide a quantity, enter a whole number. If you want to provide a rating, enter a number between 1 and 5.
* **Additional Details**: Provide any additional relevant information about the movie.
* **Agreement**: Select any options that apply to your movie preference.
* **Estimated Value**: This field is optional. If you provide a value, use decimal format.
* **Preferred Time**: Enter a time for scheduling if required.
* **Final Comments or Notes**: This is an optional field for any closing remarks.
