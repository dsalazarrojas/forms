# hvac_defrost_termination_fan_delay_wiring_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you verify or troubleshoot fan wiring configurations in HVAC systems for defrost termination. It's a checklist that guides you through identifying and documenting the fan wiring type, color, and termination type, as well as the defrost termination location and any additional notes or photos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review the questions on each page and select the relevant information for your specific fan wiring configuration.
2. Answer each question accurately and honestly.
3. Use the provided options for fan wiring type, color, defrost termination type, defrost termination location, and any additional notes.
4. Upload any photos of the fan wiring as necessary.
5. Review your answers for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **hvac_defrost_termination_fan_delay_wiring_checklist (Page 1)**: Enter a brief description of the fan wiring configuration you're checking.
* **fan_wiring_type (Page 2)**: Select the type of wiring used for the fan, with options 1, 2, or 3.
* **fan_wiring_color (Page 2)**: Select the color of the fan wiring, with options 1, 2, or 3.
* **defrost_termination_wiring_type (Page 3)**: Select the type of defrost termination wiring, with options 1, 2, or 3.
* **defrost_termination_wiring_color (Page 3)**: Select the color of the defrost termination wiring, with options 1, 2, or 3.
* **defrost_termination_location (Page 4)**: Select the location of the defrost termination, with options 1, 2, or 3.
* **notes (Page 5)**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the fan wiring configuration.
* **photos (Page 6)**: Upload any photos of the fan wiring for further clarification or reference.
