# sports_academy_admission_checklist_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is designed to assist sports academies in the process of gathering necessary information from students and their parents before admission. It helps to ensure that all relevant details are collected in a structured and organized manner, reducing the likelihood of missing important information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling in the introduction page, if you wish to provide context or a brief welcome message.
2. On the next page, enter the relevant details about the sports academy, such as its name, location, contact information, and any additional information you deem necessary to share with applicants.
3. On the following page, provide your personal information as a student, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant details that you feel is necessary to share.
4. If you are a parent, please enter your personal information and that of your child on the next page.
5. On the following page, provide emergency contact information for the student, including name, contact information, and relationship to the student.
6. The next page is for medical information, such as any allergies or medical conditions that may impact the student's participation in sports activities.
7. The following page is for student details, including name, date of birth, contact information, and any other relevant details that you feel is necessary to share.
8. On this page, please describe any requirements that must be met for admission to the sports academy, such as prerequisites or eligibility criteria.
9. On the last page, confirm your payment information, including payment method and amount.
10. Finally, read and agree to the terms and conditions of the sports academy, which outlines the rules and expectations for participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1, text): A brief welcome message or context about the sports academy.
* **Sports Academy Info** (Page 2, text): Information about the sports academy, including its name, location, contact details, and any other relevant information.
* **Personal Info** (Page 3, text, required): Student's name, contact details, and any other relevant personal information.
* **Parent Info** (Page 4, text, optional): Parent's name, contact information, and any other relevant personal information about themselves and their child.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (Page 5, text): Emergency contact information for the student, including name, contact information, and relationship to the student.
* **Medical Info** (Page 6, text): Medical information about the student, including any allergies or medical conditions that may impact their participation in sports activities.
* **Student Details** (Page 7, text, required): Student's name, date of birth, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Admission Requirements** (Page 8, text, optional): Requirements that must be met for admission to the sports academy, such as prerequisites or eligibility criteria.
* **Payment Info** (Page 9, text): Information about payment method and amount for the sports academy's programs or services.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Page 10, text): A statement outlining the rules and expectations for participation in the sports academy, which includes liability waiver, consent, and other relevant agreements.

Note that this form template has no affiliation or endorsement from other form platforms, but has been designed to provide a useful tool for sports academies to collect necessary information from applicants in a structured and organized manner.
