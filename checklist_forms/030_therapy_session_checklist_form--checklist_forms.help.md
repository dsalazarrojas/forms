# Therapy Session Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document and track the progress of therapy sessions, enabling effective organization and follow-up on the client-therapist relationship.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete the therapy session checklist form, simply fill out the relevant fields for each section. This form is organized into several fields:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Therapy Session Checklist Form** (1):
	+ A space to note the overall progress of the session.
* **Session Goal** (2):
	+ A field to outline the primary objective or focus of the current session.
* **Session Progress** (3):
	+ A field to record any notable advancements or challenges during the session.
* **Next Steps** (4):
	+ A field to note the planned next steps or follow-up actions for the client.
* **Notes** (5):
	+ A text area for any additional comments or observations from the client.
* **Therapist Notes** (6):
	+ A text area for the therapist to record their own notes and insights.
* **Client Signature** (7):
	+ A field for the client to sign and confirm the accuracy of the session notes.
* **Therapist Signature** (8):
	+ A field for the therapist to sign and confirm the accuracy of the session notes.
* **Session Date** (9):
	+ A field to record the date of the therapy session.
* **Session Time** (10):
	+ A field to record the time of the therapy session.

### Tips
- Please ensure you complete the form accurately and thoroughly to maintain a clear and organized record of your therapy sessions.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your therapist or support team.
- This form is designed to be a collaborative tool for both you and your therapist, so feel free to ask questions or discuss any concerns you may have during the session.
