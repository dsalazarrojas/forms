# Corporate Event Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Event Checklist Form is designed to collect information about corporate events. This form helps to gather details, dates, location, and agenda of events, ensuring that all necessary information is captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the details of the event in the "Details" field.
2. Enter the dates of the event in the "Dates" field. Use the date format "Month/Day/Year" (e.g., February 10, 2024).
3. Type the location of the event in the "Location" field.
4. Enter the agenda of the event in the "Agenda" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Details** (`corporate_event_checklist_form_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the event, including its name, purpose, or a short summary.
* **Dates** (`corporate_event_checklist_form_dates`, date, optional): Enter the start and end dates of the event in the format "Month/Day/Year" (e.g., February 10, 2024 - February 12, 2024).
* **Location** (`corporate_event_checklist_form_location`, text, optional): Enter the physical address or venue name where the event will take place.
* **Agenda** (`corporate_event_checklist_form_agenda`, text, optional): Enter a detailed list of activities, speakers, or topics that will be covered during the event.
