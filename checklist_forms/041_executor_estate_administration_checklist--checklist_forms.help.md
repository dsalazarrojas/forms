# Executor Estate Administration Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the Executor Estate Administration Checklist form, explaining each field and how to complete it. The form is used to provide an overview of the estate administration process and should only be completed when the process is ongoing.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select one of the two options for "Estate Administration Checklist": "Yes" or "No".
2.  Enter the name of the executor in the "Executor Name" field.
3.  Enter the address of the executor in the "Executor Address" field.
4.  Enter the value of the estate in the "Estate Value" field.
5.  Select one of the three options for "Executor Inheritance": Inheritance 1, Inheritance 2, or Inheritance 3.
6.  Select "Yes" or "No" for "Beneficiaries".
7.  Select "Yes" or "No" for "Estate Administrator".
8.  Enter the contact information of the estate administrator in the "Estate Administrator Contact" and "Estate Administrator Phone" fields.
9.  Select "Yes" or "No" for "Legal Professional".
10. Enter the contact information of the legal professional in the "Legal Professional Contact" and "Legal Professional Phone" fields.
11.  Select one of the two options for "Executor Status": Active or Inactive.
12.  Select one of the two options for "Estate Administration Progress": "Yes" or "No".
13.  Enter the domicile of the executor in the "Executor Domicile" field.
14.  Select one of the two options for "Estate Administration Progress Status": Active or Inactive.
15.  Enter the date of the estate administration progress in the "Estate Administration Progress Date" field.
16.  Select one of the two options for "Estate Administration Progress Description": Active or Inactive.
17.  Enter the fees of the estate administrator in the "Legal Fees" field.
18.  Enter any comments about the estate administration progress in the "Estate Administration Progress Comments" field.
19.  Enter any notes about the executor in the "Executor Notes" field.
20.  Enter any legal expenses in the "Legal Expenses" field.
21.  Enter any comments about the estate administration progress in the "Estate Administration Progress Comments" field.
22.  Enter any fees of the executor in the "Executor Fees" field.
