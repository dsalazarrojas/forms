# Equipment Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Checklist Form is used by emergency responders to report on the status of various pieces of equipment. This form helps to track equipment condition, location, and status, which is crucial for inventory management and maintenance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the relevant equipment options from the list provided.
2. Indicate whether the equipment is in good, bad, or unknown condition.
3. Choose the location where the equipment is currently situated.
4. Specify the hours you are on duty.
5. Select the emergency contacts that need to be notified in case of an emergency.
6. Enter the phone numbers for fireman and law enforcement officers.
7. Indicate whether the equipment is assigned to someone or not.
8. Specify whether the equipment is assigned to an individual or a group.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **fireman_equipment_list**: Select all fireman equipment you are reporting on, such as Fireman Helmet, Fireman Suit, or Fireman Gloves. 
* **law_enforcement_equipment**: Choose one piece of law enforcement equipment you are reporting on, such as Police Helmet, Police Vest, or Police Radio.
* **other_equipment**: If there are other relevant equipment not listed under fireman or law enforcement, enter them here.
* **duty_hours**: Select the hours you are on duty, Morning, Afternoon, or Night.
* **duty_location**: Choose all locations where you are on duty, such as Station, City, or Town.
* **emergency_contact**: Select all emergency contacts that need to be notified, such as Emergency 1, Emergency 2, or Emergency 3.
* **fireman_phone**: Enter the phone number of the fireman officer in charge.
* **law_enforcement_phone**: Enter the phone number of the law enforcement officer in charge.
* **equipment_status**: Indicate the status of the equipment you are reporting on, Good, Bad, or Unknown.
* **equipment_location**: If the equipment is not currently at the station, choose all locations where it is situated, such as Station, City, or Town.
* **equipment_description**: Indicate the condition of the equipment, Good, Bad, or Unknown.
* **remarks**: If you have any additional comments or notes about the equipment, enter them here.
* **assigned**: Indicate whether the equipment is assigned to someone or not, Yes or No.
* **assigned_to**: If the equipment is assigned, specify whether it is assigned to an individual or a group.

Note: This form is designed to be completed in a quick and efficient manner, so please take a few minutes to read through the form carefully before starting, and don't hesitate to ask for clarification if needed.
