# Monthly Task Checklist For Children - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help parents or guardians create a monthly task checklist for children, allowing them to track and document tasks completed, effort level, and receive feedback from guardians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the task list with the tasks completed by the child.
2. Select the effort level of the task (1, 2, or 3).
3. Choose any challenges the child faced during the task (select multiple if applicable).
4. Provide feedback from the guardian (optional).
5. Add a signature from the guardian (optional).
6. Enter the date and time of completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Task List** (`task_list`, number, optional): List the tasks completed by the child, one per line.
- **Effort Level** (`effort_level`, select_one, optional): Rate the difficulty level of the task, with 1 being easier and 3 being more challenging.
- **Challenge List** (`challenge_list`, select_multiple, optional): Select any challenges the child faced during the task, with 1, 2, or 3 being available options.
- **Guardian Feedback** (`guardian_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback or comments from the guardian.
- **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Add a signature from the guardian to confirm completion.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of completion.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of completion.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
- If a field is not required, you can skip it if not applicable.
- Use the effort level and challenge list to track the child's progress and identify areas where they may need extra support.
- Review the guardian feedback regularly to understand their perspective and provide guidance as needed.
- Keep the signature and date fields up to date to maintain a record of completion.
