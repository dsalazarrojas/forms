# Energy Audit Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Energy Audit Checklist is a form designed to help you assess and document the energy-related needs and concerns of a specific site. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to gather information on site name, building status, equipment ID, facility type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the accurate and relevant information.
2. Select the correct options from the provided lists.
3. Provide detailed comments for any safety concerns or action items.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Name**: Enter the name of the site you are conducting the energy audit on. (Text input field, required)
* **Building Status**: Select the current status of the building, which could be "Option A" or "Option B". (Select one option, required)
* **Equipment ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the equipment at the site. (Number input field, required)
* **Facility Type**: Select the type of facility, which could be "Option A" or "Option B". (Select one option, required)
* **Comments - General**: Provide any general comments or observations related to the site. (Text input field, optional)
* **Facility Safety Concerns**: Select any safety concerns related to the facility, which could be multiple options. (Select multiple options, optional)
* **Comments - Safety-Related**: Provide any safety-related comments or observations. (Text input field, optional)
* **Comments - Site-Specific**: Provide any comments or observations specific to the site. (Text input field, optional)
* **Comments - Energy-Related**: Provide any comments or observations related to energy efficiency or usage at the site. (Text input field, optional)
* **Comments - Action Items**: Provide any action items or recommendations for future improvement or maintenance. (Text input field, optional)

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
- Select only relevant options for Facility Safety Concerns.
- Use detailed comments to provide context and clarity for any concerns or action items.
