# Section 8 Housing Compliance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Section 8 Housing Compliance Checklist is used to collect information about rental units for Section 8 housing programs. This form helps to ensure that rental units meet all the necessary requirements and regulations for compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the rental unit address in the **Rental Unit Address** field.
2. Select the location of the rental unit in the **Rental Unit Location** field.
3. Indicate if the property is a **Rental Property Type** (Yes/No).
4. Enter the **Number of Units** in the property.
5. Enter the **Total Number of Units** in the property.
6. Check if there are any **Inspection Results** (Yes/No).
7. Enter the **Date and Time of Inspection**.
8. Enter the **Date of Last Renewal**.
9. Indicate the **Last Renewal Status** (Active/Inactive).
10. Select the **Section 8 Compliance Status** (Active/Inactive).
11. Enter any **Notes** about the rental unit.
12. Enter the **Email** contact information.
13. Enter the **Phone Number** of the contact person.
14. Enter the **Contact Name**.
15. Enter the **Contact Number**.
16. Enter the **Contact Email**.
17. Enter the **Contact Address**.
18. Enter the **Contact City**.
19. Enter the **Contact State**.
20. Enter the **Contact Zip**.
21. Enter any **Contact Comments**.
22. Enter the **Contact Relationship** with the owner/agent.
23. Enter the **Contact Title**.
24. Enter the **Contact Company**.
25. Enter the **Contact Position**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rental Unit Address**: Enter the physical address of the rental unit.
* **Rental Unit Location**: Enter the location of the rental unit (e.g., city, state, or zip code).
* **Property Type**: Indicate if the property is a rental property (Yes/No).
* **Number of Units**: Enter the total number of units in the property.
* **Total Number of Units**: Enter the total number of units in the property.
* **Inspection Results**: Check if there are any inspection results (Yes/No).
* **Date and Time of Inspection**: Enter the date and time of the inspection.
* **Date of Last Renewal**: Enter the date of the last renewal.
* **Last Renewal Status**: Indicate the status of the last renewal (Active/Inactive).
* **Section 8 Compliance Status**: Select the compliance status of the rental unit (Active/Inactive).
* **Notes**: Enter any notes about the rental unit.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Address**: Enter the address of the contact person.
* **Contact City**: Enter the city of the contact person.
* **Contact State**: Enter the state of the contact person.
* **Contact Zip**: Enter the zip code of the contact person.
* **Contact Comments**: Enter any comments about the contact person.
* **Contact Relationship**: Enter the relationship of the contact person with the owner/agent.
* **Contact Title**: Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Contact Company**: Enter the company of the contact person.
* **Contact Position**: Enter the position of the contact person.
