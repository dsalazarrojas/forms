# industrial_maintenance_and_troubleshooting_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for tracking and reporting industrial maintenance and troubleshooting activities to ensure that equipment is properly maintained and issues are quickly identified and resolved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **General Info** section with any relevant details about the equipment or issue at hand.
2. Enter the **Equipment ID** of the machinery being maintained or troubleshot.
3. Select the current **Equipment Status** from the available options.
4. Enter the date of the **Inspection Date** in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. In the **Maintenance Work Completed** section, select all the maintenance work that has been done.
6. In the **Notes** section, add any additional comments or observations about the maintenance activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the equipment or issue at hand.
* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`, text, required): Enter the unique ID of the machinery being maintained or troubleshot.
* **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the equipment from the available options (e.g. Operational, Non-Operational, Inactive).
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the inspection in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Maintenance Work Completed** (`maintenance_work_completed`, select_multiple, required): Select all the maintenance work that has been done.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the maintenance activity.

## Tips
* Make sure to be specific and accurate when filling out the **Equipment ID** field.
* Select the correct **Equipment Status** that reflects the current condition of the machinery.
* Be thorough in documenting the **Maintenance Work Completed** to ensure all necessary work is accounted for.
* Use the **Notes** field to provide additional context or details about the maintenance activity.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure accurate and up-to-date information is captured.
