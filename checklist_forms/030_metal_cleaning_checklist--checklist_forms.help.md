# Metal Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Metal Cleaning Checklist is a standardized form for documenting metal cleaning work. This form ensures that all relevant information about the metal surface conditions and other important details are captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the metal surface conditions that apply to the metal being cleaned. For most fields, select one or multiple options from the provided list.
2. Enter the date and time of service, ensuring that these fields are filled out.
3. Provide any additional comments about the service performed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Metal Cleaning Work** (`metal_cleaning_work`, `text`, required)
This field is where you should describe the metal cleaning work that was performed. Please provide a brief description of the work.

### 2. **Metal Surface Conditions** (`metal_surface_condition`, `select_one`, required)
This field requires you to select one option that describes the condition of the metal surface being cleaned. Select the option that best represents the condition of the surface.

### 3-8. **Metal Surface Condition 2-8** (`metal_surface_condition_2`, `select_multiple`, required/optional)
These fields are for selecting additional conditions of the metal surface. If applicable, select one or multiple options to provide more details about the condition of the surface.

### 9. **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, `date`, required)
Enter the date when the metal cleaning work was performed.

### 10. **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, `time`, required)
Enter the time when the metal cleaning work was performed.

### 11. **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required)
Enter any additional comments about the service performed.
