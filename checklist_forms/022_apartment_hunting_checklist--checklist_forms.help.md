# Apartment Hunting Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you evaluate and compare different apartments based on various features and characteristics. By filling out this form, you will be able to collect detailed information about a property, including its address, price, and features such as number of bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage, and amenities. This will help you make informed decisions when searching for an apartment that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the property's address in the "Property address" field.
2. Fill in the "Listed rental price" field with the monthly rent of the property.
3. Enter the date you viewed the property in the "Date of viewing" field.
4. If applicable, enter the name of the real estate agent who showed you the property.
5. Enter the number of bedrooms and bathrooms in the corresponding fields.
6. Rate the overall condition of the apartment, kitchen, bathroom, and flooring in the respective fields.
7. Indicate the type of flooring in the "Flooring type" field.
8. Select the appliances that are included with the property.
9. Indicate which utilities are included in the rent.
10. Choose the parking availability for the property.
11. Indicate the pet policy.
12. Assess the noise level of the property.
13. Evaluate the neighborhood safety.
14. Enter the length of the lease term (if applicable).
15. Enter the total upfront costs of moving in (if applicable).
16. Rate the property overall, from 1 to 10.
17. Provide any pros and cons about the property.
18. Finally, decide if you would apply for this apartment by choosing "Yes, definitely", "Maybe", or "False".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property address**: Enter the exact address of the apartment you are reviewing. This will help us get a clear picture of the location.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Listed rental price**: Fill in the monthly rent of the property. This will help you evaluate the affordability of the apartment.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: Yes
* **Date of viewing**: Enter the date you viewed the property. This will help us know when you last visited the property.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: Yes
* **Real estate agent name**: If you had a real estate agent show you the property, enter their name here. This will help us know who to contact for more information.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Number of bedrooms**: Enter the number of bedrooms in the apartment.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: Yes
* **Number of bathrooms**: Enter the number of bathrooms in the apartment.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: Yes
* **Square footage**: Enter the total square footage of the apartment.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: No
* **Overall condition of apartment**: Rate the overall condition of the apartment, from Excellent to Poor.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Kitchen condition and appliances**: Rate the condition and quality of the kitchen, from Excellent to Poor.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Bathroom condition and fixtures**: Rate the condition and quality of the bathroom, from Excellent to Poor.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Flooring type**: Enter the type of flooring in the apartment, e.g., wood, carpet, tile, etc.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Flooring condition**: Rate the condition of the flooring, from Excellent to Poor.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Walls and paint condition**: Rate the condition of the walls and paint, from Excellent to Poor.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Natural lighting quality**: Rate the natural lighting quality, from Excellent to Poor.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **HVAC system condition**: Rate the condition of the HVAC system, from Excellent to Poor.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Storage and closet space adequacy**: Rate the adequacy of storage and closet space, from Excellent to Poor.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Appliances included**: Select all the appliances that are included with the property, e.g., refrigerator, stove, dishwasher, etc.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: Yes
* **Utilities included**: Select all the utilities that are included in the rent, e.g., water, electricity, gas, internet, etc.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: Yes
* **Parking availability**: Choose the type of parking availability, e.g., dedicated parking, street parking, parking garage, etc.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Pet policy**: Choose the pet policy, e.g., pets allowed, cats only, dogs only, no pets allowed, etc.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Noise level assessment**: Rate the noise level of the property, from Quiet to Very noisy.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Neighborhood safety**: Rate the neighborhood safety, from Very safe to Unsafely
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Lease term length**: Enter the length of the lease term, if applicable.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: No
* **Move-in costs**: Enter the total upfront costs of moving in, if applicable.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: No
* **Overall rating**: Rate the overall property, from 1 to 10.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: Yes
* **Pros**: Enter any pros or things you liked about the property.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Cons**: Enter any cons or things you disliked about the property.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Would you apply?**: Decide if you would apply for this apartment by choosing "Yes, definitely", "Maybe", or "False".
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
