# Water Leak Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and track water leak inspections for a building.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Building Location field with the address of the building being inspected.
2. Choose the correct Building Type from the dropdown menu.
3. Describe the Plumbing System in the text field.
4. Enter the Inspector's Name and any additional information.
5. Enter the Date and Time of the inspection.
6. Choose the Water Leak Status by selecting one or more options from the dropdown menu.
7. Provide any information about Potential Leaks.
8. Enter the Repair Cost, if applicable.
9. Provide an Action Plan for addressing the leak.
10. Enter the Inspector's Notes, if any.
11. Add any additional Contact Information.
12. Enter the Email Address of the person responsible for following up on the issue.
13. Enter the Assigned Tool, if any.
14. Enter the Date and Time when assigned.
15. Enter the Assigned Date and Time of the task.
16. Enter the Date when the form was submitted.
17. Enter the Date and Time when the form was completed.
18. Enter any Comments.
19. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
20. Enter the Time when the form was submitted.
21. The Water Leak Inspection Checklist Form field is a note field for any additional information.
22. Enter the name of the user assigned to the task.
23. Enter the version of the Water Leak Inspection Checklist Form.
24. Enter any User Comments.
25. Enter the Submitted Status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Building Location**: Enter the address of the building being inspected.
* **Building Type**: Choose the correct type of building from the dropdown menu.
* **Plumbing System**: Describe the plumbing system in the building.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the person performing the inspection.
* **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the inspection.
* **Water Leak Status**: Choose the status of the water leak (Active or Inactive).
* **Potential Leaks**: Provide any information about potential leaks.
* **Repair Cost**: Enter the estimated cost of repair, if applicable.
* **Action Plan**: Describe the steps to be taken to address the leak.
* **Inspector Notes**: Enter any additional information from the inspector.
* **Contact Info**: Enter any additional contact information for the person responsible for following up on the issue.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for following up on the issue.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter any tools assigned to the task.
* **Assigned Date**: Enter the date when the task was assigned.
* **Assigned Time**: Enter the time when the task was assigned.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Completed Date**: Enter the date and time when the form was completed.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the inspection.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Time**: Enter the time when the form was submitted.
* **Water Leak Inspection Checklist Form**: Enter any additional information in this note field.
* **Assigned User**: Enter the name of the user assigned to the task.
* **Water Leak Inspection Checklist Version**: Enter the version of the checklist form.
* **User Comments**: Enter any comments from the user.
* **Submitted Status**: Enter the status of the form submission.
