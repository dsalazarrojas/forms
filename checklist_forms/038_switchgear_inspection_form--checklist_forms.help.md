# switchgear_inspection_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The `switchgear_inspection_form` is used to collect information for the inspection of switchgear equipment. This form helps maintenance personnel or technicians gather relevant details about the equipment's condition and any issues that require attention.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before starting the inspection.
2. Fill out the form according to the required fields and provide any additional information as necessary.
3. Make sure to select the correct switchgear item from the options provided.
4. Enter the inspection date and time accurately.
5. Provide a thorough inspection report and any comments or notes as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **switchgear_inspection** (1, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the inspection, including any notable findings or observations.
* **switchgear_item** (2, select_one, optional): Select the switchgear item being inspected from the list of available options.
* **switch_report** (3, text, optional): Provide any relevant switchgear inspection report or documentation.
* **switchgear_inspection_result** (4, text, optional): Enter the inspection result, indicating any issues or concerns found during the inspection.
* **switchgear_comments** (5, note, optional): Add any comments or additional information about the inspection.
* **switchgear_inspection_notes** (6, note, optional): Enter any notes or remarks about the inspection process or findings.
* **switchgear_inspection_date** (7, date, optional): Enter the inspection date in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YY).
* **switchgear_inspection_time** (8, time, optional): Enter the inspection time in the correct format (e.g., HH:MM AM/PM).
* **switchgear_inspection_user** (9, text, optional): Enter the name of the person performing the inspection.
