# Product Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate product evaluation and feedback collection from various stakeholders. It helps gather information about products, including ratings, pros and cons, and other relevant details. The form is intended to be completed by different teams within the company, such as product managers, QA teams, and buyers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the product information provided.
2. Rate the product using the rating field.
3. Provide your opinion on the product's quality.
4. List the pros and cons of the product in the pros and cons field.
5. If you have any feedback or comments, enter them in the feedback field.
6. Select the product manager, QA team, and buyer responsible for the product.
7. Decide if you want to submit or not submit your evaluation.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter any additional phone number or contact information.
10. Choose the date and time of your rating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Product Information
* **Product Information** (`product_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the product being evaluated.

### Rate this product
* **Rate this product** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate the product on a scale of 1-10.

### Quality
* **Quality** (`quality`, `number`, required: false): Assess the product's quality on a scale of 1-10.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros and Cons** (`pros_cons`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all pros and cons that apply to the product.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the product.

### Product Manager
* **Product Manager** (`product_manager`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the product manager responsible for the product.

### QA Team
* **QA Team** (`qa_team`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the QA team responsible for the product.

### Buyer
* **Buyer** (`buyer`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the buyer responsible for the product.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required: false): Decide if you want to submit your evaluation or not.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional phone number or contact information.

### Rating Date
* **Rating Date** (`rating_date`, `date`, required: false): Choose the date of your rating.

### Rating Time
* **Rating Time** (`rating_time`, `time`, required: false): Choose the time of your rating.

### Note
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the product.

### Buyer
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): This field is likely a duplicate of the previous note field and can be ignored.
