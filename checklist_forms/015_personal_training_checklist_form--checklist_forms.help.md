# Personal Training Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help individuals or organizations keep track of a person's personal training status. It gathers information on general information, training goals, health status, medical conditions, and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, please fill out the "General Information" field with your name, date of birth, and any other relevant identifying information.
2.  Next, describe your training goals in the "Training Goals" field. This is a good opportunity to outline your objectives, goals, and any specific training you have received or plan to receive.
3.  In the "Health Status" field, describe your current health status, including any medical conditions or health issues you are currently dealing with.
4.  In the "Medical Conditions" field, list any medical conditions or concerns you have.
5.  The "Emergency Contacts" section is where you should list up to 3 emergency contacts that need to be reached in case of an emergency. These can be family members, friends, or other contacts you want to be notified in case of an emergency. Enter their names, phone numbers, and relationships to the person filling out this form.
