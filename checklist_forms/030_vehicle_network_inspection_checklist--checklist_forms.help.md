# vehicle_network_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Network Inspection Checklist is a form designed to facilitate the inspection of vehicles in a network setting. It helps to ensure that all essential systems and components are checked and documented during the inspection process. This form is a comprehensive tool for network inspectors to gather and document critical information about the vehicle's condition, systems, and components.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by selecting the **Connected Systems Checklist** field and choosing the systems that are present on the vehicle.
2. In the **Checklist Items** field, select the items that need to be checked or inspected.
3. Provide a detailed description of each selected **Checklist Item** in the **Checklist Item Description** field.
4. Choose the status of each **Checklist Item** from the options provided.
5. Upload relevant files, such as images or documents, in the **File Upload 1** to **File Upload 6** fields.
6. Add any additional comments or notes in the **Vehicle Network Inspection Checklist Comments** field.
7. Sign the form by filling out the **Vehicle Inspection Checklist Signature** fields, including your name, title, and signature date, time, and location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Network Checklist**: This field is a note field where you can provide any general information about the vehicle network.
* **Vehicle Details**: Enter a brief description of the vehicle, including its make, model, year, and any other relevant details.
* **Connected Systems Checklist**: Choose all the systems that are present on the vehicle.
* **Connected Systems Description**: Provide a description of each connected system.
* **Checklist Items**: Select all the items that need to be checked or inspected.
* **Checklist Item Description**: Provide a detailed description of each selected **Checklist Item**.
* **Checklist Item Status**: Choose the status of each **Checklist Item** from the options provided.
* **File Upload 1-6**: Upload relevant files, such as images or documents, that support the inspection findings.
* **Vehicle Network Inspection Checklist Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Vehicle Inspection Checklist Signature**: Your signature as the inspector, including your name, title, signature date, time, and location.
* **Vehicle Inspection Checklist Signature Date**: The date when the signature is made.
* **Vehicle Inspection Checklist Signature Time**: The time when the signature is made.
* **Vehicle Inspection Checklist Signature Initials**: The initials of the person signing the form.
* **Vehicle Inspection Checklist Signature Title**: The title of the person signing the form.
* **Vehicle Inspection Checklist Signature Location**: The location where the signature is made.
* **Vehicle Inspection Checklist Signature Notes**: Additional notes about the signature.
* **Vehicle Inspection Checklist Signature Position**: The position or department of the person signing the form.
* **Vehicle Inspection Checklist Signature Position Location**: The location of the person signing the form.

Note: This form is a comprehensive checklist that helps to ensure a thorough inspection of vehicles in a network setting. It's essential to fill out all fields accurately and completely to provide a thorough report of the vehicle's condition.
