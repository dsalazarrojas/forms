# Site Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Site Survey Template is a form designed to collect information from users about their experience with the site. The purpose of this form is to gather qualitative data about the site's performance and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your general contact information in the "General Info" section.
2. Check all the services you have used at the site in the "Services Performed" section.
3. Rate the quality of the site's support team in the "Quality Rating" section.
4. Rate the communication of the site's support team in the "Communication Rating" section.
5. Rate your overall experience with the site in the "Overall Experience" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`text`, required: false): This section is for providing your general contact information, such as your name and email address.
* **Services Performed** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Check all the services you have used at the site.
	+ Option 1: Option 2: Option 3
* **Quality Rating** (`number`, required: false): Rate the quality of the site's support team, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Communication Rating** (`number`, required: false): Rate the communication of the site's support team, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Overall Experience** (`number`, required: false): Rate your overall experience with the site, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can include them in the "General Info" section.
* If you've used multiple services at the site, you can select all the relevant options in the "Services Performed" section.
