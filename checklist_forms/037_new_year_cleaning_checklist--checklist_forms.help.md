# new_year_cleaning_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_year_cleaning_checklist form is a tool to help users document and plan their new year's cleaning tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in any cleaning tasks you have planned for the new year. You can select multiple tasks.
2.  Choose the areas you want to focus on for cleaning.
3.  Enter the start and end times for your cleaning sessions.
4.  Select how often you want to clean, with options ranging from "Option 1" to "Option 5".
5.  Choose how many times you want to clean, with options ranging from "Yes" to "No".


## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **new_year_cleaning_checklist** (<new_year_cleaning_checklist>, Text, Optional): This is the title of your cleaning checklist.
*   **cleaning_tasks** (<cleaning_tasks>, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the cleaning tasks you have planned, such as "Yes", "No", etc.
*   **areas_to_focus_on** (<areas_to_focus_on>, Select One, Optional): Choose the areas you want to focus on for cleaning, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", etc.
*   **schedule_checking** (<schedule_checking, Date, Optional): Enter the date you want to start and end your cleaning sessions.
*   **start_time** (<start_time, Time, Optional): Enter the start time of your cleaning session.
*   **end_time** (<end_time, Time, Optional): Enter the end time of your cleaning session.
*   **frequency** (<frequency, Select One, Optional): Select how often you want to clean, with options ranging from "Option 1" to "Option 5".
*   **duration** (<duration, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how many times you want to clean, with options ranging from "Yes" to "No".
