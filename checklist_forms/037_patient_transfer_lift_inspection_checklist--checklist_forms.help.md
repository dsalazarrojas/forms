# Patient Transfer Lift Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to inspect a patient's lift during transfer, ensuring that it is safe and ready for use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct type of lift equipment used for the patient transfer (Lift, Stairway, or Other).
2. Indicate if the patient was transferred (Yes or No).
3. Record the clearance date and time of the lift for safe use.
4. Enter any comments about the lift inspection, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Equipment Checks** (`routine_equipment_checks`, required, number):
This field is used to record the number of equipment checks performed on the lift.

### **Inspections** (`lift_inspections`, required, number):
This field is used to record the number of inspections performed on the lift.

### **Lift Equipment** (`lift_equipment`, select_one, Lift, Stairway, Other):
Choose the type of lift equipment used for the patient transfer (Lift, Stairway, or Other).

### **Transfer** (`patient_transfer`, select_multiple, Yes, No):
Indicate whether the patient was transferred (Yes or No).

### **Clearance** (`lift_clearance`, required, number):
Record the clearance of the lift for safe use.

### **Clearance Date** (`lift_clearance_date`, required, date):
Record the date of the lift's clearance for safe use.

### **Clearance Time** (`lift_clearance_time`, required, time):
Record the time of the lift's clearance for safe use.

### **Comments** (`comments`, optional, note):
Enter any comments about the lift inspection, if necessary.
