# operational_compliance_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track operational compliance inspections within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Operational Compliance Inspection** (`title`, text, **required**): Enter the title of the operational compliance inspection being performed.
* **Compliance Inspection Checklist** (`description`, text, **required**): Enter a brief description of the inspection process or activity.
* **Date of Inspection** (`date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date of the inspection, if applicable.
* **Location** (`location`, text, **optional**): Enter the location where the inspection was conducted, if applicable.
* **Time of Inspection** (`time`, time, **optional**): Enter the time of the inspection, if applicable.
* **Findings** (`findings`, text, **required**): Describe any findings or issues identified during the inspection.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, **optional**): Indicate whether the assigned personnel or tool is "Yes" or "No".
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, **optional**): Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **optional**): Enter the email address of the staff member or personnel responsible for the inspection, if applicable.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **optional**): Enter the phone number of the staff member or personnel responsible for the inspection, if applicable.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, **required**): Select the assigned tool or equipment used during the inspection from the options provided.
* **Assigned to (confirm)** (`assigned_to`, select_one, **optional**): Confirm whether the inspection was assigned to the staff member or personnel by selecting "Yes" or "No".

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all required fields.
* If a field is not required, it is not necessary to fill it out.
* Use clear and concise language to describe findings and comments.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the responsible personnel for clarification.
