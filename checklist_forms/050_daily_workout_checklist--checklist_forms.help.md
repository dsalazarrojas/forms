# Daily Workout Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Daily Workout Checklist" form is designed to help users track their daily workout activities. This form gathers information about their morning workout, exercise type, duration, intensity, and other relevant details. By filling out this form, users can provide valuable insights into their workout habits and make informed decisions to improve their physical fitness.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct answer for each field.
2. Select multiple options for the "Morning Workout" field, as it's a multiple-choice question.
3. For the "Exercise Type" field, select only one option from the available choices.
4. Enter a valid time for the "Workout Time" field.
5. Enter a valid email address for the "Email" field, if required.
6. Enter a valid phone number for the "Phone" field, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Morning Workout**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you worked out in the morning or not.
* **Exercise Type**: Choose the type of exercise you performed, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Exercise Duration**: Enter the duration of your exercise in minutes or hours.
* **Exercise Intensity**: Choose the intensity level of your exercise, such as "Low", "Medium", or "High".
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your workout, such as the exercises you performed or the progress you made.
* **Workout Date**: Enter the date of your workout, in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Workout Time**: Enter the time of your workout, in the format "HH:MM".
* **Email**: Enter your email address, if required. This will help us contact you with any important updates or notifications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if required. This will help us contact you with any important updates or notifications.

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form, especially when selecting your workout type and intensity.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* This form is designed to be used daily, so please fill it out regularly to track your progress and stay on track with your fitness goals.
