# AED Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AED Inspection Checklist is a form used to conduct regular inspections of Automated External Defibrillator (AED) devices. This form helps inspectors gather information about the device's condition, functionality, and compliance with safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Enter your name and email address as the inspector or auditor.
3. Record the date and time of the inspection, if possible.
4. Document the location or site inspected.
5. Provide information about the AED device, including its model, serial number, and installation date.
6. Assess the device's battery status, charge level, and pad condition.
7. Report the results of any functional tests performed on the device.
8. Evaluate the overall compliance status of the device and assign a safety score.
9. List any identified issues and suggest recommended corrective actions.
10. Schedule a follow-up inspection date, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector/Auditor name**: Enter your full legal name, as you are representing the organization conducting the inspection. (<name>, , required)
* **Inspector email address**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes. (<email>, , false)
* **Inspector phone number**: Enter your phone number, including the country code. (<phone>, , false)
* **Inspection date**: Record the date of the inspection, if possible. (<date>, , true)
* **Inspection time**: Record the time of the inspection, if possible. (<time>, , false)
* **Device location or site inspected**: Enter the location or site where the AED device was inspected. (<text>, , true)
* **AED model**: Enter the manufacturer's model number of the AED device. (<text>, , true)
* **AED serial number**: Enter the serial number of the AED device. (<text>, , true)
* **AED installation date**: If known, enter the date the AED was installed. (<date>, , false)
* **Last maintenance date**: If applicable, enter the last date the AED was maintained. (<date>, , false)
* **Battery status**: Evaluate the condition of the AED's battery: New, Good, Fair, Poor, or Not Tested. (<select_one>, , true)
* **Battery charge (%)**: Record the battery charge level, if known. (<decimal>, , false)
* **Pad condition**: Evaluate the condition of the AED pads: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Not Tested. (<select_one>, , true)
* **AED functionality test**: Report the results of the last functional test: Pass, Fail, Not Tested. (<select_one>, , true)
* **AED test date**: If applicable, record the date of the last functional test. (<date>, , false)
* **Overall compliance status**: Evaluate the device's compliance status: Fully compliant, Minor issues, Major issues, Non-compliant. (<select_one>, , true)
* **Safety score**: Assign a score from 1 to 10, indicating the device's safety level. (<integer>, , true)
* **Issues identified**: Select all applicable issues: Safety hazard, Documentation gap, Equipment failure, Training needed, Cleanliness, Structural issue, Battery failure, Pad failure, Software update needed. (<select_multiple>, , false)
* **Recommended corrective actions**: Describe actions to resolve any identified issues. (<text>, , false)
* **Follow-up inspection date**: Schedule a follow-up inspection date, if necessary. (<date>, , false)
* **Additional comments or notes**: Provide any other observations or comments. (<text>, , false)
