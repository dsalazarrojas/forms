# Regulatory Record Archival Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Regulatory Record Archival Checklist is a tool designed to help ensure that regulatory records are properly archived and maintained. This form helps collect relevant information about the record, including the type of record, department responsible, date of archival, current location, and other important details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the type of regulatory record being archived from the options provided.
2. Choose the department responsible for the record from the list of available options.
3. Enter the date of archival.
4. Specify the current location of the record.
5. Indicate if the record has been properly reviewed and approved.
6. Confirm if the record is up-to-date and current.
7. Enter the reason for archival.
8. List any associated documents or files that need to be stored with the record.
9. (Optional) Upload any relevant files related to the record.
10. Provide additional file upload details if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What type of regulatory record is being archived?** (`regulatory_record_type`, Select One)
This field is used to identify the type of regulatory record being archived, such as Record 1, Record 2, or Record 3.
- **Which department is responsible for the record?** (`department`, Select One)
This field is used to specify which department is responsible for maintaining and storing the record.
- **What is the date of archival?** (`date_of_archival`, Date)
Enter the date when the record was archived.
- **What is the current location of the record?** (`current_location`, Text)
Specify the current location where the record is being stored.
- **Has the record been properly reviewed and approved?** (`reviewed_and_approved`, Checkbox)
Check this box to confirm if the record has been properly reviewed and approved.
- **Is the record up-to-date and current?** (`up_to_date`, Checkbox)
Check this box to confirm if the record is up-to-date and current.
- **What is the reason for archival?** (`reason_for_archival`, Text)
Enter the reason why the record is being archived.
- **Are there any associated documents or files that need to be stored with the record?** (`associated_documents`, Text)
List any documents or files that are associated with the record.
- **File Upload** (`file_upload`, Text)
( Optional) Upload any relevant files related to the record.
- **File Upload Details** (`file_upload_details`, Text)
( Optional) Provide additional details about the uploaded file.
- **File Upload** (`file_upload`, Text)
This field is for uploading files that are associated with the record.
