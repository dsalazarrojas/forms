# Lockout Tagout Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lockout Tagout Safety Checklist is a form used to track and document the safety and maintenance of equipment in industrial settings. It helps maintenance personnel and safety inspectors to identify potential safety issues, schedule regular inspections, and make recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Initial Check**: Enter the Equipment ID number and the date and time of the initial check.
2. **Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
3. **Time**: Enter the time of the inspection.
4. **Status**: Select the status of the equipment (Normal, Abnormal, or Critical).
5. **Issue**: Enter any issues found during the inspection.
6. **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
7. **Inspection Frequency**: Select the frequency of the inspection (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annual, or Annual).
8. **Last Inspection**: Enter the date and time of the last inspection.
9. **Last Inspection Status**: Select the status of the last inspection (Normal, Abnormal, or Critical).
10. **Last Inspection Frequency**: Select the frequency of the last inspection (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annual, or Annual).
11. **Inspection History**: Enter any relevant inspection history.
12. **Inspection Summary**: Enter a brief summary of the inspection.
13. **Recommendations**: Enter any recommendations for improvement.
14. **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
