# roofing_repair_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The roofing repair checklist form is used to organize and track roofing repair tasks, including inspections, necessary repairs, cost estimates, and progress tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the **Roof Inspection Checklist** (Page 1). Check off any items that require inspection, and provide notes or comments if necessary.
2. In the **Repair Needs** section (Page 2), select all the repair options that apply. This will help you identify the necessary repairs.
3. In the **Cost Estimate** section, provide a detailed cost estimate for each repair option.
4. Update the **Repair Status** field with the current status of the repair (e.g., Pending, Completed, In Progress).
5. Record any **Additional Comments** or **Notes** regarding the repair.
6. Provide **Contact Details** (e.g., Name, Phone, Email, Address) in case further communication is required.
7. Select the relevant **Roofing Contractor** from the provided options.
8. Capture the **Client Signature** and **Contractor Signature** for official approval.
9. Enter the **Date Completed** and **Time Completed** for this repair item.
10. Set the **Priority Level** (High, Medium, Low) based on the urgency of the repair.
11. Provide a brief explanation for the **Priority Reason**, if applicable.

* **Roof Inspection Checklist (Page 1)**: Check off any items that require inspection and provide notes or comments if necessary.
