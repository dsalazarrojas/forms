# Global Stage Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Stage Maintenance Checklist is a form used to collect and track important information about a global stage's condition, equipment, and issues. It helps maintenance teams to:

* Assess the stage's overall condition
* Identify any issues that need to be addressed
* Create a list of equipment that requires maintenance or repair
* Plan follow-up activities to ensure the stage is in good working condition

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Global Stage Maintenance Checklist, follow these steps:

1. Enter the General Information section with the following details:
	* **General Information** (`text`, required): This is the main section where you will provide an overview of the stage's condition. Please describe the current state of the stage, including any notable observations, comments, or concerns.
2. Provide Stage Details:
	* **Stage Details** (`text`, required): In this section, you will list any specific details about the stage, such as its location, dimensions, or any recent events that may impact its condition.
3. Create a list of equipment that requires maintenance or repair:
	* **Equipment List** (`text`, required): List all the equipment associated with the stage, including any that are not functioning correctly or require maintenance.
4. Document any issues or concerns:
	* **Issues** (`text`, required): Describe any problems or concerns you have about the stage's condition, including any safety risks or potential hazards.
5. Plan follow-up activities:
	* **Follow-ups** (`text`, required): In this section, outline any follow-up actions or checks that need to be performed to ensure the stage is in good working condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (text, required)**: This section is used to provide an overview of the stage's condition. Please provide a brief description of the current state of the stage.
* **Stage Details (text, required)**: In this section, you will provide any specific details about the stage that are relevant to its condition. This can include information about its location, dimensions, or any recent events that may impact its condition.
* **Equipment List (text, required)**: List all the equipment associated with the stage that requires maintenance or repair.
* **Issues (text, required)**: Describe any problems or concerns you have about the stage's condition, including any safety risks or potential hazards.
* **Follow-ups (text, required)**: In this section, outline any follow-up actions or checks that need to be performed to ensure the stage is in good working condition.
