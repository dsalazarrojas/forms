# Security Uniform Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the inspection and assessment of security uniforms. It collects information about the inspection date, facility name, inspectee name, and security personnel involved in the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Inspection Date** in the designated date field. This is the date the security uniform inspection was conducted.
2. Enter the **Facility Name** in the text field provided. This is the name of the facility being inspected.
3. Enter the **Inspectee Name** in the text field. This is the name of the person or team being inspected.
4. Enter the **Security Personnel** in the text field. This is the name of the security personnel involved in the inspection process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date** (`security_uniform_checklist_form_1`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY (MM = month number, DD = day, YY = year).
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Inspectee Name** (`security_uniform_checklist_form_3`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person or team being inspected.
* **Security Personnel** (`security_uniform_checklist_form_4`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the security personnel involved in the inspection process.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a thorough inspection record.
* Ensure that you are using the correct format for the **Inspection Date** field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
