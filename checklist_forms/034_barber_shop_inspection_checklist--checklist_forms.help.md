# Barber Shop Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to conduct regular inspections of barber shops to ensure they meet the necessary standards for safety, sanitation, and overall compliance. The inspection covers various aspects, including professional licensing, sanitation and hygiene, facility and safety, and overall compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information, including the inspector's name, barber shop name, date of inspection, and type of inspection.
2. Check the status of the barber shop's license, ensuring it is currently displayed and valid.
3. Verify that all barbers' licenses are properly displayed and valid.
4. Check the barbercide / disinfectant jars to ensure they are properly filled and fresh.
5. Inspect non-disposable tools to ensure they are properly sterilized.
6. Check the cleanliness of the area, including the separation of clean and dirty towels, and the presence of a fire extinguisher.
7. Evaluate the proper labeling and storage of chemicals.
8. Record the final inspection score and required corrective actions, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Administration**:
	+ This section includes general information about the inspection, such as the date and type of inspection.
* **Inspector Full Name**: 
	+ Enter the full name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
* **Barber Shop Name**: 
	+ Enter the name of the barber shop being inspected.
* **Date of Inspection**: 
	+ Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Type of Inspection**: 
	+ Select the type of inspection being conducted (e.g., monthly routine, quarterly safety audit, health department mock inspection, or final pre-opening review).
* **Is the Shop License currently displayed and valid?**: 
	+ Check if the barber shop's license is currently displayed and valid. Ensure the expiration date is up to date.
* **Are all barber licenses displayed and valid?**: 
	+ Check if all barber licenses are properly displayed and valid. Cross-check with the current staff list.
* **Barbicide / Disinfectant Jars Properly Filled?**: 
	+ Check if the barbercide / disinfectant jars are properly filled and fresh.
* **All non-disposable tools properly sterilized?**: 
	+ Check if all non-disposable tools (e.g., scissors, clipper guards, combs) are properly sterilized.
* **Clean and dirty towels separated and stored properly?**: 
	+ Check if clean and dirty towels are separated and stored in their respective bins.
* **Floor free of hair and debris around stations?**: 
	+ Check if the floor is free of hair and debris around stations.
* **Fire extinguisher(s) present and current?**: 
	+ Check if fire extinguishers are present and have a current inspection tag.
* **Proper labeling and storage of chemicals?**: 
	+ Check if chemicals are properly labeled and stored.
* **Total Inspection Score (%)**: 
	+ Enter the total inspection score as a percentage (e.g., 95).
* **Required Corrective Actions**: 
	+ List any failures and deadlines for correction.
* **Final Inspection Result**: 
	+ Record the final inspection result (e.g., approved, conditional approval, failed).
