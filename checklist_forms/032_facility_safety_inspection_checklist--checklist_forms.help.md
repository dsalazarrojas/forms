# Facility Safety Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Safety Inspection Checklist is a tool used to record and report on facility safety conditions, identifying potential risks and hazards, and tracking progress on addressing them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility being inspected.
2. **Facility Type**: Select the type of facility being inspected (e.g., Manufacturing, Office Building, Restaurant).
3. **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility.
4. **Facility Owner**: Select the owner(s) of the facility (e.g., Facility A, Facility B, Facility C).
5. **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
6. **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the inspector.
7. **Safety Rating**: Enter a numerical value to rate the facility's safety level (e.g., 1-10).
8. **Hazards Found**: Describe any hazards or risks identified during the inspection.
9. **Corrective Actions**: Describe any corrective actions taken or planned to address the hazards found.
10. **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date for the next scheduled follow-up inspection.
11. **Follow-up Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the inspector assigned to conduct the follow-up inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Note): A brief note for the inspector to provide context or comments on the inspection.
* **facility_address** (Text): The address of the facility being inspected.
* **facility_type** (Select One): The type of facility being inspected.
* **facility_name** (Text): The name of the facility.
* **facility_owner** (Select Multiple): The owner(s) of the facility.
* **inspection_date** (Date): The date of the inspection.
* **inspector_name** (Text): The name of the inspector.
* **safety_rating** (Number): A numerical value to rate the facility's safety level.
* **hazards_found** (Text): Describe any hazards or risks identified during the inspection.
* **corrective_actions** (Text): Describe any corrective actions taken or planned to address the hazards found.
* **follow_up_date** (Date): The date for the next scheduled follow-up inspection.
* **follow_up_inspector_name** (Text): The name of the inspector assigned to conduct the follow-up inspection.

Please note that all fields are optional unless otherwise specified as "required" in the form definition.
