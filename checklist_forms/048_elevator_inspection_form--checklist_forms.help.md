# elevator_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The elevator inspection form is designed to help users track and record elevator inspections. It captures essential information about the inspection, including date, elevator type, and status, as well as maintenance and safety-related data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the inspection date and complete the date fields for the maintenance due date (if applicable).
2. Choose the elevator type (Passenger, Freight, or Goods).
3. Indicate the inspection status (Complete or Not Complete).
4. Fill out the building name and floor number fields.
5. For maintenance, select "Complete" or "Not Complete" and describe the maintenance status and any associated costs.
6. Record any notes or comments about the inspection or maintenance.
7. Report any identified safety issues and describe the necessary action to address them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection in `MM/DD/YYYY` format.
* **Elevator ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the elevator being inspected.
* **Building Name**: Type the name of the building where the elevator is located.
* **Floor Number**: Enter the floor number of the building where the elevator is located.
* **Elevator Type**: Choose the type of elevator being inspected (Passenger, Freight, or Goods).
* **Inspection Status**: Indicate whether the inspection is Complete or Not Complete.
* **Maintenance Status**: Select "Complete" or "Not Complete" for any maintenance performed on the elevator.
* **Maintenance Description**: If the maintenance status is Complete, describe the maintenance work done.
* **Maintenance Cost**: Enter the cost of any maintenance performed.
* **Inspection Comments**: Add any comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Safety Issues**: Report any safety issues identified during the inspection.
* **Safety Action**: Describe any actions taken or planned to address the safety issues. 
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
