# Restaurant Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to inspect or verify information about a restaurant's physical conditions and operations. It will help ensure that the restaurant is compliant with health and safety regulations and standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the restaurant's address in the `Restaurant Address` field.
2. Provide the restaurant's name in the `Restaurant Name` field.
3. Enter the restaurant's business phone number in the `Business Phone` field.
4. Enter the restaurant's business email in the `Business Email` field.
5. Specify the business hours of operation in the `Business Hours` field.
6. Provide the name of the emergency contact person in the `Emergency Contact Name` field.
7. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person in the `Emergency Contact Phone` field.
8. Check if the restaurant has business insurance in the `Business Insurance` field.
9. Select the types of food services offered by the restaurant from the `Food Services` field.
10. If the restaurant offers other types of food services, specify them in the `Other (please specify)` field.
11. Check the date of the last restrooms inspection in the `Restroom Conditions` field (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
12. Check if trash and receptacles are available in the `Trash and Receptacles` field.
13. Check if fire exit signs are available in the `Fire Exit Signs` field.
14. Check if fire extinguishers are available in the `Fire Extinguisher` field.
15. Check if emergency exits are available in the `Emergency Exit` field.
16. Check if electrical equipment is in good condition in the `Electrical` field.
17. Check the current status of the water supply in the `Water Supply` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Restaurant Address (1)**: Enter the address of the restaurant.
* **Restaurant Name (2)**: Enter the name of the restaurant.
* **Business Phone (3)**: Enter the phone number of the restaurant.
* **Business Email (4)**: Enter the email address of the restaurant.
* **Business Hours (5)**: Specify the business hours of operation.
* **Emergency Contact Name (6)**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (7)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Business Insurance (8)**: Check if the restaurant has business insurance.
* **Food Services (9)**: Select the types of food services offered by the restaurant (e.g., Food Service, Bar Service, Catering).
* **Other (please specify) (10)**: If the restaurant offers other types of food services, specify them here.
* **Restroom Conditions (11)**: Check the date of the last restrooms inspection (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Trash and Receptacles (12)**: Check if trash and receptacles are available.
* **Fire Exit Signs (13)**: Check if fire exit signs are available.
* **Fire Extinguisher (14)**: Check if fire extinguishers are available.
* **Emergency Exit (15)**: Check if emergency exits are available.
* **Electrical (16)**: Check if electrical equipment is in good condition.
* **Water Supply (17)**: Check the current status of the water supply.
