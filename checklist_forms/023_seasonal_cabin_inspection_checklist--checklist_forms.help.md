# Seasonal Cabin Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seasonal Cabin Inspection Checklist is designed to help you inspect the cabin for any maintenance issues before the season begins. This form will guide you through the inspection process, ensuring that you don't miss any critical areas that require attention.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `cabin_inspection_date` field with the date of your inspection.
2. Enter the `cabin_inspection_time` field with the time when you conducted the inspection.
3. In the `property_manager_notes` field, provide any notes or comments about the property's condition.
4. In the `owner_notes` field, include any comments or concerns you want to pass on to the owner.
5. In the `maintenance_team_comments` field, provide any maintenance-related comments or recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **cabin_inspection_date**: Enter the date when you conducted the inspection.
* **cabin_inspection_time**: Enter the time when you conducted the inspection.
* **property_manager_notes**: Describe the overall condition of the property, including any issues or concerns you have.
* **owner_notes**: Share any comments or concerns with the owner, or provide feedback on the property's maintenance status.
* **maintenance_team_comments**: Record any maintenance-related comments or recommendations for the maintenance team.

## Tips

* Be thorough and detailed when filling out the form to ensure accuracy and clarity.
* If you're unsure about any aspect of the inspection, consult with your team or the owner before completing the form.
* Make sure to keep a copy of the inspection form for future reference.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure it remains relevant and accurate.
