# ISMS Assessment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ISMS Assessment Checklist is a tool used to evaluate the Information Security Management System (ISMS) of an organization. It consists of a series of questions and fields that assess the current state and progress of various aspects of ISMS.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **ISMS Policy**: This field is used to provide a brief description of your organization's ISMS policy. Enter a short statement that summarizes the policy.
2. **Team Leads**: In this field, you must indicate whether team leads are actively participating in ISMS activities. Select all applicable options (e.g., "Yes" if applicable).
3. **Control Status**: This field determines the current status of ISMS controls. Select "Active" if the controls are currently active or "Inactive" if they are not.
4. **Maturity Levels**: Select the current maturity level of your organization's ISMS. Choose either "High" or "Low".
5. **Improvement Priorities**: Indicate which ISMS improvements are priorities. Select all applicable options (e.g., "Yes" if applicable).
6. **Status Update**: Provide a brief status update on the current state of ISMS.
7. **Next Steps**: Outline the next steps required to complete ISMS tasks.
8. **Review and Signoff**: Indicate whether the ISMS review and signoff process is complete.
9. **Completed Date**: Enter the date on which the ISMS assessment was completed.
10. **Completed By**: Enter the name of the person who completed the ISMS assessment.
11. **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the ISMS assessment.
12. **Review and Signoff Approvals**: Indicate whether review and signoff approvals are complete.
13. **Review and Signoff Comments**: Add any comments or notes about review and signoff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **ISMS Policy** (`title`, `text`, required): A brief description of your organization's ISMS policy.
* **Team Leads** (`team_leads`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate whether team leads are actively participating in ISMS activities.
* **Control Status** (`controls`, `select_one`, required): The current status of ISMS controls.
* **Maturity Levels** (`maturity_levels`, `select_one`, required): The current maturity level of your organization's ISMS.
* **Improvement Priorities** (`improvement_priorities`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate which ISMS improvements are priorities.
* **Status Update** (`status_update`, `text`, required): A brief status update on the current state of ISMS.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, required): Outline the next steps required to complete ISMS tasks.
* **Review and Signoff** (`review_and_signoff`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether the ISMS review and signoff process is complete.
* **Completed Date** (`completed_date`, `date`, required): The date on which the ISMS assessment was completed.
* **Completed By** (`completed_by`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who completed the ISMS assessment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the ISMS assessment.
* **Review and Signoff Approvals** (`review_and_signoff_approvals`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether review and signoff approvals are complete.
* **Review and Signoff Comments** (`review_and_signoff_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Add any comments or notes about review and signoff.
