# Irrigation System Sanitation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Irrigation System Sanitation Checklist is used to assess the sanitation status of an irrigation system at a specific site. This includes evaluating the type and status of pumps, the condition and maintenance of pipes, and other key aspects of the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the site name in the `site_name` field, which will help identify the specific location being inspected.
2. Provide the address of the site in the `site_address` field, which will help locate the site in case of any issues.
3. Enter the contact information of the site owner or administrator in the `site_contact` field.
4. Select the type of pump present at the site in the `pump_type` field.
5. Check the status of the pump in the `pump_status` field.
6. Indicate if the pump requires maintenance in the `pump_maintenance` field.
7. Assess the water quality at the site in the `water_quality` field.
8. Report the water level at the site in the `water_level` field.
9. Evaluate the condition of the pipes in the `pipe_condition` field.
10. Check if there are any leaks in the `pipe_leakage` field.
11. Assess if pipes are properly insulated in the `pipe_insulation` field.
12. Check if pipes have proper support in the `pipe_support` field.
13. Evaluate if pipes have adequate cover in the `pipe_cover` field.
14. Assess the alignment of pipes in the `pipe_alignment` field.
15. Report the clearance of pipes in the `pipe_clearance` field.
16. Record the date of the last pipe inspection in the `pipe_inspection_date` field.
17. Report the date of the last pump inspection in the `pump_inspection_date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Name** (`site_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the irrigation system site.
* **Site Address** (`site_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the irrigation system site.
* **Site Contact** (`site_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the site owner or administrator.
* **Pump Type** (`pump_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of pump present at the site.
* **Pump Status** (`pump_status`, select_one, optional): Assess the status of the pump at the site.
* **Pump Maintenance** (`pump_maintenance`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if the pump requires maintenance.
* **Water Quality** (`water_quality`, select_multiple, optional): Assess the quality of water at the site.
* **Water Level** (`water_level`, select_one, optional): Report the water level at the site.
* **Pipe Condition** (`pipe_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the condition of the pipes at the site.
* **Pipe Leakeage** (`pipe_leakage`, select_one, optional): Check if there are any leaks in the pipes.
* **Pipe Insulation** (`pipe_insulation`, select_multiple, optional): Assess if pipes are properly insulated.
* **Pipe Support** (`pipe_support`, select_multiple, optional): Check if pipes have proper support.
* **Pipe Cover** (`pipe_cover`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate if pipes have adequate cover.
* **Pipe Alignment** (`pipe_alignment`, select_multiple, optional): Assess the alignment of pipes.
* **Pipe Clearance** (`pipe_clearance`, number, optional): Report the clearance of pipes.
* **Pipe Inspection Date** (`pipe_inspection_date`, date, optional): Record the date of the last pipe inspection.
* **Pump Inspection Date** (`pump_inspection_date`, date, optional): Report the date of the last pump inspection.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
* Use the options provided to select the correct answers for each field.
* Double-check your answers to ensure they reflect the actual situation at the site.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with authorized personnel or the site owner/administrator.
* This form is designed to be a comprehensive guide for evaluating the sanitation status of irrigation systems.
