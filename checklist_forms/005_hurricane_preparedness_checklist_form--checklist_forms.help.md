# hurricane_preparedness_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals and families prepare for a hurricane by collecting essential information on emergency plans, supplies, and other critical details. Completing this form will help you create a comprehensive emergency plan and ensure you are ready for any hurricane-related disaster.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each field description to understand what information it requires.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are indicated by "required" label.
3. For fields that are not required, provide as much information as possible to create a complete emergency plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Plans** (`emergency_plans`, `text`, required: false): This field asks for any emergency plans you have in place, such as evacuation routes, emergency contact numbers, and other critical plans to help you and your family stay safe during a hurricane.
* **Emergency Procedure** (`emergency_procedure`, `text`, required: false): Describe the emergency procedures you have established, including the actions to take before, during, and after a hurricane.
* **Supplies** (`supplies`, `text`, required: false): List any essential items you should stock up on, such as food, water, first aid kits, and other critical supplies to help you survive during the hurricane.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide contact information for your emergency contacts, including phone numbers and any other relevant contact details.
* **Evacuation Plan** (`evacuation_plan`, `text`, required: false): Outline your evacuation plan, including the safest routes to take and any critical information for your family members and pets.
* **Insurance** (`insurance`, `text`, required: false): List any insurance policies, such as flood, wind, or property insurance, that can help you recover from a hurricane disaster.
* **Water** (`water`, `text`, required: false): Note any sources of water, including bottled water, water purification tablets, or other critical water supplies.
* **First Aid** (`first_aid`, `text`, required: false): Prepare a first aid kit with essentials, such as bandages, antiseptic ointment, and pain relievers, to treat injuries during a hurricane.
* **Food** (`food`, `text`, required: false): List three days' worth of food and water, including non-perishable items, to ensure you have enough supplies to last during the hurricane.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): Gather any essential medications and medical equipment that you and your family members may need during the disaster.
* **Phone Numbers** (`phone_numbers`, `text`, required: false): List phone numbers of family members and emergency contacts, including out-of-state contacts.
* **Evacuation Route** (`evacuation_route_goals`, `text`, required: false): Identify your safest evacuation routes and any critical information you need to know before evacuating.
* **Emergency Funds** (`emergency_funds`, `text`, required: false): Save some cash and other valuable items to help you recover from the hurricane disaster.
* **Insurance Documents** (`insurance_documents`, `text`, required: false): Keep copies of all insurance policies, identification cards, and other critical documents in a safe place.
* **Emergency Contact List** (`emergency_contact_list`, `text`, required: false): Make a list of your emergency contacts, including phone numbers and other critical contact information.
* **Power and Lighting** (`power_and_lighting`, `text`, required: false): Identify any essential power sources, such as generators or flashlights, and keep them in working condition.
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, `text`, required: false): List any safety equipment, such as helmets, ropes, or other essential safety gear.
* **Tools and Equipment** (`tools_and_equipment`, `text`, required: false): Prepare any essential tools and equipment, such as a portable charger, first aid kit, or other critical tools.
* **Important Documents** (`important_docs`, `text`, required: false): Keep essential documents, such as insurance policies, identification cards, and other critical documents, in a safe place.
* **Cash and Valuables** (`cash_and_valuables`, `text`, required: false): Stock up on some cash and other valuable items to help you recover from the hurricane.
* **Vehicle Info** (`vehicle_info`, `text`, required: false): Record your vehicle make, model, and any critical vehicle information, such as VIN or license plate number.
* **Vehicle Maintenance** (`vehicle_maintenance`, `text`, required: false): Prepare your vehicle for a hurricane disaster by checking the oil, gas, and other critical maintenance tasks.
* **Vehicle Equipment** (`vehicle_equipment`, `text`, required: false): List any vehicle equipment, such as a spare tire, jumper cables, or other critical tools.
* **Other Items** (`other_items`, `text`, required: false): List other essential items you may need during a hurricane, such as a battery-powered radio or other communication tools.
* **Final Prep Check** (`final_prep_check`, `text`, required: false): Review and finalize your emergency plan, ensuring you have all critical information and supplies in place.

Please note that this is a general guide and should be reviewed and customized based on your specific needs and location.
