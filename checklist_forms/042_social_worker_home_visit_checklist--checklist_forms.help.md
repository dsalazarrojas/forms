# Social Worker Home Visit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Worker Home Visit Checklist is a tool to help social workers gather information about a client's home environment, safety, and resources during a home visit. This form aims to assist social workers in identifying areas of concern and providing targeted support to the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the client's information, including their name, address, and any relevant background information.
2. Assess the client's home safety, using the options provided (Safe, Not Safe, Unable to Determine).
3. Evaluate the client's support systems, such as their access to resources and services.
4. Determine the client's level of access to resources, using the options provided (Safe, Not Safe, Unable to Determine).
5. Assess the client's environment, using the options provided (Safe, Not Safe, Unable to Determine).
6. Evaluate the client's culture and how it may impact their well-being.
7. Take notes on any observations or concerns from the home visit.
8. Review the client's photos, if any were taken during the visit.
9. Summarize any additional comments or concerns.
10. Determine if follow-up recommendations are needed and indicate the review status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (Client Information, text, required/optional): This field is for entering the client's name, address, and any relevant background information.
* **Assessment** (Assessment, select_one, required/optional): Select the level of safety in the client's home (Safe, Not Safe, Unable to Determine).
* **Home Safety** (Home Safety, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any safety concerns in the client's home (Safe, Not Safe, Unable to Determine).
* **Client Support Systems** (Client Support Systems, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any support systems available to the client (Safe, Not Safe, Unable to Determine).
* **Client Resources** (Client Resources, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the client's level of access to resources (Safe, Not Safe, Unable to Determine).
* **Client Environment** (Client Environment, select_one, required/optional): Select the level of safety in the client's environment (Safe, Not Safe, Unable to Determine).
* **Client Culture** (Client Culture, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any cultural factors that may impact the client's well-being (Safe, Not Safe, Unable to Determine).
* **Home Visit Notes** (Home Visit Notes, text, required/optional): Enter any notes or observations from the home visit.
* **Home Visit Photos** (Home Visit Photos, select_multiple, required/optional): Select if any photos were taken during the visit (True, False, Unable to Determine).
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns.
* **Follow Up Recommendations** (Follow Up Recommendations, select_multiple, required/optional): Select if follow-up recommendations are needed (True, False, Unable to Determine).
* **Review Status** (Review Status, select_one, required/optional): Select the review status (Review Needed, Completed, Not Started).
