# Store Visit Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Store Visit Checklist is designed for assessing Loss Prevention, Safety, Security, Quality, and Cleanliness during in-person store visits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the "Assessment Areas" section and choose the relevant categories for the store visit.
2. Fill out each page with your observations and ratings in the corresponding sections.
3. Ensure you complete the "Date and Time" field accurately.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Areas** (Select Multiple):
	+ This field allows you to select the areas you will assess during the store visit. Choose all applicable categories.
* **Date and Time** (Date):
	+ Enter the date and time of your store visit to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
* **Store Visit Checklist 1-22** (Text):
	+ These text fields are for capturing detailed observations and notes during your store visit.
	+ Please fill out each field as thoroughly as possible to provide accurate assessments.
* Note: Each "Store Visit Checklist" field is optional, but filling them out will help provide a comprehensive assessment.

## Tips
- Take your time to fill out the form accurately.
- Review each field carefully to ensure completeness and accuracy.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, refer back to the store's documentation or contact the store's representative for assistance.
