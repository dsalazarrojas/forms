# sports_trading_card_royalty_tracking_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track royalty payments for sports trading cards. It's intended to help users record and manage various aspects of royalty payment processes, including licensing periods, reporting progress, royalty payment status, and other related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields, such as contact name and phone number (though they're not required for now, it's best to fill them out for completeness sake).
2. Update the licensing periods, reporting progress, and other relevant fields as needed.
3. Select the relevant status for royalty payment and reporting from the dropdown options.
4. Add any additional comments or explanations in the "Comments" and "Additional Comments" fields.
5. Enter the reporting date and time, and any other relevant details.
