# eGFR Degradation Study Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The eGFR Degradation Study Checklist is a form used to document and track the details of an EGFR degradation study. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and recorded accurately, facilitating a thorough understanding of the experimental results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out each page of the form according to the information you have on hand.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed, but note that this form allows for optional fields to be filled out as well.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
