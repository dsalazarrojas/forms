# Family Reunion Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Reunion Checklist Form is a comprehensive tool to collect information from family members to plan and organize a family reunion effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Guest Name" to indicate who is filling out the form.
2. Choose your RSVP status by selecting "True" to confirm attendance, "False" to decline, or "Maybe" to indicate uncertainty.
3. Choose the activities you'll be participating in.
4. Select your preferred mode of travel, food options, accommodation, and transportation methods.
5. Add any additional notes or comments if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Reunion Details** (`family_reunion_details`, text, required): Please provide a brief overview of the family reunion, including the purpose, expected number of attendees, and any other relevant details.
* **Guest List** (`guest_list`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Guest Name
	+ Guest Phone
	+ Guest Email
	+ Guest Address
	+ Guest Type
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Describe the event details, such as the location, date, and time.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the family reunion.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the family reunion.
* **RSVP Status** (`rsvp_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose your RSVP status:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe
* **Activity List** (`activity_list`, select_multiple, required): Choose the activities you'll be participating in.
* **Accommodation** (`accommodation`, text, optional): If you need assistance with accommodation, please describe your needs.
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **RSVP Deadline** (`rsvp_deadline`, date, optional): Enter the date by which you need to RSVP.
* **RSVP Reminder** (`rsvp_reminder`, date, optional): Set a reminder date for your RSVP.
* **Family Reunion Budget** (`family_reunion_budget`, text, optional): Provide an estimated budget for your family reunion.
* **Family Reunion Notes** (`family_reunion_notes`, text, optional): Add any notes or comments about the family reunion.
* **Family Reunion Comments** (`family_reunion_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the family reunion.
* **Family Reunion Attendees** (`family_reunion_attendees`, text, optional): List the family members attending the reunion.
* **Family Reunion Attendee Notes** (`family_reunion_attendee_notes`, text, optional): Add any notes or comments about family reunion attendees.
* **Family Reunion Activities** (`family_reunion_activities`, text, optional): Describe the activities you'd like to participate in.
* **Family Reunion Travel** (`family_reunion_travel`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred mode of travel:
	+ Flight
	+ Train
	+ Bus
* **Family Reunion Food** (`family_reunion_food`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred food options:
	+ Breakfast
	+ Lunch
	+ Dinner
	+ Snacks
* **Family Reunion Housing** (`family_reunion_housing`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred accommodation options:
	+ Hotel
	+ Airbnb
	+ Camping
* **Family Reunion Transport** (`family_reunion_transportation`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred transportation methods:
	+ Car
	+ Train
	+ Bike
* **Family Reunion Equipment** (`family_reunion_equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred equipment options:
	+ Tent
	+ Table
	+ Chair

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for assistance.
* The information collected will be used to create a comprehensive family reunion plan.
* If you have any conflicts or changes in your plans, please let us know as soon as possible.
