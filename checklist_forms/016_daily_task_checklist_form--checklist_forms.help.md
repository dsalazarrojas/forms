# Daily Task Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a daily task checklist for a specific user.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all tasks that you need to complete on the **Task List** page.
2. Choose how you will approach **Task 1** and **Task 2** by selecting one of the options.
3. If you have a specific **Start Time** and **End Time** for your tasks, you can select the correct time for each.
4. If you want to record the date you completed your tasks, select the correct **Date**.
5. Add any additional **Notes** about your tasks if needed.
6. If applicable, select the **Assignee**, who will be responsible for completing these tasks.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Task List (select_multiple)**: 
  Select all the tasks you need to complete today.
* **Task 1 (select_one)**: 
  Choose how you will approach Task 1. If you're not sure what to choose, you can simply select 'No' and explain your approach in the **Notes** field.
* **Task 2 (select_multiple)**: 
  Select how you will approach Task 2.
* **Start Time (time)**: 
  Enter the time when you started your tasks.
* **End Time (time)**: 
  Enter the time when you finished your tasks.
* **Date (date)**: 
  Enter the date you completed your tasks.
* **Notes (note)**: 
  Add any additional comments or explanations about your tasks, such as why you chose a particular approach or why you were unable to complete a task.
* **Assignee (select_one)**: 
  Select the person responsible for completing these tasks, if applicable. If not applicable, you can simply select 'No'.
