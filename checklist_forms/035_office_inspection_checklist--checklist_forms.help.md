# Office Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Office Inspection Checklist is a form designed to help office administrators or maintenance personnel inspect and assess the condition of an office space. This form is intended to provide a systematic approach to identifying potential issues, gathering information, and tracking progress in maintaining a safe and healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and field carefully.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Complete each field according to the instructions provided.
4. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Office Inspection Checklist** (`office_inspection_checklist_label`, text, Optional)
	+ A brief description of the office area or room being inspected.
* **Premises Quality Assessment** (`premises_quality_assessment_label`, text, Optional)
	+ Rate the current condition of the premises, providing details on any observed issues, such as cleanliness, safety, or maintenance concerns.
* **Information Gathered** (`information_gathered_label`, text, Optional)
	+ Record any relevant information or observations made during the inspection, including photos, notes, or concerns.

## Tips
- Take your time to carefully examine the office area before answering the questions.
- Be specific and descriptive when answering each field.
- If unsure about any aspect of the form, feel free to contact the form author at form@gic.mx for guidance.
