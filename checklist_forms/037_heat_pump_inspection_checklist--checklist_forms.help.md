# Heat Pump Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heat Pump Inspection Checklist is a form used to document the condition and performance of a heat pump system. It guides the inspector or technician through a comprehensive inspection process, ensuring all necessary areas are covered and providing a clear report of the system's status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **General Info**: Enter the general information about the heat pump system, including the location and any relevant details.
2. **System Performance**: Describe the overall performance of the system, including any issues or concerns.
3. **Safety Issues**: Identify any safety concerns or hazards associated with the system.
4. **System Components**: Select all applicable components that are part of the system, such as the Compressor, Fan, and Coils.
5. **Control Panel**: Provide any information about the control panel, including its condition and performance.
6. **Safety Controls**: Select all applicable safety controls, such as Pressure-Relief Valve and Temperature Gauge.
7. **Electrical System**: Describe the electrical system's condition and performance.
8. **Mechanical System**: Describe the mechanical system's condition and performance.
9. **Piping System**: Describe the piping system's condition and performance.
10. **Insulation**: Check if the system's components have proper insulation.
11. **Other Findings**: Record any other relevant findings or issues not covered in the previous fields.
12. **Additional Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the system.
13. **Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
14. **Time**: Enter the time of the inspection.
15. **User Input**: Enter a numerical value indicating the level of the system's performance (e.g., 1-10).
16. **Inspector Name**: Enter your name and any relevant contact information.
