# Trailer Breakdown Process Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the trailer breakdown process, helping to gather all necessary details for a thorough breakdown process in the logistics or transportation industries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "General Info" page, providing any general information that is relevant to the breakdown process.
2. Proceed to the "Trailer Info" page and enter all necessary details about the trailer, such as its description, type, or other relevant information.
3. On the "Driver Info" page, provide any information about the driver who was operating the trailer at the time of the breakdown.
4. For "Safety Protocols", list any safety protocols that were followed or failed to be followed during the breakdown.
5. Next, enter any repair information on the "Repair Info" page, including details about the repairs made or actions taken.
6. If there were witnesses to the breakdown, provide their information on the "Witness Info" page.
7. Finally, use the "Notes" field for any additional information that may be relevant to the breakdown process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`General Info`, text, optional): This field is intended to capture any general information that is relevant to the breakdown process.
* **Trailer Info** (`Trailer Info`, text, required): This field requires a detailed description of the trailer, including its type, description, or other relevant information.
* **Driver Info** (`Driver Info`, text, optional): This field is meant to provide information about the driver who was operating the trailer at the time of the breakdown.
* **Safety Protocols** (`Safety Protocols`, text, optional): This field is intended to list any safety protocols that were followed or failed to be followed during the breakdown.
* **Repair Info** (`Repair Info`, text, required): This field is required to enter details about the repairs made or actions taken during the breakdown.
* **Witness Info** (`Witness Info`, text, optional): This field is meant to capture information about any witnesses to the breakdown.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information that may be relevant to the breakdown process.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, such as "Trailer Info" and "Repair Info", to ensure a thorough breakdown process.
- Be as detailed as possible when providing information in each field to aid in the breakdown process.
