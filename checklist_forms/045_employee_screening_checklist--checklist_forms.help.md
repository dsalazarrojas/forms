# Employee Screening Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and verify employee information for HR purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "New Employee" page to start filling out the form.
2. Enter the contact details of the employee in the "Contact Details" field.
3. Choose the employment status of the employee from the "Employment Status" dropdown.
4. Enter the job title of the employee.
5. Select the employment type of the employee (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Temporary).
6. Enter the employee's date of birth.
7. Enter the hire date of the employee.
8. Enter the termination date of the employee (if applicable).
