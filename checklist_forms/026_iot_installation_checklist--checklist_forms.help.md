# iot_installation_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The iot_installation_checklist form is a checklist for documenting and tracking IoT installation details. It helps to ensure that all required and optional fields are completed correctly to guarantee accurate and reliable data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to understand what information is required and optional.
2.  Enter or select the correct information for each field.
3.  Make sure to fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **site_details (text)**: Enter a brief description of the installation site.
*   **install_location (select_one)**: Select 'Yes' if the installation is complete, 'No' otherwise.
*   **install_date (date)**: Enter the date of the installation.
*   **verify_connectivity (text)**: Enter the results of verifying the installation's connectivity.
*   **attach_photos (number)**: Attach the number of photos taken during the installation.
*   **attach_notes (text)**: Attach any additional notes or comments about the installation.
*   **install_location2 (time)**: Enter the time of the installation. This field seems to be redundant with the 'install_location' field and 'install_date' field. However, it's possible that it's intended for a specific time zone or location, so it's best to fill it in according to the specific requirements. 
*   **install_location (select_one)**: This field seems to be redundant with the 'install_location2' field. However, it's possible that it's intended for a different aspect of the installation location. It's best to fill it in according to the specific requirements.
