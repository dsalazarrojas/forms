# Receiving CheckList - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Receiving CheckList form is designed to help drivers report on the condition and status of the vehicles they are receiving. This form captures relevant information about the vehicle's condition, such as its job number, delivery company, vehicle type, delivery date, and the status of its lights and beam.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the "Job Number" field and enter the correct job number for the vehicle you are receiving.
2. Select the "Delivery Company" field and enter the name of the delivery company for the vehicle.
3. Choose the type of vehicle by selecting "Vehicle Type" field from the list of options (Truck, Car, or Motorcycle).
4. Select the "Delivery Date" field and enter the date of delivery.
5. For the "Side Lights" field, select "Working" if the side lights are functioning properly or "Not Working" if they are not.
6. For the "Fog Lights" field, select "Working" if the fog lights are functioning properly or "Not Working" if they are not.
7. For the "Main Beam" field, select "Working" if the main beam is functioning properly or "Not Working" if it is not.
8. In the "Notes" field, enter any additional comments or notes about the vehicle's condition.
9. (Optional) You can enter the job number again in the "Job Number" field for confirmation.
10. (Optional) Enter the name of the delivery company in the "Delivery Company" field for confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Number** (`job_number`, number, required/optional): Enter the job number assigned to the vehicle.
* **Delivery Company** (`delivery_company`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the delivery company for the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of vehicle (Truck, Car, or Motorcycle).
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of delivery.
* **Side Lights** (`side_lights`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Working" if the side lights are functioning properly or "Not Working" if they are not.
* **Fog Lights** (`fog_lights`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Working" if the fog lights are functioning properly or "Not Working" if they are not.
* **Main Beam** (`main_beam`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Working" if the main beam is functioning properly or "Not Working" if it is not.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the vehicle's condition.

## Tips

* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* Use the "Notes" field to capture any additional details that may not fit into the other fields.
* Ensure that you have selected the correct vehicle type.
* Review your submission for accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always ask for guidance from your supervisor or the person receiving the vehicle.
