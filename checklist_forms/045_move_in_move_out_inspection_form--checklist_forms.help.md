# Move In Move Out Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to record the details of a move-in and move-out property inspection. It helps to identify any damages, issues, or items that need attention before and after the tenant moves out.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select all the areas of the property that require attention before moving in.
* Provide any additional details about the property in the "Move In Move Out Inspection Form Details" field.
* Leave comments or observations about the inspection in the "Move In Move Out Inspection Form Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Appliances** (Appliances, Select Multiple, Required: False): Check all the appliances that are not in good working condition or are missing.
* **Bedrooms** (Bedrooms, Select Multiple, Required: False): Indicate the number of bedrooms that are not in good condition or are missing.
* **Bathrooms** (Bathrooms, Select Multiple, Required: False): Check the number of bathrooms that are not in good condition or are missing.
* **Floors** (Floors, Select Multiple, Required: False): Select all the floors that are not in good condition or are missing.
* **Hallways** (Hallways, Select Multiple, Required: False): Check all the hallways that are not in good condition or are missing.
* **Kitchen** (Kitchen, Select Multiple, Required: False): Indicate the kitchen area that is not in good condition or is missing.
* **Living Room** (Living Room, Select Multiple, Required: False): Check the living room area that is not in good condition or is missing.
* **Parking** (Parking, Select Multiple, Required: False): Select all the parking areas that are not in good condition or are missing.
* **Stairs** (Stairs, Select Multiple, Required: False): Indicate the stairs that are not in good condition or are missing.
* **Yard** (Yard, Select Multiple, Required: False): Check all the yard areas that are not in good condition or are missing.
* **Move In Move Out Inspection Form Details** (Move In Move Out Inspection Form Details, Text, Required: False): Provide any additional details about the property or inspection process.
* **Move In Move Out Inspection Form Comments** (Move In Move Out Inspection Form Comments, Note, Required: False): Leave comments or observations about the inspection process or property condition.

## Tips
* Make sure to thoroughly inspect the property before and after the move-out inspection.
* Take note of any damages or issues that need attention.
* Review and update the form as needed to reflect the actual condition of the property.
* Store the form securely and make sure it is accessible to authorized personnel for future reference.
* This form is a helpful tool to ensure a smooth and conflict-free move-in and move-out process.
