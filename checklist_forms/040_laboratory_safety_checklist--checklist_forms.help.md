# Laboratory Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to check the safety aspects of a laboratory environment, ensuring the workplace is free from hazards and well-maintained.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, go through each page and select one of the provided options for each field. You do not need to complete every field, as some sections may not apply to your specific lab setting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lab Inspection** (`lab_inspection`, text, optional):
  Check the lab area for any spills, stains, or other hazards. Describe any concerns or issues you've found.
* **Equipment Maintenance** (`equipment_maintenance`, select_one, optional):
  Choose how often lab equipment is maintained:
  * Regularly: Maintenance is performed regularly as needed.
  * Occasionally: Maintenance is performed every few months or as needed.
  * Rarely: Maintenance is performed infrequently or not at all.
  * Never: Maintenance is not performed.
* **Electrical Hazards** (`electrical_hazards`, select_multiple, optional):
  Choose the electrical hazards present in the lab:
  * Check for frayed cords: Look for cords that are frayed, damaged, or worn out.
  * Check for loose connections: Verify that all electrical connections are secure and not loose.
  * Check for exposed wiring: Inspect the lab for exposed wiring or electrical components.
* **Chemical Hazards** (`chemical_hazards`, select_one, optional):
  Choose the status of chemical handling in the lab:
  * Present: Chemicals are present or used in the lab.
  * Absent: Chemicals are not used or present in the lab.
* **Fire Hazards** (`fire_hazards`, select_multiple, optional):
  Choose the fire safety status in the lab:
  * Present: Fire extinguishers or fire alarms are present and functioning.
  * Absent: Fire extinguishers or fire alarms are not present or functioning.
* **Chemical Stores** (`chemical_stores`, select_multiple, optional):
  Choose the status of chemical storage in the lab:
  * Present: Chemicals are stored properly with labels and signs.
  * Absent: Chemicals are not stored properly or have no labels and signs.
* **Chemical Labels** (`chemical_labels`, select_multiple, optional):
  Choose the status of chemical labeling in the lab:
  * Present: Chemicals are properly labeled and identified.
  * Absent: Chemicals are not labeled or identified.
* **Fire Supervision** (`fire_supervision`, select_one, optional):
  Choose the status of fire supervision in the lab:
  * Regularly: Fire drills and checks are performed regularly.
  * Occasionally: Fire drills and checks are performed occasionally.
  * Rarely: Fire drills and checks are performed rarely.
  * Never: No fire drills or checks are performed.
* **Emergency Exit Routes** (`emergency_exit_routes`, select_one, optional):
  Choose the status of emergency exit routes in the lab:
  * Present: Emergency exit routes are clearly marked and functional.
  * Absent: Emergency exit routes are not marked or functional.
* **Emergency Exit Lights** (`emergency_exit_lights`, select_one, optional):
  Choose the status of emergency exit lights in the lab:
  * Present: Emergency exit lights are working and functional.
  * Absent: Emergency exit lights are not working or functional.
* **Safety Shoes** (`safety_shoes`, select_one, optional):
  Choose the safety shoe policy in the lab:
  * Required: Safety shoes are required for all lab workers.
  * Recommended: Safety shoes are recommended but not mandatory.
  * Not Required: No safety shoes are required.
* **Lab Insurance** (`lab_insurance`, select_one, optional):
  Choose the status of lab insurance coverage:
  * Present: Lab insurance is in place and covers lab workers and equipment.
  * Absent: Lab insurance is not in place or does not cover lab workers and equipment.
* **Lab Equipment** (`lab_equipment`, select_multiple, optional):
  Choose the status of lab equipment maintenance:
  * Present: Lab equipment is well-maintained and functional.
  * Absent: Lab equipment is not well-maintained or functional.
* **Lab Safety Signs** (`lab_safety_signs`, select_multiple, optional):
  Choose the status of lab safety signs and postings:
  * Present: Lab safety signs and postings are present and clear.
  * Absent: Lab safety signs and postings are not present or unclear.

## Tips

* Be thorough and methodical when completing this form.
* Check for any issues or concerns and document them.
* Review and address any discrepancies found during the lab inspection.
