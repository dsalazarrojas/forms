# orientation_completion_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to track and document the completion of an employee's orientation process. It collects information about the introduction, completion date, facilitator, location, topics covered, employee details, and supervisor information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, marked as "required" in each field.
2. Enter the employee's introduction (if necessary).
3. Input the completion date (if completed).
4. Mention the orientation facilitator (if applicable).
5. Specify the location of the orientation session.
6. List the topics covered during the orientation (up to 10 topics).
7. Provide the employee's name and position.
8. Enter the employee's department.
9. Mention the employee's job title.
10. Input the supervisor's name and department.
11. Specify the supervisor's position and job title (if applicable).
12. Mention any additional notes regarding the orientation completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief description of the employee's introduction during the orientation session.
* **Completion Date**: The date when the employee completed the orientation.
* **Completion Time**: The time when the employee completed the orientation.
* **Orientation Facilitator**: The person who led the orientation session.
* **Orientation Location**: The location where the orientation session took place.
* **Orientation Topic 1-10**: List the topics covered during the orientation session.
* **Employee Name**: The name of the employee who attended the orientation.
* **Employee Position**: The position of the employee who attended the orientation.
* **Department**: The department of the employee who attended the orientation.
* **Job Title**: The job title of the employee who attended the orientation.
* **Supervisor Name**: The name of the employee's supervisor.
* **Supervisor Department**: The department of the employee's supervisor.
* **Supervisor Position**: The position of the employee's supervisor.
* **Supervisor Job Title**: The job title of the employee's supervisor.
* **Orientation Completed By**: The person who marked the orientation as completed.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or notes regarding the orientation completion.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If a field is not applicable, please mark it as "not applicable" or leave it blank.
