# WHMIS Compliance Program Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help organizations evaluate and improve their WHMIS (Workplace Health and Safety) Compliance Program. It covers various aspects of WHMIS compliance, including training management, hazard identification, hazard control, incident reporting, and continuous improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question based on your organization's current practices and policies.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. Use the form as a guide to identify areas for improvement and implement changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **WHMIS Compliance Program Checklist** (Page 1): This page is a general introduction to the form.
* **What is the scope of your WHMIS program?** (Page 2): Select all the aspects of WHMIS compliance that your program covers, such as WHMIS Training, Hazard Identification, Hazard Prevention, Hazard Recognition, Hazard Evaluation, Hazard Control, and Record Keeping.
* **Training Management** (Page 3): Select the person responsible for training management, which can be a Manager, Supervisor, or Worker.
* **How often are WHMIS training sessions held?** (Page 4): Select how frequently WHMIS training sessions are held, such as Annually, Bi-Annually, Quarterly, or Monthly.
* **Instructor Qualifications for WHMIS training** (Page 5): This field is optional, but if you have any specific qualifications or requirements for WHMIS instructors, please provide them.
* **What WHMIS training materials are used?** (Page 6): Select the materials used for WHMIS training, such as PowerPoint, Video, Manual, or Interactive.
* **How is WHMIS training evaluated?** (Page 7): Select who evaluates WHMIS training, which can be a Manager, Supervisor, or Worker.
* **What is your current recordkeeping method for WHMIS records?** (Page 8): Select the method used to keep WHMIS records, such as Manual or Electronic.
* **When are WHMIS records reviewed?** (Page 9): Select how frequently WHMIS records are reviewed, such as Annually, Bi-Annually, Quarterly, or Monthly.
* **How do you identify hazards on the job site?** (Page 10): Select who is responsible for identifying hazards, which can be Supervisors, Employees, or both.
* **What are your control methods for identified hazards?** (Page 11): Select the methods used to control hazards, such as Engineering, Personal protective equipment (PPE), Administrative, or a combination of both.
* **How do you recognize and evaluate hazards on the job site?** (Page 12): Select who is responsible for recognizing and evaluating hazards, which can be Supervisors, Employees, or both.
* **Incident Reporting Procedure** (Page 13): This field is optional, but if you have any specific incident reporting procedures, please describe them.
* **Are follow-up actions taken after incidents?** (Page 14): Select who is responsible for taking follow-up actions, which can be a Manager, Supervisor, or Worker.
* **Is continuous improvement performed on your WHMIS program?** (Page 15): Select who is responsible for continuous improvement, which can be a Manager, Supervisor, or Worker.
* **Other** (Page 16): This field is optional, but if you have any additional information or comments about your WHMIS program, please provide them.

## Tips

* Please review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Use this form as a guide to identify areas for improvement and implement changes.
* Consult with local authorities or regulatory experts if you have any doubts or concerns.
