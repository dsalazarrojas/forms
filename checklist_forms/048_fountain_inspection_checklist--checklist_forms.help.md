# # Fountain Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Fountain Inspection Checklist" form is designed to help fountain operators and maintenance personnel inspect and evaluate the condition of a fountain, covering various aspects such as safety, maintenance, and water quality. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose and how to complete it effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form's sections and fields to understand what information is required.
2. Select the relevant options for each field based on your observations during the inspection.
3. Fill in the fields with the selected options, making sure to provide accurate and relevant information.
4. Take photos of any notable issues or observations, and attach them to the "Photos" field.
5. Include any special comments or additional information in the "Special Comments" field.
6. Record the water flow rate and inspection date in the respective fields.
7. Enter the inspector's name and any general comments in the "General Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Routine Checks** (`routine_checks`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether the following routine checks were performed: Yes or No.
- **Safety Issues** (`safety_issues`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether any safety issues were found: Yes or No.
- **Maintenance Needs** (`maintenance_needs`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether any maintenance needs were identified: Yes or No.
- **Routine Maintenance** (`routine_maintenance`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether routine maintenance was performed: Yes or No.
- **Special Equipment** (`special_equipment`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether any special equipment is in need of attention: Yes or No.
- **Water Quality** (`water_quality`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the water quality: Good or Poor.
- **Water Flow** (`water_flow`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether there is adequate water flow: Yes or No.
- **Basin Condition** (`basin_condition`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether the basin condition is satisfactory: Yes or No.
- **Surrounding Area** (`surrounding_area`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether the surrounding area is safe: Yes or No.
- **General Comments** (`general_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any general comments about the inspection.
- **Photos** (`photos`, `note`, required: false): Attach photos of any notable issues or observations.
- **Special Comments** (`special_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any special comments or additional information.
- **Water Flow Rate** (`water_flow_rate`, `number`, required: false): Record the water flow rate.
- **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, required: false): Record the date of the inspection.
- **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and role as the inspector.

## Tips
- Always fill in all required fields accurately.
- Take clear and detailed photos of any notable issues.
- Use the "General Comments" field to enter any general information or observations.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
