# Cottage Safety Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cottage Safety Inspection Checklist is a form designed for inspecting the safety of a cottage. It covers various safety aspects of the cottage, including electrical, plumbing, fire, and general safety precautions. This form helps to identify potential hazards and areas that require attention to ensure the comfort and safety of guests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the form, checking the options that best describe your findings in each area.
2. For each section, carefully select one of the provided options that match your assessment.
3. Complete all mandatory fields (marked as "required") by providing a detailed answer.
4. Submit the completed form to the designated authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Safety Precautions** (`safety_precautions`, text, required)
Enter any safety measures taken to minimize the risk of accidents or injuries in the cottage.

### 2. **Emergency Exits** (`emergency_exits`, text, required)
Identify any emergency exits in the cottage. Please ensure that they lead outside the cottage but not through a staircase or exterior door that leads back into the same area as the entrance.

### 3. **Electrical Safety** (`electrical-safety-checks`, select_one, optional)
Select one of the options provided to describe the condition of electrical safety checks in the cottage:
	* No issues with electrical
	* Some issues with electrical
	* Major electrical issues

### 4. **Plumbing Safety** (`plumbing-safety-checks`, select_one, optional)
Select one of the options provided to describe the condition of plumbing safety checks in the cottage:
	* No issues with plumbing
	* Some issues with plumbing
	* Major plumbing issues

### 5. **Fire Safety** (`fire_safety`, select_one, optional)
Select one of the options provided to describe the condition of fire safety in the cottage:
	* No issues with fire safety
	* Some issues with fire safety
	* Major fire safety issues

### 6. **Heating/Cooling** (`heating_cooling`, select_one, optional)
Select one of the options provided to describe the condition of heating and cooling in the cottage:
	* No issues with heating/cooling
	* Some issues with heating/cooling
	* Major heating/cooling issues

### 7. **Emergency Exits Adjacent** (`emergency_exits_adjacent`, select_one, optional)
Check whether there are emergency exits adjacent to the cottage:
	* Yes
	* No

### 8. **Fire Extinguisher** (`fire_extinguishers`, text, required)
Enter the number and type of fire extinguishers available in the cottage.

## Tips
- Be thorough in inspecting the safety aspects of the cottage.
- Ensure all mandatory fields are completed accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the authorities or seek further guidance.
