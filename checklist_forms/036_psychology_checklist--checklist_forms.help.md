# Psychology Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Psychology Checklist is a clinical form used to evaluate the mental health and wellness of individuals. It assesses the severity of a patient's psychological condition and provides a basis for creating a treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and ensure you understand the questions and options provided.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately, considering the severity of the patient's psychological condition.
3. Select the most appropriate options for each question, considering the patient's symptoms and experiences.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed, as indicated by the "required" label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment** (Select multiple options): This question assesses the patient's current mental state. Select all options that apply to the patient's condition.
* **Diagnosis** (Select one option): This question determines the severity of the patient's psychological condition. Select the option that best describes the patient's condition, from Mild to Severe.
* **Treatment Plan** (Text): Provide a brief description of the treatment plan for the patient, including any necessary steps or recommendations.
* **Education** (Select multiple options): This question evaluates the patient's educational background and any relevant training or certifications. Select all options that apply to the patient's education.
* **Quality Assurance** (Select one option): This question assesses the patient's quality assurance, or the level of confidence they have in their own abilities. Select the option that best describes the patient's quality assurance, from Formal to None.
* **Assessment Plan** (Text): Provide a detailed description of the assessment plan for the patient, including any necessary steps or procedures.
* **Follow-Up** (Select multiple options): This question determines the frequency and duration of follow-up appointments or check-ins. Select all options that apply to the patient's follow-up needs.
* **Evaluation** (Select multiple options): This question assesses the patient's overall evaluation, or their subjective experience of their condition. Select all options that apply to the patient's evaluation.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Answer each question honestly and accurately, considering the patient's symptoms and experiences.
* Review and revise the form as necessary to ensure accurate and comprehensive assessment of the patient's psychological condition.
* Consult with other medical professionals as needed to ensure a thorough and accurate assessment.
