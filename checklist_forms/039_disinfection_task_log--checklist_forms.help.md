# Disinfection Task Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track details of disinfection tasks performed to sanitize surfaces, equipment, or environments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the date and time of the disinfection task from the calendar or date picker.
2. Enter the location where the disinfection task was performed.
3. Choose the type of disinfection method used (Chemical Disinfection, Steam Disinfection, or Other).
4. Select the chemicals used during the task.
5. Choose the staff member(s) who performed the task.
6. Enter any comments or notes about the task.
7. Select the status of the task (Completed, In Progress, or Not Started).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date and time** (1): Select the date and time of the disinfection task. This field is optional, but it helps to track the timing of the task.
* **Location** (2): Enter the area where the disinfection task was performed, such as a room, equipment, or surface.
* **Disinfection type** (3): Choose the type of disinfection method used, such as Chemical Disinfection, Steam Disinfection, or Other.
* **Chemicals used** (4): Select the chemicals used during the task, such as Chemical 1, Chemical 2, or Chemical 3.
* **Staff details** (5): Choose the staff member(s) who performed the task, such as Employee 1, Employee 2, or Employee 3.
* **Comments** (6): Enter any additional comments or notes about the task, such as challenges faced or observations.
* **Signatures** (7): Select the staff member(s) who signed off on the task, such as Signature 1, Signature 2, or Signature 3.
* **Staff comments** (8): Enter any comments from staff members about the task, such as feedback or suggestions.
* **Staff signatures** (9): Select the staff member(s) who signed off on the task, such as signature 1, signature 2, or signature 3.
* **Chemicals required** (10): Choose the chemicals required for the task, such as Chemical 1, Chemical 2, or Chemical 3.
* **Disinfection status** (11): Select the status of the task, such as Completed, In Progress, or Not Started.
* **Notes** (12): Enter any additional notes or observations about the task, such as future actions or next steps.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and legible information to ensure that the data is tracked correctly.
* If you are unsure which form to use or have any questions, please reach out to your supervisor.
* This form is designed to be used by multiple users, so make sure to save your progress regularly and avoid conflicts.
