# road_trip_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The road_trip_checklist_form is a comprehensive checklist designed to gather information about your road trip plans. It helps you stay organized and ensures you're prepared for a safe and enjoyable journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and ensure you've answered all required questions.
2. Select the relevant options for each field.
3. Provide accurate and complete information in each field.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Travel Planner** (`travel_planner`, select_multiple, required): Select the travel planning method for your trip (Car Insurance, Driving License, Roadside Assistance).
- **Vehicle Info** (`vehicle_info`, text, required): Enter your vehicle's information, such as make, model, and year.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, required): Choose your emergency contact person.
- **Accommodation Info** (`accommodation_info`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of accommodation you plan to use (Hotel, Motel).
- **Travel Documents** (`travel_documents`, select_multiple, required): Select the travel documents you'll need (Passport, Visa, Driver's License).
- **Travel Planning** (`travel_planning`, select_multiple, required): Select the mode of transportation for your trip (Flight, Train, Bus).
- **Road Trip Checklist** (`road_trip_checklist_form_road_trip_checklist_form`, select_multiple, required): Select the items you'll need for your road trip (Vehicle Check, Emergency Kit, First Aid Kit).
- **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, required): Enter the start and end dates of your trip.
- **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, time, required): Enter the travel start and end times.
- **Travel Place** (`travel_place`, select_one, required): Choose the destination of your trip.
- **Travel Mode** (`travel_mode`, select_one, required): Select the mode of transportation for your trip (Car, Bus, Train).
- **Travel Distance** (`travel_distance`, number, optional): Enter the distance of your trip.
- **Travel Time Duration** (`travel_time_duration`, time, optional): Enter the duration of your trip.
- **Travel Cost** (`travel_cost`, number, optional): Enter the estimated cost of your trip.
- **Travel Notes** (`travel_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your trip.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of vehicle you'll be using (Sedan, SUV, Truck).
- **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of your vehicle.
- **Vehicle License Plate** (`vehicle_license_plate`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle's license plate number.
- **Vehicle Insurance** (`vehicle_insurance`, select_one, optional): Select the type of vehicle insurance you have (Comprehensive, Collision).
