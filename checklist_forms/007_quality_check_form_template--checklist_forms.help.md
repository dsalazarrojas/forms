# Quality Check Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a product's condition and quality, allowing for the inspection and evaluation of its quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the product information in the "Product info" field.
2. Check if there are any defects on the product and select "Yes" or "No" in the "Defects" field.
3. Provide a quality assessment of the product in the "Quality Assessment" field.
4. Rate the product's quality in the "Quality Rating" field, selecting either "Good" or "Poor".
5. Optionally, add any comments about the product in the "Comments" field.
6. Sign and date the form as the inspector.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product info** (product_info, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the product being inspected.
* **Defects** (defects, select_one, required: false): Check if the product has any defects or issues.
* **Quality Assessment** (quality_assessment, text, required: false): Provide a detailed evaluation of the product's quality.
* **Quality Rating** (quality_rating, select_one, required: false): Rate the product's quality as either "Good" or "Poor".
* **Comments** (comments, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the product.
* **Inspector's Signature** (signature, text, required: false): Sign and date the form as the inspector.
* **Date Submitted** (date_submitted, date, required: false): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Time Submitted** (time_submitted, time, required: false): Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Assigned to** (assigned_to, select_one, required: false): Check if the product is assigned to anyone or anything.
