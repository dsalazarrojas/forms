# RBT Fidelity Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RBT Fidelity Checklist is a tool used to assess the fidelity of a Return to Base (RBT) process, a systematic approach to reviewing and refining a process or system to ensure it meets predefined standards and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and fill in the relevant information as prompted.
2. Be sure to answer all required fields, as indicated by the required label.
3. Use the provided space for comments or notes to elaborate on your answers when necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Initial Assessment** (`rbt_fidelity_checklist_1`, text, required/optional): This is the first section of the checklist where you will start by assessing the initial state of the RBT process. Provide a brief description of the process as it currently stands.
* **Plan Implementation** (`rbt_fidelity_checklist_2`, text, required/optional): In this section, describe the plan for implementing any changes or improvements identified in the initial assessment.
* **Data Collection** (`rbt_fidelity_checklist_3`, text, required/optional): This section is for collecting and documenting any data or information related to the RBT process, such as metrics or statistics.
* **Behavior Plan** (`rbt_fidelity_checklist_4`, text, required/optional): Here, you will outline the behavioral aspects of the RBT process, including any changes or improvements to be implemented to address specific behaviors.
* **RBT Review and Revision** (`rbt_fidelity_checklist_5`, text, required/optional): This section is for documenting any revisions or updates made to the RBT process based on the assessment and plan.
* **RBT Signature** (`rbt_fidelity_checklist_6`, text, required/optional): In this section, you will sign off on the revised RBT process, indicating that you have reviewed and approved it.
* **Date and Time** (`rbt_fidelity_checklist_7`, date, required/optional): Record the date and time of the RBT review and revision process.
* **RBT Comments** (`rbt_fidelity_checklist_8`, text, required/optional): Provide any comments or feedback on the RBT process in this section.
* **Assessor Comments** (`rbt_fidelity_checklist_9`, text, required/optional): This section is for the assessor to provide any comments or feedback on the RBT process.
* **Assessor Signature** (`rbt_fidelity_checklist_10`, text, required/optional): The assessor will sign off on the revised RBT process, indicating that they have reviewed and approved it.

## Tips
* Be sure to review each section carefully and fill in the relevant information as prompted.
* Use the provided space for comments or notes to elaborate on your answers when necessary.
* Remember to sign off on the revised RBT process after completing all sections.
* If you are unsure about any field or section, don't hesitate to ask for clarification or assistance.
