# retail_store_closing_procedures_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to guide retail store employees through the closing procedures at the end of each day. It helps ensure that all necessary tasks are completed, and any incidents that occur are properly documented and reported.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the "end of day tasks" field with a list of tasks that were completed during the day.
2. For each task, indicate whether it was completed or not by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "tasks completed" field.
3. Use the "notes" field to add any additional comments or observations about the day's events.
4. If an incident occurred during the day, report it in the "incidents" field.
5. If you reported an incident, provide more details about it in the "incident details", "incident cause", and "incident resolution" fields.
6. Specify the incident location and time in the "incident location" and "incident time" fields, respectively.
7. Enter the date the store closed in the "closing date" field.
8. Provide the name of the store and its location in the "store name" and "location name" fields, respectively.
9. Indicate the status of the incident by selecting "Active" or "Inactive" in the "incident status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **end of day tasks**: List the tasks completed during the day. (Text, required)
* **tasks completed**: Indicate whether each task was completed or not. (Select multiple, optional)
* **notes**: Add any additional comments or observations about the day's events. (Text, optional)
* **incidents**: Report any incidents that occurred during the day. (Text, required)
* **incident details**: Provide more details about the incident that occurred. (Text, optional)
* **incident cause**: Specify the cause of the incident. (Text, optional)
* **incident resolution**: Describe how the incident was resolved. (Text, optional)
* **incident location**: Specify the location where the incident occurred. (Text, optional)
* **incident time**: Specify the time the incident occurred. (Time, required)
* **incident status**: Indicate the status of the incident, either "Active" or "Inactive". (Select multiple, optional)
* **store name**: Enter the name of the store. (Text, required)
* **location name**: Enter the name of the store's location. (Text, required)
* **closing date**: Enter the date the store closed. (Date, required)
* **prophets**: Select the correct option for this field. (Select one, optional)
* **incident resolution**: (this field seems to be a duplicate, please ignore or rename this field)
