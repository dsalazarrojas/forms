# Safety Meeting Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Meeting Checklist is a form used to document the details of safety meetings. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and easily accessible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate response for each field based on the safety meeting's specifics.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Fill out the form as thoroughly as possible.
4. Submit the form after completing all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Safety Meeting Checklist** (checklist_form_1, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the items that were covered in the safety meeting.
* **Checklist Items** (checklist_form_2, select_multiple, required: false): List all the items that were covered in the second part of the safety meeting.
* **Meeting Notes** (meeting_notes_1, text, required: false): Record any important notes from the meeting.
* **Meeting Leader** (meeting_leader_1, select_one, required: false): Choose the leader of the safety meeting.
* **Date** (date_1, date, required: false): Enter the date of the safety meeting.
* **Time** (time_1, time, required: false): Enter the time of the safety meeting.
* **Location** (location_1, text, required: false): Enter the location of the safety meeting.
* **Attendees** (attendees_1, select_multiple, required: false): List the attendees of the safety meeting.
* **Alternate Meeting Leader** (meeting_leader_2, select_one, required: false): Choose an alternate leader for the safety meeting.
* **Checklist Items 2** (checklist_form_2, select_multiple, required: false): List the items covered in the second part of the safety meeting.
* **Re-meet Date** (date_2, date, required: false): Enter the date for the next safety meeting.
* **Re-meet Time** (time_2, time, required: false): Enter the time for the next safety meeting.
* **Checklist Items 3** (checklist_form_3, select_multiple, required: false): List the items covered in the third part of the safety meeting.
* **Alternate Meeting Leader 2** (meeting_leader_3, select_one, required: false): Choose an alternate leader for the next safety meeting.
* **Alternate Attendees** (attendees_2, select_multiple, required: false): List the alternate attendees for the next safety meeting.
* **Next Meet Date** (date_3, date, required: false): Enter the date for the next safety meeting.
* **Next Meet Time** (time_3, time, required: false): Enter the time for the next safety meeting.
* **Next Meet Location** (location_2, text, required: false): Enter the location of the next safety meeting.
* **Next Meet Leader** (next_meet_leader, select_one, required: false): Choose the leader for the next safety meeting.
* **Next Meet Attendees** (next_meet_attendees, select_multiple, required: false): List the attendees for the next safety meeting.
* **Next Meet Location Alt** (next_meet_location_alt, text, required: false): Enter the alternate location for the next safety meeting.
* **Next Meet Leader Alt** (next_meet_leader_alt, select_one, required: false): Choose an alternate leader for the next safety meeting.

## Tips
- Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are filled out.
- Double-check the information for accuracy.
- Save a copy of the form for future reference.
- If you need clarification on any field, consult with the person in charge of the meeting or your supervisor.
