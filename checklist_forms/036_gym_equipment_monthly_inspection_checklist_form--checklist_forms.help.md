# Gym Equipment Monthly Inspection Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Equipment Monthly Inspection Checklist Form is used to inspect and report the status of various gym equipment on a monthly basis. This form helps identify any issues with the equipment, ensure that all equipment is in good working condition, and prevent accidents or injuries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the equipment type that you are inspecting from the provided options.
2. Choose the status of the equipment from the options provided (good, needs attention, or defective).
3. Enter any additional comments or notes about the equipment in the "Notes" field.
4. Submit the form to complete the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pump** (`pump`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the status of the pump equipment you are inspecting. Select one of the options: good, needs attention, or defective.
* **cardio_equipment** (`cardio_equipment`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the status of the cardio equipment you are inspecting. Choose one or more of the options: good, needs attention, or defective.
* **electrical_equipment** (`electrical_equipment`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the status of the electrical equipment you are inspecting. Select one of the options: good, needs attention, or defective.
* **air_equipment** (`air_equipment`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the status of the air equipment you are inspecting. Select one of the options: good, needs attention, or defective.
* **weights_machine_equipment** (`weights_machine_equipment`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the status of the weights machine equipment you are inspecting. Select one of the options: good, needs attention, or defective.
* **free_weight_equipment** (`free_weight_equipment`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the status of the free weight equipment you are inspecting. Choose one or more of the options: good, needs attention, or defective.
* **other_equipment** (`other_equipment`, `text`, required: false): Enter a description of any other equipment you are inspecting.
* **notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the equipment in the "Notes" field.

## Tips
* Be thorough when inspecting the equipment and choosing the status from the options.
* Make sure to enter any additional comments or notes if you find any issues with the equipment.
* Complete this form regularly to maintain a safe and well-maintained gym environment.
