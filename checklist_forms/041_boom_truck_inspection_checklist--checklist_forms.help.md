# Boom Truck Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the results of a boom truck inspection. The inspection checks various aspects of the vehicle's condition, including physical state, mechanical functioning, and any defects or issues found.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each field to select or enter the relevant information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
3. Use the hints provided for each field to ensure you are entering or selecting the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of this inspection.
* **Truck ID or License Plate**: Enter the vehicle identification number or license plate number.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of this inspection.
* **Current Mileage**: Enter the current odometer reading.
* **Boom Arm Condition**: Select the overall condition of the boom arm:
	+ Excellent: The boom arm is in excellent condition with no visible signs of wear or damage.
	+ Good: The boom arm is in good condition with minor signs of wear or damage.
	+ Fair: The boom arm is in fair condition with noticeable signs of wear or damage.
	+ Poor: The boom arm is in poor condition with significant signs of wear or damage.
	+ Damaged: The boom arm has some level of damage or wear.
	+ Needs repair: The boom arm requires immediate repair or replacement.
* **Boom Extension Test**: Select the condition of the boom extension mechanism:
	+ Functional: The extension mechanism is working properly.
	+ Slow: The extension mechanism is slow to operate.
	+ Not working: The extension mechanism is not functioning.
	+ Needs maintenance: The extension mechanism requires regular maintenance.
* **Hydraulic System**: Select the condition of the hydraulic system:
	+ Normal: The hydraulic system is functioning properly.
	+ Low pressure: The hydraulic system is experiencing low pressure.
	+ Leaking: The hydraulic system is leaking.
	+ Damaged hoses: The hydraulic system has damaged hoses.
	+ Needs service: The hydraulic system requires service.
* **Emergency Stop Button**: Select the condition of the emergency stop button:
	+ Working: The emergency stop button is functioning properly.
	+ Sluggish: The emergency stop button is slow to respond.
	+ Not working: The emergency stop button is not functioning.
	+ Needs repair: The emergency stop button requires immediate repair.
* **Safety Restraint System**: Select the condition of the safety restraints:
	+ Good condition: The safety restraints are in good condition.
	+ Minor wear: The safety restraints have some minor wear.
	+ Torn or damaged: The safety restraints are torn or damaged.
	+ Needs replacement: The safety restraints require immediate replacement.
* **Light System**: Select the condition of the light system:
	+ All working: All lights are functioning properly.
	+ Some not working: Some lights are not functioning.
	+ Major issues: There are major issues with the light system.
	+ Needs repair: The light system requires repair or replacement.
* **Tire Condition**: Select the condition of the tires:
	+ Good: The tires are in good condition.
	+ Fair: The tires are in fair condition.
	+ Worn: The tires are worn out.
	+ Needs replacement: The tires require immediate replacement.
	+ Flat or damaged: The tires are flat or damaged.
* **Brake System**: Select the condition of the brake system:
	+ Good: The brake system is functioning properly.
	+ Fair: The brake system is experiencing some wear or issues.
	+ Poor: The brake system is in poor condition.
	+ Needs service: The brake system requires service.
	+ Dangerous: The brake system is hazardous or dangerous.
* **Defects or Issues Found**: Enter any defects or issues found during the inspection.
* **Inspection Result**: Select the overall inspection result:
	+ Pass: The vehicle passed the inspection with no issues.
	+ Pass with minor notes: The vehicle passed the inspection with some minor issues.
	+ Conditional pass: The vehicle passed the inspection with some conditions.
	+ Fail - repair needed: The vehicle failed the inspection and requires repair.
	+ Fail - unsafe: The vehicle failed the inspection and is unsafe to operate.
