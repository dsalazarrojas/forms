# Recruitment Process Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Process Checklist is a form designed to guide hiring managers and recruiters through the various steps involved in the recruitment process. It ensures that all necessary steps are taken and that the process is completed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in each field accurately and comprehensively.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have not missed any steps.
3. Use the options provided as guidance for your answers.
4. Save your changes before navigating away from the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Apply For This Opportunity** (`applicant_submission`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter the details of the applicant submission, including the job title, job description, and any other relevant information.
* **Resume Submission** (`resume_submission`, text, optional): This field is for entering the details of the resume submission, including any relevant information about the applicant's qualifications and experience.
* **Interview Scheduling** (`interview_scheduling`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if an interview has been scheduled or 'No' if not.
* **Candidate Screening** (`candidate_screening`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the relevant criteria for candidate screening, such as background checks, reference checks, etc.
* **Job Offer Made** (`job_offer`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if a job offer has been made or 'No' if not.
* **Hire Decision** (`hire_decision`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if a hire decision has been made or 'No' if not.
* **Onboarding** (`onboarding`, note, optional): Take notes on the onboarding process, including any relevant dates, times, and locations.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments on the recruitment process.
* **Notes** (`notes`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the relevant notes, such as any additional information or concerns.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you have contact information for the applicant or 'No' if not.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the relevant communication channels used, such as email, phone, etc.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the next steps to be taken, such as follow-up interviews or further testing.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the recruitment process, such as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if follow-up is required or 'No' if not.
* **Outcome** (`outcome`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if the outcome of the recruitment process is positive or 'No' if not.

## Tips
* Always save your changes before navigating away from the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please seek clarification from the relevant stakeholders.
* This form is intended to be used for tracking and documenting the recruitment process. Please ensure that you complete it accurately and comprehensively.
