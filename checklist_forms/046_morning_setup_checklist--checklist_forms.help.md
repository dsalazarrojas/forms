# Morning Setup Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Morning Setup Checklist is a form designed to help users set up their morning routine effectively. It collects information about their start time, task list, task status, and any issues or goals they need to address during the day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your start time for the day, indicating when you plan to begin your morning routine.
* Select your morning tasks that you plan to accomplish, choosing 'Yes' for tasks you've completed and 'No' for those you haven't.
* Choose the status of your morning tasks, indicating whether they're 'Morning Ready' (completed), 'Not Ready' (in progress or incomplete), or 'Late' (overdue).
* Describe any issues or goals you have for the day in the 'Issue or Goal' field, providing detailed information to help you stay focused.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Start Time
**Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required/optional): Enter your planned start time for the day.

### Morning Task List
**Morning Task List** (`morning_task_list`, select multiple, required/optional): Select 'Yes' for tasks you've completed and 'No' for those you haven't. This will help you keep track of your progress.

### Morning Task Status
**Morning Task Status** (`morning_task_status`, select one, required/optional): Choose the status of your morning tasks: 'Morning Ready' for completed tasks, 'Not Ready' for incomplete tasks, or 'Late' for overdue tasks.

### Issue or Goal
**Issue or Goal** (`issue_or_goal`, note, required/optional): Describe any issues or goals you need to address during the day. This field is for providing detailed information to help you stay focused and on track.
