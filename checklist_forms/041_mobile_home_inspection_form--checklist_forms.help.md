# Mobile Home Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile Home Inspection Form is a tool used for assessing the structural and functional integrity of mobile homes. This form gathers information about the home's address, type, size, layout, and various systems, including electrical, plumbing, and gas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the home's address in the "Home Address" field.
2. Select the type of mobile home, whether it's a single, double, triple, or quad unit in the "Mobile Home Type" field.
3. Choose the size of the home, whether it's small, medium, or large in the "Home Size" field.
4. Describe the home's structure in the "Home Structure" field.
5. Describe the home's layout in the "Home Layout" field.
6. Enter the total number of units in the "Total Units" field.
7. Enter the total square footage of the home in the "Total Sq Ft" field.
8. Select the type of roof, whether it's asphalt, metal, wood, or tile in the "Roof Type" field.
9. Describe the age of the roof in the "Roof Age" field.
10. Select the type of mobile home, whether it's single, double, triple, or quad unit in the "Mobile Home Type" field.
11. Describe the electrical system in the "Electrical System" field.
12. Describe the plumbing system in the "Plumbing System" field.
13. Describe the sewer type in the "Sewer Type" field.
14. Describe the water supply system in the "Water Supply" field.
15. Describe the waste disposal system in the "Waste Disposal" field.
16. Describe the fire suppression system in the "Fire System" field.
17. Describe the type of electrical outlet in the "Electrical Outlet Type" field.
18. Enter the total number of electrical outlets in the "Electrical Outlet Count" field.
19. Describe the location of the electrical outlets in the "Electrical Outlet Location" field.
20. Enter the total number of gas outlets in the "Gas Outlets" field.
21. Describe the type of gas outlet in the "Gas Outlet Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter the complete address of the mobile home, including the street address and any additional relevant details.
* **Home Type** (`home_type`, text, required): Describe the type of mobile home, whether it's single, double, triple, or quad unit.
* **Home Size** (`home_size`, select_one, required): Choose the size of the home, whether it's small, medium, or large.
* **Home Structure** (`home_structure`, text, required): Describe the structure of the home, including its foundation and any other relevant details.
* **Home Layout** (`home_layout`, text, required): Describe the layout of the home, including the number of bedrooms and bathrooms.
* **Total Units** (`total_units`, number, required): Enter the total number of units in the home.
* **Total Sq Ft** (`total_sqft`, number, required): Enter the total square footage of the home.
* **Roof Type** (`roof_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of roof, whether it's asphalt, metal, wood, or tile.
* **Roof Age** (`roof_age`, text, required): Describe the age of the roof.
* **Mobile Home Type** (`mobile_home_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of mobile home, whether it's single, double, triple, or quad unit.
* **Electrical System** (`electrical_system`, text, required): Describe the electrical system, including the type and age of the components.
* **Plumbing System** (`plumbing_system`, text, required): Describe the plumbing system, including the type and age of the components.
* **Sewer Type** (`sewer_type`, text, required): Describe the type of sewer system, including its age and condition.
* **Water Supply** (`water_supply`, text, required): Describe the water supply system, including the source and age of the components.
* **Waste Disposal** (`waste_disposal`, text, required): Describe the waste disposal system, including the type and age of the components.
* **Fire System** (`fire_system`, text, required): Describe the fire suppression system, including the type and age of the components.
* **Electrical Outlet Type** (`electrical_outlet_type`, text, required): Describe the type of electrical outlet, including its location and condition.
* **Electrical Outlet Count** (`electrical_outlet_count`, text, required): Enter the total number of electrical outlets.
* **Electrical Outlet Location** (`electrical_outlet_location`, text, required): Describe the location of the electrical outlets.
* **Gas Outlets** (`gas_outlets`, text, required): Enter the total number of gas outlets.
* **Gas Outlet Type** (`gas_outlet_type`, text, required): Describe the type of gas outlet, including its location and condition.
