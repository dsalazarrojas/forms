# plumbing_inspection_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plumbing Inspection Checklist form is designed for plumbers and inspection teams to conduct thorough and standardized inspections of various aspects of a plumbing system. This form helps them to identify potential issues and ensure that the system is functioning properly and safely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the applicable items from the Plumbing Inspection Checklist list. You can choose one or more options from the list.
2. Enter the Inspection Date and Inspection Time for the inspection. These fields are optional but help in recording the inspection details.
3. Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to save your inspection report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Plumbing Inspection Checklist
* **Plumbing Inspection Checklist** (`plumbing_inspection_checklist`, select_multiple, required): This is a list of items to be inspected. Select all the applicable items from the list to indicate that they have been inspected.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection. This helps in tracking the inspection history.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the inspection. This helps in recording the time taken for the inspection.

## Tips
- Make sure to select all the relevant items from the Plumbing Inspection Checklist to ensure a thorough inspection.
- If you are using this form for future reference, make sure to save the inspection report and update it accordingly.
- In case of any discrepancies, report them to the relevant authorities for further action.
