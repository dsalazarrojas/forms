# Apartment Comparison Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Apartment Comparison Checklist is designed to help you compare two apartments and make an informed decision based on their features and details.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling out the searcher's information section, which includes your name and search start date.
2.  For each apartment, fill out the following fields:

    *   **Apartment name**: The official name of the apartment building or property.
    *   **Address**: The full address of the apartment building or property.
    *   **Monthly Rent**: The base rent amount for the apartment.
    *   **Security Deposit**: The deposit required for the apartment.
    *   **Number of Bedrooms**: The number of bedrooms in the apartment.
    *   **Number of Bathrooms**: The number of bathrooms in the apartment.
    *   **Square Feet**: The total area of the apartment.
    *   **Amenities**: Select all the amenities that are included in the apartment, such as in-unit laundry, dishwasher, air conditioning, etc.
    *   **Building Amenities**: Select all the building amenities, such as fitness center, pool, parking, etc.
    *   **Utilities Included**: Select all the utilities that are included in the rent, such as water, sewer, etc.
    *   **Pet Policy**: Whether pets are allowed in the apartment.
    *   **Lease Term**: The length of the lease.
    *   **Availability Date**: The date when you can move in.
    *   **Condition Rating**: Rate the condition of the apartment, such as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
    *   **Notes**: Additional observations or comments about the apartment.
3.  For the second apartment, repeat the same fields as above.
4.  Once you have filled out both apartments, compare your answers and note your thoughts on the overall comparison.
5.  Select your preferred choice and the key decision factors that mattered most in your decision.
