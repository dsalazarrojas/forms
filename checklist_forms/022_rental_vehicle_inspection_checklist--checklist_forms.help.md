# Rental Vehicle Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Vehicle Inspection Checklist is designed to help assess the condition of a rental vehicle before or after a trip. This form is intended to be filled out by the vehicle inspector or owner to identify any issues or damages and provide a thorough report of the vehicle's state.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the vehicle carefully and answer each question accurately based on what you observe.
2. For each field, select the option that best describes the vehicle's condition.
3. Make sure to fill out all fields that are marked as "required" to ensure that the form is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Condition at Return** (`vehicle_condition_at_return`, Note, optional): This field is a note field for the condition of the vehicle upon return. You can add any relevant comments or information about the vehicle's condition in this field.

* **Vehicle Condition at Pickup** (`vehicle_condition_at_pickup`, Note, optional): This field is a note field for the condition of the vehicle at pickup. You can add any relevant comments or information about the vehicle's condition in this field.

* **Exterior** (`exterior`, Text, optional): Describe any scratches, dents, or other damage on the vehicle's body and wheels.

* **Interior** (`interior`, Text, optional): Describe any cleanliness or conditions of the vehicle's interior, such as stains, odors, or trash.

* **Dashboard** (`dashboard`, Select One, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ **goals**: The dashboard is in good condition.
	+ **no damage**: The dashboard is in normal condition with no damage.
	+ **scratches**: The dashboard has scratches.

* **Odometer** (`odometer`, Number, optional): Record the vehicle's odometer reading.

* **Engine** (`engine`, Select Multiple, optional): Check the engine for any issues and choose one or multiple of the following options:
	+ **fuel leak**: There is a fuel leak.
	+ **minor damage**: The engine has minor damage or wear.
	+ **major damage**: The engine has major damage or wear.

* **Tires** (`tires`, Select Multiple, optional): Check the tire condition and choose one or multiple of the following options:
	+ **good**: The tires are in good condition.
	+ **minor wear**: The tires have minor wear.
	+ **major wear**: The tires have major wear.

* **Brakes** (`brakes`, Select Multiple, optional): Check the brake condition and choose one or multiple of the following options:
	+ **good**: The brakes are in good condition.
	+ **minor wear**: The brakes have minor wear.
	+ **major wear**: The brakes have major wear.

* **Suspension** (`suspension`, Select Multiple, optional): Check the suspension condition and choose one or multiple of the following options:
	+ **good**: The suspension is in good condition.
	+ **minor wear**: The suspension has minor wear.
	+ **major wear**: The suspension has major wear.

* **Battery** (`battery`, Number, optional): Record the battery condition.

* **Wipers** (`wipers`, Select Multiple, optional): Check the wiper blade condition and choose one or multiple of the following options:
	+ **good**: The wipers are in good condition.
	+ **minor wear**: The wipers have minor wear.
	+ **major wear**: The wipers have major wear.

* **Glass** (`glass`, Select Multiple, optional): Check the glass condition and choose one or multiple of the following options:
	+ **good**: The glass is in good condition.
	+ **minor wear**: The glass has minor wear.
	+ **major wear**: The glass has major wear.

* **Steering** (`steering`, Select Multiple, optional): Check the steering condition and choose one or multiple of the following options:
	+ **good**: The steering is in good condition.
	+ **minor wear**: The steering has minor wear.
	+ **major wear**: The steering has major wear.

* **Chassis** (`chassis`, Select Multiple, optional): Check the chassis condition and choose one or multiple of the following options:
	+ **good**: The chassis is in good condition.
	+ **minor wear**: The chassis has minor wear.
	+ **major wear**: The chassis has major wear.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer each question accurately based on your observation.
- If you are unsure about a particular condition, you can add a note in the corresponding field to provide more information.
- If you find any issues or damages on the vehicle, make sure to document them accurately and thoroughly.
