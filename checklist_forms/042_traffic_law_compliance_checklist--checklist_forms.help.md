# traffic law compliance checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users collect and record information on traffic law compliance matters, focusing on vehicle checks, compliance items, observations, and dates.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting the form.
2.  Select the correct options for "Compliance Items" that apply to your specific situation.
3.  Enter any observations or comments related to traffic law compliance in the "Observations" field.
4.  Enter the date for "Vehicle Check" and "Date."
5.  Review your answers to ensure accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vehicle Check** (`vehicle_check`, `text`, required): Enter the details of the vehicle check performed, such as the type of inspection or test.
*   **Compliance Items** (`compliance_items`, `select_multiple`, false): Select all the applicable compliance items that pertain to your situation. These are critical items that must be checked for compliance.
*   **Observations** (`observations`, `note`, required): Use this field to note any observations or comments regarding traffic law compliance. This is a free-text field where you can enter your thoughts or findings.
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, true): Enter the date of the compliance check. This is a crucial field to ensure accurate recording and tracking.
*   **Vehicle Check Date** (`vehicle_check_date`, `date`, false): This field is optional but can be useful to record the date of the vehicle check if it differs from the compliance date.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your answers carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you need to capture additional information not included in this form, consider expanding on the fields or creating a custom solution.
- This form is a template; you are free to modify and adapt it according to your needs.
