# Water Safety Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Water Safety Checklist is designed to ensure that facilities meet the necessary safety standards for water quality and treatment. This form helps facility administrators or operators collect and document information on the current water quality and safety conditions, which can be used for regular maintenance, inspections, or emergency response.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2. For each page, ensure you have access to the relevant information and documentation before starting.
3. Enter your responses in the designated fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Information** (`page_1`, `note`, required: false)
This page is for general information about the facility. Please provide any relevant details that might be useful for the assessment.
* **Water Quality Control** (`page_2`, `select_multiple`, required: true)
Select all applicable options for the current water quality control status:
	+ Good
	+ Poor
* **Hazards Found** (`page_3`, `select_one`, required: true)
Indicate if any hazards have been found during the assessment:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Page 4** (`page_4`, `text`, required: false)
Enter any additional comments or notes about the facility's water safety conditions.
* **Temperature** (`page_5`, `number`, required: true)
Enter the current temperature reading for the water source or treatment system.
* **Date** (`page_6`, `date`, required: true)
Enter the date of the assessment or inspection.
* **Time** (`page_7`, `time`, required: true)
Enter the time the assessment or inspection was completed.
* **Water Quality Notes** (`page_8`, `note`, required: false)
Enter any additional comments or notes about the water quality.
* **Page 10** (`page_10`, `text`, required: false)
Enter any additional comments or notes about the facility's water safety conditions.

## Tips
- Always complete the form with accurate and honest information.
- Review and update the form after each assessment or inspection.
- Store the completed form in a secure location, such as a designated folder or database.
- Refer to local and national regulations for specific requirements and guidelines.
