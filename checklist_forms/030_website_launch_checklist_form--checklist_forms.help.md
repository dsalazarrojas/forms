# website_launch_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and record the launch status and details of a website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Launch Date` field with the date when your website was launched.
2. Enter the `Launch Time` field with the time when your website was launched.
3. Provide the `Launch URL` of your website in the text field.
4. Select the `Launch Status` from the dropdown list to indicate the current status of your website.
5. Use the `Launch Notes` field to add any relevant notes or comments about the launch.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Launch Date** (`launch_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when your website was launched. Use the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Launch Time** (`launch_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when your website was launched.
* **Launch URL** (`launch_url`, text, required/optional): Provide the URL of your website that was launched.
* **Launch Status** (`launch_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the current status of your website, which can be Not Launched, Launched, or On Hold.
* **Launch Notes** (`launch_notes`, note, required/optional): Add any notes or comments about the launch of your website.
