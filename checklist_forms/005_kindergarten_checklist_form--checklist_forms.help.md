# Kindergarten Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a child's kindergarten readiness, development, and any potential needs or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form carefully and accurately, taking care to provide all relevant information about the child.
- Ensure you have the child's parent/guardian's permission before completing this form.
- Review the entire form before starting to ensure you understand the questions and their purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parents Name** (`parents_name`, text, required): Please enter the parent(s) name(s) who will be responsible for the child.
- **Childs Birthday** (`childs_birthday`, date, required): Enter the child's date of birth.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required): Please enter the child's home address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the child's contact phone number.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the child's email address (if applicable).
- **Allergy Info** (`allergy_info`, text, optional): If the child has any allergies, please list them here.
