# Emergency Assembly Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of an emergency assembly meeting. It helps gather essential information about the assembly, including the number of attendees, incidents that occurred, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Attendance**: Enter the total number of people present during the assembly.
2. **Incidents**: Describe any incidents that occurred during the assembly, such as injuries or near-miss events.
3. **Roll Call Number**: Enter the roll call number, which is a unique identifier for the assembly.
4. **Location**: Enter the location where the assembly took place.
5. **Emergency Contact**: Enter the contact information for the emergency contact person, which should be a phone number and email address.
6. **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
7. **Emergency Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
8. **Incidents Description**: Describe the incidents that occurred in this field.
9. **Incidents Type**: Select the type of incident that occurred (Fire, Medical, Injury, etc.).
10. **Incidents Location**: Select the location where the incidents occurred (Inside, Outside, Both, etc.).
11. **Incidents Description**: Describe the incidents in more detail.
11. **Incidents Status**: Select the status of the incidents (Pending, Resolved, Closed, In Progress).
