# Investment Advisory Client Onboarding Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients to provide personalized investment services. It's intended for initial or updated client onboarding and should not be completed if you've done so within the past 12 months.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with your personal and financial information.
2. Choose your preferred investment type (High-Risk, Low-Risk, or Balanced).
3. Select your financial status (Single, Married, Divorced, or Widowed).
4. Enter your income and net worth information.
5. Choose your investment horizon (time period for your investments).
6. Select your investment frequency (date for regular investments).
7. Describe your investment objectives.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Investment Type**: Select the type of investment you're interested in (High-Risk, Low-Risk, or Balanced).
* **Risk Tolerance**: Enter a number representing your risk tolerance level. Please note that this is not a standardized scale and your response will influence the investment strategy.
* **Investment Goals**: Describe your investment goals and objectives.
* **Current Investments**: Describe your current investments (if any).
* **Financial Status**: Select your current financial status (Single, Married, Divored, or Widowed).
* **Income**: Enter your annual income.
* **Net Worth**: Enter your current net worth.
* **Investment Horizon**: Select the time period for your investments.
* **Investment Frequency**: Enter the date for regular investments.
* **Investment Objectives**: Describe your investment objectives.
 
## Tips
Please ensure you answer all fields truthfully to provide the most accurate and suitable investment advice. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your advisor for clarification.
