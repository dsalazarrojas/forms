# Easter Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Easter Checklist is a form designed to help individuals plan and prepare for a successful Easter celebration. It collects information about the Easter date, the contents of the Easter basket, and the details of the Easter hunt.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Choose the date of the Easter celebration
* List the contents of the Easter basket
* Describe the Easter hunt details

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Easter date
* **Easter date** (`easter_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the Easter celebration, which is typically a Sunday in March or April.

### Easter basket
* **Easter basket** (`easter_basket`, `text`, optional): List the items you plan to include in the Easter basket, such as chocolate bunnies, marshmallow Peeps, or other treats.

### Easter hunt
* **Easter hunt** (`easter_hunt`, `text`, optional): Describe the details of the Easter hunt, including any clues or challenges you plan to include for the children to find the hidden eggs or treats.

### Easter bonnet
* **Easter bonnet** (`easter_bonnet`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about the Easter bonnet, such as its size, color, or style.

## Tips
* Be accurate when entering the Easter date to ensure the celebrations align with the correct holiday.
* Use creative and fun items for the Easter basket to make the holiday special for the children.
* Consider including clues or challenges for the Easter hunt to make it more exciting and engaging for the children.
