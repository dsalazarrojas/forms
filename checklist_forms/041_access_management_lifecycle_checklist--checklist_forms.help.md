# Access Management Lifecycle Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Access Management Lifecycle Checklist is a tool designed to guide users through the process of modifying or revoking user access to an organization's system. This form ensures that all necessary steps are taken to properly manage and monitor access, and track progress and completion of each phase.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of lifecycle event from the options provided (Provisioning (New Hire), Modification (Role Change), or Deprovisioning (Termination or Exit)).
2. Enter the subject's full name and employee/staff ID number.
3. Check if the formal request and approval are documented, and identify the name of the approving manager.
4. Select all applicable accounts created or activated during provisioning.
5. Verify that the least privilege principle was applied.
6. Select completed deprovisioning steps.
7. Verify that audit logs have been checked for successful revocation.
8. Enter the initials of the compliance officer.
9. Enter the final sign-off date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Type of Lifecycle Event**: Choose the reason for this checklist, which could be provisioning for a new hire, modification for a role change, or deprovisioning for termination or exit.
* **Subject Full Name**: Enter the full name of the person whose access is being modified or revoked.
* **Employee or Staff ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the person whose access is being modified or revoked.
* **Is the formal request and approval documented?**: Check if the formal request and approval are documented in the ticketing system or via email.
* **Approving Manager Name**: Enter the name of the person who authorized the change.
* **Select accounts created or activated**: Select all applicable accounts that were created or activated during provisioning.
* **Was the least privilege principle applied?**: Confirm that the least privilege principle was applied, ensuring that users are only granted the necessary access.
* **Select completed steps**: Verify that all necessary deprovisioning steps were completed.
* **Have the audit logs been checked for successful revocation?**: Confirm that the audit logs have been verified for successful revocation.
* **Compliance Officer Initials**: Enter the initials of the person verifying the lifecycle event.
* **Final Sign-off Date**: Enter the date the process was completed and signed off.
