# earthquake_preparedness_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users quickly gather and organize essential contact information and response plans in the event of an earthquake or other emergency situations. The form is not required, but it's highly recommended to fill it out to ensure you have a plan in place to keep your loved ones and property safe.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Go through each page of the form, one by one.
2. For each field, provide the requested information or select the correct option.
3. Make sure to save the form once you've completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Contact Info (1)** (`emergency_contact_info`, `note`, optional): This field is for listing your emergency contact's name, address, phone number, and any other important details.
* **Emergency Contact Info 2 (2)** (`emergency_contact_info_2`, `note`, optional): Another field for listing an additional emergency contact's information.
* **Contact Info (3)** (`contact_info`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a specific contact person or person to contact in the case of an emergency.
* **Contact Info (4)** (`contact_info`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of your contact person.
* **Contact Info 2 (5)** (`contact_info_1`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a backup contact person or group.
* **Contact Info (6)** (`contact_info_2`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to review and update your emergency contact information.
* **Contact Info (7)** (`contact_info_3`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you plan to review and update your emergency contact information.
* **Contact Info (8)** (`contact_info_4`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes about your emergency contact.
* **Contact Info (9)** (`contact_info_5`, `number`, optional): Enter your contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Info (10)** (`contact_info_6`, `number`, optional): Enter your contact person's alternative phone number.
* **Contact Info (11)** (`contact_info_7`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a contact person that can help you in an emergency.

## Tips
* Make sure to keep your emergency contact information up-to-date and reviewed regularly.
* In the event of an emergency, quickly refer to this form for critical contact information.
* Store a copy of this form in a safe and easily accessible location, such as a fireproof container or a waterproof bag.
