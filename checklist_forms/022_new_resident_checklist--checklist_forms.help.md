# New Resident Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Resident Checklist is a form used to collect information from new residents to create a comprehensive record of their move-in. This information is essential for our team to provide excellent service and ensure a smooth transition into your new home.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form in order from page 1 to page [insert page count].
2.  Ensure you answer all required fields ( fields with asterisk*) and provide accurate information.
3.  Double-check your answers before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, *):* Enter the date you are moving into your new residence.
* **Name** (`name`, text): Provide your name as you would like it to be used in your account.
* **Address** (`address`, text): Enter your address, including apartment number (if applicable), for billing and service purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number for contact and communication purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, *email*): Enter your email address for secure communication.
* **Move In Time** (`move_in_time`, time, *): Enter the time you plan to move in.
* **Rent** (`rent_amount`, number, *):* If applicable, enter your rent amount for billing purposes.
* **Move In Method** (`move_in_method`, select multiple, *): Select all methods by which you plan to move in (e.g., Drive, Taxi, Uber, Walk, Bike).
* **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, date): Enter the date you plan to move out of the property, if known.
