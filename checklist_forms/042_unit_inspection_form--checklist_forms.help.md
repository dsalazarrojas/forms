# Unit Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Unit Inspection Form is designed to gather essential information about a unit's condition, tenant status, and lease details during the inspection process. This form helps to standardize the inspection process and ensures that all relevant information is collected and documented accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in a clear and legible handwriting or typing, ensuring that all fields are completed thoroughly.
2. Start by providing the tenant's information, including their name and status.
3. Select the correct move-in and move-out dates from the calendar.
4. Describe the unit's condition before and after inspection, highlighting any notable issues or concerns.
5. Indicate the photos taken during the inspection to support the assessment.
6. Choose the correct inspectors for both move-in and move-out inspections.
7. Select the current tenant's status and provide any additional comments or recommendations.
8. Enter the lease term and its corresponding dates.
9. Specify the lease's length and monthly rent.
10. Choose the correct rent frequency and indicate the utility costs.
11. Enter any other relevant information that may be required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Unit Inspection Form (1)**: This is the header of the form.
* **Tenant Information (2)**: This is where you will enter the tenant's details.
	+ **Move In Date (3)**: Enter the date the tenant moved in.
	+ **Move Out Date (4)**: Enter the date the tenant moved out.
* **Unit Condition (5)**: Describe the unit's condition before and after inspection.
* **Before Inspection (6)**: Describe the unit's condition before inspection.
* **After Inspection (7)**: Describe the unit's condition after inspection.
* **Damage Areas (8)**: List any areas that were damaged during the inspection.
* **Recommendations (9)**: Enter any recommendations or comments.
* **Photos (10)**: Add photos taken during the inspection to support the assessment.
* **Move In Inspector (11)**: Choose the inspector who conducted the move-in inspection.
* **Move Out Inspector (12)**: Choose the inspector who conducted the move-out inspection.
* **Tenant Status (13)**: Select the current status of the tenant.
* **Unit Status (14)**: Select the current status of the unit.
* **Reason for Vacancy (15)**: Provide any reason for the unit's vacancy.
* **Lease Term (16)**: Enter the lease's term.
* **Lease Start Date (17)**: Enter the start date of the lease.
* **Lease End Date (18)**: Enter the end date of the lease.
* **Lease Length (19)**: Enter the length of the lease.
* **Security Deposit (20)**: Enter the security deposit amount.
* **Rent (21)**: Enter the rent amount.
* **Rent Frequency (22)**: Choose the rent frequency.
* **Utility Cost (23)**: Enter the utility cost.
* **Other (24)**: Enter any additional information that may be required.
