# Employee Recognition Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Recognition Checklist Form is designed to gather information about employee recognition and appreciation. It helps to identify the recipient of the recognition, the department they belong to, and other relevant details. This form is used to show appreciation and acknowledgment for a team member's hard work and contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the first name of the employee or team member you wish to recognize.
2.  Enter the last name of the employee or team member you wish to recognize.
3.  Select the department the employee or team member belongs to from the available options.
4.  Choose the date you want to recognize the employee or team member.
5.  Choose the time you want to recognize the employee or team member.
6.  Choose the type of recognition: monetary or non-monetary.
7.  Select the person or team leader assigned to give the recognition.
8.  Enter any additional comments or description about the recognition.
9.  Select the person submitting the recognition (e.g., the team leader or manager).
10.  Enter the date and time you submitted the recognition form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the employee or team member you wish to recognize.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the employee or team member you wish to recognize.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email of the employee or team member you wish to recognize.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department the employee or team member belongs to from the available options.
* **Recognition Date** (`recognition_date`, date, required): Choose the date you want to recognize the employee or team member.
* **Recognition Time** (`recognition_time`, time, required): Choose the time you want to recognize the employee or team member.
* **Recognition Type** (`recognition_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of recognition: monetary or non-monetary.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select the person or team leader assigned to give the recognition.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or description about the recognition.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Select the person submitting the recognition (e.g., team leader or manager).
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted the recognition form.
*   **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, required): Choose the time you submitted the recognition form.
