# Managed Mobility Program Employee Onboarding Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Managed Mobility Program Employee Onboarding Checklist is a form designed to facilitate a seamless onboarding process for new employees into the Managed Mobility Program. It collects necessary information from both the employee and the requestor to ensure a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **New Hire Details**: Enter the new hire's details.
2. **Device Handoff**: Enter the date and time for the device handoff.
3. **Services Offered**: Select all the services that will be provided to the employee.
4. **Mobility Services**: Select the services that will be offered to the employee.
5. **Requestor**: Enter the requestor's information.
6. **Email**: Enter the requestor's email.
7. **Phone**: Enter the requestor's phone number.
8. **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments.
9. **Approval Date**: Enter the approval date.
10. **Approved By**: Select the individuals who have approved the request.
11. **Device Handoff Status**: Update the status of the device handoff.
12. **Email**: Enter the employee's email.
13. **Phone**: Enter the employee's phone number.
14. **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Hire Details** (`new_hire_details`, text, required): Enter the new hire's details such as name and ID number.
* **Device Handoff** (`device_handoff`, text, required): Enter the date and time for the device handoff.
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, select_one, optional): Select all the services that will be provided to the employee.
* **Mobility Services** (`mobility_services`, select_one, optional): Select the services that will be offered to the employee.
* **Date Of Handoff** (`date_of_handoff`, date, optional): Enter the date of the device handoff.
* **Time Of Handoff** (`time_of_handoff`, time, optional): Enter the time of the device handoff.
* **Requestor** (`requestor`, select_one, optional): Select the individual who is requesting the handoff.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the requestor's email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the requestor's phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, required): Enter the approval date.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, select_multiple, optional): Select the individuals who have approved the request.
* **Device Handoff Status** (`device_handoff_status`, select_multiple, optional): Update the status of the device handoff.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the employee's email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Requestor Phone** (`requestor_phone`, text, required): Enter the requestor's phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.

## Tips
* Be thorough when completing the form to ensure accurate information.
* Verify the correctness of entered information before submitting.
* Use the form for new hires only.
* Update the form as necessary to reflect changes in information.
