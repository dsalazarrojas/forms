# Job Pre Employment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from job candidates before hiring them. It is designed to help recruiters and HR personnel evaluate a candidate's suitability for a job position. The form includes various fields for gathering details such as pre-employment tasks, supporting documents, and personal contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the pre-employment tasks sections with the tasks that the candidate has completed or is willing to complete.
2. Add any relevant supporting documents in the supporting documents sections.
3. Enter the candidate's contact information, including phone number and email.
4. Select the required options for the job pre-employment checklist sections.
5. Enter any additional information that is relevant to the job position.
