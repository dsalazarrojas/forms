# Gas Station Audit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a gas station's audit, which is a crucial process to evaluate and improve the safety, cleanliness, and overall performance of a gas station. The purpose of this form is to ensure that the gas station meets the required standards and guidelines set by regulatory bodies and the company.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**:
	* Please provide a brief introduction to the gas station, including its name, address, and any other relevant details.
2. **Station Details**:
	* Please select all the relevant options that apply to the gas station's details, such as its location, size, and number of pumps.
3. **Safety Measures**:
	* Please select all the safety measures that are implemented at the gas station, such as emergency exits, fire extinguishers, and first aid kits.
4. **Cleanliness Standards**:
	* Please select all the cleanliness standards that are met at the gas station, such as clean floors, washrooms, and restrooms.
5. **Safety Standards**:
	* Please select all the safety standards that are met at the gas station, such as proper lighting, signage, and emergency equipment.
6. **Station Address**:
	* Please provide the address of the gas station, including the street number, city, state, and zip code.
7. **Contact Person**:
	* Please provide the name and contact information of the contact person at the gas station.
8. **Contact Number**:
	* Please provide the contact number of the gas station.
9. **Findings**:
	* Please provide any additional comments or findings related to the audit, such as areas of concern or improvement.
10. **Remarks**:
	* Please provide any additional remarks or suggestions related to the audit.
