# nurse_shift_report_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by nurses to report their shift activities, patient information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Make sure you have all necessary information before completing the form.
3. If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or refer to your employee handbook for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the patient you cared for during your shift.
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, text, optional): Enter the time you started your shift.
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you ended your shift.
