# ethics_exam_preparation_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a medical student's preparation for an ethics exam in a medical setting. It covers various aspects related to medical ethics, including knowledge areas, ethics exam questions, readiness status, follow-up schedules, study progress, next steps, feedback, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing an introduction that briefly explains your current level of understanding about ethics topics.
2. Answer the question "knowledge_areas" by selecting either "Yes" or "No", indicating whether you have a good understanding of the areas covered in the ethics exam.
3. In the "ethics_exam_questions" field, select all the questions you have already studied or are familiar with.
4. Indicate your readiness status by selecting either "Active" or "Inactive", depending on your current level of preparation.
5. If you have any follow-up schedules or appointments, select "Yes" to indicate their existence, or "No" otherwise.
6. Enter the current date in the "study_progress" field, representing the date of your last study session.
7. In the "next_steps" field, enter a brief description of your plan for future study or actions to be taken.
8. If you have any feedback or comments, type them in the "feedback" field.
9. Finally, provide any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (text): This field is for you to briefly explain your current level of understanding about ethics topics.
* **knowledge_areas** (select_one): Select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have a good understanding of the areas covered in the ethics exam.
* **ethics_exam_questions** (select_multiple): Select all the questions you have already studied or are familiar with.
* **readiness_status** (select_one): Indicate your current readiness status by selecting either "Active" or "Inactive".
* **follow_up_schedules** (select_multiple): Select "Yes" to indicate the existence of follow-up schedules or appointments, or "No" otherwise.
* **study_progress** (date): Enter the date of your last study session.
* **next_steps** (text): Enter a brief description of your plan for future study or actions to be taken.
* **feedback** (text): Provide any feedback or comments you have.
* **notes** (note): Provide any additional notes or comments.
* **email** (email): Enter your email address for contact.
* **phone** (text): Enter your phone number for contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each question carefully and honestly, as this form is meant to help you assess your preparation for the ethics exam.
* Double-check your answers in the "next_steps" field to ensure you've included all necessary actions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us through the email or phone number you provided.
