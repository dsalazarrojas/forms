# Palpitations Symptom Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a checklist for recording palpitations symptoms. It is intended to help identify potential causes of palpitations, a feeling of irregular heartbeats or rapid heartbeats. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each page carefully and select the corresponding symptoms that you are experiencing.
2. For symptoms with multiple options (e.g., `palpitations_symptom_2`), choose all that apply.
3. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
4. If you are unsure or do not understand a question, ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
