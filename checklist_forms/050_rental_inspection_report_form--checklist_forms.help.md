# Rental Inspection Report Form - Help Guide

The Rental Inspection Report Form is used to document the results of a rental property inspection, including any issues found, repair requests, and other relevant information.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the property inspection section, which includes any issues found during the inspection.
2. In the "Issues Found" section, select all the issues that were detected during the inspection.
3. In the "Repair Requests" section, choose the best option to return the property to its original state.
4. If necessary, take photos or provide evidence to support your findings in the "Photos and Evidence" section.
5. Add any comments or observations in the "Comments" section.
6. Fill out the "Date Inspected", "Time Inspected", and "Date Completed" fields with the relevant dates.
7. Indicate if the form was completed by the original inspector or another technician in the "Completed By" section.

* **Photos and Evidence**: If you have taken photos or collected evidence during the inspection, select "Yes" and attach the relevant files or links.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or observations about the inspection, including any additional information that may be helpful to the landlord or property manager.

* **Time Inspected**: Fill out the time when the inspection was conducted.
