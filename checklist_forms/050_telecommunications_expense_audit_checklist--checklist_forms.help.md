# Telecommunications Expense Audit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist in the audit of telecommunicaions expenses to ensure that all relevant information is collected and documented. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the telecom agreement to ensure you understand the terms and conditions.
2. Gather and review the relevant invoices and provide any necessary details.
3. Verify telecom usage patterns to ensure they align with the agreement.
4. Select the correct rate plan from the options provided.
5. Identify any unusual telecom usage patterns.
6. Review the vendor invoices and confirm their accuracy.
7. Identify any unusual charges on the invoices.
8. Verify that the invoices match the contract terms.
9. Verify that the usage matches the contract terms.
10. Identify any missing invoices.
11. Review the contract terms and conditions.
12. Document any findings from the review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Review telecom agreement
* **Review telecom agreement** (`review_telecom_agreement`, `note`, required/optional): Review the telecom agreement to ensure you understand the terms and conditions of the service.

* **Review invoices** (`review_invoices`, `text`, required): This is where you can provide a copy of the invoice and any relevant details about it.

* **Verify telecom usage patterns** (`verify_telecom_usage_patterns`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'Yes' if the usage patterns match the agreement and 'No' if they do not.

* **Verify rate plans** (`verify_rate_plans`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct rate plan that applies to your telecom service.

* **Identify unusual patterns** (`identify_unusual_patterns`, `text`, optional): If you have any concerns or suspicions about unusual usage patterns, provide details here.

* **Review vendor invoices** (`review_vendor_invoices`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'True' if the vendor invoices match the agreement and 'False' if they do not.

* **Identify unusual charges** (`identify_unusual_charges`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'True' if the charges are unusual and 'False' if they are not.

* **Verify invoices match contract** (`verify_invoices_match_contract`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'True' if the invoices match the contract and 'False' if they do not.

* **Verify usage matches contract** (`verify_usage_match_contract`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'True' if the usage matches the contract and 'False' if it does not.

* **Identify missing invoices** (`identify_missing_invoices`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'True' if any invoices are missing and 'False' if they are not.

* **Review contract terms and conditions** (`review_contract_terms_and_conditions`, `text`, optional): If you have any comments or concerns about the contract terms and conditions, provide them here.

* **Document findings** (`document_findings`, `text`, required): Document any findings from the review and provide details as necessary.

* **Verify invoices received from vendor** (`verify_invoices_received_from_vendor`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'True' if the invoices were received from the vendor and 'False' if they were not.
