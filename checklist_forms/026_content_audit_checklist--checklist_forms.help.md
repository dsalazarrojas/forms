The "Content Audit Checklist" form is designed to collect information on the quality and performance of a webpage or asset, focusing on its content, SEO status, and other essential metrics. This form helps ensure that the content is up-to-date, well-written, and aligns with the organization's content strategy. The purpose of this form is to identify areas for improvement and suggest updates or changes to enhance the content's quality and user experience.

The form collects data on the following aspects:

* Auditor's details
* Audit date
* Page evaluation and content quality rating
* SEO status
* Readability score
* Content freshness
* Update priorities and recommendations
* Key performance indicators (KPIs) affected
* Confirmation of audit completion
* Additional comments or feedback
* Confirmation of accuracy of information provided

The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of content audit. This guide will walk you through how to complete each section and field:

# Content Audit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Content Audit Checklist" form is designed to assess the quality and performance of a webpage or asset. Its primary goal is to identify areas for improvement and provide recommendations for updates or changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Auditor Full Name" field (required).
2. Select the audit date in the "Audit Date" field (required).
3. Evaluate the page's content quality in the "Content Quality Rating" field (required), with ratings from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
4. Determine the SEO status of the page in the "SEO Status" field (required), selecting from "Optimized", "Partially Optimized", "Needs Optimization", or "Critical Issues".
5. Assess the content's freshness in the "Content Freshness" field (required), choosing from "Up-to-date", "Needs Review", "Outdated", or "Archive Needed".
6. For any KPIs affected, select all relevant options from the "Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Affected" field (optional).
7. Confirm that you have completed the audit in the "Audit Completed?" field (required).
8. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).
9. Confirm that the information provided is accurate in the "I Confirm" field (required).
10. Enter the date of audit submission in the "Date of Audit Submission" field (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Auditor Full Name** (`auditor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the auditor.
* **Audit Date** (`audit_date`, date, required): Select the date of the audit.
* **Content Quality Rating** (`content_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Evaluate the content's quality, with ratings from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **SEO Status** (`seo_status`, select_one, required): Determine the SEO status of the page, choosing from "Optimized", "Partially Optimized", "Needs Optimization", or "Critical Issues".
* **Content Freshness** (`content_freshness`, select_one, required): Assess the content's freshness, choosing from "Up-to-date", "Needs Review", "Outdated", or "Archive Needed".
* **Update Priorities and Recommendations** (`update_recommendations`, text, required): Provide specific recommendations for updates.
* **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Affected** (`key_performance_indicators`, select_multiple, optional): Select all KPIs affected by the page's content.
* **Confirmation of Audit Completion** (`audit_completed`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have completed the audit.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **I Confirm** (`signature_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Date of Audit Submission** (`audit_submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of audit submission.

Note: All fields marked as required are necessary for the form to be complete.
