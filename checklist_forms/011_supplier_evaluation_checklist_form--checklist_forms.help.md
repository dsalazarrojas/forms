# supplier_evaluation_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from suppliers for evaluation purposes, including their name, contact details, ratings, and comments. It is likely used in a business or procurement context to assess the supplier's quality, reliability, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the supplier's name in the **supplier_name** field.
2. Fill in the **contact_person** field with the individual responsible for contacting the supplier.
3. Enter the supplier's email address in the **contact_email** field.
4. Provide the supplier's phone number in the **contact_phone** field.
5. Enter the supplier's address in the **supplier_address** field.
6. Provide the supplier's website in the **supplier_website** field.
7. Rate the supplier's overall performance using the **supplier_rating** field.
8. Add any additional comments or observations about the supplier in the **supplier_comments** field.
9. Rate the supplier's quality, delivery, and service using the **quality_score**, **delivery_score**, and **service_score** fields respectively.
10. Calculate and enter your overall score of the supplier using the **overall_score** field.
11. Add any additional comments about the evaluation in the **evaluation_comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **supplier_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the supplier.
* **contact_person** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person to contact at the supplier.
* **contact_email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the supplier.
* **contact_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the supplier.
* **supplier_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the supplier.
* **supplier_website** (Text, Optional): Enter the website of the supplier.
* **supplier_rating** (Number, Optional): Rate the supplier's overall performance (1-5).
* **supplier_comments** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the supplier.
* **overall_score** (Number, Optional): Calculate and enter your overall score of the supplier (1-5).
* **quality_score** (Number, Optional): Rate the supplier's quality (1-5).
* **delivery_score** (Number, Optional): Rate the supplier's delivery (1-5).
* **service_score** (Number, Optional): Rate the supplier's service (1-5).
* **supplier_score** (Number, Optional): Calculate and enter your overall score of the supplier (1-5).
* **evaluation_comments** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments about the evaluation.
