# Patient Safety Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Safety Check Form is designed to collect relevant information about patient safety and medical conditions for healthcare professionals to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your name (Page 1 - patient_information)
2. List any medical conditions or concerns (Page 1 - medical_condition)
3. List any ongoing medications (Page 1 - medication)
4. List any allergies (Page 1 - allergies)
5. Describe your medical history (Page 1 - medical_history)
6. List emergency contacts (Page 1 - emergency_contacts)
7. Describe your emergency plan (Page 1 - emergency_plan)
8. Report your overall patient status (Page 1 - patient_status)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_information** (`patient_information`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **medical_condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required/optional): List any medical conditions or concerns you may have.
* **medication** (`medication`, text, required/optional): List any ongoing medications you are currently taking.
* **allergies** (`allergies`, text, required/optional): List any allergies you may have.
* **medical_history** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Describe your medical history.
* **emergency_contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required/optional): List your emergency contacts.
* **emergency_plan** (`emergency_plan`, text, required/optional): Describe your emergency plan.
* **patient_status** (`patient_status`, text, required/optional): Report your overall patient status.

Please note that fields "patient_status2" to "patient_status17" have been omitted due to redundancy and lack of clarity. 
You may choose to ignore or delete them for a more streamlined experience. 
If any of these fields are actually relevant in your practice, please adjust the form accordingly. 

## Tips
This form is a simplified tool to help gather essential information. 
Please ensure to provide accurate and detailed answers to help healthcare professionals make informed decisions. 
If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities or healthcare professionals.
