# hvac_preventive_maintenance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document information for preventive maintenance activities for HVAC systems and equipment. It helps maintain accurate records, track progress, and ensure that scheduled maintenance is performed on time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the system's current status and identify the equipment that requires maintenance.
2. Select the maintenance interval and type (Planned, Preventive, Corrective) as per the organization's policies.
3. Indicate if the equipment is in operation or under maintenance.
4. Fill in the reading values (temperature, humidity, voltage, amperage, pressure, water level) as applicable.
5. Add any comments or notes relevant to the maintenance activity.
6. Select the date and time of the maintenance activity.
7. Enter the location and notes regarding the equipment.
8. Add any comments or notes about the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **System Name** (`system_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the system being maintained.
* **Maintenance Interval** (`maintenance_interval`, Text, Optional): Enter the interval at which maintenance is performed.
* **Equipment List** (`equipment_list`, Select Multiple, Optional): Check the boxes indicating which equipment requires maintenance.
* **Maintenance Type** (`maintenance_type`, Select One, Optional): Select the type of maintenance (Planned, Preventive, Corrective) as per organization's policies.
* **Next Maintenance Date** (`next_maintenance_date`, Date, Optional): Record the date of the next scheduled maintenance.
* **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the maintenance technician performing the work.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Add any comments or notes regarding the maintenance activity.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, Text, Optional): Record the tool or equipment used for maintenance.
* **System Status** (`system_status`, Select One, Optional): Indicate the system's status (Operating, Shut down, Under maintenance, Off line, Out of order, Other).
* **Temperature Reading** (`temperature_reading`, Number, Optional): Record the temperature reading as applicable.
* **Humidity Reading** (`humidity_reading`, Number, Optional): Record the humidity reading as applicable.
* **Voltage Reading** (`voltage_reading`, Number, Optional): Record the voltage reading as applicable.
* **Amperage Reading** (`amperage_reading`, Number, Optional): Record the amperage reading as applicable.
* **Pressure Reading** (`pressure_reading`, Number, Optional): Record the pressure reading as applicable.
* **Water Level Reading** (`water_level_reading`, Number, Optional): Record the water level reading as applicable.
* **Other Reading** (`other_reading`, Text, Optional): Record any other relevant reading not captured above.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Add any notes or comments regarding the equipment.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Optional): Record the date of the maintenance activity.
* **Time** (`time`, Time, Optional): Record the time of the maintenance activity.
* **Location** (`location`, Text, Optional): Enter the location of the equipment.
* **Equipment Notes** (`equipment_notes`, Text, Optional): Add any notes or comments regarding the equipment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Add any comments or notes regarding the activity.
* **Temperature Reading 2** (`temperature_reading2`, Number, Optional): Record the temperature reading 2 as applicable.
* **Humidity Reading 2** (`humidity_reading2`, Number, Optional): Record the humidity reading 2 as applicable.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (`assigned_tool2`, Text, Optional): Record the tool or equipment used for maintenance 2.
