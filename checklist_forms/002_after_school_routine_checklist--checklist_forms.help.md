# After School Routine Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The After School Routine Checklist is a tool for parents and guardians to track their child's daily progress after school. It helps monitor homework completion, healthy habits, and positive behaviors, ensuring your child stays on track and develops a sense of accountability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Student Name**: Enter your child's name in this text field.
2. **Date**: Select the date of the current day using the calendar interface.
3. **Homework Completion Status**: Choose one of the options to indicate whether your child has completed all, partially completed, or has no homework assigned.
4. **Snack or Meal Time**: Select whether a healthy snack or a regular meal was provided during the day.
5. **Leisure Time Activity**: Choose one of the options to indicate the primary activity your child engaged in during free time.
6. **Reading Time (minutes)**: Enter the number of minutes spent reading.
7. **Bedtime**: Enter the actual time your child went to sleep.
8. **Daily Progress Notes**: Record any notable events or achievements from the day.
9. **Areas of Improvement**: Note any challenges or difficulties faced during the day.
10. **Materials Needed**: Select any materials required for the next day.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): This is where you enter your child's name.
* **Date** (`checklist_date`, date, required): Please select the date of the current day using the provided calendar interface.
* **Homework Completion Status** (`homework_status`, select_one, required): This field asks about your child's homework completion status. Options are:
	+ All Completed
	+ Partially Completed
	+ None Completed
	+ No Homework Assigned
* **Snack or Meal Time** (`snack_time`, select_one, required): Select whether your child had a healthy snack or a regular meal.
	+ Yes - Healthy Snack
	+ Yes - Regular Meal
	+ No Snack
* **Leisure Time Activity** (`leisure_activity`, select_one, required): This field asks about the primary activity your child engaged in during free time. Options are:
	+ Outdoor Play
	+ Screen Time (Games/TV)
	+ Arts and Crafts
	+ Socializing with Friends
	+ Other
* **Reading Time (minutes)** (`reading_minutes`, number, optional): Enter the number of minutes spent reading.
* **Bedtime** (`bedtime`, time, optional): Enter the actual time your child went to sleep.
* **Daily Progress Notes** (`notes_header`, note, optional): Record any events or achievements from the day.
* **Areas of Improvement** (`areas_improvement`, text, optional): Note any challenges or difficulties faced during the day.
* **Materials Needed** (`materials_needed`, select_multiple, optional): Select any materials needed for the next day. Options are:
	+ Sports Kit
	+ Musical Instrument
	+ Field Trip Slip
	+ Library Books
	+ Special Project Supplies

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out this form daily to track consistent progress.
* Review and discuss the form with your child to encourage accountability and healthy habits.
* Use the data collected to identify areas where your child may need extra support or guidance.
* Adjust the form as needed to accommodate your child's unique needs and schedule.
