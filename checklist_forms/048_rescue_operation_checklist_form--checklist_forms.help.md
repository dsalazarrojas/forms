# Rescue Operation Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Rescue Operation Checklist Form is used to document and track the details of rescue operations, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and recorded accurately. This form helps teams ensure that rescue operations are conducted in a standardized and thorough manner, reducing errors and improving outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the rescue task and resource allocation fields, indicating whether the task has been assigned and which team members are involved.
2. Select the relevant rescue equipment and vehicle used during the operation.
3. Record the location where the rescue took place and the current status of the operation.
4. Add any necessary comments or notes about the operation.
5. Provide the contact information (phone number and email) of the person or team in charge of the operation.
6. Complete the date and time fields to capture the duration of the operation.
7. Add any additional notes about the operation.
8. If the operation has been assigned to a specific team or tool, enter the relevant ID number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rescue Task** (`rescue_task`): Describe the task or activity being conducted during the rescue operation.
* **Resource Allocation** (`resource_allocation`): Enter the number of resources (e.g., personnel, equipment) allocated for the operation.
* **Rescue Team** (`rescue_team`): Select whether a team is involved in the operation.
* **Rescue Equipment** (`rescue_equipment`): Choose the equipment used during the operation.
* **Rescue Vehicle** (`rescue_vehicle`): Select the vehicle used during the operation.
* **Rescue Location** (`rescue_location`): Record the location where the rescue took place.
* **Rescue Status** (`rescue_status`): Enter the current status of the operation.
* **Rescue Comment** (`rescue_comment`): Add any additional comments or notes about the operation.
* **Rescue Phone** (`rescue_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person or team in charge of the operation.
* **Rescue Email** (`rescue_email`): Enter the email address of the person or team in charge of the operation.
* **Rescue Date** (`rescue_date`): Record the date of the operation.
* **Rescue Time** (`rescue_time`): Record the duration of the operation.
* **Rescue Notes** (`rescue_notes`): Add any additional notes about the operation.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Enter the name of the person or team assigned to the operation.
* **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`): Enter the name of the person or team that assigned the operation.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Enter the ID number of the tool used for the operation.
* **Operation Type** (`operation_type`): Enter the type of operation (e.g., search and rescue).
* **Rescue Type** (`rescue_type`): Enter the type of rescue operation (e.g., medical evacuation).
* **Operation Category** (`operation_category`): Enter the category of the operation (e.g., emergency response).
* **Operation Number** (`operation_number`): Enter the operation number or ID.
* **Operation Date** (`operation_date`): Record the date of the operation.
* **Operation Time** (`operation_time`): Record the duration of the operation.
* **Operation Notes** (`operation_notes`): Add any additional notes about the operation.
* **Assigned Form ID** (`assigned_form_id`): Enter the ID number of the assigned form.
