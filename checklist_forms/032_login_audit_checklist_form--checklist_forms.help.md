# login_audit_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and ensure that all necessary steps for conducting a login audit are properly covered and documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the login form data section, which includes fields related to the login form's details, such as login controls and login controls reviewed.
2. Review and document all login controls to ensure they are functioning as expected.
3. Find and document any issues with login controls and take follow-up actions as necessary.
4. Find and track any issues with login controls and take follow-up actions as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **login audit form data** (`login_audit_form_data`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information related to the login form audit.
* **review login controls** (`review_login_controls`, `text`, optional): Describe the results of your review of the login controls.
* **find and document** (`find_and_document`, `text`, optional): Describe any findings or issues with the login controls.
* **find and track** (`find_and_track`, `text`, optional): Describe any follow-up actions taken.
* **login controls reviewed** (`login_controls_reviewed`, `number`, optional): Enter the total number of login controls reviewed.
* **follow up actions** (`find_follow_up_actions`, `text`, optional): Describe any follow-up actions taken or recommended.
* **date last login controls reviewed** (`date_last_login_controls_reviewed`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the login controls were last reviewed.
* **time last login controls reviewed** (`time_last_login_controls_reviewed`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the login controls were last reviewed.
* **reviewed by** (`reviewed_by`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person or team performing the review.
* **login controls reviewed by** (`login_controls_reviewed_by`, `select_one`, optional): Select the person or team that reviewed the login controls.
* **find follow up actions by** (`find_follow_up_actions_by`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the person or team that will take follow-up actions.
* **login controls reviewed date** (`login_controls_reviewed_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the login controls were reviewed.
* **time controls reviewed** (`time_controls_reviewed`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the login controls were reviewed.
* **reviewed by time** (`reviewed_by_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the login controls were reviewed.
* **login controls reviewed** (`login_controls_reviewed`, `text`, optional): Describe the login controls that were reviewed.
* **login controls find and track** (`login_controls_find_and_track`, `text`, optional): Describe any issues found with the login controls.
* **login controls follow up actions** (`login_controls_follow_up_actions`, `text`, optional): Describe any follow-up actions taken or recommended.
* **find find follow up actions** (`find_find_follow_up_actions`, `text`, optional): Describe any follow-up actions taken or recommended.
* **login controls last reviewed date** (`login_controls_last_reviewed_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the login controls were last reviewed.
* **find find follow up actions time** (`find_find_follow_up_actions_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the login controls were last reviewed.
* **login controls last reviewed time** (`login_controls_last_reviewed_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the login controls were last reviewed.
* **login controls reviewed by time** (`login_controls_reviewed_by_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the login controls were reviewed by.
* **login controls last reviewed by** (`login_controls_last_reviewed_by`, `text`, optional): Enter the person or team that last reviewed the login controls.
* **login controls last find and track** (`login_controls_last_find_and_track`, `text`, optional): Describe any issues found with the login controls that have not yet been documented.
* **login controls last follow up actions** (`login_controls_last_follow_up_actions`, `text`, optional): Describe any follow-up actions taken or recommended for the login controls that have not yet been reviewed.

## Tips

* Make sure to carefully review and document all login controls to ensure accuracy.
* Enter any relevant information for follow-up actions in the "find and document" and "find and track" fields.
* Select the correct options for "login controls reviewed by" and "find follow up actions by" fields.
* Enter the correct date and time for fields related to review and follow-up actions.
