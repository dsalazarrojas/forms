# Wild Goose Chase Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wild Goose Chase Checklist is a form designed to help users track and record their progress while participating in the Wild Goose Chase, a series of challenges. This form is intended to be a simple and intuitive tool for users to document their experiences and provide feedback on each challenge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each challenge description and provide a brief description of your experience in the corresponding text field.
* Type in your response in the provided text field for each challenge.
* You can add as many or as few details as you like, depending on your comfort level.
* If you have any feedback on any challenge, use the corresponding text field for feedback.
* When you have completed all challenges, review your responses and feedback for any further action or reflection needed.
