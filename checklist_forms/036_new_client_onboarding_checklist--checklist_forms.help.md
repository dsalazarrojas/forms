# New Client Onboarding Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to guide new clients through the onboarding process, ensuring they have all the necessary information and documents ready before starting work with the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and enter your information as prompted.
2. For each field, provide clear and accurate details about your project, contact information, and preferences.
3. Make sure to complete each field thoroughly to avoid missing essential information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_step** (`new_client_onboarding_checklist_first_step`, text, optional): This field is the first step in the onboarding process. Please enter a brief description of your project.
* **second_step** (`new_client_onboarding_checklist_second_step`, number, optional): This field is used to track the current step of the onboarding process. Please enter a numerical value indicating the current step.
* **third_step** (`new_client_onboarding_checklist_third_step`, number, optional): This field is another step in the onboarding process. Please enter a numerical value indicating the progress you've made so far.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to your designated contact for clarification.
* You can always contact the organization's support team if you need further assistance.
