# Hospital Safety Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the safety and compliance of a hospital or healthcare facility, ensuring that all safety protocols and procedures are in place to protect both patients and staff.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section of the form, which covers various aspects of hospital safety.
2. For each question, select the correct answer based on your facility's current status.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, as this will help you identify areas that need improvement.
4. Once completed, submit the form to ensure that your hospital's safety protocols are up-to-date and effective.
