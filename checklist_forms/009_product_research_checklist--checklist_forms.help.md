# Product Research Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Research Checklist is a form designed to gather and organize information about a product. It helps users to efficiently gather and track product details, research areas, scores, and personal preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product name and URL if available.
2. Provide a brief description of the product.
3. Choose relevant research areas related to the product.
4. Enter a score for the product based on your assessment.
5. Add any personal comments or notes about the product.
6. Select relevant team members for further evaluation.
7. Provide any additional comments or notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product Info** (`product_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief summary of the product.
- **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a more detailed description of the product.
