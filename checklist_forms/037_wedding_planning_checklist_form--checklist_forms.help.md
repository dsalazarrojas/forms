# Wedding Planning Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Planning Checklist Form is designed to help you organize and prepare for your wedding by collecting important details such as venue and guest count, ceremony and reception information, attire, and other essential elements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and answer the questions according to your needs. Make sure to fill out all required fields and select all relevant options for each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Venue And Guest Count** (`Page 1`): Select the estimated number of guests you expect to attend your wedding (50, 75, 100, 125).
* **Ceremony Details** (`Page 2`): Choose the type of ceremony you will be having (Outdoor, Indoor, Cultural).
* **Dress And Attire** (`Page 3`): Provide details about the dress and attire information for you and your partner.
* **Food And Beverage** (`Page 4`): Enter details about food and beverage information.
* **Wedding Cake And Cake Cutting** (`Page 5`): Choose the type of wedding cake you will have (Wedding cake, Cupcakes, No wedding cake).
* **Photography And Videography** (`Page 6`): Select the types of photography and videography services you want (Still photography, Video, Both).
* **Music And Entertainment** (`Page 7`): Choose the types of music and entertainment you want (DJ, Band, Both).
* **Reception And Decorations** (`Page 8`): Select the type of reception you want (Outdoor, Indoor, Cultural decorations).
* **Wedding Transportation** (`Page 9`): Choose the type of transportation you want for your guests (Limousine, Bus, Taxi).
* **Wedding Attire** (`Page 10`): Provide details about wedding attire information.
* **Wedding Flowers** (`Page 11`): Enter details about wedding flowers information.
* **Wedding Favors** (`Page 12`): Enter details about wedding favors.
* **Wedding Invitations** (`Page 13`): Choose the type of invitations you want to send (Digital, Paper, Both).
* **Wedding Invitations Content** (`Page 14`): Enter details about wedding invitations content.
* **Wedding Invitations Design** (`Page 15`): Enter details about wedding invitations design.
* **Wedding Invitations RSVP** (`Page 16`): Choose the type of RSVP method you want to use (Online, Paper, Both).
* **Wedding Invitations Tracking** (`Page 17`): Select the method of tracking RSVPs (Online, Paper, Both).
* **Wedding Invitations Response Deadline** (`Page 18`): Enter the deadline for RSVPs.
* **Wedding Invitations Send Date** (`Page 19`): Enter the date you want to send out invitations.
* **Wedding Invitations Send Time** (`Page 20`): Enter the time you want to send out invitations.
* **Wedding Invitations Delivery Method** (`Page 21`): Choose the method of delivering invitations (Email, USPS, Both).
* **Wedding Invitations Message** (`Page 22`): Enter a message you want to include with the invitations.
* **Wedding Invitations RSVP** (`Page 23`): Enter details about wedding invitations RSVP information.
* **Wedding Invitations Tracking Response Deadline** (`Page 24`): Enter the deadline for tracking RSVPs.
* **Wedding Invitations Additional Info** (`Page 25`): Enter any additional information about wedding invitations.
