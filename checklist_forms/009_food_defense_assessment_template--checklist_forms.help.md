# Food Defense Assessment Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Defense Assessment Template is designed to help food establishments assess their current food defense measures and identify areas for improvement to ensure the safety of food products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review your company's food safety policies and procedures before completing this form.
2. Answer all fields honestly and accurately.
3. Use the provided options for each field, and if you're unsure, contact your supervisor or food safety team for guidance.
4. Complete all fields, and update your previous answers as necessary to reflect the current state of your food defense measures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Assessment** (`general_assessment`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe your food defense measures and their effectiveness.
* **Food Safety Plan** (`food_safety_plan`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all food safety plans in place, such as Food Defense, Food Control, Food Recall, Food Recall Procedure, Food Recall Plan.
* **Recall Plan** (`recall_plan`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether a recall plan is in place.
* **Corrective Action** (`corrective_action`, `text`, required: false): Describe the corrective action taken to address contamination.
* **Review Frequency** (`review_frequency`, `number`, required: false): Select the frequency of reviews (e.g., once, daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether a training program is in place.
* **Food Defense Plan** (`food_defense_plan`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether a food defense plan is in place.
* **Corrective Action Plan** (`corrective_action_plan`, `text`, required: false): Describe the corrective action plan.
* **Food Control Measures** (`food_control_measures`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all food control measures in place, such as Traceability, SOP, Hazard Analysis.
* **Monitoring Plan** (`monitoring_plan`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether a monitoring plan is in place.
* **Quantity of Food Recalled** (`food_recalled_quantity`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the quantity of food recalled (e.g., 1000, 500, 100, 1-100, 101-1000).
* **Other** (`food_recalled_quantity_other`, `text`, required: false): Provide other details about the recalled quantity.
* **Recall Date** (`recall_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the last recall.
* **Last Recall Frequency** (`last_recall_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the frequency of the last recall (e.g., once, multiple, daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Training Program Frequency** (`training_program_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the frequency of the training program (e.g., once, multiple, daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Food Defense Frequency** (`food_defense_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the frequency of food defense measures (e.g., once, daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly).
* **Monitoring Frequency** (`monitoring_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the frequency of monitoring (e.g., once, daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly).

* **Corrective Action Status** (`corrective_action_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of corrective actions (e.g., in progress, complete, not started).
* **Corrective Action Completed** (`corrective_action_completed`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether corrective actions have been completed.
