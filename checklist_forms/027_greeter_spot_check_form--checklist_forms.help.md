# Greeter Spot Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Greeter Spot Check Form is used to record information about a customer interaction, including the greeting method, location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the fields as follows:
	* "Greeting": Enter a brief description of the greeting or interaction method used.
	* "Smile": Enter a brief description of the customer's smile during the interaction.
	* "Eye Contact": Enter a brief description of the customer's eye contact during the interaction.
	* "Greet Time": Enter the time of day when the greeting occurred.
	* "Greeting Type": Select all applicable greeting types (e.g., "Greeting", "Introduction", "Hello", "Good morning", "Good afternoon", "Good evening").
	* "Greet Location": Enter the location where the greeting occurred.
	* "Smile Score": Enter a numerical value to rate the customer's smile (e.g., 1-5).
	* "Contact Time": Enter the amount of time spent in contact with the customer.
	* "Follow Up": Enter any additional information about the interaction or follow-up instructions.
	* "Additional Comments": Enter any other relevant details about the interaction.
