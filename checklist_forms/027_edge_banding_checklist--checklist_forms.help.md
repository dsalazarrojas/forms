# Edge Banding Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Edge Banding Checklist is designed to help inspect and record the condition and status of edge banding on machinery or equipment. This form guides the inspector through a series of questions and prompts to ensure a thorough and accurate inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Edge Banding" option for the first question to indicate that this is an edge banding inspection.
2. Choose the condition of the edge banding from the options for the "Edge Banding Condition" question. Options include "Good", "Fair", and "Poor".
3. Identify the area being inspected from the options for the "Inspection Area" question. Options include "Area 1", "Area 2", and "Area 3".
4. Indicate the status of the edge banding for each area from the options for the "Edge Banding Status" question. Options include "True" and "False".
5. Enter the date and time of the inspection in the "Date of Inspection" and "Time of Inspection" fields, respectively.
6. Select the result of the inspection from the options for the "Inspection Result" question. Options include "Pass" and "Fail".
7. Provide any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
8. Sign and date the form in the "Supervisor Signature" and "Operator Signature" fields to confirm that the form has been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspect Edge Banding** (`1`, `select_one`, required): This question is used to indicate that this is an edge banding inspection.
* **Edge Banding Condition** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the condition of the edge banding, where:
	+ Good: The edge banding is in good condition.
	+ Fair: The edge banding is in fair condition.
	+ Poor: The edge banding is in poor condition.
* **Inspection Area** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Identify the area being inspected, where:
	+ Area 1: Area 1 of the machinery or equipment.
	+ Area 2: Area 2 of the machinery or equipment.
	+ Area 3: Area 3 of the machinery or equipment.
* **Edge Banding Status** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate the status of the edge banding, where:
	+ True: The edge banding is present.
	+ False: The edge banding is not present.
* **Date of Inspection** (`5`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the inspection in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time of Inspection** (`6`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the inspection in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Inspection Result** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Select the result of the inspection, where:
	+ Pass: The edge banding meets the required standards.
	+ Fail: The edge banding does not meet the required standards.
* **Notes** (`8`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes regarding the inspection.
* **Assigned Inspector** (`9`, `text`, optional): Record the name of the assigned inspector.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`10`, `email`, required): Sign and date the form in this field to confirm that the form has been completed.
* **Operator Signature** (`11`, `email`, required): Sign and date the form in this field to confirm that the form has been completed.
* **Comments** (`12`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of comments, where:
	+ Comments: Additional comments regarding the inspection.
	+ Other: Other comments or issues.
* **Edge Banding Comments** (`13`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes regarding the edge banding.
* **Additional Comments** (`14`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes regarding the inspection.
* **Edge Banding Checklist** (`15`, `select_one`, required): Select "Completed" to indicate that the form has been completed.
