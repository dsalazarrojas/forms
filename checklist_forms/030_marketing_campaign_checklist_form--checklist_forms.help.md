# Marketing Campaign Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture all the necessary details about a marketing campaign to ensure its successful execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the Campaign Details page with the campaign's title, objectives, and a brief description.
2. On the Target Audience page, select the correct target audience options for the campaign, including their language and location.
3. On the Marketing Channels page, choose the marketing channels you plan to use for the campaign, such as social media, email, or print.
4. On the Budget Allocation page, select the correct budget allocation for the campaign, including video, image, audio, social media, and email.
5. Finally, review the campaign details and ensure they are accurate and complete before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Campaign Details** (Campaign Details)
	* This page captures the campaign's title, objectives, and description.
### 2. **Campaign Objectives** (campaign objectives)
	* Please enter a brief description of the campaign's objectives.
### 3. **Target Audience** (Target Audience)
	* Select the target audience options for the campaign, including their gender and age group.
### 4. **Key Performance Metrics** (Key Performance Metrics)
	* This field is intended for future campaign analysis and is not currently active.
### 5. **Campaign Budget** (Campaign Budget)
	* Enter the total budget allocated for the campaign.
### 6. **Start Date** (Start Date)
	* Enter the campaign's start date.
### 7. **End Date** (End Date)
	* Enter the campaign's end date.
### 8. **Campaign Description** (Campaign Description)
	* Enter a brief description of the campaign.
### 9. **Campaign Status** (Campaign Status)
	* Select the campaign's status: Active or Inactive.
### 10. **Marketing Channels** (Marketing Channels)
	* Choose the marketing channels to be used for the campaign, such as social media, email, or print.
### 11. **Target Audience Gender** (Target Audience Gender)
	* Select the target audience's gender options.
### 12. **Target Audience Age Group** (Target Audience Age Group)
	* Select the target audience's age group options.
### 13. **Campaign Duration** (Campaign Duration)
	* This field is not currently active.
### 14. **Campaign Spend** (Campaign Spend)
	* Enter the total campaign spend.
### 15. **Target Audience Interests** (Target Audience Interests)
	* Enter the target audience's interests.
### 16. **Campaign Language** (Campaign Language)
	* Select the campaign's language.
### 17. **Target Audience Location** (Target Audience Location)
	* Enter the target audience's location.
### 18. **Budget Allocation** (Budget Allocation)
	* Choose the budget allocation for the campaign, including video, image, audio, social media, and email.
