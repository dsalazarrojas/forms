# Military Academy Admission Checklist Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Military Academy Admission Checklist Form Template is designed to guide applicants through the process of providing necessary information for military academy admission. This form gathers essential details about the applicant, including demographic information, application dates and times, academic history, medical information, references, upload documents, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:
* Please fill out all required fields, denoted by an asterisk \*.
* Enter your name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
* Select the correct option under "Submit" that best reflects your current application status.
* For fields with no clear instructions (e.g., "Academic History", "Test Results", "Medical Information", "References", and "Upload Documents"), please provide as much detail as possible about your educational background, test scores, any medical conditions, and any relevant professional or personal references.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information** (1): Please provide your full name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Application Date** (2): Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **Application Time** (3): Enter the time of day you submitted your application.
* **Academic History** (4): Describe your educational background, including any degrees earned, schools attended, and relevant courses or training.
* **Test Results** (5): If applicable, provide any test scores relevant to your application, such as standardized test scores (e.g., SAT, ACT, GRE).
* **Medical Information** (6): If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please describe them briefly.
* **References** (7): List your professional or personal references, including their name, job title, company, and contact information.
* **Upload Documents** (8): Upload any supporting documents relevant to your application, such as transcripts, ID photos, etc.
* **Submit** (9): Select one of the options:
	+ Option 1: My application is complete and ready for review.
	+ Option 2: I have provided all required documents, but I need more time to review my application before submitting.
	+ Option 3: I am still gathering required documents and will submit them when ready.
* **Email** (10): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (11): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We recommend saving your application form before submitting it for review to avoid losing any unsaved work.
* If you are experiencing technical difficulties while filling out the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
