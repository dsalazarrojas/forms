# Building Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document the results of a building inspection. It is designed to be completed by a trained inspector, and is meant to provide a detailed and objective assessment of the building's condition. The form includes sections for each major system in the building, including exterior, roof, foundation, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, interior, and fire safety systems. The inspector will evaluate each system and report on its condition, providing notes and recommendations as necessary.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact information in the "Inspector Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the building being inspected in the "Building Name" field.
3. Enter the address of the building in the "Building Address" field.
4. Select the type of building being inspected from the "Property Type" dropdown list.
5. Enter the date of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field.
6. Evaluate each system in the building, selecting "Pass" if it is in good condition, "Fail" if it is not, "Needs Repair" if it needs maintenance or repair, or "Not Assessed" if it was not inspected.
7. Enter any notes or comments for each system in the corresponding notes field.
8. Summarize any major issues or concerns in the "Major Issues Found" field.
9. Provide any recommended actions or suggestions in the "Recommendations" field.
10. Select the overall result of the inspection from the "Overall Inspection Result" dropdown list.
11. Sign and date your certification in the "Inspector Certification" field, indicating that you have accurately completed the inspection.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Be thorough and objective in your evaluations and notes.
* Use clear and concise language in your notes and recommendations.
* Take photos or videos to document any issues or concerns.
* Review and update this form regularly to reflect changes in building inspection procedures and standards.
