# dry-type-transformer-maintenance-checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide users through the maintenance process of a dry-type transformer, helping track and record essential information for routine inspections, maintenance records, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Answer each field accurately and thoroughly.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Routine Inspections** (page 1, label: Routine Inspections, type: text, required: false)
	+ This field is for recording any routine inspections performed on the transformer. Enter any notes or observations made during the inspection.
* **Maintenance Records** (page 2, label: Maintenance Records, type: select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select all maintenance activities performed on the transformer, such as oil level checks, transformer type, and transformer age.
* **Notes** (page 3, label: Notes, type: note, required: false)
	+ Record any additional notes or comments about the transformer.
* **Date** (page 4, label: Date, type: date, required: false)
	+ Enter the date the maintenance was performed or the inspection was done.
* **Time** (page 5, label: Time, type: time, required: false)
	+ Enter the time of the maintenance or inspection.
* **Transformer Status** (page 6, label: Transformer Status, type: select_one, required: true)
	+ Select the current status of the transformer, either Online, Offline, Offline due to low oil level, or Offline due to high temperature.
* **Transformer Oil Level** (page 7, label: Transformer Oil Level, type: select_multiple, required: true)
	+ Select the current oil level of the transformer, either High, Normal, or Low.
* **Transformer Type** (page 8, label: Transformer Type, type: select_multiple, required: true)
	+ Select the type of transformer, either Dry Type, Liquid Type, or Oilless Type.
* **Transformer Age** (page 9, label: Transformer Age, type: number, required: false)
	+ Enter the age of the transformer.
* **Assigned To** (page 10, label: Assigned To, type: select_one, required: true)
	+ Select the person responsible for the transformer, either John, Jane, or Jim.
* **Site Visit Status** (page 11, label: Site Visit Status, type: select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select the status of the site visit, either Completed, In Progress, or Pending.
* **Transformer Maintenance Status** (page 12, label: Transformer Maintenance Status, type: select_one, required: true)
	+ Select the maintenance status of the transformer, either Active, Inactive, or Inactive due to high temperature.
* **Maintenance Schedule** (page 13, label: Maintenance Schedule, type: date, required: false)
	+ Enter the next scheduled maintenance date.
* **Remarks** (page 14, label: Remarks, type: note, required: false)
	+ Record any additional comments or remarks about the transformer.
