# Hose Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for inspecting and reporting on the condition of a hose in different sections of a building.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information and photos.
2. Complete the form by answering all required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (\*).
3. If a section is not relevant, you can leave it blank or indicate "Not Applicable" in the field.
4. Take clear and well-lit photos of any issues found, and include them in the corresponding fields.
