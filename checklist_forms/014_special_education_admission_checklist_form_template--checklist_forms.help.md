# special_education_admission_checklist_form_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from students applying to or enrolled in special education programs. The purpose of this form is to help administrators understand the student's background, needs, and placement status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: Start by filling out the introduction section, which is not required but provides a brief description of the form's purpose.
2. **Student information**: Select all the relevant information about the student, such as their name, ID, and birthdate.
3. **Guardian information**: If applicable, select the relevant information about the student's guardians, such as their names and contact details.
4. **Educational history**: Describe the student's educational history, including any previous institutions attended and the student's current grade level.
5. **Medical history**: Provide any relevant medical information about the student, such as allergies or medical conditions that may impact their education.
6. **Special education needs**: Select all the special education needs that apply to the student, such as Autism Spectrum Disorder or Visual Loss.
7. **Additional needs**: Select any additional support services the student may require, such as physical or speech therapy.
8. **Placement status**: Choose the student's current placement status, such as currently enrolled or on a waitlist.
9. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the student's situation.
10. **Signature**: Finally, sign the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
