## Purpose

The Recruitment Checklist Form is a crucial tool for recruiters to gather and document information for new job openings. It is essential to ensure a streamlined hiring process and maintain records for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by providing general information about the job posting, including the job description, job posting date, and company name.
2. Select the relevant job title and category for the position.
3. Choose the suitable job type for the role.
4. Document the candidate selection process, including the number of candidates selected and their details.
5. Perform a reference check on the selected candidates, if applicable.
6. Arrange and conduct an interview with the selected candidate(s).
7. Administer a test or assessment to evaluate the candidate's skills and aptitude.
8. Create an offer letter with the agreed-upon terms and conditions.
9. Record and document any comments or feedback from the candidate or company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Posting** (`job_posting`): Enter a brief description of the job posting, including the job title and category.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Select the accurate job title for the position.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`): Choose the relevant job category for the role.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`): Determine the type of job (full-time, part-time, or contract).
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter the company's name and address.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Provide the contact person's name and details.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the company's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Provide the company's phone number.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter the company's physical address.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the city where the company is located.
* **State** (`state`): Enter the state or province where the company is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`): Enter the company's zip code.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any additional comments or feedback from the candidate or company.
* **Job Posting Date** (`job_posting_date`): Record the date the job was posted.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`): Document the date of the interview.
* **Salary Range** (`salary_range`): Enter the expected salary range for the position.
* **Salary Amount** (`salary_amount`): Enter the agreed-upon salary amount.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`): List any benefits or perks offered by the company.
* **Candidate Selection** (`candidate_selection`): Select the number of candidates chosen for the position and provide details about each.
* **Reference Check** (`reference_check`): Perform a reference check on the selected candidate(s), if applicable.
* **Interview** (`interview`): Arrange and conduct an interview with the selected candidate(s).
