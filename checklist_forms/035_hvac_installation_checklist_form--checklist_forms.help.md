# HVAC Installation Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential details from installers during the installation of HVAC systems, ensuring accurate and comprehensive tracking of such installations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Installer Name** in the designated field to identify yourself as the installer.
2. Provide the **Address** of the installation site where the HVAC system was installed.
3. Select all the **Equipment Installed**, including heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems.
4. Enter the **Installation Date**, when the HVAC system was installed.
5. Enter the **Start Date**, when the system began operating.
6. Enter the **End Date**, when the system ended its operation (if applicable).
7. Enter your **Technician Name**, who performed the installation.
8. Enter your **Supervisor Name**, who oversaw the installation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Installer Name (Installer Name, text, required)**: Enter your name to identify yourself as the installer.
* **Address (Address, text, required)**: Provide the address of the installation site.
* **Equipment Installed (Equipment Installed, select_multiple, required)**: Select all the equipment installed, including heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems.
* **Installation Date (Installation Date, date, required)**: Enter the date when the HVAC system was installed.
* **Start Date (Start Date, date, required)**: Enter the date when the system began operating.
* **End Date (End Date, date, required)**: Enter the date when the system ended its operation (if applicable).
* **Technician Name (Technician Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the technician who performed the installation.
* **Supervisor Name (Supervisor Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the supervisor who oversaw the installation process.
* **Comments (Comments, text, optional)**: Provide any additional comments or remarks about the installation.

## Tips
* Ensure that you complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the information provided, especially the dates, to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any additional comments or remarks, use the **Comments** field to provide context.
