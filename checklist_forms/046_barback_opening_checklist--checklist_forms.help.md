# Barback Opening Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Barback Opening Checklist is a comprehensive report for bar staff to ensure the bar area is safe and ready for service. Staff use this form to report on their progress during the opening shift, covering essential aspects like safety, cleanliness, equipment, and inventory.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, following the instructions provided for each field.
2. Review each section carefully, making sure to address all relevant questions and options.
3. If you're unsure about any field, use your best judgment and consult with your supervisor or colleagues if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Checklist Date** (`checklist_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the opening shift.
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, `time`, required): Record the start time of your shift.
* **Barback Name** (`barback_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Manager on Duty** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): Identify the name of the supervising manager on duty.
* **Bar Section/Station** (`bar_section`, `text`, optional): Select the bar section or station you're opening.
* **Doors Unlocked Safely** (`doors_unlocked`, `select_one`, required): Check if the doors are unlocked safely.
* **All Lights Turned On** (`lights_turned_on`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if all lights are turned on, including under-bar and back-bar areas.
* **Air Circulation Started** (`air_circulation`, `select_one`, required): Determine if air circulation (AC or fans) is turned on.
* **Bar Area Cleanliness Inspection** (`cleanliness_inspection`, `select_one`, required): Assess the bar area's cleanliness, considering any debris from the previous night.
* **Floor Clean and Dry** (`floor_condition`, `select_one`, required): Check the floor's condition, ensuring it's clean and dry.
* **Bar Counter Wiped Down** (`bar_counter_wiped`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if the bar counter is wiped down.
* **Bar Sink Clean** (`bar_sink_clean`, `select_one`, required): Check if the bar sink is clean and ready for use.
* **Ice Machine Stocked** (`ice_machine_stocked`, `select_one`, required): Verify if the ice machine has enough ice for service.
* **Glasses Stocked and Ready** (`glasses_stocked`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if all glass types are stocked and ready for use.
* **Liquor Bottles Organized** (`bottles_organized`, `select_one`, required): Check if the liquor bottles are properly organized by type and brand.
* **Fresh Garnishes Prepared** (`garnishes_fresh`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if fresh garnishes (like limes, lemons, cherries) are prepared.
* **Mixers and Sodas Stocked** (`mixers_stocked`, `select_one`, required): Verify if mixers and sodas are stocked.
* **Equipment Working Properly** (`equipment_check`, `select_one`, required): Check if all equipment (like blenders, juicers, shakers) is working properly.
* **Beer Taps Flowing Properly** (`taps_flowing`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm if beer taps are flowing properly (if applicable).
* **Cash Register Set Up** (`register_setup`, `select_one`, required): Check if the cash register is set up and counted.
* **POS System Started** (`pos_system_started`, `select_one`, required): Verify if the POS system is started.
* **Bar Supplies Inspected** (`supplies_inspected`, `select_one`, required): Check if bar supplies (like napkins, straws, stirrers) are adequately stocked.
* **Safety Hazards Noted** (`safety_hazards`, `text`, optional): Report any safety hazards observed.
* **Opening Checklist Complete** (`opening_complete`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if the opening checklist is complete.

Note: Please review the form carefully and answer each field honestly. If you're unsure about any field, use your best judgment and consult with your supervisor or colleagues if necessary.
