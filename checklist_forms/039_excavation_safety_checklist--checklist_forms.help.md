# Excavation safety checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Excavation Safety Checklist is a form used to evaluate the safety standards and protocols in place on an excavation site. It is designed to assess the presence of basic safety measures and identify areas of concern.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully and answer truthfully based on your current practices and protocols.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. For text fields, provide a brief explanation or description of the item listed.
4. Ensure you answer all questions to provide a comprehensive assessment of the site's safety standards.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Excavation Safety Concerns** (`excavation_safety_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): List any concerns or risks you've identified on the excavation site.
- **Personal Protection Equipment** (`personal_protection_equipment`, select_one, optional): Please select the correct type of personal protection equipment used by workers on site, such as hard hats, harnesses, or safety glasses.
- **Site Boundary** (`site_boundary`, text, optional): Describe the site boundary, including any markings, signs, or fencing that define the limits of the site.
- **Hazard Identification** (`hazard_identification`, note, optional): Describe any hazards identified on the site, including but not limited to, physical hazards, environmental hazards, or other risks.
- **Emergency Procedure** (`emergency_procedure`, select_one, optional): Please select if an emergency procedure is in place, such as emergency contact numbers or evacuation plans.
- **Safety Training** (`safety_training`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if the workers on site have received safety training, such as on-site training or regular refreshers.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for emergency contact, including their phone number and email.
- **Reporting Incident** (`reporting_incident`, select_one, optional): Please select if incidents are reported and documented, including near misses or actual incidents.
- **Equipment Checklist** (`equipment_checklist`, select_multiple, optional): List the equipment that is regularly inspected and maintained on site, such as cranes, excavators, or other machinery.
