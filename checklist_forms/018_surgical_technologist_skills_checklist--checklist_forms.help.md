# Surgical Technologist Skills Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to assess and evaluate the skills and competencies of surgical technologists in various areas, including medical skills, teamwork, and leadership.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. For select_one fields, choose one option that best describes your skills or experience.
3. For select_multiple fields, choose all the options that apply to you.
4. Enter free-text answers for fields with a text type.
5. Make sure to answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Facial Plexus**: This field assesses your knowledge and skills in managing medical plexus. Choose 'Yes' if you have experience or training in this area.
* **Medical Finger**: This field evaluates your ability to handle and manage medical finger-related tasks. Choose all the options that apply to you.
* **Medical Hand**: This field checks your proficiency in medical hand-related skills. Select all the options that apply.
* **Patient Positioning**: This field tests your expertise in positioning patients for surgery. Choose 'Yes' if you have experience or training in this area.
* **Surgical Site**: Enter the name of the surgical site you have experience with.
* **Scrub In**: This field assesses your ability to prepare patients for surgery. Choose all the options that apply.
* **Sterile Technique**: This field evaluates your adherence to sterile techniques in the operating room. Enter a brief description of your practices.
* **Surgical Technology**: Enter the name of the surgical technology you are familiar with.
* **Surgical Waste**: Enter the name of the surgical waste management practices you follow.
* **Teamwork**: This field evaluates your ability to work effectively with the surgical team. Choose all the options that apply.
* **Patient Safety**: This field assesses your commitment to patient safety. Enter a brief description of your practices and procedures.
* **Surgical Technologist Communication**: This field evaluates your communication skills with patients, families, and colleagues. Choose all the options that apply.
* **Leadership**: This field assesses your leadership skills within the surgical team. Enter a brief description of your leadership practices.
* **Patient Family Education**: Enter the name of the patient education practices you are familiar with.
* **Patient Family Counseling**: Enter the name of the patient counseling practices you follow.
* **Patient Family Support**: Enter the name of the patient support practices you implement.
* **Patient Family Education and Counseling**: Enter the name of the patient education and counseling practices you follow.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each field.
* Take your time to review each field carefully.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or colleagues.
* Complete the form to the best of your abilities and knowledge.
