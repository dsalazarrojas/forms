# Weight Control Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you track and assess your weight control habits. It is a tool to monitor your progress and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
2. Select the options that best describe your current habits and situation.
3. Provide details about your morning habits, exercise frequency and intensity, sleep patterns, stress levels, and nutritional plans.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exercise frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, select_one): Choose one of the following options that best describes your exercise frequency:
	+ Less than 1 time/week
	+ 1-2 times/week
	+ 3-4 times/week
	+ 5+ times/week
* **Sleep hours** (`sleep_hours`, number): Enter the number of hours you sleep each night.
* **Water intake** (`water_intake`, number): Enter the amount of water you consume each day.
