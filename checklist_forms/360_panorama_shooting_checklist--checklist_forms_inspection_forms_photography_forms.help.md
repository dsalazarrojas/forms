# 360 Panorama Shooting Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 360 Panorama Shooting Checklist is a form used to collect and record essential information about the 360-degree panorama shoot. This form helps ensure that all relevant details are captured and documented for post-processing and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by following the instructions on each page.
2. Start by entering the project and contact information on page 1.
3. Move on to page 2 to enter the shoot date and start time.
4. On page 3, enter the location description, GPS coordinates, and other relevant details.
5. On page 4, specify the camera and lens settings, including the tripod and panorama capture mode.
6. Enter the exposure settings, number of shots captured, and file format on page 5.
7. On page 6, indicate how you will handle backups and overlaps.
8. Finally, provide any additional information about the environmental conditions and post-processing instructions on page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project or assignment name**: A text field where you enter the name of the project or assignment you are shooting for. (Required)
* **Photographer / primary contact name**: Enter the full name of the photographer or primary contact for the project. (Required)
* **Email address**: Enter the email address of the primary contact or best address for delivery and follow-up. (Required)
* **Shoot date**: Enter the scheduled date of the panorama capture in the format MM/DD/YYYY. (Required)
* **Shoot start time**: Enter the local time when the capture begins. (Required)
* **Location / venue description**: Enter a description of the location or venue, including the address or room name. (Required)
* **GPS latitude**: Enter the decimal degrees of the GPS latitude (optional but helpful). (Optional)
* **GPS longitude**: Enter the decimal degrees of the GPS longitude (optional but helpful). (Optional)
* **Camera model**: Enter the model of the camera used, including any relevant firmware version. (Required)
* **Lens used / focal length**: Enter the lens used, including the focal length (e.g., 8mm fisheye, 24mm rectilinear). (Optional)
* **Tripod / head used and stability confirmed**: Confirm if a stable tripod and pano head are used. (Required)
* **Panorama capture mode**: Choose the capture projection or workflow used (e.g., Spherical, Equirectangular, Cylindrical, Cubemap). (Required)
* **Bracketing / HDR approach**: Indicate the bracketing approach used (e.g., None, Exposure bracketing, Focus bracketing, Exposure + focus). (Optional)
* **Exposure settings**: Enter the aperture, shutter, ISO, and bracket EV settings used. (Required)
* **Total number of shots captured**: Enter the total number of exposures across the full nodal sweep. (Required)
* **Primary file format captured**: Choose the format in which the images were captured (e.g., RAW, JPEG, RAW + JPEG). (Required)
* **On-site backup method**: Indicate how the images were protected immediately after capture (e.g., Redundant SD cards, On-site backup drive, Cloud upload (cell/wifi), No backup). (Required)
* **Recommended overlap percentage**: Enter the recommended overlap percentage for the shoot. (Required)
* **Weather / environmental conditions**: Note any weather or environmental conditions that may affect the shoot (e.g., Clear / calm, Partly cloudy, Overcast, Rain / wet, Windy, Night / low light). (Optional)
* **Site permissions and power availability**: Select all that apply for logistics (e.g., Location permit obtained, Client on-site / authorized, Power outlet available, Restricted public access, No special permissions required). (Optional)
* **Pre-shoot checks completed**: Confirm whether the pre-shoot checks were completed. (Required)
* **Post-processing instructions and notes**: Enter any additional information or notes about post-processing, stitching, color-matching, delivery format, or special requests. (Optional)

## Tips

* Ensure accuracy in entering the date and time to avoid confusion in post-processing and delivery.
* Be specific when entering the camera and lens settings to ensure proper stitching and blending.
* Use the recommended overlap percentage to ensure proper overlap for stitching and blending.
* Confirm the pre-shoot checks to ensure that everything is in order for the shoot.
* Enter all relevant details in the post-processing instructions and notes for smooth delivery and client communication.
