# Implanted Stimulator MRI Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential details related to the MRI scan of a patient with an implanted stimulator. The purpose of this form is to ensure that the radiologist is aware of the implanted device and its potential impact on the scan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding with the scan.
2. Fill in the relevant fields with accurate information about the patient's screening details, patient authorization, patient information, radiologist notes, radiologist recommendations, scan date, scan technology, scan location, patient name, medical record number, medical condition, implant type, implant material, implant size, and patient age.
3. If the patient has any implanted medical devices that may affect the MRI scan, make sure to fill out the relevant fields with the correct information.

## Tips
- If you're unsure about any information, consult with the referring physician or the patient's medical team.
- This form is designed to be completed by the radiologist or radiographer responsible for conducting the MRI scan.
- Make sure to review the form carefully before proceeding with the scan.
- If the patient has a pacemaker or other implanted medical device, fill out the relevant fields to ensure accurate diagnosis and patient safety.
