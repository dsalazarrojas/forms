# Digital Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Checklist Form is designed to collect comprehensive information about operations, facilities, and teams. It is intended to be used for documentation and decision-making purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Navigate to the first page, "Operation".
2. Select the "Operation" using the "select_one" option.
3. Fill in the "Operations Date" using the date field.
4. Enter the "Start Time" using the time field.
5. Provide the "Operation Location" using the text field.
6. For each subsequent page, follow the same steps as the first page.
7. When filling out the form, ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Operation (select_one)**: Select the operation being documented. Choose from the options provided.
* **Operations Date (date)**: Enter the date of the operation.
* **Start Time (time)**: Enter the start time of the operation.
* **Operation Location (text)**: Enter the location of the operation.
* **Facilities (select_multiple)**: Select all applicable facilities related to the operation. Choose from the options provided.
* **Facilities Date (date)**: Enter the date of the facilities related to the operation.
* **Start Time (time)**: Enter the start time of the facilities related to the operation.
* **Facility Location (text)**: Enter the location of the facilities related to the operation.
* **Teams (select_multiple)**: Select all applicable teams related to the operation. Choose from the options provided.
* **Teams Date (date)**: Enter the date of the teams related to the operation.
* **Start Time (time)**: Enter the start time of the teams related to the operation.
* **Team Location (text)**: Enter the location of the teams related to the operation.
* **Note (note)**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the operation.
* **Note Field (text)**: Enter any additional information or comments about the operation.
