# Refrigerator Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refrigerator Maintenance Checklist is a tool designed to help you monitor and maintain your refrigerator, ensuring it remains in good condition. This form will guide you through a series of questions to help you identify areas that require attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question with one of the options provided. For each question, choose "Yes" if the corresponding aspect of your refrigerator requires attention, or "No" if it does not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check the Temperature**: Check the refrigerator's temperature settings. Is the temperature set correctly? (`check_temperature`): 
  • Check if the temperature settings are appropriate for your food storage needs.
* **Check Doors**: Check the condition of your refrigerator's doors. Are they in good condition? (`check_doors`): 
  • Inspect the doors for any signs of wear, tear, or damage.
* **Clean Seals**: Check and clean the seals around the doors. Is it clean? (`clean_seals`): 
  • Check the condition of the seals and wipe them clean if necessary.
* **Check Icebox**: Check the icebox (if applicable). Is it properly functioning? (`check_icebox`): 
  • Ensure the icebox is working correctly and not producing excessive frost or ice.
* **Check Labels**: Check the labels on the shelves. Are they in good condition? (`check_labels`): 
  • Inspect the labels for any signs of wear or damage.
* **Check Door Seals**: Check the seals on the doors. Are they in good condition? (`check_door_seals`): 
  • Inspect the door seals for any signs of wear or damage.
* **Check Drips**: Check for any signs of water leaks or drips. Is there any water present? (`check_drips`): 
  • Look for any signs of water leaks or excessive moisture.
* **Check Fans**: Check the refrigerator's fans. Are they working properly? (`check_fans`): 
  • Ensure the fans are spinning smoothly and not producing any unusual noises.
* **Check Shelves**: Check the condition of your refrigerator's shelves. Are they in good condition? (`check_shelves`): 
  • Inspect the shelves for any signs of wear, tears, or damage.
* **Check Rear**: Check the back of the refrigerator. Is it clean and in good condition? (`check_rear`): 
  • Inspect the back for any signs of wear or damage.
* **Check Bottom**: Check the bottom of the refrigerator. Is it clean and in good condition? (`check_bottom`): 
  • Inspect the bottom for any signs of wear, tears, or moisture.
* **Check Top**: Check the top of the refrigerator. Is it clean and in good condition? (`check_top`): 
  • Inspect the top for any signs of wear or damage.
* **Check Front**: Check the front of the refrigerator. Is it clean and in good condition? (`check_front`): 
  • Inspect the front for any signs of wear or damage.
* **Check Back**: Check the back of the refrigerator. Is it clean and in good condition? (`check_back`): 
  • Inspect the back for any signs of wear or damage.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer each question carefully and truthfully to ensure accurate results.
- If you are unsure about any aspect of your refrigerator's condition, consult the manufacturer's instructions or contact a professional for assistance.
- Regularly maintain your refrigerator to prevent minor issues from becoming major problems.
