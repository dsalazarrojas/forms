# Preschool Classroom Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the safety and emergency preparedness of a preschool classroom. It helps administrators and teachers evaluate the effectiveness of their safety measures and identify potential risks. Completing this form will help ensure that your classroom is prepared for any situation that may arise during the school day.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have the necessary information to complete it accurately.
2. On each page, select the answer that best describes your classroom's safety measures or preparedness.
3. For select_multiple fields, select all applicable options.
4. For select_one or select_multiple fields with only two options, choose between "Yes" or "No".
5. Ensure you have checked all pages and submitted the form only when all fields are filled.
