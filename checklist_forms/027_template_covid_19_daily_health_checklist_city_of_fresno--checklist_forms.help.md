# Template Covid 19 Daily Health Checklist City Of Fresno - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Template Covid 19 Daily Health Checklist is a tool used to collect information about an individual's health status related to COVID-19. It helps City Of Fresno employees assess their health risks and take necessary precautions to prevent the spread of the virus.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your Employee Name in the first field.
* Enter your Employee Email in the second field.
* For each question, select all that apply from the provided options.
* Make sure to answer all questions honestly to ensure accurate tracking of your health status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee record.
* **Employee Email**: Enter your work email address, which is often used for official communication.
* **Have You Experienced COVID-19 Symptoms?**: Select all symptoms that you are experiencing, such as:
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Shortness of breath or difficulty breathing
	+ Sore throat
	+ Headache
	+ Fatigue
	+ Muscle or body aches
	+ Diarrhea
	+ Nausea or vomiting
	+ New loss of taste or smell
	+ New onset or worsening of a cough
	+ New onset or worsening of shortness of breath
	+ New onset or worsening of a sore throat
* **Have You Had Contact with Someone with COVID-19?**: Select the category that best describes your contact with someone with COVID-19, such as:
	+ Family member or household member
	+ Friend or social friend
	+ Co-worker
	+ Classmate
	+ Teacher or professor
	+ Neighbor
	+ Other
* **Have You Traveled to a COVID-19 Hotspot Recently?**: Select 'True' if you have traveled to an area with a high risk of COVID-19, 'False' otherwise.
* **Have You Traveled Outside of the US Recently?**: Select 'True' if you have traveled outside of the US, 'False' otherwise.
* **Have You Received a COVID-19 Vaccination?**: Select 'True' if you have received a COVID-19 vaccination, 'False' otherwise.
