# Machine Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document information on machine maintenance, including the equipment used, location, type of maintenance, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of equipment used (e.g., machine, device, etc.).
2. Indicate the location of the equipment.
3. Choose the type of maintenance performed (e.g., routine, repair, replacement, etc.).
4. Enter the date of the last maintenance performed.
5. Provide the next scheduled maintenance date.
6. Select the maintenance status (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
7. Enter any additional maintenance type description if required.
8. Add any comments or notes about the maintenance activity.
9. Sign and date the form as the supervisor.
10. Sign and date the form as the technician.
11. Enter the date the form was submitted.
12. Enter the equipment model.
13. Enter the equipment serial number.
14. Enter the equipment reorder point.
15. Enter the last maintenance cost.
16. Enter the planned maintenance cost.
17. Enter the actual maintenance cost.
18. Select whether maintenance is performed frequently or not.
19. Select whether the next maintenance is scheduled or not.
20. Add any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Used** (equipment_used): Select the type of equipment being maintained.
* **Location** (location): Enter the location where the equipment is installed or operated.
* **Type of maintenance** (type_of_maintenance): Choose the type of maintenance performed (e.g., routine, repair, replacement, etc.).
* **Date last maintenance** (date_last_maintenance): Enter the date of the last maintenance performed.
* **Next maintenance due** (next_maintenance_due): Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance.
* **Maintenance status** (maintenance_status): Select the maintenance status (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
* **Maintenance type description** (maintenance_type_description): Enter any additional information about the maintenance type if required.
* **Comments** (comments): Add any comments or notes about the maintenance activity.
* **Supervisor signature** (supervisor_signature): Sign and date the form as the supervisor.
* **Technician signature** (technician_signature): Sign and date the form as the technician.
* **Date submitted** (date_submitted): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Equipment model** (equipment_model): Enter the equipment model.
* **Equipment serial number** (equipment_serial_number): Enter the equipment serial number.
* **Equipment reorder point** (equipment_reorder_point): Enter the equipment reorder point.
* **Last maintenance cost** (last_maintenance_cost): Enter the cost of the last maintenance performed.
* **Planned maintenance cost** (planned_maintenance_cost): Enter the planned maintenance cost.
* **Actual maintenance cost** (actual_maintenance_cost): Enter the actual maintenance cost.
* **Maintenance frequency** (maintenance_frequency): Select whether maintenance is performed frequently or not.
* **Next maintenance frequency** (next_maintenance_frequency): Select whether the next maintenance is scheduled or not.
* **Notes** (notes): Add any additional notes about the maintenance activity.
* **Supervisor name** (supervisor_name): Enter the name of the supervisor.
