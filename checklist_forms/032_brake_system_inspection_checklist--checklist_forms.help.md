# Brake System Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a detailed inspection checklist for the brake system of a vehicle. The form is designed to gather information about the condition of the brake system and identify any potential issues that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the "Inspection Date" field and enter the date of the inspection.
2. Enter the "Inspector Name" field with the name of the person conducting the inspection.
3. Enter the "Vehicle Information" field with the year, make, and model of the vehicle.
4. Check the brake fluid level in the "Brake Fluid Level" field. Select the appropriate option based on the level of the fluid.
5. Check the brake fluid condition in the "Brake Fluid Condition" field. Select the option that best describes the color and clarity of the fluid.
6. Check for any evidence of fluid leaks in the "Evidence of Fluid Leaks" field.
7. Measure the thickness of the front and rear brake pads in the "Front Pad Thickness" and "Rear Pad Thickness" fields, respectively. Select the option that best describes the thickness of the pads.
8. Check for uneven pad wear in the "Uneven Pad Wear" field.
9. Check the condition of the brake rotors in the "Rotor Surface Condition" field. Select the option that best describes the condition of the rotors.
10. Check the thickness of the rotors in the "Rotor Thickness Check" field.
11. Check the condition of the brake hoses in the "Brake Hoses Condition" field. Select the option that best describes the condition of the hoses.
12. Check if the calipers operate properly in the "Caliper Operation" field.
13. Select the overall inspection result in the "Overall Inspection Result" field based on the findings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date** (2): Enter the date of the inspection in this field. This will help track when the inspection was performed.
* **Inspector Name** (3): Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Vehicle Information** (4): Enter the year, make, and model of the vehicle being inspected.
* **Brake Fluid Level** (7): Check the brake fluid level in the reservoir or master cylinder. Select the option that best describes the level:
	+ Full: The fluid level is at or above the recommended level.
	+ Good: The fluid level is slightly below the recommended level.
	+ Low: The fluid level is well below the recommended level.
	+ Critical: The fluid level is critically low and needs to be addressed immediately.
	+ Not visible: The fluid level is not visible or accessible.
* **Brake Fluid Condition** (8): Check the color and clarity of the brake fluid. Select the option that best describes the condition:
	+ Clear: The fluid is clear and free of contaminants.
	+ Slightly discolored: The fluid is slightly cloudy or discolored.
	+ Dark: The fluid is dark or almost black.
	+ Black or burnt: The fluid is black or burnt.
	+ Cloudy or contaminated: The fluid is cloudy or has contaminants.
* **Evidence of Fluid Leaks** (9): Check for any signs of fluid leaks around the brake system. Select the option that best describes the condition:
	+ No leaks: No fluid leaks are present.
	+ Minor weeping: There are minor fluid leaks present.
	+ Active leak: There is an active fluid leak.
	+ Severe leak: There is a severe fluid leak.
* **Front Pad Thickness** (11): Check the thickness of the front brake pads. Select the option that best describes the thickness:
	+ 5+ mm (good): The pads are above the recommended thickness.
	+ 3-5 mm (fair): The pads are slightly above the recommended minimum thickness.
	+ 1-3 mm (worn): The pads are below the recommended minimum thickness.
	+ Less than 1 mm (critical): The pads are critically worn and need to be replaced.
* **Rear Pad Thickness** (12): Check the thickness of the rear brake pads. Select the option that best describes the thickness:
	+ 5+ mm (good): The pads are above the recommended thickness.
	+ 3-5 mm (fair): The pads are slightly above the recommended minimum thickness.
	+ 1-3 mm (worn): The pads are below the recommended minimum thickness.
	+ Less than 1 mm (critical): The pads are critically worn and need to be replaced.
* **Uneven Pad Wear** (13): Check for uneven pad wear. Select the option that best describes the condition:
	+ Uniform wear: The pads are wearing evenly.
	+ Slightly uneven: The pads are wearing slightly unevenly.
	+ Very uneven: The pads are wearing very unevenly.
	+ Severely worn: The pads are severely worn and need to be replaced.
* **Rotor Surface Condition** (15): Check the condition of the brake rotors. Select the option that best describes the condition:
	+ Smooth and clean: The rotors are smooth and clean.
	+ Light scoring: The rotors have light scoring.
	+ Heavy scoring: The rotors have heavy scoring.
	+ Warped or cracked: The rotors are warped or cracked.
	+ Failed: The rotors are failed and need to be replaced.
* **Rotor Thickness Check** (16): Check the thickness of the rotors. Select the option that best describes the condition:
	+ Good thickness: The rotors are within the recommended thickness.
	+ At minimum limit: The rotors are at the minimum recommended thickness.
	+ Below minimum: The rotors are below the recommended minimum thickness.
	+ Cannot determine: The thickness of the rotors cannot be determined.
* **Brake Hoses Condition** (18): Check the condition of the brake hoses. Select the option that best describes the condition:
	+ Excellent: The hoses are in excellent condition.
	+ Good: The hoses are in good condition.
	+ Fair: The hoses are slightly worn or damaged.
	+ Cracked or worn: The hoses are cracked or worn.
	+ Failed: The hoses are failed and need to be replaced.
* **Caliper Operation** (19): Check if the calipers operate properly. Select the option that best describes the condition:
	+ Proper operation: The calipers operate properly.
	+ Slightly sluggish: The calipers are slightly slow or stiff.
	+ Very sluggish: The calipers are very slow or stiff.
	+ Seized: The calipers are seized or stuck.
	+ Not applicable: The calipers are not applicable for this vehicle.
* **Overall Inspection Result** (21): Select the overall inspection result based on the findings:
	+ Safe - passes inspection: The vehicle passes the inspection with no issues.
	+ Safe with minor repairs needed: The vehicle passes the inspection but needs minor repairs.
	+ Unsafe - requires repair: The vehicle is unsafe and needs repairs.
	+ Unsafe - vehicle not drivable: The vehicle is not safe to drive.
