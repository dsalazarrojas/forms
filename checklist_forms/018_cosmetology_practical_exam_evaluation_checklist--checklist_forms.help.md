# Cosmetology Practical Exam Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate a cosmetologist's practical exam. It is meant to be filled out by a proctor or examiner after the cosmetologist has completed their practical exam.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the cosmetologist's exam preparation, including their knowledge of cosmetology techniques, laws, and regulations.
2. Evaluate the cosmetologist's performance in areas such as safety procedures, sanitation and infection control, and client care.
3. Review the cosmetologist's answers to state board questions.
4. Check all the boxes and provide comments for each section as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exam Preparation**: This section assesses the cosmetologist's preparation for the exam, including their knowledge of cosmetology techniques, laws, and regulations. 
* **Cosmetologist Performance**: This section evaluates the cosmetologist's performance during the exam, including their ability to apply cosmetology techniques safely and effectively. 
* **Safety Procedures**: This section assesses the cosmetologist's ability to follow safety procedures, including proper use of equipment and chemicals. 
* **Sanitation and Infection Control**: This section evaluates the cosmetologist's ability to maintain a clean and safe environment for clients, including proper hand washing and sanitation techniques. 
* **Client Care**: This section assesses the cosmetologist's ability to provide excellent client care, including understanding and respecting client needs and boundaries. 
* **State Board Questions**: This section evaluates the cosmetologist's knowledge of state board questions, including their understanding of cosmetology laws and regulations. 
* **State Board Questions 2-18**: These sections evaluate the cosmetologist's knowledge of additional state board questions, including their understanding of cosmetology laws and regulations. 

## Tips
* Make sure to thoroughly review each section and provide specific comments for each item.
* Check all the boxes for each section, and include specific comments as needed.
* Be thorough and detailed in your feedback, as this will help the cosmetologist identify areas for improvement.
