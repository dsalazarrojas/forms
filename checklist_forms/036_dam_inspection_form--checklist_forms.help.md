# Dam Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dam Inspection Form is used to collect information about dams during an inspection, ensuring that the data collected is reliable and useful for further analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of dam being inspected (Culvert, Dam, Levee, Weir, or Other).
2. Choose the inspection method used (Visual, Non-Visual, Acoustic, or Other).
3. Enter the date of the inspection.
4. Enter the time of the inspection.
5. Identify the lead of the inspection team.
6. Provide any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
7. Report the condition of the dam (Good, Fair, Poor, or Unknown).
8. Record the current water level (Normal, Low, High, or Unknown).
9. Note the current water flow (Normal, Low, High, or Unknown).
10. Enter the current water temperature (if applicable).
11. Check if a flow control device is present (True or False).
12. Provide any additional inspection notes.
13. Schedule the next inspection date.
14. Schedule the next inspection time.
15. Specify the frequency of future inspections (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, or Unknown).
16. Enter any comments from the inspector.
17. Provide the address or location of the facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dam Type (1)**: Select the type of dam being inspected (Culvert, Dam, Levee, Weir, or Other).
* **Inspection Method (3)**: Choose the method used to inspect the dam (Visual, Non-Visual, Acoustic, or Other).
* **Date Of Inspection (4)**: Enter the date of the inspection in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time Of Inspection (5)**: Enter the time of the inspection in HH:MM:SS format.
* **Inspection Team Lead (6)**: Identify the lead of the inspection team.
* **Comments (7)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Dam Condition (8)**: Report the condition of the dam (Good, Fair, Poor, or Unknown).
* **Water Level (9)**: Record the current water level of the dam (Normal, Low, High, or Unknown).
* **Water Flow (10)**: Note the current water flow of the dam (Normal, Low, High, or Unknown).
* **Water Temperature (11)**: Enter the current water temperature.
* **Flow Control Device (12)**: Check if a flow control device is present (True or False).
* **Inspection Notes (13)**: Provide any additional inspection notes.
* **Next Inspection Date (14)**: Schedule the next inspection date.
* **Next Inspection Time (15)**: Schedule the next inspection time.
* **Inspection Frequency (16)**: Specify the frequency of future inspections (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, or Unknown).
* **Inspector Comments (17)**: Enter any comments from the inspector.
* **Facility Location (18)**: Provide the address or location of the facility.
