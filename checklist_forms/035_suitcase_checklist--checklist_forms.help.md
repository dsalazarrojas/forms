# Suitcase Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Suitcase Checklist form is designed to assist you in organizing the essential items you need to pack for a trip. By providing a structured layout, you can ensure you don't forget anything important and pack only what you need, making your travel experience more efficient and stress-free.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields for each category, such as Clothing, Shoes, Socks, Accessories, and Travel Docs.
* Use the "Other items" field to add any additional items that don't fit into the other categories.
* You can add notes for each category, if needed, by using the "Pack items note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pack items** (`items_to_pack`, text, required): Enter a list of items you want to pack for your trip.
* **Pack items note** (`items_to_pack_note`, note, required): Add any additional notes about the items you are packing.
* **Clothing** (`clothing`, text, required): List the clothing items you plan to pack.
* **Shoes** (`shoes`, text, required): List the shoe items you plan to pack.
* **Socks** (`socks`, text, required): List the sock items you plan to pack.
* **Accessories** (`accessories`, text, required): List the accessory items you plan to pack.
* **Travel docs** (`travel_docs`, text, required): List the travel documents you need, such as passports, visas, or health insurance cards.
* **Other items** (`other_items`, text, optional): Add any additional items that don't fit into the other categories.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when listing items in each category.
* Use this form as a starting point and adjust it according to your personal preferences and needs.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure you have all the necessary items.
