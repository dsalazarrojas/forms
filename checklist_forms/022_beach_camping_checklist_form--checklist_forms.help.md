# Beach Camping Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive checklist for planning and organizing a beach camping trip. It helps you prepare for a safe and enjoyable experience by gathering information on trip details, equipment needs, and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the fields as follows:

* Enter your trip leader's name and contact details (fields 1-3).
* Specify the trip dates (fields 4-5).
* Enter the number of campers participating in the trip (fields 6-8).
* Choose the necessary equipment and gear for the trip (fields 9-13).
* Select the plan for food and water (field 15) and transportation (field 16).
* Confirm permits and weather checks (fields 17 and 18).
* Provide emergency contact information (fields 19-20).
* If necessary, specify any special needs or considerations (field 21).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trip Leader Name** (1) (`trip_leader_name`, required): Enter your full name as the trip leader.
* **Email Address** (2) (`email_address`, required): Enter your contact email address for trip updates and communications.
* **Phone Number** (3) (`phone_number`, required): Enter your contact phone number for emergency situations.
* **Trip Start Date** (4) (`trip_start_date`, required): Enter the start date of your beach camping trip.
* **Trip End Date** (5) (`trip_end_date`, required): Enter the end date of your beach camping trip.
* **Number of Campers** (6) (`number_of_campers`, required): Enter the total number of people attending the trip.
* **Number of Adults** (7) (`number_of_adults`, required): Enter the number of adults (18 and over) attending the trip.
* **Number of Children** (8) (`number_of_children`, required): Enter the number of children (under 18) attending the trip.
* **Campsite Location** (9) (`campsite_location`, required): Enter the name of the beach or campground where you will be staying.
* **Shelter Items** (10) (`shelter_items`, required): Choose the shelter items you will be bringing (e.g., Tent, Beach Umbrella, etc.).
* **Sleeping Gear** (11) (`sleeping_gear`, required): Choose the sleeping gear you will be bringing (e.g., Sleeping Bags, Air Mattresses, etc.).
* **Cooking Equipment** (12) (`cooking_equipment`, required): Choose the cooking equipment you will be bringing (e.g., Camp Stove, Cooler, etc.).
* **Beach Gear** (13) (`beach_gear`, required): Choose the beach gear you will be bringing (e.g., Beach Chairs, Beach Towels, etc.).
* **Safety Items** (14) (`safety_items`, required): Choose the safety items you will be bringing (e.g., First Aid Kit, Sunscreen, etc.).
* **Food and Water Plan** (15) (`food_water`, required): Choose how you will handle meals and water (e.g., bring all food and water, buy food and water there, etc.).
* **Transportation Method** (16) (`transportation`, required): Choose the primary transportation method for the trip.
* **Permits Needed** (17) (`permits_needed`, required): Confirm if you need any permits for the trip.
* **Weather Forecast Checked** (18) (`weather_check`, required): Confirm if you have checked the weather forecast for the trip.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (19) (`emergency_contact`, required): Enter the name and phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (20) (`emergency_contact_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Special Needs or Considerations** (21) (`special_needs`, optional): Specify any special needs or considerations (e.g., medical conditions, accessibility requirements).
* **Checklist Review Complete** (22) (`checklist_complete`, required): Confirm if you have reviewed all the fields in this checklist.
