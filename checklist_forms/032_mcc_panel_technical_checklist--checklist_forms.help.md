# MCC Panel Technical Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MCC Panel Technical Checklist form is a tool for the Maintenance and Construction Committee (MCC) to perform technical checklists on panels. This form helps to ensure that the panel is properly inspected and recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **General Checklist** (General Checklist)
    * (Free-text field)

### 2. **Electrical Checklist** (Electrical Checklist)
    * (Select-one field) - Choose one of the options: *Panel lights*, *Panel lights 2*, *Panel lights 3*

### 3. **Mechanical Checklist** (Mechanical Checklist)
    * (Select-multiple field) - Choose multiple options: *Panel 1*, *Panel 2*

### 4. **Safety Checklist** (Safety Checklist)
    * (Free-text field)

### 5. **Maintenance Checklist** (Maintenance Checklist)

## Tips
