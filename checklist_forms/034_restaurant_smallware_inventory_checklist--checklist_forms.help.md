# Restaurant Smallware Inventory Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to track inventory and restocking needs for multiple restaurants. It collects information about restocking needs, inventory checklists, and other relevant details from staff members.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Select the relevant sections
Complete each section by selecting the options that apply to the current restaurant and stock needs.

### Step 2: Enter details
Fill in the relevant details for each section, such as restocking needs, inventory checklists, and comments.

### Step 3: Review and submit
Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Restocking Needs** (`restocking_needs`): Select the restocking needs from the list.
	+ Restocking Needed: Check if restocking is required.
	+ None: If no restocking is needed.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the inventory check.
* **Staff Member** (`staff_member`): Select the staff member who is checking the inventory.
* **Restaurant** (`restaurant`): Enter the name of the restaurant.
* **Inventory Checklist** (`inventory_checklist`): Enter the inventory checklist details.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments about the inventory check.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`): Enter the name of the staff member who submitted the form.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`): Enter the name of the staff member who is performing the inventory check.
* **Staff Contact** (`staff_contact`): Enter the contact information of the staff member.
* **Restocking Needs 2** (`restocking_needs_2`): Select the restocking needs from the list.
* **Inventory Checklist 2** (`inventory_checklist_2`): Enter the inventory checklist details.
* **Item Number** (`item_number`): Enter the item number.
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`): Enter any additional comments about the inventory check.
* **Date 2** (`date_2`): Enter the date of the inventory check.
* **Staff Member 2** (`staff_member_2`): Select the staff member who is performing the inventory check.
* **Restaurant 2** (`restaurant_2`): Enter the name of the restaurant.
* **Restocking Needs 3** (`restocking_needs_3`): Select the restocking needs from the list.
* **Comments 3** (`comments_3`): Enter any additional comments about the inventory check.
* **Inventory Checklist 3** (`inventory_checklist_3`): Enter the inventory checklist details.
* **Staff Contact 2** (`staff_contact_2`): Enter the contact information of the staff member.
