# Golf Course Maintenance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golf Course Maintenance Checklist Form is designed to help golf course administrators record and manage routine maintenance activities. This form gathers information on tasks to be performed, their status, and the frequency and tools used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and ensure you are answering the questions relevant to your specific situation.
2. For each field, provide the requested information, using the provided options or entering free-text answers when necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Maintenance Form**: A text field where you can describe the specific tasks or areas of the golf course that require maintenance.
- **Maintenance Checklist**: A text field for listing specific tasks or activities that need to be performed.
- **Maintenance Tasks**: A text field for detailing the maintenance tasks or activities.
- **Maintenance Frequency**: Select a number to indicate how often this task needs to be performed (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
- **Task Status**: Choose whether the task is currently Active or Inactive.
- **Maintenance Type**: Select whether the maintenance is performed or not (Yes/No).
- **Assigned Tool**: Choose whether a specific tool is assigned to perform this task (Yes/No).
- **Comments**: A free-text field for providing additional information or notes about the maintenance activity.

## Tips
- Be sure to review and update this form regularly to ensure accurate and up-to-date information.
- Use the provided fields to the best of your ability, and do not hesitate to add notes or comments when necessary.
- If you are unsure about any field or its purpose, consult with your team or refer to the original form documentation.
