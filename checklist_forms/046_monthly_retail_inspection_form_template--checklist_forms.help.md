# monthly_retail_inspection_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for monthly retail inspection checklists to evaluate a store's performance in different areas.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form on a monthly basis to provide regular evaluation and feedback.
2. Make sure to answer each field accurately and completely.
3. For select_one fields, choose a score from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
