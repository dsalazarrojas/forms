# van_inspection_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The van_inspection_form is a tool used to inspect and record information about a van, its condition and other relevant details. This form is used to ensure that all necessary information is collected accurately and efficiently, and to reduce potential issues during the vehicle inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form and its sections carefully before starting the inspection.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Be sure to answer all required questions and fill in all relevant fields.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Van Condition** (`van_condition`, text, required: false): This field is used to describe the overall condition of the van, including any notable issues or concerns. Please provide a brief but detailed description of the van's current state.
* **Van Year** (`van_year`, number, required: false): Enter the year the van was manufactured. This will help identify the van's age and potential issues related to its age.
* **Van Mileage** (`van_mileage`, number, required: false): Record the current mileage of the van. This will help track the van's usage and potential maintenance needs.
* **Vehicle License** (`vehicle_license`, text, required: false): Enter the van's license plate number or registration number.
* **VIN Number** (`vin_number`, text, required: false): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the van. This is a unique identifier for the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required: false): Identify the van's make, e.g., Ford, Chevrolet, Toyota, etc.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required: false): Identify the van's model, e.g., Transit, Silverado, Corolla, etc.
* **Engine Condition** (`engine_condition`, text, required: false): Describe the condition of the van's engine, including any notable issues or concerns.
