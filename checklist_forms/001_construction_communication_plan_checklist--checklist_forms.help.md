# Construction Communication Plan Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to ensure that your project team is following the best practices for construction communication and project management. It helps to establish a clear plan for how you'll communicate with internal and external stakeholders, track progress, and address any issues that arise during the project. Completing this form will help you to identify potential risks and establish a solid foundation for effective communication, ensuring that your project stays on track and meets the required standards of quality and safety.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the project information: Enter the official name of your project and the name of the project manager.
2. Set up your communication frequency: Choose how often your team will meet internally (e.g., daily, weekly, bi-weekly) and how you'll communicate with clients (e.g., email reports, formal meetings, phone calls, project portal access).
3. Establish your emergency contact protocol: Confirm whether you have a clear procedure for urgent site issues.
4. Define your reporting and documentation standards: Choose when daily site logs must be filed and how often progress photos should be uploaded.
5. Set up your budget variance reporting: Specify the percentage variance that triggers immediate notification.
6. Verify your stakeholder list: Confirm whether your master contact list has been reviewed this month.
7. Add any additional comments or requirements: Enter any special requirements not covered above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name (Required)**: Enter the full official name of the construction project.
* **Project Manager Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the project manager.
* **Checklist Completion Date (Required)**: Enter the date you are completing this form.
* **Internal Team Meeting Frequency**: Choose how often your core project team meets (Daily Stand-up, Twice Weekly, Weekly, Bi-weekly).
* **Primary Client Communication Method**: Choose how you'll primarily communicate with clients (Email Reports, Formal Meetings, Phone Calls, Project Portal Access).
* **Emergency Contact Protocol Established**: Confirm whether you have a clear procedure for urgent site issues (Yes - Fully Documented, In Progress, False).
* **Daily Site Log Submission Time**: Specify the time when daily site logs must be filed.
* **Progress Photo Upload Frequency**: Choose how often site photos should be uploaded (Daily, Three Times Weekly, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Budget Variance Reporting Threshold**: Specify the percentage variance that triggers immediate notification.
* **Stakeholder Contact List Verified**: Confirm whether your master contact list has been reviewed this month (True, False).
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any special requirements or comments not covered above.
* **Additional Communication Notes**: Enter any special communication requirements or needs.

This form is a comprehensive checklist to help you establish a solid communication plan for your construction project, ensuring that you're on track and meeting all necessary standards.
