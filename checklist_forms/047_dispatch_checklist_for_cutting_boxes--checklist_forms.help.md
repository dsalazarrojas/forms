# Dispatch Checklist For Cutting Boxes - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dispatch Checklist For Cutting Boxes" form is used to document the cutting process of boxes for future reference and to ensure that all necessary information is captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the box number, which is a unique identifier for the box being cut.
2. Select the date when the box was cut.
3. Enter the name of the person who cut the box.
4. If applicable, select the person who checked the cut box.
5. Enter the date when the check was performed by the selected person.
6. Optional: Enter any notes about the cut box.
7. Optional: Select the option to sign off on the check.
8. Optional: Enter the email of the person who performed the check.
9. Optional: Enter the phone number of the person who performed the check.
10. Optional: Enter any additional notes about the dispatch.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Box Number** (`box_number`, required): Enter a unique identifier for the box being cut.
* **Cut Date** (`cut_date`, required): Select the date when the box was cut.
* **Cut By** (`cut_by`, required): Enter the name of the person who cut the box.
* **Checked By** (`checked_by`, optional): Select the person who performed the check, if applicable.
* **Checked Date** (`checked_date`, required): Select the date when the check was performed by the selected person.
* **Notes** (`notes`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the cut box.
* **Checked Signature** (`checked_signature`, optional): Select one of the options to sign off on the check.
* **Checked Email** (`checked_email`, optional): Enter the email of the person who performed the check.
* **Checked Phone** (`checked_phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who performed the check.
* **Dispatch Notes** (`dispatch_notes`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the dispatch.

## Tips

* Always fill out all required fields.
* Make sure to select the correct options for the checked by and checked signature fields.
* Enter accurate dates and names for future reference.
* Use the notes field to capture any additional information not covered by the other fields.
* This form is designed to be completed by the person who cut the box and the person who checked the cut box.
