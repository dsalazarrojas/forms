# Job Offer Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect and evaluate details about job offers from candidates. It helps hiring teams track and compare different job offers in an organized way.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out this form for each new job offer you receive.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank or provide a note explaining why it's not applicable.
4. Use the form to compare and evaluate different job offers based on their details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Offer Details** (`job_offer_details`, text, optional): Briefly describe the job offer, including any relevant details that might be useful for comparison.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of job offer, such as a full-time or freelance position.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the job offer, such as "Open", "Closed", or "Archived".
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the job offer.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_one, optional): Select the level of the job offer, such as entry-level, mid-level, or senior-level.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the job offer.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the job offer.
* **Job Salary** (`job_salary`, number, optional): Enter the salary of the job offer.
* **Job Salary Frequency** (`job_salary_frequency`, text, optional): Specify the frequency of the salary, such as monthly or bi-weekly.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the job offer.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the job offer.
* **Job Hours** (`job_hours`, text, optional): Enter the number of job hours.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of job.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, text, optional): Enter the status of the job.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, text, optional): Enter the category of the job.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company.
* **Team** (`team`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team.
* **Team Leader** (`team_leader`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team leader.
* **Job Tags** (`job_tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple tags that describe the job offer.
* **Job Tags Description** (`job_tags_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the job tags.
* **Job Tags Tags** (`job_tags_tags`, text, optional): Enter a description of the job tags.
* **Job Tags Tags Description** (`job_tags_tags_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of job tags.
* **Job Tags Tags Tags** (`job_tags_tags_tags`, text, optional): Enter a description of job tags.
* **Job Comments** (`job_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the job offer.
* **Job Rating** (`job_rating`, select_one, optional): Select a rating for the job offer, such as "High", "Low", or "Medium".
