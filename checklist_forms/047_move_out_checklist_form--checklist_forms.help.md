# Move Out Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Move Out Checklist Form is a document used to record the state of a rental property after a tenant vacates. It helps ensure that all items are inventoried and documented, preventing potential disputes and charges for repairs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and requirements.
2. Answer each question accurately and truthfully.
3. Complete the form with the required information and sign it accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Move Out Form** (`tenant_move_out_form`): A text field for any notes or comments regarding the move-out process.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`): A text field for any relevant details about the property, such as its condition or any concerns.
* **Tenant Move Out Checklist** (`tenant_move_out_checklist`): A multiple-choice question to select which items have been moved out of the property. Options include:
	+ Move out date
	+ Property condition
	+ Additional notes
* **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`): A date field to record the actual move-out date.
* **Property Condition** (`property_condition`): A selection field to describe the property condition at the time of move-out. Options include:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): A text field for any additional comments or notes.
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`): An email field for the tenant or property manager to confirm their email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): A text field for the tenant or property manager to enter their phone number.
* **Move Out Signature** (`move_out_signature`): A date field to record the signature date of the move-out process.
* **Signature** (`signature`): A selection field to indicate which party signed the form. Options include:
	+ Tenant
	+ Landlord
	+ Property Manager
* **Move Out Date Signature** (`move_out_date_signature`): A date field to record the date of the signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`): A date field to record the date of the signature.
* **Property Manager** (`property_manager`): A text field for the property manager's name and contact information.
* **Landlord Signature** (`landlord_signature`): A date field to record the landlord's signature date.
* **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`): A date field to record the tenant's signature date.
* **Property Manager Signature** (`property_manager_signature`): A date field to record the property manager's signature date.
* **Move Out Checklist Form Complete** (`move_out_checklist_form_complete`): A selection field to indicate the form's completion status. Options include:
	+ Complete
	+ Incomplete
	+ Partially Complete
* **Move Out Checklist Form Incomplete Reason** (`move_out_checklist_form_incomplete_reason`): A text field for any reason why the form is incomplete.
* **Move Out Checklist Form Permanent Damage** (`move_out_checklist_form_permanent_damage`): A selection field to indicate if there is permanent damage to the property. Options include:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Move Out Checklist Form Normal Wear and Tear** (`move_out_checklist_form_normal_wear_and_tear`): A selection field to indicate if there is normal wear and tear on the property. Options include:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Move Out Checklist Form Unreported Items** (`move_out_checklist_form_unreported_items`): A selection field to indicate if there are unreported items. Options include:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Move Out Checklist Form Missing Items** (`move_out_checklist_form_missing_items`): A selection field to indicate if there are missing items. Options include:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Move Out Checklist Form Unrepaired Damage** (`move_out_checklist_form_unrepaired_damage`): A selection field to indicate if there is unrepaired damage on the property. Options include:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Move Out Checklist Form Damage** (`move_out_checklist_form_damage`): A selection field to indicate if there is damage on the property. Options include:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Move Out Checklist Form Repairs** (`move_out_checklist_form_repairs`): A selection field to indicate if repairs were made on the property. Options include:
	+ True
	+ False

## Tips
* Review the form carefully to understand the questions and requirements.
* Answer each question accurately and truthfully.
* Complete the form with the required information and sign it accordingly.
* Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness.
* Make sure to review any existing documentation or communication from the tenant or property manager before filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any question or item, consult with the relevant parties or seek clarification before proceeding.
