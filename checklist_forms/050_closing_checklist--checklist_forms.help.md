# Closing Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Closing Checklist is a comprehensive form used to ensure that a retail store is properly closed at the end of the shift. This form helps to document and confirm that all necessary tasks have been completed to maintain store security, organization, and customer satisfaction. It's essential for employees to follow this checklist to guarantee a smooth closing process and prevent potential issues during the next shift.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Employee Identification**: Enter your name in the "Employee Name" field. This is your name as it appears on your employee ID.
2. **Store Information**: Enter the store location where you are closing in the "Store Location" field.
3. **Shift Information**: Choose the type of shift you are closing in the "Shift Type" field. This will help the next shift know the status of the store.
4. **Closing Time**: Enter the time you completed closing in the "Closing Completion Time" field.
5. **Signatures**: Sign your name in the "Employee Signature" field and the manager's name in the "Manager Signature" field, if applicable.
6. **Complete each section**: Go through each section of the form, marked with a heading, and select the status of each task. For example, if you have completed the task, select "Complete". If you are still working on it, select "In Progress".
7. **Report any issues**: If you have encountered any issues during closing, select "True" in the "Any Issues to Report" field and describe the issue in the "Issue Details" field.
