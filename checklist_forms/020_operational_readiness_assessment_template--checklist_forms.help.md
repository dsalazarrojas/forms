# operational_readiness_assessment_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The operational readiness assessment template is a tool used to evaluate the current state of operational readiness of a system, process, or project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and answer "Yes" or "No" as needed.
2. For select_multiple and select_one fields, select all applicable options that apply to your situation.
3. Fill in the Assessment Date field with the date of the assessment.
4. For fields that are not applicable or have no answer, you can leave them blank.
5. Fill in the Assigned to field with the name of the person responsible for the assessment.
