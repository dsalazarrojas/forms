# Master Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Master Checklist Form is a tool for creating and tracking master checklists for projects or workflows. It helps to track progress and assign tasks and deadlines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Master Checklist Form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **workflow** field (id: 1) and fill in the name of the workflow or project.
2. Choose the **priority** (id: 2) by selecting "High", "Medium", or "Low" from the dropdown list.
3. In the **progress** field (id: 3), enter a numerical value representing the progress of the project or task.
4. For the **task** field (id: 4), enter the name of the task or activity to be performed.
5. Select the **due date** (id: 5) by clicking on the date picker and choose the desired date for completion.
6. Choose the **due time** (id: 6) by clicking on the time picker and select the desired time for completion.
7. Fill in the **assigned to** field (id: 7) with the name or alias of the person responsible for the task.
8. The **priority date** (id: 8) and **priority time** (id: 9) fields are currently not in use and do not require input.
9. Finally, add any additional notes or comments in the **notes** field (id: 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **workflow**: Enter the name of the project or workflow.
* **priority**: Select the level of priority: High, Medium, or Low.
* **progress**: Enter a numerical value to represent the progress of the project or task.
* **task**: Enter the name of the task or activity to be performed.
* **due_date**: Select the date by which the task is due for completion.
* **due_time**: Select the time by which the task is due for completion.
* **assigned_to**: Enter the name or alias of the person responsible for the task.
* **priority_date**: Currently not in use, please ignore.
* **priority_time**: Currently not in use, please ignore.
* **notes**: Add any additional comments or notes for the task.

## Tips

* Make sure to save the form after completing all necessary fields.
* Review and update the form regularly to track progress.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the support team.
