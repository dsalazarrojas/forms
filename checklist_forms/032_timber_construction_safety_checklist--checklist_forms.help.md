# Timber Construction Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to ensure that timber construction sites adhere to the necessary safety standards and protocols, reducing the risk of accidents and injuries.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, follow the instructions for each field carefully.
2. For each field, please provide a brief description or explanation of the safety measure or protocol being implemented on your site.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields (marked as required).
4. If a field is optional, you may skip it if it's not relevant to your site.
5. Once you have completed the form, submit it for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Construction** (`general_construction`, text, not required): Describe the general construction safety measures in place on your site, such as site-specific safety policies and procedures.
* **Fall Protection** (`fall_protection`, text, required): Describe the fall protection measures in place on your site, including personal protective equipment (PPE) and harnessing systems.
* **Scaffolding** (`scaffolding`, text, required): Describe the scaffolding safety measures in place on your site, including regular inspections and maintenance.
* **Confined Spaces** (`confined_spaces`, text, not required): Describe the confined spaces safety measures on your site, including ventilation systems and emergency procedures.
* **Fire Safety** (`fire_safety`, text, required): Describe the fire safety measures on your site, including fire extinguishers, exit routes, and emergency procedures.
* **Emergency Exit Routes** (`emergency_exit_routes`, text, required): Describe the emergency exit routes on your site, including signs, alarms, and evacuation procedures.
* **First Aid** (`first_aid`, text, not required): Describe the first aid measures in place on your site, including first aid kits, training, and emergency response plans.
* **Electrical Hazard Prevention** (`electrical_hazard_prevention`, text, not required): Describe the electrical hazard prevention measures on your site, including electrical shock prevention and arc flash protection.
* **Fall Hazard Prevention** (`fall_hazard_prevention`, text, required): Describe the fall hazard prevention measures on your site, including fall protection systems and netting.
* **Ladder Safety** (`ladder_safety`, text, required): Describe the ladder safety measures on your site, including ladder training, inspection, and usage guidelines.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields carefully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your site supervisor or safety officer for guidance.
* This form is intended to be used for general safety reporting and compliance checks. It is not a substitute for regular safety audits or inspections.
