# emergency_exit_light_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency exit light inspection form is used to assess the status of emergency exit lights in a location, typically in an electrical panel or on the ceiling of a building. This form is used to check if the lights are functioning correctly and if there are any issues that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Review the Form

- Take a moment to review the form and understand what it asks you to check. The form is divided into seven pages, each with questions or options related to emergency exit lights.

### Step 2: Complete Each Section

- Answer each question or select an option for each section of the form. Make sure to answer based on the actual status of the emergency exit lights in your location.

### Step 3: Submit the Form

- Once you have completed all sections, submit the form to complete the assessment.
