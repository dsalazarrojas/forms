# Church Facility Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a maintenance checklist for a church facility, designed to help inspectors assess and report on the condition of various areas of the building. The form is divided into three main sections: Interior, Exterior and Grounds, and Maintenance Summary.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form on your computer or mobile device.
- Complete each field carefully, following the instructions and options provided.
- If you are unsure about any question, use your best judgment and provide as much detail as possible.
- If a field is not applicable or not possible to answer (e.g., due to a specific situation), leave it blank and move on to the next question.
- Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the person performing the inspection.
- **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection, in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Building/Area Inspected**: Select the area of the building that was inspected, from the options provided.
  - Main Sanctuary
  - Fellowship Hall
  - Classrooms/Offices
  - Exterior/Parking Lot
  - Kitchen/Pantry
  - Restrooms
- **Interior Lighting Status**: Select the status of the interior lighting in the area inspected, from the options provided.
  - All Operational
  - Bulbs Need Replacement
  - Fixture Repair Needed
- **HVAC Filters Inspected**: Select the status of the HVAC filters in the area inspected, from the options provided.
  - Clean
  - Replaced
  - Needs Replacement
  - Professional Service Required
- **Plumbing Fixtures Status**: Select the status of the plumbing fixtures in the area inspected, from the options provided.
  - No Issues
  - Minor Leak
  - Repair Required
- **Smoke/Carbon Monoxide Detectors**: Select the status of the smoke and carbon monoxide detectors in the area inspected, from the options provided.
  - Tested and Working
  - Battery Replaced
  - Faulty - Replace Immediately
- **Roof and Gutters**: Select the status of the roof and gutters in the area inspected, from the options provided.
  - Clear and Intact
  - Needs Cleaning
  - Repair Needed
- **Walkway and Parking Lot Safety**: Select the status of the walkway and parking lot in the area inspected, from the options provided.
  - Safe
  - Minor Issues
  - Immediate Attention Needed
- **Landscaping and Irrigation**: Select the status of the landscaping and irrigation in the area inspected, from the options provided.
  - Satisfactory
  - Needs Attention
  - System Leak Detected
- **Overall Priority Level of Repairs**: Select the overall priority level of repairs needed based on the inspection results, from the options provided.
  - Low (Routine)
  - Medium (Soon)
  - High (Urgent/Safety)
- **Detailed Findings and Work Needed**: Provide specific locations and descriptions of any issues found during the inspection.
- **Estimated Materials or Parts Needed**: List any materials or parts required for the necessary repairs.
- **Is a professional contractor required?**: Select whether a licensed professional is needed to handle the repairs, from the options provided.
  - No - Volunteer/Staff can handle
  - Yes - Licensed professional needed
- **Inspector Signature (Type Name)**: Enter your full name as the inspector.
