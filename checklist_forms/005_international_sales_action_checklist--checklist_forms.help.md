# International Sales Action Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help sales teams streamline their workflow and ensure that all necessary actions are taken for a sale to proceed smoothly. It guides teams through key steps, from review and compliance checks to logistics and customer confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Sales Team Review**: Enter the details of the sale in this section, including any necessary information that the sales team needs to confirm.
2. **Sales Manager Review**: Review the sale to ensure everything is in order, and make any necessary corrections or approvals.
3. **Export Documentation**: Select the type of export documentation required for the sale, such as Export 1, 2, or 3.
4. **Compliance Check**: Conduct a compliance check to ensure all necessary steps have been taken for the sale.
5. **Logistics Steps**: Choose multiple options for the logistics process, such as Logistics 1, 2, or 3.
6. **Customer Confirmation**: Get the customer's confirmation on the sale, which can be 'Yes' or 'No'.
7. **Date**: Record the date of the sale.
8. **Time**: Record the time of the sale.
9. **Email**: Enter the email associated with the sale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sales Team Review**: A text box where the sales team can enter and review sale details.
* **Sales Manager Review**: A text box for the sales manager to review and confirm the sale.
* **Export Documentation**: A dropdown menu to select the type of export documentation required for the sale (e.g., Export 1, 2, or 3).
* **Compliance Check**: A text box for the sales team to conduct a compliance check and ensure all necessary steps have been taken.
* **Logistics Steps**: A dropdown menu to select multiple options for the logistics process (e.g., Logistics 1, 2, or 3).
* **Customer Confirmation**: A dropdown menu to get the customer's confirmation on the sale ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Date**: A date field to record the date of the sale.
* **Time**: A time field to record the time of the sale.
* **Email**: An email field to enter the email associated with the sale.

## Tips

* Make sure to review each section carefully to ensure that all necessary steps have been taken for the sale.
* Use the form as a checklist to streamline your workflow and reduce errors.
* Keep the form open as you work through each step to ensure that you don't miss any important details.
