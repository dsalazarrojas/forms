# Doctor Visit Checkout Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doctor Visit Checkout Checklist is a form used to ensure that all necessary steps are taken before discharging a patient.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the discharge steps that need to be taken by the patient before leaving the hospital or clinic.
2. Schedule the next appointment date and time with the patient.
3. Schedule follow-up visits or check-ups for the patient.
4. Confirm the discharge of the patient, specifying the next steps and responsibilities.
5. Allow the patient to express their thoughts, concerns, or feedback about their visit.
6. Allow the doctor to add comments or notes about the patient's visit.
7. Record the status of the patient's visit, such as whether they need further evaluation or treatment.
8. Schedule the next check-up date and time with the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Discharge Steps** (discharge_steps): Select the necessary steps to be taken by the patient before leaving the hospital or clinic.
* **Next Appointment** (next_appointment): Schedule the next appointment date and time with the patient.
* **Confirm Discharge** (confirm_discharge): Confirm the discharge of the patient, specifying the next steps and responsibilities.
* **Confirm Follow-up** (confirm_followup): Schedule follow-up visits or check-ups for the patient.
* **Next Follow Up** (next_follow_up): Schedule the next follow-up date and time with the patient.
* **Next Visit** (next_visit): Schedule the next visit date and time with the patient.
* **Confirm Visit** (confirm_visit): Confirm the visit details with the patient.
* **Next Check-up** (next_checkup): Schedule the next check-up date and time with the patient.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all necessary discharge steps for the patient.
* Double-check the next appointment schedule to ensure it is accurate.
* Schedule follow-up visits or check-ups according to the patient's needs.
* Be sure to confirm the discharge with the patient, specifying next steps and responsibilities.
* Patient comments should be taken seriously and addressed accordingly.
* Doctor comments should be kept private and confidential.
* Visit status should be updated regularly to ensure accurate patient records.
* Schedule next check-ups according to the patient's needs and health status.
