# dental_clinic_design_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental clinic design checklist is used to evaluate the facilities and infrastructure of a dental clinic, ensuring that they meet the required standards for patient comfort, safety, and efficiency.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Select each option based on the status of the corresponding feature in your dental clinic.
2. Answer all questions, even if you're unsure or need to research more information.
3. Save your progress and come back to it later if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **rooms** (`rooms`, text, optional): Describe the number and types of rooms in your dental clinic, including treatment rooms, waiting areas, and staff rooms.
* **equipment** (`equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Check the status of essential equipment such as chairs, tables, suction machines, and other dental tools.
* **safety** (`safety`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the safety features of your clinic, including emergency exits, fire alarms, and first aid kits.
* **accessibility** (`accessibility`, select_multiple, optional): Assess the accessibility of your clinic for patients with disabilities, including wheelchair accessibility and Braille signage.
* **renovation** (`renovation`, select_multiple, optional): Check if any renovations or repairs have been made to the clinic in the past few years, including electrical and plumbing updates.
* **emergency_exit** (`emergency_exit`, select_multiple, optional): Check the status of emergency exits, including the number and accessibility of exits, and if they are well-lit and clearly marked.
* **handwashing** (`handwashing`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the availability and maintenance status of handwashing facilities, including soap and paper towel supplies.
* **ventilation** (`ventilation`, select_multiple, optional): Check the status of ventilation systems, including air filters and regular maintenance schedules.
* **lighting** (`lighting`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the brightness and quality of lighting throughout the clinic, including in treatment rooms and waiting areas.
* **flooring** (`flooring`, select_multiple, optional): Check the condition and type of flooring in each room, including slip-resistance and maintenance status.
* **electrical** (`electrical`, select_multiple, optional): Assess the electrical infrastructure, including circuit breakers, outlets, and fire alarms.
* **plumbing** (`plumbing`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the condition and maintenance status of plumbing fixtures, including toilets, sinks, and water heaters.
* **glazing** (`glazing`, select_multiple, optional): Check the type and condition of glazing, including windows, doors, and skylights.
* **fire_suppression** (`fire_suppression`, select_multiple, optional): Check the status of fire suppression systems, including sprinkler systems and fire extinguishers.
* **security** (`security`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the security features of your clinic, including locks, alarms, and CCTV cameras.
* **storage** (`storage`, select_multiple, optional): Check the availability and organization of storage facilities, including shelves and cabinets.
* **waste_disposal** (`waste_disposal`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the waste disposal facilities, including trash cans and recycling bins.
* **patient_waiting_area** (`patient_waiting_area`, select_multiple, optional): Assess the waiting area for patients, including seating, lighting, and climate control.
* **reception_area** (`reception_area`, select_multiple, optional): Check the status of reception areas, including waiting areas, check-in counters, and patient records storage.
* **dental_station** (`dental_station`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the dental station, including chairs, instruments, and storage for supplies.
* **office** (`office`, select_multiple, optional): Check the status of office spaces, including desks, chairs, and filing cabinets.
* **break_room** (`break_room`, select_multiple, optional): Assess the break room for staff, including kitchenette, fridge, and seating.
* **nurse_station** (`nurse_station`, select_multiple, optional): Check the status of the nurse station, including supplies, equipment, and emergency procedures.
* **waiting_area** (`waiting_area`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the waiting area for patients, including seating, lighting, and climate control.
* **other** (`other`, select_multiple, optional): Describe any other features or amenities not listed here that are unique to your clinic.

## Tips
* Take your time to answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about a feature, research more information before selecting an option.
* Save your progress and come back to it later if needed.
* This form is for internal use only and should not be shared with external parties.
