# Conference Setup Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps conference planners and coordinators verify the readiness of a conference setup by tracking progress and completion of various tasks and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Event Name field with the name of your conference.
2. Select the date of your conference in the Event Date field.
3. Enter the name of the venue where the conference will take place.
4. Indicate who is responsible for coordinating the checklist in the Checklist Coordinator field.
5. Select the current status of your checklist in the Overall Readiness Status field.
6. For each section, mark the correct status for each task:
	* "Complete" if the task is fully completed.
	* "In Progress" if the task is ongoing but not yet finished.
	* "Not Started" if the task has not been initiated.
	* "Not Applicable" if the task is not relevant to your conference.
7. In the Additional Tasks field, provide any other important items that require attention.
8. Finally, select "I Confirm" to verify that all tasks have been completed or "Not Complete" to indicate that more work is needed.
