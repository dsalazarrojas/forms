# Drivetrain Installation Quality Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information and feedback on the quality of a drivetrain installation. This form is used to track and record key details about the installation, including customer information, drivetrain specifics, and any issues or comments that may arise during or after the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the installation ID and drivetrain model number to identify the specific installation you are reporting on.
2. Provide the customer's name, phone number, and email to ensure accurate contact information.
3. Enter the drivetrain serial number and model year to verify the installation details.
4. Record the installation date and time, and provide any relevant comments or issues that occurred during the installation process.
5. Describe the drivetrain condition, any inspection points, and any issues found.
6. Enter the technician's and customer's signatures and dates to confirm completion and review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Installation ID** (`installation_id`): Enter the unique ID assigned to the installation to track and identify specific installation records.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter the customer's name to ensure accurate contact information.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter the customer's phone number for further communication.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter the customer's email address for further communication.
* **Drivetrain Model Number** (`drivetrain_model_number`): Enter the model number of the drivetrain for reference.
* **Drivetrain Model Year** (`drivetrain_model_year`): Enter the model year of the drivetrain for reference.
* **Drivetrain Serial Number** (`drivetrain_serial_number`): Enter the serial number of the drivetrain for reference.
* **Install Date** (`install_date`): Record the date the drivetrain was installed.
* **Install Time** (`install_time`): Record the time the drivetrain was installed.
* **Drivetrain Condition** (`drivetrain_condition`): Describe the current condition of the drivetrain.
* **Inspection Points** (`inspection_points`): List any inspection points or areas of concern.
* **Issues Found** (`issues_found`): Describe any issues or problems encountered during or after the installation.
* **Technician Signature** (`technician_signature`): Enter the technician's signature to confirm completion and review.
* **Technician Signature Date** (`tech_signature_date`): Record the date the technician signed the form.
* **Technician Signature Time** (`tech_signature_time`): Record the time the technician signed the form.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`): Enter the customer's signature to confirm review.
* **Customer Signature Date** (`customer_signature_date`): Record the date the customer signed the form.
* **Customer Signature Time** (`customer_signature_time`): Record the time the customer signed the form.
* **Issue ID** (`issue_id`): If an issue was identified, enter the unique ID assigned to the issue.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`): Describe the issue or problem encountered.
* **Issue Date** (`issue_date`): Record the date the issue was identified.
* **Issue Status** (`issue_status`): Select the current status of the issue (Active, Pending, Resolved, Cancelled, Duplicate, Unconfirmed, or In Progress).
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the installation or issue.

Note: Fields marked as required are mandatory and must be completed for the form to be submitted.
