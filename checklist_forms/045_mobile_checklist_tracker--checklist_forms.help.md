# Mobile Checklist Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Mobile Checklist Tracker form is designed to record and track inspection data for facilities and assets.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the Facility ID (Field 1) and Asset ID (Field 2) in the respective fields.
2. Select the inspection Date (Field 3) and time (Field 4) using the calendar feature.
3. Enter the name and title of the Inspector (Field 5).
4. Add any comments or remarks (Field 6).
5. Select the Inspection Result (Field 7) from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter the Email (Field 9) and Phone (Field 10) of the facility or asset manager.
7. Categorize the Asset Status (Field 11) and Asset Condition (Field 12) using the dropdown menus.
8. Select the Asset Priority (Field 13) from the dropdown menu.
9. Enter the Facility Location (Field 14) and the name of the Facility Manager (Field 15).
10. Enter the Asset Manager (Field 16) and the Asset Type (Field 17).
11. Enter the Asset Make and Model details (Fields 18 and 19).
12. Enter the Asset Serial Number and Sno (Fields 20 and 21).
13. Select the inspection Frequency (Field 22) from the dropdown menu.
14. Select the interval of the inspection schedule (Field 23).
15. Select the frequency of the last inspection (Field 24).
16. Select the frequency of the next inspection (Field 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility ID** (Field 1): Enter the unique identifier for the facility being inspected.
* **Asset ID** (Field 2): Enter the unique identifier for the asset being inspected.
* **Date** (Field 3): Select the date of the inspection using the calendar feature.
* **Time** (Field 4): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspector** (Field 5): Enter the name and title of the inspector conducting the inspection.
* **Comments** (Field 6): Record any comments or remarks about the inspection.
* **Inspection Result** (Field 7): Select the result of the inspection from the dropdown menu.
* **Notes** (Field 8): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (Field 9): Enter the Email of the facility or asset manager.
* **Phone** (Field 10): Enter the Phone number of the facility or asset manager.
* **Asset Status** (Field 11): Categorize the status of the asset from the dropdown menu.
* **Asset Condition** (Field 12): Categorize the condition of the asset from the dropdown menu.
* **Asset Priority** (Field 13): Select the priority of the asset from the dropdown menu.
* **Facility Location** (Field 14): Enter the location of the facility.
* **Facility Manager** (Field 15): Enter the name of the facility manager.
* **Asset Manager** (Field 16): Enter the name of the asset manager.
* **Asset Type** (Field 17): Enter the type of asset.
* **Asset Make** (Field 18): Enter the make of the asset.
* **Asset Model** (Field 19): Enter the model of the asset.
* **Asset Serial Number** (Field 20): Enter the serial number of the asset.
* **Asset Sno** (Field 21): Enter the Sno of the asset.
* **Inspection Frequency** (Field 22): Select the frequency of inspections from the dropdown menu.
* **Inspection Frequency Interval** (Field 23): Select the interval of inspections from the dropdown menu.
* **Inspection Frequency Last** (Field 24): Select the frequency of the last inspection from the dropdown menu.
* **Inspection Frequency Next** (Field 25): Select the frequency of the next inspection from the dropdown menu.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the Inspection Result and Asset Status fields for accuracy.
* Use the calendar feature to select the inspection Date and time.
* Use the dropdown menus for Asset Condition and Asset Priority for correct categorization.
* Enter all relevant details for Facility Location and Asset Manager.
* Ensure that the inspection schedule is accurate and up-to-date.
