# Post Repair Appliance Checkoff Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information after an appliance repair job is completed. It helps to ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of appliance that was repaired using the dropdown menu.
2. Provide the make and model of the appliance, if available.
3. Enter the serial number of the appliance, if known.
4. Select the date of the repair from the calendar.
5. List any items that were checked off after the repair using the multi-select option.
6. Sign the form to confirm that you have reviewed the checklist.
7. Sign the form to confirm that the repair was completed and that the checklist was reviewed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Appliance Type**: Select the type of appliance that was repaired from the dropdown menu.
* **Make Model**: Enter the make and model of the appliance, if available. This helps to identify the specific appliance and its relevant specifications.
* **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the appliance, if known. This is a unique identifier for the appliance and helps to track its history.
* **Repair Date**: Select the date of the repair from the calendar. This ensures that the repair is dated accurately.
* **Post Repair Checklist Items**: List any items that were checked off after the repair using the multi-select option. This helps to ensure that all necessary checks are completed.
* **Customer Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that you have reviewed the checklist and agree with the post-repair details.
* **Technician Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that the repair was completed and that the checklist was reviewed accurately.
