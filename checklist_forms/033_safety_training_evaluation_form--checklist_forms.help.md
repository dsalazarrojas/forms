# Safety Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect and evaluate information about safety training programs attended by employees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the Introduction section with a brief description of the safety training program.
2. In the Safety Training section, provide general information about the program.
3. In the Safety Training Programs section, select which programs you have attended.
4. Enter the date and time you received the training.
5. Enter your name, position, and any notes about the training.
6. Evaluate the safety training program by selecting whether it is completed, not completed, or partially completed.
7. Provide feedback about the program, including whether you have evaluated it and why.
8. Select the status of the safety training program, including whether it is active, inactive, or pending.
9. Provide any additional information or comments from your manager or supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`id: 1`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the safety training program.
* **Safety Training** (`id: 2`, `text`, required: false): Provide general information about the program.
* **Safety Training Programs** (`id: 3`, `select_one`, required: false): Select which safety training programs you have attended.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Training Date** (`id: 4`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you received the training.
* **Training Time** (`id: 5`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of day you received the training.
* **Employee Name** (`id: 6`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Employee Position** (`id: 7`, `text`, required: false): Enter your position.
* **Supervisor Name** (`id: 8`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Employee Notes** (`id: 9`, `note`, required: false): Enter any notes about the training.
* **Safety Training Evaluation** (`id: 10`, `note`, required: false): Evaluate the safety training program.
* **Employee Comments** (`id: 11`, `note`, required: false): Provide feedback about the program.
* **Safety Training Completed** (`id: 12`, `text`, required: false): Select whether the safety training program is completed.
* **Safety Training Recommended** (`id: 13`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information or comments.
* **Completed Status** (`id: 14`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether the safety training program is completed, not completed, or partially completed.
* **Completed Date** (`id: 15`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the safety training program was completed.
* **Evaluated** (`id: 16`, `text`, required: false): Select whether the safety training program has been evaluated.
* **Evaluated Status** (`id: 17`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether the safety training program has been evaluated.
* **Status** (`id: 18`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the safety training program.
* **Status Reason** (`id: 19`, `text`, required: false): Enter the reason for the status.
* **Employee Evaluated Status** (`id: 20`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether the safety training program has been evaluated by you.
* **Employee Status** (`id: 21`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the employee.
* **Manager Evaluated Status** (`id: 22`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether the safety training program has been evaluated by the manager.
* **Manager Status** (`id: 23`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the manager.
* **Manager Comments** (`id: 24`, `note`, required: false): Enter any comments from the manager.
* **Manager Evaluated Status** (`id: 25`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether the safety training program has been evaluated by the manager.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use the dropdown menus to select options for select-one and select_multiple fields.
* Use the text fields to enter any additional information about the safety training program.
* Make sure to provide feedback about the safety training program in the evaluation and comments fields.
* Use the manager's comments field to provide any additional feedback from the manager.
