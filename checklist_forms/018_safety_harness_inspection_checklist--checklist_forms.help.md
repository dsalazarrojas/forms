# Safety Harness Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to inspect safety harnesses in the workplace to ensure they are in good condition and have been properly inspected. It includes questions about the harness type, condition, inspection status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the type of harness you are inspecting (if applicable).
2. Answer each question truthfully, indicating whether the harness is in the condition specified (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
3. If applicable, provide the name of the person or department responsible for inspecting the harness.
4. Enter the date the harness was inspected.
5. Complete all sections as required or recommended.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Harness Type**: Select the type of safety harness you are inspecting.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Harness Condition**: Assess the condition of the safety harness. Indicate if it is in good working order.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Harness Label**: Enter any label or identification number on the safety harness.
	+ A text field for manual entry.
* **Harness Label Condition**: Check if the label is in good condition.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Harness Inspected**: Indicate if the harness has been inspected recently.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Harness Inspect By**: Enter the name of the person or department that inspected the harness.
	+ A text field for manual entry.
* **Harness Inspection Date**: Enter the date the harness was inspected.
	+ A date field for manual entry.
* **Harness Inspect By Title**: Indicate if the person or department that inspected the harness has the authority to do so.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Harness Inspect By Department**: Enter the name of the department that inspected the harness.
	+ A text field for manual entry.
* **Harness Inspect By Job Title**: Enter the job title of the person that inspected the harness.
	+ A text field for manual entry.
* **Harness Inspect By Phone Ext**: Enter a phone extension of the person or department that inspected the harness.
	+ A text field for manual entry.

Tips:
* Ensure you answer each question truthfully and accurately.
