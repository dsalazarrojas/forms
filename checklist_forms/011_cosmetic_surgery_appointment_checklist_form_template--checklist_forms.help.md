# Cosmetic Surgery Appointment Checklist Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a comprehensive checklist for cosmetic surgery appointments. It captures essential information from the patient, such as contact details, medical history, and medical notes, to facilitate a smooth and efficient surgical process. This form can be used by healthcare professionals to ensure that all necessary information is gathered and documented during the consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the patient's information, including their name, contact number, and email.
2.  Record the patient's medical history, including any previous surgeries, illnesses, or allergies.
3.  Provide the patient's contact details, such as address and emergency contact information.
4.  Document the surgical details, including the procedure and any relevant medical notes.
5.  Record the patient's allergies and medical conditions.
6.  Sign the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the information provided.
7.  Have the doctor sign the form to confirm their agreement with the patient's information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is where the patient provides their basic contact information. Please fill in the patient's name, contact number, and email address.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): This section is where the patient provides their medical history, including any previous surgeries, illnesses, or allergies.
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): This section is where the patient provides their contact details, such as their address and emergency contact information.
*   **Surgical Details** (`surgical_details`, `text`, required: false): This section is where the patient provides details about the surgical procedure, such as the type of surgery and any relevant medical notes.
*   **Anesthesia** (`anesthesia`, `text`, required: false): This section is where the patient provides information about the anesthesia they will be administered during the surgery.
*   **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): This section is where the patient lists any medications they are currently taking.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required: false): This section is where the patient lists any allergies they have.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required: false): This section is where the patient documents any medical conditions they have.
*   **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, `text`, required: false): This section is where the patient documents the medical procedure that will be performed.
*   **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `text`, required: false): This is where the patient signs to acknowledge that they have read and understood the information provided.
*   **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, `text`, required: false): This is where the doctor signs to confirm their agreement with the patient's information.
*   **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, `text`, required: false): This section is where the doctor notes any medical information relevant to the patient's treatment.
*   **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes_text`, `text`, required: false): This section is a duplicate of the `Medical Notes` field, intended for the doctor to provide additional notes.
*   **Date** (`medical_notes_date`, `date`, required: false): This section is where the doctor documents the date of the consultation.
