# excavator_attachment_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The `excavator_attachment_inspection_checklist` form is designed to collect information about the frequency and items inspected during an excavator attachment inspection. This form helps in ensuring that all necessary components are checked regularly to prevent potential issues and ensure safe operation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the frequency of the inspection (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly) for the excavator attachment.
2. Choose all the items that are included in the inspection (Boom, Bucket, Winch, Hydraulic system, Tracks).
3. If there are any other items that are included in the inspection that are not listed, specify them in the "Specify other items included in this inspection" field.
4. Enter the date and time of the last inspection.
5. Choose whether you want to receive email or phone notification on completion of the inspection.
6. Select the status of the inspection (Approved, Disapproved, or Not Required).
7. If you have any additional comments, specify them in the "Specify comments on this inspection" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the inspection frequency of this excavator** (`inspection_frequency`): This field asks for the frequency of the inspection (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly) for the excavator attachment.
* **What items are included in this inspection** (`inspection_item`): This field allows you to select all the items that are included in the inspection (Boom, Bucket, Winch, Hydraulic system, Tracks).
* **Specify other items included in this inspection** (`inspection_item_other`): If there are any other items that are included in the inspection that are not listed, you can specify them here.
* **When was the last inspection** (`date_last_inspected`): Enter the date of the last inspection in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **What time was the last inspection** (`time_last_inspected`): Enter the time of the last inspection in HH:MM:SS AM/PM format.
* **Will you require email notification on completion of this inspection** (`email_notification`): Choose whether you want to receive an email notification on completion of the inspection.
* **Will you require phone notification on completion of this inspection** (`phone_notification`): Choose whether you want to receive a phone notification on completion of the inspection.
* **Are there any comments on this inspection** (`comments`): Select all the comments that apply to the inspection (Approved, Disapproved, or Not Required).
* **Specify comments on this inspection** (`comments_other`): If you have any additional comments, specify them here.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authorities or personnel.
* Ensure that all the items selected are inspected regularly to prevent potential issues.
