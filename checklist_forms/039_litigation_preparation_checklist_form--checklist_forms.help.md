# Litigation Preparation Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information related to a legal case for preparation and reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
* For fields with options, select the most relevant one.
* For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.
* For date fields, enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* For fields with phone numbers, enter the number without any special characters or symbols.
* For email fields, enter a valid email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Case Number
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, text, required): Enter the unique number assigned to the case.

### 2. Plaintiff Name
* **Plaintiff Name** (`plaintiff_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or entity involved in the case.

### 3. Case Location
* **Case Location** (`case_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the case is being handled.

### 4. Attorney Name
* **Attorney Name** (`attorney_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the attorney involved in the case.

### 5. Client Name
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the client or person involved in the case.

### 6. Date of Incident
* **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, optional): Enter the date when the incident occurred. (MM/DD/YYYY)

### 7. Case Type
* **Case Type** (`case_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of case (e.g., Case 1, Case 2, Case 3).

### 8. Insurance Coverage
* **Insurance Coverage** (`insurance_coverage`, select_multiple, optional): Select all insurance coverage types that apply (e.g., True, False).

### 9. Attorney Fees
* **Attorney Fees** (`attorney_fees`, number, optional): Enter the fee for the attorney services.

### 10. Court Fee
* **Court Fee** (`court_fee`, number, optional): Enter the fee for the court services.

### 11. Filing Date
* **Filing Date** (`filing_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the case was filed. (MM/DD/YYYY)

### 12. Hearing Date
* **Hearing Date** (`hearing_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the hearing. (MM/DD/YYYY)

### 13. Case Status
* **Case Status** (`case_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the case (e.g., Open, Closed).

### 14. Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to the case.

### 15. Client Number
* **Client Number** (`client_number`, text, optional): Enter the client number or ID.

### 16. Address
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the client or case location.

### 17. Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.

### 18. Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.

### 19. Case Number Prefix
* **Case Number Prefix** (`case_number_prefix`, text, optional): Enter a prefix for the case number.
