# Small Business Cyber Security Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide small business owners in assessing their cybersecurity posture and identifying areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply go through each page and select the relevant information for your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Data Backup and Recovery (Network Security, id: 7)**: Please enter the number of times you back up your data per month.
* **Incident Reporting (Incident Reporting, id: 8)**: Indicate whether you have an incident reporting process in place (True/False) or if it's only partially implemented (Partially).
* **Security Policy (Security Policy, id: 9)**: Select whether your security policy is documented (Documented), incomplete (Incomplete), or not documented at all (Not documented).
* **Monitoring and Review (Monitoring and Review, id: 10)**: Indicate how often you review your security measures (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Security Testing (Security Testing, id: 11)**: Choose the frequency of your security testing (Manual, Automated, or Quarterly).
* **Patch Management (Patch Management, id: 12)**: Indicate whether you have a patch management process in place (True/False) or if it's not applicable (Not applicable).
* **Security Incident (Security Incident, id: 13)**: Select whether security incidents are reported (Reported) or not reported (Not reported).
* **Security Audit (Security Audit, id: 14)**: Choose the frequency of your security audits (Annual, Quarterly, or Semi-annually).
* **Security Training (Security Training, id: 15)**: Indicate whether you've completed your security training (Completed), are still in progress (In Progress), or haven't started it (Not Started).
* **Security Compliance (Security Compliance, id: 16)**: Select which compliance standards your business adheres to (PCI, HIPAA, or Other).
* **Incident Response (Incident Response, id: 17)**: Enter any information about your incident response process.
* **Vulnerability Management (Vulnerability Management, id: 18)**: Enter any information about your vulnerability management process.
* **Change Control (Change Control, id: 19)**: Indicate whether you have a process for managing security changes (True/False) or if it's not applicable (Not applicable).
* **Compliance Audit (Compliance Audit, id: 20)**: Choose the frequency of your compliance audits (Annual, Quarterly, or Semi-annually).
* **Incident Response Plan Status (Incident Response Plan Status, id: 21)**: Select the status of your incident response plan (In Progress, Completed, or Not Implemented).
* **Incident Response Plan Review (Incident Response Plan Review, id: 22)**: Indicate how often you review your incident response plan (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Incident Response Plan Update (Incident Response Plan Update, id: 23)**: Select whether your incident response plan is up-to-date (In Progress, Completed, or Not updated).
* **Incident Response Plan Review Frequency (Incident Response Plan Review Frequency, id: 24)**: Choose the frequency of your incident response plan review (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Security Compliance (Security Compliance, id: 25)**: Enter any other compliance standards your business adheres to.
