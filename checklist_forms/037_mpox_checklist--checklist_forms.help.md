# Mpox Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Checklist is a form designed to collect information on preparation, prevention, and response strategies for Mpox. This form will help collect valuable insights on how to effectively manage and mitigate the spread of Mpox.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by selecting the relevant section from the "Preparation", "Prevention", or "Response" pages, depending on the information you want to provide.
*   Read each field carefully and fill in the required information. Some fields are optional and only required if you want to provide additional context.
*   Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Preparation (mpox_checklist_preparation)**: This section is for collecting information on preparation measures taken before the outbreak. You can add any relevant information you have on preparation strategies, resources, or plans in place.
*   **Prevention (mpox_checklist_preventing)**: This section is for gathering information on prevention measures taken to reduce the spread of Mpox. You can add any relevant information you have on prevention strategies, resources, or plans in place.
*   **Response (mpox_checklist_response)**: This section is for providing information on response actions taken after the outbreak. You can add any relevant information you have on response strategies, resources, or plans in place.

## Tips

*   Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submission.
