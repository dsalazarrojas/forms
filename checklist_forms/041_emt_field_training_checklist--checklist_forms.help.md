# EMT Field Training Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on EMT field training sessions, ensuring accurate and thorough documentation of all relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the session details such as date, time, and location.
2. Choose the correct session type from the provided options.
3. List all instructors and trainees who attended the session.
4. Evaluate the session using the provided options.
5. Add any additional comments or observations about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): This field is used to capture any additional information about the session that doesn't fit into other categories.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of session you're reporting on. This could be a training session, evaluation, or other types of EMT activities.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the session.
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the session.
* **Instructors** (`instructors`, select_multiple, optional): List all instructors who delivered the session.
* **Trainees** (`trainees`, select_multiple, optional): List all trainees who attended the session.
* **Evaluations** (`evaluations`, select_one, optional): Choose the evaluation of the session. This could be 'Eval 1' or 'Eval 2', depending on the context of the session.
* **Observations** (`observations`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the session.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any further comments or notes about the session.
* **Instructors** (`trainees`, select_multiple, optional): *This is a duplicate field. Please fill in only one of the two fields.*: This field is likely a duplicate of the "Instructors" field, and you should only fill in one of them.
* **Trainees** (`instructors`, select_multiple, optional): *This is a duplicate field. Please fill in only one of the two fields.*: This field is likely a duplicate of the "Trainees" field, and you should only fill in one of them.
