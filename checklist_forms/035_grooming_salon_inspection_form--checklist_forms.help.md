# Grooming Salon Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and ratings from customers or clients about their experience with a grooming salon.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions based on your actual experience with the salon.
2. For checklist questions, select all that apply from the provided options.
3. For the "pet_experience" question, rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being low and 5 being excellent.
4. Fill out all applicable fields, but do not feel obligated to answer all questions if they do not apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Quality Checklist 1** (select_multiple): Rate the quality of service you received from the salon. Select all that apply.
* **Customer Service** (select_one): Rate the customer service you received from the salon staff. Choose the best option that describes your experience.
* **Cleanliness Checklist 1** (select_multiple): Rate the cleanliness of the salon facility. Select all that apply.
* **Facility Checklist 1** (select_multiple): Rate the equipment and facilities provided by the salon. Select all that apply.
* **Staff Attitude Checklist 1** (select_multiple): Rate the attitude and professionalism of the salon staff. Select all that apply.
* **Pet Experience** (number): Rate your overall experience with the salon on a scale of 1-5. 1 = low, 2 = average, 3 = high, 4 = good, 5 = excellent.
* **Communication Quality Checklist 1** (select_multiple): Rate the quality of communication from the salon staff. Select all that apply.
* **Price Checklist 1** (select_multiple): Rate the prices charged by the salon. Select all that apply.
* **Equipment Checklist 1** (select_multiple): Rate the equipment used by the salon. Select all that apply.
* **Safety Checklist 1** (select_multiple): Rate the safety measures in place at the salon. Select all that apply.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions based on your actual experience with the salon.
* If a question does not apply to your experience, do not feel obligated to answer it.
* Take your time to answer all questions carefully and accurately.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and your input will help us improve our services.
