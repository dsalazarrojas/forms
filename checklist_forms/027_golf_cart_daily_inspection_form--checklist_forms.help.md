# Golf Cart Daily Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golf Cart Daily Inspection Form is used to inspect the mechanical and electrical components of golf carts to identify any potential issues or problems.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the golf cart's condition carefully and answer the questions based on your observations.
2. For select_one fields (Brakes, Steering Wheel, Horn and Signals, Lights and Signals), choose the option that best describes the current condition of the component.
3. For text fields (Mechanical Condition, Steering and Suspension, Tire Condition, Electrical System, Battery, Suspension System), describe the condition of the component in your own words.
4. Make sure to complete all required fields (Brakes, Steering Wheel, Horn and Signals, Lights and Signals).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mechanical Condition**: Describe the overall condition of the golf cart's mechanical components, such as the engine, transmission, and drivetrain.
* **Brakes**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Braking system: The cart's braking system is functioning correctly.
	+ Good condition: The brakes are in good working condition.
	+ Warning: There is a minor issue with the brakes, but they are still operational.
	+ Failed: The brakes are not functioning correctly.
* **Steering and Suspension**: Describe the condition of the cart's steering and suspension system, including any issues with the steering wheel, tie rods, and shock absorbers.
* **Tire Condition**: Describe the condition of the cart's tires, including any signs of wear, uneven wear, or damage.
* **Electrical System**: Describe the condition of the cart's electrical system, including the battery, wiring, and electrical components.
* **Steering Wheel**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Normal: The steering wheel is in good condition.
	+ Loose: The steering wheel is loose or wobbly.
	+ Worn: The steering wheel is showing signs of wear.
	+ Missing: The steering wheel is missing or damaged.
* **Horn and Signals**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Working: The horn and signals are functioning correctly.
	+ Not Working: The horn and signals are not functioning correctly.
* **Battery**: Describe the condition of the cart's battery, including its state of charge and any signs of wear.
* **Lights and Signals**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Working: The lights and signals are functioning correctly.
	+ Not Working: The lights and signals are not functioning correctly.
* **Suspension System**: Describe the condition of the cart's suspension system, including any issues with the shock absorbers, springs, or other components.
* **Note**: You can add any additional notes or comments about the golf cart's condition if you have any concerns or questions.
