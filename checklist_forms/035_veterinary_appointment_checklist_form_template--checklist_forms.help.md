# veterinary_appointment_checklist_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for non-urgent veterinary appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the general information page and fill in the necessary details.
2. On the pet owner information page, choose the pet's owner from the dropdown list.
3. On the appointment details page, select the date and time of the appointment.
4. On the payment information page, choose the payment method.
5. Add any necessary medical or veterinary notes on the respective pages.
6. Select the signature of the person scheduling the appointment.
7. Confirm that the form has been filled out correctly.
8. Finally, select "Yes" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info**: A page to input the appointment title and other general information about the appointment.
* **pet_owner_info**: A dropdown list of pet owners. Choose the pet's owner from the list.
* **appointment_date**: The date of the appointment.
* **appointment_time**: The time of the appointment.
* **veterinary_info**: A text box to add any veterinary information or notes.
* **medical_notes**: A text box to add any medical notes or information.
* **payment_info**: A dropdown list of payment methods. Choose the payment method used for the appointment.
* **veterinary_notes**: A text box to add any veterinary notes or information.
* **payment_notes**: A text box to add any payment notes or information.
* **signature**: A dropdown list of people who can sign the form. Choose the person scheduling the appointment.
* **confirm**: A confirmation checkbox to confirm that the form has been filled out correctly.
* **submit**: A dropdown list to confirm that you are ready to submit the form. Choose "Yes" to submit the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and correctly.
* Double-check the appointment details for accuracy.
* Use the correct payment method and confirm that you have received payment.
* Use the correct veterinary and medical notes if necessary.
* Ensure that you have signed the form correctly.
* Review the form before submitting it for accuracy and completeness.
