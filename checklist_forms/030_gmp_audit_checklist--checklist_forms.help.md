# gmp_audit_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a GMP audit checklist for facilities that have already been identified as requiring a GMP audit. It is used to evaluate the compliance of a facility with regulatory standards and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions based on your knowledge of the facility's processes and systems.
2. Use the provided options to select the correct response for each question.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please provide additional information in the text fields to explain your answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Audit Scope** (Page 1, `id: 1`, `type: text`): Describe the scope of the audit, including the specific processes and systems that were evaluated.
* **GMP Policy** (Page 1, `id: 2`, `type: number`): Indicate whether the facility has a GMP policy in place (`0` for no policy, `1` for a policy in place but not up-to-date, `2` for a policy that is up-to-date and effective). 
* **Regulatory Authorization** (Page 2, `id: 3`, `type: select_one`): Indicate whether the facility has regulatory authorization for its products or processes (`Yes` or `No`).
* **Audit Frequency** (Page 2, `id: 4`, `type: select_multiple`): Select all frequencies at which the facility has conducted audits in the past (e.g., `Yes` for annual audits).
* **Management Systems** (Page 3, `id: 5`, `type: text`): Describe the facility's management systems, including any documentation or records that are maintained.
* **GMP Certifications** (Page 3, `id: 6`, `type: text`): List the facility's relevant GMP certifications, including any certifications that are about to expire or need to be renewed.
* **Regulatory Standards** (Page 4, `id: 7`, `type: time`): Enter the date on which the facility last reviewed or updated its regulatory standards.
* **Audit Scheduling** (Page 4, `id: 8`, `type: date`): Enter the date on which the next scheduled audit is due or the date of the last completed audit.
* **Training and Experience** (Page 5, `id: 9`, `type: text`): Describe the training and experience of personnel involved in the audit, including any relevant certifications or qualifications.
* **Personnel Involved** (Page 5, `id: 10`, `type: text`): List the names and roles of personnel involved in the audit, including any external auditors or consultants.
* **Equipment and Vehicles** (Page 6, `id: 11`, `type: text`): Describe the equipment and vehicles used during the audit, including any that are shared or used by other facilities.
* **Records and Documents** (Page 6, `id: 12`, `type: text`): List the facility's relevant records and documents, including any that are stored electronically or on paper.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when answering questions, and provide any additional information that you believe would be helpful for the audit review.
* If you are unsure about any question, please provide explanations or supporting evidence in the text fields.
