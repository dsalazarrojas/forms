# Student Support Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Support Checklist Form is a comprehensive checklist designed to help students describe their support needs and provide relevant information for support staff to assist them effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

*   Enter a brief title to describe the form purpose and content.
*   Provide a detailed description of your support needs and concerns.
*   Select the category that best describes your support request.
*   Select the tool or service that is most relevant to meeting your support needs.
*   Enter a unique ID for your form, if required.
*   Enter the name of the category you selected earlier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Student Support Checklist Form** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title to describe the form purpose and content.
*   **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your support needs and concerns. This field is used to give support staff a clear understanding of your situation.
*   **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes your support request. For example, if you're experiencing technical issues, select "Checklist Forms".
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool or service that is most relevant to meeting your support needs. For example, "chatjimmy" if you need assistance with a specific tool.
*   **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique ID for your form, if required.
*   **Category Name** (`category_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the category you selected earlier.

## Tips
*   Please be as detailed as possible when providing descriptions to help support staff understand your needs better.
*   Select the most relevant category and tool to ensure that support staff can assist you efficiently.
*   If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask support staff for guidance.
*   This form is designed to be completed only once per support request. If you have multiple support needs, please submit separate forms for each need.
