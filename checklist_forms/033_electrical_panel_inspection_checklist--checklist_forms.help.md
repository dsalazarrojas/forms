# Electrical Panel Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for electrical professionals to conduct a thorough inspection of electrical panels. It helps ensure that all safety and regulatory requirements are met, and provides a systematic way to document the inspection process and results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the panel name in the "Electrical Panel Name" field.
2. Select the inspection date in the "Inspection Date" field.
3. Choose the panel status as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Panel Status" field.
4. Answer "Yes" or "No" for each of the subsequent fields regarding ground faults, electrical panels, panel type, circuit breakers, fuses, safety equipment, fire suppression, and arc flares.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Panel Condition" field, indicating whether the panel is in good condition.
6. Enter any inspection notes in the "Inspection Notes" field.
7. Enter the inspector's name in the "Inspector Name" field.
8. Select whether the department is "Yes" or "No", indicating the department responsible for the panel.
9. Select whether the panel is assigned to a specific person or group as "Yes" or "No".
10. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Inspector Signature" field, indicating whether the inspector's signature is present.
11. Enter the date and time of completion in the "Inspection Date Completed" and "Time Completed" fields.
12. Enter the location where the inspection took place.
13. Enter any additional notes from the inspector.
14. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Electrical Panel Inspection Certificate" field, indicating whether the panel was issued a certificate.
15. Finally, enter the assigned tool or equipment used during the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Electrical Panel Name**: Enter the name of the electrical panel being inspected.
* **Inspection Date**: Select the date of the inspection.
* **Panel Status**: Choose "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the panel's status.
* **Ground Faults**: Answer "Yes" or "No" if the panel has any ground faults.
* **Electrical Panels**: Answer "Yes" or "No" if the panel has any electrical panels.
* **Electrical Panel Type**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the panel is of the correct type.
* **Circuit Breakers**: Answer "Yes" or "No" if the circuit breakers are functioning properly.
* **Fuses**: Answer "Yes" or "No" if the fuses are present and functioning.
* **Safety Equipment**: Answer "Yes" or "No" if safety equipment is present and in good condition.
* **Fire Suppression**: Answer "Yes" or "No" if fire suppression systems are present and functioning.
* **Arc Flares**: Answer "Yes" or "No" if arc flares are present and in good condition.
* **Panel Condition**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the panel is in good condition.
* **Inspection Notes**: Enter any notes or comments from the inspection.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the inspector performing the inspection.
* **Department**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the department is responsible for the panel.
* **Assigned To**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the panel is assigned to a specific person or group.
* **Inspector Signature**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the inspector's signature is present.
* **Inspection Date Completed**: Enter the date the inspection was completed.
* **Time Completed**: Enter the time the inspection was completed.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the inspection took place.
* **Inspector Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the inspector.
* **Electrical Panel Inspection Certificate**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the panel was issued a certificate.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool or equipment used during the inspection.
