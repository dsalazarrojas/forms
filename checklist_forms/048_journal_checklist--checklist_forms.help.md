# Journal Checklists - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Journal Checklists" form is a tool designed to help users track their daily or weekly progress, habits, and self-care activities. It provides a structured way to record metrics and reflections, enabling users to identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions about their routines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page to ensure you are in the correct section (e.g., Tasks Completed, Focus Time, etc.).
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. For fields with a "select_multiple" type, choose all applicable options that apply to your activities.
4. Enter any relevant notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tasks Completed** (`tasks_completed`, `number`, required): Record the number of tasks you completed today or this week.
* **Focus Time** (`focus_time`, `number`, required): Record the amount of time you spent focused on tasks, in minutes.
* **Breaks Taken** (`breaks_taken`, `number`, required): Record the number of breaks you took today or this week.
* **Water Intake** (`water_intake`, `number`, required): Record the amount of water you consumed today or this week.
* **Exercise Done** (`exercise_done`, `number`, required): Record the amount of exercise or physical activity you completed today or this week.
* **Time Wasted** (`time_wasted`, `number`, required): Record any time you feel you wasted due to distractions or lack of focus.
* **Self Care** (`self_care`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the self-care activities you engaged in today or this week, such as meditation, journaling, sleep, exercise, or socializing.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or reflections about your day or week.
