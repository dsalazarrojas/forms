# Language School Admission Checklist Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from students seeking admission to a language school, including language school type, student demographics, contact information, and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) next to the label.
2. Select the language school type from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter the student's age in numerical format.
4. Choose the student's gender from the drop-down menu.
5. Enter the student's language proficiency, if applicable.
6. Enter the student's contact information, including contact number.
7. Provide the emergency contact's name and contact number.
8. If necessary, provide any relevant medical details about the student.
9. Finally, indicate whether the parent has given their sign-off for the student's admission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Language School Admission Checklist Form Template**: This is the title of the form, which is not editable.
* **Language School Admission Details**: Enter a brief description of the student's admission details, including their language proficiency and any other relevant information.
* **Language School Type**: Select from the drop-down menu to indicate whether the language school is Online or Offline.
* **Student Age**: Enter the student's age in numerical format.
* **Student Gender**: Choose from the drop-down menu to indicate the student's gender.
* **Student Language**: Enter the student's native language.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the student's contact number.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact number of the emergency contact.
* **Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the student's guardian.
* **Guardian Number**: Enter the contact number of the student's guardian.
* **Student Medical Details**: Provide any relevant medical details about the student.
* **Parent Sign-off**: Indicate whether the parent has given their sign-off for the student's admission.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in correctly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification from the language school staff.
* This form is for informational purposes only and is not a contract or agreement.
