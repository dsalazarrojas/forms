# Dog Obedience Training Workout Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and document a dog's obedience training sessions. It's a crucial tool for trainers to record and review progress, identify areas of improvement, and make data-driven decisions for future training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the training date from the calendar or enter the date manually.
2. Enter the trainer's name.
3. Select your dog's breed from the available options.
4. Select the exercises completed during the session from the list.
5. Add any additional notes about the session.
6. Provide your phone number and email for further communication.
7. Indicate the practice frequency and duration for future reference.
8. Enter any comments from the trainer.
9. Review and submit the form to save the data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training session** (`training_session`, number, required: false): This field is used to select the training session number.
* **Training date** (`training_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Trainer name** (`trainer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the trainer's name who conducted the session.
* **Dog breed** (`dog_breed`, text, required: false): Select your dog's breed to ensure accurate tracking.
* **Exercises completed** (`exercises_completed`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the exercises completed during the session. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
	+ Option 5
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the session.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Practice frequency** (`practice_frequency`, text, required: false): Indicate the frequency of practice sessions.
* **Practice duration** (`practice_duration`, text, required: false): Indicate the duration of practice sessions.
* **Trainer comments** (`trainer_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments from the trainer about the session.

## Tips
- Ensure to complete this form accurately and consistently for each training session.
- Review and submit the form to save the data.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the provided options or seek guidance from the trainer.
