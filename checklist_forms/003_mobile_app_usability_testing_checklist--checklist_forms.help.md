# Mobile App Usability Testing Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you report on your app usability testing experience. It is used to gather essential information about your app's usability, ensuring it meets the required standards for a smooth and user-friendly experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "App Name" field and enter your app's name as it appears on the app store.
2. Enter the "App Version" field with the version number of the app you tested (e.g., 2.1.0).
3. Choose the "Operating System" field where you tested the app (Android, iOS, or Windows).
4. Select the "Screen Size" field according to where you tested the app (Smartphone, Tablet, or Desktop).
5. Enter the "App Launch Time" field with the actual time it took for the app to launch on your device.
6. Rate the "App Usability Rating" field by providing a number between 1 and 10, indicating your overall experience with the app's usability.
7. Describe any issues you encountered in the "Issue Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **App Name** (required): Enter the name of the app you tested.
- **App Version** (required): Enter the version of the app you used for testing.
- **Operating System** (optional): Select the operating system where you tested the app (Android, iOS, or Windows).
- **Screen Size** (optional): Choose the screen size where you tested the app (Smartphone, Tablet, or Desktop).
- **App Launch Time** (required): Enter the actual time it took for the app to launch on your device.
- **App Usability Rating** (required): Provide a rating from 1 to 10, indicating your overall usability experience with the app.
- **Issue Description** (required): Describe any issues or problems you encountered while using the app.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
- If you are testing on a non-standard screen size, such as a custom device, please select "Other" and specify in the "Issue Description" field.
- Be as detailed as possible when describing the issues you faced, so we can better understand your experience.
- If you have any further comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "Issue Description" field as well.
- If you need further support or clarification, do not hesitate to reach out for help.
