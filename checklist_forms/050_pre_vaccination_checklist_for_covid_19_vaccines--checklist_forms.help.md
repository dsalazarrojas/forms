# Pre Vaccination Checklist For COVID 19 Vaccines - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Vaccination Checklist For COVID 19 Vaccines is a comprehensive form used to document patient information and vaccination history prior to administering a COVID-19 vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the First Visit Date, which is the date of the patient's initial visit for vaccination.
* Select the type of COVID-19 vaccine the patient received, if applicable.
* Enter the patient's age and weight, if applicable.
* Provide information about any medical devices the patient may be using, including any medical devices with serial numbers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Visit Date** (`first_visit_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the patient's initial visit for vaccination.
* **Vaccination Type** (`vaccination_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of COVID-19 vaccine the patient received, if applicable.
* **Dose Number** (`dose_number`, number, required): Enter the number of doses of the vaccine the patient has received.
* **Vaccination Site** (`vaccination_site`, select_one, required): Select the location where the patient received the vaccination (e.g., Clinic, Outpatient, Inpatient).
* **Patient ID Number** (`patient_id_number`, number, optional): Enter the patient's ID number, if available.
* **Date of Last Vaccination** (`date_of_last_vaccination`, date, optional): Enter the date of the patient's last vaccination, if applicable.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, optional): Enter the patient's age.
* **Patient Weight** (`patient_weight`, number, optional): Enter the patient's weight.
* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's gender.
* **Allergy History** (`allergy_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the patient's allergy history.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions the patient has.
* **Medical Device** (`medical_device`, text, optional): List any medical devices the patient may be using.
* **Medical Device Model** (`medical_device_model`, text, optional): Enter the model number of the medical device.
* **Medical Device Serial Number** (`medical_device_serial_number`, text, optional): Enter the serial number of the medical device.
* **Medical Device Lot Number** (`medical_device_lot_number`, text, optional): Enter the lot number of the medical device.
* **Medical Device Expiry Date** (`medical_device_expiry_date`, date, optional): Enter the expiry date of the medical device.
* **Medical Device Manufacturer** (`medical_device_manufacturer`, text, optional): Enter the manufacturer of the medical device.
* **Medical Device Warranty Start Date** (`medical_device_warranty_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the warranty for the medical device.
* **Medical Device Warranty End Date** (`medical_device_warranty_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the warranty for the medical device.
* **Medical Device Warranty Status** (`medical_device_warranty_status`, text, optional): Enter the status of the medical device warranty.
* **Medical Device Serialized Warranty** (`medical_device_serialized_warranty`, text, optional): Enter any serialized warranty details for the medical device.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact for the patient.

Note: The fields marked as optional can be left blank if the information is not available or not relevant to the patient's vaccination history.
