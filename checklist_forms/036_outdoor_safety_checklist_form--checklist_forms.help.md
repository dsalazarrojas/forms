# Outdoor Safety Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outdoor Safety Checklist Form is a simple and accessible tool designed to help assess and record the safety measures taken during outdoor activities. This form is intended to be completed by participants to provide a quick and easy way to collect information on their safety.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete each section of the form carefully, making sure to provide accurate and truthful information.
2.  For the "Risk Level" field, select the level of risk that best describes the activity.
3.  If you found any hazards during the activity, select "Yes" and provide a brief description in the "Hazard Found" field.
4.  In the "Control Measures" field, describe any measures taken to mitigate or address the hazards found.
5.  If safety measures were taken, select "Yes" in the "Action Taken" field.
6.  Provide any additional steps that will be taken to ensure participant safety in the "Next Steps" field.
7.  Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Participant Safety" field to indicate their overall safety.
8.  If you have any comments or notes regarding participant safety, enter them in the "Participant Safety Notes" field.
9.  Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Form Completion" field to indicate whether the form has been completed.
10. Enter the date and time of submission in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
11. Enter the email of the person submitting the form in the "Form Submitted By" field.
12. If applicable, enter the tool or equipment used in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Info (Participant Info)**: This section is used to collect basic information about the participant, such as their name, email, and date/time of submission.
*   **Risk Level (Risk Level)**: This field is used to determine the level of risk during the activity. Select the level of risk that best describes the activity.
*   **Hazard Found (Hazard Found)**: If you found any hazards during the activity, select "Yes" and provide a brief description.
*   **Hazard Description (Hazard Description)**: Provide a brief description of any hazards found during the activity.
*   **Control Measures (Control Measures)**: Describe any measures taken to mitigate or address the hazards found.
*   **Action Taken (Action Taken)**: Select "Yes" if safety measures were taken.
*   **Next Steps (Next Steps)**: Provide any additional steps that will be taken to ensure participant safety.
*   **Participant Safety (Participant Safety)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the participant's overall safety.
*   **Participant Safety Notes (Participant Safety Notes)**: Enter any comments or notes regarding participant safety.
*   **Form Completion (Form Completion)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the form has been completed.
*   **Date (Date)**: Enter the date of submission.
*   **Time (Time)**: Enter the time of submission.
*   **Form Submitted By (Form Submitted By)**: Enter the email of the person submitting the form.
*   **Assigned Tool (Assigned Tool)**: If applicable, enter the tool or equipment used.
