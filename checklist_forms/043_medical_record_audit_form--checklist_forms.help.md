# Medical Record Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Record Audit Form is designed to help auditors review and verify the information contained within a patient's medical record. It ensures that the record is accurate and complete, and identifies any errors or discrepancies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

* Review the patient's medical record carefully and answer each question based on the information found in the record.
* Select the relevant information from the options provided for each field.
* Complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Review Medical Record** (`medical_record_review`, 1, required: false): This field is a text box where you can enter any comments or notes about the patient's medical record. Be thorough and accurate in your review.
* **Clinical Data Entry** (`clinical_data_entry`, 2, required: false): This field is a multiple-choice question that asks if the patient's clinical data is accurate. Select 'Yes' if it is accurate, and 'No' if it is not.
* **Patient Identification** (`patient_identification`, 3, required: false): Enter the patient's unique identifier number or other identifying information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, 4, required: false): This is a text box where you can enter any comments or notes about the patient's medical history.
* **Medication History** (`medication_history`, 5, required: false): This field is a multiple-choice question that asks if the patient's medication history is accurate. Select 'Yes' if it is accurate, and 'No' if it is not.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, 6, required: false): Enter the patient's treatment plan, including any relevant dates or times.
* **Medical Errors** (`medical_errors`, 7, required: false): This field is a multiple-choice question that asks if there are any medical errors in the patient's record. Select 'Yes' if there are errors, and 'No' if there are none.
* **Medical Compliance** (`medical_compliance`, 8, required: false): Enter the date of the patient's medical compliance, if applicable.
* **Audit Completion Date** (`audit_completion_date`, 9, required: false): Enter the date of completion of the audit.
* **Auditor Name** (`auditor_name`, 10, required: false): Enter your name as the auditor.
* **Auditor Title** (`auditor_title`, 11, required: false): Enter your title or position.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, 12, required: false): Enter the name of the facility where the patient was treated.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, 13, required: false): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Medical Record Date** (`medical_record_date`, 14, required: false): Enter the date of the patient's medical record.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, 15, required: false): Enter the address of the facility where the patient was treated.
* **Auditor Comments** (`auditor_comments`, 16, required: false): Enter any comments or notes about the audit.
* **Auditor Contact** (`auditor_contact`, 17, required: false): Enter your contact information as the auditor.
* **Auditor Signature** (`auditor_signature`, 18, required: false): Enter your signature as the auditor.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when completing this form.
* Review the patient's medical record carefully before starting the audit.
* Use the options provided for each field to ensure accurate and consistent responses.
* Save the completed form for future reference.
