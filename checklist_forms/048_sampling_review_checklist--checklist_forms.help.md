# Sampling Review Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from a reviewer about their review process and feedback on a sample.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide sample details, including any relevant information about the sample you are reviewing.
2. Select the sample steps you have reviewed, choosing from the options provided.
3. Choose any applicable sample approvals, selecting multiple options if necessary.
4. Enter any notes or comments about the review process.
5. Specify the review date and time.
6. Enter your name and email address, so we can contact you for further information.
7. Enter your phone number, in case we need to contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sample Details** (`sample_details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the sample being reviewed.
* **Sample Steps Review** (`sample_steps_review`, select_one, required: false): Choose the steps you have reviewed, selecting one of the provided options.
* **Sample Approvals** (`sample_approvals`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the approvals you agree with, selecting multiple options if necessary.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any comments or observations about the review process.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you completed the review.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you completed the review.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name, so we can contact you for further information.
* **Reviewer E-Mail** (`reviewer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address, so we can contact you for further information.
* **Reviewer Phone** (`reviewer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number, in case we need to contact you.

## Tips

* Make sure to review all provided options before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
* If you need to review multiple samples, you may need to complete this form multiple times.
