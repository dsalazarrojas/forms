# Security Shift Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Security Shift Checklist is used by shift leaders to record essential details about their shifts. This guide will walk you through how to complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

1. Enter your name as the Shift Leader.
2. Type the correct Shift Number.
3. Choose the date and start time for the shift.
4. Choose the start time and end time of the shift.
5. Enter your email address (optional) if you are the shift leader.
6. Enter your phone number (optional).
7. Add any additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shift Leader Name** (`shift_leader_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the shift leader. This is an essential field that helps the team know who was in charge during this shift.
* **Shift Number** (`shift_number`, number, required): Enter the correct number assigned to this shift. This helps with record-keeping and reporting.
* **Date Shift Started** (`date_shift_started`, date, required): Choose the date this shift started from the calendar.
* **Time Shift Started** (`time_shift_started`, time, required): Choose the start time of the shift from the clock.
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, time, required): Choose the end time of the shift from the clock.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any important notes or comments about this shift. This is for your personal reference and can help the team review and understand the shift.
* **Security Lead Email** (`email_security_lead`, email, required): Enter the email of the person leading the security team.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you are the shift leader or need to be contacted in case of an emergency.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review and complete each field accurately to ensure the record of your shift is correct.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or team lead for guidance.
* This form is for your shift records, so keep it up to date and complete.
