# Exit Interview Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for exiting employees to provide their thoughts and feedback about their experience with the company. The information gathered will help us understand the reasons behind their decision to leave and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exit Interview Checklist** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This title field is to be filled with the title of the form, "Exit Interview Checklist".
* **Company Name** (`2`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the company you are exiting.
* **Job Title** (`3`, `text`, optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Department** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter your current department name.
* **Reason for Departure** (`5`, `text`, optional): Please provide a brief explanation for your decision to leave the company.
* **Last Day Worked** (`6`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your last day of work at the company.
* **Exit Interview Comments** (`7`, `text`, optional): Share any thoughts, comments, or suggestions you have about your exit experience.
* **How would you rate the company?** (`8`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the options: Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Name of Manager** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your manager or supervisor.
