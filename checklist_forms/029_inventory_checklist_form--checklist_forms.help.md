# Inventory Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track items in the inventory. It is intended for use by authorized personnel to record information about each item, including its status, quantity, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, first review the item's information and ensure it matches the current status.
*   Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about the item.
*   Make sure to save changes after filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Checked By** (`last_checked_by`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to indicate who last checked the item. Please select the person who last checked the item from the provided options.
*   **Item Name** (`item_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the item being tracked.
*   **Item Description** (`item_description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the item.
*   **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the current quantity of the item.
*   **Date Received** (`date_received`, `date`, required): Record the date the item was received.
*   **Last Used Date** (`date_last_used`, `date`, required): Record the date the item was last used.
*   **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of the item (Active, Inactive, Lost).
*   **Status** (`checked_by`, `select_one`, optional): Select who last checked the item (if not checked by authorized personnel, use "Unknown").
*   **Item Weight** (`item_weight`, `number`, required): Enter the item's weight.
*   **Item Price** (`item_price`, `number`, required): Enter the item's price.
*   **Item Value** (`item_value`, `number`, required): Enter the item's value.
*   **Item Color** (`item_color`, `text`, required): Enter the item's color.
*   **Item Size** (`item_size`, `text`, required): Enter the item's size.
*   **Item Material** (`item_material`, `text`, required): Enter the item's material.
*   **Item Type** (`item_type`, `text`, required): Enter the item's type.
*   **Item Status** (`item_status`, `text`, required): Enter the item's status.
*   **Item Condition** (`item_condition`, `text`, required): Enter the item's condition.
*   **Item Brand** (`item_brand`, `text`, required): Enter the item's brand.
*   **Item Model** (`item_model`, `text`, required): Enter the item's model.
*   **Item Manufacturer** (`item_manufacturer`, `text`, required): Enter the item's manufacturer.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the item.

## Tips

*   Always review the information before filling out the form to ensure accuracy and consistency.
*   If unsure about an item's information, please check with the inventory team or the item's owner before proceeding.
*   Save changes after filling out the form to update the item's record.
