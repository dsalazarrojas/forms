# Manufacturing Training Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track and record manufacturing training progress and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Training Checklist" field with the current training status.
2. Provide supervisor comments and feedback in the "Supervisor Comments" field.
3. Enter the date you completed the training in the "Date Completed" field.
4. Add any additional comments from the safety team in the "Safety Team Comments" field.
5. Select the "Training Status" field to indicate if the training is Completed, In Progress, or Not Started.
6. Choose who completed the training from the "Completed By" field.
7. Enter the number of training hours spent in the "Training Hours" field.
8. Add any feedback or comments in the "Feedback" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
11. Select who submitted the form from the "Submitted By" field.
12. Enter the completed date in the "Completed Date" field.
13. Enter the training location in the "Training Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Checklist** (Training Checklist, text, optional): Fill out this field with the current training status.
* **Supervisor Comments** (Supervisor Comments, text, optional): Provide any comments or feedback from the supervisor.
* **Date Completed** (Date Completed, date, optional): Enter the date you completed the training.
* **Safety Team Comments** (Safety Team Comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments from the safety team.
* **Training Status** (Training Status, select multiple, optional): Select if the training is Completed, In Progress, or Not Started.
* **Completed By** (Completed By, select one, optional): Choose who completed the training from the options.
* **Training Hours** (Training Hours, number, optional): Enter the number of training hours spent.
* **Feedback** (Feedback, note, optional): Add any feedback or comments.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By, select one, optional): Select who submitted the form from the options.
* **Completed Date** (Completed Date, date, optional): Enter the completed date.
* **Training Location** (Training Location, text, optional): Enter the training location.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* Ensure that the form is submitted with accurate and up-to-date information.
