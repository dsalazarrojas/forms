# Buddy Shift Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Buddy Shift Checklist is a form used to facilitate the handoff process between outgoing and incoming employees during shifts. It helps to ensure that all necessary information is communicated, including supplies, equipment, and security protocols.

## How To Complete This Form

* To complete this form, start by entering the Outgoing Employee Name, which is the employee ending their shift.
* Next, enter the Incoming Employee Name, who will be taking over the shift.
* Then, select the Shift Date from the calendar picker.
* Choose the Shift Type from the dropdown menu.
* Enter the Department where the shift is taking place.
* Select the status of Register or Cash Drawer, whether it is Balanced and Closed or not.
* Choose whether the Inventory Levels are Verified.
* Select the status of Supplies, whether they are Fully Restocked, Partially Restocked, or Not Restocked.
* Indicate if Area Cleaning and Organization are completed.
* Check the Equipment Status and Safety, whether it is in Good condition, has Minor issues, or Major issues.
* Confirm if the Security Checklist is completed.
* Note any Pending Issues or Tasks that need to be addressed.
* Enter any Critical Information for the Incoming Shift.
* Enter any Customer or Guest Information that needs to be passed on.
* Choose the Follow-up Communication Method, such as In-person handoff, Written notes, Email, Phone call, or Other.
* Finally, confirm that the Incoming Employee acknowledges receipt of the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Outgoing Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee ending their shift.
* **Incoming Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee taking over the shift.
* **Shift Date**: Select the date of the shift.
* **Shift Type**: Choose the type of shift, such as Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night, or Weekend.
* **Department**: Enter the department where the shift is taking place.
* **Register or Cash Drawer Balanced and Closed**: Check whether the register or cash drawer is balanced and closed.
* **Inventory Levels Verified**: Indicate whether inventory levels are verified.
* **Supplies Status**: Select the status of supplies, such as Fully Restocked, Partially Restocked, or Not Restocked.
* **Area Cleaned and Organized**: Check if the area is cleaned and organized.
* **Equipment Status and Safety**: Check the status of equipment, such as Good condition, Minor issues, or Major issues.
* **Security Checklist Completed**: Confirm whether the security checklist is completed.
* **Pending Issues or Tasks**: Enter any issues or tasks that need to be addressed.
* **Critical Information for Incoming Shift**: Enter any critical information for the incoming shift.
* **Customer or Guest Information**: Enter any customer or guest information that needs to be passed on.
* **Follow-up Communication Method**: Choose the method to communicate with the incoming employee, such as In-person handoff, Written notes, Email, Phone call, or Other.
* **Incoming Employee Acknowledges Receipt of Information**: Confirm that the incoming employee acknowledges receipt of the information.
