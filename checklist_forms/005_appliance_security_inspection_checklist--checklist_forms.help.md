# Appliance Security Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to guide inspectors through a structured process to assess the safety and security of various appliances in a workplace or facility. This form ensures that all critical aspects of appliance safety are evaluated and documented, helping to prevent accidents and maintain overall safety.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Date of Inspection**: Enter the date of the inspection in the designated field.
2. **Inspector Name**: Enter your name and contact information for identification purposes.
3. **Location - Site Name**: Enter the location where the inspection was performed.
4. **Appliance Type**: Select the type of appliance being inspected from the provided options (e.g., Refrigerator, Oven - Range, etc.).
5. **Safety and Security Checks**: This section includes various checks for the appliance's safety and security features. Select the corresponding option for each check:
	* **Electrical Cord and Plug Condition**: Choose from Satisfactory, Requires Attention, or Dangerous - Unplugged.
	* **Proper Grounding Verified**: Choose from True, False, or Unknown.
	* **Adequate Clearance from Combustibles**: Choose from True or False.
	* **Ventilation Paths Clear**: Choose from Clear, Partially Blocked, or Obstructed.
	* **Control Knobs and Buttons Functional**: Choose from True or False.
	* **Gas or Water Leak Inspection**: Choose from No Leaks Detected, Minor Leak Found, or Major Leak - Immediate Action Taken.
6. **Overall Security Rating**: Rate the overall security of the appliance from 5 (Very Safe) to 1 (Unsafe).
7. **Repairs or Maintenance Required**: If any issues were found, describe them in this field.
8. **Maintenance Ticket Number**: If a maintenance ticket was opened, enter the number here.
9. **Recommended Next Inspection Date**: Enter the date for the next scheduled inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Inspection (2)**: This field is used to record the date when the inspection was performed.
* **Inspector Name (3)**: This field is for the inspector's name and contact information.
* **Location - Site Name (4)**: Enter the name of the location where the inspection took place.
* **Appliance Type (5)**: Select the type of appliance being inspected from the provided options.
* **Electrical Cord and Plug Condition (7)**: Check the condition of the electrical cord and plug for signs of wear, damage, or fraying.
* **Proper Grounding Verified (8)**: Check if the appliance is properly grounded.

* **Ventilation Paths Clear (10)**: Check that the ventilation paths are clear of obstructions.
* **Control Knobs and Buttons Functional (11)**: Test the functionality of control knobs and buttons.
* **Gas or Water Leak Inspection (12)**: Check for signs of gas or water leaks.
* **Overall Security Rating (14)**: Rate the overall security of the appliance.

* **Maintenance Ticket Number (16)**: If a maintenance ticket was opened, enter the number here.
