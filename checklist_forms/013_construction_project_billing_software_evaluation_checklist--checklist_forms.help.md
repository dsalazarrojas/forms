# Construction Project Billing Software Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights about construction project billing software. It should be completed by an authorized evaluator from the organization to document their assessment and recommendation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title in the "Evaluator name" field.
2. Enter the name of your company or organization in the "Company name" field.
3. Select all the core billing features that the software requires from the list in the "Core billing features needed" field.
4. Specify the systems that the software must integrate with in the "Integration requirements" field.
5. Rate the ease of use of the software in the "Usability rating" field (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
6. Enter an estimate of the software's annual licensing or subscription cost.
7. Select the recommended course of action: Purchase, Pilot, or Do not pursue.
8. Provide a clear and concise recommendation for the software, considering the information above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator name** (`Evaluator name`, `text`, required): Enter your name and title.
* **Company name** (`Company name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Core billing features needed** (`Core billing features needed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the required capabilities of the software, such as Progress billing, Retainage management, Change order tracking, Automated invoicing, and Reporting dashboard.
* **Integration requirements** (`Integration requirements`, `text`, optional): Specify the systems that the software must integrate with.
* **Usability rating** (`Usability rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the ease of use of the software as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Estimated annual cost** (`Estimated annual cost`, `number`, optional): Enter an estimate of the software's annual licensing or subscription cost.
* **Recommended action** (`Recommended action`, `select_one`, required): Select the recommended course of action: Purchase, Pilot, or Do not pursue.
* **Estimated annual cost** (`Estimated annual cost`, `number`, optional): Note: This field is not visible, as it should be removed from the actual form, but it's mentioned here for consistency with the provided YAML structure.
