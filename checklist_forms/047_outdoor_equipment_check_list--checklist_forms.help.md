# Outdoor Equipment Check List - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on the outdoor equipment used during an outdoor activity or event. It will gather details about the participant, equipment, and activity, and participant's email for follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the participant's details in the "participant_details" field.
2. Describe the outdoor equipment used in the "equipment_details" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the activity or event in the "activity_details" field.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field.
5. Enter the participant's email address in the "participant_email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Enter the name, age, and any other relevant information about the participant taking part in the outdoor activity or event.

### Outdoor Equipment
* **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`, text, optional): Describe the type, brand, and any other relevant details about the equipment used during the outdoor activity or event.

### Outdoor Activity
* **Activity Details** (`activity_details`, text, optional): Describe the type of outdoor activity or event (e.g., hiking, camping, etc.).

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the activity or event.

### Participant Email
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter the participant's email address for follow-up purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about the participant, equipment, and activity for thorough record-keeping and follow-up purposes.
* You can add or remove fields as needed to suit your specific use case.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
