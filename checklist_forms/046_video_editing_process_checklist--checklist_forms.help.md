# Video Editing Process Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps you document and track the video editing process, ensuring all critical steps are completed and tasks are finished on time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each step of the video editing process.
2. Check the box next to each step if you have completed it. If not, explain why not.
3. If you are the reviewer, enter your comments and signature after completing the final review.
4. Upload any files associated with the project.
5. Update deadline and task status as needed.
6. Enter project deadline and review date.
