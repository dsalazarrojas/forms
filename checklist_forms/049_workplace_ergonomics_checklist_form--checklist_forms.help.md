# Workplace Ergonomics Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the ergonomic factors of a workspace and provide insights to improve employee comfort and productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly, based on your observation and experience of the workspace.
2. For each question, choose the most appropriate option or provide your own answer.
3. If an option does not apply, select "None" and provide a brief explanation in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workstations Evaluation** (`workstations_evaluation`, text, optional): Briefly describe the overall condition and comfort level of the workstations in the workspace.
* **Work Surface** (`work_surface`, text, optional): Describe the type and condition of the work surface, e.g., desk, chair, or table.
* **Work Height** (`work_height`, number, required): Measure the height of the workspace, including the floor height of the desk or surface, to ensure it's within the recommended range.
* **Work Distance** (`work_distance`, number, required): Measure the distance between the workspace and the nearest reference point (e.g., wall or neighboring workspace).
* **Chair Adjustability** (`chair_adjustability`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from Adjustable or Fixed to indicate if the chair can be adjusted to fit different individuals' needs.
* **Chair Type** (`chair_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of chair used, such as Chair or Standing Desk.
* **Monitor Height** (`monitor_height`, number, required): Measure the height of the monitor to ensure it's at or below the user's eye level.
* **Keyboard Position** (`keyboard_position`, select_one, required): Choose from Near, Far, or None to indicate the distance between the keyboard and the user.
* **Mouse Position** (`mouse_position`, select_one, required): Choose from Near, Far, or None to indicate the distance between the mouse and the user.
* **Ergonomic Posture** (`ergonomic_posture`, time, optional): Measure the time spent in an ergonomic posture, e.g., 30 minutes or more.

## Tips
* Be honest and descriptive in your answers to ensure accurate assessment.
* Consider the needs of different employees with varying abilities and needs.
* If you're unsure about any aspect, consult with experts or seek guidance from your organization's ergonomics committee.
