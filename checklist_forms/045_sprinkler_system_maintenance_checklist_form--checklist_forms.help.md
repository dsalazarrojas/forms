# Sprinkler System Maintenance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sprinkler System Maintenance Checklist Form is designed to help you maintain and inspect your sprinkler system regularly. This form is meant to be completed by authorized personnel to ensure that your sprinkler system is functioning properly and to identify any potential issues that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant checklist items that apply to your sprinkler system from the first page.
2. Enter the desired frequency of maintenance for your sprinkler system on the second page.
3. Review and submit the form to schedule the maintenance task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Sprinkler System Checklist
* **Sprinkler System Checklist** (`sprinkler_system_checklist`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from the following options:
	+ Checklist 1a: Check the system's control valves for proper function and adjust as needed.
	+ Checklist 1b: Inspect the sprinkler pipes for any signs of leaks or damage.
	+ Checklist 1c: Check the system's pressure regulator and adjust as necessary.
* **Sprinkler Flow Rate** (`sprinkler_flow_rate`, `number`, required: false): Enter the average flow rate of your sprinkler system.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the maintenance frequency that applies to your sprinkler system:
	+ Monthly: Perform regular maintenance on the system every month.
	+ Bi-Monthly: Perform maintenance on the system every two months.
	+ Quarterly: Perform maintenance on the system every three months.
	+ Semi-Annual: Perform maintenance on the system every six months.
	+ Annual: Perform maintenance on the system once a year.

## Tips
* Be sure to review and complete the form regularly to ensure your sprinkler system is maintained properly.
* If you are unsure about any item on the form, consult the relevant documentation or contact a professional for assistance.
* Remember to schedule the maintenance tasks based on the frequencies selected on the form.
