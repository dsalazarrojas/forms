# Fire Door Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help users conduct a fire door inspection by evaluating the condition of various components of the fire door.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
2. Check the options for each field carefully, as they are meant to guide your inspection.
3. Answer each field according to your observation of the door's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Door Condition** (`door_condition`, select_one, Required): Check if the door is functional and can open and close properly.
* **Hinges** (`door_hinges`, select_multiple, Required): Evaluate the condition of the door's hinges.
* **Door Frame** (`door_frame`, select_one, Required): Check the condition of the door frame, paying attention to any signs of wear or damage.
* **Door Screws** (`door_screws`, select_multiple, Required): Check the condition of the door screws, looking for any signs of wear or damage.
* **Door Locks** (`door_locks`, select_one, Required): Check if the door has a functional lock and can be locked/unlocked properly.
* **Door Gasket** (`door_gasket`, select_multiple, Required): Evaluate the condition of the door gasket, paying attention to any signs of wear or damage.
* **Door Weatherstripping** (`door_weatherstripping`, select_multiple, Required): Check the condition of the door weatherstripping, looking for any signs of wear or damage.
* **Hinges Condition** (`hinges_condition`, select_one, Required): Evaluate the condition of the door's hinges.
* **Repairs** (`door_repairs`, note, Required): Record any necessary repairs or maintenance for the door.
* **Comments** (`door_comments`, note, Required): Add any additional comments or observations about the door's condition.
