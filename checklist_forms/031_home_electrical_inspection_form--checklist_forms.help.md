# Home Electrical Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Electrical Inspection Form is a document used to collect information about the electrical system of a property. It's designed to help inspectors gather data on the electrical panel, circuit breakers, wiring, electrical hazards, and other essential aspects of the electrical system. This form is intended for use by homebuyers, sellers, or property managers to ensure that the electrical system is safe and functional.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully before starting your inspection.
2. For each section, carefully observe and document your findings.
3. Select the relevant options for each field based on your observations.
4. Fill out any notes or comments as necessary to provide additional context.
5. Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **electrical_system_summary** (optional)
* This field is a note section where you can summarize your overall observations about the electrical system.
* Provide a brief summary of your findings, including any concerns or issues you've identified.

### 2. **electrical_panel** (optional)
* Choose one of the options that best describes the type of electrical panel.
* If none of the options are suitable, select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.

### 3. **circuit_breakers** (optional)
* Select all the options that describe the state of the circuit breakers.
* Make sure to consider the condition, type, and age of the circuit breakers.

### 4. **wiring** (optional)
* Select all the options that describe the type, condition, and presence of electrical wiring.
* Be sure to note any evidence you've found that supports your selection.

### 5. **electrical_hazards** (optional)
* Select all the options that describe the electrical hazards you've identified.
* Make sure to note the location and evidence of each hazard.

### 6. **electrical_inspection_summary** (optional)
* Provide a brief summary of your inspection findings.
* Include any concerns or issues you've identified, and suggest any necessary actions or recommendations.

### 7. **electrical_inspection_recommendations** (optional)
* Write a detailed list of recommendations for improving or addressing any electrical issues you've identified.
* Be specific and provide context for each recommendation.

### 8. **electrical_certification_documentation** (optional)
* Select all the options that describe the type, evidence, and location of electrical certification documentation.
* Ensure that you've obtained or reviewed any relevant documentation.

### 9. **electrical_certification_status** (optional)
* Choose one of the options that describe the status of electrical certification.
* If none of the options are suitable, select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.

### 10. **electrical_certificate_expiration** (optional)
* If applicable, enter the date of electrical certification expiration.
* Make sure to provide any additional information or context if necessary.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
- Use clear and concise language when describing your findings and providing recommendations.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- Attach any supporting documentation or evidence as necessary.
