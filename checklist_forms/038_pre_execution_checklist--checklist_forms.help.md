# Pre Execution Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre-Execution Checklist is a form designed to help you prepare for a project or task by verifying that all necessary steps have been taken before starting. It's an essential tool to ensure a successful execution and reduce potential risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review the checklist carefully to understand the items that need to be checked.
2. For each page, read the label and the options (if applicable) to understand what's being asked.
3. Select or enter the required information for each field.
4. Once completed, review your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Initial Review** (`Pre Execution Checklist 1`, `text`, required: false): This field is intended to capture any initial review comments or notes that may be relevant to the project or task at hand. Enter any observations, comments, or questions you may have before proceeding.
* **Checklists Completed** (`Checklists Completed`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to select which checklist has been completed. Choose the appropriate option from the list provided (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Sign-Off Confirmed** (`Sign-Off Confirmed`, `select_multiple`, required: false): In this field, select all the items that have been confirmed or signed off. You can choose one or multiple options (1, 2, or 3).

## Tips
* Be thorough when completing the checklist to ensure you haven't missed any critical steps.
* Double-check your answers to guarantee accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any field or option, refer back to the project or task requirements for guidance.
