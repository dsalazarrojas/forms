# Babysitter Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from babysitters about the household, child care needs, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and make sure to understand the questions and information required.
2. Fill out the form honestly and accurately, providing all necessary information.
3. Make sure to sign the acknowledgment section at the end of the form, indicating that you have read and understand the instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Primary Parent Phone Number** (`primary_parent_contact`, text, required): This is the primary parent's phone number, where the babysitter can reach out to in case of an emergency.
* **Secondary Parent Phone Number** (`secondary_parent_contact`, text, optional): If there is a secondary parent or guardian, please enter their phone number here.
* **Trusted Neighbor Name and Number** (`emergency_neighbor_contact`, text, optional): If there is a trusted neighbor who can be reached in case of an emergency, please enter their name and phone number here.
* **Pediatrician Name and Phone** (`pediatrician_info`, text, required): Enter the name and phone number of the child's pediatrician.
* **-- Child Specific Needs --** (`child_needs_header`, note, required): This section is for listing any specific needs of the child, such as dietary restrictions or medical conditions.
* **Food and Environmental Allergies** (`allergy_information`, text, required): List any known allergies or food allergies the child has.
* **Bedtime Routine and Times** (`bedtime_routine`, text, required): Enter the child's bedtime routine, including the times for bath, reading, and lights out.
* **Permitted Meals and Snacks** (`allowed_meals_snacks`, text, required): List the permitted meals and snacks for the child.
* **Medication Dosage and Timing** (`medication_instructions`, text, optional): If the child takes medication, please list the dosage and timing here.
* **-- Household Rules and Boundaries --** (`rules_header`, note, required): This section is for listing any household rules and boundaries that the babysitter needs to be aware of.
* **Screen Time and Device Limits** (`screen_time_limits`, text, required): Enter the limits for screen time and device use for the child.
* **Disciplinary Approach and Guidelines** (`disciplinary_guidelines`, text, required): Describe the disciplinary approach and guidelines for handling issues or timeouts.
* **Household Safety and Access Notes** (`household_safety_notes`, text, optional): If there are any specific safety concerns or access notes that the babysitter needs to be aware of, please list them here.
* **I have read and understand these instructions** (`checklist_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Please select the option indicating that you have read and understand the instructions.
