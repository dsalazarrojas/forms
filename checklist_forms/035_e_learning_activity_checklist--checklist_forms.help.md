# e-learning-activity-checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to monitor student progress and submission status in educational activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the activities that the student has completed.
2. Enter the submission date of the activity.
3. Record student progress in the note field.
4. Enter the student's name.
5. Select the submission status of the activity (Completed, Incomplete, or Missing).
6. Enter the due date of the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Activity Checklist** (`activity_checklist`, select_multiple, Required): Select all the activities that the student has completed. This field allows you to choose multiple activities from the list.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date when the student submitted the activity.
* **Student Progress** (`student_progress`, note, Optional): Record student progress in this field. This can include any notes or comments about the student's progress.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, Optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, Required): Select the submission status of the activity (Completed, Incomplete, or Missing).
* **Due Date** (`submission_due_date`, date, Optional): Enter the due date of the submission.
