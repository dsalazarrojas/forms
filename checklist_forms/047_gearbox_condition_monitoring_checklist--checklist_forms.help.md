# gearbox_condition_monitoring_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track and record the condition of a gearbox in a vehicle. It includes various fields to assess the gearbox's cleanliness, oil level, temperature, and other critical parameters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the condition of the gearbox using the "Gearbox Condition" field.
2. For the "Oil Level Check", choose the correct level from the provided options.
3. Record the temperature in Celsius in the "Temperature Check" field.
4. For each subsequent field, select the correct condition or status using the provided options.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Inspection Notes" field.
6. Fill in the gearbox model and serial number in the respective fields.
7. Record the vehicle model and serial number.
8. Enter the current mileage, last service mileage, and next service mileage.
9. Select the service interval from the provided options.
10. Fill out the gearbox health status, inspection status, and gearbox inspection comments.
11. Choose the gearbox action plan from the provided options.
12. Select the inspection result.
13. Enter any final remarks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gearbox Condition** (1, select_one): Select the current condition of the gearbox from the provided options, such as "Gearbox is clean" or "Gearbox is dirty".
* **Oil Level Check** (2, select_one): Choose the correct oil level from Level 1 to Level 5.
* **Temperature Check** (3, number): Record the temperature in Celsius in this field.
* **Oil Leak Check** (4, select_one): Select "No leak" or "Leaking" based on the gearbox's condition.
* **Bearing Condition** (5, select_one): Choose the condition of the bearings from "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Bearing Lubrication** (6, select_one): Select whether the bearings are lubricated or not.
* **Seal Condition** (7, select_one): Select the condition of the seals from "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Seal Leak Check** (8, select_one): Choose whether there's a leak or not.
* **Inspection Date** (9, date): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Technician** (10, text): Enter your name and signature.
* **Inspection Notes** (11, note): Add any additional notes about the inspection.
* **Gearbox Model** (12, text): Enter the gearbox model number.
* **Gearbox Serial** (13, text): Enter the gearbox serial number.
* **Vehicle Model** (14, text): Enter the vehicle model number.
* **Vehicle Serial** (15, text): Enter the vehicle serial number.
* **Mileage** (16, number): Record the current mileage.
* **Last Service Mileage** (17, number): Record the last service mileage.
* **Next Service Mileage** (18, number): Record the next service mileage.
* **Service Interval** (19, select_one): Select the service interval from 1 month to 6 months.
* **Gearbox Health Status** (20, select_one): Select the health status of the gearbox from "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Gearbox Inspection Status** (21, select_one): Select whether the gearbox has been inspected or not.
* **Gearbox Inspection Comments** (22, note): Add any comments about the inspection.
* **Gearbox Action Plan** (23, select_one): Choose the action plan for the gearbox from the provided options.
* **Gearbox Inspection Result** (24, select_one): Select whether the inspection passed or failed.
* **Gearbox Remarks** (25, note): Add any additional remarks about the inspection.
