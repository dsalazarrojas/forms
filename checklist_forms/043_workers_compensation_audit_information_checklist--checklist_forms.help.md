# workers_compensation_audit_information_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Workers' Compensation Audit Information Checklist** is a collection of fields that help auditors and administrators gather relevant information about an incident or claim involving a worker. This form is designed to ensure that all necessary details are collected and accurately recorded for auditing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the **Business Name**, which is the name of the company or business related to the incident or claim.
2. Provide the **Policy Year**, which is the year the workers' compensation policy was in effect at the time of the incident.
3. Enter the **Audit Details**, which is a brief description of the audit process or procedures that were followed.
4. Select the relevant **Supporting Documents** that are related to the incident or claim (e.g., medical records, witness statements).
5. Enter the **Date of Incident**, which is the date when the incident or claim occurred.
6. (Optional) Enter the **Time of Incident**, which is the time when the incident or claim occurred.
7. (Optional) Enter the **Location of Incident**, which is the place where the incident or claim occurred.
8. Enter the **Employee Name**, which is the name of the worker involved in the incident or claim.
9. (Optional) Enter the **Employee Job Title**, which is the job title of the worker involved in the incident or claim.
10. (Optional) Enter the **Employee ID Number**, which is the identification number of the worker involved in the incident or claim.
11. (Optional) Enter the **Employee Address**, which is the address of the worker involved in the incident or claim.
12. Enter the **Supervisor Name**, which is the name of the supervisor of the worker involved in the incident or claim.
13. (Optional) Enter the **Supervisor Address**, which is the address of the supervisor involved in the incident or claim.
14. Enter the **Insurance Provider**, which is the name of the insurance provider related to the workers' compensation policy.
15. (Optional) Enter the **Adjuster Name**, which is the name of the adjuster handling the claim.
16. (Optional) Enter the **Adjuster Address**, which is the address of the adjuster handling the claim.
17. Select the **Claim Status**, which is the current status of the claim (e.g., pending, open, closed).
18. (Optional) Enter the **Date of Closure**, which is the date when the claim was closed.
19. (Optional) Enter the **Reason for Closure**, which is a brief description of why the claim was closed.
20. (Optional) Enter the **Claim Amount**, which is the amount paid out for the claim.
21. (Optional) Enter the **Claim Number**, which is the unique identifier of the claim.
22. (Optional) Enter the **Claim Date**, which is the date when the claim was filed.
23. (Optional) Enter the **Employer Name**, which is the name of the employer related to the worker involved in the incident or claim.
24. (Optional) Enter the **Employer Address**, which is the address of the employer related to the worker involved in the incident or claim.
25. (Optional) Enter the **Employer Phone**, which is the phone number of the employer related to the worker involved in the incident or claim.
