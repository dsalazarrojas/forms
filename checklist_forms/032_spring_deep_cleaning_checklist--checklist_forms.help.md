# spring_deep_cleaning_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Spring Deep Cleaning Checklist is a form designed to help users track and plan their spring cleaning activities, including specifying the season, location, and frequency of cleaning tasks. This form will aid users in organizing and managing their cleaning schedule effectively, making it easier to keep their living or working space clean and organized.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the season for which you want to plan your cleaning activities.
2. Specify the location where the cleaning will take place.
3. List the tasks you need to complete during the spring cleaning season.
4. Add any additional notes for the cleaning activity.
5. Select the tasks you've already completed from the list provided.
6. Choose a date for refreshing or performing the cleaning activity.
7. Select the frequency at which you'd like to perform the cleaning activity (e.g., Spring, Every 3 months, Quarterly, Bi-Annual, Annual).
8. Input your preferred frequency of cleaning for the season.
9. Add any additional notes related to your preferred frequency.
10. Input the frequency for refreshing or performing the cleaning activity.
11. Add any additional notes related to your seasonal frequency.
12. Input any feedback or comments on your cleaning activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Season** (1): Specify the season for which you want to plan your cleaning activities. For example, if it's a spring season, input "Spring."
* **Location** (2): Enter the location where the cleaning will take place, such as your home, office, or specific room.
* **Task List** (3): List the tasks you need to complete during the spring cleaning season, such as cleaning the floors, dusting the furniture, etc.
* **Notes** (4): Add any additional notes for the cleaning activity, such as specific cleaning products or tools needed.
* **Completed Tasks** (6): Select the tasks you've already completed from the list provided. This will help you track your progress and stay organized.
* **Refresh Date** (7): Input the date for when you'd like to refresh or perform the cleaning activity. This will help you plan ahead and stay on schedule.
* **Select a cleaning frequency** (8): Choose how frequently you'd like to perform the cleaning activity, such as Spring, Every 3 months, Quarterly, Bi-Annual, Annual.
* **Seasonal Cleaning Frequency** (9): Input your preferred frequency of cleaning for the season.
* **Seasonal Frequency Notes** (10): Add any additional notes related to your preferred frequency.
* **Spring Cleaning Frequency Notes** (11): Add any additional notes related to your spring cleaning frequency.
* **Refresh Frequency** (12): Input the frequency for refreshing or performing the cleaning activity.
* **Seasonal Frequency** (13): Input your preferred frequency of cleaning for the season.
* **Feedback** (14): Provide any feedback or comments on your cleaning activity.
* **Seasonal Frequency Notes** (15): Add any additional notes related to your seasonal frequency.
* **Seasonal Refresh Notes** (16): Add any additional notes related to your seasonal refresh.
* **Seasonal Frequency Refresh Notes** (17): Add any additional notes related to your seasonal frequency refresh.
* **Seasonal Frequency 2** (18): Input your preferred frequency of cleaning for the season.
