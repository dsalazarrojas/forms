# Nut Tree Compliance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nut Tree Compliance Checklist form is used to verify the compliance status of nut trees with respect to various regulations and specifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question carefully, providing accurate information about the nut tree.
2. For the "species" field, select the applicable species from the dropdown list.
3. If a species is not listed, you may enter the specific species name in the "species_name" field.
4. Enter the date of the inspection in the "inspection_date" field.
5. Enter the time of the inspection in the "inspection_time" field.
6. For the "species_specific_compliance_check" field, select all applicable compliance checks.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes about the nut tree in the "notes" field.
8. If applicable, provide contact information for the person responsible for the nut tree, including their email address in the "contact_email" field and phone number in the "contact_phone_number" field.
9. If applicable, provide the name of the person assigned to the nut tree, their phone number in the "assignee_phone" field, and their email address in the "assignee_email" field.
10. Enter the designation or title of the person assigned to the nut tree in the "assignee_designation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **species**: Select the species of the nut tree.
* **species_name**: Enter the specific name of the tree species if it is not listed in the dropdown list.
* **inspection_date**: Enter the date the tree was inspected.
* **inspection_time**: Enter the time the tree was inspected.
* **species_specific_compliance_check**: Select all applicable compliance checks specific to the species.
* **orchard_management**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the management of the nut tree.
* **notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the nut tree.
* **contact_email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **contact_phone_number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **assignee_name**: Enter the name of the person assigned to the nut tree.
* **assignee_phone**: Enter the phone number of the person assigned to the nut tree.
* **assignee_email**: Enter the email address of the person assigned to the nut tree.
* **assignee_designation**: Enter the designation or title of the person assigned to the nut tree.
