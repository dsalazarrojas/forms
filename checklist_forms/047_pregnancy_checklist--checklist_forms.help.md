# Pregnancy Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a pregnant woman's pregnancy details. It includes fields for tracking the first day of the last menstrual cycle, pregnancy test date, gestation weeks, due date, baby's sex, number of children, and other essential information. This data will help healthcare professionals monitor and manage pregnancy-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the relevant pages**: Each page is designed to collect specific information about the pregnancy.
   - Page 1: First day of last menstrual cycle
   - Page 2: Pregnancy test date
   - Page 3: Gestation weeks
   - Page 4: Due date
   - Page 5: Baby's sex
   - Page 6: Number of children
   - Page 7: First name
   - Page 8: Last name
   - Page 9: Phone number
   - Page 10: Email
   - Page 11: Birth date (not applicable to every pregnancy)
   - Page 12: Baby weight (not applicable to every pregnancy)
   - Page 13: Baby height (not applicable to every pregnancy)
   - Page 14: Baby length (not applicable to every pregnancy)
   - Page 15: Birthplace
   - Page 16: Delivery date (not applicable to every pregnancy)
   - Page 17: Doctor's name (not applicable to every pregnancy)
   - Page 18: Blood type (not applicable to every pregnancy)
   - Page 19: Baby's name
   - Page 20: Birth weight
   - Page 21: Birth length
   - Page 22: Birth time
   - Page 23: Baby's gender

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pregnancy test date** (`pregnancy_test_date`, date, required): Enter the date the pregnancy was confirmed through a test.
* **Due date** (`due_date`, date, required): Enter the expected due date of the baby.
* **Gestation weeks** (`gestation_weeks`, number, required): Enter the gestational weeks of the pregnancy.
* **Baby's sex** (`babysex`, select_one, required): Select the sex of the baby (Boy, Girl, Other).
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the mother.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the mother.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the mother.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email of the mother.
* **Baby's name** (`baby_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the baby.
* **Birthplace** (`birthplace`, select_one, required): Select the place of birth of the baby (Hospital, Home, Clinic, Other).
* **Doctor's name** (`doctor_name`, text, not applicable to every pregnancy): Enter the name of the doctor attending the birth (if applicable).
* **Blood type** (`blood_type`, select_multiple, not applicable to every pregnancy): Select the blood type of the mother (A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+, O-).
* **Number of children** (`number_of_children`, number, optional): Enter the number of children the mother has had previously.
* **Baby's weight** (`baby_weight`, number, not applicable to every pregnancy): Enter the weight of the baby at birth (if applicable).
* **Baby's height** (`baby_height`, number, not applicable to every pregnancy): Enter the height of the baby at birth (if applicable).
* **Baby's length** (`baby_length`, number, not applicable to every pregnancy): Enter the length of the baby at birth (if applicable).
* **Delivery date** (`delivery_date`, date, not applicable to every pregnancy): Enter the date of delivery (if applicable).
* **Birth weight** (`birth_weight`, number, not applicable to every pregnancy): Enter the birth weight of the baby (if applicable).
* **Birth length** (`birth_length`, number, not applicable to every pregnancy): Enter the birth length of the baby (if applicable).
* **Birth time** (`birth_time`, time, not applicable to every pregnancy): Enter the time of birth (if applicable).
* **Baby's gender** (`baby_gender`, select_one, not applicable to every pregnancy): Select the sex of the baby (Male, Female, Other).
* **Blood pressure** (`blood_pressure`, number, not applicable to every pregnancy): Enter the blood pressure of the mother (if applicable).
* **Body temperature** (`body_temperature`, number, not applicable to every pregnancy): Enter the body temperature of the mother (if applicable).
