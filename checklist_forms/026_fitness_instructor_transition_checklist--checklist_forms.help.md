# Fitness Instructor Transition Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Instructor Transition Checklist is a form designed to aid in the process of transferring clients between fitness instructors. This form collects essential details to ensure a smooth transition and minimize disruptions to the client's fitness journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the required and optional fields.
2. For each field, provide the necessary information to ensure a successful client transfer.
3. Be thorough and accurate in your response, as this information will be used to facilitate the transition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Transfer Details** (`client_transfer_details`, text, required/optional): This field is used to capture any specific details about the client transfer process. Please provide a brief description of the reason for the transfer and any relevant circumstances.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, text, required/optional): These notes will help the incoming instructor understand the client's specific needs and requirements.
* **Schedule Coverage** (`schedule_coverage`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more of the available options to indicate the coverage schedule for the client.
* **Open Items** (`open_items`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the most relevant option to indicate any outstanding items or concerns that need to be addressed during the transfer process.
* **Client Contact Info** (`client_contact_info`, text, required/optional): Provide the client's contact information, including their email and phone number.
* **Studio Info** (`studio_info`, select_one, required/optional): Select the relevant studio information to ensure the client is correctly transitioned to the new instructor.
* **Staff Transfer Info** (`staff_transfer_info`, text, required/optional): Provide any relevant details about the staff transfer process, including any specific instructions or requirements.
* **Staff Email** (`staff_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the staff member handling the transfer process.
* **Gym Info** (`gym_info`, select_one, required/optional): Select the relevant gym information to ensure the client is correctly transitioned to the new instructor.
* **Staff Phone** (`staff_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the staff member handling the transfer process.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
* This form is designed to be user-friendly, so feel free to reach out if you need assistance with completing it.
