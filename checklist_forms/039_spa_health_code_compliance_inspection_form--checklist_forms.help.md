# Spa Health Code Compliance Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to document and record health code compliance inspections of spas, ensuring that all relevant information is captured and reported accurately. Please complete this form to provide detailed information about the facility, including its address, contact details, and compliance status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure that you have all the necessary information and documents required to complete this form before proceeding.
2. Complete each section of the form accurately and thoroughly, providing all relevant details.
3. Review and verify the information provided before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Checklist** (`spa_health_code_compliance_inspection_form_checklist`, text, required): This field is for listing any health code violations found during the inspection. Please provide a detailed description of each violation, including its location and any corrective actions taken.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the spa facility being inspected.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the spa facility being inspected.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required): Enter the date the inspection was conducted.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
* **Facility License Numbers** (`facility_license_numbers`, text, required): Enter the license numbers of the spa facility.
* **Violations Found** (`violations_found`, text, required): This field is for listing any health code violations found during the inspection. Please provide a detailed description of each violation, including its location and any corrective actions taken.
* **Corrective Action Taken** (`corrective_action_taken`, text, required): This field is for documenting any corrective action taken or planned to address the health code violations identified during the inspection.
* **Next Inspection Date** (`next_inspection_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection.
* **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`, note, required): Sign and date the inspection report, confirming that the information provided is accurate.
* **Facility Manager Signature** (`facility_manager_signature`, note, required): Sign and date the facility manager's acknowledgment of the inspection report.
* **Inspector Name 2** (`inspector_name_2`, text): Enter the name of the secondary inspector, if applicable.
* **Facility Manager Name 2** (`facility_manager_name_2`, text): Enter the name of the secondary facility manager, if applicable.
* **Inspector Notes** (`inspector_notes`, text): This field is for adding any additional comments or notes from the inspector.
* **Facility Manager Notes** (`facility_manager_notes`, text): This field is for adding any additional comments or notes from the facility manager.
* **Facility Contact Info** (`facility_contact_info`, text): Enter the contact information for the spa facility, including phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Facility Email** (`facility_email`, email): Enter the email address of the spa facility.
* **Facility Phone** (`facility_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the spa facility.
* **Facility Fax** (`facility_fax`, text): Enter the fax number of the spa facility.
* **Alternate Phone** (`facility_alternate_phone`, text): Enter an alternate phone number of the spa facility.
* **Facility Website** (`facility_website`, text): Enter the website of the spa facility.
* **Facility Hours** (`facility_hours`, text): Enter the operating hours of the spa facility.
* **Facility Services** (`facility_services`, text): List the services offered by the spa facility.
* **Facility Services Notes** (`facility_services_notes`, text): Add any additional comments or notes about the services offered by the spa facility.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text): This field is for adding any additional comments or notes about the facility.

Please ensure that you complete each field accurately and thoroughly, and do not duplicate any effort by completing the form multiple times.
