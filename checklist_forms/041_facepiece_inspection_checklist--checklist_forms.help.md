# Facepiece Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for facepiece inspection checklists, used to ensure that facepieces are properly inspected and maintained. It helps users identify any corrective actions or follow-ups needed for facepieces.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by navigating through the pages of the form, which are represented by the numbers at the top of each section.
2. For each page, read the label and hint provided for each field, which will guide you through what information is expected.
3. Enter the required information in the corresponding fields, making sure to select the correct options from the dropdown lists where applicable.
4. Use the "Notes" field to provide any additional comments or information that may be relevant to the inspection.
5. Once you have completed all the pages, review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Initial Check** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter any initial observations or findings from the inspection.
* **Corrective Actions** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any corrective actions that need to be taken for the facepiece.
* **Follow-ups** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose any follow-up actions needed for the facepiece.
* **Equipment Check 1** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Record the condition or status of the first equipment check.
* **Equipment Check 2** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Record the condition or status of the second equipment check.
* **Equipment Check 3** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Record the condition or status of the third equipment check.
* **Notes** (`7`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Equipment Check 4** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Record the condition or status of the fourth equipment check.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct options from the dropdown lists for corrective actions and follow-ups.
- Be detailed and accurate when entering information in the text fields.
- Use the "Notes" field to provide any additional context or information that may be useful for future reference.
