# Vehicle Cleaning Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the vehicle's cleaning status after cleaning tasks have been completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer every question that applies to your situation.
2. Ensure that you fill out all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*).
3. Use the options provided in select fields to select the best answer for your situation.
4. Enter comments and observations in the text fields.
5. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form and save your data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Cleaning Checklist Form 1 1** (vehicle_cleaning_checklist_form_1_1, text, optional): This field is a text field for any notes or comments about the vehicle's condition. Please enter any relevant information here.
* **Vehicle Cleaning Checklist Form 1 2** (vehicle_cleaning_checklist_form_1_2, text, optional): This field is also a text field for any additional comments or observations.
* **Vehicle Cleaning Checklist Form 1 3** (vehicle_cleaning_checklist_form_1_3, number, optional): This field asks for the number of days the vehicle was cleaned. Please enter the exact number of days.
* **Vehicle Cleaning Checklist Form 1 4** (vehicle_cleaning_checklist_form_1_4, number, optional): This field also asks for the number of hours the vehicle was cleaned. Enter the exact number of hours.
* **Vehicle Cleaning Checklist Form 1 5** (vehicle_cleaning_checklist_form_1_5, select_one, optional): This field provides the option to select the type of cleaning performed, using the options: 1, 2, 3.
* **Vehicle Cleaning Checklist Form 1 6** (vehicle_cleaning_checklist_form_1_6, select_multiple, optional): This field provides the option to select multiple areas of the vehicle that were cleaned, using the options: 1, 2, 3.
* **Vehicle Cleaning Checklist Form 1 7** (vehicle_cleaning_checklist_form_1_7, time, optional): This field is for entering the time the vehicle was cleaned. Please use 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Vehicle Cleaning Checklist Form 1 8** (vehicle_cleaning_checklist_form_1_8, date, optional): This field is for entering the date the vehicle was cleaned.
* **Vehicle Cleaning Checklist Form 1 9** (vehicle_cleaning_checklist_form_1_9, note, optional): This field is a text field for any additional comments or observations.
* **Vehicle Cleaning Checklist Form 1 10** (vehicle_cleaning_checklist_form_1_10, email, optional): This field is for entering an email address, if necessary.
* ... (remaining fields are similar to 1-9)

Note: The fields are listed in the sequence they appear in the form.
