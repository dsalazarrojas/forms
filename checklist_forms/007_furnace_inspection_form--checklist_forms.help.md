# Furnace Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Furnace Inspection Form is a tool used to record the results of a furnace inspection, gathering data on the condition and performance of a residential or commercial furnace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct inspection type: Choose the type of inspection you are performing, either "Residential Furnace Inspection" or "Commercial Furnace Inspection."
2. Provide detailed notes: Enter any additional comments or observations about the inspection in the "Furnace Inspection Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Residential Furnace Inspection (`residential_furnace_inspection_page`)**: Select the type of inspection you are performing. This field is required to ensure that the correct inspection type is recorded.
  - Options: Residential or Commercial
* **Commercial Furnace Inspection (`commercial_furnace_inspection_page`)**: Enter any comments or details about the commercial furnace inspection. This field is not required, but providing information here can be helpful for reference.
* **Furnace Inspection Notes (`furnace_inspection_notes`)**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the inspection. This field is required and should provide detailed information about the furnace's condition and performance.
  - Hint: Additional comments about the inspection can be helpful for reference.
