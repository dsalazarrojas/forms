# Nursing School Admission Checklist Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is designed to collect information required for admission into a nursing school program. It includes fields for personal details, education history, medical certifications, work experience, skills, and other relevant credentials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by reviewing the form and understanding the purpose of each field.
2.  Fill in your personal details, including your address, city, state, zip, and phone number.
3.  Provide information about your education history, including your education level, institution, degree, and field of study.
4.  Check off any medical certifications you have obtained.
5.  Confirm whether you have a medical license and indicate whether it's up to date.
6.  Choose relevant skills you have acquired, such as communication, leadership, and problem-solving.
7.  Enter any medical license number and expiration date if applicable.
8.  Review and confirm your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Application** (`new_application`, text, optional): This field is for new applicants to introduce themselves and provide a brief description of their application.
* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, note, optional): This field is for providing personal details such as address, city, state, and zip.
* **Education History** (`education_history`, note, optional): This field is for providing information about your education history, including any relevant details about your education institution and degree.
* **Educational History** (`education_history_2`, note, optional): This field is similar to the previous one, providing more details about your education history.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, note, optional): This field is for providing information about your work experience, including any relevant details about your job title, company, and duration.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, true): This field asks you to choose from a list of relevant skills acquired, such as communication, leadership, and problem-solving.
* **Medical Certifications** (`medical_certifications`, text, optional): This field is for listing any medical certifications you have obtained, such as CPR certification.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, select_one, true): This field asks whether you have a medical license and whether it's up to date.
* **Medical License Expiration** (`medical_license_expiration`, date, true): This field is for entering the expiration date of your medical license, if applicable.
* **Medical Degree** (`medical_degree`, select_multiple, true): This field asks whether you have a medical degree and which fields of study it applies to.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, text, true): This field is for entering your medical license number if you have a license.
* **Medical Board** (`medical_board`, text, true): This field is for entering the name of your medical board or certification authority, if applicable.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): This field is for entering your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): This field is for entering your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): This field is for entering your state.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): This field is for entering your zip code.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is for entering your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is for entering your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, true): This field is for entering your date of birth.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, true): This field asks you to choose your education level, such as high or low.
* **Education Institution** (`education_institution`, text, true): This field is for entering the name of your education institution.
* **Education Degree** (`education_degree`, select_multiple, true): This field asks whether you have an education degree and which fields of study it applies to.
* **Education Field** (`education_field`, select_multiple, true): This field asks whether you have studied in specific fields, such as nursing or business.
* **Education Year** (`education_year`, select_multiple, true): This field asks whether you graduated in a specific year, such as 2019 or 2022.

Tips:

* Review the form carefully before filling it out to ensure you answer all required fields.
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can always ask for clarification or assistance.
* Double-check your answers for errors before submitting the form.
* If you have any medical certifications or degrees, make sure to include them in the relevant fields.
