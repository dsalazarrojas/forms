# Crash Cart Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess the condition and maintenance status of a crash cart. It guides you through a series of questions to ensure that the cart is properly maintained, inspected, and updated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields with the relevant information about the crash cart. For each field, answer as accurately as possible based on the current condition of the cart.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Crash Cart Battery Age**: The age of the battery in the crash cart. This is important for tracking the lifespan of the battery and planning for replacement.
* **Crash Cart Type**: The type of battery used in the crash cart. This helps identify the specific model and manufacturer for maintenance or replacement purposes.
* **Crash Cart Age**: The age of the crash cart itself, not including the battery. This is crucial for tracking the total lifespan of the cart.
* **Last Inspection Date**: The date of the last inspection of the cart. This helps ensure that maintenance is up-to-date and records are kept track of.
* **Next Scheduled Inspection**: The date of the next scheduled inspection for the cart. This ensures that maintenance is planned and performed on a regular basis.
* **Crash Cart Location**: The current location of the cart, which is useful for tracking and locating carts in different areas.
* **Crash Cart Accessories**: Check if the cart has any functional or non-functional accessories. This helps identify if any parts need to be replaced or maintained.
* **Crash Cart Maintenance Records**: Check if the maintenance records are up-to-date for the cart. This ensures that all necessary records are kept for future reference.
* **Defibrillator Functionality**: Check if the defibrillator functionality is working properly. This is critical for emergency response situations.
* **Crash Cart Battery Capacity**: The capacity of the battery in the crash cart, which is important for assessing its lifespan and performance.
* **Crash Cart Manual**: Additional information or notes about the cart, which can include instructions or guidelines for use and maintenance.
* **Crash Cart Training**: Check if the crash cart user has completed training on the device. This ensures that users are familiar with its operation and safety procedures.
* **Crash Carts in Location**: A count of how many crash carts are currently located in the area. This helps in inventory management and resource allocation.
* **Last Battery Change**: The date of the last battery change, which is essential for tracking battery replacement and maintenance.
* **Next Battery Change**: The date of the next scheduled battery change, which helps plan for future replacements.
* **Crash Cart Serial Number**: The serial number of the crash cart, which can be useful for identification and tracking purposes.
* **Crash Cart Model Number**: The model number of the crash cart, which helps identify the specific make and model for maintenance and replacement purposes.
* **Page 18**: This field is currently empty and not used in the form.
