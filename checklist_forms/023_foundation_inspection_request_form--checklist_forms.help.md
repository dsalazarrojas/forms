# Foundation Inspection Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foundation Inspection Request Form is designed to document the findings of an inspection of a foundation, providing a standardized way to gather and report information about the condition of a foundation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each section of the form carefully and accurately, providing detailed information as required.
2. Review the options for the "foundation inspection report" field and select the most accurate description of the report.
3. Enter comments, if any, about the inspection or findings.
4. Enter any tools or equipment assigned for the inspection.
5. Identify the person assigning the inspection.
6. Enter the date and time of the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Concrete Inspection**: This field is for noting any defects or issues found during the concrete inspection.
* **Concrete Block Inspection**: This field is for noting any defects or issues found during the concrete block inspection.
* **Foundation Walls**: This field is for noting any defects or issues found during the foundation wall inspection.
* **Footing Inspection**: This field is for noting any defects or issues found during the footing inspection.
* **Waterproofing**: This field is for noting any issues with waterproofing, if applicable.
* **Drainage**: This field is for noting any issues with drainage, if applicable.
* **Foundation Inspection Report**: This field allows you to select the status of the inspection report, either "foundation inspection complete" or "incomplete foundation inspection report".
* **Comments**: This field is for entering any additional comments or notes about the inspection or findings.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is for entering any tools or equipment assigned for the inspection.
* **Assigned By**: This field is for identifying the person assigning the inspection.
* **Submitted By**: This field is for identifying the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted At**: This field is for entering the date and time of submission.
* **Inspection Date**: This field is for entering the date of the inspection.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when completing each field.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all necessary information is included.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the "Foundation Inspection Request Form" purpose and use your best judgment.

Note: The above guide is based on the assumption that the form is used in a construction or real estate context. If the form is used in a different context, the guide may need to be adjusted accordingly.
