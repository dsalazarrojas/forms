# HIPAA Special Enrollment Compliance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for HIPAA special enrollment compliance checks for employees. The form gathers information about employees' enrollment status, department, job classification, date of hire, department head, manager, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the introductory note on page 1 carefully before proceeding.
2. Complete each section accurately and thoroughly.
3. Ensure that all fields are filled out completely and correctly.
4. Submit the completed form to the relevant authorities for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Section 1
* **Section 1** (`section_1`, `note`, not required): This page provides a brief introduction and note about the purpose and use of this form.

### Section 2
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, not required): This field is for entering the name of the employee being checked.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, not required): This field is for entering the unique ID of the employee.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, not required): Please select the department where the employee works.
* **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, `select_multiple`, not required): Check all that apply to classify the job type (Admin, Non-Admin, Contractor, Other).

### Section 3
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of birth of the employee.
* **Date Hire** (`date_hire`, `date`, not required): This field is for entering the date the employee was hired.
* **Date of Hire** (`date_of_hire`, `date`, not required): This field is likely an alias for `date_hire`. Please enter the date the employee was hired.
* **Department Head** (`department_head`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the department head or supervisor.
* **Manager** (`manager`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the employee's manager.

### Section 4
* **Date Last Review** (`date_last_review`, `date`, not required): This field is for entering the date of the last review or evaluation.
* **Date Next Review** (`date_next_review`, `date`, not required): This field is for entering the date of the next review or evaluation.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, not required): Enter the job title or position of the employee.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the employee's manager.

### Section 5
* **Employee Type** (`employee_type`, `select_one`, not required): Please select whether the employee is benefits eligible or not.
* **Special Enrollment Status** (`special_enrollment_status`, `select_one`, not required): Check the status as active, inactive, or pending.
* **Special Enrollment Reason** (`special_enrollment_reason`, `select_multiple`, not required): Check all that apply to classify the reason for special enrollment (New Hire, Return from Leave, Terminated, Other).
* **Special Enrollment Termination Reason** (`special_enrollment_termination_reason`, `text`, not required): Enter the reason for termination.
* **Date Termination** (`date_termination`, `date`, not required): This field is for entering the date of termination.
* **Date Reenrollment** (`date_reelection`, `date`, not required): This field is for entering the date of reenrollment.
* **Special Enrollment Reinstatement Reason** (`special_enrollment_reinstatement_reason`, `text`, not required): Enter the reason for reinstatement.

### Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Review the form carefully before submission.
- If you are unsure about any information, please consult with relevant authorities or HR personnel.
- If the form is incomplete or contains errors, do not submit it.
