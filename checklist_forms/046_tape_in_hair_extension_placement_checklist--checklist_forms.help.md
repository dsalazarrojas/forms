# tape_in_hair_extension_placement_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tape In Hair Extension Placement Checklist is a tool used by professionals in the hair industry to ensure accurate and consistent placement of tape-in hair extensions. This form helps in identifying the type of hair, length, and other relevant details of the client's hair, enabling a more precise and personalized hair extension placement process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the client's information in the `client_info` field.
2.  Select the type of hair you are working with by choosing one of the options in the `hair_type` field.
3.  Indicate the length of the hair by selecting one or more options in the `hair_length` field.
4.  If you're using a numerical value, enter the hair thickness in the `hair_thickness` field.
5.  Choose the type of tape-in hair extension method you'll be using by selecting one of the options in the `tape_in_type` field.
6.  Determine if the hair section is sectioned or not by selecting one of the options in the `sectioned` field.
7.  Identify the hair section that will be receiving the hair extension by selecting one or more options in the `hair_section` field.
8.  If necessary, add additional comments or notes in the `comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, required: false): Enter the client's name and contact information.
* **Hair Type** (`hair_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of hair you're working with. Options include Micro Loop, Tape in, and Hair Bond.
* **Hair Length** (`hair_length`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more lengths of the client's hair.
* **Hair Thickness** (`hair_thickness`, number, required: false): Enter the thickness of the client's hair using a numerical value.
* **Tape In Type** (`tape_in_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of tape-in hair extension method you'll be using. Options include Hand-tied, Root, Micro, and Stick-Tie.
* **Sectioned** (`sectioned`, select_one, required: false): Determine if the hair section is sectioned or not. Select 'True' for sectioned or 'False' for non-sectioned.
* **Hair Section** (`hair_section`, select_multiple, required: false): Identify the hair section that will be receiving the hair extension by selecting one or more options.
* **Hair Length Section** (`hair_length_section`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the hair length section.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the client's hair or the hair extension placement process.
