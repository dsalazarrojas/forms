# Factory Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the inspection of factory equipment and environment. It will help you identify potential issues and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and make sure to answer all the questions truthfully.
2. Select all applicable options for the "Equipment and Environment" field.
3. Enter any notes or observations in the "Inspection Details" and "Location Details" fields.
4. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
5. Choose "Yes" or "No" in the "Location" field as applicable.
6. Use the "Other Comments" field for any additional notes or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Equipment and Environment** (`factory_inspection_checklist_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all the equipment and environmental conditions that apply. This field has multiple options to select from.
  * 'Yes': The equipment and environment meet the standards.
  * 'No': The equipment and environment do not meet the standards.
- **Inspection Details** (`factory_inspection_checklist_2`, text, required: false): Enter any notable points or observations about the inspection.
- **Contact Information** (`factory_inspection_checklist_3`, text, required: false): Provide your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
- **Location** (`factory_inspection_checklist_4`, select_one, required: false): Check "Yes" if you are inspecting the location, "No" if not.
- **Location Details** (`factory_inspection_checklist_5`, text, required: false): Enter any details about the location being inspected.
- **Other Comments** (`factory_inspection_checklist_6`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or concerns you have about the inspection.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all fields truthfully and thoroughly.
* Review your answers before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language in your answers.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or seek additional guidance.
