# Shift Handover Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shift Handover Checklist is a simple, user-friendly form designed to ensure a smooth transition between shifts by gathering essential information from the departing employee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what each field represents.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
3. Select the relevant actions that need to be taken or completed during the next shift.
4. Enter the start and end times of the shift.
5. Provide any important comments or notes for the next shift employee.
6. Enter any assigned tools or equipment that the next shift employee will need.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Checklist (1)
* **Checklist** (`checklist`, text, required/optional): A list of essential items to be completed or checked during the next shift.

### Actions (2)
* **Actions** (`actions`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the actions that need to be taken or completed during the next shift.

### Next Shift Employee (3)
* **Next Shift Employee** (`next_shift_employee`, select_one, optional): Identify the employee who will be taking over the next shift.

### Start Time (4)
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the expected start time of the next shift.

### End Time (5)
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the expected end time of the next shift.

### Important Actions (6)
* **Important Actions** (`important_actions`, select_multiple, optional): Select any critical actions that need to be taken or completed during the next shift.

### Comments (7)
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or notes for the next shift employee.

### Assigned Tool (8)
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter any assigned tools or equipment that the next shift employee will need to use.

# Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Review the form carefully to understand what each field represents.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
- Provide clear and concise information to ensure a smooth transition for the next shift employee.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities or supervisors for clarification.
