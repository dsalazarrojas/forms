# Fire Safety Training Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Training Checklist form is designed to record and track the progress of fire safety training sessions. It helps facility managers and safety officers monitor the implementation of training across an organization or facility.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the status of the session by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' for each field.
2. For each field, select the corresponding option that accurately reflects the session's details.
3. Enter the location and start and end times of the session.
4. Choose whether a training officer was present during the session.
5. Indicate whether participants were present during the session.
6. Record the date the session was completed.
7. Provide any additional comments about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Info** (`session_info`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the session was conducted.
- **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether participants were present during the session.
- **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the exact location where the fire safety training session took place.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the fire safety training session was completed.
- **Topic Coverage** (`topic_coverage`, text, optional): Briefly describe the topic or subject covered during the session.
- **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the fire safety training session.
- **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the fire safety training session.
- **Training Officer** (`training_officer`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether a training officer was present during the session.
- **Participant** (`participant`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether participants were present during the session.
- **Completed Date** (`completed_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the session was marked as completed.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or details about the session.

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately fill out all required fields.
- Double-check the options selected for each field to ensure they reflect the actual session details.
- Use this form to track and monitor fire safety training sessions effectively.
