# Staff Duty Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Staff Duty Checklist is a form designed to help staff members track their work hours, assignments, or other relevant duties. This form allows management to monitor and coordinate staff's availability, making it easier to allocate tasks and resources efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

### Overview
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary permissions to access and complete the form.
2. Click on the form in your system to access it.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

#### Step 1: Select your answer to "duty_shift"
* **duty_shift** (`duty_shift`, number, required/optional): Indicate your duty shift. If you have no duty shift, select "0" or leave blank.

#### Step 2: Select your answer to "staff_member"
* **staff_member** (`staff_member`, select_one, required/optional): If you have a duty, select "Yes" to indicate you have a duty assigned. If you don't have a duty, select "No".

#### Step 3: Select your answer to "assigned_by"
* **assigned_by** (`assigned_by`, select_one, required/optional): If your duty was assigned by someone, select "Yes" to indicate this. If not, select "No".

#### Step 4: Select your answer to "assigned_to"
* **assigned_to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required/optional): If you've assigned a duty to someone, select "Yes" to indicate this. If not, select "No".

#### Step 5: Enter your "start_time" and "end_time"
* **start_time** and **end_time** (time, required/optional): If you have a fixed start and end time for your duty, enter it here. If not, leave these fields blank.

#### Step 6: Add any relevant notes
* **notes** (note, required/optional): If you have any comments, reminders, or other information related to your duty, add it to the notes field.

## Tips

* Ensure the information you provide is accurate to avoid errors in staff duty tracking.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or IT department for assistance.
* This form is a mandatory part of staff duty tracking, so please ensure you complete it in a timely manner to allow for efficient coordination of work hours and assignments.
