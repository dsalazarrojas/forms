# Transit Temperature Control Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transit Temperature Control Checklist is a form used to collect and track information about temperature control during the transit of goods or units. Its primary purpose is to ensure that checks and inspections are performed correctly, corrective actions are taken when necessary, and essential details about the unit are recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the checks that were performed during in-transit (in-transit_checks).
2. Record any document inspections that were conducted (document_inspections).
3. Indicate whether corrective action was taken (corrective_action_taken).
4. Choose who inspected the unit (inspected_by).
5. Record the temperature reading from the unit or device (temperature_recorded).
6. Add any notes about the unit or situation (notes).
7. Select whether action was taken (action_taken).
8. Enter the date and time for the next check-in and check-out (next_check_in and next_check_out).
9. Indicate whether the unit was checked by the correct person (checked_by).
10. Indicate whether the unit was signed by the correct person (signed_by).
11. Indicate whether the unit was verified by the correct person (verified_by).
12. Enter the ID of the temperature device used (temperature_device_id).
13. Enter the model of the temperature device used (temperature_device_model).
14. Enter the type of temperature device used (temperature_device_type).
15. Enter the type of unit being transported (unit_type).
16. Enter the size of the unit (unit_size).
17. Enter the weight of the unit (unit_weight).
18. Enter a description of the unit's content (unit_content).
19. Enter the name of the person or organization responsible for shipping the unit (unit_shipper).
20. Enter the name of the person or organization receiving the unit (unit_receiver).
21. Enter the acceptable temperature range for the unit (temperature_range).
22. Enter the maximum storage duration for the unit (max_storage_duration).
23. Enter the minimum storage temperature for the unit (min_storage_temperature).
24. Enter the maximum storage temperature for the unit (max_storage_temp).
