# disaster_management_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "disaster_management_checklist_form" is a comprehensive form designed to assess a company's readiness and preparedness in case of disaster scenarios.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each category and understand the questions being asked.
2. For each question, select the most accurate option that applies to your situation.
3. For categories like 'Check equipment', 'Check supplies', 'Check tools', 'Check vehicles', and 'Check medical', you can select multiple options.
4. In categories like 'Check water source' and 'Check power source', select the appropriate date or time that best reflects your current status.
5. In the 'Note' field, provide any additional comments or notes related to your answer.
6. In the 'Contact phone', 'Contact email', and 'Check address' fields, provide your contact information as required.
7. Finally, review your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your company's disaster management status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check disaster plan** (`check_disaster_plan`, text, optional): This field is used to check if the company has a disaster management plan in place.
* **Check equipment** (`check_equipment`, select_one, optional): This field is used to check if the company has the necessary equipment to manage and respond to disasters.
* **Check supplies** (`check_supplies`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to check if the company has the necessary supplies to manage and respond to disasters.
* **Check water source** (`check_water_source`, date, optional): This field is used to check if the company has a reliable water source in case of disasters.
* **Check power source** (`check_power_source`, time, optional): This field is used to check if the company has a reliable power source in case of disasters.
* **Note** (`check_note`, note, optional): This field is used to provide any additional comments or notes related to your answer.
* **Contact phone** (`check_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to provide your contact phone number.
* **Contact email** (`check_email`, email, optional): This field is used to provide your contact email address.
* **Check address** (`check_address`, select_one, optional): This field is used to check if you have a contact address or not.
* **Check vehicles** (`check_vehicles`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to check if you have the necessary vehicles to manage and respond to disasters.
* **Check tools** (`check_tools`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to check if you have the necessary tools to manage and respond to disasters.
* **Check medical** (`check_medical`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to check if you have the necessary medical facilities to manage and respond to disasters.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer accurately and honestly to ensure the effectiveness of the assessment.
* If you are unsure about any category, select the option that best reflects your current status.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
