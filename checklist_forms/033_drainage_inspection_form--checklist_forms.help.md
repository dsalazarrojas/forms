# Drainage Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information during a drainage inspection, which is a crucial process for identifying and addressing potential issues with drainage systems. The form helps inspectors to systematically record observations, notes, and results to ensure consistency and accuracy in the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the checklist section to ensure that all required items are checked or completed.
2.  Enter the date of the inspection.
3.  Indicate if the assigned inspector is "Yes" or "No".
4.  Choose the status of the inspection ("Active" or "Inactive").
5.  Describe the drainage system, including its location, type, and size.
6.  Indicate if pipe inspections have been performed ("Yes" or "No").
7.  Enter the inspection results, providing any relevant details.
8.  Add any additional notes from the inspector.
9.  Indicate if photos or videos were taken during the inspection ("Yes" or "No").
10. Attach any relevant documents or materials to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drainage Inspection Form Checklist**: This section contains a list of key items to consider during the inspection.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the inspection in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Assigned Inspector**: Indicate if the inspector is assigned to this inspection. Choose "Yes" if they are, "No" if they are not.
* **Inspection Status**: Select the status of the inspection. Choose "Active" if the system is currently active, "Inactive" if it is not.
* **Drainage System Status**: Select the status of the drainage system. Choose "Active" if it is currently active, "Inactive" if it is not.
* **System Description**: Provide a detailed description of the drainage system, including its location, type, and size.
* **System Location**: Enter the location of the drainage system.
* **System Type**: Enter the type of the drainage system.
* **System Size**: Enter the size of the drainage system.
* **Pipe Inspections**: Indicate if pipe inspections have been performed during this inspection. Choose "Yes" if they have been, "No" if they have not.
* **Inspection Results**: Enter the results of the inspection, including any relevant details or observations.
* **Inspector Notes**: Add any additional notes from the inspector.
* **Photos Taken**: Indicate if photos were taken during the inspection. Choose "Yes" if they were, "No" if they were not.
* **Videos Taken**: Indicate if videos were taken during the inspection. Choose "Yes" if they were, "No" if they were not.
* **Attachments**: Attach any relevant documents or materials to the form.

Note: Please make sure to carefully review each field and enter accurate information to ensure that the form is completed correctly. If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask for assistance.
