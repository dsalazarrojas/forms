# Fire Damper Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Damper Inspection Form is used to collect information during a fire damper inspection, which is a process to check the condition and performance of a fire damper, a critical component of a heating system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reviewing the general inspection section.
2. Select the correct system type from the options provided.
3. If necessary, add any additional comments.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Inspection** (`general_inspection`, text, optional): This section allows you to document any general observations, issues, or findings from the inspection.
- **System Type** (`system_type`, select_one, optional): This field requires you to select the type of system that the fire damper is part of. Choose from the options: `damper leakage`, `Other`.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Use this field to add any additional information or notes about the inspection or the fire damper.
- **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): This field is intended for your signature to confirm you have reviewed the information provided. 
- **Inspector's Signature** (`inspector_signature`, note, required): This field is for the inspector's signature to confirm that they have completed and reviewed the form.
