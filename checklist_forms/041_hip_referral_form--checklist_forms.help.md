# Hip Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hip Referral Form is designed to collect necessary information about patients who need referrals to specialists. This form will guide the referring specialist in evaluating the patient's suitability and provide the necessary details for a smooth referral process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Gather all the necessary information about the patient and fill in the relevant fields.
2. Select the relevant specialties for the referral from the options provided.
3. Choose the correct reasons for the referral from the options provided.
4. Enter the date and time of the referral, if applicable.
5. Ensure that you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, optional)
This field is used to enter the patient's information. Please provide the patient's name and any other relevant details.

### 2. **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional)
This field is used to document the patient's medical history. Select all the relevant medical conditions or allergies that apply to the patient.

### 3. **Referral Details** (`referral_details`, select_multiple, optional)
Choose the specialists to whom the patient needs to be referred to, such as Orthopaedic Specialist, Cardiovascular Specialist, Pulmonologist, or Rheumatologist.

### 4. **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, select_one, optional)
Select the reason for the referral from the options provided, such as Hip Replacement, Hip Fracture, or Hip Pain.

### 5. **Hip Replacement** (`hip_replacement`, date, optional)
If the referral is related to a hip replacement, enter the date of the hip replacement.

### 6. **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional)
Enter the patient's name.

### 7. **Goals** (`goals`, select_multiple, optional)
Indicate whether the patient has any specific goals or expectations from the referral.

### 8. **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, text, optional)
Provide any additional medical information about the patient, such as their medical record.

### 9. **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional)
Enter the patient's contact number.

### 10. **Medical History 1** (`medical_history_1`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the relevant medical conditions or allergies that apply to the patient.

### 11. **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the relevant medical conditions or allergies that apply to the patient.

### 12. **Medical History 3** (`medical_history_3`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the relevant medical conditions or allergies that apply to the patient.

### 13. **Medical History 4** (`medical_history_4`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the relevant medical conditions or allergies that apply to the patient.

### 14. **Medical History 5** (`medical_history_5`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the relevant medical conditions or allergies that apply to the patient.

### 15. **Referral Outcome** (`referral_outcome`, note, optional)
Add any additional information about the referral outcome or any other relevant details.

### 16. **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, optional)
The patient is required to sign this form to confirm their acceptance of the referral.

### 17. **Date** (`date`, date, optional)
Enter the date of the referral.

### 18. **Time** (`time`, time, optional)
Enter the time of the referral.
