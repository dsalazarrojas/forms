This help guide will explain the Anesthesia Equipment Checklist form's purpose, how to complete it, and each field's meaning in a clear and concise manner.

The Anesthesia Equipment Checklist form is designed to ensure that all necessary equipment is checked and ready for an anesthesia procedure. By following this guide, users will be able to quickly and accurately complete the form, which will help in maintaining high-quality care and preventing potential risks.

The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of fields and options. The user is required to complete the form in a specific order, starting with the top section and moving down.

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to verify that all anesthesia equipment is in good working order before use. It helps identify any potential issues and ensures that the necessary equipment is available and ready for the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out the top section, which includes the Inspector/Technician Name, Operating Room Number, and Date of Inspection. These fields are required and must be completed accurately.
2. Next, move on to the equipment checks, starting with the Anesthesia Machine System Check. Select the status of each equipment item, choosing from Pass, Fail, or N/A.
3. Continue with the oxygen supply levels, selecting Full, Partial, or Low.
4. Check the backup ventilation status, choosing Present or Missing.
5. Confirm the suction system's operational status, selecting True or False.
6. Move on to the monitoring and supplies section, which includes selecting all applicable monitors, and the status of laryngoscopes and endotracheal tubes.
7. Add any additional comments from the technician in the Technician Comments field.
8. Finally, type the initials to confirm the digital sign-off.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector / Technician Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person performing the inspection.
* **Operating Room Number** (`operating_room_number`, text, required): Enter the number of the operating room.
* **Date of Inspection** (`inspection_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Anesthesia Machine System Check** (`anesthesia_machine_checked`, select_one, required): Choose whether the anesthesia machine is in Pass, Fail, or N/A status.
* **Primary Oxygen Supply Levels** (`oxygen_supply_levels`, select_one, required): Select the status of the primary oxygen supply, choosing from Full, Partial, or Low.
* **Backup Ventilation (Ambu Bag)** (`backup_ventilation_ambu_bag`, select_one, required): Choose whether the backup ventilation is present or missing.
* **Suction System Operational** (`suction_operational`, select_one, required): Select whether the suction system is operational or not.
* **Monitoring and Supplies** (`section_monitoring_supplies`, note, required): This is a note section where you can add any comments about the monitoring and supplies.
* **Patient Monitors Functional** (`monitors_functional`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable monitors that are functional.
* **Laryngoscopes and Blades Status** (`laryngoscopes_and_blades`, select_one, required): Choose the status of laryngoscopes and blades, selecting from Checked and Working or Needs Maintenance.
* **Endotracheal Tubes (Assorted Sizes)** (`endotracheal_tubes_assorted_sizes`, select_one, required): Select the status of endotracheal tubes, choosing from In Stock or Low Stock.
* **Additional Technician Comments** (`technician_comments`, text, required): Add any additional comments from the technician.
* **Digital Sign-off** (`digital_sign_off`, text, required): Type the initials to confirm the sign-off.
