# scuba_diving_gear_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scuba_diving_gear_checklist_form is designed to collect information about scuba diving gear and procedures before and after a dive. It helps divers prepare for safe dives and ensures that they are aware of their equipment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the diver's name in the **Diver Name** field.
2. Specify the **Dive Site** where you will be diving.
3. Enter the **Dive Date** and **Dive Time** of your dive.
4. Select the **Safety Equipment** you have with you, such as a safety harness, dive knife, primary mask, alternate air source, reels, weight belt, weights, and dive lights.
5. Describe the **Emergency Procedure** you would follow in case of an emergency.
6. Check the status of your **Scuba Gear** (Ready, Not Ready, or Unknown).
7. Provide information about your **Dive Companion** (if any).
8. Include any additional information in the **Dive Report** field.
9. Specify any **Other Equipment** you are using.
10. Describe any **Special Condition** (if applicable).
11. Select the **Dive Type** (Recreational, Technical, or Rescue).
12. Choose your **Dive Level** (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
13. Choose the **Dive Site Visibility** (Open Ocean, Limited Visibility, or Dark Dive Site).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Diver Name**: Your name as a diver.
* **Dive Site**: The location where you will be diving.
* **Dive Date**: The date of your dive.
* **Dive Time**: The time of your dive.
* **Safety Equipment**: List the scuba diving safety equipment you have with you.
* **Emergency Procedure**: Describe the procedure you would follow in case of an emergency.
* **Scuba Gear Status**: Check if your scuba gear is ready, not ready, or unknown.
* **Dive Companion**: If you're diving with someone, provide their name.
* **Dive Report**: Include any additional information about your dive.
* **Other Equipment**: List any other equipment you are using.
* **Special Condition**: Describe any special condition or circumstances that may affect your dive.
* **Dive Type**: Select the type of dive you will be performing (Recreational, Technical, or Rescue).
* **Dive Level**: Choose your level of expertise (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Dive Site Visibility**: Select the visibility of the dive site (Open Ocean, Limited Visibility, or Dark Dive Site).

## Tips
This form is designed to be completed before and after each dive to ensure that you are prepared and aware of your equipment status. Please review the form carefully and answer all questions truthfully to ensure safe diving practices.
