# Rental Property Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information about a rental property, aiding in the evaluation and management of properties.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the property's address in the **Property Address** field. This will serve as the property's identifier and will help locate the property within our records.
2. Select the property type from the **Property Type** field. This will help classify the property according to its intended use or characteristics (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
3. Indicate the property's condition by selecting all applicable options from the **Property Condition** field. This field allows you to specify multiple conditions that apply to the property.
4. Enter the rent price for the property in the **Rent Price** field. This is the amount the renter pays for the use of the property.
5. Enter the start and end dates of the lease in the **Lease Start Date** and **Lease End Date** fields, respectively. These dates should reflect the duration of the rental agreement.
6. Identify the property's owner in the **Property Owner** field. This should be the person or entity responsible for the property's management.
7. Specify the property manager's contact information in the **Property Manager** field. This will aid in communication and coordination with the property manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address**: Enter the property's full address, including street number and city/state ZIP code.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Property Condition**: Indicate any of the following conditions that apply:
	+ Select one or both of the options for **Yes** or **No** to indicate whether the property has a garage or elevator.
* **Rent Price**: Enter the amount paid by the renter for the property's use.
* **Lease Start Date**: Enter the date the lease began.
* **Lease End Date**: Enter the date the lease is set to end.
* **Property Owner**: Enter the name or identifier of the property owner.
* **Property Manager**: Enter the name or identifier of the property manager.
