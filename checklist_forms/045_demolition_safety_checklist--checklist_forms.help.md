# Demolition Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Demolition Safety Checklist is a critical form used to assess the safety of a demolition site. It helps site inspectors and supervisors evaluate the presence and control of hazards, site clearance, and other essential safety aspects. The form is designed to promote a safe working environment, prevent accidents, and ensure compliance with safety regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Demolition Safety Checklist, follow these steps:

1. **Site Visit Preparation**: Enter a brief description of your site visit, including the date, location, and purpose of the visit.
2. Choose the correct answers for the questions that follow, using the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Site Visit Preparation
* **Site Visit Preparation (Text Field)**: Enter a brief description of your site visit, including the date, location, and purpose of the visit. This will serve as a reference for the rest of the questions on this form.

### Hazards Control
* **Hazards Identified (Select One)**: Choose whether hazards were identified during the site visit. If yes, select "Hazards Identified." If no, select "No Hazards Identified."

### Site Clearance
* **Site Clearance (Select Multiple)**: Choose the status of site clearance by selecting all that apply:
	+ All Clear: If the site is completely clear of hazards.
	+ Not All Clear: If the site is not fully clear of hazards.
	+ Partially Clear: If the site is partially clear of hazards.

### Site Inspection
* **Site Inspection (Text Field)**: Enter a brief description of your site inspection, including any notable findings or observations.

### Site Preparation Checklist
* **Site Preparation Checklist (Text Field)**: Enter a brief description of the site preparation checklist, including any items that were inspected or checked.

### Site Hazards
* **Site Hazards (Select One)**: Choose whether hazards were identified during the site visit. If yes, select "Hazards Identified." If no, select "No Hazards Identified."

### Site Clearance Date
* **Site Clearance Date (Date Field)**: Enter the date when the site was cleared, if applicable.

### Site Inspector
* **Site Inspector (Text Field)**: Enter the name of the site inspector who conducted the site visit.

### Site Supervisors
* **Site Supervisors (Text Field)**: Enter the names of site supervisors present during the site visit.

### Site Hazards Description
* **Site Hazards Description (Text Field)**: Enter a brief description of any hazards identified during the site visit.

## Tips
* Always review and complete the form thoroughly to ensure accurate and up-to-date information.
* Use the form as a reference for future site visits to track improvements and areas for improvement.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, consult with a supervisor or safety expert.
