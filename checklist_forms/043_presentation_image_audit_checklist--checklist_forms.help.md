# Presentation Image Audit Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Presentation Image Audit Checklist form is a tool to assess the visual aspects of your presentation. It helps identify potential issues and provide suggestions for improvement to make your presentation more engaging and effective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form and ensure you have all the necessary information and resources available.
2. Select the most accurate option for each field based on your presentation's slide visuals, image content, visual flow, image consistency, slide layout, and image size.
3. Answer each field carefully, taking into account the options provided and your presentation's characteristics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Slide Visuals** (Page 1, Required)
  - This field checks the overall visual appearance of your slides. Select "Image" if your slides primarily use images, "Design" if they primarily use design elements, or another option if they have a mix of both.
* **Image Content** (Page 2, Optional)
  - This field indicates the type of content used in your images. Choose "Informational" if your images provide information, "Persuasive" if they aim to persuade, or "Persuasive/Informational" if they do both.
* **Visual Flow** (Page 3, Required)
  - This field checks the flow of your slides. Select "1" if your slides have a logical flow, "2" if they have a random flow, or another option if they have a mix of both.
* **Image Consistency** (Page 4, Required)
  - This field checks if your images are consistent in style and theme throughout the presentation. Choose "Consistent" if they are, "Inconsistent" if they are not, or another option if they have a mix of both.
* **Slide Layout** (Page 5, Required)
  - This field checks the organization of your slides. Select "Well-Organized" if they are well-structured and easy to follow, "Cluttered" if they are cluttered and hard to follow, or another option if they have a mix of both.
* **Image Size** (Page 6, Optional)
  - This field checks the size of your images. Choose "Proportionate" if they are well-sized, "Too Small" if they are too small, "Too Large" if they are too large, or another option if they have a mix of both.
* **Slide Content** (Page 7, Required)
  - This field indicates the type of content used in your slides. Choose "Text" if your slides primarily contain text, "Image" if they primarily use images, or "Both" if they have a mix of both.
