# Assignment Planning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help educators plan and organize assignments for their students. It provides a comprehensive checklist to capture key details about each assignment, ensuring that all necessary information is gathered and communicated effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student who will be completing the assignment.
2. **Assignment Title**: Provide a brief title for the assignment.
3. **Course or Class Name**: Enter the name of the class or course this assignment is for.
4. **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the instructor responsible for the assignment.
5. **Due Date**: Enter the date when the assignment is due.
6. **Planning Date**: Enter the date when you plan to begin working on the assignment.
7. **Assignment Description**: Describe the requirements and expectations of the assignment.
8. **Learning Objectives**: Outline what the student should learn or achieve from this assignment.
9. **Estimated Time Required**: Estimate the number of hours needed to complete the assignment.
10. **Priority Level**: Indicate the level of priority for this assignment (High, Medium, or Low).
11. **Research Required**: Check if any research is needed for this assignment.
12. **Sources to Consult**: List potential sources that may be consulted for this assignment.
13. **Required Materials or Resources**: List any materials or resources needed to complete the assignment.
14. **Do You Have All Materials?**: Check if all required materials are available.
15. **Clarifications Needed**: Check if any clarifications are needed from the instructor.
16. **Collaboration or Help Needed**: Check if collaboration is required for this assignment.
17. **Collaborators or Group Members**: List any collaborators or group members.
18. **Planned Start Date**: Enter the date when you plan to begin working on the assignment.
19. **Target Date for First Draft**: Enter the date when the first draft should be completed.
20. **Revision and Review Date**: Enter the date when the assignment will be revised and reviewed.
21. **Final Review and Submission Date**: Enter the date when the final review and submission is due.
22. **First Task**: Describe the initial task or step in the assignment.
23. **Second Task**: Describe the next task or step in the assignment.
24. **Additional Steps**: List any additional tasks or steps in the assignment.
25. **Notes or Concerns**: Capture any notes or concerns regarding the assignment.
