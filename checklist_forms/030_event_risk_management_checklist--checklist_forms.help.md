# Event Risk Management Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a systematic approach to evaluating and managing risks associated with events and events-related activities. It helps users identify potential hazards, plan for emergency responses, and ensure participant and event-goer safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each page and section carefully to identify potential risks and hazards.
2. Answer each field honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Use the hints and guidance provided for each field to ensure you complete the form accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction** (`intro`, `note`, `false`)
This page introduces the form and its purpose. Read it carefully before proceeding to understand the scope and objectives of the form.
### 2. **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, `text`, `false`)
This field allows you to describe any potential risks or hazards that may be present at the event. Be specific about the type of risk, its likelihood, and potential consequences.
### 3. **Emergency Planning** (`emergency_planning`, `text`, `false`)
Describe your plan for responding to emergencies, including evacuation procedures, first aid, and communication protocols.
### 4. **Emergency Response** (`emergency_response`, `text`, `false`)
Explain your plan for providing emergency response services, including the types of equipment, personnel, and resources available.
### 5. **Evacuation Plan** (`evacuation_plan`, `text`, `false`)
Describe your plan for evacuating participants and event-goers safely and efficiently in case of an emergency.
### 6. **First Aid** (`first_aid`, `text`, `false`)
Describe your plan for providing first aid and medical response to injured or ill event-goers.
### 7. **Medical Response** (`medical_response`, `text`, `false`)
Explain your plan for responding to medical emergencies, including the types of medical personnel and equipment available.
### 8. **Injury Reporting** (`injury_reporting`, `text`, `false`)
Describe your process for reporting injuries and incidents that occur during the event.
### 9. **Incident Reporting** (`incident_reporting`, `text`, `false`)
Explain your process for reporting incidents that occur during the event, including near misses and actual incidents.
### 10. **Training** (`training`, `text`, `false`)
Describe any relevant training or drills that have been conducted to prepare event staff and personnel for emergencies.

## Tips
- Be thorough and detailed in your answers.
- Use specific examples and scenarios to illustrate your plans.
- Review and update this form regularly to ensure it remains relevant and effective.
- Consult with experts and stakeholders to ensure your answers are accurate and comprehensive.
- Keep this form as a living document, reviewing and updating it after each event to improve future responses.
