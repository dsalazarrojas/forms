# Kitchen Planning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kitchen Planning Checklist is a document used to collect and store information about a kitchen project's requirements and timeline.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the project requirements: Select all the requirements that apply to your kitchen project from the list of Label 1, Label 2, and Label 3.
2. Enter your design preferences: Type in your preferences for the kitchen design, such as color schemes, materials, or styles.
3. Set the project timeline: Choose a start and end date for the project, ensuring you have enough time for planning, design, and execution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Requirements** (`project_requirements`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select multiple project requirements that apply to your kitchen project. Choose from Label 1, Label 2, and Label 3 to document your project's needs.
* **Design Preferences** (`design_preferences`, `text`, required: false): Enter your personal preferences for the kitchen design, such as color schemes, materials, or styles that you like or dislike.
* **Timeline Checklist** (`timeline_checklist`, `date`, required: false): Select a start and end date for the project, ensuring you have sufficient time for planning, design, and execution.

## Tips
* Make sure to review all fields carefully and select all relevant options for your project.
* This form is a general guide; adjust it as needed to fit your specific project requirements.
* Use this form as a starting point for discussion with your contractor or designer to ensure everyone is on the same page.
* Save the completed form for future reference or share it with others involved in the project.
* This form is designed to be adaptable; feel free to add or remove fields as needed.
