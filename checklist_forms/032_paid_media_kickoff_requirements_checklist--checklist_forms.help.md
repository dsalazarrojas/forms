# Paid Media Kickoff Requirements Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paid Media Kickoff Requirements Checklist is a form used to collect and organize information necessary for paid media kickoffs. This form helps to ensure that all required details are captured and communicated effectively to the stakeholders involved in the campaign setup process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, making sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.
2. Review each field's explanation for guidance on what to enter.
3. Save your progress and return to the form if you need to make changes or additions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Marketing Team Details** (`marketing_team_details`, text, optional): Enter the names and contact information of your marketing team members responsible for this campaign.
* **Creative Assets** (`creative_assets`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type(s) of creative assets you will be using for this campaign (video, image, animation, sound, or other).
* **Ad Serving URL** (`ad_serving_url`, text, optional): Enter the ad serving URL for your chosen ad serving platform.
* **Ad Serving Type** (`ad_serving_type`, select_one, optional): Select the ad serving platform you are using (e.g., Google DoubleClick, Adobe Media, or Demandbase).
* **Ad Serving ID** (`ad_serving_id`, text, optional): Enter the Ad Serving ID for your selected platform.
* **Ad Serving Username** (`ad_serving_username`, text, optional): Enter your Ad Serving Username.
* **Ad Serving Password** (`ad_serving_password`, text, optional): Enter your Ad Serving Password.
* **Ad Serving API Key** (`ad_serving_api_key`, text, optional): Enter your Ad Serving API Key.
* **Campaign Details** (`campaign_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional campaign details or notes that are relevant to the campaign.
* **Launch Date** (`launch_date`, date, optional): Select the date when you plan to launch the campaign.
* **Launch Time** (`launch_time`, time, optional): Enter the launch time for the campaign.
* **Launch Duration** (`launch_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the campaign launch.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional): Enter the campaign budget.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, optional): Enter the target audience for your campaign.
* **Target Location** (`target_location`, text, optional): Enter the target location for your campaign.
* **Media Channels** (`media_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Select the media channels you will be using for the campaign (e.g., Google Ads, Facebook Ads, Instagram Ads).
* **Media Spend** (`media_spend`, number, optional): Enter the media spend for your campaign.
* **Creative Assets Review** (`creative_assets_review`, select_multiple, optional): Select which creative assets you will be reviewing (video, image, animation, sound, or other).
* **Launch Status** (`launch_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current launch status of your campaign (pending, launched, or paused).
* **Launch Notes** (`launch_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes about the campaign launch.
* **Budget Allocation** (`budget_allocation`, number, optional): Enter your budget allocation for the campaign.
