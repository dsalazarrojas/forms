# Important Reminders Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Important Reminders Checklist" form is used to collect and manage important reminders. It's not a replacement for the actual reminder system but rather a supplemental tool for reviewing and updating reminders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each section of the form and ensure the information is up-to-date and accurate.
2. Fill out the form with the necessary details for each reminder.
3. Save your changes to update the reminder information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Reminders
* **Reminders** (`important_reminders`, note, required/optional): Enter a list of important reminders you want to manage. You can add as many reminders as needed, and each reminder should have its own description and associated details.

### Deadlines (Deadlines)
* **Deadlines** (`deadlines`, date, required/optional): Enter the deadline for each reminder. This will help ensure you don't forget important due dates.

### Priorities (Priorities)
* **Priorities** (`priorities`, select_one, required/optional): Select the priority level for each reminder as High, Low, or Medium. This will help you focus on the most critical reminders first.

### Ownership (Ownership)
* **Ownership** (`ownership`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more teams or departments that own the reminder, such as Operations or HR. This will help ensure the correct teams are aware of their responsibilities.

### Description (Description)
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of each reminder. This will help you and others quickly understand the context of each reminder.

### Follow-up (Follow-up)
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, time, required/optional): Enter the follow-up date and time for each reminder. This will help you stay on track with your tasks.

### Status (Status)
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required/optional): Update the status of each reminder as Open, In Progress, or Closed. This will help you track the progress and completion of each reminder.

### Assigned To (Assigned To)
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the name of the person or team assigned to handle each reminder. This will help ensure the correct person is aware of their task.

Note: This form is not exhaustive, and you may add or remove sections as needed to suit your specific requirements.
