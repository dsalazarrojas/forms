# Pediatric Symptom Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pediatric Symptom Checklist is a tool used by pediatricians to gather information about a child's health and medical history. It is essential for the pediatrician to have a thorough understanding of the child's condition to make an accurate diagnosis and develop an effective treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with the requested information.
2. Enter the child's name and pediatrician's name in the respective fields.
3. Enter the child's age in the "Age" field.
4. Select any symptoms the child is currently experiencing from the "Symptoms" field.
5. Enter any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.
6. If applicable, enter any allergies the child has in the "Allergies" field.
7. Enter any contact information for the child's parents or guardians in the "Contact Information" field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes for Pediatrician" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pediatrician's Name** (`pediatric_symptom_checklist_1`, text, required): Enter the name of the pediatrician seeing the child.
* **Child's Name** (`pediatric_symptom_checklist_2`, text, required): Enter the name of the child.
* **Age** (`pediatric_symptom_checklist_3`, number, required): Enter the child's age.
* **Date of Birth** (`pediatric_symptom_checklist_4`, date, required): Enter the child's date of birth.
* **Symptoms** (`pediatric_symptom_checklist_5`, select_multiple, required): Select any of the following symptoms the child is experiencing:
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Runny Nose/Throat
	+ Sore Throat
	+ Diarrhea
	+ Vomiting
	+ Rash
	+ Eye Infection
	+ Ear Infection
* **Medical History** (`pediatric_symptom_checklist_6`, note, optional): Enter any relevant medical history, such as previous illnesses or treatments.
* **Allergies** (`pediatric_symptom_checklist_7`, note, optional): Enter any allergies the child has.
* **Contact Information** (`pediatric_symptom_checklist_8`, text, optional): Enter any contact information for the child's parents or guardians.
* **Notes for Pediatrician** (`pediatric_symptom_checklist_9`, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes for the pediatrician to review.
