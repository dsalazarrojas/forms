# Dining Out Decision Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dining Out Decision Checklist is a form used to gather information from your group about their dining preferences and needs before booking a restaurant.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to access the form.
2. Select the options that best describe your group's preferences and needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Group Preferences (Group Preferences, note, required: false)**: This section is for any special notes or requests you'd like to share with the restaurant about your group.
* **Vegetarian Diet (Vegetarian Diet, select_one, required: false)**: Choose whether all members of your group follow a vegetarian diet.
* **Gluten Free Food (Gluten Free Food, select_multiple, required: false)**: Select all the types of gluten-free food you'd like to have available.
* **Dairy Free Food (Dairy Free Food, select_multiple, required: false)**: Select all the types of dairy-free food you'd like to have available.
* **No Seafood (No Seafood, select_multiple, required: false)**: Select all the types of seafood you'd like to avoid.
* **No Nuts (No Nuts, select_multiple, required: false)**: Select all the types of nuts you'd like to avoid.
* **Other Dietary Restrictions (Other Dietary Restrictions, text, required: false)**: If you have other dietary restrictions, please specify them here.
* **Smoking Section (Smoking Section, text, required: false)**: Is there a smoking section in the restaurant?
* **Parking Facility (Parking Facility, select_multiple, required: false)**: Select whether the restaurant has a parking facility.
* **Outdoor Seating (Outdoor Seating, select_one, required: false)**: Choose whether you'd like to have outdoor seating.
* **Credit Card Accepted (Credit Card Accepted, select_one, required: false)**: Choose whether the restaurant accepts credit cards.
* **Dining Hours (Dining Hours, date, required: false)**: If you'd like to dine at a specific time, please enter the date and time.
* **Reservation Required (Reservation Required, select_multiple, required: false)**: Select whether you'd like to make a reservation.
* **Dining Location (Dining Location, select_multiple, required: false)**: Select the location where you'd like to dine (e.g. rooftop, patio, etc.).
* **Form Comments (Form Comments, text, required: false)**: If you have any additional comments or requests, please share them here.
* **Special Requests (Special Requests, text, required: false)**: If you have any special requests, please specify them here.
* **Group Size (Group Size, number, required: false)**: Enter the number of members in your group.
* **Time of Day (Time of Day, time, required: false)**: If you'd like to dine at a specific time, please enter the time.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info, email, required: false)**: Enter your contact information (e.g. email, phone number).
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact, text, required: false)**: Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address (Email Address, email, required: false)**: Enter your email address.
* **Organization (Organization, text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the organization or company.
* **Address (Address, text, required: false)**: Enter the street address or name of the location.
* **Form ID (Form ID, number, required: false)**: This is an internal field and can be ignored.
