# railcar_cleaning_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The railcar cleaning inspection checklist is a form used to record the status of a railcar's cleanliness after maintenance. It is used to track the cleanliness of the railcar, identify areas that need attention, and assign tasks to maintenance personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "railcar cleaning inspection checklist" form.
2. Select the date and time of the inspection from the corresponding fields.
3. Enter the railcar ID number.
4. Choose the method used for the cleaning (e.g., "clean").
5. Select the area(s) of the railcar that was cleaned (e.g., "side of railcar", "underneath railcar", "other").
6. Choose the status of the cleanliness (e.g., "clean", "dirty", "pending").
7. Add any additional comments, if necessary.
8. Select the maintenance personnel assigned to perform the task.
9. Choose the follow-up action(s) to be taken (e.g., "schedule next check", "schedule recurring checks", "other").
10. Add any additional comments, if necessary.
11. Select who submitted the form (e.g., "maintenance person", "supervisor", "manager").
12. Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
13. Enter the name and phone number of the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This section is a note field where the maintenance supervisor can provide a brief description of the inspection.
* **Cleaning Inspection Date**: Enter the date the inspection was conducted. Use the date format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Cleaning Inspection Time**: Enter the time the inspection was conducted. Use the time format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Railcar ID**: Enter the railcar ID number. This will help in identifying the railcar being inspected.
* **Cleaning Method**: Enter the method used for cleaning the railcar. Examples include "clean", "wipe down", "steam clean", etc.
* **Cleaning Area**: Select the area of the railcar that was inspected. Choose from "side of railcar", "underneath railcar", or "other".
* **Cleanliness Status**: Select the status of the railcar's cleanliness. Choose from "clean", "dirty", or "pending".
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the railcar's cleanliness.
* **Assigned Maintenance**: Select the maintenance personnel assigned to perform the task.
* **Follow-up Action**: Choose the follow-up action to be taken. Select from "schedule next check", "schedule recurring checks", or "other".
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments about the inspection or maintenance process.
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted the form. Choose from "maintenance person", "supervisor", or "manager".
* **Submit Date**: Enter the date the form was submitted. Use the date format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Submit Time**: Enter the time the form was submitted. Use the time format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Submitter Name**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by maintenance personnel to record the status of the railcar's cleanliness after maintenance.
