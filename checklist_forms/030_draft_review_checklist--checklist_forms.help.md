# draft_review_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a checklist designed to facilitate multiple reviewers to input their opinions and feedback on various aspects of a draft, including project management, editor review, design, content, language, usability, and accessibility.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each field carefully and answer the questions based on your expertise and knowledge.
2.  Enter your name and role (e.g., Project Manager, Editor, Designer, etc.) in the "Role" field.
3.  Select the relevant fields based on your area of expertise.
4.  Provide detailed and constructive feedback in the text fields.
5.  Save your progress and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Manager Review** (`project_manager_review`, text, optional): This field is for the project manager to review and provide feedback on the project's overall progress and direction.
* **Editor Review** (`editor_review`, text, optional): This field is for the editor to review and provide feedback on the language, grammar, and overall clarity of the content.
* **Design Review** (`design_review`, text, optional): This field is for the designer to review and provide feedback on the visual aspects of the draft, including layout, color scheme, and overall aesthetic.
* **Content Review** (`content_review`, text, optional): This field is for reviewers to review and provide feedback on the content's accuracy, completeness, and relevance.
* **Language Review** (`language_review`, text, optional): This field is for reviewers with a linguistic or language background to review and provide feedback on the language usage and style.
* **Usability Review** (`usability_review`, text, optional): This field is for reviewers with a user experience or usability background to review and provide feedback on the user experience and user interface.
* **Accessibility Review** (`accessibility_review`, text, optional): This field is for reviewers with a disability access or accessibility background to review and provide feedback on the accessibility of the content and design.
* **Design Revision** (`design_revision`, text, optional): This field is for reviewers to provide feedback on changes or revisions needed in the design.
* **Role** (`title`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your name and role in the review process.

## Tips
* Make sure to keep your feedback focused on the specific aspects of the draft being reviewed.
* Be constructive and respectful in your feedback, avoiding personal opinions and biases.
* Use specific examples and references to support your feedback.
* Review the draft carefully before submitting your feedback to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* You can save your progress and submit the form at your convenience.
* Reviewer feedback is invaluable in ensuring the high quality of the draft.
