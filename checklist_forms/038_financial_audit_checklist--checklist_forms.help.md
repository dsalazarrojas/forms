# Financial Audit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Audit Checklist is a document used to record the procedures and findings of the financial audit process. It is a crucial part of maintaining accurate financial records and identifying any discrepancies or areas that require attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the financial statements carefully, ensuring that all relevant information has been considered.
2.  Check each field carefully, selecting "Yes" or "No" for the audit procedures, and "Yes" or "No" for the financial statements.
3.  Record the review dates in the "Review Dates" field.
4.  Enter any findings or comments in the "Audit Finding" field.
5.  Provide any additional comments or notes regarding the review in the "Financial Statements Review Notes" field.
6.  Enter any comments from the reviewer in the "Reviewer Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Audit Procedures** (`audit_procedures`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose "Yes" if the auditor performed the procedure, "No" otherwise.
* **Financial Statements** (`financial_statements`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose "Yes" if financial statements are present, "No" otherwise.
* **Financial Statements Review** (`financial_statements_review`, `time`, required/optional): This field is not applicable to the current financial statements audit.
* **Review Dates** (`review_dates`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the review in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Audit Finding** (`audit_finding`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any findings or comments regarding the audit.
* **Financial Statements Review Notes** (`financial_statements_review_notes`, `email`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the review.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any comments or feedback from the reviewer.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when completing this form to ensure consistency and accuracy in the audit process.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are completed correctly.
