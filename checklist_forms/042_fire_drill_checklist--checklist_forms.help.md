# fire_drill_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fire drill checklist form is used to gather information about the fire drill procedures in place within an organization. This form helps safety officers or facility managers review and document the effectiveness of fire drill procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the fire drill procedures in place within your organization.
2. Ensure that you are familiar with the current policies and protocols.
3. Answer each question on the form accurately based on the procedures in place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### fire_drill_supervisors_present
 * **fire drill supervisors present** (select_multiple): 
   Indicates the number of supervisors present during the fire drill. 
   Select "Yes" if there were supervisors present.

### fire_drill_location
 * **fire drill location** (text): 
   Enter the location where the fire drill was conducted.

### fire_drill_evacuation_plan
 * **fire drill evacuaton plan** (text): 
   Enter the fire evacuation plan used during the drill.

### fire_drill_sirens
 * **fire drill siren** (select_one): 
   Select "Yes" if fire sirens were sounded during the drill.

### fire_drill_fire_extinguishers
 * **fire drill fire extinguishers** (select_multiple): 
   Select which types of fire extinguishers were used or inspected during the drill.

### exit_routes
 * **exit routes** (text): 
   Enter any exit routes that were used or inspected during the drill.

### assembly_points
 * **assembly points** (text): 
   Enter any assembly points where personnel assembled during the drill.

### fire_drill_announcements
 * **announcements** (select_one): 
   Select "True" if announcements were made during the drill.

### roll_call
 * **roll call** (select_one): 
   Select "True" if a roll call was conducted.

### count_down
 * **count down** (select_one): 
   Select "True" if a countdown was used during the drill.

### count_start
 * **count start** (select_one): 
   Select "True" if the countdown started during the drill.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are familiar with the current fire drill procedures before completing this form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that you answer each question accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, consult with your supervisor or safety officer.
