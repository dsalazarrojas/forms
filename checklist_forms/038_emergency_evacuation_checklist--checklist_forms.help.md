# Emergency Evacuation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Evacuation Checklist is a form used to collect essential contact and emergency planning information. It is intended to provide a structured and organized approach to gathering critical details, especially in emergency situations. This form helps to quickly gather information from individuals, such as contact details, emergency contact information, and evacuation plans, which are essential for effective emergency response and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Emergency Evacuation Checklist, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields:
    *   **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number, ensuring you can be quickly reached in case of an emergency.
    *   **Email**: Provide your email address, enabling others to contact you if necessary.
*   Choose your response to the **Emergency Contact List** question, indicating whether you have an emergency contact list or not.
*   If you have an emergency contact list, select the options that apply to you from the provided list.
*   If you don't have an emergency contact list, simply answer 'No' to the question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Info**: This field is used to collect your contact details, which are essential for emergency response. Enter your phone number accurately, as it will be used to reach you in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Contact**: This field is intended to collect contact information for an emergency contact person or organization. You can provide the name, phone number, and other details of your emergency contact.
*   **Evacuation Routes**: This field is designed for emergency responders and those assisting in the response effort to plan evacuation procedures. Describe the safest evacuation routes, locations, and any hazards to be aware of.
*   **Assembly Points**: In an emergency, assembly points can be crucial for gathering and counting those affected. Provide the locations where people are likely to gather, such as assembly points, shelters, or other safe areas.
*   **Emergency Contact Info Page**: If you have additional emergency contact information, such as a specific emergency contact person's phone number or email, enter it here. This can be helpful for responders to contact the right person quickly.
*   **Emergency Contact List**: If you have an emergency contact list, check the corresponding option. This list can help emergency responders reach your contacts in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Evacuation Plan**: In this field, describe your evacuation plan, including the steps to take in case of an emergency, such as the best route to take, any hazards to avoid, or specific actions to perform. This plan can serve as a reference for emergency responders and those affected.
*   **Emergency Contact List**: If you don't have an emergency contact list, answer 'No' to this question.

# Tips

*   Be as accurate and detailed as possible when providing your emergency contact and contact information.
*   Update your emergency contact list regularly to ensure the information provided is up-to-date.
*   Share this guide with others who may need to access or use the Emergency Evacuation Checklist.
