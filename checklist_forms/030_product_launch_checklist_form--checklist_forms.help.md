# Product Launch Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Launch Checklist Form is used to gather essential information about a product launch, facilitating a smooth and successful deployment to the market.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the status of the product launch from the options provided.
2. Enter the date of the product launch.
3. Select the time of the product launch.
4. Choose one or more team members responsible for the product launch.
5. Add any launch notes that need to be taken or referenced during the launch process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Launch Status** (`product_launch_status`, select_one, **required**): Choose the status of the product launch from the options "Launched", "In Development", or "Cancelled".
* **Product Launch Date** (`product_launch_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of the product launch.
* **Product Launch Time** (`product_launch_time`, time, **required**): Choose the time of the product launch.
* **Product Launch Responsible** (`product_launch_responsible`, select_multiple, **required**): Select one or more team members who will be responsible for the product launch.
* **Launch Notes** (`launch_notes`, note, **optional**): Enter any notes or comments that need to be taken or referenced during the launch process.
