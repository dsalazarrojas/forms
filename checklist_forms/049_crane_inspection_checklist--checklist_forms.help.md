# Crane Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crane Inspection Checklist is a tool used to identify potential issues with crane equipment before they become safety hazards or cause damage to the equipment or surrounding structures. This form ensures a systematic approach to inspecting cranes and reduces the risk of accidents or equipment failure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the crane's operating condition, type, and model. If unsure, consult the crane's documentation or contact the manufacturer/supplier.
2. Check the crane's condition, including any signs of wear, damage, or neglect.
3. Select the correct condition of the crane based on your findings.
4. Enter the crane's model (if applicable).
5. Note the crane's capacity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Checklist Description**: This field is used to provide a brief description of the crane's condition or any notable issues found during the inspection.
* **crane_type**: Enter the type of crane you are inspecting (e.g., mobile, tower, gantry, etc.).
* **crane_condition**: Check all the boxes that apply to the current state of the crane:
	+ Yes: The crane is in good condition.
	+ No: The crane has some issues, or you have concerns about its condition.
* **crane_model**: If applicable, enter the crane's model number for reference.
* **crane_capacity**: Note the crane's lifting capacity (in tons) and any other relevant information.

## Tips
* Review the crane's operating instructions and manufacturer's documentation before completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the inspection, consult the crane's documentation or contact the manufacturer/supplier.
* Ensure you thoroughly examine all areas of the crane, including its components and surrounding structures.
