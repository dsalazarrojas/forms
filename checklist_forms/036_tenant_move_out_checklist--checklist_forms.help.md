# Tenant Move Out Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a tenant's move out process, including property details, tenant details, move in and move out dates, rent frequency, and rent amount, as well as any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each field. The form is divided into the following pages and fields:

*   **Page 1: Property Info**
*   **Page 2: Tenant Info**
*   **Page 3: Move In Date**
*   **Page 4: Move Out Date**
*   **Page 5: Rent Frequency**
*   **Page 6: Rent Amount**
*   **Page 7: Notes**

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Property Info

*   **Property Info**: Enter the property number and other identifying information about the property.
### Page 2: Tenant Info

*   **Tenant Info**: Enter the tenant's name and any other relevant details.
### Page 3: Move In Date

*   **Move In Date**: Enter the date the tenant moved into the property.
### Page 4: Move Out Date

*   **Move Out Date**: Enter the date the tenant moved out of the property.
### Page 5: Rent Frequency

*   **Rent Frequency**: Enter the frequency at which the tenant paid rent (e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly).
### Page 6: Rent Amount

*   **Rent Amount**: Enter the amount of rent the tenant paid.
### Page 7: Notes

*   **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the tenant's move out process.

### Tips

*   Be sure to fill in as much information as possible to ensure accurate tracking and documentation of the tenant's move-out process.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification on a field, contact the relevant personnel or refer to the property management guidelines.
*   Remember to save your progress and revisit this form if you need to make changes or corrections before submitting it.
