# quality_assurance_onboarding_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The quality assurance onboarding checklist is a form used to evaluate the readiness and progress of new hires during the onboarding process. It helps to identify areas where the employee needs support or clarification to ensure a successful transition into the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them accurately.
2. For each question, select the appropriate response option or provide a brief explanation.
3. If you're unsure about any question, ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
4. Complete all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Readiness** (`New Hire Readiness`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your current level of understanding of the quality assurance process and any areas where you need support or clarification.
* **Onboarding Steps** (`Onboarding Steps`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the onboarding steps you have completed, such as Onboarding Step 1, Onboarding Step 2, etc.
* **Internal Processes** (`Internal Processes`, `note`, optional): If you have any internal processes related to quality assurance, provide a brief description or list them.
* **Submission Organization** (`Submission Organization`, `date`, optional): If you have any existing submission organization, provide the date of completion.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `select_one`, optional): Select your phone number from the list of options.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses to ensure the quality assurance onboarding checklist is completed thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask for help.
* Complete this form as soon as you start your onboarding process to ensure a smooth transition into the organization.
