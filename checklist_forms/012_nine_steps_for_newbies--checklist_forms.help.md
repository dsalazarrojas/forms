To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Welcome** (text, optional): Enter a personal welcome message to get started with the system.
2. **Set Up Your Workspace** (text, optional): Describe your workspace and environment.
3. **Create Your Project** (text, optional): Outline your project goals and objectives.
4. **Add Your Partners** (select multiple, required): Select all partners involved in your project.
	* Choose 'Yes' to indicate the presence of partners.
	* Choose 'No' to indicate no partners.
5. **Set Your Goals** (text, optional): Define your project goals and objectives.
6. **Create Your Schedule** (text, optional): Set up your work schedule and timeline.
7. **Track Your Progress** (select one, required): Choose whether you will track your progress.

	* Select 'No' to decline tracking progress.
8. **Get Support** (text, optional): Ask for help or feedback from your partners or mentors.
9. **Review Your Progress** (text, optional): Regularly check and review your progress.
