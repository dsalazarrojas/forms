# Hydraulic Press Operation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hydraulic Press Operation Checklist is used to ensure that hydraulic press operations are performed correctly and safely, reducing the risk of equipment failure and personal injury. It helps operators and maintenance personnel to verify that all necessary safety protocols and operational procedures are followed, and that equipment is properly maintained.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the options for each field and select the applicable responses.
2. For each field, select either "Yes" or "No" based on your assessment.
3. Make sure to review and complete all fields on each page.
4. Once you have completed all fields, click the "Submit" button to save your checklist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Press Type** (`press_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all press types that apply to this operation. This includes selecting "Yes" if you are using a hydraulic press, a mechanical press, or any other type of press.
* **Safety Protocols** (`safety_protocols`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all safety protocols that are relevant to this operation. This includes selecting "Yes" if you have followed protocols for proper equipment alignment, safety harnesses, and emergency shutdown procedures.
* **Operational Procedures** (`operational_procedures`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all operational procedures that are relevant to this operation. This includes selecting "Yes" if you have followed procedures for proper press alignment, start-up and shut-down procedures, and emergency stop procedures.
* **Maintenance Schedule** (`maintenance_schedule`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all maintenance schedules that are relevant to this operation. This includes selecting "Yes" if you have followed schedules for regular inspections, cleaning, and lubrication routines.

## Tips
- Make sure to review and complete all fields carefully, as incomplete checklists may lead to equipment failure and personal injury.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult with a supervisor or subject matter expert.
- This form is intended for internal use only, and responses will only be reviewed by authorized personnel.
