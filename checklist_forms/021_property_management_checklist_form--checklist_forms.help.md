# Property Management Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Management Checklist Form is a comprehensive tool for property managers to track and manage various aspects of property maintenance, inspections, and communication with tenants. This form helps ensure that all necessary tasks are completed, inspections are performed regularly, and important information is recorded and shared.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the maintenance tasks that need to be performed, such as "Yes" or "No" for each task listed.
2. Provide a brief description for each selected maintenance task to help identify the scope of work.
3. Indicate whether the property is currently active or inactive.
4. Record any maintenance requests from tenants and provide a brief description of each.
5. Enter the contact information of the property manager, including their email address.
6. Choose the method of contact (phone, email, etc.).
7. Indicate whether the tenant has been notified of any issues.
8. Record feedback from the tenant.
9. Add any notes about the property and its status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Tasks** (`maintenance_tasks`): Check the tasks that need to be performed on the property, selecting "Yes" for tasks that require attention.
	+ Briefly describe the scope of each selected task in the **Description of Tasks** field.
* **Inspections** (`inspections`): Check if inspections are required or have already been performed for the property, selecting "Yes" or "No".
	+ Provide the date of the last inspections in the **Inspections Date** field.
	+ Record the time of the last inspections in the **Inspections Time** field.
* **Tenant Comments** (`tenant_comments`): Enter any comments or feedback received from the tenant.
* **Property Status** (`property_status`): Choose the current status of the property, selecting "Active" or "Inactive".
	+ Provide a description of the property status in the **Property Status Description** field.
* **Maintenance Requests** (`maintenance_requests`): Check if maintenance requests are required or have already been addressed, selecting "Yes" or "No".
	+ Briefly describe each maintenance request in the **Description of Requests** field.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter the contact information of the property manager, including their email address.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`): Select the method of contact (phone, email, etc.).
	+ Describe the method of contact in the **Description of Contact Method** field.
* **Tenant Notified** (`tenant_notified`): Indicate whether the tenant has been notified of any issues, selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Tenant Feedback** (`tenant_feedback`): Record feedback from the tenant.
* **Property Notes** (`property_notes`): Add any additional notes about the property.
* **Manager Notes** (`manager_notes`): Add any notes about the property manager.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`): Describe the next steps to be taken for the property.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Check if a tool or equipment is assigned to the property, selecting "Yes" or "No".
	+ Describe the tool or equipment assigned in the **Description of Assigned Tool** field.
* **Form Id** (`form_id`): Select "Yes" if the form is assigned to the property.
	+ Describe the purpose of the form id in the **Description of Form Id** field.
* **Created By** (`created_by`): Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Description of Created By** (`created_by_description`): Describe the role or position of the person who created the form.
