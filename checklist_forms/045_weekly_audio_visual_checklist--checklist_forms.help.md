# Weekly Audio Visual Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to aid in the reporting and documentation of audio-visual (AV) equipment status in specific locations. It's a checklist designed for facilities or maintenance staff to report on the status of AV equipment and prevent issues that may arise from malfunctioning equipment.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately to the best of your abilities.
2. Fill in the required fields. These fields are marked as "required" and cannot be left blank.
3. If an item is not working properly, select "Not Done" for the status.
4. If an item is working properly, select "Done" for the status.
5. Add any relevant comments or issue log entries in the designated fields.
6. Select "Submit" to complete and save your report, or choose "Save and Continue Later" to exit without saving.
7. You can also select "Cancel" to discard your report and exit without saving.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Section Auditorium (Section-auditorium)**: Describe the condition of the auditorium, including any issues or concerns.
* **Section Auditorium Equipment (Section-auditorium-equipment)**: List any issues or concerns with the auditorium equipment.
* **Section Sound Systems (Section-sound-systems)**: Check if the sound systems are working properly.
* **Section Lighting and Power (Section-lighting-and-power)**: Check if the lighting and power systems are functioning as they should.
* **Section Accessories (Section-accessories)**: List any issues or concerns with accessories.
* **Section Curtains (Section-curtains)**: Check if the curtains are working properly.
* **Section Signs (Section-signs)**: Check if the signs are visible and clear.
* **Section Communication (Section-communication)**: Describe how communication is functioning in the space.
* **Section Sign Off (Section-sign-off)**: Describe any issues or concerns you found during the inspection.
* **Issue Log (Issue-log)**: Record any issues or problems encountered during the inspection.
* **Comments (Comments)**: Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Photo Evidence (Photo-evidence)**: Attach any photos or evidence that support your report.
* **Submit (Submit)**: Select "Submit" to complete and save your report.
* **Cancel (Cancel)**: Select "Cancel" to discard your report and exit without saving.
* **Email (Email)**: You may optionally enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (Phone)**: You may optionally enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date (Date)**: You may optionally select a date for the inspection.
* **Time (Time)**: You may optionally select a time for the inspection.
* **Section Photos (Section-photos)**: Attach any photos or evidence that support your report.
* **Section Video Equipment (Section-video-equipment)**: Describe the condition of the video equipment.
* **Section Technology (Section-technology)**: Describe the condition of the technology used.
* **Section Technical (Section-technical)**: Describe any technical issues or concerns.
* **Section Audio-Visual (Section-audio-visual)**: Describe the condition of the audio-visual equipment.
* **Section AV Status (Section-av-status)**: Select the status of the audio-visual equipment, "Not Done" for issues or "Done" for working properly.
