# home_management_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on various aspects of home management, helping you to have a better understanding of your household's daily activities, expenses, and other related details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page carefully and fill in the relevant sections.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled in, indicated by an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`1`, `text`, optional): This section is a brief overview of the household, including its location, size, and any notable features.
* **Household Tasks** (`2`, `text`, optional): List the tasks that need to be completed on a daily or weekly basis, such as laundry, cooking, or cleaning.
* **Maintenance Scheduling** (`3`, `text`, optional): Provide the schedule for any regular maintenance tasks, like cleaning, repairs, or maintenance checks.
* **Maintenance Tasks** (`4`, `text`, optional): List any one-time or irregular maintenance tasks, like replacing a filter or fixing a leaky faucet.
* **Household Expenses** (`5`, `text`, optional): Record any notable expenses for the household, such as utility bills, groceries, or entertainment costs.
* **Household Members** (`6`, `text`, optional): List the names and ages of all household members.
* **Household Pets** (`7`, `text`, optional): Describe any household pets, including their names, ages, and breeds.
* **Household Equipment** (`8`, `text`, optional): List any notable household equipment, such as appliances, furniture, or fixtures.
* **Household Furniture** (`9`, `text`, optional): Describe any notable furniture pieces, including their condition and location within the household.
* **Household Decor** (`10`, `text`, optional): Describe any notable decorations, artwork, or other aesthetic elements in the household.
* **Household Plants** (`11`, `text`, optional): List any household plants, including their names, types, and location within the household.
* **Household Waste** (`12`, `text`, optional): Record any notable waste-related issues, such as recycling or trash disposal.
* **Welcome** (`13`, `text`, optional): Write a welcome message for visitors or guests.
* **Household Purpose** (`14`, `text`, optional): Describe the purpose or function of the household, such as a residence or office.
* **Notes** (`15`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the household.
* **Email** (`16`, `email`, optional): Enter a contact email address for the household.
* **Phone** (`17`, `text`, optional): Enter a contact phone number for the household.
* **Date** (`18`, `date`, optional): Record the current date.
* **Time** (`19`, `time`, optional): Record the current time.
* **Note** (`20`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Select One** (`21`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one option from the list, such as A, B, or C.
* **Select Multiple** (`22`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple options from the list, such as A, B, C, D, or E.
* **Integer** (`23`, `number`, optional): Enter a whole number, such as 1, 2, or 3.
* **Decimal** (`24`, `number`, optional): Enter a decimal number, such as 3.14 or 0.5.
* **Text** (`25`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional text information.

## Tips
* Be thorough when filling out this form to ensure accurate information.
* If unsure about a field, try to provide the best information available.
* This form is designed to be a snapshot of the current state of the household, so updates should be made regularly to reflect changes.
