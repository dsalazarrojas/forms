# Grooming Parlour Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grooming Parlour Inspection Checklist is designed to gather necessary information about a groom parlour's operations, including their address, insurance status, animal handling practices, and contact details. This form helps ensure that the parlour adheres to regulations and standards for animal care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and fill out the form according to the instructions provided.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Check your answers for consistency and relevance to the form's purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Groom Parlour Address**: This is the address of the groom parlour, where you should enter the physical location of the parlour.
* **Date of Last Inspection**: This field is for the date of the last inspection performed on the groom parlour.
* **Groom Parlour Licence**: This field is for the licence number of the parlour, which is a crucial piece of information for identifying the parlour.
* **Insurance Status**: This field is for the status of the parlour's insurance, which may be active or inactive.
* **Licence Number**: This field is for the number of the parlour's licence.
* **Insurance Number**: This field is for the parlour's insurance number.
* **Animal Handling**: This field is for information about the parlour's animal handling practices and procedures.
* **Animal Handling Licence**: This field is for the licence number of the parlour's animal handling.
* **Groom Parlour Name**: This is the name of the parlour.
* **Date of Birth**: This field is for the date of birth of the parlour's employees or owner.
* **Groom Parlour Employees**: This field is for the number of employees working at the parlour.
* **Contact Number**: This is the contact number of the parlour.
* **Inspection Date**: This field is for the date of the current inspection.
* **Groom Parlour Inspection Results**: This field is for the outcome of the inspection, including any issues or concerns.
* **Animal Handling Certification**: This field is for information about the parlour's animal handling certification.
* **Animal Handling Certification Number**: This field is for the number of the parlour's animal handling certification.
* **Insurance Status 1/2/3**: These fields are for the status of the parlour's insurance for different types.
* **Groom Parlour Certificate**: This field is for the parlour's certificate number.
* **Animal Handling Licence Certification**: This field is for the parlour's animal handling licence certification.
* **Contact Person**: This field is for the person responsible for contact with the parlour.
* **Animal Handling Certificate**: This field is for information about the parlour's animal handling certificate.
* **Inspection Frequency**: This field is for the frequency of inspections performed on the parlour.

Note: Please review each field carefully to ensure you fill out the form accurately.
