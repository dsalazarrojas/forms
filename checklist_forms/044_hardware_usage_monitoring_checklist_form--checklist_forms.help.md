# Hardware Usage Monitoring Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hardware Usage Monitoring Checklist Form is a tool used to monitor and track the usage and status of company assets. It helps administrators, users, and managers keep up-to-date records of asset usage, issues, and status to ensure efficient maintenance, inventory, and asset management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Review the form fields and their corresponding questions below to understand what information is required.
*   Enter your information in the relevant fields, ensuring accurate and complete data.
*   If you're unable to determine the status or answer a question, you may leave it blank or seek assistance from the relevant team or department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Usage History** (`usage_history`, `note`, optional): A free-text field for recording any historical information regarding the asset's usage. This can include notable events, issues, or notable changes in the asset's condition.
*   **Issues** (`usage_issues`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check any issues or problems related to the asset's usage. Select all relevant issues to ensure they are documented.
*   **Next User** (`next_user`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact details (email and phone number) of the next user who will be using the asset.
*   **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the current status of the equipment, such as 'Available', 'Maintenance', or 'Offline'.
*   **Usage Date** (`usage_date`, `date`, optional): Record the date the asset was last used or updated.
*   **Usage Time** (`usage_time`, `time`, optional): Record the time of the last usage or update.
*   **Asset Condition** (`asset_condition`, `select_one`, optional): Assess the asset's condition, choosing from 'Good', 'Bad', or 'Damaged'.
*   **Last User** (`last_user`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact details (email and phone number) of the last user who used the asset.
*   **User Comments** (`user_comment`, `note`, optional): Leave any additional comments or observations about the asset's usage or condition.
*   **Asset Location** (`asset_location`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the location where the asset is currently situated, such as 'Location 1', 'Location 2', or 'Location 3'.
*   **Asset Tag** (`asset_tag`, `text`, optional): Enter any identifying tag or label on the asset.
*   **Usage Frequency** (`usage_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how frequently the asset is used, such as 'Daily', 'Weekly', or 'Monthly'.
*   **Last Updated By** (`last_updated_by`, `select_one`, optional): Identify the person or team responsible for the last update, such as 'Admin', 'User', or 'Manager'.
*   **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the asset.
*   **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, `text`, optional): Enter the type of equipment, if any.
*   **Asset Status** (`asset_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the asset's current status, such as 'Available', 'Offline', or 'Maintenance'.
*   **Asset Model** (`asset_model`, `text`, optional): Record the asset's model number or version.
*   **Usage Count** (`usage_count`, `text`, optional): Record the number of times the asset has been used.
*   **Last Updated On** (`last_updated_on`, `date`, optional): Record the date the asset's information was last updated.
*   **Last Updated At** (`last_updated_at`, `time`, optional): Record the time the asset's information was last updated.
*   **Next User Name** (`next_user_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the next user who will be using the asset.
*   **Next User Email** (`next_user_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email of the next user who will be using the asset.
*   **Next User Phone** (`next_user_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the next user who will be using the asset.
*   **Usage Status** (`usage_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the asset's usage status, such as 'Active', 'Inactive', or 'Pending'.
*   **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the team or department that the asset is assigned to, such as 'Team A', 'Team B', or 'Team C'.
