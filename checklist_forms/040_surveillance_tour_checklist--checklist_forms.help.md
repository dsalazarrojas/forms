# Surveillance Tour Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on a surveillance tour, which is a critical aspect of security and patrol operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patrol details, including the Patrol ID.
2. Select the date of the patrol.
3. Enter the start and end times of the patrol.
4. Check if any incidents were encountered during the patrol.
5. Select the checkpoints visited during the patrol.
6. Enter any additional notes about the patrol.
7. Enter any contact information collected during the patrol (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patrol Details**: Enter a brief description of the patrol, including the location and purpose of the tour.
* **Patrol ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the patrol.
* **Patrol Date**: Select the date on which the patrol was conducted.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the patrol.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the patrol.
* **Incidents**: Check all instances where incidents were encountered during the patrol. This is not a required field.
* **Checkpoints**: Check all checkpoints that were visited during the patrol. This is not a required field.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the patrol, including any notable events or actions taken.
* **Contact Info**: Enter any contact information collected during the patrol, such as phone numbers or email addresses.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask your supervisor or team leader for guidance.
* This form is subject to change, so please check for updates before submitting your information.
