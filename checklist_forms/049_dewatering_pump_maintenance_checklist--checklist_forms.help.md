# Dewatering Pump Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dewatering Pump Maintenance Checklist is used to track and maintain the status of dewatering pumps in a facility or site.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Identify the pump location.
2. Select the pump's current condition.
3. Check the pump's operating status.
4. Check for pipe leaks or damage.
5. Enter any comments or maintenance notes.
6. Enter the job site and the person assigned to perform the maintenance.
7. Enter the pump type, model, serial number, make, year manufactured, year installed, and hours of operation.
8. Enter the start and end dates of the last pump maintenance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pump Location** (`pump_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the pump, such as room number or building name.
* **Pump Condition** (`pump_condition`, text, required: false): Describe the current condition of the pump, including any signs of wear or damage.
* **Pump Pressure** (`pump_pressure`, number, required: false): Enter the current pressure reading of the pump, if applicable.
* **Pipe Leaks** (`pipe_leakage`, select multiple, required: false): Check for any leaks or damage to the pipes connected to the pump.
* **Pump Operation** (`pump_operation`, select one, required: false): Select the operating status of the pump, whether it is currently active or inactive.
* **Pipe Damage** (`pipe_damage`, select multiple, required: false): Check for any signs of damage or wear to the pipes.
* **Pump Sound** (`pump_sound`, text, required: false): Describe any unusual sounds or noises coming from the pump.
* **Pump Movement** (`pump_movement`, text, required: false): Describe any unusual movements or vibrations coming from the pump.
* **Pump Leaks** (`pump_leakage`, select multiple, required: false): Check for any leaks or damage to the pump.
* **Pump Temperature** (`pump_temperature`, number, required: false): Enter the current temperature reading of the pump, if applicable.
* **Pipe Inspection** (`pipe_inspection`, select multiple, required: false): Check for any signs of wear or damage to the pipes.
* **Start Date** (`pump_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the last maintenance was started.
* **End Date** (`pump_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the last maintenance was ended.
* **Maintenance Status** (`maintenance_status`, select one, required: false): Select the current status of the maintenance, whether it is active or inactive.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the maintenance.
* **Job Site** (`job_site`, text, required: false): Enter the site where the maintenance was performed.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, required: false): Enter the person or team performing the maintenance.
* **Pump Type** (`pump_type`, text, required: false): Enter the type of pump, such as centrifugal or positive displacement.
* **Pump Model** (`pump_model`, text, required: false): Enter the model number of the pump.
* **Pump Serial Number** (`pump_serial_number`, text, required: false): Enter the serial number of the pump.
* **Pump Make** (`pump_make`, text, required: false): Enter the make of the pump.
* **Pump Year Manufactured** (`pump_year_manufactured`, text, required: false): Enter the year the pump was manufactured.
* **Pump Year Installed** (`pump_year_installed`, text, required: false): Enter the year the pump was installed.
* **Pump Hours** (`pump_hours`, number, required: false): Enter the number of hours the pump has been in operation.
* **Job Number** (`job_number`, text, required: false): Enter the job number or reference number.
