# operations_learning_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess employee learning and development progress, leadership skills, and confidence levels, as well as gather feedback from supervisors and track completion dates and times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your current progress on the "Checklist Progress" field.
2. Choose your leadership skills from the "Team Leadership" list.
3. Enter your current confidence level as a number.
4. Provide feedback from your supervisor on the "Supervisor Feedback" field.
5. Enter the date and time you completed the learning activity on the "Date Completed" and "Time Completed" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Checklist Progress** (1): 
Select the option that best represents your current progress in the learning activity.
* **Team Leadership** (2): 
Choose all that apply from the options, as this field is a multiple selection. The options represent leadership skills or competencies.
* **Confidence Level** (3):
	+ Enter a number between 0 and 100 to indicate your current confidence level.
* **Supervisor Feedback** (4):
	+ Enter any feedback or comments from your supervisor.
* **Date Completed** (5):
	+ Enter the date you completed the learning activity.
* **Time Completed** (6):
	+ Enter the time you completed the learning activity.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and honest progress, leadership skills, and confidence level.
- Provide clear and constructive feedback from your supervisor.
- Enter the correct date and time completed, to the best of your ability.
