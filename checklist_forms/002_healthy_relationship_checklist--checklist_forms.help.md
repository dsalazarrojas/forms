# Healthy Relationship Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the dynamics of a healthy relationship. It consists of seven questions that cover various aspects of relationships, including the type of relationship, communication, physical intimacy, conflict resolution, partner's emotional maturity, and relationship strength. Completing this form will help you assess the quality and health of your relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of relationship from the dropdown menu. Choose the most accurate description of your relationship.
2. Describe your communication style in the text field provided.
3. Indicate your level of physical intimacy using the number scale (e.g., 1-10).
4. Answer "True" or "False" to questions about conflict resolution. This will help you identify potential areas for improvement.
5. For "emotional maturity", describe your partner's emotional maturity level in the text field provided.
6. If applicable, list some strategies you use to resolve conflicts in the text field provided (this field is optional).
7. Select the strength of your relationship from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Relationship Survey (Partnership, Friendship, Family Members)**:
    This question helps determine the nature of your relationship. Choose the option that best describes your relationship.
* **How do you communicate with your partner?**:
    Describe how you express yourself in conversations with your partner, including verbal and non-verbal cues.
* **What is your level of physical intimacy?**:
    Indicate how physically close you are to your partner on a scale of 1-10. This can include factors such as physical touch, shared activities, or intimate encounters.
* **How do you resolve conflicts?**:
    Answer "True" if you generally resolve conflicts successfully, "False" if you struggle with conflict resolution, and "Not Applicable" if you don't typically experience conflicts.
* **What is your partner's emotional maturity level?**:
    Describe your partner's ability to handle emotions, manage stress, and respond to challenging situations.
* **What are some strategies you use to resolve conflicts?**:
    (Optional) List specific methods you use to address and resolve disagreements with your partner.
* **What is the strength of your relationship?**:
    Select the strength of your relationship from the options: Strong, Medium, or Weak. This will give you an idea of how secure and stable your relationship is.
