# Kindergarten Admission Checklist Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Kindergarten Admission Checklist Form Template is a comprehensive form designed to collect essential information from the parent or guardian of a kindergarten student. This form is intended to be completed by the parent or guardian and will help the school gather necessary details for the student's admission.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information section with the student's and parent/guardian's contact details.
2. Provide the student's information, including their name, birthdate, and any other relevant details.
3. Answer the medical information section, including any allergies, conditions, or medical procedures relevant to the student.
4. List any emergency contacts, including their names, phone numbers, and relationship to the student.
5. Add any additional information about the student, such as special needs or educational background.
6. Sign and date the form as the student's representative (parent or guardian).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**:
  * **Contact Name**: Please enter the name of the person completing the form (parent or guardian).
  * **Phone Number**: Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
  * **Address**: Enter the parent or guardian's address.
  * **Email**: Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
  * **Additional Information**: Provide any other relevant contact information for the parent or guardian.

* **Student Information**:
  * **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
  * **Birthdate**: Enter the student's birthdate.
  * **Student ID Card**: Please upload a student ID card or provide a photo of the student.

* **Medical Information**:
  * **Allergies and Conditions**: List any allergies, conditions, or medical procedures relevant to the student.
  * **Health Information**: Provide any other relevant medical information about the student.
  * **Immunizations**: List any immunizations the student has received.
  * **Medical History**: Provide a brief medical history for the student.
  * **Medical Procedure**: List any medical procedures the student has undergone.

* **Emergency Contacts**:
  * **Emergency Contact Name**: List the name of the emergency contact person.
  * **Phone Number**: Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
  * **Relationship**: Describe the relationship between the student and the emergency contact person.
  * **Address**: Enter the emergency contact person's address.
  * **Additional Information**: Provide any other relevant information about the emergency contact person.

* **Emergency Contact Relationship**:
  * **Relationship**: Describe the relationship between the student and the emergency contact person.

* **Emergency Contact Phone**:
  * **Phone Number**: Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.

* **Emergency Contact Email**:
  * **Email**: Enter the emergency contact person's email address.

* **Emergency Contact Address**:
  * **Address**: Enter the emergency contact person's address.

* **Emergency Contact Additional Information**:
  * **Additional Information**: Provide any other relevant information about the emergency contact person.

* **Student ID Card**:
  * **Student ID Card**: Please upload a student ID card or provide a photo of the student.

* **Student Photo**:
  * **Student Photo**: Please upload a photo of the student.

* **Student Signature**:
  * **Student Signature**: Please sign the form as the student (if applicable).

* **Teacher Signature**:
  * **Teacher Signature**: Please sign the form as the teacher (if applicable).

* **Date**:
  * **Date**: Enter the date the form was completed.

* **Time**:
  * **Time**: Enter the time the form was completed.

* **Submitted By**:
  * **Submitted By**: Check 'Yes' if the form was submitted by the student's representative (parent or guardian), or 'No' otherwise.
