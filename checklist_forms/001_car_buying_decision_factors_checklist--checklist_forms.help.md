# Car Buying Decision Factors Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Buying Decision Factors Checklist is a tool designed to help users make informed decisions when purchasing a car. It's meant to be a comprehensive guide that considers various aspects of a vehicle purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and select the options that best match your preferences.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. If you have any other concerns or preferences, use the "Additional Notes" section.
4. Fill in your answers as accurately as possible, considering your true preferences and needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What car make and model are you considering?** (car_make_model, text)
This field is for you to specify the car's make and model that you're interested in buying.
* **Your budget range** (budget_range, number)
This field requires you to enter the maximum amount you're willing to spend on a vehicle. Please be as specific as possible about your budget limits.
* **Preferred fuel type** (fuel_type_preference, select_one)
Choose from Gasoline, Diesel, Hybrid, Electric, or Plug-in Hybrid, depending on your preference.
* **New or used vehicle?** (vehicle_condition, select_one)
Select New, Certified Pre-owned, or Used, indicating whether you're interested in a new or previously owned vehicle.
* **Preferred transmission** (transmission_type, select_one)
Choose from Manual, Automatic, or CVT, depending on your preference.
* **Required seating capacity** (seating_capacity, select_one)
Choose from 2, 4, 5, 6, or 7+, indicating the number of passengers you need to accommodate.
* **How important is the warranty?** (warranty_importance, select_one)
Select Very Important, Important, Somewhat Important, or Not Important, based on how crucial warranty is to your decision.
* **Which safety features are essential? (select all that apply)** (safety_features_checklist, select_multiple)
Check all the safety features that you deem vital for your vehicle, such as Airbags, Anti-lock Braking System, Stability Control, etc.
* **Most important interior features (select up to 3)** (interior_features_preference, select_multiple)
Choose up to 3 interior features that are essential to you, like Leather Seats, Heated Seats, or Navigation System.
* **How important is fuel efficiency?** (fuel_efficiency_priority, select_one)
Indicate whether fuel efficiency is Critical, Very Important, Somewhat Important, or Not a Priority to your decision.
* **Do you need towing capacity?** (towing_capacity_needed, select_one)
Choose from True, False, or Not Sure, based on whether you'll need to tow a trailer or boat.
* **Estimated maintenance costs - major concern?** (maintenance_costs_consideration, select_one)
Select Very Concerned, Somewhat Concerned, Slightly Concerned, or Not Concerned, based on how much maintenance costs matter to you.
* **Have you scheduled a test drive?** (test_drive_scheduled, select_one)
Choose from True, False, or Planning To, indicating whether you've arranged a test drive or plan to do so.
* **Number of competing vehicle options you are considering** (competing_options, number)
Enter the number of vehicles you're considering, if any.
* **How important is dealer reputation?** (dealer_reputation_importance, select_one)
Choose from Very Important, Important, Somewhat Important, or Not Important, based on the significance of dealer reputation to your decision.
* **Preferred color** (color_preference, select_one)
Select from White, Black, Gray, Silver, Blue, Red, Brown, or Other, depending on the color you prefer.
* **Preferred financing option** (financing_option, select_one)
Choose from Cash, Financing from Dealer, Bank Financing, or Leasing, based on your preferred financing method.
* **Do you have a vehicle to trade in?** (trade_in_available, select_one)
Choose from True or False, indicating whether you're trading in your existing vehicle.
* **When do you plan to make a purchase decision?** (time_decision_deadline, select_one)
Select from Within 1 week, Within 2 weeks, Within 1 month, Within 3 months, or No Specific Timeline, based on your planned timeline.
* **Any additional preferences or concerns?** (additional_notes, text)
Write down any other factors that might be essential to your decision, even if not included in the previous questions.
