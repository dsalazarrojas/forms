# Vendor Offboarding Checklist Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of a vendor who is being offboarded. It captures essential information that helps in the offboarding process and ensures that all necessary steps are taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that the vendor has been notified of their offboarding and has acknowledged it.
2. Fill in the vendor's details such as name, ID, and type.
3. Select the correct status of the vendor (Active, Inactive, or On-Hold).
4. Enter the date the vendor was offboarded and the date the vendor's status was last updated.
5. Provide a reason for offboarding the vendor (if applicable).
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the vendor.
7. Assign a user to follow up with the vendor and the date they were assigned.
8. Enter the vendor's address, phone number, email, website, and other social media handles (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Offboarding Checklist Questions**: This section is where you will enter the specific questions that need to be answered for the offboarding process. This is an open-ended field where you can add any questions or instructions that are relevant to the vendor offboarding process.
* **Vendor Offboarding Checklist Status**: Select the status of the vendor from the drop-down menu (Active, Inactive, or On-Hold). This will help in tracking the vendor's status and ensure that all necessary actions are taken.
* **Date Offboarded**: Enter the date the vendor was offboarded.
* **Date Terminated**: Enter the date the vendor's service was terminated.
* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the vendor.
* **Vendor ID**: Enter the vendor's ID.
* **Reason Offboarding**: Provide a brief reason for offboarding the vendor (if applicable).
* **Vendor Type**: Select the type of vendor from the drop-down menu (Active, Inactive, or On-Hold).
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the vendor.
* **Assigned User**: Assign a user to follow up with the vendor and the date they were assigned.
* **Assigned Date**: Enter the date the user was assigned.
* **Status Updated**: Enter the date the status was last updated.
* **Vendor Address**: Enter the vendor's address.
* **Vendor Phone**: Enter the vendor's phone number.
* **Vendor E-mail**: Enter the vendor's email address.
* **Vendor Website**: Enter the vendor's website.
* **Vendor Facebook**: Enter the vendor's Facebook handle.
* **Vendor Twitter**: Enter the vendor's Twitter handle.
* **Vendor LinkedIn**: Enter the vendor's LinkedIn handle.
* **Vendor Instagram**: Enter the vendor's Instagram handle.
* **Vendor YouTube**: Enter the vendor's YouTube handle.
* **Vendor Pinterest**: Enter the vendor's Pinterest handle.
* **Vendor Tiktok**: Enter the vendor's Tiktok handle.
* **Vendor Other Social Media**: Enter any other social media handles for the vendor.
* **Vendor Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about the vendor.
