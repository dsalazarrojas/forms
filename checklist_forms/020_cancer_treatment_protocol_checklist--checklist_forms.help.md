# Cancer Treatment Protocol Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cancer Treatment Protocol Checklist is a comprehensive form designed to ensure the safe and successful administration of cancer treatment. It guides healthcare professionals through the essential steps and checks required before, during, and after treatment sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each section carefully before proceeding to the next.
3. If you need help or clarification on any field, consult with your supervisor or refer to the relevant medical guidelines.
4. Double-check all critical fields before signing as the administering nurse.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: This is a required field where you will enter the patient's full legal name for verification purposes.
	+ Type: <text>
	+ Description: Enter the patient's full legal name for verification purposes.
* **Medical Record Number**: This is a required field where you will enter the patient's medical record number for patient safety and record linking.
	+ Type: <text>
	+ Description: Enter the patient's medical record number for patient safety and record linking.
* **Protocol Name**: This is a required field where you will specify the oncology regimen (e.g. ABVD, FOLFOX) being administered.
	+ Type: <text>
	+ Description: Select the name of the oncology regimen being administered (e.g. ABVD, FOLFOX).
* **Cycle Number**: This is a required field where you will enter the number of the treatment cycle.
	+ Type: <number>
	+ Description: Enter the cycle number of the treatment being administered.
* **Date of Treatment**: This is a required field where you will enter the date of the treatment session.
	+ Type: <date>
	+ Description: Enter the date of the treatment session.
