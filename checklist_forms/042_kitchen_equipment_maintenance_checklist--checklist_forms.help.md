# kitchen_equipment_maintenance_checklist - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the list of equipment and determine which ones require maintenance.
2. Select the type of maintenance (Annual, Bi-Annual, Monthly, or Quarterly) for each piece of equipment that needs it.
3. Provide a brief description of the equipment that requires maintenance.
4. Check the status of the equipment (Active, Inactive, or Maintenance Required).
5. Indicate the frequency of required maintenance (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual).
6. Select the estimated cost range of the maintenance ($0-$100, $101-$1000, $1001-$5000, $5001-$10000, $10001-$20000, $20001-$50000, $50001-$100000).
7. Enter the date of the last maintenance performed, if any.
8. Add any additional notes about the maintenance process or equipment issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Maintenance Type** (maintenance_type, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of maintenance required for this equipment (Annual, Bi-Annual, Monthly, or Quarterly).
* **Equipment Description** (equipment_description, text, required: false): Briefly describe the equipment requiring maintenance.
* **Equipment Status** (equipment_status, select_one, required: false): Check the status of the equipment (Active, Inactive, or Maintenance Required).
* **Maintenance Frequency** (maintenance_frequency, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable frequencies of maintenance (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual).
* **Maintenance Cost** (maintenance_cost, select_one, required: false): Estimate the maintenance cost range ($0-$100, $101-$1000, $1001-$5000, $5001-$10000, $10001-$20000, $20001-$50000, $50001-$100000).
* **Maintenance Date** (maintenance_date, date, required: false): Enter the date of the last maintenance performed, if any.
* **Additional Notes** (maintenance_notes, note, required: false): Add any extra notes or comments about the maintenance process or equipment issues.
