# Road Trip Vehicle Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help vehicle owners conduct a thorough check on their vehicle before embarking on a road trip. It covers various aspects of the vehicle's condition and safety features, ensuring a safe and enjoyable journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of vehicle you are checking (e.g., Car, Truck, Motorcycle, RV).
2. Check the fuel level of your vehicle.
3. Verify the tire pressure of your vehicle.
4. Check the fluid levels (e.g., oil, brake fluid, coolant, etc.).
5. Determine the condition of your vehicle's brake pads.
6. Check the air filter condition.
7. Check the battery level.
8. Ensure the parking brakes are engaged.
9. Verify the seatbelts are fastened.
10. Check if the mirrors are adjusted properly.
11. Check the license plate is present.
12. Check the status of your vehicle's license.
13. Select the types of roadside assistance you have (e.g., AAA, insurance, etc.).
14. List your emergency contact numbers and insurance card.
15. Select the vehicle documents you have (e.g., registration, insurance card, vehicle title).
16. Determine if you have a spare tire.
17. Check if you have a first aid kit.
18. Check if you have a fire extinguisher.
19. Check if you have reflector flares.
20. Determine if you have a jack and wrench.
21. Check if you have a spare battery.
22. Check if you have jumper cables.
23. Check if you have reflector flares.
24. Check if you have a reflective vest.
25. Check if you have emergency rations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, `select_one`, required): This field asks you to select the type of vehicle you are checking.
* **Fuel Level** (`fuel_level`, `number`, required): Enter the current fuel level of your vehicle.
* **Tire Pressure** (`tire_pressure`, `number`, required): Enter the current tire pressure of your vehicle.
* **Fluid Levels** (`fluid_levels`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all fluid levels that are currently present in your vehicle (e.g., oil, brake fluid, coolant, etc.).
* **Brake Pads** (`brake_pad`, `select_one`, required): Choose the condition of your vehicle's brake pads (Good or Bad).
* **Air Filter** (`air_filter`, `select_one`, required): Choose the condition of your vehicle's air filter (Clean or Dirty).
* **Battery Level** (`battery_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of your vehicle's battery (Full or Low).
* **Parking Brakes** (`parking_brakes`, `select_one`, required): Ensure the parking brakes are engaged.
* **Seatbelts** (`seatbelts`, `select_one`, required): Choose if your seatbelts are fastened or unfastened.
* **Mirrors** (`mirrors`, `select_one`, required): Choose if your mirrors are adjusted or not adjusted.
* **License Plate** (`license_plate`, `note`, required): Enter your vehicle's license plate number if present.
* **Vehicle License** (`vehicle_license`, `select_one`, required): Choose the status of your vehicle's license (Valid or Invalid).
* **Roadside Assistance** (`roadside_assistance`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all types of roadside assistance you have (e.g., AAA, insurance, etc.).
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, `select_multiple`, required): List your emergency contact numbers and insurance card details.
* **Vehicle Documents** (`vehicle_docs`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all vehicle documents you have (e.g., registration, insurance card, vehicle title).
* **Spare Tire** (`spare_tire`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you have a spare tire or not.
* **First Aid Kit** (`first_aid_kit`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you have a first aid kit or not.
* **Fire Extinguisher** (`fire_extinguisher`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you have a fire extinguisher or not.
* **Reflector Flares** (`reflector_flares`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you have reflector flares or not.
* **Jack And Wrench** (`jack_and_wrench`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you have a jack and wrench or not.
* **Spare Battery** (`spare_battery`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you have a spare battery or not.
* **Jumper Cables** (`jumper_cables`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you have jumper cables or not.
* **Reflective Vest** (`reflective_vest`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you have a reflective vest or not.
* **Emergency Ration** (`emergency_ration`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you have emergency rations or not.
