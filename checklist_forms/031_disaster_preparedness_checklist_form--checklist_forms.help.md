# Disaster Preparedness Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Disaster Preparedness Checklist Form is designed to collect essential information from you in preparation for emergency situations. This form helps us to ensure that you and your emergency contacts are prepared and able to take necessary steps in case of an emergency. Please complete this form to provide us with accurate and up-to-date contact information and any relevant emergency documents.

## How to Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the page you would like to complete, "Emergency Contact Info" or "Important Documents".
2. Enter the required information for the selected page, following the guidelines below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Emergency Contact Info (Page 1)

* **Name (contact_name)**: Enter your name.
* **Phone Number (contact_phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email (contact_email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Relationship (emergency_contact_relationship)**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., parent, spouse, friend).
* **Contact Name (emergency_contact_name)**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (emergency_contact_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email (emergency_contact_email)**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2 (emergency_contact_relationship_2)**: Describe the relationship between you and the emergency contact person.
* **Other (specify) (emergency_contact_other)**: If the emergency contact person does not fit any of the above categories, describe how you are connected to them.
* **Other Emergency Contact Info (emergency_contact_other_details)**: Enter any other information about the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship Type (emergency_contact_other_type)**: Select the type of relationship (e.g., Family, Friend, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Other Info (emergency_contact_other_info)**: Enter any other details about the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Other Phone (emergency_contact_other_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Other Email (emergency_contact_other_email)**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Notes (emergency_contact_other_note)**: Enter any additional notes about the emergency contact person.

### Important Documents (Page 2)

* **Document Type (document_type)**: Select the type of document you need to provide (e.g., Driver's License, Passport, Social Security Card).
* **Document Name (document_name)**: Enter the name of the document.
* **Document Number (document_number)**: Enter the document number.
* **Location (document_location)**: Enter the location where the document is stored.
* **Date (document_date)**: Enter the date the document was issued or last updated.
* **Other Documents (second_page_other)**: If you need to provide additional documents, enter their names and information here.
* **Second Page Other Note (second_page_other_note)**: Enter any notes about the other documents.
* **Other Document Info (second_page_other_info)**: Enter any additional information about the other documents.
