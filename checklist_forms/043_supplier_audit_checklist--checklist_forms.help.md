# supplier_audit_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The supplier audit checklist is a form used to collect information from suppliers regarding their products, services, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required from the supplier.
2. Enter the supplier's information in the "supplier info" field.
3. Provide detailed information about the supplier's products in the "product info" field.
4. Enter information about the supplier's services in the "service info" field.
5. Answer questions about the supplier's administration in the "administration info" field.
6. Enter payment details in the "payment info" field.
7. Provide delivery information in the "delivery info" field.
8. Describe the warranty information in the "warranty info" field.
9. Specify the return policy in the "return policy" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **supplier info**: This field is where you enter the supplier's name, contact details, and other relevant information.
* **product info**: This field is where you provide detailed information about the supplier's products, including their descriptions, prices, and any other relevant details.
* **service info**: This field is where you enter information about the services offered by the supplier, including their descriptions and prices.
* **administration info**: This field is where you answer questions about the supplier's administration, such as their business practices and policies.
* **payment info**: This field is where you enter payment details, including payment methods and schedules.
* **delivery info**: This field is where you enter information about the supplier's delivery methods and schedules.
* **warranty info**: This field is where you describe the warranty information provided by the supplier, including duration and coverage.
* **return policy**: This field is where you specify the return policy of the supplier, including conditions and procedures.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate in completing the form to ensure the supplier's information is up-to-date and comprehensive.
* Double-check your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you need assistance or have questions about the form, contact the help desk for support.
* You can modify this form as needed to fit your specific requirements, but be sure to give credit to the original form's creator.
