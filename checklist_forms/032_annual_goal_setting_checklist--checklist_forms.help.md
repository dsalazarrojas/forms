# Annual Goal Setting Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you set and achieve your goals for the year, using the SMART criteria (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound). It guides you through a structured process to create a clear and actionable plan for your goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and contact email address.
2. Select the year for which you are setting goals.
3. Choose a category for your goal (e.g., Personal Development, Career, Health and Fitness).
4. Enter your main objective (Primary Goal).
5. Describe your goal in detail (Goal Description).
6. Specify what you want to achieve (Specific).
7. Measure how you will track your progress (Measurable).
8. Assess the realism of your goal (Achievable).
9. Explain why your goal is important (Relevant).
10. Set a deadline for your goal (Time-Bound).
11. Identify potential obstacles and solutions for them.
12. Choose a progress tracking method and frequency.
13. Select a reward system for achieving milestones.
14. Enter your motivation statement.
15. Set a start date for your goal.
16. Sign your commitment to the goal.
17. (Optional) List any additional goals you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name for easy identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address in case we need to reach you.
* **Goal Year**: Select the year for which you are setting this goal.
* **Goal Category**: Choose a category for your goal (e.g., Personal Development, Career).
* **Primary Goal**: Enter your main objective (e.g., "Increase productivity").
* **Goal Description**: Describe your goal in detail (e.g., "Increase productivity by 20% through effective time management").
* **Specific**: Specify what you want to achieve (e.g., "Complete a project within 3 months").
* **Measurable**: Measure how you will track your progress (e.g., "Track time spent on task using a timer").
* **Achievable**: Assess the realism of your goal (e.g., "Yes, Very Achievable").
* **Relevant**: Explain why your goal is important (e.g., "To improve work-life balance").
* **Time-Bound**: Set a deadline for your goal (e.g., "Complete within 3 months").
* **Milestone 1**: Enter the first key milestone (e.g., "Complete project proposal").
* **Milestone 1 Deadline**: Set a deadline for the first key milestone (e.g., "Within 2 weeks").
* **Milestone 2**: Enter the second key milestone (e.g., "Complete project development").
* **Milestone 2 Deadline**: Set a deadline for the second key milestone (e.g., "Within 6 weeks").
* **Milestone 3**: Enter the third key milestone (e.g., "Launch project").
* **Milestone 3 Deadline**: Set a deadline for the third key milestone (e.g., "Within 12 weeks").
* **Resources Needed**: Identify resources required to achieve your goal (e.g., "Time, Money, Training").
* **Potential Obstacles**: Identify potential obstacles (e.g., "Lack of resources").
* **Solutions for Obstacles**: Enter solutions for potential obstacles (e.g., "Seek help from colleagues").
* **Accountability Partner**: Choose an accountability partner (e.g., "Friend, Family Member").
* **Check-In Frequency**: Set the frequency for progress tracking (e.g., "Weekly").
* **Progress Tracking Method**: Choose a method for tracking progress (e.g., "Journal, App or Software").
* **Reward System**: Enter a reward system for achieving milestones (e.g., "Celebrate with a party").
* **Motivation Statement**: Enter a motivation statement (e.g., "I am committed to achieving this goal because I want to improve my productivity").
* **Start Date**: Set the start date for your goal (e.g., "Next Monday").
* **Commitment Signature**: Sign your commitment to the goal (e.g., "By signing below, I commit to achieving this goal.").
* **Additional Goals**: (Optional) List any additional goals you have. 

Please note that this is not a one-size-fits-all solution, and you may need to adjust the form and its explanations according to your specific needs and context.
