# Contact Lens Insertion And Removal Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the training process for contact lens insertion and removal procedures. It ensures that trainees understand and demonstrate correct techniques for inserting and removing contact lenses safely and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
* Complete all fields with the correct information.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trainee name**: Please enter the name of the person being trained.
* **Trainer name**: Enter the name of the person supervising the training.
* **Training date**: Record the date of the training session.
* **Hand hygiene completed**: Check 'True' if the trainee washed and dried their hands before the training session.
* **Lens inspection performed**: Check 'True' if the trainee checked the lens for damage or debris before the training session.
* **Insertion technique demonstrated**: Check 'True' if the trainee demonstrated correct insertion steps.
* **Removal technique demonstrated**: Check 'True' if the trainee demonstrated safe lens removal.
* **Comfort after insertion**: Check the level of comfort after placing lenses: 'Comfortable', 'Minor discomfort', or 'Significant discomfort'.
* **Follow up advice provided**: Check 'True' if aftercare and handling advice was given to the trainee.
* **Competency confirmed by trainer**: Check 'True' if the trainer confirms that the trainee can safely handle lenses.
* **Trainer signature collected offline**: Check if you recorded the trainer's signature on paper or a digital system.
