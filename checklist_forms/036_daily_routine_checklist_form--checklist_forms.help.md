# Daily Routine Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Daily Routine Checklist Form is a tool to assist you in maintaining a daily routine, promoting productivity, and ensuring you stay on track with your daily tasks, meetings, and work hours.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete each field according to your daily activities and experiences. This form is designed to be straightforward, so answer each section based on your actual daily events.
2.  For the "Morning Tasks" field, type in any tasks you have accomplished during your morning routine, such as exercise, studying, or breakfast.
3.  In the "Exercises" section, select "Yes" if you have engaged in any physical activity during the day; otherwise, select "No".
4.  In the "Meetings" section, select all that apply if you have attended any meetings, conferences, or discussions relevant to your work or personal projects.
5.  In the "Work Hours" field, input your work hours according to the format: "HH:MM" or "HH:MM".
6.  In the "Notes" field, provide any notes or comments about your day, including any successes, challenges, or areas for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Morning Tasks (morning_tasks)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is where you document any activities or tasks you completed during your morning routine, such as exercise, reading, or having breakfast.
*   **Exercise (exercise)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you engaged in any physical activity, like running, yoga, or weightlifting, or select "No" if you didn't.
*   **Meetings (meetings)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply if you attended any meetings or discussions relevant to your work or personal projects.
*   **Work Hours (work_hours)** (`time`, required/optional): Input your work hours in the format "HH:MM" (e.g., "08:00" for 8:00 AM or "17:30" for 5:30 PM).
*   **Notes (notes)** (`note`, required/optional): Write down any notes or comments about your day, including successes, challenges, or areas for improvement.
