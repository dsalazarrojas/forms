# quick_release_steering_wheel_installation_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The quick_release_steering_wheel_installation_checklist form is designed to document the details of a steering wheel installation, ensuring that all necessary information is recorded accurately and thoroughly. This form is specifically intended for steering wheel installations and should not be used for other vehicle-related tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vehicle details section with accurate information about the vehicle being serviced, including the year and make/model of the vehicle.
2. Select the correct type of steering wheel from the options provided.
3. Choose the release type that corresponds with the installation.
4. Add any relevant notes or comments about the installation.
5. Sign and date the form to indicate completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, text, optional): This section is where you will enter the vehicle details, including the year and make/model of the vehicle. Ensure that the information is accurate and legible.
* **Driver License** (`driver_license`, text, optional): This field is for the driver's license number, but in this case, it might be unclear. It is possible that it's for the driver's name or another piece of information related to the driver. Please enter the relevant information accordingly.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): This field is for the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make/Model** (`vehicle_make_model`, text, optional): Enter the make and model of the vehicle being serviced.
* **Vehicle Year Makes** (`vehicle_year_makes`, select_one, optional): Select the make of the vehicle from the options provided.
* **Steering Wheel Type** (`steering_wheel_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the correct type of steering wheel that was installed.
* **Release Type** (`release_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of release that was used during the installation.
* **Installation Notes** (`installation_notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about the installation.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, optional): Sign the form to indicate that you, the customer, have reviewed and agree with the information provided.
* **Mechanic Signature** (`mechanic_signature`, text, optional): Sign the form to indicate that you, the mechanic, have completed the installation and the information is accurate.
* **Completed By** (`completed_by`, text, optional): Enter your name and title to indicate that you completed the form.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was completed.
