**Form Purpose**
The **Daily Bathroom Checklist** form is designed to collect information on the cleanliness and availability of various bathroom fixtures. Its primary use case is for bathroom maintenance or inspection tasks, such as checking if the towels are available for use after a cleaning cycle or if there are essential items like soap and toothbrushes in the bathroom. This form will help users efficiently track and report on the status of each item, facilitating better management and maintenance of the bathroom facilities.

**How To Complete This Form**
To complete this form, navigate through the pages of questions, and for each question:

1.  Select the relevant answer from the provided options (e.g., "Yes" or "No" for availability, or "Clean" or "Dirty" for counter status).
2.  Make sure to complete all questions, but note that some fields are optional.
3.  Use the available options as a guide to make an informed decision about each item's status.
