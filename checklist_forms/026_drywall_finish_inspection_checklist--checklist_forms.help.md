# Drywall Finish Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Drywall Finish Inspection Checklist is a tool designed to assess the quality and condition of drywall finishes in a given location. This form helps inspectors evaluate the drywall finish type, color, quality, and detect any defects or imperfections. It's essential for ensuring that the drywall finish meets the desired standards and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Drywall Finish Inspection Checklist, follow these steps:

1. Select the finish type of the drywall from the options provided (All Purpose, Custom Finish, Texture Finish, Paint, Special Finish).
2. Choose the finish color of the drywall (White, Gray, Brown, Other).
3. Rate the finish quality as Good, Fair, or Poor.
4. Identify the inspection location (e.g., specific room, area, or building).
5. Provide any additional comments about the location.
6. Check any finish defects that are present (Cracks, Bubbles, Holes, Dents, Other).
7. Specify the location of the finish defects (Corner, Edge, Surface).
8. Record the inspection date and time.
9. Enter the inspector's name, company, and title (if applicable).
10. Add any comments or observations about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Finish Type** (`finish_type`): Select the type of drywall finish used (All Purpose, Custom Finish, Texture Finish, Paint, Special Finish).
* **Finish Color** (`finish_color`): Choose the color of the drywall finish (White, Gray, Brown, Other).
* **Finish Quality** (`finish_quality`): Evaluate the quality of the drywall finish as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Inspection Location** (`inspection_location`): Specify the location of the inspection (e.g., specific room, area, or building).
* **Location Comments** (`location_comments`): Add any additional comments about the inspection location.
* **Finish Defects** (`finish_defects`): Check any defects or imperfections present on the drywall finish (Cracks, Bubbles, Holes, Dents, Other).
* **Finish Defect Location** (`finish_defect_location`): Identify the location of the finish defects (Corner, Edge, Surface).
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`): Record the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`): Record the time of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`): Enter the name of the inspector.
* **Inspector Company** (`inspector_company`): Enter the name of the company or organization performing the inspection.
* **Inspector Title** (`inspector_title`): Enter the title or position of the inspector (if applicable).
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or observations about the inspection.
* **Signature** (`signature`): This field is intended for the inspector's signature; however, it is not currently functional.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to provide a comprehensive inspection report.
* Double-check the finish type, color, and quality ratings to ensure they are consistent with the actual conditions of the drywall finish.
* Use the finish defect and location fields to provide detailed and specific information about any defects or imperfections found.
* Complete all fields as much as possible to provide a complete and accurate inspection report.
