# Receiving Temperature Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Receiving Temperature Form is used to document and collect temperature readings from shipments or products received at our facility. This form ensures that accurate and up-to-date information is recorded, enabling us to track and manage received items effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: **temperature_reading**, **reading_date**, and **reading_time**.
2. Provide a brief description of the shipment or product being received in the **shipment_details** field.
3. If applicable, enter the contact information (**email** and **phone**) for the supplier or sender.
4. Add any relevant notes or comments about the shipment or product in the **notes** field.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipment Details**: A brief description of the shipment or product being received, including its contents and any special handling requirements.
* **Temperature Reading**: The actual temperature reading of the shipment or product, as taken by the receiving staff.
* **Reading Date**: The date the temperature reading was taken.
* **Reading Time**: The time the temperature reading was taken.
* **Signature**: A digital signature to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* **Email**: The contact information for the supplier or sender, if applicable.
* **Phone**: The phone number for the supplier or sender, if applicable.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or notes about the shipment or product, such as special instructions or concerns.

## Tips
* Ensure that each field is filled out only once and with the correct information.
* Double-check the accuracy of the temperature reading and other data before submitting the form.
* Keep the **notes** field concise and focused on essential information only.
* If you're unsure about any field or have questions, please reach out to your supervisor or the logistics team.
