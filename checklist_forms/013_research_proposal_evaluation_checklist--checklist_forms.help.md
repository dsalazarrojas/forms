# research-proposal-evaluation-checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The research-proposal-evaluation-checklist is a form designed to evaluate research proposals in a structured and comprehensive manner. It consists of several fields that cover key aspects of a proposal, ensuring that each reviewer has a clear understanding of the proposal's strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the proposal and familiarize yourself with its contents.
2. Fill in the required fields, starting from the first page (Proposal Summary).
3. Provide clear and concise answers to each question or section.
4. Use the options provided for Proposal Evaluation to select the relevant assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Proposal Summary (1)**: A brief overview of the proposal, summarizing its main points and key findings. Please provide a concise and clear description of the proposal.
* **Research Question (2)**: The central research question or hypothesis being addressed in the proposal. Be specific and provide enough context for the proposal to be understood.
* **Research Objectives (3)**: A clear description of the research goals and objectives, including any relevant background information.
* **Background (4)**: A detailed explanation of the background research or context that supports the proposal.
* **Methodology (5)**: A description of the methods and approaches used to address the research question or objectives.
* **Results (6)**: A summary of the results or findings from the research, including any relevant data or statistics.
* **Conclusion (7)**: A final statement summarizing the main takeaways and outcomes from the proposal.
* **Proposal Evaluation (8)**: Select the options that best describe the evaluation of the proposal:
	+ Option 1: The proposal provides a clear and well-structured approach to addressing the research question.
	+ Option 2: The proposal lacks a clear and well-structured approach to addressing the research question.
	+ Option 3: The proposal provides a comprehensive and thorough review of the literature.
	+ Option 4: The proposal lacks a comprehensive and thorough review of the literature.
* **Additional Questions (9)**: Any additional comments or questions that you may have regarding the proposal.
* **Reviewer Comments (10)**: Your thoughts and feedback on the proposal, including any suggestions or recommendations.

## Tips
* Please answer each question clearly and concisely.
* Use the options provided for Proposal Evaluation to select the most accurate assessment.
* Provide specific examples or references to support your answers where possible.
* Review the entire form before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled in.
