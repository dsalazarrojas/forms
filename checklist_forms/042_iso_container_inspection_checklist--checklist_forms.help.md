# iso_container_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to record the inspection details for an ISO container. It's a tool to help ensure that the shipping and inspection process is done efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields to provide accurate information.
2. Provide additional details when necessary using the optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping Company** (`Shipping Company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company that is shipping the ISO container.
* **Iso Standards** (`Iso Standards`, text, optional): Describe the ISO standards that apply to this shipment.
* **Date Of Issue** (`Date Of Issue`, date, required): Enter the date when the inspection is initiated.
* **Condition** (`Condition`, note, optional): Add any notes about the condition of the container.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about the container.
* **Date Of Completion** (`Date Of Completion`, date, required): Enter the date when the inspection is completed.
* **Shipping Method** (`Shipping Method`, select_one, optional): Select the method used for shipping (Air, Land, Sea).
* **Ship From** (`Ship From`, select_multiple, required): Select the warehouses where the container is shipped from.
* **Ship To** (`Ship To`, select_multiple, required): Select the warehouses where the container is shipped to.
* **Date Of Arrival** (`Date Of Arrival`, date, optional): Enter the date of arrival.
* **Date Of Return** (`Date Of Return`, date, optional): Enter the date of return.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, text, required): Add a signature to confirm the inspection.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Remarks** (`Remarks`, note, optional): Add any additional remarks about the shipment.
* **Remarks 2** (`Remarks 2`, note, optional): Add any additional remarks about the shipment.
* **Ship To Address** (`Ship To Address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the ship-to location.
* **Ship From Address** (`Ship From Address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the ship-from location.
* **Ship To Contact** (`Ship To Contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person at the ship-to location.
* **Ship From Contact** (`Ship From Contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person at the ship-from location.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the shipment.
* **Ship From Email** (`Ship From Email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the contact person at the ship-from location.
* **Ship From Phone** (`Ship From Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person at the ship-from location.
* **Ship From Website** (`Ship From Website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the ship-from location.
* **Ship From Other** (`Ship From Other`, text, optional): Enter any other information about the ship-from location.
