# Topical Medication Competency Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Topical Medication Competency Checklist is a form used to assess a healthcare professional's knowledge and understanding of topical medications. This form is designed to evaluate a user's competence in administering topical medications correctly, identifying potential side effects, and adhering to contraindications.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please review each question and select the answer that accurately reflects your knowledge and actions.
- For select type questions (Assessment Questions, Medication Names, Correct Administration Techniques, Common Side Effects, Allergies, Special Contradictions, and Observation and Monitoring), choose the response that best matches your expertise.
- For the Documentation field, provide any relevant notes or comments that you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Assessment Questions** (`assessment_questions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This section asks you to confirm your knowledge on various aspects of topical medication administration, such as dosage, contraindications, and potential side effects.
- **Medication Names** (`medication_names`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the name of the specific topical medication you are familiar with from the options provided.
- **Correct Administration Techniques** (`correct_administration_techniques`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate the correct ways to administer topical medications, such as applying creams or ointments to specific skin areas.
- **Common Side Effects** (`common_side_effects`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the known side effects of topical medications, such as skin irritation or allergic reactions.
- **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Confirm if you are aware of any allergies associated with topical medications.
- **Special Contradictions** (`special_contraindications`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Identify any special conditions or situations that may contraindicate the use of topical medications.
- **Observation and Monitoring** (`observation_and_monitoring`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate how you would observe and monitor patients receiving topical medications for potential side effects or issues.
- **Documentation** (`documentation`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments about your knowledge or administration of topical medications.
