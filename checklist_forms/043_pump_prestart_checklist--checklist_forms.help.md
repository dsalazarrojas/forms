# Pump Prestart Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pump Prestart Checklist is a form designed to collect information about the state of a pump before starting it. This form helps ensure that the pump is properly prepared and safe to start, reducing the risk of accidents and downtime.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the safety check question to verify that the pump is safe to start.
2. Choose the status of the pump (Running or Not Running).
3. Enter the expected start time of the pump.
4. Enter the actual start time of the pump.
5. Select whether the pump is securely latched or not.
6. Select whether the pump is preheated or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Safety Check**: This field is used to verify that the pump is safe to start. Answer this question with a "yes" or "no".
* **Pump Operational**: Choose whether the pump is currently Running or Not Running.
* **Expected Start Time**: Enter the time at which the pump is expected to start. This can be in any format accepted by your device or browser.
* **Actual Start Time**: Enter the time at which the pump actually started. This can be in any format accepted by your device or browser.
* **Pre Start Checklist**: This field is not used in the current form. Please disregard this field.
* **Pump Is Securely Latched**: Select whether the pump is securely latched or not. This is a critical safety feature that ensures the pump is properly attached to its surroundings.
* **Pump Is Preheated**: Select whether the pump is preheated or not. This is important for ensuring the pump is ready to operate at optimal performance.
