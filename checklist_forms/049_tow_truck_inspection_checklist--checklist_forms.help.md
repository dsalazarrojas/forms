# Tow Truck Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tow Truck Inspection Checklist is a form used to collect information about the details of a tow truck, including its driver, vehicle condition, equipment, and inspection results. This form is essential for ensuring that the truck is in good working condition and meets the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the details about the tow truck, including its vehicle type, year, and any specific equipment assigned to it.
2. Provide information about the driver, such as their name and contact information (if applicable).
3. Check the vehicle's condition and select any relevant options from the list of equipment and inspection results.
4. Take notes if you have any additional comments or observations.
5. Assign a tool or equipment to the truck, if necessary.
6. Enter the form's ID for tracking purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Truck Details** (`truck_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the tow truck, including its make, model, and any specific features or modifications.
* **Driver Info** (`driver_info`, email, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the driver, if applicable.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, text, optional): Describe the overall condition of the vehicle, including any issues or concerns.
* **Equipment List** (`equipment_list`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to indicate the type of equipment available on the truck.
* **Vehicle Inspection** (`vehicle_inspection`, select_multiple, optional): Check multiple options to indicate the results of the vehicle's inspection.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Take notes or comments about the vehicle's condition or any issues identified during the inspection.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to indicate the type of vehicle (e.g., flatbed, tow truck, etc.).
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tool or equipment assigned to the truck.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique ID of the form for tracking purposes.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to indicate the person who created the form.
