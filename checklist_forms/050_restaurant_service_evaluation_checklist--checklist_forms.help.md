# Restaurant Service Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers after a visit to a restaurant or other service location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions as accurately and completely as possible.
2. Be honest and provide constructive feedback.
3. Use the provided options as guidance for your answers, but feel free to provide additional comments if you wish.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Location Visited** (`location_visited`, text, required: false): Please describe the location you visited (e.g. address, city, state).
* **Date Visited** (`date_visited`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your visit (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Staff Member Name** (`staff_member_name`, text, required: false): If you were assisted by a staff member, please enter their name.
* **Overall Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required: false): Select a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your overall impression of the service.
* **Food Quality** (`food_quality`, number, required: false): Rate the quality of the food on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, number, required: false): Rate the quality of the service on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, number, required: false): Rate the cleanliness of the location on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Atmosphere** (`atmosphere`, number, required: false): Rate the atmosphere of the location on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Value Added** (`value_added`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): This field is not currently being used, but you can enter any additional comments or information if you wish.
