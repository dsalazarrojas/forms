# Quantitative Research Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quantitative Research Checklist is a form designed to help researchers and project managers gather and organize information about quantitative research projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go through each page and fill in the relevant information for your project.
2. For select_multiple, select all the applicable options for each question.
3. For select_one, choose the most relevant option for each question.
4. For text fields, enter your answers in the corresponding fields.
5. Review your answers carefully to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information (id: 1)
* **General Information** (`text`, optional): This field is for entering any general information about your project. You can enter text to describe your project's background, goals, or any other relevant details.

### Project Readiness (id: 2)
* **Project Readiness** (`select_multiple`, required): Please indicate if your project is ready to start, including factors like data collection methods, study design, and other essential components.

### Timeline (id: 3)
* **Timeline** (`date`, optional): Enter the expected start and end dates for your project.

### Study Steps (id: 4)
* **Study Steps** (`select_one`, required): Select the most relevant study steps for your project, such as Key Study Steps, Study Design, or Study Methods.

### Data Collection (id: 5)
* **Data Collection** (`select_one`, required): Choose the most suitable data collection method for your project, either Manual Collection or Automated Collection.

### Data Review (id: 6)
* **Data Review** (`select_one`, required): Select the approach you will use for data review, either Manual Review or Automated Review.

### Workflows (id: 7)
* **Workflows** (`text`, optional): This field is for entering any information about your project's workflows or procedures.

### study_steps_1 (id: 8)
* **study_steps_1** (`text`, optional): This field is for entering any additional study steps or procedures not covered by the previous question.

### data_collection_1 (id: 9)
* **data_collection_1** (`text`, optional): This field is for entering additional information about data collection methods or procedures.

### data_review_1 (id: 10)
* **data_review_1** (`text`, optional): This field is for entering any additional information about data review procedures.

### workflows_1 (id: 11)
* **workflows_1** (`text`, optional): This field is for entering any additional workflows or procedures related to your project.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have questions, contact form@gic.mx.
