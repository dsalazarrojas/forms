# Semi Truck Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Semi Truck Inspection Form is used to record and document the inspection of a semi truck's condition, including its mechanical and operational aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the relevant information for the semi truck being inspected.
2. Ensure you have all the necessary documentation or verification from external sources before completing fields that require them.
3. Use the provided options for select fields to ensure accuracy and completeness.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Inspection Date
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required): The date and time of the inspection.
*   Enter the date and time in the standard date format (e.g., 2024-02-20 08:00).

### Driver Name
*   **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, optional): The name of the person driving the semi truck.
*   Enter the driver's name as per their official documentation or identification.

### Truck Number
*   **Truck Number** (`truck_number`, text, optional): The unique identification number of the semi truck.
*   Enter the number as per the truck's official documentation or manufacturer's tag.

### Truck Type
*   **Truck Type** (`truck_type`, select one, optional): The type of semi truck (e.g., Semi Truck, Tractor, Tanker).
*   Select the option that best describes the semi truck's type.

### Engine Number
*   **Engine Number** (`engine_number`, text, optional): The engine serial number of the semi truck.
*   Enter the number as per the engine's official documentation or manufacturer's tag.

### VIN
*   **VIN** (`vin`, text, optional): The vehicle identification number of the semi truck.
*   Enter the number as per the vehicle's official documentation or manufacturer's tag.

### Driver License Number
*   **Driver License Number** (`driver_license_number`, text, optional): The driver's official identification number.
*   Enter the number as per the driver's official documentation or identification.

### Vehicle Weight
*   **Vehicle Weight** (`vehicle_weight`, number, optional): The weight of the semi truck.
*   Enter the weight in the standard unit of measurement (e.g., kg or lbs).

### Tire Pressure
*   **Tire Pressure** (`tire_pressure`, number, optional): The pressure of the semi truck's tires.
*   Enter the pressure in the standard unit of measurement (e.g., PSI or bar).
