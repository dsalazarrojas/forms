# Tea Party Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help hosts gather essential information for a successful tea party. It covers party details, food and drink preferences, decorations, schedule, budget, and guest information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question carefully and accurately. If you're unsure about any question, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Party Details** (1, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your tea party, including the theme, date, and time.
- **Food And Drinks** (2, text, optional): List the food and drinks you plan to serve at your tea party.
- **Party Food** (3, text, optional): Describe the food options you'll have at your tea party.
- **Party Drinks** (4, text, optional): List the drinks you'll serve at your tea party.
- **Party Decorations** (5, text, optional): Describe the decorations you plan to use for your tea party.
- **Party Favors** (6, text, optional): List any party favors you'll give to your guests.
- **Party Invitations** (7, text, optional): Describe the invitation process for your tea party.
- **Party Activities** (8, text, optional): List any activities or games you plan to include in your tea party.
- **Party Schedule** (9, text, optional): Create a schedule for your tea party, including start and end times.
- **Party Budget** (10, number, optional): Set a budget for your tea party, including costs for food, drinks, and decorations.
- **Food Budget** (11, number, optional): Allocate a budget for food costs.
- **Decoration Budget** (12, number, optional): Set a budget for decoration costs.
- **Activity Budget** (13, number, optional): Allocate a budget for activity costs.
- **Total Budget** (14, number, optional): Calculate the total budget for your tea party.
- **Actual Budget** (15, number, optional): Record the actual expenses for your tea party.
- **Contact Information** (16, text, optional): Provide contact information, such as your phone number and email, in case guests need to reach you.
- **Host And Guests** (17, select_multiple, optional): Select the host and guests attending the party.
- **Guest Information** (18, text, optional): List the names and contact information of your guests.
- **Guest 1** (19, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your first guest.
- **Guest 2** (20, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your second guest.
- **Guest 3** (21, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your third guest.
- **Guest 4** (22, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your fourth guest.
- **Guest 5** (23, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your fifth guest.
- **Guest 6** (24, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your sixth guest.
- **Guest 7** (25, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your seventh guest.
