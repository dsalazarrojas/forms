# Daily Vehicle Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a vehicle's condition after a daily inspection, enabling the tracking of its status and providing a summary of the inspection findings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct answers for each field based on your observations during the inspection.
2. Provide detailed comments for any issues found during the inspection in the "Comments" field.
3. Choose the vehicle's status from the options provided in the "Vehicle Status" field.
4. Indicate the maintenance status of the vehicle in the "Maintenance Status" field.
5. Select the condition of the vehicle from the options provided in the "Vehicle Condition" field.
6. Add any relevant maintenance notes in the "Maintenance Notes" field.
7. Sign the "Driver Signature" field with your name as proof of completion.
8. You can upload or add a link to a photo of the vehicle's current state if needed, but it's not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`):
	+ This field is for entering the vehicle's details such as make, model, and registration number.
* **Inspection Items** (`inspection_items`):
	+ Select the issues found during the inspection. This could include anything from minor scratches to major mechanical issues.
* **Comments** (`comments`):
	+ Provide detailed comments for any issues found during the inspection.
* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`):
	+ Select the current status of the vehicle. Options include:
		+ Good
		+ Needs Maintenance
		+ Do Not Operate
* **Maintenance Status** (`maintenance_status`):
	+ Indicate the maintenance status of the vehicle. Options include:
		+ Needs Maintenance
		+ Completed Maintenance
		+ No Maintenance Needed
* **Driver Details** (`driver_details`):
	+ Enter your driver details such as name, contact number, and driver ID.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`):
	+ Select the condition of the vehicle from the options provided.
		+ Excellent
		+ Good
		+ Fair
		+ Poor
		+ Needs Repair
* **Maintenance Notes** (`maintenance_notes`):
	+ Add any relevant maintenance notes for the vehicle.
* **Driver Signature** (`driver_signature`):
	+ Sign this field with your name as proof of completion.
* **Vehicle Image** (`vehicle_image`):
	+ Upload or add a link to a photo of the vehicle's current state.
