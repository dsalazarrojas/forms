# travel_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The travel_checklist form is designed to collect and document travel information, including pre-trip planning, flight details, travel documents, and other relevant data. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is captured and recorded for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Select the options relevant to your travel needs for each field.
3. Fill out the form accordingly, and make sure to enter accurate and clear information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre Trip Planning (1, select_multiple)**: Select Yes or No to indicate if pre-trip planning has been done.
* **flight_details (2, select_multiple)**: Select Yes or No to indicate if flight details are available.
* **Travel Documents (3, select_multiple)**: Select Yes or No to indicate if travel documents are required or available.
* **Flight Numbers (4, select_multiple)**: Select Yes or No to indicate if flight numbers are available.
* **Flight Dates (5, select_multiple)**: Select Yes or No to indicate if flight dates are available.
* **Travel Agency Info (6, select_multiple)**: Select Yes or No to indicate if travel agency information is available.
* **Contact Info (7, select_multiple)**: Select Yes or No to indicate if contact information is available.
* **Travel Type (8, select_multiple)**: Select Yes or No to indicate if travel type is available.
* **travel_dates_time (9, select_multiple)**: Select Yes or No to indicate if travel dates and time are available.
* **travel_destination (10, text)**: Enter the destination of your travel.
* **departure_port (11, text)**: Enter the departure port or airport code.
* **flight_number (12, text)**: Enter the flight number.
* **flight_number1 (13, text)**: Enter an alternative flight number (if applicable).
* **travel_time (14, text)**: Enter the travel time.
* **travel_date (15, date)**: Enter the travel date.
* **travel_date1 (16, date)**: Enter an alternative travel date (if applicable).
* **travel_date2 (17, date)**: Enter another travel date (if applicable).
* **travel_date3 (18, date)**: Enter another travel date (if applicable).
* **travel_time1 (19, time)**: Enter the travel time for your first destination.
* **travel_time2 (20, time)**: Enter the travel time for your second destination (if applicable).
* **travel_time3 (21, time)**: Enter the travel time for your third destination (if applicable).
* **travel_note (22, note)**: Enter any additional travel notes or comments.
* **travel_note1 (23, note)**: Enter an alternative travel note (if applicable).
* **travel_note2 (24, note)**: Enter another travel note (if applicable).
* **Assigned To (25, select_one)**: Select Yes or No to indicate if the travel is assigned to someone else.
