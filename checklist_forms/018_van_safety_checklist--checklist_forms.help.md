# Van Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Van Safety Checklist is a set of forms designed to assess your van safety practices and help ensure safe driving habits. The forms are divided into four categories: Van Safety Checklist Form 1, Van Safety Checklist Form 2, and Van Safety Checklist Form 3, which cover various aspects of van usage such as commuting, family transportation, and cargo transport. By completing these forms, you will be able to evaluate your van safety practices and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Choose the relevant form based on your van usage:
	* Van Safety Checklist Form 1: For vans used for commuting or family transportation.
	* Van Safety Checklist Form 2: For vans used for commuting.
	* Van Safety Checklist Form 3: For vans used for cargo transport.
2. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, selecting the relevant options for each field.
3. Review your answers carefully to ensure you are providing accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Van Safety Checklist Form 1
* **What type of van do you have?** (`van_safety_checklist_1_form`): Please enter the type of van you are using.
* **How often is the van driven?** (`van_safety_checklist_1_frequency_of_use`): Select one of the following options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly.
* **What is the van used for?** (`van_safety_checklist_1_usage`): Select all that apply: Commuting to work, Family transportation, Cargo transport.
* **Are all passengers wearing seatbelts while traveling in the van?** (`van_safety_checklist_1_seatbelts`): Select True or False.
* **Has the van undergone regular maintenance (oil changes, tire rotations, etc.)?** (`van_safety_checklist_1_maintenance`): Select True or False.
* **What is your driving experience with vans?** (`van_safety_checklist_1_driving_experience`): Select all that apply: Less than 1 year, 1-5 years, More than 5 years.
* **How many passengers can fit in the van comfortably?** (`van_safety_checklist_1_passengers`): Select all that apply: Less than 5, 5-10, More than 10.
* **Is the van equipped with a first aid kit?** (`van_safety_checklist_1_first_aid_kit`): Select True or False.
* **Have you received training on van safety and usage?** (`van_safety_checklist_1_training`): Select True or False.
* **What is your estimated annual mileage?** (`van_safety_checklist_1_mileage`): Select all that apply: Less than 10,000 miles, 10,000-20,000 miles, More than 20,000 miles.
* **Does the van have a functioning emergency exit?** (`van_safety_checklist_1_emergency_exit`): Select True or False.
* **Do you follow recommended van maintenance schedules?** (`van_safety_checklist_1_maintenance_schedule`): Select True or False.
