# User Testing Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from users who participate in testing sessions. It helps to document their experience, testing environment, and objectives, and collects feedback and comments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand what each field means.
2. Complete each field based on your actual experience and knowledge.
3. Be as thorough as possible, as your input will help to improve the product or application being tested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Tester Information** (`user_tester_information`): Please provide your name, title, and any other relevant information that can help us to understand your background and expertise in the area being tested.
* **User Testing Environment** (`user_testing_environment`): Describe your testing environment, including the device, browser, or equipment used during the test session.
* **Testing Session Objectives** (`testing_session_objectives`): Outline the goals and objectives of the testing session, including any specific requirements or outcomes you were looking for.
* **Test Plan** (`test_plan`): Describe the test plan you used during the testing session, including any preparation or documentation that was done prior to the test.
* **User Testing Session** (`user_testing_session`): Provide a narrative summary of the test session, including any notable events, issues, or challenges that arose.
* **Tester Feedback** (`tester_feedback`): Share your thoughts and opinions about the testing experience, including any suggestions or recommendations for improvement.
* **Tester Comments** (`tester_comments`): Add any additional comments or observations that are not captured in the other fields.
* **Testing Session Notes** (`testing_session_notes`): Record any important notes or events that occurred during the test session.
* **Test Plan Update** (`test_plan_update`): Choose whether the test session was a **Testing Session** or a **Test Plan** update. 

# Tips

* Be as detailed and thorough as possible when completing each field.
* Use your actual experience and knowledge to inform your responses.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or contact the testing team.
* Your input is valuable and will help to improve the product or application being tested.
