# Job Safety Analysis Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather and document essential job safety information, including risks, control measures, and review details. It is meant to be filled out for each job or task to ensure a thorough risk assessment and to provide a framework for ongoing safety reviews.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Job Safety Analysis Form Template**: This form serves as a starting point for your job safety analysis. Please fill out each section as per the instructions below.
2. **Job Safety Risk**: Describe the potential hazards or risks associated with the job or task.
3. **Job Safety Control Measures**: Detail the actions taken to mitigate or control the identified risks.
4. **Job Safety Frequency**: Specify how often the job or task is performed or reviewed.
5. **Job Safety Impact**: Indicate the potential impact or consequence of not performing the job or task safety.
6. **Job Safety Preventative Action**: Outline any preventative actions taken to ensure job safety.
7. **Job Safety Review Date**: Enter the date on which the review of this job or task will take place.
8. **Job Safety Review Frequency**: Select the frequency at which this job or task will be reviewed.
9. **Job Safety Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for reviewing this job or task.
10. **Job Safety Reviewer Phone**: Enter the contact information of the person responsible for reviewing this job or task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Safety Analysis Form Template** (text): A brief title for the analysis, which should be unique for each job or task.
* **Job Safety Risk** (text): Describe any hazards or risks associated with the job or task.
* **Job Safety Control Measures** (text): List any actions taken or planned to mitigate or control the identified risks.
* **Job Safety Frequency** (text): Specify the frequency at which the job or task is performed or reviewed.
* **Job Safety Impact** (text): Indicate the potential impact or consequence of not performing the job or task safety.
* **Job Safety Preventative Action** (text): Outline any preventative actions taken to ensure job safety.
* **Job Safety Review Date** (date): Enter the date on which the review of this job or task will take place.
* **Job Safety Review Frequency** (select_one): Select how often this job or task will be reviewed (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Job Safety Reviewer Name** (text): Enter the name of the person responsible for reviewing this job or task.
* **Job Safety Reviewer Phone** (text): Enter the contact information of the person responsible for reviewing this job or task.

## Tips
- Be as specific and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
- Use clear and concise language in your descriptions.
- Ensure that all fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Review and update this form regularly to reflect any changes in the job or task.
