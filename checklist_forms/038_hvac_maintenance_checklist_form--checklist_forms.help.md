# HVAC Maintenance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help maintenance personnel and stakeholders track and record HVAC maintenance activities, including tasks, locations, types, and due dates. By using this form, you can:

* Record and document maintenance activities
* Track progress and stay organized
* Improve communication and collaboration among team members

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "General Tasks" field and select the specific tasks that need to be performed.
2. In the "Filter By Location" field, select the location of the equipment that requires maintenance.
3. In the "Filter By Type" field, select the type of maintenance that needs to be performed (Heating, Ventilation, or Air-Conditioning).
4. Enter the "Date Last Maintained" if applicable.
5. Enter the "Next Maintenance Due Date" if applicable.
6. In the "Comments" field, provide any comments or notes regarding the maintenance activity.
7. If you are signing off on the maintenance, enter the "Signature Date" and provide your signature in the "Signature" field.
8. If you are assigning this maintenance activity to someone else, enter their name and contact information in the "Assignee Name," "Assignee Email," and "Assignee Phone" fields.
9. If you are reviewing or approving this maintenance activity, enter your name and contact information in the "Reviewer Name," "Reviewer Email," "Reviewer Phone," and "Reviewer Comments" fields.
10. Enter any comments or approvals in the "Reviewer Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Tasks** (`general_tasks`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the specific tasks that need to be performed during the maintenance activity.
* **Filter By Location** (`filter_by_location`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select the location of the equipment that requires maintenance.
* **Filter By Type** (`filter_by_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the type of maintenance that needs to be performed (Heating, Ventilation, or Air-Conditioning).
* **Date Last Maintained** (`date_last_maintained`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date when the equipment was last maintained.
* **Next Maintenance Due Date** (`next_maintenance_due_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date when the next maintenance is due.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any comments or notes regarding the maintenance activity.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date when you are signing off on the maintenance activity.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required):
	+ Provide your signature.
* **Assignee Name** (`assignee_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name and contact information of the person assigned to perform the maintenance activity.
* **Assignee Email** (`assignee_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email of the person assigned to perform the maintenance activity.
* **Assignee Phone** (`assignee_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of the person assigned to perform the maintenance activity.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name and contact information of the person reviewing the maintenance activity.
* **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email of the person reviewing the maintenance activity.
* **Reviewer Phone** (`reviewer_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of the person reviewing the maintenance activity.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any comments or approvals from the reviewer.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name and contact information of the person approving the maintenance activity.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email of the person approving the maintenance activity.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of the person approving the maintenance activity.
* **Approver Comments** (`approver_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any comments or approvals from the approver.
* **Approver Signature Date** (`approver_signature_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date when you are approving the maintenance activity.
* **Approver Signature** (`approver_signature`, text, optional):
	+ Provide your signature.
* **Reviewer Signature Date** (`reviewer_signature_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date when you are reviewing the maintenance activity.
* **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, text, optional):
	+ Provide your signature.
