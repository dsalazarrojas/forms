# vessel_navigation_bridge_check_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vessel_navigation_bridge_check_form is a critical tool for ensuring the safety of the vessel and its crew during navigation operations. The form is designed to be completed by the crew member on the navigation bridge, and it provides a quick and easy way to report the status of various systems on the bridge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct status of each system on the bridge.
2. Enter any additional comments or notes about the systems' status.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Is Bridge Station Keeping** (`vessel_status`, select_multiple, required): This field asks if the vessel is currently keeping station. Select "Yes" if the vessel is maintaining its position, or "No" if it is not.
* **Navigation Station** (`vessel_helm`, text, optional): Enter the location of the navigation station on the bridge, such as "helm" or "chart table".
* **Main Engine** (`vessel_engine`, text, optional): Enter the status of the main engine, such as "running", "off", or "understand".
* **Generator** (`vessel_generator`, text, optional): Enter the status of the generator, such as "running", "off", or "understand".
* **Bilge Pump** (`vessel_bilge_pump`, text, optional): Enter the status of the bilge pump, such as "running", "off", or "understand".
* **Additional Comments** (not a field on the form, but a best-effort interpretation): You may enter any additional comments or notes about the systems' status in this text field, e.g., "engine is overheating" or "generator is on standby".

## Tips
* Ensure you accurately report the status of each system on the bridge.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you are unsure about the status of a system, consult with the bridge crew or operations team before submitting the form.
