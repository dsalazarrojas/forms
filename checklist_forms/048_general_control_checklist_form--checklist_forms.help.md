# General Control Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Control Checklist Form is a tool used to gather information about a facility's general control systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the form with the requested information about your facility.
3. Be as accurate as possible when providing information about the facility's control systems.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <label> Facility Name</label> (`facility_name`, `text`, required: false)
This field is for entering the name of your facility.

### <label> Facility Location</label> (`facility_location`, `text`, required: false)
This field is for entering the location of your facility.

### <label> Facility Type</label> (`facility_type`, `select_one`, options: Manufacturing, Warehouse, Office, required: false)
This field is for selecting the type of facility you are working with. Select one of the following options: Manufacturing, Warehouse, or Office.

### <label> Control Type</label> (`control_type`, `select_multiple`, options: Mechanical, Electrical, Pneumatic, required: false)
This field is for selecting the type of control systems your facility has. Select one or more of the following options: Mechanical, Electrical, or Pneumatic.

### <label> Control Status</label> (`control_status`, `select_one`, options: Active, Inactive, Unknown, required: false)
This field is for selecting the status of your facility's control systems. Select one of the following options: Active, Inactive, or Unknown.

### <label> Follow-up Date</label> (`follow_up_date`, `date`, required: false)
This field is for entering a date for follow-up checks on your facility's control systems.

### <label> Follow-up Time</label> (`follow_up_time`, `time`, required: false)
This field is for entering a time for follow-up checks on your facility's control systems.

### <label> Follow-up Note</label> (`follow_up_note`, `note`, required: false)
This field is for adding any additional notes or comments about follow-up checks on your facility's control systems.

### <label> Facility Email</label> (`facility_email`, `email`, required: false)
This field is for entering the email address of your facility.

### <label> Facility Phone</label> (`facility_phone`, `text`, required: false)
This field is for entering the phone number of your facility.
