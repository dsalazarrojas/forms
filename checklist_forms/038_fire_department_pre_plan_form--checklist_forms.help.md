# Fire Department Pre Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Department Pre Plan Form is a critical document used by the fire department to gather information about your building's fire safety features and systems. This form helps them prepare for potential fire emergencies and respond effectively in case of an alarm.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are authorized to complete it.
2. Answer all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. For optional fields, provide any relevant information that will aid in the fire department's response.
4. Be detailed and accurate in your responses, as they will guide the fire department's actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Electrical Panel** (electrical_panel, select_one, required): Select the electrical panel from which the fire department will draw power in case of an emergency.
* **Fire Extinguishers** (fire_extinguishers, select_multiple, optional): List the fire extinguishers available in the building.
* **Fire Hydrant** (fire_hydrant, select_one, required): Identify the fire hydrant location that the fire department will use in case of an emergency.
* **Fire Alarm System** (fire_alarm_system, select_one, required): Specify the fire alarm system type that the building uses.
* **Fire Sprinkler System** (fire_sprinkler_system, select_one, required): Identify the fire sprinkler system type that the building uses.
* **Fire Sprinkler Flow** (fire_sprinkler_flow, number, optional): Provide the flow of the fire sprinkler system.
* **Fire Alarm Location** (fire_alarm_location, text, optional): Specify the location of the fire alarm.
* **Fire Extinguisher Location** (fire_extinguisher_location, text, optional): Note the location of fire extinguishers.
* **Fire Hydrant Location** (fire_hydrant_location, text, optional): Identify the location of the fire hydrant.
* **Building Details** (building_details, text, optional): Provide any additional building-specific information relevant to the fire department's response.
* **Contact Person** (contact_person, text, optional): Specify the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, optional): Provide the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email, text, optional): Provide the contact person's email address.
