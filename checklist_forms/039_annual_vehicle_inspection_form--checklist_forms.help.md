# Annual Vehicle Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Vehicle Inspection Form is a checklist used by mechanics or inspectors to record the condition of a vehicle's various components during a routine inspection. This form is used to ensure that the vehicle meets safety and regulatory standards, and to identify any needed repairs or maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the inspection date and record the date of the inspection.
2. Enter the name and certification details of the inspector.
3. Record the vehicle owner's name and vehicle registration number.
4. Provide the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) and vehicle year.
5. Enter the vehicle's make and model.
6. Record the current mileage and previous inspection mileage.
7. Check the condition of each vehicle system and component, using the provided options.
8. Enter any repairs or maintenance recommended.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date**: Record the date of the inspection. This date is used to track when the inspection was performed.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter the full name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
* **Inspector Certification Number**: Enter the certification or license number of the inspector.
* **Vehicle Owner Name**: Enter the name of the vehicle owner or registered user.
* **Vehicle Registration Number**: Enter the vehicle's license plate or registration ID.
* **Vehicle VIN**: Enter the 17-character Vehicle Identification Number.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of manufacture.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the vehicle's make (e.g. Honda, Ford, etc.).
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the vehicle's model name.
* **Current Mileage**: Enter the current odometer reading.
* **Previous Inspection Mileage**: Enter the mileage reading from the last recorded inspection (if applicable).
* **Brake System - Pads and Rotors**: Check the condition of the brake system. Select 'Pass - good condition' if the brakes are functioning properly, 'Pass - adequate condition' if they are worn but still functional, 'Fail - replacement needed' if they are damaged or worn out, or 'Fail - unsafe' if they are a hazard.
* **Tire Condition and Tread**: Check the condition and tread depth of the tires. Select 'Pass - good condition' if the tires are in good condition, 'Pass - adequate condition' if they are worn but still functional, 'Fail - replacement needed' if they are damaged or worn out, or 'Fail - unsafe' if they are a hazard.
* **Windshield Wipers**: Check the condition of the windscreen wipers. Select 'Pass - functional' if they are working properly, 'Pass - needs replacement soon' if they are worn out but still functional, or 'Fail - needs replacement' if they are not working.
* **Lights - Headlights, Taillights, Signals**: Check the proper functioning of all lights. Select 'Pass - all functional' if all lights are working properly, 'Pass - minor issues' if there are minor issues, or 'Fail - repairs needed' if there are more serious problems.
* **Horn**: Check the function of the horn. Select 'Pass - functional' if the horn is working properly, or 'Fail - not working' if it is not.
* **Steering System**: Check the condition of the steering system. Select 'Pass - good condition' if the system is functioning properly, 'Pass - acceptable' if it is worn but still functional, or 'Fail - repairs needed' if it is damaged or worn out.
* **Suspension and Shocks**: Check the condition of the suspension and shocks. Select 'Pass - good condition' if they are functioning properly, 'Pass - acceptable' if they are worn but still functional, or 'Fail - repairs needed' if they are damaged or worn out.
* **Exhaust System**: Check the condition of the muffler and exhaust pipes. Select 'Pass - good condition' if they are functioning properly, 'Pass - acceptable' if they are worn but still functional, or 'Fail - repairs needed' if they are damaged or worn out.
* **Emissions Test**: Check the vehicle's emissions compliance. Select 'Pass - compliant' if the vehicle meets emissions standards, 'Conditional pass' if there are minor issues, 'Fail - non-compliant' if the vehicle does not meet standards, or 'Exempt' if the vehicle is exempt from emissions testing.
* **Fuel System and Tank**: Check for any leaks in the fuel system and fuel tank. Select 'Pass - no leaks' if there are no leaks, 'Pass - acceptable' if there are minor issues, or 'Fail - repairs needed' if there are more serious problems.
* **Seatbelts and Safety Features**: Check the condition and function of the seatbelts and airbags. Select 'Pass - functional' if they are working properly, 'Pass - needs adjustment' if they need adjustment, or 'Fail - repairs needed' if they are damaged or not functioning.
* **Overall Vehicle Safety Rating**: Assess the overall safety of the vehicle. Select 'Pass - safe to operate' if the vehicle is safe, 'Conditional pass - minor repairs' if there are minor issues, or 'Fail - unsafe to operate' if the vehicle is a hazard.
* **Repairs Recommended**: Enter any recommended repairs or maintenance.
* **Next Inspection Due Date**: Enter the date for the next inspection, if applicable. 

## Tips
* Ensure that you have all the necessary information and tools before starting the inspection.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and any specific requirements.
* Use the options provided to accurately assess the condition of each vehicle system and component.
* Enter any recommended repairs or maintenance.
* Date the next inspection due date, if applicable.
* Submit the form once complete, or save for later reference.
