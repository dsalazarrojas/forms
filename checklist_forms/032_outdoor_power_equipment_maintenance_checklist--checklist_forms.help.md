# Outdoor Power Equipment Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track and manage the maintenance of outdoor power equipment, such as lawn mowers, trimmers, and other outdoor power equipment. It helps ensure that regular checks and servicing are performed to prevent breakdowns and extend the lifespan of the equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by selecting the type of equipment you are maintaining, such as Mower, Trimmer, or Other.
2. Enter the last inspection date, which is not required but can be useful for tracking historical data.
3. If required, enter the next inspection due date.
4. Select the maintenance needed for the equipment, such as Oil, Filter, Blade, or Chain.
5. Indicate the current status of the maintenance, whether it's None, Completed, or Scheduled.
6. Add any comments or notes related to the maintenance.
7. Describe the equipment condition, if necessary.
8. Provide information on any service needed.
9. Enter the last maintenance cost, if applicable.
10. Enter the date of the last maintenance, if applicable.
11. Enter the name of the technician who performed the last maintenance.
12. Enter the facility or location where the equipment is being maintained.
13. Enter the location where the equipment is being kept.
14. Add any remarks or comments.
15. Enter the equipment number or identifier.
16. Enter the equipment model number.
17. Enter the equipment serial number.
18. Enter the manufacturer of the equipment.
19. Enter the year the equipment was purchased.
20. Enter the name of the person who performed the last inspection.
21. Enter the name of the person who is scheduled to perform the next inspection.
22. Enter the date by which maintenance is due.
23. Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Item** (`maintenance_item`): Enter the specific maintenance task or issue with the equipment. This could be related to the selected maintenance needed, or any other notes you'd like to add.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`): Select the type of equipment being maintained, such as Mower, Trimmer, or Other.
* **Last Inspection Date** (`last_inspection_date`): Enter the date of the last inspection, if applicable.
* **Next Inspection Due Date** (`next_inspection_due_date`): Enter the date by which the next inspection is due, if required.
* **Maintenance Needed** (`maintenance_needed`): Select the specific maintenance required, such as Oil, Filter, Blade, or Chain.
* **Maintenance Status** (`maintenance_status`): Indicate the current status of the maintenance, whether it's None, Completed, or Scheduled.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any comments or notes related to the maintenance.
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`): Describe the current condition of the equipment.
* **Service Needed** (`service_needed`): If service is needed, provide more information here.
* **Last Maintenance Cost** (`last_maintenance_cost`): Enter the cost of the last maintenance, if applicable.
* **Last Maintenance Date** (`last_maintenance_date`): Enter the date of the last maintenance, if applicable.
* **Technician** (`technician`): Enter the name of the person who performed the last maintenance.
* **Facility** (`facility`): Enter the location or facility where the equipment is being maintained.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where the equipment is kept.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`): Add any final comments or remarks.
* **Equipment Number** (`equipment_number`): Enter the equipment number or identifier.
* **Equipment Model** (`equipment_model`): Enter the equipment model number.
* **Equipment Serial Number** (`equipment_serial_number`): Enter the equipment serial number.
* **Manufacturer** (`manufacturer`): Enter the manufacturer of the equipment.
* **Year of Purchase** (`equipment_year_of_purchase`): Enter the year the equipment was purchased.
* **Last Inspection By** (`last_inspection_by`): Enter the name of the person who performed the last inspection.
* **Next Inspection By** (`next_inspection_by`): Enter the name of the person who is scheduled to perform the next inspection.
* **Maintenance Due By** (`maintenance_due_by`): Enter the date by which maintenance is due.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Enter the name of the person assigned to perform the maintenance.
