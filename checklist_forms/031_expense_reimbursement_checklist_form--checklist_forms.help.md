# Expense Reimbursement Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Reimbursement Checklist Form is used to collect information about employee expenses for reimbursement purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
* Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), such as Expense Date, Expense Amount, Expense Type, Expense Submitter, and Expense Additional Comments.
* For fields with the label "Expense Type", "Expense Category", "Expense Subcategory", and "Expense Vendor", select the relevant options from the dropdown menu.
* For fields with the label "Expense Additional Attachments" and "Expense Attachment", you can upload files to support your expense claim.
* Fill in any additional comments or notes as required.

* **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`, number, *): The total amount of the expense.
