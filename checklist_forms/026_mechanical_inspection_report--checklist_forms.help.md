# Mechanical Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mechanical Inspection Report form is used to document the results of a mechanical inspection of a vehicle. This form helps mechanics and vehicle inspectors to gather information about the vehicle's condition, status, and any issues found during the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand the questions and fields that need to be completed.
2. Enter the vehicle's details, such as the vehicle's type, condition, and status.
3. Answer the questions regarding the vehicle's inspection result, test drive, odometer reading, and any comments.
4. Enter the date and time of the inspection.
5. Review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Vehicle Details
* **Vehicle Details** (vehicle_details, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's make, model, and year.
### 2. Vehicle Type
* **Vehicle Type** (vehicle_type, select_one, optional): Choose the type of vehicle (Yes/No).
### 3. Vehicle Condition
* **Vehicle Condition** (vehicle_condition, select_multiple, optional): Choose the vehicle's condition (new, used, certified).
### 4. Engine Details
* **Engine Details** (engine_details, select_one, optional): Choose the engine's condition (Yes/No).
### 5. Inspection Result
* **Inspection Result** (inspection_result, select_one, optional): Choose the outcome of the inspection (Yes/No).
### 6. Test Drive
* **Test Drive** (test_drive, number, optional): Enter the number of test drives conducted.
### 7. Odometer Reading
* **Odometer Reading** (odometer_reading, number, optional): Enter the current odometer reading.
### 8. Vehicle Status
* **Vehicle Status** (vehicle_status, select_one, optional): Choose the vehicle's status (Active/Inactive).
### 9. Vehicle Condition Rating
* **Vehicle Condition Rating** (vehicle_condition_rating, number, optional): Enter a rating of the vehicle's condition (1-5).
### 10. Comments
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the vehicle.
### 11. Date Inspected
* **Date Inspected** (date_inspected, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection.
### 12. Time Inspected
* **Time Inspected** (time_inspected, time, optional): Enter the time of the inspection.
### 13. Vehicle ID
* **Vehicle ID** (vehicle_id, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's ID or registration number.

Note: All fields are optional, but the more information you provide, the more comprehensive the report will be.
