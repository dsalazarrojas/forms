# Trading Card Organization Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trading Card Organization Checklist is designed to help users track and assess the condition, storage, and grading of their trading cards. This form provides a comprehensive evaluation of the cards' front and back sides, their condition, storage, and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each section carefully, ensuring you answer each question accurately to provide a complete assessment of your trading cards.
2.  Start with the front side checklist, where you will be asked about the condition, completeness, and any other notable features of the front side of your cards.
3.  Move on to the back side checklist, which will inquire about the condition, completeness, and any notable features of the back side of your cards.
4.  The condition checklist will ask you questions about the overall condition of the card, including scratches, creases, and tears.
5.  The card storage checklist will assess how you store and organize your trading cards, including their placement, order, and protection.
6.  The organization checklist will help you categorize and prioritize your trading cards, ensuring you keep them sorted and easily accessible.
7.  Finally, the grading scale will enable you to evaluate the rarity and value of your cards, based on your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Front Side Checklist** (`front_side_checklist`, text, required/optional): This section helps you evaluate the front side of your trading card. Describe the card's condition, including any scratches, creases, or tears. Also, indicate if the card is complete or incomplete.
- **Back Side Checklist** (`back_side_checklist`, text, required/optional): This section assesses the back side of your trading card. Describe the card's condition, including any scratches, creases, or tears. Also, indicate if the card is complete or incomplete.
- **Condition Checklist** (`condition_checklist`, text, required/optional): This section asks about the overall condition of your card. Evaluate its current state, including any scratches, creases, or tears. Consider indicating any other notable features that impact the card's condition.
- **Card Storage Checklist** (`card_storage_checklist`, text, required/optional): In this section, you will assess how you store and organize your trading cards. Describe the methods you employ to keep your cards in a safe and secure environment, including their placement, order, and protection.
- **Organization Checklist** (`organization_checklist`, text, required/optional): This section helps you categorize and prioritize your trading cards. Describe the methods and systems you use to keep your cards sorted and easily accessible.
- **Grading Scale** (`grading_scale`, text, required/optional): This final section enables you to evaluate the rarity and value of your cards, based on their condition and other factors. Use a rating system or scale to indicate the card's worth or desirability.
