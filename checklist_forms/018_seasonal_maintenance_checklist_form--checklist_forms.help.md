# Seasonal Maintenance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and record seasonal maintenance tasks for properties. It helps property managers, owners, or caretakers to track and manage tasks effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the season for which you want to create a maintenance schedule.
2. Choose the type of property you are checking (e.g., Single family home, Condo, etc.).
3. List the tasks to be performed during the selected season.
4. Enter any additional details or comments about the property's condition.
5. Select the date the maintenance schedule was last updated.
6. Add or remove tasks as needed from the list of seasonal maintenance tasks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Seasonal Tasks List (ID: 2, Label: Seasonal Tasks List)
* **Seasonal Tasks List** (`seasonal_tasks_list`, `select_multiple`, required: false): List the tasks you want to perform during the selected season. Choose one or more from the options: Spring cleaning, Summer maintenance, Winter maintenance.

### Property Details (ID: 3, Label: property_details)
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the property, such as its condition or recent changes.

### Season (ID: 4, Label: season)
* **Season** (`season`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the season for which you are creating a maintenance schedule. Options: Spring, Summer, Winter, Autumn.

### Property Type (ID: 5, Label: property_type)
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of property you are maintaining. Options: Single family home, Condo, Townhouse, Duplex.

### Contact Name (ID: 6, Label: contact_name)
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person responsible for maintenance, if any.

### Contact Email (ID: 7, Label: contact_email)
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the person responsible for maintenance, if any.

### Contact Phone (ID: 8, Label: contact_phone)
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for maintenance, if any.

### Seasonal Notes (ID: 9, Label: seasonal_notes)
* **Seasonal Notes** (`seasonal_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the property's condition or maintenance needs.

### Seasonal Maintenance Tasks (ID: 10, Label: seasonal_maintenance_tasks)
* **Seasonal Maintenance Tasks** (`seasonal_maintenance_tasks`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose one or more tasks to be performed during the selected season. Options: Plumbing, Electrical, HVAC, Exterior, Interior.

### Last Updated (ID: 11, Label: last_updated)
* **Last Updated** (`last_updated`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when the maintenance schedule was last updated.

### Property Season (ID: 12, Label: last_updated)
* **Property Season** (`last_updated`, `date`, required: false): No explanation for this field.

## Tips
* Use this form to create a comprehensive maintenance schedule for your property.
* Review and update your maintenance tasks regularly to stay on top of your property's needs.
* Consider adding or removing tasks as your property's condition or needs change over time.
