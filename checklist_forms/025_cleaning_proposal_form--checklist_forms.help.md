# Cleaning Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cleaning Proposal Form is designed to capture all the necessary information to create a comprehensive cleaning proposal for a client. This form guides the user through a series of sections to gather details about the client, their cleaning needs, and the proposal itself.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in a clear and legible manner.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Review and proofread the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it to the client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Company Information --** (Section 1)
	+ This section is for the cleaning company's information.
	+ **Company Name** (2): Enter the name of your company.
	+ **Company Address** (3): Enter your company's business address.
	+ **Company Phone** (4): Enter your company's business phone number.
	+ **Company Email** (5): Enter your company's business email address.
* **Client Information** (Section 2)
	+ **Client Name** (7): Enter the name of the client.
	+ **Client Email** (8): Enter the client's email address.
	+ **Client Phone** (9): Enter the client's phone number.
	+ **Client Address** (10): Enter the address of the property to be cleaned.
* **Proposal Details** (Section 3)
	+ **Proposal Date** (12): Enter the date this proposal is created.
	+ **Proposal ID Number** (13): Enter a unique identifier for this proposal.
	+ **Proposal Valid Until** (14): Enter the date the proposal expires.
* **Services Proposed** (Section 4)
	+ **Primary Service Type** (16): Select the type of cleaning service proposed (e.g., One-Time Cleaning, Recurring Weekly).
	+ **Service Frequency** (17): Select how often the service will be provided (e.g., One-Time, Weekly).
	+ **Proposed Start Date** (18): Enter when services will begin.
	+ **Property Type** (19): Select the type of property to be cleaned (e.g., Residential (Single Family), Residential (Apartment)).
	+ **Property Size (sq ft)** (20): Enter the approximate square footage of the property.
	+ **Areas to be Cleaned** (21): Select all areas to be cleaned (e.g., Kitchen, Bathrooms).
* **Pricing** (Section 5)
	+ **Service Cost per Cleaning** (23): Enter the price per service visit.
	+ **Frequency Discount** (24): Enter any discount for recurring service.
	+ **Additional Services Cost** (25): Enter the cost of any extra services.
	+ **Estimated Monthly Cost** (26): Enter the estimated monthly cost based on proposed frequency.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Section 6)
	+ **Cancellation Policy** (30): Enter the terms for canceling service.
	+ **Additional Terms and Conditions** (31): Enter any special agreements or conditions.
* **Acceptance** (Section 7)
	+ **I Accept** (32): Select to accept the terms of this proposal.

Note: This guide is a general help document and might not cover every possible scenario. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please refer to your supervisor or contact us.
