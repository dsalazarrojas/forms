# Wildlife Observation Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect wildlife observation data for conservation or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one species for each observation from the drop-down menu.
2. Enter the location of the observation.
3. Choose the behavior(s) you observed.
4. Enter the date and time of the observation.
5. Add any additional notes about the observation.
6. Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you if needed.
