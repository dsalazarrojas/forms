# CPR Chest Compression Feedback Device Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by medical professionals to inspect and evaluate the condition of a CPR Chest Compression Feedback Device. It helps ensure that the device is in good working order and meets the necessary standards for safe and effective use in medical emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, enter the date of the inspection in the "Date of Inspection" field.
2.  Enter your name and title in the "Inspector Name" field.
3.  Provide the unique identifier or serial number of the device in the "Device ID or Serial Number" field.
4.  Specify the type of device in the "Device Type" field (if applicable).
5.  Enter the location where the device is stored or used in the "Device Location" field.
6.  If applicable, enter the date of the last inspection in the "Date of Last Inspection" field.
7.  Evaluate the physical condition of the device in the "Physical Condition" field, using the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Damaged).
8.  Check if the display screen is functional in the "Display Screen Functional" field (True or False).
9.  Verify if all buttons and controls are working properly in the "All Buttons and Controls Working" field (True or False).
10. Check the battery status (Excellent - full charge, Good - adequate charge, Fair - low charge, Poor - needs replacement) and if replacement is needed in the "Battery Status" and "Battery Replacement Needed" fields.
11. Confirm the sensor calibration status (In calibration, Out of calibration, Unknown) in the "Sensor Calibration Status" field.
12. Check the accuracy of the sensor (Accurate, Minor discrepancy, Major discrepancy, Not tested) in the "Sensor Accuracy Check" field (if applicable).
13. Verify that all cable connections are secure in the "Cable Connections Secure" field (True or False).
14. Confirm that the audio feedback function is working (True, False, Partially) in the "Audio Feedback Function" field.
15. Check if the software is updated to the latest version in the "Software Updated to Latest Version" field (True or False, Unknown).
16. Confirm if the device has been cleaned and sanitized in the "Device Cleaned and Sanitized" field (True or False).
17. Evaluate the inspection approval status (Approved - ready to use, Approved with caution, Conditional - needs repair, Not approved - remove from service) in the "Inspection Approval Status" field.
18. If necessary, describe any maintenance or repairs needed in the "Maintenance or Repairs Needed" field.
19. Schedule the next inspection date in the "Scheduled Next Inspection Date" field.
