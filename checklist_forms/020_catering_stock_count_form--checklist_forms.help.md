# Catering Stock Count Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assist in maintaining an accurate stock count of catering supplies and inventory.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the count date and time, this will ensure that the count is accurate for the specific date and time being recorded.
2. Fill out the 'Count Location' field, specifying the specific location or site where the stock count is being recorded from.
3. Enter the 'Counted By' field with the name of the staff member completing the count.
4. For the perishable item stock levels, enter the quantities in pounds or units, according to the type of item (e.g. proteins, vegetables, dairy products, etc.).
5. For non-perishable items such as grains, pasta, oils, condiments, spices, seasonings, canned goods, and beverages, enter the quantities in units.
6. Finally, add any additional notes or observations about the stock count.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Count Date** (`count_date`, required): Enter the date the stock count is being recorded for.
* **Count Time** (`count_time`, required): Enter the time the stock count is being recorded for.
* **Count Location** (`count_location`, required): Enter the specific location or site where the stock count is being recorded from.
* **Counted By (Staff Name)** (`counted_by_name`, required): Enter the name of the staff member completing the count.
* **Proteins (lbs)** (`proteins_stock`, required): Enter the quantity of protein-based items (e.g. chicken, beef, pork, fish etc.).
* **Vegetables (lbs)** (`vegetables_stock`, required): Enter the quantity of fresh vegetables.
* **Dairy Products (units)** (`dairy_stock`, required): Enter the quantity of dairy products (e.g. milk, cheese, butter, yogurt etc.).
* **Fresh Produce (units)** (`produce_stock`, required): Enter the quantity of fresh fruits and salad greens.
* **Grains and Pasta (lbs)** (`grains_pasta`, required): Enter the quantity of grains and pasta.
* **Oils and Condiments (units)** (`oils_condiments`, required): Enter the quantity of oils and condiments (e.g. olive oil, vinegar, sauces etc.).
* **Spices and Seasonings (units)** (`spices_seasonings`, required): Enter the quantity of spices and seasonings (e.g. salt, pepper, herbs, spice mixes etc.).
* **Canned Goods (units)** (`canned_goods`, required): Enter the quantity of canned goods (e.g. canned vegetables, fruits, beans etc.).
* **Coffee and Tea (units)** (`coffee_tea`, optional): Enter the quantity of coffee and tea (e.g. bags, boxes, containers etc.).
* **Juices and Soft Drinks (units)** (`juice_beverages`, optional): Enter the quantity of juices and soft drinks (e.g. bottles, cases, cartons etc.).
* **Alcoholic Beverages (units)** (`alcoholic_beverages`, optional): Enter the quantity of alcoholic beverages (e.g. wine, beer, spirits etc.).
* **Serving Ware (units)** (`serving_ware`, optional): Enter the quantity of plates, utensils, napkins, etc.
* **Packaging Supplies (units)** (`packaging_supplies`, optional): Enter the quantity of packaging materials (e.g. containers, bags, wrap, foil etc.).
* **Items Needing Reorder** (`shortage_items`, optional): List any items that need to be reordered due to low stock levels.
* **Additional Notes or Observations** (`inventory_notes`, optional): Record any additional notes or observations about the stock count (e.g. expiration dates, damage, quality issues etc.).

## Tips

* Be accurate and thorough when filling out this form to ensure an up-to-date stock count.
* Review this form before starting the process to ensure you understand its purpose and requirements.
* Complete all required fields to ensure the stock count is accurate and comprehensive.
* Use the 'Items Needing Reorder' field to identify items that need to be restocked.
* The 'Additional Notes or Observations' field can be used to document any issues or concerns with the stock count.
