# Drone Preflight Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The drone preflight checklist is a critical tool for drone pilots to verify that their drone is ready for safe and successful flights.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and select the correct options for your drone's current status.
2. Ensure that you have the most up-to-date information about your drone's configuration and status.
3. Update this form as needed based on changes to your drone's configuration or status.
