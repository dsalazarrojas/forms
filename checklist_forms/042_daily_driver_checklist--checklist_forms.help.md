> # daily_driver_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The daily driver checklist is a form used to record and track information about a vehicle's status, condition, and maintenance history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vehicle details page with the vehicle's make, year, model, and license number.
2. Record the odometer reading and fuel level of the vehicle.
3. Select the current status of the vehicle (e.g., Vehicle Available, Vehicle Not Available, etc.).
4. Choose the vehicle's condition (e.g., Excellent, Fair, Poor).
5. Enter the driver's name, phone number, and email.
6. Select the dates for the last maintenance, service, inspection, next maintenance, and next service.
7. Record any comments or observations about the vehicle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vehicle Details**: Input the vehicle's make, year, and model number.
  - **Make**: Enter the vehicle's make (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.).
  - **Year**: Enter the vehicle's year.
  - **Model**: Enter the vehicle's model number.
  - **License Number**: Enter the vehicle's license number.
- **Vehicle Status**: Select the current status of the vehicle.
  - **Vehicle Available**: The vehicle is available for use.
  - **Vehicle Not Available**: The vehicle is not available for use.
  - **Vehicle In Maintenance**: The vehicle is currently undergoing maintenance.
  - **Vehicle In Repair**: The vehicle is currently being repaired.
  - **Vehicle In Transit**: The vehicle is in transit or being transported.
- **Vehicle Condition**: Select the current condition of the vehicle.
  - **Excellent**: The vehicle is in excellent condition.
  - **Fair**: The vehicle is in fair condition.
  - **Poor**: The vehicle is in poor condition.
- **Driver Information**: Input the driver's details.
  - **Driver Name**: Enter the driver's name.
  - **Driver Phone**: Enter the driver's phone number.
  - **Driver Email**: Enter the driver's email.
- **Maintenance and Service**: Record the dates for the last maintenance, service, and inspection.
  - **Last Maintenance**: Record the date of the last maintenance.
  - **Last Service**: Record the date of the last service.
  - **Last Inspection**: Record the date of the last inspection.
  - **Next Maintenance**: Record the date of the next maintenance.
  - **Next Service**: Record the date of the next service.
- **Warranty and Registration**: Record the dates for the last and next registration, and last warranty expiration.
  - **Last Registration**: Record the date of the last registration.
  - **Last Warranty**: Record the date of the last warranty.
  - **Last Warranty Expiration**: Record the date of the last warranty expiration.
  - **Next Registration**: Record the date of the next registration.
- **Additional Information**: Record any comments or observations about the vehicle.
