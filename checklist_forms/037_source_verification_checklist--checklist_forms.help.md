# Source Verification Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect and verify information about an individual or entity, covering various aspects such as background checks, contact details, employment history, and more. It is intended to provide a comprehensive overview of the individual's or entity's background and history.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. For background checks, please provide as much information as possible about your employment history, social media, and other relevant details.
3. Ensure all sections are completed thoroughly to avoid delays in the verification process.
4. Attach any supporting documents or proof as required, where applicable.
