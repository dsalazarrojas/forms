# Event Budget Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Budget Checklist Form is designed to gather information about an event's budget and logistical details. This form is used to ensure that all relevant information is collected and organized in one place.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by reviewing the form's pages to understand the sequence of fields.
2. Start by filling out the first page with the title and event details.
3. Move on to the subsequent pages, filling out each field according to the instructions provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Budget Checklist Form 1**: Enter the title of the event.
* **Budget Venue**: Enter the budget allocated for the venue (in dollars).
* **Budget Catering**: Enter the budget allocated for catering services (in dollars).
* **Budget Entertainment**: Enter the budget allocated for entertainment and activities (in dollars).
* **Budget Marketing**: Enter the budget allocated for marketing and promotion (in dollars).
* **Venue Name**: Enter the name of the venue where the event will take place.
* **Venue Address**: Enter the address of the venue where the event will take place.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event (in the format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event (in the format: HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the event.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for the event.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the event.
* **Budget Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the budget.
* **Event Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the event.
* **Marketing Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about marketing and promotion.
* **Event Budget Total**: Enter the total budget for the event (in dollars).
* **Venue Price**: Enter the price of the venue rental (in dollars).
* **Entertainment Price**: Enter the cost of entertainment and activities (in dollars).
* **Marketing Price**: Enter the cost of marketing and promotion (in dollars).

Note: All fields are optional and can be filled out as necessary.
