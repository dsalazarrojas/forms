# Your Dream Home Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Your Dream Home Checklist Form" is a tool designed to help you identify and prioritize the features and characteristics of your ideal home. This form will guide you through a series of questions that cover various aspects of a property, from its location to its amenities. By completing this form, you will be able to create a comprehensive list of requirements that will help you find your dream home.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reviewing the available options for each field. Select your desired property type, location, and other features from the dropdown menus.
2. Fill in the price range and square footage of your desired property.
3. Answer the questions about the number of bedrooms, bathrooms, and other features you are looking for in your home.
4. Consider the lot size and orientation you prefer.
5. Indicate whether you want a property with or without a porch, yard, pool, garage, basement, attic, fireplace, garden, play area, security system, elevator, fire suppression system, water source, sewage disposal, or any other features that are important to you.
6. Finally, review your selections to ensure they accurately reflect your dream home requirements.
