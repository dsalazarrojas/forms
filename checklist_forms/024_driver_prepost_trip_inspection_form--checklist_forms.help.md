# driver_prepost_trip_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data for the pre and post trip inspection of a vehicle. It helps to ensure that the vehicle is in good condition before and after a trip, and to record any issues that may have occurred during transportation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the options for each field carefully to ensure you are selecting the correct information.
2. Fill out the form for both the pre and post trip inspections.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre Trip** (Note): This is a general notes field where you can record any notes before the trip.
* **Vehicle Type** (Select One):
