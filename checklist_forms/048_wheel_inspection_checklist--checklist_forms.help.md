# Wheel Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wheel Inspection Checklist is a form used to collect data on vehicle wheels. It includes fields for inspection status, condition, and other relevant information to ensure that vehicles are properly maintained and safe for use.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Vehicle ID field with the unique identifier for the vehicle being inspected.
2. Select the Inspection Date from the calendar to indicate when the wheel inspection was performed.
3. Choose the Inspection Status from the dropdown menu to indicate the overall status of the wheel inspection (Normal, Abnormal, or Repair).
4. Enter any additional Notes about the inspection in the Notes field.
5. Select the Asset ID to identify the asset being inspected (if applicable).
6. Enter the Equipment ID for the vehicle's equipment (if applicable).
7. Select the Equipment Condition from the dropdown menu to indicate the overall condition of the equipment (Good, Fair, or Poor).
8. Choose the Wheel Rotation Status from the dropdown menu to indicate whether the wheels are rotated (Right, Left).
9. Enter the Wheel Pressure reading.
10. Select the Wheel Temperature from the dropdown menu to indicate the temperature of the wheel (High, Low, or Normal).
11. Select the Wheel Alignment from the dropdown menu to indicate the alignment of the wheel (Correct, Incorrect).
12. Choose the Wheel Braking status from the dropdown menu (Good, Fair, or Poor).
13. Select the Wheel Tires status from the dropdown menu (Good, Fair, or Poor).
14. Choose the Wheel Lamp status from the dropdown menu (Normal, Abnormal).
15. Select the Wheel Condition from the dropdown menu to indicate the overall condition of the wheel (Good, Fair, or Poor).
16. Enter any additional comments or notes in the Comments field.
17. Enter the name of the user who submitted the form.
18. Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
19. Enter the name of the user who last updated the form (if applicable).
20. Enter the date and time the form was last updated (if applicable).
21. Enter the name of the user who created the form.
22. Enter the date and time the form was created.
23. Select the Asset Status from the dropdown menu to indicate the status of the asset (Active, Dormant, or Decommissioned).
24. Select the Wheel Type from the dropdown menu to indicate the type of wheel (Aluminum, Steel, or Composite).
