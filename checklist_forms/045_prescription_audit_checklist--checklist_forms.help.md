# Prescription Audit Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Prescription Audit Checklist is a form used for auditing and verifying prescriptions to ensure that they are being properly filled and managed by healthcare professionals. This form helps identify any discrepancies or issues with prescriptions, ensuring that patients receive the correct medication and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the Patient Details section with the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant medical information.
2. In the Medication Name field, enter the name of the prescribed medication.
3. Enter the Medication Dosage in the corresponding field, which should be a numerical value representing the amount of medication to be taken.
4. In the Start Date field, select the date the medication was prescribed.
5. Enter the End Date field, if applicable, representing the date the medication was expected to end.
6. If necessary, add a Medical Condition that the medication is intended to treat.
7. Enter the Medication Frequency, which indicates how often the patient should take the medication.
8. In the Doctor Name field, enter the name of the prescribing doctor.
9. In the Prescription Number field, enter the unique identifier number for the prescription.
10. Leave the Patient Signature and Doctor Signature fields for the patient and doctor to sign and date, respectively, when the form is reviewed.
11. Finally, fill in the Pharmacy Name field with the name of the pharmacy where the prescription was filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details** (text, required): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant medical information.
* **Medication Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the prescribed medication.
* **Medication Dosage** (number, required): Enter the amount of medication to be taken.
* **Start Date** (date, required): Select the date the medication was prescribed.
* **End Date** (date, optional): If applicable, enter the date the medication was expected to end.
* **Medical Condition** (text, optional): If necessary, enter the medical condition the medication is intended to treat.
* **Medication Frequency** (text, optional): Enter how often the patient should take the medication.
* **Doctor Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the prescribing doctor.
* **Prescription Number** (text, optional): Enter the unique identifier number for the prescription.
* **Patient Signature** (note, required): Leave space for the patient to sign and date when the form is reviewed.
* **Doctor Signature** (note, required): Leave space for the doctor to sign and date when the form is reviewed.
* **Pharmacy Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the pharmacy where the prescription was filled.
