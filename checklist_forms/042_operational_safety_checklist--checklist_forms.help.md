# Operational Safety Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Operational Safety Checklist is used to document and track safety checks performed on equipment, work areas, and teams. This helps in maintaining a safe and healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form to ensure that you have all the necessary information to complete it accurately.
2. Select the correct equipment type from the options provided.
3. List all safety equipment available for each selected equipment type.
4. Enter the work area or zone number where the safety check was performed.
5. Enter the date and time of the safety check (in hours and minutes).
6. List the team members involved in the safety check.
7. Add any additional comments or notes regarding this submission, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Operational Safety Checklist Form** (`introduction`, `text`, not required):
  This field is a heading for the form, and should not be filled out. It is a placeholder for the form title.

* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, `select_one`, not required):
  Select the type of equipment that you are checking (e.g., General Safety Equipment, Specialized Equipment, Other).

* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, `text`, not required):
  List all safety equipment available for the selected equipment type.

* **Work Area** (`work_area`, `text`, not required):
  Enter the work area or zone number where the safety check was performed.

* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, not required):
  Enter the date of the safety check in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, `time`, not required):
  Enter the time of the safety check in hours and minutes.

* **Team Members Involved** (`team_members`, `text`, not required):
  List the names and roles of team members involved in the safety check.

* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required):
  Add any additional comments or notes regarding this submission, if necessary.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have all necessary information before completing this form.
- Review the form carefully to avoid errors or missed information.
- If you are unsure about any information, please ask your supervisor or safety officer for clarification.
- Please keep the additional comments field for any additional information that may be helpful for the safety team.
