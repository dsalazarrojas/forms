# Donor Records Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Records Checklist Form is used to collect and verify donor information, including donation frequency, amount, and date, as well as contact details such as name, phone number, email, and address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as required.
2. Select the correct frequency of your donations from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the amount of your donation.
4. Enter the date of your donation.
5. Add any additional notes about your gifts (optional).
6. Enter your contact information, including name, phone number, email, address, city, state, and country.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, text, optional): This field is used to capture any additional information about the donor.
* **Gift Frequency** (`gift_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of your donations from the options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Gift Amount** (`gift_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Gift Date** (`gift_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your donation.
* **Gift Notes** (`gift_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your gifts.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field is used to capture any additional information about the contact person.
* **Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Address** (`contact_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the contact person.
* **City** (`contact_city`, text, required): Enter the city of the contact person's address.
* **State** (`contact_state`, text, required): Enter the state of the contact person's address.
* **Zip** (`contact_zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code of the contact person's address.
* **Country** (`contact_country`, text, required): Enter the country of the contact person's address.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the contact person.
