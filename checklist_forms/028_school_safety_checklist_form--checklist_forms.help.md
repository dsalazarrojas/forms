# School Safety Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about the school's safety measures, emergency contact details, and procedures for responding to emergencies. It helps school administrators and staff assess the school's safety preparedness and take corrective actions if necessary.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and answer all the questions truthfully.
2. Enter the required information for each field.
3. For fields with multiple options (e.g., select_multiple, select_one), choose the most accurate option(s) that apply to your situation.
4. Ensure that all fields marked as "required" are filled out completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the person who should be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **First Aider Location** (`first_aider_location`, text, required): Describe the location of the first aid room or the nearest emergency exit.
* **Fire Extinguishers** (`fire_extinguishers`, select_multiple, required): Confirm the presence and availability of fire extinguishers in the school. Choose 'Yes' if they are present and functioning correctly.
* **Fire Drills** (`fire_drills`, select_one, required): Confirm if fire drills are conducted regularly and according to school procedures. Choose 'Yes' if they are conducted.
* **Emergency Exit Routes** (`emergency_exit_routes`, text, required): Describe the emergency exit routes and their locations within the school.
* **Fire Drill Procedures** (`fire_drill_procedures`, select_one, required): Confirm if the school has procedures for conducting fire drills. Choose 'Yes' if they do.
* **Emergency Contact Numbers** (`emergency_contact_numbers`, select_multiple, required): Confirm the presence of emergency contact numbers. Choose 'Yes' if numbers are available.
* **Safety Measures** (`safety_measures`, text, required): Describe the safety measures in place for the school, such as evacuation procedures and fire extinguisher maintenance.
* **Fire Alarm System** (`fire_alarm_system`, select_multiple, required): Confirm the presence and functionality of the fire alarm system. Choose 'Yes' if it is active and working correctly.
* **Emergency Response Procedures** (`emergency_response_procedures`, text, required): Describe the emergency response procedures in place for the school.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the person who should be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required): Provide any additional contact information for the emergency contact person.
* **Fire Drill Frequency** (`fire_drill_frequency`, select_one, required): Confirm if fire drills are conducted regularly. Choose 'Yes' if they are conducted at least once a month.
* **Emergency Contact Backup** (`emergency_contact_backup`, select_one, required): Confirm if a backup emergency contact is available. Choose 'Yes' if a backup contact is designated.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required): Enter any additional contact information for the backup emergency contact.
* **Fire Alarm System Frequency** (`fire_alarm_system_frequency`, select_one, required): Confirm if the fire alarm system is tested and maintained regularly. Choose 'Yes' if it is tested and maintained.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_alternate`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of an alternate emergency contact person.
* **Safety Measures Procedures** (`safety_measures_procedures`, text, optional): Describe the safety measures in place for the school, such as fire extinguisher maintenance and inspection schedules.
* **Emergency Exit Route Directions** (`emergency_exit_route_directions`, text, required): Describe the directions to follow in case of an emergency exit.
* **Fire Extinguisher Location** (`fire_extinguisher_location`, text, required): Describe the location of fire extinguishers in the school.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_alternate_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information for the alternate emergency contact person.
* **Safety Measures Review** (`safety_measures_review`, text, required): Describe the last safety review and any recommended improvements.
* **Fire Alarm System Status** (`fire_alarm_system_status`, select_one, required): Confirm the status of the fire alarm system. Choose 'Active' if it is functional and working correctly.
* **Alternate Emergency Exit Route** (`emergency_exit_route_alternate`, select_one, optional): Confirm if an alternate emergency exit route is available. Choose 'Yes' if a route is designated.
* **Fire Extinguisher Status** (`fire_extinguisher_status`, select_one, required): Confirm the status of the fire extinguishers. Choose 'Active' if they are available and functional.
 
Note: Some fields may appear to be redundant or have unclear labels, please use the best-effort explanation above as a guidance.
