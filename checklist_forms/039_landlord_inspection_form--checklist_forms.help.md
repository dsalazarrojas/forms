# Landlord Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the condition and safety features of a property, allowing you to assess its suitability for rental or purchase.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information available.
2. Select the relevant options for each field from the provided lists.
3. Enter the required information for each field.
4. Submit the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Appliances** (`appliances`): Select the appliances present in the property, such as Dishwasher, Refrigerator, Stove, and Microwave.
* **Heating System** (`heating_system`): Choose the primary heating system used in the property, such as Gas, Electric, or Solar.
* **Plumbing Systems** (`plumbing_systems`): Select the plumbing systems present in the property, such as Water heater, Sinks, or Toilets.
* **Electrical Systems** (`electrical_system`): Choose the electrical systems present in the property, such as Fuses, Circuit breakers, or Electrical panel.
* **Fire Suppression** (`fire_suppression`): Select the fire suppression system present in the property, such as Fire alarm or Fire extinguisher.
* **Fire Safety** (`fire_safety`): Choose the fire safety features present in the property, such as Smoke detector, Emergency exit, or Fire alarm.
* **Safety Features** (`safety_features`): Select the safety features present in the property, such as Carbon monoxide detector, Security cameras, or Smoke detector.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the contact information for the emergency contact person.
* **Building Features** (`building_features`): Choose the building features present in the property, such as Pool, Balcony, or Parking lot.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`): Enter the address of the property.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Additional Comments** (not shown in the field list, but implied by the context): If you have any additional comments or observations, please enter them in the comments field.
