# unclaimed_property_verification_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to verify the existence of unclaimed properties. It is completed by someone with knowledge about the property in question to gather information and confirm its status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reading the introduction carefully and understanding the purpose of the form.
2. Enter the property details in the "Property Details" field.
3. Indicate the verification teams involved in the process by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Verification Teams" field.
4. Enter any relevant information about the claimant in the "Claimant Info" field (if required).
5. Select the review status of the property in the "Review Status" field.
6. Enter the date the property was reviewed in the "Date Reviewed" field.
7. Enter the time the property was reviewed in the "Time Reviewed" field.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field (if required).
9. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (if required).
10. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (if required).
11. Upload any supporting documents in the "Uploaded Documents" field.
12. Enter any additional review notes in the "Review Notes" field (if required).
13. Enter the name of the person who verified the property in the "Verified By" field (if required).
14. Enter the date the property was verified in the "Verified On" field.
15. Select the verification status of the property in the "Verification Status" field.
16. Select the verification type in the "Review Type" field.
17. Upload any additional documents in the "More Documents" field.
18. Upload any further documents in the "Additional Documents" field.
19. Enter any additional review notes in the "Notes" field (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Details (2)**: Enter a brief description of the unclaimed property.
* **Supporting Documents (3)**: Upload any documents related to the property.
* **Verification Teams (4)**: Select "Yes" if you are a verification team, otherwise select "No".
* **Claimant Info (5)**: Enter any relevant information about the claimant (if required).
* **Review Status (6)**: Select the current review status of the property.
* **Date Reviewed (7)**: Enter the date the property was reviewed.
* **Time Reviewed (8)**: Enter the time the property was reviewed.
* **Notes (9)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the property (if required).
* **Email (10)**: Enter your email address (if required).
* **Phone (11)**: Enter your phone number (if required).
* **Signature (12)**: Enter your signature (required).
* **Uploaded Documents (13)**: Upload any additional documents related to the property.
* **Review Notes (14)**: Enter any additional review notes (if required).
* **Verified By (15)**: Enter the name of the person who verified the property (if required).
* **Verified On (16)**: Enter the date the property was verified.
* **Verification Status (17)**: Select the current verification status of the property.
* **Review Type (18)**: Select the type of review (if required).
* **More Documents (19)**: Upload any additional documents related to the property.
* **Additional Documents (20)**: Upload any further documents related to the property.
* **Further Documents (21)**: Upload any further documents related to the property.
* **Uploaded Documents 2 (23)**: Upload any additional documents related to the property.
* **Uploaded Documents 3 (24)**: Upload any additional documents related to the property.
