# First Aid Kit Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to inspect and report the status of a First Aid Kit in your area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will be asked to answer several questions regarding the status of the First Aid Kit. Please answer each field as accurately as possible and indicate your findings.
