# product-research-checklist-for-online-sellers - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to help online sellers plan and prepare for product launch by gathering information about product research, target market, pricing, competition, launch planning, and budget. This form is designed to be used as a checklist to ensure that all necessary aspects of a product launch are considered before launching the product.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please fill out the following fields, taking into account the specific requirements and options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product idea** (`product_idea`): Provide a brief description of your product, including its main features and benefits.
* **Target market** (`target_market`): Identify your target audience, including their demographics, needs, and preferences.
* **Product description** (`product_description`): Give a detailed description of your product, including its features, benefits, and unique selling points.
* **Price range** (`price_range`): Specify the price range of your product, including the minimum and maximum prices.
* **Competition research** (`competition_research`): Provide a brief summary of your research on the competition, including their pricing, features, and market presence.
* **Product research checklist** (`product_research_checklist`): Select the topics that you want to research for your product, including Pricing strategy, Product features, Product benefits, Product positioning, Target audience, Market trends, Competition, and Other.
* **Launch planning** (`launch_planning`): Indicate whether you have a plan for product launch, and if so, what kind of plan you have.
* **Product launch plan** (`product_launch_plan`): Provide a detailed description of your product launch plan, including any specific details and timelines.
* **Sourcing and launch** (`sourcing_and_launch`): Select the channels through which you plan to source and launch your product, including Amazon, eBay, Facebook, Instagram, and Other.
* **Sourcing and launch plan** (`sourcing_and_launch_plan`): Provide a detailed description of your plan for sourcing and launching your product through the selected channels.
* **Sourcing channel** (`sourcing_channel`): Choose the specific channel through which you plan to source and launch your product, including Amazon, eBay, Facebook, Instagram, and Other.
* **Sourcing channel plan** (`sourcing_channel_plan`): Provide a detailed description of your plan for sourcing and launching your product through the selected channel.
* **Launch channel** (`launch_channel`): Select the channels through which you plan to launch your product, including Amazon, eBay, Facebook, Instagram, and Other.
* **Launch channel plan** (`launch_channel_plan`): Provide a detailed description of your plan for launching your product through the selected channel.
* **Launch channel lead time** (`launch_channel_lead_time`): Specify the lead time for each launch channel, including the number of days or weeks between product launch and launch completion.
* **Launch channel lead time plan** (`launch_channel_lead_time_plan`): Provide a detailed description of your plan for handling the lead time for each launch channel.
* **Product launch date** (`product_launch_date`): Specify the date of product launch, if available.
* **Product launch date plan** (`product_launch_date_plan`): Provide a detailed description of your plan for product launch, including any specific details and timelines.
* **Product launch budget** (`product_launch_budget`): Specify the budget for product launch, including any costs associated with sourcing, marketing, and other expenses.
* **Product launch budget plan** (`product_launch_budget_plan`): Provide a detailed description of your plan for managing the budget for product launch.
* **Product launch risk** (`product_launch_risk`): Identify the potential risks associated with product launch, including market risks, competition risks, and other risks.
* **Product launch risk plan** (`product_launch_risk_plan`): Provide a detailed description of your plan for mitigating the identified risks.
* **Product launch review** (`product_launch_review`): Specify any reviews or feedback that you have received about your product, including positive and negative reviews.
* **Product launch review plan** (`product_launch_review_plan`): Provide a detailed description of your plan for handling product reviews and feedback.
* **Product launch review date** (`product_launch_review_date`): Specify the date when the first product review was received, if available.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the fields to ensure a comprehensive plan for product launch.
* Review and update your plan regularly to account for any changes in market trends, competition, or other factors that may impact your product launch.
* Consider seeking advice from experts or colleagues to ensure that your plan is thorough and effective.
