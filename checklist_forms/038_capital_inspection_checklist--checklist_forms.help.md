# Capital Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Capital Inspection Checklist is used to document the condition of capital assets and identify maintenance needs.

## How To Complete This Form
This form should be completed by the inspector after inspecting a capital asset. Please follow the steps below:

1.  Read the introduction to understand the purpose and importance of the form.
2.  Complete the required fields as accurately as possible.
3.  Use the sections to rate and describe the condition of the asset.
4.  Select all recommended actions based on the condition of the asset.
5.  Enter the estimated repair or maintenance cost if applicable.
6.  Complete any additional comments or observations.

**Tips**

*   This form is used to document the condition of capital assets and identify maintenance needs.
*   The form is divided into several sections, each addressing a different aspect of the asset's condition.
*   Complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the asset is properly maintained and any issues are addressed in a timely manner.
*   Use the form for capital assets only.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the relevant section for clarification.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or facility management.
