# framing_punch_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and track tasks performed during framing and installation of punch list items.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the appropriate options for "required_tools" and ensure you check all the tools that apply to the task.
2. In the "task_list" field, list the tasks that need to be completed during the installation.
3. In the "equipment_used" field, list the equipment used during the task, one per line.
4. In the "task_requirements" field, provide any specific requirements or notes about the task.
5. In the "additional_notes" field, provide any additional notes or comments about the task.
6. In the "tools_used" field, list the tools used during the task, one per line.
7. In the "equipment_notes" field, provide any notes or comments about the equipment used.
8. In the "assigned_tool" field, list any tools that were assigned to specific technicians for the task.
9. In the "assigned_technician" field, list any technicians assigned to specific tasks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **task_list** (`task_list`, text, required/optional): Enter a list of tasks that need to be completed during the installation.
* **required_tools** (`required_tools`, select multiple, required/optional): Check all the tools that apply to the task.
* **equipment_used** (`equipment_used`, text, required/optional): List the equipment used during the task.
* **task_requirements** (`task_requirements`, text, required/optional): Provide any specific requirements or notes about the task.
* **additional_notes** (`additional_notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the task.
* **tools_used** (`tools_used`, text, required/optional): List the tools used during the task.
* **equipment_notes** (`equipment_notes`, text, required/optional): Provide any notes or comments about the equipment used.
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required/optional): List any tools that were assigned to specific technicians for the task.
* **assigned_technician** (`assigned_technician`, text, required/optional): List any technicians assigned to specific tasks.
